# disciplinary_notes_and_communications_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Disciplinary Notes and Communications Form** is used to document disciplinary actions and notes related to employee conduct. This form is intended for use in workplace settings where employee behavior or performance may need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of communication from the options provided: Termination, Warning, Praise, or Meeting.
2. Enter the date of the communication (if applicable).
3. Enter the time of the communication (if applicable).
4. Provide a brief description of the communication details.
5. Document any disciplinary actions taken or recommended.
6. Assess the employee's conduct as Good, Bad, or Neutral.
7. Obtain signatures from both the employee and the manager to confirm understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Communication Types**: Select the type of communication that occurred. This helps categorize and track different types of interactions.
* **Communication Date**: Enter the date the communication took place. This helps with record-keeping and tracking progress over time.
* **Communication Time**: Enter the time the communication took place. This provides more detailed information for future reference.
* **Communication Details**: Provide a brief description of what was discussed or addressed during the communication.
* **Disciplinary Actions**: Document any actions taken or recommended as a result of the communication.
* **Employee Conduct**: Assess the employee's conduct as Good, Bad, or Neutral. This helps evaluate performance and guide future interactions.
* **Employee Signature**: Obtain a signature from the employee to confirm understanding and agreement with the documented communication.
* **Manager Signature**: Obtain a signature from the manager to confirm understanding and agreement with the documented communication.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date the employee and manager signed the form. This ensures both parties are aware of the timestamp.

## Tips
* Make sure to keep the form concise and clear, with enough detail to facilitate thorough documentation.
* Use the correct communication type to categorize and track interactions.
* Ensure both the employee and manager sign the form to confirm understanding and agreement.
* Keep the form updated and accessible for future reference.
