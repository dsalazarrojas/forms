# Disciplinary Action Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Action Absence Form is used to document instances of employee absences from work, including reasons and duration, and to record the disciplinary action taken.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the employee name from the employee directory.
2.  Enter the start date of the absence in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3.  Enter the end date of the absence in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. If this is a one-time absence, please leave this field blank.
4.  Select the reason for the absence from the dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the number of days absent in numerical format.
6.  Enter the manager's name as it appears on their business card or ID card.
7.  Enter the date of the last contact with the employee in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. If this is not applicable, leave blank.
8.  Enter the date of the disciplinary action in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. If this is not applicable, leave blank.
9.  Enter a brief reason for the disciplinary action (if applicable).
10.  Enter the manager's signature as it appears on their business card or ID card.
11.  Enter the date of the manager's signature in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
12.  Enter the manager's title.
13.  Enter the employee's ID.
14.  Enter the manager's ID.
15.  Enter the form ID.
16.  Enter the version of the form.
17.  Enter the ID of the person who created the form.
