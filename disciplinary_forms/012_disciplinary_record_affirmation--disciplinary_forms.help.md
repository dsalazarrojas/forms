# Disciplinary Record Affirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track incidents within the organizational context. It captures essential details about incidents, such as date, time, location, and outcome, to ensure accurate and comprehensive record-keeping. The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and consistent way to report and manage incidents, facilitating effective communication and decision-making within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date and time of the incident in the corresponding fields. Use the 24-hour format for time.
2. Provide a brief description of the incident in the Incident Description field.
3. Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g., verbal warning, written warning, suspension, or termination) from the Incident Type dropdown menu.
4. Briefly describe the incident outcome (e.g., resolved, pending, or pending review) in the Incident Outcome field.
5. If applicable, provide details about the participants involved in the incident.
6. Specify the action taken as a result of the incident in the Action Taken field.
7. Indicate who took the action (e.g., HR, supervisor, or manager) in the Action Taken by field.
8. Enter a unique identifier for the disciplinary record in the Disciplinary Record ID field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the incident in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Describe the incident in a few sentences.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of incident that occurred from the dropdown menu.
* **Incident Participants** (`incident_participants`, text, required: false): List the individuals involved in the incident.
* **Incident Outcome** (`incident_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Choose the outcome of the incident from the dropdown menu.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the action taken as a result of the incident.
* **Action Taken by** (`action_taken_by`, select_one, required: false): Select who took the action from the dropdown menu.
* **Disciplinary Record ID** (`disciplinary_record_id`, number, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for the disciplinary record.

## Tips
- Ensure that the date and time fields are filled in correctly to ensure accurate tracking of incidents.
- Use the dropdown menus to select the correct incident type and outcome.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing incident descriptions, participant information, and action taken details.
- This form is intended for recording incidents within the organizational context, so ensure that all information provided is relevant and accurate.
