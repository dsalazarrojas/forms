# Employee Misconduct Termination Documentation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the termination of an employee's position due to misconduct, as per company policy. It ensures that all relevant details are captured and approved before proceeding with the termination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that the employee has acknowledged and signed the form.
3. Have the supervisor sign off on the form, indicating their approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the employee who has been terminated.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, **optional**): Enter the job title of the employee who has been terminated.
* **Date of Termination** (`date_of_termination`, date, **optional**): Enter the date the employee's position was terminated.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, **optional**): Briefly describe the reason for the employee's termination due to misconduct.
* **Misconduct Details** (`misconduct_details`, text, **optional**): Provide a detailed description of the misconduct that led to the termination.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_multiple, **optional**): The supervisor must confirm that they have reviewed and approved the termination.
	+ Select 'Yes' to indicate approval.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, text, **optional**): The employee must acknowledge that they have been terminated due to misconduct.
* **Date of Approval** (`date_of_approval`, date, **optional**): The date the supervisor approved the termination.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, **optional**): The employee must sign and date the form to confirm they have been terminated.
	+ Note: This is the final step in the termination process.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed before proceeding with the termination.
* The supervisor's approval and the employee's signature must be obtained before the termination is considered complete.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be used for external purposes.
* If the employee has been terminated multiple times, use the "Termination of Employment" form instead.
