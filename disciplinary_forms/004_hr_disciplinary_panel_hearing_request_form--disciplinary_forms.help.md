# HR Disciplinary Panel Hearing Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Disciplinary Panel Hearing Request Form is used to request a hearing for an employee's performance or conduct issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's details, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the type of hearing request you are making (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Provide details about the hearing, including the date and time.
4. Select the duration of the hearing.
5. Enter the meeting location and timezone (if applicable).
6. Enter the manager's name and contact information.
7. Select the status of the employee and manager.
8. Check the boxes indicating the reason for the disciplinary action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`): Enter the employee's name, email, and phone number.
* **Hearing Request** (`hearing_request`): Select "Yes" if you are requesting a hearing, "No" otherwise.
* **Disciplinary Action** (`disciplinary_action`): Select the type of disciplinary action you want to take.
* **Hearing Details** (`hearing_details`): Enter a brief description of the hearing.
* **Hearing Date** (`hearing_date`): Enter the date of the hearing (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Hearing Time** (`hearing_time`): Enter the time of the hearing (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Hearing Duration** (`hearing_duration`): Enter the duration of the hearing (HH:MM).
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): Enter the location of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Meeting Timezone** (`meeting_timezone`): Enter the time zone of the meeting (if applicable).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your name as the manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`): Select the status of the employee (Active or Inactive).
* **Manager Status** (`manager_status`): Select your status as the manager (Active or Inactive).
* **Reason for Disciplinary Action** (`reason_for_disciplinary_action`): Check the boxes indicating the reasons for the disciplinary action.
