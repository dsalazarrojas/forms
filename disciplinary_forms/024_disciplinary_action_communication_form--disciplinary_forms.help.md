# Disciplinary Action Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and communicate disciplinary actions taken against employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about the employee involved, including their name and ID.
2. Provide details about the incident, including the date and time it occurred, and the location.
3. Describe the incident and any actions taken.
4. Select the disciplinary action taken, if any.
5. Provide the effective and terminated dates, if applicable.
6. Explain the reason for termination, if applicable.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee involved in the disciplinary action.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID number of the employee involved in the disciplinary action.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time at which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, note, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`action_taken`, text, optional): Describe the actions taken as a result of the incident.
* **Disciplinary Action** (`disciplinary_action`, select_one, optional): Select the disciplinary action taken (Warning, Suspension, or Termination).
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the disciplinary action took effect.
* **Terminated Date** (`terminated_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of termination, if applicable.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, optional): Explain the reason for termination, if applicable.
* **Disciplinary Form Signature** (`disciplinary_form_signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
* **Signature**: Sign your name below to acknowledge that you have completed this form.

This form requires completion by a supervisor or other authorized representative to ensure accuracy and fairness in disciplinary actions. Please ensure that all relevant information is provided and the form is signed and dated by the person completing it.
