# Threat Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Threat Assessment Form is a tool used to document and assess potential threats or hazards in the workplace, ensuring that employees are aware of and can address risks to their safety and security.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed. You can submit your assessment for review and consideration by your supervisor or relevant department representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Threat Assessment Form Input** (`threat_assessment_form_input`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the potential threat or hazard.
* **Threat Assessment Form Question** (`threat_assessment_form_question`, `text`, optional): Describe the threat or hazard in more detail.
* **Threat Assessment Form Risk** (`threat_assessment_form_risk`, `number`, optional): Assess the risk level of the threat (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Threat Assessment Form Probability** (`threat_assessment_form_probability`, `number`, optional): Assess the probability of the threat occurring (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Threat Assessment Form Safety Precautions** (`threat_assessment_form_safety_precautions`, `note`, optional): Provide any safety precautions or procedures that can be taken to mitigate the threat or hazard.
* **Threat Assessment Form Recommendations** (`threat_assessment_form_recommendations`, `select_one`, optional): Select any recommended actions to address the threat or hazard (e.g., Label 1, Label 2).
* **Threat Assessment Form Comments** (`threat_assessment_form_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information relevant to the assessment.
* **Threat Assessment Form Date** (`threat_assessment_form_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the threat or hazard assessment.
* **Threat Assessment Form Time** (`threat_assessment_form_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the threat or hazard assessment.
* **Threat Assessment Form Status** (`threat_assessment_form_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the status of the assessment (e.g., Label 3, Label 4).
* **Threat Assessment Form Email** (`threat_assessment_form_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Threat Assessment Form Phone** (`threat_assessment_form_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Threat Assessment Form Additional Comments** (`threat_assessment_form_additional_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information or comments related to the assessment.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form as thoroughly and accurately as possible to ensure that your assessment is taken seriously.
* You can submit your assessment for review at any time.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult your supervisor or relevant department representative for guidance.
