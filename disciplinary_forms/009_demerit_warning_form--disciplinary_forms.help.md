# demerit_warning_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and record incidents that may have led to a student being given a warning.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
3.  Double-check your answers for completeness and consistency.
4.  Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name who was involved in the incident.
* **Rule Violation** (`rule_violation`, text, optional): Briefly describe the rule that was violated or the incident that occurred.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, optional): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Details** (`details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter your name and title for identification purposes.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any witness statements or additional information from witnesses.
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, text, optional): Describe any actions taken as a result of the incident.
* **Date Issued** (`date_issued`, date, optional): Enter the date when the warning was issued.
* **Issued By** (`issued_by`, text, optional): Enter the name and title of the person issuing the warning.
* **Signature Issued By** (`signature_issued_by`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the person issuing the warning for identification purposes.
* **Note 1-10** (`note_1`, `note_2`, `note_3`, `note_4`, `note_5`, `note_6`, `note_7`, `note_8`, `note_9`, `note_10`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the incident.
