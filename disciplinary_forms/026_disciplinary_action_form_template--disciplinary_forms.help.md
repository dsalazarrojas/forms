# Disciplinary Action Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disciplinary Action Form Template is designed to help organizations document and manage incidents, ensuring accuracy and consistency in handling disciplinary actions. This form captures relevant information about the incident, employee involvement, and the disciplinary action taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the incident that led to the disciplinary action.
2. Select the action taken as a result of the incident (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, Suspension, or Termination).
3. Enter the employee's name who was involved in the incident.
4. Record the date and time of the incident.
5. Provide a detailed description of the reason for the disciplinary action.
6. Identify the disciplinary officer responsible for handling the incident.
7. Enter a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
8. Record the date and time of the disciplinary action taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of the incident that led to the disciplinary action.
* **Action Taken (2)**: Select the disciplinary action taken as a result of the incident (e.g., Verbal Warning, Written Warning, Suspension, or Termination).
* **Employee Name (3)**: Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
* **Incident Date (4)**: Record the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time (5)**: Record the time of the incident.
* **Reason for Action (6)**: Provide a detailed description of the reason for the disciplinary action.
* **Disciplinary Officer (7)**: Identify the disciplinary officer responsible for handling the incident.
* **Incident Description (8)**: Enter a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Date of Disciplinary Action (9)**: Record the date of the disciplinary action taken.
* **Time of Disciplinary Action (10)**: Record the time of the disciplinary action taken.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand your organization's policies and procedures before using this form.
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Store this form in a secure location to maintain the integrity of the data.
* Use this form as a guide to adapt to your organization's specific needs and policies.
