# Performance Improvement Notice - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and address performance issues with employees, ensuring clear communication and prompt corrective action. It is essential for identifying areas of improvement and implementing necessary changes to enhance job performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Gather the employee's information, including their name, job title, and date of hire.
2. Clearly describe the performance issues that need to be addressed.
3. Specify the expected improvements and corrective actions to be taken.
4. Schedule a follow-up meeting or meeting to review progress.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm completion and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Information** (`performance_improvement_notice_form`, text, required): Enter the employee's name and other relevant details as prompted. This section helps to identify the employee and their role.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): The employee's name as it appears on their identification documents or company records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): The employee's job title or position within the company.
* **Employee Date of Hire** (`employee_date_of_hire`, date, optional): The date the employee was first hired by the company. This information helps to contextualize the employee's tenure and performance history.
* **Performance Issues** (`performance_issues`, text, required): Describe the specific performance issues or areas where the employee needs to improve. Be as detailed as possible to ensure clarity.
* **Improvement Expectations** (`improvement_expectations`, text, required): Specify what improvements are expected from the employee to address the identified issues. This section should include clear and measurable targets.
* **Corrective Action** (`corrective_action`, text, required): Outline the specific steps the employee will take to address the performance issues. This section should include a plan for implementation and deadlines.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Schedule a follow-up meeting or review to check on the employee's progress.
* **Follow-up By** (`follow_up_by`, text, optional): The person responsible for following up on the employee's progress.
* **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): The manager's signature confirms their approval and commitment to support the employee's improvement plan.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): The employee's signature indicates their understanding and commitment to the improvement plan.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): The date the form is signed and submitted.
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, text, optional): Additional comments or suggestions from the manager to support the employee's improvement process.
* **Comments** (`performance_improvement_notice_form_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments or feedback from the manager or employee.
