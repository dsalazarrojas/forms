# Employee Discipline Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Discipline Form is used to document an employee's disciplinary action, including the reason for the action and the action taken.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into several pages, each with a different section of fields that need to be completed. Please complete each section carefully and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Discipline Form 1** (employee_discipline_form_1, text, required): Enter a brief description of the issue that led to the disciplinary action.
* **Reason for Discipline** (employee_discipline_form_2, text, required): Explain the reason for the disciplinary action, including any relevant details or context.
* **Description of Issue** (employee_discipline_form_3, text, required): Describe the specific issue that led to the disciplinary action.
* **Date of Issue** (employee_discipline_form_4, date, required): Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Time of Issue** (employee_discipline_form_5, time, required): Enter the time the issue occurred.
* **Employee Discipline Form Action** (employee_discipline_form_6, select_one, required): Choose the appropriate action to take for the disciplinary issue. Options include Warning, First Level 1 Day Suspension, One Day Suspension, 3 Days Suspension, One Week Suspension, and More Than One Week Suspension.
* **Employee Discipline Form Signature** (employee_discipline_form_7, text, required): Sign and date the form to indicate that it has been completed.
* **Date Signed** (employee_discipline_form_8, date, required): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (employee_discipline_form_9, time, required): Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Review Date** (employee_discipline_form_10, date, required): Enter the date the form was reviewed.
* **Review Time** (employee_discipline_form_11, time, required): Enter the time the form was reviewed.
* **Reviewer's Signature** (employee_discipline_form_12, text, required): Sign and date the form to indicate that it has been reviewed.
* **Reviewer's Comments** (employee_discipline_form_13, text, required): Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer's Name** (employee_discipline_form_14, text, required): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer's Title** (employee_discipline_form_15, text, required): Enter the reviewer's title.
* **Reviewer's Department** (employee_discipline_form_16, text, required): Enter the reviewer's department.
* **Reviewer's Email** (employee_discipline_form_17, email, required): Enter the reviewer's email.
* **Reviewer's Phone** (employee_discipline_form_18, text, required): Enter the reviewer's phone number.
* **Review Date** (employee_discipline_form_19, date, required): Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Review Time** (employee_discipline_form_20, time, required): Enter the time the review was completed.
* **Reviewer's Title** (employee_discipline_form_21, text, required): Enter the reviewer's title.
* **Reviewer's Department Title** (employee_discipline_form_22, text, required): Enter the reviewer's department title.
* **Reviewer's Comments** (employee_discipline_form_23, text, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer's Department Title** (employee_discipline_form_24, text, required): Enter the reviewer's department title.
* **Reviewer's Email** (employee_discipline_form_25, email, required): Enter the reviewer's email.

Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified. Please fill out each field carefully and thoroughly.
