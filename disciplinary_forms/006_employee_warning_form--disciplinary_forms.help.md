# Employee Warning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Warning Form is used to document and record incidents or behavior that have resulted in a warning being issued to an employee. The form is used to provide clear details of the incident, the action planned to address the issue, and any subsequent actions taken. This form helps maintain accurate and transparent records of employee performance and ensures that all employees are held to the same standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the employee's ID (if available).
3. Provide a detailed description of the reason for the warning, including the specific behavior or incident that led to the warning.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Enter a detailed description of the incident.
6. Describe the action planned to address the issue.
7. Describe any actions taken as a result of the warning.
8. Outline the next steps to be taken by the employee and the employer.
9. If applicable, enter the employee's signature date and sign the form to confirm receipt of the warning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter the employee's last name.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, not required): Enter the employee's ID number, if available.
* **Reason for Warning** (Reason for Warning, note, required): Provide a detailed description of the reason for the warning, including the specific behavior or incident that led to the warning.
* **Date of Incident** (Date of Incident, date, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time of Incident** (Time of Incident, time, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Details of Incident** (Details of Incident, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the incident or behavior.
* **Action Planned** (Action Planned, text, required): Describe the action planned to address the issue.
* **Action Taken** (Action Taken, text, required): Describe any actions taken as a result of the warning.
* **Next Steps** (Next Steps, text, required): Outline the next steps to be taken by the employee and the employer.
* **Employee Signature Date** (Employee Signature Date, date, not required): If applicable, enter the employee's signature date to confirm receipt of the warning.
* **Employee Signature** (Employee Signature, text, not required): If applicable, sign the form to confirm receipt of the warning.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information in all fields.
* Be specific when describing the reason for the warning and the incident.
* Use proper format for dates and times (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM).
* Ensure that the employee signs the form if applicable.
