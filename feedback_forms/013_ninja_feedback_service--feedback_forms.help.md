# Ninja Feedback Service - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about their experience with our ninja feedback service. Your feedback will help us to improve our service and make it more user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the **feedback** field with your comments or suggestions.
* Provide a rating of your experience with our service (1-5).
* Enter your email address if you want us to contact you directly.
* Provide your phone number if you want us to contact you about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback (text)**: Enter a brief description of your experience with our service. Be as specific as possible so we can understand your issue or suggestion.
* **Rating (number)**: Rate your experience with our service on a scale of 1-5. A higher rating indicates a better experience.
* **Email (email)**: If you want us to contact you directly about your feedback, enter your email address here.
* **Phone (text)**: If you want us to contact you about your feedback, enter your phone number here.

## Tips
* Please be specific and clear in your feedback. We appreciate it when you provide concrete examples or suggestions.
* Do not resubmit if you have already provided feedback on this form.
* We will use your feedback to improve our service and make it better for everyone.
