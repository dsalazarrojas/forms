# Development Plan Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Development Plan Review form is designed to collect feedback from reviewers on the development plan. It is used to ensure consistency and accuracy in the review process by gathering structured and objective information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the reviewer in the "Reviewer name" field. This is an optional field, but it is recommended for anonymous feedback.
2. Select the "Plan section being reviewed" field to specify which part of the development plan you are reviewing.
3. Evaluate the "Clarity of plan content" field by selecting one of the options (Very clear, Clear, Somewhat unclear, or Very unclear) that best describes your assessment.
4. If you have concerns or potential issues with the development plan, describe them in the "Concerns or potential issues" field. This field is optional.
5. If you have suggested changes or improvements, enter them in the "Suggested changes or improvements" field. This field is optional.
6. In the "Recommendation for approval" field, select one of the options (Approve, Approve with changes, or Do not approve) that best reflects your review result.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the reviewer. This is an optional field for anonymous feedback.
* **Plan section being reviewed** (`plan_section`, text, required): Select the specific part of the development plan you are reviewing.
* **Clarity of plan content** (`clarity_of_plan`, select_one, required): Evaluate the clarity of the plan content by selecting one of the four options.
* **Concerns or potential issues** (`concerns`, text, optional): Describe any concerns or potential issues you have with the development plan.
* **Suggested changes or improvements** (`suggested_changes`, text, optional): Enter any suggested changes or improvements for the development plan.
* **Recommendation for approval** (`recommendation`, select_one, required): Select one of the three options to indicate your recommendation for approval or rejection of the development plan.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing concerns or potential issues.
* Clearly state any suggested changes or improvements.
* Make sure to select one of the three options for "Recommendation for approval" to ensure consistency in the review result.
