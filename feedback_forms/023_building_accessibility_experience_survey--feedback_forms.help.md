# Building Accessibility Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Building Accessibility Experience Survey is designed to collect feedback on the accessibility features and limitations of a building or facility. This form is meant to help us understand the effectiveness of our accessibility efforts and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address (optional).
2. Select the date of your visit.
3. Choose the building you visited.
4. Select the purpose of your visit.
5. Rate the accessibility of the building's parking, entrance, doors, elevator, ramps, restrooms, signage, and lighting.
6. Select any barriers you encountered during your visit.
7. Provide any positive features you noticed during your visit.
8. Share any suggestions for improvement.
9. Rate your overall accessibility experience and whether you would return to the building.
10. Indicate whether you would recommend the building to others with accessibility needs.
11. Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, Optional): Please fill in your name as you would like to be addressed in the survey results.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, Optional): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, date, Required): Select the date of your visit to the building.
* **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, Required): Choose the name of the building you visited.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`visit_purpose`, select_one, Required): Select the reason you visited the building.
* **Mobility Aid Used** (`mobility_aid`, select_one, Required): If you used a mobility aid during your visit, please select which one.
* **Parking Accessibility Rating** (`parking_accessibility`, select_one, Required): Rate the accessibility of the parking area.
* **Entrance Accessibility** (`entrance_accessibility`, select_one, Required): Rate the accessibility of the building's entrance.
* **Door Access** (`door_access`, select_one, Required): Rate the ease of door operation.
* **Elevator Accessibility** (`elevator_accessibility`, select_one, Required): Rate the accessibility of the elevator.
* **Ramp Condition** (`ramp_condition`, select_one, Required): Rate the quality of ramps in the building.
* **Restroom Accessibility** (`restroom_accessibility`, select_one, Required): Rate the accessibility of the restrooms.
* **Signage Clarity** (`signage_clarity`, select_one, Required): Rate the clarity of signs in the building.
* **Braille Signage** (`braille_signage`, select_one, Required): Select if braille signs were available throughout the building.
* **Lighting Quality** (`lighting_quality`, select_one, Required): Rate the adequacy of lighting in the building.
* **Floor Surface Condition** (`floor_surface`, select_one, Required): Rate the ease of navigation on the floor.
* **Staff Assistance** (`staff_assistance`, select_one, Required): Rate the helpfulness of staff during your visit.
* **Barriers Encountered** (`barriers_encountered`, select_multiple, Required): Select any barriers you encountered during your visit.
* **Positive Features Noted** (`positive_features`, text, Optional): Share any positive features you noticed during your visit.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, Optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the building's accessibility.
* **Overall Accessibility Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, Required): Rate your overall accessibility experience.
* **Would You Return** (`would_return`, select_one, Required): Indicate whether you would return to the building based on accessibility.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, Required): Indicate whether you would recommend the building to others with accessibility needs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional): Share any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer the required questions honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
