# Weekly Wellbeing Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly Wellbeing Check In form is designed to help you gauge your emotional state and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select an option for each of the 25 wellbeing scales**:
	* For each scale, choose the option that best reflects your emotional state at this moment.
	* Take your time, as some questions may be more difficult to answer than others.
