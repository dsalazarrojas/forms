# digital_product_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on digital products, allowing them to report bugs, suggest features, or provide general feedback. The form is used to gather information such as contact details, feedback description, and submission metadata.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email or phone number.
2. Describe your feedback in a text field.
3. If you have found a bug, report it using the "Bug Report" field.
4. If you have a feature request, enter it in the "Feature Request" field.
5. Choose the category of your feedback (Bug Report, Feature Request, or Other).
6. Select the type of feedback (Bug, Feature, or General).
7. Provide any additional submission comments.
8. Enter your Form ID and JotForm User (if applicable).
9. Enter the date and time of submission.
10. Enter your IP address, browser, operating system, device, city, region, and country.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Your name and contact information.
* **Feedback Description**: A text field for you to describe your feedback.
* **Bug Report**: If you have found a bug, enter a detailed description of the issue.
* **Feature Request**: If you have a feature request, enter it in this field.
* **Category**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Bug Report
	+ Feature Request
	+ Other
* **Feedback Type**: Choose from the following options:
	+ Bug
	+ Feature
	+ General
* **Submission Details**: Any additional comments about your submission.
* **Submission Comments**: Any additional comments about your submission.
* **Form ID**: Enter your Form ID.
* **JotForm User**: Enter your JotForm user information (if applicable).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission IP**: Enter your IP address.
* **User Browser**: Enter your browser type.
* **User OS**: Enter your operating system.
* **User Device**: Enter your device type.
* **User City**: Enter your city.
* **User Region**: Enter your region.
* **User Country**: Enter your country.
