# Wine And Meal News - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on wine and meal experiences. It's meant to be a simple and concise way to collect information on whether users had wine and meal, their rating, and any comments they might have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you had wine and meal.
2. Rate your experience (if applicable).
3. Leave a comment about your experience (if applicable).
4. Enter the date and time of your experience.
5. Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted).
6. Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wine and Meal News** (`wine_and_meal_news`): This is a note field where you can write any additional comments or news about your wine and meal experience.
* **Select a wine and meal** (`wine_and_meal`): Choose whether you had wine and meal or not.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your experience from 1 to 5 (if you select that you had wine and meal).
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`): Leave a comment about your rating (if you select that you had wine and meal).
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of your wine and meal experience (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of your wine and meal experience (in HH:MM format).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about your experience.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about your experience.
* **Wine and Meal Comments** (`wine_and_meal_news_comments`): Leave any additional comments about your wine and meal experience.
