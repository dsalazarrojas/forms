# Flavor Profile Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flavor profile assessment is a form designed to gather information about your flavor preferences and eating habits. It consists of a series of questions that will help us create a personalized experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your flavor profile.
2. Answer the questions truthfully, as your responses will help us provide a more tailored experience.
3. Please ensure that you are providing accurate information, as it will be used to create a customized experience for you.
