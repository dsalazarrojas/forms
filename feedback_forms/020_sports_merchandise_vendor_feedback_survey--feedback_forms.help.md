# Sports Merchandise Vendor Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from sports merchandise vendors on their experience with our company. It will help us understand the quality of our products and services, identify areas for improvement, and provide valuable insights to enhance our business operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an (*):
	* **Vendor Name**: Enter your official name or company name.
	* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you attended.
	* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you purchased or were involved with.
	* *Score*: Rate your overall satisfaction with the product/service on a scale of 1-5.
	* *Email*: Enter your professional email address for contact purposes.
	* *Phone*: Enter a contact number.
	* *Vendor ID*: Enter your unique ID number.
	* *Event Date*: Enter the date of the event.
	* *Product Price*: Enter the price of the product.
	* *Communication Time*: Enter the time it took for you to receive our communication (e.g., email, phone call).
	* *Staff Availability*: Rate the availability of our staff during the event.
	* *Sales Reps*: Rate the sales representatives' performance.
	* *Event Location*: Rate the event location's overall experience.
	* *Vendor Price*: Enter the price of the product or service.
	* *Communication Time*: Enter the time it took for you to receive our communication (e.g., email, phone call).
	* *Staff Attendance*: Rate the overall attendance of our staff during the event.

2. Provide feedback in the **Feedback** text field.
3. Select the options that best fit your experience for the following questions:
	* **Product Quality**: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
	* **Product Value**: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
	* **Communication**: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
	* **Service**: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
	* **Staff Service**: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
	* **Overall Experience**: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
	* **Product Suitability**: True or False.
	* **Recommendation**: True or False.
	* **Staff Attendance**: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
