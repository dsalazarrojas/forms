# Mystery Shopping Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on various aspects of a store's service quality. As a mystery shopper, you'll be evaluating your experience in different stores, providing valuable insights on how well the store meets customer expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer them honestly and thoroughly.
2. Fill out the form in a new tab or window to avoid any issues with your browser.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Quality <br>** (Customer Service Quality, text, required): This field is about how well the store's customer service team interacted with you. Describe their communication, helpfulness, and overall attitude.
* **Store Atmosphere <br>** (Store Atmosphere, text, required): Describe the store's atmosphere, including the decor, lighting, and overall ambiance.
* **Product Knowledge <br>** (Product Knowledge, text, required): Evaluate the store staff's knowledge about the products they sell, including their ability to answer questions and provide recommendations.
* **Cleanliness <br>** (Cleanliness, text, required): Rate the store's cleanliness, including the condition of the premises, equipment, and overall tidiness.
* **Friendliness <br>** (Friendliness, text, required): Describe the store staff's friendliness and approachability, including their ability to smile, make eye contact, and engage with customers.
* **Communication <br>** (Communication, text, required): Assess the store staff's communication skills, including their clarity, conciseness, and effectiveness in explaining products and services.
* **Employee Appearance <br>** (Employee Appearance, text, required): Rate the store staff's appearance, including their attire, grooming, and overall presentation.
* **Employee Courtesy <br>** (Employee Courtesy, text, required): Describe the store staff's courtesy, including their willingness to help, politeness, and respect for customers.
* **Employee Conduct <br>** (Employee Conduct, text, required): Evaluate the store staff's conduct, including their behavior, attitude, and overall professionalism.
* **Employee Attentiveness <br>** (Employee Attentiveness, text, required): Assess the store staff's attentiveness, including their ability to pay attention to customers, respond to questions, and provide personalized service.
* **Store Layout <br>** (Store Layout, text, required): Describe the store's layout, including the organization, signage, and overall ease of navigation.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your evaluation.
* If you're unsure about any aspect, try to provide as much detail as possible.
* Remember, your feedback will help improve customer service and overall store experience.
