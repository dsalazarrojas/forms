## ict_tools_usage_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the usage of ICT tools within our organization. Your input will help us understand how ICT tools are being used and inform future decisions on tool selection, usage, and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page and answer the questions according to your experience and knowledge.
2.  For questions where you have multiple options (e.g., "Yes" or "No"), select all that apply.
3.  For questions with text fields (e.g., "comment"), provide as much detail as possible.
4.  Enter your email and phone number (if you have one) if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up.
