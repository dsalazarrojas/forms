# Active Breaks Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey aims to collect feedback from participants about their experiences and perceptions of the Active Breaks program. Your responses will help us understand how often you participate, what benefits you perceive from the program, and how we can improve it to better suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the following sections:

*   Provide your name (if you'd like to be identified).
*   Choose how often you participate in active breaks.
*   Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
*   Select the physical benefits you've noticed from participating.
*   Indicate how active breaks affect your mental energy.
*   Choose your preferred active break activities.
*   Select the ideal duration for active breaks.
*   Choose your preferred timing for active breaks.
*   Select any barriers that prevent you from participating.
*   Indicate the impact of active breaks on your work productivity.
*   Provide any suggestions for improving the program.
*   Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name (Optional)**: If you'd like to be identified, provide your name for feedback purposes.
*   **Department**: Please select your department in the organization.
*   **How Often Do You Participate in Active Breaks**: Choose how frequently you participate in active breaks. Choose from "Daily", "Several times per week", "Once a week", "Occasionally", or "Never".
*   **Overall Satisfaction with Active Breaks**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program. Choose from "Very satisfied", "Satisfied", "Neutral", "Dissatisfied", or "Very dissatisfied".
*   **Physical Benefits Perceived**: Select any physical benefits you've noticed from participating in active breaks, such as "Significant improvement", "Some improvement", "No change", or "Feeling worse".
*   **Mental Energy Improvement**: Indicate how active breaks affect your mental energy. Choose from "Significantly", "Moderately", "Slightly", or "Not at all".
*   **Favorite Active Break Activities**: Select your preferred active break activities, such as "Stretching exercises", "Walking", "Desk yoga", "Breathing exercises", "Strength exercises", "Dance movements", "Mindfulness", or "Other".
*   **Ideal Duration for Active Breaks**: Choose the ideal length for an active break. Select from "5 minutes", "10 minutes", "15 minutes", "20 minutes", or "More than 20 minutes".
*   **Preferred Timing for Active Breaks**: Choose your preferred timing for active breaks. Select from "Morning", "Mid-morning", "After lunch", "Late afternoon", or "Before end of day".
*   **Barriers to Participation**: Select any barriers that prevent you from participating in active breaks, such as "Lack of time", "Lack of space", "Lack of motivation", "Work interruptions", "Physical limitations", or "No barriers".
*   **Impact on Work Productivity**: Indicate how active breaks impact your work productivity. Choose from "Significantly improve", "Moderately improve", "Slightly improve", "No impact", or "Decrease productivity".
*   **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving the Active Breaks program.
*   **Additional Feedback**: Add any other comments or feedback you'd like to share about the program.
