# cultural-awareness-training-evaluation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the cultural-awareness training program, providing feedback on its effectiveness in terms of training level, instructional methods, and participant experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Participant ID to identify the individual who attended the training session.
2. Select the Session Date to indicate the date of the training session.
3. Enter the Evaluation Score to rate the training session based on your experience.
4. Add any Comments about the training session.
5. Choose the Category for the training session (e.g., Cultural Awareness Training).
6. Select multiple Subcategories relevant to your experience (e.g., Program Evaluation, Session Evaluation, Feedback).
7. Enter the Instructional Methods used during the training session.
8. Enter the Instructor Name responsible for the session.
9. Evaluate the Instructor's performance.
10. Select your Ethnicity to provide demographic information.
11. Choose your Gender.
12. Enter your Age.
13. Select the Country of Residence where you attended the training.
14. Evaluate the session's previous Training Evaluation (if applicable).
15. Provide the reason for the previous Training Evaluation (if applicable).
16. Choose the Training Level you feel you have achieved.
17. Evaluate the Training Evaluation.
18. Select the reason for the Training Evaluation.
19. Enter the Areas to Improve for the training session.
20. Enter the Instructional Effectiveness score.
21. Select reasons for the Instructional Effectiveness score.
22. Evaluate the Enjoyment of the training session.
23. Select reasons for the Enjoyment score.
24. Enter the Liking for the training session.
25. Select reasons for the Liking score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID (1)**: Enter your unique Participant ID to identify yourself.
* **Session Date (2)**: Select the date you attended the training session.
* **Evaluation Score (3)**: Rate the training session based on your experience, using a numerical score.
* **Comments (4)**: Add any comments or feedback about the training session.
* **Category (5)**: Choose the Category for the training session (e.g., Cultural Awareness Training).
* **Subcategory (6)**: Select multiple Subcategories relevant to your experience (e.g., Program Evaluation, Session Evaluation, Feedback).
* **Instructional Methods (7)**: Enter the methods used during the training session.
* **Instructor Name (8)**: Enter the name of the instructor responsible for the session.
* **Instructor Evaluation (9)**: Evaluate the instructor's performance.
* **Participant Ethnicity (10)**: Select your Ethnicity to provide demographic information.
* **Gender (11)**: Choose your Gender.
* **Age (12)**: Enter your Age.
* **Country of Residence (13)**: Select the Country where you attended the training session.
* **Previous Training Evaluation (14)**: Evaluate the session's previous Training Evaluation (if applicable).
* **Previous Training Evaluation Reason (15)**: Provide the reason for the previous Training Evaluation (if applicable).
* **Training Level (16)**: Choose the level of training you feel you have achieved.
* **Training Evaluation (17)**: Evaluate the training session.
* **Training Evaluation Reason (18)**: Select reasons for the Training Evaluation.
* **Areas to Improve (19)**: Enter the Areas to Improve for the training session.
* **Instructional Effectiveness (20)**: Enter the score for Instructional Effectiveness.
* **Instructional Effectiveness Reason (21)**: Select reasons for the Instructional Effectiveness score.
* **Enjoyment (22)**: Evaluate the Enjoyment of the training session.
* **Enjoyment Reason (23)**: Select reasons for the Enjoyment score.
* **Liking (24)**: Enter the Liking score.
* **Liking Reason (25)**: Select reasons for the Liking score.

**Tips**

* Please answer the questions based on your honest experience with the training session.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and comments.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your instructor or supervisor for guidance.
* Take your time to complete the form accurately.
