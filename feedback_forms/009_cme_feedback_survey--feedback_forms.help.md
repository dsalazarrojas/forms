# CME Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on Continuing Medical Education (CME) events. Your input will help evaluate the quality of the events and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the CME event you are providing feedback on.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
3. Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (1): Your name as a participant of the CME event.
	+ Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2): Your email address for certificate delivery.
	+ Please enter your email address accurately.
* **Profession** (3): Your healthcare profession.
	+ Select one of the provided options (e.g., Physician, Nurse, Pharmacist, etc.).
* **Specialty** (4): Your area of specialization (if desired).
	+ This is optional and only needed for further context.
* **Institution** (5): Your workplace or hospital.
	+ Please enter your workplace or hospital name for reference.
* **CME Event Name** (6): The name of the CME event you are providing feedback on.
	+ Please enter the name of the event accurately.
* **Event Date** (7): The date you attended the CME event.
	+ Please enter the date you attended in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Overall Event Rating** (8): Your overall rating of the event.
	+ Rate your experience as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Content Quality** (9): The rating of the educational content.
	+ Rate the content as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Speaker Quality** (10): The rating of the presenters.
	+ Rate the speakers as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Relevance to Practice** (11): The level of relevance to your work.
	+ Rate as Highly Relevant, Very Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Slightly Relevant, or Not Relevant.
* **Learning Objectives Met** (12): Whether or not the learning objectives were met.
	+ Select Completely, Mostly, Partially, Minimally, or Not At All.
* **Quality of Materials** (13): The quality of the materials provided.
	+ Rate as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Venue Rating** (14): The rating of the venue (if applicable).
	+ Select Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Technology Platform Rating** (15): The rating of the technology used for the event (if applicable).
	+ Rate as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Will This Change Your Practice?** (16): The impact on your work.
	+ Select Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, or Definitely No.
* **CME Credits Needed** (17): The number of credit hours you earned.
	+ Enter the correct number of credit hours.
* **Topics for Future Events** (18): Suggestions for future CME topics.
	+ Enter any suggestions or ideas you have for future events.
* **Recommend to Colleagues** (19): Whether or not you would recommend this event.
	+ Select Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, or Definitely No.
* **Additional Comments** (20): Any other feedback or comments.
	+ Enter any additional feedback or comments you have about the event.

Note: Please fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback. Your input will help improve future CME events.
