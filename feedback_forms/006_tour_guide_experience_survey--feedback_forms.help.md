# tour_guide_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from users who have gone on a guided tour with our company. We aim to understand your experience, preferences, and suggestions to improve our tour guide services.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into multiple pages, each with its own set of questions. Please complete each page carefully, and make sure to select the relevant options that apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Tour Guide Experience
* **Tour Guide Experience** (`tour_guide_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer this question by selecting whether you found the tour guide to be 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand your experience with our tour guides.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide your feedback and comments about the tour guide experience. Be as detailed as possible to help us improve our services.
* **Tour Rating** (`tour_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your tour guide experience as 'Good' or 'Fair'. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the tour.

### Page 2: Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall experience with our tour guide on a scale of 1-5.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, optional): If you want to provide the date of your tour, please enter it in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, `time`, optional): If you want to provide the time of your tour, please enter it in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Tour Rating Comment** (`tour_rating_comment`, `select_multiple`, optional): Provide your comments about the tour rating. Be specific about your reasons for your tour rating.

### Page 3: Feedback
* **Travel Complaint** (`travel_complaint`, `text`, optional): If you have any complaints or concerns about the tour, please provide them in this field.
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with our tour guides.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer this question by selecting whether you found your experience to be 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand your experience.

### Page 4: Contact Information
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, optional): Answer this question by selecting whether you would recommend our tour guide services to others.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `note`, optional): If you want to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your contact information in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you want to provide your email address, please enter it in the format of [user@example.com](mailto:user@example.com).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide your phone number, please enter it in the format of (123) 456-7890.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide your company name, please enter it.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide your department name, please enter it.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide your position name, please enter it.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide your contact phone number, please enter it in the format of (123) 456-7890.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): If you want to provide your contact email address, please enter it in the format of [user@example.com](mailto:user@example.com).
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide your website URL, please enter it.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide a contact message, please enter it.
* **Contact Message 2** (`contact_message_2`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide an additional contact message, please enter it.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide a category, please enter it.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, `text`, optional): If you want to provide an additional category, please enter it.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall experience with our tour guide on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Rating Comment** (`overall_rating_comment`, `text`, optional): Provide your comments about your overall rating.
