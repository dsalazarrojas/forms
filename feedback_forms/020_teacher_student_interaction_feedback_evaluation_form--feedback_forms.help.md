# Teacher Student Interaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experiences with their teachers, focusing on aspects like support, clarity, and availability of the teacher.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- If you have no opinion or experience with the teacher, it's okay to skip or select "Neutral" for the interaction type and "0" for any numerical questions.
- You can use the drop-down menu to select the options for each question.
- Make sure to fill out all questions, even if you're unsure, as your opinion is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **interaction_type** (`interaction_type`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select a description of your interaction with the teacher: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **teacher_supportiveness** (`question1`, Number, optional):
	+ Rate how supportive the teacher was during your interaction, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **teacher_effectiveness** (`question2`, Number, optional):
	+ Rate how effective the teacher was in explaining things, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **student_learning_outcome** (`question3`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select how much you gained from the interaction: a lot, some, a little, or none.
* **teacher_clarification** (`question4`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select whether the teacher provided clear explanations: Yes or No.
* **teacher_availability** (`question5`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select how often the teacher was available to interact with you: Always, Mostly, Sometimes, or Rarely.
* **teacher_helpfulness** (`question6`, Number, optional):
	+ Rate how helpful the teacher was, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **student_frustration_level** (`question7`, Number, optional):
	+ Rate how frustrated you felt during the interaction, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **teacher_frustration_level** (`question8`, Number, optional):
	+ Rate how frustrated you felt with the teacher, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **student_satisfaction_level** (`question9`, Number, optional):
	+ Rate how satisfied you were with the interaction, on a scale of 0 to 10.

## Tips

- Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve future teacher-student interactions.
- If you have any suggestions or concerns, feel free to add them in the space provided at the end of the form.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide us with your feedback!
