# Farming Cooperative Monthly Meeting Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Farming Cooperative Monthly Meeting Attendance" form is designed to gather information on members' attendance and feedback on meetings. This data helps the cooperative to assess member engagement, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions for future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields (if applicable), which are indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
2. Ensure you are accurate with the meeting date.
3. Enter your member name and role in the cooperative (if applicable).
4. Indicate whether you attended the meeting and select your role.
5. Provide any comments or feedback you have about the meeting (if applicable).
6. Choose how you want to receive comments from other members.
7. Enter your email address for correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date** (`1`, `date`, *false*): Enter the date of the meeting. If you're unsure, refer to meeting notes or minutes.
* **Member Name** (`2`, `text`, *false*): If you're a member of the cooperative, enter your name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Role** (`3`, `text`, *false*): If you're a member of the cooperative, enter your role (e.g., farmer, staff, or volunteer).
* **Attended** (`4`, `select_one`, *false*): Select "Member" if you attended the meeting, "Non-Member" if you didn't, or "Unable to attend" if you had a valid reason not to attend.
* **Comments** (`5`, `note`, *false*): Provide any thoughts, suggestions, or feedback about the meeting, if you have any.
* **Feedback** (`6`, `select_multiple`, *false*): Select how you'd like to receive feedback from other members: "Feedback on meetings", "Suggestions", or "Other".
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, *false*): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (`8`, `text`, *false*): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Comments Email** (`9`, `select_multiple`, *false*): Select how you'd like to receive comments from other members: "Send comments directly", "Send comments via email", or "Do not send comments".
* **Email Address** (`10`, `email`, *false*): Enter the email address where comments from other members will be sent (if selected in the previous field).
* **Tips**:
* Ensure the information entered is accurate and consistent with other meeting records.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to meeting minutes or discussion notes.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, use the "Comments" field to provide them.
* If you'd like to receive feedback, select the relevant option in the "Feedback" field.
