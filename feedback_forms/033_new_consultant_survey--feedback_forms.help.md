# New Consultant Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from new consultants about their experiences, feedback, and suggestions for improving our service. It helps us to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the consultant experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the survey questions carefully and provide your honest feedback.
2. Choose the correct options for each question where applicable.
3. Fill in your response for each question.
4. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **survey_questions** (Text, Optional): This is a general question where you can provide any feedback or comments about your experience as a new consultant.
* **check_email** (Text, Optional): Please check the email address you would like us to use when contacting you about your feedback. If you have any issues with your current email address, please let us know and we'll update it for you.
* **submission_email** (Text, Optional): If you have any attachments or documents to submit with your feedback, please provide your email address here and we'll get in touch with you to arrange for submission.
* **survey_1** (Select One, Optional): How likely are you to recommend our service to a friend or colleague? 
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **survey_2** (Select Multiple, Optional): Which of the following services would you like to see added or improved? 
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **survey_3** (Date, Optional): If you have any specific dates or deadlines for your feedback submission, please provide them here.
* **time** (Time, Optional): If you have any specific time constraints for your feedback submission, please provide them here.
* **comments** (Note, Optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

**Tips**

* Please be as detailed as possible in your responses.
* If you have any issues with your feedback submission, please contact us directly.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
