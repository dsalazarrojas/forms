# Faith Based Study Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants in faith-based studies. The purpose of this form is to collect information about their experiences, identify areas for improvement, and help us understand their overall satisfaction with the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and complete feedback.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Select the most appropriate response for each question based on your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Evaluation (1)** (`Session Evaluation`, Number, required: false): Evaluate your session with us. Please rate your experience on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is "not satisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **Overall Experience (2)** (`Overall Experience`, Number, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our study, also on a scale of 0-5.
* **Improvement Areas (3)** (`Improvement Areas`, Select Multiple, required: false): Select all areas where you think we could improve our services.
	+ Yes: Select this option if you think we could improve in this area.
	+ No: Select this option if you do not think we could improve in this area.
* **Data Collection (4)** (`Data Collection`, Select One, required: false): Please indicate whether you're comfortable with our data collection methods.
	+ Yes: Select this option if you're comfortable with our data collection methods.
	+ No: Select this option if you're not comfortable with our data collection methods.
* **Next Steps (5)** (`Next Steps`, Text, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for next steps or actions we could take to improve our services.
* **Additional Comments (6)** (`Additional Comments`, Text, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience with us.
