# Library Resources Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on library resources, such as books, articles, and media. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide effective feedback, please follow these steps:

1.  **Provide your input**: Start by filling out the "User Input" field. Share your thoughts about your experience with the library resources.
2.  **Specify the resource**: Choose the category for the resource you're providing feedback on, such as "Books", "Articles", or "Media", under the "Resource Category" field. You can select one or multiple categories.
3.  **Provide details**: Enter the title of the resource, the location where you found it, and any suggestions you might have in the corresponding fields.
4.  **Rate the resource**: Rate the resource's availability and your overall satisfaction with it using the "Resource Availability" and "Satisfaction Rating" fields.
5.  **Give your contact details**: Optionally, provide your contact number and email address so we can get back to you for further discussion.
6.  **Submit**: Once you've filled out all the fields, submit your feedback form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input**: 
    *   **User Input** (text): This field is where you can share your thoughts about your experience with the library resources. Please provide your honest feedback so we can improve.
*   **Resource Title**: 
    *   **Resource Title** (text): Enter the title of the resource you're providing feedback on.
*   **Resource Category**: 
    *   **Resource Category** (select multiple): Choose one or multiple categories for the resource, such as "Books", "Articles", or "Media".
*   **Resource Location**: 
    *   **Resource Location** (text): If you have access to the resource, enter the location where you found it. This helps us know where to find more resources like it.
*   **Comments**: 
    *   **Comments** (text): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the resource.
*   **Suggestions**: 
    *   **Suggestions** (text): If you have any suggestions for improving the resource or similar resources, enter them here.
*   **Contact Number**: 
    *   **Contact Number** (text): Optionally, provide your contact number in case we need to get back to you for further discussion.
*   **Contact Email**: 
    *   **Contact Email** (email): If you have a contact email, share it with us so we can reach out to you.
*   **Resource Rating**: 
    *   **Resource Rating** (number): Rate the resource from 1 to 5, indicating your overall satisfaction with it.
*   **Rating Scale**: 
    *   **Rating Scale** (select one): Rate your experience with the resource's availability, using the scale 1-5.
*   **Overall Resource Satisfaction**: 
    *   **Overall Resource Satisfaction** (select one): Rate your overall satisfaction with the resource, using the scale 1-5.
*   **Improvement Suggestions**: 
    *   **Improvement Suggestions** (text): Share your ideas on how we can improve the resource or similar resources.
*   **Resource Availability**: 
    *   **Resource Availability** (select one): Rate the availability of the resource, choosing from "Always Available", "Sometimes Available", "Rarely Available", or "Never Available".
*   **Availability Rating**: 
    *   **Availability Rating** (select one): Rate the resource's availability, using the scale 1-5.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide feedback on specific resources, not general topics or services.
*   Be as detailed as possible when sharing your experience and suggestions.
*   If you have questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
