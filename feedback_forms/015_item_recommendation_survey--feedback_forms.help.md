# item_research_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and research on items from customers. It's divided into multiple pages, each containing questions and fields that help us understand your thoughts and preferences. Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the same flow as the pages below. For each question, provide your honest and accurate answer in the relevant field. You may skip fields that are not required or are not applicable to your specific situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Title** (`title`, `text`, not required): This field is not a required question, and you may skip it if you don't have a title for the item you're providing feedback on.
- **Survey Page 1** (`survey_page_1`, `text`, not required): This is a text field where you can provide any relevant comments or information about the item. You may skip it if you don't have any comments.
- **Product Interests** (`product_interests`, `select_one`, not required): Please select 'Yes' if you have an interest in the product or service, or 'No' if you don't. 
- **Survey Page 2** (`survey_page_2`, `text`, not required): Like **Survey Page 1**, this is another field for any further comments or information about the item. You can skip it if you don't have anything to add.
- **Item Recommendations** (`item_recommendations`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all the recommendations that apply to the product or service.
- **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, `number`, not required): Please rate your satisfaction with the product or service on a scale of 1-10.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, not required): This field is for your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you regarding your feedback.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, not required): This field is for your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
- **Survey Page 3** (`survey_page_3`, `text`, not required): Another text field for any further comments or information.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): You can provide any additional comments or feedback in this field.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out, even if you don't have an interest or preference for the product/service.
