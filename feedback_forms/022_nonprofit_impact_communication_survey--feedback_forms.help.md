# Nonprofit Impact Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through how to complete the Nonprofit Impact Communication Survey form, which is designed to gather feedback and suggestions on how a nonprofit's communication can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form sections from top to bottom.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as you need to answer it truthfully.
3. You can skip any section that is not relevant to you or your nonprofit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> (Nonprofit Impact Communication Survey)
* This is the title of the form, which is to be filled out and provided as a heading for the form.

### How did we do (id: 2)
* This question asks for your honest opinion on how well the nonprofit's communication has performed. Choose one of the options: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### What would you like to see more of (id: 3)
* Provide any suggestions you have on what you would like to see more of in the nonprofit's communication. This could be more frequent updates, clearer messages, or something else.

### How Important (id: 4)
* This question asks for your feedback on how important the nonprofit's communication is to your organization. You can provide a numerical value between 0 and 100, where 0 is not important at all and 100 is very important.

### Nonprofit Impact Communication Survey Form Comments (id: 5)
* This is an open-ended question where you can provide any comments or feedback you have on the nonprofit's communication. This could be suggestions, compliments, or concerns.

### Email Address (id: 6)
* This is where you can provide your email address to receive updates or communications from the nonprofit. If you have a public email address, please use it. If you prefer not to, you can leave this blank.

### Phone Number (id: 7)
* This is where you can provide your phone number to receive updates or communications from the nonprofit. If you have a public phone number, please use it. If you prefer not to, you can leave this blank.

### Nonprofit Organization (id: 8)
* This is where you can provide your nonprofit organization's name and any relevant information about your role within the organization.

### Date (id: 9)
* This is where you can provide the date you are filling out the form. This helps the nonprofit track when feedback and suggestions are received.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as truthfully as you can.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the nonprofit directly.
* This form is for one-time feedback only, so please provide your most up-to-date opinions and suggestions.
