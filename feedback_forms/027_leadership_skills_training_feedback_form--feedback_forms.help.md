# leadership_skills_training_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a leadership skills training session. The feedback is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the training and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Select the date of the training session in the "Session Date" field.
3. Choose the type of training you attended in the "Training type" field.
4. Rate the effectiveness of the training in the "Effectiveness" field using a numerical value.
5. Provide any recommendations for future trainings in the "Recommendations" field (optional).
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Suggestions" field (optional).
7. Enter any comments or feedback from the participant in the "Participant Comments" field (optional).
8. Rate the trainer's performance using the rating system in the "Trainer Rating" field.
9. Add any additional comments for the trainer in the "Trainer Comments" field (optional).
10. Rate the overall quality of the training in the "Overall Rating" field.
11. List the session facilitators in the "Session facilitators" field (optional).
12. List any additional session facilitators in the "Session facilitators 2" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your participant name.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date of the training session.
* **Training type** (`training_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of training you attended. Options: Leadership Skills, Team Collaboration, Communication.
* **Effectiveness** (`effectiveness`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of the training using a numerical value.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any recommendations for future trainings.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the participant.
* **Trainer Rating** (`trainer_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the trainer's performance. Options: 1-2, 3, 4, 5.
* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments for the trainer.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall quality of the training. Options: 1-2, 3, 4, 5.
* **Session facilitators** (`session_facilitators`, text, optional): List the session facilitators.
* **Session facilitators 2** (`session_facilitators_2`, text, optional): List any additional session facilitators.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help improve future trainings.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the optional fields.
