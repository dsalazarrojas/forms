# Full Width Contact Page Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Full Width Contact Page Responsive form is a contact page designed to collect user feedback and information. It is intended to be a simple and user-friendly way to reach out to our team with questions, suggestions, or comments. This form is typically used by customers, users, or visitors to share their thoughts and experiences with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a text field where you can type in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is also a text field where you can type in your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is a special type of text field that requires the format of a standard email address (e.g., [username]@[domain]).
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This is a text field where you can type in your phone number in the standard format (e.g., +1234567890).
5. Enter your **Message** in the "Message" field. This is the main section where you can type in your thoughts, comments, or questions for our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a standard email address in the format [username]@[domain].
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the standard format (e.g., +1234567890).
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Type in your thoughts, comments, or questions for our team.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the fields that you feel are relevant to your message.
* If you have any attachments or documents to share with our team, you can attach them separately or describe them in the "Message" field.
* Our team will respond to your message as soon as possible.

Note: The fields are not required, so you can skip any of them if you don't have the information or prefer not to share it. However, please be aware that providing your contact information might help our team respond to your message more effectively.
