# Volunteer Coordinator Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to track the exit offboarding process of a volunteer coordinator, ensuring that their departure is smooth and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by reviewing the offboarding process on page 2.
2. On page 2, select the level of satisfaction with the offboarding process.
3. On page 3, provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
4. On page 4, indicate your interest in continuing to volunteer or if you are unsure.
5. On page 5, provide any additional comments or feedback.
6. On page 6, note the date of your last volunteer activity.
7. On page 7, provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Offboarding Process** (`page_2`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of satisfaction with the offboarding process. Please choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
- **Future Opportunities** (`page_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your interest in continuing to volunteer. Choose from Continue volunteering, Maybe, or None.
- **Comments** (`page_5`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
- **Last Volunteer Activity Date** (`page_6`, `date`, required): Note the date of your last volunteer activity.
- **Additional Comments** (`page_7`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
