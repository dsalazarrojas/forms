# Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect testimonials from users about their experiences with a product or service. It's meant to be a general-purpose form that can be used in various contexts, such as customer feedback, reviews, or ratings. The form gathers information about the public or private nature of the testimonial, its content, and other optional fields like image and video uploads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine whether your testimonial is public or private. If it's public, select "Public" from the "Public" dropdown menu on page 1. If it's private, choose "Private".
2. Choose the type of testimonial you're submitting. Select one of the options from the "Testimonial Type" dropdown menu on page 1.
3. Enter your testimonial content in the text box on page 2.
4. If you'd like to attach an image to your testimonial, upload it on page 2.
5. If you'd like to add a video to your testimonial, upload it on page 2.
6. Check the "Private" box on page 2 if you want to keep your testimonial private.
7. Select the status of your testimonial, such as "draft" or "published", on page 2.
8. Enter the date and time when you wrote your testimonial on page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Public** (Select One, Page 1): Choose whether your testimonial is public or private.
- **Testimonial Type** (Select One, Page 1): Select the type of testimonial you're submitting.
- **Testimonial Content** (Text, Page 2): Enter the content of your testimonial.
- **Testimonial Image Upload** (Text, Page 2): Upload an image to accompany your testimonial.
- **Testimonial Video Upload** (Text, Page 2): Upload a video to add context to your testimonial.
- **Private** (Select One, Page 2): Check this box if you want to keep your testimonial private.
- **Status** (Select One, Page 2): Select the status of your testimonial, such as "draft" or "published".
- **Date** (Date, Page 2): Enter the date when you wrote your testimonial.
- **Time** (Time, Page 2): Enter the time when you wrote your testimonial. 

## Tips

* Please make sure you're comfortable with the information you're sharing before submitting your testimonial.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
