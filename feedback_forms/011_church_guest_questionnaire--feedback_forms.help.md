# Church Guest Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from church guests to understand their experiences and preferences. It will help us improve our services and activities to better serve our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Answer the questions about your visit, such as when you first visited and how often you come.
3. Rate your experience with our music, teaching, and atmosphere.
4. Share what you liked best about your visit.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please let us know.
6. Tell us if you plan to return and what you are interested in (select all that apply).
7. If you have a prayer request, you can share it with us.
8. Finally, let us know if you would like to receive information about church activities and events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (guest_name)**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address (email)**: Enter your email address so we can stay in touch.
* **Phone Number (phone)**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Age Range (age_range)**: Select the correct age range for you.
* **When Was Your First Visit (first_visit_date)**: Enter the date of your first visit.
* **How Many Times Have You Visited (visit_count)**: Select how many times you've visited our church (including today).
* **How Did You Hear About Our Church (how_heard)**: Select all ways you heard about our church.
* **How Would You Rate the Music (music_rating)**: Rate our music as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How Would You Rate the Teaching (teaching_rating)**: Rate our teaching as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How Would You Rate the Atmosphere (atmosphere_rating)**: Rate our atmosphere as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How Would You Rate Our Facilities (facilities_rating)**: Rate our facilities as Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor (optional).
* **What Did You Like Best (best_part)**: Share your highlights and experiences about your visit.
* **Do You Have Any Suggestions (suggestions)**: Share any suggestions for improvement.
* **Do You Plan to Return (return_visit)**: Select if you plan to return.
* **What Are You Interested In (interested_in)**: Select all that apply (Membership classes, Bible study groups, Youth programs, etc.).
* **Do You Have a Prayer Request (prayer_request)**: Share a prayer request if you have one.
* **Would You Like to Receive Information (connect_card)**: Let us know if you would like to receive information about church activities and events.
