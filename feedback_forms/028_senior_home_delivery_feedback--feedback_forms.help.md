# Senior Home Delivery Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from seniors on their recent home delivery experience. The feedback is used to help improve the quality of care and services provided by healthcare organizations or delivery services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page to ensure you have all the necessary information to provide a complete feedback.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, as your feedback is valuable for improving services.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields, if any.
4. Use the space provided to add any additional comments or suggestions.
5. Once you have finished, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What did you think of the home delivery?** (senior_home_delivery_feedback, Note, Optional):
	+ This is a text field where you can provide your thoughts and opinions on the home delivery experience.
* **Rating score** (rating_score, Number, Optional):
	+ This is a numerical field where you can rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date, Date, Optional):
	+ This field is used to record the date of the delivery.
* **Comments** (comments, Text, Optional):
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Recipient Name** (recipient_name, Text, Optional):
	+ This field is used to record the name of the person who received the delivery.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please include them in the comments field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please add them in the comments field as well.
* Once you have finished, click the submit button to send your feedback.
* If you are unsure about anything, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant personnel.
