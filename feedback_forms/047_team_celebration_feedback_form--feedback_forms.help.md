# Team Celebration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about a team celebration, helping us better understand what worked well and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions and prompts on each page to the best of your knowledge and experience.
* Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your thoughts and feelings.
* For the "favorite participants" questions, you can select one or more options from the list of provided choices.
* When rating the celebration overall, use the scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not satisfactory" and 5 is "outstanding".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Celebration
* **Celebration** (`team_celebration_feedback_form`, text, optional): Describe your favorite moment(s) from the celebration.

### Page 2: Questions
* **What were your favorite moments?** (`feedback_questions`, text, optional): Share about your favorite moments from the celebration.
* **What did you learn from the celebration?** (`feedback_questions`, text, optional): Think about any new skills or insights you gained from the celebration.

### Page 3: Feedback
* **How could we improve the celebration?** (`team_celebration_feedback_form`, text, optional): Suggest any changes or improvements that could make future celebrations even better.
* **Favorite participants** (`favorite_participants`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the participants that made the celebration stand out to you.
* **Favorite participants** (`favorite_participants_2`, select_multiple, optional): (This field is a duplicate of the previous one, please select the same participants as before.)

### Page 4: Rating
* **How would you rate the celebration overall?** (`team_celebration_feedback_form`, number, optional): Use the scale of 1-5 to rate how well the celebration met your expectations.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback, as it will help us create more enjoyable and engaging celebrations in the future.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "how could we improve" field.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
