# Nail Studio Clean Beauty Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and interests regarding our nail studio clean beauty services. It will help us understand your preferences and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the type of nail service you're interested in (Nail Type) from the list on page 1.
2. Choose the nail product you prefer (Nail Product) from the list on page 1.
3. Select all the nail colors you like (Nail Color) from the list on page 1. You can choose as many colors as you like.
4. Choose the nail solution you need (Nail Solution) from the list on page 1.
5. Select the nail beauty trends that interest you (Nail Beauty Trends) from the list on page 1. You can choose as many trends as you like.
6. Indicate your level of satisfaction with our services (Nail Satisfaction) on page 1.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments about our services (Nail Comments) on page 1.
8. Enter your email address (Contact Info) on page 1.
9. Enter your phone number (Contact Phone) on page 1.
10. Enter your address (Contact Address) on page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nail Type (French, Acrylic, Gel, Dip, Naked)**: Select the type of nail service you're interested in.
* **Nail Product (Lacquer, Gel Polish, Dip Powder, Nail Art, Other)**: Choose the nail product you prefer.
* **Nail Color (Red, Pink, Purple, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Black, White, Brown)**: Select all the nail colors you like.
* **Nail Solution (Base Coat, Top Coat, Cleanser, Remover, Moisturizer)**: Choose the nail solution you need.
* **Nail Beauty Trends (Natural and Organic, Sustainable and Eco-Friendly, Innovative and Trendy, Other)**: Select all the nail beauty trends that interest you.
* **Nail Satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)**: Indicate your level of satisfaction with our services.
* **Nail Comments**: Optionally, provide any additional comments about our services.
* **Contact Info (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your address.

Note: Please fill out this form completely and honestly to help us improve our services.
