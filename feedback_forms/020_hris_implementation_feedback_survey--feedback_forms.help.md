# HRIS Implementation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HRIS Implementation Feedback Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from users about the Human Resource Information System (HRIS) implementation. This feedback is essential for identifying areas of improvement and ensuring that the HRIS meets the needs of its users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button to start completing the form.
- Select the "Feedback Category" from the drop-down menu that best describes your feedback.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field for contact purposes.
- If you wish to provide more information, type it in the "Additional Feedback" field.
- Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information**: This field is optional and allows you to enter any general information you think is relevant to your feedback.
  * **Feedback Score**: This field allows you to rate your overall satisfaction with the HRIS implementation, using a numeric value.
  * **Feedback Details**: This field is optional and allows you to provide more specific details about your feedback.
  * **Email**: This field is required and should be filled with your work email address. This is for contact purposes and will not be made public.
  * **Phone Number**: This field is optional and should be filled with your work phone number. This will help us contact you if needed.
  * **Feedback Category**: This field allows you to select one of the three categories that best describes your feedback: HRIS, Non-HRIS, or Other.
  * **Additional Feedback**: This field is optional and allows you to provide more information about your feedback.
  * **Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated with the current date and is not editable.
