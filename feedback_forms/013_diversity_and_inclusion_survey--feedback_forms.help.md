# Diversity And Inclusion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Diversity and Inclusion Survey aims to gather your thoughts, feedback, and suggestions on diversity and inclusion within the organization. This information will be used to understand and address any concerns or areas of improvement, making our organization a more inclusive and welcoming place for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form sections and questions carefully.
2. For each section, follow the provided instructions and guidelines for inputting your information.
3. If you're unsure about any field or question, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Survey Questions (Optional)
• **Survey Questions**: This is an optional section that will be filled later. You can ignore this section.

### Demographic Information (Required)
• **Demographic Information**: Please select your gender from the provided options: Male, Female, Non-Binary, or Prefer Not To Say.

### Age (Optional)
• **Age**: If you'd like to share your age, please enter a number. This information will help us understand our diverse workforce.

### Job Title (Required)
• **Job Title**: Please enter your current job title or position within the organization.

### Department (Required)
• **Department**: Please enter your department within the organization.

### Job Function (Optional)
• **Job Function**: If you'd like to share your job function or responsibilities, please enter a brief description.

### Years of Experience (Optional)
• **Years of Experience**: If you'd like to share your years of experience, please enter a number.

### Feedback (Optional)
• **Feedback**: Please share any feedback or comments you have about diversity and inclusion within the organization.

### Suggestions (Optional)
• **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions on how we can improve diversity and inclusion, please share them here.

### Comments (Optional)
• **Comments**: Please share any additional comments or thoughts about diversity and inclusion.

### Additional Comments (Optional)
• **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional thoughts or feedback, please share them here.

## Tips

* Be honest and genuine in your responses.
* Keep your answers brief and clear.
* If you're unsure about any question or field, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.
