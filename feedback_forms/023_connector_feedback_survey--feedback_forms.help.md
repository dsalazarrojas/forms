# Connector Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us improve the Connector you are using. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and ensure that the Connector meets your needs. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields with your feedback.
3. For fields with hints, please read the hint carefully and answer accordingly.
4. You can leave fields blank if you do not have an answer for them.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Date**: Please enter the date you last used the Connector. This will help us track your feedback over time.
* **Your Name**: This is your name, and it is optional. You can remain anonymous if you prefer.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address if you would like us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Organization**: This is the name of your company or organization.
* **Connector Name**: Please enter the name of the Connector you are providing feedback on.
* **Connector Version**: If you know the version number of the Connector you used, please enter it here.
* **How Long Have You Used This Connector**: Please select the duration of time you have used the Connector.
* **How Often Do You Use This Connector**: Please select the frequency of use.
* **Primary Use Case**: Please select the primary use case for the Connector.
* **Ease of Initial Setup**: Please rate the ease of initial setup.
* **Ease of Use**: Please rate the ease of use.
* **Reliability Rating**: Please rate the reliability of the Connector.
* **Performance Rating**: Please rate the performance of the Connector.
* **Quality of Documentation**: Please rate the quality of the documentation.
* **Support Experience**: Please rate the quality of technical support you received.
* **Satisfaction with Features**: Please rate your satisfaction with the features of the Connector.
* **Missing Features**: If you would like to see any additional features, please describe them here.
* **Issues Encountered**: If you experienced any issues, please describe them here.
* **How Often Do Issues Occur**: Please select the frequency of issues.
* **How Well Are Issues Resolved**: Please rate the effectiveness of issue resolution.
* **Quality of Integration**: Please rate the quality of integration with your systems.
* **Value for Cost**: Please rate the value for cost of the Connector.
* **Would You Recommend This Connector**: Please select the likelihood of recommending the Connector to others.
* **Suggested Improvements**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please describe them here.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional feedback, please describe them here.

Note that this form is not a one-time use form and can be completed multiple times if you have different experiences with the same Connector. Please make sure to fill in your feedback for each time period of using the Connector separately.
