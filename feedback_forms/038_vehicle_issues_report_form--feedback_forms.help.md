# vehicle_issues_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about vehicle issues reported by users. It helps to understand the problems encountered with a vehicle, including the description, date, time, and condition of the vehicle, and any related repair history. This data can be used to identify common issues and improve maintenance schedules to prevent future problems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button on the vehicle_issues_report_form page.
2. Enter the vehicle details (e.g., year, make, model, etc.) in the **Vehicle Details** field. 
3. Describe the problem in the **Problem Description** field.
4. Select the **Problem Type** from the available options (e.g., Problem with Vehicle, Problem with Engine, etc.).
5. Enter the date of when the problem occurred in the **Problem Date** field.
6. Enter the time of when the problem occurred in the **Problem Time** field.
7. Rate the condition of the vehicle using one or more of the options (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor, Bad) in the **Vehicle Condition** field.
8. Enter any additional vehicle problems in the **Vehicle Problem**, **Vehicle Problem 2**, and **Vehicle Problem 3** fields, as needed.
9. Provide a brief description of the vehicle repair history in the **Vehicle Repair History** field.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Details**: Enter the make, model, year, and other relevant details about the vehicle.
* **Problem Description**: Describe the problem in as much detail as possible.
* **Problem Type**: Select the category of the problem (e.g., Problem with Vehicle, Problem with Engine).
* **Problem Date**: Enter the date when the problem occurred.
* **Problem Time**: Enter the time when the problem occurred.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Rate the condition of the vehicle (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor, Bad).
* **Vehicle Problem**: Enter any additional vehicle problems, as needed.
* **Vehicle Problem 2**: Enter any additional vehicle problems, as needed.
* **Vehicle Problem 3**: Enter any additional vehicle problems, as needed.
* **Vehicle Repair History**: Provide a brief description of any previous repairs or maintenance done on the vehicle.
