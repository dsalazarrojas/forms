# Service Customization Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their preferences and needs for service customization. It's a survey that asks questions about their top preferences and what services they'd like to customize. The goal is to understand how to best serve them and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question on client preferences.
3. If you have a specific service in mind that you'd like to customize, type it into the "Service Customization" field.
4. If you'd like to provide contact information, select all the relevant options (e.g., "Yes" for both "Yes" options).
5. Submit your survey by typing a brief message into the "Submission" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the survey**: This is a note field to introduce you to the survey and its purpose.
* **What is your top preference?**: This is a yes/no question to gauge your top preference. Answer "Yes" if you have a strong preference, and "No" otherwise.
* **Which of the following services would you like to customize?**: This is a text field where you can type in specific services you'd like to customize. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Please provide your contact information**: This is a multiple select field where you can choose to provide your email address and/or phone number. Select "Yes" for each option if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Please submit your survey**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or questions about the survey or services you'd like to see customized.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, use the "Service Customization" field to type it in.
* You can also add any additional comments or questions in the "Submission" field.
