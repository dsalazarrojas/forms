# Call To Action Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the clarity, sense of urgency, and relevance of a call to action (CTA) in a campaign or asset. It will help us understand how well the CTA communicates its message to the target audience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Reviewer Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "Reviewer Email" field. This will allow us to follow up with you if needed.
3. Enter the name of the campaign or asset being reviewed in the "Campaign Name or Asset" field.
4. Copy and paste the exact CTA text you are reviewing into the "Call To Action Text" field.
5. Choose how clear, urgent, and relevant the CTA is to the target audience in the "Clarity Rating", "Sense of Urgency Rating", and "Relevance to Audience" fields, respectively.
6. Provide specific suggested improvements to the CTA in the "Suggested Improvements" field.
7. Enter the expected action the user should take after clicking on the CTA in the "Expected User Action" field.
8. Finally, provide any additional comments or observations in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your full name to help us understand who is providing feedback on the CTA.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter your contact email so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Campaign Name or Asset**: Enter the name of the page, email, or ad containing the CTA being reviewed.
* **Call To Action Text**: Copy and paste the exact CTA text you are reviewing into this field.
* **Clarity Rating**: Select how clear the CTA is to the target audience, with options: Very Clear, Clear, Neutral, Unclear, Very Unclear.
* **Sense of Urgency Rating**: Select how well the CTA conveys a sense of urgency, with options: Strong, Moderate, Weak, None.
* **Relevance to Audience**: Select how relevant the CTA is to the target audience, with options: Highly Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, Not Relevant.
* **Suggested Improvements**: Provide specific suggestions to improve the CTA wording, placement, or design.
* **Expected User Action**: Enter the action the user should take after clicking on the CTA.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the CTA.
