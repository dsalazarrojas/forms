# Event Meal Contribution Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Meal Contribution Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from attendees about their experience with meal contributions at events. This feedback helps us understand the quality of meal contributions and identify areas for improvement to provide better experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose your rating for the meal contribution.
2. Provide detailed feedback about your experience in the "Comments" field.
3. Answer the question "Would You Re-contribute?".
4. Indicate your interest in contributing to future meal contributions.
5. Suggest improvements or changes to the meal contribution process.
6. Share your contact information so we can follow up with you.
7. If you'd like to repeat any answers you've already provided, you can enter them in the "Repeat of previous questions" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with the meal contribution using one of the scales: Scale 1, Scale 2, Scale 3, Scale 4, or Scale 5.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required): Provide detailed feedback about your experience, such as what worked well or what didn't.
* **Would You Re-contribute?** (`Would You Re-contribute?`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would be interested in contributing to future meal contributions.
* **Future Contribution Interest** (`Future Contribution Interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how likely you are to contribute to future meal contributions, using one of the options: True, False, Maybe, Very Likely, Highly Likely, or Not Likely.
* **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, `text`, required): Suggest improvements or changes to the meal contribution process.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Share your contact information so we can follow up with you.
* **Repeat 1**, **Repeat 2**, **Repeat 3** (`Repeat 1`, `text`, `Repeat 2`, `text`, `Repeat 3`, `text`, required): If you'd like to repeat any answers you've already provided, enter them in the corresponding sections.
