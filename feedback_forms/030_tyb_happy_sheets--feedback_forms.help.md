# TYB Happy Sheets - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users of the 'TYB Happy Sheets' service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your information**: Fill in the fields with your details, including who reported this feedback, your contact phone number, and your email address.
2. **Rate your experience**: Select a number to rate your overall experience and your satisfaction level with the service.
3. **Provide additional feedback**: If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the 'Additional comment' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Who reported this feedback?
* **Who reported this feedback?** (`reporter`, `text`, required): This field is used to indicate who reported this feedback, such as a customer or a staff member.
### 2. Overall rating
* **Overall rating** (`feedback_form_1_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with the service, using a numerical value.
### 3. How satisfied were you?
* **How satisfied were you?** (`feedback_form_1_satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction level with the service, using a numerical value.
### 4. Would you recommend this service?
* **Would you recommend this service?** (`recommended`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would recommend this service to others.
### 5. Time of visit
* **Time of visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit.
### 6. Date of visit
* **Date of visit** (`date_of_visit`, `text`, required): Enter the date of your visit.
### 7. Contact phone
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
### 8. Contact email
* **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
### 9. Additional comment
* **Additional comment** (`feedback`, `note`, required): Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the service.
