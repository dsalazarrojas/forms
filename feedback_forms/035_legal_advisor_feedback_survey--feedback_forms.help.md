# Legal Advisor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Advisor Feedback Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from Legal Advisors about our legal services. This survey helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that you only fill out this form once, as subsequent submissions will be deleted.
3. Provide your First Name, Last Name, and Email to allow us to follow up on any issues you may raise.
4. If applicable, provide your Company, Job Title, and Department to help us understand your role and context.
5. Rate our legal services from 1 to 5, providing feedback on the quality of service you received.
6. Share any suggestions for how we can improve our services.
7. Choose how you would like to be contacted, and if you select "Other", please specify how you would prefer to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents and records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents and records.
* **Email**: Enter the email address where you can be reached regarding any issues or concerns.
* **Company**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company you work for.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Department**: Enter the department you work in within the company.
* **How would you rate the quality of our legal services?**: Rate our legal services from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Have you got any suggestions for improvement?**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have on how we can improve our services.
* **Rating of our legal services (1-5)**: Rate our legal services from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How do you prefer to contact us?**: Select how you would like to be contacted, such as Contact by phone, Email, or In-person. If you select "Other", please enter a specific preference.
* **Other**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide an explanation of how you would prefer to be contacted.
