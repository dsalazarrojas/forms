# Customer Feedback Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Feedback Monitoring Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with our company's services. Please take a few minutes to answer this form honestly to help us understand what we did well and what we can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Customer Feedback** section with a brief description of your experience with our company.
2. Select a rating between 1 and 5 stars to indicate your satisfaction level.
3. If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please type them in the **Comments** field.
4. If applicable, select which tool was assigned to you from the list.
5. Enter your name and email address so that we can contact you if needed.
6. Provide your company and department information if applicable.
7. Finally, enter your phone number so that we can contact you if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback** (Customer feedback, note, optional): Please provide a brief description of your experience with our company.
* **Rating** (Rating, select_one, optional): Select a rating between 1 and 5 stars to indicate your satisfaction level.
* **Satisfaction Level** (Satisfaction Level, number, optional): This field is not currently being used and will be removed in the next version.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please type them in this field.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, optional): If applicable, select which tool was assigned to you from the list.
* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, optional): Please enter your name so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Customer Email** (Customer Email, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Customer Phone** (Customer Phone, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Company** (Company, text, optional): Provide your company information if applicable.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Provide your department information if applicable.
