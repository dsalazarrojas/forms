# Aerospace Program Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the aerospace program. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and enhance future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Complete all required fields marked as "true" with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. Use the "Add Note" option to provide additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information_section`, not required): A brief introduction to the form, explaining that you are providing feedback on the aerospace program.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, not required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your name tag or identification card.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, not required): Your email address for follow-up communication (if you wish to receive updates or notifications).
* **Organization** (`participant_organization`, not required): Name of your organization or institution.
* **Role or Position** (`participant_role`, not required): Your role or position in your organization.
* **Program Information** (`program_information_section`, not required): A section providing background information about the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, required): The name of the aerospace program you attended.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, required): The date the program took place.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, not required): Where the program was held.
* **Program Duration** (`program_duration`, not required): The duration of the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction_section`, not required): A rating of your overall satisfaction with the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, required): How satisfied were you with the program overall (1-5).
* **Content Relevance Rating** (`content_relevance_rating`, required): How relevant was the content to your needs (1-5).
* **Speaker Quality Rating** (`speaker_quality_rating`, required): How would you rate the quality of the speakers/presenters (1-5).
* **Program Organization Rating** (`program_organization_rating`, required): How well was the program organized (1-5).
* **Technical Depth Satisfaction** (`technical_depth_satisfaction`, required): Was the technical depth of the content appropriate for your level (Too Basic, Adequate, Challenging but Appropriate, Too Advanced)?
* **Industry Relevance** (`industry_relevance`, required): How relevant were the topics to current aerospace industry trends (1-5)?
* **Innovation Coverage** (`innovation_coverage`, required): Did the program adequately cover emerging technologies and innovations (1-5)?
* **Practical Applications** (`practical_applications`, required): Were practical applications of the concepts well explained (1-5)?
* **Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunities_section`, not required): A section for providing feedback on networking opportunities.
* **Networking Quality Rating** (`networking_quality_rating`, not required): How would you rate the quality of networking opportunities (1-5)?
* **Peer Interaction Opportunities** (`peer_interaction_opportunities`, not required): Were there sufficient opportunities to interact with peers (True, Somewhat, False)?
* **Expert Access Opportunities** (`expert_access_opportunities`, not required): Were there sufficient opportunities to interact with experts (True, Somewhat, False)?
* **Logistics and Facilities** (`logistics_facilities_section`, not required): A section for providing feedback on logistics and facilities.
* **Venue Satisfaction** (`venue_satisfaction`, required): How satisfied were you with the venue and facilities (1-5)?
* **Catering Satisfaction** (`catering_satisfaction`, not required): How satisfied were you with the catering and refreshments (1-5)?
* **Technical Equipment Quality** (`technical_equipment_quality`, required): How would you rate the quality of technical equipment provided (1-5)?
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions_section`, not required): A section for providing suggestions for improvement.
* **Most Valuable Aspect** (`most_valuable_aspect`, required): What was the most valuable aspect of the program for you.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, required): What improvements would you suggest for future programs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, not required): Any other feedback or comments about the program.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, required): How likely are you to recommend this program to colleagues (1-5)?

## Tips
* Answer all required fields thoroughly and honestly.
* Be specific with your answers to provide valuable feedback.
* Use the "Add Note" option to provide more details or explanations.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us at [contact information].
