# UX/UI Team Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UX/UI Team Performance Evaluation Form is a tool to help assess and improve the performance of the UX/UI team. It collects feedback on team leads and individual team members' contributions, aiming to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the team's overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form fields according to your knowledge and experience with the UX/UI team.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback and comments.
4. If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, text, optional): This field is meant for the team lead's name, if applicable. If the team lead is not applicable or not known, please leave this field blank.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`, text, optional): This field is for the first team member's name. You can list as many team members as you'd like, but please provide separate input for each team member in the following fields.
* **Team Member 2** (`team_member_2`, text, optional): This field is for the second team member's name. If there are more than two team members, please add additional team member fields as necessary.
* **Team Lead Rating** (`team_lead_rating`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the following options to rate the team lead's performance: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Team Lead Comments** (`team_lead_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the team lead's performance. This can include specific examples or instances where they demonstrated exceptional leadership or areas where they could improve.
* **Team Member** (no label, text, optional): This field is for individual team members to provide comments and feedback about their own performance. Please be honest and specific when providing input.
* **Team Member 2** (no label, text, optional): This field is for individual team members to provide comments and feedback about their own performance. Please be honest and specific when providing input.

## Tips
* Please be respectful and constructive when providing feedback and comments.
* Avoid duplicate or redundant information.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, don't hesitate to ask.
* Remember, this form is a tool to improve the team's performance, so aim to provide actionable and specific feedback that can help the team grow and improve.
