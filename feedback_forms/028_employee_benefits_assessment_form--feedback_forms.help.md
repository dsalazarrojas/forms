# Employee Benefits Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experiences and opinions regarding benefits offered by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and fill in all required fields.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible when filling in the optional fields.
3. Submit your feedback once you have completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): This field asks for your name to be used as a reference.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): This field is for your official job title.
* **Department** (Department, text, required): Please provide your department name.
* **Employee Email** (Employee Email, email, required): This is your work email address.
* **Employee Phone** (Employee Phone, text, required): This is your work phone number.
* **Job Title Work With** (Job Title Work With, select_multiple, optional): If you work with a specific job title, please select them here.
* **Job Title Report To** (Job Title Report To, select_multiple, optional): If you report to a specific job title, please select them here.
* **Benefits Packages** (Benefits Packages, text, optional): This field is for you to provide information about your benefits packages.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (Overall Satisfaction, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with a number (1-10).
* **Benefits Packages Rating** (Benefits Packages Rating, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the benefits packages. Options are: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Terrible.
* **Benefits Packages Rating Reasons** (Benefits Packages Rating Reasons, text, optional): Provide reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction with the benefits packages.
* **Benefits Packages Suggestion** (Benefits Packages Suggestion, text, optional): Suggest any changes or improvements for the benefits packages.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): This is an open-ended field for any other feedback you have.
* **Benefits Satisfaction Level** (Benefits Satisfaction Level, select_multiple, optional): If you want to rate the satisfaction level of your benefits, please select here.
* **Benefits Urgency** (Benefits Urgency, select_multiple, optional): If you want to rate the urgency level of your benefits needs, please select here.
* **Department Other** (Department Other, text, optional): If your department is not listed, please describe it here.
* **Benefits Frequency** (Benefits Frequency, select_multiple, optional): If you want to rate the frequency of your benefits needs, please select here.
* **Benefits Expectations** (Benefits Expectations, select_multiple, optional): If you want to rate the expectations from your benefits, please select here.
* **Benefits Pain Points** (Benefits Pain Points, select_multiple, optional): If you want to rate the pain points from your benefits, please select here.
* **Benefits Suggested Improvements** (Benefits Suggested Improvements, select_multiple, optional): If you have suggestions for benefits improvements, please select here.
* **Benefits Open Comments** (Benefits Open Comments, text, optional): This is an open-ended field for any other comments you have.
* **Created By** (Created By, text, optional): This field is not intended for employee use, it is for tracking purposes only.
