# Feminine Guidebook Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the "Feminine Guidebook" project, a resource for women, by women. Your input will help us improve the guidebook's content, layout, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. If you have an email address, please provide it so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
3. In the "Feedback" field, describe your thoughts, suggestions, and comments on the guidebook.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, as you would like it to be displayed in the guidebook.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, as you would like it to be displayed in the guidebook.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback, enter your email address.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share your thoughts, suggestions, and comments on the guidebook. Be as detailed or as brief as you'd like. We appreciate any feedback that can help us improve the guidebook.

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* If you have a specific suggestion, please try to provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have a question or need help with your feedback, don't hesitate to contact us at contact@yourdomain.com.
