# Philanthropy Summit Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the Philanthropy Summit. Your input will help us improve future events and enhance your overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and fill in the relevant fields.
2. Rate the event and sessions on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "bad" and 5 is "excellent".
3. Select your preferred option for each question.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Rating**: Rate your overall experience of the Philanthropy Summit. This will help us gauge the success of the event.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the event. Is there something you particularly enjoyed or disliked? Do you have suggestions for improvement?
* **Suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or ideas you have for future events.
* **Event Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Session Ratings**: Rate each session on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "bad" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Session Quality**: Rate each session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Session Relevance**: Rate how relevant the sessions were to your interests and needs.
* **Speaker Presentation**: Rate the quality of the speaker's presentation on a scale of 1-5.
* **Catering**: Rate the quality of the catering on a scale of 1-5.
* **Venue Facilities**: Rate the quality of the venue facilities on a scale of 1-5.
* **Networking Opportunities**: Rate the opportunities for networking on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Impression**: Rate your overall impression of the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Will Attend AG**: Let us know if you plan to attend the next Philanthropy Summit.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for future events.

## Tips

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions, please be specific and concrete.
* Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation.
