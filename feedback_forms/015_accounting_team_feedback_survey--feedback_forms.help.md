# Accounting Team Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from members of the accounting team regarding their experiences, job satisfaction, and areas for improvement. Your responses will help us understand the team's strengths and weaknesses, enabling us to make informed decisions to enhance team performance and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Optional: Enter your name** if you wish to be identified. This information will be kept confidential and solely used for internal purposes.
2. Select your role within the accounting team.
3. Indicate how long you have been part of the team.
4. Rate your overall job satisfaction, workload manageability, quality of team collaboration, effectiveness of team communication, availability of necessary resources, and level of management support.
5. **Optional: Select all major challenges you face** in your role.
6. Provide suggestions for team improvement.
7. Share any additional feedback or comments about the team or work environment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name (Optional)** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name if you wish to be identified.
* **Role in the Accounting Team** (`role_in_team`, `select_one`, required): Select your position in the accounting department. This helps us understand your perspective and responsibilities within the team.
* **Years with the Accounting Team** (`years_with_team`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how long you have been part of this accounting team. This will help us assess your level of experience and team engagement.
* **Overall Job Satisfaction** (`overall_job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with your role in the accounting team. This will help us understand your level of engagement and motivation.
* **Workload Manageability** (`workload_manageability`, `select_one`, required): Rate how manageable your current workload is. This will help us identify potential workload issues and areas for improvement.
* **Quality of Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration_quality`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the collaboration within the accounting team. This will help us assess the team's dynamics and communication.
* **Effectiveness of Team Communication** (`communication_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the effectiveness of team communication. This will help us identify potential communication gaps and areas for improvement.
* **Availability of Necessary Resources** (`resource_availability`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have access to the resources you need to perform your job effectively. This will help us identify potential resource constraints.
* **Professional Development Opportunities** (`professional_development_opportunities`, `select_one`, required): Assess the availability of professional growth and development opportunities. This will help us understand the team's potential for growth and development.
* **Level of Management Support** (`management_support_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate the level of support provided by management to the accounting team. This will help us understand the team's relationship with management.
* **Major Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all major challenges you face in your role. This will help us identify areas for improvement and support.
* **Suggestions for Team Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): Provide suggestions on how the accounting team can improve its performance and efficiency.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any other comments or feedback about the team or work environment.
