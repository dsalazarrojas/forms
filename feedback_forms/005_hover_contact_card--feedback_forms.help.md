# Hover Contact Card - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hover Contact Card form is designed to collect feedback from users about contacts, providing a structured way to share their thoughts, suggestions, or concerns in an organized and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Hover Contact Card form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your Full Name, which is required only if you're reporting an issue or concern.
2. Enter your Email address to enable us to contact you regarding your feedback.
3. Enter your Phone number, if applicable, to allow for quicker follow-up discussions.
4. Select the Department that best relates to your issue or concern from the dropdown list.
5. Share any additional Resources that might be helpful for us to understand the context of your issue.
6. Provide Comments about the issue or concern you're reporting.
7. If you have an Assigned Tool related to the issue, select it from the dropdown list.
8. Finally, provide a brief Description of the issue or concern.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (text, required/optional)**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as the reporter of the issue or concern.
* **Email (email, required/optional)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: If you have a phone number you'd like to share, enter it here.
* **Department (select_one, optional)**: Choose the department that best relates to the issue or concern you're reporting.
* **Resources (note, optional)**: Provide any additional resources that might be helpful for us to understand the context of your issue.
* **Comments (text, optional)**: Share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about the issue or concern.
* **Assigned Tool (select_multiple, optional)**: If you have an Assigned Tool related to the issue, select it from the dropdown list.
* **Description (text, optional)**: Provide a brief description of the issue or concern you're reporting.
