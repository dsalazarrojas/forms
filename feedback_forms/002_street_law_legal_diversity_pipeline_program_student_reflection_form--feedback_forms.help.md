# street_law_legal_diversity_pipeline_program_student_reflection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student feedback and reflections for the Street Law and Legal Diversity Pipeline Program. It is meant to be completed by students participating in the program and is used to gather their thoughts, opinions, and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Grade in the Grade field.
4. Enter the School Year in the School Year field.
5. Enter the name of your school in the School Name field.
6. Enter the name of your teacher/educator in the Teacher Name field.
7. Enter any comments or feedback you have in the Comments field.
8. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
9. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
10. Select a Date and Time for your submission in the Date and Time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Grade** (text, optional): Enter your current grade or level of education (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
* **School Year** (text, optional): Enter the current year you are in school.
* **School Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your teacher or educator.
* **Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select a date for your submission.
* **Time** (time, optional): Select a time for your submission.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can fill them in as you see fit. However, it is recommended to provide as much information as possible for better reference and evaluation.
