# Theater Group Participant Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants who have been discharged from a theater group, which could be a group of people working together on a project or a team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are a participant who has been discharged from a theater group.
2. Read the instructions and questions carefully, and answer them as accurately as possible.
3. Enter your answers in the required fields, as marked.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Discharge
* **Discharge** (`1`, `number`, optional): This is the first question on the form. Please enter your discharge number here.
### Discharge Date
* **Discharge Date** (`2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your discharge here.
### Discharge Time
* **Discharge Time** (`3`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your discharge here.
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name here.
### Group Name
* **Group Name** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the group you were a part of here.
### Group Leader Name
* **Group Leader Name** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the group leader here.
### Discharge Cause
* **Discharge Cause** (`7`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the reason for your discharge here.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`8`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them here.
### Participant Rating
* **Participant Rating** (`9`, `number`, optional): This is a rating of your satisfaction level, and should be one of the following:
	+ 0: Very Satisfied
	+ 1: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ 3: Very Dissatisfied
### Participant Satisfaction
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options to rate your level of satisfaction:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
### Suggestions
* **Suggestions** (`11`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, enter them here.
