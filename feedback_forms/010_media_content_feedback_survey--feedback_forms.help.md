# Media Content Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on various types of media content from users, helping us improve our content and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to select the correct media type (video, podcast, article, or campaign) for which you are providing feedback.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for our contact purposes.
4. Provide your rating and suggestions.
5. Select a category that best describes your feedback.
6. If you have any other comments or feedback, please enter it in the feedback text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Feedback** (`video_feedback`, text, required): Enter your feedback on the video content. This is an essential field that helps us understand what you liked or disliked about the video.
* **Podcast Feedback** (`podcast_feedback`, text, optional): If you're providing feedback on a podcast, enter your comments here. This field is not required but can help us improve our podcast content.
* **Article Feedback** (`article_feedback`, text, optional): If you're providing feedback on an article, enter your comments here. This field is not required but can help us improve our article content.
* **Campaign Feedback** (`campaign_feedback`, text, optional): If you're providing feedback on a campaign, enter your comments here. This field is not required but can help us improve our campaign content.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience with the content, from 1 to 5.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our content, enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Enter any other comments or feedback you may have.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select all categories that apply to your feedback. This field helps us identify the type of feedback you're providing.
* **Select a category** (`category_options`, text, optional): This field is not required but can be used to provide more specific feedback if you have additional comments or suggestions.
