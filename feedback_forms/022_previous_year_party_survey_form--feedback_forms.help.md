## previous_year_party_survey_form - Help Guide
### Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about the previous year's party. It aims to capture information about the party's overall quality, themes, attendees, and activities. Please fill this form only if you attended the previous year's party.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Select the year of the party you want to provide feedback for.
2. Enter the date of the party.
3. Describe the theme of the party.
4. Indicate the number of guests you attended the party with.
5. Provide a brief description of the party food.
6. Describe the party drinks.
7. List any games or activities you participated in during the party.
8. Enter any notes about the party.
9. Provide your overall rating of the party (e.g., 1-5 stars).
10. (Optional) Enter your rating for any particular aspect of the party (e.g., music, decorations, etc.).

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **party_year** (text, required): Enter the year of the party you are providing feedback for.
* **party_date** (date, required): Enter the date of the party in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **party_theme** (text, optional): Describe the theme or atmosphere of the party.
* **party_guests** (number, required): Indicate the number of guests you attended the party with.
* **party_food** (text, optional): Describe the type and quality of food provided at the party.
* **party_drinks** (text, optional): Describe the type and quality of drinks provided at the party.
* **party_games** (text, optional): List any games or activities you participated in during the party.
* **party_activities** (text, optional): List any other activities you participated in during the party.
* **party_notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the party.
* **overall_rating** (text, required): Provide your overall rating of the party (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **rating_1** (text, optional): Enter your rating for any particular aspect of the party (e.g., music, decorations, etc.).
