# Building Occupancy Optimization Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
### This form is used to gather information about your experiences and opinions regarding the building's occupiable spaces. The goal is to understand how well the space meets your needs and identify areas for improvement. Please answer all questions truthfully to provide valuable insights for optimizing the building's occupancy.

## How To Complete This Form
### This form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific aspect of the building's occupiable space. Please answer the questions in the following order:
1. Select the date when you completed this survey.
2. Enter the name of the building you are currently in.
3. If applicable, enter the floor number where you are located.
4. Select the type of workspace you typically use (e.g., office, meeting room, open area).
5. Choose how often you use this space.
6. Estimate the average hours spent daily in this space.
7. Rate the space's adequacy to meet your needs.
8. Assess the level of privacy in the space.
9. Evaluate the availability of collaboration spaces.
10. Rate the overall comfort level of the space.
11. Rate the temperature and climate control in the space.
12. Evaluate the lighting quality.
13. Rate the noise level.
14. Evaluate the technology support in the space.
15. Rate the quality of furniture and ergonomics.
16. Rate the accessibility of amenities (e.g., break rooms, coffee machines).
17. (Optional) Suggest ways to improve space usage.
18. (Optional) List any missing or needed facilities.
19. Finally, rate your overall satisfaction with the building's space.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date**: The date when you completed this survey. Please select the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Building Name**: Enter the name of the building where you are currently working or located.
* **Floor Number**: If you are on a specific floor, enter the floor number. If you are not on a floor, leave this field blank.
* **Type of Workspace You Use**: Select the type of workspace you typically use, such as office, meeting room, or open area.
* **How Often Do You Use This Space**: Indicate how often you use this space. Select one of the following options: Every Day, Several Times Per Week, Weekly, Few Times Per Month, or Rarely.
* **Average Hours Spent Daily**: Estimate the average hours spent daily in this space. You can enter a number value.
* **Space Adequacy for Your Needs**: Rate the space's adequacy to meet your needs. Select one of the following options: More Than Adequate, Adequate, Somewhat Adequate, or Inadequate.
* **Privacy Level**: Assess the level of privacy in the space. Select one of the following options: Very Private, Private, Moderate Privacy, Limited Privacy, or No Privacy.
* **Collaboration Space Availability**: Evaluate the availability of collaboration spaces. Select one of the following options: Abundant, Adequate, Limited, or Insufficient.
* **Overall Comfort Level**: Rate the overall comfort level of the space. Select one of the following options: Very Comfortable, Comfortable, Neutral, Uncomfortable, or Very Uncomfortable.
* **Temperature and Climate Control**: Rate the temperature and climate control in the space. Select one of the following options: Always Comfortable, Usually Comfortable, Sometimes Uncomfortable, or Often Uncomfortable.
* **Lighting Quality**: Evaluate the lighting quality in the space. Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Noise Level**: Rate the noise level in the space. Select one of the following options: Very Quiet, Quiet, Moderate, Noisy, or Very Noisy.
* **Technology Support**: Evaluate the technology support in the space, such as WiFi, outlets, and connectivity. Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Furniture and Ergonomics**: Rate the quality and comfort of furniture in the space. Select one of the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Amenities Access**: Evaluate the accessibility of amenities, such as break rooms, coffee machines, or other facilities. Select one of the following options: Very Accessible, Accessible, Somewhat Accessible, or Not Accessible.
* **Space Usage Could Be Optimized**: Suggest ways to improve space usage if you feel it could be done more efficiently. Please enter your suggestions.
* **Missing or Needed Facilities**: List any missing or needed facilities that would improve the space. Please enter your suggestions.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Space**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the building's space. Select one of the following options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.

Thinking...
This guide is intended for end-users who want to provide their honest feedback on their experiences with the building's occupiable spaces. This form is designed to be completed by individuals who use the space regularly or occasionally. Please answer the questions based on your actual experiences and observations. If you're unsure about any question, try your best to provide an honest answer.

Please note that this form is not intended for multiple responses, so please complete it once. If you have already completed this form, please do not fill it out again. Your feedback is valuable, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve the building's spaces.
