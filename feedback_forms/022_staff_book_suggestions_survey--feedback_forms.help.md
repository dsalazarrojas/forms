# Staff Book Suggestions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect book suggestions from staff members. The goal is to collect a variety of book suggestions that can be used to inform purchasing decisions for the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out each field completely and accurately.
2. Make sure you have checked the current catalog to avoid duplicating suggestions.
3. Select only the options that apply to you.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite book suggestion?** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_form`, text, required)
	+ A field where you can enter your book suggestion.
* **Name** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your name, or leave this field blank if you prefer.
* **Email** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_phone`, text, required)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Book Suggestions** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_book_suggestions`, text, required)
	+ Enter your book suggestions in detail.
* **Department** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_department`, text, required)
	+ Enter the department you belong to (e.g., Marketing, IT, etc.).
* **Comments** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_comments`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Do you agree to share your email and phone number?** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_agree`, select_one, required)
	+ Select one of the options: Agree, Disagree, or Undecided.
* **Agree to share your email and phone number?** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_agree_to_share`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This field is not required, but it's a good idea to select Agree if you're comfortable sharing your email and phone number.
* **Submit** (`staff_book_suggestions_survey_submit`, text, required)
	+ Click to submit your completed form.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask your supervisor or the IT department.

Note: The form fields are designed to collect relevant information, and some fields have specific requirements (e.g., email and phone numbers). Please fill out each field according to its label.
