# customer_experience_enhancement_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with the company. Your input will help us understand your thoughts and opinions, allowing us to make improvements and better serve you and our other customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose how you feel about your recent interaction with us by selecting a rating in the "How Do You Feel" field.
2. Share any specific improvements or suggestions you have in mind in the "Suggest Improvements" field.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Choose the category of your feedback in the "Category" field. This will help us understand which area of our business you are commenting on.
5. If you have any contact information, please enter your email address in the "Email" field, so we can follow up with you directly.
6. If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Us** (`contact_form`, text, optional): This is a text field where you can enter any information you would like to share with us. This can be your email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
* **How Do You Feel** (`feedback_form`, select_one, optional): Choose how you feel about your recent interaction with us. You can select from the following options: 1 - 5, 6 - 10, 11 - 15, 16 - 20.
* **Suggest Improvements** (`suggestion_form`, text, optional): Share any specific improvements or suggestions you have in mind. This is a text field where you can provide detailed feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`feedback_form`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have. This is a text field where you can add any extra information.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of your feedback. You can select from the following options: Product, Service, Overall.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have any contact information, please enter your email address here, so we can follow up with you directly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory, but providing your contact information will help us follow up with you and understand your feedback better.
