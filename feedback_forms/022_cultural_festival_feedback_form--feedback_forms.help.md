# Cultural Festival Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from festival attendees on the cultural festival event they attended. We appreciate your input to help us improve future festivals!

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Festival Name** field with the name of the festival you attended.
2. Select the **Event Date** of the festival you attended in the date picker.
3. Enter the **Time Attended**, if applicable.
4. Type any **Feedback** you have about the festival in the text area.
5. Enter your **Email**, if you would like to be contacted back about your feedback.
6. Enter your **Phone**, if you would like to be contacted by phone about your feedback.
7. Choose your **Rating** (1-5) to indicate your overall satisfaction with the festival.
8. Enter any **Comments** you have about the festival in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Festival Name**: Enter the name of the festival you attended. This will help us understand which festival you are providing feedback about.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the festival you attended. This is required to associate your feedback with the correct festival.
* **Time Attended**: If you attended for a specific time, enter it here. If you didn't attend for a specific time, just skip this field.
* **Feedback**: Write down your thoughts about your experience at the festival. Be as detailed or brief as you like!
* **Email**: If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, enter your email address here. We'll be happy to follow up with you!
* **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted by phone about your feedback, enter your phone number here.
* **Rating**: Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the festival. A 1-2 indicates a poor experience, 3 is average, 4-5 indicates a great experience.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments you have about the festival here. This is your chance to elaborate on your feedback!
