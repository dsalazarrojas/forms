# crisis_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users regarding a crisis response. It is designed to gather information about the user's experience and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each one as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Enter your feedback response in the first field (Feedback Response).
3. Rate your response by providing a number rating in the second field (Rate Response).
4. Provide any additional suggestions or comments in the third field (Suggestion).
5. Enter your name in the fourth field (Your Name).
6. Submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Response** (Feedback Response, text, required: false)
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter your feedback about the crisis response. Please provide a brief description of your experience and any thoughts or concerns you have.
* **Rate Response** (Rate Response, number, required: false)
	+ This field is a numerical rating system where you can provide a rating for your response. Please rate your experience from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Suggestion** (Suggestion, text, required: false)
	+ This field is an open-ended text box where you can suggest any improvements or changes that you think could be made to the crisis response.
* **Your Name** (Your Name, text, required: false)
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter your name so that we can acknowledge your feedback and follow up with you if needed. 

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback and suggestions.
* Keep your responses and suggestions concise and clear.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please feel free to include them in the Suggestion field.
