# Student Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from students on their experiences in a course. Your input will help us improve the course content, organization, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field as it comes up.
2. Answer each question as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
3. You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your experience with the course.
4. When filling out the select_one fields (communication with instructor, course materials), choose the option that best fits your opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Student Identifier (1)** 
Student Identifier (1): text
This field asks for your unique identifier to help us track your response.

*   *Student Identifier (1)* (`student_identifier`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student number or any other unique identifier given to you by your institution or teacher.

### **Course Name (2)** 
Course Name (2): text
Enter the name of the course you took.

*   *Course Name (2)* (`course_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the course you took.

### **Instructor Name (3)** 
Instructor Name (3): text
Enter the instructor's name.

*   *Instructor Name (3)* (`instructor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the instructor's name that taught the course.

### **Course Satisfaction (4)** 
Course Satisfaction (4): number
 Rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.

*   *Course Satisfaction (4)* (`course_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.

### **Communication with Instructor (5)** 
Communication with Instructor (5): select_one
Rate your satisfaction with communication with the instructor.

*   *Communication with Instructor (5)* (`communication_with_instructor`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with communication with the instructor.

### **Course Materials (6)** 
Course Materials (6): select_one
Rate your satisfaction with course materials.

*   *Course Materials (6)* (`course_materials`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with course materials.

### **Feedback (7)** 
Feedback (7): text
Enter any comments or suggestions you have for improvement.

*   *Feedback (7)* (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments or suggestions you have for improvement.

### **Overall Satisfaction (8)** 
Overall Satisfaction (8): number
Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.

*   *Overall Satisfaction (8)* (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.
