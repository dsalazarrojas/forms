# thumbnail_design_feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on a design project, focusing on thumbnail design. The form is intended to elicit responses from users on the design's visual elements, color scheme, and overall aesthetic. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Please provide your First Name and Last Name on the first two fields, which are text input fields for personal information.
3. Enter your Email address on the third field, ensuring it matches your actual email address to allow for future correspondence.
4. In the final field, provide your feedback on the design, describing your thoughts on its visual elements, color scheme, and overall aesthetic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly for correspondence.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Share your thoughts on the design project's visual elements, color scheme, and overall aesthetic. Be as specific and detailed as possible to help us better understand your feedback. 

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback, as it will help us improve the design project.
* Please keep your feedback concise and clear, making it easier for us to understand and implement changes.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification on the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
