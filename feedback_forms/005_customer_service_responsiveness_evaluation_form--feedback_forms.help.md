# Customer Service Responsiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Customer Service Responsiveness Evaluation Form" is a tool used to evaluate the performance of customer service representatives. The form helps to identify areas of improvement and provide feedback to the representatives and the company as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Navigate through the pages of the form, each page with a specific set of questions.
2.  For each question, carefully read the label and the hint to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3.  Select the best answer to the question, using the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Overall Service Responsiveness

*   **Overall Service Responsiveness** (`customer_service_responsiveness_rating`, number, required: false)
    *   How would you rate your overall experience with the customer service representative? Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### Customer Service Attitude

*   **Customer Service Attitude** (`customer_service_attitude`, select\_multiple, required: false)
    *   How would you describe the customer service representative's attitude? Choose all that apply:
    *   Very friendly and welcoming
    *   Somewhat friendly and welcoming
    *   Not very friendly or welcoming
    *   Very unfriendly and unwelcoming

### Service Representative Knowledge

*   **Service Representative Knowledge** (`customer_service_knowledge`, number, required: false)
    *   How would you rate the service representative's knowledge about the service or product? Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### Time Taken to Resolve Issue

*   **Time Taken to Resolve Issue** (`resolution_time`, time, required: false)
    *   How long did it take for the representative to resolve the issue? Choose the answer from the provided options.

### Was the Issue Resolved?

*   **Was the Issue Resolved?** (`resolution_success`, select\_one, required: false)
    *   Was the issue resolved? Choose either True or False.

### Other Solution

*   **Other Solution** (`resolution_success_had_other_solution`, text, required: false)
    *   If the issue was resolved, what was the other solution? Describe it in a few words.

### Customer Service Responsiveness Rating 2

*   **Customer Service Responsiveness Rating 2** (`customer_service_responsiveness_rating_2`, number, required: false)
    *   Rate your overall experience with the customer service representative for the second interaction. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### Customer Service Attitude 2

*   **Customer Service Attitude 2** (`customer_service_attitude_2`, text, required: false)
    *   Describe the customer service representative's attitude for the second interaction.

### Customer Service Knowledge 2

*   **Customer Service Knowledge 2** (`customer_service_knowledge_2`, number, required: false)
    *   Rate the customer service representative's knowledge about the service or product for the second interaction. Rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### Was Representative Trying to Help?

*   **Did you feel like the representative was trying to help you?** (`customer_service_knowledge_3`, select\_one, required: false)
    *   Did you feel like the representative was trying to help you? Choose either True or False.

### Customer Service Knowledge 4

*   **Were you able to get the help you needed?** (`customer_service_knowledge_4`, select\_one, required: false)
    *   Were you able to get the help you needed? Choose either True or False.

## Tips

*   Fill out the form carefully, as it will help the company improve its customer service quality.
*   Answer the questions honestly, based on your real experience.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the customer service team.
