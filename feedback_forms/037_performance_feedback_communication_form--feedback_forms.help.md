# Performance Feedback Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Feedback Communication Form is a tool for users to provide input on their performance. It's meant to gather relevant and timely feedback that will help shape future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete each field as required and relevant, using your best judgment for optional fields.
3. Select relevant answers from the options provided.
4. Click the submit button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your thoughts and ideas in this text field. You can provide as much or as little information as you see fit.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Please enter your job title to help us understand your role and responsibilities better.
* **department** (`department`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the feedback is about your departmental performance, 'No' otherwise.
* **performance_feedback** (`performance_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant questions from the options provided to give us a complete understanding of your performance.
* **feedback_question_2** (`feedback_question_2`, text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback about this question. You can use this field to give us a more detailed explanation of your thoughts.
* **feedback_question_3** (`feedback_question_3`, time, optional): Enter the date and time when this feedback applies, if applicable. Please note that this field is primarily for historical context.
* **feedback_question_4** (`feedback_question_4`, number, optional): Enter a numerical value (e.g., '10', '5', etc.) to rate this feedback on a scale of 1-10.
* **feedback_question_5** (`feedback_question_5`, number, optional): Enter another numerical value (e.g., '10', '5', etc.) to rate this feedback on a scale of 1-10.
* **feedback_question_6** (`feedback_question_6`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you agree, 'No' if you disagree with the feedback statement.
* **feedback_question_7** (`feedback_question_7`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant questions from the options provided to give us a complete understanding of your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be concise and clear in your feedback.
* Ensure that your feedback is specific and relevant to the performance topic.
* If you have any attachments or additional information to support your feedback, please include them as attachments.
