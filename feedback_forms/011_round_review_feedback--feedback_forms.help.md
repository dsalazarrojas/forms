# Round Review Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Round Review Feedback form is a tool for participants to provide their honest feedback on their experience with an event or service. This feedback will help event organizers and staff to identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as a participant in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Provide the Event ID number to help us track and associate your feedback with the correct event.
3. Select your rating for the event from the "Rating Scale" dropdown menu.
4. Share your comments about the event in the "Comment" field. This is an opportunity to provide detailed feedback and suggestions.
5. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: This field is used to identify you as the participant who is providing feedback. Please enter your name as it appears on your badge or ID card.
* **Event Id**: This field is used to track your feedback to the correct event. Please enter the Event ID number you received when you registered for the event.
* **Rating Scale**: This field allows you to rate your experience with the event. Select "Excellent", "Good", "Poor", or "Not at all" to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Comment**: This field is an opportunity for you to provide detailed feedback and suggestions about the event. Share your thoughts, likes, and dislikes to help us improve.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input is valuable to us.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improvement, please include them in the "Comment" field.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please mention them in the "Comment" field as well.
