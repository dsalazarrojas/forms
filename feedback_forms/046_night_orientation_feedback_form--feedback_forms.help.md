# Night Orientation Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Night Orientation Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from individuals who have attended or participated in a night orientation event. This form helps us understand your experience, identify areas for improvement, and gather valuable insights to enhance future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the event you attended or participated in.
2. Enter your name.
3. Choose the type of event you attended or participated in (Night Orientation, Day Orientation, or Other).
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
5. If you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up, please enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Date (event_date)
* **Event Date** (Date, Required: false)
	+ Enter the date of the event you attended or participated in.

### Name (name)
* **Name** (Text, Required: false)
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be displayed.

### Event Type (event_type)
* **Event Type** (Select One, Required: false)
	+ Choose the type of event you attended or participated in:
		+ Night Orientation
		+ Day Orientation
		+ Other

### Feedback (feedback)
* **Feedback** (Text, Required: false)
	+ Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about your experience.

### Contact Phone (contact_phone)
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required: false)
	+ If you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up, enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any concerns or complaints, please let us know so we can address them accordingly.
* We appreciate your time and input in helping us improve our events!
