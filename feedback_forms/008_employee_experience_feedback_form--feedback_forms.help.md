# Employee Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Experience Feedback Form is a tool for regular check-ins between managers and their employees. It allows both parties to share their thoughts, opinions, and suggestions on how to improve their working relationship.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a feedback frequency (e.g., New Hire, Monthly, Quarterly, Semi-Annually, Annually).
2. Choose the channels you have used to receive feedback from your manager (e.g., Email, Phone, Chat, In-person).
3. Select the type of feedback you have received from your manager (e.g., Positive, Negative, Neutral).
4. Provide your manager's rating of their performance (on a scale of 1-10).
5. Give your own rating of your manager's performance (on a scale of 1-10).
6. Provide feedback and suggestions for improvements (text area).
7. Your manager's comments on your feedback (text area).
8. Your comments on your manager's feedback (text area).
9. Your manager's recommendations for improvements (text area).
10. Your recommendations for improvements (text area).
11. Your manager's conclusion on your feedback (text area).
11. Your conclusion on your manager's feedback (text area).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Experience**: This field is for you to describe your overall experience working with your manager.
* **Manager Name**: Please enter your manager's name.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions on how to improve your working relationship with your manager.
* **Date of Experience**: Enter the date you started working with your manager.
* **Feedback Frequency**: Select how often you receive feedback from your manager.
* **Feedback Channel**: Choose the methods you use to receive feedback from your manager.
* **Feedback Type**: Select the type of feedback you typically receive from your manager.
* **Manager Rating**: Rate your manager's performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Employee Rating**: Rate your manager's performance based on your experience (on a scale of 1-10).
* **Feedback Suggestions**: Provide suggestions on how to improve your working relationship.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter your manager's comments on your feedback.
* **Employee Comments**: Enter your comments on your manager's feedback.
* **Manager Recommendations**: Enter your manager's recommendations for improvements.
* **Employee Recommendations**: Enter your own recommendations for improvements.
* **Manager Conclusion**: Enter your manager's conclusion on your feedback.
* **Employee Conclusion**: Enter your conclusion on your manager's feedback.

## Tips

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback and suggestions.
* Keep in mind that this form is for regular check-ins, so be sure to review and update your feedback regularly.
* If you have any pressing concerns, please report them to your manager or HR department directly.
* Remember that this form is a two-way conversation, so be sure to read and respond to your manager's feedback as well.
