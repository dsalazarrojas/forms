# Availability Survey For Observances - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on your availability for specific observances or events. Please answer the questions to help us schedule and coordinate your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your full name as it appears on your schedule.
2. Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
3. Select the name of the event or holiday for which you are available to attend.
4. Choose the days you are available by selecting multiple dates from the calendar.
5. Choose your preferred time slot for the event (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
6. If you are willing to travel a certain distance, enter the number of miles. (Optional)
7. If you require any accommodations for accessibility needs, please specify.
8. Provide any additional comments or information about your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your schedule.
- **Respondent Email** (`respondent_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
- **Observance Name** (`observance_name`, Text, Required): Choose the event or holiday you are interested in.
- **Available Dates** (`availability_dates`, Select Multiple, Required): Select multiple dates you are available to attend.
- **Preferred Times** (`preferred_times`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred time slot for the event.
- **Maximum Travel Distance** (`maximum_distance`, Number, Optional): If you are willing to travel, enter the maximum distance you are willing to travel to attend the event. (Note: This is a numeric value, but you can enter 0 if you do not have a specific limit)
- **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, Text, Optional): If you require any accommodations for accessibility, please describe your needs.
- **Additional Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your availability.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have already responded to this survey for the same event before completing this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
- We appreciate your participation and look forward to reviewing your response.
