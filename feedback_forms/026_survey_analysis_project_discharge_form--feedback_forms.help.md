# Survey Analysis Project Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Analysis Project Discharge Form is a tool used to gather feedback and evaluation information from users about a specific project. This form helps to understand user experiences, identify areas of improvement, and provide valuable insights for future project developments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Project Discharge Form section with the project's details.
2. Provide your feedback in the Feedback section.
3. Evaluate the project in the Evaluation section.
4. Select the Category that best represents your experience with the project.
5. Rate your experience with the project using the Rating field.
6. Enter the Date and Time of when you interacted with the project.
7. Enter your Email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
8. Enter your Phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Discharge Form**: This is a text field where you can provide details about the project you are discharging feedback for. Please enter a brief description of the project.
* **Feedback**: This is where you can provide any comments, suggestions, or thoughts about the project. Be as specific as possible and provide concrete examples.
* **Evaluation**: In this field, rate the project based on your experience (e.g., 1-5 stars).
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your experience with the project. This will help us understand the type of feedback you are providing.
* **Rating**: Use the stars to rate your experience with the project. The more stars, the better.
* **Date**: Enter the date when you interacted with the project. This will help us understand the timeline of your experience.
* **Time**: Enter the time when you interacted with the project. This will help us understand the specific timing of your experience.
* **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, provide your email address. We will keep your email private and only use it for project-related purposes.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number. We will only use your phone number for project-related purposes and with your explicit consent.
