# Fitness Park Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fitness Park Feedback Survey Form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience at the Fitness Park. This feedback will help us to identify areas of improvement and make the park a better place for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to you.
3. Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fitness Park Feedback** (`fitness_park_feedback`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a text-based question where you can share your thoughts and opinions about your experience at the Fitness Park. Please provide a brief answer to the question in a few sentences.
- **Equipment** (`fitness_park_equipment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the equipment that you used during your visit to the Fitness Park. This will help us understand what facilities are being used and how we can improve them.
  - True: This equipment was available and usable during my visit.
  - False: This equipment was not available or not in good working condition during my visit.
- **Cleanliness** (`fitness_park_cleanliness`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select all the areas of the park that you found to be clean or not clean. This will help us understand where we can improve our cleaning standards.
  - True: This area was clean and well-maintained.
  - False: This area was not clean or not well-maintained.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`fitness_park_overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/true): Please select how satisfied you were with your overall experience at the Fitness Park.
  - Satisfied: I am satisfied with my experience.
  - Dissatisfied: I am not satisfied with my experience.
- **Ratings** (`fitness_park_ratings`, `text`, required/optional): This field is an optional comments section where you can provide any additional feedback or ratings about your experience at the Fitness Park. Please use this space to provide any further comments or suggestions.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the "Ratings" field to provide them.
