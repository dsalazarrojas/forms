# Public Policy Initiative Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Public Policy Initiative Feedback Questionnaire" is a tool designed to gather feedback from users about the public policy initiative. It has 25 sections, each focusing on different aspects of the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to understand what feedback is being requested.
2. Answer all questions in each section to provide comprehensive feedback.
3. Use the provided options to select your ratings.
4. Enter your email address and name for contact purposes.
5. Submit your feedback once you have completed all sections.
