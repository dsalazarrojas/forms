# get_in_touch_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "get_in_touch_form" is a contact form designed to help users get in touch with the organization. Fill out this form to express your interest in contacting us for any reason, and we will get back to you soon.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your response to "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you're interested in getting in touch with the organization.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field, which is a required field. This will ensure that we can address you correctly.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field, which is also a required field. This will be used as the primary contact method.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field, which is not required but recommended for further communication.
5. Type your message in the "message" field, which is a required field. Please provide a clear and concise message to enable us to understand your query.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **get_in_touch_form (Yes/No)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you're interested in getting in touch with the organization, and "No" otherwise.
* **name (prophets_name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name here. This is how we'll address you.
* **email (email_address)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address here. This is how we'll contact you primarily.
* **phone (phone_number)** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number here, if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **message (message_body)** (`text`, required): Type your message here. Be clear and concise so we can understand your query.
* **send (submit)** (`note`, optional): Click this button to submit your form.
