# Workplace Wellness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Wellness Feedback Form is a tool for employees to provide feedback on the effectiveness and overall impact of the workplace wellness program. This form helps the administration understand your thoughts, feelings, and suggestions, which in turn helps us improve the program for your benefit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to reach out to the administration for clarification.
4. Please ensure you're not duplicating feedback as each submission is unique.
5. Submit your completed form once you've reviewed and verified the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Feedback (General Feedback, text, optional)
This is where you can share any general thoughts or suggestions about the workplace wellness program.

### Program Satisfaction (Program Satisfaction, select_multiple, required)
Choose one or more of the options that best describe your level of satisfaction with the program:
* Satisfied
* Dissatisfied

### Suggestions (Suggestions, note, optional)
This is where you can provide additional suggestions or ideas for improving the program.

### Employee Demographics (Employee Demographics, text, optional)
Please provide any information about your department, job title, or other relevant details about yourself.

### Contact Information (Contact Information, select_one, required)
Select one of the following options to provide us with contact information:
* Email
* Phone
* Other (please specify)

### Program Effectiveness (Program Effectiveness, number, optional)
If you answered the previous questions, we'd love to know your overall assessment of the program's effectiveness on a scale of 0-10.

### Overall Wellness (Overall Wellness, time, optional)
If you could rate your overall wellness level on a scale of 0-24 hours, please do so.

### Department (Department, select_one, required)
Select your department from the list:
* Human Resources
* Operations
* IT

### Frequency (Frequency, select_one, required)
Select the frequency of your participation in the program:
* Daily
* Weekly
* Monthly

### Comments (Comments, note, optional)
This is the final opportunity to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
