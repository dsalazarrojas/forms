# website-management-feedback-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "website-management-feedback-form" is designed to gather feedback from users on various aspects of a website, including overall performance, usability, and feature preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply from the options for "Website Management Feedback" and "Feature Categories" fields.
2. Enter your feedback on the "Overall Performance", "Usability", "Feature Performance", "User Experience", "Overall Usability", "Feature Enhancements", and "Improvement Areas" fields.
3. Ensure that you provide feedback on all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Website Management Feedback Form
* **Website Management Feedback Form (<type>: select_multiple)**: Select all that apply from the options to provide feedback on the website management.
* **Overall Performance (<type>: number)**: Provide feedback on the overall performance of the website.
* **User Features Preferences (<type>: text)**: Enter your feedback on the user features preferences.
* **Feature Categories (<type>: select_one)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have feedback on feature categories.
* **Usability (<type>: text)**: Enter your feedback on the usability of the website.
* **Feature Performance (<type>: text)**: Enter your feedback on the feature performance.
* **User Experience (<type>: text)**: Enter your feedback on the user experience.
* **Overall Usability (<type>: text)**: Enter your feedback on the overall usability of the website.
* **Feature Enhancements (<type>: text)**: Enter your feedback on feature enhancements.
* **Improvement Areas (<type>: text)**: Enter your feedback on improvement areas.

## Tips
* Be specific and concrete in your feedback to help us improve the website.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Improvement Areas" field.
* If you have feedback on multiple areas, please ensure that you provide feedback on all required fields.
