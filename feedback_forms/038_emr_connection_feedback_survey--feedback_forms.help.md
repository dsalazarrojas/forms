# EMR Connection Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EMR Connection Feedback Survey is designed to collect your opinions and suggestions about the electronic medical records (EMR) connection system. Your feedback will help us understand your experiences and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, simply answer the questions to the best of your ability. You can skip any questions that don't apply to you. If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to add them in the "System Usability Comments" field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **EMR Connection**: A free-text field for you to describe your experience with the EMR connection.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the EMR connection system, on a scale from 1-5.
* **System Usability**: Rate the usability of the EMR connection system, on a scale from 1-5.
* **What Works**: Select all features or aspects of the EMR connection system that you found to be working well.
* **What Doesn't Work**: Select all features or aspects of the EMR connection system that you found to be not working well.
* **System Usability Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience with the EMR connection system.
* **User Experience**: The date you last used the EMR connection system.
* **System Usability Improvement**: Select the improvement you would suggest for the EMR connection system.
* **User Experience Improvement**: Select the improvement you would suggest for the user experience of the EMR connection system.
* **User Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in the feedback.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can complete the survey at your own pace. Your feedback is valued, and we appreciate your time!
