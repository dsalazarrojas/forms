# Service Reliability Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Reliability Customer Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback on the reliability of our services from customers. This form helps us understand how our services meet or fall short of their expectations, guiding us to make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the options from the "Service Reliability Feedback" dropdown menu to indicate how often you experience service disruptions.
2.  In the "Service Disruption" field, provide a brief description of any disruptions you've experienced with our service.
3.  For "Satisfaction", choose how satisfied you are with our service's reliability from the provided options.
4.  In the "Service Reliability Rating" field, rate our service's reliability on a scale from 1 to 10.
5.  In the "Feedback" field, provide any additional thoughts, suggestions, or comments about our service.
6.  Enter your email if you'd like to be contacted back about your feedback (optional).
7.  Select who from the "Support Team" you would like to be contacted about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Reliability Feedback**: Select one of the options to indicate how often you experience service disruptions.
  - Option 1: Frequently
  - Option 2: Occasionally
  - Option 3: Rarely
  - Option 4: Never
- **Service Disruption**: This field is optional and requires a brief description of any service disruptions experienced.
- **Satisfaction**: This indicates your satisfaction level with our service's reliability. Options include Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither Agree Nor Disagree, Somewhat Disagree, and Strongly Disagree.
- **Service Reliability Rating**: Rate our service's reliability on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Feedback**: This optional field is for any additional thoughts, suggestions, or comments about our service.
- **Email**: Enter your email if you'd like to be contacted back about your feedback.
- **Support Team**: Select who from the support team you'd like to be contacted about your feedback. Options include Account Manager, Technical Support, Billing Support, and General Support.
