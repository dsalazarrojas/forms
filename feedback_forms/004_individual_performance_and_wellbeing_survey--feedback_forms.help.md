# individual_performance_and_wellbeing_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on individual employees' performance, well-being, and job satisfaction. The feedback will help identify areas of strength and weakness for the employee and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Provide accurate and honest feedback for each question.
3. Save your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
- **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, optional): Rate your performance on a scale of 1-5.
- **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, text, optional): Describe how you feel about your job.
- **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, optional): Share about your current level of motivation at work.
- **Stress Levels** (`stress_levels`, text, optional): Rate your stress levels at work.
- **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Describe your experience with team communication.
- **Team Work** (`team_work`, text, optional): Share about your experience working with your team.
- **Work Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, text, optional): Describe your balance between work and personal life.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, text, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your job.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
- Take your time to provide thorough answers.
- If unsure about a question, try to explain best-effort.
