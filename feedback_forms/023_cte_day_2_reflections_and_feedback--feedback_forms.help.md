# CTE Day 2 Reflections And Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants on the second day of a professional development session. Your input will help us improve and refine the content and logistics for future events. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions honestly, and we'll use your feedback to make a positive impact on education and training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introductory note, which will give you a brief overview of the day's events.
2. If you'd like, provide your name, but this is optional.
3. Answer the reflection questions, providing your most significant takeaways from the day.
4. Choose which session you found most valuable.
5. Describe how you plan to apply the learning from the day to your classroom or office.
6. If you have any remaining questions from the day, please feel free to ask.
7. Provide feedback on the event's logistics, including the venue and catering.
8. Rate the pacing of the day.
9. Give an overall rating for the day.
10. Finally, make any suggestions for Day 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- CTE Day 2 Feedback and Reflection --** (`feedback_intro`, `note`, optional): This is an introductory note that sets the context for the form. It's not required to answer, but it will give you a better understanding of what we're looking for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your name for feedback purposes, please enter it here. This is optional.
* **School or Division** (`school_division`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your school or division.
* **-- Daily Reflections --** (`reflection_header`, `note`, optional): This is a header for the reflection section. Again, it's not required to answer.
* **What was your most significant takeaway from today's sessions** (`key_takeaway`, `text`, required): Please take a moment to think about what you found most valuable from the day's sessions.
* **Which session did you find most valuable** (`session_most_valuable`, `select_one`, required): Choose the session that resonated with you the most.
* **How will you apply today's learning to your classroom or office** (`application_to_practice`, `text`, required): Describe how you plan to use the learning from the day in your teaching or work.
* **Do you have any remaining questions from Day 2** (`unanswered_questions`, `text`, optional): If you have any questions, feel free to ask.
* **-- Event Logistics Feedback --** (`logistics_header`, `note`, optional): This is a header for the feedback on event logistics.
* **Rate the venue and facilities** (`venue_rating`, `number`, required): Please rate the venue and facilities on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Were you satisfied with the catering options** (`catering_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how satisfied you were with the catering.
* **How was the pacing of the day** (`pacing_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the pacing of the day.
* **Overall rating for Day 2** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): Give an overall rating for the day.
* **Suggestions or requests for Day 3** (`suggestions_for_day_3`, `text`, optional): Make any suggestions or requests for Day 3.
* **Are you interested in a follow-up coaching session** (`follow_up_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you're interested in a follow-up coaching session.
