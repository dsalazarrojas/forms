# Contact Page Orange And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback, complaints, and suggestions from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Contact Us Today" button to open the form in a new window.
2.  Fill out the required fields with your information:
	*  Full Name: Enter your full name.
	*  Email Address: Enter your email address.
	*  Type of Inquiry: Select the type of issue or concern you have.
	*  Department: Select the department you would like to contact.
	*  Subject: Enter a brief subject for your issue.
	*  Overall Rating: Rate your experience.
	*  Contact Preference: Select your preferred method of contact.
3.  Optionally, fill out any other fields that are not marked as required:
	*  Phone Number: Enter your phone number.
	*  Additional Comments: Provide any additional information about your issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Full Name (text)**: Enter your full name.
*  **Email Address (email)**: Enter your email address.
*  **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number.
*  **Type of Inquiry (select one)**: Select the type of issue or concern you have:
	+ Question
	+ Comment
	+ Suggestion
	+ Complaint
	+ Compliment
	+ Other
*  **Department (select one)**: Select the department you would like to contact:
	+ Sales
	+ Support
	+ Billing
	+ Marketing
	+ General
*  **Subject (text)**: Enter a brief subject for your issue.
*  **Details (text)**: Provide full details about your issue.
*  **Overall Rating (select one)**: Rate your experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Below Average
	+ Poor
*  **Contact Preference (select one)**: Select your preferred method of contact:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ No Response Needed
*  **Additional Comments (text)**: Provide any additional information about your issue.
