# Internal Process Friction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the internal processes used within the organization, with the goal of identifying areas that can be improved to reduce friction points and make it easier for users to accomplish their tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions on each page according to your experience with the internal processes.
2. Use the options provided for each question as a guide for your answer.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please use the "Do you have any suggestions for improvement" text field.
4. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional comments" note field.
5. Finally, provide your contact information so that the organization can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Feedback (page_one)

* **Feedback (text)**: Use this field to provide a general comment or observation about your experience with the internal processes.

### Pain points (page_two)

* **Pain points (text)**: List specific difficulties or challenges you have encountered while using the internal processes.

### Frequency (page_three)

* **Frequency (select_multiple)**: Select one or multiple frequencies to indicate how often you experience the pain points.
	+ High: Frequently
	+ Medium: Occasionally
	+ Low: Rarely

### Impact (page_four)

* **Impact (select_one)**: Select the impact of the pain points on your work.
	+ High: Significant
	+ Medium: Some
	+ Low: No impact

### Time wasted (page_five)

* **Time wasted (number)**: Estimate the average time you spend on tasks due to the pain points.

### Frequency_of_Experience (page_six)

* **Frequency_of_Experience (text)**: Describe the frequency of your experience with the pain points.

### How_do_you_feel_about_this_experience (page_seven)

* **How_do_you_feel_about_this_experience (select_one)**: Select your emotional response to your experience with the internal processes.
	+ Positive: You felt good about your experience
	+ Negative: You felt bad about your experience
	+ Neutral: You felt neither good nor bad about your experience

### Do_you_have_any_suggestions_for_improvement (page_eight)

* **Do_you_have_any_suggestions_for_improvement (text)**: Provide any suggestions or ideas for improving the internal processes.

### Additional_comments (page_nine)

* **Additional_comments (note)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the internal processes.

### Contact (page_ten)
