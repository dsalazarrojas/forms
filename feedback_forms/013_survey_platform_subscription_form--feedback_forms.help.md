# Survey Platform Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Survey Platform Subscription Form to help you understand its purpose and usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "email" field.
2. Enter your company name in the "company_name" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Type your feedback in the "feedback_message" field.
5. Choose your subscription frequency in the "subscription_frequency" field.
6. Select your subscription length in the "subscription_length" field.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the "survey_questions" field.
8. Select your subscription status from the options provided in the "subscription_status" field.
9. Add any additional notes in the "survey_platform_subscription_notes" field.
10. Choose a date for your subscription to start in the "subscription_start_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email (email, text, required)**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Company Name (company_name, text, required)**: Enter your company's name.
* **Phone (phone, text, required)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Feedback Message (feedback_message, note, required)**: Share your thoughts on your experience with the Survey Platform.
* **Subscription Frequency (subscription_frequency, text, required)**: Choose how often you would like to subscribe to the Survey Platform.
* **Subscription Length (subscription_length, number, required)**: Choose how long you would like to subscribe to the Survey Platform.
* **Survey Questions (survey_questions, select_one, required)**: Answer "Yes" if you are interested in taking surveys, or "No" if not.
* **Subscription Status (subscription_status, select_one, required)**: Select your current subscription status from the options provided.
* **Survey Platform Subscription Notes (survey_platform_subscription_notes, text, required)**: Add any additional notes about your subscription.
* **Subscription Start Date (subscription_start_date, date, required)**: Choose a date to start your subscription.
