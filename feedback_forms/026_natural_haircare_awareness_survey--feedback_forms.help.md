# Natural Haircare Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your haircare habits, preferences, and awareness of different haircare products and practices. The goal is to help us understand your attitudes and behaviors related to haircare, which will inform our efforts to improve haircare products and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your habits and preferences.
2. Choose the options that best represent your haircare routine and habits.
3. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
4. Enter your thoughts and reasons in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, required): This is a brief description of who you are and why you're taking this survey.
* **Haircare Routine** (`haircare_routine`, select_one, required): Please choose how often you follow a haircare routine. You can select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ natural: Natural haircare practices (e.g., using natural ingredients, avoiding harsh chemicals)
	+ chemical: Chemical-based haircare products (e.g., shampoos, conditioners)
* **Haircare Product Usage** (`haircare_product_usage`, select_multiple, required): Please select all haircare products you use:
	+ Option 1: Shampoo
	+ Option 2: Conditioner
* **Haircare Importance** (`haircare_importance`, number, required): Rate the importance of your haircare routine:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
* **Preferred Haircare Product** (`haircare_preferred_product`, select_one, required): What type of haircare product do you prefer?
	+ Option 1: Shampoo
	+ Option 2: Conditioner
	+ Option 3: Serum
* **Haircare Frequency** (`haircare_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): How often do you perform haircare activities?
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ Option 2: Weekly
* **Haircare Habit** (`haircare_habit`, select_one, optional): When do you perform haircare activities?
	+ Option 1: Morning
	+ Option 2: Evening
	+ Option 3: Nighttime
* **Haircare Frequency Time** (`haircare_frequency_time`, date, optional): Please enter the date and time you perform haircare activities.
* **Haircare Frequency Duration** (`haircare_frequency_duration`, number, optional): How long do you perform haircare activities?
* **Haircare Product Quality** (`haircare_product_quality`, select_one, optional): How would you rate the quality of haircare products?
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
* **Haircare Product Cost** (`haircare_product_cost`, number, optional): How much do you spend on haircare products?
* **Haircare Product Importance** (`haircare_product_importance`, select_multiple, optional): How important is the price of haircare products to you?
	+ Option 1: High
	+ Option 2: Moderate
	+ Option 3: Low
* **Haircare Product Satisfaction** (`haircare_product_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your haircare products?
	+ Option 1: Satisfied
	+ Option 2: Neutral
	+ Option 3: Dissatisfied
* **Awareness** (`haircare_awareness`, select_multiple, optional): What do you know about natural and chemical-based haircare products?
	+ Option 1: Natural
	+ Option 2: Chemical-based
* **Awareness Source** (`haircare_awareness_source`, select_multiple, optional): Where do you get your information about haircare products?
	+ Option 1: Social media
	+ Option 2: Advertising
	+ Option 3: Friends
* **Awareness Reason** (`haircare_awareness_reason`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about why you have awareness about haircare products.
* **Awareness Note** (`haircare_awareness_note`, note, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to share about your haircare awareness?
