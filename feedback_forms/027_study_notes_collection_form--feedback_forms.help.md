# Study Notes Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Notes Collection Form is used to gather information about studies from students or users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Study Notes** (`study_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the study.
* **Study Materials** (`study_materials`, text, optional): Enter the study materials used.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool assigned for the study from the options: `chatjimmy` or `Other`.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the study.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the study.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the study.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the category for the submission from the options: `Feedback Forms`, `Study Materials`, or `Other`.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the study.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the study.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, time, optional): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who the submission is for from the options: `User`, `Admin`, or `Both`.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the study.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of attachment from the options: `Image`, `Audio`, or `Video`.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): Enter the attachment file name.
* **Study Level** (`study_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the study level from the options: `Beginner`, `Intermediate`, or `Advanced`.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, optional): Enter the subject of the study.
* **Class** (`class`, text, optional): Enter the class name.
* **Instructor** (`instructor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Course** (`course`, text, optional): Enter the course name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the study.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, text, optional): Enter the names of the attendees.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the submission status from the options: `Pending`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, or `Archived`.
* **Submitted By User** (`submitted_by_user`, select_one, optional): Choose who submitted the study from the options: `User`, `Admin`, or `Both`.
* **Submission Type** (`submission_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the submission type from the options: `Individual`, `Group`, or `Both`.

## Tips
* Be sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the correct format for date and time fields.
* Choose the correct submission status and submission type.
* Attach any required attachments.
* Review your submission before submitting.
