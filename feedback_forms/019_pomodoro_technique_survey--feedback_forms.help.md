# pomodoro_technique_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the effectiveness of the Pomodoro technique in your work and daily life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully, providing accurate information about your experiences with the Pomodoro technique.
2. Answer each question truthfully, and select the options that best fit your situation.
3. If a question does not apply to you, please select "No" to indicate that you do not use the Pomodoro technique for that particular aspect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, optional): This is a general introduction to the form. Please take a moment to read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Technique Effectiveness** (`technique_effectiveness`, `number`, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the Pomodoro technique in helping you stay focused during work. Use the scale provided to select your answer.
* **Time Estimate** (`time_estimate`, `time`, optional): Estimate the amount of time you typically work in focused sessions using the Pomodoro technique.
* **Focus Level** (`focus_level`, `number`, optional): Rate your level of focus during work sessions using the Pomodoro technique. Use the scale provided to select your answer.
* **Sessions** (`sessions`, `select_one`, optional): Do you use the Pomodoro technique in your work sessions?
	+ Yes: You apply the Pomodoro technique in your work sessions.
	+ No: You do not use the Pomodoro technique in your work sessions.
* **Sessions Frequency** (`sessions_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): When using the Pomodoro technique, do you have:
	+ Yes: Regularly scheduled work sessions.
	+ No: No regular work sessions.
* **Sessions Duration** (`sessions_duration`, `select_multiple`, optional): When using the Pomodoro technique, do you have:
	+ Yes: Work sessions of varying lengths.
	+ No: Fixed length work sessions.
* **Work Time** (`work_time`, `select_multiple`, optional): When using the Pomodoro technique, do you take breaks to:
	+ Yes: Work on tasks other than the current one.
	+ No: Focus on a single task.
* **Break Time** (`break_time`, `select_multiple`, optional): When using the Pomodoro technique, do you take breaks:
	+ Yes: Take regular breaks.
	+ No: Do not take breaks.
* **Sessions Number** (`sessions_number`, `select_one`, optional): Do you aim for a specific number of sessions in your work period?
	+ Yes: You have a target number of sessions.
	+ No: You do not have a target number of sessions.

## Tips
