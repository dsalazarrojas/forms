# Product Review Posting Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers regarding their experience with a specific product. It will help in identifying potential issues, gathering valuable insights, and ensuring that any concerns raised by customers are addressed in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name as the reviewer.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Rate your experience with the product.
4. Write a review about your experience with the product.
5. Decide if you want to publish your review on the website.
6. Confirm that you understand the website terms of service and give your consent to publish your review.
7. Confirm that you have read and understand the website terms of service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reviewer Name** (Reviewer Name, text, required): Please enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): You can enter your email address if you choose to provide it.
* **Rating** (Rating, select_one, required): Choose a rating based on your experience with the product. Options are "Good" and "Fair".
* **Review** (Review, text, optional): Write a brief review about your experience with the product.
* **Publish Review** (Publish, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you want to publish your review on the website.
* **I understand that I am giving the permission to publish the review on the website.** (Terms and Conditions, text, required): This is a confirmation that you understand that by publishing your review, you are giving permission for it to be published on the website.
* **I agree to the terms of service of the website.** (Consent to Publish, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" to confirm that you agree to the terms of service.
* **I give my consent to publish the review on the website.** (Review Agreement, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" to confirm that you give your consent to publish your review.
* **I confirm that I have read and understand the website terms of service.** (Agreement 2, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understand the website terms of service.
