# Gratitude and Recognition Voting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gratitude and Recognition Voting form is designed to collect feedback and recognition votes from team members on nominations submitted by individuals.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for a legitimate purpose, such as recognizing a team member's hard work or acknowledging a milestone achieved.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Nomination Name (<name>, text, required)
Enter the name of the person being nominated.

### Nominator Email (<name>, email, required)
Enter the email address of the person who submitted the nomination.

### Nominator Phone (<name>, text, optional)
Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the nomination (if available).

### Nominator's Comment (<name>, note, optional)
Provide a brief comment or reason for the nomination, explaining why the person deserves recognition.

### Nomination Title (<name>, select_one, required)
Select the title of the nomination, either "Nomination" or "Other".

### Nomination Text (<name>, select_multiple, required)
Select the specific details of the nomination that you would like to recognize. Please choose one or more from the provided options.

### Vote (<name>, select_multiple, required)
Select the reason(s) for voting for the nominee. Please choose one or more from the provided options.

### Vote Count (<name>, number, required)
Enter the number of votes you cast for this nomination.

### Comments (<name>, time, optional)
Provide additional comments or thoughts about the nomination (if you have the time).

### Nomination Date (<name>, date, optional)
Enter the date of the nomination (if applicable).

### Nomination Time (<name>, time, optional)
Enter the time of the nomination (if applicable).

### Nomination Time Note (<name>, note, optional)
Provide any additional information about the nomination time (if necessary).

### Vote Count Note (<name>, note, optional)
Provide any additional information about the vote count (if necessary).

### Vote Note (<name>, note, optional)
Provide any additional information about the vote (if necessary).
Please be as thorough and detailed as possible when filling out this form, as it will help the team and organization make informed decisions about recognition and gratitude.
Note: This form is subject to change as per organizational needs. If you experience any issues while filling out this form, please reach out to the form administrators for assistance.
