# Senior Activity Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Activity Attendance Form is used to track the attendance and participation details of seniors in a specific program or activity. It helps staff and administrators gather essential information to monitor the attendance and engagement of seniors in various programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the Program Name, which is the title of the specific program or activity being reported.
2. Enter the Date of the activity in the Date field. Select the correct date using the date picker or enter it manually.
3. Enter the Attendees Count, which is the number of participants who attended the activity.
4. In the Participant Names field, list the names of the participants who attended, one per line. You can select from a list of options provided.
5. In the Participant Comments field, provide any comments or feedback about the participants' engagement or behavior during the activity.
6. If the program had any staff members assisting or supervising, select their names from the list in the Staff field.
7. If you are submitting the form on behalf of someone else, select 'Yes' or 'No' in the Submitted By field, indicating if you are submitting the form on behalf of the program staff or not.
8. Finally, confirm the time you submitted the form in the Time Submitted field, using the 24-hour clock format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name (Program Name)** (`program_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the specific program or activity being reported.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the activity in the format of month/day/year or use the date picker to select the date.
* **Attendees Count (attendees_count**, number, required/optional): Enter the total number of participants who attended the activity.
* **Participant Names (participant_names**, select_multiple, options: List participant names, one per line): List the names of participants who attended the activity. Select from the provided list of options.
* **Participant Comments (participant_comments, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the participants' engagement or behavior during the activity.
* **Staff Comments (staff_comments, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from staff members about the activity.
* **Email (email, email, required/optional): Enter the contact email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone Number (phone_number, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Program Length (program_length, number, required/optional): Enter the length of the program in hours.
* **Program Type (program_type, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating if the program type is relevant.
* **Staff (staff, select_one, required): Select the name of the staff member assisting or supervising the program.
* **Submitted By (submitted_by, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' or 'No' indicating if you are submitting the form on behalf of the program staff or not.
* **Date Submitted (date_submitted, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you submitted the form.
* **Time Submitted (submitted_time, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you submitted the form in 24-hour clock format.
