# Pool Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Pool Visit Feedback Form" is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with our pool visits. It helps us understand your opinions, concerns, and suggestions, which we use to improve our services and provide a better experience for our visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information page with your details.
2. On the Safety page, select the correct rating for the safety aspect of our pool visit.
3. On the Service Quality, Staff Interaction, Facilities, and Overall Experience pages, select the rating that best describes your experience.
4. In the Submission page, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your visit.
5. Finally, fill in your Name and provide your Email and Phone number to contact you back.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information** (text, optional): Enter your general information, including any details you think are relevant to your visit.
### Safety
* **Safety** (select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you felt safe during your visit. Select "No" if you felt unsafe.
### Service Quality
* **Service Quality** (select one, optional): Rate the service quality of our staff, from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### Staff Interaction
* **Staff Interaction** (select one, optional): Rate the interaction with our staff, from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### Facilities
* **Facilities** (select one, optional): Rate the facilities, from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (select one, optional): Rate your overall experience, from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
### Submission
* **Submission** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your visit.
### Name
* **Name** (text, optional): Enter your name for us to contact you back.
### Email
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
### Phone
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you back.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve.
* You can submit your feedback anonymously if you prefer.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please don't hesitate to share them.
