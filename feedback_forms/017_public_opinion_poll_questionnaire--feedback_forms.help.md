# Public Opinion Poll Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions and feedback from the community on various aspects of our community. It is an anonymous and voluntary form that will help us understand the community's perceptions and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer honestly. You can select one or more options from the provided choices or provide a text response if required. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think is the most pressing issue in our community?** (`public_opinion_1`, text, required: false): This question is open-ended, and you can provide your thoughts and opinions on any issue you think is affecting the community.
* **How do you rate our customer service?** (`customer_service`, select_one, required: false): Please select the option that best describes your experience with our customer service.
	+ Excellent: Our customer service team has exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: Our customer service team has met your expectations.
	+ Fair: Our customer service team has been average.
	+ Poor: Our customer service team has not met your expectations.
* **What are your thoughts on community engagement?** (`community_engagement`, text, required: false): This question is open-ended, and you can provide your thoughts and opinions on community engagement in our community.
* **How often do you visit our social media pages?** (`social_media`, select_one, required: false): Please select the frequency of your visits to our social media pages.
	+ Daily: You visit our social media pages every day.
	+ Weekly: You visit our social media pages a few times a week.
	+ Monthly: You visit our social media pages about once a month.
	+ Rarely: You do not visit our social media pages.
* **How often do you shop at local businesses?** (`local_businesses`, select_one, required: false): Please select the frequency of your shopping trips to local businesses.
	+ Frequently: You shop at local businesses often.
	+ Occasionally: You shop at local businesses occasionally.
	+ Rarely: You do not shop at local businesses.
	+ Never: You have never shopped at local businesses.
* **How do you feel about our community's overall quality of life?** (`quality_of_life`, text, required: false): This question is open-ended, and you can provide your thoughts and opinions on our community's quality of life.
* **What do you think we should improve in terms of public transportation?** (`public_transportation`, text, required: false): This question is open-ended, and you can provide your suggestions and opinions on public transportation in our community.
* **Do you have any suggestions for new events or activities?** (`events_activities`, text, required: false): This question is open-ended, and you can provide your ideas and suggestions for new events or activities in our community.
* **What do you think is the most important issue in our community?** (`public_opinion_2`, text, required: false): This question is open-ended, and you can provide your thoughts and opinions on the most important issue in our community.
* **Customer Service 2** (`customer_service_2`, select_one, required: false): Please select the option that best describes your experience with our customer service.
	+ Excellent: Our customer service team has exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: Our customer service team has met your expectations.
	+ Fair: Our customer service team has been average.
	+ Poor: Our customer service team has not met your expectations.
* **How do you rate our community facilities?** (`community_facilities`, select_one, required: false): Please select the option that best describes your experience with our community facilities.
	+ Excellent: Our community facilities have exceeded your expectations.
	+ Good: Our community facilities have met your expectations.
	+ Fair: Our community facilities have been average.
	+ Poor: Our community facilities have not met your expectations.
* **Shopping Frequency** (`shopping_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Please select the frequency of your shopping trips.
	+ Frequently: You shop often.
	+ Occasionally: You shop occasionally.
	+ Rarely: You do not shop.
	+ Never: You have never shopped.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This question is not applicable as it is not meant to collect contact information. Please do not fill this out.
