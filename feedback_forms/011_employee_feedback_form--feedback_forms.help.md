# Employee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Feedback Form is a tool to collect feedback from employees regarding their work experience and overall satisfaction with their job.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal contact information:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
2. Select your department:
	* Choose the department you belong to
3. Rate your job title:
	* Type or select your job title
4. Provide feedback about your experience:
	* Feedback Score: Rate your experience as "Yes" or "No"
	* Comments: Share your thoughts and suggestions
5. Add any additional comments or notes:
	* Comments: Type in any other comments or suggestions

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Choose the department you belong to, such as "Yes" or "No" for "Yes" or "No" options or other department names for select_one type field.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Type or select your job title, such as "Software Engineer" or "Manager"
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate your experience as "Yes" or "No" for multiple options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts and suggestions about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `number`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your feedback.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID for tracking purposes.
* **Manager Id** (`manager_id`, `number`, required): Enter your manager's ID for tracking purposes.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your experience from 1 to 5.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5.
* **Comments Count** (`comments_count`, `number`, required): Count the number of comments you provided.
* **Rating Count** (`rating_count`, `number`, required): Count the number of ratings you provided.
* **Feedback Count** (`feedback_count`, `number`, required): Count the number of feedbacks you provided.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, required): The date and time you created this feedback.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, required): The last update date and time of this feedback.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, `date`, required): The date and time this feedback was deleted.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `select_one`, required): Choose who created this feedback, such as "Yes" or "No" for "Yes" or "No" options or other creator names for select_one type field.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `select_one`, required): Choose who updated this feedback, such as "Yes" or "No" for "Yes" or "No" options or other updater names for select_one type field.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, `select_one`, required): Choose who deleted this feedback, such as "Yes" or "No" for "Yes" or "No" options or other deleter names for select_one type field.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the category of this feedback, such as "Yes" or "No" for "Yes" or "No" options or other category names for select_one type field.
* **Form Status** (`form_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of this form, such as "Active" or "Inactive" for "Active" or "Inactive" options or other status names for select_one type field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Use the options provided for select_one fields to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your supervisor or HR department.
* The form is meant to be filled out by employees, not managers or supervisors.
* This form is a one-time feedback collection, not an ongoing performance evaluation.
