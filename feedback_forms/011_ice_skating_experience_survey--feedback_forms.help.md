# Ice Skating Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information about your ice skating experiences. We want to understand your thoughts and opinions about our ice skating services, products, or experiences. Your feedback will help us improve our offerings and provide better services to you and our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
4. If you need help or have questions, contact us for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ice Skater Info** (`skater_info`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your name or the name of the person who is filling out the form.
* **Ice Skater** (`ice_skate`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the ice skate you are using or have experience with.
* **Skating Experience** (`skating_experience`, number, required: false): Rate your experience level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Ice Skate Type** (`ice_skate_type`, text, required: false): Describe the type of ice skate you are using or have experience with (e.g., figure skates, hockey skates, etc.).
* **Ice Skate Length** (`ice_skate_length`, number, required: false): Measure the length of your ice skate in inches.
* **Skate Size** (`skate_size`, select_one, required: false): Choose the size of your ice skate from Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Skating Frequency** (`skating_frequency`, time, required: false): Rate how often you go ice skating, using a time unit (e.g., minutes, hours, etc.).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your ice skating experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false): Enter your email address or phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter your name or title for reference.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, required: false): Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Submitted From** (`submitted_from`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you submitted this form, from Browser, Email, or Phone.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose who you submitted this form to, from Ice Skater, Coach, or Other.
* **Submitted With** (`submitted_with`, select_one, required: false): Choose the browser or app you used to submit this form, from Chrome, Firefox, or Safari.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your submission.
* **Surveyor** (`surveyor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who completed this survey.
* **Survey Completed** (`survey_completed`, text, required: false): Enter the date and time you completed this survey.
* **Submitted By Surveyor** (`submitted_by_surveyor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who submitted the survey.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of your submission, from Submitted, Pending, or Rejected.
* **Submission Status Notes** (`submission_status_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes about the status of your submission.
* **Submission Notes** (`submission_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes about your submission.
