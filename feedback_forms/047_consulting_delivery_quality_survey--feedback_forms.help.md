# Consulting Delivery Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey aims to gather feedback from clients on the quality of consulting services delivered. Your input is valuable in helping our consultancy improve and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are filling out this form in the context of a consulting engagement.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
3. Choose the rating options for each question that best describe your experience with the consultancy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name** (Client name, text, required: false):
	+ This field is optional and should only be filled out if you wish to provide a name for reference purposes.
* **Project name** (Project name, text, required: true):
	+ Please fill out the name of the consulting engagement or project.
* **Quality of work** (Quality of work, select_one, required: true):
	+ Rate the quality of work delivered by the consultancy as:
		- Excellent: The work met all expectations and was of high quality.
		- Good: The work was satisfactory and met most expectations.
		- Fair: The work was average and met some expectations.
		- Poor: The work was of low quality and did not meet expectations.
* **Timeliness of delivery** (Timeliness of delivery, select_one, required: true):
	+ Rate the timeliness of delivery:
		- On time: The consultancy delivered the work on schedule.
		- Minor delays: The consultancy experienced minor delays.
		- Significant delays: The consultancy experienced significant delays.
* **Clarity of recommendations** (Clarity of recommendations, select_one, required: true):
	+ Rate the clarity of recommendations:
		- Clear and actionable: The recommendations were well-presented and actionable.
		- Somewhat clear: The recommendations were somewhat understandable, but could be clearer.
		- Not clear: The recommendations were not clear or understandable.
* **Likelihood to recommend** (Likelihood to recommend, select_one, required: true):
	+ Rate your likelihood to recommend the consultancy:
		- Very likely: You would highly recommend the consultancy.
		- Likely: You would recommend the consultancy.
		- Unlikely: You would not recommend the consultancy.
		- Very unlikely: You would not recommend the consultancy at all.
* **Additional comments** (Additional comments, text, required: false):
	+ This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional feedback or suggestions about the consultancy's services.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact form@gic.mx.
