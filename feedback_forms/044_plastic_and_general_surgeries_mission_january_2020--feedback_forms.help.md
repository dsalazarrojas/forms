# plastic_and_general_surgeries_mission_january_2020 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Plastic and General Surgeries Mission January 2020 form is designed to collect feedback from volunteers and assess their experience with the mission. This form aims to gather information about your experience with our mission, including your overall rating, leadership, communication, logistics support, safety and protection, medical supplies, medical facilities, cultural immersion, and team cohesion.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your feedback about your experience with our mission in the "Volunteer Experience" field.
2. Rate your overall experience with our mission by selecting a number from the "Overall Mission Rating" dropdown menu.
3. Provide details about your experience with the "Mission Leadership" field.
4. Indicate the level of communication, logistics support, and medical supplies you experienced during your mission.
5. Describe the quality of medical facilities you had access to.
6. Rate your experience with cultural immersion during your mission.
7. Choose the level of team cohesion you experienced during your team's activities.
8. Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Experience With Our Mission** (Required): Describe your personal experience with the mission. This field is a text input where you can type your thoughts, feelings, and observations about your time with us.
* **Overall Mission Rating (1-5)**: Indicate your rating of the overall mission experience. A rating of 1-5 will help us know how well we met your expectations.
* **Mission Leadership** (Required): Share any feedback about the leadership you encountered during the mission. This field is a text input where you can type your thoughts and observations about the leaders who guided you.
* **Communication** (Required): Indicate how clear or unclear the communication was during the mission. This field is a multi-select option where you can choose from "clear", "unclear", "good", or "great".
* **Logistics Support** (Required): Describe the level of support you received for logistics and operations during the mission. This field is a text input where you can type your thoughts and observations about the logistics support.
* **Safety and Protection** (Optional): Rate the quality of safety and protection you experienced during the mission. This field is a single-select option where you can choose from "clear", "unclear", "good", or "great".
* **Medical Supplies** (Required): Indicate the quality of medical supplies you had access to during the mission. This field is a multi-select option where you can choose from "clear", "unclear", "adequate", or "excellent".
* **Medical Facilities** (Required): Describe the quality of medical facilities you had access to during the mission. This field is a text input where you can type your thoughts and observations about the medical facilities.
* **Cultural Immersion** (Optional): Rate the level of cultural immersion you experienced during the mission. This field is a single-select option where you can choose from "clear", "unclear", "good", or "great".
* **Team Cohesion** (Required): Indicate the level of team cohesion you experienced during the mission. This field is a multi-select option where you can choose from "clear", "unclear", "good", or "great".

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields.
* Be as detailed and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
