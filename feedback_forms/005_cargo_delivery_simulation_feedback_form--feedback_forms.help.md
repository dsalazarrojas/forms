# Cargo Delivery Simulation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from participants about their experience with the cargo delivery simulation. It helps us understand how realistic and challenging the simulation was, and what skills and knowledge were gained or improved during the process. This feedback will be used to continuously improve and refine the simulation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Please provide your feedback by selecting the corresponding fields and typing or clicking accordingly.
2.  For each field, please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
3.  Be as detailed as possible in the text fields, especially in the "Additional Comments or Feedback" section.
4.  Select all the skills you practiced during the simulation by checking the corresponding boxes in the "Skills Practiced" section.
5.  Rate your overall experience, realism, and difficulty level using the provided scales.
6.  If you experienced any technical issues during the simulation, describe them in detail in the "Describe Any Technical Issues" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is for your convenience, but we do not require your name to provide feedback. Feel free to leave it blank if you prefer.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, optional): If you want to receive simulation results or have any follow-up questions, please provide your email address.
*   **Simulation Date** (`simulation_date`, date, required): Please select the date you completed the simulation.
*   **Type of Simulation Completed** (`simulation_type`, select_one, required): Select the scenario you experienced during the simulation.
*   **Overall Simulation Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with the simulation.
*   **How Realistic Was the Simulation** (`realism_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how realistic the simulation was compared to real-world scenarios.
*   **Learning Objectives Assessment** (`learning_objectives`, note, optional): This field is for additional feedback on learning objectives.
*   **Were the Learning Objectives Clear** (`objectives_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the learning objectives were.
*   **Were the Learning Objectives Achieved** (`objectives_met`, select_one, required): Rate how well the learning objectives were achieved.
*   **Simulation Components Rating** (`simulation_components`, note, optional): This field is for additional feedback on simulation components.
*   **Scenario Design Quality** (`scenario_design`, select_one, required): Rate how well the scenario was structured.
*   **Clarity of Instructions** (`instructions_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the instructions were.
*   **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty_level`, select_one, required): Rate how challenging the simulation was.
*   **Time Allocation** (`time_allocation`, select_one, required): Rate how much time was allocated for the simulation.
*   **Skills Practiced** (`skills_practiced`, select_multiple, required): Select all the skills you practiced during the simulation.
*   **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, select_one, required): Rate how confident you feel applying what you learned.
*   **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, select_one, required): Rate the type of technical issues you experienced.
*   **Describe Any Technical Issues** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Provide details about any technical issues you experienced.
*   **Quality of Instructor or Facilitator Support** (`instructor_support`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of support provided by the instructor or facilitator.
*   **Would You Recommend This Simulation to Colleagues** (`recommend_simulation`, select_one, required): Rate whether you would recommend the simulation to your colleagues.
*   **Improvements Suggested** (`improvements_suggested`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements for the simulation.
*   **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other thoughts or feedback about the simulation.

**Tips**

*   Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
*   Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation.
