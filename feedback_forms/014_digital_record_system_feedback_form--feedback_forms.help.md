# Digital Record System Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the Digital Record System. Your input will help us improve the system and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
3. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Can you provide some feedback about your experience with the Digital Record System?** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts, positive or negative, about your experience with the Digital Record System.
* **How would you rate your experience with the Digital Record System?** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the following options to rate your experience:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Bad
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Have you used the Digital Record System in the past?** (`select`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the boxes that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
* **What is your preferred date of use for the Digital Record System?** (`date`, `date`, required): Choose a date using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Do you have any additional comments or notes about your experience with the Digital Record System?** (`note`, `note`, required): Share any additional thoughts or comments you have.
* **Please submit your feedback.** (`submit`, `text`, required): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit your feedback.
* **What is your preferred time of use for the Digital Record System?** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose a time in the standard 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
