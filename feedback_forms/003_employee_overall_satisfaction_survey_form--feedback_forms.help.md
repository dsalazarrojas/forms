# Employee Overall Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your overall satisfaction with the company, including how well you understand the company's mission and whether you would recommend it to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. If you have any comments or feedback, use the "comments" field to provide additional information.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Overall Happiness (Page 1)
* **Overall Happiness** (Number)
	+ This question asks you to rate your overall happiness with your work experience at the company.
	+ Please provide a numerical value between 1 and 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### Mission Understanding (Page 2)
* **Mission Understanding** (Text)
	+ This question asks you to briefly describe how well you understand the company's mission.
	+ Please provide a short paragraph or sentence explaining your understanding of the company's goals and objectives.

### Recommend Company (Page 3)
* **Recommend Company** (Select One)
	+ This question asks whether you would recommend the company to your friends and family, colleagues, or acquaintances.
	+ Please choose one of the options:
		- None
		- Would you recommend our company to your friends and family?
		- Would you recommend our company to your colleagues?
		- Would you recommend our company to your acquaintances?

### Comments (Page 4)
* **Comments** (Note)
	+ This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your work experience at the company.
	+ Feel free to share as much or as little as you like, but please make sure to keep it professional and respectful.

### Contact Information (Page 5)
* **Email** (Email)
	+ This is an optional field where you can provide your email address.
	+ Please enter your company-provided email address.
* **Phone** (Text)
	+ This is an optional field where you can provide your phone number.
	+ Please enter your company-provided phone number.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any concerns or need clarification on any question, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and provide valuable feedback!
