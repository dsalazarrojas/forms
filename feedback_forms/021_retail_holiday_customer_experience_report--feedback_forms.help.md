# Retail Holiday Customer Experience Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Holiday Customer Experience Report is a form designed to gather data on customer experiences during holiday seasons. This information helps the retail team to understand customer behavior, preferences, and satisfaction levels during this time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the holiday location where you visited.
2. Choose the timing of your visit.
3. Indicate your customer type (Male, Female, or Other).
4. Select the customer gender (Male, Female, or Other).
5. Enter your age.
6. Select the purchase amount.
7. Choose your customer satisfaction level.
8. Select all the stores you visited.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Leave any additional customer comments.
12. Leave a comment from the retail manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Holiday Location**: Enter the location of your visit.
* **Visit Timing**: Select when you visited the store (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Customer Type**: Indicate your type (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Customer Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Customer Age**: Enter your age.
* **Purchase Amount**: Enter the amount you spent.
* **Customer Satisfaction**: Choose your satisfaction level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Stores Visited**: Select all the stores you visited (Store 1, Store 2, Store 3).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Comment**: Leave any additional comments.
* **Retail Manager Comment**: Leave a comment from the retail manager.
* **Visited Date**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who helped you.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store.
* **Customer Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Customer Address**: Enter your address.
