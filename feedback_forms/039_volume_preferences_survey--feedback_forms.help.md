# Volume Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to understand your customers' volume preferences, allowing us to provide better services and tailor our offerings to your needs. This form will gather information about your customer's volume, volume preferences, and email preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page of the form attentively and answer all required fields marked as "required".
2. For non-required fields, you can choose to fill them in or skip them if you don't have information to provide.
3. Use the "Submit" button to send the form and review your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your customer preferences**: This field is for you to describe your customer's general preferences, such as volume, frequency, and any other characteristics that might affect our services. Please provide a brief text that accurately reflects your customer's preferences.
 
* **Volume**: This field is for you to specify your customer's current volume levels. Please select a number that best represents your customer's volume.
 
* **Volume Preferences**: Please select all that apply from the volume preferences options. This will help us understand your customer's volume preferences.
 
* **Time**: This field is not currently applicable, as there are no options provided.
 
* **Frequency**: This field is for you to specify how often your customer interacts with our service. Please select one of the frequency options provided.
 
* **Submit**: This is a note field for additional comments or feedback. Please use it to provide any final comments or questions you may have.
 
* **Email**: This field is for you to provide your email address, which will be used to contact you if we need more information or to provide updates on your volume preferences.

**Tips**: If you have any other questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. We are here to help you and appreciate your feedback.
