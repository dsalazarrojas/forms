# Peer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect peer feedback on a team member's performance. Please complete the survey to provide a comprehensive and constructive evaluation of the team member's skills, work habits, and contributions. Your input is valuable in helping the team member grow and improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully before answering. Make sure you have a good understanding of the question before providing feedback.
2. Answer each question truthfully and constructively. Avoid giving false or misleading information.
3. Select the relevant options for each question. If you're unsure about an option, choose the one that best matches your feedback.
4. Complete all required fields. If a field is optional, feel free to provide as much or as little feedback as you're comfortable with.
5. Ensure you're providing feedback on the team member in question, not on the team member's personal qualities or character.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Survey (Team Survey)**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide a brief description of the team member's performance and strengths. Please be as specific as possible and focus on their technical skills, work habits, and contributions to the team.
* **Feedback Question 1 (Feedback question 1)**: Select the option that best describes your feedback on this specific area.
	+ Option 1: Team member consistently delivers high-quality work.
	+ Option 2: Team member sometimes delivers high-quality work, but could improve.
	+ Option 3: Team member rarely delivers high-quality work.
* **Feedback Question 2 (Feedback question 2)**: Select all the options that apply to your feedback on this specific area.
	+ Option 1: Team member consistently contributes to team discussions and meetings.
	+ Option 2: Team member sometimes contributes to team discussions and meetings.
	+ Option 3: Team member rarely contributes to team discussions and meetings.
* **Feedback Question 3 (Feedback question 3)**: Provide a specific example of a situation where the team member excelled or demonstrated a positive work habit. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Feedback Question 4 (Feedback question 4)**: Select the option that best describes your feedback on this specific area.
	+ Option 1: Team member consistently takes the initiative and demonstrates proactive behavior.
	+ Option 2: Team member sometimes takes the initiative and demonstrates proactive behavior.
	+ Option 3: Team member rarely takes the initiative and demonstrates proactive behavior.
* **Feedback Question 5 (Feedback question 5)**: Provide a specific example of a situation where the team member demonstrated a positive work habit or characteristic. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Date Completed**: This field is for recording the date when you completed the survey. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Performance Area (Performance area)**: Select all the options that apply to your feedback on the team member's performance areas. Please be specific as possible.

Note: The date completed field is used to track when the survey was completed for record-keeping purposes only. Do not include it in your feedback.
