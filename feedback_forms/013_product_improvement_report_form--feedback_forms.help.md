# Product Improvement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Improvement Report Form is a tool for users to provide feedback and suggestions on how to improve our products and services. Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time in helping us make our products better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with your thoughts and suggestions.
2.  Ensure that you have not recently submitted a report on the same issue or topic to prevent duplication of effort.
3.  Submit your report by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **product_improvement_report_form_4 (Select One)**: Select the category of your report. This field is required and will help us to prioritize and categorize your feedback.
	+ Select "Improvement" if you have a suggestion for how we can improve our product.
	+ Select "Issue" if you have encountered a problem or bug with our product.
* **product_improvement_report_form_15 (Text)**: Describe your report in detail. This field is for providing context and supporting information for your feedback.
* **product_improvement_report_form_20 (Text)**: Attach any relevant files or images that support your report.
* **product_improvement_report_form_16 (Text)**: Share additional information if you have any other comments or observations.
