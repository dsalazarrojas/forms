# Grievance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grievance Form is a tool for reporting and tracking grievances or complaints that users may have. It helps to identify issues, gather more information, and provide a resolution in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please ensure you have not already submitted a similar grievance.** If you have already submitted a grievance, please do not resubmit the same issue. This will help us to track and resolve grievances more efficiently.
2. To fill out the form, please navigate through each page using the buttons at the bottom of the page.
3. Enter your information in the corresponding fields, making sure to follow the format and guidelines provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grievance Form Page 1** (`grievance_form_page_1`, Text, Required: False): This page is the first part of the form where you will enter your grievance or complaint.
* **Grievance Description** (`grievance_description`, Text, Required: False): Describe your grievance or complaint in as much detail as possible. This will help us to understand the nature of your issue.
* **Grievance Type** (`grievance_type`, Select One, Required: False): Choose the category that best describes your grievance. This will help us to group similar issues and provide a more efficient resolution.
* **Grievance Detail** (`grievance_detail`, Text, Required: False): Provide more information about your grievance or complaint. Be as detailed as possible to facilitate effective resolution.
* **Resolution** (`grievance_resolution`, Text, Required: False): Describe how you would like your grievance to be resolved.
* **Resolution Date** (`grievance_resolution_date`, Date, Required: False): Enter the date when you would like the resolution to happen.
* **Resolution Time** (`grievance_resolution_time`, Time, Required: False): Enter the time when you would like the resolution to happen.
* **Grievance Attachment** (`grievance_attachment`, Text, Required: False): Attach any relevant documents or images that support your grievance.
* **Grievance Form Submitter** (`grievance_form_submitter`, Select One, Required: False): Choose how you want to submit your grievance (e.g., anonymously, with your name).
* **Grievance Form Submitter Name** (`grievance_form_submitter_name`, Text, Required: False): If you want to submit your grievance with your name, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure efficient resolution of your grievance.
* If you need help or guidance while filling out the form, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* If you have already submitted a similar grievance, do not resubmit. Contact your supervisor or HR representative to inquire about the status of your previous submission.
