# Sunshine Circles Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with Sunshine Circles, a program aimed at creating positive social connections among participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the text fields (participant_ratings, participant_insights, facilitator_comments, school_comments, program_leaders_comments, and participant_suggestions) with your honest and thorough responses.
2. For the date of the session you are providing feedback for, fill in the session_date field. If you are not sure about the exact date, you can check with your facilitator or the program leaders.
3. Choose the submit option to indicate whether you want to submit your feedback immediately or save it as a draft.
4. If you have an email address, enter it in the participant_email field. This will help us stay in touch with you for any follow-up questions.
5. Enter your phone number in the participant_phone field if you are comfortable sharing it. This will help us contact you for any further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_ratings**: Please rate your overall experience with Sunshine Circles, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **session_date**: Please enter the date of the Sunshine Circles session you are providing feedback on.
* **session_time**: Please enter the start and end time of the Sunshine Circles session, if you know it.
* **participant_insights**: Share your thoughts on what you enjoyed most about the session or what you found beneficial.
* **facilitator_comments**: Please provide any comments or feedback about the facilitator who led your session.
* **school_comments**: If you attended the session at your school or university, please provide any comments or feedback about the school's support or resources.
* **program_leaders_comments**: Please provide any comments or feedback about the program leaders who organized the session.
* **participant_suggestions**: Suggest any ideas or improvements you think can be made to enhance the program.
* **submit**: Choose whether you want to submit your feedback immediately or save it as a draft.
* **participant_email**: If you have an email address, enter it here so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number if you are comfortable sharing it for further discussion.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve the program and create better experiences for everyone.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your contribution to the program and look forward to implementing your ideas!
