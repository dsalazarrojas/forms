# skin_tone_finder_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "skin_tone_finder_quiz" form is a tool to help users discover their skin tone and recommended beauty products based on their individual skin characteristics. The form is designed to be simple and user-friendly, guiding users through a series of questions to determine their skin undertone, tone, and recommended product types.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start with the first page, "Skin Tone Finder Quiz", which is the first field in the form.
2. Select your skin tone from the "Select Skin Tone" dropdown menu.
3. Select your undertone from the "Select Undertone" dropdown menu.
4. Select the skin tone characteristics that best match your skin from the "Select Skin Tone" dropdown menu.
5. Select your beauty type from the "Select Beauty Type" dropdown menu.
6. Select the accurate recommendation(s) you receive from the "Select Accurate Recommendation" dropdown menu.
7. Select the product type(s) you're interested in from the "Select Product Type" dropdown menu.
8. Optionally, confirm if you've completed the quiz.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Skin Tone Finder Quiz** (page 1, text): This is the first page of the form. Enter your response to begin the quiz.
* **Select Skin Tone** (page 2, select_one): Select your skin tone:
	+ Light: Fair and pale skin with a light complexion.
	+ Medium: A mix of light and dark skin tones.
	+ Dark: Dark skin with a deep, rich complexion.
* **Select Undertone** (page 3, select_one): Choose your undertone:
	+ Cool: Pink or blue undertones.
	+ Warm: Yellow or golden undertones.
	+ Neutral: Balanced undertones.
* **Select Skin Tone** (page 4, select_multiple): Choose the skin tones that best match your skin:
	+ Fair: Light, pale skin with a slight undertone of pink or peach.
	+ Porcelain: Pale, delicate skin with a subtle complexion.
	+ Olive: Golden or olive-hued skin with a mix of warm and cool undertones.
	+ Golden: Warm, sun-kissed skin with a golden undertone.
	+ Light: Light skin with a mix of light and medium undertones.
	+ Medium: Balanced skin with a mix of light and dark undertones.
	+ Dark: Deep, rich skin with a dark undertone.
* **Select Beauty Type** (page 5, select_multiple): Choose your beauty type:
	+ Fair: Delicate, porcelain-like skin with a subtle complexion.
	+ Porcelain: Pale, smooth skin with a subtle undertone.
	+ Olive: Golden or olive-hued skin with a mix of warm and cool undertones.
	+ Golden: Warm, sun-kissed skin with a golden undertone.
* **Select Accurate Recommendation** (page 6, select_multiple): Select the accurate recommendations for your skin tone:
	+ Fair: Recommended for fair skin types.
	+ Porcelain: Recommended for porcelain skin types.
	+ Olive: Recommended for olive skin types.
	+ Golden: Recommended for golden skin types.
* **Select Product Type** (page 7, select_multiple): Choose the product type you're interested in:
	+ Fair: Recommended for fair skin types.
	+ Porcelain: Recommended for porcelain skin types.
	+ Olive: Recommended for olive skin types.
	+ Golden: Recommended for golden skin types.
* **Results Summary** (page 8, text): This field will display a summary of your results.
* **Select Quiz Completed** (page 9, select_multiple): Confirm if you've completed the quiz:
	+ Yes: I've finished the quiz.
	+ No: I'd like to continue or revisit previous pages.
