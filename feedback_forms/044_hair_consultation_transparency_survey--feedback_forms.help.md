# Hair Consultation Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with our hair consultation services. The information collected through this form will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for our clients.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each section and provide your feedback. For the fields that have multiple options, select all that apply. You can write as much or as little as you need in the "Additional comments" field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required): This field is for you to share any comments or thoughts you have about your experience with our hair consultation services.
* **Consultation Type** (`consultation_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of consultation you received. Options include: 
	+ On-site consultation
	+ Phone call consultation
	+ Video call consultation
* **Pricing Clarity** (`price_clarity`, select_multiple, required): For each option you selected in the previous field, indicate how clear our prices were:
	+ Clear and transparent
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Not clear
* **Expectations** (`expectations`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply:
	+ Price and services
	+ Service quality
	+ Consultation experience
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, not required): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): This field is for you to provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): This field is for you to provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

Note: You can skip any fields that do not apply to your experience.
