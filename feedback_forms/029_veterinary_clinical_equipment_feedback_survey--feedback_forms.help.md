# Veterinary Clinical Equipment Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather information about veterinary clinical equipment usage. Your feedback is essential in helping us improve the equipment and services provided to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

* Click on the 'Next' button to navigate through the form pages.
* Select or enter the required information for each field.
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of equipment you are providing feedback on (e.g., General Equipment, Diagnostics, Treatment).
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the locations where the equipment is used (e.g., Central Sterilization, Procedure Room, Clinic).
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false):
	+ Rate the equipment's performance (1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback about the equipment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your work email address.
* **Email Hint** (`email_hint`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any email hint or note.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your contact person's name.

## Tips

* Be specific and objective when providing feedback.
* Focus on your personal experience with the equipment.
* If you have any issues or problems with the equipment, describe them clearly.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and equipment.
