# ctl_u_leasing_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CTL-U Leasing survey is designed to gather feedback from users on the CTL-U Leasing tool. The information collected will help us understand users' satisfaction levels, reasons for choosing or not choosing the tool, and any other comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the **User Name** field.
2. Select your satisfaction rating from 1 to 5 in the **Satisfaction Rating** field.
3. Choose the main reason for your choice to use or not use the tool in the **Main Reason for Choosing** field.
4. Select the frequency of use for the tool in the **Frequency of Use** field.
5. Enter any comments you have about the tool in the **Comments** field.
6. Rate the service quality from 1 to 5 in the **Service Quality** field.
7. Select your level of agreement on the responsiveness of the support team in the **Support Responsiveness** field.
8. Choose the type(s) of tools you use for the tool in the **Tool Type** field.
9. Enter any feedback on the output of the tool in the **Output Feedback** field.
10. Select your age range in the **Age Range** field.
11. Rate the ease of navigation of the website in the **Website Ease of Navigation** field.
12. Select the quality of the output of the tool in the **Output Quality** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name (text)**: Please enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Satisfaction Rating (select_one)**: Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the tool. The options are:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Main Reason for Choosing (select_one)**: Choose the main reason for your choice to use or not use the tool. The options are:
	+ Price
	+ Quality
	+ Service
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Frequency of Use (select_one)**: Select the frequency of use for the tool. The options are:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Comments (text)**: Enter any comments you have about the tool.
* **Service Quality (select_multiple)**: Select the ratings from 1 to 5 for service quality. You can choose multiple ratings.
* **Support Responsiveness (select_one)**: Select the level of agreement on the responsiveness of the support team. The options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Other
* **Tool Type (select_multiple)**: Select all the types of tools you use for the tool. The options are:
	+ Drill
	+ Saw
	+ Welder
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Output Feedback (text)**: Enter any feedback on the output of the tool.
* **Age Range (select_one)**: Select your age range. The options are:
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55-64
	+ 65+
* **Website Ease of Navigation (select_multiple)**: Select the ratings from 1 to 5 for website ease of navigation. You can choose multiple ratings.
* **Output Quality (select_one)**: Select the quality of the output of the tool. The options are:
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please feel free to add them in the comments field.
