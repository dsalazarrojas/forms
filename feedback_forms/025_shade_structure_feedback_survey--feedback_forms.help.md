# Shade Structure Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shade Structure Feedback Survey is a tool designed to collect feedback from users on the shade structure's functionality, usability, and overall satisfaction. This feedback will help the development team identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully and answer all required fields.
2. For each field, provide detailed and concrete information to help the development team understand your feedback.
3. If a field is optional, you may skip it if you're not sure or don't have information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shade Structure Feedback** (`shade_structure_feedback_survey_page_1`, `text`, required): Please provide your overall feedback on the shade structure. Describe your experience, likes, and dislikes, and any suggestions for improvement.
* **Shade Canopy Details** (`shade_structure_feedback_survey_page_2`, `text`, required): Please describe the shade structure's canopy, including its condition, cleanliness, and any notable features.
* **Shade Structure Type** (`shade_structure_feedback_survey_page_2_1`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the type of shade structure you experienced (e.g., shade structure type). Note that this field is optional, so you can skip it if you're not sure or don't have information to provide.
* **Shade Structure Location** (`shade_structure_feedback_survey_page_2_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all locations where the shade structure was found or used (e.g., shade structure location on/off). Note that this field is optional, so you can skip it if you're not sure or don't have information to provide.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any images or videos that can help illustrate your feedback, please attach them to your response.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please provide them in the "Shade Structure Feedback" field.
