# Poverty-Reduction-Policy-Awareness-Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your opinions and perceptions on various policies and initiatives to reduce poverty. Your feedback will help policymakers and organizations develop more effective poverty-reduction strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Fill out the form in its entirety before submitting it.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required)
	+ This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **Demographic** (`Demographic`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Please select your demographic (male, female, or other) from the dropdown menu.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required)
	+ Please enter your age in whole numbers.
* **Income** (`Income`, `number`, required)
	+ Please enter your annual income.
* **Education** (`Education`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Please select all the education levels you have achieved (primary, secondary, high school, some college, degree, some university).
* **Employment** (`Employment`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Please select your employment status (employed, not employed, self-employed, student, retired, or other).
* **Household Income** (`Household Income`, `number`, required)
	+ Please enter the total household income.
* **Awareness** (`Awareness`, `select_multiple`, required)
	+ Please select how aware you are of the poverty-reduction policies (high, medium, low).
