# Product Innovation Judges Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from Product Innovation Judges about a product or idea. The feedback is crucial for product development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Name" field with your name.
2. Enter your "Email" address so that we can contact you for further clarification.
3. Provide a "Feedback Title" that summarizes your feedback.
4. Write a detailed "Feedback Description" of your thoughts and suggestions.
5. Select the "Score" you give to the product or idea.
6. Choose the relevant "Product Innovation Judges Feedback Category" for your feedback.
7. If necessary, provide additional feedback in the "Feedback Category 2" and "Feedback Category 3" fields.
8. Finally, click on "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note):
	+ This is a non-essential field that provides a brief introduction to your feedback.
* **Name** (Text):
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (Email):
	+ Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Feedback Title** (Text):
	+ Enter a short summary of your feedback.
* **Feedback Description** (Text):
	+ Provide a detailed description of your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Score** (Number):
	+ Select a score between 1-10 that represents your overall rating of the product or idea.
* **Product Innovation Judges Feedback Category** (Select Multiple):
	+ Choose one or more categories that best describe your feedback.
* **Date** (Date):
	+ Select the date when you submitted this feedback.
* **Product Innovation Judges Feedback Category 2** (Select One):
	+ Choose one category that best describes your feedback.
* **Product Innovation Judges Feedback Category 3** (Select One):
	+ Choose one category that best describes your feedback.
* **Submit** (Note):
	+ Click on this button to submit your feedback.
* **Submit 2**, **Submit 3**, **Submit 4** (Notes):
	+ These buttons are not intended for user input. They are for internal use only.

Note: The "Product Innovation Judges Feedback Category 2" and "Product Innovation Judges Feedback Category 3" fields are required fields, and you must select one option from each field to submit your feedback.
