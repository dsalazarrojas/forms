# Survey Participation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Participation Intake Form is a form designed to collect information from participants who are interested in or have already been assigned to participate in a survey. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure that you are included in the survey and to receive further instructions or clarification from the survey administrators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your name and any other required information.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address to receive further instructions or clarification about the survey.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it to enable us to contact you for any additional information.
4. Provide a valid date of birth to help us understand your demographics.
5. If you have a time of birth, enter it to help us further understand your demographics.
6. Review and submit your form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Participation** (`survey_participation`, `text`, required: false): This field is a text field where you can indicate your interest in participating in the survey or confirm your assignment to participate. Please enter a brief description of your participation in the survey.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you have access to, as we will use it to send further instructions or clarification about the survey.
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): This field is for your date of birth. Please enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **time_of_birth** (`time_of_birth`, `time`, required: false): This field is for your time of birth. Please enter your time of birth in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for your phone number. If you have a phone number, please enter it to enable us to contact you for any additional information.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you have access to, as we will use it to send further instructions or clarification about the survey.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all the information is correct.
* If you are not interested in participating in the survey, do not fill out this form.
