# Workshop Speaker Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from workshop participants about their experience with the speaker and the session they attended. Your input will help us evaluate and improve our workshop offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct ratings for each question, based on your actual experience with the session.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Comments" field to share them.
4. If you wish to provide feedback but do not want your email address to be associated with your response, you can leave the "Email" field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Rating** (1): Rate your overall experience with the session, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Speaker Name**: Please enter the name of the speaker who presented the session.
* **Session Quality**: Rate the quality of the session, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Session Content**: Provide any comments about the session content, such as what you enjoyed or would like to see changed.
* **Speaker Presentation**: Rate the speaker's presentation style, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Speaker Experience**: Rate the speaker's experience with the topic, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Session Organization**: Rate the organization of the session, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the session, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please share them here.
* **Email**: If you wish to be notified of any follow-up actions or future workshop updates, enter your email address here. If you'd rather not be contacted, leave this field blank.
