# Debrief Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from individuals or teams after an event or project has been completed. It aims to identify what went well, what could be improved, and overall effectiveness, providing valuable information for future events and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select all applicable options for "What Went Well," "What to Improve," and "Overall Effectiveness Rating" fields using checkboxes or single selection.
3. Provide open-ended feedback in the "Open-Ended Feedback" field, explaining any thoughts or suggestions you have about the event or project.
4. In the "Next Steps" field, outline any follow-up actions or suggestions you have for future events or projects.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Summary** (`event_summary`, text, optional): Describe the event or project in your own words, highlighting the key points or outcomes.
* **What Went Well** (`what_went_well`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways in which the event or project went well, such as:
	+ Success: The project was completed within the timeline and budget.
	+ Challenges: The team faced unexpected difficulties during the project.
	+ Opportunities: The event or project presented opportunities for growth and learning.
	+ Other (please specify): Provide any other positive aspects of the event or project.
* **What to Improve** (`what_to_improve`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas where the event or project could be improved, such as:
	+ Communication: Clear and timely communication was not always present.
	+ Timing: The project timeline was not well-planned or managed.
	+ Resources: Insufficient resources were available for the project.
	+ Other (please specify): Provide any other areas that need improvement.
* **Overall Effectiveness Rating** (`effectiveness_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall effectiveness of the event or project, choosing from:
	+ Very Effective
	+ Somewhat Effective
	+ Not Effective
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Open-Ended Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts, suggestions, or comments about the event or project.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline any follow-up actions or suggestions for future events or projects.

Note: Completing the survey will help us gather valuable information to improve future events and projects. Your feedback is highly appreciated and will remain confidential.
