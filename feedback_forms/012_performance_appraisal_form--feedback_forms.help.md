# Performance Appraisal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Appraisal Form is designed to evaluate employee performance and provide a comprehensive assessment of their skills, progress, and areas of improvement. This form helps to identify strengths and weaknesses, setting the foundation for future development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's name and job title in the respective fields.
2. Choose the performance period for which you're evaluating the employee's performance.
3. Select all areas of improvement for the employee.
4. Enter the evaluation date.
5. Optionally, provide a performance score, if applicable.
6. Finally, submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please fill in the employee's name as it appears on their identification or HR records.
* **Performance Period** (`performance_period`, select_one, required): Choose the quarter for which you're evaluating the employee's performance (First Quarter, Second Quarter, Third Quarter, Fourth Quarter).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the employee's job title as it's reflected in the company's organizational chart.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the employee's role, duties, and responsibilities.
* **Performance Score** (`performance_score`, number, optional): If applicable, enter a numerical score to reflect the employee's performance. Note that this field is not required but can be useful for tracking performance trends.
* **Areas of Improvement** (`areas_of_improvement`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas where the employee needs improvement, choosing from the options (First Quarter, Second Quarter, Third Quarter, Fourth Quarter).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation, when the performance was last assessed.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, optional): Enter your name and title as the person evaluating the employee's performance.

## Tips
* Ensure you're providing accurate and fair evaluations, based on concrete facts and observations.
* Use this form to help employees grow and develop their skills, rather than just critiquing their performance.
* Review and discuss the evaluation with the employee, addressing their areas of improvement.
* Keep the performance appraisal form up-to-date and regularly review it for consistency and fairness.
