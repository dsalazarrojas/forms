# Sports Facility User Satisfaction Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on their overall satisfaction with a sports facility. It aims to evaluate the user's experience with the facility, including their rating of the facility, its staff, and various aspects of their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
3. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Users Satisfaction** (user_satisfaction): This field is used to capture the user's overall satisfaction with the facility.
* **Rating** (rating): Rate your experience with a number from 1 to 5.
* **Rating Scale** (rating_scale): Select the rating scale that best matches your experience, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Feedback** (feedback): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the facility.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Contact Number** (phone): Enter your contact number in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Sports Facility** (sports_facility): Indicate whether or not you visited a sports facility.
* **Facility Staff** (facility_staff): Indicate whether or not you interacted with facility staff during your visit.
* **Date of Visit** (date_of_visit): Enter the date of your visit to the facility.
* **Time of Visit** (time_of_visit): Enter the time of your visit to the facility.
* **Rating for Facility Service** (rating_facility_service): Rate the facility's service as Good or Fair.
* **Rating for Crowd** (rating_crowd): Rate the crowd at the facility as Good or Fair.
* **Overall Experience** (overall_experience): Indicate whether or not you had an overall good experience at the facility.

Note: All questions are optional, unless marked as required.
