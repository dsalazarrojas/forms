# Offboarding Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Offboarding Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from departing employees to understand their reasons for leaving, areas where the company can improve, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant option for each question.
3. Enter any additional feedback or comments in the "Other Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Offboarding Questions (Page 1)
* **Why are you leaving** (Required): Select one of the following reasons for leaving:
	+ Option 1: Reason 1
	+ Option 2: Reason 2
	+ Option 3: Reason 3
* **What is your reason for leaving** (Required): Provide a brief description of your reason for leaving.
* **How can we improve** (Required): Select all the ways you think the company can improve:
	+ Option A: Improvement A
	+ Option B: Improvement B
	+ Option C: Improvement C

### Other Feedback (Page 2)
* **Do you have any other feedback** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
