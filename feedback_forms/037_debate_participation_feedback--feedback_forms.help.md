# debate_participation_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The debate participation feedback form is designed to gather information about your experience in the debate event. Your feedback will help us improve future events and make them more enjoyable for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide honest and thorough answers to all questions.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Debater Info** (`debater_info`, select_one, required): Please select your gender.
* **Debate Topic** (`debate_topic`, select_multiple, required): Choose all topics that relate to your experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Share your thoughts and opinions about the event.
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, number, optional): If applicable, please estimate the time you spent on the debate.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Provide the date of your participation in the debate.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share any other comments or feedback you have about the event.
