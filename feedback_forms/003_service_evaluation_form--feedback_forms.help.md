# Service Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from participants and facilitators about the service provided. The feedback is used to improve the service's quality, make informed decisions about future programming, and to enhance the user experience. Please make sure to submit your feedback in a honest and constructive manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please enter your name and company information in the corresponding fields.
2.  Provide the title of the course that you are evaluating.
3.  Enter your telephone number and email address to ensure we can contact you if needed.
4.  Enter the facilitator's name, as provided by the facilitator.
5.  Evaluate your knowledge of the subject matter presented in the course.
6.  Provide any suggestions you have for improving the program.
7.  State whether you would recommend this course to your colleagues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (`service_evaluation_form_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official ID or business card.
*   **Company** (`service_evaluation_form_company`, text, required): Please enter your company's name as it appears on your official ID or business card.
*   **Course Title** (`service_evaluation_form_course_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the course that you are evaluating.
*   **Telephone** (`service_evaluation_form_telephone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX or XXX-XXX-XXXX.
*   **Email** (`service_evaluation_form_email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format your_name@your_company.com.
*   **Facilitator Name** (`service_evaluation_form_facilitator_name`, text, required): Enter the facilitator's name as provided by the facilitator.
*   **Knowledge of Subject Matter** (`service_evaluation_form_knowledge`, text, required): Rate your knowledge of the subject matter presented in the course. Please answer on a scale from 0 to 100.
*   **What suggestions do you recommend for improving the program?** (`service_evaluation_form_suggestions`, text, required): Please provide any suggestions you have for improving the program.
*   **Would you recommend this course to your colleagues?** (`service_evaluation_form_recommend`, text, required): State whether you would recommend this course to your colleagues.
*   **Comments** (`service_evaluation_form_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns about the program, please enter them here.
*   **What fields need an improvement?** (`service_evaluation_form_improvement_needed`, text, required): Please specify any fields that you think need improvement in the program.
