# Moving Coordination Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moving Coordination Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from users regarding moving coordination activities. It's intended to help identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for better service delivery. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the pages and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Select the correct page numbers corresponding to each section of the feedback.
3. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Submit the completed form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Page 1** (`page_1`, text, required): Provide a brief description or title for your feedback. This field is a general introduction to your comments.
* **Page 2** (`page2`, text, required): Share your thoughts and insights regarding the moving coordination activity. This field is where you can elaborate on your feedback.
* **Page 3** (`page3`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them here. This field is not mandatory but is a good opportunity to provide further details.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure your comments are easily understood.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the form's administrator at form@gic.mx.
