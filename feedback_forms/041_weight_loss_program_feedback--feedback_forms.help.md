# Weight Loss Program Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants of the weight loss program. It aims to understand how you experienced the program, what you liked or disliked, and what we can do to improve it. Your input will help us refine the program to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best describe your experience, and type any additional comments you'd like to share. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by *) and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you hear about this program?** (`1`, `select_multiple`, false) - Please select how you heard about the weight loss program. This could be from a friend, social media, a referral, or any other source.
* **Weight loss program** (`2`, `text`, false) - Briefly describe the weight loss program you participated in, including the name or title of the program.
* **How long did you participate in the program?** (`3`, `select_one`, false) - Select the duration of your participation in the program. This will help us understand your overall experience with the program.
* **What type of program did you participate in?** (`4`, `select_multiple`, false) - Please select the type of program you participated in, such as Online, In-person, Hybrid, or Virtual.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`5`, `select_one`, false) - Rate your overall experience with the program, using the provided options (1/5, 2/5, 3/5, 4/5, 5/5).
* **Why did you rate your experience this way?** (`6`, `text`, false) - Provide a brief explanation for your rating, including any specific reasons why you felt this way.
* **What were your thoughts about your experience?** (`7`, `text`, false) - Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the program.
* **Were you satisfied with your experience?** (`8`, `select_one`, false) - Indicate whether you were satisfied or not satisfied with your experience.
* **Why did you feel this way?** (`9`, `select_multiple`, false) - Select any reasons why you felt this way about your experience. These might include Lack of time, Cost was too high, Program was too difficult, Too many commitments, or Other (please specify in the box below).
* **Other (please specify)** (`10`, `text`, false) - If you selected Other in the previous field, provide a brief explanation of why that was your experience.
* **What could we improve?** (`11`, `text`, false) - Share any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve the program.
* **Additional comments** (`12`, `text`, false) - Provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Email** (`13`, `email`, false) - Enter your email address to help us contact you if we need more information or clarification.
* **Phone** (`14`, `text`, false) - Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

Please note that while most of the fields are optional, filling out as much detail as possible will help us better understand your experience.
