## Customer Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback about your experience with our company, including your current location, satisfaction with our customer service, and whether you'd be willing to refer us to your family and friends. Please answer these questions to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields according to your experience. For each question, select one or multiple options that apply to you. If a question is not applicable to your experience, you can choose "No" or do not select any options for that field. You can access this form [insert link or location].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What state do you live in?
* **What state do you live in?** (`customer_state`, select_one): Please select the state where you currently reside.

### 2. Who provided you with your quote?
* **Who provided you with your quote?** (`customer_agent`, select_multiple): Check the boxes of any of the following options that apply to who provided you with your quote:
  * Yes
  * No

### 3. How satisfied are you with the customer service?
* **How satisfied are you with the customer service?** (`customer_service_satisfied`, select_one): Please select one of the following:
  * Yes
  * No

### 4. How likely are you to refer us to your family and friends?
* **How likely are you to refer us to your family and friends?** (`likely_to_refer`, select_multiple): Check the boxes of any of the following options that apply to your likelihood of referring us:
  * Yes
  * No

## Tips
* Please answer all questions that apply to your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the comments section (not shown in this form).
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
