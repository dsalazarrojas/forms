# moodboard_creation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the moodboard_creation_survey form, a tool designed to gather information about your desired moodboard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through the introduction to get an understanding of what the survey is asking.
2.  In the "What's the goal of your moodboard?" field, type in your desired outcome for the moodboard.
3.  Select the image you'd like to use for your moodboard from the provided options.
4.  If you'd like to use a custom image for your moodboard, provide the URL of the image in the "Image URL" field.
5.  Describe your moodboard in the "Describe your moodboard" field.
6.  Select a primary color for your moodboard from the provided options.
7.  If needed, choose additional colors for your moodboard from the list of options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What's the goal of your moodboard?** (Required): This field helps us understand the purpose of your moodboard.
    *   Please enter your desired outcome for the moodboard.
*   **Please choose an image to use as your moodboard** (Required): Select the image that best represents your moodboard.
    *   Choose from the options provided to select a pre-defined image.
*   **Image URL** (Optional): If you don't find an image in the options or need a custom image, provide the URL here.
    *   Enter the URL of the image you'd like to use.
*   **Describe your moodboard** (Required): Provide a description of your moodboard.
    *   This will help us get a better understanding of your desired outcome.
*   **Select a color for your moodboard** (Required): Choose a primary color for your moodboard.
    *   Select one of the provided colors.
*   **Select another color for your moodboard** (Optional): Choose additional colors for your moodboard.
    *   Select from the provided options.
*   **Select multiple colors for your moodboard** (Optional): Choose additional colors for your moodboard.
    *   Select from the provided options.

## Tips

*   Use the "Image URL" field only if you need a custom image.
*   Select the colors that best represent your moodboard's theme or purpose.
*   Be as specific as possible when describing your moodboard to help us better understand your desired outcome.
