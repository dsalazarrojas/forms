# Staff Golf Season Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect staff feedback on the golf season. It will help event organizers and HR department identify areas of improvement, suggestions, and overall staff satisfaction and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the Event Organizer who led the golf season from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose who you would like to give feedback to from the dropdown menu.
3. Complete the remaining fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Golf Season Satisfaction (id: 1)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the golf season. Please use the numeric scale to indicate your level of satisfaction, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Staff Satisfaction (id: 2)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the staff who led the golf season, if you have had any interaction with them. Please use the numeric scale to indicate your level of satisfaction, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Program Suggestions (id: 3)**: If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve the golf program, please provide them here. This is a free-text field, so you can type as much or as little as you like.
* **Staff Comments (id: 4)**: If you have any comments or feedback about your experience with the staff, please provide them here. This is a free-text field, so you can type as much or as little as you like.
* **Additional Comments (id: 5)**: If you have any general comments or feedback that do not fit in the other fields, please provide them here. This is a free-text field, so you can type as much or as little as you like.
* **Email (id: 6)**: Please provide your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (id: 7)**: Please provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Event Organizer (id: 8)**: Please select the name of the event organizer who led the golf season from the dropdown menu.
* **Feedback To (id: 9)**: Please select who you would like to give feedback to, from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or more options, such as HR Department or Event Organizers.
