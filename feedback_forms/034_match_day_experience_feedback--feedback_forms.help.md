# Match Day Experience Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience on Match Day. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the overall atmosphere, facilities, staff, and other aspects of the event, in order to identify areas for improvement and provide a better experience for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your feedback and comments. Each field is explained in more detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`match_day_experience_feedback_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief title or description of your feedback, if desired.
* **Match Day** (`match_day_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall experience on Match Day as Very Good, Good, or Poor.
	+ Select one or more of the following options: Very Good, Good, Poor.
* **Match Day Venue** (`match_day_venue`, select_one, optional): Choose the venue where you attended the Match Day event.
	+ Select one of the following options: Stadium 1, Stadium 2, Stadium 3.
* **Match Day Club** (`match_day_club`, select_multiple, optional): Select the teams you watched or participated in during Match Day.
	+ Select one or more of the following options: Team A, Team B.
* **Match Day Score** (`match_day_score`, number, optional): Enter the score of the event, if applicable.
* **Match Day Atmosphere** (`match_day_atmosphere`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall atmosphere of the event on Match Day.
	+ Select one of the following options: Very Good, Good, Poor.
* **Match Day Staff** (`match_day_staff`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the staff's behavior and performance on Match Day.
	+ Select one or more of the following options: Very Good, Good, Poor.
* **Match Day Food and Beverage** (`match_day_food_beverage`, select_multiple, optional): Rate the quality of the food and beverage provided on Match Day.
	+ Select one or more of the following options: Very Good, Good, Poor.
* **Match Day Safety** (`match_day_safety`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall safety of the event on Match Day.
	+ Select one of the following options: Very Good, Good, Poor.
* **Match Day Value For Money** (`match_day_value_for_money`, select_one, optional): Rate the value for money of the event on Match Day.
	+ Select one of the following options: Very Good, Good, Poor.
* **Match Day Recommendation** (`match_day_recommend`, text, optional): Enter a brief recommendation for future events.
* **Recommend To** (`match_day_recommend_to`, text, optional): Enter the name or contact information of who you would recommend this event to.
* **Match Day Improve Areas** (`match_day_improve_areas`, text, optional): Enter any areas that you think could be improved for future events.
* **Match Day Additional Comments** (`match_day_additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **Match Day Experience Rating** (`match_day_experience_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience on Match Day.
	+ Select one of the following options: Very Good, Good, Poor.
* **Match Day Form Submitted By** (`match_day_form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name or contact information, if desired.
