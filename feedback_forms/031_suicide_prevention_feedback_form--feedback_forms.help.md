# Suicide Prevention Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients regarding the services provided by our organization for suicide prevention. The form is intended to help us identify areas where we can improve our services and better support those who need it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Name, which is your name as a client or individual receiving services from our organization.
2. Provide your Contact Number, which is the number we can use to contact you for follow-up or additional information.
3. Answer the question "What can we improve?" in the feedback question field, sharing your thoughts on how we can better serve you.
4. Select the services you would like to see improved from the options provided in the "What services would you like to see?" field. You can select multiple services.
5. Provide any additional comments or thoughts in the "Comments" field.
6. If you are filling out this form on behalf of someone else, please fill in the "Name of Contact Person" field with the name of the person you are representing.
7. Finally, describe the role of the contact person in the "Role of Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Name (1)
Client Name is your personal name as a client or individual receiving services from our organization.

### Contact Number (2)
Contact Number is the number we can use to contact you for follow-up or additional information.

### Feedback Question (3)
What can we improve? Share your thoughts on how we can better serve you and improve our services.

### Services (4)
Select the services you would like to see improved from the options provided:

* Services
* Support Groups
* Education
* Hotlines

### Comments (5)
Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about our services.

### Name of Contact Person (6)
If you are filling out this form on behalf of someone else, please fill in the name of the contact person.

### Role of Contact (7)
Describe the role of the contact person, e.g., family member, friend, or healthcare provider.
