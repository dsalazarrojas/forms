# School Communication Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from parents on the school's communication strategy, helping the school to identify areas of improvement and ensure that parents are effectively informed and engaged.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Parent Name** field.
2. Type the name of the school in the **School Name** field.
3. Select your answer to **How effectively do you feel the school communicates with you**:
	* Choose **Communication is clear and timely** if you feel the school's communication is regular and understandable.
	* Choose **Communication is somewhat clear but not always timely** if you feel that the school's communication is mostly clear, but sometimes not up-to-date.
	* Choose **Not clear or timely** if you feel that the school's communication is not clear or not regular.
4. Select all the **communication channels** you use to interact with the school:
	* Choose **Emails** if you frequently receive emails from the school.
	* Choose **Text messages** if you receive text messages from the school.
	* Choose **Phone calls** if you frequently receive phone calls from the school.
	* Choose **In-person meetings** if you interact with school staff in person.
	* Choose **Other** if you use a different channel that is not listed above.
5. Select your answer to **How effectively do you feel our communication helps you stay up-to-date**:
	* Choose **Very effective** if you feel that the school's communication is very helpful in keeping you informed.
	* Choose **Somewhat effective** if you feel that the school's communication is somewhat helpful.
	* Choose **Not effective** if you feel that the school's communication is not helpful.
6. If you have any additional feedback, please type it in the **Do you have any additional feedback for us?** field.
7. If you have a preferred contact method, you can enter it in the **Email** and **Contact Phone** fields. However, please note that these fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Name** (text, optional): This field is used to identify the parent submitting the feedback.
- **School Name** (text, optional): This field is used to identify the school with which the parent is interacting.
- **How effectively do you feel the school communicates with you** (select one):
	+ This field asks for your feedback on the clarity and timeliness of the school's communication.
- **How often do you use our communication channels?** (select multiple):
	+ This field asks you to select all the communication channels you use to interact with the school.
- **How effectively do you feel our communication helps you stay up-to-date** (select one):
	+ This field asks for your feedback on the effectiveness of the school's communication in keeping you informed.
- **Do you have any additional feedback for us?** (text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
- **Email** (email, optional):
	+ This field is for you to provide your preferred email address for communication.
- **Contact Phone** (text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
- **Contact Email** (email, optional):
	+ This field is for you to provide your email address if you prefer to be contacted by email.

## Tips
- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback.
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- If you have any technical issues or concerns, please contact the school's support team.
