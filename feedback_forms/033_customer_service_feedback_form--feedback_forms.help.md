# customer_service_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer service feedback form is a tool for customers to provide feedback about their experience with our customer service. This form helps us understand areas of improvement and gather insights to better serve our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the Email field, which is required to ensure we can contact you.
2. Rate your experience with our customer service using the Rating Scale or select one of the numbers below it.
3. Provide a brief comment about your experience in the Comment field.
4. Select the Feedback Subject that best describes your feedback.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the Comments field.
6. Finally, click the Submit button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`, required): Your contact email address, which we will use to contact you about your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating_scale`, required): Rate your experience with our customer service using the Rating Scale or select one of the numbers below it.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, required): Enter your numerical rating score.
* **Feedback Subject** (`feedback_subject`, required): Select the subject that best describes your feedback.
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Phone** (`phone`, not required): Your phone number, which we will use to contact you about your feedback.
* **Customer Service Rating Score** (`customer_service_rating_score`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service rating score.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale_2`, not required): Select your rating scale from 1 to 5.
* **Customer Service Score** (`customer_service_score`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service score.
* **Feedback Subject 2** (`feedback_subject_2`, not required): Select the subject that best describes your feedback, this is a duplicate field.
* **Customer Service Score 2** (`customer_service_score_2`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service score.
* **Customer Service Score 3** (`customer_service_score_3`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service score.
* **Customer Service Score 4** (`customer_service_score_4`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service score.
* **Customer Service Score 5** (`customer_service_score_5`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service score.
* **Customer Service Score 6** (`customer_service_score_6`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service score.
* **Customer Service Score 7** (`customer_service_score_7`, not required): Enter your numerical customer service score.

Note: All fields are required for the form to be submitted, except Phone and Customer Service Score fields which are not required. 

Please note that some fields have similar labels and functionalities, but they are meant to be duplicates for different purposes.
