# Beta Product Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Beta Product Feedback Form is a tool used by product developers to collect valuable feedback from users about the beta version of our product. The form allows users to provide their thoughts, opinions, and experiences with the product, helping us improve its performance and overall user experience. Please ensure that you fill out the form only once per session, and do not submit multiple entries if you've already provided feedback for the same product.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include your country code if you're outside the local region. This field is not required but will help us get in touch with you faster.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, date, required): Select the relevant date related to your feedback.
* **Primary selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best applies to your experience.
* **Estimated value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Provide a value if it's applicable. Please use the decimal format.
* **Preferred time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required or relevant.
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information that will help us improve the product.
* **Please confirm agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select any of the options that apply to your experience.
* **Final comments or notes** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Add any closing remarks or comments you want to share.

Note: The fields marked as 'optional' can be left blank if not applicable to your experience.
