# Share Your Special Moments - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to share your special moments, thoughts, and experiences with others in the organization. This form allows you to provide feedback and share your feelings with family, friends, colleagues, or the public.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Share Your Special Moments" form.
2. Select the category of whom you want to share your special moment with: family, friends, colleagues, or public.
3. Choose how you want to share your special moment: through text, photo, video, or audio content.
4. Fill in the details of your special moment, including the date and time you want to share it.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your special moment.
6. Enter your email address and phone number (optional).
7. Click "Submit" to save your special moment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Share a Special Moment (Special Moment)**: A text field where you can describe your special moment in as much detail as you like.
* **With Whom (Share With)**: Select the category of whom you want to share your special moment with: family, friends, colleagues, or public.
* **How do you want to share it (Share Type)**: Choose how you want to share your special moment: text, photo, video, or audio.
* **Share Date (Share Date)**: A date field where you can select the date you want to share your special moment.
* **Share Time (Share Time)**: A time field where you can select the time you want to share your special moment.
* **Share Note (Share Note)**: A text field where you can add any additional notes or comments about your special moment.
* **Share Email (Share Email)**: An email field where you can enter your email address (optional).
* **Share Phone (Share Phone)**: A text field where you can enter your phone number (optional).
* **Share File (Share File)**: A text field where you can upload a file to share with your special moment (optional).
