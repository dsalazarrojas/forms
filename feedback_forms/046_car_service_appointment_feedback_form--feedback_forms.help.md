# Car Service Appointment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our car service appointment. The form aims to understand your satisfaction with the service, communication, and overall experience. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following sections:

1. **Customer Information**: This section is for your reference only and will not be used for any marketing purposes.
2. **Service Information**: Please provide details about your visit, such as the date and type of service you received.
3. **Service Experience**: Rate your satisfaction with the service, including the scheduling process, wait time, and overall quality of service.
4. **Additional Comments**: If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please share them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): For follow-up if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Contact number.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): When was your appointment scheduled?
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): What service did you receive?
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, text, optional): The year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): The manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): The model of your vehicle.
* **Service Advisor** (`service_advisor`, text, optional): Who helped you during your visit.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied were you with the service?
* **Scheduling Experience** (`scheduling_experience`, select_one, required): Rate the scheduling process.
* **Appointment Availability** (`appointment_availability`, select_one, required): How easy was it to get an appointment?
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`, select_one, required): How long did you wait?
* **Wait Time Acceptable** (`wait_time_acceptable`, select_one, required): Was the wait time acceptable?
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of service.
* **Technician Skill** (`technician_skill`, select_one, required): Rate the technician's skill.
* **Service Advisor Rating** (`service_advisor_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your service advisor.
* **Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): How well was the service advisor able to communicate?
* **Explanation of Services** (`explanation_of_services`, select_one, required): Were services explained clearly?
* **Facility Cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`, select_one, required): Rate the cleanliness of the facility.
* **Waiting Area Comfort** (`waiting_area_comfort`, select_one, optional): Rate the comfort of the waiting area.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Was the value worth the price?
* **Pricing Transparency** (`pricing_transparency`, select_one, required): Was the pricing clear?
* **Time to Complete** (`time_to_complete`, select_one, required): Was the service completed on time?
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_one, required): How was your vehicle returned to you?
* **Problem Resolved** (`problem_resolved`, select_one, required): Was the problem resolved?
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, select_one, required): Would you recommend us?
* **Likelihood to Return** (`likelihood_to_return`, select_one, required): Will you return to us?
* **Best Aspect** (`best_aspect`, text, optional): What did we do well?
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): What could we improve?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback.
* **Contact Permission** (`contact_permission`, select_one, optional): Can we contact you for follow-up?
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Today's date.

Note: Fields marked as required are mandatory and must be filled out. Please fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete and accurate.
