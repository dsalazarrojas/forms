# digital_wellness_education_series_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The digital wellness education series evaluation form is designed to gather feedback from participants in the series, helping us understand their experience and improve future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to answer all questions that are marked as "required."
3. For questions that allow multiple selections, choose all relevant options that apply.
4. For questions that require a written response, provide a clear and concise answer.
