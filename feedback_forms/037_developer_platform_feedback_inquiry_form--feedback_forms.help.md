# Developer Platform Feedback Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Developer Platform Feedback Inquiry Form is designed to collect feedback from developers on their experiences and suggestions for improving our platform. Your input is crucial in helping us provide a better platform for developers to build, test, and deploy their applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a category for your feedback: Select the category that best describes your feedback, such as Bug reports, Feature suggestions, or Other.
2. Provide a brief description: Write a short description of your feedback.
3. Enter your email address: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or clarifications.
4. Provide your feedback: Share your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve our platform.
5. Rate your satisfaction: Choose how satisfied you are with our platform.
6. Provide additional comments: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have.
7. Rate the developer platform team: Rate how available and responsive our developer platform team is.
8. Rate the product manager: Rate how available and responsive our product manager is.
9. Rate the team lead: Rate how available and responsive our team lead is.
10. Rate the engineering manager: Rate how available and responsive our engineering manager is.
11. Rate the QA manager: Rate how available and responsive our QA manager is.
12. Rate the designer: Rate how available and responsive our designer is.
13. Rate the project manager: Rate how available and responsive our project manager is.
14. Rate the release manager: Rate how available and responsive our release manager is.
