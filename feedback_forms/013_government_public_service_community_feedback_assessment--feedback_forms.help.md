# Government Public Service Community Feedback Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from the public about their experience with government public services. It is intended to be completed by individuals who have interacted with these services and wish to provide feedback on their experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information and submit it. You can skip any fields that are not applicable to your experience.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is an optional introductory note to provide context for your feedback.
* **How would you rate the service**: Rate your experience with the service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **What would you like to see changed or improved**: Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have for improvement on the service.
* **Contact Information**: Check this box if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Contact Information 2**: This is an optional field for you to provide any additional contact information if you are willing to be contacted.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the service.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Date**: Select the date you interacted with the service.
* **Time**: Select the time you interacted with the service.
* **Date 2**: Select the date you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Time 2**: Select the time you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or to provide additional information.
* **Service name**: Enter the name of the service you interacted with.
* **Service type**: Enter the type of service you received (e.g. "Tax assistance", "Dispute resolution", etc.).
* **Service level**: Enter the level of service you received (e.g. "Basic", "Advanced", etc.).
* **Department**: Enter the department you interacted with (e.g. "Police department", "Social services", etc.).
* **Location**: Enter the location where you interacted with the service.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee you interacted with.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee you interacted with.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the email of the employee you interacted with.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager of the employee you interacted with.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the manager of the employee you interacted with.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email of the manager of the employee you interacted with.
* **Submit**: Please review all the information and submit the form to provide your feedback.
