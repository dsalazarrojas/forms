# Peer Network Interest Exchange - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Network Interest Exchange form is designed to facilitate the exchange of information between members of a peer network. By filling out this form, members can identify potential connections and collaborations based on their interests, offer their skills and services, and address mutual needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out your name and selecting your interests from the list.
2. List your skills and services that you can offer to other members.
3. Specify your needs that you are looking to fulfill through the network.
4. Repeat steps 1-3 for the second member.
5. Select the connections you have with other members.
6. Specify the algorithms that you use to match with other members.
7. Provide any additional information about yourself or your needs (e.g., prophets).
8. Repeat this process for members 3-5.
9. Select the connections you have with other members (for members 2-5).
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member 1's Name**: Provide your name as a member of the peer network.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out your profile.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your interests, offerings, and needs.
* Review and proofread your form before submitting it.
* Use this form as a starting point for further discussions and collaborations with other members.
