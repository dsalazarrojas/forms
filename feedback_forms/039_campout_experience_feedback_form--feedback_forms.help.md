# Campout Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Campout Experience Feedback Form is a survey designed to gather feedback from participants on their experiences with a campout event. This form aims to gather information on various aspects of the event, including facilities, programs, and overall satisfaction. The collected data will help the event organizers to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and role at the campout in the "Respondent Name" and "Role at Camp" fields, respectively.
2. Select the camp session you attended from the "Camp Session Attended" field.
3. Rate your overall experience with the campout in the "Overall Experience" field.
4. Indicate if you would recommend the campout to others in the "Would You Recommend This Campout" field.
5. Answer the questions related to facilities and accommodations, programs and activities, staff, and social experience.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Respondent Name (Text, Optional)**: Please enter your name.
- **Role at Camp (Select One)**: Choose your role at the campout (Camper, Counselor, Staff, Parent/Guardian, or Volunteer).
- **Camp Session Attended (Text)**: Select the camp session you attended.
- **Overall Experience (Select One)**: Rate your overall experience with the campout (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor).
- **Would You Recommend This Campout (Select One)**: Indicate if you would recommend the campout to others (Strongly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, Do Not Recommend, or Strongly Do Not Recommend).
- **Quality of Sleeping Area (Select One)**: Rate the quality of the sleeping area (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
- **Bathroom and Restroom Facilities (Select One)**: Rate the quality of the bathroom and restroom facilities (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
- **Quality of Food and Dining (Select One)**: Rate the quality of the food and dining experience (Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor).
- **Variety of Activities Offered (Select One)**: Rate the variety of activities offered (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor).
- **Enjoyment of Activities (Select One)**: Rate how much you enjoyed the activities (Loved them, Enjoyed them, Neutral, Did not enjoy them, or Hated them).
- **Favorite Activity (Text)**: Enter your favorite activity, if any.
- **Helpfulness and Friendliness of Staff (Select One)**: Rate the helpfulness and friendliness of the staff (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor).
- **Quality of Program Leadership (Select One)**: Rate the quality of the program leadership (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor).
- **I Made New Friends (Select One)**: Indicate if you made new friends during the camp (Strongly Agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly Disagree).
- **Sense of Community and Belonging (Select One)**: Rate the sense of community and belonging you felt during the camp (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor).
- **Areas for Improvement (Text)**: Suggest any areas that need improvement.
- **Missing Activities (Text)**: Enter any activities you wish were included.
- **Additional Comments or Feedback (Text)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
