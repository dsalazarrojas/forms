# BMX Cruise Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information from riders about their preferences and preferences for BMX cruises. By filling out this form, you will help the organizers create a more enjoyable and safe experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your honest and accurate information about your preferences and concerns.
2. Answer only the questions that apply to you, and leave the others blank.
3. Be sure to provide your email address to receive updates about the event.
4. If you have any injuries or physical concerns, please inform us so we can take necessary precautions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rider Name** (`rider_name`, text, required): Please enter your name or nickname.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated about the event.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select your level of experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Pro
* **What Type of BMX Bike Do You Ride?** (`bike_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of bike you ride:
	+ Street
	+ Park
	+ Dirt
	+ Freestyle
* **Preferred Cruise Type** (`preferred_cruise_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of cruise you would enjoy most:
	+ Urban street cruise
	+ Park exploration
	+ Dirt trail riding
	+ Mixed terrain
	+ Social casual ride
	+ Fast-paced speed ride
* **Preferred Group Size** (`group_size_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the size of group you would like to participate in:
	+ Small group (3-5 riders)
	+ Medium group (6-10 riders)
	+ Large group (11-20 riders)
	+ Very large (20+ riders)
	+ No preference
* **How Often Would You Like Cruises?** (`cruise_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select how often you would like to participate in cruises:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ As often as possible
* **Preferred Days for Cruises** (`preferred_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days you would enjoy participating in cruises:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Preferred Times for Cruises** (`preferred_times`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the times you would enjoy participating in cruises:
	+ Morning (8am-12pm)
	+ Afternoon (12pm-5pm)
	+ Evening (5pm-8pm)
	+ Night (8pm+)
* **Route Suggestions or Ideas** (`route_suggestions`, text, optional): Describe your favorite locations or routes you would like to ride.
* **Must-Have Features for Cruises** (`must_have_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select what's important to you:
	+ Good road surfaces
	+ Scenic views
	+ Tricks spots
	+ Food/drink stops
	+ Photography opportunities
	+ Night lighting
	+ Low traffic
	+ Beginner-friendly
* **How Would You Get to the Meet Location?** (`transportation`, select_one, optional): Select how you would get to the meet location:
	+ Riding to the location
	+ Car
	+ Public transport
	+ Walk
	+ Other
* **Any Injuries or Physical Concerns We Should Know?** (`injuries_concerns`, text, optional): Inform us about any injuries or physical concerns you have.
* **Would you help organize or lead cruises?** (`willing_to_help`, select_one, optional): Select if you would like to help organize or lead cruises:
	+ Yes, very interested
	+ Maybe
	+ No, just want to participate
* **Additional Feedback or Ideas** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or ideas you have about the event.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Leave blank any questions that don't apply to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
