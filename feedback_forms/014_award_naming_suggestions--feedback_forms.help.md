# Award Naming Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The award naming process aims to create a fitting and meaningful title for the award. This form is designed to gather suggestions from employees and staff members, providing a wide range of ideas for the award's name.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name and email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
2. Select your department or division where you belong to give context to your suggestions.
3. Choose the number of years you have been with the organization to indicate your experience level.
4. Select the type of award you are naming (e.g., Employee of the year, Leadership excellence).
5. Suggest up to three award names, along with a brief explanation of each suggestion.
6. Indicate your preferred tone for the award name.
7. Determine if the award should honor a person or keep it generic.
8. If the award should honor a person, suggest the name of the person to be honored.
9. Suggest a physical trophy design idea.
10. Choose a suitable timing for the award presentation ceremony (e.g., annual gala, quarterly meeting).
11. Optionally, suggest a prize or benefit for the winners.
12. Finally, add any additional comments about the award.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Your Name (submitter_name) 
- **Your Name** (Optional - enter your name)
  - This field is meant for you to enter your name for contact and follow-up purposes.
  - Please provide your name as you would like it to be displayed in connection with your submission.

### 2. Email Address (submitter_email)
- **Email Address** (For follow-up if your name is selected)
  - This field is where you can enter your email address to ensure we can get in touch with you if your name is selected as a suggestion.

### 3. Department or Division (department)
- **Department or Division** (Where do you work)
  - This field is meant to give context to your suggestions, indicating the department or division you belong to.

### 4. Years with Organization (years_with_organization)
- **Years with Organization** (How long have you been here)
  - Please select how long you have been with the organization to provide a basis for your suggestions.

### 5. Award Category (award_category)
- **Award Category** (Which award are you naming)
  - Here, you will select the type of award you are naming, such as Employee of the year or Innovation award.

### 6. Suggested Award Name 1 (suggested_name_1)
- **Suggested Award Name 1** (Your first name suggestion)
  - Enter your first name suggestion for the award, along with a brief explanation.

### 7. Rationale for Name 1 (name_rationale_1)
- **Rationale for Name 1** (Why did you choose this name)
  - Please provide a brief explanation of why you chose this name suggestion.

### 8. Suggested Award Name 2 (suggested_name_2)
- **Suggested Award Name 2** (Your second name suggestion)
  - If you'd like to suggest an additional name, enter it here, along with an explanation.

### 9. Rationale for Name 2 (name_rationale_2)
- **Rationale for Name 2** (Why did you choose this name)
  - If applicable, provide a brief explanation for this second name suggestion.

### 10. Suggested Award Name 3 (suggested_name_3)
- **Suggested Award Name 3** (Your third name suggestion)
  - If you have a third name, enter it here, along with an explanation.

### 11. Rationale for Name 3 (name_rationale_3)
- **Rationale for Name 3** (Why did you choose this name)
  - If applicable, provide a brief explanation for this third name suggestion.

### 12. Naming Inspiration (naming_inspiration)
- **Naming Inspiration** (What inspired your suggestions)
  - Select the inspiration behind your suggestions, such as company values or industry terminology.

### 13. Preferred Tone (tone_preference)
- **Preferred Tone** (What tone should the award convey)
  - Select the tone you prefer for the award name, such as formal and prestigious.

### 14. Should Award Honor a Person (should_honor_person)
- **Should Award Honor a Person** (Should the award be named after someone)
  - Decide if the award should honor a person or keep it generic.

### 15. Suggested Honoree (suggested_honoree)
- **Suggested Honoree** (If yes, who should it honor)
  - If the award should honor a person, suggest a name.

### 16. Award Criteria Suggestion (award_criteria_suggestion)
- **Award Criteria Suggestion** (What should recipients be recognized for)
  - Optionally, suggest what recipients should be recognized for.

### 17. Trophy or Plaque Design Idea (trophy_design_idea)
- **Trophy or Plaque Design Idea** (Any ideas for the physical award)
  - If you have any ideas for the physical trophy or plaque, enter them here.

### 18. When Should Award Be Presented (presentation_timing)
- **When Should Award Be Presented** (Best timing for the ceremony)
  - Select a suitable timing for the award presentation, such as an annual gala or quarterly meeting.

### 19. Prize or Benefit Suggestion (prize_suggestion)
- **Prize or Benefit Suggestion** (What should winners receive)
  - If you'd like to suggest a prize or benefit for the winners, do so here.

### 20. Additional Comments (additional_comments)
- **Additional Comments** (Any other thoughts about this award)
  - Enter any other thoughts or ideas you may have about the award.
