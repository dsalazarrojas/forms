# Daily Gratitude Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Gratitude Journal is designed to help you reflect on the good things that happened during the day, fostering a positive mindset and promoting gratitude.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a date for the day you want to journal about.
2. In the text fields, write down three to five things that you are grateful for, such as:
	* A good conversation with a friend or family member.
	* A delicious meal you enjoyed.
	* A beautiful sunset you witnessed.
	* A successful project you completed.
	* A funny joke or meme that made you laugh.
3. Be as specific and detailed as possible when describing your gratitudes.
