# peer_feedback_effectiveness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a team member's effectiveness and collaboration. It's used to assess their strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and provide suggestions for growth.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
2. Answer question 1: "What is the current level of collaboration in your team?" This question helps to gauge the overall team dynamic.
3. Rate your team member's effectiveness in question 2.
4. Identify important skills for your role in question 3.
5. Suggest areas for improvement in question 4.
6. Provide a recommendation for improvement in question 5.
7. Rate your experience in this survey in question 9.
8. Choose how you'd like your organization to follow up with you on improvement in question 10.
9. Add any additional comments in question 11.
10. Review and submit the form.
