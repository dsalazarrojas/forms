# Digital Legislation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Legislation Feedback Form is a tool for users to provide feedback on digital legislation. This form helps gather information on how users perceive and interact with digital legislation, which is essential for improving its effectiveness and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Digital Legislation Feedback Form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Feedback Title** (Field 1). This is a brief description of the feedback you'd like to share.
2. Enter your **Name** (Field 2) as a way to identify yourself.
3. Provide your **Email** (Field 3) if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
4. Share your **Feedback** (Field 4) in as much detail as possible.
5. If applicable, select the **Legislation ID** (Field 6) that your feedback is related to from the dropdown menu.
6. Finally, click the **Submit** (Field 7) button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Title** (`feedback_title`, text, Optional): This field is for a brief description of your feedback. Please keep it concise, but clear and descriptive.
* **Name** (`name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as a way to identify yourself. This will help us understand who submitted the feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, provide your email address here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Optional): Share your thoughts, concerns, and suggestions about digital legislation. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Legislation ID** (`legislation_id`, select_one, Optional): If your feedback is related to a specific piece of digital legislation, select the corresponding ID from the dropdown menu.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, Optional): Click this button to send your feedback. You'll receive a confirmation message after submitting your feedback.
