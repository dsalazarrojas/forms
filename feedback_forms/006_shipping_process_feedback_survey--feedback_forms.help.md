# shipping_process_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on your experience with our shipping process. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Follow the instructions below for each field to fill out the form accurately.
2. Ensure you answer each question to the best of your knowledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was your shipping process** (`how_was_your_shipping_process`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your recent shipping experience.
* **Shipping cost** (`shipping_cost`, number, required): Indicate the actual cost incurred for shipping, if any.
* **Delivery date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received your order or the date you expected delivery.
* **Shipping time** (`shipping_time`, time, optional): If available, provide the estimated time taken for shipping.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience from 1-10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or details regarding your experience.
* **Shipping company** (`shipping_company`, select_one, required): Did you use a shipping company for this order? Yes/No.
* **Shipping service** (`shipping_service`, select_multiple, required): Which shipping services did you use for this order? Yes/No.
* **Customer contact method** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Did you contact our customer support team during this order? Yes/No.
* **Shipping method** (`shipping_method`, select_multiple, required): Which shipping method did you use for this order? Yes/No.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share any final thoughts or suggestions regarding your experience.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Be as specific as possible in your answers.
- If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to our customer support team.
