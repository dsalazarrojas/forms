# Discord Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discord Feedback Form is designed to collect user feedback, bug reports, and suggestions about the Discord platform. This form aims to gather information from users to improve the platform's functionality, fix issues, and enhance the overall user experience.
 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is an optional field, but providing your email address can help us contact you for further questions or follow-up regarding your feedback.
3. Type your message in the "Message" field, describing your feedback, bug report, or suggestion.
4. Choose your rating from the "Rating" field, using the stars to select a number from 1 to 10. This helps us understand the severity of your feedback.
5. Select one or more categories from the "Category" field, choosing from: Community Feedback, Bug Report, Suggestion, or Question.
6. Optionally, click the "Submit" field to submit your form. If you do not select this field, your feedback will not be submitted.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your name.
* **Email**: Your email address (optional). This will help us contact you about your feedback.
* **Message**: Describe your feedback, bug report, or suggestion.
* **Rating**: Choose a number from 1 to 10 to indicate the severity of your feedback.
* **Category**: Select one or more categories for your feedback, such as: Community Feedback, Bug Report, Suggestion, or Question.
* **Submit**: Click this field to submit your form and send your feedback.
