# Tell Us What You Think - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from end-users about their experience with a travel agency. It is intended to be filled out by customers who have had a recent interaction with the agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions that apply to your experience.
2. Fill out all the fields that are marked as "required" and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Experience (1)** (`customer_experience`, text, optional):
	+ This field is a text area where you can describe your experience with the travel agency.
* **Services Provided (2)** (`services_provided`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can describe the services you received from the agency, such as booking, customer support, or travel planning.
* **Communication (3)** (`communication`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can describe how the agency's communication with you was, such as responsiveness, clarity, or helpfulness.
* **Overall Rating (4)** (`overall_rating`, number, optional):
	+ This field is where you can give a numerical rating of your overall experience with the agency, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Customer Service (5)** (`customer_service`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can describe your experience with the agency's customer service team, such as helpfulness, friendliness, or responsiveness.
* **Travel Agency (6)** (`travel_agency`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide additional information about the travel agency, such as the location, amenities, or services offered.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have had multiple interactions with the agency, you can submit this form multiple times, once for each interaction.
* If you have any attachments or files you'd like to attach to your feedback, such as photos or emails, you can do so via the upload feature on this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
