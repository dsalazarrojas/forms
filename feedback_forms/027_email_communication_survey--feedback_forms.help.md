# Email Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback on your email communication preferences. Your responses will help us improve our email communication strategy to better suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question honestly, and select all that apply.
2. For questions with a select_multiple type, you can select multiple options by checking multiple boxes.
3. For questions with a select_one type, select only one option that best answers the question.
4. You can submit the form at your own pace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you like to receive emails?** (email_preferences): This question asks about your email reception frequency preference. Select 'Yes' if you would like to receive emails frequently, and 'No' otherwise.
* **What is the open rate of emails you receive?** (email_open_rate): This question asks about the average number of times you open an email in your inbox. Please enter the average open rate of emails you receive.
* **What type of content do you like in emails?** (email_content): This question asks about the type of content you like to see in emails. Select 'Yes' for content you like, and 'No' for content you don't like.
* **How often do you get emails?** (email_frequency): This question asks about the frequency of emails you receive. Select 'Yes' if you receive emails frequently, and 'No' if you don't.
* **What format of emails do you like?** (email_format): This question asks about the format you prefer for emails. Select 'Yes' for your preferred format, and 'No' for any other format.
* **What is your preferred method of communication?** (communication_channels): This question asks about your preferred way of communication. Select 'Yes' for your preferred method, and 'No' for any other method.
* **At what time do emails usually send?** (email_sending_time): This question asks about the time of day emails are usually sent. Please use the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 pm).
* **Would you like to share your contact info?** (contact_info): This question asks about your willingness to share your contact information. Select 'Yes' if you would like to share, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Email preferences** (email_preferences_2): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your email preferences. 
* **Email content** (email_content_2): This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about the content you like in emails. 

Remember, your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your honesty.
