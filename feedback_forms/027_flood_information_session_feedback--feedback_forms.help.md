# Flood Information Session Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a flood information session. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved in future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please complete the following steps:

1. Enter the Session ID number to associate your feedback with the specific session you attended.
2. Select the date you attended the session.
3. Rate the session from 1 to 5.
4. Provide any comments you have about the session.
5. Choose the topics you would like to see covered in future sessions.
6. Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
7. Optionally, provide your phone number if you would like us to contact you.
8. Add any additional comments about the session.
9. Enter the average rating for the session (if you know it).
10. Enter the average rating for the topics (if you know it).
11. Rate the performance of the emergency manager and local authority (if you attended one).
12. Rate the performance of the community group (if you attended one).
13. Enter the rating scale used (if you know it).
14. Provide any help or clarification about the rating scale.
15. Enter the date and time you submitted this feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session ID** (`session_id`, Number, Required): Enter the number of the session you attended.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Number, Optional): Rate the session from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any comments you have about the session.
* **Topics** (`topics`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the topics you would like to see covered in future sessions.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Optionally, provide your phone number if you would like us to contact you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments about the session.
* **Session Rating Average** (`session_rating_average`, Text, Optional): Enter the average rating for the session (if you know it).
* **Topic Rating Average** (`topic_rating_average`, Text, Optional): Enter the average rating for the topics (if you know it).
* **Emergency Manager Rating** (`emergency_manager_rating`, Select One, Optional): Rate the performance of the emergency manager (if you attended one).
* **Emergency Manager Performance** (`emergency_manager_performance`, Text, Optional): Provide any comments about the emergency manager's performance.
* **Local Authority Rating** (`local_authority_rating`, Select One, Optional): Rate the performance of the local authority (if you attended one).
* **Local Authority Performance** (`local_authority_performance`, Text, Optional): Provide any comments about the local authority's performance.
* **Community Group Rating** (`community_group_rating`, Select One, Optional): Rate the performance of the community group (if you attended one).
* **Community Group Performance** (`community_group_performance`, Text, Optional): Provide any comments about the community group's performance.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, Text, Optional): Enter the rating scale used (if you know it).
* **Rating Scale Help** (`rating_scale_help`, Text, Optional): Provide any clarification about the rating scale.
* **Submitted on** (`submitted_on`, Date, Optional): Enter the date and time you submitted this feedback.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, Text, Optional): Enter your name or title.
