# Watched Content Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on watched content, aiming to evaluate the effectiveness of the content in engaging or informing the audience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the instructions and guidelines before starting.
2. Answer each question truthfully, based on your experience with the content.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Did you watch the content?** (watched_content_feedback_form_1): This question asks whether you watched the content at all. Please choose the answer that best describes your experience.
	+ True: You watched the content.
	+ False: You did not watch the content.
* **What type of content was it?** (watched_content_type): Choose the type of content that you watched (Video, Article, or Other).
* **How many times did you watch it?** (watched_content_count): If you watched the content multiple times, please type the number of times you watched it.
* **What was the rating?** (watched_content_rating): Please rate the content on a scale (the exact scale is not specified in the form, so assume it's a 1-10 scale).
* **What was your comment on the content?** (watched_content_comment): Write your thoughts and opinions on the content, providing any specific feedback you'd like to share.
* **Would you watch it again?** (watched_content_watched_again): Choose whether you would watch the content again.
	+ True: You would watch it again.
	+ False: You would not watch it again.
* **When was the last time you watched it?** (watched_content_watched_at): If you've watched the content, provide the date of the last time you watched it.
* **What time did you watch it?** (watched_content_watched_at_time): If you've watched the content, provide the time of the last time you watched it. Note: This field is not required.
* **How did you access the content?** (watched_content_access_method): Describe how you accessed the content (e.g., website, social media, mobile app, etc.).
* **Was the content relevant to you?** (watched_content_relevance): Choose whether the content was relevant to you.
	+ True: The content was relevant to you.
	+ False: The content was not relevant to you.
* **Was the content easy to follow?** (watched_content_followability): Choose whether the content was easy to follow.
	+ True: The content was easy to follow.
	+ False: The content was not easy to follow.
* **Any other comments?** (watched_content_other_comments): If you have any additional feedback or comments, feel free to share them here.
