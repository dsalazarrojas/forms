# Laundry Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers to help us improve our laundry service. Your input will help us ensure we deliver exceptional service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply answer the questions and provide any additional comments you have. You can rate the service using the numbers provided next to each question.
2. Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
	+ Label: Introduction
	+ Type: Note
	+ Description: This is a brief section for you to describe your experience with our laundry service. Please provide any background information or context about your visit.
* **Overall Quality**:
	+ Label: Overall Quality
	+ Type: Number (scale: 1-5)
	+ Description: Rate how you would rate the overall quality of our laundry service. A rating of 1 means poor, and 5 means excellent.
* **Service Attentiveness**:
	+ Label: Service Attentiveness
	+ Type: Number (scale: 1-5)
	+ Description: Rate how attentive you felt the staff was during your visit. A rating of 1 means poor, and 5 means excellent.
* **Communication**:
	+ Label: Communication
	+ Type: Number (scale: 1-5)
	+ Description: Rate how well you felt the communication with the staff was during your visit. A rating of 1 means poor, and 5 means excellent.
* **Laundry Punctuality**:
	+ Label: Laundry Punctuality
	+ Type: Number (scale: 1-5)
	+ Description: Rate how punctual our service was. A rating of 1 means poor, and 5 means excellent.
* **Staff Friendly**:
	+ Label: Staff Friendly
	+ Type: Number (scale: 1-5)
	+ Description: Rate how friendly you felt the staff was during your visit. A rating of 1 means poor, and 5 means excellent.
* **Would You Recommend**:
	+ Label: Would You Recommend
	+ Type: Number (scale: 1-5)
	+ Description: Rate how likely you are to recommend our service. A rating of 1 means would not recommend, and 5 means would definitely recommend.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Label: Additional Comments
	+ Type: Note
	+ Description: Please share any additional comments or suggestions you have about our service. This is a great opportunity to provide any feedback that didn't fit in the previous questions.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" section.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide us with your feedback!
