# Plant Cell Experiment Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students after a lab experiment. It helps instructors or lab supervisors to assess the effectiveness of the lab, get feedback from students, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in each field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Lab Report
* **Lab Report** (`lab_report`, text, optional): Please provide a brief summary of your experience during the lab experiment.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of your instructor.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please provide your name.
* **Lab Group** (`lab_group`, text, optional): If you were part of a lab group, please provide the group name.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments you have about the lab experiment.
* **Date Collected** (`date_collected`, date, optional): Date you completed the lab experiment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Lab Rating** (`lab_rating`, select_multiple, required): Please rate the lab experience (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Lab Improvement Ideas** (`lab_improvement`, select_multiple, required): Suggest ways to improve the lab experience (Equipment, Instructions, Materials, Other).
* **Student Learning** (`student_learning`, select_one, required): How well did you learn during the lab experience (Very Good, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement).
* **Teacher Comment** (`teacher_comment`, text, optional): If you have any comment for your teacher.
* **Teacher Suggestion** (`teacher_suggestion`, text, optional): If you have any suggestion for your teacher.
* **Student Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Any feedback you have for your fellow students.
* **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, text, optional): Any feedback you have for your instructor.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional): Please sign with your name.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`, text, optional): Please sign with your name.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Date you submitted this form.
* **Lab Supervisor** (`lab_supervisor`, text, optional): If you know the name of your lab supervisor.
* **Student Attendance** (`student_attendance`, text, optional): If you attended all the lab sessions.
* **Student Participation** (`student_participation`, text, optional): If you were actively participating in the lab sessions.
* **Lab Facilitators** (`lab_facilitators`, text, optional): If you know the name of your lab facilitators.
* **Equipment Used** (`equipment_used`, text, optional): List the equipment you used during the lab experiment.
* **Lab Duration** (`lab_duration`, time, optional): The duration of the lab experiment.
* **Lab Objectives** (`lab_objectives`, text, optional): The objectives of the lab experiment.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Any other comments you have.
* **Lab Facilitators 2** (`lab_facilitators_2`, text, optional): If you know the name of your lab facilitators 2.
* **Lab Supervisor 2** (`lab_supervisor_2`, text, optional): If you know the name of your lab supervisor 2.
* **Student Attendance 2** (`student_attendance_2`, text, optional): If you attended all the lab sessions 2.
* **Student Participation 2** (`student_participation_2`, text, optional): If you were actively participating in the lab sessions 2.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields.
* Use specific examples to provide detailed feedback.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact your instructor or lab supervisor.
* The form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please be concise.
* If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank and ask for help.
