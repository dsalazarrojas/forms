# Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from trainees after a training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Session ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to each session.
2. Choose the Date of Session, when you attended the training.
3. Type your name as the Trainer or Trainee.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the session, using a scale from 1 to 5.
5. Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted).
6. Optionally, you may provide any comments or suggestions for improvement.
7. Choose whether you are the Trainer or Trainee who submitted this feedback.
8. Enter the date and time you are submitting this feedback.
9. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session ID** (1): Enter the Session ID assigned to this session.
* **Date of Session** (2): Select the date you attended the training.
* **Trainer Name** (3): Type your name as the Trainer or Trainee who attended the session.
* **Feedback** (4): Describe your overall feedback about the session. Be concise and provide specific suggestions for improvement.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (5): If you have any ideas for how the session could be improved, type them here.
* **Rating** (6): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session, using a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Email** (7): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up or further discussions.
* **Phone** (8): You may enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Comments** (9): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the session.
* **Submitted By** (10): Choose whether you are the Trainer or Trainee who submitted this feedback.
* **Submitted At** (11): Enter the date and time you are submitting this feedback.
