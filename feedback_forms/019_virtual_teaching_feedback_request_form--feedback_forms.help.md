# Virtual Teaching Feedback Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for instructors to provide feedback on their teaching experience. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from instructors to help us improve our virtual teaching quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Course Name` field with the name of the course you took.
2. Select `Yes` or `No` to indicate your level of `Teaching Experience`.
3. Enter your `Email` address (if applicable).
4. Provide any additional `Feedback` in the text field.
5. Select the level of `Teaching Quality` (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor) and your level of `Communication Skills` (Yes or No).
6. Select the types of `Course Materials` you used (Yes or No).
7. Select the types of `Course Format` you used (Yes or No).
8. Select your overall `Overall Satisfaction` (Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Highly Dissatisfied).
9. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the `Suggest Improvement` text field.
10. Select the level of `Teaching Support` you received (Yes or No).
11. Enter any thoughts on your `Student Experience` in the text field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the course you took.
* **Teaching Experience** (select_multiple, optional): Select `Yes` if you have prior teaching experience, or `No` if you don't.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for any follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Feedback** (note, optional): Provide any feedback you have about your teaching experience.
* **Teaching Quality** (select_one, optional): Select the level of teaching quality you experienced (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Communication Skills** (select_multiple, optional): Select the level of communication skills you experienced (Yes or No).
* **Course Materials** (select_multiple, optional): Select the types of course materials you used (Yes or No).
* **Course Format** (select_multiple, optional): Select the types of course formats you used (Yes or No).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (select_one, optional): Select the level of overall satisfaction you experienced (Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Highly Dissatisfied).
* **Suggest Improvement** (text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in this field.
* **Teaching Support** (select_multiple, optional): Select the level of teaching support you received (Yes or No).
* **Student Experience** (text, optional): Enter any thoughts on your student experience, if applicable.
