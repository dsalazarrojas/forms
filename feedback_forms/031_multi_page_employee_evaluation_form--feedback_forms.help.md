# Multi Page Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate performance evaluations of employees, allowing the evaluator to assess an employee's performance, provide feedback, and track goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions on each page:

### Page 1: Employee Information
- Enter your employee name.
- Enter your job title.
- Enter your department.
- Enter your email address.
- Enter your phone number.

### Page 2: Evaluator Information
- Enter your name.
- Enter your title.
- Enter your email address.
- Enter your phone number.

### Page 3: Evaluation
- Select one or more options to provide performance feedback (Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory).
- Select one or more options to evaluate goals and objectives (Achieved, Not Achieved, In Progress).

### Page 4: Additional Comments
- Enter any additional comments from the evaluator.
- Enter any additional comments from the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Evaluator Title** (`evaluator_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your title.
* **Evaluator Email** (`evaluator_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Evaluator Phone** (`evaluator_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments from the evaluator.
* **Performance Feedback** (`performance_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more options to provide performance feedback (Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, Unsatisfactory).
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals_and_objectives`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more options to evaluate goals and objectives (Achieved, Not Achieved, In Progress).
* **Evaluator Comments** (`evaluator_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments from the evaluator.
* **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments from the employee.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is meant to be a helpful tool for performance evaluations, not a replacement for regular feedback and check-ins.
