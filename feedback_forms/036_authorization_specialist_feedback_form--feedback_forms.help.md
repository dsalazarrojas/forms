# Authorization Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from individuals who have interacted with an Authorization Specialist. This feedback will help improve the services offered by the Authorization Specialists.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date of the interaction with the Authorization Specialist.
2. Provide your name and email address (required fields).
3. Enter the name of the facility or provider you work with (required field).
4. Enter the name of the Authorization Specialist.
5. Select the date of the recent interaction (required field).
6. Choose the type of interaction you had with the specialist (required field).
7. Rate the specialist's responsiveness (required field).
8. Rate the specialist's knowledge and expertise (required field).
9. Rate the specialist's problem resolution ability (required field).
10. Rate the specialist's communication clarity (required field).
11. Rate the specialist's professional demeanor (required field).
12. Rate the specialist's courtesy and respect (required field).
13. Rate the specialist's follow-through on commitments (optional field).
14. Rate the specialist's accuracy (required field).
15. Rate the specialist's process efficiency (required field).
16. Rate the documentation quality (required field).
17. Enter any issues or problems you experienced (optional field).
18. Describe any positive aspects of your experience (optional field).
19. Provide suggestions for improvement (optional field).
20. Rate your overall satisfaction (optional field).
21. State whether you would recommend this specialist (optional field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Date**: When you are providing feedback, enter the date of the interaction you are referring to.
* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Facility/Provider Name**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider you work with.
* **Authorization Specialist Name**: Enter the name of the Authorization Specialist you are providing feedback for.
* **Interaction Date**: Select the date of the recent interaction.
* **Type of Interaction**: Choose the type of interaction you had with the specialist (e.g., phone call, email, in-person).
* **Responsiveness**: Rate the specialist's responsiveness, with options ranging from "Extremely Slow" to "Very Quick".
* **Knowledge and Expertise**: Rate the specialist's knowledge and expertise, with options ranging from "Lacking Knowledge" to "Expert Level".
* **Problem Resolution Ability**: Rate the specialist's ability to resolve problems, with options ranging from "Could Not Help" to "Excellent Solution".
* **Communication Clarity**: Rate the specialist's communication clarity, with options ranging from "Very Unclear" to "Very Clear".
* **Professional Demeanor**: Rate the specialist's professional demeanor, with options ranging from "Very Unprofessional" to "Very Professional".
* **Courtesy and Respect**: Rate the specialist's courtesy and respect, with options ranging from "Very Disrespectful" to "Very Respectful".
* **Follow-Through on Commitments**: Rate the specialist's follow-through on commitments, with options ranging from "Never" to "Always" (optional field).
* **Authorization Accuracy**: Rate the specialist's accuracy, with options ranging from "Frequently Incorrect" to "Always Correct".
* **Process and Procedure Efficiency**: Rate the specialist's process and procedure efficiency, with options ranging from "Very Inefficient" to "Very Efficient".
* **Documentation Quality**: Rate the specialist's documentation quality, with options ranging from "Poor Quality" to "Excellent Quality".
* **Issues/Problems Experienced**: Enter any issues or problems you experienced during the interaction (optional field).
* **Positive Aspects of Experience**: Describe any positive aspects of your experience with the specialist (optional field).
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Provide any suggestions for improving the specialist's service (optional field).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction, with options ranging from "Very Unsatisfied" to "Very Satisfied" (optional field).
* **Would Recommend**: State whether you would recommend this specialist, with options ranging from "Definitely Not" to "Definitely Yes" (optional field).

Please note that this form should be completed by users who have interacted with an Authorization Specialist, and the feedback should be specific to their personal experience.
