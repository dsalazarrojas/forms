# Feedback On Denial Process - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the denial process experienced or witnessed by users. This information will help identify areas for improvement and refine the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate Time Zone for the denial process that occurred.
2. Choose the User(s) who submitted or are responsible for the denial process.
3. Enter the Denial Reason, which is a required field.
4. Enter the Denial Date and Denial Time in the correct format (date and time, respectively).
5. If you have any additional information or comments about the denial process, you can add them in the Comments field.
6. Finally, select the Submission Date and Submission Time of when the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Denial Reason (Required)** 
  A text field where you can enter the reason for the denial.
* **Denial Date** 
  A date field where you should enter the date of the denial process in the correct format.
* **Denial Time** 
  A time field where you should enter the time of the denial process in the correct format.
* **Time Zone (Required)** 
  A select field where you should choose the time zone of the denial process.
* **Submitted By (Required)** 
  A select field where you should choose the user(s) who submitted or are responsible for the denial process.
* **Comments (Optional)** 
  A text field where you can add any additional comments or information about the denial process.
* **Submission Date** 
  A date field where you should enter the date of when the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** 
  A time field where you should enter the time of when the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter the correct Time Zone for the denial process.
- Select the correct User(s) who submitted or are responsible for the denial process.
- Be as detailed as possible when entering the Denial Reason.
- If you have any additional information or comments, use the Comments field to provide more context.
