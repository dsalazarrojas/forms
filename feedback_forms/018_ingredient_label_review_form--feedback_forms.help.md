# Ingredient Label Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on ingredient labels from various stakeholders, including regulatory personnel, manufacturers, and others with a vested interest in the accuracy and quality of labels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit form" button to begin.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your information.
3. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns.
6. If you are representing a company, enter the company name in the "Company" field.
7. Select the relevant role from the "Role" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the date you reviewed the ingredient label in the "Review Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit form (form_submit, text, not required):** This is the button to submit your form. Click on it to send your feedback.
* **First name (first_name, text, not required):** Enter your first name here.
* **Last name (last_name, text, not required):** Enter your last name here.
* **Email (email, email, not required):** Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you with any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Company (company, text, not required):** If you are representing a company, enter the name of your company here.
* **Role (role, select_one, not required):** Select the role that best describes your relationship with the ingredient label:
	+ Stakeholder
	+ Regulatory
	+ Manufacturer
* **Review Date (review_date, date, not required):** Enter the date you reviewed the ingredient label.
