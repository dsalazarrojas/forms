# Employee Retention Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Retention Interview Form is designed to gather feedback and information from employees about their job satisfaction, communication with their manager, and other relevant details. This form is intended to help managers and HR representatives understand the employees' experiences and concerns, which can be used to improve job satisfaction, retention, and overall work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback or describing your experiences.
4. Make sure to review the form for completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Employee Retention Interview Form** (`employee_retention_interview_form`, text, optional): This field is a brief introduction to the form, providing a friendly and welcoming message to set the tone for the feedback session.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the five options to describe your overall job satisfaction, where:
	+ Very High: I am extremely happy with my job and role.
	+ High: I am satisfied with my job and role, but there's room for improvement.
	+ Neutral: I'm average, neither extremely happy nor extremely dissatisfied with my job.
	+ Low: I'm not satisfied with my job and role.
	+ Very Low: I'm extremely unhappy with my job and role.
* **Communication with Manager** (`communication_with_manager`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the frequencies that apply to describe how often you have one-on-one meetings with your manager, where:
	+ Daily: We meet daily.
	+ Weekly: We meet weekly.
	+ Monthly: We meet monthly.
	+ Never: We never meet one-on-one.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share any specific feedback or concerns you have about your manager or work environment. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have a personal email address that you'd like to share with your manager or HR representative, enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a personal phone number that you'd like to share with your manager or HR representative, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, if you feel it's relevant to the feedback session.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth, if you feel it's relevant to the feedback session.
* **Tips**:
	+ Be honest and thorough in your responses.
	+ Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
	+ If you're unsure about any question or option, it's okay to ask for clarification.
