# Knock Down Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Knock Down Experience Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from customers regarding their experience with our product or service. The goal of this survey is to identify areas of improvement and ensure that we are meeting our customers' expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your level of satisfaction with our product or service.
2. If you have any safety concerns, describe them in as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any improvement opportunities, share your ideas and suggestions for how we can improve.
4. Add any additional comments or concerns you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with our product or service? Please select one or more of the following options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

* **Safety Concerns**: If you have experienced any safety issues or concerns while using our product or service, please describe them in detail.
* **Improvement Opportunities**: If you have any suggestions or ideas for how we can improve our product or service, please share them.
* **Additional Comments**: Do you have any other thoughts or concerns that you would like to share with us?
