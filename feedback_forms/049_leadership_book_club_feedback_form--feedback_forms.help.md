# Leadership Book Club Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on books they've read for the Leadership Book Club. The purpose of this form is to gather information on the user's experience and opinion on the book, including their rating, book recommendation, and any comments they may have. This form is used to provide valuable insights to the book club administrators to improve future book selections.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to help us contact you in case we need more information.
3. Rate the book using the rating field. You can select a number between 1 and 5.
4. Provide a book recommendation if you think there's a similar book you'd like to read.
5. Share your thoughts and opinions on the book in the Feedback field.
6. Optionally, add any comments you'd like to include.
7. Select the category of books you think the recommended book falls under (Leadership, Fiction, or Non-Fiction).
8. Finally, choose the person you'd like to assign this feedback to (Jim or Jane).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
`First Name` (`first_name`, text, optional): This is where you enter your first name.

### Last Name
`Last Name` (`last_name`, text, optional): This is where you enter your last name.

### Email
`Email` (`email`, email, optional): This is where you enter your email address. Please enter a valid email address to help us contact you in case we need more information.

### Rating
`Rate` (`rating`, number, optional): This is where you rate the book using a number between 1 and 5.

### Book Recommendation
`Book Recommendation` (`book_recommendation`, text, optional): If you think of a similar book you'd like to read, please enter the title here.

### Feedback
`Feedback` (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions on the book.

### Comments
`Comments` (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments you'd like to include.

### Category
`Category` (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category of books you think the recommended book falls under (Leadership, Fiction, Non-Fiction).

### Assigned To
`Assigned to` (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Choose the person you'd like to assign this feedback to (Jim or Jane).

### Tips
* Please fill in all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete and accurate.
* Use a valid email address to help us contact you in case we need more information.
* Rate the book using a number between 1 and 5.
* Be specific and clear in your book recommendations and feedback.
* Select the correct category for the book recommendation.
* Choose the correct person to assign the feedback to.
