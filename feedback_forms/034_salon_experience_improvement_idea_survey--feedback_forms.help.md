# Salon Experience Improvement Idea Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users about the salon experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To provide feedback, please fill out the form with your thoughts and suggestions.
2. Answer each field according to your experience and opinions.
3. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting.
4. Once you've filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback and suggestions to the salon.

* **Feedback Ideas (Feedback Ideas)**: Share your ideas on how to improve the salon experience.
* **Salon Feedback (Salon Feedback)**: Rate your overall feedback on the salon experience (Great, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Upgrade Suggestion (Upgrade Suggestion)**: Suggest any upgrades or improvements you think the salon should make.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

* **Date (Date)**: If you want to schedule a follow-up, enter the date.
* **Time (Time)**: If you want to schedule a follow-up, enter the time.
* **Note (Note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Submit (Submit)**: Confirm that you have filled out the form correctly.
* **Submit Confirmation (Submit Confirmation)**: Confirm that you have submitted the form.
* **Email Confirmation (Email Confirmation)**: Confirm that you would like to be contacted via email for follow-up.
* **Follow-up (Follow-up)**: Confirm if you would like the salon to follow up with you.
* **Next Steps (Next Steps)**: Suggest any next steps for the salon to take.

* Please answer honestly and provide detailed feedback.
* If you would like to be contacted, please provide your contact information.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please feel free to contact the salon directly.
