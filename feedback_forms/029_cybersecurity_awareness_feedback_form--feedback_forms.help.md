# Cybersecurity Awareness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness of our cybersecurity awareness training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and any other relevant information.
2. Answer the questions about the training effectiveness and your level of understanding.
3. Provide any comments or feedback you may have.
4. Rate the training's effectiveness.
5. Fill out any other required fields, such as your email address and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as the participant of the training session.
* **Training Effectiveness**: Select one of the following options to indicate how effective you found the training:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Cybersecurity Knowledge**: Select one or more of the following options to indicate your level of understanding:
	+ Basic
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the training session.
* **Rating**: Rate the training's effectiveness:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Participant Company**: Please enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Participant Location**: Please enter your location (if applicable).
* **Participant Job Title**: Please enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Participant Department**: Please enter your department (if applicable).
* **Participant Manager**: Please enter your manager's name (if applicable).
* **Participant Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
* **Organization**: Please enter your organization's name (if applicable).
* **Training Date**: Please enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time**: Please enter the time of the training session.
