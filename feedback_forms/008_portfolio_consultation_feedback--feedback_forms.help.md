# Portfolio Consultation Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Portfolio Consultation Feedback form is used to gather input from users on the quality and effectiveness of a portfolio consultation session. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are submitting feedback for a session that has recently occurred or will be taking place soon.
2. Complete the form with the requested information, paying attention to the required fields.
3. Choose the corresponding rating and select options as per the form instructions.
4. Type in any additional comments you have regarding your experience with the session.
5. Finally, review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to capture any personal thoughts or comments you may have about the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the session you attended or will be attending.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, `number`, required: false): Choose a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 represents "Highly Unsatisfied" and 5 represents "Highly Satisfied."
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the options provided, such as "Highly Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", etc., to express your level of satisfaction with the session.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please type them in this field.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address for internal reference purposes only.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): This field can be left blank, as internal use is minimal.
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the consultant who conducted the session, if you wish to give specific credit.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): If the session took place at or is associated with a school, you can enter the school's name.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose one or more of the provided options (e.g., "Career Coach", "Consultant", "School") that best describes the nature of the session.
