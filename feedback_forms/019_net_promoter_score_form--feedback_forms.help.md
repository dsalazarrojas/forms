# Net Promoter Score Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Promoter Score Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the company. This form is likely used by customer service or marketing teams to gauge customer satisfaction and loyalty, which can help the company understand areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the question about how likely you are to recommend our company.
2. If you are not likely to recommend our company, please explain why.
3. Choose how likely you are to refer a friend or family member to our company.
4. Add any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely are you to recommend our company?** (`net_promoter_score`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question is designed to measure customer loyalty and satisfaction. Please answer on a scale from 1 (Not at all likely) to 5 (Very likely).
* **Why would you not recommend our company?** (`why_no_recommendation`, `note`, optional): This field is for customers who are not likely to recommend our company. Please provide a brief explanation for your answer.
* **How likely are you to refer a friend or family member to our company?** (`how_likely_recommend`, `select_multiple`, optional): This question is designed to measure the likelihood of customers to refer others to our company. Please select from 0 (none) to 5 (all).
* **Any additional comments?** (`comments`, `note`, optional): This field is for customers to provide any additional feedback or comments they would like to share about their experience with our company.
