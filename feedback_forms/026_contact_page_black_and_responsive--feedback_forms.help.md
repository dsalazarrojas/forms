# Contact Page Black And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to get in touch with our team for various types of inquiries, such as general information, customer support, sales questions, technical issues, or partnership opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Contact Us" button on the top of the page.
2. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
3. Select the subject of your inquiry from the provided options.
4. Choose your preferred response method and priority (if applicable).
5. Select your preferred newsletter subscription option (if applicable).
6. Let us know how you found us (if applicable).
7. Click on the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Us**: This section is not a field but a label for the next section. Please click on it to proceed.
* **Your Name** (`visitor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you directly.
* **Subject** (`subject`, text, required): Choose a clear and concise subject that describes your inquiry.
* **Inquiry Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your inquiry from the provided options.
* **Your Message** (`message`, text, required): Write a brief description of your inquiry, providing as much detail as possible.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Choose a priority level for our response, if applicable.
	+ **Low**: We'll respond within 24-48 hours.
	+ **Normal**: We'll respond within 1-2 business days.
	+ **High**: We'll respond within 1 business hour.
	+ **Urgent**: We'll respond as soon as possible.
* **Preferred Response Method** (`preferred_response`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted back.
	+ **Email**: We'll respond via email.
	+ **Phone Call**: We'll contact you via phone call.
	+ **Either**: We'll use either method.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter`, select_one, optional): Check if you'd like to receive updates from us.
* **How Did You Find Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Let us know how you found us.
	+ **Search Engine**: You found us through a search engine.
	+ **Social Media**: You found us through social media.
	+ **Friend Referral**: You were referred by a friend.
	+ **Advertisement**: You saw an ad.
	+ **Direct Visit**: You visited our website directly.
	+ **Other**: Please specify.
