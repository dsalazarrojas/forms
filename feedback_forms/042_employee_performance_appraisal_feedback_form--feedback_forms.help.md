# Employee Performance Appraisal Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on an employee's performance, goals, and areas for improvement. This feedback will help managers and administrators evaluate an employee's progress and make informed decisions to support their growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Evaluate the employee's performance**: Choose a rating from "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to indicate how well the employee is meeting their performance expectations.
2. **Provide specific feedback**: Type in your comments and suggestions for improvement in the second text field.
3. **Add any additional feedback**: Use the third field to include any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to share with the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_performance_appraisal_form_1** (<type>: select_one, <label>: employee_performance_appraisal_form_1_label): This question asks you to evaluate the employee's overall performance. Select "Very Good" if the employee is meeting or exceeding their performance expectations, "Good" if they are meeting expectations, "Fair" if they are partially meeting expectations, or "Poor" if they are not meeting expectations.
* **employee_performance_appraisal_form_2** (<type>: text, <label>: feedback_form_2): This field is for providing specific, constructive feedback to the employee on their performance. Please be as detailed and specific as possible.
* **employee_performance_appraisal_form_3** (<type>: note, <label>: employee_performance_appraisal_form_3_label): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with the employee. Please use this space to provide any further thoughts or suggestions.

## Tips
* Be honest and specific with your feedback, and try to provide actionable suggestions for improvement.
* Keep your feedback focused on the employee's performance and behavior, rather than personal opinions or biases.
* Proofread your comments to ensure they are clear and concise.
* Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.
