# Submit Your Responses - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers on their satisfaction with a service or product. We use this feedback to understand their experiences and make improvements accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit your responses. You can skip any optional fields that are not relevant to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Your Responses Form** (`submit_your_responses_form`, text, required: no): This is the main form that collects your responses. It is a text field where you can type in your feedback. 
* **Customer Satisfaction Rating** (`customer_satisfaction_rating`, number, required: no): Provide a rating of your satisfaction with the service or product, using numbers 1-5 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **Customer Satisfaction Comment** (`customer_satisfaction_comment`, text, required: no): Add any comments you have about your experience with the service or product.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, required: no): Suggest any improvements you think we can make to our service or product.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required: no): Click this button to submit your responses and send them to us.
