# Health Clinic Lecture Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students who have attended a health clinic lecture. The feedback will help the clinic to identify areas of improvement and provide better services to its audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about the lecture's quality and your overall experience.
2. Provide any recommendations you may have for improving the clinic's services.
3. If you wish to be contacted by the clinic in the future, please provide your name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How good was the lecture?** (`lecture_quality`, text, optional):
	+ Please rate the quality of the lecture by providing a brief description of what you thought of the presentation.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional):
	+ Share your thoughts on your overall experience at the health clinic, including any positive or negative comments you may have.
* **What do you recommend?** (`recommendations`, text, optional):
	+ Suggest any improvements or changes you think the clinic could make to better serve its audience.
* **Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional):
	+ If you would like to be contacted by the clinic in the future, please provide your name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ If you would like to be contacted by the clinic, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional):
	+ If you would like to be contacted by the clinic, please provide your phone number.
