# Occasion Gift Bag Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have received an Occasion Gift Bag.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
2. Share your overall experience with the Occasion Gift Bag in the "Feedback Form" field.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, please add them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_form`, text, required): This is where you can share your thoughts about the Occasion Gift Bag. Please be as detailed as possible about your experience.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Please provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Additional Comments** (`occasion_gift_bag_feedback_form_extra`, text, optional): If you have any extra comments or thoughts about the Occasion Gift Bag, please add them here.
