# Customer Retention Strategy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the customer retention strategy of our company. It aims to gather feedback and ratings from customers to improve our customer service and overall customer experience. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us better understand your experience with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the fields as accurately as possible.
2. Be honest in your feedback and ratings.
3. Make sure to provide detailed explanations when necessary.
4. Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Feedback
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your experience with our company. This could be a positive or negative feedback, and it will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve on.

### Retention Strategy
* **Retention Strategy** (`retention_strategy`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate how effective you think our retention strategy is. You can select one or more of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair

### Satisfaction Level
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our product or service. A higher number indicates higher satisfaction.

### Loyalty Level
* **Loyalty Level** (`loyalty_level`, `number`, required: false): Rate how loyal you are to our company. A higher number indicates higher loyalty.

### Date Created
* **Date Created** (`date_created`, `date`, required: false): This is the date when you created your account or interacted with our company. Please fill in the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### Time Spent
* **Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `number`, required: false): Estimate the amount of time you've spent interacting with our company. This can be helpful for us to understand your engagement level.

### Customer Service Rating
* **Customer Service Rating** (`customer_service_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the quality of our customer service. You can select one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair

### Customer Experience Rating
* **Customer Experience Rating** (`customer_experience_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall customer experience. You can select one of the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair

### Customer Loyalty
* **Customer Loyalty** (`customer_loyalty`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you feel loyal to our company? You can select one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Date Updated
* **Date Updated** (`date_updated`, `date`, required: false): This is the date when you last interacted with our company. Please fill in the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
