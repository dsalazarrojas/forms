# employee_diversity_workshop_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants of the employee diversity workshop. Your input will help us improve future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the text box labeled "First Name".
2. Provide your email address in the text box labeled "Email".
3. Share your thoughts on the workshop in the text box labeled "Workshop Feedback".
4. Rate your overall experience by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Overall Experience" field.
5. Indicate your level of attendance by selecting one of the options in the "Attendance" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address here.
* **Workshop Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): Share your thoughts on the workshop in this field. This is a text area where you can write as much or as little as you like.
* **Overall Experience** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the options to indicate your level of attendance. Options are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your first name and email address accurately.
* Be as honest and detailed as possible when providing your feedback on the workshop.
* Select the correct number to rate your overall experience.
* Select the correct level of attendance to give us a clear picture of your participation.
