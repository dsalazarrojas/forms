# Design Evolution Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Design Evolution Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from users on the current design evolution process. This feedback is used to improve and refine the design process to better meet the needs of our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Take a few minutes to read the introduction and understand the purpose of this survey.
* Respond to each question truthfully, using the options provided or describing your thoughts in your own words.
* Answer each question in the spaces provided, making sure to save your progress as you go along.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction to the Design Evolution Feedback Survey (id: 1)** (`text`, optional):
	+ This section is an introduction to the design evolution feedback survey, explaining its purpose and what you can expect from the survey.
* **What do you think about the current design evolution process? (id: 2)** (`text`, optional):
	+ In this section, please share your thoughts and opinions about the current design evolution process. Be as specific as you can, and provide examples or details to help us understand your feedback.
* **Select one option that best describes your feedback (id: 3)** (`select_one`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to choose one option that best describes your feedback about the design evolution process. You can select one of the options provided or choose "I'm not sure".
* **Select multiple options that best describe your feedback (id: 4)** (`select_multiple`, optional):
	+ In this section, you can choose multiple options that best describe your feedback about the design evolution process.
* **When was your last feedback given? (id: 5)** (`date`, optional):
	+ Please provide the date of your last feedback given, if applicable.
* **What time was your last feedback given? (id: 6)** (`time`, optional):
	+ If you have provided feedback in the past, please provide the time of day when you gave it.
* **What type of design preferences do you have for the current design evolution process? (id: 7)** (`select_multiple`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to select multiple options that best describe your design preferences for the current design evolution process.
* **Demographic Information (id: 8)** (`text`, optional):
	+ In this section, please provide your demographic information for analysis purposes. This will help us understand who our users are and how we can better serve them.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and honest as possible when providing your feedback.
* You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the free text fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
