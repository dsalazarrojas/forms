# Kitchen Renovation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Renovation Feedback form is designed to collect information from customers about their experiences with a kitchen renovation project. This feedback will help us improve our services and provide better support to our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name to provide us with your identity.
2. Enter your Email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
3. Type in your Phone number, which is optional but recommended for us to contact you directly if needed.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with our services by selecting one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
5. Optionally, provide a Satisfaction Score to give a more detailed rating of your experience.
6. If you have referred someone to us, please fill in their First Name and Phone number so we can follow up with them.
7. Add any comments about your experience, if you have any.
8. Provide additional comments, if you have any.
9. Enter the Work Order Number of your renovation project, which is optional.
10. If applicable, fill in the name of your Project Manager and Project Contact.
11. Specify the Date of Completion of your project, which is a required field.
12. Optionally, enter the Time of Completion.
13. Enter the duration of your project, which is optional.
14. Enter the Total Cost of your project, which is optional.
15. Enter the date when you submitted this feedback, which is a required field.
16. Finally, type in your name as the person submitting this feedback, which is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Type in your phone number, which is recommended for us to contact you directly if needed.
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our services by selecting one of the options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Satisfaction Score** (`Satisfaction Score`, `number`, optional): Optionally, provide a Satisfaction Score to give a more detailed rating of your experience.
* **Referral Name** (`Referral Name`, `text`, optional): If you have referred someone to us, please fill in their First Name.
* **Referral Contact** (`Referral Contact`, `text`, optional): If you have referred someone to us, please type in their Phone number.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, optional): Add any comments about your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `note`, optional): Provide additional comments, if you have any.
* **Work Order Number** (`Work Order Number`, `text`, optional): Enter the Work Order Number of your renovation project, which is optional.
* **Project Manager** (`Project Manager`, `text`, optional): If applicable, fill in the name of your Project Manager.
* **Project Contact** (`Project Contact`, `text`, optional): If applicable, type in the name of your Project Contact.
* **Date of Completion** (`Date of Completion`, `date`, required): Enter the date when your project was completed.
* **Time of Completion** (`Time of Completion`, `time`, optional): Optionally, enter the time of completion.
* **Project Duration** (`Project Duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your project, which is optional.
* **Total Cost** (`Total Cost`, `number`, optional): Enter the total cost of your project, which is optional.
* **Submission Date** (`Submission Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you submitted this feedback.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required): Type in your name as the person submitting this feedback.
