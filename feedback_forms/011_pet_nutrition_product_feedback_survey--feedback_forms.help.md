# Pet Nutrition Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on pet nutrition products to help us improve their development and design for better pet health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the pet nutrition product you are providing feedback on.
2. Rate the product's effectiveness.
3. Provide feedback on your satisfaction with the product.
4. Share any health risks or concerns you have for your pet.
5. Describe any treatment outcomes you've experienced.
6. Confirm the end of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Nutrition Product** (`pet_nutrition_product_id`): Please enter the name of the pet nutrition product you are providing feedback on.
* **Pet Nutrition Product Rating** (`pet_nutrition_product_rating`): Rate the effectiveness of the product on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Pet Nutrition Product Satisfaction** (`pet_nutrition_product_satisfaction`): Select how satisfied you are with the product (e.g., Satisfied, Dissatisfied).
* **Pet Health Feedback** (`pet_health_feedback`): Select how satisfied you are with the product's impact on your pet's health (e.g., Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Pet Health Risk Factors** (`pet_health_risk_factors`): If you are aware of any health risks associated with the product, please describe them in detail.
* **Pet Health Concerns** (`pet_health_concerns`): If you have any concerns about your pet's health, please describe them in detail.
* **Pet Treatment Outcomes** (`pet_treatment_outcomes`): Select if you've experienced any positive or negative treatment outcomes with the product (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Pet Nutrition Product 2** (`pet_nutrition_product_id_2`): If you have feedback on a second pet nutrition product, please enter its name.
* **Survey End** (`survey_end`): Confirm the end of the survey by selecting a date.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the space provided.
* We appreciate your time and feedback, and it will help us improve pet nutrition products for better pet health and well-being.
