# Training Platform Usability Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect your opinions on the usability of the Training Platform. Your feedback will help us improve the platform and make it more user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. For multi-select questions, choose all that apply.
3. For text fields, provide brief answers to the questions.
4. Don't worry about grammar or spelling errors; we just want your honest feedback.
5. Once you've completed all the questions, click the "submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **training_platform_usability_poll_form** (Page 1): This field is for you to enter any general comments or thoughts about the platform.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **usability** (Page 2): How would you rate the overall usability of the platform?
	+ Type: select_one (choose 1-4)
	+ Options: 1 - Very Good, 2 - Good, 3 - Fair, 4 - Poor
	+ Required: No
* **content_relevance** (Page 3): How relevant is the content on the platform to you?
	+ Type: select_multiple (choose 1-4)
	+ Options: 1 - Very relevant, 2 - Somewhat relevant, 3 - Not very relevant, 4 - Not at all relevant
	+ Required: No
* **overall_satisfaction** (Page 4): How satisfied are you with the platform overall?
	+ Type: select_one (choose 1-5)
	+ Options: 1 - Very satisfied, 2 - Somewhat satisfied, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Somewhat dissatisfied, 5 - Very dissatisfied
	+ Required: No
* **suggestions** (Page 5): Do you have any suggestions for improving the platform?
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **email** (Page 6): Enter your email address (optional):
	+ Type: email
	+ Required: No
* **phone** (Page 7): Enter your phone number (optional):
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **comments** (Page 8): Add any additional comments you'd like to make:
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: No
* **additional_comments** (Page 9): Add any additional comments:
	+ Type: note
	+ Required: No
* **submit** (Page 10): Click this button to send your feedback.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
* **cancel** (Page 11): Click this button to cancel and exit the form.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: No
