# MRI Procedure Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) procedures. Your input will help our medical staff to better understand and improve the quality of care provided to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name, the date and time of your MRI procedure, and any comments about your experience.
2. Select your gender (Male, Female, or Other) and choose your preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or Address).
3. Choose the type of MRI you underwent (Open MRI, Closed MRI, or Other).
4. If applicable, enter the dose of contrast material used during your procedure.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
6. Finally, enter the name of the staff member who assisted you during your MRI procedure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (Text)**: Enter your name to identify you as the patient who underwent the MRI procedure.
* **Date of MRI (Date)**: Enter the date you underwent the MRI procedure.
* **Time of MRI (Time)**: Enter the time you underwent the MRI procedure.
* **Patient Gender (Select One)**: Choose your gender from the options provided.
* **Room Number (Text)**: Enter the room number where you underwent the MRI procedure.
* **MRI Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of MRI you underwent.
* **Dose Used (Text)**: If applicable, enter the dose of contrast material used during your procedure.
* **Comments (Note)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Patient Contact Info (Select Multiple)**: Choose your preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or Address).
* **Staff Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the staff member who assisted you during your MRI procedure.
