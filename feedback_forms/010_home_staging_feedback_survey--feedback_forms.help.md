# Home Staging Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the home staging service provided by our company. We value your opinion and would like to hear about your experience with our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback On Staged Property** (<code>1</code>): 
    * This field is where you can provide your feedback on the property that was staged. Please describe any issues that you experienced, positive or negative, with the service.
* **Rating** (<code>2</code>): 
    * This is an opportunity for you to rate your overall experience with our home staging service. You can choose a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Suggestions** (<code>3</code>): 
    * If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our service, please provide them in this field. This will help us to better understand your needs and make changes where needed.
* **Email** (<code>4</code>): 
    * This is your email address where you would like to be contacted if we need to discuss any further details about your experience or provide you with additional information.
* **Phone** (<code>5</code>): 
    * This is your phone number where you can be contacted if we need to discuss any further details about your experience or provide you with additional information.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your answers.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, feel free to contact us for clarification.
- We appreciate your feedback and look forward to hearing about your experience with our service.
