# Daycare Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from parents about their experience at a daycare center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions in the provided fields.
2. Make sure to provide your contact information (email and phone number).
3. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Comments" field.
4. Once you've completed the form, submit it to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daycare Parent Survey Form** (`daycare_parent_survey_form`): This is the main form where you'll be answering the questions.
* **What is your child's name** (`feedback_question_1`): Please enter your child's name for reference.
* **How many days have you been with this daycare center** (`feedback_question_2`): Please enter the number of days you've been using the daycare services.
* **What time of day does your child usually attend?** (`feedback_question_3`): Please choose the time of day when your child typically visits the daycare center.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of care?** (`feedback_question_4`): Please rate the quality of care using the provided scale (1-5).
* **Comments** (`feedback_question_5`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use this field.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the questions and provide your contact information to ensure we receive your feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
