# leadership_effectiveness_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership effectiveness questionnaire is designed to measure the proficiency of a leader across various domains, facilitating a thorough assessment of their performance and providing guidance for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by selecting the most accurate answer for each field, based on the leader's skills and behavior.
- For select_one fields, choose one of the provided options that best describes the leader's ability or tendency.
- For select_multiple fields, select all relevant options that apply to the leader's behavior or abilities.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Overall leadership skills** (`overall_leadership_skills`, number, optional): This question assesses the leader's general leadership abilities, such as vision, communication, and decision-making. Answer with a numerical value that represents the leader's skills level.
* **2. Strategic vision** (`strategic_vision`, select_one, optional): This question evaluates the leader's ability to define and pursue a clear, long-term vision for the organization. Choose the option that best describes their approach:
	+ Leadership Focus: Emphasizing leadership and people development.
	+ Strategic Vision: Defining a clear, long-term direction.
	+ Operational: Focusing on day-to-day execution.
	+ People Development: Prioritizing people development and growth.
* **3. Decision making** (`decision_making`, select_multiple, optional): This question assesses the leader's ability to make effective and decisive decisions. Select all options that apply:
	+ Effective and decisive: Decisions are well-informed and timely.
	+ Well-considered and collaborative: Decisions are made after thorough discussion and consideration.
	+ Impulsive and action-oriented: Decisions are made quickly, sometimes without fully considering alternatives.
	+ Not effective: Decisions are often poor or uninformed.
* **4. Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_one, optional): This question evaluates the leader's ability to work collaboratively with others. Choose one option that best describes their approach:
