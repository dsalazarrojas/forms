This form is designed to collect feedback from users about a specific lesson plan. It is meant to be completed by students or instructors who have experienced the lesson plan. The form is divided into five questions that ask about the structure and quality of the lesson plan, as well as the teacher's performance and the materials used. The form is optional, and users can choose to answer any or all of the questions, depending on their feedback. 

# Lesson Plan Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lesson Plan Feedback Form is a tool to help gather feedback on the effectiveness and quality of a lesson plan. This feedback can be used to improve the lesson plan and make it more effective for future students and instructors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as instructed. If you have no feedback or comments, you can leave the field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was this lesson plan?** (id: 1, name: lesson_plan_feedback, type: select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the option that best describes your overall experience with the lesson plan:
		- Excellent
		- Very Good
		- Neutral
		- Poor
		- Bad
* **What was the lesson plan structured?** (id: 2, name: lesson_plan_structure, type: select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all options that apply:
		- Well-organized
		- Disorganized
		- Too long
		- Too short
* **What was your experience with the teacher?** (id: 3, name: teacher_performance, type: select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the option that best describes your experience with the teacher:
		- Excellent
		- Very Good
		- Neutral
		- Poor
		- Bad
* **Do you have any feedback for the teacher?** (id: 4, name: feedback_to_teacher, type: text, required: false):
	+ Type any feedback or comments you have for the teacher.
* **Were the materials helpful?** (id: 5, name: lesson_plan_materials, type: select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the option that best describes your experience with the materials:
		- Yes
		- No

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- Try to be as objective and constructive as possible.
- If you have no feedback or comments, you can leave the field blank.
