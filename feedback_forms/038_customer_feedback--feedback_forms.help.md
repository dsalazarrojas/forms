# Customer Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with our company. It will help us identify areas of improvement and provide a better service. Please provide as much information as possible to help us serve you better.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Email**, and **Contact Number**.
2. Rate our **Product Rating** from 1 to 5.
3. Choose the **Department** you interacted with.
4. Enter the **Date of Visit** and **Time of Visit**.
5. Check if you would like a **Follow-up**.
6. Provide your **Additional Comments**.
7. Choose how you would rate our **Customer Feedback Score** from 1 to 5.
8. If you have any suggestions, type them in the **Customer Suggestion** field.
9. Finally, click on **Submit** to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Message** (`feedback_message`, optional): Tell us what's on your mind about your experience.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, optional, 1-5): Rate our product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, optional): Enter your contact number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Company** (`company_name`, optional): Enter the name of the company you represent or are associated with.
* **Department** (`department`, optional): Select the department you interacted with. This will help us direct your feedback to the right team.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, optional): Enter the date of your visit if you've been here before.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, optional): Enter the time of your visit if you've been here before.
* **Time of Call** (`time_of_call`, optional): Enter the time of your call, if applicable.
* **Time of Response** (`time_of_response`, optional): Enter the time of your response if you've responded to one of our calls.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Tell us more about your experience.
* **Customer Feedback Score** (`customer_feedback_score`, optional, 1-5): Rate your overall experience with us.
* **Customer Suggestion** (`customer_suggestion`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them here.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, optional): Check if you'd like a follow-up call.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to share.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter more about your experience.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, optional): Select the person or team member who will follow up with you.
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`, optional): This field is not editable and will be populated automatically.
* **Closed At** (`closed_at`, optional): This field is not editable and will be populated automatically.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, optional): This field is not editable and will be populated automatically.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, optional): This field is not editable and will be populated automatically.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, optional): This field is not editable and will be populated automatically.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, optional): This is a unique customer ID for internal use.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, optional): Select the person who submitted the form.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, optional): This is a unique form ID for internal use.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, optional): This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Form ID** (`customer_form_id`, optional): This is a unique customer form ID for internal use.

Note: The fields marked as optional are not required, but providing information for these fields will help us serve you better.
