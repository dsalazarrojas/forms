# virtual_doctor_consultation_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about their experience with the virtual doctor consultation service. Your input will help us improve our service and provide better care for our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible about your experience.
- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to use the "Any additional comments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, Text, Optional): Please enter the name of the provider you consulted with.
- **What was your experience like?** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Describe your experience with the virtual doctor consultation service.
- **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, Number, Optional): Rate your satisfaction with the care you received, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Provider Communication** (`provider_communication`, Number, Optional): Rate the communication skills of your healthcare provider, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Provider Listening Skills** (`provider_listening_skills`, Number, Optional): Rate the listening skills of your healthcare provider, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Provider Clinical Skills** (`provider_clinical_skills`, Number, Optional): Rate the clinical skills of your healthcare provider, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Schedule Conflict** (`schedule_conflict`, Text, Optional): If scheduling was a conflict, please describe the issue.
- **Follow-up Instructions** (`followup_instructions`, Text, Optional): Were you provided with clear follow-up instructions?
- **Telehealth Equipment** (`telehealth_equipment`, Number, Optional): Rate your ease of access and use of the telehealth equipment, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Technology Support** (`technology_support`, Number, Optional): Rate the support provided by our telehealth technology, on a scale of 1-5.
- **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, Select One, Optional): Would you recommend this service to a friend? Select from "I would definitely recommend", "I would recommend", "I am unsure", or "I would not recommend".
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Text, Optional): Do you have any suggestions for improving our service?
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Any additional comments or feedback about your experience?

Note: All fields are optional, but your feedback is valuable to us, even if it's just a few sentences. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
