# 360 Degree Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather comprehensive and constructive feedback on an individual's performance and areas for development from multiple stakeholders.

## How to Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, answering all required questions.
2. Rate the individual's performance on a scale of 1-10 for various skills and attributes.
3. Select any strengths and weaknesses that you have observed.
4. Provide specific examples or suggest areas for development.
5. Make a recommendation for the individual's role progression, if applicable.
6. Choose whether you would like to share your identity with the individual being reviewed or remain anonymous.
7. Add any additional comments or context to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your full name**: Leave blank if you prefer to submit anonymously.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address if you prefer to be contacted, or leave blank for anonymity.
* **Relationship to subject**: Select the option that best describes your working relationship with the individual being reviewed.
* **Employee being reviewed (full name)**: Enter the person’s legal or preferred full name.
* **Role / job title of the person being reviewed**: Enter their current role or job title.
* **Department / team**: Select the department or team the person belongs to.
* **Date of this review**: Select the date of this review.
* **Overall performance rating**: Rate the individual's overall performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Teamwork / collaboration**: Rate the individual's teamwork and collaboration skills on a scale of 1-10.
* **Communication skills**: Rate the individual's communication skills on a scale of 1-10.
* **Problem solving & decision making**: Rate the individual's problem-solving and decision-making skills on a scale of 1-10.
* **Key strengths observed**: Select any strengths you have observed in the individual's performance.
* **Areas for development**: Select any areas where the individual could improve.
* **Specific examples or situations**: Provide specific examples or situations that demonstrate the individual's performance or areas for development.
* **Suggestions for development**: Suggest any training, coaching, or other support that could help the individual grow.
* **Recommendation for role progression**: Make a single recommendation for the individual's role progression.
* **Disclosure preference**: Choose whether you would like to share your identity with the individual being reviewed or remain anonymous.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any further information that could help interpret this feedback.
* **Anonymity preference**: Choose whether you would like to share your identity with the individual being reviewed or remain anonymous.
* **Additional comments or context**: Provide any additional comments or context that may help the individual being reviewed understand this feedback.
