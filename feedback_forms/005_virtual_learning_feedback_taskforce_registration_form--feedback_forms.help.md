# Virtual Learning Feedback Taskforce Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to register unique and actionable feedback from Virtual Learning Taskforce participants. This form helps to:

* Gather valuable insights from participants about their experiences with Virtual Learning
* Ensure that all submitted feedback is unique and non-duplicating
* Allow stakeholders to review and address the feedback

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your first name, last name, and any other required information.
2. Enter your email address to help us contact you with updates on our actions.
3. Provide your job title and organization to give context to your feedback.
4. Enter your work email to ensure we can reach you directly.
5. Type your feedback and additional notes to help us understand your concerns or suggestions.
6. Click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates on our actions.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number, if you would like us to contact you over the phone.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your job title to give context to your feedback.
* **Organization** (Organization, text, required): Enter the name of your organization to give context to your feedback.
* **Work Email** (Work Email, email, required): Enter your work email address so we can contact you directly.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required): Type your feedback and concerns, and any suggestions you have for improvement.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, optional): If you have any additional information or context for your feedback, enter it here.
