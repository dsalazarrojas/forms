# Thanksgiving Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Thanksgiving Contact Form is used to collect contact information and messages about guests attending the Thanksgiving event. This form helps us keep track of the guests and their attendants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information about yourself and the guests you're submitting.
2. Ensure that the email addresses and phone numbers are correct and up to date.
3. Enter a message about each guest you're submitting, including any relevant details or preferences.
4. Repeat this process for each guest you'd like to include in the event's guest list.
5. Click submit to send the form.
