# Geology Course Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about the geology course you're taking or have taken. Your feedback will help instructors understand what's working well and what areas need improvement, allowing them to create a more engaging and effective learning experience for you and your peers.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill in the required and optional fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **<title>** (Geology Course Feedback Survey) - This is the title of the form, providing a clear understanding of its purpose.
* **Course Ratings** (Course Ratings, text field, optional): This section is where you can rate the course overall, providing a general assessment of your experience. You can provide a brief description of your thoughts on the course content, teaching methods, and overall quality.
* **Instructor Evaluations** (Instructor Evaluations, text field, optional): This section is designed to give feedback on your instructor's performance, including their teaching style, effectiveness, and interaction with you and your peers. Be honest and specific about your thoughts on what worked well and what didn't.
* **Student Suggestions** (Student Suggestions, text field, optional): If you have any ideas or suggestions on how to improve the course, this is the place to share them. Your input can help shape the learning experience and make the course more engaging and enjoyable for everyone.

## Tips
* Be honest and respectful in your feedback, as it will help create a constructive and supportive learning environment.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback, making it more valuable and actionable.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, simply type 'N/A' or 'Not Applicable' to indicate you didn't find it relevant.
* If you need more information or clarification on any field, you can ask your instructor or contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
