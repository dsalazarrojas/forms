# Contact Page Blue And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide contact information, including their name, email, phone number, and other relevant details, to initiate contact with the organization. The form also allows users to select a topic of inquiry and an urgency level for the contact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Get In Touch" button to initiate contact.
2. Fill in the "Your Name" field with your full name as requested.
3. Enter a valid "Email Address" to ensure we can reach out to you.
4. If applicable, provide a "Phone Number" for further communication.
5. Select a suitable "Topic" from the given options (e.g., Product Information, Service Inquiry, Support Request, etc.).
6. Indicate the "Urgency Level" required, such as Not Urgent, Somewhat Urgent, Urgent, or Critical.
7. Enter the details of your "Message" in the provided text area.
8. If necessary, choose a "Best Time to Contact" from the options (optional).
9. If you have previously contacted us, select "True" or "False" accordingly.
10. If relevant, provide a "Reference Number" for your contact.
11. Attach any supporting document(s) if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Get In Touch** (Header): This is the title of the contact page.
* **Your Name** (Name, Text, Required): Please enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email, Text, Required): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Optional): If you prefer contact through phone, enter your phone number.
* **Topic** (Topic, Select One, Required): Choose a suitable topic from the given options (e.g., Product Information, Service Inquiry, etc.).
* **Message** (Message, Text, Required): Provide a clear and concise message with your query or concern.
* **Urgency Level** (Urgency Level, Select One, Required): Select the urgency level that best describes your situation (e.g., Not Urgent, Somewhat Urgent, Urgent, Critical).
* **Best Time to Contact** (Best Time to Contact, Select One, Optional): Choose a time that suits you best for communication (optional).
* **Have You Contacted Us Before** (Previous Contact, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you have previously contacted us, if applicable.
* **Reference Number** (Reference Number, Text, Optional): If applicable, provide a reference number for your contact.
* **Attach File** (Attachment, Text, Optional): Attach any supporting document(s) if needed (optional).
