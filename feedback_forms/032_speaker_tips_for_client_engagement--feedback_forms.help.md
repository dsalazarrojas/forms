# Speaker Tips For Client Engagement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Speaker Tips For Client Engagement" form is designed to gather information and feedback from speakers about their experiences and tips for engaging with clients in a professional setting. This form will help event organizers, clients, and speakers improve their relationships and communication. By filling out this form, you'll contribute to a valuable resource for the speakers' community and help shape the way we connect and interact with each other.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Speaker participation" to indicate whether you were actively engaged with the client.
2. Provide a brief description of the "Event objectives" that you had in mind before the meeting.
3. Choose one or multiple answers for "Speaker participation" from the options "Yes", "No", and "Partially" to indicate your level of engagement.
4. Describe your "Speaker objectives" for the meeting or engagement.
5. Share any tips, strategies, or recommendations you found helpful during the meeting or engagement.
6. Offer recommendations or suggestions for the client or event organizers.
7. Provide feedback on your overall experience with the form.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
