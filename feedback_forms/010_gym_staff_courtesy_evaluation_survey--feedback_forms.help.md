# Gym Staff Courtesy Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the gym staff. Your input will help us understand our strengths and weaknesses and improve our services to provide a better experience for all our members.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below, providing your honest and constructive feedback.
2. The form is divided into several sections. Each section is designed to gather feedback on a specific aspect of your experience with the gym staff.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome Message**: This is a text field where you can share any initial comments or thoughts about your visit.
* **How do you rate the staff's interaction with you?**: This question is meant to evaluate how well the staff interacted with you during your visit. Please rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How do you rate the staff's communication style?**: This question assesses how effectively the staff communicates with you. Please rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Cleanliness**: This field is for evaluating the cleanliness and overall condition of our facilities.
* **How would you rate the facilities provided?**: This question is intended to gauge how well our facilities meet your needs and expectations.
* **Overall Experience**: This field allows you to rate your overall experience at the gym, including the staff's service and facilities.
* **Improvement Areas**: Here, you can select all areas where we can improve, such as Staff Communication, Staff Timeliness, Facilities, or Parking.
* **Staff Courtesy**: This question asks you to rate the staff's courtesy and friendliness.
* **Would you like to contact us for more info?**: Please indicate whether you'd like to get in touch with us regarding anything.
* **Any additional feedback?**: This is a text field for you to share any further comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Email**: Please enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted back.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted back.
* **Name**: Please enter your name, so we can address you personally.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can skip any questions that don't apply to you.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
