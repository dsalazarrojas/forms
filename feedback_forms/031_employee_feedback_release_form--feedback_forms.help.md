# Employee Feedback Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Feedback Release Form is a tool used to gather feedback from employees on the completion of projects or tasks. It's an essential tool for improving work processes, identifying areas of improvement, and recognizing the quality of work done by team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate option for "Employee Feedback" to indicate whether the project was completed successfully or not.
2. Answer the two feedback questions, providing as much detail as possible to help the team understand your experience.
3. Enter your name and job title to help us identify who you are and what you do.
4. Enter your contact email and phone number so we can reach out to you for further questions or discussions.
5. Choose the date and time of completion to keep track of project timelines.
6. Add any additional notes you have about the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, select_one, required): This field asks you to indicate whether the project was completed successfully or not.
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`, text, optional): This is the first question where you can provide feedback about your experience with the project. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`, text, optional): This is the second question where you can provide feedback about your experience with the project. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you have a department or team that worked on this project, please enter it here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as it appears on your employee ID or badge.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email so we can reach out to you for further questions or discussions.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you for further questions or discussions.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): This is not a required field, but you can use it to submit additional information or comments if needed.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`, date, optional): Choose the date when the project was completed.
* **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, optional): Choose the time when the project was completed.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments you may have about the project.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out this form accurately and honestly.
* Only submit this form once for each project, and avoid submitting multiple forms for the same project.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
