# public_library_usage_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation data from public library users to help library administrators assess the effectiveness of their services and make data-driven decisions to enhance user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Go to the first page and select the "Public Library Usage" option that best describes your interaction with the library.
2. On the second page, choose the services you used during your visit or interaction with the library.
3. On subsequent pages, provide your suggestions for improvement, contact information, and any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Library Usage** (Page 1, id: 1)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Public Library Usage
  * This field allows you to describe your interaction with the library. Please select the option that best fits your experience.

* **Services Used** (Page 2, id: 2)
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Label: Services Used
  * Select all the services you used during your visit or interaction with the library. You can choose multiple services from the list.

* **Suggestions** (Page 3, id: 3)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Suggestions
  * This field is for providing any suggestions you may have for improving the library services.

* **Contact Method** (Page 4, id: 4)
  * Type: Select One
  * Label: Contact Method
  * Please select the method you would prefer to use to contact the library in case of an inquiry or question.

* **Contact Person** (Page 5, id: 5)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Contact Person
  * If you selected "Contact Form" or "Email" as your preferred contact method, please provide the name of the person you would like to contact.

* **Contact Email** (Page 6, id: 6)
  * Type: Email
  * Label: Contact Email
  * If you selected "Contact Form" or "Phone Call" as your preferred contact method, please provide your email address.

* **Contact Phone** (Page 7, id: 7)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Contact Phone
  * If you selected "Phone Call" as your preferred contact method, please provide your phone number.

* **Contact Notes** (Page 8, id: 8)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Contact Notes
  * If you selected "Other" as your preferred contact method, please provide any additional notes about your contact preferences.

* **Other Contact Method** (Page 9, id: 9)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Other Contact Method
  * This field is for providing any other contact method you would like to use (e.g. WhatsApp, etc.).

* **Other Contact Email** (Page 10, id: 10)
  * Type: Email
  * Label: Other Contact Email
  * If you selected "Other" as your preferred contact method, please provide your email address.

* **Other Contact Phone** (Page 11, id: 11)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Other Contact Phone
  * If you selected "Other" as your preferred contact method, please provide your phone number.

* **Other Public Library Usage** (Page 12, id: 13)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Other Public Library Usage
  * If none of the provided options fit your experience, please describe your interaction with the library.

* **Other Services Used** (Page 13, id: 14)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Other Services Used
  * If none of the provided services fit your experience, please select any other services you used during your visit or interaction.

* **Other Suggestions** (Page 14, id: 15)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Other Suggestions
  * If none of the provided suggestions fit your idea, please provide any other suggestions you have.

* **Additional Comments** (Page 15, id: 16)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Additional Comments
  * If you have any other comments or concerns, please provide them in this field.

* **Additional Contact Method** (Page 16, id: 17)
  * Type: Select One
  * Label: Additional Contact Method
  * Please select the method you would prefer to use to contact the library in case of an inquiry or question.

* **Additional Public Library Usage** (Page 17, id: 18)
  * Type: Text
  * Label: Additional Public Library Usage
  * If you selected "Other" as your preferred contact method, please describe your interaction with the library.

## Tips

* Please provide clear and concise answers to each question.
* If you selected "Other" as an option, please provide a brief explanation.
* Remember to check your answers for any typos or grammar errors before submission.
* If you have any difficulties or need further clarification, contact the library administration for assistance.
