# Packaging Design Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packaging Design Feedback Questionnaire is a tool used by the design team to collect feedback on our packaging designs. This form helps us understand the design's effectiveness and gather insights to improve our products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be as detailed as possible when filling out each field.
3. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the `feedback` field at the end of the form.
4. Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Each field is designed to gather specific information from you about the packaging design. Below is a brief explanation of each field:

* **product-information (id: 2)**: This field is to provide information about the product being packaged. Please input the number that best describes the product category.
* **color-scheme (id: 3)**: Describe the color scheme of the packaging in the text field.
* **typography (id: 4)**: Select all the typographical options that you feel are most appealing for the packaging design.
* **packaging-type (id: 5)**: Select the packaging type that best suits the product. You may choose more than one option if necessary.
* **material (id: 6)**: Describe the material used for the packaging in the text field.
* **packaging-size (id: 7)**: Provide the packaging size of the product.
* **product-name (id: 8)**: Input the name of the product being packaged.
* **packaging-image (id: 9)**: Input any additional comments or observations about the packaging image.
* **overall-satisfaction (id: 10)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the design using the number scale provided.
* **feedback (id: 11)**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in this text field.

## Tips
- Be honest in your responses, as they will help us create the best packaging design for our customers.
- If the product you are providing feedback on is not a packaging design, but rather the product inside the packaging, please let us know so we can direct your feedback to the correct team.
- If you are having trouble with any of the fields, please contact the design team for assistance.
