# Social Work Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Work Feedback Form is a tool used by the organization to collect feedback from clients about their experiences with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question as thoroughly as possible.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Use the text fields to provide as much detail as needed.
4. For the feedback questions (Service Provided, Service Recipient, What did we do well?, What could we do better?, Do you have any suggestions?), provide a brief but detailed answer.
5. For the Select One field (Feedback Score), choose one of the options provided.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Provided** (*required*): Describe the service you received from our organization.
* **Service Recipient** (*required*): Identify yourself as the recipient of the service.
* **What did we do well?** (*required*): Provide feedback on what we did well during your interaction with our organization.
* **What could we do better?** (*required*): Suggest areas where we could improve our service.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (*required*): Share any additional ideas or suggestions you have for our organization.
* **Service Recipient (other)** (*optional*): If you are not the recipient of the service, please provide the name and contact information of the recipient.
* **Email Address** (*required*): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (*required*): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with updates or follow-up questions.
* **Date Visited** (*optional*): If you can remember, please enter the date of your visit.
* **Time Visited** (*required*): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Staff Member** (*required*): Identify the staff member you interacted with.
* **Feedback Score** (*required*): Choose how satisfied you are with our service. Choose 'Yes' if you were satisfied, or 'No' if you were not satisfied.
* **Comments** (*optional*): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them here.
* **Submit** (*optional*): This button will submit your feedback form when you are ready.
* **Please enter a brief message** (*required*): Please enter a brief message to provide more context or details about your feedback.

Note: The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
