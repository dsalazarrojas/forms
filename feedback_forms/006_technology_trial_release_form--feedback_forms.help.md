# Technology Trial Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for technology reviewers within a company to provide feedback on a technology trial or release. It is a tool to gather input from internal stakeholders to help us better understand their thoughts, comments, and suggestions on the technology being evaluated. This form is not for external use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with your information, making sure to provide accurate and honest feedback.
2. Review and select the correct technology category that best describes the technology being evaluated.
3. Enter your company size, which is the number of employees in your organization.
4. Provide your feedback on the technology, and if necessary, include additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your role in your company?** (`technology_trial_release_form`, text, required): This field is to identify your role within the company to help us better understand your perspective on the technology.
* **Company Name** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your company to associate your feedback with your organization.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is for your first name to help us personalize our communication with you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field is for your last name to personalize our communication with you.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title to help us understand your position within the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-7890.
* **Which technology category are we evaluating?** (`technology_category`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes the technology being evaluated. Options include:
	+ Audio/Video
	+ IT & Networking
	+ Software Development
	+ Web Development
	+ AI/ML
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, number, required): Enter the number of employees in your organization.
* **What do you think about this technology?** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide your honest feedback on the technology being evaluated.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us understand your thoughts on the technology.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [insert email or contact info].
