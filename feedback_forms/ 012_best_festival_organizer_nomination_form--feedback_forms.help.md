# Best Festival Organizer Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather feedback on our festival organizers. Your participation will aid us in understanding what went well and areas for improvement, ensuring future events are more enjoyable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately, paying close attention to each section.
* You can use the "Back" button to go back to a previous page if you made a mistake.
* Use your real email address for the contact information to receive responses from our team.
* If you need help with any part of the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (`full_name`, text, required):** Please enter your legal name exactly as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address (`email`, email, required):** Enter a valid email address for us to respond to any questions or follow up about your feedback.
* **Phone Number (`phone`, text, not required):** Include your country code if you're outside our local region.
* **Date (`reference_date`, date, required):** Select the most relevant date for your feedback, for instance, a specific festival date or a period of feedback.
* **Primary Selection (`primary_selection`, select one, required):** Choose the option that best reflects your experience with the festival organizers.
* **Quantity or Rating (`quantity_or_rating`, number, not required):** Enter a whole number to indicate the level of satisfaction or quantity of a specific aspect.
* **Additional Details (`additional_details`, text, not required):** Provide any extra information that will help us understand your feedback better.
* **Agreement (`agreement`, select multiple, required):** Select all that apply from the options provided to confirm your agreement.
* **Estimated Value (`estimated_value`, number, not required):** Use decimal format if you're estimating a monetary value.
* **Preferred Time (`preferred_time`, time, not required):** Enter a time if scheduling is an essential part of your feedback.
* **Final Comments or Notes (`final_comments`, text, not required):** Share any last thoughts or remarks you would like to add.

Note: This form is intended to gather honest and constructive feedback to improve our festival organization. Your input will help us create a better experience for everyone involved.
