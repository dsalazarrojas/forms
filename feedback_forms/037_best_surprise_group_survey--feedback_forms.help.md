# Best Surprise Group Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals about their surprise event experience. It consists of several sections, each focusing on different aspects of the event. By providing feedback, you're helping us understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing us to improve future surprise events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through each section carefully and answer the questions based on your actual experience.
2.  Fill out all required fields. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  If you're unsure about any question, try to answer based on your best recollection.
4.  Be as detailed as possible in your answers, as your feedback is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name** (`respondent_full_name`, text, \*): Please enter your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
*   **Your Email Address** (`respondent_email_addr`, email, \*): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Event or Surprise Name** (`surprise_event_name`, text, \*): Describe the event or surprise you're providing feedback for.
*   **Date of Event** (`event_date_calendar`, date, \*): Enter the date of the event.
*   **On a scale of 1 to 10, how surprised were you** (`surprise_intensity_scale`, select\_one, \*): Rate your level of surprise during the event.
*   **Did you have any prior suspicions** (`prior_suspicion_level`, select\_one, \*): Be honest about whether you suspected anything before the surprise.
*   **What was your initial reaction** (`initial_reaction_desc`, text, \*): Describe your first thoughts or words when you realized the surprise.
*   **Favorite part or moment of the surprise** (`favorite_moment_desc`, text, \*): Share what stood out to you the most during the surprise.
*   **How well was the secret kept** (`secret\_keeping\_rating`, select\_one, \*): Rate how well the surprise was kept secret from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Quality of Group Coordination** (`group_coordination_rating`, select\_one, \*): Rate the planning and execution of the event from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Venue and Location Suitability** (`venue_suitability_rating`, select\_one, \*): Rate the venue and location from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Food and Beverage Quality** (`hospitality_quality_rating`, select\_one, \*): If applicable, rate the food and beverage quality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **One thing that could have made the surprise even better** (`surprise_better_suggestion`, text, \*): Share your ideas for improvement.
*   **Any logistical hiccups you noticed** (`logistical_hiccups_desc`, text, \*): Describe any small issues that didn't go perfectly.
*   **Suggestions for future events** (`future_event_suggestions`, text, \*): Share your ideas for what we could do differently next time.
*   **Any other comments for the organizers** (`organizer_additional_feedback`, text, \*): Express your appreciation or provide specific notes for the organizers.
*   **Date of Feedback** (`feedback_submission_date`, date, \*): Enter the date you're submitting your feedback.
