# Christmas Wish Form 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about holiday wishes, traditions, and gratitude. It is meant to be a fun and engaging way for users to share their holiday spirit with others.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Full Name" field. This is a required field and will be used to identify you in the results.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This is an optional field, but it will allow you to receive email updates or notifications if you wish.
3. Select your age group from the "Age Group" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the category of your holiday wish from the "Wish Category" dropdown menu.
5. Select who the wish is for from the "Who Is This Wish For" dropdown menu.
6. Give your wish a title in the "Wish Title" field.
7. Share your holiday wish in the "Your Christmas Wish" field.
8. Rate the importance of your wish from the "How Important Is This Wish" dropdown menu.
9. Indicate if you have ever had a wish come true before from the "Has This Wish Come True Before" dropdown menu.
10. Describe what you would do if your wish came true in the "What Would You Do If Wish Came True" field.
11. Choose whether you would like to share your wish with others from the "Share Your Wish With Others" dropdown menu.
12. Select whether you want to inspire others with your wish from the "Do You Want to Inspire Others" dropdown menu.
13. Share a favorite Christmas memory in the "Favorite Christmas Memory" field.
14. Share a favorite holiday tradition in the "Favorite Christmas Tradition" field.
15. Select whether you want to share your wish with others, either privately or publicly.
16. Share a message to the world in the "Message to the World" field.
17. Rate how you found this form from the "How Did You Find This Form" dropdown menu.
18. Choose whether you want to join the holiday newsletter from the "Join Holiday Newsletter" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`wisher_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be known.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to receive updates or notifications.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age range from 12 to 65 or older.
* **Wish Category** (`wish_category`, select_one, required): Choose the type of wish you are sharing, such as Personal, Family, or World Peace.
* **Who Is This Wish For** (`wish_for`, select_one, required): Select who the wish is for, such as yourself, your family, or someone special.
* **Wish Title** (`wish_title`, text, required): Give your wish a title or name.
* **Your Christmas Wish** (`your_wish`, text, required): Share your holiday wish with the world.
* **How Important Is This Wish** (`wish_importance`, select_one, required): Rate the importance of your wish as Very important, Somewhat important, Nice to have, or Just for fun.
* **Has This Wish Come True Before** (`wish_come_true_before`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have ever had a wish come true before.
* **What Would You Do If Wish Came True** (`what_would_you_do`, text, optional): Describe what you would do if your wish came true.
* **Wish Details** (`section_wisher_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional details about your wish.
* **Share Your Wish With Others** (`share_with_others`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to share your wish privately or publicly.
* **Do You Want to Inspire Others** (`inspire_others`, select_one, required): Select whether you want to inspire others with your wish.
* **Favorite Christmas Memory** (`christmas_memory`, text, optional): Share a special memory.
* **Favorite Christmas Tradition** (`christmas_tradition`, text, optional): Share a favorite holiday tradition.
* **Message to the World** (`message_to_world`, text, optional): Share a message with the world.
* **How Did You Find This Form** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Rate how you found this form.
* **Join Holiday Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to join the holiday newsletter to receive updates and inspiration.
