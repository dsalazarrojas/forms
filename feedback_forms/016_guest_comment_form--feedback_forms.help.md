# Guest Comment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Comment Form is a tool for gathering feedback from guests. This form helps us understand their opinions, thoughts, and experiences with our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your guest name if you feel comfortable doing so.
2. Provide your comment, a brief description of your experience.
3. Choose a rating for your comment.
4. Select the rating value you'd like to associate with your rating.
5. If applicable, specify a date and time for the feedback.
6. If you have a preferred email address, enter it for contact purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. Choose the category related to your comment.
9. Select the department(s) your comment applies to.
10. Finally, select the type of comment you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as a guest for our records.
• **Comment** (`comment`, Text, Optional): Provide your comment for our team to read and learn from.
• **Rating** (`rating`, Number, Optional): Select a rating for your comment.
• **Rating Value** (`rating_value`, Number, Optional): Select the value associated with your rating.
• **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter a date if you'd like to specify when the event occurred.
• **Time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Enter a time if you'd like to specify when the event occurred.
• **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
• **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
• **Category** (`category`, Select One, Optional): Choose the category related to your comment.
• **Department** (`department`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the department(s) your comment applies to.
• **Comment Type** (`comment_type`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of comment you'd like to provide.
