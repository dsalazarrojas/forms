# Discussion Guideline Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help us evaluate the effectiveness of the discussion guidelines. Please answer the questions as honestly as possible to provide us with valuable feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page of the discussion guidelines and answer the questions that follow.
2. Answer each question as truthfully as possible, providing your honest assessment of the guidelines.
3. Make sure to save your progress and submit the form at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Discussion Guideline** (`discussion_guideline_survey_form`): This is the main section of the form where you will be providing your overall opinion on the guidelines. Please type your thoughts and observations in this field.
* **Overall Quality** (`overall_quality_rating`): Please rate the overall quality of the guidelines on a scale from 1-3, where 1 is "not good" and 3 is "very good".
* **Clarity** (`clarity_rating`): Rate how clear and easy to understand the guidelines are on a scale from 1-3, where 1 is "not clear" and 3 is "very clear".
* **Usefulness** (`usefulness_rating`): Rate how useful the guidelines are in helping you understand the discussion on a scale from 1-3, where 1 is "not useful" and 3 is "very useful".
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have on the guidelines.
* **Rating Scale 1** (`rating_scale_1`): Choose all the rating scales that apply to your feedback. Please select all that are relevant to your assessment.
* **Rating Scale 2** (`rating_scale_2`): Choose all the rating scales that apply to your feedback. Please select all that are relevant to your assessment.
* **Rating Scale 3** (`rating_scale_3`): Choose all the rating scales that apply to your feedback. Please select all that are relevant to your assessment.
