# Veterinary Staff Performance Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Staff Performance Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from staff members about their performance and work environment. This form is meant to help management understand the overall satisfaction and engagement of their team, and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the staff member whose performance you are evaluating from the list of available options.
2. Choose the type of feedback you would like to provide (Formal or Informal).
3. Rate your feedback score out of 5.
4. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
5. Enter your email address to receive a confirmation email.
6. Select the date you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Staff Member**: This field allows you to choose the staff member whose performance you are evaluating. Please select the correct staff member from the list.
* **Feedback Type**: This field allows you to choose the type of feedback you would like to provide. Please select either "Formal" for more formal feedback or "Informal" for general feedback.
* **Feedback Score**: This field is a rating system from 1 to 5, where 1 means "Not Satisfied" and 5 means "Extremely Satisfied".
* **Feedback Comments**: This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Submit**: This field is a confirmation field that will send the form once you are finished.
* **Email**: This field is your email address, where you will receive a confirmation email.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is automatically populated with the current date and time.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback.
* Be specific about your experiences and suggestions.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please raise them in the comments section.
* If you have any questions, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
