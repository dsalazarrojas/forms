# Information Completeness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the quality and relevance of a particular topic or dataset. It collects feedback on various aspects of the information, including its clarity, thoroughness, usefulness, and accuracy. This feedback will help us identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the **Main Information** field with a brief description of the topic or dataset.
2. Select the relevant options for **info_type**, indicating the type of information being assessed.
3. Choose the source of the information for **info_source**.
4. Enter the date and time of your assessment for **date** and **time** (if applicable).
5. Provide a note or comment for **note** (if necessary).
6. Enter your email and phone number for **email** and **phone** (if you want to receive feedback).
7. Press **Submit** to complete the form.
8. You will be returned to the **Back** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Information** (`main_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of the topic or dataset being assessed.
* **info_type** (`info_type`, select multiple, required): Choose the type of information being assessed (e.g., data, report, document).
* **info_source** (`info_source`, select one, required): Select the source of the information (e.g., internal, external, public).
* **date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of your assessment (if applicable).
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your assessment (if applicable).
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide a comment or note about the assessment.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for feedback (if desired).
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for feedback (if desired).
* **info_clarity** (`info_clarity`, select one, required): Assess the clarity of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_thoroughness** (`info_thoroughness`, select one, required): Assess the thoroughness of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_usefulness** (`info_usefulness`, select one, required): Assess the usefulness of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_relevance** (`info_relevance`, select one, required): Assess the relevance of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_accuracy** (`info_accuracy`, select one, required): Assess the accuracy of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_precision** (`info_precision`, select one, required): Assess the precision of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_completeness** (`info_completeness`, select one, required): Assess the completeness of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_timeliness** (`info_timeliness`, select one, required): Assess the timeliness of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_quantitative** (`info_quantitative`, select one, required): Assess the quantitativeness of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_sufficiency** (`info_sufficiency`, select one, required): Assess the sufficiency of the information (High, Medium, Low).
* **info_cohesion** (`info_cohesion`, select one, required): Assess the cohesion of the information (High, Medium, Low).
