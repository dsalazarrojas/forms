# Digital Interactive Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users about their digital interactive experience. Its purpose is to collect qualitative and quantitative data to identify areas of improvement and opportunities for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the appropriate option for each field and provide any necessary information. You can submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

* **Educator** (`educator`, text, optional): Enter the name or title of the educator related to the event.
