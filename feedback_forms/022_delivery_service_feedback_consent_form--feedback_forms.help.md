# Delivery Service Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our delivery service. It helps us identify areas for improvement and ensures we are providing the best possible service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below:
- Answer each question carefully and provide as much detail as needed.
- For the 'delivery service improvement' field, select 'True' if you have suggestions for how we can improve our service.
- For the 'contact number' field, enter your contact number where you can be reached for further discussion.
- For the 'email address' field, enter your email address where you can be reached for further discussion.
- The 'feedback details' field is where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience with our delivery service.
- For the 'consent' field, you are asked to confirm whether you consent to our processing your personal data for feedback purposes.
- For the 'date' field, select the date you would like to receive the feedback report.
- For the 'time' field, select the time you would like to receive the feedback report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback** (<name>Customer Feedback</name>, <type>Text</type>, <required>False</required>): This field is where you can enter your overall feedback about your experience with our delivery service.
* **Delivery Service Improvement** (<name>Delivery Service Improvement</name>, <type>Select One</type>, <required>False</required>): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our delivery service, please select 'True' from this field.
* **Contact Number** (<name>Contact Number</name>, <type>Text</type>, <required>False</required>): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further discussion.
* **Email Address** (<name>Email Address</name>, <type>Email</type>, <required>False</required>): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further discussion.
* **Feedback Details** (<name>Feedback Details</name>, <type>Text</type>, <required>False</required>): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience with our delivery service.
* **Consent** (<name>Consent</name>, <type>Select Multiple</type>, <required>False</required>): Confirm that you consent to our processing your personal data for feedback purposes.
* **Date** (<name>Date</name>, <type>Date</type>, <required>False</required>): Select the date you would like to receive the feedback report.
