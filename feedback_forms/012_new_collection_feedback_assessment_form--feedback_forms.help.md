# New Collection Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Collection Feedback Assessment Form is used to gather feedback from users about a new collection. This feedback will help us better understand user opinions and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a **new_collection_id** if you have one to link your feedback to the specific collection.
2. Type in the **collection_name** of the new collection you are providing feedback on.
3. Share the **collection_image_url** of the new collection, if available.
4. Answer the three question questions:
	* How likely is it that customers will purchase a product with this collection? (Select one of the options: Likelihood of purchasing, Somewhat likely, Not likely)
	* How does this collection meet your expectations? (Select one of the options: Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Fails to meet expectations)
	* How would you rate the quality of this collection? (Select one of the options: High, Medium, Low)
5. Add any additional comments or thoughts about the collection in the **additional_comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_collection_id** (Number, Not required): Enter a unique identifier for the new collection.
* **collection_name** (Text, Not required): Enter the name of the new collection.
* **collection_image_url** (Text, Not required): Enter the URL of an image representing the new collection.
* **How likely is it that customers will purchase a product with this collection?** (Select multiple, Not required): Choose one or more of the following options: Likelihood of purchasing, Somewhat likely, Not likely.
* **How does this collection meet your expectations?** (Select multiple, Not required): Choose one or more of the following options: Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Fails to meet expectations.
* **How would you rate the quality of this collection?** (Select one, Not required): Choose one of the following options: High, Medium, Low.
* **additional_comments** (Text, Not required): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the collection.
