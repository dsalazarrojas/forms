> # Exit Ticket - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide feedback on students' performance and understanding during a course. It helps teachers evaluate the effectiveness of the course and identify areas for improvement. This feedback form is designed to be completed by students and submitted to the teacher after the course completion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
2. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter your teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
4. Select your understanding level on the course content in the "What is the students understanding level" field.
5. Add any comments about the course in the "Comments" field.
6. Select the date you exited the course in the "Exit Date" field.
7. Choose the relevant submission(s) from the list in the "Submission" field.
8. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you just completed.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a student.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter your teacher's name who taught the course.
* **What is the students understanding level** (`student_understanding_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your level of understanding on the course content, where:
	+ Excellent: You understood the course content well.
	+ Good: You had some understanding of the course content.
	+ Satisfactory: You partially understood the course content.
	+ Poor: You did not understand the course content.
	+ Needs Improvement: The course content was not well understood.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any comments about the course.
* **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you exited the course.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the relevant submission(s) from the list:
	+ Submission 1
	+ Submission 2
	+ Submission 3

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide feedback that is specific and constructive.
* Make sure to complete all the fields that apply to your experience in the course.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
