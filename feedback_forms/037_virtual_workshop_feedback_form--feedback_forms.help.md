# virtual_workshop_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users after attending a virtual workshop or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the **Virtual Workshop ID** (page 1) to identify the workshop you are providing feedback on.
2. Select the **Feedback Type** (page 2) to indicate whether you are providing feedback on the event or the session.
3. Enter your **Rating** (page 3) to rate your experience with the event or session.
4. Provide your **Comment** (page 4) to elaborate on your rating.
5. Enter your **Email** (page 5) if you would like to receive a confirmation email after submitting your feedback.
6. Enter your **Phone** (page 6) if you would like to provide an alternative contact method.
7. Confirm your **Created At** (page 7) and **Updated At** (page 8) timestamps.
8. Confirm your **Submitted At** (page 9) timestamp.
9. Select your **Session ID** (page 10) to provide feedback on a specific session.
10. Enter your **Session Rating** (page 11) to rate your experience with the session.
11. Enter your **Event Rating** (page 12) to rate your experience with the event.
12. Enter your **Overall Rating** (page 13) to rate your overall experience with the event.
13. Provide your **Suggestion** (page 14) for the event or session.
14. Provide your **Session Suggestion** (page 15) for a specific session.
15. Provide your **Event Suggestion** (page 16) for a specific event.
16. Confirm your **Session Attendance** (page 17) status.
17. Confirm your **Event Attendance** (page 18) status.
18. Confirm your **Overall Attendance** (page 19) status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Workshop ID** (`virtual_workshop_id`):
	+ Page 1: Enter the virtual workshop ID associated with the workshop you attended.
* **User ID** (`user_id`):
	+ Page 2: Enter your user ID number.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`):
	+ Page 3: Enter the ID of the virtual event you attended.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`):
	+ Page 4: Select 'Event' to provide feedback on the overall event or 'Session' to provide feedback on a specific session.
* **Rating** (`rating`):
	+ Page 5: Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to express your level of satisfaction with the event or session.
* **Comment** (`comment`):
	+ Page 6: Provide a brief description of your experience with the event or session.
* **Email** (`email`):
	+ Page 7: Enter your email address if you would like to receive a confirmation email after submitting your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`):
	+ Page 8: Enter your phone number if you would like to provide an alternative contact method.
* **Created At** (`created_at`):
	+ Page 9: Confirm the date and time you created your feedback.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`):
	+ Page 10: Confirm the date and time you updated your feedback.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`):
	+ Page 11: Confirm the date and time you submitted your feedback.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`):
	+ Page 12: Enter the ID of the session you attended.
* **Session Rating** (`session_rating`):
	+ Page 13: Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to express your level of satisfaction with the session.
* **Event Rating** (`event_rating`):
	+ Page 14: Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to express your level of satisfaction with the event.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`):
	+ Page 15: Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to express your overall level of satisfaction.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`):
	+ Page 16: Provide a brief description of your suggestion for improvement.
* **Session Suggestion** (`session_suggestion`):
	+ Page 17: Provide a brief description of your suggestion for improvement specific to the session.
* **Event Suggestion** (`event_suggestion`):
	+ Page 18: Provide a brief description of your suggestion for improvement specific to the event.
* **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`):
	+ Page 19: Confirm if you attended the session.
* **Event Attendance** (`event_attendance`):
	+ Page 20: Confirm if you attended the event.
* **Overall Attendance** (`overall_attendance`):
	+ Page 21: Confirm if you attended the overall event.
