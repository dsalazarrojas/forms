# Procedures Review Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from staff and stakeholders about the procedures in place. Your input is valuable and will help us improve the procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each question and provide your feedback. Please make sure to review each field carefully and provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff survey** (Select one): Choose the role that best represents your position in the team. Options: Team Member, Supervisor, Manager.
* **Stakeholder survey** (Select one or multiple): Choose the types of stakeholders that best represent your audience. Options: Stakeholder, Other.
* **Staff survey 5** (Text): Describe your experience with the procedures. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Stakeholder survey 5** (Text): Describe your experience with the procedures. Please provide as much detail as possible.
* **Prophets survey 5** (Date): Note the date of your experience with the procedures.
* **Overall score** (Number): Provide your score out of 10 for the procedures.
* **Overall Rating** (Number): Rate the procedures on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback** (Note): Share your thoughts and comments about the procedures. Please provide any suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **What do you like about the procedures?** (Text): Describe what you like about the procedures. Please be specific.
* **Stakeholder survey 1** (Note): Share any comments or feedback about the procedures.
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the procedures.
* **Prophets survey comments** (Note): Share any comments or feedback about the procedures.
* **Staff survey** (Select one): Choose the role that best represents your position in the team. Options: Team Member, Supervisor, Manager.
* **Stakeholder survey** (Select multiple): Choose the types of stakeholders that best represent your audience. Options: Stakeholder, Other.
* **Staff survey prophets** (Select multiple): Choose whether the procedures are being followed or not. Options: Yes, No.
* **Prophets survey** (Select one): Choose the role that best represents your position in the team. Options: Team Member, Supervisor, Manager.
