# Community Ownership Webinar Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of the Community Ownership Webinar to improve future webinars and better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the Webinar you attended from the list.
2. Select the date you attended the Webinar.
3. Rate the overall quality of the Webinar.
4. Evaluate the content quality.
5. Assess the presenter's performance.
6. Choose the pace of the presentation.
7. Select the length of the Webinar.
8. Indicate whether the learning objectives were met.
9. Enter your key takeaways from the Webinar.
10. Identify the most valuable part of the Webinar.
11. Provide suggestions for improvement.
12. Suggest topics for future Webinars.
13. Choose whether you would recommend the Webinar to a colleague.
14. Select whether you want additional resources.
15. Decide if you'd like a follow-up consultation.
16. Optionally, select your organization type.
17. Choose your role in your organization.
18. Add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which Webinar Did You Attend** (`webinar_title`, `select_one`, required): Choose the Webinar you attended from the list to help us understand which session's feedback is being collected.
* **Date Attended** (`webinar_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you attended the Webinar to help us track attendance.
* **Overall Webinar Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall quality of the Webinar on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Quality of Content** (`content_quality`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the quality of the content presented during the Webinar.
* **Presenter Effectiveness** (`presenter_rating`, `select_one`, required): Assess the presenter's performance and ability to effectively convey information.
* **Pace of Presentation** (`pacing`, `select_one`, required): Select the pace of the presentation, whether it was too fast, just right, or too slow.
* **Length of Webinar** (`length`, `select_one`, required): Choose the length of the Webinar, whether it was too short, just right, or too long.
* **Were Learning Objectives Met** (`learning_objectives`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether the learning objectives were met or not.
* **What Were Your Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`, `text`, required): Enter the main points you took away from the Webinar.
* **What Was Most Valuable** (`most_valuable`, `text`, required): Identify the most valuable part of the Webinar.
* **What Could Be Improved** (`improvements`, `text`, required): Suggest improvements for future Webinars.
* **What Topics Would You Like in Future Webinars** (`topics_future`, `text`, required): Suggest topics for future Webinars.
* **Would You Recommend to a Colleague** (`recommend_colleague`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you would recommend the Webinar to a colleague.
* **Would You Like Additional Resources** (`additional_resources`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you want additional resources.
* **Would You Like a Follow-Up Consultation** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Decide if you'd like a follow-up consultation.
* **Type of Organization** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, optional): Optionally, select your organization type.
* **Your Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Optionally, choose your role in your organization.
* **Any Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
