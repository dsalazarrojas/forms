# Inclusive Wellness Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to collect your feedback on the Inclusive Wellness Program. Your input helps us improve the services and offerings of the program, ensuring it meets the needs of all participants. Your responses are completely voluntary and will not affect any decisions about your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field to understand what information it collects.
2.  Answer all fields that apply to you.
3.  For fields with select options, choose the answer that best fits your needs or preferences.
4.  If a field does not apply to you, leave it blank.
5.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Program Name (program_name)**: Please enter the name of the program you are providing feedback on.
*   **Program Length (program_length)**: If you've participated in the program, please indicate how long it lasted (number of weeks or months).
*   **Program Fees (program_fees)**: If you paid any fees for the program, please indicate the cost (number of dollars).
*   **Select Program Length (select_program_length)**: Choose the program length from the options below that best fits your experience. If your program length was not listed, please select "Other" and provide a brief description.
    +   Short
    +   Medium
    +   Long
    +   Unknown
    +   Other (Please specify the length in the comments section below.)
*   **Select Program Fees (select_program_fees)**: You can select all the fees that apply to your participation in the program. If none of the options fit your experience, please select "Other" and provide a description in the comments section.
    +   Free
    +   Paid
    +   Varies
*   **Comments (comments)**: Share any additional thoughts or experiences related to your participation in the program. This is the perfect place to provide more details that weren't covered by the other fields.
*   **Email (email)**: Please enter your email address so that we can follow up on any questions or provide information about future programs.
*   **Phone (phone)**: If you'd like to be contacted directly, please enter your phone number. If not applicable, leave it blank.
*   **Date (date)**: If your feedback is related to a specific date or period of participation, please enter the date or range of dates.
*   **Time (time)**: If your feedback is related to a specific time or period of day, please enter the time or range of times.

Note: If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, don't hesitate to contact us. We value your feedback and look forward to improving the services and offerings of the Inclusive Wellness Program.
