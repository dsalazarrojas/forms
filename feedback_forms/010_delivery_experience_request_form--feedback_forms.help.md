# Delivery Experience Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Delivery Experience Request Form is designed to collect feedback on the delivery experience from customers. It is used to gather information on the date and time of the delivery, the delivery team, the location, and other relevant details. This form helps to improve the delivery service by identifying areas for improvement and providing insights into customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery date and time in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
2. Select the delivery team from the dropdown list.
3. Choose the delivery location from the multiple selection options.
4. Provide detailed feedback in the text area.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the delivery service using the numbered rating system.
6. Identify any issues encountered during the delivery from the dropdown list.
7. Choose the customer service experience from the multiple selection options.
8. Enter any additional comments, if applicable.
9. Enter a valid email address.
10. Enter the contact number.
11. Select the delivery person from the dropdown list.
12. Choose the delivery vehicle from the multiple selection options.
13. Enter the order ID, which should match the corresponding order ID on your order confirmation document.
14. Select the delivery date range that matches your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`): Enter the date and time of the delivery in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Delivery Team** (`delivery_team`): Select the team that was responsible for your delivery.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`): Choose the location where the delivery was made.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide detailed feedback about your delivery experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your satisfaction with the delivery service using the numbered rating system.
* **Issue** (`issue`): Identify any issues you encountered during the delivery from the dropdown list.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`): Rate your experience with the customer service team.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your delivery experience (optional).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for follow-up or further information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number.
* **Delivery Person** (`delivery_person`): Select the person who made the delivery.
* **Delivery Vehicle** (`delivery_vehicle`): Choose the vehicle used for the delivery.
* **Order ID** (`order_id`): Enter the order ID from your order confirmation document.
* **Delivery Date Range** (`delivery_date_range`): Select the delivery date range that matches your experience.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to provide helpful feedback.
* Use the format `MM/DD/YYYY` for the delivery date and time.
* Select only one location from the delivery location options.
* If you have any issues or concerns, be sure to select them from the dropdown list.
* Enter a valid email address and contact number for follow-up or further information.
* Use the numbered rating system to rate your satisfaction with the delivery service.
