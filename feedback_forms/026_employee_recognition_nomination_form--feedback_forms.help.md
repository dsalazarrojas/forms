# Employee Recognition Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a way for employees to recognize and reward their colleagues who have made significant contributions to the company. This form allows employees to nominate their peers for recognition in a structured and formalized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Choose the correct role of the employee being nominated (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
3. Select the relevant department(s) of the employee being nominated (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Enter the name of the employee being nominated.
5. Enter the email address of the employee being nominated.
6. Enter the phone number of the employee being nominated.
7. Click submit to submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination Form**: This field is a text area where you can provide a brief description of why you are nominating this employee.
* **Name**: Enter the name of the employee you are nominating.
* **Role**: Select the role of the employee you are nominating (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Department**: Select the department(s) of the employee you are nominating (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Nominee Name**: Enter the name of the employee you are nominating.
* **Nominee Email**: Enter the email address of the employee you are nominating.
* **Nominee Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee you are nominating.
