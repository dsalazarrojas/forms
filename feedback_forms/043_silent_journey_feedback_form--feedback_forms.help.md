# Silent Journey Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Silent Journey Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience at the silent retreat. This form helps us to understand what worked well and what didn't, so we can improve future events. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the first field.
2. Select all that apply from the second field regarding your experience.
3. Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for future events).
4. If you wish, enter your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, Required)
Enter your name as you would like to be referred to in our communication.

### **What was most enjoyable?** (`feedback_question`, select_multiple, Optional)
Select all the options that apply to your experience. This will help us understand what we did well and what you enjoyed most about the silent journey.

*   Was the experience of being in nature a positive or negative experience
*   Did you feel a sense of community or connection with others
*   Was the experience challenging or easy
*   Would you like to participate in another silent retreat like this one

### **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, Optional)
If you would like to be contacted by us for future events, please enter your email address here. This is optional, but it will help us keep you informed about upcoming events.

### **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, Optional)
If you have a phone number and would like to be contacted by us, you can enter it here. This is optional and not required for communication.

## Tips
- Please be as honest and thorough as possible when completing this form.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the space provided at the end of the form.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on anything, we're here to help!
