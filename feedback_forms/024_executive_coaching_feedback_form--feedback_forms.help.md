# executive_coaching_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The executive coaching feedback form is used to gather feedback from clients on their executive coaching sessions. This feedback is essential for the coach to understand their strengths and areas for improvement, which will aid in providing better coaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Select the "Session Number" field and enter the number of the coaching session you are providing feedback on.
2. Choose the type of feedback you are providing by selecting from the "What type of feedback" dropdown menu, which includes "Feedback on session content", "Feedback on session facilitation", or "Feedback on overall experience".
3. Rate your overall satisfaction on the session by entering a number between 1-5 in the "Overall Satisfaction" field.
4. If you have any action items to be addressed after the session, enter them in the "Action Items" field.
5. Provide any recommendations for future sessions in the "Recommendations" field.
6. Specify the name of the session facilitators in the "Session Facilitators" field.
7. Describe any session materials used during the session in the "Session Materials" field.
8. Enter the location of the session in the "Session Location" field.
9. Enter the duration of the session in minutes in the "Session Length" field.
10. Enter the start and end time of the session in the "Session Start Time" and "Session End Time" fields.
11. If you have agreed on any action items, enter the number of agreed actions in the "Action Items Agreed On" field.
12. If you have assigned due dates for any action items, enter them in the "Action Items Due Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Number**: Enter the number of the coaching session you are providing feedback on.
* **What type of feedback**: Choose from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of feedback you are providing.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction on the session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Action Items**: Describe any action items you have for the coach to improve on.
* **Recommendations**: Provide any suggestions for future sessions.
* **Session Facilitators**: Specify the name of the session facilitators.
* **Session Materials**: Describe any materials used during the session.
* **Session Location**: Enter the location of the session.
* **Session Length**: Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Session Start Time**: Enter the start time of the session.
* **Session End Time**: Enter the end time of the session.
* **Action Items Agreed On**: Enter the number of agreed actions taken on.
* **Action Items Due Date**: Enter the due date for any action items agreed on.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are providing feedback on the correct session number to ensure that your feedback is accurately tracked.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your coach or session facilitator for clarification.
* This form is used to gather feedback that will aid in improving the coaching services provided. Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
