# Brokers Open Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brokers Open Feedback Form is designed to collect agent feedback on the organization and presentation of Brokers Open events. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Agent Name and Brokerage or Company.
2. Provide your Email Address to enable us to contact you for any follow-up questions.
3. Enter the Date of the Brokers Open Event you attended.
4. Enter the Property Address that was shown during the event.
5. Choose how well the event was organized.
6. Choose how well the property was presented.
7. Choose the quality of catering and refreshments (if applicable).
8. Choose how you would rate the venue and facilities.
9. Indicate how likely you are to show this property to your clients.
10. Provide any additional feedback about the property.
11. Suggest improvements for future Brokers Open events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agent Name (Agent Name)** (`agent_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as an agent.
* **Brokerage or Company (agent_brokerage)** (`agent_brokerage`, `text`, `required`): Enter your brokerage or company name.
* **Email Address (agent_email)** (`email`, `text`, `required`): Enter a valid email address where we can reach you for any follow-up questions.
* **Date of Brokers Open Event (event_date)** (`date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Property Address (property_address)** (`text`, `text`, `required`): Enter the address of the property that was shown during the event.
* **Event Organization (event_organization)** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose how well the event was organized (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Property Presentation (property_presentation)** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose how well the property was presented (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Catering and Refreshments (catering_refreshments)** (`select_one`, `required` (if applicable), `optional`): Choose the quality of catering and refreshments (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Not Applicable).
* **Venue and Facilities (event_facilities)** (`select_one`, `required`): Choose how well you would rate the venue and facilities (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Likely to Show (likely_to_show)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate how likely you are to show this property to your clients (Very Likely, Likely, Maybe, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Additional Property Feedback (property_feedback)** (`text`, `optional`): Provide any additional feedback about the property.
* **Improvement Suggestions (improvement_suggestions)** (`text`, `optional`): Suggest improvements for future Brokers Open events.
* **Contact Information for Offers (agent_contact_for_offers)** (`text`, `optional`): Enter your contact information if offers are made.
* **Would Attend Again (would_attend_again)** (`select_one`, `required`): Indicate if you would attend a similar event in the future (Yes, Definitely, Probably, Maybe, Probably Not, False).
