# Supervised Visit Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervised Visit Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from visits conducted by supervisors. It is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the visit and gather information for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (*).
2. For each section, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your feedback.
3. Use the available options for ratings and select "1", "2", "3", "4", or "5" for each rating field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Outcome**: Please describe the outcome of the session in a few sentences.
  - (text field)

- **Observation**: Share any observations or notable points from the visit.
  - (text field)

- **Session Notes**: Record any additional notes from the session.
  - (text field)

- **Session Outcome Rating**: Rate the outcome of the session, using 1-5 stars.
  - (5-star rating, 1-5 options)

- **Visit Outcome**: Describe the outcome of the visit in a few sentences.
  - (text field)

- **Visit Notes**: Record any additional notes from the visit.
  - (text field)

- **Feedback**: Share any additional feedback or comments about the visit.
  - (text field)

- **Visit Outcome Rating**: Rate the outcome of the visit, using 1-5 stars.
  - (5-star rating, 1-5 options)

- **Visit Satisfaction**: Select all the ways the visit satisfied your expectations.
  - (multi-choice field with 5 options)

- **Supervisor Notes**: This section is for the supervisor to record any additional notes.
  - (text field, not visible to visitors)

Note: This form is intended to be filled out once per visit, so please ensure you are providing feedback for only one visit at a time.
