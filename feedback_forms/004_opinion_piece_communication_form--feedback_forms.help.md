# Opinion Piece Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opinion Piece Communication Form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users on a particular article or piece of writing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read and Engage with the Article**: Before filling out this form, make sure you have read and engaged with the relevant article to provide meaningful feedback.
2. **Fill out the Required Fields**: The following fields are required to complete this form:
	* **Article Title** (`article_title`): Enter the title of the article you are providing feedback on.
	* **Article URL** (`article_url`): Enter the URL of the article you are providing feedback on.
	* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide your feedback or comments about the article.
	* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate the article out of 5 stars.
3. **Submit Your Feedback**: Once you have completed the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Article Title** (`article_title`): The title of the article you are providing feedback on. Please enter the exact title of the article.
* **Article URL** (`article_url`): The URL of the article you are providing feedback on. Please enter the exact URL of the article.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide your feedback or comments about the article. This can include what you liked or disliked about the article, or suggestions for improvement.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate the article out of 5 stars, where 1 star is the lowest rating and 5 stars is the highest rating.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so that the author can understand your comments.
* Try to provide constructive suggestions for improvement, instead of simply stating what you dislike.
* Remember to rate the article based on its content, not on your personal opinions or biases.
