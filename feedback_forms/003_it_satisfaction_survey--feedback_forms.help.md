# it_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your satisfaction with a service or product. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as prompted.
2. For select fields, choose the relevant option that best describes your experience.
3. Enter any additional comments in the text field.
4. Submit the form to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (Text): A brief description of your experience with the service or product.
* **Satisfaction Level** (Number): Please rate your overall satisfaction level, where 1 is Very Poor and 5 is Excellent.
* **Technical Support** (Select One):
	+ Very Poor: You were not satisfied with the technical support provided.
	+ Poor: The technical support was not helpful.
	+ Average: The technical support was satisfactory.
	+ Good: The technical support was helpful.
	+ Excellent: The technical support was outstanding.
* **Communication** (Select Multiple):
	+ Yes: You were well-communicated with throughout the process.
	+ No: You were not well-communicated with.
* **Recommendations** (Select Multiple):
	+ Yes: You would recommend the service or product to others.
	+ No: You would not recommend the service or product to others.
* **Comments** (Text): Additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
* **Email** (Email): Your email address, so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Contact Number** (Text): Your phone number, which we can use to contact you about your feedback.
