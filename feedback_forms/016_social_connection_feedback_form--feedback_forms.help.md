# Social Connection Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Connection Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experiences with the organization's social connections. This feedback is used to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about future services and amenities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be as detailed and specific as possible in your answers.
3. If you have any comments, suggestions, or questions, you can type them into the "comments" field at the end of this form.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field if you would like to be contacted by phone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Title**: (text) - This is the title of the form, which is used to identify its purpose.
- **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, select_multiple, optional):
  This field allows you to select how you heard about the organization. Please choose all that apply.
  - Option 1: Social media
  - Option 2: Friend or family member
  - Option 3: Online search
- **How Often Do You Visit?** (`how_often_do_you_visit_us`, select_one, optional):
  This field allows you to select how often you visit the organization's social connections.
  - Option 1: Daily
  - Option 2: Weekly
  - Option 3: Rarely
- **How Did You Feel About Your Last Visit?** (`how_did_you_feel_about_your_last_visit`, number, optional):
  This field allows you to rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
- **What Did You Do?** (`what_did_you_do_here`, select_multiple, optional):
  This field allows you to select what you did while visiting the organization's social connections.
  - Option 1: Participated in events
  - Option 2: Spoke with staff
  - Option 3: Learned about new services
- **What Barriers Did You Experience?** (`what_barriers_did_you_experience`, select_multiple, optional):
  This field allows you to select any barriers you experienced while visiting the organization's social connections.
  - Option 1: Difficulty finding the location
  - Option 2: Limited seating
  - Option 3: Poor Wi-Fi
- **How Would You Rate Your Experience?** (`how_would_you_rate_your_experience`, number, optional):
  This field allows you to rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional):
  This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your experience.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
  This field is for your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
  This field is for your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone about your feedback.
