# Theater Performance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Performance Feedback Form is designed to gather detailed feedback on various aspects of a theater performance. By completing this form, users can provide their honest feedback, which will be invaluable in helping improve future productions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant pages and fields that pertain to your experience.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as such).
3. Answer each field truthfully, considering the options provided.
4. Save your progress before navigating away from the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Theater Performance** (`feedback_form_1`, required/optional: true)
* This is the primary field where you can write a brief description of the overall performance.

### 2. **Overall Performance** (`overall_performance`, required/optional: true)
* This field allows you to rate the overall quality of the performance on a scale of 1-5.

### 3. **Performance Quality** (`performance_quality`, required/optional: true)
* This field enables you to rate the quality of the performance on a scale of 1-5.

### 4. **Cast Performance** (`cast_performance`, required/optional: true)
* This field allows you to select from three options: Excellent, Fair, or Poor, describing the cast's performance.

### 5. **Theater Atmosphere** (`theater_atmosphere`, required/optional: true)
* Select the most suitable options: Cozy, Comfortable, or Uninviting, which describe the theater's atmosphere.

### 6. **Communication** (`communication`, required/optional: true)
* Choose from the options Clear, Unclear, or Not clear at all, to rate the communication with the cast and crew.

### 7. **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, required/optional: true)
* Select True, False, or Undecided, indicating whether you would recommend this performance to others.

### 8. **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, required/optional: false)
* This text field is an optional section for users to provide any additional comments or suggestions for the performance.

### 9. **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, required/optional: true)
* Enter the date of the performance you are providing feedback on.

### 10. **Start Time** (`start_time`, required/optional: true)
* Enter the start time of the performance.

### 11. **End Time** (`end_time`, required/optional: true)
* Enter the end time of the performance.

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Try to provide specific examples or details to support your comments.
* This form will help us improve future performances, so your input is greatly appreciated!

Note: This help guide will help end-users understand the form's purpose and how to complete it efficiently. It covers each field in detail, ensuring they provide the best possible feedback for the theater performance.
