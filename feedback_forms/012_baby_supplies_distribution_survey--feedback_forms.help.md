# Baby Supplies Distribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from families who have received baby supplies from the distribution service. Your input will help us understand how we can improve the service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
- Answer all questions truthfully, as this will help us understand your experiences.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure you haven't missed any questions or fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Family or Primary Caregiver Name**: Please enter the name of the primary person in your household who received the baby supplies. This information will help us address any specific needs or concerns you may have.
- **How many infants (0-24 months) are in your household?**: Let us know how many infants are in your household so we can better understand your needs. This information will help us provide the right amount of supplies.
- **Which supplies did you receive during the last distribution?**: Select all the supplies that you received during the last distribution. This will help us understand what we are providing and what we can improve on.
- **What is your current most needed diaper size?**: If you currently need a specific diaper size, please let us know so we can adjust our inventory accordingly.
- **Is the distribution frequency sufficient for your needs?**: Rate how often you think we should be distributing supplies to you. This will help us balance our stock and ensure we're meeting your needs.
- **Rate the quality of the supplies received**: Please rate the quality of the supplies we provided. This will help us understand areas for improvement.
- **Was the pickup or distribution process easy to navigate?**: Let us know if you had trouble accessing the supplies or if the process was straightforward.
- **Are there any other baby essentials you are struggling to obtain?**: If you need anything else, please let us know so we can help.
- **How can we make this program better for you?**: Share any suggestions or ideas you have to improve the service.
- **May we contact you for more information?**: If you're willing, we'd like to reach out to you for further discussion or to gather more information.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed in your answers, as this will help us improve the service.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
- We value your feedback and appreciate your time in completing this form.
