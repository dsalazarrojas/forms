# Employee Engagement Platform Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experiences and perceptions of the Employee Engagement Platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and thoughtfully, providing specific examples or anecdotes where possible.
2. Use the options provided for multiple-choice questions. If none of the options match your answer, choose "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the text field that follows.
3. Be honest and objective in your feedback, providing constructive suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback 1** (`feedback_1`, Text, Required): Please share your overall feedback on the platform, describing your experience and any suggestions you may have.
* **Satisfaction 1** (`satisfaction_1`, Number, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the platform on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 10 being very satisfied.
* **Suggestions 1** (`suggestions_1`, Select One, Required): Do you have a suggestion for improving the platform? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Suggestions 2** (`suggestions_2`, Select Multiple, Required): What specific features or functionalities would you like to see added or improved on the platform? Choose all that apply.
* **Category 1** (`category_1`, Select One, Required): How would you categorize your feedback on the platform? Choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' (with a brief explanation in the text field that follows).
* **Platform Usage 1** (`platform_usage_1`, Text, Required): How often do you use the platform? Please provide details about your usage patterns.
* **Platform Usage 2** (`platform_usage_2`, Text, Required): Are there any specific features or functionalities you use frequently on the platform? Please describe.
* **Platform Features 1** (`platform_features_1`, Select One, Required): Are the current platform features meeting your expectations? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Platform Features 2** (`platform_features_2`, Select Multiple, Required): Which platform features do you wish to see improved or added? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Implement 1** (`platform_implement_1`, Select Multiple, Required): Are you satisfied with the platform's implementation and usability? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Implement 2** (`platform_implement_2`, Select Multiple, Required): Which platform features or functionalities do you think are not well-implemented or need improvement? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Roadmap 1** (`platform_roadmap_1`, Select Multiple, Required): Are you aware of the platform's roadmap and future developments? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Roadmap 2** (`platform_roadmap_2`, Select Multiple, Required): Which features or functionalities do you think should be added to the platform's roadmap? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Mission 1** (`platform_mission_1`, Select Multiple, Required): Are you aligned with the platform's mission and vision? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Mission 2** (`platform_mission_2`, Select Multiple, Required): Which platform features or functionalities do you think are essential to achieving the platform's mission? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Vision 1** (`platform_vision_1`, Select Multiple, Required): Do you have a clear vision for the platform's future development? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Vision 2** (`platform_vision_2`, Select Multiple, Required): Which platform features or functionalities do you think will shape its future vision and development? Choose all that apply.
* **Feedback 2** (`feedback_2`, Text, Required): Please provide any additional feedback or comments about the platform.
* **Suggestions 3** (`suggestions_3`, Select Multiple, Required): Do you have any final suggestions or recommendations for the platform? Choose all that apply.
* **Category 3** (`category_3`, Select One, Required): How would you categorize your feedback on the platform? Choose from 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' (with a brief explanation in the text field that follows).
* **Platform Features 3** (`platform_features_3`, Select Multiple, Required): Are the current platform features meeting your expectations? Choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Platform Implement 3** (`platform_implement_3`, Select Multiple, Required): Are you satisfied with the platform's implementation and usability? Choose all that apply.
* **Platform Roadmap 3** (`platform_roadmap_3`, Select Multiple, Required): Are you aware of the platform's roadmap and future developments? Choose all that apply.

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully, considering your experience with the platform.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please include them in the text fields provided.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations, please choose the relevant options and provide a brief explanation in the text field that follows.
