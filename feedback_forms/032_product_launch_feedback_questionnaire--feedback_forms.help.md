# product_launch_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback about the product launch, helping us understand what customers think about the product and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by a red star *).
3. If you have any improvement suggestions, please provide them in the "product launch improvement suggestions" field.
4. If you have any additional information or attachment to support your suggestions, please provide it in the "product launch improvement suggestions attachment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Launch Feedback** (*product_launch_feedback*): This field is for you to provide any feedback about the product launch.
* **Product Launch Rating** (*product_launch_rating*): This field is for you to rate your overall experience with the product launch.
* **Product Launch Implementation Date** (*product_launch_implementation_date*): This field is for you to provide the date when the product was launched.
* **Product Launch Implementation Date Range** (*product_launch_implementation_date_range*): This field is for you to select the range of time when the product was launched.
* **Product Launch Feedback Form Fields** (*product_launch_feedback_form_fields*): This field is for you to select which specific fields of the product you would like to provide feedback on.
* **Product Launch Improvement Suggestions** (*product_launch_improvement_suggestions*): This field is for you to provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Product Launch Improvement Suggestions Email** (*product_launch_improvement_suggestions_email*): This field is for you to provide an email address where you can be contacted for any further questions or discussions about your suggestions.
* **Product Launch Improvement Suggestions Phone** (*product_launch_improvement_suggestions_phone*): This field is for you to provide a phone number where you can be contacted for any further questions or discussions about your suggestions.
* **Product Launch Improvement Suggestions Note** (*product_launch_improvement_suggestions_note*): This field is for you to provide any additional notes or details about your suggestions.
* **Product Launch Improvement Suggestions Attachment** (*product_launch_improvement_suggestions_attachment*): This field is for you to attach any supporting documents or files to your suggestions.
