# Webinar Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from attendees of webinars to help improve the quality and content of future webinars.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select options, choose only one option that best describes your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, type them in the "Additional" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think (text, required)**: Please share your overall impressions of the webinar.
* **Content (select multiple, optional)**: Rate how useful was the content of the webinar. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, Poor.
* **Presentation (select one, optional)**: Rate how useful was the presentation. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, Poor.
* **Facilitator (select multiple, optional)**: Rate how useful was the facilitator. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Neutral, Poor.
* **Overall (text, optional)**: Please share your overall impressions of the webinar.
* **Additional (note, optional)**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, type them here.
* **Webinar Title (text, optional)**: The title of the webinar.
* **Webinar Date and Time (date, optional)**: The date and time of the webinar.
* **Webinar Duration (number, optional)**: The duration of the webinar.
* **Webinar Platform (select one, optional)**: The platform used for the webinar. Choose from the options: Zoom, Google Meet, GoTo, Skype.
* **Webinar Type (text, optional)**: The type of webinar.
* **Webinar Topic (text, optional)**: The topic of the webinar.
* **Attendee Name (text, optional)**: Your name as an attendee.
* **Attendee Email (email, optional)**: Your email address.
* **Attendee Phone (text, optional)**: Your phone number.
* **Attendee Company (text, optional)**: The company you represent (if applicable).
* **Attendee Job (text, optional)**: Your job title or role.
* **Attendee Role (text, optional)**: Your role in the company.
* **Attendee Location (text, optional)**: The location you attended from.
* **Attendee Department (text, optional)**: The department you belong to.
* **Attendee Job Title (text, optional)**: Your job title or role.
* **Attendee Team (text, optional)**: Your team or organization.
* **Attendee Job Function (text, optional)**: Your job function or area of expertise.
* **Follow Up (select one, optional)**: Whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up opportunities. Choose from the options: 'True', 'False'.
* **Follow Up Comments (text, optional)**: Any comments you have regarding follow-up opportunities.
