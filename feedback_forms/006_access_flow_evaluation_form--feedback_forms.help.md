# Access Flow Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help evaluate the accessibility of a particular location or service, assessing its compliance with accessibility standards and providing recommendations for improvement. It will help assessors evaluate the accessibility of the location, including physical barriers, digital accessibility, staff training, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and date of evaluation in the respective fields. Your name is optional, but the date of evaluation is required.
2. Select the type of access evaluation being conducted (e.g., physical, digital, service, communication, or transportation access).
3. Rate the overall accessibility of the location or service, using the provided scale.
4. Answer the questions regarding physical barriers, such as entrance accessibility, parking availability, and navigation assistance.
5. Assess the digital accessibility of the location or service.
6. Evaluate the level of staff training in assisting people with disabilities.
7. Identify any available accommodations, such as wheelchair accessibility, visual aids, or sign language interpreters.
8. Rate the communication accessibility of the location or service.
9. Provide improvement suggestions for enhancing accessibility.
10. Mention any positive aspects of the accessibility evaluation.
11. Rate the compliance of the location or service with accessibility standards.
12. Finally, state whether you would recommend this location or service to others with accessibility needs, considering your overall evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, optional): Your name and contact information for follow-up questions.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Date of the evaluation.
* **Location Evaluated** (`location_evaluated`, text, required): Name and address of the facility or service location.
* **Evaluation Purpose** (`evaluation_purpose`, select_one, required): Type of access evaluation being conducted (e.g., physical, digital, service, communication, transportation).
* **Overall Accessibility Rating** (`accessibility_rating_overall`, select_one, required): Overall rating of the location or service, using the provided scale.
* **Entrance Accessibility** (`entrance_accessibility`, select_one, required): How accessible is the main entrance?
* **Parking Availability** (`parking_availability`, select_one, required): Availability of accessible parking spaces.
* **Navigation Assistance** (`navigation_assistance`, select_one, required): Availability of wayfinding aids for people with disabilities.
* **Physical Barrier Issues** (`physical_barrier_issues`, select_multiple, optional): Identify any physical barriers encountered.
* **Digital Accessibility** (`digital_accessibility`, select_one, required): How accessible is the digital interface (website, kiosk, app)?
* **Staff Training Level** (`staff_training_level`, select_one, required): How well trained are staff in assisting people with disabilities?
* **Accommodations** (`accommodation_options`, select_multiple, optional): What accommodations are available (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, visual aids, sign language interpreters)?
* **Communication Accessibility** (`communication_accessibility`, select_one, required): How accessible is communication at this location?
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest improvements for enhancing accessibility.
* **Positive Aspects** (`positive_aspects`, text, optional): Mention any positive aspects of the accessibility evaluation.
* **Compliance Rating** (`compliance_rating`, select_one, required): How well does this location comply with accessibility standards?
* **Recommendation Status** (`recommendation_status`, select_one, required): Would you recommend this location to others with accessibility needs?

## Tips

- Be as specific as possible when answering each question, providing concrete examples and details.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions, use the improvement suggestions field.
- Remember that your name and contact information may be used for follow-up questions.
- Answer every question to provide a comprehensive assessment.
