# Beauty Brand Accelerator Market Research Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather market research data on the beauty industry, including demographics, purchasing habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address (optional) on pages 1-2.
2. Select your age group and gender on pages 3-4.
3. On page 5, enter your location (city and state).
4. On page 6, select how much you spend on beauty products monthly.
5. On page 7, select all the product categories you purchase.
6. On page 8, select all the channels where you shop for beauty products.
7. On page 9, select how loyal you are to brands.
8. On page 10, select the top 3 factors that influence your purchases.
9. On page 11, select how interested you are in trying new products.
10. On page 12, select how important price is when making a purchase.
11. On page 13, select how important it is to read labels and understand ingredients.
12. On page 14, select how important sustainability is to you.
13. On page 15, select how much social media influences your purchases.
14. On page 16, describe any pain points you experience with beauty products.
15. On page 17, describe any unmet needs in the beauty industry.
16. Finally, on page 18, provide any additional feedback or comments.
