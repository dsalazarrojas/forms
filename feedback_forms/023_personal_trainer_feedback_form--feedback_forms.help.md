# Personal Trainer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from trainees on their experience with a personal trainer. This form will help us improve the training programs and services offered by our trainers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please provide a brief review of your experience with the personal trainer, including any notable events or issues that occurred during the session.
2.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
3.  Rate the effectiveness of the training session.
4.  Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have on your experience with the trainer.
5.  If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address and/or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback on Session** (`personal_trainer_feedback_form`, text, optional): A brief review of your experience with the personal trainer, including any notable events or issues that occurred during the session.
* **Training Session** (`training_session_id`, number, optional): The ID number of the training session you attended. If you're unsure, you can ask your trainer or refer to your schedule.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rate Training Session** (`rate_training_session`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the training session. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Rate Trainer** (`rate_trainer`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with the trainer. Choose from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have on your experience with the trainer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your phone number.
