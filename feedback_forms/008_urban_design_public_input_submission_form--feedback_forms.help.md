# Urban Design Public Input Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record public feedback on urban design ideas for the benefit of the community. Please take a moment to fill out the form below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field according to your intentions and provide as much detail as possible. You can submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Submit Title</u> (`submit_title`, text, required)
* A brief and descriptive title of your urban design idea. This title will be displayed for other users to see.

### <u>Your Name</u> (`name`, text, required)
* Your name and contact information will be kept private and will only be used to contact you for follow-up questions or to confirm your submission.

### <u>Email</u> (`email`, email, required)
* Your email address where you can be reached for further discussion or clarification.

### <u>Question</u> (`question`, text, required)
* A question or query related to your urban design idea. Please provide any relevant details or context that will help us understand your suggestion.

### <u>Description</u> (`description`, text, required)
* A detailed description of your urban design idea, including any visuals, diagrams, or supporting information.

### <u>Location</u> (`location`, text, optional)
* If your urban design idea is specific to a particular location or area, please mention it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### <u>Contact</u> (`contact`, text, optional)
* If you have any additional comments or information about yourself that you'd like to provide, feel free to include it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.

### <u>Submit</u> (`submit_button`, select_one, required)
* To finalize your submission, select "Submit" to send your feedback to the community. You can also choose "Save Draft" to save your progress and return later, or "Cancel" to discard your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your urban design ideas with us!
