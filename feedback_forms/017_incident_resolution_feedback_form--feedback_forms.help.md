# Incident Resolution Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Resolution Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users about the resolution of a specific incident. This form helps us understand your experience with our incident resolution process and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your level of satisfaction with the incident resolution process.
2. Enter the time it took for the incident to be resolved.
3. Enter the date the incident was resolved.
4. Provide any additional comments about the incident resolution process.
5. Enter your contact email address.
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Select the method(s) used to resolve the incident.
8. Answer follow-up questions about the incident resolution process (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident-resolution-feedback** (Select One, Required): Please select how satisfied you are with the resolution of the incident.
	+ Very Satisfied: You are extremely satisfied with the resolution.
	+ Satisfied: You are satisfied with the resolution.
	+ Not Satisfied: You are not satisfied with the resolution.
* **resolution-time** (Time, Required): Enter the time it took for the incident to be resolved.
* **resolution-date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the incident was resolved.
* **feedback-comments** (Note, Required): Provide any additional comments about the incident resolution process.
* **contact-email** (Email, Required): Enter your contact email address so we can follow up with you.
* **contact-phone** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **resolution-method** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the method(s) used to resolve the incident.
	+ Email: Resolution was done via email.
	+ Phone: Resolution was done via phone.
	+ Other: Resolution was done via another method.
	+ Chat: Resolution was done via chat.
