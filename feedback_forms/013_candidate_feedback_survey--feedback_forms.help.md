# Candidate Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from candidates who have gone through an interview process. It helps us understand their experience, satisfaction, and areas of improvement for future interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, email (if you have one), and the job title you were interviewed for.
2. Select the type of interview you went through.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the interview, the communication before and after, and other aspects of the interview experience.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: A text field where you can optionally provide your name. If you don't feel comfortable sharing your name, you can leave it blank.
* **Email Address**: An email field where you can optionally provide your email address. This is for follow-up purposes only, if needed.
* **Position Interviewed For**: A text field where you can enter the job title for which you were interviewed.
* **Interview Date**: A date field where you can select the date of your interview.
* **Type of Interview**: Select the type of interview you went through (phone screening, video interview, in-person, etc.).
* **Interviewer Name**: A text field where you can optionally provide the name of the interviewer.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Interview**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the interview experience (very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
* **Pre-Interview Communication**: Rate the quality of communication before the interview (excellent, very good, good, fair, poor).
* **Interview Scheduling Experience**: Rate the ease or difficulty of scheduling the interview (very easy, easy, neutral, difficult, very difficult).
* **Interview Environment**: Rate your comfort level with the interview environment (very comfortable, comfortable, neutral, uncomfortable, very uncomfortable).
* **Clarity of Interview Questions**: Rate the clarity of the questions asked during the interview (very clear, clear, neutral, unclear, very unclear).
* **Relevance of Questions**: Rate the relevance of the questions asked during the interview (very relevant, relevant, neutral, not very relevant, not relevant).
* **Were You Given Enough Time to Respond**: Rate your satisfaction with the response time (yes - more than enough, yes - adequate, neutral, no - rushed, no - very rushed).
* **Interviewer Knowledge and Expertise**: Rate the interviewer's knowledge and expertise (excellent, very good, good, fair, poor).
* **Interviewer Engagement**: Rate the interviewer's engagement (very engaged, engaged, neutral, disengaged, very disengaged).
* **Opportunity to Ask Questions**: Rate the opportunity to ask questions during the interview (yes - plenty of opportunity, yes - some opportunity, neutral, no - limited opportunity, no - no opportunity).
* **Were Your Questions Answered**: Rate your satisfaction with the answers provided (yes - completely, yes - mostly, partially, no - not really, did not ask questions).
* **Clarity of Next Steps**: Rate the clarity of the next steps explained after the interview (very clear, clear, somewhat clear, unclear, not explained).
* **If Offered Position - How Likely to Accept**: Rate your interest level in accepting the offered position (very likely, likely, neutral, unlikely, very unlikely).
* **What Impressed You Most**: A text field where you can optionally provide what impressed you most about the interview experience.
* **Any Concerns or Reservations**: A text field where you can optionally provide any concerns or reservations you may have had during or after the interview.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: A text field where you can provide suggestions on how to improve the interview process.
* **Additional Comments**: A text field where you can provide any additional feedback.
* **Survey Date**: A date field where you can select the date you completed this survey.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective in your responses.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions for improvement, please provide specific examples.
- You can leave any optional fields blank if you don't feel comfortable providing that information.
