# Emotional Intelligence Raffle Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emotional Intelligence Raffle Entry form is designed to collect participant information for an emotional intelligence assessment. This form requires participants to provide their personal details, reflections, and other information to help assess their emotional intelligence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have not already submitted an entry with the same contact information.
2.  Fill in the contact name, phone number, and email address in the relevant fields.
3.  If you are a participant, fill in your name, age, education, occupation, and any other relevant details.
4.  Write down your reflections about your emotional experiences in the participant reflections field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant reflections**: This field is where you can provide any reflections or thoughts about your emotional experiences. Please be as detailed and honest as possible.
*   **Contact name**: This is the name of the person who is submitting this entry, and it's required for identification purposes.
*   **Contact phone**: This is the phone number of the person who is submitting this entry, and it's required for contact purposes.
*   **Contact email**: This is the email address of the person who is submitting this entry, and it's required for contact purposes.
*   **Participant name**: This is the name of the participant who is taking the emotional intelligence assessment, and it's required for identification purposes.
*   **Participant age**: This field is for the participant's age, which is required for the assessment.
*   **Participant education**: This field is for the participant's level of education, which is required for the assessment.
*   **Participant occupation**: This field is for the participant's occupation, which is required for the assessment.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your contact person for clarification.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
