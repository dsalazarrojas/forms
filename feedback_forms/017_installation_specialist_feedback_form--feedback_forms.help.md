# Installation Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with an installation specialist. The information gathered from this form will help us understand areas for improvement and enhance the overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your first name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number (optional).
4. Enter the date of your installation (or the latest date known).
5. Answer the select questions regarding the service quality and communication you experienced during the installation.
6. Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have about your experience.
7. Rate your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
8. Select the level of satisfaction with your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please fill in your first name to help us identify you in case we need to follow up on your feedback.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you for further questions or clarifications, if needed.
* **Customer Phone**: Your phone number (optional) will be used to follow up with you, if needed.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date of your installation to help us understand when your experience occurred.
* **Service Quality**: Indicate the level of satisfaction with the service quality (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Communication**: Select the level of satisfaction with the communication you received from our team (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor), or Poor, or Very poor if it was extremely poor).
* **Additional Feedback**: Share any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with us (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Overall Experience**: Select the level of satisfaction with your overall experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
