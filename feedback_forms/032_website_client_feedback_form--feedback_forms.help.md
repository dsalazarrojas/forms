# Website Client Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Client Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with our website. This feedback helps us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Contact Name and Email (if you want to receive a response).
2. Rate your experience using the rating options provided.
3. Select the Category that best describes your feedback.
4. Provide additional comments or feedback in the Message field.
5. If you have a Phone number, you can include it in the Phone field.
6. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`page1`, text, required: false): This is the main field where you can provide your feedback about our website.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name if you want to receive a response to your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address if you want to receive a response to your feedback.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Provide additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate your experience using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false): Select the Category that best describes your feedback: Website Usability, Website Content, Website Design, Website Performance, or Website Features.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): If you want to provide a specific date for your feedback, you can enter it here.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): If you want to provide a specific time for your feedback, you can enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to receive a call-back from us.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, required: false): You can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
