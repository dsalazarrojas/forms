# Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about a parent's or guardian's role and relationship with their child. It helps us update or review a child's records and is crucial for maintaining accurate and up-to-date information about the child's educational and support needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all required information.
2. For each field, enter the relevant information in the format specified.
3. Review your entries before submitting to ensure they are correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (id: 2, name: student_name, label: Student Name, hint: null, type: text, required: false): Enter your child's full name as it appears on their official documents, such as their passport or birth certificate.
* **Parent Name** (id: 3, name: parent_name, label: Parent Name, hint: null, type: text, required: false): Enter your name as a parent or guardian of the child.
* **Parent Email** (id: 4, name: parent_email, label: Parent Email, hint: null, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent Phone** (id: 5, name: parent_phone, label: Parent Phone, hint: null, type: text, required: false): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Teacher Name** (id: 6, name: teacher_name, label: Teacher Name, hint: null, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher responsible for your child's educational program.
* **Student Skills** (id: 7, name: student_skills, label: Student Skills, hint: null, type: select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if your child has the skills listed, or 'No' if they do not.
* **Teacher Notes** (id: 8, name: teacher_notes, label: Teacher Notes, hint: null, type: note, required: false): Enter any important notes or comments about your child's educational program.
* **Teacher Comments** (id: 9, name: teacher_comments, label: Teacher Comments, hint: null, type: text, required: false): Add any other comments or feedback you would like to provide about your child's educational program.

## Tips

* Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for guidance.
* Review your entries before submitting to ensure they are complete and correct.
