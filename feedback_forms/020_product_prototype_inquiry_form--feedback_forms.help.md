# Product Prototype Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about product prototypes. Your feedback will help us understand your product requirements and preferences, which will inform our product development process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the product title and description in the fields below.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Select the category that best describes your product.
4. If you have any feedback or comments, please provide them in the field below.
5. Click the submit button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Title** (`product_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Describe the product in a few sentences.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact EMail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): If you have any feedback or comments, please provide them.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category that best describes your product. This will help us understand your product's characteristics.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the product version you are inquiring about.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (if any).
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We value your feedback and will do our best to address it.

This help guide explains each field's purpose and expected input, making it easier for end-users to complete the form accurately and effectively.
