# Kindergarten Orientation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from parents and guardians on their experience with the school's kindergarten orientation program. Please take a few minutes to provide your feedback and suggestions to help us improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely, including your contact information, to provide as much information as possible.
2. If a field is optional, feel free to leave it blank, but keep in mind that your feedback will be more valuable if you complete all the fields.
3. Use clear and concise language when answering the questions, and avoid using abbreviations or jargon.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (<type:text>, required): This is where you enter your name and contact information so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Student Information** (<type:text>, required): This is where you enter your child's name and any other relevant student information.
* **How can the school improve?** (<type:text, optional): This is where you can provide any feedback or suggestions on how the school can improve the orientation program.
* **What suggestions do you have?** (<type:text, optional): This is similar to the previous question, but feel free to add any additional suggestions you may have.
* **Comments** (<type:text, optional): This is where you can add any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **How can we contact you?** (<type:email, optional): This is where you enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **How can we contact you?** (<type:text, optional): This is where you enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Date** (<type:date, optional): This is where you enter the date of the orientation program you attended.
* **Time** (<type:time, optional): This is where you enter the time of the orientation program you attended.
* **Page Number** (<type:number, optional): This is where you enter the page number of the orientation program's handbook that you received.
* **Select One** (<type:select_one, required>: You can choose one of the options provided, and please select the option that best represents your opinion.
* **Select Multiple** (<type:select_multiple, required>: You can choose multiple options that apply to your opinion.
