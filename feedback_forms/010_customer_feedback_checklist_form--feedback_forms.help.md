# Customer Feedback Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Feedback Checklist Form is designed to collect feedback from customers, enabling us to understand their experiences and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the "Customer Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Select your preferred **Contact Method** from the options provided (Mobile, Email, or Phone).
3. Enter your **Email** address (if applicable).
4. Enter your **Phone** number (if applicable).
5. Select the relevant options for each **Question**:
   - **Question 1**: Choose one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
   - **Question 2**: Choose one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
   - **Question 3**: Choose one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
   - **Question 4**: Choose one option that applies to your experience.
   - **Question 5**: Choose one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
6. Click the **Submit** button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Text Field, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Method** (Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted in response to this feedback.
* **Email** (Email Field, Optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted via email.
* **Phone** (Text Field, Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Question 1** (Select Multiple, Required): Select one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
* **Question 2** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
* **Question 3** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
* **Question 4** (Select One, Optional): Select one option that applies to your experience.
* **Question 5** (Select Multiple, Required): Select one or multiple options that apply to your experience.
* **Submit** (Submit Button, Required): Click to send your feedback for review.
