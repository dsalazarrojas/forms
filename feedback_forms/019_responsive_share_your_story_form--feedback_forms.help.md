# Responsive Share Your Story Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback, share stories, and gather details about customer experiences. It asks for information that will help us understand your perspective and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with as much detail as possible.
2. For text fields, enter your response in the text box.
3. For select-one fields, choose one option from the list.
4. For select-multiple fields, choose as many options as applicable.
5. For date and time fields, use the calendar or time picker to select a date or time.
6. For notes fields, enter any additional information that is not covered by other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Story** (`customer_story`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your story, experience, or feedback.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience with our service or product.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or comments about our service or product.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information or clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for more information or clarification.
* **Story Details** (`story_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about your story or experience.
* **Business** (`business`, `text`, required: false): Describe your business or organization related to your story or experience.
* **Service** (`service`, `text`, required: false): Describe the service or product you used in relation to your story or experience.
* **Experience Date** (`experience_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of your experience.
* **Experience Time** (`experience_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of your experience.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you were assigned to someone or a team for your experience.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who assigned you for your experience.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you used a specific tool for your experience.
* **Story Type** (`story_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of story or experience you had.
* **Customer** (`customer`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you are a customer or not.
* **Customer Group** (`customer_group`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose which customer group you belong to.
* **Story Tags** (`story_tags`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any relevant tags or categories for your story or experience.
* **Story Tags 2** (`story_tags_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose any additional relevant tags or categories for your story or experience.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information not covered by other fields.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of your submission.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of your submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who submitted the story or experience.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who the submission was submitted to.
* **Submitted Tool** (`submitted_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the tool used for submission.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique identifier for your submission.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language in text fields.
* Choose the correct options for select-one and select-multiple fields.
* Use the calendar or time picker for date and time fields.
* Enter your email and phone number so we can contact you for more information or clarification.
