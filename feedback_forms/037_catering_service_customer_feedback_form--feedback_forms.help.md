# Catering Service Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience with our catering service. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us understand what we did well and what we could improve on.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and email address on page 1.
2. On page 1, select the date of your event from the calendar.
3. On page 2, choose the type of event you had with us (e.g., wedding, birthday party).
4. Answer all the questions on page 3, rating your experience with our catering service.
5. On page 4, write in any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (1): This is where you enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2): This is where you enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussions.
* **Phone Number** (3): This is where you enter your contact phone number.
* **Event Date** (4): This is the date of your event where our catering service was held.
* **Event Type** (5): Select the type of event you had with us (e.g., wedding, corporate event).
* **Event Location** (6): This is where your event was held.
* **Number of Guests** (7): Enter the number of guests at your event.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (8): Rate your overall satisfaction with our catering service (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Food Quality** (9): Rate the quality of the food served (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Food Presentation** (10): Rate the presentation of the food (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Food Temperature** (11): Rate the temperature of the food served (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Portion Size** (12): Rate the size of the food portions (More Than Enough, Just Right, Somewhat Small, Too Small).
* **Menu Variety** (13): Rate the variety of food options we offered (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Service Quality** (14): Rate the quality of our service staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Staff Professionalism** (15): Rate the professionalism of our staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Staff Friendliness** (16): Rate the friendliness of our staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Timeliness** (17): Rate the timeliness of our service (Early, On Time, Slightly Late, Very Late).
* **Setup Quality** (18): Rate the quality of the setup (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Value for Money** (19): Rate whether you thought our service was worth the cost (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value).
* **Dietary Accommodation** (20): Rate whether we accommodated your dietary needs (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Poor, Not Applicable).
* **Would You Recommend Us** (21): Rate whether you would recommend our service to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Would You Use Again** (22): Rate whether you would use our service again (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **What Did You Like Best** (23): Write in what you enjoyed most about our service.
* **Areas for Improvement** (24): Write in any areas where we could improve.
* **Additional Comments** (25): Write in any additional feedback you have.

Note: You can skip fields that don't apply to your experience, but please fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) in this guide.
