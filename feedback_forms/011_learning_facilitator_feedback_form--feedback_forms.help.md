# Learning Facilitator Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from learning facilitators who have conducted a course. The form helps us understand their overall experience, assess the quality of the course content and facilitation, and gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information. You can skip any optional fields if you do not have the information or if it is not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role as a learning facilitator?** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This field is an optional field for you to provide a brief description of your role as a learning facilitator.
* **How would you rate your overall experience conducting this course?** (`feedback_on_conducting`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience conducting this course on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the course content?** (`feedback_on_content`, `number`, required: false): Rate the course content on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the facilitator?** (`feedback_on_facilitator`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the facilitator on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is "Poor", 2 is "Fair", 3 is "Good", and 4 is "Excellent".
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement?** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving our courses or facilitation.
* **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address for us to reach out to you regarding your feedback.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for us to know who submitted the feedback.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department name (e.g. "IT", "Marketing", etc.).
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required: false): Enter your role (e.g. "Trainer", "Coach", etc.).
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Course facilitator name** (`course_facilitator`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the course facilitator. 

## Tips
- Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues, we will use this feedback to improve our courses and facilitation.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
