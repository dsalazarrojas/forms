# dinner_reception_menu_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dinner reception feedback form is designed to collect guest feedback on the dinner menu and overall experience. This helps event organizers understand what guests enjoyed or didn't enjoy, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Review the questions and answer them truthfully and thoroughly.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dinner Reception Feedback** (`reception_feedback`, `note`, required: false): This is a free-text area where you can share your thoughts and opinions about the dinner reception experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **How would you rate the dinner menu?** (`menu_rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would rate the dinner menu, from 1 star to 5 stars. This helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the menu.
* **Do you have any menu suggestions or improvements?** (`menu_suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any ideas for future menus or improvements, feel free to share them here.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, from Email, Phone, SMS, or Social.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the experience.
