# Most Likely To Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users about the most likely scenarios involving superheroes in a fictional world.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your title (e.g., "Superhero Fanatic").
2. Select a category for the feedback you are providing (e.g., "Feedback Forms").
3. Provide a brief description of your thoughts and opinions (e.g., "I think Batman is the best superhero").
4. Indicate who you think would be most likely to spy (e.g., "James Bond").
5. Indicate who you think would be most likely to know (e.g., "Sherlock Holmes").
6. Provide a brief description of your answer for the most likely to spy and most likely to know questions (e.g., "Because of his skills and experience").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Most Likely To Survey Title (1)**: A brief title for your feedback and opinions.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Category (2)**: Select the category that best describes your feedback and opinions.
	+ Type: Select one option from the dropdown list.
	+ Required: No.
* **Description (3)**: Provide a brief description of your thoughts and opinions.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Most Likely To Spy (4)**: Choose who you think would be most likely to spy.
	+ Type: Select multiple options from the dropdown list.
	+ Required: No.
* **Most Likely To Know (5)**: Choose who you think would be most likely to know.
	+ Type: Select multiple options from the dropdown list.
	+ Required: No.
* **Description 2 (6)**: Provide a brief description of your answer for the most likely to spy and most likely to know questions.
	+ Type: Text input field.
	+ Required: No.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer these questions based on your thoughts and opinions.
* Provide a brief description for each answer to explain your reasoning.
* You can use as much space as you need for the descriptions.
* If you are unsure or have no answer for a question, you can leave it blank or provide a brief explanation for why you didn't choose an option.
