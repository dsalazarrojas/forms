# pairwise_comparison_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on pairwise comparisons, where users can compare and rate options side by side. The form is intended to be used for product development, user experience, or market research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions as they appear.
2. On page 1, you will be asked about your preference for a question, with options to choose from.
3. Continue to the next page where you will be presented with another question and options to choose from.
4. On page 3, you will be asked about a selection of options, where you can choose multiple options that apply to you.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your preference?** (`question1`, `text`, required/optional): Answer this question with a brief description of your preference.
* **Option 1** (`choice1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your preference.
* **Option 2** (`choice2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your preference.
* **What is your preference?** (`question2`, `text`, required/optional): Answer this question with a brief description of your preference.
* **Option 3** (`choice3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your preference.
* **Do you like?** (`question3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the options that apply to you.
* **What is your preference?** (`question4`, `text`, required/optional): Answer this question with a brief description of your preference.
* **Option 4** (`choice4`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your preference.

## Tips

* Make sure to read each question carefully and answer according to your actual preference.
* If you have no preference, you can choose "No" or "None of the above" as applicable.
* Take your time to answer each question, and don't hesitate to ask for clarification if needed.
