# Runner Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from runners to help us better understand their habits, preferences, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name, Last name, and Email in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone number and Date of birth, if applicable.
3. Select your Running frequency from the options provided.
4. Provide your Running distance and Running time, if applicable.
5. Add any Comments about your running experiences or preferences.
6. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you for tracking.
7. If you are a customer, provide your Customer name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your given first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your given last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time of day** (`time_of_day`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of day you usually run.
* **Running frequency** (`running_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Select the frequency of running you prefer:
	+ Option 1: Daily
	+ Option 2: Several times a week
	+ Option 3: Rarely
* **Running distance** (`running_distance`, text, required/optional): Enter the average distance you run in a single session.
* **Running time** (`running_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the average time you spend running.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments about your running experiences or preferences.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you for tracking your runs.
* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): If you are a customer, enter your name as it appears on your order or contract.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are filling out the form only once for each runner to avoid duplication.
* If you need help with any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations above.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
