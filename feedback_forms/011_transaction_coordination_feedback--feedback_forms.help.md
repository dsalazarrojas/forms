# Transaction Coordination Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for clients and agents to share their feedback on the transaction coordination experience. This feedback is valuable in helping us improve our services and ensure that your needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure that the fields are relevant to your experience.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
3. Be honest and specific in your feedback, and provide examples if possible.
4. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Transaction ID (1)**: A unique identifier for the transaction you are providing feedback on.
* **Transaction Status (3)**: Select the current status of the transaction, whether it's Active or Inactive.
* **What Happened (4)**: Describe what happened next, giving context to your feedback.
* **Client Feedback (5)**: Share your thoughts on your experience with the transaction, including any concerns or issues you faced.
* **Client Satisfaction (6)**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the transaction, using a numerical scale.
* **Agent Feedback (7)**: Share your thoughts on the service provided by the transaction agent.
* **Agent Satisfaction (8)**: Rate the agent's performance, using a numerical scale.
* **Transaction Agent Communication (9)**: Evaluate the communication with the transaction agent, selecting Good or Bad.
* **Transaction Agent Availability (10)**: Rate the agent's availability, selecting from Excellent to Poor. 

## Tips
* Be honest and specific in your feedback, and provide examples if possible.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please raise them in the "Client Feedback" field.
* Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve our services, so thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
