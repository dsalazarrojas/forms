# Food Quality Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Quality Assessment Quiz is a form used to gather customer feedback on the quality of food products. It helps us to understand what customers think about our food and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Food Quality Assessment Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answers for each question on the following pages.
2. On the "Customer Feedback" page, provide a brief description of your thoughts and feelings about the food product.
3. For the "Rating Scale" question, select one or more of the available ratings (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent) that best describe your experience with the food product.
4. On the "Food Rating" page, rate the quality of the food on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is the lowest and 4 is the highest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback** (customer_feedback, text, required: false): This field is where you can share your thoughts and feelings about your experience with the food product. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand what you liked or disliked about it.
* **Rating Scale** (rating_scale, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the ratings that best describe your experience with the food product. You can select as many options as you think apply.
* **Food Rating** (food_rating, number, required: false): Rate the quality of the food on a scale of 1-4, where 1 is the lowest and 4 is the highest. This will help us understand your overall impression of the food product.
