# platform_engagement_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The platform_engagement_survey form aims to collect user feedback on their usage of the platform. This helps us understand how users interact with the platform and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read through the form questions carefully and answer each one to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, provide as much detail as you're willing to share.
3. Submit the form when you've completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **title**: platform_engagement_survey (This is the title of the form. You've already started by opening it!)
* **Introduction**: (This is a text field for you to type in. Feel free to share anything you'd like about your experience with the platform, e.g., what led you to the platform, what you use it for.)
* **What do you use the platform for?**: (This question is asking you to type in how you've used the platform. Try to be as specific as possible, e.g., what features you use most often, or what specific tasks you complete with it.)
* **How often do you use the platform?**: (This question is asking you to type in how frequently you use the platform. Be honest about your usage, e.g., daily, weekly, rarely, etc.)
* **How likely are you to recommend the platform to a friend or colleague?**: (This question has four options: very-likely, somewhat-likely, not-very-likely, and not-at-all-likely. Think about whether you'd recommend the platform based on your experiences with it.)
* **How can we improve the platform?**: (This question is for you to provide any suggestions on how we can improve the platform. Feel free to offer ideas on features, layout, or anything else that you think would make the platform better.)

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
* If a question doesn't apply to you, you can still submit the form with your answers to the questions that do.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to add them in the text area at the top.
