# Lot Survey Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lot Survey Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from users on a specific lot survey. This form is designed to gather information about the condition of the lot, comments, rating, date, time, contact information, surveyor and developer involvement, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
3. Ensure you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lot Survey Feedback Form** (`lot_survey_feedback_form`): This is the title of the form, but you do not need to fill in anything here.
* **Comments** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information about the lot survey. This field is optional.
* **Rating** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_rating`): Rate the survey from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Date** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_date`): Enter the date of the survey in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_time`): Enter the time of the survey in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_note`): Provide any additional note about the survey. This field is optional.
* **Surveyor** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_surveyor`): Select the surveyor who conducted the survey from the list of options.
* **Developer** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_developer`): Select the developer who was involved in the survey from the list of options.
* **Real Estate** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_real_estate`): Select the real estate involved in the survey from the list of options.
* **Other Surveyor** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_other_surveyor`): Select any other surveyor who was involved in the survey from the list of options.
* **Other Developer** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_other_developer`): Select any other developer who was involved in the survey from the list of options.
* **Other Real Estate** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_other_real_estate`): Select any other real estate involved in the survey from the list of options.
* **Other** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_other`): Provide any additional information about the survey. This field is optional.
* **Surveyor Notes** (`lot_survey_feedback_form_surveyor_notes`): Provide any additional notes about the surveyor who conducted the survey. This field is optional.

**Tips**

* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in all fields.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries (DD/MM/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM, respectively).
* Select all relevant options in the surveyor, developer, real estate, other surveyor, other developer, and other real estate fields.
* If you have any other relevant information about the survey, provide it in the "Other" field.
* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields.
