# Green Waste Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Green Waste Management Survey is designed to help the organization better understand the current state of waste management practices within the organization. It aims to gather information on employee engagement programs, departmental responsibilities, and the main reasons for engaging in waste management practices. This survey is completely anonymous and should take only a few minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct options from the provided fields. If a field is not required, you can skip it. You can submit this form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Green Waste Management Survey** (`green_waste_management_survey`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is a dropdown menu with two options: "Yes" or "No". Please select "Yes" if you participate in an employee engagement program for waste management, and "No" otherwise.
* **organization** (organization, text, required: false): Please provide the name of your organization.
* **name of contact** (name_of_contact, text, required: false): Please provide your name so we can contact you with the survey results.
* **phone** (phone, text, required: false): Please provide your phone number so we can contact you with the survey results.
* **email** (email, email, required: false): Please provide your email address so we can contact you with the survey results.
* **Do you currently participate in an employee engagement program for waste management?** (question_1, select_one, required: false): Please select "True" if you participate in an employee engagement program for waste management, "False" otherwise, or "Not Applicable" if this doesn't apply to your organization.
* **Which department is responsible for waste management within your organisation?** (question_2, select_one, required: true): Please select the department responsible for waste management within your organization from the options provided.
* **Which department is responsible for waste management within your organization?** (question_3, select_one, required: true): Please select the department responsible for waste management within your organization from the options provided. **Note:** This question is identical to question 2, please only select one answer.
* **Which department is responsible for waste management within your organisation?** (question_4, select_one, required: true): Please select the department responsible for waste management within your organization from the options provided. **Note:** This question is identical to question 2, please only select one answer.
* **What is the main reason why your organisation engages in waste management practices?** (question_5, select_multiple, required: true): Please select the main reason why your organization engages in waste management practices from the options provided. You can select more than one reason.
* **Additional Notes** (note, note, required: false): Please provide any additional information you would like to share about waste management practices within your organization.
