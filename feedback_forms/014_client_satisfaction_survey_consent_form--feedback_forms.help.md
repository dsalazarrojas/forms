# Client Satisfaction Survey Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect feedback from clients about their experiences with our services or products. It helps us identify areas of improvement and ensures we provide the best possible experience for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review our Privacy Policy before answering any questions that involve personal data collection.
3. Be honest and open in your feedback, as it will help us improve our services or products.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Satisfaction Survey and Consent**: A heading to introduce the next section.
* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number. This is optional.
* **Company or Organization Name (if applicable)**: Enter the name of your company or organization if you have one.
* **Survey Date**: The date you completed the survey.
* **Consent and Privacy Agreement**:
	+ **I understand this survey is voluntary and I can withdraw at any time.** This confirms that you understand the survey is optional and you can stop participating at any moment.
	+ **I consent to the collection of my personal information for survey purposes**. This is to ensure we respect your privacy.
	+ **I consent to my feedback being used to improve services and products**. This allows us to use your feedback for product/service development.
	+ **I understand my responses will be kept confidential and used only for stated purposes**. This ensures your responses won't be shared or used outside the agreed-upon purposes.
* **Voluntary Participation, Data Collection, Data Use, and Confidentiality Consents**: Select "Yes" if you agree with the above statements.
* **I prefer to complete this survey anonymously**. This is an option for those who wish to remain anonymous.
* **You may be contacted for follow-up questions about your responses**. Select "Yes" if you agree to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **-- Service Satisfaction --**:
	+ **Overall satisfaction with our service or product**. Choose how satisfied you are with our service or product, on a scale from Very Unsatisfied to Very Satisfied.
	+ **Rate your satisfaction - 1 to 10**. Rate your satisfaction with our service or product, with 1 being Very Unsatisfied and 10 being Very Satisfied.
	+ **Quality of service or product**. Choose the quality of our service or product, from Poor to Excellent.
	+ **Services met or exceeded my expectations**. Choose how well our services met or exceeded your expectations, from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree.
* **Value and Support**:
	+ **I received good value for my money**. Choose how you feel about the value of our service or product, from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree.
	+ **Quality of customer support provided**. Choose the quality of our customer support, from Poor to Excellent.
	+ **Support team was responsive to my needs**. Choose how responsive our support team was, from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree.
* **Loyalty and Recommendation**:
	+ **I would recommend this company to others**. Choose if you would recommend our company to others, from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree.
	+ **Likelihood of using our services again - 1 to 10**. Rate how likely you are to use our services again, with 1 being Not at all likely and 10 being Extremely Likely.
* **Feedback and Comments**:
	+ **What did we do well?**. Provide any positive comments about our services or product.
	+ **What could we improve?**. Provide any negative comments about our services or product.
	+ **Additional feedback or comments**. Provide any other comments or suggestions you may have.
