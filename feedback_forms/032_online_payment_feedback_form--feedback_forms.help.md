# Online Payment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers after they have made an online payment. Please answer the questions below to provide us with information on your experience with the payment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Scroll through each page to review the questions and provide information as required or optional.
* Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* Use the provided options for select type questions.
* You can add notes and comments to provide more detailed feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional details about your payment, such as the date or time of payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the amount of the payment you made.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your payment: `pending`, `successful`, or `failed`.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your level of satisfaction with your payment experience. Choose all that apply: `satisfied`, `not satisfied`.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about your payment experience.
* **Feedback Sent Date** (`feedback_sent_date`, date, optional): If you have already sent feedback, select the date it was sent.
* **Payment Details Time** (`payment_details_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your payment.
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, note, optional): This is a field for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Feedback Sent Date** (`customer_feedback_sent_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you sent your feedback.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, optional): Select the user who you believe should be notified about your feedback.
* **Customer Contact Info** (`customer_contact_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, social media, or chat).
* **Customer Feedback Sent Date Time** (`customer_feedback_sent_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you sent your feedback.
* **Customer Feedback Sent Location** (`customer_feedback_sent_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select where you sent your feedback (store, website).
