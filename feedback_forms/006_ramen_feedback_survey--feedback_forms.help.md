# Ramen Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ramen Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at Ramen. It's essential to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve our services.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Please provide your name (first and last) to help us understand who you are.
* Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you (if necessary).
* Select the type(s) of feedback you want to provide (food quality, service, or ambiance).
* Rate the food, service, and ambiance on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text):
  A required field to identify you. Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text):
  A required field to identify you. Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (email):
  Enter a real email address where we can contact you (if necessary).
* **Feedback Type** (select multiple):
  Choose the type(s) of feedback you want to provide:
	+ Food Quality
	+ Service
	+ Ambiance
* **How would you rate the food** (number):
  Rate the food on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How would you rate the service** (number):
  Rate the service on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **How would you rate the ambiance** (number):
  Rate the ambiance on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Additional Comments** (note):
  Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* If you have any concerns or issues, we'll be happy to address them.
* We appreciate your time and consideration in providing feedback.
