# Online Merchandise Store Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to share their thoughts, opinions, and experiences after a purchase from the online merchandise store. Your feedback will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Make sure to provide your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
3. You can rate your purchase to help us understand your satisfaction level.
4. Share any comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
5. If you have any issues with your order, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Purchase details**: Here you can briefly describe your purchase, including the item(s) you bought and any relevant details about the order.
* **Rate this purchase**: Use this option to rate your satisfaction with the purchase. This will help us understand how well we're meeting our customers' expectations.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience with our store. This is your chance to tell us what we're doing well and what we can improve.
* **Email address**: Please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you to follow up on your feedback and to keep you informed about any updates or changes to our store.
* **Phone number**: You can optionally provide your phone number if you'd like us to contact you directly.
* **Comments**: Use this field to share any additional comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
* **Product id**: Here you can enter the product id number that corresponds to the item(s) you purchased.
* **Product id**: This field is not visible to customers and is only used by our system to track orders. Please do not fill out this field.
