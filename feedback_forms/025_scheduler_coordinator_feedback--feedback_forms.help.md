# Scheduler Coordinator Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from the scheduler coordinator on their performance in coordinating schedules. The goal is to provide an opportunity for the scheduler to express their thoughts and opinions on their work, allowing for constructive feedback and growth.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and concisely, providing as much detail as possible where applicable.
2. For questions with options, select the most accurate choice that reflects your opinion.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field to express them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`scheduler_coordinator_feedback`, text, required): This is the primary field where you can provide any feedback or comments on the scheduler's performance. Be as specific as possible and try to focus on their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): This field is similar to the previous one, providing an opportunity to express any additional thoughts or opinions on the scheduler's work.
* **Scheduling Performance** (`scheduling_performance`, number, required): Here, rate the scheduler's performance on a scale (e.g., 1-10) considering their efficiency and effectiveness in coordinating schedules.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, number, required): Rate the scheduler's communication skills, considering how well they interact with staff, clients, or other stakeholders.
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, text, required): Comment on the scheduler's level of professionalism, such as punctuality, reliability, and respect for deadlines.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, text, required): Provide feedback on how well the scheduler manages their time, including their ability to prioritize tasks, meet deadlines, and avoid procrastination.
* **Scheduling Staff** (`scheduling_staff`, select_one, required): Choose the most accurate option that reflects your opinion on whether the scheduler effectively schedules staff or not.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Describe any specific goals or areas where the scheduler has shown improvement or has the potential to grow.
* **Areas For Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required): Identify any areas where the scheduler could improve or require additional support.
* **Action Plans** (`action_plans`, text, required): Suggest specific actions or steps that can be taken by the scheduler to address areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have, or any other relevant information that can help in the scheduler's growth and development.
