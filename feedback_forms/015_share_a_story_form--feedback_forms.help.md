# share_a_story_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "share_a_story_form" is a form designed to share experiences and stories related to social work. It's a platform for social workers to connect with each other, exchange feedback, and learn from one another's stories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have a genuine reason for filling out this form. This form is not meant for sharing sensitive or confidential information.
2. Fill in the required fields (Social Worker Id, Story, Email, and categories) completely and accurately.
3. Provide a truthful and detailed story in the "Story" field.
4. Choose the correct options for the "categories" field according to your experience.
5. Decide whether you would like to share your story publicly ("Yes" or "No") in the "Share" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social Worker Id**: This is the unique identifier for the social worker submitting the story. Please enter your social worker ID number.
* **Story**: This is the core of the form, where you can share your story, experience, or feedback related to social work. Please be as detailed and honest as possible.
* **Feedback**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback related to the story.
* **Email**: This is the contact information for the social worker submitting the story. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone**: This is the contact information for the social worker submitting the story. Please ensure that it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Share**: This field determines whether your story will be shared publicly or not. Please select "Yes" if you would like your story to be shared, and "No" if you would like to keep it private.
* **Date**: This field is for selecting the date your story occurred. If this is not relevant, leave it blank.
* **Time**: This field is for selecting the time your story occurred. If this is not relevant, leave it blank.
* **Note**: This is an optional field for providing any additional notes related to the story.
* **Categories**: This field allows you to select the categories that best describe your story. Please choose one or more categories that accurately represent your experience.
