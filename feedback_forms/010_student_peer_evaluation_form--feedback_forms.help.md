# Student Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by students to evaluate their peers in a class setting. It's designed to gather feedback and ratings about a peer's performance in a particular class or project.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Student ID** of the peer you're evaluating. This is a required field and should be the student's unique identifier.
2. Enter the name of the **teacher who assigned this project or class**. This is also a required field.
3. Enter the name of the **peer you're evaluating**. This is a required field.
4. Provide **feedback** about your peer's performance, focusing on what they did well and areas for improvement. This is a required field.
5. If you have any **suggestions for improvement**, enter them in this field. This is an optional field, so feel free to provide feedback or not.
6. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the **comments** field. This is an optional field.
7. Provide a **rating** for your peer's overall performance. This is a required field.
8. Provide a **peer rating** for your peer's overall performance. This is a required field.
9. Provide an **overall rating** for your peer's overall performance. This is a required field.
