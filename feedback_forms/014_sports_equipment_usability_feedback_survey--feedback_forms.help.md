# Sports Equipment Usability Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about the usability of sports equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions or select the options that best reflect your thoughts and experiences.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by the "required" property in each section.
3. Provide as much detail as possible, especially in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction (id: 1)
* **Introduction**: A short text field to introduce yourself and provide any relevant background information.

### General Feedback (id: 2)
* **General Feedback (text)**: Please share your overall thoughts about the sports equipment.

### Ease of Use (id: 3)
* **Ease of Use (select_one)**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the equipment: 
  + Very Easy
  + Somewhat Easy
  + Neutral
  + Somewhat Hard
  + Very Hard

### Comfort (id: 4)
* **Comfort (select_one)**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the equipment: 
  + Very Comfortable
  + Somewhat Comfortable
  + Neutral
  + Somewhat Uncomfortable
  + Very Uncomfortable

### Quality (id: 5)
* **Quality (select_one)**: Select the option that best describes your experience with the equipment: 
  + Excellent
  + Good
  + Fair
  + Poor

### Recommendation (id: 6)
* **Recommendation (select_one)**: Select the option that best describes your likelihood to recommend the equipment to others: 
  + Very Likely
  + Somewhat Likely
  + Neutral
  + Somewhat Unlikely
  + Very Unlikely
