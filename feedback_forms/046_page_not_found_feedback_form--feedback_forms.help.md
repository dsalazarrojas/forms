# Page Not Found Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand what users were trying to access when they got the "Page Not Found" error, and how we can improve our website to prevent such issues in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. You can use this form to provide feedback on a "Page Not Found" error you encountered on our website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What link?** (`what_link`, text, optional): This field is for you to tell us what link you were trying to access when you got the "Page Not Found" error.
* **Title of the page?** (`page_not_found_title`, text, optional): If you remember, please let us know what you think the title of the page should have been.
* **Which URL?** (`page_not_found_url`, text, optional): Please provide the URL you were trying to access when you got the "Page Not Found" error.
* **How was the issue impacted you?** (`how_issue_impacted`, text, optional): Please describe how the "Page Not Found" error affected your experience on our website.
* **What should we do to fix it?** (`what_to_fix`, text, optional): Suggest a solution to prevent similar issues in the future.
* **Additional feedback?** (`feedback`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them here. 

## Tips

* Please fill out as many fields as you think are relevant to your feedback.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can simply leave it blank.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we'll use it to improve our website.
