# Volunteer Effectiveness Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Effectiveness Monitoring Form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness of volunteers in your program or project. This form helps volunteers and program managers understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the rating scale on the next page to ensure you understand how to use it.
2. Rate the volunteer's performance based on your experience with them.
3. Provide specific comments on their strengths and areas for improvement.
4. Add any additional comments or suggestions for future projects.
5. Select the rating frequency to indicate how often you've interacted with the volunteer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating** (`Rating`, `select_one`, not required): Choose one of the options to rate the volunteer's overall performance.
  * Option 1: Excellent
  * Option 2: Good
  * Option 3: Fair
* **Rating Scale** (`Rating Scale`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose one or more options to rate the volunteer's performance in specific areas.
  * Option 1: Communication
  * Option 2: Teamwork
  * Option 3: Time Management
* **Rating Comment** (`Rating Comment`, `text`, not required): Provide a brief comment on the rating you selected.
* **Performance Comments** (`Performance Comments`, `text`, not required): Add any specific comments on the volunteer's performance.
* **Recommendations** (`Recommendations`, `text`, not required): Suggest any areas for improvement or recommendations for future projects.
* **Next Steps** (`Next Steps`, `text`, not required): Outline the next steps for the volunteer or program manager to improve performance.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, not required): Add any extra comments or suggestions.
* **Rating Frequency** (`Rating Frequency`, `select_one`, not required): Choose how often you've interacted with the volunteer.
  * Option 1: Once
  * Option 2: Multiple times
  * Option 3: Never
