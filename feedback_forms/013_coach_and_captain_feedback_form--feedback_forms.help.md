# Coach And Captain Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coach And Captain Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback on coaching and player performance from coaches, captains, and players. This feedback will help the organization to better understand their strengths and areas for improvement, enabling them to provide more effective coaching and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your role as Coach, Captain, or Player from the "Role" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the "Respondent name" field.
3. Choose the date of the game or practice you are providing feedback on.
4. Describe the "Strengths observed" during the game or practice in the text field.
5. Provide "Areas for improvement" in the coaching or support you received in the text field.
6. Rate the "Overall performance" of the game or practice on a scale of 1-5.
7. If "Support needed from the organization" is required, provide the specific resources or training in the text field.
8. Check if "Follow up required" is necessary to ensure further action is taken.
9. Add any "Additional comments" in the text field if you have any other feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Feedback --**: This is a placeholder for the form title.
* **Respondent name**: Enter your name as a coach, captain, or player.
* **Role**: Choose your role as Coach, Captain, or Player.
* **Date of game or practice**: Enter the date of the game or practice you are providing feedback on in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Strengths observed**: Describe the key positive aspects or strengths you observed during the game or practice.
* **Areas for improvement**: Specify the areas where you feel coaching or support could be improved.
* **Overall performance rating**: Rate the overall performance of the game or practice on a scale of 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Support needed from organization**: If you require additional support or resources, describe them in this field.
* **Follow up required**: Check if you need further follow up or action to be taken.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
