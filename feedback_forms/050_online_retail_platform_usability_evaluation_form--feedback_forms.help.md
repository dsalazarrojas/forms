**Online Retail Platform Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your opinions and experiences about our online retail platform. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and your responses will help us identify areas of improvement and make our platform more user-friendly for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter your answers to each question according to your experiences. If a question is not relevant to you, feel free to skip it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Platform Experience** (`platform_experience`, `number`, required: false): Please rate your overall experience on our platform, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Search Experience** (`platform_search`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience with searching for products on our platform.
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Did you find all the necessary information about the products you were looking for? 
* **Cart & Checkout** (`cart_checkout`, `select_one`, required: false): How easy was the checkout process for you?
* **Account** (`account`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Did you have to create an account to make a purchase or access certain features?
* **Payment** (`payment`, `select_one`, required: false): Was the payment process smooth and secure?
* **Return Policy** (`return_policy`, `note`, required: false): If you had to return a product, how did you experience our return policy?
* **Customer Support** (`customer_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Did you contact our customer support, and if so, how was your experience?
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience on our platform, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name (if you'd like to be addressed as such in follow-up communications).
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name (if you'd like to be addressed as such in follow-up communications).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title (if you'd like to be addressed as such in follow-up communications).
* **How did you feel about the overall experience on the platform?** (`platform_experience_2`, `text`, required: false): Describe your overall feelings about our platform.
* **What did you like about the experience?** (`what_did_you_like`, `text`, required: false): Share what you enjoyed most about your experience.
* **What didnt you like about the experience?** (`what_didnt_you_like`, `text`, required: false): Share what you didn't enjoy most about your experience.
* **Do you have any suggestions or recommendations for us?** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving our platform, please share them.
