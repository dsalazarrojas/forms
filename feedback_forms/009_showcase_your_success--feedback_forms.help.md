# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user feedback and showcase success stories from the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief headline for your success story.
2. Describe what you have achieved recently.
3. Provide a detailed description of your achievement.
4. If applicable, upload supporting files for your story (e.g., images, videos, or documents).
5. Select the type of file you are uploading.
6. Enter the URL of the file if it's hosted online.
7. Provide a description of the file.
8. If required, select the date and time of your achievement.
9. Choose a location where your achievement was realized.
10. Select any recognition programs you've participated in.
11. Enter a brief description of your success story.
12. If desired, upload an image or video of your community spotlight.
13. Finally, confirm your consent for the publication of your story.
