# Avatar Design Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Avatar Design Feedback Form is a tool used by the design team to gather opinions from respondents on the visual aspects of an avatar design. This form is used to collect feedback on the overall design and features of the avatar, which will help the design team improve and refine their work.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your information, including your full name and email address, as this will help us understand who is providing the feedback and ensure we can contact you if needed.
2. Provide the name or ID of the project or game that the avatar belongs to, so we can associate the feedback with the correct project.
3. Answer the questions about your initial reaction to the design, such as its overall appeal, clarity, and color palette.
4. Select which features of the avatar you like or dislike, such as its visual style and aesthetic.
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the design.
6. Finally, rate the overall design from 1 to 10 and indicate if it's ready for production or if it needs improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it will be used to identify you and contact you if needed.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or for further feedback.
* **Project Name** (4, text, required): Provide the name of the project or game that the avatar belongs to, so we can associate the feedback with the correct project.
* **Avatar Name or ID** (5, text, required): Enter the name or ID of the avatar you are providing feedback on.
* **Overall Visual Appeal** (7, select_one, required): Rate the overall appeal of the design, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Clarity of Representation** (8, select_one, required): Rate how well the design represents the character's role or personality, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Color Palette Rating** (9, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the color choices, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Visual Style and Aesthetic** (10, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply, such as Realistic and Detailed, Stylized and Cartoonish, Minimalist and Clean, Cyberpunk and Futuristic, or High-Fantasy and Ornate.
* **Key Features Liked** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select any features of the avatar you liked, such as Head and Hair, Facial Expression, Clothing and Outfits, Accessories and Equipment, or Pose and Silhouettes.
* **Areas to Improve** (12, text, required): Describe any areas of the design that you would improve or change.
* **Overall Rating** (13, number, required): Rate the overall design from 1 to 10.
* **Ready for Production?** (14, select_one, required): Indicate if the design is ready for production or if it needs improvements.
* **Additional Feedback** (15, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the design.
