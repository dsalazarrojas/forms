# YouTube Video Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about YouTube videos. The information collected helps us understand what users like or dislike about a video, and what they think could be improved. The form is usually filled out by users who have watched a specific YouTube video and have a suggestion or comment about it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately with your feedback about the YouTube video you watched.
2.  Make sure to provide your email address, so we can contact you if needed to follow up on your feedback.
3.  Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Video ID (video_id)** (`video_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the YouTube video you are providing feedback about. This helps us identify which video your feedback is related to.
*   **User Name (user_name)** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name or the name you would like to be associated with your feedback. This allows us to address you personally in our response.
*   **Email (email)** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted to discuss your feedback further.
*   **Rating (rating)** (`rating`, `number`, required): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the video. This helps us understand the general consensus about the video.
*   **Suggestion (suggestion)** (`suggestion`, `note`, optional): If you have a specific suggestion or comment about the video, enter it here. This can be a great opportunity to tell us what we can do better.
*   **Video Content (video_content)** (`video_content`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the video content, enter it here.
*   **Comments (comments)** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about the video, enter them here.
*   **Contact Email (contact_email)** (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted back for further discussion or follow-up on your feedback.
*   **Contact Phone (contact_phone)** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone for further discussion or follow-up on your feedback.
*   **Contact Address (contact_address)** (`contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact address if you would like to be contacted at your address for further discussion or follow-up on your feedback.
*   **Submit (submit)** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click this button to send your feedback to us.

Note: All fields marked as optional can be left blank, but filling out these fields can provide additional helpful information for us to follow up on your feedback.
