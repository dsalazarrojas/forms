# intern_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The intern_feedback_questionnaire is a form designed to collect feedback from interns about their experience with the company. The form aims to gather information about the company culture, overall rating, and rating of various aspects of the internship program. This feedback is crucial in helping us improve our internship program and provide a better experience for future interns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name.
2.  Enter your Last Name.
3.  Enter your Email.
4.  Enter your Work Unit.
5.  Answer the question "What did you learn about the company culture?"
6.  Provide any suggestions for improvement.
7.  Rate the overall program out of 5.
8.  Enter the Date of Completion.
9.  Enter the Time of Completion.
10. Rate the Satisfaction with the internship program.
11. Rate the Skills of the supervisor.
12. Rate the Supervisor's Quality.
13. Rate the Supervisor's Communication.
14. Rate the Supervisor's Friendliness.
15. Rate the Internship Program.
16. Rate the Program as a whole.
17. Rate the Mission of the program.
18. Rate the Environment of the program.
19. Rate the Leadership of the program.
20. Rate the Team of the program.
21. Rate the overall Supervisor Rating.
22. Rate the overall Supervisor Support.
23. Rate the overall Supervisor Quality.
24. Rate the Supervisor Communication.
25. Rate the overall Supervisor Friendliness.
25. Rate the Internship as a whole.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
*  **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
*  **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email.
*  **Work Unit** (`work_unit`, text, required): Enter your work unit.
*  **What did you learn about the company culture?** (`feedback_question`, text, required): This question is designed to gather information about your experience with the company culture.
*  **What suggestions do you have for improvement?** (`feedback_comment`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
*  **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate the program out of 5.
*  **Date of Completion** (`date_of_completion`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the internship.
*  **Time of Completion** (`time_of_completion`, time, required): Enter the time you completed the internship.
*  **Rating of Satisfaction** (`rating_of_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the program.
*  **Rating of Skills** (`rating_of_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the supervisor's skills.
*  **Rating of Supervisor** (`rating_of_supervisor`, select_one, required): Rate the supervisor.
*  **Rating of Resources** (`rating_of_resources`, select_one, required): Rate the resources provided.
*  **Rating of Mission** (`rating_of_mission`, select_one, required): Rate the mission of the program.
*  **Rating of Culture** (`rating_of_culture`, select_one, required): Rate the company culture.
*  **Rating of Environment** (`rating_of_environment`, select_one, required): Rate the work environment.
*  **Rating of Leadership** (`rating_of_leadership`, select_one, required): Rate the leadership of the program.
*  **Rating of Team** (`rating_of_team`, select_one, required): Rate the team.
*  **Rating of Supervisor Rating** (`rating_of_supervisor_rating`, select_multiple, required): Rate the supervisor's rating.
*  **Rating of Supervisor Support** (`rating_of_supervisor_support`, select_multiple, required): Rate the supervisor's support.
*  **Rating of Supervisor Quality** (`rating_of_supervisor_quality`, select_multiple, required): Rate the supervisor's quality.
*  **Rating of Supervisor Communication** (`rating_of_supervisor_communication`, select_multiple, required): Rate the supervisor's communication.
*  **Rating of Supervisor Friendly** (`rating_of_supervisor_friendly`, select_multiple, required): Rate the supervisor's friendliness.
*  **Rating of Internship** (`rating_of_internship`, select_multiple, required): Rate the internship.
*  **Rating of Program** (`rating_of_program`, select_multiple, required): Rate the overall program.
