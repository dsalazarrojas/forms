# web_tracking_evaluation_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers feedback on web tracking technology experience.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant information for each field:
* Select which web tracking technology you use.
* Choose the types of data you collect.
* Select how you collect data: server side, browser side, or both.
* Choose where you store data: local server, remote server, or both.
* Select how you protect your data: encryption, pseudonymization, or hashing.
* Rate your survey experience (optional).
* Rate your user experience (optional).
* Provide your overall experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which web tracking technology do you use?** (`tracking_technology`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks which web tracking technology you currently use. Examples include web tracking cookies, web beacons, and web pixels.
* **What type of data do you collect?** (`tracking_data`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks what types of data you collect. Examples include user data, browser history, and search history.
* **How do you collect data?** (`data_collection`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks how you collect data. Examples include server side, browser side, and both.
* **Where do you store data?** (`data_storage`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks where you store data. Examples include local server, remote server, and both.
* **How do you protect your data?** (`data_security`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks how you protect your data. Examples include encryption, pseudonymization, and hashing.
* **How would you rate your survey experience?** (`survey_experience`, number, required/optional): This field asks how you rate your experience with this form.
* **How would you rate your user experience?** (`user_experience`, number, required/optional): This field asks how you rate your experience with our platform.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required/optional): This field is open for you to provide any additional comments on your experience with this form.
