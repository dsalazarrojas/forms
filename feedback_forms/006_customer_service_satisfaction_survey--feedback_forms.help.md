# Customer Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to measure your satisfaction with our customer service. Your feedback will help us improve our services and ensure that you have a positive experience with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your answers to each question from the provided options.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. Submit your survey at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied are you with our service?** (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor)
	+ Select your level of satisfaction with our service.
* **How would you rate the quality of our service?** (1-5)
	+ Please provide a rating from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **How helpful were the staff members you interacted with?** (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Not very helpful, Not at all helpful)
	+ Select the level of helpfulness of our staff members.
* **How would you rate the communication with our staff?** (1-5)
	+ Please provide a rating from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **How quickly were your issues resolved?** (Very quickly, Somewhat quickly, Not very quickly, Not at all quickly)
	+ Select the speed at which your issues were resolved.
* **How long did it take for your issue to be resolved?** 
	+ Note: This question is not used for scoring and will not be calculated as part of your overall satisfaction.
* **Was the staff available to help you during your visit?** (Always, Most of the time, Sometimes, Never)
	+ Select the level of staff availability.
* **How would you like to have your issue resolved?** (Email, Phone, Chat, In-person, Other)
	+ Select your preferred method for resolving issues.
* **Do you want to be contacted by email?** 
	+ Select "True" if you want to be contacted by email.
* **Do you want to be contacted by phone?** 
	+ Select "True" if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **Would you like to be contacted by chat?** 
	+ Select "True" if you want to be contacted by chat.
