# Route Efficiency Rider Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Route Efficiency Rider Inquiry Form is designed to collect feedback from riders on the efficiency of a particular route or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your response to the question "Route Efficiency" in the text field below.
2. Select all applicable answers for "Delays" from the drop-down list.
3. Choose your level of satisfaction from the drop-down list.
4. Provide any additional information or suggestions you may have in the text field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Route Efficiency** (`route_efficiency_rider_inquiry_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe your experience with the route efficiency. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your perspective.
- **Delays** (`route_efficiency_rider_inquiry_form_delays`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the times you have experienced delays on this route. You can choose from "Delay", "On Time", "Frequently", and "Rarely".
- **Satisfaction** (`route_efficiency_rider_inquiry_form_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the route service.
- **Suggestions** (`route_efficiency_rider_inquiry_form_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or comments about the route service, please provide them here.
- **Additional Information** (`route_efficiency_rider_inquiry_form_additional_information`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional context or information that may be helpful to us.

## Tips
- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
- If you have experienced frequent or infrequent delays, please select all applicable answers.
- Remember that your feedback is invaluable to us, and the more information you provide, the better we can improve the route efficiency.
