# Bird Feeder Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers who own or have used our bird feeders. Your input will help us improve the product and provide a better experience for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant answers based on your experience with our bird feeder.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive any updates or notifications from us.
* **City or Region** (`respondent_location`, text, optional): If you'd like to share your location, please do so in the city or region where you live.
* **Feedback Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Please select the date you are providing feedback.
* **Bird Feeder Model** (`product_model`, select_one, required): Please select the model of bird feeder you own or have used.
	+ Classic Tube Feeder
	+ Hopper Feeder
	+ Platform Feeder
	+ Suet Feeder
	+ Thistle Feeder
	+ Hummingbird Feeder
	+ Other Model
* **How Long Have You Owned This Feeder** (`ownership_duration`, select_one, required): Please select how long you have owned this bird feeder.
	+ Less than 1 Month
	+ 1-3 Months
	+ 3-6 Months
	+ 6-12 Months
	+ Over 1 Year
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the bird feeder on a scale of 1-10.
* **Product Quality** (`quality_rating`, select_one, required): Please rate the quality of the bird feeder.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Durability and Weather Resistance** (`durability_rating`, select_one, required): Please rate how well the bird feeder holds up to weather conditions.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

	+ Very Easy
	+ Fairly Easy
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Ease of Refilling** (`refill_ease`, select_one, required): Please rate how easy it is to refill the bird feeder.
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Somewhat Difficult
	+ Difficult
* **Seed Capacity** (`capacity_rating`, select_one, required): Please rate the bird feeder's seed capacity.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good

	+ Daily Multiple Times

	+ Several Times per Week
	+ Weekly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never

	+ Chickadees

	+ Sparrows
	+ Woodpeckers
	+ Blue Jays
	+ Other Species

	+ Suet
