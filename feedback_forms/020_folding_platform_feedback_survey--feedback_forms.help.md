# Folding Platform Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and input from users on the Folding Platform, which will help us improve and refine the platform to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an *.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your feedback.
3. Use the options list for select fields to choose one or more relevant options.
4. If a field is optional, feel free to skip it or leave it blank if it does not apply to your feedback.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (text, *): Enter your first name.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (email, *): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (text, optional): Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about the Folding Platform.

### Rating
* **Rating** (number, *): Rate your experience on the Folding Platform from 1 to 5 stars.

### Suggestion
* **Suggestion** (text, optional): If you have an idea for improvement, share it with us.

### Platform Feature
* **Platform Feature** (select_one, optional): Choose one of the available options to help us understand your preference.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further feedback.

### Date
* **Date** (date, *): Enter the date you used the Folding Platform.

### Time
* **Time** (time, optional): If you'd like to provide more specific feedback, enter the time you used the Folding Platform.

### Note
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or details about your experience.

### Category
* **Category** (select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more categories that apply to your feedback.

### Product Name
* **Product Name** (text, optional): If you were using a specific product during your feedback, enter its name.

### Category
* **Category** (select_multiple, optional): Note: This field should be used to select multiple categories, not product.
