# E Commerce Shopping Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their shopping experience on the e-commerce platform.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your product name or description in the `product_name` field.
2. Rate your experience by selecting one of the rating options in the `rating` field.
3. Share your feedback about your shopping experience in the `feedback` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `email` field to ensure we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
5. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field, but this is optional.
6. Select the date you made the purchase from the calendar in the `purchase_date` field.
7. Select the time of day you made the purchase in the `purchase_time` field.
8. Enter the amount you spent on the purchase in the `purchase_amount` field.
9. Choose the product you purchased from the options provided in the `product_id` field.
10. If you'd like to add any additional comments or information, you can do so in the `comments` field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`): Enter the name or description of the product you purchased.
* **Rating** (`rating`): Rate your shopping experience on a scale, with one star being the lowest and five stars being the highest.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Share your thoughts and opinions about your shopping experience.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number, but this is optional.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`): Select the date you made the purchase from the calendar.
* **Purchase Time** (`purchase_time`): Select the time of day you made the purchase.
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`): Enter the amount you spent on the purchase.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`): Choose the product you purchased from the options provided.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or information about your purchase.
