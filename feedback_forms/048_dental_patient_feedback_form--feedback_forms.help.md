# dental_patient_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental_patient_feedback_form is a patient feedback form used to gather comments and ratings on the quality of service provided by the dental practice. This form helps the practice identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select one or more options from the "Feedback Form" page to provide comments and ratings.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide a rating in the "Rating" field.
4. Type your feedback in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, Select Multiple, required/optional): Select one or more options to provide comments and ratings on your experience. Please choose all that apply.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, required/optional): Enter your name to provide a clear and concise way for us to identify you and acknowledge your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Number, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, required/optional): Provide a detailed and constructive comment about your experience. Be specific about what we could improve on or what we're doing well.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide specific feedback to help us improve.
- Keep your comments concise and clear.
- If you have any suggestions, please include them in your feedback section.
- If you have any questions, feel free to ask us.
