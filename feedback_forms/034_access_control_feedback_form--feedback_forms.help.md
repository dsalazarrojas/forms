# Access Control Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on the access control system's performance and usability. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make the system more efficient and effective for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you would like to be contacted for further details on your feedback.
2. Rate the reliability of card readers and the speed of gate or door response after a scan.
3. Rate the ease of use of the mobile app or portal, if applicable.
4. Check any issues you regularly face with the access control system.
5. Describe any specific technical issues you experience with the system.
6. Suggest one thing you would change about the access control system.
7. Rate your overall user experience with the system, from 1 to 10.
8. If you have any issues with security measures, select "Yes" or "No".
9. Select your primary role in the building to help us understand your perspective.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Role** (id: 3): Select the option that best represents your position in the building (Employee, Visitor, Contractor, or Security Staff).
* **Rate the reliability of card readers** (id: 5): Indicate the level of reliability you experience with the card readers, from "Very Reliable" to "Unreliable".
* **Rate the speed of gate or door response after scan** (id: 6): Rate the efficiency of the system's response time after a scan, from "Fast" to "Very Slow".
* **If using a mobile app or portal, rate the ease of use** (id: 7): Evaluate your experience with the digital interface, from "Very Easy" to "N/A" if you don't use the app.
* **Do you feel the current access control measures are effective?** (id: 9): Select "Yes" if you feel secure, "No" if you don't.
* **Select any issues you regularly face** (id: 10): Check all issues that apply to help us identify common problems.
* **Describe any specific technical issues in detail** (id: 11): Provide context and explanations for any issues you experience.
* **Suggest one thing you would change about the access control system** (id: 13): Offer constructive feedback on how to improve the system.
* **Overall User Experience Rating** (id: 15): Rate your overall satisfaction with the system, from 1 to 10.
* **May we contact you for further details on your feedback?** (id: 16): Select "Yes" to allow us to follow up on your feedback.
