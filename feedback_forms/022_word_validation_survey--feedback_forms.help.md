# Word Validation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides a step-by-step explanation of the Word Validation Survey form, including its purpose, how to complete it, and an explanation of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the word you are validating in the **Survey Title** field.
2. Enter your name (optional) in the **Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select one option from the **Select One** dropdown menu.
6. Select one or multiple options from the **Select Multiple** checkbox list.
7. Enter a specific date in the **Date** field.
8. (Optional) Enter a time in the **Time** field.
9. Add any additional comments in the **Note** field.
10. Click the **Submit** button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, `text`, required): A brief description of the word you are validating.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Your name, if you choose to input it.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Your email address that will be used for further contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Your phone number.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one option from the dropdown menu.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or multiple options from the checkbox list.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): A specific date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): A time (not required).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Submit your feedback.
