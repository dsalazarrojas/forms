# Ocean Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a tour or travel-related service. It helps the service provider understand the quality of their tour guides, enjoyable aspects of the tour, and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For questions with select_one options, choose the answer that best fits your experience.
3. For questions with select_multiple options, choose all the answers that apply.
4. For the text field, provide any additional comments or feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate the quality of our tour guides?** (`question_1`, `select_one`): This question asks you to rate the quality of our tour guides on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. Choose the answer that best describes your experience.
* **What did you find most enjoyable about our tour?** (`question_2`, `text`): This question is an open-ended question where you can describe the most enjoyable part of your tour.
* **Was the tour duration reasonable?** (`question_3`, `select_one`): This question asks you to rate whether the tour duration was reasonable or not. Choose the answer that best fits your experience.
* **Was the tour cost reasonable?** (`question_4`, `select_one`): This question is similar to the previous one, asking you to rate whether the tour cost was reasonable or not.
* **Did you experience any difficulties with the booking process?** (`question_5`, `select_multiple`): This question asks you to choose all the difficulties you experienced with the booking process, if any.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (`comment`, `text`): This question is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **How would you rate our overall experience?** (`question_6`, `select_one`): This question asks you to rate the overall experience on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. Choose the answer that best fits your experience.
