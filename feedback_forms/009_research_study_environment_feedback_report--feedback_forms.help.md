# Research Study Environment Feedback Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Study Environment Feedback Report form is designed to collect feedback on the study environment, settings, and support from users who have participated in a research study. This form helps to identify areas that need improvement and provide valuable insights for future study setups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the study environment and settings that you have experience with.
2. Provide your honest feedback on the environment, settings, and support.
3. Select the options that best describe your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Environment** (`study_environment`): A brief description of the study environment, including any notable features or challenges you faced.
* **Study Site** (`site_id`): Enter the ID number of the study site where you participated in the study.
* **Study Phase** (`study_phase`): Select the phase of the study you participated in (e.g., Site Initiation, Study Execution, Site Closure, or Other).
* **Site Name** (`site_name`): The name of the study site where you participated.
* **Research Question** (`research_question`): A brief description of the research question or topic of the study.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the study environment and settings.
* **Study Settings** (`study_setting_feedback`): Select the options that best describe the study settings you experienced (e.g., Environment, Resources, Equipment, Support).
* **Support Needed** (`support_needed`): Select the type of support you needed or would like to have during the study (e.g., Phone, Email, Video, Chat).
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Select who you were assigned to for support (e.g., Chat, Jim, Other).
