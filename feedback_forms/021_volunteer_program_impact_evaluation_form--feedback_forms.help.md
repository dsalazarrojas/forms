# Volunteer Program Impact Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Program Impact Evaluation Form is designed to gather information about the effectiveness and participant satisfaction with the volunteer program. This form is intended for program participants to provide their feedback and evaluation of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each field label and instruction to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Complete each field according to your honest and informed feedback.
3. Be as detailed as possible in the fields that ask for text-based comments or ratings.
4. Select the most accurate and honest ratings for each question.
5. Make sure to complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
6. Use the provided instructions for any additional guidance on specific fields, such as the date and time fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Program Effectiveness** (`1`, `number`, required: false): 
    This field is a numerical rating of the program's effectiveness. Please rate the program's effectiveness on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being not effective at all and 10 being very effective.
* **Program Objectives** (`2`, `text`, required: false): 
    Please provide a brief description of the program's objectives and how they were met or not met.
* **Participant Demographics** (`3`, `note`, required: false): 
    This field is for demographic information, but it seems to be a note type field. Please provide any relevant demographic information about your participation in the program.
* **Feedback Questions** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): 
    This field asks how likely you think the program was effective. Please select one of the following options: Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Not Likely, Not at All Likely.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`5`, `number`, required: false): 
    This field is another numerical rating, similar to the first field. Please rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-10.
* **Evaluation Form Comments** (`6`, `text`, required: false): 
    This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have about the program.
* **Additional Comments** (`7`, `text`, required: false): 
    Use this field to provide any additional information or feedback about the program.
* **Program Participant Rating** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Please select one or more of the following ratings for your participation in the program: Poor, Average, Good, Excellent.
* **Program Participant Rating 2** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Another rating field similar to the previous one.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Please select one or more of the following ratings for your satisfaction: Poor, Average, Good, Excellent.
* **Program Participant Rating 3** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Yet another rating field similar to the previous ones.
* **Form Completion Date** (`12`, `date`, required: false): 
    Please select the date when you completed this form.
* **Form Completion Time** (`13`, `time`, required: false): 
    Please select the time when you completed this form.

Note: This form is not a mandatory form, but it is suggested that all questions and fields should be completed as accurately and honestly as possible.
