# Online Coaching Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help coaches collect valuable information from clients during their coaching sessions. It helps coaches gather essential details about each client's progress, goals, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Client Name** field with the name of the client you are coaching.
2. Select a date for the **Session Date** field that matches the date of the session.
3. Choose a time for the **Session Time** field that matches the time of the session.
4. Type in the **Coaching Topic** field the main subject or area of discussion during the session.
5. Enter the client's **Client Goals** in the respective field.
6. Describe the client's **Client Progress** in the given space.
7. Write any important notes or comments from the coach in the **Coaches Note** field.
8. Record the client's **Client Feedback** in the field provided.
9. Select a date for the **Client Next Session** field if applicable.
10. In the **Coaches Comments** field, add any additional comments, observations, or insights from the coach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (Client Name)**: Enter the name of the client you are coaching.
* **Session Date (Session Date)**: Select the date of the session. This helps the coach keep track of the coaching progress over time.
* **Session Time (Session Time)**: Choose the time of the session to match the selected date.
* **Coaching Topic (Coaching Topic)**: Type in the main subject or area of discussion during the session.
* **Client Goals (Client Goals)**: Enter the client's goals for the session.
* **Client Progress (Client Progress)**: Describe the client's progress in the session.
* **Coaches Note (Coaches Note)**: Write any important notes or comments from the coach.
* **Client Feedback (Client Feedback)**: Record the client's feedback in the session.
* **Client Next Session (Client Next Session)**: Select a date for the next session if applicable.
* **Coaches Comments (Coaches Comments)**: Add any additional comments, observations, or insights from the coach.
