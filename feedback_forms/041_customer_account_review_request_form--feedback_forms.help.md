# Customer Account Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and requests for review of customer accounts. It helps our team to identify and process customer needs in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address to identify yourself as the customer.
2. Select the type of review request you are making (e.g., Review Request, Cancel Account, or Upgrade Account).
3. Provide any additional account details that might be helpful for our team to understand your request.
4. If you have any comments or notes about your request, please include them in the Review Request Comment field.
5. Choose the method you would like to be contacted (e.g., Phone, Email, Video Call, or In Person).
6. Select the date of your last contact with us.
7. Choose the method you used for your last contact (e.g., Phone, Email, Video Call, or In Person).
8. Provide details about your last contact.
9. Select the due date for your review request.
10. Finally, select the contact method you prefer to be contacted (e.g., Phone, Email, Video Call, or In Person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Account Review Request Form** (`customer_account_review_request_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address associated with your account.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Review Request Type** (`review_request_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of review request you are making (e.g., Review Request, Cancel Account, or Upgrade Account).
* **Account Details** (`account_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your account that might help our team understand your request.
* **Review Request Comment** (`review_request_comment`, text, required/optional): Add any comments or notes about your request.
* **Date of Last Contact** (`date_of_last_contact`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your last contact with us.
* **Last Contact Method** (`last_contact_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the method you used for your last contact (e.g., Phone, Email, Video Call, or In Person).
* **Last Contact Details** (`last_contact_details`, text, required/optional): Provide details about your last contact.
* **Review Request Due Date** (`review_request_due_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date by which you would like to receive your review request.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Phone, Email, Video Call, or In Person).
* **Review Request Status** (`review_request_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of your review request (e.g., Pending, Processing, or Completed).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Please do not fill this field. It is used by our team to assign a tool to process your request.

Note: This form is not intended to be completed multiple times. If you have already submitted a review request, please do not submit another request until the status of the previous request is "Completed".
