# Workplace Self Awareness Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Self Awareness Evaluation form is designed to help employees assess their self-awareness, self-regulation, and self-motivation skills, as well as their ability to navigate workplace dynamics and conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account your actual work experience and performance.
3. Review and update previous responses if any changes occur after completing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What motivates you to work** (`workplace_self_awareness_evaluation`, text, required): This question asks about what drives you to come to work every day. Think about what motivates you, whether it's the desire to learn, achieve a sense of accomplishment, or simply the paycheck. Provide a brief description of what gets you excited to go to work.
* **Self-awareness level** (`self_awareness_level`, number, required): Indicate on a scale of 1 to 10 how self-aware you are. This self-awareness is crucial for recognizing your strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement. A higher score doesn't always mean better; it's about being aware of yourself.
* **Self-perception skills** (`self_perception_skills`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply regarding your self-perception skills:
	+ Yes: You're aware of your self-perception skills.
	+ No: You don't think you have self-perception skills.
* **Communication skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your communication skills:
	+ Yes: You're a skilled communicator.
	+ No: You don't think you're a skilled communicator.
* **Emotional intelligence** (`emotional_intelligence`, number, required): Indicate on a scale of 1 to 10 how emotionally intelligent you are. This is about understanding and managing your emotions and those of others.
* **Time management skills** (`time_management_skills`, number, required): Indicate on a scale of 1 to 10 how well you manage your time. This is about prioritizing tasks and meeting deadlines.
* **Conflict resolution skills** (`conflict_resolution_skills`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your conflict resolution skills:
	+ Yes: You're skilled at resolving conflicts.
	+ No: You don't think you're skilled at resolving conflicts.
* **Feedback availability** (`feedback_availability`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply regarding your feedback availability:
	+ Yes: You're open to feedback.
	+ No: You don't think you're open to feedback.
* **Self development interest** (`self_development_interest`, text, required): Describe what interests you about self-development, whether it's learning a new skill, attending workshops, or reading books.
* **Workplace dynamics** (`workplace_dynamics`, note, required): Write a brief description of the dynamics in your workplace, including any challenges or successes.
* **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, number, required): Indicate on a scale of 1 to 10 how well you balance your work and personal life. A higher score doesn't mean more work; it's about finding a balance.
* **Self conflict resolution** (`self_conflict_resolution`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your self-conflict resolution skills:
	+ Yes: You're skilled at resolving conflicts.
	+ No: You don't think you're skilled at resolving conflicts.
* **Self reflection** (`self_reflection`, text, required): Describe your self-reflection habits, including how often you take time for self-reflection and what topics you reflect on.
* **Self improvement** (`self_improvement`, number, required): Indicate on a scale of 1 to 10 how much you value self-improvement. This is about recognizing your areas for growth and developing strategies to improve.
* **Self monitoring** (`self_monitoring`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply regarding your self-monitoring skills:
	+ Yes: You're skilled at monitoring yourself.
	+ No: You don't think you're skilled at monitoring yourself.
