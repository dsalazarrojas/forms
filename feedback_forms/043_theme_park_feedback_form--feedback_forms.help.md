# theme_park_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to a theme park, allowing us to gauge their overall experience, identify areas of improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the park's offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page to provide as much detail as possible about your experience at the theme park.
2. Select your favorite ride from the options provided.
3. Rate your ride satisfaction level by selecting all that apply.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience** (<type> text): Describe your overall experience at the theme park. Please provide a brief but detailed account of your visit, including any notable highlights or areas that need improvement.
* **Favorite Ride** (<type> select_one): Choose the ride that you enjoyed the most during your visit. Select one of the options: 
	+ The Incredible Journey
	+ Space Blasters
	+ Wild West
* **Ride Satisfaction** (<type> select_multiple): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the rides at the theme park. You can select one or more of the options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not at All Satisfied
* **Park Staff Interaction** (<type> number): Rate your interaction with park staff. Please select a number to indicate how responsive and helpful the staff were during your visit.
* **Ride Satisfaction** (<type> select_multiple): Indicate your level of satisfaction with each ride at the theme park. You can select one or more of the options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not at All Satisfied
* **Time** (<type> time): If you visited the theme park, please enter the time of your visit.
* **Date** (<type> date): Enter the date of your visit.
* **E-mail** (<type> email): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.
* **Phone** (<type> text): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.

## Tips
Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question. Your feedback is invaluable to us in improving the theme park experience.
