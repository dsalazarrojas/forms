# leadership_empathy_index_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership_empathy_index_survey form is designed to collect feedback on a leader's ability to demonstrate empathy in the workplace. This form is meant to be used by leaders to gauge their own self-awareness and self-improvement, and by team members to provide constructive feedback on their leader's performance.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoughtfully.
2. For each field, provide your genuine feedback on the leader's behavior or performance.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers, providing specific examples or anecdotes where relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. leadership_empathy_index**: Please select a numerical value from 1-5 to rate your perception of the leader's empathy index. 
* **2. leadership_skills**: Describe the leader's leadership skills, such as decision-making, problem-solving, or delegating tasks. 
* **3. communication_skills**: Describe the leader's communication style, including their verbal and non-verbal cues, active listening, and ability to articulate their expectations. 
* **4. employee_feedback**: Select the option that best describes your feedback about the leader's behavior towards employees: 
	+ Option 1: The leader shows genuine interest in employees' well-being and is approachable.
	+ Option 2: The leader is somewhat supportive, but could improve on showing interest in employees' well-being.
	+ Option 3: The leader is not very supportive of employees' well-being.
* **5. supportive_culture**: Select all options that describe the leader's role in fostering a positive and supportive work culture: 
	+ Option 1: The leader promotes a culture of respect and trust among employees.
	+ Option 2: The leader encourages open communication and active listening among team members.
	+ Option 3: The leader creates a safe space for employees to share their thoughts and opinions.
* **6. leader_feedback**: Select the option that best describes your feedback on the leader's behavior: 
	+ Option 1: The leader demonstrates high emotional intelligence.
	+ Option 2: The leader is somewhat effective in their emotional intelligence.
	+ Option 3: The leader struggles with emotional intelligence.
* **7. communication_effectiveness**: Please select a numerical value between 1-5 to rate the leader's ability to communicate effectively with employees. 
* **8. empathy_score**: Select a numerical value between 1-5 to rate the leader's empathy towards employees. 
* **9. leader_empathy_index**: Please select a numerical value between 1-5 to rate the leader's overall empathy index. 

Note: This form is intended for feedback and self-assessment purposes only. The scores are meant to provide a general indication of the leader's performance, not a definitive measurement.
