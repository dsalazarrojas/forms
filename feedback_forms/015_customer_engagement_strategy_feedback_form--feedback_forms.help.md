# Customer Engagement Strategy Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on our customer engagement strategy. It helps us understand their thoughts, opinions, and experiences with our customer support team, which enables us to refine our approach and provide better services. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide us with your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes your feedback.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., your business card).
4. Enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
5. If you have a phone number, please enter it for our follow-up.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with our customer engagement strategy (from 1 to 5).
7. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use this field.
8. Optionally, provide the date and time you submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to classify your feedback under a specific category. Please choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your experience with our customer engagement strategy. Be as specific as possible.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for our follow-up.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our customer engagement strategy (from 1 to 5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use this field.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): Optionally, enter the time you submitted this form.
