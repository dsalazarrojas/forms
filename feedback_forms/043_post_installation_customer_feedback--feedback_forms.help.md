# post_installation_customer_feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather post-installation feedback from customers who have already installed our product. It's a crucial step in helping us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the first field. This is a text field where you can enter your name.
2. Enter your **Email** in the next field. This is the email address we'll use to contact you if needed.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the following field. This will help us reach out to you if we need to discuss your feedback further.
4. Enter your **Company** name, if applicable.
5. Choose the **Product Used** from the list of options provided.
6. Select your **Satisfaction Level** with the product by choosing one of the options.
7. Share your **Comments** about your experience with the product in the text area.
8. Choose the date of the **Installation Date** of your product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): This is the field to enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): If you're representing a company, enter your company name.
* **Product Used** (`product_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the product you've installed from the provided options.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the options to express your satisfaction level with the product.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Share your thoughts and experiences with the product in this text area.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the product installation.

**Tips**

* Please fill in as much information as you can to help us understand your experience better.
* If you have any issues or concerns about your product installation, feel free to mention them in the comments field.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
