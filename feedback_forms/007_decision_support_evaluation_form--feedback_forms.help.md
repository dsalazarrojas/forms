# decision_support_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to evaluate the decision support provided by a service. We want to understand how satisfied you are with the decision support and gather information about your experience with the service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each page carefully.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a short introduction to the form. You can review the form and its purpose here.
* **Section 1: Section 1**: This section has a single question that asks how satisfied you are with the decision support. Select one of the options:
	+ I strongly agree (If you strongly agree with the support you received.)
	+ I somewhat agree (If you somewhat agree with the support you received.)
	+ I neither agree nor disagree (If you feel neutral about the support you received.)
	+ I somewhat disagree (If you somewhat disagree with the support you received.)
	+ I strongly disagree (If you strongly disagree with the support you received.)
* **How satisfied are you with the decision support?**: This question is a multiple-choice question that asks about your satisfaction level with the decision support. Select one of the options:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **What is the time you first interacted with this service?**: This question asks you to provide the date and time you first interacted with the service.
* **Briefly describe your experience with this service**: This question is a text field where you can describe your experience with the service in a few sentences.
* **How much time did you spend on this interaction?**: This question asks you to provide the amount of time you spent on the interaction with the service. This should be a numerical value.
* **How often will you use this service in the future?**: This question has multiple-choice options that ask about your future usage of the service. Select one or more of the options:
	+ Very often
	+ Somewhat often
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **How many times have you used this service?**: This question asks you to provide the number of times you have used this service.
* **Have you ever used this service before?**: This question is a single-choice question that asks if you have ever used the service before. Select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Additional feedback or suggestions?**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have about the service.
