# Falling Kisses Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with Falling Kisses. The purpose of this form is to allow event organizers and brands to understand user satisfaction and improve their offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose your answers from the provided options for each question.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Type your answer in the text field for open-ended questions.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the text field.
5. If you wish to provide your contact information, select the corresponding option.
