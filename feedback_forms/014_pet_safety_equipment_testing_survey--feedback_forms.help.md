# Pet Safety Equipment Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Safety Equipment Testing Survey is designed to collect feedback and information from users about pet safety equipment, including testing methods and results. This data will help us to better understand the safety and effectiveness of pet safety products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a category for the product you are testing (Pet Toys, Pet Clothing, or Pet Accessories).
2. Enter the name and description of the product you are testing.
3. Upload or enter a product image (optional).
4. Choose the testing method used (Mechanical, Electrical, or Chemical).
5. Enter any test results.
6. Select a test date.
7. Enter the time of the test (in 24-hour format, e.g., 14:30).
8. Enter your name and email address (for contact information).
9. Enter your phone number (for further contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select a category for the product you are testing (Pet Toys, Pet Clothing, or Pet Accessories).
* **Product name**: Enter the name of the product you are testing.
* **Product description**: Enter a brief description of the product.
* **Product image**: Upload or enter a product image (optional).
* **Testing method**: Choose the testing method used (Mechanical, Electrical, or Chemical).
* **Results**: Enter any test results, including any safety concerns or issues.
* **Test date**: Select a test date (in the format YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Test time**: Enter the time of the test (in 24-hour format, e.g., 14:30).
* **Contact name**: Enter your name for contact information.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address for further contact.
* **Contact phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
