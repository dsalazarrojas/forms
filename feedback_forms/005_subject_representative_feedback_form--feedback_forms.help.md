# Subject Representative Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subject Representative Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on a subject representative's performance, communication, and overall experience. This form ensures that users have a clear and efficient way to share their concerns and suggestions with the subject representative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant option for your concern or suggestion from the "Concerns and suggestions" dropdown menu.
2. Choose a "Concern type" from the "Concern type" dropdown menu to specify the nature of your concern.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure that the subject representative can contact you for further discussion.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field to provide an additional way for the subject representative to contact you.
5. Select a date and time for when your concern or suggestion occurred in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Enter any additional information or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Enter your school name in the "School name" field to provide context.
8. Enter the school ID in the "School ID" field to ensure that the subject representative is aware of your affiliation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Term** (`term`, number, optional): This field allows you to select the term during which your concern or suggestion occurred.
* **Concerns** (`concern`, text, optional): Please specify your concerns or issues with the subject representative.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestion`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas to improve the subject representative's performance or communication, enter them here.
* **Concerns and suggestions** (`concerns_suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu to indicate that your concern is either a "Concern" or a "Suggestion".
* **Concern type** (`concern_type`, select_one, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu to specify the nature of your concern.
* **School name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school where you had contact with the subject representative.
* **University name** (`university_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the university where you had contact with the subject representative.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that the subject representative can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to provide an additional way for the subject representative to contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date on which your concern or suggestion occurred.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time on which your concern or suggestion occurred.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information or comments you may have.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the school where you had contact with the subject representative.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure that the subject representative has a complete understanding of your concern or suggestion.
* Use the "Concerns and suggestions" dropdown menu to indicate that your concern is either a "Concern" or a "Suggestion".
* If you have any additional information or comments, enter them in the "Note" field.
* If you are concerned about your concern, choose the "Concern" option from the "Concerns and suggestions" dropdown menu.
* Make sure to enter your school ID and school name accurately to ensure that the subject representative is aware of your affiliation.
