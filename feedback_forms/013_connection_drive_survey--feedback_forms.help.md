# Connection Drive Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Connection Drive Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness and quality of the Connection Drive event. Your input helps us understand what we do well and where we can improve. Please provide your honest feedback to help us build better connections and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply fill out the form with your answers and feedback. Each section is labeled with a brief explanation of what we're asking. Please answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I participated as a* (select_one)**: Please select the category that best describes your role in the Connection Drive event.
	+ Attendee / Participant: You attended the event as a participant.
	+ Organizer / Staff: You were part of the organizing team or staff.
	+ Partner / Sponsor: You represented a partner or sponsor.
	+ Volunteer: You volunteered at the event.
* **Which Connection Drive location did you visit?* (select_one)**: Choose the location of the event you attended.
	+ Main Campus: You visited the main campus location.
	+ Downtown Hub: You visited the downtown hub location.
	+ Virtual / Online: You participated in an online event.
	+ Satellite Office: You visited a satellite office location.
* **How satisfied were you with the overall event?* (select_one)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
	+ 5 - Extremely Satisfied
	+ 4 - Satisfied
	+ 3 - Neutral
	+ 2 - Dissatisfied
	+ 1 - Extremely Dissatisfied
* **How would you rate the quality of connections you made?* (select_one)**: Evaluate the quality of connections you made during the event.
	+ 5 - Excellent
	+ 4 - Good
	+ 3 - Average
	+ 2 - Poor
	+ 1 - N/A
* **What was your single most valuable takeaway?* (text)**: Share your most valuable experience or takeaway from the event.
* **How well-organized was the event?* (select_one)**: Rate the organization of the event.
	+ 5 - Very well-organized
	+ 4 - Well-organized
	+ 3 - Adequately organized
	+ 2 - Poorly organized
	+ 1 - Disorganized
* **Was the timing and duration of the event appropriate?* (select_one)**: Evaluate the timing and duration of the event.
	+ Just right
	+ Too long
	+ Too short
* **Would you participate in a future Connection Drive?* (select_one)**: Indicate your interest in participating in future Connection Drives.
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Not sure
	+ False
* **May we contact you for further feedback or case studies?* (select_one)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or case studies.
	+ Yes, my email is below
	+ No, keep it anonymous
* **Email Address (optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or case studies, provide your email address.
