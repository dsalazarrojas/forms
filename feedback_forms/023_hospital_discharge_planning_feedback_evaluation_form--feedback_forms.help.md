# Hospital Discharge Planning Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their hospital discharge planning experience. The form aims to gather information on their satisfaction with the discharge process, communication with hospital staff, and overall experience in the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our services.
3. Don't hesitate to add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Discharge Planning Form Section (id: 1)**: This section is a brief introduction to the form and provides context for the questions that follow.
* **Patient Feedback (id: 2)**: Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience during the hospital discharge process. Please be honest and provide specific examples.
* **Discharge Experience (id: 3)**: Describe your experience during the hospital stay, including any challenges you faced, what went well, and what didn't.
* **Discharge Planning (id: 4)**: Share your thoughts on how well the hospital staff communicated with you about your discharge plan.
* **Communication Effectiveness (id: 5)**: Provide feedback on how effectively the hospital staff communicated with you during your stay.
* **Overall Rating (id: 6)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the hospital discharge planning process on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Recommendations (id: 7)**: If you have any suggestions or recommendations for improving the hospital discharge planning process, please share them here.
* **Additional Feedback (id: 8)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Patient Satisfaction (id: 9)**: Rate your satisfaction with the care you received from the hospital staff.
* **Discharge Planning Process (id: 10)**: Describe how well the hospital staff explained the discharge process to you.
* **Communication Effectiveness 1-5 (ids: 11-15)**: Rate the hospital staff's communication effectiveness in different areas (e.g., explaining test results, medication, etc.).
* **Hospital Staff (ids: 16-20)**: Rate the hospital staff's professionalism, empathy, and overall quality of care you received.
* **Hospital Environment (ids: 21-23)**: Describe your experience with the hospital environment, including cleanliness, comfort, and amenities.
* **Submission (id: 25)**: Enter your contact information to receive a copy of your submission and any follow-up communication from the hospital.

Note: Each field is a text input field, so please provide as much detail as possible in each section. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
