# Cocktail Equipment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on cocktail equipment from bar professionals and enthusiasts. It's meant to help manufacturers and suppliers of cocktail equipment understand their products' strengths and weaknesses, allowing them to improve their offerings and provide better products for the industry. To ensure accurate and helpful feedback, please fill out this form carefully and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of equipment you're reviewing.
2. Provide your name and email address.
3. Answer questions about your profession and the equipment's performance.
4. Describe your experience with the equipment, including how long you've used it and how often you use it.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the equipment and its value for money.
6. Provide specific feedback on the equipment's quality and performance.
7. Mention any strengths or weaknesses you've observed.
8. Decide whether you'd recommend the equipment to others.
9. Share any additional comments or thoughts about the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, Text, Required): This is the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): This is the contact email address of the person providing feedback.
* **Bar or Establishment Name** (`bar_name`, Text, Optional): If you're not a bar professional, you can leave this field blank.
* **Profession** (`profession`, Select One, Required): Select your profession from the options: Professional bartender, Home bartender, Enthusiast, Student, or Other.
* **Equipment Name or Product** (`equipment_name`, Text, Required): Describe the equipment you're reviewing.
* **Equipment Category** (`equipment_category`, Select One, Required): Choose a category from the options: Shakers and mixing tools, Jiggers and measures, Glassware, Strainers and filters, Bottle pourers, Ice tools, Barware sets, or Other.
* **Manufacturer or Brand** (`manufacturer`, Text, Optional): If you're familiar with the manufacturer, feel free to specify.
* **Date of Purchase** (`purchase_date`, Date, Optional): If you purchased the equipment, you can provide the date of purchase.
* **How Long Have You Used This Equipment?** (`duration_used`, Select One, Required): Answer how long you've used the equipment, from Less than 1 month to Over 1 year.
* **Frequency of Use** (`frequency_use`, Select One, Required): Rate how often you use the equipment, from Daily to Rarely.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): Choose how satisfied you are with the equipment, from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
* **Quality and Durability** (`quality_rating`, Select One, Required): Rate the equipment's quality and durability, from Excellent to Very poor.
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, Select One, Required): Rate how easy or difficult it is to use the equipment, from Very easy to Very difficult.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, Select One, Required): Choose whether the equipment's price is fair, from Excellent value to Very poor value.
* **What do you like about this equipment?** (`strengths`, Text, Required): Describe the strengths of the equipment.
* **What could be improved?** (`weaknesses`, Text, Required): Mention any weaknesses or areas for improvement.
* **Would you recommend this equipment?** (`would_recommend`, Select One, Required): Decide whether you'd recommend the equipment to others, from Definitely to Definitely not.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the equipment.
