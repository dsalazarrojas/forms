# Employee Grievance Handling Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees provide feedback on how their grievances were handled during the resolution process. It helps managers assess and improve the grievance handling process, gathering information on the employee's experience, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the grievance handling rating: Provide a rating on how you think your grievance was handled.
2. Describe the issue: Clearly explain the problem you experienced.
3. Provide feedback: Share your thoughts on how the problem was resolved (if at all).
4. Suggestions for improvement: Offer any suggestions you have to make the grievance handling process better.
5. Personal details: Enter your contact information and employee details.
6. Resolution evaluation: Rate how satisfied you are with the resolution.
7. Comments: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have.
8. Sign off: Sign and date the form to confirm completion.
