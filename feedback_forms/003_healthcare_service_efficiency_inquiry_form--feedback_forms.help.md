# Healthcare Service Efficiency Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients on the efficiency of healthcare services they received. By using this form, patients can report their experiences and suggestions for making healthcare services more efficient and patient-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your Patient Name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your visit in the "Visit Date" field. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the name of your doctor in the "Doctor Name" field.
4. Describe the service bottleneck you experienced during your visit in the "Service Bottleneck" field.
5. Select the specific service bottleneck you would like to report by choosing one of the provided options in the "Select a Service Bottleneck" field.
6. Rate the efficiency of your visit on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "Visit Efficiency" field.
7. Provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "E-Mail" field (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).
9. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (if you'd like to be contacted by phone).
10. If you've been assigned a tool to help with your care, enter its name in the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. If you'd like to attach a file with more information, enter its name in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Visit Date** (`Visit Date`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the doctor who treated you.
* **Service Bottleneck** (`Service Bottleneck`, text, required): Describe the issue you experienced during your visit, e.g., long waiting time, communication problems.
* **Select a Service Bottleneck** (`Select a Service Bottleneck`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options that best describes your issue:
	+ Waiting time
	+ Communication between doctor and nurse
	+ Medical staff shortage
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Visit Efficiency** (`Visit Efficiency`, number, required): Rate the efficiency of your visit on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving healthcare services.
* **E-Mail** (`E-Mail`, email, required): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, required): If you were assigned a tool to help with your care, enter its name.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, text, required): If you'd like to attach a file with more information, enter its name.
