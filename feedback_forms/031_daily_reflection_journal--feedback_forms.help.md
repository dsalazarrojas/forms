# Daily Reflection Journal - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Reflection Journal form is designed to help you reflect on your daily experiences, emotions, and thoughts. It's a tool for introspection and personal growth, allowing you to identify your emotional state, reflect on significant events or thoughts of the day, make note of your goals, and record suggestions for improvement. This form is meant to be a quick and simple way to capture your daily reflections, helping you in your personal or professional development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **How you feel today**: In the first field, simply type or write how you feel today. This can be as short or as detailed as you like, depending on what comes up for you.
2. **Thought of the Day**: This field is for recording a significant thought you had during the day. Try to summarize your thoughts concisely.
3. **Emotional state**: Here, select how you felt most of your day. This helps in understanding your emotional highs and lows. You can choose from options such as Happy, Sad, Angry, Frustrated, or Bored.
4. **Feelings**: Write down your feelings about your day. This could be about anything that stood out to you, positive or negative. 
5. **Reflections**: This field is for recording any reflections or insights you had today. Think of anything that struck you as noteworthy or valuable.
6. **Goals**: Note down any personal or professional goals you're working towards or have set for yourself. This is your chance to review and update them.
7. **Suggestions**: If you have ideas on how to improve things, jot them down here. This could be about your work, your personal life, or anything else that comes to mind.
8. **Next steps**: Lastly, think about the next steps you need to take. This could be about implementing new ideas, tackling challenges, or moving forward with projects.

## Tips

* Be honest and as detailed as you need to be in each field.
* This form is for personal use, so feel free to be as brief or as expansive as you like.
* It's okay to skip questions if they don't apply to you on a particular day.
* Remember, the more you use this form, the more you'll benefit from it.

This guide is designed to be straightforward and easy to follow, helping users get the most out of the "Daily Reflection Journal" form.
