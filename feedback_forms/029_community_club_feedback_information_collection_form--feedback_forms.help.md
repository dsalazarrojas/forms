# Club Feedback Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting feedback from club members on their experience and engagement with the club. Feedback is essential for the club to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance member experience and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your name if you wish to remain anonymous, you can skip this field.
2.  Select your current membership status.
3.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the club.
4.  Choose your favorite activities offered by the club. You can select multiple activities.
5.  Provide suggestions for improvement for the club.
6.  Rate your likelihood of recommending the club to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional)
This field is for your name if you wish to remain identified. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can skip this field.

### **Membership status** (`membership_status`, `select_one`, required)
Select your current status as a member of the club. Choose from the following options:

*   Current member
*   Former member
*   Non member

### **Overall satisfaction with club** (`satisfaction_overall`, `select_one`, required)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the club. Choose from the following options:

*   Very satisfied
*   Satisfied
*   Neutral
*   Dissatisfied
*   Very dissatisfied

### **Favorite club activities** (`favorite_activities`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select all the activities that you enjoy the most from the following options. You can choose multiple activities:

*   Social events
*   Workshops
*   Networking
*   Competitions

### **Suggestions for improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional)
Provide your suggestions on how the club can better serve members. Be as specific as possible with your comments.
