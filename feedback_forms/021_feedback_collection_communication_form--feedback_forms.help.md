# feedback_collection_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and communication information from users, allowing our team to address specific issues and concerns efficiently. This form should be used for issues that require personal contact information and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form header with your preferred header text.
2. Select your contact details from the options provided.
3. Enter your valid email address.
4. Provide your phone number (if applicable).
5. Share your feedback in the text field.
6. Choose "submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header** (1, text, optional): This field is for you to enter a brief description of the issue or concern you are reporting.
* **contact_details** (2, select, optional): Select your contact details from the options provided.
* **email** (3, email, required): Enter your valid email address for our team to contact you.
* **phone** (4, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for our team to contact you more efficiently.
* **feedback** (5, text, required): Share your feedback regarding the issue or concern.
* **submit** (6, select, optional): Choose "submit" to send the form to our team, or "cancel" to discard your entry.
