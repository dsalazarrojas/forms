# Certification Advisor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with a certification advisor. The information gathered will help improve the quality of services provided by the advisors and enhance the overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with accurate information about your experience with the certification advisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Date**: The date on which you submitted this feedback.
* **Client Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **Certification Advisor Name**: Enter the name of your assigned certification advisor.
* **Advisory Period**: Select the duration of the advisory relationship. (Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, Over 1 year)
* **Certification Program**: Enter the name of the certification program you are pursuing.
* **Meeting Frequency**: Select how often you met with your advisor (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, As needed).
* **Advisor Knowledge**: Rate your advisor's knowledge of the certification process. (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Advisor Responsiveness**: Rate how responsive your advisor was. (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Communication Clarity**: Rate how clear your advisor's communication was. (Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Very unclear)
* **Guidance Quality**: Rate the quality of guidance provided by your advisor. (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Support Level**: Rate how supported you felt throughout the process. (Very supported, Supported, Neutral, Unsupported, Very unsupported)
* **Advisor Preparedness**: Rate how well-prepared your advisor was for meetings. (Always prepared, Usually prepared, Sometimes prepared, Rarely prepared, Never prepared)
* **Goal Setting Assistance**: Rate whether your advisor helped you set clear goals. (Exceeded expectations, Met expectations, Below expectations, No goal setting provided)
* **Resource Provision**: Rate whether your advisor provided helpful resources. (Excellent resources, Good resources, Adequate resources, Limited resources, No resources provided)
* **Problem Solving Ability**: Rate how well your advisor helped resolve issues. (Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, Poor)
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your advisor. (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied)
* **Would Recommend**: Would you recommend this advisor to others? (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably not, Definitely not)
* **Advisor Strengths**: Enter any strengths or positive qualities of your advisor.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Enter any areas where your advisor could improve.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other feedback about your advisory experience.
