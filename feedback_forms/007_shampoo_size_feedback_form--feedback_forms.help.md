# Shampoo Size Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shampoo Size Feedback Form is used to collect feedback on the size of a shampoo product. This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our product and make improvements as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your title and feedback in the designated fields.
2. Answer the questions about the size of the shampoo, selecting the options that best describe your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can add them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your feedback, such as "Feedback on Shampoo Size".
* **How do you like the size of this shampoo?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the size of the shampoo. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Is the size too small?** (`too_small`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if the shampoo is too small, "False" if it is not too small, or "Not Sure" if you are unsure.
* **Is the size too large?** (`too_large`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if the shampoo is too large, "False" if it is not too large, or "Not Sure" if you are unsure.
* **Is this the perfect size?** (`perfect_size`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: "True", "False", or "Not Sure".
* **What size do you think this should be?** (`size_opinion`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following options: "Small", "Medium", "Large", or "Extra Large".
* **Date of Purchase** (`date_of_purchase`, date, optional): Enter the date you purchased the shampoo.
* **Day** (`date_of_purchase_day`, text, optional): Enter the day you purchased the shampoo.
* **Month** (`date_of_purchase_month`, text, optional): Enter the month you purchased the shampoo.
* **Year** (`date_of_purchase_year`, text, optional): Enter the year you purchased the shampoo.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns you have about the shampoo size.
* **Submitted Via** (`submitted_via`, select_one, optional): Select how you submitted this form, such as "Web", "Mobile", or "In-Person".
* **Submitted to** (`submitted_to`, select_multiple, optional): Select who you submitted this form to, such as "Sales", "Customer Service", "Marketing", or "Other".
* **Submitted from** (`submitted_from`, select_multiple, optional): Select where you submitted this form from, such as "Store", "Online", or "Other".
* **Store Information** (`store_info`, text, optional): Enter any information about the store where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the store where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the store where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`, text, optional): Enter the email of the store where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Store Website** (`store_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the store where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional): Enter the hours of operation of the store where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`, text, optional): Enter the name of the store manager where you purchased the shampoo.
* **Comments** (`store_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the store where you purchased the shampoo.
