# Law Enforcement Performance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals who have interacted with law enforcement. Your input is crucial in helping us identify areas of improvement and enhance the quality of service provided by our officers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you've answered all required questions.
2. Select the correct options for each question, using the provided dropdown menus or typing in the fields.
3. Provide clear and concise comments where requested.
4. Submit your feedback to help us improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. How was your interaction with the police?**
	+ Select all that apply: 
		+ Yes
		+ No
* **2. What was the name of the police officer you interacted with?**
	+ Enter the full name of the officer you interacted with.
* **3. Which police department did you interact with?**
	+ Enter the name of the police department that served you.
* **4. What date did you interact with the police on?**
	+ Enter the date (mm/dd/yyyy) of your interaction.
* **5. What time did you interact with the police on?** (Optional)
	+ Enter the time of day (HH:MM format) of your interaction, if applicable.
* **6. Rate your satisfaction with the police interaction** (Required)
	+ Rate your satisfaction on a scale of 1-10.
* **7. Comments about your interaction with the police** (Optional)
	+ Provide any additional comments or observations about your experience.
* **8. Your email address** (Required)
	+ Enter your active email address for follow-up or further communication.
* **9. Your phone number** (Required)
	+ Enter your active phone number for further communication.
* **10. Additional comments** (Optional)
	+ Provide any additional comments or observations that may be helpful in our improvement efforts.
* **11. Additional feedback about the police interaction** (Optional)
	+ Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **12. Did you experience any issues during your interaction with the police?** (Required)
	+ Select True if you experienced issues; otherwise, select False.
* **13. Can you provide more details about the incident?** (Optional)
	+ Provide any additional information about the issues you experienced.
* **14. What follow-up action do you recommend the police department take as a result of your feedback?** (Optional)
	+ Select all that apply:
		+ Yes

Note: All input fields are subject to review to ensure they align with the intended use of the information collected.
