# Enterprise Support Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for customers or employees to provide feedback on their experience with our organization. This form is used to gather information about their satisfaction and any issues they may have encountered. The feedback will be reviewed and used to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one or more options from the "Feedback Form" dropdown menu to indicate your level of satisfaction.
2. Provide a rating of your experience (if applicable).
3. Enter any comments you may have about your experience.
4. Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up).
5. Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted via phone).
6. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback Form (Select Multiple)
 * **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, Select Multiple, optional): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to indicate your level of satisfaction. You can choose "Yes" if you are satisfied with your experience, or "No" if you are not.

### Rating (Number, optional)
 * **Rating** (`rating`, Number, optional): If you have a comment, you can also provide a rating of your experience. Please note that this is not required.

### Comment (Text, optional)
 * **Comment** (`comment`, Text, optional): Enter any comments you may have about your experience. This is a good place to provide details about any issues you encountered or things we could improve on.

### Email (Email, optional)
 * **Email** (`email`, Email, optional): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address here.

### Phone (Text, optional)
 * **Phone** (`phone`, Text, optional): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number here.
