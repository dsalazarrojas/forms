# Photography Client Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Photography Client Feedback Form" is a tool used by photography clients to provide their feedback about their experience with a photography service. This form is designed to collect information about their satisfaction and help photographers improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form.
2. Enter your name as a client.
3. Select how you felt about your experience (Not Satisfied, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied).
4. Provide a detailed description of your experience.
5. Enter your email address if you want to be contacted.
6. Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
7. Add any additional comments or information.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.
9. Click the "Cancel" button to cancel the form and start again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the form title.
* **Client Name** (text, optional): Enter your name as a client.
* **How Was Your Experience?** (select_one, optional): Select one of the three options: Not Satisfied, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied.
* **Feedback** (text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your experience.
* **Feedback Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted.
* **Feedback Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.
* **Form Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Submit** (text, optional): Click this button to complete and submit your feedback.
* **Cancel** (text, optional): Click this button to cancel the form and start again.
* **Error** (text, optional): This field is not used in the form, so you can ignore it.
* **Info** (text, optional): This field is not used in the form, so you can ignore it.
* **Warning** (text, optional): This field is not used in the form, so you can ignore it.

Note that all fields are optional, but providing more information will help photographers understand your feedback better. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
