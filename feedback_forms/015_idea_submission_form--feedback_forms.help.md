# Idea Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Idea Submission Form is a platform for our employees, customers, and partners to provide feedback, suggestions, and ideas about our company's performance, culture, and overall experience. This form helps us gather valuable insights and input from various stakeholders, which we use to identify areas for improvement and implement changes that benefit everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
* Make sure to review all pages of the form before submitting it.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, please be specific and provide concrete examples.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the best way to improve our company?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 1`, `text`, optional): Please share your thoughts on how our company can be improved. This could be related to any aspect of our business, such as products, services, processes, or culture.
* **How do you think you can improve our company?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 2`, `text`, optional): This question allows you to specify how you, as an individual or organization, can contribute to improving our company.
* **What is the most significant improvement you have observed in your time at the company?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 3`, `select_multiple`, required): This question is asking about any major positive changes you've noticed during your time with us. You can select all that apply from the options provided.
* **Do you have any other feedback or suggestions?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 4`, `note`, optional): This is an open-ended question for any additional thoughts or ideas you may have.
* **How do you feel about our company culture?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 5`, `select_one`, required): Please choose one of the options to describe your feelings about our company culture.
* **Is there anything else we can do to make our company better?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 6`, `date`, optional): This question gives you the opportunity to suggest any other improvements or changes we can make.
* **How often would you like to receive emails from us?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 7`, `select_one`, required): Please select how frequently you'd like to receive emails from us.
* **How do you rate our customer service?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 8`, `number`, optional): This question allows you to rate our customer service on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Have you had a positive experience with our customer service department?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 9`, `select_one`, required): Please choose yes or no, indicating whether you've had a positive experience with our customer service.
* **Any other comments?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 10`, `note`, optional): This is an open-ended question for any other comments or thoughts you may have.

* **What is the best time to contact you?** (`Idea Submission Form Page 12`, `time`, optional): This question helps us know when you're available to be contacted.
