# Museum Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Museum Survey Form" is designed to collect feedback from visitors to the museum, allowing us to improve and create a better environment for future visits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely.
2. Provide as much detail as you can about your visit experience.
3. Answer every question honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Hello** (`museum_survey_form_1`, text, Optional): This is a text field where you can briefly introduce yourself.
* **Personal Information** (`personal_info`, text, Optional): Please provide a short description of yourself, including your name, email address (if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback), and any other relevant details.
* **Visited Times** (`museum_visit`, number, Optional): How many times have you visited our museum? This will help us understand your familiarity with our museum.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or have any questions, please provide your email address.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, Optional): Share your thoughts about your visit, what you liked or disliked, and any suggestions you have for improvement.
