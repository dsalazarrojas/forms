# Constructive Feedback And Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals reflect on their performance, identify areas for improvement, and provide a summary of peer feedback received.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Respondent name** (if you want to provide your name for this feedback process).
2. Enter your **Role** (Job title or function).
3. List your **major accomplishments** during the period under **Achievements Last Period**.
4. Identify **key areas for improvement** under **Areas for Improvement**.
5. Summarize **Peer feedback received** under **Peer Feedback Summary** (if applicable).
6. Outline **Concrete steps to improve performance** under **Development Actions Planned** (if applicable).
7. Choose your **Overall Self Rating** from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent name**: A field to provide your name, if you want to be identified for this feedback process. This is optional.
* **Role**: Enter your job title or function to help provide context for your reflections.
* **Achievements last period**: List the significant accomplishments you've achieved during the period being evaluated. This will help you reflect on your successes and growth.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify the key areas where you'd like to improve or work on during the next period. Be specific and honest with yourself.
* **Peer feedback summary**: Summarize feedback received from your peers, if any. This will help you understand how others perceive your performance and identify any blind spots.
* **Development actions planned**: If you've received feedback or identified areas to improve, outline specific actions you plan to take to address these areas. Be concrete and realistic about your plans.
* **Overall self rating**: Evaluate your performance during the period being evaluated, using the provided options: Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Needs improvement.
