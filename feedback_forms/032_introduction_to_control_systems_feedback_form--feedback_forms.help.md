# Introduction To Control Systems Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants about their experience with control systems courses. The purpose of this form is to help course administrators understand what aspects of the course are working well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Answer the "Course" question by selecting "Yes" if you have taken the course, and "No" otherwise.
3. Answer the "Session" question by selecting "Yes" if you have attended the session, and "No" otherwise.
4. Rate your overall feedback experience in the "Feedback Rating" field by selecting "Good" or "Fair".
5. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field.
6. Suggest improvements or changes in the "Suggestions" field.
7. Rate your participant-instructor experience in the "Participant Instructor Rating" field.
8. Estimate the time spent on the "Content" field.
9. Evaluate the instructor's facilitation in the "Instructor Facilitation" field.
10. Answer the "Materials" question by selecting "Yes" if the materials were sufficient, and "No" otherwise.
11. Calculate the average feedback rating in the "Average Feedback Rating" field.
12. Rate your overall experience in the "Overall Rating" field.
13. Evaluate the overall average rating in the "Overall Average Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please provide your name as a participant.
* **Course** (`course_id`, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if you have taken the course, and "No" otherwise.
* **Session** (`session_id`, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if you have attended the session, and "No" otherwise.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, select_multiple, required): Rate your overall feedback experience by selecting "Good" or "Fair".
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required): Suggest improvements or changes.
* **Participant Instructor Rating** (`participant_instructor_rating`, number, required): Rate your participant-instructor experience.
* **Content** (`content`, time, required): Estimate the time spent on content.
* **Instructor Facilitation** (`instructor_facilitation`, number, required): Evaluate the instructor's facilitation.
* **Materials** (`materials`, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if materials were sufficient, and "No" otherwise.
* **Average Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating_average`, select_one, required): Calculate the average feedback rating.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience.
* **Overall Average Rating** (`overall_average_rating`, select_one, required): Evaluate the overall average rating.

**Tips**

* Be honest and provide detailed feedback to help us improve our courses.
