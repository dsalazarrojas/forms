# cybersecurity_awareness_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cybersecurity awareness survey form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their perceptions of cybersecurity threats, reporting procedures, and security measures. It aims to help the company understand employees' knowledge, threat practices, and training and improvement suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page according to your knowledge and experience.
2. Fill in the text fields with your thoughts and suggestions.
3. Select the options that best match your answers from the provided lists.
4. Make sure to review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Knowledge** (page 1):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on your current knowledge of cybersecurity threats and best practices.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your understanding of the topic.
* **Threat and Practices** (page 2):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the types of threats you are aware of and the practices you use to protect against them.
	+ Select all the threats and practices that you are familiar with or use.
* **Training and Improvement** (page 3):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the training and improvement suggestions for the company's cybersecurity policies and procedures.
	+ Please provide any suggestions you have for enhancing the company's cybersecurity training and improvement plans.
* **Cybersecurity Threats** (page 4):
	+ This field asks for your awareness of the types of cybersecurity threats you are familiar with.
	+ Select all the threats you are aware of.
* **Reporting Procedure** (page 5):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the reporting procedure for cybersecurity incidents.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the procedure you use or suggest improvements.
* **Reporting Frequency** (page 6):
	+ This field asks for your preferred reporting frequency for cybersecurity incidents.
	+ Select the frequency that best suits your needs.
* **Reporting Channels** (page 7):
	+ This field asks for your preferred channels for reporting cybersecurity incidents.
	+ Select all the channels you would prefer to use.
* **Reporting Responsible** (page 8):
	+ This field asks for your preferred authority to report to for cybersecurity incidents.
	+ Select the authority you would prefer to report to.
* **Training Topics** (page 9):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the cybersecurity training topics that are most important.
	+ Select all the topics that you think are essential.
* **Reporting Procedure** (page 10):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the reporting procedure for cybersecurity incidents.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the procedure you use or suggest improvements.
* **Feedback** (page 11):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or feedback on the survey or the company's cybersecurity policies and procedures.
	+ Please use this space to provide any suggestions or concerns.
* **Security Policies** (page 12):
	+ This field asks for your awareness of the types of security policies that are in place.
	+ Select all the policies you are familiar with.
* **Data Breach** (page 13):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on whether you think the company has experienced a data breach.
	+ Select 'True' if you think the company has experienced a data breach.
* **Security Training** (page 14):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the types of security training provided to employees.
	+ Select all the training topics that you think are essential.
* **Incident Response** (page 15):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the incident response procedures in place.
	+ Select all the procedures you are familiar with.
* **Security Measures** (page 16):
	+ This field asks for your awareness of the types of security measures in place.
	+ Select all the measures you are familiar with.
* **Security Budget** (page 17):
	+ This field asks for your thoughts on the company's security budget.
	+ Provide a numerical value for your answer.
* **Reporting Frequency Aware** (page 18):
	+ This field asks for your awareness of the reporting frequency for cybersecurity incidents.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your answer.
