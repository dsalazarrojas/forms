# Library Digital Resource Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about library digital resources. It helps us understand their experiences and opinions about the resources, allowing us to improve and refine them for better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your user information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide information about the library digital resource you are providing feedback on.
3. Share your feedback about the resource, including any comments or suggestions.
4. Rate the resource using the provided scale (1-5).
5. Explain your rating with a brief comment.
6. If you have any contact information, such as an email or phone number, you can provide it for follow-up communication.
7. If you'd like, you can include the date you used the resource.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, optional): Please provide any relevant details about yourself, such as your name, department, or role.
* **Resource Information** (Resource Information, text, optional): Describe the library digital resource you are providing feedback on, including its title, URL, or any other relevant details.
* **Resource Feedback** (Resource Feedback, text, optional): Share your thoughts, opinions, or suggestions about the resource, including what worked well and what didn't.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the resource.
* **Rating** (Rating, number, optional): Rate the resource using the provided scale (1-5).
* **Rating Scale** (Rating Scale, number, optional): Note: This field is not actually a field but an explanatory label for the rating field. You're already using the rating field, so feel free to remove this one.
* **Rating Explanation** (Rating Explanation, text, optional): Provide a brief comment explaining your rating.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): If you have any email address you'd like us to contact you at, please provide it.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): If you have any phone number you'd like us to contact you at, please provide it.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): If you'd like to include the date you used the resource, please provide it.
