# Legal Service Quality Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the quality of legal services provided by our organization. Your input is valuable in helping us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Provide a clear and concise rating of your experience (if applicable).
3. Describe your experience with the legal service, including any strengths and weaknesses.
4. If you have a suggestion for improvement, please provide it.
5. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up).
6. If you have a phone number, please enter it.
7. Select whether you submitted your feedback anonymously or if you want it to be attributed to you.
8. Select the person you submitted your feedback to (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a space for you to briefly explain why you are providing feedback. Please keep it short and to the point.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, not required): If you have had an experience with our legal service, please provide a rating from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Legal Service** (`legal_service`, `text`, not required): Describe the specific legal service you experienced.
* **Lawyer** (`lawyer`, `text`, not required): If you were working with a lawyer, please provide their name (or a general description).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): Please share any comments, suggestions, or concerns you have about our legal service.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): If you want to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): If you have a phone number, please enter it.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, not required): Please note the date you submitted your feedback.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, not required): Select whether you submitted your feedback anonymously or if you want it to be attributed to you.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_multiple`, not required): If you submitted your feedback to someone specific, select their name from the list.
 
## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Avoid using profanity or abusive language.
* We value your input and will use it to improve our services.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
