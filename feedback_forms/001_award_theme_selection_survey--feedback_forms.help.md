# Award Theme Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Award Theme Selection Survey is used to gather information from respondents on their preferences and suggestions for award themes. This helps us understand what themes are important to you and what we should consider for future award ceremonies.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure you are completing this form for yourself, as the responses will be reviewed for individual preferences.
2.  Fill in your full name, email address, and department or team name as prompted.
3.  Select your top three theme preferences from the list, and rate the importance of theme selection to you.
4.  If you have any additional comments or theme suggestions, please share them in the text boxes provided.
5.  If you have any feedback on the theme selection process, please share it with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name** (`respondent_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the award ceremony.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): We will use this to contact you about the award, so please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Department or Team** (`department_or_team`, Text, Required): Indicate which department or team you represent.
*   **First Theme Choice** (`theme_preference_1`, Select One, Required): Select your top choice for the theme of the award ceremony.
*   **Second Theme Choice** (`theme_preference_2`, Select One, Optional): If you have a second preference, select it here (if not, you can skip this field).
*   **Third Theme Choice** (`theme_preference_3`, Select One, Optional): Select your third preference for the theme of the award ceremony (if not, you can skip this field).
*   **How Important Is The Theme Selection To You?** (`importance_rating`, Number, Required): Rate from 1 (not important) to 10 (very important) how important the theme selection is to you.
*   **Do You Have A Theme Suggestion Not Listed Above?** (`theme_suggestion`, Text, Optional): If you have a theme suggestion not listed above, please describe it here.
*   **Should Budget Be A Consideration In Theme Selection?** (`budget_consideration`, Select One, Optional): Choose how much budget should be a consideration in theme selection.
*   **Venue Compatibility Rating** (`venue_compatibility`, Select One, Optional): Rate how well your theme matches the available venues.
*   **Summarize Attendee Feedback On Themes** (`attendee_feedback`, Text, Optional): Share any feedback you have heard from attendees on the themes.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Share any other thoughts or suggestions you may have.
