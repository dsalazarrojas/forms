# internship_placement_success_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Placement Success Assessment Form is designed to collect feedback and data on the success of an internship placement. This form helps assess the effectiveness of the internship program in meeting its goals and provides valuable insights for future improvement. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the data collected is reliable and actionable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your knowledge.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* Save your progress frequently to avoid losing any entered information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Internship Program**: Please enter the name of the internship program that the intern was placed in. (text, required: false)
* **Feedback**: Please provide any feedback you may have on the internship placement process. (text, required: false)
* **Employer**: How would you rate the performance of the intern's employer during the placement? (select_one, options: [Positive, Neutral, Negative])
* **Intern**: How would you rate the intern's performance during the placement? (select_multiple, options: [Strongly Disagree, Somewhat Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree])
* **Program Duration**: Please enter the duration of the internship placement. (date, required: false)
* **Placement Date**: Please enter the date of the internship placement. (date, required: false)
* **Placement Status**: What is the current status of the placement? (select_one, options: [Active, Inactive])
* **Feedback Date**: Please enter the date you are providing feedback on. (date, required: false)
* **Placement Type**: Please enter the type of placement, if applicable. (text, required: false)
* **Internship Goals**: Please enter the goals of the internship program. (text, required: false)
* **Job Placement**: Any additional comments on the job placement, if applicable. (note, required: false)
* **Internship Outcome**: Was the intern successfully placed in a job? (select_one, options: [Yes, No])
* **Job Placement Date**: Please enter the date of the job placement, if applicable. (date, required: false)
* **Job Placement Status**: What is the status of the job placement? (select_one, options: [Active, Inactive])
* **Email**: Please enter the email of the employer or contact person. (email, required: false)
* **Phone**: Please enter the phone number of the employer or contact person. (text, required: false)
* **Job Title**: Please enter the job title of the placement. (text, required: false)
* **Placement Notes**: Any additional comments on the placement. (note, required: false)
* **Employer Comments**: Any comments from the employer. (text, required: false)
* **Intern Comments**: Any comments from the intern. (text, required: false)
* **Job Placement Comments**: Any comments on the job placement. (text, required: false)
* **Feedback Comments**: Any additional feedback on the internship placement process. (text, required: false)
* **Placement ID**: Please enter the ID of the placement. (text, required: false)
* **Internship ID**: Please enter the ID of the internship. (text, required: false)
* **Assigned User**: The user assigned to this placement. (text, required: false)
