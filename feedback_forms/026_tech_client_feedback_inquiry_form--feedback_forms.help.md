# Tech Client Feedback Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Client Feedback Inquiry Form is a tool for clients to report technical issues they have encountered while using our product or service. It helps the development team understand the problem and provide effective solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, making sure it is formatted correctly (e.g. "john.doe@example.com").
3. Select the company you represent in the "Company" field. This is optional, but it helps us understand the context of your feedback.
4. Describe the product or service you are using in the "Product/Service" field.
5. Select the type of feedback you are providing in the "Feedback Type" field:
	* "Yes" if you are reporting an issue
	* "No" if you are providing general feedback
6. Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing in the "Issue Description" field.
7. If you would like the development team to follow up with you, select "Yes" in the "Follow-up Request" field.
8. If you would like the development team to contact you via a specific medium (e.g. phone, email, etc.), enter the relevant information in the "Follow-up Contact" field.
9. If you have a preferred frequency for follow-up (e.g. weekly, biweekly, etc.), enter it in the "Follow-up Frequency" field.
10. If you would like to be contacted via a specific medium (e.g. phone, email, etc.) for follow-up, enter it in the "Follow-up Medium" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`<name>`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`<name>`, email, required): Enter your email address, making sure it is formatted correctly (e.g. "john.doe@example.com").
* **Company** (`<name>`, text, optional): Select the company you represent. This helps us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Product/Service** (`<name>`, text, optional): Describe the product or service you are using.
* **Feedback Type** (`<name>`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you are reporting an issue, "No" if you are providing general feedback.
* **Issue Description** (`<name>`, text, required): Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing.
* **Follow-up Request** (`<name>`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you would like the development team to follow up with you.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`<name>`, text, optional): If you would like the development team to contact you via a specific medium, enter the relevant information.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`<name>`, text, optional): If you have a preferred frequency for follow-up, enter it here.
* **Follow-up Medium** (`<name>`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted via a specific medium for follow-up, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly.
* If you are reporting an issue, select "Yes" in the "Feedback Type" field.
* If you are providing general feedback, select "No" in the "Feedback Type" field.
