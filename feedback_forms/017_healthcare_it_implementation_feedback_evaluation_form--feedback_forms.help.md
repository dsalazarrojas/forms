# Healthcare IT Implementation Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the healthcare IT implementation from stakeholders and team members. Your input will help us better understand the strengths and weaknesses of the implementation, identify areas for improvement, and guide future development.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete this form by following these steps:

* Read through each section carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
* Fill in the form title, name, and category to provide context about the implementation you're providing feedback on.
* Provide a brief description of the implementation, including its key features and functionalities.
* Rate each of the provided rating questions, using the numbers provided (e.g., 1-5).
* Enter any comments or observations you have about the implementation in the comments section.
* If you have any recommendations for future development or improvements, please enter them in the recommendations section.
* Finally, indicate whether you want to submit your feedback by selecting "Yes" from the submit options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional):
	+ A brief title of the form, which will help us understand the specific implementation you're providing feedback on.
* **form_name** (`form_name`, text, optional):
	+ The full name of the form, which will help us associate the feedback with the relevant implementation team or project.
* **form_category** (`form_category`, text, optional):
	+ The category of the form, which will help us group feedback by implementation type or feature.
* **form_description** (`form_description`, text, optional):
	+ A brief description of the implementation, including its key features and functionalities.
* **overall rating** (`form_rating`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your overall impression of the implementation.
* **comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Any additional comments or observations you have about the implementation.
* **recommendations** (`form_recommendations`, text, optional):
	+ Any suggestions or recommendations for future development or improvement.
* **rating 2** (`form_rating2`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on a specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., ease of use).
* **rating 3** (`form_rating3`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., effectiveness).
* **rating 4** (`form_rating4`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., usability).
* **rating 5** (`form_rating5`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., documentation).
* **rating 6** (`form_rating6`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., performance).
* **rating 7** (`form_rating7`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., security).
* **rating 8** (`form_rating8`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., support).
* **rating 9** (`form_rating9`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., integration).
* **rating 10** (`form_rating10`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., scalability).
* **rating 11** (`form_rating11`, number, optional):
	+ A number rating from 1 to 5, indicating your opinion on another specific aspect of the implementation (e.g., maintainability).
* **submit** (`form_submit`, select_one, required):
	+ Select "Yes" to submit your feedback, or "No" to indicate that you don't want to submit your feedback.

Note: Please make sure to submit your feedback if you have any suggestions or concerns about the implementation.
