# Favorite Notification Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect user feedback on their experience with notifications. Users can provide feedback on various aspects of notifications, such as their favorite notification type, method, time, and frequency. This form helps us understand what our users like and dislike about notifications and make improvements accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Favorite Notification Feedback" link to access the form.
2. Select your preferred notification type from the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred notification method from the options provided.
4. Specify your preferred notification time.
5. If you have any additional suggestions or comments, please provide them in the text fields.
6. Select your preferred notification frequency from the options provided.
7. Optionally, provide feedback on your favorite notification channel, notification order, and notification method.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.
