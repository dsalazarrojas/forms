# Brainstorming Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants on a brainstorming session. It helps us to assess the effectiveness of the session and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, providing specific and concrete feedback.
2. If a question does not apply to you, feel free to skip it or indicate that it's not applicable.
3. Use the options provided for each question, or describe your answer in the text fields.
4. Take your time to review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): For follow-up communications, please enter a valid email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Your department or team.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Date of the brainstorming session.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, required): Main topic of the session.
* **Session Facilitator** (`session_facilitator`, text, required): Name of the facilitator.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): The length of the session. Select from: Less than 30 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, or More than 4 hours.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied were you with the session? Select from: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Objectives Clarity** (`objectives_clarity`, select_one, required): How clear were the session objectives? Select from: Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, or Very unclear.
* **Facilitator Effectiveness** (`facilitator_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the facilitator's performance. Select from: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Idea Generation** (`idea_generation`, select_one, required): How effective was idea generation during the session? Select from: Very effective, Effective, Neutral, Ineffective, or Very ineffective.
* **Your Participation Level** (`participation_level`, select_one, required): How much did you participate in the session? Select from: Very active, Active, Moderate, Minimal, or Did not participate.
* **Group Dynamics** (`group_dynamics`, select_one, required): Rate the group interaction. Select from: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Environment Rating** (`environment_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the physical or virtual environment. Select from: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one, required): Was time well managed? Select from: Excellent, Very good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Tools and Techniques** (`tools_techniques`, select_one, required): How helpful were the brainstorming tools and techniques? Select from: Very helpful, Helpful, Neutral, Not helpful, or Hindering.
* **Ideas Documented** (`ideas_documented`, select_one, required): Were ideas well documented? Select from: Yes, thoroughly, Yes, adequately, Somewhat, or False.
* **Actionable Outcomes** (`actionable_outcomes`, select_one, required): Did the session produce actionable items? Select from: Yes, many, Yes, some, Few, or None.
* **Best Part of Session** (`best_part`, text, optional): What worked well in the session.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_improvement`, text, optional): What could be improved in the session.
* **Favorite Technique** (`favorite_technique`, text, optional): Which technique did you find most helpful.
* **Suggestions for Future Sessions** (`suggestions_future`, text, optional): Any recommendations for future sessions.
* **Would Participate Again** (`would_participate_again`, select_one, required): Would you participate in future sessions? Select from: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, or Definitely no.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback or comments.
