# Transportation Fare Change Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with transportation fare changes. It helps gather information on how fare changes affect users' transportation choices and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of feedback you want to provide (Feedback Type): Choose from Fare Increase, Fare Decrease, or No Effect.
2. Describe the type of transportation affected by the fare change (What type of transportation was affected by fare change?).
3. Specify how often you ride that type of transportation (How often did you ride the affected type of transportation?).
4. Rate the effectiveness of communication about fare changes (Rate the effectiveness of communication about fare changes).
5. Provide any additional comments or suggestions (Additional comments).
6. Enter your email address (Email Address) and phone number (Phone Number) for contact information.
7. Describe the fare change you experienced (What fare change did you experience?).
8. Choose how the fare change will affect your transportation choice (How will the fare change affect your transportation choice?).
9. Suggest alternative fare changes (What would you suggest as an alternative fare change option?).
10. Rate your overall satisfaction with the fare change experience (Rate the overall satisfaction with the fare change experience).
11. Submit a date for your feedback (Submission).
12. Share your feedback on the fare change (What is your feedback on public transportation fare change?).
13. Check if you experienced any inconvenience due to the fare change (Did you experience any inconvenience due to fare change?).
14. Rate the inconvenience caused by the fare change (How would you rate the inconvenience caused by fare change?).
15. Provide additional information about the inconvenience (Additional Information Regarding Inconvenience).
16. Choose how likely you are to suggest this change to others (How likely are you to suggest this change to others?).
17. Specify how often you ride public transportation (How often do you ride public transportation?).
18. Choose the mode of public transportation you use most often (What mode of public transportation do you use most often?).
19. Specify how long you usually spend on public transportation (How long do you usually spend on public transportation?).
20. Choose the payment method you use for public transportation (What type of payment method do you use for public transportation?).
21. Rate your overall satisfaction with the experience (How satisfied are you with your overall experience?).
22. Choose how likely you are to continue using public transportation after this fare change (How likely are you to continue using public transportation after this fare change?).
23. Specify what would make you more likely to use public transportation after this fare change (What would make you more likely to use public transportation after this fare change?).
24. Choose what would make you less likely to use public transportation after this fare change (What would make you less likely to use public transportation after this fare change?).
25. Rate how fair you think the fare change was (Do you feel the fare change was fair?).
