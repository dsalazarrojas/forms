# sales_update - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to update sales information and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Click on the "sales_update_form" page.
3. Fill out the "sales_update_details" field with the relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sales_update_form**: This is the main page where you will enter your sales update information. It's a text box where you can type in your answer.
* **sales_update_details**: Enter the details of your sales update. This field accepts text input. You can enter as much or as little information as you need.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
