# Coffee Machine Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with a coffee machine. It's meant to be used by coffee machine users, maintenance staff, or facility managers to gather information about the machine's condition, usage, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide your name, department, and email address (if applicable).
* Provide information about the coffee machine's location, brand, and frequency of use.
* Rate the machine's coffee quality, temperature, and strength options.
* Indicate the machine's cleanliness, functionality, and overall condition.
* Rate your experience with ease of use, wait time, and variety of drink options.
* If you've encountered any issues or have suggestions for improvement, please list them in the corresponding text fields.
* Finally, rate your overall satisfaction with the machine and provide a feedback date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or business card.
* **Department**: If applicable, enter your department name to help us route feedback to the right team.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address, if you'd like to receive updates or responses to your feedback.
* **Coffee Machine Location**: Where the machine is located (e.g., break room, cafeteria, etc.).
* **Machine Brand or Model**: Enter the machine's brand and model (if known).
* **Frequency of Use**: Rate how often you use the machine:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Weekly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
* **Coffee Taste Quality**: Rate the taste of the coffee:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Coffee Temperature**: Rate the machine's coffee temperature:
	+ Too hot
	+ Ideal
	+ Warm but acceptable
	+ Cold
* **Coffee Strength Options**: Rate the machine's coffee strength options:
	+ Excellent range
	+ Good options
	+ Limited options
	+ No options for adjustment
* **Machine Cleanliness**: Rate the machine's cleanliness:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Machine Functionality**: Rate the machine's functionality:
	+ Works perfectly
	+ Works well with minor issues
	+ Works but has issues
	+ Frequently malfunctions
	+ Broken
* **Ease of Use**: Rate your experience with using the machine:
	+ Very easy
	+ Easy
	+ Moderate difficulty
	+ Difficult
	+ Very difficult
* **Average Wait Time**: Rate the time it takes to get coffee:
	+ Very quick
	+ Quick
	+ Acceptable
	+ Long
	+ Very long
* **Variety of Drink Options**: Rate the machine's drink options:
	+ Excellent selection
	+ Good selection
	+ Adequate selection
	+ Limited selection
	+ Very limited
* **Issues or Problems**: If you've encountered any issues, list them here.
* **Maintenance Needed**: If you think the machine requires maintenance, list it here.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the machine:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any suggestions for improving the machine or service, list them here.
* **Feedback Date**: Enter the date you're submitting this feedback.
