# Carpool Service Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from carpool users about their experience with the service. It will help us improve and better serve our users.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Complete all the fields that apply to your experience. You can skip sections if they don't pertain to your trip.
*   Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing specific details about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1.  **Your Name (optional)**
    *   Leave blank if you prefer anonymity.
    *   Otherwise, enter your name as you'd like to be referred to.

### 2.  **Your Email (optional)**
    *   If you provide an email, we may follow up with any further questions or offers related to your experience.
    *   Otherwise, you can leave it blank.

### 3.  **Date of Trip**
    *   Enter the date of your trip using the provided date format.

### 4.  **Driver Name**
    *   Enter the name of the driver you traveled with.

### 5.  **Rate the Driver's Professionalism**
    *   Rate the driver's courtesy, communication, and overall behavior during the trip.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is not professional at all and 5 is extremely professional.

### 6.  **How Safe Did You Feel During the Trip?**
    *   Rate how safe you felt during the trip, considering the driver's adherence to traffic laws and road rules.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is not safe at all and 5 is extremely safe.

### 7.  **Rate the Cleanliness of the Vehicle**
    *   Rate the overall cleanliness of the vehicle, including the interior and exterior.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is not clean at all and 5 is extremely clean.

### 8.  **Was the Driver On Time for Pickup?**
    *   Choose how early or late the driver was for your pickup.
    *   Select from the options provided: Early, On Time, 5 to 10 min late, 10 plus min late.

### 9.  **Was the Route Efficient?**
    *   Choose whether the route taken was efficient.
    *   Select from the options provided: True, False, Needs Improvement

### 10. **Overall Comfort of the Ride**
    *   Rate the overall comfort of the ride, considering factors like temperature, music, and seating.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is not comfortable at all and 5 is extremely comfortable.

### 11.  **Rate the Ease of Booking the Carpool**
    *   Rate how easy or difficult it was to book the carpool.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 (very difficult) to 5 (extremely easy).

### 12. **Rate the Carpool App Interface**
    *   Rate the overall usability and features of the carpool app.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 to 5, where 1 is not user-friendly at all and 5 is extremely user-friendly.

### 13. **If You Contacted Support, Rate the Response**
    *   Rate how you felt the support response was, considering the help and assistance provided.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 (poor) to 5 (exceptional).

### 14.  **Overall Carpool Experience**
    *   Rate your overall experience with the carpool service, considering all factors.
    *   Use the scale provided to rate from 1 (poor) to 10 (exceptional).

### 15. **Would You Recommend This Service to Others?**
    *   Choose whether you would definitely, likely, neutral, unlikely, or definitely not recommend the service to friends or colleagues.

### 16.  **Will You Use This Carpool Service Again?**
    *   Choose whether you will definitely, maybe, or definitely not use the service again.

### 17.  **Suggestions and Comments**
    *   Provide any specific feedback or suggestions to improve the service.
    *   Type any other comments or concerns you may have.

### 18.  **Any Positive Feedback for the Driver?**
    *   Share any positive experiences or feedback for the driver you traveled with.

### 19.  **Additional Comments or Concerns**
    *   Provide any other feedback, comments, or concerns you may have about your experience.
    *   Type your name as a digital signature.
