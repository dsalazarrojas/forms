# Donor Experience Mapping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Experience Mapping Survey is a tool used to collect feedback from donors on their interactions with our organization. The survey aims to understand how well we are performing in areas such as communication, reliability, responsiveness, and innovation. By completing this survey, you will be providing valuable insights that will help us improve our services and better serve our donors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing a **Survey Title**, a short description of your experience with our organization.
2. Select a **Category** from the options provided, such as "Feedback Forms" or "Other", to categorize your feedback.
3. Choose the **values** you hold most important in our organization from the options: **Innovation**, **Reliability**, **Responsiveness**, and **Communication**.
4. Identify the areas where you feel we are doing well, by selecting all that apply from the options: **Communication**, **Responsiveness**, **Innovation**.
5. Indicate the areas where we need improvement, by selecting all that apply from the options: **Communication**, **Reliability**.
6. Rate our **communication** from 1-5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
7. Provide the **hours you were available to work**, if applicable.
8. Enter the **number of hours you were available to work**, if applicable.
9. Enter your **Email** address, so we can contact you for further feedback or to follow up on your response.
10. Enter your **Phone** number, if you would like to be contacted.
11. Enter your **Donor Name** and **Donor Title**, if you are a donor representative.
12. Enter your organization's **Name**.
13. Enter your organization's **Contact** information.
14-18: These fields are currently not used in the survey but are placeholders for future questions, feel free to add your thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (ID 1): A short description of your experience with our organization.
* **Category** (ID 2): Select a category that best describes the purpose of your feedback. 
* **What do you value most** (ID 3): Select the values you hold most important in our organization from the options: **Innovation, Reliability, Responsiveness, and Communication**.
* **What we do well** (ID 4): Choose all areas where you feel we are performing well, from the options: **Communication, Responsiveness, Innovation**.
* **What we need to improve** (ID 5): Choose all areas where you feel we need improvement, from the options: **Communication, Reliability**.
* **How would you rate our communication** (ID 6): Rate our communication from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
* **What hours were you available to work** (ID 7): If applicable, enter the hours you were available to work.
* **Number of hours available to work** (ID 8): If applicable, enter the number of hours you were available to work.
* **Email** (ID 9): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further feedback.
* **Phone** (ID 10): If you would like to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Donor Name** (ID 11): Enter your donor name.
* **Donor Title** (ID 12): Enter your donor title.
* **Organization** (ID 13): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Contact** (ID 14): Enter your organization's contact information.
