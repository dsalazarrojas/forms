# Global Food Export Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on the global food export market. Your input is crucial in helping us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the export market that best describes your export operations.
2. Choose the products you are exporting to the selected market.
3. Enter the logistics information for your export operations.
4. Provide the time frames for your delivery.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.
6. Enter your email address and phone number for future contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Global Food Export Survey** (`global_food_export_survey_page_1`, text, optional)
This is the title of the form and is not a required field.

### 2. **exportMarkets** (`export_markets`, select_one, optional)
Select the market where you are exporting food products. Choose one of the following options:
* Market A
* Market B
* Market C

### 3. **exportProducts** (`export_products`, select_multiple, optional)
Select all the products you are exporting to the selected market. Choose one or more of the following options:
* Product 1
* Product 2
* Product 3

### 4. **logistics** (`logistics`, date, optional)
Enter the logistics information for your export operations.

### 5. **time** (`time`, time, optional)
Enter the time frames for your delivery.

### 6. **notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
Provide any additional notes or comments about your export operations.

### 7. **email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter your email address for future contact.

### 8. **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number for future contact.

### 9. **required_info** (`required_info`, text, optional)
This field is not currently used and is intended for future reference.

Tips:
* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort.
