# Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on products from users. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form with your honest thoughts, and we'll use your input to improve our products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form one page at a time, from top to bottom.
2. Answer all fields that are marked as "required".
3. If a field has a label with "(e.g., email address)", use that format as a guide.
4. If you're unsure about what to enter in a field, please leave it blank and move on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Product Name
• **product_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.

### Product Description
• **product description** (text, optional): Describe your experience with the product in a few sentences. This will help us better understand your thoughts.

### Feedback
• **feedback** (text, optional): Share your thoughts about the product. What did you like or dislike about it? What did you think was missing or unnecessary?

### Email
• **email** (text, email address, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted with updates about the product or for further questions.

### Phone Number
• **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further questions or follow-up.

### Date
• **date** (date, optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific date, please enter it in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).

### Time
• **time** (time, optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific time, please enter it in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 PM).

### How Satisfied Are You?
• **how_satisfied** (select one): Choose how satisfied you are with the product from the options below:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Categories
• **categories** (select multiple): Select all that apply:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3

### Notes
• **notes** (note, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback that didn't fit in the previous fields, you can add them here.

## Tips
- If you have any issues with the form, please let us know by contacting us at your email address or phone number.
- We appreciate your honest feedback and will use it to improve our products.
