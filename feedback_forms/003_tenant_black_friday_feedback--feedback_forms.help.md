# Tenant Black Friday Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Black Friday Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from tenants about their experience with Black Friday. This form allows you to provide feedback on performance, product, or experience. Your input will help us improve our service. Please note that this form is for Black Friday events, and your feedback will be considered for future improvements and enhancements. We do not use this form for sales or marketing purposes. If you've filled this form before, please make sure to only submit new feedback, as we will not consider duplicate submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button, and the form will open in a new window or tab.
2. Read through each field carefully before filling it out.
3. Fill out each field according to the instructions below.
4. Double-check your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Black Friday Performance**: Please describe your experience with Black Friday, including any issues or concerns you may have had. (Required)
* **Feedback**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed. (Optional)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like us to contact you. (Optional)
* **Select One**: Choose one of the options that best describes your experience. (Optional)
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
* **Select Multiple**: Choose all that apply from the options below. (Optional)
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Date**: Please enter the date you experienced Black Friday. (Optional)
* **Time**: Please enter the time you experienced Black Friday. (Optional)
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments. (Optional)
* **Black Friday Performance Score**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. (Optional)

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have a lot to say, please feel free to add more comments in the Note field.
* If you'd like to be contacted, please enter your email and/or phone number.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and it will help us improve our service.
