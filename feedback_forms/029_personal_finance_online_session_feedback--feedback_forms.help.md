# Personal Finance Online Session Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Finance Online Session Feedback form is a tool used to gather feedback from participants on their experience with our online workshops on personal finance. By filling out this form, you will help us understand how to improve our sessions to better serve you and our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through the following sections and fill out the required information.

1. At the top of the form, select the options regarding whether you attended the workshop and what finance topic the workshop was about.
2. Choose how satisfied you were with the session, providing feedback on the quality of the session.
3. Provide any suggestions you have for future sessions. This is your chance to tell us how we can improve.
4. Select the date of the workshop you attended.
5. Choose the start time of the workshop you attended.
6. Enter the duration of the session.
7. Enter the location where the session took place.
8. If you were not attending alone, let us know with whom you attended the session.
9. Lastly, provide your name and email address so we can contact you with any further questions or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Did you attend the workshop**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to let us know if you attended the workshop.
- **What finance topic was the workshop about**: Select one of the options for the topic of the workshop.
- **How satisfied were you with the session**: Choose one of the options to rate your satisfaction with the session.
- **Do you have any suggestions for future sessions**: Enter any ideas or suggestions you have for future workshops.
- **Session date**: Enter the date of the workshop you attended in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Session start time**: Enter the start time of the workshop you attended in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Session duration**: Enter the length of the session.
- **Session location**: Enter the location where the workshop took place.
- **Session facilitator name**: If you wish to provide the name of the session facilitator.
- **Session facilitator contact information**: If you want to provide the contact information of the session facilitator.
- **Participant name**: Enter your name so we can refer to you in any further communication.
- **Participant email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or information.
- **With whom did you attend the workshop**: Choose whether you attended the workshop alone, with friends, or with family.
