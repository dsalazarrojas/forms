# Product Range Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with the product range, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
3. Select the correct options from the available choices.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
- **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the product on a scale of 1-5.
- **Price Range** (`price_range`, `select_one`, required): Select the price range you purchased the product for.
- **Category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the category that best describes the product.
- **Comments** (`comment`, `note`, required): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the product.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Website** (`website`, `text`, required): Enter your website URL (if applicable).
- **Social Media** (`social_media`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the social media platforms you use.
- **How did you find us?** (`how_found_us`, `select_one`, required): Select how you found us (e.g., social media, search engine, referral, etc.).
- **Did you visit us before?** (`visit_us_before`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you had previously visited us.
- **Last Visit** (`visit_us_last_visit`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your last visit.
- **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you spent visiting.
- **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how often you visit us.
- **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, required): Select your rating on a scale of 1-5.
- **Would you purchase again?** (`purchase_again`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would purchase again.
- **Would you recommend?** (`recommend_friends`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would recommend us to friends.
- **Price Range Purchased** (`purchase_price`, `text`, required): Enter the price range you purchased the product for.
- **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how many times you purchased from us.
- **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age.
- **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender.
- **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): Enter your occupation.
- **Income Range** (`income`, `select_one`, required): Select your income range.
- **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your education level.
- **Visit Source** (`visit_source`, `select_multiple`, required): Select where you found us (e.g., social media, search engine, referral, etc.).
