# Telehealth Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our telehealth service. Your feedback is crucial in helping us identify areas for improvement and providing better care to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Provide your email address for us to contact you if needed.
3. Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, Select Multiple, required: false): This question asks how you would rate your overall experience with the telehealth service. Choose from the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, required: false): Share any thoughts, comments, or suggestions you have about the service. This is your opportunity to tell us what worked well and what didn't work so well.

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, required: false): If you have any more thoughts or comments about the service, please share them here.

* **Name** (`name`, Text, required: false): Please enter your name so we can contact you if needed.

* **Telephone** (`telephone`, Text, required: false): Enter your contact telephone number so we can reach out to you if needed.

* **Service Score** (`service_score`, Number, required: false): Rate the quality of the service you received.

* **Service Score Value** (`service_score_value`, Number, required: false): Enter a value for your service score.

Note: Fields with 'required: false' are not mandatory but are useful for providing more information if you want to. You can skip them if you're not comfortable sharing.
