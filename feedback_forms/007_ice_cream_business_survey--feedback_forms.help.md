# ice_cream_business_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after visiting an ice cream business to gather information about their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Customer Name and Email address as required fields.
2. Choose one of the options in the Overall Rating field.
3. If you'd like to provide more detailed feedback, type in the Feedback field (optional).
4. Input the Visit Date and Visit Time of your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name (Required)
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Email (Required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address.

### Overall Rating (Required)
* **Overall Rating**: Choose one of the three options: Low, Neutral, or High, to rate your experience.

### Feedback (Optional)
* **Feedback**: Type in any additional comments about your visit.

### Visit Date (Required)
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of your visit to the ice cream business.

### Visit Time (Required)
* **Visit Time**: Input the time of your visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide.
