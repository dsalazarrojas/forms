# Soft Material Crafting Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help you complete the Soft Material Crafting Survey form. The survey is designed to collect information from users on their soft material crafting projects, including project categories, types, materials, durations, and statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and ensure you have the necessary information to complete each field.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Provide feedback on your experience with soft material crafting.
6. Select the rating of your experience (Poor, Average, Good, Excellent).
7. Select the category of your project (Fabric, Yarn, Soft Craft).
8. Select the type of your project (Knitting, Crochet, Tapestry, Weaving, Embroidery, Needlework, Cross-stitch, Mixed-media, Other).
9. Select the materials used in your project (Cotton, Wool, Merino, Acrylic, Nylon, Other).
10. Enter the start date of your project.
11. Enter the end date of your project.
12. Select the status of your project (Planned, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
13. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your survey.
