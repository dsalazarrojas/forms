# Ride Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ride Evaluation Survey is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from users about their recent ride experiences. This information will help us identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the overall experience for our users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field carefully and honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to share them in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Once you have completed the form, click "Submit" to submit your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Title**: (optional)
* This field is not applicable as it is a title and not a field for user input.

### **Rating**: (number, optional)
* This field is for you to rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### **Feedback**: (text, optional)
* Please provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience in this field. This will help us to understand your perspective.

### **Satisfaction Rating**: (number, optional)
* This field is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with the ride on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### **Driver Performance**: (text, optional)
* Please describe your experience with the driver, including their behavior, communication, and overall performance.

### **Rating 2**: (number, optional)
* This field is for you to rate your experience on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### **Additional Comments**: (text, optional)
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts, feel free to share them here.

### **Overall Rating**: (number, optional)
* This field is for you to rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### **Category**: (select_one, optional)
* Please select the category that best describes your experience. The options are:
	+ Category 1
	+ Category 2
	+ Category 3
