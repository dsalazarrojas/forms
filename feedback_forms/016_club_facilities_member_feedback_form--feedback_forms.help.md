# Club Facilities Member Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from club facilities members to help us understand their opinions and needs. Your responses will help us improve our services and facilities to better serve you and other members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as `*` (asterisk) with your information and feedback.
2. Select the rating options for each field carefully, as they will be used to help us understand your experience.
3. You may add any additional comments or suggestions in the `Additional Comments or Suggestions` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Your Name (*)
Your name will be used to associate your feedback with your membership.

### 2. Email Address (*)
Your email address will be used to contact you with any questions or to follow up on your feedback.

### 3. Feedback Date (*)
This field is to record the date of your visit or when you received a service.

### 4. Membership Status (*)
Select your current membership status to help us understand your relationship with the club.

### 5. Facility Section (*)
Select all the sections of the facility you visited or used during your visit.

### 6. Cleanliness and Maintenance (Very Good)
Rate the cleanliness and maintenance of the facilities you used during your visit.

### 7. Equipment and Amenities (Excellent)
Rate the quality and availability of equipment and amenities at the club.

### 8. Accessibility and Comfort (Excellent)
Rate how easy it was to navigate and use the facilities during your visit.

### 9. Temperature Control (optional)
Rate the temperature of the facilities during your visit. This field is optional, but your feedback is valuable!

### 10. Lighting Quality (optional)
Rate the lighting quality of the facilities during your visit. This field is optional.

### 11. Noise Level (optional)
Rate the noise level of the facilities during your visit. This field is optional.

### 12. Staff Helpfulness (optional)
Rate the helpfulness of staff during your visit. This field is optional.

### 13. Overall Facility Satisfaction (Very Satisfied)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the facilities and services you used during your visit.

### 14. Most Needed Improvements (optional)
Provide any suggestions or improvements you think the club needs to make.

### 15. Additional Comments or Suggestions (optional)
Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the club's facilities and services.
