# front_desk_operations_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The front_desk_operations_feedback_form is a survey designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with the front desk operations team. This form helps us understand what we do well and where we can improve, ultimately enhancing our service quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your ability, and feel free to elaborate on any point if you have more to say.
2. If you have already provided feedback on this topic in the past, please let us know so that we can review and update our records.
3. Select the contact method(s) that you prefer, or leave it blank if you're not comfortable with any of the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General feedback (1)** (`General feedback`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience or any comments you'd like to share about your stay or interaction with the front desk.
* **How was your stay? (2)** (`How was your stay?`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5. This will help us understand the quality of service we provided.
* **What was the most impressive part of your stay? (3)** (`What was the most impressive part of your stay?`, text, optional): If there was something that stood out about your experience, please let us know. This helps us identify what we do well and how we can do more of it.
* **Whose contact info should we reach out to for further discussion? (4)** (`Front Desk Staff`, Room Attendant`, Manager, select_one, optional): Choose from the following options if you'd like us to reach out to someone in particular for further discussion.
* **What method should we use to contact you? (5)** (`phone`, email, text, select_multiple, optional): Select the contact method you prefer, or leave it blank if you're not comfortable with any of the options.
* **Additional comments (6)** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): If you'd like to add more information or comments, please use this space.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback or comments.
* If you're unable to rate your stay, please explain why in the additional comments section.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! It helps us improve our service and better serve you in the future.
