# Tournament Spectator Facilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Tournament Spectator Facilities Survey, a form designed to collect feedback from tournament spectators on various aspects of the event's facilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Review the survey questions**: Carefully read each question to understand what information the form is asking you to provide.
2. **Answer each question**: Respond to each question according to your experience with the tournament's facilities.
3. **Submit your feedback**: Once you've answered all questions, click the submit button to send your feedback to the event organizers.
