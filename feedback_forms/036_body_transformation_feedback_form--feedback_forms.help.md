# Body Transformation Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Body Transformation Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from individuals who have undergone a body transformation program. The purpose of this form is to gather information on their experiences, successes, and challenges during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields.
2. Select the duration of the program you have completed in the "Program Duration" field.
3. Choose the type of program you have followed in the "Type of Program" field.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the training program, trainer support, difficulty level, and overall satisfaction with the transformation in the respective fields.
5. Select all the physical changes you have noticed during the program in the "Physical Changes Noticed" field.
6. Choose how motivated you are and how well you followed the program in the "Motivation Level" and "Program Adherence" fields.
7. Select all the challenges you faced during the program in the "Biggest Challenge" field.
8. Provide your feedback on what contributed to your success and what you would recommend to others in the "Success Factors" and "Would Recommend This Program" fields.
9. Finally, provide any suggestions for program improvement in the "Improvement Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (<name>)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (<email>)**: Enter your email address for future contact.
* **Program Start Date (<program_start_date>)**: The date you started the transformation program.
* **Program Duration (<program_duration>)**: Select how long you were in the program (e.g., 1-4 weeks, 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Type of Program (<program_type>)**: Choose the type of program you followed (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, etc.).
* **Program Start Date (date)**: Choose the date you started the program.
* **Program Duration (select_one)**: Select how long you were in the program (e.g., 1-4 weeks, 1-3 months, etc.).
* **Type of Program (<type>)**: Choose the type of program you followed (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, etc.).
* **Your Trainer or Coach Name (<trainer_name>)**: If applicable, enter your trainer's or coach's name.
* **Weight Change (number)**: If applicable, enter the weight change you experienced.
* **Goal Achievement (select_one)**: Rate how close you are to your goal (e.g., achieved, on track, halfway, etc.).
* **Physical Changes Noticed (select_multiple)**: Select all the physical changes you noticed during the program (e.g., weight loss, muscle gain, etc.).
* **Energy Levels (select_one)**: Rate your energy levels after the program (e.g., significantly increased, increased, etc.).
* **Confidence Improvement (select_one)**: Rate how much your confidence improved after the program.
* **Satisfaction with Training Program (select_one)**: Rate your satisfaction with the training program.
* **Trainer Support and Guidance (select_one)**: Rate your trainer's support and guidance.
* **Difficulty Level of Program (select_one)**: Rate the difficulty level of the program.
* **Quality of Nutrition Guidance (select_one)**: Rate the quality of nutrition guidance you received.
* **Motivation Level (select_one)**: Rate your motivation level after the program.
* **Program Adherence (select_one)**: Rate how well you followed the program.
* **Biggest Challenge (text)**: If applicable, enter the biggest challenge you faced during the program.
* **Success Factors (text)**: If applicable, enter what contributed to your success.
* **Would Recommend This Program (select_one)**: Rate whether you would recommend the program to others.
* **Suggestions for Program Improvement (text)**: If applicable, enter any suggestions for program improvement.
* **Future Fitness Plans (text)**: If applicable, enter your future fitness plans.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Transformation (select_one)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the transformation.
