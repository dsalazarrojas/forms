# Wildlife Management Strategy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Wildlife Management Strategy Survey is designed to collect feedback and opinions from stakeholders on the current state of wildlife management policies and conservation plans. Your input will help inform decision-making processes and contribute to the development of effective strategies for wildlife conservation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter its name.
5. Select your role within the organization.
6. If applicable, select your department within the organization.
7. Identify your biggest concerns about wildlife policies by selecting one or more options from the list.
8. Suggest conservation plans that you think should be implemented, also selecting one or more options from the list.
9. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
10. Finally, select "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to get in touch with you about your feedback.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter its name.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Select your role within the organization (e.g. manager, conservationist, researcher).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you have a department within your organization, enter its name.
* **What is your biggest concern about wildlife policies?** (`wildlife_policies`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following concerns:
	+ Habitat destruction
	+ Hunting regulations
	+ Conservation funding
	+ Species extinction
	+ Climate change
	+ Human-wildlife conflict
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What conservation plans do you think should be implemented?** (`conservation_plans`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following plans:
	+ Increase funding
	+ Improve regulations
	+ Enhance research
	+ Community engagement
	+ Habitat preservation
	+ Other (please specify)
	+ None
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

Note: Please select at least one option for both "wildlife_policies" and "conservation_plans" fields to provide relevant and useful feedback.
