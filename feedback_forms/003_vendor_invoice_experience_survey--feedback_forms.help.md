# Vendor Invoice Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and experiences from vendors regarding their interactions with our organization. The information collected will help us understand their experiences and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your contact information at the top of the form.
3. Answer the questions in the sections below, providing as much detail as possible.
4. Select the options that best describe your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email, required): This section is where you can enter your contact information for our records.
* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`, text, required): Enter your vendor company name or other relevant information.
* **Invoice Details** (`invoice_details`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have been invoiced correctly or not.
* **Payment Experience** (`payment_experience`, select_multiple, required): Describe your payment experience with our organization.
* **Factors Affecting Payment Experience** (`payment_factors`, select_one, required): If you have had any issues with payment, indicate the factors that caused the issue.
* **Vendor Comments** (`vendor_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about your experience, enter them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Methods of Payment** (`payment_methods`, select_multiple, required): Select all the methods of payment you have used with our organization.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the vendor you are responding on behalf of.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the vendor.
* **Rating** (`vendor_rating`, select_one, optional): If you would like to rate your experience, select one of the options provided.
* **Vendor Rating Label** (`vendor_rating_label`, text, optional): This is the label for the vendor rating options.
* **Vendor Rating Choices** (`vendor_rating_choices`, select_one, optional): Select one of the rating options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Comments Label** (`comments_label`, text, optional): This is the label for the comments field.
* **Comments Label 2** (`comments_label_2`, text, optional): This is another label for the comments field.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to submit your form.

Note: Fields marked as `required` must be filled out, while optional fields can be left blank.
