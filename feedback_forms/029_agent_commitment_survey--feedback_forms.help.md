# Agent Commitment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Commitment Survey is designed to gather information about an individual's level of commitment and alignment with organizational goals, as well as their willingness to participate in further discussions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have the necessary information and time to complete the form accurately.
2. Carefully read and understand each question before providing a response.
3. Select the relevant response for each field, and fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agent name** (`agent_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents or as commonly known in the company.
* **Role** (`role`, Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your role or title within the company.
* **Tenure months** (`tenure_months`, Number, Optional): If you've been with the company for more than 10 months, enter the number of months.
* **Current commitment level** (`commitment_level`, Select One):
	+ **Highly committed**: You're fully committed to achieving the organizational goals.
	+ **Committed**: You're mostly committed but have some concerns or issues.
	+ **Neutral**: You're not particularly committed or disengaged.
	+ **Disengaged**: You're not committed and have no intention of achieving the goals.
* **Alignment with organizational goals** (`alignment_with_goals`, Select One):
	+ **Strongly aligned**: You feel very connected to the goals and believe in their importance.
	+ **Aligned**: You're somewhat aligned but have some reservations or concerns.
	+ **Neutral**: You're not particularly connected to the goals.
	+ **Not aligned**: You don't feel connected to the goals.
* **Barriers to commitment** (`barriers_to_commitment`, Text, Optional): If you have any obstacles or challenges that prevent you from being more committed, describe them here.
* **Support needed from management** (`support_needed`, Text, Optional): If you require any resources or help from management to improve your commitment, describe them here.
* **Recommended actions** (`recommended_actions`, Text, Optional): Suggest actions that you think management could take to improve your commitment.
* **Willing to participate in follow-up** (`willing_to_participate_in_followup`, Select One):
	+ **Yes**: You're open to further discussions and willing to participate.
	+ **No**: You're not willing to participate in follow-up discussions.

Please ensure you fill out all required fields carefully and accurately. If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
