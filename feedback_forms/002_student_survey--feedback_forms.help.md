# Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Survey is designed to gather your opinions and thoughts about the curriculum and teaching materials of your educational institution. This form is a way to provide feedback on what you think about the course content, materials, and facilities. Your input will help the educators and administrators understand what works well and what can be improved, ultimately contributing to the creation of a better learning environment for all students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the link to the Student Survey form.
2. Read each question carefully to understand what we are asking for.
3. Type your answers in the text box provided for each question.
4. Click on "Submit" when you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **what do you think about the curriculum**: This question asks for your opinion on the curriculum, what you think is working well and what can be improved.
	+ This is an open-ended question, feel free to write your thoughts and suggestions about the curriculum.
* **what about the materials**: This question is asking about the teaching materials provided, such as textbooks, notes, and other resources.
	+ Be honest about what you think about the materials, are they helpful or not?
* **facilities**: This question is asking about the facilities and resources provided for your learning experience.
	+ Think about the classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and other amenities, and let us know what you think about them.

## Tips

* Please be honest and sincere in your answers.
* Your input will help us create a better learning environment for all students.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them.
* You can write as much or as little as you like for each question, there is no minimum or maximum word limit.
