# Employee Peer Review Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Peer Review Template is designed to facilitate thorough and objective evaluations of employees' performance. The form allows reviewers to provide feedback, suggestions, and an overall rating of the employee's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the employee being reviewed and ensure that they are aware that this review is taking place.
2. Answer each field based on your honest and fair evaluation of the employee's performance.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4. Use the "feedback" field for any comments or suggestions you have, and the "suggestions" field for any action items or improvements you recommend.
5. Provide your overall rating using the provided scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Please enter the job title of the employee being reviewed.
- **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, required): Please enter the job function of the employee being reviewed.
- **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, required): Describe the employee's strengths and areas where they excel.
- **Areas for Improvement** (`areas for improvement`, text, required): Identify areas where the employee could improve or develop new skills.
- **Overall Rating** (`overall rating`, number, required): Choose a rating from 1-10 based on your evaluation of the employee's performance.
- **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
- **Reviewers Contact Information**:
  - **Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the reviewer.
  - **Title** (`reviewer_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as the reviewer.
  - **Department** (`reviewer_department`, text, required): Enter your department as the reviewer.
  - **Job Title** (`reviewer_job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as the reviewer.
  - **Email** (`reviewer_email`, email, required): Enter your email as the reviewer.
  - **Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, required): Sign and date the review.
  - **Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
  - **Suggestions** (`reviewer_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any action items or recommendations.
- **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, text, required): Ask the employee for any comments or feedback.
- **Employee Suggestions** (`employee_suggestions`, text, optional): Ask the employee for any action items or recommendations.
- **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any comments or feedback from the manager.
- **Manager Suggestions** (`manager_suggestions`, text, optional): If applicable, ask the manager for any action items or recommendations.
- **Approver Comments** (`approver_comments`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any comments or feedback from the approver.
- **Approver Suggestions** (`approver_suggestions`, text, optional): If applicable, ask the approver for any action items or recommendations.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Be honest and fair in your evaluation and provide concrete examples.
- Use the "suggestions" field for actionable items and the "feedback" field for comments.
