# leadershipTransformationImpactSurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information on the impact of leadership transformation efforts on employees and the organization. It asks about the effectiveness of leadership development programs, training methods, and evaluation methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For select_one and select_multiple questions, choose the option that best represents your response.
3. For date and number fields, enter the relevant dates or numbers.
4. For note fields, provide a brief description or comment.
5. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback (1)**: This field is for providing general feedback on the impact of leadership transformation efforts on employees.
* **Select Training Programs (2)**: This field is for indicating whether employees have access to leadership development programs.
* **Program Evaluation (3)**: This is a field for providing a brief note on the evaluation of training programs.
* **Performance Impact (4)**: This field is for indicating the impact of leadership development on employee performance.
* **Engagement Impact (5)**: This field is for indicating the impact of leadership transformation on employee engagement.
* **Training Evaluation (6)**: This field is for indicating the effectiveness of training programs on a scale of 1-10.
* **Leadership Development (7)**: This field is for indicating the level of leadership development among employees.
* **Organizational Impact (8)**: This field is for indicating the impact of leadership transformation on the organization as a whole.
* **Training Effectiveness (9)**: This field is for indicating whether training programs have been effective.
* **Leadership Skills (10)**: This field is for providing general feedback on leadership skills among employees.
* **Leadership Development Programs (11)**: This field is for indicating whether employees have access to leadership development programs.
* **Training Goals (12)**: This field is for indicating whether employees have a clear understanding of the training goals.
* **Training Objectives (13)**: This field is for providing brief notes on the objectives of training programs.
* **Impact Metrics (14)**: This field is for indicating the metrics used to measure the impact of leadership development.
* **Performance Metrics (15)**: This field is for indicating the metrics used to measure performance.
* **Training Methods (16)**: This field is for indicating the methods used for training.
* **Leadership Behaviors (17)**: This field is for indicating whether employees exhibit leadership behaviors.
* **Training Frequency (18)**: This field is for indicating the frequency of training sessions.
* **Training Satisfaction (19)**: This field is for indicating employee satisfaction with training programs.
* **Leadership Impact (20)**: This field is for providing general feedback on leadership impact.
* **Evaluation Methods (21)**: This field is for indicating the methods used for evaluation.
* **Training Effectiveness Scale (22)**: This field is for indicating the effectiveness of training programs on a scale of 1-10.
* **Leadership Development Goals (23)**: This field is for indicating whether employees have development goals.
* **Training Evaluation Satisfaction (24)**: This field is for indicating employee satisfaction with evaluation.
* **Evaluation Frequency (25)**: This field is for indicating the frequency of evaluation.

Note: Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
