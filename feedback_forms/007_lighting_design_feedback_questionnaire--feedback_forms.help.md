# Lighting Design Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on lighting design projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your project information in the "Project Info" field.
2. Describe your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Indicate whether a design team was involved in the project in the "Design Team" field.
4. Select the design style used in the project in the "Design Style" field.
5. Choose the color scheme used in the project in the "Color Scheme" field.
6. Select the lighting level in the project in the "Lighting Level" field.
7. Provide a feedback score for the project in the "Feedback Score" field.
8. Enter any additional comments about the project in the "Feedback Comments" field.
9. Review and submit your feedback in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Info (1)**: Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Project Description (2)**: Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Design Team (3)**: Indicate whether a design team was involved in your project. Select "Yes" if the project had a design team, and "No" otherwise.
* **Design Style (4)**: Choose the design style used in your project from the available options.
* **Color Scheme (5)**: Select the color scheme used in your project from the available options.
* **Lighting Level (6)**: Choose the lighting level in your project from the available options.
* **Feedback Score (7)**: Enter a score between 1 and 10 to indicate your feedback on the project's lighting design.
* **Feedback Comments (8)**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the project's lighting design.
* **Submit (9)**: Review and submit your feedback form.
