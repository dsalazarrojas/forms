# Workplace Stress Management Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and suggestions from employees on stress management in the workplace. Please fill out this form to help us understand your concerns and ideas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Section 1: Employee Details**
	* Please enter your employee name exactly as it appears on your ID badge or personnel record.
	* Select your department from the provided list.
2. **Section 2: Feedback & Suggestions**
	* Choose one of the provided measures to describe how well your workplace is handling stress management: Measures 1, Measures 2, or Measures 3.
	* Provide a brief description or feedback about your experience with stress management in the workplace.
	* Suggest any tools or resources that could help improve stress management (select all that apply).
3. **Section 3: Submission Information**
	* Enter your email address to contact you about this submission.
	* Enter your phone number in case we need to call you about this submission.
	* Select the date and time you are submitting this form.
4. **Section 4: Evaluation Information**
	* Select who submitted this feedback: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
	* Select the type of submission: Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3.
	* Select the status of your submission: Status 1, Status 2, or Status 3.
	* Enter the name of the person who submitted this form.
	* Enter the position of the person who submitted this form.
	* Enter the department of the person who submitted this form.
	* Select who will evaluate this submission: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
	* Enter the name of the person who will evaluate this submission.
	* Enter the position of the person who will evaluate this submission.
	* Enter the department of the person who will evaluate this submission.
	* Select the evaluation status: Status 1, Status 2, or Status 3.
