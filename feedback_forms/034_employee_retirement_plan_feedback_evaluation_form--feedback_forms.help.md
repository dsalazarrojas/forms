# Employee Retirement Plan Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from employees on their experience with the company's retirement plan, aiming to gather insights that can help improve the plan and better serve employee needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide valuable feedback, please complete the following steps:

1.  Answer all questions truthfully, selecting the options that best suit your opinions.
2.  For fields with dropdown options, choose the response that best fits your situation.
3.  Provide detailed feedback in the 'Suggestions' field if you have ideas for improvements.
4.  Ensure your responses are accurate and up-to-date, as this information will help inform the future development of the retirement plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, text, required/optional): A free text field where you can express your thoughts on your experience with the retirement plan. This is your opportunity to share any positive or negative comments about the plan.
* **Retirement Plan Satisfaction** (`retirement_plan_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose one or more of the options to describe your level of satisfaction with the plan. Please select all that apply.
* **Retirement Plan Satisfaction 2** (`retirement_plan_satisfaction_2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how often you are satisfied with the plan on a scale from 1 to 3.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, required/optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the retirement plan. This is your chance to contribute to its development.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Select the department you work in, as this information helps tailor the plan's benefits and offerings to your specific needs.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title, which is useful for targeted feedback and plan adjustments.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate how often you provide feedback, helping us understand your level of engagement with the plan.
* **Retirement Plan Rating** (`retirement_plan_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the plan on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name for reference and follow-up discussions.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, text, required/optional): Input your manager's email address, which is useful for contacting them directly.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, required/optional): Include your manager's phone number for easy contact.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you are submitting this feedback, helping us track progress and trends over time.
* **Retirement Plan Rating 2** (`retirement_plan_rating_2`, number, required/optional): Provide an additional rating of your overall satisfaction with the plan.
* **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's title, which provides context to their role and involvement.
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you are submitting this feedback, which helps us gauge the timing and urgency of your comments.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, which allows us to address your feedback and follow up on suggestions.
* **Employee Position** (`employee_position`, text, required/optional): Include your job position or role, which helps us understand your perspective based on your responsibilities.
* **Manager Name 2** (`manager_name_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's name again for verification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Input your email address, which is useful for contacting you directly or keeping you updated on changes to the plan.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Include your phone number for easy contact.
* **Department 2** (`department_2`, text, required/optional): Select the department where you work, which aids in tailoring plan benefits and offerings.
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title again for reference and plan adjustments.
* **Retirement Plan Rating 3** (`retirement_plan_rating_3`, number, required/optional): Provide an additional rating of your overall satisfaction with the plan.
* **Retirement Plan Rating 4** (`retirement_plan_rating_4`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the plan for the fourth time.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title once more for targeted feedback and plan modifications.

By following these steps and completing the form carefully, you will be providing valuable insights that can contribute to improving the company's retirement plan and better serving your needs.
