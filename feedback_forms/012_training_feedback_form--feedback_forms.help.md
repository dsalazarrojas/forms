# Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from trainees about their experience with a particular training session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct Session Date and Session Time of the training session you attended.
2. Choose the Training Facilitator who led the session from the list of options.
3. Select all the Training Sessions you participated in (if multiple).
4. Provide a rating of your experience with the session (if you choose to do so).
5. Enter any comments or thoughts you have about the session.
6. Finally, provide any additional feedback you would like to share about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Feedback** (`Training Feedback Form`, note, not required): This section is where you can provide any general feedback about the session. Please keep your feedback concise and focused on the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, not required): Please select the date of the training session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, not required): Please select the time of the day the training session took place.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, select_one, not required): Choose the facilitator who led the session.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the training sessions you participated in.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Please provide a rating of your experience with the session (if you choose to do so).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): This is an open-ended field where you can enter any additional comments or thoughts about the session.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Please use this field to provide any additional feedback you would like to share about the session.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Focus on how the session could be improved, not just what went well.
* Keep your feedback concise and clear.
* If you have multiple sessions to feedback on, please submit one feedback form per session.
* If you have already submitted feedback for this session, please do not submit duplicate feedback.
