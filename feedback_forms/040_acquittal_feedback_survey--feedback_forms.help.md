# Acquittal Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the acquittal process from respondents. The goal is to collect opinions and suggestions on how to improve the process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly.
- Enter your name and role in the case, if applicable.
- Rate your perception of the clarity, professionalism, and timeliness of the process.
- Rate your perception of the fairness of the outcome.
- Provide suggestions for improvement and any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Feedback on acquittal process --**: This is a brief introduction to the feedback process.
- **Respondent name**: Enter your name if you wish to be identified, or leave it blank for anonymous feedback.
- **Role in case**: If you were involved in the case, enter your role (e.g., defendant, counsel, witness, observer). If not applicable, leave it blank.
- **Clarity of communication during process**: Rate how clear the updates and explanations were during the process (Very clear, Clear, Neutral, Unclear, Very unclear).
- **Professionalism of staff**: Rate the professionalism of the staff involved in the process (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
- **Timeliness of proceedings**: Rate how reasonable the timelines were and how they were observed (Very timely, Timely, Delayed, Very delayed).
- **Perception of fairness of outcome**: Rate how fair the process felt (Very fair, Fair, Neutral, Unfair, Very unfair).
- **Suggestions for improvement**: Provide any recommendations on how to improve the process.
- **Additional feedback**: Enter any other comments about the acquittal process.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- Try to provide specific examples or details when answering the questions.
- If you have any suggestions or comments that don't fit into the provided fields, use the "Additional feedback" field.
