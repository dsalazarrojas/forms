# survey_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and communication information from users. It is used to gather data on how to contact the user and their preferred method of communication. This form is only necessary for users who want to provide feedback or report an issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the "communication method" option that best suits your needs.
2. Enter your email address in the "email address" field if you want to receive email communication.
3. Enter your contact number if you want to receive a phone call or text message.
4. Share any additional comments or feedback in the "additional comments" field.
5. Select the date and time you completed the survey.
6. If applicable, enter the person you would like to contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Method** (communication_method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method of communication. This will determine how we contact you.
	+ Select one of the options: email, phone, or in person.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive email communication. 
* **Contact Number** (contact_number, text, optional): Enter your contact number if you want to receive a phone call or text message.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience. 
* **Survey Response Time** (survey_response_time, time, optional): Enter the time it took you to complete the survey. 
* **Date Survey Completed** (date_survey_completed, date, optional): Enter the date and time you completed the survey.
* **Survey Completed** (survey_completed, text, optional): Enter a brief description of what was surveyed. 
* **Contact Person** (contact_person, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted, enter the name of the person you would like to contact.
