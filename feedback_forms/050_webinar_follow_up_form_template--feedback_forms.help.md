# Webinar Follow Up Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees after a webinar has taken place. It aims to gauge their overall satisfaction with the webinar, gather suggestions for improvement, and provide a platform for attendees to share their thoughts on the presentation. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Webinar Host" field with the name of the person who hosted the webinar.
2. In the "Attendee Feedback" field, provide a brief description of your experience with the webinar. What did you think of the content, the host, and the overall presentation?
3. Rate your satisfaction with the webinar in the "Satisfaction Rating" field. This will help us understand how well the webinar met your expectations.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvements, please provide them in the "Suggest Improvements" field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Webinar Host**
* (`webinar_host`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who hosted the webinar.

### **Attendee Feedback**
* (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on the webinar's content, presentation, and overall experience. Be as specific as possible so we can learn from your feedback.

### **Satisfaction Rating**
* (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the webinar on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".

### **Suggest Improvements**
* (`suggest_improvements`, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improvements, share them with us. Your input is valuable in helping us refine our webinars for future attendees. 

## Tips
* Please be as honest and constructive in your feedback as possible.
* You can add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the webinar.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our webinars!
