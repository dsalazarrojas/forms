# Dynamic Presentation Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on presentations, specifically for the speaker and session. It helps us understand the quality of the speaker's presentation and the overall session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please answer each field carefully and thoroughly. You can fill out this form as many times as you need.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Number** (`presentation_id`, number, not required): Please enter the session number for which you are providing feedback.
* **Speaker Number** (`speaker_id`, number, not required): Please enter the speaker number for the presentation you attended.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, not required): Please choose the type of session (e.g., 1, 2, or 3).
* **Rating (1-5)** (`session_rating`, number, not required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the session, from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Please provide any comments or thoughts about the session.
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, not required): Please provide feedback about the session, such as what worked well or what could be improved.
* **Speaker Feedback** (`speaker_feedback`, text, not required): Please provide feedback about the speaker, such as their communication skills or presentation style.
* **Session Improvement** (`session_improvement`, text, not required): Please suggest ways to improve the session in the future.
* **Speaker Improvement** (`speaker_improvement`, text, not required): Please suggest ways to improve the speaker's presentation in the future.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, not required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the speaker, from 1 to 5.
* **Will Attend Again** (`will_attend_again`, select_one, not required): Please indicate whether you would attend the same session again, either "Yes" or "No".
