# Spine Care Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience at Spine Care facilities. Your input helps us improve the quality of care we provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of your experience at Spine Care in the **Feedback Form** field.
2. If you're a patient, please enter your name.
3. If you have a valid email address, please enter it for us to follow up on your feedback.
4. If you have a phone number, please enter it in case we need to contact you.
5. If you have any comments about your visit, please enter them in the **Comments** field.
6. If you're satisfied with the care you received, please rate your experience on a scale of 0 to 10.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form (Text)**: Enter a brief description of your experience at Spine Care.
* **Name (Text)**: Enter your name, if you're a patient or the patient's representative.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address, if you have one, so we can follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number, if you have one, in case we need to contact you.
* **Rating (Number)**: Rate your experience on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is "Not Satisfied" and 10 is "Extremely Satisfied".

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when describing your experience.
* Be honest about your satisfaction with the care you received.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, include them in the **Comments** field.
