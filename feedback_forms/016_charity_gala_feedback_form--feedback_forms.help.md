# Charity Gala Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the charity gala event to help us better understand their experience, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section completely and truthfully.
2. Answer each question according to your actual experience at the event.
3. Use the rating scale provided for each section to rate your experience (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Average, Poor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: This is your name or the name of the person who attended the gala (optional).
* **Email Address**: Your email address, which will be used for follow-up communications (optional).
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number, which will be used for follow-up communications (optional).
* **Name of Gala Event**: The name of the gala event you attended.
* **Date of Gala**: The date you attended the gala event.
* **Venue Quality and Elegance**: Rate the overall quality and elegance of the venue.
* **Overall Atmosphere and Ambiance**: Rate the mood and feel of the event.
* **Decorations and Setup**: Rate the quality of decorations and setup.
* **Quality of Dinner**: Rate the food you had at the gala.
* **Quality and Selection of Beverages**: Rate the quality and selection of drinks available.
* **Service Quality**: Rate the quality of service provided by staff.
* **Quality of Entertainment**: Rate the quality of performances or entertainment provided.
* **Was the Program Length Appropriate**: Rate the length of the program, was it too long, just right, or too short.
* **Clarity of Charitable Mission**: Rate how clear the charitable mission was.
* **Event Inspired You to Donate**: Rate how much the event motivated you to donate.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Gala**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
* **Recommendations for Next Gala**: Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvements for future events.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and honestly.
* Rate your experience based on your actual feelings and impressions.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations, please include them in the "Recommendations for Next Gala" field.
