# creative_writing_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on a creative writing piece, such as an article, story, or poem.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your feedback. The form is divided into the following sections:

* Personal information: First name, Last name, and Email address (optional)
* Rating and Comments: Rate your experience and provide any comments or feedback
* Time and Date: How much time did you spend reading the piece, and what date and time did you access it?
* Demographics: Select your favorite day of the week and the months you are available

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email address**: Optional field for your email address.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback on the creative writing piece.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the creative writing piece. Choose from Excellent, Good, Not bad, or Poor.
* **How much time you spend reading it?**: Estimate the amount of time you spent reading the piece.
* **Date**: Select the date you accessed the creative writing piece.
* **Time**: Select the time you accessed the creative writing piece.
* **Day**: Select your favorite day of the week.
* **Month**: Select the months you are available.
* **Year**: Select the year you accessed the creative writing piece.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate and honest feedback is greatly appreciated.
