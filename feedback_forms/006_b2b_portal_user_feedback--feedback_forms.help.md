# B2B Portal User Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from businesses that use the B2B portal. Your input will help us understand how we can improve the portal to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide helpful feedback, please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly. You can select multiple options for the questions that allow multiple selections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Company Name** (`respondent_company`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Your Job Title** (`respondent_title`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your job title or position, please enter it here.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **How often do you use the B2B portal?** (`portal_usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Please select the frequency with which you interact with the portal.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
* **Overall portal satisfaction rating** (`portal_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the portal, from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 10 (very satisfied).
* **How easy is the portal to navigate?** (`ease_of_navigation`, select_one, required): Choose how easy it is to navigate the portal.
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Are the portal features effective for your needs?** (`feature_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Rate the effectiveness of the portal features.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Not Applicable
* **Which features do you find most useful?** (`most_useful_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select the features that you find most useful for your business needs.
	+ Order Management
	+ Inventory Tracking
	+ Pricing Information
	+ Account Management
	+ Reports and Analytics
	+ Communication Tools
	+ Document Storage
	+ Mobile Access
* **Which features are least useful?** (`least_useful_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select the features that you find least useful for your business needs.
	+ Order Management
	+ Inventory Tracking
	+ Pricing Information
	+ Account Management
	+ Reports and Analytics
	+ Communication Tools
	+ Document Storage
	+ Mobile Access
* **How would you rate support experience?** (`support_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with our support team.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Have not used support
* **What new features would you like to see?** (`feature_requests`, text, optional): Provide specific suggestions for new features that you would like to see in the portal.
* **What could be improved?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Offer specific suggestions for areas that could be improved.
* **Would you recommend this portal to other businesses?** (`recommend_portal`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you would recommend the portal to others.
	+ Definitely Yes
	+ Probably Yes
	+ Unsure
	+ Probably No
	+ Definitely No
