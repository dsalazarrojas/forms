# Airline Flight Experience Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airline Flight Experience Inquiry Form is designed to collect feedback from passengers about their recent flight experience. Your responses will help the airline identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about its operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill out the form completely, providing your Passenger Name, Email Address, Flight Number, and Travel Date.
3. Select the Cabin Class for your flight.
4. Rate the following aspects of your flight experience:
	* Check-in and Boarding Satisfaction
	* Cabin Cleanliness
	* Seat Comfort
	* Staff Professionalism and Service
	* Food and Beverage Quality (if applicable)
	* In-flight Entertainment Options (if applicable)
	* Overall Flight Experience Rating
5. Provide any Additional Comments or Suggestions you may have.
6. Indicate whether you would recommend our airline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address associated with your flight booking.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number as it appears on your boarding pass or ticket.
* **Travel Date**: Enter the date of your flight.
* **Cabin Class**: Select the class of service you flew (Economy, Premium Economy, Business, or First Class).
* **Check-in and Boarding Satisfaction**: Rate your experience with the check-in and boarding process, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Cabin Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the cabin, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Seat Comfort**: Rate the comfort of your seat, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Staff Professionalism and Service**: Rate the staff's professionalism and service, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Food and Beverage Quality**: Rate the quality of the food and beverages provided, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). (Only applicable if you were served food and beverages on your flight.)
* **In-flight Entertainment Options**: Rate the entertainment options available on your flight, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). (Only applicable if you were provided with in-flight entertainment options.)
* **Overall Flight Experience Rating**: Rate your overall flight experience, from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your flight experience.
* **Would you recommend our airline?**: Indicate whether you would recommend the airline, with options for "True", "False", or "Maybe".

Please note that some fields are required for the form to be processed. Please fill out the form completely and honestly to provide valuable feedback.
