# Science Advisory Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in science advisory sessions. The goal is to gather input on the quality and effectiveness of our science advisory services. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our programs and better serve our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information (Participant Name, Email, Phone, Research Group Name, and Assigned Researcher Name).
2. Provide your session details (Session ID, Date of Session, Start Time, Session Goals, and Session Objectives).
3. Report your session attendance and overall satisfaction.
4. Share your feedback and additional comments about the session.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier given to you for this session.
* **Date of Session** (`date_of_session`, date, optional): If you attended the session, enter the date you attended. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): If you attended the session, enter the start time of the session. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Session Goals** (`session_goals`, note, optional): Briefly describe the goals discussed during the session. (Note: This field is not displayed on the form due to its nature, but please provide a brief description of the session goals.)
* **Session Objectives** (`session_objectives`, text, required): Describe the objectives or topics discussed during the session.
* **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`, select_one, optional): Estimate the number of attendees at the session. Choose from the following options: 1-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20, 21-25, 26 or more.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session. Choose from the following options: Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Very Satisfied, Extremely Satisfied.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts on the session, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Research Group Name** (`research_group_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your research group.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Researcher Name** (`assigned_researcher_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the researcher assigned to you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them here.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): If you attended the session, estimate the duration of the session in minutes. If not, leave this field blank.
