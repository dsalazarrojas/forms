# Online Series Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about online series episodes. It helps us understand your thoughts and opinions about the series and its creators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the episode title in the `Episode Title` field.
2. Rate the episode by selecting one of the options in the `Rate this episode` field.
3. Write your thoughts and feedback in the `Your feedback` field.
4. If you have any recommendations, select them in the `Recommendation` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `E-mail` field, if you wish to receive feedback.
6. Fill in any other relevant details, such as `Season Number`, `Episode Number`, `Date`, `Time`, `Category`, `Creator`, `Publisher`, `Team`, and `Form ID` if you have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Episode Title** (`episode_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of the episode.
* **Rate this episode** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to rate this episode: Good, Fair, or other options.
* **Your feedback** (`review`, note, optional): Write your thoughts and feedback about this episode.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more recommendations about this episode.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive feedback.
* **Season Number** (`season_number`, number, optional): Enter the season number of the episode.
* **Episode Number** (`episode_number`, number, optional): Enter the episode number of the series.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you watched or experienced the episode.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you watched or experienced the episode.
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the series.
* **Creator** (`creator`, text, optional): Enter the name of the creator of the series.
* **Publisher** (`publisher`, text, optional): Enter the name of the publisher of the series.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): Enter the team involved in the production of the series.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select to submit your feedback, or cancel to discard.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows when the feedback was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows when the feedback was last updated.
* **Published At** (`published_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows when the episode was published.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows when the episode was deleted.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows the user ID.
* **Email ID** (`email_id`, number, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows the email ID.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows the form ID.
* **Category ID** (`category_id`, number, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows the category ID.
* **Series ID** (`series_id`, number, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows the series ID.
* **Episode ID** (`episode_id`, number, optional): This field is automatically generated and shows the episode ID.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
* You can submit your feedback anonymously or with your email address.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at [support email].

This guide helps users understand the purpose and usage of the online series feedback form, ensuring they can provide valuable and helpful feedback to the creators of the series.
