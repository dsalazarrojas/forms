# learning_resources_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and survey information from users about learning resources at the institution. The goal is to understand user perceptions and experiences with these resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by answering the questions on each page. You can navigate through the pages using the next and back buttons.
2.  When answering the questions, select the options that best match your experiences and perceptions.
3.  If you have any comments or suggestions, use the "Do you have any comments or suggestions?" field to share them.
4.  Finally, provide the date and time you completed the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role at the institution?** (`course_info`, select_one, required: false): Answer this question to help us understand your affiliation with the institution.
* **What learning resources are you using?** (`learning_resources_used`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all the resources that apply to your experiences.
* **How would you rate the effectiveness of these resources?** (`resource_efficacy`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of the resources you selected.
* **How would you rate the accessibility of these resources?** (`accessibility`, select_one, required: false): Choose a rating that reflects your experience with the resources.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, note, required: false): Share any comments or suggestions you may have about the resources.
* **What date did you take this survey on?** (`date_submitted`, date, required: false): Provide the date you completed the survey.
* **What time did you take this survey on?** (`time_submitted`, time, required: false): Provide the time you completed the survey.
