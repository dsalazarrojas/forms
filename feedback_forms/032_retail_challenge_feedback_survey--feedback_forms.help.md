# Retail Challenge Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Challenge Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with a retail challenge. This form will help us understand customer satisfaction and help us improve future retail challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Answer questions about your experience with the retail challenge, such as whether you participated in the challenge, where you participated, and what score you gave it.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the challenge.
5. Review the "Follow Up" section to provide further information about follow-up actions that need to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Contact Name**
* (contact_name): Enter the name of the person submitting the feedback.
### 2. **Contact Email**
* (contact_email): Enter the email address of the person submitting the feedback.
### 3. **Challenge Date**
* (challenge_date): Enter the date of the retail challenge you participated in.
### 4. **Challenge Type**
* (challenge_type): Select 'Yes' if you participated in the retail challenge, 'No' otherwise.
### 5. **Feedback**
* (feedback): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the challenge.
### 6. **Challenge Location**
* (challenge_location): Select 'Yes' if the retail challenge took place at the store, 'No' otherwise.
### 7. **Challenge Score**
* (challenge_score): Enter the score you gave the retail challenge.
### 8. **Contact Phone**
* (contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the feedback.
### 9. **Contact Phone Extension**
* (contact_phone_extension): Enter the extension of the phone number, if applicable.
### 10. **Challenge Comments**
* (challenge_comments): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the challenge.
### 11. **Sales Incentive**
* (sales_incentive): Select 'Yes' if you were offered a sales incentive, 'No' otherwise.
### 12. **Other Comments**
* (other_comments): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the challenge.
### 13. **Contact Person**
* (contact): Enter the name of the person who will be contacted for follow-up.
### 14. **Follow Up**
* (follow_up): Select 'Yes' if follow-up is required, 'No' otherwise.
### 15. **Follow Up Comments**
* (follow_up_comments): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about follow-up actions.
### 16. **Follow Up Date**
* (follow_up_date): Enter the date for follow-up actions to be taken.
### 17. **Follow Up Time**
* (follow_up_time): Enter the time for follow-up actions to be taken.
### 18. **Follow Up Contact Person**
* (follow_up_contact): Enter the name of the person who will be contacted for follow-up.
### 19. **Follow Up Phone**
* (follow_up_phone): Enter the phone number of the person who will be contacted for follow-up.
### 20. **Follow Up Status**
* (follow_up_status): Select 'Active' if follow-up actions are still required, 'Inactive' otherwise.
### 21. **Follow Up Comments**
* (follow_up_comments): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about follow-up actions.
### 22. **Follow Up Location**
* (follow_up_location): Select 'Yes' if follow-up actions will be taken at the store, 'No' otherwise.
### 23. **Follow Up Score**
* (follow_up_score): Enter the score given to follow-up actions.
### 24. **Follow Up Note**
* (follow_up_note): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about follow-up actions.
