# College Course Feedback Form Instructor - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experiences with a college course instructor. The feedback collected will help instructors identify areas of strength and weakness, allowing them to improve their teaching methods and engage students more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2.  For each question, select the rating that best describes your experience.
3.  If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the appropriate fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Course and Section** (`course_section`, text, required): Enter the name of the course and section you took with the instructor.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
*   **Semester or Term** (`semester`, text, required): Enter the semester or term you took the course in.
*   **Submit Anonymously** (`student_anonymous`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you wish to remain anonymous, or "No" if you wish to provide your name and contact information.
*   **Subject Matter Knowledge and Expertise** (`knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's subject matter knowledge and expertise, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Communication and Explanation Skills** (`communication_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's ability to explain concepts clearly, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Enthusiasm and Engagement** (`enthusiasm_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's enthusiasm and engagement during the course, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Responsiveness to Student Questions and Concerns** (`responsiveness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's responsiveness to student questions and concerns, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Quality and Timeliness of Feedback** (`feedback_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality and timeliness of feedback from the instructor, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Course Organization and Structure** (`organization_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's ability to organize and structure the course material, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Fairness and Impartiality** (`fairness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's fairness and impartiality, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Accessibility and Office Hours** (`accessibility_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's availability and willingness to provide help outside of class, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Overall Instructor Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the instructor's overall performance, with options ranging from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
*   **Instructor Strengths** (`strengths_comment`, text, required): Describe what the instructor did very well.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_comment`, text, required): Describe areas where the instructor could improve.
*   **Most Valuable Teaching Technique or Approach** (`most_valuable`, text, optional): Describe the most valuable teaching method or approach used by the instructor.
*   **Would you recommend this instructor to other students?** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): Select "Definitely Yes" if you would recommend the instructor, or select "Definitely No" if you would not.
*   **Would you take another course with this instructor?** (`take_again`, select_one, required): Select "Definitely Yes" if you would take another course with the instructor, or select "Definitely No" if you would not.
*   **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback or suggestions for the instructor.
