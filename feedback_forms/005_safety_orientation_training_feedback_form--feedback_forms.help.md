# Safety Orientation Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees who have attended the safety orientation training program. The goal is to gather information on the program's effectiveness, content, and overall experience. Your input will help us improve the program and provide a better experience for future attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, as each field is an essential part of the feedback process.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your thoughts and suggestions.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This field is used to indicate that you have reached the first page of the form and are ready to proceed with the feedback process.
* **Employee Information**: Please provide your name, employee ID, or any other relevant identifying information.
* **Training Information**: Describe the training program you attended, including its title, date, and any other relevant details.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and suggestions about the training program. What did you like or dislike about it? What improvements would you suggest?
* **More Comments (First Page)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Employee Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the training program. Please select one of the provided options (1-10).
* **More Comments (Training Effectiveness)**: If you have any thoughts about the effectiveness of the training program, please provide them here.
* **More Comments (Second Page)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Training Effectiveness**: Select one of the provided options (Highly Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Effective) to rate the training program's effectiveness.
* **More Comments**: If you have any further thoughts or suggestions, please provide them here.
