# Peer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Peer Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from peers on individual performance and collaboration. This feedback will help team members grow and improve in their roles.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Overall Rating**: This section is optional, but if you choose to use it, you will rate your peer overall. Please select a number between 1 and 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
- **How likely is this peer to be a future collaborator?**: You can select from a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being "not likely" and 5 being "very likely". This question helps assess the likelihood of working with the peer again.
- **How well did you find our team's communication effective?**: Select all that apply from the list "Not very effective", "Somewhat effective", and "Very effective". This helps to evaluate the effectiveness of team communication.
- **What are some areas for improvement?**: Provide specific, constructive text feedback on areas where the peer could improve.
- **What are some areas where this peer excels?**: Describe specific strengths of the peer in this field.
- **Overall Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your peer.

## Tips

* Be specific and constructive with your feedback.
* Focus on the peer's performance and collaboration, not personal opinions or biases.
* Review the form carefully to ensure you provide a comprehensive and accurate assessment.

This help guide provides a clear and concise explanation of each field, ensuring users understand the purpose and correct usage of the Peer Feedback Form.
