# Government Feedback Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to allow citizens to send feedback to the government, helping to identify areas of improvement, express concerns, and provide suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (if any) and provide as much information as possible for a complete feedback.
2. Select the correct category and department that best match your feedback.
3. Enter a clear and concise subject and body to describe your feedback.
4. Enter your contact information (citizen's name and email) so that we can get in touch with you.
5. Review your submission before clicking the "submit" button to ensure everything is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **citizen_name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you.
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact number for additional communication.
* **message** (Note, Optional): Enter a detailed description of your feedback, concern, or suggestion.
* **category** (Select One, Optional): Choose the category that best describes your feedback (Feedback, Suggestion, or Other).
* **department** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the department that is most relevant to your feedback (Department 1, Department 2, Department 3).
* **submit** (Text, Optional): This is a submit button, do not fill in anything here.
* **email_to** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address where you would like to receive the response.
* **subject** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your feedback.
* **body** (Note, Optional): Enter a detailed description of your feedback, concern, or suggestion.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise when filling in the form.
* If you're unsure which category or department best suits your feedback, you can select "Other".
* Make sure to enter your contact information so that we can get in touch with you.
* Review your submission before sending it to ensure everything is accurate.
