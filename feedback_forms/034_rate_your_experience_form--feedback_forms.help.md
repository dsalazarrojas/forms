# Rate Your Experience Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rate Your Experience Form is a survey designed to collect feedback from customers about their interaction with our service. It aims to understand how satisfied they are with our service, communication, responsiveness, empathy, conflict resolution, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category for each question based on your experience.
2. Choose one option per category.
3. Make sure to submit your responses before leaving the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience**: Select "Yes" if you overall experience was positive, "No" otherwise.
* **Satisfaction With Service**: Select all options that apply to your level of satisfaction with our service, e.g., "Satisfied", "Dissatisfied".
* **Service Quality**: Select "Good" if you were satisfied with the quality of our service, "Poor" otherwise.
* **Communication Skills**: Select "Yes" if you felt that our communication skills were effective, "No" otherwise.
* **Responsiveness**: Select "Yes" if you felt that we responded to your needs and concerns, "No" otherwise.
* **Empathy**: Select "Yes" if you felt that we showed empathy towards you, "No" otherwise.
* **Conflict Resolution**: Select "Yes" if you were satisfied with how we resolved any conflicts, "No" otherwise.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select "Satisfied" if you were satisfied with your overall experience, "Dissatisfied" otherwise.
* **Will Recommend**: Select "Yes" if you would recommend our service to others, "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field (not shown in this form).
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
