# Food Truck Market Research Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from customers about their experiences with food trucks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Choose your rating of the food truck experience in the "Rating" field.
3. Select your favorite flavors in the "Favorite Flavors" field.
4. Enter your favorite cuisines in the "Favorite Cuisines" field.
5. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience in the "Feedback Comments" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
8. If you have visited the food truck recently, enter the date of your last visit in the "Date of Last Visit" field.

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like to be known.

* **Favorite Cuisines** (`favorite_cuisines`, text, required: false): Enter your favorite cuisines or leave this field blank if you don't have a preference.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_last_visit`, date, required: false): If you have visited the food truck recently, enter the date of your last visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
