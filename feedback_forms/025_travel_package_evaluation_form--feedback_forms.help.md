# Travel Package Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluations from travelers about the travel packages they have experienced. This form will help us to understand their satisfaction level with the package, identify areas of improvement, and provide valuable insights to enhance our travel services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title of the travel package you are evaluating.
2. Provide details about your travel package experience.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the package.
4. Share any feedback you may have about the package.
5. Rate the package on a scale of 1-10.
6. Indicate whether you would recommend the package to others.
7. Choose the package that matches your experience (yes or no).
8. Enter your contact information (email).
9. Confirm that you have actually experienced the travel package (yes or no).
10. Finally, provide a time stamp of when you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Package Evaluation Form** (`travel_package_evaluation_form`, text, required): Enter a brief title of the travel package you are evaluating. This will help us to understand which package you are referring to.
* **Travel Package Details** (`travel_package_details`, number, optional): Provide details about your travel package experience, such as duration, type of trip, or notable events. This will help us to get a better understanding of your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the travel package on a scale of satisfied or dissatisfied.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback or comments you may have about the travel package. This will help us to identify areas of improvement.
* **Travel Package Rating** (`travel_package_rating`, number, required): Rate the travel package on a scale of 1-10.
* **Recommend Travel Package** (`recommend_travel_package`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the travel package to others.
* **Will Recommend** (`will_recommend`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which specific travel packages you would recommend to others (yes or no).
* **Travel Package Rating 2** (`travel_package_rating_2`, number, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, but we are not sure what it is used for.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Rate the travel package on a scale of 1-10.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or follow-up actions.
* **Submit Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Provide a time stamp of when you submitted the form. This will help us to track the submission time.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): This field is similar to the previous one, but we are not sure what it is used for.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): This field is not clear and we are not sure what it is used for. We would recommend skipping it for now.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number if you have any questions or concerns about the travel package.
* **Travel Package** (`travel_package`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have actually experienced the travel package (yes or no).
