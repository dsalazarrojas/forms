## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about project progress, adoption, and feedback. It helps to assess the current state of projects and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant project progress level from the "Project Progress" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the current status of your project's milestones from the "Project Milestones" dropdown menu.
3. Indicate whether your project has been adopted by the organization or not.
4. Select the level of project adoption (high, medium, or low).
5. Provide your email address (if applicable).
6. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
7. Enter the date and time of project completion (if applicable).
8. Select the time zone where the project is being worked on.
9. Enter any additional comments about the project.
10. Select the status of your project (in progress, completed, or cancelled).
11. Enter any additional comments about the project.
12. Enter the name and description of your project (if applicable).
13. Enter the start and end dates and times of your project (if applicable).
14. Enter the total hours worked on the project (if applicable).
