# Library Resources Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Library Resources Assessment Form is used to gather feedback on the availability and accessibility of library resources. This form helps library administrators and staff evaluate their resources and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and provide your honest feedback.
2. Answer each question with the corresponding option that best describes your experience or availability of library resources.
3. You can select multiple options for select_multiple fields.
4. Submit your feedback by selecting the "Submit" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): This field is for you to provide a brief introduction or background information about your response.
* **Library resources** (`library_resources`, `text`, required: false): Describe your experience or availability of library resources, e.g., books, journals, research databases, or digital media.
* **Library hours of operation** (`library_hours_of_operation`, `text`, required: false): Mention the days and hours when library resources are available.
* **Library collections** (`library_collections`, `text`, required: false): Specify the types of library resources available, e.g., books, journals, research databases, or digital media.
* **Electronic collection** (`library_electronic_collection`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all types of electronic resources you have access to, such as:
	+ Books and e-books
	+ Journals and articles
	+ Research databases
	+ Digital media
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Access to resources** (`access_to_resources`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how often you have access to library resources:
	+ Always available
	+ Sometimes available
	+ Not available
* **Resource availability during library hours** (`resource_availability_during_library_hours`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the days when you have access to library resources during regular operating hours:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Resource availability outside library hours** (`availability_of_resources_outside_library_hours`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if library resources are available outside regular operating hours:
	+ Available
	+ Not available
* **Resource availability at other sites** (`availability_of_resources_at_other_sites`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if library resources are available at other sites:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Availability of print resources** (`availability_of_print_resources`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify if print resources are available:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Access to faculty** (`access_to_faculty`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who has access to library resources:
	+ Faculty members
	+ Students
	+ Staff
* **Access to library resources for non-majors** (`access_to_library_resources_for_non_majors`, `select_one`, required: false): Specify who has access to library resources:
	+ Non-majors
	+ Majors
	+ All students
* **Access to library resources for majors** (`access_to_library_resources_for_majors`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose who has access to library resources:
	+ Majors
	+ Non-majors
	+ All students
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about library resources.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Submit" to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your abilities.
* If you're unsure about a particular question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to help library administrators and staff improve their resources, so your input is valuable!
