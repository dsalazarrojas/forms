# digital_care_communication_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and experiences from users regarding their recent visit. It's meant to be completed by patients who have had a visit at a healthcare institution and want to share their thoughts and opinions on their communication experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer the questions on each page carefully, as they are designed to help us understand your experience.
2. You can select multiple options on the second page.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
4. If a page has a "Please select one" option, pick the one that best describes your situation.
5. If a page asks for a date, enter the date in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
6. If a page asks for a time, enter the time in the correct format (e.g., 03:45 PM).
7. Finally, submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the primary issue with your recent visit?** (*select_multiple*, Required): This question is asking about the main problem you faced during your visit. You can select multiple reasons that apply to your experience. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Select one of the following options** (*select_multiple*, Not Required): This is a list of potential issues you might have faced. You can choose one or more that apply to you.
* **Please choose at least 2 from the list** (*select_multiple*, Not Required): This is a reminder to choose at least two options from the list provided.
* **Please select one** (*select_one*, Required): This is a single select option where you have to choose one of the two given choices. Select the one that best describes your situation.
* **Other** (*select_one*, Not Required): This option is for when none of the other choices apply, and you have a unique experience to share.
* **Please enter a date** (*date*, Not Required): This is where you enter the date of your visit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Please enter a time** (*time*, Not Required): This is where you enter the time of your visit in 12-hour format (e.g., 03:45 PM).
* **Enter your email** (*email*, Not Required): This is where you enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or responses.
