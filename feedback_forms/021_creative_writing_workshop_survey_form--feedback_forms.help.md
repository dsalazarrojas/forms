# Creative Writing Workshop Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Writing Workshop Survey Form is designed to gather feedback from participants who have attended the workshop. This form helps us understand your experience, satisfaction, and any suggestions you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the required fields.
2. For optional fields, provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
3. Use the options provided for each field to select the most accurate response.
4. If you have any other comments or suggestions not listed in the optional fields, please use the text box provided for "Comments" or "Any other goals" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Workshop Participants
* **Workshop Participants**: This field asks whether you participated in the workshop. Please select "Yes" if you attended.

### Name
* **Name**: Please enter your name as it is spelled.

### Email Address
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.

### Feedback
* **Feedback**: This is an optional text box for you to provide any comments or suggestions about the workshop.

### Comments (optional)
* **Comments (optional)**: This is another optional text box for any additional comments or suggestions.

### Did you attend the workshop?
* **Did you attend the workshop?**: Please select "Yes" if you attended the workshop.

### Date attended
* **Date attended**: If you attended the workshop, please select the date you attended.

### Time attended
* **Time attended**: If you attended the workshop, please select the time you attended.

### Rating
* **Rating**: This field asks for your rating of the workshop. Please select "Good" if you are satisfied with your experience.

### What was your rating for the workshop?
* **What was your rating for the workshop?**: This field is similar to "Rating" and asks for your evaluation of the workshop.

### Reasons for your rating
* **Reasons for your rating**: Provide the reasons why you rated the workshop as you did.

### How do you rate your satisfaction?
* **How do you rate your satisfaction?**: This field asks for your satisfaction level with the workshop. Please select "Satisfied" if you are satisfied.

### Any suggestions
* **Any suggestions**: This is an optional text box for any additional comments or suggestions.

### Which topics were covered?
* **Which topics were covered?**: This field asks about the topics that were covered during the workshop. Please select the ones that apply to you.

### Any other topics
* **Any other topics**: This field is for any additional topics that were not listed.

### What were your goals for attending the workshop?
* **What were your goals for attending the workshop?**: This field asks about your goals for attending the workshop. Please select the ones that apply to you.

### Any other goals
* **Any other goals**: This field is for any additional goals you may have had.

### What outcomes did you achieve?
* **What outcomes did you achieve?**: This field asks about the outcomes you achieved during the workshop. Please select the ones that apply to you.

### Any other outcomes
* **Any other outcomes**: This field is for any additional outcomes you may have achieved.

### Would you recommend this workshop?
* **Would you recommend this workshop?**: Please select "Yes" if you would recommend the workshop to others.

### Why did you recommend this workshop?
* **Why did you recommend this workshop?**: This field asks for your reasons for recommending the workshop.

### Reasons for your recommendation
* **Reasons for your recommendation**: This field is to provide any additional reasons why you recommended the workshop.

### Can we follow up with you?
* **Can we follow up with you?**: Please select "Yes" if you are available for follow-up.

### Preferred contact method
* **Preferred contact method**: This field asks for your preferred contact method. Please select the method that works best for you.

### Method of contact
* **Method of contact**: This field asks about the method we should use to contact you. Please select the one that applies to you.

This guide should help users complete the form efficiently and effectively. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
