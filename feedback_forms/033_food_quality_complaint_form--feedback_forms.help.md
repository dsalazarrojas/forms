# Food Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about food quality complaints from customers or their representatives. It allows them to report their concerns and provide a photo of the affected food item, if available.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the category that best describes your complaint from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or multiple options.
2.  Write a brief description of your complaint in the text field.
3.  If you have a photo of the affected food item, you can upload it in the designated field.
4.  Provide your contact email address so we can reach out to you for further follow-up.
5.  Enter your contact phone number for additional communication.
