# training_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "training_feedback_form" is a form designed to gather feedback from users about their training experience. It allows users to provide their thoughts and opinions about the training program, which helps us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button.
2. Fill in the required fields as prompted. You can type your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
3. Rate your overall experience by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback (Feedback)** (`feedback`, text, optional): Use this field to provide a detailed description of your training experience. Please be as specific as possible, and feel free to include any positive or negative comments you have about the training.
* **Rating (rating)** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with our training program. Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
