# Student Internet Access At Home Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their internet access at home, which is an essential factor in online learning and remote work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and understand its purpose.
2.  Answer the questions truthfully and accurately.
3.  Fill out all required fields (*).
4.  Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Internet Access At Home
 * **At Home** (`at_home`, `select_one`, required): This field asks whether you have internet access at home. Please select 'Yes' if you have internet access at home, and 'No' if you do not.

### Feedback
 * **Feedback** (`internet_access_at_home_feedback`, `note`, optional): This field is for providing additional comments or feedback about your internet access at home. You can type any information you think is relevant to help us understand your situation.

### Options
 * **Options** (`internet_access_at_home_feedback_options`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows you to choose any options that apply to your internet access at home. Please select all that apply.

### Date
 * **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): This field is for you to enter the date you are filling out this form.
