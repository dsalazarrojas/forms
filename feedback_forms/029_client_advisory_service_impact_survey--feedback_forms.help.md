# Client Advisory Service Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Advisory Service Impact Survey is a form designed to gather information about the impact of our advisory services on your business. This form will help us understand how our services have contributed to your company's growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Provide specific examples or details when required.
3. Use the options provided for select-type questions.
4. Fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: This is the name of your company as we have recorded it in our records. Please correct any errors or provide the correct name.
* **How long have you been receiving our advisory services?**: Choose the duration of time you have been receiving our advisory services. This will help us understand the scope of our collaboration.
* **Primary Advisor Name (optional)**: If you have a primary advisor, please enter their name. This information will help us understand who has been working closely with you.
* **Strategic Impact Rating (optional)**: This question is to rate the impact of our services on your strategic direction. Please answer based on your experience.
* **Impact on Revenue Growth**: Rate the impact of our services on your revenue growth. Answer based on your company's experience.
* **Impact on Operational Efficiency**: Rate the impact of our services on your operational efficiency. Please answer based on your company's experience.
* **Impact on Strategic Clarity and Direction**: Rate the impact of our services on your strategic clarity and direction. Answer based on your company's experience.
* **Impact on Risk Mitigation**: Rate the impact of our services on your risk mitigation. Please answer based on your company's experience.
* **What has been the most valuable piece of advice or service provided?**: Share with us the most valuable advice or service we have provided you. This will help us understand where we have had the most significant impact.
* **Tangible Results Description (optional)**: If you have achieved tangible results or milestones as a result of our services, please describe them. This will help us understand the specifics of our impact.
* **Advisor Accessibility and Responsiveness**: How would you rate your advisor's accessibility and responsiveness? Please answer based on your experience.
* **Value for Investment**: Do you feel our services provide good value for the investment? Answer based on your experience.
* **Areas of Improvement**: If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our services, please provide them. This will help us understand areas for growth and development.
* **Likelihood to Renew or Continue**: How likely are you to renew or continue our advisory services? Please answer based on your experience.
* **Willingness to Provide Testimonial (optional)**: If you are willing to provide a testimonial, please select this option. This will help us understand your experience with our services.
* **Your Role/Title**: Please enter your role or title within your company. This will help us understand your perspective on our services.
* **Date**: Please enter the current date to help us track your feedback.
* **Thank you for your Partnership**: This is a thank you note to express our appreciation for your participation.
