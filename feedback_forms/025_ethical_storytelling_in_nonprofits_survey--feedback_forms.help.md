# Ethical Storytelling In Nonprofits Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand how nonprofits tell stories and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question as accurately and concisely as possible. You can select multiple options for some questions, and provide a brief text response for others. If you have any other comments or questions, please use the final question at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What kind of stories do you currently tell?** (`section_1_title`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question helps us understand the type of stories you share with your audience. Please select one of the options: Story of impact, Story of transformation, or Other.
* **What stories do you currently tell?** (`section_1_answer`, `text`, required/optional): This question allows you to provide a brief description of the stories you share with your audience.
* **What is the goal of the story?** (`section_2_title`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question helps us understand the purpose of the stories you share. Please select one of the options: To educate, To entertain, To engage, To inspire, To motivate, or Other.
* **Section 2 Answer** (`section_2_answer`, `text`, required/optional): This question is where you can provide a brief description of your story's goal.
* **Which story-telling practices does your organization currently use?** (`story_telling_practices`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question helps us understand how your organization shares stories. Please select all that apply from the options: Storytelling workshops or training, Storytelling sessions or panels, Social media or digital platforms, Print or broadcast media, Other, or No story-telling practices.
* **Story Telling Practices Answer** (`story_telling_practices_answer`, `text`, required/optional): This question is where you can provide more details about your story-telling practices.
* **What are some challenges you face when telling stories?** (`challenges_title`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question helps us identify areas where you may need support. Please select all that apply from the options: Lack of resources, Lack of time, Lack of skills, Difficulty reaching audience, Other, or No challenges.
* **Challenges Answer** (`challenges_answer`, `text`, required/optional): This question is where you can provide more details about the challenges you face when telling stories.
* **What suggestions do you have for organizations to improve story-telling?** (`suggestions`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This question helps us understand how you think we can improve story-telling. Please select all that apply from the options: More resources, More training, More time, More feedback, Other, or No suggestions.
* **Suggestions Answer** (`suggestions_answer`, `text`, required/optional): This question is where you can provide more details about your suggestions.
* **What are your next steps?** (`next_steps`, `select_one`, required/optional): This question helps us understand your future plans. Please select one of the options: Continue with current practices, Try new practices, Change practices completely, or Not sure.
* **Next Steps Answer** (`next_steps_answer`, `text`, required/optional): This question is where you can provide more details about your next steps.
* **Do you have any other comments or questions?** (`final_question`, `text`, required/optional): This question is where you can provide any additional thoughts or questions you may have.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions as accurately and concisely as possible.
- If you have any other comments or questions, please use the final question at the end of the form.
- If you are unsure about any question, please select "Not sure" or "Other" and provide a brief explanation.
