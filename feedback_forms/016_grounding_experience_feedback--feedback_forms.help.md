# Grounding Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their grounding experience. The form is intended to gather ratings and comments from users to improve the product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as needed and click on the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Field 1: rating_1
* **rating_1**: Optional numerical rating from 1-5

### Field 2: rating_2
* **rating_2**: Optional numerical rating from 1-5

### Field 3: comments
* **comments**: Optional comments field for any additional feedback

### Field 4: rating_3
* **rating_3**: Optional numerical rating from 1-5

### Field 5: rating_4
* **rating_4**: Optional numerical rating from 1-5

### Field 6: comments_2
* **comments_2**: Optional comments field for any additional feedback

### Field 7: rating_5
* **rating_5**: Optional numerical rating from 1-5

## Tips
* Please provide a rating if you feel it accurately reflects your experience.
* Use the comments fields to provide any additional feedback or comments.
* You can provide ratings for all or some of the fields, depending on your experience.
