## Basketball Referee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect feedback on a basketball referee's performance during a game. Feedback is essential for the referee to improve their skills and provide better performance in future games.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the game date.
2.  Enter the game location.
3.  Select the home team.
4.  Select the away team.
5.  Enter the referee's name.
6.  Select the referee's level (Youth, High School, College, or Professional).
7.  Enter your name as the feedback provider.
8.  Select your role (Coach, Athletic Director, Player, Observer, or Other).
9.  Rate the referee's performance on the following aspects: 
    *   Knowledge of basketball rules (Scale 1-5).
    *   Court positioning and visibility (Scale 1-5).
    *   Consistency of calls (Scale 1-5).
    *   Communication and clarity (Scale 1-5).
    *   Fairness and impartiality (Scale 1-5).
10. Enter any specific incidents or concerns about the referee's performance.
11. Enter the referee's main strengths.
12. Enter areas for improvement.
13. Rate the overall performance of the referee (Scale 1-5).
14. Select whether you would request this referee again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Game date** (`game_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the game that the feedback is about.
*   **Game location** (`game_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the game that the feedback is about.
*   **Home team** (`home_team`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the home team in the game.
*   **Away team** (`away_team`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the away team in the game.
*   **Referee name** (`referee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the referee who was evaluated.
*   **Referee level** (`referee_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of the referee (Youth, High School, College, or Professional).
*   **Your name** (`feedback_provider`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the feedback provider.
*   **Your role** (`feedback_role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role (Coach, Athletic Director, Player, Observer, or Other).
*   **Knowledge of basketball rules** (`rule_knowledge`, `number`, required): Rate the referee's knowledge of basketball rules (Scale 1-5).
*   **Court positioning and visibility** (`positioning`, `number`, required): Rate the referee's positioning and visibility on the court (Scale 1-5).
*   **Consistency of calls** (`consistency`, `number`, required): Rate the referee's consistency of calls (Scale 1-5).
*   **Communication and clarity** (`communication`, `number`, required): Rate the referee's communication and clarity (Scale 1-5).
*   **Fairness and impartiality** (`fairness`, `number`, required): Rate the referee's fairness and impartiality (Scale 1-5).
*   **Referee's main strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, optional): Enter the main strengths of the referee.
*   **Areas for improvement** (`areas_improvement`, `text`, optional): Enter areas where the referee can improve.
*   **Any specific incidents or concerns** (`specific_incidents`, `text`, optional): Enter any specific incidents or concerns about the referee's performance.
*   **Overall referee rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the overall performance of the referee (Scale 1-5).
*   **Would you request this referee again** (`would_request_again`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you would request this referee again.
