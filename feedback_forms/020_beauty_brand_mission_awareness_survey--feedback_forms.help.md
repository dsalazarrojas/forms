# Beauty Brand Mission Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your awareness and perception of a beauty brand's mission, values, and communication. Your participation will help the brand understand its target audience and improve its mission awareness strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the following questions to help us understand your relationship with the beauty brand and its mission. The survey has 25 questions, and it's divided into five sections: respondent information, brand awareness, mission awareness, values alignment, and communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Respondent Information (Questions 1-3)
* **Full Name**: This is a text field where you can enter your full name. Please type your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to help us contact you if needed.
* **Age Group**: Select your age group from the options provided. This will help us understand your demographic.
* **Are You a Current Customer**: Choose your relationship with the brand from the options provided. This will help us understand your level of engagement with the brand.

### Brand Awareness (Questions 4-11)
* **How Familiar Are You with This Brand**: Choose how familiar you are with the brand from the options provided. This will help us understand your level of awareness.
* **How Did You First Discover This Brand**: Select how you first discovered the brand from the options provided. This will help us understand your discovery channels.
* **Overall Brand Perception**: Choose how you perceive the brand from the options provided. This will help us understand your overall impression.
* **Are You Aware of the Brand Mission**: Choose your level of awareness about the brand's mission from the options provided. This will help us understand your mission-related knowledge.
* **Understanding of the Brand Mission**: If you are aware of the brand's mission, describe your understanding in the text field. This will help us understand your interpretation.
* **Awareness of Sustainability Practices**: Choose your level of awareness about the brand's sustainability practices from the options provided. This will help us understand your knowledge about the brand's sustainable efforts.
* **Awareness of Ethical Practices**: Choose your level of awareness about the brand's ethical practices from the options provided. This will help us understand your knowledge about the brand's commitment to ethics.
* **Perceived Positive Impact on Society**: Choose how positively you perceive the brand's impact on society from the options provided. This will help us understand your perception about the brand's social responsibility.
