# observer_feedback_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from observers about an event, helping us understand their experience and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select an answer** (id: 1)
	* This field is used to indicate whether you've had the opportunity to observe the event.
2. **Enter your name** (id: 2)
	* Type your name in the provided text field to help us identify you as an observer.
3. **Enter the event title** (id: 3)
	* This field is used to reference the event you're providing feedback on. Please type the title of the event as seen on our promotional materials.
4. **Enter your email address** (id: 4)
	* This field is required for us to contact you if we need more information or to send you updates.
5. **Enter your phone number** (id: 5)
	* This field is optional, but including your phone number will help us reach you more efficiently.
6. **Provide feedback comments** (id: 6)
	* This is a text area where you can share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about the event. Please be as detailed as possible.
7. **Rate your experience** (id: 7)
	* This field asks you to rate your experience on a scale. Please select a number from 1 to 5 to reflect your satisfaction level.
8. **Rate the likelihood you'll recommend** (id: 8)
	* Similar to the previous field, this one asks you to rate your likelihood of recommending the event. Please select a number from 1 to 5.
9. **Recommend or not** (id: 9)
	* In this field, you'll select whether or not you recommend this event. Please choose 'Recommended' if you think others should attend.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you've already submitted feedback for this event or are not duplicating your response.
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve our events.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
