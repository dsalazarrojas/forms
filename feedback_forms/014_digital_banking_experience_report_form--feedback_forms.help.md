# digital_banking_experience_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their digital banking experience. It will help the bank identify areas for improvement and provide better service to its customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Select the correct options for each multiple-choice question.
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, use the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What type of account do you have with the bank?** (`account_details`, text, required: false)
This field asks about the type of account you hold with the bank. Please enter the type of account, such as checking, savings, or credit card.

### 2. **Can you describe any issues you experienced with your account?** (`issue_description`, text, required: false)
This field is for any issues or problems you may have encountered with your account. Please describe the issue in as much detail as possible.

### 3. **Do you think the bank's service was helpful?** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required: false)
This field asks about your overall experience with the bank's service. Select "Yes" if you found the service helpful, and "No" if you did not.

### 4. **How would you rate the bank's service?** (`feedback_rating`, select_multiple, required: false)
Rate the bank's service on a scale of Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.

### 5. **What is your bank's contact information?** (`bank_contact`, text, required: false)
Enter the contact information for your bank, including phone number, email, or any other relevant contact details.

### 6. **Bank Phone** (`bank_phone`, text, required: false)
Enter the phone number of your bank.

### 7. **Bank Email** (`bank_email`, email, required: false)
Enter the email address of your bank.

### 8. **Bank Website** (`bank_website`, text, required: false)
Enter the website of your bank.

### 9. **Is the bank active on social media?** (`bank_contact`, checkbox, required: false)
Check "Option A" if the bank is active on social media, and "Option B" if it is not.

### 10. **What is your account number?** (`account_number`, text, required: false)
Enter your account number.

### 11. **Account Type** (`account_type`, text, required: false)
Enter the type of account you have with the bank.

### 12. **How do you like the bank's mobile app?** (`bank_app`, text, required: false)
Rate your experience with the bank's mobile app.

### 13. **Who is your account manager's name?** (`account_manager`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of your account manager.

### 14. **Do you have any additional feedback for the bank?** (`feedback_message`, text, required: false)
Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the bank.

### 15. **What type of account number do you have?** (`account_number_type`, text, required: false)
Enter the type of account number you have.

### 16. **Bank Social Media 2** (`bank_social_media_2`, checkbox, required: false)
Check "Option A" if the bank is active on social media, and "Option B" if it is not.

### 17. **Please provide any additional details about your account.** (`account_details_2`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional details about your account.

### 18. **Can you provide more details about any issues you experienced with your account?** (`issue_description_2`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional details about the issues you experienced with your account.

## Tips

* Answer each question carefully and honestly.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the bank's support team.
