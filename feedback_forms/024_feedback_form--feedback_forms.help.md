# Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users on a product. The form is designed to be simple and easy to use, asking for minimal information to provide a clear understanding of the user's thoughts and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your feedback in the **Feedback Form** field (page 1).
2. Type in the name of the **Product Name** (page 2).
3. Provide a brief description of the **Product Description** (page 2).
4. If you have any **Suggestion(s)**, type it in (page 3).
5. If you have an **Email**, type it in (page 4), in the format of `example@example.com`.
6. If you have a **Phone**, type it in (page 4).
7. Select your **Rating** (page 5) from the scale: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.
8. Type in any additional **Comments** (page 6) you may have.
9. Select one or more **Category** (page 7) that best describe your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, text, required): This field is where you can enter your thoughts, suggestions, and feedback about the product.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, not required): Type in the name of the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, not required): Briefly describe the product you are providing feedback on.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, not required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Type in your email if you want us to contact you back about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Type in your phone number if you want us to contact you back about your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, not required): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your satisfaction with the product.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Type in any additional comments you may have about your feedback.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more categories that best describe your feedback.
