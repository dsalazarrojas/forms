# Publishing Reader Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Publishing Reader Feedback Report Form is designed to gather feedback from readers about published articles. This feedback helps the publishing team to improve the quality of future publications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the magazine title from the list of available options.
2. Select the type of feedback you have for the article (e.g. positive, negative, neutral).
3. Answer the question about the article's quality by selecting all that apply from the available options.
4. Answer the question about the relevance of the article by selecting one option from the available list.
5. If you have a personal email address, enter it in the "Email" field to receive follow-up questions or your feedback summary.
6. Type any additional comments or thoughts about the article in the "Message" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Magazine Title** (`magazine_title`, text, not required): Enter the title of the magazine that published the article.
* **Type of Feedback** (`feedback_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of feedback you have for the article. This will help the publishing team to categorize your feedback.
* **What was the quality of the article?** (`question1`, select_multiple, not required): Select all that apply to describe your opinion on the article's quality.
* **Was the article relevant?** (`question2`, select_one, not required): Select one option to describe your opinion on the article's relevance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address to receive follow-up questions or your feedback summary.
* **Message** (`message`, note, not required): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about the article (optional).
