# Bedroom Design Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bedroom Design Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from clients on their bedroom design project. It helps us refine the design and make adjustments to create a space that meets the client's needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name and title of the project.
2. Indicate which phase of the design you are providing feedback on (Initial Concept / Moodboard, Furniture Layout / Floor Plan, Color Palette and Textiles, or Final Rendering).
3. Rate the design elements on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being "unsatisfied" and 5 being "satisfied", for:
	* Overall Spatial Layout and Flow
	* Color Palette and Mood
	* Furniture Style and Selection
	* Adequacy of Storage Solutions
	* Lighting Plan and Fixtures
4. Provide specific comments on what you love most about the design and what specific element you would like to change or remove.
5. Let us know if the current design feels aligned with your budget.
6. Decide if you are ready to move to the next phase based on this design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your name to help us identify your feedback.
* **Which phase of the design are you providing feedback on?** (`project_phase`, select_one, required): This will help us understand which part of the design you are commenting on. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Initial Concept / Moodboard
	+ Furniture Layout / Floor Plan
	+ Color Palette and Textiles
	+ Final Rendering
* **Overall Spatial Layout and Flow** (`spatial_layout_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate the overall spatial layout of the design on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being "unsatisfied" and 5 being "satisfied".
* **Color Palette and Mood** (`color_palette_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate the color palette and mood of the design on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being "unsatisfied" and 5 being "satisfied".
* **Furniture Style and Selection** (`furniture_selection_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate the furniture style and selection of the design on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being "unsatisfied" and 5 being "satisfied".
* **Adequacy of Storage Solutions** (`storage_solutions_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate the adequacy of storage solutions in the design on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being "unsatisfied" and 5 being "satisfied".
* **Lighting Plan and Fixtures** (`lighting_plan_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate the lighting plan and fixtures of the design on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being "unsatisfied" and 5 being "satisfied".
* **What element of the design do you love the most?** (`element_you_love_most`, text, required): Please specify which element of the design you enjoy the most.
* **What specific element would you like to change or remove?** (`element_to_change`, text, required): Specify which element of the design you would like to change or remove.
* **Are there any new requirements or items to be added?** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any new requirements or items to be added to the design, please specify them here.
* **Does the current design feel aligned with your budget?** (`budget_alignment`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ Mostly
	+ Needs adjustment
	+ Unsure
* **Are you ready to move to the next phase based on this design?** (`next_steps_readiness`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Yes, proceed
	+ Yes, with the noted changes
	+ No, I need a revised concept
* **Date** (`feedback_submission_date`, date, required): Please select the date you are submitting this feedback.
* **Are there any comments you would like to add?** (`specific_critique`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please add them here.
