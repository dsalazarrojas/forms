# Nonclinical Study Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in nonclinical studies. It helps us understand your experience and opinions about our study, allowing us to make improvements for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You can submit your feedback as a completed form or save it for later and submit it when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Nonclinical Study Feedback Form
* **Title**: This is the title of the form, which is currently set to "Nonclinical Study Feedback Form". You can leave this field as is or change it if you have a specific title in mind.

### 2. Research Questions
* **Research Questions**: This field is for you to provide any questions you had about the study or its process. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your concerns.

### 3. Feedback Rating
* **Feedback Rating**: This field is for you to rate your overall experience with the study. Please select a rating from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good) and provide a reason for your rating in the next field if you'd like.

### 4. Feedback Comments
* **Feedback Comments**: Here you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the study. This is a great space to share your thoughts and help us improve.

### 5. Feedback Rating Reason
* **Feedback Rating Reason**: This field is for you to explain your reason for selecting the feedback rating. Please use this space to provide more detail about your experience.

### 6. Contact Email
* **Contact Email**: If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address here. We'll keep your email address confidential.

### 7. Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone**: If you'd rather be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number here. We'll keep your phone number confidential.

### 8. Contact Other
* **Contact Other**: If none of the above options apply, please let us know how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., mail, email, phone, etc.).
