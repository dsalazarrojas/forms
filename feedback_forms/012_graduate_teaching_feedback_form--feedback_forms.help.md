# Graduate Teaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Graduate Teaching Feedback Form is designed to collect information from students about their learning experience. It is an essential tool for us to evaluate and improve our teaching and learning programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, and answer all the required questions.
2. If you are providing feedback as a student, please fill in your name and faculty name.
3. For each question, provide as much detail as possible, keeping it concise and clear.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your name, as a student.
  - Example: John Doe

- **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`, text, required: false): Fill in the name of the faculty member who taught you.
  - Example: Jane Smith

- **Semester** (`semester`, number, required: false): Specify which semester you are providing feedback for.
  - Example: 1

- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Provide your thoughts about the course.
  - Example: The course was well-structured and engaging.

- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Specify the date you took the course.
  - Example: 2022-02-01

- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Specify the time you took the course.
  - Example: 10:00 AM

- **Grade** (`grade`, number, required: false): Specify the grade you received in the course.
  - Example: 85

- **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction level with the course.
  - Options: Satisfied, Dissatisfied

- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Write any additional comments about the course.
  - Example: I would have liked more hands-on exercises.

- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Your email address (optional, but recommended).
  - Example: [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)

- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Your phone number (optional, but recommended).
  - Example: 123-456-7890

- **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you have submitted the form.
  - Options: Yes, No

- **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false): The date you submitted the form.
  - Automatically populated by the system.

- **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required: false): The time you submitted the form.
  - Automatically populated by the system.

- **Submitter ID** (`submitter_id`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you are the submitter.
  - Options: Yes, No

- **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required: false): If you are the submitter, fill in your name.
  - Example: John Doe

- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you submitted the form.
  - Options: Yes, No

- **Comments 2** (`comments2`, note, required: false): Write any additional comments about the course or your experience.
  - Example: I would like to see more resources available online.

- **Submitter ID 2** (`submitter_id_2`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you are the second submitter.
  - Options: Yes, No

- **Submitter 2** (`submitter2`, text, required: false): If you are the second submitter, fill in your name.
  - Example: Jane Doe

- **Submitted By 2** (`submitted_by_2`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you submitted the form as the second submitter.
  - Options: Yes, No

- **Submitted By 2 ID** (`submitted_by_2_id`, text, required: false): If you are the second submitter, fill in your ID.
  - Example: 123

- **Submitted By 2 By** (`submitted_by_2_by`, text, required: false): Fill in the name of the second submitter.
  - Example: Jane Doe

- **Submitted By 2** (`submitted_by_2`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you are the second submitter.
  - Options: Yes, No

- **Submitted By 2 By 2** (`submitted_by_2_by_2`, text, required: false): Fill in the name of the second submitter.
  - Example: John Doe

## Tips

* Keep your feedback honest and specific.
* Be clear and concise in your responses.
* Only provide feedback from your own experiences.
* Do not duplicate feedback.

Note: This guide is subject to change as we review and improve the form.
