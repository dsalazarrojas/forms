# lash_artist_skill_perception_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from lash artists about their skill level, experience, and work habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the answers that best describe your experience and work habits.
2. For each field, choose one option from the list of available choices.
3. If a field is not required, you can still provide your feedback, but it is not mandatory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your overall skill level?** (lash_artist_survey_1)
	+ This question helps us understand your self-assessment of your skill level as a lash artist.
	+ Please select High, Medium, or Low based on your expertise.
* **How often do you perform lash applications?** (lash_artist_survey_2)
	+ This question helps us understand your frequency of work.
	+ Please select Every day, Several times a week, or Rarely.
* **How many years have you been doing lash work?** (lash_artist_survey_3)
	+ This question helps us understand your level of experience.
	+ Please select Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, or More than 2 years.
* **What type of lash application technique do you use?** (lash_artist_survey_4)
	+ This question helps us understand your working style.
	+ Please select the type of technique you commonly use, such as Traditional lash curl, Hand drawn lash curl, or Machine lash.
* **Which type of product do you usually work with?** (lash_artist_survey_5)
	+ This question helps us understand the types of products you use.
	+ Please select Mega volume lashes or Individual lashes.
* **Do you have any formal education or training in lash art?** (lash_artist_survey_6)
	+ This question helps us understand your educational background.
	+ Please select Yes or No.
* **How many clients do you have per week?** (lash_artist_survey_7)
	+ This question helps us understand your workload.
	+ Please select Less than 10, 10-20, or More than 20.
* **Average client session duration (in minutes)?** (lash_artist_survey_8)
	+ This question helps us understand the time you spend with each client.
	+ Please enter a numerical value.
* **Do you have any certifications or diplomas?** (lash_artist_survey_9)
	+ This question helps us understand your credentials.
	+ Please select Yes or No.
* **Do you have any other hobbies?** (lash_artist_survey_10)
	+ This question helps us understand your interests outside of work.
	+ Please provide any other hobbies or activities you enjoy in your free time.
* **Do you have any other work experience in the beauty industry?** (lash_artist_survey_11)
	+ This question helps us understand your experience in related fields.
	+ Please select Yes or No.
* **How do you rate your overall satisfaction level?** (lash_artist_survey_12)
	+ This question helps us understand your job satisfaction.
	+ Please select High, Medium, or Low.
* **What is your average session price?** (lash_artist_survey_13)
	+ This question helps us understand your pricing.
	+ Please enter a numerical value.
* **Would you like to receive more feedback surveys?** (lash_artist_survey_14)
	+ This question helps us understand your interest in feedback.
	+ Please select Yes or No.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve our products and services.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the space provided (lash_artist_survey_10).
