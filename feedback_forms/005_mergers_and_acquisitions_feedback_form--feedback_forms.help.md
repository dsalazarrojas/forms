# Mergers And Acquisitions Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mergers And Acquisitions Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback and information about mergers and acquisitions from team members. This form is used to help the team track and analyze the progress of mergers and acquisitions, and to provide a platform for team members to express their thoughts and concerns. **Please note that this form is not for soliciting sensitive or confidential information.**

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Click on the "Submit" button**: This will bring you to the form page where you can start filling out the form.
2. **Select the relevant pages**: The form is divided into several pages. Each page will have a brief description or hint to guide you on what to fill in that page. Please read the description carefully before starting to fill in the form.
3. **Fill in the required fields**: Each field is marked as "required" or "optional". Please fill in the required fields as they will be necessary for the form to be completed and to ensure that you can submit the form.
4. **Submit your feedback**: Once you have filled in all the required fields, click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Merger Details (1)**: This is the first page of the form. Here, you can provide a brief description of the merger or acquisition.
* **Acquisition Cost (2)**: This field is used to record the financial impact of the merger or acquisition.
* **Financial Impact (3)**: This field is used to record any potential financial risks or benefits associated with the merger or acquisition.
* **Integration Status (4)**: Please select the current status of the integration process.
* **Integration Risks (5)**: This field is used to record any integration risks. Please select all relevant risks.
* **Communication Channels (6)**: This field is used to record all channels of communication used in the integration process. Please select all relevant channels.
* **Feedback (7)**: This field is a text box where you can provide any additional feedback about the merger or acquisition.
* **Email (8)**: This field is for your email address. Please enter your email address here if you want to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Phone (9)**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number here if you want to be contacted for further discussion.

Please note that all fields are optional except for **Integration Status (4)** and **Integration Risks (5)** and **Communication Channels (6)**, which are required fields.
