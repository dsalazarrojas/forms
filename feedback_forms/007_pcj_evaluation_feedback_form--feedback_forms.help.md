# PCJ Evaluation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PCJ Evaluation Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers or users about their experience with a PCJ evaluation. This form helps us identify areas that need improvement and gather suggestions to enhance the overall customer journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Feedback Score, which indicates your overall satisfaction level with the PCJ evaluation experience. Use the provided rating options (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor) to choose your score.
3. Provide more detailed Feedback Text to describe your experience and suggest areas for improvement.
4. Enter your E-mail and Phone number for communication purposes.
5. If applicable, select a Date and Time of your PCJ evaluation.
6. Add any additional Notes about your experience.
7. Optionally, provide Feedback Comments for further clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, required): Select your overall satisfaction level with the PCJ evaluation experience using the provided rating options (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, required): Describe your experience and suggest areas for improvement in this field.
* **E-mail** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of your PCJ evaluation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of your PCJ evaluation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Select one of the provided rating options (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor) to indicate your overall satisfaction level.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
