# Adult Interaction Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their interactions with adult individuals, such as colleagues, clients, or customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date when the interaction occurred.
2. Select the type of interaction that took place (e.g., Meeting, Phone Call, Email Exchange, etc.).
3. List the names of individuals involved in the interaction.
4. Briefly describe the purpose of the interaction.
5. Rate the overall quality of communication on a scale of 1-5.
6. Indicate whether the participants demonstrated active listening skills.
7. (Optional) Describe what aspects of the interaction were particularly effective.
8. (Optional) Identify areas where improvement could be made in future interactions.
9. (Optional) Indicate whether the purpose of the interaction was fully resolved, partially resolved, not resolved, or not applicable.
10. (Optional) Indicate whether follow-up action is required after this interaction.
11. Provide any additional comments or observations about the interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the interaction occurred.
* **Type of Interaction** (`interaction_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of interaction that took place (e.g., Meeting, Phone Call, Email Exchange, etc.).
* **Participants Involved** (`interaction_participants`, text, required): List the names of individuals involved in the interaction.
* **Purpose of Interaction** (`interaction_purpose`, text, required): Briefly describe the purpose of the interaction.
* **Communication Quality Rating** (`communication_quality_rating`, number, required): Rate the overall quality of communication on a scale of 1-5.
* **Active Listening Observed** (`active_listening_observed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the participants demonstrated active listening skills.
* **Communication Strengths** (`communication_strengths`, text, optional): Describe what aspects of the interaction were particularly effective.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Identify areas where improvement could be made in future interactions.
* **Resolution Achieved** (`resolution_achieved`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the purpose of the interaction was fully resolved, partially resolved, not resolved, or not applicable.
* **Follow Up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether follow-up action is required after this interaction.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other observations or feedback about the interaction.
