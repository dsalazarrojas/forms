# Sports Facilities Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Facilities Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with sports facilities. This feedback will be used to help facility managers and maintenance teams improve the overall quality of the facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "sports_facilities_feedback_form" link to access the form.
2.  Review the form to ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3.  Answer each question as honestly and thoroughly as possible to provide valuable feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sports_facilities_feedback_form_label** (`sports_facilities_feedback_form`, note, required: false): This field is a note field where you can provide general comments or suggestions about the sports facility. Please be as detailed as possible to help us improve the facility.
* **feedback_score_label** (`feedback_score`, number, required: false): This field allows you to rate your overall experience with the sports facility. Please rate on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **comment_label** (`comment`, text, required: false): This field is a text area where you can provide more detailed comments or feedback about your experience with the sports facility. Please be as specific as possible to help us understand your concerns or suggestions.

## Tips

* Please only submit this form once per facility, to help us avoid duplicate submissions.
* Be as honest and thorough as possible when providing feedback, as this will help us make data-driven decisions to improve the facilities.
* If you have any suggestions or concerns that are not addressed by this form, please contact us directly for further assistance.
