# Student Support Resource Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students on the support resources provided by the institution. This feedback is used to improve the support services and make them more effective for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your feedback. You can choose from two options for certain fields, and you can also provide additional comments or suggestions in the "General Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Feedback (General feedback)** (`general_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for providing general feedback on your experience with the support resources. Please use this field to describe your overall experience and any comments or suggestions you have.
* **Feedback on services (feedback_on_services)** (`feedback_on_services`, text, required: false): This field is for providing feedback on specific services offered by the institution. Please select all services that you have used and describe your feedback on each one.
* **Support Service (Support Service)** (`support_service`, select_one, required: true): This field is for indicating whether you have received support from the institution's support services. Please select "Yes" if you have received support, and "No" if you have not.
* **Email address (Email address)** (`email_address`, email, required: false): This field is for providing your email address. This will help us contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
* **Phone number (Phone number)** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): This field is for providing your phone number. This will help us contact you if we need more information or clarification on your feedback.
* **Time of visit (Time of visit)** (`time_of_visit`, date, required: false): This field is for selecting the date of your visit to the institution. Please select the date of your visit to provide context to your feedback.
* **Time of visit time (Time of visit time)** (`time_of_visit_time`, time, required: false): This field is for selecting the time of your visit. Please select the time of your visit to provide context to your feedback.
* **Support area (Support area)** (`support_area`, select_one, required: false): This field is for indicating which support area you received help with. Please select "Yes" if you received support in this area, and "No" if you did not.
* **Staff member (Staff member)** (`staff_member`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for indicating which staff members you interacted with. Please select all staff members who provided support to you.
* **Other Feedback (Other feedback)** (`other_feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for providing any additional comments or suggestions you have.
