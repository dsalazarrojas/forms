# Wildlife Safari Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the Wildlife Safari Feedback form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the first question, "First Safari Experience", please describe your initial experience at the Wildlife Safari.
3. For the "Tour Operator" question, please provide the name of the tour operator who guided you.
4. For "Guide Performance", "Safety Procedures", and "Safety Procedures 2", please rate your experience on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
5. For "Guide Attitude", please describe the attitude of your guide.
6. For "Tour Guide ID" and "Tour Guide Name", please choose from the dropdown menu or enter the name of the guide who escorted you.
7. Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Safari Experience** (`first_safari_experience`, text, required): Describe your initial experience at the Wildlife Safari.
* **Tour Operator** (`tour_operator`, text, required): Provide the name of the tour operator who guided you.
* **Guide Performance** (`guide_performance`, number, required): Rate your experience on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Safety Procedures** (`safety_procedures`, number, required): Rate your experience on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Guide Attitude** (`guide_attitude`, text, required): Describe the attitude of your guide.
* **Safety Procedures 2** (`safety_procedures_2`, text, required): Provide feedback on safety procedures during the safari.
* **Tour Guide ID** (`tour_guide_id`, select_one, required): Choose the ID of the tour guide from the dropdown menu.
* **Tour Guide Name** (`tour_guide_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tour guide who escorted you.

## Tips
* Please provide specific examples or details when providing feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or recommendations, please include them in the "Safety Procedures 2" field.
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
