# Art Exhibition Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Exhibition Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from visitors to the exhibition, allowing us to improve their experience and create better future exhibitions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is invaluable to us.
2. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Improvement Suggestion" field.
3. If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address in the "Contact For Follow-up" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Visitor Context --** (`visitor_context_header`): This section is a brief introduction to the feedback form. Please feel free to ignore it.
* **Date of Your Visit** (`visit_date`): This field asks for the date of your visit to the exhibition.
* **Is this your first visit to our gallery?** (`frequent_visitor`): This question helps us understand your familiarity with our gallery. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **-- Exhibition Experience --** (`art_experience_header`): This section is a brief introduction to the exhibition experience.
* **Which artwork or artist resonated with you the most?** (`favorite_piece_or_artist`): Please tell us about the artwork that had the greatest impact on you.
* **Rate the layout and flow of the exhibition** (`exhibition_layout_rating`): Use the scale to rate how well the exhibition was organized and flowed.
* **How effective was the lighting and presentation of artworks?** (`lighting_and_presentation`): Rate how well the artworks were showcased in the exhibition space.
* **How helpful were the artwork descriptions and labels?** (`informative_labels_rating`): Choose how helpful you found the information provided about the artworks.
* **-- Atmosphere and Service --** (`atmosphere_and_service_header`): This section is a brief introduction to the atmosphere and service of the exhibition.
* **How helpful was the gallery staff?** (`gallery_staff_helpfulness`): Rate how helpful you found the staff during your visit.
* **Rate the overall ambiance of the exhibition space** (`overall_ambiance_rating`): Use the scale to rate the atmosphere of the exhibition space.
* **If there was an entry fee, how did you find the value for money?** (`pricing_perception`): If there was a fee, choose how you felt about the value you received for the money.
* **-- Impact and Suggestions --** (`impact_and_suggestions_header`): This section is a brief introduction to the impact and suggestions of the exhibition.
* **How did the exhibition make you feel?** (`emotional_impact_description`): Share your emotional response to the exhibition.
* **What is one thing we could improve for future exhibitions?** (`improvement_suggestion`): Please provide one suggestion for how we can improve future exhibitions.
* **Would you recommend this exhibition to others?** (`would_recommend_exhibition`): Choose how likely you are to recommend the exhibition.
* **What topics or themes would you like to see in future exhibitions?** (`future_topics_interest`): Share your interest in potential future exhibition topics.
* **May we contact you regarding your feedback?** (`contact_for_followup`): If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address.
