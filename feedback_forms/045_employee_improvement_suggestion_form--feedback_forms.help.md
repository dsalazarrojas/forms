# Employee Improvement Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and document employee suggestions, ideas, and feedback to aid management in improving work processes and addressing employee needs. To ensure relevance, please submit only new suggestions, and avoid duplicating ideas that have already been suggested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name in the "name" field.
2. Write a brief description of your idea or feedback in the "description" field.
3. Offer a specific suggestion for improvement in the "suggestion" field.
4. If applicable, indicate whether you think the suggested improvement would be beneficial (category "Yes") or not (category "No").
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field, so we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
6. If you prefer to be contacted by phone, provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or notes, please include them in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`employee_improvement_suggestion`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of your idea or feedback.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **description** (`description`, text, required: false): Describe your idea or feedback in detail.
* **suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, required: false): Offer a specific suggestion for improvement.
* **category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate whether you think the suggested improvement would be beneficial (category "Yes") or not (category "No").
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes related to your suggestion.
