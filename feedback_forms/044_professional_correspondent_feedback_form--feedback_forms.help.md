# Professional Correspondent Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from correspondents on their experience with our team. Your input is valuable in helping us understand how we can improve our services. Please answer the questions honestly, and feel free to share any comments or suggestions you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide your feedback on your experience with our team. This is your opportunity to share any positive or negative comments, suggestions, or concerns you may have.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name or affiliation.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or team you are part of.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select multiple, optional): Select the level of your job role: Senior, Junior, Mid-level, or Intern.
* **Frequency of contact** (`frequency_of_contact`, select one, optional): Indicate how often you contact us: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Never.
* **Contact method** (`contact_method`, select one, optional): Choose how you usually contact us: Email, Phone, In-person, Video call, or None.
* **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select multiple, optional): Rate your satisfaction with your job: Satisfied, Unsatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Feedback frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select multiple, optional): Indicate how often you provide feedback: Often, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Type of feedback** (`feedback_type`, select multiple, optional): Choose the type of feedback you provide: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select one, optional): Indicate if you want to be followed up: 'True', 'False', or Maybe.
* **Follow-up timeframe** (`follow_up_timeframe`, select multiple, optional): Choose the timeframe for follow-up: Immediately, Within a day or two, Within a week or two, Within a month or so, or Never.
* **Follow-up method** (`follow_up_method`, select one, optional): Choose the method for follow-up: Phone, Email, In-person, Video call, or None.
* **Follow-up comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments for follow-up.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with a number (e.g., 1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of your feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of your feedback.
* **Job performance** (`job_performance`, select multiple, optional): Rate your job performance: Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Neutral.
* **Job performance comments** (`job_performance_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments on your job performance.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields before submitting your feedback.
* Use the select multiple options where applicable.
* You can add any additional comments or suggestions in the Comments field.
* If you want to be followed up, make sure to select the correct follow-up method and timeframe.
* You can rate your overall satisfaction with a number.
