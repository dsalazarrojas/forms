# Hospitality AI Chatbot Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather your feedback on your experience with our chatbot. By completing this form, you will help us improve and refine our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question according to your experience with our chatbot.
2. Select one or multiple options for each field as instructed.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate our service?** (`survey_page`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with our chatbot on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **How often have you used our chatbot in the past?** (`hospitality_service_usage`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you have used our chatbot from the options: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often.
* **How was your overall experience with our chatbot?** (`user_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with our chatbot on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Which of the following features would you like to see added to our chatbot?** (`chatbot_feature`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple features you'd like us to add to our chatbot from the options: Chatbot Response, Contextualization, Customization, Integration with third-party services.
* **How did you interact with our chatbot?** (`interaction_type`, select_one, optional): Choose how you interacted with our chatbot from the options: Text, Voice, Visual.
* **What type of feedback do you have for our chatbot?** (`feedback_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple feedback types you'd like to provide from the options: Technical, Functional, User Experience.
* **How would you rate our chatbot?** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate our chatbot on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?** (`comment`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our chatbot.
* **How would you like to get in touch with us?** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you'd like to get in touch with us from the options: Phone, Email, Chat.
* **Your email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to get in touch with us.
* **Your name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you'd like to get in touch with us.
* **Company name (if applicable)** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Company website (if applicable)** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company website if applicable.
