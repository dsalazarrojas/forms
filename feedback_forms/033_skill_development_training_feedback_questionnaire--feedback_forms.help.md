# Skill Development Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the effectiveness, quality, and overall experience of a training session provided by our team. Your feedback will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Follow the step-by-step guide above to complete the form.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or ratings as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall training experience?** (`training_experience`): This field asks about your overall experience with the training session. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
	+ **Yes**: You had a positive experience with the training session.
	+ **No**: You had a negative experience with the training session.
* **How effective was the training?** (`training_effectiveness`): This field asks about the effectiveness of the training session. Please select all options that best describe your experience.
	+ **Yes**: The training was effective.
	+ **No**: The training was not effective.
* **How would you rate the session quality?** (`session_quality`): This field asks you to rate the quality of the session on a scale of 1-10.
* **What suggestions would you give for improvement?** (`future_suggestions`): This field is for any suggestions you have for improving the training session.
* **Any other comments?** (`participant_comments`): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about the training session.
* **Email address** (`email_address`): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`): This field is optional, but if you would like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or business card.
* **Participant organization** (`participant_organization`): If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of your organization.
* **Session date** (`session_date`): If you are unsure of the session date, please ask the trainer or check your calendar.
* **Session time** (`session_time`): If you are unsure of the session time, please ask the trainer or check your calendar.
* **Session location** (`session_location`): If you are unsure of the session location, please ask the trainer or check the session materials.
* **Trainer name** (`trainer_name`): Please enter the name of the trainer who led the session.
* **Training duration** (`training_duration`): If you are unsure of the training duration, please ask the trainer or check the session materials.
* **Number of hours** (`training_hours`): If you are unsure of the training duration, please ask the trainer or check the session materials.
* **Training start date** (`training_date`): This is the date the training started.
* **Participant rating** (`participant_rating`): Please rate your overall experience with the training session on a scale of 1-10.
* **Session start time** (`session_start_time`): If you are unsure of the session start time, please ask the trainer or check the session materials.
* **Session duration** (`session_duration`): If you are unsure of the session duration, please ask the trainer or check the session materials.
* **Participant notes** (`participant_notes`): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about the training session.
* **Session notes** (`session_notes`): This field is for any additional comments or thoughts the trainer had about the session.
* **Has feedback given?** (`feedback_given`): Please select "Yes" if you have given feedback on this session.
* **Has feedback received?** (`feedback_received`): Please select "Yes" if you have received feedback on this session.
* **Feedback type** (`feedback_type`): Please select "Yes" if the feedback you received was helpful.
* **Participant survey** (`participant_survey`): This field is for any additional feedback you have on the participant survey.
