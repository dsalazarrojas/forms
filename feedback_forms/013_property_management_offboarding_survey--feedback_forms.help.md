# Property Management Offboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Management Offboarding Survey is designed to collect feedback from tenants about their offboarding experience with a property management company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current rent status from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as it appears on the lease agreement.
3. Provide the property's address.
4. Enter the start date of your lease.
5. Enter the end date of your lease, if applicable.
6. Choose the reason(s) you left the property from the options provided.
7. If you wish to provide any additional comments, please use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current rent status** (select_one): Please select the current status of your rent payments. This is used to understand your payment history with the property management company.
	+ Rent Paid: You are currently paying rent on time.
	+ Rent Late: You are paying rent late, but not significantly late.
	+ Rent Negotiable: You and the property management company have agreed to negotiate the terms of your rent payments.
	+ Rent Not Paid: You are not paying rent due to financial difficulties or other reasons.
* **What is your name** (text): Please enter your full name as it appears on the lease agreement.
* **What is the property address** (text): Please provide the address of the property you were renting from.
* **What date did you start your lease** (date): Enter the date when your lease began.
* **What date did you end your lease** (date): If you are no longer renting the property, enter the date when your lease ended.
* **What reason did you leave the property** (select_multiple): Choose the reason(s) you left the property from the options provided.
	+ Job Change: You changed jobs and moved to a new location.
	+ Financial Reasons: You were unable to afford the rent or property taxes.
	+ Relationship Issues: You had conflicts with the property management company or other tenants.
	+ Health Issues: Your health or medical condition required you to move to a new location.
	+ Other: Select this option if none of the above reasons apply.
* **Any additional comments** (note): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.
* **What is the property manager's name** (text): Please provide the name of the property manager you interacted with during your tenancy.
