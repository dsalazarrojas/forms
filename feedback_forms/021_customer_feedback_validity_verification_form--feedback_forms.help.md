# Customer Feedback Validity Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our product or service. It helps us verify the authenticity of customer feedback and ensures that we are aware of any issues or concerns that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your genuine feedback in the "rating_comment" field.
3. Ensure that the "date_submitted" and "time_submitted" fields are filled in correctly, as they will help us track when and how you submitted your feedback.
4. Enter your "customer_name" and "customer_phone" fields, if you wish to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up.
5. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Feedback Form (`customer_feedback_form`)
* This field is where you can enter your feedback about your experience with our product or service. Be as detailed and honest as possible.
### 2. Email (`email`)
* Enter your email address to help us verify your identity and ensure that you have access to your email account.
### 3. Customer Rating (`customer_rating`)
* Rate your experience with our product or service on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
### 4. Rating Value (`rating_value`)
* Enter the actual value of your rating (e.g., 4 out of 5).
### 5. Rating Comment (`rating_comment`)
* Enter your reasons for your rating in the text field below.
### 6. Date Submitted (`date_submitted`)
* Enter the date you submitted your feedback (mm/dd/yyyy).
### 7. Time Submitted (`time_submitted`)
* Enter the time you submitted your feedback (hh:mm am/pm).
### 8. Customer Name (`customer_name`)
* Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license).
### 9. Customer Phone (`customer_phone`)
* Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure that we can verify the validity of your feedback.
* If you have any issues or concerns that you would like to discuss, you can contact us directly.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our product or service.
