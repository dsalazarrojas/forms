# Product Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our products and services. Your responses will help us understand your perceptions and satisfaction, as well as identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, one at a time.
2. For "Products and Services Used", select all the products you have used from our company.
3. For "Other Products/Services Used", you can enter any other products or services you have used that are not listed.
4. For questions with the "Very good", "Good", "Neutral", "Bad", and "Very bad" options, select the one that best represents your opinion.
5. For "Overall Experience" and "Your Satisfaction", please enter your thoughts and comments in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Long Have You Been With Us**: This is the first question on the form. Please enter the length of time you have been using our products or services. This will help us understand how long you have had experience with our company.
* **Using our products/services**: This question is asking about your experience with our products and services. Please select the length of time you have been using them.
* **How do you perceive our competitors**: This question is asking about your perception of our competitors. Please select how you perceive them in relation to us.
	+ **Very good**: Our competitors are performing exceptionally well.
	+ **Good**: Our competitors are performing well.
	+ **Neutral**: Our competitors are performing similarly to us.
	+ **Bad**: Our competitors are performing poorly.
	+ **Very bad**: Our competitors are performing very poorly.
* **Products and services impression**: This question is asking about your impression of our products and services. Please select all that apply.
	+ **Very good**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Neutral**
	+ **Bad**
	+ **Very bad**
* **Your satisfaction**: This question is asking about your satisfaction with our products and services. Please select the level of satisfaction you feel.
	+ **Very high**
	+ **High**
	+ **Low**
	+ **Very low**
* **Overall experience**: Please enter your thoughts and comments about your overall experience with our company.
* **Other comments**: Please enter any other comments or suggestions you have about our products and services.
* **Products and services used**: This question is asking about the products and services you have used from our company. Please select all that apply.
* **Other products/services used**: This question is asking about any other products or services you have used from other companies. Please enter any that are not listed.
* **How would you rate**: This question is asking about how you would rate our products and services. Please select the level of satisfaction you feel.
	+ **Very good**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Neutral**
	+ **Bad**
	+ **Very bad**
