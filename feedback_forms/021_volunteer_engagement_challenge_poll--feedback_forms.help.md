# Volunteer Engagement Challenge Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Volunteer Engagement Challenge Poll" form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from volunteers regarding their current challenges and experiences with our organization's programs. This information helps us understand areas where we can improve and better support our volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the questions and understand the context.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the relevant option that best describes your situation.
4. For text-based questions, provide detailed and clear responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Engagement Challenge Poll 1 (#)** (`Volunteer Engagement Challenge Poll 1`, Number, Required: false): This is the main prompt for the form, and you are not required to provide an answer for this field.
* **Suggestions (#)** (`Suggestions`, Text, Required: false): Please provide any suggestions or ideas you may have regarding how we can improve our volunteer engagement challenges. 
* **Experience (#)** (`Experience`, Text, Required: false): Please share your past experiences with our organization's programs, including any notable successes or challenges you've faced.
* **Programs (#)** (`Programs`, Select Multiple, Required: false): Check all the programs that you have participated in or are currently participating in. This will help us understand which programs are working well for you and which ones need improvement.
* **Other Programs (#)** (`Other Programs`, Text, Required: false): If you have participated in programs not listed in the previous field, please mention them here. This is an optional field for providing additional information about programs not included in the previous list.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide detailed answers.
* If you're unsure about any question, try your best to answer based on your general understanding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us directly.
