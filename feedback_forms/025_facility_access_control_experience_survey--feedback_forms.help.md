# Facility Access Control Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our facility access control system. We would like you to fill out this form if you have recently used the system. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make the system more user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields (marked with \*).
2. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
3. Enter your Email in the field labeled "Email".
4. Rate your experience with the facility access control system in the field labeled "Facility Access Control" (1-5).
5. Provide any Comments you may have about your experience in the field labeled "Comments".
6. If you have additional comments, you can enter them in the field labeled "Additional Comments".
7. If you want to assign this feedback to someone, enter their name in the field labeled "Assigned To".
8. If you have any comments for the assigned user, enter them in the field labeled "Comments On Assigned User".
9. Finally, enter your name in the field labeled "Submitted By".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (*)
* A required field for your first name.
* Please enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* Example: John
### Email (*)
* A required field for your email address.
* Please enter a valid email address.
* Example: johndoe@example.com
### Facility Access Control (*)
* A required field to rate your experience.
* Please rate your experience from 1 to 5, with 1 being Very Poor and 5 being Very Good.
* Example: 4
### Comments
* An optional field for any comments you may have about your experience.
* You can enter anything you like here, but please keep it brief and concise.
* Example: The system was easy to use, but the access control process could be improved.
### Additional Comments
* An optional field for any additional comments you may have.
* You can enter anything you like here, but please keep it brief and concise.
* Example: The new feature was well-implemented and improved my workflow significantly.
### Assigned To
* An optional field to assign this feedback to someone.
* Please enter the name of the person you want to assign this feedback to.
* Example: John Doe
### Comments On Assigned User
* An optional field for any comments for the assigned user.
* Please keep them brief and concise.
* Example: Thanks for the feedback, we'll look into it.
### Facility Access Control Experience
* A required field to select your experience.
* Please select one of the following options:
	+ Very Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Average
	+ Somewhat Poor
	+ Very Poor
	+ No Answer
* Example: Very Good
### Additional Comments
* An optional field for any additional comments.
* You can enter anything you like here, but please keep it brief and concise.
* Example: The system was easy to use, but the access control process could be improved.
### Assigned User
* An optional field to assign this feedback to someone.
* Please enter the name of the person you want to assign this feedback to.
* Example: John Doe
### Comments On Assigned User
* An optional field for any comments for the assigned user.
* Please keep them brief and concise.
* Example: Thanks for the feedback, we'll look into it.
### Submitted By
* An optional field to indicate who submitted the feedback.
* Please enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* Example: John Doe
