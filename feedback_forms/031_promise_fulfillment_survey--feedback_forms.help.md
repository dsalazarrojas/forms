# Promise Fulfillment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to gather your feedback on your experience with our organization. It should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your relationship with our organization (Client, Colleague, Customer, or Other) in question 1.
2. For each question, rate how well our organization met its promise (Score 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high). If you're not satisfied, please provide your comments in the comment box below each question.
3. Fill out your contact information at the bottom of the form, including your name, email address, and job title if applicable.
4. If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, please provide your preferred contact method (phone, email, or mail).
