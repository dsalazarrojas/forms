# Hand Sanitizer Stand Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your experience with our hand sanitizer stand and gather feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Rate your overall usage of the hand sanitizer stand.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the hand sanitizer stand.
3. Share any improvement suggestions you may have for the hand sanitizer stand.
4. Enter your email address (if you'd like us to follow up with you).
5. Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Usage** (`usage_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall usage of the hand sanitizer stand on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the hand sanitizer stand on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Improvement Suggestion** (`improvement_suggestion`, note, required: false): Share any thoughts on how we can improve the hand sanitizer stand.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can about your experience with the hand sanitizer stand.
* If you have any specific concerns or issues, please let us know so we can address them.
