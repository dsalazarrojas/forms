# Cafeteria Menu Improvement Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the cafeteria menu and services to help improve the quality and variety of food options available to students, staff, and faculty. Your responses will contribute to creating a better dining experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Provide your basic information, such as your role (Student, Staff Member, or Faculty) in the cafeteria.
2.  Indicate how often you eat in the cafeteria.
3.  Choose your preferred food types (e.g., vegetarian options, international cuisine, etc.).
4.  Indicate if you have any dietary restrictions.
5.  Rate the current menu's variety, food quality, and price-to-value ratio.
6.  Suggest menu additions, removals, and overall improvements.
7.  Express your interest in using an online ordering system and suggest additional features.
8.  Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **-- Your Information --** (Page 1)
    +   This page is for your basic information, including your role in the cafeteria.
*   **What is your role?** (Page 2)
    +   Please select one of the following options: Student, Staff Member, or Faculty.
    +   This question helps us understand who is responding to the poll.
*   **Department or School** (Page 3)
    +   If you're a student, staff member, or faculty member, please enter your department or school name.
*   **How often do you eat in the cafeteria?** (Page 4)
    +   Choose your eating frequency: Daily, 3-4 times per week, 1-2 times per week, Rarely, or Never.
*   **-- Food Preferences --** (Page 5)
    +   This page is for your food preferences.
*   **What types of food do you prefer? (select all that apply)** (Page 6)
    +   Choose all the food types that you like: vegetarian options, international cuisine, etc.
*   **Do you have dietary restrictions?** (Page 7)
    +   Indicate if you have any dietary restrictions: Yes or No.
*   **Please specify your dietary restrictions** (Page 8)
    +   If you have dietary restrictions, please describe them in detail.
*   **-- Current Menu Feedback --** (Page 9)
    +   This page is for your feedback on the current menu.
*   **How would you rate the variety of menu options?** (Page 10)
    +   Choose your rating: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
*   **How would you rate the quality of food?** (Page 11)
    +   Rate the quality of food: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
*   **How would you rate the price-to-value ratio?** (Page 12)
    +   Rate the price-to-value ratio: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Very Poor.
*   **-- Suggestions for Improvement --** (Page 13)
    +   This page is for your suggestions for improvement.
*   **What food items would you like to see added to the menu?** (Page 14)
    +   Suggest new food items that you'd like to see on the menu.
*   **What food items would you like to see removed from the menu?** (Page 15)
    +   Suggest food items that you'd like to see removed from the menu.
*   **What overall improvements would enhance your dining experience?** (Page 16)
    +   Suggest any overall improvements that would make your dining experience better.
*   **Should we improve allergen and nutritional information?** (Page 17)
    +   Indicate if you think we should improve allergen and nutritional information: Yes, Somewhat important, or Not important.
*   **Would you use an online ordering system?** (Page 18)
    +   Indicate your interest in using an online ordering system: Yes, Very interested, Maybe, or No.
*   **Additional Comments** (Page 19)
    +   Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve the cafeteria menu and services.
*   If you have any dietary restrictions, please be specific about your needs.
*   Your responses will be kept confidential and used to create a better dining experience for everyone.
