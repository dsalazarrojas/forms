# Venue Customer Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience at a venue. This form helps us understand their satisfaction with various aspects of their visit, such as staff helpfulness, facilities, and overall experience. The information collected will be used to improve our services and make future events more enjoyable for all customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant options for your visit from the "Event Visit" dropdown menu.
2. Choose whether you had a venue manager available during your visit.
3. Select all the events you attended during your visit.
4. Rate your overall experience using the numbered scale.
5. Rate the helpfulness of staff, facilities, and other aspects of your visit.
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback.
7. If you have any contact information or event details, please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Visit**: This dropdown menu allows you to select the events you attended during your visit. Please choose all that apply.
	+ Select one or more options from the dropdown menu.
* **Venue Manager Meeting**: This option is asking whether a venue manager was available during your visit.
	+ Select "Yes" if a venue manager was available, "No" otherwise.
* **Event Details**: This field allows you to provide information about the events you attended.
	+ Enter all the events you attended.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience using the numbered scale.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Staff Helpfulness**: Rate the helpfulness of the staff during your visit.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Facilities**: Rate the condition and availability of facilities during your visit.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Food and Beverage**: Rate the quality of food and beverage services during your visit.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Venue Atmosphere**: Rate the atmosphere of the venue.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Staff Knowledge**: Rate the knowledge of the staff about the events and venue.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness of the staff.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Staff Punctuality**: Rate the punctuality of the staff.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Communication**: Rate the communication of the staff.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Value For Money**: Rate the value for money of your experience.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your visit.
	+ Select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
	+ Enter your thoughts or suggestions.
* **Contact Info**: Provide any contact information you'd like to share.
	+ Enter your email address or phone number.
* **Event Manager Name**: Provide the name of the event manager (if applicable).
	+ Enter the name.
* **Event Date**: Provide the date of the event.
	+ Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time**: Provide the time of the event.
	+ Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Event Location**: Provide the location of the event.
	+ Enter the venue or location.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
	+ Enter your thoughts or suggestions.
* **Follow Up Email**: Provide your email address for follow-up purposes.
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Follow Up Phone**: Provide your phone number for follow-up purposes.
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Follow Up Date**: Provide the date you'd like to be followed up on.
	+ Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Follow Up Time**: Provide the time you'd like to be followed up on.
	+ Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
