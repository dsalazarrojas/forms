# Pre Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Experience Feedback Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from participants after an event. This feedback is essential for event organizers to understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing them to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible. Each question is designed to help us understand your experience and preferences. You can submit your responses at your own pace, and you can skip questions that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **How did you hear about this event?** (`participant_how_heard`, text, required: false): This question helps us understand how people learn about our events. Please respond with the channel that brought you here (e.g., social media, email, friend, etc.).
* **How would you rate this event?** (`participant_rating`, number, required: false): Rate from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. This helps us gauge your overall satisfaction.
* **What was your favorite part of the event?** (`participant_favorite_part`, text, required: false): Share with us what made your experience stand out. Was it a particular speaker, activity, or aspect of the event?
* **How would you rate the event organization?** (`participant_event_rating`, number, required: false): Again, rate from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. This helps us evaluate our event planning and execution.
* **What is your background in relation to this field/event?** (`participant_background`, text, required: false): Share a brief overview of your experience and expertise in the field or related events.
* **How long have you been working/practicing in this field?** (`participant_experience_length`, number, required: false): This question helps us understand your level of experience and expertise.
* **What motivated you to participate in this event?** (`participant_motive`, text, required: false): Respond with reasons such as interest in the topic, recommendation, or personal goals.
* **Do you have any experience in events similar to this one?** (`participant_experience`, select_one, required: false):
  - 'Yes', 'No': Answer either Yes or No.
* **If you don’t have experience in events similar to this one, can you describe your experience?** (`participant_experience_other`, text, required: false): If you selected "No" for the previous question, please share a brief description of your relevant experience.
* **What were your expectations for this event?** (`participant_expectations`, select_multiple, required: false):
  - 'Yes', 'No': Answer either Yes or No for each option.
* **If you don't have any expectations, please describe why?** (`participant_expectations_other`, text, required: false): If you selected "No" for the previous question, please explain.
* **How were your expectations met?** (`participant_expectations_met`, text, required: false): Share how your expectations were met or not met.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`participant_suggestion`, text, required: false): This is your chance to share ideas on how we could make future events better.
* **What was your background in relation to the event organization?** (`event_organizer_background`, text, required: false): Share any relevant experience you have with event organizers or planning.
* **Is there anything you'd like to add or comment about your experience?** (`participant_additional_info`, text, required: false): This is your space to share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
