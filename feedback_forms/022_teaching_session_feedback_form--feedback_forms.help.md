# Teaching Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students after a teaching session. It helps trainers evaluate the effectiveness of the session and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Student Name.
2. Enter the Session Date and Session Time.
3. Provide the Session Duration.
4. Rate the Overall Rating of the session (1-5).
5. Type in your Feedback about the session.
6. Enter the Trainer Name.
7. If you have any Suggestions, type them in the note field.
8. If you have any Comments, type them in the Comments field (optional).
9. If you have any Additional Comments, type them in the Additional Comments field (optional).
10. Enter your Email address.
11. If you have a Phone number, type it in the Phone field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a student.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the session in the format DD/MM/YY or MM/YY, e.g., 12/02/2022 or 02/22.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the session in the format 12:00 or 12:00 PM.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the total duration of the session in minutes.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the session from 1 to 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide detailed feedback about the session.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, required): Type any suggestions you have for future sessions.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the session.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
