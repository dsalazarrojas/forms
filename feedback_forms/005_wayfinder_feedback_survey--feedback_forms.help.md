# Wayfinder Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their navigation experience with Wayfinder. The purpose of this survey is to help us understand how well we've met your navigation needs, and to gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please take a few minutes to answer each section carefully.
2. Enter your feedback in the designated fields. If a field is not required, feel free to skip it.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Navigation** (`text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can describe any issues you faced while navigating our site or provide any general feedback about your experience.
* **Signage Clarity** (`number`, required: false): Please rate how clear our signage is on a scale of 1-5. The more stars you select, the clearer you think our signage is.
* **Wayfinding Experience** (`text`, required: false): Describe your experience in finding your way through our site or service.
* **Suggestions** (`text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them here.
* **Comments** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or observations you have about your experience.
* **Contact Information** (`text`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please enter your contact information.
* **Submit** (`select multiple`, required: true): How would you rate your overall experience with Wayfinder? (Select one of the following: Very Good, Good, Bad)

Note: Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and your responses will help us improve our services!
