# Restaurant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience at a restaurant.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the restaurant you are providing feedback for.
2. Rate your overall experience at the restaurant (1-5).
3. Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about your visit.
4. If you have an email address, please enter it so we can contact you for follow-up.
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. If you have a specific date and time of your visit, please enter it.
7. Select the category that best describes your feedback (Food, Service, or Cleanliness).
8. Please select who you would like to be assigned to (either chatjimmy prophets or Other).
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restaurant Name**: Enter the name of the restaurant you are providing feedback for. Make sure it matches the name on the menu board or your receipt.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall experience at the restaurant. The rating is on a scale of 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about your visit. Try to be as specific as possible about what you liked or disliked.
* **Feedback**: This is an optional field where you can provide more detailed feedback about your experience.
* **Email**: If you have an email address, please enter it so we can contact you for follow-up. This is required to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone**: If you have a phone number, you can enter it. This is optional.
* **Date**: If you remember the date and time of your visit, please enter it. This will help us track your visit and provide more accurate feedback.
* **Time**: If you know the time of your visit, please enter it. Note that this field is not the same as the Date field.
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes your feedback. Please choose from Food, Service, or Cleanliness.
* **Assigned To**: Please select who you would like to be assigned to for your feedback. If you want to speak to the owner or manager, choose chatjimmy prophets. Otherwise, choose Other.

# Tips

* Please be as honest and specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Avoid using profanity or negative language.
* If you have any photos or videos that can support your feedback, please attach them to your submission.
* We will review all feedback and use it to improve the restaurant's services.
