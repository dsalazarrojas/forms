# ARFID Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ARFID Feedback Survey is designed to gather insights from respondents about their experiences with your service or product. This form helps you to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance your service or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Respondent Information**: If you'd like to share your name and email (optional), please fill in your name and email address.
2. Choose the category that best describes your feedback.
3. Select your overall satisfaction level with our service or product.
4. Rate your experience with our service or product.
5. Indicate how often you use our service or product.
6. Tell us how likely you are to recommend our service or product.
7. Share any positive aspects of our service or product (if applicable).
8. Suggest areas for improvement.
9. If you have any feature requests, let us know.
10. If you'd like to share any complaints or negative experiences, please do so in the "Complaint Details" section.
11. If you've interacted with our support, rate your experience.
12. Share your perception of the value you're getting from our service or product.
13. If you wish to remain anonymous, please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.

* **Name**: Enter your name, if you'd like to share it. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address, if you'd like to share it. (Optional)
* **Feedback Category**: Choose the category that best describes your feedback.
	+ Product Quality

* **Frequency of Use**: Indicate how often you use our service or product.
	+ Daily

* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Tell us how likely you are to recommend our service or product.
	+ Very Likely

* **Positive Aspects**: Share any positive aspects of our service or product.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Suggest areas that could be improved.

* **Support Interaction Rating**: If you've interacted with our support, rate your experience.
	+ Excellent

* **Value Perception**: Share your perception of the value you're getting from our service or product.
