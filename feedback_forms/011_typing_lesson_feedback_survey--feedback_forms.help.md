# Typing Lesson Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from instructors on the effectiveness and satisfaction of their delivered typing lessons. Your input will help us improve the quality of our lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review each question and provide an honest answer to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, or enter a brief text response if required.
3. Ensure you fill out all applicable questions, as your feedback is invaluable to us.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey the lesson effectiveness**: Select "Yes" if you believe the lesson was effective in teaching typing skills, and "No" otherwise. This will help us gauge the overall effectiveness of the lesson.
* **Lesson content**: Rate how engaging and relevant the lesson content was for you. Choose "Yes" if you found the content valuable, and "No" if you didn't.
* **Lesson difficulty**: Enter the number of minutes you felt the lesson should last. This will help us adjust the lesson duration for future sessions.
* **Instructor support**: Rate how supportive and helpful the instructor was during the lesson. Choose "Yes" if you received good support, and "No" if you didn't.
* **Additional comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about the lesson. This is your chance to provide more detailed feedback.
* **What is the purpose of this lesson?**: Select "Yes" if you know the purpose of the lesson, and "No" otherwise. This will help us ensure that our lesson objectives are clear.
* **How much time did you feel this survey took?**: Enter the number of minutes you spent on the survey. This will help us gauge the survey's time efficiency.
* **How did you feel about this lesson?**: Rate how satisfied you were with the lesson. Choose "Yes" if you were satisfied, and "No" if you weren't.
* **How satisfied are you with this lesson?**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the lesson. Choose "Yes" if you were satisfied, and "No" if you weren't.
* **What do you think we can improve on for future lessons?**: Share your ideas and suggestions on how we can improve our lessons.
* **Instructor name**: Enter the name of the instructor who delivered the lesson.
* **Instructor email**: Enter the email of the instructor who delivered the lesson.
* **Instructor phone**: Enter the phone number of the instructor who delivered the lesson.
* **Student feedback**: Rate how much you value student feedback during the lesson. Choose "Yes" if you found it helpful, and "No" if you didn't.
* **Submit**: Double-check your answers and submit the form to ensure your feedback is recorded.
* **How much time do you think we should spend on this lesson?**: Enter the number of minutes you think we should spend on the lesson.
* **How long should the lesson be?**: Enter the number of minutes you think the lesson should last.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about the lesson.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions on how we can improve our lessons.
* **Lesson improvements**: Share any ideas on how we can improve our lessons.
* **Ideas for future lessons?**: Share any ideas you have for future lessons.
* **Improvement ideas**: Share any ideas on how we can improve our lessons.
* **Instructor rating**: Rate the instructor's performance, with a higher number indicating better performance.
* **Why did you leave?**: Select "Yes" if you left the lesson early, and "No" otherwise.

This guide should help you navigate the form with ease and provide us with valuable feedback to improve our typing lessons. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us. Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts!
