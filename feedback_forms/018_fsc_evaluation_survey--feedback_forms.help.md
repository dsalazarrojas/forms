# FSC Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting feedback and ratings from users about their evaluations or interactions with your organization. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your first name in the text field "First Name".
2.  Enter your last name in the text field "Last Name".
3.  Enter your valid email address in the text field "Email".
4.  Enter your valid phone number in the text field "Phone".
5.  Select your rating for "Rating 1" using the dropdown menu.
6.  Select all the relevant ratings for "Rating 2" using the dropdown menu.
7.  Enter the date of your evaluation in the "Date of Evaluation" field.
8.  Enter the time of your evaluation in the "Time of Evaluation" field.
9.  Enter your feedback about your evaluation in the "Feedback" field.
10.  Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name. This is the first name of the person filling out this form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name. This is the last name of the person filling out this form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address. This will be the contact email of the person filling out this form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter a valid phone number. This will be the contact phone number of the person filling out this form.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, `select_one`, required: false): This is a rating field. Select a rating from 1 to 5.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This is a rating field. Select multiple ratings from 1 to 5.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`date_of_evaluation`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your evaluation. 
* **Time of Evaluation** (`time_of_evaluation`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your evaluation. 
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): Enter any feedback about your evaluation. 
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments. 

Please fill out all the fields you think are relevant to your evaluation. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organization's support team. 

Note: This form is for collecting feedback and ratings and should be filled out once per evaluation. Please do not fill out the form multiple times.
