# Critical Care Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees of the Critical Care Workshop on their overall experience, facilities, materials, presentation quality, and other aspects of the workshop. This feedback will help us to identify areas for improvement and enhance future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the corresponding fields with your honest feedback. For each question, please select the answer that best reflects your opinion, or type in your response in the provided text box. Make sure to save your responses so you don't have to start over from the beginning in case you need to add more information or edit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was your overall experience of the Critical Care Workshop?** (`workshop_participation`, text, required): This question asks for a brief description of your overall experience at the workshop. Please type in a few sentences to share your thoughts on what you enjoyed or didn't enjoy about the workshop.
* **Were the facilities and equipment adequate for the workshop?** (`workshop_facilities`, select_multiple, required): For this question, you will select from the options whether the facilities and equipment were excellent, adequate, or subpar. Please choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Were the materials and handouts provided useful?** (`workshop_materials`, select_one, required): Please select from the options whether the materials and handouts were very useful, somewhat useful, not very useful, or not at all useful. This will help us to understand how we can better support your learning experience.
* **Was the workshop too long or too short?** (`workshop_length`, select_one, required): For this question, please select whether the workshop was just right, too long, too short, or if you have no opinion on this matter.
* **How would you rate the presentation quality?** (`workshop_presentation`, number, required): You will be asked to rate the presentation quality on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is not useful and 5 is excellent.
* **Were the food and drink provided sufficient?** (`workshop_food_drink`, text, required): This question asks for a brief description of your experience with the food and drink provided. Please type in a few sentences to share your thoughts.
* **How would you rate the workshop speaker?** (`workshop_speaker`, number, required): Similar to the previous question, please rate the workshop speaker on a scale of 0-5, where 0 is not useful and 5 is excellent.
* **How would you rate the learning experience?** (`workshop_learning`, select_one, required): For this question, please select from the options whether the learning experience was excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Do you have any comments about the workshop?** (`workshop_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or suggestions about the workshop, please type them in this text box.
