# Managerial Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Managerial Communication Effectiveness Survey is designed to assess the communication effectiveness of managers with their team or staff. The survey will help you evaluate how well your manager communicates with you and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer according to your experience.
2. Select one option for each question that best describes your perception of your manager's communication style.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **intro** (`text`, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **communication_skills** (`select_one`, optional): How would you rate the effectiveness of your manager's communication skills? Choose from:
	+ Very Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **approachability** (`select_multiple`, optional): How would you rate the approachability of your manager? Choose all that apply:
	+ Somewhat Approachable
	+ Somewhat Distant
	+ Somewhat Very Distant
	+ Very Distant
* **listening_skills** (`select_one`, optional): How would you rate the listening skills of your manager? Choose one:
	+ Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **manager_support** (`select_one`, optional): How would you rate the level of support from your manager? This question is optional. Choose one:
	+ Very Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Poor
	+ Very Poor
* **manager_help** (`select_multiple`, optional): How would you rate the level of help you receive from your manager? Choose all that apply:
	+ Very Much
	+ Somewhat
	+ A Little
	+ Not at All
* **manager_concerns** (`select_one`, optional): How often do you feel your manager expresses concerns about your work or performance? Choose one:
	+ None
	+ Somewhat
	+ Some
	+ Very
* **comments** (`text`, optional): If you have any additional comments about your manager's communication style, please provide them here.
* **manager_abilities** (`text`, optional): If you have any thoughts on your manager's skills or areas for improvement, please describe them here.
* **manager_actions** (`text`, optional): If you have any suggestions on actions your manager could take to improve their communication, please describe them here.
* **manager_develop** (`select_one`, optional): How would you rate the development opportunities provided by your manager? Choose one:
	+ Very Good
	+ Somewhat Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Poor
	+ Very Poor

## Tips
* Answer honestly and objectively, based on your real experience with your manager.
* If you're unsure about some questions, choose the option that best describes your situation.
* This survey is intended to provide feedback that will help your manager improve their communication style. Your input is valuable!
