# Homeless Veteran Support Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeless Veteran Support Awareness Survey is a form designed to gather information from individuals who have direct contact with homeless veterans. The data collected will help us identify gaps in support services available to homeless veterans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the "Homeless Veteran Support" section, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For the "Support Areas" section, select all the areas that you believe are currently available to homeless veterans.
3. In the "Other" fields, if there is an area that is not listed, please use the corresponding "Other (Specify)" field to provide more information.
4. Finally, provide your contact information in the "Submitter" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **Veteran Status**: Select "Active" if you are a veteran who is homeless, "Inactive" if you are a veteran who is not homeless.
* **Veteran Status Other (Specify)**: If you have a unique status that is not listed, please describe it in this field.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization you are representing, if any.
* **Organization Type**: Select "Yes" if the organization you are representing provides support services to homeless veterans. If the organization you are representing does not provide support services, select "No".
* **Organization Other Type (Specify)**: If the organization you are representing provides a different type of support, please describe it in this field.
* **Support Areas**: Select all the areas that you believe are currently available to homeless veterans.
* **Support Areas Other (Specify)**: If there is an area that is not listed, please provide more information in this field.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Submitter Info**: This section is not required, but please note that it is intended for our internal use only.
* **Submitter Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **Submitter Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide more information.
* **Submitter Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are representing, if any.
