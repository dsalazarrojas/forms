# customer_support_escalation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer support escalation request form is used to report issues that require special attention from our support team. This form helps us understand the problem's severity and provide the necessary support to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Choose the correct escalation reason to explain why the issue needs to be escalated.
3. Describe the expected resolution or outcome.
4. If applicable, add attachments, such as files, images, or videos, to provide more context.
5. Choose the person to whom the issue will be assigned.
6. Add any comments, if necessary, to provide additional information.
7. Optionally, schedule a follow-up date if further action is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact details so we can get back to you regarding your issue.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the issue you are experiencing, including any relevant details.
* **Escalation Reason** (`escalation_reason`, `text`, required): Explain why you are escalating this issue to us, and what we need to do to resolve it.
* **Expected Resolution** (`expected_resolution`, `text`, required): State what you expect from us to resolve the issue.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `select_multiple`, optional): Add any supporting files, images, or videos that help us understand the issue.
	+ File
	+ Image
	+ Video
	+ Audio
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required): Choose the person to whom the issue will be assigned.
	+ chatjimmy
	+ support team
	+ other user (please specify)
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information you think will help us understand the issue.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `date`, optional): Schedule a follow-up date, if you need to track progress or follow up with us.
