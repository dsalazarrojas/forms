# Virtual Assistant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Assistant Feedback Form is designed to help us understand your experience with our AI-powered virtual assistant and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form with your honest feedback on how we can improve our virtual assistant.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, but you can leave optional ones blank if you're not sure or don't wish to provide more information.
- If you're providing contact information, please make sure it's correct and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, Not required): This field is where you'll type your name so we can know who's providing feedback.
* **How can we improve our assistant?** (`question`, Text, Not required): Share your thoughts on how we can make our virtual assistant better. Be as specific as possible.
* **Rate the assistant** (`rating`, Select One, Not required): Rate your experience with our virtual assistant on a scale of Not satisfied, Satisfied, or Very satisfied.
* **Suggestion or feedback** (`suggestion`, Text, Not required): If you have a specific idea or concern, share it with us so we can learn from it.
* **Email address** (`email`, Email, Not required): If you're willing, share your email address so we can follow up with you for more information or to show our appreciation for your feedback.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, Text, Not required): If you'd like to provide your phone number, we can call or text you back for more information or clarification.
* **Category** (`category`, Select One, Not required): Choose a category that best describes the purpose of your feedback (Support, Feedback, Suggestion).
* **Comment** (`comment`, Note, Not required): Share any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to add to your feedback.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Not required): Choose the tool that you were using when you encountered the issue (ChatJIMMY, chatjimmy-2).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, Select Multiple, Not required): Choose who submitted the feedback (User, Admin, Other).
* **Submitted Date** (`submit_date`, Date, Not required): The date and time when you submitted your feedback.
* **Submitted Time** (`submit_time`, Time, Not required): The time of day when you submitted your feedback.
* **Submitted Location** (`submit_location`, Select Multiple, Not required): Where you were when you submitted the feedback (Office, Home, Other).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, Select One, Not required): Choose who submitted the feedback (User, Admin, Other).
* **Submitter's Email** (`submitted_by_email`, Email, Not required): If you're willing, share your email address so we can follow up with you for more information or to show our appreciation for your feedback.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, Text, Not required): If you'd like to provide your phone number, we can call or text you back for more information or clarification.
* **Submitter's IP** (`submitted_by_ip`, Select One, Not required): Choose your IP type (Local, Public).
* **Submitter's Browser** (`submitted_by_browser`, Select One, Not required): Choose your browser type (Safari, Chrome, Firefox).
* **Submitter's Platform** (`submitted_by_platform`, Select One, Not required): Choose your platform type (Windows, Mac, Linux).
* **Submitter's OS** (`submitted_by_os`, Select One, Not required): Choose your operating system (Windows 10, Windows 7, Mac OS).
* **Submitter's Language** (`submitted_by_language`, Select One, Not required): Choose your language (English, French, Spanish).
* **Submitter's Country** (`submitted_by_country`, Select One, Not required): Choose your country (United States, Canada, Mexico).
* **Submitter's Region** (`submitted_by_region`, Select One, Not required): Choose your region (North America, South America, Europe).
* **Submitter's City** (`submitted_by_city`, Select One, Not required): Choose your city (New York, London, Paris).
* **Submitter's Device** (`submitted_by_device`, Select One, Not required): Choose your device type (Desktop, Laptop, Mobile).
