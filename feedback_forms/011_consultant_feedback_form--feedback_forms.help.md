# Consultant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients who have received consulting services from our organization. Your input is valuable in helping us improve our services and ensure that our consultants provide the best possible experience for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your feedback.
2. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) are filled out.
3. Take your time to provide thoughtful and detailed responses to each question.
4. Select the most relevant options for each question, even if you are unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): This is your full name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): This is your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): This is your organization's name (if applicable).
* **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required): This is the name of the consultant you worked with.
* **Engagement Date** (`engagement_date`, date, required): This is the date when you received the consultation.
* **Service Type Received** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of consulting service you received (e.g., Strategy, Management, Financial, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service received (e.g., Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, etc.).
* **Consultant Knowledge and Expertise** (`consultant_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the consultant's expertise (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Consultant Communication Skills** (`consultant_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the consultant's communication skills (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Consultant Professionalism** (`consultant_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the consultant's professionalism (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Value Received for Cost** (`value_received`, select_one, required): Rate the value you received for the cost of the service (e.g., Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, etc.).
* **Were Your Expectations Met** (`expectations_met`, select_one, required): Select if your expectations were met (e.g., Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Partially Met, etc.).
* **Most Helpful Aspects** (`helpful_aspects`, text, required): Describe the most valuable aspects of the service received.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, required): Describe areas where the service could be improved.
* **Have You Implemented Recommendations** (`recommendations_implemented`, select_one, optional): Select if you have implemented any recommendations from the consultant.
* **Results Achieved** (`results_achieved`, text, optional): Describe any results achieved from the consultation.
* **Would You Recommend This Consultant** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Select if you would recommend the consultant to others.
* **Would You Use This Consultant Again** (`would_use_again`, select_one, required): Select if you would work with the consultant again.
* **Interested in Additional Services** (`additional_services_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you are interested in.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback or comments you have.
* **May We Use Your Feedback as a Testimonial** (`testimonial_permission`, select_one, optional): Select if we can quote your feedback as a testimonial.
