# Injector Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our Injectors Service. This form helps us to identify areas of improvement and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, date and time of service, service technician, and service type.
2. Rate your experience with our service, including overall satisfaction, service quality, value, and staff quality.
3. Provide any comments or issues you encountered during your visit.
4. Specify whether you experienced any problems or issues during your visit.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Had Problem** (`rating_had_problem`, `number`, optional): If you experienced any issues during your visit, rate how serious the problem was, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, required): Please provide any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Service Technician** (`service_technician`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the service technician who attended to you.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you received (Yes or No).
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required): Please provide any details about your experience, including any issues you faced.
* **Issue** (`issue`, `text`, required): Describe any problems or issues you encountered during your visit.
* **Solution** (`solution`, `text`, optional): If you experienced an issue, please provide any suggestions or solutions you think we could implement to improve the service.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating of Service** (`rating_service`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating of Service Quality** (`rating_service_quality`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating of Service Value** (`rating_service_value`, `number`, required): Rate the value of our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating of Service Warranty** (`rating_service_warranty`, `number`, required): Rate the warranty of our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating of Service Staff** (`rating_service_staff`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our service staff, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Service Value 2** (`rating_service_value_2`, `number`, required): Rate the value of our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Service Warranty 2** (`rating_service_warranty_2`, `number`, required): Rate the warranty of our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Service Quality 2** (`rating_service_quality_2`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our service, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating Service Staff 2** (`rating_service_staff_2`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of our service staff, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Type List** (`service_type_list`, `text`, required): Select the type of service you received.

Note: The rating fields are on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
