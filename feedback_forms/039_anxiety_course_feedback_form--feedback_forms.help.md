# Anxiety Course Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants on the anxiety course to help us understand what we do well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read through the form carefully, and answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in your participant name if you wish to be acknowledged (optional but appreciated).
3. Provide the date you attended the course.
4. Rate your experience using the provided ratings scale.
5. Answer the remaining questions to the best of your ability, providing any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Course Feedback --** (`section_course_feedback`, `note`, required/optional) - This is the main feedback section where you can provide any thoughts or opinions on the course. 
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional) - Please enter your name, if you wish to be identified as the feedback contributor. 
* **Course date attended** (`course_date`, `date`, required) - Enter the date you attended the course, to help us understand your specific experience.
* **Instructor effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required) - Rate your experience with the instructor on a scale of 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Course content relevance** (`course_content_relevance`, `select_one`, required) - Choose how relevant the course material was to your needs.
* **Skills or techniques gained** (`skills_gained`, `text`, optional) - List any practical skills or techniques you learned from the course.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required) - Provide a general evaluation of your experience with the course, using a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional) - Share any ideas on how we could improve the course, including content, logistics, or support.

## Tips

* Please be honest and sincere in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions, please be specific and provide concrete ideas.
* Thank you for taking the time to help us improve the anxiety course!
