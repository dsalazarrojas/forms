# Transmission Issues Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transmission Issues Feedback Survey form is a tool used to gather information about any problems you have encountered with your transmission. This form will help us understand the nature of the issue, the steps taken to address it, and how we can improve our service to better assist you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page to provide us with as much detail as possible.
2. For each question, select the most suitable answer from the options provided.
3. If you are unsure or don't see an option that matches your situation, select "Describe Your Issue" and provide a detailed explanation.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, submit your feedback for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is your transmission issue

* **What is your transmission issue** (<code>transmission_issues_survey</code>, <type>text</type>, required): Describe the problem you are experiencing with your transmission, such as difficulty shifting gears, slipping, or other issues.

### 2. Car Model

* **Car Model** (<code>car_model</code>, <type>text</type>, required): Please enter the make and model of your vehicle.

### 3. Describe Your Transmission Problem

* **Describe Your Transmission Problem** (<code>transmission_problem_description</code>, <type>text</type>, required): Provide a detailed explanation of the problem you are experiencing with your transmission.

### 4. Have You Visited a Service Center

* **Have You Visited a Service Center** (<code>service_center_visit</code>, <type>select_one</type>, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1: Yes
	+ Option 2: No

### 5. What Did Mechanic Say

* **What Did Mechanic Say** (<code>mechanic_comment</code>, <type>text</type>, required): If you visited a service center, please describe what the mechanic said about the issue with your transmission.

### 6. Service Order Number

* **Service Order Number** (<code>service_order_number</code>, <type>text</type>, required): If you visited a service center, please enter the service order number provided by the mechanic.

### 7. Transmission Problem Description

* **Transmission Problem Description** (<code>issue_description</code>, <type>text</type>, required): Describe the problem in more detail, including any symptoms, error messages, or other relevant information.

### 8. Problem Solved

* **Problem Solved** (<code>problem_solved</code>, <type>select_one</type>, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Problem Solved
	+ Problem Not Solved

### 9. Will You Contact Us Again

* **Will You Contact Us Again** (<code>will_contact_again</code>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required): Please select all the following options that apply:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Possibly

### Tips
- Please answer all questions as accurately as possible to help us better understand your issue.
- If you are unsure about any question, please describe the issue in more detail in the corresponding field.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in the "What Did Mechanic Say" field.
