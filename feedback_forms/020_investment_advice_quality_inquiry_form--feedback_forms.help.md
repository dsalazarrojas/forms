# Investment Advice Quality Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of investment advice provided by financial institutions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by the *required* label.
2. Select your answer from the dropdown menu for "investment_advice_quality".
3. Enter your feedback in the text box for "feedback" and "additional_comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **investment_advice_quality_inquiry_form**: This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description of the investment advice you received.
* **name**: Your name will be used to acknowledge you as the user submitting the feedback.
* **email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you if we need more information.
* **investment_advice_quality**: Please select the level of satisfaction you feel about the investment advice you received:
	+ Highly Satisfied: You felt the investment advice was excellent and met your expectations.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: The investment advice was good, but not perfect.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: The investment advice was not satisfactory.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: The investment advice was bad and did not meet your expectations.
* **satisfaction_rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the investment advice on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is highly satisfied.
* **feedback**: Enter your detailed feedback about the investment advice you received. Please provide specific examples or suggestions for improvement.
* **additional_comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have about the investment advice you received.
* **submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
