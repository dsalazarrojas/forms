# Course Instructor Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from instructors on students, courses, or other aspects of their teaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Instructor ID (ID number assigned to the instructor)
2. Fill in the Student ID (ID number assigned to the student)
3. Select the Feedback Type (type of feedback: Yes/No)
4. Enter the Feedback (text input)
5. Fill in the Course ID (ID number assigned to the course)
6. Select the Feedback Date (date of feedback submission)
7. Enter the Additional Feedback (text input)
8. Enter the Email (instructor's email address)
9. Enter the Phone (instructor's phone number)
10. Enter the Instructor Name (instructor's name)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Instructor ID**: Your ID number assigned by the institution.
* **Student ID**: The ID number assigned to the student.
* **Feedback Type**: Select "Yes" if you have feedback to provide, "No" otherwise.
* **Feedback**: Enter your comments and suggestions for the student.
* **Course ID**: The ID number assigned to the course.
* **Feedback Date**: The date you submit your feedback.
* **Additional Feedback**: Enter any extra comments or suggestions.
* **Email**: Your institution email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Instructor Name**: Your name. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to be specific and detailed in your feedback to help improve student engagement and learning experience.
* Please note that the feedback form is anonymous, so feel free to provide constructive feedback without fear of judgment.
