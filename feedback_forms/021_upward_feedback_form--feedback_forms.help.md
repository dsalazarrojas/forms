# Upward Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Upward Feedback Form is designed to allow employees to provide feedback on their performance. This form is meant to be filled out by supervisors to give an honest assessment of an employee's work and help improve future performance and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
### General Instructions
*   Please fill out this form completely and honestly to ensure accurate feedback.
*   Make sure to complete all the fields required and provide as much detail as possible.
*   You can save your progress as you go and review your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (1, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee you are providing feedback on.
*   **Employee Title** (2, text, optional): Enter the title of the employee's role or position.
*   **Supervisor Name** (3, text, optional): Enter your name as the supervisor of the employee.
*   **Overall Performance Rating** (4, number, optional): Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Needs Improvement" and 5 is "Exceeds Expectations."
*   **Feedback** (5, text, optional): Provide specific examples or anecdotes about the employee's strengths and weaknesses, and any areas for improvement.

## Tips
*   Be honest and constructive in your feedback, focusing on specific behaviors or actions rather than personal traits.
*   Keep your feedback focused on the employee's performance and work-related issues.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR representative for guidance.

This guide provides the steps and explanations to help you fill out the Upward Feedback Form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your HR representative.
