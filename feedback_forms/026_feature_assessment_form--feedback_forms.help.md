# Feature Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Feature Assessment Form is used to document and review the features of a product or service, gathering information about their priority, impact, and other important characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the feature assessment form with the following information:
	* Feature Priority (Select the priority level of the feature: High, Medium, Low)
	* Product Features (Select all applicable features that are related to the current feature)
	* Feature Rationale (Explain the purpose and goals of the feature)
	* Feature Impact (Enter a numerical value to represent the feature's impact)
	* Business Value (Select the business value of the feature: High, Medium, Low)
	* Product Owner (Enter the name of the product owner)
	* Stakeholders Involved (Select all applicable stakeholders involved in the feature development)
	* Business Risks (Enter any potential business risks associated with the feature)
	* Cost of Changes (Enter the estimated cost of changes to the feature)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feature Assessment** (Feature 1, text, required)
	* This field is for entering the feature being assessed.
* **Feature Priority** (Feature Priority, select_one, required: false)
	* Select the priority level of the feature: High, Medium, Low.
* **Product Features** (Product Features, select_multiple, required: false)

* **Business Value** (Business Value, select_one, required: false)
	* Select the business value of the feature: High, Medium, Low.
* **Product Owner** (Product Owner, text, required: false)
	* Enter the name of the product owner.
* **Stakeholders Involved** (Stakeholders Involved, select_multiple, required: false)
	* Select all applicable stakeholders involved in the feature development.
* **Business Risks** (Business Risks, text, required: false)
	* Enter any potential business risks associated with the feature.
* **Cost of Changes** (Cost of Changes, number, required: false)
