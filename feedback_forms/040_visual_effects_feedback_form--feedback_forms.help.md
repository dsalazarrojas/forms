# Visual Effects Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings about visual effects projects from users. The feedback and ratings will help us improve our visual effects services and provide better experiences for our clients and customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form title**: Enter a brief description of your project in the "form_title" field.
2. **Select project types**: Choose the types of projects that your visual effect is related to (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, or Option 5).
3. **Provide feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the project in the "feedback" field.
4. **Rate the project**: Rate the project from 1 to 10 in the "project_rating" field.
5. **Add notes or comments**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the project in the "project_notes" field.
6. **Upload an image**: If you have an image related to your project, you can upload it in the "project_image" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **form_title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
- **project_type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of projects that your visual effect is related to (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, or Option 5).
- **feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the project.
- **project_rating** (`project_rating`, number, optional): Rate the project from 1 to 10.
- **project_notes** (`project_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the project.
- **project_image** (`project_image`, text, optional): If you have an image related to your project, you can upload it.

## Tips

* Please be as descriptive as possible when filling out the form title.
* Choose all applicable project types that your visual effect is related to.
* Be honest and constructive with your feedback.
* Provide clear and concise ratings.
* Use this form to help us improve our services, and don't hesitate to contact us if you need further assistance.
