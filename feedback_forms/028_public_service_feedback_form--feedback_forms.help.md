# public_service_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The public service feedback form is designed to gather feedback on public services from users, ensuring that services meet the needs and expectations of the community. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve and tailor our services to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all the required fields marked as "Required".
2.  For fields with "Select One" or "Select Multiple" options, choose the relevant option(s) that apply to your circumstances.
3.  Be as detailed as possible when filling out the "Feedback" field.
4.  Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your email address that we may use to contact you for follow-up questions or to send you updates.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the type of service you are providing feedback on (e.g., service 1, service 2, etc.).
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select the areas in which you received the service (e.g., area 1, area 2, etc.).
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, **Required**): Rate your experience with the service on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, **Required**): Describe your experience with the service, including any positive or negative aspects, and any suggestions for improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, **Optional**): Add any additional comments or observations that may be helpful in providing feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date of your experience with the service.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **Optional**): Enter the time of your experience with the service.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further clarification or follow-up questions.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, `email`, **Optional**): Enter your email address to confirm your identity and ensure that we contact you for further questions or follow-up.
* **First Language** (`first_language`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your first language (e.g., English, Spanish, Mandarin, etc.).
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select the languages you prefer to communicate with (e.g., English, Spanish, Mandarin, etc.).
* **Additional Languages** (`additional_languages`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select any additional languages you speak (e.g., English, Spanish, Mandarin, etc.).
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your gender (e.g., Male, Female, Other, etc.).
* **Ethnicity** (`ethnicity`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select your ethnicity (e.g., Asian, Black, White, etc.).
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, **Required**): Enter your age.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your age group (e.g., 18-24, 25-34, etc.).
* **Income** (`income`, `number`, **Required**): Enter your annual income.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your employment status (e.g., Employed, Self-Employed, Student, etc.).
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your education level (e.g., High School, College, University, etc.).
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your employment type (e.g., Full-Time, Part-Time, Freelance, etc.).
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your occupation or job title.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, **Required**): Click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

Note: The submit button will only be visible when all required fields are filled.
