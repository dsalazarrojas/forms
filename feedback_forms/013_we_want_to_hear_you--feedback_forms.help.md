# we_want_to_hear_you - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about our services. It allows them to share their thoughts, suggestions, or complaints, helping us to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the form section of our website or application.
2. Fill in your contact information in the "Contact Info" field, as this is a required field.
3. Share your feedback in the "Feedback" field. This field is not required, so feel free to share as much or as little as you want.
4. If you have a rating for our services, please provide it in the "Rating" field. This is also not required, but it will help us understand your opinion.
5. If you need to attach any additional information or a file, please use the "Attachment" field. This is optional.
6. If you have a file to upload, please use the "File" field. This is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required): Please fill in your contact information so we can get back to you.
- **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or complaints about our services.
- **Rating** (Rating, number, optional): If you have a rating for our services, please provide it.
- **Attachment** (Attachment, note, optional): If you need to attach any additional information or a file, please use this field.
- **File** (File, text, optional): If you have a file to upload, please use this field.

## Tips
- Please fill in the "Contact Info" field so we can get back to you.
- If you have any additional information or a file to share, use the "Attachment" or "File" field.
- You can share as much or as little feedback as you want, so don't hesitate to provide your thoughts.
