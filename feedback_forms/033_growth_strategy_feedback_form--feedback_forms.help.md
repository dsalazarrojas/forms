# Growth Strategy Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers, partners, or other stakeholders on their growth strategy. The purpose of this form is to gather information on their experiences, challenges, and areas for improvement. The feedback provided will help us to refine and adapt our growth strategy, ensuring we continue to deliver value to our clients and partners.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address. Your first name and last name are optional but required for organization and department fields.
*   Select your Organization: If you are affiliated with an organization, please select 'Yes' or 'No' based on whether your organization uses our services or products. This is required for our records.
*   Enter your current Position and Department. Please provide the name of the department you work in or the team you belong to.
*   Select your job title, if any.
*   Provide feedback about your experience with our growth strategy: Please select the category that best describes your experience with our growth strategy, i.e. 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   Optionally, provide any additional comments on your experience with our growth strategy, i.e., what worked well, what did not work well, etc.
*   Lastly, if you have a specific recommendation for how we can improve our growth strategy, please provide it in the field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`feedback_form_first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback record.
*   **Last Name** (`feedback_form_last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the feedback record.
*   **Email** (`feedback_form_email`, email, required): This is the email address associated with your organization or you personally. Please provide this in order to receive feedback follow-up and updates from us.
*   **Organization** (`feedback_form_organization`, select_multiple, required): Please select if you are affiliated with an organization that uses our services. This information helps us understand the context of your experience with our growth strategy.
*   **Position** (`feedback_form_position`, text, optional): Enter your current position or role in your organization for reference.
*   **Department** (`feedback_form_department`, text, required): Please enter the name of the department you work in or belong to for reference.
*   **Job Title** (`feedback_form_job_title`, text, optional): If you have a job title, please provide it for reference.
*   **Feedback Category** (`feedback_form_feedback_category`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes' or 'No' to describe your experience with our growth strategy. This helps us understand the nature of the feedback.
*   **Comments** (`feedback_form_comments`, text, optional): Optionally, provide any additional comments on your experience with our growth strategy. This can help us better understand the context and provide more tailored support.
*   **Recommendation** (`feedback_form_recommendation`, text, optional): If you have a specific recommendation, please provide it here.

By following these instructions and providing clear and honest feedback, you are helping us to improve our growth strategy, ensuring we continue to deliver the best experience for our clients and partners. Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback.
