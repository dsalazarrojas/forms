# ExamPreparationResourceFeedbackQuestionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ExamPreparationResourceFeedbackQuestionnaire is a form designed to gather feedback from users on the effectiveness of exam preparation resources. This feedback is used to improve and refine the resources, making them more effective for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, making sure to consider the resource's strengths and weaknesses.
3. Use the "comments" section to provide any additional feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (1, text, required/optional): A short description of the resource and your experience with it. Please keep it concise and focused on your experience with the resource.
* **What were the topics covered?** (2, select_multiple, required): Check all the topics that were covered in the resource. This will help us understand what aspects of the exam the resource addresses.
* **What were the difficulty levels?** (3, select_one, required): Rate the difficulty level of the resource, from High to Low.
* **Comments** (4, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the resource. This is your chance to share your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Rating** (5, select_one, required): Rate the resource overall, from Good to Fair.
* **Email** (6, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or feedback, provide your email address.
* **Phone** (7, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone for further discussion or feedback, provide your phone number.
* **Time** (8, time, optional): If you'd like to provide the amount of time you spent using the resource.
* **Date** (9, date, optional): If you'd like to provide the date you last used the resource.
* **Notes** (10, note, optional): Use this section to provide any additional notes or thoughts.
* **Additional Feedback** (11, text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them here.
