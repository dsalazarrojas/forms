# SBI Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at the branch. The purpose of this form is to gather information about their first visit, overall satisfaction, and other aspects of their visit. It also aims to collect contact information for follow-up and verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the corresponding information:
	* Branch Visit: Describe their branch visit.
	* Overall Satisfaction: Choose an option that best describes their overall satisfaction with the experience.
	* Staff Helpfulness: Enter a number between 1 (very helpful) and 10 (not helpful at all).
	* Staff Friendliness: Write a brief description about staff friendliness.
	* Branch Cleanliness: Choose an option that best describes the cleanliness of the branch.
	* Communication: Describe the communication with branch staff.
	* Staff Availability: Enter the staff availability during their visit.
	* Additional Feedback: Write any additional comments or suggestions.
	* Email: Enter their email address for follow-up purposes.
	* Phone: Enter their phone number.
	* Name: Enter their name.
	* First Visit Date: Enter the date of their first visit.
	* Staff Name: Enter the name of the staff member they interacted with.
	* Branch Name: Enter the name of the branch they visited.
	* Location: Enter the location of the branch.
	* Department: Enter the department they visited.
	* Shift: Enter the shift of the staff member they interacted with.
	* Staff ID: Enter the ID of the staff member they interacted with.
	* Staff Role: Enter the role of the staff member they interacted with.
	* Branch ID: Enter the ID of the branch they visited.
	* Branch ID Verification: (Optional) Enter the ID of the branch they visited for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Branch Visit**: Describe their branch visit.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose an option that best describes their overall satisfaction with the experience. Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied.
* **Staff Helpfulness**: Enter a number between 1 (very helpful) and 10 (not helpful at all).
* **Staff Friendliness**: Write a brief description about staff friendliness.
* **Branch Cleanliness**: Choose an option that best describes the cleanliness of the branch. Options: Very Clean, Somewhat Clean, Neutral, Somewhat Dirty, Very Dirty.
* **Communication**: Describe the communication with branch staff.
* **Staff Availability**: Enter the staff availability during their visit.
* **Additional Feedback**: Write any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Email**: Enter their email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter their phone number.
* **Name**: Enter their name.
* **First Visit Date**: Enter the date of their first visit.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member they interacted with.
* **Branch Name**: Enter the name of the branch they visited.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the branch.
* **Department**: Enter the department they visited.
* **Shift**: Enter the shift of the staff member they interacted with.
* **Staff ID**: Enter the ID of the staff member they interacted with.
* **Staff Role**: Enter the role of the staff member they interacted with.
* **Branch ID**: Enter the ID of the branch they visited.
* **Branch ID Verification**: (Optional) Enter the ID of the branch they visited for verification purposes.

Note: Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form. Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve our services.
