# pre_employment_assessment_practice_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Pre-Employment Assessment Practice Feedback** form is used to gather feedback from candidates who have completed a pre-employment assessment. This feedback is essential for us to understand the candidate's experience and performance in the assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Practice Assessment** page, which is the first page of the form.
2. On this page, you will find a note field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
3. When you have completed your comments, click Next to proceed to the next page.
4. On the second page, you will find the **Candidate Feedback** field, where you can provide any feedback about the assessment.
5. Enter your feedback in the provided text field.
6. Click Next to proceed to the next page.
7. On the third page, you will find the **Relevance Score** field, where you can rate your relevance score from 1 to 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Practice Assesment** (`note`, optional): Please provide any additional comments or notes about your experience with the pre-employment assessment. This is a free-text field where you can write anything you think is relevant.
* **Candidate Feedback** (`text`, optional): Please provide any feedback about the assessment. What did you like or dislike about it? What would you improve if you were in charge?
* **Relevance Score** (`number`, optional): Rate your relevance score from 1 to 5, where 1 is not relevant at all, and 5 is very relevant. Please base your score on how well the assessment reflected your skills and experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the Candidate Feedback field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [contact email/phone number].
