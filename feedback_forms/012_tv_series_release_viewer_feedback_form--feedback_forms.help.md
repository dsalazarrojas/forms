# tv_series_release_viewer_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users who have viewed a tv series release. The form is meant to be filled out by viewers who have opinions on the series and wish to provide constructive feedback to the release team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: **Category**, **Submit**.
2. You can skip the **title** field if you don't have any comments or suggestions.
3. For the **rating** field, select a number that best represents your overall opinion of the series.
4. In the **comments** field, type in any comments or suggestions you may have.
5. Select the **genre** categories that apply to the series (you can select multiple).
6. Fill out your **email** and **phone** if you wish to be contacted back for further discussion.
7. Finally, select **Submit** to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **tv_series_title**: This field is optional and can be skipped if you don't have any comments or suggestions about the title of the series.
	+ Please type in the title of the series you're providing feedback on.
* **tv_series_rating**: This field represents your overall opinion of the series.
	+ Select a number that best represents your opinion: 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **tv_series_comments**: This field is optional and can be used to type in any comments or suggestions you may have about the series.
	+ Keep it brief and concise, focus on one or two key points.
* **category**: This field is required and should be selected from the provided options: Drama, Action, Comedy.
	+ Select all that apply to the series.
* **genre**: This field is optional and can be used to select multiple categories that apply to the series.
	+ Select 'Yes' for categories that apply, 'No' for categories that don't.
* **email**: This field is optional and can be used to provide your contact information.
	+ If you wish to be contacted back for further discussion, please fill out this field.
* **phone**: This field is optional and can be used to provide your phone number.
	+ If you wish to be contacted back for further discussion, please fill out this field.
* **category**: (same as above)
* **submit**: This field is required and should be selected to submit your feedback.
	+ Please select 'Submit' to send your feedback to the release team.
