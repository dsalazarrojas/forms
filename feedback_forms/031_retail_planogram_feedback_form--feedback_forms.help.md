# Retail Planogram Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a retailer's planogram, a visual representation of products in a store. This feedback will help the retailer make informed decisions about product placement and layout.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question in the order it appears.
2. Ensure that each field is filled out as required.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What items do you think are not well presented?** (`feedback_item_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks for your feedback on which items are not well presented in the store.
* **How satisfied are you with the store layout?** (`store_layout_2`, `text`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the store layout using text to describe your feelings.
* **What items do you recommend adding to the store?** (`feedback_item_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Suggest new items that you think would be beneficial to add to the store.
* **How easy is it to navigate the store?** (`store_layout_4`, `text`, required: false): Describe how easy or difficult you find it to navigate the store.
* **Do you have any comments about the store layout?** (`comments_5`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the store layout.
* **Which items should we add or remove from the planogram?** (`feedback_item_6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Suggest items to add or remove from the planogram.
* **How satisfied are you with product placement?** (`product_placement_7`, `text`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with product placement using text to describe your feelings.
* **What could we do to improve the customer experience?** (`store_experience_8`, `text`, required: false): Offer suggestions on how to enhance the customer experience.
* **How satisfied are you with your overall store experience?** (`store_experience_9`, `text`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the store experience.
* **Are there any comments about the store you would like to add?** (`comments_10`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the store.
* **What is your contact information?** (`contact_11`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address or other contact information, if desired.
* **What is the store contact information?** (`store_contact_12`, `email`, required: false): Share the store's contact information, if known.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
- Use the provided options or text fields to provide clear and concise feedback.
