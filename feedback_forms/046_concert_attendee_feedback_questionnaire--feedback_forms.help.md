# Concert Attendee Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from concert attendees to help event organizers improve future concerts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide honest and thorough answers to each question.
3. You can skip questions that don't apply to you.
4. Use the options provided for rating questions.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback ID (1)** (`Feedback ID`, `text`, `required`): A unique identifier for your feedback submission.
* **Submission Date (2)** (`submission_date`, `date`, `required`): The date you submitted this feedback.
* **Attendee Name (3)** (`attendee_name`, `text`, `not required`): Your full name, if you feel comfortable sharing it.
* **Email Address (4)** (`email_address`, `email`, `not required`): Your email address, if you feel comfortable sharing it.
* **Age Range (5)** (`age_range`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your age range to help us understand our audience demographics.
* **Concert Name (6)** (`concert_name`, `text`, `required`): The name of the concert you attended.
* **Concert Date (7)** (`concert_date`, `date`, `required`): The date of the concert.
* **Concert Venue (8)** (`concert_venue`, `text`, `required`): The name of the venue where the concert took place.
* **Attendance Frequency (9)** (`attendance_frequency`, `select_one`, `required`): How often you attend concerts.
* **Overall Satisfaction (10)** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the concert.
* **Music Quality (11)** (`music_quality`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the music quality of the concert.
* **Performance Energy (12)** (`performance_energy`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the energy level of the performance.
* **Sound Quality (13)** (`sound_quality`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the sound quality of the concert.
* **Lighting and Visuals (14)** (`lighting_visuals`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the lighting and visuals of the concert.
* **Venue Comfort (15)** (`venue_comfort`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the comfort of the venue.
* **Ticket Value (16)** (`ticket_value`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the value you got for your money.
* **Concession Quality (17)** (`concession_quality`, `select_one`, `not required`): Rate the quality of food and beverage options.
* **Staff Friendliness (18)** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the friendliness of the staff.
* **Security Effectiveness (19)** (`security_effectiveness`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the effectiveness of security services.
* **Entry and Exit Process (20)** (`entry_exit_process`, `select_one`, `required`): Rate the entry and exit process.
* **Highlight Moment (21)** (`highlight_moment`, `text`, `not required`): Describe the best part of the concert.
* **Areas for Improvement (22)** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, `not required`): Suggest areas for improvement.
* **Would Attend Again (23)** (`would_attend_again`, `select_one`, `required`): Would you attend another concert here.
* **Would Recommend (24)** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, `required`): Would you recommend this concert to friends.
* **Additional Comments (25)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `not required`): Any other feedback or suggestions.
