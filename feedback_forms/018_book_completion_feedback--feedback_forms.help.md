## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Full Name**: Enter your full name to associate your feedback with your account.
• **Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to contact you, but don't worry, it's not for spamming.
• **Book Title**: Provide the full title of the book you're rating.
• **Book Author**: Enter the author's name for the book.
• **Date Completed**: Enter the date you finished reading the book in mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy format.
• **Overall Rating**: Rate the book overall using the 5-star scale.
• **Plot Rating**: Rate the plot using the 5-star scale.
• **Character Development Rating**: Rate the character development using the 5-star scale.
• **Writing Style Rating**: Rate the writing style using the 5-star scale.
• **Pacing Rating**: Rate the pacing using the 5-star scale.
• **Favorite Character**: If you have a favorite character, enter their name.
• **Favorite Scene or Moment**: Describe your favorite scene or moment.
• **Least Favorite Aspect**: If there's an aspect you didn't enjoy, explain it here.
• **Brief Summary Without Spoilers**: Write a 2-3 sentence summary of the book without giving away any spoilers.
• **Would You Recommend This Book**: Indicate whether you would recommend the book to others.
• **Who Would Enjoy This Book**: Select the target audience for the book.
• **Similar Books You Would Compare To**: List any similar books you'd recommend for comparison.
• **What Format Did You Read**: Indicate how you read the book.
• **How Long Did It Take to Read**: Select how long it took you to finish the book.
• **Emotional Impact**: Describe how the book affected you emotionally.
• **How Likely Will You Reread This Book**: Indicate your likelihood of rereading the book.
• **Detailed Review**: Share your full thoughts on the book.
• **Content Warnings to Note**: If there's any content others should be aware of, mention it here.
• **Feedback Submission Date**: Enter the date you're submitting your feedback.
