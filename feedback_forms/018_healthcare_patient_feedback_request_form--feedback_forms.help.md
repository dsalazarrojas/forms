# Healthcare Patient Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their visit to a healthcare facility. It helps us understand the quality of care provided and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure that we receive accurate and helpful feedback, please complete the form according to the following steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're answering all the required questions.
2. Fill in your general information, including your name, contact details, and visit date.
3. Select the doctor you visited from the list provided.
4. Describe your visit experience, including the doctor's performance and any issues you encountered.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the visit and provide additional comments.
6. Choose whether you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up or not.
7. If necessary, provide additional comments or details about your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `general_info`, required: false): Enter your name, contact information, and other general details about yourself.
* **Name of Doctor Assisted** (`2`, `doctor_assisted`, required: true): Select the name of the doctor who assisted you during the visit.
* **Date Visited** (`3`, `date_visited`, required: true): Enter the date you visited the healthcare facility.
* **Name of Doctor** (`4`, `name_of_doctor`, required: true): Enter the doctor's name who performed the visit.
* **Contact Information** (`5`, `contact`, required: true): Provide your contact information so we can reach out to you for further follow-up.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Feedback** (`7`, `feedback`, required: true): Describe your experience with the doctor and the visit in general.
* **Satisfaction** (`8`, `satisfaction`, required: true): Rate your satisfaction with the visit (Very Good, Good, Not, Fair, Bad).
* **Rating** (`9`, `rating`, required: false): If you feel comfortable doing so, please rate your visit on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating Comment** (`10`, `rating_comment`, required: false): Provide more comments about your rating, if desired.
* **Follow-up** (`11`, `follow_up`, required: false): Check if you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up.
* **Contact Again** (`12`, `contact_again`, required: false): If you're willing, please let us know if you'd like to be contacted again for any reason.
* **Additional Comments** (`13`, `additional_comments`, required: false): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about your visit, please add them here.
* **Name of Clinic** (`14`, `name_of_clinic`, required: false): If you'd like to specify the name of the clinic where you visited.
* **Name of Hospital** (`15`, `name_of_hospital`, required: false): If you'd like to specify the name of the hospital where you visited.
* **Department** (`16`, `department`, required: false): If you'd like to specify the department where you received care.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`17`, `doctor_specialty`, required: false): If you'd like to specify the doctor's specialty.
* **Patient ID** (`18`, `patient_id`, required: true): Enter your patient ID number.
* **Visit Date** (`19`, `visit_date`, required: true): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time** (`20`, `visit_time`, required: true): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Visit Duration** (`21`, `visit_duration`, required: true): Enter the length of your visit.
* **Follow-up Date** (`22`, `follow_up_date`, required: false): If necessary, please specify the date for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Time** (`23`, `follow_up_time`, required: false): If necessary, please specify the time for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Contact** (`24`, `follow_up_contact`, required: false): If you're willing, please let us know if you'd like to be contacted again for any reason.
* **Patient Name** (`25`, `patient_name`, required: true): Enter your full name.
* **Visit Note** (`26`, `visit_note`, required: false): If you'd like to add any additional notes about your visit, please do so.

## Tips

* Ensure that you're providing accurate and honest feedback.
* Please fill out the form completely and correctly.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to receiving it.
