# Volunteer Appreciation Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Appreciation Program Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from volunteers who have participated in the program. Your input will help us improve and refine the program to better meet your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2.  If you're unsure about a question or have questions about the form, don't hesitate to contact us.
3.  Make sure to save your progress as you go along, as you can only submit the form once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will help us contact you if we need further information.
* **Program Attendance** (`program_attendance`, select_one, optional): Choose the program you attended (Volunteer Appreciation Program or Other).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share as much detail as possible about your experience. Be specific about what you enjoyed, what you didn't, and what we could improve.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how satisfied you are with your experience (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Slightly Satisfied).
* **How Frequently** (`how_frequently`, number, optional): Please enter a number to indicate how often you visit or participate in our program.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): If you were assigned a tool during your participation, choose the correct tool (ChatJimmy or Other).
* **Submission Date** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This will be automatically populated with the current date and time.

Note: This form is designed to be completed once, so please submit your responses and save your progress.
