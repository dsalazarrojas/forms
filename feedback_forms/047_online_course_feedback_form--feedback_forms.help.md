# Online Course Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from course participants about their experience with the online course. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we use it to improve and refine our courses to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information as instructed. You can save your progress at any time, and come back to finish it later if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the title of the course?**: Enter the title of the course you're providing feedback on. This will help us identify which course your feedback is about.
* **What was your experience of the content?**: Share with us your thoughts and feelings about the course content. Be as specific as possible to help us understand what worked well or not for you.
* **What was your overall experience?**: Write a brief note about your overall experience with the course. This will help us understand your general impression and any suggestions you might have for improvement.

## Tips
* Please be as concise and specific as possible when filling in the form.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "What was your overall experience?" field.
* If you've finished the form, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.
