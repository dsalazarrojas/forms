# library_services_community_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "library_services_community_feedback_form" is a form designed to collect feedback from library users about the services and facilities offered by the library. This feedback will help us improve our services and better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the library branch where you visited or used our services.
2. Indicate whether you found the facilities to be adequate or not.
3. Choose whether the staff provided excellent service or not.
4. Check if you participated in any programs or services offered by the library.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you if needed.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
7. Optionally, enter the date and time you visited or used our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Library Branch**: Please select the library branch where you visited or used our services.
* **Facilities**: Check if you found the facilities to be adequate or not. If you have any specific feedback about the facilities, please use the "Comments" field below.
* **Staff Service**: Check if the staff provided excellent service or not.
* **Programs**: Check if you participated in any programs or services offered by the library.
* **Comments**: Enter your comments, suggestions, or feedback about our services, facilities, or staff.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date**: Optionally, enter the date you visited or used our services.
* **Time**: Optionally, enter the time you visited or used our services.
* **Submit**: Click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not submitted feedback within the last 30 days.
* You can submit feedback about multiple library branches or services in separate submissions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our library staff.
