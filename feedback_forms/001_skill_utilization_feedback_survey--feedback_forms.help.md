# Skill Utilization Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the utilization of skills in a specific role. This information will help to better understand how skills are used in different positions and make data-driven decisions for employee development and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question "Do you use this skill for your current role?".
3. Select all that apply from the options for "How well are your skills used in your current role?".
4. (Optional) Enter a numerical value in "How important is this skill for your current role?" if you think it's relevant.
5. Provide additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" field, if you have any comments or suggestions.
6. Enter the date and time of submission in the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields, respectively.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, although this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID.
* **Do you use this skill for your current role?**: Indicate whether you use this skill in your current role by selecting "Yes" or "No". If you're not sure or it's not applicable, select "Not Applicable".
* **How well are your skills used in your current role?**: Select all that apply from the options to describe how well your skills are used in your current role.
* **How important is this skill for your current role?**: If you think this skill is important, enter a numerical value (if applicable). If you're not sure or don't want to provide a numerical answer, leave this field blank.
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any comments or suggestions you have about the skill utilization in your current role.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you're submitting this form.
* **Time Submitted**: Enter the time you're submitting this form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for follow-up communication).
