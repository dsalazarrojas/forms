# Creative Project Follow Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from creative professionals on their experience with a project. The feedback gathered will be used to improve future projects and help us better understand our team's strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For the rating section, please rate the project from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
3. For the additional feedback section, please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely are you to recommend our work?** (`project_feedback_question_1`, select_one, optional): Please select Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, or Not Likely based on your experience with the project.
* **What was the most enjoyable part of the project?** (`project_feedback_question_2`, text, optional): Describe the part of the project that you enjoyed the most, or the part that you thought was the most impressive.
* **What was the biggest challenge we faced?** (`project_feedback_question_3`, text, optional): Describe the biggest challenge you faced during the project, or the part that you struggled with the most.
* **How can we improve on this project?** (`project_feedback_question_4`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or ideas on how we could improve on this project in the future.
* **Rating (1-10)** (`project_rating`, number, optional): Rate the project from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Additional feedback** (`project_feedback_message`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the project.
