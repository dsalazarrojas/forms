# Jira Integration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with integrating Jira. It's a tool for us to better understand what we can do to improve the integration and ensure it meets our users' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each field based on your experience with the Jira integration.
2. Be as detailed as possible in your responses to help us understand your feedback.
3. Don't hesitate to provide suggestions for improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Jira Integration** (id: 1): Enter a brief description of your experience with the Jira integration, including any positive or negative comments about the process. 
* **Issue** (id: 2): Choose whether you experienced any issues with the integration.
	+ If you select "Yes", please describe the issue in the "Comment" field.
* **Integration Point** (id: 3): Indicate whether you feel that the integration is at its best integration point.
* **Comment** (id: 4): Provide any specific comments or details about your experience.
* **Suggestion** (id: 5): If you have an idea for improvement, type it in this field.
* **Email** (id: 6): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted directly about your feedback.
* **Phone** (id: 7): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Date** (id: 8): Choose the date you're submitting your feedback.
* **Time** (id: 9): Select the time you're submitting your feedback.
* **Note** (id: 10): This field is for any additional notes about your feedback.
* **Jira Integration Form Comments** (id: 11): Indicate whether you'd like to share your feedback with the Jira team.
* **Assigned Tool** (id: 12): Choose whether you've assigned the integration to a tool.

Please note that all fields are optional, except for the "Jira Integration" field.
