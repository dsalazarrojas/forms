# Student Tutoring Program Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Tutoring Program Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from students on their experience with the tutoring program. The form helps us evaluate the effectiveness of the program, the quality of communication between the tutor and the student, and the overall value of the program to the student.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely, providing all required information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or in the school records.
* **Tutoring Session Date**: Select the date of the tutoring session you are providing feedback on.
* **Session Start Time**: Select the start time of the tutoring session.
* **Session End Time**: Select the end time of the tutoring session.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the session (in minutes).
* **Session Quality**: Select how you would rate the quality of the session:
	+ Excellent: The session was highly effective and met my needs.
	+ Good: The session was effective, but could be improved.
	+ Fair: The session was somewhat effective, but had some issues.
	+ Poor: The session was not effective.
* **Communication Effectiveness**: Select how effective the communication was:
	+ Highly Effective: The communication was clear and effective.
	+ Somewhat Effective: The communication was somewhat clear and effective.
	+ Not Very Effective: The communication was not clear.
	+ Not at All: The communication was not effective.
* **Understanding**: Select how well you understood the material:
	+ Excellent: I understood the material very well.
	+ Good: I understood the material somewhat well.
	+ Fair: I had some difficulty understanding the material.
	+ Poor: I did not understand the material at all.
* **Session Materials**: Select how relevant the materials were:
	+ Highly Relevant: The materials were highly relevant to the session.
	+ Somewhat Relevant: The materials were somewhat relevant to the session.
	+ Not Very Relevant: The materials were not very relevant to the session.
	+ Not at All: The materials were not relevant at all.
* **Overall Experience**: Select how positive or negative your overall experience was:
	+ Highly Positive: I had a very positive experience.
	+ Somewhat Positive: I had a somewhat positive experience.
	+ Neutral: I had a neutral experience.
	+ Somewhat Negative: I had a somewhat negative experience.
	+ Highly Negative: I had a very negative experience.
* **Session Value**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions about the session value.
* **Suggestions**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions for improving the program.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
