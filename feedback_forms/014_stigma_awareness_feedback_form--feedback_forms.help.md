# Stigma Awareness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stigma Awareness Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from end-users about the impact of a program on stigma and mental health. This form will help the program's creators understand how the program affects individuals' perceptions of stigma and mental health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out all the required fields. If a field is marked as 'required: false,' you can skip it.
2.  For fields with multiple options, select one or more of the provided options that best answer your question.
3.  For fields asking for numbers, enter only numbers.
4.  For fields asking for email or phone numbers, enter the correct format (e.g., email: <username>@<domain>, phone: 123-456-7890).
5.  Read the "Hint" labels for additional guidance and context for the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`1`, `text`, `required: false`): This section is for providing information about yourself. Please fill out your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
* **Program Impact** (`2`, `number`, `required: false`): Enter the number of your response to "How many times did our program impact you?"
* **Program Impact Questions** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Answer the questions about the impact of our program on your life. Select all that apply:
	+ Impact of our program on stigma
	+ Impact of our program on your mental health
	+ Overall impact of our program on your life
* **Program Support** (`4`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Answer the question: "Is our program support helpful?"
* **Community Group** (`5`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for entering your community group name (if applicable).
* **School Name** (`6`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your school name (if you're a student or staff).
* **School Email** (`7`, `email`, `required: false`): Enter your school email address (if you're a student or staff).
* **School Phone** (`8`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your school phone number (if you're a student or staff).
* **Nonprofit** (`9`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for entering your nonprofit name (if applicable).
* **Nonprofit Email** (`10`, `email`, `required: false`): Enter your nonprofit email address (if applicable).
* **Nonprofit Phone** (`11`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your nonprofit phone number (if applicable).
* **Program Locations** (`12`, `text`, `required: false`): Enter your school or program location.
* **Program Duration** (`13`, `number`, `required: false`): Enter the number of days/weeks/months/years you've been in our program.
* **Stigma Perceptions** (`14`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Answer the questions about your stigma perceptions:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Program Questions** (`15`, `text`, `required: false`): Describe your thoughts on our program.
* **Program Questions Hint** (`16`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for additional guidance and context for the question above.
* **Program Support Questions** (`17`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): Answer the questions about our program support:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Program Support Questions Hint** (`18`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for additional guidance and context for the question above.
* **Program Support** (`19`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Answer the question: "Is our program support helpful?"
* **Program Support Hint** (`20`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for additional guidance and context for the question above.
* **User Mental Health** (`21`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Answer the question:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Other (please specify)
* **User Mental Health Hint** (`22`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for additional guidance and context for the question above.
* **Stigma Perceptions Question 1** (`23`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Answer the question: "Do you think our program reduces stigma?"
* **Stigma Perceptions Question 1 Hint** (`24`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for additional guidance and context for the question above.
* **Stigma Perceptions Question 2** (`25`, `select_one`, `required: false`): Answer the question: "Do you think our program increases stigma?"
* **Stigma Perceptions Question 2 Hint** (`26`, `text`, `required: false`): This is for additional guidance and context for the question above.

Please take your time and answer all the questions truthfully. If you have any additional comments or questions, don't hesitate to let us know. Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback with us.
