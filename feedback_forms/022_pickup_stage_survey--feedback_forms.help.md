# Pickup Stage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pickup Stage Survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our services. This survey is used to measure customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your experience with our service.
2. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
3. Submit your survey to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What time did you arrive?
* **What time did you arrive?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_1`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you arrived for your service. This will help us understand when our service was available.
### Was the driver friendly?
* **Was the driver friendly?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer this question based on your experience with the driver. If the driver was friendly, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
### Was the vehicle clean?
* **Was the vehicle clean?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer this question based on the condition of the vehicle. If the vehicle was clean, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
### How would you rate the overall service?
* **How would you rate the overall service?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our service. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
### Do you have any comments?
* **Do you have any comments?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_5`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience. This is an opportunity to tell us what we did well and what we could improve on.
### How long were you waiting in line?
* **How long were you waiting in line?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of time you spent waiting in line for your service.
### What is your name?
* **What is your name?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name so we can personally address any concerns or respond to your feedback.
### What is your phone number?
* **What is your phone number?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
### How satisfied are you with your experience?
* **How satisfied are you with your experience?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_9`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service. Select one of the options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Unsatisfied, or Very Unsatisfied.
### Do you have any other feedback?
* **Do you have any other feedback?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_10`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience.
### How long were you waiting in line for the driver?
* **How long were you waiting in line for the driver?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_11`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of time you spent waiting in line for the driver.
### Was the vehicle clean?
* **Was the vehicle clean?** (`pickup_stage_survey_page_12`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer this question based on the condition of the vehicle. If the vehicle was clean, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
