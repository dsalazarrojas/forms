# Sustainability Certification Value Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the Sustainability Certification Value Survey form, which aims to collect information about individuals or organizations that have been certified in various sustainability programs. The form is designed to be completed by stakeholders, including internal employees and external individuals or organizations. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Select the applicable sustainability certification from the dropdown menu.
3.  Enter your name, email, and phone number in the required fields.
4.  Answer the question about whether you are certified in sustainability.
5.  Rate your sustainability performance on a scale.
6.  Provide any additional comments about your sustainability performance, if desired.
7.  Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Sustainability Certification** (`select_sustainability_certification`, Select One, Required): This field is used to select the type of sustainability certification you or your organization holds. Choose from the provided options: GRS (Greenhouse Gas Protocol), ISO 14001, B Corp, or Other.
* **Name** (`name`, Text, Required): This field is used to enter your name or the name of your organization.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Is Sustainability Certified?** (`is_sustainability_certified`, Select One, Required): Answer this question by selecting "True" if you are certified in a sustainability program or "False" if you are not.
* **Sustainability Rating** (`sustainability_rating`, Number, Required): Rate your sustainability performance on a scale in this field.
* **Comment on Sustainability** (`comment_on_sustainability`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about your sustainability performance in this field.
* **Page 1: Header** (`header1`, Note, Optional): This field is not currently being used.
*   **Page 1: Submitter** (`sub_header1`, Note, Optional): This field is not currently being used.
*   **Page 1: Name** (`Page1 Name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the first page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 1: Phone** (`Page1 Phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the first page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 1: Email** (`Page1 Email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the first page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 1: Comment** (`Page1 Comment`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about the first page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 1: Sustainability Certified** (`Page1 Sustainability Certified`, Select One, Required): Answer this question by selecting "True" if the first page of your certification is certified in a sustainability program or "False" if it is not.
*   **Page 1: Sustainability Rating** (`Page1 Sustainability Rating`, Number, Required): Rate the sustainability performance of the first page of your certification on a scale in this field.
*   **Page 1: Sustainability Comment** (`Page1 Sustainability Comment`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about the sustainability performance of the first page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 2: Header** (`header2`, Note, Optional): This field is not currently being used.
*   **Page 2: Submitter** (`sub_header2`, Note, Optional): This field is not currently being used.
*   **Page 2: Name** (`Page2 Name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the second page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 2: Phone** (`Page2 Phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the second page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 2: Email** (`Page2 Email`, Email, Required): Enter the email address of the second page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 2: Comment** (`Page2 Comment`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about the second page of your certification in this field.
*   **Page 2: Sustainability Certified** (`Page2 Sustainability Certified`, Select One, Required): Answer this question by selecting "True" if the second page of your certification is certified in a sustainability program or "False" if it is not.
*   **Page 2: Sustainability Rating** (`Page2 Sustainability Rating`, Number, Required): Rate the sustainability performance of the second page of your certification on a scale in this field.
*   **Page 2: Sustainability Comment** (`Page2 Sustainability Comment`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments about the sustainability performance of the second page of your certification in this field.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, Text, Optional): This field is not currently being used.

**Tips**

*   Please review the form carefully and ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.
*   Be truthful when answering the questions about your sustainability certification and performance.
*   If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact the relevant authorities.
