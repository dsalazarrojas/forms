# Gym Class Participation Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from gym class participants about their motivation and experience with gym class. This information will help us better understand their needs and preferences, enabling us to improve the effectiveness and enjoyment of our gym class program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose and goals of the survey.
2. Choose how often you attend gym class (page 2).
3. Select the motivations that apply to you for attending gym class (page 3).
4. Rate your satisfaction level with the gym class experience (page 4).
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback (page 5).
6. Enter your email address (page 6).
7. Enter your phone number (page 7).
8. Enter your name (page 8).
9. Optionally, provide any other comments or feedback (page 9).
10. Select which tool you are currently using (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (note, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. You can read it to understand the purpose and goals of the survey.

### Attendance Frequency
* **How often do you attend gym class?** (select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the options: Gym Class Regularly, Rarely, or Never. This field helps us understand how frequently you participate in gym class.

### Motivation Level
* **What motivates you to attend gym class?** (select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose all the options that apply to you:
		- Enjoy physical activity
		- Improve physical fitness
		- Improve mental well-being
	+ If none of the above options apply, you can select "Other (please specify)" and provide a brief description of your motivation.

### Satisfaction Level
* **How satisfied are you with the gym class experience?** (number, required)
	+ Rate your satisfaction level from 1-10, where 1 is not satisfied at all and 10 is extremely satisfied. This field helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the gym class experience.

### Feedback
* **Any other comments?** (note, optional)
	+ Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback about your gym class experience. You can write a brief description or ask questions.

### Email
* **Email** (email, required)
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information or to follow up on your feedback.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (text, required)
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or to follow up on your feedback.

### Name
* **Name** (text, required)
	+ Enter your name for identification purposes.

### Other Comments
* **Other comments** (text, optional)
	+ Use this field to provide any additional comments or feedback that didn't fit in the previous fields.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned tool** (select_one, required)
	+ Choose the tool that you are currently using for the gym class (e.g. chatjimmy).
