# Beverage Flavor Satisfaction Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from respondents about their experience with a specific beverage flavor. The feedback will help manufacturers or retailers understand customer satisfaction and preferences, ultimately informing product development and improvement decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and email address, to allow us to follow up with you for further communication.
2. Provide information about the product you purchased, including its name, flavor, and size.
3. Share your experience with the flavor, including your overall satisfaction, authenticity, and intensity.
4. Compare the flavor to similar products and share your willingness to repurchase and recommend it to others.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Your name as the person providing the feedback.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Your email address for follow-up communications.
* **Location or Store Name** (`location`, `text`, optional): The store or location where you purchased or consumed the beverage.
* **Date of Purchase or Consumption** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required): The date when you tried the beverage.
* **Product Name or Brand** (`product_name`, `text`, required): The name or brand of the beverage.
* **Flavor Name** (`flavor_name`, `text`, required): The specific flavor variety you purchased.
* **Container Size** (`container_size`, `select_one`, required): The size of the package you purchased.
* **Type of Purchase Location** (`purchase_location_type`, `select_one`, required): The type of location where you bought the beverage.
* **Overall Satisfaction with Flavor** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Your overall satisfaction with the flavor.
* **How Authentic is the Flavor** (`flavor_authenticity`, `select_one`, required): Your opinion on the authenticity of the flavor.
* **Flavor Intensity** (`flavor_intensity`, `select_one`, required): Your assessment of the flavor's intensity.
* **Sweetness Level** (`sweetness_level`, `select_one`, required): Your opinion on the sweetness level of the flavor.
* **Aftertaste Quality** (`aftertaste`, `select_one`, required): Your assessment of the aftertaste quality.
* **Compared to Competitor Products** (`compared_to_competitors`, `select_one`, optional): How does this flavor compare to similar products?
* **Would You Repurchase This Flavor?** (`repurchase_intention`, `select_one`, required): Your willingness to repurchase the flavor.
* **Would You Recommend to Others?** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, required): Your willingness to recommend the flavor to others.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`flavor_improvements`, `text`, optional): Any suggestions for enhancing the flavor.
* **Is the Price Fair for the Quality?** (`price_fairness`, `select_one`, required): Your opinion on the price fairness.
* **Other Flavors You Would Try** (`likely_purchase_variants`, `text`, optional): Any other flavors that interest you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other feedback or suggestions?

**Tips**

* Please provide accurate and honest feedback to help us improve the flavor.
* You can provide as much or as little information as you'd like, but try to be as detailed as possible.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
