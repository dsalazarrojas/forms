# customer_testimonial_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "customer_testimonial_form" is designed to collect feedback and testimonials from customers, helping us understand their satisfaction and identify areas of improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "What was your experience like prophets" field with a brief description of your experience.
2. Provide your rating of our service, using the scale provided in the "rating" field.
3. Let us know if you would recommend our service by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "do you recommend us" options.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, if you'd like to be contacted back.
5. Enter your phone number in the "contact" field, if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
6. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the "comments" field.
7. Select the date of your experience from the "date" field.
8. Select the time of your experience from the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What was your experience like prophets** (`testimonial`, text, optional): Describe your experience with us.
- **rating** (`rating`, number, required): Provide a rating of our service, using a scale provided.
- **do you recommend us** (`recommend`, select multiple, required): Let us know if you would recommend our service to others.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted back, please enter your email address.
- **contact** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
- **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
- **date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of your experience.
- **time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of your experience.

## Tips
- Please be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve our service.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "comments" field.
- If you'd like to be contacted back, please make sure to enter a valid email address.
- If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please make sure to enter a valid phone number.
