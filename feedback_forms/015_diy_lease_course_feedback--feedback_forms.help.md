# DIY Lease Course Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience with the DIY Lease Course. The form is designed to gather information about the course's effectiveness, usability, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the course name**: If you've taken the DIY Lease Course, please enter the name of the course in the "Course Name" field. This will help us track your specific experience with the course.
2. **Rate your feedback**: Choose one of the options that best describes your level of satisfaction with the course. You can select "Not Satisfied", "Satisfied", or "Very Satisfied" from the drop-down menu.
3. **Provide additional comments**: If you have any specific thoughts or suggestions about the course, please use the "Comment" field to share them with us. This is your chance to give more details about your experience.
4. **Enter your email address (optional)**: If you'd like to receive a confirmation email about your submission, please enter your email address. This is optional and only required if you want to receive feedback on your feedback.
5. **Submit your feedback**: Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you've taken, if any.
  - This helps us track your specific experience with the course.
  - Please enter the name of the course as you know it.
- **Feedback** (`feedback_score`, select_one, **required**): Rate your satisfaction with the course.
  - Choose one of the options: "Not Satisfied", "Satisfied", or "Very Satisfied".
  - This will help us understand your overall impression of the course.
- **Comment** (`comment`, note, **required**): Share your thoughts or suggestions about the course.
  - Use this field to give more details about your experience.
  - Be specific about what you liked or disliked about the course.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a confirmation email.
  - This is not required but helps us reach out to you with any follow-up questions.
  - Enter a valid email address if you'd like to receive feedback on your feedback.

## Tips
- Please fill out all the required fields to ensure your feedback is processed correctly.
- Be as specific as possible when providing comments or suggestions.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help.
