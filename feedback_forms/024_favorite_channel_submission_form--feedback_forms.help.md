# Favorite Channel Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect user feedback on their favorite channel, which helps us better understand their preferences and interests. The collected data will be used to improve our channel offerings and provide a more personalized experience for our audience. Please ensure you're submitting feedback on a channel you have a genuine interest in, as this will ensure the accuracy of the data we collect.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your favorite channel from your usual viewing habits.
2.  Choose your preferred time to visit the channel.
3.  Indicate how often you visit the channel.
4.  Select the type of content you usually consume on the channel.
5.  Choose whether you would recommend the channel to a friend.
6.  Optionally, provide additional comments about your favorite channel.
7.  Indicate what makes our channel unique from others.
8.  Enter your email address if you'd like to receive updates about the channel.
9.  Enter your phone number (optional).
10.  Briefly describe your favorite channel in one sentence.
11.  Indicate how likely you are to recommend our channel to a friend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite channel** (`channel_1`, text, required: false): This is where you can provide the name of your favorite channel. Please enter the actual name of the channel you have a genuine interest in.
* **When is your favorite time to visit** (`time_1`, time, required: false): Select a 24-hour format like "HH:MM" to indicate when you usually visit your channel. For example, 08:00 or 14:30.
* **How often do you visit your favorite channel** (`frequency_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the options "Never", "Occasionally", "Frequently", or "Always" to indicate your visiting frequency.
* **How do you usually interact with your channel** (`interaction_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply from the options "Social Media", "Mobile App", or "Website" to indicate how you interact with your channel.
* **What type of content do you usually consume on your channel** (`type_1`, select_one, required: false): Choose one option from "Live stream", "Video on-demand" to indicate the type of content you usually consume.
* **Would you recommend our channel to a friend** (`recommend_1`, select_one, required: false): Select from the options "Yes", "No", or "Other" to indicate whether you would recommend our channel to a friend.
* **Tell us more about your favorite channel** (`more_info_1`, note, required: false): If you'd like to provide additional comments about your favorite channel, you can enter them here.
* **What makes our channel unique from others?** (`channels_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply from the options "Content variety", "Content quality", "User engagement", "Community interaction", "Unique features", or "Other" to indicate what makes our channel unique.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to receive updates about the channel, please enter your email address in the format example@example.com.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to provide your phone number, please enter it here.
* **Briefly describe your favorite channel** (`channel_name`, text, required: false): Please enter a brief description of your favorite channel in one sentence.
* **How likely are you to recommend our channel to a friend?** (`channels_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose one option from "Not likely", "Somewhat likely", "Very likely", or "Extremely likely" to indicate how likely you are to recommend our channel to a friend.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional, but filling them in can help us better understand your preferences. Please fill out as many fields as possible to provide a more comprehensive feedback.
