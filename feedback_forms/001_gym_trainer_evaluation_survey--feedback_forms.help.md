# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a gym trainer. The data collected will help us improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields with your honest feedback and ratings. You can answer every question or just those that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey** (1, text, required): Fill in the title of the gym trainer you are evaluating.
* **Trainer Professionalism** (2, number, optional): Rate the trainer's professionalism on a scale of 1-5.
* **Communication** (3, text, optional): Describe the trainer's communication skills.
* **Motivation** (4, text, optional): Describe the trainer's motivation level.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (5, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the trainer on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips
Please fill in the form only once per trainer. You can submit any additional comments or feedback in the **Communication** field.
