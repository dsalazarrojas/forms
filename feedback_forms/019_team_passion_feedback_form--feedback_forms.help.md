# Team Passion Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Team Passion Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from team members to understand their level of satisfaction with their current job, the team they work with, and the workplace culture. This feedback is used to identify areas for improvement and implement changes to enhance the work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your personal details: email address and phone number (fields 6 and 7).
2. Answer the following questions:
   * How do you feel about your current job? (field 2, job_satisfaction)
   * How do you feel about the team you work with? (field 3, team_dynamics)
   * What would improve your feelings about the team you work with? (field 13, team_dynamics_improvement)
3. Describe your thoughts about your current job, the team, and the workplace culture in the text fields:
   * job_satisfaction_why (field 8)
   * team_dynamics_why (field 9)
   * workplace_culture_why (field 10)
4. Suggest improvements for your current job, the team, and the workplace culture:
   * job_satisfaction_improvement (field 12)
   * team_dynamics_improvement (field 14)
   * workplace_culture_improvement (field 15)
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions:
   * additional_comments (field 17)
6. If you want to highlight any specific factors that are crucial to your job satisfaction:
   * job_satisfaction_important (field 16)
   * job_satisfaction_not_important (field 19)
   * job_satisfaction_what_improves (field 22)
7. If you want to highlight any specific factors that are crucial to your team dynamics:
   * team_dynamics_important (field 17)
   * team_dynamics_not_important (field 20)
   * team_dynamics_what_improves (field 23)
8. If you want to highlight any specific factors that are crucial to your workplace culture:
   * workplace_culture_important (field 18)
   * workplace_culture_not_important (field 21)
   * workplace_culture_what_improves (field 24)
9. Finally, click 'Submit' to send your feedback.
