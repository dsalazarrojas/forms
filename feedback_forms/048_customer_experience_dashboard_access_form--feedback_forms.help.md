# customer_experience_dashboard_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive customer feedback, including demographic information, feedback type, visit details, and other relevant data. It's meant to help analyze and improve customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address as it appears on your contact information.
3. Select your role in the organization (admin, user, or manager).
4. Choose the type of feedback you're providing (comment, suggestion, or complaint).
5. If you have a photo or document related to your feedback, enter it in the provided field.
6. Provide information about the location where you visited.
7. Enter your contact number and email for follow-up communication.
8. If you have a website associated with your feedback, enter it here.
9. Share any relevant social media links if applicable.
10. Optionally, provide additional notes or comments about your experience.
11. Choose when you visited the location (date and time).
12. Enter the time you spent on the visit.
13. Rate your experience.
14. Provide a detailed feedback about your experience.
15. Fill in your assigned ID and the date you submitted the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email as it appears on your contact information.
* **role** (select_one, required): Select your role in the organization (admin, user, or manager).
* **feedback_type** (select_multiple, required): Choose the type of feedback you're providing (comment, suggestion, or complaint).
* **comment** (text, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **date_visited** (date, optional): Choose the date you visited.
* **time_visited** (time, optional): Choose the time you visited.
* **time_spent** (number, required): Enter the time you spent on the visit.
* **rating** (number, required): Rate your experience.
* **feedback** (text, required): Provide detailed feedback about your experience.
* **photo** (text, required): Enter any relevant photo or document related to your feedback.
* **location** (text, required): Enter the location where you visited.
* **contact_number** (text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **contact_email** (email, required): Enter your email for follow-up communication.
* **website** (text, required): Enter your website if applicable.
* **social_media** (text, optional): Share any relevant social media links.
* **notes** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **assigned_to** (text, required): Enter your assigned ID.
* **date_submitted** (date, required): Enter the date you submitted the feedback.
* **user_id** (number, optional): Optional field for user ID, not always required.
* **created_by** (text, optional): Optional field for created by, not always required.
* **created_at** (text, optional): Optional field for created at, not always required.
* **updated_by** (text, optional): Optional field for updated by, not always required.
