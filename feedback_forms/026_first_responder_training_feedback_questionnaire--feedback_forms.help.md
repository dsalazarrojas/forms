# first_responder_training_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The first_responder_training_feedback_questionnaire is a feedback form designed to gather information about the quality of training received by first responders. It will help us understand their overall experience, the effectiveness of the training, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **first_responder_name** field.
2. Select your **first_responder_role** from the provided options.
3. Rate your overall experience with the training using the **overall_rating** field.
4. Enter any comments or feedback in the **comments** field.
5. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the **additional_comments**, **additional_comments_2**, and **additional_comments_3** fields.
6. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
7. If you would like to provide your phone number, enter it in the **phone** field.
8. Finally, select the date you completed the training in the **first_responder_date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_responder_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **first_responder_role**: Select the role you played in the training (e.g. firefighter, EMT, police officer).
* **overall_rating**: Rate your overall experience with the training on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about the training.
* **additional_comments**, **additional_comments_2**, and **additional_comments_3**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **email**: Enter your official email address for contact purposes.
* **phone**: If you wish to be contacted for further discussion or information, enter your phone number.
* **first_responder_date**: Select the date you completed the training.
* **rating_date** and **comments_date**: These fields are not required for most users.
* **phone**: Note that this field is optional, but if you have any concerns or questions, you can enter your phone number for us to reach out to you.
