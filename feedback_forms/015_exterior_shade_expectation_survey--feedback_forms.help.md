# Exterior Shade Expectation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Exterior Shade Expectation Survey is a form designed to collect information from users about their external shade expectations. This includes contact details, location and type of shade, and preferred contact method. This form is meant to be completed by users who have an expectation for external shade, such as for a living room, bedroom, or bathroom, and need to share their requirements with the relevant department for implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Exterior Shade Expectation Survey, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the contact method you prefer to receive updates about your external shade expectation (Email, Phone, or Post).
3. Choose the location where you would like to have the external shade installed (Living Room, Bedroom, Bathroom, Kitchen, or Other).
4. Select the type(s) of external shade you require (Light Filter, Darkening, Blinds, Shades, or Other).
5. Add any additional comments about your external shade expectation.
6. Fill in your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you about your external shade expectation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: This field is for you to provide your contact details, including your name and email address. This will allow us to get in touch with you about your external shade expectation.
* **Contact Number**: This field is for you to provide your phone number, which we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Here, you can choose how you would like to receive updates about your external shade expectation (Email, Phone, or Post).
* **Shade Location**: This field is for you to select the location where you would like to have the external shade installed. Select the relevant location from the options provided (Living Room, Bedroom, Bathroom, Kitchen, or Other).
* **Shade Type**: In this field, you can select the type(s) of external shade you require (Light Filter, Darkening, Blinds, Shades, or Other).
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for you to add any additional comments or details about your external shade expectation.
* **Submitter Name**: Here, you can provide your name as the submitter of the external shade expectation.
* **Submitter Email**: This field is for you to provide your email address so we can get in touch with you about your external shade expectation.
* **Submitter Phone**: This field is for you to provide your phone number, which we can use to contact you about your external shade expectation.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date to help us understand your external shade expectation properly.
* If you have any questions or need help with completing this form, feel free to contact us.
* If you have any additional expectations not covered in this form, please let us know in the "Additional Comments" field.
* Please note that some fields are optional, but providing all required fields will help us serve you better.
