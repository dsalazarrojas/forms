# Conservation Efforts Community Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conservation Efforts Community Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information from community members about their involvement and interest in local conservation efforts. This form aims to gather data on their awareness, participation, and interests in conservation activities, allowing the organization to better understand the community's needs and tailor its efforts accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your inquiry date in the "Inquiry Date" field.
2. Enter your name, email address (optional), and phone number (optional) in their respective fields.
3. Select your residency status, length of residency, and awareness level in their respective fields.
4. Choose the natural resources you value most.
5. Indicate your past participation history, types of activities you've participated in, and barriers to participation.
6. Express your interest in future participation and preferred activities.
7. Specify how you'd like to receive information and what conservation issues are most important to you.
8. Consider donating to conservation efforts if you're interested.
9. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Date**: The date you're submitting this inquiry.
* **Your Name**: Your name (optional).
* **Email Address**: Your email address (optional).
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number (optional).
* **Residency Status**: Select your residency status (Full-time resident, Part-time resident, Business owner, Property owner, Visitor, or Other).
* **Length of Residency**: Select how long you've lived in the area (Less than 1 year, 1 to 5 years, 5 to 10 years, 10 to 20 years, More than 20 years, or Lifetime).
* **Awareness Level**: Select how aware you are of local conservation efforts (Very aware, Somewhat aware, Neutral, Not very aware, or Not aware at all).
* **Value Resources**: Select the natural resources you value most (Clean water, Forests and trees, Wildlife habitat, Parks and open space, Agricultural land, Wetlands, Trails and recreation, Scenic views, Air quality, or Other).
* **Valued Resources (Other)**: If you selected "Other," specify the natural resources you value most.
* **Participation History**: Indicate your past participation history (Yes - Regularly, Yes - Occasionally, Yes - Once, No - Never).
* **Types of Activities**: Select the types of activities you've participated in (Volunteer events, Educational workshops, Fundraising, Advocacy, Citizen science, Trail maintenance, Tree planting, Clean-up events, or None).
* **Barriers to Participation**: Select the barriers that prevent you from participating more (Lack of time, Lack of information, Physical limitations, Transportation issues, Scheduling conflicts, Not interested, Cost, or None - I participate regularly).
* **Interest in Future Participation**: Express your interest in future participation (Very interested, Somewhat interested, Neutral, Not very interested, or Not interested).
* **Preferred Activities**: Select the activities you'd like to see more of (Educational programs, Family-friendly events, Volunteer opportunities, Guided nature walks, Workshops, Youth programs, Senior programs, Evening events, Weekend events, or Other).
* **Communication Preference**: Specify how you'd like to receive information (Email, Social media, Postal mail, Phone calls, Community bulletin, Website, Local newspaper, None).
* **Important Issues**: Select the conservation issues that are most important to you (Water quality, Wildlife protection, Climate change, Habitat restoration, Invasive species, Land preservation, Sustainable agriculture, Recreation access, Environmental education, or Other).
* **Important Issues (Other)**: If you selected "Other," specify the conservation issues that are most important to you.
* **Will to Donate**: Consider donating to conservation efforts (Yes - Definitely, Yes - Possibly, Unsure, or False).
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you may have for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other comments or feedback you may have.
