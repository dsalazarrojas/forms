# Singles Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal experiences and feedback.
*   Be honest and sincere in your responses, and try to provide as much detail as possible.
*   If you're unsure about any question, just do your best to provide the most likely answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
*   **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event (e.g. concert, festival, workshop).
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event (e.g. venue name, address).
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the event (e.g. 2022-01-01).
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event (e.g. 10:00 AM).
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the event (e.g. 2022-01-01).
*   **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the event (e.g. 02:00 PM).
*   **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, number, required): Enter how long the event lasted in hours.
*   **Event Attendees** (`event_attendees`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether the event was attended by others or not (e.g. yes, no).
*   **Event Ambiance** (`event_ambiance`, select_multiple, required): Choose the ambiance of the event (e.g. crowded, relaxed).
*   **Event Food and Drink** (`event_food_and_drink`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether food and drink were available at the event (e.g. yes, no).
*   **Event Sound Level** (`event_sound_level`, select_multiple, required): Choose the sound level of the event (e.g. high, low).
*   **Event Climate** (`event_climate`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the climate of the event (e.g. hot, cold).
*   **Event Safety** (`event_safety`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the event was safe or not (e.g. yes, no).
*   **Event Comments** (`event_comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the event.
*   **Event Rating** (`event_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): Leave a message for the event organizer.
*   **Event Suggestion** (`event_suggestion`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you may have for future events.
*   **Event Liking** (`event_likings`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you liked or disliked certain aspects of the event (e.g. music, performances, venue).
*   **Event Dislike** (`event_dislikes`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you disliked certain aspects of the event (e.g. music, performances, venue).
*   **Event Rating 2** (`event_rating2`, number, optional): Enter a second rating for the event on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Event Rating 3** (`event_rating2`, number, optional) is not included in the form yaml so we do not include it in the help guide.
