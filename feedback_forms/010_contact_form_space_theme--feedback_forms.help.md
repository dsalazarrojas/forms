# Contact Form Space Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for sending feedback or requests to the Space Theme team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name, email address, and a brief subject.
2. Provide detailed information in the "Message" field.
3. If relevant, select the "Importance level" that best describes your request.
4. Choose your preferred contact method, if you want to be contacted via email or phone.
5. Decide if our team should follow up with you.
6. Provide any additional information that might be helpful for our response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor name** (`visitor_name`, `text`, required): Your full name.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): The email address you want us to use to reply to you.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the subject of your message.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Provide detailed information about your request or feedback.
* **Importance level** (`importance_level`, `select_one`, optional): If you select "High", our team will prioritize your request.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred contact method, email or phone.
* **Require follow up** (`follow_up`, `select_one`, required): Decide if our team should contact you to follow up on your request.
* **Additional information** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any other context that might be helpful for our response.
* **-- Space Theme Contact --** (`title`, `note`, not a field but a title): This is the form title, do not fill out this field.
