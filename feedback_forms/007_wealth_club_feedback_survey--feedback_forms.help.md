# Wealth Club Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wealth Club Feedback Survey is a short and simple form that collects your feedback on your experience with our program. This survey helps us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Review each page of the form carefully.
2.  For each question or field, provide as much detail as possible.
3.  Use the available options to select your responses where applicable.
4.  Submit your completed form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Satisfaction** (`Member Satisfaction`, number, optional):
	+ This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the Wealth Club program.
	+ Please select a number between 1 and 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction, where 1 is "Very Unsatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`Improvement Suggestions`, note, optional):
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions or ideas you have for how we can improve the Wealth Club program.
	+ Please type in your thoughts and ideas, and we will review them carefully.
* **Referral Intent** (`Referral Intent`, select multiple, optional):
	+ This question asks how likely you are to refer friends or family to the Wealth Club program.
	+ Select all the options that apply to you, such as "Friend", "Family", or "Social Media".
* **Satisfaction with Program** (`Satisfaction with Program`, select one, optional):
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with our program.
	+ Please select one of the available options: "Very Satisfied", "Somewhat Satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat Unsatisfied", or "Very Unsatisfied".

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Please keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you have any questions or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time taking to complete this form.
