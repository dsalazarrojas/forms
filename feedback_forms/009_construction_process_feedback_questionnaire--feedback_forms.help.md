# Construction Process Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from responders on their experience with the construction process. It aims to identify areas for improvement and gather insights to enhance the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and the name of the project being evaluated.
2.  Select the stage of the construction process you are providing feedback on.
3.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the process stage.
4.  Describe the primary issues you encountered.
5.  Share any positive aspects of the process that worked well.
6.  Suggest practical improvements for the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Responder name**: This is an optional field where you can provide your name. If you prefer to remain anonymous, you can leave it blank.
* **Project name**: Please enter the name of the project being evaluated. This will help us associate your feedback with the specific project.
* **Process stage**: This field asks you to select which part of the construction process you are providing feedback on (Design, Procurement, Construction, or Handover).
* **Satisfaction level**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the process stage. The options are Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, and Very dissatisfied.
* **Biggest issues encountered**: Describe the primary issues you faced during the process.
* **Positive aspects**: Share any positive aspects of the process that worked well for you.
* **Improvement recommendations**: Suggest practical improvements for the process. Be as specific as possible to help us make informed changes.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the process.
* If you have already submitted feedback on this process, review and update your response if necessary, as the process stage and satisfaction level may have changed since your original submission.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
