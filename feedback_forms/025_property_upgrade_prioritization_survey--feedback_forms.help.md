# property_upgrade_prioritization_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **property_upgrade_prioritization_survey** form is a user-facing form used to gather information from property owners or their representatives about their upgrade requests. This form helps us to prioritize and schedule upgrade requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Enter your property address in the **Property Address** field.
3. Select the type of property you are requesting an upgrade for (e.g. Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
4. Choose the size of your property (e.g. Large, Extra Large).
5. Select your priority request (e.g. High, Medium, Low).
6. Describe your upgrade request in the **Request Description** field.
7. If you have a specific budget in mind, select the corresponding budget allocation option.
8. Enter your contact number and email address for follow-up communication.
9. Provide any additional details or comments in the **Additional Message** field (optional).
10. Confirm your upgrade request by selecting the **Confirm Upgrade Request** checkbox.
11. Finally, agree to the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Address** (`property_address`): Enter your property address where the upgrade request applies.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`): Select the type of property you are requesting an upgrade for (e.g. Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Property Size** (`property_size`): Choose the size of your property (e.g. Large, Extra Large).
* **Priority Request** (`priority_request`): Select your priority request (e.g. High, Medium, Low).
* **Request Description** (`request_description`): Describe your upgrade request in detail.
* **Budget Allocation** (`budget_allocation`): Select a budget allocation option (e.g. 1-1000, 1001-5000, 5001-10000).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter your contact number for follow-up communication.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Additional Message** (`message`): Provide any additional details or comments about your request.
* **Confirm Upgrade Request** (`confirm_upgrade`): Check this box to confirm your upgrade request.
* **I Agree** (`terms_and_conditions`): Select this option to agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields to ensure your request is processed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your provided contact number or email address.
* We will review and prioritize your upgrade request based on the information provided.
