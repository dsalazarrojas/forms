# Contact Page Red And Responsive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users who have visited our contact page. This form is used by internal teams to process user inquiries and provide personalized support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Name** in the `Name` field. This is a required field to ensure we can identify you properly.
2.  Enter your **Email** address in the `Email` field. This is also a required field to ensure we can contact you back.
3.  If you have a **Phone Number**, you can enter it in the `Phone` field. This is an optional field.
4.  Provide your **Location** in the `Your Location` field. This is an optional field.
5.  Select the **Service of Interest** that you're looking for. This is a required field to help us direct your inquiry to the right team.
6.  Choose the **Project Timeline** when you need our help. This is an optional field to help us gauge your urgency.
7.  Select the **Budget Range** that applies to your project. This is an optional field to help us understand your budget.
8.  Write a brief **Message** describing your inquiry. This is a required field to ensure we understand your needs.
9.  Let us know how you **Heard About Us**. This is an optional field to help us track our marketing efforts.
10.  Finally, grant us **Consent to Contact** you to proceed with your inquiry. This is a required field to ensure we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name**: Your full name, used to identify you.
*   **Email**: Your email address, used to contact you back.
*   **Phone**: Your phone number, used to contact you (optional).
*   **Your Location**: Your city and state, used to identify your location.
*   **Service of Interest**: Select the service that interests you, to direct your inquiry to the right team.
*   **Project Timeline**: When do you need our help, to gauge your urgency.
*   **Budget Range**: Select the budget that applies to your project, to help us understand your budget.
*   **Message**: A brief description of your inquiry, to ensure we understand your needs.
*   **How Did You Hear About Us**: Let us know how you heard about us, to track our marketing efforts.
*   **Consent to Contact**: Grant us consent to contact you, to proceed with your inquiry.

## Tips

*   Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we can assist you properly.
*   Make sure to check with our team first if you have any previous submissions.
*   We'll get back to you as soon as possible after receiving your submission.
