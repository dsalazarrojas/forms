# quarterly_qapi_meeting_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees on the quarterly QAPI (Quality Assurance and Performance Improvement) meeting. This meeting is a regularly scheduled event where key stakeholders discuss and review the current state of quality improvement initiatives. The feedback collected through this form will help identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for future meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the meeting date for which you are providing feedback.
2. Enter the meeting time (AM/PM) if applicable.
3. Provide the location of the meeting (room number, building name, etc.).
4. Briefly describe the meeting topic discussed during the meeting.
5. Rate the overall quality of the meeting (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
6. Add any additional feedback or comments about the meeting in the feedback notes field.
7. Enter your email address for contact purposes, in case we need to follow up on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Feedback** (`meeting_feedback`, text, optional): Enter your overall impression of the meeting, what was effective, and what can be improved.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the meeting you are providing feedback on.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting (AM/PM) if applicable.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of the meeting (room number, building name, etc.).
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, optional): Briefly describe the main topic discussed during the meeting.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall quality of the meeting (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
* **Feedback Notes** (`feedback_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback or comments about the meeting.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes, in case we need to follow up on your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, so we can effectively use your input for future improvements.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the feedback notes field.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the QAPI meeting process. Thank you for taking the time to provide your input!
