# Website Visitor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from website visitors about their general experiences and feedback about the website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your honest and accurate answers.
2. Provide your basic contact information, which will be kept confidential.
3. Share your feedback about your experience on the website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (required: false):
	* This field is optional and asks for a brief statement about your visit, including the reason you visited and any comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Experience** (required: false):
	* Describe your experience on the website, including what you liked or disliked about it.
* **Contact Info** (required: false):
	* This field is where you can provide your contact information, such as email or phone number, to help us get in touch with you.
* **Feedback** (required: false):
	* Share any specific feedback or suggestions you have about the website, including what we did well and what we could improve on.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Provide your feedback in a clear and concise manner.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the website's terms and conditions.
