# Student Daily Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Daily Feedback Form is designed to allow students to provide feedback on a class or educator on a daily basis. This feedback is essential for educators to improve their teaching methods and create a better learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Make sure to select the correct options for each field.
3.  Use the provided text fields to give detailed feedback.
4.  Use the date and time fields to provide accurate information about your last class.
5.  Use the category fields to provide feedback on specific topics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name? (student_name)**: Please enter your name as it appears in your school records.
* **What question do you want to give feedback on? (feedback_question)**: Please enter the specific question or topic that you are providing feedback about.
* **What feedback do you want to give? (student_feedback)**: Please describe in detail what you learned or what was unclear in the question or topic.
* **How did the educator respond? (educator_response)**: Describe how the educator responded to your questions or topics.
* **What is your category? (category)**: Select the category that best describes your response, such as math, science, or history.
* **What is your email address? (student_email)**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **What is your phone number? (student_phone)**: Enter your phone number, but this is optional.
* **What is the date of your last class? (date)**: Use the date picker to select the date of your last class.
* **What time is your last class? (time)**: Use the time picker to select the time of your last class.
* **Do you have any additional notes or comments? (note)**: If you have anything else you would like to add or comment on, use this field.
* **What are your comments about the class? (student_comments)**: Please add any general comments about the class.
* **What is your response to other category? (category_other)**: This field is optional, but if you have a response that does not fit into one of the provided categories, add it here.
* **What is your response to category 1? (category_1)**: Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **What is your response to category 2? (category_2)**: Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **What is your response to category 3? (category_3)**: Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **Do you have any other categories you would like to add? (student_category)**: If you have any other category that is not listed, select all that apply.
