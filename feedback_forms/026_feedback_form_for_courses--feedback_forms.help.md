# feedback_form_for_courses - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Feedback Form for Courses" is designed to collect user feedback about courses, allowing course administrators to identify areas of improvement and enhance the overall course experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
   * Select a feedback type from the options provided.
   * If you have any comments or suggestions, provide them in the comments field.
   * If you have a specific contact person or email address that we can reach out to, provide it in the contact field.
   * Enter your email address in the email field.
2. Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
3. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Type**: Select the type of feedback you are providing:
	+ **general**: general comments or suggestions about the course.
	+ **technical**: technical issues or problems with the course.
	+ **course materials**: feedback about the course materials or content.
	+ **support**: feedback about the course support or administration.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, provide them here.
* **Suggest**: If you have any specific suggestions for improvement, provide them here.
* **Contact**: If you have a specific contact person or email address that we can reach out to, provide it here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if necessary.
* **Submit Feedback**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
