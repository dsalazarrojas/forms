# Tax Document Submission Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the user's experience with the tax document submission process. It will help us understand their satisfaction with the clarity, support, and overall process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your response for each field and submit the form. There is no need for a signature or other attachments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Upload Process
* **Upload Process** (<type>: select_one, <name>: upload_process): Please select how you experienced the upload process during your recent tax document submission: Yes or No.
### 2. Clarity of Upload Process
* **Clarity of Upload Process** (<type>: select_multiple, <name>: clarity): How clear was the upload process? Please select all that apply: Yes, No.
### 3. Level of Support
* **Level of Support** (<type>: select_one, <name>: support): How would you rate the level of support you received during the upload process? High, Low.
### 4. Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (<type>: select_one, <name>: overall_xperience): Do you feel your tax document submission experience was positive overall? Yes, No.

## Tips
* Be honest in your responses to help us improve our service.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" section, if provided.
* You can submit this form multiple times, but each response will be reviewed individually.
