# Zoo Visitor Exhibit Interest Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from visitors about their interests, motivations, and potential feedback regarding their visit to the zoo. This information will help us better understand and serve our visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the form with your basic information, including any additional details you'd like to provide.
2. Select the exhibit you are most interested in visiting.
3. Choose your motivation for visiting the zoo (e.g., Educational, Recreational, Both).
4. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please type them in the text box.
5. If you would like to receive updates about new exhibits and events, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Checkin (checkin)**: A text field for visitors to provide any additional details about their visit.
* **Most Interested Exhibit (exhibit_interest)**: This is a select-one field to help us understand which exhibit you are most interested in visiting.
* **Visit Motive (visit_motive)**: This is a select-multiple field where you can choose one or more reasons for visiting the zoo (e.g., Educational, Recreational, Both).
* **Feedback (feedback)**: A text field for visitors to provide any feedback or suggestions about their visit.
* **Email (email)**: A field to enter your email address if you would like to receive updates about new exhibits and events.
* **Phone (phone)**: A text field for visitors to enter their phone number (optional).
