# Hotel Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after their stay at the hotel. Your feedback will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our guests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your feedback, being as detailed as possible.
2. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (Hotel Name and Email).
3. If you want to be contacted by us, please fill out your Phone number.
4. Once you've filled out the form, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Name** (`hotel_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the hotel you are providing feedback about.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Describe your experience, including what you liked or disliked about your stay.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you want us to contact you about your feedback, please enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have a complaint, please try to be constructive and suggest a solution.
* If you have a compliment, we appreciate the feedback and will pass it on to our staff.

Note: The Phone field is optional, but filling it out will allow us to contact you directly about your feedback.
