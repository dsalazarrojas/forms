# Environmental Survey Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Survey Check In Form is a tool used to collect data from users for environmental surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Project Name** (if applicable) in the designated field. This field is not mandatory.
2.  Provide your **Email** address for contact purposes. This field is not mandatory.
3.  Enter your comments in the **Comments** fields (1-16), which are optional.
4.  Select your current **Category** (Environmental or Other) from the options provided.
5.  Choose your **Location** (Office or Home) from the options provided.
6.  Enter the **Date** of the survey in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7.  Select the **Time** of the survey in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
8.  Rate your experience with a score from 1 to 5.
9.  Review and provide comments for each of the **Comments** fields as needed.
