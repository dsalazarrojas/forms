# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Patient Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with your healthcare organization. This form helps us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve to better serve our patients.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Feedback" page and provide your thoughts about your recent visit or interaction with our organization.
2. On the "Rating" page, choose how you would rate your experience (Good, Fair).
3. If you'd like to provide more details about your experience, you can add a comment on the "Comment" page.
4. On the second "Rating" page, choose how you would rate another aspect of your experience (Good, Fair).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`page_1`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts and feedback about your recent visit or interaction with our organization. This is a text field where you can type anything you'd like.
* **Rating** (`page_2`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your overall experience with our organization (Good, Fair).
* **Comment** (`page_3`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide more details about your experience, this is the place to do it. You can type as much or as little as you'd like.
* **Rating (again)** (`page_4`, `select_one`, optional): Rate another aspect of your experience with our organization (Good, Fair).
* **Date** (`page_5`, `date`, optional): This is not a required field, but if you have a specific date you'd like to reference, you can select it from the calendar. 

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible in the "Feedback" and "Comment" fields.
* If you're unsure about which rating to select, choose "Good" if you were satisfied with your experience and "Fair" if you had some issues.
* Remember, this form is for your feedback, so don't hesitate to share your thoughts and opinions.
