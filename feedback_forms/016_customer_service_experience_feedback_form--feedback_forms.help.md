# Customer Service Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of the Customer Service Experience Feedback Form is to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with your organization's customer service team. This form is used to gauge the effectiveness of our customer service efforts and identify areas for improvement. Please only complete this form once per interaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Choose one or more options for the "Channel" field, if applicable.
* Select the "Interaction Type" of your experience (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
* Rate your satisfaction with our customer service using the "Customer Service Rating" field.
* Enter your contact information (email and/or phone number) in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, if you want us to follow up with you.
* Provide specific feedback and improvement suggestions in the "Feedback" field.
* Enter your name and the name of the customer service representative you interacted with in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Service Rep" fields.
* Finally, submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Experience**: This field is where you can describe your experience with our customer service team.
	+ Please be as detailed as possible, including any positive or negative aspects of your experience.
* **Interaction Type**: Choose the type of interaction you had with our customer service team (e.g., phone, email, in-person).
* **Channel**: Select all applicable channels you used to interact with our customer service team (e.g., phone, email, social media, in-person).
* **Customer Service Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with our customer service using the options: Satisfied, Unsatisfied, or Neutral.
* **Satisfaction Score**: This field is not a numerical score but rather a text field where you can provide more specific details about your satisfaction.
* **Feedback**: Please provide specific feedback about your experience with our customer service team.
* **Improvement Suggestions**: If you have suggestions on how we can improve our customer service, please enter them here.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address if you want us to follow up with you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want us to follow up with you.
* **Customer Service Rep**: Enter the name of the customer service representative you interacted with.
* **Follow-up**: This field is not a required field and is used to indicate if you want us to follow up with you.
  (If you select "yes", it means you are expecting a follow-up on your feedback)
