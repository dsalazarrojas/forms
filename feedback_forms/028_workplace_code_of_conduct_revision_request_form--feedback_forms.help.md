# Workplace Code Of Conduct Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Workplace Code Of Conduct Revision Request Form" is designed to facilitate the process of revising the company's Code of Conduct. This form allows employees or staff to submit suggestions, changes, or improvements to the Code of Conduct by providing a clear and concise description of their request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a clear and concise title for your request in the `Workplace Code Of Conduct Revision Request Form` field.
2. Provide a brief description of the change or suggestion you'd like to make to the Code of Conduct.
3. Select the relevant `policy_area` and `policy_section` where the change should be applied.
4. Specify the `policy_clause` where the change should be implemented.
5. Enter a clear and concise description of the reason for your request in the `reason_for_request` field.
6. Provide specific changes and suggestions for improvement in the `suggest_changes` field.
7. Enter your name in the `submitter_name` field.
8. Enter your email address in the `submitter_email` field.
9. Enter your phone number in the `submitter_phone` field.
10. Enter your department in the `submitter_department` field.
11. Enter your job title in the `submitter_title` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workplace Code Of Conduct Revision Request Form**: This is the title of the form, which should be clear and concise.
- **description**: Provide a brief description of the change or suggestion you'd like to make to the Code of Conduct.
- **policy_area**: Select the area of the Code of Conduct that the change should be applied to (e.g., "Area of Code of Conduct", "Area 1", "Area 2").
- **policy_section**: Select multiple sections where the change should be applied (e.g., "Section 1", "Section 2", "Section 3").
- **policy_clause**: A specific clause or rule within the policy or section where the change should be implemented.
- **reason_for_request**: Enter a clear and concise description of the reason for your request.
- **suggest_changes**: Provide specific changes and suggestions for improvement.
- **submitter_name**: Enter your full name as the submitter of the request.
- **submitter_email**: Enter your email address as the submitter of the request.
- **submitter_phone**: Enter your phone number as the submitter of the request.
- **submitter_department**: Enter your department as the submitter of the request.
- **submitter_title**: Enter your job title as the submitter of the request.
