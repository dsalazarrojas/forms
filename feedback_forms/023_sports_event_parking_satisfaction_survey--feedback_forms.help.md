# Sports Event Parking Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their parking experience at the sports event. Your responses will help us improve the parking facilities and services provided.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select your answer from the options provided.
3. You can skip questions that do not apply to you.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the comments section at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, text, optional): This is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it before answering the questions.
* **How was your overall parking experience?** (`parking_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall parking experience using the provided options (Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
* **How long did you spend in the parking lot?** (`park_in_minutes`, number, optional): Enter the actual time you spent in the parking lot.
* **How far is this parking lot from your destination?** (`park_distance`, select_one, optional): Choose the distance from the parking lot to your destination using the provided options (0-5 minutes, 5-10 minutes, 10-15 minutes, 15-30 minutes, More than 30 minutes).
* **How would you rate the availability and quality of the parking facilities?** (`parking_facilities`, select_one, optional): Rate the parking facilities using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How would you rate the signage and directions for the parking lot?** (`parking_signage`, select_one, optional): Rate the signage and directions using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How would you rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the parking staff?** (`parking_staff`, select_one, optional): Rate the parking staff using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **How would you rate your overall experience with parking?** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall experience with parking using the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions about your parking experience?** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions you have about your parking experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or comments).

Tips:

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* Use the provided options to rate your experience.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please use the comments section.
