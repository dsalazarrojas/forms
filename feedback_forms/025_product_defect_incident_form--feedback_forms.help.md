# Product Defect Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Defect Incident Form is designed to collect information about product defects or incidents that have occurred. This form will help your team or organization gather data and analyze issues that might have affected a specific product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Answer each field thoroughly and accurately.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the "Incident Details" section.
* Attach images of the product and incident if applicable.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out, but note that most fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Defect Form**: This section is an introduction to the form.
* **Product Description**: Describe the product involved in the incident briefly.
* **Incident Details**: Describe the incident as much as possible, including the context and any relevant details.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product affected by the incident.
* **Product Type**: Enter the type of product involved.
* **Defect Description**: Describe the defect or issue that occurred.
* **Resolution**: Explain how the issue was resolved.
* **Root Cause**: Identify the root cause of the incident.
* **Action Taken**: Describe any actions taken to address the incident.
* **Product Image**: Attach an image of the product affected by the incident.
* **Incident Image**: Attach an image of the incident or the product in question.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers to each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to the "Incident Details" section for guidance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
