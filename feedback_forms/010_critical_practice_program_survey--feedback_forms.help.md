# Critical Practice Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Practice Program Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants on the Critical Practice Program, a program aimed at promoting critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the Critical Practice Program Survey** (`introduction`, `text`, not required): This page welcomes you to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **Participant Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): This field is for you to enter your email address.
* **Department Name** (`department`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to enter your department name.
* **What do you think of the Critical Practice Program?** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to provide your thoughts and opinions on the program.
* **How do you rate the Critical Practice Program?** (`rating`, `number`, not required): This field is for you to rate the program on a scale.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or thoughts you have on the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, `select_one`, not required): This field allows you to select a program name from the provided options.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field allows you to select department names from the provided options.
