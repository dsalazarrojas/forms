# Eye Tracking Interaction User Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a web application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "How long did you spend on this page?" by providing a numerical value.
2. Select the navigation flow you used by choosing one of the options: A, B, or C.
3. Rate your experience by providing a score between 1 and 5.
4. Provide any suggestions for our team by typing in the text field.
5. Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
6. Enter your phone number for our records.
7. Enter the date and time you used the application.
8. Enter the time spent interacting with the application.
9. Provide any additional notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How long did you spend on this page** (interaction_time): This field is asking you to report the amount of time you spent interacting with this page. Please provide a numerical value.
* **What navigation flow did you use?** (navigation_flow): This field is asking you to select the navigation flow you used while interacting with the application. Please choose one of the options: A, B, or C.
* **How would you rate your experience?** (feedback): This field is asking you to rate your experience with the application. Please provide a score between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Do you have any suggestions for our team?** (suggestion): This is an open-ended field where you can provide any suggestions or comments you have about your experience.
* **Email** (email): This is where you enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone number** (phone): This is where you enter your phone number for our records.
* **Date** (date): This is where you enter the date you used the application.
* **Time** (time): This is where you enter the time you spent interacting with the application.
* **Notes** (notes): This is where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when answering these questions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
