# Organizational Alignment Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizational leaders and stakeholders assess the impact of the organizational strategy on various aspects of the organization, including employees, clients, and the community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand each question and its relevance to your organizational strategy.
2. Answer each question based on your honest assessment of the current situation.
3. Use the "Feedback Questions" section to provide additional comments or feedback.
4. If you have any contact information (email or phone number), please provide it for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organizational Alignment** (`strategy_alignment`, `text`, optional): This section is meant to provide an overall statement about the organizational strategy. Please describe the current alignment between the organizational strategy and the actual progress towards achieving its goals.
* **Feedback Questions** (`feedback_questions`, `select_multiple`, optional): These questions aim to gather specific feedback about the organizational strategy from your perspective. Please select the options that best fit your response to each question.
	+ **Yes** / **No** / **Maybe**: Please answer whether you have noticed the impact of the organizational strategy on employees, clients, and the community.
* **Strategy Alignment Scale** (`strategy_alignment_scale`, `select_one`, optional): This question helps to assess the current level of strategy alignment. Please select the option that best fits your assessment:
	+ **Very Strong** / **Somewhat Strong** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Weak** / **Very Weak**
* **Impact on Employees** (`impact_on_employees`, `select_one`, optional): This question measures the level of impact of the organizational strategy on employees:
	+ **Significantly Impact** / **Somewhat Impact** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Negative** / **Very Negative**
* **Impact on Our Clients** (`impact_on_our_clients`, `select_one`, optional): This question assesses the level of impact of the organizational strategy on clients:
	+ **Significantly Impact** / **Somewhat Impact** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Negative** / **Very Negative**
* **Impact on the Community** (`impact_on_the_community`, `select_one`, optional): This question measures the level of impact of the organizational strategy on the community:
	+ **Significantly Impact** / **Somewhat Impact** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Negative** / **Very Negative**
* **Strategy Alignment Impact** (`strategy_alignment_impact`, `select_one`, optional): This question helps to assess the overall impact of the organizational strategy:
	+ **Significant** / **Somewhat Significant** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Negative** / **Very Negative**
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the organizational strategy.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Strategy Alignment Frequency** (`strategy_alignment_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): This question measures how frequently the organizational strategy is being implemented:
	+ **Always** / **Mostly** / **Sometimes** / **Rarely** / **Never**
* **Strategy Alignment Importance** (`strategy_alignment_importance`, `select_one`, optional): This question assesses the importance of the organizational strategy:
	+ **Very Important** / **Somewhat Important** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Unimportant** / **Very Unimportant**
* **Strategy Alignment Level** (`strategy_alignment_level`, `select_one`, optional): This question measures the level of the organizational strategy alignment:
	+ **Senior Leadership** / **Departmental** / **Team Level** / **Individual**
* **Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): This question assesses how frequently communication about the organizational strategy occurs:
	+ **Always** / **Mostly** / **Sometimes** / **Rarely** / **Never**
* **Strategy Alignment Awareness** (`strategy_alignment_awareness`, `select_one`, optional): This question measures the level of awareness about the organizational strategy:
	+ **Very Aware** / **Somewhat Aware** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Unaware** / **Very Unaware**
* **Strategy Alignment Training** (`strategy_alignment_training`, `select_one`, optional): This question assesses whether training or support is provided for the organizational strategy:
	+ **Yes** / **No** / **Maybe**
* **Strategy Alignment Maturity** (`strategy_alignment_maturity`, `select_one`, optional): This question measures the level of strategy alignment maturity:
	+ **Mature** / **Somewhat Mature** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Immature** / **Immature**
* **Strategy Alignment Commitment** (`strategy_alignment_commitment`, `select_one`, optional): This question assesses the level of commitment to the organizational strategy:
	+ **Strongly Committed** / **Somewhat Committed** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Uncommitted** / **Uncommitted**
* **Strategy Alignment Effectiveness** (`strategy_alignment_effectiveness`, `select_one`, optional): This question measures the effectiveness of the organizational strategy:
	+ **Very Effective** / **Somewhat Effective** / **Neutral** / **Somewhat Ineffective** / **Very Ineffective**
* **Strategy Alignment Evaluation** (`strategy_alignment_evaluate`, `select_one`, optional): This question assesses how frequently the organizational strategy is evaluated:
	+ **Regularly** / **Occasional** / **Rarely** / **Never**
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question for you to provide any additional feedback about the organizational strategy.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Strategy Alignment Impact on Others** (`strategy_alignment_impact_on_other`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question for you to provide any feedback about the impact of the organizational strategy on others.
* **Strategy Alignment Impact on Me** (`strategy_alignment_impact_on_me`, `text`, optional): This is an open-ended question for you to provide any feedback about the impact of the organizational strategy on you.
