# returns_process_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experiences with the return process. Your responses will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have a genuine reason for submitting feedback.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Enter your feedback in the provided text fields.
4. Select the status of your return (Active or Inactive) from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose a date for your return.
6. Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.
7. Enter any contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Service
* **Customer Service** (customer_service, text, optional): This field is for providing any comments or concerns you have about your interaction with our customer service team.

### Return Process
* **Return Process** (return_process, text, optional): This field is for providing feedback on any issues or difficulties you experienced with the return process.

### Return Status
* **Return Status** (return_status, select_one, optional): Select the status of your return, whether Active or Inactive.

### Return Date
* **Return Date** (return_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your return.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.

### Suggest Improvements
* **Suggest Improvements** (suggestions, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving our return process, please enter them here.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your contact information here.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our return process.
