# Animal Sanctuary Visitor Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to the animal sanctuary, gathering information on their overall experience, favorite exhibits, and areas for improvement. The data collected will help the sanctuary's management make informed decisions to enhance the visitor experience and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name (optional).
2. Select the date of your visit.
3. Choose the time of your visit (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
4. Indicate the number of people in your group, including yourself.
5. Select the composition of your group (Family with Young Children, Family with Teenagers, Adults Only, etc.).
6. Confirm if this is your first visit to the sanctuary.
7. Choose how many times you've visited before (if applicable).
8. Rate the overall experience (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
9. Select your favorite exhibit or area (select all that apply).
10. If you have any concerns, mention the area that could use improvement.
11. Evaluate the care and welfare of animals (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
12. Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
13. Rate the cleanliness of the facility (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
14. Rate the clarity of signage and information (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
15. Evaluate the value for admission price (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Not Applicable).
16. If you took a guided tour, rate its quality (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
17. Rate the educational value of the visit (Learned a Great Deal, Learned Something New, Somewhat Educational, Not Very Educational, Not Educational at All).
18. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the sanctuary to others (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
19. Rate your likelihood of visiting again (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
20. Share any suggestions for improvement.
21. Finally, include any additional comments or thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Date of Visit**: Select the date of your visit using the calendar picker.
* **Time of Visit**: Choose the time of your visit (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Group Size**: Indicate the number of people in your group, including yourself.
* **Group Composition**: Select the composition of your group (Family with Young Children, Family with Teenagers, Adults Only, etc.).
* **Is This Your First Visit**: Confirm if this is your first visit to the sanctuary.
* **How Many Times Have You Visited Before**: If this is not your first visit, select the number of times you've visited before.
* **Overall Experience Rating**: Rate your overall experience (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Favorite Exhibit or Area**: Select all the exhibits or areas that you enjoyed most during your visit.
* **Area for Improvement**: If you have any concerns, mention the area that could use improvement.
* **Animal Welfare Rating**: Evaluate the care and welfare of animals (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Staff Friendliness and Helpfulness**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Facility Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the facility (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Signage Clarity**: Rate the clarity of signage and information (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Value for Admission Price**: Evaluate the value for admission price (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Not Applicable).
* **Quality of Guided Tour**: If you took a guided tour, rate its quality (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Educational Value of Visit**: Rate the educational value of the visit (Learned a Great Deal, Learned Something New, Somewhat Educational, Not Very Educational, Not Educational at All).
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the sanctuary to others (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Likelihood to Visit Again**: Rate your likelihood of visiting again (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **Improvement Suggestions**: Share any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: Include any additional comments or thoughts.
