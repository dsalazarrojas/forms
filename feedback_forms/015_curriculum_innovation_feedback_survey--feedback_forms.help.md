# Curriculum Innovation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from teachers on their experiences with curriculum initiatives, including their school name, email, and specific feedback on the initiatives. The form is designed to be completed by teachers who have attended training sessions or have used curricula provided by training providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
3. Provide a brief comment about your experience with the curriculum in the "Curriculum Initiative" field.
4. Rate your overall feedback on the initiative in the "Feedback Rating" field.
5. Select the training provider that delivered the curriculum (if applicable) in the "Training Provider" field.
6. Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on in the "Course Name" field (if applicable).
7. Optionally, add any additional comments or thoughts in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, optional): This field is not required but is an optional way to provide your name.
* **Curriculum Initiative** (`curriculum_initiative`, required): Provide a brief comment about your experience with the curriculum. Be as specific as possible and include any details that would help us understand your feedback.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, required): Select one or more of the options below to rate your feedback on the curriculum.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, optional): If you received training from a specific provider, select the one that delivered it. This will help us understand who delivered the training.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, required): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
* **Department** (`department`, optional): This field is not required but is an optional way to provide more information about the course or curriculum.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the curriculum.
