# Donor Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from donors about their experiences with the organization. Your input will help us improve our services and build a stronger bond with our donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your name, email address, and phone number on pages 1 and 2.
2. Answer the experience rating questions on page 3 (1-5) based on your overall experience with the organization.
3. On page 4, select all the methods of donation that you prefer (e.g., donations by check, online, in-person, or other).
4. On page 5, select the least favorite method of donation you prefer.
5. On page 6, type in a brief explanation of why you didn't donate, if applicable.
6. On page 7, type in a brief explanation of what we could do to improve our services, if you have any suggestions.
7. On page 8, select the date you last visited the organization.
8. On page 9, select the first interaction you had with the organization (mail, phone, in-person, or online).
9. On page 10, select all the ways in which our staff interacted with you (friendly, professional, unhelpful, or none).
10. On page 11, select one option that best describes the quality of communication you experienced with our staff (excellent, good, fair, poor).
11. On page 12, select all the ways in which we helped you (provided information, offered solutions, provided emotional support, or none).
12. On page 13, select all the ways in which we didn't help you (provided misinformation, offered unsuitable solutions, didn't offer any help, or none).
13. On page 14, select one option that best describes the helpfulness of our staff (extremely helpful, somewhat helpful, not very helpful, not at all helpful).
14. On page 15, select one option that best describes the friendliness of our staff (extremely friendly, somewhat friendly, not very friendly, not at all friendly).
15. On page 16, select one option that best describes the knowledgeability of our staff (extremely knowledgeable, somewhat knowledgeable, not very knowledgeable, not at all knowledgeable).
16. On page 17, select one option that best describes whether you would recommend our organization to others (true or false).
17. Finally, on page 18, if you have any additional comments or suggestions on why you wouldn't recommend us, type them in.
