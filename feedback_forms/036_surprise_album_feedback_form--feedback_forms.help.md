# Surprise Album Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Surprise Album Feedback Form is designed to collect user feedback on a surprise album. The form helps us understand how users experience and perceive the album, allowing us to improve future album surprises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the link provided by the surprise team to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with your feedback on the album.
3. Make sure to answer all the questions that apply to you.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What's The Album Title?** (`album_title`, `text`, Optional): This field is for the album title you received as a surprise. Try to enter the actual title of the album as it appears on the packaging or digital copy.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, Optional): If you know the release date of the album, please enter it here. This helps us understand the album's release timeline.
* **Rate The Album** (`rating`, `number`, Optional): Rate your overall experience with the album on a scale of 1-5.
* **Favorite Track** (`favorite_track`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your favorite track from the album's tracklist. If you don't have a favorite track, you can leave this field blank.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, Optional): Share any comments or thoughts you have about the album. This helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to be included in future album surprise updates, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, Optional): Please review your answers and make sure everything is accurate. Then, click the submit button to send us your feedback.
