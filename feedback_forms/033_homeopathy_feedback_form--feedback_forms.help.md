# Homeopathy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users regarding homeopathic treatments. It is meant to be a straightforward and user-friendly way to gather valuable insights and opinions from clients, helping our team to improve and refine our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2. Select the overall rating of your experience with our homeopathic services.
3. Provide your feedback about your experience in the text field.
4. If applicable, provide comments about your experience.
5. Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or further discussion.
6. Enter your name for identification purposes.
7. Enter your contact number for easier communication.
8. Enter the date of your treatment.
9. Select the time of your treatment.
10. Select the type of treatment you received (e.g., Acupuncture, Homeopathy, or Herbalism).
11. Choose the dosage of treatment you received (if applicable).
12. Indicate whether you'd like to be followed up on regarding your treatment (True or False).
13. If you'd like to be followed up on, provide additional details in this field.
14. Confirm if you've been assigned to a specific person for your treatment (Yes or No).
15. Select the tool used for your treatment (e.g., chatjimmy, chat bobby, or chat jimmy).
15. Select the date of assignment (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Rating (1)** (`overall_rating`, number, required): A rating to express your overall satisfaction with our homeopathic services. Please select a rating between 1 and 5.
- **Feedback (2)** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts about your experience with our homeopathic services.
- **Comments (3)** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or observations about your experience, please share them here.
- **Email (4)** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or follow-up, please enter your email address.
- **Name (5)** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name for identification purposes.
- **Contact Number (6)** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for easier communication.
- **Date of Treatment (7)** (`date_of_treatment`, date, optional): The date of your homeopathic treatment.
- **Time of Treatment (8)** (`time_of_treatment`, time, optional): The time of your homeopathic treatment.
- **Type of Treatment (9)** (`type_of_treatment`, select_one, required): Select the type of homeopathic treatment you received (e.g., Acupuncture, Homeopathy, or Herbalism).
- **Dosage (10)** (`dosage_of_treatment`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, choose the dosage of your homeopathic treatment (e.g., 30 minutes, 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 6 months).
- **Follow up (11)** (`follow_up`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you'd like to be followed up on regarding your treatment (True or False).
- **Follow up Details (12)** (`follow_up_details`, text, optional): If you'd like to be followed up on, please provide additional details here.
- **Assigned to (13)** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Confirm if you've been assigned to a specific person for your treatment (Yes or No).
- **Assigned Tool (14)** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool used for your treatment (e.g., chatjimmy, chat bobby, or chat jimmy).
- **Assigned Date (15)** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): If you were assigned to a specific person or tool, please enter the date of assignment (if applicable).
