# Remediation Services Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to rate and provide feedback on our remediation services. We value your input and will use this information to continuously improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the rating question (page 1) by selecting a number between 3 and 4.
2. Add a comment (page 2) about your experience with our remediation services.
3. Enter your name (page 3) to ensure we have a record of who is providing feedback.
4. Enter your email address (page 4) to enable us to contact you if necessary.
5. If you have a contact number (page 5), please enter it so we can reach out to you.
6. Select your rating (page 6) by choosing between 3 and 4.
7. Answer the multiple-choice question (page 7) by selecting one or more options.
8. Answer the select-one question (page 8) by choosing one option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with our remediation services on a scale of 3-4.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `note`, required: false): Please provide any feedback or comments about your experience with our remediation services.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name to ensure we have a record of who is providing feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a contact number, please enter it so we can reach out to you.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with our remediation services on a scale of 3-4.
* **Multiple Choice Question** (`question_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more options that best describe your experience.
* **Select-One Question** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one option that best describes your experience.
