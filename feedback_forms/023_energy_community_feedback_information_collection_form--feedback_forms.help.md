# Energy Community Feedback Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from energy community members to help us understand their experiences and opinions about our initiatives and improve our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Member Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Email address if you have one (this will help us contact you for further information if needed).
3. Choose a specific energy community initiative that you would like to provide feedback on (Community Solar, Community Wind, Community Hydro).
4. Type your comments about your experience with the selected initiative.
5. Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
6. Enter the date of your experience with the initiative.
7. Provide the time of your experience with the initiative.
8. Describe the location where you had your experience with the initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Name
• **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, false): Your name or identifier, which will help us identify you as a contributor to the energy community.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, false): Your email address, if you have one, which will help us contact you for further information if needed.

### Initiative
• **initiative** (`initiative`, select_one, false): Select a specific energy community initiative (Community Solar, Community Wind, Community Hydro) that you would like to provide feedback on.

### Comments
• **Comments** (`comments`, text, false): Write your comments and suggestions about your experience with the selected initiative.

### Rating
• **Rating** (`rating`, number, false): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Date
• **Date** (`date`, date, false): Enter the date of your experience with the initiative.

### Time
• **Time** (`time`, time, false): Enter the time of your experience with the initiative.

### Location
• **Location** (`location`, text, false): Describe the location where you had your experience with the initiative.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* If you have any questions or need help, you can contact us at [insert contact information].
