# Summer Camp Feedback Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents who have attended summer camp. Please take a few minutes to provide your honest feedback, and help us improve the camp experience for future campers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of feedback you would like to provide: Overall rating, Comments, or a combination of both.
2. Fill out the relevant fields based on your selection.
3. Once you've completed the form, submit it to ensure your feedback is received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the camp?** (`summer_camp_feedback_form_for_parents`, text, optional): A text field where you can provide your overall thoughts on the summer camp experience. Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your perspective.
* **Overall rating** (`summer_camp_rating`, number, optional): If you've selected to provide an overall rating, please rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments** (`summer_camp_comments`, text, optional): If you've selected to provide comments, use this field to share any additional feedback or thoughts about your experience.
* **First name** (`parent_first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name for your reference and for us to know who submitted the feedback.
* **Last name** (`parent_last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name for your reference and for us to know who submitted the feedback.
* **Camper name** (`camper_name`, text, optional): If your child was at the camp, please enter their name for reference and to help us understand their experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to receive any updates or communications from us, please enter your email address here. This will also help us get in touch with you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number for reference and in case we need to contact you.
* **Program type** (`summer_camp_program_type`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of camp program your child attended (Day Camp, Overnight Camp, or Both).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you'd like to specify a date for your feedback, please enter the date you attended the camp.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you'd like to specify a time for your feedback, please enter the time you attended the camp.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): You can use this field for any additional information or notes about your experience.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, optional): If you'd like to add a signature to confirm your feedback, please do so here.
* **Summer Camp Feedback Form For Parents Comments** (`summer_camp_feedback_form_for_parents_comments`, note, optional): Please use this field for any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
