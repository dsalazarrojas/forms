# Values Review Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on various aspects of our service, including values and preferences. The purpose of this form is to help us understand your opinions and experiences, which will inform our future development and improvement of our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your employee ID in the "employee id" field (if applicable).
4. Select your choice for "value1" from the dropdown menu.
5. Select multiple values for "value2" by checking the checkboxes next to each option.
6. Fill in the text for "value3" if you have anything to share.
7. For "value4", select a date from the calendar picker.
8. For "value5", select a time from the time picker.
9. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "value6" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "value7" field (if applicable).
11. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (`first_name`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **last name** (`last_name`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **employee id** (`employee_id`, optional): Enter your employee ID (if you have one).
* **value1** (`value1`, required): Select one of the options to express your opinion on our service.
* **value2** (`value2`, required): Select multiple options by checking the boxes next to each to express your preferences.
* **value3** (`value3`, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **value4** (`value4`, optional): Select a date from the calendar picker.
* **value5** (`value5`, optional): Select a time from the time picker.
* **value6** (`value6`, optional): Type any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **value7** (`value7`, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **value8** (`value8`, optional): Enter any additional text or information you want to share.
* **value9** (`value9`, optional): Enter any further comments or feedback.

Note: This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to complete. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
