# HYROX_Event_Feedback_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants in the HYROX event, aiming to understand their satisfaction and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on the "Overall Satisfaction" page.
2. Provide feedback on the event location on the "Event Location" page.
3. If applicable, rate your satisfaction with the event organization on the "Event Organization" page.
4. Enter the date of the event on the "Event Date" page.
5. Provide feedback on the event time slot on the "Event Time" page.
6. Enter your contact number on the "Contact Number" page.
7. Enter your participant name on the "Participant Name" page.
8. Provide any general feedback comments on the "Feedback Comments" page.
9. If applicable, rate your satisfaction with an additional aspect of the event on the "Participant Satisfaction3" page.
10. Provide feedback on whether the event location was available on the "Event Location2" page.
11. If necessary, enter the time slot of the event on the "Event Time Slot" page.
12. Finally, enter any improvement suggestions on the "Improvement Suggestions" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (1)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, using a numerical scale.
* **Event Location (2)**: Provide feedback on the event location, selecting one or multiple options from the list.
* **Event Organization (3)**: Rate your satisfaction with the event organization, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Event Date (4)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time (5)**: Enter the time slot of the event.
* **Contact Number (6)**: Enter your contact number for any follow-up communication.
* **Participant Name (7)**: Enter your participant name.
* **Feedback Comments (8)**: Provide any general feedback comments on the event.
* **Event Location2 (9)**: Select whether the event location was available, if applicable. **Please note that this field is a duplicate and should not be filled in.**
* **Participant Satisfaction3 (10)**: Rate your satisfaction with an additional aspect of the event, if applicable. This field is optional.
* **Event Time Slot (11)**: Enter the time slot of the event, if necessary.
* **Improvement Suggestions (12)**: Enter any improvement suggestions you may have for the event.
