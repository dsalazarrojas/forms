# faq_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The faq_submission_form is designed to collect feedback, questions, and suggestions from users. This form serves as a channel for users to share their experiences, report issues, and propose ideas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit your feedback, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to the "Category" question, indicating whether your submission falls under a specific category (e.g., bug, feature request, or other).
2. Provide a brief "Subject" of your submission, giving a concise summary of your query or issue.
3. Write a detailed "Content" describing your feedback, question, or suggestion in as much detail as possible.
4. Enter your "Email" address, which we'll use to contact you for further clarification or follow-up discussions (if required).
5. (Optional) If applicable, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field for additional contact details.
6. Add any relevant attachments or files by selecting up to six "File 1" through "File 6" fields (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: "Yes" or "No" to specify the category of your submission.
* **Subject**: A brief description of your query or issue.
* **Content**: A detailed description of your feedback, question, or suggestion.
* **Email**: Your contact email address for follow-up discussions.
* **Phone**: (Optional) Your phone number for additional contact details.
* **File 1** through **File 6**: (Optional) Attach relevant files or documents to support your submission.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible in your "Content" field to help us understand your issue or suggestion.
- Ensure you enter a valid "Email" address so we can contact you for further clarification or discussion.
- Only attach files if they are relevant to your submission; excessive file uploads may delay processing.
- If you're reporting an issue, please check if it's already addressed in our FAQs or previous submissions before submitting.
