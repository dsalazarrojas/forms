# Hybrid Work Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hybrid Work Satisfaction Poll is designed to gauge employee satisfaction with the hybrid work environment. Your responses will help us understand your experience and preferences, allowing us to make informed decisions to improve your work life.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Use the options provided for each question.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What is your current employee experience like?** (`question1`, text, optional)
This question is an open-ended text field where you can describe your current experience as an employee. Please share your thoughts and feelings about your work environment.

### **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with our organization?** (`question2`, number, optional)
Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 10 is "very satisfied".

### **How satisfied are you with our hybrid work environment?** (`question3`, select_one, optional)
Choose one of the following options:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'
* Other

### **Do you feel that your work-life balance is currently adequate?** (`question4`, checkbox, optional)
Check one of the following:
* 'True'
* 'False'

### **How many hours a week do you work on your most time-consuming tasks?** (`question5`, time, optional)
Enter the number of hours you work per week on your most time-consuming tasks.

### **How satisfied are you with your work overall?** (`question6`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose all that apply:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'
* Other

### **Which of the following best describes your experience with our hybrid work environment?** (`question7`, select_one, optional)
Choose one of the following:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'
* Other

### **Do you feel that your time commitment is reasonable?** (`question8`, radio, optional)
Choose one:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'
* Other

### **Do you feel that our hybrid work environment supports your work-life balance?** (`question9`, checkbox, optional)
Check one of the following:
* 'True'
* 'False'

### **What is your work satisfaction level?** (`question10`, number, optional)
Enter a number to indicate your level of satisfaction, on a scale of 1-10.

### **How satisfied are you with our work environment?** (`question11`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose all that apply:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'
* Other

### **Question12** (`question12`, select_one, optional)
Choose one of the following:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'
* Other

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, feel free to include them in the space provided.
* Your responses will help us understand your experience and make informed decisions to improve your work life.
