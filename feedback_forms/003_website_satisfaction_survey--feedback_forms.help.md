# Website Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback on their experience with our website. Your input will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

* Provide your honest feedback in the "User Feedback" field (id: 1).
* Choose a rating from 1 to 5 in the "Rate Our Site" field (id: 2).
* Type any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement in the "What would you like to improve?" field (id: 3).
* If you have any additional suggestions, type them in the "Do you have suggestions?" field (id: 4).
* Select one or more options from "Email", "Phone", or "Other" in the "Contact us" field (id: 6).
* If you selected "Other" in the previous field, type your preferred contact method in the "Other contact method" field (id: 7).
* Rate your satisfaction level with our website on a scale of 1 to 5 in the "How satisfied are you with our website?" field (id: 8).
* Select how easy or difficult it was to navigate on our website from "Very easy", "Somewhat easy", "Neutral", "Somewhat difficult", or "Very difficult" in the "How easy was it to navigate on our website?" field (id: 9).
* If you have any additional comments on website usability, type them in the "Do you have any comments on website usability?" field (id: 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Feedback (id: 1)**: Provide your honest feedback on your experience with our website.
* **Rate Our Site (id: 2)**: Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your satisfaction level.
* **What would you like to improve? (id: 3)**: Type any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
* **Do you have suggestions? (id: 4)**: If you have any additional suggestions, type them here.
* **Contact us (id: 6)**: Select one or more options for how you would like to contact us ("Email", "Phone", or "Other").
* **Other contact method (id: 7)**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, type your preferred contact method.
* **How satisfied are you with our website? (id: 8)**: Rate your satisfaction level with our website on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **How easy was it to navigate on our website? (id: 9)**: Select how easy or difficult it was to navigate on our website from "Very easy", "Somewhat easy", "Neutral", "Somewhat difficult", or "Very difficult".
* **Do you have any comments on website usability? (id: 10)**: If you have any additional comments on website usability, type them here.
