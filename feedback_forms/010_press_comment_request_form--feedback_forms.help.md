# press_comment_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for press and media outlets to provide comments, feedback, or other media-related information. It is not required, but please fill it out to help us better understand your concerns or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `form_title` field with a brief description of your comment or feedback.
2. Enter your `name` in the next field.
3. Provide your `email` address to help us contact you if needed.
4. Enter your comment or feedback in the `comment` field.
5. If you are a media outlet, select your `category` from the dropdown menu (e.g., Feedback Forms, Press Releases, or News Articles).
6. If you have a media logo, enter it in the `media_logo` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title**: A brief description of your comment or feedback.
* **name**: Your name or username for contact purposes.
* **email**: Your email address for us to contact you if needed.
* **comment**: A detailed comment or feedback about our services or media-related information.
* **category**: The type of media you represent (e.g., Feedback Forms, Press Releases, or News Articles).
* **media_name**: Not required, but if you have a specific name for your media outlet, enter it here.
* **media_url**: Not required, but if you have a specific URL for your media outlet, enter it here.
* **media_logo**: Optional media logo (if you have one).
