# Hospice Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospice Feedback Form is designed to collect patient feedback on hospice care services provided during their stay. Your responses will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page with the relevant information (Patient Info, Feedback, or Hospice Info).
2. Fill in all the required fields (marked as "Required" below).
3. Provide as much detail as possible to help us better serve you.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info
* **Patient ID** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name or ID number as used by our team for your care sessions.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`date`, required): Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.
* **Session ID** (`text`, required): Enter the ID number assigned to your care session.

### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`text`, `note`, required): Share your thoughts on your hospice care experience. Be as detailed as possible, so we can identify areas of improvement.

### Hospice Info
* **Hospice ID** (`text`, required): Enter the ID number assigned to your hospice care team.
* **Hospice Date of Service** (`date`, required): Enter the start date of your hospice care service.
* **Care Location** (`text`, required): Enter the location of your care, such as a specific room or facility.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed in your feedback, as it will help us improve our services.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
- Your feedback is a valuable tool in our continuous improvement process.
