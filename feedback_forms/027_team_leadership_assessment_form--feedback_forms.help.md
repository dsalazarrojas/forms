# Team Leadership Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Leadership Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the skills and qualities that team leaders should possess to effectively lead their teams. The form aims to identify strengths and areas for improvement, enabling teams to work more efficiently and harmoniously.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand each question before starting.
2. Select the options that best describe your team's leadership style, communication approach, and performance indicators.
3. Fill in all the required fields, as marked by a asterisk (\*) symbol.
4. For multiple-choice fields, you can select more than one option.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Leadership Style** (`team_leadership_1`, select_one): This field assesses the type of leadership style exhibited by your team. Select the leadership style that best fits your team's behavior.
* **Team Communication** (`team_leadership_2`, select_multiple): This field evaluates the team's communication style, including teamwork, collaboration, and communication aspects.
* **Team Adaptability** (`team_leadership_3`, select_one): This field assesses the team's adaptability and ability to adapt to changing situations.
* **Team Performance** (`team_leadership_4`, select_multiple): This field examines the team's overall performance, including teamwork, trust, adaptability, communication, and time management.
* **Time Management** (`team_leadership_5`, select_one): This field evaluates the team's time management skills and ability to prioritize tasks.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`team_leadership_6`, select_one): This field identifies areas where your team needs improvement, either in terms of performance or other aspects.
* **Team Leadership Skills** (`team_leadership_7`, select_multiple): This field assesses the team's leadership skills, including leadership, communication, problem-solving, time management, and adaptability.
* **Teamwork** (`team_leadership_8`, select_multiple): This field evaluates the team's ability to work together effectively, including teamwork, trust, adaptability, communication, and time management.
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_leadership_9`, select_multiple): This field assesses the team's collaboration and teamwork aspects, including teamwork, collaboration, and communication.
* **Team Performance Indicators** (`team_leadership_10`, select_multiple): This field examines the team's performance indicators, including teamwork, trust, adaptability, communication, and time management.

Note: Make sure to review your answers carefully, as the form's results will reflect the collective input of the team members. If you're unsure about any field, consult with a team member or supervisor for guidance.
