# Nail Salon Customer Retention Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather insights from customers about their experience with our nail salon, helping us to improve our services and customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field to ensure you understand the question being asked.
2. Select your answers based on your experience with our nail salon.
3. Fill in any required fields with accurate information.
4. Submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, Required: False): This section is a brief introduction to the survey.
* **Satisfaction with Services** (`Satisfaction with Services`, `select_one`, Required: False): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the services we provided at our nail salon. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Slightly Below Average, or Poor.
* **Satisfaction with Staff** (`Satisfaction with Staff`, `select_one`, Required: False): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the staff we provided at our nail salon. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Slightly Below Average, or Poor.
* **Visit Habits** (`Visit Habits`, `select_multiple`, Required: False): This question asks about your usual visit habits at our nail salon. Select all that apply from the options: For nail care, For nail art, For nail extensions, or For other.
* **Loyalty Program** (`Loyalty Program`, `select_one`, Required: False): This question asks if you participate in our loyalty program. Select True if you do, False if you don't.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `note`, Required: False): This section is for you to provide any additional feedback about your experience with our nail salon.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, Required: False): If you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, Required: False): Please enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, Required: False): If you would like to be contacted for further information or to receive special offers, please enter your contact number.
