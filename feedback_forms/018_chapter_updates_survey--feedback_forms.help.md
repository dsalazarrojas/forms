# Chapter Updates Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Chapter Updates Survey" is a form designed to gather information about updates within the chapter. It's essential to complete this form to inform members about the latest activities, needs, and announcements. By filling out this form, you'll be providing valuable insights to help chapter members stay informed and involved.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reviewing the form sections and familiarize yourself with each field.
2.  Scroll down to the desired section and fill in the corresponding field.
3.  Ensure you've completed all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Chapter Updates --** (`header`, note, required: false): This is an introductory section to provide a brief overview of the updates. You can add any relevant information, such as chapter activities, needs, or announcements.
*   **update\_title** (`update_title`, text, required: true): Enter a brief headline for the update. This field should concisely summarize the update's main topic.
*   **update\_summary** (`update_summary`, text, required: true): Provide a short description of the update, elaborating on the update\_title field. This should give readers an idea of what's happening within the chapter.
*   **impacted\_members** (`impacted_members`, select\_multiple, required: false): Select the members affected by this update. You can choose from the following options:
    *   All members
    *   Leadership
    *   Volunteers
    *   Specific committee
*   **urgency\_level** (`urgency_level`, select\_one, required: true): Indicate the priority level of the update. Choose from the following options:
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
*   **planned\_action** (`planned_action`, text, required: false): Describe what will be done in response to this update. If there's no action planned, simply type "No action" or "None."
*   **requested\_support** (`requested_support`, text, required: false): Specify any assistance required from members or leadership. If none is needed, type "None."
*   **follow\_up\_contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the person to contact for more information about the update.
*   **update\_date** (`update_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date the update was submitted.
*   **attachments** (`attachments`, text, required: false): Attach any supporting documents related to the update.

## Tips

*   Always ensure you've reviewed the form carefully before submitting it.
*   Double-check the required fields to avoid missing any essential information.
*   You can add attachments to support your update with more information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the chapter's leadership or support team for assistance.
