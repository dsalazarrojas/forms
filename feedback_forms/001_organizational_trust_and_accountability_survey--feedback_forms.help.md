# Organizational Trust And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gauge the level of trust and accountability within the organization, gathering input on various aspects of the work environment and processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin with the "Introduction" page, where you can read about the purpose and importance of the survey.
2. Answer the questions on the subsequent pages, providing honest and thoughtful responses.
3. Select the appropriate options for each question, using the provided choices.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, indicated by a red star (*) symbol.
5. Click "Submit" to send the form, or "Cancel" to discard your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Page 1, Text): This page provides a brief overview of the survey's purpose and objectives.
* **Trust** (Page 2, Text): Please describe the level of trust you have in the organization's leadership, policies, and procedures.
* **Accountability** (Page 3, Text): Share your thoughts on the sense of accountability within the organization, including how individuals are held responsible for their actions and decisions.
* **Leadership** (Page 4, Text): Comment on the leadership's role in fostering a positive work environment, including their communication and example-setting.
* **Communication** (Page 5, Select Multiple): Indicate whether you have sufficient communication channels within the organization to address concerns and receive feedback.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Feedback** (Page 6, Text): Share any additional feedback or suggestions you may have for improving the organization.
* **Submission** (Page 7, Time): Enter the date and time you are submitting this form.
* **Submission Date** (Page 8, Date): Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Email** (Page 9, Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Page 10, Text): Enter your phone number for follow-up or additional contact.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as your input will contribute to the betterment of the organization.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
- Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to ask for assistance if needed.
