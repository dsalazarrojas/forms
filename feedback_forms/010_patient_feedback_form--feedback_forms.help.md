# Patient Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients about their experience with the clinic. This feedback will help us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are providing accurate and clear feedback.
2. Fill out the form completely and honestly to help us understand your experience.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your overall experience with the clinic?** (`how_would_you_rate_your_overall_experience`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to rate your overall experience with the clinic on a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 means you were not satisfied and 10 means you were very satisfied.
* **How would you rate the interaction with staff?** (`staff_interaction`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to rate the interaction with our staff on a scale from 0 to 10, where 0 means you were not satisfied and 10 means you were very satisfied.
* **Waiting Time** (`waiting_time`, time, required: false)
	+ This question asks you to estimate how much time you had to wait before or after your appointment.
* **Appointment Schedule** (`appointment_schedule`, date, required: false)
	+ This question asks for the date of your appointment.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
	+ This text field is for any additional feedback or comments you would like to share about your experience with the clinic.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false)
	+ This text field is for any additional information you would like to share about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	+ This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for your contact phone number.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, note, required: false)
	+ This field is not actively used, you may leave it empty.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false)
	+ This field is not actively used, you may leave it empty.
