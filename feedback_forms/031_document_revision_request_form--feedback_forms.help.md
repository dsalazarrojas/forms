# document_revision_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to request a document revision or update. It allows users to provide information about the document they are requesting to be revised, including whether the revision is required, the date of the request, and any comments or attachments they may need to include with their request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if a document revision is requested. This will help us understand your needs and prioritize the revision accordingly.
2. Provide the date you are requesting the document revision for in the "date_of_revision_request" field. This is the date you would like the revised document delivered by.
3. Enter any comments or additional information you want to provide with your request in the "comments" field.
4. If you are attaching any files to support your request, select "Yes" or "No" in the "file_type" field, indicating whether you are including attachments.
5. If you have attached files, enter the name(s) of the file(s) in the "file_name" field(s).
6. Enter the URL(s) of the attached file(s) in the "file_url" field(s).
7. Enter the date(s) of the attached file(s) in the "file_date" field(s).
8. If you are including tags with the attached file(s), select "Yes" or "No" in the "file_tags" field, indicating whether tags are included.
9. Enter a brief description of the attachment(s) in the "file_description" field.
10. If you want to provide further information on the tags attached, enter it in the "file_tags_note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Request**: <code>Yes/No</code> - Select "Yes" if you are requesting a document revision, otherwise, select "No".
* **Document Revision Request**: <code>text</code> - Enter the specific document you are requesting to be revised.
* **Date of Revision Request**: <code>date</code> - Enter the date you would like to receive the revised document by.
* **Comments**: <code>text</code> - Enter any additional information you want to provide with your request.
* **Uploads**: <code>Yes/No</code> - Select "Yes" if you are attaching any files, otherwise, select "No".
* **Uploads Note**: <code>text</code> - Enter any notes or comments about the attachments.
* **File Name**: <code>text</code> - Enter the name(s) of the attached file(s).
* **File URL**: <code>text</code> - Enter the URL(s) of the attached file(s).
* **File Date**: <code>text</code> - Enter the date(s) of the attached file(s).
* **File Type**: <code>Yes/No</code> - Select "Yes" if you are including attachments, otherwise, select "No".
* **File Size**: <code>number</code> - Enter the size of the attached file(s) in bytes or kilobytes.
* **File Version**: <code>text</code> - Enter the version of the attached file(s).
* **File Description**: <code>text</code> - Enter a brief description of the attachment.
* **File Tags**: <code>Yes/No</code> - Select "Yes" if you are including tags with the attachments, otherwise, select "No".
* **File Tags Note**: <code>text</code> - Enter any additional information about the tags attached.
