# Allegation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Allegation Form is used to collect detailed information about customer complaints and allegations of misconduct within the company. This information is used to investigate and address the issue efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your complaint ID, submission date, name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the complaint: Choose the type of allegation, describe the incident, and indicate any witnesses or evidence available.
3. Describe the impact: Explain how the incident affected you and what resolution you desire.
4. Set expectations: Choose your preferred contact method and indicate when you can be reached.
5. Confirm your signature: Ensure that you certify the information as accurate and true.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint ID** (`complaint_id`): Enter the unique identifier assigned to your complaint.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`): Enter the date you submitted this complaint.
* **Your Name** (`complainant_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or official records.
* **Email Address** (`complainant_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`complainant_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`complainant_address`): Enter your full mailing address (optional but recommended for correspondence).
* **Customer Type** (`customer_type`): Select your relationship to the company (e.g., current customer, former customer, etc.).
* **Type of Allegation** (`allegation_type`): Choose the category of your complaint (e.g., product quality, service issue, etc.).
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`): Enter the approximate time of the incident (optional).
* **Location of Incident** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Employees Involved** (`employees_involved`): List the names of employees involved (if applicable).
* **Department Involved** (`department_involved`): List the department(s) involved (if applicable).
* **Detailed Description** (`detailed_description`): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Were There Witnesses?** (`witnesses_present`): Indicate if there were any witnesses to the incident.
* **Witness Names** (`witness_names`): List the names of witnesses (if any).
* **Evidence Available** (`evidence_available`): Indicate if you have any evidence, such as documents, photos, or videos.
* **Evidence Description** (`evidence_description`): Describe the evidence you have (if any).
* **Previous Complaints Filed** (`previous_complaints`): Indicate if you have filed previous complaints (with the same or different issues).
* **How Has This Affected You?** (`impact_description`): Describe how the incident has affected you.
* **Desired Resolution** (`desired_resolution`): Describe the resolution you desire for this issue.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`): Choose the urgency level of your complaint (e.g., critical, high, medium, low).
* **Consent to Investigate** (`consent_to_investigate`): Indicate if you consent to an investigation.
* **Confidentiality Request** (`confidentiality_request`): Indicate if you want to keep your identity confidential.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose your preferred method of contact.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`): Enter the best time for us to contact you (optional).
* **Considering Legal Action?** (`legal_action_consideration`): Indicate if you are considering legal action (optional).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information you want to provide.
* **Signature Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`): Ensure that you certify the information as accurate and true.

Note: Fields marked as optional may be skipped if not relevant to your complaint.
