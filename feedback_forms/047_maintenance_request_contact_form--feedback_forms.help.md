# Maintenance Request Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users for maintenance requests, allowing you to address their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "User name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Contact email" field.
3. Select the type of request you have by choosing from the options in the "Request type" field.
4. Optionally, provide a description of your request in the "Description" field.
5. You may also choose to attach any relevant files to your request using the "Attachments" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User name** (`user_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name for reference.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Request type** (`request_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose from the options:
	+ Maintenance
	+ Technical Support
	+ Other
	+ Billing
	+ Product/Service
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, **optional**): Optionally, describe the maintenance request in detail.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, **optional**): You may choose to attach relevant files to your request.
* **Note** (`note`, `text`, **optional**): A field for you to take notes, but not required for submission.
* **Attachment 2** (`attachment_2`, `text`, **optional**): Another field for attachments.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, `text`, **optional**): Another field for attachments.
* **Attachment 2** (`attachment_2`, `text`, **optional**): Yet another field for attachments.
* ... (Fields 8-25 are not necessary and can be removed.)

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure a prompt response.
* Choose the correct request type to expedite processing.
* If you have any attachments, please make sure they are relevant to your request.
