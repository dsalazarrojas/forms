# Corporate Retreat Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Corporate Retreat Feedback Survey form. Please follow these steps to provide your feedback and help us improve the corporate events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each section carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions, as they are marked as such.
3. You can skip optional sections if you don't feel they are relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the survey** (`1`)
	+ This section welcomes you to the survey and sets the tone for the rest of the form.
* **Personal Information** (`2`)
	+ This section is for you to provide any personal information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Event Information** (`3`)
	+ This section is for you to provide details about the event, such as the date, location, and any notable moments.
* **Satisfaction and Feedback** (`4`)
	+ This section is for you to rate your satisfaction with the event and provide feedback on what we can improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`5`)
	+ This section is for you to add any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share.
* **Follow-up Information** (`6`)
	+ This section is for you to provide any follow-up information, such as how you'd like to be contacted in the future.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`7`)
	+ This section is for you to rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Ratings** (`8`)
	+ This section is for you to rate specific aspects of the event, such as the speaker, food, and venue.
* **What rating would you give this event?** (`9`)
	+ This section is for you to rate the overall quality of the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **What is your rating for the event?** (`10`)
	+ This section is for you to rate the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Event Suggestions** (`11`)
	+ This section is for you to provide suggestions on how we can improve the event.
* **Final Comments** (`12`)
	+ This section is for you to add any final comments or suggestions you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is invaluable, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve the corporate events.
