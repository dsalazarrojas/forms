# Science Simulation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from students and teachers about their experience with science simulations. It aims to gather information on the effectiveness, usability, and overall experience of the simulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer all required questions on each page. While the form is not entirely required, providing feedback is optional.
2. Complete each page, and then move on to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 (science_simulation_feedback_form_page_1)
* **Page 1** (`text`, required/optional): This is the first page of the form. You can start by writing your thoughts about the science simulation experience in this field.

### Page 2 (teacher_info)
* **Teacher Information** (`text`, required/optional): Provide any relevant information about the teacher who conducted the simulation, such as their name, department, or institution.

### Page 2 (science_simulation_feedback_form_page_2)
* **Page 2** (`text`, required/optional): This is the second page of the form. Write your feedback and thoughts about the science simulation experience here.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and concise feedback about your experience with the science simulation.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, feel free to share them in the form.
* Remember, your feedback is valuable and helps us improve the science simulation experience for everyone.
