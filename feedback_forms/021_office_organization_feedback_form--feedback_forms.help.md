# Office Organization Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Office Organization Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from employees about their experience within the office organization. This form helps us understand the strengths and weaknesses of our internal structure, allowing us to make informed decisions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown list.
3. Rate your experience using the number scale.
4. Provide any feedback or suggestions in the "Feedback" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: (`employee_name`, `text`, required/optional: Optional) - Please enter your name as it appears on your employee card.
* **Department**: (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional: Optional) - Please select your department (Accounting, Human Resources, Management, or Operations) to help us understand your feedback specific to your team.
* **Rating**: (`rating`, `number`, required/optional: Optional) - Use the number scale to rate your experience with the office organization. The higher the number, the better you feel about our internal structure.
* **Feedback**: (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional: Optional) - Share any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you have about the office organization. This is a space for you to express yourself freely.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Keep your suggestions concise and easy to understand.
* We value your input and will take all feedback into consideration when making decisions for improvement.
