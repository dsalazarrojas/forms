# payroll_helpdesk_experience_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with the pay helpdesk. It is intended to help the helpdesk team understand their performance and make improvements to better serve employees. Please complete the form honestly to provide valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields as instructed and click submit. You may skip any fields that are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Response Time
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, number, required: false): This field asks how long it took to get a response from the helpdesk. If it took less than 2 minutes, select 1. If it took more than 2 minutes, select 2 or 3.

### 2. Professionalism
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, select_one, required: true): This field rates the helpfulness and politeness of the helpdesk staff. Choose Very Good if the staff was very helpful and polite. If they were slightly helpful, select Slightly Good. If they were not helpful at all, select Very Poor.

### 3. Issue Resolution
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, select_multiple, required: false): This field evaluates how well the helpdesk resolved the issue you faced. Choose Very Good if it was resolved completely to your satisfaction. If it was partially resolved, select Slightly Good. If it was not resolved, select Very Poor.

### 4. Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for your email address. Please enter your company email address.

### 5. Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is for your phone number. Please enter your work phone number.

### 6. Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): In this field, please type any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the helpdesk. This is a great place to suggest improvements or provide further details about your case.

### 7. Do we need email or phone?
* **Do we need email or phone?** (`email_phone`, select_multiple, required: false): This field helps us understand what communication channel you prefer. Choose both Email and Phone if you would like to be contacted through both. If you only prefer one, choose that option. If you do not want to be contacted at all, select None.
