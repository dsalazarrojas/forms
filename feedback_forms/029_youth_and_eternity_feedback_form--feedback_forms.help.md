# Youth And Eternity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth And Eternity Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users, helping us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the text field.
2. Rate your experience in the rating field.
3. Type any additional comments or feedback in the comments field.
4. Select your age group from the dropdown menu.
5. If you'd like to receive a confirmation email, enter your email address in the email field.
6. If you'd like to share your phone number, fill it in the phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (Text, **Optional**): Enter your name to help us know who you are.
* **rating** (Number, **Optional**): Rate your experience using the numbers 1-5.
* **comments** (Note, **Optional**): Write your thoughts or feedback about your experience.
* **age_group** (Select One, **Optional**): Select your age group from the available options.
* **email** (Email, **Optional**): Enter your email address if you'd like to receive a confirmation email.
* **phone** (Text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number if you'd like to share it with us.

Note: You can skip any fields that don't apply to you. Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and we'll do our best to improve our services based on your input!
