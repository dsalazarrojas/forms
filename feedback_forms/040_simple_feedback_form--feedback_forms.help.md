# Simple Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Simple Feedback Form is a tool for end-users to share their thoughts, opinions, and suggestions with our team. It is meant to be used for constructive feedback, not for urgent or critical issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Feedback Form**: Enter your feedback in the text area provided. This is the main area where you can express your thoughts and opinions.
2. **How can we improve?**: Select all the ways you think we can improve from the options provided. You can choose one, multiple, or none of the options that best represent your thoughts.
3. **Name**: Enter your name so we can address the feedback to you directly.
4. **Email**: If you have an email address associated with your account or wish to provide a valid contact method, enter it here. This helps us get back to you if needed.
5. **Feedback Question 2**: Choose one option that best answers this question. It is similar to the "How can we improve?" section but with a slightly different context.
6. **Message**: Type your personal message to provide more detailed feedback. This section is optional but highly appreciated.
7. **Time**: If you're providing feedback about a specific time-sensitive issue, please fill out this field. Otherwise, you can leave it empty.
8. **Date**: Enter the date when you experienced the issue, if applicable.
9. **Phone**: If you have a phone number associated with your account or wish to provide a valid contact method, enter it here. This is optional but helps us get back to you if needed.
10. **Note**: Leave any additional comments or notes in this section, if you have more information to provide.
