# Ethics Consultation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather patient feedback about their experience with the service. The collected information will help us understand their satisfaction and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your personal information and contact details in the "Patient Information", "Contact Person", and "Contact Information" fields.
2. Answer the questions in the "How did you feel about your consultation?", "What was the outcome?", and "Were any other issues discussed?" fields, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the options for the questions with Likert scale ("How likely are you to recommend our service?", "What was the reason for the delay?", "How would you rate your overall satisfaction?", "What did you find most prophets?", "How satisfied were you with the service?", "How likely are you to use our service again?", "What would you like to see improved?", "What could we do to improve the service?") by choosing the most appropriate option.
4. Provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Enter the date and time of your visit, and the date and time when providing this feedback, in the "Date of Visit", "Time of Visit", "Date of Feedback", and "Time of Feedback" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (text)**: Please enter your personal details such as name and ID number for identification purposes.
* **Contact Person (text)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person who attended the consultation with you.
* **Contact Information (text)**: Enter the contact information of the person who filled out this form (e.g. email, phone number, etc.).
* **How did you feel about your consultation? (text)**: Describe your feelings about your consultation experience.
* **What was the outcome? (text)**: Describe the outcome of your consultation.
* **Were any other issues discussed? (text)**: If yes, specify what issues were discussed.
* **Prophets Note (text)**: Enter any additional information or notes from the prophet.
* **Prophets Note 2 (text)**: Enter any additional information or notes from the prophet.
* **How likely are you to recommend our service? (select one)**: Choose how likely you are to recommend our service (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5).
* **What was the reason for the delay? (select multiple)**: Choose all applicable options from the list.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction? (select one)**: Choose how satisfied you were with the service (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5).
* **What did you find most prophets? (select multiple)**: Choose all applicable options from the list.
* **How satisfied were you with the service? (select one)**: Choose how satisfied you were with the service (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5).
* **How likely are you to use our service again? (select one)**: Choose how likely you are to use our service again (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5).
* **What would you like to see improved? (text)**: Suggest improvements to our service.
* **What could we do to improve the service? (text)**: Suggest ways we could improve our service.
* **Date of Visit (date)**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit (time)**: Enter the time of your visit.
* **Date of Feedback (date)**: Enter the date when you provided this feedback.
* **Time of Feedback (time)**: Enter the time when you provided this feedback.
* **Comments (text)**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Date of Feedback (date)**: Enter the date of your feedback.
* **Time of Feedback (time)**: Enter the time of your feedback.
* **Comments (text)**: Enter any additional comments about your experience.
* **Date of Visit (date)**: Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit (time)**: Enter the time of your visit.

Note: The form fields are labeled with the same name as in the YAML configuration, which may not be ideal for human consumption. The labels have been renamed for clarity and consistency.
