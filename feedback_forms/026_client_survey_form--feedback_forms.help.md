# Client Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experiences with our services. Your responses will help us improve our processes and offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select the service or product you used.
3. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4. Use the text fields to provide any additional comments or feedback.
5. Select the scale for your experience rating and the likeliness of recommending us.
6. Choose your response for each question, and provide a comment if desired.
7. Click submit to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, required/optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **When did you interact with us?** (`contact_date`, optional): Select the date you interacted with us.
* **What service or product did you use?** (`service_type`, required): Select the service or product you used.
* **Overall, how was your experience with us?** (`overall_experience`, required): Select how your experience was on a scale from Very Poor to Excellent.
* **Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10** (`experience_rating`, required): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **The quality of service met my expectations** (`quality_of_service`, required): Select whether the quality of service met your expectations.
* **Our staff was professional and knowledgeable** (`professionalism`, required): Select how professional our staff was.
* **Services were completed in a timely manner** (`timeliness`, required): Select whether services were completed in a timely manner.
* **Communication throughout the process was clear** (`communication_quality`, required): Select how clear the communication was.
* **Response time to questions or concerns** (`response_time`, required): Select how fast our response time was.
* **Support staff was helpful and friendly** (`support_quality`, required): Select how helpful our support staff was.
* **We provided good value for the price** (`value_for_money`, required): Select whether we provided good value for the price.
* **Pricing was transparent and no surprises** (`pricing_transparency`, optional): Select whether pricing was transparent.
* **How likely are you to recommend us to others?** (`recommend_likelihood`, required): Select on a scale from 0 to 10 how likely you are to recommend us.
* **Would you use our services again?** (`would_return`, required): Select whether you would use our services again.
* **What did we do well?** (`what_went_well`, optional): Provide additional comments on what we did well.
* **What could we improve?** (`improvements`, optional): Provide additional comments on what we could improve.
* **Additional comments or feedback** (`additional_feedback`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
