## Volunteer Service Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from volunteers after they have been discharged from service. The form aims to understand the reason for discharge, the volunteer's experience with the service, and their overall satisfaction. This information will help us improve our services and better support our volunteers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2.  Enter your **Volunteer Name**. This is the name of the volunteer who was recently discharged from service.
3.  Enter the name of the **Organization** they were working with. This is the organization where the volunteer provided their service.
4.  Enter the name of the **Program** they were participating in. This is the specific program within the organization where the volunteer worked.
5.  Enter the **Discharge Date** when the volunteer's service ended. If you're unsure, check with the volunteer or review your records.
6.  Provide a brief reason for the **Discharge Reason**. This is an opportunity to share any comments or feedback about the volunteer's experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name**: Enter the full name of the volunteer who was recently discharged from service.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization where the volunteer provided their service.
* **Program**: Enter the name of the specific program within the organization where the volunteer worked.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date when the volunteer's service ended, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Discharge Reason**: Enter a brief reason for the volunteer's discharge. This is an optional field, and you can leave it blank if not applicable.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the fields to ensure accurate feedback.
* If you need to fill out this form for multiple volunteers, feel free to print it or duplicate the fields as needed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
