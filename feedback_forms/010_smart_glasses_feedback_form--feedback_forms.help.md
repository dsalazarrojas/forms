# Smart Glasses Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Glasses Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experience with the Smart Glasses product. Your input will help us understand your satisfaction, identify areas for improvement, and provide us with suggestions for future enhancements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and fields.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the product.
4. Select all applicable features from the list that apply to your experience with the product.
5. Indicate whether you would recommend this product to others.
6. Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have.
7. Rate the product's image quality and indicate whether you experienced issues with its usability.
8. Describe your experience with the product in the feedback description field.
9. Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is not currently used in the form and is for future reference.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product on a scale of Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Any feedback or suggestions you may have for improvements.
* **Product Usability** (`product_usability`, number, required): Select a number from 1-10 to rate the product's usability.
* **Product Features** (`product_features`, select_multiple, required): Select all features that apply to your experience with the product, such as image quality, ease of use, and others.
* **Experience** (`experience`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you experienced issues with the product's image quality.
* **Product Image Quality** (`product_image_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the product's image quality as Good or Poor.
* **Would you recommend this product?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend this product to others.
* **Your Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Your Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Your Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Feedback Description** (`feedback_description`, text, required): Describe your experience with the product.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
