# Employee Annual Review Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluation on an employee's performance and development during the annual review process. It's a collaborative tool for reviewers, managers, and the employee being reviewed to assess performance, identify areas for improvement, and create a development plan for future growth and success.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the instructions and explanations carefully to understand the purpose and requirements of each section.
2. Answer each section as thoroughly and specifically as possible.
3. Be concise and avoid general comments.
4. Reviewers and managers will use this form to assess your performance and create a development plan based on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Review** (performance_review, text, required: false): This section is where you'll provide a brief summary of your performance over the past year. Be specific about your achievements and key accomplishments.
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on the strengths and areas of your performance that you feel have been particularly strong or weak.
* **Goals** (goals, text, required: false): Describe your goals and objectives for the upcoming year. Be specific about what you want to achieve and how you plan to accomplish it.
* **Areas for Improvement** (areas_for_improvement, text, required: false): Identify areas where you'd like to improve or develop skills. Be specific about what you want to improve and how you plan to address it.
* **Development Plan** (development_plan, text, required: false): Create a plan to address the areas for improvement and achieve your goals. Be specific about the steps you'll take and the resources you'll need to succeed.
