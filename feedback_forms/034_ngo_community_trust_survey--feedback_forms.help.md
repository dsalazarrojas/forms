# NGO Community Trust Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NGO Community Trust Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from the community about their trust in our services. Your input is valuable in helping us understand where we excel and where we need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information on each page.
2. Answer all questions and select all relevant options.
3. Type any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.
4. Ensure your email address is entered correctly in the "Email" field, as we may contact you for more information.
5. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Community Survey** (`community_survey`, text, optional): Please provide any general comments or feedback about the community you are a part of.
- **Service Improvements** (`service_improvements`, text, optional): Share any ideas or suggestions on how we can improve our services.
- **Feedback On Services** (`feedback_on_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
  - Yes, we have confidence in the services we receive.
  - No.
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, note, optional): If you have any additional ideas or comments, please type them in this field.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Please provide any further comments or feedback.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please be honest and open in your feedback.
- Your answers will help us improve our services and build a stronger relationship with the community.
- We value your time and will use your feedback to make informed decisions.
