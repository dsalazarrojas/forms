# hospital_care_models_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients about their care experience. By completing this form, you will be helping healthcare providers improve the care model to better serve patients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Answer all questions honestly, providing as much detail as necessary.
3. Use the provided options for select_one and select_multiple fields to choose your response.
4. You can add additional comments in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This page serves as an introduction to the survey and provides some background information about the purpose and scope of the form.

* **Hospital Care Model** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with the hospital care model. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No

* **Satisfaction with Care Team** (`3`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the care team on a scale of 1-10.

* **Satisfaction with Physical Care** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with physical care on a scale of 1-10.

* **Satisfaction with Nursing Care** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with nursing care on a scale of 1-10.

* **Satisfaction with Emergency Care** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with emergency care on a scale of 1-10.

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

* **Additional Comments** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your experience. This is your chance to provide more information about your experience.

* **Patient Care Team** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose who you interacted with during your care experience. Select all that apply:
	+ Dr. John Smith
	+ Dr. Jane Doe
	+ Nurse 1
	+ Nurse 2

Note: Fields with "required: false" are optional and you can skip them if you don't have an opinion or information to provide.
