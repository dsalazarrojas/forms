# Life Insurance Policy Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about their experience with a life insurance policy. This information will be used to improve the policy and provide better service to customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and make sure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer each field according to your experience with the policy.
3. Make sure to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Policy Rating (<type: number>):** Rate your overall experience with the policy on a scale of 1-5.
* **Policy Rating Description (<type: text>):** Describe your policy rating in detail.
* **Overall Satisfaction (<type: select_one>):** Choose your level of overall satisfaction with the policy:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Policy Recommendation (<type: select_multiple>):** Select all that apply to indicate your level of agreement or disagreement with the following statements:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Neither Agree nor Disagree
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ No Opinion
* **Additional Comments (<type: note>):** Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with the policy.
* **Email Address (<type: email>):** Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone Number (<type: text>):** Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your feedback.
* **Company Name (<type: text>):** If you are a representative of your company, enter your company name.
* **Company Website (<type: text>):** If you are a representative of your company, enter your company website.
* **Policy Name (<type: text>):** If you are providing feedback about a policy, enter the policy name.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide honest and accurate feedback.
- Please keep your comments concise and relevant.
- If you have any questions or need clarification about any field, contact us at [insert contact info].
