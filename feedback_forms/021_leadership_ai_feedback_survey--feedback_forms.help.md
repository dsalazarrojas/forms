# Leadership AI Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Leadership AI Feedback Survey is designed to gather constructive feedback from employees on the effectiveness of leadership within the organization. Your responses will help identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights to enhance leadership skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page of the survey form, which is divided into multiple pages.
2. For each page, read the label (question) and provide your honest feedback in the field below.
3. You can select multiple options from the drop-down menus where applicable.
4. You can also provide additional comments or feedback in the text field.
5. When you have completed all pages, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback on Leadership Effectiveness** (`survey_form_1`): This field is open for you to provide your honest feedback on the leadership effectiveness. Please provide your thoughts and suggestions on how the leadership team can improve.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with the leadership team's performance. Please select one of the options:
	+ High
	+ Neutral
	+ Low
* **Leadership Skills** (`leader_performance_1`, `leader_performance_2`, `leader_performance_3`): These three fields measure different aspects of leadership skills. Please rate the leader's performance on each of these skills, using numbers from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Low" and 5 is "High".
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`): This question asks about how often you provide feedback to the leadership team. Please select one of the options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Feedback Channel** (`feedback_channel`): This question asks about the most convenient way for you to provide feedback to the leadership team. Please select one of the options:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-person
* **Feedback Source** (`feedback_source`): This question asks about who you usually provide feedback to in the leadership team. Please select all that apply:
	+ Leadership team
	+ Peer
	+ Supervisor
	+ Other
* **Feedback Recipient** (`feedback_recipient`): This question asks about who you usually receive feedback from within the leadership team. Please select all that apply:
	+ CEO
	+ Leadership team
	+ Manager
	+ Other
* **Satisfaction with the Current Survey** (`survey_form_2`): This question asks about your level of satisfaction with this feedback form itself. Please select one of the options:
	+ Strongly Disagree
	+ Somewhat Disagree
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Agree
	+ Strongly Agree
