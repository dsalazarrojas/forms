# legal_client_service_experience_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The legal client service experience report form is designed to collect feedback from clients about their experiences with the legal service provided. This form helps the organization to understand the client's satisfaction and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Client Feedback**: Enter a brief description of the client's feedback or comments about the service provided.
2.  **Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the service on a scale from 1 to 3.
3.  **Service Type**: Select all the services that were provided to the client.
4.  **Description**: Provide a detailed description of the services provided, if required.
5.  **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person who interacted with the client.
6.  **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
7.  **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
8.  **Date of Service**: Enter the date when the service was provided.
9.  **Time of Service**: Enter the time when the service was provided.
10. **Follow-up**: Provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for follow-up actions.
11.  **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale from 1 to 3.
12.  **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the service.
13.  **Assigned Tool**: (Optional) Enter any tools or resources used to provide the service.
14.  **Created At**: (Optional) Enter the date and time when the service was created.
15.  **Updated At**: (Optional) Enter the date and time when the form was last updated.
16.  **Submitted At**: (Optional) Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
