# Weight Loss Journey Testimonial Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weight Loss Journey Testimonial Form is designed to collect testimonials from satisfied clients who have achieved their weight loss goals through the program. Your input will help us understand your experience and the effectiveness of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with accurate and truthful information about your experience with the Weight Loss Journey program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Name
 * **Client Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. Testimonial
 * **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, `text`, required): Share your story of success with the Weight Loss Journey program. Describe your weight loss experience, including any challenges you faced and how you overcame them.

### 3. Weight Loss Goal
 * **Weight Loss Goal** (`weight_loss_goal`, `text`, required): Please describe the weight loss goal you had when you started the program. This could be a specific amount of weight you wanted to lose, a body type you aimed to achieve, or any other relevant detail.

### 4. Start Date
 * **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started the Weight Loss Journey program. Use the format `MM/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 02/01/2022).

### 5. Weight Loss Program
 * **Weight Loss Program** (`weight_loss_program`, `select_one`, required): Select the weight loss program you completed, which is:
 * Weight Loss Journey (the program you are testifying about)
 * Other (if you are testifying about a different program)
 * None (if you did not complete a weight loss program)

### 6. Share Permission
 * **Share Permission** (`share_permission`, `select_one`, required): Is it okay to share your testimonial on social media? Please select:
 * True
 * False

**Tips**

* Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* Your testimonial will help us understand your experience and improve the program for future clients. Thank you for participating!
