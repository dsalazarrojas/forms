# Healthcare Professional Wellbeing Culture Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and insights on the wellbeing culture within a healthcare professional setting. It is meant to gather opinions and experiences from healthcare professionals about their work environment, relationships, and overall wellbeing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately and honestly as you can.
2. Please note that all fields are text inputs, and you can type your answers in the spaces provided.
3. If you need help or clarification on any of the questions, you can contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. first_page**: Please share your thoughts about the overall healthcare professional wellbeing culture in your workplace.
- **2. second_page**: What are some strengths and positive aspects of your work environment?
- **3. third_page**: Can you think of some challenges or difficulties you face in your daily work? 
- **4. fourth_page**: Describe an experience or situation where you felt supported or valued in your role.
- **5. fifth_page**: Can you share a story or example about a time when you felt overwhelmed or stressed?
- **6. sixth_page**: Have you ever felt like you were able to express yourself freely in your work environment?
- **7. seventh_page**: Are there any suggestions or ideas you have for improving your work wellbeing culture?
- **8. eighth_page**: Is there anything else you'd like to add or discuss about your wellbeing culture?

## Tips
- Please keep your answers concise and clear.
- If you have a lot to say, feel free to write as much as you need.
- You can use the space provided to draw diagrams or sketch if it helps you express your thoughts.
- If you're unsure about anything, just ask!
