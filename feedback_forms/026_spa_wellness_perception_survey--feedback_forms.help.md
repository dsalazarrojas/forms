# Spa Wellness Perception Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users on their spa wellness experience, focusing on various aspects of the visit, including satisfaction rating, comments, service improvement, staff professionalism, overall experience, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the survey.
2. Select the date of your visit from the calendar.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the spa services provided.
4. Provide any comments or feedback about your visit.
5. Suggest ways to improve the service.
6. Rate the staff's professionalism.
7. Select the time of your visit.
8. Choose the wellness specialty you visited for.
9. Rate your overall experience at the spa.
10. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form and submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of your visit to the spa.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the spa services provided, on a scale.
* **Comments** (`rating_comment`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about your visit.
* **Suggestions for Service Improvement** (`service_improvement`, select_multiple, required: false): Suggest ways to improve the service provided by the spa.
* **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, required: false): Rate the staff's professionalism, with options such as Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Overall Wellness Experience** (`overall_wellness`, select_one, required: false): Rate your overall wellness experience at the spa, with options such as Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Visit** (`spa_visit`, select_one, required: false): Select the time of your visit, with options such as Morning, Afternoon, Evening, and Other.
* **Wellness Specialty** (`wellness_specialty`, select_one, required: false): Choose the wellness specialty you visited for, with options such as Massage, Yoga, Other, and Not Sure.
* **Overall Experience** (`spa_experience`, select_one, required: false): Rate your overall experience at the spa, with options such as Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor.
