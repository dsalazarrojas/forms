# Feedback On Giving And Receiving - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the giving and receiving of feedback within an organization or team, aiming to identify areas for improvement and promote a more positive and constructive feedback culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question based on your experiences and perceptions.
2. Be honest and provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please provide your best effort to answer it.
4. When finished, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form (`feedback_form`):** This is the main form that captures your overall feedback on giving and receiving feedback. Please answer this question based on your experiences and perceptions.
* **Feedback Giver (`feedback_giver`):** Please indicate whether you give feedback, and describe your role in the feedback process.
* **Feedback Receiver (`feedback_receiver`):** Please indicate whether you receive feedback, and describe your role in the feedback process.
* **Giving and Receiving (`giving_and_receiving`):** Please describe your overall experiences and perceptions on giving and receiving feedback.
* **Feedback Challenges (`feedback_challenges`):** Please list any challenges you faced while giving or receiving feedback, and describe how you overcame them.
* **Feedback Suggestions (`feedback_suggestions`):** Please provide any suggestions for improving the feedback process within your organization or team.
* **Feedback Culture (`feedback_culture`):** Please rate your overall satisfaction with the feedback culture within your organization or team.
* **Overall Experience (`overall_experience`):** Please describe your overall experience with giving and receiving feedback.
* **Give Feedback (`give_feedback`):** Please answer this question based on your willingness to give feedback. Do you give feedback?
* **Receive Feedback (`receive_feedback`):** Please answer this question based on your willingness to receive feedback. Do you receive feedback?
* **Suggest Improvement (`suggest_improvement`):** Please suggest any areas for improvement within the feedback process.
* **Challenge Feedback (`challenge`):** Please describe any challenges you faced while giving or receiving feedback.
* **Suggestion (`suggestion`):** Please provide any additional suggestions for improving the feedback process.
* **Feedback Culture Score (`feedback_culture_score`):** Please rate your overall satisfaction with the feedback culture within your organization or team.
* **Give Score (`give_score`):** Please rate your willingness to give feedback.
* **Receive Score (`receive_score`):** Please rate your willingness to receive feedback.
* **Improvement Score (`improvement_score`):** Please rate your satisfaction with areas for improvement within the feedback process.
* **Challenge Score (`challenge_score`):** Please rate the difficulties you faced while giving or receiving feedback.
* **Suggestion Score (`suggestion_score`):** Please rate your satisfaction with the feedback suggestions you provided.
* **Overall Score (`overall_score`):** Please rate your overall satisfaction with the feedback process.
* **Feedback Frequency (`feedback_frequency`):** Please rate how frequently you provide or receive feedback.
* **Feedback Type (`feedback_type`):** Please indicate whether you provide or receive feedback regularly or occasionally.
* **Feedback Channel (`feedback_channel`):** Please indicate whether you prefer to provide or receive feedback through email, in-person, or other channels.
* **Feedback Frequency Type (`feedback_frequency_type`):** Please indicate how frequently you provide or receive feedback of different types (e.g., informal, formal, anonymous).
* **Submission (`submission`):** Please indicate when you submit feedback, if at all.

Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and thank you for your valuable input!
