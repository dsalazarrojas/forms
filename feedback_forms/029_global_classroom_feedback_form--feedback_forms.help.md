# Global Classroom Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users on the Global Classroom experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information:
	* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
	* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
	* **Phone Number** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
2. Provide a message to us:
	* **Message** (`form_message`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or suggestions you have about the Global Classroom.
3. Rate your experience:
	* **Rating** (`form_rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience from 1-5.
4. Select any options that apply:
	* **Option 1** (`form_select_option1`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options that best describe your experience.
	* **Option 2** (`form_select_option2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options that apply to your experience.
5. Select a date (if applicable):
	* **Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): Select a date that is relevant to your feedback.
6. Select a time (if applicable):
	* **Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Select a time that is relevant to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number. This will help us contact you if we need more information.
* **Message**: Enter any feedback or suggestions you have about the Global Classroom.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience from 1-5.
* **Option 1**: Choose one of the options that best describe your experience.
* **Option 2**: Choose one or more of the options that apply to your experience.
* **Date**: Select a date that is relevant to your feedback.
* **Time**: Select a time that is relevant to your feedback.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as completely and honestly as possible.
* If you've already filled out a form recently, please do not submit duplicate responses.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, you can upload them as attachments with your form submission.
* We appreciate any feedback, suggestions, or comments you may have.
