# App Advertising Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with in-app advertisements. The form aims to gather information on the effectiveness, relevance, and overall quality of the advertisements displayed within the app. The data collected will help us improve the advertising experience for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Click on the form link to access the form.
* Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Select an option from the dropdown menus for the corresponding questions.
* Enter any additional comments or suggestions in the free text field if you wish.
* Click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date you noticed the ads within the app.
* **Your name or username** (`respondent_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or username for reference.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, optional): If you'd like us to contact you for further feedback or follow-up, enter your email address here.
* **App name** (`app_name`, text, required): Select the app where you saw the ad.
* **Device type** (`device_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of device you used to access the app.
* **How long have you used this app?** (`app_user_duration`, select_one, required): Select the duration you've used the app.
* **Type of advertisement seen** (`ad_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of ad you saw.
* **Frequency of seeing ads** (`ad_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you see ads within the app.
* **How relevant was the advertisement?** (`ad_relevance`, select_one, required): Choose how relevant the ad was to your interests.
* **Quality of the advertisement** (`ad_quality`, select_one, required): Choose the quality of the ad.
* **Was the ad intrusive or disruptive?** (`ad_intrusiveness`, select_one, required): Select how intrusive the ad was.
* **Ad loading and performance** (`ad_loading_speed`, select_one, required): Choose how well the ad loaded and performed.
* **Did you engage with the advertisement?** (`ad_engagement`, select_one, required): Choose how you interacted with the ad.
* **Interest in advertised product or service** (`product_interest`, select_one, required): Choose how interested you are in the advertised product or service.
* **Would you consider purchasing advertised product?** (`product_consideration`, select_one, required): Choose if you'd consider purchasing the advertised product.
* **Did the ad distract from your app experience?** (`ad_distraction`, select_one, required): Choose how much the ad distracted you.
* **Preferred ad placement in app** (`placement_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred ad placement.
* **Preferred ad frequency** (`ad_frequency_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like to see ads.
* **Suggestions for improving in-app ads** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving the ad experience.
* **Overall rating of ad experience** (`overall_ad_rating`, select_one, required): Choose your overall rating for the ad experience.
