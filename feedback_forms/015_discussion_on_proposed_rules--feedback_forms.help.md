# Discussion On Proposed Rules - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on proposed rules, which will help us refine and improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read through each field explanation below to understand its purpose and requirements.
* Fill out the form with your feedback, and don't worry if you need to refer back to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Policy Name (Policy name)** (`policy_name`, text, optional):
    This field is where you can enter the name of the policy or rule you are commenting on. Please enter the actual name of the policy, and not a summary or description.
* **Review Date Range (Review date range)** (`review_date_range`, date, optional):
    This field is where you can enter a date range to review the policy. Please format it as a date range (e.g., 01/01/2023 - 01/31/2023).
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional):
    Use this field to provide your comments or suggestions on the proposed rule. Be as detailed as you'd like, and feel free to share any additional information that might be helpful.
* **Rating Scale (Rating scale)** (`rating_scale`, select_one, required):
    This field has four rating options: 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5. Use this to rate how you think the proposed rule should be rated. A higher rating indicates a more positive view.
* **Rating Scale Label (Rating scale label)** (`rating_scale_label`, text, optional):
    This field is not required, but please use it to provide a label for your rating, e.g., "Effective," "Ineffective," or "Needs Improvement." This will help us understand your feedback more clearly.
* **Rating Value (Rating value)** (`rating_value`, number, optional):
    This field is not required, but if you want to provide a more detailed rating value, please enter a number between 1 and 5. This will help us understand the specific score you're providing.
* **Submitter Name (Submitter name)** (`submitter_name`, text, optional):
    This field is optional, but please enter your name so we can address you directly in case we need more information from you.
* **Organization (Organization)** (`organization`, text, optional):
    If you're submitting feedback on behalf of an organization, please enter its name here. This will help us understand the context and perspective of your feedback.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as you'd like in your comments and feedback.
* If you have similar feedback on a different policy, feel free to submit it separately.
* We appreciate your input and will review all feedback carefully.
