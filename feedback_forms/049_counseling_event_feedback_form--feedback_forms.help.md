# Counseling Event Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in a counseling event. Please take a moment to provide your honest evaluation of the event, and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your evaluation of the event, including any feedback or comments you may have.
2. Select an event name from the provided options (if available).
3. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as a participant.
4. Provide feedback and suggestions for future events.
5. Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale of 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Feedback** (`event_feedback`, number, required)
	* This field is used to provide an overall feedback on the event. Please type your feedback here.
### 2. **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required)
	* This field is used to identify the event you are providing feedback on.
### 3. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required)
	* This field is used to collect your name as a participant in the event.
### 4. **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required)
	* This field is used to collect your email address as a participant.
### 5. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required)
	* This field is used to provide any additional feedback or comments on the event.
### 6. **Suggested Improvements** (`suggestions`, text, optional)
	* This field is used to suggest any improvements for future events.
### 7. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
	* This field is used to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
### 8. **Rating** (`rating`, number, required)
	* This field is used to rate your overall experience at the event on a scale of 1-5.
### 9. **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required)
	* This field is used to submit your evaluation. You can also choose to cancel your evaluation at this step.
### 10. **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, not required)
	* This field is used to cancel your evaluation and leave the form without submitting it.

Note: The cancel button will not submit any data.
