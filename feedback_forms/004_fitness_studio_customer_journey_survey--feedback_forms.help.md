# Fitness Studio Customer Journey Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Studio Customer Journey Survey is a feedback form designed to help us understand your experiences with our services and facilities. Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Read the questions carefully and answer them honestly.
2. If you have any specific feedback, use the "Feedback" field at the end.
3. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you or your experience.
4. Please note that this form is optional and your responses will be kept anonymous.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief note to introduce the form and explain its purpose.
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, number, required: false): How often do you visit our studio?
	+ Select a number to indicate how many times you have visited our studio.
* **Service Experience** (`service_experience`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate your experience with our services?
	+ Excellent (we exceeded your expectations)
	+ Good (our services met your expectations)
	+ Fair (our services were average)
	+ Poor (we did not meet your expectations)
* **Staff Interaction** (`staff_interaction`, number, required: false): How would you rate your interaction with our staff?
	+ Please note that this field is not currently being used and is likely a placeholder.
* **Facilities Experience** (`facilities_experience`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate your experience with our facilities?
	+ Excellent (our facilities exceeded your expectations)
	+ Good (our facilities met your expectations)
	+ Fair (our facilities were average)
	+ Poor (our facilities did not meet your expectations)
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate your overall experience with our studio?
	+ Excellent (we exceeded your expectations)
	+ Good (our services met your expectations)
	+ Fair (our services were average)
	+ Poor (we did not meet your expectations)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Your phone number (optional).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required: false): Any specific feedback or suggestions for improvement.
