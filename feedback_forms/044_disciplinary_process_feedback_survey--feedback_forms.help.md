This form is intended to be used for providing feedback on disciplinary processes within an organization. The form will help employees and managers to document and communicate about incidents, their resolutions, and actions taken. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all relevant information is captured and that the process is completed correctly.

**disciplinary_process_feedback_survey - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is for providing feedback on incidents related to disciplinary actions taken within the organization. It helps to ensure that all relevant information is captured and that the disciplinary process is properly documented and communicated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out each section thoroughly and accurately.
2.  Make sure to review and update each field as needed.
3.  If you're unsure about any section, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`page_2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the employee involved in the incident.
*   **Department** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the department involved in the incident (e.g., Finance, HR, Marketing).
*   **Incident Description** (`page_4`, `text`, required: false): Describe the incident, including any details about what happened.
*   **Date Incident Happened** (`page_5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Time Incident Happened** (`page_6`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the incident occurred.
*   **Resolution Outcome** (`page_7`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the outcome of the disciplinary action (e.g., Discipline, Not Disciplined, Pending).
*   **Comments** (`page_8`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments about the incident or the disciplinary process.
*   **Submit** (`page_9`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions, add them here.
*   **Employee Email** (`page_10`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the employee involved.
*   **Employee Phone** (`page_11`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the employee involved.
*   **Manager Name** (`page_12`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the manager involved.
*   **Manager Email** (`page_13`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the manager involved.
*   **Manager Phone** (`page_14`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the manager involved.
*   **Departmental Action Taken** (`page_15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the departmental actions taken as a result of the incident (e.g., Disciplinary Action, Warning, No action).
*   **Follow-up Comments** (`page_16`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional follow-up comments about the incident or the disciplinary process.
*   **Next Steps** (`page_17`, `note`, required: false): Describe any next steps or follow-up actions that should be taken.

Note: All fields are optional, but filling them out accurately and thoroughly will ensure that the form is completed correctly and that all relevant information is captured.
