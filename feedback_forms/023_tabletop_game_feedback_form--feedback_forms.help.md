# Tabletop Game Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and suggestions on our Tabletop Game, helping us refine and improve the game experience, content, and overall user satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your comments, suggestions, and ratings.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the feedback field.
3. Choose a rating to indicate your level of satisfaction.
4. Enter your first and last name (if you choose to provide it).
5. Enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up discussions).
6. Select the game you played.
7. Provide the date and time you played the game.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name, if you wish to share it.
- **Rating** (`2`, `number`, required: false): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to express your satisfaction with the game.
  - 1: Very dissatisfied
  - 2: Dissatisfied
  - 3: Neutral
  - 4: Satisfied
  - 5: Very Satisfied
- **Comment** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Describe your thoughts and feelings about the game. You can share any ideas for improvement or suggestions.
- **Email** (`4`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for further discussions or updates on your feedback, please enter your email address.
- **Game Name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the game you played.
- **Feedback** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Share any detailed feedback, thoughts, and suggestions you have about the game.
- **Playtest Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you played the game, e.g., month/day/year.
- **Playtest Time** (`8`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you played the game, e.g., 09:00 or 12:30 PM.
- **Played With** (`9`, `text`, required: false): If you played with others, enter their names or a brief description of your gaming group.
