# Wireless Technology Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on wireless technology services. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us understand your experience with our wireless services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Rate your overall experience**: Enter a number from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience with our wireless services.
2. **Describe the issue**: If you are experiencing any problems with our wireless services, please provide a brief description of the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
3. **Choose a category**: Select the category that best describes the issue you are experiencing.
4. **Select your device(s)**: Choose the wireless device(s) you are experiencing issues with.
5. **Rate our wireless service**: Enter a number from 1 to 5 to rate our wireless service.
6. **Add any comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comments" field.
7. **Submit your feedback**: Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience with our wireless services, from 1 to 5.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the issue you are experiencing with our wireless services.
* **Category** (`issue_category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category that best describes the issue you are experiencing.
* **Device(s)** (`device`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the wireless device(s) you are experiencing issues with.
* **Wireless Service** (`wireless_service`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of wireless service you are experiencing issues with.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Wireless Service Rating** (`wireless_service_rating`, `number`, required): Rate our wireless service, from 1 to 5.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, `email`, optional): If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions you may have on how we can improve our wireless services.
