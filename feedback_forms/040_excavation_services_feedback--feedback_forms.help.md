# Excavation Services Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excavation Services Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with the company's excavation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your honest feedback on the excavation services you received by typing in the `feedback` text box on Page 1.
2. Next, select your rating of the service from the `rating` text box on Page 1 using the options provided.
3. In the `rating_scale` dropdown menu on Page 1, choose the corresponding rating scale that best represents your rating.
4. If you have any comments or feedback on the service, please type them in the `comments` text box on Page 1.
5. If you have any recommendations for the company on how to improve the service, please type them in the `recommendations` text box on Page 1.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback (`feedback`)
* Label: `feedback`
* Type: `text`
* Explanation: This is the text box where you can provide your honest feedback on the excavation services you received.

### Rating (`rating`)
* Label: `rating`
* Type: `text`
* Explanation: Please select your rating for the service from the options provided in this text box. Your rating will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the service.

### Rating Scale (`rating_scale`)
* Label: `rating_scale`
* Type: `select_one`
* Explanation: Choose the rating scale that best represents your rating for the service. This will help us understand the level of satisfaction you have with the service.

### Comments (`comments`)
* Label: `comments`
* Type: `text`
* Explanation: If you have any comments or feedback on the service, please type them in this text box. This will help us understand your thoughts and feelings about the service.

### Recommendations (`recommendations`)
* Label: `recommendations`
* Type: `text`
* Explanation: If you have any recommendations for the company on how to improve the service, please type them in this text box. Your input will help us make the service better for future customers.
