# Staff Training Content Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the staff training content provided to employees. Your input will help us refine and improve the content to better meet your needs and learning objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the session date of the training you attended.
2. Provide the trainer's name.
3. Indicate the location where the training took place.
4. Select the topics covered during the training (you can select multiple topics).
5. Choose the evaluation scale that best describes your experience with the content.
6. Add any evaluation comments about the content.
7. Make recommendations for future improvements.
8. Describe the outcome of the training (e.g., did you achieve the learning objectives?).
9. Finally, add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session you're evaluating.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required: false): Type the name of the trainer who delivered the training.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required: false): Indicate the location where the training took place.
* **Content Topics** (`content_topics`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the topics that were covered during the training. You can choose multiple topics.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, required: false): Choose the evaluation scale that best describes your experience with the content. Options are Scale 1, Scale 2, and Scale 3.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, note, required: false): Add any comments about the content. This is a free-text field for any thoughts or suggestions you may have.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, note, required: false): Suggest any improvements or changes you'd like to see in future training content.
* **Training Outcome** (`training_outcome`, text, required: false): Describe the outcome of the training, e.g., did you achieve the learning objectives?
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the training content or process.
