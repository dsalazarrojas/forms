# covid_19_related_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **covid_19_related_complaint_form** is a form designed to collect information about complaints related to COVID-19 incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe the incident, including what happened, where it happened, and when it happened.
3. If applicable, attach any relevant images, videos, or audio files to support your complaint.
4. Provide any additional details about your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact details
* **Contact details** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of how you can be contacted.

*   **Contact person** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person you are contacting us about.

*   **Contact email** (`email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.

*   **Contact phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you.

### 2. Incident details
*   **Incident details** (`text`, required: false): Describe the incident, including what happened, where it happened, and when it happened.

*   **Incident date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date of the incident.

*   **Incident time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time of the incident.

*   **Incident location** (`text`, required: false): Enter the location of the incident.

*   **Incident description** (`text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the incident.

*   **Incident image** (`text`, required: false): Attach an image that supports your complaint.

*   **Incident video** (`text`, required: false): Attach a video that supports your complaint.

*   **Incident audio** (`text`, required: false): Attach an audio file that supports your complaint.

### 3. Complaint details
*   **Complaint details** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your complaint.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   Ensure that you provide all required and relevant information to help us better understand and address your complaint.
*   If you have any supporting media, please attach it to the corresponding fields.
*   We will review your complaint and respond accordingly.
