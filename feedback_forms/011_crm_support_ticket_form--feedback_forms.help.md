# CRM Support Ticket Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers or users who need technical support for their CRM system.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Contact Information

- Please fill in your full name in the "Your Full Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used by our support team to contact you directly.
- Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.

### Step 2: Provide Issue Details

- Select the category of the issue from the "Issue Category" dropdown.
- Choose the priority level of the issue from the "Priority Level" dropdown.
- Enter a brief summary of the issue in the "Subject" field.
- Provide a detailed description of the issue, including steps to reproduce if applicable, in the "Detailed Description" field.
- Select the frequency of the issue from the "Frequency of Issue" dropdown.

### Step 3: Provide System Information (Optional)

- If you have a screenshot of the error, please indicate this in the "Do you have a screenshot of the error" field. If you have a screenshot, you can email it to us after submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Your Full Name** (text, required): Your full name, as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email Address** (email, required): The email address you would like us to use to contact you directly.
- **Company Name** (text, required): The name of the company you represent.
- **Issue Category** (select_one, required): Select the category of the issue, such as Login/Access, Data Sync, Reporting Error, etc.
- **Priority Level** (select_one, required): Select the priority level of the issue, such as Low - Cosmetic, Medium - Impacting Workflow, High - Urgent, etc.
- **Subject** (text, required): A brief summary of the issue.
- **Detailed Description** (text, required): A detailed description of the issue, including steps to reproduce if applicable.
- **Frequency of Issue** (select_one, required): Select the frequency of the issue, such as One-time occurrence, Intermittent, Constant/Every time.
- **CRM Instance URL** (text, required): The URL of your CRM instance, e.g. company.crm.com.
- **Browser and Version** (text, optional): The browser and version you are using.
- **Operating System** (select_one, optional): The operating system you are using, such as Windows, macOS, Linux, etc.
