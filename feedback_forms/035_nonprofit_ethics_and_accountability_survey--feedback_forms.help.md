# Nonprofit Ethics And Accountability Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from end-users on nonprofit ethics and accountability practices. Your input will help the organization better understand areas of concern and identify potential improvements to enhance their practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question according to your concerns and suggestions.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions, such as "What are your concerns about nonprofit ethics?" and "What accountability measures would you like to see implemented?"
3. Rate your overall experience with the survey using the number rating system.
4. Provide your name and email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
5. Share any additional comments or feedback in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction (Page 1)
* **Welcome to our survey!** (`title`): This is a welcome message to get you started.
### 2. What are your concerns about nonprofit ethics? (Page 2)
* **What are your concerns about nonprofit ethics?** (`ethics_practices`, `select_multiple`): This question allows you to select multiple concerns about nonprofit ethics. Choose all that apply:
	+ Trust
	+ Transparency
	+ Accountability
	+ Integrity
	+ Fairness
### 3. What accountability measures would you like to see implemented? (Page 3)
* **What accountability measures would you like to see implemented?** (`accountability_measures`, `select_multiple`): This question allows you to select multiple accountability measures you'd like to see. Choose all that apply:
	+ Financial
	+ Performance
	+ Governance
	+ Communication
	+ Conflict of Interest
### 4. What are your concerns about our organizational culture? (Page 4)
* **What are your concerns about our organizational culture?** (`organizational_culture`, `text`): Share your concerns about our organizational culture in the text field.
### 5. How would you rate your overall experience with this survey? (Page 5)
* **How would you rate your overall experience with this survey?** (`survey_experience`, `number`): Rate your experience using the number rating system.
### 6. How would you like to be contacted about your feedback? (Page 6)
* **How would you like to be contacted about your feedback?** (`contact_us`, `select_one`): Choose how you'd like to be contacted:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
	+ Online
### 7. Name (Page 7)
* **Name** (`name`, `text`): Enter your name if you'd like to be contacted.
### 8. Email (Page 8)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
### 9. Comments (Page 9)
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`): Share any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Be honest and open in your feedback to help us better understand your concerns and suggestions.
* Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our nonprofit ethics and accountability practices.
