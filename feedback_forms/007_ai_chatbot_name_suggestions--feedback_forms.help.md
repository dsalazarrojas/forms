# AI Chatbot Name Suggestions - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for suggesting names for AI chatbots that cater to specific projects. Please provide information about the chatbot, its tone and style, target markets, and your contact email for follow-up. Your input will help us create suitable names for the chatbot.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  **Submitter name (Optional)**: Enter your name if you'd like to be credited for your submission.
2.  **Project description**: Give a brief description of the chatbot and its intended audience.
3.  **Preferred tone and style**: Select the tone you'd like the chatbot's name to convey: Friendly, Professional, Playful, or Authoritative.
4.  **Naming constraints**: If there are any specific length or trademark considerations, mention them here.
5.  **Suggested names**: Provide up to five name suggestions for the chatbot.
6.  **Rationale for suggestions**: Explain why each suggested name suits the project.
7.  **Target markets or languages**: Specify the regions or languages to consider when suggesting names.
8.  **Contact email for follow-up**: Enter your email for any further communication or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Submitter name**: Optional name for credit, if you'd like to be acknowledged for your submission.
*   **Project description**: A brief description of the chatbot and its intended audience.
*   **Project description**: Same as above, this field is required to ensure we have a good understanding of the project.
*   **Preferred tone and style**: Select one of the following options: Friendly, Professional, Playful, or Authoritative.
*   **Naming constraints**: Provide any specific length or trademark considerations for the name.
*   **Suggested names**: Provide up to five name suggestions for the chatbot.
*   **Rationale for suggestions**: Briefly explain why each suggested name suits the project.
*   **Target markets or languages**: Choose one or more regions or languages to consider when suggesting names.
*   **Contact email for follow-up**: Enter your email for any further communication or questions.

## Tips
*   Please provide a clear and concise project description.
*   Ensure your tone and style selection aligns with the chatbot's purpose.
*   Suggest up to five names for the chatbot.
*   Explain why each suggested name is suitable for the project.
*   Provide your email for any follow-up communication.
