# Anonymous Question Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to submit anonymous questions to the organization, ensuring that their concerns or inquiries can be addressed in a private and confidential manner. The form is intended to provide a safe and comfortable way for users to seek guidance or clarification on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the category that best describes the type of question you are submitting (e.g., General Inquiry, HR Related, IT Support, etc.).
2. Enter a brief title for your question that captures its essence.
3. Write your question in detail, providing as much context as possible.
4. Confirm that you have provided enough context for the question to be answered accurately.
5. Select the department that you believe should address your question.
6. Indicate the level of urgency you require for a response.
7. Choose how you would like to receive the answer (e.g., email, posted FAQ, team meeting discussion).
8. Confirm that you have checked existing FAQs related to your question.
9. If necessary, provide additional context or background information that may help answer your question.
10. Confirm that you understand this submission is anonymous.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Question Type** (`question_type`, required, select_one): This field helps us understand the category of your question. Please select the option that best matches the type of question you have.
* **Question Title** (`question_title`, required, text): This field is for a brief title of your question. It should be a one-liner that summarizes the essence of your question.
* **Your Question** (`question_body`, required, text): Here, you can write your question in detail, providing as much context as possible.
* **Have you provided enough context** (`context_provided`, required, select_one): Please confirm that you have provided enough information for the question to be answered accurately.
* **Related Department** (`related_department`, optional, select_one): If you know which department should address your question, please select it. If not, it's okay to leave this field blank.
* **How urgent is your question** (`urgency`, required, select_one): Indicate the level of urgency you require for a response.
* **Preferred Response Format** (`preferred_response_format`, required, select_one): Choose how you would like to receive the answer.
* **Have you checked existing FAQs** (`similar_questions`, required, select_one): Confirm that you have reviewed available resources before submitting your question.
* **Additional Context or Background** (`additional_context`, optional, text): If necessary, provide any additional information that may help answer your question.
* **I understand this submission is anonymous** (`anonymous_confirmation`, required, select_one): Confirm that you understand that this submission is anonymous.

Please note that this form is designed to ensure a safe and private experience for users. All questions submitted will be reviewed and addressed accordingly.
