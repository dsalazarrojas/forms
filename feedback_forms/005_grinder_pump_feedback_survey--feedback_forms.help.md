# Grinder Pump Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grinder Pump Feedback Survey is designed to gather user feedback on the grinder pump. This survey will help us understand your experience with the grinder pump, ensuring that we can improve its performance and make it more efficient for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your level of satisfaction with the grinder pump from the "How satisfied are you with the grinder pump?" field.
2. In the "What issues have you experienced with the grinder pump?" field, type any problems you've encountered while using the grinder pump.
3. Answer the "How often do you run the grinder pump?" field by selecting one of the options provided.
4. In the "What suggestions do you have for improvements to the grinder pump?" field, type any ideas you may have for making the grinder pump better.
5. Please enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Your Email" field. This is not publicly displayed, so please don't worry about sharing it.
7. If you have any additional contact information, you can type it in the "What is your contact information?" field.
8. Finally, press the "Submit" button to submit your feedback. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the grinder pump?**: This field has four options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Not satisfied, and Not at all satisfied. Please select the one that best reflects your experience with the grinder pump.
* **What issues have you experienced with the grinder pump?**: This field is a text field where you can type any problems you've encountered while using the grinder pump.
* **How often do you run the grinder pump?**: This field has four options: Daily, Several times per week, Once a week, and Less than once a week. Please select the one that best describes your usage.
* **What suggestions do you have for improvements to the grinder pump?**: This field is a text field where you can type any ideas you have for making the grinder pump better.
* **Your Name**: Please enter your name so we know who is submitting the feedback.
* **Your Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion or questions.
* **What is your contact information?**: This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional contact details.
* **Submit**: Press this button to submit your feedback. 

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when describing your issues with the grinder pump.
* If you have any suggestions, please make sure they are feasible and practical.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve the grinder pump.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
