# Customer Service Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our customer service team. The goal is to understand their satisfaction and identify areas for improvement to enhance their overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have answered all the required questions.
2. For each question, provide your honest feedback about your recent experience with our customer service team.
3. Use the available space to elaborate on your answers where necessary.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your experience with our customer service?** (`customer_experience`, `text`, required: false): This question allows you to describe your overall experience with our customer service team, if you have had any recent interactions.
* **How would you rate our customer service?** (`customer_service_rating`, `number`, required: true): We ask you to rate our customer service on a scale that ranges from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied". Please select the rating that best reflects your experience.
* **What would you like to see improved in our customer service?** (`customer_service_improvement`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve our customer service, this is the place to share them.
* **Have you had any recent interactions with our customer service team?** (`customer_service_interaction`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all the interactions that apply to your recent experience with our customer service team.
* **Can you provide your email address for follow-up communication?** (`customer_service_email`, `email`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up communication, please provide your email address here.
* **Can you provide your phone number for follow-up communication?** (`customer_service_phone`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up communication, please provide your phone number here.
* **Have you ever had to wait a long time to get help from our customer service team?** (`customer_service_wait_time`, `checkbox`, required: true): We want to know if you have ever experienced delays in getting help from our team. Please select "True" if this has happened.
* **Can you describe your recent experience with our customer service team?** (`customer_service_feedback`, `note`, required: false): This is an open space for you to provide a more detailed description of your experience with our customer service team.
* **Would you like to be contacted for follow-up communication?** (`customer_service_follow_up`, `checkbox`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please select "True" here.
* **How satisfied are you with our customer service?** (`customer_service_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: true): We ask you to rate your satisfaction with our customer service on a scale from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Do you have any suggestions for our customer service team?** (`customer_service_suggestion`, `text`, required: false): If you have any ideas or suggestions on how we can improve our customer service, this is the place to share them.
* **Is this form complete and accurate?** (`customer_service_confirmation`, `checkbox`, required: false): Please review the form and select "True" if it is complete and accurate.

Please note that all required fields are marked with a red asterisk (\*).
