# Power Supply Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a general feedback and survey form designed to collect information on your experience with power supply products.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each question.
3. Enter your email address if you would like to receive a follow-up on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think?** (`general_feedback`, text): This is the main feedback section where you can type your general thoughts and opinions about the product.
* **Which product are you using?** (`product`, select_one): Please select the product that you are using from the options provided.
* **Rate this product** (`rating`, number): Rate the product on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Do you have any suggestions for us?** (`suggestions`, text): If you have any suggestions or ideas for our products or services, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you would like to receive a follow-up on your feedback.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`contact_info`, select_multiple): Please select how you heard about us from the options provided.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **When did you start using the product?** (`date_used`, date): Enter the date you started using the product.
* **When did you purchase this product?** (`date_purchased`, date): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **How often do you use this product?** (`how_often_used`, time): Enter the time you use the product each day.
* **For how long have you used this product?** (`time_used`, time): Enter the time you have used the product.
* **At what time of day do you use this product?** (`time_of_day`, select_multiple): Please select the time of day you use the product from the options provided.
* **At what time of week do you use this product?** (`time_of_week`, select_multiple): Please select the time of week you use the product from the options provided.
* **At what time of month do you use this product?** (`time_of_month`, select_multiple): Please select the time of month you use the product from the options provided.
* **At what time of year do you use this product?** (`time_of_year`, select_multiple): Please select the time of year you use the product from the options provided.
* **At what time of day do you use this product again?** (`time_of_day_again`, select_multiple): Please select the time of day you use the product again from the options provided.
* **How often do you use this product again?** (`how_often_used_again`, select_multiple): Please select the frequency of use you use the product again from the options provided.
* **What type of product are you using?** (`product_type`, select_one): Please select the type of product you are using from the options provided.
* **What is your product usage?** (`product_usage`, select_multiple): Please select your usage of the product from the options provided.
* **Product Serial Number** (`product_serial_number`, text): Enter the serial number of the product.
* **Purchase Date of Serial Number** (`purchase_date_serial`, date): Enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, number): Enter the price you paid for the product.
* **Warranty Start Date** (`warranty_start_date`, date): Enter the start date of the warranty.
* **Warranty End Date** (`warranty_end_date`, date): Enter the end date of the warranty.
* **What is your contact preference?** (`contact_preference`, select_one): Please select your contact preference from the options provided.
