# leadership_stories_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect leadership stories from individuals, allowing them to share their experiences and achievements in leadership roles. This form will help track and manage the submission process, including the status of each story, who submitted it, and when it was submitted or approved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **What's your leadership story (up to 500 words)**: This is the main content of your leadership story. Please provide a detailed description of your leadership experience, including any relevant events, challenges, and achievements.
2.  **Category**: Select the category that best fits your story, such as "leadership," "culture-building," or "communications."
3.  **Story title**: Enter a brief title for your story that captures its essence.
4.  **More of your story**: Provide additional details about your story, such as any challenges you faced, how you overcame them, and what you learned from the experience.
5.  **Submission status**: Select the current status of your submission, such as "approved" or "draft."
6.  **Update the submission status**: If you need to update the status of your submission, select the new status and provide a date for when you made the change.
7.  **Who submitted**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the story.
8.  **Date submitted**: Enter the date when the story was submitted.
9.  **Time submitted**: Enter the time when the story was submitted.
10.  **Who approved**: Enter the name of the person who approved the story.
11.  **Date approved**: Enter the date when the story was approved.
12.  **Time approved**: Enter the time when the story was approved.
13.  **Additional submission status notes**: Provide any additional notes about the submission status.
14.  **Update the submission status date notes**: Provide any additional notes about the submission status date.
15.  **Update the submission status time notes**: Provide any additional notes about the submission status time.
16.  **Brief description of the image**: If you attached an image to your story, enter a brief description of it.
17.  **Brief description of the audio**: If you attached an audio file to your story, enter a brief description of it.

*   **What's your leadership story (up to 500 words)** (`story_1`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of your leadership experience, including any relevant events, challenges, and achievements.
