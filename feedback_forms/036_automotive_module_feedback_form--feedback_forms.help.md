# Automotive Module Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their recent experience with our automotive services. It helps us understand what we did well and what we could improve on to provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details at the top of the form, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the service or module that you received from us.
3. Choose how you rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
4. Rate the quality, timeliness, and professionalism of the staff member who worked with you.
5. Provide any specific strengths and weaknesses of the service you received.
6. Optionally, provide any additional feedback or specific issues you'd like to share.
7. Decide whether you would recommend our service to others and how likely you are to use it again in the future.
8. Finally, indicate if you'd like us to follow up with you to discuss your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so that we can address your feedback to you personally.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with any follow-up questions or discuss your feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for any follow-up discussions.
* **Customer ID or Account Number** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter your customer ID or account number if you have one.
* **Service or Module Used** (`service_received`, select_one, required): Choose the service or module that you received from us.
* **Date of Service** (`service_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received the service.
* **Staff Member Worked With** (`staff_member`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide feedback about the staff member who worked with you, enter their name here.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service on a scale of Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Quality of Work or Service** (`quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the service on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Timeliness of Service** (`timeliness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the timeliness of the service on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Staff Professionalism and Knowledge** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the staff professionalism and knowledge on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate the value for money on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What did we do well?** (`strengths`, text, required): Describe the positive aspects of the service you received.
* **What could we improve?** (`improvements`, text, required): Identify areas for improvement or concerns you have about the service.
* **Any Specific Issues or Concerns** (`specific_issues`, text, optional): Enter any specific issues or concerns you'd like to share.
* **Would you recommend us to others?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Decide whether you would recommend our service to others on a scale of Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably Not, or Definitely Not.
* **How likely are you to use our service again?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood of using our service again on a scale of Very Likely, Likely, Uncertain, Unlikely, or Very Unlikely.
* **May we contact you to discuss feedback?** (`contact_followup`, select_one, required): Decide if you'd like us to follow up with you to discuss your feedback on a scale of Yes, or No.
* **Date of Feedback** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're providing this feedback.
