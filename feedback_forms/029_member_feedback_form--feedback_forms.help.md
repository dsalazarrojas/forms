# Member Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Member Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from members about their experience with our service. Your input will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve, enabling us to provide a better service for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer them as accurately as possible.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required") and skip the optional ones if you don't feel comfortable providing information on those topics.
3. Use the available space in each field to provide as much detail as you need to answer the question.
4. When you've completed all the fields, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header** (Note): This is the title of the form and is used to provide context about what the form is for.
* **Feedback Question** (Text): Please provide a brief description of your experience with our service. Be as specific as possible and mention what you liked or disliked.
* **Member Rating** (Select Multiple): Rate your experience with our service on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being "Very Bad" and 5 being "Very Good".
* **Service Improvement** (Select One): Choose one option that describes how we could improve our service to better meet your needs.
	+ Very Good: Our service already meets your expectations.
	+ Good: Our service is good, but there is room for improvement.
	+ Fair: Our service is not meeting your expectations.
	+ Bad: Our service is not good enough.
	+ Very Bad: Our service is very poor.
* **Comments** (Text): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about our service, please provide them here.
* **Email Address** (Email): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address. We'll keep your email address private and only use it to follow up with you if necessary.
* **Member Contact** (Text): If you'd like to be contacted directly by a member of our team, please provide your name and contact information.
* **Form Footer** (Note): This is the end of the form and is used to provide final instructions or information about how to proceed.

Note: Remember to be honest and specific with your feedback. Your input will help us provide a better service for everyone.
