# Consent Awareness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
<thinking>
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from trainees on the effectiveness of the consent awareness training program. The form is designed to collect information on several aspects of the training, including the date of submission, the trainee's name and contact information (optional), the training session attended, the facilitator's performance, the quality of the training materials, and the trainee's level of comfort and confidence before and after the training. The feedback will help us improve the training program and make it more effective for future trainees.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your relevant information.
2. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields blank.
3. Answer each question truthfully, and choose the option that best describes your experience.
4. Be as specific as possible when providing your feedback, especially in the "Key Learnings" and "Additional Comments" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`): The date you are submitting your feedback.
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`): Your name (optional).
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): Your email address (optional).
* **Training Session Attended** (`training_session`): The name of the training session you attended.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`): The date the training was held.
* **Facilitator Name** (`facilitator_name`): The name of the facilitator.
* **Organization** (`organization`): Your organization or institution.
* **Your Role** (`role`): Your position or role.
* **Prior Knowledge of Consent** (`prior_knowledge`): Rate your prior knowledge of consent before the training.
	+ Very knowledgeable: You had a good understanding of consent before the training.
	+ Somewhat knowledgeable: You had some understanding of consent before the training.
	+ Neutral: You had no prior knowledge of consent before the training.
	+ Limited knowledge: You had limited knowledge of consent before the training.
	+ No prior knowledge: You had no knowledge of consent before the training.
* **Clarity of Content** (`content_clarity`): Rate how clear the training content was.
	+ Very clear: The content was easy to understand and clear.
	+ Clear: The content was clear and understandable.
	+ Neutral: The content was neutral and neither clear nor unclear.
	+ Unclear: The content was difficult to understand.
	+ Very unclear: The content was extremely difficult to understand.
* **Facilitator Effectiveness** (`facilitator_effectiveness`): Rate how effective the facilitator was.
	+ Excellent: The facilitator was very effective in presenting the material.
	+ Good: The facilitator was effective in presenting the material.
	+ Fair: The facilitator was somewhat effective in presenting the material.
	+ Poor: The facilitator was not effective in presenting the material.
	+ Very poor: The facilitator was extremely not effective in presenting the material.
* **Quality of Training Materials** (`materials_quality`): Rate the quality of the provided materials.
	+ Excellent: The materials were of high quality and helpful.
	+ Good: The materials were of good quality and helpful.
	+ Fair: The materials were somewhat helpful.
	+ Poor: The materials were not helpful.
	+ Very poor: The materials were extremely not helpful.
* **Understanding of Consent Improved** (`understanding_improved`): Rate how much your understanding of consent improved after the training.
	+ Significantly improved: Your understanding of consent improved greatly after the training.
	+ Moderately improved: Your understanding of consent improved somewhat after the training.
	+ Slightly improved: Your understanding of consent improved a little after the training.
	+ No change: Your understanding of consent did not change after the training.
	+ Worse: Your understanding of consent became worse after the training.
* **Key Learnings** (`key_learnings`): Describe the main takeaways from the training.
* **Confidence Level Before Training** (`confidence_level_before`): Rate your confidence in discussing consent before the training.
	+ Very confident: You felt very confident in discussing consent before the training.
	+ Confident: You felt confident in discussing consent before the training.
	+ Neutral: You felt neutral in discussing consent before the training.
	+ Not confident: You felt not confident in discussing consent before the training.
	+ Very unconfident: You felt very unconfident in discussing consent before the training.
* **Confidence Level After Training** (`confidence_level_after`): Rate your confidence in discussing consent after the training.
	+ Very confident: You feel very confident in discussing consent after the training.
	+ Confident: You feel confident in discussing consent after the training.
	+ Neutral: You feel neutral in discussing consent after the training.
	+ Not confident: You feel not confident in discussing consent after the training.
	+ Very unconfident: You feel very unconfident in discussing consent after the training.
* **More Comfortable Discussing Consent** (`comfortable_discussing`): Rate how much more comfortable you feel discussing consent after the training.
	+ Yes - Much more comfortable: You feel much more comfortable discussing consent after the training.
	+ Yes - Somewhat more comfortable: You feel somewhat more comfortable discussing consent after the training.
	+ No change: You feel no change in discussing consent after the training.
	+ Less comfortable: You feel less comfortable discussing consent after the training.
* **Will You Apply What You Learned** (`will_apply_learning`): Rate how likely you are to apply what you learned from the training.
	+ Definitely will: You will definitely apply what you learned from the training.
	+ Probably will: You will probably apply what you learned from the training.
	+ Might: You might apply what you learned from the training.
	+ Probably will not: You will not apply what you learned from the training.
	+ Definitely will not: You will not apply what you learned from the training.
* **How Will You Apply This Learning** (`application_context`): Describe how you will apply what you learned from the training.
* **Pace of Session** (`session_pace`): Rate how well-paced the training session was.
	+ Too fast: The session was too fast-paced.
	+ Just right: The session was well-paced.
	+ Too slow: The session was too slow-paced.
* **Length of Session** (`session_length`): Rate how long the training session was.
	+ Too short: The session was too short.
	+ Just right: The session was well-paced.
	+ Too long: The session was too long.
* **Comfort Level of Environment** (`environment_comfort`): Rate how comfortable you felt in the learning environment.
	+ Very comfortable: You felt very comfortable in the learning environment.
	+ Comfortable: You felt comfortable in the learning environment.
	+ Neutral: You felt neutral in the learning environment.
	+ Uncomfortable: You felt uncomfortable in the learning environment.
	+ Very uncomfortable: You felt very uncomfortable in the learning environment.
* **Topics You Would Like Added** (`topics_to_add`): Describe additional topics you would like to see added to the training.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`improvements_suggested`): Describe how we can improve the training.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`): Rate how likely you are to recommend this training to others.
	+ Definitely yes: You will definitely recommend this training to others.
	+ Probably yes: You will probably recommend this training to others.
	+ Neutral: You feel neutral about recommending this training to others.
	+ Probably no: You will not recommend this training to others.
	+ Definitely no: You will not recommend this training to others.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the training.
