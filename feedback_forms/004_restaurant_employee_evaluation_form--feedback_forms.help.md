# Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restaurant Employee Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback and evaluation of restaurant employees. This form helps managers and supervisors assess an employee's performance, provide coaching, and identify areas for improvement. It is used to ensure that employees are meeting the expected standards of the restaurant and to help them grow professionally.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee Name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the employee's position in the "Position" field.
3. Enter the Supervisor Name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
4. Select the Evaluation Date in the "Evaluation Date" field.
5. Enter an Overall Rating in the "Overall Rating" field.
6. Provide Performance Comments in the "Performance Comments" field.
7. Identify Areas for Improvement in the "Areas for Improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Position**: Enter the job title or position of the employee.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the person who filled out this form (the supervisor).
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date when this evaluation was performed.
* **Overall Rating**: Enter an overall rating of the employee's performance, typically a number between 1-5.
* **Performance Comments**: Enter any comments about the employee's performance, including strengths and weaknesses.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Enter any areas where the employee needs improvement or development.
 
## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Be as specific and objective as possible when providing feedback.
- Use this form as a guide for coaching and development discussions with employees.
