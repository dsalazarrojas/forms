# real_estate_agent_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_agent_feedback_form is a tool to collect feedback from real estate agents, aiming to improve the quality of services provided by the organization. This feedback will help in identifying areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to better serve the real estate agents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering a title for the feedback in the "form_title" field.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. In the "feedback" field, describe your overall experience with the real estate services.
5. Rate the services provided in the "services_rating" field.
6. Rate the staff's performance in the "staff_rating_1" to "staff_rating_5" fields.
7. Indicate whether you have interacted with specific staff members in the "staff_name_1" to "staff_name_4" fields.
8. Select the category of feedback in the "category" field.
9. If necessary, provide additional comments in the "comments" field.
10. If you have any further information, enter it in the "additional_info" field.
11. If you want to be contacted, provide your name and email address in the "contact_name" and "contact_email" fields respectively.
12. Select the date and time of your feedback in the "date" and "time" fields.
13. Finally, confirm whether the staff members you've interacted with were available ("Yes" or "No") in the "staff_name_1" to "staff_name_4" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title**: Enter a title for the feedback, describing your experience or concern.
* **name**: Enter your name for the feedback purpose.
* **email**: Provide your email address to be contacted if necessary.
* **feedback**: Describe your overall experience with the real estate services.
* **services_rating**: Rate the quality of services provided.
* **staff_rating_1**: Rate the performance of staff member 1.
* **staff_rating_2**: Rate the performance of staff member 2.
* **staff_rating_3**: Rate the performance of staff member 3.
* **staff_rating_4**: Rate the performance of staff member 4.
* **staff_rating_5**: Rate the performance of staff member 5.
* **staff_name_1**: Confirm whether staff member 1 was available.
* **staff_name_2**: Confirm whether staff member 2 was available.
* **staff_name_3**: Confirm whether staff member 3 was available.
* **staff_name_4**: Confirm whether staff member 4 was available.
* **comments**: Provide additional comments if necessary.
* **additional_info**: Enter any other information you may have regarding your feedback.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **date**: Select the date of your feedback.
* **time**: Select the time of your feedback.
* **category**: Select the category of your feedback.
* **sub_category**: Select the sub-category of your feedback.
* **category**: Select the category of your feedback.
