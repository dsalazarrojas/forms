# whatsapp_automation_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on the WhatsApp automation feature, allowing us to understand how we can improve it.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select a feedback type from the options provided.
2. Write your feedback in the text box.
3. Enter your email address (if you want to receive a response).
4. Enter a phone number (if you want to be contacted about your feedback).
5. Write any additional message or comments.
6. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.
7. If you've changed your mind, click the "Cancel" button to reset the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, required: false): Please select one of the options to provide feedback on the WhatsApp automation feature.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Write your thoughts on how we can improve the WhatsApp automation feature.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you want to receive a response about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required: false): Write any additional message or comments about your feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to send your feedback.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required: false): Click this button to reset the form and start again.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible with your feedback so we can better understand your concerns or suggestions.
* If you have any attachments or files to include with your feedback, you can attach them separately.
* We appreciate your time and effort in providing feedback to help us improve the WhatsApp automation feature.
