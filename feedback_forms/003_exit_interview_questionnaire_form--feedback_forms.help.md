# Exit Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and reasons for leaving the company. Your responses will help us understand what we can do to improve and make our workplace better for future employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out the "Introduction" section, which provides some background information about the form.
2. In the "Why Leave" section, explain why you are leaving the company. Be as specific as possible.
3. The "Manager Relationship" section asks you to describe your relationship with your manager.
4. "Team Collaboration" is where you can share your experiences with teamwork and collaboration within the company.
5. In "Job Satisfaction", rate your overall satisfaction with your job using the scale provided.
6. "Communication" is about rating the effectiveness of your manager's communication with you.
7. "Job Security" is a measure of how secure you felt in your position.
8. "Career Development" rates the opportunities you had for career growth within the company.
9. "Work Life Balance" is how you rate your balance between work and personal life.
10. "Overall Experience" is a final assessment of your time at the company.
11. "Feedback" is an optional field for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
12. "Suggestions" is a space for any specific ideas you may have for the company.
13. "Comments" is another optional field for any additional comments you may have.
14. "Exit Date" is the date of your last day of work.
15. "Last Day of Work" is the same as Exit Date.
16. "Contact Info" is where you can provide any contact information if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Why Leave**: A text field where you can explain the reasons why you are leaving the company.
* **Manager Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your manager.
* **Team Collaboration**: Describe your experiences with teamwork and collaboration within the company.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with your job using the provided scale.
* **Communication**: Rate the effectiveness of your manager's communication with you.
* **Job Security**: Rate the level of job security you felt in your position.
* **Career Development**: Rate the opportunities you had for career growth within the company.
* **Work Life Balance**: Rate your balance between work and personal life.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the company.
* **Feedback**: A text field for any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Suggestions**: A space for any specific ideas you may have for the company.
* **Comments**: Another optional field for additional comments.
* **Exit Date**: The date of your last day of work.
* **Last Day of Work**: This is the same as Exit Date.
* **Contact Info**: A field to provide any contact information if you wish.
