# Pro Bono Agent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from pro bono agents on their experience with the services provided by the program. This feedback will help us improve our services and better serve our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best suit your experience and provide your comments if you have any. The form has the following sections:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pro Bono Agent Feedback** (`pro_bono_agent_feedback`, text, required): Please provide your overall feedback about the service you received. This will help us understand your general impression of our services.
* **Pro Bono Service Quality Rating** (`pro_bono_service_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the service you received. Options are:
	+ Very Poor
	+ Poor
	+ Fair
	+ Good
	+ Excellent
* **Pro Bono Service Usefulness Rating** (`pro_bono_service_usefulness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how useful the service was to you. Options are:
	+ Not at All
	+ A Little
	+ Somewhat
	+ Quite a bit
	+ Very Much
* **Pro Bono Service Outcome Rating** (`pro_bono_service_outcome_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well the service met your needs. Options are:
	+ Not Met
	+ Somewhat Met
	+ Mostly Met
	+ Almost Met
	+ Completely Met
* **Pro Bono Service Value Rating** (`pro_bono_service_value_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how valuable the service was to you. Options are:
	+ Not at All
	+ A Little
	+ Somewhat
	+ Quite a bit
	+ Very Much
* **Pro Bono Agent Name** (`pro_bono_agent_name`, text, required): Please provide your name as the pro bono agent who provided the service.
* **Pro Bono Service Description** (`pro_bono_service_description`, text, required): Describe the service you provided in detail.
* **Pro Bono Agent Training** (`pro_bono_agent_training`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you received any training related to the service. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Pro Bono Agent Comments** (`pro_bono_agent_comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the service.
* **Pro Bono Service Outcome Impact** (`pro_bono_service_outcome_impact`, text, required): Describe the impact of the service on your clients or the community.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact the pro bono program administrators.
