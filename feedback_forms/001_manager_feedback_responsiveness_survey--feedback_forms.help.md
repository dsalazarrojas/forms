# manager_feedback_responsiveness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from employees on their manager's responsiveness and behavior. It's designed to help managers understand their strengths and weaknesses and make data-driven decisions to improve their relationship with their team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant feedback options for each question based on your experience with your manager.
2. Answer all questions, as each one is required for a complete assessment.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (`employee_feedback`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This question asks if you've had any feedback provided by your manager.
* **Manager Response** (`manager_response`, text, required):
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any comments or feedback about your manager's response to your requests.
* **Time to Response** (`time_to_response`, date, required):
	+ This question asks about the time it takes for your manager to respond to your requests.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses how well your manager communicates with you.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, number, required):
	+ This question asks how often you receive feedback from your manager.
* **Manager Attitude** (`manager_attitude`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses your manager's attitude towards you and your work.
* **Employee Satisfaction** (`employee_satisfaction`, number, required):
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your manager.
* **Employee Confidence** (`employee_confidence`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses your confidence in your manager's ability to support you.
* **Feedback Given** (`feedback_given`, number, required):
	+ This question asks about the quality of feedback you receive from your manager.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses your overall experience with your manager.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional):
	+ This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions or comments about your experience with your manager.
* **Follow-up** (`follow-up`, select_one, optional):
	+ This question asks if you've received follow-up from your manager after providing feedback.
* **Manager Helpfulness** (`manager_helpfulness`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses how helpful your manager is in resolving issues.
* **Manager Timeliness** (`manager_timeliness`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses your manager's timeliness in responding to your requests.
* **Employee Feeling** (`employee_feeling`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This question asks about how you feel about your manager's behavior.
* **Feedback Quality** (`feedback_quality`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This question assesses the quality of feedback you receive from your manager.
* **Manager Effectiveness** (`manager_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This question assesses your manager's effectiveness in resolving issues.
* **Manager Response Rate** (`manager_response_rate`, select_one, required):
	+ This question asks about the response rate of your manager.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select_one, required):
	+ This question asks about the frequency of feedback you receive from your manager.
* **Time to Respond** (`time_to_respond`, select_one, required):
	+ This question asks about the time it takes for your manager to respond to your requests.
* **Manager Support** (`manager_support`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses your manager's support for you.
* **Employee Support** (`employee_support`, select_one, required):
	+ This question assesses your support for your manager.
* **Employee Satisfaction Score** (`employee_satisfaction_score`, number, required):
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your manager.
* **Manager Satisfaction Score** (`manager_satisfaction_score`, number, required):
	+ This question asks about your manager's overall satisfaction with their job.
* **Manager Responsiveness Score** (`manager_responsiveness_score`, number, required):
	+ This question asks about your manager's overall responsiveness.

## Tips
- Take your time to answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
- Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
- Don't hesitate to provide suggestions or comments in the "Suggestions" field.
