# movie_critique_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "movie_critique_form" is designed to collect user feedback about a movie, allowing viewers to share their thoughts and opinions about the film.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the movie you watched in the "Movie Title" field.
2. Provide your name and title as the reviewer in the "Reviewer Name" field.
3. Rate the movie using the "Rating" field, where 1-5 stars represent different levels of satisfaction.
4. Select the genre of the movie that best fits your experience in the "Genre" field.
5. Write a brief review of the movie in the "Review" field, sharing your thoughts and opinions about the film.
6. If applicable, specify what you enjoyed most about the movie in the "What did you enjoy most about the movie?" field.
7. If applicable, specify what you disliked most about the movie in the "What did you dislike most about the movie?" field.
8. Decide whether you would recommend this movie to others and select your answer in the "Would you recommend this movie?" field.
9. Choose your rating using the "Rating Scale" field, which represents a different scale than the "Rating" field.
10. Finally, provide your contact information (email or phone number) in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, which are optional but can be used for follow-up communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Movie Title (text)**: Enter the title of the movie you watched.
* **Reviewer Name (text)**: Provide your name as the reviewer.
* **Rating (number)**: Rate the movie using 1-5 stars, where 1 star represents a low rating and 5 stars represent a high rating.
* **Genre (select_one)**: Select the genre that best fits your experience with the movie (Action, Comedy, Drama, Horror, Sci-Fi, Romance, Thriller, Documentary).
* **Review (text)**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the movie in this field.
* **What did you enjoy most about the movie? (text)**: If applicable, write a brief description of what you enjoyed most about the movie.
* **What did you dislike most about the movie? (text)**: If applicable, write a brief description of what you disliked most about the movie.
* **Would you recommend this movie? (select_multiple)**: Decide whether you would recommend this movie to others and select your answer (True, False, Maybe).
* **Rating Scale (select_one)**: Choose your rating using this scale (1-5 stars).
* **Email (email)**: Provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone (text)**: Provide your phone number for follow-up communication.

## Tips

* Please be as specific and honest as possible when filling out the form.
* If a field is not relevant to your experience, feel free to skip it.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
