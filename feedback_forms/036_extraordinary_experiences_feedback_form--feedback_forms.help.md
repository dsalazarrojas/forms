# Extraordinary Experiences Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on extraordinary events that have occurred. Please submit this form only after an exceptional event has happened, and not for general feedback or complaints.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** field with the name of the event.
2. Enter the **Event Date** field with the date of the event.
3. Select the **Type of Event** field with the type of event that occurred.
4. Choose all that apply for **Suggestions** for improvements or changes.
5. Enter your **Comments** about the event in the **Comments** field.
6. Provide your **Feedback** on the event in the **Feedback** field.
7. Enter your **Contact Email** if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the event that occurred.
* **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Type of Event** (select_one): Select the type of event that occurred.
* **Suggestions** (select_multiple): Choose all that apply for improvements or changes to be considered.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Comments** (note): Enter your comments about the event in as much detail as possible.
* **Feedback** (text): Provide your feedback on the event.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you submit this form only after an extraordinary event has occurred.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide as much detail as possible in the **Comments** and **Feedback** fields.
* This form will be reviewed by the relevant teams, and your responses will be taken into consideration.
* Do not resubmit this form if you have already submitted it.
