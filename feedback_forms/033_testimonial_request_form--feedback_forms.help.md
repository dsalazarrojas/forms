# Testimonial Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect testimonials from customers and users who have had a positive experience with us. The form allows you to share your appreciation and feedback about our services, and we value your input to improve and grow.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide a testimonial**: In the first field, "What would you like to say about us", please share your genuine thoughts and words about your experience with us. This will help us understand your perspective and improve our services.
2. **Rate our services**: In the second field, select one of the options from the list to rate our services: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
3. **Publish your testimonial**: In the third field, choose whether you consent to publish your testimonial on our platform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What would you like to say about us** (Text field, Optional): This is the main field where you can express your thoughts and opinions about our services. Please keep it brief but sincere.
* **Rate our services** (Select One field, Optional): Choose one of the rating options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. This helps us understand how we're doing.
* **Publish my testimonial** (Select Multiple field, Optional): Select "Yes, I consent to publish my testimonial" if you're okay with us sharing your testimonial with others. If you're not comfortable with it, select "No, do not publish my testimonial".

## Tips

* Be honest and genuine in your testimonial.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time.
