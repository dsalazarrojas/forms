# Digital Marketing Instructor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from instructors on the digital marketing aspects of the course, providing valuable insights to improve the course's online presence and marketing effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Instructor ID in the designated field.
2. Provide your Name and Email address if available.
3. Specify the Course Name related to your feedback.
4. Share your feedback on the course's digital marketing.
5. Suggest any changes or improvements you think are necessary.
6. Provide additional feedback or comments.
7. Rate the course's digital marketing on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
8. Indicate whether you would recommend this course.
9. If you would recommend, state why.
10. Enter a valid Email address to contact you.
11. Provide any comments or suggestions.
12. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Instructor ID** (`instructor_id`, number, required): This field is for you to enter your unique instructor ID.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name to help us identify you.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
- **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the course's digital marketing.
- **Suggest Changes** (`suggest_changes`, text, optional): If you think there's a way to improve, let us know.
- **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts.
- **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, optional): Rate the course's digital marketing on a scale.
- **Would You Recommend?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend this course.
  - Option 1: Yes
  - Option 2: No
  - Option 3: Not sure
  - Option 4: Other (please specify)
- **Why Recommended** (`why_recommended`, text, optional): If you would recommend, tell us why.
- **Contact** (`contact`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
- **Comments** (`comment`, text, optional): Provide any final comments or thoughts.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to complete the form and submit your feedback.

This guide is designed to be concise and practical, providing a clear understanding of each field's purpose and requirements.
