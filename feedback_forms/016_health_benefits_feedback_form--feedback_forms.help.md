# Health Benefits Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the health benefits provided by the company. It will help us understand what benefits are working well, what benefits need improvement, and what benefits are not being utilized. This information will be used to make informed decisions about future health benefits offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the list of health benefits programs provided.
2. Select the programs that you are currently using or interested in using.
3. Rate your satisfaction with each program using the provided rating scale.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback on the programs.
5. Enter your name to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Health Benefits Program** (`health_benefits_program`, select_one, required: false): Choose the health benefits program that best describes the reason you are using or interested in using our company's health benefits. Options include:
	+ Health and Wellness
	+ Employee Assistance Program
	+ Life Insurance
	+ 401k
	+ Other (please specify in the "Additional Comments" field)
* **Program Satisfaction** (`program_satisfaction`, text, required: false): Describe your level of satisfaction with the health benefits program you selected.
* **Program Satisfaction Rating** (`program_satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Program Comments** (`program_satisfaction_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback on the program.
* **Employee Assistance Program** (`employee_assistance_program`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you are currently using or interested in using the Employee Assistance Program.
* **Life Insurance** (`life_insurance`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are currently covered by or interested in life insurance.
* **401k** (`401k`, select_one, required: false): Check if you are currently enrolled in or interested in our 401k program.
* **Other Benefits** (`other_benefits`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you are currently using or interested in any other benefits not listed above.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the health benefits offered.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to submit the form.

Note: All fields are optional, but your feedback and comments are greatly appreciated!
