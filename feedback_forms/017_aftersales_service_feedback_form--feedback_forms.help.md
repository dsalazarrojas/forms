# Aftersales Service Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback about their aftersales service experience. Please provide information about the service you received in order to help us improve our service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account the instructions provided in the field-by-field explanations below.
3. Make sure to provide your contact email if you'd like us to follow up on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback, please provide your email address.
* **Product purchased** (`product_purchased`, text, required): Describe the product related to the aftersales service you received.
* **Service date** (`service_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the aftersales service occurred.
* **Overall satisfaction rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Select the level of satisfaction you experienced with the service. Please choose one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Timeliness of service** (`timeliness`, select_one, required): Select whether the service was completed on time. Please choose one of the options: On time, Slight delay, Major delay.
* **Technician professionalism** (`technician_professionalism`, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism of the technician who provided the service. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Was the issue resolved** (`resolution_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the issue after service. Please choose one of the options: Resolved, Partially resolved, Not resolved.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or concerns, please let us know in the additional comments section.
