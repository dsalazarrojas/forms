# Gym Class Scheduling Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences for gym classes to help us schedule classes that cater to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your preferred days of the week for classes.
2. Choose your preferred class types (e.g., yoga, spinning, etc.).
3. Select your preferred time slots (e.g., morning, afternoon, etc.).
4. Choose your preferred class duration (e.g., 30-45 minutes, etc.).
5. Select your preferred coaches.
6. Choose your preferred equipment (e.g., dumbbells, barbells, etc.).
7. Select your preferred class size (e.g., small, medium, large, etc.).
8. Choose how often you would like to attend classes (e.g., daily, 2-3 times a week, etc.).
