# natureConnectionExperienceSurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a nature-based program. The purpose of this form is to collect data on how users perceived the quality and value of the program, as well as their overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to rate the overall quality of the program on a numerical scale.
3. Select your preferred option for the program's value and satisfaction.
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have about the program.
5. Fill out your contact information, including your email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program**: Please enter the name of the program you are providing feedback on.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the nature-based program?**: Rate the program on a numerical scale, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **nature connection**: Please describe your experience with the nature-based program.
* **Program Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with the program, selecting all that apply.
* **Program Value**: Select one of the options that best describe the value you got from the program.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Name**: Enter your name so we can personally address your feedback.
* **Submitted**: Please note the date and time you submitted the form.
