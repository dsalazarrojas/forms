## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback on the effectiveness of nutritional counseling services provided. Your input will help us understand your experiences and improve our services.

To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields below, providing as much detail as you feel comfortable sharing.

* **Rating Scale (Page 2)**: Please provide your rating for the effectiveness of our counseling services on a scale.
* **Feedback (Page 3)**: A text field to provide your thoughts and feelings about your experience with our counseling services.
