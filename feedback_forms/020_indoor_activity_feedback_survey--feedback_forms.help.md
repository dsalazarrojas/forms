# Indoor Activity Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users after an indoor activity. Your input will help us understand your experience and suggest improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Choose the activity you want to provide feedback on from the options provided.
2. Select the rating that best describes your experience.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event** (`event`, text, required: false): This field is where you'll enter the name or description of the indoor activity you're providing feedback on. If you're unsure about the event's name, you can leave this field blank.
* **What activity?** (`activity`, select_one, required: false): Here, you can select the specific indoor activity you want to provide feedback on from the list of options provided. If you don't see your activity listed, please contact us so we can update the list.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have about the indoor activity. You can type as much or as little as you like.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): This is where you'll select a rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your satisfaction with the indoor activity.
