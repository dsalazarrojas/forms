# Tournament Safety Measures Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather detailed feedback on safety measures implemented during a tournament. It's intended for event attendees to report their experiences and provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Make sure to provide as much detail as you can in the "Event Details" field.
3. Be thorough when reporting your experience and providing suggestions.
4. Submit your feedback when you're finished filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/true): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Tournament Name** (`Tournament Name`, `text`, required/true): Enter the name of the tournament you attended.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `text`, required/true): Provide as much detail as possible about your experience during the event.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date you attended the tournament.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required/true): Enter the time you attended the tournament.
* **Venue** (`Venue`, `text`, required/true): Enter the location where the tournament took place.
* **Attendees** (`Attendees`, `number`, required/true): Report the number of attendees you saw during the event.
* **Safety Measures** (`Safety Measures`, `text`, required/true): Report any safety measures you encountered during the event.
* **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, `text`, required/true): Provide any suggestions you have for improving safety protocols.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional/false): If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to include them.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, required/true): Report any tools or equipment used during the event.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, `text`, required/true): Enter a unique identifier for this form submission.
* **Form Version** (`Form Version`, `number`, required/true): Enter the version number of this form.
* **Timestamp** (`Timestamp`, `number`, required/true): Enter the timestamp of your form submission.
* **Submitted At** (`Submitted At`, `date`, optional/true): Optional field for tracking when you submitted the form.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, `text`, required/true): Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted From** (`Submitted From`, `text`, required/true): Enter your IP address or location.
* **Submitted User Agent** (`Submitted User Agent`, `text`, required/true): Report your user agent.
* **Submitted OS** (`Submitted OS`, `text`, required/true): Report your operating system.
* **Submitted Device Type** (`Submitted Device Type`, `text`, required/true): Report your device type.
* **Submitted Country** (`Submitted Country`, `text`, required/true): Report your country of origin.
