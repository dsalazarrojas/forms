# Language School Student Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The language school student feedback form is a tool for students to express their opinions and suggestions on their experience with the language school. This form is designed to help the school improve its services and offerings to better meet the needs and expectations of its students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about the course content, selecting one of the options that best fits your experience.
2. Choose the rating for the course instructor, selecting one of the numbers from the list.
3. Rate the facilities, selecting one of the options from the list.
4. If you have any additional feedback that is not mentioned in the previous questions, use the "Other Feedback" field to provide more details.
5. Enter your email address for contact purposes (this is not required but recommended in case we need to follow up on your feedback).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Content (Select One)**: This question asks about your experience with the course content. Choose the option that best describes your thoughts:
	+ English: This refers to the quality and relevance of the course content related to English.
	+ French: This refers to the quality and relevance of the course content related to French.
	+ Spanish: This refers to the quality and relevance of the course content related to Spanish.
	+ German: This refers to the quality and relevance of the course content related to German.
* **Course Instructor (Select One)**: This question asks about your rating of the course instructor. Choose the number that best fits your experience:
	+ 1: Excellent
	+ 2: Good
	+ 3: Fair
	+ 4: Poor
* **Facilities (Select One)**: This question asks about your rating of the facilities provided by the language school. Choose the option that best fits your experience:
	+ Excellent: The facilities were very good and met your needs.
	+ Good: The facilities were satisfactory, but not excellent.
	+ Fair: The facilities were average, but not bad.
	+ Poor: The facilities were not satisfactory.
* **Other Feedback (Text)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions that are not mentioned in the previous questions, please use this field to provide more details.
* **Email (Email)**: This is a required field if you would like us to follow up on your feedback. You can enter your email address here, and we will contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide specific examples when answering the questions.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, try to be concrete and provide specific ideas.
- We will review your feedback and use it to make changes and improvements to our services.
