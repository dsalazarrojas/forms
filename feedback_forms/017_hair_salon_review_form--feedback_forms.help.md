# Hair Salon Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their visit to a hair salon. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and let us know about your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely with your honest feedback.
2. Enter your name so we know who is providing the feedback.
3. Rate your overall experience (1-5 stars).
4. Share your thoughts about your visit in the review field.
5. If you visited the salon recently, enter the date of your visit.
6. Indicate how you rate the service quality and rating of the services you received.
7. Click submit to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can address you personally and follow up with any issues.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the salon (1-5 stars).
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your visit. Be as detailed as you can.
* **Date Visited** (`date_visited`, date, optional): If you want to provide the exact date of your visit, this is the field to enter it.
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of service you received (1-5 stars).
* **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the services you received (1-5 stars).

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible about your experience.
* If you have any issues or concerns, we'll be happy to help.
