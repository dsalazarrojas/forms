# Digital Assessment Feedback Quality Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on the quality of a digital assessment. Your input will help the team understand your experience and make improvements to future assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the fields as requested. You can select the options that apply to your experience, and add any comments or feedback you may have. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction** (`note`, not required): This section is here to provide some general context and instructions for the form. Please read through it to understand the purpose and goals of this survey.

### 2. Questions
* **Questions** (`select_multiple`, not required): This page contains questions about the quality of the digital assessment. Choose all the options that apply to your experience:
  - Yes: This question was clear and easy to understand.
  - No: This question was not clear and difficult to understand.

### 3. Comments
* **Comments** (`text`, not required): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the digital assessment.

### 4. Overall Score
* **Overall Score** (`number`, not required): This field is to rate the overall quality of the digital assessment. Please rate it from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 5. Feedback
* **Feedback** (`note`, not required): This is another text field where you can provide any additional feedback you may have about the digital assessment.

### 6. Email
* **Email** (`email`, not required): This is a field to enter your email address. Please enter the email address you used to access the digital assessment. 

### 7. Name
* **Name** (`text`, not required): This field is to enter your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your user profile. 

### 8. Email (duplicate)
* **Email (duplicate)** (`email`, not required): This is another field to enter your email address. Please note that this is a duplicate field and you should fill it out only if you want to provide an additional email address. 

### 9. Overall Score (duplicate)
* **Overall Score (duplicate)** (`number`, not required): This is another field to rate the overall quality of the digital assessment. Please note that this is a duplicate field and you should fill it out only if you want to provide additional feedback. 

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and let us know if you have any questions or need further assistance.
