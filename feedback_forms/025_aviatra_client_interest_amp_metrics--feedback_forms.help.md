# Aviatra Client Interest and Metrics - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your business profile, metrics, and interest in the GROW program. We will use this data to understand your business needs and provide tailored support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
2. Use the provided hints for guidance on the type of information we are looking for.
3. Make sure to select all applicable options for the "Specific Program Goals" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id 2, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Business Name** (id 3, required): Enter your business's registered name or DBA (Doing Business As name).
* **Email Address** (id 4, required): Enter your business email address, which we will use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number** (id 5, required): Enter your business phone number, which we will use to contact you.
* **Current Revenue Stage** (id 7, required): Select the annual gross revenue range that best describes your business.
* **Number of Employees** (id 8, required): Enter the total number of employees, including yourself and any part-time staff.
* **Years in Business** (id 9, required): Enter the number of years your business has been official registered.
* **Primary Industry** (id 10, required): Select the category that best describes your business's primary industry.
* **Interest Level in GROW Program** (id 12, required): Indicate how committed you are to joining the program, from Very High (Ready to start) to Low (Just browsing).
* **Specific Program Goals** (id 13, required): Select all the goals that apply to you and your business from the list provided.
* **Current Biggest Business Challenge** (id 14, required): Briefly describe your main pain point or challenge your business is facing.
* **How did you hear about Aviatra?** (id 15, required): Select how you became aware of the GROW program.
* **Preferred Workshop Timing** (id 16, required): Indicate when you are most likely to attend workshop sessions.
* **Participant Acknowledgment** (id 17, required): Please confirm that you agree to share your business data for program metrics.

Please complete this form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us. Thank you for participating in the GROW program.
