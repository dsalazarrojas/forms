# Quality Of Life July 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and quality of life information from employees or team members to help management or HR departments understand how employees are doing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address.
3. If applicable, select your **Department** and indicate if you are a **Team Member**.
4. Answer the optional questions about your manager and supervisor status.
5. In the **Feedback** field, provide any comments or suggestions you have about your work-life balance.
6. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can add them in the **Feedback 2** and **Feedback 3** fields.
7. Finally, enter any additional information about your shift length, break length, time of visit, and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Department**: If you have a department, select it from the list.
* **Team Member**: Check if you are a team member.
* **Manager**: Check if you have a manager.
* **Supervisor**: Check if you have a supervisor.
* **Feedback**: Provide any comments or suggestions you have about your work-life balance.
* **Feedback 2** and **Feedback 3**: Additional comments or feedback, if needed.
* **Shift Length**: Enter the length of your shift, if applicable.
* **Break Length**: Enter the length of your breaks, if applicable.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of your visit, if applicable.
* **Lunch Break**: Enter any information about your lunch break, if applicable.
* **Breaks**: Enter any additional information about your breaks, if applicable.
* **Shift Length 2** and **Break Length 2**: Enter the length of your second shift and break, if applicable.
* **Shift Length 3**: Enter the length of your third shift, if applicable.
* **Feedback 2** and **Feedback 3**: Additional comments or feedback, if needed.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any trouble with the form, contact your HR department.
* If you have any feedback or comments that are not covered in this form, feel free to include them in the **Feedback** fields.
