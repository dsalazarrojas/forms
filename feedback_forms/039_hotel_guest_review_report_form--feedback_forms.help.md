# Hotel Guest Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from hotel guests about their stay at our hotel. It's intended to collect information about their experience, including their ratings and opinions on various aspects of their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select a date for your stay from the calendar.
2. Choose your rating for the following:
	* Room Quality
	* Cleanliness
	* Value For Money
	* Service
	* Food and Beverage
	* Location
3. Choose your overall satisfaction rating.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stay Date** (`stay_date`, Date, Required: False)
	+ This field is for selecting the date of your stay.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Select a rating between 1-5 to rate your overall experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Required: False)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with this stay.
* **Checkin/Check out** (`checkin_check_out`, Text, Required: False)
	+ Type the dates of your check-in and check-out.
* **Room Quality** (`room_quality`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Choose one or more of the following:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Choose one or more of the following:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Value For Money** (`value_for_money`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Choose one or more of the following:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Service** (`service`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Choose one or more of the following:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Food and Beverage** (`food_and_beverage`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Choose one or more of the following:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Location** (`location`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Choose one or more of the following:
		- Excellent
		- Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
* **Overall Recommendation** (`overall_recommendation`, Select Multiple, Required: False)
	+ Choose whether you would recommend this hotel or not.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Required: False)
	+ If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them here.
