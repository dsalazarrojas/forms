# Support Interaction Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on customer interactions with our support team. We want to know how well we performed, what we could improve on, and any suggestions you may have for better serving our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer feedback in the space provided on the "Customer Feedback" page.
2. Rate our service using the options on the "Rate Our Service" page.
3. Indicate your overall satisfaction with our service on the "Overall Satisfaction" page.
4. Choose how effective or ineffective you found our communication style on the "Communication Style" page.
5. Rate your experience with the resolution of your issue on the "Resolution Level" page.
6. Select the skills you experienced in our interactions on the "Interactions Skills" page. You can choose one or multiple options.
7. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can add them in the "Additional Comments" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your experience with our support team. This will help us understand your perspective and improve our services.
* **Rate Our Service** (`rating_scale`, number, required): Rate our service on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_level`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_one, required): Choose how effective or ineffective you found our communication style. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Effective
	+ Ineffective
	+ Somewhat Ineffective
	+ Not Effective
	+ Not Applicable
* **Resolution Level** (`resolution_level`, number, required): Rate your experience with the resolution of your issue on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Interactions Skills** (`interaction_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select the skills you experienced in our interactions. Please choose one or multiple options from the following:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
	+ Weak
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can add them here. This is an optional field, so feel free to skip it if you don't have anything to add.
