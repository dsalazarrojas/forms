# Property Digital Experience Index Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This help guide is intended to be used by end-users who are providing feedback on the digital experience of a property. The purpose of this form is to gather information about their experience, including what they like and dislike about the digital services provided, how to improve those services, and demographic information such as contact details and language preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Survey Title** field with a brief title of your feedback.
2. Enter your **Contact First Name**, **Contact Last Name**, and **Email** in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your **Contact Telephone** number if you wish to be contacted regarding your feedback.
4. Answer the three **Survey Questions**:
   * What do you like about our digital services?
   * What do you dislike about our digital services?
   * How can we improve our digital services?
5. Choose your level of agreement on the two **Agree** questions.
6. Select which **Languages** you understand or would like to respond in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your feedback.
  This will help us understand your comments better in the context of the feedback.

  *Hint:* This will help us keep your feedback organized.

- **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
  This will help us contact you regarding your feedback.

  *Hint:* Please provide your first name to help us contact you.

- **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
  This will help us contact you regarding your feedback.

  *Hint:* Please provide your last name to help us contact you.

- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
  This will help us contact you regarding your feedback.

  *Hint:* Please enter your email address to help us contact you.

- **Contact Telephone** (`contact_telephone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
  This will help us contact you regarding your feedback.

  *Hint:* Please enter your contact phone number to help us contact you.

- **What do you like about our digital services?** (`survey_question_1`, text, required: false): Enter what you like about our digital services.
  This will help us understand your positive experiences.

  *Hint:* Try to be specific.

- **What do you dislike about our digital services?** (`survey_question_2`, text, required: false): Enter what you dislike about our digital services.
  This will help us understand areas that need improvement.

  *Hint:* Try to be specific.

- **How can we improve our digital services?** (`survey_question_3`, text, required: false): Enter how we can improve our digital services.
  This will help us understand areas that need improvement.

  *Hint:* Be as specific as possible.

- **Agree** (`survey_agree_1`/`survey_agree_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose how much you agree with our digital services.
  This will help us understand your sentiment about our services.

  *Hint:* Choose how much you agree, disagree, or are neutral.

- **Important** (`survey_important_1`/`survey_important_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose which aspects are important to you.
  This will help us understand your priorities.

  *Hint:* Choose which aspects are very important, somewhat important, or not at all important.

  This will help us track the date of your feedback.

  *Hint:* This will be filled in automatically.

- **Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your submission.
  This will help us track the time of your feedback.

  *Hint:* This will be filled in automatically.

- **Notes** (`survey_note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments.
  This will help us understand your feedback in more depth.

  *Hint:* Be as specific as possible.

- **Submission Date** (`form_submission_date`, date, required: false): This is automatically populated with the date of your submission.
  This is automatically populated with the date of your submission.

- **Submission Time** (`form_submission_time`, time, required: false): This is automatically populated with the time of your submission.
  This is automatically populated with the time of your submission.

- **Survey or?** (`survey_surveyor`, select_one, required: false): Choose what this form is for you (Survey, Questionnaire, or Feedback Form).
  This will help us understand which type of feedback this is.

  *Hint:* Choose which type of form this is.

- **Language** (`survey_language`, select_one, required: false): Select which language you'd like to respond in.
  This will help us tailor your experience to your needs.

  *Hint:* Select your preferred language.

- **Other Language** (`survey_other_language`, select_multiple, required: false): Select other languages you'd like to respond in (if not English, Spanish, or French).
  This will help us understand which languages you'd like to respond in.

  *Hint:* Select other languages if not English, Spanish, or French.
