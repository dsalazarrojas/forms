# School Graduation Ceremony Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the school graduation ceremony, providing an opportunity for you to share your opinions and suggestions on its organization and quality.

## How To Complete This Form
Please take a few minutes to answer the following questions and provide your feedback. Select the most accurate options and provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have. Remember, your feedback will help us create a better event for future graduates and their families.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ceremony Attendance** (`ceremony_attendance`, `select_multiple`, required): Were you present at the graduation ceremony? Select 'Yes' if you attended, 'No' otherwise.
* **Were you a guest at the graduation ceremony?** (`ceremony_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Please respond with 'True' if you were a guest, 'False' otherwise.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the graduation ceremony?** (`graduation_ceremony`, `select_one`, required): Rate the ceremony on a scale from 'Excellent' to 'Poor'.
* **Were you assisted by a staff member during the graduation ceremony?** (`graduation_ceremony_assisted`, `select_one`, required): Respond with 'True' if you were assisted, 'False' otherwise.
* **What could be improved in the graduation ceremony?** (`graduation_ceremony_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required): Select areas where you think improvements are needed.
* **Any additional comments?** (`graduation_ceremony_comments`, `text`, required): Share your thoughts and suggestions on anything you would like to be improved or mention.
* **How many people attended the graduation ceremony?** (`graduation_ceremony_attendance`, `number`, required): Estimate the number of people who attended.
* **How long did you stay at the graduation ceremony?** (`graduation_ceremony_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration that best reflects how long you stayed.
