# Weekly Meetings Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from participants in weekly meetings. It is meant to be a straightforward and user-friendly way to collect valuable information that can help improve future meetings and foster a more engaging and productive environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Select the meeting date from the calendar picker or enter the date manually.
2. Write a brief description of the meeting in the "Meeting Notes" field.
3. Fill in your name and company (if applicable) in the "Participant Name" and "Participant Company" fields.
4. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
5. Describe your role in the meeting in the "Participant Role" field.
6. Answer the question "What did you learn?" in the "What did you learn?" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (1): Enter the date of the meeting you are providing feedback on. 
* **Meeting Notes** (2): Briefly describe what happened during the meeting, including key takeaways or important points discussed.
* **Participant Name** (3): Type your name as it appears on your business card or email signature.
* **Participant Email** (4): Enter your professional email address to help us contact you for follow-up questions or clarification.
* **Participant Company** (5): If you work for a company, enter your company name.
* **Participant Role** (6): Describe your role or position within your company (e.g., CEO, Project Manager, Team Member).
* **What did you learn?** (7): Share your insights, key takeaways, or lessons learned from the meeting.
* **Comments** (8): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions for future meetings.
