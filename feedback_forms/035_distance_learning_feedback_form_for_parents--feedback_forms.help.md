# distance_learning_feedback_form_for_parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents on their experience with distance learning programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please enter your feedback and suggestions for improvement in the text boxes provided.
2. Each text box has a label indicating the specific question or topic you are providing feedback on.
3. You can submit your feedback by clicking the "submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **feedback_form_for_parents** (`feedback_form_for_parents`, text, required/optional): A text box where you can enter your general feedback on the distance learning program.
* **feedback_question_1** (`feedback_question_1`, text, required/optional): A text box where you can provide feedback on the first specific question/topic.
* **feedback_question_2** (`feedback_question_2`, text, required/optional): A text box where you can provide feedback on the second specific question/topic.
* **feedback_question_3** (`feedback_question_3`, text, required/optional): A text box where you can provide feedback on the third specific question/topic.
* **feedback_question_4** (`feedback_question_4`, text, required/optional): A text box where you can provide feedback on the fourth specific question/topic.
* **feedback_question_5** (`feedback_question_5`, text, required/optional): A text box where you can provide feedback on the fifth specific question/topic.
* **feedback_question_6** (`feedback_question_6`, text, required/optional): A text box where you can provide feedback on the sixth specific question/topic.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): A button to submit your feedback.
Note: All fields are text boxes, and each has a label indicating the specific question or topic you are providing feedback on.
