# Food Taste Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your honest feedback about a food product's taste. Your input will help us understand your preferences and make informed decisions to improve our food offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the food product you're providing feedback on in the "Product Name" field.
2. Describe your experience with the product in the "Food Description" field.
3. Rate the taste of the food on a scale from 1-5 in the "Taste Rating" field.
4. If you have a strong opinion about the food's taste, select the "Strongly Like" or "Strongly Dislike" option in the "Like/Dislike Scale" field.
5. If you have a nuanced opinion, choose "Somewhat Like" or "Somewhat Dislike" in the "Like/Dislike Scale" field.
6. Finally, rate the overall experience with the food on a scale from 1-5 in the "Overall Rating" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Taste Testing Form** (`food_taste_testing_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form that appears on the page.
* **Taste Rating** (`taste_rating`, number, required): Rate the taste of the food on a scale from 1-5.
* **Food Description** (`food_description`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the food in as much detail as possible.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the title of the food product you're providing feedback on.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the overall experience with the food on a scale from 1-5.
* **Like/Dislike Scale** (`like_dislike_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your strong or nuanced opinion about the food's taste. Select one or more of the options: "Strongly Like", "Somewhat Like", "Neither", "Somewhat Dislike", or "Strongly Dislike".

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the "Food Description" field.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you've already filled out this form for the same product, consider revising your existing response rather than resubmitting.
