# in_car_communication_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with in-car communication tools.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review each page carefully and fill out the relevant fields. The form is split into several pages, each with a specific topic.
2. You can move between pages using the navigation buttons.
3. Fill out each field according to the explanations below.
4. Don't worry if you're not sure about anything; just do your best, and we'll help you with any questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **In-Car Communication Feedback Form (Page 1)** (`intro_page`, note, required: false): This page is just a brief introduction to let you know what to expect in this form. Don't worry about filling anything out here.
* **What did you think about the communication style of your driver?** (`intro_question`, text, required: false): Please type your thoughts about how clear and effective the communication was. Be as specific as possible.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style_score`, number, required: false): Rate the communication style of your driver, from 1 to 10.
* **How comfortable were you during the trip?** (`communication_comfort`, number, required: false): Rate how comfortable you felt during the trip, from 1 to 10.
* **How clear were the instructions given to you?** (`communication_clarity`, number, required: false): Rate how clear you thought the instructions were, from 1 to 10.
* **Clarity Scale** (`communication_clarity_scale`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the levels that apply to how clear the instructions were. Choose from 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7-8, or 9-10.
* **How did you feel during the trip?** (`communication_emotion`, select_one, required: false): Select how you felt during the trip from Anxious, Comfortable, Neutral, Happy, Angry, Sad, Surprised, Scared, Relaxed.
* **How satisfied were you with the communication?** (`communication_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the communication, from 1 to 10.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): If you'd like to add any additional information or comments about your experience, this is the place to do so.
* **Tool Used** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select which tool you used for in-car communication, from chatjim, chatbot, or webapp.
