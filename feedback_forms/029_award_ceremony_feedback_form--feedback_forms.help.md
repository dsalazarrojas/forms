# Award Ceremony Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of an award ceremony event. It aims to gather information about their experience, satisfaction, and overall perception of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Attendee Full Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Email Address**, which will be used to contact you if needed.
3. Indicate the **Ceremony Name**, which is the title of the event you attended.
4. Choose the date of the **Ceremony Date** when you attended the event.
5. Rate the following aspects of the event:
	* **Overall Event Satisfaction** (1 to 5)
	* **Venue and Atmosphere Quality** (1 to 5)
	* **Food and Beverage Quality** (1 to 5)
	* **Ceremony Pacing and Timing** (1 to 5)
	* **Host and MC Performance** (1 to 5)
6. If you have a favorite part of the ceremony, **What part did you like most?** (optional)
7. If you have suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the **Areas for Improvement** field (optional)
8. Indicate your **Likelihood of Attending Next Year** (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely)
9. Choose where you **Hear about the event** (Direct Email, Social Media, Colleague/Friend, Company Website, Newsletter)
10. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments** or general feedback you might have (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Ceremony Name** (`ceremony_name`, text, required): Type the title of the event you attended.
* **Ceremony Date** (`ceremony_date`, date, required): Select the date you attended the event.
* **Overall Event Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event from 1 to 5.
* **Venue and Atmosphere Quality** (`venue_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the venue and atmosphere from 1 to 5.
* **Food and Beverage Quality** (`catering_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of food and beverages from 1 to 5.
* **Ceremony Pacing and Timing** (`pacing_timing`, select_one, required): Rate the pacing and timing of the ceremony from 1 to 5.
* **Host and MC Performance** (`mc_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the performance of the host and MC from 1 to 5.
* **What part did you like most?** (`favorite_part`, text, optional): Describe the highlight of the ceremony.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Suggest changes or improvements for future events.
* **Likelihood of Attending Next Year** (`attendance_likelihood`, select_one, required): Indicate your likelihood of attending the event next year.
* **How did you hear about the event?** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Choose how you learned about the event.
* **Any other comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional thoughts or feedback about the event.
