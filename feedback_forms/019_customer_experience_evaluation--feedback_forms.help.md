# Customer Experience Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your experience with our services and gather feedback for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1.  Review the introduction page and understand the purpose of the form.
2.  For the "Rating" page, select your level of satisfaction with our services using the options provided.
3.  On the "Comment" page, provide any suggestions or comments about your experience with our services.
4.  On the "Contact Information" page, provide your personal contact information, including your name, job title, and email address.
5.  On the "Preferences" page, select your preferred contact method(s) and provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Welcome to the customer experience evaluation form**:
    *   This is the introduction page that explains the purpose of the form and its importance.
*   **How satisfied are you with our services**:
    *   This is a select-one question where you can choose your level of satisfaction with our services. The options are Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, and Dissatisfied.
*   **What would you like to suggest**:
    *   This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions or comments about your experience with our services.
*   **How would you like to be contacted**:
    *   This is a text field where you can enter how you would like to be contacted (e.g. phone, email, etc.).
*   **When did you last visit us?**:
    *   This is a date field where you can enter the date of your last visit.
*   **At what time did you last visit us?**:
    *   This is a time field where you can enter the time of your last visit.
*   **Any other comments**:
    *   This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
*   **Thank you for taking the time to help us improve**:
    *   This is a text field where you can enter any closing comments or messages.
*   **Name**:
    *   This is a text field where you can enter your name.
*   **Company**:
    *   This is a text field where you can enter your company name.
*   **Job Title**:
    *   This is a text field where you can enter your job title.
*   **Email**:
    *   This is an email field where you can enter your email address.
*   **Preferred contact method**:
    *   This is a select-multiple question where you can choose your preferred contact method. The options are Phone, Email, and Other (please specify).

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled in order to submit the form. The fields marked as optional can be left blank if not desired.
