# Prosthetic Solutions Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients on their experience with Prosthetic Solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide a description of your experience with Prosthetic Solutions in the "Feedback" field.
4. Rate your experience by selecting one of the options in the "Rating" field. The options are "Good", "Fair", and "Bad".
5. Select the corresponding rating score in the "Rating" fields 1-5.
6. Finally, submit the form by selecting "Yes" in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (patient_name, text, required): Enter the name of the patient who is providing feedback.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address (if you want to be contacted for follow-up).
* **Feedback** (feedback, text, optional): Describe your experience with Prosthetic Solutions. This can include any comments, suggestions, or complaints you may have.
* **Rating** (rating, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with Prosthetic Solutions as "Good", "Fair", or "Bad".
* **Rating 1** (rating_0, text, optional): Select the corresponding rating score for a 1-star rating.
* **Rating 2** (rating_1, text, optional): Select the corresponding rating score for a 2-star rating.
* **Rating 3** (rating_2, text, optional): Select the corresponding rating score for a 3-star rating.
* **Rating 4** (rating_3, text, optional): Select the corresponding rating score for a 4-star rating.
* **Rating 5** (rating_4, text, optional): Select the corresponding rating score for a 5-star rating.
* **Submit** (submit_button, select_one, optional): Submit your feedback form by selecting "Yes". 

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Please be as descriptive as possible in the "Feedback" field.
- If you have any issues with submitting the form, please contact Prosthetic Solutions support team.
