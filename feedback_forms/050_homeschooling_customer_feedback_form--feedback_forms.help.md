# Homeschooling Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from homeschooling customers, providing valuable insights to improve the quality and effectiveness of homeschooling programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`): A text field where you can share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about your experience with the homeschooling program.
2. **Name** (`name`): Enter your name to help us identify you and understand your feedback better.
3. **Email** (`email`): Your email address will be used to contact you if we need more information or clarification about your feedback.
4. **Program Enrollment** (`program_enrollment`): Select the type of program you are enrolled in, such as:
	* Homeschooling
	* Public School
	* Private School
5. **Program Type** (`program_type`): Indicate the type of program you are enrolled in, such as:
	* Full-time
	* Part-time
6. **School District ID** (`school_distrct_id`): This is an optional field for those who want to provide additional information about their school district.
7. **Teacher Interaction** (`teacher_interaction`): If you have any suggestions or comments about your teacher's interaction, please share them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback that can help improve the program.
* If you have any concerns or suggestions, please provide as much detail as possible.
* You can add as many lines as you need to provide your feedback, and you can also upload attachments or files if necessary.
* Once you submit your feedback, you will receive a confirmation email with a copy of your form responses.
