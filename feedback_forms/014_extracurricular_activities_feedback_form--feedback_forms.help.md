# Extracurricular Activities Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Extracurricular Activities Feedback Form is a tool for gathering feedback from participants in extracurricular activities. It is used to help organizers and staff members understand what participants thought of the event, and to identify areas where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as requested. You will be asked to provide your name and email address, and to choose how often you participate in extracurricular activities and how you found the event. If you have a favorite part of the event, you can also share that with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`main_page_1_name`, text, required): This is the field for your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`main_page_1_name_1`, email, required): This is the field for your email address. Please enter your official email address where you can be contacted.
* **How often do you participate in extracurricular activities?** (`main_page_1_name_2`, select_one, optional): This is where you tell us how often you take part in extracurricular activities. Please choose one of the options provided:
	+ Every week
	+ Every 2 weeks
	+ Every 4 weeks
	+ Rarely
* **How often do you participate in extracurricular activities?** (`main_page_1_name_2_1`, select_one, optional): This is another way of asking the same question as the above field. Please choose one of the options:
	+ Every week
	+ Every 2 weeks
	+ Every 4 weeks
	+ Rarely
* **How did you find the event?** (`main_page_2_1`, select_multiple, optional): This is where you share your overall experience of the event. Please choose from the options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Rating** (`main_page_2_1_1`, number, optional): This is where you can rate your experience of the event on a scale of 1-10. Please note that this field is not required.
* **What was your favorite part of the event?** (`main_page_2_1_2`, text, optional): This is where you can share your favorite part of the event. Please be as specific as possible and tell us what made this part of the event special for you.
