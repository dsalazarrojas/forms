# Service Desk Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback on our service desk's performance. Your input will help us identify areas where we're doing well and areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly to the best of your knowledge.
2. For numerical fields, rate your experiences on a scale of 0 to 10 or select from the provided options.
3. For fields asking about communication channels, select the one(s) you most often use or prefer.
4. If you have any issues or concerns that have not been resolved, please provide the date and time it started and ended (or has not ended yet).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
• **Welcome to the Survey** (Introduction, Note, not required): A brief introduction to the survey, explaining its purpose and importance.

### Service Quality
• **How Do You Rate the Service Quality?** (Number, 0-10, not required): Rate our service quality on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is very poor and 10 is excellent.

### Communication Style
• **How Do You Rate the Communication Style?** (Number, 0-10, not required): Rate our communication style on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is very poor and 10 is excellent.

### Resolution Time
• **How Well Was Your Issue Resolved?** (Number, 0-10, not required): Rate how well your issue was resolved on a scale of 0 to 10, where 0 is not at all and 10 is completely resolved.

### Communication Channel
• **Which Communication Channel Were You Most Often Use?** (Select one, Email, Phone, Web, In-app, not required): Select the communication channel you most often use to interact with our service desk.

### Communication Channel 2
• **How Did You Prefer to Get in Touch?** (Select multiple, Phone, Email, Web, In-app, not required): Select the communication channels you prefer to get in touch with our service desk.

### Resolution Time 2
• **How Quick Was Your Issue Resolved?** (Select one, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, More than 6 hours, Not yet resolved, not required): Select the time frame in which your issue was resolved.

### Resolution Time 3
• **How Long It Took to Resolve Your Issue** (Date, not required): If available, provide the date and time it started and ended (or has not ended yet) for any unresolved issues.

### Resolution Time 4
• **How Long It Took to Resolve Your Issue 2** (Number, not required): Rate the time it took to resolve your issue on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is less than an hour and 10 is more than 6 hours.

### Resolution Time 5
• **How Long Did It Take to Resolve Your Issue** (Time, not required): Rate the time it took to resolve your issue in hours and minutes.
