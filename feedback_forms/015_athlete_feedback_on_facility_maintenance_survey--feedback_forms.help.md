# Athlete Feedback on Facility Maintenance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from athletes on the maintenance and condition of sports facilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the primary facility you use most frequently from the dropdown menu. 
2. Choose your frequency of use from the options provided.
3. Enter your primary sport or activity.
4. Rate the overall cleanliness of the facility from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
5. Rate the restrooms and locker rooms hygiene from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
6. Rate the surface cleanliness (e.g. floors, mats, turf) from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
7. Rate the equipment functionality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
8. Rate the lighting quality and visibility from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
9. Rate the temperature and ventilation from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
10. Describe any broken or unsafe equipment.
11. Rate your overall feeling of safety within the facility from 1 (Very Unsafe) to 5 (Very Safe).
12. Rate the condition of safety and directional signage from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
13. Rate the accessibility of first aid kits and AEDs from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
14. Provide suggestions for improvement.
15. Specify the top maintenance priority.
16. Provide general suggestions for facility enhancement.
17. Select whether you would like a response from the facility manager.
18. Enter your email address if you would like a response.
19. Enter the date of your feedback submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Facility Evaluated** (`facility_selected`): Select the primary facility you use most frequently.
* **Frequency of Facility Use** (`frequency_of_use`): Choose your frequency of use.
* **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`): Enter your primary sport or activity.
* **Overall Facility Cleanliness** (`overall_cleanliness`): Rate the overall cleanliness of the facility from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Restroom and Locker Room Hygiene** (`hygiene_rating`): Rate the restrooms and locker rooms hygiene from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Surface Cleanliness** (`surface_cleanliness`): Rate the surface cleanliness from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Equipment Functionality** (`equipment_functionality`): Rate the equipment functionality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Lighting Quality and Visibility** (`lighting_quality`): Rate the lighting quality and visibility from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Temperature and Ventilation** (`climate_control`): Rate the temperature and ventilation from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Did you notice any broken or unsafe equipment?** (`broken_equipment_notes`): Describe any broken or unsafe equipment you have noticed.
* **Overall Feeling of Safety within the Facility** (`safety_feeling`): Rate your overall feeling of safety within the facility from 1 (Very Unsafe) to 5 (Very Safe).
* **Condition of Safety and Directional Signage** (`signage_condition`): Rate the condition of safety and directional signage from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Accessibility of First Aid Kits and AEDs** (`first_aid_accessibility`): Rate the accessibility of first aid kits and AEDs from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`maintenance_priority`): Provide suggestions for improvement.
* **Top Maintenance Priority** (`general_suggestions`): Specify the top maintenance priority.
* **General Suggestions for Facility Enhancement** (`response_requested`): Provide general suggestions for facility enhancement.
* **Would you like a response from the facility manager?** (`response_requested`): Select whether you would like a response from the facility manager.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address if you requested a response.
* **Date of Feedback Submission** (`feedback_date`): Enter the date of your feedback submission.
