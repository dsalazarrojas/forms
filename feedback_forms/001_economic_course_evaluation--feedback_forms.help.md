# Economic Course Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students on various aspects of an economic course, including content, organization, and rating. Your input will help instructors and administrators improve the course and provide a better learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the name of the course.
2. Provide the instructor's name.
3. Indicate your experience with the course outcome (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
4. Rate the relevance of the course content (1-5, 6-10, 11-15).
5. Rate the course organization (1-5, 6-10, 11-15).
6. Share any suggestions for improvement.
7. Select your level of satisfaction with the course (Very High, High, Medium, Low).
8. Rate the overall satisfaction (1-5, 6-10, 11-15).
9. Indicate whether you highly recommend, recommend, or not recommend the course.
10. Provide additional feedback on the course.
11. Rate and reason your rating on multiple rating scales (1-5, 6-10, 11-15).
12. Provide overall comments on the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course instructor.
* **Course Outcome** (`course_outcome`, select_one, optional): Select how you experienced the course outcome (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
* **Course Relevance** (`course_relevance`, number, optional): Rate the course content from 1-5, 6-10, or 11-15.
* **Course Organization** (`course_organization`, number, optional): Rate the course organization from 1-5, 6-10, or 11-15.
* **Suggestion For Improvement** (`suggestion_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for improving the course.
* **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of satisfaction with the course (Very High, High, Medium, Low).
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the course from 1-5, 6-10, or 11-15.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the course from 1-5, 6-10, or 11-15.
* **Course Recommendation** (`course_recommendation`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you highly recommend, recommend, or not recommend the course.
* **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback on the course.
* **Course Comments** (`course_comments`, text, optional): Share any comments about the course.
* **Course Rating Scale** (`course_rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Rate the course on multiple rating scales (1-5, 6-10, 11-15).
* **Course Rating** (`course_rating`, number, optional): Rate the course.
* **Course Rating Reason** (`course_rating_reason`, text, optional): Explain your rating reason.
* **Course Recommendation 2** (`course_recommendation_2`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether you highly recommend, recommend, or not recommend the course.
* **Overall Comments** (`overall_comments`, text, optional): Share any overall comments about the course.

## Tips

- Please be as specific as possible when filling out the form to provide valuable feedback.
- You can select multiple options for certain fields, such as course satisfaction and course recommendation.
- If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Feedback Details" or "Overall Comments" fields.
- If you experience any issues while filling out the form, please contact the course administrators.
