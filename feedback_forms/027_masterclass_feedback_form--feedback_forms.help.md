# Masterclass Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback about your experience with Masterclass. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our services and provide a better experience for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please complete the following form. Each step is described below:

1. Fill out the **First name** field with your first name.
2. Fill out the **Last name** field with your last name.
3. Provide your **Feedback** about your Masterclass experience in the text field. Be as detailed as you'd like.
4. Rate your overall **Rating** of the Masterclass, on a scale of 1-5.
5. Enter your **Email** address if you'd like us to follow up with you about your feedback.
6. If you have a **Phone** number, we'd be happy to call you back.
7. Select a **Date** when you attended the Masterclass, if known.
8. Select a **Time** when you attended the Masterclass, if known.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, optional): This is the first part of your name.
* **Last name** (text, optional): This is the last part of your name.
* **Feedback** (text, optional): This is where you can provide detailed feedback about your Masterclass experience.
* **Rating** (number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like us to follow up with you.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to call you back.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you attended the Masterclass, if known.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you attended the Masterclass, if known.
