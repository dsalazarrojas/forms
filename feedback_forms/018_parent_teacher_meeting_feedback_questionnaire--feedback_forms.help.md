# parent_teacher_meeting_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent-teacher meeting feedback questionnaire is designed to collect students' opinions on their meetings with teachers. This helps teachers and administrators identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the teaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the questionnaire, follow these steps:

1. **Meeting Details** (Text Field): Please describe the meeting you attended, including any notable events or discussions that took place.
2. Enter the details in the text field provided.
3. Proceed to the next question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Details (1)** (meeting_details, Text, Required: False): Describe the meeting you attended. This will help teachers understand what happened during the meeting.
* **Meeting Outcome (2)** (meeting_outcome, Number, Required: False): Rate the overall outcome of the meeting, with 1 being Very Unsatisfied and 5 being Very Satisfied.
* **Teacher Performance (3)** (teacher_performance, Select Multiple, Required: False): Choose all that apply from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **Teacher Support (4)** (teacher_support, Number, Required: False): Rate how well the teacher supported you during the meeting, with 1 being Very Unsatisfied and 5 being Very Satisfied.
* **Meeting Satisfaction (5)** (meeting_satisfaction, Select One, Required: False): Choose your level of satisfaction with the meeting:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Unsatisfied
	+ Very Unsatisfied
* **Comments (6)** (comments, Note, Required: False): If you have any additional comments about the meeting, please enter them here.
* **Email (7)** (email, Email, Required: False): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Contact Number (8)** (contact_number, Text, Required: False): If you have a preferred contact number, please enter it.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* Rate your experience based on how you felt about the meeting.
* If you need help, don't hesitate to ask.
* All fields are optional, but your feedback is valuable, so please complete as many as you can.
