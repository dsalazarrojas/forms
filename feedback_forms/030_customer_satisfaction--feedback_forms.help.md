# Customer Satisfaction - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from customers who have interacted with the company. This information will help us understand your experience and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question in the form, and select the most accurate option for each multiple-choice question.
2. If a question is not applicable to you, please mark the "N/A" or "Not Applicable" option.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the text box at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or contact information.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): If you want to be contacted or would like more information from us, enter your email address here.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **What is your relationship with this company?** (`question_1`): Select the option that best describes your connection with our company.
* **What was the main purpose of your visit?** (`question_2`): Choose the options that apply to your visit or interaction with us.
* **How do you rate the quality of our product?** (`question_3`): Rate our product from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Have you contacted our customer service?** (`question_4`): Select the option that applies to your experience with our customer service.
* **Were you aware of any technical issues during your last visit?** (`question_5`): Mark all technical issues you experienced during your visit.
* **Have you any suggestions for improvement?** (`question_6`): Share your thoughts on how we could improve our services.
* **Do you want to receive future updates from us?** (`question_7`): If you'd like to stay informed about new developments, select "Yes".
* **Do you want to be entered in our survey pool?** (`question_8`): If you're willing to participate in future surveys, choose "Yes".
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`question_9`): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Do you want to get in contact with us?** (`question_10`): If you have any questions or need assistance, select "Yes".
* **Do you have any comments?** (`question_11`): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions you have about our services.
* **Do you want to be entered in our survey pool?** (`question_12`): This question is a duplicate of question 8 and is intended for consistency purposes only.

Tips:

* Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If a question does not apply to you, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable".
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
