# Storytelling Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your thoughts and feelings about a story you've read. Your responses will help us create engaging and relatable stories that resonate with our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each question and answer according to your true feelings and experiences. You can type in the answer boxes provided. If you're not sure or have no opinion, choose 'unsure', 'False', or 'True' accordingly. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How would you describe your favorite story?** (`story_form_1`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your favorite story. This will help us understand what aspects of a story you enjoy.
- **What do you like about this story?** (`story_form_2`, text, required/optional): Share your thoughts on what you like about this story. This will help us identify engaging elements that resonate with our audience.
- **What do you dislike about this story?** (`story_form_3`, text, required/optional): If there's anything you don't like about this story, please share it with us. This will help us address any issues and improve the storytelling process.
- **How likely are you to recommend this story?** (`story_form_4`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how likely you are to recommend this story to a friend or family member.
- **How likely are you to participate in storytelling activities?** (`story_form_5`, number, required/optional): Indicate how likely you are to participate in more storytelling activities.
- **What is the most important element of this story?** (`story_form_6`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the most critical aspect of the story that resonated with you.
- **How would you rate the engagement of this story?** (`story_form_7`, number, required/optional): Rate how engaging you found the story to be.
- **What would you like to see more of in this story?** (`story_form_8`, text, required/optional): Share what you think would enhance the story and make it more engaging.
- **What would you like to see less of in this story?** (`story_form_9`, text, required/optional): If there's anything you dislike in the story, please let us know.
- **How important is storytelling to you?** (`story_form_10`, number, required/optional): Rate the importance of storytelling to you.
- **What is the main theme of this story?** (`story_form_11`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the underlying theme of the story.
- **Is there anything you want to add about this story?** (`story_form_12`, text, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the story.
- **Is the story clear and easy to understand?** (`story_form_13`, select_one, required/optional): Let us know if the story is clear and easy to comprehend.
- **How long did it take you to read the story?** (`story_form_14`, time, required/optional): Estimate the time it took for you to read the story.
- **What do you like most about this story?** (`story_form_15`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select what you enjoyed most about the story.
- **What do you dislike most about this story?** (`story_form_16`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select what you least enjoyed about the story.
- **Would you like to see more diverse representation in this story?** (`story_form_17`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if you'd like to see more diverse representation in the story.
- **Would you like to see more inclusive representation in this story?** (`story_form_18`, select_one, required/optional): Select if you'd like to see more inclusive representation in the story.
- **Would you like to see more representation of diverse perspectives in this story?** (`story_form_19`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if you'd like to see more diverse perspectives in the story.
- **Would you like to see more representation of underrepresented groups in this story?** (`story_form_20`, select_one, required/optional): Select if you'd like to see more underrepresented groups in this story.
- **How likely are you to recommend this story to a friend?** (`story_form_21`, select_one, required/optional): Choose how likely you are to recommend this story to a friend or family member.
- **How likely are you to participate in more storytelling activities?** (`story_form_22`, select_one, required/optional): Select how likely you are to participate in more storytelling activities.
- **Would you like to see more educational value in this story?** (`story_form_23`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if you'd like to see more educational value in the story.
- **How likely are you to purchase this story?** (`story_form_24`, select_one, required/optional): Select how likely you are to purchase this story.
- **Is the story relevant to your life?** (`story_form_25`, select_one, required/optional): Choose if the story resonates with your life experiences.
