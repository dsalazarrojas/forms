# Mission Statement Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on the effectiveness of the organization's mission statement. Please provide your honest and constructive feedback to help us improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the mission statement and make sure you are familiar with its content.
2. Provide feedback on the mission statement by filling in the "Mission" text field (user_input_1).
3. Specify the specific aspects of the mission statement that you found helpful or unclear (user_input_2).
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions for the mission statement, use the "Note" field (note).
5. Choose which stakeholder group you think the mission statement best serves (stakeholder).
6. Select all that apply for any of the following options:
	* True or False (submit)
	* Stakeholder 1
	* Stakeholder 2
	* Stakeholder 3
7. Enter your email address for us to contact you with updates (email).
8. Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion (phone).
9. Enter any additional comments or notes (date, time, date_time, decimal, integer, text_area, select_one_1, select_multiple_1, select_multiple_2, select_multiple_3, select_multiple_4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mission**: The "Mission" field is where you can enter your feedback on the mission statement.
* **Feedback**: The "Feedback" field is where you can specify the specific aspects of the mission statement that you found helpful or unclear.
* **Mission Statement**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have for the mission statement.
* **Stakeholder**: Please choose which group of stakeholders you think the mission statement best serves.
* **Submit**: Select all that apply to indicate if you found the mission statement helpful or not. True or False will be displayed as a checkbox in the form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Note**: This field is for any additional comments or notes.
* **Date**: Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time**: Enter a time in the format HH:MM.
* **Date Time**: Enter a date and time in the format YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM.
* **Decimal**: Enter a decimal value.
* **Integer**: Enter an integer value.
* **Text Area**: This field is for entering a longer piece of text.
* **Select 1**: Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple 1**: Choose all that apply from the list.
* **Select Multiple 2**: Choose all that apply from the list.
* **Select 2**: Choose one option from the list.
* **Select 3**: Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple 3**: Choose all that apply from the list.
* **Select Multiple 4**: Choose all that apply from the list.
* **Date Range**: Enter a date range in the format Start Date - End Date.
* **Time Range**: Enter a time range in the format Start Time - End Time.
* **Decimal Range**: Enter a decimal range in the format Start Decimal - End Decimal.
* **Integer Range**: Enter an integer range in the format Start Integer - End Integer.
* **Select 1**: Choose one option from the list.
* **Select 2**: Choose one option from the list.
* **Select 3**: Choose one option from the list.

## Tips
* Please provide clear and constructive feedback to help us improve the mission statement.
* Take your time to review the mission statement before providing feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or additional comments, use the "Note" field.
* We appreciate your input and will review all feedback carefully.
