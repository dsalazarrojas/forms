# Cross Team Collaboration Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand the dynamics of cross-team collaboration. It will help us to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about team management and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all team members who you have collaborated with in the past week/month/quarter (depending on the context).
2. Provide your feedback about your experiences with the team.
3. Choose your perception of the team's collaboration level, communication level, conflict resolution, trust level, time management, organization level, quality of work, work style, team leadership, team dynamics, and team work style.
4. Leave any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Members**: Select all team members you have collaborated with in the past week/month/quarter. This can include team members who you have worked with directly or indirectly.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts, opinions, and experiences about your collaboration with the team. Be honest and specific about what went well and what didn't.
* **Collaboration Level**: Choose how high or low you think the team's collaboration level is. This will help us understand how well team members are working together.
* **Communication Level**: Choose how high or low you think the team's communication level is. This includes how well team members share ideas and information with each other.
* **Conflict Resolution**: Choose whether or not you have experienced any conflicts within the team. If you've had any issues, we'd love to hear about them so we can learn from your experiences.
* **Trust Level**: Choose how high or low you think the team members trust each other. This is crucial for effective collaboration and decision-making.
* **Time Management**: Choose whether or not you think the team is effective with time management. This includes meeting deadlines, prioritizing tasks, and avoiding procrastination.
* **Organization Level**: Choose how high or low you think the team's organization level is. This includes how well the team is structured, managed, and resourced.
* **Quality of Work**: Choose how good or poor you think the team's quality of work is. This can include factors like timeliness, accuracy, and attention to detail.
* **Work Style**: Choose whether or not you think the team's work style is effective. This includes how well team members adapt to changes, take initiative, and support each other.
* **Feedback Frequency**: Leave any additional comments or suggestions you may have about how often the team provides feedback.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have for improving team collaboration and performance.
* **Open Comments**: Leave any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the team.
* **Team Leadership**: Choose whether or not you think the team leadership is effective.
* **Team Dynamics**: Choose whether or not you think the team's dynamics are good or poor.
* **Team Work Style**: Choose whether or not you think the team's work style is effective.
