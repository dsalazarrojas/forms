# Activity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants on their experience with the activity. Your input will help us to identify areas of improvement and make future events more enjoyable for you and others.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your name if you wish to be identified in the feedback.
2.  Enter the name of the activity you participated in.
3.  Select the date you participated in the activity from the calendar.
4.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the activity on a 5-point scale.
5.  Rate the quality of the activity and the performance of the staff based on your experience.
6.  Rate the condition of the facilities.
7.  Choose how you think the activity provided value for money.
8.  Indicate how likely you are to recommend the activity to others (1-5 scale).
9.  (Optional) Mention what you liked most about the activity.
10. (Optional) Provide any suggestions for improvement.
11. (Optional) Enter any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Optional)**: Please enter your name if you wish to be identified in the feedback. This is not required.
* **Name of Activity**: Enter the name of the activity you participated in. This information is required.
* **Date of Activity**: Select the date you participated in the activity from the calendar. This is required.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the activity on a 5-point scale:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Quality of the Activity**: Rate the quality of the activity:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Staff Performance**: Rate the performance of the staff:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Condition of Facilities**: Rate the condition of the facilities:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Value for Money**: Choose how you think the activity provided value for money:
	+ Excellent value
	+ Good value
	+ Fair value
	+ Poor value
	+ Very poor value
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Indicate how likely you are to recommend the activity to others on a 1-5 scale (1 = Not likely, 5 = Highly likely).
* **Positive Aspects**: If you wish, provide what you liked most about the activity. This is optional.
* **Areas for Improvement**: If you wish, suggest what could be improved about the activity. This is optional.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other feedback about the activity. This is optional.
