# Coaching Session Client Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from coaching clients about their experiences in a coaching session. The collected feedback will help coaches refine their skills, identify areas for improvement, and provide a better experience for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Answer all questions thoroughly and honestly.
*   Make sure you have attended a coaching session before submitting your feedback.
*   You can use the provided hints for guidance on each question.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Your Name (id: 2)
*   **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please provide your first and last name.
*   *Note: This information is required to ensure we know which client you are.*

### 2. Coach Name (id: 3)
*   **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Please provide the name of your coach.
*   *Note: This information is required to ensure we know which coach you are referring to.*

### 3. Session Date (id: 4)
*   **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Please provide the date of the coaching session.
*   *Note: This information is required to ensure we track the session accurately.*

### 4. Primary Focus of this Session (id: 5)
*   **Primary Focus of this Session** (`session_focus`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the primary focus of the session.
*   *Note: This information is optional, but will help the coach to better understand the session context.*

### 5. Overall Satisfaction (id: 7)
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session, from 1 (very unsatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand your overall experience with the coach.*

### 6. Did this Session Meet your Expectations? (id: 8)
*   **Did this Session Meet your Expectations?** (`session_met_expectations`, select_one, required): Rate how well the session met your expectations, from 1 (far below expectations) to 5 (far exceeded expectations).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand areas for improvement.*

### 7. Coach Performance (id: 9)
*   **Coach Performance** (`coach_preparation`, note, required): This is a note section for additional feedback on the coach's performance.
*   *Note: You can provide any additional comments or feedback on the coach's performance.*

### 8. Coach Preparation (id: 10)
*   **Coach was well-prepared and organized** (`coach_preparation`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's preparation for the session, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand if the coach was prepared for the session.*

### 9. Coach Listening (id: 11)
*   **Coach Listened attentively to your concerns** (`coach_listening`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's listening skills, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand if the coach listened attentively to your concerns.*

### 10. Coach Engagement (id: 12)
*   **Coach was engaged and enthusiastic** (`coach_engagement`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's engagement, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand if the coach was engaged and enthusiastic during the session.*

### 11. Coach Expertise (id: 13)
*   **Coach demonstrated strong expertise** (`coach_expertise`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's expertise, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand if the coach demonstrated strong expertise during the session.*

### 12. Learned Something (id: 15)
*   **You learned something valuable** (`learned_something`, select_one, required): Rate the value you gained from the session, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand if you found the session valuable.*

### 13. Received Actionable Advice (id: 16)
*   **Received actionable advice and tools** (`actionable_advice`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the advice and tools provided, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand if you found the advice and tools helpful.*

### 14. Clear on Next Steps (id: 17)
*   **Clear on next steps and action items** (`clear_next_steps`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of the next steps and action items, from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree).
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand if you were clear on what to do next.*

### 15. Feedback for Improvement (id: 18)
*   **Feedback for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, note, required): This is a note section for you to provide any feedback or suggestions on how the coach can improve.
*   *Note: You can provide any constructive feedback or suggestions on how the coach can improve.*

### 16. What Went Well (id: 19)
*   **What went well in this session** (`what_went_well`, text, optional): Please provide any positive feedback or experiences you had during the session.
*   *Note: This information is optional, but will help the coach to understand what they are doing well.*

### 17. Improvement Suggestions (id: 20)
*   **What could be improved** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Please provide any constructive feedback or suggestions on areas for improvement.
*   *Note: This information is optional, but will help the coach to identify areas for improvement.*

### 18. Would You Recommend (id: 22)
*   **Would you recommend this coach to others** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Rate your willingness to recommend the coach to others, from "Yes, definitely" to "No, definitely not".
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand the coach's reputation and effectiveness.*

### 19. Future Sessions (id: 23)
*   **Would you like to book another session** (`future_sessions`, select_one, optional): Rate your interest in booking another session with the coach, from "Yes" to "No".
*   *Note: This question will help us to understand your interest in future sessions.*
