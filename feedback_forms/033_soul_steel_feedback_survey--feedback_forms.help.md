# Soul_Steel_Feedback_Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with Soul Steel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit it when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome_to_Soul_Steel**: This is a brief introduction page that provides background information about the form.
  - **How_satisfying_was_the_soul_steel_event**: Rate how satisfying the event was, on a scale of 1-10.
  - **How_satisfying_were_the_soul_steel_staff**: Rate how satisfying the staff was, on a scale of 1-10.
  - **Suggest Improvements**: Provide any suggestions for improvements for future events.
  - **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback.
  - **phone**: Enter your phone number for our records and in case we need to contact you.
Note: These fields are not required, but it's helpful for us to have your contact information.
