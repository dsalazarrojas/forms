# Transportation Safety Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on transportation-related incidents or near-misses. The purpose of this form is to gather information on the circumstances surrounding the incident, including the mode of transportation used, the type of vehicle involved, and any comments or observations. This feedback will help us to improve transportation safety policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the transportation mode that best describes the incident**: On the first page of the form, select the mode of transportation that was involved in the incident (e.g. car, bus, train, etc.).
2. **Choose all applicable vehicle types**: On the second page, select all types of vehicles that were involved in the incident (e.g. car, truck, bike, etc.).
3. **Rate the safety level**: On the third page, rate the safety level of the incident on a scale of 1-100, where 1 is a high-risk incident and 100 is a low-risk incident.
4. **Submit your comments**: On the final page, enter any additional comments or observations about the incident that you think are relevant to the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transportation Mode**: (`transportation_mode`, `select_one`, required): Select the mode of transportation that was involved in the incident (e.g. car, bus, train, etc.).
* **Vehicle Type**: (`vehicle_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all types of vehicles that were involved in the incident (e.g. car, truck, bike, etc.).
* **Safety Level**: (`safety_level`, `number`, required): Rate the safety level of the incident on a scale of 1-100, where 1 is a high-risk incident and 100 is a low-risk incident.
* **Comments**: (`comments`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or observations about the incident that you think are relevant to the feedback.

## Tips

* Please select all modes of transportation that were involved in the incident.
* If you are unsure about the safety level, please rate it based on your best judgment.
* Use the comments field to provide as much detail as possible about the incident.
