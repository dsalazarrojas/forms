# theme-park-attraction-inquiry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The theme-park-attraction-inquiry-form is designed to collect information and feedback from customers about their experiences with theme park attractions. This data helps theme park management identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the ride that you visited and are inquiring about from the dropdown menu on Page 1.
2. Enter your name and any other relevant details on Page 1.
3. Choose a date for the visit or the date when you plan to visit on Page 2.
4. Enter your feedback or comments on Page 2.
5. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure that all required fields (if any) are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Ride 1** (`Select one of the following options`):
* Choose the name of the ride you are inquiring about from the dropdown menu. This is the first step in providing accurate feedback.

### **Name** (`Text Field`):
* Enter your name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or as you wish to be identified in the inquiry.

### **Date** (`Date Field`):
* Select the date of your visit or the date when you plan to visit. This helps us track the timing and frequency of visits.

### **Feedback** (`Text Field`):
* Share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns about your experience with the theme park. Your feedback is valuable to us and will contribute to improving our services.

### **Tips**:
* Please note that all fields marked as required will need to be filled in for a successful submission. If you're unsure about a particular field, you can contact the form administrators for assistance.
* If you've experienced any issues during your visit, please provide as much detail as possible for our team to better understand the situation.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts about your theme park experience. Your input helps us make informed decisions to improve our services.
