# Contact Form Busy City - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Form Busy City is a tool for event attendees to provide feedback and follow-up requests related to events organized by our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the type of message you want to send using the "Feedback type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
3. If you have a phone number, please provide it in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select the event you attended from the "Event attended" field.
5. Provide a brief summary of your message in the "Message summary" field.
6. If you need a follow-up from our team, select "True" in the "Follow up required" field.
7. Choose your preferred contact method by selecting "Email", "Phone", or "Text" in the "Preferred contact method" field.
8. If you have any accessibility needs, please provide a note in the "Accessibility needs" field.
9. Finally, add any additional comments or information to help our team assist you in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **-- Event Contact Form --** (Header): This field is a note that indicates that you are about to enter contact information related to an event.
- **Attendee name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be addressed.
  * Please use your real name for official follow-up requests.
- **Attendee email address** (email, required): Enter your best email address for event updates and follow-up requests.
  * Use a valid email address that you check regularly.
- **Phone number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for urgent updates or if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
- **Event attended** (text, required): Select the event or booth you attended from the list.
  * If you are unsure which event or booth, you can check your event schedule or ask a member of our team.
- **Feedback type** (select_one, required): Select the type of message you want to send:
  * General feedback
  * Question
  * Complaint
  * Volunteer inquiry
  * Partnership request
- **Message summary** (text, required): Provide a brief summary of your request or feedback.
  * Keep it short and concise to help our team understand your needs.
- **Follow up required** (select_one, required): Check if you need a follow-up from our team:
  * True if you need a follow-up
  * False otherwise
- **Preferred contact method** (select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
  * Email
  * Phone
  * Text
- **Accessibility needs** (text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please provide a note.
  * This is optional but helpful for our team to understand your requirements.
- **Additional comments** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information to help our team assist you.
  * Keep it brief and to the point to ensure our team can address your needs efficiently.
- **True** (select_one, required) and **False** (select_one, required): These two options are used to determine if a follow-up is required.
