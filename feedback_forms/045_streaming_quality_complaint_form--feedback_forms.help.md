# Streaming Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and complaints from users regarding streaming quality issues. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us improve our streaming services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue you encountered in the "Issue Description" field.
3. Specify whether the issue occurred on a "Streaming Service" and select all that apply.
4. Enter the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" of when you experienced the issue.
5. Confirm if the "Issue Time Zone" applies to your report.
6. Enter your "Report User ID" and the "Report Date" and "Report Time".
7. Confirm if the "Report Time Zone" applies to your report.
8. Select the "Streaming Service Provider" affected by the issue.
9. Enter your comments on the issue in the "Report Comment" field (optional).
10. Specify the "Report Category", "Report Status", "Report Priority", "Report Assignee", "Report Resolution", "Report Closed", and "Report Closed Date" and "Report Closed Time" fields accordingly.
11. Confirm if the "Report Closed Time Zone" applies to your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (1): Enter your name to associate your feedback with your account.
* **Issue Description** (2): Provide a detailed description of the streaming quality issue you encountered.
* **Streaming Service** (3): Specify whether the issue occurred on a streaming service. Select all that apply.
* **Issue Date** (4): Enter the date when you experienced the issue.
* **Issue Time** (5): Enter the time when you experienced the issue.
* **Issue Time Zone** (6): Confirm if the issue time zone applies to your report.
* **Report User ID** (8): Enter your unique report user ID.
* **Report Date** (9): Enter the date of your report.
* **Report Time** (10): Enter the time of your report.
* **Report Time Zone** (11): Confirm if the report time zone applies to your report.
* **Streaming Service Provider** (12): Select the provider affected by the issue.
* **Report Comment** (13): Enter your comments on the issue (optional).
* **Report Category** (14): Specify the report category.
* **Report Status** (15): Specify the current report status.
* **Report Priority** (16): Specify the report priority.
* **Report Assignee** (17): Confirm who the report was assigned to.
* **Report Resolution** (18): Confirm if the report was resolved.
* **Report Closed** (19): Confirm if the report was closed.
* **Report Closed Date** (20): Enter the date when the report was closed.
* **Report Closed Time** (21): Enter the time when the report was closed.
* **Report Closed Time Zone** (22): Confirm if the report closed time zone applies to your report.
* **Report Comment** (23) and **Report Comments** (24): Enter your comments on the issue (optional).
