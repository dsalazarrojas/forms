# Reading Progress Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reading Progress Log form is used to track the reading progress of a student, collecting information about their reading activity and providing a log of their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the `student` field.
2. Provide information about their reading activity in the `reading activity` field.
3. Enter the frequency of their reading in the `reading frequency` field.
4. Enter the duration of their reading in the `reading duration` field.
5. Enter the date of their most recent reading in the `reading date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Reading Activity** (`reading_activity`, text, required): Describe the student's reading activity, such as book title, chapter, or page number.
* **Reading Frequency** (`reading_frequency`, number, required): Enter the number of times the student reads per week or month.
* **Reading Duration** (`reading_duration`, time, required): Enter the time spent reading, such as hours, minutes, or seconds.
* **Reading Date** (`reading_date`, date, required): Enter the date of their most recent reading.
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about the student's reading progress.
