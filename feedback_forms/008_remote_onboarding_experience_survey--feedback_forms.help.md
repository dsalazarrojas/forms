# Remote Onboarding Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from new hires about their onboarding experience. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make the onboarding process more efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the correct answers for the multiple choice questions.
3. Enter your email address to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Hire Details** (`new_hire_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information you'd like to share about your new hire experience. Please type in any relevant details that will help us better understand your situation.
* **New Hire Experience** (`new_hire_experience`, select_one, optional): This question asks you to rate your overall experience with the new hire process. Please select one of the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Bad
* **New Hire Satisfaction** (`new_hire_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): This question asks you to choose all the ways in which you felt satisfied or dissatisfied with the new hire process. Please select one or more of the options:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or suggestions you have about the new hire process. Please use this space to share any thoughts or ideas you have.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is to collect your email address so we can contact you (if necessary) and to help us keep track of your submission. Please enter your email address in the correct format (e.g. `example@example.com`).

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can provide as much or as little information as you'd like.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to reach out to us at `form@gic.mx`.
