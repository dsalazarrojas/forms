# Multiple Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multiple Contact Form is designed to gather contact information and feedback from customers. It is intended to be used for customer support and issue resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. In the "Feedback" field, describe your experience or concern.
5. If you have any other concerns or issues, select "True" in the "Do you have any other concerns or issues?" field.
6. If you have any other information to share, enter it in the "Do you have any other information to share?" field.
7. If you want to provide additional details about your concerns, enter them in the "Additional information about your other concerns" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `number`, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_field`, `text`, required): Describe your experience or concern in as much detail as possible.
* **Do you have any other concerns or issues?** (`select_variety`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you have any other concerns or issues, and we'll get back to you about it.
* **Do you have any other information to share?** (`other_info_field`, `text`, required): Enter any other relevant information that might help us better understand your concern.
* **Additional information about your other concerns** (`other_info_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about your concerns or issues if you want to.
