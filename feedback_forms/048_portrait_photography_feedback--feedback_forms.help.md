# Portrait Photography Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers who have had a portrait photography session. It helps us understand their experience and preferences, and ensures we can improve our services for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the rating that best describes your overall experience with the portrait photography session.
2. Provide any comments about your session.
3. Choose your favorite image from the session.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or mail).
7. Choose how often you'd like to be contacted (daily, weekly, or monthly).
8. Enter the ID of your portrait photography session.
9. Choose the date of your session.
10. Choose the time of your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Portrait Session Feedback (1)**: Select the rating that best describes your overall experience with the session.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Comments (2)**: Please provide any comments or feedback about your session.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Favorite Image (3)**: Choose the image that you liked the most from your session.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Email (4)**: Enter your email address.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Preferred Contact Method (6)**: Select how you'd like to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Preferred Contact Frequency (7)**: Choose how often you'd like to be contacted (daily, weekly, or monthly).
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Portrait Photography Session ID (8)**: Enter the ID of your portrait photography session.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Session Date (9)**: Choose the date of your session.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.
- **Session Time (10)**: Choose the time of your session.
  - This field is required and should be completed before proceeding.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing comments and feedback.
* Ensure your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us directly and we'll be happy to help.
