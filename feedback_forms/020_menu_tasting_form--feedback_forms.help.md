# Menu Tasting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Tasting Form is a tool for users to provide feedback on their menu tasting experience. Please use this form to give your honest feedback on the menu items you tasted.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
2. Select all applicable menu items you would like to taste from the Menu Items field.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts in the Comments field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of the menu tasting event.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of the menu tasting event.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
- **Menu Items** (`menu_items`, select multiple, required): Select all the menu items you would like to taste.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
## Tips
- Please fill out all fields completely and accurately.
- Select all applicable menu items that you would like to taste.
- Be as detailed as possible in the Comments field to help us understand your experience better.
