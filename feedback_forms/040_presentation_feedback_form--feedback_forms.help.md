# Presentation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The presentation feedback form is designed to collect feedback from users to help improve future presentations. It's meant for users to provide their honest opinion on the content, organization, and overall quality of the presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and provide thoughtful answers.
2. For the "Feedback for Presenters" and "Feedback for Organizers" sections, type in your comments and suggestions.
3. In the "Suggestions for Improvement" section, type any ideas you think could enhance the presentation.
4. For the "Rating" section, choose a number from 1 to 5 that reflects your overall impression of the presentation.
5. If you wish to receive a follow-up or have specific comments, type them in the "Comments" field.
6. For "Presentation Title", simply type the title of the presentation you're providing feedback on.
7. For "Date" and "Time" fields, type the actual date and time you attended the presentation.
8. You can provide a "Phone Number" if you'd like to be reached for further questions or discussions.
9. If applicable, choose one "Select One" option or multiple "Select Multiple" options from the given lists.
10. Lastly, if you have additional notes or comments, type them in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback for Presenters** (`presenter_feedback`, text, optional): Share your constructive feedback with the presenter on how to improve their skills.
- **Feedback for Organizers** (`organizer_feedback`, text, optional): Provide feedback on how the organizers did with the event.
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest ways to enhance the presentation or event.
- **comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or additional thoughts on the presentation.
- **Presentation Title** (`presentation_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the presentation.
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the presentation overall (1-5).
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you attended the presentation.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you attended the presentation.
- **email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address if you'd like to be reached for further questions or discussions.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be reached.
- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list.
- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
