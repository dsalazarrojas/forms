# waiting_room_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The waiting_room_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience in a waiting room. This form will help us understand areas for improvement and enhance the overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe your experience in the waiting room.
2. For the "Waiting Room Services" section, select all that apply.
3. Provide any additional suggestions or comments in the "Waiting Room Suggestions" field.
4. Enter your contact information (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Waiting Room Experience**: Please select how you would rate the cleanliness of the waiting room. This will help us understand if we are meeting our standards.
	+ Waiting Room Was Clean
	+ Waiting Room Was Not Clean
* **Waiting Room Services**: Please select all that apply regarding the quality of services provided in the waiting room. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Bad
* **Overall Experience**: Please rate your overall experience in the waiting room. This will help us understand the overall satisfaction level.
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Bad
* **Waiting Room Time**: Please select how you felt about the waiting time. This will help us balance our wait times.
	+ Too Long
	+ Just Right
	+ Too Short
* **Waiting Room Suggestions**: Please provide any additional suggestions or comments you may have about the waiting room. This is your chance to tell us what we can do better.
* **Contact Information**: If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your contact information. This is optional, but it will help us get in touch with you if we need more information.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when completing this form.
* Only submit feedback once per visit to avoid duplication.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Waiting Room Suggestions" field.
