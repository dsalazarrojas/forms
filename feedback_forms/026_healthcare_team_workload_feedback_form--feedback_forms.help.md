# Healthcare Team Workload Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Healthcare Team Workload Feedback Form" is designed to gather information from healthcare team members about their workload, stress levels, and satisfaction with their job, communication, collaboration, and overall impressions.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of multiple sections with different types of questions. Please follow these steps to complete it:

1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, taking into account your genuine experience.
3. Provide detailed comments in the space provided for open-ended questions.
4. Make sure to save your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall**: (<label>Overall</label> (<name>overall_satisfaction</name>, <type>number</type>, required/false>): This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with your current workload. Rate it on a scale from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Stress Level**: (<label>Stress Level</label> (<name>stress_level</name>, <type>number</type>, required/false>): This question measures how stressed you feel about your current workload. Rate it on a scale from 1 (very stressed) to 5 (very little stressed).
* **Workload Hours**: (<label>Workload Hours</label> (<name>workload_hours_worked</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>): Please estimate how many hours you work per week. This will help us understand your workload volume.
* **Workload Satisfaction**: (<label>Workload Satisfaction</label> (<name>workload_satisfaction</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/false>): Please select all that apply to describe your satisfaction with your current workload. Options are: Very Low, Low, Medium, High, Very High.
* **Job Satisfaction**: (<label>Job Satisfaction</label> (<name>job_satisfaction</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/false>): This question measures how satisfied you are with your job overall. Options are: Very Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Satisfied, Very Satisfied.
* **Work Life Balance**: (<label>Work Life Balance</label> (<name>work_life_balance</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/false>): Please select all that apply to describe your work-life balance. Options are: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Very Good.
* **Support from Management**: (<label>Support from Management</label> (<name>support_from_management</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/false>): This question measures how satisfied you are with the support from your management team. Options are: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Very Good.
* **Communication with Colleagues**: (<label>Communication with Colleagues</label> (<name>communication_with_colleagues</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/false>): Please select all that apply to describe your communication with colleagues. Options are: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Very Good.
* **Collaboration with Other Teams**: (<label>Collaboration with Other Teams</label> (<name>collaboration_with_other_teams</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/false>): This question measures how satisfied you are with collaboration with other teams. Options are: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Very Good.
* **Time Management Skills**: (<label>Time Management Skills</label> (<name>time_management_skills</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>): This field is for you to describe your time management skills and how you manage your workload effectively.
* **Feedback Frequency**: (<label>Feedback Frequency</label> (<name>feedback_frequency</name>, <type>number</type>, required/false>): Please indicate how often you receive feedback from your management or team members. Options are: Less than once a month, About once a month, About once a week, About once a day, More than once a day.
* **Feedback Quality**: (<label>Feedback Quality</label> (<name>feedback_quality</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/false>): Please select all that apply to describe the quality of feedback you receive. Options are: Very Low, Low, Medium, High, Very High.
* **Communication with Supervisors**: (<label>Communication with Supervisors</label> (<name>communication_with_supervisors</name>, <type>select_one</type>, required/false>): This question measures how satisfied you are with communication with your supervisors. Options are: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Very Good.
* **Change in Workload Satisfaction**: (<label>Change in Workload Satisfaction</label> (<name>workload_satisfaction_change</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/false>): If you have experienced a change in your workload satisfaction level, please select the corresponding change in satisfaction level. Options are: Significantly Decreased, Decreased, Stable, Increased, Significantly Increased.
* **Comments**: (<label>Comments</label> (<name>comments</name>, <type>note</type>, required/false>): Please provide your comments about your current workload and any other issues you'd like to bring up.
* **Suggestions**: (<label>Suggestions</label> (<name>suggestions</name>, <type>note</type>, required/false>): This field is for any suggestions you have for improving your workload and the team's overall performance.
* **Open-Ended**: (<label>Open-Ended</label> (<name>open_ended</name>, <type>note</type>, required/false>): Please provide any other comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Overall Impressions**: (<label>Overall Impressions</label> (<name>overall_impressions</name>, <type>note</type>, required/false>): Please summarize your overall impressions about your current workload and the team's performance.
* **Additional Comments**: (<label>Additional Comments</label> (<name>additional_comments</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Action Planning**: (<label>Actions Planning</label> (<name>action_planning</name>, <type>note</type>, required/false>): Please describe the actions you recommend for your current workload and the team's overall development.
* **Follow-up**: (<label>Follow-up</label> (<name>follow_up</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/false>): Please select all that apply to describe the follow-up frequency. Options are: Yes, No, Not Applicable.
* **Date of Last Contact**: (<label>Date of Last Contact</label> (<name>date_of_last_contact</name>, <type>date</type>, required/false>): Please provide the date of your last contact with your management or team members.
* **Last Action Taken**: (<label>Last Action Taken</label> (<name>last_action_taken</name>, <type>time</type>, required/false>): This field is for the time of your last action taken on your current workload.
* **Frequency of Contacts**: (<label>Frequency of Contacts</label> (<name>frequency_of_contacts</name>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required/false>): Please select all that apply to describe the frequency of contacts with your management or team members. Options are: Less than once a month, About once a month, About once a week, About once a day, More than once a day.
* **Assigned Tool**: (<label>Assigned Tool</label> (<name>assigned_tool</name>, <type>text</type>, required/false>): This field is for you to describe any tools assigned to you for your current workload.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Select the relevant options for each question, taking into account your genuine experience.
* Use clear and concise language when providing comments and suggestions.
* Make sure to save your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to reach out to your management or team members for clarification.
* The information collected from this form will be used to better understand your experiences and make informed decisions to improve the team's performance.
