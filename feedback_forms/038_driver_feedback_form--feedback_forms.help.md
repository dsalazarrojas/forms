# driver_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a driver. Fill out this form to share your thoughts and opinions on the service, cleanliness, communication, and overall satisfaction with the driver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Answer all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.
3. For fields with multiple options, select only one response that best describes your experience.
4. Leave comments and feedback in the "Comments" field if you have any additional thoughts or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction (optional)**: Please provide a brief introduction or any background information about your experience with the driver.
* **How do you rate the service of the driver?**: Rate the service of the driver, where:
	+ Excellent: The driver provided exceptional service.
	+ Good: The driver provided adequate service.
	+ Bad: The driver provided poor service.
* **Communication skills of the driver**: Rate the driver's communication skills, where:
	+ Excellent: The driver effectively communicated with you throughout the ride.
	+ Good: The driver communicated adequately, but with some room for improvement.
	+ Bad: The driver did not communicate effectively.
* **Cleanliness and appearance of the vehicle**: Rate the cleanliness and appearance of the vehicle, where:
	+ Excellent: The vehicle was spotless and well-maintained.
	+ Good: The vehicle was clean and well-appeared, but not perfect.
	+ Bad: The vehicle was dirty or poorly maintained.
* **Attitude of the driver**: Rate the driver's attitude, where:
	+ Excellent: The driver was friendly, courteous, and professional.
	+ Good: The driver was pleasant, but not extremely friendly or professional.
	+ Bad: The driver was not friendly or welcoming.
* **Overall satisfaction with the driver**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the driver, where:
	+ Highly Satisfied: You were extremely satisfied with the driver's service.
	+ Moderately Satisfied: You were somewhat satisfied with the driver's service.
	+ Not Satisfied: You were not satisfied with the driver's service.
* **Comments (optional)**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the driver.
* **Driver phone number (optional)**: If you'd like to contact the driver directly, please provide their phone number.
