# Streaming Service Engagement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and engagement reports from users about streaming services. It helps us understand your preferences and experiences with different types of content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred category (e.g. Sports, Entertainment, Music, Gaming).
2. Enter your name and a valid email address.
3. Choose how often you watch the channel (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
4. Provide your name for the channel you're reporting on.
5. Enter a valid email address for the channel.
6. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the channel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your preferred category?** (`category_select`, `select_one`, required): Please select one option from the list to indicate your preferred category (e.g. Sports, Entertainment, Music, Gaming).
* **What's your name?** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, which will help us understand your identity.
* **What's your email?** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address, which will help us contact you if needed.
* **How often do you watch the channel?** (`channel_watched`, `select_one`, required): Select one option from the list to indicate how often you watch the channel (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Name** (`channel_watch_time`, `text`, required): Enter your name for the channel you're reporting on.
* **Email** (`email_chan`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for the channel.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the channel.
* **Feedback about channel** (`channel_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the channel.
* **Select categories** (`categories_select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple categories that interest you (e.g. Sports, Entertainment, Music, Gaming).
* **Channel Name Again** (`channel_name_again`, `text`, optional): Enter your name again for the channel.
* **Email Chan Again** (`email_chan_again`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address again for the channel.
* **Comments** (`channel_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the channel.
* **Feedback** (`channel_feedback_again`, `text`, optional): Provide any feedback or comments about the channel.
* **Channel Cat Select Multiple Again** (`channel_cat_select_multiple_again`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select multiple categories again (e.g. Sports, Entertainment, Music, Gaming).
* **Channel Name Again Again** (`channel_name_again_again`, `text`, optional): Enter your name again for the channel.
* **Email Chan Again Again** (`email_chan_again_again`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address again for the channel.
* **Channel Additional Comments** (`channel_additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the channel.
* **Final feedback** (`channel_final_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any final feedback or comments about the channel.
