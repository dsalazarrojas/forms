# Veterans Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from veterans on their experiences with a particular program or service. The form will help in understanding the areas of improvement and will aid in making data-driven decisions for future services and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your title (e.g. "Veteran's Feedback Survey")
2. Select the category of the form (e.g. "Feedback Forms")
3. Provide a brief description of your experience
4. Rate the overall satisfaction with the service
5. Rate the service quality
6. Rate the value of the program
7. Provide any improvement suggestions
8. Provide any additional comments
9. Enter your contact information (phone number, email, date, time, name, organization, and program name)
10. Indicate if you would like to be followed up on and how

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the survey, which should be a brief description of your experience.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the form, e.g. "Feedback Forms".
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your experience.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating_overall`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Service Rating** (`rating_service`, number, required): Rate the quality of the service.
* **Value Rating** (`rating_value`, select_one, required): Rate the value of the program.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, note, required): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Program** (`program`, text, required): Enter the name of the program.
* **Program Service Rating** (`rating_program_service`, number, required): Rate the quality of the program service.
* **Program Value Rating** (`rating_program_value`, select_one, required): Rate the value of the program.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you have any prophets or not.
* **Follow Up** (`followup`, select_one, required): Indicate if you would like to be followed up on.
* **Follow Up By** (`followup_by`, text, required): If you selected "Yes" for follow up, enter the name of the person to follow up with.
* **Follow Up Time** (`followup_time`, date, required): Enter the date and time for the follow up.
* **Follow Up Phone** (`followup_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow up.
* **Follow Up Email** (`followup_email`, email, required): Enter your email for follow up.
* **Follow Up Comments** (`followup_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback for follow up.
