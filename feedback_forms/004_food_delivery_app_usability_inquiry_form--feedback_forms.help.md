# Food Delivery App Usability Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to provide feedback on their recent experience with our food delivery app. The feedback will help us to identify areas of improvement to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide feedback, follow the steps below:

1. Complete all required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Answer each question truthfully, and provide detailed explanations whenever possible.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, you can choose "None of the above" as a default option.
4. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address in the format of [username@domain.com](mailto:username@domain.com).
* **Phone number** (Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone. If you do not want to be contacted, leave this field blank.
* **How would you rate your recent food delivery experience with our app?** (Required): Choose the best option that describes your experience.
	+ Very poor: You were extremely dissatisfied with the service.
	+ Poor: You were somewhat dissatisfied with the service.
	+ Fair: You were neutral about the service.
	+ Good: You were somewhat satisfied with the service.
	+ Excellent: You were very satisfied with the service.
	+ Very good: You were extremely satisfied with the service.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback about your experience?** (Optional): If you have any further comments or concerns, please provide them here.
* **How would you prefer to be contacted for follow-up?** (Required): Choose the preferred method of contact. Please note that if you choose "Phone" or "In-app message", we will make sure to follow up with you accordingly.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to share with us?** (Optional): If there is any other feedback you wish to provide, please enter it here.
