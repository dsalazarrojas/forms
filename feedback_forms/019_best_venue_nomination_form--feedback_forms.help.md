# Best Venue Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Best Venue Nomination Form" is designed to help you nominate a venue that you have had a positive experience with. Your feedback will help us recognize and reward outstanding venues that provide excellent service and facilities for events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name, email address, and organization (if applicable) in the "Your Name", "Email Address", and "Your Organization" fields.
2. Enter the name of the venue you are nominating in the "Venue Name" field.
3. Provide the full address of the venue in the "Venue Address" field.
4. Enter the city where the venue is located in the "City" field.
5. Specify the state or province where the venue is located in the "State or Province" field.
6. Select the type of venue from the list provided (e.g., Conference Center, Hotel Ballroom, etc.) in the "Venue Type" field.
7. Choose the type of event you held at the venue (e.g., Wedding, Corporate Event, etc.) in the "Event Type Held" field.
8. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field (yyyy-mm-dd format).
9. Estimate the approximate number of guests who attended the event in the "Number of Guests" field.
10. Rate the venue's capacity to accommodate your group size in the "Venue Capacity Rating" field.
11. Evaluate the venue's facility quality, staff service, cleanliness, available amenities, and value for money in the "Facility Quality Rating", "Staff and Service Rating", "Cleanliness Rating", "Amenities Rating", and "Value for Money Rating" fields respectively.
12. Select the standout features that made this venue stand out from the list provided (e.g., Beautiful Architecture, Modern Technology, etc.) in the "Standout Features" field.
13. Briefly explain why you are nominating this venue in the "Why Are You Nominating This Venue" field.
14. Let us know if you would book this venue again in the "Would You Book This Venue Again" field.
15. Finally, add any additional comments or feedback about the venue in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name to associate your feedback with your identity.
* **Email Address**: We will use this email to send you confirmation and follow-up communication.
* **Your Organization**: If you represent an organization, enter your company or organization name.
* **Venue Name**: Name of the venue you are nominating.
* **Venue Address**: Full address of the venue.
* **City**: City where the venue is located.
* **State or Province**: State or province where the venue is located.
* **Venue Type**: Select the type of venue from the list provided.
* **Event Type Held**: Choose the type of event you held at the venue.
* **Event Date**: Date of the event in yyyy-mm-dd format.
* **Number of Guests**: Estimate the approximate number of guests who attended the event.
* **Venue Capacity Rating**: Rate the venue's capacity to accommodate your group size.
* **Facility Quality Rating**: Evaluate the venue's facility quality.
* **Staff and Service Rating**: Evaluate the quality of staff and service provided by the venue.
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of the venue.
* **Amenities Rating**: Evaluate the available amenities at the venue.
* **Value for Money Rating**: Evaluate the value for money provided by the venue.
* **Standout Features**: Select the standout features that made this venue exceptional.
* **Why Are You Nominating This Venue**: Briefly explain why you are nominating this venue.
* **Would You Book This Venue Again**: Let us know if you would book this venue again.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the venue.
