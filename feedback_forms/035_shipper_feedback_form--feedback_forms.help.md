# Shipper Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their shipping experiences with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your honest and constructive feedback in the text fields.
3. Select the rating and feedback options that best reflect your experience.
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name* (**`customer_name`):** Enter your name as it appears on your identification or passport.
* **How was your experience with our company?* (`feedback`):** Share your thoughts on your experience with us. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Rating* (**`rating`):** Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How would you rate the employee who served you?* (**`employee_rating`):** Rate the employee's service on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **What did the employee do well?** (`feedback_employee`):** Share specific examples of what the employee did well.
* **What could the employee improve on?** (`feedback_employee`):** Share specific examples of areas where the employee could improve.
* **Overall Experience* (**`overall_experience`):** Indicate whether you had an overall positive (`Yes`) or negative (`No`) experience.
* **Do you have any other suggestions for us?** (`suggestions`):** Share any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
* **Email Address** (`email`):** Enter your email address so we can get back to you if needed.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`):** Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`):** Share any additional information or comments you'd like to add.
* **Category** (`category`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Subcategory** (`sub_category`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Shipper Name** (`shipper_name`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Shipper Number** (`shipper_number`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Branch** (`branch`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Date Shipped** (`date_shipped`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Date Picked Up** (`date_picked_up`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Driver Number** (`driver_number`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Driver License** (`driver_license`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Driver Vehicle** (`driver_vehicle`):** Not applicable in this form.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license_number`):** Not applicable in this form.

Note: Only the fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are required fields.
