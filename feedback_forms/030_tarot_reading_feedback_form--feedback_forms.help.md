# Tarot Reading Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tarot Reading Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from clients or customers who have undergone a tarot reading session with a tarot reader. This feedback is meant to help the tarot reader improve their skills and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided fields, being as detailed and honest as possible.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience and opinions.
3. Don't hesitate to provide additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4. Submit your feedback when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall experience?**: Choose one of the options provided, or type in a brief description of your overall experience.
* **How would you rate the tarot reading?**: Rate the tarot reading on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **What was the tarot reader's demeanor during the reading?**: Select "Yes" if you felt comfortable with the tarot reader's behavior, or "No" if you felt uncomfortable or unsure.
* **Were you able to connect with the tarot reader?**: Choose "True" if you felt a strong connection with the tarot reader, or "False" if you didn't feel a connection.
* **Your understanding of the tarot reading?**: Type in a brief description of what you understood from the reading, or any insights you gained.
* **Was the tarot reading too long or too short?**: Select one of the options provided, depending on your experience.
* **What was the most helpful part of the tarot reading?**: Choose one or more of the options provided, depending on what resonated with you.
* **What was the most unhelpful part of the tarot reading?**: Choose one or more of the options provided, depending on what didn't resonate with you.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**: Type in any thoughts, ideas, or suggestions you have for the tarot reader.
* **Do you have any suggestions for the tarot reader?**: Type in any ideas or suggestions you have for the tarot reader.
* **Did you experience any discomfort or difficulty?**: Select "True" if you felt any discomfort or difficulty, or "False" if you didn't.
* **How likely are you to recommend this service?**: Rate the tarot reading service on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Was the tarot reading a good value for the price?**: Select "True" if you felt it was worth the cost, or "False" if you didn't.
* **Did you think this service is a good representation of what you would expect from a tarot reading?**: Select "True" if you felt it met your expectations, or "False" if it didn't.
* **Was this your first tarot reading with the tarot reader?**: Select "True" if it was your first reading with this tarot reader, or "False" if it wasn't.
* **Do you want to book another tarot reading with the tarot reader?**: Select "True" if you'd like to book another reading with this tarot reader, or "False" if you won't.
* **What was the most important part of the tarot reading?**: Choose one or more of the options provided, depending on what resonated with you.
* **Would you like to be contacted for further follow-up?**: Select "True" if you'd like to be contacted, or "False" if you don't.
* **Did you feel a strong connection with the tarot reader?**: Select "True" if you felt a strong connection, or "False" if you didn't.
* **Was the tarot reading a good representation of what you were looking for?**: Select "True" if it met your expectations, or "False" if it didn't.
* **How would you rate the tarot reader's expertise?**: Rate the tarot reader's expertise on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Was the tarot reader knowledgeable about their subject matter?**: Select "True" if you felt they were knowledgeable, or "False" if you didn't.
* **Do you have any further requests for the tarot reader?**: Type in any thoughts, ideas, or suggestions you have for the tarot reader.
* **Is there anything else you would like to say?**: Type in any final thoughts or comments you have.
* **Would you like to give feedback on the reading?**: Type in any feedback or suggestions you have for the tarot reader or the reading process.
