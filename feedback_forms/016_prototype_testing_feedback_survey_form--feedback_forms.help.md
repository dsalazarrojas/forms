# Prototype Testing Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prototype Testing Feedback Survey Form is designed to collect feedback from users who test a prototype, a preliminary version of a product or service. This form helps the development team gather information about the user's experience with the prototype and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and detailed in your feedback.
3. If you have suggestions for improvement, share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Feedback** (`user_feedback`, text, optional): Describe your overall experience with the prototype. What did you think of it? Was it user-friendly? Did you find it confusing? Be as specific as possible.
* **Prototype ID** (`prototype_id`, text, optional): If you were provided with a Prototype ID, enter it here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the prototype on a scale of 1-10. One represents a very poor experience, while 10 represents a very good experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any specific feedback you have about the prototype. What did you like or dislike about it? Be as specific as possible.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for how we can improve the prototype, enter them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Enter your name or identifier to submit your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with follow-up questions or to send you updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with follow-up questions or to schedule further discussions.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you tested the prototype, enter the time you spent testing it. This can help us gauge the efficiency of our testing process.
Please note that all fields are optional, but filling out all fields will give us more valuable feedback.
