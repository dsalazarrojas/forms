# Motivational Speaker Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motivational Speaker Feedback Form is designed to collect qualitative and quantitative feedback about the speaker's performance during an event or presentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Speaker Name in the text box provided.
2. Rate the speaker's performance in the "How would you rate the speaker's performance?" dropdown menu.
3. Provide your thoughts about the speaker's talk in the text box.
4. Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 1-5 in the number fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the motivational speaker you are providing feedback about.
* **How would you rate the speaker's performance?** (`speaker_performance`, Select, Optional): Choose one of the options provided to rate the speaker's performance: Excellent, Good, Fair.
* **What did you think about the speaker's talk?** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about the speaker's presentation.
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, Number, Optional): Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 1.
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, Number, Optional): Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 2.
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, Number, Optional): Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 3.
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, Number, Optional): Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 4.
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, Number, Optional): Rate the speaker's performance on a scale of 5.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide specific examples or anecdotes when giving feedback in the "What did you think about the speaker's talk?" field.
* Choose a rating that reflects your genuine opinion about the speaker's performance.
* If you want to skip a question, feel free to do so, as the optional fields are not required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
