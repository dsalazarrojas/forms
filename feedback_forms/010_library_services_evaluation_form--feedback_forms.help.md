# Library Services Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the library's services. The information collected will help us understand how well we're meeting your needs and identify areas for improvement. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts, and let us know how we can better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide your feedback, follow these steps:

* Please enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
* Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
* Provide your contact email in the **Email** field. This helps us stay in touch with you and respond to any follow-up questions.
* If you wish, you can also provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* Answer the question, "How would you rate our services?" in the **Rate Our Services** field by selecting one of the provided options.
* In the **Additional Comments** field, you can share any extra thoughts or observations about your experience.
* If you enjoyed anything specific about your visit, select **What did you enjoy most** from the provided options. If not applicable, you can choose the "Other (please specify)" option.
* Similarly, if you think we could improve in any aspect, select **What can we improve** from the provided options. If not applicable, you can choose the "None" or "Other" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_1_first_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`form_1_last_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`form_1_email`, Email, Optional): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`form_1_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Rate Our Services** (`form_1_rate_our_services`, Select One, Optional): Select one of the provided options to rate our services (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional Comments** (`form_1_additional_comments`, Note, Optional): Write down any further thoughts, observations, or suggestions that you didn't find in the other fields.
* **What Did You Enjoy Most** (`form_1_what_did_you_enjoy_most`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all that apply from the given options that best describe your experience (Book selection, Quiet space, Knowledgeable staff, Other (please specify)).
* **What Can We Improve** (`form_1_what_can_we_improve`, Select One, Optional): Choose one option that best represents areas where we can improve (None, Response time, Availability of materials, Other).
* **Any Other Comments** (`form_1_any_other_comments`, Note, Optional): If there's anything else you'd like to tell us, enter it here.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
* If you've selected "Other" for any of the options, please specify what that "Other" means to you.
* Your feedback will help us improve and better serve you.
* Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts!
