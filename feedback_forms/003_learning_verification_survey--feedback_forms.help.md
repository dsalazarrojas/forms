# learning_verification_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of a training program, allowing participants to provide their thoughts on the program's helpfulness and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the participant's name and email address. These will be used to identify the participant and ensure that we can reach out to them for further discussion.
2. Choose a rating from 1 to 3 to indicate how effective the training program was. A rating of 1 means it was not effective, 2 means it was moderately effective, and 3 means it was highly effective.
3. Provide any additional comments you may have about the program.
4. If you have any suggestions for how the program can be improved, please provide them.
5. Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
6. If applicable, select the category and duration of the program.
7. Finally, enter any comments or thoughts you have about the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name**: Enter your full name as it is on your business card or identification.
* **training_program_name**: Enter the name of the training program you participated in.
* **effectiveness**: Choose a rating that best describes your experience with the program.
* **feedback**: Enter any comments or thoughts you have about the program.
* **program_improvements**: Suggest any improvements you think the program could have.
* **participant_rating**: Enter a number rating that best describes your experience with the program.
* **participant_email**: Enter your business email address so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **program_category**: If applicable, select the category of the program.
* **program_duration**: If applicable, select the duration of the program.
* **participant_comments**: Enter any additional thoughts or comments you have about the program.
* **participant_email**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for any follow-up questions.
