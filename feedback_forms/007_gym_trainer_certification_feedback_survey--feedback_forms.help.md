# Gym Trainer Certification Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from gym members about their training experience with their certified trainers. The form helps us understand the effectiveness of our certification program and trainers' performance. Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page for context.
2. Choose your rating for the trainer's certification level (Trainer Certification).
3. Select the quality of the training experience (Training Quality).
4. Provide comments about your experience (Comments).
5. If necessary, enter your name, phone number, and email address.
6. Choose the type of training you received (Training Type).
7. Enter the number of training hours you attended.
8. Specify the duration of the certification (Certification Duration).
9. Describe your training schedule (Training Schedule).
10. Enter your Member ID.
11. Enter your Trainer ID.
12. Optionally, select the time you spent submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Trainer Certification** (`Trainer Certification`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the level of certification of your trainer, from Excellent, Good, to Fair.
* **Training Quality** (`Training Quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more qualities of the training experience, from High, Medium, to Low.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts about your experience with the trainer.
* **Trainer Name** (`Trainer Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your trainer's name, if you want to provide feedback specific to them.
* **Member Name** (`Member Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, so we know who's providing feedback.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Provide your email address, so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, if you want us to contact you for more information.
* **Staff Development** (`Staff Development`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose how we can improve our staff development, from Increase, Improve, to No Action.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether to submit your feedback, save it for later, or exit without submitting.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you spent submitting this form.
* **Certification** (`Certification`, `text`, required: false): Enter your certification details, if you have any.
* **Certification Type** (`Certification Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose whether you're certified or not.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional feedback you have about your experience.
* **Training Type** (`Training Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of training you received, from Group Class, One-On-One, or Other.
* **Training Hours** (`Training Hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours you spent in training.
* **Certification Duration** (`Certification Duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of your certification in hours.
* **Training Schedule** (`Training Schedule`, `text`, required: false): Describe your training schedule, if you have any.
* **Member ID** (`Member ID`, `text`, required: false): Enter your member ID number, for reference.
* **Trainer ID** (`Trainer ID`, `text`, required: false): Enter your trainer's ID number, for reference.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, `text`, required: false): Enter a unique form ID, for tracking purposes.
* **Submit Time** (`Submit Time`, `time`, required: false): Record the time you spent submitting this form.
* **Created At** (`Created At`, `date`, required: false): Automatically generated field, showing the date and time you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you're unsure about any field, just skip it and move on.
* If you have any additional feedback, feel free to share it in the "Feedback" field.
