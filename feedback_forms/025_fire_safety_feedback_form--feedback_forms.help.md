# Fire Safety Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience during a fire safety training session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with your name and any other information you're comfortable sharing.
2. Provide the event date to help us associate your feedback with the specific training.
3. Briefly answer the questions about the training, including what you found most important and any areas where you felt it could be improved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: A text field where you can enter your name, if you'd like.
* **Event Date**: A date field to help us track the specific training session you're referencing.
* **Training Topic**: A text field to provide additional context about the training topic, if you'd like.
* **What was the most important thing you learned during the training?**: A text field where you can share your thoughts on what resonated with you the most.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of the training?**: A rating field with four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, and Poor. Select one that best reflects your experience.
* **Were there any areas where you felt the training could be improved?**: A text field where you can provide any constructive feedback you have.
* **Participant Signature**: A text field where you can add your signature, if you'd like. Please note that it's optional, but it can help us confirm your identity.

## Tips
- Be honest and specific in your feedback, as it will help us improve future trainings.
- Please keep your responses brief, but feel free to provide as much detail as you need.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
