# Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Feedback Form is designed to collect attendee feedback on meetings, providing valuable insights for future improvement and evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Meeting Attendee's Name` field.
2. Type in your email address in the `Email` field, if you have one.
3. Describe your experience during the meeting in the `Description` field.
4. Rate the meeting's helpfulness using the dropdown menu.
5. Select whether or not you've attended other meetings.
6. Provide the most valuable takeaway from the meeting in the `Most Valuable Takeaway` field.
7. Choose whether the meeting was relevant to your goals using the radio button.
8. Choose the duration of the meeting (in minutes) using the time field.
9. Check if the meeting was scheduled on time using the checkbox.
10. Describe any technical difficulties that occurred during the meeting.
11. Provide suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Attendee's Name** (`attendee_name`, text): Enter your name as an attendee of the meeting.
* **Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you have one. This field is optional, but it helps us to contact you if we need to follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Description** (`feedback_comments`, text): Share your experience during the meeting. What did you like or dislike about the meeting?
* **Rate the Meeting's Helpfulness** (`meeting_helpfulness_rating`, select_one): Rate the meeting's helpfulness. Select 'Yes' if you found it helpful, 'No' if not, or select 'Other' if it was helpful, but not in the way you expected.
* **Attended Other Meetings** (`attended_other_meetings`, checkbox): Have you attended other meetings? Select this option if you have.
* **Most Valuable Takeaway** (`most_valuable_takeaway`, text): What's the most valuable takeaway from the meeting? Share your thoughts.
* **Meeting Relevance** (`meeting_relevance`, radio): Was the meeting relevant to your goals? Select 'True' if it was, 'False' if not.
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_duration`, time): How long was the meeting? Enter the duration in minutes.
* **Meeting On Time** (`meeting_on_time`, checkbox): Was the meeting scheduled on time? Select this option if it was.
* **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, text): Were there any technical difficulties during the meeting? Describe them in this field.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): What can we improve for future meetings? Share your thoughts.
