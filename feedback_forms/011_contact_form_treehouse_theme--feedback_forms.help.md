# Contact Form Treehouse Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback or report issues from users in the Treehouse community. Please use this form to share your thoughts, questions, or concerns about the Treehouse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **Name** in the `Name` field. This is where you can enter your full name.
2. Enter your **Contact Email** in the `Email address` field. This is the email where you can expect a reply from our team.
3. Provide a **Subject** that summarizes your feedback or concern in the `Subject` field.
4. Type your **Message** in the `Message` field. This is where you can provide a detailed explanation of your feedback or concern.
5. If you believe your issue is urgent, select your **Urgency Level** from the options provided. This will help our team prioritize your request.
6. If you need to attach files to support your feedback, please use the email address mentioned in the `Attach files via email if needed` field.
7. Finally, add any **Additional Comments** in the `Additional comments` field if you have more context to share with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to help us address your feedback or concern effectively.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide your email address so our team can get back to you with a reply.
* **Subject** (`subject`, `text`, required): Briefly summarize your feedback or concern in a few words.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required): Type a detailed explanation of your feedback or concern.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`, `select_one`, optional): If you believe your issue requires immediate attention, select the urgency level that best fits your concern. The options are: Low, Medium, High.
* **Attach files via email if needed** (`attach_files_note`, `note`, optional): If you need to attach files to support your feedback, please use the email address mentioned in this field.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional context or information that can help our team better understand your concern.
 
# Tips
* Please ensure that your feedback is not a duplicate of an existing issue to help us save time and focus on new concerns.
