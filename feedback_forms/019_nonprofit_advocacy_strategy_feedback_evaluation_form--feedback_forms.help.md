# Nonprofit Advocacy Strategy Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of your nonprofit's advocacy strategy. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your thoughts on how we can improve our advocacy efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your email address so we can contact you with a follow-up.
2. Rate the current state of our Nonprofit Advocacy Strategy using the scale provided.
3. Indicate how likely you are to recommend our advocacy strategy to a friend or colleague.
4. Share your thoughts on how we can improve our nonprofit's advocacy strategy.
5. Add any additional comments you may have.
6. Provide your name and job title, if applicable.
7. Confirm your contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback on Nonprofit Advocacy Strategy** (`strategy_1`, select_one, required): Rate the current state of our Nonprofit Advocacy Strategy using the scale provided (Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Very Effective, Not At All Effective).
- **Likelihood of Recommendation** (`strategy_2`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend our advocacy strategy to a friend or colleague.
- **Improvement Suggestions** (`strategy_3`, text, required): Share your thoughts on how we can improve our nonprofit's advocacy strategy. Please provide specific and concrete suggestions.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you may have about our nonprofit's advocacy strategy.
- **Your Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can contact you with a follow-up.
- **Your Name** (`name`, text, required): Please provide your name, if you have one.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Provide your job title, if you have one.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Provide the name of your organization, if you have one.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Confirm your contact email address.

# Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and concisely.
* Provide specific and concrete suggestions for improvement.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
