# Network Service Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Network Service Evaluation Survey" is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our network services. This survey aims to understand the level of satisfaction and quality of service provided, as well as any areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill in the required information for each field. You can type in your answers for text fields, select from the options provided for drop-down fields, and type in dates or times as required for date and time fields.

* **1. Network Service** (text, optional): This field is where you can provide your general comments or observations about the network service you've used.
* **2. Survey** (text, optional): Please provide any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience.
* **3. Network Performance** (number, optional): Rate your experience with the network performance on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being poor and 10 being excellent.
* **4. Quality Of Service** (number, optional): Rate your experience with the overall quality of service on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being poor and 10 being excellent.
* **5. Network Service** (select one, optional): Choose one of the following options to rate your experience with the network service: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **6. Survey** (select multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to your experience:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **7. Date** (date, optional): Please type in the date you used the network service.
* **8. Time** (time, optional): Please type in the time you used the network service.
* **9. Additional Comments** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **10. Email** (email, optional): Type in your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **11. Phone** (text, optional): Type in your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
