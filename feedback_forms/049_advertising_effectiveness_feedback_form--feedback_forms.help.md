## Purpose
The Advertising Effectiveness Feedback Form is a tool used to collect feedback from users about an advertising campaign. It's designed to understand how effective the campaign is in grabbing the user's attention, conveying a clear message, creating an emotional connection, and influencing purchase intent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide detailed feedback about the advertising campaign.
2. Choose the correct options for each question based on your personal experience and opinion.
3. Don't worry if you're unsure about some questions; provide your best guess or answer to the best of your ability.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure you've provided accurate and thoughtful feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Age Group** (`18-24`, `25-34`, `35-44`, `45-54`, `55-64`, `65+`)
Your age group is a demographic factor that can influence your perception of an advertisement. Please select the age group that best represents you.
### 2. **Gender** (`Male`, `Female`, `Non-binary`, `Prefer not to say`)
This question is designed to collect information about your gender identity. Please select the option that best represents you.
### 3. **Occupation** (text)
Your current occupation or field of work can influence your opinion of an advertisement. Please provide your current occupation or field of work.
### 4. **Campaign Name Brand** (text)
Please provide the name of the advertising campaign you're providing feedback on.
### 5. **Ad Format Seen** (`TV Commercial`, `Online Video`, `Social Media`, `Print Advertisement`, `Radio`, `Billboard`, `Email`, `Other`)
This question helps us understand in what format you saw the advertisement.
### 6. **Exposure Frequency** (`Once`, `2-3 times`, `4-6 times`, `More than 6 times`, `Can't recall`)
Please select how often you've seen the advertisement.
### 7. **Ad Memorability** (`Very memorable`, `Somewhat memorable`, `Neutral`, `Not very memorable`, `Not memorable at all`)
How memorable was the advertisement?
### 8. **Key Message Recall** (text)
This question asks you to recall the main message of the advertisement. Please provide a brief description.
### 9. **Brand Recall** (text)
This question asks you to recall the brand being advertised. Please provide a brief description.
### 10. **Attention Grabbing** (`Very effective`, `Somewhat effective`, `Neutral`, `Not very effective`, `Not effective at all`)
How effective was the ad at grabbing your attention?
### 11. **Message Clarity** (`Very clear`, `Clear`, `Neutral`, `Unclear`, `Very unclear`)
How clear was the message conveyed in the advertisement?
### 12. **Emotional Connection** (`Strongly agree`, `Agree`, `Neutral`, `Disagree`, `Strongly disagree`)
Did the advertisement create an emotional connection with you?
### 13. **Purchase Intent** (`Very likely`, `Somewhat likely`, `Neutral`, `Not very likely`, `Not likely at all`)
How likely are you to purchase the product/service after seeing the ad?
### 14. **Recommendation Likelihood** (`Very likely`, `Somewhat likely`, `Neutral`, `Not very likely`, `Not likely at all`)
How likely are you to recommend the brand to others after seeing the ad?
### 15. **Overall Impression** (text)
Please provide your overall impression of the advertising campaign.

Note: The form's purpose is to gather feedback from users about an advertising campaign, and the fields are designed to collect this information in a structured and organized way.
