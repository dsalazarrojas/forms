# Presentation Peer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on presentations given by peers in a professional or educational setting. It helps trainers, educators, or facilitators assess the quality and effectiveness of their peers' presentations.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the presentation in the "Presentation Peer Feedback" field.
2. Identify the person who gave the presentation in the "Peer Who Presented" field.
3. Rate the presentation using the "How would you rate the presentation?" field.
4. Provide specific feedback on the presentation in the "Provide Feedback" field.
5. Select any additional comments from the available options in the "Comments" field.
6. Optionally, enter the date and time of the presentation in the "Date Presented" and "Time Presented" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Peer Feedback** (`title`, text, required/optional): This field is used to record the title of the presentation.
* **Peer Who Presented** (`speaker`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person who gave the presentation.
* **How would you rate the presentation?** (`rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the presentation on a scale (the exact scale is not specified, so assume it's a standard 1-5 scale).
* **Provide Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required/optional): Be as specific as possible when providing feedback on the presentation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any additional comments from the available options.
* **Date Presented** (`date`, date, required/optional): Optionally, enter the date the presentation was given.
* **Time Presented** (`time`, time, required/optional): Optionally, enter the time the presentation was given.

## Tips
- Be specific and constructive with your feedback to help the presenter improve.
- Keep the "Provide Feedback" field concise and focused on the main points.
- Use the available comments options to add context to your feedback.
