# status_reacquisition_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please provide your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you if needed.
3. Provide your feedback in the "Feedback" field. Be as specific and detailed as possible.
4. If you'd like to rate your experience, select a rating from 1 to 5 in the "Rating" field. The options are:
	* 1 - Prophets of Doom (very dissatisfied)
	* 2 - Not impressed
	* 3 - Satisfied
	* 4 - Happy
	* 5 - Ecstatic
5. If you have any additional comments, you can enter them in the "Comments" field.
6. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will help us reach you if you'd like to discuss further.
7. Finally, select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" field. You can choose one or multiple options:
	* Mobile
	* Email
	* Phone

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name to help us know who is providing this feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, **required**): Share your thoughts and suggestions about your experience with our organization.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, **optional**): Rate your experience with us. Choose from 1 to 5, with 1 being "Prophets of Doom" and 5 being "Ecstatic".
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose your preferred way to contact you: Mobile, Email, or Phone.
