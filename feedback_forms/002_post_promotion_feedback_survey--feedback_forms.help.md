# Post Promotion Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from users who have interacted with a promotion. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the user's experience and opinions on the promotion, helping us to refine and improve future promotions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **What did you think of the event?**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the promotion. This is an open-ended field where you can provide any feedback you have.
2. **Rate the event**: Rate your overall experience with the promotion. You will be presented with a scale of options from 1 to 5.
3. **Event rating scale**: Select the correct rating from the given scale (from 1 to 5) to rate your experience.
4. **Do you want to receive more information about this promotion in the future?**: Choose whether you are interested in receiving updates about future promotions.
5. **Any additional comments**: If you have any other feedback or comments, please share them here.
6. **Contact information**: If you want to get in touch with us, provide your contact information.
7. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
8. **Website**: If you have a personal website or blog, share the URL here.
9. **File upload**: Upload any supporting files or documents related to your feedback.
10. **File URL**: If you have a file hosted online, provide the URL here.
11. **Other comments or feedback**: If you think of anything else you'd like to share, please add it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you think of the event?** (`post_promotion_feedback_survey_page`, `text`, required/optional): Share your thoughts, opinions, and experiences about the promotion.

* **Event rating scale** (`event_rating_scale`, `select_one`, required/true): Select the correct rating from the given scale (from 1 to 5).
* **Do you want to receive more information about this promotion in the future?** (`post_promotion_feedback_survey_page_2`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose whether you are interested in receiving updates about future promotions.

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required/optional): If you have a personal website or blog, share the URL here.
