# Loyalty Program Features Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experiences with our loyalty program. Your responses will help us understand your satisfaction with our program and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and answer the required fields (marked as "required") with your honest opinions.
* For numerical rating questions (e.g., "Points Satisfaction"), rate your satisfaction on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* For multiple-choice questions (e.g., "Rewards Received"), select all that apply.
* For free-text questions (e.g., "Program Improvement"), provide a brief description of your thoughts.
* If a question has a hint, please take it into account while answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Points Satisfaction (#1)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the points system used in our loyalty program.
* **Rewards Received (#2)**: Select all rewards you have received from our loyalty program (e.g., Cashback, Discounts, Free products/services, Other).
* **Surprise Preferences (#3)**: Choose the types of surprises you would like to receive from our program (e.g., Gift cards, Exclusive offers, Special events, Other).
* **Excluded Offer (#4)**: Select one of the options that best describes an offer you would exclude from our program (e.g., Option A, Option B).
* **Communication Quality (#5)**: Rate your satisfaction with our communication (e.g., 1-5).
* **Program Improvement (#6)**: Provide suggestions for improving our loyalty program.
* **Program Usage (#7)**: Rate how frequently you use our loyalty program.
* **Overall Satisfaction (#8)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our loyalty program (e.g., 1-5).
* **Recommendation (#9)**: Rate how likely you are to recommend our loyalty program to others (e.g., Very likely, Somewhat likely, Neutral, Somewhat unlikely, Very unlikely).
* **Program Appeal (#10)**: Provide a brief description of what appeals to you about our loyalty program.
* **Feedback on Points (#11)**: (Optional) Provide any additional feedback you have about our points system.
* **Feedback on Rewards (#12)**: (Optional) Provide any additional feedback you have about our rewards.
* **Feedback on Surprises (#13)**: (Optional) Provide any additional feedback you have about the surprises we offer.
* **Feedback on Exclusive Offers (#14)**: (Optional) Provide any additional feedback you have about our exclusive offers.
* **Customer Satisfaction (#15)**: Rate your satisfaction with the customer service you have received from our loyalty program (e.g., 1-5).

Note: The optional fields are there to collect additional feedback, feel free to answer or not as you wish.
