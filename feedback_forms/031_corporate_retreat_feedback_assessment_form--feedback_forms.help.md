# Corporate Retreat Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after a corporate retreat. It will help the organizers understand the participant's experiences, satisfaction levels, and attendance details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: participant name, role, and email.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with the participant_satisfaction field.
3. Provide any feedback or comments in the feedback_comments field.
4. Choose the correct answers for participant_attendance_days, session_attendance_days, and team_meeting_attendance fields.
5. Select the type of session attended (Meeting, Team meeting, or Workshop) in the session_type field.
6. Enter the correct time for participant_start_time, participant_end_time, session_start_time, and session_end_time fields.
7. Enter the team_member_count and participant_duration fields.
8. Fill in the team_name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: The title of the form.
* **participant_satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction level with the participant_satisfaction field. This field is a number between 1 and 10.
* **feedback_comments**: Provide any feedback or comments about the corporate retreat.
* **email**: Enter your work email address.
* **phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **participant_name**: Enter your name as a participant.
* **role**: Enter your role in the company.
* **company_department**: Enter your department.
* **feedback**: Select the reasons why you provided feedback (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **date**: This field is not currently being used. 
* **time**: This field is not currently being used. 
* **location**: This field is not currently being used. 
* **team_name**: Enter the name of the team you belonged to.
* **team_member_count**: Enter the number of team members.
* **participant_attendance_days**: Choose how many days you attended the corporate retreat.
* **participant_start_time**: Enter the start time of your participant sessions.
* **participant_end_time**: Enter the end time of your participant sessions.
* **participant_duration**: Enter the duration of your participant sessions in hours and minutes.
* **session_attendance_days**: Choose how many days you attended the corporate retreat for sessions.
* **session_start_time**: Enter the start time of your session.
* **session_end_time**: Enter the end time of your session.
* **session_duration**: Enter the duration of your session in hours and minutes.
* **team_meeting_attendance**: Choose how many team meetings you attended.
* **session_type**: Select the type of session you attended (Meeting, Team meeting, or Workshop).
