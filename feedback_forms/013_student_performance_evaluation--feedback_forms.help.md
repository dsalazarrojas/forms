# Student Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Performance Evaluation form is used by teachers or instructors to assess a student's performance in specific subject areas. This form helps in gathering feedback and identifying areas of improvement for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name.
2. Choose the grade level of the student (e.g., 1, 2, or 3).
3. Select the subject areas the student was assessed on (e.g., Math, Language Arts, or Social Studies).
4. Enter the evaluation date.
5. Select the method used for assessment (e.g., note, discussion, or project).
6. Enter the student's ID number.
7. Enter your name as the teacher or instructor evaluating the student.
8. Provide any additional comments about the student's performance.
9. Select the evaluation result (e.g., Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Needs Improvement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, required): Enter the student's name for whom you are evaluating their performance.
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, select_one, required): Choose the student's grade level (e.g., 1, 2, or 3).
* **Subject Areas** (`Subject Areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select the subject areas the student was assessed on (e.g., Math, Language Arts, or Social Studies).
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Assessment Method** (`Assessment Method`, note, optional): Briefly describe the method used for assessment (e.g., note, discussion, or project).
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Teacher Name** (`Teacher Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher or instructor evaluating the student.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the student's performance.
* **Evaluation Result** (`Evaluation Result`, select_one, required): Select the evaluation result (e.g., Satisfactory, Unsatisfactory, or Needs Improvement).
