# photo_video_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users on photos and videos. It allows users to provide a rating and comments on the content, helping to understand their thoughts and opinions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the photo or video you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter your rating (1-5) to express your opinion.
3. Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about the content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Photo Video Feedback** (`photo_video_feedback`, text, optional)
This field is where you can provide your overall feedback on the photo or video. Please enter any comments or thoughts you have about the content.

### 2. **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional)
This field is for you to rate the photo or video on a scale of 1-5. A higher number indicates a more positive opinion.

### 3. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
Here, you can add any additional comments or thoughts about the photo or video. This is an optional field, but your input is valuable in helping us improve our content.
