# Job Importance Rating Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Importance Rating Survey is a tool to help managers and HR teams understand employees' perceptions of their job importance, satisfaction, and other work-related aspects. This survey aims to gather feedback on 18 different job-related questions to help employees provide a comprehensive understanding of their job performance and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the number that best represents your rating for each question.
2. Please answer all questions, as they are all required.
3. Fill out the form in one session, rather than saving and revisiting it later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Importance** (`job_importance_question`, Number, Required): Rate how important you think your job is. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not important at all" and 10 is "very important."
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction_question`, Number, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your current job. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 10 is "very satisfied."
* **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance_question`, Number, Required): Rate how well you balance your work and personal life. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not balanced at all" and 10 is "very well balanced."
* **Work Expectation** (`work_life_expectation_question`, Number, Required): Rate how well your job meets your expectations. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not met at all" and 10 is "very met."
* **Job Engagement** (`job_engagement_question`, Number, Required): Rate how engaged you feel with your job. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not engaged at all" and 10 is "very engaged."
* **Overwhelmed at Work** (`overwhelmed_question`, Number, Required): Rate how often you feel overwhelmed at work. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "never" and 10 is "very often."
* **Work Breaks** (`breaks_question`, Number, Required): Rate how often you take breaks during work hours. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "almost never" and 10 is "very often."
* **Salary Satisfaction** (`salary_satisfaction_question`, Number, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your salary. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 10 is "very satisfied."
* **Autonomy** (`autonomy_question`, Number, Required): Rate how much autonomy you have in your job. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not at all" and 10 is "very much."
* **Colleague Satisfaction** (`colleague_satisfaction_question`, Number, Required): Rate how satisfied you are with your colleagues. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 10 is "very satisfied."
* **Working on Weekends** (`weekend_work_question`, Number, Required): Rate how often you work on weekends. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "almost never" and 10 is "very often."
* **Job Enjoyment** (`enjoyment_question`, Number, Required): Rate how enjoyable you find your job. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not enjoyable at all" and 10 is "very enjoyable."
* **Benefits Package** (`benefits_question`, Number, Required): Rate your satisfaction with your benefits package. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 10 is "very satisfied."
* **Travel for Work** (`travel_question`, Number, Required): Rate how often you travel for work. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "almost never" and 10 is "very often."
* **Workspace Satisfaction** (`workspace_question`, Number, Required): Rate how satisfied you are with your workspace. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 10 is "very satisfied."
* **Work Stress** (`stress_question`, Number, Required): Rate how stressed you feel at work. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not stressed at all" and 10 is "very stressed."
* **Job Title Satisfaction** (`job_title_question`, Number, Required): Rate how satisfied you are with your job title. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "not satisfied at all" and 10 is "very satisfied."
* **Deadlines** (`deadline_question`, Number, Required): Rate how often you feel rushed due to deadlines. Please select a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is "almost never" and 10 is "very often."
