# personal-safety-plan-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a personal safety plan, which is a document used to assess your safety needs and develop strategies to protect yourself from harm.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions on each page to create a comprehensive safety plan.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and complete information.
3. If you are unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Safety Needs Assessment (1)**: Please describe your current safety concerns, such as feeling threatened or experiencing abuse.
* **Safety Plan Goals (2)**: Describe your goals for your safety, such as feeling safe in your home or developing a plan to leave an abusive relationship.
* **Safety Plan Strategies (3)**: Describe the steps you will take to achieve your safety goals, such as contacting a domestic violence agency or staying in a safe location.
* **Safety Network (4)**: List your emergency contacts, such as friends, family, or a support group.
* **Emergency Contact Info (5)**: Provide information about your emergency contacts, including their phone numbers and addresses.
* **Safety Plan Review (6)**: Review your current safety plan and make any necessary updates.
* **Emergency Plan Review (7)**: Review your emergency plan with your emergency contacts.
* **Follow-up Plan (8)**: Check if you will need follow-up actions, and choose 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
* **Next Steps (9)**: Describe the next steps you will take to continue your safety plan, such as seeking support or resources.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for help or clarification.
* This form is not a substitute for professional help or support. If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services or a crisis hotline.
