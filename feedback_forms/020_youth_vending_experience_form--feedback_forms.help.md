Youth Vending Experience Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
The Youth Vending Experience Form is designed to gather feedback from youth vendors and their participants about their experiences with our youth vending programs. The information collected through this form helps us to understand the effectiveness and impact of our programs, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions to enhance the overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start on the first page, which is the introduction.
2. Read the instructions carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
3. Fill out each field with the requested information.
4. Make sure to submit your feedback before leaving the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Introduction </label> (1)
* This is a short introductory page that explains the purpose and context of the form. Read it carefully before proceeding.

### <label> Event Date </label> (2)
* Please select the date on which you attended the event. This will help us track the timeline of our youth vending programs.

### <label> Event Time </label> (3)
* Choose the time slot during the event when you attended. This will aid in scheduling and coordination for future events.

### <label> Event Type </label> (4)
* Indicate whether your experience was a "Yes" or "No" to the event. This helps us understand the scope and reach of our programs.

### <label> Participants </label> (5)
* Select all the participants involved in the event, if any. This information will aid in assessing program effectiveness and engagement.

### <label> Comments </label> (6)
* Write any additional comments or thoughts about your experience. This is an opportunity to provide specific feedback and suggestions for improvement.

### <label> Rating </label> (7)
* Rate your experience with a number, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent. This will help us identify areas that need improvement.

### <label> Rating Scale </label> (8)
* Choose your rating from the scale provided: Good, Fair, or Poor. This helps us understand the overall satisfaction with the event.

### <label> Follow-up </label> (9)
* Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for further information or follow-up about the program. This ensures we keep you updated and engaged in the process.

Tips: Please fill out as much information as possible and answer honestly. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in the youth vending experience form.
