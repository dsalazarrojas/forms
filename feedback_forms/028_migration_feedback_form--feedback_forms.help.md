# Migration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migration Feedback Form is a tool to help us understand how to improve the migration process. Your feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights for future migrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Describe your migration experience in the `migration_process` field.
2. Provide your feedback on your experience in the `feedback` field.
3. Select the category that best describes your experience in the `category` field.
4. Optionally, provide your email address in the `email` field so we can follow up with you.
5. Optionally, provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
6. Rate your migration experience on a scale of 1-10 in the `migration_experience` field.
7. If you have any suggestions for improving the migration process, type them in the `suggestion` field.
8. Select the options that apply to your experience in the `submit` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **migration_process**: Please describe your migration experience, including any challenges you faced and what you liked or disliked about the process.
- **feedback**: Share your thoughts on how the migration process could be improved.
- **category**: Select the category that best describes your experience:
    - Option 1: Excellent
    - Option 2: Good
    - Option 3: Fair
- **email**: If you want us to follow up with you, please provide your email address.
- **phone**: If you would rather we contact you by phone, please provide your phone number.
- **migration_experience**: Rate your migration experience on a scale of 1-10.
- **suggestion**: If you have any suggestions for improving the migration process, type them here.
- **submit**: Select the options that apply to your experience:
    - Option 1: I was able to complete the migration successfully
    - Option 2: I had some difficulty with the migration
    - Option 3: I was not able to complete the migration
