# Quality Incident Treatment Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide feedback on the quality incident treatment process. It is intended for employees, customers, or other stakeholders to provide their input on the treatment process after an incident has been resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you are providing feedback on a quality incident that has been resolved.
2. Read each field carefully and complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Description** (required): A clear and concise description of the incident, including the specific details of what happened.
* **Incident Details** (optional, select one):
	+ Not Applicable: No incident occurred.
	+ Other: Provide a different category for the incident, if necessary.
	+ Quality Incident: A specific quality incident occurred.
* **Severity** (optional, select multiple):
	+ Low: The incident was minor and had minimal impact.
	+ Medium: The incident had some impact on the outcome.
	+ High: The incident had significant impact and resulted in major problems.
* **Action Taken** (required): A detailed description of the actions taken to address the incident.
* **Feedback** (optional): Any additional comments or feedback you have about the incident or the treatment process.
* **Category** (optional, select one):
	+ Category A: A predefined category for the incident.
	+ Category B: A second predefined category for the incident.
* **Contact Details** (optional, email): Contact information for additional communication.
* **Phone Numbers** (optional, select multiple):
	+ +1 123 456 7890: A specific phone number for communication.
	+ +1 987 654 3210: A second specific phone number for communication.
	+ +2 111 111 1111: A third specific phone number for communication.
* **Submit** (required): Click to submit your feedback.
