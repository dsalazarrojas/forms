# Store Layout Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their satisfaction with the store's layout. The purpose is to help the store identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about the design and organization of the store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your satisfaction level with the store's layout.
2. Add any additional suggestions you have for improving the store's layout.
3. Provide any additional comments you have about your experience at the store.
4. If you'd like to provide your contact information, you can do so below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**1. How satisfied are you with the store's layout?** (`store_layout_satisfaction`, number, required: false)
	* This question asks how you feel about the store's layout, with options to choose from Very satisfied, Satisfied, Not satisfied.

**2. Do you have any additional suggestions for improving the store's layout?** (`suggestions`, text, required: false)
	* If you have any ideas for improving the store's layout, type them in here. This is a good opportunity to share your thoughts and help the store make positive changes.

**3. Do you have any additional comments about your experience at the store?** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false)
	* If you'd like to share more about your experience, such as positive or negative comments, write them here.

**4. If you'd like to provide your contact information, you can do so below.** (`email`, email, required: false)
	* If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address and phone number. This is optional, but it will help the store follow up on your suggestions and get in touch with you.

**5. If you'd like to provide your phone number, you can do so below.** (`phone_number`, text, required: false)
	* If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your phone number. This is optional, but it will help the store follow up on your suggestions and get in touch with you.

**6. What is the name of the store you visited?** (`store_name`, text, required: false)
	* If you'd like to provide this information, please type the name of the store you visited.

**7. What were the store's hours of operation during your visit?** (`store_hours`, time, required: false)
	* If you'd like to provide this information, please type the store's hours of operation during your visit.

Note: This form is not required and you can skip any questions you don't feel comfortable answering.
