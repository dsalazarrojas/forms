# Board Discussion Points Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Discussion Points Survey is designed to collect feedback from board members on the quality and effectiveness of board meetings. The survey will help the board improve its meeting processes and communication.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Respondent Name**: Enter your name, if you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave this field blank.
2. **Your Role**: Select your position within the board (e.g., Board Member, Board Chair, Executive Director).
3. **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the board meeting you attended.
4. **Meeting Type**: Choose the type of meeting (e.g., Regular Board Meeting, Special Board Meeting, Annual Meeting, Committee Meeting, or Retreat).
5. **Key Discussion Topics**: Select all topics discussed during the meeting that you feel are relevant.
6. **Most Important Topic**: Identify the most critical topic discussed.
7. **Quality of Discussion**: Rate the overall quality of the discussion.
8. **Time Allocation**: Choose if time was allocated appropriately for the discussion.
9. **Topics Needing More Time**: Select topics that needed more attention.
10. **Topics That Felt Rushed**: List any topics that felt hurried.
11. **Quality of Pre-Meeting Materials**: Rate the quality of materials provided in advance.
12. **Clarity of Action Items**: Rate how clearly action items were defined.
13. **Follow-Up Needed**: List any topics requiring further discussion.
14. **Suggestions for Next Meeting**: Provide any recommendations for the next meeting.
15. **Overall Meeting Effectiveness**: Rate this meeting's effectiveness.
16. **Additional Comments**: Add any other feedback or comments.
