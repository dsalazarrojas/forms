# Healthcare Experience Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients about their experiences with our healthcare services. We use this feedback to understand what we do well and what we can improve on to provide better care for our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field provided.
2. If you have a phone number, provide it to help us contact you for further questions or clarification.
3. If you have a visit date, select the date you visited our healthcare services.
4. Rate your experience using the stars provided.
5. Provide any comments or feedback about your experience.
6. If you have an email address, provide it so we can contact you if needed.
7. Select the department where you received treatment (if applicable).
8. Answer the questions regarding your experience (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required: false): Enter your name in this field.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, provide it to help us contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Visit Date** (Visit Date, date, required: false): If you have the date of your visit, select it in the drop-down menu.
* **Rating** (Rating, select_one, required: false): Rate your experience as Excellent, Good, Neutral, Bad, or Very Bad.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): If you have an email address, provide it so we can contact you if needed.
* **Department** (Department, select_multiple, required: false): If you received treatment from one of our departments, select it.
* **What were you experiencing before coming in?** (What were you experiencing before coming in?, note, required: false): Describe your symptoms or concerns that led you to visit our healthcare services.
* **How did your visit go?** (How did your visit go?, note, required: false): Share your thoughts about your visit and what happened.
* **What can we improve?** (What can we improve?, note, required: false): Suggest ways we can improve our services based on your experience.
