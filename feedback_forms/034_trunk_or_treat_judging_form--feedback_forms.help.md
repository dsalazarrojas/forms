# Trunk Or Treat Judging Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings on the quality and standards of Trunk or Treat events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the corresponding options for each question that best describe your experience at the Trunk or Treat event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Event Name
 * **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the Trunk or Treat event you are providing feedback on. This will help us associate your feedback with the correct event.

### 2. Category
 * **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one of the following options to categorize your feedback:
  * 'Yes'
  * 'No'

### 3. Treat Type
 * **Treat Type** (`treat_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
  * 'Yes'
  * 'No'

### 4. Decoration Standards
