# Community Needs Assessment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Needs Assessment Evaluation Form is designed to help assessors evaluate the needs of a community area, identify key issues, and recommend actions to address them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the person completing the evaluation in the "Evaluator name" field.
2. Describe the community area being assessed in the "Community area assessed" field.
3. Summarize the main issues or needs identified in the "Key needs identified" field.
4. Rank the priority of interventions in the "Priority ranking for interventions" field, if applicable.
5. Rate the quality of feedback collected from stakeholders in the "Stakeholder feedback quality" field.
6. List any missing information needed in the "Data gaps" field.
7. Provide a short list of recommended actions to address the identified needs in the "Recommended actions" field.
8. Specify any resources required to implement the recommended actions in the "Resources required to implement actions" field.
9. Enter a contact email for follow-up in the "Contact email for follow up" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator name (Evaluator name)** (`text`, required): Enter your name as the person completing the evaluation.
* **Community area assessed (Community area assessed)** (`text`, required): Describe the community area being evaluated, such as a neighborhood or district.
* **Key needs identified (Key needs identified)** (`text`, required): Summarize the main issues or needs discovered during the evaluation.
* **Priority ranking for interventions (Priority ranking for interventions)** (`text`, optional): Rank the top interventions to address the identified needs, if applicable. Note: this field is not required but can provide additional context.
* **Stakeholder feedback quality (Stakeholder feedback quality)** (`select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of feedback collected from stakeholders as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Data gaps (Data gaps)** (`text`, optional): List any missing information needed to complete the evaluation.
* **Recommended actions (Recommended actions)** (`text`, required): Provide a short list of recommended actions to address the identified needs.
* **Resources required to implement actions (Resources required to implement actions)** (`text`, optional): Specify any resources required to implement the recommended actions, such as funding or staffing.
* **Contact email for follow up (Contact email for follow up)** (`email`, optional): Enter a contact email for follow-up, if applicable.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the community area and key needs identified.
* Use the "Priority ranking for interventions" field to provide additional context, if applicable.
* When rating the quality of stakeholder feedback, consider factors such as clarity, accuracy, and completeness.
* Make sure to list specific resources required to implement recommended actions.
* Enter a contact email for follow-up if you need to get in touch with someone after the evaluation.
