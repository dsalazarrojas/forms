# Municipality Citizen Input Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from citizens about their experiences and interactions with the municipality. It helps us to identify areas of improvement and ensure that our services meet the needs and expectations of our citizens. Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your input is taken into account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and understand what each field is asking for.
2.  Fill in all required fields with accurate and truthful information.
3.  Take note of any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
4.  Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Feedback (1)**: This field is the main area where you can type in any comments, concerns, or suggestions you have about the municipality services. Be specific about your experiences and mention any positive or negative aspects of your interactions.
*   **Resident Department (2)**: Please select all the departments that you have interacted with during your visit. This will help us to better understand your experience.
*   **Service Concerns (3)**: If you have any concerns or issues with the services you received, please type them in here. This will help us to identify areas where we need to improve.
*   **Resident Information (4)**: Please select the category that best describes your interaction with the municipality. This will help us to categorize your feedback.
*   **Contact Number (5)**: Provide your phone number so that we can contact you if needed. Please make sure to fill in your correct contact number.
*   **Email (6)**: If you have an email address, please fill it in so that we can contact you. This is not required, but it's recommended in case we need to get in touch with you.
*   **Date (7)**: Please select the date of your interaction with the municipality.
*   **Time (8)**: Please select the time of your interaction with the municipality.
*   **Additional Comments (9)**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please type them in here. This is the place to provide any additional information.
*   **Resident Feedback Rating (10)**: Please select the rating that best describes your satisfaction with the municipality services. This will help us to measure our performance.
*   **Additional Comments (11)**: If you have any other comments, suggestions, or concerns, please type them in here.

## Tips
Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form. If you need any help or clarification, do not hesitate to contact us.
