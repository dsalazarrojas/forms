# Quality Control Process Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Quality Control Process Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from end users on the quality control process. This feedback is used to identify areas of improvement and help optimize the process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the department that corresponds to your role or responsibility.
2. Choose the category of quality control issues that pertain to your feedback.
3. Fill in any relevant details about the quality control process that you would like to comment on.
4. Answer the numerical questions regarding specific events or incidents.
5. Choose the relevant quality control processes that apply to your feedback.
6. Select the review type that corresponds to your feedback.
7. Provide your email address so that we can contact you for further clarification or to follow up on your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department**: This field allows you to select the department that you belong to or are responsible for. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Feedback Category**: This field allows you to select the category of quality control issues that pertain to your feedback. Choose the options that best describe your feedback.
* **Process Name**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any relevant details about the quality control process that you would like to comment on.
* **Question 1**: This field asks you to provide a numerical answer regarding a specific event or incident. Please enter a whole number.
* **Question 2**: This field also asks for a numerical answer regarding another event or incident. Please enter a whole number.
* **Question 3**: This field asks for a date when the incident or event occurred.
* **Question 4**: This field asks for a time when the incident or event occurred.
* **Quality Control Process**: This field allows you to select the relevant quality control processes that apply to your feedback. Choose the options that best describe your feedback.
* **Review**: This field allows you to select the type of review that corresponds to your feedback. This will help us understand the scope and nature of your feedback.
* **Date**: This field asks for the date when you are providing the feedback.
* **Email**: This field is optional and allows you to provide your email address so that we can contact you for further clarification or to follow up on your feedback.
