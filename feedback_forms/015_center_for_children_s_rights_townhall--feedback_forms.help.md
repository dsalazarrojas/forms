# Center For Children's Rights Townhall - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at the Center for Children's Rights Townhall event. Your input will help us improve future events and better serve the community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a way that reflects your honest opinion on each of the presented questions.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. If you wish to be contacted for future events, please provide your email address in the "Your Email Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Townhall**: Please enter the date of the townhall event you attended.
* **Your Full Name**: This is an optional field where you can provide your name if you wish to be contacted for future events. If you choose to remain anonymous, you can simply leave this field blank.
* **How did you hear about this townhall**: This question aims to understand how you became aware of the event. Select the method that best describes how you found out about the townhall.
* **Clarity of Advocacy Goals**: Rate how clear the goals presented by the Urban League were. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Presentation on Children's Rights**: Rate how well the speakers explained current children's rights issues. Choose from the options: Very well, Somewhat well, Not very well.
* **Adequacy of Q and A Time**: Indicate whether you felt there was enough time for participant questions. Choose from the options: True, False, Somewhat.
* **Most Important Topic Discussed**: Share with us what you thought was the most impactful or relevant topic discussed during the event.
* **Issues to Prioritize**: Select which areas you think the Center for Children's Rights should focus on. You can choose one or more from the options: Education Equity, Healthcare Access, Juvenile Justice Reform, Foster Care Support, Community Safety.
* **Future Involvement**: Indicate your interest in future advocacy events. Choose from the options: Yes - Add me to the list, False.
* **Your Email Address**: If you wish to be contacted for future events, please provide your email address.
* **Comments or Suggestions**: Share any other thoughts or suggestions you might have for the Center for Children's Rights.
