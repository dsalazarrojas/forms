# Coworking Space Member Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from coworking space members on their experience, satisfaction, and any comments they might have regarding their visit. It's a required form to help us improve our services and make the coworking space a better place for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with your feedback, satisfaction, and comments.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and truthfully.
3. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the email field.
4. Select the most relevant category from the dropdown menu for your feedback.
5. Choose the most relevant tag from the dropdown menu for your feedback.
6. Indicate which coworking space your feedback is about.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (`page_1`): Please provide your thoughts and opinions about your recent visit to the coworking space.
* **Satisfaction** (`page_2`): Rate your level of satisfaction with our services, with 1 being the least satisfied and 10 being the most satisfied.
* **Comments** (`page_3`): Share any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
* **Email** (`page_4`): Enter a valid email address so we can follow up with you, if needed.
* **Phone** (`page_5`): Enter a phone number where we can reach you, if we need to discuss your feedback further.
* **Category** (`page_6`): Select the category that best describes your feedback, such as "Feedback" or "Other".
* **Tags** (`page_7`): Choose one or more tags that best describe your feedback, such as "Feedback", "Tag", or "Other".
* **Coworking Space** (`page_8`): Indicate which coworking space your feedback is about.
* **Description** (`page_9`): (Optional) Provide a brief description of your feedback.
* **Submit** (`page_10`): Click this button to submit your feedback and send it to us.
