# Nile River Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nile River Experience Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from guests after a tour or experience with a Nile River guide or tour operator.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the type of tour you experienced (e.g. Nile Cruise, Nile River Tour, or Egyptian Desert Tour).
2. Enter the date of your tour or experience (if applicable).
3. Provide your Guest Name.
4. Rate your tour experience (on a scale of 1-10).
5. Share any comments or suggestions you may have.
6. Enter your email address (if you would like to be contacted for follow-up).
7. If you were assigned a tool, please enter the name of the tool used.
8. Enter your name, so we can attribute your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Type**: The type of tour you experienced, such as Nile Cruise, Nile River Tour, or Egyptian Desert Tour.
* **Tour Date**: The date of your tour or experience. If you didn't have a tour date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Guest Name**: Your name, so we can attribute your feedback.
* **Tour Rating**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comment**: Share any comments or suggestions you may have about your tour or experience.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Assigned Tool**: If you were assigned a tool, such as a guide or equipment, please enter the name of the tool used.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name, so we can attribute your feedback.
