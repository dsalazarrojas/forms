## Purpose
The Personal Configuration Survey form is designed to collect information about your personal preferences, contact details, and feedback. This form is intended to gather data that will help us improve our services and tailor our communication to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please go through each page of the form and fill out the required fields.
2. Be careful to select only one option for fields marked as "Select One".
3. You can select multiple options for fields marked as "Select Multiple".
4. Enter accurate information in text fields, such as your name, email, and phone number.
5. Use the calendar to select a date for fields labeled "Date".
6. Choose the correct time for fields labeled "Time".
7. For fields marked as "Number", please enter a numerical value.
8. Use the "Note" field to provide additional comments or feedback.
