# Remote Work Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your remote work experience. It will help us understand your habits, preferences, and challenges related to remote work. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Use the space provided to type in your response.
4. Select the options that apply to your remote work experience.
5. If you have any other feedback or comments, type it in the "Do you have any other feedback?" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Work Experience

*   **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `text`, required): Please describe your current or previous work experience, including the type of work you do, the industry, and any relevant details.

### 2. How many days a week do you work remotely?

*   **How many days a week do you work remotely?** (`remote_work_frequency`, `number`, required): Indicate how many days a week you work remotely. Please choose a whole number between 1 and 7.

### 3. Where do you work?

*   **Where do you work?** (`work_location`, `text`, required): Describe the location where you typically work. This can be your home, a co-working space, or a dedicated office.

### 4. Work location details

*   **Work location details** (`work_location_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about your work location, such as its size, noise level, or natural light availability.

### 5. What kind of work environment do you typically work in?

*   **What kind of work environment do you typically work in?** (`work_environment`, `select_one`, required): Choose one of the options:
    *   Office
    *   Home
    *   Co-working space
    *   Outdoor

### 6. What kind of schedule do you typically keep?

*   **What kind of schedule do you typically keep?** (`work_schedule`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all that apply:
    *   Regular business hours
    *   Flexible hours
    *   Flexible hours with core hours
    *   Variable

### 7. What tools and software do you use to stay productive while working remotely?

*   **What tools and software do you use to stay productive while working remotely?** (`productivity_tools`, `text`, required): List the tools and software you use to stay productive while working remotely, such as video conferencing tools, project management software, or communication apps.

### 8. What challenges do you face while working remotely?

*   **What challenges do you face while working remotely?** (`challenges_faced`, `text`, required): Describe the challenges you face while working remotely, such as staying motivated, managing distractions, or establishing boundaries with colleagues.

### 9. What benefits do you think you get from working remotely?

*   **What benefits do you think you get from working remotely?** (`benefits_of_remote_work`, `text`, required): Describe the benefits you receive from working remotely, such as increased flexibility, improved work-life balance, or reduced commuting time.

### 10. Do you have any other feedback?

*   **Do you have any other feedback?** (`feedback`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in this section.
