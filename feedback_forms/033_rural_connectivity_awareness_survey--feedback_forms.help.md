# rural_connectivity_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rural_connectivity_awareness_survey form is designed to gather information on your experience with internet access, speed, and reliability, as well as any challenges you face in getting online. This data will help us better understand and address the needs of rural communities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please navigate through each page and fill out the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Internet Access** (`Internet Access`, select_one, required): Please select the best description of your current internet access situation.
	+ Option 1: Mobile data
	+ Option 2: Fixed (wired) connection
	+ Option 3: Mobile (wireless) connection
* **Internet Speed** (`Internet Speed`, number, optional): Enter your average internet speed.
* **Internet Reliability** (`Internet Reliability`, number, optional): Enter a number between 1 and 5 to rate the reliability of your internet connection.
* **Connectivity Challenges** (`Connectivity Challenges`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Poor signal strength
	+ Option 2: High latency
	+ Option 3: Limited data caps
	+ Option 4: Outages or disruptions
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about your internet access.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, select_one, required): Select the name of your organization.
	+ Option 1: Community Center
	+ Option 2: School/University
	+ Option 3: Local Government
* **Role** (`Role`, select_multiple, required): Select your role within your organization.
	+ Option 1: Director
	+ Option 2: Staff
	+ Option 3: Student
* **Department** (`Department`, select_one, required): Select the name of your department.
	+ Option 1: IT
	+ Option 2: Community Development
	+ Option 3: Education
* **Comments Organization** (`Comments Organization`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your organization.
* **Comments Phone** (`Comments Phone`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your phone experience.
* **Comments Email** (`Comments Email`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your email experience.
* **Comments Department** (`Comments Department`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your department experience.
* **Comments Role** (`Comments Role`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your role experience.
* **Comments Internet Speed** (`Comments Internet Speed`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your internet speed experience.
* **Comments Internet Access** (`Comments Internet Access`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your internet access experience.
* **Comments Internet Reliability** (`Comments Internet Reliability`, text, optional): Share any additional comments about your internet reliability experience.
* **Connectivity Challenges** (`Connectivity Challenges`, select_multiple, required): Check all that apply:
	+ Option 1: Poor signal strength
