# Downtown Visitor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to Downtown, allowing us to understand their experiences and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To submit your feedback, simply select the categories that apply to your visit, and provide any additional details you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Category** (`feedback_category`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more categories that best describe your experience. Please select all that apply.
  - Parking: issues with parking availability or accessibility
  - Cleanliness: concerns with cleanliness or maintenance in public areas
  - Accessibility: challenges with mobility or accessibility features
  - Other: anything else you'd like to share
- **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, text, optional): If you're willing, please provide your name so we can follow up with you personally.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for more information, please provide your email address.
- **Visitor Message** (`visitor_message`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about your visit.
  - Please be as detailed as possible to help us understand your experience.
- **Date of Visit** (`date_visited`, date, optional): Let us know when you visited Downtown so we can track any changes over time.
