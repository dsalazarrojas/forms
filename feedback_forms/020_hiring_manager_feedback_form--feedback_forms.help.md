# Hiring Manager Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by hiring managers to provide feedback on candidates after an interview or assessment. Your feedback will help us understand how well the candidate performed and will aid in our decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the candidate(s) you would like to provide feedback on.
2. Complete the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
3. Submit the form once you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Candidate Rating** (optional: no)
Please rate the candidate based on their performance, using a scale from 1-5, with 1 being "Needs Improvement" and 5 being "Exceeds Expectations".

### 2. **Overall Score** (optional: no)
Enter a score from 1 to 5 for the candidate's overall performance.

### 3. **Candidate Comments** (optional: no)
Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for the candidate.

### 4. **Manager Comments** (optional: no)
This is your opportunity to provide any additional context or suggestions for the candidate's development.

Tips:
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Please provide specific examples of what the candidate did well or could improve on.
* Your feedback will be kept confidential and only used by the HR department and hiring team.
