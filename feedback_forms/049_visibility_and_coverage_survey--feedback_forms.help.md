# Visibility And Coverage Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Visibility And Coverage Survey" is a form designed to gather feedback and insights from customers to understand their needs and preferences related to our product or service. This form is intended to help us improve and better serve our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
2. If a question does not apply to you, please select "Not applicable" or "Not relevant" where possible.
3. Use the provided options for select questions. For text fields, provide a brief explanation or description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**:
	+ **Organization** (`survey_organization`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your organization.
* **What is the purpose of this survey?**:
	+ **What is the purpose of this survey?** (`question1`, text, optional): This question helps us understand the reason behind this survey.
* **To understand customer needs and preferences**:
	+ **To understand customer needs and preferences** (`question1_answer`, text, optional): Please provide a brief explanation of the purpose of this survey.
* **How would you rate the visibility of our product/service?**:
	+ **How would you rate the visibility of our product/service?** (`question2`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
		- Not visible
		- Somewhat visible
		- Highly visible
* **How would you rate the coverage of our product/service?**:
	+ **How would you rate the coverage of our product/service?** (`question3`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply:
		- Not covered
		- Somewhat covered
		- Highly covered
		- Very highly covered
		- Extremely highly covered
* **Have you ever used this product/service before?**:
	+ **Have you ever used this product/service before?** (`question4`, select_one, optional): Please select one of the following options:
		- True
		- False
* **What feedback do you have about our product/service?**:
	+ **What feedback do you have about our product/service?** (`feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have about our product or service.
* **What would you suggest we do next?**:
	+ **What would you suggest we do next?** (`question5`, text, optional): Please provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement.
* **How likely are you to recommend our product/service to a friend?**:
	+ **How likely are you to recommend our product/service to a friend?** (`question6`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
		- Not likely
		- Somewhat likely
		- Highly likely
		- Very likely
		- Extremely likely
* **How likely are you to use our product/service again?**:
	+ **How likely are you to use our product/service again?** (`question7`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
		- Not likely
		- Somewhat likely
		- Highly likely
		- Very likely
		- Extremely likely
