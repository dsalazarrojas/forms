# Hospital Community Relations Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gauge your overall satisfaction with your visit to the hospital, as well as your experiences with our services and communication. Your feedback will help us improve our services and better serve our community. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey, and help us create a more positive experience for all patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, and click "Next" to proceed to the next page.
2. You will be asked to rate your satisfaction with your visit and services used.
3. You will be able to choose which services you used during your visit.
4. You will have the opportunity to provide feedback on our community communication.
5. Once you have completed all pages, click "Submit" to send your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your overall satisfaction with your visit?** (`satisfaction`, number, required: false)
	+ This is a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not satisfied" and 5 is "Extremely satisfied". Please select a number to rate your overall satisfaction with your visit.
* **Which services used at the hospital?** (`services_used`, select multiple, required: false)
	+ Please select all services you used during your visit. You can select more than one service.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **How would you rate our community communication?** (`community_communication`, text, required: false)
	+ Please provide a brief description of your experience with our community communication. This will help us understand how we can improve.
