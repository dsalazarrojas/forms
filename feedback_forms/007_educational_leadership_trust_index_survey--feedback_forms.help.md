# Educational Leadership Trust Index Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Leadership Trust Index Survey is a tool used to measure the level of trust that educational leaders have in their school's management and policies. It's designed to gather opinions and perceptions of leaders in various aspects of trust, which can help identify areas of strength and weakness, and inform improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

*   Start by filling in your school's name in the "Name of the School" field.
*   Answer each question truthfully, and choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
*   Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as "Required").

### Step 2: Complete Each Field
Each field in this survey is labeled with a brief description of what the field is for. Please read each label carefully before answering:

*   **Name of the School** (`school_name`, text, Required): Enter the name of your school.
*   **Select one** (`select_one`, select_one, Optional): Choose one option that best reflects your opinion.
*   **Educational leadership level** (`edu_leadership_level`, select_one, Optional): Choose one option that best reflects your level of educational leadership.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter your email address.
*   **Role in the School** (`role_in_school`, text, Optional): Select all that apply.
*   **Years working in the School** (`years_working_in_school`, select_one, Optional): Choose one option that best reflects your years working in the school.
*   **Time in School** (`time_in_school`, time, Optional): Enter your time spent working in the school.
*   **What is your Role in the School?** (`role_in_school_1`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all that apply.
*   **How long have you worked in the School?** (`years_working_in_school_1`, select_one, Optional): Choose one option that best reflects how long you have worked in the school.
*   **Role In School 3** (`role_in_school_3`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all that apply.
*   **Role In School 4** (`role_in_school_4`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all that apply.

## Tips

*   Please be honest in your answers, as the survey relies on accurate opinions and perceptions.
*   If you have any questions or need help, please contact your supervisor or administrator.
*   The results of this survey will be used to inform improvements to the school's policies and practices.
