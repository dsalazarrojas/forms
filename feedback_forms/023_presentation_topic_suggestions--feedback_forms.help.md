# Presentation Topic Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect presentation topic suggestions from users. It is a simple and straightforward tool for users to provide their ideas and feedback on presentation topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with your presentation topic suggestion.
* You can add any additional information or notes to support your suggestion.
* Choose how you want to submit your topic: "Submit and Review" will send your suggestion for review and potential inclusion in a future presentation, "Save as Draft" will save your suggestion for later, and "Cancel" will discard your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Presentation Topic Suggestions Form** (`text`, required/optional): This is the main text area where you can type in your presentation topic suggestion. Please make sure to provide a clear and concise idea.
* **Topic Name** (`text`, optional): This field is for you to provide a title for your presentation topic suggestion. It can be brief or detailed, as you see fit.
* **Topic Description** (`text`, optional): Use this field to provide additional information about your presentation topic suggestion. This can be a brief paragraph or a more detailed description.
* **Presenter Name** (`text`, optional): If you are proposing a speaker, please provide their name here.
* **Topic Category** (`text`, optional): Choose a category that best fits your presentation topic suggestion.
* **Topic Tags** (`text`, optional): Provide any relevant tags or keywords that describe your presentation topic suggestion.
* **Topic Image** (`text`, optional): If you have an image that relates to your presentation topic suggestion, you can provide a link or the file name.
* **Topic Video** (`text`, optional): If you have a video that relates to your presentation topic suggestion, you can provide a link or the file name.
* **Submit Topic** (`select_one`, required): Choose how you want to submit your topic: "Submit and Review" will send your suggestion for review, "Save as Draft" will save your suggestion for later, and "Cancel" will discard your changes.
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): This is an additional text area for you to provide any additional information or notes about your presentation topic suggestion.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide a clear and concise idea for your presentation topic suggestion.
* You can add any additional information or notes to support your suggestion.
* If you are proposing a speaker, please make sure to check their availability before submitting your suggestion.
* If you are providing a topic image or video, please ensure that it is relevant and not copyrighted.
* Submitting a suggestion does not guarantee that it will be used, but it will be reviewed and considered for inclusion in future presentations.
