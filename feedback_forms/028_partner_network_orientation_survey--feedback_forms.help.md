This form, "Partner Network Orientation Survey", is designed to collect feedback and information from partners regarding their experiences with the partner network. It is intended for both new and existing partners to help us better understand their needs, preferences, and areas of improvement. 

The goal of this form is to ensure that the partner's feedback and experiences are accurately reflected in the survey and that the information gathered is used to improve the overall partner experience and network effectiveness.

This form should not be filled out by individuals who have not been part of the partner network, as it is meant for partners specifically.

## Partner Network Orientation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This survey is designed to collect feedback and information from partners about their experiences with our partner network. It helps us understand their needs, preferences, and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select all the fields that apply to your experiences with our partner network.
2.  Answer the questions accurately and concisely.
3.  Make sure to provide all required fields (marked with \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *User Input*: This field is used to identify which type of partner you are. 
*   *Feedback*: Please provide any comments or observations you have about your experience with our partner network.
*   *Partner Name*: This field is for entering the name of your partner company. 
*   *Contact Number*: This field is for entering your contact number for future communication. 
*   *Contact Email*: This field is for entering your email address for future communication. 
*   *Business Name*: This field is for entering your business name. 
*   *Business Description*: This field is for entering a brief description of your business. 
*   *Onboarding Process*: This field is for entering your onboarding process. 
*   *Onboarding Process Comments*: This field is for any additional comments about your onboarding process. 
*   *Partner Resources*: This field is for entering any additional resources you used during your onboarding process. 
*   *Additional Resources Requested*: This field is for entering any additional resources you would like to use in the future. 
*   *Support Experience*: Please select yes or no if you have had a positive or negative experience with our support. 
*   *Support Team*: Please select yes or no if you have had a positive or negative experience with our support team. 
*   *Support Level*: Please select yes or no if you feel our support level is high or low. 
*   *Communication Channel*: Please select yes or no if you prefer to communicate with us through a specific channel. 
*   *Communication Frequency*: Please select yes or no if you would like to communicate with us more or less frequently. 
*   *Communication Effectiveness*: Please select yes or no if you feel our communication is effective or not. 
*   *Feedback Frequency*: Please select yes or no if you would like to receive feedback more or less frequently. 
*   *Feedback Quality*: Please select good or poor if you feel the quality of our feedback is good or poor. 
*   *Communication Clarity*: This field is for entering any comments about the clarity of our communication. 
*   *Feedback Improved*: This field is for entering any additional comments about how our feedback has improved your experiences. 
*   *Support Improved*: This field is for entering any additional comments about how our support has improved your experiences. 
*   *Communication Improved*: This field is for entering any additional comments about how our communication has improved your experiences. 
*   *Overall Experience*: Please select yes or no if you have had a positive or negative overall experience with our partner network. 
*   *Additional Comments*: This field is for entering any additional comments or suggestions you have.

Note: The fields with an asterisk \* are required. Please make sure to fill them out accurately.
