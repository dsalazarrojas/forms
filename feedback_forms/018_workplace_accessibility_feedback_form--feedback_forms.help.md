# Workplace Accessibility Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on accessibility issues in the workplace. It ensures that employees can report any difficulties they face due to accessibility barriers, allowing the management to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to create a more inclusive environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details in the "User Input" field.
2.  Specify the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
3.  Identify any accessibility issues you are experiencing by selecting one or multiple options from the "Accessibility Issues" field.
4.  Provide any suggestions you may have to improve accessibility in the "Suggestions" field.
5.  If you have any specific concerns or input, enter them in the "Employee Input" field.
6.  Date and time of submission in the "Date Submitted" and "Time Submitted" fields.
7.  Choose who to submit this feedback to by selecting "Manager", "Supervisor", "HR", or "Other" from the "Submitted To" field.
8.  Select one option from the "Submitted By" field to indicate who is submitting the feedback.
9.  If you have any further comments or details, enter them in the "Comments" field.
10.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field (optional).
11.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`text`, required/optional): This is a personal input field where you can add any information about yourself.

### Department
* **Department** (`text`, required/optional): In this field, please provide the department you belong to.

### Accessibility Issues
* **Accessibility Issues** (`select_multiple`, required): Select one or more options from the drop-down menu to specify the accessibility issues you are experiencing.

### Suggestions
* **Suggestions** (`text`, required/optional): In this field, enter any suggestions you have to improve accessibility in your workplace.

### Employee Input
* **Employee Input** (`text`, required/optional): If you have any specific concerns or input, please enter them here.

### Date Submitted
* **Date Submitted** (`date`, required): This field should have the date of your submission.

### Time Submitted
* **Time Submitted** (`time`, required): This field should have the time of your submission.

### Submitted To
* **Submitted To** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose who this feedback should be submitted to.

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`select_one`, required): Select who is submitting this feedback.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`note`, required/optional): In this field, please enter any further comments or details about your submission.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): If you wish to be contacted about this feedback, please enter your email address here.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`text`, required/optional): If you would like to be contacted about this feedback, enter your phone number here.

Note: The fields marked as `required` must be filled in to complete the form.
