# Cheer Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees of cheer events to provide feedback on their experiences. It's crucial for us to understand your thoughts and suggestions to improve the overall quality of our events.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit your feedback, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and role at the event (e.g., Athlete, Coach, Parent, etc.).
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the facility and organization, including venue suitability, event organization, and parking accessibility.
4. Choose your favorite performance or team that stood out to you.
5. Provide your perception of the judging fairness.
6. Share any suggestions for improvements for next year's event.
7. Let us know if you'd like to attend future events.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Cheer Event**: Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Your Role at the Event**: Choose your role at the event, such as Athlete, Coach, Parent, or Spectator.
* **Overall Event Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Rating of Venue Suitability**: Rate your satisfaction with the venue's suitability, considering factors like seating, lighting, and floor quality.
* **How well was the event Organized**: Rate how well you felt the event was organized, including schedule, check-in, and announcements.
* **Ease of Parking and Access**: Rate how easy or difficult it was to park and access the venue. (Optional)
* **Most Memorable Team or Performance**: Choose the team or performance that stood out to you.
* **Perception of Judging Fairness**: Rate how fair you felt the judging process was. (Note: this might be subjective.)
* **Suggestions for Next Year**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving our events.
* **Would you attend our future events?**: Let us know if you're interested in attending future events.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Share any additional thoughts or feedback you'd like to provide.
* **Date Submitted**: This field is automatically populated with the current date and time.
