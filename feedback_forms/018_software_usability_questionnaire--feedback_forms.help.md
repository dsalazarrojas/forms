# software_usability_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The software usability questionnaire is designed to collect feedback on the usability of our software. This will help us understand your experience and identify areas where we can improve the software for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure you've used the software before and have an opinion on its usability.
3. Fill out all sections of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **software_usability_questionnaire** (select_multiple):
	+ Select one or more of the options below that apply to your experience with the software.
	+ This will help us understand your overall experience.
* **overall_rating** (number):
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the software, from 1-10.
	+ A higher rating indicates that you're very satisfied with the software.
* **feedback** (text):
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions on how we can improve the software.
	+ This is an opportunity to share your thoughts and ideas on how we can make the software more user-friendly.
* **rating_scale** (number):
	+ This field is currently not being used. Please do not fill it out.
* **comments** (text):
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions on how we can improve the software.
	+ This is an opportunity to share your thoughts and ideas on how we can make the software more user-friendly.
* **recommended_for_use** (select_one):
	+ Select whether you would recommend the software to others based on your experience.
	+ This will help us understand the impact of our usability improvements.
