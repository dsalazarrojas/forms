# Subscription Content Quality Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of our subscription content. It helps us understand what our customers think of our content and make improvements for a better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, and be as specific as possible in your feedback.
2. If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "Additional Comments" field to share your thoughts.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with a \* symbol next to the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What do you think of the latest content?** (*subscription_content_quality*): Tell us your overall impression of the content you viewed. This field is required.
* **What do you like most about our service?** (*satisfaction*): Let us know what you appreciate most about our service. This field is optional, so feel free to answer or skip it if not applicable.
* **Do you have any recommendations for improvement?** (*recommendations*): If you have any ideas on how we can improve our service, share them with us. This field is optional.
* **How can we contact you for follow-up discussions?** (*contact_info*): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussions or to receive updates, provide your email address or other contact information. This field is optional.
* **Email address** (*email*): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your email address. This field is optional.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (*additional_comments*): Share any additional thoughts or feedback you may have. This field is optional.
* **What is your current subscription status?** (*subscription_status*): Please select your current subscription status. This field is required.
* **What type of content do you usually consume on our platform?** (*content_type*): Choose the type of content you usually access on our platform. This field is required.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (*additional_comments*): Share any additional thoughts or feedback you may have. This field is optional.
