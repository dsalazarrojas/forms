# Training Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate a training program and provide feedback on its quality and effectiveness. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the participant who attended the training session.
2. Enter the date you attended the session.
3. Provide the time the session took place.
4. Select all the topics covered during the session (you can select multiple options).
5. Rate the participant's overall satisfaction with the session.
6. Rate the session itself, considering its quality and relevance.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
8. If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them in the text field.
9. Share any comments about the session, if applicable.
10. Enter your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Training Program Evaluation Form** (`form_title`, text): This is the title of the form, which you can use to identify the session being evaluated.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text): Enter your name as the participant who attended the session.
- **Session Date** (`session_date`, date): Enter the date the session took place.
- **Session Time** (`session_time`, time): Enter the time the session took place.
- **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, select_multiple): Select all the topics covered during the session.
  - You can select multiple options by holding down the Ctrl key and clicking on multiple items.
  - Use the provided options to select the relevant topics.
- **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session, on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, number): Rate the session itself, considering its quality and relevance, on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Session Satisfaction** (`session_satisfaction`, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session, on a scale from 1 to 10.
- **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): If you have any suggestions for improvement, enter them here.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text): Share any comments about the session, if applicable.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): Enter your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Try to provide constructive feedback that can be used to improve future training sessions.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, make sure to include them in the corresponding fields.
* Enter your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you.
