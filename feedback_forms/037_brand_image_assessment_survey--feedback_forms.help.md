# Brand Image Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the brand image of our organization, gathering information on various aspects of the brand, including brand perception, attributes, emotional connection, trustworthiness, and likelihood of recommendation. The data collected will help us understand how our brand is perceived by our customers and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with the required information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
*   Take your time to think through each question carefully before providing your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Brand Perception Survey --** (`perception_header`, note, required: false)
    This section serves as an introduction to the survey, providing an overview of what to expect.
*   **Customer ID / Name (Optional)** (`cust_id_opt`, text, required: false)
    This field allows you to provide your Customer ID or Name, if you wish to do so. However, it is optional and will not impact your survey responses.
*   **How familiar are you with our brand?** (`familiarity_lvl`, select_one, required: true)
    Please select one of the provided options: Very Familiar, Somewhat Familiar, Not Very Familiar, or Just Heard of It, indicating your level of familiarity with our brand.
*   **-- Brand Attributes --** (`attributes_header`, note, required: false)
    This section serves as an introduction to the attributes of our brand, providing more context for the questions that follow.
*   **How would you rate the quality of our products and services?** (`quality_score`, select_one, required: true)
    Please rate our products and services as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
*   **How would you rate the value for money?** (`value_score`, select_one, required: true)
    Rate our value for money as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
*   **To what extent do you perceive our brand as innovative?** (`innovation_score`, select_one, required: true)
    Select Highly Innovative, Somewhat Innovative, Not very Innovative, or Not at all Innovative, describing your perception of our brand's innovation level.
*   **-- Emotional Connection --** (`emotional_header`, note, required: false)
    This section provides context for the emotional connection questions that follow.
*   **What three words come to mind when you think of our brand?** (`brand_words`, text, required: true)
    Please provide three words that come to mind when you think of our brand.
*   **How much do you trust our brand?** (`trust_score`, select_one, required: true)
    Rate our brand as Completely Trust, Mostly Trust, Neutral, Somewhat Distrust, or Completely Distrust.
*   **-- Comparison --** (`comparison_header`, note, required: false)
    This section provides context for the comparison question that follows.
*   **What makes our brand stand out compared to competitors?** (`competitor_diff`, text, required: true)
    Describe what makes our brand distinct from competitors.
*   **How likely are you to recommend our brand to others?** (`recommend_likelihood`, select_one, required: true)
    Select Extremely Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Extremely Unlikely, indicating your likelihood of recommending our brand to others.
*   **Any other comments on how we can improve our brand image?** (`image_improve_feedback`, text, required: false)
    Provide any additional thoughts or suggestions on how to improve our brand image.
*   **Survey Date** (`surv_dt`, date, required: true)
    This field will automatically capture the date and time of your submission, and you do not need to fill it out manually.

Tips: 

*   Take the time to carefully consider each question before providing your responses.
*   Remember that this survey is designed to help us understand our brand image and identify areas for improvement, so please be as honest and detailed as possible in your answers. 
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the survey, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
