# Store Features Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with a store, allowing the store owners to understand customer satisfaction with various aspects of the store's features and services. By filling out this form, you will help the store understand how to improve and better serve its customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most applicable response for each question.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. What is your overall impression of the store's appearance? (Overall Impression)
* **Overall Impression (1)** (`overall_impression`, text, optional): Please provide your honest feedback about the store's appearance. This will help the store understand how well they are presenting themselves to customers.

### 2. How would you rate the store's product variety? (Product Variety)
* **How would you rate the store's product variety?** (`product_variety`, select_one, required): Rate the variety of products available at the store. You can choose from: Excellent (9/10), Good (7/10), Fair (6/10), or Poor (5/10).

### 3. How would you rate the store's customer service? (Customer Service)
* **How would you rate the store's customer service?** (`customer_service`, select_one, required): Rate the helpfulness and friendliness of the store's staff. You can choose from: Excellent (9/10), Good (7/10), Fair (6/10), or Poor (5/10).

### 4. What is your favorite product or feature at the store? (Favorite Product)
* **What is your favorite product or feature at the store?** (`favorite_product`, text, optional): Specify the product or feature that you enjoy the most.

### 5. How easy was it to navigate the store's website? (Website Navigation)
* **How easy was it to navigate the store's website?** (`website_navigation`, select_one, required): Rate the ease of navigation on the store's website. You can choose from: Excellent (9/10), Good (7/10), Fair (6/10), or Poor (5/10).

### 6. How would you rate the store's product quality? (Product Quality)
* **How would you rate the store's product quality?** (`product_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of products available at the store. You can choose from: Excellent (9/10), Good (7/10), Fair (6/10), or Poor (5/10).

### 7. How would you rate the store's prices? (Prices)
* **How would you rate the store's prices?** (`prices`, select_one, required): Rate the store's prices in comparison to other stores. You can choose from: Excellent (9/10), Good (7/10), Fair (6/10), or Poor (5/10).

### 8. How would you rate the store's environment? (Store Environment)
* **How would you rate the store's environment?** (`store_environment`, select_one, required): Rate the store's cleanliness and comfort. You can choose from: Excellent (9/10), Good (7/10), Fair (6/10), or Poor (5/10).

### 9. How would you rate the store's checkout process? (Checkout Process)
* **How would you rate the store's checkout process?** (`checkout_process`, select_one, required): Rate the efficiency of the checkout process. You can choose from: Excellent (9/10), Good (7/10), Fair (6/10), or Poor (5/10).

### 10. Would you return to this store in the future? (Return to Store)
* **Would you return to this store in the future?** (`return_to_store`, checkbox, required): Indicate your likelihood of returning to the store. You can choose: True or False.

### 11. What did you like most about the store? (Like Most About Store)
* **What did you like most about the store?** (`like_most_about_store`, text, optional): Specify the feature or product you liked most.

### 12. What suggestions do you have for the store's improvement? (Store Suggestions)
* **What suggestions do you have for the store's improvement?** (`store_suggestions`, text, required): Provide feedback to help the store improve.
