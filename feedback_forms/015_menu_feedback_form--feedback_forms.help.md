# Menu Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about menu options. Your input will help us improve the menu to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant menu category from the dropdown menu.
2. Rate your experience (if you wish to provide a numerical rating).
3. Optionally, provide comments about your experience.
4. If you have a preferred contact method, you can optionally provide your email address or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **MenuCategory** (`MenuCategory`, required, select_one): Choose the category of the menu option you are giving feedback on.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, optional, number): Rate your experience (1-5) or leave this field blank if you don't want to rate.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, optional, text): Share your thoughts about your experience.
* **Email** (`Email`, optional, email): Provide your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, optional, text): Enter your phone number for us to reach you.
