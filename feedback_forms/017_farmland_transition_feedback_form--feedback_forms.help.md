# Farmland Transition Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users regarding their experience with farmland transition. Your feedback will help us improve the services and support provided to you and other users in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as prompted. You will be asked for your experience rating, satisfaction rating, and any support needs.
2. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them in the "Notes" field.
3. If you need to contact us about your experience, please provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Review your answers carefully and submit the form when you are ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Land Transfer** (`farmland_transfer`, text, required): A brief description of the land transfer you are providing feedback on. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Experience Rating** (`experience_rating`, number, required): Rate your experience with the land transfer from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the land transfer from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`, select_multiple, optional): If you need any support, select all that apply. This will help us understand where we can improve.
* **Transfer Date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): The date of the land transfer. This will help us track your experience over time.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Any additional feedback or comments you may have about your experience.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you need to contact us about your experience, please provide your contact number.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* Your feedback is appreciated and will help us improve our services and support for you and other users.
