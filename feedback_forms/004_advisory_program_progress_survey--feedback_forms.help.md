# Advisory Program Progress Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from participants in the advisory program to help improve the program's effectiveness and provide valuable information to both advisors and program administrators. The survey aims to evaluate your experience, progress, and satisfaction with the program.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible in the text fields.
*   For multiple-choice questions, select the most accurate option from the provided choices.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to provide a complete and accurate feedback.
*   If you have any suggestions or additional feedback, please use the text fields to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, optional): Enter your unique participant identification number.
*   **Advisory Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the advisory program you are participating in.
*   **Advisor Name** (`advisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your assigned advisor.
*   **Program Duration Completed** (`program_duration_completed`, select_one, required): Rate how much of the advisory program you have completed.
	+   Just started
	+   1-3 months
	+   3-6 months
	+   6-12 months
	+   Over 12 months
*   **Initial Goals Set at Program Start** (`initial_goals_set`, text, required): Describe your primary goals when you joined the program.
*   **Progress Towards Initial Goals** (`progress_towards_goals`, number, required): Rate your progress towards achieving your initial goals (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is not much progress and 5 is complete progress).
*   **Have Your Goals Changed Since Starting the Program?** (`goals_updated`, select_one, required): Indicate whether your goals have changed since the start of the program.
	+   True
	+   False
*   **Details of Updated Goals** (`goals_updated_details`, text, optional): If your goals changed, please describe the new goals.
*   **Satisfaction with Meeting Frequency** (`meeting_frequency_satisfaction`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the frequency of meetings with your advisor.
*   **Quality of Advice Received** (`advice_quality_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of advice and guidance received from your advisor.
*   **Effectiveness of Communication** (`communication_effectiveness`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of communication with your advisor.
*   **Key Skills Developed Through the Program** (`skills_developed`, select_multiple, required): Select the skills you have developed or improved through this advisory program.
	+   Leadership
	+   Strategic thinking
	+   Communication
	+   Problem-solving
	+   Networking
	+   Financial management
	+   Project management
	+   Industry knowledge
	+   Decision-making
	+   Confidence
*   **Measurable Outcomes Achieved** (`measurable_outcomes`, text, required): Describe the measurable outcomes you have achieved through the program.
*   **Overall Program Recommendation Rating** (`program_recommendation_rating`, number, required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend this advisory program to others (e.g., 1-5, where 1 is not likely and 5 is very likely).
*   **Suggestions for Program Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the advisory program.
*   **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback about the advisory program.
