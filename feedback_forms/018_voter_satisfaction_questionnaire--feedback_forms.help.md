# Voter Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voter Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback on your experience with the election process. It aims to improve future elections by understanding your satisfaction and any issues you may have encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the sections using the next and previous buttons. Fill in your contact information, select your satisfaction level, and add any comments you may have. Make sure to review each field carefully before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`voter_satisfaction_questionnaire`, text, required: false): This is the main section where you can provide any comments or suggestions about your experience with the election process.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if we need to follow up on your comments or provide additional information.
* **Voting Date** (`vote_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you voted.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): This is your phone number. Please enter it if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required: false): Choose how satisfied you are with the election process using the options provided.
	+ Option 1: Very Satisfied
	+ Option 2: Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Option 3: Not Satisfied
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Share any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with the election process.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address so we can contact you if needed.
* **City** (`contact_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city where you reside.
* **Province** (`contact_province`, text, required: false): Enter the province or region where you reside.
* **Postal Code** (`contact_postal_code`, text, required: false): Enter your postal code or zip code.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully before proceeding.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- Your feedback is voluntary and will be kept confidential.
- Your contact information will only be used to follow up on your comments or provide additional information.
