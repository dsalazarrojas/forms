# Product Upgrade Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the product upgrade. Your input will help us understand what worked and what didn't in the latest release, and guide our future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have. Please note that some fields are not required, but are optional and can help us better understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about this release?** (`user_input_1`, text, required/optional)
This is the main question where you can express your thoughts and opinions about the latest release. Be as brief or detailed as you like.
* **Was the update useful?** (`user_input_2`, select_multiple, required/optional)
Choose from "Option A", "Option B", or "Option C" to express how useful you found the update. Please select all that apply.
* **What do you think about the new feature?** (`user_input_3`, select_one, required/optional)
Select one option from "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3" to express your thoughts about the new feature.
* **Did you experience any issues?** (`user_input_4`, select_multiple, required/optional)
Choose from "Option A", "Option B", or "Option C" if you experienced any issues during the update. Please select all that apply.
* **How would you rate the overall experience?** (`user_input_5`, number, required/optional)
Rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **What would you like to see in future updates?** (`user_input_6`, text, required/optional)
This is the place to suggest new features or improvements you'd like to see in future updates.
* **Can you provide a feedback or comment?** (`user_input_7`, text, required/optional)
Share any additional thoughts or comments you have about your experience.
* **Do you have any other comments or questions?** (`user_input_8`, text, required/optional)
This is another opportunity to provide any additional thoughts or questions you may have.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to add?** (`user_input_9`, text, required/optional)
Share any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **How likely are you to recommend this product?** (`user_input_10`, number, required/optional)
Rate how likely you are to recommend this product, from 1-5, where 1 is not at all likely and 5 is very likely.
* **Is the product easy to use?** (`user_input_11`, select_one, required/optional)
Choose "True", "False", or "Undecided" to express how easy or difficult you found the product to use.
* **How does this compare to what you expected?** (`user_input_12`, select_multiple, required/optional)
Choose from "Much better", "Slightly better", "About the same", "Slightly worse", or "Much worse" to express how the product meets your expectations.
* **What's most important for us to improve on?** (`user_input_13`, text, required/optional)
Suggest areas where you think we can improve the most.
* **Do you have a Product ID?** (`user_input_14`, text, required/optional)
If you have a Product ID, please provide it so we can associate your feedback with your product.
* **Product Version?** (`user_input_15`, text, required/optional)
If you know the version of the product you are using, please let us know.
* **Operating System?** (`user_input_16`, text, required/optional)
Let us know which operating system you are using.
* **What is your job title or role?** (`user_input_17`, text, required/optional)
Please share your job title or role so we can better understand your perspective.
* **What is your job category?** (`user_input_18`, text, required/optional)
Share your job category so we can better understand your work context.
* **How many employees does your organization have?** (`user_input_19`, number, required/optional)
Provide the number of employees in your organization.
* **What is your company size?** (`user_input_20`, number, required/optional)
Share your company size so we can better understand your work environment.
* **How many users does your organization have?** (`user_input_21`, number, required/optional)
Provide the number of users in your organization.
* **Can we contact you for follow-up questions?** (`user_input_22`, select_one, required/optional)
Choose "True" or "False" to indicate if you are available for follow-up questions.
* **Can you share your contact information?** (`user_input_23`, select_one, required/optional)
Choose "True" or "False" to indicate if you are willing to share your contact information.
* **Can we contact your team lead?** (`user_input_24`, select_one, required/optional)
Choose "True" or "False" to indicate if you are willing to share the contact information of your team lead.
* **Do you want to be entered in a drawing for a prize?** (`user_input_25`, select_one, required/optional)
Choose "True" or "False" to indicate if you are interested in participating in a prize drawing.

**Tips**

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* You can provide multiple answers for most fields by selecting multiple options.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Can you provide a feedback or comment?" field.
* If you are willing to be contacted for follow-up questions, please provide your contact information in the "Can you share your contact information?" field.
