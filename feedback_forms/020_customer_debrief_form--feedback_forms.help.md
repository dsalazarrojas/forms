# Customer Debrief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Debrief Form is a tool used to gather feedback from customers after visiting our office. This feedback is essential for us to understand your thoughts and suggestions on our services, allowing us to make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, especially the required fields.
2. Ensure that you answer each question honestly and provide as much detail as possible in the text areas.
3. If you're not sure about a particular field, please leave it blank and move on to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the client you're providing feedback on.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit to our office.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Tell us what you liked about your experience and what we did well.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Share any suggestions you have on how we could improve next time.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method you would like to be contacted for follow-up (Mobile, Email, or Phone).
* **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more contact methods you're willing to be contacted through (Email, Phone, Mobile).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): This field is optional and used for any further comments or thoughts.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the client you're providing feedback on.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the client you're providing feedback on.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any comments or observations from the staff member who interacted with the client.
* **Internal Notes** (`internal_notes`, `text`, not required): This field is optional and only visible to your colleagues, use it for any internal notes that might be useful to our team.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, not required): Enter any next steps or actions that the client or we as a team should take.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any comments or observations that might be useful for future reference.
