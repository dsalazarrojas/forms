# Psychology Expert Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Psychology Expert Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their sessions with psychologists and therapists. This form helps improve the quality of services provided by our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisks (\*) to ensure that you provide all necessary information.
2. Enter your thoughts and comments in the designated text or note fields.
3. Ensure you submit the completed form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Feedback** (1): This is a text field where you can enter any comments or concerns you had during the session. Please be as detailed as possible to help our team understand your experience.
* **Therapist Name** (2): Enter the name of the psychologist or therapist who conducted the session.
* **Therapist Email** (3): Enter the email address of the psychologist or therapist who conducted the session. This is not required to be completed for every session.
* **Psychologist Name** (4): Enter the name of the psychologist who conducted the session.
* **Psychologist Email** (5): Enter the email address of the psychologist who conducted the session. This is not required to be completed for every session.
* **Session Date** (6): Enter the date of the session in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Session Start Time** (7): Enter the start time of the session in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Session Duration** (8): Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **Session Topics Discussed** (9): Provide details on what topics were covered during the session.
* **Client Feedback 2** (10): This is another text field for any additional comments or feedback on the session.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly to help us improve our services.
