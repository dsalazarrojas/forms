# worker_feedback_summary_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "worker_feedback_summary_request_form" is a form used to collect feedback from workers in a workplace. This form is designed to gather information about a specific job, including job title, job type, job duration, and more. The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive summary of job details to help improve the workplace experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the job title, which should include a concise description of the job, such as a manager, engineer, or administrative role.
2. Next, select the job category from the dropdown menu, which should match the industry or department you work in (e.g., "Feedback Forms" for IT or "Other" for miscellaneous tasks).
3. You can also provide an optional "category other" description if your job doesn't fit into the standard categories.
4. Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates or further questions.
5. Fill in your phone number, which should be a valid phone number where you can be reached during work hours.
6. Enter your current or past employer's name as it appears on your business cards or other professional documentation.
7. Select or type in the job type, which should reflect your job responsibilities or duties.
8. Enter the department or team you belong to.
9. Select the start and end dates of the job, which should match your contract or employment period.
10. Enter the start and end times of the job, which should reflect your typical work hours.
11. Fill in the job duration in a format that makes sense to you (e.g., hours, days, weeks, months).
12. Provide any comments or feedback about your job, such as positive or negative experiences.
13. Select a rating from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience with the job.
14. Provide a brief explanation for your rating.
15. You may optionally submit any suggestions or recommendations for improving the workplace.
16. Finally, submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.
