# e_book_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-book feedback survey is designed to collect feedback from users who have read the e-book. This feedback is used to improve the content, structure, and overall quality of the e-book and its future editions. By providing your feedback, you are helping us to create a better experience for readers like you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name**.
2. Enter your **Email Address**.
3. Provide your **Feedback** about the e-book.
4. Select your **Rating** (if applicable).
5. Choose if you would **Recommend** this e-book.
6. Describe how you found the e-book **Engaging**, **Boring**, **Confusing**, **Clear**, or **Too Complex**/Too Simple**.
7. Select the types of content you would **Like to See** in future editions.
8. Choose what you would like to do next:
	* **Read more from this author**.
	* **Read a different book**.
	* **Read another book from the same author**.
	* **Read no books**.
	* **None of the above**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Your name as it appears on your identity card or document. This is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't want to share it.
* **Email Address** (email): The email address where you can be reached for further discussions or questions. This is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't want to share it.
* **Feedback** (text): Share your thoughts about the e-book. What did you like or dislike about it? What would you change or improve? Be as specific as possible.
* **Rating** (number): Rate your overall experience with the e-book. Please use a numerical value (e.g., 1-5) or a descriptive value (e.g., excellent, average, poor).
* **Would Recommend** (select one): Choose whether you would recommend this e-book to others or not.
* **How Did You Find It** (select multiple): Select all that apply from the list:
	+ **Engaging**: The e-book kept you interested and motivated.
	+ **Boring**: The e-book did not keep you interested.
	+ **Confusing**: The e-book was hard to understand.
	+ **Clear**: The e-book was easy to understand.
	+ **Too Complex**: The e-book was overly complicated.
	+ **Too Simple**: The e-book was too basic.
* **Would Like to See** (select multiple): Select all that apply from the list:
	+ **More**: More content like this in future editions.
	+ **More but Different**: More content like this, but with some changes or improvements.
	+ **Less**: Less content like this in future editions.
	+ **No Preference**: You are neutral about what you would like to see.
* **Next Steps** (select one): Choose what you would like to do next:
	+ **Read more from this author**: Explore more content from the same author.
	+ **Read a different book**: Try a different book or content.
	+ **Read another book from same author**: Read another book from the same author.
	+ **Read no books**: Do not read any more books.
	+ **None of the above**: Other options not listed here.
