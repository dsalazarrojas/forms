# Complaints, Compliments And Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to provide feedback, compliments, or complaints to the organization. It helps us understand your experience and concerns, allowing us to address any issues and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your personal details, including your **Full Name** and **Email Address**. This information will be used to contact you if needed.
2. Select the **Nature of your feedback** (Complaint, Compliment, Suggestion, or General Inquiry) and choose the **Department Involved** if applicable.
3. If you're providing feedback about a specific experience, please enter the **Date of Experience**.
4. Provide as much detail as possible in the **Your Message** field, so we can understand your concern or compliment.
5. Rate your **Overall Satisfaction** with our service (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
6. Indicate if you'd like a **Follow-up regarding this feedback**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name to ensure we know who you are and can contact you.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address to receive any follow-up from us.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, but this is not required.
* **Feedback Type**: Select the type of feedback you're providing (Complaint, Compliment, Suggestion, or General Inquiry).
* **Department Involved**: If your feedback concerns a specific department, choose the relevant one.
* **Date of Experience**: If you're providing feedback about an experience, enter the date it took place.
* **Your Message**: Provide as much detail as possible about your feedback.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with our service.
* **Follow-up Requested**: Indicate if you'd like us to contact you after submitting this form.

Tips:
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can address your feedback effectively. If you have any concerns or compliments, please do provide as much detail as possible.
