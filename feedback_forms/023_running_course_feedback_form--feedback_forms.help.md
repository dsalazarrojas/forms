# running_course_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "running_course_feedback_form" is designed to collect feedback from course participants on their experience with the running course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill it out completely.
2. Choose the relevant options for each question that best describe your experience.
3. Provide accurate and detailed comments in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your name and email (optional) so we can contact you for follow-up discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form for Coaches** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a brief introduction to the feedback form, explaining its purpose.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you rate your overall experience with the course as "Good" or "Fair".
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or both of "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" to express your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you received the training program, "No" otherwise.
* **Coaching Support** (`coaching_support`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you received coaching support, "No" otherwise.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you received effective communication from the trainers, "No" otherwise.
* **Training Environment** (`training_environment`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you had an adequate training environment, "No" otherwise.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you had a positive overall impression of the course, "No" otherwise.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have any recommendations for the course, "No" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the course.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name so we can address any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if you received the training program, "No" otherwise.
* **Coaching Support** (`coaching_support`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if you received coaching support, "No" otherwise.
