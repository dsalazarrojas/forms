# Vacation Rental Exit Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Exit Offboarding Form is used to document the status of a vacation rental exit. This form should only be used when a vacation rental is exiting the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information at the time of exit.
2. Review the fields carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check Out** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form`): This field is the title of the form.
* **Check Out Date** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_check_out_date`): This field is used to select the date of the vacation rental's check out.
* **Check Out Time** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_check_out_time`): This field is used to select the time of the vacation rental's check out.
* **Check Out Time Zone** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_check_out_time_zone`): This field is used to indicate if the vacation rental's check out time zone is different from the default time zone.
* **Location** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_location`): This field is used to capture the location of the vacation rental's check out.
* **Note** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_note`): This field is used for additional information or comments about the vacation rental's exit.
* **Check Out Status** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_check_out_status`): Select all that apply. This field is used to track the status of the vacation rental's exit (Active, Inactive).
* **Check Out Comment** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_check_out_comment`): This field is used to capture any comments or notes related to the vacation rental's exit.
* **Additional Comments** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_additional_comments`): This field is used to capture any additional comments or information related to the vacation rental's exit.
* **Check Out Status** (`vacation_rental_exit_offboarding_form_check_out_status`): This field is used to select the status of the vacation rental's exit (Active, Inactive).

Note: Please review the fields carefully to ensure accuracy, and submit the form once complete.
