# Eco Retreat Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants of the Eco Retreat. Completing this form will help us understand your experience and make it better for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the **Participant Name** field.
2. Provide your **Email Address** in the **Email Address** field.
3. Rate your experience in the **Rating** field. You can choose a rating from 1 to 5.
4. Enter the **Date of Experience** when you attended the retreat.
5. Specify the **Duration of Stay** in days.
6. If you have any additional **Feedback**, please provide it in the **Feedback** field.
7. Finally, add any extra **Comments** you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Participants (id: 1)**: Please enter the number of participants in your group.
- **Participant Name (id: 2)**: Enter your name as you wish to be referred to in our correspondence.
- **Email Address (id: 3)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
- **Feedback (id: 4)**: Provide any comments or suggestions about your experience.
	+ This field is optional, and your feedback will help us improve.
- **Rating (id: 5)**: Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5.
- **Date of Experience (id: 6)**: Enter the date you attended the retreat.
- **Duration of Stay (id: 7)**: Enter the number of days you stayed at the retreat.
- **Comments (id: 8)**: Add any extra comments or suggestions you have about your experience.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to help us improve future retreats.
- If you have already provided feedback, please let us know so we can avoid duplicates.
