# Whiskey Tasting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about whiskey tastings, allowing you to evaluate and refine your whiskey products. It's not a mandatory form, and you can complete it at your own pace. Please answer the questions based on your personal experience and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through its sections and fill out each field as instructed. You can go back to any section and edit your answers at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Aroma**: Select the type of aroma you detected in the whiskey. You can choose from the following options: Bourbon, Fruity, Floral, and Spicy.
* **Flavor**: Choose all the flavors you noticed in the whiskey. You can select multiple options from: Fruity, Vanilla, and Oaky.
* **Appearance**: If you're asked to provide a date, please enter the date of your whiskey tasting experience.
* **Finish**: If you're asked to provide a finish time, please enter the length of time the whiskey's flavors lasted in your mouth.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate the whiskey on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and based on your personal experience.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, feel free to include them in the "intro" section.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, don't hesitate to ask for help.
