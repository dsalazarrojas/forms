# Logistics Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the logistics services provided by our company. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully to understand what information it requires.
2. Rate your experience with the logistics service using the provided options.
3. Provide a brief explanation for your rating, if necessary.
4. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Service rating** (Optional)
* **Service rating** (`number`, Optional): This field allows you to rate your experience with the logistics service. Please select a number between 1 (poor) and 5 (excellent).

### 2. **Why did you rate the service as it did?** (Optional)
* **Why did you rate the service as it did?** (`text`, Optional): This field is an opportunity to provide a brief explanation for your rating. If you rated the service high, you can describe what you liked about it. If you rated it low, you can explain what issues you encountered.

### 3. **Was the communication with the delivery person clear and helpful?** (Optional)
* **Was the communication with the delivery person clear and helpful?** (`select_one`, Optional): This field asks about the effectiveness of communication with the delivery person. Select 'True' if the communication was clear and helpful, 'False' if it was not, or 'Not Sure' if you're not sure.

### 4. **How fast was the delivery?** (Optional)
* **How fast was the delivery?** (`select_one`, Optional): This field asks about the speed of the delivery. Select 'Fast' if the delivery was quick, 'Average' if it was standard, or 'Slow' if it took longer than expected.

### 5. **Was the packaging of the delivered product good?** (Optional)
* **Was the packaging of the delivered product good?** (`select_multiple`, Optional): This field asks about the condition and quality of the packaging. Select all options that apply (e.g., 'True', 'True', 'True').

### 6. **Did you experience any issues with customer support?** (Optional)
* **Did you experience any issues with customer support?** (`select_one`, Optional): This field asks about any difficulties you encountered with customer support. Select 'True' if you experienced issues, 'False' if you didn't, or 'Not Sure' if you're not sure.

### 7. **Was the product as expected?** (Optional)
* **Was the product as expected?** (`select_one`, Optional): This field asks about the quality of the product received. Select 'True' if it matched your expectations, 'False' if it didn't, or 'Not Sure' if you're not sure.

### 8. **What was your overall experience?** (Required)
* **What was your overall experience?** (`select_one`, Required): This field asks about your overall impression of the logistics service. Select one of the provided options: 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.

### 9. **Email** (Optional)
* **Email** (`email`, Optional): This field is for your contact information, in case we need to follow up with you.

### 10. **Phone Number** (Optional)
* **Phone Number** (`text`, Optional): This field is for your contact information, if you prefer to communicate over phone.

### 11. **Overall experience** (Optional)
* **What was your overall experience?** (`select_one`, Optional): This field is a duplicate field from 8. If you've already answered that question, please skip this field.
