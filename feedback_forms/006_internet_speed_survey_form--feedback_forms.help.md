# Internet Speed Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your internet speed experience at your location, so we can better serve you and improve our internet services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields: Place, Internet Service Provider, Download Speed, Upload Speed, Router Type, and Place (if applicable).
2. Select the relevant options for the select fields.
3. Enter the speeds accurately in the number fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Place (1):** Enter the name of the place where you take your internet speed measurements, such as your home address or workplace.
* **Internet Service Provider (2):** Choose the name of the internet service provider you are using, such as Comcast or AT&T.
* **Download Speed (3):** Enter the download speed (in Mbps) of your current internet connection.
* **Upload Speed (4):** Enter the upload speed (in Mbps) of your current internet connection.
* **Router Type (5):** Choose all that apply from the options listed, such as "Wi-Fi", "Ethernet", or "Modem".
* **Place (6):** If you have multiple locations, select "Yes" if you would like to report on multiple places. If you don't want to report on multiple places, select "No".
* **Internet Service Provider (7):** Choose "Yes" if you have your own internet service provider, "No" otherwise.
* **Internet Connection Speed (8):** Enter the speed of your current internet connection, if you have one (in Mbps). If you don't know, you can leave this field blank.
* **Connection Speed (9):** This field is similar to Connection Speed 8, but has a different label to avoid confusion with the field above.
* **Router (10):** Choose all that apply from the options listed, such as "Yes" for having a router or "No" for not having one.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or contact us for guidance.
* This form should only be filled out once per location, to help us better understand your internet speed experience.
