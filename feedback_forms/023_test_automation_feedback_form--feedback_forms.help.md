# test_automation_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and comments from users about their experience using a specific set of automated testing processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and ensure you understand what the form is asking you to comment on.
2. Read each field carefully and provide detailed thoughts and opinions.
3. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much information as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`test_automation_feedback_form_page_1`, text, required: false): This is the main feedback section where you can provide any comments or thoughts about your experience with the test automation process. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **User Experience** (`test_automation_feedback_form_page_2`, text, required: false): In this field, you can describe your overall experience with the automated testing processes, including any positive or negative aspects of the experience.
* **Testing Processes** (`test_automation_feedback_form_page_3`, text, required: false): Here, you can provide feedback on the testing processes, including any improvements or suggestions you may have.
* **Quality Assurance Strategies** (`test_automation_feedback_form_page_4`, text, required: false): In this field, you can discuss any strategies or approaches that you think could be used to improve the quality assurance of the testing process. 

## Tips
