# corporate_training_discharge_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their experience with corporate training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
2. For the "training_effectiveness" field, select one of the four options:
	* Very good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
3. In the "future_programs" field, describe the types of programs you would like to see in the future.
4. For the "data_collection" field, select all that apply:
	* True
	* False
5. In the "session_insights" field, provide any additional insights or comments about your experience with the training session.
6. Enter the date of the training session in the "date" field.
7. Enter the time of the training session in the hours and minutes format in the "time" field.
8. Select your department from the options:
	* HR
	* Sales
	* Support
	* Marketing
9. Select the person responsible for your training session from the options:
	* John
	* Jane
	* Jim

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **training_effectiveness** (Select One):
	* A measure of how effective you found the training session to be.
* **future_programs** (Text):
	* Describe the types of programs you would like to see in the future.
* **data_collection** (Select Multiple):
	* Whether or not you are comfortable with data collection during the training session.
* **session_insights** (Note):
	* Any additional insights or comments about your experience.
* **date** (Date):
	* The date of the training session.
* **time** (Time):
	* The time of the training session in hours and minutes.
* **department** (Select One):
	* Your department.
* **assigned_to** (Select One):
	* The person responsible for your training session.
