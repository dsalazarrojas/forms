# 30 Day Review Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 30 Day Review Feedback Form is a tool to gather feedback from new hires to understand their experience and perceptions during their first month in the company. The insights gained from this form will be used to refine our onboarding process, ensuring it is effective and enjoyable for all future employees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out your employee details such as full name, email address, and start date.
2. Answer the questions regarding your satisfaction, overall rating, and feedback on the onboarding process.
3. Indicate how often you engage with core team processes, select all the best aspects of the onboarding and first month, and mention any areas for improvement.
4. Evaluate the support from your manager and the clarity of goals for the next 60 days.
5. Specify any additional training or resources needed, and whether you would recommend the company/team to new hires.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions to help us improve the onboarding process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name (first and last name) as per the company's records.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your work email address as per the company's records.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about this feedback, please provide your phone number, including the country code.
* **Start date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you began your role.
* **Role / job title** (`role_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title.
* **How satisfied are you with your first 30 days?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your first 30 days from the following options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Overall rating (1-10)** (`overall_rating`, integer, required): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1 to 10, where 1 is the worst and 10 is the best.
* **How often do you engage with core team processes?** (`usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you interact with core processes from the following options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely, First time.
* **Best aspects of the onboarding and first month (select all that apply)** (`best_aspects`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the best aspects of the onboarding and first month from the list provided.
* **What would you improve about the onboarding or first month?** (`areas_to_improve`, text, optional): Be specific about the processes, tools, or communications that could be improved.
* **Manager support and feedback quality** (`manager_support`, select_one, required): Evaluate the support from your manager from the following options: Excellent, Good, Adequate, Poor, Not applicable.
* **Clarity of goals for the next 60 days** (`goals_understanding`, select_one, required): Choose how clear your goals for the next 60 days are from the following options: Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Not set.
* **Additional training or resources needed** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas where more support would be beneficial from the list provided.
* **Would you recommend this company/team to new hires?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend the company/team from the following options: Definitely, Probably, Not sure, Probably not, False.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other observations, concerns, or praise you have about the onboarding process.
