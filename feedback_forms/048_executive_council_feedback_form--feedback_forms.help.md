# Executive Council Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Council Feedback Form is a tool for attendees to share their thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the executive council meetings. This form allows them to provide feedback on meeting satisfaction, initiative suggestions, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name, email, and organization.
2.  Indicate if you attended the meeting and select the date you attended (if applicable).
3.  Provide your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
4.  Share any suggestions or ideas for future initiatives.
5.  Share any other comments or feedback about the meeting.
6.  Select the submit option to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your official email address where you can be reached.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter your current organization or department.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter your department name if you are affiliated with an organization. 
* **Meeting Attended** (`meeting_attended`, `select_one`, required: false): Check if you attended the meeting.
* **Meeting Attended Other** (`meeting_attended_other`, `text`, required: false): If you select "Meeting Attended" as "False" above, enter the date you actually attended the meeting.
* **Initiative Suggestion** (`initiative_suggestion`, `text`, required: false): Share any suggestions or ideas for future initiatives.
* **Meeting Satisfaction** (`meeting_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Provide your satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1-5.
* **Meeting Satisfaction Other 2** (`meeting_satisfaction_other_2`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments on meeting satisfaction.
