# counseling_method_preferences_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on your preferences for counseling methods. Your responses will help us understand how you would like to approach counseling and what methods you prefer for our sessions. This will aid us in tailoring our services to your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reading through the form carefully and ensuring you understand each question.
2. Select the answers that best fit your preferences for each question.
3. Fill in any additional notes you'd like to provide.
4. Click submit to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide any background information or context about your preferences.
* **Approach to Counseling**: Here, you can select the approach to counseling that resonates with you the most. Options include:
	+ Solution Focused: This approach focuses on finding solutions and ways to achieve your goals.
	+ Cognitive Behavioral: This approach helps you become aware of your thoughts, feelings, and actions, and how they impact your life.
	+ Mindfulness: This approach combines cognitive and behavioral techniques to increase your awareness and presence.
* **Frequency of Sessions**: Please select all the frequencies that appeal to you. Options include:
	+ Daily: Meeting with you daily.
	+ Weekly: Meeting with you weekly.
	+ Monthly: Meeting with you monthly.
* **Time of Day**: Please enter your preferred time of day for our sessions. You can use the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 12:00).
* **Preferred Length of Sessions**: This field is for entering the length of time you prefer for our sessions.
* **Preferred Location**: Here, you can select the location where you would like to receive counseling.
	+ In-person: Meeting in person at a designated location.
	+ Phone: Meeting over the phone.
	+ Online: Meeting remotely using a video conferencing platform.
* **Communication Style**: Please select all the communication styles that you'd prefer for our sessions.
	+ Formal: Direct and professional communication.
	+ Informal: Friendly and casual communication.
	+ Neutral: Balanced between formal and informal.
* **Session Frequency**: Please select all the frequencies that you prefer for our sessions.
	+ One-time: A single session.
	+ Multiple: Multiple sessions over time.
	+ Continuous: Continuous support over a long period.
* **Preferred Method**: Here, you can select all the methods for receiving counseling.
	+ Online chat: Messaging through an online platform.
	+ Email: Messaging through email.
	+ Phone call: Talking over the phone.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional notes or information about your preferences and needs are welcome here.
