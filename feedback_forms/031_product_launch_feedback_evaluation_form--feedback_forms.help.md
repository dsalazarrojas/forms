# Product Launch Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Launch Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to collect qualitative and quantitative feedback from users on a product launch. The form aims to gauge the user's likelihood of success, identify areas for improvement, and gather feedback on new features and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide an honest assessment of the product launch, answering each question to the best of your ability.
2. If applicable, select all options that apply to your feedback, such as "Very likely" or "Feature 1" under "What feature would you like to see improved the most?".
3. Enter your email address and phone number, which will allow us to contact you for follow-up discussions, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How likely do you think the product launch will be a success?** (`product_launch_feedback_form_page_2`, Select One, Optional): This question aims to gauge your overall confidence in the product's success. Please select "Very likely", "Somewhat likely", or "Not likely at all" based on your assessment.
* **What feature would you like to see improved the most?** (`product_launch_feedback_form_page_3`, Select Multiple, Optional): This question allows you to specify which feature(s) you think needs improvement. Select all that apply, such as "Feature 1", "Feature 2", or "Feature 3".
* **What features would you like to see added?** (`product_launch_feedback_form_page_4`, Select Multiple, Optional): Similar to the previous question, this one allows you to suggest new features you'd like to see added to the product.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, Email, Optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up discussions or have any questions.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, Text, Optional): This is an optional field to provide your phone number for future communication.
* **Any other comments or feedback?** (`product_launch_feedback_form_page_5`, Note, Optional): This is an open-ended question to gather any additional comments or feedback you may have.
