# Negative Feedback Mechanism Explanation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect and track feedback from customers, team members, or other stakeholders. It is intended to ensure that feedback is addressed promptly and effectively, and that any issues are resolved in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Negative Feedback" field with a brief description of the feedback received.
2. Identify the "Process Owner" who is responsible for addressing the feedback.
3. Provide a detailed "Description of Negative Feedback" to help understand the issue.
4. Specify the "Root Cause of Negative Feedback" to pinpoint the underlying problem.
5. Select the "Feedback Recipient" who will be contacted regarding the feedback.
6. Choose the "Feedback Frequency" at which feedback is received (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
7. Select the "Feedback Channel" used to communicate feedback (e.g., Email, Phone, In-person, Other).
8. Enter the "Feedback Response Time" for the process owner to respond to feedback.
9. Enter the "Positive Feedback Ratio", "Negative Feedback Ratio", and "Neutral Feedback Ratio" to track the feedback balance.
10. Enter the "Overall Satisfaction" to gauge customer satisfaction with the feedback resolution.
11. Enter the "Average Response Time" to track the time taken to respond to feedback.
12. Specify the "Feedback Resolution" status (e.g., Resolved, Not Resolved, Pending).
13. Provide a "Root Cause of Resolution" to identify the underlying cause of the feedback resolution.
14. Describe the "Improvement Action Taken" to address the feedback.
15. Specify the "Follow-up Actions" taken to ensure feedback is addressed.
16. Choose the "Follow-up Frequency" at which follow-up actions are taken (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
17. Select the "Follow-up Channel" used for follow-up actions (e.g., Email, Phone, In-person, Other).
18. Enter the "Follow-up Response Time" for follow-up actions.
19. Enter the "Overall Satisfaction Follow-up" to gauge customer satisfaction with follow-up actions.
20. Enter the "Average Follow-up Time" to track the time taken for follow-up actions.
21. Identify the "Root Cause of Backlog" to understand the underlying cause of any backlog.
22. Describe the "Improvement Action Taken Follow-up" to address any issues.
