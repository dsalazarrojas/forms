# Donor Retention Resource Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Donor Retention Resource Request Form is designed to facilitate communication between non-profit staff and volunteers who need to request resources and support from donors, helping to foster stronger relationships and increase donor retention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Enter your nonprofit's name in the "Nonprofit Name" field.
3. Provide a clear and concise request for resources in the "Resource Request" field.
4. Leave "Feedback" blank unless you have specific feedback regarding the request.
5. Enter your email address and phone number, if applicable, to facilitate follow-up.
6. Select the date and time you would like the request to be reviewed.
7. Choose who you would like the request to be submitted to (Donor Engagement Team, Donor Retention Team, or Nonprofit).
8. Add any additional comments that will help the review team understand your request.
9. Finally, select the team you would like to receive your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nonprofit Name** (`Nonprofit Name`, text, **required**): Enter your non-profit's name, as this will help us direct your request to the correct team.
* **Resource Request** (`Resource Request`, text, **not required**): Please provide a clear and concise description of the resource or support you need.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, **optional**): If you have any feedback regarding the requested resource or support, please share it here.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address, if you would like to be contacted regarding your request.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted via phone regarding your request.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, **optional**): Select the date you would like the request to be reviewed.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, **optional**): Select the time you would like the request to be reviewed.
* **Submit To** (`Submit To`, select_one, **required**): Choose who you would like to receive your request (Donor Engagement Team, Donor Retention Team, or Nonprofit).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, note, **optional**): Provide any additional context or details that will help the review team understand your request.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, select_multiple, **not required**): Select which team(s) you would like to receive your request.

Note: Some fields are not required but are optional to help us better understand your needs and follow up with you more efficiently. Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
