# favorite_dishes_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The favorite_dishes_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback about favorite dishes. It's intended to help us understand what you enjoy eating and what aspects of the dishes make them appealing to you.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, please follow these steps:
1. Choose a restaurant where you recently had a favorite dish.
2. Rate the dish using the provided rating system (e.g., Good, Fair).
3. Provide additional context or notes about the dish, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **restaurant_feedback (Text)**: Enter the name of the restaurant where you had your favorite dish. This will help us identify the specific restaurant you're referring to.
* **dish_rating (Number)**: Select the rating for the dish from the provided options (Good, Fair). This will help us gauge your overall satisfaction with the dish.
* **dish_rating_2 (Select One)**: Choose whether the dish was Good or Fair, as per your experience.
* **dish_rating_3 (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more of the options that apply to your experience with the dish (Good, Fair).
* **dish_rating_4 (Date)**: Enter the date you had the dish, if you recall it.
* **dish_rating_5 (Time)**: Enter the time you had the dish, if you recall it.
* **dish_rating_6 (Note)**: Provide any additional notes about the dish, if needed.
* **dish_rating_7 (Email)**: Enter your email address if you want to be notified about any updates or follow-ups on your feedback.
* **dish_rating_8 (Text)**: Provide any other relevant information about the dish.
* **dish_rating_9 - dish_rating_23 (Text)**: These fields are intended for additional notes or information about the dish. You can enter as much or as little as you need to provide context.

Remember, the more specific and detailed your feedback, the more valuable it will be to us. Thank you for taking the time to share your experience with your favorite dishes!
