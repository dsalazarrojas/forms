# Music Lesson Feedback Poll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and suggestions from students after a music lesson, allowing the instructor to assess their understanding of the material, gauge the effectiveness of the session, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your thoughts on what you learned in this session, using the text box on the first page.
2. Rate your session by selecting one of the options in the second page.
3. If you have any further comments or suggestions, use the text boxes provided on pages three and four.
4. Let us know if you'd like to attend another session with us, and if so, choose a preferred time and day.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or thoughts you may have about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What did you learn in this session?** (music_lesson_feedback): Please use this text box to describe what you felt you learned or gained from this session.
* **How would you rate this session?** (music_lesson_rating): Rate your experience by selecting "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" from the dropdown menu.
* **What did you enjoy most about this session?** (music_lesson_comments): This is a good opportunity to let us know about any aspects of the session that you particularly enjoyed.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (music_lesson_suggestions): If you have any ideas about how we could improve for future sessions, please share them here.
* **Is there anything I could improve on for your next session?** (music_lesson_improve): If there's anything you'd like to see changed or improved for your next session, please let us know here.
* **Would you like to attend another session with me?** (music_lesson_next_session): Please select whether you'd like to attend another session, and if so, choose a preferred time and day.
* **Do you have any other comments?** (music_lesson_comments_2): This is an optional box for any additional thoughts or comments you may have about the session.
* **How would you like to be contacted?** (music_lesson_email): Please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Do you have a phone number I can contact you at?** (music_lesson_phone): This is optional, but if you have a phone number you'd like to share, please enter it here.
* **Any additional comments?** (music_lesson_additional_comments): This is an optional box for any further comments or thoughts you may have about the session.
