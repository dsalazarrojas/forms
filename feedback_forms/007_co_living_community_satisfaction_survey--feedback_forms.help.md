# Co Living Community Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge the overall satisfaction of co-living community residents and identify areas for improvement. Your input will help the community management team understand your experiences and make informed decisions to enhance the community's amenities and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the required information such as your name (if you prefer anonymity, you can leave this field blank), unit/room number, and email address.
*   Answer the questions about your stay in the community, including your move-in date, length of stay, and overall satisfaction.
*   Evaluate the cleanliness, maintenance response, and value for money of the community.
*   Rate the friendliness of staff, quality of amenities, and sense of community.
*   Select the amenities you regularly use from the provided list.
*   Provide feedback on the noise levels and safety in the community.
*   Indicate your participation in social events and suggest events you would like to see.
*   Share any challenges you faced during your stay and what you enjoy most about the community.
*   Finally, state whether you would recommend the community to others and express your likelihood to renew your lease.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Name** (`resident_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name, or leave this field blank if you prefer anonymity.
*   **Unit or Room Number** (`unit_number`, `text`, optional): Provide your unit or room identifier to help the community management team locate your feedback.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up on your feedback.
*   **Move-In Date** (`move_in_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you moved in to the community.
*   **Length of Stay** (`length_of_stay`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you have lived in the community (Less than 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, Over 1 year).
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the co-living experience (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
*   **Cleanliness of Common Areas** (`cleanliness_rating`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the cleanliness of shared spaces (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Maintenance Response** (`maintenance_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the maintenance response (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Community Atmosphere** (`community_atmosphere`, `select_one`, required): Rate the sense of community (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the friendliness of staff (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Rate the value relative to cost (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Amenities Quality** (`amenities_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of amenities provided (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Amenities You Use** (`amenities_used`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the amenities you regularly use (Kitchen, Laundry Facilities, Gym or Fitness Area, Co-Working Space, Lounge Areas, Outdoor Space, Parking, Bike Storage).
*   **Noise Level Satisfaction** (`noise_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with noise levels (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
*   **Safety and Security** (`safety_security`, `select_one`, required): Rate your safety and security in the community (Very Safe, Safe, Neutral, Unsafe, Very Unsafe).
*   **Social Events Participation** (`social_events`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often you participate in social events (Always, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never).
*   **Event Suggestions** (`event_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Suggest events you would like to see in the community.
*   **Biggest Challenge Faced** (`biggest_challenge`, `text`, optional): Share the biggest challenge you faced during your stay.
*   **Best Aspect of Co-Living** (`best_aspect`, `text`, optional): Describe what you enjoy most about the community.
*   **Would You Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): State whether you would recommend the community to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably No, Definitely No).
*   **Likelihood to Renew Lease** (`renew_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your likelihood to renew your lease (Very Likely, Likely, Not Sure, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
*   **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any other comments or suggestions you have.

Note: Please provide specific, honest, and respectful feedback to help the community management team improve the co-living experience.
