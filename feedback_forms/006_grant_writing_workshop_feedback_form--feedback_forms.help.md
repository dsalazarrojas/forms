# Grant Writing Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Grant Writing Workshop Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from attendees on the workshop's content, organization, and materials. Your input will help us improve and refine the workshop to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions in the order they appear on the form.
2. Provide detailed answers to each question.
3. Be as specific as possible with your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is the first page of the form where you can briefly introduce yourself and provide any background information you feel is relevant to your feedback.
* **Grant Writing Experience**: This question asks about your level of experience in grant writing. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
	+ Grant Writing 0-2 years: You have little to no experience in grant writing.
	+ Grant Writing 2-5 years: You have some experience in grant writing.
	+ Grant Writing 5-10 years: You have a good amount of experience in grant writing.
	+ Grant Writing more than 10 years: You have extensive experience in grant writing.
* **Feedback on Workshop Content**: This question asks for your feedback on the workshop's content. Please be specific about what you liked or disliked about the workshop's content.
* **Feedback on Workshop Organization**: This question asks for your feedback on the workshop's organization. Please comment on how well the workshop flowed, if it was well-structured, and if there were any issues with logistics.
* **Feedback on Workshop Materials**: This question asks for your feedback on the materials provided during the workshop. Please comment on their usefulness, relevance, and overall quality.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: If you have any suggestions for improving the workshop, please provide them here.
* **Open-Ended Comments**: This is an open-ended question where you can provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the workshop. This is a great place to add any comments or ideas you didn't mention in the previous questions.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* Be specific and provide examples when possible.
* Your feedback will help us improve the workshop and provide better content for future attendees.
