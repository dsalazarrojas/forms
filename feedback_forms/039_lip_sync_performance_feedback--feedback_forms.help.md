# Lip Sync Performance Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on Lip Sync performances, which are events where a performer synchronizes their movements with a video or audio track.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the performance to rate from the "Performance to Compare" dropdown list.
2. Fill out the performance rating and comments sections.
3. Select the time and date range for the performance.
4. Choose the time of day and performance to email for any follow-up communication.
5. Enter the performance date and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance to Compare (1)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the performance you are rating from the list.
* **Sync Rating (2)** (`number`, optional): Rate the performance's synchronization with the video or audio track.
* **Stage Presence (3)** (`number`, optional): Rate the performer's stage presence.
* **Creativity (4)** (`number`, optional): Rate the performance's creativity.
* **Comments (5)** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the performance.
* **Rating Comments (6)** (`note`, optional): Enter any further comments or feedback on the performance's rating.
* **Overall Rating (7)** (`number`, optional): Rate the overall performance.
* **Performance to Compare (8)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple performances to compare from the list.
* **Email (9)** (`email`, optional): Enter the email address to contact for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone (10)** (`text`, optional): Enter the phone number to contact for any follow-up communication.
* **Email Performance (11)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the performance to email.
* **Performances to Email (12)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple performances to email.
* **Date (13)** (`date`, optional): Select the date of the performance.
* **Time (14)** (`time`, optional): Select the time of the performance.
* **Date Range (15)** (`date`, optional): Select the date range of the performance.
* **Time Range (16)** (`time`, optional): Select the time range of the performance.
* **Time of Day (17)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the time of day for the performance.
* **Performances to Time (18)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple performances to time.
* **Performance Date (19)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the date of the performance.
* **Performances Dates (20)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple performance dates.
* **Date Range (21)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the date range of the performance.
* **Performances Date Ranges (22)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple performance dates.
* **Time Range (23)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the time range of the performance.
* **Performances Time Ranges (24)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose multiple performances time ranges.
* **Performance to Phone (25)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the performance to phone.

Note: All required fields are marked with `required: true`. Please make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form.
