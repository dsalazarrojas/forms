# Seasonal Collection Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This feedback poll is designed to collect opinions on our latest lineup and gather information on what you think about our collection. Your input will help us understand how well we're meeting your expectations and what we can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions on each page according to your honest opinion.
2. Make sure to select all relevant options that apply to you.
3. If you're interested in being contacted for future releases, provide your email address and a message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your impression of our latest lineup?** (`latest_lineup`, text, required): Please share your thoughts about our latest collection. Be as specific as possible.
* **Which product are you most interested in?** (`product_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all products that interest you. You can choose multiple options.
* **What is your overall impression of Product A?** (`product_impression_product_a`, select_one, required): Rate your overall impression of Product A.
* **What is your overall impression of Product B?** (`product_impression_product_b`, select_one, required): Rate your overall impression of Product B.
* **What is your overall impression of Product C?** (`product_impression_product_c`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall impression of Product C, if applicable.
* **How seasonal do you find our collection?** (`seasonality`, select_one, required): Choose how well you think our collection fits the current season.
* **Will you be interested in our future releases?** (`future_release`, select_one, required): Let us know if you'd like to be informed about our upcoming releases.
* **Would you like to be contacted for future releases?** (`contact_info`, select_one, required): If yes, provide your email address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, provide your phone number.
* **Message** (`contact_message`, note, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, leave a message for us to know how to reach out to you.

Note: This form is for feedback purposes only. We value your opinion and will consider it when deciding on future collections.
