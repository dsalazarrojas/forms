# Shoe Purchase Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with purchasing shoes from us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields. These are marked as required in the form.
2. For optional fields, choose any or all of the options that apply to your experience.
3. Be as specific as possible in your answers, so we can better understand your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your purchase from us.
* **Product ID** (`product_id`, `number`, required): If you have the product ID number, please enter it here.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up or future promotions.
* **Purchase Amount** (`purchase_amount`, `number`, optional): If you are willing, please enter the amount you spent on the purchase.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our product. Choose from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): How do you prefer to be contacted for follow-up or promotions? Choose from:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
* **Shoe Size** (`shoe_size`, `select_multiple`, optional): What is your shoe size? Choose from:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
	+ Extra Large
* **Purchase Location** (`purchase_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Where did you make the purchase from? Choose from:
	+ Online
	+ Store
	+ Other
* **Shoe Color** (`shoe_color`, `select_one`, optional): What is your preferred shoe color? Choose from:
	+ Red
	+ Blue
	+ Green
	+ Yellow
* **Purchase Channel** (`purchase_channel`, `select_multiple`, optional): How did you hear about us? Choose from:
	+ Social Media
	+ Friends
	+ Family
	+ Online Ads
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_multiple`, optional): What is your gender? Choose from:
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, `select_multiple`, optional): What is your age range? Choose from:
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55+
* **Purchase Frequency** (`purchase_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): How often do you purchase from us? Choose from:
	+ Never
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
* **Purchase Frequency Other** (`purchase_frequency_other`, `text`, optional): If other, please specify.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, `select_one`, optional): How satisfied are you with your purchase? Choose from:
	+ Extremely Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Extremely Dissatisfied
* **Purchase Purpose** (`purchase_purpose`, `select_multiple`, optional): Why did you make the purchase? Choose from:
	+ Gift
	+ Personal Use
	+ Other
* **Customer Satisfaction Other** (`customer_satisfaction_other`, `select_one`, optional): If other, please specify.
* **Purchase Reason Other** (`purchase_reason_other`, `select_one`, optional): If other, please specify.
* **Shoe Type** (`shoe_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): What type of shoe is it? Choose from:
	+ Sneaker
	+ Boots
	+ Sandal
	+ Other
* **Shoe Style** (`shoe_style`, `select_multiple`, optional): What style of shoe is it? Choose from:
	+ Casual
	+ Dress
	+ Athletic
	+ Other
* **Shoe Material** (`shoe_material`, `select_multiple`, optional): What material is it made of? Choose from:
	+ Leather
	+ Synthetic
	+ Cotton
	+ Other
* **Purchase Reason** (`purchase_reason`, `select_multiple`, optional): Why do you purchase our shoes? Choose from:
	+ Quality
	+ Price
	+ Brand
	+ Other
* **Customer Recommendation** (`customer_recommend`, `select_multiple`, optional): Would you recommend our shoes? Choose from:
	+ True
	+ False
