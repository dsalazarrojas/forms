# Open House Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Open House Feedback Form" is a feedback mechanism to gather information from attendees about their experience with the open house event. We want to know if they think the event was prophetic, what they think of the event's rating, and any additional comments or suggestions they might have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the provided fields with your thoughts and feedback. The form is divided into several pages, each with a few questions related to the event. You can choose whether or not to fill out each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendees Feedback
- **Attendees Feedback** (`attendees_feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can write any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the event. You can write as much or as little as you like, and we welcome all kinds of thoughts and ideas.

### Event Prophetic
- **Event Prophetic** (`event_prophets`, `select_one`, optional): Please select "Yes" if you think the event was prophetic, or "No" if it was not.

### Rating Score
- **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall experience with the event, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.

### Feedback
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is similar to "Attendees Feedback" and is also where you can write any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the event.

## Tips
* Please be as honest and detailed as possible with your feedback.
* You can choose whether or not to fill out each field.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Attendees Feedback" field.
