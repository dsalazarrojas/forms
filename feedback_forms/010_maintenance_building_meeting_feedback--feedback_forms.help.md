# Maintenance Building Meeting Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Maintenance Building Meeting Feedback form is used to collect feedback from users after a maintenance meeting held in a building. The feedback gathered through this form will help the maintenance team to better understand the meeting's outcome, identify areas for improvement, and take necessary actions to address any issues that were raised during the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant Maintenance Team member who was present during the meeting.
2. Enter the date of the meeting.
3. If the meeting was held at a specific time, enter the time.
4. Specify the location of the meeting.
5. Check if there were any attendees, and if so, list their names.
6. Describe any issues that were found during the meeting.
7. Provide an account of the actions taken to address the issues raised during the meeting.
8. Share any suggestions for future improvements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Team** (`maintenance_team`, select_one, required): Select the member of the maintenance team that was present during the meeting. This will help ensure that the correct team member is aware of the feedback and can follow up on it.
* **Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting. This is essential to keep track of the meeting's timeline.
* **Time** (`feedback_time`, time, optional): If the meeting was held at a specific time, enter the time. This will provide additional information about the meeting's schedule.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where the meeting was held. This will help the team to easily identify the site and reference the meeting's location.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required): Check if there were any attendees at the meeting, and if so, list their names. This will ensure that everyone who was present is accounted for and informed.
* **Issues Found** (`issues_found`, text, optional): Describe any issues that were found during the meeting. This will help the team to identify potential problems and address them accordingly.
* **Actions Taken** (`actions_taken`, text, optional): Provide an account of the actions taken to address the issues raised during the meeting. This will help the team to track their progress and follow up on the issues.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions for future improvements. This will allow the team to gather valuable insights from the users and implement them for better performance.
