# Headphone Purchase Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their headphones purchase experience. We value your input and would like to hear about your satisfaction with your purchase, any feedback you may have, and any suggestions you might have for us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and select your responses from the available options. You can skip any fields that are not relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your purchase experience** (`headphone_purchase_feedback`): Rate your overall experience with your headphones purchase. Choose from: Good, Fair.
* **What headphones did you buy?** (`headphones`): Tell us about the headphones you purchased. This will help us understand the specific product you're referring to.
* **Purchase date** (`purchase_date`): Select the date you made the purchase.
* **Satisfaction rating** (`satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with your purchase on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Your contact email** (`contact_email`): If you'd like to be contacted with follow-up questions or to receive updates, please provide your email address.
* **Do you have any feedback for us?** (`feedback`): Share any comments or concerns you might have about your purchase. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`suggestions`): If you have any ideas or suggestions on how we can improve our products or services, please share them with us.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments or details about your experience.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your contact email address.
- Your feedback is valuable to us and will help us improve our products and services.
