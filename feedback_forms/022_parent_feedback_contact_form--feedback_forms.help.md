# Parent Feedback Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents to provide feedback on our school's service. Your input will help us improve our services and better understand your concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please fill in all required fields.
2. Enter your first name (optional but recommended).
3. Provide your email address (we'll use this to contact you if needed).
4. Describe the feedback or concern you want to share with us.
5. Suggest any improvements or additional features you'd like us to consider.
6. Enter your child's name.
7. If applicable, provide the name of the school your child attends.
8. If applicable, enter your contact number.
9. Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`parent_feedback_form`, text, optional): Enter your first name, if you'd like us to know your identity.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, optional): Describe the feedback or concern you want to share with us. Be as specific as possible.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements or additional features you'd like us to consider.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name, this will help us associate the feedback with your child's account.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): If your child attends one of our schools, enter the name of the school.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number so we can call or text you to discuss the feedback.
* **Additional Message** (`message`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share with us.
