# Therapy Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from patients or clients about their therapy sessions. This feedback is used to help therapists improve their practice and provide better services to their clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field. If you have attended a therapy session, please fill out the form with your honest feedback. If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask your therapist for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Therapy Feedback (Yes, No) </label> 
**therapy_feedback_consent_form** (1) (select_multiple, required: false): This field asks if you received therapy feedback during your session. Please select 'Yes' if the therapist provided feedback, and 'No' if not.

### <label> Email </label> 
**email** (2) (email, required: false): This field is for your email address. Please enter your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up feedback or to receive additional information from the therapist.

### <label> Phone </label> 
**phone** (3) (text, required: false): This field is for your phone number. You can enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the therapist for follow-up feedback.

### <label> Name </label> 
**name** (4) (text, required: false): This field is for your name. Please enter your name if you want to provide feedback about your session.

### <label> Session Date </label> 
**session_date** (5) (date, required: false): This field is for the date of your therapy session. Please enter the date of your session if you want to provide feedback about it.

### <label> Session Notes </label> 
**session_notes** (6) (note, required: false): This field is for any additional notes about your session. Please enter any relevant information about your session, such as what you discussed with the therapist.

### <label> Therapist Name </label> 
**therapist_name** (7) (text, required: false): This field is for the name of your therapist. Please enter the name of your therapist if you want to provide feedback about them.

### <label> Session Length </label> 
**session_length** (8) (number, required: false): This field is for the length of your therapy session. Please enter the length of your session in minutes.

### <label> Session Location </label> 
**session_location** (9) (text, required: false): This field is for the location of your therapy session. Please enter the location of your session.

### <label> Rating </label> 
**rating** (10) (select_one, required: false): This field is to rate your session. Please select 'Good' if you were satisfied with your session, and 'Fair' if not.

### <label> Feedback </label> 
**feedback** (11) (select_multiple, required: false): This field is to provide additional feedback about your session. Please select 'Yes' if you received useful feedback, and 'No' if not.

### <label> Comments </label> 
**comments** (12) (text, required: false): This field is for any additional comments you may have about your session. Please enter any relevant information that may help the therapist improve their practice.
