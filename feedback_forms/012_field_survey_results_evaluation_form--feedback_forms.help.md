# Field Survey Results Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the Field Survey results and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each page in order, starting with the Survey Results Page and ending with the Submit Page.
- Ensure that you have selected the correct contact method on the Contact Info Page.
- Enter the date and time of the Field Survey on the Date and Time Pages.
- Enter any additional comments on the Comments Page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Results Page**: Enter and summarize the Field Survey results.
* **Evaluation Page**: Evaluate the Field Survey results.
* **Feedback Page**: Provide feedback about this form, e.g., suggestions for improvement.
* **Contact Info Page**: Select how you would like to contact you regarding this form, either by Phone, Email, or Both.
* **Comments Page**: Enter any additional comments about the Field Survey.
* **Submit Page**: Choose how you would like to proceed with the form, either by Submitting, Saving as a Draft, or Canceling.
* **Date Page**: Enter the Date of the Field Survey.
* **Time Page**: Enter the Time of the Field Survey.

Note: This guide assumes that the form has been properly set up with the required fields and options. If you are unsure about any field or option, please consult the form's administrator.
