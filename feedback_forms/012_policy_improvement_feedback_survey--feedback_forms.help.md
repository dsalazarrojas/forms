# Policy Improvement Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on policy improvement areas, current and proposed processes, and other relevant information. This feedback will help us understand areas where policies can be improved and how processes can be enhanced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form as much or as little as you want, depending on your input.
2. Start by selecting areas where you think policies need improvement.
3. Describe the current process.
4. Propose a new process for improvement.
5. Select areas where you want to see changes.
6. Provide any additional comments.
7. Enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
