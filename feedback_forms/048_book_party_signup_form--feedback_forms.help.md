# Book Party Signup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Book Party Signup Form is designed for guests to sign up for a book-themed party. The information collected through this form will be used to tailor party activities and ensure a enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your name in the "Attendee name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used to keep you updated about the event.
3. Indicate the number of guests that will be attending in the "Number of guests" field.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them in the "Dietary restrictions" field.
5. Choose your preferred book genre from the "Preferred book genre" dropdown menu.
6. If you have any comments or accessibility notes, please include them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`Attendee name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, email, required): Enter your email address for event updates.
* **Number of guests** (`Number of guests`, number, required): Indicate the number of guests that will be attending with you.
* **Dietary restrictions** (`Dietary restrictions`, text, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions, e.g. vegetarian, gluten-free.
* **Preferred book genre** (`Preferred book genre`, select_one, optional): Choose from Fiction, Non fiction, Mystery, Romance, or Sci-fi/Fantasy.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or accessibility notes about your attendance.
