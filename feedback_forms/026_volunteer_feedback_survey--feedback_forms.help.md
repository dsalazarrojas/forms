# Volunteer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Feedback Survey is designed to gather information from volunteers about their experiences, preferences, and availability to help the organization improve its volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and options presented.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to submit your feedback before moving to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1: Welcome** (`page_1`, note, optional)
  This page serves as an introduction to the survey and provides a brief overview of the questions that follow.
- **Page 2: Demographics** (`page_2`, select_multiple, required)
  This page asks about your volunteer type, selecting all that apply. Please choose from:
  • Volunteer Type: General
  • Volunteer Type: Leader
  • Volunteer Type: General
  - **Activity** (`page_3`, date, optional)
    This page asks for the dates you have participated in volunteer activities. Please enter in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Hours Worked** (`page_4`, number, required)
  This page asks for the total number of hours you have worked as a volunteer. Please enter a whole number without any decimal points.
- **Availability** (`page_5`, select_one, optional)
  This page asks about your availability for future volunteer activities. Please choose one of the following options:
  • Morning
  • Afternoon
  • Evening
- **Phone Number** (`page_6`, text, optional)
  This page asks for your phone number, which will help us contact you for future volunteer opportunities.
- **Email** (`page_7`, email, optional)
  This page asks for your email address, which will help us keep you updated on volunteer opportunities and events.
- **Notes** (`page_8`, text, optional)
  This page is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
- **Additional Comments** (`page_9`, text, optional)
  This page is for any further comments or suggestions you'd like to share with us.
- **Page 10** (`page_10`, date, optional)
  This page asks for a date, which is currently blank, but you can enter a date if you'd like.
- **Time** (`page_11`, time, optional)
  This page asks for a time, but it's optional and currently blank.
- **Volunteer Type** (`page_12`, select_one, optional)
  This page asks to select one of the following options:
  • Volunteer Type: General
  • Volunteer Type: Leader
  Please note that this option is optional.
