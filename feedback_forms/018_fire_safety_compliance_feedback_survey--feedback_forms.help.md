# Fire Safety Compliance Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to provide feedback on fire safety compliance in facilities, such as their condition, frequency of drills, safety practices, equipment status, and maintenance history. The collected data will be used to monitor and address potential fire safety risks and improve overall compliance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this survey as a representative of the facility.
- Answer all questions thoroughly, but feel free to leave any fields blank if the information is not applicable or unknown.
- If you're unsure about any question, please contact the relevant team or authority for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility** (text): Enter the name or ID of the facility.
  - This field is used to identify the facility for which you are providing feedback.
- **Equipment Condition** (number): Rate the overall condition of the fire equipment in the facility.
  - This field helps us understand the current state of fire equipment, which is crucial for risk assessment and prioritization.
- **Drill Frequency** (text): Describe the frequency of fire drills conducted at the facility.
  - Provide details on how often the facility conducts fire drills and any notable issues or challenges faced during such exercises.
- **Safety Practices** (select one): Rate the safety practices followed by the facility.
  - Select "Very Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to indicate the level of adherence to fire safety guidelines.
- **Comments** (text): Add any additional comments or concerns about fire safety in the facility.
  - Feel free to provide any information that you think would be helpful in assessing and addressing fire safety risks.
- **Fire Equipment** (number): Report the quantity of fire equipment available in the facility.
  - Enter the number of fire extinguishers, smoke detectors, and other equipment present.
- **Fire Equipment Status** (select multiple): Specify the status of fire equipment in the facility.
  - Choose "Working", "Not working", or "Unknown" for each piece of equipment to help us understand its current condition.
- **Last Inspection Date** (date): Provide the date of the last fire equipment inspection.
  - This helps us track the history of inspections and identify any trends or issues.
- **Next Inspection Date** (date): Enter the date of the next planned or scheduled fire equipment inspection.
  - This information helps us plan and prepare for upcoming inspections.
- **Last Inspection Result** (select one): Report the outcome of the last fire equipment inspection.
  - Select "Pass", "Fail", or "Unknown" to indicate the results of the inspection.
- **Maintenance History** (text): Describe the maintenance history of fire equipment in the facility.
  - Provide details on when, what, and how equipment was maintained or replaced.
- **Emergency Contact Info** (text): Provide contact information for emergency contacts or key personnel.
  - Enter names, phone numbers, and any other relevant details for easy communication during emergencies.
