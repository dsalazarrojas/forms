# Educational Outreach Impact Study - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from participants in educational outreach programs to help us understand their effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your participant details (page 1)
2. Rate the activities you participated in (page 2)
3. Rate the participants in the activities (page 3)
4. Provide additional participant feedback (page 4)
5. Select your ratings for the activities (page 5)
6. Select your participant ratings (page 6)
7. Add any comments or feedback (page 7)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about your participation in the educational outreach program.
* **Activity Rating** (`activity_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the activities you participated in on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Good".
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your experience as a participant in the educational outreach program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Good".
* **Participant Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience as a participant.
* **Ratings** (`ratings`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your ratings for the activities you participated in from "Good" or "Fair".
* **Activity Ratings** (`activity_ratings`, select_one, required: false): Choose your rating for the overall activity from "Good" or "Fair".
* **Participant Ratings** (`participant_ratings`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your rating as a participant from "Good" or "Fair".
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience as a participant.
