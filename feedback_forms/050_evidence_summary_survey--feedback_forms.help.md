# Evidence Summary Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evidence Summary Survey form is a tool for collecting and organizing user-provided evidence or findings. This form serves as a means for users to share their insights and results in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form sections in order, from top to bottom.
2. For the "Select Relevant Evidence or Findings" section, choose the relevant option(s) that best describe the evidence or findings you've gathered.
3. In the "Summary of Findings or Evidence" section, provide a brief summary of the selected evidence or findings.
4. In the "Impact Statement" section, describe the impact of the evidence or findings on the outcome.
5. In the "Recommendation" and "Detailed Description of Evidence or Findings" sections, provide any applicable recommendations for future action or further investigation, and a detailed description of the evidence or findings, respectively.
6. If applicable, provide a reference or citation for the evidence or findings in the "Reference or Citation" section.
7. If not applicable, you can select "Other" and provide a brief description of the evidence or findings.
8. Finally, select the assigned tool used to collect the evidence or findings, and provide any relevant participant information in the "Participant Information" and "Participant Info Tool" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information**: This field allows you to provide general information about your submission. Please enter any relevant details that are not included in the other fields.
* **Select Relevant Evidence or Findings**: This section is where you choose the type of evidence or findings you've gathered. You can select one or more options that best describe your evidence or findings.
* **Summary of Findings or Evidence**: This field requires a brief summary of the selected evidence or findings.
* **Impact Statement**: Describe the impact of the evidence or findings on the outcome.
* **Recommendation**: Provide any applicable recommendations for future action or further investigation.
* **Detailed Description of Evidence or Findings**: Provide a detailed description of the evidence or findings.
* **Reference or Citation**: Provide a reference or citation for the evidence or findings.
* **Other**: If the above options do not apply, select "Other" and provide a brief description of the evidence or findings.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool used to collect the evidence or findings.
* **Participant Information**: Provide any relevant participant information related to the evidence or findings.
* **Participant Info Tool**: Provide any additional information about the participant in this section.
* **Other (please specify)**: This field is for additional information that does not fit in the other fields. Please be brief.
