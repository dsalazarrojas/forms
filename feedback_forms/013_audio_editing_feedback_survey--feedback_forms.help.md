# Audio Editing Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Audio Editing Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from clients who have worked with an audio editing service. It helps us understand their experience with the service, including their satisfaction with the editing process, communication, and value for money.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please fill out each section with your honest feedback.
2. Be as specific as possible in your responses.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name**: The name of the client providing feedback.
* **Email Address**: The contact email address of the client.
* **Project Name**: The name or title of the audio project.
* **Project Type**: The type of audio project (e.g. Podcast Episode, Music Track, Audiobook, etc.).
* **Completion Date**: The date when the project was delivered.
* **Audio Editor Name**: The name of the editor who worked on the project.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the editing process (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Audio Quality Rating**: Rate the final audio quality (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Editing Precision**: Rate the precision and accuracy of edits (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Noise Reduction Quality**: Rate the noise reduction and cleanup (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not applicable).
* **Pacing and Timing**: Rate the pacing and timing of the edit (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication Rating**: Rate communication with the editor (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Turnaround Time**: Rate the turnaround time for the project (Delivered early, Delivered on time, Slightly delayed, Significantly delayed).
* **Revision Requests**: If you needed to request any revisions, select the number of revisions required.
* **Revision Handling**: Rate the handling of revisions (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Not applicable).
* **Value for Money**: Rate the value for the price paid (Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value).
* **Would Recommend**: If you would recommend our services, select your answer (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Would Use Again**: If you would use our services again, select your answer (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Strengths**: List what we did well in the project.
* **Areas for Improvement**: List what we could improve on.
* **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback or suggestions you may have.
