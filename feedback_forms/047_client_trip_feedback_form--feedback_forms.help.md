# Client Trip Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from clients after their trip, to help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and email address to help us identify you and follow up with you if needed.
2. Answer the questions about your trip, including where you traveled, when you traveled, and what type of trip you took.
3. Rate your experience on various aspects, such as the quality of the accommodations, activities, and overall satisfaction.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Client Name, Text, **Required**): Please enter your name as it appears on your passport or identification document.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, **Required**): Please enter your valid email address, so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Trip Destination** (Trip Destination, Text, **Required**): Please enter the name of the destination you traveled to.
* **Trip Date** (Trip Date, Date, **Required**): Please select the date(s) when you traveled.
* **Trip Duration** (Trip Duration, Text, **Optional**): If you have any information about the length of your trip, please enter it here.
* **Trip Purpose** (Trip Purpose, Select One, **Required**): Please select the reason for your trip (e.g., Leisure vacation, Business travel, etc.).
* **Travel Package Used** (Travel Package Used, Select One, **Optional**): If you used a travel package, please select the type you used.
* **Overall Trip Satisfaction** (Overall Trip Satisfaction, Select One, **Required**): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the trip.
* **Quality of Accommodations** (Quality of Accommodations, Select One, **Required**): Please rate the quality of your accommodations.
* **Quality of Activities and Experiences** (Quality of Activities and Experiences, Select One, **Required**): Please rate the quality of the activities and experiences you had during your trip.
* **Value for Money** (Value for Money, Select One, **Required**): Please rate the value you received for the price you paid.
* **Quality of Travel Guide or Agent Support** (Quality of Travel Guide or Agent Support, Select One, **Optional**): If you received support from a travel guide or agent, please rate their quality of support.
* **Food and Dining Experience** (Food and Dining Experience, Select One, **Optional**): Please rate the food and dining experience at the destination.
* **Local Attractions and Sights** (Local Attractions and Sights, Select One, **Optional**): If you visited local attractions, please rate their quality.
* **Would You Return to This Destination?** (Would You Return to This Destination?, Select One, **Required**): Please indicate if you would return to this destination.
* **Would You Recommend This Trip to Others?** (Would You Recommend This Trip to Others?, Select One, **Required**): Please indicate if you would recommend this trip to others.
* **Best Part of Your Trip** (Best Part of Your Trip, Text, **Optional**): If you have a favorite experience from your trip, please describe it here.
* **What Could Be Improved?** (What Could Be Improved?, Text, **Optional**): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please describe them here.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (Additional Comments or Suggestions, Text, **Optional**): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
