# editorial_process_feedback_poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the editorial process, aiming to identify areas of improvement and gather suggestions for enhancing the overall content creation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page and ensure you have all the information needed for each field.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the most applicable option that best describes your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a brief but concrete explanation or suggestion.
4. If you're unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or ask if you need help.
