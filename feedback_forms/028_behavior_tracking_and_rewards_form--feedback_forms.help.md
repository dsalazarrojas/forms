# Behavior Tracking And Rewards Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track student behavior over a specific period and determine whether they have earned a reward based on their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's grade level in the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
3. Select the start and end dates for tracking in the "Tracking Period Start" and "Tracking Period End" fields, respectively.
4. Choose the target behaviors for the student to work on in the "Target Behaviors to Increase" field (select multiple options).
5. Choose the behaviors the student should decrease in the "Behaviors to Decrease" field (select multiple options).
6. Rate the student's behavior for each day of the week using the "Monday Rating" to "Friday Rating" fields.
7. Enter any comments or notes for each day in the "Monday Notes" to "Friday Notes" fields.
8. Enter the total points earned for the week in the "Total Points Earned" field.
9. Select the reward the student is working towards in the "Reward Goal" field.
10. Confirm whether the student has earned their reward in the "Reward Earned This Week" field.
11. Enter the parent or guardian's initials in the "Parent Initials" field (optional).
12. Enter any overall comments for the week in the "Teacher Comments" field (optional).
13. Set goals for the student's next week in the "Goals for Next Week" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name.
* **Student ID** (optional): If applicable, enter the student's ID number.
* **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Tracking Period Start** and **Tracking Period End**: Enter the start and end dates for tracking, respectively.
* **Target Behaviors to Increase**: Select multiple behaviors the student should focus on improving.
* **Behaviors to Decrease** (optional): Select behaviors the student should reduce or avoid.
* **Monday Rating** to **Friday Rating**: Rate the student's behavior for each day using the dropdown menu.
	+ **Excellent (5 points)**: The student demonstrated excellent behavior.
	+ **Good (4 points)**: The student demonstrated good behavior.
	+ **Satisfactory (3 points)**: The student demonstrated satisfactory behavior.
	+ **Needs Improvement (2 points)**: The student needs improvement.
	+ **Poor (1 point)**: The student's behavior was poor.
* **Monday Notes** to **Friday Notes**: Enter any comments or notes for each day.
* **Total Points Earned**: Enter the sum of points earned for the week.
* **Reward Goal**: Select the reward the student is working towards from the dropdown menu.
* **Reward Earned This Week**: Confirm whether the student has earned their reward.
	+ **Yes earned and received**: The student earned their reward.
	+ **Yes earned not yet received**: The student earned their reward but have not received it yet.
	+ **No did not earn**: The student did not earn their reward.
	+ **N/A**: Not applicable.
* **Parent Initials** (optional): Enter the parent or guardian's initials.
* **Teacher Comments** (optional): Enter overall comments for the week.
* **Goals for Next Week**: Enter goals for the student's next week.
