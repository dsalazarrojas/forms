# employee_survey_results_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect employee feedback on their own performance and satisfaction levels. This data is used by management to better understand employee needs and improve job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category under which you would like your feedback recorded (Feedback Forms).
2. Enter a brief description of the form, if desired.
3. Select the tool assigned to you (e.g., chatjimmy).
4. Choose your level of satisfaction with your current role (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Satisfied).
5. List specific suggestions for improvement, if applicable.
6. Choose the method used to collect data (Online Form, In Person, Other).
7. Enter the date and time of the survey.
8. Add any additional comments, if desired.
9. Enter your email, phone number, and manager's name, email, and phone number, if applicable.
10. Choose your department, team, and region, if applicable.
11. Enter your date of birth and years of service, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Form Title** (`form_title`, Text, Optional)
This field is for entering a brief title of the form, if you'd like to add one.

### 2. **Category** (`form_category`, Select One, Optional)
Choose the category under which you would like your feedback recorded. This will help management organize and track feedback.

### 3. **Description** (`form_description`, Text, Optional)
Enter a brief description of the form, if you'd like to add one.

### 4. **Output File** (`form_file`, Text, Optional)
This field is for selecting a file to output with your feedback.

### 5. **Form ID** (`form_id`, Text, Optional)
Enter a unique ID for your form submission.

### 6. **Assigned Tool** (`form_assigned_tool`, Select One, Optional)
Select the tool assigned to you for this survey.

### 7. **Employee Survey Satisfaction** (`employee_survey_satisfaction`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Rate your level of satisfaction with your current role. Choose from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.

### 8. **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, Select Multiple, Optional)
List specific suggestions for improvement, if applicable.

### 9. **Data Collection Method** (`data_collection_method`, Select One, Optional)
Choose the method used to collect data (Online Form, In Person, Other).

### 10. **Date of Survey** (`date_of_survey`, Date, Optional)
Enter the date of the survey.

### 11. **Time of Survey** (`time_of_survey`, Time, Optional)
Enter the time of the survey.

### 12. **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional)
Add any additional comments, if desired.

### 13. **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional)
Enter your email address.

### 14. **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional)
Enter your phone number.

### 15. **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, Text, Optional)
Enter your manager's name.

### 16. **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, Email, Optional)
Enter your manager's email address.

### 17. **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, Text, Optional)
Enter your manager's phone number.

### 18. **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, Note, Optional)
Add any additional comments from your manager, if desired.

### 19. **Department** (`department`, Select One, Optional)
Choose your department (Marketing, Sales, Operations).

### 20. **Team** (`team`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose the team you belong to (Sales Team, Management, Marketing Team).

### 21. **Region** (`region`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose your region (North, South, East).

### 22. **Manager Region** (`manager_region`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose your manager's region (North, South, East).

### 23. **Manager Team** (`manager_team`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose your manager's team (Sales Team, Management, Marketing Team).

### 24. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Optional)
Enter your date of birth.

### 25. **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, Number, Optional)
Enter your years of service.
