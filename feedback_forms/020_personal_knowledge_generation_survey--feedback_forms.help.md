# Personal Knowledge Generation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your personal knowledge generation habits and practices. It will help us understand how you gather, share, and utilize knowledge in your daily work or studies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please carefully read the instructions for each field and answer the questions accordingly. If you have any difficulties or questions, don't hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your current role** (1. survey_start, select_one, required)
	+ This question helps us understand your current role or position, whether you're a student, professional, or have any other designation.
* **Other (specify)** (2. role_other, text, optional)
	+ If you have a specific role that is not listed, please describe it here.
* **What are your primary sources of knowledge?** (3. knowledge_sources, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select all the sources of knowledge that you regularly use, such as books, articles, research papers, online forums, or others.
* **Other (specify)** (4. knowledge_sources_other, text, optional)
	+ If you have any other sources of knowledge not listed, please describe them here.
* **Who do you turn to when you need advice?** (6. prophets, select_one, required)
	+ This question helps us understand who you rely on for guidance or advice.
* **How many advisors do you have in your network?** (7. advisors, select_one, required)
	+ Please select the number of advisors you have in your network.
* **How do you share and disseminate your knowledge?** (8. knowledge_distribution, select_one, required)
	+ Select the methods you use to share and disseminate your knowledge, such as through teaching, mentoring, or online platforms.
* **Do you teach or mentor others?** (9. knowledge_self_teaching, select_one, required)
	+ This question helps us understand if you have a teaching or mentoring role.
* **Have you received feedback from others?** (10. feedback, select_one, required)
	+ Please select if you have received feedback from others, which helps us understand how you value external feedback.
* **Do you assess your own knowledge?** (11. knowledge_self_assessment, select_one, required)
	+ This question helps us understand how often you evaluate your own knowledge.
* **How often do you assess your knowledge?** (12. self_knowledge_assessment_frequency, select_one, required)
	+ Please select how often you regularly assess your knowledge.
* **Do you generate new knowledge?** (13. knowledge_self_generation, select_one, required)
	+ This question helps us understand your ability to create new knowledge.
* **Do you reflect on your knowledge?** (14. knowledge_self_reflection, select_one, required)
	+ This question helps us understand how often you reflect on your knowledge.
* **Other sources of knowledge?** (15. prophets_other_sources, select_multiple, required)
	+ Please select any other sources of knowledge not listed, such as books, articles, or research papers.
* **Knowledge distribution channels prophets use?** (16. knowledge_distribution_channels, select_multiple, required)
	+ Select the channels you use to disseminate your knowledge, such as online platforms, social media, or in-person discussions.
* **Other knowledge distribution channels?** (17. advisors_other_channels, select_multiple, required)
	+ Please select any other channels you use to share your knowledge not listed.
* **How do you utilize knowledge management systems?** (18. knowledge_management_systems, text, optional)
	+ If you use any knowledge management systems, please describe them here.
* **How many advisors?** (19. advisors_number, number, optional)
	+ Please enter the number of advisors you have in your network.
* **What kind of knowledge management systems?** (20. knowledge_management, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Please select the type of knowledge management systems you use, such as document management or collaboration tools.
* **How many prophets?** (21. prophets_number, number, optional)
	+ Please enter the number of prophets you have in your network.
* **Other?** (22. other_prophets, text, optional)
	+ If you have any other prophets or sources of knowledge not listed, please describe them here.
* **Other?** (23. other_advisors, text, optional)
	+ If you have any other advisors not listed, please describe them here.
* **How often do you teach or mentor?** (24. knowledge_self_teaching_frequencies, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Please select how often you teach or mentor others.
* **How often do you reflect on your knowledge?** (25. knowledge_self_reflection_frequencies, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Please select how often you reflect on your knowledge.
