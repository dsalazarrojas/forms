# Online Platform User Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with our platform. It is intended to be filled out by users who have used our platform and wish to provide their honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **User Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field, which will help us contact you if needed.
3. Answer the following questions by selecting one of the provided options:
	* **What do you think of the overall user interface of our platform?** (Select one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5)
	* **What do you think of the overall user experience of our platform?** (Select one of the options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5)
	* You can add any additional feedback in the text fields:
	* **feedback_question_3**: Type in any additional feedback you have about our platform.
	* **feedback_question_4**: Type in any additional feedback you have about our platform.
	* **feedback_question_5**: Type in any additional feedback you have about our platform.
	* **feedback_question_6**: Type in any additional feedback you have about our platform.
