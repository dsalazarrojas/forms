# Outlet Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Outlet Evaluation Form is designed to collect relevant information about a store, including its name, address, phone number, email, and category, to help evaluate its performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for the store from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the store's name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Provide a brief description of the store, if necessary.
4. Enter the date of the evaluation.
5. Enter the name of the person evaluating the store.
6. Leave any additional comments or notes about the store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outlet Evaluation Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the evaluation.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the store being evaluated (e.g., "Feedback Forms" or "Other").
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the store, if necessary.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the store being evaluated.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, required): Enter the store's physical address.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, required): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Email** (`store_email`, email, optional): Enter the store's email address.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluated By** (`evaluated_by`, text, required): Enter your name as the person evaluating the store.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about the store.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the evaluation is complete and comprehensive.
* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure the evaluation is fair and reliable.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, contact your supervisor or the evaluation team.
