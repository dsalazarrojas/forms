# Delivery Personnel Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the delivery experience, specifically on the punctuality, professionalism, communication, and overall satisfaction of the delivery personnel. Please fill out this form to provide your honest opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant rating for each field based on your experience.
2. Fill out all required fields to ensure that your feedback is submitted correctly.
3. Review your responses before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Punctuality** (`delivery_punctuality`, number, required: false): Rate the delivery personnel's punctuality, with options ranging from 0 to 5, where 5 is "Always on time" and 0 is "Often late".
* **Professionalism** (`professionalisme`, number, required: false): Rate the delivery personnel's professionalism, with options ranging from 0 to 5, where 5 is "Excellent" and 0 is "Poor".
* **Communication** (`communication`, number, required: false): Rate the delivery personnel's communication, with options ranging from 0 to 5, where 5 is "Excellent" and 0 is "Poor".
* **Overall Delivery Satisfaction** (`overall_delivery_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the delivery experience, with options ranging from 0 to 5, where 5 is "Very Satisfied" and 0 is "Very Dissatisfied".
