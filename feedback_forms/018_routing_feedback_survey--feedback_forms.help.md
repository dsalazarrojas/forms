# Routing Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Routing Feedback Survey form is a tool to gather user feedback about their routing experience. This feedback will help us improve the performance and efficiency of our routing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide valuable feedback, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you've only submitted feedback if you haven't recently done so.
2. Enter your name and any other necessary identifying information in the `User Name` field.
3. Rate your overall experience with the options below:
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
4. Provide any comments about your experience in the `Comments` field.
5. Enter the route number in the `Route Number` field.
6. Describe your experience in the `Route Description` field.
7. Rate our system performance in the `System Performance` field.
8. Suggest any improvements for our system in the `System Improvement` field.
9. Provide any additional feedback in the `Feedback` field.
10. Enter the date and time you submitted your feedback in the `Submitted At` field.
11. Enter the name of the person or team that assigned you to the route in the `Assigned To` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or any other identifying information you want to provide.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience using the options below:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments about your experience.
* **Route Number** (`route_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of the route you took.
* **Route Description** (`route_description`, text, optional): Describe your experience in as much detail as you need.
* **System Performance** (`system_performance`, select_one, optional): Rate our system performance:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **System Improvement** (`system_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest any improvements for our system.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback about your experience.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time you submitted your feedback.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or team that assigned you to the route.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or team that assigned you to the route.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is only here to help us remember any comments you made previously. You can add or edit these comments if you need to.
