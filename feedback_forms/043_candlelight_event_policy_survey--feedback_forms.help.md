# Candlelight Event Policy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candlelight Event Policy Survey is a tool for gathering feedback on our current candlelight event policies. Your input is valuable in helping us refine and improve these policies to ensure the safety and enjoyment of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each form section carefully and thoroughly.
2. For each multiple-choice question, select the option that best aligns with your thoughts.
3. You may submit any additional comments or suggestions in the text fields.
4. Review all fields for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`candlelight_event_name`, Text, Required): What was the name of the gathering.
	+ This field is used to record the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`candlelight_event_date`, Date, Required): The date of the event.
	+ Please select the date of the event from your calendar.
* **Organizer Name** (`candlelight_organizer_name`, Text, Required): Primary contact for the event.
	+ Enter the name of the person or group that organized the event.
* **Organization or Group** (`candlelight_organization_group`, Text, Required): Which club or department hosted this event.
	+ Identify the group or department that hosted the event.
* **Clarity of the Candlelight Policy** (`candlelight_policy_clarity`, Select One, Required): Was the information easy to understand?
	+ Select the option that best describes your experience with the policy clarity.
* **Adequacy of Safety Guidelines** (`candlelight_safety_guidelines_rating`, Select One, Required): Did the rules feel sufficient for fire prevention?
	+ Choose the option that best reflects your assessment of the guidelines.
* **Satisfaction with Fire Safety Requirements** (`candlelight_fire_safety_satisfaction`, Select One, Required): How satisfied are you with the fire safety requirements?
	+ Select the option that describes your level of satisfaction.
* **Specific Policies** (`specific_policies_section`, Note, Optional): Feedback on certain rules.
	+ Enter any specific policies you felt were unclear or confusing.
* **Support for Battery-Operated Candles Only** (`battery_candle_policy_support`, Select One, Required): Stance on banning open flames.
	+ Select your stance on banning open flames.
* **Drip Guard Policy Opinion** (`drip_guard_policy_opinion`, Select One, Required): Use of protective layers to prevent mess.
	+ Choose your opinion on the necessity of drip guards.
* **Candle Count Policy Opinion** (`candle_count_policy_opinion`, Select One, Required): Limits on the number of candles in one space.
	+ Select your opinion on the reasonableness of candle count limits.
* **Any Issues Encountered** (`candlelight_event_issues`, Text, Optional): Describe any problems with compliance or safety.
	+ If you encountered any issues, please provide details.
* **Feedback on Approval Process** (`approval_process_feedback`, Text, Optional): How was your experience getting the event cleared?
	+ Share your thoughts on the approval process.
* **Likelihood of Hosting Another Candlelight Event** (`host_again_likelihood`, Number, Required): How likely are you to host another candlelight event?
	+ Select a number from 1 to 10, where 1 means not at all likely and 10 means very likely.
* **Top Three Suggestions for Policy Improvement** (`policy_improvement_suggestions`, Text, Optional): Specific ideas for change.
	+ Enter three suggestions for improving the policy.
* **Commitment to Policy Agreement** (`policy_agreement_final_check`, Select One, Required): Commitment to following the updated rules.
	+ Select whether you agree to the current policy terms.
* **Date of Feedback Submission** (`feedback_submission_date`, Date, Required): Date of Feedback.
	+ Select the date you are submitting this feedback.
