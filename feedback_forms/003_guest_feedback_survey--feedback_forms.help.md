# guest_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The guest feedback survey is designed to collect honest opinions from hotel guests about their experience at the hotel. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we will use it to improve our services and provide better experiences for our guests in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant option for each question from the dropdown menu.
3. If a question does not apply to your experience, please select "N/A" (Not Applicable).
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Once you have completed all the questions, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Feedback** (`guest_feedback`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience at the hotel.
* **Room Service** (`room_service`, select_one, required): How would you rate the quality of room service provided? Please select from the following options:
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Bad
	+ Very bad
* **Cleanliness** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): How would you rate the cleanliness of the hotel? Please select from the following options:
	+ Very clean
	+ Clean
	+ Average
	+ Dirty
	+ Very dirty
* **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, select_one, required): How would you rate the quality of the food provided? Please select from the following options:
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Bad
	+ Very bad
* **Staff Attitude** (`staff_attitude`, select_one, required): How would you rate the attitude of the hotel staff? Please select from the following options:
	+ Very helpful
	+ Helpful
	+ Neutral
	+ Unhelpful
	+ Very unhelpful
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, required): What was your overall experience at the hotel? Please provide a brief description.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Any additional comments or suggestions about your stay?
* **Feedback On Service** (`feedback_on_service`, text, required): How can we improve our service?
* **Feedback On Environment** (`feedback_on_environment`, text, required): How can we improve our hotel environment?
* **Feedback On Facilities** (`feedback_on_facilities`, text, required): How can we improve our hotel facilities?
* **Feedback On Staff** (`feedback_on_staff`, text, required): How can we improve our hotel staff?

Note: All required fields are marked with an asterisk (*) in the above explanations. 

Tips:

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we will use it to improve our services and provide better experiences for our guests in the future.
