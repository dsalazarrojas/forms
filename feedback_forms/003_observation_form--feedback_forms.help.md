# Observation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and observations from employees. It's an opportunity for both employees and their supervisors to share their thoughts and opinions on work-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Employee Feedback" field to provide your input on your experience and areas for improvement.
2. Choose one of the performance areas listed in the "Performance Areas" field that best describe your observation.
3. Enter the date of your observation in the "Observation Date" field.
4. Optionally, provide your supervisor's feedback in the "Supervisor Feedback" field.
5. Describe the action steps you've taken or plan to take based on your observation in the "Action Steps" field.
6. Provide your comments on the observation in the "Observation Comments" field.
7. Identify the next steps you'll take in the "Next Steps" field.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the observation in the "Overall Rating" field.
9. Confirm if the form was submitted in the "Observation Form Submitted" field.
10. Provide additional comments from your supervisor in the "Supervisor Comments" field.
11. Confirm if the form was completed in the "Observation Form Completed" field.
12. Add any additional comments for your supervisor in the "Supervisor Comments" field.
13. Confirm your employee's acknowledgement in the "Employee Acknowledgement" field.
14. Confirm your supervisor's acknowledgement in the "Supervisor Acknowledgement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Feedback** (Required): This field is for you to provide your input on your experience and areas for improvement.
* **Performance Areas** (Required): Choose one of the performance areas that best describes your observation.
* **Observation Date** (Optional): Enter the date of your observation.
* **Supervisor Feedback** (Optional): Provide feedback from your supervisor, if applicable.
* **Action Steps** (Optional): Describe the action steps you've taken or plan to take based on your observation.
* **Observation Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional comments on the observation.
* **Next Steps** (Optional): Identify the next steps you'll take.
* **Overall Rating** (Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the observation.
* **Date of Submission** (Optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Observation Form Submitted** (Optional): Confirm if the form was submitted.
* **Supervisor Feedback** (Optional): Provide feedback from your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Signature Date** (Optional): Enter the date of your supervisor's signature.
* **Supervisor Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments from your supervisor.
* **Observation Form Completed** (Optional): Confirm if the form was completed.
* **Employee Comments** (Optional): Add any additional comments from your employee.
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (Optional): Confirm your employee's acknowledgement.
* **Supervisor Acknowledgement** (Optional): Confirm your supervisor's acknowledgement.

Note: The required fields must be filled out before submission, while optional fields are for additional information and comments.
