# SharePoint Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SharePoint Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users, allowing team members to provide suggestions, comments, and insights about the platform. This form will not be used for formal complaints or urgent matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in the **Category** field by selecting one of the provided options: "Feedback Forms" or "Other".
*   Provide a brief **Description** of the issue or suggestion you'd like to make.
*   Enter your **Name** if you'd like your feedback to be acknowledged.
*   If you're providing your email address, enter your **Email** for contact purposes.
*   Enter your **Phone** number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
*   In the **Comments** field, please provide a detailed description of your feedback.
*   Choose a date and time when you're providing feedback in the **Date** and **Time** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Category** (Feedback Forms, Other):
    *   This field allows you to specify the type of feedback you're providing.
*   **Description**:
    *   Provide a brief description of the issue or suggestion you'd like to make.
*   **Name**:
    *   Enter your name if you'd like your feedback to be acknowledged.
*   **Email**:
    *   If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email address.
*   **Phone**:
    *   Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted directly.
*   **Comments** (text area):
    *   In this field, please provide a detailed description of your feedback.
*   **Date**:
    *   Choose a date when you're providing feedback.
*   **Time**:
    *   Choose a time when you're providing feedback.
