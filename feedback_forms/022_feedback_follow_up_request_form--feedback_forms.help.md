# Feedback Follow Up Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request feedback follow-up on a previously submitted feedback request. This form helps us understand your concerns and provide you with the necessary information to resolve the issue efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Briefly describe the subject of your feedback request in the "Subject" field.
6. Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing in the "Description" field.
7. Enter the feedback ID number you received when you initially submitted your feedback request in the "Feedback ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, optional)**: Enter your first name, which will help us to address you personally in our response.
* **Company (Text, optional)**: Provide the name of your company to help us understand your context.
* **Email (Email, required)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with further updates or clarification on your feedback.
* **Phone (Text, optional)**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you via phone if needed.
* **Subject (Text, optional)**: Briefly describe the subject of your feedback request to help us understand the context.
* **Description (Text, optional)**: Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing to help us understand your concern.
* **Feedback ID (Number, optional)**: Enter the feedback ID number you received when you initially submitted your feedback request. If you are unsure, please leave this field blank and we will help you find the correct ID.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the "Email" field to receive updates or follow-up information on your feedback request.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
