# Student Portal Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Portal Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience on the student portal. It's intended to be a helpful and user-friendly form that allows students to share their thoughts and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the level of satisfaction with the student portal using the "Portal Satisfaction" dropdown menu.
* Choose how often you use the "Feature Usage" options.
* If you have any technical issues, please type them in the "Technical Issues" text field.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in the "Suggestions" text field.
* Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your feedback.
* Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you about your feedback.
* Enter your full name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your School ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Portal Satisfaction** (`Portal Satisfaction`, select_one, required): This question asks you to choose how satisfied you are with the student portal. Please select the level that best fits your experience: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **Feature Usage** (`Feature Usage`, select_multiple, required): This question asks how often you use certain features of the student portal. Please select all the features that apply to you: Very Often, Sometimes, Rarely, or Never.
* **Technical Issues** (`Technical Issues`, text, required): If you have any technical issues with the student portal, please describe them in this text field.
* **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, note, required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in this text field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **School ID** (`School ID`, number, required): Please enter your School ID number.
