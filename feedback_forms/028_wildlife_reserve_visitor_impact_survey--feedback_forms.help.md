# Wildlife Reserve Visitor Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Reserve Visitor Impact Survey is a form designed to gather information about your visit to the park. Your feedback and input will help us understand how we can improve your experience and make the park a better place for you and our other visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information (optional).
2. Answer the questions about your visit (questions 1-8).
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the comments section.
4. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact or to be reached for any park updates.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for any follow-up contact or to be reached for any park updates.
* **How many times have you visited the park?** (`question_1`, number, required: false): Tell us about your visit frequency.
* **What time of year were you in the park?** (`question_2`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the seasons you visited the park in (Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter).
* **How long were you in the park?** (`question_3`, time, required: false): Please enter the duration of your visit in hours and minutes.
* **How likely are you to recommend the park?** (`question_4`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how likely you are to recommend the park to friends and family (Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Not Likely, Not at All).
* **How many people were in your party?** (`question_5`, number, required: false): Tell us about the size of your group.
* **Did you visit with a guided tour?** (`question_6`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select yes if you had a guided tour, no otherwise.
* **How satisfied are you with your visit?** (`question_7`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose how satisfied you were with your visit (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **What type of activities did you participate in?** (`question_8`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all the activities you participated in during your visit (Hiking, Wildlife watching, Picnicking, Camping, Fishing, Other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
