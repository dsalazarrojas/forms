# Tenant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Feedback Form is a tool for tenants to share their experiences and concerns with our company. This form helps us gather valuable insights and address any issues promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "How was your experience" option to rate your experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
2. Enter your feedback message in the "Your Feedback" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field for us to contact you.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field for us to contact you.
5. Briefly describe the subject of your feedback in the "Subject" field.
6. Write your message to us in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How was your experience** (`feedback_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with our company, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Your Feedback** (`feedback_message`, text, required): Share your thoughts and experiences with our company. Please be specific about what happened and how we can improve.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Provide a phone number so we can reach out to you about your feedback.
* **Subject** (`contact_subject`, text, required): Give a brief description of your feedback, so we can prioritize our response.
* **Message** (`contact_message`, text, required): Write a detailed message about your concerns and experiences with our company. Be as specific as possible, so we can address the issue promptly.
