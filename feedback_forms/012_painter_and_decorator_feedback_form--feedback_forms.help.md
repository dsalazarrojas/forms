# Painter And Decorator Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Painter And Decorator Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with a painter and decorator service. This form helps the business understand what customers like and dislike about the service and make improvements accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide a valid phone number.
3. Share your feedback on your experience.
4. Indicate how satisfied you were with your visit.
5. Select whether you will recommend this service to others.
6. Rate your overall experience.
7. Add any additional comments.
8. Enter the date and time of your visit.
9. Select the gender of the staff member you interacted with.
10. Confirm if the staff member wore gloves during your visit.
11. Confirm if the staff member wore a mask during your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`form_1`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_2`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`form_3`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`form_4`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Feedback** (`form_5`, note, required): Share your experience with our service, including what you liked and disliked.
* **How Satisfied** (`form_6`, select_one, required): Select how satisfied you were with your visit, from 'Very Satisfied' to 'Not Satisfied'.
* **Will Recommend** (`form_7`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you will recommend this service to others, 'True' or 'False'.
* **Rate Service** (`form_8`, number, required): Rate your overall experience, from 1 to 5.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_9`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Date Visited** (`form_10`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time Visited** (`form_11`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Staff Interacted** (`form_12`, select_one, required): Select the gender of the staff member you interacted with, from 'Male', 'Female', or 'Non Binary'.
* **Staff Wore Gloves** (`form_13`, select_one, required): Confirm if the staff member wore gloves during your visit.
* **Staff Wore Mask** (`form_14`, select_multiple, required): Confirm if the staff member wore a mask during your visit.
