# Bishop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the Bishop Feedback Survey form, which is designed to gather your honest feedback about the bishop's leadership and communication effectiveness. Your input will assist the bishop in understanding the community's concerns and make informed decisions to serve the parish better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing your name and email address (optional). These details will help us ensure that your feedback is accurately attributed and addressed.
2. Rate the bishop's clarity in communicating the diocesan vision (5-point scale: 1 - Unclear, 2 - Somewhat Unclear, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Clear, 5 - Very Clear).
3. Rate the bishop's accessibility for pastoral matters (5-point scale: 1 - Poor, 2 - Fair, 3 - Good, 4 - Very Good, 5 - Excellent).
4. Rate the bishop's financial stewardship (5-point scale: 1 - Poor, 2 - Fair, 3 - Good, 4 - Very Good, 5 - Excellent).
5. Rate the bishop's community engagement efforts (5-point scale: 1 - Poor, 2 - Fair, 3 - Good, 4 - Very Good, 5 - Excellent).
6. Rate the frequency of communication (5-point scale: 1 - Too Infrequent, 2 - Infrequent, 3 - Appropriate, 4 - Frequent, 5 - Too Frequent).
7. Share any specific strengths you've observed about the bishop's leadership.
8. Identify areas where the bishop could improve.
9. Rate your overall satisfaction with the bishop's leadership (5-point scale: 1 - Very Dissatisfied, 2 - Dissatisfied, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Satisfied, 5 - Very Satisfied).
10. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
11. If you're willing, indicate your willingness to discuss your feedback further.
12. Finally, select the date of form completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Bishop Leadership Feedback Survey --** (`feedback_intro`): This is an introductory note that explains the purpose of the form and encourages your honest feedback.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): Your name (optional) and email address (optional) will help us accurately attribute your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`): Your email address (optional) will help us keep you updated on any responses or follow-up actions.
* **Parish Name** (`parish_name`): Please enter your parish's name to help us understand which community you are providing feedback for.
* **Rate the Bishop's Clarity** (`leadership_clarity_rating`): Rate the bishop's ability to communicate the diocesan vision on a 5-point scale.
* **Rate Pastoral Care** (`pastoral_care_rating`): Rate the bishop's accessibility for pastoral matters on a 5-point scale.
* **Rate Financial Stewardship** (`financial_stewardship_rating`): Rate the bishop's financial management on a 5-point scale.
* **Rate Community Engagement** (`community_engagement_rating`): Rate the bishop's efforts to engage with the community on a 5-point scale.
* **Rate Communication Frequency** (`communication_frequency_rating`): Rate the bishop's communication frequency on a 5-point scale.
* **Bishop's Strengths** (`strengths_feedback`): Share any specific strengths you've observed about the bishop's leadership.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`): Identify areas where the bishop could improve.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the bishop's leadership on a 5-point scale.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Discuss Further** (`willing_to_discuss`): If you're willing, indicate your willingness to discuss your feedback further.
* **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_completion_date`): Select the date of form completion.

## Tips
- Be honest and constructive in your feedback. Your input will help the bishop understand your concerns and make informed decisions.
- You can choose to provide your name and email address or remain anonymous.
- Please answer all questions and provide as much detail as possible, especially in the 'Bishop's Strengths' and 'Improvement Areas' sections.
- If you're willing, we'll be happy to discuss your feedback further to provide more insights and actions.
