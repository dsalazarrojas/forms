# Post Occupancy Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from occupants about their experiences in the building. The survey aims to gather information about the building's functionality, comfort, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please select the relevant answers from the options provided for each field.
- You may answer all questions or leave some blank if you don't have an opinion or experience.
- Please be as specific and honest as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. <label> Very comfortable/ Somewhat comfortable/ Not very comfortable/ Not at all comfortable </label> (comfort_and_satisfaction)
- This question asks about your comfort level in the building.
- Select the option that best reflects your feelings about the building's comfort.

### 2. <label> Building Functionality </label> (building_functionality)

### 3. <label> What would you like to improve? </label> (improvement_suggestions)
