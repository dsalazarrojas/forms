# Winter Apparel Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Apparel Feedback Survey is designed to collect your opinions and feedback on your experience with winter apparel. Your input will help us better understand your needs and preferences, allowing us to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the quality rating question (page 1) by selecting a number that reflects your rating of the winter apparel's quality.
2. On page 2, select all the features you like about the winter apparel from the list (features_rating).
3. On page 3, select all the features you wish were included in the winter apparel from the list (recommendations).
4. Answer the overall price value question (page 4) by selecting a number that reflects your opinion of the price.
5. On page 5, select the rating that best describes the brand's reputation (brand_reliability).
6. On page 6, select how often you buy winter apparel from the brand (purchase_frequency).
7. Answer the customer service rating question (page 7) by selecting a number that reflects your rating.
8. On page 8, select your overall experience with the winter apparel (overall_experience).
9. On page 9, select the location where you made the purchase (purchase_location).
10. Answer the overall satisfaction question (page 10) by selecting a number that reflects your level of satisfaction.
11. On page 11, select whether you would recommend this winter apparel to a friend (recommendation).
12. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them in the comments field (page 12).
