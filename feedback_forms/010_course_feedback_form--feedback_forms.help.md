# Course Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with a course. It is a crucial part of the course evaluation process, as it helps instructors and course administrators understand areas of strength and weakness in the course content, delivery, and overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the course in the "Course Title" field.
2. Describe your experience with the course in the "Course Description" field (optional).
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the course using the "Rating Score" field.
4. Type your feedback message in the "Feedback Message" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure we can contact you if needed.
6. Choose your level of satisfaction with the course using the "How satisfied are you with this course?" dropdown menu.
7. If you have any additional comments, you can provide them in the "Comments" field (optional).
8. Enter any contact details if you would like to be contacted for further questions or discussions.
9. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course you are providing feedback on.
* **Course Description**: This field is optional, but it helps us understand more about your experience with the course. Please describe the course in a few sentences.
* **Rating Score**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Unsatisfied" and 5 is "Highly Satisfied".
* **Feedback Message**: This is your opportunity to provide detailed feedback about your experience with the course.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. We do not share your email address with anyone else.
* **How satisfied are you with this course?**: Choose your level of satisfaction with the course from the dropdown menu.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the course, you can provide them here.
* **Contact Details**: If you would like to be contacted for further questions or discussions, please provide your contact details here.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
