# BIPOC Experience Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and experiences from BIPOC individuals about their participation in our program or organization. Your responses will help us better understand their experiences and make improvements to create a more inclusive and supportive environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Select your organization or program from the third field.
3. Answer the questions about your experience in the following fields:
	* How long have you been involved?
	* Overall experience rating
	* Do you feel a sense of belonging in this space?
	* How culturally relevant is the program?
4. If applicable, select all the types of support you received during your participation.
5. Answer the question about leadership representation.
6. If you faced any challenges, please describe them in the final field.
7. Answer the last question about discrimination or bias.
8. Optionally, suggest any improvements you think we could make.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Your Name** (text, required)
Your name is the first field that will be displayed in the form. Please fill in your name as it appears on your official documents.

### 2. **Email Address** (email, required)
Please fill in your email address so we can contact you if we need to follow up on any questions or concerns.

### 3. **Organization or Program Name** (text, required)
Select the name of the program or organization you participated in from the list below.

### 4. **How long have you been involved?** (text, optional)
Please describe how long you have been involved with the program or organization.

### 5. **Overall Experience Rating** (number, required)
Rate your overall experience from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).

### 6. **Do you feel a sense of belonging in this space?** (select_one, required)
Select one of the following options:
	* Strongly Yes
	* True
	* Neutral
	* False
	* Strongly No

### 7. **How culturally relevant is the program?** (select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options:
	* Highly relevant
	* Relevant
	* Somewhat relevant
	* Not relevant
	* Unknown

### 8. **What support did you experience?** (select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply:
	* Emotional support
	* Professional development
	* Networking opportunities
	* Leadership training
	* Financial assistance
	* Mental health resources
	* Community building
	* Career guidance

### 9. **Is there adequate BIPOC representation in leadership?** (select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options:
	* Yes, excellent
	* Yes, adequate
	* Somewhat
	* False
	* Unsure

### 10. **What challenges have you faced?** (text, optional)
Describe any challenges you faced during your participation in the program or organization.

### 11. **Have you experienced discrimination or bias?** (select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options:
	* True
	* False
	* Not sure
	* Prefer not to say

### 12. **What improvements would you suggest?** (text, optional)
Describe any suggestions you think would make the program or organization more inclusive and supportive.

### 13. **Would you recommend this program to others?** (select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options:
	* Definitely Yes
	* Probably Yes
	* Unsure
	* Probably No
	* Definitely No

### 14. **Will you continue to be involved?** (select_one, optional)
Select one of the following options:
	* Yes, definitely
	* Probably
	* Unsure
	* Probably not
	* No, I am leaving

### 15. **Additional Comments** (text, optional)
If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please include them here.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and openly, as all answers will be kept confidential and anonymous.
- If you select "Prefer not to say" for the question about discrimination or bias, we will not follow up on this question.
- If you have any feedback or concerns, please email us at [email address].
