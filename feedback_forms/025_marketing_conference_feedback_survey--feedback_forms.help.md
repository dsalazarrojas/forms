# Marketing Conference Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the marketing conference to gauge their overall experience and suggestions for improvement. It's essential to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us understand what we did well and where we can improve for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the required information.
2. Choose your answer for "Attended Conference" on **Page 1**.
3. Rate your **Overall Experience** on a scale of 1-10 on **Page 2**.
4. Enter your **Feedback** on **Page 3**, and provide as much detail as possible.
5. Enter your **Email** address on **Page 4** to help us contact you with any questions or follow-up.
6. Fill in your **Name** on **Page 5**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Conference Attended
* **Attended Conference** (`attended_conference`, select_one, **required**): Indicate whether you attended the conference. Please select 'Yes' if you did attend, and 'No' if you did not.

### Page 2: Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, **required**): Rate your overall experience at the conference on a scale from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.

### Page 3: Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **required**): Provide your thoughts and suggestions on how we can improve the conference. Be as detailed as possible.

### Page 4: Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or follow-up on your feedback.

### Page 5: Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name so we can address you by name in any communication.

### Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback.
* Please provide specific examples or suggestions for improvement.
* If you have any questions, contact us at [insert contact info].
