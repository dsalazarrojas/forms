## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users on the music leadership team's performance, performance notes, and overall rating of events, as well as follow-up actions taken as a result of the feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), as they are necessary for the form to be submitted.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, such as the overall rating or event type.
3. Provide a brief description in the performance notes field to elaborate on your feedback.
4. If applicable, provide an email address and phone number for follow-up contact.
5. Select the relevant options for the music department, ensemble, and feedback given to.
6. Choose the follow-up actions and dates for any follow-up actions taken.
7. Submit the form to complete it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Music Director** (`music_director_form`, text, optional): This field is used to provide feedback on the music director's performance. Please enter any comments or suggestions you have for their improvement.
* **Performance Notes** (`performance_notes`, text, optional): Describe the performance and what you liked or disliked about it.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall performance on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Constructive Feedback** (`constructive_feedback`, text, optional): Provide any constructive feedback to help the music director improve.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Location of Event** (`location_of_event`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of event (Recital, Rehearsal, or Event).
* **Music Department** (`music_department`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether the music department was involved in the event (Yes or No).
* **Music Ensemble** (`music_ensemble`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether the music ensemble was involved in the event (Yes or No).
* **Feedback Given To** (`feedback_given_to`, select_one, optional): Choose whether feedback was given to the music director (Yes or No).
* **Follow up Action** (`follow_up_action`, text, optional): Describe any follow-up actions taken as a result of feedback.
* **Follow up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the follow-up action.
* **Follow up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the follow-up action.
* **Follow up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes on the follow-up action.
* **Instrumental** (`music_department_instrumental`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether the music department played an instrumental role in the event (Yes or No).
* **Vocal** (`music_department_vocal`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether the music department played a vocal role in the event (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Please provide constructive and specific feedback to help the music director improve.
* Make sure to submit all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review and edit your feedback before submitting it to ensure it accurately reflects your thoughts and feelings.
