# Exercise Physiology Course Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from students who have participated in an Exercise Physiology course. This form helps us understand their thoughts, feelings, and suggestions about the course content, instruction, and overall learning experience. We value your input and use it to improve our courses and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your feedback in the designated fields.
2. Fill in your contact information, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
3. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exercise Physiology Course Feedback** (`exercise_physiology_course_feedback`, text, optional): A text field where you can provide any comments or suggestions about the course content, instruction, or overall learning experience.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you attended the course or when you are providing feedback.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, number, optional): The ID of the course session you attended.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the course on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Instructor ID** (`instructor_id`, number, optional): The ID of your instructor, if you know it.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the course or your learning experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide more detailed feedback about your experience with the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department where you believe this course falls under.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the course or your learning experience.

Note: All fields are optional, but providing feedback and contact information is highly encouraged to help us improve our services.
