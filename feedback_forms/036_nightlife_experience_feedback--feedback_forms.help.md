# Nightlife Experience Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the overall experience of customers with your nightlife services. It will help us to identify areas of improvement and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

* Answer all questions that are marked as required.
* Select the correct options for each question.
* Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
* Ensure that you submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required)
This question asks you to rate your overall experience with our services.
Please choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 2. **Service Quality** (`service`, number, required)
This question asks you to rate the quality of service you received.
Please choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### 3. **Ambiance** (`ambiance`, text, optional)
This question asks you to describe the ambiance of the location you visited.
Please provide a detailed description of your experience.

### 4. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
This is an open-ended question for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

### 5. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
This question asks for your email address.
Please enter a valid email address, but this is not required for submission.

### 6. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required)
This question asks if a tool was assigned to you.
Please select 'True' if a tool was assigned, and 'False' otherwise.

### 7. **Submission Date** (`submitted`, date, required)
This question is automatically populated with the current date.
Please do not edit this field.

### 8. **Tool Type** (`assigned_tool_type`, select_one, required)
This question asks for the type of tool assigned to you.
Please select the correct type of tool from the available options.

### 9. **Comments on Tool** (`comments_on_tool`, text, optional)
This question allows you to provide comments or feedback on the tool assigned to you.
Please provide any relevant details about the tool's performance or functionality.
