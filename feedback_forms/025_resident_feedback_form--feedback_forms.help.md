# Resident Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Resident Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from residents about their experiences with our organization. This form is intended to help us understand their satisfaction level and identify areas of improvement. Please fill out this form to share your thoughts and help us better serve you and our community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button once you have filled out all the required fields.
2. Fill out each field as prompted, and use the provided hints to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Info** (`resident_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your stay or experience with us. You can type as much or as little as you like.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, comments, or suggestions about your experience. This is your opportunity to tell us what we did well and what we could improve on.
* **Resident Satisfaction** (`resident_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select how satisfied you are with your experience on a scale from Satisfied to Dissatisfied.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): If you'd like to share your contact information with us, this is the place to do so. Please be cautious when sharing personal details.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Provide your contact number if you'd like to be reached back about your feedback.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like us to contact you about your feedback.
