# Extension Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a feedback and evaluation tool to help us understand how you are using our extension. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide a better user experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
2. Select your answers according to your actual usage experience with the extension.
3. Fill out any additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Extension Usage Survey Form**: This is the main form that contains all the questions.
* **Feedback**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience with the extension. (Optional)
* **Extension Rating**: Choose how often you use the extension (Option 1: Rarely, Option 2: Occasionally, Option 3: Often).
* **Extension Experience**: Select all the ways you have used the extension (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4).
* **Rating Value**: Enter a number rating of your overall satisfaction with the extension.
* **Suggest Improvement**: Provide any suggestions for how we can improve the extension. (Optional)
* **Extension Features**: Choose the features of the extension you use (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Suggest Improvement Details**: Provide any additional information about your suggestions. (Optional)
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about your experience with the extension. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted directly. (Optional)
* **Date**: Enter the date you last used the extension.
* **Time**: Enter the time of day you last used the extension.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience. (Optional)
* **Submit**: Review and submit your feedback.
