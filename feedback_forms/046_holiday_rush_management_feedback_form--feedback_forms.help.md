# holiday_rush_management_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers during peak holiday seasons. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form title to begin.
2. Enter your name and email address (if you feel comfortable sharing).
3. Provide a brief feedback message about your experience.
4. Choose the type of event you experienced during your visit (e.g. New Year's, Easter, etc.).
5. If you were at a store location, please specify which one.
6. If you had a specific employee assisting you, please provide their name and title.
7. Rate your overall satisfaction with a number (1-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Rush Management Feedback Form** (`form_title`, text, required: no): This is the title of the form. Please leave this field blank.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: no): Enter your name as you would like us to address you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: no): If you feel comfortable sharing your email address, please enter it. This will help us get in touch with you to follow up on your feedback.
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, note, required: no): Please provide a brief message about your experience. Try to be as specific as possible.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required: no): Choose the type of event you experienced during your visit (e.g. New Year's, Easter, etc.).
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, required: no): If you were at a store location, please specify which one.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: no): If you had a specific employee assisting you, please provide their name.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, required: no): If you knew the employee's title, please provide it.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, number, required: no): Rate your overall satisfaction with a number from 1-10.
