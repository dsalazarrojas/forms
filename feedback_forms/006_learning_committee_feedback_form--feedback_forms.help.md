# Learning Committee Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Learning Committee Feedback Form is a tool for students to provide input on their learning experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter a title in the "form_title" field to describe the feedback you'd like to provide.
2. Provide your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. If applicable, choose a department from the list of options in the "department" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "comment" field.
7. Finally, submit your feedback in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (text, required): A brief title for your feedback, describing what you'd like to provide input on.
* **name** (text, optional): Your name for reference purposes.
* **email** (email, optional): Your email address where you can be reached for follow-up.
* **phone** (text, optional): Your phone number where you can be reached for further discussion.
* **department** (text, optional): The department you're providing feedback for, if applicable.
* **comment** (text, optional): Any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **feedback** (text, optional): Your detailed feedback and suggestions for improvement.
