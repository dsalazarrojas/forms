# Testqual Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the Testqual system. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the quality and user experience of the system. Please take a moment to provide your thoughts, suggestions, and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your feedback in the **Testqual Feedback** field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your feedback in the **Description** field.
3. Select a category that best fits your feedback from the **Category** dropdown menu. Choose from **General**, **Technical**, or **Business**.
4. Type your feedback in the **Feedback** field. Be as detailed and concise as possible.
5. (Optional) If you'd like to provide additional comments, you can do so in the **Category** field (second instance).
6. (Optional) Enter any additional information or details about your feedback in the **Description** field (second instance).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Testqual Feedback** (form_title, text, required): Enter a brief title for your feedback.
* **Description** (form_description, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your feedback, including any context or background information that may be relevant.
* **Category** (form_category, select_one, optional): Select a category that best fits your feedback, such as **General**, **Technical**, or **Business**.
* **Feedback** (form_feedback, text, optional): Type your feedback in this field. Be as detailed and concise as possible.
* **Category** (form_category, text, optional): If you've already selected a category, feel free to ignore this field. If not, provide any additional comments or details about your feedback that may not fit into the previous category selection.
* **Description** (form_description, text, optional): If you've already provided a description, feel free to ignore this field. If not, enter any additional information or details about your feedback that may not fit into the previous description.
