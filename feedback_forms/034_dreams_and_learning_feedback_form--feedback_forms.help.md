# Dreams and Learning Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dreams and Learning Feedback Form is used to collect information about users' dreams and learning experiences. This form helps us understand what users enjoy, dislike, and what could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Start on the first page and work your way down.
3. Answer each question as thoroughly as you can.
4. Use the available space to provide any additional information that might be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating** (Rating, number, required): Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Dream Topic** (Dream Topic, text, required): Describe the topic or subject of your dream or learning experience.
* **Learning Experience** (Learning Experience, text, required): Share your thoughts about your learning experience. What did you enjoy? What was challenging?
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required): Provide any feedback or comments about your experience. What did you like or dislike about it?
* **Suggestions** (Suggestions, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas for how we could improve the program or activity, please share them here.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, please provide your email or other contact information.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): If you'd like to provide your email address for future communication.
* **Submit** (Submit, note, required): Click the submit button to send your feedback form.

Note: All fields are required unless marked as optional. Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
