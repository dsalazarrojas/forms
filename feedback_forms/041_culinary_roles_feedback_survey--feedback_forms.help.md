# CulinaryRolesFeedbackSurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Roles Feedback Survey aims to gather feedback from individuals with a culinary background, focusing on their experience and perception of our services. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve and tailor our offerings to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each field and provide your honest feedback. The form consists of four sections:

1.  **What is your current culinary background?** (1st page)
   - This section is optional. Please enter your current position, if any, to help us understand your perspective.

2.  **What is your current position in the kitchen?** (Select Multiple, 2nd page)
   - Select all that apply to your current role in the kitchen. You can pick one or more from the list.
   - This section is also optional.

3.  **How many years of experience do you have in the culinary industry?** (Number, 3rd page)
   - Please enter a valid number to indicate your level of experience.
   - This field is optional.

4.  **Feedback** (Note, 4th page)
   - Share your thoughts about our services using this text area.
   - Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
   - This field is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
The following is a detailed explanation of each field:

*   **What is your current culinary background?** (`surveyintroduction`, `text`, Optional)
    - This is a space for you to share your current position or background in the culinary industry. If you are a student, this could be your current course of study or a relevant apprenticeship.
*   **What is your current position in the kitchen?** (`positioninrestaurant`, `select_multiple`, Optional)
    - Choose your current role in the kitchen from the list provided. You can select one or more as applicable.
*   **How many years of experience do you have in the culinary industry?** (`experienceyears`, `number`, Optional)
    - Enter a valid number to indicate your years of experience in the culinary industry.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, Optional)
    - Share your thoughts, opinions, and feedback about our services. Be as detailed as possible and specify areas of improvement or praise.
    - Please keep in mind that this field is for feedback, so feel free to share any suggestions or comments you have about your experience with us.
