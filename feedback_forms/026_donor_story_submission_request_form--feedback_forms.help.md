# Donor Story Submission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your donation experience, allowing us to share your story with our community and inspire others to give back.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information, including:
	* Your name and email address
	* A title and description of your story
	* The text of your story
	* Any feedback you may have
	* The date and time of your donation
	* Your time zone
	* Whether you would like to submit your story with certain forms
	* If you have any additional information to share
2. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name**: This is where you will enter your name as a donor.
* **Donor Email**: This is your email address, which we will use to contact you about your submission.
* **Story Title**: This is a brief title for your story, which will be used on our website.
* **Story Text**: This is the main content of your story. Please share as much detail as you like about your experience.
* **Feedback**: If you have any feedback or suggestions about our organization, this is the place to share them.
* **Date/Time**: This is where you can enter the date and time of your donation.
* **Time Zone**: Please select the time zone in which you made your donation.
* **Submission Form**: Please select which form you would like your story to be submitted with.
* **Submission Form Options**: If you would like to include additional options with your submission form, please select from the dropdown list.
* **Additional Info**: Select if you have additional information to share about your story.
* **Additional Info Options**: If you selected "yes" in the previous field, please select from the dropdown list.
* **Assigned Form Label**: This is a placeholder for the form that will be assigned to you based on your submission.
* **Form Label**: This field will be filled in by the system automatically once a form is assigned to you.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your submission carefully before clicking "submit" to ensure that all the information is correct.
* If you have any trouble submitting this form, please contact us for assistance.
* If you are a returning user, please log in to access your existing submissions and edit them if needed.
