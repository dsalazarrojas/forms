# Charity Event Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Charity Event Feedback Survey Form, a helpful tool for charity organizations to gather valuable insights from attendees. By completing this form, you'll be contributing to the improvement of future charity events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address (optional).
2. Specify the name of the charity event you attended.
3. Choose the date you attended the event.
4. Rate your overall experience, from Excellent to Poor.
5. Evaluate how well the event was organized, from Very well organized to Very poorly organized.
6. Rate the venue, from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
7. Assess the quality of the program content, from Excellent to Poor.
8. Evaluate the networking opportunities, from Excellent to Poor.
9. Rate the quality of food and beverages, from Excellent to Poor, or Not provided if not applicable.
10. Assess the value for time and money, from Excellent value to Very poor value.
11. Highlight the best aspect of the event.
12. Suggest any improvements for future events.
13. Indicate if you would attend the event again, from Definitely yes to Definitely no.
14. State how likely you are to recommend the event to others, from Very likely to Very unlikely.
15. Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address (Optional)**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address.
* **Event Name**: Specify the name of the charity event you attended.
* **Event Date**: Choose the date you attended the event.
* **Overall Experience Rating**: Rate your overall experience, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Event Organization**: Evaluate how well the event was organized, from Very well organized to Very poorly organized.
* **Venue Satisfaction**: Rate the quality of the venue, from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
* **Program Content Quality**: Assess the quality of the program content, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Networking Opportunities**: Evaluate the networking opportunities, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Food and Beverage**: Rate the quality of food and beverages, from Excellent to Not provided.
* **Value for Time and Money**: Assess the value for time and money, from Excellent value to Very poor value.
* **Best Aspect**: Highlight the best aspect of the event.
* **Improvement Needed**: Suggest any improvements for future events.
* **Attending Again**: Indicate if you would attend the event again, from Definitely yes to Definitely no.
* **Recommendation Likelihood**: State how likely you are to recommend the event to others, from Very likely to Very unlikely.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Tips
* Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to provide valuable feedback.
* Be specific and concrete when providing suggestions for improvements.
* If you attended the event multiple times, rate your overall experience based on your last attendance.
