# mcc_feedback_committee - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mcc_feedback_committee form is designed to collect feedback from users regarding their experiences and suggestions. It is used by the MCC (MCC Feedback Committee) to gather valuable insights and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your feedback in the `Feedback` field, which is a required text field that asks for your thoughts, opinions, or ideas.
2. Select one or more options from the "Choose" field using the `select_one` and `select_multiple` fields. You will find three options to choose from, and you must select at least one option for "Choose" and at least one option for "Select".
3. If you have a specific date and time you would like to reference in your feedback, enter it in the `Date/Time` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `Email` field, which is optional but recommended.
5. Enter a date in the `Date` field, which is optional but recommended.
6. Enter a time in the `Time` field, which is optional but recommended.
7. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the `Note` field.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Enter your thoughts, opinions, or ideas about your experience.
* **Email** (`feedback_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further clarification.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, required): Enter a date and time you would like to reference in your feedback.
* **Choose** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Select one option from the list to provide more specific feedback.
* **Select** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more options from the list to provide more specific feedback.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a date if you have any specific date-related feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter a time if you have any specific time-related feedback.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you have.
