# E Commerce Chatbot Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Commerce Chatbot Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users who have interacted with the E Commerce chatbot. This feedback is crucial for improving the chatbot's performance and user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email on the first page.
2. Rate your experience with the chatbot.
3. Provide comments or feedback about your experience (optional).
4. If you have additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them on the second page.
5. Choose why you didn't answer or provide additional comments (optional).
6. Add any additional comments or feedback (optional).
7. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`form_first_page_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to help us identify you.
* **Last Name** (`form_first_page_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name to help us identify you.
* **Email** (`form_first_page_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Rating** (`form_first_page_rating`, number, required): Rate your experience with the chatbot, from 1-5 stars.
* **Comments** (`form_first_page_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or feedback about your experience, please provide them here.
* **What would you improve?** (`form_second_page_what_would_you_improve`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve the chatbot, please provide them here.
* **Why didn't you answer?** (`form_second_page_why_didnt_answer`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more reasons why you didn't answer or complete the chatbot.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_second_page_additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional feedback or comments you have about your experience.
* **Submitted** (`form_second_page_submitted`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in and displays the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Time** (`form_second_page_submitted_time`, time, optional): This field is automatically filled in and displays the time you submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`form_second_page_submitted_date`, date, optional): This field is automatically filled in and displays the date you submitted the form.
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required): Press this button to submit your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* We appreciate your feedback and will review it to improve the chatbot's performance.
