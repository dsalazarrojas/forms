# Publishing Experience Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the publishing experience from users who have undergone a review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question based on your actual experience with the review process.
2. For multiple-choice questions (questions with options), select the number that best represents your answer.
3. For text fields (questions without options), provide a brief description of your experience.
4. Please answer all the questions, as they are optional (except for any questions marked as required).
5. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How clear was the review process** (<process_clarity>): This question asks about the clarity of the review process. Please select a number that best represents how clear the process was for you.
* **How was the communication during the review process** (<communication>): This question is about the quality of communication during the review process. Select a number that best represents how you felt about the communication.
* **How much support was provided during the review process** (<support>): This question is about the level of support you received during the review process. Select a number that best represents how much support you felt you received.
* **Overall experience with the review process** (<overall_experience>): This question asks about your overall experience with the review process. Select a number that best represents your experience.
* **Comments about the review process** (<comments>): This question is an open-ended text field where you can provide any comments or thoughts you have about the review process.
* **Suggestion for improving the review process** (<suggestions>): This question is also an open-ended text field where you can provide any suggestions you have for improving the review process.
* **Additional comments** (<additional_comments>): This question is another open-ended text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts you have.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your answers.
* Keep your answers concise and to the point.
* Please provide specific examples or details when answering questions that ask for open-ended responses.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please make sure to include them in the corresponding fields.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
