# Substitute Teacher Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Substitute Teacher Feedback Form is a tool for students or others to provide feedback about their experience with a substitute teacher. This feedback is intended to help substitute teachers improve their performance and better understand the needs of their students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Substitute Teacher` field with the name of the teacher you are providing feedback about.
2. Select the rating that best describes your experience with the teacher.
3. Provide any additional feedback about your experience in the `Additional Feedback` field.
4. Select the options that best describe the teacher's lesson plan, materials, classroom environment, student behavior, and other aspects of their performance.
5. Select the overall rating for the teacher's performance.
6. Indicate whether the teacher's availability and communication were satisfactory.
7. Check if you would like to provide feedback to the principal about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Substitute Teacher** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the teacher you are providing feedback about.
- **Rating** (`teacher_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the rating that best describes your experience with the teacher. Options include:
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience with the teacher.
- **Lesson Plan Provided** (`lesson_plan`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the options that best describe the quality of the lesson plan provided by the teacher. Options include:
  - True
  - False
- **Materials Provided** (`materials`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the options that best describe the quality of the materials provided by the teacher. Options include:
  - True
  - False
- **Classroom Environment** (`classroom_environment`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the options that best describe the classroom environment during your experience with the teacher. Options include:
  - Good
  - Neutral
  - Poor
- **Student Behavior** (`student_behavior`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the options that best describe the student behavior during your experience with the teacher. Options include:
  - Good
  - Neutral
  - Poor
- **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the overall rating for the teacher's performance. Options include:
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Teacher Availability** (`teacher_availability`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the options that best describe the teacher's availability during your experience. Options include:
  - Good
  - Neutral
  - Poor
- **Communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the options that best describe the teacher's communication during your experience. Options include:
  - Good
  - Neutral
  - Poor
- **Feedback to Principal** (`feedback_to_principal`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if you would like to provide feedback to the principal about your experience. Options include:
  - True
  - False

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your feedback to help the teacher improve.
- Select the options that best describe your experience, even if it may not be what you expected.
- If you are unsure about an option, choose the one that best fits the situation.
- Remember to give credit to the creator of this form if you decide to modify or sell it.
