## Business Accelerator Program Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

### Purpose
This questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from participants of the Business Accelerator Program. Your responses will help us improve the program and better serve future entrepreneurs.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each section. You can refer to the hints provided for each field for guidance.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

#### Participant Information (1)
* **Participant Information (1)** (Not Required): This is a brief overview of who you are and your background.

#### Full Name (2)
* **Full Name (2)** (Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents.

#### Company Name (3)
* **Company Name (3)** (Required): Please enter the name of your startup or business.

#### Program Cohort (4)
* **Program Cohort (4)** (Required): Select the cohort you participated in.

#### Program Duration (5)
* **Program Duration (5)** (Required): Select the length of your participation in the program.

#### Industry (6)
* **Industry (6)** (Required): Select the industry related to your business.

#### Program Content (7)
* **Program Content (7)** (Not Required): This is a free text field for your feedback on the program content.

#### Content Quality (8)
* **Content Quality (8)** (Required): Rate the quality of the program content.

#### Content Relevance (9)
* **Content Relevance (9)** (Required): Rate how relevant the content was for your business.

#### Curriculum Rating (10)
* **Curriculum Rating (10)** (Required): Rate the overall curriculum.

#### Mentorship (11)
* **Mentorship (11)** (Not Required): This is a free text field for your feedback on mentorship.

#### Mentor Quality (12)
* **Mentor Quality (12)** (Required): Rate the quality of the mentors.

#### Mentor Availability (13)
* **Mentor Availability (13)** (Required): Rate how available the mentors were.

#### Mentorship Impact (14)
* **Mentorship Impact (14)** (Required): Rate the impact of the mentorship.

#### Resources and Support (15)
* **Resources and Support (15)** (Not Required): This is a free text field for your feedback on resources provided.

#### Resource Quality (16)
* **Resource Quality (16)** (Required): Rate the quality of resources.

#### Networking Opportunities (17)
* **Networking Opportunities (17)** (Required): Rate the networking opportunities.

#### Facilities and Infrastructure (18)
* **Facilities and Infrastructure (18)** (Required): Rate the physical or virtual facilities.

#### Program Outcomes (19)
* **Program Outcomes (19)** (Not Required): This is a free text field for your results from the program.

#### Business Growth (20)
* **Business Growth (20)** (Required): Select how your business has grown.

#### Funding Secured (21)
* **Funding Secured (21)** (Required): Select if you secured funding through the program.

#### Skills Gained (22)
* **Skills Gained (22)** (Required): Select the skills you gained from the program.

#### Would You Recommend This Program (23)
* **Would You Recommend This Program (23)** (Required): Select if you would recommend the program to other entrepreneurs.

#### Overall Satisfaction (24)
* **Overall Satisfaction (24)** (Required): Select your overall satisfaction with the program.

#### Suggested Improvements (25)
* **Suggested Improvements (25)** (Not Required): This is a free text field for your suggestions on how the program can be improved.

#### Additional Comments (26)
* **Additional Comments (26)** (Not Required): This is a free text field for any other feedback or comments.

### Tips
Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, and rate your answers based on your personal experience with the program.
