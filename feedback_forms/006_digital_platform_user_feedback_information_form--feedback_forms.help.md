# Digital Platform User Feedback Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and suggestions about our digital platform. Your input will help us improve the platform's features, performance, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Choose the relevant options for each field.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Feedback (1)** (`<type>`: text, **required**: true): This field is where you can type in any comments or suggestions you have about your experience with our platform. Please be specific and detailed in your feedback.
* **Overall Rating (2)** (`<type>`: number, **required**: true): This field is where you can rate your overall experience with our platform, from 0 to 10. 
* **Would you recommend this platform? (3)** (`<type>`: select_one, **required**: false): Choose 'Yes' if you would recommend our platform to others, or 'No' if not.
* **Platform Name (4)** (`<type>`: text, **required**: false): This field is for you to mention the name of the platform you are using or referring to.
* **Platform Category (5)** (`<type>`: select_one, **required**: true): Choose the category that best describes the platform you are interacting with.
* **Contact Method (6)** (`<type>`: select_multiple, **required**: true): Select all the methods by which you would like to be contacted (e.g. email, phone, or in-app).
* **Platform Features (7)** (`<type>`: text, **required**: false): This field is where you can list any specific features of the platform that you'd like to see improved or added.
* **Additional Feedback (8)** (`<type>`: note, **required**: false): This field is a place for you to add any extra comments or details that are not covered in the previous fields.
* **Contact Us (9)** (`<type>`: email, **required**: false): This field is for you to provide your contact information (e.g., email address).
* **User ID (10)** (`<type>`: number, **required**: true): Please enter your unique User ID that we use to track your interactions with our platform.
* **Timestamp (11)** (`<type>`: date, **required**: false): This field is automatically populated and is not editable.

Tips:
- Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for clarification.
- Your input is invaluable, and we appreciate your participation!
