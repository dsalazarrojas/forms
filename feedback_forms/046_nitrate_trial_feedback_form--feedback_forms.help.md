# Nitrate Trial Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trial participants on their experiences during a trial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Trial Participant" field with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide feedback in the "Feedback" field.
3. Enter the "Trial Location" where the trial took place.
4. Select the "Trial Date" when the trial occurred.
5. Rate your experience using the "Rating" field (out of 10).
6. Optionally, provide the duration of the trial in hours in the "Trial Duration" field.
7. Enter any additional feedback or notes about the trial in the "Feedback Details" field.
8. Provide any additional notes or comments about the trial in the "Trial Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trial Participant** (`trial_participant`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Trial Location** (`trial_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the trial took place.
* **Trial Date** (`trial_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the trial occurred.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your experience (out of 10).
* **Trial Duration** (`trial_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the trial in hours.
* **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, text, required): Provide additional feedback or notes about the trial.
* **Trial Notes** (`trial_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the trial.
