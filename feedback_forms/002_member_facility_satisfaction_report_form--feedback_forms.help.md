# Member Facility Satisfaction Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from members on their experience at a facility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Email.
3. Enter the Facility Name.
4. Select the Date Visited.
5. Choose your Satisfaction Level.
6. Select multiple options for Service Quality and Cleanliness.
7. Choose a level for Staff Friendliness.
8. Choose a level for Facilities.
9. Select whether the parking was available.
10. Enter your Overall Satisfaction rating.
11. Provide any Additional Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name, text, required)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Facility Name (Facility Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the facility you visited.
* **Date Visited (Date Visited, date, required)**: Select the date you visited the facility.
* **Satisfaction Level (Satisfaction Level, select_one, optional)**: Choose a level that best describes your experience.
* **Service Quality (Service Quality, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all the options that apply to your experience.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Cleanliness (Cleanliness, select_multiple, optional)**: Select all the options that apply to your experience.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Staff Friendliness (Staff Friendliness, select_one, optional)**: Choose a level that best describes your experience.
* **Facilities (Facilities, select_one, optional)**: Choose a level that best describes your experience.
* **Parking (Parking, select_one, optional)**: Select whether the parking was available.
* **Overall Satisfaction (Overall Satisfaction, number, optional)**: Enter your rating on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, note, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
