# psychiatry feedback form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their experience with our psychiatric services. Your input will help us understand how to improve our care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Age (if applicable).
4. Enter a valid Email (we'll use it to contact you if needed).
5. Enter your Phone Number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): If you're 18 or older, please enter your age for demographic purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid Email address we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you via phone.

## Tips
* Please fill in your feedback as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* You can contact us at the provided email if you have any questions or need assistance with completing this form.
