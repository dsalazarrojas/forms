# Subscription Cancellation Offboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on your subscription cancellation experience to help us understand why you decided to cancel and to improve our services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in sections, saving your progress if needed.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing detailed and honest feedback.
- If you need help, you can contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your reasons for canceling your subscription.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address you used for your subscription.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (optional, but helps us contact you for more information if needed).
* **Reasons** (`reason`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more options that best describe your reasons for canceling (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Please provide a rating of your experience with our service (1-5 stars).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name (optional, but helps us contact you for more information if needed).
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company name (optional, but helps us understand your business context).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title (optional, but helps us understand your role in your company).

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide detailed feedback.
- You can add or remove items from each field as you see fit.
- This form will help us improve our services for future subscribers.
