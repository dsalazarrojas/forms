# Insurance Customer Satisfaction Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our team, providing us with valuable insights to improve our services. Please ensure that you're not submitting duplicate feedback, and that your comments are directly related to your interaction with our team.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form and ensure you're filling it out for the correct purpose.
2.  Ensure your feedback is concise and clear, providing us with actionable insights to improve our services.
3.  Be as specific as possible when describing your experience.
4.  If you have any attachments or evidence to support your feedback, you may attach them separately and reference them in your feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Policyholder Feedback** (`policyholder_feedback`, text, required): This is the main feedback field where you can provide a detailed description of your experience. Please be as specific as possible, including any positive or negative comments about your interaction with our team.
*   **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, select_one, required): Please select 'Yes' if the issue you're reporting is related to your insurance company's policy or service. If this doesn't apply, select 'No'.
*   **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, number, optional): If you have a policy number, please provide it to help us identify the specific policy associated with your feedback. This is an optional field, but it will help us to provide more efficient support.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth to help us confirm your identity and to ensure that your feedback is genuine.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): This field is where you can provide a detailed description of your experience, including any positive or negative comments about your interaction with our team.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to provide your email address, we'll be able to respond to your feedback and keep you updated about any actions taken regarding your feedback.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number, we'll be able to contact you directly and discuss your feedback in more detail.

## Tips

*   Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please ask a member of our team for assistance.
*   Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our services.
