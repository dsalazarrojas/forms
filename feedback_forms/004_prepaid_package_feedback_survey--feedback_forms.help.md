# Prepaid Package Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a prepaid package. Your feedback will help us understand your satisfaction with the package and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title and introduction section (Field 1: form_title).
2. Rate our service and provide feedback in the rating and feedback section (Fields 2-8).
3. Provide your contact information if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions (Fields 9-10).
4. Answer the rating and feedback questions (Fields 11-22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Field 1: form_title**: Type your name to identify you as the person who completed the survey.
* **Field 2: rating**: Rate our service on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Highly satisfied
* **Field 3: purchase_reason**: Select the main reason you purchased this package.
	+ Highly satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Not satisfied
* **Field 4: suggestions**: Type any suggestions or comments you have about our service.
* **Field 5: contact_information**: Select whether you want to be contacted for follow-up questions.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Field 6: email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Field 7: phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Field 8: rating2**: Rate our package on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Highly satisfied
* **Field 9: rating2_label**: Explain your rating for Field 8.
* **Field 10: rating2_reason**: Type the reason for your rating in Field 8.
* **Field 11: rating3**: Rate how likely you are to purchase again.
	+ 1: Very unlikely
	+ 2: Somewhat unlikely
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat likely
	+ 5: Very likely
* **Field 12: rating3_label**: Explain your rating for Field 11.
* **Field 13: rating3_reason**: Type the reason for your rating in Field 11.
* **Field 14: rating4**: Rate how likely you are to recommend our service.
	+ 1: Very unlikely
	+ 2: Somewhat unlikely
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat likely
	+ 5: Very likely
* **Field 15: rating4_label**: Explain your rating for Field 14.
* **Field 16: rating4_reason**: Type the reason for your rating in Field 14.
* **Field 17: rating5**: Rate the quality of our support.
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Somewhat poor
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat good
	+ 5: Very good
* **Field 18: rating5_label**: Explain your rating for Field 17.
* **Field 19: rating5_reason**: Type the reason for your rating in Field 17.
* **Field 20: rating6**: Rate how likely you are to purchase from us again.
	+ 1: Very unlikely
	+ 2: Somewhat unlikely
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat likely
	+ 5: Very likely
* **Field 21: rating6_label**: Explain your rating for Field 20.
* **Field 22: rating6_reason**: Type the reason for your rating in Field 20.
* **Field 23: rating7**: Rate the quality of our support.
	+ 1: Very poor
