# Leadership Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on leadership performance from employees. This form is a platform for open communication, allowing employees to express their thoughts and suggestions on how their leaders can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name.
2. Select the role of the leadership team member you are providing feedback on.
3. Describe the specific feedback question or area of improvement.
4. Provide additional feedback on the feedback question.
5. (Optional) Select the name(s) of the person(s) who submitted the feedback.
6. Enter the date when the feedback was given, and the time it was given.
7. Add your email address (optional).
8. Add your phone number (optional).
9. Add any additional note or comment.
10. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your employee name to associate with your feedback.
* **Role** (select one, optional): Select the role of the leadership team member you are providing feedback on (CEO, Manager, Team Member, Intern, Other).
* **Feedback Question** (text, optional): Describe the specific feedback question or area of improvement.
* **Feedback** (text, optional): Provide additional feedback on the feedback question.
* **Date Given** (date, required): Enter the date when the feedback was given.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time when the feedback was given.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional note or comment.
* **Submitted By** (select multiple, optional): Select the name(s) of the person(s) who submitted the feedback (John, Jane, Bob).
* **Submitted By Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the feedback (optional).
* **Submitted By Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the feedback (optional).
* **Created** (date, required): This field is automatically generated and displays the date the form was created.
* **Updated** (date, required): This field is automatically generated and displays the date the form was last updated.
* **Submitted** (date, required): This field is automatically generated and displays the date the form was submitted.
