# Superhero Theme Song Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for you to provide feedback on a superhero theme song. We're interested in hearing your thoughts on its title, description, genre, and other aspects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your thoughts and opinions on the superhero theme song.
2. Answer the questions and provide any additional comments or information you think are relevant.
3. If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask and we'll be happy to clarify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Superhero Form Questions**: This section is for general questions and comments about the superhero theme song.
  - **Superhero Theme Song**: Please enter the title of the superhero theme song you're commenting on.
  - **Song Title**: Enter a short description of the song, e.g., its genre, mood, or any notable lyrics.
  - **Song Genre**: Select the genre of the song (e.g., rock, pop, jazz).
  - **Liked**: Choose how much you liked the song (e.g., Song Title, Other).
  - **Song Title (if Other)**: If you chose "Other" for Liked, enter a brief description of why you liked the song.
  - **Superhero Score**: Rate how well the song matches the theme of a superhero (on a scale from 0 to 10).
  - **Superhero Score Other**: If you have any other comments or reasons for your score, enter them here.
  - **Submit**: Once you've filled out all the fields, click this button to submit your feedback.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be called about your feedback.
  - **Comments**: If you have any general comments about the song, enter them here.
  - **Comments Other**: If you chose "Other" for Comments, provide any additional information here.
  - **Theme**: Select the theme of the song (e.g., action, adventure).
  - **Theme Other**: If you chose "Other" for Theme, enter a brief description of what you think the theme is.
  - **Theme Other Other**: If you have any additional information, enter it here.
  - **Song**: Enter any other song-related information you think is relevant.
  - **Song Other**: If you chose "Other" for Song, enter a brief description of why you think it's relevant.
  - **Song Other Other**: If you have any additional information, enter it here.
  - **Submit 2**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
  - **Email**: Your email address will be used to contact you if necessary.
  - **Phone**: Your phone number will be used to contact you if necessary.
  - **Comments**: General comments about the song.
  - **Comments Other**: Additional comments about why you chose "Other" for Comments.
  - **Theme**: Theme of the song.

  - **Song**: Enter any other song information you think is relevant.
  - **Song Other**: If you chose "Other" for Song, enter a brief description of why.
  - **Song Other Other**: Any additional information about the song.
  - **Submit 2**: Submit your feedback.
