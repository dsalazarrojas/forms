# group_therapy_client_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The group_therapy_client_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback from clients who have attended group therapy sessions. It helps us understand their overall satisfaction with the therapy process, their rating of the therapist, and their progress in achieving their goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure you have attended at least one session and have a clear understanding of your goals and expectations from the therapy process.
3. Fill out the form on one page at a time, using the page numbers to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required: `false`): This is the title of the form, which is displayed at the top of the page. You can ignore this field, as it is not a required field.
* **Group_therapy_client_feedback_form** (`name`, `text`, required: `false`): This is the page title, which is displayed at the top of each page. You can ignore this field, as it is not a required field.
* **Therapist rating** (`therapist`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your therapist on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **How many sessions have you attended?** (`sessions`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the number of sessions you have attended.
* **Overall satisfaction rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the therapy process on a scale of 1-10.
* **Goals for therapy** (`goals`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your goals for attending therapy.
* **Goals achieved** (`goals_achieved`, `number`, required: `false`): Indicate how many of your goals you have achieved so far.
* **Why did you achieve these goals?** (`goals_achieved_why`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide a brief explanation of how you achieved your goals.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: `false`): Share any suggestions you have for how we can improve our services.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: `false`): Add any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you if we need more information.

Note: This form is designed to be completed in a way that is helpful and constructive, without any negative or critical tone.
