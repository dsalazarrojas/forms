# Online Course Completion Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Completion Evaluation Form is used to collect feedback from students about their completion of an online course. It's a way for students to provide their opinions and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your university/college records.
2. **Course ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to you for the course.
3. **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you completed.
4. **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date you completed the course.
5. **Evaluation Time**: Enter the time you took to complete the course.
6. **Overall Satisfaction**: Choose your level of satisfaction with the course:
	* Excellent: The course exceeded your expectations.
	* Good: The course was satisfactory.
	* Fair: The course was adequate.
	* Poor: The course did not meet your expectations.
7. **Course Materials**: Choose the quality of the course materials provided:
	* Course materials were adequate
	* Course materials were excellent
	* Course materials were poor
	* Course materials were fair
8. **Course Format**: Choose the format of the course:
	* Online
	* On-site
	* Hybrid
9. **Course Duration**: Enter the length of time you spent on the course.
10. **Additional Feedback**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the course.
11. **Student Comment**: Provide any comments or thoughts about your experience with the course.
12. **Course Instructors**: Choose the quality of the instructors:
	* Instructors were helpful
	* Instructors were adequate
	* Instructors were poor
	* Instructors were not helpful
13. **Course Support**: Choose the quality of the support provided:
	* Support was helpful
	* Support was adequate
	* Support was poor
	* Support was not helpful

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your university/college records.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID number assigned to you for the course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the course you completed.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you took to complete the course.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select one, required/optional): Choose your level of satisfaction with the course.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose the quality of the course materials provided.
* **Course Format** (`course_format`, select one, required/optional): Choose the format of the course.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of time you spent on the course.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have about the course.
* **Student Comment** (`student_comment`, text, required/optional): Provide any comments or thoughts about your experience with the course.
* **Course Instructors** (`course_instructors`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose the quality of the instructors.
* **Course Support** (`course_support`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose the quality of the support provided.
