# Post Session Reflection Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and reflections from users after a session. It is essential to provide clear and accurate information to fill out this form correctly to ensure that the feedback is helpful and relevant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Please answer all the questions that are marked as "required."
2.  You can skip questions that are marked as "optional" if you don't want to provide an answer.
3.  Fill in your answers in the designated text boxes.
4.  Choose the appropriate options for the "Session Satisfaction" question.
5.  Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Reflection** (`session_reflection`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your thoughts and reflections about the session.
* **Session Satisfaction** (`rating`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Very Good: The session met or exceeded my expectations.
	+ Good: The session met some of my expectations.
	+ Fair: The session met some of my expectations, but with some issues.
	+ Poor: The session did not meet my expectations.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: true): Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `number`, required: false): Please rate your experience on the session, using a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional comments about the session.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the next steps you plan to take as a result of the session.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any suggestions for improvement for future sessions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to provide the best possible feedback.
* Please proofread your answers carefully to ensure that they are accurate and clear.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us so we can assist you further.
