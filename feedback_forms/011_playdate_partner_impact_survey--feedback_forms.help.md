# Playdate Partner Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and impact data from our partners on their experience with our playdate program. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve to create a better partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all the questions in the fields below. You can type your answers in the text boxes provided.
2. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them with us in the "Suggestions for improvement" field.
3. Ensure that your email and phone number (if provided) are accurate and up-to-date.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Playdate Partner Impact Survey Form**:
	+ This is the main section of the form where you will provide your feedback and answers to our questions.
* **School Name**:
	+ Please enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Feedback**:
	+ Share your thoughts, concerns, and suggestions about your experience with our playdate program.
* **Playdate partnership outcomes**:
	+ Describe the positive outcomes you've experienced as a result of the playdate partnership.
* **Suggestions for improvement**:
	+ Share your suggestions on how we can improve our playdate partnership.
* **School contact info**:
	+ Please provide the contact information for your school or institution.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**:
	+ Provide your phone number for us to get in touch with you.
* **Submitted on**:
	+ This field will be automatically populated with the date you submit the form.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your feedback to help us create a better partnership.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Please ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* We value your input and appreciate your time in completing this form.
