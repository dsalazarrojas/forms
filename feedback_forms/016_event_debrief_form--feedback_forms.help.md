# Event Debrief Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Event Debrief Form is designed to collect detailed information and feedback about events, such as meetings, workshops, or conferences. This form helps to identify areas for improvement and provide insights for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide the main purpose of the event**: In the first field, "What was the purpose of the event?", describe the overall goal or objective of the event.
2. **Enter the event date**: In the second field, "When did the event take place?", enter the date when the event occurred.
3. **Enter the venue**: In the third field, "Where was the event held?", enter the location of the event.
4. **Specify the duration**: In the fourth field, "What was the duration of the event?", provide the length of time the event lasted.
5. **Name the debriefer**: In the fifth field, "Who was the debriefer?", enter the name of the person who conducted the debriefing process.
6. **Reason for debriefing**: In the sixth field, "What was the reason for debriefing the event?", describe the motivation behind debriefing the event.
7. **Rate your experience**: In the seventh field, "How did you find the debriefing process?", select one of the four options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
8. **Suggest improvements**: In the eighth field, "Any suggestions for improvement?", provide any ideas you have for improving the debriefing process.
9. **Share concerns**: In the ninth field, "Did you have any concerns during the debriefing process?", describe any issues you faced during the debriefing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the purpose of the event?** (`event_debrief_form_1`, text, required): This field is for describing the overall goal or objective of the event.
* **When did the event take place?** (`event_debrief_form_2`, date, required): Enter the date when the event occurred.
* **Where was the event held?** (`event_debrief_form_3`, text, required): Specify the location of the event.
* **What was the duration of the event?** (`event_debrief_form_4`, number, required): Provide the length of time the event lasted in hours or minutes.
* **Who was the debriefer?** (`event_debrief_form_5`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who conducted the debriefing process.
* **What was the reason for debriefing the event?** (`event_debrief_form_6`, text, required): Describe the motivation behind debriefing the event.
* **How did you find the debriefing process?** (`event_debrief_form_7`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with the debriefing process (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Any suggestions for improvement?** (`event_debrief_form_8`, text, optional): Provide any ideas you have for improving the debriefing process.
* **Did you have any concerns during the debriefing process?** (`event_debrief_form_9`, text, optional): Describe any issues you faced during the debriefing process.
