# team_collaboration_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team collaboration feedback questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from team members on their experiences and perceptions of team collaboration. This form is used to assess the effectiveness of collaboration practices, identify areas for improvement, and inform changes to team dynamics.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Choose the options that best match your experiences and perceptions.
3. Provide specific examples or comments where possible.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Collaboration Feedback Questionnaire** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is the title of the form, which is meant to introduce the purpose and scope of the form.
* **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose one of the options that best describes your collaboration frequency:
		- Collaboration is a core part of my role
		- Collaboration is a minor part of my role
		- I do not collaborate much
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose all applicable communication channels used for collaboration:
		- Email
		- Chat
		- Phone
		- Video conference
		- In-person
		- Other (please specify)
* **Collaboration Tools** (`collaboration_tools`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose all collaboration tools used by your team:
		- Trello
		- Asana
		- Slack
		- Microsoft Teams
		- Other (please specify)
* **Collaboration Barriers** (`collaboration_barriers`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Identify any collaboration barriers you experience:
		- Lack of clear goals and objectives
		- Limited time or resources
		- Technical issues
		- Communication barriers
		- Lack of feedback and accountability
		- Other (please specify)
* **Communication Styles** (`communication_styles`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose your preferred communication styles:
		- Direct and assertive
		- Indirect and collaborative
		- Indirect and passive
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, `number`, required: false):
	+ Provide a numerical value for the frequency of feedback you receive:
		- (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
* **Feedback Methods** (`feedback_methods`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose all feedback methods used by your team:
		- Regular one-on-one check-ins
		- Regular team meetings
		- Ad-hoc meetings as needed
		- Other (please specify)
* **Feedback Recipients** (`feedback_recipients`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Identify who receives feedback from you:
		- Team lead/manager
		- Peers
		- Subordinate(s)
		- Other (please specify)
* **Collaboration Outcomes** (`collaboration_outcomes`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose the benefits you experience from collaboration:
		- Improved team collaboration and productivity
		- Enhanced communication and teamwork
		- Increased employee engagement and motivation
		- Other (please specify)

Note: Please submit your feedback honestly and accurately, and remember that this form is for your team's benefit to improve collaboration and communication.
