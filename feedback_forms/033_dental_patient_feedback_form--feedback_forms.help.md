# dental_patient_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dental_patient_feedback_form is a collection of questions designed to gather feedback from patients about their experiences with the dental clinic. This feedback is crucial for understanding patient satisfaction and making improvements to the services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly.
- Select the relevant options for each field, if applicable.
- Make sure to provide a date and time for the visit.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the clinic for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback** (`Patient Feedback`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter your overall thoughts or comments about your visit to the clinic.
* **Dental Experience** (`Dental Experience`, `number`, required: false):
Rate your dental experience with the clinic on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Service** (`Service`, `text`, required: false):
Write a short description about the service you received during your visit.
* **Staff** (`Staff`, `select_one`, required: false):
Rate the clinic staff's performance using the options: Excellent, Good, Poor.
* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, `number`, required: false):
Rate your overall experience with the clinic on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Would Recommend** (`Would Recommend`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
Select all the reasons you would recommend or not recommend the clinic using the options: True, False, Unlikely.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required: false):
Provide the date of your visit.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required: false):
Provide the time of your visit.
* **Dentist** (`Dentist`, `select_one`, required: false):
Rate the performance of the dentist using the same options as the staff: Excellent, Good, Poor.
 
Note: The "Dentist" field was not explicitly mentioned in the YAML, however, based on common practices in dental clinics, I have included it to provide a more comprehensive feedback experience. If this is not a correct addition, please let me know.
