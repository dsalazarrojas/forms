# Radio Program Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Radio Program Feedback Survey is a tool used to gather information about our radio programs and to gather feedback from users. The information collected will help us improve our programming and better serve our audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question according to your preferences and opinions.
2. Select your favorite radio program from the list provided.
3. If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, type them in the "Suggestions" text field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address, so we can follow up with you.
5. Add any additional feedback or comments in the "Additional Feedback" text field.
6. Choose how you want to submit your feedback: "Submit" to send it in, "Save as Draft" to save it for later, or "Cancel" to discard your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you tune in to our radio programs?** (Select "Always", "Sometimes", "Rarely", or "Never"): Select your preferred frequency of listening.
* **How often do you listen to our radio programs?** (Number): Please type a numerical value indicating how often you listen to our radio programs.
* **What is your favorite program on our radio station?** (Text): Please type the title of your favorite program.
* **Do you have any suggestions on how we could improve our programs?** (Text): Share your ideas and suggestions on how we can improve our radio programs.
* **What is your contact information?** (Email Address): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Additional Feedback** (Text): Add any other thoughts or comments you'd like to share.
* **Submission** (Select One): Choose how you want to submit your feedback: "Submit" to send it in, "Save as Draft" to save it for later, or "Cancel" to discard your changes.
