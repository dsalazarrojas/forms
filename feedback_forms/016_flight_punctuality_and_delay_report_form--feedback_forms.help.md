# Flight Punctuality And Delay Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on flight punctuality and delays for the support team's reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of report you want to submit: "Flight Report" or "Delay Report".
2. Enter the flight number and date.
3. Select the flight status (On Time, Delayed, Cancelled, or Diverted).
4. Choose the reason for the delay (Weather, Mechanical, Air Traffic, Ground Traffic, or Other).
5. Select the support team's involvement (Primary Contact, Secondary Contact, or Support Team).
6. Enter your personal contact information, including your name, company, position, and contact details.
7. Select the time zone and time you are in.
8. Enter any additional comments if needed.
9. Submit the form by selecting "Submit Now".
