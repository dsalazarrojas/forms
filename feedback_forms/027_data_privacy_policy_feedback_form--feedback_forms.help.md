# Data Privacy Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Privacy Policy Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from users about data privacy policies. This form allows users to provide comments, suggestions, or concerns related to data privacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Your Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Your Email** field.
3. Select a category from the **Select an Option** field.
4. Choose as many applicable options from the **Select 2 Options** field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the **Your Date** field.
6. Enter your time of submission in the **Your Time** field.
7. Add any additional comments in the **Note** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Data Privacy Policy Feedback Form** (`form_title`): This is the title of the form, which is used to identify the purpose of the form.
* **Your Name** (`name`): Enter your name to provide a way for us to contact you.
* **Your Email** (`email`): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you back.
* **Select an Option** (`select`): Choose one of the available options to indicate your concern or suggestion.
* **Select 2 Options** (`select_2`): Choose as many applicable options as relevant to your concern or suggestion.
* **Your Date** (`date`): Enter your date of birth.
* **Your Time** (`time`): Enter the time of submission.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional comments or notes related to your feedback.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`): Add any additional comments or notes related to your feedback.
