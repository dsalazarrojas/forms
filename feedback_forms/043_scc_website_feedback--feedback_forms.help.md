# SCC Website Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from end-users about their experience with the SCC website.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select "Yes" or "No" if you have any issues with the website.
2.  Enter a brief comment about your experience with the website.
3.  Rate your satisfaction with the website on a scale of 1-10.
4.  Enter a numerical score for your satisfaction with the website.
5.  Enter any comments or feedback about the website.
6.  Enter your email address.
7.  Enter your phone number.
8.  Enter any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the website.
9.  Enter any other comments or feedback about the website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website**: `scc_website_feedback` (`scc_website_feedback`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to indicate whether you have encountered any issues with the website.
* **Website Comment**: `scc_website_comment` (`scc_website_comment`, `text`, required: true)
	+ This field is a place to enter a brief comment about your experience with the website.
* **Website Rating**: `scc_website_rating` (`scc_website_rating`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to rate your satisfaction with the website on a scale of 1-10.
* **How Satisfied**: `scc_website_score` (`scc_website_score`, `number`, required: false)
	+ This field is optional and allows you to enter a more detailed opinion about your satisfaction with the website.
* **Website Comments**: `scc_website_comments` (`scc_website_comments`, `text`, required: true)
	+ This field is a place to enter any comments or feedback about the website.
* **Email**: `scc_email` (`scc_email`, `email`, required: true)
	+ This field is a required field for us to contact you for further clarification or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone**: `scc_phone` (`scc_phone`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is optional and allows you to enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Website Feedback**: `scc_website_feedback` (`scc_website_feedback`, `text`, required: true)
	+ This field is a place to enter any feedback or suggestions you have for improving the website.
* **Improve**: `scc_website_improve` (`scc_website_improve`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is optional and allows you to enter any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the website.
* **Suggestions**: `scc_website_suggestions` (`scc_website_suggestions`, `text`, required: true)
	+ This field is a place to enter any other comments or feedback about the website.
