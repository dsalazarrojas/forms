# Visitor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from visitors to our site. We appreciate any comments, suggestions, or concerns you may have, as they help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Rate your experience by selecting one of the ratings in the "Rating" field.
5. Provide any feedback or comments in the "Feedback" field.
6. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
7. Once you have completed all the fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (<name>, <type> text, required):** Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (<name>, <type> text, required):** Enter your last name.
* **Feedback (label, <type> text, optional):** Type any comments, suggestions, or concerns you have about your experience on our site.
* **Email (<name>, <type> email, required):** Enter your email address. We will use this email to contact you if necessary.
* **Phone (<name>, <type> text, optional):** If you have a phone number, please enter it.
* **Rating (label, <type> select_one, required):** Select the rating that best describes your experience (1-5).
* **Comment (label, <type> text, optional):** Provide any additional comments or feedback.
 
## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields to help us improve our services.
- Make sure to enter a valid email address.
- Select a rating that reflects your honest opinion.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please let us know in the "Feedback" field.
- If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Comment" field.
