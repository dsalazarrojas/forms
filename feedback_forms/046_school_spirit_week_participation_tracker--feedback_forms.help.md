# School Spirit Week Participation Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the School Spirit Week Participation Tracker form. The form is used to track participants' activities and comments during school spirit week events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct spirit week activity from the dropdown list for each event.
2. Choose your spirit role (Student, Teacher, or Faculty) and select which day of the week you participated.
3. Enter your school name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Optionally, enter comments or feedback on the events.
6. Choose a date and time for your participation.
7. Review and submit the form.
