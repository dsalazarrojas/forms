# moving_crew_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals who have interacted with our moving crew. It includes questions about their experience, such as their name, contact information, and ratings for our service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Type in your Email address.
4. Provide your Phone number.
5. If applicable, enter the date of your move.
6. If applicable, select the time of your move.
7. If you are representing a company, please enter the name of the company.
8. Share any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
9. Rate your experience with the moving crew using the provided scale (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
10. If you have any additional comments or insights, feel free to type them in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1) (Text, Required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (2) (Text, Required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (3) (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (4) (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Move Date** (5) (Date, Optional): If you have a specific date for your move, enter it here.
* **Move Time** (6) (Time, Optional): If you have a specific time for your move, enter it here.
* **Company** (7) (Text, Optional): If you represent a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Feedback** (8) (Text, Required): Share any thoughts or opinions about your experience with the moving crew.
* **Rating** (9) (Select One, Required): Rate your experience with the moving crew using the scale provided (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional Comments** (10) (Text, Optional): Share any further thoughts or comments about your experience.

## Tips
* Please be honest and specific when answering the questions.
* If you have any concerns or issues, please do not hesitate to share them.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your cooperation.
