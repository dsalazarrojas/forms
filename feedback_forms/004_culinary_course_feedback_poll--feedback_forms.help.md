# Culinary Course Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students or participants who have attended a culinary course or workshop. It is meant to gauge their opinions on different aspects of the course, including course rating, instructor rating, course material, time management, and overall satisfaction. The form also collects contact information for further communication and allows participants to provide additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your rating for the course from the dropdown list.
2. Rate your overall satisfaction with the instructor.
3. Provide feedback about the course in a text format.
4. Indicate if you attended the workshop.
5. Choose how you would rate the course material provided.
6. Evaluate the time management of the course.
7. Rate the facilities provided by the course.
8. Choose how you would rate the food service provided.
9. Select your overall satisfaction with the course.
10. Enter your email address for further communication.
11. Enter your phone number for further communication.
12. (Optional) Add any additional comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Rating**: Select from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor to indicate your overall experience with the course.
* **Instructor Rating**: Choose from Highly rated, Average, or Below average to indicate your satisfaction with the instructor's performance.
* **Feedback**: This is a free-text field where you can provide any feedback or comments about the course.
* **Attended Workshop**: Select True or False to indicate if you attended the workshop.
* **Course Material Provided**: Select from Sufficient or Not Sufficient to indicate if the course material was adequate for your learning.
* **Time Management**: Choose from Good, Fair, or Poor to indicate how well the course was time-managed.
* **Facilities**: Select from Good, Fair, or Poor to rate the facilities provided by the course.
* **Food Service**: Choose from Good, Fair, or Poor to rate the food service provided.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Select from Satisfied or Not Satisfied to indicate your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Comments**: (Optional) Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Additional Comments**: (Optional) Add any other comments or feedback about your experience.
