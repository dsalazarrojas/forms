# Workplace Training Materials Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the quality of workplace training materials. It will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the training and provide insights to improve the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and email address.
2. Provide the name of the training materials you used.
3. Choose a date and time when you attended the training.
4. Choose the quality of the training from the options provided.
5. Enter any comments or thoughts about the training.
6. Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
7. Provide feedback about the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User First Name (`user_first_name`, Text, Optional):** This field is for entering your first name.
- **Email (`email`, Text, Optional):** This field is for your contact email address.
- **Training Materials Name (`training_materials_name`, Text, Optional):** Please enter the name of the training materials you used.
- **Training Date (`training_date`, Date, Optional):** Enter the date you attended the training.
- **Training Start Time (`training_start_time`, Time, Optional):** Enter the start time of the training.
- **Training End Time (`training_end_time`, Time, Optional):** Enter the end time of the training.
- **Evaluation (`evaluation`, Select One, Required):** Please rate the quality of the training using the options provided.
- **Comments (`comments`, Text, Optional):** Enter any comments or thoughts about the training.
- **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional):** Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
- **Feedback (`feedback`, Text, Optional):** Enter feedback about the training.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve the training process.
* Please provide specific examples or experiences to support your feedback.
* You can provide feedback about any aspect of the training, including content, format, or overall experience.
