# Team Meeting Discussion Topics Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Meeting Discussion Topics Form is a tool to help the meeting leader prepare for the discussion by collecting relevant topics and tasks to be covered during the team meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the meeting date and time from the calendar.
2. Choose a specific meeting location (if applicable).
3. List the attendees who will be participating in the meeting.
4. Submit any new discussion topics or action items for the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Topic** (`meeting_topic`, text, required): A brief description of the discussion topic or main theme of the meeting.
* **Agenda Item** (`agenda_item`, text, required): A specific action or task that needs to be discussed or decided upon during the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): The date on which the meeting is scheduled to take place.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required): The time of the meeting.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required if applicable): The physical location where the meeting is happening.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required): List the team members participating in the meeting.
* **Discussion Topic** (`discussion_topic`, text, optional): Additional discussion topic or theme that wasn't covered in the main topic.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, optional): List any action items that need to be decided upon or completed following the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form only for meetings that have already been scheduled.
* Double-check that you're not duplicating information that's already been submitted or discussed.
* Be clear and concise in your submission.
