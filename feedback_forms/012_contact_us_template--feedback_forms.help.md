# Contact Us Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Contact Us Template is a form used to collect feedback and inquiries from users or customers. This form allows you to reach out to us with your questions, concerns, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Sender Details section, which includes your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select the appropriate subject category from the Inquiry Subject dropdown menu.
3.  Enter your message or description of the issue in the Message/Description of the Issue field.
4.  Indicate whether you require a response to your message by selecting True or False.
5.  Choose your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or No preference).
6.  If you're interested, sign up for our newsletter by selecting True or False.
7.  Finally, indicate how you found out about us (Search Engine, Social Media, Referral, Advertisement, or Other).
8.  Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting True or False.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
*   **Your Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up.
*   **Your Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
*   **Inquiry Subject** (`subject_header`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of your inquiry from the dropdown menu.
    +   General Question
    +   Technical Support
    +   Billing Inquiry
    +   Feedback
    +   Request Call Back
    +   Partnership
    +   Careers
*   **Message/Description of the Issue** (`message_details`, `text`, required): Enter a clear and concise description of your issue or question.
*   **Do you require a response to this message?** (`follow_up_needed`, `select_one`, required): Select True if you'd like us to follow up on your message, False otherwise.
*   **How should we contact you?** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted for follow-up (Email, Phone, or No preference).
*   **Sign up for our newsletter?** (`newsletter_opt_in`, `select_one`, optional): Select True if you'd like to sign up for our newsletter, False otherwise.
*   **How did you hear about us?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how you found out about us (Search Engine, Social Media, Referral, Advertisement, or Other).
*   **I agree to the terms and conditions** (`privacy_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select True to agree to the terms and conditions.
