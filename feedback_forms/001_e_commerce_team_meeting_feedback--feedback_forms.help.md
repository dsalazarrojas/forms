# e-commerce_team_meeting_feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from team members about their recent e-commerce team meeting. This feedback will help the team identify areas of improvement and create action items to address any issues raised.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the "Meeting Rating" section by selecting a rating from 1 to 5 for the meeting overall.
2.  In the "Overall Satisfaction" field, rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1 to 5.
3.  In the "Action Items" field, list any action items that you would like to see addressed.
4.  In the "Action Item 1", "Action Item 2", "Action Item 3", "Action Item 4", and "Action Item 5" fields, list specific actions you would like to see taken.
5.  In the "Additional Comments" field, include any additional information or comments about the meeting.
6.  Finally, enter your "Email" and "Phone" contact information so that we can reach out to discuss any further questions or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Rating**: Rate your overall rating for the meeting on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **rating_1**, **rating_2**, **rating_3**, **rating_4**, **rating_5**: Rate the quality of the meeting on a scale of 1 to 5 for each of the corresponding aspects.
* **Meeting Rating Comment**: Provide a brief comment about your overall rating.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Action Items**: List any action items you would like to see addressed.
* **Action Item 1**, **Action Item 2**, **Action Item 3**, **Action Item 4**, **Action Item 5**: List specific actions you would like to see taken.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments about the meeting.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can reach out to discuss any further questions or concerns.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to discuss any further questions or concerns.
* **Name**: Enter your name so that we can identify who provided the feedback.

This help guide is to assist you in completing this form correctly and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
