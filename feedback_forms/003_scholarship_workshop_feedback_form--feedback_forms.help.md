# Scholarship Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees of the scholarship workshop on their experience and opinion about the event. The feedback will be used to improve the workshop and future events.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, type a brief summary of your thoughts about the scholarship workshop. This is where you can express your overall opinion about the workshop.
2. On the second page, select the categories that your feedback falls under from the options provided.
3. On the third page, provide any additional comments you have about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What did you think of the scholarship workshop?** (Text input, optional): Please type a brief summary of your thoughts about the workshop. This is your chance to share your overall opinion about the workshop.
* **Feedback Score** (Number input, optional): This is a numerical score out of a certain range (not specified in the form, but assumed to be a standard 1-5 or 1-10 scale) to indicate how well the workshop met your expectations.
* **Any additional comments?** (Text input, required): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the workshop. This is an opportunity to share any specific aspects of the workshop that you think could be improved.
* **What category does this feedback fall under?** (Select multiple options, required): Please select all the categories that your feedback falls under. The options provided are:
	+ Yes (presumably indicating that your feedback falls under this category)
	+ No (presumably indicating that your feedback does not fall under this category)

## Tips
- Please be as honest and specific as possible when providing feedback.
- You can select multiple categories if they apply to your feedback.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, please include them in the "Any additional comments?" field.
