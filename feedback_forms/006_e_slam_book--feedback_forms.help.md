# e_slam_book - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_slam_book form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the e_slam_book service. This feedback will help us understand how to improve the service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further follow-up questions.
3. Rate your overall experience with the e_slam_book service.
4. Answer the four questions to provide detailed feedback on your experience.
5. Submit the form to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (text): A brief introduction to your feedback, explaining what you're about to share.
* **Name** (text, optional): Enter your name, if you'd like to be addressed by name in our follow-up questions.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted by us for further questions.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by us for further questions.
* **Rating** (number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the e_slam_book service, using a scale of 1 to 5.
* **question1** (text, optional): Provide feedback on your experience with our service, specific to question 1.
* **question2** (text, optional): Provide feedback on your experience, specific to question 2.
* **question3** (text, optional): Provide feedback on your experience, specific to question 3.
* **question4** (text, optional): Provide feedback on your experience, specific to question 4.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your feedback, as it will help us improve the service.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the introduction field.
* We'll use your feedback to improve the e_slam_book service and provide a better experience for our users.
