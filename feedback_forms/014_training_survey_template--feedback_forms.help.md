# Training Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Survey Template is a form used to gather feedback from learners after training sessions. This form helps us understand how our training programs are performing and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name in the first field (`training_survey_template_1`).
2. Enter the number of hours you spent in the training session (`training_survey_template_2`).
3. Select the option that best describes the level of difficulty of the training content (`training_survey_template_3`).
4. Choose all the options that apply to your experience with the training material (`training_survey_template_4`).
5. Enter the date of the training session (`training_survey_template_5`).
6. Select a time for the training session (`training_survey_template_6`).
7. Enter any additional feedback or comments about the training session (`training_survey_template_7`).
8. Enter your email address for follow-up contact (`training_survey_template_8`).
9-25. Fill in the remaining text fields with any relevant information about your experience with the training material.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **training_survey_template_1** (`text`, required): Enter your name.
* **training_survey_template_2** (`number`, required): Enter the number of hours you spent in the training session.
* **training_survey_template_2** (`select_one`, required): Select the level of difficulty of the training content (e.g., Easy, Medium, Hard).
* **training_survey_template_4** (`select_multiple`, not required): Select all the options that apply to your experience with the training material.
* **training_survey_template_5** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **training_survey_template_6** (`time`, not required): Select a time for the training session.
* **training_survey_template_7** (`note`, not required): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the training session.
* **training_survey_template_8** (`email`, not required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **training_survey_template_9-25** (`text`, not required): Enter any relevant information about your experience with the training material.
