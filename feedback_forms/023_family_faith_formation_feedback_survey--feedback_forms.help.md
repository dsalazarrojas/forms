# Family Faith Formation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from family members about their experience with Family Faith Formation, a program aimed at nurturing faith and providing spiritual growth opportunities for families. The information collected through this form will help us understand your experiences, suggestions, and ratings, which will be used to improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter the names of all the family members who participated in the program.
2. Indicate if each family member has a role in the program (e.g., parent, child, leader).
3. Rate your satisfaction with the program (e.g., Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
4. Provide any suggestions or comments about the program.
5. Enter the date and time you are providing the feedback.
6. Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
7. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Names** (`family_names`, text, required): Please enter the names of all family members who participated in the program.
* **Family Member Roles** (`family_roles`, select_multiple, false): Indicate if each family member has a role in the program (e.g., parent, child, leader). Select all that apply.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, false): Rate your satisfaction with the program (e.g., Satisfied or Dissatisfied).
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required): Provide any suggestions or comments about the program.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the program.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are providing the feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are providing the feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact (optional).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to send your feedback.

Note: Please fill out as much or as little information as you like, but be sure to fill out all required fields.
