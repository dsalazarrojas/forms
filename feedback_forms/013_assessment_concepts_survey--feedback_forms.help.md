# Assessment Concepts Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assessment Concepts Survey is a form designed to gather feedback and insights from various stakeholders about their experiences and opinions on assessments. The survey aims to understand their preferences, challenges, and expectations regarding assessments, helping to inform the development of more effective assessment practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your name in the "Your Name" field (optional).
* Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field (optional).
* Select your role in the "Your Role" field.
* Indicate your organization type in the "Organization Type" field.
* Choose how familiar you are with assessments in the "Experience with Assessments" field.
* Select how often you use assessments in the "How Often Do You Use Assessments" field.
* Choose your preferred assessment type in the "Preferred Assessment Type" field.
* Select all the reasons why you use assessments in the "Primary Purpose of Assessments" field.
* Indicate your comfort level with digital assessments in the "Comfort with Digital Assessments" field.
* Check if you currently use online assessments in the "Do You Use Online Assessments" field.
* Select all the challenges you face with assessments in the "Biggest Assessment Challenges" field.
* Indicate the importance of immediate feedback in the "Importance of Immediate Feedback" field.
* Choose how you prefer assessments to be delivered in the "Preferred Assessment Format" field.
* Select how you prefer assessments to be graded in the "Preferred Grading Method" field.
* Select all the accessibility features you need in "Accessibility Features Needed" field.
* Provide your suggestions for improving assessments in the "What Would Improve Assessments" field.
* List the technology tools you currently use for assessments in the "Assessment Technology Tools Used" field.
* Indicate your interest in assessment training in the "Interest in Assessment Training" field.
* Add any additional comments or thoughts in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
* Finally, select the date of completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Provide your email address (if you wish to be contacted).
* **Your Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role, such as Student, Teacher, or Instructor.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your organization type, such as K-12 School, College, or Corporate.
* **Experience with Assessments** (`assessment_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose how familiar you are with assessments, such as Very Experienced, Limited Experience, or No Experience.
* **How Often Do You Use Assessments** (`assessment_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you use assessments, such as Daily, Weekly, or Rarely.
* **Preferred Assessment Type** (`preferred_assessment_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred assessment type, such as Multiple Choice Tests or Written Essays.
* **Primary Purpose of Assessments** (`assessment_purpose`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the reasons why you use assessments, such as Measure Knowledge or Evaluate Skills.
* **Comfort with Digital Assessments** (`technology_comfort`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your comfort level with digital assessments, such as Very Comfortable or Very Uncomfortable.
* **Do You Use Online Assessments** (`online_assessment_use`, `select_one`, required): Check if you currently use online assessments, such as Always or Never.
* **Biggest Assessment Challenges** (`assessment_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the challenges you face with assessments, such as Time Constraints or Scoring Consistency.
* **Importance of Immediate Feedback** (`feedback_importance`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the importance of immediate feedback, such as Very Important or Not Important.
* **Preferred Assessment Format** (`assessment_format_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer assessments to be delivered, such as Timed Tests or Project Based.
* **Preferred Grading Method** (`grading_method_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select how you prefer assessments to be graded, such as Pass or Fail or Rubric Based.
* **Accessibility Features Needed** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the accessibility features you need, such as Extended Time or Screen Reader Support.
* **What Would Improve Assessments** (`assessment_improvement`, `text`, required): Provide your suggestions for improving assessments.
* **Assessment Technology Tools Used** (`technology_tools_used`, `select_multiple`, required): List the technology tools you currently use for assessments, such as Learning Management Systems or Online Quiz Platforms.
* **Interest in Assessment Training** (`training_interest`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your interest in assessment training, such as Very Interested or Not Interested.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of completion.
