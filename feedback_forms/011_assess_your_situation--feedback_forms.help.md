# Assess Your Situation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you assess your current situation and provide a clear understanding of your needs and preferences. Please fill out this form to the best of your ability, and you will be guided through each step to ensure you have completed all necessary fields.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your full name and email address.
2. Select the type of situation you are currently facing.
3. Rate the urgency level of your situation and describe its duration.
4. Describe your situation in detail.
5. Rate the impact on your daily life.
6. Rate your current stress level.
7. Indicate your support system and whether you have any resources available to you.
8. If applicable, describe any previous attempts to address this situation.
9. Select the resources you have available to you.
10. Describe any main obstacles you face.
11. Specify your desired outcome.
12. Indicate your preferred timeline for resolution.
13. Rate your willingness to make changes.
14. Choose your preferred type of assistance.
15. If applicable, specify your budget for solutions.
16. Choose how you would like to be contacted.
17. Lastly, provide the date for this assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Your complete name that we will use for communication.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): The email address we will use for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, not required): Your best contact number for communication.
* **Type of Situation** (4, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to describe the area of your situation:
	+ Personal Life
	+ Career or Work
	+ Financial
	+ Health and Wellness
	+ Relationships
	+ Education
	+ Business or Organization
* **Urgency Level** (5, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to rate the urgency level of your situation:
	+ Not Urgent - Can Wait
	+ Somewhat Urgent - This Week
	+ Urgent - Within 24 Hours
	+ Critical - Immediate Attention
* **Duration** (6, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to describe the duration of your situation:
	+ Less than a week
	+ 1 to 4 weeks
	+ 1 to 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ More than 1 year
* **Situation Description** (7, text, required): Provide details about what you are currently facing.
* **Impact on Daily Life** (8, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to rate the impact on your daily life:
	+ Minimal Impact
	+ Moderate Impact
	+ Significant Impact
	+ Severe Impact
* **Stress Level** (9, number, required): Rate your current stress level from 1 to 10.
* **Support System** (10, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to describe your support system:
	+ Strong Support Network
	+ Some Support
	+ Limited Support
	+ No Support
* **Previous Attempts** (11, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to describe any previous attempts to address this situation:
	+ Yes - Successfully
	+ Yes - Unsuccessfully
	+ No - First Time
* **Resources Available** (12, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to the resources you have available:
	+ Financial Resources
	+ Time Availability
	+ Professional Help
	+ Family Support
	+ Community Services
	+ Online Resources
	+ None
* **Main Obstacles** (13, text, required): Describe any main obstacles you face.
* **Desired Outcome** (14, text, required): Specify your desired outcome.
* **Timeline for Resolution** (15, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to indicate your preferred timeline for resolution:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Within 3 months
	+ Within 6 months
	+ Within 1 year
	+ No specific timeline
* **Willingness to Make Changes** (16, select_one, required): Select one of the following options to rate your willingness to make changes:
	+ Very Ready - Take Action Now
	+ Ready - Soon
	+ Considering - Need Guidance
	+ Not Sure Yet
* **Preferred Type of Assistance** (17, select_one, required): Select one of the following options for the type of assistance you prefer:
	+ Professional Counseling
	+ Financial Advice
	+ Career Coaching
	+ Legal Consultation
	+ Medical Help
	+ Educational Resources
	+ Peer Support
* **Budget for Solutions** (18, select_one, not required): If applicable, select one of the following options for your budget for solutions:
	+ No Budget - Need Free Options
	+ Limited - Under $500
	+ Moderate - $500 to $2000
	+ Flexible - Over $2000
* **Preferred Contact Method** (19, select_one, required): Select one of the following options for how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone Call
	+ Text Message
	+ Video Call
* **Assessment Date** (20, date, required): Provide the current date for this assessment.

## Tips
- Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible for accurate assessment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us.
