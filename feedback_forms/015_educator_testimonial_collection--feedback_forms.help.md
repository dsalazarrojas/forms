# Educator Testimonial Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educator Testimonial Collection form is a tool to gather feedback from educators on their experiences with our educational programs. This feedback is invaluable in helping us understand the impact of our initiatives and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" to the "What is your school district" question.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Provide a testimonial about your experience in the "Testimonial" field.
6. Enter the name of your school in the "School name" field.
7. Enter the name of the course you completed in the "Course name" field.
8. Enter the date you completed the course in the "Date completed" field.
9. Enter the time you completed the course in the "Time completed" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your school district**: Select "Yes" if you are an educator from a school district that has worked with our organization. This helps us understand which districts we have had the most impact on.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed with your testimonial.
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address where you can be contacted for follow-up.
* **Testimonial**: Share your honest feedback about your experience with our educational program. This can include what you liked or disliked, what you learned, and any recommendations you have.
* **School name**: Enter the name of the school where you work.
* **Course name**: Enter the name of the course you completed with our organization.
* **Date completed**: Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Time completed**: Enter the time you took to complete the course.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
