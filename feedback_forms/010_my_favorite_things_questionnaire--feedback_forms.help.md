# My Favorite Things Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The My Favorite Things Questionnaire is a form designed to collect information about a student's and teacher's preferences and interests. The information gathered will be used to create more targeted and effective learning experiences for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Preferences** (`Student Preferences`, `text`, not required): This is where you can enter your thoughts and preferences as a student. Please feel free to write down anything you think is important for us to know.
* **Teacher Preferences** (`Teacher Preferences`, `text`, not required): This is where you can enter your thoughts and preferences as a teacher. Please feel free to write down anything you think is important for us to know.
* **Teacher Interests** (`Teacher Interests`, `text`, not required): This is where you can enter your thoughts and interests as a teacher. Please feel free to write down anything you think is important for us to know.

## Tips
- Please make sure to enter relevant and accurate information.
- You are free to write as little or as much as you'd like for each field.
- If you're not sure what to write, try to think about things that you enjoy or are passionate about.
