# Construction Defects Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on construction defects reported on-site. It is intended to be used by anyone who has observed a potential issue to provide a detailed report.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant information for each field carefully.
2. Fill in all required fields to ensure your report is complete.
3. Use the provided fields for guidance on what information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter name** (`reporter_name`, text, required): This is the name of the person submitting the defect report.
* **Reporter contact email** (`reporter_contact`, email, optional): This is the email address for follow-up communication.
* **Project reference or ID** (`project_reference`, text, required): Provide the job number or project name associated with the defect.
* **Defect location** (`defect_location`, text, required): Describe the exact location where the defect was observed.
* **Defect type** (`defect_type`, select_one, required): Choose the most applicable category from the options provided:
	+ Structural
	+ Finish
	+ Mechanical
	+ Electrical
	+ Other
* **Severity level** (`severity`, select_one, required): Rate the severity of the defect:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
	+ Critical
* **Date observed** (`date_observed`, date, optional): Enter the date when the defect was first noticed.
* **Defect description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the defect, including any immediate risks.
* **Photos provided** (`photos_provided`, select_one, optional): Note whether photos will be uploaded separately.
* **Recommended action** (`recommended_action`, text, optional): Suggest immediate next steps if known.
