# National Marketing Program Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers on the National Marketing Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field as instructed:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional):
  Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, optional):
  Choose how you would like to be contacted:
  • Phone
  • Email
  • Address
* **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, optional):
  Please describe your issue or concern about the National Marketing Program.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, optional):
  If you have a suggestion for improvement, please provide it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional):
  Additional comments or information about your experience with the program.
* **Marketing Program** (`marketing_program`, select_multiple, optional):
  Have you participated in the National Marketing Program?
  • Yes
  • No
* **Marketing Initiative** (`marketing_initiative`, text, optional):
  (Please note: This field will be removed in the future)
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, optional):
  Select the date of your feedback, or leave blank if not applicable.

Tips:

* Please be as concise as possible while still providing relevant information.
* If you have any attachments or additional information, please contact us separately.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don’t hesitate to reach out to us.
