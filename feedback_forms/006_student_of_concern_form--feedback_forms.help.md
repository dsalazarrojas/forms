# Student Of Concern Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student of Concern Form is used to document incidents or concerns involving students that require administrative support. This form captures relevant information about the student, incident, and support provided. It helps track the progress and resolution of the incident.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on "Start Form" to begin filling out the form. The form will guide you through each section.
2. Enter the student's name and relevant details in the "Student Info" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident or concern in the "Incident Info" field.
4. Select the relevant classroom or location related to the incident in the "Classroom Info" field.
5. Choose the support system(s) involved in the "Support System" field. This could include the Counselor, Academic Support Team, or Other Support.
6. Record the date and time of the incident in the "Concern Date" and "Concern Time" fields.
7. Indicate whether the teacher has reviewed the incident in the "Teacher Signature" field.
8. Confirm whether the student has reviewed the incident in the "Student Signature" field.
9. Add any comments or notes related to the incident in the "Comments" field.
10. Record the teacher's comments on the incident in the "Teacher Comments" field.
11. Add any support comments from the support system in the "Support Comments" field.
12. Record the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
13. Enter the resolution or outcome of the incident in the "Resolution" field.
14. Outline any follow-up actions taken in the "Follow Up Actions" field.
15. Record any follow-up comments from the support system in the "Follow Up Comments" field.
16. Record the review date and time in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.
17. Record the reviewer's name in the "Reviewed By" field.
18. Enter any review comments in the "Review Comments" field.
19. Select the status of the follow-up in the "Follow Up Status" field.
20. Add any follow-up notes in the "Follow Up Notes" field.
21. Record the follow-up date in the "Follow Up Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief welcome message.
* **Student Info** (`Student Info`, text, optional): Enter the student's name and any other relevant details.
* **Incident Info** (`Incident Info`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the incident or concern.
* **Classroom Info** (`Classroom Info`, text, optional): Select the relevant classroom or location related to the incident.
* **Support System** (`Support System`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the support system(s) involved in the incident. This could include the Counselor, Academic Support Team, or Other Support.
* **Concern Date** (`Concern Date`, date, required): Record the date of the incident.
* **Concern Time** (`Concern Time`, time, required): Record the time of the incident.
* **Teacher Signature** (`Teacher Signature`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the teacher has reviewed the incident.
* **Student Signature** (`Student Signature`, select_one, required): Confirm whether the student has reviewed the incident.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes related to the incident.
* **Teacher Comments** (`Teacher Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the teacher.
* **Support Comments** (`Support Comments`, text, optional): Record any comments from the support system.
* **Incident Date** (`Incident Date`, date, required): Record the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`Incident Time`, time, required): Record the time of the incident.
* **Resolution** (`Resolution`, text, optional): Enter the resolution or outcome of the incident.
* **Follow Up Actions** (`Follow Up Actions`, text, optional): Outline any follow-up actions taken.
* **Follow Up Comments** (`Follow Up Comments`, text, optional): Record any follow-up comments from the support system.
* **Review Date** (`Review Date`, date, optional): Record the review date.
* **Review Time** (`Review Time`, time, optional): Record the review time.
* **Reviewed By** (`Reviewed By`, text, optional): Record the reviewer's name.
* **Review Comments** (`Review Comments`, text, optional): Enter any review comments.
* **Follow Up Status** (`Follow Up Status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the follow-up.
* **Follow Up Notes** (`Follow Up Notes`, text, optional): Add any follow-up notes.
* **Follow Up Date** (`Follow Up Date`, date, optional): Record the follow-up date.
