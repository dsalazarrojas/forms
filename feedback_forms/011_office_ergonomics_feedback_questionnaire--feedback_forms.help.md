# office_ergonomics_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office_ergonomics_feedback_questionnaire is a form designed to gather feedback on the comfort and ergonomics of the office space. It's intended to collect information from office workers on their experiences and suggestions regarding their workspace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure that you have not already submitted feedback on the same topic, and that your comments are specific and constructive.
* Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
* Use the drop-down menus and text fields as instructed to provide accurate and clear information.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure it's complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Text): 
This is a brief introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance.
* **Comfort Level** (Number): 
Indicate your comfort level in the office, on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is "not at all comfortable" and 10 is "very comfortable".
* **Ergonomic Equipment** (Select Multiple): 
Choose all the ergonomic equipment you have access to in the office, such as:
	+ Office chair
	+ Standing desk
	+ Monitor height adjustment
	+ Keyboard tray
* **Feedback** (Text): 
Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have regarding the comfort and ergonomics of the office space.
* **Email** (Email): 
Enter your work email address, as this will help us contact you with any follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Phone** (Text): 
Enter your work phone number, in case we need to contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Date** (Date): 
Enter the date you are submitting this feedback, as this will help us track when feedback is received.
* **Time of Day** (Time): 
Enter the time of day you are submitting this feedback, as this will help us track when feedback is received.
* **Phone** (Phone): 
This field is not used in this form. Please disregard.
