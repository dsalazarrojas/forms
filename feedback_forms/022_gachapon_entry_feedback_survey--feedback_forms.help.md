# gachapon_entry_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and scores from users. It's a simple, one-page form that asks for a player's name, their score, and any comments they'd like to share. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and help us improve our game.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Feedback" field and type your feedback in the text box.
2. Enter your "Player Name" in the second field.
3. Enter your "Score" in the third field. You can use any whole number between 0 and 100.
4. If you'd like to add more details, type your comments in the fourth field.
5. Click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback** (text, required/optional): This is where you can type any comments or feedback you'd like to share with us. Please be as brief or detailed as you'd like.
* **Player Name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as you'd like to be known. We'll use this to personalize any follow-up communication.
* **Score** (number, required/optional): Rate your experience with a score between 0 and 100. This will help us gauge our game's overall quality.
* **Comments** (text, required/optional): Share any additional thoughts or observations you'd like to make about your experience.
