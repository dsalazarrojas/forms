# Family Benefits Application Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on family benefits applications. It helps to identify areas of improvement and makes adjustments to the application process as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Applicant Name**, which is your name as an applicant.
2. Enter your **Email**, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. Select the name of the **Agency Name** that handled your application.
4. Provide a rating from 1 to 5 based on your experience with the family benefits application.
5. Add any **Comments** you have about the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required: false): This is the name of the applicant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This is your email address for communication purposes.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required: false): This is the name of the agency that handled your application.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required: false): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the application process.

## Tips

* Please answer the form questions truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any additional feedback, feel free to add it in the **Comments** field.
* This form is designed to be completed by family benefits applicants.
* If you need any further assistance, contact form@gic.mx.
