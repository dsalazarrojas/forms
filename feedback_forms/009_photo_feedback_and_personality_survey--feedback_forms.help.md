# photo_feedback_and_personality_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and personality survey data from users about a photo. It's meant to gather opinions and ratings on various aspects of the photo, as well as demographic information and personality traits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief introduction to the photo, if applicable.
2. Answer the question "What do you think about this photo?" (Page 1, photo_description).
3. Select one personality trait that you think best describes the photo (Page 2, personality_scale).
4. Choose all the emotions that you feel when viewing the photo (Page 2, emotion).
5. Write your reaction to the photo (Page 3, image_reactions).
6. Provide demographic information, such as your age (Page 4, age), and whether you want to be contacted (Page 8, contact_details).
7. Rate the overall quality of the survey (Page 10, survey_rating).
8. Enter your email address and phone number (Page 11, email and page 12, phone).
9. Enter your name (Page 13, name).
10. Provide any additional feedback (Page 14, feedback).
11. If you're rating personality traits, use the Likert scale for Agreeableness (Page 15, agreeable_likertscale), Conscientiousness (Page 16, conscientiousness_likertscale), Neuroticism (Page 17, neurotic_likertscale), Extraversion (Page 18, extraversion_likertscale), and Openness (Page 19, openness_likertscale).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1): A brief introduction to the photo, if available.
* **What do you think about this photo?** (Page 2, photo_description): Please provide your thoughts and opinions about the photo.
* **Which personality trait?** (Page 3, personality_scale): Select one option that best describes the photo.
* **How does this photo make you feel?** (Page 4, emotion): Choose all the emotions that you experience when viewing the photo.
* **What do you react to this photo?** (Page 5, image_reactions): Please write your reaction to the photo.
* **About you** (Page 6, demographics): This is a demographic section where you can select your personal characteristics.
* **How old are you?** (Page 7, age): Please enter your age.
* **Do you want to be contacted?** (Page 8, contact_details): Choose whether you want to be contacted for further information.
* **How good is this survey?** (Page 10, survey_rating): Rate the overall quality of the survey.
* **Your email** (Page 11, email): Please enter your email address.
* **Your phone number** (Page 12, phone): Please enter your phone number.
* **Your name** (Page 13, name): Please enter your name.
* **Any comments?** (Page 14, feedback): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Agreeableness Likert scale** (Page 15, agreeable_likertscale): Rate your agreeableness using the Likert scale.
* **Conscientiousness Likert scale** (Page 16, conscientiousness_likertscale): Rate your conscientiousness using the Likert scale.
* **Neuroticism Likert scale** (Page 17, neurotic_likertscale): Rate your neuroticism using the Likert scale.
* **Extraversion Likert scale** (Page 18, extraversion_likertscale): Rate your extraversion using the Likert scale.
* **Openness Likert scale** (Page 19, openness_likertscale): Rate your openness using the Likert scale.
* **Survey Rating Likert scale** (Page 20, survey_rating_likertscale): Rate the overall quality of the survey using the Likert scale.
