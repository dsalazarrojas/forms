# Say Hello To The City - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to gather feedback and information from users about their experience in the city, which will help city administrators or event planners to better understand how the city is being utilized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your feedback or comments about your experience in the city.
2. Provide the date of your visit.
3. Enter your contact information (email or phone number) if you want to be contacted for more information.
4. Choose one option from the first select field.
5. Select multiple options from the second select field.
6. Enter the time of your visit (optional).
7. Add any additional note about your experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Form 1** (text, optional): This field is for you to provide any feedback or comments about your experience in the city.
- **Date of Visit** (date, optional): Enter the date of your visit to the city.
- **Contact Information** (email, optional): Enter your email or phone number if you want to be contacted for more information.
- **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Select One** (select, required): Choose one option from the list.
- **Select Multiple** (select multiple, required): Select multiple options from the list.
- **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
- **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional note about your experience in the city.
