# Financial Statement Review Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the financial statement review process. Your responses will help us understand your satisfaction with the process and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and respond to the questions according to your experience.
2. Use the provided fields to answer each question in as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to submit your response to ensure your feedback is recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall Impression** (Note, Not required): Please describe your overall impression of the financial statement review process.
- **Financial Analyst Experience** (Text, Not required): Tell us about your experience with the financial analyst.
- **Clarity of Financial Statement** (Number, Required): Rate how clear the financial statement was.
- **Understanding of Review Process** (Text, Not required): Describe how well you understood the review process.
- **Issues or Concerns** (Text, Not required): If you have any issues or concerns about the process, please share them with us.
- **Additional Comments** (Text, Not required): If there's anything else you'd like to comment on or suggest, please add it here.
- **Timeliness** (Number, Required): Rate how timely the review process was.
- **Necessary Information** (Select One, Required): How much of the necessary information were you able to receive?
- **Recommendation** (Select Multiple, Required): Would you recommend using the financial statement review process?
- **Likelihood of Use** (Number, Required): Rate how likely you are to use this process again.
- **Previous Use** (Checkbox, Required): Have you used this process before?
- **Source of Information** (Select Multiple, Required): Where did you receive the information for this review process?
- **Contact Info** (Email, Optional): If you'd like to be contacted for more information, please enter your email address here.
- **Page 14, 15, 16, 17, 18** (Text/Note/Email, Not required): These pages are for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide, but they are not required fields.

Please take a few minutes to review each page carefully and provide as much detail as possible in each field. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time.
