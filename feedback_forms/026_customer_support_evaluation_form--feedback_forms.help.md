# Customer Support Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Support Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from customers to help us improve our customer support services. This information will be used to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. For fields with options, select the correct answer that best describes your experience.
3. You can add additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field if you have any further comments or concerns.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): This field is used to record the name of the customer submitting the feedback.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if necessary.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the issue you experienced with our support service. Be as detailed as possible so we can understand the problem.
* **Support Service** (`support_service`, select_one, required): Select the type of support service you used (Phone, Email, Chat, or In Person).
* **Issue Resolution** (`issue_resolution`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how your issue was resolved (Solved, Not Solved, Partially Solved).
* **Customer Support Rating** (`customer_support_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with our support service on a scale of 0-5.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Feedback on Support Team** (`feedback_on_support_team`, select_one, required): Choose which support team was handling your issue (Support Team A, Support Team B, Support Team C).
* **Feedback on Support Team** (`feedback_on_support_team`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which support team member(s) you interacted with during your issue (Team A, Team B, Team C).
