# Winning Move Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback about your experience with a winning move. Your input helps us understand what worked well and where we can improve our communication and strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Survey Title** field, a brief description of the winning move.
2. Select your **Role** in the team or organization.
3. Choose how you were **Communicated** with:
	* Email
	* In Person
	* Both
4. Select the **Communication Method** used:
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Chat
5. Indicate who you were **Communicated To**:
	* Team
	* Manager
	* Employee
6. Describe how you were **Communicated With** in a few sentences.
7. Assess the **Impact** of the winning move:
	* High
	* Medium
	* Low
8. Identify the **Area For Improvement**:
	* Communication
	* Clarity
	* Timeliness
9. Add any **Improvements To Make** in the text field.
10. Provide any **Additional Feedback**.
11. Enter your **Submitted By** name.
12. Select the **Submitted On** date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Title** (text): A brief description of the winning move.
* **Role** (select_one): Your role in the team or organization.
	+ Team Lead: A team leader who makes strategic decisions.
	+ Manager: A person who oversees and guides the team.
	+ Employee: A team member who executes tasks.
* **Who Communicated** (select_multiple): How you were communicated with:
	+ Email: Information was sent via email.
	+ In Person: Face-to-face communication.
	+ Both: Information was sent both via email and in person.
* **Communication Method** (select_multiple): How the communication was done:
	+ Email: Information was sent via email.
	+ Phone: Information was communicated over the phone.
	+ Chat: Information was sent through a chat platform.
* **Who Was Communicated To** (select_multiple): Who received the communication:
	+ Team: The team was communicated to.
	+ Manager: The manager was communicated to.
	+ Employee: A team member was communicated to.
* **How Was It Communicated** (text): Describe how you were communicated with.
* **Impact** (select_one): Assess the impact of the winning move:
	+ High: The impact was significant and far-reaching.
	+ Medium: The impact was moderate and noticeable.
	+ Low: The impact was minor and not significant.
* **Area For Improvement** (select_multiple): Identify areas for improvement:
	+ Communication: Clear and effective communication was a challenge.
	+ Clarity: Information was not clear or concise.
	+ Timeliness: Information was not provided in a timely manner.
* **Improvements To Make** (text): Add any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Feedback** (text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Submitted By** (text): Enter your name.
* **Submitted On** (date): Select the date you submitted the form.
