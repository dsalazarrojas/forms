# Summer Stage Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from event attendees to help event organizers improve future summer stage events. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts and suggestions about the event you attended.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the event you are providing feedback for from the "Event Organizer" field.
2. Enter the event organizer's email address in the "Event Organizer Email" field.
3. Enter the event organizer's phone number in the "Event Organizer Phone" field (optional).
4. Choose the type of feedback you are providing by selecting one of the options in the "Feedback Type" field.
5. Enter your feedback in the "Feedback Text" field (optional).
6. Rate the event's quality by selecting one or more ratings in the "Feedback Rating" field (optional).
7. If applicable, enter the event date and time in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields, respectively.
8. Enter the event venue in the "Event Venue" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, **required**): The person or organization responsible for hosting the event. Please select the name of the event organizer from the list.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`, email, **required**): The email address of the event organizer. Enter their email address in this field.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (`event_organizer_phone`, text, **optional**): The phone number of the event organizer. If you have it, please enter their phone number here.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the type of feedback you are providing. You can select either "Feedback for event" or "Other".
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, **optional**): A field for you to type your thoughts and suggestions about the event. If you have specific feedback or comments, please enter them here.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, select_multiple, **optional**): Rate the event's quality by selecting one or more ratings. You can choose from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, **optional**): If you attended the event, please enter the date you attended.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, **optional**): If you attended the event, please enter the time you attended.
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, text, **optional**): The location where the event took place. If you remember, please enter the venue name.
