# Galvanizing Process Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Galvanizing Process Feedback form is designed to gather information on the galvanizing process, including issues, satisfaction, and production details. This data helps identify areas for improvement and optimizes the process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select all applicable issues encountered during the galvanizing process.
3. Rate your satisfaction with the galvanizing process.
4. Enter production details, including production time, cost, and date.
5. Identify the operator of the galvanizing machine.
6. List any maintenance procedures followed during the galvanizing process.
7. Enter customer contact information.
8. Identify the type and model of the galvanizing machine used.
9. Check if the issue was reopened.
10. Enter the date and time of the galvanizing process run.
11. Enter the hours run on the galvanizing machine.
12. Identify who ran the galvanizing process.
13. Enter any cost savings from the galvanizing process.
14. Enter the cost of the galvanizing process.
15. Enter the yield of the galvanizing process.
16. Identify the capacity of the galvanizing machine.
17. Enter any additional notes about the galvanizing process.
18. Check if the galvanizing process was completed successfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Galvanizing Process Feedback** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your experience with the galvanizing process.
* **Issues Encountered** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all issues that occurred during the galvanizing process.
	+ Issues 1, 2, and 3 are options for you to choose from.
* **Satisfaction** (`3`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the galvanizing process, on a scale of 0-100.
* **Process Comments** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about the galvanizing process.
* **Galvanizing Process Satisfaction** (`5`, `number`, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the galvanizing process, on a scale of 0-100.
* **Production Time** (`6`, `number`, optional): Enter the production time for the galvanizing process.
* **Production Cost** (`7`, `number`, optional): Enter the production cost for the galvanizing process.
* **Production Date** (`8`, `date`, optional): Enter the production date for the galvanizing process.
* **Galvanizing Machine Operator** (`9`, `select_one`, optional): Identify the operator of the galvanizing machine.
	+ Operator 1, 2, and 3 are options for you to choose from.
* **Galvanizing Maintainance Procedures Followed** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): List any maintenance procedures followed during the galvanizing process.
	+ Procedure 1, 2, and 3 are options for you to choose from.
* **Customer Comments** (`11`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments from the customer.
* **Customer Contact** (`12`, `text`, optional): Enter the customer contact information.
* **Galvanizing Machine Type** (`13`, `select_one`, optional): Identify the type of galvanizing machine used.
	+ Machine 1, 2, and 3 are options for you to choose from.
* **Galvanizing Machine Model** (`14`, `text`, optional): Enter the model number of the galvanizing machine.
* **Issue Reopened** (`15`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the issue was reopened.
	+ 'True' or 'False' are options for you to choose from.
* **Galvanizing Process Run Date** (`16`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the galvanizing process was run.
* **Galvanizing Process Run Time** (`17`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the galvanizing process was run.
* **Galvanizing Machine Hours Run** (`18`, `number`, optional): Enter the hours run on the galvanizing machine.
* **Galvanizing Process Run By** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Identify who ran the galvanizing process.
	+ Person 1, 2, and 3 are options for you to choose from.
* **Galvanizing Process Savings** (`20`, `number`, optional): Enter any cost savings from the galvanizing process.
* **Galvanizing Process Cost** (`21`, `number`, optional): Enter the cost of the galvanizing process.
* **Galvanizing Process Yield** (`22`, `number`, optional): Enter the yield of the galvanizing process.
* **Galvanizing Machine Capacity** (`23`, `text`, optional): Identify the capacity of the galvanizing machine.
* **Galvanizing Process Notes** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the galvanizing process.
* **Galvanizing Process Completed** (`25`, `select_one`, optional): Check if the galvanizing process was completed successfully.
	+ 'True' or 'False' are options for you to choose from.
