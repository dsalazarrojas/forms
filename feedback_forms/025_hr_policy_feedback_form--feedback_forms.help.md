# HR Policy Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The HR Policy Feedback Form is a tool for employees to provide their thoughts and experiences on company policies. This form helps the HR department understand employee perspectives and gather data to improve policies and create a better work environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to provide your name and contact details so we can reach out to you for additional information if needed.
2. Provide feedback on the policy or policies you have experience with. Be as specific as possible and include any relevant details.
3. Rate the policy using the provided rating system (1-5).
4. Include any additional details or suggestions you may have.
5. Attach any supporting documents or information you think would help us better understand your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official personnel file.
* **Policy Number**: Provide the number or title of the policy you are providing feedback on (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Write a short description of your experience and the policy you are commenting on.
* **Section Title**: This field may not apply to all employees, only fill it in if it's relevant to your feedback.
* **Feedback**: Provide detailed, specific feedback on the policy. Be as concrete and constructive as possible.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the policy on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, provide them here.
* **Contact Details**: Choose how you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Additional Details**: Add any additional information you think would be helpful for us to consider your feedback.
* **Signature**: Include your electronic or handwritten signature as proof of authenticity.
* **Attachment**: Upload any supporting documents or files relevant to your feedback.
