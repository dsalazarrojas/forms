># Bureaucracy Improvement Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and suggestions from employees about areas of improvement within the organization's bureaucratic processes. This feedback will help the organization to identify and prioritize areas to address, ultimately improving the work experience for all employees.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the sections below for a detailed explanation of each field.
- Fill in the required fields marked as `required` in the field-by-field explanations.
- Use the provided options for fields with dropdown menus or checkboxes.
- Enter your contact email if you are willing to be contacted for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Respondent name** (`respondent_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name. If you prefer to remain anonymous, leave this field blank.
- **Respondent affiliation** (`respondent_affiliation`, text, required/optional): If you are affiliated with a department or organization, please enter your affiliation.
- **Process area** (`process_area`, select_one, required): Please select the area of the bureaucratic process that you are providing feedback on. Options are:
  - Hiring and onboarding
  - Procurement and purchasing
  - Approvals and signoffs
  - Reporting and compliance
  - Other
- **Major pain points** (`pain_points`, text, required): Briefly describe the main issues you have encountered.
- **Impact severity** (`impact_severity`, select_one, required): Rate the impact of this issue on your work. Options are:
  - Low
  - Medium
  - High
  - Critical
- **Improvement suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have specific suggestions for improvement, please provide them in detail.
- **Estimated time saved per instance** (`estimated_time_saved`, number, optional): If you can estimate the time saved by addressing this issue, please enter the number of minutes.
- **Priority for change** (`priority_for_change`, select_one, required): Indicate your priority level for implementing this improvement. Options are:
  - Low
  - Medium
  - High
- **Willing to participate** (`willing_to_participate`, select_one, optional): If you are willing to participate in follow-up discussions, please select True. If not, select False.
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): If you agreed to participate, please enter your contact email.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing pain points and improvement suggestions.
- Consider the impact on your work and rate it accordingly.
- Provide an estimated time saved if you have an idea for how much time could be saved.
- Indicate your priority level for implementing this improvement.
- If you are willing to participate in follow-up discussions, please provide your contact email.
