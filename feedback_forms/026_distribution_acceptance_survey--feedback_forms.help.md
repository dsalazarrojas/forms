# Distribution Acceptance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Acceptance Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on how they perceive our distribution process. This feedback will help us refine and improve the process for all customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer them as honestly as possible.
2. Choose one or more options for each question, depending on your preferences.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the comments box.
4. Enter your email address and/or phone number for contact information.
5. Click submit to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution Acceptance Survey** (`distribution_acceptance_survey_page`, note, required: false): This is the introduction page of the survey. Read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How do you rate our distribution process?** (`distribution_acceptance_survey_question_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Rate our distribution process as:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **What do you like most about our distribution process?** (`distribution_acceptance_survey_question_2`, select_one, required: false): Choose the option that best describes what you like most about our distribution process:
	+ Reliability
	+ Speed
	+ Communication
	+ Punctuality
* **What do you like least about our distribution process?** (`distribution_acceptance_survey_question_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more options that describe what you like least about our distribution process:
	+ Long delays
	+ Poor communication
	+ Unreliability
	+ Lack of punctuality
* **Do you have any suggestions for us?** (`distribution_acceptance_survey_question_4`, select_multiple, required: true): If you have any suggestions for improvement, choose the option that best describes your suggestion:
	+ Yes, please let us know in the comments box.
	+ No, we have no comments for this time.
	+ We are still trying to process this.
* **Comments** (`distribution_acceptance_survey_comments`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for follow-up.

Note: This form will only submit if all required fields are filled out.
