# Business Notepad Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Notepad Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our company's product or service. This form is a structured way for users to share their opinions, thoughts, and suggestions to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by reading the form carefully and understand the questions asked.
2. Fill out each field according to your thoughts and experiences.
3. Make sure to give specific examples and details when asked for feedback.
4. Take your time and be as honest as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Your Feedback Matters --** (`section_feedback_intro`): This is the introduction to the form. Please read this carefully and understand that your feedback is valuable to us.
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`): This is an optional field for you to provide your name. If you don't want to provide your name, you can leave it blank.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`): This is an optional field for you to provide your email address. If you don't want to provide your email address, you can leave it blank.
* **Your Department** (`respondent_department`): This is an optional field for you to indicate which department you are part of.
* **-- Satisfaction Rating --** (`section_satisfaction`): This section will ask you to rate your overall satisfaction with our service.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level** (`overall_satisfaction`): Please select one of the given options to rate your overall satisfaction.
* **Quality of Our Product - Service** (`product_quality`): This question asks you to rate the quality of our product or service.
* **Customer Service Quality** (`customer_service`): This question asks you to rate the quality of our customer service.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`): This question asks you to rate whether our offering provides good value for money.
* **-- Detailed Feedback --** (`section_feedback`): This section is for you to provide detailed feedback about your experience.
* **What Went Well** (`what_went_well`): This question asks you to describe what we did right.
* **What Needs Improvement** (`what_needs_improvement`): This question asks you to describe what we could do better.
* **Did You Experience Any Specific Issues** (`specific_issues`): This question asks you to describe any specific issues you experienced.
* **-- Recommendations --** (`section_recommendations`): This section is for you to provide recommendations on what we could improve or add.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us** (`likelihood_recommend`): This question asks you to rate your likelihood of recommending us, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Requested Features or Services** (`feature_requests`): This question asks you to suggest any features or services you would like us to offer.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): This question asks you to provide any other feedback or comments you have.
* **May We Follow Up With You** (`followup_permission`): This question asks if you would like us to follow up with you about this feedback.
