# learning_motivation_during_hybrid_classes_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experiences during hybrid classes. The purpose of this form is to collect data on various aspects of the students' experiences, including their motivations, learning outcomes, and interactions with the teacher and peers. This will help the instructor to evaluate the effectiveness of the class format and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. For multiple choice questions, select only one option that best describes your experience.
3. For open-ended questions, type your response in the text box.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, please type them in the "Any comments" field.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that you have provided accurate and complete responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Question 1: What is the important thing you did during class?
* **What_is_the_important_thing_you_did_during_class** (`question_1`, `text`, required/optional): Describe in a few sentences what you think was the most important thing you did or learned during the class.

### Question 2: How did you feel during class?
* **How_did_you_feel_during_class** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): How did you feel during class? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Not satisfied

### Question 3: How much time did you spend on homework?
* **How_much_time_did_you_spend_on_homework** (`question_3`, `number`, required/optional): Estimate the amount of time you spent on homework.

### Question 4: What did you learn during class?
* **What_did_you_learn_during_class** (`question_4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): What did you learn during class? Choose all that apply:
	+ Learned new concepts
	+ Applied previous knowledge
	+ Didn't learn anything

### Question 5: How did you feel during class?
* **How_did_you_feel_during_class** (`question_5`, `time`, required/optional): How did you feel during class? Please provide a brief answer, including any time-related aspects of your experience.

### Question 6: How well did you understand the material?
* **How_well_did_you_understand_the_material** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required/optional): How well did you understand the material? Choose one of the following options:
	+ Very well
	+ Somewhat
	+ Not at all

### Question 7: How much attention did you get from the teacher?
* **How_much_attention_did_you_get_from_the_teacher** (`question_7`, `select_one`, required/optional): How much attention did you get from the teacher? Choose one of the following options:
	+ A lot
	+ Some
	+ None

### Question 8: How much support did you get from classmates?
* **How_much_support_did_you_get_from_classmates** (`question_8`, `select_one`, required/optional): How much support did you get from classmates? Choose one of the following options:
	+ A lot
	+ Some
	+ None

### Question 9: What did you find most helpful?
* **What_did_you_find_most_helpful** (`question_9`, `text`, required/optional): What did you find most helpful during the class?

### Question 10: What did you find most difficult?
* **What_did_you_find_most_difficult** (`question_10`, `text`, required/optional): What did you find most difficult during the class?

### Question 11: Do you like hybrid classes?
* **Do_you_like_hybrid_classes** (`question_11`, `select_one`, required/optional): Do you like hybrid classes? Choose one of the following options:
	+ True
	+ False

### Question 12: Any comments?
* **Any_comments** (`question_12`, `note`, required/optional): Do you have any feedback or comments about the class or this form? Please provide any additional information here.
