# App Functionality Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the overall performance and usability of the app. Your input will help us improve the app's functionality and provide a better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate information about your technical environment, device, and app version.
2. Answer the questions according to your experience with the app, using the scale provided for each question.
3. For any issues you encountered, please provide a description in the "Describe any bugs or errors encountered" field.
4. Make sure to answer each question honestly and thoroughly, as your feedback is essential for us to improve the app.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Technical Environment --** (Page 1)
	+ This is a note field where you can provide any relevant technical information about your environment.
* **Device Model** (Page 2)
	+ Please enter your device model, e.g., iPhone 13, Samsung S21.
* **Operating System Version** (Page 2)
	+ Enter the version of your operating system.
* **App Version** (Page 2)
	+ If you know the version of the app you are using, please enter it. You can usually find this information in the app's settings or about section.
* **Overall Functionality Rating** (Page 3)
	+ Rate the overall functionality of the app, from 1 (buggy) to 5 (flawless).
* **Loading Time Satisfaction** (Page 3)
	+ Choose how fast the app opens:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Satisfied
		- Neutral
		- Dissatisfied
* **Stability and Crash Frequency** (Page 4)
	+ Rate the app's stability:
		- Never Crashes
		- Rarely Crashes
		- Occasionally Crashes
		- Frequently Crashes
* **Ease of Navigation** (Page 4)
	+ Rate the ease of navigation:
		- Number 1-5, where 1 is difficult and 5 is easy.
* **Offline Functionality Performance** (Page 4)
	+ Choose how the app performs offline:
		- Works Great
		- Works with Limitations
		- Doesn't Work
		- N/A
* **Data Syncing Speed** (Page 5)
	+ Choose how fast the data syncing happens:
		- Instant
		- Fast
		- Slow
		- Failed Syncs
* **Notification Reliability** (Page 5)
	+ Choose how reliable the app's notifications are:
		- Always On Time
		- Sometimes Delayed
		- Never Received
		- N/A
* **Describe any bugs or errors encountered** (Page 6)
	+ Please describe any issues you experienced with the app.
* **Impact of issues on your productivity** (Page 6)
	+ Choose how much the issues affected your productivity:
		- No Impact
		- Minor Inconvenience
		- Significant Delay
		- Total Blocker
