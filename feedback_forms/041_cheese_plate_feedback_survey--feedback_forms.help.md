# Cheese Plate Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from guests about their experience with the cheese plate. Please take a few minutes to share your thoughts and help us improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the required information for each section, and provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have. Don't worry, we value your feedback and will use it to improve our cheese plate experience for future guests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
Here's an explanation of each field in the form:

* **Guest Name** (`guest_full_name_opt`, text, required/optional): This is the name of the guest who is submitting the form.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_occurrence_date`, date, required): This is the date when the guest visited the cheese plate.
* **Rating of Cheese Variety and Selection** (`cheese_variety_rating_score`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to rate how well the cheese selection met your expectations.
* **Rating of Freshness and Quality** (`cheese_freshness_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the freshness and quality of the cheese selection.
* **Rating of Plate Presentation / Aesthetics** (`plate_presentation_rating_score`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to rate the appearance of the cheese plate.
* **Favorite Cheese Item** (`favorite_cheese_item`, text, optional): If you had a favorite cheese on the plate, let us know what it was.
* **Which Pairings Did You Enjoy Most** (`pairing_satisfaction_choices`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the pairings that you enjoyed the most.
* **Were the Portion Sizes Appropriate** (`portion_size_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options to rate the portion sizes.
* **Overall Value for Money** (`value_for_money_rating_score`, select_one, required): Rate the overall value for money of the experience.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`general_guest_comments_area`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Server's Knowledge about Cheeses** (`staff_service_rating_score`, select_one, optional): Rate the server's knowledge about the cheeses.
* **Submission Date** (`feedback_submission_timestamp`, date, required): This is the date when the form is submitted.

Please note that all fields are labeled with their respective names, labels, and types. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
