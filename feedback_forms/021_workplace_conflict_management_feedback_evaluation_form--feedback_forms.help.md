# Workplace Conflict Management Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees provide feedback on workplace conflicts that have not been resolved through open communication and mediation. It's essential for maintaining a safe and respectful work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant category from the "Feedback Forms" dropdown menu, which is usually "Feedback Forms".
2.  Provide a clear and concise description of the conflict or issue that you're experiencing or have experienced.
3.  Choose the tool or system that was (or is) being used to manage the conflict. Please select "chatjimmy" if that's the assigned tool, otherwise choose "Other".
4.  Enter your unique form ID if you're filling out this form, otherwise leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Feedback Forms**
	* (Select Multiple, Required: false)
	* Please select the relevant category for your feedback from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand the context and purpose of your feedback.

### 2. **description**
	* (Text, Required: false)
	* Briefly describe the conflict or issue that you're experiencing. Be as clear and concise as possible to help us understand the situation.

### 3. **assigned tool**
	* (Select One, Required: true)
	* Choose the tool or system used to manage the conflict, such as "chatjimmy" or another tool. This helps us understand the specific context and resolution process used.

### 4. **form id**
	* (Number, Required: false)
	* If you're filling out this form, enter your unique form ID (if applicable). This is an optional field and usually not required, unless you're specifically asked to do so. 

## Tips
- Be truthful and honest in your feedback.
- Try to be specific about the details and circumstances surrounding the conflict.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to your supervisor or HR department for guidance.
- Ensure this form is not filled out for every interaction or issue; only for serious conflicts that require attention and resolution.
