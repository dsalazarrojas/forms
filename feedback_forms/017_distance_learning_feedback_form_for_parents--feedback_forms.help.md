# Distance Learning Feedback Form For Parents - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents about their child's distance learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child enrolled in the distance learning program.
* **Class Name**: Enter the name of the class your child is enrolled in.
* **Comments/Feedback**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about the distance learning program.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improving the program, select all that apply.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when you are submitting this form.
* **Submitted By**: This field is automatically populated based on the user's ID.
