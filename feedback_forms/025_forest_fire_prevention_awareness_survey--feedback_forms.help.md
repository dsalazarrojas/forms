# forest_fire_prevention_awareness_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The forest fire prevention awareness survey aims to collect information on users' wildfire risk perceptions and their awareness on prevention measures. This data will help forest fire authorities and experts understand the public's perception of wildfires and their willingness to take actions to prevent them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant page and fill in the provided fields.
2. Enter your general information in the "General Info" page.
3. Assess your wildfire risk in the "Wildfire Risk" page.
4. Describe the benefits of prevention measures in the "Prevention Benefits" page.
5. List the actions you take to prevent wildfires in the "Prevention Actions" page.
6. Indicate your prevention behaviors in the "Prevention Behaviors" page.
7. Choose your fire safety habits in the "Fire Safety" page.
8. Mention prevention measures you use in the "Prevention Measures" page.
9. Provide your awareness date in the "Awareness" page.
10. Enter your contact email and information in the "Contact Info" and "Email" pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (General Info, text, required)**: This is the introductory page where you provide your general information, which is not specified, so just enter the information you feel is relevant.
* **Wildfire Risk (Wildfire Risk, select_one, required)**: Here, you select your perception of the risk of wildires, choosing from High, Low, or Medium.
* **Prevention Benefits (Prevention Benefits, text, required)**: In this field, describe the benefits you get from preventing wildfires, e.g., protecting your property, environment, etc.
* **Prevention Actions (Prevention Actions, text, required)**: List the actions you take to prevent wildfires, such as creating a defensible space or using fire-resistant materials.
* **Prevention Behaviors (Prevention Behaviors, text, required)**: Indicate your daily habits and behaviors that contribute to wildfire prevention, such as clearing dry leaves or not throwing lit cigarettes on the forest floor.
* **Fire Safety (Fire Safety, select_multiple, required)**: Choose when you are always, sometimes, or never taking fire safety precautions.
* **Prevention Measures (Prevention Measures, text, required)**: Describe the measures you take to prevent wildfires, such as fire breaks or fire-resistant roofs.
* **Awareness (Awareness, date, required)**: Provide the date you became aware of the prevention measures.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, email, required)**: Enter your name and email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.

Note: This form is designed to collect feedback, not to be a comprehensive survey on wildfire prevention, so the field explanations are brief and focused on the most critical information.
