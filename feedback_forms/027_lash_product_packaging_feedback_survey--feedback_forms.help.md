# Lash Product Packaging Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Product Packaging Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from customers on the packaging of a particular product. This survey aims to help us better understand your experience with our product packaging, identify areas for improvement, and gather suggestions for future product development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, follow these steps:

1. Read through the survey questions carefully and answer the ones that apply to your experience with our product.
2. Choose the options that best match your thoughts and opinions.
3. Fill out any required fields, which are marked as "required".
4. Submit your feedback and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <product> (ID 2)
* **Product**: Choose "Yes" if the product packaging was satisfactory, or "No" if you have any issues or concerns.

### <rating> (ID 3)
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the product packaging on a scale of 0-10, where 10 is the best.

### <packaging> (ID 4)
* **Packaging**: Choose "Yes" if the product packaging meets your needs and expectations, or "No" if you think there are any issues with it.

### <suggestions> (ID 6)
* **Suggestions**: Choose "Yes" if you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the product packaging, or "No" otherwise.

### <email> (ID 7)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your feedback.

### <name> (ID 9)
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like us to address you.

### <phone> (ID 8)
* **Phone**: This is optional, but if you have any phone number where we can contact you, please enter it.

### <contact_email> (ID 10)
* **Contact Email**: This is optional, but if you have a preferred email address for us to contact you, please enter it.

### <contact_phone> (ID 11)
* **Contact Phone**: This is optional, but if you have a preferred phone number for us to contact you, please enter it.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
* All feedback and suggestions are confidential and will not be shared with anyone without your permission.
