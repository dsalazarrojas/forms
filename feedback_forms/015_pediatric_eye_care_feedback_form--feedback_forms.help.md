# pediatric_eye_care_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents about their experience with pediatric eye care services. It's intended to help improve the quality of care provided to children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you're not submitting duplicate feedback for the same case or incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide detailed feedback about your experience with pediatric eye care services.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please provide the name of the parent or caregiver who received services.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This field is for your contact information, in case we need to reach out to you for further clarification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is specifically for providing feedback about your experience with pediatric eye care services.
- **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with the service provided, using a number (e.g., 1-10).
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is for reporting the time of your experience with pediatric eye care services.
- **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): If you have any specific goals or expectations for future care, please specify them here.
- **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically populated with the date and time the form was submitted.
