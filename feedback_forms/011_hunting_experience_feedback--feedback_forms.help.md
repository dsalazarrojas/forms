# Hunting Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from hunters after their experience with a trip or adventure. Please take a few minutes to complete the form to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall experience with the trip, including the trip itself, guides, and accommodations.
2. Rate your experience with each of the following aspects: trip, guides, and accommodations.
3. Upload any photos you took during the trip to help us get a better understanding of your experience.
4. Share any comments or feedback you have about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with the trip, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Trip Id** (`trip_id`, `number`, required: false): This field is for internal use only and not required for your feedback.
* **Hunter Rating** (`hunter_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience as a hunter, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Guide Rating** (`guide_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with the guide, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Accommodations** (`accommodations`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of the accommodations provided, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Food** (`food`, `number`, required: false): Rate the quality of the food provided, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `number`, required: false): Rate the communication with the guide, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience again, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Photos** (`photos`, `text`, required: false): Upload any photos you took during the trip to help us get a better understanding of your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any comments or feedback you have about your trip.
* **Trip Photo** (`trip_photo`, `text`, required: false): Upload a photo of the trip, which will be displayed on our website.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when rating your experience.
* You can upload multiple photos, separated by commas or newlines.
* You can share your comments or feedback in as much detail as you would like.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
