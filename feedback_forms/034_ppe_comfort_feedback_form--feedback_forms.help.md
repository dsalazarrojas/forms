# PPE Comfort Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback about the comfort level of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) used in the workplace. Please complete this form to provide your honest feedback on the PPE.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please start by filling in the "General Info" field with any additional information you'd like to provide.
2. In the "PPE Info" field, enter any details about the specific PPE you are providing feedback on.
3. If you are not comfortable with the PPE provided, select the corresponding comfort level in the "PPE Comfort" field. If you are comfortable, you can leave this field blank.
4. In the "Comfort Level" field, select how comfortable you are with the PPE from the available options.
5. Enter any comments or observations in the "Comments" field if you'd like to provide more information.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to ensure we can follow up with you, if necessary.
7. Type your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, if you'd like to be contacted.
8. Select your department from the available options in the "Department" field.
9. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
10. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: Enter any additional information about the PPE you are providing feedback on.
* **PPE Info**: Enter any details about the specific PPE you are providing feedback on, such as model number, size, or type.
* **PPE Comfort**: If you are not comfortable with the PPE, select how not comfortable you are from the available options. If you are comfortable, leave this field blank.
* **Comfort Level**: Select how comfortable you are with the PPE from the available options.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations about the PPE.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you, if necessary.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Department**: Select your department from the available options.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.
