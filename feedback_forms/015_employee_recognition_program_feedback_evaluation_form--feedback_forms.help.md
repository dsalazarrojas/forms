# Employee Recognition Program Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Recognition Program Feedback Evaluation Form is a form used to evaluate the effectiveness of the Employee Recognition Program. It is intended to gather feedback from employees and reviewers to help identify areas of improvement and provide insights for future program development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee ID number of the employee being evaluated.
2. Select a rating for the employee's performance (Good, Fair, or another option).
3. Provide feedback on the employee's performance in the text box.
4. If applicable, enter any suggestions for improvement.
5. Answer whether or not the employee is part of a recognition program.
6. Enter the employee's name.
7. Enter the name of the employee's team.
8. Enter the department the employee works in.
9. Answer whether or not the employee received feedback on their performance.
10. Enter the date and time the feedback was submitted.
11. Enter the name and email of the reviewer who submitted the feedback.
12. Enter the name of the team the reviewer is a part of.
13. Select the status of the review (Active or Inactive).
14. Enter the date and time the review was completed.
15. Enter the email of the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, Number, **Required**): Enter the ID number of the employee being evaluated.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Select One, **Required**): Select one of the following options: Good, Fair.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, **Optional**): Provide any additional feedback or comments on the employee's performance.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, Text, **Optional**): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **Recognition Programs** (`recognition_programs`, Select Multiple, **Required**): Select whether or not the employee is part of a recognition program.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the employee's team.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the department the employee works in.
* **Received Feedback** (`feedback_type`, Select One, **Required**): Select whether or not the employee received feedback on their performance.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, Date, **Optional**): Enter the date the feedback was submitted.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Date, **Optional**): Enter the date the feedback was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, Time, **Optional**): Enter the time the feedback was submitted.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, Note, **Optional**): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, Email, **Optional**): Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, Date, **Optional**): Enter the date the feedback was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, Time, **Optional**): Enter the time the feedback was submitted.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, Select One, **Optional**): Select the status of the review (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, Date, **Optional**): Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, Time, **Optional**): Enter the time the review was completed.
* **Reviewer Team** (`reviewer_team`, Text, **Optional**): Enter the team the reviewer is a part of.

Note: The fields with **Required** are mandatory and must be completed before submitting the form.
