# Fitness Class Instructor Energy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Class Instructor Energy Survey is designed to collect feedback and information from fitness class instructors about their energy levels, availability, and preferences. This data will be used for scheduling and resource allocation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. First Name: Enter your name as it appears in your personal records.
2. Last Name: Enter your surname (optional).
3. Email: Enter your personal or work email address (required).
4. Phone Number: Enter your mobile or landline number (optional).
5. Feedback: Share any comments or suggestions you may have about the fitness class schedule (required).
6. Availability: Select the days you are available to work (not required).
7. Time of Day: Choose the hour when you are most available (not required).
8. Energy Level: Select your energy level, which can be High, Low, or any other available option.
9. Exercise Intensity: Choose the intensity of your exercise classes, which can be Low, Medium, or High.
10. Instructor Type: Select the type of instructor you are, which can be Personal, Small Group, Large Group, or Outdoor.
11. Experience Level: Select your experience level, which can be Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
12. Certification: Choose your certification status, which can be Certified, Non-Certified, or No Preference.
13. Preferred Time: Select the hour when you would like to work (not required).
14. Preferred Location: Choose whether you prefer to work at this location (not required).
15. Preferred Location Type: Select the type of location you prefer, which can be yes or no.
16. Available Days: Select the days you are available to work (not required).
17. Available Time: Choose whether you are available to work at the selected time (not required).
18. Comments: Select whether you have any comments or concerns (not required).
