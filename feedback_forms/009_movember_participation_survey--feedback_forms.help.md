# Movember Participation Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect participation data from individuals and organizations interested in Movember initiatives. The collected data will be used to understand user engagement and tailor future initiatives to better suit the community's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your Participant ID (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
2. Provide your name.
3. Enter your email address (required to ensure we can contact you).
4. Select your organization's participation type (Nonprofit, Company, or Community group).
5. Choose your motivations for participating in Movember (optional, but helps us understand your interests).
6. Provide your feedback on our initiatives (required to help us improve).
7. Indicate your willingness to participate in future Movember events (required).
8. Select any other interests you have in Movember (optional, but helps us tailor future initiatives).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your Participant ID, if you have one. This helps us track your participation in our initiatives.
* **Name** (`name`, text): Please enter your name as you would like to be known in the Movember community.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about Movember initiatives.
* **Organization Participation** (`organization_participation`, select_one, required): Select whether you represent a Nonprofit, Company, or Community group.
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your motivations for participating in Movember. Select all that apply.
	+ Help others
	+ Be part of something bigger
	+ Get involved in the Movember community
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Share your thoughts about our initiatives. This helps us improve and adapt our efforts.
* **Future Participation** (`future_participation`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are willing to participate in future Movember events.
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Other interest** (`other_interest`, select_multiple, optional): If you have other interests in Movember, select all that apply.
	+ Movember event participation
	+ Movember social media engagement
	+ Movember newsletter subscription
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Other interest in Movember** (`other_interest_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other (please specify)" in the previous field, provide a brief description of your other interest in Movember.
