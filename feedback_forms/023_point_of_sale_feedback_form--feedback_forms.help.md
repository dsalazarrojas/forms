# point_of_sale_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from customers after a point of sale interaction. The goal of this form is to gather valuable insights from customers about their experience, which can be used to improve customer service and employee performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name and contact information.
2. Provide feedback about your experience with the product and store.
3. Rate the product and store on a scale of 1-5.
4. Choose the store and product you interacted with.
5. Indicate whether you interacted with an employee during your visit.
6. Select any other additional information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to receive a response to your feedback.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, Text, Optional): Share your thoughts and comments about your experience with the product and store.
* **Product Rating** (Product Rating, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Reason** (Reason, Text, Optional): If you'd like to provide more details about your feedback, please use this field.
* **Date** (Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time** (Time, Time, Optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Store Name** (Store Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the store where you made your purchase.
* **Employee Name** (Employee Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee who assisted you.
* **Store ID** (Store ID, Text, Optional): Enter the store ID number.
* **Product Name** (Product Name, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the product you purchased.
* **Customer ID** (Customer ID, Text, Optional): Enter your customer ID number.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, Text, Optional): Enter the ID number of the employee who served you.
* **Reason 2** (Reason 2, Text, Optional): If you have additional feedback, please use this field.
* **Store Rating** (Store Rating, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the store on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Rating 2** (Product Rating 2, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Name 2** (Product Name 2, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the product you're referring to in your feedback.
* **Employee Name 2** (Employee Name 2, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee who served you.
* **Customer Name 2** (Customer Name 2, Text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **Store Name 2** (Store Name 2, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the store where you made your purchase.
* **Store Rating 2** (Store Rating 2, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the store on a scale of 1-5.
* **Employee Rating** (Employee Rating, Number, Optional): Rate the employee's performance on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Rating 3** (Product Rating 3, Number, Optional): Rate your experience with the product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Customer Rating** (Customer Rating, Number, Optional): Rate your experience as a customer on a scale of 1-5.
* **Store** (Store, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the stores you interacted with.
* **Product** (Product, Select One, Optional): Select the product you interacted with.
* **Employee** (Employee, Select One, Optional): Select whether you interacted with an employee during your visit.
* **Customer** (Customer, Select Multiple, Optional): Select whether you interacted with a customer during your visit.
