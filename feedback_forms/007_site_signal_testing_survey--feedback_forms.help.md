# Site Signal Testing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Site Signal Testing Survey" is designed to gather feedback about your experience with site signal strength, type, quality, performance, and any issues you may have encountered while using our site. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and provide a better experience for all users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each page and understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question in the best way you can, based on your actual experience with our site.
3. Submit your feedback once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This page provides a brief explanation of the purpose and goals of the survey.
* **How strong is your site signal?** (`how_strong_is_your_site_signal`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose one of the options: weak, medium, or strong.
	+ This question helps us understand the overall strength of your site signal.
* **What type of signal do you have?** (`signal_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose one of the options: cellular, wifi, or wired.
	+ This question helps us understand the type of signal you are using to access our site.
* **What is your signal quality?** (`signal_quality`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose all that apply: weak, medium, strong.
	+ This question helps us understand the overall quality of your signal.
* **How good was your performance?** (`performance`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose one of the options: slow, average, fast.
	+ This question helps us understand how your performance was during the site usage.
* **How many dropouts did you experience?** (`dropouts`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of times you experienced a dropout, if any.
	+ This question helps us track any issues you may have encountered while using our site.
* **Did you experience any coverage issues?** (`coverage`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose one of the options: true or false.
	+ This question helps us know if you experienced any issues with coverage while using our site.
* **How many signal drops did you experience?** (`signal_drops`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of signal drops you experienced, if any.
	+ This question helps us track any signal drops you encountered.
* **How many coverage issues drops did you experience?** (`coverage_issues_drops`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of coverage issues you experienced.
	+ This question helps us understand how many times you encountered issues due to coverage.
* **How many performance issues did you experience?** (`performance_issues`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the number of performance issues you experienced.
	+ This question helps us track any performance issues you encountered.
* **Total Score** (`total_score`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your total score, based on your experience with our site.
	+ This question helps us calculate your overall score and identify areas for improvement.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately, based on your actual experience with our site.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the site and provide a better experience for all users.
