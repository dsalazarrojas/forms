# Exhibition Visitor Experience Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from visitors on their experiences, interests, and opinions about the exhibition. It will help the exhibition organizers to understand what worked well and what didn't, and to make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the most appropriate option for each question, and provide honest answers based on your experience. You can select multiple options for some questions, as indicated in the question label. Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (`Overall Experience`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your overall experience at the exhibition. Select one or more of the options that best describe your experience.

### 2. How interested were you in the exhibition?
* **How interested were you in the exhibition?** (`How interested were you in the exhibition?`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your level of interest in the exhibition. Select the option that best describes your level of interest.

### 3. What level of interest do you have in the exhibition?
* **What level of interest do you have in the exhibition?** (`What level of interest do you have in the exhibition?`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your level of interest in the exhibition. Select the option that best describes your level of interest.

### 4. What value do you get from attending this exhibition?
* **What value do you get from attending this exhibition?** (`What value do you get from attending this exhibition?`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about the value you get from attending the exhibition. Select one or more of the options that best describe your experience.

### 5. What impression do you have of the exhibition?
* **What impression do you have of the exhibition?** (`What impression do you have of the exhibition?`, `select_multiple`, required): This question asks about your impression of the exhibition. Select one or more of the options that best describe your impression.

### 6. Level of Impression?
* **Level of Impression?** (`Level of Impression?`, `number`, required): This question asks about the level of impression you have of the exhibition. Enter a numerical value between 1 and 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.

### 7. Do you recommend this exhibition to others?
* **Do you recommend this exhibition to others?** (`Do you recommend this exhibition to others?`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your recommendation to others. Select the option that best describes your recommendation.

### 8. How relevant is this exhibition to your needs?
* **How relevant is this exhibition to your needs?** (`How relevant is this exhibition to your needs?`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the relevance of the exhibition to your needs. Select the option that best describes your relevance.

### 9. Will you attend this exhibition again?
* **Will you attend this exhibition again?** (`Will you attend this exhibition again?`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about your willingness to attend the exhibition again. Select the option that best describes your willingness.

### 10. How relevant is this exhibition to your interests?
* **How relevant is this exhibition to your interests?** (`How relevant is this exhibition to your interests?`, `select_one`, required): This question asks about the relevance of the exhibition to your interests. Select the option that best describes your relevance.
