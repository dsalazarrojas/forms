# start_and_stop_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The start_and_stop_evaluation_form is used to collect feedback and evaluation data from end-users on a specific process. This form is designed to gather information about the process and its associated evaluation period.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "start_and_stop_evaluation_form" to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter your contact information, including contact number, contact email, and contact phone.
4. Choose a rating for the process (low or high).
5. Select the start and stop dates for the evaluation period.
6. Provide a reason for stopping the evaluation (if applicable).
7. Click on the submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the organization associated with this evaluation.
* **Process Name** (`Process Name`, `text`, \*): Enter the name of the process being evaluated.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the process.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the person associated with this evaluation.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email of the person associated with this evaluation.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact phone number of the person associated with this evaluation.
* **Rating** (`Rating`, `select_one`, \*): Select a rating for the process (low or high).
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, \*): Enter the start date of the evaluation period.
* **Stop Date** (`Stop Date`, `date`, \*): Enter the stop date of the evaluation period.
* **Reason** (`Reason`, `text`, \*): Provide a reason for stopping the evaluation (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all necessary permissions and approvals before completing this form.
* Be as detailed as possible in your responses to provide useful feedback.
* If you're unable to provide a reason for stopping the evaluation, select "N/A" in the Reason field.
