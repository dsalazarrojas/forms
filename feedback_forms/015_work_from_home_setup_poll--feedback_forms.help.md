# Work From Home Setup Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work From Home Setup Poll is a form designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the current work-from-home setup. This feedback will help us understand how to improve the work-from-home experience for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in each field as instructed, and make sure to answer honestly.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Home Office Setup** (`home_office_setup`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can provide feedback about your home office setup. Please type out your thoughts on how we can improve the space, equipment, and overall environment.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to mention any work-related locations outside your home that you visit frequently (e.g., office, coffee shop, co-working space).
* **Remote Work Status** (`remote_work_status`, `select_one`, optional): This question asks about your current remote work status, and you can choose between "Active" (you work from home regularly) or "Inactive" (you work from home occasionally or not at all).
* **Setup and Support** (`setup_support`, `select_multiple`, optional): Here, you can select all that apply to your experience with work-from-home setup and support. You can choose from "Yes" (you have received adequate support) and "No" (you have not received adequate support).
* **Work Arrangements** (`work_arrangements`, `text`, optional): This field is for you to share any work arrangements that you have in place while working from home, such as a schedule, dedicated workspace, or other relevant information).

Note: Remember to be as detailed and specific as possible when answering these questions, and feel free to add any additional comments or suggestions. Your feedback is crucial to help us improve the work-from-home experience.
