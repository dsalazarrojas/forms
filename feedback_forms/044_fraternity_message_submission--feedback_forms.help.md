# Fraternity Message Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a message submission tool for the fraternity organization, allowing you to provide feedback, ask questions, or share concerns with the fraternity. Please use this form to submit your thoughts and opinions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Fraternity Message Submission Form" page.
- Fill in the required fields (marked as required) and provide any additional information you would like to share.
- Select any applicable options from the "Do you have any questions?" dropdown menu.
- Choose the submit or reset button at the bottom of the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fraternity Message Submission Form** (`fraternity_message_submission_form`, text, required: false):
  This is the main form where you can provide your message, feedback, or questions.
- **Questions and Feedback** (`questions_and_feedback`, text, required: false):
  Enter your questions, concerns, or feedback about the fraternity.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required: false):
  Provide your name so that we can associate your message with you.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
  Enter your email address so that we can contact you if necessary.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false):
  Provide a contact number in case we need to call you directly.
- **Message** (`message`, note, required: false):
  Write your message, feedback, or questions here.
- **Do you have any questions?** (`question`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Select all that apply:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
- **Date** (`date`, date, required: false):
  Enter the date you are submitting your message.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required: false):
  Enter the time you are submitting your message.
- **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false):
  - **Submit**: Send your message to us.
  - **Reset**: Clear the form and start over.
## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
- Be clear and concise in your message.
- Use a valid email address if you want us to contact you.

Note: This help guide is based on the assumption that the "submit" button is for submitting the form, but it's worth double-checking with further context to confirm.
