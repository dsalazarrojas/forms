# Customer Feedback Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our company. It is essential to provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your feedback in the "Feedback Description" field.
3. Choose your level of satisfaction with our service from the "Satisfaction" dropdown menu.
4. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can write them in the "Note" field.
5. Optionally, you can include your email address in the "Email Address" field so we can contact you if needed.
6. Select a date and time for when you interacted with our service in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Feedback Description** (`feedback`, text, required): Describe your experience with our company. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of satisfaction with our service. Our options are:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us here. Please note that this field is not required.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): If you would like us to contact you regarding your feedback, please enter your email address here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you interacted with our service.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time you interacted with our service.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, you can write them here. This field is not required.
