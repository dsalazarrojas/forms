# IPad Cases Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with iPad cases. Your input will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field, if you wish to receive a copy of your feedback.
3. Share your thoughts, ideas, and experiences in the "feedback" field.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the rating field (1-5).
5. Select a category for your feedback from the "category" dropdown list (e.g., "iPad Cases").
6. Add any additional comments or thoughts in the "comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name to help us identify you and personalize your feedback.
* **email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you wish to receive a copy of your feedback. This is optional and not required to submit the form.
* **feedback** (`text`, required: false): Share your thoughts, experiences, and ideas about the iPad case. This could be positive or negative feedback.
* **rating** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the ratings that apply to your experience (1, 2, 3, 4, 5).
* **category** (`select_one`, required: false): Select a category that best describes your feedback (e.g., "iPad Cases", "Other").
* **comment** (`text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with the iPad case.
 
## Tips
* Please be honest and sincere in your feedback. Your input will help us improve our products and services.
* You can select multiple ratings to provide a more comprehensive review of your experience.
