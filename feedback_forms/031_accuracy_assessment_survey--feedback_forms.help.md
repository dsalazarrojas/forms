# Accuracy Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is intended to gather feedback on the accuracy and precision of our product or service. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance our offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Fill out the required fields: **Product or service**, **Accuracy rating**, and **Frequency of errors**.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the **Additional comments** field if you feel it's necessary.
4. Select your willingness to discuss further with our team in the **Willingness to discuss further** field if you're open to it.
5. You can skip the optional fields: **Respondent name** (if you prefer to remain anonymous), **Impact of inaccuracy**, **Examples of inaccuracy**, and **Suggestions for correction**, if you don't have any information to provide.
6. Review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product or service** (Text, Required): This is the item you are assessing the accuracy of. For example, a product, service, or feature.
* **Accuracy rating** (Select one, Required): Rate the accuracy or precision of the product or service. You can choose from: Very accurate, Accurate, Acceptable, Inaccurate, or Very inaccurate.
* **Frequency of errors** (Select one, Required): Describe how often you observe inaccuracies in the product or service. You can choose from: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, or Always.
* **Respondent name** (Text, Optional): If you're willing to share your name, please enter it here. This is not required.
* **Accuracy rating** (Select one, Required): Rate the accuracy or precision of the product or service. You can choose from: Very accurate, Accurate, Acceptable, Inaccurate, or Very inaccurate.
* **Frequency of errors** (Select one, Required): Describe how often you observe inaccuracies in the product or service. You can choose from: Never, Rarely, Sometimes, Often, or Always.
* **Impact of inaccuracy** (Text, Optional): Describe the consequences of inaccuracies you've observed.
* **Examples of inaccuracy** (Text, Optional): If you have specific examples, please provide them here.
* **Suggestions for correction** (Text, Optional): Offer practical steps to improve the accuracy of the product or service.
* **Willingness to discuss further** (Select one, Optional): If you're open to discussing your feedback with our team, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
* **Additional comments** (Text, Optional): Any other feedback or comments about the product or service.

Tips:
* Please answer the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* We value your feedback and will consider it carefully in our decision-making process.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
