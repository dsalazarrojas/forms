# Book Club Post Discussion Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from book club members after each discussion meeting. Your input will help us understand your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Meeting Date** in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter the **Book Title** and **Author** of the book discussed.
3.  Select your level of **Overall Satisfaction** with the discussion, from **Very Satisfied** to **Very Dissatisfied**.
4.  Rate the **Quality of Discussion**, from **Excellent** to **Poor**.
5.  Evaluate the **Facilitator Performance**, from **Excellent** to **Poor**. (Note: This field is optional.)
6.  Choose your level of **Participation** in the discussion, from **Very Active** to **Did Not Participate**.
7.  Indicate how you felt during the discussion, from **Very Comfortable** to **Very Uncomfortable**.
8.  Select the most **Valuable Aspect** of the discussion, from **Different perspectives shared** to **Personal connections**.
9.  Enter any topics you would like to explore more in the **Topics You Wanted More Discussion On** field.
10.  Choose how you felt about the **Meeting Length**, from **Too Short** to **Too Long**.
11.  Rate the **Meeting Location**, from **Excellent** to **Poor**. (Note: This field is optional.)
12.  Indicate whether you would **Recommend this book club** to others, from **Definitely Yes** to **Definitely No**.
13.  Choose how likely you are to attend the **Next Meeting**, from **Very Likely** to **Very Unlikely**.
14.  Provide any **Improvement Suggestions** for future discussions.
15.  Enter any additional **Additional Comments** you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name, first and last.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Your contact email.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required): Date of the discussion meeting.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, `text`, required): Title of the book discussed.
* **Book Author** (`book_author`, `text`, required): Author of the book.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): How satisfied were you with today's discussion.
	+ **Very Satisfied**: Very positive experience.
	+ **Satisfied**: Positive experience.
	+ **Neutral**: Neither positive nor negative experience.
	+ **Dissatisfied**: Negative experience.
	+ **Very Dissatisfied**: Very negative experience.
* **Quality of Discussion** (`discussion_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of discussion.
	+ **Excellent**: High-quality discussion.
	+ **Very Good**: Good discussion.
	+ **Good**: Average discussion.
	+ **Fair**: Below-average discussion.
	+ **Poor**: Low-quality discussion.
* **Facilitator Performance** (`facilitator_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the discussion facilitator.
	+ **Excellent**: High-quality facilitation.
	+ **Very Good**: Good facilitation.
	+ **Good**: Average facilitation.
	+ **Fair**: Below-average facilitation.
	+ **Poor**: Low-quality facilitation.
* **Participation Level** (`participation_level`, `select_one`, required): How much did you participate.
	+ **Very Active**: Highly active participant.
	+ **Moderately Active**: Moderately active participant.
	+ **Somewhat Active**: Somewhat active participant.
	+ **Mostly Listened**: Mostly listened.
	+ **Did Not Participate**: Did not participate at all.
* **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, `select_one`, required): How comfortable did you feel sharing.
	+ **Very Comfortable**: Very comfortable sharing.
	+ **Comfortable**: Comfortable sharing.
	+ **Neutral**: Neither comfortable nor uncomfortable sharing.
	+ **Uncomfortable**: Uncomfortable sharing.
	+ **Very Uncomfortable**: Very uncomfortable sharing.
* **Most Valuable Aspect** (`most_valuable_aspect`, `select_one`, required): What did you find most valuable.
	+ **Different perspectives shared**: Sharing diverse perspectives.
	+ **Deep analysis of themes**: In-depth analysis of themes.
	+ **Character discussions**: Character-driven discussions.
	+ **Historical context**: Historical context provided.
	+ **Personal connections**: Personal connections made.
	+ **Group dynamics**: Group dynamics.
* **Topics You Wanted More Discussion On** (`topics_wanted`, `text`, optional): What topics you would like to explore more.
* **Meeting Length** (`meeting_length`, `select_one`, required): How was the meeting length.
	+ **Too Short**: Too short.
	+ **Just Right**: Just right.
	+ **Too Long**: Too long.
* **Meeting Location Rating** (`location_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the meeting location.
	+ **Excellent**: High-quality location.
	+ **Very Good**: Good location.
	+ **Good**: Average location.
	+ **Fair**: Below-average location.
	+ **Poor**: Low-quality location.
* **Would You Recommend** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Would you recommend this book club.
	+ **Definitely Yes**: Definitely recommend.
	+ **Probably Yes**: Probably recommend.
	+ **Not Sure**: Not sure.
	+ **Probably No**: Probably not recommend.
	+ **Definitely No**: Definitely not recommend.
* **Interest in Next Meeting** (`next_meeting_interest`, `select_one`, required): How likely will you attend next meeting.
	+ **Very Likely**: Very likely attend.
	+ **Likely**: Likely attend.
	+ **Not Sure**: Not sure.
	+ **Unlikely**: Unlikely attend.
	+ **Very Unlikely**: Very unlikely attend.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other feedback to share.

## Tips

- Be honest and sincere in your feedback.
- Provide specific examples when giving suggestions for improvement.
- Try to answer all questions, but if you have no opinion, select "Not Sure" or "Neutral" if applicable.
