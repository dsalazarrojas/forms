# TNR Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The TNR Experience Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from volunteers and animal welfare organizations involved in Trap-Neuter-Return (TNR) programs. This survey aims to gather information on the effectiveness and challenges of TNR programs, allowing us to better understand the experiences of those involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the survey date and time when the TNR program was conducted.
2. Enter the name of the animal that participated in the program (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
3. Select the species of the animal (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
4. Enter the name of the volunteer(s) who participated in the program.
5. If the volunteer is part of a group, enter the name of the group.
6. Enter the name of the shelter or organization where the program was conducted.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments about the experience.
8. Rate your satisfaction with the TNR program.
9. Describe any challenges encountered during the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the TNR program was conducted.
* **Survey Time** (`survey_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of day when the TNR program was conducted.
* **Animal Name** (`animal_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the animal that participated in the program.
* **Animal Species** (`animal_species`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the species of the animal (e.g., Cat, Dog, Other).
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the volunteer who participated in the program.
* **Volunteer Group** (`volunteer_group`, text, required: false): If the volunteer is part of a group, enter the name of the group.
* **Shelter Name** (`shelter_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the shelter or organization where the program was conducted.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Describe any challenges encountered during the program.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, required: false): Describe any challenges encountered during the program.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the TNR program (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Opportunities** (`opportunities`, text, required: false): Describe any opportunities for improvement or growth.

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field to help us better understand your experience.
* If you are a volunteer, please ensure that the shelter or organization's name is accurate.
* This survey is completely anonymous, and your responses will not be shared without your consent.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
