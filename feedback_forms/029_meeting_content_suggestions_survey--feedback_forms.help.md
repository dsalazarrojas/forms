# Meeting Content Suggestions Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to gather user input on meeting content and frequency. Your responses will help the meeting organizers create more engaging and effective meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each field with your thoughts and answers.
2. You can add or remove pages depending on the specific questions that apply to you.
3. If you have any additional feedback, feel free to type it in the 'Meeting Content Suggestions Survey 4'.
4. Make sure to enter a valid email address in 'Meeting Content Suggestions Survey 5'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **What topics should we cover in our next meeting?**
* (Meeting Content Suggestions Survey 1)
	+ This field is where you can suggest topics for the next meeting.
	+ Enter a brief description of the topic you'd like to cover.

### 2. **What time of day would you prefer for our next meeting?**
* (Meeting Content Suggestions Survey 2)
	+ Please select a date and time for the meeting that suits you best.
	+ You can choose from a standard 24-hour clock format.

### 3. **How often should we meet?**
* (Meeting Content Suggestions Survey 3)
	+ Select all frequencies that apply.
	+ You can choose 'Yes' for meeting frequencies you prefer, or 'No' for those you don't.

### 4. **Any other feedback on our meetings?**
* (Meeting Content Suggestions Survey 4)
	+ This is an open-ended field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions.
	+ Enter your thoughts on how we can improve our meetings.

### 5. **What is your email address?**
* (Meeting Content Suggestions Survey 5)
	+ This is your email address where we'll follow up with meeting details and updates.
	+ Please ensure it's a valid email address.
