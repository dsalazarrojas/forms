# Curb Adapter Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curb Adapter Feedback Form is a tool for customers to provide feedback on their overall satisfaction and experiences with the Curb Adapter product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are providing feedback on the Curb Adapter product.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk*) and answer any additional questions that are relevant to your experience.
3. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Installation Details** (`installation_details`, `text`, *): This field is used to gather information about your installation experience. Please provide a brief description of your installation, including any relevant details about the installation process.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, `number`, *): This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the Curb Adapter product. Please select a number from 1-5, with 1 being dissatisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, *): This field is an optional text area where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with the Curb Adapter product.
* **Installation Method** (`installation_method`, `select_one`, *): This field is used to select how you installed the Curb Adapter product. Please choose from the options: "installed", "not installed", or "other" if your installation method does not match any of the provided options.
* **Installed By** (`installed_by`, `select_multiple`, *): This field is used to select who installed the Curb Adapter product. Please choose from the options: "installer company", "contractor", or "self installation".
* **Adapter Model** (`adapter_model`, `select_multiple`, *): This field is used to select the type of Curb Adapter model used. Please choose from the options: "adapter model 1", "adapter model 2", or "other" if your adapter model does not match any of the provided options.
* **Adapter Performance** (`adapter_performance`, `number`, *): This field is used to rate the performance of the Curb Adapter product. Please select a number from 1-5, with 1 being poor performance and 5 being excellent performance.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, *): This field is used to rate your overall experience with the Curb Adapter product. Please select a number from 1-5, with 1 being a low rating and 5 being a high rating.
* **Comments on Performance** (`comments_on_performance`, `text`, *): This field is an optional text area where you can provide any comments or suggestions about the performance of the Curb Adapter product.
* **Date Installed** (`date_installed`, `date`, *): This field is used to select the date you installed the Curb Adapter product. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Installed** (`time_installed`, `time`, *): This field is used to select the time you installed the Curb Adapter product. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
