# Receptionist AI Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receptionist AI Feedback Form is designed to gather your feedback about your experience with our Receptionist AI. Your responses will help us understand what we can improve to provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your abilities.
2. Make sure to answer all the fields marked as required.
3. You can skip questions that are not required.
4. Click submit to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Receptionist AI Feedback Form** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, just ignore it, it's here to help with navigation.
* **What is your feedback?** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form_question_1`, text, optional): Please provide us with your honest feedback about your experience with the Receptionist AI.
* **Was the receptionist helpful?** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form_question_2`, select_one, optional): Please select whether the receptionist was helpful or not.
* **What would you like to see improved?** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form_question_3`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions, this is the place to provide them.
* **Receptionist service quality** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form_question_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to describe the quality of service you received.
* **How long did it take to answer the call?** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form_question_5`, time, optional): Please provide the duration of the call.
* **How satisfied are you?** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form_question_6`, select_one, optional): Select how satisfied you are with the service.
* **Would you recommend?** (`receptionist_aid_feedback_form_question_7`, select_one, optional): Please select whether you would recommend the Receptionist AI to others.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete.
* Be honest and provide concrete feedback.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
