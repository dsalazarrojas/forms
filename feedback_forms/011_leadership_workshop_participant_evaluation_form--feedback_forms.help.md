# leadership_workshop_participant_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Leadership Workshop Participant Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from participants on the leadership workshop they attended. This form helps to assess the effectiveness of the workshop, identify areas for improvement, and provide feedback to instructors. Please fill out this form to provide valuable feedback on your workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields marked with an asterisk (*) or marked as "required" in the field label.
2. Enter your information accurately and completely, including any additional comments you have about your workshop experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide any additional information about the participant, such as their name, title, or other relevant details.
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, `text`, required: true): Enter the title of the workshop you attended, as provided by the instructor or on the workshop schedule.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: true): Rate the workshop on a scale of 0-5, with 5 being the highest rating.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you attended the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you attended the workshop.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide any session-specific details, such as session numbers or IDs.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the provided options to describe your overall experience with the workshop. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the workshop instructor.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required: true): Enter the department or team that sponsored or organized the workshop.
* **Division** (`division`, `text`, required: true): Enter the division or departmental area related to the workshop.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: true): Enter the location where the workshop took place.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, required: true): Enter the team that you represent or are affiliated with.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, required: true): Enter your role within the team or organization.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter your work phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.
