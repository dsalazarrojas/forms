# Parking Facilities Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience with our parking facilities. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Please fill in your answers to each question in the text fields provided.
2. If you have any specific preferences or suggestions for our parking facilities, please share them in the text field "What can we improve in terms of parking facilities and services?"
3. For the "How often do you visit our facility?" question, select the option that best describes your frequency of visiting our parking facilities.
4. If you have any additional comments or information you'd like to share, please use the text field "Is there anything else you would like to tell us about your experience?"
5. Finally, provide your contact information so we can follow up with you, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your experience with our parking facilities?**: Please share your genuine thoughts and feelings about your experience with our parking facilities. This will help us understand what we're doing well and what needs improvement.
* **How satisfied are you with our parking facilities?**: Please rate your satisfaction level on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvements for our parking facilities?**: This is your chance to provide specific ideas on how we can improve our parking facilities and services.
* **What can we improve in terms of parking facilities and services?**: Please select all the options that apply to you, as this will help us understand what we can improve.
* **How often do you visit our facility?**: Please select the option that best describes how often you visit our parking facilities.
* **Is there anything else you would like to tell us about your experience?**: This is your chance to share any additional thoughts or comments that didn't fit into the other questions.
* **Contact Information**: Please provide your email address and phone number so we can follow up with you, if desired.
* **Phone Number**: Please provide your phone number so we can follow up with you, if desired.
