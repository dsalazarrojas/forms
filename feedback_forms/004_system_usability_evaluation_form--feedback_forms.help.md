# System Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about their experience with the system's usability. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest opinions about your interaction with the system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page number to navigate through the form.
2. Read the label or question for each field carefully.
3. Answer each field according to your experience with the system.
4. Use the provided options or type in your own answer if it's a text field.
5. Don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or feedback if you have more information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. System Usability Evaluation Form** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is an introduction page that explains the purpose of the form and its importance. Please read it carefully and proceed with the form.
* **2. productusability** (`product_usability`, `text`, required: false): Please describe your experience with the product's usability. What did you find easy or difficult to use? Provide any comments or suggestions.
* **3. navigation** (`navigation`, `select_one`, required: false): How would you rate the navigation of the system? Choose one of the options:
	+ User Friendly
	+ Easy to use
	+ Difficult to use
* **4. searchability** (`searchability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): How would you rate the searchability of the system? Choose one or more of the options:
	+ Search is not available
	+ Search is available
	+ I don't know
* **5. feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with the system.
* **6. satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the system, on a scale of 1-10.
* **7. improvement** (`improvement`, `text`, required: false): Suggest any improvements to the system that would make it more user-friendly.
* **8. recommendation** (`recommendation`, `text`, required: false): Would you recommend the system to others? Provide any reasons why or why not.
* **9. rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your experience with the system, on a scale of 1-5.
* **10. comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the system.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them with us.
* Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and input.
