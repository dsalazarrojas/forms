# Building Block Advisor Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and recommendations from advisors on a project or program. Please provide your honest assessment and insights to help improve the project or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information:
	* **Your name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
	* **Your title or role**: Enter your current title or role.
	* **Organization or affiliation**: Enter your current organization or affiliation.
	* **Email address**: Enter your professional email address.
2. Provide your expertise areas:
	* **Areas of expertise**: Briefly describe your areas of expertise.
3. Assess the project or program:
	* **Overall assessment**: Evaluate the project or program using the provided scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Inadequate).
4. Identify strengths and weaknesses:
	* **Key strengths and highlights**: Describe the project or program's key strengths and highlights.
	* **Areas for improvement**: Identify areas that need attention.
5. Provide specific feedback:
	* **Project or program name**: Confirm the name of the project or program being reviewed.
6. Make recommendations:
	* **Top 3 priority recommendations**: List your top 3 priority recommendations.
7. Identify resource gaps:
	* **Critical resource gaps**: Identify any critical resource gaps that need to be addressed.
8. Identify success factors:
	* **Key success factors**: Describe the key factors that will make the project or program successful.
9. Provide risk mitigation strategies:
	* **Risk mitigation strategies**: Suggest ways to address identified risks.
10. Confirm support:
	* **Willing to provide ongoing support**: Confirm your willingness to provide ongoing support.
	* **Type of support**: Indicate the type of support you can provide (Technical expertise, Mentoring, Connections/networking, Resource recommendations, Funding guidance, Strategic advice, Other).
11. Schedule follow-up:
	* **Willing to meet for follow-up discussion**: Confirm your willingness to meet for a follow-up discussion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advisor Information** (`advisor_info`): A brief note about your expertise and experience.
* **Your name** (`advisor_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Your title or role** (`advisor_title`): Enter your current title or role.
* **Organization or affiliation** (`advisor_organization`): Enter your current organization or affiliation.

* **Areas of expertise** (`advisor_expertise`): Briefly describe your areas of expertise.
* **Project or program name** (`project_name`): Confirm the name of the project or program being reviewed.
* **Overall assessment** (`overall_assessment`): Evaluate the project or program using the provided scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Inadequate).
* **Key strengths and highlights** (`strengths`): Describe the project or program's key strengths and highlights.
* **Areas for improvement** (`weaknesses`): Identify areas that need attention.
* **Specific feedback** (`feedback_section`): A note about specific feedback areas.
* **Top 3 priority recommendations** (`priority_recommendations`): List your top 3 priority recommendations.
* **Critical resource gaps** (`resource_needs`): Identify any critical resource gaps that need to be addressed.
* **Key success factors** (`success_factors`): Describe the key factors that will make the project or program successful.
* **Risk mitigation strategies** (`risk_mitigation`): Suggest ways to address identified risks.
* **Willing to provide ongoing support** (`willing_to_support`): Confirm your willingness to provide ongoing support.
* **Type of support** (`support_type`): Indicate the type of support you can provide (Technical expertise, Mentoring, Connections/networking, Resource recommendations, Funding guidance, Strategic advice, Other).
* **Willing to meet for follow-up discussion** (`follow_up_meeting`): Confirm your willingness to meet for a follow-up discussion.
* **Additional comments or observations** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or observations.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide clear and concise answers, and that you have reviewed the project or program's information before completing this form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the project or program team.
