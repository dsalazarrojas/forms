# cultural event audience satisfaction evaluation form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural event audience satisfaction evaluation form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a cultural event. Your input will help us understand what you think about the event and how we can improve for next time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. Make sure to provide your actual contact information for follow-up if desired.
3. Answer all the required questions, marked with *.
4. For optional questions, select all that apply.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Please provide feedback regarding the Cultural Event** (`introquestion`, `text`, `required: false`): 
Please share your thoughts and opinions about the event. Be as specific as possible.
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with the event** (`eventrating`, `number`, `required: false`): 
Please rate your overall satisfaction with the event, using the numbers 1-5 where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **How would you rate the quality of the event** (`eventquality`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): 
Choose all that apply from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" to describe the quality of the event.
* **How would you rate the impact of the event** (`eventimpact`, `number`, `required: false`): 
Please rate the impact of the event on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Low" and 5 is "Very High".
* **Would you recommend this event?** (`recommend`, `select_one`, `required: false`): 
Choose "Yes" if you would recommend this event to others.
* **Do you have any comments or suggestions** (`comments`, `note`, `required: false`): 
Share any thoughts or ideas you have for future events.
* **How can we contact you** (`contactinfo`, `email`, `required: false`): 
Please enter your contact information, and we will follow up with you.
* **What is your preferred method of contact** (`contactmethod`, `select_multiple`, `required: false`): 
Choose all that apply, or select "Other" if you have a different preference.
* **Intro Page** (`introPage`, `text`, `required: false`): 
This page is for any additional comments or questions not answered elsewhere in the form.
