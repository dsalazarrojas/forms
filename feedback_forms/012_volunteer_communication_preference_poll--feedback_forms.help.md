# Volunteer Communication Preference Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Volunteer Communication Preference Poll form. The form is designed to help us understand how to best contact you as a volunteer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions and make sure you are eligible to participate.
2. Select your preferred methods of communication.
3. Choose how often you would like to receive updates.
4. Indicate your preferences for receiving updates via different channels.
5. Summarize your preferences in the notes section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Communication Method** (`volunteer_communication_method`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks which methods of communication you are interested in, such as email, phone, or text message. Select all the methods that apply to you.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, select_one, optional): This field asks how often you would like to receive updates. Please select the option that best suits your schedule.
* **Volunteer Notification Channels** (`volunteer_notification_channels`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks which channels you would like to receive updates through, such as email, phone, or text message. Select all the channels that apply to you.
* **Volunteer Update Method** (`volunteer_update_method`, select_one, optional): This field asks how you would like to receive updates. Please select the option that best suits your preferences.
* **Volunteer Notification Frequency** (`volunteer_notification_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks how often you would like to receive updates. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Volunteer Notification Method** (`volunteer_notification_method`, select_one, optional): This field asks which method you would like to receive updates through. Please select the option that best suits your preferences.
* **Volunteer Communication Frequency** (`volunteer_communication_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks how often you would like to communicate with us. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Volunteer Communication Preferences** (`volunteer_communication_preferences`, note, optional): This field is for you to summarize your preferences. Please use this space to provide any additional information that will help us understand your preferences better.

Note: Please ensure that you are only selecting options that apply to you. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us directly.
