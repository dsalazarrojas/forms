# Business Sustainability Awards Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from attendees of the Business Sustainability Awards event. By providing feedback, you will help the organizers improve the event in future iterations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details: Your Name, Email Address, and Organization or Company Name.
2. Indicate your role at the event (e.g., Award Finalist, Sponsor, Attendee, etc.).
3. Answer the following questions about your experience at the event:
	* Overall event quality and organization
	* Venue and facilities rating
	* Event schedule and timing
	* Award selection and nomination process clarity
	* Relevance of award categories
	* Networking opportunities
	* Quality of speakers and presentations
	* Relevance of content to sustainability
	* Ease of registration and check-in
	* Quality of pre-event communications
	* Value received for time and participation
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
5. Indicate if you will attend future Business Sustainability Awards events and if you would recommend the event to others.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Organization or Company Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Your Role at the Event** (`attendee_role`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you participated in the event (e.g., Award Finalist, Sponsor, Attendee, etc.).
* **Is this your first time attending?** (`first_time_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Answer yes or no if this is your first time attending the event.
* **Overall event quality and organization** (`event_overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the overall quality and organization of the event (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Venue and facilities rating** (`venue_facilities`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the venue and facilities (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Event schedule and timing** (`event_schedule`, `select_one`, required): Rate how well the event schedule and timing were managed (Well organized, Generally organized, Somewhat disorganized, Very disorganized).
* **Award selection and nomination process clarity** (`award_selection_process`, `select_one`, required): Rate how clear the award selection and nomination process was (Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear, Very unclear).
* **Relevance of award categories** (`award_categories_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the relevance of the award categories to sustainability (Highly relevant, Relevant, Neutral, Somewhat irrelevant, Irrelevant).
* **Networking opportunities** (`networking_opportunities`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of networking opportunities (Excellent opportunities, Good opportunities, Fair opportunities, Limited opportunities, No opportunities).
* **Quality of speakers and presentations** (`speakers_presentations`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of speakers and presentations (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Relevance of content to sustainability** (`content_relevance`, `select_one`, required): Rate the relevance of the content to sustainability (Highly relevant, Relevant, Somewhat relevant, Minimally relevant, Not relevant).
* **Ease of registration and check-in** (`registration_process`, `select_one`, required): Rate how easy it was to register and check-in (Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very difficult).
* **Quality of pre-event communications** (`communication_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of pre-event communications (Email, announcements, information, etc.) (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, Poor).
* **Value received for time and participation** (`event_value`, `select_one`, required): Rate the value you received for attending the event (Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value, No value).
* **How could the event be improved?** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Will you attend future awards events?** (`future_participation`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you will attend future Business Sustainability Awards events (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Unsure, Probably no, Definitely no).
* **Would you recommend this event to others?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you would recommend this event to others (Definitely would recommend, Probably would recommend, Unsure, Probably would not, Definitely would not).
* **Any additional comments or feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.
