# Patient Feedback Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients about their visits to our healthcare facility. The form is designed to gather information about the patient's experience, including their overall rating of the visit, the quality of care provided, and any recommendations they may have. By completing this form, patients can provide valuable feedback that helps us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "Do you have any recommendations or feedback?" field.
* If you have any further comments, please use the "Would you like to leave us a comment?" field.
* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly, as this information is required.
* If you need help, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating** (`patient_feedback_form_1`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the visit, using a scale of 1-4 (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback_form_2`, `text`, required): Describe your experience, including any positive or negative aspects of your visit.
* **Quality of Visit** (`feedback_question_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate the overall quality of your visit, using the options Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Care Provided** (`feedback_question_2`, `select_one`, required): Rate the care provided by your doctor, using the options Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Dignity and Respect** (`feedback_question_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if you were treated with dignity and respect during your visit.
* **Medical Information** (`feedback_question_4`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if you were provided with all necessary medical information about your visit.
* **Recommendations** (`patient_feedback_form_7`, `note`, required): Share any recommendations or feedback you may have about our services.
* **Comment** (`patient_feedback_form_8`, `note`, optional): Leave any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Date** (`patient_feedback_form_11`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time** (`patient_feedback_form_12`, `time`, required): Enter the time of your visit.
