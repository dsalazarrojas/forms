# Completed Transaction Survey 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback on a completed transaction. Your input will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please take a few minutes to complete this survey, which consists of several questions about your experience with the transaction.
- Answer each question based on your actual experience.
- You can provide additional comments or suggestions at the end of the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Transaction ID** (Transaction ID, text, required): Enter the ID from your receipt to ensure that your feedback is associated with the correct transaction.
* **Transaction Date** (Transaction Date, date, required): Select the date of the transaction.
* **-- Service Ratings --** (Service Ratings, note, required): This section is where you'll be rating the quality of service you received during the transaction.
*   * **Efficiency of Transaction** (Efficiency of Transaction, select_one, required): Rate how quickly your transaction was processed.
*   * **Staff Professionalism** (Staff Professionalism, select_one, required): Rate the professionalism and politeness of the staff members you interacted with.
*   * **Clarity of Information** (Clarity of Information, select_one, optional): Rate how clear the information provided during the transaction was.
*   * **Did you encounter any issues?** (Did you encounter any issues?, select_one, required): Select any issues you experienced during the transaction.
*   * **Suggestions for Improvement** (Suggestions for Improvement, text, optional): Provide any suggestions you have for how we can improve our services.
*   * **Likelihood to Recommend** (Likelihood to Recommend, number, optional): On a scale of 1-10, rate how likely you are to recommend our services to others.
*   * **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Provide any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.
*   * **Can we contact you for more details?** (Can we contact you for more details?, select_one, required): Select yes if you're willing to be contacted for more information about your feedback.
