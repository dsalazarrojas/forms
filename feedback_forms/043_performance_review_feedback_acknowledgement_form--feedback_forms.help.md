# performance_review_feedback_acknowledgement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and comments from employees after a performance review. It helps to acknowledge and document the reviewer's and employee's input, ensuring a record of the feedback process.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Enter the "review_date" and "submission_date" in the required date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Enter your "employee_name" and "reviewer_name" in the format as prompted.
4. Choose the "performance_review_rating" (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5) that best reflects your current performance level.
5. Add any "comments" you have for the reviewer or employee.
6. Enter your "email" address if you wish to receive the feedback form submission via email.
7. Enter your "phone_num" if you have a phone number to contact you.
8. Enter the "comments_from_reviewer" if any comments were received from the reviewer.
