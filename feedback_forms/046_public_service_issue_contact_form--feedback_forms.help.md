# public_service_issue_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report public service issues encountered by citizens. It will guide you through the necessary steps to provide all required information to ensure efficient handling of your issue.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Fill in your information as requested in the "Citizen Information" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the issue in the "Issue Description" field.
3. Specify the location where the issue occurred in the "Location" field.
4. Provide the date and time of when the issue occurred in the "Issue Date" and "Issue Time" fields.
5. Add any additional details about the issue in the "Issue Details" field.
6. If applicable, enter any relevant contact information in the "Contact Information" field.
7. Select the category and priority of your issue using the dropdown menus in the "Category," "Select One," "Priority," "Status," and other similar fields.
8. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
9. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form and submit your report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Citizen Information** (citizen_information, text, required): Please fill in your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Issue Description** (issue_description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue you are experiencing.
* **Location** (location, text, required): Specify the exact location where the issue occurred.
* **Issue Date** (issue_date, date, required): Enter the date the issue occurred.
* **Issue Time** (issue_time, time, required): Enter the time the issue occurred.
* **Issue Details** (issue_details, text, required): Provide any additional details about the issue.
* **Contact Information** (contact_information, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by our team, please provide your contact information.
* **Select One** (select_one, select_one, optional): Select one option from the provided list.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options from the provided list.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Note** (note, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the issue.
* **Category** (category, select_one, required): Select a category that best describes your issue.
* **Priority** (priority, select_one, required): Select the priority level of your issue.
* **Status** (status, select_one, required): Select the status of your issue.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required): Click this button to submit your report.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide as much detail as possible about the issue you are experiencing.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact our team at [Your Contact Information].
* Please note that the system will check for duplication based on the issue details and location. If the issue is a duplicate, you will be notified and asked to resubmit with additional details.
