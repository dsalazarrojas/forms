# Vendor Feedback Consolidation Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Feedback Consolidation Report Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with vendors. The feedback collected through this form will be used to help improve the quality of services provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the vendor you are providing feedback about.
2. Choose a rating for the vendor's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
3. If you chose "Poor" as your rating, please provide a brief reason for your feedback.
4. Choose the frequency of your interactions with the vendor (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
5. Enter any additional comments about your experience with the vendor.
6. If you have an email address associated with the vendor, please enter it so we can follow up with them.
7. If you want to provide further feedback, please use the "RATING" fields (RATING 1, RATING 2, etc.) to give more detailed ratings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### VENDOR NAME
* **VENDOR NAME** (text): Enter the name of the vendor you are providing feedback about.

### FEEDBACK RATING
* **FEEDBACK RATING** (select_one): Choose a rating for the vendor's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).

### FEEDBACK RATING REASON
* **FEEDBACK RATING REASON** (text): If you chose "Poor" as your rating, provide a brief reason for your feedback.

### RATING SCALE
* **RATING SCALE** (select_multiple): Choose the frequency of your interactions with the vendor (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).

### FEEDBACK COMMENTS
* **FEEDBACK COMMENTS** (text): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the vendor.

### VENDOR EMAIL
* **VENDOR EMAIL** (email): If you have an email address associated with the vendor, enter it so we can follow up with them.

### RATING FREQUENCY
* **RATING FREQUENCY** (select_multiple): Choose the frequency of your interactions with the vendor (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).

### RATING 1
* **RATING 1** (text): If you want to provide further feedback, please use this field to give more detailed ratings.

**Tips**

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, especially when choosing "Poor" as your rating.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
* The feedback collected through this form will be treated confidentially and used to improve the quality of services provided.
