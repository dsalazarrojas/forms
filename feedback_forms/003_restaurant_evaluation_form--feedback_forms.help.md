# Restaurant Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience at the restaurant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Restaurant Rating: Select the number of stars that best represents your rating of the restaurant (5/5 stars, 4/5 stars, 3/5 stars, 2/5 stars, 1/5 stars)
	* Email: Enter your email address (required for us to contact you)
	* Phone: Enter your phone number (required for us to contact you)
	* Date of Visit: Select the date you visited the restaurant
	* Time of Visit: Select the time you visited the restaurant
	* Customer Name: Enter your name (required)
2. Fill in the optional fields:
	* Food Quality: Rate the quality of the food you had at the restaurant (1-5 stars)
	* Overall Service Quality: Rate the quality of the service you received (1-5 stars)
	* Cleanliness: Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant (1-5 stars)
	* Order Accuracy: Rate the accuracy of your order (1-5 stars)
	* Speed of Service: Rate the speed of service (1-5 stars)
	* Customer Service: Rate the friendliness of the staff (1-5 stars)
	* Value for Money: Rate the value of the money you paid (1-5 stars)
	* Food Presentation: Rate the presentation of the food (1-5 stars)
	* Restaurant Atmosphere: Rate the atmosphere of the restaurant (1-5 stars)
	* Food Price: Rate the price of the food (1-5 stars)
	* Overall Experience: Rate your overall experience (1-5 stars)
	* Staff Friendliness: Rate the friendliness of the staff (1-5 stars)
	* Food Value: Rate the value of the food (1-5 stars)
3. Provide additional feedback in the Additional Feedback field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Rating (5/5 stars, 4/5 stars, 3/5 stars, 2/5 stars, 1/5 stars)**: This is a required field where you rate the overall experience at the restaurant.
* **Email (required)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone (required)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date of Visit**: Select the date you visited the restaurant.
* **Time of Visit**: Select the time you visited the restaurant.
* **Customer Name (required)**: Enter your name.
* **Food Quality (1-5 stars)**: Rate the quality of the food you had.
* **Overall Service Quality (1-5 stars)**: Rate the quality of the service you received.
* **Cleanliness (1-5 stars)**: Rate the cleanliness of the restaurant.
* **Order Accuracy (1-5 stars)**: Rate the accuracy of your order.
* **Speed of Service (1-5 stars)**: Rate the speed of service.
* **Customer Service (1-5 stars)**: Rate the friendliness of the staff.
* **Value for Money (1-5 stars)**: Rate the value of the money you paid.
* **Food Presentation (1-5 stars)**: Rate the presentation of the food.
* **Restaurant Atmosphere (1-5 stars)**: Rate the atmosphere of the restaurant.
* **Food Price (1-5 stars)**: Rate the price of the food.
* **Overall Experience (1-5 stars)**: Rate your overall experience.
* **Staff Friendliness (1-5 stars)**: Rate the friendliness of the staff.
* **Food Value (1-5 stars)**: Rate the value of the food.
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Staff Name**: Enter the name of the staff member who served you.
* **Staff Title**: Enter the title of the staff member who served you.
* **Staff Image**: Enter the image of the staff member who served you.
* **Customer Name (required)**: Enter your name (duplicates the field above, please ignore).
