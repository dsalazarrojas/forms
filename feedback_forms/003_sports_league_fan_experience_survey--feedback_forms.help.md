# sports_league_fan_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports league fan experience survey is designed to gather feedback from fans to improve their overall experience with the sports league. This survey aims to understand fan satisfaction, attendance frequency, preferred activities during events, desired experiences on game days, preferred communication channels, and any additional feedback or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and select the best answer for each one.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, type them in the "Feedback" field.
4. Enter your contact number (if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions).
5. Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted via email).
6. Click "Submit" to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate your overall fan satisfaction with the sports league?** (`fan_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): This question helps us understand your overall satisfaction with the sports league. Please select how you feel about your experience with the league.
* **How often do you attend sports events?** (`engagement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question helps us understand how often you participate in sports events. Select all that apply to indicate your level of engagement.
* **What activities do you enjoy during sports events?** (`fan_engagement_activities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question helps us understand what you enjoy doing during sports events. Select all that apply to indicate your favorite activities.
* **What experiences would you like to see on game day?** (`game_day_experiences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question helps us understand what experiences you'd like to have on game day. Select all that apply to indicate your preferences.
* **What methods do you prefer for league communication?** (`communication_channels`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question helps us understand how you'd like to be contacted by the league. Select all that apply to indicate your preferred communication methods.
* **Do you have any additional feedback or suggestions?** (`feedback`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please type them in this field.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address.
