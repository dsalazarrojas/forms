# National Data Collection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Data Collection Survey is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with the organization. This form is meant to be completed by participants to provide their thoughts and opinions on specific questions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Name" field with your name.
2. Enter your "Organization" name.
3. Provide your "Email" address.
4. Select your answer to "Feedback Question 1" from the options provided.
5. Select your answers to "Feedback Question 2" from the options provided.
6. Type any additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" text field.
7. Skip "Date Participated" and "Time Participated" unless you are required to provide this information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name in this field. This will help us identify you and provide personalized feedback.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or follow-up information.
* **Feedback Question 1**: Select from the options how you feel about your experience.
  * Positive: You are very satisfied with your experience.
  * Negative: You are not satisfied with your experience.
  * Neutral: You are indifferent or neutral about your experience.
* **Feedback Question 2**: Select all that apply from the options to describe your feelings.
  * Very Satisfied: You are extremely happy with your experience.
  * Somewhat Satisfied: You are somewhat happy with your experience.
  * Neutral: You are indifferent or neutral about your experience.
  * Somewhat Dissatisfied: You are somewhat unhappy with your experience.
  * Very Dissatisfied: You are extremely unhappy with your experience.
* **Additional Feedback**: This field is for you to type any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Date Participated**: Skip this field unless you are required to provide this information. This field is not used for feedback purposes.
* **Time Participated**: Skip this field unless you are required to provide this information. This field is not used for feedback purposes.
* **Phone Number**: This field is not part of the feedback process and should be skipped.
