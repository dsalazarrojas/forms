# Continuous Improvement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Continuous Improvement Form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users to help the organization improve its processes and service quality. Your input is valuable in identifying areas of improvement and implementing positive changes that benefit everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief header for your improvement suggestion in the first field.
2. If you wish to share your name, please enter it in the second field. If not, you can leave this field blank.
3. Select the department or process affected by the issue in the third field.
4. In the fourth field, describe the situation as it exists today.
5. In the fifth field, specify the particular problem or bottleneck.
6. In the sixth field, indicate how often the issue occurs.
7. In the seventh field, share your idea for a better way to work.
8. In the eighth field, describe your proposed improvement idea in detail.
9. Select the primary benefit of the change in the ninth field.
10. If applicable, estimate the impact of the proposed improvement in the tenth field.
11. If necessary, list any specific resources or tools needed for the change.
12. In the twelfth field, suggest the priority level for the improvement.
13. Enter the date you submit the form.
14. Finally, let us know if you'd like to be part of the implementation team in the fourteenth field.
15. If you're providing your name, please enter your email address in the last field for follow-up communication.
