# Reporting Channel Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form allows end-users to report issues or concerns to the support team, providing a quick and easy way to report any problems they may experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To submit a report, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose a report type: Select the type of issue you're reporting, such as "Reporting Concern," "Issue," "Feedback," or "Other."
2. Provide a description: Enter a detailed explanation of the issue, including any relevant context or background information.
3. Add issue details: If applicable, provide more information about the issue, such as specific dates or times.
4. Enter contact information: Enter your email address or phone number so we can reach out to you for further questions or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report Type (1)**: Select the type of issue you're reporting.
	+ This field allows you to categorize your report, helping us prioritize and address it efficiently.
* **Description (2)**: Enter a detailed explanation of the issue.
	+ Please provide as much context as possible, including any relevant dates, times, or background information.
* **Issue Details (3)**: Enter any additional information about the issue.
	+ If applicable, provide specific details that will help us understand the issue better.
* **Contact E-mail (4)**: Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for further questions or updates.
	+ This field is optional, but recommended for our communication.
* **Contact Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
	+ This field is also optional, but helps us reach you more easily.
* **Contact Other (6)**: Enter any other contact information you'd like us to use.
	+ This field is optional, but can be helpful if you have a preferred way to be contacted.
