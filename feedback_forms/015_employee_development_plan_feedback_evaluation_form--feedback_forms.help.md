# Employee Development Plan Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to provide feedback on an employee's progress in achieving their growth objectives outlined in their employee development plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field thoroughly and honestly to provide valuable feedback for the employee.
2. Use the fields to provide detailed feedback on the employee's progress, strengths, and areas for improvement.
3. The form should be completed only once for each employee, and not for general performance evaluations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Development Plan Feedback** (`employee_development_plan_feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is where you will write your overall feedback on the employee's progress in achieving their growth objectives. Please provide a detailed account of their strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.
* **Employee Growth Objectives** (`employee_growth_objectives`, `text`, optional): This field is where you will list the employee's growth objectives that you are evaluating. Please ensure that these objectives are specific and measurable.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the employee's progress on a scale of 1-5, where:
	+ 1: The employee is not meeting their objectives.
	+ 2: The employee is partially meeting their objectives.
	+ 3: The employee is meeting their objectives.
	+ 4: The employee is exceeding their objectives.
	+ 5: The employee is significantly exceeding their objectives.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, optional): This field is where you will provide additional comments on the employee's progress, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your feedback is specific, objective, and actionable.
* Use examples or specific instances where the employee has met or exceeded their objectives.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact the form owner for support.
