# Team Performance Reflection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on team performance from team members. It's a self-assessment tool to help teams identify areas for improvement and provide a comprehensive understanding of their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the team name and size for each team you're evaluating.
2. Rate the team's performance using the number rating system provided.
3. Select the leadership position within the team (Team Lead or Team Member).
4. Answer the questions regarding leadership quality, communication, time management, collaboration, innovation, trust, and conflict resolution for each team.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 for each additional team you're evaluating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the team being evaluated.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of members in the team.
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate the team's performance using a number from 1 to 10.
* **Team Leadership** (`team_leadership`, `select_one`, optional): Select the leadership position within the team (Team Lead or Team Member).
* **Leadership Quality** (`leadership_quality`, `select_multiple`, optional): Assess the leadership quality within the team using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Disagree).
* **Communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, optional): Evaluate the communication within the team using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Disagree).
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, `select_multiple`, optional): Assess the time management within the team using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Disagree).
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, `select_multiple`, optional): Evaluate the collaboration within the team using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Disagree).
* **Innovation** (`innovation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Assess the innovation within the team using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Disagree).
* **Trust** (`trust`, `select_multiple`, optional): Evaluate the trust within the team using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Disagree).
* **Conflict Resolution** (`conflict_resolution`, `select_multiple`, optional): Assess the conflict resolution within the team using the provided options (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Somewhat Disagree, Disagree).

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when evaluating each team's performance.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
* Repeat the process for each team you're evaluating to ensure a comprehensive understanding of their strengths and weaknesses.
