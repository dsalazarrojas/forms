# Student Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Satisfaction Survey Form is designed to gather feedback from students about their experience at our institution. This form is optional and anonymous, and its purpose is to help us understand your perceptions of the school's mission and vision, the quality of the coursework, the quality of the education, the facilities, and your overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Answer each question in the space provided.
3. Select your response from the available options.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to finish the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you perceive the school's mission and vision** (`mission_vision`, text, optional):
  This question asks you to describe your understanding of the school's mission and vision. Please provide a brief written answer.
* **How would you rate the quality of the coursework** (`coursework_quality`, number, optional):
  This question asks you to rate the quality of the coursework on a scale. Choose a number from 1 to 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the quality of education** (`education_quality`, text, optional):
  This question asks you to rate the quality of education. Please select one of the available options to provide your answer.
* **How would you rate the facilities** (`facilities`, number, optional):
  This question asks you to rate the quality of the facilities. Choose a number from 1 to 10 to indicate your level of satisfaction.
* **How satisfied are you with the overall education you have received** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
  This question asks you to indicate how satisfied you are with your overall education experience. Please select one of the available options: Extremely Satisfied, Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
