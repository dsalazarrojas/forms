# Festival Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you understand how to complete the Festival Experience Feedback form and what each field represents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Click on the form and begin filling out the fields as requested.
2.  For each field, provide an answer or selection from the options provided.
3.  Once you have finished, click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating** (required: false, 1): This field allows you to rate your overall experience at the festival, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Favorite Artist** (required: false, select multiple): Choose the artist(s) you enjoyed the most during the festival.
* **Favorite Activity** (required: false, select multiple): Select the activity(ies) you enjoyed the most during the festival.
* **Favorite Food** (required: false, select multiple): Choose the food vendor(s) you enjoyed the most during the festival.
* **Overall Experience** (required: false, number): Rate your overall experience at the festival, on a scale of 0 to 10.
* **Improvement Ideas** (required: false, text): Share any ideas you have for how the festival could be improved.
* **Favorite Part** (required: false, select one): Choose the part of the festival you enjoyed the most.
* **Attended Workshop** (required: false, select one): Indicate whether you attended any workshops during the festival.
* **Feedback** (required: false, text): Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as this helps us understand how to better serve you and future attendees.
* You can provide multiple selections for fields with multiple options.
* Do not repeat the same response for multiple fields. This ensures your feedback is unique and valuable to us.
