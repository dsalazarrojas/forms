# Customer Feedback Process Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and evaluation on our customer service process. It will help us identify areas of improvement and provide us with valuable insights to enhance our customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as requested. Each field is designed to gather specific information about your experience with our customer service process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* 
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required): Share your thoughts, comments, and suggestions about our customer service process.
* 
### Service Satisfaction
* **Service Satisfaction** (`service_satisfaction`, select_one, required): How satisfied are you with our service?
	+ Very Satisfied: I am extremely happy with our service.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: I am somewhat happy with our service.
	+ Neutral: I am neutral about our service.
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied: I am somewhat unhappy with our service.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: I am extremely unhappy with our service.
* 
### Rating
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with us from 1 to 5.
* 
### Suggestions
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Do you have any suggestions for us to improve our customer service process?
* 
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or information about your experience.
* 
### Areas for Improvement
* **Areas for Improvement** (`service_improvement`, select_multiple, optional): Select areas where we can improve our customer service process.
	+ Communication
	+ Speed
	+ Quality
	+ Service
* 
### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Social Media
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, optional): Which social media platform do you prefer to communicate with us?
	+ Facebook
	+ Twitter
	+ Instagram
	+ LinkedIn
* 
### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Website
* **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter your website address for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_multiple, optional): Are you open to follow-up communication from us?
	+ Yes
	+ No
* 
### Follow-up Frequency
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_one, optional): How often do you want us to follow-up with you?
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* 
### Follow-up Method
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_multiple, optional): How do you want us to follow-up with you?
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Mail
* 
### Follow-up Content
* **Follow-up Content** (`follow_up_content`, text, optional): Provide any specific content you would like us to include in our follow-up communication.
* 
### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Contact Website
* **Contact Website** (`contact_website`, text, optional): Enter your website address for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Contact Address
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address for follow-up purposes.
* 
### Follow-up Status
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_one, optional): What is the follow-up status with you?
	+ Done
	+ Not Done
* 
### Follow-up Note
* **Follow-up Note** (`follow_up_note`, text, optional): Provide any additional note about your follow-up status.
* 
### Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_multiple, optional): What are the next steps we should take for follow-up purposes?
	+ Follow-up Call
	+ Follow-up Message
	+ Follow-up Email
	+ Escalate to Manager
* 
### Next Follow-up Date
* **Next Follow-up Date** (`next_follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the next follow-up.
