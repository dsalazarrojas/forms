# Neighborhood Watch Program Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neighborhood Watch Program Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions on the Neighborhood Watch Program. Your feedback will help us understand how to improve the program to better serve our community.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions that are marked as required (indicated by "Yes" next to the label).
2. For the select one question (How often do you visit your neighborhood?), please select one of the provided options (daily, weekly, monthly, or never).
3. Use the text fields to provide any feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about the Neighborhood Watch Program?** (`neighborhood_watch_program_feedback_evaluation_form_1`, text, required): This is the main question where you can provide your overall thoughts, opinions, or experiences with the Neighborhood Watch Program.
* **How often do you visit your neighborhood?** (`neighborhood_watch_program_feedback_evaluation_form_2`, select_one, required): Please select how often you visit your neighborhood to help us understand your engagement with our community.
* **Do you have any feedback?** (`neighborhood_watch_program_feedback_evaluation_form_3`, text, optional): If you have any comments, complaints, or suggestions about the Neighborhood Watch Program, please share them here.
* **How can we improve the Neighborhood Watch Program?** (`neighborhood_watch_program_feedback_evaluation_form_4`, text, required): We value your suggestions on how to improve our services. Please share your ideas to help us become better.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (`neighborhood_watch_program_feedback_evaluation_form_5`, text, optional): If you have any additional ideas or suggestions that didn't fit in the previous question, please share them here.
* **Can we contact you?** (`neighborhood_watch_program_feedback_evaluation_form_6`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, please provide your contact information here.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and concisely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input.
