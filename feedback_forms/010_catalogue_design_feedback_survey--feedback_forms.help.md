# Catalogue Design Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the design of a catalogue, allowing us to improve and refine our design to better represent the brand's identity and messaging.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions honestly, providing your genuine opinions and thoughts on the design.
- Make sure you have reviewed the catalogue carefully before providing your feedback.
- You may choose any of the provided options or add additional comments if you wish.
- If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please provide them in the designated text fields.

	+ Stakeholder: Any individual or group with a vested interest in the design.
	+ Test User: Someone testing the catalogue without being directly involved in its creation.
	+ Other: Please specify your role in the comments field.
