# Fashion Show Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of fashion shows. The collected feedback will help improve future fashion shows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the fashion show you attended.
2. Select the time slot of the fashion show you attended.
3. Enter the name of the fashion show.
4. Answer the feedback questions:
	* How would you rate the overall quality of the event?
	* How would you rate the organization of the event?
	* Was the event value for money?
	* How likely are you to attend another fashion event like this in the future?
5. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
6. Enter your name.
7. Enter your email address.

## Tips

* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* If you have any specific comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact email or phone number].
* All feedback will be kept confidential and used to improve future fashion shows.
