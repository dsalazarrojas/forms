# user_documentation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect user feedback, documentation requests, support issues, and other relevant information. It's intended for user support and feedback management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and provide the requested information.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required") to complete the form.
3. For fields with options, select the most relevant choice that applies to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`feedback_input`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your feedback or concerns.
* **Documentation Request** (`documentation_input`, text, required/optional): If you're requesting documentation, describe what you're looking for in detail.
* **Support Issues** (`support_issue_input`, select_one, required/optional): Select the most relevant issue that aligns with your concern.

* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required/optional): If applicable, provide the date your issue was submitted or the date you're requesting documentation.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, required/optional): If applicable, provide the time you submitted your issue or the time you're requesting documentation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes you'd like to include.
* **Category** (`category_input`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant categories that apply to your issue or concern.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about a field or option, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to help us better understand your concerns and improve our services. Your feedback is valuable to us!
