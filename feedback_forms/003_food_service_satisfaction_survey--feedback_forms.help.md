# Food Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the food service. The survey aims to measure customer satisfaction with the food service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly to provide accurate feedback.
2. Select all the options that apply to your experience.
3. For the free-text fields (Comments, Contact Details, Contact Number, Email Address, Date of Visit), you can provide as much detail as you need.
4. Make sure to submit the form to ensure your feedback is received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you like?** (`what_do_you_like`, select_multiple, Not required):
    Select all the options that describe what you liked about your experience with the food service.
* **Rate Your Satisfaction** (`rate_your_satisfaction`, number, Not required):
    Provide a rating from 1 to 5, where 5 is the highest satisfaction level.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, Not required):
    Describe your experience with the food service, providing any feedback or comments you may have.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, Not required):
    Provide any additional contact details or information that may be helpful for us to contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, Not required):
    Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, Not required):
    Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, Not required):
    Provide the date you visited the food service.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, Not required):
    Provide the time you visited the food service.
* **How Do You Feel?** (`how_do_you_feel`, select_one, Not required):
    Select how you feel about your experience with the food service.
* **Would You Recommend?** (`would_you_recommend`, select_multiple, Not required):
    Select all the options that describe how likely you are to recommend the food service.
