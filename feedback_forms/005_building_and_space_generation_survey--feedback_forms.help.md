# Building And Space Generation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the spaces within a facility. It is meant to be used by the facility management team to understand the needs and comfort level of the users who utilize the space.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the designated space provided.
2. Choose the best option that describes your experience with each question.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering the questions that ask for open-ended comments.
4. If you have any additional suggestions or concerns, please provide them in the text boxes at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Date (1)** (`date`, required): This is the date you are completing this survey.
* **Facility Name (2)** (`text`, required): This is the name of the building or space you are evaluating.
* **Primary Department Using Space (3)** (`text`, required): This is the team or group that primarily uses this space.
* **Type of Space (4)** (`select_one`, required): Choose the type of space you are evaluating (e.g. Office, Meeting Room, etc.).
* **Adequate Occupancy Capacity (5)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if the space fits your needs (Fully Adequate, Mostly Adequate, Somewhat Adequate, Not Adequate).
* **Space Layout and Functionality (6)** (`select_one`, required): Rate how well designed is the space (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Lighting Quality (7)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if the lighting is adequate (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Temperature and Climate Control (8)** (`select_one`, required): Rate how comfortable the space is (Always Comfortable, Usually Comfortable, Sometimes Uncomfortable, Always Uncomfortable).
* **Noise Level (9)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate if the noise level is acceptable (Quiet and Controlled, Mostly Acceptable, Too Noisy, Extremely Noisy).
* **Accessibility and Mobility (10)** (`select_one`, required): Rate if the space is accessible to all users (Fully Accessible, Mostly Accessible, Limited Accessibility, Not Accessible).
* **Technology Readiness (11)** (`select_one`, required): Rate the technology infrastructure (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Safety and Security Concerns (12)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate any safety concerns (None, Minor, Moderate, Significant).
* **Suggested Improvements (13)** (`text`, optional): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Spaces Needed (14)** (`text`, optional): Suggest any additional spaces you would like to see.
* **Respondent Name (15)** (`text`, optional): Provide your name for follow-up questions.
* **Email Address (16)** (`email`, optional): Provide your email address for follow-up questions.

Note: Fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be filled out to submit the form.
