# Purpose:
This form is designed to gather information about customer incidents, providing a platform for customers to report and describe their experiences. It aims to collect necessary details to facilitate effective communication and resolution of issues.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Fill in the **Contact Person** field with the name of the person involved or responsible for the incident.
2.  Enter the **Contact Email** of the person in charge of the incident.
3.  Provide the date of the incident in the **Incident Date** field.
4.  Select the time of the incident using the **Incident Time** field.
5.  In the **Incident Description** field, describe the incident in detail.
6.  Choose the type of customer service request made in the **Customer Service Request** field.
7.  Select the current **Issue Status** of the incident.
8.  Add any additional comments or notes in the **Incident Comments** field.
9.  Choose how satisfied the customer is with the service they received in the **Customer Input** field.
10.  Enter any additional comments or feedback in the **Customer Comments** field.
11.  If the incident is related to a specific tool, choose the relevant **Assigned Tool**.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Person**: Provide the name of the person involved or responsible for the incident.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person in charge of the incident.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Select the time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description**: Write a detailed description of the incident.
* **Customer Service Request**: Choose the type of service request made, which can be **Customer Care** or **Complaint**.
* **Issue Status**: Select the current status of the issue, which can be **Open** or **Resolved**.
* **Incident Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Customer Input**: Choose how satisfied the customer is with the service they received, which can be **Very Satisfied**, **Somewhat Satisfied**, **Somewhat Dissatisfied**, or **Very Dissatisfied**.
* **Customer Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the incident.
* **Assigned Tool**: If the incident is related to a specific tool, choose the relevant tool from the options, or select **Other** if it's not specified.
## Tips
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the **Incident Description** field.
- Choose the correct **Issue Status** to reflect the current state of the incident.
- Be specific with the **Customer Service Request** to ensure accurate categorization.
- Choose the correct **Customer Input** to reflect your satisfaction level with the service.
