# Customer Feedback Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our service. Please take a few minutes to complete this form to help us better understand your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. Provide honest and accurate feedback about your experience with our service.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Feedback" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Feedback Page** (`customer_feedback_page`, text): This is the title of the form. Please enter your feedback in this field.
- **Rate our service** (`feedback_question_1`, number, 1-5): Rate our service from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
- **Why did you choose our service** (`feedback_question_2`, text): Please enter a brief explanation of why you chose our service.
- **Did we meet your expectations** (`feedback_question_3`, select_one, True/False/Undecided): Answer whether we met your expectations or not.
- **How would you rate our customer service** (`customer_service_rating`, number, 1-5): Rate our customer service from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
- **Is there anything we can improve** (`additional_feedback`, text): If you have any suggestions or complaints, please include them here.
- **How would you rate our product or service** (`feedback_question_4`, number, 1-5): Rate our product or service from 1 to 5, with 1 being poor and 5 being excellent.
- **Would you recommend our service** (`contact_us`, select_multiple, True/False): Answer whether you would recommend our service to others.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text): Please enter your name for communication purposes.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Please enter your email address for us to contact you.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you.
- **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, note): If you have any comments or suggestions, please include them here.
