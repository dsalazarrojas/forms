# Master Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Master Program Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from program participants on their experience within the program. This feedback is crucial in helping us understand the program's effectiveness, accessibility, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please fill out the form thoroughly and honestly, providing detailed comments where applicable.
* Ensure that you provide feedback on all fields.
* If you are not able to complete the form in one sitting, you can save your progress and return to it later.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Comments** (`program_comments`, text, optional): 
Please provide any comments or feedback you have about your experience within the program. This can include suggestions for improvement, positive or negative experiences, or any other observations you have.
* **Accessing the Information** (`accessibility_info`, number, optional): 
Rate your overall level of accessibility to the information and resources provided within the program. Please respond on a scale from 1 (not accessible) to 5 (very accessible).
* **Clarity of Policies** (`policy_clarity`, number, optional): 
Rate the clarity of the program's policies and guidelines. Please respond on a scale from 1 (not clear) to 5 (very clear).
* **Overall Faculty Issues** (`faculty_issues`, number, optional): 
Rate the overall quality of faculty support and guidance within the program. Please respond on a scale from 1 (not satisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Academic Support** (`academic_support`, number, optional): 
Rate the level of academic support you received within the program. Please respond on a scale from 1 (not satisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): 
Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience within the program. This can include suggestions for improvement, positive or negative experiences, or any other observations you have.
