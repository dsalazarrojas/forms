# online-store-checkout-feedback-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after their online store checkout experience. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us improve our store operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with your feedback.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field if you would like to be contacted in response to your feedback.
3. Enter your feedback in a clear and concise manner in the "Comments" field.
4. Select your rating from the "Rating" field, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
5. Check "Yes" or "No" in the "Submitted By" field to indicate if you made the order online or in-store.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of your recent order.
* **Item Purchased** (`item_purchased`, `text`, required: false): Describe the item you purchased or ordered.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date and time of your recent checkout.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with your recent checkout experience. Choose from 1 to 5 stars.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter your feedback on your recent checkout experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address if you'd like to be contacted in response to your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted in response to your feedback.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required: false): Check "Yes" if you made the order online, "No" if you made the order in-store.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your feedback to help us improve our store operations.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact info].
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our store for future customers.
