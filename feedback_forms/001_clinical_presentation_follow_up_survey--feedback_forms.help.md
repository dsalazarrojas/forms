# Clinical Presentation Follow Up Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your clinical presentation and follow-up needs. It helps us ensure that your health status is up-to-date and that any issues are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form in the following steps:

*   Enter your name and medical record number in the corresponding fields.
*   Provide the date of your last clinical presentation.
*   Answer the questions regarding your current symptom status, treatment adherence, and medication compliance.
*   Rate your overall quality of life and sleep quality.
*   Indicate whether you need further treatment or evaluation.
*   Enter your phone number and email address for follow-up contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Enter your full name as recorded in your medical records.
*   **Patient ID Number** (Patient ID Number, text, required): Enter your medical record number or patient identifier.
*   **Date of Last Clinical Presentation** (Date of Last Clinical Presentation, date, required): Enter the date of your previous visit or clinical presentation.
*   **How is your primary symptom or condition?** (How is your primary symptom or condition?, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate your current symptom status.
*   **Current Symptom Severity Rating** (Current Symptom Severity Rating, number, required): Rate your current symptom severity from 0 (none) to 10 (worst).
*   **Have any new symptoms developed?** (Have any new symptoms developed?, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate whether new symptoms have developed.
*   **Describe any new symptoms** (Describe any new symptoms, text, required): Provide a description of any new symptoms you are experiencing.
*   **How well have you followed the treatment plan?** (How well have you followed the treatment plan?, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate your adherence to treatment.
*   **Have you taken all prescribed medications as directed?** (Have you taken all prescribed medications as directed?, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate your medication compliance.
*   **Have you experienced any side effects from medications?** (Have you experienced any side effects from medications?, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate whether you have experienced any side effects.
*   **Describe side effects** (Describe side effects, text, optional): Provide a description of any side effects you have experienced.
*   **Impact on daily activities and work** (Impact on daily activities and work, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate the impact on your daily activities and work.
*   **How has your sleep been?** (How has your sleep been?, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to rate your sleep quality.
*   **Overall quality of life rating** (Overall quality of life rating, number, required): Rate your overall quality of life from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent).
*   **Do you need further treatment or evaluation?** (Do you need further treatment or evaluation?, select_one, required): Select one of the options to indicate whether you need further treatment or evaluation.
*   **Additional concerns or questions for clinician** (Additional concerns or questions for clinician, text, optional): Provide any additional concerns or questions you have for your clinician.
*   **Phone Number for Follow-up** (Phone Number for Follow-up, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
*   **Email Address for Follow-up** (Email Address for Follow-up, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.

Note: Please fill out this form honestly and to the best of your ability. If you need further assistance or clarification, please reach out to your healthcare provider.
