# Profile Coordinator Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from profile coordinators, allowing us to gauge their experience with our services and identify areas for improvement. Please fill out this form to provide your honest feedback, and help us make our services better for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as you feel necessary.
2. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the "Additional Comments" field.
3. If you wish to contact us for further discussion or to report an issue, you can use the "Contact Us" field.
4. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) next to each field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Profile Coordinator Feedback** (`profile_coordinator_feedback_form`, text, not required):
	+ Please describe your experience as a profile coordinator, including any positive or negative aspects of our services.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select one of the following options to rate your overall experience:
		+ Very Satisfied
		+ Somewhat Satisfied
		+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
		+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Profile Coordinator Helpful?** (`profile_coordinator_helpful`, number, not required):
	+ Rate how helpful our profile coordinators have been, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required):
	+ Share any thoughts or observations about our services that didn't fit into the previous questions.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, not required):
	+ If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them here.
* **Contact Us** (`contact_us`, text, not required):
	+ If you need to get in touch with us for any reason, use this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required):
	+ If you'd like to provide your email address for us to follow up with you, please enter it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required):
	+ Any further comments or suggestions you'd like to share?
