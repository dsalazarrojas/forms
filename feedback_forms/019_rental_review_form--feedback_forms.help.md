# rental_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rental review form is used to gather feedback from renters about their experience with a property. This form is designed to help us improve our services by providing insight into what worked well and what didn't for each rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: Please provide your name to identify yourself as the reviewer.
2. **Enter your email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
3. **Enter your phone number**: This is optional, but helps us get in touch with you faster.
4. **Rate your experience**: Please rate your overall experience with the property on a scale of 1 to 10.
5. **Leave feedback**: Share your thoughts and suggestions about your experience with the property. This is a text field where you can type as much or as little as you like.
6. **Submit your review**: Once you're done, click the 'Submit Review' button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name**: This is the field where you enter your name as the reviewer. Please ensure to spell it correctly.
* **Email**: Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: This is an optional field where you can enter your phone number. It's not required but can help us reach out to you quickly.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your experience with the property on a scale of 1 to 10. This will help us understand how well we're meeting your expectations.
* **Review Comments**: Share as much or as little as you like about your experience with the property. This helps us understand what worked well and what didn't.
* **Submit Review**: This is the final step to send your review to us. Please click this button to submit your feedback.
