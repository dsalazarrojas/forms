# Performance Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Review form is a tool used to facilitate regular discussions between managers and employees on performance goals, progress, and feedback. It helps to identify areas of improvement and recognition, ensuring continuous growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
