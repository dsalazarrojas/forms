# Judging Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have attended a judging workshop. It will help us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. On the first page, take a moment to reflect on your experience at the judging workshop. We want to hear your honest thoughts!
3. On the second page, select one of the options that best describes your experience: "Yes", "No", "Very Poor", "Average", "Good", "Excellent", or "Outstanding".
4. If you feel that none of the above options accurately reflect your experience, please provide additional comments in the "Any Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, note, optional): Take a moment to reflect on your experience at the judging workshop and provide any general feedback you have.

	+ "Outstanding"
