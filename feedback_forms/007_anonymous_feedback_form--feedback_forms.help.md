# Anonymous Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect anonymous feedback from users about a service, program, or staff interaction. This feedback will help us improve our services and ensure that we meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the area your feedback relates to from the provided options.
2. Provide an overall rating from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
3. Describe your experience or suggest improvements in the text field.
4. If you have provided contact details, you can choose how you'd like to be followed up.
5. Provide any additional comments or attachments you'd like to describe.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Topic** (`feedback_topic`, select_one, required): Select the area your feedback relates to from the provided options.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Provide an overall rating from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Feedback Details** (`feedback_text`, text, required): Describe your experience or suggest improvements in this text field.
* **Would You Recommend Us** (`would_recommend`, select_one, optional): Help us measure your satisfaction by indicating if you would recommend us.
* **Contact Email if Follow Up Requested** (`contact_email_optional`, email, optional): Leave your contact email address if you'd like us to follow up with you.
* **Preferred Follow Up Method** (`follow_up_preference`, select_one, optional): If you provided contact details, select your preferred follow-up method.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or attachments you'd like to describe.
