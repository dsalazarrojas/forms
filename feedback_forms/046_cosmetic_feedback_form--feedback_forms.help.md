# Cosmetic Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on your experience with a cosmetic product or service. Your input will help our team improve our products and services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of feedback you want to provide by selecting "Product" or "Service" from the "Feedback Type" dropdown menu.
2. If you're providing feedback on a product, select the product from the "Product" dropdown menu and fill in the "Product Feedback" and "Product Description" fields.
3. If you're providing feedback on a service, select the service from the "Service" dropdown menu and fill in the "Service Feedback" and "Service Description" fields.
4. Add any additional comments about your experience.
5. If relevant, fill in the "Product Image" field to provide a URL or a description of the product image.
6. If relevant, fill in the "Product Rating" and "Product Price" fields to provide a rating and price range for the product.
7. If you've written a review for the product, fill in the "Product Review" field.
8. Finally, click the submit button to send your feedback.
