# medical_receptionist_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical receptionist feedback form is designed to collect feedback from patients on their experience with the front desk staff at our medical receptionist's office. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our front desk staff provides excellent service to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the front desk staff member you interacted with from the "frontDeskStaff" dropdown menu.
2. Rate their communication style by selecting one of the following options:
	* Poor
	* Fair
	* Good
	* Excellent
3. Rate their overall professionalism by selecting one of the following options:
	* Poor
	* Fair
	* Good
	* Excellent
4. Check if you had a positive or negative reception experience:
	* Yes
	* No
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the text box.
6. Rate your overall experience on a numerical scale (1-5).
7. (Optional) Provide feedback on a scale of 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Front Desk Staff (frontDeskStaff, select_one, required)**: Choose the front desk staff member you interacted with to ensure that your feedback is specific and directed towards the correct person.
* **Communication (communication, select_one, required)**: Rate the communication style of your front desk staff. How would you rate their communication style? (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent)
* **Professionalism (professionalism, select_one, required)**: Rate the overall professionalism of your front desk staff. How would you rate their professionalism? (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent)
* **Reception Experience (reception_experience, select_multiple, required)**: Check if you had a positive or negative reception experience. (Yes, No)
* **Comments (comments, text, required)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with the front desk staff.
* **Rating (rating, number, required)**: Rate your overall experience on a numerical scale from 1 to 5.
* **Rating Scale (ratingScale, number, required)**: Provide feedback on a scale of 1 to 5, if you have any comments or concerns.
