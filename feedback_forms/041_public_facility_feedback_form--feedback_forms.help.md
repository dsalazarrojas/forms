# Public Facility Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect public feedback on facilities, allowing users to provide ratings and comments about their experience. Fill out this form to give us valuable insights on how we're doing and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Public Facility**: Enter the name of the public facility you are providing feedback on.
2. **Facility Rating**: Choose a rating for the facility from 1 to 5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
3. **Facility Comment**: Type in your comment about the facility. This is an optional field, but it will help us understand the reason behind your rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Public Facility**: 
  - **Public Facility (Public Facility)** (text, required): Enter the name of the facility you are providing feedback on. This field is required to ensure we know which facility you are commenting on.
* **Facility Rating**: 
  - **Facility Rating (Facility Rating)** (number, required): Rate the facility's quality on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent). This helps us measure the overall performance of the facility.
* **Facility Comment**: 
  - **Facility Comment (Facility Comment)** (text, optional): Provide any additional comments you'd like to share about your experience with the facility. This optional field gives us more context to your rating and helps us understand how we can improve.
 
## Tips

- Please be specific and factual in your feedback, as this will help us make the best improvements.
- Keep in mind that your input is greatly appreciated and helps us serve you and others better.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
