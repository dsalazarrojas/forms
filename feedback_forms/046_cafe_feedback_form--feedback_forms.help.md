# Cafe Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cafe Feedback Form is used to gather customer feedback about their experience at our cafe. This helps us to identify areas of improvement, make necessary adjustments, and provide a better experience for our customers. To ensure that we hear your honest feedback, please fill out this form with as much detail as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your feedback. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*). If a field does not have an asterisk, you can leave it blank if you don't have information about it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Visit** (*, date): Enter the date you visited our cafe. This helps us to associate your feedback with your visit.
* **Approximate Time of Visit** (optional, time): If you're unsure or didn't keep track of the exact time, you can estimate it here. This helps us to better understand your experience during a specific time period.
* **Type of Visit** (*, select_one):
	+ Dine-in: You visited our cafe to dine-in.
	+ Takeaway: You ordered takeout from our cafe.
	+ Online Delivery: You received online delivery from our cafe.
* **Quality of Food** (*, number): Rate the quality of our food from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Quality of Beverages** (*, number): Rate the quality of our beverages from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Speed of Service** (*, select_one):
	+ Fast: Our service was very fast.
	+ Reasonable: Our service was at a normal pace.
	+ Slow: Our service was slower than expected.
	+ Very Slow: Our service was much slower than expected.
* **Staff Friendliness and Professionalism** (*, number): Rate the friendliness and professionalism of our staff from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Cleanliness of Dining Area and Restrooms** (*, select_one):
	+ Excellent: Our cafe was very clean.
	+ Good: Our cafe was clean enough.
	+ Average: Our cafe was somewhat clean.
	+ Poor: Our cafe was not clean.
* **Cafe Ambiance and Music** (optional, select_one):
	+ Very Pleasant: The ambiance was very pleasant.
	+ Good: The ambiance was okay.
	+ Neutral: The ambiance was neutral.
	+ Too Loud/Distracting: The ambiance was too loud or distracting.
* **WiFi Performance** (optional, select_one):
	+ Fast: Our WiFi performed well.
	+ Reliable: Our WiFi was stable.
	+ Slow/Unreliable: Our WiFi was slow or unreliable.
	+ Did not use: You didn't use our WiFi.
* **Value for Money** (*, select_one):
	+ Excellent: You felt you got a great value for your money.
	+ Good: You felt you got a reasonable value for your money.
	+ Fair: You felt you got a mediocre value for your money.
	+ Poor: You felt you didn't get a good value for your money.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (*, number): Rate how likely you are to recommend our cafe to a friend from 1 (Not Likely) to 5 (Extremely Likely).
* **General Comments/Suggestions** (optional, text): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your experience.
* **Join Loyalty Program** (optional, select_one):
	+ True: You'd like to join our loyalty program.
	+ False: You don't want to join our loyalty program.
	+ Already a member: You're already a member.
* **Email Address for Follow-up** (optional, email): If you'd like to be contacted for any follow-up, please enter your email address.

## Tips
Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible. If you have any suggestions for improvement, we'd love to hear them. Your feedback is crucial to helping us provide the best experience for our customers.
