# Air Care Service Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the Air Care Service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your customer name (if you're willing to provide it).
2. Enter the date of the service you received.
3. Rate the professionalism and conduct of the technician who served you.
4. Let us know if the service was on time as scheduled.
5. Indicate whether the issue you reported was resolved.
6. Rate your satisfaction with the pricing of the service.
7. Choose how likely you are to recommend our service to others.
8. If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to type them in the text box.
9. Let us know if you would like to receive a follow-up from our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name**: Enter your name, if you're willing to provide it. This helps us identify you and personalize our follow-up.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Service date**: Enter the date of the appointment you received the service.
	+ Type: date
	+ Required: no
* **Technician professionalism**: Rate the professionalism and conduct of the technician who served you.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
	+ Required: yes
* **Service timeliness**: Let us know if the service was on time as scheduled.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: On time, Late, Very late
	+ Required: yes
* **Issue resolved**: Indicate whether the issue you reported was resolved.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: True, Partially, False
	+ Required: yes
* **Value for money**: Rate your satisfaction with the pricing of the service.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
	+ Required: no
* **Would you recommend us**: Choose how likely you are to recommend our service to others.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: True, False, Maybe
	+ Required: no
* **Suggestions for improvement**: If you have any ideas on how we can improve our service, type them in this box.
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: no
* **Follow-up requested**: Let us know if you would like a follow-up from our team.
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Options: True, False
	+ Required: no
