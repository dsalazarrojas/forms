# Educational Support Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Educational Support Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from educational support staff members about their experiences and challenges in their roles. It helps us understand their needs and areas for improvement in providing educational support, ultimately enhancing the quality of services offered to students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and answer all questions relevant to your role and experiences.
* Select the options that best describe your role and experiences.
* Provide detailed responses to open-ended questions.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your role? (page 1)**:
	+ This question is asking about your current position in the educational institution.
	+ Please select the option that best describes your role: Teacher, Instructor, Tutor, or Other.
* **What is your department? (page 1)**:
	+ This question is asking about the specific department you work in (e.g., Math, Science, English, or Other).
	+ Select all options that apply to your department.
* **What is your role in the department? (page 1)**:
	+ This question is asking about your specific role within the department (e.g., Faculty, Staff, or Student).
	+ Select the option that best describes your role.
* **How do you usually interact with students? (page 1)**:
	+ This question is asking about your primary method of communication with students (e.g., In person, Online).
	+ Select all options that apply to your interactions with students.
* **Educational Support Questionnaire (page 2)**:
	+ This section is asking you to provide a brief title and description of this form.
	+ You can provide any additional context or information you think is relevant.
* **What do you think is the biggest challenge in your educational support role? (page 2)**:
	+ This question is open-ended and asking about the most significant challenge you face in your role.
	+ Provide a detailed response to this question.
* **Lack of resources (page 2)**:
	+ This question is asking about specific challenges related to limited funding, equipment, or training.
	+ Select all options that apply to your experiences.
* **Limited expertise (page 2)**:
	+ This question is asking about specific challenges related to limited training or resources.
	+ Select all options that apply to your experiences.
* **Lack of support (page 2)**:
	+ This question is asking about specific challenges related to limited support or time.
	+ Select all options that apply to your experiences.
* **What do you think would help you in your educational support role? (page 3)**:
	+ This question is open-ended and asking about potential solutions or resources that would aid you in your role.
	+ Provide a detailed response to this question.
* **Training (page 3)**:
	+ This question is asking about specific types of training or support that would be helpful.
	+ Select all options that apply to your experiences.
* **What other comments do you have about your educational support role? (page 4)**:
	+ This is an open-ended question for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your role.
	+ Provide a detailed response to this question.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions relevant to your role and experiences.
* Be as specific as possible when responding to open-ended questions.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, use the "What other comments do you have about your educational support role?" section.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification or guidance.
