# workplace_satisfaction_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experience in the workplace. It will help us understand your satisfaction levels in various aspects of your job and provide valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages of questions and answer each one accordingly. Fill out as much or as little information as you feel comfortable with, but keep in mind that required fields are marked as such.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **workplace_satisfaction_section**: This section is an introduction to the form, explaining the purpose and importance of filling out this survey.
* **job_satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your level of satisfaction with your job on a scale of 1-5. Please choose a number that best represents your feelings.
* **work_life_balance** (`work_life_balance`, `text`, required: `false`): Describe your current work-life balance. How do you manage your work and personal life?
* **communication** (`communication`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): How would you rate the quality of communication in your workplace? Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **management** (`management`, `select_one`, required: `false`): How would you rate your immediate supervisor or manager's leadership style? Choose one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **work_life_balance_2** (`work_life_balance_2`, `time`, required: `false`): Estimate the number of hours you spend on work-related tasks outside of work hours (e.g., evenings, weekends).
* **feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience in the workplace.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): This field is optional and will not be used for any purpose other than as a contact method to follow up with you if we need more information. If you do not provide an email address, we will not contact you, but your feedback will still be processed and reviewed.

# Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- You can skip any sections that do not apply to you.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
