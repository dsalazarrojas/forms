# System Upgrade Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the recent system upgrade from users. The feedback is crucial to understanding the effectiveness and usability of the system upgrade, and will be used to inform future development and improvement decisions. Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Select the most relevant option for each question.
3. Enter any additional feedback or suggestions in the "Feedback" field.
4. If you have any other suggestions or comments, please enter them in the "Suggestions" field.
5. Enter your email address and any other relevant contact information in the "Email address", "Phone number", "User name", "User organization", "User department", "User job title", and "User location" fields.
6. Finally, review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Experience** (`User experience`, select_one, required): This question asks how you experienced the system upgrade. Please select "Very Poor", "Average", or "Excellent" to provide your feedback.
* **Upgrades Frequency** (`Upgrades frequency`, date, optional): If you have any concerns or suggestions regarding the frequency of upgrades, please provide the date you think the upgrades should be done.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, note, optional): This is a text field where you can enter any additional feedback or comments about the system upgrade. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Suggestions** (`Suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please select "Text" or "Other" and enter your suggestion.
* **Email Address** (`Email address`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number where you can be reached, please enter it here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned tool`, select_one, optional): Please select the tool that was assigned to you for the system upgrade.
* **Jotform Account** (`Jotform account`, email, optional): If you have a Jotform account, please enter your email address here.
* **User Name** (`User name`, text, optional): Please enter your name, as it appears on your badge or ID card.
* **User Organization** (`User organization`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your organization.
* **User Department** (`User department`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your department.
* **User Job Title** (`User job title`, text, optional): Please enter your job title.
* **User Location** (`User location`, text, optional): Please enter your physical location.
* **Feedback Form** (`Feedback form`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of feedback form you are using (Text, Dropdown, Matrix).
* **Submission Date** (`Submission date`, date, optional): If you want to track the date of your submission, please enter the date here.
* **Submission Time** (`Submission time`, time, optional): If you want to track the time of your submission, please enter the time here.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted by`, select_one, optional): Please select who submitted the form (Automated or Human).
