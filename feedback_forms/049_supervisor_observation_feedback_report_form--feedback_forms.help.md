# Supervisor Observation Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supervisor Observation Feedback Report Form is used by supervisors to provide feedback to employees, peers, or students on their performance, behavior, or progress. This form helps gather constructive feedback to support professional development, address performance issues, or recognize outstanding performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the purpose and the fields below.
2. Complete each field with relevant information:
	* Page 1:
		+ **Supervisor Observation Feedback Report** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_report_form_page_1`): Enter a brief summary of your observation or feedback.
	* Page 2:
		+ **Supervisor Observation Feedback Form Comments** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_form_comments`): Add any additional comments or details about your observation or feedback.
	* Page 3:
		+ **Supervisor Observation Feedback Form Rating** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_form_rating`): Select one of the following ratings: Good, Fair.
	* Page 4:
		+ **Supervisor Observation Feedback Form Score** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_form_score`): Enter a numerical score out of 5 to represent your observation or feedback (e.g., 3.5).
	* Page 5:
		+ **Supervisor Observation Feedback Report Comments** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_report_form_comments`): Add any additional comments or details about your observation or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supervisor Observation Feedback Report** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_report_form_page_1`)
	+ This field is for a brief summary of your observation or feedback.
	+ Enter a short paragraph to concisely describe your observation or feedback.
* **Supervisor Observation Feedback Form Comments** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_form_comments`)
	+ Add any additional comments or details about your observation or feedback.
	+ This field is for providing more depth and context to your observation or feedback.
* **Supervisor Observation Feedback Form Rating** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_form_rating`)
	+ Select one of the following ratings: Good, Fair.
	+ This field is for evaluating the quality or effectiveness of the observed behavior or performance.
* **Supervisor Observation Feedback Form Score** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_form_score`)
	+ Enter a numerical score out of 5 to represent your observation or feedback (e.g., 3.5).
	+ This field is for quantifying your observation or feedback on a numerical scale.
* **Supervisor Observation Feedback Report Comments** (`supervisor_observation_feedback_report_form_comments`)
	+ Add any additional comments or details about your observation or feedback.
	+ This field is for providing more information or context to your observation or feedback.

## Tips
* Be objective and specific when providing feedback, focusing on facts and data rather than personal opinions.
* Use clear and concise language in all fields.
* Avoid using jargon or technical terms that may be unfamiliar to the reader.
* Proofread carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and clarity.
