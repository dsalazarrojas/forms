# Fitness Instructor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Instructor Feedback Survey is a form used to collect feedback and information about fitness instructors. This survey is designed to help instructors provide valuable insights and feedback about their performance, teaching style, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as a fitness instructor.
2. Select the programs you currently instruct to provide context for your feedback.
3. Provide your feedback and suggestions for improvement.
4. Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about your teaching experience.
5. Enter your phone number and availability information.
6. Select your level of experience, certifications, and languages spoken.
7. Choose your level of professionalism and communication style.
8. Indicate your fee structure and schedule.
9. Select any relevant licenses or certifications you hold.
10. Enter your years of experience and hours of availability.
11. Finally, indicate your level of English proficiency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as a fitness instructor.
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you.
* **Programs Instructed** (`instructor_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Select the programs you currently instruct to provide context for your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`, text, optional): Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have about your teaching experience.
* **Suggest Improvements** (`instructor_suggestions`, text, optional): Add any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
* **Comments** (`instructor_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **Instructor Phone** (`instructor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Availability** (`instructor_availability`, select_one, optional): Select your availability schedule.
* **Instructor Experience** (`instructor_experience`, select_one, optional): Choose your level of experience as an instructor.
* **Certifications** (`instructor_certifications`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant certifications you hold.
* **Language** (`instructor_language`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the languages you speak or communicate in.
* **English Level** (`instructor_english_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of English proficiency.
* **Professionalism** (`instructor_professionalism`, select_one, optional): Select your level of professionalism and communication style.
* **Fees** (`instructor_fees`, select_one, optional): Indicate your fee structure.
* **Schedule** (`instructor_schedule`, time, optional): Enter your schedule, if applicable.
* **Licenses** (`instructor_licenses`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant licenses or certifications you hold.
* **Years of Experience** (`instructor_experience_years`, number, optional): Enter your years of experience as an instructor.
* **Hours of Availability** (`instructor_hours`, number, optional): Enter your available hours of instruction.
* **English** (`instructor_english`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of English proficiency.
