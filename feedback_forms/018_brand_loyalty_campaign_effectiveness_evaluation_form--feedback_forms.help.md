# Brand Loyalty Campaign Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help evaluate the effectiveness of a brand loyalty campaign. It will guide you in assessing the campaign's reach, engagement, and conversion impact, as well as gathering feedback for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the name or identifier of the campaign you are evaluating. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
2. Next, select the type of respondent you are. This will help us differentiate between customer, internal, or partner feedback.
3. Evaluate the campaign's reach effectiveness by selecting one of the three options: Very effective, Effective, or Ineffective. This will help us understand how well the campaign was able to reach its target audience.
4. Determine the engagement level of the participants by selecting one of the three options: High, Moderate, or Low. This will help us understand how invested the audience was in the campaign.
5. Assess the conversion impact of the campaign by selecting one of the three options: Significant, Moderate, or None. This will help us understand how the campaign influenced customer behavior, such as sign-ups or purchases.
6. Provide any relevant key success metrics and results for the campaign. This will help us understand what was achieved and what can be improved.
7. Offer improvement suggestions for future campaigns based on your experience.
8. Finally, provide any additional comments or observations about the campaign.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Campaign name
* **Campaign name** (`Campaign name`, text, required): Enter the name or identifier of the campaign being evaluated.
### Respondent type
* **Respondent type** (`Respondent type`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a customer, internal reviewer, or partner.
### Reach effectiveness
* **Reach effectiveness** (`Reach effectiveness`, select_one, required): Select how well the campaign reached its target audience (Very effective, Effective, or Ineffective).
### Engagement level
* **Engagement level** (`Engagement level`, select_one, required): Choose the level of engagement the campaign generated (High, Moderate, or Low).
### Conversion impact
* **Conversion impact** (`Conversion impact`, select_one, optional): Assess the campaign's influence on customer behavior (Significant, Moderate, or None).
### Key success metrics
* **Key success metrics** (`Key success metrics`, text, optional): Note any relevant metrics and results achieved during the campaign.
### Improvement recommendations
* **Improvement recommendations** (`Improvement recommendations`, text, optional): Suggest improvements for future campaigns.
### Additional comments
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, text, optional): Share any final observations or thoughts about the campaign.
