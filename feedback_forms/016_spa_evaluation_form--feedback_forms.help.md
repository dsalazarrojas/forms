# spa_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for evaluating spas and wellness centers to gather information about their products, services, and customer experiences. It helps spa professionals provide detailed insights into their operations and services for quality control or improvement purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely, leaving no fields blank.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information in the form fields.
3. If you need to provide additional comments or notes, use the "Form Data" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (Form Data, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes you may have.
* **Product Name** (Product Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being evaluated.
* **Product Type** (Product Type, text, optional): Indicate the type of service or product offered by the spa.
* **Customer Data** (Customer Data, note, optional): Provide any relevant customer information, such as their name, contact information, or other details.
* **Medical Info** (Medical Info, text, optional): Enter any medical-related information or concerns related to the product or service.
* **Customer Experience** (Customer Experience, text, optional): Evaluate the customer's overall experience at the spa.
* **Wish to Avail Services** (Wish to Avail Services, text, optional): Express the customer's desire for specific services.
* **Product Safety** (Product Safety, number, optional): Rate the safety of the product or service using a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5 or 1-10). 

## Tips

- Be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out the form.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
- For quality control purposes, please fill out the form in its entirety.
