# feedback_resolution_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide feedback and complaints about your experiences. It will help us understand your concerns and take action to resolve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please select "Technical", "Quality", or "Service" for "complaint_type" to specify the type of complaint you are submitting.
* Provide a detailed description of your complaint in the "description" field.
* If you'd like to be contacted about your complaint, please add your contact number in the "contact_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Complaint Form** (`complaint_form`, text, required: false): Please provide a detailed and specific description of your complaint.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required: false): Please include your contact information or any relevant details about yourself, such as your name, email address, or phone number.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, select_one, required: false): Select "Technical", "Quality", or "Service" to indicate the type of complaint you are submitting.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Please provide a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted about your complaint, please add your contact number here.
