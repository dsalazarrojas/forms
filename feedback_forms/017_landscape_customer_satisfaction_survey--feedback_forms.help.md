# Landscape Customer Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Landscape Customer Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather customer feedback on their experience with the company's landscaping services. This feedback is essential for the company to better understand its strengths and weaknesses and improve its overall service quality.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant option for each question from the provided options.
2. Enter any additional information or comments in the text fields.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Overall Experience**: This question assesses your overall experience with the company's services. Please select one of the options that best describes your experience:
	+ Very Good: You were extremely satisfied with the services provided.
	+ Good: You were satisfied with the services provided.
	+ Fair: You had some issues with the services provided.
	+ Poor: You were not satisfied with the services provided.
* **Landscaping Services**: Please select all services that were provided to you:
	+ Cutting Lawn Mowers: Lawn mowing services.
	+ Trimming Trees: Tree trimming services.
	+ Pruning Bushes: Pruning bushes and shrubs.
	+ Planting Flowers: Planting flowers and plants.
	+ Fertilizing Soil: Fertilizing soil services.
	+ Pest Control: Pest control services.
* **Communication with Technician**: Please describe any interactions or conversations you had with the technician who provided your services.
* **Customer Service**: This question assesses the quality of customer service you received:
	+ Excellent: You received exceptional customer service.
	+ Good: You received satisfactory customer service.
	+ Fair: You had some issues with customer service.
	+ Poor: You were not satisfied with customer service.
* **Technician Attitude**: Please select all attributes that describe the technician's attitude:
	+ Friendly: The technician was friendly and approachable.
	+ Helpful: The technician was helpful and knowledgeable.
	+ Professional: The technician was professional and courteous.
	+ Knowledgeable: The technician was knowledgeable about the work being done.
	+ Courteous: The technician was courteous and respectful.
* **Technician Knowledge**: Please select all attributes that describe the technician's knowledge:
	+ Good: The technician had a good understanding of the work being done.
	+ Excellent: The technician had an exceptional understanding of the work being done.
	+ Fair: The technician had some knowledge but struggled with some aspects.
	+ Poor: The technician had minimal knowledge about the work being done.
* **Technician Workmanship**: Please select all attributes that describe the technician's work quality:
	+ Good: The technician's work was satisfactory.
	+ Excellent: The technician's work was exceptional.
	+ Fair: The technician's work had some issues.
	+ Poor: The technician's work was not satisfactory.
* **Customer Information**: Please enter any additional information about yourself, such as your contact details.
* **Feedback**: Please enter any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the company.
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your name.
* **Customer Email**: Please enter your email address (optional).
* **Customer Phone**: Please enter your phone number (optional).

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the text fields provided.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact the company directly for guidance.
