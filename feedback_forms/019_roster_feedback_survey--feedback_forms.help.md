# Roster Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants after a session or event, with the goal of improving the quality of future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Answer the "feedback_question" field with your thoughts about the session.
3. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10 in the "rating" field.
4. Add any comments you have about the session in the "comments" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field, which will allow us to contact you with more information.
6. If applicable, enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Indicate if you attended the session in the "attended" field.
8. Rate the quality of the session in the "session_quality" field.
9. Indicate your overall satisfaction with the session in the "overall_satisfaction" field.
10. Let us know if you will recommend the session to others in the "will_recommend" field.
11. Indicate if you will attend future sessions in the "will_attend" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **feedback_question** (text, required/optional): Answer this field with your thoughts about the session.
* **rating** (number, required/optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **comments** (text, required/optional): Add any comments you have about the session.
* **email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **phone** (text, required/optional): If applicable, enter your phone number.
* **attended** (select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate if you attended the session.
* **session_quality** (select_one, required/optional): Rate the quality of the session.
* **overall_satisfaction** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **will_recommend** (select_one, required/optional): Let us know if you will recommend the session.
* **will_attend** (select_one, required/optional): Indicate if you will attend future sessions.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields that apply to you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve future sessions.
