# Red Banner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Red Banner Contact Form is designed to collect user feedback and issues related to the "Red Banner" feature. This form helps us understand your concerns, questions, or suggestions, allowing us to improve the feature and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Write your feedback or message in the "message" field.
4. If your issue is related to the "Red Banner", select "Yes" or "No" in the "red_banner" field.
5. If you'd like to receive responses from other users who have encountered similar issues, select "Yes" or "No" in the "get_user_responses" field.
6. Finally, choose how you'd like to see the feature styled in the "style" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (text, required): This field is for your name. Please enter your full name to help us identify you in our response.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you regarding your feedback.
* **message** (text, required): Write your feedback or message in this field. This could be regarding any issue or concern you have with the "Red Banner" feature.
* **red_banner** (text, required): Select "Yes" if your issue is related to the "Red Banner" feature, "No" otherwise.
* **get_user_responses** (select_multiple, required): If you'd like to receive responses from other users who have encountered similar issues, select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
* **style** (select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to see the "Red Banner" feature styled. This will help us understand your preferences.
* **style options:** "Yes" (you prefer the current style), "No" (you do not like the current style), "Maybe" (you're undecided or neutral).

## Tips

* Please ensure that your feedback is accurate and detailed.
* If you've already submitted feedback, it's not necessary to resubmit.
* We'll use your feedback to improve the "Red Banner" feature and provide better service.
