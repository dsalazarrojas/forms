# Appointment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Appointment Feedback Form is designed to gather your opinions and experiences about your recent appointment at our facility. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we use it to improve our services and provide better experiences for our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your information in the provided fields:
	* If you have an email address, please enter it accurately.
	* If you have a phone number, enter it if you feel comfortable sharing it with us.
3. Provide details about your appointment:
	* Choose the correct service you received during the appointment.
	* Choose how satisfied you were with the service quality and provider's professionalism.
	* Choose how satisfied you are with the cleanliness and hygiene of our facility.
	* Choose the correct option for the wait time.
4. Share your experience with our facility:
	* Choose how satisfied you are with the overall comfort and atmosphere of our facility.
	* Check if the results of your appointment met your expectations.
	* Choose how you feel about the value for money you received.
5. Finally, answer the following questions:
	* Would you recommend our facility to others?
	* Do you have any suggestions for improvement?
	* Share any additional comments you'd like to make about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, \*): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, \*): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you feel comfortable sharing it with us.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, \*): Enter the date of your appointment.
* **Service Received** (`service_received`, select_one, \*): Choose the service you received during the appointment:
	+ Hair styling
	+ Facial
	+ Massage
	+ Nail service
	+ Medical consultation
	+ Other
* **Service Provider Name** (`service_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, \*): Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Quality of Service** (`service_quality`, select_one, \*): Choose how satisfied you are with the service quality:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Provider Professionalism** (`provider_professionalism`, select_one, \*): Choose how satisfied you are with the provider's professionalism:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Cleanliness and Hygiene** (`cleanliness`, select_one, \*): Choose how satisfied you are with the cleanliness and hygiene:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`, select_one, optional): Choose your experience with the wait time:
	+ No wait
	+ Minimal wait
	+ Acceptable wait
	+ Long wait
	+ Excessive wait
* **Facility Experience** (`facility_experience`, note, optional): Share your thoughts about your experience with our facility.
* **Comfort of Facility** (`facility_comfort`, select_one, optional): Choose how comfortable you felt in our facility:
	+ Very comfortable
	+ Comfortable
	+ Average
	+ Uncomfortable
	+ Very uncomfortable
* **Atmosphere and Ambiance** (`facility_atmosphere`, select_one, optional): Choose your experience with the atmosphere and ambiance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Results and Value** (`results_satisfaction`, note, optional): Share your thoughts about the results and value of your appointment.
* **Did Results Meet Expectations?** (`results_expectations`, select_one, \*): Choose if the results met your expectations:
	+ Exceeded expectations
	+ Met expectations
	+ Partially met expectations
	+ Did not meet expectations
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, optional): Choose how you feel about the value for money:
	+ Excellent value
	+ Good value
	+ Fair value
	+ Poor value
	+ Very poor value
* **Would Recommend?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, \*): Choose if you would recommend our facility to others:
	+ Yes, definitely
	+ Yes, probably
	+ Maybe
	+ Probably not
	+ Definitely not
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you may have for us to improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

Note: This help guide is intended to be comprehensive and clear, but it's not exhaustive. If you're unsure about any field, please ask a member of our staff for assistance.
