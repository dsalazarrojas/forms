# Wash Bay And Paint Bay Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the cleanliness and overall experience of the Wash Bay And Paint Bay facility. It will help maintenance and improvement efforts, ensuring that the facility remains a safe and satisfactory environment for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Cleanliness" page and choose a number rating (1-5) that reflects your assessment of the facility's cleanliness.
2. If you have any comments or observations about the cleanliness, type them in the "Cleanliness Comments" field.
3. Move to the "Equipment" page and type your answer to the question, "Was the equipment available and in good working order?"
4. For the "Staff" page, select all the staff members who provided you with excellent service during your visit. You can choose multiple staff members.
5. On the "Overall Experience" page, provide your overall assessment of the facility's experience (1-5).
6. If you have any comments about the overall experience, type them in the "Overall Experience Comments" field.
7. Finally, provide any comments about the staff's performance in the "Staff Comments" field and any comments about the facility's equipment in the "Equipment Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Cleanliness** (Number, Required: No): Rate the cleanliness of the Wash Bay And Paint Bay facility from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Equipment** (Text, Required: No): Answer the question, "Was the equipment available and in good working order?" with a yes or no response.
* **Staff** (Select Multiple, Required: No): Select all staff members who provided you with excellent service during your visit.
* **Overall Experience** (Note, Required: No): Provide your overall assessment of the facility's experience from 1 to 5.
* **Cleanliness Comments** (Text, Required: No): Type any comments about the cleanliness of the facility.
* **Equipment Comments** (Text, Required: No): Type any comments about the facility's equipment.
* **Staff Comments** (Text, Required: No): Type any comments about the staff's performance.
* **Overall Experience Comments** (Text, Required: No): Type any additional comments about your overall experience.
* **Facility Rating** (Number, Required: No): Rate your overall assessment of the facility from 1 to 5.
