# Placement Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the placement of products, services, or features within our system. The goal is to gather information on how well users like or dislike certain placements, and what they think could be improved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
   * Click on the "Feedback Form" button.
   * Fill out each field as instructed.
   * Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your overall feedback about the placement. Please type in your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Placement Experience** (`placement_experience`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the placement. How did you feel about it? Was it helpful or unhelpful?
* **Positive Feedback** (`positive_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the things that you found positive about the placement. Choose from the options: Positive, Negative, Neutral.
* **Placement Pros** (`placement_pros`, text, optional): Describe the pros (advantages) you found with the placement.
* **Placement Cons** (`placement_cons`, text, optional): Describe the cons (disadvantages) you found with the placement.
* **Placement Rating** (`placement_rating`, number, optional): Rate the placement on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the placement on a scale from 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Additional comments or suggestions you have about the placement.
