# Clothing Brand Customer Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
Our clothing brand is committed to providing the best possible experience for our customers. To help us achieve this, we need your feedback on your recent purchase. This form will guide you through the different aspects of your experience, from customer information to product feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
2. If you have no experience with our brand, please do not fill out the form.
3. Please complete the form in a single sitting to avoid duplicate submissions.
4. Please answer all required fields (indicated by `required: true`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Your Name (Customer Name)** (customer_name, text, not required)
Your name is the name we will associate with your feedback. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 2. **Email Address** (email_address, email, not required)
Your email address will be used for follow-up purposes only. If you do not wish to receive emails from us, please do not enter an email address.
### 3. **Date of Purchase** (purchase_date, date, required)
Please enter the date you made the purchase from our brand.
### 4. **Type of Product Purchased** (product_type, select_one, required)
Please select the type of product you purchased (e.g., T-shirt, Dress, Pants, etc.).
### 5. **Product Quality Rating** (product_quality_rating, number, required)
Rate the quality of the product from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
### 6. **Fit Satisfaction** (fit_satisfaction, select_one, required)
Rate your satisfaction with the fit of the product (e.g., Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied).
### 7. **Material Comfort** (material_comfort, select_one, required)
Rate the comfort and quality of the material from Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
### 8. **Color Accuracy** (color_accuracy, select_one, required)
Rate the accuracy of the product color from Yes, perfect match; Close enough; or Not accurate.
### 9. **Expected Durability** (durability_expectation, select_one, not required)
Please indicate how long you expect the product to last (e.g., Several months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5+ years).
### 10. **Website Ease of Use** (website_ease_of_use, select_one, required)
Rate the ease of use of our website from Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, or Very difficult.
### 11. **Checkout Process** (checkout_experience, select_one, required)
Rate the checkout process from Yes, Somewhat, or No.
### 12. **Shipping Speed** (shipping_speed, select_one, required)
Rate the shipping speed from Faster than expected, As expected, or Slower than expected.
### 13. **Packaging Quality** (packaging_quality, select_one, required)
Rate the quality and presentation of the product packaging from Excellent, Good, Average, or Poor.
### 14. **Brand Loyalty** (brand_loyalty, number, required)
Rate your likelihood to purchase from us again from 1 (unlikely) to 5 (very likely).
### 15. **Recommend Brand** (brand_recommendation, select_one, required)
Indicate if you would definitely, probably, or probably not recommend our brand to friends or family.
### 16. **Value for Money** (value_for_money, select_one, required)
Rate the value of the product compared to its price (e.g., Yes, great value; Fair value; Overpriced).
### 17. **Improvement Suggestions** (improvement_suggestions, text, not required)
Please provide any additional feedback or suggestions on how we can improve our brand, products, or services.
### 18. **Additional Product Feedback** (product_feedback, text, not required)
If you have any comments, compliments, or criticisms about our products, please provide them here.

Thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts with us! Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we will use it to make our brand the best it can be.
