# sports_photography_satisfaction_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback about the sports photography service experience. The goal is to gather information on image quality, professionalism, delivery time, overall satisfaction, and any additional comments or suggestions. This feedback will help us improve the service to better meet the needs of our clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review each question carefully and answer according to your experience with the sports photography service.
2. Select the most suitable option for each question from the provided choices.
3. Fill in your email address and phone number if you have any additional comments or suggestions.
4. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Sports Photography Satisfaction Survey

*   **Sports Photography Satisfaction Survey** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a general note about the service. You can provide any comments or feedback about your experience.
*   **What was the image quality of the photos?** (`image_quality`, `select_one`, required: false):
    *   Select one of the options:
        +   Excellent
        +   Good
        +   Fair
        +   Poor
*   **How would you rate the professionalism of the photographer?** (`professionalism`, `select_one`, required: false):
    *   Select one of the options:
        +   Excellent
        +   Good
        +   Fair
        +   Poor
*   **What was the delivery time of the photos?** (`delivery_time`, `select_one`, required: false):
    *   Select one of the options:
        +   Fast
        +   Average
        +   Slow
*   **What was your overall satisfaction with the photography service?** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false):
    *   Select one of the options:
        +   Satisfied
        +   Neutral
        +   Dissatisfied
*   **Do you have any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please type them here.
*   **Your E-mail** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Your Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips

*   Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback.
*   If you have any concerns or issues, please provide a detailed explanation.
*   Your feedback is valuable to help us improve the service.
*   If you would like to remain anonymous, you can request this option, but your contact information will still be required for our records.
*   We appreciate your time and input, and will use it to improve the service for future clients.
