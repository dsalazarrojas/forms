# Laptop Rental Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have used our laptop rental service. It helps us understand your experience and gather information to improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the form completely, but only once for each laptop rental experience.
* Select all that apply for the "Laptops Condition" field.
* For "Support Quality," select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
* In the "Overall Experience" field, select one option that best describes your experience.
* In the "Comments" field, type a brief description of your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Laptops Condition**: How is the laptop you rented in terms of its condition? Select all that apply:
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Support Quality**: How satisfied are you with our support team? (1 = Poor, 5 = Excellent)
* **Overall Experience**: How would you rate your overall experience with our laptop rental service? (Select one)
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments**: Provide a brief description of your experience with our laptop rental service.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input helps us improve our service.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
* We value your feedback and appreciate the time you take to provide it.
