# Henna Experience Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Henna Experience Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with henna services. This form helps us understand how our customers feel about their session, whether they were satisfied, and if they would recommend our services to others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answer to the question "Henna Artist" from the options provided.
2. Enter the date of your henna session in the "Session Date" field. Make sure to use the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
3. Choose how you felt about your session by selecting one of the options in the "Client Satisfaction Rating" field.
4. If you'd like to add any additional comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.
5. Let us know if you would recommend our services to others by selecting one or more of the options in the "Recommendation" field.
6. If you'd like to provide your email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
7. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Henna Artist
* **Henna Artist** (`artist`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select the henna artist who served you during your session. Please choose the correct artist from the options provided.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your henna session in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).

### Client Satisfaction Rating
* **Client Satisfaction Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you felt about your session by selecting one of the options: Satisfied or Dissatisfied.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to add any additional comments about your experience, enter them here.

### Recommendation
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `select_multiple`, optional): Let us know if you would recommend our services to others by selecting one or more of the options: Yes or No.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you'd like to provide your email address, enter it here. This will allow us to contact you for follow-up.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.

### Session Date
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `time`, optional): This field is not used. Please ignore it and focus on the other fields.

Tips:

* Make sure to enter your comments in a clear and concise manner.
* Select the correct artist and session date to ensure we have accurate information.
* If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email and/or phone number.
