# Heath Challenge Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Heath Challenge Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback on the challenges provided by the Heath Challenge program. We value your input and would like to hear about your experience with the challenges you've participated in.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each field to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback on Challenge** (`challenge_type`, text, required/optional): Please provide your general feedback on the challenge you participated in. This can include your thoughts on the challenge's effectiveness, the materials provided, and any suggestions you may have for improvement.
* **Challenge Name** (`challenge_name`, text, required/optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific challenge, please mention the challenge's name so we can look into it specifically.
* **Challenge Category** (`challenge_category`, select_one, required/optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific challenge, please select the challenge category it falls under (e.g. Fitness, Weight Loss, Healthy Eating).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific challenge, please enter the start date of the challenge.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): If you're providing feedback on a specific challenge, please enter the end date of the challenge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
* Please be considerate and respectful in your comments.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please let us know.
