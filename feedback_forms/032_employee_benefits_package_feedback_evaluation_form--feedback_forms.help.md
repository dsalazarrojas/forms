# Employee Benefits Package Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Benefits Package Feedback Evaluation Form is a tool designed to collect feedback on the employee benefits package offered by the organization. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and ensures that the benefits package meets the needs and expectations of employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This is the best way to ensure that your feedback reaches the intended recipients.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field, if you prefer to be contacted directly.
5. Enter the name of the benefits package you are evaluating in the "package name" field.
6. Rate your satisfaction with the benefits package on a scale from 1 to 5 in the "rating" field.
7. Select all the benefits that are included in the package in the "benefits list" field. Note that some benefits may be required for all packages, so select all that apply.
8. If you have any comments or suggestions about the benefits package, select one of the options in the "comments" field.
9. Enter the date you received the benefits package in the "date" field.
10. Enter the time you received the benefits package in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address to ensure your feedback reaches the intended recipients.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number, if you prefer to be contacted directly.
* **Package name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the benefits package you are evaluating.
* **Rating** (number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the benefits package on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (select_one, required: false): Select one of the options if you have any comments or suggestions about the benefits package.
* **Benefits list** (select_multiple, required: true): Select all the benefits that are included in the package.
* **Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date you received the benefits package.
* **Time** (time, required: false): Enter the time you received the benefits package.
