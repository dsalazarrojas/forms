# Seminar Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Feedback Form is designed to gather attendees' feedback on the seminar they've attended. Your input helps us understand what worked well and what could be improved, allowing us to provide better experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the seminar feedback in the text field labeled "Feedback". Please provide a clear and concise description of your experience and any suggestions you might have.
* Enter your name in the text field labeled "Name".
* Rate your overall experience with the number field labeled "Rating".
* Select any comments or suggestions you'd like to add using the checkboxes labeled "Comments".
* Enter the date you attended the seminar using the date field labeled "Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name so we know who's submitting the feedback. Please enter your full name as you'd like to be addressed.
* **Feedback** (Text, Required): This is where you provide your thoughts about the seminar. Be honest and clear in your description, focusing on what you liked, disliked, and any suggestions you might have.
* **Rating** (Number, Required): Rate your overall experience on a scale (e.g., 1-5). This helps us gauge the quality of the seminar.
* **Comments** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all that apply from the list of options to add additional comments or suggestions about the seminar.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you attended the seminar. This helps us track when you provided feedback and ensure it's related to the correct event.
