# smoking_management_course_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants to help course creators improve the smoking management course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions truthfully, providing specific examples when possible.
2. Rate the course on a scale of 1-5 for the relevant questions.
3. Provide detailed comments for the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction and background**: Describe how you learned about this course.
* **How did you learn about this course?**: Explain how you discovered this course, e.g., social media, referral, search engine.
* **Who is this course intended for?**: Who do you think the course is suitable for, or who would benefit from it.
* **How relevant is the course content to your needs?**: Rate how well the course content matched your expectations.
* **How would you rate the quality of the information presented?**: Rate the accuracy and usefulness of the course content.
* **What were the most helpful topics or modules?**: Specify the topics or modules that you found most useful.
* **How would you rate the pace of the course?**: Rate how comfortable you were with the pace of the course.
* **How would you rate the organization and layout of the course materials?**: Rate how well the course materials were structured.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the course?**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **How would you rate your engagement with the course content?**: Rate how interested you were in the course content.
* **What were the most positive aspects of the course?**: Specify the aspects that you found most valuable.
* **What were the most negative aspects of the course?**: Specify the aspects that you found least useful.
* **How would you rate the technical quality of the course materials (e.g., videos, images, audio)?**: Rate the quality of the course materials.
* **How would you rate the logistical support from the course organizers?**: Rate how well you were supported throughout the course.
* **How would you rate the availability of resources and materials?**: Rate how well resources and materials were available.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the course content?**: Propose changes to make the course more effective.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the course format and organization?**: Propose changes to make the course more engaging.
* **Do you have any other suggestions for the course?**: Additional suggestions for improvement.
* **Participant Comments 1**: Share any comments or thoughts about the course.
* **Participant Rating 1**: Rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.
* **Participant Comments 2**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about the course.
* **Participant Rating 2**: Rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.
* **Participant Comments 3**: Share any further comments or thoughts about the course.
* **Participant Rating 3**: Rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.
* **Participant Comments 4**: Share any final comments or thoughts about the course.
* **Participant Rating 4**: Rate your satisfaction with the course on a scale of 1-5.

Please note that this form is designed to provide valuable feedback to course creators, so be honest and detailed in your answers.
