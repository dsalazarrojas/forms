# survey_invitation_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The survey_invitation_consent_form is a form designed to gather participant information, survey details, and obtain consent for participation in a research study.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, simply fill in the requested information in the corresponding fields.
- Ensure that you provide accurate contact information for future communication.
- You may be asked to provide additional comments if you have any other information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participants Information**: This is the section where you will provide general information about yourself, such as your name and title.
- **Contact Information**: Please enter your contact information so that we can reach you for future communication.
- **Survey Details**: Here you will provide information about the survey you are participating in.
- **Consent**: Please indicate your consent for participating in the survey. You can select one of the available options.
- **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for any follow-up questions or updates.
- **Phone**: Provide your phone number for future communication.
- **Additional Comments**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or information you would like to share about your participation.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at our contact information below.
