# Mentor Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mentor Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from mentees on their experiences with their mentors. This feedback is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the mentorship program and identify areas for improvement. Please complete this form to help us understand your experiences and suggest ways to enhance our mentorship program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
5. **Date**: Select the date of your mentorship experience.
6. **Time**: Select the time of your mentorship experience.
7. **Challenges Encountered**: Choose the challenges you faced during your mentorship experience. Select all that apply.
	* Time management
	* Communication
	* Teamwork
	* Problem-solving
	* Interpersonal
	* Adaptability
8. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.
9. **Evaluation Score**: Rate your overall evaluation score out of 5.
10. **Score Scale**: Select the scale that best describes your evaluation score.
	* 1
	* 2
	* 3
	* 4
	* 5
11. **Evaluation**: Choose how you would evaluate your mentorship experience. Select all that apply.
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
12. **Recommendations**: Suggest ways to improve the mentorship program.
	* Improve time management
	* Improve communication skills
	* Improve problem-solving skills
	* Improve adaptability
	* Improve interpersonal skills
13. **Challenges Solved**: Choose the challenges you successfully solved during your mentorship experience. Select all that apply.
	* Time management
	* Communication
	* Teamwork
	* Problem-solving
	* Interpersonal
14. **Challenges Not Solved**: Choose the challenges you did not solve during your mentorship experience. Select all that apply.
	* Time management
	* Communication
	* Teamwork
	* Problem-solving
	* Interpersonal
15. **Evaluation Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your evaluation.
16. **Final Evaluation**: Choose how you would evaluate your overall satisfaction with the mentorship program.
	* Highly Satisfactory
	* Moderately Satisfactory
	* Needs Improvement
17. **Mentor Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your mentor.
18. **Final Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your experience.
