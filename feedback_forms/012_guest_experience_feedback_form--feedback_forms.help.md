# Guest Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from guests about their experience at our establishment. We value your opinion and would like to hear about your stay to improve our services and facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in the order of the pages as shown below.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If a question requires a numerical rating, please rate it on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
4. For questions with options, select the one that best describes your experience.
5. Be as detailed as possible in your answers to help us understand your stay better.
6. If you have any feedback, please fill it in the "Any other feedback?" section at the end of the form.
