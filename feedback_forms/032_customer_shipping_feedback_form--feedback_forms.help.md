# Customer Shipping Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts and opinions about our shipping service. We value your feedback and use it to improve our operations and provide a better experience for you and all our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by typing in the required fields.
2. Provide as much detail as you can about your shipping experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can add them in the provided notes section.
4. Click submit to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What do you think about our shipping service (Page 1)
* **What do you think about our shipping service** (`customer_shipping_feedback_form_first_page`, text, required: false): Please share your thoughts and opinions about our shipping service. Be as specific as you can about what you liked or disliked about your experience.

### Rate our customer service (Page 2)
* **Rate our customer service** (`customer_shipping_feedback_form_second_page`, text, required: false): How would you rate our customer service? Choose from the options: Very Good, Good, Fair.

### Shipping Experience (Page 3)
* **Shipping Experience** (`shipping_experience`, select_one, required: false): How would you rate your overall shipping experience? Choose from the options: Very Good, Good, Fair.
* **Shipping Speed** (`shipping_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): How fast was your shipping experience? Choose from the options: Fast, Average, Slow.

### Contact Information (Page 4)
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by us, please enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If you'd like to be contacted by us, please enter your phone number.

### Notes (Page 5)
* **Note** (`customer_shipping_feedback_form_note`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them here.
* **Any other comments** (`customer_shipping_feedback_form_note_2`, note, required: false): Do you have any other comments or suggestions about our shipping service? Please add them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and detailed in your feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us.
* We value your feedback and will use it to improve our shipping service.
