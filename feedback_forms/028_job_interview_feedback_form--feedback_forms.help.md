# Job Interview Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from interviewers on their interactions with candidates. This feedback is used to improve the candidate experience and identify areas for growth for the interviewers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and fill out each field with the relevant information.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Provide clear and constructive feedback in the feedback comments field.
4. Click the submit button to save your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Candidate Rating
**Candidate Rating** (`candidate_rating`, number, required: false)
Please rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-5.

### 2. Job Interviewer Rating
**Job Interviewer Rating** (`job_interviewer_rating`, number, required: false)
Please rate the job interviewer's performance on a scale of 1-5.

### 3. Feedback Comments
**Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comment`, text, required: false)
Please provide any comments or feedback you have about the interaction with the candidate.

### 4. Date of Interview
**Date of Interview** (`date_of_interview`, date, required: false)
Please enter the date of the interview in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 5. Time of Interview
**Time of Interview** (`time_of_interview`, time, required: false)
Please enter the time of the interview in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

### 6. Job Title
**Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false)
Please enter the job title of the position that was interviewed for.

### 7. Job Description
**Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false)
Please enter a brief summary of the job description.

### 8. Job Type
**Job Type** (`job_type`, text, required: false)
Please enter the type of job (e.g. full-time, part-time, contractor).

### 9. Interviewer's Name
**Interviewer's Name** (`interviewers_name`, text, required: false)
Please enter the name of the interviewer who conducted the interview.

### 10. Interview Location
**Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, required: false)
Please enter the location where the interview was conducted.
