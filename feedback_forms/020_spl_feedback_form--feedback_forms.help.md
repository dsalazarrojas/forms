# SPL Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SPL Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback on a specific performance, skill, or event in sports-related activities such as soccer, basketball, tennis, and volleyball.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your comments on the general performance.
2. Select the specific skills you'd like to give feedback on.
3. Enter the date and time of the performance.
4. Enter your goals for this performance.
5. Add any additional comments you'd like to make.
6. Enter your phone number for follow-up, if desired.
7. Select a category for your feedback (Soccer, Basketball, Tennis, or Volleyball).
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback on general performance** (`general_performance`, text, required): This field is where you provide your overall comments on the performance. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Feedback on specific skills** (`specific_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select the skills you'd like to give feedback on from the options: Strengths, Weaknesses, or Other. You can select more than one skill.
* **Date of performance** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when this performance occurred.
* **Time of performance** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time when this performance occurred, if applicable.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Enter your goals for this performance.
* **Additional comments** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up, if desired.
* **Select Category** (`select_category`, select_one, required): Select a category for your feedback: Soccer, Basketball, Tennis, or Volleyball.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your feedback.
