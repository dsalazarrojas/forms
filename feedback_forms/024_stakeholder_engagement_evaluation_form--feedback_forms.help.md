## Stakeholder Engagement Evaluation Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the level of engagement between your team/stakeholders. It is essential to complete this form to provide feedback on the quality of service, level of engagement, and other relevant information that helps us improve our services.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Review each page carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2.  Select the correct options for each field based on your experience and knowledge.
3.  Be honest and provide accurate information in the text fields.
4.  Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Intro Page** (`intro-page`, `note`, required: false): This page provides a brief introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and guidelines of the form.
*   **Contact Details Page** (`contact-details-page`, `select_one`, required: false): Select `Yes` if you have made contact with the team/stakeholders or `No` otherwise.
*   **Engagement Level Page** (`engagement-level`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the level of engagement with the team/stakeholders on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
*   **Stakeholder Level Page** (`stakeholder-level`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all that apply from High, Low, or other relevant options.
*   **Frequency of Engagement Page** (`frequency-of-engagement`, `select_one`, required: false): Select `Yes` if the engagement occurs frequently or `No` otherwise.
*   **Quality of Service Page** (`quality-of-service`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from Good, Poor, or other relevant options.
*   **Frequency of Contact Page** (`frequency-of-contact`, `select_one`, required: false): Select `Yes` if you contact frequently or `No` otherwise.
*   **Stakeholder Engagement Level Page** (`stakeholder-engagement-level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose from High, Low, or other relevant options.
*   **Comments Page** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the team/stakeholders.
*   **Additional Info Page** (`additional-info`, `text`, required: false): You can provide any additional information you think is relevant.
*   **Rating Page** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Enter a rating on a scale from 1-10.
*   **Feedback Page** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have about how we can improve our services.
*   **Email Page** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
*   **Phone Page** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.
