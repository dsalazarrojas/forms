# Identify Your Culinary Style - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify your personal culinary style by asking you to select your favorite cuisines, cooking techniques, dish types, and flavors. The form is meant to be a fun and interactive way to explore your taste buds!

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best represent your favorite cuisines, cooking techniques, dish types, and flavors from the respective dropdown menus.
2. Make sure to select all options that apply to you, as you can choose multiple options in some fields.
3. If you're unsure about any option, feel free to select "Other" or describe your preference in the "Notes" field (if provided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Favorite Cuisines** (`cuisines`, `select_multiple`): Select all the cuisines that you enjoy eating or cooking. This will help us understand your global culinary interests.
* **Cooking Techniques** (`cooking_techniques`, `select_one`): Choose the cooking techniques that you often use or enjoy using. This will help us understand your personal approach to cooking.
* **Favorite Dish Types** (`dish_types`, `select_one`): Select the types of dishes that you often cook or enjoy eating. This will help us understand your go-to meals or recipes.
* **Preferred Flavors** (`flavor_profiles`, `select_one`): Choose the flavors that you enjoy or often use in your cooking. This will help us understand your taste preferences.
