# Building Instructions Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on building instructions to help improve their quality, clarity, and overall usability. It is meant for respondents who have used the instructions and have valuable insights to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your name**: Enter your name as you would like to be recognized in the response.
2. **Indicate your role or position**: Describe your role or position that relates to the instructions.
3. **Enter your company or organization**: If you represent a company or organization, enter its name.
4. **Enter your email address**: This is for contact purposes and will not be shared publicly.
5. **Specify the type of instructions reviewed**: Choose the type of instructions you reviewed, such as assembly, operating manual, or maintenance guide.
6. **Provide the title or name of the instruction**: Enter the title or name of the instructions that you reviewed.
7. **Indicate the version or date of the instruction**: If available, please enter the instruction's version or date.
8. **Context for the review**: Choose the context in which you reviewed the instructions.
9. **Evaluate the overall usefulness and other aspects**: Rate the instructions in terms of their overall usefulness, clarity, completeness of information, organization and structure, formatting and visual presentation, technical accuracy, language level, step-by-step clarity, and overall usability.
10. **Provide feedback on specific areas for improvement**: Enter your comments or suggestions on specific areas where the instructions can be improved.
11. **Make specific suggestions for improvement**: Enter your suggestions on how to improve the instructions, including content that should be added or removed.
12. **Assess usability and accessibility**: Rate the instructions in terms of usability and accessibility for diverse users.
13. **Overall user experience**: Rate your overall user experience with the instructions.
14. **Recommend the instructions**: Choose whether or not you would recommend the instructions as is or suggest changes.
15. **Additional comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, **Text**): Enter your name.
* **Your Role or Position** (`respondent_role`, **Text**): Describe your role or position that relates to the instructions.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_organization`, **Text**): Enter the name of the company or organization.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, **Email**): Enter your email address for contact purposes only.
* **Type of Instructions** (`instruction_type`, **Select One**): Choose the type of instructions you reviewed, such as assembly, operating manual, or maintenance guide.
* **Instructions Title** (`instruction_title`, **Text**): Enter the title or name of the instruction that you reviewed.
* **Instruction Version or Date** (`instruction_version`, **Text**): If available, enter the instruction's version or date.
* **Context for Review** (`review_context`, **Select One**): Choose the context in which you reviewed the instructions.
* **Overall Usefulness** (`overall_assessment`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their overall usefulness, from excellent to very poor.
* **Clarity of Instructions** (`clarity_rating`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their clarity, from excellent to very poor.
* **Completeness of Information** (`completeness_rating`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of completeness, from excellent to very poor.
* **Organization and Structure** (`organization_rating`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their organization and structure, from excellent to very poor.
* **Formatting and Visual Presentation** (`formatting_quality`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their formatting and visual presentation, from excellent to very poor.
* **Technical Accuracy** (`technical_accuracy`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their technical accuracy, from excellent to very poor.
* **Language Level and Terminology** (`language_level`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their language level and terminology, from excellent to very poor.
* **Step-by-Step Clarity** (`step_clarity`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their step-by-step clarity, from excellent to very poor.
* **Overall Usability** (`usability`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their overall usability, from excellent to very poor.
* **Physical Format Preferences** (`physical_format`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their physical format, from excellent to very poor.
* **Accessibility for Diverse Users** (`accessibility_rating`, **Select One**): Rate the instructions in terms of their accessibility for diverse users, from excellent to very poor.
* **Main Weaknesses or Problem Areas** (`main_weaknesses`, **Text**): Enter your comments on any main weaknesses or problem areas you noticed.
* **Missing or Unclear Content** (`missing_content`, **Text**): Enter your comments on any content that was missing or unclear.
* **Specific Suggestions for Improvement** (`improvement_suggestions`, **Text**): Enter your suggestions on how to improve the instructions.
* **Content to be Added** (`additions_needed`, **Select Multiple**): Choose any content that you think should be added to the instructions.
* **Additional Comments or Observations** (`additional_comments`, **Text**): Enter any additional comments or observations you might have.

## Tips
* Please be specific and clear in your answers.
* Use examples or references to support your ratings or suggestions.
* Keep your answers concise and brief, and avoid repeating information already provided.
* If you are unsure about any question or rating, please ask us and we will be happy to help.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback; it is highly valued and will help to improve the instructions for the benefit of all users.
