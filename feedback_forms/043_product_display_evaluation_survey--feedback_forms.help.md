# Product Display Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect feedback from customers or store managers on the product display in a store.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, select the page you wish to evaluate.
2. Review each field and choose the corresponding answer based on your experience. Some fields have options while others are free text responses.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your opinion or experience.
4. For text fields, provide a brief explanation or response.
5. Once you've completed all pages and fields, submit the form to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Display** (page 1, label: **Product Display**)
	+ This question is meant to gauge your opinion on the general display of products in the store.
	+ Choose "Yes" if the display is satisfactory or "No" otherwise.
* **Display Quality** (page 1, label: **Display Quality**)
	+ This question asks you to rate the quality of product display. Please respond with a number (1-10).
* **Product Visibility** (page 2, label: **Product Visibility**)
	+ In this field, please provide a text response describing where the product was located (e.g., shelf, floor, etc.).
* **Product Relevance** (page 2, label: **Product Relevance**)
	+ This question is about the relevance of the product to your needs or interests. Please respond with a number (1-10).
* **Overall Experience** (page 3, label: **Overall Experience**)
	+ This question is about your overall experience with the product display. Choose "Yes" if it was positive or "No" otherwise.
* **Product Name** (page 3, label: **Product Name**)
	+ This field is for providing the name of the product you evaluated.
* **Customer Feedback** (page 4, label: **Customer Feedback**)
	+ Please provide any additional feedback you have about your experience.
* **Display Position** (page 4, label: **Display Position**)
	+ In this field, please describe where the product was displayed (e.g., near the entrance, on a shelf, etc.).
* **Product Color** (page 5, label: **Product Color**)
	+ This question is about whether the product was displayed with its original color or not. Choose "Yes" if it was displayed correctly or "No" otherwise.
* **Employee Name** (page 6, label: **Employee Name**)
	+ Please provide the name of the employee who attended to your needs.
* **Product Weight** (page 6, label: **Product Weight**)
	+ This question is about the weight of the product. Please respond with a number.
* **Product Size** (page 7, label: **Product Size**)
	+ In this field, please describe the size of the product.
* **Customer Email** (page 7, label: **Customer Email**)
	+ Please provide your email address for follow-up or further communication.
* **Customer Phone** (page 8, label: **Customer Phone**)
	+ This field is for providing your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Customer Location** (page 8, label: **Customer Location**)
	+ In this field, please describe your location (e.g., city, state, country).
* **Product Price** (page 9, label: **Product Price**)
	+ This question is about the price of the product. Please respond with a number.
* **Product Brand** (page 9, label: **Product Brand**)
	+ Please provide the name of the product brand.
* **Customer Age** (page 10, label: **Customer Age**)
	+ This question is about your age. Please respond with a number.
* **Product Weight Unit** (page 10, label: **Product Weight Unit**)
	+ This question is about the unit of measurement for the product weight. Choose "Yes" if it was displayed correctly or "No" otherwise.
* **Product Category** (page 11, label: **Product Category**)
	+ This question is about the product category. Choose "Yes" if it was displayed correctly or "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Loyalty** (page 11, label: **Customer Loyalty**)
	+ This question is about your customer loyalty level. Respond with a number (1-10).
* **Customer Loyalty Status** (page 12, label: **Customer Loyalty Status**)
	+ In this field, choose "Active" if you are a loyal customer or "Inactive" if you are not.
* **Display Orientation** (page 12, label: **Display Orientation**)
	+ This question is about the display orientation. Choose "Yes" if it was satisfactory or "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Gender** (page 13, label: **Customer Gender**)
	+ This question is about your gender. Choose "Yes" if it was accurately displayed or "No" otherwise.
* **Product Description** (page 13, label: **Product Description**)
	+ In this field, please provide a brief description of the product.
