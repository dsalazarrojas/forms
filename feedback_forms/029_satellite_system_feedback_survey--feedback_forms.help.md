# Satellite System Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our satellite system service. It will help us understand what customers like and dislike about our service, and identify areas where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form and fill in the required fields.
2. Rate our service on a scale of 1-5.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
4. Enter your email or phone number so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Satellite System Feedback Form** (`satellite_system_feedback_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Rate the service** (`rating_score`, number, required): Rate our service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback about your experience.
* **Satellite Service** (`satellite_service`, select_one, optional): Choose how you rate the satellite service (Good, Fair, Bad).
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date you visited our service.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time you visited our service.
* **Date and Time of Visit** (`date_of_visit_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your visit.
* **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Rate the service provider** (`rating_service_provider`, number, optional): Rate the service provider on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **Service Type** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_service_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of service you used (Maintenance, Support, Sales, Other, Subscription).
* **Service Provider Name** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_service_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Satellite Service Name** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_satellite_service`, text, optional): Enter the name of the satellite service.
* **Location** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the service.
* **Phone** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Additional Comments** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
* **System Name** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_system_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the system.
* **Service Name** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_service_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the service.
* **Service Address** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_service_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the service.
* **Service Zip** (`satellite_system_feedback_survey_service_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the service.
