# Charity Donor Experience Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Donor Experience Inquiry Form is used to gather feedback from donors about their experience with our organization. This feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and communication with donors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Enter your information in the fields as prompted.
3.  Fill out all required fields, indicated by a asterisk symbol \*.
4.  Once you have filled out all required fields, click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Donor Name \*\* (Optional)
• **donor_name**: Enter your name if you feel comfortable sharing it. If not, you may leave this field blank.

### 2. Email Address \*\* (Optional)
• **donor_email**: Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes.

### 3. Donation Date \*\* (Optional)
• **donation_date**: Enter the date of your most recent donation.

### 4. Satisfaction with Process \*\*
• **satisfaction_with_process**: Rate your satisfaction with the donation process using the provided scale.
    - Very dissatisfied
    - Dissatisfied
    - Neutral
    - Satisfied
    - Very satisfied

### 5. Ease of Donation \*\*
• **ease_of_donation**: Rate how easy the donation process was for you.
    - Very difficult
    - Difficult
    - Neutral
    - Easy
    - Very easy

### 6. Communication Feedback (Optional)
• **communication_feedback**: Enter any feedback you have about the communication you received from us.

### 7. Improvement Suggestions (Optional)
• **improvement_suggestions**: Enter any suggestions you have for improving the donor experience.

### 8. Willingness to Recommend (Required)
• **willingness_to_recommend**: Rate your willingness to recommend our organization to others.
    - Definitely not
    - Probably not
    - Maybe
    - Probably yes
    - Definitely yes

### 9. Follow-up Request (Optional)
• **follow_up_request**: Choose whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up purposes.
    - True
    - False

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when answering questions.
- If you have any issues or concerns with your donation, please contact us at [insert contact information].
- We value your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
- Thank you for taking the time to share your feedback with us!
