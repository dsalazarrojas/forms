# Maternal Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from participants of a maternal workshop, providing an opportunity for you to share your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the workshop's content, organization, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer truthfully.
2. Enter your name and email address (if you feel comfortable sharing this information).
3. Share your feedback and suggestions in the 'Feedback' section.
4. Provide a rating of your overall experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you attended. This will help us understand which workshop you are providing feedback on.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records and future communications.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address (if you feel comfortable sharing this information). This will allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts, opinions, and suggestions about the workshop. Be specific about what you liked, disliked, or found confusing.
* **Rating**: Provide a rating of your overall experience, using the scale provided. This will help us understand the quality of the workshop.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your comments.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them with us.
