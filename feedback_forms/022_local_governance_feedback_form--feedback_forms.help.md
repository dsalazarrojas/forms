# Local Governance Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on local governance from the public, allowing officials and stakeholders to gain valuable insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. If you have any attachments (e.g., photos, documents) that support your feedback, you can upload them using the "attachment" fields.
3. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "email" field, if you want to receive a copy of your feedback submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Title** (`feedback_title`, text, not required): Enter a brief title that summarizes your feedback.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, text, not required): Enter your email address to receive a copy of your feedback submission (optional).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Describe your feedback in the text area. Keep it concise and clear.
* **Attachments** (`attachment`, note, not required): Attach any supporting materials that you think will help us understand your feedback.
* **Attachments 1-8** (`attachment_1` to `attachment_8`, note, not required): Attach up to 8 additional supporting materials.

## Tips

* Please be specific and clear in your feedback, so we can understand your concerns.
* You can attach up to 8 supporting materials, so feel free to share as much as you need.
* If you have any follow-up questions or need clarification, you can contact us at form@gic.mx.
