# Customer Satisfaction Audit Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Customer Satisfaction Audit Checklist is a tool to assess how well the customer interaction was conducted and identify areas for improvement. It's meant to be used after an initial interaction with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the first interaction with the customer.
2. Describe the communication that took place during the interaction.
3. Rate the problem-solving skills of the customer-facing team member.
4. Check whether the customer has sufficient product knowledge.
5. Check whether the resolution met the customer's needs.
6. Rate your satisfaction with the interaction on a scale of 1-10.
7. Provide any additional feedback.
8. Enter the customer's email address.
9. Enter the customer's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Interaction
* **First Interaction** (1, date, required): Enter the date of the first interaction with the customer.
### Communication
* **Communication** (2, text, required): Describe the communication that took place during the interaction. Please try to be as detailed as possible.
### Problem Solving
* **Problem Solving** (3, number, required): Rate the problem-solving skills of the customer-facing team member on a scale of 1-10.
### Product Knowledge
* **Product Knowledge** (4, select_multiple, required): Check whether the customer has sufficient product knowledge. Select all that apply.
	+ Yes
	+ No
### Resolution
* **Resolution** (5, select_one, required): Check whether the resolution met the customer's needs.
	+ Yes
	+ No
### Satisfaction
* **Satisfaction** (6, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the interaction on a scale of 1-10.
### Feedback
* **Feedback** (7, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback about the interaction.
### Email
* **Email** (8, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (9, text, required): Enter the customer's phone number.
