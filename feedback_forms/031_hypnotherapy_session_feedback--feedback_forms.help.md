# Hypnotherapy Session Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients who have undergone hypnotherapy sessions. Your input will help us evaluate the effectiveness of our services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the corresponding session number from the dropdown list (if applicable).
2. Rate your overall experience with the session (Very good, Good, Neutral, Bad, Very bad).
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you might have.
4. Enter your email (if you want to be contacted for follow-up).
5. Enter your name (if you want to be identified in feedback responses).
6. Enter the date and time of your session.
7. If applicable, identify the therapist you worked with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Number** (Session Number): Select the number of the session you are providing feedback about.
* **Rating** (Rating): Indicate how you felt about the session using the scale (Very good, Good, Neutral, Bad, Very bad).
* **Comments** (Comments): Add any thoughts, suggestions, or observations you'd like to share about the session.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email if you want us to contact you for follow-up or to provide additional information.
* **Name** (Name): Enter your name (if you want to be identified in feedback responses).
* **Session Date** (Session Date): Enter the date of the session you are providing feedback about.
* **Session Time** (Session Time): Enter the time of the session you are providing feedback about.
* **Therapist Name** (Therapist Name): If applicable, enter the name of the therapist you worked with.
* **Feedback** (Feedback): Add any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the session.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, especially when it comes to the rating and comments sections.
* If you have suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the comments section.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to contact us for follow-up.
