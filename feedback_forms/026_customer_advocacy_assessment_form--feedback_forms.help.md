# Customer Advocacy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on their experience with our company. It is intended for customers who have interacted with us in the past or have a reason to contact us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of the company you are representing.
2. Enter your Full Name as it appears on your business card or introduction.
3. Provide your business email address to help us reach back to you.
4. Enter your business phone number for contact purposes.
5. Choose your Satisfaction Level based on your experience with our company.
6. Select your Loyalty Level based on how loyal you feel towards our company.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our company.
8. Choose your Overall Satisfaction level based on your experience with our company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the company you are representing. For example, "ABC Corporation".
* **Full Name** (`name`, Text, Optional): Enter your Full Name as it appears on your business card or introduction.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your business email address to help us reach back to you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your business phone number for contact purposes.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, Select One, Optional): Choose the level that best describes your satisfaction with our company.
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Satisfied
* **Loyalty Level** (`loyalty_level`, Select One, Optional): Choose the level that best describes your loyalty to our company.
	+ Very Low
	+ Somewhat Low
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat High
	+ Very High
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our company.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Note, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to share with us.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Select One, Optional): Choose the level that best describes your overall satisfaction with our company.
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Slightly Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Extremely Satisfied
