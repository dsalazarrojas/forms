# Off Road Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your recent off-road experience. It is essential to provide honest and detailed feedback about your trip, including any suggestions for improvement and comments. Your input will help us improve our service and provide a better experience for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required information on each page.
2. If you have any additional comments, use the "comments" field.
3. Make sure to sign the form with your signature at the end.
4. Date and time of your signature are also required.
5. Review and agree to the terms of use, which can be found below the signature fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Experience Date** (`experience_date`, Date, Required): Please select the date of your off-road experience.
* **Vehicle Type** (`off_road_vehicle_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of vehicle you used for the experience (Aek, Car, Bike, Quad, Horse, or Other).
* **Trail Condition** (`trail_condition`, Select One, Required): Select the difficulty level of the trail (Difficult, Easy, or Medium).
* **Trail Length** (`trail_length`, Number, Required): Please enter the total length of the trail in miles or kilometers.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, Select Multiple, Required): Rate your overall experience as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, Text, Optional): Share any suggestions or ideas for improving our service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Location** (`location`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the location of your off-road experience (Mountain, Desert, Forest, or Other).
* **Guide Name** (`guide_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your guide.
* **Guide Rating** (`guide_rating`, Number, Required): Rate your guide's performance as 1, 2, 3, or 4.
* **Guide Experience** (`guide_experience`, Select One, Required): Rate your guide's overall experience as Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad.
* **Guide Skills** (`guide_skills`, Select Multiple, Required): Rate your guide's skills as Excellent, Good, Average, or Bad.
* **Safety Guidance** (`safety_guidance`, Select Multiple, Required): Rate your guide's safety guidance as Excellent, Good, Average, or Bad.
* **Goals** (`goals`, Text, Optional): Enter your goals for the experience.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, Text, Optional): Enter any follow-up information or questions.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, Select Multiple, Required): Select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Other).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Optional): Add any additional feedback or comments.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, Text, Required): Sign your name to confirm your submission.
* **Guide Signature** (`guide_signature`, Text, Optional): Enter your guide's signature if applicable.
* **Signature Timestamp** (`signature_timestamp`, Time, Required): Date and time of your signature.
* **Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, Select Multiple, Required): Review and agree to the terms of use.
