# Faculty Live Demo Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on a live demo presentation given by a faculty member. The purpose of this form is to collect ratings and comments on the presenter's performance, suggest areas for improvement, and collect contact information for follow-up communication. Please note that your feedback is crucial in helping us understand what works well and what doesn't, so please take a few minutes to fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate the presenter's performance by selecting one of the options provided for each question.
2. Provide your thoughts and comments on the presentation by typing in the relevant text fields.
3. Share any suggestions for improvement you may have.
4. If you have any contact information, please provide it so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What did you rate the presenter's performance? (`presenter_rating`, select_one, required)
* This question helps us understand how you perceive the presenter's performance. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 2. What were your thoughts on the presentation? (`feedback`, text, required)
* Please share your thoughts on the presentation, including any positive or negative comments you may have.

### 3. How effective was the presenter? (`presenter_effectiveness`, select_one, required)
* This question helps us understand how well the presenter delivered the presentation. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 4. Do you have any suggestions for improvement? (`suggestions`, text, required)
* Please share any suggestions you may have for improving future presentations.

### 5. Comments (`comments`, text, required)
* This is an opportunity to share any additional comments or feedback you may have.

### 6. Your email (`email`, email, required)
* Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you.

### 7. Phone number (`phone_number`, text, required)
* This is an optional field for you to share your phone number if you'd like.

### 8. Comments to email (`comments_email`, text, optional)
* If you have any comments or feedback, please type it in here.

### 9. Phone number (`phone`, text, optional)
* Similar to the previous field, please share your phone number if you'd like.

### 10. Comments (`comments_2`, text, optional)
* Another field for sharing additional comments or feedback.

### 11. Suggestion for improvement (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional)
* This is another field to share any suggestions you may have.

### 12-13. Comments (`comments_phone_2`, text, optional)
* Two more fields for sharing comments or feedback from phone or other contact methods.

### 14-15. Email and phone (`email_2`, `phone`, optional)
* Optional fields for sharing email and phone number again.

Tips:
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Your input is crucial in helping us improve our live demos.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
