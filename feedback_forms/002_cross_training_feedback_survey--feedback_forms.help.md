# Cross Training Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Training Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience with our training programs. This feedback is essential for us to understand the effectiveness of our training and identify areas for improvement. Your input will help us provide better training experiences for our participants in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Email in the respective fields.
2. Select the Training Program you participated in from the dropdown menu.
3. Share your Feedback on your experience.
4. Provide Suggestions for improvements.
5. Rate your experience on the Rating scale.
6. Add any Comments you'd like to share.
7. Confirm your Email for verification purposes.
8. If you have a Participant Phone Number, please provide it.
9. Enter the Trainer's Name who conducted the training.
10. Add any Additional Comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Training Program** (select_one): Choose the training program you participated in from the dropdown menu.
* **Feedback** (text): Share your thoughts on your experience with the training.
* **Suggestions** (text): Provide ideas for improvements for future trainings.
* **Rating** (number): Rate your experience on the scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (note): Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
* **Email Confirmation** (email): Confirm your email address for verification.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Participant Name** (text): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (email): Enter your email address as the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (text): Enter your phone number as the participant.
* **Trainer Name** (text): Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the training.
* **Training Location** (select_one): Choose the location of the training from the dropdown menu.
* **Attended Training** (select_one): Indicate whether you attended the training.
* **Expected Outcome** (select_multiple): Select the expected outcomes from the dropdown menu.
* **Actual Outcome** (select_multiple): Select the actual outcomes from the dropdown menu.
* **Rating Score** (number): Enter a rating score out of 5.
* **Rating Scale** (select_one): Choose the rating scale from the dropdown menu.
* **Training Hours** (number): Enter the number of training hours.
* **Training Length** (select_one): Choose the length of the training from the dropdown menu.
* **Session Hours** (number): Enter the number of session hours.
* **Session Length** (select_one): Choose the length of the session from the dropdown menu.
* **Additional Comments** (text): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Comments** (note): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Actual Outcome** (select_multiple): Select the actual outcomes from the dropdown menu.
* **Trainer Name** (text): Enter the name of the trainer.
* **Comments** (note): Add any additional comments or feedback.

Tips:

* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve our training programs.
