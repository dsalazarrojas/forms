# Waste Disposal Contractor Review Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from facilities on their experience with waste disposal contractors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you are not submitting duplicate reviews for the same contractor.
2. Review each section carefully and fill in the required information.
3. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
4. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name**: Please enter the name of the facility where you received waste disposal services.
* **Contractor Name**: Please enter the name of the waste disposal contractor.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date when you reviewed the contractor.
* **Service Quality**: Please select the level of service quality you experienced with the contractor (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Communication**: Please select the level of communication you experienced with the contractor (Responsive, Timely, Unresponsive, Uncommunicative).
* **Reliability**: Please enter a numerical value that represents the reliability of the contractor (e.g. 1-5).
* **Price**: Please enter the price you paid for the service.
* **Contract Term**: Please select the contract term (Month, Quarter, Half Year, Year).
* **Contract Start Date**: Enter the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date**: Enter the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Notes**: Please enter any notes about the contract.
* **Facility Contact Name**: Please enter the name of the facility contact.
* **Facility Contact Phone**: Please enter the facility contact's phone number.
* **Facility Contact Email**: Please enter the facility contact's email address.
* **Notes**: Please enter any notes about your experience.
* **Review Comments**: Please enter any comments about your experience.
* **Overall Rating**: Please enter a numerical value that represents your overall rating of the contractor (e.g. 1-5).
* **Would Recommend**: Please select if you would recommend the contractor (True or False).
* **Review Date 2**: Enter the date when you reviewed the contractor again.
* **Service Notes**: Please enter any notes about the service.
* **Review Comments 2**: Please enter any comments about your experience.
* **Review Date 3**: Enter the date when you reviewed the contractor again.
* **Contract Term 2**: Please select the contract term (Month, Quarter, Half Year, Year).
* **Price 2**: Please enter the price you paid for the service again.
* **Notes 2**: Please enter any notes about your experience.
