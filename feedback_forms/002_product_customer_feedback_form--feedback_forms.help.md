# Product Customer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on our products. It's a quick and easy way to let us know about your experience with our products. Your input will help us improve our products and services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply:
1. Answer the questions on each page to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that apply to you.
3. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Customer Feedback Form** (`page_1`, text, optional): This is the form title, please ignore.
* **Client Information** (`page_2`, text, optional): Please enter your name, company, or any relevant contact information to help us identify your feedback.
* **Product Selection** (`page_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the products you have interacted with.
* **Feedback** (`page_4`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your experience with our products.
* **Rating (1-5)** (`page_5`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with our products.
* **Product Selection** (`page_6`, select_one, optional): Select the specific product you are providing feedback on.
* **Additional Comments** (`page_7`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions about your experience with our products.
* **Submit** (`page_8`, text, optional): Click to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us directly.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
