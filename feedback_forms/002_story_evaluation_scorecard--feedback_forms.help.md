# Story Evaluation Scorecard - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Story Evaluation Scorecard form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from evaluators on a story. This form is used to provide a comprehensive view of the story's strengths and weaknesses, allowing evaluators to provide a detailed assessment and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Story Evaluation Scorecard form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which include:
	* Story ID
	* Rating (on a scale from 1 to 5)
2. Provide feedback and comments on the story as needed.
3. Optional fields, such as Judge ID and scorecard comments, are available for additional context and information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Story ID** (`story_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the story being evaluated.
* **Rating** (`story_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the story on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback_comment`, text, optional): Provide any feedback or comments on the story.
* **Scorecard Rating 1** (`scorecard_rating`, text, optional): Enter your first scorecard rating for the story.
* **Scorecard Comments** (`scorecard_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback on the story's scorecard rating.
* **Scorecard Rating 2** (`scorecard_rating_2`, text, optional): Enter your second scorecard rating for the story.
* **Scorecard Rating 3** (`scorecard_rating_3`, text, optional): Enter your third scorecard rating for the story.
* **Scorecard Rating 4** (`scorecard_rating_4`, text, optional): Enter your fourth scorecard rating for the story.
* **Scorecard Rating 5** (`scorecard_rating_5`, text, optional): Enter your fifth scorecard rating for the story.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter the overall score for the story.
* **Score out of 5** (`score_out_of`, number, optional): Enter the maximum score out of 5 for the story.
* **Judge ID** (`judge_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the judge providing the scorecard.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional): Display the date and time the scorecard was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, date, optional): Display the last updated date and time of the scorecard.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, date, optional): Display the date and time the scorecard was deleted.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback on the scorecard.
* **Scorecard Comments** (`scorecard_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the scorecard.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as this will help provide a comprehensive view of the story's strengths and weaknesses. If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please feel free to ask.
