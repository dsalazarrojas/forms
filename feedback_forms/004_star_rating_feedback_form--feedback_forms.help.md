# Star Rating Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Star Rating Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on a specific issue or product. This form allows users to rate their experience on a scale of 1 to 5 stars and provide additional comments if needed. It also captures their email and phone number if they wish to be contacted for further discussion. The form is optional and can be filled out at any time.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Star Rating**: Select a star rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the product or service. The ratings are:
	* 1 Star: Very dissatisfied
	* 2 Stars: Somewhat dissatisfied
	* 3 Stars: Neutral
	* 4 Stars: Satisfied
	* 5 Stars: Very satisfied
2. **Comment (Optional)**: If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in this text area.
3. **Email (Optional)**: If you would like to be contacted by us for further discussion, please provide your email address.
4. **Phone Number (Optional)**: If you would like to be contacted by us for further discussion, please provide your phone number.
5. **Rated On**: Select the date when you rated the product or service (this will default to the current date if left blank).
6. **Your Name**: Please provide your name for our records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Star Rating (star_rating)** (Select One, Required: False): Select a star rating from 1 to 5 to indicate your level of satisfaction with the product or service.
* **Comment (rating_comment)** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email (email)** (Email, Optional): If you would like to be contacted by us for further discussion, provide your email address.
* **Phone Number (phone)** (Text, Optional): If you would like to be contacted by us for further discussion, please provide your phone number.
* **Rated On (rating_date)** (Date, Optional): Select the date when you rated the product or service. If left blank, it will default to the current date.
* **Your Name (name)** (Text, Optional): Please provide your name for our records.
