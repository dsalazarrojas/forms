# Product Feedback Group Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register and collect information from teams and team members about their feedback and progress on projects. It is used to gather valuable insights and feedback from teams and team members, enabling us to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the First Name field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the Email field.
3. Choose your **Group Name**.
4. Select your **Department**.
5. Enter your **Phone** number.
6. Provide your **Team Name**, **Team Size**, and **Project Duration**.
7. Choose your **Role in Group** and **Feedback Type**.
8. Select your **Team Status** and **Project Status**.
9. Enter **Team Description** and **Submit Time**.
10. Select your **Submitter's Email** and **Submitter's Location**.
11. Choose how you would like to **Submit** this form. You can submit now or save and continue later.
12. Set the **Submit Date** for your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Group Name**: Enter the name of your team or group.
* **Department**: Select your department from the options provided (Development, Sales, Support).
* **Team Leader**: Enter the name of your team leader.
* **Role**: Enter your role or position in the team.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Location**: Enter your location.
* **Role in Group**: Select your role in the group (Team Lead, Member, Other).
* **Feedback Group**: Select the type of feedback you are providing (Product, Customer Success, Other).
* **Submitter**: Select the person submitting this form (Team Lead, Member, Other).
* **Role in Group in Project**: Select your role in the group for the project (Team Lead, Member, Other).
* **Role in Project**: Select whether you are involved in the project (Yes, No).
* **Team Size**: Enter the size of your team.
* **Project Duration**: Enter the duration of the project.
* **Project Status**: Select the status of the project (Active, Inactive, Completed).
* **Team Status**: Select the status of your team (Active, Inactive, Completed).
* **Feedback Type**: Select the type of feedback you are providing (Product, Service, Other).
* **Team Description**: Provide a brief description of your team.
* **Submit Date**: Set the date you would like to submit this form.
* **Submit Time**: Set the time you would like to submit this form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Location**: Enter the location of the person submitting this form.
* **Submit**: Choose how you would like to submit this form (Submit Now, Save and Continue).
