# New Client Testimonial Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Client Testimonial Form is designed to collect feedback and testimonials from clients after a project or service has been completed. This form will help us gauge the quality of our services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the New Client Testimonial Form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Share your testimonial about our services in the "Testimonial" field. Please be as specific and detailed as possible.
3. If you would like to share the name of the project you worked on, please enter it in the "Project Name" field.
4. If you have a website or URL related to the project, you can provide it in the "Project URL" field.
5. If you have a email address where we can contact you, please enter it in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (id: 2, label: Client Name, type: text, required: false)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Testimonial (id: 3, label: Testimonial, type: text, required: false)**: Share your testimonial about our services. Be specific and detailed about your experience.
* **Project Name (id: 4, label: Project Name, type: text, required: false)**: If you would like to share the name of the project you worked on, please enter it here.
* **Project URL (id: 5, label: Project URL, type: text, required: false)**: If you have a website or URL related to the project, you can provide it here.
* **Email (id: 6, label: Email, type: email, required: false)**: Please enter your email address where we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields (although most fields are optional, some information is crucial to us).
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when sharing your testimonial.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents related to the project, you can share them separately with us.
* We appreciate your feedback and are looking forward to hearing about your experience with our services.
