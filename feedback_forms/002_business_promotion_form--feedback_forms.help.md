# Business Promotion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather customer feedback and preferences for business promotions, aiming to improve customer engagement and inform marketing strategies.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, click on each field to select or input your information.
2. Fill in all required fields marked with a red star.
3. For select fields, choose one or multiple options as applicable.
4. Provide a detailed description for the "What would you like to purchase today?" field if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`customer_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name to help us better understand who you are.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Enter your contact email for us to reach out to you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **required**): Provide your mobile or landline number for us to contact you.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, **optional**): If you'd like to provide your residential address, please enter it here.
* **City** (`city`, text, **optional**): Enter your city for geographical purposes.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, **optional**): Enter your postal code for geographical purposes.
* **How did you hear about our promotion?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, **required**): Select how you heard about our promotion from the options provided.
* **How often do you visit our store?** (`store_visit_frequency`, select_one, **required**): Choose how often you visit our store.
* **What products interest you most?** (`product_interests`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all products categories that interest you most.
* **What would you like to purchase today?** (`wish_to_purchase`, text, **optional**): Describe the products or categories you're interested in purchasing.
* **Which promotions interest you most?** (`promotion_interest`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all promotions that interest you most.
* **What discount range would encourage you to shop?** (`preferred_discount_range`, select_one, **optional**): Choose a discount range that would motivate you to shop.
* **Would you like to join our loyalty program?** (`loyalty_program_interest`, select_one, **required**): Let us know if you're interested in joining our loyalty program.
* **Do you prefer shopping online or in-store?** (`online_shopping_preference`, select_one, **optional**): Choose your preferred shopping method.
* **How would you like to receive promotions?** (`communication_preferences`, select_multiple, **required**): Select your preferred method of receiving promotions.
* **Rate your overall shopping experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, **required**): Rate your shopping experience from excellent to poor.
* **How can we improve your shopping experience?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, **optional**): Share any suggestions on how we can improve your shopping experience.

## Tips

* Be honest and detailed in your responses to help us better understand your preferences.
* Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
