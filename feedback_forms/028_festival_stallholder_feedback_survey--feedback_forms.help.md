# Festival Stallholder Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Stallholder Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from festival vendors about their experience with our festival event. This feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and provide valuable insights to enhance future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall experience by selecting a number value in the "How would you rate your overall experience" field.
2. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional comments" field.
3. Select your preference for participating in our next event by choosing one of the options in the "Would you like to participate in our next event" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
6. Enter the vendor's name in the "Vendors name" field.
7. Enter the vendor's contact information in the "Vendors contact" field (optional).
8. Select the date of the event you attended in the "Event date" field.
9. Select the time of the event you attended in the "Event time" field.
10. Choose the duration of the event in the "Event duration" field.
11. Report your experience with the vendor's setup and take down in the "Vendors setup and take down" field.
12. Rate your experience with communication in the "Communication" field.
13. If you need help with any of the following, select all that apply in the "Do you need help with any of the following" field (optional).
14. If you have any other comments or feedback, please type it in the "Other" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate your overall experience** (`vendor_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall experience by selecting a number value. This will help us understand how we can improve our festival experience for you.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: False): If you have any additional comments or feedback about your experience, please type them in this field.
* **Would you like to participate in our next event** (`future_participation`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you would like to participate in our next event by selecting one of the options.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates about our event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you if needed.
* **Vendors name** (`vendor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the vendor who participated in the event.
* **Vendors contact** (`vendor_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have a contact person for the vendor, please enter their name and information.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event you attended.
* **Event time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the event you attended.
* **Event duration** (`event_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the duration of the event you attended.
* **Vendors setup and take down** (`vendor_setup`, `select_one`, required): Report your experience with the vendor's setup and take down.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with communication from the vendor.
* **Do you need help with any of the following** (`help`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you need help with any of the following, select all that apply.
* **Other** (`other_help`, `text`, optional): If you have any other comments or feedback, please type it in this field.
