# user_training_materials_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with the training materials and the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability and provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session** (`user_training_session`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of the training session you attended.
* **Feedback On Materials** (`feedback_on_materials`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your feedback on the materials used during the training session. 
* **Trainer Performance** (`trainer_performance`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select how you would rate the trainer's performance: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Supportiveness** (`trainer_supportiveness`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select how often you felt supported during the training session: Always, Mostly, Rarely, Never.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Please provide the date of the training session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Please provide the time of the training session.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Please provide any additional notes or comments you have about the training session.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the training session.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any additional feedback you have about the training session.
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the level of training you received.
* **Materials Used** (`materials_used`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the materials used during the training session.
* **Outcome** (`training_outcome`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the outcome of the training session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the length of the training session: 2 hours, 4 hours, 6 hours.
* **Session Fees** (`session_fees`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any fees associated with the training session.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the name of the trainer.
* **Trainee Attendance** (`trainee_attendance`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the number of trainees in attendance.

Note: The fields marked as `required/optional` are only required if the user has attended the training session.
