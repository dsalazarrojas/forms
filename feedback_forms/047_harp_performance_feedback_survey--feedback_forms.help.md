# harp-performance-feedback-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The harp-performance-feedback-survey is a tool used to collect feedback from the audience about the harpist's performance. This feedback will help us improve future performances and better meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and answer the questions accordingly. You can select one or multiple options for some questions, depending on the type of question. If you're unsure about a question, feel free to provide as much detail as you need in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro** (`intro_page`, text, optional): This is a brief introduction page where you can provide any additional information or context about your experience.
* **Was the harp performance enjoyable?** (`performance`, select_one, optional): Please select how enjoyable you found the harp performance. You can choose from the options: Very Good, Good, Neutral, Bad, or Very Bad.
* **How would you rate the harpist's skill level?** (`skill_level`, select_one, optional): Please rate the harpist's skill level on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **What could be improved about this performance?** (`suggest_for_improvement`, text, optional): If there's anything you'd like to see improved, please describe it in the text box.
* **Was there a favorite song?** (`favorite_song`, select_multiple, optional): If there was a song that you particularly enjoyed, please select the option 'Yes' and we'll take note.
* **If yes, what was it?** (`favorite_song_name`, text, optional): If you selected a favorite song, please provide its name in the text box.
* **How would you rate the event organizer's performance?** (`event_organizer`, select_one, optional): Please rate the event organizer's performance on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **How would you rate the venue?** (`venue`, select_one, optional): Please rate the venue on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **How did you like the music venue?** (`music_venue`, select_one, optional): Please rate how much you liked the music venue on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Would you recommend this harpist to a friend?** (`recommend_harpist`, select_one, optional): Please rate how likely you are to recommend this harpist to a friend on a scale of 1 to 10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.

## Tips
* Be honest about your experience, and don't hesitate to provide any additional information that you think will help us improve future performances.
* If you're unsure about a question, use the text box to provide more details.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide feedback! Your input is invaluable to us.
