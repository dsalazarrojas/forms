# Cleaning Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a cleaning service. The information gathered from this form will help the cleaning service company to improve its services and provide better experiences for its customers.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability. If you have any issues or concerns, please provide specific details or examples. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Customer Name 
* **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### 2. Email Address 
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you used to contact the cleaning service company. This will help us follow up with you if needed.

### 3. Date of Service 
* **Date of Service** (`Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the cleaning service was provided.

### 4. Property Address 
* **Property Address** (`Property Address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the service was provided.

### 5. Type of Cleaning Service 
* **Type of Cleaning Service** (`Type of Cleaning Service`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you received (e.g. Regular Cleaning, Deep Cleaning, Move-In/Move-Out, Window Cleaning, Carpet Cleaning, Post-Construction, Other).

### 6. Cleaning Crew Leader Name 
* **Cleaning Crew Leader Name** (`Crew Leader Name`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the supervisor or crew leader who provided the service.

### 7. Overall Satisfaction with Service 
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service. Options are: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.

### 8. Overall Satisfaction Score 
* **Overall Satisfaction Score** (`Satisfaction Score`, `number`, required): Rate the overall satisfaction from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).

### 9. Quality of Cleanliness Achieved 
* **Quality of Cleanliness** (`Quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of cleanliness. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 10. Attention to Detail 
* **Attention to Detail** (`Attention to Detail`, `select_one`, required): Rate the attention to detail. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 11. Thoroughness of Work 
* **Thoroughness of Work** (`Thoroughness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the thoroughness of work. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 12. Staff Professionalism 
* **Staff Professionalism** (`Staff Professionalism`, `select_one`, required): Rate the professionalism of the staff. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 13. Staff Courtesy and Respect 
* **Staff Courtesy and Respect** (`Staff Courtesy`, `select_one`, required): Rate the staff courtesy. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 14. Punctuality and Timeliness 
* **Punctuality** (`Punctuality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the punctuality. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 15. Communication and Updates 
* **Communication** (`Communication`, `select_one`, required): Rate the communication. Options are: Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 16. Value for Money 
* **Value for Money** (`Value for Money`, `select_one`, required): Rate the value for money. Options are: Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value, Very Poor Value.

### 17. Did Service Meet Price Expectations? 
* **Service Value** (`Price Expectations`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether the service met your price expectations. Options are: Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Below Expectations.

### 18. Were There Any Issues or Concerns? 
* **Were There Issues** (`Were There Any Issues`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether there were any issues or concerns. Options are: No Issues, Minor Issues, Major Issues.

### 19. Please Describe Issues 
* **Issues Description** (`Issues Description`, `text`, required): If you selected "Yes, Minor" or "Yes, Major" in the previous field, please describe the issues or concerns you experienced.

### 20. What Did We Do Well? 
* **Strengths** (`What Did We Do Well`, `text`, optional): If you are satisfied with the service, please mention what we did well.

### 21. What Could Be Improved? 
* **Improvements** (`What Could Be Improved`, `text`, optional): If you are not satisfied, please provide suggestions for improvement.

### 22. Would You Recommend Us to Others? 
* **Would Recommend** (`Would You Recommend`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether you would recommend the service. Options are: Yes, Definitely, Probably Yes, Not Sure, Probably Not, False.

### 23. Will You Book Our Service Again? 
* **Will Book Again** (`Future Service`, `select_one`, required): Rate whether you would book our service again. Options are: Yes, Definitely, Probably, Not Sure, Probably Not, False.

### 24. Additional Comments 
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any other feedback, please provide it here.
