# Pet Sitter Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from pet owners after a pet sitting session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the visit date that the pet sitting session took place.
2. Enter the name of the pet that was visited.
3. Choose the type of visit that occurred (Home Visit, In-Home Visit, or At-Home Visit).
4. Enter the name of the pet sitter who visited the pet.
5. Rate the quality of service provided by the sitter (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. Provide any additional feedback or comments about the visit.
7. Check if you would like to be followed up with about any further visits.
8. Select the quality of service provided by the sitter (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
9. Enter any additional comments about the visit.
10. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Date**: The date of the visit.
* **Pet Name**: The name of the pet that was visited.
* **Visit Type**: The type of visit (Home Visit, In-Home Visit, or At-Home Visit).
* **Sitter Name**: The name of the pet sitter who visited the pet.
* **Sitter Rating**: A rating of the quality of service provided by the sitter (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Feedback**: Any comments or concerns about the visit.
* **Follow Up Request**: A checkbox to request follow up with the pet owner about future visits.
* **Service Quality**: A rating of the quality of service provided by the sitter (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or notes about the visit.
* **Signature**: A blank space for the pet owner to sign and date the form.
* **Pet Owner Signature**: A blank space for the pet owner to sign the form.
* **Sitter Signature**: A blank space for the pet sitter to sign the form.
* **Date Modified**: The date and time the form was last modified.
