# Wire Management Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on the wire management system used in the facility. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the most accurate and relevant information.
3. If you are unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Feedback** (`facility_feedback`, text, optional): Please provide your overall feedback about the wire management system used in the facility. This could include suggestions for improvement, observations about how the system works, or any issues you have encountered.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Describe the issue you have encountered with the wire management system. Be as detailed as possible so we can fully understand the problem.
* **Problem Area** (`problem_area`, select_one, optional): Identify where you experienced the problem (e.g., "Production", "Receiving", "Shipping", etc.).
* **Recurring Issues** (`recurring_issues`, select_multiple, optional): If the issue you described above is a recurring one, please select all the relevant issues that you have encountered. You can choose multiple options if applicable.
* **Process Improvement** (`process_improvement`, text, optional): Share your ideas for improving the wire management process. This could include suggestions for changes to the system, new features, or anything else that could help us improve the process.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations you have about the wire management system. This is a good place to ask questions or provide further clarification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please provide an email address where we can contact you with follow-up questions or for more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Facility Feedback 2** (`facility_feedback_2`, text, optional): This is another field for you to provide additional feedback about the wire management system. 

## Tips
* Please note that all fields are optional, and you can choose not to fill out any of them if you don't have any feedback or suggestions.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* We appreciate your input and any suggestions you can provide to help us improve the wire management system.
