# Social Media Engagement Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Social Media Engagement Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback about your social media habits and preferences. This will help us better understand how to improve our social media engagement and provide more relevant content to our audience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **What is your favorite social media platform**: Please select your favorite social media platform from the options provided.
2. **How often do you post on social media**: Choose how often you post on social media, if at all. 
3. **Posting frequency**: If you select "Several times a week" or "Every day" in the previous question, please specify your preferred posting frequency.
4. **Time of posting**: If you have a preferred time of posting, please select it from the provided options.
5. **What do you like most about our social media interaction with friends**: Tell us in the open-ended text field.
6. **What do you like most about our social media interaction with our brand (if applicable)**: If you have interacted with our brand on social media, tell us what you like most about it.
7. **The type of educational content you would like to see on our social media**: Provide your preferences in the open-ended text field.
8. **The type of entertainment content you would like to see on our social media**: Provide your preferences in the open-ended text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your favorite social media platform** (`id: 1`, `type: select_one`): Select your favorite social media platform, such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, or another.
* **How often do you post on social media** (`id: 2`, `type: text`): Enter the name of your preferred social media platform, e.g., "Facebook", "Twitter", etc.
* **How often do you post on social media** (`id: 3`, `type: select_one`): Select how often you post on social media, such as "Facebook", "Twitter", "Instagram", "TikTok", or "Pinterest".
* **Posting frequency** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`): If you post on social media several times a week or every day, specify your preferred posting frequency.
* **Time of posting** (`id: 5`, `type: time`): If applicable, select your preferred time of posting.
* **What do you like most about our social media interaction with friends** (`id: 7`, `type: text`): Tell us in an open-ended text field about what you like most about our interactions with your friends on social media.
* **What do you like most about our social media interaction with our brand** (`id: 8`, `type: text`): If applicable, tell us what you like most about our social media interaction with our brand.
* **The type of educational content you would like to see on our social media** (`id: 9`, `type: text`): Provide your preferences in an open-ended text field.
* **The type of entertainment content you would like to see on our social media** (`id: 10`, `type: text`): Provide your preferences in an open-ended text field.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when answering the open-ended questions.
- If you have any trouble with the form or have questions, feel free to contact us for help.
- Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and it will help us improve our social media presence.
