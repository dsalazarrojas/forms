# Simulation Test Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Simulation Test Survey, designed to collect feedback from participants after a simulation test. It is used to gather information on their overall experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Overall Rating" question, which asks you to rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
2. Select all the options that apply for "How Would Rate This Simulation", indicating which aspects of the simulation improved or didn't improve your experience.
3. If you'd like to elaborate on what improved or didn't improve, use the corresponding text fields.
4. In the "Additional Feedback" section, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: true)
Overall rating of your experience, on a scale of 1-5.

### 2. **How Would Rate This Simulation** (`rating_scale`, `select_multiple`, required: true)
Select all options that apply to indicate which aspects of the simulation improved or didn't improve your experience.

### 3. **What Improved** (`what_improved`, `text`, required: false)
Explain what aspects of the simulation improved your experience. This is an optional field, but if you'd like to elaborate, please use this space.

### 4. **What Didn't Improve** (`what_didnt_improve`, `text`, required: false)
Explain what aspects of the simulation didn't improve your experience. This is an optional field, but if you'd like to provide feedback, please use this space.

### 5. **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false)
Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the simulation test. This is an optional field, and you are free to share as much or as little information as you'd like.
