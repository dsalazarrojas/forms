# training_program_evaluation_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and evaluation of our training programs. Please provide your honest and detailed answers to help us improve our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the questions below and ensure you understand the purpose of each one.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. For questions with multiple options, choose the best response from the available choices.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please include them in the "Participant Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name**: <program_name> (<type: text>): Please enter the name of the training program you participated in.
* **Program Rating**: <program_rating> (<type: number>): Rate the program on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Evaluation Scale**: <evaluation_scale> (<type: select_one>): Choose how satisfied you were with the program:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Program Improvement**: <program_improvement> (<type: text>): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please enter them here.
* **Participant Feedback**: <participant_feedback> (<type: select_multiple>): Please choose all the feedback options that apply to you:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
	+ Highly Satisfied
	+ Highly Dissatisfied
* **Program Satisfaction**: <program_satisfaction> (<type: time>): How satisfied are you with the program, with 100% being the highest? 
* <program_satisfaction> is for program satisfaction on a scale from 0-100.
* **Participant Expectation**: <participant_expectation> (<type: note>): Please provide any additional comments or questions you may have about the program.
* **Evaluation Period**: <evaluation_period> (<type: date>): When was the program you evaluated?
