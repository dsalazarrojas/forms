# P2P Crypto Trading Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users regarding their experience with peer-to-peer (P2P) crypto trading. It will help us understand areas of improvement and ensure that our platform meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, click on the "Submit Feedback" button to open the form.
2. Read through each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
3. For each field, provide a thoughtful and concise response.
4. If you have any additional comments, use the "Additional Comments" field at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`title`, `text`, optional): A brief introduction or statement about your experience with P2P crypto trading on our platform.
* **Overall Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall experience with P2P crypto trading on our platform. Please select a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Describe your feedback on your experience with P2P crypto trading on our platform.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Any additional comments about your experience with P2P crypto trading on our platform.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the category that best describes your feedback, such as "Crypto Exchange" or "P2P Trading Platform".
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your overall rating.
* **Support** (`support`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate the support you received from our platform, such as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Security** (`security`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate the security of our platform, such as "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
* **Comments on Support** (`comments_support`, `text`, optional): Any additional comments about the support you received.
* **Comments on Security** (`comments_security`, `text`, optional): Any additional comments about the security of our platform.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about P2P crypto trading on our platform.
