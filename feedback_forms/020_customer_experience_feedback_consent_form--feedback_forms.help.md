# Customer Experience Feedback Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our services. Please complete this form to help us understand your satisfaction and any areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the type of service you received.
3. Indicate the frequency of service you received.
4. Rate your satisfaction with the service(s) you received.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your consent to participate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Experience Feedback Consent Form** (title): This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Service Type** (service_type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of service you received.
	+ Options: Service 1, Service 2, Service 3
* **Service Frequency** (service_frequency, number, required: false): Indicate how often you received the service.
* **Service Satisfaction** (service_satisfaction, select_multiple, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the service(s) you received.
	+ Options: Very Good, Good, Fair
* **Comments** (comments, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Customer Signature** (signature, note, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent to participate.
* **Date of Service** (date_of_service, date, required: false): Enter the date you received the service.
* **Time of Service** (time_of_service, time, required: false): Enter the time you received the service.
* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required: false): Enter your name.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- If you have any concerns or questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this process.
