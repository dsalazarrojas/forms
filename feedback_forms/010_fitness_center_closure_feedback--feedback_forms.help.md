# Fitness Center Closure Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about the impact of a fitness center closure on their business. The goal is to gather information about how the closure affected your visit or plans and to help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select one of the options for "Closure Impact" that best describes how the closure affected your business.
2.  Choose all the options that best describe the effectiveness of our communication about the closure for you.
3.  Indicate if you have a strong or somewhat intent to return to our fitness center.
4.  If you selected "Closure Impact" as having a significant effect, you can optionally provide the date of the closure in MM/DD/YY format.
5.  If you would like to provide more information about the impact of the closure, you can do so in the "Closure Reason" field.
6.  In the "Member Insight" field, you can provide any additional insight you have about your visit or experience.
7.  Finally, if you would like to share any feedback about your experience, you can do so in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*  **Closure Impact** (Closure Impact):
	+ Select one of the options:
		- Closure has minimal effect on my business
		- Closure has moderate effect on my business
		- Closure has significant effect on my business
*  **Communication Quality** (Communication Quality):
	+ Select all the options that best describe how well we communicated the closure with you:
		- Effective
		- Ineffective
		- Somewhat Effective
		- Not Effective
*  **Intent to Return** (Intent to Return):
	+ Select one of the options:
		- Strongly Intent to Return
		- Somewhat Intent to Return
		- Not Intend to Return
*  **Closure Date** (Closure Date):
	+ Optional: enter the date of the closure in MM/DD/YY format.
*  **Closure Duration** (Closure Duration):
	+ Optional: enter the duration of the closure in a number.
*  **Closure Reason** (Closure Reason):
	+ Optional: provide more information about why you selected a particular closure impact.
*  **Member Insight** (Member Insight):
	+ Optional: share any additional insight you have about your visit or experience.
*  **Feedback** (Feedback):
	+ Optional: share any feedback about your experience.
