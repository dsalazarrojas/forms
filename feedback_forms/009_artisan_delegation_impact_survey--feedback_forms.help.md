# Artisan Delegation Impact Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Artisan Delegation Impact Survey is designed to collect information about the effects of artisan delegation programs on participants. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you provide accurate and helpful feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please complete each section carefully and honestly.** This survey is anonymous, and your responses will be kept confidential.
2. **Start by filling in your details**:
	* **Respondent Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or contact information.
	* **Artisan Group or Network Name**: Enter the name of the organization or network you are affiliated with.
3. **Provide your role in the organization**:
	* **Your Role in the Organization**: Choose the best description of your role from the options provided.
4. **Delegation program details**:
	* **Survey Date**: Enter the date on which the delegation program took place or ended.
	* **Delegation Duration - Months**: Enter the duration of the delegation program in months. (Note: This field is optional.)
5. **Program impact**:
	* **Rate Overall Impact - Scale 1-10**: Choose a score between 1 and 10 to indicate the overall impact of the program on you and your organization.
	* **Economic Impact on Participants**: Choose one of the options provided to describe the economic impact of the program on participants.
6. **Skills and outcomes**:
	* **Skills Developed or Enhanced**: Choose one or more skills that you developed or enhanced as a result of the program. (Note: This field is optional.)
7. **Challenges and sustainability**:
	* **Main Challenges Faced**: Describe any challenges you faced during or after the delegation program.
	* **Program Sustainability Outlook**: Choose one of the options provided to indicate your outlook on the program's sustainability.
8. **Future improvements and recommendations**:
	* **Suggested Improvements**: Describe any suggestions you have for improving the program.
	* **Recommendations for Future Delegations**: Provide any recommendations you have for future delegation programs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or contact information.
* **Artisan Group or Network Name** (`artisan_group`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or network you are affiliated with.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which the delegation program took place or ended.
* **Your Role in the Organization** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose the best description of your role in the organization.
* **Delegation Duration - Months** (`delegation_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the delegation program in months.
* **Rate Overall Impact - Scale 1-10** (`impact_rating`, number, required): Choose a score between 1 and 10 to indicate the overall impact of the program on you and your organization.
* **Economic Impact on Participants** (`economic_impact`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options provided to describe the economic impact of the program on participants.
* **Skills Developed or Enhanced** (`skill_development`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more skills that you developed or enhanced as a result of the program.
* **Main Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you faced during or after the delegation program.
* **Program Sustainability Outlook** (`sustainability`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options provided to indicate your outlook on the program's sustainability.
* **Key Success Factors** (`success_factors`, text, optional): Provide any key success factors you observed or experienced during the program.
* **Suggested Improvements** (`improvements_suggested`, text, optional): Describe any suggestions you have for improving the program.
* **Recommendations for Future Delegations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any recommendations you have for future delegation programs.
* **Interest in Follow-up Interviews** (`follow_up_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you are interested in follow-up interviews.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
