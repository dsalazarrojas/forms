# Thesis Defense Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Thesis Defense Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from reviewers on the thesis defense of a PhD candidate. The form is used to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of the defense, and to provide suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided background information to ensure you understand the context of the thesis defense.
2. Provide a brief description of your background and experience in the relevant field.
3. Select the evaluation criteria that best reflects your assessment of the defense.
4. Write a short text describing your evaluation for each of the criteria selected in step 3.
5. Provide a brief description of the relevance and impact of the research presented.
6. Describe the originality and contribution of the research.
7. Outline the research design and methodology used.
8. Summarize the results and discussion of the research.
9. Provide an overall evaluation of the defense, and select the corresponding option.
10. Provide any suggestions for improvement.
11. Add any additional comments about the defense.
12. Rate the defense on a scale of 1-5.
13. Enter your background information, including any potential conflicts of interest.
14. Provide your contact information.
15. Sign the form to confirm that you are submitting feedback as a reviewer.
16. Enter your declaration of conflict of interest, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Background Information** (required): Enter a brief description of the background information for the thesis defense.
* **Reviewer's Background** (required): Provide a brief description of your background and experience in the relevant field.
* **Evaluation Criteria** (required): Select one or more evaluation criteria that best reflect your assessment of the defense. Options include:
	+ Content and Structure
	+ Methodology and Method
	+ Results and Discussion
	+ Overall Impression
* **Relevance and Impact** (required): Write a short text describing the relevance and impact of the research presented.
* **Originality and Contribution** (required): Describe the originality and contribution of the research.
* **Research Design and Methodology** (required): Outline the research design and methodology used.
* **Results and Discussion** (required): Summarize the results and discussion of the research.
* **Overall Evaluation** (required): Select the overall evaluation of the defense. Options include:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (required): Provide any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (required): Add any additional comments about the defense.
* **Rating** (required): Rate the defense on a scale of 1-5.
* **Review Background 2** (required): Enter any additional background information about yourself.
* **Reviewers Contact Information** (required): Enter your contact information.
* **Reviewers Digital Signature** (required): Sign the form to confirm that you are submitting feedback as a reviewer.
* **Conflicts of Interest Declaration** (optional): Check if you have any conflicts of interest and describe them.
* **Final Submission** (required): Confirm that you are submitting feedback as a reviewer.
