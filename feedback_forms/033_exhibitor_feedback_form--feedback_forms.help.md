### Exhibitor Feedback Form - Help Guide

#### Purpose
The Exhibitor Feedback Form is designed to allow users to provide constructive feedback on their experience with the exhibitor at our event. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and provide a means for exhibitors to receive feedback from their audience. By completing this form, you will help us make future events even better for everyone.

#### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Exhibitor Name** in the first field. This is the name of the exhibitor you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the second field. This is optional but helps us contact you if we need to follow up on your feedback.
3. Rate your experience with the exhibitor using the **Rating** field. Choose from 1-2 stars, 3-4 stars, or 5 stars to indicate your satisfaction.
4. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the **Comment** field. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand your thoughts and opinions.
5. Finally, type any additional feedback you have in the **Feedback** field. This is also an optional field, but it allows you to provide specific details about your experience.

#### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Name** (Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Please enter the name of the exhibitor you are providing feedback on.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional: Optional): This field is optional, but we recommend providing your email address so we can follow up with you if necessary.
* **Rating** (Select One, Required/Optional: Optional): Rate your experience with the exhibitor from 1-2 stars to 3-4 stars, or 5 stars.
* **Comment** (Note, Required/Optional: Optional): If you have any additional thoughts or opinions about your experience, please type them here.
* **Feedback** (Text, Required/Optional: Optional): Provide any specific details or suggestions about your experience in this field.
