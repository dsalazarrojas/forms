# Spa Ambient Light Preference Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your preferences for lighting in a spa environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To get started, carefully read through each question and answer based on your honest preferences.
2.  Select your preferred options for each question.
3.  Make sure to submit your answers once you have completed all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your favorite type of lighting in a spa?** (`spa_ambient_light_preference_survey_1`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Dimmable
		- Bright
		- Moderate
		- Very bright
		- Very dim
* **How bright do you like the lighting in a spa?** (`spa_ambient_light_preference_survey_2`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Bright
		- Not too bright
		- Moderate
		- Very bright
		- Too bright
		- Dim
		- Too dim
* **Which of the following colors do you most associate with relaxation?** (`spa_ambient_light_preference_survey_3`, select_multiple, required/optional)
	+ Choose one or more of the following colors:
		- Blue
		- Green
		- Yellow
		- Red
		- Purple
* **What is the color temperature of the lighting in a spa?** (`spa_ambient_light_preference_survey_4`, select_multiple, required/optional)
	+ Choose one or more of the following colors:
		- Cool
		- Warm
		- Neutral
* **Are you sensitive to glare in the spa?** (`spa_ambient_light_preference_survey_5`, select_one, required/optional)
	+ Choose one of the following options:
		- Yes
		- No
		- Not sure

## Tips
- Answer honestly based on your personal preferences.
- If unsure, choose the option that best fits your feelings.

Please remember that this form is for your own preference, not for any external use.
