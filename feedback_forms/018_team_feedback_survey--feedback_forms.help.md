# Team Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your team's experience and satisfaction with their work environment. Your feedback will help your team leaders understand what's working well and what areas need improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey to provide your valuable insights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer the ones that are relevant to your experiences.
2.  Provide honest and thoughtful responses to each question.
3.  Use the options provided for select-one, select-multiple, and select-one questions.
4.  Enter your answers to text and number questions.
5.  Click Submit when you've completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Leadership (team-leadership)**: Please provide your thoughts on your team leadership's approach. This is a text field.
*   **Communication (communication)**: How would you rate your team's communication style? Choose one of the following options:
    + Strongly disagree
    + Somewhat disagree
    + Neutral
    + Somewhat agree
    + Strongly agree
*   **Team Work Environment (team-work-environment)**: Please provide any thoughts or comments about your team's work environment. This is a note field.
*   **Collaboration (collaboration)**: How often do you collaborate with team members on tasks and projects? Choose two or more of the following options:
    + Team members often work together on tasks and projects
    + Team members often share knowledge and ideas
    + Team members often help each other with work
    + Team members often communicate effectively
*   **Problem-Solving (problem-solving)**: How satisfied are you with the time spent on tasks? Choose one of the following options:
    + Less than 20
    + 21-40
    + 41-60
    + 61-80
    + 81-100
*   **Decision-Making (decision-making)**: How does your team approach decision-making? Choose one of the following options:
    + Top-down
    + Bottom-up
    + Both
*   **Conflict Resolution (conflict-resolution)**: Please describe any conflicts or issues that you've experienced with team members. This is a text field.
*   **Recognition (recognition)**: How often do you receive recognition from your team leaders? Choose one of the following options:
    + Not often
    + Occasionally
    + Often
    + Almost always
*   **Feedback (feedback)**: Please provide any feedback or suggestions you have for improving the team's dynamics. This is a text field.
*   **Communication Channels (communication-channels)**: How do you usually communicate with team members? Choose two or more of the following options:
    + Email
    + Phone
    + Instant Messenger
    + In-person
*   **Collaboration Tools (collaboration-tools)**: Which collaboration tools do you use with your team? Choose two or more of the following options:
    + Asana
    + Trello
    + Slack
    + Microsoft Teams
*   **Work Satisfaction (work-satisfaction)**: How satisfied are you with your overall work experience? Choose one of the following options:
    + Very dissatisfied
    + Somewhat dissatisfied
    + Neutral
    + Somewhat satisfied
    + Very satisfied
*   **Time Spend (time-spend)**: How satisfied are you with the time spent on tasks? Choose one of the following options:
    + Less than 20
    + 21-40
    + 41-60
    + 61-80
    + 81-100
