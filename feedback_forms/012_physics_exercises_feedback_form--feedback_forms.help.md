# Physics Exercises Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physics Exercises Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from students on their experience with physics exercises. This feedback is crucial to improving the quality of the exercises and providing a better learning experience for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for "Physics Exercises Problems" to indicate which exercises you completed.
2. Provide your "Teacher Name" and "Student Name" to help us identify you and your feedback.
3. Type in the "Problem Title" of the exercise(s) you completed.
4. Provide your feedback on your experience with the exercise using the "Feedback" field.
5. Share what you did well in the "What did you do well in this assignment?" field.
6. Suggest improvements for future exercises in the "What do you suggest to make it better?" field.
7. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physics Exercises Feedback** (`physics_exercises_feedback_form_1`, text, required): This field is where you can provide general feedback on your experience with the exercises.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Please type in the name of your teacher who assigned the exercises.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Type in your name as a student who completed the exercises.
* **Problem Title** (`problem_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the exercise(s) you completed.
* **Physics Exercises Problems** (`physics_exercises_problems`, select_multiple, required): Select all the problems you completed from the list of options provided.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts on what you liked or disliked about the exercises.
* **What did you do well in this assignment?** (`physics_exercises_feedback_form_8`, text, required): Describe what you did well in completing the exercises.
* **What do you suggest to make it better?** (`physics_exercises_feedback_form_9`, text, required): Suggest improvements for future exercises.
* **Additional Comments** (`physics_exercises_feedback_form_10`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
