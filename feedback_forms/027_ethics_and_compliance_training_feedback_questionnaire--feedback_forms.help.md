# Ethics And Compliance Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants on the effectiveness and quality of the ethics and compliance training they received. This feedback will help the training team to identify areas of improvement and make the training more valuable and engaging for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer it as honestly as possible.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `text`, required: false`): Please provide a brief introduction to your feedback, if any.
* **Training Satisfaction** (`2`, `number`, required: true`): Rate your satisfaction with the training on a scale of 1-5.
* **Instructors Performance** (`3`, `select_one`, required: true`): Choose one of the following ratings for the instructor's performance: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Course Materials** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false`): Select all that apply from the list of options for the quality of course materials: Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Feedback On Training** (`5`, `text`, required: true`): Please provide detailed feedback on the training, including what was good, what was not good, and any suggestions for improvement.
* **Additional Comments** (`6`, `text`, required: false`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.
