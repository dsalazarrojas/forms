# information_session_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The information session feedback form is a tool used to collect feedback from participants after an information session. This feedback is crucial for us to understand what participants learned from the session, what they found valuable, and if they have any suggestions for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name in the first field.
2. Provide the Session Date and Session Time in the next two fields, respectively.
3. Enter the Session Topic to help us better understand the context of your feedback.
4. Provide the Session Location to give us more information about where the session was held.
5. Answer the three feedback questions to the best of your ability.
6. Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
7. If you have anything else you'd like to say, use the "Is there anything else you'd like to say?" field.
8. Finally, enter your Email and Phone number for us to contact you back.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the participant of the session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the session you attended.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the session topic.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the session was held.
* **What did you learn from the session?** (`feedback_question_1`, `text`, required: false): Share with us what you learned from the session.
* **What did you find most valuable from the session?** (`feedback_question_2`, `text`, required: false): Let us know what aspect of the session you found most valuable.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future sessions?** (`feedback_question_3`, `text`, required: false): Share any suggestions you have for future sessions.
* **Rate your overall experience** (`participant_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to say?** (`participant_comment`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or thoughts you have.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you back.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you back.
