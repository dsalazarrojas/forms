# Museum Visitor Story Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Visitor Story Submission Form is designed to collect stories, feedback, and other information from museum visitors. By filling out this form, you're helping the museum better understand your experience and improve its services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as per your preference.
2. For fields with a dropdown menu, select the most suitable option.
3. Be as descriptive as possible when filling in the text fields.
4. Attach photos or share a URL if you want to share visual content.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Visitor Story** (`visitor_story`, text, optional)
A brief description of your visit or experience at the museum.

### 2. **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional)
Your photo or a link to a photo you'd like to share.

### 3. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional)
Any feedback or comments you have about the museum or your visit.

### 4. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Your email address, if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up.

### 5. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted via phone for follow-up.

### 6. **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional)
Choose a category that best describes your story:
	* General
	* Art
	* History

### 7. **Subcategory** (`subcategory`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose one or more subcategories that best describe your story:
	* Yes
	* No

### 8. **Story Details** (`story_details`, text, optional)
A more in-depth description of your story.

### 9. **Photo Description** (`photo_description`, text, optional)
A brief description of your photo or story.

### 10. **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional)
A link to a photo or story you'd like to share.

### 11. **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, optional)
Would you be available for follow-up contact:
	* True
	* False

### 12. **Follow-up Reason** (`follow_up_reason`, text, optional)
If you're not available for follow-up, please explain why.

### 13. **Category Other** (`category_other`, text, optional)
A custom category for your story (if none of the above options fit).

### 14. **Subcategory Other** (`subcategory_other`, text, optional)
A custom subcategory for your story (if none of the above options fit).

### 15. **Story** (`story`, text, optional)
A more detailed description of your story.

### 16. **Story URL** (`story_url`, text, optional)
A link to your story or photo.

### 17. **Story Date** (`story_date`, date, optional)
The date of your visit or story.

### 18. **Story Time** (`story_time`, time, optional)
The time of your visit or story.

### 19. **Follow-up Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, text, optional)
Additional contact information for follow-up.

### 20. **Follow-up Contact Phone** (`follow_up_contact_phone`, text, optional)
Your phone number for follow-up contact.

### 21. **Follow-up Contact Email** (`follow_up_contact_email`, email, optional)
Your email address for follow-up contact.

### 22. **Follow-up Contact URL** (`follow_up_contact_url`, text, optional)
A link for follow-up contact.

### 23. **Story Image** (`story_image`, text, optional)
An image or link to an image related to your story.

### 24. **Feedback Description** (`feedback_description`, text, optional)
More detailed feedback or comments about your visit.

### 25. **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, optional)
Any additional comments or information about your visit.

Please fill out the form as per your preference, and we appreciate your feedback and input to improve our services.
