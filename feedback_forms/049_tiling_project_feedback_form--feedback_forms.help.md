# Tiling Project Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about a specific tiling project. Please answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible to provide valuable insights for the project team.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.
2.  Fill in the required fields (denoted by a red asterisk \*).
3.  Provide as much detail as possible for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project ID** (`project_id`, text, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the project, if you have one.
* **Feedback Title** (`feedback_title`, text, optional): Briefly describe the feedback you want to provide. This is the title of your feedback.
* **Feedback Description** (`feedback_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your feedback, including any specific issues or concerns you have.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Choose a rating for your feedback: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* If you have any specific issues or concerns, describe them clearly.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, provide them in the comments field.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, contact form@gic.mx.
