# Health & Safety Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health & Safety Leadership Survey is a tool used by management to assess the current state of employee safety efforts, initiatives, and concerns. This survey aims to gather information on employee perceptions of safety efforts, identify areas of improvement, and measure overall safety satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your name, department name, and safety initiative name.
2. Rate your current safety efforts and initiatives by selecting the corresponding options (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
3. Provide comments and suggestions for improvement in the designated text fields.
4. Answer the select-one and select-multiple questions, selecting the options that best apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your department.
* **Safety Initiative Name** (`safety_initiative_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the safety initiative you are participating in.
* **Current Level of Safety Efforts** (`current_level_of_safety_efforts`, number, required): Rate your current safety efforts on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being "Very Low" and 5 being "Very High".
* **Safety Goals for Improvement** (`safety_goals_for_improvement`, text, required): Describe your goals for improving safety efforts in the workplace.
* **Employee Suggestion for Improvement** (`employee_suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving safety efforts.
* **Management Support** (`management_support`, select_one, required): Select whether your management team offers "Yes" or "No" support for safety efforts.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required): Select the options that best apply to your experience with communication about safety efforts, including "Yes", "No" or any other options.
* **Training and Equipment** (`training_and_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best apply to your experience with training and equipment for safety efforts, including "Yes", "No" or any other options.
* **Reporting Frequency** (`reporting_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select whether you report on safety concerns frequently, occasionally, or never.
* **Reporting Method** (`reporting_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method you use to report on safety concerns, such as "Yes", "No" or any other options.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any safety concerns you may have.
* **Safety Concerns Comments** (`safety_concerns_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on your safety concerns.
* **Employee Safety Concerns** (`employee_safety_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best apply to your safety concerns, including "Yes", "No" or any other options.
* **Supervisor Safety Concerns** (`supervisor_safety_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best apply to your supervisor's safety concerns, including "Yes", "No" or any other options.
* **Safety Concerns Resolution** (`safety_concerns_resolution`, select_one, optional): Select whether your safety concerns are resolved, not resolved, or you are unsure.
* **Safety Concerns Resolution Comments** (`safety_concerns_resolution_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on your safety concerns resolution.
* **Safety Meettings** (`safety_meetings`, select_one, optional): Select whether you attend safety meetings, no longer attend, or are unsure.
* **Safety Meeting Comments** (`safety_meeting_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments on your safety meeting experience.
* **Employee Safety Involvement** (`employee_safety_involvement`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best apply to your safety involvement, including "Yes", "No" or any other options.
* **Management Safety Involvement** (`management_safety_involvement`, select_multiple, optional): Select the options that best apply to your management team's safety involvement, including "Yes", "No" or any other options.
* **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have received safety training, or are waiting for it.
* **Safety Training Comments** (`safety_training_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments on your safety training experience.
* **Overall Safety Satisfaction** (`overall_safety_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your overall safety satisfaction, being satisfied or dissatisfied.
* **Overall Safety Satisfaction Comments** (`overall_safety_satisfaction_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments on your overall safety satisfaction.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing answers and comments.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Unknown" or provide a comment explaining your uncertainty.
* Please submit the form once you have completed all the fields.
