# store_feedback_collection_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers or visitors about their experiences with our store. We value your input and want to hear about your thoughts, concerns, and suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply navigate to our feedback page on our website.
2. Select the "Visit Details" field to describe your visit, including any relevant details such as the date and time of your visit.
3. Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
4. Share your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
5. Provide any additional comments or thoughts in the "Comment" field.
6. Once you've completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visit Details** (`visit_details`, text, optional): Please describe your visit, including any relevant details such as the date and time of your visit.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and opinions about your experience.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience. 

(Note: The Comment field is a free-formatted text field, which means you can write as much or as little as you'd like.) 

## Tips
* Be honest and open in your feedback, as it helps us understand how to improve.
* Make sure to include any relevant details about your visit to help us better understand your experience.
* We'll do our best to respond to all feedback, but if you have an urgent concern, please contact us directly instead of using this form. 

This help guide will ensure that end users understand the purpose of the form and how to fill it out correctly. Each field is explained clearly, and the tips section offers helpful advice on how to provide valuable feedback.
