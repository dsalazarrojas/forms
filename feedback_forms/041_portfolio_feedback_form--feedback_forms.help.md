# Portfolio Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Portfolio Feedback Form is a tool for users to give feedback on a portfolio, helping to improve and refine the content.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name**: Provide your name so we can contact you if needed.
2. **Enter your email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if further information is required.
3. **Enter your feedback**: Type in your thoughts and comments about the portfolio.
4. **Choose your overall rating**: Select a rating from 1 to 4 to rate the overall quality of the portfolio.
5. **Rate each portfolio question**: Answer each question with a rating from 1 to 4.
6. **Submit your feedback**: Click the submit button to send your feedback.
