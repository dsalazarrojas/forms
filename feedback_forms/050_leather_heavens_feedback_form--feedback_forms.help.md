# leather_heavens_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "leather_heavens_feedback_form" is a tool used by users to provide feedback about their experience with the Leather Heavens service. It helps us understand user needs and preferences, allowing us to make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required and optional fields are filled out.
2. Enter your feedback in the "feedback_form" field, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Fill out your name in the "name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. If you have a phone number you'd like to be contacted, enter it in the "phone" field.
6. Write your comment in the "comment" field, describing your experience and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **feedback_form** (`feedback_form`, text, optional): Enter your feedback about your experience with the Leather Heavens service. Be as detailed as possible, providing suggestions or comments that can help us improve.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name to associate it with your feedback. This will help us address your concerns and acknowledge your contribution.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further clarification or to thank you for your feedback.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by a member of our team. We'll use this information only with your consent.
* **comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Describe your experience and suggestions in this field, providing any relevant details that might help us improve.
