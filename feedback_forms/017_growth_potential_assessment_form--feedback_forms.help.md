# Growth Potential Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Growth Potential Assessment Form is designed to collect information on a company or employee's growth potential. This form is used to evaluate their ability to grow, meet their goals, and establish contact with a key person.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable options for each section.
3. Enter your contact information at the end of the form to ensure we can follow up with you.
4. Please submit the form when you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Label (company label, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Check if your company is currently growing or has growth potential.
* **Goals (goals, Select One, Optional)**: Check if your company has goals and if you are working towards meeting them.
* **Employee Name (Employee Name, Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the employee being assessed.
* **Employee Title (Employee Title, Text, Optional)**: Enter the title or position of the employee.
* **Employee Department (employee_department, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Check if your company has a department or team for the employee.
* **Manager Name (Manager Name, Select One, Optional)**: Check if you have a manager or supervisor for the employee.
* **Manager Title (Manager Title, Text, Optional)**: Enter the title or position of the manager.
* **Company Name (Company Name, Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the company being assessed.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person, Text, Optional)**: Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, Email, Optional)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, Text, Optional)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, Text, Optional)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (alternative). 

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please answer the question to the best of your ability. If you need help with anything, please don't hesitate to reach out.
