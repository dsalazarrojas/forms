# Customer Onboarding Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Onboarding Feedback Request Form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their onboarding experience with our company. This form helps us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our onboarding process is meeting our customers' expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address to help us contact you regarding any issues or questions you may have.
3. Share your phone number with us so we can reach out to you if needed.
4. Provide your feedback about your onboarding experience with us. Be as specific as possible about what worked well and what didn't.
5. Describe your expected outcomes from our onboarding process.
6. Share your actual outcomes from our onboarding process.
7. Share any recommendations for improvement you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your first name for identification purposes.

### 2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your last name for identification purposes.

### 3. **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding any issues or questions.

### 4. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false)
Share your phone number with us so we can reach out to you if needed.

### 5. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
Provide your feedback about your onboarding experience with us. Be specific about what worked well and what didn't.

### 6. **Expected Outcomes** (`expected_outcomes`, text, required: false)
Describe the outcomes you expected from our onboarding process.

### 7. **Actual Outcomes** (`actual_outcomes`, text, required: false)
Share your actual outcomes from our onboarding process.

### 8. **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required: false)
Share any recommendations you may have for improving our onboarding process.

### 9. **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false)
Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
