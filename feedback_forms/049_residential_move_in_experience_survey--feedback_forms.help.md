# Residential Move In Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Residential Move In Experience Survey" form collects information about a tenant's experience during their recent move-in process. This helps us understand how well we're meeting your needs as a tenant. By filling out this form, you'll help us identify areas of improvement and create a better living environment for you and your community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the "Move In Experience" field and provide a brief description of your experience during the move-in process.
2. For the "Property Manager" field, enter the name and contact information of the property manager who assisted you with your move-in.
3. If applicable, fill in the "Property Details" field with any notes about your property, such as the address, location, or unit number.
4. Enter the date you moved in for the "Move In Date" field.
5. If you'd like to share more details, you can add a description of your experience in the "Comments" field.
6. If you've already provided your email address, you can enter it in the "Email" field for us to contact you.
7. For the "Phone" field, enter your contact phone number.
8. If you know the property manager's contact phone number, please enter it in the "Property Manager Phone" field.
9. Similarly, if you know the property manager's email, you can enter it in the "Property Manager Email" field.
10. Finally, you can enter the Tenant ID or Property ID if you have one handy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Move In Experience
* **Move In Experience**: Describe your experience during the move-in process.

### Property Manager
* **Property Manager (Name)**: Enter the name of the property manager who assisted you.

### Property Details
* **Property Details**: Enter any notes about your property, such as the address, location, or unit number.

### Move In Date
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date you moved in.

### Comments
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.

### Property Manager Phone
* **Property Manager Phone**: Enter the property manager's contact phone number if you know it.

### Property Manager Email
* **Property Manager Email**: Enter the property manager's email address if you know it.

### Tenant ID
* **Tenant ID**: Enter your Tenant ID if you have one.

### Property ID
* **Property ID**: Enter your Property ID if you have one.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any information, don't hesitate to ask your property manager for help.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve the living experience for you and your community.
