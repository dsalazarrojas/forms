# Justice And Dignity Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience with the service, specifically on how they feel they have been treated fairly, whether they received respect and dignity, and what areas of improvement are needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Select the options that best describe your experience.
3. Make sure to provide your email address so that we can follow up with you.
4. Submit the form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Please enter your first name. This will help us know who is providing the feedback.
  (Type: text, Required: false)
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you.
  (Type: text, Required: false)
- **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
  (Type: email, Required: false)
- **Date**: Select the date you visited our service.
  (Type: date, Required: false)
- **Time**: Select the time you visited our service.
  (Type: time, Required: false)
- **Note**: Provide any additional comments or feedback.
  (Type: note, Required: false)
- **Last Name**: Please enter your last name. This will help us know who is providing the feedback.
  (Type: text, Required: false)
- **Feedback Question 1**: Select how often you feel you have been treated fairly in our service.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: false)
- **Feedback Question 2**: Select whether you were treated with respect and dignity in our service.
  (Type: select_one, Required: false)
- **Feedback Question 3**: Select how likely you are to recommend our service to a friend or family member.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Rating**: Please rate your overall experience.
  (Type: number, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 4**: Select what services were you using when you felt not fairly treated.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 5**: Select what the circumstances were surrounding your experience.
  (Type: select_one, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 6**: Select how you would describe the reason you chose to leave the service early or not use it at all.
  (Type: select_one, Required: false)
- **Feedback Question 7**: Select whether you had to wait for a service that was delayed.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 8**: Select how satisfied you are with the service overall.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 9**: Select how satisfied you are with the service staff.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 10**: Select how satisfied you are with the service environment.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 11**: Select how satisfied you are with the service communication.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 12**: Select whether you had to contact customer service.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 13**: Select whether you had any issues with your service that were not addressed.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 14**: Select whether you had any issues with your service that were not addressed due to your language or cultural background.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)
- **Feedback Question 15**: Select whether you had any issues with your service that were not addressed due to your age or disability.
  (Type: select_multiple, Required: true)

## Tips

* Make sure to provide honest and accurate feedback.
* Provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us.
