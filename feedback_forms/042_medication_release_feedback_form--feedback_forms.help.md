# medication_release_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medication_release_feedback_form is a form used to collect feedback from patients who have been released from the hospital. It is intended to gather information about the release process, incident details, and demographic information about the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the patient_feedback field with your thoughts and comments about your experience during the hospital stay.
2. Provide information about the release_process, including any issues or concerns you had.
3. Enter details about the incident that led to your release, if applicable.
4. Enter your contact information, including release_nurse_name, release_nurse_phone, patient_name, patient_phone, patient_email.
5. Enter the release_date and release_reason for your release.
6. Enter the hospital_name, unit_number, and hospital_name_2.
7. Enter the patient_dob, release_nurse_department, and release_nurse_name_2.
8. Enter the patient_name_2, release_nurse_phone_2, patient_phone_2, and patient_email_2.
9. Enter the hospital_name_3 and hospital_name_3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any thoughts or comments about your experience during your hospital stay.
* **Release Process** (`release_process`, text, optional): Enter any issues or concerns you had during the release process.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, note, optional): If you had an incident that led to your release, please describe it here.
* **Release Nurse Name** (`release_nurse_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the release nurse who handled your case.
* **Release Nurse Phone** (`release_nurse_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the release nurse who handled your case.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Patient Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Patient Email** (`patient_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your release from the hospital.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): If you had an incident, enter the date it occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): If you had an incident, enter the time it occurred.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital where you were released.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, text, required): Enter the unit number of the hospital where you were released.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for your release from the hospital.
* **Release Notes** (`release_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your release.
* **Hospital Name 2** (`hospital_name_2`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital.
* **Unit Number 2** (`unit_number_2`, text, required): Enter the unit number of the hospital.
* **Release Nurse Department** (`release_nurse_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the release nurse who handled your case.
* **Patient Name 2** (`patient_name_2`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Release Nurse** (`release_nurse_name_2`, text, required): Enter the name of the release nurse who handled your case.
* **Release Nurse Phone 2** (`release_nurse_phone_2`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the release nurse who handled your case.
* **Patient Phone 2** (`patient_phone_2`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Patient E-mail** (`patient_email_2`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Hospital Name 3** (`hospital_name_3`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital.
