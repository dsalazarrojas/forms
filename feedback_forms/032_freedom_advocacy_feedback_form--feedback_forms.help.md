# Freedom Advocacy Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals about their experiences with Freedom Advocacy, a campaign or event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields, respectively.
2. In the "Campaign Name" field, enter the name of the Freedom Advocacy campaign or event you are providing feedback about.
3. If you participated in a Freedom Advocacy event, enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
4. For "Perceived Impact", select the one option that best describes your experience with Freedom Advocacy.
	+ Select "Freedom of assembly" if you believe it was a success in terms of protecting or promoting the right to peaceful assembly.
	+ Select "Peaceful assembly" if you believe it was a success in terms of promoting peaceful assembly.
	+ Select "Other (please specify)" if you have a different perspective on the impact of Freedom Advocacy.
5. In the "Perceived Importance" field, describe how important you believe Freedom Advocacy is in the context of the event or campaign.
6. For "Future Outreach", select all the methods you think Freedom Advocacy should use to reach out to the public in the future.
	+ Select "Twitter" if you think social media is an effective way for Freedom Advocacy to reach the public.
	+ Select "Facebook" if you think social media is an effective way for Freedom Advocacy to reach the public.
	+ Select "Other (please specify)" if you have a different perspective on how Freedom Advocacy should reach out to the public.
7. In the "Feedback" field, provide any comments or suggestions you might have for Freedom Advocacy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Campaign Name**: Enter the name of the Freedom Advocacy campaign or event you are providing feedback about.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the Freedom Advocacy event you participated in.
* **Perceived Impact**: Select the option that best describes your experience with Freedom Advocacy.
* **Perceived Importance**: Describe how important you believe Freedom Advocacy was in the context of the event or campaign.
* **Future Outreach**: Select all the methods you think Freedom Advocacy should use to reach out to the public in the future.
* **Feedback**: Share any comments or suggestions you might have for Freedom Advocacy.
