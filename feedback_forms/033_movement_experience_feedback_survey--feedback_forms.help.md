# Movement Experience Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Movement Experience Feedback Survey is designed to collect insights and feedback from participants on a specific event or experience related to a movement or activity. This feedback is crucial for understanding the effectiveness of the experience and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions and fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for the fields that are not text or note fields.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you find the experience?** (`movement_experience_feedback_survey`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your experience. Be as detailed as possible and provide any specific comments or concerns you may have.
* **Date** (`movement_experience_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of the experience you are providing feedback on.
* **Time** (`movement_experience_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the experience you are providing feedback on.
* **Event feedback** (`event_feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any comments or feedback you have on the event itself.
* **Category** (`movement_category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all categories that apply to your experience. If none of the categories apply, select "No".
* **Activity** (`movement_activity`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you participated in the activity or not.
* **Participant feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required/optional): Provide any comments or feedback you have on the participant experience.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, or the name of the participant you are providing feedback on.
* **Participant email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the participant you are providing feedback on.
* **Participant phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the participant you are providing feedback on.
* **Movement notes** (`movement_notes`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or context about the experience that may be helpful for the organizers.
