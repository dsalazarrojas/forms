# Therapy Service Release & Client Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for clients to provide feedback and testimonials about their therapy sessions after receiving service. It is an essential tool for us to understand client satisfaction and to share their positive experiences with potential clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the page to fill out:
	* Client Review: Share your thoughts and experiences about your therapy sessions.
	* Feedback: If you have any feedback or suggestions, please type them here.
2. Fill out the form fields:
	* **Testimonial Release**: Choose how you'd like to release your testimonial:
		+ True: Release your testimonial publicly (e.g., on social media, website).
		+ False: Do not release your testimonial.
		+ Undecided: Leave the decision up to us.
	* **Share Testimonial**: Select how you'd like to share your testimonial:
		+ Publicly: Share your testimonial publicly (e.g., on social media, website).
		+ Privately: Share your testimonial only with authorized personnel.
		+ On request: Share your testimonial only when you request it.
	* **Share on Facebook/Share on Twitter**: Enter your social media handles (e.g., @username) for sharing.
	* **Email/Phone**: Enter your email address or phone number.
	* **Date/Time**: Enter the date and time of your therapy session.
	* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
	* **Client Consent**: Enter any additional comments or agreements related to sharing your testimonial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Review**: Share your thoughts and experiences about your therapy sessions.
* **Feedback**: If you have any feedback or suggestions, please type them here.
* **Testimonial Release**: Choose how you'd like to release your testimonial.
* **Share Testimonial**: Select how you'd like to share your testimonial.
* **Share on Facebook**: Enter your Facebook social media handle.
* **Share on Twitter**: Enter your Twitter social media handle.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your therapy session (mm/dd/yyyy format).
* **Time**: Enter the time of your therapy session (hh:mm am/pm format).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
* **Client Consent**: Enter any additional comments or agreements related to sharing your testimonial.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, as they help us prevent duplicate entries.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- Contact us if you have any questions or concerns!
