# Postcard Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the postcard, allowing us to understand their thoughts and opinions. Your input will help us improve our services and products.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Go through each page of the form, answering the questions and selecting options as required.
2.  For each page, make sure to fill out all the required fields before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about this postcard** (`form_1_page_1`): Share your thoughts and opinions about the postcard.
* **Select an image** (`postcard_image`): Please choose one of the provided images that best represents the postcard.
* **Have you ever been to this location** (`form_1_page_2`): Let us know if you have visited the location associated with the postcard.
* **Enter any additional comments** (`postcard_text_area`): Provide any additional information or thoughts you'd like to share about the postcard.
* **Your email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **How likely are you to recommend us** (`form_1_page_3`): Rate your likelihood of recommending our service or product.
* **Select one** (`select_1`): Choose one option from the provided list.
* **Select multiple** (`select_2`): Choose multiple options from the provided list.
* **Date and time** (`date_time`): Enter the date and time of the interaction with the postcard.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the interaction with the postcard.
* **Additional comments** (`form_1_page_5`, `form_1_page_6`, `form_1_page_7`, `form_1_page_8`, `form_1_page_11`, `form_1_page_12`, `form_1_page_13`, `form_1_page_14`, `form_1_page_15`): Provide any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share about the postcard.
* **Your email 2** (`email_2`): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Your phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to include them in the "Additional comments" field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at your email address.
