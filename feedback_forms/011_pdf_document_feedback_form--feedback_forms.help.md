# pdf_document_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pdf_document_feedback_form is meant to gather feedback on PDF documents from users who have viewed or interacted with the document. It's a tool for providing input on the document's content, structure, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the document that you are providing feedback on to ensure you have a good understanding of its content.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as you can.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "comments" field.
4. Once you've completed the form, click the "submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What You Like**: This section is for you to select what you like or appreciate about the document. You can choose one or more options from the list.
* **What You Don't Like**: This section is for you to select what you dislike or don't appreciate about the document. You can choose one option from the list.
* **Date of Publication**: This field is for you to provide the date when the document was published. This helps us understand the document's publication timeline.
* **Date of Publication End**: This field is for you to provide the date when the document's publication is expected to end. This helps us understand the document's publication duration.
* **Email**: This field is for you to provide your email address if you'd like to be notified when the document is updated or revised.
* **Phone**: This field is for you to provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further discussions or clarification.
* **Note**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you might have about the document.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or feedback you might have about the document.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed the form, click this button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please answer each question as thoroughly as you can.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
* We appreciate your feedback and value your input!
