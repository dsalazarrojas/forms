# Mobile Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect feedback from users about their experience with our mobile services. It helps us identify areas of improvement and ensure that our services meet user expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or more of the options for "Mobile Service Feedback" to indicate your overall satisfaction with our service. 
2. Choose a rating between 1 and 5 to express your level of satisfaction with our service.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the "Comment" field.
4. Let us know if you want us to contact you about your feedback. Select "Yes" or "No".
5. Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
6. If you'd like us to call or text you, enter your phone number.
7. Provide the name of the service you're giving feedback on.
8. Describe the service in a few words.
9. Choose whether or not we should follow up with a recommendation.
10. Decide if you'd like us to follow up on your submission.
11. Select "Yes" or "No" for the last option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mobile Service Feedback** (`service_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field is a multiple-choice question that asks if our mobile service met your expectations. Select "Yes" if you were satisfied, "No" if there were some issues, or "Other" if you'd like to provide more information.
- **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-5.
- **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in this field.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_one`, required: false): Let us know if you want us to contact you about your feedback. Select "Yes" if you'd like us to reach out.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you'd like us to call or text you, enter your phone number.
- **Service Name** (`service_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of the service you're giving feedback on.
- **Service Description** (`service_description`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the service you're providing feedback on.
- **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose whether or not we should follow up with a recommendation.
- **Submission** (`submission`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Decide if you'd like us to follow up on your submission. Select "Yes" or "No".
