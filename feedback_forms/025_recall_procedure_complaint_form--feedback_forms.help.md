This is a guide to help users understand and fill out the Recall Procedure Complaint Form. The form is designed to collect information about a company's recall procedure and help ensure that the company is aware of any issues related to their products. This form will not accept submissions for non-product related issues or duplicate complaints. Each field in the form should be filled out according to the instructions below.

# Recall Procedure Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recall Procedure Complaint Form is designed to help our team track and address potential issues related to a company's products. Please use this form to report any problems you have experienced with our products.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, select the option "Company" or "Individual" as the contact type.
- Fill in the required fields:
	+ Company Name: Type in the name of the company that produced the problematic product.
	+ Complaint Description: Briefly describe the issue you have experienced.
	+ Product Name: Enter the name of the product related to your complaint.
	+ Submit: Click this button to finalize your submission.
- For non-mandatory fields, you can enter any relevant information:
	+ Serial Number: If you have the product's serial number, please enter it.
	+ Contact Name: If you have a contact person at the company, please enter their name.
	+ Phone Number: If you have the company's phone number, please enter it.
	+ Email: If you have the company's email, please enter it.
	+ Company Email: If you have the company's email, please enter it.
	+ Company Phone: If you have the company's phone number, please enter it.
	+ Assigned Agent: If you would like to be assigned an agent to follow up on your complaint, select "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Type the name of the company that produced the product you are complaining about.
* **Complaint Description**: Briefly explain the issue you have experienced with the product.
* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product that has caused the issue.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the serial number of the product, if you have it.
* **Contact Name**: If you have a contact person at the company, enter their name.
* **Phone Number**: If you have the company's phone number, enter it.
* **Email**: If you have the company's email, enter it.
* **Company Email**: If you have the company's email, enter it.
* **Company Phone**: If you have the company's phone number, enter it.
* **Assigned Agent**: Select "Yes" if you would like to be assigned an agent to follow up on your complaint. Select "No" if you do not want to be assigned an agent.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your complaint form and finalize your submission.
