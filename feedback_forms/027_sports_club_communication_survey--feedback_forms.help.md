# Sports Club Communication Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from sports club members on their experiences and perceptions of the club's communication. Your input is valuable in helping us improve our communication methods and ensure that we are meeting your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, answering all required fields.
2. Please provide honest and constructive feedback in the "feedback" field.
3. Select the type of feedback you have (positive, negative, or both).
4. Enter your name and email address (required for identification and follow-up communication).
5. Provide your phone number (optional, but helps us contact you if needed).
6. If you have any feedback, enter it in the "feedback" field.
7. If you feel like rating your overall experience, use the "rating" field. This is optional, but helps us understand your satisfaction.
8. Select a date for the feedback (the date you're providing feedback on).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Enter your name as a member of the sports club.
* **email** (2): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **phone** (4): Enter your phone number (optional, but helps us contact you).
* **Feedback Type** (5): Select what type of feedback you're providing. Choose one or multiple options that apply:
	+ Positive: You're happy with our communication.
	+ Negative: You're unhappy with our communication.
	+ Both: A mix of positive and negative feedback.
* **Feedback** (6): Enter your detailed feedback. This can include any suggestions or complaints.
* **Rating** (7): Rate your overall experience, from 1 to 10.
* **Date** (8): Select the date this feedback refers to.
