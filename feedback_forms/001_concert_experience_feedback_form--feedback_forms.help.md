# Concert Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from concert attendees about their overall experience at a recent concert event. By providing detailed information about their experience, attendees can help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us deliver high-quality entertainment experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. Select options from the drop-down menus for rating questions.
4. If you have any additional comments, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Information 
*   **Full Name (*)**: Enter your first and last name.

### Full Name 
*   **Email Address (*)**: Enter your email address. We may contact you for follow-up questions.

### Email Address 
*   **Phone Number**: Optional contact number.

### Concert Details 
*   **Concert Date (*)**: Select the date of the concert you attended.

### Concert Venue 
*   **Concert Venue (*)**: Where the concert was held.

### Ticket Type 
*   **Ticket Type (*)**: What type of ticket did you purchase (General Admission, VIP, etc.).

### Overall Experience 
*   **Overall Experience Rating (*)**: Rate your overall experience at the concert.

### Sound Quality 
*   **Sound Quality Rating (*)**: Rate the audio and sound system.

### Lighting and Visuals 
*   **Lighting and Visuals Rating**: Rate the lighting and visual effects. (Note: This field is optional)

### Venue Comfort 
*   **Venue Comfort**: Rate how comfortable you felt at the venue. (Note: This field is optional)

### Staff Friendliness 
*   **Staff Friendliness**: Rate the friendliness of venue staff. (Note: This field is optional)

### Performance Feedback 
*   **Performance Feedback**: Share your thoughts about the performance.

### Favorite Songs 
*   **Favorite Songs or Performances**: Share your favorite moments from the concert.

### Performance Rating 
*   **Performance Rating (*)**: Rate the overall performance.

### Would You Recommend 
*   **Would You Recommend (*)**: Would you recommend this concert to others.

### Additional Comments 
*   **Additional Comments**: Any other feedback you would like to share.

### Future Attendance 
*   **Future Attendance**: If you would like to attend future concerts at this venue. (Note: This field is optional)

This form is designed to gather feedback on various aspects of the concert experience. Please fill out as much as you can to help us improve future events. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
