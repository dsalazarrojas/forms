# resolution_time_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the resolution and response times of support requests or issues. The data collected will help the support team to improve their performance and provide better services to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the introduction, which is a brief description of your experience with the support service.
2. Rate the response speed of the support team using the number field.
3. Rate the resolution speed of the support team using the number field.
4. If you think the resolution speed is important, select "Very Important" from the dropdown list.
5. Provide the date and time when you received the response from the support team using the date field.
6. Provide the date and time when the issue was resolved using the date field.
7. Indicate if you had a team member assigned to your issue, select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
8. If you had a team member assigned, provide their name in the text field.
9. Indicate if the team leader was involved in resolving your issue, select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
10. Provide any additional comments about your experience with the support service in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your experience with the support service. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for the support team to understand the context of your feedback.
* **Response Speed** (`Response Speed`, number, required: false): Rate the speed of the response from the support team. This will help the team to identify areas where they can improve their time-to-response metrics.
* **Resolution Speed** (`Resolution Speed`, number, required: false): Rate the speed of resolution of the issue. This will help the team to identify areas where they can improve their time-to-resolution metrics.
* **Resolution Speed Important** (`Resolution Speed Important`, select_one, required: false): If you think the resolution speed is important for your issue, select "Very Important" from the dropdown list. This will help the team to prioritize issues based on their importance.
* **Response Time** (`Response Time`, date, required: false): Provide the date and time when you received the response from the support team.
* **Resolution Time** (`Resolution Time`, date, required: false): Provide the date and time when the issue was resolved.
* **Team Member** (`Team Member`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you had a team member assigned to your issue, select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
* **Team Member Name** (`Team Member Name`, text, required: false): If you had a team member assigned, provide their name in this text field.
* **Team Leader** (`Team Leader`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if the team leader was involved in resolving your issue, select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your experience with the support service.
