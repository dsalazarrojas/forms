# Property Insurance Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Insurance Process Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with the property insurance process. It covers various stages of the process, from the interaction with the insurance broker to the final outcome of the policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply answer each question according to your experience with the property insurance process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the property insurance process on a scale of 1-10.
* **customer_comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with the property insurance process.
* **insurance_broker**: Rate your satisfaction with the insurance broker's performance on a scale of highly satisfied, somewhat satisfied, somewhat dissatisfied, highly dissatisfied.
* **policy_issue**: Identify any policy issues you encountered during the process, such as problems with the insurer, broker, or process.
* **claim_process**: Rate the effectiveness of the claim process on a scale of very good, somewhat good, neutral, very poor, poor.
* **communication**: Rate the level of communication between you and the insurance broker on a scale of 1-10.
* **timeliness**: Rate the timeliness of the insurance process on a scale of 1-10.
* **overall_experience**: Rate your overall experience with the property insurance process on a scale of 1-10.
* **insurer_availability**: Rate the availability of the insurance company on a scale of mostly available, somewhat available, mostly unavailable, completely unavailable.
* **broker_availability**: Rate the availability of the insurance broker on a scale of mostly available, somewhat available, mostly unavailable, completely unavailable.
* **documentation**: Rate the level of documentation provided during the process on a scale of mostly documented, somewhat documented, mostly undocumented, completely undocumented.
* **follow_up**: Rate the follow-up from the insurance broker on a scale of mostly followed, somewhat followed, mostly unfollowed, completely unfollowed.
* **insurance_broker_fees**: Rate the reasonableness of the insurance broker fees on a scale of 1-10.
* **policy_documentation**: Rate the clarity of the policy documentation provided on a scale of 1-10.
* **insurer_fees**: Rate the reasonableness of the insurer fees on a scale of 1-10.
* **additional_services**: Indicate whether you availed of any additional services from the insurance broker (True/False).
* **policy_price**: Rate the reasonableness of the policy price on a scale of 1-10.
* **broker_fees**: Rate the reasonableness of the broker fees on a scale of 1-10.
* **policy_claim**: Indicate whether you had a claim (no claim, claim in progress, claim paid out).
* **policy_cancellation**: Indicate whether your policy was cancelled (no cancellation, cancelled, cancelled with reason).
* **policy_renewal**: Indicate whether your policy was renewed (no renewal, renewed, not yet renewed).
* **policy_changes**: Indicate whether any changes were made to your policy (no changes, yes changes, pending changes).
* **insurer_commission**: Rate the level of transparency of the insurer commission on a scale of 1-10.
* **broker_commission**: Rate the level of transparency of the broker commission on a scale of 1-10.
* **comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with the property insurance process.

## Tips
Please answer each question based on your actual experience with the property insurance process. Your feedback will help us improve the process and better serve our customers.
