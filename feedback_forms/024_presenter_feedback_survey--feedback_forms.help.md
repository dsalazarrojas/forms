# Presenter Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presenter Feedback Survey is designed to collect feedback from presenters on their experience with our organization. Your input will help us improve our presenter services and provide better experiences for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out the "Presenter Information" section:
	* Enter your First Name.
	* Enter your Last Name.
	* Enter your Email (if you have one).
2. For the "Seminar Details" section:
	* Enter the name of your Company.
	* Enter your Job Title.
	* Enter your feedback, suggestions, and comments about your experience.
	* Rate your experience (1-5) using the Rating Scale.
	* Enter the Seminar Date and Time.
	* Enter your Presenter Name.
3. Please provide additional information in the "Additional Comments" field if you have any further feedback or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your professional email address (if you have one).
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): The name of your company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your job title.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your experience and feedback about our services.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share your suggestions on how we can improve.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience (1-5).
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the rating scale for your experience (One, Two, Three, Four, Five).
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, date, optional): The date of the seminar.
* **Seminar Time** (`seminar_time`, time, optional): The time of the seminar.
* **Presenter Name** (`presenter_name`, text, optional): The name of the presenter.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department of the presenter.
* **Team** (`team`, text, optional): The team of the presenter.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Your contact information (phone number or email address).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): The name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): The date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): The time the form was submitted.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Comments about your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Further comments or questions about your experience.
