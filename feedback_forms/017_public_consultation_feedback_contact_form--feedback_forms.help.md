# public_consultation_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect public consultation feedback from users. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the user's experience and suggestions, allowing us to improve our public consultations. Please note that some fields are required, marked with an asterisk (\*). Failure to fill in these fields will prevent your feedback from being submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*) with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide a clear and detailed message in the "Message" field.
3. Select the relevant "Project" and "Initiative" options that best describe your feedback.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.
