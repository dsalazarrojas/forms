# Mockup Design Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from stakeholders on a mockup design. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the design you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter your name as the stakeholder providing feedback.
3. Rate the usability of the design (1-5).
4. Provide any comments or suggestions you have on the design.
5. Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
6. If desired, provide comments for the design team to refine the concept.
7. If desired, provide comments for the developer to address technical issues.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mockup Title (Optional)**: Enter a brief title for the design.
* **Stakeholder Name (Required)**: Enter your name as the stakeholder providing feedback.
* **Usability Rating (Required)**: Rate the design's usability using a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments (Optional)**: Provide any comments or suggestions you have on the design.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone (Optional)**: Enter your phone number (not required for feedback).
* **Comments for Design Team (Optional)**: Provide actionable comments for the design team to refine the concept.
* **Comments for Developer (Optional)**: Provide technical comments or questions for the developer to address.

Note that fields with a "(Required)" label are mandatory. Please ensure you fill them out before submitting the form. 

## Tips
- Please provide specific, actionable comments that can help the design team improve the design.
- Please do not provide general feedback or comments that are not specific to the design.
- If you are unsure about a field, feel free to ask and we will be happy to clarify.
