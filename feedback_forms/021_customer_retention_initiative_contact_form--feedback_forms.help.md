# Customer Retention Initiative Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Retention Initiative Contact Form is designed to gather information from customers who are at risk of not meeting certain customer retention metrics. This form is meant to identify the reasons why a customer is at risk and collect relevant data to help improve customer retention initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the `customer_name` field.
2. Provide the customer's email address in the `customer_email` field.
3. Select all the reasons why the customer is at risk from the `contact_reasons` field.
4. If applicable, enter the date the customer was at risk in the `at_risk_date` field.
5. If applicable, enter the time the customer was at risk in the `at_risk_time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name (<code>customer_name</code>)
* The customer's name is required to identify the individual customer.

### Customer Email (<code>customer_email</code>)
* The customer's email address is required to contact the customer for follow-up.

### Reasons Why Customer is at Risk (<code>contact_reasons</code>)
* Select all the reasons why the customer is at risk of not meeting customer retention metrics.

### At Risk Date (<code>at_risk_date</code>)
* Enter the date when the customer was at risk of not meeting customer retention metrics.

### At Risk Time (<code>at_risk_time</code>)
* Enter the time when the customer was at risk of not meeting customer retention metrics.

## Tips
* Please only submit this form for customers who are at risk of not meeting customer retention metrics.
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
