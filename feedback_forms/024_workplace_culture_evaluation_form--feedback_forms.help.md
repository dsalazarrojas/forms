# Workplace Culture Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Culture Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback on the workplace culture, including job satisfaction, work-life balance, communication style, and team cohesion. This form helps management identify areas of improvement and ensure a positive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Introduction" field, which provides a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
2. Scroll down to the "department_name" field and enter the name of your department or team.
3. In the "job_title" field, enter your job title or role.
4. In the "job_description" field, describe your job responsibilities and expectations.
5. Answer the questions regarding your job satisfaction, work-life balance, and communication style by selecting the corresponding options.
6. Choose whether you experience team cohesion within your team.
7. Provide any additional feedback in the "feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **Department Name**: Enter the name of your department or team to help management identify specific areas for improvement.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or role to provide context for your feedback.
* **Job Description**: Describe your job responsibilities and expectations to help management understand your role.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Indicate if you are satisfied or dissatisfied with your job, which helps management gauge job happiness.
* **Work-Life Balance**: Answer whether your work-life balance is satisfactory or not, which informs management on work schedule and demands.
* **Communication Style**: Select how you prefer to communicate with your colleagues and management, which helps tailor communication approaches to suit your needs.
* **Team Cohesion**: Choose whether you experience a sense of unity and cooperation within your team, which helps management foster better teamwork.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional comments, suggestions, or concerns you may have about the workplace culture.
