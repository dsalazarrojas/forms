# Masterclass Support And Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Masterclass Support And Feedback Form is intended to be used by customers to provide feedback or support on their masterclass experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name, email, and phone number to help our support team contact you.
2. Describe the issue you are experiencing with your masterclass.
3. If relevant, attach any additional files or documentation to support your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (`customer name`, text, optional):* Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email (`customer email`, email, optional):* Please enter your email address that you check for communication from our support team.
* **Customer Phone (`customer phone`, text, optional):* If you have a preferred phone number to reach you, please enter it here.
* **Issue Description (`issue description`, note, optional):* Describe the issue you are experiencing with your masterclass, including any relevant details or steps leading up to the issue.
* **Attachment (`attachment`, text, optional):* If you have any additional files or documentation that support your feedback, please attach them here.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise in your issue description to help our support team understand and resolve the issue quickly.
* If you have any supporting files, such as screenshots or error logs, please attach them to this form.
* If you have any further questions or concerns after receiving a response from our support team, please do not hesitate to follow up.
