# Passenger Service Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Passenger Service Feedback Request Form is a tool used to gather feedback from passengers about their recent travel experience. This form helps us to identify areas of improvement and ensure that our service meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the "Form Page 1" tab to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "General Feedback" field with your overall experience.
3. Select "Issues?" on "Form Page 2" if you had any problems during your journey.
4. Choose the rating of your experience in the "Service Rating" field on "Form Page 2".
5. Enter your rating value in the "Rating Value" field on "Form Page 2".
6. In the "Passenger Name" field on "Form Page 3", enter your name as it appears on your travel documents.
7. Enter your "Contact Info" (email address) on "Form Page 3", so we can contact you about your feedback.
8. Enter your "Phone" number on "Form Page 3" for additional contact information.
9. Select the "Passenger Service Type" (Bus, Train, Flight) on "Form Page 3".
10. Enter the "Route Number" on "Form Page 4".
11. Enter the "Route" (e.g. "New York to Chicago") on "Form Page 4".
12. Finally, select the "Route" on "Form Page 4" (this field is a duplicate, but it seems to be a required field).
13. Select the "Form Page 3" on "Form Page 4" (another duplicate field).
14. Enter the "Route ID" on "Form Page 4".
15. Finally, provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field on "Form Page 4", if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Feedback** (Form Page 1): Enter your overall experience with our service.
* **Issues?** (Form Page 2): If you had any problems during your journey, select the corresponding option.
* **Service Rating** (Form Page 2): Choose a rating for your experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Rating Value** (Form Page 2): Enter a value for your rating (e.g., 1-5).
* **Passenger Name** (Form Page 3): Enter your name as it appears on your travel documents.
* **Contact Info** (Form Page 3): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.
* **Phone** (Form Page 3): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.
* **Passenger Service Type** (Form Page 3): Select the type of service you used (Bus, Train, Flight).
* **Route Number** (Form Page 4): Enter the number of your route.
* **Route** (Form Page 4): Enter the name of your route (e.g., "New York to Chicago").
* **Route ID** (Form Page 4): Enter the ID of your route.
* **Form Page 3** (Form Page 4): Select the "Form Page 3" option (this field is required, but seems to be a duplicate).
* **Additional Comments** (Form Page 4): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
