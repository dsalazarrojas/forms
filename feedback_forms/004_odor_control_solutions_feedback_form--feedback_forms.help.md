# Odor Control Solutions Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with odor control solutions. The goal is to gather information about the effectiveness, performance, and user experience with these products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Information section with your name and contact details.
2. Provide feedback about your experience with odor control solutions in the Feedback section.
3. Rate the performance of the odor control solutions in the Product Performance section.
4. Select the manufacturers, facilities, and service providers that you have used.
5. Choose a follow-up option to indicate your next steps.
6. Confirm the submission status of your feedback.
7. Select any additional follow-up options and provide a submission ID if required.

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This section is used to collect your name and contact details.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the odor control solutions.
* **Product Performance** (`performance`, number, optional): Rate the performance of the odor control solutions on a scale of 1-10.
* **User Experience** (`user_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall user experience with the odor control solutions.

	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submission Date/Time** (`submission_date_time`, date, optional): This field is not visible to users.
* **Submission Comment** (`submission_comment`, note, optional): This field is not visible to users.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more manufacturers that you have used:

	+ Product 1
