# reactivation_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The reactivation feedback survey is a tool to gather information from non-reactivated customers. It helps us understand why customers didn't re-reactivate and what we can do to improve our product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review each question carefully and select the answers that best fit your experience.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an \* \*. If a field is optional, feel free to answer or skip it as you see fit.
*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Reactivation Feedback Survey**: A note section providing an introduction to the form and a brief explanation of its purpose.
*   *question1*: What was the main reason you didn't re-reactivate? Please select one of the provided options that best describes your experience.
*   *question2*: What would make you more likely to re-reactivate? Select all options that apply to your experience.
*   *question3*: How did you hear about this product/service? Please select the option that best describes where you found out about it.
*   *question4*: What would make you recommend this product/ service? Select all options that apply to your experience.
*   *question5*: Have you previously used this product/ service? Please select one of the provided options that best describes your experience.
*   *question6*: How satisfied are you with this product/ service? Please select one of the provided options that best describes your satisfaction level.
*   *question7*: How satisfied are you with the overall value? Please select one of the provided options that best describes your satisfaction level.
*   *question3* and *question4* are meant to gather information on how you heard about and what would make you recommend this product/service.
*   *question5* and *question6* are meant to gather information on your previous experience and satisfaction level with the product/service.
*   *question7* is meant to gather information on your satisfaction level with the overall value of the product/service.
