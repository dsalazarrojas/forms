# Course Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experiences with a particular course. The form is used to gather information that can help instructors and course administrators improve the course content, structure, and overall learning experience. Please complete this form only if you are a student who has taken or is currently taking the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and click the "Submit" button at the end. You can navigate through the form pages by clicking on the navigation buttons at the top or by using the "Next" and "Previous" buttons. Make sure to complete each page before moving to the next.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course ID** (`course_id`, Number, Required): This field is not actually used to collect a course ID, but rather to provide an empty field to be completed by the form designer. It is required, but you don't have to put anything in it. 
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or any other name you would like to be associated with your feedback.
* **Question One** (`question_one`, Text, Optional): This is a text field for you to provide any initial thoughts or comments about the course.
* **Question Two** (`question_two`, Text, Optional): This is another text field for you to provide additional thoughts or comments about the course.
* **Question Three** (`question_three`, Text, Optional): Similar to Question One and Question Two, use this field to provide more feedback about the course.
* **Question Four** (`question_four`, Text, Optional): Another text field for you to provide more thoughts or comments about the course.
* **Question Five** (`question_five`, Text, Optional): This is the last text field for you to provide any final thoughts or comments about the course.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Note, Required): This is the final step to submit your feedback. You must click this button to complete the form. 

Note: The fields for questions one through five are intended to collect free-form text comments from students. While they are marked as "optional", it's best to provide as much feedback as you can to help us improve the course.
