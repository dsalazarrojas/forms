# Consumer Behavior Patterns Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist you in completing the Consumer Behavior Patterns Survey. The survey aims to collect information on your demographics, shopping habits, and preferences to help businesses and researchers understand consumer behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your Respondent ID, if available. This is optional and will help us identify your responses.
2. Select your age group from the provided options.
3. Choose your gender from the given options.
4. Select your household income range.
5. Specify your highest level of education completed.
6. Indicate your current employment status.
7. Choose how often you shop.
8. Select where you mostly shop.
9. Choose the online platforms you use for shopping.
10. Select the factors that influence your purchase decisions.
11. Indicate your level of brand loyalty.
12. Choose where you spend most of your money.
13. Select the typical amount you spend on transactions.
14. Choose your preferred payment method.
15. Rate the impact of promotions on your purchasing habits.
16. Assess the influence of social media on your purchases.
17. Rate the importance of eco-friendliness.
18. Indicate how often you return items.
19. Rate the importance of customer service.
20. Share any additional shopping preferences or suggestions for improvement.
21. List your favorite brands, if any.
22. Provide suggestions on how businesses can improve their services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent ID** (`respondent_id`, text, optional): An optional identification number to help us identify your responses.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): The date you completed the survey.
* **Age Range** (`age_range`, select_one, required): Select your age group: Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender: Male, Female, Non-Binary, Prefer Not to Say, Other.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, select_one, optional): Select your household income range: Under $25,000, $25,000-$49,999, $50,000-$74,999, $75,000-$99,999, $100,000-$149,999, $150,000+.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, optional): Select your highest education level: High School or Less, Some College, Associate Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Doctorate, Other.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current employment status: Employed Full-Time, Employed Part-Time, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Student, Retired, Other.
* **Shopping Frequency** (`shopping_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you shop: Daily, Several Times a Week, Weekly, 2-3 Times a Month, Once a Month, Less Than Once a Month.
* **Shopping Channel** (`preferred_shopping_channel`, select_one, required): Choose where you mostly shop: Online Only, In-Store Only, Mostly Online, Mostly In-Store, Equal Mix of Both.
* **Online Platforms Used** (`online_platforms_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select the online platforms you use for shopping: Amazon, eBay, Walmart, Target, Brand Websites, Social Media Shops, Etsy, Other.
* **Factors Influencing Purchases** (`purchase_decision_factors`, select_multiple, required): Select the factors that influence your purchase decisions: Price, Quality, Brand Reputation, Reviews, Recommendations, Convenience, Sustainability, Promotions, Product Features, Availability.
* **Brand Loyalty** (`brand_loyalty`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of brand loyalty: Very Loyal, Somewhat Loyal, Neutral, Switch Frequently, Always Looking for Deals.
* **Spending Category** (`spending_category_primary`, select_one, required): Choose where you spend most of your money: Groceries, Clothing, Electronics, Home Goods, Entertainment, Dining, Health and Beauty, Other.
* **Average Transaction Amount** (`average_transaction_amount`, select_one, required): Select the typical amount you spend per purchase: Under $25, $25-$50, $51-$100, $101-$200, $201-$500, Over $500.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Digital Wallet, Buy Now Pay Later, Check.
* **Promotion Response** (`promotional_response`, select_one, required): Rate the impact of promotions on your purchasing habits: Always Look for Deals, Often Use Promotions, Sometimes Use Promotions, Rarely Use Promotions, Never Use Promotions.
* **Social Media Influence** (`social_media_influence`, select_one, required): Assess the influence of social media on your purchases: Very Influential, Somewhat Influential, Neutral, Not Very Influential, Not Influential at All.
* **Sustainability Importance** (`sustainability_importance`, select_one, required): Rate the importance of eco-friendliness: Very Important, Important, Neutral, Not Very Important, Not Important.
* **Return Frequency** (`return_frequency`, select_one, required): Indicate how often you return items: Very Often, Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never.
* **Customer Service Importance** (`customer_service_importance`, select_one, required): Rate the importance of customer service: Very Important, Important, Neutral, Not Very Important, Not Important.
* **Shopping Preferences** (`shopping_preferences`, text, optional): Share any additional shopping preferences or suggestions for improvement.
* **Favorite Brands** (`favorite_brands`, text, optional): List your favorite brands, if any.
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Provide suggestions on how businesses can improve their services.
