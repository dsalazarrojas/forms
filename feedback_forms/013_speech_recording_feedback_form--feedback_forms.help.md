# Speech Recording Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speech Recording Feedback Form is a tool for users to provide feedback on their experience with speech recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the speaker's name and any other relevant details about the person who made the recording.
2. Describe your experience with the recording, including any positive or negative aspects.
3. Offer an evaluation of the recording, including any comments or criticisms.
4. Share any suggestions for improvement, if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker**: Enter the name of the person who made the recording.
* **Recording**: Provide a brief description of the recording, including any notable features or issues.
* **Evaluation**: Write a short review of the recording, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Suggestion**: If you have any ideas for improvement, enter them here.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your experience.
* If you're unsure about what to write, just fill out as much of the form as you can, and we'll take it from there.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
