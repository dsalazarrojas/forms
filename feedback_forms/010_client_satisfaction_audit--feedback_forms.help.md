# Client Satisfaction Audit - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Satisfaction Audit is a form designed to collect feedback from clients about their experience with your company's services. The goal is to assess areas of strength and weakness, identify opportunities for improvement, and gauge overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are the authorized representative from your team who is completing the form.
2. Complete the form within 24 hours of the audit.
3. Fill out the form for each Client Satisfaction Audit.
4. Be honest and provide accurate information about your experience with the company's services.
5. If you have any additional feedback or concerns, please include them in the "Any additional feedback or concerns?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name or Company**: Enter the name of the client company or the name of the individual representative.
* **Your Name**: Enter the name of the person completing the form.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person completing the form.
* **Date of Audit**: Enter the date of the Client Satisfaction Audit.
* **How long have you been a client?**: Select the length of time you have been a client from the options provided.
* **Client Account Type**: Select the type of client account you have with the company.
* **Product or Service Quality Rating - 1 to 10**: Rate the quality of the company's products or services from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Our products/services meet your business needs**: Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **Are there features or capabilities you need that we do not offer?**: If there are any features or capabilities you feel are missing, please list them here.
* **What product improvements would increase your satisfaction?**: If there are any specific improvements you would like to see, please list them here.
* **Overall Service Quality Rating - 1 to 10**: Rate the quality of the company's services from 1 to 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **How responsive is our customer support team?**: Select how responsive you feel the customer support team is.
* **Our support team resolves issues effectively**: Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **What service improvements would enhance your experience?**: If there are any specific improvements you would like to see, please list them here.
* **Our pricing is fair relative to the value provided**: Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **Rate satisfaction with return on investment - 1 to 10**: Rate your satisfaction with the return on investment of the company's services from 1 to 10.
* **Our pricing structure is transparent and clear**: Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **How would you rate your account management experience?**: Select the level of satisfaction with the account management experience.
* **Communication with your account team is adequate**: Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **Our team understands your business and goals**: Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **How likely are you to recommend us to others?**: Enter a number rating of how likely you are to recommend the company to others.
* **Overall, how loyal are you to our company?**: Select how strongly you agree or disagree with the statement.
* **Any additional feedback or concerns?**: If you have any additional feedback or concerns, please include them here.
