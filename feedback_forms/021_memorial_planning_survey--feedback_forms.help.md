# Memorial Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Memorial Planning Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from users on memorial planning, allowing us to understand their preferences and needs for this service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Review the available options on each page carefully.
2.  Answer each question based on your preferences.
3.  Ensure all required fields are completed.
4.  Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Memorial Planning Survey** (`1`, `text`, `false`): This is the title of the survey, and you can ignore it as you complete the form.
*   **Contact Information** (`2`, `select_one`, `true`): Select your first name, last name, phone number, or email address to provide your contact information.
    *   First Name: Enter your first name.
    *   Last Name: Enter your last name.
    *   Phone: Enter your phone number.
    *   Email: Enter your email address.
*   **Memorial Preferences** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select the memorial preferences that you would like to include in the service.
    *   Preferred Service Location: Choose the location where you would like the memorial service to take place.
    *   Preferred Casket Type: Choose the type of casket you would like.
    *   Preferred Urn Type: Choose the type of urn you would like.
    *   Preferred Cremation Urn Type: Choose the type of cremation urn you would like.
*   **Memorial Services** (`4`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select the services that you would like to include in the memorial service.
    *   'Yes': Choose this option if you want to include memorial services.
*   **Funeral Preferences** (`5`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select the funeral preferences that you would like to include.
    *   'Yes': Choose this option if you want to include funeral services.
*   **Memorial Preferences 2** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `false`): This field is not required and you can ignore it.
*   **Contact Details** (`7`, `select_one`, `true`): Select whether you would like to provide your contact details.
    *   'Yes': Choose this option if you want to provide your contact details.
*   **Submit** (`8`, `note`, `false`): This is a submit button and you can ignore it as it is not required.
*   **Confirm Submission** (`9`, `select_one`, `false`): Confirm your submission before finalizing it.
    *   'Yes': Choose this option to confirm your submission.
    *   'No': Choose this option if you do not want to confirm your submission.
