# natural-overlay-feedback-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the natural overlay feature. The purpose of this form is to help us understand user's opinion on the feature's functionality, usability, and overall performance. By collecting user feedback, we can identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the feature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the rating of your satisfaction with the natural overlay feature from the options provided.
2. Provide a brief description of your feedback about the feature.
3. Specify the type of issue you encountered while using the feature.
4. Share any suggestions you have for improving the feature.
5. Choose the type of device and product you were using when experiencing the issue.
6. Select the type of feedback you are providing (e.g., product, UX, or support).
7. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
8. Add any additional comments about your experience.
9. Finally, sign and date your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate our natural overlay feature (Very good, Fair, Bad)**: Rate your satisfaction with the natural overlay feature using the provided options.
* **Feedback**: Provide a brief description of your feedback about the feature.
* **Issue**: Specify the type of issue you encountered while using the feature.
* **Suggestion**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the feature.
* **Device type (Mobile, Desktop, Other)**: Choose the type of device you were using when experiencing the issue.
* **Product type (Mobile, Desktop, Other)**: Choose the type of product you were using when experiencing the issue.
* **Feedback on (Product, UX, Support)**: Select the type of feedback you are providing (e.g., product, UX, or support).
* **How would you rate your satisfaction (Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied, Very unsatisfied)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the feature.
* **Issue description**: Provide a brief description of the issue you encountered.
* **Issue replica**: Provide a brief description of the issue you encountered (if needed).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comment**: Add any additional comments about your experience.
* **Attach files (optional)**: If you have any attachment, you can upload it here.
* **Signature**: Enter your name.
* **Signature date**: Enter the date of your signature.
* **Signature time**: Enter the time of your signature.
* **Signature verified (True, False)**: Check if your signature has been verified by a designated person.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Job title**: Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Department**: Enter your department name (if applicable).
* **Job**: Enter your current job name (if applicable).

Note: Fields marked as required are denoted by an asterisk (\*) and must be filled out.
