# Spa Facility Comfort Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from spa facility visitors on their comfort experiences during their visit. It aims to gather insights on various aspects of the facility's comfort, such as temperature, seating, beds, pillows, towels, and overall comfort.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select the correct ratings for each question based on your experience.
3. Be sure to provide specific examples or details when answering questions like "What was the most uncomfortable part of your visit?" to help us improve our services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Required): This section is a brief introduction to the survey. Please read it carefully and proceed to answer the questions.
* **What type of spa visit are you on?** (Optional): Please select the type of visit you had, such as a regular visit or a special occasion like a wedding or birthday.
* **How would you rate your overall comfort?** (Required): Rate your overall comfort experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the least comfortable and 5 is the most comfortable.
* **What was your impression of the ambiance?** (Optional): This is an open-ended question where you can provide any thoughts or feelings you had about the ambiance during your visit.
* **How would you rate the temperature?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Select the temperature that best describes your experience. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the seating?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the seating was during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the bed?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the bed was during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the pillows?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the pillows were during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the towels?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the towels were during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the robes?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the robes were during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the slippers?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the slippers were during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the blanket?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the blanket was during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the pillow?** (Optional): Select how comfortable the pillow was during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **How would you rate the comfort of the blanket and pillow?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Select how comfortable both the blanket and pillow were during your visit. Options include "Good" and "Fair".
* **What was the most uncomfortable part of your visit?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Please select the most uncomfortable part of your visit and provide specific details or examples.
* **Is there anything we could have improved?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Please provide any suggestions or thoughts on how we could improve our services.
* **Have you visited us before?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Select if you have visited us before.
* **Do you visit frequently?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Select if you visit us frequently.
* **How many visits do you make per year?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Select the frequency of your visits.
* **What do you like most about our facility?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Select what you like most about our facility.
* **What do you dislike most about our facility?** (Optional, Multiple Selection): Select what you dislike most about our facility.
* **Would you recommend our facility to a friend?** (Optional): Select if you would recommend our facility to a friend.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (Optional, Text Area): Provide any suggestions or thoughts on how we could improve our services.
* **Would you like to receive updates from us?** (Optional): Select if you would like to receive updates from us.
* **Do you consent to our use of this data?** (Optional): Select if you consent to our use of this data.

Note: Please answer all questions honestly and provide specific details when requested. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services.
