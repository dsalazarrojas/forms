# Employee Team Collaboration Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Team Collaboration Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from team members on the quality of team collaboration and performance. This form will help team leaders and managers make informed decisions to improve team dynamics and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the team name from the provided options.
*   Choose how you rate the quality of communication within the team.
*   Rate the effectiveness of meetings.
*   Rate how respectful team members are to each other's opinions and ideas.
*   Rate how open to change and improvement the team is.
*   Choose whether team decisions are made collaboratively.
*   Rate whether the team's work is organized in a way that minimizes conflicts.
*   Rate your satisfaction with the team's performance.
*   Rate your satisfaction with the team's communication style.
*   Provide any suggestions for team improvement.
*   Enter the team member's name, email, and position.
*   Choose how you would describe your role as a reviewer.
*   Enter your name, email, and position.
*   Choose the feedback submission date and time.

*   **Team Name** (`team_name`): Select the name of your team from the provided options.
*   **Team Collaboration Feedback** (`feedback_form_1`): Rate the quality of communication within your team.
*   **What is the quality of communication within the team?** (`question_1`): Choose how you rate the quality of communication within the team.
*   **Are meetings organized and led effectively?** (`question_2`): Rate the effectiveness of meetings within your team.
*   **Do team members respect each other's opinions and ideas?** (`question_3`): Rate how respectful team members are to each other's opinions and ideas.
*   **Is the team open to change and improvement?** (`question_4`): Rate how open to change and improvement the team is.
*   **Are team decisions made collaboratively?** (`question_5`): Choose whether team decisions are made collaboratively.
*   **Is the team's work organized in a way that minimizes conflicts?** (`question_6`): Rate whether the team's work is organized in a way that minimizes conflicts.
*   **How satisfied are you with your team's performance?** (`question_7`): Rate your satisfaction with the team's performance.
*   **How satisfied are you with the team's communication style?** (`question_8`): Rate your satisfaction with the team's communication style.
*   **Do you have any suggestions for team improvement?** (`suggestion`): Provide any suggestions for team improvement.
*   **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`): Enter your team member name.
*   **Team Member Email** (`team_member_email`): Enter your team member email.
*   **Team Member Position** (`team_member_position`): Enter your team member position.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter your manager's name.
*   **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter your manager's email.
*   **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Choose how you would describe your role as a reviewer.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
*   Your feedback will help team leaders and managers make informed decisions to improve team collaboration and performance.
*   The information gathered will be used internally within the team, so please keep your feedback confidential.
*   If you have any suggestions for team improvement, please provide them in the "Do you have any suggestions for team improvement?" field.
