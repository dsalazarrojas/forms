**User Feedback Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The User Feedback Form is a tool for users to provide feedback and ratings about our service or product. This form helps us understand your experience and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please select the "User Information" page**.
2. Enter your **User Information** in the designated fields.
3. Select the desired **Overall Rating** on the scale of 1-5.
4. Provide your **Feedback** about your experience.
5. If you want to contact us, you can enter your **Email** and/or **Contact Phone**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information)**: Enter your name, username, or any other relevant identifier to help us understand who you are.
* **Overall Rating (Overall Rating)**: Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Feedback (Feedback)**: Share your thoughts, comments, and suggestions about your experience. This is a free-text field where you can type in as much as you'd like.
* **Email (Email)**: If you want to contact us directly, enter your email address here. We'll use this to follow up with you if needed.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter your phone number if you want to contact us through phone. This is optional, but helps us to reach out to you quickly if you need assistance.
