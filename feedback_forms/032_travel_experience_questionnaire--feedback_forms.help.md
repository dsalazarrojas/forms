# Travel Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This travel experience questionnaire is used to collect feedback about your recent trip. Please fill it out accurately and honestly to provide valuable insights to improve future travel experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read each question carefully** and answer accordingly.
2. **Fill out every required field** (marked as required in this guide).
3. **Submit your feedback** after completing all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please provide a brief introduction to your trip, including the destination, duration, and any notable events or activities you participated in.
* **Trip Details**: Please describe your trip, including the type of transportation, accommodation, and any other relevant details.
* **Accommodations**: Please select the type of accommodation(s) you used during your trip (e.g., hotel, Airbnb, hostel, etc.). Choose all that apply.
* **Overall Satisfaction** and **Trip Satisfaction**: Rate your overall and trip satisfaction on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your trip.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Use specific details to describe your experiences.
* If you have any concerns or issues with your trip, please mention them in the comments section.
