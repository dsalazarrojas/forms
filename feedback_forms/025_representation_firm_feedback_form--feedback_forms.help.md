# Representation Firm Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Representation Firm Feedback Form is a tool designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with a representation firm. This feedback is crucial in helping the firm to understand its performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter a title that describes the reason for your feedback.
2. Rate the firm's overall quality using the firm_rating field.
3. Enter the name of the representation firm you are providing feedback about.
4. Enter your experience with the firm in the experience field.
5. Rate the firm's service quality in the service_rating field.
6. Provide any comments or concerns you have about the firm's services using the feedback field.
7. Enter your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up.
8. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the comments field.
9. Indicate whether your submission is new or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. form_title** (`form_title`, required, text): Enter a title that describes the reason for your feedback.
- **2. firm_rating** (`firm_rating`, required, number): Rate the firm's overall quality from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
- **3. firm_name** (`firm_name`, optional, text): Enter the name of the representation firm you are providing feedback about.
- **4. experience** (`experience`, optional, date): Enter the date of your interaction with the firm.
- **5. service_rating** (`service_rating`, required, number): Rate the firm's service quality from 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
- **6. feedback** (`feedback`, required, text): Provide any comments or concerns you have about the firm's services.
- **7. email** (`email`, required, email): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for follow-up.
- **8. phone** (`phone`, optional, text): Enter any additional comments or notes you have about the firm.
- **9. assigned_user** (`assigned_user`, optional, select): Select the user who was assigned to your case, if applicable.
- **10. submitted** (`submitted`, required, select): Indicate whether your submission is new or not.
- **11. comments** (`comments`, optional, select multiple): Select any additional comments or notes you have about the firm's services.
- **12. submit** (`submit`, optional, note): Once you have completed the form, click this button to submit your feedback.
