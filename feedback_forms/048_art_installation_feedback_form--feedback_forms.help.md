# Art Installation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Installation Feedback Form is designed to gather information from visitors about their experiences with the art installations at the art center. Completing this form will help us understand how engaging the installations are and what improvements we can make to create a better experience for all visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Visitor name** in the first field. This is an optional field, and you can leave it blank if you prefer not to share your name.
2. In the second field, enter the **Installation name** or title that you visited. This is a required field, as it helps us understand which installation you are providing feedback on.
3. In the third field, select the **Visit date** of your visit using the calendar tool. This is a required field, as it helps us track the timing of your visit.
4. Choose your **Engagement level** by selecting one of the four options: Very engaging, Somewhat engaging, Neutral, or Not engaging. This will help us understand how much you enjoyed the installation.
5. If you experienced any **Accessibility issues**, please enter a description of the issue in this field. This will help us identify areas for improvement.
6. Provide feedback on your **Sensory experience** in this field. If you have any comments or suggestions about the installation's audio, lighting, or interactivity, please share them here.
7. In the **Learning outcome** field, describe what you learned from the installation. This will help us understand how the installation's content and design impact our visitors.
8. Finally, indicate whether you would **Recommend to others** by selecting True or False. This is a required field, as it helps us understand how our visitors perceive the installation.
9. If you have any **Suggestions** for how we can improve the installation, please share them in this field. This is an optional field, and your feedback will help us make changes for future visitors.
10. If you would like to be contacted for a follow-up conversation, please enter your email address in the **Contact for follow up** field. This is an optional field, and we will only use it to follow up with you about your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor name**: A field to enter your name, which is optional. If you prefer not to share your name, you can leave it blank.
* **Installation name**: A required field to enter the name or title of the installation you visited. This helps us understand which installation you are providing feedback on.
* **Visit date**: A required field to select the date of your visit. This helps us track the timing of your visit.
* **Engagement level**: A field to choose how engaging the installation was for you, with options: Very engaging, Somewhat engaging, Neutral, or Not engaging.
* **Accessibility experience**: If you experienced any accessibility issues, please describe them here.
* **Sensory experience**: Provide feedback on your sensory experience with the installation, including comments on the audio, lighting, and interactivity.
* **Learning outcome**: Describe what you learned from the installation, which helps us understand how the content and design impact our visitors.
* **Recommend to others**: Indicate whether you would recommend visiting the installation, with options: True or False.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions for how we can improve the installation in this field.
* **Contact for follow up**: If you would like to be contacted for a follow-up conversation, enter your email address here.
