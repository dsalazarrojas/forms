# Bracelet Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with the Bracelet product. The goal is to understand how customers perceive the product's quality, value, and overall satisfaction. This survey aims to help the company improve the product and provide better customer experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Your Name (field 2)
	* Quality Rating (field 10)
	* Product Quality Rating (field 11)
	* Value for Price Rating (field 12)
	* Comfort Rating (field 13)
	* Are you satisfied with the design? (field 15)
	* Would you recommend this Bracelet? (field 18)
	* Would you purchase from us again? (field 21)
2. Provide feedback on the following:
	* Which style of Bracelet did you purchase? (field 6)
	* What is the material type of the Bracelet? (field 8)
	* Color Matches Product Images (field 16)
	* Suggestions for Improvement (field 19)
	* Additional Comments (field 20)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (field 2)** (`text`, Optional): Provide your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address (field 3)** (`email`, Optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **When Did You Purchase This Bracelet (field 4)** (`date`, Optional): Provide the date of your purchase.
* **Bracelet Style (field 6)** (`text`, Optional): Which style of Bracelet did you purchase? (e.g. leather, silver, gemstone)
* **Bracelet Color (field 7)** (`text`, Optional): What is the color of the Bracelet you purchased?
* **Material Type (field 8)** (`select_one`, Optional): What is the material type of the Bracelet you purchased? (e.g. Sterling silver, Gold, Beaded, Leather, Fabric, Gemstone, Mixed materials, Other)
* **Product Quality Rating (field 10)** (`select_one`, Required): How would you rate the quality of the Bracelet?
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Value for Price Rating (field 11)** (`select_one`, Required): Was it worth the price?
	+ Excellent value
	+ Good value
	+ Fair value
	+ Below average value
	+ Poor value
* **Comfort Rating (field 13)** (`select_one`, Required): How comfortable is the Bracelet?
	+ Very comfortable
	+ Comfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Uncomfortable
	+ Very uncomfortable
* **Are you satisfied with the design? (field 15)** (`select_one`, Required): Does it look as expected?
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Would you recommend this Bracelet? (field 18)** (`select_one`, Required): Would you suggest to others?
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Uncertain
	+ Probably no
	+ Definitely no
* **Would you purchase from us again? (field 21)** (`select_one`, Required): Likelihood of repeat purchase?
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Uncertain
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very unlikely

## Tips

* Be honest and provide feedback based on your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
