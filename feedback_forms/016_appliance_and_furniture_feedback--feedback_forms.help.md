# Appliance And Furniture Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your experience with the appliances and furniture you have purchased. Your input will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your customer information (name and email address).
* Select the product you purchased from the list.
* Enter the model/brand of the product.
* Provide the date of purchase.
* If you have a receipt or order number, please enter it.
* Answer the questions about your overall satisfaction with the product, product quality, value for money, and durability.
* Rate your satisfaction with the delivery and setup experience.
* Check if you encountered any issues with the product and describe them, if applicable.
* Finally, let us know if you would recommend this product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address as it is registered with us.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number for follow-up, please enter it.
* **What product did you purchase?** (`product_purchased`, select_one, required): Select the product you purchased from the options provided.
* **Product Model/Brand** (`product_model`, text, required): Please enter the model/brand of the product you purchased.
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you purchased the product.
* **Receipt or Order Number** (`receipt_number`, text, optional): If you have a receipt or order number, please enter it.
* **Overall satisfaction with the product** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the product from the provided options.
* **Product Quality** (`product_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the product quality from the provided options.
* **Value for money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Choose how you perceive the value for the money from the provided options.
* **Durability** (`durability`, select_one, optional): Rate the product durability from the provided options.
* **How does it look compared to the product image?** (`appearance`, select_one, optional): Choose how the product looks compared to the image from the provided options.
* **Have you encountered any issues with the product?** (`issues_encountered`, select_one, required): Check if you have encountered any issues with the product.
* **Please describe any issues** (`issue_description`, text, optional): If you have issues, please describe them in detail.
* **Would you recommend this product?** (`recommendations`, select_one, required): Let us know if you would recommend this product.
* **Additional feedback or suggestions** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide them.
