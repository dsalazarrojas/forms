# Science Center Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Center Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from visitors to help us improve our services and create a more enjoyable and enriching experience for everyone. Your input is invaluable in helping us understand what we do well and what we can do better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the most suitable options for each question, based on your actual experience.
3. Take your time to provide thoughtful and detailed feedback in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`first_name`, Text, Optional)
Enter your first name for reference purposes.

### Last Name (`last_name`, Text, Optional)
Enter your last name for reference purposes.

### Email Address (`email_address`, Email, Optional)
Enter your valid email address to receive our follow-up questions or surveys.

### Contact Number (`contact_number`, Text, Optional)
Enter your contact number (phone or other) for further communication.

### Feedback (`feedback`, Text, Optional)
Provide a detailed description of your experience, including what you liked, disliked, or found confusing.

### Exhibit (`exhibit`, Select One, Optional)
Select the exhibit you visited or interacted with during your visit.

### Program (`program`, Select Multiple, Optional)
Choose the programs (Hands-on activities, Guided tours, or Lectures) you participated in during your visit.

### Overall Experience (`overall_experience`, Date, Optional)
Date of your visit.

### Visit Time (`visit_time`, Time, Optional)
Time of your visit.

### Staff Interaction (`staff_interaction`, Select One, Optional)
Rate your experience with our staff (Very good, Good, Fair, Poor).
