**Form Name:** K 2D Feedback Survey

**Form Purpose:** This form is designed to gather feedback from students, teachers, or parents about their 2D experience. It helps us understand what worked well and what didn't, so we can improve the experience for everyone.

**How To Complete This Form:**

1. Fill in the required fields (marked with an asterisk *)
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the free-text fields (e.g., Feedback, Comments, Additional Comments)
3. Select a rating for your experience
4. Click on the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**
1. **First Name** (text, not required): Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name** (text, not required): Enter your last name.
3. **Email** (email, not required): Enter your email address.
4. **Teacher Name** (text, not required): If you are a teacher, enter your name here.
5. **Department** (text, not required): Enter your department name if you are affiliated with one.
6. **Student ID** (number, not required): If you are a student, enter your student ID number.
7. **Feedback** (text, not required): Describe your experience and any suggestions you have.
8. **Comments** (text, not required): Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
9. **Rating** (select one): Select how you would rate your experience:
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Needs Improvement
10. **Submit** (text, not required): Click this button to send your feedback.
11. **Email** (email, not required): Re-enter your email address for confirmation.
12. **Phone** (text, not required): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
13. **Date** (date, not required): Enter the date of your feedback.
14. **Time** (time, not required): Enter the time of your feedback.
15. **Note** (note, not required): Add any additional notes or details about your feedback.
16. **Department ID** (text, not required): If you are a department, enter your ID number.
17. **Teacher** (text, not required): Enter the name of your teacher.
18. **Parent Name** (text, not required): If you are a parent, enter your name.
19. **Parent Email** (email, not required): Enter your email address if you are a parent.
20. **Parent Phone** (text, not required): Enter your phone number if you are a parent.
21. **Student Name** (text, not required): Enter the name of the student.
22. **Student Email** (email, not required): Enter the email address of the student.
23. **Student Phone** (text, not required): Enter the phone number of the student.
24. **Student ID** (number, not required): Re-enter the student ID number.

**Tips:**

* Please ensure you have already submitted feedback with the same Student ID before filling this form.

* Provide as much detail as possible in the free-text fields.
