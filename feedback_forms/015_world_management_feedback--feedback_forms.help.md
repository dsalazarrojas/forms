# World Management Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the management structure and style of an organization, to help improve its management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out each field in the form as required
* Provide honest and constructive feedback
* Use a clear and concise format for your answers
* Double-check your work before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: World Management Feedback

* **World Management Feedback** (`world_management_feedback_form`, text, required): This is the main field for providing feedback on the world management structure and style.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you are providing feedback on.
* **Management Level** (`management_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following management levels:
	+ Top Management
	+ Middle Management
	+ Line Management
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, number, optional): Enter the evaluation period.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, optional): Enter the evaluation score out of 100.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback you have on the evaluation.
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the organization.
* **Management Style** (`management_style`, text, optional): Enter the management style of the organization.
* **Evaluation Areas** (`evaluation_areas`, text, optional): Enter any specific areas that were evaluated.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, text, optional): Enter the method used for the evaluation.

## Tips

* Be specific and honest in your feedback
* Provide constructive criticism to help improve the organization's management structure and style
* Use clear and concise language in your answers
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form
