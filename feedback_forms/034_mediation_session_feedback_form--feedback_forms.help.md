# Mediation Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mediation Session Feedback Form is designed to gather your thoughts and feelings about a recent mediation session. Your feedback will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Participant Name.
2. Select the Session Date for which you are providing feedback.
3. Share your thoughts on what you enjoyed most about the session (Feedback on Session).
4. Tell us what didn't quite feel right to you (What didn't feel right).
5. Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 10 (Overall Rating).
6. Suggest any improvements for future sessions (Suggest Improvements).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
* **Session Date** (session_date, date, required: false): Select the date you attended the mediation session.
* **Feedback on Session** (feedback_on_session, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on what you enjoyed most about the session. Be as specific as possible so we can learn from your experience.
* **What did you enjoy most?** (what_did_you_enjoy, text, required: false): Describe the aspect of the session that you found most valuable or enjoyable.
* **What didn't quite feel right?** (what_didnt_fell_right, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on what didn't quite feel right or what you would change for future sessions.
* **Overall Rating** (overall_rating, number, required: false): Rate your experience on a scale from 1 to 10. This will help us understand your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **Suggest Improvements** (suggest_improvements, text, required: false): If you have any suggestions or ideas for future improvements, please share them here.
