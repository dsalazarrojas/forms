# frequently_asked_questions_faq - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and inquiries from users in an efficient and user-friendly way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Common Inquiries" field and type your question or feedback.
2. Click on the "Receive Answers" field and type the answer to the question you just submitted.
3. (Optional) If you'd like to submit a new question, click on the "Submit Question" field and type your question. (Note: This field is not required.)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Common Inquiries** (`Common Inquiries`, `text`, **required**): This is the main field where you can type your question or feedback. Please make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your inquiry.
* **Receive Answers** (`Receive Answers`, `text`, **required**): This field is where you can type the answer to the question you just submitted. Please provide a clear and concise answer to the best of your knowledge.
* **Submit Question** (`Submit Question`, `text`, **optional**): This field is optional, but if you'd like to submit a new question, you can type it here. Please note that this field is not required, and you can always submit questions in the "Common Inquiries" field instead.
