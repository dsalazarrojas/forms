# Learner Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Learner Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from learners about their learning experience. This form helps us understand how our teaching methods and materials have met your learning needs, and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed below. You do not need to be an expert in the fields, just answer based on your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Type** (`FeedbackForm`, `OtherForm`, `TestForm`, `ProjectForm`, `ProjectFormFeedback`): Please select the type of feedback you are providing. This will help us understand what kind of feedback you are giving.
* **Rating** (`Rating`): Provide a score between 1-5 to rate your overall learning experience. 1 is very poor, 5 is very good.
* **Comment** (`Comment`): Please provide any additional comments you have about your learning experience. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts.
* **Email** (`Email`): Enter a valid email address to contact you about your feedback. If you do not provide an email address, we will not be able to contact you about your feedback.
* **Contact Number** (`ContactNumber`): Enter a phone number or other contact method where we can reach you. If you do not provide a contact number, we will not be able to contact you about your feedback.
* **Submit** (`Submit`): Once you have completed all fields, click submit to send your feedback to us. 

## Tips
- Please be honest and clear in your feedback. Your input will help us understand your learning needs.
- Make sure to enter valid email and phone numbers.
- You can only fill out this form once per project or test. If you need to provide feedback for multiple projects or tests, please fill out the form once for each.

Note: Fields marked as `required` are fields that need to be completed to submit the form.
