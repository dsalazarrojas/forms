# real_estate_advisor_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "real_estate_advisor_feedback" form is a feedback collection tool for users to provide input about their experience with a real estate advisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below.

1. Choose an option for "real_estate_advisor" to select the name of the real estate advisor you are providing feedback about.
2. Rate the advisor's performance using the "satisfaction_level" options.
3. Provide any comments or feedback in the "comments" field.
4. If you have an email address, provide it in the "feedback_email" field.
5. If you have a phone number, provide it in the "feedback_phone" field.
6. Select the date of your interaction with the advisor.
7. Select the time of your interaction with the advisor.
8. Select the agency of the real estate advisor from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, note, required: false): This form contains questions that will help us understand your experience with the real estate advisor.
* **Rate Advisor** (`advisor_rating`, number, required: false): Please rate the advisor's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **What Did You Think?** (`comments`, text, required: false): Share any thoughts or comments you may have about the advisor's performance.
* **Email** (`feedback_email`, email, required: false): If you have an email address, please provide it so we can contact you for further feedback or clarification.
* **Phone** (`feedback_phone`, text, required: false): If you have a phone number, please provide it so we can contact you for further feedback or clarification.
* **Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required: false): Please select the date of your interaction with the advisor.
* **Time** (`feedback_time`, time, required: false): Please select the time of your interaction with the advisor.
* **Real Estate Advisor** (`real_estate_advisor`, select_one, required: false): Please select the name of the real estate advisor you are providing feedback about.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select all the options that describe your level of satisfaction with the advisor's performance.
* **Real Estate Agency** (`real_estate_agency`, select_one, required: false): Please select the agency that the real estate advisor works for. 

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your feedback. Your input will help us to improve our services.
* Please fill out as much of the form as you can, but it is not required to fill out every question.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
