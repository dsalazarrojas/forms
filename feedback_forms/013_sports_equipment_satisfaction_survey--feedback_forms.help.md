# Sports Equipment Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback about your experience with sports equipment. Your input will help us improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill out the relevant fields as instructed. You can skip pages or questions that are not applicable to your experience. When finished, click submit to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sports Equipment Satisfaction Survey** (Page 1): This is the introduction page with the survey title.
* **What type of equipment did you use?** (Page 2): Select the type of equipment you used (e.g., Treadmill, Stationary Bike, etc.).
* **How would you rate the quality of the equipment?** (Page 3): Rate the quality of the equipment (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **How would you rate the durability of the equipment?** (Page 4): Rate the durability of the equipment (e.g., Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor).
* **Would you recommend using this equipment?** (Page 5): Answer yes, no, or maybe to whether you would recommend using this equipment.
* **Any additional comments?** (Page 6): Enter any other comments or feedback you may have about your experience with the equipment.
* **Your email address** (Page 7): Enter your email address for us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Your phone number** (Page 8): Enter your phone number for us to contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates.
* **Date last used the equipment** (Page 9): Enter the date you last used the equipment.
* **Time used equipment last** (Page 10): Enter the time you last used the equipment.
* **Hours used equipment last** (Page 11): Enter the number of hours you used the equipment last.
* **Equipment name** (Page 12): Enter the name of the equipment you used.
* **Equipment used for** (Page 13): Select the purpose for which you used the equipment (e.g., Physical Fitness, Yoga or Pilates, etc.).

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately to help us improve our services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
* You can skip questions that are not applicable to your experience.
* Enter your email address and phone number if you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or updates.
