# Laboratory Experiment Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with a laboratory experiment. Your input will help instructors evaluate and improve the experiment design and teaching methods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Each question is a rating system, with higher numbers indicating better performance.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) on the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **How would you rate the instructor's availability during the experiment?* (Instructor Availability, Number, Required) 
This question assesses how easily the instructor was available to answer your questions and address your needs during the experiment.

### **How would you rate the instructor's knowledge on the subject matter?* (Instructor Knowledge, Number, Required)
This question evaluates how well-versed the instructor is in the subject matter of the laboratory experiment.

### **How would you rate the instructor's support during the experiment?* (Instructor Support, Number, Required)
This question rates how willing the instructor was to help you with any difficulties you encountered during the experiment.

### **How would you rate the overall organization of the course?* (Overall Organization, Number, Required)
This question measures how well the laboratory experiment was organized and structured, including the flow of activities and the use of resources.

### **How would you rate the presentation of the course materials?* (Course Presentation, Number, Required)
This question assesses the quality and clarity of the course materials, including handouts, demonstrations, and lectures.

### **How would you rate the overall experience with the experiment?* (Overall Experience, Number, Required)
This question is a general evaluation of your experience with the laboratory experiment as a whole, including both positive and negative aspects.

## Tips
- Be honest in your feedback, as it will help instructors improve the lab experiment and your learning experience.
- Make sure to answer all questions to provide a comprehensive evaluation.
- If you need clarification on any question, contact your instructor or the experiment administrator.
