# Provision Management Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Provision Management Survey" is designed to collect information on stock management processes, including types of stock, restocking frequency, stock management methods, challenges faced, and improvement ideas. This will help organizations optimize their stock management practices and improve their overall efficiency and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the purpose and questions asked.
2. Select the relevant options for each question based on your stock management practices.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to the open-ended questions.
4. Ensure that you complete all required fields.
