# Botanical Garden Visitor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors to the botanical garden, allowing us to understand their experiences and improve the garden's offerings.

## How To Complete This Form
Please take a few minutes to complete this form. The process is self-explanatory, but here's a brief step-by-step guide:

* Start by selecting the "Visit Date" field, which is the date of your visit to the garden.
* If you'd like, you can also select an "Arrival Time" to provide more specific details about your visit.
* Choose your "Visitor Type" from the options provided, indicating how often you visit the garden.
* Select your "Group Composition" to describe who you visited with today.
* Enter the "Number in Party" to provide a count of the people in your group.
* If applicable, enter the "Children Count" field to provide information about how many children under 18 are in your group.
* Enter your "ZIP Code" for demographic analysis purposes, if you're willing to share.
* Select your "Primary Purpose of Visit" from the options to describe why you visited the garden today.
* Choose the "How Did You Hear About Us" field to indicate what prompted you to visit the garden.
* Select all applicable "Garden Areas Visited" to provide information about your experience.
* If you have a favorite area, select it in the "Favorite Area" field.
* Rate your "Overall Experience Rating" to provide feedback about your visit.
* Evaluate the "Value for Money" to indicate how you feel about the admission price.
* Rate the "Facilities" and "Staff Helpfulness" to provide feedback about your experience.
* Provide any "Suggestions for Improvement" you may have.
* Indicate your "Likelihood to Return" to gauge your willingness to visit again.
* If interested, select "Membership Interest" to learn more about membership benefits.
* Finally, provide your "Email Address" for follow-up and newsletter purposes, if you'd like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Visit Date (1)**: The date of your visit to the garden.
*   **Arrival Time (2)**: The time you arrived at the garden.
*   **Visitor Type (3)**: How often you visit the garden (First-time visitor, Occasional visitor, etc.)
*   **Group Composition (4)**: Who you visited with (alone, with spouse/partner, etc.)
*   **Number in Party (5)**: The total number of people in your group.
*   **Children Count (6)**: The number of children under 18 in your group. 
*   **ZIP Code (7)**: Your home ZIP code for demographic analysis.
*   **Primary Purpose of Visit (8)**: What brought you to the garden today (leisure, education, etc.).
*   **How Did You Hear About Us (9)**: What source led you to visit the garden (website, social media, etc.).
*   **Garden Areas Visited (10)**: Select all areas you explored during your visit.
*   **Favorite Area (11)**: The area you enjoyed most.
*   **Overall Experience Rating (12)**: Rate your overall experience.
*   **Value for Money (13)**: Evaluate the admission price.
*   **Facilities (14)**: Rate the facilities, including restrooms and pathways.
*   **Staff Helpfulness (15)**: Rate the helpfulness of our staff and volunteers.
*   **Suggestions for Improvement (16)**: Any suggestions for us to improve your experience.
*   **Likelihood to Return (17)**: How likely are you to visit again.
*   **Membership Interest (18)**: If interested, learn more about membership benefits.
*   **Email Address (19)**: Optional - for follow-up and newsletter purposes.
