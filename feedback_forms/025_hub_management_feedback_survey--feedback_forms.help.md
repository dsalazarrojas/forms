# Hub Management Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hub Management Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect your honest feedback about your experiences with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the initial section, "Survey Start", which serves as an introduction.
2. Answer the questions posed in each subsequent section, providing as much detail as you like.
3. For questions where multiple options are provided (e.g., "Select one of the options," "Select multiple options," or "Rate"), choose the best option that applies to you.
4. For questions requiring a date or time, enter a specific date and time from memory or estimate to the best of your ability.
5. Finally, include any additional thoughts or comments you may have in the "Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?" section, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Start** (`survey_start`, note, required: false): This section is just an introduction to our survey, and you can usually leave it blank.
* **What do you think of our services?** (`question_1`, text, required: true): Please share your honest opinion about our services, providing as much detail as you like.
* **How can we improve our services?** (`question_2`, text, required: true): Share your thoughts on how we can improve our services to better meet your needs.
* **Rate our services (1-5)** (`question_3`, number, required: true): Rate our services on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **How likely are you to recommend us?** (`question_4`, select_one, required: true): Choose how likely you are to recommend our services, with options being: Very Likely, Somewhat Likely, Not Likely.
* **How would you rate our communication?** (`question_5`, select_multiple, required: true): Rate our communication by selecting one or multiple options: Good, Fair, Poor.
* **When was your last interaction with us?** (`question_6`, date, required: true): Enter the date of your last interaction with us or estimate to the best of your memory.
* **What time of day was your last interaction with us?** (`question_7`, time, required: true): Enter the time of your last interaction with us or estimate to the best of your memory.
* **Is there anything else you'd like to tell us?** (`question_8`, note, required: false): Share any additional thoughts or comments you may have about your experience.
* **Your Email** (`question_9`, email, required: true): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Your Phone** (`question_10`, text, required: true): Enter your phone number so we can contact you, if needed.
