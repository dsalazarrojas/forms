# Support Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have received support from our organization. It helps us understand how we can improve our service and provides valuable insights for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, such as your email address, phone number, and name.
2. Select how satisfied you were with our support service.
3. Choose how you contacted us (e.g., call, email, chat).
4. Select how long it took for us to resolve your issue.
5. Describe how your issue was resolved.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
7. Rate our support service.
8. Choose whether you would like to be contacted again for support.
9. If you answered "yes" to the previous question, describe how you would like to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Support Satisfaction Survey** (`survey_title`): A text field where you can enter a title for your feedback.
* **What was your satisfaction with our support service?** (`satisfaction_level`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **How did you contact us?** (`support_type`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Call
	+ Email
	+ Chat
	+ Other
* **What time of day did you contact us?** (`contact_time`): Select a time of day.
* **How long did it take us to resolve your issue?** (`resolution_time`): Enter a number.
* **How was your issue resolved?** (`resolution_method`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Live Chat
	+ In Person
	+ Other
* **Do you have any further comments or suggestions?** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Call
	+ Email
	+ Live Chat
	+ Other
* **Please describe how your issue was resolved.** (`resolution_method_details`): Describe how your issue was resolved.
* **Please describe how long it took for your issue to be resolved.** (`resolution_time_details`): Describe how long it took for your issue to be resolved.
* **Overall Support Rating** (`support_rating`): Rate our support service on a scale.
* **Would you like to be contacted again for support?** (`follow_up`): Select yes or no.
* **Please describe how you would like to be contacted.** (`follow_up_details`): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, describe how you would like to be contacted.
* **E-Mail** (`email_2`): Enter your email address (if different from the previous question).
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`): Enter your phone number (if different from the previous question).
* **Additional Comments** (`comments_2`): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Contact Time** (`contact_time_2`): Select a time of day (if different from the previous question).
* **Resolution Time** (`resolution_time_2`): Enter a number (if different from the previous question).
* **Support Rating** (`support_rating_2`): Rate our support service on a scale (if different from the previous question).
* **Resolution Method** (`resolution_method_2`): Select one of the following options:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Live Chat
	+ In Person
	+ Other
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up_2`): Select yes or no.
* **Follow Up Details 2** (`follow_up_details_2`): If you answered "yes" to the previous question, describe how you would like to be contacted.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly to provide us with the best possible feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to share them with us.
* We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our support service.
