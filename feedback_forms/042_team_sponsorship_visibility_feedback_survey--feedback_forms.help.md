# Team Sponsorship Visibility Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the visibility of sponsorships within our team. The feedback will help us understand how our team's progress and performance are perceived by team members and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
2.  For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
3.  For open-ended comments, please provide detailed and constructive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, Optional): This field is where you can provide any additional context or background information about the form. Please feel free to ask questions or clarify any points you'd like.
* **Sponsorship Visibility** (`sponsorship_visibility`, `select_one`, Optional): This field asks about the level of sponsorship visibility within our team. Please select the option that best describes your experience:
	+ High: Our team's sponsorships are highly visible and well communicated.
	+ Medium: Our team's sponsorships are somewhat visible, but there is room for improvement.
	+ Low: Our team's sponsorships are not well communicated or hard to find.
* **Sponsorship Impact** (`sponsorship_impact`, `select_multiple`, Optional): This field asks about the impact of sponsorships on our team. Please select all that apply:
	+ High: Our team's sponsorships have had a significant impact on our progress and performance.
	+ Medium: Our team's sponsorships have had some impact on our progress and performance.
	+ Low: Our team's sponsorships have had little to no impact on our progress and performance.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, Optional): This field asks for your satisfaction level with our sponsorships. Please enter a number between 1-5, where 1 is "Not Satisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **Open-Ended Comments** (`open_ended_comments`, `text`, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide. Please be detailed and constructive in your feedback.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, Optional): This field asks about the contact information that would be helpful for our team to receive feedback from you. Please select all that apply:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_multiple`, Optional): This field asks about your preferred contact method for receiving feedback from our team. Please select all that apply:
	+ Phone
	+ Email
	+ Mail
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide. Please be detailed and constructive in your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, Optional): This field is for any final feedback or suggestions you'd like to make. Please be detailed and constructive in your feedback.

Note: The required fields for this form are `comments` and `feedback`, but all fields are optional and can be skipped if you'd like.
