# Language Course Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their experience with a language course. The feedback collected through this form will help instructors and course administrators evaluate the course and make data-driven decisions to improve it.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Use the options provided for select-one and select-multiple fields.
3. For text fields (e.g., School Name, Other Suggestions), provide as much information as possible.
4. Do not leave any fields blank without a valid reason.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (1, text, required): Please provide the name of the school you've taken the course at.
* **Teacher Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Course Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the course you're evaluating.
* **Start Date** (4, date, required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (5, date, required): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Student ID** (6, text, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Grade** (7, number, required): Please provide your grade in the course.
* **Course Satisfaction** (8, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the course overall.
* **Course Quality** (9, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the quality of the course materials and instruction.
* **Student Satisfaction** (10, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your overall learning experience.
* **Teacher Satisfaction** (11, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your teacher's performance.
* **Teacher Satisfaction** (12, select_one, required): This field is a duplicate and should be ignored.
* **Course Materials** (13, select_multiple, required): Choose how well prepared the course materials were for you.
* **Course Facilities** (14, select_multiple, required): Choose how well the course facilities (e.g., classroom, lab) supported your learning.
* **Course Support** (15, select_multiple, required): Choose how well you felt supported by the course staff and resources.
* **Student Experience** (16, note, optional): Write any comments or suggestions about your overall experience in the course.
* **Teacher Experience** (17, note, optional): Write any comments or suggestions about your teacher's performance.
* **School Environment** (18, select_multiple, required): Choose how well you felt supported by the school environment (e.g., library, facilities).
* **School Climate** (19, select_multiple, required): Choose how well you felt supported by the school's climate (e.g., diversity, inclusiveness).
* **Student Learning** (20, select_multiple, required): Choose how well you felt you learned and engaged with the material.
* **Student Progress** (21, select_multiple, required): Choose how well you felt you progressed in your learning.
* **Teacher Attendance** (22, select_multiple, required): Choose how well your teacher was available to answer questions and provide support.
* **Other Suggestions** (23, text, optional): Write any other suggestions or comments about the course or school.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (24, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with your overall experience in the course.
* **Suggestions** (25, text, optional): Write any final suggestions or comments about the course or school.
