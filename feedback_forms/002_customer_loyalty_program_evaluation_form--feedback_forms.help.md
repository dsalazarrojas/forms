# Customer Loyalty Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers on their experience with our customer loyalty program. We value your opinion and would like to know how we can improve the program to better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please provide your name and contact method in case we need to follow up with you.
2. Answer each question honestly, and please be as detailed as possible.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the designated text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Program Name**: Please enter the name of the customer loyalty program you are evaluating.
* **Customer Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Comments**: Share your thoughts and feedback about the program. This is a text field where you can express your opinions and suggestions.
* **Overall Program Satisfaction**: Select how satisfied you are with the program, with options: very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, neutral, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied.
* **What We Can Do Better**: Share specific suggestions or ideas on how we can improve the program.
* **Best Contact Method**: Choose your preferred method of communication from email, phone, or in-person. This helps us know how to best contact you if we need to follow up on your feedback.
* **How Often to Contact**: Choose how often you would like us to contact you about the program, with options: never, rarely, sometimes, or often. This helps us tailor our communication to your needs.
* **What Program Value Means to You**: Indicate how important the program value is to you, with options: very important, somewhat important, not at all important.
* **Program Value Scale**: Rate how important the program value is on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not at all important and 5 is very important.
* **Other Contact Method**: If none of the provided contact methods appeal to you, please describe your preferred method in this text field.
* **Other Contact Frequency**: If you would like to be contacted more or less frequently than the provided options, please describe your preference in this text field.
* **Other Contact Method Details**: Please provide more details about your preferred contact method if it is not listed.
* **Overall Customer Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our customer service, on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with our customer loyalty program.
* **Other Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
