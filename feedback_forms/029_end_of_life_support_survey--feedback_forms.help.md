**End Of Life Support Survey - Help Guide**

**Purpose**
This form is designed to gather feedback and information from patients and their caregivers about their end-of-life care experience. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support in the future.

**How To Complete This Form**
1. **Introduction**: Read this section to understand the purpose and scope of this survey.
2. **Patient Information** (Page 2): Enter your personal details such as date of birth, gender, and other relevant information.
3. **Care Team Involved** (Page 3): Identify the care team members who were involved in your care.
4. **Care Location** (Page 4): Select the location where you received care.
5. **Symptoms** (Page 5): Describe the symptoms you experienced during your care journey.
6. **Type of Care** (Page 6): Choose the type of care you received.
7. **Quality of Care** (Page 7): Rate the quality of care you received.
8. **Emotional Support** (Page 8): Indicate the level of emotional support you received.
9. **Spiritual Support** (Page 9): Indicate the level of spiritual support you received.
10. **Pain Levels** (Page 10): Rate the levels of physical, emotional, and spiritual pain you experienced.
11. **Overall Satisfaction** (Page 11): Choose how satisfied you are with your care experience.
12. **Comments** (Page 12): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
13. **Recommendations** (Page 13): Suggest ways we can improve our services.
14. **Contact Information** (Page 14): Enter your contact information for follow-up and feedback.
15. **Survey Complete** (Page 15): Review and submit your survey.
