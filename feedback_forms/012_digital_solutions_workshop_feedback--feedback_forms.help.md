# Digital Solutions Workshop Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from workshop participants about their experience and suggestions for improvement for the Digital Solutions Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your details as a workshop participant:
    *   **Workshop Participant Information**: Provide a brief description of your background or affiliation with the workshop.
*   Share your feedback:
    *   **Feedback**: Describe your experience at the workshop, including any positive or negative aspects.
*   Provide a suggestion:
    *   **Suggestion**: Share any ideas you have for improving the workshop in the future.
*   Enter your email address:
    *   **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workshop Participant Information (id: 1)**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of your background or affiliation with the workshop.
    *   **Feedback (id: 2)**: This field is where you can describe your experience at the workshop, including any positive or negative aspects.
    *   **Suggestion (id: 3)**: This field is where you can share any ideas you have for improving the workshop in the future.
*   **Email (id: 4)**: This field requires a valid email address where you can be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
*   Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback! It will help us improve the workshop in the future.
