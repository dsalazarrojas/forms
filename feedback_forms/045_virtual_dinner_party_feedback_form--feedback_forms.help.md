# Virtual Dinner Party Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Dinner Party Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experience at a virtual dinner party. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us improve our events and provide better experiences for our guests in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on the "Submit" button after filling out each field. You can navigate between fields using the navigation buttons.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Virtual Party Feedback** (`virtual_dinner_party_feedback_form`, text, required): This is the main feedback area where you can type any comments, suggestions, or concerns about your experience. Please be as detailed as possible to help us improve.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience from 1 to 5 stars. This will help us understand the overall satisfaction level of our virtual dinner party.
* **Party Date** (`party_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the virtual dinner party you attended.
* **Party Time** (`party_time`, time, required): Enter the time when the virtual dinner party started. Use the format `HH:MM:SS AM/PM` for accuracy.
* **Caterer** (`caterer_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the caterer who provided food for the virtual dinner party.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): This is an optional section for any additional comments or concerns you may have. Please be specific and detailed so we can improve our services for future events.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your feedback.
- You can type any text into the "Virtual Party Feedback" field, including emojis and proper formatting.
- Use the correct format for the "Party Time" field (e.g., `08:00:00 PM`).
