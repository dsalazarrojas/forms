# Dietary Leadership Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants in the dietary leadership program. Your input will help us make the program more effective and beneficial for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your comments or questions about the program in the `Feedback Question` field.
2. Provide your answer or response to the question or comment in the `Feedback Answer` field.
3. Rate your experience using one of the provided options (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor) in the `Rating` field.
4. If you want to share more specific details, such as your email or phone number, you can do so in the `Leader Email`, `Leader Phone`, `Submitter Name`, `Submitter Email`, and `Submitter Phone` fields. Note that these fields are optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Leader Name (<label>)
This field is for the name of the leader you are providing feedback on.

*   **Leader Name:** This field is for the name of the leader.
### Feedback Question (<label>)
This field is where you can ask questions or provide comments about the program.

*   **Feedback Question:** Please type in your question or comment here.
### Feedback Answer (<label>)
This field is where you provide your answer or response to the question or comment.

*   **Feedback Answer:** Type in your response to the question or comment here.
### Rating (<label>)
This field is for rating your experience with the leader.

*   **Rating:** Rate your experience as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
### Feedback Date (<label>)
This field is for the date when you filled out this form.

*   **Feedback Date:** The date when you are submitting your feedback.
### Leader Email (<label>)
This field is optional for the email address of the leader.

*   **Leader Email:** If you want to share your email with the leader, type it here.
### Leader Phone (<label>)
This field is optional for the phone number of the leader.

*   **Leader Phone:** If you want to share your phone number with the leader, type it here.
### Submitter Name (<label>)
This field is for your name, the person submitting the feedback.

*   **Submitter Name:** Type in your name here.
### Submitter Email (<label>)
This field is optional for your email address.

*   **Submitter Email:** If you want to share your email with us, type it here.
### Submitter Phone (<label>)
This field is optional for your phone number.

*   **Submitter Phone:** If you want to share your phone number with us, type it here.

Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and let us know how we can improve the program!
