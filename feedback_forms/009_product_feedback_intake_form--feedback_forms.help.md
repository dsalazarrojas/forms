# Product Feedback Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your thoughts, opinions, and feedback about our products. Your input is valuable in helping us improve and refine our offerings. Please take a few minutes to share your experiences and suggestions. Your responses will be treated confidentially and used to make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the relevant options for each question from the provided list.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional comments?" field.
4. Finally, review your answers and submit the form.
