# Real Estate Open House Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Real Estate Open House Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from real estate agents and their clients about their experiences with open houses they have attended. This feedback will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our clients in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name and contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number)
2. Answer the questions about your experience with the open house, including its condition, the agent's helpfulness, and the asking price reasonableness.
3. Rate the agent and the open house separately.
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Was the property well-kept and clean?** (`feedback_question_1`, `select_one`, required/true): Select one of the options to indicate the condition of the property you visited.
* **Was the asking price reasonable?** (`feedback_question_2`, `select_one`, required/true): Select one of the options to indicate if you thought the asking price was reasonable.
* **Was the agent helpful?** (`feedback_question_3`, `select_one`, required/true): Select one of the options to rate the helpfulness of the agent.
* **What was the condition of the property?** (`feedback_question_4`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select all the options that apply to the condition of the property you visited.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback_message`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Real Estate License Number** (`real_estate_license_num`, `number`, required/optional): If you are an agent, enter your real estate license number.
* **Agent Name** (`real_estate_agent_name`, `text`, required/optional): If you are an agent, enter your name.
* **Agent Rating** (`feedback_agent_rating`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the agent's performance.
* **Agent Comments** (`feedback_agent_comments`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any comments about the agent, please provide them here.
* **Open House Rating** (`feedback_open_house_rating`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the open house's performance.
* **Open House Comments** (`feedback_open_house_comments`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any comments about the open house, please provide them here.
* **Open House Address** (`real_estate_open_house_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the open house.
* **City** (`real_estate_open_house_city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city of the open house.
* **State** (`real_estate_open_house_state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state of the open house.
* **Zip Code** (`real_estate_open_house_zip`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the zip code of the open house.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Additional feedback" field.
* If you are an agent, please make sure to enter your real estate license number and your name.
* If you are a client, please make sure to enter your contact information, so we can contact you if needed.
