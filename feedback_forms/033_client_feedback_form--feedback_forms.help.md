# Client Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their experience with our services. This form will help us to assess the quality of our service, staff courtesy, response time, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select an answer from the dropdown menus for each field.
3. Type your response in the text fields.
4. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`client_first_name`, `text`, *): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (`client_last_name`, `text`, *): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address (3)** (`client_email`, `email`, *): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`client_phone`, `text`, ): Enter your best contact number.
* **Date of Service (5)** (`service_date`, `date`, *): Enter the date you received our service.
* **Service Type Received (6)** (`service_type`, `select_one`, *): Select the type of service you received.
	+ Product Purchase
	+ Consulting Service
	+ Support Service
	+ Training Session
	+ Maintenance Service
	+ Professional Service
	+ Other
* **Staff Member Who Assisted You (7)** (`staff_member`, `text`, ): Enter the name of the staff member who assisted you.
* **Overall Satisfaction (8)** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, *): Select your overall satisfaction with our service.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Service Quality (9)** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, *): Select the quality of service you received.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Staff Courtesy (10)** (`staff_courtesy`, `select_one`, *): Select the level of staff courtesy you experienced.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Response Time (11)** (`response_time`, `select_one`, *): Select how quickly we responded to your needs.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Value for Money (12)** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, *): Select whether you think our service was worth the cost.
	+ Excellent Value
	+ Good Value
	+ Fair Value
	+ Poor Value
	+ Very Poor Value
* **Expectations Met (13)** (`expectations_met`, `select_one`, *): Select whether your expectations were met.
	+ Exceeded Expectations
	+ Met Expectations
	+ Mostly Met Expectations
	+ Did Not Meet Expectations
	+ Far Below Expectations
* **Facility Cleanliness (14)** (`facility_cleanliness`, `select_one`, ): Select the cleanliness of our facility, if you visited in person.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Not Applicable
* **Ease of Doing Business (15)** (`ease_of_process`, `select_one`, *): Select how easy or difficult it was to do business with us.
	+ Very Easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very Difficult
* **Did You Encounter Any Problems? (16)** (`problem_encountered`, `select_one`, *): Select whether you encountered any problems during service.
	+ No Problems

	+ Major Problem
* **Describe the Problem (17)** (`problem_description`, `text`, ): Type a description of the problem you experienced.

	+ Yes, Completely
	+ Yes, Partially
	+ False
	+ Not Applicable
* **How Likely Are You to Return? (19)** (`likely_to_return`, `select_one`, *): Select how likely you are to return to us.
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very Unlikely
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend Us? (20)** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, *): Select how likely you are to recommend us to friends or colleagues.
	+ Very Likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very Unlikely
* **What Did You Like Best? (21)** (`best_aspect`, `text`, ): Type what you liked best about your experience.
* **What Could We Improve? (22)** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, ): Type areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments (23)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, ): Type any additional comments or feedback.
* **Feedback Date (24)** (`feedback_date`, `date`, *): Select the date you are providing this feedback.
