# Internship Program Results Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from interns who have completed an internship program within your organization. Your input will help us improve our internship program and better serve future interns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your honest feedback, including your name and contact information (email and phone number) so we can reach you for follow-up questions.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field, as it will help us understand your experience.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for skills gained, and select a rating for the overall satisfaction and program quality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **organization**: Type the name of the organization where you interned.
* **position**: Enter the position you held during the internship.
* **program_length**: Describe the duration of the internship program you participated in.
* **skills_gained**: Indicate which skills you gained during the internship by selecting "Yes" next to each relevant skill.
* **overall_satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the internship experience (low, medium, high).
* **suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have for improving the program.
* **recommendations**: Provide any recommendations for future interns.
* **comments**: Share any comments you have about your experience.
* **rating**: Rate the quality of the internship program (low, medium, high).
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can reach you for follow-up questions.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **submitted_by**: Select the role of the person submitting the form (intern, supervisor, other).
* **submitted_on**: Date you are submitting this form.

## Tips
* Please submit your feedback within a reasonable timeframe after completing the program.
* Be as detailed and honest as possible, as your input will help us improve our internship program.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
