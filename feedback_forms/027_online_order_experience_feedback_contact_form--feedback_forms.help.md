# online_order_experience_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_order_experience_feedback_contact_form is designed to collect customer feedback on their online order experience, helping us understand how to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields:

*   Provide your customer information, including name and contact number.
*   Enter your email address for our follow-up correspondence.
*   Rate your overall experience with our online order.
*   Enter your order number for reference.
*   Provide the date you placed your order.
*   Enter any comments you may have about your experience.
*   Select the type of product you ordered.
*   Indicate your experience with our customer service team.
*   Choose whether you would like to be followed up on.
*   Select the time of day you interacted with us.
*   Confirm if you interacted with us on a weekday or weekend.
*   Confirm if you interacted with us on a specific month.
*   Confirm if you interacted with us on a weekday or weekend day.
*   Confirm the quarter in which you interacted with us.
*   Confirm the year in which you interacted with us.
*   Enter any additional comments you may have.
*   Enter an alternate email address for follow-up correspondence.
*   Enter your alternate contact number.
*   Select the type of product you ordered again.
*   Indicate your experience with our customer service team again.
*   Choose whether you would like to be followed up on again.
*   Enter the time of your next interaction with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (`Customer Information`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your customer information, including name and contact number.
*   **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for us to contact you.
*   **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for our follow-up correspondence.
*   **Rating** (`Rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with our online order.
*   **Order Number** (`Order Number`, number, optional): Enter your order number for reference.
*   **Order Date** (`Order Date`, date, optional): Provide the date you placed your order.
*   **Comment** (`Comment`, note, optional): Enter any comments you may have about your experience.
*   **Product Type** (`Product Type`, select one, required): Select the type of product you ordered.
*   **Customer Service** (`Customer Service`, select multiple, required): Indicate your experience with our customer service team.
*   **Follow Up** (`Follow Up`, select one, optional): Choose whether you would like to be followed up on.
*   **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Select the time of day you interacted with us.
*   **Time of Day** (`Time of Day`, select one, optional): Confirm if you interacted with us on a weekday or weekend.
*   **Day of Week** (`Day of Week`, select one, optional): Confirm if you interacted with us on a weekday or weekend day.
*   **Day of Month** (`Day of Month`, select one, optional): Confirm if you interacted with us on a specific month.
*   **Month of Year** (`Month of Year`, select one, optional): Confirm the month in which you interacted with us.
*   **Week Day** (`Week Day`, select one, optional): Confirm if you interacted with us on a weekday or weekend day.
*   **Quarter** (`Quarter`, select one, optional): Confirm the quarter in which you interacted with us.
*   **Year** (`Year`, select one, optional): Confirm the year in which you interacted with us.
*   **Comment 2** (`Comment 2`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments you may have.
*   **Email 2** (`Email 2`, email, optional): Enter an alternate email address for follow-up correspondence.
*   **Contact Number 2** (`Contact Number 2`, text, optional): Enter your alternate contact number.
*   **Product Type 2** (`Product Type 2`, select one, optional): Select the type of product you ordered again.
*   **Customer Service 2** (`Customer Service 2`, select multiple, optional): Indicate your experience with our customer service team again.
*   **Follow Up 2** (`Follow Up 2`, select one, optional): Choose whether you would like to be followed up on again.
*   **Time 2** (`Time 2`, time, optional): Enter the time of your next interaction with us.

## Tips
Please fill out the form carefully and honestly, as your feedback is valuable to us.
