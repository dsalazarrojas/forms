# Product Quality Score Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback about the quality of our products, allowing us to gauge customer satisfaction and identify areas for improvement. Please fill out this form to provide a detailed account of your experience with our product.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Start by selecting the feedback type (e.g., Bug, Enhancement, New Feature).
2. Rate the product quality using one of the following options: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Okay, Good, or Excellent.
3. Provide a comment about your experience with the product (optional).
4. Enter the date you received the product (if applicable).
5. If applicable, enter the time you received the product.
6. Select the team you received the product from (e.g., Sales, Customer Service, Support Team).
7. Enter the email or phone number of the person you received the product from (if applicable).
8. If you have an image of the product, please provide the URL.
9. Enter the URL of the product.
10. Provide a detailed description of any issues you encountered with the product (optional).
11. Specify the version of the product you used (if applicable).
12. Indicate whether your experience was positive or negative.
13. Enter a confidence level for your product rating (on a scale of 0-10).
14. Enter the date you received the product.
15. Enter the time you received the product.
16. Select who reported the issue (e.g., Self, Manager).
17. Provide a detailed description of the issue (optional).
18. Enter a description of any actions taken to address the issue.
19. Enter the outcome or result of any action taken.
20. Enter any follow-up information or next steps (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Rating**: Select a rating for the product quality (Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Okay, Good, Excellent).
* **Product Rating Comment**: Enter a comment about your experience with the product.
* **Product Quality**: Select a rating for the product quality (Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Okay, Good, Excellent).
* **Received Date**: Enter the date you received the product.
* **Received Time**: Enter the time you received the product.
* **Feedback Type**: Select the type of feedback (e.g., Bug, Enhancement, New Feature).
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name (optional).
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email (optional).
* **Product Rating Score**: Enter a numerical rating score for the product (on a scale of 0-10).
* **Received From**: Select the team that provided the product (e.g., Sales, Customer Service, Support Team).
* **Received From Email**: Enter the email or phone number of the person who provided the product.
* **Product Image URL**: Enter the URL of a product image (if applicable).
* **Product URL**: Enter the URL of the product.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a detailed description of any issues you encountered with the product.
* **Product Version**: Enter the version of the product you used (if applicable).
* **Comment**: Enter a comment about your experience with the product.
* **Confidence Level**: Enter a confidence level for your product rating (on a scale of 0-10).
* **Received On**: Enter the date you received the product.
* **Received At**: Enter the time you received the product.
* **Issue Reported By**: Select who reported the issue (e.g., Self, Manager).
* **Issue Description**: Provide a detailed description of the issue.
* **Action Taken**: Enter a description of any actions taken to address the issue.
* **Result**: Enter the outcome or result of any action taken.
* **Follow-up**: Enter any follow-up information or next steps.
