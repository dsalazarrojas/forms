# mental_health_metrics_monitoring_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The mental health metrics monitoring form is used to record and track an individual's mental health status, symptoms, and treatment plans. The form helps healthcare professionals to monitor progress, identify potential issues, and provide targeted support to ensure the best possible mental health outcome.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field and select the relevant options or enter the required information.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Provide detailed comments and descriptions where necessary.
4. Submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mental Health Status** (`mental_health_status`, Select One, Required: False):
	+ Select the current mental health status of the individual: 'good', 'fair', or 'poor'.
* **Mental Health Symptoms** (`mental_health_symptoms`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter any mental health symptoms experienced by the individual.
* **Mental Health Diagnoses** (`mental_health_diagnoses`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter any relevant mental health diagnoses or conditions.
* **Mental Health Treatment Plan** (`mental_health_treatment_plan`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter any existing mental health treatment plans or therapies in place.
* **Mental Health Prognosis** (`mental_health_prognosis`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter any relevant mental health prognosis or outlook.
* **Mental Health Outcome** (`mental_health_outcome`, Select One, Required: False):
	+ Select the current mental health outcome: 'improved', 'same', or 'worsened'.
* **Mental Health Outcome Comments** (`mental_health_outcome_comments`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Provide any comments or explanations for the chosen outcome.
* **Mental Health Referrals** (`mental_health_referrals`, Select Multiple, Required: False):
	+ Select whether any mental health referrals have been made: 'True' or 'False'.
* **Mental Health Notes** (`mental_health_notes`, Note, Required: False):
	+ Enter any additional mental health information or notes.
* **Mental Health Comments** (`mental_health_comments`, Text, Required: False):
	+ Enter any relevant comments or discussions on the mental health status.
* **Mental Health Next Checkup** (`mental_health_next_checkup`, Date, Required: False):
	+ Enter the date of the next mental health checkup.
* **Mental Health Next Checkup Time** (`mental_health_next_checkup_time`, Time, Required: False):
	+ Enter the time of the next mental health checkup.
* **Mental Health Next Checkup Date** (`mental_health_next_checkup_date`, Date, Required: False):
	+ Enter the date of the next mental health checkup.
