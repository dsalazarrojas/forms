# EHR Training Class Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a collection of questions and ratings designed to gather feedback from trainees who have recently attended an EHR Training Class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. You may skip any question that does not apply to you or your experience with the training session. You will need to enter your name and email address to ensure that we can contact you regarding any follow-up questions or discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Date (`session_date`, date, optional)
* The date of the training session you attended. This will help us match your feedback with the specific training session you're referencing.

### Session Start Time (`session_start_time`, time, optional)
* The time the training session started. This is used to confirm the timing of your session.

### Session End Time (`session_end_time`, time, optional)
* The time the training session ended. This helps us understand how the session progressed.

### How Was the Training Session Organized? (`feedback_question_1`, text, optional)
* Please describe how the training session was organized, including any notable events or activities.

### What Was Covered (`feedback_question_2`, text, optional)
* What topics or activities were covered during the training session. Be as detailed as possible.

### What Was Covered Better? (`feedback_question_3`, text, optional)
* If you felt that certain aspects of the training were particularly useful or effective, please describe them.

### What Did Not Get Covered? (`feedback_question_4`, text, optional)
* If there were any topics or activities that you felt should have been included or covered in more detail, please describe them.

### Rating of Training Session (`rating_of_training_session`, number, optional)
* Rate the overall quality of the training session, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Rating of Instructor (`rating_of_instructor`, number, optional)
* Rate the instructor's performance, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.

### Comments (`comment_section`, note, optional)
* Any additional comments or suggestions you have regarding the training session.

### Name (`name`, text, optional)
* Enter your name so we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.

### Email (`email`, email, optional)
* Enter your email address so we can contact you with any follow-up questions or discussions.

### Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
* Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted by phone regarding your feedback).

Note: All fields are optional, but providing your name and email address will help us ensure that we can follow up with you regarding any questions or discussions.
