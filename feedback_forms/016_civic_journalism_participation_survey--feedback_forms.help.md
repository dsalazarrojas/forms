# Civic Journalism Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and insights from participants in civic journalism initiatives. The information collected will help the organizers of the initiative understand their participants' needs and make informed decisions about the development and improvement of the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the participant information section (questions 2-4).
2. In the involvement section (questions 5-8), select all the ways you have been involved in civic journalism.
3. In the experience section (questions 9-12), rate your overall experience and the impact you feel it has had on you.
4. In the feedback section (questions 13-16), share your suggestions and opinions on how the initiative could be improved.
5. Finally, answer the questions in the future involvement and referrals sections (questions 17-20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you with any further questions or to follow up on your feedback.
* **Organization or Affiliation** (4, text, optional): If you have an affiliation with an organization or institution, please enter it.
* **How have you been involved - select all that apply** (6, select_multiple, required): Select all the ways you have been involved in civic journalism.
	+ **Citizen reporter**: This option is for those who have reported or written news stories.
	+ **Story contributor or source**: This option is for those who have contributed to or sourced news stories.
	+ **Community event covered**: This option is for those who have covered community events.
	+ **Audience member or reader**: This option is for those who have read or viewed news stories.
	+ **Social media engagement**: This option is for those who have engaged with civic journalism on social media.
	+ **Workshop participant**: This option is for those who have participated in workshops or training related to civic journalism.
	+ **Advisory board member**: This option is for those who have been on an advisory board for a civic journalism initiative.
	+ **Volunteer**: This option is for those who have volunteered their time to a civic journalism initiative.
	+ **Other**: This option is for those who have been involved in other ways that are not listed here.
* **How long have you been involved** (7, select_one, required): Select how long you have been involved in civic journalism.
	+ **Less than 3 months**: This option is for those who have been involved for less than 3 months.
	+ **3-6 months**: This option is for those who have been involved for 3-6 months.
	+ **6-12 months**: This option is for those who have been involved for 6-12 months.
	+ **1-2 years**: This option is for those who have been involved for 1-2 years.
	+ **2+ years**: This option is for those who have been involved for 2+ years.
* **Number of stories or contributions submitted** (8, number, optional): Enter the approximate number of stories or contributions you have submitted.
* **Rate your overall experience with civic journalism** (10, select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with civic journalism.
	+ **Excellent**: This option is for those who have had an excellent experience with civic journalism.
	+ **Good**: This option is for those who have had a good experience with civic journalism.
	+ **Fair**: This option is for those who have had a fair experience with civic journalism.
	+ **Poor**: This option is for those who have had a poor experience with civic journalism.
* **Do you feel your participation makes a difference** (11, select_one, required): Rate how much you feel your participation makes a difference.
	+ **Strongly agree**: This option is for those who strongly agree that their participation makes a difference.
	+ **Agree**: This option is for those who agree that their participation makes a difference.
	+ **Neutral**: This option is for those who feel neutral about their participation.
	+ **Disagree**: This option is for those who disagree that their participation makes a difference.
	+ **Strongly disagree**: This option is for those who strongly disagree that their participation makes a difference.
* **Has civic journalism helped you feel more connected to community** (12, select_one, required): Rate how much civic journalism has helped you feel more connected to your community.
	+ **Very much**: This option is for those who feel very much more connected to their community.
	+ **Somewhat**: This option is for those who feel somewhat more connected to their community.
	+ **Neutral**: This option is for those who feel neutral about their connection to their community.
	+ **Not much**: This option is for those who feel not much more connected to their community.
	+ **Not at all**: This option is for those who feel not at all more connected to their community.
* **Impact on trust in journalism and media** (13, select_one, optional): Rate the impact of civic journalism on your trust in journalism and media.
	+ **Increased trust significantly**: This option is for those who have increased their trust in journalism and media significantly.
	+ **Somewhat increased trust**: This option is for those who have increased their trust in journalism and media somewhat.
	+ **No change**: This option is for those who have not changed their trust in journalism and media.
	+ **Decreased trust**: This option is for those who have decreased their trust in journalism and media.
	+ **Not sure**: This option is for those who are not sure about their trust in journalism and media.
* **What do you see as strengths of this civic journalism initiative** (15, text, optional): Enter any strengths you see in this civic journalism initiative.
* **What could be improved** (16, text, optional): Enter any suggestions or areas for improvement you see in this civic journalism initiative.
* **Barriers to more engagement - select all that apply** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select all the barriers to more engagement you face.
	+ **Lack of time**: This option is for those who have lacked time to participate in civic journalism.
	+ **Lack of skills or training**: This option is for those who have lacked the necessary skills or training to participate in civic journalism.
	+ **Technical difficulties**: This option is for those who have encountered technical difficulties while participating in civic journalism.
	+ **Limited topics of interest**: This option is for those who have limited topics of interest or focus areas.
	+ **Unclear process**: This option is for those who have had an unclear process or guidelines for participating in civic journalism.
	+ **Lack of feedback on submissions**: This option is for those who have not received feedback on their submissions.
	+ **Other**: This option is for those who have faced other barriers not listed here.
* **What support or resources would help you participate more** (18, text, optional): Enter any support or resources that you feel would help you participate more in civic journalism.
* **Will you continue participating in civic journalism** (19, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood to continue participating in civic journalism.
	+ **Yes, definitely**: This option is for those who will definitely continue participating.
	+ **Probably yes**: This option is for those who probably will continue participating.
	+ **Not sure**: This option is for those who are not sure about continuing to participate.
	+ **Probably no**: This option is for those who probably will not continue participating.
	+ **Not**: This option is for those who will not participate in the future.
* **Would you recommend civic journalism to others** (20, select_one, optional): Rate your likelihood to recommend civic journalism to others.
	+ **Very likely**: This option is for those who feel very likely to recommend.
	+ **Likely**: This option is for those who feel likely to recommend.
	+ **Neutral**: This option is for those who feel neutral about recommending.
	+ **Unlikely**: This option is for those who feel unlikely to recommend.
	+ **Very unlikely**: This option is for those who feel very unlikely to recommend.
