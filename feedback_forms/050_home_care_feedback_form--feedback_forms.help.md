# home_care_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from patients or their representatives about their experience with our home care services. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us to improve the quality of care we provide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please select the relevant options for each field, and provide clear and concise answers to each question.
2. Make sure to submit the form only once for each patient, to ensure that your feedback is recorded accurately.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, required): This field is where you can provide any comments or concerns you have about your experience with our home care services. Please be as honest and detailed as possible.
* **Care Services** (`care_services`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the care services you received during your recent visit. This will help us understand what services were beneficial and which areas need improvement.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with our home care services, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about our services, please provide them here.
* **Contact us** (`contact_us`, note, optional): If you need to contact us for any reason, please use this field to leave a message or ask a question.
