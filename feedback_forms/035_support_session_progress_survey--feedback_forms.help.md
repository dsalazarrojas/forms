# Support Session Progress Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Session Progress Survey is a tool that helps us understand how well our support sessions are going. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us identify areas of improvement and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Business Name**: Enter the name of the business you were working with.
	* **Session Start Time**: Enter the start time of the support session.
	* **Session End Time**: Enter the end time of the support session.
	* **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the session (Excellent, Very Good, Okay, Not Good, Not at all).
	* **Email**: Enter your contact email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
2. If necessary, fill in any additional fields:
	* **Session Duration**: If you want to provide more information about the session duration.
	* **Open-Ended Question 1**: Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
	* **Open-Ended Question 2**: Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
	* **Open-Ended Question 3**: Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
	* **Close-Ended Question 1**: Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
	* **Close-Ended Question 2**: Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
	* **Progress Survey**: Rate your satisfaction with the support session.
	* **Follow-up Needed**: Indicate if you need any follow-up from our support team.
	* **Follow-up Comments**: Share any additional comments or questions about the session.
	* **Follow-up Phone Number**: If you have a phone number you'd like to share for follow-up.
	* **Follow-up Email**: If you have an email address you'd like to share for follow-up.
	* **Follow-up Comments**: Share any additional comments or questions about the session.
	* **Open-Ended Question 4**: Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
	* **Close-Ended Question 3**: Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
	* **Follow-up Phone Number**: If you have a phone number you'd like to share for follow-up.
	* **Follow-up Email Number**: If you have an email address you'd like to share for follow-up.
	* **Follow-up Comments**: Share any additional comments or questions about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the business you were working with.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, date, required): Enter the start time of the support session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the support session.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the session (Excellent, Very Good, Okay, Not Good, Not at all).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): If you want to provide more information about the session duration.
* **Open-Ended Question 1** (`open_ended_question_1`, text, optional): Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
* **Open-Ended Question 2** (`open_ended_question_2`, text, optional): Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
* **Open-Ended Question 3** (`open_ended_question_3`, text, optional): Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
* **Close-Ended Question 1** (`close_ended_question_1`, number, optional): Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
* **Close-Ended Question 2** (`close_ended_question_2`, number, optional): Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
* **Progress Survey** (`support_session_progress_survey`, select_multiple, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the support session (Highly Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, Not Satisfied, Not at all, Not Sure).
* **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you need any follow-up from our support team (True, False).
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or questions about the session.
* **Follow-up Phone Number** (`follow_up_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to share for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Email** (`follow_up_email`, email, optional): If you have an email address you'd like to share for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or questions about the session.
* **Open-Ended Question 4** (`open_ended_question_4`, text, optional): Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
* **Close-Ended Question 3** (`close_ended_question_3`, number, optional): Answer any questions or share any additional comments.
* **Follow-up Phone Number** (`follow_up_phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like to share for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Email Number** (`follow_up_email_number`, text, optional): If you have an email address you'd like to share for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or questions about the session.

## Tips
* Please answer any questions or share any additional comments truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, just provide the best information you can.
* We appreciate any feedback that will help us improve our support sessions!
