# feedback_on_transition_process - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "feedback_on_transition_process" form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the transition process. This form helps us understand how to improve the process and make it more user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields. You'll need to provide some basic contact information and select from the options provided in the "Transition Type" dropdown menu. Then, provide more details about your transition experience and rate your satisfaction with the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback On Transition Process (1)
* **Feedback On Transition Process** (1, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your experience with the transition process. Try to be as specific as possible.

### Transition Type (2)
* **Transition Type** (2, select_one, optional): Select the type of transition you experienced. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.

### Date (3)
* **Date** (3, date, optional): Enter the date when you experienced the transition. This will help us understand the timing of your feedback.

### Time (4)
* **Time** (4, time, optional): Enter the time when you experienced the transition. This will help us understand the timing of your feedback.

### Location (5)
* **Location** (5, text, optional): Enter the location where you experienced the transition. This will help us understand the environment of your feedback.

### Details (6)
* **Details** (6, text, optional): Provide a more detailed description of your transition experience. Try to be as specific as possible.

### Feedback (7)
* **Feedback** (7, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your transition experience.

### First Name (8)
* **First Name** (8, text, optional): Please enter your first name for our records.

### Last Name (9)
* **Last Name** (9, text, optional): Please enter your last name for our records.

### Email (10)
* **Email** (10, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.

### Contact Number (11)
* **Contact Number** (11, text, optional): Enter your contact number in case we need to reach you.

### Rating (12)
* **Rating** (12, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the transition process.

### Comment (13)
* **Comment** (13, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your transition experience.

### Submit (14)
* **Submit** (14, note, optional): Click this button to submit your feedback. Please make sure to complete all the required fields before submitting.
