# School Withdrawal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect relevant information from students who have withdrawn from their school for various reasons. The information collected will help school administrators or staff track and analyze the reasons behind student withdrawals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start filling out the form, begin with the first page: "Student Info".
2. Fill out the "Student Name", "Contact Number", and "School Name" fields with the relevant information.
3. Proceed to the "Grade" page and fill out the grade level of the student.
4. On the "Student Filled Out Form" page, provide a brief description of the student's filled out form (if applicable).
5. If the student has filled out a form, you can add your comments or observations on the "Thoughts" page.
6. If the student has any disappointments or concerns, you can document them on the "Disappointments" page.
7. The "Parent Info" page is for capturing any information related to the student's parents or guardians.
8. On the "Reason For Leaving" page, select the reason why the student has withdrawn from the school from the provided options (Moving to a different school, Financial difficulties, Family reasons, Personal reasons, or Other).
9. If the reason chosen is "Other", provide additional information on the "Other" page.
10. Review and confirm your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, optional): This page is where you'll enter basic information about the student who has withdrawn from school.
* **Grade** (`grade`, number, optional): Enter the grade level of the student who has withdrawn.
* **Student Filled Out Form** (`student_filled_out_form`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the student's filled out form (if applicable).
* **Thoughts** (`thoughts`, text, optional): Add any comments or observations about the student's withdrawal.
* **Disappointments** (`disappointments`, text, optional): Document any disappointments or concerns of the student or their parents.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, optional): Capture any information related to the student's parents or guardians.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the student's contact number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school the student has withdrawn from.
* **Reason For Leaving** (`reason_for_leaving`, select_one, optional): Choose the reason why the student has withdrawn from the school from the provided options.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): If you chose "Other" as the reason for leaving, provide a brief explanation of the reason on this page.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, optional): This page is not actually part of the original form, it seems to be a duplicate of "Student Info". However, it's included here for completeness.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): This page is intended for entering additional information related to the "Other" reason for leaving, but since the "Reason For Leaving" field already has an "Other" option, this page is redundant.
