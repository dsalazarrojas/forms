# Multisport Improvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about various sports, allowing us to understand what our users think about each sport.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select one or more sports that you are most interested in from the list provided.
2. Type in your feedback score for the selected sports.
3. Provide any additional comments about the sports you selected.
4. Enter your name, club name, email, and phone number (if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback).
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sports** (select multiple): Choose one or more sports from the dropdown list.
	+ This field allows you to select the sports you are most interested in.
* **feedback_score** (number): Rate your interest in the selected sport from 1 to 10.
	+ Please enter a number between 1 and 10 to indicate your level of interest in the selected sport.
* **comments** (text): Add any additional comments or suggestions about the sport you selected.
	+ This is an open field where you can type in any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **athlete_name** (text): Enter your name.
	+ Please enter the name you would like to be associated with your feedback.
* **club_name** (text): Enter your club name (if applicable).
	+ If you are representing a club, please enter the name of your club.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
	+ We may use this to contact you for further feedback or questions.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
	+ Please enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.
* **submit** (text): Click this button to submit your feedback.
	+ This button will save your feedback and we'll review it accordingly.
