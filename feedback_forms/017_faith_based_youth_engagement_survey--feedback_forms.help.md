# Faith Based Youth Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from youth who participate in faith-based events or activities. The survey will gather data on demographics, event details, and engagement metrics. This data will help faith-based organizations better understand and serve their youth populations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the event and your demographic details.
2. Fill in the requested fields, which are marked as required.
3. For the select multiple and select one fields, choose the options that best match your demographics and event details.
4. For the number fields, enter the correct count of participants, adults, youth, and children.
5. For the date, time, and what time of day fields, use the formats MM/DD/YY and 12:00 AM/PM, respectively.
6. For the select one fields, choose the option that best matches your purpose of the event.
7. For the notes field, provide any additional information you feel is relevant to the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Age Range** (3, select_one, required): Choose the age range that best matches you.
* **Church Organization** (4, select_multiple, required): Select all the church organizations you are associated with.
* **Comments** (5, note, required): Provide any additional comments about the event or organization.
* **Phone** (6, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date of the event in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Time** (8, time, required): Enter the time of the event in 12:00 AM/PM format.
* **Name of Ministry** (9, text, required): Enter the name of the ministry or organization hosting the event.
* **Name of Event** (10, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Location** (11, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Number of Participants** (12, number, required): Enter the total number of participants.
* **Number of Adults** (13, number, required): Enter the number of adults participating.
* **Number of Youth** (14, number, required): Enter the number of youth participating.
* **Number of Children** (15, number, required): Enter the number of children participating.
* **Gender** (16, select_one, required): Choose your gender.
* **Race/Ethnicity** (17, select_multiple, required): Select all the races/ethnicities that apply to you.
* **How long** (18, time, required): Enter the duration of the event.
* **What Time of Day** (19, select_one, required): Choose the time of day of the event.
* **What Day of Week** (20, select_one, required): Choose the day of the week of the event.
* **What Month** (21, select_one, required): Choose the month of the event.
* **Purpose of Event** (22, select_one, required): Choose the purpose of the event.
* **Number of Hours** (23, number, required): Enter the number of hours of the event.
* **Number of Rooms** (24, number, required): Enter the number of rooms used for the event.
* **Notes** (25, note, required): Provide any additional comments or information about the event.
