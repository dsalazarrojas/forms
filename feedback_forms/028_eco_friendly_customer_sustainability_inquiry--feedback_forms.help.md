# Eco Friendly Customer Sustainability Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Friendly Customer Sustainability Inquiry form aims to gather information about a customer's interest in environmentally friendly programs and initiatives. This form will help customers share their sustainability concerns and interests, enabling our business to better understand their needs and provide more tailored solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to save your answers.
*   Fill out the form in English.
*   Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Sustainability Priority** (`customer_sustainability_priority`, Select multiple, Not required): This question asks about your personal priority level on sustainability. Please choose from the two options "Yes" or "No", depending on how important you consider sustainability.
*   **Eco-Programs Interest** (`eco_programs_interest`, Select multiple, Not required): This question asks about your interest in various eco-friendly programs. Please choose from the two options "Yes" or "No", depending on how much you value these programs.
*   **Customer Sustainability Note** (`customer_sustainability_note`, Note, Not required): This field is for additional comments or thoughts about sustainability. You can type any information you'd like to share with us.
*   **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`, Text, Not required): This field is for your first name.
*   **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, Text, Not required): This field is for your last name.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, Email, Not required): Please enter your email address. This will help us contact you if needed.
*   **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, Text, Not required): Enter your phone number to help us contact you if necessary.
