# Altitude Attainment Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants in altitude attainment training sessions. The purpose of this form is to understand their overall satisfaction, relevance, and perceived value of the training content. The feedback collected will help us improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information for each page.
2. Review each field carefully and answer according to your true feelings and experiences.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, **required**)
This field collects the name of the participant. Please enter your full name.

### 2. **Date of Training** (`date_of_training`, `date`, **required**)
Enter the date of the training session you attended.

### 3. **Program Name** (`program_name`, `select_one`, **required**)
Select the type of program you attended: Leadership Development, Technical Skills Workshop, Team Building Retreat, Communication Mastery, or Strategic Planning Session.

### 4. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, **required**)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session. Choose from Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.

### 5. **Instructor Rating** (`instructor_rating`, `select_one`, **required**)
Rate the performance of the instructor. Choose from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Very Poor.

### 6. **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, `select_one`, **required**)
Rate how relevant the training content was to your job. Choose from Highly Relevant, Relevant, Somewhat Relevant, or Not Relevant.

### 7. **Key Takeaways** (`key_takeaways`, `text`, **optional**)
List 1-3 key things you learned from the training session. This field is optional, but will help us understand what was most valuable to you.

### 8. **Facility Feedback** (`facility_feedback`, `text`, **optional**)
Provide comments on the venue, equipment, or food. This field is optional, but any feedback will help us improve our facilities.

### 9. **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_improvement`, `text`, **optional**)
Provide any suggestions you have for improving future sessions. This field is optional, but your input will help us improve.

### 10. **Would You Recommend?** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, **required**)
Answer whether you would recommend this training to a colleague. Choose True or False.
