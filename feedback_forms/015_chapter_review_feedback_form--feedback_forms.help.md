# Chapter Review Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chapter Review Feedback Form is a tool used to gather feedback from chapter members on their experience at a meeting or event organized by the chapter. This form helps the chapter understand what went well and what can be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are filling out the form for the correct meeting or event.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Provide honest and fair feedback.
4. Use the provided fields to rate and describe your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Member Information
* **Chapter Member Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Member Contact Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up and verification purposes.
* **Chapter Organization Name**: Enter the full name of the chapter being reviewed.

### Meeting Details
* **Date of Meeting Reviewed**: Enter the date the meeting took place.
* **Type of Meeting or Event**: Select the category of the meeting (e.g. Standard Chapter Meeting, Executive Board Meeting, etc.).
* **Attendance Format**: Select how you attended the meeting (e.g. In-Person, Virtual (Live), Hybrid, etc.).

### Meeting Assessment
* **Agenda Clarity Rating**: Rate how clear the meeting's agenda was (1-5).
* **Time Management Rating**: Rate the effectiveness of the meeting's time management (1-5).
* **Member Engagement Level**: Select the level of engagement of the participants (e.g. High Engagement, Moderate Engagement, Low Engagement, etc.).
* **Effectiveness of Leadership**: Rate the effectiveness of the meeting leaders (1-5).

### Detailed Feedback
* **Positive Highlights of the Meeting**: Describe what went particularly well.
* **Areas for Improvement Suggestions**: Suggest ways to improve future meetings.
* **Relevance of Content Presented**: Rate the relevance of the content presented (1-4).
* **Additional Comments or Thoughts**: Add any additional comments or thoughts you may have.

### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Meeting Experience Rating**: Rate your overall experience (1-5).
* **Recommendation of Future Events**: Select whether you would recommend this type of meeting to others (e.g. Highly Recommend, Recommend, Neutral, etc.).
* **Confirmation of Feedback Submission**: Confirm that you are providing honest and fair feedback.
* **Date of Submission**: Select today's date.
* **Additional Comments or Thoughts**: Add any additional comments or thoughts.
