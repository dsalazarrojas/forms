# Retail Store Layout Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from various departments and stakeholders about the retail store layout. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for future changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have a unique form ID (Store ID) for each submission.
2. Select the type of feedback you are providing (Feedback Type).
3. Choose the sections and sub-sections of the store that relate to your feedback.
4. Select the type(s) of product(s) that correspond to your feedback (Product Type).
5. If necessary, enter the specific product ID or a brief description of the product.
6. Rate the layout of the selected section or sub-section (Layout Rating).
7. Rate the product (Product Rating).
8. Add any comments or suggestions you have for improvement.
9. Enter your contact information (Contact) so we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Type** (Feedback Type, Select One, Optional): Choose one of the following:

* **Product Type** (Product Type, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all product types that correspond to your feedback. For example, "Smartphones" and "Tablets".
* **Product ID** (Product ID, Text, Optional): Enter the specific product ID or a brief description of the product. If you're unsure, leave this field blank.

* **Contact** (Contact, Email, Optional): Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.

Please note that all fields are optional, but your input will help us better understand your concerns and preferences. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
