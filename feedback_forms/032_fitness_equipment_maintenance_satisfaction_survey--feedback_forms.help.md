# Fitness Equipment Maintenance Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about the fitness equipment maintenance experience. It helps maintenance staff evaluate their work and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name or a unique identifier to help us track your feedback.
2. Choose the category that best describes your interaction with the maintenance staff.
3. Rate the condition of the equipment that was maintained.
4. Rate the performance of the maintenance staff.
5. Enter any comments or feedback you have about the experience.
6. Enter the date of your visit for accuracy and tracking purposes.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback about the staff that interacted with you.
