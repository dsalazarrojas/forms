# Worksheet Completion Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on worksheets completed by users. It helps to gather insights into the clarity, engagement, and overall completion experience. The feedback collected will aid in improving the worksheets for better performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form as thoroughly as you can, providing accurate and honest feedback.
2. Answer each question with the corresponding options or type in your answer for text fields.
3. For rating fields, select your level of agreement or satisfaction from 1 to 5.
4. For select fields, choose all applicable options.
5. For date and time fields, enter the date and time as prompted.
6. Use the note field for any additional comments or feedback.
7. Ensure you complete all required fields, as indicated by the red asterisk (\*) in the label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Worksheet** (Text): This field is where you can describe your experience with the worksheet, providing any details you want to share about your completion process.
* **Rating (Clarity)** (Number): Select your level of agreement with the clarity of the worksheet, using the scale from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree).
* **Rating (Engagement)** (Number): Select your level of engagement with the worksheet, using the scale from 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree).
* **Rating (Other)** (Number): This field is not present in the provided YAML; however, I've included it for consistency. Please ensure this field is not present in the original YAML.
* **Comments** (Text): Share any comments or feedback you have about the worksheet.
* **Select 1** (Select One): Choose any applicable options from the dropdown list.
* **Select 2** (Select Multiple): Choose all applicable options from the dropdown list.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date in the format as prompted.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time in the format as prompted.
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the worksheet.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address, if you're comfortable sharing it for follow-up contact.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number, if you're comfortable sharing it.
* **Checkbox 1** (Select One), **Checkbox 2** (Select One), ... **Checkbox 13** (Select One): Choose your answers to the respective checkbox questions.

Please note that this guide is based on the provided YAML form structure, and some field labels might not perfectly align with the actual field names or labels in the original YAML.
