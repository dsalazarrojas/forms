# Share Your Change Experiences - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Share Your Change Experiences" form is designed to collect feedback and experiences from users about their change experiences. This form helps us understand your perspectives and insights on how change affects you, and how you navigate through it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category and sub_category that best describe your change experience.
2. Enter a brief description of your change experience, including any challenges you faced.
3. Share the outcomes or results of your change experience.
4. Provide any advice or insights you have gained from your experience.
5. Optionally, select a date and time of your change experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **category** (Select One): Choose a category that best describes your change experience. This will help us understand the context of your experience.
* **sub_category** (Select One): Select a sub_category that further describes your change experience. This will help us understand the specific type of change you experienced.
* **date_of_change** (Date): If you are comfortable sharing the date of your change experience, please enter it here. This will help us track your progress over time.
* **time_of_change** (Time): If you are comfortable sharing the time of your change experience, please enter it here. This will help us understand the duration of your change experience.
* **story** (Text): Please enter a brief description of your change experience, including any challenges you faced and the outcomes you achieved. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts and insights on how change affects you.
* **advice** (Text): If you have any advice or insights to share with others who may be going through similar experiences, please enter them here.
* **challenges** (Text): If you faced any challenges during your change experience, please enter them here.
