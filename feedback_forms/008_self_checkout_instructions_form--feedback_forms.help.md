# Self Checkout Instructions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers after they have attempted to use the self-checkout system. The primary goal is to understand their experience and address any issues they may have encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please select the reason why you had difficulty with the self-checkout system.
2.  If you have comments or additional information about your experience, please use the "Comments" field.
3.  If you wish to be contacted to discuss your experience, please provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4.  If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Please submit your form by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback (Customer Feedback)**: 
    *   This field is used for customers to provide their feedback about their experience with the self-checkout system.
*   **Instructions Affected (Instructions Affected)**: 
    This field is required and will help us identify the problem you encountered with the self-checkout system. You can select one of the provided options or type something else.
*   **Self-Checkout Experience (Self-Checkout Experience)**: 
    *   If you feel that your experience with the self-checkout system was affected and you want to rate it on a scale of 1-5, please use this field.
*   **Comments (Comments)**: 
    *   This field is optional and can be used to provide additional information or context about your experience.
*   **Email Address (Email Address)**: 
    *   If you would like to be contacted to discuss your experience, please provide your email address.
*   **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: 
    *   If you would like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
*   **Submitted (Submitted)**: 
    *   This field is automatically populated with the current date and time when you submit the form.
*   **Submitted At (Submitted At)**: 
    *   This field is automatically populated with the current date and time when you submit the form.
