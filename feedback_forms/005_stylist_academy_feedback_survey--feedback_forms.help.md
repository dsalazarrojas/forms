# Stylist Academy Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience with the Stylist Academy, helping the academy to identify areas of improvement and monitor user engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and provide feedback on the satisfaction and recommendation sections.
2. Enter your name and contact email in the required fields.
3. Add any additional comments or improvements you would like to see implemented.
4. Choose your preferred date to submit the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Note, not required)
	+ This section is an optional space for you to provide any additional context or information about your experience with the Stylist Academy.
* **Satisfaction** (Number, not required)
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the Stylist Academy on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommendation** (Select One, not required)
	+ Choose a reason why you would recommend the Stylist Academy (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Comments** (Text, not required)
	+ If you have any comments or suggestions about the Stylist Academy, enter them here.
* **Satisfaction 2** (Number, not required)
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the Stylist Academy on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommendation 2** (Select One, not required)
	+ Choose a reason why you would recommend the Stylist Academy (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Comments 2** (Text, not required)
	+ If you have any comments or suggestions about the Stylist Academy, enter them here.
* **Name** (Email, required)
	+ Enter your name (will be used to contact you if needed).
* **Contact Email** (Email, required)
	+ Enter your contact email.
* **Phone** (Text, not required)
	+ Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Course** (Text, not required)
	+ If applicable, enter the course you are providing feedback on.
* **Date** (Date, not required)
	+ Enter the date you completed the course (optional).
* **Phone** (Text, not required)
	+ Enter your phone number (optional).

Note: Fields with a "not required" label are optional and do not impact the submission of your feedback.
