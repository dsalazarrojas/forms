# Seminar Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees of a seminar. The purpose of this form is to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of the seminar, and to gather suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as instructed. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions. You may skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Evaluation** (`seminar_evaluation_survey_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **What was the quality of the seminar** (`seminar_evaluation_what_was_the_quality_of_the_seminar`, text, required/optional): Please rate the quality of the seminar, e.g. good, average, poor.
* **What was effective for the participants** (`seminar_evaluation_what_was_effective_for_the_participants`, text, required/optional): Describe what you found most helpful or effective during the seminar.
* **What skills were acquired** (`seminar_evaluation_what_skills_were_acquired`, text, required/optional): List the skills or knowledge you acquired during the seminar.
* **What was improved** (`seminar_evaluation_what_was_improved`, text, required/optional): Describe any positive improvements or changes you made as a result of the seminar.
* **What will be done differently** (`seminar_evaluation_what_will_be_done_differently`, text, required/optional): Suggest any changes or improvements that could be made to future seminars.
* **What was helpful** (`seminar_evaluation_what_was_helpful`, text, required/optional): Describe any resources or tools that were helpful during the seminar.
* **What was not helpful** (`seminar_evaluation_what_was_not_helpful`, text, required/optional): Describe any resources or tools that were not helpful during the seminar.
* **What was missing** (`seminar_evaluation_what_was_missing`, text, required/optional): Suggest any missing elements or topics that were not covered during the seminar.
* **What was improved** (`seminar_evaluation_what_was_improved`, text, required/optional): Describe any positive improvements or changes you made as a result of the seminar.
* **What can be improved** (`seminar_evaluation_what_can_be_improved`, text, required/optional): Suggest any areas for improvement for future seminars.
* **Recommend this seminar** (`seminar_evaluation_recommend_this_seminar`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you would recommend this seminar to others.
* **Comments** (`seminar_evaluation_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the seminar.
* **Additional comments** (`seminar_evaluation_additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the seminar.
* **Seminar name** (`seminar_evaluation_seminar_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the seminar.
* **Seminar date** (`seminar_evaluation_seminar_date`, text, required/optional): Enter the date of the seminar.
* **Seminar time** (`seminar_evaluation_seminar_time`, text, required/optional): Enter the time of the seminar.
* **Seminar duration** (`seminar_evaluation_seminar_duration`, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the seminar.
* **Instructor** (`seminar_evaluation_instructor`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the instructor.
