# entrepreneurship_coaching_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from coaching sessions with entrepreneurs and business owners. The feedback is intended to help coaches evaluate their coaching practice and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form for individual coaching sessions.
- Make sure to provide feedback for only one coaching session per submission.
- Ensure you answer each question clearly and concisely.
- If a field is marked as "required", please fill it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coaching Session** (`coaching_session`, text, optional): Briefly describe the coaching session you are providing feedback for.
* **Goal Setting** (`feedback_on_goal_setting`, text, optional): Provide feedback on the goal setting process during the coaching session.
* **Relevance to Goals** (`relevance_to_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Effectiveness of Coaching** (`effectiveness_of_coaching`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of effectiveness of the coaching:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Identify areas where the coach could improve.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the coach.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Suggest any next steps or recommendations for the coach.
* **Coach Notes** (`coach_notes`, note, optional): Additional notes from the coach about the coaching session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person providing feedback (for follow-up purposes only).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person providing feedback (for follow-up purposes only).

## Tips
- Please answer each question truthfully and accurately.
- Keep your responses clear and concise.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
