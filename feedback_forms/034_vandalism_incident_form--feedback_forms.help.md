# Vandalism Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report a vandalism incident. Please fill out as much information as possible to help us investigate and resolve the incident efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the Incident Report section with a brief description of the incident.
- Proceed to fill out the Incident Details section with any relevant details about the incident.
- If applicable, fill out the Suspect Info section with information about the suspect vehicle.
- If you witnessed the incident, fill out the Witness Info section with your details and provide a statement about what you saw.
- Finally, enter any relevant information about the suspect vehicle, such as its make, model, and color.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`, text, required):
    Enter a brief description of the incident, including what happened and any relevant details.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required):
    Provide more details about the incident, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Suspect Info** (`suspect_info`, text, required):
    Enter any information about the suspect vehicle, including its make, model, and color.
* **Witness Info** (`witness_info`, text, required):
    Fill out your contact information so we can reach out to you for further questions or clarification.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, required):
    Provide a detailed account of what you witnessed, including what you saw and any notable actions or behaviors.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, required):
    Enter the date and time of the incident.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required):
    Enter the location of the incident.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, text, required):
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, email, required):
    Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or clarification.
* **Suspect Vehicle** (`suspect_vehicle`, text, required):
    Enter any information about the suspect vehicle, including its make and model.
* **Suspect Vehicle Type** (`suspect_vehicle_type`, text, required):
    Describe the type of vehicle, such as car, motorcycle, or truck.
* **Suspect Vehicle Description** (`suspect_vehicle_description`, text, required):
    Provide a detailed description of the suspect vehicle, including its color and any distinctive features.
* **Suspect Vehicle License** (`suspect_vehicle_license`, text, required):
    Enter the license plate number of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle Color** (`suspect_vehicle_color`, text, required):
    Enter the color of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle Make** (`suspect_vehicle_make`, text, required):
    Enter the make of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle Model** (`suspect_vehicle_model`, text, required):
    Enter the model of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle Year** (`suspect_vehicle_year`, text, required):
    Enter the year of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle License Number** (`suspect_vehicle_license_number`, text, required):
    Enter the license number of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle State** (`suspect_vehicle_state`, text, required):
    Enter the state where the suspect vehicle is registered.
* **Suspect Vehicle Country** (`suspect_vehicle_country`, text, required):
    Enter the country where the suspect vehicle is registered.
* **Suspect Vehicle Registration** (`suspect_vehicle_registration`, text, required):
    Enter the registration number of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle Color Code** (`suspect_vehicle_color_code`, text, required):
    Enter the color code of the suspect vehicle (e.g. red, blue, etc.).
* **Suspect Vehicle Country of Origin** (`suspect_vehicle_country_of_origin`, text, required):
    Enter the country of origin of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle Weight** (`suspect_vehicle_weight`, text, required):
    Enter the weight of the suspect vehicle.
* **Suspect Vehicle Additional Info** (`suspect_vehicle_additional_info`, text, required):
    Enter any additional information about the suspect vehicle that might be useful for the investigation.

Please note that some fields may not be applicable for every incident. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
