# IYJ Program Feedback Form Participant - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IYJ Program Feedback Form is designed to gather information on your experience and satisfaction with the IYJ Program. Your feedback will help us improve the program for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name (participant_program_name)** (`participant_program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you participated in.
* **Your Name (participant_name)** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification card.
* **Feedback (feedback)** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your overall feedback about the program. Describe your experience, what you liked and disliked, and any suggestions you may have.
* **Rating (rating)** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the program on a scale of 1-5.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, date, optional): If you want to provide a specific date for your feedback, you can enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, time, optional): If you want to provide a specific time for your feedback, you can enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further feedback.
* **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for further feedback.
* **Participant Feedback (participant_feedback)** (`participant_feedback`, select_multiple, required): Indicate if you were a participant in the program by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Program Areas (program_areas)** (`program_areas`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the areas of the program that you were involved in by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out for your feedback to be recorded.
