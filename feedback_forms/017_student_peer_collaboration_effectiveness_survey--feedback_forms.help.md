# Student Peer Collaboration Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their peer collaboration experience. It helps us understand how well the group work component of our program is meeting your needs. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and share your honest opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction page for context.
2. Rate your experience with each aspect of peer collaboration on a scale from 1-5.
3. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the text fields.
4. Submit your responses when you've finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**:
	+ **(No label)** (note, not required): This page provides background information about the survey and its purpose.
* **Group Work** (1, number, required): Rate your experience with group work, from 1 (not very effective) to 5 (very effective).
* **Communication** (2, number, required): Rate your experience with communication within your group, from 1 (not very effective) to 5 (very effective).
* **Challenges** (3, number, required): Rate any challenges you faced during group work, from 1 (many) to 5 (none).
* **Feedback** (4, text, optional): Share any specific feedback about your group work experience.
* **Suggestions** (5, text, optional): Suggest ways we could improve the group work component of the program.
* **Comments** (6, text, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.

Note: Please be honest and complete all required fields. Your feedback is valuable to us!
