# Travel Blog Reader Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from our travel blog readers to improve our services and better understand their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below, and we'll take care of the rest!

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Feedback** (`your_feedback`, text, required)
	+ Please share your thoughts, suggestions, and experiences about our travel blog. This will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve on.
* **Your Name** (`your_name`, text, required)
	+ Provide us with your name so we can address you properly when we respond to your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required)
	+ Enter your email address so we can get back to you regarding your feedback, collaboration inquiries, or other questions we may have.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
	+ If you have a phone number, please include it for our team to contact you more efficiently.
* **Your Message** (`your_message`, text, required)
	+ Share as much detail as possible about your experience, questions, or requests, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
* **How did you find us?** (`how_did_you_find_us`, select_one, required)
	+ Choose the most accurate way you found our travel blog:
		+ Yes: Through an advertisement.
		+ Yes: From a search engine.
		+ Other (please specify in the "Your Feedback" section).
* **How can we help you?** (`how_can_we_help_you`, select_multiple, required)
	+ Choose all the ways you'd like us to help you:
		+ Yes: Provide more travel-related information.
		+ Yes: Answer travel-related questions.
		+ Other (please specify in the "Your Feedback" section).
* **Collaboration Inquiries** (`collaboration_inquiries`, note, optional)
	+ If you're interested in collaborating with us on a project, please provide more information about your proposal and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
