# team_building_event_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from team members after a team-building event. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to help the team improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have attended the event in question and have a good understanding of its purpose and activities.
2. Read the form carefully to understand what information is being asked.
3. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
4. Double-check that you have not already filled out this form for the same event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Event Name
* **Event Name**: This field is where you should enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on. Please make sure to spell it correctly and use the exact title of the event.

### 2. Event Date
* **Event Date**: This field is where you should enter the date of the event. Use the date format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure accurate sorting and filtering.

### 3. Event Time
* **Event Time**: This field is where you should enter the time of the event. Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM) for accurate scheduling.

### 4. Feedback
* **Feedback**: This field is where you should provide your honest feedback on what went well and what didn't go well during the event. Be as detailed as possible to help the team improve.

### 5. Suggestions
* **Suggestions**: In this field, you can provide suggestions for future team-building events. Be creative and think about what would make the next event even more enjoyable and productive.

### 6. Participant Name
* **Participant Name**: This field is where you should enter your name as a participant of the event. Make sure to use your actual name or your nickname that is commonly used by the team.

### 7. Participant Email
* **Participant Email**: This field is where you should enter your work email address. This information is used to verify that you are a team member and to contact you if needed.

### 8. Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone**: This field is where you should enter your work phone number. This information is used to contact you if the team lead or event organizer needs to follow up with you.

### 9. Number of Participants
* **Number of Participants**: This field is where you should enter the number of people who attended the event. This information helps the team organizers plan future events with the right number of participants in mind.

### 10. Event Organizer
* **Event Organizer**: This field is where you should enter the person's name or initials who organized the event. This helps the team track who is responsible for the event's success or areas for improvement.
