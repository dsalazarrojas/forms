# Employee Feedback On Work Hours Policy - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from employees regarding the company's work hours policy. It aims to gather their perceptions and opinions on various aspects of their work experience, such as work hours, work-life balance, satisfaction, and other related topics. This feedback will be used to identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to the policy to ensure it aligns with the needs and expectations of all employees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer them honestly to the best of your ability.
2.  Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers whenever possible.
3.  Use the space provided for each question to type in your answers.
4.  Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Work hours** (`employee_work_hours`, text, optional): This question is asking for your general thoughts or opinions about the current work hours policy. Please type in any comments or suggestions you have regarding your working hours, such as any challenges or benefits you experience.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This question is an open-ended field where you can share any additional comments or feedback you have about your work experience that are not covered in the previous question.
*   **Work life balance** (`work_life_balance`, number, optional): Please rate how well your work hours align with your personal life, with 1 being "very poorly" and 5 being "very well".
*   **Work satisfaction** (`work_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with your current work hours, with 1 being "very low" and 5 being "very high".
*   **Communication** (`communication`, number, optional): Please rate how well you feel you communicate with your manager and colleagues regarding your work hours, with 1 being "very poorly" and 5 being "very well".
*   **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, number, optional): Please rate how well you collaborate with your colleagues regarding your work hours, with 1 being "very poorly" and 5 being "very well".
*   **Work lifestyle** (`work_lifestyle`, number, optional): Please rate how well your work hours fit into your overall lifestyle, with 1 being "very poorly" and 5 being "very well".
*   **Job satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with your current job, with 1 being "very low" and 5 being "very high".
*   **Company climate** (`company_climate`, number, optional): Please rate the overall climate of your company, with 1 being "very poor" and 5 being "very good".
*   **Job ambiguity** (`job_ambiguity`, number, optional): Please rate how clear your job expectations are, with 1 being "very unclear" and 5 being "very clear".

Note: All questions are optional, but providing answers will help us better understand your opinions and suggestions.
