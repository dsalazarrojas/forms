# Agent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a real estate agent. This form helps the agent improve their service quality and build stronger relationships with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name in the **Customer Full Name** field (id: 2).
2. Enter the **Name of Real Estate Agent** who served you (id: 3).
3. Provide the **Date of Transaction or Interaction** (id: 4).
4. Select the **Type of Service** provided (id: 5).
5. Evaluate the **Professionalism and Courtesy of the Agent** (id: 7).
6. Rate the **Agent's Knowledge of the Local Market** (id: 8).
7. Assess the **Effectiveness of Communication and Responsiveness** (id: 9).
8. Rate the **Negotiation Skills and Outcome Satisfaction** (id: 10).
9. Evaluate the overall **Overall Satisfaction** with the agent's service (id: 11).
10. Provide **Feedback on Strengths** (id: 12) if the agent did something exceptionally well.
11. If needed, offer **Constructive Criticism** (id: 13) for areas where the agent could improve.
12. Finally, indicate if you would **Use the Agent Again in the Future** (id: 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Full Name** (id: 2): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Name of Real Estate Agent** (id: 3): Type in the name of the real estate agent who provided you service.
* **Date of Transaction or Interaction** (id: 4): Enter the date of the transaction or last interaction with the agent (e.g., 2023-02-20).
* **Type of Service** (id: 5): Select the type of service you received (e.g., Buying a home, Selling a home, etc.).
* **Professionalism and Courtesy of the Agent** (id: 7): Evaluate the agent's professionalism on a scale from 1 to 5.
	+ 1 - Poor: The agent was not very professional or courteous.
	+ 2 - Unprofessional: The agent was not professional or showed a lack of courtesy.
	+ 3 - Average: The agent was just average in terms of professionalism.
	+ 4 - Professional: The agent was professional and showed good courtesy.
	+ 5 - Exceptional: The agent was extremely professional and showed excellent courtesy.
* **Agent's Knowledge of the Local Market** (id: 8): Evaluate the agent's knowledge of the local market.
	+ Highly Expert: The agent had an exceptional understanding of the market.
	+ Knowledgeable: The agent had a good understanding of the market.
	+ Fair: The agent had a basic understanding of the market.
	+ Poor Knowledge: The agent had a weak understanding of the market.
* **Effectiveness of Communication and Responsiveness** (id: 9): Evaluate the agent's communication and responsiveness.
	+ Always Responsive: The agent responded promptly and was always available.
	+ Mostly Responsive: The agent responded mostly promptly.
	+ Slow Response: The agent responded slowly or was not always available.
	+ Difficult to Reach: The agent was difficult to contact.
* **Negotiation Skills and Outcome Satisfaction** (id: 10): Evaluate the outcome of the negotiation.
	+ Extremely Satisfied: The agent got you a great deal or exceeded your expectations.
	+ Satisfied: The agent got you a good deal or met your expectations.
	+ Neutral: The outcome was neutral or not particularly impressive.
	+ Dissatisfied: The agent didn't get you a good deal or met your expectations.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (id: 11): Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the agent's service.
	+ Excellent: You were very satisfied with the service.
	+ Good: You were mostly satisfied with the service.
	+ Average: You were just average with the service.
	+ Below Average: You were not very satisfied with the service.
	+ Poor: You were not satisfied at all with the service.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (id: 12): Enter any positive feedback about what the agent did exceptionally well.
* **Improvement Areas** (id: 13): Enter any constructive criticism about areas where the agent could improve.
* **Would You Use Again?** (id: 14): Indicate if you would use this agent again in the future.

Note: Please answer each question honestly, and provide specific examples or details when possible. Your feedback is valuable to us!
