# feedback_authenticity_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the authenticity of feedback received from customers. It helps in understanding the type and reason for feedback and the method used to verify it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Choose the type of feedback from the "Type of Feedback" dropdown menu.
4. If the feedback is inauthentic, provide a reason for it in the "Reason for Inauthenticity" field.
5. Select one or more verification methods that were used to verify the feedback in the "Verification Method" field.
6. If other verification methods were used that are not listed, enter them in the "Other" field.
7. Enter the date and time the feedback was verified in the "Date Verified" and "Time Verified" fields.
8. Enter the name of the person who verified the feedback in the "Verified By" field.
9. Provide any additional feedback in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer who provided the feedback.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer who provided the feedback.
* **Type of Feedback**: Select the type of feedback from the dropdown menu:
	+ Authentic
	+ Non Authentic
* **Reason for Inauthenticity**: If the feedback is inauthentic, enter the reason why.
* **Verification Method**: Select one or more methods used to verify the feedback:
	+ Phone call
	+ Video call
	+ In-person meeting
	+ Document verification
* **Other**: Enter any other methods used to verify the feedback, if not listed above.
* **Date Verified**: Enter the date the feedback was verified.
* **Time Verified**: Enter the time the feedback was verified.
* **Verified By**: Enter the name of the person who verified the feedback.
* **Feedback**: Provide any additional feedback.
