# Gathering Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience or thoughts on a particular product or service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as prompted. If a field is marked as required, it must be filled out before submitting the form. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What did you think?** (`feedback_form_1`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts and opinions about the experience or product you've used.
- **How would you rate this experience?** (`feedback_form_2`, select_one, optional): Rate the experience from Good to Fair.
- **What was the outcome?** (`feedback_form_3`, note, optional): Share any details about the outcome or results of your experience.
- **What could be improved?** (`feedback_form_4`, text, optional): Suggest ways to improve the product or service.
- **Additional feedback** (`feedback_form_5`, text, optional): Share any further comments or thoughts about your experience.
- **Rating** (`feedback_form_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant ratings for your experience.
- **Contact** (`feedback_form_7`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, enter your email address.
- **Additional comment** (`feedback_form_8`, text, optional): This field is currently not used. Please disregard this field.
- **Time** (`feedback_form_9`, time, optional): Not applicable.
- **Date** (`feedback_form_10`, date, optional): Not applicable.
