# Workout Feature Usability Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your feedback on our workout feature. Your input will help us improve the feature to better meet your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions in the form below as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. For each question, provide detailed and specific information to help us understand your thoughts.
3. You can submit your response once you've completed all the fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, you can use the "Additional Comments" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How would you describe your experience with the current feature?**
  This question helps us understand your familiarity with the feature. Please share your thoughts on how you've been using the feature, including any positive or negative experiences.
  - **What is the feature you are currently using or have used recently?**
    This question helps us pinpoint the specific feature you're referring to. Please provide the name or a description of the feature.
  - **How would you describe the feature's functionality or purpose?**
    This question helps us understand the feature's core functionality. Please explain how the feature is used or what it's intended to do.
  - **How would you rate the feature's usefulness to you?**
    On a scale of 1-5, rate how useful you find the feature to be in your workouts.
  - **What would you improve in this feature?**
    Share your thoughts on what the feature could be improved, whether it's a bug, a missing feature, or any changes you think would make it more useful.
  - **Do you have any suggestions for this feature?**
    If you have any additional ideas or suggestions for enhancing the feature, please share them here.
  - **On a scale from 1 to 5, how would you rate the feature?**
    Rate how much you like the feature, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
  - **Do you have any additional comments about the feature?**
    If you'd like to elaborate on your answers or share any other thoughts, you can do so here.
