# Volunteer Satisfaction Survey Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate the satisfaction of volunteers with our program. We value your feedback and opinions, and this form helps us understand how to better serve you and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
2. If you selected "Yes" for "Volunteer Role", please select all relevant roles you have been involved in.
3. Please select one of the three satisfaction options for "How satisfied are you with our volunteer program?".
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please provide them in the "Additional Feedback" field.
5. If you have a personal email or phone number, please enter them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Feedback** (`volunteer_satisfaction_survey_evaluation_form`, text, optional): This is the main question where you can provide your feedback about our volunteer program.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name so we can address you personally.
* **Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_multiple, optional): If you have been involved in multiple roles, please select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ Yes
* **How satisfied are you with our volunteer program?** (`volunteer_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select one of the three options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted directly, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number you'd like us to contact you on, please enter it.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): If you know the date you last participated, please enter it.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If you know the time you last participated, please enter it.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): You can provide any additional notes or comments here. 

Note: This form is meant to be completed at your own pace, and you don't have to answer all the questions if you're not comfortable providing information. However, your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your honesty.
