# Compassion Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and stories of compassionate acts that have occurred within our organization or community. It helps us understand the impact of these acts and identify areas for improvement in fostering a kinder culture.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form header to begin.
2. Fill out each field carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Select the most relevant options for each dropdown field.
4. Enter your consent to share your feedback publicly or internally.
5. Click "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Sharing a Compassionate Moment --** (`compassion_event_header`, note, required: false): This field is a header section for you to describe the compassionate act that occurred.
* **Who performed the act of compassion?** (`compassionate_giver`, text, required: true): Enter the name of the person or group that performed the compassionate act.
* **Who received the act of compassion?** (`compassionate_recipient`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or group who received the compassionate act.
* **Approximately when did this occur?** (`event_date`, date, required: true): Enter the date of the event.
* **Where did this take place?** (`event_location`, text, required: true): Enter the location where the event occurred.
* **Describe the act of compassion** (`act_description`, text, required: true): Provide a brief story of what happened.
* **What was the impact of this act on you or the community?** (`impact_rating`, select_one, required: true): Choose the most relevant description of the impact from the dropdown options.
* **How often do you observe acts like this in our environment?** (`compassion_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Choose the frequency with which you observe compassionate behavior.
* **Suggestions for fostering a kinder culture** (`fostering_kindness_suggestions`, text, required: false): Provide any suggestions you have for encouraging more compassionate behavior.
* **Your Name (Optional)** (`submitter_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name, or remain anonymous.
* **Do we have permission to share this story publicly or internally?** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required: true): Choose how you'd like us to share your feedback.
