# Photography Studio Management Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from clients about their experience with our photography studio. The feedback gathered will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the option that best describes your experience with our studio.
2. Choose the services you used.
3. Rate our service from 1 to 5.
4. Provide any additional comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was your experience with us?** (`client_feedback`, text, required): This is a open-ended text field where you can describe your experience with our studio. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* **What services did you use?** (`studio_services`, select_one, required): Choose one or more of the options that best describe the services you used. You can select only one service or multiple services if you used multiple options.
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **How would you rate our service?** (`rating`, number, required): Rate our service from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. Think about the quality of service you received.
* **Do you have any comments?** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to share them here. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback that may not be possible in the previous fields.
