# Reconnect_with_us_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us reconnect with users who were unable to complete their previous interactions. It's a crucial piece of feedback that helps us understand how to improve and better serve our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your contact information, such as your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you.
3. If you're representing a business, fill out the business name, position, and department.
4. Select the date and time you last interacted with us.
5. Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.
6. Rate your experience with us using the rating scale.
7. Choose how you'd like to be contacted back.
8. Confirm whether you'd like to be followed up with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First_Name (Text)**: Your first name.
* **Last_Name (Text)**: Your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Your phone number.
* **Business_Name (Text)**: If you're representing a business, enter the name of the business.
* **Position (Text)**: If you're representing a business, enter your position within the company.
* **Department (Text)**: If you're representing a business, enter the department you're associated with.
* **Company (Text)**: If you're representing a business, enter the name of the company.
* **Date (Date)**: The date of your last interaction with us.
* **Time (Time)**: The time of your last interaction with us.
* **Comments (Text)**: Share any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Rating (Number)**: Rate your experience with us on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Rating_Options (Select One)**: Choose how you'd rate your experience (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Rating_Scale (Number)**: Rate your experience with us on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Reconnect_With_Us (Select One)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted back (True, False).
* **Follow_Up (Select One)**: Choose whether you'd like to be followed up with (True, False).
