# Community Health Initiative Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect feedback from the community on the "Community Health Initiative" program. Your input will help us better understand your experience and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the age range that applies to you from the options provided.
2. Indicate if you have attended the program.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction level with the program.
4. Choose the component that was most helpful to you (text field for free response).
5. Share any additional comments or suggestions you have for the program.
6. Tell us if you would recommend the program to others.
7. Provide your email address if you want to be contacted for follow-up discussions.
8. Consent to share your feedback anonymously for reporting purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age range** (`respondent_age_range`, select_one, required): Select the range that applies to you:
	+ Under 18
	+ 18 to 34
	+ 35 to 54
	+ 55 and over
* **Have you attended the program** (`attended_program`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have participated in the program:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Overall satisfaction level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, required): Rate your experience:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Most helpful component** (`most_helpful_component`, text, optional): This is where you can share which part of the program was most helpful to you. Write as much or as little as you like.
* **Suggested improvements** (`suggested_improvements`, text, optional): Share any ideas you have for improving the program. This is an opportunity to provide detailed feedback.
* **Would you recommend this program** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): Let us know if you would recommend the program:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any other thoughts or comments on the program.
* **Contact for follow up** (`contact_for_follow_up`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, provide your email address here.
* **Consent to share feedback anonymously** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Agree to share your responses anonymously for reporting purposes:
	+ True
	+ False
