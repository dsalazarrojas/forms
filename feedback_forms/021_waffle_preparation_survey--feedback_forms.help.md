# waffle_preparation_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your waffle preferences, experiences, improvement areas, likings, and dislikes. It will help us understand your waffle preferences and help us tailor our waffle-making skills to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your waffle preferences from the dropdown menu on page 1.
2. Enter your waffle experiences, including any relevant details, on page 2.
3. Share your waffle improvement areas, where you think we could improve, on page 3.
4. List your waffle likings, such as favorite toppings or flavors, on page 4.
5. Share any waffle dislikes, such as foods or ingredients you don't like, on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Waffle Preferences**: Select the waffles that you like or dislike. This will help us know what type of waffles you prefer.
 	+ **Waffle Preferences**: `Yes/No` options will be displayed. Please select all options that apply to you.
* **Waffle Experiences**: Describe any waffle experiences you've had, including any relevant details. This will help us understand your familiarity with waffles.
 	+ **Waffle Experiences**: Enter a short description of your waffle experiences.
* **Waffle Improvement Areas**: Identify any areas where you think we could improve our waffle-making skills.
 	+ **Waffle Improvement Areas**: Enter a short description of how we can improve, such as "more toppings" or "fresher ingredients".
* **Waffle Likings**: Share your waffle likings, including favorite toppings, flavors, or other preferences.
 	+ **Waffle Likings**: Enter a list or a brief description of your waffle likings.
* **Waffle Dislikes**: Share any waffle dislikes, including foods or ingredients you don't like.
 	+ **Waffle Dislikes**: Enter a list or a brief description of your waffle dislikes.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact us.
- Your input will help us improve our waffle-making skills and better serve your waffle needs.
