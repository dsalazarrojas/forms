# Q1 Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and comments from users about their experiences with our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide as much information as you can about your experience. The form has multiple pages, each with several fields. Please go through each page and fill out the fields in the order they appear.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (first_name, text, Optional): Enter your first name.
  - **Last name** (last_name, text, Optional): Enter your last name.
  - **Email** (email, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
  - **Phone** (phone, text, Optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
  - **Feedback** (feedback, text, Optional): Share your thoughts and comments about your experience.
  - **Date of visit** (date_of_visit, date, Optional): If you'd like to specify the date of your visit, enter it here.
  - **Time of visit** (time_of_visit, time, Optional): If you'd like to specify the time of your visit, enter it here.
  - **Rating** (rating, number, Optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
  - **Comments** (comments, text, Optional): Additional comments or suggestions.
  - **Select one** (select_one, select_one, Optional): Choose one option that best describes your experience.
  - **Select multiple** (select_multiple, select_multiple, Optional): Choose multiple options that best describe your experience.
  - **Note** (note, note, Optional): Any additional information you'd like to include.
  - **Email sent** (email_sent, email, Optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted.
  - **Phone sent** (phone_sent, text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
  - **Date sent** (date_sent, date, Optional): If you'd like to specify the date you were contacted, enter it here.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback.
- If you have any concerns or issues, please provide as much information as you can about them.
- If you've already completed this form, you don't need to do so again.
