# Content Creator Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Content Creator Recommendation Form is designed to collect feedback and recommendations on content creators. This form helps identify and promote talented and reliable professionals in the industry. It's essential for the recommender to provide honest and constructive feedback to help creators grow and improve their skills.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the full name and email of the content creator you're recommending.
3. Select how you know the content creator (e.g., former employer, current employer, colleague, etc.).
4. Rate the content creator's quality of work, creativity, reliability, communication skills, professionalism, and ability to meet deadlines.
5. Provide your relationship duration with the content creator.
6. Describe the content creator's specialty (e.g., video content, written content, etc.).
7. Rate the content creator's strengths and areas for growth.
8. Optionally, describe any notable projects they've worked on and provide additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recommender Name** (`recommender_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person providing the recommendation.
* **Recommender Email** (`recommender_email`, `email`, required): This is the email address of the person providing the recommendation. This will be used for verification purposes.
* **Recommender Phone** (`recommender_phone`, `text`, optional): This is the contact number of the person providing the recommendation.
* **Recommender Title** (`recommender_title`, `text`, required): This is the job title or position of the person providing the recommendation.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the company or organization the recommender belongs to.
* **Recommendation Date** (`recommendation_date`, `date`, required): This is the date of the recommendation.
* **Content Creator Name** (`creator_name`, `text`, required): This is the full name of the content creator being recommended.
* **Content Creator Email** (`creator_email`, `email`, optional): This is the email address of the content creator being recommended.
* **Relationship to Creator** (`relationship_to_creator`, `select_one`, required): This is how the recommender knows the content creator (e.g., former employer, current employer, etc.).
* **Relationship Duration** (`relationship_duration`, `select_one`, required): This is how long the recommender has known the content creator.
* **Creator Specialty** (`creator_specialty`, `select_one`, required): This is the type of content the creator specializes in (e.g., video content, written content, etc.).
* **Quality of Work** (`quality_of_work`, `select_one`, required): This rate the quality of the content creator's work (e.g., excellent, very good, etc.).
* **Creativity Rating** (`creativity_rating`, `select_one`, required): This rate the content creator's creativity (e.g., exceptional, very creative, etc.).
* **Reliability Rating** (`reliability_rating`, `select_one`, required): This rate the content creator's reliability (e.g., extremely reliable, very reliable, etc.).
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, `select_one`, required): This rate the content creator's communication skills (e.g., excellent, very good, etc.).
* **Professionalism** (`professionalism`, `select_one`, required): This rate the content creator's professionalism (e.g., excellent, very good, etc.).
* **Ability to Meet Deadlines** (`ability_to_meet_deadlines`, `select_one`, required): This rate the content creator's ability to meet deadlines (e.g., always on time, usually on time, etc.).
* **Teamwork Skills** (`teamwork_skills`, `select_one`, required): This rate the content creator's teamwork skills (e.g., excellent, very good, etc.).
* **Would You Hire or Work With Them Again?** (`would_hire_again`, `select_one`, required): This is how likely you are to hire or work with the content creator again (e.g., definitely yes, probably yes, etc.).
* **Recommendation Strength** (`recommendation_strength`, `select_one`, required): This rate how strongly you recommend the content creator (e.g., highly recommend, recommend, etc.).
* **Creator Strengths** (`strengths`, `text`, required): This is a description of the content creator's greatest strengths.
* **Areas for Growth** (`areas_for_growth`, `text`, optional): This is a description of areas the content creator could improve in.
* **Notable Projects** (`notable_projects`, `text`, optional): This is a description of notable projects the content creator has worked on.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): This is any other information you'd like to share about the content creator.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective in your ratings and comments.
* Provide specific examples when describing the content creator's strengths and areas for growth.
* Ensure that you're providing a genuine recommendation and not submitting duplicate forms for the same creator.
