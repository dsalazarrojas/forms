# New Year Event Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year Event Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a New Year event. The feedback will help the organizers understand the attendees' experiences, likes, and dislikes, which will be used to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and submit your feedback. Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback, as it will be used to improve the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address to allow us to follow up with you in case we need more information or to send you a confirmation email.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Please provide your honest feedback about the event. Be as detailed as possible, including what you liked and disliked about the event.

## Tips
* Please be respectful and constructive in your feedback.
* Be as detailed as possible in your feedback, including specific examples or suggestions.
* If you have any attachments or photos to support your feedback, you may attach them to your email response.
* If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please email us at form@gic.mx.
