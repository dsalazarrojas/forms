# retail_product_placement_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retail product placement feedback form is designed to gather customer feedback on retail products, allowing the business to refine their product offerings and improve customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
2. Select a rating for the product from the "Product Rating" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the product price, if applicable.
4. Choose the aisle where the product is located from the "Product Placement" dropdown menu.
5. Provide a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
6. Enter the store hours of operation.
7. Enter the name of the employee who assisted you with the product.
8. Enter your name as the customer.
9. Enter your email address, if you would like to receive a response to your feedback.
10. Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Product Name (id: 1, text, required: false)
• Enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on.

### Product Rating (id: 2, select_one, required: false)
• Select a rating for the product from the dropdown menu: 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5.

### Product Price (id: 3, number, required: false)
• Enter the price of the product, if applicable.

### Product Placement (id: 4, select_multiple, required: false)
• Choose the aisle where the product is located from the dropdown menu: Aisle 1, Aisle 2, Aisle 3, Aisle 4, or Aisle 5.

### Product Description (id: 5, text, required: false)
• Provide a brief description of the product.

### Store Hours (id: 6, time, required: false)
• Enter the store hours of operation.

### Employee Name (id: 7, text, required: false)
• Enter the name of the employee who assisted you with the product.

### Customer Name (id: 8, text, required: false)
• Enter your name as the customer.

### Email (id: 9, email, required: false)
• Enter your email address, if you would like to receive a response to your feedback.

### Phone (id: 10, text, required: false)
• Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback to help the retail business improve their product offerings.
- You can choose to remain anonymous if you do not provide an email or phone number.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the retail business directly.
