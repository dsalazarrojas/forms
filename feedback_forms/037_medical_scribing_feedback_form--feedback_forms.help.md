# Medical Scribing Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Scribing Feedback Form is used to gather feedback on scribes and clinicians. This form collects information on clinic and scribe performance, including their availability, feedback score, and ratings. The data collected will help in making informed decisions about future scheduling and staffing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete all required fields.
2. Select the correct values from the dropdown menus for scribe and clinician shift, level, and availability.
3. Enter the feedback score and ratings according to your experience with the scribe and clinician.
4. Fill in the scribe and clinician comments sections with any additional feedback you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Clinic Name** (`clinic_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the clinic where you interacted with the scribe or clinician.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, required: true): Rate your experience with the scribe on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: true): Provide any comments or feedback on your experience with the scribe.
* **Scribe Name** (`scribe_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the scribe who provided care to you.
* **Scribe Email** (`scribe_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the scribe who provided care to you.
* **Scribe Phone** (`scribe_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the scribe who provided care to you.
* **Scribe Shift** (`scribe_shift`, select_one, required: true): Select the shift during which you interacted with the scribe.
* **Scribe Level** (`scribe_level`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the level of experience of the scribe who provided care to you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: true): Enter the date during which you interacted with the scribe.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time during which you interacted with the scribe. (Note: If left blank, it will be considered as unknown)
* **Scribe Comment** (`scribe_comment`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the scribe.
* **Clinician Name** (`clinician_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the clinician who provided care to you.
* **Clinician Email** (`clinician_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the clinician who provided care to you.
* **Clinician Phone** (`clinician_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the clinician who provided care to you.
* **Clinician Shift** (`clinician_shift`, select_one, required: true): Select the shift during which you interacted with the clinician.
* **Clinician Level** (`clinician_level`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the level of experience of the clinician who provided care to you.
* **Scribe Scheduling** (`scribe_scheduling`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your availability for scribe scheduling.
* **Scribe Availability** (`scribe_availability`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your availability for scribe shifts.
* **Clinician Availability** (`clinician_availability`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the availability of the clinician who provided care to you.
* **Scribe Notes** (`scribe_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the scribe.
* **Clinician Notes** (`clinician_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the clinician.
* **Scribe Rating** (`scribe_rating`, number, required: true): Rate your experience with the scribe on a scale of 1-10.
* **Clinician Rating** (`clinician_rating`, number, required: true): Rate your experience with the clinician on a scale of 1-10.
* **Clinician Comments** (`clinician_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback on your experience with the clinician.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: true): Enter your name, initials, or other identifier.

This form is used to gather feedback on scribes and clinicians to improve their performance and scheduling. Please take a few minutes to thoughtfully complete this form with your experience. Your input will help make our team better.
