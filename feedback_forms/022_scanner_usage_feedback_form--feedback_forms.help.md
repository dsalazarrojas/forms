# Scanner Usage Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scanner Usage Feedback Form is designed to gather information about your experience with the scanner device. Your feedback is crucial to help us improve the device's performance, usability, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant device from the Scanner Device dropdown menu.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the scanner device on a scale of 0-100.
3. Choose one or more categories from the Feedback Category dropdown menu.
4. Provide additional comments in the Scanner Usage Comments field.
5. Enter any additional comments or concerns in the Additional Comments field.
6. Enter your contact information, including your email address.
7. Provide the date and time you scanned using the device.
8. Enter the date you scanned the document or file.
9. Enter the time you scanned the document or file.
10. Enter the version of the scanner device you used (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your experience.
* **Scanner Device** (`feedback_device`, select_one, required: false): Select the specific device you were using when you experienced the issue or had the feedback.
* **User Satisfaction** (`user_satisfaction`, number, required: false): Rate your satisfaction with the scanner device on a scale of 0-100.
* **Feedback Category** (`feedback_category`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more categories that best describe your feedback.
* **Scanner Usage Comments** (`scanner_usage_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or details about your experience with the scanner device.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any other comments or concerns you may have about the scanner device or our service.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email address, in case we need to follow up with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required: false): Provide the date and time you scanned using the device.
* **Date Scanned** (`date_scanned`, date, required: false): Enter the date you scanned the document or file.
* **Time Scanned** (`time_scanned`, time, required: false): Enter the time you scanned the document or file.
* **Scanner Version** (`scanner_version`, text, required: false): Enter the version of the scanner device you used (if known).
