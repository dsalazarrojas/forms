# Feedback On Recitation Concepts - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on recitation concepts. Your input will help us improve the recitation experience for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form with your feedback, and click the "Submit" button at the end.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
3. If you're unsure about any field, use the available options or provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session** (`session`, `number`, **required:** No): Select the session number where you attended the recitation. If you're providing feedback on multiple sessions, you can select all relevant sessions.
* **Questions Asked** (`questions`, `select_one`, **required:** No): Choose whether you were able to understand and answer the questions asked during the recitation. If you were confused, select "No".
* **Concepts Covered** (`concepts`, `text`, **required:** No): Describe any concepts that you found difficult to understand or would like to see covered in more detail.
* **Difficulty Level** (`difficulty`, `select_multiple`, **required:** No): Indicate how challenging you found the recitation experience to be. Select all relevant difficulty levels that apply.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, **required:** No): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the recitation concepts.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, **required:** No): Provide any suggestions you have for improving the recitation experience.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, **required:** No): This is the final step to submit your feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required:** No): Enter your email address for follow-up or to receive further updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required:** No): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Recitation Time** (`recitation_time`, `date`, **required:** No): Provide the time when the recitation took place, if known.
* **Recitation Day** (`recitation_day`, `date`, **required:** No): Provide the date when the recitation took place, if known.
* **Recitation Date** (`recitation_date`, `date`, **required:** No): This field seems to be a duplicate of "Recitation Day". If you're unsure, use "Recitation Day" instead.
* **Recitation Location** (`recitation_location`, `text`, **required:** No): Enter the location where the recitation took place, if known.
* **Recitation Room** (`recitation_room`, `text`, **required:** No): Enter the room number or name where the recitation took place, if known. 

Note: The fields marked as "required: No" are optional and can be skipped if not applicable.
