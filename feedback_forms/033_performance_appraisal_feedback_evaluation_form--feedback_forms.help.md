# Performance Appraisal Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on performance appraisals after an appraisal meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the first page by providing your feedback on the performance appraisal process. This includes your rating of your performance (Improved, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory) and any comments you may have.
2.  Choose the correct rater (Manager, Director, or Team Member) who evaluated your performance.
3.  Enter the appraisal date in the designated date field.
4.  If you have any comments about the performance appraisal process, please type them in the comments field.
5.  If you have suggestions for improvement, choose from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Performance Appraisal Feedback** (`performance_appraisal_feedback`): This field is for providing your overall rating of your performance (Improved, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory). Please choose the one that best reflects your performance.
*   **Performance Appraisal Comments** (`performance_appraisal_comments`): If you have any comments about your performance, please type them here. This is an optional field, but your feedback will help improve the performance appraisal process.
*   **Performance Improvement Actions** (`performance_improvement_actions`): Choose from the options provided to suggest areas where you feel your performance can be improved.
*   **Rater** (`performance_rater`): Please select the rater (Manager, Director, or Team Member) who evaluated your performance.
*   **Appraisal Date** (`performance_appraisal_date`): Enter the date of the performance appraisal meeting.
*   **Page 2** (`performance_appraisal_feedback_evaluation_form_page_2`): This field is not required, but it's here in case you need to add more information or comments.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
*   Make sure to review your feedback carefully before submitting it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the contact listed at the top of this help guide.
