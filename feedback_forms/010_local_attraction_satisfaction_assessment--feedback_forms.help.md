# Local Attraction Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Local Attraction Satisfaction Assessment is a set of questions designed to gauge visitors' satisfaction and perceptions of a local attraction. Its purpose is to collect feedback on various aspects of the attraction, such as staff service, facilities, and overall experience.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Be honest in your answers, as your feedback is valuable to us.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Comments" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Feedback** (`visitor_feedback`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any comments or feedback about your visit. If you didn't notice anything notable, just mention so.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Experience** (`score_1`, select one, optional): Rate your overall experience as Very Good, Somewhat Good, Somewhat Bad, or Very Bad.
* **Facilities** (`score_2`, select one, optional): Rate the facilities as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What would improve facilities?** (`facilities_improve`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our facilities, mention them here.
* **Staff Service** (`staff_service`, select one, optional): Rate the staff service as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Staff Welcomed By** (`staff_welcomed_by`, select one, optional): Indicate who welcomed you during your visit (Staff, Tour Guide, or Other).
* **Visit Purpose** (`visit_purpose`, select one, optional): Indicate the purpose of your visit (Tour, Exhibit, Park, or Other).
* **Visit Frequency** (`visit_frequency`, select one, optional): Indicate how many times you have visited the attraction (First Time, Second Time, Third Time, or More Than Three Times).
* **Staff Knowledge** (`staff_knowledge`, select one, optional): Rate the staff's knowledge as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the attraction as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, mention them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you want to receive any updates or special offers, provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you want to receive any updates or special offers.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are visiting on behalf of your company, please enter the name of your company.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, optional): The date of your visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, time, optional): The time of your visit.
* **Visit Duration** (`visit_duration`, number, optional): The duration of your visit.
* **Recommend** (`recommend`, select one, optional): Would you recommend this attraction? (True, False, or Maybe).
* **Visit Again** (`visit_again`, select one, optional): Would you visit this attraction again? (True, False, or Maybe).

Note that all fields are optional, so feel free to skip any that don't apply to your experience.
