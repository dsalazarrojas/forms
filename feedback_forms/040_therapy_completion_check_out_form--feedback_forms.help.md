# Therapy Completion Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Completion Check Out Form is a tool used by therapists to document the completion of a therapy session. It allows therapists to record important details about the session, including client feedback, session date and time, and next session information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete all required fields.
3. Enter your client's name in the "Client Name" field.
4. Enter your client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
5. Enter the session date in the "Session Date" field.
6. Select the status of the session in the "Session Status" field.
7. If scheduling a next session, enter the status and date of the next session in the "Next Session Status" and "Next Session Date" fields respectively.
8. Sign the form with your client's signature in the "Client Signature" field.
9. Sign the form with your therapist's signature in the "Therapist Signature" field.
10. Click "Submit" to complete and save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback (1)** (`Session Feedback`, text, required/optional): Enter any feedback you want to share with your client after the session.
* **Session Date (2)** (`Session Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **Client Name (3)** (`Client Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email (4)** (`Client Email`, email, required/optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone (9)** (`Client Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Session Time (5)** (`Session Time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Therapist Assigned (10)** (`Assigned Therapist`, select multiple, required): Select the therapist who conducted the session.
* **Client Signature (11)** (`Client Signature`, note, required/optional): Sign the form with the client's signature.
* **Therapist Signature (12)** (`Therapist Signature`, note, required/optional): Sign the form with the therapist's signature.
* **Session Status (13)** (`Session Status`, select one, required): Select the status of the session.
* **Next Session Status (14)** (`Next Session Status`, select one, required/optional): Select the status of the next session.
* **Next Session Date (15)** (`Next Session Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the next session.
* **Next Session Time (16)** (`Next Session Time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the next session.
* **Next Session Location (17)** (`Next Session Location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the next session.
* **Session Notes (7)** (`Session Notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any notes about the session.
* **Session Time (8)** (`Session Time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Next Session (8)** (`Next Session`, text, required/optional): Enter the next session information. 

Note: Please ensure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
