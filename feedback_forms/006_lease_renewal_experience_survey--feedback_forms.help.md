# Lease Renewal Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the lease renewal process. It's essential to provide your honest feedback to help us improve this process for future users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields, which are marked as "required".
3. You can leave fields that are not marked as "required" blank, but please don't forget to click "Submit" to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Lease Renewal Process** (lease_renewal_process)
A brief description of your experience with the lease renewal process.

### 2. **Satisfaction with Renewal** (satisfaction_with_renewal)
Please rate your satisfaction with the lease renewal process on a scale of:

* High: Very satisfied
* Low: Very dissatisfied
* Medium: Somewhat satisfied

### 3. **Suggestions for Improvement** (suggestions_for_improvement)
If you have any suggestions for improving the lease renewal process, please provide them here.

### 4. **Contact for Renewal** (contact_for_renewal)
Please enter the contact information for the person who handled your lease renewal.

### 5. **Communication Frequency** (communication_frequency)
Please select the frequency of communication you received from us during the renewal process:

### 6. **Renewal Date** (renewal_date)
Enter the date of the lease renewal.

### 7. **Lease Length** (lease_length)
Enter the length of your lease.

### 8. **Renewal Process Easiness** (renewal_process_ease)
Please rate your experience with the ease of the renewal process:

* Very eager
* Somewhat eager
* Not very eager
* Not at all eager

### 9. **Lease Terms** (lease_terms)
Please provide any additional comments about your lease terms.

### 10. **Comments** (comments)
Any other comments or concerns you'd like to share?

Please click "Submit" when you're finished to save your changes.
