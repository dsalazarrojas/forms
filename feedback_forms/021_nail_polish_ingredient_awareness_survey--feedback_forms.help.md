# Nail Polish Ingredient Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Nail Polish Ingredient Awareness Survey aims to gather information about your level of knowledge and concerns regarding the ingredients used in nail polishes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the introduction page.
2. Select your level of knowledge about the ingredients used in nail polishes.
3. Choose the ingredients you have heard of or used before.
4. Identify your concerns about nail polish ingredients.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How well do you know about the ingredients used in nail polishes?** (`intro_question`, select_one):
	+ This question assesses your level of understanding about the ingredients commonly used in nail polishes.
	+ Choose the option that best describes your familiarity with these ingredients.
* **Which of the following ingredients have you heard of or used before?** (`ingredients_question`, select_multiple):
	+ Select all the ingredients from the list you have some level of familiarity with.
	+ These options include popular ingredients used in nail polishes.
* **Which of the following concerns do you have about nail polish ingredients?** (`concerns_question`, select_multiple):
	+ Identify your concerns from the list of options provided.
	+ You can select multiple concerns if you have multiple concerns.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text):
	+ Enter any additional comments or thoughts about nail polish ingredients.
	+ This is the final step to submit your responses.
