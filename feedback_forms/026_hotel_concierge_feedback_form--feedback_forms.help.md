# Hotel Concierge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from customers, guests, or other stakeholders about their experience with the hotel's concierge services. It helps us understand what we're doing well and where we need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide feedback, simply fill out the form below and click the "Submit" button when you're done. You can skip any fields that don't apply to you. If you have a specific issue or complaint, please provide as much detail as possible so we can address it effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Feedback** (`general_feedback`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience with the concierge team. This could be positive or negative feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide more detailed comments or feedback about your experience. This is a great place to share specific stories or suggestions for improvement.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to receive a response to your feedback, please provide your email address. We'll do our best to respond within a few days.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide a phone number where you can be reached in case we need more information.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your name so we can personalize our response to you.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you've had a problem, please describe it clearly so we can understand and address it.
* If you have a suggestion or idea, we're all ears!
