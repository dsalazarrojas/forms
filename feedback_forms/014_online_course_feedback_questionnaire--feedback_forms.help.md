# Online Course Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Course Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather user feedback on the course content, structure, and overall experience. The feedback will help instructors improve the course and provide a better learning experience for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1.  Read each field carefully and understand its purpose.
2.  Provide clear and concise feedback in the **overall_impression** and **feedback** fields.
3.  Rate the course honestly and objectively in the **rating** field.
4.  Provide accurate completion information in the **course_completion_info** field.
5.  Select the correct course name from the **course_name** field.
6.  Select all applicable courses from the **courses_submitted** field.
7.  Provide your name in the **submitted_by** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Briefly describe your overall impression of the course (1 point)**: 
    *   A text field for users to provide a brief description of their overall impression of the course.
*   **Feedback on the course content (2 points)**: 
    *   A text field for users to provide detailed feedback on the course content.
*   **Course Rating (3 points)**: 
    *   A number field where users can rate the course on a scale of 1-10.
*   **Date and Time of Course Completion (4 points)**: 
    *   A date field for users to provide the date and time of course completion.
*   **What is the name of the course you took? (5 points)**: 
    *   A select-one field with options 'Course 1', 'Course 2', or 'Course 3' for users to select the correct course name.
*   **Which courses have you submitted so far? (6 points)**: 
    *   A select-multiple field with options 'Course 1', 'Course 2' for users to select all applicable courses.
*   **Your name (7 points)**: 
    *   A text field for users to provide their name.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and objective when providing feedback.
*   Provide as much detail as possible in the **overall_impression** and **feedback** fields.
*   Double-check the **course_name** field to ensure you select the correct course.
*   Select all applicable courses in the **courses_submitted** field.
*   Ensure that your name is accurate and up-to-date in the **submitted_by** field.
