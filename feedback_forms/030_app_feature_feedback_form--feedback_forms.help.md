# App Feature Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about specific features in the app. This form is designed to gather information on how users interact with features, their experience, and suggestions for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the feature you are providing feedback for in the "Which feature are you providing feedback for?" field.
2. Enter the app version you are using in the "App Version Used" field (optional).
3. Indicate how often you use the feature by selecting one of the options in the "How often do you use this feature?" field.
4. Rate the feature's ease of use from 1 to 5 in the "How easy is this feature to use?" field.
5. Rate the feature's functionality by selecting one of the options in the "Does the feature perform its intended function well?" field.
6. Rate the visual design and layout of the feature by entering a number from 1 to 5 in the "Rating of Visual Design and Layout" field.
7. If you encountered any bugs or errors, describe them in the "Did you encounter any bugs or errors?" field (optional).
8. If you have any suggestions for improvement for the feature, enter them in the "What one thing would you improve about this feature?" field.
9. Indicate the importance of the feature to your overall app experience by selecting one of the options in the "How important is this feature to your overall app experience?" field.
10. If you would recommend this feature to other users, select the corresponding option in the "Would you recommend this feature to other users?" field (optional).
11. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments or Feedback" field (optional).
12. If you would like us to follow up with you, enter your email address in the "Your Email (Optional" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Which feature are you providing feedback for?** (`feature_name`, required): This field allows you to select the specific feature you are providing feedback about. Please choose the feature that best corresponds to your feedback.
* **App Version Used** (`app_version`, optional): If you want us to know the version of the app you are using, enter it here.
* **How often do you use this feature?** (`frequency_of_use`, required): Indicate how frequently you use this feature by selecting one of the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **How easy is this feature to use?** (`ease_of_use_rating`, required): Rate the feature's ease of use from 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy).
* **Does the feature perform its intended function well?** (`functionality_rating`, required): Rate the feature's functionality by selecting one of the options: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Below Expectations, or Fails to Work.
* **Rating of Visual Design and Layout** (`visual_design_rating`, required): Rate the feature's visual design and layout from 1 to 5.
* **Did you encounter any bugs or errors?** (`bugs_encountered`, optional): If you experienced any issues with the feature, describe them here.
* **What one thing would you improve about this feature?** (`feature_improvement`, required): Enter a specific suggestion for improvement for the feature.
* **How important is this feature to your overall app experience?** (`feature_importance`, required): Rate the feature's importance by selecting one of the options: Crucial, Important, Nice to have, or Unnecessary.
* **Would you recommend this feature to other users?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, optional): If you would recommend the feature, select the corresponding option.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`final_comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the feature.
* **Your Email (Optional)** (`user_email`, optional): If you would like us to follow up with you, enter your email address.
