# Countdown Widget Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Countdown Widget Feedback Form is used to provide feedback on the countdown widget. This form helps us understand how users like or dislike the widget, what they like or dislike about it, and any suggestions they may have for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Feedback**: Please provide a brief description of your experience with the countdown widget.
2. **Widget Rating**: Rate the widget based on your experience, using the provided scale.
3. **Widget Features**: Select any features of the widget that you would like to see improved or added.
4. **Submit**: This field is optional and is only for users who wish to provide more information or contact you directly.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to receive follow-up questions or updates from us.
6. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you would like to receive a call from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Feedback** (`user_feedback`, text, required): Please describe your experience with the countdown widget. This could include your likes, dislikes, or any issues you encountered while using the widget.
* **Widget Rating** (`widget_rating`, number, required): Rate the widget based on your experience, using the provided scale of 1-5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Outstanding.
* **Widget Rating Scale** (`widget_rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the rating scale you used to rate the widget. This will help us understand your thought process.
* **Widget Features** (`widget_features`, select_multiple, optional): Select any features of the widget that you would like to see improved or added.
* **Submit** (`submit`, time, optional): This field is optional and is only for users who wish to provide more information or contact you directly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive follow-up questions or updates from us.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to receive a call from us.
