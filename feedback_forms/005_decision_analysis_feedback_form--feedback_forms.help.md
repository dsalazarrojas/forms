# Decision Analysis Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Decision Analysis Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback from decision-makers and stakeholders on the effectiveness and clarity of decisions made within the organization. This form is intended to be used by team members, managers, and stakeholders to provide input on decisions made within their department or team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, rate the decision's clarity on a scale of 1-5.
2. On the second page, rate the decision's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5.
3. On the third page, rate your level of confidence in the decision on a scale of 1-5.
4. On the fourth page, provide any suggestions or additional comments.
5. On the following pages, select your stakeholder ID, team, department, role, date, time, and location accordingly.
6. Enter your email address in the required format (name@example.com).
7. Select your department from the given options (Marketing, Sales, IT, Engineering).
8. Select your role from the given options (Team member, Manager, Stakeholder, Other).
9. Enter the date and time of the decision.
10. Select your location from the given options (Office, Remote, Online).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How clear was the decision?** (`decision_clarity`, number, required): Rate the decision's clarity on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not clear and 5 is very clear.
* **How effective was the decision?** (`decision_effectiveness`, number, required): Rate the decision's effectiveness on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not effective and 5 is very effective.
* **How confident are you in the decision?** (`confidence`, number, required): Rate your level of confidence in the decision on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is not confident and 5 is very confident.
* **Do you have any suggestions?** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any suggestions or additional comments you have on the decision.
* **Stakeholder ID** (`stakeholder_id`, number, required): Select your unique stakeholder ID.
* **Team** (`team_id`, number, optional): Select your team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of name@example.com.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department from the given options (Marketing, Sales, IT, Engineering).
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Select your role from the given options (Team member, Manager, Stakeholder, Other).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the decision.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the decision.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required): Select your location from the given options (Office, Remote, Online).

## Tips

* Ensure that you are filling out the form for a specific decision.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when rating the decision's clarity, effectiveness, and confidence.
* If you have any suggestions, provide them in the additional comments field.
* Select your stakeholder ID, team, department, role, date, time, and location correctly.
* Ensure that your email address is in the correct format (name@example.com).
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or department representative.
