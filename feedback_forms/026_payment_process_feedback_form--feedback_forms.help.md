# Payment Process Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide feedback on their experience with the payment process. Please fill it out if you have actually gone through the payment process and want to share your thoughts on how it went.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields below. Each section is explained in more detail on the next page. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, **optional**): Please enter your name as you wish to be addressed in our response.
- **Payment Method Used** (`payment_method_used`, select_one, **optional**): Please select how you paid for your transaction, e.g., Payment by credit card, Payment by bank transfer, Payment by cash, or Payment by other (if none of the above).
- **Feedback on Payment Process** (`feedback_on_payment_process`, note, **optional**): Be as detailed as possible about your experience with the payment process. What went well, what didn't? Any issues you faced?
- **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address so we can respond to your feedback.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): If you'd rather we contact you over the phone, please enter your phone number.
- **Rate this form** (`rating`, select_one, **optional**): Please rate your experience with this form itself, on a scale of 1 to 5.
- **Additional feedback** (`additional_feedback`, text, **optional**): If there's anything else you'd like to add about your experience, please do so here.
