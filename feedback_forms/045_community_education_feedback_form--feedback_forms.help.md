# Education Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants about their educational experience. It provides an opportunity for them to rate the effectiveness of instructors, relevance of content, and quality of logistics, as well as share suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. Choose relevant options for your responses, where applicable.
4. If you wish to remain anonymous, you can leave your name field blank.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your name if you wish to receive recognition or updates about the session or future events.
* **Course or workshop name** (`course_name`, text, required): This field should have the title of the session you attended, which helps us to track the feedback with the specific session.
* **Instructor effectiveness** (`instructor_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how effective you found the instructor to be, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Content relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Choose how relevant you found the content to be, with options ranging from 'Very relevant' to 'Not relevant'.
* **Logistics and facility quality** (`logistics_and_facility`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of the venue and materials, with options 'Excellent', 'Good', 'Fair', or 'Poor'.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any suggestions or comments for future session improvements.

## Tips
- Be specific and sincere in your feedback, as it helps us to provide better experiences for future participants.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We value your input and appreciate your participation in this process.
