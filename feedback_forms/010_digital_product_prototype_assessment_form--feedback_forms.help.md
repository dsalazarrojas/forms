# digital product prototype assessment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on a digital product prototype assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the product prototype and consider your honest opinion on its design, usability, and functionality.
2. For each field, provide a brief but thoughtful response.
3. Answer all fields that apply to you, even if the product was not used for a specific purpose or did not meet your expectations.
4. If you have no feedback on a particular field, please leave it blank.
5. Once you have completed all fields, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback**: Please provide your overall impression of the product, including any comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Rating**: Please rate the product from 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the product.
* **Feedback on Design**: Please provide feedback on the product's design, including any likes or dislikes.
* **Feedback on Usability**: Provide feedback on the product's usability, including any difficulties or ease of use.
* **Feedback on Functionality**: Please provide feedback on the product's functionality, including any features that worked or did not work as expected.
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you may have for improving the product.
* **Feedback on Ease of Use**: Please rate the product's ease of use from 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
* **Feedback on Overall Impressions**: Please provide your overall impression of the product, including any likes or dislikes.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, please select the tool that was used to access the product.
* **Form ID**: This is a unique identifier for the form and is for internal use only.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible in your responses.
* Use proper spelling and grammar when providing feedback.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask and we will clarify.
* Your feedback is valued and will help us improve the product.
* Please do not provide any confidential or sensitive information.
