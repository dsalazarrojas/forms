# Counter Usage Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with a counter.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, but only if you have used the counter before.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. If a field is not relevant to you, you may leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **counter_usage_survey_page_1_label** (`counter_usage_survey_page_1`, text, not required): This page is an introduction to the survey, providing some context and guidance on how to fill out the form.
* **counter_usage** (`counter_usage`, number, not required): Please enter the frequency at which you use the counter. You can select any number between 1 and 100.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Share any feedback or comments you may have about your experience with the counter.
* **suggestions** (`suggestions`, select_multiple, not required): If you have any suggestions for improving the counter, please select from the list below:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the counter, on a scale of 1 to 100.
* **comments** (`comments`, note, not required): If you have any additional comments about your experience with the counter, please enter them below.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, select_one, not required): If you would like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, please select "Yes" or "No".
* **email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.

## Tips

* You can skip any fields that are not relevant to your experience.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any issues or concerns about the counter, please contact us directly.
