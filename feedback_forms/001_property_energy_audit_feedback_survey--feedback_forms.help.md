# Property Energy Audit Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on properties that have undergone an energy audit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the **Property Name**, which should be a brief description of the property.
*   Fill in the **Address** of the property.
*   Enter the **Date Completed** of the energy audit.
*   Select the **Building Type** from the provided options (e.g., Single Family Home, Condo, Apartment, etc.).
*   Enter the **Number of Floors** for the property.
*   Enter the **Number of Units** for the property.
*   Enter the **Energy Audit Rating**, which represents the property's energy consumption and efficiency score.
*   Provide any **Comments** regarding the energy audit process or findings.
*   Enter the **Email** contact information for follow-up communication.
*   Enter the **Phone** contact information for follow-up communication.
*   Assign a specific **Assigned Contact** for the property.
*   Select the **Assigned Tool** used for the energy audit from the provided options (e.g., chatjimmy, jenny, etc.).
*   Add any **Notes** regarding the property's energy audit status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *Property Name* (**property_name**): Enter the name of the property.
*   *Address* (**address**): Enter the address of the property.
*   *Date Completed* (**date_completed**): Enter the date the energy audit was completed.
*   *Building Type* (**building_type**): Select the type of building from the provided options.
*   *Number of Floors* (**num_floors**): Enter the number of floors in the property.
*   *Number of Units* (**num_units**): Enter the number of units in the property.
*   *Energy Audit Rating* (**energy_audit_rating**): Enter the energy audit rating score.
*   *Comments* (**comments**): Enter any comments regarding the energy audit process or findings.
*   *Email* (**email**): Enter the email contact information for follow-up communication.
*   *Phone* (**phone**): Enter the phone contact information for follow-up communication.
*   *Assigned Contact* (**assigned_contact**): Assign a specific contact for the property.
*   *Assigned Tool* (**assigned_tool**): Select the tool used for the energy audit from the provided options.
*   *Notes* (**notes**): Enter any notes regarding the property's energy audit status.
*   *Building Size* (**building_size**): Select the size of the building from the provided options.
