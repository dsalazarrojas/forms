# promotional_offer_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on promotional offers, helping us refine and improve our promotional strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the type of offer you are providing feedback on from the list.
2. Rate your experience with the offer (if applicable).
3. Provide your feedback and suggestions on the offer.
4. Select the outcome you expected from the offer.
5. Select the outcome you actually experienced from the offer.
6. Provide any additional comments or contact information if you wish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### offer_type (Required)
* Offer type 1, 2, or 3: Select the type of offer you are providing feedback on.
### rating (Optional)
* A numeric value between 1 and 5 to rate your experience with the offer.
### feedback (Required)
* A free-form text area to provide your thoughts on the offer.
### suggestions (Optional)
* A free-form text area to provide any suggestions or additional comments.
### expected_outcome (Optional)
* The outcome you expected from the offer.
### actual_outcome (Required)
* The outcome you actually experienced from the offer.
### additional_comments (Optional)
* A free-form text area for any additional comments or questions.
### email (Optional)
* Your email address for follow-up contact.
### phone (Optional)
* Your phone number for follow-up contact.
### contact_details (Optional)
* A free-form text area for any contact information or additional details.
