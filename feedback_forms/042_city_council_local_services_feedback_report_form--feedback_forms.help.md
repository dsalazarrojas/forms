# City Council Local Services Feedback Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your experiences with local services in the city, which will help the city council improve services and provide better support to the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your name and email address in the "Your Information" section.
*   Provide your address in the "Your Address" field.
*   Select the service category that best describes the issue you are experiencing.
*   Provide the location of the issue.
*   Describe the issue in detail in the "Description of the Issue" field.
*   Indicate if the issue is a safety hazard or not.
*   Estimate the number of people affected by the issue.
*   Specify how long the issue has existed.
*   Choose the preferred method of follow-up contact from the city council.
*   Provide a reasonable timeframe for the resolution of the issue.
*   Describe what you would like the city council to do to resolve the issue.
*   Optionally, attach a photo or image of the issue.
*   If you have previously reported this issue, please provide the report number.
*   Add any additional comments or information about the issue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Your Name** (`your_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where the city council can contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like the city council to contact you by phone.
*   **Your Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your identification.
*   **Service Category** (`service_category`, select_one, required): Choose the service category that best describes the issue you are experiencing.
*   **Location of Issue** (`issue_location`, text, required): Enter the street address or intersection where the issue is located.
*   **Description of the Issue** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the issue in as much detail as possible.
*   **Is This a Safety Hazard?** (`safety_hazard`, select_one, required): Indicate if the issue is a safety hazard or not.
*   **Number of People Affected** (`affected_people`, select_one, optional): Estimate the number of people affected by the issue.
*   **How Long Has This Issue Existed?** (`duration`, select_one, optional): Select how long the issue has existed.
*   **Photo or Image Attached?** (`photo_attached`, select_one, optional): If you have attached a photo or image, select 'Yes'.
*   **Have You Reported This Issue Before?** (`previous_report`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have previously reported this issue.
*   **Previous Report Number (if known)** (`previous_report_number`, text, optional): Enter the report number if you have previously reported this issue.
*   **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of the issue.
*   **What Resolution Would You Like?** (`resolution_request`, text, optional): Describe what you would like the city council to do to resolve the issue.
*   **Reasonable Timeframe for Resolution** (`reasonable_timeframe`, select_one, optional): Select a reasonable timeframe for the resolution of the issue.
*   **Preferred Follow-up Method** (`followup_method`, select_one, required): Select the preferred method of follow-up contact from the city council.
*   **Best Time to Contact You** (`best_time_contact`, text, optional): Enter the best time for the city council to contact you.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the issue.

## Tips
*   Please be as detailed as possible when describing the issue.
*   Attach photos or images if they can help the city council understand the issue better.
*   If you have previously reported this issue, please provide the report number to help the city council track the issue.
*   Select the preferred method of follow-up contact to ensure the city council contacts you in a way that suits your needs.
