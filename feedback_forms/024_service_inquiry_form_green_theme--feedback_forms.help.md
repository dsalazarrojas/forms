# Service Inquiry Form Green Theme - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end users about their service inquiry. It helps us understand their needs and preferences for our services, allowing us to provide better support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information and submit it. Make sure to fill out all the fields marked as "required" to ensure that your inquiry is properly processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Inquiry** (`id: 1`): A text field where you can describe your service inquiry.
* **Feedback Type** (`id: 2`): A dropdown menu with options "Yes" and "No". This field is not required, but it helps us understand the nature of your inquiry.
* **Service Type** (`id: 3`): A dropdown menu with options "Yes" and "No". This field is not required, but it helps us understand the type of service you are inquiring about.
* **Service Name** (`id: 4`): A text field where you can enter the name of the service you are inquiring about.
* **Service ID** (`id: 5`): A numeric field where you can enter the ID number of the service you are inquiring about.
* **Email** (`id: 6`): A field where you can enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 7`): A text field where you can enter your phone number.
* **Message** (`id: 8`): A text field where you can describe your message or question.
* **Feedback Message** (`id: 9`): A text field where you can provide additional feedback about your experience.
* **Submit** (`id: 10`): A submit button to send your inquiry.
* **Billing Service ID** (`id: 11`): A dropdown menu with options "Yes" and "No". This field is not required, but it helps us understand your billing preferences.
* **Service Account ID** (`id: 12`): A dropdown menu with options "Yes" and "No". This field is not required, but it helps us understand your service account preferences.
* **General Service Inquiry** (`id: 13`): A dropdown menu with options "Yes" and "No". This field is not required, but it helps us understand the nature of your general service inquiry.
