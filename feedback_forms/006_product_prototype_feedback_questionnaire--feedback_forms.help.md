# Product Prototype Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on a product prototype, providing an opportunity for you to share your thoughts and opinions on the product's design, features, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions that apply to you.
2. Be as descriptive and specific as possible in your answers.
3. Use the space provided for each field to provide your feedback.
4. Do not hesitate to ask if you are unsure about any question or field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **This is just a demo** (id: 1)
	+ This field is a brief introduction to the form, and you can ignore it.
* **Product Prototype** (id: 2)
	+ This field is a text box where you can describe the product prototype you are providing feedback on.
* **Product Image** (id: 3)
	+ This field is a text box where you can enter a URL or paste an image of the product prototype.
* **Product Description** (id: 4)
	+ Please describe your experience with the product prototype. This could include your first impressions, features that you like or dislike, and any issues you encountered.
* **Product Satisfaction** (id: 5)
	+ On a scale of 1-5, rate how satisfied you are with the product prototype. This will help us understand your overall experience.
* **Product Score** (id: 6)
	+ Select one of the options (Excellent, Good, Bad, Neutral, Unsatisfied) that best describes your experience with the product prototype.
* **Product Score Options** (id: 7)
	+ Select all that apply from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Bad, Neutral, Unsatisfied). This will help us understand your specific experiences with the product prototype.
* **Product Comments** (id: 8)
	+ Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about the product prototype. This is your opportunity to share your thoughts on how to improve the product.
* **Your Website** (id: 9)
	+ This field is optional, but if you have a website or a blog related to the product prototype, you can enter it here.
* **Your Email** (id: 10)
	+ This field is optional, but if you want to be contacted for further feedback or to receive updates on the product, please enter a real email address.
* **Your Phone** (id: 11)
	+ This field is optional, but if you want to be contacted for further feedback or to discuss the product, you can enter your phone number.
* **Prototype Feedback** (id: 12)
	+ This field is optional, but if you have any feedback or comments about the product prototype, you can enter it here.
* **Time Spent** (id: 13)
	+ This field is optional, but if you want to provide an estimate of the time you spent with the product prototype, please enter it here.
* **Agreed Comments** (id: 14)
	+ This field is optional, but if you have any comments or suggestions for the product prototype, you can enter them here.
* **Agreed Comments Email** (id: 15)
	+ This field is optional, but if you want to be contacted for further feedback or to receive updates on the product, please enter a real email address.

Note: This guide is not exhaustive, and if you are unsure about any field or question, please do not hesitate to ask.
