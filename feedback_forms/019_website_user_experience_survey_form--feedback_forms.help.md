# Website User Experience Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and user experience data from website visitors, helping the website owners refine their online presence and improve the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your overall experience with our website from the options provided (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Bad).
2. Choose your favorite feature(s) from the options listed (Homepage, About, Support, or Blog).
3. In the text field, describe how easy or difficult you find it to navigate our website.
4. Answer whether you feel helped or not.
5. If you have any feedback or suggestions, type them in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was your overall experience with our website?** (`Very Good`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Bad`): This field asks you to rate your overall experience with our website. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **What is your favorite feature on our website?** (`Homepage`, `About`, `Support`, `Blog`): Choose one or more options that you find most useful or interesting.
* **How easy is it to navigate our website?** (`text`, no options): Describe in a few words how easy or difficult you find it to navigate our website.
* **Do you feel helped?** (`True`, `False`): Answer with a simple yes or no.
* **Do you have any feedback for us?** (`text`, no options): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or ideas you have for us.
