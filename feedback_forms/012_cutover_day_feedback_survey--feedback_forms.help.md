# Cutover Day Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cutover Day Feedback Survey is designed to gather information about your experience with the cutover day. Your feedback will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future cutover days.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your experience rating from the list of options.
2. Choose one of the options for communication, if applicable.
3. Provide a brief description of your experience in the Experience Description field.
4. If you reported any issues, describe them in the Issue Reported field.
5. Indicate if the reported issue had an impact on your work or productivity.
6. Suggest any improvements or suggestions for future cutover days.
7. If applicable, enter the date and start and end times of the cutover day.
8. Enter your email address for follow-up, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Experience Rating** (<experience_rating>): Rate your overall experience with the cutover day.
	+ Good: The cutover day was smooth and did not impact my work.
	+ Fair: The cutover day had some impact on my work, but it was manageable.
	+ Poor: The cutover day had a significant impact on my work and was not productive.
* **Communication** (<communication>): Choose "Yes" if you felt well-informed and communicated with during the cutover day.
* **Experience Description** (<experience_description>): Describe your experience with the cutover day in a few sentences.
* **Issue Reported** (<issue_reported>): If you experienced any issues, describe them here.
* **Issue Impact** (<issue_impact>): If the issue reported had an impact on your work, select "Yes".
* **Improvement Suggestions** (<improvement_suggestions>): Suggest any improvements or changes that could be made for future cutover days.
* **Cutover Day** (<cutover_day>): If applicable, enter the date of the cutover day.
* **Start Time** (<start_time>): If applicable, enter the start time of the cutover day.
* **End Time** (<end_time>): If applicable, enter the end time of the cutover day.
* **Email** (<email>): Enter your email address for follow-up, if needed.
