# Community Outreach Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to collect feedback from participants in our community outreach programs. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, enabling us to make adjustments and improve future programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you're providing thorough feedback.
2. Answer all required questions, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Choose the most relevant options for each select-type questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. **Name of Outreach Program** (\*)
* A text input field where you can enter the name of the outreach program you participated in.

### 3. **Date of Participation** (\*)
* Enter the date you participated in the outreach program. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 4. **Overall Satisfaction** (\*)
* Rate your overall satisfaction with the program using the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.

### 5. **Rate the Relevance of Program Content** (\*)
* Choose how relevant you found the program content using the options: Highly Relevant, Relevant, Neutral, or Not Relevant.

### 6. **Rate the Instructors or Organizers** (\*)
* Assess the effectiveness of the instructors or organizers using the options: Very Effective, Effective, Neutral, or Ineffective.

### 7. **What was the most valuable part of the program?** (\*)
* Describe the aspect of the program that had the greatest impact on you.

### 8. **What part of the program could be improved?** (Optional)
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please share them here.

### 9. **How did you hear about this program?** (\*)
* Select all applicable channels through which you became aware of the program: Social Media, Local Newspaper, Word of Mouth, Community Center, Flyer, or Other.

### 10. **Other Channel - Please specify** (Optional)
* If you selected "Other" as your channel, please provide more details about how you heard about the program.

### 11. **Would you recommend this program to others?** (\*)
* Indicate whether you would recommend the program to others using the options: True or False.

### 12. **Do you have any additional suggestions for future outreach?** (Optional)
* If you have any ideas for future programs, please share them here.

### 13. **Would you like to be notified about future events?** (\*)
* Select whether you'd like to be informed about future outreach programs.

### 14. **Contact Email Address** (Optional)
* If you wish to receive notifications about future events, please provide your email address.

### 15. **-- Thank You --**
* This is just a courtesy note to thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback. Your input is invaluable to us.

Tips:

* Be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us make informed decisions about future programs.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Future Suggestions" field.
* We will keep your contact information confidential and only use it to send you notifications if you've opted-in.
