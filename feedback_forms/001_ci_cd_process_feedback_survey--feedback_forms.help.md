# CI/CD Process Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience with the Continuous Integration/Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) process in their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select your primary role in the organization, enter the team name, choose how often you use the pipeline, and answer the other questions as they apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your primary role in the organization? (Software Engineer, select_one, required)**: This question helps us understand who is responding to the survey.
* **Which team do you belong to? (team name, text, required)**: Enter the name of the team you are a part of.
* **How often do you use the CI/CD pipeline in your daily work? (pipeline_frequency, select_one, required)**: Choose how frequently you use the pipeline for your work.
* **Overall satisfaction with the CI/CD pipeline (overall_satisfaction, select_one, required)**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the pipeline on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **How would you rate the speed of deployments? (deployment_speed, select_one, required)**: Rate the speed of deployments from Excellent to Poor.
* **Rate the reliability of the pipeline (reliability_score, select_one, required)**: Rate the reliability of the pipeline on a scale of 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **What are the main pain points with the current pipeline? (pain_points, select_multiple, required)**: Select all the pain points that you experience with the current pipeline.
* **What improvements would have the most impact? (improvement_suggestions, text, optional)**: Provide suggestions for improvements if you wish.
* **How satisfied are you with pipeline visibility and monitoring? (monitoring_visibility, select_one, required)**: Rate the visibility and monitoring of the pipeline on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Are the current CI/CD tools adequate for your needs? (tooling_feedback, select_one, required)**: Rate the adequacy of the current tools from Yes to Very Poor.
* **How well does the team communicate pipeline changes and updates? (communication_quality, select_one, required)**: Rate the team's communication about pipeline changes on a scale from Excellent to Very Poor.
* **Are you satisfied with the training provided for the CI/CD pipeline? (training_satisfaction, select_one, required)**: Rate the satisfaction with the training on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **Any additional comments or feedback? (additional_comments, text, optional)**: If you have any thoughts about the survey or would like to provide additional feedback, enter them here.
* **May we contact you for follow-up questions? (contact_for_followup, select_one, optional)**: Select "Yes" or "No" if you are willing or not to be contacted.
* **Email address (respondent_email, email, optional)**: If you are willing to be contacted for follow-up questions, provide your email address here.
