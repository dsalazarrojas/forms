# unique_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants about their unique experience with our organization, helping us to improve future events and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly to help us understand your experience.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields. The required fields are indicated by a * next to the label.
3. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_questions_1** (`text`, required: false): This is the starting question where you can describe the unique experience you had with our organization.
- **event_details** (`text`, required: false): Provide some details about the event you participated in, including any relevant dates or times.
- **participant_satisfaction** (`number`, required: false): Rate your level of satisfaction with the experience on a scale of 0 to 10.
- **participant_experience** (`text`, required: false): Share more details about your experience, such as what you enjoyed most or what could be improved.
- **additional_comments** (`note`, required: false): If you have any other comments or thoughts, please add them here.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
* If you have already completed the form for a similar experience, please do not resubmit it.
* If you have questions or need help, please reach out to our support team.
