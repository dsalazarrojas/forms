# weekly_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experiences with our organization on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field, but feel free to enter your preferred name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is also a required field, but please note that you can enter your preferred last name.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This is an optional field, but please make sure to enter a valid email address.
4. If you are representing a company, enter your **Company** in the "Company" field. This is an optional field, but please be aware that it will help us know your affiliation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: The first name field is where you can enter your given name or the name you prefer to be called.
* **Last Name**: The last name field is where you can enter your surname or the name you prefer to be known by.
* **Email**: The email field is where you can enter your valid email address. Please note that we will not use your email for spamming or any other purpose without your consent.
* **Company**: The company field is an optional field where you can enter your organization's name, especially if you are representing a company. This will help us know your affiliation, but it's not a required field.
