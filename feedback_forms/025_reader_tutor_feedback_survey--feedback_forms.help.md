# Reader Tutor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect feedback from readers about their experiences with a tutor, to help improve the tutoring program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant fields that apply to your session.
2. Answer each question honestly and as accurately as possible.
3. You can select multiple options for select multiple fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the text fields to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey subjects**: This is the starting point for the form, where you can select the subjects that relate to your session.
* **Session notes**: This field is for you to add any additional notes about your session.
* **Session topics**: This field allows you to select the topics covered during your session.
* **Learning goals**: This field allows you to select the goals you had in mind for your session.
* **Subject difficulty**: This field allows you to rate how difficult or challenging the subject was.
* **Overall experience**: This field allows you to rate your overall experience during the session.
* **Tutor performance**: This field allows you to rate the performance of your tutor.
* **Session length**: This field allows you to select the length of your session.
* **Communication skills**: This field allows you to select the communication skills used during your session.
* **Session date**: This field allows you to select the date of your session.
* **Feedback for tutor**: This field is for you to provide any additional feedback about your tutor.
* **Areas for improvement**: This field allows you to select areas where the tutor can improve.
* **Feedback for tutoring program**: This field is for you to provide any additional feedback about the tutoring program.
* **Feedback for second tutoring program**: This field is for you to provide any additional feedback about the second tutoring program.
* **Session goals**: This field is for you to provide any additional goals related to your session.
* **Communication channels**: This field allows you to select the communication channels used during your session.
* **Student interest**: This field allows you to select the areas of interest for you as a student.
* **Subject interest**: This field allows you to select the subjects that interest you.
* **Session notes 2**: This field is for you to add any additional notes about your session.
* **Communication style 2**: This field allows you to select the communication style used during your session.
* **Overall experience 2**: This field allows you to rate your overall experience during your session.
* **Feedback for second tutoring program**: This field is for you to provide any additional feedback about the second tutoring program.
* **Communication channels 2**: This field allows you to select the communication channels used during your session.
* **Session length 2**: This field allows you to select the length of your session.
