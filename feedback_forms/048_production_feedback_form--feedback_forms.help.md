# Production Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Feedback Form is designed to collect information about a production and gather feedback from individuals who have experienced issues with it. This form is meant to be completed by those who have had problems with a specific production and wish to inform the team about the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Producer" field.
2. Select the production date from the calendar provided.
3. If you have any feedback or issues, describe them in the "Feedback Message" field.
4. You can add team members who were involved in the production to the "Team Members" field.
5. Optionally, you can add an "Issue Description" to provide more context about the problem you experienced.
6. Finally, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Production Feedback Form** (1): A placeholder title for the form.
* **Producer (2)** (`producer_name`, text, required/false): Enter your name, which will be displayed in the feedback message.
* **Production Date (3)** (`production_date`, date, required/false): Select the date of the production from the calendar provided.
* **Production Time (4)** (`production_time`, time, required/false): Select the time of the production from the calendar provided.
* **Team Name (5)** (`team_name`, text, required/false): Enter the name of the team that produced the content.
* **Team Members (6)** (`team_members`, text, required/false): List the team members involved in the production, separated by commas.
* **Issue Description (7)** (`issue_description`, text, required/false): Provide a brief description of the issue you experienced.
* **Feedback Message (8)** (`feedback_message`, text, required/false): Write your feedback or message to the team about the issue.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing the issue you experienced.
* Provide any relevant details about the production, such as screenshots or logs, if possible.
* Keep your feedback constructive and helpful to the team.
