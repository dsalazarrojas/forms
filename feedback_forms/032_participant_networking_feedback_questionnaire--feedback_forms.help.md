# Participant Networking Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants about their networking experience at our event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or labeled as "required" in the form.
2. Choose from the options provided for each field.
3. Enter your answers in the space provided for each field.
4. If you want to add more information about a particular field, feel free to use the "Other (please specify)" option or enter your additional comments in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, text, required): This field allows you to enter a brief description of the event you attended.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, select_one, required): Select the location where the event took place from the options provided.
* **Event Type** (`Event Type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of event you attended from the options provided.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you attended.
* **Networking Details** (`Networking Details`, text, required): Briefly describe your networking experience at the event.
* **What did you do at the event?** (`What did you do at the event?`, select_multiple, required): Choose the activities you participated in during the event from the options provided.
* **What do you hope to accomplish at this event?** (`What do you hope to accomplish at this event?`, select_multiple, required): Choose the goals you had for the event from the options provided.
* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (`How would you rate your overall experience?`, number, required): Rate your overall experience at the event on a scale.
* **What did you find most valuable at the event?** (`What did you find most valuable at the event?`, text, required): Describe what you found most valuable during the event.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, text, required): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.
