# Client Relationship Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the client's relationship with your company, gathering feedback on various aspects such as communication, issue resolution, value, and trust. The feedback is crucial to understand the client's perception and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and company name in the respective text fields.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, such as relationship duration, satisfaction level, communication frequency, and quality of our products or services.
3. Rate the value you receive from our company and the level of trust you have in our business partnership.
4. Provide any improvement suggestions in the text field.
5. Enter your email address for follow-up discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Your Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **How long have you been working with us?** (`relationship_duration`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to indicate the length of your relationship with us.
* **Name of your primary contact at our company** (`primary_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have a primary contact person at our company, please enter their name.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with our relationship?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate your overall satisfaction.
* **How would you rate the frequency of our communication?** (`communication_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate the frequency of our communication.
* **Our communication is clear and effective** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate the effectiveness of our communication.
* **How effectively do we address and resolve your concerns?** (`issue_resolution_rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate our issue resolution and support.
* **How would you rate our response time to your requests?** (`response_time`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate our response time.
* **Do we proactively provide support and suggestions?** (`proactive_support`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to indicate our proactive support level.
* **Rate the value we provide to your organization - 1 to 10** (`value_rating`, number, required): Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate the value we provide.
* **How much do you trust us as a business partner?** (`trust_level`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate the level of trust.
* **How would you rate the quality of our products or services?** (`quality_rating`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate the quality of our products or services.
* **We consistently meet our delivery commitments** (`delivery_consistency`, select_one, required): Choose one of the provided options to rate our delivery consistency.
* **How likely are you to recommend us to others?** (`recommend_likelihood`, number, required): Enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate the likelihood of recommendation.
* **What is the most important improvement we could make?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Please provide specific suggestions for improvement.
* **Your Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up discussions.
