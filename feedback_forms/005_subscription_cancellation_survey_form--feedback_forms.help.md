> # Subscription Cancellation Survey Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their subscription cancellation. It's a crucial data collection mechanism to understand why customers are canceling their subscriptions and what we can improve on our services. Your input will help us serve you better in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate information. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in each field to help us understand your concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Required): A brief introduction to this form and the purpose of the cancellation.
* **Reason for Cancellation** (Required): Describe the reason why you are canceling your subscription. This will help us understand what went wrong and where we can improve.
* **How Can We Improve** (Required): Share your thoughts on how we can improve our service to prevent cancellations in the future.
* **Recommended Service** (Required): Suggest alternative services that we can offer you instead of the one you are canceling.
* **Feedback** (Required): Share any additional feedback you have about your experience with our service.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): If you have any other comments or concerns that are not mentioned above, please share them here.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further assistance.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Business Name** (Required): Enter your business name for our records.
* **Business Address** (Required): Enter your business address for our records.
* **Service Name** (Required): Enter the name of the service you are canceling.
* **Date of Cancellation** (Required): Enter the date you plan to cancel your subscription.
* **Time of Cancellation** (Required): Enter the time you plan to cancel your subscription.
* **Service Type** (Required): Describe the type of service you are canceling.
* **Cancellation Date** (Required): Enter the date of cancellation.
* **Cancellation Time** (Required): Enter the time of cancellation.
* **Cancellation Reason** (Required): Describe the reason for cancellation.
* **Customer ID** (Required): Enter your customer ID for our records.
* **Customer Message** (Required): Enter a message for our team to contact you.
* **Staff ID** (Required): Enter the ID of the staff member you interacted with.
* **Manager ID** (Required): Enter the ID of the manager who handled your case.
* **Branch ID** (Required): Enter the branch ID where you interacted with us.
* **Department ID** (Required): Enter the department ID where you interacted with us.
* **Reason for Cancellation (Other)** (Optional): If the reason for cancellation is not listed above, describe it here.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (Required): Describe your overall satisfaction with our service.
