# Essential Oils Massage Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from clients who have received a massage to improve the overall experience and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the options provided for each field, ensuring you're answering based on your genuine experience.
2. Rate your satisfaction for each field, using the options provided as a guide.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the overall session.
  + Excellent: Highly satisfied with the session.
  + Good: Satisfied with the session.
  + Average: Neutral, could be improved.
  + Fair: Not satisfied with the session.
  + Poor: Very dissatisfied with the session.
* **Therapist Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the therapist's performance.
  + 5 stars: Highly satisfied with the therapist.
  + 4 stars: Satisfied with the therapist.
  + 3 stars: Neutral, could be improved.
  + 2 stars: Not satisfied with the therapist.
  + 1 star: Very dissatisfied with the therapist.
* **Comments**: Share any thoughts or suggestions you have to improve our service.
* **Contact Information**: Verify if your contact information is accurate.
  + True: Your contact information is accurate.
  + False: Your contact information is incorrect.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Therapist Name**: Enter the name of the therapist who provided your massage.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of your session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of your session.
* **Duration**: Enter the length of your session.
* **Massage Oils Used**: Select the oils used during your session.
  + Lavender: Used lavender.
  + Peppermint: Used peppermint.
  + Lemon: Used lemon.
  + Eucalyptus: Used eucalyptus.
  + Frankincense: Used frankincense.
  + Other: Specify the oil used.
* **Massage Type**: Select the type of massage you received.
  + Swedish: Received Swedish massage.
  + Deep Tissue: Received deep tissue massage.
  + Hot stone: Received hot stone massage.
  + Aromatherapy: Received aromatherapy massage.
  + Other: Specify the type of massage.
* **Area Massaged**: Select the area(s) of your body massaged.
  + Back: Massaged the back.
  + Head/Neck: Massaged the head and neck.
  + Shoulders: Massaged the shoulders.
  + Arms: Massaged the arms.
  + Legs: Massaged the legs.
  + Feet: Massaged the feet.
  + Other: Specify the area massaged.
* **Area Massaged Other**: Specify the area(s) massaged (if not selected previously).
* **Massage Oils Used Other**: Specify the oils used (if not selected previously).
* **Massage Pressure**: Rate the pressure applied during the massage.
  + Very Light: Light pressure was applied.
  + Light: Gentle pressure was applied.
  + Medium: Moderate pressure was applied.
  + Firm: Firm pressure was applied.
  + Very Firm: Firm pressure was applied.
* **Massage Environment**: Select the atmosphere of the environment.
  + Quiet: The environment was quiet.
  + Relaxing: The environment was relaxing.
  + Soothing: The environment was soothing.
  + Busy: The environment was busy.
  + Other: Specify the atmosphere.
* **Massage Environment Other**: Specify the atmosphere (if not selected previously).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction.
  + 5: Highly satisfied.
  + 4: Satisfied.
  + 3: Neutral.
  + 2: Not satisfied.
  + 1: Very dissatisfied.
* **Will You Recommend**: Will you recommend this massage?
  + True: Yes, I will recommend.
  + False: No, I will not recommend.
* **Will You Recommend Therapist**: Will you recommend this therapist?
  + True: Yes, I will recommend.
  + False: No, I will not recommend.
* **Follow Up Comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Next Follow Up Date**: Enter the date for your next follow-up.

## Tips

* Please answer all fields that apply to your experience.
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you're unsure about a field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our services.
