# Property Staff Responsiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from property staff on their manager's responsiveness. It will help the property management team understand their strengths and weaknesses, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully, and answer each question honestly.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that best describe your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a brief and clear answer.
4. You can skip questions that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your property name?** (`property_name`): Please enter the name of the property you are currently managing.
* **Who is your property manager?** (`staff_name`): Please enter the name of your property manager.
* **What is your property manager's email?** (`staff_email`): Please enter your property manager's email address.
* **How would you rate your communication with your property manager?** (`communication_quality`): Choose how good you think the communication is between you and your manager, from Excellent to Poor.
* **What is your average response time to your enquiries?** (`response_time`): Choose how quickly you usually get a response from your manager, from 1-30 minutes to Never responded.
* **What is your average availability of your property manager?** (`staff_availability`): Choose how available your manager is for you to contact them, from 8 to 24 hours or more.
* **How would you rate your general service quality?** (`service_quality`): Choose how good you think the overall service quality is.
* **How would you rate your property manager's helpfulness?** (`staff_helpfulness`): Choose how helpful you think your manager is in general.
* **What channels do you most often use to contact your property manager?** (`communication_channels`): Choose one or multiple channels you usually contact your manager through, such as Phone, Email, or Live chat.
* **Do you want to be sent follow-up emails?** (`email_follow_up`): If you want to receive follow-up emails from your manager, enter "yes" (with or without quotes).
* **Do you want to be sent follow-up calls?** (`phone_follow_up`): If you want to receive follow-up calls from your manager, enter "yes" (with or without quotes).
* **Would you want to receive email notifications?** (`email_notification`): If you want to receive email notifications from your manager, enter "yes" (with or without quotes).
* **What time of day is most preferable for you to be contacted?** (`time_of_contact`): Choose a time of day when you are most available to be contacted.
* **What contact method do you prefer?** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose your preferred contact method, such as Email, Phone, or Live chat.
* **What frequency would you like to receive responses to your enquiries?** (`response_frequency`): Choose how quickly you want to receive responses from your manager.
* **What language do you prefer to communicate with your property manager?** (`preferred_language`): Choose your preferred language for communication.
* **Please provide us with your contact details** (`contact_details`): Please enter your contact information so we can follow up with you.
* **How would you rate your property?** (`property_rating`): Choose how good you think the property is, from Excellent to Poor.
* **How satisfied are you with your overall property experience?** (`overall_satisfaction`): Choose how satisfied you are with your experience, from Excellent to Poor.
* **Would you recommend your property to family and friends?** (`property_recommend`): If you would recommend your property to family and friends, enter "yes" (with or without quotes).
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **What areas would you like your property manager to improve on?** (`staff_improvement`): Choose one or multiple areas your manager can improve on, such as Communication or Response time.
* **What would you like to tell us about your property manager?** (`staff_feedback`): Please provide any comments or suggestions you have about your manager.
* **What are your suggestions for your property's resident satisfaction?** (`resident_satisfaction`): Choose how satisfied you are with your resident satisfaction, from Excellent to Poor.
* **What areas would you like your property manager to improve on?** (`staff_improvement`): Choose one or multiple areas your manager can improve on.
* **What would you like to tell us about your property manager?** (`staff_feedback`): Please provide any comments or suggestions you have about your manager.
