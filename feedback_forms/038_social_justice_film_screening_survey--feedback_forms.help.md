# Social Justice Film Screening Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the film screening. Your input will help us improve the screening process for future events. Your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the survey.
2. Select the page with the label "page_one" and enter your response in the text field provided.
3. Answer the questions in the text fields provided, and rate your experience in the number field.
4. Enter your email address in the email field.
5. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (`page_one`, text, optional): This is the first page of the survey where you can enter your thoughts and reflections about the screening.
* **What was your experience of the screening?** (`question_1`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the screening, including any positive or negative aspects.
* **How likely are you to attend another screening?** (`question_2`, text, optional): Share your likelihood of attending another screening based on your experience.
* **Why or why not?** (`question_3`, text, optional): Explain why you would or wouldn't attend another screening.
* **Rate your experience** (`question_4`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the screening on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Email** (`question_5`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`question_6`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions or concerns.
* **Question 8** (`question_7`, text, optional): This field is currently not used, please skip it.
* **Question 9** (`question_9`, text, optional): This field is currently not used, please skip it.
