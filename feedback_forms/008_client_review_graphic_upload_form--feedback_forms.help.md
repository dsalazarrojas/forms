# Client Review & Graphic Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for clients and designers to interact on design projects, providing feedback and uploading graphics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Provide accurate information in each field.
3. Upload necessary graphics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to input the client's full name or company name.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the project name, what the client or project is called.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This is the email address for correspondence and file sharing.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): This is the contact number, which is optional.
* **Type of Project** (`project_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the type of project, such as logo design, marketing collateral, etc.
* **Revision Round Number** (`revision_round`, `number`, required): This field is for the number of the current revision round.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to rate the current design state, from "Needs Major Revisions" to "Ready to Finalize".
* **Specific Feedback** (`specific_feedback`, `text`, required): This field is for providing detailed feedback and comments on the design.
* **Color Feedback** (`color_feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing feedback on the colors used in the design.
* **Typography Feedback** (`typography_feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing feedback on the typography used in the design.
* **Layout Feedback** (`layout_feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing feedback on the layout and composition of the design.
* **Messaging Feedback** (`messaging_feedback`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing feedback on the messaging and copy used in the design.
* **Priority Changes** (`priority_changes`, `text`, required): This field is for listing the top 3 priority changes needed in the design.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to determine the current approval status, from "Needs Review and Approval" to "Approved as Is".
* **Stakeholders Approved** (`stakeholders_approved`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to determine if all stakeholders have reviewed and approved the design.
* **Revision Deadline** (`revision_deadline`, `date`, optional): This field is for specifying when the revised version is needed.
* **Reference Files** (`reference_files`, `text`, optional): This field is for uploading reference files, inspiration, or comparison images.
* **Marked-up Design** (`marked_up_design`, `text`, optional): This field is for uploading a marked-up or annotated version of the design if available.
* **Additional Resources** (`additional_resources`, `text`, optional): This field is for uploading any additional resources or brand guidelines.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): This field is for providing any additional comments or questions.
