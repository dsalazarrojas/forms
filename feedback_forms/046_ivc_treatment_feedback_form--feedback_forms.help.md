# ivc_treatment_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "ivc_treatment_feedback_form" is a form designed to collect feedback about a treatment's impact on patients, aiming to improve patient care and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Patient Experience" page and provide a brief description about your experience with the treatment.
2. Click on the "Side Effects" page and describe any side effects you have encountered.
3. Finally, click on the "Suggestions" page and share any suggestions you have for improving the treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Experience** (`patient_experience`, text, required/optional): This field is intended for you to describe your overall experience with the treatment. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Side Effects** (`side_effects`, text, required/optional): This field is where you can report any side effects you experienced after taking the treatment. Be specific and provide as much information as you can about the effects you encountered.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, required/optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving the treatment, such as dosage adjustments, alternative treatments, or anything else that you think could enhance patient care.

## Tips
- Be honest and detailed in your responses.
- Provide as much information as you can about your experience with the treatment.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
