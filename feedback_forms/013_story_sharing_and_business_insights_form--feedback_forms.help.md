# Story Sharing And Business Insights Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather user feedback and business insights on story sharing. It is designed to collect multiple select options, a single select option, numerical data, and text information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Select one or more options for "story_sharing_and_business_insights_form" from the list.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "business_insights_form".
3. Enter a numerical value for "effective_data_collection" to provide more information.
4. Type in your "customer_stories" in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **story_sharing_and_business_insights_form (Select multiple)**: This field allows you to choose one or more options from the list provided. Select the options that best fit your story.
* **business_insights_form (Select one)**: This field asks whether or not you have business insights related to your story. Choose "Yes" if you have insights, "No" otherwise.
* **effective_data_collection (Number)**: Please enter the numerical value for the effective data collection for your story.
* **customer_stories (Text)**: This field is where you can provide more details about your story. Please enter a brief description.

## Tips
* Please be as concise as possible with your input to ensure data quality.
* If unsure about any field, please feel free to ask if you need further clarification.
