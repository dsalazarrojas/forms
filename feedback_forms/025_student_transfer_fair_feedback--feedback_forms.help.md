# Student Transfer Fair Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Transfer Fair Feedback form is a tool for students to provide their thoughts and opinions on the Student Transfer Fair event. Your feedback will help us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by reading the introduction on the first page.
- Answer the questions on the subsequent pages in the following order:
  1. Rate your overall experience of the event.
  2. Provide feedback on how well college information was presented.
  3. Share what you learned from the event.
  4. Note any events or programs that stood out to you.
  5. Comment on what you liked or disliked about the presenters.
  6. Comment on what you liked or disliked about the colleges.
  7. Indicate if you would attend this event again.
  8. Add any additional comments or thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Welcome to this form**: This page serves as an introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
- **How did you experience this event?**: Rate your overall experience of the event, from 1 (very poor) to 5 (very good).
- **How well was college information presented?**: Share your thoughts on how well the college information was presented. This is a text field for any feedback or comments you may have.
- **What did you learn from the event?**: Describe what you learned from the event in this text field.
- **Were there any events or programs that stood out to you?**: Share any events or programs that caught your attention or interest.
- **What did you like about the presenters?**: Share your thoughts on what you liked about the presenters. This is a text field for any comments or feedback.
- **What did you dislike about the colleges?**: Share your thoughts on what you disliked or were not satisfied with regarding the colleges.
- **Would you attend this event again?**: Select whether you would attend this event again, with options for 'Yes', 'No', and 'Unsure'.
- **Additional comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have, no matter how brief or lengthy they may be.
