# Vendor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Complaint Form is designed to collect feedback from vendors about their experiences with the system or services provided. It helps us identify potential issues and areas for improvement to better serve our vendors and improve overall system performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions accurately and honestly about your experience with the system or services provided.
2. Ensure that you have selected the correct options for each field.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of your complaint or issue.
4. Fill out the form completely, including your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Complaint Form** (`vendor_complaint_form`, `select_multiple`): This field allows you to indicate if you have experienced any complaints or issues with our system or services provided. Choose one or more of the options: "Yes" and "No".
* **Description** (`description`, `text`): Please provide a clear and concise description of your complaint or issue. This will help us understand your experience and address your concerns.
* **Product/Service** (`product_service`, `select_one`): Indicate if the issue was with our product or service. Choose one of the options: "Yes" and "No".
* **Complaint** (`complaint`, `text`): Describe the specific issue or problem you faced. This will help us understand the root cause and provide a solution.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Please provide your email address so that we can contact you to follow up on your submission.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`): Provide your phone number so that we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`): Indicate if you submitted this form as a vendor or not. Choose one of the options: "Yes" and "No".
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `select_one`): Indicate if you submitted this form to a specific person or team. Choose one of the options: "Yes" and "No".
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`): The date and time when you submitted this form.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`): The time when you submitted this form.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`): Choose one of the categories to help us further identify and address your issue.
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`): Indicate the current status of your issue. Choose one of the options: "Active" and "Inactive".
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to help us understand your issue.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Message" field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
