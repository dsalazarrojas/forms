# Wildlife Park Visitor Expectation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from visitors to our wildlife park. By providing us with your age, contact information, and visit details, you will help us better understand your expectations and experiences during your visit. This information will be used to improve our park facilities, services, and overall visitor experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Survey Questions" page to begin the survey.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. Click "Submit" to submit your survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Questions** (1):
	+ This is the starting page of the survey where you will be asked a series of questions.
* **What is your age** (2):
	+ Please enter your age to help us understand our audience demographics.
* **email** (3):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Phone** (4):
	+ Enter your phone number to help us contact you if needed.
* **Time of Visit** (5):
	+ Enter the time you visited the park, using the format "HH:MM AM/PM" or "HH:MM 24h".
* **Date of Visit** (6):
	+ Enter the date of your visit, using the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
