# Skin Tone Matching Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you find the best match for your skin tone. We'll ask you about your current skin tone, the tone you prefer, and your experience with matching your skin tone to find the most suitable products or services for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer all questions that are marked as required.
2. Choose one option for each question from the available options.
3. If a question is optional, feel free to provide an answer if you find it relevant to your experience.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, you can use the "comments" field to provide them.
