# Doctor Appointment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctor Appointment Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from patients who have visited a medical office. It's used to understand patient satisfaction with their doctor and the scheduling process, as well as their experience with the office staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the rating that best describes your overall satisfaction with your doctor.
2. Choose the rating that best describes your experience with the appointment scheduling process.
3. Choose the rating that best describes your experience with the office staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with your doctor?** (`feedback_question_1`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the options that best describes your overall satisfaction with your doctor. You can choose from "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **How would you rate your appointment scheduling process?** (`feedback_question_2`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking about your experience with the process of scheduling your appointment. Please select one of the options that best describes your experience, "Good" or "Fair".
* **How would you rate your experience with the office staff?** (`feedback_question_3`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking about your experience with the staff at the medical office. Please select one of the options that best describes your experience, "Good" or "Fair".

## Tips
- Please answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the space provided.
- Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback. It will help us to improve our services.
