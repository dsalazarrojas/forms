This survey aims to understand your experiences and perceptions of company communications, helping us to identify areas of improvement and optimize our internal communications strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Select the options that best match your experiences and perceptions.
* You can choose multiple options for questions that allow it.
* You can skip questions that do not apply to you or your role.
