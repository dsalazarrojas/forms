# empowerment_content_submission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The empowerment_content_submission_form is designed to collect feedback and submissions for original stories, articles, and media content. This form ensures that all submissions are properly labeled and categorized, providing a consistent and accurate record of user-submitted content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and the name of your organization or company in the corresponding fields.
2.  Provide a title for your content (e.g., article, story, or media piece).
3.  Choose the type of content you are submitting (e.g., original story, article, or media).
4.  Select the type of media (e.g., story, photo, video, or audio) if you are submitting media content.
5.  Enter any additional notes or comments about your submission.
6.  Fill in the submission date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`contributor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Contributing Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Article Title** (`title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for your article.
* **What type of content you are submitting** (`story_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose from "original story", "article", or "media".
* **Type of media** (`content_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from "story", "photo", "video", or "audio" if submitting media content.
* **Story** (`story`, text, required: false): Enter your story or text content.
* **Article** (`article`, text, required: false): Enter your article content.
* **Media** (`media`, text, required: false): Enter your media content.
* **Story or Media** (`story_media`, text, required: false): Enter your story or media content.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your submission (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your submission.
* **Submission Date/Time** (`submission_date_time`, text, required: false): Enter the date and time of your submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your submission.
* **What type of content you are submitting** (`story_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose from "original story", "article", or "media". 
* **Type of media** (`content_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from "story", "photo", "video", or "audio" if submitting media content. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If you are submitting media content, select all applicable types of media.
* Double-check your submission date and time for accuracy.
