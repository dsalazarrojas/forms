# Conference Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from conference attendees about their experience at the event. It covers various aspects of the conference, including content, speakers, venue, organization, and overall satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address (if you want us to follow up with you).
2. Provide the name of the conference you attended.
3. Enter the date you attended the conference.
4. Answer the rating questions using the provided scale (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
5. Provide detailed feedback in the open-ended fields (e.g., Best Aspects, Areas for Improvement).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

1. **Your Name** (`attendee_name`): Your personal details, optional.
2. **Email Address** (`attendee_email`): Enter your email address to allow us to follow up with you, optional.
3. **Conference Name** (`conference_name`): The name of the conference you attended, required.
4. **Conference Date** (`conference_date`): The date you attended the conference, required.
5. **Overall Ratings** (`overall_ratings`): Rate your overall experience at the conference.
6. **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`): Rate your satisfaction with the conference on a scale (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
7. **Content Quality** (`content_quality`): Rate the quality of the session content.
8. **Speaker Quality** (`speaker_quality`): Rate the quality of the speakers and their presentations.
9. **Venue Rating** (`venue_rating`): Rate the conference venue and facility.
10. **Organization Rating** (`organization_rating`): Rate the event management and organization.
11. **Specific Ratings** (`specific_ratings`): Provide detailed feedback about your experience.
12. **Registration Process** (`registration_process`): Rate the registration experience (optional).
13. **Check-In Experience** (`check_in_experience`): Rate the check-in process (optional).
14. **Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunities`): Rate the opportunities to connect with others (optional).
15. **Catering Quality** (`catering_quality`): Rate the food and beverage provided (optional).
16. **Materials Provided** (`materials_provided`): Rate the handouts and resources provided (optional).
17. **Value Assessment** (`value_assessment`): Provide a qualitative assessment of your experience (optional).
18. **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`): Rate the value of the conference in relation to the cost (required).
19. **Met Expectations** (`met_expectations`): Rate whether the conference met your expectations (required).
20. **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`): Would you recommend this conference to others (required).
21. **Would Attend Again** (`would_attend_again`): Would you attend this conference again (required).
22. **Best Aspects** (`best_aspects`): Provide feedback on what you enjoyed most about the conference (optional).
23. **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`): Provide feedback on what could be improved (optional).
24. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Share any other thoughts or suggestions you have (optional).
25. **Topics for Future Conferences** (`future_topics`): Suggest topics for future conferences (optional).

Note: The fields marked as `required` are mandatory fields that must be filled out for the form to be considered complete.
