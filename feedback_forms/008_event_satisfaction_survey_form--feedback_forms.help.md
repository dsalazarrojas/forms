# Event Satisfaction Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from event attendees about their overall satisfaction with an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select your choices for each multiple-choice question.
3. Provide any comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Describe the location where the event took place.
* **Content** (`content`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the content aspects that you found satisfactory.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Price** (`price`, `text`, required: false): Provide the price you paid for the event.
* **Speakers** (`speakers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the speakers as satisfactory or not.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Describe the organization that hosted the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the location where the event took place.
	+ City Hall
	+ Park Place
	+ Park Center
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required: false): Provide the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required: false): Provide the time of the event.
* **Event Price** (`event_price`, `number`, required: false): Provide the price you paid for the event.
* **Event Speakers** (`event_speakers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the event speakers as satisfactory or not.
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Provide any comments or suggestions about the event.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Your feedback is appreciated and will help us improve future events.
- You can skip any questions that are not relevant to you.
