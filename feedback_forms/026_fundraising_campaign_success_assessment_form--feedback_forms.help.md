# Fundraising Campaign Success Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and assess the success of our fundraising campaign. It is essential for us to understand your experience and opinions to improve future campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form honestly and thoroughly. We value your feedback and opinions.
2. Please answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Please provide your name and contact information. This will help us understand how you were involved in the campaign.
* **How likely are you to participate in future fundraising campaigns?**: Rate from 1-5, where 1 is unlikely and 5 is very likely.
* **Donor Information**: Please provide your name and contact information, so we can follow up with you about future campaigns.
* **How likely are you to donate to this nonprofit?**: Rate from 1-5, where 1 is unlikely and 5 is very likely.
* **Please share any feedback about this fundraising campaign**: This is your chance to tell us about your experience. What did we do well? What could we improve on?
* **How satisfied are you with the experience?**: Rate from 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **How satisfied are you with the donation experience?**: Rate from 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Would you like to be contacted for future campaigns?**: Check "True" if you are interested in being contacted.
* **Additional Notes**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them here.
* **How much did you enjoy the experience?**: Rate from 1-5, where 1 is very unenjoyable and 5 is very enjoyable.
* **Please share any additional experience notes**: If there's anything else you'd like to mention about your experience.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about future campaigns.
* **Donor Email**: Enter your email address if you are a donor.
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as a donor.
* **Donor Address**: Enter your address as a donor.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Donor Phone**: Enter your phone number as a donor.
* **Participant Notes**: If you have any participant-specific notes, please share them here.
* **Donor Notes**: If you have any donor-specific notes, please share them here.
* **Additional Notes**: If you have any other thoughts or ideas, please share them here.
* **How satisfied are you with our participant experience?**: Rate from 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **How satisfied are you with our donor experience?**: Rate from 1-5, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 5 is very satisfied.
* **Would you like to donate to this campaign?**: Check "True" if you are interested in donating.
* **Relationship to participant or donor**: Describe your relationship to the participant or donor.
* **Additional Notes**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them here.
