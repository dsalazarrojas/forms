# Emergency Room Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Room Experience Survey is a tool for patients to share their thoughts and opinions about their visit in the emergency room. Your feedback will help us to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to enhance the experience of future patients.

## How To Complete This Form
*   Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
*   You can skip questions that do not apply to your experience.
*   If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Emergency Room Visit
*   **Emergency Room Visit** (`emergency_room_visit`, text, optional): Describe your visit to the emergency room, including the reason for your visit and any notable events or interactions that occurred during your stay.
### Time In Wait
*   **Time In Wait** (`time_in_wait`, number, optional): Estimate the amount of time you spent waiting in the emergency room before being seen by a doctor. Please enter the time in minutes.
### Time With Doctor
*   **Time With Doctor** (`time_with_doctor`, number, optional): Estimate the amount of time spent with the doctor during your visit. Please enter the time in minutes.
### Nurse Attention
*   **Nurse Attention** (`nurse_attention`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the nursing staff, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
### Doctor And Nurse Courtesy
*   **Doctor And Nurse Courtesy** (`doctor_nurse_courtesy`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the doctor and nurse's courtesy, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
### Staff Professionalism
*   **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the staff's professionalism, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
### Cleanliness Of Environment
*   **Cleanliness Of Environment** (`cleanlieness_of_environment`, number, optional): Rate your experience with the cleanliness of the environment, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
### Overall Satisfaction
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, text, optional): Describe your overall satisfaction with your experience in the emergency room.
### Additional Comments
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them here.

## Tips
*   Please keep your answers concise and honest.
*   Your feedback is confidential and anonymous.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at [Your Contact Information].
