# 1 On 1 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 1 On 1 feedback form is designed for managers and employees to have regular one-to-one meetings to discuss performance, goals, blockers, and development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, simply fill out the required fields:

1. Provide your full name as it appears on your payroll.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Enter your phone number (including your country code).
4. Select your department.
5. Enter your job title.
6. Enter the name of your manager or 1:1 lead.
7. Choose a date of hire.
8. If applicable, select the number of years you have been employed.
9. Select a date for the 1 On 1 meeting.
10. Choose your typical meeting frequency (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
11. Rate your satisfaction with the 1 On 1 program.
12. Rate the communication from management.
13. Select whether you feel supported in your professional development.
14. (Optional) Select areas of strength and areas of concern.
15. (Optional) List up to three goals for the next period.
16. (Optional) List agreed-upon action items with owner and due date.
17. (Optional) Select your preferred development support (e.g., training, mentorship).
18. (Optional) Suggest a follow-up date for the next 1 On 1.
19. Rate your work-life balance (1-10).
20. Enter any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: As shown on your payroll. This is the name you are known by in the organization.
* **Email address**: Your work email address, which should match the company's email policy.
* **Phone number**: Include your country code for easier contact. This is optional, but recommended for scheduling meetings.
* **Department**: Choose your current department or team.
* **Job title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Manager/1:1 lead**: Enter the name of your manager or 1:1 lead.
* **Date of hire**: Enter your date of joining the organization.
* **Years employed**: If you've been employed for more than a year, enter the number of years you've been working.
* **Date of 1 On 1**: Schedule a date for the 1 On 1 meeting with your manager.
* **Time of meeting**: If desired, enter the time for the meeting.
* **Typical meeting frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to meet with your manager (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
* **Satisfaction with 1 On 1**: Rate your satisfaction with the 1 On 1 program.
* **Communication from management**: Rate the quality of communication from management.
* **Supported in professional development**: Select whether you feel supported in your professional development.
* **Areas of strength**: Select areas where you excel.
* **Areas of concern**: Select areas where you face challenges.
* **Goals for next period**: List up to three goals for the next period, with brief timelines.
* **Agreed action items**: List agreed-upon action items with owner and due date.
* **Preferred development support**: Select your preferred development support (e.g., training, mentorship).
* **Suggested follow-up date**: If known, suggest a date for the next 1 On 1 meeting.
* **Work-life balance**: Rate your work-life balance (1-10).
* **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have.
