# Waitstaff Assistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experiences with our waitstaff. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our service and make it more helpful for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the best option that describes your experience.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, you can include them in the text field at the bottom.
4. Please don't complete the form more than once per instance of a waitstaff interaction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Header** (`survey_header`, `note`, **not required**): This is just a placeholder field to separate the different questions in the form. You can leave this field blank.
* **Is the waitstaff friendly?** (`waitstaff_friendly`, `select_one`, **not required**): Choose 'Yes' if the waitstaff was friendly and welcoming. Choose 'No' if you didn't receive the service you expected.
* **How quickly did the waitstaff respond to your questions?** (`responsiveness_time`, `select_one`, **not required**): This question is about how quickly the waitstaff responded to your requests. Choose the option that best describes your experience:
	+ None: The waitstaff didn't respond or didn't answer your questions.
	+ Option 1: The waitstaff responded quickly, within 1 minute.
	+ Option 2: The waitstaff responded within 2-3 minutes.
	+ Option 3: The waitstaff responded within 4-5 minutes.
	+ Option 4: The waitstaff responded after 5 minutes or more.
* **How long did it take for the waitstaff to resolve your issue?** (`issue_resolution_time`, `select_one`, **not required**): This question is about how long it took for the waitstaff to resolve your issue. Choose the option that best describes your experience:
	+ None: The waitstaff didn't resolve the issue or didn't help you at all.
	+ Option 1: The waitstaff resolved the issue immediately.
	+ Option 2: The waitstaff resolved the issue within 1-2 minutes.
	+ Option 3: The waitstaff resolved the issue within 3-4 minutes.
	+ Option 4: The waitstaff resolved the issue after 5 minutes or more.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, **not required**): If you have any additional comments or concerns about your experience, you can include them here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **not required**): This is an optional field where you can include your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or clarification.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **not required**): This is an optional field where you can include your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up or clarification.

Note: Please fill out as much or as little information as you need to help us understand your experience with our waitstaff.
