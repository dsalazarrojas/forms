# wildlife_habitat_preservation_awareness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wildlife habitat preservation awareness survey is a form designed to gather information about users' awareness and understanding of wildlife habitat preservation. It is used to collect feedback on the current state of wildlife habitat preservation and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer all the questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. For each page, ensure you have filled out all the required fields.
3. Take your time to provide detailed comments and explanations where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1 - First Page
* **First Page** (`wildlife_habitat_preservation_awareness_survey_first_page`, Text, Optional): This is the first page of the form where you can provide your initial thoughts and comments about wildlife habitat preservation.
* **Comments** (`wildlife_habitat_preservation-awareness_survey_first_page_comments`, Note, Optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or explanations about your thoughts on wildlife habitat preservation.

### Page 2 - Questions
* **Questions** (`wildlife_habitat_preservation_awareness_survey_first_page_questions`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose all the options that apply to your understanding of wildlife habitat preservation.
    - **Option 1**: This option is related to the preservation of natural habitats for endangered species.
    - **Option 2**: This option is related to the conservation of wildlife habitats through sustainable land-use practices.
    - **Option 3**: This option is related to the protection of wildlife habitats from human activities.

### Page 3 - Second Page
* **Second Page** (`wildlife_habitat_preservation_awareness_survey_second_page`, Text, Optional): This is the second page of the form where you can provide your further thoughts and comments about wildlife habitat preservation.
* **Questions** (`wildlife_habitat_preservation_awareness_survey_second_page_questions`, Select One, Optional): Choose one of the options that best reflects your understanding of wildlife habitat preservation.
    - **Yes**: You have a good understanding of wildlife habitat preservation.
    - **No**: You do not have a good understanding of wildlife habitat preservation.
* **Comments** (`wildlife_habitat_preservation_awareness_survey_second_page_comments`, Note, Optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or explanations about your thoughts on wildlife habitat preservation.
