# gym_trial_experience_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the purpose and process of the gym trial experience survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, please select the option that best describes your experience for each question. You can choose from the provided options for each field, or type in your thoughts in the text box if an option doesn't fit. Ensure you answer all required fields to provide us with accurate feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### How do you rate your overall experience of our gym?
* **How do you rate your overall experience of our gym?** (`trial_experience`, number, required: false): This question asks you to rate your overall experience at our gym, on a scale of 1-5. You can choose a number that best describes your feelings.

### Which service did you use during your trial?
* **Which service did you use during your trial?** (`services`, select_one, required: true): Please select one of the provided options: Recreational activities, Nutrition advice, or Fitness classes. This will help us understand which services we should focus on improving.

### Were the trainers helpful?
* **Were the trainers helpful?** (`trainers`, select_multiple, required: true): Select all that apply: Not helpful, Somewhat helpful, or Very helpful. This question helps us know how effective our trainers are in assisting you during your trial.

### How would you rate our facilities?
* **How would you rate our facilities?** (`facilities`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, or Fair. This feedback will assist us in maintaining a clean and well-maintained environment for our members.

### How clean was the facility?
* **How clean was the facility?** (`cleanliness`, number, required: false): Please rate the cleanliness of our facilities on a scale of 1-5. Although not required, your feedback is valuable in keeping our facilities spotless.

### How did you feel about our atmosphere?
* **How did you feel about our atmosphere?** (`atmosphere`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. Your feedback helps us create a comfortable and welcoming environment for all.

### How helpful were the staff?
* **How helpful were the staff?** (`staff`, select_one, required: true): Choose one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. This will help us improve our customer support and service.

### What could we improve on?
* **What could we improve on?** (`overall`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts on how we can improve our services to better serve you. Your input is valuable in refining our services.

Note: Please take a moment to review each question carefully and answer them truthfully. Your feedback is crucial in helping us provide an even better experience for you and all our members.
