# Studio Usage Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from studio users about their overall experience, providing valuable insights for us to improve and refine our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select an overall session rating from the dropdown menu.
2. Add any additional comments or feedback in the text field labeled "Comments".
3. Rate the facilities and staff using the provided dropdown menus.
4. Indicate how helpful the staff was using the dropdown menu.
5. Rate your overall experience using the dropdown menu.
6. Answer the following questions: 
    * Will you recommend this studio to a friend or colleague?
    * Will you return to this studio again?
    * Will you use this studio again in the future?
7. Fill in your contact information, including your email and phone number.
8. Click submit to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Session Rating**: This field allows you to rate your overall session experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments**: This field is for adding any additional comments or feedback about your session.
* **Equipment Rating**: Rate the quality of the equipment used during your session.
* **Facilities Rating**: Rate the facilities used during your session.
* **Staff Rating**: Rate the staff members who assisted you during your session.
* **How Helpful Was the Staff**: Rate how helpful the staff was during your session.
* **Overall Experience**: Rate your overall experience with the studio.
* **Will Recommend**: Indicate if you would recommend this studio to a friend or colleague.
* **Will Return**: Indicate if you would return to this studio again.
* **Will Use Again**: Indicate if you would use this studio again in the future.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your session.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your contact information, including your email and phone number.
