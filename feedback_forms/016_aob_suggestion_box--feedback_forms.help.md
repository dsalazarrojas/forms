# AOB Suggestion Box - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and suggestions from our community members, allowing us to better understand their needs and preferences. This helps us identify areas for improvement and provide more effective services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Submit your name (optional)**: You can optionally provide your name if you wish to be contacted for follow-up.
2. **Enter your email (optional)**: If you want to be contacted regarding your feedback, enter your email address here.
3. **Select your membership status**: Choose whether you are currently a member, a former member, a non-member, or a prospective member.
4. **Choose the type of feedback**: Select the type of feedback you are providing: Suggestion, Complaint, Compliment, Idea for Improvement, or General Comment.
5. **Select the category of feedback**: Choose the area your feedback relates to, such as Programs and Events or Membership Services.
6. **Enter a brief title or subject**: Provide a concise title or subject of your feedback.
7. **Enter your detailed feedback**: Provide your detailed feedback or suggestion.
8. **Select the impact level**: Choose how significantly your suggestion would impact the organization (Minor, Moderate, Major, or Transformative).
9. **Select the urgency level**: Choose how urgent this matter is (Low, Medium, High, or Immediate Attention Required).
10. **Choose your anonymity preference**: Select whether you wish to remain anonymous.
11. **Request follow-up**: Decide if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Name**: *Name* (`submitter_name`, `text`, optional): Your name (if you choose to provide it).
* **Submitter Email**: *Email* (`submitter_email`, `email`, optional): Your email (if you wish to be contacted for follow-up).
* **Membership Status**: *Membership Status* (`membership_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you are currently a member, a former member, a non-member, or a prospective member.
* **Feedback Type**: *Type of Feedback* (`feedback_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of feedback you are providing (Suggestion, Complaint, Compliment, Idea for Improvement, or General Comment).
* **Feedback Category**: *Feedback Category* (`feedback_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the area your feedback relates to (Programs and Events, Membership Services, Facilities, Communication, Website, Volunteer Opportunities, or Other).
* **Subject of Feedback**: *Brief title or subject* (`feedback_subject`, `text`, required): Provide a concise title or subject of your feedback.
* **Detailed Feedback**: *Detailed Feedback* (`detailed_feedback`, `text`, required): Provide your detailed feedback or suggestion.
* **Impact Level**: *Impact Level* (`impact_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how significantly your suggestion would impact the organization (Minor, Moderate, Major, or Transformative).
* **Urgency Level**: *Urgency Level* (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how urgent this matter is (Low, Medium, High, or Immediate Attention Required).
* **Anonymity Preference**: *Anonymity Preference* (`anonymity_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you wish to remain anonymous.
* **Follow-up Requested**: *Follow-up Requested* (`follow_up_requested`, `select_one`, optional): Decide if you would like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
