# Fitness Class Booking Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with fitness classes. Your input will help our fitness center understand what works well and what doesn't, so we can improve our services and provide a better experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the date you attended your fitness class.
2. Enter the name of the instructor who led the class.
3. Choose the type of class you attended (Group, Private, Virtual, or Other).
4. Enter the name of the class you took.
5. Select your rating of the facility where you took the class (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
6. Rate the instructor's performance (Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent).
7. Indicate how likely you are to book another class (Unlikely, Likely, Very Likely, Definitely).
8. Share any additional comments about your experience.

## Tips
* Please answer questions based on your actual experience.
* Be as specific as possible when providing information.
* If you're unsure about a question, feel free to answer based on your best effort.
* Your feedback will help us improve our services, thank you for taking the time to share your thoughts!
