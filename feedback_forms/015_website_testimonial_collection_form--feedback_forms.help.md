# Website Testimonial Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect testimonials from users about their experiences with our website. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your testimonial in the text box provided.
2. Add a title to your testimonial.
3. Add an optional description to your testimonial.
4. Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars to rate your experience.
5. Select a category for your testimonial from the dropdown menu.
6. Add any relevant tags to your testimonial.
7. Provide a valid email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
8. If desired, add a phone number and/or website URL to your testimonial.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Testimonial Input** (`testimonial_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your experience with our website.
* **Testimonial Title** (`testimonial_title`, text, optional): Add a title to your testimonial to give it a clear and descriptive heading.
* **Testimonial Description** (`testimonial_description`, text, optional): Add a more detailed description of your experience, if desired.
* **Rating** (`testimonial_rating`, select_one, required): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars to rate your experience.
  - Rating of 1 star indicates a poor experience.
  - Rating of 2 stars indicates an average experience.
  - Rating of 3 stars indicates a good experience.
  - Rating of 4 stars indicates an excellent experience.
  - Rating of 5 stars indicates an outstanding experience.
* **Testimonial Date** (`testimonial_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you experienced our website.
* **Testimonial Time** (`testimonial_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you experienced our website.
* **Email** (`testimonial_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`testimonial_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact, if desired.
* **Website** (`testimonial_website`, text, optional): Enter your website URL for further reference, if desired.
* **Comments** (`testimonial_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience, if desired.
* **Category** (`testimonial_category`, select_one, optional): Select a category for your testimonial from the dropdown menu.
* **Tags** (`testimonial_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Select relevant tags to describe your testimonial.
* **Image URL** (`testimonial_image_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL to an image that best represents your experience. 

## Tips
- Please be honest and truthful in your testimonial.
- Do not include any personal or sensitive information.
- Use proper grammar and spelling in your testimonial.
- We may use your testimonial on our website or social media pages.
