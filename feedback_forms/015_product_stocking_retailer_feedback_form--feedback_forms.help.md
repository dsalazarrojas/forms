# Product Stocking Retailer Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from retailers on their experience with the product stocking process. Your input will help us to identify areas for improvement and enhance the overall experience for all our valued customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information about your experience.
2. Select the relevant communication channels that you use to contact us.
3. Choose the frequency of contact that best describes your interaction with us.
4. Provide feedback on any issues you have encountered with stocking, ordering, and delivery.
5. Share any additional notes or comments you have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stocking Issues** (Stocking Issues, text, required): Describe any issues you have encountered while stocking the product, such as delayed or missing stock, incorrect quantities, etc.
- **Ordering Experience** (Ordering Experience, text, required): Share your experience with placing orders with us. Describe any difficulties or challenges you faced.
- **Delivery Issues** (Delivery Issues, text, required): Share any issues you have encountered with the delivery of the product, such as delayed or missed deliveries, incorrect quantities, etc.
- **Promotional Needs** (Promotional Needs, text, required): Describe what you need to see in terms of promotional support to help you sell the product to your customers.
- **Communication Channels** (Communication Channels, select_one, required): Select the ways you usually contact us, such as through email, phone, in-app, or social media.
  - **Email**
  - **Phone**
  - **In-app**
  - **Social Media**
- **Frequency Of Contact** (Frequency Of Contact, select_one, required): Choose how often you contact us:
  - **Daily**
  - **Weekly**
  - **Monthly**
  - **Quarterly**
  - **Never**
- **Feedback On Stocking** (Feedback On Stocking, date, required): If you have any feedback on the stocking process, please provide the date of the event that triggered your feedback.
- **Feedback On Ordering** (Feedback On Ordering, time, required): If you have any feedback on the ordering process, please provide the time of the event that triggered your feedback.
- **Feedback On Delivery** (Feedback On Delivery, date, required): If you have any feedback on the delivery process, please provide the date of the event that triggered your feedback.
- **Feedback On Promotional Needs** (Feedback On Promotional Needs, date, required): If you have any feedback on the promotional needs, please provide the date of the event that triggered your feedback.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **In-app** (In-app, text, required): If you have in-app as a contact channel, please enter your in-app username or ID.
- **Social Media** (Social Media, text, required): If you have social media as a contact channel, please enter your social media handle.
- **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Share any additional comments or insights you have about your experience.
- **Date Of First Contact** (Date Of First Contact, date, required): Enter the date of your first contact with us.
