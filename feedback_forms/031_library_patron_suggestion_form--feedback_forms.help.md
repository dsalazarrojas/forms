# Library Patron Suggestion Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Library Patron Suggestion Form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from patrons to improve the library's collections and services. This form is used to submit ideas and comments on materials and services that could benefit the patron community.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely, providing all requested information in each field.
- For fields with options, select the most relevant choice.
- If you have any additional comments or concerns, please use the "additional_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Library Patron Suggestion Form**: Enter a title for your suggestion, e.g., "More fiction novels" or "More programming for children".
* **Patron Name**: Enter your name, so we can acknowledge your suggestion and address any follow-up questions or requests.
* **Patron Email**: Enter your email address, so we can follow up with you regarding your suggestion.
* **Suggest a Material or Service**: Enter a brief description of your suggestion, e.g., "We should have more e-book lending options" or "More programming for children".
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information or concerns you have regarding your suggestion.
* **Will you want to be followed up on this suggestion?**: Select "Follow up on this suggestion" to indicate that you would like to receive feedback on the status of your suggestion.
* **Category of Suggestion**: Select the category that best fits your suggestion (e.g., Materials, Services).
* **Materials**: Select the types of materials you are suggesting (e.g., Books, Audiobooks, Ebooks).
* **Priority**: Select the priority level of your suggestion (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Service Suggestion**: Enter any additional information or concerns you have regarding your suggestion.
* **Service**: Enter any additional information or concerns you have regarding your suggestion.
* **Follow-up Name**: Enter your name, so we can follow up with you regarding your suggestion.
* **Follow-up Email**: Enter your email address, so we can follow up with you regarding your suggestion.
* **Follow-up Phone**: Enter your phone number, so we can follow up with you regarding your suggestion.
* **Follow-up Service**: Enter any additional information or concerns you have regarding follow-up.

Tips:
- Please be as specific and clear as possible when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the library staff.
- We appreciate your input and will do our best to address your suggestions.
