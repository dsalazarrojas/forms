# Service Feedback And Discount Offer - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about a service they may have received. It also allows them to provide a discount offer if they feel they did not receive the service as expected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the service you used, if applicable.
2. Rate the service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
3. Indicate if you would like to provide a discount offer for the service.
4. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
5. Provide the date and time of your appointment.
6. Type a message describing your experience with the service.
7. Select the reason for your discount offer, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Feedback**: This field should be a brief description of the service you used, if you wish to provide feedback.
* **Service Rating**: This field is for you to rate the service on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Discount Offer**: Select "Yes" if you would like to provide a discount offer for the service. This is a subjective indication of your willingness to offer a discount.
* **Email**: This is where you enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Phone Number**: This is where you enter your phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Appointment Date**: This is where you enter the date of your appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: This is where you enter the time of your appointment.
* **Message**: This field should contain a brief description of your experience with the service.
* **Offer**: This field allows you to select "Yes" if you would like to provide a discount offer, and "No" otherwise.
* **Offer Reason**: This field allows you to provide a brief description of the reason why you are offering a discount, if you choose "Yes" in the "Offer" field.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and helpful feedback and a clear offer reason, if applicable.
* Please enter a valid email address and phone number, as we need to contact you for further discussion.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
