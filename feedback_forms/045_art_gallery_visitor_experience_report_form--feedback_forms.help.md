# Art Gallery Visitor Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from visitors about their experience at the art gallery. Please take a few minutes to complete it and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name (optional, but it helps us to contact you if needed).
2. Enter the date of your visit.
3. Rate your overall experience with the gallery (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
4. Rate the quality of the exhibits (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
5. Rate the helpfulness and friendliness of the staff (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
6. Rate the cleanliness and amenities of the facilities (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. Rate the value for money (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
8. Let us know if you would recommend this gallery to others.
9. If you have any suggestions on how we can improve, please share them with us.
10. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor name**: Enter your name, if you're willing to share it (optional).
* **Visit date**: The date you visited the gallery.
* **Overall rating**: Rate your overall experience at the gallery (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Exhibits rating**: Rate the quality of the exhibits (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Staff service rating**: Rate the helpfulness and friendliness of the staff (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Facilities rating**: Rate the cleanliness and amenities of the facilities (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Value for money rating**: Rate the value for money (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Recommend to others**: Let us know if you would recommend this gallery to others.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions on how we can improve, please provide them.
* **Contact for follow up**: Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow up.
