# Gym Accessibility For Disabled Members Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from members with disabilities about their experience at the gym, with a focus on accessibility features and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the general questions on the first page.
2. On the second and third pages, select all the accessibility features that are not available or are inadequate.
3. On the fourth page, provide any further feedback you may have.
4. On the last two pages, enter your contact details and date of visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you generally feel about your experience at our gym?** (Page 1)
	+ This is a general question to help us understand your overall experience at the gym.
* **Accessibility of the parking facilities?** (Page 2)
	+ Please select all the parking features that are not available or are inadequate.
	+ For example, if there is no parking lot or the parking lot is too small.
* **Accessibility of the entrance and exit?** (Page 3)
	+ Please select all the entrance and exit features that are not available or are inadequate.
	+ For example, if the entrance is not wheelchair accessible or the exit is not well-lit.
* **What accessibility features do you need?** (Page 4)
	+ Select all the accessibility features that you need but are not available at the gym.
	+ For example, wheelchair accessible entrance, accessible restrooms, etc.
* **What accessibility features do you need?** (Page 5)
	+ This is a duplicate question of the previous one, please select all the accessibility features that you need but are not available at the gym.
* **Do you have any further feedback?** (Page 6)
	+ Please provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have about the gym.
* **Please provide your contact details** (Page 7)
	+ Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Date of Visit** (Page 8)
	+ Enter the date of your visit to the gym.

Note: Please provide your feedback honestly and accurately, and your responses will be kept confidential and anonymous.
