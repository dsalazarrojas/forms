# pet-training-program-satisfaction-survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants of the pet training program on their overall satisfaction level with the program, including aspects such as training duration, facilitator quality, and communication effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the required questions about your experience in the pet training program.
2. Select your preferred response options for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including phone number and feedback submission time.
5. Submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **pet-training-program-satisfaction-survey** (`text`, required): A brief description of the form.
- **pet-training-program-participants** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you have participated in the pet training program.
- **overall-satisfaction-level** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate your overall satisfaction level with the program. Options are "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
- **training-duration** (`number`, required): Specify the length of time you have spent in the training program.
- **training-facilitator** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the training facilitator.
- **communication-effectiveness** (`number`, required): Indicate the effectiveness of communication during the program. Enter a numerical value.
- **program-content** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate the level of satisfaction with the program content. Options are "Yes" and "No".
- **follow-up-communication** (`select_multiple`, required): Select "Yes" if you received follow-up communication after the program.
- **additional-comments** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
- **feedback-submitted** (`date`, required): Enter the date you submitted the feedback.
- **feedback-submitted-time** (`time`, required): Enter the time you submitted the feedback.
- **feedback-submitter** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **phone-number** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **comments-and-suggestions** (`select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you have comments or suggestions.
- **program-satisfaction-level** (`select_multiple`, required): Indicate your overall satisfaction level with the program. Options are "Satisfied" and "Dissatisfied".
- **training-areas-of-improvement** (`select_multiple`, required): Specify areas of the program where you would like to see improvement.
- **overall-satisfaction-2** (`text`, optional): Provide an open-ended response about your overall satisfaction.
- **overall-satisfaction-2-2** (`number`, optional): Enter a numerical value for your overall satisfaction level.
- **overall-satisfaction-2-3** (`text`, optional): Provide a brief description of your overall satisfaction.
- **overall-satisfaction-2-4** (`text`, optional): Provide another brief description of your overall satisfaction.
- **overall-satisfaction-2-5** (`text`, optional): Provide a further brief description of your overall satisfaction.
- **overall-satisfaction-2-6** (`text`, optional): Provide an additional brief description of your overall satisfaction.
- **overall-satisfaction-2-7** (`text`, optional): Provide an even further brief description of your overall satisfaction.
- **overall-satisfaction-2-8** (`text`, optional): Provide an additional brief description of your overall satisfaction.
- **submitter** (`select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you submitted the feedback.

Note: The "overall-satisfaction-2-2", "overall-satisfaction-2-3", "overall-satisfaction-2-4", "overall-satisfaction-2-5", "overall-satisfaction-2-6", "overall-satisfaction-2-7", and "overall-satisfaction-2-8" fields are optional and may not be necessary for your response.
