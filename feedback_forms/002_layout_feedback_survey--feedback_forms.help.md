# Layout Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Layout Feedback Survey form is designed to collect individual feedback on the layout of a design project. The form is used for internal purposes only and should not be used for actual submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number (if you have one).
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether or not the current layout meets your needs.
6. Enter the width and height of the current layout.
7. Provide any additional feedback you have on the layout.
8. If you have already submitted the current layout, enter the date and time of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your Email address. This will be used for internal purposes only.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will be used for internal purposes only.
* **Layout Type** (`layout_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether or not the current layout meets your needs.
* **Layout Width** (`layout_width`, `number`, required: false): Enter the width of the current layout.
* **Layout Height** (`layout_height`, `number`, required: false): Enter the height of the current layout.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback you have on the layout.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `note`, required: false): If you have already submitted the current layout, enter a note or comment here.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your previous submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of your previous submission.
