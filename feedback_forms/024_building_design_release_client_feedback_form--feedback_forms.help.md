# Building Design Release Client Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from clients on their experience with the building design release. The feedback gathered will help the design team understand areas that require improvement and make informed decisions for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review the questions and select the relevant answers for each field.
2. Enter your project information in the fields provided.
3. Select the corresponding answers for each question.
4. Add any additional comments or notes if needed.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Project Information
* **Project name**: Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
* **Client name or organization**: Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Your name and title**: Enter your name and title for the purpose of this feedback.

### Design Phase
* **Design phase being reviewed**: Select the design phase you are providing feedback on from the dropdown menu.
*   Schematic Design (SD)
*   Design Development (DD)
*   Construction Documents (CD)
*   Tender Documents
*   Other

### Release Information
* **Design package release date**: Enter the date the design package was released.
* **Overall feedback**: Provide a brief overall feedback on the design.
*   Very satisfied
*   Satisfied
*   Neutral
*   Dissatisfied
*   Very dissatisfied

### Aesthetics
* **Aesthetic appeal of design**: Select the level of aesthetic appeal of the design from the dropdown menu.
*   Excellent
*   Good
*   Acceptable
*   Poor
*   Unacceptable

### Functionality
* **Spatial layout and flow**: Select the quality of the spatial layout and flow of the design from the dropdown menu.
*   Excellent - Well designed
*   Good - Functional
*   Acceptable
*   Needs revision
*   Poor - Major issues

* **Space adequacy for intended use**: Select the level of space adequacy from the dropdown menu.
*   More than adequate
*   Adequate
*   Somewhat adequate
*   Inadequate
*   Very inadequate

* **Accessibility and inclusivity**: Select the level of accessibility and inclusivity of the design from the dropdown menu.
*   Excellent - Fully accessible
*   Good - Mostly accessible
*   Adequate - Meets minimum standards
*   Poor - Limited accessibility
*   Not addressed

### Technical Aspects
* **Quality of design documents**: Select the quality of the design documents from the dropdown menu.
*   Excellent - Clear and complete
*   Good - Mostly clear
*   Adequate
*   Poor - Unclear
*   Incomplete

* **Technical feasibility**: Select the level of technical feasibility of the design from the dropdown menu.
*   Highly feasible
*   Feasible
*   Somewhat feasible
*   Challenging
*   Not feasible

* **Buildability and constructability**: Select the level of buildability and constructability of the design from the dropdown menu.
*   Excellent - Easy to build
*   Good - Standard construction
*   Acceptable - Some complexity
*   Challenging - Complex details
*   Unrealistic - Not buildable

### Cost and Timeline
*   **Design aligns with budget expectations**: Select if the design aligns with your budget expectations.
*   Yes, within budget
*   Likely within budget
*   Uncertain
*   Likely exceeds budget
*   Significantly exceeds budget

*   **Timeline appears realistic**: Select if the timeline for the design appears realistic.
*   Yes, very realistic
*   Yes, achievable
*   Uncertain
*   Tight but possible
*   Unrealistic

### Concerns and Issues
*   **Major concerns with design**: Enter any major concerns or issues with the design.
*   **Required changes or revisions**: Enter any required changes or revisions to the design.
*   **Optional suggestions for improvement**: Enter any optional suggestions for improvement.

### Approval Status
*   **Design approval status**: Select the design approval status from the dropdown menu.
*   Approved - No revisions needed
*   Approved with minor revisions
*   Conditional approval - requires changes
*   Not approved - major revisions needed
*   Rejected - redesign required

*   **Preferred timeline for next phase**: Select your preferred timeline for the next phase of the project.
*   Proceed immediately
*   Proceed within 1 week
*   Allow 2-3 weeks for review
*   Need extended timeline
*   Pending other decisions

### Additional Comments
*   **Additional comments or notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes you have for the design team.
