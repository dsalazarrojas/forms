# university_library_services_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to gather feedback from end-users about their experiences and satisfaction with the library services. This feedback will aid in identifying areas of improvement and enhance the overall service quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and fields.
2. Answer each question based on your genuine experience and satisfaction with the library services.
3. Provide specific examples or details when possible to help us understand your feedback better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall satisfaction with library services**: This question assesses your general satisfaction with the library services. Rate your satisfaction on a scale, if applicable.
* **How helpful are the library staff**: This question is meant to gauge your perception of the helpfulness of the library staff. Select one of the options: very helpful, somewhat helpful, or not helpful.
* **Availability of library resources**: This question is about the availability of library resources, such as ebooks, journals, and articles. Check all the resources that you have access to or would like to have access to.
* **Quality of library services**: This question is about your overall satisfaction with the quality of the library services. If your option list is empty, rate your satisfaction on a scale, if applicable.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improving our services**: This open-ended question invites you to provide any suggestions or comments about how the library services can be improved.
* **How do you want to be contacted**: This question is about your preferred method of contact, which will help us reach out to you regarding your feedback.
* **Email**: This question asks for your email address, which we will use to contact you about your feedback and any further assistance.
* **None (Form Footer)**: This section is a placeholder for any additional comments or information.
