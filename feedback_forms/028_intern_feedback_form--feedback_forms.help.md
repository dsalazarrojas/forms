# intern_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from interns about their experiences and perceptions during the internship program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

* Fill in the required fields with your personal details and feedback.
* For questions 1-5, select all that apply from the list of options.
* For fields with date labels (Date Completed and Date Started), enter the dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* For fields with note labels (Supervisor Comments and Intern Comments), type your thoughts and suggestions.
* Make sure to submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Supervisor Name
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name as you know it.

### Supervisor Email
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's email address as you know it.

### Intern Name
* **Intern Name** (`intern_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your own name as you would like to be referred to.

### Internship Title
* **Internship Title** (`internship_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the internship you participated in.

### Supervisor Title
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's title or position.

### Feedback Question 1
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ What was the most valuable skill you gained during your internship?
	+ What was the most challenging part of your internship?
	+ What did you enjoy most about your internship?
	+ Would you like to work with this company again?

### Feedback Question 2
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ What was the most surprising part of your internship?
	+ Were there any challenges you faced that you did not have the necessary resources or support to overcome?
	+ Did you have the opportunity to take on responsibilities that matched your career goals?
	+ Were there any instances where you felt undervalued or unappreciated?

### Feedback Question 3
* **Feedback Question 3** (`feedback_question_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Were there any company policies or practices that you felt were not in line with your personal values or goals?
	+ Did you feel like you had a clear understanding of your role and responsibilities?
	+ Were there any times where you felt like you had to work outside of your role?
	+ Were there any company resources or tools that were not available to you?

### Feedback Question 4
* **Feedback Question 4** (`feedback_question_4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Was there a moment where you felt like you had to work with someone who was not a good fit for the team?
	+ Were there any times where you felt like you had to work on something that was not a good fit for your skills or interests?
	+ Did you feel like you had a good relationship with your supervisor or manager?
	+ Were there any times where you felt like you were not given the necessary guidance or support?

### Feedback Question 5
* **Feedback Question 5** (`feedback_question_5`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply:
	+ Did you feel like you were given the opportunity to take on leadership roles or responsibilities?
	+ Were there any times where you felt like you were micromanaged?
	+ Were there any times where you felt like you were not given the autonomy to make decisions?
	+ Were there any times where you felt like you were not given the freedom to work independently?

### Date Completed
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the internship in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Date Started
* **Date Started** (`date_started`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started the internship in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Supervisor Comments
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, note, required/optional): Type any comments or suggestions from your supervisor.

### Intern Comments
* **Intern Comments** (`intern_comments`, note, required/optional): Type any comments or suggestions from you as the intern.

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required/optional): Your name or a reference to who submitted this form.
