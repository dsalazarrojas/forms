# Virtual Accounting Services Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for customers to share their feedback about their experience with our virtual accounting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your personal details.
2. Select the correct **Service Type** that you interacted with.
3. Choose one or more **Service Levels** that best describe your experience.
4. Provide your **Phone** number and any additional notes or comments about your experience in the **Note** field.
5. Select the date you interacted with our virtual accounting services in the **Date** field.
6. Choose the time of your interaction with our virtual accounting services in the **Time** field.
7. Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience in the **Feedback** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Please fill in your first name as it appears on your personal identification.
- **Last Name**: Please fill in your last name as it appears on your personal identification.
- **Email**: Please enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone**: Please enter your phone number for further communication.
- **Feedback**: This is a text field for you to provide your feedback and comments about your experience with our virtual accounting services.
- **Service Type**: Please select one or more service type(s) that you interacted with.
  - **Yes**: You selected that you interacted with this service type.
  - **No**: You did not interact with this service type.
- **Service Level**: Please select one or more service level(s) that best describe your experience with our virtual accounting services.
  - **High**: You experienced a high level of service quality.
  - **Low**: You experienced a low level of service quality.
- **Date**: Please select the date you interacted with our virtual accounting services.
- **Time**: Please select the time you interacted with our virtual accounting services.
- **Note**: This is a text field for you to provide any additional notes or comments about your experience with our virtual accounting services.
