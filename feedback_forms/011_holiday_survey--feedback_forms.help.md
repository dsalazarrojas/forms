# Holiday Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holiday Survey form is designed to collect feedback from users about their holiday experiences and experiences with specific tools, such as "Chatjimmy". This form will help managers and administrators understand customer experiences and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category that best describes the purpose of your feedback:
	* Feedback Forms
	* Client Feedback
	* Product Feedback
2. Provide a brief description of your holiday experience or your experience with "Chatjimmy".
3. Choose the tool you used for your holiday experience or feedback:
	* Chatjimmy
	* Other (optional)
4. If you would like to receive your feedback in a file, please enter a file name and format (e.g., PDF, CSV, JSON).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select a category that best describes the purpose of your feedback.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your holiday experience or your experience with "Chatjimmy".
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select_one, optional): Choose the tool you used for your holiday experience or feedback.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, text, optional): If you would like to receive your feedback in a file, please enter a file name and format (e.g., PDF, CSV, JSON).

## Tips
* Be as detailed and objective as possible when describing your experience.
* If you are using a tool other than "Chatjimmy", please select "Other" in the "Assigned Tool" field.
* If you are not sure what category best describes your feedback, please select "Feedback Forms" as a default option.
