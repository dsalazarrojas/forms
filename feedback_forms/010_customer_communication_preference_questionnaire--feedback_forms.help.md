# Customer Communication Preference Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers on their preferred methods and frequencies of communication to help us understand their needs and tailor our approach to meet them.


## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Customer Communication Preference Questionnaire, follow these steps:

1.  Read through each page carefully.
2.  Select your preferred communication channels from the options provided.
3.  Choose your preferred contact time from the options provided.
4.  Select all the contact methods you prefer.
5.  Choose your preferred contact frequency from the options provided.
6.  Select all the days you prefer to be contacted on.
7.  If you have any additional comments, write them in the "Customer Notes" field.
8.  Review your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the form.
    *   Label: Introduction
    *   Type: Note
*   **Communication Channels**: Choose from the options to select your preferred communication channels.
    *   Label: Communication Channels
    *   Type: Select One
    *   Options: Yes/No
*   **Preferred Contact Time**: Choose from the options to select your preferred contact time.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Time
    *   Type: Select One
    *   Options: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Other (please specify)
*   **Preferred Contact Method**: Select all the contact methods you prefer.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Method
    *   Type: Select Multiple
    *   Options: Phone, Email, In Person, Chat, Text Message
*   **Preferred Contact Frequency**: Choose from the options to select how often you like to be contacted.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Frequency
    *   Type: Select Multiple
    *   Options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Never
*   **Preferred Contact Days**: Select all the days you prefer to be contacted on.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Days
    *   Type: Select Multiple
    *   Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
*   **Introduction Text**: This field is for any additional comments about your preferences.
    *   Label: Introduction Text
    *   Type: Text
*   **Communication Channels Text**: This field is for any additional comments about your preferred channels.
    *   Label: Communication Channels Text
    *   Type: Text
*   **Preferred Contact Time Text**: This field is for any additional comments about your preferred contact time.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Time Text
    *   Type: Text
*   **Preferred Contact Method Text**: This field is for any additional comments about your preferred contact methods.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Method Text
    *   Type: Text
*   **Preferred Contact Frequency Text**: This field is for any additional comments about your preferred contact frequency.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Frequency Text
    *   Type: Text
*   **Preferred Contact Days Text**: This field is for any additional comments about your preferred contact days.
    *   Label: Preferred Contact Days Text
