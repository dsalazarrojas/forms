# Task Acceptance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Task Acceptance Survey is designed to gather feedback on the clarity and acceptance of tasks assigned to team members or stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the task carefully to ensure you've understood the requirements and expectations.
2. Answer the questions about the task's clarity and acceptance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Clear" and 5 is "Very Clear".
3. Provide comments about the task if you have any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How clear was the task** (`task_acceptance_survey_task_clarity`, Number, Required: False)
	+ Answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Clear" and 5 is "Very Clear".
	+ This question helps us understand how well the task was defined.
* **How clear was the task acceptance** (`task_acceptance_survey_task_acceptance`, Number, Required: False)
	+ Answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Clear" and 5 is "Very Clear".
	+ This question helps us understand how well the expectations and goals of the task were communicated.
* **Comments** (`task_acceptance_survey_comments`, Text, Required: False)
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the task.
* **How clear was the task clarity** (`task_acceptance_survey_task_clarity_rate`, Number, Required: False)
	+ Answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Clear" and 5 is "Very Clear".
	+ This is another question about the task's clarity, providing another opportunity to provide feedback.
* **How clear was the task acceptance** (`task_acceptance_survey_task_acceptance_rate`, Number, Required: False)
	+ Answer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Clear" and 5 is "Very Clear".
	+ Another question about the task acceptance, allowing for more comprehensive feedback.
* **Comments** (`task_acceptance_survey_comments`, Text, Required: False)
	+ Additional comments about the task.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as you can.
* Remember to review the task carefully before filling out this form.
* If you have any additional feedback, please use the comments field.
* This form is designed to be completed once per task, so please ensure you're not duplicating efforts.
