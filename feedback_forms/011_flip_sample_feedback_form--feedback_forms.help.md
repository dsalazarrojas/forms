# flip_sample_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is designed to gather feedback about your experience with the sample product. Your responses will help us understand what we did well and what we could improve on.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `rating`: Rate your experience with the product.
	* `what_would_you_buy`: Indicate if you would buy the product again.
2. Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience:
	* `comments`
	* `what_did_like`
	* `what_did_not_like`
3. Provide more details about yourself:
	* `email`
	* `name`
	* `company_name`
	* `job_title`
	* `department`
	* `job_function`

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sample_product_experience**: Describe your experience with the product. (text)
* **rating**: Rate your experience with the product on a scale of 1-10. (number)
* **comments**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about your experience. (text)
* **email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you. (email)
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you. (text)
* **name**: Enter your name. (text)
* **company_name**: Enter your company name. (text)
* **job_title**: Enter your job title. (text)
* **department**: Enter your department. (text)
* **job_function**: Enter your job function. (text)
* **how_many_times**: Select how many times you have purchased from this company. (select_one)
	+ Once
	+ Two or more
* **frequency_of_use**: Select your frequency of use. (select_multiple)
	+ Occasional
	+ Regularly
	+ Frequently
* **how_satisfying_is_product**: Rate your satisfaction with the product on a scale of 1-10. (number)
* **what_did_like**: Share what you liked most about the product. (text)
* **what_did_not_like**: Share what you disliked most about the product. (text)
* **what_would_you_buy**: Indicate if you would buy the product again. (select_one)
	+ True
	+ False

## Tips
* Be honest and detailed in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or improvements, please let us know.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and helps us improve our products and services.
