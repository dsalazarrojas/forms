# Customer Service Response Time Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Service Response Time Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the customer service response time. This form helps us understand how well we respond to customer inquiries and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a numerical value to rate your response time experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is "much slower than expected" and 5 is "much faster than expected".
2. Select a specific date when you interacted with the customer service team.
3. Click submit to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Response Time Evaluation** (`customer_service_response_time_evaluation`, Number, Optional):
	+ Use this field to rate your overall response time experience.
* **Customer Service Response Time** (`customer_service_response_time`, Number, Optional):
	+ This field is not currently used in this form. It appears to be an unnecessary field, but we'll keep it as part of the form for now.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, Date, Optional):
	+ Select the date when you interacted with the customer service team.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, use the "Additional Comments" field to provide more information.
* If you have already filled out this form, please do not fill it out again.
