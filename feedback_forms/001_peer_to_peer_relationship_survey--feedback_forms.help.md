# Peer To Peer Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your peer relationships. It will ask you questions about your interactions and perceptions of your peers, as well as any additional comments you may have. The purpose of this form is to collect data that can help us better understand and improve our peer relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reading through the questions and understanding what is being asked.
2. For each question, select the answer that best reflects your opinion or experience.
3. If you have additional comments or thoughts that don't fit in the space provided, feel free to add them in the "Additional comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: This is the name of this form, which is "Peer To Peer Relationship Survey".
* **peers first name** (`peers first name`, `text`, required): This field is for your first name, and it's required because we need to know who you're referring to in the questions that follow.
* **peers last name** (`peers last name`, `text`, required): This field is for your last name, which is also required.
* **relationship duration** (`relationship duration`, `number`, optional): This field is where you can indicate how long you've known your peer. You can enter a number of years or months you've known them.
* **How often do you think you interact with your peer in a given week?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required): This question is about how often you see or speak with your peer. Choose the answer that best fits your experience.
* **How do you perceive your peer's behavior?** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, required): This question is about your opinions on your peer's behavior. Select all answers that apply.
* **How do you rate your relationship?** (`question3`, `select_one`, required): This question is about how you rate your relationship with your peer. Choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
* **How would you describe your feelings about your peer?** (`question4`, `select_multiple`, required): This question is about your feelings towards your peer. Select all answers that apply.
* **Additional comments** (`question5`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional thoughts or comments about your peer that you didn't cover in the previous questions, this is the field to add them in.
