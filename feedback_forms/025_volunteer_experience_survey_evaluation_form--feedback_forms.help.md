# Volunteer Experience Survey Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Experience Survey Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather information from volunteers about their experiences with our organization. This form helps us understand how our volunteers are utilizing their time and skills, and to identify areas of improvement in our volunteer program. Completing this form will provide valuable insights to help us better support our volunteers and enhance their experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and select the relevant information for each section.
2. Answer all required fields.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
4. Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (Page 1)** (id: 2, type: date, required: false)
    * This field is used to record the date of the event you participated in as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Role (Page 1)** (id: 3, type: select_one, required: false)
    * This field helps us understand the role you played as a volunteer (e.g., Volunteer, Non-Volunteer).
* **Volunteer Organization (Page 1)** (id: 4, type: select_one, required: false)
    * This field indicates whether you were a volunteer for an organization (Yes/No).
* **Event Frequency (Page 1)** (id: 5, type: select_one, required: false)
    * This field helps us understand how often you participate in events as a volunteer (e.g., Once, Ongoing, Multiple, Rarely).
* **Feedback (Page 1)** (id: 6, type: text, required: false)
    * This field is for any feedback or comments you may have about your volunteer experience.
* **Additional Comments (Page 1)** (id: 7, type: text, required: false)
    * This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Email (Page 1)** (id: 8, type: email, required: false)
    * This field is for your email address, which will help us contact you in case we need more information.
* **Phone (Page 1)** (id: 9, type: text, required: false)
    * This field is for your phone number, which will help us contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Event Location (Page 1)** (id: 10, type: select_multiple, required: false)
    * This field indicates where the event took place (e.g., On-Site, Virtual, Both).
* **Volunteer Manager (Page 1)** (id: 11, type: select_one, required: false)
    * This field indicates whether the person you reported to was a volunteer (Volunteer/Non-Volunteer).
* **Volunteer Manager Title (Page 1)** (id: 12, type: text, required: false)
    * This field is for the title of the volunteer manager.
* **Event Type (Page 1)** (id: 13, type: select_multiple, required: false)

* **Evaluation Comments (Page 1)** (id: 25, type: text, required: false)

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields carefully and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* We value your feedback and comments, as they will help us improve our volunteer program.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
