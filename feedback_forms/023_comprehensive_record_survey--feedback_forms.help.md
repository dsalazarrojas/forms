# Comprehensive Record Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Comprehensive Record Survey is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our service provider. This form aims to collect information about the service provided, its quality, and other relevant factors that influence customer satisfaction and loyalty.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) symbol. 
2. For each field, use the provided options or provide a free text answer if allowed.
3. Ensure you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey ID** (`survey_id`, `text`, *):* A unique identifier for the survey completion.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, `date`, *):* The date you completed the survey.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): Your full name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): Your email address.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, `text`, optional): Your customer account number.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, `date`, *):* The date you received the service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_one`, *):* The type of service you received (e.g., Product Purchase, Technical Support, etc.).
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, `select_one`, *):* Where did you receive the service (e.g., In Store, Online, Phone, etc.).
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): Name of the employee who assisted you.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, *):* Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* **Service Quality Rating** (`service_quality_rating`, `select_one`, *):* Rate the quality of the service.
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, *):* Rate the friendliness of the staff.
* **Staff Knowledge** (`staff_knowledge`, `select_one`, *):* Rate the knowledge and expertise of the staff.
* **Response Time** (`response_time`, `select_one`, *):* Rate the response time.
* **Problem Resolution** (`problem_resolution`, `select_one`, *):* Was your problem resolved (Yes, Completely, Yes, Partially, No, Not Resolved, No Problem Reported).
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, *):* Rate the value for money.
* **Cleanliness Rating** (`cleanliness_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the cleanliness of the facility.
* **Wait Time** (`wait_time`, `select_one`, *):* Rate the wait time.
* **Likelihood to Recommend** (`likelihood_to_recommend`, `select_one`, *):* How likely are you to recommend our service to others.
* **Likelihood to Return** (`likelihood_to_return`, `select_one`, *):* How likely are you to return to our service.
* **Best Aspect** (`best_aspect`, `text`, optional): What was the best aspect of your experience.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, `text`, optional): What could we improve.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other feedback.
* **Follow Up Permission** (`follow_up_permission`, `select_one`, *):* May we contact you about your feedback.
* **Survey Complete** (`survey_complete`, `select_one`, *):* Have you completed the survey.

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve our service. Thank you for taking the time to complete this survey.
