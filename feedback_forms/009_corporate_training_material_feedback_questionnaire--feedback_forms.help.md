# Corporate Training Material Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on corporate training materials, focusing on various aspects such as content, relevance, and overall experience. Your input will help us understand how to improve our training materials and sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Yes" or "No" as your response to each question, as applicable.
2. If "Yes" is not applicable, please select "No" for the corresponding question.
3. Provide a brief description for text fields.
4. Enter your email address for the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number for the "phone" field.
6. Enter the length of the corporate training session for the "corporate training session length" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate training material feedback questionnaire** (Text): This is a required field where you can provide your feedback on the corporate training material you've used.
* **Category** (Select One): Select "Yes" if the material covered the category you were expecting, "No" otherwise.
* **Description** (Text): Provide a brief description of the training material you've used.
* **Assigned tool** (Text): If any tool was assigned to you during the training, provide the name of the tool.
* **Training session** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" if you attended a training session, "No" if you did not.
* **Date attended** (Date): Enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Time spent** (Number): Enter the total time you spent on the training session.
* **Feedback** (Text): Provide your overall feedback on the training material.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Corporate training department** (Text): If you're providing feedback on a specific department, enter the name of the department.
* **Corporate training location** (Text): If you're providing feedback on a specific location, enter the name of the location.
* **Corporate training date** (Date): Enter the date of the corporate training session.
* **Training facilitator** (Text): If you're providing feedback on a specific facilitator, enter the name of the facilitator.
* **Overall experience** (Select One): Select "Yes" if you had a positive overall experience, "No" otherwise.
* **Feedback to improve** (Text): Provide suggestions on how the training material can be improved.
* **Corporate training materials quality** (Select Multiple): Select "Good", "Poor", or provide a description of the materials quality.
* **Relevance** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" if the training material covered relevant topics, "No" otherwise.
* **Content** (Select Multiple): Select "Yes" if the training material covered relevant content, "No" otherwise.
* **Organization** (Text): Enter your organization name for reference.
* **Recommendations** (Text): Provide recommendations for future improvements.
* **Submitted by** (Text): Enter your name.
* **Submitted date** (Date): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submitted time** (Time): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Corporate training session length** (Number): Enter the length of the corporate training session.
