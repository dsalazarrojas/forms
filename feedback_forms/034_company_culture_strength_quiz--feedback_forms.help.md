# Company Culture Strength Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your perspective on our company culture. It's a confidential and anonymous survey that will help us identify areas of strength and opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your name (optional)**: This is an optional field, so you can fill it in or leave it blank.
2. **Enter your email address (optional)**: This field is also optional and is for follow-up purposes only.
3. **Choose your department**: Select the department you belong to.
4. **Choose your employment length**: Select how long you've been with the company.
5. **Choose your work location**: Select where you work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`employee_name`): This is an optional field, and you can leave it blank if you prefer not to identify yourself.
	+ Optional input field.
* **Email Address** (`employee_email`): This field is optional and is for follow-up purposes only.
	+ Optional input field.
* **Department** (`department`): This is the department you belong to.
	+ Please select one of the provided options.
* **How long have you been with the company?** (`tenure`): This is the length of your employment with the company.
	+ Please select one of the provided options.
* **Work Location** (`location`): This is where you work.
	+ Please select one of the provided options.
* **Communication** (`section_communication`): This section is about how we communicate in the organization.
	+ This is a rating section, and you'll be asked to rate the following statements:
	+ "People feel comfortable speaking up in this organization."
	+ "Information is shared openly and transparently."
	+ "Constructive feedback is encouraged and acted upon."
* **Collaboration** (`section_collaboration`): This section is about how we work together as a team.
	+ This is a rating section, and you'll be asked to rate the following statements:
	+ "Teams collaborate effectively across departments."
	+ "Knowledge and expertise are freely shared."
	+ "There is minimal silo mentality between teams."
* **Values and Purpose** (`section_values`): This section is about our shared values and purpose.
	+ This is a rating section, and you'll be asked to rate the following statements:
	+ "My personal values align with company values."
	+ "Company values are consistently demonstrated in daily work."
	+ "I understand how my work contributes to company purpose."
* **Recognition and Growth** (`section_recognition`): This section is about how we recognize and support your growth and development.
	+ This is a rating section, and you'll be asked to rate the following statements:
	+ "Good work is recognized and appreciated."
	+ "There are opportunities for professional growth."
	+ "Promotions are handled fairly and transparently."
* **Well-being and Balance** (`section_wellbeing`): This section is about how we support your well-being and work-life balance.
	+ This is a rating section, and you'll be asked to rate the following statements:
	+ "I have a healthy work-life balance."
	+ "Workload and stress levels are manageable."
	+ "I feel psychologically safe at work."
* **Leadership** (`section_leadership`): This section is about our leadership and management.
	+ This is a rating section, and you'll be asked to rate the following statements:
	+ "I trust senior leadership to make good decisions."
	+ "My manager supports my success and well-being."
	+ "Leadership has a clear vision for the future."
* **Diversity and Inclusion** (`section_inclusion`): This section is about our inclusive environment.
	+ This is a rating section, and you'll be asked to rate the following statements:
	+ "I feel included and valued regardless of my background."
	+ "Diverse perspectives are sought and respected."
* **Overall Assessment** (`section_overall`): This section is the final part of the survey.
	+ Please rate the following statements:
		- "I would recommend this company as a great place to work."
		- "I see myself working here in 2 years."
		- "Overall, how would you rate our company culture?"
		- "What are the biggest strengths of our culture?"
		- "What areas need improvement?"
		- "What specific changes would improve our culture?"
		- "Any additional comments?"

Please note that this form is confidential and anonymous, so you can answer honestly without fear of repercussions.
