# kaizen_improvement_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions for kaizen improvements in various workflows, products, or services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your suggestion or feedback in the "Form Title" field.
2. Select the relevant workflow, product, or service that needs improvement.
3. If applicable, choose the products or services you'd like to suggest for improvement.
4. Specify the service you'd like to submit feedback for, if relevant.
5. Provide your suggestion or feedback in the "Suggestion" field.
6. Choose yourself as the submitter of the suggestion (optional).
7. Set the date and time when you submitted the suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title for your suggestion or feedback.
* **Workflow** (`workflow`, select_one, optional): Select the workflow that needs improvement.
* **Product** (`product`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the products that need improvement (if applicable).
* **Service** (`service`, select_one, optional): Select the service that requires feedback (if applicable).
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, text, required): Provide your suggestion or feedback.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, select_one, optional): Select yourself as the submitter (if desired).
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required): Set the date when you submitted the suggestion.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required): Set the time when you submitted the suggestion.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your suggestion or feedback is clear and concise.
- Be specific about the area that needs improvement.
- Use the "Form Title" field to make it easy to identify your suggestion or feedback.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
