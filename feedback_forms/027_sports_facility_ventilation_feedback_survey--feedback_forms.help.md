# sports_facility_ventilation_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about their experience with the sports facility's ventilation system.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read this guide carefully before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form with your honest feedback about your experience with the sports facility's ventilation system.
3. Provide your contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide a brief description of your experience with the sports facility. Please write a short statement about your visit.
* **Air Quality Rating** (`2`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate the air quality of the sports facility. Please rate the air quality from 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Ventilation Rating** (`3`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate the ventilation system of the sports facility. Please rate the ventilation system from 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Comfort Rating** (`4`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate the overall comfort of the sports facility. Please rate the comfort from 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Overall Experience** (`5`, `number`, required: false): This field is used to rate your overall experience with the sports facility. Please rate your experience from 1-5, where 1 is very poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Suggestions** (`6`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide any suggestions you may have for improving the sports facility's environment.
* **Contact Information** (`7`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide your contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`8`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
* **Email Address** (`9`, `email`, required: false): This field is used to provide your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.

## Tips
* Please be honest in your feedback.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "Suggestions" field.
* If you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions, please provide your contact information.
