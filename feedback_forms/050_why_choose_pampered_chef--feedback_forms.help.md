# Why Choose Pampered Chef - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on why they chose to use Pampered Chef products. By completing this form, customers can share their experiences and preferences with the company, which will help Pampered Chef to better understand their needs and improve their products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the two fields provided:

*   Name: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   Email: Enter your email address where you would like to receive a response to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name (name)**: This is the first field in the form. Enter your name as it appears on your official documents. This will help Pampered Chef to associate your feedback with your identity.
*   **Email (email)**: This field is where you enter your email address. Please make sure to enter a valid email address where you would like to receive a response to your feedback.
*   **Tips**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the open-ended feedback field. This will help Pampered Chef to better understand your preferences and improve their products and services.
