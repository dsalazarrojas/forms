# Health and Wellness Program Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health and Wellness Program Survey is designed to collect information about your habits and preferences related to health and wellness. This information will help us understand your needs and provide you with personalized guidance and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Make sure to complete all the sections that apply to you.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional notes" field at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your age?** (`age`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your age. Please note that this information is not required, but it will help us understand your demographic information.
* **preferred-time-for-exercise** (`preferred_time_for_exercise`, select_one, optional): This field asks if you prefer exercising at a specific time of the day. Please select "Yes" if you prefer exercising at a particular time, or "No" if you do not have a preference.
* **Exercise frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about your exercise habits. Please select all the times that apply to you, such as "Yes, in the morning", "Yes, in the evening", etc.
* **sleep-quality** (`sleep_quality`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe your sleep quality. Please provide any information you'd like to share about your sleep habits.
* **nutrition-preferences** (`nutrition_preferences`, select_one, optional): This field asks about your dietary preferences. Please select "Yes" if you follow a specific diet, or "No" if you do not have a specific diet.
* **healthy-habits** (`healthy_habits`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you can select all the healthy habits you follow, such as "Yes, I exercise regularly", "Yes, I eat healthy foods", etc.
* **wellness-goals** (`wellness_goals`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about your wellness goals. Please select all the goals that apply to you, such as "I want to lose weight", "I want to reduce stress", etc.
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or questions you have. Please use this space to share any information you think is relevant to your health and wellness journey.
