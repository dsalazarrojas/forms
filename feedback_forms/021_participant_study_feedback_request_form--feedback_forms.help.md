# Participant Study Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Study Feedback Request Form is designed to gather feedback from research participants to improve future research sessions. This form helps us understand your experiences and gather suggestions for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answer that best represents your experience on each page. You will not need to provide any additional information unless you wish to add a note in the second page. If you are unsure about the answer, please choose the option that best represents your feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How was your research experience? 
* **How was your research experience?** (`Very Satisfied`, `Somewhat Satisfied`, `Neither Satisfied nor Dissatisfied`, `Somewhat Dissatisfied`, `Very Dissatisfied`): Please choose the option that best describes your overall experience in our research session. This will help us understand the quality of our service.

### 2. What would you like to see in our future research sessions? 
* **What would you like to see in our future research sessions?** (Text box): Please type any suggestions or ideas you have to improve our research sessions. This is an open text field for your comments.

### 3. How may we contact you? 
* **How may we contact you?** (`<email>`): Please enter your email address where you can be contacted for any further communication or follow-up questions.
