# Infrastructure Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your thoughts about our infrastructure services. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and ensure you have a great experience with our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer the questions honestly, providing a brief description for each field.
2. Select "other" as an option for any field where the provided options do not fit your experience.
3. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you if needed.
4. Choose the assigned tool and category for your feedback.
5. Upload a file if you'd like to attach any supporting documentation.
6. Enter the form ID for internal tracking purposes.
7. Select the scale for each of the quality and satisfaction questions.
8. Provide additional comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Service Quality**: Briefly describe your experience with our user-facing services.
* **Satisfaction With Services**: What do you think about the services we provide?
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with our services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Delivery Time**: Estimate the time taken to deliver our services.
* **User Experience**: Share your thoughts about your experience using our services.
* **Service Quality**: Describe any issues you've encountered with our service quality.
* **Satisfaction With Service**: Rate your satisfaction with the service you received.
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or suggestions you have for improving our services.
* **Improvement Ideas**: If you have any ideas for how we can improve, share them here.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or questions you'd like to ask.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have more to say, add it here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you if necessary.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool associated with your feedback (e.g., ChatJimmy).
* **Category**: Choose a category for your feedback (e.g., Feedback Forms).
* **Form ID**: Enter the unique ID for this form, if provided.
* **Output File**: Select the file to upload for your feedback.
* **Created At**: This is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Updated At**: This is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Form ID**: This is automatically generated and not editable.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool associated with your feedback.
* **User Service Quality Scale**: Rate your experience with our user-facing services on a scale of 1-5.

Please take a few minutes to complete this form, and we'll appreciate your feedback.
