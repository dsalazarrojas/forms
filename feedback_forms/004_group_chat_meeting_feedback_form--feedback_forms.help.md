# Group Chat Meeting Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users after group chat meetings. It allows users to rate their experience and provide comments about the meeting. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to help us improve our group chat meeting experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the rating score that best represents your experience with the meeting.
2. Provide any comments or suggestions you may have about the meeting.
3. Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive updates or responses to your comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Chat Meeting Feedback** (`group_chat_meeting_feedback`, `note`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the meeting. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, `number`, required: false): Choose a score between 1 and 5 to rate your experience with the meeting. A higher score indicates a more positive experience.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `text`, required: false): Share any comments or suggestions you may have about the meeting. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): If you'd like to receive updates or responses to your comments, please enter your email address. We'll do our best to keep you informed about any follow-up actions or changes.
