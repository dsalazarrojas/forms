# Product Review Removal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Review Removal Request Form is for customers who want to request the removal of a review that is no longer relevant or accurate. This form helps the customer to provide a valid reason for the removal request and evidence to support their claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Product Review Removal Request** (`product_review_removal_request_form`): Enter the product review that you no longer want to keep on the site.
2. **Reason for Removal Request** (`reason_for_removal_request`): Provide a valid reason for requesting the review removal. Please be specific and concise in your explanation.
3. **Link to Original Review**: If the review is publicly available, please provide the link to the original review.
4. **Evidence**: Provide any evidence that supports your removal request.
5. **Manage Data Collection**: Choose whether to manage data collection for the removed review (this is a critical step to ensure data privacy).
6. **Capture Evidence**: Select multiple options to capture evidence of your issue (e.g., screenshot, document, etc.).
7. **Date of Removal Request** (`date_of_removal_request`): You can choose a date for the removal request.
8. **Time of Removal Request** (`time_of_removal_request`): You can choose a time for the removal request.
9. **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
10. **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (optional).
11. **Note** (`note`): Add any additional information or comments about the review.
