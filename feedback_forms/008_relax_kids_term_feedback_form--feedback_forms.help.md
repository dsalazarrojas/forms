# relax_kids_term_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from parents or caregivers about their experience with our organization, focusing on the relaxation and term experience of their child(ren).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "How would you rate your overall experience?" question with a number rating from 0-10.
2. Choose a rating for "Rating" using the 1-5 star scale.
3. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the "Comment" field.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Enter your name for identification purposes.
6. If applicable, provide a contact phone number for further communication.
7. Finally, input the date and time of your child's session(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate your overall experience?** (Testimonial, Optional): This field asks for your opinion on your overall experience with our organization.
* **Rating** (Rating, Optional): Choose a rating for your experience using the 1-5 star scale.
* **1-5 star scale** (Select One, Optional): Select a rating from 1 to 5 stars to reflect your experience.
* **Comment** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes, so we can reach out to you for further information or clarification.
* **Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name for identification purposes, so we can address you by name in our communication.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): If you have a preferred contact phone number, enter it here for further communication.
* **Session Date** (Date, Optional): Input the date of your child's session(s).
* **Session Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your child's session(s).

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form, providing detailed feedback about your experience.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your preferred contact email or phone number.
