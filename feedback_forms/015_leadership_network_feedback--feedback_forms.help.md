# leadership_network_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership_network_feedback form is used to collect feedback from participants in leadership programs, events, and other communication channels. This feedback is crucial to improve the quality and effectiveness of these initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the leadership_network_feedback form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you are providing feedback for a specific leadership program, event, or communication channel.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Answer each question carefully, as your feedback is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leadership Program Feedback** (`Leadership Program Feedback`, text, optional): This field is for providing general feedback about the leadership program. Please share your thoughts, suggestions, or comments about the program.
* **Event Attendance** (`Event Attendance`, select_one, optional): This field asks if you attended the event or program. Select 'True' if you attended, 'False' if you did not.
* **Communication Channels** (`Communication Channels`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks which communication channels you have used or would like to use for future communication. Please select all that apply.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`Event Satisfaction`, number, optional): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the event or program on a numerical scale. Please respond with a number between 1 and 5.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`Communication Effectiveness`, text, optional): This field is for providing feedback about the effectiveness of our communication. Please share your thoughts or suggestions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback is valuable to us.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please feel free to include them in the `Communication Effectiveness` field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* This form should only be completed once per leadership program, event, or communication channel, to avoid duplication of feedback.
