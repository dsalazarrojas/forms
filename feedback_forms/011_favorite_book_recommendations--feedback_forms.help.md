# Favorite Book Recommendations - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your book-related feedback, suggestions, and reading habits to help publishers, authors, and educators better understand their audience's reading preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the book you would like to recommend.
2. Provide your rating (1-10) of the book.
3. Answer the questions about the book's genre, author, and reading habits.
4. Fill in your contact information if you'd like to be contacted.
5. Add any comments or suggestions about the book.

* **Book Suggestions**: Choose the book you would like to recommend.

* **Rating (out of 10)**: Give your rating of the book.

* **Favorite Genre**: Choose your favorite genre of the book.
