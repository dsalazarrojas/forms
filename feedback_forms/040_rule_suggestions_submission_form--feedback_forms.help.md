# Rule Suggestions Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rule Suggestions Submission Form is designed to collect feedback and rule suggestions from submitters. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your suggestions are properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the appropriate option for "Submit Rule Suggestion". This will determine whether you want to submit a suggestion or not.
2. Provide a detailed description of your suggestion or feedback.
3. Select the relevant category for your suggestion or feedback.
4. Enter your name, email, department, position, and other required information.
5. If necessary, add any additional comments or attachments.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your suggestion or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Rule Suggestion** (`rule_suggestion_submit`, select_one): Choose the option "True" to submit a rule suggestion or "False" to cancel your submission.
* **Description** (`rule_suggestion_description`, text): Please provide a detailed description of your rule suggestion or feedback.
* **Category** (`submitter_category`, select_one): Select the category that best fits your rule suggestion or feedback.
* **Name** (`submitter_name`, text): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the submitted suggestion.
* **Email** (`submitter_email`, email): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Department** (`submitter_department`, text): Enter the department or team you belong to.
* **Position** (`submitter_position`, text): Enter your position or role within your department.
* **Comment** (`submitter_comment`, text): If you have any additional comments or notes about your suggestion or feedback, please add them here.
* **Attachment** (`submitter_attachment`, text): If you have any relevant attachments or files to support your suggestion or feedback, please upload them here. This field is not required.
* **Notes** (`submitter_notes`, note): This field is not required and can be used to add any additional notes or comments.
* **Submit** (`submit_form_submit`, text): Click this button to submit your suggestion or feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text): This is a duplicate of the "Submit" button. Please only click this button once.
* **Submitter Email 2** (`submitter_email_2`, email): This is a duplicate field for your email address. Please enter the same email address as in the "Email" field.
* **Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text): If you have a phone number that you would like to be reached at, please enter it here. This field is not required.
* **Submitter Attachment 2** (`submitter_attachment_2`, text): This field is not required and can be used to add any additional attachments or files.
* **Submitter Position 2** (`submitter_position_2`, text): This field is not required and can be used to add any additional information about your position.
* **Submitter Department 2** (`submitter_department_2`, text): This field is not required and can be used to add any additional information about your department.
