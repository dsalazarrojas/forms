# salesforce_integration_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on our Salesforce integration. Your input helps us refine the integration and make it more efficient for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Salesforce Integration" from the first page.
2. Answer the two text questions, "What do you think about the current integration?" and "How can we improve the current integration?" in the spaces provided.
3. Choose the category that best describes your feedback from the "Category" dropdown menu.
4. Review your answers and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Salesforce Integration** (`salesforce_integration`)
* This section is where you select the first topic of your feedback.
* Please choose "Salesforce Integration" to begin.

### **What do you think about the current integration?** (`feedback_question_1`)
* This question is an opportunity to share your thoughts on the current state of our Salesforce integration.
* Be as specific as possible and provide concrete details.

### **How can we improve the current integration?** (`feedback_question_2`)
* This question allows you to suggest ways we can enhance our integration.
* Think creatively and help us identify areas for improvement.

### **Category** (`category`)
* Please select one or more categories that best describe your feedback from:
	+ **Bug**: Report issues or defects with the integration.
	+ **Feature request**: Suggest new features or functionality.
	+ **General**: Share general comments or suggestions.

## Tips
* Be honest and constructive with your feedback.
* Your input will help us make our Salesforce integration more efficient and effective.
* If you're unsure about a field or have questions, please let us know and we'll be happy to assist.
