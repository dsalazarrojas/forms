# Video Synthesis Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Video Synthesis Feedback Form is designed to collect opinions and comments on the video synthesis process from users. This feedback will help us improve the quality and realism of our video files.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your rating for each of the provided fields:
	* Video Realism: Rate how realistic the video looks to you.
	* Audio Sync: Rate how well the audio and video are synchronized.
	* Overall Quality: Rate the overall quality of the video.
2. Provide additional feedback in the "Feedback" field, if needed.
3. Enter your Email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Video Realism** (`video_realism_rating`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate how realistic the video looks to you, using the ratings:
		- 1.0
		- 1.5
		- 2.0
		- 2.5
		- 3.0
		- 3.5
		- 4.0
		- 4.5
		- 5.0
* **Audio Sync** (`audio_sync_rating`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate how well the audio and video are synchronized, using the same ratings as above.
* **Overall Quality** (`overall_quality_rating`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate the overall quality of the video, using the same ratings as above.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, required):
	+ Enter any additional comments or suggestions about the video synthesis process.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Enter your Email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number, if you'd like us to contact you via phone.
* **Audio Sync Quality** (`audio_sync_quality_rating`, number, optional):
	+ Rate how well the audio quality of the video is, using a numerical value (e.g., 1-5).
* **Video Realism Hints** (`video_realism_hints`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Check any hints or suggestions you have about improving video realism.
* **Audio Sync Hints** (`audio_sync_hints`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Check any hints or suggestions you have about improving audio sync.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required):
	+ Click this button to submit your feedback.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the "Feedback" field.
* Make sure to enter your Email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or discussions.
