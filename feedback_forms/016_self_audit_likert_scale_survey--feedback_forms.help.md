# self_audit_likert_scale_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self_audit_likert_scale_survey form is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experiences and perceptions of the workplace culture, safety, job satisfaction, and overall work environment. This form helps management gather honest feedback and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. For each question, choose the response that best reflects your feelings or experiences.
2. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are marked as required.
3. Provide honest and thoughtful feedback in the open-ended questions (fields 7, 8).
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, the workplace culture is** (`overall_workplace_culture`, select_one, **required**):
  * Choose the option that best describes your overall feeling about the workplace culture.
  * Options: Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor
* **I feel a sense of ownership for my work** (`job_ownership`, select_one, **required**):
  * Choose the option that best describes how you feel about your job ownership.
  * Options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Disagree, Strongly Disagree
* **The level of workplace safety is** (`workplace_safety`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe your feelings about workplace safety.
  * Options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neither Agree nor Disagree, Disagree, Strongly Disagree
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe your overall job satisfaction.
  * Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Very Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied
* **Overall, my feedback on culture and safety** (`culture_and_safety`, select_one, **required**):
  * Choose the option that best describes your overall feedback on culture and safety.
  * Options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Neither Agree nor Disagree, Disagree, Strongly Disagree
* **Overall, I am satisfied with my workplace** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe your overall satisfaction with your workplace.
  * Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Very Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied
* **Feedback on my current culture** (`feedback_culture`, text, **optional**):
  * Provide any additional feedback or comments about your current culture.
  * Please keep your response concise and clear.
* **Feedback on my current workplace safety** (`feedback_safety`, text, **optional**):
  * Provide any additional feedback or comments about your current workplace safety.
  * Please keep your response concise and clear.
* **Overall, I feel safe in my workplace** (`overall_safety`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe how you feel about safety in your workplace.
  * Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Very Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied
* **I am satisfied with my job overall** (`job_satisfaction_2`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe your overall job satisfaction.
  * Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Very Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied
* **My workplace is satisfying** (`workplace_satisfaction_2`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe your satisfaction with your workplace.
  * Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Very Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied
* **I feel ownership for my work** (`feelings_of_ownership`, select_one, **required**):
  * Choose the option that best describes how you feel about your job ownership.
  * Options: Strongly Agree, Agree, Disagree, Strongly Disagree
* **I am satisfied with the culture of my workplace** (`culture_satisfaction`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe your satisfaction with the culture of your workplace.
  * Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Very Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied
* **Overall Safety 2** (`overall_safety_2`, select_multiple, **required**):
  * Choose all the options that best describe how you feel about safety in your workplace.
  * Options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Very Satisfied, Not Very Unsatisfied, Very Unsatisfied

## Tips

* Be honest and clear in your answers.
* Provide constructive feedback that can help improve the workplace.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the open-ended fields (7 and 8) to provide them.
