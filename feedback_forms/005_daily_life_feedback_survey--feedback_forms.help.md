# Daily Life Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Life Feedback Survey is a tool for evaluating your work life balance and satisfaction. It consists of multiple questions that ask about your feelings towards your job, work hours, and communication skills. The collected data will help to identify areas for improvement in your work and personal life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the answers to each question based on your current feelings about your work and personal life.
2. Select your answers from the options provided for each question.
3. Ensure that you fill in all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Questions** (`select_multiple`, `Yes/No`): Answer each question based on how you feel about the corresponding area in your work and personal life. Select 'Yes' for questions where you feel well, and 'No' for questions where you feel poorly.
  - **Work Life Balance** (`select_one`, `Yes/No`): Choose 'Yes' if you feel that your work life balance is good and 'No' otherwise.
  - **Work Satisfaction** (`select_multiple`, `Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Select 'Satisfied' for questions where you feel well about your work and 'Dissatisfied' otherwise.
  - **Personal Satisfaction** (`select_one`, `Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Choose 'Satisfied' if you feel that your personal life is good and 'Dissatisfied' otherwise.
  - **Daily Life Balance** (`select_one`, `Yes/No`): Select 'Yes' if you feel that your daily life balance is good and 'No' otherwise.
  - **Communication Skills** (`select_multiple`, `Yes/No`): Select 'Yes' if you have good communication skills and 'No' otherwise.
  - **Work Life Satisfaction** (`select_one`, `Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Choose 'Satisfied' if you feel that your work life satisfaction is good and 'Dissatisfied' otherwise.
  - **Job Satisfaction** (`select_one`, `Satisfied/Dissatisfied`): Select 'Satisfied' if you're satisfied with your job and 'Dissatisfied' otherwise.
  - **Work Life Balance Score** (`number`, score): Rate your work life balance as a score (e.g. 1-5).
  - **Daily Life Balance Score** (`number`, score): Rate your daily life balance as a score (e.g. 1-5).
  - **Time Offered** (`time`, hours): Enter the hours offered to you to complete the form.
  - **Daily Life Score** (`number`, score): Rate your daily life as a score (e.g. 1-5).
  - **Job Score** (`number`, score): Rate your job as a score (e.g. 1-5).
  - **Communication Score** (`number`, score): Rate your communication skills as a score (e.g. 1-5).
  - **Work Life Score** (`number`, score): Rate your work life as a score (e.g. 1-5).
  - **Work Satisfaction Score** (`number`, score): Rate your work satisfaction as a score (e.g. 1-5).
  - **Work Life** (`select_multiple`, `Yes/No`): Select 'Yes' if you feel that you have a good work life balance and 'No' otherwise.
