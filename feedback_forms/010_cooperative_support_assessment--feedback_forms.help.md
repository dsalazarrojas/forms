# Cooperative Support Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cooperative Support Assessment is a form meant for gathering feedback and assessment of various services provided by a cooperative or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Rate your experience with each of the services provided by the cooperative/organization using the scales provided for "rating_1", "rating_2", and "rating_4".
2. Provide your rating for "rating_4" which is meant for overall satisfaction with the services provided.
3. In the "comments" field, provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the services provided.
4. In the "comments_2" field, provide any additional information you would like to mention.
5. Select which services you received from "service_1", "service_2", and "service_3" that are applicable to your experience.
6. Finally, provide your name and email address to allow for follow-up with you, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rating 1**: Rate your experience with **Service 1** (scale of 1-5)
* **Rating 2**: Rate your experience with **Service 2** (scale of 1-5)
* **Rating 3**: Rate your experience with **Service 3** (scale of 1-5)
* **Rating 4**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the services provided (scale of 1-5)
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your experience with the services provided.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information you would like to mention.
* **Service 1**: Select the service(s) you received from **Service 1**.
	+ Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
* **Service 2**: Select the service(s) you received from **Service 2**.
	+ Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
* **Service 3**: Select the service(s) you received from **Service 3**.
	+ Options: option 1, option 2, option 3
* **Email**: Please enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Name**: Please enter your name for follow-up purposes.
