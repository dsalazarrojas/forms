# Drought Awareness Workshop Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after a drought awareness workshop. It helps us evaluate the workshop's effectiveness and make necessary adjustments for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please ensure you have the following information ready:

1.  Your name and contact number
2.  Your experience with the workshop content and presentation
3.  Any additional comments or questions
4.  The session you attended
5.  How you would like to be followed up
6.  Your email address
7.  Your feedback about the workshop

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Details** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Enter your name and contact number for us to contact you if needed.
*   **Ratings** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Rate your experience with the workshop content and presentation on a scale provided in the options.
*   **Comments** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Share your thoughts about your experience during the workshop.
*   **Participant Interest** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): Indicate if you have any interest in the topic and if so, specify how.
*   **Additional Comments** (<name>, <type>, required/optional): If you have any further questions or comments, please provide them here.
*   **Session Attended** (<name>, <type, options>): Select which session of the workshop you attended from the options.
*   **Follow Up** (<name>, <type, options>): Choose how you would like to be followed up (if at all).
*   **Email Sent** (<name>, <type, options>): Select if you would like to be followed up via email (if at all).
*   **Participant Feedback** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Provide feedback about the workshop, including any dates and times relevant to your experience.
*   **Follow Up Message** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter a follow-up message if you would like to receive further communication.
*   **Email Follow Up Message** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter a message if you would like to be contacted via email.
*   **Follow Up Sent** (<name>, <type, options>): Select if you were followed up or not.
*   **Feedback** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter the date and time of your feedback (if applicable).
*   **Feedback** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the workshop.
*   **Contact Number** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter your contact number for us to contact you.
*   **Feedback Email** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
*   **Additional Questions** (<name>, <type, required/optional): If you have any additional questions, please provide them here.
*   **Participant Signature** (<name>, <type, required/optional): This field is not applicable for user input.
*   **Session Notes** (<name>, <type, required/optional): This field is not applicable for user input.
*   **Follow Up Date** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter the date when you would like to be followed up (if at all).
*   **Feedback** (<name>, <type, required/optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the workshop.
