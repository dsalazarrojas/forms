# Collaboration Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your collaboration preferences, working style, and communication methods. Your input will help us improve our collaboration tools and processes to better support team members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. For select-type questions, choose one option that best describes your preference or experience.
3. For select_multiple-type questions, choose all options that apply to you.
4. For text-type questions, provide your answer in the text field.

* **Full Name** (1): Enter your full name. This is optional and will help us address you personally if needed.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your work email address. This is optional but will help us reach out to you if needed.
* **Department** (3): Enter your department or team.

* **Communication Methods You Use Regularly** (8): Select all methods you use regularly, such as email, Slack, Zoom, etc.

* **Satisfaction with Current Collaboration Tools** (13): Choose how satisfied you are with your current collaboration tools.
* **Preferred Work Style** (14): Choose how you work best, such as independently or in a team.

Note: Please take a few minutes to read through each field and ensure you answer all questions to the best of your ability. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
