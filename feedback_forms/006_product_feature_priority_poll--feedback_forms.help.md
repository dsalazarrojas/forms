# Product Feature Priority Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather suggestions and feedback on new product features from customers.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
*   Fill out the form header with a suggestion or idea for a new product feature.
*   Enter the name of the suggested feature.
*   Describe the feature in detail.
*   Select the priority level for the suggested feature (High, Medium, or Low).
*   Choose one or more expected outcomes of the feature (e.g., Increased customer satisfaction, Improved customer engagement, etc.).
*   Enter the main goal of the feature.
*   Enter your email address for follow-up and discussion.
*   Optionally, share any additional feedback you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Product Feature Suggestion**: Enter a brief idea or suggestion for a new product feature.
*   **Product Feature Name**: Enter the name of the suggested feature. (e.g., "Feature A", "Feature B", etc.)
*   **Description**: Describe the feature in detail, explaining its benefits and how it would work. 
*   **Priority**: Choose the priority level for the feature: (High, Medium, Low)
*   **Expected Outcomes**: Choose one or more expected outcomes for the feature (e.g., Increased customer satisfaction, Improved customer engagement, etc.)
*   **Main Goal**: Enter the main goal of the feature.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up and discussion. (This will help us contact you with any questions or updates)
*   **Feedback**: Share any additional feedback you have about the feature or the process.
