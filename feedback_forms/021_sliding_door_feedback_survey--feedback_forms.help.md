# Sliding Door Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with the sliding door brand. Your input will help us understand your satisfaction and preferences, enabling us to improve our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your current sliding door brand from the list.
2. Rate your experience with the sliding door on a scale of 1-5.
3. Choose how satisfied you are with the current brand.
4. Enter any additional comments about the brand.
5. Provide your name (if you wish to provide it).
6. Enter your email address (if you wish to provide it).
7. Choose how you heard about us.
8. Enter any additional comments.
9. If applicable, specify the product model and version you're using.
10. Enter the date you purchased the product.
11. If applicable, specify where you purchased the product.
