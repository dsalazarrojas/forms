# juice_production_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers regarding the production of a product. It helps us understand their experience and opinions about the product, allowing us to make improvements and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, start by entering your Customer Name in the "Customer Name" field.
2.  Next, enter the name of the product you are providing feedback on in the "Product Name" field.
3.  Select the production line for the product from the drop-down menu in the "Production Line" field.
4.  Answer the feedback questions in the "How likely are you to recommend this product?" field by selecting one of the options provided.
5.  Enter your thoughts on what you liked about the product in the "What did you like about this product?" field.
6.  Enter your thoughts on what you disliked about the product in the "What did you dislike about this product?" field.
7.  Rate your overall satisfaction with the product in the "Rating" field.
8.  Enter any additional comments about your experience in the "Comments" field, if applicable.
9.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field for us to contact you.
10.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
11.  Enter the date and time you are submitting this form in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.
12.  Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field for us to contact you regarding your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`Customer Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like it to be addressed.
*   **Product Name** (`Product Name`, `text`, required: true): The name of the product you are providing feedback on.
*   **Production Line** (`Production Line`, `text`, required: true): Select the production line for the product from the provided options.
*   **How likely are you to recommend this product?** (`select_one`, required: true): Select the level of recommendation provided in the options.
*   **What did you like about this product?** (`text`, required: true): Enter your thoughts on what you liked about the product.
*   **What did you dislike about this product?** (`text`, required: true): Enter your thoughts on what you disliked about the product.
*   **Rating** (`number`, required: true): Rate your overall satisfaction with the product from 1 to 5.
*   **Comments** (`text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the product.
*   **Email** (`email`, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
*   **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
*   **Submission Date** (`date`, required: true): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
*   **Submission Time** (`time`, required: true): Enter the time you are submitting this form.
*   **Customer Email** (`email`, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
