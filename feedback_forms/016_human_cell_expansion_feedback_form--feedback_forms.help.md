# Human Cell Expansion Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Human Cell Expansion Feedback Form is a tool designed to collect feedback from users on the success or failure of human cell expansion. The information collected through this form helps researchers and scientists in assessing the effectiveness of various cell expansion methods and instruments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **User Input**: Please enter the relevant information about yourself as the user filling out this form.
2. **Feedback**: Describe your experience with the human cell expansion, including any observations, comments, or insights you may have.
3. **Expansion Status**: Select the status of the expansion from the provided options (Fully Expanded, Partially Expanded, or Not Expanded).
4. **Lab Instrumentation**: List the lab instrumentation used during the expansion process, selecting all that apply.
5. **Sample Type**: Choose the type of sample used for expansion.
6. **Expansion Method**: Choose the method used for expansion.
7. **Expansion Notes and Comments**: Add any additional comments or observations about the expansion process.
8. **Cell Count**: Enter the number of cells obtained after expansion, if applicable.
9. **Cell Viability**: Enter the viability of the cells after expansion, if applicable.
10. **Expansion Rate**: Enter the rate of expansion, if applicable.
11. **Expansion Efficiency**: Enter the efficiency of expansion, if applicable.
12. **Cell Expansion Status**: Select the final status of the cell expansion (Expanded or Not Expanded).
13. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or observations about the experiment.
14. **Submitted By**: Enter your name or the name of the person who submitted the feedback.
15. **Submitted Date**: Select the date when the feedback was submitted.
16. **Submitted Time**: Select the time when the feedback was submitted.
17. **Lab Instrument**: Enter the lab instrument used, if applicable.
18. **Lab Instrument Type**: Enter the type of lab instrument used, if applicable.
19. **Lab Instrument Model**: Enter the model of the lab instrument used, if applicable.
20. **Instrument Notes**: Add any notes about the lab instrument used.
21. **Lab Technician**: Enter the name of the lab technician who performed the expansion, if applicable.
22. **Lab Technician Signature**: Enter the signature of the lab technician, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: A field for entering information about the user submitting the feedback.
* **Feedback**: A text field for providing comments or observations about the human cell expansion.
* **Expansion Status**: A select-one field with three options (Fully Expanded, Partially Expanded, or Not Expanded) indicating the status of the cell expansion.
* **Lab Instrumentation**: A multiple-select field for listing the lab instrumentation used during the expansion process.
* **Sample Type**: A select-one field with three options (Sample 1, Sample 2, or Sample 3) indicating the type of sample used for expansion.
* **Expansion Method**: A select-one field with three options (Method 1, Method 2, or Method 3) indicating the method used for expansion.
* **Expansion Notes and Comments**: A text field for entering any additional comments or observations about the expansion process.
* **Cell Count**: A numeric field for entering the number of cells obtained after expansion, if applicable.
* **Cell Viability**: A numeric field for entering the viability of the cells after expansion, if applicable.
* **Expansion Rate**: A numeric field for entering the rate of expansion, if applicable.
* **Expansion Efficiency**: A numeric field for entering the efficiency of expansion, if applicable.
* **Cell Expansion Status**: A select-one field with two options (Expanded or Not Expanded) indicating the final status of the cell expansion.
* **Notes**: A text field for entering any additional comments or observations about the experiment.
* **Submitted By**: A text field for entering the name of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **Submitted Date**: A date field for selecting the date when the feedback was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: A time field for selecting the time when the feedback was submitted.
* **Lab Instrument**: A text field for entering the lab instrument used, if applicable.
* **Lab Instrument Type**: A text field for entering the type of lab instrument used, if applicable.
