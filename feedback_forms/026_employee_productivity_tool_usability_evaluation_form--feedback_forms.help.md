# Employee Productivity Tool Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from employees on the usability of our company's productivity tool. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make the tool more user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: In the "First Name" field, please type your first name as it appears on your employee ID or other official documents.
2. **Enter your Email**: In the "Email" field, enter your company email address.
3. **Enter the Tool Name**: In the "Tool Name" field, enter the name of the productivity tool you are evaluating (e.g., "Trello", "Asana", etc.).
4. **Enter the Tool Version**: In the "Tool Version" field, enter the version of the tool you are using (if applicable).
5. **Provide Feedback**: In the "Feedback" field, describe your experiences with the productivity tool, including any difficulties you faced, suggestions for improvement, or areas where it excels.
6. **Rate the Tool**: In the "Rating" field, select one of the options:
	* Excellent: The tool is meeting or exceeding expectations.
	* Good: The tool is meeting some expectations, but with some room for improvement.
	* Fair: The tool is meeting some, but not all, expectations.
	* Poor: The tool is not meeting expectations.
7. **Provide Additional Comments**: In the "Comments" field, add any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your employee ID or other official documents.
* **Email**: Your company email address.
* **Tool Name**: The name of the productivity tool you are evaluating.
* **Tool Version**: The version of the tool you are using (if applicable).
* **Feedback**: Describe your experiences with the productivity tool, including any difficulties, suggestions for improvement, or areas where it excels.
* **Rating**: Select one of the four options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Submit**: Once you've completed the form, click the "Submit" button to send your feedback to us.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form honestly and thoroughly.
* Do not duplicate your feedback, as this will help us to efficiently process your responses.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
