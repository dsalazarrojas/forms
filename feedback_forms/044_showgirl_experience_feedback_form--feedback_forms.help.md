# showgirl_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with Showgirls. The feedback will help us understand what you think about the Showgirl's performance, attitude, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions about the Showgirl's performance, such as her category, performance, attitude, service, and value.
2. Rate the Showgirl's ease, care, and willingness.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Suggestions" field.
4. Enter your email address and contact information, if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
5. Enter the date and time of your experience with the Showgirl.
6. If you'd like to rate the Showgirl's attendance, please select from the provided options.
7. Finally, submit the form to share your feedback.
