# lash_salon_waiting_time_experience_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience at a lash salon. It aims to gather information on various aspects of their visit, including the friendliness of the reception staff, the waiting time, and the overall service provided. The goal is to identify areas of improvement and provide a better experience for future customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by reading the introduction, which provides an overview of the form's purpose.
2.  Answer each question according to your experience at the lash salon.
3.  For questions with multiple options, select the one that best describes your experience.
4.  If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can provide them in the feedback field.
5.  Finally, provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction (1)**: This is a brief overview of the form's purpose.
* **Experience at Reception (2)**: Rate your experience with the reception staff, selecting "Yes" if you were greeted warmly and "No" if you were not.
* **Experience at Reception (Hint) (3)**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the reception staff on a scale of 1-5.
* **Waiting Time (4)**: Select the time you spent waiting, using the time format (e.g., 30 minutes).
* **Waiting Time Satisfaction (5)**: Rate your satisfaction with the waiting time on a scale of 1-5.
* **Experience at Service (6)**: Rate your experience with the lash technician, selecting "Yes" if you were satisfied and "No" if you were not.
* **Experience at Service (Hint) (7)**: Rate the friendliness and helpfulness of the lash technician on a scale of 1-5.
* **Experience with Service (8)**: Select your overall experience with the service provided.
* **Experience with Service Hint (9)**: Rate the service you received on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Experience (10)**: Rate your overall experience at the lash salon on a scale of 1-5.
* **Feedback (11)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

Note: Fields with a hint are optional and provide additional context for the question.
