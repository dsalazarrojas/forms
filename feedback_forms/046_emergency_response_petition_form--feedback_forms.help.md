# emergency_response_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_response_petition_form is a tool used to collect information and support for emergency response efforts. It helps organizations receive and manage feedback, petitions, and requests from users in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are submitting an emergency response petition and not a general inquiry or support request.
2. Enter your organization's name in the `organization_name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `email` field, as this is a required field for contact purposes.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field, if applicable.
5. In the `message` field, type a clear and concise description of your emergency response petition.
6. Select one or more options from the `support` field to specify the type of support you are seeking.
7. If relevant, choose a specific date for the `initiative` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **organization_name**: Enter the name of the organization or entity you represent.
  This field is used to identify the organization submitting the request.
- **email**: Provide your email address so the organization can contact you directly.
  This field is required for contact purposes.
- **phone**: Enter your phone number, if applicable.
  This field is optional and provides an additional contact method.
- **message**: Describe the emergency response petition in a clear and concise manner.
  This field should include relevant details and context.
- **support**: Select one or more options to specify the type of support you are seeking.
  This field helps the organization understand the nature of your request.
- **initiative**: If relevant, select a specific date for your emergency response petition.
  This field is optional and provides a clear date for organization reference.
