# Individual Strategy Lab Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants of an individual strategy lab, allowing us to gather their opinions and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your title as the participant in the `Participant Name` field.
2. Enter your organization name in the `Participant Organization` field, if applicable.
3. Provide your contact information, including your email address, in the `Participant Email` field and your phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
4. Describe your experience in the `Participant Feedback` field, including any suggestions or comments you may have.
5. Rate your satisfaction with the lab experience using the `Participant Satisfaction` field.
6. Enter any feedback or comments you have about the lab in the `Lab Feedback` field.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have in the `Participant Suggestions` field.
8. Enter any comments you have about this form in the `Form Comments` field, if needed.
9. Finally, upload any attachments related to your feedback in the `Form Attachments` field and describe the attachment in the `Attachment Description` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is already filled in.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the participant.
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Lab Name** (`lab_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the lab you participated in.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the lab session you participated in.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required): Describe your experience and any feedback you have about the lab.
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the lab experience using the provided options.
* **Lab Feedback** (`lab_feedback`, text, required): Enter any additional feedback or comments you have about the lab.
* **Participant Suggestions** (`participant_suggestions`, text, required): Add any suggestions you have for improving the lab experience.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments you have about your experience.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments you have about this form.
* **Form Attachments** (`form_attachments`, text, required): Upload any attachments related to your feedback and enter a description of the attachment.
* **Attachment Description** (`attachment_description`, text, required): Describe the attachment you uploaded.
* **File 1** (`file_1`, text, optional): Upload a file and enter a description of the file.
* **File 2** (`file_2`, text, optional): Upload a file and enter a description of the file.
* **File 3** (`file_3`, text, optional): Upload a file and enter a description of the file.
* **File 4** (`file_4`, text, optional): Upload a file and enter a description of the file.
* **File 5** (`file_5`, text, optional): Upload a file and enter a description of the file.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the participant.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as the participant.
* **Participant Comments 2** (`participant_comments_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you have.
* **Form Comments 2** (`form_comments_2`, text, optional): Enter any comments you have about this form.
