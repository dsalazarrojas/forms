# rheumatology_patient_feedback_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from patients who have received care related to rheumatology, a branch of medicine dealing with rheumatic diseases. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the patient's experience, outcome, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Read each field carefully and answer accordingly.
3. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction**: A brief text field to explain the purpose and context of the survey. Please write a short introduction to help us understand the survey's purpose.
* **patient_name**: A text field to record the patient's name.
* **care_experience**: A text field to describe the patient's care experience. Please share your thoughts and feelings about the care you received.
* **care_outcome**: A text field to report the outcome of your care. Please select one of the options or describe the outcome in your own words.
* **care_outcome_other**: A text field for any other outcome not listed in the previous field.
* **other**: A text field for any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
* If you need assistance or clarification, please contact us.
* This form is for anonymous feedback, so don't worry about your identity being revealed.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the care we provide.
