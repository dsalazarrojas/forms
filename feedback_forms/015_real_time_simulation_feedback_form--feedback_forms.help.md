# real_time_simulation_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on real-time simulation sessions. It is essential to fill it out for each session you participate in to help us improve our sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to begin the form.
- Select the correct options for "Session ID" (if applicable), "Session Start Time", and "Session End Time".
- Enter your Participant Name and Facilitator Name if you're a participant or facilitator.
- Provide a brief Feedback on the Scenario, your Session Improvement Plan, and Evaluation of the session and the Facilitator.
- Rate your overall Satisfaction and let us know if you will participate in future sessions.
- Fill in any additional Comments, Suggestions, and Evaluation Comments you'd like to make.
- Select the Evaluation Scale to rate the session's overall quality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or badge number.
* **Session ID**: If you have an ID for the session, enter it to help us track your feedback.
* **Session Start Time**: Select the start time of your session using the date picker.
* **Session End Time**: Select the end time of your session using the date picker.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of your session.
* **Feedback on Scenario**: Provide a brief feedback on the simulation scenario.
* **Facilitator Name**: Enter the name of the facilitator leading your session.
* **Facilitator Notes**: Provide any notes or suggestions for the facilitator's next session.
* **Participant Notes**: Provide any personal notes or insights you'd like to share.
* **Session Improvement Plan**: Suggest improvements or changes for future sessions.
* **Next Session Date**: Enter the date you plan to participate in the next session.
* **Next Session Start Time**: Enter the start time of your next session.
* **Participant Feedback**: Provide a brief feedback on your experience with the session.
* **Facilitator Feedback**: Provide a brief feedback for the facilitator's performance.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the session (1-5 scale).
* **Will Participate In Future**: Let us know if you are interested or willing to participate in future sessions (True or False).
* **Rating Scale**: Rate the overall quality of the session (1-5 scale).
* **Comments**: Fill in any additional comments you'd like to make about your session experience.
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or recommendations for session improvement.
* **Follow Up Questions**: Fill in any follow-up questions you have about the session.
* **Session Evaluation**: Evaluate the session and provide a brief summary of your experience.
* **Participant Evaluation**: Evaluate your own performance in the session and provide a brief summary.
* **Facilitator Evaluation**: Evaluate the facilitator's performance in the session and provide a brief summary.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Fill in any additional comments you have about the session and the facilitator.
* **Evaluation Scale**: Rate the overall quality of the session and facilitator (1-5 scale).
