# Transportation Service Release Industry Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Service Release Industry Feedback Form is designed to capture feedback from users about their experience with the release of a transportation service. This form is used to gauge the service's quality, usability, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Navigate through the pages and fill in the fields as required.
2. Ensure that you answer each field truthfully and accurately.
3. Provide feedback on each section, using the space provided for notes and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Release Feedback**: This field is used to describe your experience with the service release. Please provide a brief description of what you experienced during the release.
* **Release Date**: Please provide the date of the service release, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Transportation Service Release Name**: Enter the name of the transportation service that was released.
* **Partners Involved**: Select all partners that were involved in the service release. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Clients Affected**: Select all clients that were affected by the service release. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Satisfaction Score**: Please provide a score between 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, to indicate your satisfaction with the service release.
* **Feedback**: Please provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the service release.
* **Email Contact**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Release Date Time**: Please provide the time of the service release in the format 12:00 PM or 13:30.
* **Release Notes**: Please provide any additional notes or comments about your experience with the service release.
* **Partner Affected**: Select the partner that was affected by the service release.
* **Client Affected**: Select all clients that were affected by the service release. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Satisfaction Score 2**: Please provide a score between 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, to indicate your satisfaction with the second aspect of the service release.
* **Feedback 2**: Please provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with the second aspect of the service release.
* **Email 2**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Phone 2**: Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further discussion or clarification.
* **Release Date Time 2**: Please provide the time of the second aspect of the service release in the format 12:00 PM or 13:30.

Note: Please ensure that you answer each field accurately and truthfully, and that your feedback is relevant to the service release.
