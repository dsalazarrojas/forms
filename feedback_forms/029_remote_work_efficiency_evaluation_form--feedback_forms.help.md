# Remote Work Efficiency Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the efficiency of remote work setups for employees. It gathers data on user and supervisor information, project details, and work hours statistics to help the organization identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the following information:

* Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "User Email" field (optional).
* Enter your supervisor's name in the "Supervisor Name" field.
* Enter the name of the project you are working on in the "Project Name" field.
* Enter the start date of your remote work in the "Remote Work Start Date" field.
* Enter the end date of your remote work in the "Remote Work End Date" field.
* Enter the number of hours you work per week in the "Work Hours Per Week" field (optional).
* Enter the average number of hours you worked per week in the "Average Hours Worked Weekly" field.
* Enter the average number of hours you worked per month in the "Average Hours Worked Monthly" field.
* If you faced any challenges while working remotely, describe them in the "Remote Work Challenges" field (optional).
* Select one or more opportunities for improving the efficiency of your remote work setup in the "Efficiency Improvement Opportunities" field.
* Provide any additional comments about your remote work experience in the "Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address associated with your work account.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project you are working on.
* **Remote Work Start Date** (`remote_work_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started working remotely.
* **Remote Work End Date** (`remote_work_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end your remote work or the date you are currently working on.
* **Work Hours Per Week** (`work_hours_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week. If this number is zero, select "0" to indicate you are not working at all.
* **Average Hours Worked Weekly** (`average_hours_worked_weekly`, number, required): Enter the average number of hours you worked per week while working remotely.
* **Average Hours Worked Monthly** (`average_hours_worked_monthly`, number, required): Enter the average number of hours you worked per month while working remotely.
* **Remote Work Challenges** (`remote_work_challenges`, text, optional): Describe any challenges you faced while working remotely.
* **Efficiency Improvement Opportunities** (`efficiency_improvement_opportunities`, select_one, required): Select one or more opportunities for improving remote work efficiency.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your remote work experience.
