# Mentoring Program Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from mentees about their experience in the mentoring program. The form aims to gather information on the effectiveness of the program, the performance of the mentor, and any feedback or comments that mentees may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the space provided, being as detailed and honest as possible.
2. Make sure to provide feedback on all required fields (Mentor Performance, Program Effectiveness).
3. You can provide feedback on the Feedback field if you wish.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click the submit button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide any feedback or comments you have about your experience in the mentoring program.
* **Mentor Performance** (`mentor_performance`, `text`, required): Describe the performance of your mentor, including any strengths or areas for improvement.
* **Program Effectiveness** (`program_effectiveness`, `text`, required): Provide feedback on how effective you found the mentoring program to be, including any positive or negative aspects.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, and try to provide concrete examples when possible.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to mention them in your feedback.
* Remember, your feedback is valuable to us, and will help us improve the mentoring program for future participants!
