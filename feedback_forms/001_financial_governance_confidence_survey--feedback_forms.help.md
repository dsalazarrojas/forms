# Financial Governance Confidence Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Governance Confidence Survey is a tool designed to gauge your confidence in various aspects of financial governance. It is intended to be completed by individuals with expertise in financial governance, such as financial professionals, accountants, and business owners. The survey aims to gather insights on your confidence levels in different areas of financial governance, helping to identify areas of strength and weakness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the survey sections and answer each question based on your confidence level in that area.
2. For each section, rate your confidence level on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Not Confident" and 5 is "Very Confident".
3. Enter your answers in the provided text fields, and submit the form when you have completed all sections.
