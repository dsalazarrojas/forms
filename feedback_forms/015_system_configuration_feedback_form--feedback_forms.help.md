# System Configuration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any issues or suggestions you may have regarding the system's configuration. Please fill out this form to help us improve our system.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and its purpose.
2. Select the relevant Category for your feedback.
3. Enter your Message, including any relevant details about the issue or suggestion.
4. If you have a preferred Email address or Phone number, you can enter it so we can contact you about your feedback.
5. Choose a Date and Time that the issue occurred, or select "No" for fields that don't apply.
6. Enter any additional Notes about the issue or suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the nature of your feedback. This will help us understand the context of your issue or suggestion.
	+ Choose from: Category 1, Category 2, Category 3
* **Message**: Enter a detailed description of the issue or suggestion you have. Be as clear and concise as possible.
* **Email**: If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, enter your email address here. This will help us reach out to you directly.
* **Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here. This will help us reach out to you directly.
* **Date**: Enter the Date when the issue or suggestion occurred. This will help us understand the timeline of the issue.
* **Time**: Enter the Time when the issue or suggestion occurred. This will help us understand the timing of the issue.
* **Note**: Enter any additional details or context about the issue or suggestion that may be helpful to us.

## Tips
* Please fill out one form per issue or suggestion.
* If you have already submitted feedback for the same issue, please do not re-submit it.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us and we'll do our best to help.
* Remember to enter any relevant details to help us better understand your feedback.
