# Hand Tracking Feature Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about the Hand Tracking feature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Feature Feedback** (feature_feedback, text, required): Share your insights or issues you've encountered while using the Hand Tracking feature.
2. **Comment** (comment, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the feature, please share them here.
3. **Rating** (rating, number, required): Rate your experience with the Hand Tracking feature, where 1 is 'Hand tracking is broken' and 5 is 'Hand tracking is working as expected'.
4. **Impact** (feature_impact, number, optional): If you've already given a low rating, please specify how much the issue is affecting your productivity or workflow.
5. **Suggestion** (suggestion, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for improving the Hand Tracking feature, please share them here.
6. **Expected Improvement** (expected_improvement, select_one, required): Choose how you expect the Hand Tracking feature to improve (Hand tracking is working as expected, Hand tracking needs a little more work, Hand tracking is broken).
7. **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns about the feature, please share them here.
8. **Feedback Category** (feedback_category, select_multiple, required): Choose which team should receive your feedback (AR/VR teams, Game Studios, Product developers).
9. **Email Address** (email_address, email, required): Please provide your email address so we can follow up with you if necessary.
10. **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
11. **Expected Next Steps** (expected_next_steps, select_one, required): Choose how you expect us to address your feedback (True, False, Not sure).
12. **Additional Feedback** (additional_feedback, text, optional): If you have any further feedback or comments, please share them here.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing feedback, especially when explaining your rating.
- If you have multiple suggestions, you can list them in the 'Suggestion' field.
- Please note that the 'Expected Improvement' field only allows you to choose one of the options provided.
- You can select multiple teams in the 'Feedback Category' field.
- Make sure to enter your email address so we can follow up with you if necessary.
