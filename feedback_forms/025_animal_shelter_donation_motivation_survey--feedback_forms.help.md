# Animal Shelter Donation Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to understand your motivations and preferences for supporting our animal shelter. Your input will help us improve our services and tailor our communication with you.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely, as it is designed to gather information about your experiences and preferences.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Please answer "not applicable" for questions that do not apply to your situation.
4. You can save your progress at any time and return to it later.
5. Once you have answered all the questions, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to contact you if needed.
* **Have You Donated Before**: Select how often you have donated to our shelter in the past.
	+ First-time Donor: This is your first donation.
	+ Occasional Donor: You have donated occasionally, but not regularly.
	+ Regular Donor: You have donated regularly.
	+ Major Donor: You have made significant contributions.
* **How Long Have You Supported Us**: If applicable, select how long you have supported our shelter.
	+ Less than 1 year: You have supported us for less than a year.
	+ 1-3 years: You have supported us for 1-3 years.
	+ 3-5 years: You have supported us for 3-5 years.
	+ 5-10 years: You have supported us for 5-10 years.
	+ 10+ years: You have supported us for more than 10 years.
* **-- Motivation for Giving --**: Briefly explain why you support our shelter.
* **Primary Reason for Donating**: Select the primary reason why you donate to our shelter.
	+ Love of animals: You support us because you care about animals.
	+ Personal experience with shelter: You have had a positive experience with our shelter.
	+ Belief in animal welfare: You believe in the importance of animal welfare.
	+ Tax benefits: You donate for tax purposes.
	+ Employer match: Your employer matches your donations.
	+ Helping local community: You support us because you care about your community.
	+ Religious or ethical beliefs: You donate based on your religious or ethical values.
	+ Other: Select this option if none of the above apply.
* **Emotional Connection to Cause**: Rate how connected you feel to the cause of our shelter.
	+ 1 - Not connected: You do not feel connected to our cause.
	+ 2 - Somewhat connected: You somewhat feel connected to our cause.
	+ 3 - Moderately connected: You moderately feel connected to our cause.
	+ 4 - Strongly connected: You strongly feel connected to our cause.
	+ 5 - Very strongly connected: You very strongly feel connected to our cause.
* **Do You Own Pets**: Select whether you currently own pets.
	+ Yes: You have pets.
	+ No: You do not have pets.
	+ Previously did: You used to own pets.
* **Have You Adopted from a Shelter**: Select whether you have adopted a pet from our shelter.
	+ Yes: You have adopted from our shelter.
	+ No: You have not adopted from our shelter.
	+ Plan to: You plan to adopt from our shelter.
* **-- Giving Preferences --**: Briefly explain how you prefer to give to our shelter.
* **Preferred Focus Area for Funding**: Select the area where you would like to see your funds directed.
	+ Medical care: You would like to see funds directed to medical care for animals.
	+ Food and supplies: You would like to see funds directed to food and supplies for animals.
	+ Shelter operations: You would like to see funds directed to shelter operations.
	+ Rescue programs: You would like to see funds directed to rescue programs.
	+ Adoption services: You would like to see funds directed to adoption services.
	+ Education: You would like to see funds directed to education.
	+ No preference: You have no preference.
* **Preferred Communication Frequency**: Select how often you would like to hear from us.
	+ Weekly: You would like to hear from us weekly.
	+ Monthly: You would like to hear from us monthly.
	+ Quarterly: You would like to hear from us quarterly.
	+ Annually: You would like to hear from us annually.
	+ Only for special campaigns: You would like to hear from us only for special campaigns.
	+ Minimal contact: You would like to hear from us minimally.
* **Preferred Communication Methods**: Select how you would like to hear from us.
	+ Email: You would like to receive emails from us.
	+ Phone: You would like to receive phone calls from us.
	+ Mail: You would like to receive mail from us.
	+ Text Message: You would like to receive text messages from us.
	+ Social Media: You would like to follow us on social media.
	+ Newsletter: You would like to receive our newsletter.
* **Comfortable Giving Amount**: Select the donation amount that feels comfortable for you.
	+ Under $50: You are comfortable donating under $50.
	+ $50-$100: You are comfortable donating between $50-$100.
	+ $100-$250: You are comfortable donating between $100-$250.
	+ $250-$500: You are comfortable donating between $250-$500.
	+ $500-$1000: You are comfortable donating between $500-$1000.
	+ Over $1000: You are comfortable donating over $1000.
* **Interested in Recurring Donations**: Select whether you are interested in making recurring donations.
	+ Yes: You are interested in making recurring donations.
	+ No: You are not interested in making recurring donations.
	+ Maybe: You are undecided about making recurring donations.
* **-- Impact and Outcomes --**: Briefly explain what information matters to you about the impact of your donations.
* **How Important is Impact Information**: Select how important it is for you to know how your funds are used.
	+ Very Important: It is very important to you.
	+ Important: It is somewhat important to you.
	+ Somewhat Important: It is somewhat important to you.
	+ Not Very Important: It is not very important to you.
* **What Impact Information Interests You**: Select what information you would like to know about the impact of your donations.
	+ Animals helped: You would like to know about the number of animals helped.
	+ Success stories: You would like to hear about success stories.
	+ Financial reports: You would like to see financial reports.
	+ Program results: You would like to know about the results of our programs.
	+ Staff updates: You would like to know about staff updates.
* **Importance of Public Recognition**: Select how important it is for you to be recognized as a donor.
	+ Very Important: It is very important to you.
	+ Important: It is somewhat important to you.
	+ Neutral: It is not very important to you.
* **-- Areas for Improvement --**: Briefly explain how you think we can improve our services.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share your thoughts on how we can improve our services.
* **Additional Comments or Feedback**: Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our shelter.
