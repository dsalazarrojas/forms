# customer_journey_mapping_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer journey mapping survey aims to understand customers' interactions with our customer service team. This survey gathers information on their mapping of our services and their overall satisfaction with the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Map their customer journey**: In section 1, select how their journey mapped with our service.
2. **Select relevant options**: In section 2, select all the options that apply to their visit.
3. **Enter visit details**: Enter the date and time of their visit.
4. **Rate our service**: Rate their experience with our customer service team.
5. **Score their satisfaction**: Score their overall satisfaction with our service.
6. **Share contact information (optional)**: If desired, provide their email and phone number.
7. **Leave comments (optional)**: Share any comments or feedback about their experience.

* **Section 1 (customer_journey_mapping_survey_form)**: This section asks the customer to map their journey with our service team. Select all the options that apply to their visit.
* **Section 2 (section_2)**: In this section, select all the options that apply to their visit.
* **Date of Visit (date_of_visit)**: Enter the date of their visit.
* **Time of Visit (time_of_visit)**: Enter the time of their visit.
* **Customer Service Rating (customer_service_rating)**: Rate their experience with our customer service team (e.g., 1-5).
* **Satisfaction Score (satisfaction_score)**: Score their overall satisfaction with our service (e.g., 1-5).
