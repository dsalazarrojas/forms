# Avalanche Safety Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Avalanche Safety Feedback Form is designed to gather information from visitors on their experience with avalanche safety during their visit to the ski area. This feedback is essential for ski area management to identify areas of improvement and enhance the overall safety and experience for all visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of visitor you are (e.g., Skier, Snowboarder, Backcountry Skier, etc.).
2. Select the date and time of your visit.
3. Choose the area or run you visited.
4. Indicate your skiing or riding experience level (beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert, etc.).
5. Select your level of avalanche training (awareness course only, AIARE 1 or equivalent, etc.).
6. Choose the safety equipment you carried with you (e.g., transceiver, probe pole, etc.).
7. Indicate if you performed a beacon check before heading out.
8. Select if you observed any avalanche activity during your visit.
9. Report if any safety incidents occurred during your visit.
10. Describe any incidents that occurred.
11. Select the weather conditions during your visit (clear, partly cloudy, overcast, etc.).
12. Choose the type of snow conditions you experienced (powder, packed, groomed, etc.).
13. Rate the clarity of safety signage (very clear, somewhat clear, neutral, etc.).
14. Rate the safety briefing you received (excellent, good, average, poor, etc.).
15. Select how visible the ski patrol was during your visit.
16. Rate your overall safety experience (very safe, safe, neutral, unsafe, etc.).
17. Provide any suggestions on how to improve avalanche safety.
18. Indicate if you would return to the ski area based on your experience.
19. Provide any additional comments on avalanche safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, Select One): Choose the type of visitor you are, such as Skier, Snowboarder, Backcountry Skier, etc.
* **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, Date): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`visit_time`, Time): Enter the approximate time of your visit (optional).
* **Area Visited** (`area_visited`, Select One): Choose the area or run you visited.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, Select One): Select your skiing or riding experience level (beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert, etc.).
* **Avalanche Training** (`avalanche_training`, Select One): Select your level of avalanche education (awareness course only, AIARE 1 or equivalent, etc.).
* **Safety Equipment Carried** (`safety_equipment_carried`, Select Multiple): Choose all the safety equipment you carried with you (transceiver, probe pole, etc.).
* **Beacon Check Performed** (`beacon_check_performed`, Select One): Indicate if you performed a beacon check before heading out.
* **Avalanche Observation** (`avalanche_observation`, Select One): Select if you observed any avalanche activity during your visit.
* **Incident Occurred** (`incident_occurred`, Select One): Report if any safety incidents occurred during your visit.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, Text): Describe any incidents that occurred.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, Select One): Select the weather conditions you experienced (clear, partly cloudy, overcast, etc.).
* **Snow Conditions** (`snow_conditions`, Select One): Choose the type of snow conditions you experienced (powder, packed, groomed, etc.).
* **Signage Clarity** (`signage_clarity`, Select One): Rate the clarity of safety signage (very clear, somewhat clear, neutral, etc.).
* **Safety Briefing Rating** (`safety_briefing_rating`, Select One): Rate the safety briefing you received (excellent, good, average, poor, etc.).
* **Patrol Visibility** (`patrol_visibility`, Select One): Select how visible the ski patrol was during your visit.
* **Overall Safety Rating** (`overall_safety_rating`, Select One): Rate your overall safety experience (very safe, safe, neutral, unsafe, etc.).
* **Improvement Suggestions** (`improvement_suggestions`, Text): Provide any suggestions on how to improve avalanche safety.
* **Would You Return** (`would_return`, Select One): Indicate if you would return to the ski area based on your experience.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text): Provide any additional comments on avalanche safety.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
- If you are unsure about any questions, please ask for clarification.
- Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve avalanche safety for everyone.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
