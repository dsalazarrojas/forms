# Additional Notes Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Additional Notes Submission Form is designed to collect additional information and feedback from users. Please use this form to submit any additional notes or comments that are not already captured in other forms or tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as the submitter.
2. Enter your name, if you want to be contacted about the note.
3. Provide a contact email address, if you want the team to reach out to you for clarification.
4. If applicable, reference a related project or ticket.
5. Enter a short summary of the note (Note subject).
6. Provide a detailed note or feedback in the note body.
7. Choose the priority of the note (Low, Medium, or High).
8. If you consent to be contacted about this note, please select "True".
9. Add any additional comments or information that you think is relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter details** (`submitter_details`, note, not required): Enter your details as the submitter, including your name and any relevant information.
* **Submitter name** (`submitter_name`, text, not required): If you want to be contacted about this note, enter your name.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Provide an optional contact email address for the team to reach out to you for clarification.
* **Related project or ticket** (`related_project`, text, not required): If applicable, reference a related project or ticket to help the team understand the context.
* **Note subject** (`note_subject`, text, required): Enter a short summary of the note.
* **Note body** (`note_body`, text, required): Provide a detailed note or feedback.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, not required): Choose the urgency of the note (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Attachment reference** (`attach_reference`, text, not required): If you have supporting files, provide a link to them.
* **Consent to contact** (`consent_to_contact`, select_one, not required): If you consent to be contacted about this note, select "True".
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Add any additional information or comments that you think are relevant.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide a clear and concise note subject.
* Use the priority field to help the team understand the urgency of the note.
* If you have additional comments or information, use the additional comments field to provide more details.
* Make sure to provide a contact email address if you want the team to reach out to you for clarification.
