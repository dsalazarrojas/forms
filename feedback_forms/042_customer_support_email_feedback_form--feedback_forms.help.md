# customer_support_email_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback and issues related to support. It is meant to gather specific information about the customer's experience with your product or service, including the type of feedback they want to provide, a summary of the problem they faced, and details about the resolution they received. This form is a crucial tool for understanding customer needs and improving support services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your name and email address in the designated fields.
*   Select the type of feedback you want to provide (Support, Sales, or Other).
*   Provide a clear and concise summary of the problem you faced.
*   Describe the details of the interaction with the support agent.
*   Rate your satisfaction with the resolution provided.
*   Optionally, provide additional comments about your experience.
*   Check if a follow-up is needed and provide the date when you would like it.
*   Finally, provide any additional information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name so we can address your feedback directly.
*   **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
*   **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of feedback you want to provide (Support, Sales, or Other).
*   **Problem Summary** (`problem_summary`, text, required): Describe the problem you faced in a concise and clear way.
*   **Attachment** (`attachment`, text, optional): If you have any supporting documentation or files, attach them here.
*   **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide additional context or information about the issue.
*   **Feedback Details** (`feedback_details`, text, required): Give details about the interaction and resolution of the issue.
*   **Expected Resolution** (`expected_resolution`, text, optional): Describe what you expected as a resolution for the problem.
*   **Received Resolution** (`received_resolution`, text, optional): Describe how your issue was resolved.
*   **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the resolution provided (1-5).
*   **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you want to provide.
*   **Support Agent** (`support_agent`, text, optional): If a support agent assisted you, enter their name or initials.
*   **Support Agent Contact Info** (`support_agent_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact information of the support agent (e.g., email or phone number).
*   **Customer Agent Interaction** (`customer_agent_interaction`, text, optional): Describe your interaction with the support agent.
*   **Customer Agent Response** (`customer_agent_response`, text, optional): Record the response from the customer agent.
*   **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`, select_one, optional): Check if you need a follow-up from us.
*   **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you would like the follow-up.
*   **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the follow-up.
*   **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter any customer-identifying information.
*   **Form Version** (`form_version`, text, optional): Enter the version of this form.
*   **Form Last Modified** (`form_last_modified`, text, optional): Enter the date and time this form was last modified.
*   **Form Last Updated** (`form_last_updated`, text, optional): Enter the date and time this form was last updated.
*   **Form Created** (`form_created`, text, optional): Enter the date and time this form was created.
