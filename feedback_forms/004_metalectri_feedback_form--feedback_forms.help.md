# Metalectri Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metalectri Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on various aspects of the platform. This form is intended for users who have experienced issues or have suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* Your name
	* Email (make sure to use a valid email address)
2. Answer the questions that best describe your feedback:
	* question1
	* question2
3. Provide a description of your issue or suggestion:
	* description
4. Attach any relevant files or documents to support your feedback (optional):
	* attachments
5. Select the categories that best fit your feedback:
	* category (choose one or more categories)
6. Choose your rating for the issue or suggestion:
	* rating (select one of the following: 5, 4, 3, 2, 1)
7. If applicable, choose whether or not the issue affects your work or productivity:
	* subcategory (Yes or No)
8. Add any additional comments or details:
	* comments
9. Finally, click the "submit_button" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name to help us associate your feedback with your identity.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you for follow-up questions or to provide updates on your feedback.
* **question1** (`question1`, `text`, optional): Answer this question to help us better understand the context of your feedback.
* **question2** (`question2`, `text`, optional): Answer this question to provide additional context or details about your feedback.
* **description** (`description`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the issue or suggestion you'd like to make.
* **attachments** (`attachments`, `text`, optional): Attach relevant files or documents to support your feedback.
* **category** (`category`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the categories that best fit your feedback. Choose one or more of the following:
	+ category1
	+ category2
	+ category3
* **subcategory** (`subcategory`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether or not the issue affects your work or productivity. Choose one of the following:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **tags** (`tags`, `text`, optional): Add any additional tags or keywords that describe your feedback.
* **rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your rating for the issue or suggestion. Select one of the following:
	+ 5
	+ 4
	+ 3
	+ 2
	+ 1
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Provide additional comments or details about your feedback.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of day or date when the issue occurred.
* **feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Add any additional feedback or comments.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter a phone number (optional, but if you're not comfortable providing an email address, you can provide a phone number instead).
* **submit_button** (`submit_button`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your feedback.
