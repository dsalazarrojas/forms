# post_test_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to provide feedback on a training session they have attended. The feedback will help us improve our training programs and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Be specific with your answers, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to provide a unique email address for each training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How would you rate this training session?** (`training_session_rating`, number, required: false): This question is to rate the overall quality of the training session. Please choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How useful is this training session for your job?** (`training_session_usefulness`, number, required: false): This question is to rate how useful you found the training session for your job. Please choose a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is not useful at all and 5 is very useful.
* **What did the trainer do well during the session?** (`trainer_performance`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is to provide feedback on what the trainer did well during the session. You can choose one or more options from the list.
* **What could be improved in this training session?** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, required: false): This question is to provide feedback on what could be improved in the training session.
* **What didn't work in this training session?** (`what_didnt_work`, text, required: false): This question is to provide feedback on what didn't work in the training session.
* **What are your next steps after this training session?** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): This question is to provide feedback on what you plan to do next after the training session.
* **How satisfied are you with this training session?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): This question is to rate your overall satisfaction with the training session. Please choose one option from the list.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required: false): This question is to collect your email address for future contact.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, required: false): This question is to collect your phone number for future contact.
* **Any other comments?** (`comments`, text, required: false): This question is to provide any additional feedback or comments about the training session.
* **What is the name of the trainer?** (`trainer_name`, text, required: false): This question is to provide the name of the trainer.
* **Date of training session** (`training_date`, date, required: false): This question is to record the date of the training session.
* **Start time of training session** (`training_start_time`, time, required: false): This question is to record the start time of the training session.
* **End time of training session** (`training_end_time`, time, required: false): This question is to record the end time of the training session.
* **Duration of training session** (`training_duration`, number, required: false): This question is to record the duration of the training session in hours.
* **Length of training session** (`training_session_length`, number, required: false): This question is to record the length of the training session in minutes.
* **How many participants were in the training session?** (`training_participants`, number, required: false): This question is to record the number of participants in the training session.
* **Location of training session** (`training_session_location`, text, required: false): This question is to record the location of the training session.
* **Materials used in the training session** (`training_materials`, text, required: false): This question is to record the materials used in the training session.
* **Format of the training session** (`training_session_format`, select_one, required: false): This question is to rate the format of the training session. Please choose one option from the list.
* **Type of the training session** (`training_session_type`, select_one, required: false): This question is to rate the type of the training session. Please choose one option from the list.
* **Topic of the training session** (`training_session_topic`, text, required: false): This question is to record the topic of the training session.
* **Objectives of the training session** (`training_session_objectives`, text, required: false): This question is to record the objectives of the training session.
* **Outcomes of the training session** (`training_session_outcomes`, text, required: false): This question is to record the outcomes of the training session.
* **How would you rate this trainer?** (`trainer_evaluation`, select_one, required: false): This question is to rate the quality of the trainer. Please choose one option from the list.
* **What is the name of the trainer?** (`trainer_name`, text, required: false): This question is to record the name of the trainer.
* **Training Session ID** (`training_session_id`, text, required: false): This question is to record the ID of the training session.
* **Date of training session** (`training_session_date`, text, required: false): This question is to record the date of the training session.
* **Session notes** (`session_notes`, text, required: false): This question is to record any additional notes about the training session.

Note: Please make sure to provide a unique email address for each training session.
