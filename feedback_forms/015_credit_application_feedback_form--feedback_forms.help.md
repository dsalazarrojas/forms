# Credit Application Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from end users about their credit application experience. Your input will help us understand what we do well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide effective feedback, please answer each question as accurately as you can. You can select multiple options for "Overall Experience" and "Application Outcome". If you have any additional comments or explanations for "Application Outcome", please provide a detailed description in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply to describe your overall experience with the credit application process.
* **Application Process** (`application_process`, text, optional): Describe any difficulties or challenges you faced during the application process.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience with communication from our team. Select "Yes" if you felt well-informed and "No" if you felt otherwise.
* **Application Outcome** (`application_outcome`, select_one, optional): Select the outcome of your credit application. If your application was not successful, please select "No" and provide a brief explanation in the "Application Outcome Other" field.
* **Application Outcome Other** (`application_outcome_other`, text, optional): If you selected "No" for "Application Outcome", provide a detailed explanation or specify the reason for the unsuccessful application.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experience or application outcome.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Application Process" field to provide more information.
* Your feedback will be kept confidential and will help us improve the credit application process for future users.
