# Streaming Support Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Support Feedback Form is a tool for users to provide feedback about their streaming experience. This form helps us understand the quality of our streaming service and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **username** in the **username** field. This is the name you use to log in to your account.
2. Enter your **Email** in the **Email** field. This will help us contact you if we need more information or need to follow up on your issue.
3. In the **feedback_message** field, type in a message describing your feedback. This can be about a positive or negative experience, or any suggestions for improvement.
4. Select one of the options in the **streaming_quality** field to indicate how you feel about the quality of our streaming service.
5. If you had a support interaction, select the **support_agent** field to indicate who helped you.
6. Enter the date of your support interaction in the **support_time** field.
7. If you have any additional comments or information, you can type them in the **additional_comments** field.
8. You can also add any additional comments in the **Streaming Support Feedback Form Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **username**: This is your username, the name you use to log in to your account.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you for further information or follow up on your issue.
* **feedback_message**: This is where you can describe your feedback about your streaming experience.
* **streaming_quality**: Select one of the following options to indicate how you feel about the quality of our streaming service:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **support_agent**: Select the support agent who helped you. 
* **support_time**: Enter the date and time of your support interaction.
* **additional_comments**: If you have any additional comments or information, type them here.
* **Streaming Support Feedback Form Additional Comments**: Any additional comments you have about our streaming service can be typed here.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.
* If you have a recurring issue or complaint, please submit one response for the entire issue.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in the **additional_comments** field.
* Please note that this form is for individual feedback, so please do not submit multiple responses for the same issue.
