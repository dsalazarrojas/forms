# Order Tracking Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their order tracking experience. It helps us understand how well we are tracking and fulfilling orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the information as requested. You will be asked to provide feedback on various aspects of your order tracking experience, including the efficiency of our fulfillment process, the helpfulness of our customer service, and your overall satisfaction with the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about your order tracking experience. Please feel free to write as much or as little as you like.

* **Order Experience** (`order_experience`, `note`, required: false): This field is a note where you can provide more detailed feedback about your order experience. You can write as much as you like, but please keep it concise and focused on your experience.

* **Fulfillment Process** (`fulfillment_process`, `select_one`, required: false): This is a dropdown menu where you can choose how efficient you think our fulfillment process was. Please choose one of the options: efficient, slow, or average.

* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This is a dropdown menu where you can choose which of the following words best describe our customer service: efficient, helpful, friendly, or responsive.

* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): This is a numerical field where you can rate your satisfaction with the order. Please enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

* **Communication** (`communication`, `date`, required: false): This is a date field where you can specify the date and time you last communicated with us about your order.

* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, `text`, required: false): This is a text field where you can specify how you would like to be contacted in the future, for example, email or phone.

* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This is an email field where you can enter your email address. Please enter your actual email address so we can contact you in the future.
