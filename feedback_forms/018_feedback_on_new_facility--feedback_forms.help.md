# Feedback On New Facility - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from users about their recent visit to a facility.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your **Feedback** about the facility in the "Feedback" field.
5. Rate the **Facility Rating** based on your experience.
6. Rate your **Satisfaction Rating** with the facility.
7. Enter the **Visit Date** of your visit to the facility.
8. Add any additional **Comments** about your visit, if needed.
9. Select the **Assigned Tool** used during your visit.
10. Enter the **Facility ID** of the facility you visited.
11. Choose the **Facility Type** you visited (e.g., Office, Restaurant, etc.).
12. Select the **Facility Size** from the options provided (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
13. Enter the number of **Visited Hours** you spent at the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official government-issued ID or as you prefer to be known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it is written on your official government-issued ID or as you prefer to be known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Please provide detailed feedback about your visit to the facility, including any positive or negative experiences.
* **Facility Rating** (`facility_rating`, number, required): Rate the facility based on your experience (e.g., 1-5).
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the facility (e.g., 1-5).
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit to the facility.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your visit.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Please select the tool assigned to you during your visit.
* **Facility ID** (`facility_id`, number, required): Enter the ID of the facility you visited.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of facility you visited (e.g., Office, Restaurant, etc.).
* **Facility Size** (`facility_size`, select_multiple, optional): Select the size of the facility (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
* **Visited Hours** (`visited_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of hours you spent at the facility.
