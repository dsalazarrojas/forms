# Universal Problem Solving Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is the Universal Problem Solving Survey, designed to gather feedback and problem-solving information from customers. This form helps us understand how you've experienced problems and how we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing your feedback question in the `Feedback Question` field. This field is not required, so you can skip it if you don't have any feedback.
*   If you have a problem, describe it in the `Problem Description` field. This will help us understand the nature of the problem.
*   For each problem you described, indicate whether you found any solutions or not by selecting the appropriate answer in the `Solutions` field. You can select one or multiple options.
*   Provide your rating score for the problem out of 100 in the `Rating Score` field. This is optional, but it will help us better understand your assessment of the problem.
*   Add any additional comments about your rating in the `Rating Comment` field.
*   If you want to provide any contact information, you can do so by entering your `Email` and/or `Phone` numbers. These fields are optional.
*   Finally, provide your `Name` so we can address your feedback by name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Feedback Question** (`feedback_question`, text, not required): This field allows you to provide any feedback you have about our services. Please feel free to type in anything that's on your mind.
*   **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, text, not required): Here, you can describe the problem you experienced in detail. This will help us understand the problem better.
*   **Solutions** (`solutions`, select multiple, not required): Select the solutions that you found helpful or not. You can select multiple options.
*   **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, number, not required): Provide your rating score out of 100 for the problem.
*   **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, not required): Add any additional comments about your rating.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you want to be contacted about your feedback, please provide your email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If you want to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Please provide your name so we can address your feedback by name.

## Tips

*   Please be as specific as possible when describing your problems and solutions.
*   If you have any attachments or additional information that can help us understand your feedback, please contact us separately.
*   We appreciate your time and feedback, and we will do our best to address it.
