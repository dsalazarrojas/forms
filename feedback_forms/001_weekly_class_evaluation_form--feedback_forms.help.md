# Weekly Class Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for students to provide feedback on their weekly class experience. It helps instructors gather information about the most interesting things learned, challenging aspects, and areas for improvement. The feedback will be used to enhance the course content and overall teaching experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Use the provided options for select fields, and feel free to add any additional information in text fields.
3. Make sure to submit the form before the week's end to provide timely feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Weekly Class Evaluation Form** (`form_1`, `note`, required/optional): This field is a heading for the form and does not require input.
* **What was the most interesting thing you learned this week?** (`form_2`, `text`, required/optional): Share something that caught your attention or impressed you this week.
* **What was the most challenging thing you encountered this week?** (`form_3`, `text`, required/optional): Describe an obstacle or difficulty you faced this week.
* **What did you find most useful this week?** (`form_4`, `text`, required/optional): Share something that helped you the most this week.
* **What did you find most frustrating this week?** (`form_5`, `text`, required/optional): Describe something that bothered or hindered your learning this week.
* **What would you like to see more of next week?** (`form_6`, `text`, required/optional): Suggest something you'd like to see or learn about next week.
* **How can we improve this course?** (`form_7`, `text`, required/optional): Provide suggestions for improving the course content or structure.
* **Weekly Class Evaluation Form Submission** (`form_8`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the ways you'd like to see this course improved.
* **How can we improve this course?** (`form_9`, `select_one`, required/true): Select one area where you'd like to see improvement.
* **What did you think of the course overall?** (`form_10`, `number`, required/optional): Rate your overall impression of the course on a scale of 1-5.
* **What time of week was it?** (`form_11`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time when you took the class.
* **What day of the week was it?** (`form_12`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the day of the week when you took the class.
* **Which day of the week was it?** (`form_13`, `number`, required/optional): Select the day of the week when you took the class.
* **How would you rate this course overall?** (`form_14`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the course as a whole on a scale of 1-3.
* **How would you rate this week's class?** (`form_15`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate this week's class on a scale of 1-3.
* **How would you rate the instructor?** (`form_16`, `select_one`, required/true): Rate the instructor's performance on a scale of 1-3.
* **What did you hear from your peers that was interesting?** (`form_17`, `text`, required/optional): Share something a peer shared that caught your attention.
* **What did you hear from your peers that was challenging?** (`form_18`, `text`, required/optional): Describe something a peer shared that was difficult to understand or work with.
* **How did you feel about the pace of the class?** (`form_19`, `text`, required/optional): Share your feelings about the class's pace.
* **How did you feel about the difficulty level of the class?** (`form_20`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your impression of the class's difficulty level.
* **How did you feel about the course material?** (`form_21`, `text`, required/optional): Share your feelings about the course content.
* **What was the most useful thing you used this week?** (`form_22`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the most helpful resource or tool you used.
* **What was the most challenging thing you used this week?** (`form_23`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the most difficult task or resource you encountered.
* **Did you use the textbook?** (`form_24`, `select_one`, required/true): Indicate whether you used the textbook.
* **Did you use the online resources?** (`form_25`, `select_one`, required/true): Indicate whether you used the online resources.
