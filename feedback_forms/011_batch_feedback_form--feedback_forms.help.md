# Batch Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from individuals who have completed a batch or session, to gather information about their experience and satisfaction with the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (1, text): Provide your name for follow-up purposes.
* **Email Address** (2, email): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Batch or Session Identifier** (3, text, *Required*): This is a unique identifier for the batch or session you're providing feedback on. Please ensure you enter the correct identifier to ensure your feedback is associated with the correct batch.
* **Batch Type** (4, select_one, *Required*): Select the type of batch or session you're providing feedback on (e.g. Training Session, Production Batch, etc.).
* **Completion Date** (5, date, *Required*): Enter the date you completed the batch or session.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (6, select_one, *Required*): Rate your overall satisfaction with the batch or session (e.g. Very Satisfied, Satisfied, etc.).
* **Quality Rating** (7, select_one, *Required*): Rate the quality of the batch or session (e.g. Excellent, Good, etc.).
* **Did This Meet Your Expectations** (8, select_one, *Required*): Indicate whether the batch or session met your expectations (Yes, No, etc.).
* **What Worked Well** (9, text, *Required*): Describe any positive aspects of the batch or session.
* **What Needs Improvement** (10, text, *Required*): Identify any areas that need improvement.
* **Consistency Rating** (11, select_one): Optional) Rate the consistency of the batch or session compared to others (e.g. Very Consistent, Somewhat Inconsistent, etc.).
* **Would You Recommend This** (12, select_one, *Required*): Indicate whether you would recommend this batch or session to others (e.g. Definitely Yes, Not Sure, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (13, text): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
