# Event Design Revision Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and revisions for event designs. It's intended for event organizers, planners, or designers to provide input on specific aspects of event design, such as layout, content, and overall aesthetic. The feedback collected will help refine and improve event designs for better user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
3. For each page, answer the questions and provide feedback in the text fields.
4. Scroll through the pages to review and complete each section.
5. Once you've completed all sections, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.
6. Submit the form to send your feedback and revisions to the relevant party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: This page is the starting point for your feedback. Please enter your name and email address in the respective fields to ensure we can get back to you with updates and responses.
* **Page 2-20**: Each of these pages is dedicated to collecting feedback on specific aspects of event design. Please use the text fields to provide detailed feedback on layout, content, and overall aesthetic.
* **Page 21**: This page is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have. Feel free to share anything that might be relevant to the event design.
* **Page 23**: This page is for any attachments or images you'd like to include with your feedback. You can upload relevant files or links to help support your points.

Note: Since all pages are identical in structure, you can expect similar explanations for each one. Focus on providing specific feedback for each page to help us refine the event design accurately.
