# Wild Bloom Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with Wild Bloom. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First name** and **Last name** in the first two fields. These are the only fields required for you to provide your feedback.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the third field. This will help us contact you if we need to discuss your feedback further.
3. In the **Feedback** field, describe your experience with Wild Bloom. You can share any positive or negative comments you have.
4. If you feel that your feedback is not sufficient, you can rate your experience on a scale of 1-5 in the **Rating** field.
5. You can also provide any additional **Comments** you may have about your experience with Wild Bloom.
6. Finally, if you have any suggestions or ideas about how we can improve, you can add them in the **Contact us** field. This is not required, but it's a great opportunity for us to learn from you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Your first name is requested so that we can address you personally in our correspondence.
* **Last name** (text, required): Your last name is requested so that we can address you personally in our correspondence.
* **Email** (email, required): Your email address is requested so that we can contact you if we need to discuss your feedback further.
* **Feedback** (text, required): This field is where you can share any comments or suggestions you have about your experience with Wild Bloom.
* **Rating** (number, required): This field allows you to rate your experience with Wild Bloom on a scale of 1-5.
* **Comments** (text, optional): You can add any additional comments or suggestions here. This field is not required, but it's a great opportunity for us to learn from you.
* **Contact us** (note, optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas about how we can improve, you can add them here. This field is not required, but it's a great opportunity for us to learn from you.
