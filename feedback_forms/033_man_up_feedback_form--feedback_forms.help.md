# Man Up Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Man Up Feedback Form is a tool for users to provide feedback on events. This form helps us understand your experiences and gather valuable insights for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the event details (event name, date, and time) in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide the parent name and comments about the event if applicable.
3. Enter your email address for us to contact you for follow-up questions.
4. Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case we need to discuss your feedback.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter a brief description of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): Enter the name of the event parent (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the event.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name or username of the person submitting the feedback (optional). 

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure your feedback is complete and useful.
* Double-check your email address for correctness.
* Use clear and concise language in the comments section.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
