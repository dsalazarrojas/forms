# gameplay_strategy_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gameplay strategy feedback form is designed to collect feedback from users about their gameplay experience, focusing on strategy, playstyle, and performance. This feedback is essential in helping developers refine the game design and create a better experience for users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Start by reviewing the available fields and selecting the options that best match your experience.
2.  Answer each question accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible in the text field.
3.  Use the additional comments field to provide any further thoughts or suggestions not covered by the previous questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (note): This field is a general introduction to your experience. You can describe your experience with the game in a few sentences, including any notable events or moments.
*   **Playstyle** (optional, select multiple): Choose the types of playstyles that you experienced during your gameplay, such as Casual, Competitive, Recreational, or Social.
*   **Game Mode** (optional, select multiple): Select the game mode(s) you experienced, which could be Single Player, Multiplayer, Co-op, or PvP.
*   **Performance** (optional, number): Rate the performance of the game, on a scale that seems most appropriate for this context.
*   **Minimalist** (optional, select one): Choose the level of complexity you felt the game had, Less, Medium, or Full.
*   **Playstyle Performance** (optional, select multiple): Rate the performance of the game in terms of playstyle, which could be High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Game Mode Performance** (optional, select multiple): Rate the performance of the game mode in terms of performance, which could be High, Medium, or Low.
*   **Additional Comments** (optional, text): Provide any further thoughts or suggestions about your experience with the game.

**Tips**

*   Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
*   Use clear and concise language in the text fields.
*   Proofread your answers for accuracy and completeness.
