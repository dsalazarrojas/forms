# Town Hall Meeting Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants of the Town Hall meeting. It will help us understand what you thought about the meeting and how we can improve it for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your participant details, including your name and email address.
3. Answer the feedback question and rate the meeting (if you choose to do so).
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
5. Enter any other participant details (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Details
* **Meeting Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the meeting you attended in a few words, including the date and location.
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your work email address.
### What do you think about this meeting?
* **What do you think about this meeting?** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Share your thoughts about the meeting, including what you liked and disliked.
### Rating
* **Rating** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Rate the meeting on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
### Suggestion
* **Suggestion** (`6`, `text`, required: false): If you have any suggestions for future improvements, please enter them here.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
### Participant Phone
* **Participant Phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
### Participant Organization
* **Participant Organization** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company or organization name.
### Participant Title
* **Participant Title** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title or position.

Tips:

* You can fill out this form as many times as you like, but please only do so if you attended the meeting.
* We will use the data collected from this form to improve our meetings and make sure everyone has a great experience.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to reach out.
