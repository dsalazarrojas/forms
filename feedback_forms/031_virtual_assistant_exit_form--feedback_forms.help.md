# Virtual Assistant Exit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users who are exiting the virtual assistant service. It will help us understand why users are leaving the service and what their experience was like while using it, which will enable us to improve the service and provide better support in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields below and fill out the required information.
2. Answer the questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Virtual Assistant Exit Form** (`Virtual Assistant Exit Form`, `note`, required: false): This is the title of the form. Please fill it out with your name and description of your virtual assistant experience.
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): If you are using the service on behalf of an organization or company, please enter the name of the client or company.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can enter any additional contact information for the service provider.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact number where the service provider can reach out to you.
- **Service Details** (`service_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the service you were using and any specific details about your experience.
- **Service Type** (`service_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select whether this was a test service or a production service.
  - 'Yes' for test service
  - 'No' for production service
- **Service Provider** (`service_provider`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the service provider for this experience.
  - 'Yes' for in-house
  - 'No' for out-house
- **Service Agreement** (`service_agreement`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the service agreement you were using for this experience.
  - 'Yes' for agreed on
  - 'No' for not agreed
- **Exit Reason** (`exit_reason`, `text`, required: false): Describe why you are exiting the virtual assistant service.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): If there's any other information you'd like to share with us, please do so here.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible in your answers.
- If you are not sure about any of the fields, just leave them blank.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to include them in the "Additional Information" field.
