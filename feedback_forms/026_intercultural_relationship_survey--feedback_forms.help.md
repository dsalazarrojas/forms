# Intercultural Relationship Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intercultural Relationship Survey is a tool to gather information about your experiences and interactions with people from diverse cultural backgrounds. Your input will help us better understand what works well and what could be improved in intercultural relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page to understand the different sections and questions.
2.  Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Make sure to complete all required fields. If a field is marked as required, it's essential to fill it in to ensure we can process your response accurately.
4.  Use the provided options for select fields. If you don't see an option that fits your answer, let us know in the open-ended question section.
5.  If you have any additional comments or information not covered by the questions, use the open-ended question section to share them with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (<type> text, <required> false): A brief introduction page where you can provide any context or background information about your interaction or relationship.
* **Demographics** (<type> text, <required> false): This is a text field where you can share basic demographic information about yourself, such as your age, location, or occupation. Please be as specific or general as you feel comfortable with.
* **Relationship Type** (<type> select_one, <required> false): Choose the type of relationship you are in or had with the person from a different cultural background. You can select one of the following:
	+ Cultural Exchange
	+ Business Partner
	+ Family Member
	+ Friend
	+ Romantic Partner
* **Cultural Exchange** (<type> select_multiple, <required> false): If you selected Relationship Type as Cultural Exchange, this field will be visible for you to select multiple areas where you have had cultural exchange:
	+ Communication
	+ Education
	+ Work
	+ Family
	+ Travel
	+ Food
	+ Sport
	+ Leisure
* **Relationship Satisfaction** (<type> number, <required> false): Rate your relationship satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Satisfaction Scale** (<type> select_one, <required> false): Select the level of satisfaction you have with your current relationship, using the following options:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5
* **Open-Ended Question** (<type> date, <required> false): Share any additional thoughts or information not covered by the previous questions.
* **Email** (<type> email, <required> false): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on our progress and findings.
