# Patient Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Discharge Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from patients about their discharge experience at a healthcare facility. The form aims to gather information on the quality of care provided, the approachability of the care team, and overall satisfaction with the discharge process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select a rating**: On page 1 of the form, select one of the three options for "How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the care provided?" This will give us an idea of your overall experience.
2. **Check off applicable answers**: On pages 2-5, select all the answers that apply to your experience. For example, if you found the care team approachable and friendly, check the box next to "Was the care team approachable and friendly?".
3. **Add any additional feedback**: On page 9, you can provide any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about your experience.
4. **Enter your name**: On page 10, please enter your name to help us identify you as the person providing feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback on Discharge** (Required): Please describe your overall experience with the discharge process in a few sentences.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the care provided?**: Select one of the three options to rate your overall satisfaction with the care provided.
* **Was the care team approachable and friendly?**: Check the box if you found the care team approachable and friendly.
* **Was the care team responsive to your questions?**: Check the box if you found the care team responsive to your questions.
* **Was the environment clean and safe?**: Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Was the care team knowledgeable?**: Select all the options that apply to your experience.
* **Were all your questions answered?**: Check the box if you found all your questions answered.
* **Was the discharge process smooth?**: Check the box if you found the discharge process smooth.
* **Do you have any additional feedback or comments?**: Use this field to provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Patient Name**: Please enter your name to help us identify you as the person providing feedback.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to help us improve our services. Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in completing this form.
