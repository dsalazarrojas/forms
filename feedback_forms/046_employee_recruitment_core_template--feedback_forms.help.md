The form purpose is to collect information about an employee recruitment process. This template is designed to gather essential details about a job posting, including job title, job requirements, contact information, and job posting details. It is intended to be used in a professional setting, such as a company or organization, to streamline the recruitment process.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the required fields, which are marked as "required: true" in the form.
2. Enter the "What is the job title" field with the official job title.
3. In the "Job Requirements" field, select "Yes" if the job requires specific skills or qualifications, and "No" if it does not.
4. In the "Job Requirements 2" field, select multiple relevant qualifications or skills required for the job.
5. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Fill in the "Contact Phone" field with the primary phone number to contact for the job posting.
7. Select the job type and job level from the respective dropdown menus.
8. In the "Job Posted By" field, select "Yes" if the job is posted by a team member, and "No" if it is posted by a manager or executive.
9. Select the job status as "Active" or "Inactive" based on the current status.
10. In the "Job Category" field, select multiple categories that apply to the job posting.
11. Enter the job position in the "Job Position" field.
12. Fill in the job description in the "Job Description" field.
13. Review and adjust as needed the other fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the job title**: Enter the official job title of the position being posted.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job posting, which will be used to identify the job in the system.
* **Job Requirements**: Select "Yes" if the job requires specific skills or qualifications, and "No" if it does not.
* **Job Requirements 2**: Select multiple relevant qualifications or skills required for the job.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the job posting.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person for the job posting.
* **Job Posted At**: Enter the date the job was posted, if applicable.
* **Job Posted Time**: Enter the time the job was posted, if applicable.
* **Job Type**: Select the type of job being posted.
* **Job Level**: Select the level of the job being posted.
* **Job Posted By**: Select "Yes" if the job is posted by a team member, and "No" if it is posted by a manager or executive.
* **Job Status**: Select the current status of the job posting.
* **Job Category**: Select multiple categories that apply to the job posting.
* **Job Position**: Enter the job position being posted.
* **Job Description**: Enter a detailed description of the job posting.
* **Job Description 2**: Enter a brief description of the job posting.
* **Job Description 3**: Enter another description of the job posting.
* **Job Description 4**: Enter yet another description of the job posting.
* **Job Description 5**: Enter the final description of the job posting.

Note that some fields are not required, and you can adjust them as needed. However, it's essential to ensure that all the required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
