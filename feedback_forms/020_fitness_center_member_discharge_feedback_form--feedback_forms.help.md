# Fitness Center Member Discharge Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Center Member Discharge Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from members when they are discharged or terminated from a fitness center. This form helps the staff to document the reason, method, and type of discharge, as well as gather information about the member's details and interactions with the staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permission to access the member's information and that you are not duplicating an existing feedback record.
2. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information accurately and completely.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Discharge Reason** (`discharge_reason`, text, required): Please enter the reason why the member is being discharged or terminated from the fitness center. This will help the staff understand the circumstances surrounding the member's discharge.
- **Discharge Cause** (`discharge_cause`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the cause of discharge, such as non-payment, behavioral issues, or other relevant details.
- **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): Select the date on which the member was discharged or terminated from the fitness center.
- **Member ID** (`member_id`, number, required): Enter the member's identification number or ID, as assigned by the fitness center.
- **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, required): This is the same as the "Discharge Date" field above, please select the date on which the member was discharged or terminated from the fitness center.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or comments about the member's discharge, you can enter them here.
- **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member who is responsible for discharging the member.
- **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the staff member who is responsible for discharging the member.
- **Discharge Status** (`discharge_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the discharge, either "Active" or "Inactive".
- **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the staff member who is responsible for discharging the member.
- **Gym Name** (`gym_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the fitness center where the member was discharged.
- **Gym Address** (`gym_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the fitness center where the member was discharged.
- **State** (`gym_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the fitness center is located.
- **City** (`gym_city`, text, required): Enter the city where the fitness center is located.
- **Zip** (`gym_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the fitness center.
- **Gym Phone** (`gym_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the fitness center.
- **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the member being discharged.
- **Member Email** (`member_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the member being discharged.
- **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select how often the member would like to receive feedback from the fitness center.
- **Discharge Method** (`discharge_method`, select_one, required): Select the method by which the member was discharged, either "Online Form", "Phone", or "In Person".
- **Discharge Type** (`discharge_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of discharge, either "Yes" or "No".
- **Member Type** (`member_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of member, either "Yes" or "No".
- **Member Category** (`member_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of member, either "Yes" or "No".
- **Staff Category** (`staff_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of staff, either "Yes" or "No".
- **Gym Category** (`gym_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of gym, either "Yes" or "No".
