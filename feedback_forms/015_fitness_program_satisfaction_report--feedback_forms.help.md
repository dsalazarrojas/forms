# Fitness Program Satisfaction Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from participants about their experience in the fitness program. Your responses will help us understand how we can improve the program to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Fill out the form with honest and detailed information about your experience in the fitness program.
3. Be as specific as possible when describing your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Info
* **Participant Info 1** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, optional): 
    This field is intended for you to provide any relevant background information about yourself.
* **Participant Info 2** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, optional): 
    This field is another opportunity to add any additional relevant details about your background or experience.
* **Participant Info 3** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Use this field to provide more information about your background or experience.
* ...
* **Participant Info 12** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, optional): 
    This field is the last chance to add any final details about your background or experience.

### Program Info
* **Program Info** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Describe your experience with the fitness program in a few sentences.

### Session Details
* **Session Details** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Share specifics about your sessions, such as dates, times, and locations.
* **Session Notes** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Note any important points or observations from your sessions.

### Session Satisfaction
* **Session Satisfaction** (`id: 5`, `type: select_one`, optional): 
    Choose one of the options to describe your overall satisfaction with the sessions: Very Good, Good, Average, Bad, or Terrible.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, optional): 
    Select your overall satisfaction with the program: Very Good, Good, Average, Bad, or Terrible.

### Additional Comments
* **Recommendations** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Share any suggestions or recommendations for future improvements.
* **Suggestions** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Add any additional ideas or suggestions for the program.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Provide any further comments or feedback.

### Photo Credits
* **Participant Photo Credit** (`id: 10`, `type: select_multiple`, optional): 
    If you have taken photos during the program, you can give credit to other participants in this field. Choose one or more options.
* **Trainer Photo Credit** (`id: 11`, `type: select_multiple`, optional): 
    Select if you want to credit the trainer(s) for the photos you have taken.
* **Staff Photo Credit** (`id: 12`, `type: select_multiple`, optional): 
    Choose if you want to credit the staff for the photos you have taken.
* **Additional Participants** (`id: 13`, `type: text`, optional): 
    List any additional participants involved in the photo.
* **Participant Info 1** (`id: 14`, `type: text`, optional): 
    Use this field to add any details about another participant.
* ...
* **Participant Info 10** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, optional): 
    This field is intended for additional details about another participant.

## Tips
* Please be honest and specific in your responses.
* If you have any photos, do not forget to give credit to the participants, trainers, or staff involved.
* Use the "Additional Comments" field to share any final thoughts or suggestions.
