# User Experience (UX) Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user feedback and experience from end-users, helping us understand and improve the overall User Experience (UX) of our application or system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your contact information (fields 2, 3, and 4).
2. Provide any additional contact information you feel comfortable sharing.
3. Rate your experience (field 6).
4. Select the option that best describes your current situation (field 8).
5. Choose as many relevant options as applicable for field 9.
6. Enter a specific date if applicable (field 10).
7. Enter a specific time if applicable (field 11).
8. Add any additional notes or thoughts about your experience (field 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (`contact_info`, `email_address`, `phone_number`): Enter your full contact information, including an email address and a phone number, so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **User Feedback (`user_feedback`):** Describe your overall experience with our application or system. Be as specific as possible about your needs and pain points.
* **Rating (`rating`):** Rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Select One (`select_one`):** Choose the option that best describes your current situation. This helps us understand your context and tailor our improvements to your needs.
* **Select Multiple (`select_multiple`):** Choose as many options as apply to your current situation. This helps us understand your specific needs and areas for improvement.
* **Date (`date_input`):** Enter a specific date if you have one related to your experience.
* **Time (`time_input`):** Enter a specific time if you have one related to your experience.
* **Note (`note_input`):** Add any additional notes or thoughts about your experience. This is your opportunity to share any details we might have missed or that you feel are important to mention.
