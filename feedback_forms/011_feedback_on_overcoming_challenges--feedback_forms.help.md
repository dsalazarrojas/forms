## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Reflective Story
* **Reflective Story** (`reflective_story`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the challenge you have overcome, including any relevant context or background information. This will help us understand the specific situation you faced.

### Strategies
* **Strategies** (`strategies`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the strategies or programs that helped you overcome the challenge. These might include internal or external programs, training, or support.

### Advice Given
* **Advice Given** (`advice_given`, text, optional): Provide a list of advice or guidance you received that helped you in overcoming the challenge.

### Impact
* **Impact** (`impact`, number, optional): Please estimate the impact of the challenge on your work or life, on a scale of 0-10, where 0 means no impact and 10 means a significant impact.

### Programs Improved
* **Programs Improved** (`programs_improved`, select_one, optional): If you have improved or changed any programs as a result of overcoming the challenge, please select one of the options.

### Difficulty
* **Difficulty** (`difficulty`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the difficulties or obstacles you faced while trying to overcome the challenge.

### Advice Needed
* **Advice Needed** (`advice_needed`, number, optional): If you still need advice or guidance on how to improve or overcome the challenge, please provide a number between 1-10, where 1 means "I don't need any advice" and 10 means "I need urgent advice".
