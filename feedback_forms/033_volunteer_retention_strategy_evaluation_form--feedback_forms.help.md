# Volunteer Retention Strategy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Retention Strategy Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from volunteers about their experiences and suggestions for improving the volunteer program. This form will help us understand what aspects of the program are working well and where we can make improvements to better support our volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the relevant information for each question.
3. Make sure to provide specific examples or examples to support your answers where possible.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure they accurately reflect your thoughts and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is retention** (`what_is_retention`, text, optional): This field asks you to describe what retention means to you in the context of our volunteer program. Please provide your understanding or definition of retention.
* **Importance of retention** (`importance_of_retention`, text, optional): This field asks you to explain why retention is important to you or our volunteer program. Please provide your reasons or motivations for why retention is important.
* **How to improve retention** (`how_to_improve_retention`, text, optional): This field asks you to suggest ways to improve retention in our volunteer program. Please think critically and provide specific ideas or strategies you think would be helpful.
* **Feedback on retention** (`feedback_on_retention`, note, optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you may have about our volunteer program's retention strategy. Please use this space to share your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns.
