# Thrombosis Seminar Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on the quality of the Thrombosis Seminar. Your input will help us improve future seminars and ensure that they meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page according to your true feelings and experiences about the seminar.
2. Be as specific as possible in your answers, providing details that will help us understand your perspective.
3. If you have any suggestions for future seminars, feel free to include them in the "Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How was the seminar?** (`How was the seminar?`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the answer that best describes your experience with the seminar.
* **Who was the speaker?** (`Who was the speaker?`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the speaker.
* **What was the content of the seminar?** (`What was the content of the seminar?`, text, optional):
	+ Describe the topics covered during the seminar.
* **How relevant was the seminar to your work?** (`How relevant was the seminar to your work?`, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the answer that best describes how relevant the seminar was to your work.
* **How would you rate the seminar?** (`How would you rate the seminar?`, number, optional):
	+ Enter a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Do you have any suggestions for future seminars?** (`Do you have any suggestions for future seminars?`, text, optional):
	+ Share any ideas you have for future seminars.
