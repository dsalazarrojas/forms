# Dredging Project Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dredging Project Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on a dredging project, specifically in three categories: Dredging Operations, Environmental Impact, and Project Management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **project_title** field with a brief description of the project you are providing feedback on.
2. Choose the categories that best fit your feedback by selecting multiple options in the **category** field.
3. In the **comment** field, provide your thoughts and opinions on the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_title** (text, required): Please provide a brief description of the project you are providing feedback on.
* **category** (select multiple, optional): Select all the categories that best fit your feedback:
	+ Dredging Operations
	+ Environmental Impact
	+ Project Management
* **comment** (text, optional): Provide your thoughts and opinions on the project. This is a good place to elaborate on your feedback and provide any additional information that might be helpful.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all questions and provide clear and concise responses to ensure that your feedback is valuable and accurate.
* If you have any specific concerns or questions about the project, feel free to include them in the **comment** field.
* If you have any additional feedback that doesn't fit in any of the provided categories, you can include it in the **comment** field as well.
