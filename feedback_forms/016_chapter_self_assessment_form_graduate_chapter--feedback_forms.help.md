# Chapter Self Assessment Form Graduate Chapter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to conduct an annual self-assessment for graduate chapters to evaluate their performance and governance structures. It helps chapter administrators or leaders understand how the chapter is functioning and identifies areas that need improvement or attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready, including the chapter's name, assessment period, and assessor's details.
2. Fill in the chapter name in the "chapter name" field.
3. Select the appropriate assessment period from the "assessment period" field.
4. Rate the chapter's governance rating, membership engagement, and community impact using the respective drop-down menus.
5. If applicable, list the primary challenges faced by the chapter in the "challenges identified" field.
6. Outline any improvement plans to address these challenges in the "improvement plan" field.
7. Enter your name and title as the person completing the assessment in the "assessor name" field.
8. Select the date the assessment was completed using the calendar or date picker.
9. Submit the form once you've filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **chapter name** (`chapter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your graduate chapter.
* **assessment period** (`assessment_period`, text, required): Select the timeframe for this self-assessment. This will help in understanding the context and relevance of the assessment.
* **governance rating** (`governance_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the chapter's governance structures, choosing from "Needs improvement," "Adequate," or "Strong."
* **membership engagement** (`membership_engagement`, select_one, required): Rate member involvement, choosing from "Low," "Moderate," or "High."
* **community impact** (`community_impact`, select_one, required): Rate the chapter's community initiatives, choosing from "Low," "Moderate," or "High."
* **challenges identified** (`challenges_identified`, text, optional): If you have identified any primary challenges, list them here for future reference and action.
* **improvement plan** (`improvement_plan`, text, optional): Outline actions you plan to take to address the challenges you've identified.
* **assessor name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter your name and title as the person completing this assessment.
* **submission date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Select the date the assessment was completed using the calendar or date picker.

## Tips

- Ensure you provide as much detail as possible in the "challenges identified" and "improvement plan" fields to help the chapter understand areas for improvement.
- Always submit the form once you've filled in all required fields to ensure the assessment is complete and accurate.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
- The chapter can use the information collected in this form to adjust their strategies, improve governance, and enhance member engagement and community impact.
