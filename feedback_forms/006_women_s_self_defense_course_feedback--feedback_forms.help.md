# women_s_self_defense_course_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from students who have completed the women's self-defense course. It helps us understand their perception of the course's quality, instructor performance, and overall satisfaction. Please fill out this form to provide constructive feedback that will help us improve the course and provide better services to future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Course Feedback" field and enter your thoughts about the course.
2. Select your rating for the instructor from the options provided.
3. Choose your rating for the course from the list of options.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them in the "Comments" field.
5. Rate your overall satisfaction with the course on a scale from 1 to 5.
6. Make sure to submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Feedback (Course Feedback)** (`text`, required): Please provide a brief description of your thoughts about the course, its content, and its structure. 
* **Instructor Rating (Instructor Rating)** (`select_one`, required): This question is used to rate your perception of the instructor's performance. Please select one of the options: "Excellent", "Good", "Average", "Poor", or "No Response".
* **Course Rating (Course Rating)** (`select_multiple`, required): This question allows you to rate your experience with the course's content, structure, and overall quality. Please select all the options that apply to you: "Good", "Fair".
* **Self-Defense Skills (Self-Defense Skills)** (`number`, required): On a scale from 1-5, please rate your level of satisfaction with the self-defense skills learned during the course.
* **Overall Satisfaction (Overall Satisfaction)** (`number`, required): On a scale from 1-5, please rate your overall satisfaction with the course.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`text`, optional): This field is optional, and you can use it to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the course. 

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Do not include any identifying information or personal attacks.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve the course.
