# Charity Event Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Charity Event Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather your thoughts, opinions, and experiences about a recent charity event. Your feedback will help us improve the event and provide a better experience for future attendees. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form, and let us know how we can serve you better.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, read through the form carefully and make sure you understand what each section is asking for.
2. Fill out the form with your honest and accurate feedback, and make sure to provide specific examples or details where possible.
3. For fields with dropdown menus, choose the most suitable option that best describes your experience.
4. If you have additional comments or suggestions, use the "Suggestions for Improvement" or "Additional Comments" fields to let us know.
5. Review your answers carefully and make sure they accurately reflect your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name to provide a personal touch to your feedback.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address so we can follow up with you (if necessary) and keep you informed about our future events.
* **Which Event Did You Attend** (`event_attended`, `text`, required): Describe the charity event you attended, including the event name (if provided).
* **Date of Attendance** (`attendance_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you attended the event.
* **Is this your first time attending?** (`first_time_attendee`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes first time: This is your first time attending the event.
	+ No attended before: You have attended the event before.
	+ Regular attendee: You attend this event regularly.
* **How did you hear about this event?** (`hear_about_event`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Social media
	+ Email newsletter
	+ Website
	+ Friend or family
	+ Print media
	+ Radio or TV
	+ Other (please specify)
* **How was the registration experience?** (`registration_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Very easy
	+ Easy
	+ Neutral
	+ Difficult
	+ Very difficult
* **How was the check-in experience?** (`check_in_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Very smooth
	+ Smooth
	+ Neutral
	+ Problematic
	+ Very problematic
* **How friendly was the staff?** (`staff_friendliness`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Very friendly
	+ Friendly
	+ Neutral
	+ Unfriendly
	+ Very unfriendly
* **How important is the cause to you?** (`cause_importance`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Very important
	+ Important
	+ Somewhat important
	+ Not very important
	+ Not important
* **How effective was the fundraising?** (`fundraising_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Very effective
	+ Effective
	+ Neutral
	+ Ineffective
	+ Very ineffective
* **How likely to donate in the future?** (`donation_likelihood`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Very likely
	+ Likely
	+ Neutral
	+ Unlikely
	+ Very unlikely
* **Interested in volunteering?** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Yes definitely
	+ Maybe
	+ False
* **Overall Event Rating** (`event_rating`, `select_one`, required): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 stars:
	+ 5 stars - Excellent
	+ 4 stars - Good
	+ 3 stars - Average
	+ 2 stars - Poor
	+ 1 star - Very poor
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Share any ideas or suggestions you have for improving future events.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here.
