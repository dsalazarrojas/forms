# room_spray_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our room spray products. We use this feedback to identify areas of improvement and provide better service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Customer Name` field.
2. Select one of the available `Scent` options that best describes your experience with our product.
3. Rate the `Performance` of our room spray product on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
4. Provide a brief comment about your experience in the `Comments` field.
5. Enter your `Email` address to allow us to contact you for further questions or concerns.
6. Enter your `Phone` number in case we need to reach you directly.
7. If applicable, specify which `Assigned Tool` was used during your experience.
8. You may also provide a brief description of the `Output File` generated by our product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of the product you are experiencing.
* **Scent** (`scent`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of our available scents that best describes your experience.
* **Performance** (`performance_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the performance of our room spray product on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Performance Explanation**: This field is not explicitly explained in the YAML, so we're assuming it's for the user to add an explanation for their performance rating.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Provide a brief comment about your experience with our room spray product.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you directly.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If you have an assigned tool (e.g., a technician), please specify it here.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, optional): You may provide a brief description of the output generated by our product.
