# Training Effectiveness Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from training participants about the effectiveness of the training program they attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Training Program Title** field with the title of the training program you attended.
2. Provide a brief description of the training program in the **Training Program Description** field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the training program in the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields, respectively.
4. Select one of the three options in the **Training Effectiveness** field to indicate how effective you found the training program.
5. If you have any additional comments about the training program, you can enter them in the **Comments** field.
6. Finally, rate the training program in the **Training Rating** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Program Title** (`training_program_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the training program you attended.
* **Training Program Description** (`training_program_description`, text, required): Briefly describe the training program, including its objectives and main topics covered.
* **Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the training program started.
* **End Date** (`training_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the training program ended.
* **Training Effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required): Select one of the three options to indicate how effective you found the training program: "Training was effective", "Training was somewhat effective", or "Training was not effective".
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments about the training program, enter them here.
* **Training Rating** (`training_rating`, number, required): Rate the training program by entering a number between 1 and 5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
