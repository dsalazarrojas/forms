# Post Program Feedback Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants after a program has concluded. It helps us understand their overall experience, ratings, and suggestions for improvement. Please complete this form to provide us with valuable insights to make future programs even better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields provided. Each field has a label with a brief description of what it's meant to collect. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional: optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed in our correspondence.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional: optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required/optional: optional): Rate your overall experience with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional: optional): Share any comments or feedback you have about the program.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `number`, required/optional: optional): Rate your overall experience with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional: optional): Rate your level of satisfaction with a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Improvement Area** (`improvement_area`, `text`, required/optional: optional): Suggest areas for improvement for future programs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required/optional: optional): Leave any additional comments or feedback about the program.

## Tips
Please provide honest feedback, and remember that all responses are kept confidential.
