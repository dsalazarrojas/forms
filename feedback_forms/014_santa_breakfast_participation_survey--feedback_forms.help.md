# Santa Breakfast Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool to collect feedback and participation information from attendees of Santa's Breakfast events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the event_name field with the name of the event you participated in.
2. Rate your satisfaction with the event by filling out the rating field.
3. Leave your comments in the comments field to give us detailed feedback on your experience.
4. If you have any questions or want to be contacted for further discussion, fill out the email field.
5. If you have a phone number that you'd like to be contacted at, fill out the phone_number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): This field is where you should enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Satisfaction Rating** (`rating`, number, required): This is where you rate your satisfaction with the event. Please select a number between 1 and 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Write down any comments or thoughts you have about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to be contacted about your feedback, enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone about your feedback, enter your phone number here.

## Tips
- Make sure to be as specific and detailed as possible when leaving comments.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact your event organizer.
- One survey response per person is all that's needed, so don't worry if you're not tech-savvy or if you've already filled this out.
