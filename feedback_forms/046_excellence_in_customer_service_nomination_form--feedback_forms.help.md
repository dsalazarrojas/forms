# excellence_in_customer_service_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Excellence in Customer Service Nomination Form is a tool for recognizing outstanding service representatives within an organization. This form allows users to easily nominate and acknowledge exceptional service provided by colleagues, promoting a culture of service excellence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the nomination in the "Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the reasons for the nomination in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the first name of the person being nominated.
4. Enter the last name of the person being nominated.
5. Enter the email of the person being nominated.
6. Enter the phone number of the person being nominated.
7. Select the category for the person being nominated from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the nomination. This will be displayed as a heading for the nomination.
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the reasons for the nomination. This can include specific examples or details about the service provided.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the person being nominated.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the person being nominated.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person being nominated.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person being nominated.
* **Who is the person being nominated?** (`select_one_nominated`, select_one, optional): Select from the options provided to indicate who is being nominated.
