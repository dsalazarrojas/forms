# public_room_audio_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the public room's audio quality and overall experience. It helps us understand how well the room's sound system and acoustics meet your expectations and if the event timing was suitable for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best describe your experience with the public room's audio quality and overall experience. You may need to scroll down to see all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sound Quality** (`audio_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate the sound quality of the room from 'Good' to 'Poor'.
* **Sound System** (`sound_system`, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you have access to the sound system in the room ('Yes' or 'No').
* **Room Acoustics** (`room_acoustics`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all that apply from 'Yes' to 'No' for the room acoustics (e.g., echoes, reverberation, etc.).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, date, required: false): Select the time you attended the event in the format 'hh:mm'.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you attended the event in the format 'yyyy-mm-dd' or 'yyyy/mm/dd'.
