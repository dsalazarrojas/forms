# Tenant Complaint Resolution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Complaint Resolution Survey is a form designed to collect information about complaints or issues reported by tenants. This form helps in resolving tenant complaints in a timely and effective manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes your contact details and a description of the issue.
2. Choose the type of complaint and select any relevant options from the dropdown menus.
3. Provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
4. Choose how you would like to be followed up on (Send Email, Call, or No Follow-Up).
5. Select the contact person to follow up with (Maintenance Person or Property Manager).
6. Select the follow-up date and time.
7. Indicate your satisfaction with the resolution.
8. Choose the resolution status (Resolved, Pending, or Unresolved).
9. Add any comments if needed.
10. Assign the resolution to a contact person (Maintenance Person or Property Manager).
11. Date and time stamp the creation and update of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Complaint** (`tenant_complaint_resolution_survey_page`, text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the issue or complaint.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, Optional): Enter your contact information (name, phone number, etc.).
* **Email** (`contact_details_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_details_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Complaint Type** (`complaint_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of complaint (Maintenance Issue, Rent Issue, or Other).
* **Description** (`description`, text, Optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the issue.
* **Resolution** (`resolution`, text, Optional): Provide any relevant information about the resolution.
* **Follow up Action** (`follow_up_action`, select_one, Optional): Choose how you would like to be followed up on (Send Email, Call, or No Follow-Up).
* **Contact** (`follow_up_contact`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the contact person to follow up with (Maintenance Person or Property Manager).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, Optional): Select the follow-up date.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, Optional): Select the follow-up time.
* **Tenant Satisfaction** (`tenant_satisfaction`, select_one, Optional): Indicate your satisfaction with the resolution (Satisfied or Not Satisfied).
* **Resolution Status** (`resolution_status`, select_one, Optional): Choose the resolution status (Resolved, Pending, or Unresolved).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, Optional): Add any additional comments related to the issue.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, Optional): Assign the resolution to a contact person (Maintenance Person or Property Manager).
* **Created** (`created`, date, Optional): Date and time stamp the creation of the form.
* **Updated** (`updated`, date, options, Optional): Date and time stamp the update of the form.
