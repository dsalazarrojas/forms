# Local Business Review Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with a local business. Your input will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and honestly.
2. Make sure to provide your email address so we can follow up with you.
3. Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shop Name (\*):** Enter the name of the local business you are providing feedback on.
* **Email (\*):** Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Rating (\*):** Choose how satisfied you are with your experience (Very Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied).
* **Testimonial:** Share your thoughts or feedback about your experience.
* **Shop ID (\*):** Choose whether you have used the shop before (Yes, No, Other).
* **Date (\*):** Enter the date of your visit.
* **Time:** Optional - enter the time of your visit (if applicable).
* **Note:** Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Assigned Tool (\*):** Choose which tool was used during your visit (Chatjimmy, Other).
* **Output File:** Attach any output files related to your feedback (e.g. screenshots, logs).
* **Description:** Optional - add any additional context or details about your experience.
* **Category (\*):** Choose which category this feedback falls under (Feedback Forms, Other).
* **Form ID:** Optional - enter the ID of the form you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Title:** Optional - enter the title of the form you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Description:** Optional - add any additional context or details about the form you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Category (\*):** Choose which category this form falls under (Feedback Forms, Other).
* **Assigned Tool (\*):** Choose which tool was used for this form (Chatjimmy, Other).
* **Form Output File (\*):** Attach any output files related to your feedback on this form.
* **Form Form ID:** Optional - enter the ID of the form you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Form Title (\*):** Optional - enter the title of the form you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Description (\*):** Optional - add any additional context or details about the form you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Category 2 (\*):** Choose which category this form falls under (Feedback Forms, Other).
* **Form Output File 2:** Optional - attach any output files related to your feedback on this form.
* **Assigned Tool 2 (\*):** Choose which tool was used for this form (Chatjimmy, Other).
* **Form Form Title 2:** Optional - enter the title of the form you are providing feedback on.
* **Form Description 2:** Optional - add any additional context or details about the form you are providing feedback on.
