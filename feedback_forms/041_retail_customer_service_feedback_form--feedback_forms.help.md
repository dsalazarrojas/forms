# Retail Customer Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with our retail store's customer service. This will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your answers to each question and add any additional comments you may have. Please answer each question as accurately as possible, and don't hesitate to provide feedback on your overall experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How was your customer service experience?** (`customer_service_experience`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about your overall experience with our customer service team. Please select whether your experience was 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **How would you rate your experience with our products?** (`product_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the quality and satisfaction of our products. Please select whether your experience was 'Good', 'Fair', or another option is not available.
* **How would you rate the sales support you received from our staff?** (`sales_support`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the level of support provided by our sales staff. Please select whether you found their support 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **How would you rate the climate of our store?** (`store_climate`, select_one, required=false): This question asks you to rate the overall atmosphere and ambiance of our store. Please select whether the climate was 'Good', 'Fair', or another option is not available (if so, please provide a comment in the 'Additional Comments' field).
* **How friendly was our staff?** (`staff_friendliness`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the friendliness of our staff during your visit. Please select whether our staff were 'Yes', or not.
* **How was the availability of products during your visit?** (`product_availability`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the availability of products during your visit. Please select whether products were available 'Yes', or not.
* **How easy was it to find our store?** (`store_location`, select_one, required=false): This question asks you to rate the ease of finding our store. Please select whether you found the location 'Yes', or not.
* **How helpful was our staff during your visit?** (`staff_helpfulness`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate the helpfulness of our staff during your visit. Please select whether they were 'Yes', or not.
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your shopping experience?** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with your shopping experience. Please select whether you are 'Yes', or not.
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, required=false): This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience. Please feel free to share any thoughts or suggestions you have.
