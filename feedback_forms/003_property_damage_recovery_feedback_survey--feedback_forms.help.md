# Property Damage Recovery Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from you about your property damage experience. Please provide your feedback to help us improve our processes and better understand your situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please provide a detailed description of the damage to your property.
2.  Choose the type of damage that occurred (e.g., Water damage, Fire damage, Vandalism damage, or Other).
3.  Specify the exact address where the damage occurred.
4.  Describe the damage in detail.
5.  Provide the date and time of the incident.
6.  Choose the cause of the damage (e.g., Lightning strike, Wind or weather, Vandalism or trespasser, or Other).
7.  Select all applicable methods of contact (e.g., Call, Text, Email).
8.  Enter the amount of damage to your property.
9.  Provide any additional comments you would like to include.
10. Submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **propertyName**: This is the title of your property. Please provide the exact name of your property. 
    This field is required.
*   **address**: Enter the exact address where the damage occurred. This field is required.
*   **typeOfDamage**: Please select the type of damage you experienced (e.g., Water damage, Fire damage, Vandalism damage, or Other). This field is required.
*   **descriptionOfDamage**: Describe the damage in detail. This field is required.
*   **dateOfDamage**: Enter the date of the incident. This field is required.
*   **timeOfDamage**: Enter the time of the incident. This field is required.
*   **causeOfDamage**: Choose the cause of the damage (e.g., Lightning strike, Wind or weather, Vandalism or trespasser, or Other). This field is required.
*   **contactDetails**: Select all the contact methods you would like to use (e.g., Call, Text, Email). This field is required.
*   **damageAmount**: Enter the amount of damage to your property. This field is required.
*   **comments**: Provide any additional comments or information you would like to include. This field is optional.
*   **submittedBy**: Enter your name. This field is optional.
*   **submittedOn**: Enter the date you submitted your report. This field is optional.
*   **submittedByEmail**: Enter your email address. This field is optional. 

Note: Please make sure to submit a completed form as some fields are required.
