# Cupping Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients regarding their experiences with a product or service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Provide your thoughts and opinions on the questions asked.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comments" field.
4. Enter your contact information (Name, Email, Phone) so that we can follow up with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Input** (`client_input`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant context or information about your experience with our product or service.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Share your thoughts on your experience with our product or service.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, note, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improvement.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name so that we can address any follow up communications with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your experience.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your experience.
