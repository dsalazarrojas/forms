# Community Outreach Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from community outreach program participants to help us understand their experiences and perceptions of our community outreach services. Your input will be used to identify areas for improvement and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the program you participated in from the list.
2. Choose your approximate date of participation.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the program.
4. Evaluate the quality of the program.
5. Rate the helpfulness of the staff.
6. Answer if the program met your expectations.
7. Choose the benefits you gained from the program.
8. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the program.
9. Suggest areas for improvement.
10. Provide additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which Program Did You Participate In** (Program Name, Select One, Required)
	+ Select the program you participated in from the list provided.
* **Date of Participation** (Participation Date, Date, Optional)
	+ Enter the approximate date you participated in the program.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (Overall Satisfaction, Select One, Required)
	+ Rate your overall satisfaction with the program (1-5).
* **How Would You Rate the Program Quality** (Program Quality, Select One, Required)
	+ Evaluate the quality of the program (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Staff Helpfulness Rating** (Staff Helpfulness, Select One, Required)
	+ Rate the helpfulness of the staff (Very Helpful, Helpful, Neutral, Unhelpful, Very Unhelpful).
* **Did the Program Meet Your Expectations** (Met Expectations, Select One, Required)
	+ Answer if the program met your expectations (Exceeded Expectations, Met Expectations, Below Expectations).
* **What Benefits Did You Gain** (Program Benefits, Select Multiple, Optional)
	+ Select all that apply from the list provided.
* **How Likely Are You to Recommend This Program** (Likelihood to Recommend, Select One, Required)
	+ Rate how likely you are to recommend the program (Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, Very Unlikely).
* **What Areas Need Improvement** (Areas for Improvement, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide specific suggestions for improvement.
* **What Additional Services Would You Like** (Additional Services, Text, Optional)
	+ Suggest additional services you would like to see offered.
* **How Would You Rate Our Communication** (Communication Rating, Select One, Required)
	+ Rate our communication (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Would You Participate in Future Programs** (Future Participation, Select One, Required)
	+ Indicate if you would participate in future programs (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, Definitely No).
* **Age Group** (Age Group, Select One, Optional)
	+ Choose your age group (Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, 65+).
* **Any Additional Comments or Suggestions** (Additional Comments, Text, Optional)
	+ Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.

This form is designed to be completed in approximately 10-15 minutes.
