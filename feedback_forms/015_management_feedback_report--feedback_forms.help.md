# Management Feedback Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Management Feedback Report is a form designed to collect information from staff about their management experience. This report helps managers understand staff feedback and concerns, allowing for more informed decision-making and improved management practices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the report date (Page 1).
2. Enter the manager's name (Page 2).
3. Provide any questions or concerns you have about your manager (Page 3).
4. Rate the manager's behavior on a scale of yes or no (Page 4).
5. Identify any areas of concern about your manager's behavior or performance (Page 5).
6. Rate the current staff mood (Page 6).
7. Rate staff satisfaction with their current role or responsibilities (Page 7).
8. Indicate whether you would like to report your feedback more frequently (Page 8).
9. Add any comments or suggestions for the manager (Page 9).
10. Add any comments or suggestions from the staff (Page 10).
11. Indicate how often you would like to receive feedback from the manager (Page 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the feedback report.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Questions About Manager** (`questions_about_manager`, text, optional): This is the space to provide any questions or concerns you have about your manager.
* **Manager Behavior** (`manager_behavior`, select_one, optional): Rate the manager's behavior as yes or no.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether your motivation is affected by your manager's behavior, yes or no.
* **Areas Of Concern** (`areas_of_concern`, select_multiple, optional): Identify any areas of concern about your manager's behavior or performance, yes or no.
* **Staff Mood** (`staff_mood`, select_one, optional): Rate the current staff mood, yes or no.
* **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate staff satisfaction with their current role or responsibilities, Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Report Frequency** (`report_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate how often you would like to report your feedback, yes or no.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or suggestions for the manager.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or suggestions from the staff.
* **Feedback Frequency** (`feedback_frequency`, number, optional): Indicate how often you would like to receive feedback from the manager, with options ranging from 1 to 10.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
* Be specific with your concerns and suggestions.
* Remember that this form is a confidential report, and your feedback will be treated with respect and professionalism.
