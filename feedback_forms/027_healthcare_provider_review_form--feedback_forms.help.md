# Healthcare Provider Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect patient feedback about their healthcare provider, which helps us improve our services and provide better patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all fields marked as required. These are the fields with an asterisk (*) in their label. 
2. Enter your genuine feedback in the Patient Feedback field.
3. Type the healthcare provider's name in the Healthcare Provider Name field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email Address field, as this is how we will contact you for more information.
5. Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have in the Review Comments and Additional Comments fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, optional): Please provide your honest and detailed feedback about your healthcare provider's performance.
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your healthcare provider who treated you.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with your healthcare provider's performance, using numbers 1-5.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or concerns you may have about your healthcare provider.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your healthcare experience.
