# Social Work Introduction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your personal, social, and medical history to help social workers better understand your needs and circumstances. It will be used to assess your eligibility for certain services and programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Personal Information (Page 1)**: Enter your personal details, such as your name, contact information, and demographic information.
	* Please provide your full name and any other identifying information you feel comfortable sharing.
2. **Contact Information (Page 1)**: Enter your contact details, such as your phone number and email address.
	* Please provide your primary contact information.
3. **Services Needed (Page 2)**: Select the services you are seeking from the list of options.
	* Please select the services you need or are interested in.
4. **Social Support (Page 3)**: Enter information about your social support network.
	* Please provide details about your social support system, including family, friends, or other resources.
5. **Mental Health (Page 4)**: Enter information about your mental health condition.
	* Please provide details about your mental health condition and any treatment or medication you are currently taking.
6. **Relationship Status (Page 5)**: Select your relationship status.
	* Please select your current relationship status.
7. **Income (Page 6)**: Enter your current income.
	* Please provide your current income.
8. **Employment Status (Page 7)**: Select your employment status.
	* Please select your current employment status.
9. **Hours Worked (Page 8)**: Enter the number of hours you work.
	* Please provide the number of hours you work.
10. **Hours Paid (Page 8)**: Enter the number of hours you are paid.
	* Please provide the number of hours you are paid.
11. **Social Security (Page 9)**: Select your social security status.
	* Please select your current social security status.
12. **Disability Status (Page 10)**: Select your disability status.
	* Please select your current disability status.
13. **Veterans Benefits (Page 11)**: Select your veterans benefits status.
	* Please select your current veterans benefits status.
14. **Medical Condition (Page 12)**: Enter information about any medical conditions you have.
	* Please provide details about any medical conditions you have.
15. **Mental Health Condition (Page 13)**: Enter information about any mental health conditions you have.
	* Please provide details about any mental health conditions you have.
16. **Medication (Page 14)**: Enter information about any medication you are currently taking.
	* Please provide details about any medication you are currently taking.
17. **Medical Treatment (Page 15)**: Enter information about any medical treatment you are currently undergoing.
	* Please provide details about any medical treatment you are currently undergoing.
18. **Medical Services (Page 16)**: Enter information about any medical services you need or have received.
	* Please provide details about any medical services you need or have received.
19. **Medical Supplies (Page 17)**: Enter information about any medical supplies you need or have received.
	* Please provide details about any medical supplies you need or have received.
20. **Medical Expenses (Page 18)**: Enter the total medical expenses you have.
	* Please provide the total medical expenses you have.
21. **Medical Bills (Page 18)**: Enter the number of medical bills you have.
	* Please provide the number of medical bills you have.
22. **Medical Insurance (Page 19)**: Select your medical insurance status.
	* Please select your current medical insurance status.
23. **Medical History (Page 20)**: Enter information about your medical history.
	* Please provide details about your medical history.
24. **Emergency Contact (Page 21)**: Enter information about your emergency contact.
	* Please provide details about your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Unsure" or "Not Applicable."
* If you have any medical conditions, please provide details about your medical history.
* If you are taking any medication, please provide details about your medication.
* If you are undergoing any medical treatment, please provide details about your treatment.
