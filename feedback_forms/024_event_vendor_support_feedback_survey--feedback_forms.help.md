# event_vendor_support_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from event vendors on their experience with event planning. It helps us understand their level of support and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the question "event_vendor_survey" in the first section. Please choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your level of support.
2. If you answered "Yes" in the previous question, you may skip this step. If you answered "No", please enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate your level of satisfaction with our support.
3. In the "vendor_support_issues" field, describe any issues you have faced while working with us. This will help us understand the problems you're experiencing.
4. If you have any suggestions on how we can improve, please enter them in the "suggestions" field.
5. If you would like to provide your name and email address as the event planner, please fill in the "event_planner_name" and "event_planner_email" fields. This will allow us to follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_vendor_survey** (event_vendor_survey, select multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you support our events, and "No" if you do not. This will help us understand your level of support.
* **feedback_score** (satisfaction_score, number, required): If you answered "No" to the previous question, please rate your level of satisfaction with our support on a scale of 1-10.
* **vendor_support_issues** (vendor_support_issues, text, required): Describe any issues you have faced while working with us. This will help us understand the problems you're experiencing.
* **suggestions** (suggestions, text, required): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve, please enter them here.
* **event_planner_name** (event_planner_name, text, required): Enter your name as the event planner. This will allow us to follow up with you if needed.
* **event_planner_email** (event_planner_email, email, required): Enter your email address as the event planner. This will allow us to follow up with you if needed.
* **vendor_name** (vendor_name, text, required): Enter your name as the vendor.
