# Montessori School Class Enhancement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Montessori School Class Enhancement Form is designed to gather feedback from teachers and staff on the performance of a Montessori class. This form will help the school administration to identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions for enhancing the class.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the options for "Class Enhancement Form" and select the most accurate answer.
2. Provide any additional comments or explanations in the "Class Enhancement Form Comments" field.
3. Select a teacher's name from the list of options.
4. Select a date and time for the class you're referring to.
5. Enter the email address of the teacher or staff member providing the feedback.
6. Enter the contact phone number of the teacher or staff member providing the feedback.
7. Provide additional comments or explanations in the "Class Enhancement Form Additional Comments" field.
8. Select a category for the feedback, such as "Feedback Forms" or "Other".
9. Provide a brief description of the feedback in the "Description" field.
10. Finally, provide any additional comments or explanations in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Class Enhancement Form** (`class_enhancement_form`, select_one, required/optional): Select this form to provide feedback on the performance of the Montessori class. This is a yes/no option to indicate whether you have any suggestions for improvement.
* **Class Enhancement Form Comments** (`class_enhancement_form_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations about your feedback.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, select_one, required/optional): Select the teacher who taught the class you're providing feedback on.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the teachers who taught the class you're providing feedback on.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the class you're referring to.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for the class you're referring to.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the teacher or staff member providing the feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of the teacher or staff member providing the feedback.
* **Class Enhancement Form Comments** (`class_enhancement_form_comments`, note, required/optional): Provide additional comments or explanations about your feedback.
* **Category** (`montessori_class_enhancement_form_category`, select_one, required/optional): Select a category for the feedback, such as "Feedback Forms" or "Other".
* **Description** (`class_enhancement_form_description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`class_enhancement_form_additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide additional comments or explanations about your feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct category for the feedback to help the school administration filter and prioritize the suggestions.
* Keep the feedback brief and to the point, as it's for the enhancement of the Montessori class.
