# Remote Work Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from employees about their experiences working remotely. Your input will help inform company decisions about remote work policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select a response for each question and provide as much information as you feel comfortable sharing. You can skip any fields you do not feel are relevant to your experiences working remotely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_page** (Select One, **Yes/No**): Please select "Yes" if you work remotely, and "No" if you do not work remotely.
* **remote_work_experiences** (Text, **Describe your remote work experiences**): Please share your experiences working remotely, including any challenges or successes you've had.
* **satisfaction_rating** (Number, **Rate your satisfaction**): Please rate your level of satisfaction with your remote work experience on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very Satisfied".
* **collaboration_difficulties** (Text, **Describe collaboration difficulties**): Please describe any difficulties you've faced in collaboration while working remotely.
* **policy_suggestions** (Text, **Suggest changes to our remote work policy**): Please suggest any changes you would like to see made to the company's remote work policy.
