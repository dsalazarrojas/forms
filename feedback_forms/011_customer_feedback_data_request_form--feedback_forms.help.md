# customer_feedback_data_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Feedback Form with a brief description of your experience with our product or service.
2. Choose the rating and comment options that best reflect your feelings about our product or service.
3. Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your feedback.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address.
5. Optionally, select the Likert scale options (e.g., "Good", "Fair", "Very Good", etc.) to indicate your satisfaction with our product or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Feedback Form** (`customer_feedback_form`, text, required): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your experience with our product or service.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any additional information that you would like to share with us, you can enter it in this field.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, optional): Use this field to rate your satisfaction with our product on a scale of 1-5.
* **Product Comment** (`product_comment`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about our product, enter them here.
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, number, optional): Use this field to rate your satisfaction with our service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Service Comment** (`service_comment`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about our service, enter them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your email address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): This field is for any additional feedback you would like to provide.
* **Rating (Likert Scale)** (`rating_likert_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best represents your satisfaction with our product or service.
* **Select Likert Scale** (`select_likert_scale`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your experience with our product or service.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you spent interacting with our product or service.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you interacted with our product or service.
* **Select Time** (`select_time`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best represents your experience with the time spent interacting with our product or service.
* **Select Date** (`select_date`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your experience with the date you interacted with our product or service.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to provide.
* **Select Note** (`select_note`, select_multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your experience with our product or service.
