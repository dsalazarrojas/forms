# Celebrate and Shape Future Events - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from attendees on their experience at events, allowing us to identify areas for improvement and shape future events to better meet the needs and preferences of our attendees. Your input is invaluable in helping us create a more enjoyable and engaging experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address to help us contact you regarding your feedback and potentially for future events.
2. Describe your overall experience at the event, rating it as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor.
3. Indicate your likelihood to recommend this event to others, choosing from Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Very Unlikely.
4. Rate the event's program content and quality, venue and facilities, food and beverage, and staff organization.
5. Suggest improvements and share your favorite moments from the event.
6. Provide ideas for future events, including preferred themes, activities, and frequencies.
7. Let us know if you'd like to be involved in planning future events.
8. Give us your consent to be contacted about future events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name (3)** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address (3)** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required): Provide your email address to help us contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number, please do so here.
* **Event Name or Title (6)** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Describe the name or title of the event you attended.
* **Event Date (7)** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event you attended.
* **Overall Experience (9)** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall experience as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor.
* **Likelihood to Recommend This Event (10)** (`likelihood_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Choose how likely you are to recommend this event to others, with options from Very Likely to Very Unlikely.
* **Program Content and Quality (12)** (`program_content_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the event's program content and quality as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor.
* **Venue and Facilities (13)** (`venue_facilities`, `select_one`, required): Rate the event venue and facilities as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor.
* **Food and Beverage (14)** (`food_beverage`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the event's food and beverage as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor. If not applicable, choose N/A.
* **Staff and Organization (15)** (`staff_organization`, `select_one`, required): Rate the event staff and organization as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Fair, or Poor.
* **Best Aspects (17)** (`best_aspects`, `text`, optional): Share the highlights and your favorite moments from the event.
* **Improvement Areas (18)** (`improvement_areas`, `text`, optional): Suggest areas for improvement for future events.
* **Ideas for Future Events (20)** (`theme_preferences`, `text`, optional): Share your ideas on preferred event themes, activities, or frequencies.
* **Suggested Activities or Experiences (21)** (`activity_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Suggest new activities or experiences you'd like to see at future events.
* **Preferred Event Frequency (22)** (`event_frequency`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate how often you'd like to attend events, with options from Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, Annually, or As Needed.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions (23)** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Interested in Helping Plan Future Events? (24)** (`future_involvement`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know if you'd like to be involved in planning future events.
* **Consent to be Contacted (25)** (`contact_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your consent to be contacted about future events, with options to Yes or No.
