# Animal Shelter Website Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the animal shelter website. The goal is to understand how users found the website, what they were looking for, and how they felt about its functionality, content, and overall experience. This feedback will help the animal shelter organization improve their website to better serve users.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your email address (optional) if you'd like us to follow up with you.
2. Choose when you visited the website.
3. Select the device you used to access the website.
4. If this is your first time visiting the website, please select 'Yes'.
5. Indicate how often you visit the website.
6. Choose your main reason for visiting the website.
7. Indicate if you were able to accomplish what you came to the website for.
8. Rate the overall visual appeal, color scheme, and design of the website.
9. Rate the ease of navigation and how easy it is to find information on the website.
10. Rate the relevance and quality of content on the website.
11. Rate the photo and image quality of animals listed on the website.
12. Rate the functionality and performance of the website.
13. Indicate if you encountered any broken links or errors.
14. Rate the search functionality of the website.
15. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
16. Rate your overall satisfaction level with the website.
17. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the website to others.
18. Provide suggestions for improvements.
19. Finally, add any additional feedback or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Address**: Optional - Your email address for follow-up (if you'd like us to contact you with your feedback results or for any updates).
* **Date of Visit**: The date you visited the website.
* **Device Used**: The device you used to access the website, such as a computer, tablet, or mobile phone.
* **First Time Visitor**: Yes, if this is your first time visiting the website.
* **How Often Do You Visit**: How often you visit the website, such as first visit, occasionally, monthly, or daily.
* **Main Reason for Your Visit**: Why you came to the website, such as to view available animals, adopt a pet, donate, or get information.
* **Did You Accomplish Your Goal**: If you were able to do what you came to the website for.
* **Overall Visual Appeal**: Rate the website's visual appeal, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Color Scheme and Design**: Rate the website's color scheme and design, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Layout and Organization**: Rate the website's layout and organization, from 1 (Very Confusing) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Ease of Navigation**: Rate how easy it is to navigate the website, from 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
* **Find Information**: Rate how easy it is to find information on the website, from 1 (Very Difficult) to 5 (Very Easy).
* **Menu and Navigation Structure**: Rate the website's menu and navigation structure, from 1 (Confusing) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Mobile Experience**: Rate how well the website works on mobile devices, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Relevance of Content**: Rate the relevance of the content on the website, from 1 (Irrelevant) to 5 (Very Relevant).
* **Animal Listings Quality**: Rate the quality of animal listings on the website, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Photo and Image Quality**: Rate the quality of photos and images on the website, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Website Functionality**: Rate how well the website works, from 1 (Very Slow) to 5 (Very Fast).
* **Broken Links or Errors**: If you encountered any broken links or errors on the website.
* **Search Functionality**: Rate the search functionality of the website, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Overall Satisfaction Level**: Rate your overall satisfaction level with the website, from 1 (Very Unsatisfied) to 5 (Very Satisfied).
* **Likely to Recommend**: Rate how likely you are to recommend the website to others, from 1 (Very Unlikely) to 5 (Very Likely).
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Any suggestions you have for improving the website.
* **Additional Feedback or Comments**: Any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
