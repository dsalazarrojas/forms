# Mobile Arcade Gaming Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their gaming habits and preferences. The collected data will be used to improve the overall gaming experience for our users.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer all required fields as specified in each question.
2. Select the best-suited option from the provided list for each multiple-choice question.
3. Write your answer in the text fields as clearly and concisely as possible.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you play? (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely)**: Answer how often you play mobile arcade games in a typical week.
* **Favorite Title** (`text`): If you have a favorite game, type the title here.
* **Feature Ideas** (`note`): Share any ideas you have for new features or improvements to our mobile arcade games.
* **How much time do you play? (1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, More than 6 hours)**: Estimate the average amount of time you spend playing mobile arcade games in a typical week.
* **Favorite Genre** (`Action`, `Role Playing`, `Strategy`, `Sports`, `Simulation`): Choose one or more genres that you enjoy playing the most.
* **Favorite Platform** (`Console`, `Mobile`, `PC`, `Handheld`): Select the platform(s) on which you prefer playing games.
* **Comments** (`text`): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, share them here.
* **How would you rate your experience with mobile arcade games?** (`number`): Rate your overall experience as a number between 0 and 10.
* **How often do you think you will play next month? (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely)**: Estimate how often you think you'll play next month.
* **Favorite type of game?** (`text`): If you have a favorite type of game, type it here.
* **Submit** (`text`): Click to submit your feedback.
