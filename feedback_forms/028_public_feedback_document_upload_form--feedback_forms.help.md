# Public Feedback Document Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect public feedback, including document uploads, for improvement and analysis purposes. It ensures that the submitted feedback is accurate, up-to-date, and relevant, helping to reduce duplication and improve the overall feedback process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose the type of feedback you wish to provide from the dropdown list.
2. Describe your feedback in the text box.
3. Upload a relevant document related to your feedback.
4. Provide a title for your uploaded document.
5. Enter your contact email (this information will not be shared publicly).
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Upload Document**: This is the text field where you can enter the details about the document you are uploading.
  - *Type of Feedback*: Select the category of feedback you are providing. This helps us understand the nature of your feedback.
  - *Feedback*: Please provide a clear and concise description of your feedback, making it easier for us to understand and address your concerns.
  - *Document Upload*: This is the actual file upload area where you can upload the relevant document related to your feedback.
  - *Document Title*: Give a title to your uploaded document for easy identification and reference.
  - *Submitter Info*: This field is not currently used. Please do not fill it in.
  - *Contact Email*: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback. Please note that this information will not be shared publicly.
