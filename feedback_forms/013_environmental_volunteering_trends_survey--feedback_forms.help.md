# Environmental Volunteering Trends Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information on your volunteering behavior and preferences. This survey is meant to help us better understand your involvement in environmental volunteering activities and identify trends among volunteers. Your input will contribute to the development of more effective and efficient volunteer programs in the environmental sector.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the appropriate options for each question based on your personal experiences and preferences.
2. You may skip any questions that do not apply to you.
3. For select fields, choose the most relevant option or select multiple options that apply.
4. Enter any relevant notes or comments in the provided space at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Behavior**: This field is for you to describe your general volunteering behavior and habits.
* **Volunteer Preferences**: This field is for you to share your preferences or any specific volunteering activities you enjoy or would like to participate in.
* **Feedback**: Please use this space to provide any additional feedback or suggestions on the survey or the volunteering process.
* **Type of Volunteer**: Please select whether you are a Volunteer or if you have a different role or title.
* **How many hours per week do you volunteer?**: Enter the number of hours you typically volunteer per week.
* **How many days per week do you volunteer?**: Enter the number of days you usually volunteer per week.
* **How many months per year do you volunteer?**: Enter the number of months you volunteer per year.
* **How many years have you been volunteering?**: Enter the number of years you have been volunteering.
* **How often do you volunteer?**: Select how often you usually volunteer:
	+ Once a week
	+ Twice a week
	+ Three times a week
	+ Four or more times a week
* **What is your primary reason for volunteering?**: Select your primary reason for volunteering:
	+ Environmental
	+ Social
	+ Both
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Where do you usually volunteer?**: Select all applicable locations where you usually volunteer:
	+ In my community
	+ Local park
	+ Community center
	+ Park or forest
	+ School
* **Who do you usually volunteer with?**: Select who you usually volunteer with:
	+ With friends
	+ With family
	+ With colleagues
	+ Alone
	+ Other (please specify)
* **What time of day do you usually volunteer?**: This field is for you to specify the time of day you usually volunteer.
* **What time of day do you usually volunteer?**: Please note that this field is currently not functional.
* **How long do you usually volunteer?**: Enter the number of minutes you usually volunteer.
* **What is your occupation?**: Select all applicable occupations that apply:
	+ Student
	+ Teacher
	+ Artist
	+ Engineer
	+ Scientist
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Notes**: Provide any notes or comments about your volunteering experiences or experiences.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments on the survey or the volunteering process.
