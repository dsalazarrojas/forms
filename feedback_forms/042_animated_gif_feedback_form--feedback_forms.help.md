# Animated GIF Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about animated GIFs. It aims to collect information on user satisfaction, contact details, and feedback messages.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please provide your full name. This is a required field.
2. Next, rate your overall satisfaction with the animated GIFs we provide. Choose from the following options: Excellent - Highly Satisfied, Good - Satisfied, Neutral - Fine, Poor - Dissatisfied.
3. If you'd like to share more details about your experience, please use the space provided.
4. Please type a message with your feedback about our animated GIFs.
5. If you have any suggestions for visual improvements, feel free to share them here.
6. Finally, type any specific animations or styles that you enjoy or dislike.
7. To complete the form, click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your full name so we can address our feedback to you personally.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address so we can follow up on your suggestions or contact you with any further questions.
* **Overall Satisfaction Score** (Select One, Required): Rate your satisfaction with the animated GIFs we provide. Choose from: Excellent - Highly Satisfied, Good - Satisfied, Neutral - Fine, Poor - Dissatisfied.
* **Feedback Message** (Text, Required): Share your thoughts about our animated GIFs.
* **Visual Improvement Suggestions** (Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions for how we can improve our visual elements, please share them here.
* **Thank You for Your Feedback** (Note, Optional): A friendly note to show our appreciation for your input.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to rate your overall satisfaction honestly. This will help us understand how we can improve.
* Be as detailed as possible in your feedback message.
* If you have any specific animation suggestions, please mention them so we can try to implement them.
* We value your input and will use it to improve our animated GIFs.
