# Guide for Using Online Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this online form is to provide a convenient and standardized way for users to submit feedback and suggestions on specific guides and tools within our platform. This form helps us understand your needs, preferences, and concerns, which will inform our development and improvement of guides and tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the guide or tool you are providing feedback on.
2.  Briefly describe your feedback, suggestions, or concerns about the guide or tool.
3.  Select the category for your feedback (e.g., "guide", "tool", or "general").
4.  Optionally, enter a unique form ID to help us track and reference your submission.
5.  If applicable, select the tool to which your feedback is related.
6.  Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Title** (1, text, required: false): Enter the title of the guide or tool you are providing feedback on.
*   **Description** (2, text, required: false): Briefly describe your feedback, suggestions, or concerns about the guide or tool.
*   **Category** (3, text, required: false): Select the category for your feedback (e.g., "guide", "tool", or "general").
*   **Form ID** (4, number, required: false): Optionally, enter a unique form ID to help us track and reference your submission.
*   **Assigned Tool** (5, text, required: false): If applicable, select the tool to which your feedback is related.

## Tips
*   Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
*   If you have a suggestion for improvement, please provide details on how the suggested changes can be implemented.
*   If your feedback is related to a specific guide or tool, please select the correct category and tool to ensure your feedback is directed to the right team.
*   Remember to submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button to help us improve our guides and tools.
