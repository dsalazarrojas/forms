# new_hire_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The new hire feedback form is designed to collect feedback and information about a new hire's performance, skills, and other relevant aspects of their onboarding process. This feedback will help HR or management assess their onboarding success and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully before starting to ensure you have all the necessary information to provide a thorough response.
2. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible, as your feedback will help provide valuable insights for the new hire's onboarding process.
3. Be honest and provide constructive feedback that will be helpful for the new hire's development and future references.
