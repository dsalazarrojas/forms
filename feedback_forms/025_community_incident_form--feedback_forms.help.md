# Community Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Incident Form is a tool used to collect information about incidents that occur within the community. By completing this form, you can help community leaders understand and address issues more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of incident that best describes the situation.
2.  Provide the date and approximate time of the incident.
3.  Enter the exact location where the incident occurred.
4.  Describe the incident in detail.
5.  If applicable, provide the names or descriptions of those involved.
6.  If you have any witness information, list their names or descriptions.
7.  Check if the police or local authority have been notified about the incident.
8.  If you have photos or videos of the incident, select "Yes" to attach them.
9.  If you'd like to remain anonymous, you can leave your name and contact information blank.
10.  Select the date you're reporting the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Type of Incident to Report
* **Type of Incident to Report** (`incident_type`, Select, Required): Select the category that best fits the incident you're reporting.

### Date of Incident
* **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`, Date, Required): When did this incident occur?

### Approximate Time of Incident
* **Approximate Time of Incident** (`incident_time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of the incident.

### Exact Location of Incident
* **Exact Location of Incident** (`incident_location`, Text, Required): Enter the address, intersection, or landmark where the incident occurred.

### Detailed Description of the Incident
* **Detailed Description of the Incident** (`incident_description`, Text, Required): Describe what happened in detail.

### Known Involved Parties
* **Known Involved Parties** (`involved_parties`, Text, Optional): Enter the names or descriptions of those involved, if known.

### Witness Information
* **Witness Information** (`witness_info`, Text, Optional): List any other people who saw the incident.

### Was the police or local authority notified?
* **Was the police or local authority notified?** (`police_notified`, Select, Required): Check if the police or local authority have been notified about the incident.

### Do you have photos or video of the incident?
* **Do you have photos or video of the incident?** (`photos_available`, Select, Required): Select "Yes" if you have attached photos or videos.

### Your Full Name
* **Your Full Name** (`reporter_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your full name if you'd like to be identified, or leave blank to remain anonymous.

### Your Phone or Email Address
* **Your Phone or Email Address** (`reporter_contact`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone or email address for follow-up, or leave blank to remain anonymous.

### Date Reported
* **Date Reported** (`reporting_date`, Date, Required): Select the date you're reporting this incident.

### I understand this report will be reviewed by community leaders
* **I understand this report will be reviewed by community leaders** (`privacy_acknowledgement`, Select, Required): Select "I understand" to indicate that you're aware of this policy.
