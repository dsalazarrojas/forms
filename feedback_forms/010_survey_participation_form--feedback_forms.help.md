# survey_participation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their overall experience and provide a more comprehensive view of the project's impact.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose and scope of the survey.
2. Rate your overall experience (1-5).
3. Provide any additional feedback you may have.
4. Confirm your email address to help us get in touch with you.
5. Optionally, provide your phone number and the date and time of your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (id: 1, intro, optional): This is a brief description of the project and its purpose.
* **How would you rate your overall experience** (id: 2, rating, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Can you provide more feedback** (id: 3, feedback, optional): Provide any additional thoughts or comments you may have about your experience.
* **rating_scale** (id: 4, rating_scale, optional): Select a rating from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience.
* **What is your email** (id: 5, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **What is your phone** (id: 6, phone, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **When did you participate** (id: 7, date, optional): Enter the date of your participation in the project.
* **At what time did you participate** (id: 8, time, optional): Enter the time of your participation in the project.
* **Can you confirm your email address** (id: 9, email_confirmation, optional): Select "True" if you are willing to share your email address with us.

## Tips

* Please be honest and accurate when providing feedback.
* All fields are optional, so please only fill in the fields that you are comfortable providing information in.
* If you have already participated in the feedback process, do not fill in this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your project manager or HR representative.
