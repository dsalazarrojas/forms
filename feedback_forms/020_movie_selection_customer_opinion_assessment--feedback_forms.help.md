# Movie Selection Customer Opinion Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer opinions and feedback on their movie watching habits and preferences. It aims to gather information on which movies customers have selected, why they did not watch certain movies, and their ratings and comments about the movies they have watched. This information will help us understand customer behavior and preferences, allowing us to improve our movie selection and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about the movie you have selected, such as "Selected Movie" and "Reason Why Not Watched".
2. Provide your rating for the selected movie using the "Rating" field.
3. Share your thoughts and opinions about the movie using the "Comment" field.
4. If you have any additional contact information or comments, please provide them in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Answer the questions about your movie-watching habits, such as "Watch At Home", "Watch At Theater", "Watch At Cinema", "Watch At Streaming Library", "Watch At Club Event", "Watch On Dvd", and "Watch Other".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Selected Movie**: Choose the movie that you have watched or selected. This field is optional.
* **Reason Why Not Watched**: If you did not watch a movie, please select the reason from the list. If you selected "Other (please specify)", you can provide a brief explanation in the "Other Movie" field.
* **Other Movie**: This field is for providing a brief explanation for the "Other (please specify)" option selected in the "Reason Why Not Watched" field.
* **Watch Again**: Choose "Yes" if you would watch the movie again, and "No" otherwise.
* **Rating**: Provide a rating for the selected movie on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comment**: Share your thoughts and opinions about the movie in the space provided.
* **Contact Info**: If you have any additional contact information or comments, please provide them here.
* **Watch At Home**: Choose "Yes" if you watched the movie at home, and "No" otherwise.
* **Watch At Theater**: Choose "Yes" if you watched the movie at a theater, and "No" otherwise.
* **Watch At Cinema**: Choose "Yes" if you watched the movie at a cinema, and "No" otherwise.
* **Watch At Streaming Library**: Choose "Yes" if you watched the movie at a streaming library, and "No" otherwise.
* **Watch At Club Event**: Choose "Yes" if you watched the movie at a club event, and "No" otherwise.
* **Watch On Dvd**: Choose "Yes" if you watched the movie on DVD, and "No" otherwise.
* **Watch Other**: Choose "Yes" if you watched the movie in a different way, and "No" otherwise.
* **Watch At Home 2**: This field is similar to "Watch At Home" and is for providing additional information on watching movies at home.
