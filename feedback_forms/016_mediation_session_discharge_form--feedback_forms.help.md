# mediation_session_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mediation session discharge form is used to document the details of a mediation session, including participant feedback, session specifics, and outcome status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all applicable answers for 'mediation_session_discharge_form_questions' in page 1.
2. Enter your feedback about the mediation session in 'mediation_session_discharge_form_participant_feedback' on page 1.
3. Provide the outcome details and status in 'mediation_session_discharge_form_outcome_details' and 'mediation_session_discharge_form_outcome_status' respectively in page 1.
4. Choose whether to 'mediation_session_discharge_form_sign_off' in page 1.
5. Enter the mediation session's date in 'mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_date' in page 2.
6. Enter the mediation session's start time in 'mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_start_time' in page 2.
7. Enter the mediation session's end time in 'mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_end_time' in page 2.
8. Enter the mediation session's length in 'mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_length' in page 2.
9. Select whether the participant agreed to the session in 'mediation_session_discharge_form_participant_agreed' in page 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_questions** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_questions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all applicable answers to provide feedback on the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_participant_feedback** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_participant_feedback`, text, required): Enter your thoughts and feedback about the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_outcome_details** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_outcome_details`, text, required): Enter the details of the mediation session's outcome.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_outcome_status** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_outcome_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of the mediation session's outcome (Active or Inactive).
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_sign_off** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_sign_off`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the session ended with a sign off.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_id** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_id`, number, required): Enter the unique ID of the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_date** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_time** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_start_time** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_end_time** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_length** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_mediation_session_length`, text, required): Enter the length of the mediation session.
* **mediation_session_discharge_form_participant_agreed** (`mediation_session_discharge_form_participant_agreed`, select_one, required): Select whether the participant agreed to the mediation session.
