# playground_safety_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The playground_safety_feedback_form is designed to collect feedback about the safety and condition of our playgrounds.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section completely, using the following pages as separate templates:
  - Playgrounds 1, 2, 3 (with corresponding "form_header" fields)
  - Please select each page based on the specific playground you are giving feedback about

## Field-by-Field Explanation

1. **form_header** (Required/Optional) - This is a brief introduction to each section.
2. **playground_location** (Required/Optional) - Enter the location of the playground about which you are giving feedback.
3. **rate_the_playground** (Required/Optional) - Rate the safety and condition of the playground (1-5 scale).
4. **playground_comments** (Required/Optional) - Provide more details about your experience at the playground.
5. **name** (Required/Optional) - Enter your name for identification purposes.
6. **email** (Required/Optional) - Enter your email address for contact.
7. **contact_number** (Required/Optional) - Enter your contact number for further communication.
8. **date_of_visit** (Required/Optional) - Enter the date of your visit to the playground.
9. **time_of_visit** (Required/Optional) - Enter the time of your visit to the playground.
10. **form_header1**, **playground_location1**, **rate_the_playground1**, **playground_comments1**, **name1**, **email1**, ...** (Required/Optional) - These are repeat fields for collecting feedback from different playgrounds, fill out each corresponding field for each playground.
 
## Tips

Please fill out each section completely and honestly to provide useful feedback. If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
