# home_setup_service_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from customers who have used the Home Setup Service. Feedback is used to improve the service and provide better experiences for customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter a title for your feedback**: In the "form_title" field, type a brief description of your feedback.
2. Select the **category** of your feedback: You can choose from "Feedback Forms" or "Other" to help us understand the type of feedback you're providing.
3. **Describe your experience**: In the "description" field, type a detailed description of your experience with the Home Setup Service.
4. **Provide your email**: In the "email" field, enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
5. **Enter your phone number**: In the "phone" field, enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
6. **Complete a required field**: In the "Required Field" field, you will be prompted to enter some information. This field is required to be completed before submitting your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **form_title** (text, optional)
Enter a brief title for your feedback.
### **category** (select_one, required)
Choose the category of your feedback. This will help us understand the type of feedback you're providing.
### **description** (text, optional)
Provide a detailed description of your experience with the Home Setup Service.
### **email** (email, optional)
Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
### **phone** (text, optional)
Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
### **Required Field** (text, required)
Please enter some information in this field. This is required to be completed before submitting your feedback.
