# Wedding Ceremony Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from wedding ceremony attendees. It includes questions about the ceremony, such as date, time, location, and type, as well as space for attendees to provide comments or suggestions. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill in the required fields (none of the fields are required, but you can provide any information you like).
3. Select the correct options for each field (e.g. select the correct type of ceremony, the correct organizer, etc.).
4. Provide any comments or suggestions you have for the ceremony.
5. Rate the ceremony (if you choose to).
6. Indicate whether you would recommend this ceremony to others.
7. Enter the date of the next ceremony (if you know it).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback** (`wedding_ceremony_feedback`): This is a text field for you to provide any comments or feedback about the ceremony. You can describe your experience, mention anything that went well or poorly, or suggest ways the ceremony could be improved.
* **Date of Ceremony** (`ceremony_date`): Enter the date of the wedding ceremony you attended.
* **Time of Ceremony** (`ceremony_time`): Enter the time of the wedding ceremony you attended.
* **Location of Ceremony** (`ceremony_location`): Enter the location of the wedding ceremony you attended.
* **Organized By** (`ceremony_organizer`): Select the person or organization that organized the wedding ceremony. Options include:
	+ Partner
	+ Family Member
	+ Other
* **Type of Ceremony** (`ceremony_type`): Select the type of ceremony that took place. Options include:
	+ Civil
	+ Religious
	+ Non-denominational
* **Attended By** (`ceremony_attendance`): Enter how many people attended the ceremony.
* **Comments** (`comments`): This is a text field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the ceremony.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`): This is a text field for you to provide any suggestions for future ceremonies.
* **Rating** (`ceremony_rating`): Rate your experience of the ceremony on a scale of 0-5, with 5 being the highest.
* **Recommend** (`ceremony_recommend`): Indicate whether you would recommend this ceremony to others. Options include:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Next Ceremony** (`ceremony_next_ceremony`): Enter the date of the next ceremony, if you know it.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by attendees of the wedding ceremony. If you are the wedding organizer, you may not need to complete this form.
