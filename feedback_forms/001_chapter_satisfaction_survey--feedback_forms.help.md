# Chapter Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chapter Satisfaction Survey is designed to gather feedback from chapter members on their experiences and perceptions of the chapter's events, leadership, and overall engagement. This survey helps the chapter leadership understand areas that need improvement and identify opportunities for growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and chapter name in the first two fields.
* Select your membership duration from the options provided in the third field.
* Rate your satisfaction with chapter events in the fourth field.
* Choose how often you attend chapter events in the fifth field.
* Evaluate the effectiveness of chapter leadership in the sixth field.
* Assess the quality of chapter communication in the seventh field.
* Indicate your level of engagement with the chapter community in the eighth field.
* If applicable, select all areas that you think need improvement in the ninth field.
* Answer how likely you are to recommend the chapter to others in the tenth field.
* Provide any additional feedback or suggestions in the eleventh field.
* Optionally, provide your email address for follow-up in the twelfth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name (1)** (`member_name`, required): Your full name as a member of the chapter.
* **Chapter Name (2)** (`chapter_name`, required): The name of your local chapter.
* **Membership Duration (3)** (`membership_duration`, required): Select the duration that applies to your membership.
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ 1 to 3 years
	+ 3 to 5 years
	+ More than 5 years
* **Event Satisfaction (4)** (`event_satisfaction`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with chapter events.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Event Frequency (5)** (`event_frequency`, required): Your attendance pattern with chapter events.
	+ Every event
	+ Most events
	+ About half
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Leadership Rating (6)** (`leadership_rating`, required): Evaluate the effectiveness of chapter leadership.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Communication Rating (7)** (`communication_rating`, required): Assess the quality of chapter communication.
	+ Very effective
	+ Effective
	+ Neutral
	+ Ineffective
	+ Very ineffective
* **Community Engagement (8)** (`community_engagement`, required): Indicate your level of engagement with the chapter community.
	+ Very engaged
	+ Engaged
	+ Somewhat engaged
	+ Slightly engaged
	+ Not engaged
* **Improvement Areas (9)** (`improvement_areas`, optional): Select all areas that you think need improvement.
	+ Event quality
	+ Event frequency
	+ Communication
	+ Leadership transparency
	+ Member engagement
	+ Resources and support
	+ Networking opportunities
* **Recommendation (10)** (`recommendation`, required): Answer how likely you are to recommend the chapter to others.
	+ Definitely yes
	+ Probably yes
	+ Not sure
	+ Probably no
	+ Definitely no
* **Additional Feedback (11)** (`additional_feedback`, optional): Share any other feedback or suggestions.
* **Email Address (12)** (`email`, optional): Provide your email address for follow-up if needed.
