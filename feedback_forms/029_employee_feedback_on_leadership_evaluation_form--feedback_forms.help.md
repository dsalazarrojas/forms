# Employee Feedback On Leadership Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the leadership evaluation, focusing on specific areas such as leadership effectiveness, communication skills, and suggestions for improvement. This form provides a safe and anonymous way to share honest and constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the appropriate answer for the leadership effectiveness question by choosing one of the three provided options (Very Good, Average, Fair).
* Choose all that apply for the communication skills question.
* Provide specific feedback on the leadership in the space provided.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the space provided.
* Leave any additional comments in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Leadership Effectiveness** (`leadership_effectiveness`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the effectiveness of our leader in leading the team. Please select the best description that applies to you.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about our leader's ability to communicate effectively with the team.
* **Feedback On Leadership** (`feedback_on_leadership`, text, required: false)
	+ Please share your thoughts and opinions about our leader's performance. Be specific and honest.
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, required: false)
	+ If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in here.
* **Open Comments** (`open_comments`, text, required: false)
	+ Do you have any additional comments or observations? Share them here.

## Tips
* Remember that all feedback is anonymous, and your input is appreciated.
* Be specific and concrete with your feedback.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please ask for clarification.
