# Suggestion Program Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions or feedback from users. It is intended to be a comprehensive tool for submitting ideas, issues, or concerns that can help improve the organization or company. Your input is valuable, and we appreciate your participation in helping us identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For date fields, select the date on which you experienced the issue.
3. When choosing between options (e.g., "Yes" or "No"), select the one that best answers your question.
4. For text fields, enter your response in the space provided.
5. For fields asking for attachments, please upload any relevant files that support your suggestion.
6. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your suggestion? (1)** (`title`, `text`, `required`): This field is where you can enter your idea or feedback. Please provide a clear and concise description of the issue you experienced.
* **When did you experience this issue? (2)** (`date`, `required`): Select the date when you experienced the issue you are reporting.
* **What time of day did you experience this issue? (3)** (`time`, `optional`): If you can remember, please provide the time of day when you experienced the issue.
* **Did you experience a positive or negative issue? (4)** (`select_one`, `required`): Select "Yes, positive" if the issue was a positive experience, or "No, negative" if it was a negative experience.
* **How often would you like to be contacted about this issue? (5)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Indicate how often you would like to be contacted about this issue, such as "Yes, always" or "No, never".
* **How would you rate your confidence in your suggestion? (6)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Rate your confidence in your suggestion with the options provided, such as "Very confident" or "Not at all confident".
* **What would you like to see improved? (7)** (`text`, `required`): Describe what you think could be improved.
* **Do you have any additional details about your suggestion? (8)** (`text`, `required`): If you have more information to share, please enter it here.
* **What do you think is the root cause of the issue? (9)** (`text`, `required`): Try to identify the underlying reason for the issue you experienced.
* **Do you have any suggestions for solutions? (10)** (`text`, `required`): Provide any ideas or suggestions you have for resolving the issue.
* **Would you like to submit this suggestion anonymously? (11)** (`select_one`, `required`): If you'd like to keep your identity hidden, select "Yes".
* **Would you like to receive updates on this issue? (12)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Let us know how often you'd like to receive updates on this issue.
* **How would you like to receive updates? (13)** (`text`, `optional`): Specify your preferred method for receiving updates.
* **Do you have any other comments? (14)** (`text`, `required`): Share any additional thoughts or comments related to your suggestion.
* **Would you like to attach any files to your suggestion? (15)** (`note`, `required`): If you have relevant files, attach them here.
* **Is this suggestion related to an existing issue? (16)** (`select_one`, `required`): If the issue you are reporting is related to an existing problem, select "Yes, existing issue".
* **Do you have any other questions or concerns? (17)** (`text`, `required`): Share any questions or concerns you have about your suggestion.
* **Can we follow up with you about this issue? (18)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): Let us know how often you'd like us to follow up with you about this issue.
