# Presentation Peer Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from peers on presentations. It will help you gauge the effectiveness of the presentation and identify areas for improvement. Fill out this form to provide constructive feedback to the presenter.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide feedback, follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
2.  Fill out the form with your thoughts and opinions.
3.  Make sure to complete all required fields, if any.
4.  If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
5.  Rate the presentation using the Likert scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Presentation Peer Feedback Form** (`presentation_peer_feedback_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form. Please fill out this field with your thoughts about the presentation.
*   **Rate This Presentation** (`presentation_peer_feedback_form_rating`, number, required): Rate the presentation on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest rating and 5 is the highest.
*   **Comments** (`presentation_peer_feedback_form_comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions for the presenter, please provide them here.
*   **Likert Options** (`presentation_peer_feedback_form_likert_options`, select_one, optional): This field is not actually used in the form, it seems like it was an error in the form definition. We will ignore it for now.
*   **Likert Scale** (`presentation_peer_feedback_form_likert_scale`, number, optional): Rate the presentation using the Likert scale. This will help the presenter understand your opinion on the presentation.

## Tips

*   Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
*   Provide specific examples or reasons for your ratings and comments.
*   Keep your feedback focused on the presentation itself, not on the presenter.
*   If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask the presenter for clarification.
