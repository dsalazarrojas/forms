# Kitchen Mystery Shop Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kitchen Mystery Shop Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from customers on their experience at a mystery shop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "Customer information" field.
2. Share your feedback on your experience with the shop in the "Feedback" field.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with your experience on a scale of 1-100.
4. Identify how likely you are to recommend the shop on a scale of 1-5.
5. Rate specific aspects of your experience, including ratings 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, and 100.
6. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Tips

* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Ratings should reflect your actual experience.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them.
* If you are unable to complete any field, please contact the shop's management for assistance.
