# Music Lesson Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from students about their music lessons. The feedback will help music teachers evaluate their performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, follow the steps below:
    *   Fill in the required fields:
        *   What was the most enjoyable part of the lesson? (music_lesson_feedback_form_1)
        *   What was the most challenging part of the lesson? (music_lesson_feedback_form_2)
        *   How would you rate the music teacher? (music_lesson_feedback_form_3)
        *   Do you want to continue taking music lessons with this teacher? (music_lesson_feedback_form_4)
        *   How long were you taking music lessons before this lesson? (music_lesson_feedback_form_6)
    *   Select from the available options:
        *   How would you rate the music teacher? (music_lesson_feedback_form_3)
            +   Excellent
            +   Good
            +   Fair
            +   Poor
        *   Do you want to continue taking music lessons with this teacher? (music_lesson_feedback_form_4)
            +   Yes
            +   No
        *   What instrument do you play? (music_lesson_feedback_form_7)
            +   Piano
            +   Guitar
            +   Drums
            +   Violin
    *   Provide any additional comments or feedback (music_lesson_feedback_form_5) (optional)
    *   Enter your email address (music_lesson_feedback_form_8) (optional)
    *   Enter your phone number (music_lesson_feedback_form_9) (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What was the most enjoyable part of the lesson?** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_1`, text, required)
    +   This field is where you should describe the part of the lesson that you enjoyed the most. Try to be specific and provide as much detail as possible so that the teacher can understand what made the lesson enjoyable for you.
*   **What was the most challenging part of the lesson?** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_2`, text, required)
    +   This field is where you should describe the part of the lesson that was difficult or challenging for you. Try to be specific and provide as much detail as possible so that the teacher can understand what areas they need to improve.
*   **How would you rate the music teacher?** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_3`, select_one, required)
    +   This field is a rating system where you can rate the music teacher based on their performance. You can choose from the following options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Do you want to continue taking music lessons with this teacher?** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_4`, select_multiple, required)
    +   This field is a yes/no question where you can indicate if you want to continue taking music lessons with this teacher.
*   **Additional comments or feedback** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_5`, note, optional)
    +   This field is where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the lesson.
*   **How long were you taking music lessons before this lesson?** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_6`, number, required)
    +   This field is where you should enter the number of months or years you have been taking music lessons before this lesson.
*   **What instrument do you play?** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_7`, select_multiple, optional)
    +   This field is where you can select the instrument you play. You can choose from the following options: Piano, Guitar, Drums, Violin.
*   **Email address** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_8`, email, optional)
    +   This field is where you should enter your email address.
*   **Phone number** (`music_lesson_feedback_form_9`, text, optional)
    +   This field is where you should enter your phone number.
