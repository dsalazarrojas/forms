# Garlic Product Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your opinion on the Garlic product. Your feedback will help us understand how we can improve the product to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with your honest opinions.
2. Make sure to select only one option for "How do you like the product?" and "Satisfaction".
3. You can add comments if you have more to say about the product.
4. Enter a valid email address, if you'd like to receive a response to your feedback.
5. Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted to discuss your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, not required): Tell us the name of the Garlic product you are giving feedback on.
* **How do you like the product?** (`rating`, select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the product. Choose from 1 (Very Satisfied), 2 (Somewhat Satisfied), 3 (Neutral), 4 (Somewhat Dissatisfied), 5 (Very Dissatisfied).
* **What do you think could be improved?** (`feedback`, text, not required): Share any thoughts or suggestions you have for improving the product.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address, if you'd like to receive a response to your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted to discuss your feedback.
* **Satisfaction** (`product_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how satisfied you are with the product. Select from Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): If you have more to say about the product, please add your comments here.
