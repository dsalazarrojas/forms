# Hair Salon Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your thoughts and opinions about our hair salon. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your input. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and share your experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and fill out all the required fields.
2. Choose the answers that best fit your thoughts and experiences.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, use the "feedback" text field.
4. Click the submit button to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Hair Salon Feedback Form** (`form_title`, note, not required)
This is the title of the form. It's just a heading to let you know what this form is about.

### 2. **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required)
Please rate your overall experience with our salon. Select a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how satisfied you are with our service.

### 3. **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, number, not required)
This field is not currently used for anything. You can safely ignore it.

### 4. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required)
Here's the space for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your visit. Please be honest and detailed in your feedback.

### 5. **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, not required)
What do you like most about our salon? Please share your thoughts on what makes us stand out.

### 6. **Improvements** (`improvements`, text, not required)
What do you think we can improve on? Your suggestions will help us provide better service in the future.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your feedback. Your input is valuable to us.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
