# Mission Statement Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mission Statement Collection Form is a user-facing form designed to collect information and feedback from users. It is intended for general use and does not require any specific privileges or authentication. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your mission statement in the **Mission Statement** field.
2.  Enter your **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** (if applicable) in the respective fields.
3.  Select your **Time In** and **Time Out** work hours using the time picker.
4.  Choose a **Date** for your work schedule using the date picker.
5.  In the **Select One** field, choose one of the available options.
6.  In the **Select Multiple** field, choose any number of options you desire.
7.  If you have any additional information or comments, provide them in the **Note** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mission Statement (1)**: A short statement describing your mission. You can type any text up to 255 characters.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name for record keeping purposes.
* **Email (3)**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Time In (5)**: Select your start time for your workday using the time picker.
* **Time Out (6)**: Select your end time for your workday using the time picker.
* **Date (7)**: Choose a date from the calendar.
* **Select One (8)**: Choose one option for this field.
* **Select Multiple (9)**: Choose multiple options for this field, you can select any or all.
* **Note (10)**: Provide any additional comments or information about your mission, if you need to.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you experience any errors while submitting the form, try refreshing the page or clearing the cache.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
