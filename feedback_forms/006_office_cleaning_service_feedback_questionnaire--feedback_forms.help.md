# office_cleaning_service_feedback_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office cleaning service feedback questionnaire is designed to gather information about your experience with our office cleaning services. This form will help us understand your satisfaction with our service quality, timeliness, and overall value. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions thoroughly.
2. For select fields, choose one answer per question.
3. For text fields, type your answer in the provided space.
4. Make sure to submit your completed form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Score 1** (`feedback_score_1`, text, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with our office cleaning service. Provide your honest feedback on how we did.
- **Feedback Score 2** (`feedback_score_2`, text, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with our office cleaning service. Provide your honest feedback on how we did.
- **Feedback Score 3** (`feedback_score_3`, text, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with our office cleaning service. Provide your honest feedback on how we did.
- **Feedback Score 4** (`feedback_score_4`, text, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with our office cleaning service. Provide your honest feedback on how we did.
- **Feedback Score 5** (`feedback_score_5`, text, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with our office cleaning service. Provide your honest feedback on how we did.
- **Service Quality** (`service_quality`, select_one, required/optional): How satisfied are you with our service quality? Please choose from the options below:
  - Excellent
  - Good
  - Fair
  - Poor
- **Service Value** (`service_value`, select_multiple, required/optional): How valuable did you find our service? Please choose from the options below:
  - Very valuable
  - Moderately valuable
  - Not valuable at all
  - Somewhat valuable
- **Service Timeliness** (`service_timeliness`, select_multiple, required/optional): How timely was your service experience? Please choose from the options below:
  - Very timely
  - Moderately timely
  - Not timely at all
  - Somewhat timely
- **When was it?** (`service_timeliness_hint_1`, time, required/optional): This field is not visible to the user, it is just a hint field to collect additional information about service timeliness. If you need to provide more information, type it in this field.
