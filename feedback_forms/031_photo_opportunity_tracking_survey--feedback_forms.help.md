# photo_opportunity_tracking_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo opportunity tracking survey is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users about their photo sessions. This form helps us understand the quality of our service and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your feedback about your recent photo session in the "session_feedback" field.
2. Enter the name of the photographer who handled your session in the "photographer_name" field.
3. Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest, in the "rating" field.
4. Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your session in the "comments" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions.
6. If applicable, enter the ID of your photo session in the "photo_session_id" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, optional): Enter your feedback about your recent photo session. This can be anything from your overall experience to specific moments that stood out to you.
* **Photographer's Name** (`photographer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the photographer who handled your session. This helps us track who delivered the service and gather feedback about their performance.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest. This helps us understand the overall quality of our service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your session. This can be anything from suggestions to concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with any follow-up questions or to discuss your feedback in more detail.
* **Photo Session ID** (`photo_session_id`, number, optional): If you have a photo session ID, enter it here. This helps us track your session and gather feedback specific to it.
