# Website User Acceptance Testing Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website User Acceptance Testing Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with the website. This feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments to enhance the overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "What was your overall impression of the website?" with your honest opinion. This will help us understand your first impression of the website.
2. If you encountered any issues during your visit, describe them in "If yes, please describe the issue". This is crucial for us to identify and fix problems that hinder a smooth user experience.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the website on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. This will give us an idea of how well we met your expectations.
4. If you have any suggestions for improvement, feel free to share them in "Do you have any suggestions for improvement?". This is your chance to provide us with ideas on how we can make the website better.
5. Finally, if you want to provide any additional comments or feedback, use "Do you want to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience?".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was your overall impression of the website?** (`usertestingusability`, text, required): This is a open-ended question that asks for your initial opinion on the website. Please be honest and share your thoughts.
* **Did you experience any issues during your visit?** (`userbugreport`, text, required): This question is to find out if you encountered any problems while using the website. If yes, please describe the issue in the next field.
* **If yes, please describe the issue** (`userbugreport_1`, text, required): This field is for you to elaborate on the problem you faced.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the website?** (`userusatisfy`, number, required): This question asks you to rate your satisfaction with the website on a scale of 1-5. The rating will help us understand how well we met your expectations.
* **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`usersuggestimprovement`, text, optional): This is your chance to share any ideas you have on how we can improve the website.
* **Describe your suggestion** (`userbugreport_1`, text, optional): If you have an idea for improvement, please elaborate on it in this field.
* **Do you want to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience?** (`usertestingfeedback`, text, optional): If you have anything else you'd like to share or discuss, use this field.
