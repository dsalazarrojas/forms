# Meeting Assistant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Assistant Feedback Form is a tool to gather feedback from users who have attended meetings with meeting assistants. This form will help us identify potential issues with the meeting assistant's performance and suggest improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Provide your Email (if you have one).
3. Enter the name of your Company.
4. Select the Meeting Date you attended.
5. Choose the Meeting Time you attended.
6. Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting assistant's performance (1-5).
7. Choose how well you were satisfied with the meeting assistant's performance.
8. Select the Meeting Attendees you interacted with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you represent.
* **Meeting Date**: Select the date of the meeting you attended using the date picker.
* **Meeting Time**: Choose the time of the meeting you attended using the time picker.
* **Rating**: Rate your satisfaction with the meeting assistant's performance (1-5).
* **Rating Scale**: Select how well you were satisfied with the meeting assistant's performance using the five-point scale.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Meeting Attendees**: Select all meeting attendees you interacted with from the dropdown list.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use only your own experiences, and avoid suggesting changes or improvements based on others' opinions.
* If you need help, feel free to contact us.
