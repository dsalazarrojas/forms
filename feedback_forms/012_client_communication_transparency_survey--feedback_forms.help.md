# Client Communication Transparency Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is meant to be used by clients and prospects to provide feedback on the communication practices of our organization. The survey aims to gauge the clarity, accessibility, and timeliness of our messages, as well as the overall transparency and openness of our communication channels. By completing this survey, you will help us identify areas for improvement and provide us with valuable insights to refine our communication strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, Video Call, In Person, or Mail) in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
2. Choose how often you would like us to contact you (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Only When Necessary) in the "Preferred Contact Frequency" field.
3. Select all the communication channels we have used in the "Communication Channels Used" field.
4. Identify the most effective communication channel for you (Email, Phone, Text Messages, Video Conferencing, Client Portal, In Person Meetings, or Other) in the "Most Effective Communication Channel" field.
5. Indicate how well you understand the information provided (Completely Understand, Mostly Understand, Somewhat Understand, Slightly Understand, or Do Not Understand) in the "Understanding of Information" field.
6. Rate our explanation of technical language (Always Explained, Usually Explained, Sometimes Explained, Rarely Explained, Never Explained) in the "Technical Language Explanation" field.
7. Select areas where we can improve our communication (Response Time, Clarity of Messages, Frequency of Updates, Availability of Staff, Online Resources, Written Documentation, Follow-Up Communication, or Other).
8. Indicate what we do well in our communication (Client Portal, Email, Phone, Text Messages, Video Conferencing, In Person Meetings, or Other).
9. Add any additional comments or feedback you may have about our communication practices.
10. Select how likely you are to recommend our communication practices to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, or Definitely No).
11. Finally, select a date for this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`respondent_first_name`, text, optional): Provide your first name for follow-up purposes.
- **Last Name** (`respondent_last_name`, text, optional): Provide your last name for follow-up purposes.
- **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address for survey results.
- **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, required): Select your relationship with us (Current Client, Former Client, Prospective Client, Partner Organization, or Other).
- **How Long Have You Been a Client?** (`service_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long you have been a client (Less than 1 month, 1-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or Over 5 years).
- **Rate the Clarity of Our Communications** (`communication_clarity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the clarity of our messages (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
- **How Easy Is It to Find Information?** (`information_accessibility`, select_one, required): Choose how easily you can find information (Very Easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, or Very Difficult).
- **Satisfaction With Response Times** (`response_time_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Choose how quickly we respond (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
- **Rate Our Transparency and Openness** (`transparency_rating`, select_one, required): Rate our transparency and openness (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor).
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, Video Call, In Person, Mail, or Client Portal).
- **Preferred Contact Frequency** (`contact_frequency_preference`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like us to contact you (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Only When Necessary).
- **Communication Channels Used** (`communication_channels_used`, select_multiple, required): Select all the communication channels we have used (Email, Phone, Text Messages, Video Conferencing, Client Portal, Mobile App, Social Media, In Person Meetings, Mail).
- **Most Effective Communication Channel** (`channel_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Choose the most effective communication channel for you (Email, Phone, Text Messages, Video Conferencing, Client Portal, In Person Meetings, or Other).
- **How Well Do You Understand the Information Provided?** (`information_understanding`, select_one, required): Indicate how well you understand the information provided (Completely Understand, Mostly Understand, Somewhat Understand, Slightly Understand, or Do Not Understand).
- **Is Technical Language Explained Clearly?** (`jargon_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how well we explain technical language (Always Explained, Usually Explained, Sometimes Explained, Rarely Explained, Never Explained, or Not Applicable).
- **What Could We Improve?** (`communication_improvements`, select_multiple, required): Select areas where we can improve our communication (Response Time, Clarity of Messages, Frequency of Updates, Availability of Staff, Online Resources, Written Documentation, Follow-Up Communication, or Other).
- **What Do We Do Well?** (`communication_strengths`, text, optional): Add a brief description of our communication strengths.
- **Any Other Thoughts?** (`additional_feedback`, text, optional): Add any other comments or feedback you may have about our communication practices.
- **Would You Recommend Our Communication Practices?** (`recommend_communication`, select_one, required): Select how likely you are to recommend our communication practices to others (Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Neutral, Probably No, or Definitely No).
- **Survey Completion Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): Select a date for this survey.
