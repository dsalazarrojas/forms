# invoice_transparency_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users about our invoicing process. Your input will help us understand where we can improve and provide better service to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the "How do you feel about our invoicing process?" question and provide your answer in the text field.
2. Select all that apply for "Do you have any concerns about the transparency of our invoicing process?"
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the text field.
4. If you would like to reach out to us, provide your email address and contact number.
5. Choose the date and time when you would like to be contacted.
6. Leave any additional comments or notes in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How do you feel about our invoicing process?** (`invoice_transparency_feedback`, text, optional)
	* This field is a text input where you can provide your honest opinion about our invoicing process.
- **Do you have any concerns about the transparency of our invoicing process?** (`transparency_issues`, select_multiple, optional)
	* This field is a dropdown menu with options to select any concerns you may have about our invoicing process.
- **Do you have any suggestions for improvement?** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional)
	* This field is a text input where you can provide any suggestions you have for improving our service.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
	* This field is where you can enter your email address if you would like to be contacted.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
	* This field is where you can enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional)
	* This field is where you can select a date when you would like to be contacted.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional)
	* This field is where you can select a time when you would like to be contacted.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional)
	* This field is a text input where you can leave any additional comments or notes.
- **Name** (`name`, text, optional)
	* This field is a text input where you can enter your name.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional)
	* This field is where you can enter your contact number.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional)
	* This field is where you can enter your email address.
- **Message** (`message`, note, optional)
	* This field is a text input where you can leave any additional comments or messages.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide your genuine feedback.
* Please keep your responses respectful and professional.
* If you have any concerns, you can select all that apply.
* If you would like to be contacted, please provide your email address and contact number.
* Please note that your responses will be kept confidential.
