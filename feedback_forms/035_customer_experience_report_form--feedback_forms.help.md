# Customer Experience Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather customer feedback and experiences with our company. By filling out this form, you can help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Each field is designed to gather specific information that helps us improve our customer experience.
2. Be sure to provide your genuine feedback in the free-form field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your name
**Customer Experience Report Form 1**
* This is a field to collect your name for identification purposes. Simply type in your name and proceed to the next fields.

### How would you rate your overall experience with our company
**Customer Experience Report Form 2 (Scale: 1-5)**
* Please rate your experience using the provided scale of 1-5. One means a very bad experience, while 5 is an excellent experience.

### How likely are you to recommend our company to a friend or colleague
**Customer Experience Report Form 3**
* Choose from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) that reflects your likelihood to recommend our company.

### What is your contact information
**Customer Experience Report Form 4**
* Enter your email address where we can contact you for further assistance or to follow up on your feedback.

### How can we contact you?
**Customer Experience Report Form 5**
* Choose how you'd like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, mail, etc.).

### How can we assist you further?
**Customer Experience Report Form 6**
* Choose a time that you're available for further assistance. This will help us schedule a follow-up if needed.

### Do you have any comments or suggestions?
**Field 7**
* This is a free-form field where you can share any additional comments or suggestions you have for our company.

### Do you have any other comments or suggestions?
**Customer Experience Report Form 8**
* Similar to the previous field, use this to share any other comments or suggestions you may have.
