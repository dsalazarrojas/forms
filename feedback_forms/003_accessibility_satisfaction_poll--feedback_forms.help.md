# Accessibility Satisfaction Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback from respondents about their experience with accessibility features in a given facility or service. Your input will help improve the accessibility and usability of the service for all users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the form.
2. Fill out your contact information (full name and email) in section 1.
3. In section 2, select your role at the facility or service.
4. Answer the questions about your experience with accessibility features.
5. For questions with select options, choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
6. If you have any additional comments or feedback, type them in the final section.
7. Click on "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (Optional)** (`full_name`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your full name, or you can choose to remain anonymous.
* **Email Address (Optional)** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): If you want to receive a summary of results, provide your email address.
* **Your Role** (`user_role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role at the facility or service (Employee, Visitor, Customer, Student, Resident, Other).
* **Do You Have Specific Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `select_one`, required): Answer if you have specific accessibility needs (True, False, Prefer not to say).
* **Overall Satisfaction with Accessibility Features** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with accessibility features (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Satisfaction with Physical Accessibility (ramps, elevators, signage)** (`physical_accessibility`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with physical accessibility features (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, Not Applicable).
* **Satisfaction with Digital Accessibility (website, apps, documents)** (`digital_accessibility`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with digital accessibility features (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, Not Applicable).
* **Satisfaction with Communication Accessibility (signage, announcements, interpreters)** (`communication_accessibility`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with communication accessibility features (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, Not Applicable).
* **How Helpful Was Staff in Addressing Accessibility Needs** (`staff_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required): Rate the helpfulness of staff in addressing your accessibility needs (Very Helpful, Helpful, Neutral, Unhelpful, Very Unhelpful, Did not interact with staff).
* **Did You Encounter Any Accessibility Barriers** (`barriers_encountered`, `select_multiple`, required): List any accessibility barriers you encountered during your visit.
* **What Accessibility Improvements Would You Like to See** (`improvement_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Share any suggestions for improving accessibility features.
* **Would You Recommend This Facility or Service to Others with Accessibility Needs** (`recommend_to_others`, `select_one`, required): Answer if you would recommend the facility or service to others with accessibility needs (Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably not, Definitely not).
* **Additional Comments or Feedback** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any other thoughts about your accessibility experience.
