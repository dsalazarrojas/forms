# Seasonal Inventory Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seasonal Inventory Feedback Questionnaire is designed to gather customer insights on seasonal products and services. Your feedback will help the business improve their inventory management and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly based on your experiences with the business's seasonal products.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. If you're unsure about any question or need clarification, feel free to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think of our seasonal products** (1, text, required): Share your thoughts and opinions about the seasonal products offered by the business.
* **How likely are you to recommend our seasonal products to a friend or family member** (2, select_one, required): Choose how likely you are to recommend the business's seasonal products to someone you trust.
* **What did you use from our seasonal products** (3, select_multiple, required): Select all the products you've used from the business's seasonal offerings.
* **When did you last purchase our seasonal products** (4, date, optional): If you've made a recent purchase, enter the date of the last purchase. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **What is your age** (5, number, required): Enter your age to help the business understand their customer demographics.
* **What is your email** (6, email, required): Enter your email address to allow the business to reach out to you if they need more information.
* **What is your phone number** (7, text, required): Enter your phone number to ensure you can be reached for follow-up questions.
* **How likely are you to recommend our seasonal products** (8, select_one, required): Choose how likely you are to recommend the business's seasonal products.
* **What did you like or dislike about our seasonal products** (9, text, optional): Share your positive and negative thoughts about the business's seasonal products.
* **What would you suggest we do with our seasonal products** (10, text, optional): Offer suggestions for improvement or changes you'd like to see in the business's seasonal offerings.
* **When did you last purchase our seasonal products** (11, time, optional): If you've made a recent purchase, enter the time of the last purchase. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Seasonal Inventory Feedback Questionnaire** (12, note, required): This is a mandatory field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments.
* **The Seasonal Inventory Feedback Questionnaire helps businesses gather customer insights on seasonal products to improve inventory management and customer satisfaction** (13, note, optional): This is a note to remind you of the purpose of the questionnaire.
* **What do you think of our customer service** (14, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the business's customer service.
* **What do you think of our seasonal products 2** (15, text, optional): This is a repeat of the first question to allow for further feedback.
* **When did you last purchase our seasonal products 2** (16, date, optional): If you've made a recent purchase, enter the date of the last purchase. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **What is your favorite seasonal product** (17, text, optional): Share your favorite seasonal product from the business.
* **What is your least favorite seasonal product** (18, text, optional): Share your least favorite seasonal product from the business.
* **When do you plan to purchase our seasonal products** (19, date, optional): Enter the date when you plan to make a purchase from the business.
* **How likely are you to purchase our seasonal products 2** (20, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to make a purchase from the business.
* **Seasonal Inventory Feedback Questionnaire** (21, note, optional): This is a repeat of the title to help you remember the purpose of the form.
* **How likely are you to recommend our seasonal products 2** (22, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to recommend the business's seasonal products.
* **The Seasonal Inventory Feedback Questionnaire helps businesses gather customer insights on seasonal products 2** (23, note, optional): This is a repeat of the purpose of the questionnaire.
* **What do you think of our seasonal products 2** (24, text, optional): Share your thoughts on the business's seasonal products.
* **What is your feedback** (25, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments about the business's seasonal products.

## Tips
* Answer the questions based on your real experiences with the business's seasonal products.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers.
* Don't hesitate to provide any additional feedback or comments in the open-ended questions.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask.
