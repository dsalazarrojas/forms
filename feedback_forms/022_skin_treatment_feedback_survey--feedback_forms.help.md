# Skin Treatment Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skin Treatment Feedback Survey is a form designed to collect feedback on users' experiences with skin treatments. Your input will help us improve our services and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow these steps:
  - Read each page carefully and fill in the relevant information for each field.
  - For select fields, choose one or multiple options that best describe your experience.
  - Make sure to answer all fields, especially those marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Survey** (`Feedback Survey`, text, required):
  This field is a text box where you can provide a detailed description of your skin treatment experience.
* **Treatment Experience** (`Treatment Experience`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose one or multiple options that best describe your experience with the skin treatment: Good, Very Good, Excellent, or Other (please describe "Other" in the text field above).
* **Satisfaction Level** (`Satisfaction Level`, select_one, optional):
  Select one option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the skin treatment: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied, or Other (please describe "Other" in the text field above).
* **Skin Type** (`Skin Type`, select_one, optional):
  Choose the best description of your skin type: Normal, Sensitive, Oily, Combination, Dry, or Other (please describe "Other" in the text field above).
* **Recommended Products** (`Recommended Products`, select_multiple, optional):
  Select one or multiple products you would recommend for your skin type from the list provided: Product 1, Product 2, Product 3, or Other (please describe "Other" in the text field above).
* **Treatment Area** (`Treatment Area`, select_multiple, optional):
  Choose one or multiple areas of the skin that were treated: Face, Neck, Hands, or Other (please describe "Other" in the text field above).

## Tips
- Answer all fields carefully and honestly to help us improve our services.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Feedback Survey" field.
- If you select "Other" for any field, please provide a brief explanation in the corresponding text field.
