# Report Writing Workshop Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Report Writing Workshop Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from participants on the quality and effectiveness of the workshop. Your input will help the organizers refine the workshop content, format, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select one of the options for the Participant rating and Overall satisfaction questions.
2. If you have any specific feedback on what we got right or missed, please type it in the corresponding text fields.
3. You can also provide any additional comments or suggestions in the Additional comments field.
4. Enter your Email address so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
5. Optionally, you can provide your Phone number for future communication.
6. Finally, please enter your Name to help us identify your organization or institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant rating** (`participant_rating`, select_one, required): Please select one of the options to rate your overall experience with the workshop. This will help us gauge the workshop's effectiveness.
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Indicate your satisfaction level with the workshop. This will help us adjust the content and format to better meet your needs.
* **What did we get right** (`what_did_we_get_right`, text, optional): If you feel we got something right, please describe what specifically worked well for you.
* **What did we miss** (`what_did_we_miss`, text, optional): If you feel we missed something important, please share your thoughts so we can improve.
* **What did we get wrong** (`what_did_we_get_wrong`, text, optional): If you feel we made any mistakes or did something wrong, please describe what we could do differently in the future.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any additional feedback or suggestions you have for the workshop are welcome.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your Email address so we can contact you for further clarification or follow-up.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your Name to help us identify your organization or institution.
