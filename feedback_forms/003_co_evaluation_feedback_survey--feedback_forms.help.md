# Co Evaluation Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on a colleague's performance and behavior, specifically in areas such as communication, teamwork, and problem-solving. It is meant to be completed by an individual who has worked with the colleague being evaluated, and the feedback will be used to help the colleague grow and improve in their role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps above to ensure you are providing feedback on a specific colleague and that you are not duplicating your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Your Name** (required): Enter your name to attribute your feedback.
*   **Your Email** (required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Colleague Name** (required): Enter the full name of the colleague being evaluated.
*   **How long have you worked together?** (required): Select how long you have worked together.
*   **Communication Skills** (required): Rate the colleague's communication skills.
*   **Teamwork and Cooperation** (required): Rate the colleague's ability to work effectively with others.
*   **Reliability and Dependability** (required): Rate the colleague's consistency in meeting commitments.
*   **Problem Solving Ability** (required): Rate the colleague's approach to handling challenges.
*   **Initiative and Leadership** (required): Rate the colleague's willingness to take on responsibilities.
*   **What are their key strengths?** (required): Describe the colleague's key strengths.
*   **Areas for Development** (optional): Identify areas where the colleague could improve.
*   **Specific Examples** (optional): Share concrete examples of collaboration or communication.
*   **Recommendations for Growth** (optional): Offer advice for growth and development.
*   **Survey Date** (required): Record the date of completion.
*   **This feedback will be kept confidential** (required): Verify that the feedback will be kept confidential.

## Tips

*   Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
*   Focus on specific behaviors or actions.
*   Consider multiple perspectives before submitting your feedback.
*   Use this form as an opportunity to help the colleague grow and improve.
*   Remember that feedback is a valuable resource for growth and development.
