# Neighborhood Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from residents about their experience living in a particular neighborhood. Your responses will help us understand their satisfaction and feelings about the neighborhood, which will aid in making informed decisions to improve its quality of life.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and briefly.
2. Choose the most suitable option for each question.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments" field.
4. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) if you want to be contacted for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Neighborhood name** (text): Enter the name of the neighborhood where you live.
* **Category** (select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you are a current resident of the neighborhood, 'No' otherwise.
* **How often do you visit this neighborhood?** (select_multiple): Answer 'Yes' if you frequently visit the neighborhood for various reasons, or 'No' if you do not visit often.
* **How likely are you to recommend this neighborhood?** (select_one): Answer 'Yes' if you would recommend the neighborhood to others, or 'No' if you wouldn't.
* **What is the cleanliness of the neighborhood?** (select_multiple): Choose 'Yes' for clean and well-maintained facilities, or 'No' for dirty or poorly maintained facilities.
* **How likely is it that you feel safe here?** (select_one): Answer 'Yes' if you feel secure and safe in the neighborhood, or 'No' if you do not.
* **How likely is it that you'll move here again?** (select_one): Choose 'Yes' if you would consider moving back to the neighborhood, or 'No' if you wouldn't.
* **How likely is it that you'll recommend this neighborhood?** (select_one): Answer 'Yes' if you would recommend the neighborhood to others, or 'No' if you wouldn't.
* **Additional comments** (note): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the neighborhood.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address so we can contact you for follow-up or for any additional information.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
Please be honest and clear in your responses. Your feedback is invaluable to us and will help us make informed decisions to improve the neighborhood.
