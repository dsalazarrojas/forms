# online_survey_customization_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the user to customize an online survey. You will be asked to provide the name of the organization, desired features, format, contact information, and other details. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure the survey meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the organization for which this form is intended
2. Choose the desired features of the survey
3. Select the desired format of the survey
4. Enter your contact information
5. Enter any additional feedback you may have
6. Fill out the form with the required information

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which organization is this form for?** (`organization`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the organization that this form is intended for.
* **Desired features** (`features`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the features that you would like to have in your survey, separated by commas or on separate lines. You can choose from:
	+ Feature A
	+ Feature B
	+ Feature C
* **Desired format** (`format`, select_one, required: false): Select the format you would like your survey to be in:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your survey.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about your survey.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like the survey to be created on.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Select the time you would like the survey to be created on.

## Tips
* Please be accurate when filling out the form to ensure the survey meets your needs.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us for clarification.
