# Community Suggestions And Solutions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect suggestions and solutions from community members, allowing us to understand their needs and concerns. It helps us gather information about issues and ideas from various categories, such as public safety, transportation, and parks and recreation. By submitting your suggestions, you contribute to the improvement of our community.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number (optional).
2. Select the neighborhood or area you live in.
3. Choose the most relevant category for your suggestion.
4. Provide the specific location of the issue or suggestion.
5. Write a brief title for your suggestion and describe it in detail.
6. Propose a solution and indicate its urgency level.
7. Specify who is affected by the issue.
8. (If applicable) Describe the specific group affected and provide more details.
9. Indicate how often the issue occurs.
10. If you have reported this issue before, describe the outcome of your previous report.
11. If you have supporting documents or photos, let us know.
12. Indicate your willingness to help implement the solution.
13. Choose your preferred contact method for follow-up.
14. Add any additional comments or suggestions.
15. Finally, submit your suggestion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (`resident_name`, text, optional): Your name is optional, but if you provide it, we'll be able to contact you for follow-up on your suggestion.
* **Email Address** (`resident_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your suggestion.
* **Phone Number** (`resident_phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Your Neighborhood or Area** (`neighborhood`, select_one, required): Choose the neighborhood or area where you live in the community.
* **What category does your suggestion fall under?** (`suggestion_category`, select_one, required): Select the most relevant category for your suggestion.
* **Specific Location of Issue or Suggestion** (`issue_location`, text, required): Provide the street address, intersection, or landmark where the issue or suggestion is located.
* **Brief Title for Your Suggestion** (`issue_title`, text, required): Summarize your suggestion in a few words.
* **Detailed Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the issue or suggestion in detail.
* **What solution do you propose?** (`proposed_solution`, text, required): Propose a solution for the issue or suggestion.
* **How urgent is this matter?** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Choose the priority level for your suggestion (Low, Medium, High, or Critical).
* **Who does this affect?** (`affected_population`, select_multiple, required): Select all the groups that are affected by the issue or suggestion.
* **If Specific Group, please describe** (`affected_population_detail`, text, optional): If you selected "Specific Group", provide more details about who is affected.
* **How often does this issue occur?** (`frequency_of_issue`, select_one, required): Choose how often the issue occurs (One-time, Rarely, Occasionally, Frequently, Daily, or Constantly).
* **Have you reported this before?** (`previous_reports`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have reported this issue before.
* **If yes, what was the outcome?** (`previous_report_details`, text, optional): Describe the outcome of your previous report.
* **Do others in the community support this suggestion?** (`community_support`, select_one, optional): Indicate the level of community support for your suggestion.
* **Do you have photos or documents to support your suggestion?** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you have supporting materials for your suggestion.
* **Would you be willing to help implement this solution?** (`willing_to_help`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd be willing to help implement the solution.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method you prefer for us to contact you.
* **Any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions you'd like to share.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): The current date will be automatically filled in when you submit the form.
