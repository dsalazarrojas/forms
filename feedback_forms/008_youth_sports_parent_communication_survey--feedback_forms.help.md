# Youth Sports Parent Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from parents of youth athletes on how effectively you are communicated with by your child's team or league. The information collected will help sports organizations and coaches tailor their communication approach to better engage with parents and provide a more satisfying experience for everyone involved. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Start by reading this help guide**, to understand the purpose and each field's meaning.
2. Fill out the form by selecting or typing into the corresponding fields.
3. Submit your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Communication Quality** (`Parent Communication Quality`, `text`, **required: false**): Please briefly describe how you feel about the communication from your child's team or league.
* **How Often Would You Prefer To Communicate With Your Child'S Team Or League** (`How Often Would You Prefer To Communicate With Your Child'S Team Or League`, `select_one`, **required: false**):
	+ Daily: Would like to receive daily updates or messages about your child's progress.
	+ Weekly: Would like to receive weekly updates or messages about your child's progress.
	+ Bi-Weekly: Would like to receive bi-weekly updates or messages about your child's progress.
* **What Can Be Done To Improve Parent Communication** (`What Can Be Done To Improve Parent Communication`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**):
	+ Improved Communication: Better and more regular communication from coaches and staff.
	+ Clearer Information: More detailed and accurate information about your child's progress and team news.
	+ Increased Updates: More frequent updates on your child's schedule, progress, and team news.
* **Any Additional Feedback** (`Any Additional Feedback`, `note`, **required: false`): Please provide any extra comments or suggestions you may have.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `email`, **required: false`): Please provide your email address to receive responses and further communication from us. 

Note: All fields are optional, you can answer as much or as little as you feel comfortable with.
