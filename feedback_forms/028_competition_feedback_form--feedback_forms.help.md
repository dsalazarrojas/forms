# Competition Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for peer evaluation of competitors in a competition, allowing assessors to provide feedback on a competitor's performance, skills, and overall impression. The form aims to gather specific, constructive feedback that can be used to support the competitor's growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name or ID in the "Your Name or ID" field to identify yourself as the assessor.
2. Enter the full name of the competitor being evaluated in the "Competitor Being Evaluated" field.
3. Select the ratings for each skill area (Technical Proficiency and Accuracy, Innovation and Creativity, Presentation and Delivery, and Overall Performance) using the scale provided.
4. In the "What was the competitor's greatest strength?" field, provide specific examples of the competitor's strengths.
5. In the "One specific area for development" field, suggest one area for the competitor to focus on for improvement.
6. Indicate your confidence level in the assessment in the "How confident are you in this assessment?" field.
7. Finally, select the date of evaluation from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name or ID** (`evaluator_identifier`, text, required): Enter your name or assigned ID to identify yourself as the assessor.
* **Competitor Being Evaluated** (`competitor_to_evaluate`, text, required): Enter the full name of the competitor being evaluated.
* **Technical Proficiency and Accuracy** (`technical_skill_rating_peer`, select_one, required): Rate the competitor's technical proficiency and accuracy on a scale of 1 (Needs Significant Improvement) to 5 (Exceptional).
* **Innovation and Creativity** (`innovation_creativity_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the competitor's innovation and creativity on a scale of 1 (Not Innovative) to 5 (Highly Innovative).
* **Presentation and Delivery** (`presentation_skill_rating_peer`, select_one, required): Rate the competitor's presentation and delivery on a scale of 1 (Poor Delivery) to 5 (Professional Delivery).
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_performance_rating_peer`, select_one, required): Rate the competitor's overall performance on a scale of 1 (Below Average) to 5 (Top 10%).
* **What was the competitor's greatest strength?** (`peer_strengths_comments`, text, required): Provide specific examples of the competitor's strengths.
* **One specific area for development** (`peer_improvement_suggestions`, text, required): Suggest one area for the competitor to focus on for improvement.
* **How confident are you in this assessment?** (`evaluator_confidence_level`, select_one, required): Indicate your confidence level in the assessment on a scale of Not Confident, Somewhat Confident, or Very Confident.
* **Date of Evaluation** (`evaluation_completion_date`, date, required): Select the date of evaluation from the calendar.
