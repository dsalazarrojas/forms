# Organic Social Media Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Organic Social Media Feedback" form is used to gather customer feedback on their experience with our company's social media presence. This form will help us understand what we are doing well and where we can improve to better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the form with your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address to help us reach out to you for any follow-up questions or actions.
3. Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you if needed.
4. Provide your feedback on your experience with our social media presence. Be as detailed as possible.
5. Rate your experience from Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Bad.
6. Select all categories that apply to your feedback (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
7. You can add any additional comments to provide more information.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter your first name, as you would like it to be displayed on feedback reports.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required: false): Enter your last name, as you would like it to be displayed on feedback reports.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, Text, Required: false): Provide detailed feedback on your experience with our social media presence.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Select One, Required: true): Rate your experience from Excellent, Good, Neutral, or Bad.
* **Category** (`category`, Select Multiple, Required: true): Select all categories that apply to your feedback (Positive, Negative, or Neutral).
* **Comment** (`comment`, Text, Required: false): Add any additional comments or information to help us better understand your feedback.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Date, Required: false): This is a timestamp of when you submit your feedback.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required: false): This is the time you spent providing feedback.

Tips:
* Please be honest and as detailed as possible in your feedback.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to contact us.
* You can only complete this form once a week on designated times.
