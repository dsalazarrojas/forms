# Fortune Teller Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is for customers to provide feedback about the services they have received from our Fortune Teller. The feedback form collects information about the service received and any additional feedback they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Service Received**: Please enter the name of the service you received from our Fortune Teller.
2. **Reader Name**: If you have received feedback from a specific Fortune Teller, please enter their name here.
3. **Rating**: Provide your rating of the service received, with the number indicating the level of satisfaction. Please note that ratings are on a scale of 1-5.
4. **Rating Value**: If you have provided a rating, please enter the corresponding value here. For example, if your rating is 5, you should enter 5 here.
5. **Feedback**: Share any additional feedback or comments you have about the service received.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Received** (`service_received`, text, optional): Enter the name of the service you received from our Fortune Teller.
* **Reader Name** (`reader_name`, text, optional): If you have received feedback from a specific Fortune Teller, enter their name here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Provide your rating of the service received, with the number indicating the level of satisfaction.
* **Rating Value** (`rating_value`, number, optional): Enter the corresponding value for the rating you provided. For example, if your rating is 5, enter 5 here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional feedback or comments you have about the service received.

## Tips

* Please note that all fields are optional, but providing feedback is encouraged to help us improve our services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us serve you better in the future.
