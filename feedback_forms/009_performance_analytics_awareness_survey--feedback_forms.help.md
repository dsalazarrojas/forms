# Performance Analytics Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Analytics Awareness Survey is a tool used to gather information about team or individual performance data. It's designed to help teams and individuals track and analyze their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out each field accurately and truthfully to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to select the correct options for each field that apply to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`note`, required: false): This field is a general introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and context of the survey.

### kpis
* **kpis** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) that are relevant to your team or individual performance. Please choose all that apply.

### reports
* **reports** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the types of reports that are relevant to your team or individual performance. Please choose all that apply.

### kpi_understanding
* **kpi_understanding** (`text`, required: false): Provide a brief explanation of your understanding of the KPIs selected in the previous field.

### analytics_tool
* **analytics_tool** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the analytics tool that you are using for performance tracking. Please choose one option.

### submission_tracking
* **submission_tracking** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the methods of submission tracking that you are using. Please choose all that apply.

### kpi_goal_target
* **kpi_goal_target** (`number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the target value for your KPI goal.

### submission_frequency
* **submission_frequency** (`number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the frequency of submissions.

### analytics_goal
* **analytics_goal** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, provide a brief explanation of your analytics goal.

### reporting_period
* **reporting_period** (`date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the reporting period.

### reporting_frequency
* **reporting_frequency** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, provide a brief explanation of your reporting frequency.

### analytics_frequency
* **analytics_frequency** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, provide a brief explanation of your analytics frequency.

### reporting_status
* **reporting_status** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the status of your reporting. Please choose one option.

### reporting_delay
* **reporting_delay** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the reason for any reporting delay. Please choose one option.

### reporting_method
* **reporting_method** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select the methods of reporting that you are using. Please choose all that apply.

### reporting_comments
* **reporting_comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your reporting process.

### kpi_target
* **kpi_target** (`number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the target value for your KPI.

### kpi_actual
* **kpi_actual** (`number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the actual value for your KPI.

### submission_comments
* **submission_comments** (`text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your submission process.

### submission_delay
* **submission_delay** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the reason for any submission delay. Please choose one option.

### reporting_frequency_actual
* **reporting_frequency_actual** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, provide a brief explanation of your actual reporting frequency.

### reporting_frequency_goal
* **reporting_frequency_goal** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, provide a brief explanation of your goal for reporting frequency.

### submission_frequency_actual
* **submission_frequency_actual** (`text`, required: false): If applicable, provide a brief explanation of your actual submission frequency.

### analytics_goal_actual
* **analytics_goal_actual** (`number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the actual value for your analytics goal.

### analytics_delay
* **analytics_delay** (`number`, required: false): If applicable, enter the actual delay in analytics.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.
* Remember to provide accurate and truthful information to ensure the reliability of the data collected.
