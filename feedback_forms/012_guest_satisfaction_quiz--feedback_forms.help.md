# Guest Satisfaction Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Satisfaction Quiz is a form designed to help us understand your experience with our hotel. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and provide the best possible stay for our guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your honest opinion.
2. Rate your experience with the options provided.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please feel free to share them in the text box at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to our Quiz** (`intro`, `note`, required: false): This is a brief introduction to our quiz. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How would you rate your overall experience** (`rating_score`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall experience with our hotel on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **How would you rate your staff interactions** (`staff_interactions`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you would rate your interactions with our staff:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate the quality of food** (`food_quality`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the qualities that best describe your experience with our food:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate the quality of service** (`service_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the quality of our service:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **What do you think of the ambience of our hotel** (`ambience`, `number`, required: false): Rate how much you enjoy the atmosphere of our hotel on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Do you think you got value for money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you think our prices are reasonable for the experience you had?
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Undecided
* **Overall satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with our hotel on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Do you have any suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any comments or suggestions on how we can improve, please share them with us.
