# Product Review Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Review Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from users who have actually used our products. This form helps us understand your experience, likes, and dislikes about the product, which is essential for us to improve our products and services.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form.
2.  Select the answer that best fits your experience with the product.
3.  Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Product Review Questionnaire Form** (`product_review_questionnaire_form`, select_one, required/optional): This field asks whether you have used our product. Please select "Yes" if you have actually used the product.
  - If you select "Yes", you can proceed to answer the rest of the questions.
  - If you select "No", you will not be able to answer the other questions.
- **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, number, optional): This field asks for your rating of the product, from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **Would you recommend this product?** (`product_recommend`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks whether you would recommend this product to others. Please select all the options that apply.
- **Product Comment** (`product_comment`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share about the product.
- **How much would you pay for this product?** (`product_price`, number, optional): This field is for you to tell us how much you think this product is worth the money.
- **Was this product worth the price?** (`product_worthwhile`, select_one, optional): This field asks whether you think the product is worth the price you paid for it. Select "Yes" if you think it's worth the price, and "No" if you don't.
- **What is the purpose of this product for you?** (`product_purpose`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about your reasons for using the product. Please select all the options that apply.
## Tips
* Be honest in your feedback, as it will help us improve our products and services.
* Please keep your answers concise and to the point.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
