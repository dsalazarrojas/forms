# epic_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The epic_feedback_survey form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our service. Please take a few minutes to provide detailed and honest feedback to help us improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. On the first page, please provide a brief description of the "Survey Page" in the text field.
2. On the second page, enter your participant information, such as your name, email, or other relevant details.
3. On the third page, select one of the provided options to answer the questions:
   - What did you enjoy most about the service?
   - What did you enjoy least about the service?
   - How likely are you to recommend the service?
   - You can also provide any additional comments about your experience.
4. Finally, submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Page** (1, text, required): Briefly describe the experience you are providing feedback about.
* **Participant Info** (2, text, required): Enter your participant information, such as your name, email, or other relevant details.
* **Questions** (3, select_one, required):
  + What did you enjoy most about the service?
  + What did you enjoy least about the service?
  + How likely are you to recommend the service?
  + Comments
* **Contact Info** (3, text, optional): Provide any additional contact information if you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
