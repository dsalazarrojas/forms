# Financial Advisory Service Feedback Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Advisory Service Feedback Report is a form designed to gather information about your experience with our financial advisory service. This form helps us to understand your satisfaction, identify areas for improvement, and provide better service in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the "Advisory Service" option that best describes your experience.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with the service on a scale from 1 to 10.
3. Provide your suggestions for improvement or anything else you'd like to share with us.
4. If you have any comments, feel free to add them below.
5. Enter your contact information to help us get in touch with you if needed.
6. Select the "Submitter" option that best describes who filled out this form.
7. Finally, review and submit the form to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Advisory Service**: Select the option that best describes your experience with our financial advisory service.
	+ Option 1: This option is for users who received excellent service from our team.
	+ Option 2: This option is for users who received good service, but with some areas for improvement.
	+ Option 3: This option is for users who received poor service and would like to provide feedback.
* **Satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction level with the service on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the service.
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving our service.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Last Session**: Enter the date of your last session with our service.
* **Time of Last Session**: Enter the time of your last session with our service.
* **Next Session**: Enter the date of your next session with our service.
* **Submitted**: Check the box to confirm you have submitted this form accurately and honestly.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitter**: Select the option that best describes who filled out this form.
* **Submitter Department**: Select the department of the person who filled out this form.
* **Submitter Position**: Select the position of the person who filled out this form.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person who filled out this form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who filled out this form.
* **Submitted By**: Select the option that best describes who filled out this form.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time this form was submitted.
* **Submitted By Department**: Select the department of the person who filled out this form.
* **Submitted By Position**: Select the position of the person who filled out this form.
* **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email address of the person who filled out this form.
* **Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who filled out this form.
