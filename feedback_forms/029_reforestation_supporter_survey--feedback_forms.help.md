# Reforestation Supporter Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from supporters of reforestation efforts. It will help us understand how you learned about our program, your preferred contact method, and your level of satisfaction with the experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Contact Name and Email or Phone number.
2. Let us know your Preferred Contact Method by selecting one of our available options.
3. If you have a preferred language, please select from our available options.
4. Share your Feedback about your experience.
5. Tell us How you Heard About Us by selecting one of our options.
6. Let us know How Satisfied you are with our program by selecting one of our options.
7. Optionally, add any Comments about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Contact Name** (text)
	* This field is used to collect your name.
	* Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.

### 2. **Email** (email)
	* This field is used to collect your email address.
	* Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### 3. **Phone** (text)
	* This field is used to collect your phone number.
	* If you have a phone number, please enter it in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.

### 4. **Preferred Contact Method** (select one)
	* Please select how you would like to be contacted.
	* Options: Email, Phone

### 5. **Preferred Language** (select one)
	* Please select your preferred language.
	* Options: English, French, Spanish

### 6. **Feedback** (text)
	* This field is used to collect any comments or feedback about your experience.
	* Please share your thoughts with us.

### 7. **How Did You Hear About Us** (select one)
	* Please select how you heard about us.
	* Options: Social Media, Email, Word of Mouth, Online Search, Other, Friend, Family, Print Ad, Newspaper Ad, TV Ad

### 8. **How Satisfied** (select one)
	* Please rate your level of satisfaction with our program.
	* Options: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied

### 9. **Comments** (note)
	* This field is an optional text area for you to share any additional comments about your experience.
	* You can use this space to provide more detailed feedback.
