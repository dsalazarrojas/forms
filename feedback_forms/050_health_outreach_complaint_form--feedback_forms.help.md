# Health Outreach Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from patients or individuals who have had an experience with healthcare services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information in the "Patient Info" field.
2. Describe the issue you faced in the "Complaint" field.
3. Choose one or more locations where the issue occurred in the "Complaint Location" field. (Select all that apply)
4. Select one or more categories that best describe the issue in the "Complaint Category" field. (Select all that apply)
5. Provide your contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses, in the "Contact Info" field.
6. Write your message in the "Message" field.
7. Finally, select "Submit" to send your complaint.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Patient Info)**: This field is where you can enter your personal information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Complaint (Complaint)**: Describe the issue you faced, including any relevant details that will help us understand the problem.
* **Complaint Location (Complaint Location)**: Choose one or more locations where the issue occurred, such as Hospital, Clinic, Office, or Home.
* **Complaint Category (Complaint Category)**: Select one or more categories that best describe the issue, such as Clinical, Administrative, or Billing.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)**: Provide your contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Message (Message)**: Write a brief message describing the issue and any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Submit (Submit)**: Select "Submit" to send your complaint and any supporting documentation with it. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and relevant information to help us understand the issue you faced.
* If you have previously submitted a complaint, please do not fill out this form again.
* Your feedback is valuable in helping us improve healthcare services.
