This form is designed to gather information about an individual's experiences and feelings related to crafting and its impact on their wellbeing. Crafting activities can have a profound effect on one's mental and emotional state, and this form aims to capture the positive effects of crafting on wellbeing. The form is meant to be a helpful tool for understanding the benefits of crafting and how it can be promoted within communities.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

## Crafting and Wellbeing Survey - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Introduction
This is an introduction page, and you can read through it without entering any information.
### Step 2: How many hours do you spend on crafting activities per week?
- **How many hours do you spend on crafting activities per week?** (`crafting_frequency`, `number`, required/optional): Please estimate the amount of time you spend on crafting activities each week.
### Step 3: What positive impact do you feel crafting has on your wellbeing?
- **What positive impact do you feel crafting has on your wellbeing?** (`wellbeing_impact`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more of the options that best describe the positive effects of crafting on your wellbeing.
- **Other wellbeing benefits of crafting** (`wellbeing_impact_other`, `text`, required/optional): If none of the above options apply, please describe any other benefits you feel crafting has on your wellbeing.
### Step 4: What type of crafting activities do you enjoy?
- **What type of crafting activities do you enjoy?** (`craft_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of crafting that you enjoy the most.
### Step 5: Have you been involved in a crafting community or group?
- **Have you been involved in a crafting community or group?** (`community_involvement`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you have been involved in a crafting community or group.
### Step 6: Where do you usually do your crafting?
- **Where do you usually do your crafting?** (`craft_location`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the location where you usually do your crafting.
### Step 7: Other places where you do your crafting
- **Other places where you do your crafting** (`craft_frequency_other`, `text`, required/optional): If you craft in any other places not listed above, please describe them here.
### Step 8: Why do you think crafting affects your wellbeing?
- **Why do you think crafting affects your wellbeing?** (`wellbeing_impact_other_why`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any other reasons why you think crafting affects your wellbeing, please describe them here.
### Step 9: Other ways crafting has helped you
- **Other ways crafting has helped you** (`craft_help_other`, `text`, required/optional): If you would like to share any other ways crafting has helped you, please describe them here.
### Step 10: Who supports or encourages you in your crafting activities?
- **Who supports or encourages you in your crafting activities?** (`wellbeing_support`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more people who support or encourage you in your crafting activities.
- **Other people who support your crafting activities** (`wellbeing_support_other`, `text`, required/optional): If none of the above options apply, please describe any other people who support your crafting activities.
### Tips

- Please be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
- You can choose multiple options for some of the questions.
- If you have any other thoughts or comments, feel free to add them in the "Other wellbeing benefits of crafting" or "Other ways crafting has helped you" fields.
- If you have any difficulties or concerns while filling out this form, contact [support contact information].
