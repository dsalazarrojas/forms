# Accessibility Initiative Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to collect feedback on accessibility features and services within the organization. The survey aims to understand your experience, identify areas of improvement, and gather recommendations for making the organization more accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please choose the relevant response for each question carefully, based on your actual experience.
2. You may choose more than one option in questions with "select_multiple" types.
3. For questions with "select_one" types, choose only one response that best suits your situation.
4. You can add additional feedback in the "Additional Feedback" section.
5. Answer all questions, but if a question does not apply to you, choose "Not Applicable" or "Not Aware".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Role** (1, select_one): This question helps us understand your relationship with the organization. Choose the one that best describes you.
	+ Employee: You are an employee of the organization.
	+ Customer: You are a customer or client of the organization.
	+ Visitor: You are a visitor or guest of the organization.
	+ Student: You are a student, possibly affiliated with the organization.
	+ Patient: You are a patient, possibly visiting or working at the organization.
	+ Community Member: You are a member of the community served by the organization.
	+ Other: Please choose this option if none of the above apply to you.
* **Age Group** (2, select_one): This question helps us understand your age range.
	+ Under 18: You are under 18 years old.
	+ 18-24: You are between 18 and 24 years old.
	+ 25-34: You are between 25 and 34 years old.
	+ 35-44: You are between 35 and 44 years old.
	+ 45-54: You are between 45 and 54 years old.
	+ 55-64: You are between 55 and 64 years old.
	+ 65-74: You are between 65 and 74 years old.
	+ 75 or older: You are 75 years old or older.
* **Disability Status** (3, select_one): This question is about your experience with disabilities.

	+ No: You do not have a disability.
	+ Prefer not to say: You do not want to disclose your disability status.
* **Disability Types** (4, select_multiple): If you selected "Yes" in the previous question, choose the disability types that apply to you.
