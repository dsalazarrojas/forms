# phrase_elimination_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from internal stakeholders on phrases that should be removed from the site due to their controversial or outdated nature.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Phrase Elimination Form**: Start by reviewing the available form fields, which include an introductory section, a section to indicate whether the phrase is still relevant, a section to provide a brief explanation for its elimination, a section to suggest an alternative phrase, and a section for providing any further feedback or suggestions.
2. **Current Phrase Selection**: Choose whether the phrase you are providing feedback on is still relevant or not by selecting one of the options in the "Current Phrase" field.
3. **Retire Reason**: If you select that the phrase is not relevant, provide a brief explanation of why it should be removed.
4. **Alternative Suggestion**: If you select that the phrase should be removed, suggest an alternative phrase that can replace it.
5. **Feedback**: If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, please provide it in the "Feedback" field.
6. **Proposed Changes**: Indicate whether the proposed changes should be implemented as suggested or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Phrase** (`current_phrase`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to choose whether the phrase you are providing feedback on is still relevant or not.
* **Retire Reason** (`retire_reason`, `text`, required: false): If you select that the phrase is not relevant, provide a brief explanation of why it should be removed.
* **Alternative Suggestion** (`suggested_alternative`, `text`, required: false): If you select that the phrase should be removed, suggest an alternative phrase that can replace it.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
* **Proposed Changes** (`suggested_changes`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate whether the proposed changes should be implemented as suggested or not.
