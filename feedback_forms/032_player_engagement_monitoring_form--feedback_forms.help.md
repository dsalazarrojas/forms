# Player Engagement Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and engagement data from players about their experiences in a training session or similar activity.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the player being evaluated.
* **Role** (`role`, text, not required): Describe the player's role or position within the activity.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, not required): Select a rating from 1 to 10 to express your satisfaction with the training.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, text, not required): Provide a brief explanation for your selected rating.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, not required): Share any additional feedback you have about the training session.
* **Player Satisfaction** (`player_satisfaction`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select one of the following options:
		- Very Satisfied
		- Somewhat Satisfied
		- Neutral
		- Somewhat Dissatisfied
		- Very Dissatisfied
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, not required): Add any additional information about the session that you think is relevant.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, not required): Suggest any actions or improvements you think are necessary for future sessions.
* **Submit** (`submit_button_label`, text, not required): Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback for processing.

## Tips

* Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering each question.
* If you're unsure about what to enter in a particular field, please leave it blank or ask for clarification.
* Your feedback will help us improve the training program, so please share your honest thoughts and suggestions!
