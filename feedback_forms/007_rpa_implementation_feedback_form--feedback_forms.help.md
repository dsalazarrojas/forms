# RPA Implementation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RPA Implementation Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the RPA (Robotic Process Automation) implementation. This form helps us understand the user's level of satisfaction and provide insights for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the relevant options for each select question (e.g., "Very Satisfied" or "No Outcome").
3. Enter your email address in the email field if you want to receive a follow-up email with the submitted feedback.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "comments" field.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Automation Outcomes** (ID 2):
	+ This question asks how satisfied you are with the automation outcome. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Project Team** (ID 3):
	+ Provide the name of the project team that implemented the RPA.
* **RPA Implementation Time** (ID 4):
	+ Enter the date and time when the RPA was implemented.
* **RPA Implementation Date** (ID 5):
	+ Enter the date of the RPA implementation.
* **Comments** (ID 6):
	+ Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience with the RPA implementation.
* **Email** (ID 7):
	+ Enter your email address if you want to receive a follow-up email with the submitted feedback.
* **Phone Number** (ID 8):
	+ Enter your phone number (not used in this form, but available for potential follow-up).
* **Team ID** (ID 9):
	+ Not used in this form, but available for internal use.
* **Project ID** (ID 10):
	+ Not used in this form, but available for internal use.
* **Assigned to Jotform** (ID 11):
	+ This field is related to internal use and not relevant to users. Please ignore it.
