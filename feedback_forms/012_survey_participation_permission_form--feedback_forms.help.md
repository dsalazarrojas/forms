# Survey Participation Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain participant's information and consent before they participate in a survey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Fill out the "Participant Information" page with your personal details.
3. On the "Survey Details" page, provide information about the survey you are participating in, such as its title and description.
4. On the "Survey Conduct" page, provide information about the survey's conduct, such as the location and duration.
5. On the "Survey Frequency" page, provide information about how often you plan to participate in the survey.
6. On the "Participant Consent" page, select the option that indicates you consent to participate in the survey.
7. On the "Participant Declaration" page, provide a short description of your declaration (e.g. you will follow the rules and conduct of the survey).
8. On the "Date" and "Time" page, provide the date and time you plan to participate in the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* Participant Information (1): A text field for you to enter your name, email, and any other personal information.
### Survey Details
* Survey Details (2): A text field for you to enter the survey title and description.
### Survey Conduct
* Survey Conduct (3): A text field for you to enter the survey conduct, such as location and duration.
### Survey Frequency
* Survey Frequency (4): A text field for you to enter how often you plan to participate in the survey.
### Participant Consent
* Participant Consent (5): A dropdown menu with options "None" and "Other" to select your level of consent.
### Participant Declaration
* Participant Declaration (6): A text field for you to enter a short description of your declaration.
### Date
* Date (7): A date picker field for you to enter the date you plan to participate in the survey.
### Time
* Time (8): A time picker field for you to enter the time you plan to participate in the survey.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
- If you have filled out this form previously, please do not submit this form again.
