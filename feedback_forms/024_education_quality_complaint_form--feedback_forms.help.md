# Education Quality Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to submit complaints about any issues related to education quality. Please use this form to report any issues you've experienced with your educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- Provide a detailed description of the complaint, including relevant facts and circumstances.
- Enter your contact number so that we can reach out to you for further information or clarification.
- Enter your email address, which we may use to contact you about your complaint.
- Choose the relevant institution that the complaint is related to.
- Select the type of complaint you are submitting (e.g. Academic Support, Facilities, Teaching).
- If you have any supporting evidence (e.g. documents, images, videos), please attach it to this form.
- Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (optional).
- If you have a preferred email to be contacted on, please enter it here (optional).
- Select the date and time you submitted this form (this will be automatically filled in by the system).
- If you would like to assign this complaint to a specific person, please enter their name here (optional).
- Choose the current status of your complaint (e.g. Awaiting Review, Under Review, Approved with Conditions, Declined) (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (<required>)**
	* Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Complaint Description (<required>)**
	* Provide a detailed description of the complaint, including relevant facts and circumstances.
* **Contact Number (<required>)**
	* Enter your contact number so that we can reach out to you for further information or clarification.
* **Email (<required>)**
	* Enter your email address, which we may use to contact you about your complaint.
* **Institution (<required>)**
	* Choose the relevant institution that the complaint is related to.
* **Complaint Type (<required>)**
	* Select the type of complaint you are submitting (e.g. Academic Support, Facilities, Teaching).
* **Attachment (<required>)**
	* If you have any supporting evidence (e.g. documents, images, videos), please attach it to this form.
* **Submission (<optional>)**
	* Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up (e.g. Email, Phone, Text).
* **Contact Email (<optional>)**
	* If you would like to be contacted at a specific email, please enter it here.
* **Submission Date/Time (<required>)**
	* The date and time you submitted this form, which will be automatically filled in by the system.
* **Assigned To (<optional>)**
	* If you would like to assign this complaint to a specific person, please enter their name here.
* **Review Status (<optional>)**
	* Choose the current status of your complaint (e.g. Awaiting Review, Under Review, Approved with Conditions, Declined).
