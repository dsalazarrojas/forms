# group_therapy_client_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client feedback after a group therapy session. It helps therapists track client satisfaction and make improvements to their practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields as needed. You can skip any fields that do not apply or that you are not comfortable answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session ID (Session ID, number, optional)**: This is an optional field to reference the session you are providing feedback on. If you do not know your Session ID, you can skip this field.
- **Client Name (Client Name, text, optional)**: This is an optional field to identify yourself as the client. If you do not want to provide your name, you can skip this field.
- **Therapist Name (Therapist Name, text, optional)**: This is an optional field to identify the therapist who led your session. If you do not know the therapist's name or do not want to provide it, you can skip this field.
- **Overall Rating (Overall Rating, number, optional)**: Rate the session overall, from 1 to 5, with 1 being a bad session and 5 being an excellent session.
- **Rating Questions (Rating Questions, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose one or more questions that best describe your experience. You can select multiple options from the list.
  • Was the session helpful?
  • Was the therapist professional?
  • Was the session a waste of time?
- **Feedback (Feedback, select_one, optional)**: Choose how you feel about your session.
  • Positive
  • Negative
  • Neutral
- **Session Date (Session Date, date, optional)**: This is an optional field to record the date of the session you are providing feedback on.
- **Session Time (Session Time, time, optional)**: This is an optional field to record the time of the session you are providing feedback on.
- **Therapist Rating (Therapist Rating, select_one, optional)**: Rate the therapist's performance.
  • Excellent
  • Good
  • Fair
  • Poor
- **Client Recommend (Client Recommend, select_one, optional)**: Would you recommend the therapist to other clients?
  • Yes
  • No
- **Session Notes (Session Notes, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any of the following that apply to your session.
  • Strengths
  • Weaknesses
  • Areas for improvement
