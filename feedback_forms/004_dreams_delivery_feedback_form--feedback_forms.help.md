# dreams_delivery_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on your delivery experience. We value your opinion and would like to know how we can improve our services to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your answer for each question by choosing from the options provided. If you have any comments or feedback, please use the "Any comments about your delivery experience?" field.
2. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required") to ensure that your feedback is complete and accurate.
3. For questions that ask for a date or time, you can select the date or time using the calendar or time picker provided.
4. If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often were you kept informed of your delivery status?** (`delivery_timeliness`, select_multiple): Please select how often you were informed about your delivery status. You can choose "Never", "Rarely", "Sometimes", "Often", or "Always".
* **What was the condition of your delivery product?** (`product_condition`, select_one): Please select the condition of your delivery product from "Bad", "Average", "Good", or "Great".
* **How would you rate the communication from the delivery team?** (`communication`, select_one): Please rate the communication from the delivery team as "Poor", "Fair", "Good", or "Excellent".
* **How would you rate the timeliness of your delivery?** (`timeliness`, select_one): Please select the timeliness of your delivery as "Late", "On Time", or "Early".
* **Any comments about your delivery experience?** (`comments`, note): Please provide any comments or feedback you have about your delivery experience.
* **Overall rating of your delivery experience** (`rating`, number): Please rate your delivery experience as a number from 1 to 5.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email): Please enter your email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text): Please enter your phone number.
* **What is your order number?** (`order_number`, number): Please enter your order number.
* **What is the date of your order?** (`order_date`, date): Please select the date of your order using the calendar picker.
* **What is the time of your order?** (`order_time`, time): Please select the time of your order using the time picker.
* **What is the delivery time?** (`delivery_time`, time): Please select the delivery time.
* **What is your product name?** (`product_name`, text): Please enter the name of your product.
* **What is your product description?** (`product_description`, text): Please enter a description of your product.
* **What was the price of your product?** (`product_price`, text): Please enter the price of your product.
* **What is the condition of your product?** (`product_condition_2`, text): Please enter the condition of your product.
* **What was the delivery date?** (`delivery_date`, date): Please select the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time 2** (`delivery_time_2`, time): Please select the delivery time.
* **What is your order date?** (`order_date_2`, date): Please select the date of your order.
* **What is your order time?** (`order_time_2`, time): Please select the time of your order.
* **What is your name?** (`customer_name`, text): Please enter your name.
* **What is your email?** (`customer_email`, text): Please enter your email address.
* **What is your phone?** (`customer_phone`, text): Please enter your phone number.
* **Order Number 2** (`order_number_2`, text): Please enter your order number.
* **Product Name 2** (`product_name_2`, text): Please enter the name of your product.
