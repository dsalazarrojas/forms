# Open-Ended Responses Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open-Ended Responses Survey is designed to collect open-ended, qualitative feedback from customers about a product. This form allows customers to express their thoughts, opinions, and feelings in a free-form text format.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and think about your response.
2. Type your answers in the text field provided.
3. You can respond to as many or as few questions as you like, depending on your thoughts.
4. Use as much or as little space as you need to answer each question.
5. You can provide additional comments or explanations if you feel it's necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Instructions** (Text Field, Optional): This field is a general introduction to the form. You can read through it to understand the purpose and context of the survey.
* **What do you think about this product?** (Text Field, Optional): Share your thoughts, opinions, and feelings about the product. Be as specific or general as you like.
* **How do you feel about this product?** (Text Field, Optional): Describe your emotions and feelings towards the product. You can use adjectives or phrases to express yourself.
* **Overall how would you describe your experience with this product?** (Text Field, Optional): Summarize your experience with the product in a few sentences or a few words. This field is an opportunity to provide a brief, general assessment of your experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and sincere in your responses. We value your feedback and appreciate your candor.
* You can use as much or as little space as you need to answer each question.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the additional space provided.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
