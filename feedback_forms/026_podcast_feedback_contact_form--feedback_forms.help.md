# Podcast Feedback Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Podcast Feedback Contact Form" is used to collect feedback and contact information from users who want to share their thoughts on the podcast. This form is intended to be filled out by users who have listened to a specific episode and have feedback or questions they'd like to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your feedback and contact information.
2. Select one of the "Submit" or "Cancel" options at the bottom of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Form
* **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, `text`, required: `false`): This is the main contact form field where you can type in your feedback and comments about the podcast.

### Episode Rating
* **Episode Rating** (`episode_rating`, `number`, required: `false`): Rate your experience with this episode on a scale from 1 to 10.

### Episode Feedback
* **Episode Feedback** (`episode_feedback`, `text`, required: `false`): Share your thoughts and feedback on the episode. What did you like or dislike about it?

### How Would You Rate This Episode?
* **How Would You Rate This Episode?** (`rating_2`, `number`, required: `false`): Select one of the rating options to share your opinion about the episode.

### Follow-up
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Indicate if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions about your feedback.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: `false`): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.

### Website
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have a website associated with your feedback, you can enter it here.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can type them in here.

### Permission to Contact
* **Permission to Contact** (`permission_to_contact`, `note`, required: `false`): Indicate if you give us permission to contact you about your feedback.

### Form Comments
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any comments about the form itself, you can type them in here.

### Submit and Cancel Options
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Submit" to send your feedback and contact information to us.
* **Cancel** (`form_cancel`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select "Cancel" to exit the form without sending your feedback.

### Email Error Message
* **Email Error Message** (`email_error_message`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any error message related to your email, you can type it in here.

### Additional Error Message
* **Additional Error Message** (`form_additional_error`, `text`, required: `false`): If you have any additional error messages, you can type them in here.

Note: This help guide is intended to provide a clear and concise explanation of each field and how to complete the form. Please let me know if you need any further assistance.
