# Community Engagement Event Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Community Engagement Event Feedback Evaluation Form. This form is meant to be filled out after attending one of our events, where you can share your thoughts, opinions, and experiences from attending the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Make sure to fill out all the required fields. You will need to provide your name, email address, and select an option for each question. The required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. If you have any issues or questions about filling out this form, feel free to contact us at [support email].
3. Take a few minutes to review each question carefully. Your feedback is valuable to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name (Optional)** (`Name`, `text`, *Optional*): This field is for you to share your name, but you can leave it blank.
*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, *Required*): If you wish to receive follow-up emails or updates about future events, please provide your email address.
*   **Event Name** (`Event Name`, `text`, *Required*): Identify the event you attended.
*   **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, *Required*): Indicate the date of the event.
*   **How Did You Attend** (`How Did You Attend`, `select_one`, *Required*): Select how you attended the event, i.e. In Person, Virtual, or Hybrid.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event.
*   **Event Organization** (`Event Organization`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the event organization.
*   **Content Quality** (`Content Quality`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the quality of the event content.
*   **Speaker or Presenter Quality** (`Speaker or Presenter Quality`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the quality of the speakers or presenters.
*   **Venue Quality** (`Venue Quality`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the quality of the venue.
*   **Networking Opportunities** (`Networking Opportunities`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate the networking opportunities at the event.
*   **Value for Time Spent** (`Value for Time Spent`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate how valuable you found the event for the time you spent.
*   **Favorite Part of Event** (`Favorite Part of Event`, `text`, *Optional*): Share what you enjoyed most during the event.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`Areas for Improvement`, `text`, *Optional*): Share what could be improved in future events.
*   **Did You Learn Something New?** (`Did You Learn Something New?`, `select_one`, *Required*): Indicate how much you learned from the event.
*   **Would You Attend Again?** (`Would You Attend Again?`, `select_one`, *Required*): Select how likely you are to attend future events.
*   **Would You Recommend to Others?** (`Would You Recommend to Others?`, `select_one`, *Required*): Rate how likely you are to recommend the event to others.
*   **Topics for Future Events** (`Topics for Future Events`, `text`, *Optional*): Share topics or areas you'd like to see covered in future events.
*   **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, *Optional*): Leave any other feedback or comments here.

## Tips

*   If you have trouble with the form, contact the support team at [support email].
*   Your feedback is valuable to us, so take your time and be as specific as possible.
*   If you wish to provide feedback anonymously, you can, but please provide your name for follow-up if you change your mind.
*   Your feedback will help us improve our events.
