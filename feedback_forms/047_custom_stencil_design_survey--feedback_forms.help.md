# Custom Stencil Design Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Custom Stencil Design Survey" form is designed to help you create a personalized stencil design tailored to your needs. By sharing your preferences, you will be able to create a unique and effective stencil design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages carefully.
2. For each page, select the options that best match your preferences.
3. Take your time, and make sure to provide accurate answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stencils Preferred Colors** (id: 2, select_multiple, optional):
  Select one or more colors you prefer for your custom stencil design.
  For example, you can select "Red", "Blue", "Green", "Yellow", "Purple", "Orange", "Brown", or "Pink".
* **Preferred Shapes** (id: 3, select_one, optional):
  Choose the shape that best represents your desired custom stencil design.
  Options include "Circle", "Square", "Triangle", "Heart", "Star", "Diamond", "Oval", or "Rectangle".
* **Stencil Size** (id: 4, number, optional):
  Select a size for your custom stencil design.
  Please enter a whole number that represents your preferred size.
* **Preferred Materials** (id: 5, select_multiple, optional):
  Select one or more materials you prefer for your custom stencil design.
  Options include "Paper", "Cardboard", "Plastic", "Metal", "Wood", "Fabric", "Glass", "Ceramic".
* **Customer Feedback** (id: 6, text, optional):
  Share any comments or suggestions you have about the custom stencil design.
* **Artist Feedback** (id: 7, text, optional):
  Provide feedback on the artist's interpretation of your design preferences.
* **Business Feedback** (id: 8, text, optional):
  Share any business-related comments or suggestions you have about the custom stencil design.

## Tips
* Take your time to review each page carefully.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* You can complete the form multiple times if you'd like, but it will not yield different results.
