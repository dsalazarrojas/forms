# Humanization Specialist Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers or stakeholders about their experience with our Humanization Specialist services. Your input is essential in helping us understand where we excel and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form completely, as required fields are marked as such. 
2. Please provide honest and constructive feedback in the relevant fields.
3. Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Feedback Form** 
    - **Feedback Form** (`form_1`, text, required/optional): A text field where you can provide your overall experience and suggestions for improvement.
### 2. **Rating** 
    - **Rating** (`rating_1`, number, optional): A rating field to rate your experience on a scale (though no scale is provided, please provide a number from 1-10).
### 3. **What did we do well?** 
    - **What did we do well?** (`rating_2`, text, optional): A text field to specify what aspects of our service you found to be particularly good or effective.
### 4. **What could we improve on?** 
    - **What could we improve on?** (`rating_3`, text, optional): A text field to specify what areas of our service need improvement.
### 5. **Overall Experience** 
    - **Overall Experience** (`rating_4`, text, optional): A text field to rate your overall experience.
### 6. **Submit** 
    - **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): A button to submit your feedback.

## Tips
- Please be specific and honest in your feedback.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement or questions, feel free to include them in the feedback fields.
- We will review and respond to all submitted feedback.
