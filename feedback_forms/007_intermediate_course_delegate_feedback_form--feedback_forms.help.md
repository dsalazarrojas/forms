# Intermediate Course Delegate Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from delegates on intermediate courses that need improvement or have some issues. It helps the course team to identify areas of concern and make necessary adjustments for future courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly to provide valuable feedback.
2. Ensure that you are providing feedback only for intermediate courses that you have attended or are familiar with.
3. Be specific when providing feedback, and try to address each field as much as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
* **Course Provider** (`course_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course provider or organization offering the course.
* **Course Type** (`course_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of course you are providing feedback on (e.g., "Label1", "Label2", or "Label3").
* **Course Level** (`course_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of the course (e.g., "Label1", "Label2", or "Label3"). This field is required to ensure that feedback is focused on the correct course type.
* **Start Date** (`course_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the course, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`course_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the course, if applicable.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, optional): Enter a score between 1 and 5 to rate your feedback (1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest).
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have for the course.
* **Course Status** (`course_status`, select_one, required): Select whether the course is Active or Inactive.
* **Additional Notes** (`course_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or information that you want to share about the course.

## Tips
- Be honest and specific when providing feedback.
- Try to provide feedback on courses that you are familiar with.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or skip it.
- Ensure that you have not already submitted feedback for the same course.
