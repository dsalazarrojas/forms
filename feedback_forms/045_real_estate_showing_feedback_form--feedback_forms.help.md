# real_estate_showing_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture feedback on real estate showings, including the date and time of the showing, property address, agent name, and client feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Provide your feedback about the showing in the feedback field.
5. Enter the property address where the showing took place.
6. Select the date of the showing from the calendar.
7. Choose the time of the showing using the time picker.
8. Enter the name of the agent who gave the showing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Provide detailed feedback about the showing, including any positive or negative comments.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the property that was shown.
* **Showing Date** (`showing_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the showing from the calendar.
* **Showing Time** (`showing_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the showing using the time picker.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the agent who gave the showing.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible in the feedback field to help the agent improve their showing process.
* Make sure to enter the correct property address and showing date and time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form maintainer at form@gic.mx.
