## Service Non Renewal Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have chosen not to renew their service with our company. The feedback will help us understand the reasons behind their decision and identify areas for improvement to enhance our services and increase customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions according to your experience with our service. You may select multiple reasons for service cancellation, so choose all that apply. Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed. Finally, provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Cancellation Reason** (`service_cancellation_reason`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to choose one or more reasons for cancelling your service. Select all that apply from the list of options provided.
* **Non Renewal Date** (`cancellation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you chose not to renew your service.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service, with options ranging from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Suggestion** (`suggestion`, note, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in this field.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **E mail** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to be contacted via email, please enter your email address here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience with our service.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing reasons for service cancellation.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Suggestion" or "Additional Comments" fields.
* Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our services and customer satisfaction.
