# Educational Kit Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your thoughts on educational kits. We appreciate your honest feedback, which will help us improve our kits to better serve you and others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as honestly as possible.
2. Make sure to provide as much detail as you can about the educational kit you're evaluating.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please share them in the "Feedback" field.
4. Click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **required**): Please enter the name of the school where you used the educational kit. This will help us understand which schools are using our kits.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, **required**): Please enter the name of the program that the educational kit was designed for. This will help us understand which programs are being targeted by our kits.
* **Learning Kit** (`learning_kit`, text, **optional**): If you have any additional comments or suggestions about the educational kit, please provide them here.
* **Useful** (`usefulness`, select_multiple, **required**): Please select all the options that apply to your experience with the educational kit. You can choose "Yes" or "No" for this field.
* **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, select_multiple, **required**): Please rate the quality of the content in the educational kit. You can select "Good" or "Poor" for this field.
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, text, **optional**): If you have any thoughts or suggestions on how to improve the ease of use of the educational kit, please share them here.
* **Rating** (`ratings`, number, **required**): Please give a numerical rating (1-5) of your experience with the educational kit. Higher numbers indicate a better experience.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **optional**): This is your chance to provide any additional comments or suggestions about the educational kit. We're listening! 

## Tips
* Please be specific and honest in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any field, just leave it blank and let us know why.
* Thank you for taking the time to help us improve our educational kits!
