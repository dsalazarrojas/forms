# Metaphysical Consultation Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients who have received metaphysical consultations. It helps us understand their satisfaction with the session, their thoughts on the practitioner, and any areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For select_one or select_multiple fields, choose only one or multiple relevant options.
3. For text fields, enter your feedback in the space provided.
4. Be as detailed and honest as possible in your feedback.
5. When finished, submit the form to complete the feedback process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Session Satisfaction\*** (`session_satisfaction`, number): Rate your satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1-5.

- **Practitioner Satisfaction\*** (`practitioner_satisfaction`, number): Rate your satisfaction with the practitioner on a scale of 1-5.

- **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions about the session or practitioner.

- **Improvement Notes** (`improvement_notes`, note): Enter any notes you have about how the session or practitioner could be improved.

- **Session Rating** (`session_rating`, number): Rate the overall quality of the session on a scale of 1-5.

- **Practitioner Rating** (`practitioner_rating`, number): Rate the quality of the practitioner on a scale of 1-5.

- **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the practitioner or session.

- **Session Comments** (`session_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the session.

- **Practitioner Comments** (`practitioner_comments`, text): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the practitioner.

- **Improvement Recommendations** (`improvement_recommendations`, select_multiple): Select any areas that need improvement.

- **Session Improvement Areas\*** (`session_improvement_areas`, select_one): Select any areas that need improvement in the session.

- **Practitioner Improvement Areas\*** (`practitioner_improvement_areas`, select_one): Select any areas that need improvement in the practitioner.

- **Date of Session** (`date_of_session`, date): Enter the date of your session.

- **Time of Session** (`time_of_session`, time): Enter the time of your session.

- **Practitioner Name** (`practitioner_name`, text): Enter the name of the practitioner.

- **Practitioner License** (`practitioner_license`, text): Enter the name of the practitioner's license or certification.

- **Session Length** (`session_length`, number): Enter the length of your session.

- **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text): Enter any additional notes about your session.

- **Follow-up Action** (`follow_up_action`, select_one): Select a follow-up action that is applicable to your situation.

- **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date): Enter the date of your follow-up.

- **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time): Enter the time of your follow-up.

- **Follow-up Notes** (`follow_up_notes`, text): Enter any additional notes about your follow-up.

- **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one): Select the type of session you received.

- **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one): Select the type of client you are.

- **Session Status** (`session_status`, select_one): Select the status of your session.
