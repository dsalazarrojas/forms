# Travel Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Travel Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from customers after their travel experience with our company. We value customer feedback and use it to improve our services to provide better experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the travel agency you traveled with from the dropdown list.
2. Provide a detailed feedback about your experience. Please be honest and provide as much information as possible.
3. Enter the date of your travel experience in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. (Optional) Enter the time of your travel experience.
5. (Optional) Add any additional feedback or comments in the text field.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
7. Enter your phone number (optional).
8. Finally, enter the date of your travel experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_1`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Choose the travel agency you traveled with from the list.
* **Feedback** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_2`, `text`, optional):
	+ Provide a detailed feedback about your experience. Be honest and provide as much information as possible.
* **Date** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_3`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date of your travel experience in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_4`, `time`, optional):
	+ (Optional) Enter the time of your travel experience.
* **Feedback** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_5`, `note`, optional):
	+ (Optional) Add any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_6`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_7`, `text`, optional):
	+ (Optional) Enter your phone number.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_feedback_form_fields_8`, `date`, optional):
	+ (Optional) Enter the date of your travel experience again.
