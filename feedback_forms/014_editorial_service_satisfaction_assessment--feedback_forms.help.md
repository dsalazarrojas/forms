# Editorial Service Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your feedback about the editorial service you received. It is used to assess the quality of the editorial work, communication, and project delivery time. Your input is crucial in helping us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and select the options that best match your experience.
2. For each question, choose one of the provided options (e.g., Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor) or provide a brief note with your comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What was the overall quality of the editing work?** (`prophets`, `number`, optional): Rate the overall quality of the editing work from 1 to 5, where 1 is Poor and 5 is Very Good. This will give us an idea of how well the editor performed.
* **What was the quality of the editing?** (`editing_pros`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more of the options (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor) to describe the quality of the editing work.
* **How well did we communicate with you?** (`communication`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of communication from Very Well, Well, Somewhat Well, Not Very Well, or Not Well.
* **What was the quality of communication?** (`communication_pros`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more of the options (Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor) to describe the quality of communication.
* **Was the project delivered on time?** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose Yes or No to indicate whether the project was delivered on time.
* **Project Overview** (`project_overview`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief description of the project.
* **What did you like about the project?** (`project_overview_pros`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief description of what you liked about the project.
* **Id** (`id`, `note`, optional): This field is not intended for your feedback. You can disregard it.

Note: All fields with the "optional" tag are not required, but providing feedback on these fields is highly appreciated.

## Tips

* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible in your feedback.
* Use the provided options for numerical questions, and add a brief note for open-ended questions.
* If you have any other feedback or suggestions, please feel free to add it to the "Id" field (although it's not required).

This form is designed to be completed in a few minutes. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback.
