# Key Checkout Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers after a key event or issue has been resolved. The feedback will help us understand how we can improve our service and identify areas where we can better assist our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your feedback and ratings. Please make sure to include your email address so we can follow up with you regarding your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required):
	+ This is the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional):
	+ This is a text area where you can provide detailed feedback about your experience.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose a rating that best describes your experience (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required):
	+ This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required):
	+ Enter a contact number where we can reach you for further assistance.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional):
	+ If you prefer to be contacted via email, enter your email address here.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your contact address if you'd like us to reach you at this location.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional):
	+ If you'd like to assign this form to a specific person, enter their name here.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, date, optional):
	+ This field is automatically generated and shows when the form was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, optional):
	+ This field is automatically generated and shows when the form was last updated.
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, number, optional):
	+ This field is automatically generated and shows the form's ID.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional):
	+ This field is not currently used.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required):
	+ Choose a category for your feedback (Feedback Forms, Support Tickets, Bug Reports, Feature Requests).
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional):
	+ This is a text area where you can provide a description of your feedback.
* **Assigned To User** (`assigned_tool_user`, text, optional):

* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, optional):

	+ This field is not currently used.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional):

* **Form Tags** (`form_tags`, text, optional):
