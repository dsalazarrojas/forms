# Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Feedback Form is designed to collect honest and constructive feedback from attendees of a workshop. Your input will help the organizers improve future events and provide a better experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your thoughts, opinions, and ratings. You can submit your feedback at your convenience, and you can revisit this form multiple times if you need to revise your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the workshop you attended.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your honest opinions and thoughts about the workshop. This is your chance to tell us what you liked and what you didn't.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Provide a numerical rating from 1 to 5 to rate your overall experience. 1 is low, and 5 is high.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Additional comments or suggestions you'd like to make about the workshop.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your workshop attendance (if available).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your workshop attendance (if available).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to receive a follow-up).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to provide it).

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* You can submit multiple responses if you need to provide more feedback or want to revise your answers.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your input and will use it to improve future events!
