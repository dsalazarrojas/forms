# Catering Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a catering service. It will help us to identify areas where we are meeting or failing to meet your expectations, and make adjustments accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure that your feedback is as valuable as possible.
2. Answer each question with one of the provided options or provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" section.
3. If you need clarification on any question, please ask and we will guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, Optional): Enter your name to confirm that you are providing this feedback.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, Optional): Enter your email address to facilitate further communication with us, if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, Optional): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, please enter your number here.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Required): Provide the date when the event was held to ensure that we can identify which event this feedback relates to.
* **Catering Company Name** (`catering_company`, Required): Enter the name of the catering company you used for the event.
* **Type of Event** (`event_type`, Required): Choose the type of event you were hosting to help us understand your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_rating`, Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the catering service.
* **How likely are you to recommend this catering service?** (`likelihood_recommend`, Required): Indicate how likely you are to recommend this service to others.
* **Would you use this catering service again?** (`would_use_again`, Required): Let us know if you would use this service again for another event.
* **Taste and Flavor** (`food_taste_rating`, Required): Rate your experience with the taste and flavor of the food provided.
* **Food Presentation and Appearance** (`food_presentation`, Required): Rate your experience with the presentation and appearance of the food.
* **Food Temperature** (`food_temperature`, Required): Indicate whether the food was served at an appropriate temperature.
* **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, Required): Rate your experience with the professionalism of the staff.
* **Staff Attentiveness** (`staff_attentiveness`, Required): Indicate the level of attentiveness of the staff.
* **Service Efficiency and Speed** (`service_speed`, Required): Rate your experience with the speed and efficiency of the service.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, Required): Rate your experience with the value for money provided by the catering service.
* **Pricing Transparency** (`price_transparency`, Optional): Indicate the level of pricing transparency provided by the catering service.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`comments`, Optional): Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about the service.

**Tips**:
- Be as specific as possible when answering the questions.
- Please rate your experience with honesty and integrity.
- We appreciate your feedback and look forward to hearing from you.
