# Customer Experience Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback and experience data to help us improve our customer service and overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter the information requested for each section. You can choose from the provided options or enter your own text responses as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Customer Experience
**1. Customer Experience** (<type>: select_multiple): Select all that apply. This field is required and asks about your overall experience with our company.

*   **Yes** and **No** options are provided to indicate your satisfaction level.

### Satisfaction Level
**2. satisfaction** (<type>: select_one): This field is not required and asks about your level of satisfaction with our services.

*   Choose one of the two options, **Satisfied** or **Dissatisfied**.
