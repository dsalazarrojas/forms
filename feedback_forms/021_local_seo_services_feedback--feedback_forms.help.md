# Local SEO Services Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about our local SEO services. We use this feedback to improve our services and ensure that we are providing the best possible experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your feedback. All fields are optional, so feel free to provide as much or as little information as you'd like.
2. If you have any comments or testimonials about our local SEO services, please share them with us in the Comments and Testimonial fields respectively.
3. If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your name in the Contact Name field.
4. Once you're finished, click the submit button to send us your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, optional): Please rate your overall satisfaction with our local SEO services on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or thoughts you have about your experience with our local SEO services.
* **Testimonial** (`testimonial`, text, optional): If you have a positive experience with our local SEO services, please share a brief testimonial or review.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your name here.

## Tips
* Please keep your feedback constructive and respectful. This will help us improve our services and provide the best possible experience for our customers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
* We appreciate your feedback and look forward to using it to improve our local SEO services!
