# Non Profit Initiative Feedback Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Profit Initiative Feedback Contact Form is a tool designed to collect structured feedback and contact information from individuals interested in non-profit initiatives. The form is used to gather relevant details about the user, including their name, email, feedback, and preferred contact method.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select your preferred contact method (phone, email, or mail).
3. Provide feedback about the non-profit initiative you are interested in.
4. If applicable, choose the event date and time you attended or plan to attend.
5. Add any additional comments about your experience or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it is displayed on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it is displayed on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address that is checked regularly.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, required): Share your thoughts and comments about the non-profit initiative.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ **Phone**: Call or text you at the number you provide.
	+ **Email**: Send an email to your provided email address.
	+ **Mail**: Send a letter or package to your provided address.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, optional): Enter the date you attended or plan to attend the event (if applicable).
* **Event Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time you attended or plan to attend the event (if applicable).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, optional): If applicable, enter the location where you attended or plan to attend the event.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, optional): Add any extra comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Non Profit** (`non_profit`, required): Confirm if the organization is indeed a non-profit.
* **How Did You Find Us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, required): Choose how you found out about the non-profit:
	+ **Facebook**
	+ **Google**
	+ **Instagram**
	+ **Twitter**
	+ **Word of mouth**
	+ **Other** (please describe in the provided space)
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, optional): Choose how you heard about the non-profit:
	+ **Social media**
	+ **Friend or family member**
	+ **Community event**
	+ **Online search**
	+ **Other** (please describe in the provided space)
* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, required): Confirm if a staff member is involved with the event or initiative:
	+ **True**
	+ **False**
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, required): Confirm if you are interested in further communication about the initiative:
	+ **True**
	+ **False**
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required): Enter your name as it is displayed on your identification documents.

## Tips
- Double-check all the provided fields for accuracy and completeness.
- Make sure to choose relevant and accurate options for all select fields.
- If you need further assistance, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
