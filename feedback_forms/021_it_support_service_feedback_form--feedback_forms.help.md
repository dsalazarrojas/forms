> # IT Support Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Support Service Feedback Form is a form designed to collect feedback from users on the IT Support Service provided by the organization. This form helps us understand how we can improve our service and provide better support to our users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select a category for your feedback: You can choose from "Feedback", "Suggestion", or "Other" categories to categorize your feedback.
2. Provide a brief description of your feedback or issue: Type a short description of your feedback or the issue you want to report.
3. Rate your experience: Use the rating system to rate your experience with our IT Support Service.
4. Enter your contact information: If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your contact information.
5. Add any additional comments: If you have any additional comments or information, please add them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select a category for your feedback. Please choose from "Feedback", "Suggestion", or "Other" to help us understand the nature of your comment.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required: false): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your feedback or issue.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, required: false): This field is a scale from 1 to 5 where you can rate your experience with our IT Support Service.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): If you would like to be contacted for follow-up, please provide your contact information here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): This field is where you can enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): This field is where you can add any additional comments or information that might be helpful to us.
