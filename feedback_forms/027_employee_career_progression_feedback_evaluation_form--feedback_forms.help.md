# Employee Career Progression Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluate the career progression of employees. It is intended for employees, managers, and department heads to provide feedback on an employee's performance, growth, and development in their current role. This feedback will help identify areas of strength and areas that require improvement, enabling managers to make informed decisions about employee development and advancement opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the department the employee belongs to from the Department dropdown list.
2. Enter the employee's name in the Employee Name field.
3. Enter the employee's job title in the Job Title field.
4. Provide feedback on the employee's performance, growth, and development in the Feedback Question fields (Feedback Question 1 to Feedback Question 6).
5. Select your level of satisfaction with the employee's performance in the Employee Satisfaction field.
6. Provide any additional comments or observations in the Additional Comments field.
7. Offer employee recommendations for growth and development in the Employee Recommendations field.
8. Describe any management approaches that have been effective or ineffective in the Employee Management Approaches field.
9. Provide comments from the department head in the Department Head Comments field.
