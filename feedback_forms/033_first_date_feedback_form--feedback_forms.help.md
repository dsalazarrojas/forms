# First Date Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from users after their first date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the "Date" field with the date of the first date.
2. Provide additional details about the date in the "Date details" field.
3. Rate your overall experience in the "Overall experience" field using the number scale provided.
4. Indicate whether you would recommend the date to others in the "Would recommend" field.
5. Select the reason why you didn't get along from the list in the "Why didn't we get along?" field, if applicable.
6. Select the type of feedback you wish to provide in the "Feedback" field, which can be either Positive or Negative.
7. Once you have completed the form, select "Submit" to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your first date.
* **Date details** (`feedback_date_details`, note, optional): Provide additional details about the date, such as any notable events or conversations that took place.
* **Overall experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall experience using the scale provided (e.g., 1-5, where 5 is the highest rating).
* **Would recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend the date to others.
* **Why didn't we get along?** (`why_didnt_remember`, note, optional): Select the reason why you didn't get along, if applicable.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of feedback you wish to provide, which can be either Positive or Negative.
* **Submit** (`first_date_feedback_form_submit`, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to submit your feedback.
