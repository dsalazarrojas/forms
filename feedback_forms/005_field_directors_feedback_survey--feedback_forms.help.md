# field_directors_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The field directors feedback survey is designed to gather feedback from field directors in an organization, focusing on their experiences, challenges, and ideas for improvement. This form is intended to help the organization better understand their field directors' perspectives and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the correct category for the feedback form.
2. Provide a brief description of your experience as a field director.
3. Enter your organization's name.
4. Specify your role in the organization.
5. (Optional) Provide your level of satisfaction with the current state of your department or organization.
6. If you have faced any challenges, describe them in the text box.
7. If you have ideas for improvement, share them in the text box.
8. Add any additional comments you'd like to make about your experience.
9. Provide your email address so that your feedback can be contacted.
10. Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for easier contact).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category**: Feedback Forms (Required) - Select the category for this feedback form.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your experience as a field director.
* **Organization Name**: (Required) Enter the name of your organization.
* **Role**: (Required) Specify your role in the organization.
* **Satisfaction**: (Optional) Provide your level of satisfaction with the current state of your department or organization.
* **Challenges**: (Optional) If you have faced any challenges, describe them in this text box.
* **Ideas for Improvement**: (Optional) If you have ideas for improvement, share them in this text box.
* **Comments**: (Optional) Add any additional comments you'd like to make about your experience.
* **Email**: (Required) Provide your email address so that your feedback can be contacted.
* **Phone**: (Optional) Enter your phone number for easier contact.
