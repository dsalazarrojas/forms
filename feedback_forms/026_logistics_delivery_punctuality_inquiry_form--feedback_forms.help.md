# Logistics Delivery Punctuality Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report or inquire about the punctuality of a delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

*   Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
*   Select the correct options for each field.
*   Double-check your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Delivery Date**: Enter the date of the delivery in question.
*   **Delivery Time**: Enter the time of the delivery in question.
*   **Arrived Time**: Enter the time the delivery was expected to arrive, if available.
*   **Punctuality Status**: Select the status of the delivery from the options provided.
	+   "On Time": The delivery was made on time.
	+   "Early": The delivery was made before the expected time.
	+   "Late": The delivery was made after the expected time.
	+   "Undetermined": The delivery status is unknown.
*   **Feedback Comments**: Provide any additional comments or details about the delivery.
*   **Carrier Name**: Enter the name of the carrier or delivery personnel.
*   **Carrier Rating**: Rate the service of the carrier from 1 to 5.
	+   "5": Excellent service.
	+   "4": Good service.
	+   "3": Fair service.
	+   "2": Poor service.
	+   "1": Very poor service.
*   **Customer Name**: Enter your name.
*   **Customer Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number.

Note: The form fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory, but it is recommended that you fill them out for a more complete report.
