# Mental Health Awareness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about your perceptions and experiences regarding mental health awareness. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us improve our resources and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Answer the questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Select the mental health professional category that best fits your experience.
3.  Indicate your mental health concerns and experiences.
4.  Provide any additional feedback or suggestions.
5.  Choose your preferred method of contact.
6.  If applicable, mention any other mental health professionals you've consulted.
7.  Share your mental health experiences, if you feel comfortable doing so.
8.  Check the box indicating if you've received help from a mental health professional.
9.  Select your preferred way of staying in touch.
10.  Provide comments or suggestions if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about this form?** (`mental_health_form_1`, text, optional): This field is for your thoughts and impressions about this form.
* **Have you been a mental health professional?** (`medical_professional`, select_one, optional): This field will help us understand your background or perspective as a mental health professional.
* **What mental health concerns do you have?** (`mental_health_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all mental health concerns that apply to you.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any additional thoughts or comments about mental health awareness.
* **Preferred method of contact** (`contact_method`, text, optional): Indicate your preferred method of contact, such as phone or email.
* **Other mental health professionals** (`mental_health_professionals_other`, text, optional): If you have consulted other mental health professionals, please mention them here.
* **Can you describe your mental health experiences?** (`experiences`, text, optional): Share your experiences if you feel comfortable doing so.
* **Have you received help from a mental health professional?** (`received_help`, checkbox, optional): Check this box if you have received help from a mental health professional.
* **Preferred way of staying in touch** (`stay_in_touch`, select_multiple, optional): Select how you would like to stay in touch with us.
* **Comments or suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions, please share them here.
* **Date of last visit** (`recent_visit`, date, optional): If you've had a recent visit with a mental health professional, please enter the date of your last visit.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
