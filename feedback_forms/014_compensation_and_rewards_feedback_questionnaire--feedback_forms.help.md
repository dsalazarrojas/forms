# Compensation And Rewards Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather feedback from employees on the current compensation and rewards program within the company. Your input will help us understand your opinions and suggestions to improve the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best reflects your opinion.
3. For open-ended questions, provide specific and detailed feedback.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is the compensation structure of the company easy to understand?** (`structure_clarity`): This question is about how clear the company's compensation structure is. Think about how easy or difficult it is to understand how pay scales and levels work within the company.
* **How often do you receive non-monetary recognition?** (`reward_frequency`): This question is about how frequently you receive rewards and recognition, such as praise, certificates, or small gifts.
* **Do you believe high performers are rewarded appropriately?** (`performance_reward_fairness`): This question is about whether you think that high performers are rewarded fairly based on their performance.
* **Which type of reward motivates you the most?** (`reward_preference`): This question is about which type of reward you would be most motivated by, such as spot bonuses or public recognition.
* **To what extent does the current reward system influence your decision to stay?** (`retention_driver`): This question is about how much influence the current reward system has on your decision to stay with the company.
* **Would you value a formal peer-to-peer recognition program?** (`peer_recognition_program`): This question is about whether you would value a formal program where you can give and receive recognition from peers.
* **Are the existing rewards easy to access and use?** (`current_rewards_usability`): This question is about whether the existing rewards, such as discounts or perks, are easy to use and access.
* **How can we improve our rewards and recognition program?** (`open_rewards_suggestions`): This question is for you to provide specific ideas or changes to improve the rewards program.
* **Rate your overall satisfaction with the company's reward strategy** (`general_satisfaction_rating`): This is the final question where you rate your overall satisfaction with the company's reward strategy.
