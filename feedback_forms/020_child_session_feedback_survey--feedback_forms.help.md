# Child Session Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Child Session Feedback Survey is a tool designed to help providers gather valuable feedback from parents or guardians about their experiences with a particular session. This feedback is essential for providers to understand their own performance, identify areas of improvement, and make informed decisions about future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the feedback submission from the calendar.
2. Enter the legal first name of the parent or guardian.
3. Enter the legal last name of the parent or guardian.
4. Provide the primary email address of the parent or guardian for contact purposes.
5. Enter the legal first name of the child.
6. Enter the legal last name of the child.
7. Select the date of the session being reviewed.
8. Choose the type of session (e.g., Therapy, Tutoring, Assessment, etc.).
9. Enter the name of the session provider.
10. Rate the overall satisfaction with the session.
11. Rate the provider's communication.
12. Rate the provider's professionalism.
13. Rate the child's comfort level during the session.
14. Rate the session environment.
15. Select whether the session length was appropriate.
16. Indicate whether the child's goals were adequately addressed.
17. Report any progress observed since starting sessions.
18. Indicate how helpful the discussed strategies were.
19. Rate whether you would recommend this provider to others.
20. Indicate whether you plan to continue with these services.
21. (Optional) Mention the best aspect of the session.
22. (Optional) Provide areas for improvement for future sessions.
23. (Optional) Share any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Date**: The date when the feedback is being submitted.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: The legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: The legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: The primary email address of the parent or guardian for contact purposes.
* **Child First Name**: The legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: The legal last name of the child.
* **Session Date**: The date of the session being reviewed.
* **Session Type**: The type of session (e.g., Therapy, Tutoring, Assessment, etc.).
* **Provider Name**: The name of the session provider.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **Provider Communication**: Rate the provider's communication.
* **Provider Professionalism**: Rate the provider's professionalism.
* **Child Comfort Level**: Rate the child's comfort level during the session.
* **Session Environment**: Rate the session environment.
* **Session Length**: Select whether the session length was appropriate.
* **Goals Addressed**: Indicate whether the child's goals were adequately addressed.
* **Progress Observed**: Report any progress observed since starting sessions.
* **Strategies Helpful**: Indicate how helpful the discussed strategies were.
* **Would Recommend**: Rate whether you would recommend this provider to others.
* **Continue Services**: Indicate whether you plan to continue with these services.
* **Best Aspect of Session**: (Optional) Mention the best aspect of the session.
* **Areas for Improvement**: (Optional) Provide areas for improvement for future sessions.
* **Additional Comments**: (Optional) Share any additional comments or feedback.
