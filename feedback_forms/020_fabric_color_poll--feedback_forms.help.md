# Fabric Color Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fabric Color Poll form is a tool to collect feedback and preferences from users regarding their favorite fabric colors. This form is intended to be a fun and interactive way to gather information and provide insights to the design team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the introduction page for context and background information.
2. On the fabric-color page, select your favorite fabric colors from the list.
3. On the fabric-color-preferences page, choose the color you prefer the most.
4. On the other-fabric-color page, enter any other fabric color that you like (optional).
5. On the submission page, select whether you would like to submit your preferences or explore other options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Fabric Color (fabric-color)
* **fabric-color** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more fabric colors that you like. You can choose from Blue, Green, Red, and Yellow.
### Fabric Color Preference (fabric-color-preferences)
* **fabric-color-preferences** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the color that you prefer the most from the options.
### Other Fabric Color (other-fabric-color)
* **other-fabric-color** (`text`, optional): Enter any other fabric color that you like (if different from the previous options).
### Submit
* **Submit** (`select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would like to submit your preferences or explore other options.
