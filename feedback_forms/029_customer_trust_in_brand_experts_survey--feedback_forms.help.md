# Customer Trust In Brand Experts Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers on their experience with our brand experts. It's meant to be completed by customers who have recently interacted with one of our experts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in the required fields (indicated by asterisks).
3. Select the options that best fit your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. What is your experience with our brand experts?** (`introduction`, `text`, required: false): Please share your overall experience with our brand experts.
* **2. How would you rate our brand experts on a scale of 1-5?** (`rating`, `number`, required: true): Rate your experience with our experts on a scale from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **3. What is your reason for this rating?** (`rating_comment`, `text`, required: false): Share your reason for giving the rating you did in question 2.
* **4. What is your email address?** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates.
* **5. What are your thoughts on what we did well or what we could improve?** (`feedback`, `text`, required: true): Share your thoughts on your experience with our experts.
* **6. When did you last visit one of our brand experts?** (`rating_date`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date of your last visit.
* **7. What time did you last visit one of our brand experts?** (`rating_time`, `time`, required: true): Enter the time of your last visit.
* **8. What is the name of the expert you met with?** (`expert_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the expert you met with.
* **9. What kind of expert was it?** (`expert_specialization`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the area of expertise of the expert you met with.
* **10. Where was the meeting held?** (`expert_location`, `text`, required: true): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **11. What was your overall experience with this brand expert?** (`overall_experience`, `text`, required: false): Share your overall experience with the expert.
* **12. What was the quality of the expert?** (`rating_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the quality of the expert on a scale of excellent to poor.
* **13. How friendly was the expert?** (`expert_friendliness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the friendliness of the expert on a scale from very friendly to very unfriendly.
* **14. How good was the expert listening?** (`expert_listening_skill`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the listening skill of the expert on a scale from excellent to poor.
* **15. How fast was the expert response?** (`expert_response_speed`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the response speed of the expert on a scale from very fast to very slow.
* **16. How helpful was the expert?** (`expert_helpfulness`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the helpfulness of the expert on a scale from very helpful to very unhelpful.
* **17. How professional was the expert?** (`expert_professionalism`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the professionalism of the expert on a scale from very professional to very unprofessional.
* **18. How much expertise did the expert have?** (`expert_expertise`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the expertise of the expert on a scale from very expert to very non-expert.
* **19. Would you re-visit this expert?** (`expert_will_revisit`, `select_one`, required: false): Select yes or no if you would re-visit the expert.
* **20. Would you recommend this expert to others?** (`expert_recommend`, `select_one`, required: false): Select yes or no if you would recommend the expert to others.
* **21. What is your rating average for this expert?** (`expert_rating_average`, `number`, required: true): Enter your rating average on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **22. On a scale of 1-5** (`expert_rating_scale`, `number`, required: true): Enter your rating average on a scale of 1-5.
* **23. How much time did you spend with this expert?** (`expert_rating_average_time`, `time`, required: true): Enter the time spent with the expert.
* **24. Comments** (`expert_rating_average_comments`, `text`, required: true): Enter any additional comments about your experience with the expert.
* **25. Submit** (`submitted`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select how you would like to receive a confirmation of your submission.
