# Curriculum Socialization Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Curriculum Socialization Feedback form is used to collect feedback from participants about their experiences with the facilitator and the session. This feedback is invaluable for us to improve and provide better services in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Participant Name**, **School Name**, and **Email** to provide us with your contact information.
2. Rate your **Participant Rating** of the session, on a scale from 1 to 10.
3. Share your **Comments** about the session, if any.
4. Indicate whether you **Recommended** the session or not.
5. Fill in the **Facilitator Title**, **Session Topic**, and **Session Location** details.
6. Provide your **Session Duration** and **Session Length** in hours.
7. Select the **Session Type** (e.g., workshop, lecture, etc.).
8. Choose your **Participant Attendance**, **Participant Dropped**, and **Participant No Show** ratings.
9. Rate your **Facilitator Availability** and **Overall Experience**.
10. Add any **Session Notes** you'd like to share.
11. Finally, fill in your **Phone** number for us to contact you, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school you're representing.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with the session, on a scale from 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the session.
* **Recommended** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, optional): Check if you'd like to recommend this session or not.
* **Facilitator Title** (`facilitator_title`, text, optional): Enter the facilitator's title or position.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, optional): Enter the topic covered in the session.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, time, optional): Enter the length of the session in hours.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Rate the length of the session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, text, optional): Select the type of session (e.g., workshop, lecture, etc.).
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, number, optional): Rate your attendance in the session.
* **Participant Dropped** (`participant_dropped`, number, optional): Rate whether you dropped the session.
* **Participant No Show** (`participant_no_show`, number, optional): Rate whether you were a no-show.
* **Facilitator Availability** (`facilitator_availability`, number, optional): Rate the facilitator's availability.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience with the session.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you, if needed.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information about the session.
