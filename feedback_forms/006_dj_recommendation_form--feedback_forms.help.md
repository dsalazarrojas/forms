# DJ Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help us gather information about a DJ recommendation. If you're a DJ looking for a gig, an event planner seeking a talented DJ, or a customer looking to book a DJ, this form will help us better understand your request and provide a more accurate match.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your DJ's name in the "DJ Name" field.
2. Provide your DJ's email address in the "DJ Email" field.
3. Enter the name of the event planner or organization you're working with in the "Event Planner Name" field.
4. List the names of recommended DJs in the "Recommended DJS List" field.
5. Enter the email address of the event planner or organization in the "Event Planner Email" field.
6. Enter the name of the event in the "Event Name" field.
7. Choose the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
8. Select the time of the event in the "Event Time" field (if applicable).
9. Optionally, provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Select the category of the event (e.g., Music Festival, Birthday Party) in the "Event Category" field.
11. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field (if applicable).
12. Enter a brief description of the event in the "Event Description" field (if applicable).
13. Enter a brief description of the DJ you're recommending in the "DJ Description" field (if applicable).
14. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (if applicable).
15. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field (if applicable).
16. Enter the venue of the event in the "Event Venue" field (if applicable).
17. If the event has a limited capacity, enter the number of attendees in the "Event Capacity" field.
18. If the event has a specific length, enter the number of hours or minutes in the "Event Length" field.
19. Select the format of the event (e.g., Live, Pre-recorded) in the "Event Format" field.
20. Select the status of the event (e.g., Upcoming, Passed) in the "Event Status" field.
21. Add any relevant tags to the event in the "Event Tags" field (if applicable).
22. Add any additional notes in the "Note 2", "Note 3", or "Note 4" fields (if applicable).

* **DJ Description**: Enter a brief description of the DJ you're recommending.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (if applicable).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address (if applicable).
* **Event Venue**: Enter the venue of the event (if applicable).
* **Event Capacity**: If the event has a limited capacity, enter the number of attendees.
* **Event Length**: If the event has a specific length, enter the number of hours or minutes.
* **Event Format**: Select the format of the event (e.g., Live, Pre-recorded).
