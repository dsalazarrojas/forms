# tablet_feedback_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tablet_feedback_form_template" form is designed to collect feedback from users on the overall quality of a course, their personal details, and any attachments or comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready to fill out the form.
2. Select the overall quality of the course by choosing a number from 1-5 in the "Overall Quality" field.
3. Enter your User ID.
4. Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.
5. Answer the question "What did you think of the course?" in the "What did you think of the course?" field.
6. If you have any suggestions for improvement, answer the question "How could it be improved?" in the "How could it be improved?" field.
7. If you have any attachments or comments, use the "Attachment" fields to upload or enter your comments.
8. If you want to provide your email address, enter it in the "Email" field.
9. If you want to provide your phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
10. If you want to select from the options provided, use the "Select One" or "Select Multiple" fields.
11. You can also add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Overall Quality
* **Overall Quality** (Number): This field asks you to rate the overall quality of the course on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### User ID
* **User ID** (Number): Enter your User ID.

### Course Name
* **Course Name** (Text): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback on.

### What did you think of the course?
* **What did you think of the course?** (Text): Share your thoughts about the course.

### How could it be improved?
* **How could it be improved?** (Text): Provide any suggestions for improvement.

### Attachment
* **Attachment** (Text): Attach any relevant documents or files.

### Attachment 2
* **Attachment 2** (Text): Attach any other relevant documents or files.

### Attachment 3
* **Attachment 3** (Text): Attach any other relevant documents or files.

### Email
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted.

### Phone
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted.

### Date
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Time
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time in the format hh:mm.

### Select One
* **Select One** (Select One): Choose one of the provided options.

### Select Multiple
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple): Choose multiple options from the provided list.

### Note
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes.

### Comments
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the course.
