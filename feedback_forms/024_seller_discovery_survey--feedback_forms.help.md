# Seller Discovery Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from sellers about their business details for discovery purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select your preferred contact method and availability from the options provided.
3. Enter your name and email address.
4. Write a brief description of your business and product offerings in the feedback section.
5. Choose the category of seller you belong to and the types of products you deal with.
6. Select your preferred contact method and availability.
7. Enter your time zone (if applicable).
8. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (\*)**: Enter your name to help us identify you and your business.
* **Email (\*)**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Feedback (\*)**: Please provide a brief description of your business and product offerings. This will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Location (\*)**: Enter your location so that we can better understand your business operations.
* **Seller Type**: Choose one of the following: Retailer, Wholesaler, Distributor, or Other.
* **Product Type**: Select all the categories that apply to your business (e.g., Electronics, Books, Toys, Fashion, Other).
* **Product Category**: Enter a brief description of the category of products you deal with.
* **Language**: Choose one of the following: English, Spanish, French, or Other.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose one of the following: Phone, Email, In-person.
* **Available Time**: Select a time that you are available.
* **Timezone**: Enter your time zone to ensure accurate scheduling.
* **Available Days**: Select a day of the week when you are available.
* **Phone**: Optional. Enter your phone number.
* **Seller_type**: Optional. If you don't fit into a standard category, enter other and describe your business model.
* **Product_type**: Optional. If you don't fit into standard categories, select "Other" and describe your products.
* **Product_category**: Optional. Enter a brief description of your products.
* **Available_days**: Optional. If you are available on specific days, list them.
* **Available_time**: Optional. Enter a time when you are available.
* **Phone**: Optional. Enter your phone number.
* **Seller_type**: Optional. If you don't fit into a standard category, enter other and describe your business model.
* **Product_type**: Optional. If you don't fit into standard categories, select "Other" and describe your products.
* **Product_category**: Optional. Enter a brief description of your products.
* **Available_days**: Optional. If you are available on specific days, list them.
* **Available_time**: Optional. Enter a time when you are available.
* **Timezone**: Optional. Enter your time zone.
* **Language**: Optional. Select one of the following: English, Spanish, French, or Other.
* **Preferred_contact_method**: Optional. Choose one of the following: Phone, Email, In-person.

## Tips

* Please be as specific as possible when filling in the fields to help us better understand your business.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for help.
* This form is for discovery purposes only, and the information collected will be used to improve our services.
* We will review each response carefully to ensure accuracy and consistency.
