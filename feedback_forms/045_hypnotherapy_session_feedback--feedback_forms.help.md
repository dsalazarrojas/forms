# Hypnotherapy Session Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients after a hypnotherapy session. It aims to gather information about the client's experience, satisfaction, and any additional comments they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
3. Provide your feedback in the "Feedback" field.
4. Rate your experience by selecting a number between 1 and 5 in the "Rating" field.
5. If you've had a session, specify its length in minutes in the "Session Length" field.
6. Choose the type(s) of session you attended in the "Session Type" field. You can select one or multiple options.
7. Enter the date you attended the session in the "Session Date" field.
8. If applicable, enter the time of your session in the "Session Time" field.
9. If you have a hypnotherapist assigned to you, enter their name in the "Hypnotherapist" field.
10. Provide any additional comments about your session in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts about your session.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 5.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): If you had a session, enter its length in minutes.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of session you attended.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your session.
* **Hypnotherapist** (`hypnotherapist`, text, optional): If you have a hypnotherapist assigned to you, enter their name.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your session.
