# Fan Engagement Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fan Engagement Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from fans about their experience at events or with the brand. This information will be used to improve the quality and engagement of future events and to better understand the fan base.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Begin by rating your overall satisfaction with the event on the "Feedback Satisfaction" page.
3. On the "Feedback Content" page, type a brief description of your experience with the event.
4. On the "Event Experience" page, select the time frame in which you attended the event.
5. On the "Fan Preferences" page, select all the options that best describe what you enjoyed most about the event or what you would like to see more of in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Satisfaction** (`number`, required/optional: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback Content** (`text`, required/optional: false): Type a brief description of your experience with the event. This will help us understand what made your experience enjoyable.
* **Event Experience** (`time`, required/optional: false): Select the time frame in which you attended the event. This will help us understand when you attended and what you experienced.
* **Fan Preferences** (`select_multiple`, required/optional: false): Select all the options that best describe what you enjoyed most about the event or what you would like to see more of in the future. Options include:
	+ Action figure
	+ Behind-the-scenes Content
	+ Discounts
	+ Giveaways
	+ Live Events

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing feedback to help us better understand your experience.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the "Feedback Content" field.
* You can select multiple options for "Fan Preferences" as there may be multiple things that you enjoyed or would like to see more of.
