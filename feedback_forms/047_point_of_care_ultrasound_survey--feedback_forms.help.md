# point_of_care_ultrasound_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Point of Care Ultrasound Survey" form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of care provided at the point of care, including the type of technology used, rating, and comments on the care process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide the date and time of care.
4. Select the type of technology used during the care process (e.g., USG, CT, MRI, or Ultrasound).
5. Rate your experience with the care process using the rating scale provided.
6. Add any comments or suggestions for improvement.
7. Identify who the feedback was submitted to (e.g., Ultrasound Program, Ultrasound Director, Chair, Dean, Residency Coordinator, or Other).
8. Enter the ID of the person who submitted the feedback.
9. Select the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Date of Care** (date): Enter the date of care.
* **Time of Care** (time): Enter the time of care.
* **Type of Technology** (select one): Select the type of technology used during the care process.
	+ USG: Ultrasound
	+ CT: Computed Tomography
	+ MRI: Magnetic Resonance Imaging
	+ Ultrasound: Ultrasound
* **Rating** (number): Rate your experience with the care process using the provided rating scale.
* **Comments** (text): Add any comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Submitted To** (select multiple): Identify who the feedback was submitted to.
	+ Ultrasound Program
	+ Ultrasound Director
	+ Chair
	+ Dean
	+ Residency Coordinator
	+ Other
* **Submitted By ID** (number): Enter the ID of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **Submitted On ID** (number): Enter the ID of the date and time of submission.
* **Rating Score** (number): Enter the rating score.
* **Submitted To ID** (number): Enter the ID of who the feedback was submitted to.
* **Total Score** (number): Enter the total score.

Please note that the "Submitted By ID", "Submitted On ID", "Rating Score", "Submitted To ID", and "Total Score" fields are not visible to end-users.
