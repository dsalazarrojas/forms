# Recreational Program Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Recreational Program Feedback form is designed to gather information from participants and staff members who have attended recreational programs. The feedback collected will be used to evaluate the effectiveness and impact of the programs, identify areas for improvement, and inform future program planning and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to answer each question.
2. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields (marked as "required").
3. For fields not marked as "required", you can choose whether or not to provide information.
4. Use the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the program you attended. This will help us identify the specific program being evaluated.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you attended the program. If unsure, you can enter the date range (e.g., "January 1 - February 28").
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the program was "Yes" or "No" to indicate if it was a recreational program.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name, if you are a participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you are a participant.
* **Program Attended** (`program_attended`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you attended the program ("Yes" or "No").
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the program.
* **Program Outcomes** (`program_outcomes`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable outcomes from the list to indicate what you achieved or experienced as a result of the program ("Yes" or "No").
* **Program Comments** (`program_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the program, such as what you liked or disliked.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member who led or coordinated the program.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the staff member who led or coordinated the program.
* **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the program or staff member, such as areas of improvement.
