# Contact Form Rock Background - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about the "Rock Background" feature. Please fill out this form to provide your thoughts and suggestions to help us improve the feature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form and make sure you have understood each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the required fields (indicated by `**Required**` in the explanations below).
3. Make your selection in the select_one fields by choosing the appropriate option from the dropdown list.
4. Write your comments or feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Feedback Form --** (`header_note`, note, **not required**): This is a placeholder field to separate the form header from the main content.
* **Name** (`name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name so we can identify you properly.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your contact email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Feedback category** (`feedback_category`, select_one, **required**): Select the category that best describes your feedback. You can choose from:
	+ Website design
	+ Content
	+ Functionality
	+ Other
* **Overall rating** (`rating`, number, **not required**): Rate your experience from 1 to 5.
* **Detailed feedback** (`comments`, text, **required**): Describe your thoughts and suggestions in detail.
* **Would you like a follow up?** (`follow_up`, select_one, **required**): Select `Yes` if you want our team to contact you after submitting this form.
* **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, **not required**): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
* **Additional details** (`additional_details`, text, **not required**): Share any other relevant information that will help us better understand your feedback.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can process your feedback properly.
* Be as detailed as possible in the comments field to provide valuable feedback.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, feel free to contact us through other channels.
