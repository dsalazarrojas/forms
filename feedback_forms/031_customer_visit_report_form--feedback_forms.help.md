# Customer Visit Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Customer Visit Report Form is used to collect feedback or assess the quality of service provided to customers during their visits. It's designed to gather information on various aspects of the visit and helps to improve service quality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page in sequence.
2. For each page, provide as much information as possible to help us better understand your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Visit Report Form Page 1** (Text field):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can provide any relevant comments or descriptions about your visit.
* **Customer Visit Form** (Select One field):
	+ Select the level of your visit from the options: Very Good, Very Poor, Good, Poor, or Neutral. This helps us understand the overall experience of your visit.
* **Customer Visit Report Form Page 3** (Text field):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
* **Note** (Note field):
	+ Use this field to provide any additional information about your visit that doesn't fit in the previous pages.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and specific as possible when filling out each page.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents related to your visit, you can attach them to this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your point of contact for support.
