# Enquiry Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users to provide assistance with their inquiries. Please use this template to submit your enquiry and we will do our best to respond promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the reason for your contact from the list of options.
2. Provide your contact details (phone number, email or other) if you wish to be contacted.
3. Enter your name and any additional comments or questions you have.
4. Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
5. Add any further comments or feedback you'd like to share.
6. Optionally, provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What would you like to contact us about** (`enquiry_form_template`, select_one): 
  This field allows you to select the reason for your contact from a list of pre-defined options, including "What is your feedback", "Questions", "Comments", or "Other". Please choose the one that best describes your inquiry.
* **Contact details** (`contact_details`, select_multiple): 
  If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your contact details, such as phone number, email, or other. You can select multiple options.
* **Message** (`message`, text): 
  Enter any message or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Name** (`name`, text): 
  Please enter your name for us to address you by.
* **Email** (`email`, email): 
  Enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): 
  Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you via phone.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): 
  Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Other** (`other`, text): 
  If your reason for contacting us is not listed, please describe it here.
* **Question** (`question`, text): 
  If you have any specific questions, please enter them here.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text): 
  Enter any additional feedback or comments about your experience with us.
