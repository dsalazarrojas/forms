# market_research_completion_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants who have completed a market research study. The goal is to gather information about their experience with the study and identify areas that need improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (2): This field is optional, but it's an opportunity for you to let us know who you are. Select all that apply from the list.
* **Study Details** (3): Choose the option that best describes your study experience.
* **Feedback** (4): Share your thoughts on your experience with the study. This is an essential part of helping us improve our process.
* **Demographic Information** (5): Select all that apply to help us better understand your demographic.
* **Additional Comments** (6): If you'd like to share more information or thoughts, feel free to type in here.
* **Introduction** (1): Please start by writing a brief introduction to let us know who you are and why you are participating.
Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
