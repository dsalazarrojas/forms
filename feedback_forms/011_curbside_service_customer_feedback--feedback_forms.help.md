# curbside_service_customer_feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect customer feedback on curbside service experiences. It allows customers to provide ratings and comments on the service they received, which helps us improve our services and better meet customer needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary permissions to access the contact information of the customer.
2. Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience.
3. Provide detailed comments and feedback in the relevant fields.
4. Ensure that you have formatted the date and time fields correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Contact Permission
* `Contact Permission` (`contact_permission`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field allows you to indicate whether you have permission to access the contact information of the customer.

### 2. Prophets Score
* `Prophets Score` (`prophets_score`, `number`, optional): This field is used to rate the service provider's score. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10.

### 3. Contact Number
* `Contact Number` (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the customer's contact number.

### 4. Service Rating
* `Service Rating` (`service_rating`, `select_one`, optional): This field allows you to rate the service provider's performance. You can select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### 5. Contact Email
* `Contact Email` (`contact_email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to enter the customer's email address.

### 6. Comments
* `Comments` (`comments`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide detailed comments and feedback on the service experience.

### 7. Visit Date
* `Visit Date` (`visit_date`, `date`, optional): This field is used to enter the date of the visit. Please format the date as `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 8. Visit Time
* `Visit Time` (`visit_time`, `time`, optional): This field is used to enter the time of the visit. Please format the time as `HH:MM`.

### 9. Service Type
* `Service Type` (`service_type`, `select_one`, optional): This field allows you to select the type of service provided. You can choose one of the following options:
	+ Curbside
	+ Other

### 10. Service Provider
* `Service Provider` (`service_provider`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the service provider.

### 11. Customer Name
* `Customer Name` (`customer_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the customer's name.

### 12. Customer Phone
* `Customer Phone` (`customer_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the customer's phone number.

### 13. Customer Email
* `Customer Email` (`customer_email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to enter the customer's email address.

### 14. Customer Address
* `Customer Address` (`customer_address`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the customer's address.

### 15. Employee Name
* `Employee Name` (`employee_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the employee who provided the service.

### 16. Employee Phone
* `Employee Phone` (`employee_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the phone number of the employee who provided the service.

### 17. Employee Email
* `Employee Email` (`employee_email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to enter the email address of the employee who provided the service.

### 18. Manager Name
* `Manager Name` (`manager_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the manager who was involved in the service.

### 19. Manager Phone
* `Manager Phone` (`manager_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the phone number of the manager who was involved in the service.

### 20. Manager Email
* `Manager Email` (`manager_email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to enter the email address of the manager who was involved in the service.

### 21. Department
* `Department` (`department`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the department that provided the service.

### 22. Job Title
* `Job Title` (`job_title`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the job title of the employee who provided the service.

### 23. Comments
* `Comments` (`comments`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide additional comments and feedback on the service experience.

### 24. Rating Note
* `Rating Note` (`rating_note`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide additional feedback on the rating.

### 25. Rating Note 2
* `Rating Note 2` (`rating_note_2`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide additional feedback on the rating.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access the contact information of the customer.
* Be concise and clear in your feedback to help us understand your experience.
* If you're unsure about any field or have any questions, feel free to ask.
