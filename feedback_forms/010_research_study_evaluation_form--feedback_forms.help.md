# Research Study Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Study Evaluation Form is a tool for researchers to collect and document feedback and information about a research study. This form can be used to gather insights about the study's participants and assess the effectiveness of the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Fill in the required fields as they are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Enter a brief description of the research study in the **Research Study Evaluation Form** field.
* Provide information about the researcher and their role in the **Researcher Info** field.
* Enter details about the study, such as the location and duration, in the **Study Info** field.
* Add any relevant participant information, such as their contact details, in the **Participant Info** field.
* Enter a brief assessment of the study's effectiveness in the **Study Effectiveness** field.
* Enter any additional feedback or comments about the study in the **Feedback** field.
* Enter any additional comments or observations in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Study Evaluation Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the research study.
* **Researcher Info** (`researcher_info`, text, required): This field should include the researcher's name, affiliation and contact details.
* **Study Info** (`study_info`, text, required): Enter details about the study's location, duration, and other relevant information.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant participant contact details or background information.
* **Study Effectiveness** (`study_effectiveness`, text, required): Enter a brief assessment of the study's effectiveness, including both positive and negative aspects.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the study.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the study.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the study.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the study was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time the study was completed.
