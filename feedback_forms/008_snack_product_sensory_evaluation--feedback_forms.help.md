# Snack Product Sensory Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snack Product Sensory Evaluation form is designed to gather feedback on a specific snack product. This form helps us understand how the product tastes, looks, feels, smells, and overall satisfaction. Your input will help us improve the product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the instructions and questions below to ensure you are providing feedback on the correct product.
2. Answer each question in the best way you can, based on your real experience with the product.
3. Be honest in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Taste** (`taste`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the taste of the snack product. What flavors do you sense when you eat it?
* **Texture** (`texture`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the texture of the snack product. Is it crunchy, chewy, or somewhere in between?
* **Satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with the snack product?
* **Appearance** (`appearance`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the appearance of the snack product. What does it look like?
* **Smell** (`smell`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the smell of the snack product. Is it strong or weak?
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `number`, required/optional): On a scale of 1-5, how satisfied are you with the snack product overall?
* **Would you Recommend** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required/optional): Would you recommend this snack product? Choose one of the options: "True", "False", or "not sure"
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts about the snack product. This is an opportunity to mention anything that didn't fit in the previous answers.
