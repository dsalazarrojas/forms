# Organizational Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Organizational Awareness Survey form, designed to help employees provide feedback on the company's core values and objectives. The survey aims to gather insights from employees on how they see these values and objectives reflected in their work and how they can be effectively communicated and achieved within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully and provide detailed answers to each question.
2.  Respond honestly and openly, as your feedback is crucial for the company's growth and development.
3.  If you have any suggestions or ideas, feel free to share them in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Objective Statement 1**: This section asks about the company's core values and objectives. Please describe how you've seen these values and objectives reflected in your work.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: Yes
*   **Elaborate on Objective 1**: This section is an optional field where you can provide more details about how you've seen the company's values and objectives reflected in your work.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Feedback 2**: This section asks about the impact of the company's values and objectives on your role and responsibilities.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Feedback 3**: This section is also optional and asks about areas where you think the company could improve on its values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Feedback 4**: This section is about how you think the company's values and objectives align with your personal values and goals.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Challenge 1**: This section is about the biggest challenge you face in achieving the company's values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Solution 1**: This section is about how you think the company can overcome this challenge.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Resource 1**: This section is about the resources you think the company should provide to support you in achieving its values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Recognition 1**: This section is about ways the company can recognize and reward employees for demonstrating its values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Unintended Consequences 1**: This section is about potential unintended consequences of adopting the company's values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Progress Measurement 1**: This section is about how you think the company can measure progress towards its values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Vision Statement**: This section is about your thoughts on the company's vision statement.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Communication**: This section is about how you think the company can communicate effectively with employees about its values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Diversity**: This section is about cultural or diversity-related aspects of the company that are essential to achieving its values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Training**: This section is about specific training or development opportunities that would support employees in achieving the company's values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Culture**: This section is about how you think the company can foster a culture of open communication about its values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
*   **Challenges**: This section is about potential barriers or obstacles to achieving the company's values and objectives.
    *   Type: Text Field
    *   Required: No
