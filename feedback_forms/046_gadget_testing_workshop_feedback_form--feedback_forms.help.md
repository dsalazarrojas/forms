# Gadget Testing Workshop Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants after attending a gadget testing workshop. Your responses will help us understand your experience, impressions, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Participant Name, Email, Phone, and Location) to help us keep track of your feedback.
2. Answer the questions about your workshop experience (Workshop Participation, Impressions, Design, Ease of Use, Value, and Overall Experience).
3. Provide any additional suggestions or comments about the gadget in the "Gadget Improvement" field.
4. Finally, add any additional notes or comments about your experience in the "Participant Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Participation** (Text): Describe your participation in the workshop, e.g., "I attended the workshop as a participant" or "I was a speaker/presenter".
* **What was your impression of the gadget?** (Text): Share your overall impression of the gadget, e.g., "It was amazing", "I was not impressed", or "It needs improvement".
* **How would you rate the gadget's design?** (Select One: Unsatisfactory, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent): Rate the gadget's design, focusing on its aesthetic appeal and functionality.
* **How easy was it to use the gadget?** (Select One: Very Difficult, Somewhat Difficult, Neutral, Somewhat Easy, Very Easy): Rate the ease of use of the gadget, from very difficult to very easy.
* **What value do you think the gadget provides?** (Select Multiple: Very Low, Low, Neutral, High, Very High): Rate the value of the gadget, from very low to very high.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with the workshop?** (Select One: Very Poor, Poor, Neutral, Good, Very Good, Excellent): Rate your overall experience with the workshop, from very poor to excellent.
* **What can be improved in the gadget?** (Text): Share any suggestions or ideas for improvement about the gadget.
* **Participant Name** (Text): Enter your name as a participant in the workshop.
* **Participant Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Organization** (Text): Enter your organization's name.
* **Participant Job Title** (Text): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Department** (Text): Enter your department within your organization.
* **Participant Location** (Text): Enter your location.
* **Participant Notes** (Note): Add any additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
