# Logistics Delivery Employment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Logistics Delivery Employment Feedback Form is designed to gather insights from team members on how the team can improve its delivery operations. The feedback collected through this form will help the team make informed decisions and implement changes to enhance its performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have the necessary information for each field.
2. Select the most accurate option that applies to your experience in the relevant field.
3. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please use the "feedback" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **delivery_form_page** (`page 1`): This is the first page of the form where you will find the "delivery form page" label. This field is not required and you can skip it if you're unsure.
- **feedback_form_page** (`page 2`): This is the second page of the form where you will find the "feedback form page" label. Again, this field is not required.
- **job_experience** (`select one`): Select the option that best describes your experience with the logistics delivery team:
  - Option 1: Very Good
  - Option 2: Neutral
  - Option 3: Very Bad
- **improvement_opportunities** (`select multiple`): Choose all the options that represent opportunities for improvement:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
- **job_experience** (`select one`): Select the option that best describes your experience with the team:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
- **improvement_opportunities** (`select multiple`): Choose all the options that represent opportunities for improvement:
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
- **delivery_operations** (`text`): Provide any additional feedback or comments about your delivery operations experience.
- **team_communication** (`number`): If you have any feedback regarding team communication, please provide a rating from 1-10, where 1 means "very bad" and 10 means "excellent".
- **feedback** (`text`): If you have any additional feedback or comments that don't fit in the other fields, please use this field to provide more information.
