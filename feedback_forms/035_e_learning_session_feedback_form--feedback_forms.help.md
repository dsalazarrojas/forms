# e_learning_session_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their e-learning session experiences. It helps us understand their satisfaction and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to begin.
2. Fill out the form fields as prompted. Each field is explained below.
3. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
4. Click "Submit" to send your feedback to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback (Session Feedback)**: Please provide any comments or observations about the session you attended. This is a text box for free-form feedback.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments**: Share any specific comments or thoughts about the session. This is another text box for more detailed feedback.
* **Instructor Feedback**: Indicate if you found the instructor helpful or not. You can select one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, please let us know. This is a text box for ideas and recommendations.
* **Session Date**: If you know the date of the session you attended, please enter it in this format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-03-01). This will help us understand the context of your feedback.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve future e-learning sessions.
* If you have trouble with a field or need more information, contact our support team for assistance.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by one person at a time. If you are completing the form on behalf of someone else, please ensure that you are providing their feedback accurately.
