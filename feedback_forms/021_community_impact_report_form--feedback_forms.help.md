# Community Impact Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Impact Report Form is a data collection tool designed to gather information about the impact of a project or initiative within a community. It helps to gather achievements, lessons learned, and areas for improvement for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out this form in one sitting, taking your time to provide accurate and detailed responses.
2. Answer based on your direct involvement and knowledge of the project.
3. Provide bullet points for key outcomes achieved and lessons learned.
4. Keep your responses concise, about 1-2 paragraphs per field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Impact Report --**: This is the title of the report section.
* **Reporter name**: Enter your name and title as the person reporting on this project.
	+ This field is required to identify the person responsible for the report.
* **Organization name**: Enter the name of your organization if applicable.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to contextualize the project within an institution.
* **Project name**: Enter the name of the project being reported on.
	+ This field is required to clearly identify the project.
* **Reporting period**: Enter the dates covered by this report.
	+ This field is required to specify the scope of the report.
* **Key outcomes achieved**: Enter bullet points describing the key achievements of the project.
	+ This field is required to gather information about the project's impact.
* **Metrics and evidence**: Provide data points and supporting evidence for each outcome.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to quantify the impact.
* **Challenges faced**: Describe any barriers or obstacles encountered during the project.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to identify areas for improvement.
* **Lessons learned**: Enter practical insights for future projects.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to apply knowledge gained from the project.
* **Follow up actions**: Enter next steps and responsible parties for follow-up actions.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to plan for the future.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing each field.
* Keep your responses concise, about 1-2 paragraphs per field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
