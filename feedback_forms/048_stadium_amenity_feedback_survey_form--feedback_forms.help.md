# Stadium Amenity Feedback Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with the stadium's amenities. Your input will help us understand what we do well and what we can improve to provide an even better experience for our visitors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction on the first page to understand the purpose and context of the form.
2. On the subsequent pages, fill in the requested information as accurately as possible.
3. Be honest and provide as much detail as you can about your experience.
4. If you have any additional comments, feel free to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, Number, **Required**): Rate your overall satisfaction with the stadium's amenities, from 0 (very dissatisfied) to 100 (very satisfied).
* **Amenities Experience** (`amenities_experience`, Text, **Required**): Describe your experience with the stadium's facilities and services.
* **Amenities Rating** (`amenities_rating`, Number, **Required**): Rate the quality of the stadium's facilities and services, from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, **Optional**): If you have any specific comments or suggestions about your experience, please add them here.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **Required**): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, **Optional**): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, **Optional**): If you have any additional comments or suggestions that don't fit in the above fields, please add them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide as much detail as you can about your experience.
* If you have any issues or problems during your visit, please let us know so we can address them.
* If you would like to be contacted about your feedback, please enter your email or phone number.
