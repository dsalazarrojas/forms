# staff_training_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "staff_training_feedback_form" is designed to collect feedback from staff members after attending a training session. The feedback will help us understand how the session was perceived by the staff members and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1. Select the session ID, date, and topic of the training session you attended.
2. Provide your name as the trainer.
3. Rate the session on a scale from 1 to 5.
4. Share your overall experience of the session on a scale from 1 to 5.
5. Share any comments or thoughts about the session.
6. Suggest any improvements or changes that could be made to the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_id**: Enter the ID of the training session you attended.
* **session_date**: Enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **trainer_name**: Enter the name of the trainer.
* **session_topic**: Enter the topic of the training session.
* **trainer_rating**: Rate the session from 1 to 5.
* **overall_experience**: Rate your overall experience of the session from 1 to 5.
* **comments**: Share any comments or thoughts about the session.
* **suggestions**: Suggest any improvements or changes that could be made to the session.
