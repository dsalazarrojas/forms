# Networking Event Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from attendees, organizers, and vendors about a networking event.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please complete the form only if you attended the networking event. If you're a vendor or an organizer, you're free to fill out the form too.
- Take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly. You can skip questions that don't apply to you or your experience.
- If you're unsure about any question, just write down what you think is correct or what you'd like to share with us.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction**: A brief introduction to this form. Please ignore it, you won't need to fill it out.
- **event_attended**: Choose how you attended the event:
  - Attendee: I attended as a regular attendee.
  - Organizer: I organized the event.
  - Vendor: I was a vendor at the event.
- **attendee_name**: Your name (if you're an attendee).
- **attendee_email**: Your email address (if you'd like to be contacted back).
- **feedback**: Share your thoughts about the event. What did you think about the speakers, the venue, and the overall experience?
- **suggestions**: Share any suggestions or ideas you have for future events or improvements.
- **connection_outcomes**: Select all that apply:
  - Met someone new
  - Learned something new
  - Decided to do something new
  - Other (please specify)
- **satisfaction_level**: How satisfied are you with the event? Choose a number:
  - 1 - Not Satisfied
  - 2 - Somewhat Satisfied
  - 3 - Satisfied
  - 4 - Very Satisfied
  - 5 - Extremely Satisfied
- **likely_to_attend**: How likely are you to attend future events? Choose a number:
  - 1 - Not likely
  - 2 - Somewhat likely
  - 3 - Likely
  - 4 - Extremely Likely
- **next_step**: If you have any next steps planned, select a date.
- **next_time**: If you have any next steps planned, choose a time.
- **comments**: Any additional comments or suggestions you have.
- **follow_up**: Would you like to be followed up on this form? Choose a number:
  - 1 - Yes
  - 2 - No
  - 3 - Undecided
