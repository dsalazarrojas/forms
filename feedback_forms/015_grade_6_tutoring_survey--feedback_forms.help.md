# Grade 6 Tutoring Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students who have received tutoring services. It will help us understand their experiences and gather information to improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Each field is optional, but please fill out the required fields (Student Name, Number of Grades Completed, Additional Comments, and Date Completed).
3. Use the space provided for each field to provide as much detail as possible.
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Suggestions for Improvement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Tutor Support** (`tutor_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the tutor's support, from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Number of Grades Completed** (`grade_completed`, `number`, required): Indicate the number of tutoring sessions you completed.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, optional): If you have any other information about the course you attended, please enter it here.
* **Time Spent on Each Session** (`time_spent`, `number`, optional): If you can estimate the time spent on each session, please enter it here.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required): Please enter the date when you completed the sessions.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions on how we can improve our services, please enter them here.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We appreciate your feedback and will use it to improve our services.
