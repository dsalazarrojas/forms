# Application Advisor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from students about their experience with an advisor. It helps us to understand how the advisor performed and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your feedback. If a field is marked as "optional", you can skip it if you don't have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Student name** (`student_name`, required): 
Please enter your full name as it appears on your records.

### 2. **Advisor name** (`advisor_name`, optional): 
If you remember, enter the name of the advisor you met with. If not, you can skip this field.

### 3. **Meeting date** (`meeting_date`, optional): 
Enter the date of the advising session you attended.

### 4. **Satisfaction rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, required): 
Rate your overall satisfaction with the advising session:
	* Very satisfied
	* Satisfied
	* Neutral
	* Dissatisfied
	* Very dissatisfied

### 5. **Clarity of advice** (`clarity_of_advice`, required): 
Check the box to indicate if the guidance you received was clear and actionable:
	* True: The guidance was clear and easy to follow.
	* False: The guidance was not clear or unclear.
	* Partly: The guidance was clear for some parts, but unclear for others.

### 6. **Usefulness of recommendations** (`usefulness`, required): 
Check the box to indicate how helpful the recommendations you received were:
	* Very useful
	* Somewhat useful
	* Not useful

### 7. **Improvements suggested** (`improvements_suggested`, optional): 
If you have any suggestions on how the advisor can improve their performance, provide them here.

### 8. **Follow up needed** (`follow_up_needed`, required): 
Check the box to indicate if further follow-up is needed:
	* True: Further follow-up is required.
	* False: Further follow-up is not required.

### 9. **Follow up actions** (`follow_up_actions`, optional): 
If follow-up is needed, describe the next steps you would like the advisor to take.

### 10. **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): 
If you have any other comments or feedback, you can include them here.
