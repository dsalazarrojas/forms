# Office Cleanliness Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the overall cleanliness of the office. It is used by maintenance staff to assess the cleanliness standards and make necessary changes to maintain a healthy and hygienic environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Rate the cleanliness of the office by selecting a number from 1 to 5 in the "cleanliness_rating" field.
2. If you have any concerns or issues with the office cleanliness, please describe them in the "cleanliness_concerns" field.
3. If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them in the "suggestions" field.
4. If you would like to be contacted for further information, please provide your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
5. Finally, select the "category" field to indicate that this form is related to a "Feedback Forms" or "Administrative Forms" category.
6. If you have any additional information or context that would be helpful for our staff to know, please provide it in the "description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### cleanliness_rating (Number)
* This field allows you to rate the cleanliness of the office on a scale from 1 to 5, with 1 being the least clean and 5 being the cleanest.
### cleanliness_concerns (Text)
* This field is for you to describe any concerns or issues you have with the office cleanliness.
### suggestions (Text)
* This field is for you to provide any suggestions on how to improve the office cleanliness.
### contact_info (Text)
* This field is for you to provide your contact information so our staff can reach out to you for further information or follow-up.
### category (Select One)
* This field is to select the category of feedback you are providing, which helps us to prioritize and route your feedback.
### sub_category (Text)
* This field is not currently being used, and you can ignore it.
### description (Text)
* This field is for you to provide any additional information or context that would be helpful for our staff to know.

Note: Please make sure to submit the form only once per issue, so we can efficiently address your concerns and suggestions.
