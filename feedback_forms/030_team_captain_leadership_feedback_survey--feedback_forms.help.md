# team_captain_leadership_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the performance of team captains, focusing on their leadership, communication, problem-solving, and time management skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please ensure you have the relevant context and permissions to provide feedback.
2.  Verify that this is not a duplicate submission for this team captain.
3.  Click on each field to expand it.
4.  Fill in the fields as follows:
    *   **Team Name** (`team_name`): Enter the name of the team being evaluated.
    *   **Captain Name** (`captain_name`): Enter the name of the team captain you are providing feedback for.
    *   **Leadership Score** (`captain_leadership_score`): Rate the captain's leadership skills, on a scale of 1-10.
    *   **Communication Score** (`communication_score`): Rate the captain's communication skills, on a scale of 1-10.
    *   **Problem-Solving Score** (`problem_solving_score`): Rate the captain's problem-solving skills, on a scale of 1-10.
    *   **Time Management Score** (`time_management_score`): Rate the captain's time management skills, on a scale of 1-10.
    *   **Feedback** (`feedback`): Provide any general comments or feedback about the captain's performance.
    *   **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about the captain's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Team Name** (`team_name`): This field is used to specify the name of the team being evaluated.
*   **Captain Name** (`captain_name`): Enter the name of the team captain you are providing feedback for.
*   **Leadership Score** (`captain_leadership_score`): This field is used to rate the captain's leadership skills. Please rate on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
*   **Communication Score** (`communication_score`): This field is used to rate the captain's communication skills. Please rate on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
*   **Problem-Solving Score** (`problem_solving_score`): This field is used to rate the captain's problem-solving skills. Please rate on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
*   **Time Management Score** (`time_management_score`): This field is used to rate the captain's time management skills. Please rate on a scale of 1-10, where 1 is low and 10 is high.
*   **Feedback** (`feedback`): You can provide any general comments or feedback about the captain's performance here.
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about the captain's performance here.
*   **Submit** (`submit`): Click this button to submit your feedback.
