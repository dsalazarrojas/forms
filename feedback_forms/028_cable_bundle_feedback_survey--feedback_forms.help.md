# Cable Bundle Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have experienced issues with the cable bundle service. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions to enhance the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide valuable feedback, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name (optional, but appreciated for us to address your concern directly).
2. Select the service area or region where you experienced issues (required).
3. Rate your experience with our installation process (1 low to 5 high, required).
4. Rate the reliability of our service (1 low to 5 high, required).
5. Share your experience with our customer support team (select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, required).
6. If you're willing, rate your likelihood to recommend our service (0 not at all to 10 highly likely, optional).
7. Describe any recurring problems you faced with our service (optional).
8. Share any suggestions you have for improving our service (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer name**: Enter your name, if you'd like us to address your concern directly. This is an optional field.
* **Service area or region**: Select the city or zip code where you experienced issues with our service. This is a required field, as it helps us understand which areas need improvement.
* **Installation experience rating**: Rate your experience with our installation process on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high). This is a required field, as it helps us understand what we can do better.
* **Service reliability rating**: Rate the uptime and consistency of our service on a scale from 1 (low) to 5 (high). This is a required field, as it helps us improve our service's reliability.
* **Customer support experience**: Share your experience with our customer support team. Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor. This is a required field, as it helps us understand how we can improve our support.
* **Likelihood to recommend**: If you're willing, rate your likelihood to recommend our service on a scale from 0 (not at all likely) to 10 (highly likely). This is an optional field.
* **Main issues faced**: Describe any recurring problems you faced with our service. Please be as detailed as possible. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand areas for improvement.
* **Suggestions**: Share any suggestions you have for improving our service. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand what we can do better.
