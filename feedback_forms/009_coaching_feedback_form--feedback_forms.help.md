# Coaching Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to provide coaches with feedback on their performance and to help improve their coaching style and service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and truthful information.
2. Answer each question according to your experience with the coach.
3. Provide feedback on the coach's performance, skills, and areas of improvement.
4. Rate the overall coaching experience using the provided scale.
5. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or email address.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coach you worked with.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the coaching session.
* **Your Current Performance Level** (`current_performance_level`, select_one, required): Rate your current performance level as Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Progress Since Last Coaching Session** (`progress_since_last_session`, select_one, required): Rate the progress you have made since the last coaching session as Significant, Good, Some, Little, or No progress.
* **How Useful Was This Coaching Session?** (`session_usefulness`, select_one, required): Rate the usefulness of the coaching session as Very useful, Useful, Somewhat useful, Not useful.
* **Clarity of Coach Guidance** (`clarity_of_guidance`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's guidance as Very clear, Clear, Somewhat clear, Unclear.
* **Actionable Advice Provided** (`actionable_advice`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's advice as Very actionable, Actionable, Somewhat actionable, Not actionable.
* **Coach Listening Skills** (`coach_listening_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's listening skills as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Coach Motivation and Encouragement** (`coach_motivation`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's motivation and encouragement as Very motivating, Motivating, Somewhat motivating, Not motivating.
* **Personalization of Coaching** (`personalization`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's personalization as Highly personalized, Personalized, Somewhat personalized, Generic.
* **Overall Coaching Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall coaching experience as Excellent, Good, Average, Poor.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are providing this feedback.
* **Specific Areas Where You Have Improved** (`specific_improvements`, text, optional): List specific skills or areas where you have improved.
* **What Could the Coach Improve?** (`coach_improvements`, text, optional): Provide feedback for the coach on areas they could improve.
* **What Additional Support Would Help?** (`additional_support`, text, optional): Suggest any additional support you need from the coach.
* **Specific Areas Where You Have Improved** (`specific_improvements`, text, optional): List specific skills or areas where you have improved.

## Tips

* Be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Provide specific examples and details to support your feedback.
* Use the provided scales to rate the coach's performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the coach or the support team.
* Your feedback is valuable and will help the coaches improve their services.
