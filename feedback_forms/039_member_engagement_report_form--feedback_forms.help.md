# Member Engagement Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information about your member experience with our organization. Your input will help us improve our services and better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as accurately as possible.
2. Use the options provided for each question to select your preferred response.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "What would you like to tell us about your experience?" field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for us to contact you (if you choose to provide them).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Awareness**: 
  (optional) How well do you think we have raised awareness about our events and activities among our members?
* **Member Participation**:
  (optional) How often do you participate in events and activities organized by us?
  Options: 
    * Always: I attend all events and activities.
    * Frequently: I attend many events and activities.
    * Occasionally: I occasionally attend events and activities.
    * Never: I do not attend any events and activities.
* **Communication Channels**:
  (optional, select multiple) Which channels do you prefer for receiving communications from us?
  Options: 
    * Email: Receive emails from us.
    * SMS: Receive SMS messages from us.
    * Phone: Receive phone calls from us.
    * In person: Receive face-to-face communication from us.
    * Social media: Receive messages from us on social media.
* **Communication Frequency**:
  (optional) How often do you want to receive communications from us?
  Options: 
    * Daily: Receive communications daily.
    * Weekly: Receive communications weekly.
    * Biweekly: Receive communications biweekly.
    * Monthly: Receive communications monthly.
    * Quarterly: Receive communications quarterly.
    * Yearly: Receive communications yearly.
* **Contact Us**:
  (optional, select multiple) How can we best contact you?
  Options: 
    * Email: Contact you by email.
    * Phone: Contact you by phone.
    * In person: Contact you in person.
    * Social media: Contact you on social media.
    * Postal mail: Contact you through postal mail.
* **Member Insights**:
  (optional) What kind of insights about our services would you like to share?
  Options: 
    * Demographics: Information about our member demographics.
    * Feedback: General feedback about our services.
    * Participation: Feedback about member participation.
    * Awareness: Feedback about event awareness.
    * Other: Other types of insights.
* **What would you like to tell us about your experience?**:
  (optional) This is a free text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with our organization.
* **Email**:
  (optional) If you want to be contacted by us via email, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone**:
  (optional) If you want to be contacted by us via phone, please enter your phone number here.
