# Personal Yearly Forecast Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help you reflect on your experiences, goals, and future intentions. It's meant to be a helpful guide for self-awareness and goal-setting to start a successful year.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please go through each page step-by-step. Start with the "Past Year" section and work your way through to the "Future Steps" section. 
- Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as this will help you gain the most benefit from the survey.
- Take your time, as this form may take a few minutes to complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Past Year** (`past_year`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the past year that ended (e.g., 01 Jan 2020).
* **Goals** (`goals`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you set any personal or professional goals for the past year. 
* **Reflections** (`reflections`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of what you learned or experienced in the past year.
* **Growth Areas** (`growth_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you feel you made progress or growth in any area (e.g., relationships, financial health, etc.).
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you faced any challenges or obstacles in the past year.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to take any next steps for growth or improvement in the areas mentioned above.
* **Goals and Aspirations** (`goals_and_aspirations`, `text`, optional): Describe your personal or professional goals and aspirations for the upcoming year.
* **Current State of Mind** (`current_state_of_mind`, `text`, optional): Describe how you feel about your current state of mind.
* **Emotional State** (`emotional_state`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you felt any emotions (e.g., happy, anxious, excited, etc.) in the past year.
* **Self-Care Practices** (`self_care_practices`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you engaged in any self-care practices (e.g., exercise, meditation, journaling, etc.) in the past year.
* **Relationships** (`relationships`, `text`, optional): Describe your current relationships with family, friends, or colleagues.
* **Physical Health** (`physical_health`, `text`, optional): Describe your physical health status in the past year.
* **Financial Health** (`financial_health`, `text`, optional): Describe your financial health status in the past year.
* **Spiritual Health** (`spiritual_health`, `text`, optional): Describe your spiritual health status in the past year.
* **Community Connections** (`community_connections`, `text`, optional): Describe your connections with your community.
* **Sense of Purpose** (`sense_of_purpose`, `text`, optional): Describe your sense of purpose or meaning.
* **Fulfillment and Satisfaction** (`fulfillment_and_satisfaction`, `text`, optional): Describe your level of fulfillment and satisfaction with your life.
* **Goals and Intentions** (`goals_and_intentions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you have goals and intentions for the upcoming year.
* **Self-Care Practices Next Steps** (`self_care_practices_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to continue or implement any self-care practices for the upcoming year.
* **Emotional State Next Steps** (`emotional_state_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to manage or improve your emotional state in the upcoming year.
* **Current State of Mind Next Steps** (`current_state_of_mind_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to take steps to improve your current state of mind in the upcoming year.
* **Goals and Intentions Next Steps** (`goals_and_intentions_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to set goals or intentions for the upcoming year.
* **Growth Areas Next Steps** (`growth_areas_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to focus on any growth areas for the upcoming year.
* **Relationships Next Steps** (`relationships_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to improve any relationships in the upcoming year.
* **Financial Health Next Steps** (`financial_health_next_steps`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer "Yes" if you plan to improve any financial health aspect in the upcoming year.

Please note that the field labels may not be entirely clear, so the explanations above aim to provide a more concrete understanding of each field's purpose. If you need further clarification, please contact us.
