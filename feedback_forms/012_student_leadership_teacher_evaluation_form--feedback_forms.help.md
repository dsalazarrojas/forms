# Student Leadership Teacher Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's leadership skills and abilities, as well as the strengths and weaknesses of a teacher's teaching approach.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    *   Enter the student's ID number in the `Student ID` field.
    *   Enter the name of the teacher being evaluated in the `Teacher Name` field.
    *   For each question, select the answer that best describes your perception of the student's abilities or the teacher's approach.
    *   Provide any additional comments you have about the student or teacher in the `Comments on Student` or `Comments on Teacher` fields.
    *   Enter a brief description of the student's leadership trend in the `Student Leadership Trend` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student ID (Required)**: Enter the student's unique ID number.
*   **Teacher Name (Required)**: Enter the name of the teacher being evaluated.
*   **Demonstrates (Required)**: Select how well the student demonstrates leadership skills.
    \- Strongly Agree: The student consistently shows initiative and leadership qualities.
    \- Somewhat Agree: The student occasionally shows leadership qualities.
    \- Neutral: The student neither shows nor lacks leadership qualities.
    \- Somewhat Disagree: The student rarely shows leadership qualities.
    \- Strongly Disagree: The student almost never shows leadership qualities.
*   **Leads (Required)**: Select how well the student leads others.
    \- Strongly Agree: The student consistently takes charge and leads others.
    \- Somewhat Agree: The student occasionally takes charge and leads others.
    \- Neutral: The student neither takes charge nor leads others.
    \- Somewhat Disagree: The student rarely takes charge and leads others.
    \- Strongly Disagree: The student almost never takes charge and leads others.
    \- Not at all: The student never takes charge or leads others.
*   **Takes (Required)**: Select how well the student takes responsibility.
    \- Strongly Agree: The student consistently takes responsibility for their actions.
    \- Somewhat Agree: The student occasionally takes responsibility for their actions.
    \- Neutral: The student neither takes nor lacks responsibility for their actions.
    \- Somewhat Disagree: The student rarely takes responsibility for their actions.
    \- Strongly Disagree: The student almost never takes responsibility for their actions.
*   **Comments on Student (Optional)**: Provide any additional comments about the student's behavior or performance.
*   **Comments on Teacher (Optional)**: Provide any additional comments about the teacher's teaching approach or effectiveness.
*   **Student Leadership Trend (Required)**: Enter a brief description of the student's leadership trend, e.g., "Growing in confidence and assertiveness" or "Needs improvement in delegation."
