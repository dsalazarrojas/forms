# Mastermind Group Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from participants in a Mastermind Group session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form and make sure you are providing feedback for the correct session.
2. Answer each question truthfully and provide as much detail as possible.
3. For questions with a "Category" label, select one of the provided options.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
5. Add any additional comments or notes if you have anything else you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Survey**: This field is where you can provide a brief description of what you learned during the session. Please be as specific as possible.
* **What did you learn today**: This field is where you can describe what new information or insights you gained from the session. Please try to be concise.
* **What was the most valuable part of the session**: In this field, describe the most valuable or interesting part of the session for you.
* **What did you struggle with during the session**: If there were any parts of the session where you experienced difficulties or challenges, describe them here.
* **What do you think could be improved for future sessions**: In this field, suggest any changes or improvements that you think could enhance future sessions.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional information or comments you'd like to share.
* **Category**: This field is for selecting a category that best describes your feedback. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that we can reach out to you if necessary.
* **Date**: Select the date you participated in the session, or leave it blank if you don't know it.
* **Time**: Select the time you participated in the session, or leave it blank if you don't know it.
* **Note**: This field is for any additional notes you'd like to include.
* **Category**: Another field for selecting a category that best describes your feedback. Please choose one of the provided options.
