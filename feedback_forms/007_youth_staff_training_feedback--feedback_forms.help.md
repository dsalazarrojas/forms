# Youth Staff Training Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from youth staff on their training experience. By completing this form, you will help us improve our training sessions and provide better support to our youth staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and select the corresponding answer for each field based on your experience.
2. Ensure you have the session ID number handy.
3. If a field is not relevant to your experience, do not provide an answer.
4. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
5. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session ID (1)**: Your session ID number, which will help us identify your submission.
	+ Type in the number of your session.
* **Overall Rating (2)**: Rate your overall experience with our training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Comments (3)**: Share any additional comments or feedback you have about your experience.
	+ Type in your thoughts and suggestions.
* **Suggestion (4)**: Share any suggestions you have for improving our training sessions.
	+ Type in your ideas.
* **Email (5)**: Your email address, which will help us contact you if needed.
	+ Type in your email address.
* **Phone (6)**: Your phone number, which will help us contact you if needed.
	+ Type in your phone number.
* **Trainer Performance (7)**: Rate the trainer's overall performance.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Materials (8)**: Rate the training materials provided.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Environment (9)**: Rate the environment where the training was conducted.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Timekeeping (10)**: Rate the timekeeping of the training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Content (11)**: Rate the content of the training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Facilitation (12)**: Rate the facilitation skills of the trainer.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Group Size (13)**: Rate the group size of the training session.
	+ 1 (Too small) - 5 (Just right)
* **Feedback (14)**: Select all that apply regarding your feedback on the training session.
	+ Check the boxes that apply.
* **Venue (15)**: Rate the venue of the training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Equipment (16)**: Rate the equipment provided for the training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Fellow Trainers (17)**: Rate the performance of fellow trainers.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Supervisors (18)**: Rate the performance of supervisors.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Facilities (19)**: Rate the facilities provided for the training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Parking (20)**: Rate the parking facilities provided.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Restroom (21)**: Rate the restrooms provided.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Meals (22)**: Rate the meals provided.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Safety (23)**: Rate the safety of the training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Wellness (24)**: Rate the wellness of the training session.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)
* **Others (25)**: Rate the others, including any additional comments or feedback.
	+ 1 (Poor) - 5 (Excellent)

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your feedback.
* Please select only one answer per field based on your experience.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them in the "Comments" field.
* Review your submission before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
