## Gym Facility Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about their experience at a gym facility. This form is intended to help management identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Rate your overall satisfaction with the facility.
2. Rate the helpfulness of the staff.
3. Rate the cleanliness of the facility.
4. Rate the availability of equipment.
5. Describe your experience with the facilities provided.
6. Rate the availability of parking.
7. Rate the helpfulness of the staff.
8. Rate the condition of the facilities.
9. Rate the condition of the equipment.
10. Rate the availability of amenities.
11. Rate the condition of the parking.
12. Rate the ambiance of the facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`1`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the facility, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Staff Helpfulness** (`2`, number, required): Rate how helpful the staff were during your visit, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Cleanliness** (`3`, number, required): Rate the cleanliness of the facility, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Equipment Availability** (`4`, number, required): Rate the availability of equipment, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Facilities** (`5`, text, required): Describe your experience with the facilities provided, such as the locker room, restrooms, etc.
* **Parking Availability** (`6`, number, required): Rate the availability of parking, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Staff Welcomeness** (`7`, number, required): Rate how welcoming the staff were during your visit, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Facilities Condition** (`8`, number, required): Rate the condition of the facilities, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Equipment Condition** (`9`, number, required): Rate the condition of the equipment, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Amenities Availability** (`10`, number, required): Rate the availability of amenities, such as towels, water, etc., on a scale of 1-5.
* **Parking Condition** (`11`, number, required): Rate the condition of the parking area, on a scale of 1-5.
* **Ambiance** (`12`, number, required): Rate the overall ambiance of the facility, on a scale of 1-5.
