# learning_possibilities_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning_possibilities_survey is designed to collect feedback and insights on learning possibilities. The form is composed of 12 pages with text fields to gather information on topics that users are interested in learning about.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, start by navigating through each of the 12 pages.
2. On each page, you will find a text field where you can provide your thoughts and ideas on the topic at hand.
3. Please be as detailed and specific as possible with your responses.
4. You can add or remove pages as needed.

* **page_1 - what_is_this** (Text field): This is the first page of the form. Please enter your thoughts on what you think this form is about.
