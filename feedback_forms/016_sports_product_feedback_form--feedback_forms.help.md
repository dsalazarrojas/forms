# Sports Product Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Product Feedback Form is designed to allow customers to provide feedback about our sports products, enabling us to make data-driven decisions for future product development and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Customer Information section with your name and any other identifying details you are comfortable sharing.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address and any other preferred contact method.
3. If the product is wearable or has a physical presence, provide its measurements in the Body Measures section.
4. In the Feedback section, type a clear and concise description of your experience with the product, including any suggestions for improvement or areas of concern.
5. Select the relevant Product Information option that best describes the product you are providing feedback on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false)
This section is for you to provide your name and any other identifying details you are comfortable sharing with us. This information will help us to personally address your feedback.

### 2. **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
Here, you can enter your email address or other contact details so we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or to provide updates on your feedback.

### 3. **Body Measures** (`body_measure`, text, required: false)
If the product is wearable or has a physical presence, please provide its measurements (e.g., length, width, weight, etc.) to help us better understand the product's performance.

### 4. **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
This section is where you can provide a clear and concise description of your experience with the product, including any suggestions for improvement or areas of concern.

### 5. **Product Information** (`product_info`, select_one, required: true)
Please select the option that best describes the product you are providing feedback on from the list provided. This will help us to track and categorize your feedback correctly.
