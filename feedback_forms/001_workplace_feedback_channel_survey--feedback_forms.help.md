# Workplace Feedback Channel Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Feedback Channel Survey is designed to gather feedback from employees on their experience with the channel, providing insights to help us make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Select the correct option for each dropdown field (Communication Channel, How often do you use this channel, How satisfied are you with this communication channel).
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please type them in the Comments field.
4. Enter your email address and phone number in their respective fields, if applicable.
5. Provide the date and time of your last use of the channel in the Date of last use and Time of last use fields, if applicable.
6. Enter any manager response times, if applicable.
7. You can add any additional comments or feedback in the Form Comments field.
8. Click on the Submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information**: This field is optional and open for any additional comments or feedback.
* **Communication Channel**: Select the communication channel you used for your last feedback or interaction (Channel A, Channel B, Channel C).
* **How often do you use this channel**: Indicate how often you use the channel (Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **How satisfied are you with this communication channel**: Choose how satisfied you are with the channel (Satisfied, Unsatisfied, Neutral).
* **Comments**: Add any comments or feedback you may have about the channel.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about your feedback.
* **Date of last use**: Provide the date of your last use of the channel.
* **Time of last use**: Provide the time of your last use of the channel.
* **Manager Response Time**: Enter the time it took for your manager to respond to your feedback.
* **Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the form itself or the feedback process.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send the form and submit your feedback.
