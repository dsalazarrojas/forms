# Skincare Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the "Skincare Training Feedback Form" efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
2. For each field, carefully review the explanations below for specific guidance.
3. Make sure to provide honest and constructive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the workshop you conducted. If you don't have a specific name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Trainer** (`trainer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the trainer of the workshop.
* **Feedback Question 1** (`feedback_question1`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one of the following options to rate the quality of the workshop:
	+ Very Good
	+ Good
	+ Neutral
	+ Bad
	+ Urgent
* **Feedback Question 2** (`feedback_question2`, text, required): Provide your feedback on the workshop in this text box. Be specific about what worked well and what could be improved.
* **Feedback Question 3** (`feedback_question3`, text, required): Share your suggestions on how to improve future workshops.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): If you have any other comments or suggestions, please add them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the workshop using the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Trainer Rating** (`trainer_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the trainer's performance from 1-5.
* **Attended Workshop** (`attended_workshop`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you attended the workshop:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Other** (`attended_workshop_other`, text, optional): If you answered "No" to the above question, please specify why you didn't attend.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your 10-digit phone number so we can verify your identity.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted this form using the 24-hour time format.
* **Workshop Hours** (`workshop_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours the workshop lasted.
* **Attendees Count** (`attendees_count`, number, required): Enter the number of attendees at the workshop.
* **Attendees Gender** (`attendees_gender`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply for the attendees' gender:
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Attendees Age Range** (`attendees_age_range`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply for the attendees' age range:
	+ 18-24
	+ 25-34
	+ 35-44
	+ 45-54
	+ 55-64
	+ 65+

Note: Make sure to double-check all fields before submitting the form.
