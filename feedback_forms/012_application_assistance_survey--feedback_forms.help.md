# Application Assistance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This guide is intended to aid you in completing the Application Assistance Survey form, ensuring that you provide valuable feedback to help us improve our services. Before proceeding, please note that:

<thinking>
This form is designed to gather feedback from users who have interacted with our application assistance service. It's essential to ensure that the information you provide is genuine and reflects your personal experience. Please do not fill out this form if you have already provided feedback for the same issue, as it may lead to duplicate submissions.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out each field clearly and accurately, taking into account the hints provided next to each field.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3.  If a field is optional, feel free to provide as much or as little information as you like.
4.  If you need help or have questions, please contact us using the provided contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User name
*   **User name** (text, optional): Please enter your name or alias to help us identify you in case we need to follow up on your feedback.

### 2. Service used
*   **Service used** (text, required): Select the service you received assistance with, which will help us understand your feedback.

### 3. Ease of use
*   **Ease of use** (select_one, required): Rate how easy or difficult it was to use the application. Choose from: Very easy, Easy, Neutral, Difficult, Very difficult.

### 4. Was the issue resolved
*   **Was the issue resolved** (select_one, required): Select the outcome of the assistance you received. Choose from: True, False, Partially.

### 5. Support rating
*   **Support rating** (select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the support interaction. Choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.

### 6. Response time satisfaction
*   **Response time satisfaction** (select_one, required): Rate your satisfaction with the response time. Choose from: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied.

### 7. Suggestions for improvement
*   **Suggestions for improvement** (text, optional): If you have any short suggestions on how we can improve the process, please enter them here.

### 8. Follow up contact
*   **Follow up contact** (email, optional): If we need to contact you for further assistance or follow up, please provide your email address.

### 9. Additional comments
*   **Additional comments** (text, optional): If you have any other comments, please provide them here.

### 10. Consent to contact
*   **Consent to contact** (select_one, required): Allow us to contact you regarding your feedback. Choose from: True, False.
