# Health Archive Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users on the Health Archive Effectiveness Survey. It is meant to help us understand how well our system meets the users' needs and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly, as they will help us understand your experience with the Health Archive system.
2. For the "How do you use health archive" question, please select all the ways that apply to you.
3. The "Overall Effectiveness" question is multiple-choice, where you can select all the options that apply to you.
4. In the "Suggestions" field, please provide any additional feedback or comments you may have.
5. If you have any comments or feedback, please type them in the "Comments" field.
6. Please provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field, as we may need to contact you for further information.
7. You can provide your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, but it is not required.
8. Finally, provide a brief message with any additional information you'd like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required): This is a brief explanation of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **How do you use health archive** (`how_do_you_use_health_archive`, select_multiple, required): This question asks how you currently use the Health Archive system. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Ease of use** (`ease_of_use`, select_one, required): This question asks about your experience with the ease of use of the Health Archive system. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Overall effectiveness** (`overall_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required): This question asks about the overall effectiveness of the Health Archive system. Select all the options that apply to you.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you may have about the Health Archive system.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is for any further comments or feedback you may have about the Health Archive system.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is for your contact phone number, but it is not required.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to share with us.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): This is the final step to submit your feedback. Please make sure to click this button to complete the form.
