# Editor Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Editor Recommendation Form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from users who have interacted with your work or content. This form is intended to gather information on how the editor (the person who edited your work) performed and what they did well or could improve on.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Open the form by clicking on the link provided.
2. Carefully read the form instructions and questions.
3. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4. Fill out each field as required (if any field is marked as required).
5. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Editor Feedback** (`editor_feedback_form`, Text, Optional): This field is for providing feedback on how the editor performed. Please be specific and provide constructive comments to help us improve our editing services.
* **Editor Suggestion** (`editor_suggestion`, Text, Optional): If you have any suggestions or ideas on how we can improve our editing services, please share them in this field.
* **Editor Comment** (`editor_comment`, Text, Optional): If you'd like to add any additional comments or context about your experience with our editing services, use this field.

## Tips

* Please be respectful and considerate when providing feedback and suggestions.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
* We appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our editing services.
