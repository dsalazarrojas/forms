# Earnings Call Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earnings Call Feedback Survey is a tool used by companies to gather feedback from investors after an earnings call. It helps to improve communication and engagement by collecting insights and opinions from investors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your title in the **Earnings Call Feedback Survey** field.
2. Select the category of the feedback form from the **Category** field. You can choose from **Feedback Forms** or **Other**.
3. Enter a brief description of the feedback in the **The Earnings Call Feedback Survey allows companies to gather insights...** field. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
4. If you have additional feedback, you can select more than one category from the **Feedback Forms** field.
5. Enter your comments in the **Your Comments** field.
6. If you have any supporting documents, you can upload them in the **Supporting Documents** field.
7. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Earnings Call Feedback Survey**:
	+ A text field where you can enter a title for your feedback.
	+ Required: yes
* **Category**:
	+ A dropdown field where you can select the category of the feedback form.
	+ Required: no
	+ Options: **Feedback Forms**, **Other**
* **The Earnings Call Feedback Survey allows companies to gather insights...**:
	+ A text field where you can enter a brief description of the feedback.
	+ Required: no
* **Feedback Forms**:
	+ A dropdown field where you can select additional feedback categories.
	+ Required: no
	+ Options: **Feedback Forms**, **Other**
* **Your Comments**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your comments.
	+ Required: yes
* **Your E-mail**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your email address.
	+ Required: no
* **Your Phone**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your phone number.
	+ Required: yes
* **Supporting Documents**:
	+ A text field where you can upload supporting documents.
	+ Required: no
