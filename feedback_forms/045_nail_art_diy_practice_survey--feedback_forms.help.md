# Nail Art DIY Practice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your nail art DIY practice habits, skills, and preferences. Your input will help us better understand your needs and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your information and answers.
2. Select the options that best fit your description for each field.
3. Add any additional comments or notes if you have any further information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **User Information** (1) (`user_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name, email address, and phone number for us to contact you for follow-up questions or to share your results.

### Nail Art Skills
* **Nail Art Skills** (2) (select multiple, optional): Indicate your level of expertise in nail art, from Beginner to Advanced.

### Feedback Level
* **Feedback Level** (3) (select one, optional): Rate your comfort level with giving and receiving feedback, from 1 (very easy) to 5 (very difficult).

### Difficulty
* **Difficulty** (4) (select one, optional): Choose the level of difficulty you typically experience when practicing nail art.

### Practice Frequency
* **Practice Frequency** (5) (select one, optional): Indicate how often you practice nail art, from Daily to Rarely.

### Nail Art Interest
* **Nail Art Interest** (6) (select one, optional): Rate your level of interest in nail art, from High to Low.

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (10) (note, optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have about this form or our services.

### Submit
* **Submit** (7) (text, optional): Please review your answers and submit your response to complete the form.

Note: This form is completely anonymous, and your responses will not be linked to any personal data or contact information. If you have any concerns, please contact us at [your email address].
