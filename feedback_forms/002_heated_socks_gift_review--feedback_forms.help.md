# heated_socks_gift_review - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on a gift given to someone. Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve our gifting process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with the requested information.
2. Be honest and thorough in your responses.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, `text`, required): The name of the product you gave as a gift.
* **Gift Giver Name** (`gift_giver_name`, `text`, required): Your name, the person who gave the gift.
* **Gift Giver Email** (`gift_giver_email`, `email`, required): Your email address, so we can contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Recipient Email** (`gift_reipient_email`, `email`, not required): The recipient's email address, which will help us keep you updated on the gift's status.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, `text`, required): A brief description of the product you gave as a gift.
* **Gift Suggestion** (`gift_suggestion`, `text`, required): A brief suggestion or message accompanying the gift.
* **Gift Suggestion Details** (`gift_suggestion_details`, `text`, not required): More details about the gift, if you'd like to share.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the gift-giving experience, on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the reasons why you would recommend the gift-giving experience, on a scale of 1, 2, or 3.
* **Product Rating** (`product_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the product you gave as a gift, out of 5.
* **Review** (`review`, `text`, not required): Share any additional thoughts or comments about the gift-giving experience.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, `text`, not required): A URL to a photo of the gift or recipient, if you'd like to share.
* **Attachment** (`attachment`, `text`, not required): Any attachment or file associated with the gift, if you'd like to share.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): Any additional comments or feedback about the gift-giving experience.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click the submit button to finalize your feedback and send it to us.
* **Date Given** (`date_given`, `date`, required): The date you gave the gift to the recipient.
* **Time Given** (`time_given`, `time`, required): The time you gave the gift to the recipient.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, `date`, required): The date the recipient received the gift.
* **Time Received** (`time_received`, `time`, required): The time the recipient received the gift.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `text`, not required): The time zone you're in when giving the gift.
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, `number`, not required): The timezone offset, if you know it.
* **Geo IP** (`geo_ip`, `text`, not required): The recipient's Geo IP, if you know it.
* **Device Fingerprint** (`device_fingerprint`, `text`, not required): The recipient's device fingerprint, if you know it.
* **User Agent** (`user_agent`, `text`, not required): The recipient's user agent, if you know it.
* **Referrer** (`referrer`, `text`, not required): The referrer, if you know it.
