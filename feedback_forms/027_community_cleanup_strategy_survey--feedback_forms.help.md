# Cleanup Strategy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information about the state of cleanup efforts in your neighborhood. Please complete this form to provide us with feedback on the top issues and concerns in your area.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your neighborhood or street in the "Neighborhood area" field.
2. In the "Priority cleanup issues" field, select all the issues that are currently affecting your neighborhood. You can select multiple issues.
3. If you are available to volunteer, specify the times or days when you can volunteer in the "Volunteer availability" field. Please note that this is an optional field.
4. Choose the scale of projects that you prefer for cleanup efforts in your area in the "Preferred cleanup scale" field.
5. If your organization is supporting you, describe the type of resources (training, equipment, or funding) that you need in the "Support needed from organization" field. Please note that this is an optional field.
6. In the "Additional comments or suggestions" field, provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have for improving cleanup efforts in your area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Neighborhood area**: Enter the name of your neighborhood or street to help us narrow down the focus of our efforts.
* **Priority cleanup issues**: Select all the issues that are currently affecting your neighborhood. These issues are:
	+ Litter and trash
	+ Illegal dumping
	+ Overgrown vegetation
	+ Recycling access
	+ Hazardous waste
* **Volunteer availability**: If you are available to volunteer, specify the times or days when you can volunteer. Please note that this is an optional field.
* **Preferred cleanup scale**: Choose the scale of projects that you prefer for cleanup efforts in your area. Options are:
	+ Block level
	+ Neighborhood level
	+ City wide
* **Support needed from organization**: If your organization is supporting you, describe the type of resources (training, equipment, or funding) that you need.
* **Additional comments or suggestions**: Provide any additional feedback or suggestions you may have for improving cleanup efforts in your area. This is an optional field.
