# track_year_2013 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from individuals who participated in or were exposed to educational programs or events in 2013.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Post Secondary" page and enter your information in the fields provided.
2. Indicate whether you attained post-secondary education in 2013.
3. If you did not attain post-secondary education, provide the name of the educational opportunity center you visited.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Post Secondary**: Enter your details about post-secondary education you attained or pursued in 2013.
* **Not Attained Post Secondary**: Check this box if you did not attain post-secondary education in 2013.
* **Educational Opportunity Center**: If you did not attain post-secondary education, enter the name of the educational opportunity center you visited.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
