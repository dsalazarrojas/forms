## customer_experience_feedback_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate and improve our customer experience. It is intended for customers to provide feedback on our services, including ratings and comments on various aspects of their interaction with our company. Please note that we value your feedback and will not ask you to complete this form if you've already provided feedback in the past 30 days.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose your rating for each of the questions below. You can select only one option for each question.
2. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can write them in the **Availability** field.
3. Fill out all required fields to ensure we can collect the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Feedback Rating
* **Feedback Rating** (1): Rate your overall satisfaction with our service. This is a required field.

### Service Quality
* **Service Quality** (2): Rate our service quality on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. This rating will help us understand how well we met your expectations. (Select one option)

### Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills** (3): Rate our communication skills, whether you found them friendly or not. This will help us understand how effectively we interacted with you. (Select one option)

### Response Time
* **Response Time** (4): If available, rate how quickly we responded to your inquiries. This will help us identify areas for improvement in our response times. (Choose a date, if applicable)

### Email Response
* **Email Response** (5): If you've corresponded with us via email, rate our response time. This will help us refine our email communication. (Choose a date)

### Phone Response
* **Phone Response** (6): If you've interacted with us over the phone, rate our response time. This will help us identify opportunities to improve our phone communication. (Choose a date)

### Availability
* **Availability** (7): If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can provide them here. This field is optional and not required, but it will help us understand your experience in more depth.

### Customer Service
* **Customer Service** (8): Rate our customer service on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. This will help us evaluate how well we've supported you. (Select one option)

### Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (9): Rate your overall experience with our company on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. This will help us identify areas for improvement and provide better service in the future. (Select one option)

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, as it will help us understand your experience better.
* If you have any issues or concerns that we haven't addressed in this form, please include them in the **Availability** field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
