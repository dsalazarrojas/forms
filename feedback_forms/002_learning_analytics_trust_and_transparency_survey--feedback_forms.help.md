# learning_analytics_trust_and_transparency_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey that aims to gather your feedback and opinions on our learning analytics services. It will help us understand your trust and perceptions towards our services and identify areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. You can skip any question you don't have an opinion on.
3. You can only complete this form once. If you have already filled it out, your responses will not be recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your role?** (`question1`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking about your job title or your position within our organization or company.
* **What's your experience?** (`question2`, `text`, required: false): This question is asking about your experience with our learning analytics services, such as how long you've been using them, or if you've used them before.
* **How would you rate our learning analytics capabilities?** (`question3`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking you to rate our capabilities on a scale (likely 1-5) and explain why you chose that rating. The rating is meant to help us understand your satisfaction with our services.
* **What do you want to improve?** (`question4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This question is asking you to select one or more options on how you think we can improve our services. This could be anything from better user experience to more features or functionality.
* **Is transparency important?** (`question5`, `select_one`, required: false): This question is asking you to select which option best describes your level of importance for transparency with our services.
* **What's your comment?** (`question6`, `text`, required: false): This question is an open-ended text field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about our services. 
* **How would you rate our team?** (`question7`, `number`, required: false): This question is asking you to rate our team on a scale (likely 1-5) and explain why you chose that rating. This will help us understand how satisfied you are with our team's performance.
