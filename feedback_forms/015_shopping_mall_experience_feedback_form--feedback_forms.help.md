# Shopping Mall Experience Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shopping Mall Experience Feedback Form is designed to collect users' feedback and ratings about their experiences at our shopping mall. This form helps us understand your satisfaction level and gather suggestions for improvement. Please take a few minutes to complete it honestly, and we will use your input to make our mall a better place.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Feedback" button to open the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), if applicable. If you don't have an account with us, you can still submit feedback, but your name will be displayed as "Guest" in the form submission.
3. Enter your thoughts and ratings in the provided fields.
4. Click on "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name so we can address you personally. If you don't want to share your name, you can leave this field blank.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience at the mall from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, suggestions, and experiences with us. This is your chance to tell us what you liked or disliked about the mall.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
* Keep your comments clear and concise.
* If you have any attachments or images that support your feedback, feel free to include them, but please note that we may not be able to respond to them directly.
* If you need assistance or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
