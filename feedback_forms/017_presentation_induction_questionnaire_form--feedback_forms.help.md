# Presentation Induction Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Presentation Induction Questionnaire Form is designed to collect feedback and opinions from users after a presentation or induction. This helps us understand your experience and improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out each field with the following information:
  - First Name
  - Last Name
  - Email (valid email address)
  - Phone (optional)
  - Feedback (your thoughts and comments)
  - Rating (rate your experience from 1 to 5)
  - Rating Scale (select the correct rating from the provided scale)
  - Date Given (the date of the presentation or induction)
  - Time Given (the time of the presentation or induction)
  - Note (any additional comments or notes)
  - Email Confirmation (confirm that the provided email address is correct)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to ensure you receive any follow-up communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up conversations.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and comments about the presentation or induction.
- **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience from 1 to 5.
- **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, select_one, optional): Select the correct rating from the provided scale.
  - 5: Excellent
  - 4: Good
  - 3: Fair
  - 2: Poor
  - 1: Very Poor
- **Date Given** (`date_given`, date, optional): Enter the date of the presentation or induction.
- **Time Given** (`time_given`, time, optional): Enter the time of the presentation or induction.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
- **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm that the provided email address is correct.
  - Confirmed
  - Not Confirmed

## Tips
- Please fill out each field accurately and truthfully.
- Use the provided scale for the rating and rating scale.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
