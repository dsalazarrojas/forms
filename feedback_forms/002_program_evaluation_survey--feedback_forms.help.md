# Program Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and evaluation information about a program or training session. This will help us identify areas of improvement and refine our programs to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully to ensure you have answered all relevant questions.
2. Be as thorough and honest in your responses as possible.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, please provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Demographics**: Please indicate whether you have participated in this program before. (`Yes`, `No`)
* **Program Experience**: Please select the option that best describes your experience in this program. (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4)
* **Participant Comments**: If you have any general comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Rating**: Please rate your overall experience in this program. (Note: Rating is a number input field, please enter a numerical value.)
* **Participant Feedback**: If you have any specific feedback about this program, please provide it here.
* **Participant Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improving this program, please provide them here.
* **Program Director Feedback**: If you have any feedback or comments for the program director, please provide them here.
* **Evaluation Date**: Please enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time**: Please enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Duration**: Please enter the duration of the evaluation.
* **Contact Name**: Please enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Please enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Please enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Department**: Please enter the department of the contact person.
* **Contact Position**: Please enter the position of the contact person.
* **Organization**: Please enter the organization of the contact person.
* **Evaluation Date/Time**: Please enter the date and time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Duration (Hours)**: Please enter the duration of the evaluation in hours.
* **Evaluation Duration (Minutes)**: Please enter the duration of the evaluation in minutes.
* **Evaluation Comments**: If you have any general comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Contact Email2**: Please enter the email of the contact person 2.
* **Contact Phone2**: Please enter the phone number of the contact person 2.
* **Contact Department2**: Please enter the department of the contact person 2.
* **Contact Position2**: Please enter the position of the contact person 2.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
