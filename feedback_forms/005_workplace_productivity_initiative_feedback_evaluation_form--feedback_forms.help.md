# Workplace Productivity Initiative Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Workplace Productivity Initiative Feedback Evaluation Form is designed to help us understand how well the Workplace Productivity Initiative has met your needs, expectations, and goals. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make informed decisions about future initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your level of satisfaction with the Workplace Productivity Initiative from the "How satisfied are you with the Workplace Productivity Initiative?" field.
3. Share any suggestions you have for improving the initiative in the "What suggestions do you have for improving the Workplace Productivity Initiative?" field.
4. Choose your Department and Job Title from the respective fields.
5. Enter your Years of Experience in the "Years of Experience" field.
6. Select your level of job satisfaction from the "How satisfied are you with your job?" field.
7. Indicate how often you use the Workplace Productivity Initiative from the "How often do you use the Workplace Productivity Initiative?" field.
8. Choose how you think the Workplace Productivity Initiative should be implemented from the "How do you think the Workplace Productivity Initiative should be implemented?" field.
9. Select how effective you think the Workplace Productivity Initiative is from the "How effective do you think the Workplace Productivity Initiative is?" field.
10. Choose how the Workplace Productivity Initiative has impacted your work experience from the "How has the Workplace Productivity Initiative impacted your work experience?" field.
11. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **How satisfied are you with the Workplace Productivity Initiative?** (`work_initiative_satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Select your level of satisfaction with the initiative, which can be Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What suggestions do you have for improving the Workplace Productivity Initiative?** (`feedback`, text, required): Share any suggestions you have for improving the initiative.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Choose your department, which can be Accounting, Engineering, Management, or Sales.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, select_one, required): Choose your job title, which can be Manager, Employee, Director, or Other.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of experience.
* **How satisfied are you with your job?** (`job_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your level of job satisfaction, which can be Extremely Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied.
* **How often do you use the Workplace Productivity Initiative?** (`feedback_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Indicate how often you use the Workplace Productivity Initiative, which can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Rarely.
* **How do you think the Workplace Productivity Initiative should be implemented?** (`initiative_implementation`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you think the initiative should be implemented, which can be Centralized, Decentralized, or Hybrid.
* **How effective do you think the Workplace Productivity Initiative is?** (`initiative_effectiveness`, select_multiple, required): Select how effective you think the initiative is, which can be Very Effective, Effective, Somewhat Effective, Not Effective, or Not at all Effective.
* **How has the Workplace Productivity Initiative impacted your work experience?** (`initiative_impact`, select_one, required): Choose how you think the initiative has impacted your work experience, which can be Positive, Negative, Neutral, Not at all.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
