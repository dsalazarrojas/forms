# Town Festival Contact Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to allow you to get in touch with the town festival organizers and provide them with relevant information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact name and email address or phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
2. Choose the date of the town festival event.
3. Select the event time for the event.
4. Choose one event type from the select_one field.
5. (Optional) Choose multiple event types from the select_multiple field.
6. Provide a message with any additional information about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): Fill in your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Message** (`message`, text, Optional): Provide any additional information about the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, Optional): Choose the date of the town festival event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, Optional): Choose the time of the town festival event.
* **Event Type** (`select_one`, select_one, Optional): Choose one event type from the list.
* **Interested Events** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose multiple event types from the list.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in the message field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the town festival organizers at [townfestival@email.com](mailto:townfestival@email.com).
