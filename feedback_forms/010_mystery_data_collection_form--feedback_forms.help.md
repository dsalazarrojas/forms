# Mystery Data Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and comments from users to help us improve our platform. Please provide as much detail as possible to help us understand your concerns or suggestions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and provide feedback on any aspect of the platform that you feel is important.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and relevant information.
3. Use the text areas to provide any additional comments or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, required: false): This field is not required but helps us understand any additional context about your experience. You can provide any information you feel is relevant to your feedback.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, required: true): Select how you would like to be contacted for follow-up questions or to confirm receipt of your feedback. Please choose either "email" or "phone".
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, required: false): This is the main text area where you can provide any feedback or comments you have about your experience. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to provide a numerical rating (1-5) of your overall experience.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required: false): This field is not required but helps us understand when you provided your feedback.
* **Feedback Time** (`feedback_time`, time, required: false): This field is not required but helps us understand when you provided your feedback.
* **Feedback Note** (`feedback_note`, note, required: false): This is an optional field for any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Email** (`email_address`, email, required: false): This field is not required but if you provided your email address, we may contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): This field is not required but if you provided your phone number, we may contact you for follow-up questions.
