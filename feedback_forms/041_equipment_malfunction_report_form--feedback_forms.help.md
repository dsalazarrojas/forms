**Equipment Malfunction Report Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is designed to report malfunctions or issues with equipment used in our facility. If your equipment is not functioning properly, please fill out this form to notify our maintenance team of the issue. This will help us to troubleshoot and repair the equipment as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required information in each field. The form is divided into several sections for your convenience. Please make sure to provide detailed information in each field, as this will help our maintenance team to better understand the issue and address it quickly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, text, Required: false): This field is for any additional comments or information you may have about the malfunction.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment type`, select_one, Required: false): Select the type of equipment that is experiencing the malfunction from the provided options (type1, type2, type3).
* **Equipment Description** (`Equipment Description`, text, Required: false): Provide a detailed description of the malfunctioning equipment, including any symptoms or error messages you have encountered.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, Required: false): Report the date when you first noticed the malfunction.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, Required: false): Report the time when you first noticed the malfunction.

## Tips
- Please fill out as much information as possible, but only if you are unsure about a field, just leave it blank.
- If you are reporting a recurring issue, please provide a detailed description of when it started and how often it occurs.
- If you have any supporting documentation or photos, please attach them to the form or email them to our maintenance team for further assistance.
