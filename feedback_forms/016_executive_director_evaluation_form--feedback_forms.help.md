# Executive Director Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Director Evaluation Form is designed to assess the performance and effectiveness of the Executive Director in their role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have a thorough understanding of each field and its intended purpose.
2. Select the most accurate response for each field based on your experience and interaction with the Executive Director.
3. Provide detailed and thoughtful comments for the required fields.
4. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`page_1`, `note`, required: false): This field is a placeholder for any initial comments or questions about the Executive Director's performance.
* **Employee Evaluation** (`page_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the areas where the Executive Director has demonstrated strengths or weaknesses as an employee. For example, 'Yes' for areas of strength and 'No' for areas of improvement.
* **Leadership Skills** (`page_3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the level of the Executive Director's leadership skills, where:
	+ 'Strong' indicates exceptional leadership abilities.
	+ 'Excellent' indicates excellent leadership skills.
	+ 'Good' indicates good leadership skills.
	+ 'Fair' indicates fair leadership skills.
	+ 'Poor' indicates poor leadership skills.
* **Communication Skills** (`page_4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the areas where the Executive Director has demonstrated strengths or weaknesses in communication.
* **Overall Impact** (`page_5`, `number`, required: false): Rate the Executive Director's overall impact on the organization, on a scale of 0-10.
* **Recommendations for Improvement** (`page_6`, `text`, required: false): Provide specific and actionable suggestions for areas where the Executive Director can improve.
* **Final Comments** (`page_7`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about the Executive Director's performance.
* **Executive Director Evaluation** (`page_8`, `number`, required: false): This field is intended for the evaluator's overall score of the Executive Director's performance.
* **Executive Director Evaluation Score** (`page_9`, `number`, required: false): This field is intended for the overall score of the Executive Director's performance.
* **Executive Director Evaluation Comments** (`page_10`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about the Executive Director's performance.
* **Executive Director Evaluation Score 2** (`page_11`, `number`, required: false): This field is a duplicate of the Executive Director Evaluation Score.
