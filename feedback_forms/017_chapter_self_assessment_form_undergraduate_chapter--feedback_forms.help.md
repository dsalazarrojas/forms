# Chapter Self Assessment Form Undergraduate Chapter - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from undergraduate chapters on their practices and member support. Your input will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of each chapter and provide suggestions for future growth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all required fields (marked as `required: true`).
2. For each field, provide a clear and honest answer that reflects your chapter's current situation.
3. Take note of the available tips if you're unsure or need help with any question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **chapter name** (`chapter_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your undergraduate chapter.
* **Assessment Year** (`assessment_year`, number, required): Select the year for which you are completing this self-assessment.
* **Event Participation Rate** (`event_participation`, number, optional): Estimate the percentage of member participation in events.
* **Mentorship Programs Present** (`mentorship_programs`, select_one, required): Check if your chapter offers mentorship programs.
* **Recruitment Effectiveness** (`recruitment_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of your chapter's recruitment strategies.
* **Financial Stability** (`financial_stability`, select_one, required): Indicate your chapter's financial status.
* **Key Strengths** (`key_strengths`, text, optional): List the major strengths of your chapter.
* **Action Items** (`action_items`, text, optional): Provide planned next steps for improvement.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing this form.

## Tips
* For "Mentorship Programs Present", choose "True" if your chapter offers mentorship programs, and "False" otherwise.
* For "Recruitment Effectiveness" and "Financial Stability", choose the option that best represents your chapter's current situation.
* You can be as specific as possible when answering "Key Strengths" and "Action Items" to help us better understand your chapter's performance.
* Make sure to complete all required fields, as they are marked with an asterisk (\*).
