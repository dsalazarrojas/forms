# sports_club_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from sports club members about their food and beverage preferences, as well as their ideas for improvement. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your preferred food options from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or more options.
2. Indicate how often you visit the sports club, by selecting one of the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred beverage option from the dropdown menu.
4. If you have any ideas for improving the sports club, type them in the text field. 
5. If you would like to provide your email address, please do so in the format [your email address].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Selection** (food_selection, select_multiple, required: no):
	+ Select your preferred food options from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or more options.
* **How Often** (how_often, select_one, required: no):
	+ Indicate how often you visit the sports club, by selecting one of the options provided.
* **Beverage Selection** (beverage_selection, select_one, required: no):
	+ Choose your preferred beverage option from the dropdown menu.
* **Ideas for Improvement** (ideas, text, required: no):
	+ If you have any ideas for improving the sports club, type them in this text field.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, required: no):
	+ If you would like to provide your email address, please do so in the format [your email address].
