# youtube_channel_feedback_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their experience with a YouTube channel. It is intended to help channel owners or administrators understand user satisfaction and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the YouTube channel (e.g., Gaming, Vlog, Educational, Music, or Other).
2. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the channel.
3. Choose how you feel about your experience with the channel (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, or Strongly Disagree).
4. Optionally, enter your email address and phone number if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or responses.
5. Choose a date and time if the feedback is related to a specific event or interaction on the channel.
6. Finally, select how strongly you agree with the statement "I am happy with my experience with this channel" and click "Submit" to send the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple): Choose a category that best describes the YouTube channel.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the channel.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or responses.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Date** (`date`, date): Choose the date that the feedback is related to, if relevant.
* **Time** (`time`, time): Choose the time that the feedback is related to, if relevant.
* **YouTube Channel** (`youtube_channel`, select_one): Choose a category that best describes the YouTube channel (e.g., Gaming, Vlog, Educational, Music, or Other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the channel.
* **Submit** (`feedback`, select_one): Choose how you feel about your experience with the channel (Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neutral, Somewhat Disagree, or Strongly Disagree).
