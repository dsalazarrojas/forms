# Team Communication Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of team communication within your team. It will help us understand how well you perceive the team's communication and collaboration processes, which will guide us in identifying areas for improvement and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: **team_name**, **team_size**, **team_lead_name**, **team_lead_title**, **team_lead_email**, **team_lead_phone**, and **team_lead_position**.
2. Answer the questions about your perception of the team's strengths, weaknesses, satisfaction, trust, collaboration, conflict, support, recognition, feedback, and problem-solving.
3. Provide your name and email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, required): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your team lead or supervisor.
* **Team Lead Title** (`team_lead_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title or position of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your team lead.
* **Team Lead Position** (`team_lead_position`, `text`, required): Enter the position or role of your team lead.
* **What do you think is the team's greatest strength?** (`question1`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What do you think is the team's greatest weakness?** (`question2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all that apply from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with team communication?** (`question3`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the level of trust within the team?** (`question4`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the level of collaboration within the team?** (`question5`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the level of conflict within the team?** (`question6`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the level of support within the team?** (`question7`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the level of recognition within the team?** (`question8`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the level of feedback within the team?** (`question9`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you rate the level of problem-solving within the team?** (`question10`, `select_one`, required): Choose your response from the options Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click this button to submit your survey.
* **Survey Completion** (`survey_footer`, `text`, optional): This is a confirmation message after submitting the survey.
* **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, `text`, optional): This is the final button to submit your survey response.
