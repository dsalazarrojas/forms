# Animal Behavior Study Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from participants in an animal behavior study. Researchers use this information to improve the study's procedures and ensure that the data collected is accurate and reliable.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, start by selecting the correct page (Section) from the provided options.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions and labels provided on each field.
3. Be sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, required): Enter the unique study participant identifier if assigned. This is used to track your participation in the study.
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, required): Select the date when the observation took place. This is an important detail for tracking the timing of your observations.
* **Species Observed** (`species_observed`, required): Provide the common name of the species you observed. This helps the researchers identify the species you were studying.
* **Observation Duration Hours** (`observation_duration_hours`, required): Enter the duration of the observation in hours, including decimals if needed. This helps researchers track the amount of time spent observing the species.
* **Behaviors Observed** (`behaviors_observed`, required/optional): Select all behaviors you noted during the observation. This helps researchers understand what behaviors were exhibited by the species. Choose as many as apply from the provided options:
	+ Feeding
	+ Social interaction
	+ Grooming
	+ Resting
	+ Aggression
	+ Exploratory behavior
* **Estimated Frequency of Target Behavior per Hour** (`behavior_frequency_estimate`, optional): Enter a whole number estimate of how often the target behavior occurred per hour. Leave this field blank if you're unsure.
* **Rating of Study Procedures** (`protocol_rating`, required): Rate how clear and comfortable the study procedures were for you. This helps researchers understand your experience with the study.
* Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Equipment or Protocol Issues** (`equipment_issues`, optional): Describe any issues you experienced with the equipment or protocol used during the study. This helps researchers identify areas for improvement.
* **Additional Comments or Recommendations** (`additional_comments`, optional): Use this space to provide any additional comments or recommendations for the study.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, optional): If you're willing, provide an email where the research team can contact you for follow-up questions.
* **Consent for Follow-up** (`consent_for_follow_up`, required): Indicate whether you're willing for the research team to contact you for clarification or additional information.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, required): Enter your full name, the person completing this survey.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be filled out for the form to be complete.
