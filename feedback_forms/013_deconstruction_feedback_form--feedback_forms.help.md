# Deconstruction Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deconstruction Feedback Form is used to gather feedback on the project deconstruction process. This feedback helps our deconstruction team identify areas for improvement and provide insights for refinement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide feedback on each field as it applies to your experience with the project deconstruction.
3. If a field does not apply, simply leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the project you are providing feedback on.
* **Crew ID** (`crew_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID number assigned to the project crew.
* **Site Address** (`site_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the site where the deconstruction took place.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for the project.
* **Process Quality** (`process_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the quality of the deconstruction process as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, `text`, required: false): If you experienced any safety concerns during the project, describe them here.
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, `text`, required: false): If you observed any sustainability practices during the project, describe them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Share any additional comments or thoughts on the deconstruction process.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are submitting this form (optional but recommended).
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you are submitting this form (optional but recommended).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for follow-up contact.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or context for your feedback.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Enter the tool or equipment used during the deconstruction process (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* If you are unsure about a field, simply leave it blank.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help improve our deconstruction processes.
