# HRIS Mobile And Web Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the HRIS mobile and web interfaces, allowing you to provide suggestions or report any issues you've experienced while using the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide feedback, simply fill out the form with the following information:

1. Enter your employee name.
2. Select your department (if applicable).
3. Write a comment about your experience with the HRIS system.
4. (Optional) Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
5. (Optional) Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
6. Select a date and time for the feedback (if relevant).
7. Select one option from the available list.
8. Select multiple options from the available list.
9. Add any additional notes about your experience.
10. Click the "Submit" button when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID card or badge.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, optional): If you're providing feedback about a specific department, select it from the list.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, optional): Describe your experience with the HRIS mobile and web interfaces. Be as detailed as possible so we can better understand your concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date if your feedback is related to a specific event or time.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time if your feedback is related to a specific time or event.
* **Select One** (`select_one_field`, select_one, optional): Choose one option that best describes your feedback.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple_field`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options that best describe your feedback.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your experience.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to send your feedback to the development team.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): Click this button to clear all entered fields and start again if needed.
