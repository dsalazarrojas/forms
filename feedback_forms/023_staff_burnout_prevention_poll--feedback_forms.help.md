# Staff Burnout Prevention Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help staff members identify their feelings about their current job, their experiences with burnout, and their thoughts on strategies for preventing burnout. It will help gather valuable insights into staff's well-being and help management take steps to address burnout within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Scroll through each page and read the questions carefully.
* Answer each question according to your personal feelings and experiences.
* For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
* For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you feel comfortable sharing.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are your feelings about your current job?** (`staff_burnout_prevention_poll`, text, optional): This is an open-ended question where you can express your feelings about your current job. Be honest and share your thoughts.
* **Burnout risks identified?** (`burnout_risks_identified`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the burnout risks you are experiencing, such as Burnout, Stress, Anxiety, Depression, or Trauma.
* **What prevention strategies have you implemented?** (`burnout_prevention_strategies`, select_one, optional): Choose a prevention strategy you have already used or considered, like Regular breaks and self-care or Mental health resources.
* **Have you received support for burnout?** (`support_received`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have received support for burnout or not.
* **How often do you experience burnout?** (`burnout_impact`, number, optional): Provide a number to indicate how often you experience burnout, on a scale of 1-10.
* **What channels do you use for support?** (`support_channels`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the channels you use for support, such as Manager, HR, Coworkers, or Anonymous reporting.
* **What support options have you used or considered?** (`burnout_prevention_support`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the support options you have used or considered, like Employee Assistance Program (EAP) or Employee wellness programs.
* **What burnout prevention recommendations do you have?** (`burnout_prevention_recommendations`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the recommendations you have for preventing burnout, such as More resources for employee well-being or More support for mental health.
* **What burnout prevention actions have you taken?** (`burnout_prevention_actions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the actions you have taken to prevent burnout, like Taking breaks or Staying hydrated and energized.
* **What steps will you take next to address burnout?** (`burnout_prevention_next_steps`, text, optional): Provide any additional steps you plan to take to address burnout.
* **Comments** (`burnout_prevention_comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or suggestions you have about the form or the topic.
