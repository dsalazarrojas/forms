# Spa Service Discovery Channel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended to help spa service providers gain insights into their marketing and promotional activities. The information collected will be used to identify gaps and opportunities for improvement in current marketing strategies and provide valuable insights for future marketing initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of services do you offer?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey`): Choose the type of services you provide, such as massage, haircut, nail, or facial.
* **How do you currently market your services?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_2`): Select all methods you use, such as social media, online advertising, word of mouth, or local advertising.
* **What is your average client base like?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_3`): Choose the demographics of your clients, such as age, gender, location, or client base size.
* **Which platforms do you currently use to promote your business?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_4`): Select all platforms you use, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or email marketing.
* **What percentage of your business do you attribute to social media marketing?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_5`): Enter a number between 0 and 100% to indicate the percentage of your business attributed to social media marketing.
* **How do your clients typically find out about your services?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_6`): Choose the methods your clients use to find out about your services, such as online advertising, word of mouth, or referrals.
* **How much time do you spend on social media per week?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_7`): Select the amount of time you spend on social media, such as less than 5 hours, 5-10 hours, or more than 15 hours.
* **How much time do you spend in person at events and marketing activities?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_8`): Select the amount of time you spend at events and marketing activities, such as less than 5 hours, 5-10 hours, or more than 15 hours.
* **Do you have any upcoming events or marketing activities?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_9`): Select yes or no if you have events or marketing activities planned.
* **What is the typical time of year for your events?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_10`): Choose the time of year for your events, such as winter, spring, summer, or autumn.
* **What is the typical occasion for your events?** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_11`): Select the types of occasions your events are for, such as spa day, new year, summer, or spring break.
* **Additional information** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_12`): Enter any additional information about your events or marketing activities.
* **Services offered** (`spa_service_discovery_channel_survey_13`): Select the types of services you offer, such as spa services, products, or services offered at a spa.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to select the correct options for each question.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
