# Health Care Practical Work Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Health Care Practical Work Feedback" form is designed to collect feedback from users about their clinical site experience and overall feedback. This form will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Student Placement Details" field with information about your recent placement.
2. Rate your experience in both "How Likely to Recommend" and "Overall Experience" fields using the provided scale.
3. Provide feedback about your clinical site in the "Clinical Site Details" field.
4. If you have any additional feedback, type it in the "Feedback" field.
5. Optionally, provide your email address in the "Email" field if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions.
6. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
7. If relevant, enter the date and time you completed your clinical site assignment in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
8. Optionally, enter the date you submitted this form in the "Submitted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Placement Details** (text, required): Describe your recent student placement, including any notable experiences, challenges, or observations.
* **How Likely to Recommend** (select_one, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend the clinical site to others.
* **Overall Experience** (select_one, required): Rate your overall experience with the clinical site.
* **Clinical Site Details** (text, required): Provide specific feedback about your clinical site experience, including any notable strengths or areas for improvement.
* **Feedback** (text, required): Share any additional thoughts, concerns, or suggestions about your clinical site experience.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **Date Completed** (date, optional): Enter the date you completed your clinical site assignment.
* **Time Completed** (time, optional): Enter the time you completed your clinical site assignment.
* **Submitted** (date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
