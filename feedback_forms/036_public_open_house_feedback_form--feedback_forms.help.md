# public_open_house_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering feedback from public attendees of open houses organized by our real estate agency. Your input will help us improve the quality of our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

1. Fill out each field as prompted.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Open House Details** (Text, required): Describe the open house you attended, including location, date, and time.
* **Date And Time** (Date, required): Enter the date and time of the open house you attended.
* **Feedback** (Text, optional): Share your overall impression of the open house.
* **Feedback Details** (Text, optional): Provide specific feedback about the open house, such as what you liked or disliked.
* **Contact Info** (Email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with a response.
* **Real Estate Agent** (Select, optional): Answer if you met a real estate agent during the open house and if you found them helpful.
* **Feedback Rating** (Select, optional): Choose a rating for the open house, such as "Good", "Fair", etc.
* **Comments** (Text, optional): Enter any additional comments you have about the open house.
* **Submit** (Text, required): Click this button to send your feedback.
