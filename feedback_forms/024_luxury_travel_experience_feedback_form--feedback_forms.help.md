# luxury_travel_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their luxury travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

- Choose the type of trip you experienced from the "Trip Type" dropdown menu.
- Answer the overall satisfaction question by selecting one or multiple options from the "Overall Satisfaction" dropdown menu.
- Rate the staff professionalism, services quality, and amenities quality by selecting one of the options from the "Very Good" to "Poor" scale.
- Provide additional comments about staff professionalism, services quality, and amenities quality in the corresponding additional comments fields.
- Rate the communication, facilities, and cleanliness by selecting one of the options from the "Very Good" to "Poor" scale.
- Rate the staff helpfulness, overall value, and trip value by selecting one of the options from the "Very Good" to "Poor" scale.
- Rate the trip value for money by selecting one of the options from the "Very Good" to "Poor" scale.
- Answer the "Would Recommend" question by selecting "True" or "False".
- Rate the travel experience by selecting one of the options from the "Very Good" to "Poor" scale.
- Provide additional comments about the travel experience in the corresponding additional comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the trip type**: Select the type of trip you experienced (Luxury, Adventure, Wellness, Relaxation, Cultural, Food and Wine, Beach, City).
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the trip (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Staff Professionalism**: Rate the staff's friendliness and helpfulness (1-5 scale).
* **Services Quality**: Rate the quality of the hotel's services (1-5 scale).
* **Additional comments about services quality**: Provide any additional comments about the services quality.
* **Amenities Quality**: Rate the quality of the hotel's amenities (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about amenities quality**: Provide any additional comments about the amenities quality.
* **Communication**: Rate the hotel's communication (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about communication**: Provide any additional comments about communication.
* **Facilities**: Rate the quality of the hotel's facilities (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about facilities**: Provide any additional comments about the facilities.
* **Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of the hotel (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about cleanliness**: Provide any additional comments about the cleanliness.
* **Staff Helpfulness**: Rate how friendly and helpful the hotel staff were (1-5 scale).
* **Overall Value**: Rate the overall value of the trip (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about overall value**: Provide any additional comments about the overall value.
* **Trip Value**: Rate the trip's value (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about trip value**: Provide any additional comments about the trip's value.
* **Trip Value for Money**: Rate how good the trip was for the money spent (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about trip value for money**: Provide any additional comments about the trip's value for money.
* **Staff Willingness to Help**: Rate how willing to help the hotel staff were (1-5 scale).
* **Would you recommend this trip**: Answer "True" or "False".
* **Travel Experience**: Rate the travel experience (Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Additional comments about travel experience**: Provide any additional comments about the travel experience.
