# Wildlife Photography Impact Study Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey to gather information about the impact of wildlife photography on the environment, conservation, and the local community. It is designed to collect data from individuals who are interested in sharing their thoughts and experiences related to wildlife photography.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Select a date for your last wildlife photography event or experience.
5. Indicate the time of your last wildlife photography event or experience.
6. Choose how you heard about wildlife photography impacts (e.g., through a friend, online research, social media).
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your experience in the "note" field.
8. Finally, click the "submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name**: Enter your name.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **date**: Select a date for your last wildlife photography event or experience.
* **time**: Select the time of your last wildlife photography event or experience.
* **how_did_you_hear_about_wildlife_photography_impacts**: Choose how you heard about the impacts of wildlife photography (e.g., through a friend, online research, social media).
* **note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **submit**: Click the "submit" button to submit your feedback.
