# Issue Suggestion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Issue Suggestion Form is a tool used to collect feedback from users on specific issues they encounter while interacting with a product, service, or process. Your input helps us improve and refine our services to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on "Enter Your Feedback" (Page 1) to provide a brief overview of your feedback, including your name or identifier.
2. On "Issue Description" (Page 2), describe the specific issue you encountered in detail.
3. In "Suggestion" (Page 3), provide a concrete suggestion on how to improve the issue or process related to the feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Your Feedback** (`page_1`, text, optional):
	+ A text field where you can briefly describe the issue or feedback you have.
* **Issue Description** (`page_2`, text, optional):
	+ In this text field, provide a detailed description of the issue you're facing.
* **Suggestion** (`page_3`, text, optional):
	+ Here, suggest a concrete improvement or solution related to the specific issue you described.

## Tips
* Please submit only relevant and genuine feedback to help us improve our services.
* If you have already submitted feedback on the same topic, do not resubmit.
* Your input is invaluable in helping us refine our services for a better user experience.
