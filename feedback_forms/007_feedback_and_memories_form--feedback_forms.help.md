# Feedback And Memories Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Feedback And Memories Form" is a tool for participants and organizers to share their thoughts, experiences, and stories about events, programs, or experiences. This form helps to:

*   Gather feedback on the quality of the event or experience
*   Collect personal stories and memories from participants
*   Understand willingness to share their story publicly or with specific groups

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Rate your experience by answering the "Experience Rating" question (Field 1).
2.  Share your personal story in the "Personal Story" field (Field 2).
3.  Indicate your consent to share your story publicly or with specific groups. You can answer "Yes" or "No" for each question:
    *   "Share Publicly" (Field 9)
    *   "Share with" (Field 10)
    *   "Public and participants" (Field 12)
    *   "Only participants" (Field 13)
    *   "Private" (Field 14)
    *   "Only organizers" (Field 15)
4.  If you choose to share, provide the reason for your consent in the "Sharing Consent Reason" field (Field 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Experience Rating** (Field 1): Rate your experience on a scale of 1 to 5.
*   **Personal Story** (Field 2): Share a brief story about your personal experience.
*   **Sharing Consent** (Field 3): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Sharing Consent Reason** (Field 4): This field is optional and allows you to provide a reason for your decision to share or not share your story.
*   **Photo** (Field 5): You can attach a photo related to your experience.
*   **Testimonial** (Field 6): You can write a testimonial about your experience.
*   **Sharing Consent Agreement** (Field 7): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Feedback Rating** (Field 8): Rate your feedback on a scale of 1 to 5.
*   **Share Publicly** (Field 9): Choose whether to share your story publicly.
*   **Share With** (Field 10): Select which groups you are willing to share your story with:
    *   "Yes"
    *   "No"
*   **Public** (Field 11): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Public and participants** (Field 12): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Only participants** (Field 13): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Private** (Field 14): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Only organizers** (Field 15): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Sharing Consent Agreement 2** (Field 16): This field is not used in the form.
*   **Sharing Consent Reason 2** (Field 17): This field is not used in the form.

**Tips**

*   Make sure to answer all questions clearly and honestly.
*   Provide as much detail as possible in the "Personal Story" field.
*   Choose the correct groups to share your story with, if you have consent to do so.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
