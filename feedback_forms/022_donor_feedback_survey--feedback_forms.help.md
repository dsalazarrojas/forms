# Donor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Feedback Survey form is designed to collect feedback from donors about their experiences with the organization. This form will help the organization understand what they are doing well and what they can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer them to the best of your ability.
2. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in your responses.
3. If you have any comments or feedback, use the "Comments" field to provide more information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for general information about you as a donor.
* **Donor Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select whether you are an individual, an organization, or other.
* **Contact Method** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select which methods you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Contact Name** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your contact information.
* **Feedback** (`6`, `note`, required/optional): Write your thoughts on your experience with our organization!
* **Comments** (`7`, `note`, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`8`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter your email address if you would like to be contacted by us.
* **Phone** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.

## Tips
* Please answer the form questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is valuable to us and helps us improve our services.
