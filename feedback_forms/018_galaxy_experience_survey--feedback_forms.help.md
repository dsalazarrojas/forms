# Galaxy Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about their experiences with a galaxy-themed event. The survey aims to gather information on event details, overall feedback, and satisfaction ratings to help us understand how we can improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the "Event Details" page.
2. Here, you'll be asked to provide a brief description of the event you attended. This will help us understand the specifics of your experience.
3. Next, proceed to the "Feedback" page.
4. In this section, you'll have the opportunity to share any comments or suggestions about your experience. Be as detailed as possible so we can better understand your thoughts.
5. Then, go to the "Event Rating" page.
6. Here, you'll rate your overall experience by selecting a number rating.
7. Finally, visit the "Satisfaction Rating" page.
8. Similar to the previous page, rate your satisfaction with your experience by selecting a number rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: A text field for describing the event you attended. Please provide a brief description of the event, including the date, time, and any notable occurrences. (Text, Optional)
* **Feedback**: A text field for sharing your thoughts and suggestions about your experience. Be as detailed as possible, and don't hesitate to provide constructive criticism. (Text, Optional)
* **Event Rating**: A numerical rating system to gauge your overall experience. Please select a number rating from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. (Number, Optional)
* **Satisfaction Rating**: Another numerical rating system to measure your satisfaction with your experience. Select a number rating from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest. (Number, Optional)

## Tips
- Be as concise as possible when describing the event in the "Event Details" field.
- For both "Event Rating" and "Satisfaction Rating", please choose a rating that best reflects your overall experience.
- If you have any suggestions or improvements for future events, please include them in the "Feedback" field.
