# Monthly Training Follow Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Monthly Training Follow Up Form" is designed to track participants' progress and provide a systematic way to follow up with them. It is used to collect information about each participant, including their category, description, date, training type, location, comments, ID, email, phone, training status, rating, rating scale, follow up, and participant follow up status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct **Participant Category** that best describes the participant.
2. Enter the **Participant Description** in the provided text field.
3. Choose the **Participant Date** of the training session.
4. Select the relevant **Training Type**(s) that applies to the participant.
5. Choose the **Participant Location** where the training took place.
6. Enter any additional **Participant Comments** about their experience.
7. Enter the **Participant ID** number assigned to the participant.
8. Enter the **Participant Email** address of the participant.
9. Enter the **Participant Phone** number of the participant.
10. Select the **Training Status** of the participant.
11. Choose the **Participant Rating** that best reflects their experience.
12. Select the **Rating Scale** used for rating.
13. Choose the **Follow Up** status of the participant.
14. Select the **Participant Follow Up Status**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Category**: Choose the correct category that best describes the participant.
	+ **Category a** and **Category b** are examples of participant categories.
* **Participant Description**: Enter a brief description of the participant.
* **Participant Date**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Type**: Select the type(s) of training that applies to the participant.
	+ **Training Type a** and **Training Type b** are examples of training types.
* **Participant Location**: Choose the location where the training took place.
* **Participant Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the participant's experience.
* **Participant ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to the participant.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Training Status**: Select the status of the training.
	+ **Training Status a** and **Training Status b** are examples of training statuses.
* **Participant Rating**: Choose the rating that best reflects the participant's experience.
* **Rating Scale**: Select the scale used for rating.
	+ **Rating Scale a** and **Rating Scale b** are examples of rating scales.
* **Follow Up**: Choose the follow up status of the participant.
* **Participant Follow Up Status**: Select the follow up status of the participant.

This guide is intended to help users complete the "Monthly Training Follow Up Form" accurately and efficiently.
