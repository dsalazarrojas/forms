# Checkpoint Trainer Feedback Copy - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback from trainees on the effectiveness of a training session with a specific trainer. The feedback is used to improve the training program and help the trainer grow in their role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate and honest feedback.
2. Select your answers carefully, as they will greatly impact the feedback provided to the trainer.
3. Use the space provided in the "Detailed Feedback" section to provide any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainee Name** (`trainee_full_name`): Enter the name of the trainee who attended the training session.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_full_name`): Enter the name of the trainer who delivered the training session.
* **Date of Training** (`training_session_date`): Enter the date on which the training session took place.
* **Training Module / Course Name** (`session_module_title`): Enter the name of the training module or course attended.
* **Expectations and Outcomes** (`section_expectations`): This section is not required, but if you have any comments or observations, please add them here.
* **Did the Training Meet Your Expectations** (`met_initial_expectations`): Select one of the options to indicate whether the training met your expectations:
	+ Exceeded Expectations
	+ Met Expectations
	+ Below Expectations
* **Trainer's Knowledge Rating** (`trainer_knowledge_rating`): Rate the trainer's subject knowledge:
	+ Strongly Agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
* **Clarity of Instructions** (`clarity_of_instructions_rating`): Rate the clarity of the instructions and explanations:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Not Clear
* **Most Helpful Part of the Session** (`most_helpful_training_section`): Describe the most helpful part of the training session.
* **Areas for Improvement for the Trainer** (`areas_for_improvement_trainer`): If you have any suggestions or comments, please add them here.
* **Pacing of the Training** (`pacing_of_training`): Rate the pacing of the training:
	+ Too Fast
	+ Just Right
	+ Too Slow
* **Training Environment Rating** (`training_environment_rating`): Rate the training facilities or platform:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Any Remaining Concerns or Questions** (`trainees_concerns_questions`): If you have any concerns or questions, please add them here.
* **Overall Rating for the Trainer** (`overall_trainer_score`): Rate the trainer overall:
	+ 1 Star
	+ 2 Stars
	+ 3 Stars
	+ 4 Stars
	+ 5 Stars
* **Feedback Verification Signature** (`trainee_signature_ack`): Select the option that best represents your verification of the feedback.

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, and don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or suggestions.
