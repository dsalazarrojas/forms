# professional networking event survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a professional networking event. Your input will help us improve and refine future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
*   Make sure to provide your name and contact information so we can respond to your feedback.
*   Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event type**: Please select the type of event you attended (e.g., conference, meetup, trade show, etc.).
*   **Attendee name**: This is your name, which will be used to address your feedback and respond to any questions or comments.
*   **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and experiences about the event. What did you think about the event? What were your favorite moments or aspects?
*   **Suggestions**: If you have any suggestions for improvement, share them with us. This will help us refine and tailor future events to better meet your needs.
*   **Event score**: Rate the event on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest, based on your overall experience.
*   **Networking score**: Rate the quality of networking opportunities you had during the event, on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Overall score**: Rate the event as a whole, considering all aspects, on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about the event.
*   **Email address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information or would like to follow up on your feedback.
*   **Phone number**: This is your phone number. Please enter it if you would like us to contact you directly.
*   **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts, please share them here.

## Tips

*   Please be honest and constructive with your feedback. Your input is valuable to us.
*   If you have already provided feedback, please do not submit it again.
*   We will use your feedback to improve and refine future events.
