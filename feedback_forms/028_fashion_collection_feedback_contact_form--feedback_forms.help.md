# fashion_collection_feedback_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from customers about a fashion collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Make sure to answer all questions accurately and honestly.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (1)** (`*required*`, `text`:): Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you if needed.
* **What's on your mind (2)** (`*required*`, `text`:): Describe your overall experience with the fashion collection. Be as detailed as possible.
* **Which item did you purchase (3)** (`*required*`, `select_multiple`:): Select all the items you purchased from the collection.
* **Purchase Date (4)** (`*required*`, `date`:): Enter the date you made your purchase.
* **Purchase Price (5)** (`*required*`, `number`:): Enter the price you paid for the item(s).
* **Overall Rating (6)** (`*required*`, `select_one`:): Rate your overall satisfaction with the collection, with options "Good", "Fair", and "Bad".
* **Comments (7)** (`optional`, `text`:): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
