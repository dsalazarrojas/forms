# Utility Service Reliability Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Utility Service Reliability Assessment Form" is designed to help you evaluate the reliability of various utility services you use. This form will ask you to provide feedback on recent outages, the frequency of these outages, their impact on your experience, and how well you were supported by customer service.
## How To Complete This Form
* Review the form carefully to ensure you answer all required fields.
* Answer all fields to the best of your ability, providing specific examples if possible.
* For the "outages_frequency" field, choose the option that best describes how often outages have occurred.
* For the "outages_impact" field, select the option that best describes the impact of outages on your experience.
* For the "outages_consequences" field, you may add any additional comments about the effects of outages on your experience.
* For the "customer_service_response" field, describe the most recent experience you had with customer service.
* For the "timely_response", "communication", and "helpfulness" fields, rate your experience with customer service using numbers, where higher numbers indicate better service.
* For the "overall_satisfaction" field, rate your overall satisfaction with the service, using numbers.
* In the "comments" field, you can add any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **service_reliability** (Number): 
    * Answer this question by rating how often you experience service reliability issues.
* **outages_duration** (Text): 
    * Briefly describe the most recent outages you've experienced, including how long they lasted.
* **outages_impact** (Select One): 
    * Choose the option that describes the most significant effect outages have had on you:
        + None: No impact
        + Low: Some minimal inconvenience
        + High: Considerable inconvenience or significant disruption
* **outages_frequency** (Select Multiple): 
    * Indicate how often you experience outages:
        + Frequent: Almost every week or more often
        + Occasional: Every few weeks or less often
        + Rare: Almost never
* **outages_consequences** (Text): 
    * Add any notes about the effects of outages on your experience.
* **customer_service_response** (Text): 
    * Describe your most recent experience with customer service, including how they addressed your concerns.
* **timely_response**, **communication**, and **helpfulness** (Numbers):
    * Rate your experience with customer service as:
        + 1-2: Very dissatisfied
        + 3-4: Somewhat dissatisfied
        + 5-6: Somewhat satisfied
        + 7-8: Very satisfied
* **overall_satisfaction** (Number): 
    * Rate your overall satisfaction with the service, using numbers from 1-8, where 1 is very dissatisfied and 8 is very satisfied.
* **comments** (Text): 
    * Provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience.
