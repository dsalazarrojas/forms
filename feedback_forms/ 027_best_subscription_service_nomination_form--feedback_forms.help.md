# Best Subscription Service Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for users to provide feedback and nominate their preferred subscription service. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your input is considered in the decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number (if available).
- Select the date that best applies to your nomination.
- Choose the primary selection that best describes your experience with the service.
- If required, provide a whole number for quantity or rating, estimated value, and preferred time.
- Select any agreements that apply to confirm your understanding.
- Add any additional comments or remarks to provide context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name for our records.
  * Please use your real name to ensure accuracy.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email for contact purposes.
  * This will be used to communicate with you regarding your nomination.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Include your country code for international numbers.
  * Only enter your phone number if you're outside of the local region.
* **Date** (`reference_date`, date, required): Select the date that best applies to your nomination.
  * Choose the date that corresponds to the period you're nominating.
* **Primary Selection** (`primary_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best describes your experience with the service.
  * Select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor based on your experience.
* **Quantity or Rating** (`quantity_or_rating`, number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
  * Enter a number for quantity, or a number for rating (e.g., 1-5).
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information.
  * Use this field to add context or details not covered by other fields.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Select any applicable agreements to confirm your understanding.
  * Choose from the options I Agree, I need follow up, and Not applicable.
* **Estimated Value** (`estimated_value`, number, optional): Enter the estimated value, using decimal format if necessary.
  * Use this field to provide an estimated value for your nomination.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
  * Only enter this field if scheduling is a factor in your nomination.
* **Final Comments or Notes** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Provide any final comments or notes.
  * Use this field to add a closing remark or additional context.
