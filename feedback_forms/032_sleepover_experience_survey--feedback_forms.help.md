# Sleepover Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback from users about their sleepover experience. It will help organizations or individuals in the events industry to gauge the quality of the sleepovers they host and make improvements accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Select your rating of the sleepover experience by choosing from 1 to 5.
2. If you wish, provide comments on your rating in the space provided.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
4. Select the person you would like to contact.
5. If this was a sleepover, you can select the start and end times of your event. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sleepover Experience Rating** (rating_page): Select your rating of the sleepover experience from 1 to 5.
* **Comments on rating** (rating_comment): Provide any additional comments you'd like to share about your rating.
* **Email** (email_page): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Name** (name_page): Enter your name.
* **Contact** (contact_page): Select the person you would like to contact.
* **Date** (date_page): Select the date of your sleepover, if this applies.
* **Start Time** (start_time_page): Enter the start time of your sleepover, if this applies.
* **End Time** (end_time_page): Enter the end time of your sleepover, if this applies.
* **Event** (event_page): Enter a brief description of your sleepover.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions as honestly as possible to help us improve the sleepover experience.
* If you have any other comments or suggestions, feel free to share them with us.
* We appreciate your feedback and will take it into consideration when making improvements.
