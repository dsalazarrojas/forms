# Theater Audience Information Collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Audience Information Collection form is designed to collect essential details from theater audience members for efficient audience management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to access its fields.
2. Fill out the form with the required and optional information as specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
- **Date** (`performance_date`, date, optional): Select the performance date of the event you are attending.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the performance.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Select the end time of the performance.
- **Venue** (`venue`, text, optional): Specify the venue where the event will be held.
- **Seat Number** (`seat_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of the seat assigned to you.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback about your experience at the theater.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
* If you are having trouble finding the correct date or time, refer to the event schedule provided by the theater.
* Please provide accurate information to help the theater staff plan and manage the event smoothly.
