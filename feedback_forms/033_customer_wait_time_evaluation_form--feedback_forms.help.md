# Customer Wait Time Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from customers about their wait time experience at our establishment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer wait time in minutes on the "Customer Wait Time" field.
2. Select how many staff members were present during your visit on the "Staff Attendance" field.
3. Enter the number of staff members present during your visit on the "Staff Attendance" field.
4. Rate your overall experience on the "Customer Feedback" field, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
5. Submit the form to provide your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Wait Time
* **Customer Wait Time (minutes)**: Enter the actual wait time you experienced during your visit.
### Staff Attendance
* **Staff Attendance**: This field will ask you to select how many staff members were present during your visit.
### Staff Attendance Hint
* **Staff Attendance Hint**: This field is a duplicate of the "Staff Attendance" field, where you can enter the number of staff members present during your visit.
### Customer Feedback
* **Customer Feedback**: Rate your overall experience, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
### Customer Feedback Hint
* **Customer Feedback Hint**: This field is not applicable as there is no hint provided in the YAML.
### Customer Satisfaction
* **Customer Satisfaction**: This field is not applicable as it is a duplicate of the "Customer Feedback" field.
