# Digital Learning Resource Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Learning Resource Feedback Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect feedback and evaluation from users regarding a digital learning resource.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **Feedback Form**: Provide a brief description of your experience with the resource.
2. **Resource Title**: Enter the title of the resource you are evaluating.
3. **Rating**: Choose a rating from the options provided (One Star, Two Stars, Three Stars, Four Stars, or Five Stars).
4. **Feedback**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have regarding the resource.
5. **Resource Link**: Provide a link to the resource you are evaluating.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address.
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
8. **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this feedback.
9. **Submission Time**: Enter the time you are submitting this feedback.
10. **Assigned to**: Enter the person or group to whom you are submitting this feedback.
11. **Submitted by**: Enter the name of the person submitting this feedback.
12. **Comments 2**: Enter any additional comments you may have regarding this feedback.
13. **Submission Date 2**: Enter the date you are submitting this feedback (if different from previous).
14. **Submission Time 2**: Enter the time you are submitting this feedback (if different from previous).
15. **Resource Type**: Enter the type of resource you are evaluating.
16. **Resource URL**: Enter the URL of the resource you are evaluating.
17. **Submitted By 2**: Enter the name of the person submitting this feedback (if different from previous).
18. **Submission Date 3**: Enter the date you are submitting this feedback (if different from previous).
19. **Submission Time 3**: Enter the time you are submitting this feedback (if different from previous).
20. **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or platform used to submit this feedback.
21. **Feedback Status**: Enter the status of your feedback (e.g. "completed", "in progress", etc.).
22. **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting this feedback.
23. **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting this feedback.
24. **Comments 3**: Enter any additional comments you may have regarding this feedback.
