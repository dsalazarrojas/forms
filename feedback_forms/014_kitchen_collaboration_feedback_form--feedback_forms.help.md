# Kitchen Collaboration Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from employees on their experiences with kitchen collaboration, which is used to improve communication, teamwork, and efficiency in the kitchen. Feedback is optional and can be provided by anyone who has participated or witnessed events in the kitchen.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form when you're finished. Make sure to fill in all the required fields. If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact your supervisor or HR department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it is written on your business card or ID badge.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as it is written on your business card or ID badge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your work email address.
* **What type of feedback?** (`feedback_type`, select_one, not required): Choose the type of feedback you'd like to provide:
	+ General: Share your general thoughts and experiences.
	+ Suggestion: Share a specific idea or improvement suggestion.
	+ Issue: Report a problem or concern you've encountered.
* **Comment** (`comment`, text, not required): Share your thoughts and ideas in more detail.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, not required): Enter the date when you submitted the feedback, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, not required): Enter the time when you submitted the feedback, in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Submission Date & Time** (`submission_date_time`, text, not required): Enter the date and time when you submitted the feedback, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`. 

**Tips:**

* Please be specific and objective when providing feedback.
* Try to focus on facts and experiences rather than opinions or emotions.
* If you're unsure what type of feedback to provide, simply select "General" as the default option.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (none on this form, but please be thorough with your answers).
