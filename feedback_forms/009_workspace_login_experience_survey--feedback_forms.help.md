# Workspace Login Experience Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects feedback from users about their experience with the workspace login process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly about your login experience.
2. Select the options that best fit your answers.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Satisfaction comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Frequency of login** (`login_frequency`, number, optional)
* This question asks about how often you log in to the workspace.

### 2. **Devices used for login** (`devices_used`, select_multiple, optional)
* Select all the devices you use to log in to the workspace, such as Laptop, Tablet, Desktop, Smartphone, or Other.

### 3. **Browsers used for login** (`browsers_used`, select_one, optional)
* Choose the browser you most often use to log in to the workspace, such as Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari, or Other.

### 4. **Issues encountered during login** (`issues_encountered`, text, optional)
* Describe any issues you encountered during the login process.

### 5. **Satisfaction score** (`satisfaction_score`, number, optional)
* Rate your overall satisfaction with the login experience on a scale from 1 to 5.

### 6. **Satisfaction comment** (`satisfaction_comment`, note, optional)
* Provide any comments about your satisfaction with the login experience.

### 7. **Satisfaction with device used for login** (`login_device_satisfaction`, select_one, optional)
* Choose how satisfied you are with the device you used to log in, such as Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.

### 8. **Satisfaction with browser used for login** (`login_browser_satisfaction`, select_one, optional)
* Choose how satisfied you are with the browser you used to log in, such as Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully to help us improve the login experience.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Satisfaction comment" field.
* Select all devices and browsers that apply to you.
* Rate your satisfaction score based on your overall experience.
