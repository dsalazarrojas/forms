# in-flight-service-request-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from in-flight service requests. It's designed to gather essential information for customer service teams to respond to your needs promptly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number to help us quickly identify the flight you're inquiring about.
2. **Passenger Name**: Provide your full name for our records.
3. **Request Type**: Choose whether your request is urgent or non-urgent.
4. **Issue Description**: Briefly describe the issue you'd like to report.
5. **Preferred Action**: Select the preferred action we should take for your request.
6. **Follow Up**: Choose how quickly you'd like us to follow up on your action (optional).
7. **Email**: Enter a valid email address for our team to reach out to you.
8. **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number for our team to contact you.
9. **Passenger Location**: Provide the location where you're currently situated (optional).
10. **Crew Location**: Enter the location of our crew (if known) (optional).
11. **Flight Status**: Select the current status of your flight (if known) (optional).
12. **Service Request Date**: Enter the date of the service request.
13. **Service Request Time**: Enter the time of the service request.
14. **Customer Service Agent**: Choose the customer service agent who will handle your request (if known) (optional).
15. **Customer Service Team**: Select the team handling your request (if known) (optional).
16. **Customer Service Shift**: Choose the shift of the customer service agent handling your request (if known) (optional).
17. **Service Status**: Select the current status of the service request (if known) (optional).
18. **Service Action**: Provide a detailed description of the service action required.
19-25: These fields are not currently used for the form's primary purpose. Please disregard them.
