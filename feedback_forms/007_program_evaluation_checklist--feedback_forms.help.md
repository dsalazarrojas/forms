# Program Evaluation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about a program's quality and effectiveness. The goal is to collect data that will help the program team identify areas of improvement and make informed decisions about future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Answer each question to the best of your ability based on your experience with the program.
* If you are unsure or do not have an answer, please leave the field blank.
* You can provide additional comments or suggestions in the last field.
* Please note that all fields are optional, unless otherwise specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the program’s purpose? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 1)**
	+ This question asks you to describe the main objective or goal of the program. Please provide a brief summary of what the program is intended to accomplish.
* **How well does the program meet its purpose? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 2)**
	+ Answer this question by selecting "Yes" or "No" depending on how well you feel the program meets its stated purpose.
* **What are the most effective strategies or tools used? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 3)**
	+ In this text field, please describe the most effective strategies or tools that you used while working with the program. Be specific and provide concrete examples.
* **How would you rate your satisfaction with the overall program quality? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 4)**
	+ Use the number field to rate your satisfaction with the overall quality of the program. The options range from 0 (low) to 10 (high).
* **How well does the program meet its goals and objectives? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 5)**
	+ Answer this question by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each goal and objective that the program aims to achieve.
* **What are the most significant areas of improvement? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 6)**
	+ In this text field, please describe the most significant areas where you think the program could improve. Be specific and provide concrete examples.
* **How will you apply what you have learned from this program? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 7)**
	+ In this text field, please describe how you plan to apply or use the knowledge, skills, or strategies learned from the program.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for the program team? (Program Evaluation Checklist - Program 8)**
	+ This is an open-ended text field where you can provide any additional comments or suggestions that you think would improve the program.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the program team.
* Your input is valuable to us and will help us improve the program. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
