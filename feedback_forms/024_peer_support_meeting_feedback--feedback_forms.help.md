# Peer Support Meeting Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Support Meeting Feedback form is designed to gather feedback from participants on their experience in a peer support meeting. This feedback is valuable in helping facilitators and organizers understand what worked well and what could be improved in future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the facilitator who led your meeting in the "Facilitator Name" field.
3. Provide the ID number of the session you attended.
4. Specify the date and time of the session you attended.
5. Enter the location of the session.
6. Rate your feedback on the meeting using the five rating fields.
7. Provide feedback on what you found helpful and what you didn't find helpful.
8. Suggest improvements for future sessions.
9. Add any additional comments or feedback.
10. Sign the form by providing a signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Please enter your name as a participant of the peer support meeting.
* **Facilitator Name**: Enter the name of the facilitator who led your meeting.
* **Session ID**: Provide the session ID number.
* **Date of Session**: Specify the date of the session you attended.
* **Time of Session**: Specify the time of the session you attended.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the session.
* **Feedback Rating**: Use the rating system to provide your overall feedback on the meeting.
* **What did you find helpful?**: Describe what you found helpful in the meeting.
* **What didn't you find helpful?**: Describe what you didn't find helpful in the meeting.
* **Suggestions for future sessions**: Suggest improvements for future sessions.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional feedback or comments.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign the form by providing a participant signature.
* **Facilitator Signature**: Sign the form by providing a facilitator signature.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Specify the date.
* **Time**: Specify the time.
* **Location**: Enter the location.
* **Rating 1**: Rate your feedback on the first aspect of the meeting.
* **Rating 2**: Rate your feedback on the second aspect of the meeting.
* **Rating 3**: Rate your feedback on the third aspect of the meeting.
* **Rating 4**: Rate your feedback on the fourth aspect of the meeting.
* **Rating 5**: Rate your feedback on the fifth aspect of the meeting.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or feedback.
* **Submitted by**: Enter the name of the person submitting the feedback.

## Tips
* Please fill out each field completely and accurately.
* Make sure to check the rating fields for completeness.
* Be specific in your feedback and suggestions.
* Sign the form after filling out all the fields.
