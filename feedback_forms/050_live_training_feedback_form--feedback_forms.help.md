# Live Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Training Feedback Form is used to collect feedback from participants who have attended a live training session. This feedback will help evaluate the effectiveness of the training program and improve future trainings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have reviewed this guide to understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
3. Fill out all required fields marked as "required".
4. Use the "Session Leaders" field to select the leader(s) who conducted the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the training program you attended.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the location of the training session.
* **Session Leaders** (`session_leaders`, select_one, optional): Select the leader(s) who conducted the training session.
* **Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training session (1-5).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if available).
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your feedback is collected effectively.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, contact your training coordinator or facilitator.
