# Mini Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mini Contact Form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our website, product, or service. Please use this form to share your thoughts, opinions, or suggestions on how we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Mini Contact Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name and Last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Provide your Email address to receive a response to your feedback.
3. Enter your Phone number (if you prefer to be contacted by phone).
4. Write a detailed Message with your feedback or suggestions.
5. Choose the type of feedback you are providing: Product, Website, or Other.
6. If you have additional feedback or comments, type them in the Additional feedback field.
7. (Optional) Select the animation settings to customize your experience:
	* Animation: Choose whether to enable or disable animations on the form.
	* Animation Speed: Select the speed of the animation.
	* Animation Repeat: Select how many times the animation should repeat.
8. If you have any Other information you'd like to provide, enter it in the corresponding text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to provide a personal touch to your feedback.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to complete your identity.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your Email address to receive a response to your feedback.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your Phone number to receive a call back.
* **Message** (`message`, `note`, required): Write a detailed Message with your feedback or suggestions.
* **What do you have feedback on?** (`feedback_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of feedback you are providing: Product, Website, or Other.
* **Additional feedback** (`feedback`, `note`, optional): If you have more information to provide, type it in the Additional feedback field.
* **Animation** (`animation`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether to enable or disable animations on the form.
* **Animation Speed** (`animation_speed`, `select_one`, optional): Select the speed of the animation.
* **Animation Repeat** (`animation_repeat`, `select_one`, optional): Select how many times the animation should repeat.
* **Other information** (`other_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any other information to provide, enter it in this text field.
* **Other information (repeat)** (`other_info_repeat`, `text`, optional): If you have more information to provide, enter it in this text field.
