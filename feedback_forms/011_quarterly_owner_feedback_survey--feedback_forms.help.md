# Quarterly Owner Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quarterly Owner Feedback Survey is designed to gather information on your experiences and satisfaction with our service. Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve our services and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your details, including your name, email address, and other requested information.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
3. Make sure to provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your identification.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the property you are providing feedback on.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property you own (Apartment, House, Townhouse, Commercial Property, Land, Condo, Office).
* **What would you like to feedback about the property** (`feedback`, text, required): Share your thoughts and experiences about the property.
* **How satisfied are you with our service** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, required): Select how satisfied you are with our service (Not Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Disatisfied, Satisfied).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with us.
* **Follow-up date** (`follow_up`, date, optional): If you have a specific date for a follow-up, enter it here. (This is optional and not required)
* **Time of Visit** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of your visit to the property.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide accurate information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Your feedback is appreciated and will help us improve our services.
