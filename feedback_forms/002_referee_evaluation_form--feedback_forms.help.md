# Referee Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback from game participants on a referee's performance during a sports event. This form helps sports organizations or event managers assess the quality of refereeing services and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Evaluator's Name and Email in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the Referee's Name who was evaluated.
3. Select the Date of the game or event being evaluated.
4. Choose the Competition Level of the game or event.
5. Provide written feedback on the Referee's Dress and Appearance, Pre-Game, Fitness, Attitude, Courage, Character, Consistency, Position, Mechanics, Signals, Accuracy of Decisions, Game Control, and any Additional Comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Evaluator's Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the person evaluating the referee.
* **Evaluator's Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the person evaluating the referee.
* **Referee's Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the referee being evaluated.
* **Date (Date)**: Select the date of the game or event being evaluated.
* **Competition Level (Select One)**: Choose the level of competition (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Dress & Appearance (Text)**: Describe the referee's dress and appearance during the game.
* **Pre-Game (Text)**: Describe the referee's performance before the game started.
* **Fitness (Text)**: Evaluate the referee's physical fitness during the game.
* **Attitude (Text)**: Describe the referee's attitude towards the game and players.
* **Courage (Text)**: Evaluate the referee's bravery and courage during critical situations.
* **Character (Text)**: Describe the referee's character and sportsmanship.
* **Consistency (Text)**: Evaluate the referee's consistency in enforcing rules and decisions.
* **Position (Text)**: Describe the referee's position and positioning during the game.
* **Mechanics (Text)**: Evaluate the referee's mechanics and technical skills.
* **Signals (Text)**: Describe the referee's signals and communication during the game.
* **Accuracy of Decisions (Text)**: Evaluate the referee's accuracy in making decisions.
* **Game Control (Text)**: Describe the referee's control over the game flow.
* **Additional Comments (Note)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the referee's performance.
