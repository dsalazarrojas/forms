# Driver Vehicle Maintenance Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from drivers about their vehicle maintenance practices, service quality, and overall vehicle condition. It will help us understand how we can improve our services and provide better support to our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Select the make of your vehicle from the options provided.
2. Provide the model of your vehicle in the text field.
3. Describe the maintenance practices you use or recommend any specific practices you think are essential for your vehicle's maintenance.
4. Rate the quality of service you received on your last vehicle maintenance check-in.
5. Select the date of your last vehicle check-in.
6. Rate the condition of your vehicle based on how well it is performing.
7. Leave any additional comments or recommendations for our mechanics in the text field.
8. Provide any other comments or feedback you have about your vehicle maintenance experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Maintenance Form**: This field is a text area where you can provide any additional information or comments about your vehicle maintenance practices.
* **Vehicle Make**: Choose the make of your vehicle from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle in the text field.
* **Maintenance Practices**: Describe the practices you use for maintaining your vehicle, such as regular oil changes, tire rotations, or other specific practices.
* **Service Quality**: Rate the quality of service you received on your last vehicle maintenance check-in using the options provided.
* **Maintenance Schedule**: This field is currently not used in the form, so please ignore it.
* **Last Vehicle Check-in**: Select the date of your last vehicle check-in.
* **Vehicle Condition**: Rate the condition of your vehicle based on how well it is performing.
* **Mechanic Recommendation**: This field is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or recommendations for our mechanics.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any other comments or feedback you have about your vehicle maintenance experience.
