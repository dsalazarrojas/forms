# Absensi Ibadah Persekutuan Jumat PO FEB UI - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from attendees of the Absensi Ibadah Persekutuan Jumat PO FEB UI event, allowing the organizers to evaluate the effectiveness of the event and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your full name (Nama Lengkap) in the text field.
* Enter your valid email address (Email Address) in the email field.
* Fill in your phone number (Nomor Telepon) in the text field (optional).
* Specify your year of enrollment or batch (Angkatan) in the text field.
* Choose your program of study (Program Studi) from the list provided.
* Select the date of the fellowship service you attended (Tangggal Ibadah).
* Choose your attendance status (Attendance Status) from the list:
	+ Present - attended full service
	+ Present - attended partially
	+ Absent
* If desired, rate your experience with the fellowship service on a scale of 1-5.
* If desired, rate the relevance and meaningfulness of the sermon topic (Sermon Topic Relevance).
* If desired, rate your worship experience (Worship Experience).
* If you have a prayer request, feel free to share it (Prayer Request).
* If you have any suggestions for improving future fellowship services, please share them (Suggestions).
* If you are interested in serving or volunteering in fellowship activities, let us know (Interest in Serving).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nama Lengkap (Full name)** (`nama_lengkap`, `text`, required): Fill in your full name to ensure we can contact you for any follow-up communication.
* **Email Address (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can reach out to you.
* **Nomor Telepon (Phone number)** (`nomor_telepon`, `text`, optional): If you want to be contacted via phone, please fill in your phone number.
* **Angkatan (Year of enrollment or batch)** (`angkatan`, `text`, required): Specify your year of enrollment or batch to help us understand your background.
* **Program Studi (Program of study)** (`program_studi`, `text`, required): Choose your program of study from the list provided to help us understand your area of interest.
* **Tangggal Ibadah (Date of fellowship service)** (`tanggal_ibadah`, `date`, required): Select the date of the fellowship service you attended.
* **Attendance Status (Attendance status)** (`attendance_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your attendance status to help us evaluate the effectiveness of the event.
* **Service Rating (How would you rate today's fellowship service)** (`service_rating`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your experience with the fellowship service on a scale of 1-5.
* **Sermon Topic Relevance (Was the sermon topic relevant and meaningful to you)** (`sermon_topic_relevance`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the relevance and meaningfulness of the sermon topic.
* **Worship Experience (How was your worship experience)** (`worship_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your worship experience.
* **Prayer Request (Do you have a prayer request you would like to share)** (`prayer_request`, `text`, optional): Share any prayer requests you may have.
* **Suggestions (Suggestions for improving future fellowship services)** (`suggestions`, `text`, optional): Share any suggestions you may have for improving future services.
* **Interest in Serving (Are you interested in serving or volunteering in fellowship activities)** (`interest_in_serving`, `select_one`, optional): Let us know if you are interested in serving or volunteering in fellowship activities.
