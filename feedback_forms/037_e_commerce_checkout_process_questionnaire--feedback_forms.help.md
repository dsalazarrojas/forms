# e_commerce_checkout_process_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a customer feedback tool designed to evaluate the effectiveness and usability of the e-commerce checkout process. It helps the company to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the overall customer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each page carefully and answer the questions honestly to provide accurate feedback.
- Select the options that best describe your experience with each question.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "additional_comments" field.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, optional): This is an introductory page that explains the purpose of the form. Please read it carefully to understand the context and goals of the questionnaire.
* **Checkout Process** (`checkout_process`, select_one, required): Evaluate the checkout process as too short, too long, or confusing. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Product Information** (`product_info`, select_multiple, required): Check all the options that apply to your experience with product information provided during the checkout process.
* **Payment Process** (`payment_process`, select_one, required): Rate the payment process as too complicated, too simple, or just right. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate your overall experience with the checkout process on a scale of 1-10.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your satisfaction with the checkout process on a scale of 1-5.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, select all the options that apply.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the checkout process.
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Contact Other** (`contact_other`, text, optional): If you have any alternative contact information, please provide it here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions that you think will help us improve the checkout process.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have submitted a payment or not.

Please submit the form once you have completed all the required fields. Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
