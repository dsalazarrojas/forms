# Parent Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Feedback Form is a tool designed to gather feedback from parents on their child's progress in a specific subject or topic. This feedback is used to help teachers and instructors adjust their teaching methods and materials to better meet the needs of their students.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for each field below.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in each field to give the teacher a clear understanding of the child's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the parent providing feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Write a detailed description of the child's performance in the subject or topic being evaluated.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the parent's email address to allow for further communication.
* **Feedback Date** (`feedback_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the feedback being given.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required: false): Include the parent's signature for verification purposes.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the child being given feedback on.

## Tips
* Please be as specific and detailed as possible in your feedback to help the teacher better understand the child's performance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the teacher directly.
* This form should only be filled out once for each child and topic being evaluated.
