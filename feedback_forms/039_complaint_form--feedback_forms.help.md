# Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and complaints from employees about their work experience, providing a channel for internal resolution and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  If you wish to report a complaint, please fill out the form below.
2.  Provide your name and department if you wish to be contacted for follow-up, or remain anonymous if you prefer.
3.  Enter the date of the incident or concern.
4.  Select the nature of the complaint from the list provided.
5.  Describe the issue as detailed as possible.
6.  If you have discussed this issue with your manager or HR before, select the appropriate option.
7.  Specify your desired resolution.
8.  Indicate how the issue is affecting your work performance and morale.
9.  If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them.
10.  Decide if you would like someone from HR to contact you for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Employee Improvement and Complaint Feedback --** (Feedback Form Header): This section is just a heading to introduce the form.
* **Employee Name (Optional)** (`employee_name_optional`): If you wish to remain anonymous, leave this field blank. Otherwise, provide your name for follow-up and resolution purposes.
* **Department** (`employee_department_complaint`): Enter your current working department.
* **Date of Incident or Concern** (`complaint_incident_date`): Provide the approximate date of the incident or concern. If it's an ongoing issue, provide the start date.
* **Nature of Complaint** (`complaint_type_selection`): Select the most relevant category from the options provided.
* **Detailed Description of the Issue** (`detailed_complaint_desc`): Please describe the issue as detailed as possible (Who, What, When).
* **Have you discussed this with your manager or HR before?** (`previous_discussion_status`): If you have discussed this issue before, select the appropriate option. If you do not feel comfortable discussing it, please select "I do not feel comfortable doing so."
* **What is your desired resolution?** (`desired_resolution_employee`): Specify how the company can make this better.
* **How is this issue impacting your ability to work?** (`impact_on_work_rating`): Indicate the impact on your performance and morale.
* **Suggestions for Company Improvement** (`suggestions_for_better_company`): If you have any ideas for improvement, please provide them.
* **Would you like someone from HR to contact you?** (`contact_for_followup`): If you provided your name, select "Yes, please contact me." Otherwise, select "No, just reporting for record."

## Tips

*   Ensure that you have not submitted a similar issue in the past.
*   Be as specific as possible when describing the issue.
*   If you have any additional suggestions or ideas for improvement, please provide them in the "Suggestions for Company Improvement" field.
*   If you would like someone from HR to contact you for follow-up, please select "Yes, please contact me."
