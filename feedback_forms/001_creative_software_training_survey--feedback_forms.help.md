# Creative Software Training Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users about their training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each field carefully and answer every question truthfully.
- Take your time to ensure your answers are accurate and detailed.
- You may skip questions that don't apply to your experience.
- You can add or remove spaces as needed to provide sufficient space to answer each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction**: A brief description of your training experience.
  * This is a note field where you can provide a short introduction about your training session.
  * Please keep it brief and concise.
- **Overall Impact**: How impactful was the training for you?
  * Use a number rating to rate the overall effect of the training on your work or personal projects.
  * Higher numbers indicate a more significant impact.
- **Prophet**: Which of the following describes your trainer?
  * Please choose one of the options listed.
  * This question helps us understand the performance of our trainers.
- **Training Level**: Which level best describes your training experience?
  * This question helps us evaluate the effectiveness of our training.
  * Please choose one of the options provided.
- **Date**: When did you attend the training session?
  * This is a date field. Enter the date of your training session.
- **Time**: What time did you attend the training session?
  * This is a time field. Enter the time of your training session.
- **Email**: Your contact information.
  * Please enter a valid email address for follow-up communication.
  * We may contact you for further clarification or to provide updates.
- **Phone**: Your contact information.
  * Please enter a valid phone number for further communication.
  * We may contact you for further clarification or to provide updates.
- **Additional Comments**: Any additional feedback or comments about your training experience.
  * This is a text field where you can add any comments you'd like to share.
  * Please be as detailed as possible.
- **Rating**: How would you rate your training experience?
  * Please rate your experience using the number rating provided.
  * This helps us understand the overall effectiveness of our training programs.
- **Training Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with the training?
  * Please choose one of the options provided.
  * This helps us gauge the effectiveness of our training programs.
- **Feedback**: Is there anything you'd like to add or change in our training program?
  * This is a text field where you can provide any feedback or suggestions.
  * Please be as specific as possible.
- **Suggestion**: Do you have any suggestions for improving our training programs?
  * This is a text field where you can provide any suggestions.
  * Please be as specific as possible.
- **Contact Info**: Your contact information.
  * Please enter your name or any other contact information.
  * We may contact you for further communication.
- **Session Length**: How long did your training session last?
  * Please enter the duration of your training session in minutes.
- **Topic**: What was the topic of your training session?
  * Please enter a brief description of the topic.
  * This helps us tailor our training programs to your needs.
- **Session Date**: When did your training session take place?
  * This is a date field. Enter the date of your training session.
- **Session Time**: What time did your training session take place?
  * This is a time field. Enter the time of your training session.
- **Session Location**: Where did your training session take place?
  * Please enter the location of your training session.
  * This helps us understand the effectiveness of our training locations.
- **Session Notes**: Any additional notes about your training session.
  * This is a text field where you can provide any notes.
  * Please be as detailed as possible.
- **Trainer**: Who was your trainer for the session?
  * Please enter the name of your trainer.
  * This helps us understand the performance of our trainers.
- **Co-Trainer**: Who was your co-trainer for the session?
  * Please enter the name of your co-trainer.
  * This helps us understand the performance of our co-trainers.
- **Session Co-Trainer**: Who was your co-trainer for the session?
  * Please enter the name of your co-trainer.
  * This helps us understand the performance of our co-trainers.
- **Session Topic**: What was the topic of your training session?
  * Please enter a brief description of the topic.
  * This helps us tailor our training programs to your needs.
- **Session Topic**: Duplicate of the "Session Topic" field (ID 25)
  * Please enter a brief description of the topic.
  * This helps us tailor our training programs to your needs.

This help guide is designed to guide users through the feedback form, helping them understand its purpose and how to complete it. Each field has a clear and concise explanation, making it easy for users to provide accurate and helpful feedback.
