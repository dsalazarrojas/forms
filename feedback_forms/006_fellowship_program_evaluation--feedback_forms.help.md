# fellowship_program_evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the quality and effectiveness of a fellowship program. The form aims to gather feedback and ratings from participants, which will be used to identify areas of improvement and inform future program development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Rating Scale (1)**: Select a rating from 1-5 to indicate your overall satisfaction with the program. A higher rating indicates a higher level of satisfaction.
2. **Feedback (2)**: Provide a brief written feedback about your experience with the program. This is an opportunity to share your thoughts and suggestions for improvement.
3. **Improvement Suggestion (3)**: If you have any suggestions for improving the program, type them in this field.
4. **Select Ratings (4)**: Choose all that apply from the following options:
	* Option 1
	* Option 2
	* Option 3
5. **Stakeholders (5)**: Select the stakeholders that you have interacted with during the program.
6. **Rating Date (6)**: Enter the date when you completed the rating.
7. **Rating Time (7)**: Enter the time when you completed the rating.
8. **Email (8)**: If you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback, enter your email address.
9. **Phone (9)**: If you wish to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or feedback, enter your phone number.
10. **Notes (10)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your experience with the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rating Scale (1)**: This field is used to rate your overall satisfaction with the program. Select a number from 1 to 5.
* **Feedback (2)**: This field is used to provide a brief written feedback about your experience with the program.
* **Improvement Suggestion (3)**: This field is used to suggest ways to improve the program. It is optional and only required if you have specific suggestions.
* **Select Ratings (4)**: This field is used to choose all applicable options from the list provided.
* **Stakeholders (5)**: This field is used to select the stakeholders that you have interacted with during the program.
* **Rating Date (6)**: This field is used to enter the date when you completed the rating.
* **Rating Time (7)**: This field is used to enter the time when you completed the rating.
* **Email (8)**: This field is used to enter your email address if you wish to be contacted for follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Phone (9)**: This field is used to enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone for follow-up questions or feedback.
* **Notes (10)**: This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about your experience with the program.

## Tips

* Please provide honest and constructive feedback to help us improve the program.
* If you have any suggestions for improvement, be specific and provide details.
* If you are contacted for follow-up questions or feedback, please respond promptly to help us improve the program.
