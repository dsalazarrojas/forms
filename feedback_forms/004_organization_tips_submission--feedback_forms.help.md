# Organization Tips Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organization Tips Submission form is designed to collect feedback from users about their experiences with an organization. This form helps us better understand team-related submissions and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date you submitted your feedback in the `submit_date` field.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether your submission is valid in the `submission_type` field.
3. Enter your team's name in the `team_name` field.
4. Provide a brief description of your team in the `team_description` field.
5. Enter your team's location in the `team_location` field.
6. Share any ideas or suggestions you have for the organization in the `team_ideas` field.
7. If you have a supporting file to attach, click the "Attach File" button in the `supporting_file` field.
8. If you have a file to upload, click the "Upload File" button in the `uploaded_file` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **submit_date**: Enter the date you submitted your feedback.
* **submission_type**: Select 'Yes' if your submission is valid, 'No' otherwise.
* **team_name**: Enter your team's name.
* **team_description**: Provide a brief description of your team.
* **team_location**: Enter your team's location.
* **team_ideas**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have for the organization.
* **supporting_file**: If you have a supporting file, click the "Attach File" button to attach it.
* **uploaded_file**: If you have a file to upload, click the "Upload File" button to upload it.
