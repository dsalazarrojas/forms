# customer_feedback_survey_approval_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_feedback_survey_approval_form is designed to gather feedback from customers on our service. The form collects information on the type of feedback, level of satisfaction, areas for improvement, and potential issues experienced.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of feedback you would like to provide.
2. Choose your level of satisfaction with our service.
3. Select areas that could be improved.
4. Provide more feedback on selected areas.
5. (Optional) If you have experienced issues, describe them.
6. Choose how you would like to contact us.
7. Decide whether you would like to have your feedback reviewed.
8. (Optional) Select your preferred contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What kind of feedback would you like to provide? (field_1)**: This field allows you to select the type of feedback you would like to provide. Please choose one of the options to indicate the type of feedback you would like to submit.
* **How satisfied are you with our service? (field_2)**: This field asks for your level of satisfaction with our service. Please select a number that best represents your satisfaction level.
* **Which of the following best describes your feedback? (field_3)**: This field has three options: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'. Select the option that best describes your feedback.
* **Please select areas that could be improved (field_4)**: In this field, you can select multiple areas that could be improved. Choose from the options 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other' for each area.
* **Please provide more feedback on this area (field_5)**: This field is an opportunity to provide more detailed feedback on the selected areas for improvement.
* **Can you provide more feedback on this area? (field_6)**: This field is similar to the previous one, but with a slightly different phrasing.
* **Which of the following best describes your feedback? (field_7)**: This field has three options: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'. Select the option that best describes your feedback.
* **What would you like us to improve on? (field_8)**: This field is for providing specific suggestions on how we can improve.
* **How would you like to contact us? (field_9)**: This field is for choosing how you would like to contact us. Please select one of the text options.
* **Can you describe the issues you've experienced? (field_10)**: If you have experienced issues, please provide a description in this field.
* **Would you like us to review this feedback? (field_11)**: Select one of the options to decide whether you would like us to review your feedback.
* **Would you like to contact us again? (field_12)**: This field has three options: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'. Select the option that best describes your intention to contact us.

Note: The fields with "(field_#" notation are actual field names from the provided YAML.
