# Agency Collaboration Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from evaluators on their experience working with an agency. The form helps to identify areas of strength and improvement for future collaborations, promoting effective communication, responsiveness, and quality deliverables. It's essential for agencies to receive constructive feedback to improve their services and maintain strong relationships with clients.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields.
2.  Describe your role in the collaboration and the name of the agency you worked with.
3.  Input the name of the project you worked on and provide a brief duration of the project.
4.  Rate the overall satisfaction with the collaboration, communication quality, responsiveness, and deliverable quality.
5.  Provide specific strengths and areas for improvement regarding the collaboration.
6.  Describe how effectively the agency communicated throughout the project.
7.  Rate how well the agency handled challenges and problems.
8.  Indicate your likelihood to recommend the agency and your interest in future collaborations.
9.  Leave any additional comments about the collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluator Name** (`evaluator_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an evaluator.
* **Evaluator Email** (`evaluator_email`, email, required): Input your email address for contact purposes.
* **Evaluator Role** (`evaluator_role`, text, required): Describe your role in the collaboration, such as "Client", "Project Manager", or "Designer".
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the agency you worked with.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Input the name of the project you worked on.
* **Project Duration** (`project_duration`, text, required): Provide a brief duration of the project.
* **Overall Satisfaction Rating** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the collaboration on a scale from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied".
* **Communication Quality Rating** (`communication_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the agency's communication quality on a scale from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Responsiveness Rating** (`responsiveness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the agency's responsiveness to requests and concerns on a scale from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Deliverable Quality Rating** (`deliverable_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the deliverables provided by the agency on a scale from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Deadline Adherence Rating** (`deadline_adherence_rating`, select_one, required): Rate how well the agency met agreed deadlines on a scale from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Collaboration Strengths** (`collaboration_strengths`, text, optional): Describe the strongest aspects of the collaboration.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Identify areas where the agency could improve.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the agency's communication effectiveness on a scale from "Very Effective" to "Very Ineffective".
* **Problem Resolution Ability** (`problem_resolution_ability`, select_one, required): Rate how well the agency handled challenges and problems on a scale from "Excellent" to "Poor".
* **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, select_one, required): Indicate your likelihood to recommend the agency to others on a scale from "Very Likely" to "Very Unlikely".
* **Future Collaboration Interest** (`future_collaboration_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in future collaborations with the agency on a scale from "Definitely Yes" to "Definitely No".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments about the collaboration.
