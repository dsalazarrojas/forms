# Session Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers about their experiences with events organized by our team. The information collected will help us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event organizer.
2. Enter the name of the event.
3. Select the date of the event.
4. Rate your experience from 1 to 5.
5. Provide a brief review of your experience.
6. Choose the category for your feedback (e.g., Feedback Forms, Counselor Forms, Other).
7. Write a description of your experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organizer Name** (`organizer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or organization that organized the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the event you attended.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required: false): Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Review** (`review`, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of your experience with the event.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the category that best describes your feedback (e.g., Feedback Forms, Counselor Forms, Other).
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Write a more detailed description of your experience with the event (optional).
