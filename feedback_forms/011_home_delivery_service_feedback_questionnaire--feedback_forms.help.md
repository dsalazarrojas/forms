# Home Delivery Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers about their experience with our home delivery service. It is meant to gather information on the areas of service and satisfaction levels experienced by customers. The feedback collected through this form will help us improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field according to the instructions provided for each field.
2. If you have a comment or feedback about your experience, please type it in the "Comments" field.
3. If you have any further information or contact details, please fill out the "Email" and "Phone" fields, if applicable.
4. Finally, review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Area** (`delivery_area`, text, optional): Please enter the city, state, or zip code of the area you received delivery service from us.
- **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_one, optional): Indicate your level of satisfaction with our service. Choose from the following options:
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Describe the reason for your feedback, e.g., what we did well or what we could improve.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you'd like to provide your email address for follow-up or future communication.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your phone number for follow-up or future communication.
- **Area of Service** (`area_of_service`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the areas of service you received from us, e.g., Grocery, Pharmacy, Dining, Pet supply, or Other (if applicable).

Note: The form is designed to be self-explanatory, but if you're unsure or have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
