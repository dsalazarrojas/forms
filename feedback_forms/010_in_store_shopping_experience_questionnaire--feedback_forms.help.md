# In Store Shopping Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Store Shopping Experience Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback from customers about their shopping experience in our store. This form helps us understand how we can improve our services and provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. Select the options that best fit your experience.
3. Fill in any text fields with your thoughts or comments.
4. Submit your feedback once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **In-store experience** (`page_one`, text, required): Please describe your experience when visiting our store.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, select_one, optional): Please select the type of customer you are:
	+ Customer A
	+ Customer B
	+ Customer C
* **Product info** (`product_info`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the products you purchased during your visit:
	+ Product A
	+ Product B
	+ Product C
* **Store employee** (`store_employee`, text, optional): Please describe your interaction with the store employee.
* **Store hours** (`store_hours`, time, optional): Please enter the time you visited the store.
* **What do you think of our store?** (`customer_feedback`, note, optional): Please share any thoughts or comments about your experience.
* **How satisfied are you?** (`rating`, select_one, optional): How satisfied are you with your experience:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
	+ Not at all Satisfied
* **Did the store employee help you?** (`store_employee_helped`, select_one, optional): 
	+ True

* **Store ambiance** (`store_ambiance`, select_one, optional): How would you rate the store ambiance?
	+ Good
