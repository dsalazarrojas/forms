# Training Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from trainees on the effectiveness of a training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session details: session ID, training program name, session date, start time, and end time.
2. Provide your name and contact information.
3. Rate the effectiveness of the training session using the rating scale.
4. Select whether you would recommend this training to others.
5. Add any additional comments about the training session.
6. Select the user who submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness_survey_form`): This is the main field where you can provide your overall rating of the training session.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`): Enter the unique ID of the training session.
* **Training Program Name** (`training_program_name`): Enter the name of the training program you attended.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`): Enter the start time of the training session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`): Enter the end time of the training session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Training Facilitator** (`training_facilitator`): Enter the facilitator's name.
* **Attendees Name** (`attendees_name`): Enter your name and any other attendees' names.
* **Attendees Contact Info** (`attendees_contact_info`): Enter any contact information of the attendees.
* **Training Effectiveness Rating** (`training_effectiveness_rating`): Rate the effectiveness of the training session on a scale of 1-5.
* **Would you recommend this training?** (`training_recommendation`): Select whether you would recommend this training to others. You can choose one or more of the following options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments about the training session.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Select the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
