# photography_tour_client_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **photography tour client survey** is designed to collect feedback from clients who have participated in a photography tour. This form helps us understand their experience, satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please fill in all the required fields.
2. Start by providing your **Survey Title** (optional but recommended for reference).
3. Enter your **Name** and **Email** in the respective fields.
4. Select your satisfaction level with the tour from the **Satisfaction Level** dropdown menu.
5. Describe the **Route** you took during the tour.
6. If you have any suggestions for improving the tour, enter them in the **Route Improvement** field.
7. Provide detailed instructions on how to navigate the **Tour Instructions**.
8. List the names of your **Tour Guides** who assisted you during the tour.
9. Rate the **Tour Value** by selecting a number from 1 to 5.
10. Choose your overall **Overall Satisfaction** with the tour from the **Satisfaction Level** dropdown menu.
11. Leave any additional comments in the **Comments** field (optional).
12. Enter the exact number of **Photos Taken** you took during the tour.
13. Select your experience with the tour from the **Tour Experience** dropdown menu.
14. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you would **Recommend** the tour.
15. Leave any **Additional Comments** about your experience (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (survey_title): Briefly describe the tour you participated in.
* **Name** (name): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Satisfaction Level** (satisfaction): Select how satisfied you were with the tour.
* **Route** (tour_route): Describe the route or path you took during the tour.
* **Route Improvement** (route_improvement): Suggest any improvements for future tours.
* **Tour Instructions** (tour_instructions): Provide clear instructions on how to navigate the tour.
* **Tour Guides** (tour_guides): List the names of your tour guides.
* **Tour Value** (tour_value): Rate the tour's value from 1 to 5.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (tour_satisfaction): Select your overall satisfaction with the tour.
* **Comments** (comments): Leave any additional comments about your experience.
* **Photos Taken** (photos_taken): Enter the exact number of photos taken during the tour.
* **Tour Experience** (tour_experience): Select your experience with the tour.
* **Would You Recommend?** (recommendation): Answer "Yes" or "No" if you would recommend the tour.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_info): Leave any additional comments about your experience.
