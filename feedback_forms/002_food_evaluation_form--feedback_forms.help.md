# Food Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Evaluation Form is a self-assessment tool used to gather feedback from customers about their experience with a food-related business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer each question truthfully to provide an accurate representation of your experience.
2. Choose the relevant options for each question, taking into account your genuine feelings and perceptions.
3. Provide detailed explanations for any open-ended questions to help us understand your thoughts and opinions.
4. Submit the form to share your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Food Evaluation Form** (`food_evaluation_form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form and the starting point for your feedback.
* **Food Rating** (`food_rating`, number, optional): Rate the quality of the food you ordered on a scale.
* **What did you think of the food quality?** (`food_quality`, text, optional): Describe your feelings about the food quality, providing details about taste, presentation, and overall impression.
* **Service Rating** (`service_rating`, text, optional): Rate the quality of the service you received, focusing on the friendliness, attentiveness, and efficiency of the staff.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, text, optional): Share your overall impression of your visit, including any memorable moments, disappointments, or concerns.
* **How was the price of your food?** (`food_price`, select_one, optional): Choose from Affordable, Expensive, or Average to describe the price of the food you ordered.
* **How was the price of the service?** (`service_price`, select_one, optional): Select from Affordable, Expensive, or Average to rate the price of the service you received.
* **How clean was the food environment?** (`food_environment`, text, optional): Describe the cleanliness and presentation of the food environment, including the dining area, kitchen, and restrooms.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Choose from Very Satisfied, Neutral, or Very Dissatisfied to rate your overall satisfaction with the food establishment.
* **How would you rate the food quality?** (`food_quality_rating`, select_one, optional): Select from Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, or Poor to rate the quality of the food you ordered.
* **How would you rate the service quality?** (`service_quality_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose from Excellent, Good, Average, Fair, or Poor to rate the quality of the service you received.
* **Will you come back to this restaurant?** (`will_revisit`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of True, False, or Undecided to indicate your likelihood of revisiting the restaurant.
* **Do you have any recommendations for us?** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or constructive feedback you have for the food establishment to improve its services.
