# Mental Health Stigma Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Stigma Poll is a form designed to collect feedback on mental health stigma topics. Your input will help us understand how mental health professionals, caregivers, and individuals perceive and address these issues, ultimately contributing to valuable insights that may inform future initiatives and programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Survey Questions" page and review the available options for each question. You can choose one or multiple options for each question.
2. Move to the "Stigma Topics" page, where you will be presented with a list of potential stigma topics. Select all that apply from the given options.
3. On the "Demographic Data" page, provide your demographic information, such as your age, profession, or other relevant details.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, you can provide them in the "Participant Comments" section.
5. Finally, review your responses and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions**: This page presents questions related to mental health stigma. Please review the options carefully and choose the ones that best apply to your experience or perspective.
* **Stigma Topics**: Select all the stigma topics that you believe are relevant to your feedback. This will help us better understand the scope of mental health stigma.
* **Demographic Data**: This page is for providing additional demographic information about yourself, such as your age, profession, or other relevant details.
* **Participant Information**: Please provide your email address and phone number, if applicable, for any potential follow-up communication or research coordination.
* **Participant Notes**: You can include any additional comments or concerns you may have in this section.
* **Participant Survey**: Select "Yes" if you would like to participate in further research related to mental health stigma.
* **Participant Comments**: This section is for providing any additional feedback or comments you may have regarding mental health stigma.
* **Comments**: You can include any general comments or feedback you may have about mental health stigma in this section.
