# Workstation Setup Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and information about the user's workstation setup. The purpose of this form is to collect details about the user's workstation, including workstation description, configuration, rating, and other relevant feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your user email address.
2.  Provide a short description of the workstation being evaluated.
3.  Answer the workstation configuration question.
4.  Answer the workstation setup question.
5.  Rate your workstation's performance, providing the date of your assessment.
6.  Rate your workhour and workday performance.
7.  Provide any additional comments about the workstation.
8.  Rate your workday performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email (Required)**: Please enter your email address associated with your user account.
* **Workstation Description (Required)**: Enter a brief description of the workstation being assessed.
* **Workstation Configuration (Required)**: Please choose all that apply from the given options.
* **Suggestions (Optional)**: Choose "Yes" if you have any suggestions for improving workstation performance. Choose "No" if you do not.
* **Comments (Optional)**: Add any additional comments or feedback about your workstation.
* **Rating (Required)**: Enter the date of your assessment using the correct date format.
* **Workhour Rating (Required)**: Rate your workhour performance, using the time format.
* **Workday Rating (Required)**: Enter a numeric rating for your workday performance.
* **Workday Comments (Optional)**: Add any additional comments about your workday performance.
* **Workstation Setup (Required)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you have or have not set up the workstation according to the guidelines.

## Tips
* Ensure to rate your workday performance on a scale.
* Please respond with 'Yes' or 'No' for 'Yes/No' questions.
