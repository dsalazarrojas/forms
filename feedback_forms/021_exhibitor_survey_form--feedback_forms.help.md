# exhibitor_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exhibitor_survey_form is a collection of questions and fields designed to gather feedback and information from exhibitors about their experiences and interactions with our event. This form is meant to help us understand their perspectives and make improvements for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Answer all required fields, if any.
3. Use your best judgment when filling out the form, and provide as much detail as possible.
4. Review and submit the form before completing it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event you attended.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **Comments**: Share any comments or feedback you have about the event.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the event (1-5 scale).
  * Type: number
  * Required: no
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
  * Type: email
  * Required: no
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
  * Type: text
  * Required: no
* **Date**: Enter the date you attended the event.
  * Type: date
  * Required: no
* **Time**: Enter the time you attended the event.
  * Type: time
  * Required: no
* **Notes**: Share any additional notes or comments you have about the event.
  * Type: note
  * Required: no
* **Questions**: Enter any questions or concerns you have about the event.
  * Type: note
  * Required: no

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* You can submit the form multiple times if you have different experiences or interactions with the event.
* We will keep your feedback confidential and use it to improve the event.
