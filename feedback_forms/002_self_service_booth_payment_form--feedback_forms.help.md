# Self Service Booth Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Service Booth Payment Form is used to collect information from users about their self-service booth payment. This form is intended to be completed by customers who have visited a self-service booth and would like to provide feedback or payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select the date of the purchase from the calendar in the "Purchase Date" field.
6.  Enter the amount of the purchase in the "Purchase Amount" field.
7.  Select one or more payment methods from the list in the "Payment Method" field.
8.  Enter any additional purchase details in the "Purchase Details" field.
9.  Enter any information related to the receipt in the "Receipt" field.
10. Select the rating for the self-service booth from the list in the "Review" field.
11. Enter a numerical rating for the self-service booth in the "Rating" field.
12. Enter any additional comments in the "Comment" field.
13. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form in the "Submitted by" field.
14. Select the time the form was submitted from the calendar in the "Submitted At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you about your form submission.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Purchase Date**: Select the date of the purchase from the calendar.
* **Purchase Amount**: Enter the amount of the purchase.
* **Payment Method**: Select one or more of the payment methods: Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, or Other.
* **Purchase Details**: Enter any additional information about the purchase.
* **Receipt**: Enter any information related to the receipt.
* **Review**: Select the rating for the self-service booth: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Rating**: Enter a numerical rating for the self-service booth.
* **Comment**: Enter any additional comments about the self-service booth.
* **Submitted by**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted At**: Select the time the form was submitted from the calendar.
