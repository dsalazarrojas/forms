# Customer Retention Strategy Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees on the company's customer retention strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps below to complete this form:

1. Select your role in the company and which departments you work with.
2. Provide your feedback on the customer retention strategy.
3. Enter your contact information if you'd like to be contacted.
4. Enter the date and time of your feedback.
5. Add any additional notes if you have any further comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Retention Strategy Feedback Questionnaire**: This field is the title of the form.
* **Customer Retention Strategy Feedback Questionnaire**: This is the page heading.
* **Customer Retention Strategy Feedback Questionnaire**: This is the page heading.
* **What is your role in the company?** (`customer_retention_strategy_feedback`): This field allows you to choose your role in the company. Please select the department you belong to from the list of options provided.
* **Which department do you work with?** (`customer_retention_strategy`): This field allows you to check which departments you collaborate with on a regular basis.
* **What is your feedback on our customer retention strategy?** (`customer_retention_strategy_feedback`): This field is where you can provide your thoughts on the customer retention strategy.
* **Email** (`email`): This field is for your email address. If you have any contact information, please enter it here.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This field is for your phone number. If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your number.
* **Date** (`date`): This field is for the date you're providing feedback. If you're giving feedback on a specific date, enter it here.
* **Time** (`time`): This field is for the time of your feedback. Choose the time you're providing feedback from the options provided.
* **Note** (`note`): This field is for any additional comments or notes you'd like to add to your feedback.
