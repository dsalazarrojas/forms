# Entertainment Platform Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users on our entertainment platform. It will help us understand your experience and satisfaction with the platform, identify areas for improvement, and gather specific information about your interactions with our platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Select all that apply**: In the "Feature Usage" and "Platform Use" sections, choose all the options that apply to your experience with our platform.
2. **Provide detailed feedback**: In the "Suggestions", "Comments", and "Comments2" sections, provide as much detail as possible about your experience and any suggestions for improvement.
3. **Provide accurate information**: In the "User Info", "Platform Name", "Platform URL", "Game URL", "Stream URL" fields, provide accurate and complete information.
4. **Rate your experience**: In the "Rating" section, rate your experience with our platform on a numerical scale.
5. **Choose the correct options**: In the "Product Use", "Platform Type", "Stream Type", "Game Type", and "Platform Category" sections, choose the options that best describe your experience.
