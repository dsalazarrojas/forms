# Graduation Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Clearance Form is used to record and manage the clearance status of students graduating from a program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure that you are filling out the form for a student who has graduated or is expected to graduate.
*   Enter the student's name in the field labeled "student name".
*   Enter the student's ID number in the field labeled "student id".
*   Enter the student's email address in the field labeled "student email".
*   Enter the student's phone number in the field labeled "student phone".
*   Enter the ID number of the course the student is graduating from in the field labeled "course id".
*   Enter the date of graduation in the field labeled "graduation date".
*   Select the clearance status of the student from the options provided in the field labeled "graduation status".
*   Provide any additional comments or notes about the student's graduation status in the field labeled "notes".
*   Finally, indicate who submitted the form in the field labeled "submitted by".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Graduation Clearance** (`graduation_clearance_form`): This field allows you to enter the graduation clearance status for the student.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the name of the student for whom you are recording graduation clearance status.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`): Enter the ID number of the student.
*   **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter the email address of the student.
*   **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): Enter the phone number of the student.
*   **Course ID** (`course_id`): Enter the ID number of the course the student is graduating from.
*   **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`): Enter the date of graduation.
*   **Graduation Status** (`graduation_status`): Select one of the provided options to indicate the graduation status.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional comments or notes about the student's graduation status.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Indicate who submitted the form.
