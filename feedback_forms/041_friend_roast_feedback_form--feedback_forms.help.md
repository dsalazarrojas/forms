# Friend Roast Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on the Friend Roast, a lighthearted activity where friends roast (good-naturedly) each other in a social setting. The form helps in evaluating the effectiveness and enjoyment of the roast.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the following sections with your personal information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
2. Select the event and date you attended:
	* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event where the roast took place.
	* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (if not already selected).
3. Rate the roast on different aspects:
	* **Humor Level**: Choose how funny you found the roast overall (Very Funny, Somewhat Funny, Not Funny).
	* **Humor Type**: Indicate the type of humor used during the roast (witty, sarcastic, ironic, deadpan, self-deprecating).
	* **Humor Tone**: Select the tone of the humor (constructive, deprecating, mocking, teasing, condescending).
	* **Humor Impact**: Choose how much the roast engaged or impacted you (impactful, engaging, entertaining, thought-provoking).
4. Identify the target of the roast:
	* **Humor Target**: Select who or what was being roasted (host, participant, both, neither).
5. Rate your mood during the roast:
	* **Humor Mood**: Choose your emotional state during the roast (happy, sad, neutral, confused, annoyed).
6. Provide numerical ratings for the roast:
	* **Humor Value**: Rate the overall value of the roast on a scale of 1-5.
	* **Humor Importance**: Rate how important you felt the roast was (very, somewhat, not at all).
7. Express your opinion on the roast:
	* **Would you like to repeat this activity?**: Choose if you would like to repeat the roast (would really like to have, would like to repeat, would not like to repeat).
	* **Would you suggest this activity?**: Enter any other suggestions or thoughts about the roast.
8. Finally, rate how much you liked the roast on a scale of 1-5:
	* **How much do you like this activity?**: Enter a number between 1 and 5 to express your like for the roast.
9. Add any other comments or suggestions about the roast.
