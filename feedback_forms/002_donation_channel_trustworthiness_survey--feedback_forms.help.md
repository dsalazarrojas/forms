# Donation Channel Trustworthiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to evaluate and gather feedback on different channels for donations, focusing on their trustworthiness, communication, and overall rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and provide accurate information about the donation channel in question.
2. Answer the questions according to the instructions provided with each field.
3. For fields marked as required, ensure you provide valid input to move forward.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Channel Information**: Please provide a brief description of the donation channel, including any relevant details.
* **Donation Channel Type** (`online`, `offline`, required): Select the type of donation channel you are assessing, either online or offline.
* **Channel Name** (`channel_name`, required): Enter the name of the donation channel.
* **Channel URL** (`channel_url`, optional): If the donation channel has a website, provide the URL. If not, you can leave this field empty.
* **Channel Description** (`channel_description`, optional): Provide a brief description of the donation channel, if available.
* **Trustworthiness** (`trustworthiness`, required): Assess the trustworthiness of the donation channel using the scale Very Low, Low, Medium, or High.
* **Communication** (`communication`, required): Choose the level of communication you experienced with the donation channel using the scale Not Communicative, Somewhat Communicative, or Highly Communicative.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback`, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about the donation channel, you can provide them here.
* **Rating** (`rating`, required): Assign a rating from 1-5 to the donation channel, reflecting your overall assessment.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, optional): If available, provide any contact information related to the donation channel.
* **Follow-up Information** (`follow_up`, optional): If any follow-up questions or details are relevant, you can provide them here.
