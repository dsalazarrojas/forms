# Energy Solutions Feedback Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from individuals and organizations about their experience with energy solutions. It is intended to gather their thoughts, suggestions, and concerns to help us improve and refine our energy solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the name of your company or organization in the "Company" field.
5. Choose how you would like to share your feedback:
	* "Yes, with attribution" to allow us to share your name and email with the public.
	* "Yes, without attribution" to share your feedback anonymously.
	* "No" to keep your feedback private.
6. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can provide them in the "Comments" field.
7. Finally, indicate how you would like your comments to be reused:
	* "For promotional purposes" if you want us to use your comment for marketing or promotional activities.
	* "For informational purposes" if you want us to use your comment for internal improvement.
8. If you have any further comments or suggestions, you can provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to provide a complete name identification.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address to allow us to contact you regarding your feedback.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company or organization to provide context to your feedback.
* **Permission to Share**: Choose how you would like to share your feedback:
	+ "Yes, with attribution" to allow us to share your name and email with the public.
	+ "Yes, without attribution" to share your feedback anonymously.
	+ "No" to keep your feedback private.
* **Comments**: If you have any comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **Reuse Comments**: Indicate how you would like your comments to be reused:
	+ "For promotional purposes" if you want us to use your comment for marketing or promotional activities.
	+ "For informational purposes" if you want us to use your comment for internal improvement.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any further comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Please be honest and provide constructive feedback to help us improve our energy solutions.
* Keep your messages clear and concise to facilitate our understanding of your comments.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* You can use this form multiple times if you have new comments or suggestions.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation.
