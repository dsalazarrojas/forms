# Teacher Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Training Feedback Questionnaire is a survey designed to collect feedback from teachers on their experiences with a training session. This form helps us understand their overall satisfaction, rating, and areas for improvement. Your input is crucial in helping us to refine and improve our training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question to the best of your abilities.
2. Use the provided scale for rating and select options for questions that require them.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, but don't worry if you're unsure or need help. If you need assistance, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false)
The introduction page explains the purpose and scope of the questionnaire.

### 2. **Overall session satisfaction** (Overall session satisfaction, `number`, required: false)
Please rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale of 1-5.
