# Digital Collaboration Tool Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the Digital Collaboration Tool, a software application used for team collaboration and productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the Digital Tool in the **Digital Tool Name** field.
2. Provide a brief description of what the tool is for in the **What is the tool for** field.
3. If the tool belongs to a specific category, select it from the **Which category does the tool belong** dropdown menu. If not applicable, leave blank.
4. If you'd like to provide additional context or categorization, enter the subcategory in the **Subcategory** field.
5. Rate your experience with the tool by providing a numerical score (0-5) in the **Rate the tool** field.
6. Score the tool's performance out of 5, providing a numerical score in the **Score the tool** field.
7. If you have any comments or suggestions for the tool, enter them in the **Tool Comments** field.
8. If you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion, enter your **Email** address.
9. If you'd like to be reachable for further contact, enter your **Phone Number**.
10. Finally, click the **Submit** button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Digital Tool Name**: Enter the name of the Digital Collaboration Tool you are providing feedback on.
* **What is the tool for**: Provide a brief description of what the tool is used for.
* **Which category does the tool belong**: Select the category that best describes the tool's purpose.
* **Subcategory**: If you'd like to provide additional context or categorization, enter the subcategory.
* **Rate the tool**: Rate your experience with the tool on a scale of 0-5.
* **Score the tool**: Score the tool's performance out of 5.
* **Tool Comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions you have for the tool.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback or discussion.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you'd like to be reachable for further contact.
* **Submit**: Click this button to send your feedback.

## Tips
* Please provide honest and constructive feedback.
* Be specific about your experience and suggestions.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, contact [Your Support Team].
