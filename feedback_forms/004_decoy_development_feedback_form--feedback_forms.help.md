# Decoy Development Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide feedback on Decoy Development's products or services. Your input will help us improve our offerings and ensure we meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Click on the form and select the relevant information for each field.
* Fill out all required fields (if any).
* Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Feedback Title** (`feedback_title`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief title or summary of your feedback.
* **Rating** (`rating`, Select, Optional): Choose a rating from the options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Share any detailed comments or suggestions you have.
* **Rating Comment** (`rating_comment`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more comments that apply to your rating:
	+ Positive
	+ Negative
	+ Neutral
* **Image** (`image`, Note, Optional): If you'd like to attach an image to your feedback, use this field.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you (if needed).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible with your feedback.
* If you have any attachments, make sure they are relevant to your feedback.
* If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to ask, and we'll get back to you soon.
