# feedback_form_electrode_assembly_automation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "feedback_form_electrode_assembly_automation" form is designed to collect feedback on issues or improvements related to the electrode assembly automation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To provide accurate and relevant feedback, follow these steps:

1. Complete each field according to the provided instructions below.
2. Ensure you've experienced a specific incident related to the electrode assembly automation process before submitting feedback.
3. Do not submit the same issue multiple times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the issue or improvement you'd like to report.
* **Feedback Type** (`feedback_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Error**: Report a bug or incorrect behavior.
	+ **Suggestion**: Suggest an improvement or feature idea.
	+ **Question**: Ask a question or request clarification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can get in touch with you for further discussion or follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you experienced the issue or would like to propose a change.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you experienced the issue or would like to propose a change.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional context or information about your feedback.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Enter your detailed feedback on the issue or improvement.
