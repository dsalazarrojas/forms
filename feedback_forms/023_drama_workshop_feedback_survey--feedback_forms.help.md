# Drama Workshop Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience at the Drama Workshop, providing valuable insights to help us improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Rate your overall experience**:
    a.  Answer the question by selecting a number (1-5) that best describes your experience.
2.  **Provide your suggestions**:
    a.  Write down any ideas or suggestions you have to make the workshop better.
3.  **Identify areas for improvement**:
    a.  Describe any areas you think need improvement.
4.  **Evaluate workshop content and organization**:
    a.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' based on whether you found the content and organization effective.
5.  **Assess instructor performance**:
    a.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' based on whether you found the instructor performance satisfactory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Rating
*   **Participant Rating** (Optional): This field asks you to rate your overall experience as a participant at the workshop.
    *   Please rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.

### Overall Experience
*   **Overall Experience** (Optional): Share your thoughts and impressions about your overall experience at the workshop.
    *   Be as detailed and specific as you can.

### Suggestions
*   **Suggestions** (Optional): If you have any ideas or suggestions, share them here.
    *   This will help us improve future sessions.

### Areas For Improvement
*   **Areas For Improvement** (Optional): If there are any areas you think need improvement, list them here.
    *   Your input will help us make future sessions better.

### Workshop Content
*   **Workshop Content** (Optional): Indicate whether you found the content effective.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you found the content engaging and useful; 'No' otherwise.

### Workshop Organization
*   **Workshop Organization** (Optional): Indicate whether the workshop organization was effective.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you found the organization of the workshop effective; 'No' otherwise.

### Instructor Performance
*   **Instructor Performance** (Optional): Indicate the instructor's performance.
    *   Select 'Yes' if you found the instructor's performance satisfactory; 'No' otherwise.
