# Babysitting Service Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your feedback on our babysitting service. This will help us understand how we're doing and identify areas for improvement. Your input is valuable in helping us create a better experience for future clients and families.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in the following order:
   * Parent name: This field is optional and can be filled if you wish to provide your name for our records.
   * Date of service: This is a required field where you will enter the date you used our service.
   * Sitter name: This field is optional and can be filled if you know who took care of your child.
   * Overall rating: Choose one rating from 1 (low) to 5 (high) to describe your overall experience with our service.
   * Punctuality rating: Select one of four options to rate our sitter's punctuality (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
   * Child happiness during care: Rate how well your child adjusted to the sitter (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
   * Communication with family: Rate the clarity of communication with our sitter (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
   * Would you recommend this service: Choose yes or no, indicating if you would recommend our service to other families.
   * Suggestions for improvement: Share any ideas or thoughts on how we can improve our service.
   * Additional comments: Provide any other comments or thoughts that may be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent name**: (Optional) Your name for our records.
* **Date of service**: When you used our service (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Sitter name**: (Optional) If known, the name of the sitter who cared for your child.
* **Overall rating**: Your overall rating of our service from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Punctuality rating**: Rate our sitter's timeliness (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
* **Child happiness during care**: Rate how well your child adjusted to the sitter (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
* **Communication with family**: Rate the clarity of communication with our sitter (Poor, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
* **Would you recommend this service**: Indicate if you would recommend our service to other families (True or False).
* **Suggestions for improvement**: Share any ideas on how we can improve our service.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any other comments or thoughts that may be helpful for us to know.
