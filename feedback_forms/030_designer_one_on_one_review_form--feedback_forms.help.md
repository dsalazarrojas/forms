# Designer One On One Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Designer One On One Review Form is designed to collect feedback and critiques from customers about their design experience with our company. This form helps us understand what we're doing well and what we can improve on to provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields. The required fields are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and concisely.
3. Use the space provided to give detailed feedback.
4. Submit the form once you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Critique** (`schedule_critique`, `text`, required):
	+ This is the main feedback field where you can tell us about your schedule experience with our designer.
	+ Be as detailed as possible, but keep it concise.
* **Design Feedback** (`design_feedback`, `text`, optional):
	+ This is an open-ended field where you can share any additional feedback or thoughts about the design process.
	+ You can use this field to elaborate on your schedule critique or share any new ideas that didn't fit in the previous field.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `text`, optional):
	+ This field is meant for any follow-up questions or comments you might have after using our designer service.
	+ Keep it brief, but be sure to include any important details or requests for further clarification.
