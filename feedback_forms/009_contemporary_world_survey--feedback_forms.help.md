# Contemporary World Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Contemporary World Survey is a tool designed to collect information about various aspects of global issues, including respondent demographics, attitudes, and opinions. The survey aims to gather data for research and analysis purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Respondent ID** (optional). This is a unique identifier for you as a respondent.
2.  Select your **Survey Date**. This is the date when you completed the survey.
3.  Choose your **Age Group** from the provided options.
4.  Select your **Gender** identity from the provided options.
5.  Choose your **Country of Residence** from the provided options.
6.  Select your **Education Level** from the provided options.
7.  Choose your **Employment Status** from the provided options.
8.  Select the **Most Concerning Global Issue** that affects you the most.
9.  Indicate the **Importance of Addressing Climate Change**.
10.  Rate the **Effectiveness of International Cooperation on Global Issues**.
11.  Select the **Overall Impact of Technology on Society**.
12.  Choose your **View on Globalization**.
13.  Select the **Importance of Cultural Diversity**.
14.  Rate your **Trust in Mainstream Media**.
15.  Rate your **Trust in Your National Government**.
16.  Express your **Future Optimism**.
17.  Indicate your **Willingness to Take Personal Action on Global Issues**.
18.  Enter any **Additional Comments** on global issues that are not covered in this survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent ID** (text, optional): A unique identifier for you as a respondent.
*   **Survey Date** (date, required): The date when you completed the survey.
*   **Age Group** (select_one, required): Select your age range from the provided options.
*   **Gender** (select_one, required): Select your gender identity from the provided options.
*   **Country of Residence** (select_one, required): Choose the country where you currently live.
*   **Education Level** (select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
*   **Employment Status** (select_one, required): Choose your current employment situation.
*   **Most Concerning Global Issue** (select_one, required): Select the global issue that concerns you the most.
*   **Importance of Addressing Climate Change** (select_one, required): Indicate how important addressing climate change is to you.
*   **Effectiveness of International Cooperation on Global Issues** (select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of international cooperation on global issues.
*   **Overall Impact of Technology on Society** (select_one, required): Select the overall impact of technology on society.
*   **View on Globalization** (select_one, required): Choose your view on globalization.
*   **Importance of Cultural Diversity** (select_one, required): Select the importance of cultural diversity.
*   **Trust in Mainstream Media** (select_one, required): Rate your trust in mainstream media.
*   **Trust in Your National Government** (select_one, required): Rate your trust in your national government.
*   **Future Optimism** (select_one, required): Express your future optimism.
*   **Willingness to Take Personal Action on Global Issues** (select_one, required): Indicate your willingness to take personal action on global issues.
*   **Additional Comments** (text, optional): Enter any other thoughts on contemporary world issues.

## Tips

*   Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
*   You can select only one option for each field.
*   If you have any additional comments, please use the text field to express your thoughts.
