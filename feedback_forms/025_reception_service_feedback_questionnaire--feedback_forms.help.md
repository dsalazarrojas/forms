# Reception Service Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Reception Service Feedback Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback from users about their experience with our Reception Service. The purpose of this form is to gather information on how our service meets your expectations, identify areas for improvement, and understand how you rate your overall satisfaction with our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the correct answers for each question based on your genuine experience with our Reception Service. Please fill out as much or as little of the form as you deem necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **How would you rate our Reception Service?** (reception_service_rating, number, required: false)
This question asks for your subjective rating of our Reception Service, on a scale of 1 (Very Satisfied) to 3 (Not Satisfied). Choose the option that best represents your experience.

### **Do you have any suggestions or complaints about our Reception Service?** (reception_service_suggestions, text, required: true)
Please share any comments or suggestions you have regarding our Reception Service. This will help us identify areas where we can improve.

### **How satisfied are you with our Reception Service?** (reception_service_satisfaction, select_one, required: false)
Rate your overall satisfaction with our Reception Service as Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.

### **How many times have you used our Reception Service?** (reception_service_frequent_use, select_multiple, required: false)
Choose how many times you have used our Reception Service: Once, Multiple Times, or Never.

### **Is your Reception Service one of your favorite services?** (reception_service_favourite, select_one, required: false)
Select True if our Reception Service is one of your favorites; False otherwise.

### **How can we improve our Reception Service?** (reception_service_improve, text, required: true)
Please suggest ways we can improve our Reception Service.

### **Do you have any additional comments about our Reception Service?** (reception_service_comment, text, required: false)
Share any additional comments or thoughts about your experience with our Reception Service.
