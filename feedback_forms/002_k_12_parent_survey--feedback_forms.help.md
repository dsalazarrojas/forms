# K 12 Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The K 12 Parent Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from parents of high school students. The survey will be used to understand their experiences, suggestions, and comments about their child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your parent name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Parent Email" field. Please note that this field is optional and only required if you want to receive a confirmation email.
3. If you would like to include a teacher's name in your feedback, enter it in the "Teacher Name" field.
4. If you would like to specify the class your child is in, enter the class name in the "Class Name" field.
5. Enter the date you are providing feedback in the "Feedback Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name (text)**: Enter your name as a parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Email (email)**: Enter your email address if you want to receive a confirmation email.
* **Teacher Name (text)**: If applicable, enter the name of the teacher who taught your child.
* **Class Name (text)**: If applicable, enter the class name your child is in.
* **Feedback Date (date)**: Enter the date you are providing feedback.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you've already filled out this form, do not submit it again.
* If you need assistance or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
