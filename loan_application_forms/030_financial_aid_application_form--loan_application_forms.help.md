# Financial Aid Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for financial aid as a student. It gathers information about your personal details, academic status, and financial circumstances to help determine your eligibility for loans and other forms of financial assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in each field with the requested information. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose. Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that your application is complete and processed correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form** (text): Please provide a brief description of your personal application.
* **Name** (text): Enter your full name as it should appear on your official documents.
* **Address** (text): Enter your current address, including your street address, apartment number, city, state, and zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (text): Enter your social security number or your student ID number (if applicable).
* **Income** (text): Enter your annual income from all sources, including but not limited to, employment, self-employment, and investments.
* **Loan Type** (select one): Choose the type of loan you are applying for, such as Federal Pell Grant, Direct Loans, or Private Loans.
* **Loan Amount** (number): Enter the amount of loan you are applying for.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (date): Enter the expected date of your graduation.
* **Current Enrollment Status** (select multiple): Choose your current enrollment status, such as Full Time or Part Time.
* **School Name** (text): Enter the name of your school or institution you are currently enrolled in.
* **Student ID** (text): Enter your student ID number or your national student ID number (if applicable).
* **Email** (email): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional comments or information about your application.

Note: This form is intended to be used by students seeking financial aid. If you are not a student, please do not use this form. Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your application is complete and processed correctly.
