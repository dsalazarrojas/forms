# Digital Lending Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to apply for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information (User Information page)
2. Provide details about your loan application (Loan Application Details page)
3. Enter your loan amount, interest rate, and loan term (Loan Amount, Interest Rate, and Loan Term pages)
4. Enter your employment status, type, and income (Employment Status, Employment Type, and Income pages)
5. Provide your contact details (Contact Details page)
6. Sign the form digitally (Signature page)
7. Check the terms and conditions (Terms page)
8. Confirm your consent (Consent page)
9. Finally, submit your form and the date submitted will be automatically generated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This page is used to collect your basic information such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Loan Application Details**: This page is used to collect details about your loan application, including the purpose of the loan and any other relevant information.
* **Loan Amount**: This field is used to enter the amount you wish to borrow.
* **Interest Rate**: This field is used to enter the interest rate for your loan (if applicable).
* **Loan Term**: This field is used to select the term of your loan (in months).
* **Credit Score**: This field is used to enter your credit score.
* **Employment Status**: This field is used to select your current employment status (Employed, Self-Employed, Student, or Retired).
* **Employment Type**: This field is used to select your employment type (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Other).
* **Income**: This field is used to enter your income.
* **Employment Income**: This field is used to enter your employment income.
* **Other Income**: This field is used to enter any other income sources.
* **Date of Birth**: This field is used to enter your date of birth.
* **Contact Details**: This page is used to enter your contact details such as name, email, and phone number.
* **First Name**: This field is used to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field is used to enter your last name.
* **Email**: This field is used to enter your email address.
* **Phone**: This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **Address**: This field is used to enter your address.
* **City**: This field is used to enter your city.
* **State**: This field is used to enter your state.
* **Zipcode**: This field is used to enter your zip code.
* **Signature**: This field is used to sign this form digitally.
* **Terms**: This field is used to review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Consent**: This field is used to confirm your consent to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit**: This field is used to submit your form.
* **Date Submitted**: This field will be automatically generated once you submit your form.
