# New Loan Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Loan Submission Form is used to submit loan applications, providing essential details for the loan process. It gathers information about the applicant, loan terms, and other relevant data to facilitate a thorough assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields, indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. Select relevant options for each field, if applicable.
3. Ensure accuracy and completeness of all information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Submissions** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is not directly relevant to the form, likely a leftover or placeholder.
* **Applicant Info** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the applicant, including their relevant details.
* **Loan Amount** (`3`, `number`, required: false): Enter the loan amount requested.
* **Loan Term** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the loan term is applicable.
* **Collateral Amount** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the collateral amount.
* **Annual Interest Rate** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the annual interest rate.
* **Monthly Interest Rate** (`7`, `number`, required: false): Enter the monthly interest rate.
* **Loan Status** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the loan status.
* **Loan Type** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the loan type.
* **Collateral Type** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the collateral type.
* **Collateral Details** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the collateral.
* **Loan Purpose** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Describe the purpose of the loan.
* **Credit Score** (`13`, `number`, required: false): Enter the credit score.
* **Annual Income** (`14`, `number`, required: false): Enter the annual income.
* **Employment Status** (`15`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the employment status.
* **Employment Type** (`16`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the employment type.
* **Employment Length** (`17`, `number`, required: false): Enter the employment length.
* **Annual Salary** (`18`, `number`, required: false): Enter the annual salary.
* **Loan Application Date** (`19`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the loan application.
* **Employment Institution** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Provide the name of the employment institution.
* **Loan Application Form Type** (`21`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the loan application form type.
* **Applicant Name** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Enter the applicant's name.
* **Applicant Email** (`23`, `email`, required: false): Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Applicant Phone** (`24`, `text`, required: false): Enter the applicant's phone number.
* **Applicant Notes** (`25`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional notes about the applicant.
