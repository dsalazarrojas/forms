# Car Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Loan Application Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals or businesses applying for a car loan. The form is divided into sections to make it easy to gather all necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, and social security number.
2. Provide your employment details, such as your current employer, job title, and monthly gross income.
3. Specify your loan requirements, including the loan amount, desired loan term, and down payment amount.
4. If applicable, provide information about the vehicle you intend to buy, including the make and model, and year of manufacture.
5. Verify your loan application by acknowledging the terms and conditions, and sign the application with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name_loan`, text, **Required**): Enter your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`applicant_dob_loan`, date, **Required**): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Social Security Number** (`applicant_ssn`, text, **Required**): Enter your social security number (10 digits).
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email_loan`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address for application status updates.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone_loan`, text, **Required**): Enter your primary contact number for the application.
* **Current Employer** (`current_employer`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of your current company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, **Required**): Enter your current position or title.
* **Monthly Gross Income** (`monthly_gross_income`, number, **Required**): Enter your total income before taxes.
* **Requested Loan Amount** (`loan_amount_requested`, number, **Required**): Enter the total amount to be financed.
* **Desired Loan Term** (`desired_loan_term`, select_one, **Required**): Select the length of the loan (36, 48, 60, or 72 months).
* **Down Payment Amount** (`down_payment_amount_loan`, number, **Required**): Enter the amount you will pay upfront.
* **Car Make and Model** (`vehicle_make_model_loan`, text, **Optional**): If applicable, describe the car you intend to buy.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year_loan`, number, **Optional**): If applicable, enter the year of manufacture.
* **Acknowledgement of Loan Application Terms** (`loan_application_acknowledgement`, select_one, **Required**): Verify that you have read and agree to the loan application terms and conditions.
* **Signature for Loan Application** (`signature_confirmation_loan`, text, **Required**): Type your name for loan application confirmation.
* **Additional Comments or Requests** (`additional_loan_comments`, text, **Optional**): Provide any additional details or requests for the application.
