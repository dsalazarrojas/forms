# Consumer Loan Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consumer Loan Application form is designed to gather information from applicants to assess their creditworthiness and determine the eligibility for a loan. This form is used to collect data on the applicant's personal details, employment, income, debt, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address** on page 1.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** and **Date of Birth** on page 1.
3. Provide your **Social Security Number** on page 1.
4. Enter your **Street Address**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code** on page 2.
5. Select your **Residence Type** from the options on page 2.
6. Enter your **Monthly Housing Payment** and **Monthly Expenses** on page 2.
7. Select your **Employment Status** and **Length of Employment** (if applicable) on page 3.
8. Enter your **Annual Income** and **Additional Income** (if applicable) on page 3.
9. Select your **Loan Amount Requested** and **Loan Purpose** on page 4.
10. Choose your **Preferred Loan Term** on page 4.
11. Answer questions about your **Checking Account**, **Savings Account**, **Existing Loans**, **Total Existing Debt**, and **Bankruptcy History** on page 4.
12. Sign with your **Authorization Signature** on page 5.
13. Date the **Application Date** on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: 
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your SSN for credit check purposes.

### Page 2: 
* **Street Address**: Enter your current residence address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State**: Enter the state of your residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Residence Type**: Select your housing situation from the options provided.
* **Monthly Housing Payment**: Enter the rent or mortgage amount.
* **Monthly Expenses**: Enter the sum of all your monthly obligations.

### Page 3: 
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status.
* **Length of Employment**: If employed, select the length of time at your current job.
* **Annual Income**: Enter your gross annual income.
* **Additional Income**: If applicable, enter other sources of income.

### Page 4: 
* **Loan Amount Requested**: Enter the amount you wish to borrow.
* **Loan Purpose**: Select the reason for the loan from the options provided.
* **Preferred Loan Term**: Select the desired repayment period.
* **Checking Account**: Select if you have a checking account.
* **Savings Account**: Select if you have a savings account.
* **Existing Loans**: Select if you have current loans.
* **Total Existing Debt**: If you have existing loans, enter the total amount of debt.
* **Bankruptcy History**: Select if you have filed for bankruptcy.
* **Have You Filed for Bankruptcy**: Select if you have filed for bankruptcy.

### Page 5: 
* **Authorization Signature**: Type your full name as a signature.
* **Application Date**: Date the application submission.

Note: Some fields are optional and will not affect the loan evaluation process, but are still required for completion of the form.
