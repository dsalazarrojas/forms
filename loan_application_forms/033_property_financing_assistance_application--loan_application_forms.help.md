# Property Financing Assistance Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from end-users seeking financing assistance for a property. Fill out this form to provide details about your property and financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of property you are applying for financing.
2. Choose the purpose of the loan (e.g., Cash-Out, Home Equity, etc.).
3. Enter the desired loan amount.
4. Provide your credit score.
5. Enter your income.
6. Select your employment status.
7. Enter your property address.
8. Enter your contact information.
9. If necessary, add any additional notes about your financing request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Type** (`Property Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of property you are applying for financing. Options include Single Family, Condo, Townhome, Duplex, Triplex, Fourplex, and Other.
* **Loan Purpose** (`Loan Purpose`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the purpose of the loan you are seeking. Options include Cash-Out, Home Equity, Home Improvement, Consolidate, Purchase, and Re-finance.
* **Loan Amount** (`Loan Amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount you are seeking for financing.
* **Credit Score** (`Credit Score`, `number`, required: false): Provide your current credit score.
* **Income** (`Income`, `number`, required: false): Enter your annual income.
* **Employment Status** (`Employment Status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your current employment status. Options include Employed, Unemployed, Self-Employed, Retired, and Disabled.
* **Property Address** (`Property Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the property you are applying for financing.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `email`, required: true): Enter your contact information (email address).
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about your financing request.
* **Property Financing Request** (`Property Financing Request`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your financing request.
