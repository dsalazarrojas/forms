# Dental Loan Eligibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your eligibility for a loan to cover dental services. Please fill out the form below to provide the necessary information about yourself and your loan request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, address, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your patient information, including your date of birth and any relevant medical history.
3. Enter details about the loan you are requesting, including the amount and purpose of the loan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Info (contact_info)
* **Contact Info (contact_info**, text, optional):** Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address to help us contact you about your loan application.

### Patient Info (patient_info)
* **Patient Info (patient_info, text, optional):** Please provide any relevant medical information about yourself, such as your date of birth and any prior medical conditions that may impact your eligibility for a loan.

### Loan Info (loan_info)
* **Loan Info (loan_info, text, optional):** Enter the details about the loan you are seeking, including the amount of the loan and the specific dental services you are looking to cover with the loan.
