# Mortgage Loan Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mortgage loan application form is designed to collect necessary information for a mortgage loan application. This information will be used to assess loan eligibility and facilitate the loan process.

## How To Complete This Form

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Status** (id: 11): This field is used to determine your employment status. Select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **Income Details** (id: 12): This field is used to collect information about your income. Select all applicable income sources from the options provided.
* **Type of Property** (id: 13): This field is used to determine the type of property you are applying for a loan on. Select the type of property from the options provided.
* **Property Value** (id: 14): Enter the value of the property you are applying for a loan on.
* **Loan Purpose** (id: 15): This field is used to determine the purpose of the loan. Select the loan purpose from the options provided.
* **Loan Status** (id: 16): This field is used to indicate the current status of your loan application. Select from the options provided.
* **Date of Application** (id: 22): This field is used to record the date and time you submitted your loan application.
* **Email Address** (id: 25): Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted about your loan application.
