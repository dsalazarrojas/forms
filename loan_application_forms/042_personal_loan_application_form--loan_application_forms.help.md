# Personal Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Loan Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for personal loans. This form is meant to be filled out by individuals seeking a personal loan.

## How To Complete This Form
- First, review the form carefully and understand what each field requires.
- Fill out the fields with accurate and complete information.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Once you have completed all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Required): Please fill out your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID. 
* **Last Name** (Required): Fill out your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Required): Enter your current address where you currently reside.
* **Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter a valid phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Loan Amount** (Required): Enter the loan amount you are seeking.
* **Loan Term** (Required): Enter the loan term in years.
* **Loan Interest Rate** (Required): Enter the loan interest rate as a decimal (e.g., 5.5).
* **Select Loan Purpose** (Required): Select the reason for taking the loan from the options provided.
* **Other Loan Purpose** (Optional): If you select "Other" as the loan purpose, please fill out the other field below.
* **Other Loan Purpose** (Optional): Enter a description of your other loan purpose.
* **Accept Terms** (Required): Accept the terms and conditions of the loan.
* **Submit** (Required): Click this button to submit your application.
* **Note** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your loan application.
