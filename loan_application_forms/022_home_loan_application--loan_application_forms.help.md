# home_loan_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants for a home loan application. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant details about the applicant's identity, employment status, income, and other necessary information to process the loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide a valid Email address.
3. Enter your Phone Number.
4. Enter your Social Security Number (if required).
5. Enter your Income.
6. Enter the Loan Amount you are applying for.
7. Enter the Interest Rate for the loan (if required).
8. Enter the Purpose of the loan.
9. Enter your Date of Birth.
10. Enter the Time of Application (if required).
11. Select your Employment Status (Active, Part-time, Retired, or Unemployed).
12. Enter the length of your Employment.
13. Enter your Property Address.
14. Enter the State of your Property.
15. (Optional) Enter the employment length of your Co-signer (if applicable).
16. (Optional) Check if you have a Co-signer.
17. (Optional) Enter the Co-signer's Information.
18. (Optional) Select the relationship between you and the Co-signer.
19. (Optional) Enter the Co-signer's Address.
20. (Optional) Select the Co-signer's Employment Status.
21. (Optional) Enter the Co-signer's Income.
22. (Optional) Enter your Credit Score.
23. (Optional) Enter any additional Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your first name in the format used on official documents.
* **Last Name (2)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your last name in the format used on official documents.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to which we can send notifications and further instructions.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Social Security Number (5)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your social security number for verification purposes (if required).
* **Income (6)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter your income in a numerical format (e.g., $50,000 per year).
* **Loan Amount (7)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of the loan you are applying for.
* **Interest Rate (8)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the interest rate for the loan (if required).
* **Purpose (9)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the purpose of the loan (e.g., to buy a house).
* **Date of Birth (10)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Application (11)** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you are applying for the loan.
* **Employment Status (12)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select your current employment status (Active, Part-time, Retired, or Unemployed).
* **Employment Length (13)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the length of your employment (in years).
* **Property Address (14)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your property address.
* **Property State (15)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select the state where your property is located.
* **Employment Length 2 (16)** (`text`, required/optional): (Optional) Enter the employment length of your Co-signer (if applicable).
* **Co-signer Information (17)** (`text`, required/optional): (Optional) Enter any co-signer information.
* **Co-signer Relationship (18)** (`select_one`, required/optional): (Optional) Select the relationship between you and the co-signer (Yes or No).
* **Co-signer Address (19)** (`text`, required/optional): (Optional) Enter the co-signer's address.
* **Co-signer Employment Status (20)** (`select_one`, required/optional): (Optional) Select the co-signer's employment status (Active, Inactive).
* **Co-signer Income (21)** (`text`, required/optional): (Optional) Enter the co-signer's income.
* **Credit Score (22)** (`text`, required/optional): (Optional) Enter your credit score.
* **Notes (23)** (`text`, required/optional): (Optional) Enter any additional notes.
* **Submit (24)** (`text`, required/optional): (Optional) Click this button to submit your application.
