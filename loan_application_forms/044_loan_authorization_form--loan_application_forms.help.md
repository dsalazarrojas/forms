# Loan Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and authorization for a loan. It includes user information, authorized parties, and details about the loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Complete the user information section with your name, phone number, and email.
2.  Select the authorized parties that will be involved in the loan.
3.  Choose the authorization type and level for the loan.
4.  Enter the authorization date for the loan.
5.  Add any additional notes about the loan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): This section is used to enter your personal information such as your name, phone number, and email.
* **Authorized Parties** (`authorized_parties`, select multiple, required: false): Select all the parties that will be involved in the loan. You can choose from multiple options such as Party A, Party B, and Party C.
* **Authorization Details** (`authorization_details`, select one, required: false): Choose the details of the authorization such as Option A, Option B, or Option C.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the authorization will occur.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the loan.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`, select one, required: false): Choose the type of loan such as Option A, Option B, or Option C.
* **Authorization Level** (`authorization_level`, select one, required: false): Choose the level of authorization such as Option A, Option B, or Option C.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the loan.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email, required: false): Enter your secondary email address.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, required: false): Enter your secondary phone number.
