## Cash Advance Request Form - Help Guide

### Purpose

This form is used to request a cash advance from your employer. A cash advance is an amount of money that you can borrow from your employer, usually to cover unexpected expenses or emergencies. This form will guide you through the process of requesting a cash advance, ensuring that all necessary information is provided to ensure a fair evaluation of your request.

### How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, employee ID, department, and job title.
2. Provide your employment information, including your hire date, employment status, and gross monthly salary.
3. Specify the reason for your request and the amount you are seeking.
4. Choose how you would like to repay the advance, including the frequency and number of deductions.
5. Attach any supporting documents that demonstrate your need for a cash advance.
6. Specify the deadline for the repayment and the date you expect to receive the advance.
7. Confirm that you understand the terms and conditions of the advance and that all information provided is accurate.
8. Sign the form to confirm your request.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request ID Number**: A reference number to track your request. You can choose any unique identifier for your request.
* **Request Date**: The date you are requesting the cash advance. This will help the payroll department to understand the urgency of your request.
* **Employee Information**: A brief description of your employment details, such as job title and department.
* **Full Name**: Your legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Employee ID Number**: Your unique employee number assigned by the HR department.
* **Department**: Your department or team you belong to.
* **Job Title**: Your job title or position within the company.
* **Hire Date**: The date you started working with the company.
* **Employment Status**: Your current status, whether it's full-time, part-time, temporary, or on contract.
* **Direct Supervisor Name**: The name of your direct supervisor or team lead.
* **Supervisor Email**: Your supervisor's email address.
* **Advance Details**: A brief explanation of your reason for requesting a cash advance.
* **Advance Amount**: The amount of money you are requesting for the cash advance.
* **Reason for Request**: The reason for your cash advance request, such as medical emergency or travel expenses.
* **Detailed Explanation**: A more detailed explanation of your reason for requesting a cash advance.
* **Preferred Repayment Method**: How you would like to repay the cash advance, such as through payroll deductions or direct repayment.
* **Repayment Terms**: A note for the payroll department to understand the repayment schedule.
* **Number of Deductions**: The number of deductions you are willing to take per pay period.
* **Amount Per Deduction**: The amount to be deducted per pay period.
* **First Deduction Date**: The date when you expect to start repaying the cash advance.
* **Previous Advances**: A history of previous cash advances you have received.
* **Previous Advance Dates**: The dates of previous cash advances.
* **Outstanding Balance**: Any outstanding balance from previous cash advances.
* **Gross Monthly Salary**: Your monthly salary before any deductions.
* **Net Monthly Salary**: Your monthly salary after deductions.
* **Pay Frequency**: How often you are paid, such as weekly or bi-weekly.
* **Next Pay Date**: The date of your next pay period.
* **Urgency of Request**: The urgency of your request, such as emergency or urgent.
* **Date Needed By**: The deadline for the repayment of the cash advance.
* **Supporting Documents**: Any documents that support your request, such as invoices or bills.
* **Documents Attached**: A list of documents you have attached to support your request.
* **Authorization**: A note for the manager to understand the agreements made.
* **I Authorize Payroll Deduction for Repayment**: Confirmation of your agreement to deduct from your salary for repayment.
* **I Understand the Terms and Conditions**: Confirmation that you understand the terms and conditions of the cash advance.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate**: Confirmation that all information provided is accurate.
* **Employee Signature Date**: The date you sign the form.
* **Manager Approval Status**: The status of your manager's approval, such as approved or pending.
* **Manager Comments**: Any comments or notes from your manager.
* **Manager Signature Date**: The date of your manager's signature.
* **HR Approval Status**: The status of your HR department's approval, such as approved or pending.
* **Finance Approval Status**: The status of your finance department's approval, such as approved or pending.
* **Approval Section**: A note for the finance department to understand the approval process.

### Tips

* Make sure to attach all necessary supporting documents to your request.
* Clearly explain your reason for requesting a cash advance and how you plan to repay it.
* Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Understand the terms and conditions of the cash advance and agree to them.
