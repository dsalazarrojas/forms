# Payday Loan Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from borrowers about their loan applications. It is used to document the loan terms and borrower details to ensure transparency and compliance with regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the loan application form (page 1).
2. Provide your borrower name, date of birth, and loan amount (pages 2-3).
3. Select the loan term, frequency, and type (pages 4-6).
4. Add the loan term duration and borrower signature (pages 7-8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Loan Application Form (page 1)**: This is where you can enter your loan application details, such as your loan needs or requirements.
* **Borrower Name (page 2)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (page 3)**: Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Loan Amount (page 4)**: Enter the amount you are seeking to borrow.
* **Loan Term (page 5)**: Choose the loan term that best suits your needs. This could be 6 months, 12 months, 24 months, or 36 months.
* **Loan Frequency (page 6)**: Select the frequency of the loan payments. You can choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly payments.
* **Loan Term Duration (page 7)**: Enter the length of the loan term in months.
* **Loan Type (page 8)**: Select the type of loan you are applying for. This could be a personal loan, auto loan, or mortgage.
* **Borrower Signature (page 9)**: Please sign and date this page to confirm you have reviewed and agree to the loan terms. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a representative for assistance.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid delays in processing your loan application.
