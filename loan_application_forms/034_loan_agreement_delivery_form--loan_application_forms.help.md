# Loan Agreement Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Agreement Delivery Form is a straightforward form used to collect information from borrowers regarding their loan agreements. This form gathers basic demographic and transactional details to facilitate the loan process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Borrower Information**, including any relevant details such as name, contact details, etc.
2. Select the **Loan Terms**, including any specific terms or conditions of the loan.
3. Enter the **Loan Amount**, the principal amount borrowed.
4. Choose the **Loan Interest Rate** applicable to your loan.
5. Select the **Loan Duration**, the length of time you wish to borrow for.
6. Choose how frequently you'll make **Payment Method**, selecting one or more of the available options.
7. Enter your **Email**, a contact method for communication.
8. Enter your **Phone**, a contact number.
9. Enter the date and time you agreed on the **Date** and **Time**.
10. Add any additional **Notes**, if necessary.
11. Select the **Assigned Tool**, if applicable.
12. Verify your **Form ID**.

* **Borrower Information** (Page 1): Enter your personal details and contact information.
* **Loan Terms** (Page 1): Describe any specific terms or conditions of the loan.
* **Loan Amount** (Page 1): Enter the principal amount borrowed.
* **Loan Interest Rate** (Page 1): Enter the applicable interest rate for your loan.
* **Loan Duration** (Page 1): Choose the length of time you wish to borrow for.

* **Date** (Page 1): Enter the date you agreed on the loan.
* **Time** (Page 1): Enter the time you agreed on the loan.

* **Assigned Tool** (Page 1): If applicable, select the assigned tool.
* **Form ID** (Page 1): Verify your form ID.
