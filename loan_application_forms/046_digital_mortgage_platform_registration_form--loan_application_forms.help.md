# Digital Mortgage Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Mortgage Platform Registration Form is designed to collect relevant information from individuals or organizations seeking to register on the platform, enabling them to access various mortgage services and features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully, ensuring you enter accurate and complete information.
2. Enter your personal contact details in the "Contact Details" section, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. In the "Mortgage Options" section, select the type of mortgage you are interested in (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
4. Provide loan terms, such as loan amount and interest rate, in the "Loan Terms" section.
5. Enter your details as an applicant in the "Applicant Details" section.
6. If required, upload supporting documents in the "Upload Documents" section.
7. Confirm your submission by entering your name and signature in the "Confirm Submission" section.
8. Finally, review the submitted form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Loan Application** (`loan_application`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the loan application, providing context for your mortgage needs.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to enable us to contact you regarding your registration.
- **Mortgage Options** (`mortgage_options`, text, required/optional): Select the type of mortgage you are interested in (e.g., residential, commercial, etc.).
- **Loan Terms** (`loan_terms`, text, required/optional): Enter loan amount, interest rate, and other relevant loan terms.
- **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your details as an applicant, including name, address, and other relevant contact information.
- **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, required/optional): Upload any required documents, such as identification, proof of income, or other relevant attachments.
- **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, signature, and confirm the accuracy of the submitted form to confirm your submission.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): Enter your signature, confirming your commitment to the submitted information.
- **Review Form** (`review_form`, text, required/optional): Review and verify the submitted form before confirming submission.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submission.
- Double-check the "Contact Details" section for correct contact information.
- Clearly select the correct "Mortgage Options" for your loan needs.
- Attach all required documents in the "Upload Documents" section.
- Review the submitted form carefully before confirming submission.
- If you encounter any issues, contact our support team for assistance.
