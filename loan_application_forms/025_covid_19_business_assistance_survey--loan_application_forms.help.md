# COVID 19 Business Assistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your business and how the COVID-19 pandemic has affected it.

## How To Complete This Form
Please answer each question to the best of your ability and truthfully. If a question does not apply to your business, simply skip it. You can select multiple options for some questions, so be sure to choose all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your company.
* **Business Owner or Manager Name** (`business_owner_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the owner or manager of the business.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your company phone number.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business you operate. This will help us understand the industry or sector you are in.
* **Business Location** (`business_location`, text, required): Enter the city or county where your business is located.
* **Year Business Was Established** (`business_established`, number, optional): If you'd like to share, enter the year your business was established.
* **Number of Employees (Pre-COVID)** (`number_of_employees`, number, optional): If you'd like to share, enter the number of employees you had before the pandemic.
* **Current Number of Employees** (`current_employees`, number, optional): If you'd like to share, enter the current number of employees you have.
* **Annual Revenue Before COVID-19** (`annual_revenue_before`, select_one, optional): Select the approximate revenue your business had before the pandemic.
* **Revenue Impact from COVID-19** (`revenue_impact`, select_one, required): Select how your revenue has been affected by the pandemic.
* **Percentage of Revenue Loss** (`revenue_loss_percentage`, number, optional): If you've experienced revenue loss, estimate the percentage.
* **Current Business Status** (`business_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your business.
* **Primary Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, select_multiple, optional): Select all challenges your business has faced during the pandemic.
* **Types of Assistance Needed** (`assistance_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select all types of assistance you believe your business would benefit from.
* **Have You Applied for Assistance Programs?** (`assistance_requested`, select_one, optional): Select if you've applied for any assistance programs.
* **Amount of Assistance Received** (`amount_received`, number, optional): If you've received assistance, enter the amount.
* **Expected Recovery Timeline** (`recovery_timeline`, select_one, optional): Select when you expect to return to normal operations.
* **Concerns About the Future** (`future_concerns`, text, optional): Share your greatest concerns about your business.
* **Recommendations for Assistance Programs** (`assistance_recommendations`, text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for assistance programs.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required): This will be automatically populated with the current date.
