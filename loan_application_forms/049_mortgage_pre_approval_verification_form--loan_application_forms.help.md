# Mortgage Pre Approval Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify loan details for pre-approved applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your loan details in the respective fields.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Loan Application Form 1 (Loan Application Form 1) 
* **Loan Application Form 1**: (`loan_application_form_1`, text, optional) 
  This field is not required but if you have any additional information about your loan application, you can enter it here.

### 2. Income 1 
* **Income 1**: (`income_1`, number, optional) 
  Please enter your annual income for the purpose of verifying your financial status.

### 3. Property Type 
* **Property Type**: (`property_type`, select_one, optional) 
  Please select the type of property you plan to purchase or own. Options include:
  • Home
  • Condo
  • Townhouse
  • Duplex

### 4. Purchase Price 
* **Purchase Price**: (`purchase_price`, number, optional) 
  Enter the proposed purchase price of the property you wish to purchase or refinance.

### 5. Employment Status 
* **Employment Status**: (`employment_status`, select_multiple, optional) 
  Please select your current employment status. Options include:
  • Employed
  • Self-Employed
  • Retired
  • Disabled

### 6. Employment Length 
* **Employment Length**: (`employment_length`, number, optional) 
  If applicable, enter the length of your employment in months or years.

### 7. Income 2 
* **Income 2**: (`income_2`, number, optional) 
  Enter your spousal or other income that will be used for loan eligibility verification.

### 8. Property Address 1 
* **Property Address 1**: (`property_address_1`, text, optional) 
  Enter the property address or location of the property being pre-approved for a loan.

### 9. Property Address 2 
* **Property Address 2**: (`property_address_2`, text, optional) 
  Enter the property address or location of the second property being pre-approved for a loan.

### 10. Property Address 3 
* **Property Address 3**: (`property_address_3`, text, optional) 
  Enter the property address or location of the third property being pre-approved for a loan.
