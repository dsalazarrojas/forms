# Mortgage Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Client Intake Form is used to collect information from potential clients about their loan application. This form helps the lending institution assess the client's creditworthiness and suitability for a mortgage loan.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to the lending institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This section is used to collect basic contact information for the client.
	+ **Client Information (2)**: This is a duplicate of the first Client Information field. Please ignore this field.
* **Loan Purpose**: Describe the reason for the loan.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the contact person's address.
* **Contact Date**: Enter the date and time of the contact.
* **Client Type**: Select the type of client (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **Property Type**: Select all types of properties that apply (Yes or No).
* **Property Value**: Enter the property value.
* **Property Location**: Select the property location (Yes or No).
* **Income Source**: Select the income source (Yes or No).
* **Credit Score**: Select the credit score (Yes or No).
* **Employment Status**: Select the employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Employment Length**: Select the employment length (Yes or No).
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the loan amount.
* **Loan Term**: Select the loan term (Yes or No).
* **Monthly Payment**: Enter the monthly payment amount.
* **Annual Interest**: Enter the annual interest.
* **Loan Rate**: Enter the loan rate.
* **Down Payment**: Enter the down payment amount.
* **Credit Score 2**: Select the credit score 2 (Yes or No).

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth and successful loan application process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the lending institution.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
