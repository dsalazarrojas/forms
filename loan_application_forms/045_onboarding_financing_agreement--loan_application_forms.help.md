# Onboarding Financing Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboarding Financing Agreement form is used to gather information about your loan application. Please provide the requested information to ensure that your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
2. Enter your details in the required fields.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, required): Please enter the length of time you are requesting for the loan.
* **Amount Requested** (`amount_requested`, number, required): Please enter the total amount you are requesting as a loan.
* **Purpose of Loans** (`purpose_of_loans`, select multiple, optional): Please select all purposes of the loan you are seeking. You can select multiple options.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, select one, optional): Please select the current status of your loan application.
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the type of loan you are seeking.
* **Date of Borrowing** (`date_of_borrowing`, date, optional): Please enter the date you plan to start using the loan.
* **Total Interest** (`total_interest`, number, optional): Please enter the total interest you are expecting to pay on the loan.
* **Monthly Interest Payment** (`total_monthly_payment`, number, optional): Please enter the total monthly interest payment you expect to pay.
* **Total Interest Payment** (`total_interest_payment`, number, optional): Please enter the total interest payment you expect to pay.
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, select one, required): Please select whether you have agreed to the loan terms.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments or information about your loan application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Please enter the date you signed the loan agreement.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Please enter your signature.
* **Comments on Onboarding Financing Agreement** (`onboarding_financing_agreement_form_comments`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments about the loan application process.
* **Loan Application Status** (`loan_application_status`, select one, optional): Please select the status of your loan application.
* **Notes on Loan Application** (`notes_loan_application`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments about your loan application.
* **Date of Loan Application** (`date_loan_application`, date, optional): Please enter the date you submitted your loan application.
* **Agreed Signed Date** (`agreement_signed_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date you signed the loan agreement.
* **Total Interest Rate** (`total_interest_rate`, number, optional): Please enter the total interest rate of the loan.
* **Comments on Loan Application** (`loan_application_comments`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments about your loan application.
* **Loan Application Status Date** (`loan_application_status_date`, date, optional): Please enter the date your loan application status was updated.
* **Comments on Loan Application Status** (`loan_application_status_comments`, text, optional): Please enter any additional comments about the status of your loan application.

Note: The fields with `required` as `true` are required fields and must be completed to ensure that your loan application is processed.
