# Mortgage Loan Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information required for a mortgage loan risk assessment. It's a crucial document that helps assess the creditworthiness of potential borrowers and ensures a secure transaction process for both parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the accurate information.
2. Select the correct loan product and term length from the respective drop-down menus.
3. Choose the best description of your employment status.
4. Enter your email address and phone number.
5. If you have any additional notes, please provide them in the designated text area.
6. Once you've completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your name and other identifying details, as per the required fields provided.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select your credit score from the available options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Debt to Income Ratio** (`debt_to_income`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your current debt-to-income ratio, which is a measure of your debt compared to your income.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the amount you're applying for.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the loan term length from 5, 10, or 15 years.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select your current employment status: Employed, Self-Employed, or Unemployed.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your main source of income.
* **Assets** (`assets`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of your available assets.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional information about your financial history or any other relevant details.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Loan Officer** (`loan_officer`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of your designated loan officer.
* **Loan Product** (`loan_product`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of loan you're applying for: Conventional, Government-Backed, or Non-Conforming.
* **Loan Term Length** (`loan_term_length`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the length of the loan term in years.
* **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the risk level for your loan: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Risk Comments** (`risk_comments`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments about your risk assessment.
* **Risk Score** (`risk_score`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter your risk score.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date and time you submitted your application.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time you submitted your application.
* **Submission Date and Time** (`submission_date_time`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date and time you submitted your application.
* **Submission User** (`submission_user`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the user who submitted the application.
* **Submission IP** (`submission_ip`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter your IP address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false):
	+ Provide additional comments or explanations about your application.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required: false):
	+ Click this button to submit your application.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`, note, required: false):
	+ This field will display any error messages or warnings about your application.
