### # Employee Loan Application Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information for a loan application request from employees. This form will help the loan committee to review and process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the employee information section.
2. Provide details about the loan request, including the amount and term you are seeking.
3. Select the loan interest rate and term options that apply to your request.
4. Confirm manager approval for the loan request.
5. Sign and date the form with your employee ID, name, and department.
6. Ensure that your manager signs and dates the form as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Loan Application Request** (employee_loan_application_request_form_page_1): A brief explanation of the loan request, including the purpose and details of the loan.
* **Employee Loan Request** (employee_loan_request_details): A more detailed explanation of the loan request, including any relevant financial information or supporting documents.
* **Loan Amount** (loan_amount): Enter the amount of the loan being requested. This can be a specific dollar amount or a range (e.g., up to $1,000).
* **Loan Term** (loan_term): Select whether you are requesting a fixed loan term (e.g., one month, three months) or an open-ended term.
* **Loan Term Options** (loan_term_options): Choose the specific loan term options you are requesting (e.g., one month, three months, six months, twelve months).
* **Loan Interest Rate** (loan_interest_rate): Select the loan interest rate from the available options (e.g., 5.5%, 6.5%, 7.5%, 8.5%, 9.5%).
* **Loan Interest Rate Options** (loan_interest_rate_options): Not applicable as it is redundant with the previous field.
* **Manager Approval** (manager_approval): Confirm that your manager has approved the loan request.
* **Manager Approval Options** (manager_approval_options): Not applicable as it is redundant with the previous field.
* **Employee Signature** (employee_signature): Sign and date the form with your employee ID, name, and department.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date you are submitting the loan request.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time you are submitting the loan request.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Enter your employee name.
* **Employee ID** (employee_id): Enter your employee ID.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (manager_email): Enter your manager's email.
* **Manager Phone** (manager_phone): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Department** (department): Enter your department name.
* **Manager Signature** (manager_signature): Sign and date the form with your manager's name.
