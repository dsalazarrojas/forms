# Factoring Information Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factoring Information Submission form is a tool used to collect necessary information for factoring submissions. This form helps finance teams or business stakeholders gather details from various departments or individuals within a company to facilitate the factoring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in the Factoring Request Details with a brief description of the request.
3. Provide the Company name and address, as well as the Contact Person, Email, and Phone.
4. Indicate the Business Sector and Business Type (if applicable).
5. Enter the Factoring Request Date and Amount (if applicable).
6. Upload any Supporting Documents (if applicable).
7. Enter the Loan Product, Finance Manager, Finance Department, Finance Phone, and Finance Email (if applicable).
8. Add any Other Details or comments.
9. Complete the Submitter section with their name, Email, Phone, Department, Position, Job Title, Date, and Time (if applicable).
10. Add a Note for any additional comments or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Factoring Request Details**: A brief description of the factoring submission.
  - **Company**: The name of the company requesting the factoring.
  - **Business Address**: The address of the company requesting the factoring.
  - **Contact Person**: The individual responsible for the factoring submission.
  - **Contact Email**: The email of the contact person.
  - **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
  - **Business Sector**: Indicate if the business is in a specific sector.
  - **Business Type**: Indicate if the business is a specific type.
  - **Factoring Request Date**: The date of the factoring request (if applicable).
  - **Factoring Request Amount**: The amount requested for factoring (if applicable).
  - **Supporting Documents**: Upload any documents supporting the factoring submission (if applicable).
  - **Loan Product**: The type of loan product being requested (if applicable).
  - **Finance Manager**: The finance manager for the company requesting factoring.
  - **Finance Department**: The finance department for the company requesting factoring.
  - **Finance Phone**: The phone number of the finance department (if applicable).
  - **Finance Email**: The email of the finance department (if applicable).
  - **Other Details**: Any additional comments or details about the factoring submission.
  - **Submitter**: The individual submitting the factoring request.
  - **Submitter Email**: The email of the submitter.
  - **Submitter Phone**: The phone number of the submitter (if applicable).
  - **Submitter Department**: The department of the submitter.
  - **Submitter Position**: The position of the submitter.
  - **Submitter Job Title**: The job title of the submitter (if applicable).
  - **Submitter Date**: The date of the submission (if applicable).
  - **Submitter Time**: The time of the submission (if applicable).
  - **Submitter Note**: Any additional comments or instructions from the submitter.
