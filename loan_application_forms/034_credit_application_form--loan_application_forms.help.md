# Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and loan information from potential borrowers. It's designed to gather necessary details to process a loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill out all the fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. For "income", select one of the provided options (Income, Salary, Employment, Disability, or Other).
3. If you selected "Other" for the income field, please provide a detailed explanation in the "income_value" field.
4. Enter the desired loan amount in the "loan_amount" field.
5. Select the loan term from the provided options (1 year, 2 years, or 3 years).
6. Choose the type of loan you are applying for (Installment loan, Revolving loan, or Credit card loan).
7. Provide your personal contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Lastly, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info (1)**: Enter your personal details to identify yourself for the loan application.
* **Income (2)**: Select one of the provided options to indicate your current income status.
	+ If you select "Other", please provide a detailed explanation of your income in the "income_value" field.
* **Income Value (3)**: If you selected "Other" for the income field, enter your income details here.
* **Loan Amount (4)**: Enter the amount you wish to borrow.
* **Loan Term (5)**: Select the loan term from the provided options (1 year, 2 years, or 3 years).
* **Loan Type (6)**: Choose the type of loan you are applying for (Installment loan, Revolving loan, or Credit card loan).
* **Contact Info (7)**: Enter your personal contact information for us to reach you.
* **Email (8)**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any delays in processing your loan application.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
