# loan_collateral_disclosure_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a loan collateral disclosure consent from a borrower, ensuring they are aware of the terms and conditions associated with the loan, including the type and value of the collateral being used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **I am a real person** (`1`, `text`, optional): This question confirms that you are a real person and not a representative of a business or organization. Please enter your name to answer this question.
* **Collateral Type** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the types of collateral that apply to your loan. Please choose from the following options:
	+ Home
	+ Vehicle
	+ Other
* **Collateral Value** (`3`, `number`, optional): Please enter the value of your collateral.
* **Collateral Value Currency** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the currency of your collateral value. 
	+ USD
	+ CAD
	+ EUR
* **Disclosure Acknowledgement** (`5`, `text`, optional): Please provide a short statement acknowledging that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the loan.
* **Signature** (`6`, `note`, optional): Sign your name to confirm your acknowledgement.
* **Date** (`7`, `date`, optional): Please sign and date the form.
* **Time** (`8`, `time`, optional): Please sign the time when you sign the form.
* **Lender/Broker Information** (`9`, `text`, optional): If applicable, please provide any additional information about the lender or broker.
* **Borrower Information** (`10`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional information about yourself as the borrower.
 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understand the terms and conditions before completing this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your lender or broker.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your acknowledgement. 

Note: The fields marked as `optional` can be filled in or left blank as needed.
