# Loan Repayment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track loan repayment information, including loan amount, interest, and repayment schedule details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the loan details**: Enter the loan amount, monthly installment, and total interest paid. This information will help us understand your loan obligations.
2. **Specify loan status**: Choose the loan status from the options provided (Active, Inactive, or Paid Off) and explain the reason for this status.
3. **Provide additional details**: Enter any additional information you feel is necessary to help us understand your loan situation.
