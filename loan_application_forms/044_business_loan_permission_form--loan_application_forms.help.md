# Business Loan Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from the applicant to share their personal and financial information with third parties, such as loan coordinators, for the purpose of processing a business loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the applicant's name in the "Applicant name" field.
2. Describe the purpose of the loan in the "Loan purpose" field.
3. Choose whether to grant permission to share information with third parties by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Consent to share information" field.
4. If granting permission, list the organizations that may receive information in the "Third party recipients" field.
5. Provide an email address in the "Contact email" field for loan coordinations to communicate with the applicant.
6. (Optional) Add any additional instructions for signing the permission form in the "Signature instructions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Permission for Loan Processing --**: This is the header of the form, explaining its purpose.
* **Applicant name**: Enter the name of the person granting permission to share their information.
* **Loan purpose**: Describe the reason for seeking a loan.
* **Consent to share information**: Choose "True" to grant permission to share information with third parties, or "False" to deny permission.
* **Third party recipients**: List the organizations that may receive information if permission is granted.
* **Contact email**: Provide an email address for loan coordinators to communicate with the applicant.
* **Signature instructions**: Add any additional guidance for signing the permission form, if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to grant permission only for the specific organizations that will be handling your loan application.
* Use a valid email address for loan coordinators to contact you for updates and questions.
* Review the form carefully before signing to ensure all necessary information is included.
