# Business Loan Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Business Loan Referral Form is used to facilitate the referral of business clients who are seeking loan services to our lending department. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to contact and assist your clients.

## How To Complete This Form

- Ensure you are using this form for the intended purpose.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Referrer name** (`referrer_name`, `text`, required): 
  Please enter your name as the person making the referral.
  
- **Referred business name** (`referred_business_name`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the name of the business that is being referred.
  
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): 
  Enter the email of the person or business being referred.
  
- **Loan amount estimate** (`loan_amount_estimate`, `number`, optional): 
  Enter an approximate loan amount the referred party is seeking, if applicable.
  
- **Reason for referral** (`reason_for_referral`, `text`, optional): 
  Provide a brief reason why this business is a good candidate for a loan.
  
- **Urgency** (`urgency`, `select_one`, optional): 
  Choose how soon the lender should contact the referred party (Low, Medium, High).
  
- **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): 
  Enter any additional context about the referral, if necessary.

## Tips

- Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to ensure proper referral submission.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance, please contact us.
- If you have already submitted this form, please do not resubmit it unless the information is up-to-date.
