# Loan Pre Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from businesses applying for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of your business.
2. Select the type of business you own.
3. Describe the purpose of your loan request.
4. Summarize your business plan.
5. Enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
6. Provide your business's contact address.
7. If your business has a website, Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, or YouTube presence, enter the relevant URLs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business. This will be the name that will appear on your loan application.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business you own from the options provided. This will help us understand your business's nature and industry.
* **Loan Purpose**: Describe the reason you are applying for a loan. This will help us understand how you plan to use the loan funds.
* **Business Plan Summary**: Summarize your business plan, including your goals, financials, and growth strategy.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address. This will help us stay in touch with you throughout the application process.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Business Location**: Enter the address of your business.
* **Business Website**: If your business has a website, enter the URL.
* **Business Facebook**: If your business has a Facebook presence, enter the URL.
* **Business Twitter**: If your business has a Twitter presence, enter the URL.
* **Business Instagram**: If your business has an Instagram presence, enter the URL.
* **Business LinkedIn**: If your business has a LinkedIn presence, enter the URL.
* **Business YouTube**: If your business has a YouTube presence, enter the URL.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of your contact person.
