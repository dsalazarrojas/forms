# Supplier Financing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Financing Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from suppliers to facilitate financing for their products or services. This form is typically used by suppliers to provide detailed information about themselves and their products or services to enable financing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Submit Supplier Financing Details**: Please provide a brief description of your supplier financing needs in this field. This field is not required but is helpful for us to understand your request.
2. **Financial Information**: Please select one of the provided options to specify your financial information. This field is required to proceed with the financing application.
3. **Contact Details**: Enter your contact details, including your name, email address, and any other relevant information. This field is not required but is helpful for us to contact you.
4. **Product Details**: Enter a detailed description of the product or service you are requesting financing for. This field is not required but is helpful for us to understand the scope of your request.
5. **Financing Terms**: Enter any specific financing terms you would like to discuss, such as payment duration or interest rates. This field is not required but is helpful for us to understand your financing preferences.
6. **Review Agreement**: Please review the provided agreement and select one of the options to indicate your understanding. This field is not required but is recommended for you to acknowledge the terms of financing.
7. **Terms Of Service**: This section contains important information about the terms and conditions of our financing. Please review it carefully before proceeding.
8. **Supplier Info**: Enter your company's name, address, and any other relevant information. This field is required for us to contact you and finalize the financing agreement.
9. **Submit**: Once you have completed all the fields, select one or more of the options provided to submit your application. This field is not required but is necessary to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submit Supplier Financing Details** (`supplier_financing_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your supplier financing needs.
* **Financial Information** (`financial_info`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to specify your financial information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
* **Product Details** (`product_details`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the product or service you are requesting financing for.
* **Financing Terms** (`financing_terms`, text, optional): Enter specific financing terms you would like to discuss, such as payment duration or interest rates.
* **Review Agreement** (`review_agreement`, select_one, optional): Review the provided agreement and select one of the options to indicate your understanding.
* **Terms Of Service** (`terms_of_service`, note, optional): This section contains important information about the terms and conditions of our financing.
* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, text, required): Enter your company's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the options to submit your application.
