# Homeownership Eligibility Pre Screening - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help lenders determine the eligibility of applicants for homeownership. By providing accurate information, you can get a better understanding of your chances of qualifying for a mortgage loan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all fields accurately and honestly.
2. Enter your User ID.
3. Provide details about your household income.
4. Enter your gross income.
5. Enter your net income.
6. Select your employment status.
7. Enter your credit score.
8. Confirm if your income is verified.
9. Select your income employment type.
10. Choose your current housing status.
11. Confirm if your employment is verified.
12. Select your employment types from previous years.
13-17. Select your employment status for specific timeframes.
18-19. Enter credit inquiries for previous years.
20-25. Select your employment status for specific timeframes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User ID** (1) (Number, Required): Enter your User ID to identify yourself.
* **Household Income** (2) (Number, Optional): Enter your household income.
* **Gross Income** (3) (Number, Optional): Enter your gross income.
* **Net Income** (4) (Number, Optional): Enter your net income.
* **Employment Status** (5) (Select One, Required): Select your current employment status.
* **Credit Score** (6) (Number, Required): Enter your credit score.
* **Income Verified** (7) (Select One, Required): Confirm if your income is verified.
* **Income Employment Type** (8) (Select Multiple, Optional): Select your income employment types.
* **Housing Status** (9) (Select One, Required): Choose your current housing status.
* **Income Verified Employment Type** (10) (Select Multiple, Required): Confirm if your employment is verified and select your employment types.
* **Credit Inquiries** (11) (Number, Optional): Enter credit inquiries.
* **Credit Inquiries 2 Years** (12) (Number, Optional): Enter credit inquiries for 2 years.
* **Credit Inquiries 3 Years** (13) (Number, Optional): Enter credit inquiries for 3 years.
* **Employment Status 2 Years** (14) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 2 years ago.
* **Employment Status 3 Years** (15) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 3 years ago.
* **Credit Inquiries 6 Years** (16) (Number, Optional): Enter credit inquiries 6 years ago.
* **Credit Inquiries 7 Years** (17) (Number, Optional): Enter credit inquiries 7 years ago.
* **Employment Status 5 Years** (18) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 5 years ago.
* **Employment Status 6 Years** (19) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 6 years ago.
* **Employment Status 7 Years** (20) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 7 years ago.
* **Employment Status 8 Years** (21) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 8 years ago.
* **Employment Status 9 Years** (22) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 9 years ago.
* **Employment Status 10 Years** (23) (Select One, Required): Select your employment status 10 years ago.

Please note that some fields are required to be filled in order to complete the form, and some fields are optional. The fields marked as "Required" are needed to process your application.
