# Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Application Form is used to collect information from individuals applying for a loan. This form helps loan officers and financial institutions gather essential details about the applicant, which will be used to process the loan application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and understand what information it requires.
2. Fill in all the required fields. Fields marked as "required" are necessary to submit the form.
3. If a field has a select_one or select_multiple option, select the relevant option from the list.
4. Enter all other fields with accurate and relevant information.
5. Review your form before submitting it to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **loan_application_form_1** (Note): This field is a general note field for any additional information you wish to provide about your loan application.
* **loan_application_form_2** (Date): Enter the date of the loan application.
* **loan_application_form_3** (Number): Enter the loan amount you are applying for.
* **loan_application_form_4** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **loan_application_form_5** (Text): Enter your address.
* **loan_application_form_6** (Select One): Select "Yes" if you have any outstanding loans or financial obligations.
* **loan_application_form_7** (Select Multiple): Select all applicable "Yes" options for any other financial obligations.
* **loan_application_form_8** (Time): Enter the time of the loan application.
* **loan_application_form_9** (Number): Enter the loan interest rate.
* **loan_application_form_10** (Text): Enter your employment information.
* **loan_application_form_11** (Text): Enter your employment information (continued).
* **loan_application_form_12** (Text): Enter your education history.
* **loan_application_form_13** (Number): Enter the loan term.
* **loan_application_form_14** (Number): Enter the loan amount for each loan.
* **loan_application_form_15** (Email): Enter your email address (continued).
* **loan_application_form_16** (Text): Enter any additional comments or information about your loan application.
* **loan_application_form_17** (Select One): Select "Yes" if you have any existing credit cards.
* **loan_application_form_18** (Text): Enter your loan purpose.
* **loan_application_form_19** (Text): Enter any other information about your loan purpose (continued).
* **loan_application_form_20** (Text): Enter any other information about your loan purpose (continued).
* **loan_application_form_21** (Number): Enter the loan amount.
* **loan_application_form_22** (Number): Enter the total loan amount.
* **loan_application_form_23** (Text): Enter any other information about your loan application.
* **loan_application_form_24** (Text): Enter any other information about your loan application.
* **loan_application_form_25** (Text): Enter any other information about your loan application.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your loan application is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our loan officers.
* We appreciate your prompt submission of the completed form to expedite the loan application process.
