# Mortgage Approval Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Approval Validation Form is designed to collect and verify the necessary information for a mortgage loan application. It helps to ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and that the loan application meets the necessary criteria for approval.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information about the applicant, property, and loan details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that the information provided is accurate and consistent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false)
    This field is for entering the applicant's personal information.
* **Property Info** (`property_info`, text, required: false)
    This field is for entering the property's details, such as its address and type.
* **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required: false)
    This field is for entering the applicant's credit score, which will be used to assess their creditworthiness.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the applicant's current employment status to help us assess their income and creditworthiness.
    *   Employed
    *   Self-Employed
    *   Unemployed
    *   Student
    *   Disabled
* **Income Range** (`income_range`, number, required: false)
    Enter the applicant's annual income range to help us assess their creditworthiness.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false)
    Enter the amount of the loan being applied for.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the term of the loan, from 1-5 years to 20-25 years.
    *   1-5 years
    *   5-10 years
    *   10-15 years
    *   15-20 years
    *   20-25 years
* **Loan Interest Rate** (`loan_interest_rate`, number, required: false)
    Enter the interest rate of the loan.
* **Loan Rate Type** (`loan_rate_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Select the type of interest rate for the loan.
    *   Fixed
    *   Floating
    *   Adjustable
* **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Select the type of loan being applied for.
    *   Primary Residence
    *   Investment
    *   Vacation
    *   Refinance
* **Applicant Income** (`applicant_income`, number, required: false)
    Enter the applicant's annual income.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, select_one, required: false)
    Select the current status of the loan application.
    *   Pre-Approved
    *   Approved
    *   In Progress
    *   Denied
    *   Pending
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
    Select the type of property the loan is for.
    *   Primary Residence
    *   Investment
    *   Vacation
* **Loan Security** (`loan_security`, text, required: false)
    Enter any additional details about the loan security.
* **Loan Security Details** (`loan_security_details`, text, required: false)
    Enter any additional details about the loan security.

Note: This guide is intended to help users complete the form accurately and effectively. It is not a substitute for professional advice or guidance. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the loan officer.
