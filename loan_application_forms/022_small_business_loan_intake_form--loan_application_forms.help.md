# Small Business Loan Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Business Loan Intake Form is designed to collect relevant information from small business owners to process their loan applications. This form is used to gather data about the business owner, business details, loan requirements, financial information, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Business Owner Info section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide detailed information about your business in the Business Details section.
3. Specify the loan details you need, including loan amount and purpose in the Loan Details section.
4. Enter your financial information in the Financials section.
5. Enter your contact information, including your address and preferred contact method, in the Contact Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Business Owner Info
* **Business Owner's Name** (text): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address** (text): Enter your email address that you use for business correspondence.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during business hours.

### 2. Business Details
* **Business Name** (text): Enter your business name as it appears on your ID or marketing materials.
* **Business Type** (text): Select the type of business you own (e.g., retail, service, food service, etc.).
* **Business Address** (text): Enter your business address where you can be reached.
* **Business Years in Operation** (number): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.

### 3. Loan Details
* **Loan Amount** (number): Enter the amount of money you need for your loan.
* **Loan Purpose** (text): Specify the reason you need the loan (e.g., inventory, equipment, expansion, etc.).

### 4. Financials
* **Monthly Income** (number): Enter your monthly business income.
* **Monthly Expenses** (number): Enter your monthly business expenses.
* **Available Cash** (number): Enter the cash available for your business.

### 5. Contact Info
* **Address** (text): Enter your business address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address** (text): Enter your email address that you use for business correspondence.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (text): Select your preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, mail, etc.).
