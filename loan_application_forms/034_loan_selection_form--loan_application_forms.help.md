# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the loan application form. The form is used to collect information about loan selections, applicant types, loan terms, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of loan you are applying for from the loan selection form.
2. Choose your applicant type (Individual, Business, or Non-Profit).
3. Select the type of loan you are interested in (Home Equity, Home Improvement, Personal, Vehicle, Student, or Other).
4. Choose the loan term (6 Months, 12 Months, 24 Months, 36 Months, 48 Months, or 60 Months).
5. Select your income level (Less than $25,000, $25,001 to $50,000, $50,001 to $75,000, $75,001 to $100,000, $100,001 to $150,000, $150,001 to $200,000, $200,001 to $250,000, or $250,001 to $300,000).
6. Choose the loan amount ($5,000, $10,000, $15,000, $20,000, $25,000, $30,000, $35,000, or $40,000).
7. Select your credit score (300-399, 400-499, 500-599, 600-699, 700-799, 800-899, 900-999, or 1,000).
8. Select the loan status (Approved, Denied, Pending).
9. (Optional) Enter the loan date.
10. (Optional) Enter the loan time.
11. Add any notes about the loan.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. (Optional) Enter the loan amount in cents.
14. (Optional) Enter your income in cents.
15. (Optional) Enter the loan rate.
16. (Optional) Enter the loan fees.
17. (Optional) Enter the loan fees in cents.
18. (Optional) Enter the loan fees percentage.
19. (Optional) Enter the loan term in years.
20. (Optional) Enter your age.
