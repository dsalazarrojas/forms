# Loan Application Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the applicant for the loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, date of birth, and contact details (email and phone number).
2. Provide information about your employment status and income.
3. Share details about your loan, such as the amount and term.
4. Select the type of loan you are applying for (Personal, Business, or Other).
5. Choose the loan status (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
6. Enter any additional financial aid details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, required: false): This is where you will enter your personal details, including your name.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you will provide your contact information, such as your email and phone number.
- **Employment Information** (`employment_info`, text, required: false): Here, you will provide details about your employment status.
- **Income Information** (`income_info`, text, required: false): Enter your income details here.
- **Loan Information** (`loan_info`, text, required: false): Enter details about the loan you are applying for.
- **Financial Aid** (`financial_aid`, text, required: false): If you have any financial aid, please enter details about it here.
- **Credit Bureau** (`credit_bureau`, text, required: false): Provide any information about your credit bureau.
- **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, number, required: false): Enter your credit score.
- **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the loan amount you are applying for.
- **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, number, required: false): Enter the term of the loan.
- **Loan Type** (`loan_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of loan you are applying for (Personal, Business, or Other).
- **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the loan status (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the loan officer.
