# Commercial Loan Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your business and request a loan. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details about your business and loan requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your business information, including your legal name, business structure, tax ID number, and business address.
2. Provide details about your business, such as how long you've been in operation and your estimated annual revenue.
3. Specify the purpose of the loan and the amount you are requesting.
4. Enter information about the authorized representative, including their name, title, and contact details.
5. Declare whether your business or any principal has filed for bankruptcy and authorize a credit check.
6. Provide the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1: Business Entity Information
* **Legal Name of Business** (`business_legal_name`): Enter the official name of your business.
* **Business Structure** (`business_structure`): Select the type of business structure you have (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, C-Corp, S-Corp, Non-Profit).
* **Federal Tax ID (EIN)** (`tax_id_number`): Enter your business's Federal Tax ID number.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter your business's address, including street, city, state, and ZIP.
* **Years in Operation** (`years_in_business`): Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Estimated Annual Gross Revenue** (`annual_revenue`): Enter your estimated annual gross revenue.

### Section 2: Loan Request Details
* **Requested Loan Amount** (`requested_loan_amount`): Enter the amount of loan you are requesting.
* **Purpose of Loan** (`loan_purpose`): Select the purpose of the loan (e.g., Working Capital, Real Estate Purchase, Equipment Financing, Debt Refinancing, Business Expansion, Inventory Purchase).
* **Description of Collateral** (`collateral_offered`): Optionally, enter a description of the collateral you are offering.
* **Date of Application** (`application_date`): Enter the date you are submitting this application.

### Authorized Representative
* **Full Name of Applicant** (`applicant_name`): Enter the full name of the authorized representative.
* **Title/Position in Business** (`applicant_title`): Enter the title or position of the representative in your business.
* **Business Email Address** (`applicant_email`): Enter the email address associated with your business.
* **Business Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`): Enter the phone number associated with your business.

### Declarations and Authorization
* **Has the business or any principal ever filed for bankruptcy?** (`bankruptcy_disclosure`): Declare whether your business or any principal has filed for bankruptcy.
* **Credit Check Authorization** (`credit_check_authorization`): Authorize a credit check for the loan application.
