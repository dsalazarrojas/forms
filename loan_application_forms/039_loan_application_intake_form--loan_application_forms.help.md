# Loan Application Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Loan Application Intake Form is used to gather information for loan applications. This form collects data on the type of loan application, the purpose of the loan, the applicant's details, loan terms, and other relevant credit information. Fill out this form to initiate the loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submit the form**: Fill out each required field carefully and accurately.
2. **Choose the correct loan application type**: Select the type of loan application that best suits your needs from the options provided.
3. **Specify the loan purpose**: Select all relevant purposes for which you are applying for the loan (e.g., Home improvement, Education, Car).
4. **Provide your name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
5. **Enter the loan amount**: Enter the total amount you wish to borrow.
6. **Select the loan term**: Choose the length of time you wish to take to repay the loan.
7. **Enter the interest rate**: Enter the interest rate for the loan.
8. **Enter your credit score**: Enter your current credit score.
9. **Enter your annual income**: Enter your annual income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Type**: Select the type of loan application you are submitting (Application or Refinance). This field is not required but will help the loan officer understand your needs.
* **Loan Purpose**: Select all relevant purposes for which you are applying for the loan (e.g., Home improvement, Education, Car).
	+ This field is required to understand the purpose of your loan application.
* **Applicant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
	+ This field is required to verify your identity.
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the total amount you wish to borrow.
	+ This field is required to determine the loan amount.
* **Loan Term**: Select the length of time you wish to take to repay the loan (6 months, 1 year, 5 years).
	+ This field is required to determine the loan term.
* **Loan Interest Rate**: Enter the interest rate for the loan.
	+ This field is required to determine the loan interest rate.
* **Credit Score**: Enter your current credit score.
	+ This field is required to assess your creditworthiness.
* **Annual Income**: Enter your annual income.
	+ This field is required to assess your income and creditworthiness.
