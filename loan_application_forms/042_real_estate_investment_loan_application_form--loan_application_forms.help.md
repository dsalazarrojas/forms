# real_estate_investment_loan_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The real_estate_investment_loan_application_form is designed to collect information from users to facilitate a real estate investment loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide personal details such as your name, date of birth, employment status, and annual income.
2. Enter loan details such as loan amount, loan term, interest rate, and loan type.
3. Enter property details such as property value and down payment.
4. Enter contact information such as email, phone, and address.
5. Enter credit information such as credit score and credit report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Details

* **Personal Details** (`1`, `personal_details`, Required): This section requires your personal details such as name, date of birth, employment status, and annual income.

### Loan Details

* **Loan Details** (`2`, `loan_details`, Required): This section is for entering loan amount, loan term, interest rate, and loan type.
* **Loan Amount** (`5`, `loan_amount`, Required): Enter the loan amount you are seeking.
* **Loan Term** (`6`, `loan_term`, Required): Enter the loan term in years.
* **Interest Rate** (`7`, `interest_rate`, Required): Enter the interest rate as a percentage.
* **Loan Type** (`8`, `loan_type`, Required): Select whether the loan is for Personal or Business purposes.
* **Credit Score** (`11`, `credit_score`, Required): Enter your current credit score.

### Property Details

* **Property Details** (`3`, `property_details`, Required): This section requires entering property value and down payment.
* **Property Value** (`9`, `property_value`, Required): Enter the value of the property.
* **Down Payment** (`10`, `down_payment`, Required): Enter the down payment amount.

### Contact

* **Contact** (`4`, `contact`, Required): This section requires your contact information such as email, phone, and address.
* **Email** (`19`, `email`, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`20`, `phone`, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`21`, `address`, Required): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`22`, `city`, Required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`23`, `state`, Required): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip** (`24`, `zip`, Required): Enter your zip code.

### Credit

* **Credit Report** (`12`, `credit_report`, Required): Upload your credit report.
* **Credit Score** (`11`, `credit_score`, Required): Enter your credit score.
* **Co-Borrower** (`17`, `co_borrower`, Optional): If you have a co-borrower, enter their information.

### Loan Purpose

* **Loan Purpose** (`16`, `loan_purpose`, Required): Select the purpose of the loan such as Home Improvement, Property Investment, or Debt Consolidation.

### Notes

* **Notes** (`18`, `notes`, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the loan application.
