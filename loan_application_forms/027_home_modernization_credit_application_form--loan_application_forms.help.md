# Home Modernization Credit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Modernization Credit Application Form is a tool for homeowners to apply for a home modernization credit, which can be used for home improvements, additions, or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the project details, including the type of project you are applying for.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name and contact method.
3. Indicate the type of loan you are requesting.
4. Select the type of property you are applying for.
5. Specify the amount of credit you are requesting.
6. Choose the loan term and interest rate.
7. Select the purpose of the credit.
8. Enter the loan amount.
9. Indicate the credit status.
10. Add any additional notes or comments.
11. Finally, provide your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): This field is where you provide a brief description of the project you are applying for, including the type of home improvement, addition, or repair you are seeking.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, required): Select "Homeowner" if you are the homeowner, "Lender" if you are a representative of the lender.
* **Loan Request** (`loan_request`, number, required): Enter the specific loan request amount.
* **Property Details** (`property_details`, select_one, required): Select the type of property you are applying for, such as "Single Family Home" or "Townhouse".
* **Credit Request** (`credit_request`, number, optional): This field is optional, but enter the credit request amount if you are requesting a credit.
* **Credit Amount** (`credit_amount`, number, required): Enter the actual credit amount you are requesting.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, required): Select the loan term from 3, 6, or 12 months.
* **Loan Interest Rate** (`loan_interest_rate`, number, required): Enter the loan interest rate.
* **Credit Use** (`credit_use`, select_multiple, optional): Select the purpose of the credit, such as "Home Improvement" or "Home Addition".
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required): Enter the total loan amount.
* **Credit Status** (`credit_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the credit status, such as "Approved", "Denied", or "Approved with Conditions".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Add your signature to confirm your application.

## Tips
Be sure to review each field carefully and ensure you provide accurate information. If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact our support team.
