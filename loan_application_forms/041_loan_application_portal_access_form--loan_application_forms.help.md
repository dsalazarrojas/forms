# Loan Application Portal Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form grants access to the loan application portal, a secure online platform where users can view and manage their loan applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access the portal.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. Review all fields carefully, and provide accurate information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Applicant Email** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address associated with your loan application.
* **Applicant Phone** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Lender Name** (`lender_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the lender associated with your loan.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of the loan you are applying for.
* **Loan Terms** (`loan_terms`, `text`, required): Enter any specific terms of the loan, such as the repayment term.
* **Loan Duration** (`loan_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the loan in months.
* **Loan Rate** (`loan_rate`, `number`, required): Enter the interest rate of the loan.
* **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your loan, which can be "Approved", "Denied", or "Pending".
* **Applicant Notes** (`applicant_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about your loan application.
* **Lender Notes** (`lender_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the lender.
* **Applicant Agreed** (`applicant_agreed`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate that you have agreed to the loan terms.
* **Lender Agreed** (`lender_agreed`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate that the lender has agreed to the loan terms.

## Tips
- Be accurate and truthful in your responses to ensure the smooth processing of your loan application.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the loan administrator for guidance.
