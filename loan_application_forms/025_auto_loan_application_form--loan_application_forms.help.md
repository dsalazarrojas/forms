# Auto Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Loan Application Form is designed to gather information about the applicant and their financial situation in order to determine loan eligibility and terms. The form collects personal and financial information about the applicant and the vehicle to be financed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name.
2. Enter your Social Security Number (SSN).
3. Enter your Date of Birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
4. Enter your Total Gross Annual Income.
5. Enter your monthly housing expense (e.g. rent or mortgage).
6. Enter your current employer.
7. Enter the number of years you have been working for your current employer.
8. Enter the amount you are requesting to borrow.
9. Select the desired term for the loan (24, 36, 48, 60, or 72 months).
10. Enter the down payment amount (cash or trade-in value).
11. Enter details about the vehicle being financed, including make, model, and year.
12. Select the condition of the vehicle (New, Used, or Certified Pre-owned).
13. Select whether to authorize the lender to pull your credit report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Annual Income**: Enter your total gross annual income.
* **Monthly Housing Cost**: Enter your monthly rent or mortgage expense.
* **Employer**: Enter the name of your current employer.
* **Years at Employer**: Enter the number of years you have been working for your current employer.
* **Loan Amount Requested**: Enter the total amount you are requesting to borrow.
* **Desired Term (Months)**: Select the preferred payoff period for the loan (24, 36, 48, 60, or 72 months).
* **Down Payment**: Enter the amount of cash or trade-in value you are willing to pay upfront.
* **Vehicle Details**: Enter make, model, and year of the vehicle being financed.
* **Vehicle Status**: Select the condition of the vehicle (New, Used, or Certified Pre-owned).
* **Authorize Credit Report**: Select whether to authorize the lender to pull your credit report.

**Tips**: Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely. Incomplete or inaccurate information may delay or prevent the loan application process. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our customer service department.
