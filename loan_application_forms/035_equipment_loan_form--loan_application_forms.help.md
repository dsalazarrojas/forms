# equipment_loan_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for customers to submit loan applications for equipment financing.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review each section carefully and make sure to provide accurate and relevant information.
2.  Start by filling out the Company Information section.
3.  Then, move on to the Loan Details, Equipment Information, Loan Request, and Signatures sections.
4.  Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Information (Text)**: This section is used to provide your company's basic details such as name, address, and contact information.
*   **Loan Details (Text)**: In this section, please provide information about the loan you are requesting, including loan amount and loan term.
*   **Equipment Information (Text)**: Describe the equipment you are looking to purchase or lease and its current value or purchase price.
*   **Loan Request (Text)**: State the purpose of the loan and how the funds will be used.
*   **Signatures (Text)**: Include the signatures of the relevant parties such as the applicant and the authorized person representing the company.
