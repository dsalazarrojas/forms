# Equipment Loan Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the process of requesting and managing equipment loans between partners and customers. The form helps to gather necessary information for the loan application process, ensuring that both parties are aware of their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below:

1.  Begin by selecting the type of equipment you wish to request a loan for from the "loan_form_template" field, providing a brief description of the equipment you are seeking.
2.  In the "loan_form" field, describe the specific equipment you wish to loan, ensuring it accurately reflects the details of what you are requesting.
3.  Specify the name(s) of the person or organization that will be loaned the equipment in the "loaned_to" field.
4.  In the "check_out_details" field, include any additional check-out details such as check-out dates, times, and any specific instructions for handling or storage.
5.  If your partnership or company requires a partnership agreement, fill out the "partnership_agreement" field with the necessary terms and conditions.
6.  Review the "terms" field for any additional terms or conditions that need to be acknowledged or agreed upon.
7.  Finally, click the "print_button" to generate a printed version of the loan agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **loan_form_template** (text, required/optional): A brief description of the equipment you wish to request a loan for.
*   **loan_form** (text, required/optional): A detailed description of the specific equipment you wish to loan, including any relevant specifications or conditions.
*   **loaned_to** (text, required/optional): The name(s) of the person or organization that will be loaned the equipment.
*   **check_out_details** (text, required/optional): Additional check-out details, such as check-out dates and specific instructions for handling or storage.
*   **partnership_agreement** (text, required/optional): A statement outlining any partnership or company-specific terms and conditions that need to be agreed upon.
*   **terms** (text, required/optional): Any additional terms or conditions that need to be acknowledged or agreed upon.
*   **print_button** (text, required/optional): A button used to generate a printed version of the loan agreement.
*   **print_equipment_loan_agreement** (text, required/optional): Not applicable, as this is a print button and not a field to fill out.
