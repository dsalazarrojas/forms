# Advance Loan VIP Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for applicants to provide details about themselves and their loan application. It is required for the loan company to verify the applicant's identity, assess their loan worthiness, and ensure a smooth loan process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Use a valid email address for correspondence.
3. Ensure that the requested loan amount is in the local currency.
4. Provide a clear and concise explanation of the loan purpose.
5. Enter your correct annual income.
6. Choose a preferred contact time that suits you best.
7. Check the box indicating your consent to credit checks.
8. Finally, select the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant full name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Your valid email address where we will contact you for loan correspondence.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your mobile number or any other contact number you prefer.
* **Requested loan amount** (`requested_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you are seeking as a loan in the local currency.
* **Purpose of loan** (`purpose_of_loan`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the intended use of the loan.
* **Annual income** (`annual_income`, `number`, required): Enter your gross yearly income.
* **Preferred contact time** (`preferred_contact_time`, `time`, optional): Choose a time that suits you best for us to contact you.
* **Consent to credit check** (`consent_credit_check`, `select_one`, required): Check the box to indicate your agreement to credit checks as part of the loan process.
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your loan application.
