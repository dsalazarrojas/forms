# Student Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students to apply for a loan. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your loan request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your loan amount in the "Loan Amount" field.
2. Select the loan term that best suits your needs from the "Loan Term" field.
3. Enter your monthly payment amount in the "Monthly Payment" field.
4. Select your preferred interest rate from the available options in the "Interest Rate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Loan Application** (`student_loan_application_form`, text, Required/Optional): Please enter a brief description of your loan application.
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, Required/Optional): Enter the total amount you wish to borrow.
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, Required/Optional): Select the loan term that best suits your needs. Options include 2-year, 4-year, and 6-year.
* **Monthly Payment** (`monthly_payment`, number, Required/Optional): Enter your estimated monthly payment amount.
* **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, number, Required/Optional): Select your preferred interest rate from the available options.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to avoid any delays in processing your loan request.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our loan officer for assistance.
