# Gift Letter For Mortgage - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Letter For Mortgage is a document that confirms the gifter (donor) has gifted a certain amount of money towards the borrower's mortgage.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting the form, ensure you have the following information:
* Donor's details
* Borrower's details
* Mortgage amount and details
* Lender information

1. **Donor Details**
   * **Donors Name**: The name of the person gifting the money.
   * **Donor Type**: The type of relationship between the donor and the borrower, such as "Primary Buyer" or "Other".
   * **Donor Address**: The address of the donor.
   * **Gift Amount**: The amount of money being gifted.
   * **Gift Date**: The date of the gift.
   * **Donor Institution or Company**: If the donor is a company or institution, provide their name.
   * **Donors Contact Information**: The donor's contact information.

2. **Borrower Details**
   * **Borrowers Name**: The name of the person borrowing the money.
   * **Borrower Details**: A description of the borrower's financial situation.

3. **Mortgage Details**
   * **Amount of the Mortgage**: The total amount of the mortgage.
   * **Type of Property**: The type of property being mortgaged, such as "Primary Residence" or "Vacation Home".
   * **Address of Property**: The address of the property.
   * **Lender Information**: A description of the lender's information.
   * **Lenders Name**: The name of the lender.
   * **Loan Amount**: The total amount of the loan.
   * **Loan Term**: The length of time the loan is for, such as "30 Years" or "15 Years".
   * **Loan Type**: The type of loan, such as "Fixed Rate" or "Variable Rate".
   * **Collateral Type**: The type of collateral for the loan, such as "Primary Residence" or "Vacation Home".
   * **Date of Maturity**: The date the loan will mature.
   * **Maturity Date**: Not applicable as it seems to be a duplicate field.
   * **Lender Interest Rate**: The interest rate of the loan.
   * **Borrowers Income**: The income of the borrower.
   * **Loan Status**: The status of the loan, such as "Active" or "Paid Off".

## Tips

* Ensure the accuracy of all information provided.
* Double-check for any required fields marked as "required" to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure of any information, do not hesitate to contact the relevant parties for clarification.
