# kasanag_loan_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "kasanag_loan_application_form" form is a tool used to collect and process loan applications from internal users. It is designed to gather essential information about each loan request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form fields and understand which ones are required (marked as required) and which ones are optional.
2.  Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
3.  Provide optional fields with relevant and helpful data if possible.
4.  Review your submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Loan Application Form** (Page 1)

*   **Loan Application Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is a text area where you can describe your loan request. Please provide a clear and concise overview of your loan needs.
*   **Applicant Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the applicant for this loan request.
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the applicant or your own email address for contact purposes.

### **Loan Details** (Page 2)

*   **Loan Amount** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter the exact amount you are requesting for the loan.
*   **Loan Terms** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable loan terms that apply to your request.
*   **Loan Terms List** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose a specific loan term from the provided options.
*   **Loan Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Select the date when you are planning to apply for the loan.
*   **Loan Time** (`8`, `time`, required: false): Specify the time when you are available for the loan.
*   **Loan Status** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the status of your loan application from the options provided.
*   **Assigned User** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the user assigned to process your loan application.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and honest when filling out the form to ensure a smooth and efficient loan application process.
*   If you are not sure about any field, please ask your assigned loan officer for clarification.
*   Review the form before submission to avoid any errors or mistakes.
