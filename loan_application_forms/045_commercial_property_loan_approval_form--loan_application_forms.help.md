# Commercial Property Loan Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for commercial property loan approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Assigned Loan Officer** (`loan_officer_name`, text, required)
The assigned loan officer's name and contact information will be used to contact you regarding your loan.

### 2. **Borrower Entity Name** (`borrower_entity_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the entity borrowing the commercial property loan.

### 3. **Loan Application Number** (`loan_application_number`, text, required)
Enter the loan application number assigned by the loan originator.

### 4. **Property Collateral Address** (`collateral_address`, text, required)
Enter the address of the property being used as collateral for the loan.

### 5. **Final Appraised Value** (`appraised_value`, number, required)
Enter the final appraised value of the property.

### 6. **Date of Appraisal** (`appraisal_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the final appraisal.

### 7. **Final LTV Ratio (%)** (`loan_to_value_ratio`, number, required)
Enter the loan-to-value ratio for the approved loan amount.

### 8. **Approved Principal Amount** (`approved_principal_amount`, number, required)
Enter the approved principal amount of the loan.

### 9. **Approved Interest Rate (%)** (`approved_interest_rate`, number, required)
Enter the approved interest rate for the loan.

### 10. **Rate Type** (`rate_type`, select_one, required)
Select the rate type for the loan from the options: Fixed, Variable / Floating, or Step-Up.

### 11. **Loan Maturity Term (Months)** (`loan_maturity_term`, number, required)
Enter the loan maturity term in months.

### 12. **Amortization Period (Years)** (`amortization_period`, number, required)
Enter the amortization period in years.

### 13. **Final Loan Decision** (`final_approval_decision`, select_one, required)
Select the final loan decision from the options: Approved as Structured, Approved with Conditions, or Declined.

### 14. **Specific Conditions for Funding** (`approval_conditions_list`, text, optional)
If approved with conditions, list the specific conditions for funding, e.g., Final Title Report, Insurance Binder, Phase 1 ESA.

### 15. **Credit Committee Approval Date** (`committee_approval_date`, date, required)
Enter the date of the credit committee approval.

### 16. **Authorized Approver Name** (`approver_signature_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of the authorized approver.
