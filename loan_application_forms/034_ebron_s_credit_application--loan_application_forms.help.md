# Ebrons-Credit-Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ebrons-Credit-Application form is designed to collect information from customers to assess their loan status. It is only intended for customers who have been pre-approved for a credit line. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have been pre-approved for a credit line with Ebrons before attempting to fill out this form.
2. Complete all required fields (if any) accurately.
3. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address for loan updates and notifications.
- **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
- **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the exact amount you require for the loan.
- **Loan Duration** (`loan_duration`, number, required: false): Enter the number of months you need the loan for.
- **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required: false): Record the date of the purchase or reason for applying for the loan.
- **Purchase Time** (`purchase_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the purchase or reason for applying for the loan.
- **Loan Status** (`loan_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of your loan. Options: Approved, Denied, or Pending.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter any tools assigned to you for the loan.
- **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): The file with your loan details will be generated here.
- **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, required: false): Enter your current or relevant physical address.
- **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of your current address.
- **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state of your current address.
- **Customer Country** (`customer_country`, text, required: false): Enter the country of your current address.
- **Credit Score** (`credit_score`, text, required: false): This field is currently not used in the application process. Please do not fill it out.
- **Loan Rate** (`loan_rate`, text, required: false): This field is currently not used in the application process. Please do not fill it out.
- **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, text, required: false): This field is currently not used in the application process. Please do not fill it out.
- **Loan Fee** (`loan_fee`, text, required: false): This field is currently not used in the application process. Please do not fill it out.
