# Business Financing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for businesses seeking financing for various purposes. It will help us assess your request and provide guidance on the next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information available before starting the form.
3. Double-check your submissions for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant Information
* **Applicant Name**: Your full legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Your business email address or any other contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Your business phone number or any other number that we can use to contact you.
* **Personal Address**: Your residential address, not your business address.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth as it appears on your official documents.

### Business Information
* **Business Name**: Your business's legal name.
* **Business Structure**: How your business is organized (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC, C-Corporation, S-Corporation).
* **Industry Type**: The type of industry your business falls under (Retail, Technology, Manufacturing, Services, Healthcare, Hospitality, Construction, Other).
* **Business Address**: Your business's address.
* **Date Business Established**: When your business was started.
* **Number of Employees**: Current employee count.
* **Financing Request**: This section is used to guide you through the financing process, and it contains the following fields:
	+ **Amount of Financing Requested**: Total amount you need.
	+ **Type of Financing Needed**: The type of financing you are looking for (Startup Capital, Working Capital, Equipment Financing, Expansion Financing, Debt Consolidation, Line of Credit, Other).
	+ **Use of Funds**: A brief explanation of how you plan to use the funds.
	+ **Proposed Repayment Timeline**: A timeframe to repay the loan.

### Financial Information
* **Annual Revenue (Last Year)**: Your business's revenue for the last year.
* **Average Monthly Revenue**: Your current monthly revenue.
* **Annual Profit/Loss**: Your business's profit or loss for the last year.
* **Existing Business Debt**: Total outstanding business debt.
* **Collateral or Security Offered**: A brief explanation of any assets you can offer as security.

### Collateral and Security
* **Collateral or Security Offered**: A brief explanation of any assets you can offer as security.
