# Housing Investment Loan Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their loan application for housing investment. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth loan process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.
3. Fill out all required fields, denoted by *.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Loan Application Form**: *Required: No* 
  This field allows you to choose whether you want to apply for a loan or not.
  You can select either 'Yes' or 'No' from the provided options.
  Please note that this field is not required, so you can skip it if you're not applying.

- **Applicant Details**: *Required: No* 
  Please fill in your personal details such as name, address, and other relevant information.
  Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy.

- **Property Details**: *Required: No* 
  This field asks if you have any properties you want to use as collateral for the loan.
  Select 'Yes' if you have a property you'd like to use, or 'No' if you don't.

- **Financial Details**: *Required: No* 
  Enter your financial information, such as your income and expenses.
  Make sure to provide accurate numbers.

- **Loan Amount**: *Required: No* 
  Enter the amount you're requesting for the loan.
  Please be realistic with your request, considering your financial situation.

- **Loan Term**: *Required: No* 
  Select the term of your loan, such as short-term or long-term.
  Make sure to choose the term that best suits your needs.

- **Loan Rate**: *Required: No* 
  Choose the rate of your loan from the provided options (Good or Fair).
  Be aware that the rate may affect the interest you'll pay.

- **Loan Security**: *Required: No* 
  Select whether you want to provide any collateral for the loan.
  If you choose 'Yes', make sure to provide the necessary information about your collateral.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact our customer support for clarification.
