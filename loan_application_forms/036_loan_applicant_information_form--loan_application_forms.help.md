# Loan Applicant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from loan applicants to assess their eligibility for a loan. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful loan application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Start by entering your **Applicant Name** in the first field. This is a required field that must be filled out.
3. Provide your **Contact Information** in the second field. Although not required, sharing your contact information will help the lender reach out to you in case of any updates or further questions.
4. Enter the loan amount you are interested in borrowing in the **Loan Amount** field. This field is also required to process your loan application.
5. Specify the **Loan Term** in the field provided. This will determine the duration of the loan.
6. Choose your **Employment Status** from the options provided: **Employed**, **Unemployed**, or **Self-Employed**. This field is required to assess your employment status.
7. Select all applicable sources of your **Income Source** from the options provided: **Salary**, **Self-Employment**, or **Other**. This field is required to understand your income stream.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information** (2, text, optional): Provide your email address or phone number so we can get in touch with you regarding your loan application.
* **Loan Amount** (3, number, required): Enter the amount of loan you are interested in borrowing. Please use numeric values only (e.g., 1000, 2000, 5000).
* **Loan Term** (4, text, required): Choose a loan term from the options provided to determine the duration of the loan.
* **Employment Status** (5, select_one, required): Choose your employment status from the options provided:
	+ Employed: You are currently working for an organization.
	+ Unemployed: You are not currently employed.
	+ Self-Employed: You are running your own business or have your own business entity.
* **Income Source** (6, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable sources of your income:
	+ Salary: You earn a regular salary from an employer.
	+ Self-Employment: You earn income from your own business or side hustle.
	+ Other: Other sources of income, such as alimony, inheritance, or investment.
