# Small Business Loan Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Small Business Loan Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential information from small business owners who are interested in inquiring about a loan. This form helps the loan officer to assess the borrower's eligibility and suitability for a loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. For any optional fields, provide as much detail as you feel necessary.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Business Information**
* **Business Information** (`business_info`, text, optional): This field is meant to capture a brief description of your business, including its name, location, and industry. Please provide a clear and concise answer to help the loan officer understand your business.

### 2. **Loan Term**
* **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, select_one, optional): This field allows you to select a loan term from the options provided. Please choose the loan term that best suits your business needs.

*   Option: Six Months
*   Option: Twelve Months
*   Option: Twenty Four Months

### 3. **Loan Amount**
* **Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, number, optional): This field is meant to capture the loan amount you are seeking. Please provide a specific number to help the loan officer understand your borrowing needs.

### 4. **What is the loan amount you are seeking?**
* **What is the loan amount you are seeking?** (`loan_amount_hint`, text, optional): This field is a confirmation or additional explanation of your loan amount. Please provide more details to help the loan officer understand your needs.

### 5. **Business Owner Name**
* **Business Owner Name** (`business_owner_name`, text, optional): This field is meant to capture the name of the business owner. Please provide the name of the person who will be responsible for the loan.

### 6. **Business Address**
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): This field is meant to capture the address of your business. Please provide the physical address where your business is located.

### 7. **Contact Phone**
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is meant to capture the contact phone number of the person filling out this form. Please provide a phone number that the loan officer can use to contact you.

### 8. **Contact Email**
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is meant to capture the contact email of the person filling out this form. Please provide a valid email address that the loan officer can use to contact you.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and concise as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact the loan officer directly.
* Please ensure that you have all necessary documentation and information ready before submitting this form.
