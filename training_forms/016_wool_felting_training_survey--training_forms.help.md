# wool_felting_training_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from trainees after attending a wool felting training session. It helps the training team to evaluate the effectiveness of the session and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name as the trainer and the date of the training session.
2. Rate your satisfaction level with the training session.
3. Provide any feedback or suggestions you may have for the training.
4. Enter the session length of the training (in hours).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trainer Name** (Trainer Name, text, required): Enter your name as the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Training Date** (Training Date, date, required): Enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **Session Length** (Session Length, number, required): Enter the length of the session (in hours).
* **Feedback** (Feedback, text, optional): Share any feedback or suggestions you may have for the training.
* **Satisfaction** (Satisfaction, select multiple, optional): Rate your satisfaction level with the training (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Suggestions** (Suggestions, text, optional): Provide any additional suggestions for the training.
