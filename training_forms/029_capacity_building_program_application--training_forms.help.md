# Capacity Building Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Capacity Building Program Application form is designed to gather information about your personal and professional background, educational qualifications, and motivations for applying to the program. This information will help us understand your fit for the program and guide our selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in English (all text fields require English language).
2. Be truthful and accurate when providing information.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
4. Take your time and review your answers before submitting the application.
5. If you need help, contact [your support email/number].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Capacity Building Program Application --** (Note): This is the title header for the form.
* **Apply now for our comprehensive capacity building program. This application helps us understand your background and program goals.** (Note): A brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Gender** (`gender`, optional): Select your gender.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, required/optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State/Province** (`state`, required/optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Country** (`country`, required/optional): Enter your country.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`highest_education`, required/optional): Select your highest level of education.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, optional): Enter your area of study.
* **Educational Institution** (`institution`, optional): Enter your school or university name.
* **Current Position/Title** (`current_position`, required/optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Current Organization** (`organization`, required/optional): Enter your current company or organization name.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_experience`, required/optional): Enter your total years of professional experience.
* **Primary Work Sector** (`sector`, required/optional): Select your primary work sector.
* **Key Skills and Competencies** (`key_skills`, required/optional): Describe your main skills.
* **Which capacity building program are you applying for?** (`program_choice`, required/optional): Select the program you are applying for.
* **Preferred Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to start the program.
* **Available Time Commitment** (`program_duration`, required/optional): Select your available time commitment.
* **Why are you applying for this program?** (`motivation`, required/optional): Describe your motivation for applying.
* **What do you hope to achieve through this program?** (`goals`, required/optional): Describe your goals.
* **How will you apply what you learn?** (`expected_outcomes`, required/optional): Describe how you will use the knowledge gained from the program.
