# Hospitality Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Hospitality Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's knowledge and understanding of essential hospitality procedures, including staff assessment, procedure knowledge, and emergency evacuation procedures. This assessment is crucial for ensuring that staff members have the necessary skills and knowledge to perform their roles effectively and provide excellent service in a hotel or hospitality setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully and ensure you understand the questions and requirements.
2.  Answer all required questions (indicated by "required: true").
3.  Provide accurate and honest answers to each question.
4.  If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for entering general information, such as your name, contact details, or any other relevant information.
* **Staff Assessment** (`staff_assessment`, number, optional)
	+ This field is for indicating your level of experience or confidence in performing staff assessments. Choose a number between 1 and 10 to reflect your level of understanding.
* **Procedure Knowledge** (`procedure_knowledge`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ This field is for indicating your level of knowledge about different procedures. Choose all the procedures that you are familiar with, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Procedure Performance** (`procedure_performance`, select_one, optional)
	+ This field is for indicating your ability to perform procedures. Choose the procedure that you are most comfortable performing, such as "Yes" or "No".
* **Emergency Evacuation** (`emergency_evacuation`, note, optional)
	+ This field is for providing additional information about emergency evacuation procedures, such as any relevant training or experience.
* **Fire Evacuation Plan** (`fire_evacuation_plan`, time, optional)
	+ This field is for recording the time you would take to evacuate the area during an emergency.
* **Emergency Procedure 1** (`emergency_procedure_1`, number, optional)
	+ This field is for indicating your level of confidence in performing the first emergency procedure.
* **Emergency Procedure 2** (`emergency_procedure_2`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for providing more information about the second emergency procedure, such as any specific steps or requirements.
