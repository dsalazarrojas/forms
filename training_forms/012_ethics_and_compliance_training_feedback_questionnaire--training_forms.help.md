# Ethics And Compliance Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from participants who have attended ethics and compliance training programs. It helps to gather information on the participant's perception of the training content, delivery, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your name as you would like it to appear on the feedback report.
2. Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
3. Describe the training program that you attended in as much detail as possible.
4. Select the date you attended the training.
5. Select the time you attended the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the feedback report.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`): Describe the training program that you attended in as much detail as possible.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`): Select the date you attended the training.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`): Select the time you attended the training.

## Tips

* Please provide specific details about your experience with the training program.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
