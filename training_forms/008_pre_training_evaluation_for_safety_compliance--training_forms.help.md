# pre-training-evaluation-for-safety-compliance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather general information and specific details related to pre-training evaluation for safety compliance. It is intended to be used to track and record progress, completion status, and other relevant information related to training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information, including any relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information, including a contact number.
3. If applicable, add your emergency contact information.
4. Select your current training status.
5. Answer any pre-training questions.
6. Record your training evaluations.
7. Add any additional comments or remarks.
8. Specify the emergency contact methods used.
9. Attach any relevant medical records.
10. Provide the completion status of your training.
11. Enter the date and time of completion.
12. Record the number of training hours completed.
13. Record the number of certification hours completed.
14. Specify your certification details.
15. Select the training level.
16. Record the date and time of completion.
17. Record the duration of the training completion.
18. Specify any additional training requirements.
19. Attach a copy of your training completion certificate, if available.
20. Evaluate your training experience.
21. Add any additional comments or remarks.
