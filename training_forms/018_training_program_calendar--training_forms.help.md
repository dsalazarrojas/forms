## training_program_calendar - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants to schedule and manage training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **schedule training** field with a specific date and time for the training program.
2. Enter your **participant information** in the email format.
3. Choose the **training sessions** you are interested in participating in. You can select multiple sessions.
4. Provide your **participant details** in the time format. This field is required.
5. Add any additional **notes** about your participation in the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **schedule_training (Required)** (`1`, `number`, true): Enter the date and time for the training program.
* **participant_info (Required)** (`2`, `email`, true): Enter your email address.
* **training_sessions (Optional)** (`3`, `select_multiple`, false): Select the training sessions you are interested in participating in. Choose one or more options from the list.
* **participant_details (Required)** (`4`, `time`, true): Provide your participant details in the time format.
* **notes (Optional)** (`5`, `note`, false): Add any additional notes about your participation in the training program.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all required fields.
- Be specific with your schedule and training sessions preferences.
- Enter your participant details accurately in the time format.
