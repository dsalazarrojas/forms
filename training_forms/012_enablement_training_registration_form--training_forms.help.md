# Enablement Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enablement Training Registration Form is designed to gather information from trainees for the purpose of tracking and managing training sessions. This form helps to ensure that trainees' information is up to date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

- Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
- Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you regarding the training.
- Enter your Phone number so we can get in touch with you.
- Select the correct Training Program that you are registering for.
- Choose the Training Date and Training Time that suits you.
- Provide the Location where the training will take place.
- If necessary, add any notes regarding your training in the Note field.
- Select the Training Status: Active or Inactive, depending on your current status.
- Choose the Enablement Status: Active or Inactive, based on your current status.
- Select the Assigned Tool that you are working with.
- Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the training.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, `select_one`, required): Select the training program that you are registering for.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, `time`, required): Select the time for the training session.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the training will take place.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required): Add any relevant notes regarding your training.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current Training Status: Active or Inactive.
* **Enablement Status** (`enablement_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your current Enablement Status: Active or Inactive.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool assigned to you.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Click the Submit button to complete the form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): The date and time you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, required): The time you submitted the form.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, `text`, required): The person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): The person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `text`, required): The person to whom the form was submitted.
* **Enablement Status Label** (`enablement_status_label`, `text`, required): Enter a label for your current Enablement Status.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, required): Enter a unique ID for the form.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required): Enter the category of the form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required): Enter the output file.
* **Assigned Tool Name** (`assigned_tool_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the form.
