# Lockout/Tagout Procedures Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help train individuals on the procedures for performing lockout/tagout tasks in a safe and controlled manner. It guides users through the process of completing a lockout/tagout procedure, ensuring that all necessary steps are taken to prevent accidents and ensure the safety of personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Session ID field with the ID of the training session you are participating in.
2. Enter your Employee ID in the Employee ID field.
3. Enter the date and time you tagged out the equipment in the Tagout Date and Tagout Time fields.
4. Enter the date and time you locked out the equipment in the Lockout Date and Lockout Time fields.
5. Fill in the Tagout Person field with the name of the person who performed the tagout.
6. For the Supervisor Checklist, select one of the three options provided, which indicate whether the equipment was properly locked out with all safety locks engaged, was not properly locked out with all safety locks engaged, or was properly locked out but one or more safety locks were not fully engaged.
7. If you are the person performing the lockout/tagout, enter your name in the Lockout Person field.
8. Enter any supervisor's notes in the Supervisor Notes field.
9. Record the completion date and time of the procedure in the Completion Date and Completion Time fields.
10. Finally, enter the Tagout Tag number in the Tagout Tag field.
11. The Supervisor Approvals field allows you to indicate whether the supervisor has approved the procedure. Select 'True' if approved, and 'False' if not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session ID** (Session ID, number, required): Enter the ID of the training session you are participating in.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Tagout Date** (Tagout Date, date, required): Enter the date the equipment was tagged out.
* **Tagout Time** (Tagout Time, time, required): Enter the time the equipment was tagged out.
* **Lockout Date** (Lockout Date, date, required): Enter the date the equipment was locked out.
* **Lockout Time** (Lockout Time, time, required): Enter the time the equipment was locked out.
* **Tagout Person** (Tagout Person, text, required): Enter the name of the person who performed the tagout.
* **Supervisor Checklist** (Supervisor Checklist, select_one, required): Select one of the three options to indicate whether the equipment was properly locked out with all safety locks engaged, not properly locked out with all safety locks engaged, or was properly locked out but one or more safety locks were not fully engaged.
* **Lockout Person** (Lockout Person, text, optional): Enter the name of the person performing the lockout.
* **Supervisor Notes** (Supervisor Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes from the supervisor.
* **Completion Date** (Completion Date, date, optional): Enter the date the procedure was completed.
* **Completion Time** (Completion Time, time, optional): Enter the time the procedure was completed.
* **Tagout Tag** (Tagout Tag, text, optional): Enter the tagout tag number.
* **Supervisor Approvals** (Supervisor Approvals, select_multiple, required): Select 'True' to indicate that the supervisor has approved the procedure, 'False' if not.

Tips:
* Make sure to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that all procedures are followed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with your supervisor or instructor.
* Double-check the dates and times entered for accuracy.
* Ensure that all fields are completed before submitting the form.
