# Pre Training Evaluation For Safety Compliance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Training Evaluation For Safety Compliance form is designed to gather information about an employee's safety knowledge, training history, and concerns related to safety compliance. This form aims to assess the employee's understanding and preparedness for upcoming safety training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3.  Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Employee Information
* **Employee Information** (`employee_info`, text, required: false): This field is for entering the employee's information, such as their name, ID number, or other relevant details.

### 2. Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): In this field, you should enter the name and contact information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

### 3. Training Details
* **Training Details** (`training_details`, text, required: false): Provide any relevant details about the training session, such as the date, time, and location.

### 4. Safety Knowledge
* **Safety Knowledge** (`safety_knowledge`, text, required: false): This field is for sharing any relevant safety knowledge or questions you have.

### 5. Incident Reporting
* **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, text, required: false): Report any incidents or near-miss incidents that you have experienced.

### 6. Safety Training
* **Safety Training** (`safety_training`, text, required: false): Provide information about your safety training history or any upcoming training sessions.

### 7. Training History
* **Training History** (`training_history`, text, required: false): Share any relevant information about your past training experiences.

### 8. Training Status
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, text, required: false): Report your current status regarding safety training, such as whether you are certified or not.

### 9. Safety Concerns
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, text, required: false): Identify any safety concerns or worries you may have.

### 10. Safety Concerns Response
* **Safety Concerns Response** (`safety_concerns_response`, text, required: false): Share your response to the safety concerns you listed.

### 11. Training Comments
* **Training Comments** (`training_comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about the training session.

### 12. Training Suggestions
* **Training Suggestions** (`training_suggestions`, text, required: false): Suggest any improvements or changes you would like to see in future training sessions.

Note: This form is for internal use only, and the information collected will be used to assess and improve the employee's safety knowledge and preparedness.
