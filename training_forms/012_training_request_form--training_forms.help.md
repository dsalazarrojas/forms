# Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees to request training for personal and professional development. This form is used to collect information from employees about their training needs, preferences, and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Your Information**: Fill in the "Employee" field with your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
2. Save your progress and return to the form if needed.
3. **Choose Your Training Type**: Select the reason for your training request from the dropdown menu. Please note that "Other" allows you to enter your own reason.
4. **Schedule Your Training**: Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the training. If you select "Other" under Training Type, you will not be able to schedule a specific date and time.
5. **Add Comments (Optional)**: If you have any additional information or comments about your training request, please include them in the "Comments" field.
6. **Submit**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click the submit button to send your training request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee
* **Employee**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.

### Job Title
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.

### Training Type
* **Training Type**: Select the reason for your training request. This can be:
	+ Training for new hire
	+ Training for promotion
	+ Other (if you have a different reason not listed)

### Training Date
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the training.

### Training Time
* **Training Time**: Enter the time you would like to schedule the training.

### Reason for Requesting Training
* **Reason for Requesting Training**: Enter a brief explanation of why you are requesting training. This is optional, but it can help us understand your goals and tailor the training to your needs.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool**: You do not need to select a tool. This field is optional and not used in this form.

### Comments
* **Comments**: If you have any additional information or comments about your training request, please include them here.
