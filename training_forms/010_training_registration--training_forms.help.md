# Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information for training purposes from participants. It is used to track and record relevant details about the training, including date and time, contact information, training specifics, and payment information. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by entering the basic information about yourself.
2. Then, provide your contact details, including any relevant phone numbers and email addresses.
3. Enter the date and time of the training session.
4. Provide details about the training you wish to register for, such as its description, start date, and duration.
5. If payment information is required, enter it here.
6. Enter any additional information or notes you would like to provide.
7. Confirm your intention to submit this form.
8. Finally, if there are any additional details or comments you wish to add, enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Info (1)**: Enter your name and any other basic information you would like to share.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your contact phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Date and Time (3)**: Enter the date and time of the training session.
* **Training Details (4)**: Enter a description of the training, along with its start date and duration.
* **Event Details (5)**: Enter any additional details about the training event.
* **Payment Info (6)**: Enter any payment information required for the training.
* **Confirm Info (7)**: Confirm that the information provided is correct.
* **Submit Info (8)**: Enter any information about submitting this form.
* **Notes (9)**: Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to add.
* **Confirm Submit (10)**: Confirm that you are ready to submit this form.
* **Additional Info (11)**: Enter any additional information you would like to share.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- Use clear and concise language for all entries.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the training team for assistance.
