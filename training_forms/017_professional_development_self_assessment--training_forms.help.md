# Professional Development Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Self Assessment form is designed to help employees and managers evaluate an employee's professional development needs. It is intended to be used as a tool to identify areas for growth and improvement, and to provide a framework for creating a tailored professional development plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter your manager's name in the "manager_name" field.
3. In the "skills_evaluated" field, describe your current skills and experience.
4. In the "strengths_identified" field, list your strengths and areas of expertise.
5. In the "development_goals" field, specify your professional development goals and objectives.
6. In the "training_recommendations" field, suggest any training or development opportunities you would like to pursue.
7. In the "training_topics" field, provide any relevant training or development topics you are interested in.
8. In the "performance_discussions" field, discuss any performance issues or concerns you would like to address.
9. In the "comments" field, provide any additional comments or feedback from you or your manager.
10. Click the "submit" button to submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on official records.
* **manager_name** (text, required): Enter your manager's name as it appears on official records.
* **skills_evaluated** (text, required): Describe your current skills and experience. This can include technical skills, soft skills, or areas of expertise.
* **strengths_identified** (text, required): List your strengths and areas of expertise. Be specific and provide examples.
* **development_goals** (text, required): Specify your professional development goals and objectives. Be specific and measurable.
* **training_recommendations** (text, required): Suggest any training or development opportunities you would like to pursue. Be specific and provide examples.
* **training_topics** (text, required): Provide any relevant training or development topics you are interested in. Be specific and provide examples.
* **performance_discussions** (text, required): Discuss any performance issues or concerns you would like to address. Be specific and provide examples.
* **comments** (text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback from you or your manager. Be specific and provide examples.
* **submit** (text, required): Click this button to submit your assessment.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your assessment.
* Be specific and provide concrete examples whenever possible.
* Use a standard font and formatting to ensure readability.
* Proofread your assessment before submitting it.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact your HR department or supervisor.
