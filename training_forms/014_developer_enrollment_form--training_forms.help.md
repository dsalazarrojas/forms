The purpose of this form is to collect information from developers who wish to enroll in a program or partnership. This form is designed to guide users through the process of providing their details, which will be used to facilitate the enrollment process.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Select the program(s) you are interested in from the "Developer Program" dropdown.
5. Choose your partnership option(s) from the "Partnership" dropdown.
6. Select the access type(s) from the "Access Type" dropdown.

## Developer Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Developer Enrollment Form is used to collect information from developers who wish to enroll in a program or partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as a developer.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address to enable communication with you about your enrollment.
* **Contact Number (Text, Required)**: Enter your contact number to receive updates and notifications.
* **Developer Program (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select the program(s) you are interested in enrolling in.
* **Partnership (Select One, Required)**: Choose your partnership option to associate yourself with a particular partnership.
* **Access Type (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select the access type(s) you need to complete the enrollment process.
