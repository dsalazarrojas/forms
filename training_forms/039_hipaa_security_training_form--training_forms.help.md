# HIPAA Security Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HIPAA Security Training Form is used to record the completion of HIPAA security training for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name and Employee Email.
2. Enter the date of the training session.
3. Select the location where the training was held.
4. Choose the topics that were covered during the training session.
5. Enter the number of hours spent on the training session.
6. Mark the completion status of the training (Completed or Not Completed).
7. Provide any comments related to the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name (<type:text>):** Enter the name of the employee who completed the training.
* **Employee Email (<type:email>):** Enter the email of the employee who completed the training.
* **Training Date (<type:date>):** Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Location (<type:text>):** Enter the location where the training session was held.
* **Training Topics (<type:select_multiple>):** Select all the topics that were covered during the training session:
	+ Security and Privacy
	+ HIPAA
	+ Compliance and Data
	+ Data and Information Security
	+ Data Protection
	+ Data Breaches
	+ Electronic Health Records
	+ Electronic Medical Records
* **Training Hours (<type:number>):** Enter the number of hours spent on the training session.
* **Training Completed (<type:select_one>):** Mark the completion status of the training (Completed or Not Completed).
* **Comments (<type:note>):** Provide any comments related to the training session.
