# Training Topic Suggestions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for suggesting new training topics for our training program. It allows you to propose new ideas and help us identify areas that need more knowledge sharing and skill development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Topic" field with a clear and concise description of your suggested training topic.
2. Decide whether the suggested topic falls under the "topic_format" category ("Yes" or "No").
3. Select all relevant "participant_interest" categories that apply to your suggested topic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Topic** (`page_1`, text, required): A short description of your suggested training topic.
	+ This field should be clear and concise, providing a brief overview of the topic you're suggesting.
* **Topic Format** (`topic_format`, select_one, required: false): Indicates whether the suggested topic is in the format of a presentation, workshop, or other type of training format.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you're suggesting a presentation, workshop, or other type of training format.
	+ Choose "No" if the suggested topic is not in a training format.
* **Participant Interest** (`participant_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all relevant categories that apply to your suggested topic.
	+ This field helps us understand which areas of our participants are interested in and can benefit from the suggested topic.

## Tips
* Be concise in your topic description.
* Only suggest topics that are new and have not been suggested before.
* If you have more information or supporting resources, please provide them along with your suggestion.
