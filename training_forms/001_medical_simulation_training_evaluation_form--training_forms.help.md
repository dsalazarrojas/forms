# Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation data on medical simulation training sessions. It aims to gather information on the training experience, overall satisfaction, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the simulation date.
2. Fill in the simulation start and evaluation start times.
3. Provide your name and the name of the instructor.
4. Describe the simulation session, including its goals and type.
5. Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the training session.
6. Indicate whether you will participate in another simulation session.
7. Provide feedback on your experience, including any comments on the training session.
8. Finally, add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Simulation Date**: Enter the date the simulation was conducted.
* **Simulation Start Time**: Enter the time the simulation began.
* **Evaluation Start Time**: Enter the time evaluation of the training session started.
* **Trainee Name**: This is your name.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the training session.
* **Training Session Notes**: Describe the simulation session, including its goals and type.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training session was held.
* **Simulation Goals**: Describe the goals of the training session.
* **Simulation Type**: Select the type of simulation that was conducted (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Evaluation Comments**: Provide your overall comments on the training session.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the training session (e.g., Option 4, Option 5, or Option 6).
* **Will Participate Again**: Indicate whether you will participate in another simulation session (e.g., Option 7, Option 8, or Option 9).
* **Trainee Feedback**: Provide any feedback on your experience.
* **Instructor Feedback**: Provide feedback on the instructor's performance.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or suggestions.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form.
* Provide clear and concise comments where applicable.
* Use the options provided for select-one fields as a guide for your responses.
* If you are unsure of a field, ask for clarification or leave it blank if not applicable.
