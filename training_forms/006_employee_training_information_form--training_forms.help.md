# employee_training_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_training_information_form" is used to collect information about an employee's training details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the "company" field with the company name where the training took place or is scheduled.
* Enter the "training date" when the training was held or is scheduled.
* Provide the "trainer name" in the "trainer name" field.
* Enter the duration of the training in the "training duration" field.
* Choose the "training status" of the training (e.g., completed, pending, cancelled).
* Enter the employee's "employee id" number (if applicable).
* Provide the training location in the "training location" field.
* Enter the training topic in the "training topic" field.
* Enter any notes about the training in the "training notes" field.
* Fill out the "manager comments" field with any additional comments from the manager.
* Choose the "training attendance" status (e.g., attended, absent, unknown).
* Select the "training evaluation" (e.g., good, average, poor).
* Choose the "training recommendation" (e.g., recommended, not recommended).
* Read and agree to the "employee agreement" and "training agreement" (if applicable).
* Sign the "employee signature" field with your digital signature.
* Sign the "manager signature" field with the manager's digital signature.
* Sign the "trainer signature" field with the trainer's digital signature.
* Optional fields:
	+ "training start time" - Enter the start time of the training.
	+ "training end time" - Enter the end time of the training.
	+ "training end" - Enter the date and time the training ended.
	+ "duplicate id" - This field is for internal use and should not be filled out.
	+ "final field" - This field is for internal use and should not be filled out.
	+ "date of training" - Enter the date of the training.
	+ "training end" - This field is not clearly defined and seems to be a duplicate of the "training end time" field. It is optional and should not be filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **company** (text, required): Enter the company name where the training took place or is scheduled.
* **training date** (date, required): Enter the date of the training.
* **trainer name** (text, required): Provide the name of the trainer.
* **training duration** (number, required): Enter the duration of the training in minutes or hours.
* **training time** (time, required): Enter the start and end time of the training.
* **training status** (select multiple, required): Choose the status of the training (e.g., completed, pending, cancelled).
* **employee id** (number, required): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **training location** (text, required): Provide the location of the training.
* **training topic** (text, required): Enter the topic of the training.
* **training notes** (text, required): Enter any notes about the training.
* **manager comments** (text, required): Fill out any additional comments from the manager.
* **training attendance** (select one, required): Choose the attendance status (e.g., attended, absent, unknown).
* **training evaluation** (select one, required): Select the evaluation of the training (e.g., good, average, poor).
* **training recommendation** (select one, required): Choose the recommendation status (e.g., recommended, not recommended).
* **employee agreement** (note, required): Read and agree to the employee agreement.
* **training agreement** (note, required): Read and agree to the training agreement.
* **employee signature** (text, required): Sign the form with your digital signature.
* **manager signature** (text, required): Sign the form with the manager's digital signature.
* **trainer signature** (text, required): Sign the form with the trainer's digital signature.
* **date of training** (date, required): Enter the date of the training.
* **training start time** (time, optional): Enter the start time of the training.
* **training end time** (time, optional): Enter the end time of the training.
* **training end** (text, optional): This field is not clearly defined and seems to be a duplicate of the "training end time" field. It is optional and should not be filled out.
* **duplicate id** (text, optional): This field is for internal use and should not be filled out.
* **final field** (text, optional): This field is for internal use and should not be filled out.

Note: The optional fields marked as "internal use" should not be filled out by end-users. They are for internal use only.
