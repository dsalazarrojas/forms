# hair_product_brand_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hair_product_brand_training_registration_form" is used to register and train staff on new hair and beauty products from various brands. This form is essential for the sales team to have accurate and up-to-date information about the products, their features, and benefits.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the required information.
2.  Fill in your details in the "User Details" section.
3.  Enter the product information in the "Product Information" section.
4.  Provide your contact information in the "Contact Us" section.
5.  Confirm your submission in the "Confirm Submission" section.
6.  Select the training program you would like to enroll in the "Training Program" section.
7.  Select the type of hair product in the "Product Type" section.
8.  Enter the product name and description in the "Product Name" and "Product Description" sections.
9.  Enter the product price and weight in the "Price" and "Product Weight" sections.
10.  Select the product color and size in the "Product Color" and "Product Size" sections.
11.  Enter the product brand and country of origin in the "Product Brand" and "Product Country Of Origin" sections.
12.  Enter the product manufacturer information in the "Product Manufacturer" section.
