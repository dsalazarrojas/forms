# Staff Training Content Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Training Content Evaluation Form is a tool used to evaluate the effectiveness and quality of training sessions. Its purpose is to help staff provide feedback and identify areas of improvement to enhance the overall experience of attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Details** section with the title and any other relevant information about the session you attended.
2. Enter the name of the **Instructor** who conducted the training session.
3. Enter the date of the training **Training Date** when you attended the session.
4. Choose your rating on the **Evaluation Scale** from the available options to indicate how well the session met your expectations.
5. Provide your **Overall Rating** by typing in a number from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
6. Select all applicable **Session Topics** that were covered during the session from the list of options.
7. Enter any additional **Feedback** about the session in the text box, providing specific comments or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details**: 
	+ This field is for entering a brief description of the session, including its title, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Instructor Name**: 
	+ Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the session.
* **Training Date**: 
	+ Enter the date when you attended the training session.
* **Evaluation Scale**: 
	+ Choose a rating from 1 to 3 to indicate how well the session met your expectations. A rating of 1 means the session fell short of expectations, 2 means it met some expectations, and 3 means it exceeded expectations.
* **Overall Rating**: 
	+ Enter a number from 1 to 5 to indicate your overall rating of the session, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Session Topics**: 
	+ Select all topics that were covered during the session from the list of available options.
* **Feedback**: 
	+ Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for the session.
