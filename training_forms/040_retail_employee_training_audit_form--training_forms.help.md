# Retail Employee Training Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track employee training and evaluations. It helps ensure that employee training is up-to-date and compliant with organizational requirements. The form is used by trainers and supervisors to record and evaluate employee training, including topics covered, dates, locations, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the required information:
	* Enter your Employee ID.
	* Fill in your Employee Name.
	* If applicable, select your Employee Title.
2. Enter the details of the training:
	* Provide the Training Date.
	* Enter the Training Location.
	* Specify the Training Topic.
3. Record the training outcome:
	* Fill in the Training Outcome.
4. Evaluate the training:
	* Enter the Evaluation Date.
	* Specify who Evaluated By.
5. Add any additional notes:
	* Enter any relevant Notes regarding the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID (`employee_id`)
Employee ID: Enter your employee identification number.

### Employee Name (`employee_name`)
Employee Name: Enter your name.

### Employee Title (`employee_title`)
Employee Title: If applicable, enter your job title.

### Training Date (`training_date`)
Training Date: Enter the date of the training.

### Training Location (`training_location`)
Training Location: Enter the location of the training.

### Training Topic (`training_topic`)
Training Topic: Specify the topic(s) covered during the training.

### Training Outcome (`training_outcome`)
Training Outcome: Describe the result or outcome of the training.

### Evaluation Date (`evaluation_date`)
Evaluation Date: Enter the date you evaluated the training.

### Evaluated By (`evaluation_by`)
Evaluated By: Specify who evaluated the training.

### Notes (`notes`)
Notes: Enter any additional information or comments about the training.

### Email (`email`)
Email: Enter your email address.
