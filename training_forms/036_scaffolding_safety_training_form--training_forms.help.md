# scaffolding_safety_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scaffolding safety training form is a document used to collect information and verify participant experience and certification for scaffolding safety training. This form helps ensure that participants have completed the required training and meet the necessary certification standards for scaffolding safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
* Review and confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* If necessary, select the relevant options for scaffolding license, training certification, and other specific details.
* Provide your signature at the end to confirm that the information is true and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Text): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (Text): Provide your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Participant Experience** (Text): Describe your relevant experience working with scaffolding or similar equipment.
* **Course Completed** (Text): List the name and date of the course you completed.
* **Certification Info** (Text): Enter any relevant certification information, such as the type and date of certification.
* **Safety Training Completed** (Text): Describe the safety training you have completed.
* **Participant Declaration** (Text): Make a declaration about your scaffolding experience and certification.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Text): List the contact information for emergency contacts.
* **Participant Agreement** (Text): Confirm that you have read and understand the training and certification requirements.
* **Course Completion Date** (Date): Enter the date you completed the course.
* **Scaffolding License** (Select One): Indicate if you have a scaffolding license.
* **Scaffolding Training Certification** (Select Multiple): Choose your relevant training certification.
* **Participant Signature** (Text): Sign your name to confirm the information provided.
* **Instructor Signature** (Text): Sign your name as the instructor or course provider.
* **Course Instructor** (Text): Enter your name if you are the instructor.
* **Participant Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Instructor Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Training Date** (Date): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Course Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Certification Date** (Date): Enter the date of your certification.
* **Expiration Date** (Date): Enter the expiration date of your certification.
* **Training Duration** (Text): Enter the duration of the training session.
* **Course Location** (Text): Enter the location of the course.
* **Notes** (Text): Add any additional information about the course or your experience.
* **Participant Company** (Text): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Course Provider** (Text): Enter the name of the course provider.
* **Training Provider** (Text): Enter the name of the training provider.
* **Course Type** (Text): Enter the type of course you are completing.
* **Scaffolding Type** (Text): Enter the type of scaffolding you are working with.
* **Scaffolding Category** (Text): Enter the category of scaffolding.
* **Scaffolding Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the scaffolding.
* **Scaffolding Model** (Text): Enter the model of the scaffolding.
* **Scaffolding Brand** (Text): Enter the brand of the scaffolding.
* **Scaffolding Model Year** (Text): Enter the year of the scaffolding model.

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your instructor or trainer for guidance.
