# Construction Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Safety Training Form is used to document and track safety training sessions for construction workers. It helps ensure that employees receive the necessary safety training to perform their jobs safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Use the "Training Date" field to enter the date of the training session.
3. Choose the correct "Training Type" from the provided options.
4. Enter the name of the trainer or instructor who provided the training.
5. Enter the name of the company conducting the training.
6. Enter the name of the project (if applicable).
7. Choose the correct "Training Duration" from the provided options.
8. Enter the name of the participant(s) who attended the training.
9. Enter the participant's employee ID (if available).
10. Enter the participant's company (if different from the training company).
11. Enter the participant's job title.
12. Choose the participant's years of experience in the construction industry.
13. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Previous Safety Training" (have you had similar training before).
14. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Additional Training Needed" (what other training do you need).
15. Choose the topics covered during the training from the provided options (select all that apply).
16. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Hands-On Component" (was practical training included).
17. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Training Materials Provided" (were handouts or materials given).
18. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Comprehension Check" (was understanding verified).
19. Enter the percentage score (if test was given).
20. Rate the training's effectiveness and the trainer's effectiveness.
21. Answer "Yes" or "No" for "Certification Earned" (did you receive certification).
22. Enter the certification expiry date (if applicable).
23. Enter any additional training needed.
24. Enter participant feedback or comments about the training.
25. Enter full name as signature.
26. Enter full name as signature for the trainer.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Date**: Date of the training session
* **Training Type**: Type of safety training, choose from: New Employee Orientation, OSHA 10-Hour, OSHA 30-Hour, Fall Protection, Scaffolding Safety, Excavation and Trenching, Electrical Safety, Hazard Communication, Lockout Tagout, Confined Space, Crane and Rigging, Forklift Operation, Fire Safety, First Aid and CPR, Toolbox Talk, Other
* **Trainer Name**: Name of the trainer or instructor
* **Trainer Credentials**: Qualifications or certification of the trainer
* **Company Name**: Company conducting the training
* **Project Name**: Associated project (if applicable)
* **Training Location**: Where the training was held
* **Training Duration**: Length of the training session, choose from: Less than 1 hour, 1 to 2 hours, 2 to 4 hours, 4 to 8 hours, Full Day (8+ hours), Multi-Day
* **Participant Name**: Trainee full name
* **Participant Employee ID**: Participant's employee number (if available)
* **Participant Company**: Participant's company name (if different from the training company)
* **Participant Job Title**: Participant's position or role
* **Years of Experience**: Participant's years of experience in the construction industry
* **Previous Safety Training**: Have you had similar training before, choose from: Yes, Within 1 Year, Yes, 1 to 3 Years Ago, Yes, More Than 3 Years Ago, No, First Time
* **Additional Training Needed**: What other training do you need
* **Topics Covered**: Select all that apply, choose from: Hazard Recognition, PPE Requirements, Emergency Procedures, Equipment Safety, Fall Protection, Electrical Safety, Excavation Safety, Material Handling, Fire Prevention, First Aid, Other
* **Hands-On Component**: Was practical training included, choose from: Yes, No
* **Training Materials Provided**: Were handouts or materials given, choose from: Yes, Basic, No
* **Comprehension Check**: Was understanding verified, choose from: Yes, No, Written Test, Oral Quiz, Practical Demonstration, None
* **Test Score**: Percentage score (if test was given)
* **Training Effectiveness**: Rate the training effectiveness, choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Trainer Effectiveness**: Rate the trainer's effectiveness, choose from: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor
* **Certification Earned**: Did you receive certification, choose from: Yes, No, Not Applicable
* **Certification Expiry Date**: When does certification expire (if applicable)
* **Additional Training Needed**: What other training do you need
* **Participant Comments**: Any feedback on the training
* **Participant Signature**: Type full name as signature
* **Trainer Signature**: Type full name as signature
