# Internal Training Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to measure the effectiveness of the internal training sessions. It is designed to gather feedback from participants about the usefulness, relevance, and skills gained from the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about the usefulness and relevance of the training session.
2. Indicate whether you gained any new skills as a result of the training.
3. Provide any comments or feedback about the training session.
4. If you would like to receive a follow-up email or phone call, select "yes" for email confirmation.
5. Choose the method you would prefer to receive a follow-up email confirmation, e.g., email, SMS, or no contact.
6. You can add any additional comments or feedback about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 2. usefulness
This question measures how useful you found the training session.
*   **Usefulness** (number): On a scale from 1 to 10, how useful did you find the training session?

### 3. relevance
This question measures how relevant you found the training session to your work.
*   **Relevance** (number): On a scale from 1 to 10, how relevant did you find the training session?

### 4. satisfaction
This question is open-ended and allows you to provide any feedback about your overall satisfaction with the training session.
*   **Satisfaction**: Please provide any comments or feedback about your overall satisfaction with the training session.

### 5. skills_gained
This question asks if you gained any new skills as a result of the training session.
*   **Skills Gained?** (select one): Yes, No, Partial

### 6. skills_improvement_needed
This question asks if you think you need any improvement in skills as a result of the training session.
*   **Skills Improvement Needed?** (select one): Not at all, A lot, Somewhat

### 7. time_spend
This question asks you to estimate the time you spent on the training session.
*   **Time Spent** (number): Please estimate the number of hours you spent on the training session.

### 8. training_value
This question asks you to estimate the value of the training session.
*   **Training Value** (number): On a scale from 1 to 10, how valuable do you think the training session was?

### 9. comments
This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session.
*   **Comments**: Please provide any comments or feedback about the training session.

### 10. email
This question asks for your email address.
*   **Email**: Please enter your email address.

### 11. phone
This question asks for your phone number.
*   **Phone**: Please enter your phone number.

### 12. email_confirmation
This question asks if you would like to receive a follow-up email.
*   **Email Confirmation** (select one): Yes, No

### 13. email_confirmation_method
This question asks you to choose the method for email confirmation.
*   **Email Confirmation Method** (select one): Email, SMS, None

### 14. comments
This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session.
*   **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session.

### 15. additional_comments
This question allows you to provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session.
*   **Additional Comments**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback about the training session.

### 16. submit
This question allows you to choose how to proceed with your submission.
*   **Submit** (select multiple): Submit, Save Draft, Cancel
