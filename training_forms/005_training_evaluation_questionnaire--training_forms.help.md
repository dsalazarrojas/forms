# Training Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us evaluate the effectiveness of our training programs. Please take a few minutes to complete it, providing honest feedback on your experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the instructions below for guidance on each field.
2. Complete the form carefully, answering all the required fields (marked as "required" in the instructions below).
3. Enter your response in the field indicated (e.g., date in the "Date Completed" field).
4. Use the provided options for dropdown fields (e.g., "Will You Recommend This Training").
5. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, not required):
	+ Please describe the training program you recently completed.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required):
	+ Enter the date you completed the training program.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, not required):
	+ Enter the time you completed the training program.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required):
	+ Rate your overall experience with the training program (e.g., 1-5).
* **What Did You Find Most Valuable** (`what_did_you_find_most_valuable`, text, not required):
	+ Describe the aspects of the training program that you found most valuable or helpful.
* **What Did You Find Least Valuable** (`what_did_you_find_least_valuable`, text, not required):
	+ Describe the aspects of the training program that you found least valuable or challenging.
* **Suggestions For Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, not required):
	+ Share any suggestions or ideas for improving the training program.
* **Will You Recommend This Training** (`will_you_recommend_this_training`, select_one, required):
	+ Indicate if you will recommend this training program to others (True, False, or Maybe).
* **Will Recommend To Others** (`will_recommend_to_others`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Select all the categories of people you would recommend this training program to (e.g., colleagues, supervisors, clients).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required):
	+ Add any additional comments or feedback about the training program.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Provide specific examples or examples when possible.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on a field, contact [support contact information].
