# mental_health_first_aid_training_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health first aid training enrollment form is designed for participants to enroll in mental health first aid training sessions. The form collects information about the participant and their emergency contact to ensure a safe and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your participant information in the "Participant Information" field.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Select your preferred training session from the "Training Session" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your emergency contact's information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
5. Indicate your current status in the "Participant Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant participant information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, and any other contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_one, optional): Select the date, time, or duration of the training session you prefer.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name, address, and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current status in the training program, such as Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
