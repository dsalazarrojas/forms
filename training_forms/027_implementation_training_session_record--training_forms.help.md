# implementation-training-session-record-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and track information about implementation training sessions. It captures details about each session, including the session ID, name, date, time, topics covered, participants, feedback, notes, and the assigned trainer, consultants, and HR representatives.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, navigate to each field and follow the instructions below.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed and understand the form's purpose and limitations.
3. Fill in the fields as instructed, ensuring that you provide accurate and thorough information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session ID (session-id)
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, `number`, optional): Enter a unique ID for the training session.
*   Enter a unique ID for the training session to help track and identify it.

### Session Name (session-name)
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the training session.
*   Enter a descriptive name for the training session to help identify it.

### Session Date (session-date)
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the training session.
*   Enter the date of the training session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Session Time (session-time)
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the training session.
*   Enter the time of the training session in the format 12:00 PM.

### Topic 1 (topic-1)
* **Topic 1** (`topic_1`, `text`, optional): Enter the topic(s) covered in the training session.
*   Enter a brief description of the topic(s) covered in the session.

### Topic 2 (topic-2)
* **Topic 2** (`topic_2`, `text`, optional): Enter another topic covered in the training session.
*   Enter another brief description of the topic covered in the session.

### Topic 3 (topic-3)
* **Topic 3** (`topic_3`, `text`, optional): Enter another topic covered in the training session.
*   Enter another brief description of the topic covered in the session.

### Participants (participants)
* **Participants** (`participants`, `text`, optional): Enter the names of participants in the training session.
*   Enter the names of participants in the training session.

### Feedback (feedback)
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the training session.
*   Enter any feedback or comments about the session.

### Session Notes (session-notes)
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the training session.
*   Enter any additional notes or information about the session.

### Assigned Trainer (assigned-trainer)
* **Assigned Trainer** (`assigned_trainer`, `select_one`, optional): Select the assigned trainer for the training session.
*   Select the assigned trainer for the session from the provided options.

### Assigned Consultant (assigned-consultant)
* **Assigned Consultant** (`assigned_consultant`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the assigned consultant(s) for the training session.
*   Select the assigned consultant(s) for the session from the provided options.

### Assigned HR (assigned-hr)
* **Assigned HR** (`assigned_hr`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the assigned HR representative(s) for the training session.
*   Select the assigned HR representative(s) for the session from the provided options.

### Session Status (session-status)
* **Session Status** (`session_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the training session.
*   Select the status of the session from the provided options.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you complete all required fields.
* Enter accurate and thorough information.
* Review the form's purpose and limitations before completing it.

This guide will help you understand how to use this form effectively and efficiently. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please reach out to your supervisor or the training team.
