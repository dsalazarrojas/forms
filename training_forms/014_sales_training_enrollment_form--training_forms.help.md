# sales_training_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll participants in various types of training sessions. Please provide the required information for each participant to ensure successful enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the participant's information.
2. Select the type of training the participant is interested in (Sales, Leadership, Product Knowledge, or Other).
3. Choose the training session time (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
4. Enter the enrollment date for the selected training session.
5. Provide any additional participant notes or information in the "Participant Notes" field.
6. Optional: You can add the participant's company, title, and department information if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (<type>text, <required>True): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (<type>email, <required>True): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (<type>text, <required>True): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Training Type** (<type>select_one, <required>True): Select the type of training the participant is interested in (Sales, Leadership, Product Knowledge, or Other).
* **Training Session** (<type>select_one, <required>True): Select the time of the training session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Enrollment Date** (<type>date, <required>True): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Participant Notes** (<type>note, <required(False>): Provide any additional notes or information about the participant.
* **Participant Company** (<type>text, <required(False>): Enter the participant's company if available.
* **Participant Title** (<type>text, <required(False>): Enter the participant's title if available.
* **Participant Department** (<type>text, <required(False>): Enter the participant's department if available.
