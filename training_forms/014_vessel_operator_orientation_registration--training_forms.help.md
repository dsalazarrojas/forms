# Vessel Operator Orientation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vessel Operator Orientation Registration form is used for vessel operators to register for orientation, providing essential information such as their name, vessel details, and training history. This form helps the organization collect data for training purposes and ensures operators are properly trained for their role.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Vessel Operator Info" field, where you can enter your name and other relevant information.
2.  Select your vessel operator status ("Yes" or "No") in the "Vessel Operator" field.
3.  Enter the name of your vessel in the "Vessel Name" field.
4.  Provide your operator name in the "Operator Name" field.
5.  Indicate your operator role ("Yes" or "No") in the "Operator Role" field.
6.  Enter the date you completed your orientation training in the "Orientation Date" field.
7.  Select the start and end times for your training in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields.
8.  Enter the location of your training in the "Location" field.
9.  Provide any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Enter any follow-up information in the "Follow Up" field.
13. Select the type of vessel you operate ("Yes" or "No") in the "Vessel Type" field.
14. Enter your experience in operating vessels in the "Operator Experience" field.
15. Enter the size of the vessel in the "Vessel Size" field.
16. Enter your age in the "Operator Age" field.
17. Enter the length of the vessel in the "Vessel Length" field.
18. Enter the beam of the vessel in the "Vessel Beam" field.
19. Enter your height in the "Operator Height" field.
20. Enter your weight in the "Operator Weight" field.
21. Enter the draft of the vessel in the "Vessel Draft" field.
22. Select your sex ("Yes" or "No") in the "Operator Sex" field.
23. Select the hull material of your vessel ("Yes" or "No") in the "Vessel Hull Material" field.
24. Select the type of vessel certification you have ("Yes" or "No") in the "Vessel Type Certification" field.
