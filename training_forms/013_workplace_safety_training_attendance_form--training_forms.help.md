# Workplace Safety Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial tool for tracking employee attendance and feedback after a training session. It helps in evaluating the training's effectiveness and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Training Session Details**: Enter a brief description of the training session, such as its title, date, and time.
2. **Session Date**: Enter the date of the training session (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. **Session Time**: Enter the time of the training session (HH:MM).
4. **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or as you prefer to be addressed.
5. **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears in our system.
6. **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID number.
7. **Session Attended**: Select whether you attended the session ("Yes" or "No").
8. **Attended Session**: Confirm that you attended the session ("Yes" or "No").
9. **Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the training session.
10. **Supervisor Comments** (Optional): Enter any comments or concerns from your supervisor about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session Details**: This is a text field where you can briefly describe the training session.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the training session using the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the training session using the HH:MM format.
* **Employee Name**: This is the field where you can enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears in our system.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID number.
* **Session Attended**: Select "Yes" if you attended the session, and "No" if you did not.
* **Attended Session**: Confirm that you attended the session by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about the training session.
* **Supervisor Comments**: Enter any comments or concerns from your supervisor about the training session.
