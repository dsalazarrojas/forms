# Behavioral Finance Biases Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help learners understand and identify behavioral finance biases that can affect investment decisions. The quiz is a series of questions that assess your knowledge on the topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the quiz, simply select the best answer to each question and submit it. You can access the form on a computer or mobile device with internet access. Make sure to answer all the questions, as they are marked as required.
