# Retail Automation Training Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Retail Automation Training Program. It gathers essential information about the retailer, their store, and their training preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the retailer's information (fields 1-5).
2. Select the training date (field 6).
3. Select the training time (field 7).
4. Optionally, fill out store information (fields 8-11).
5. Select the training program preferences (field 18).
6. Add any additional notes (fields 24 and 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retailer** (`Retailer`, text, required): Please enter your name as the retailer.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Select the date of the training you are interested in.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): Select the time of the training you are interested in.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): If you have a preferred location, enter it here.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required): Enter your store's name.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): If you have an address, enter it here.
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, optional): If you have a store phone number, enter it here.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional): If you have store hours, enter them here.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your store manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, text, optional): Enter your store manager's email.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your store manager's phone number.
* **Manager Hours** (`manager_hours`, text, optional): If you have manager hours, enter them here.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your store manager's name.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_multiple, required): Check the training programs you are interested in (Yes, No).
* **Retail Automation Training Program** (`retail_automation_training_program_name`, text, optional): Enter your training program name.
* **Retail Automation Training Program Location** (`retail_automation_training_program_location`, text, optional): Enter your training program location.
* **Retail Automation Training Program Date** (`retail_automation_training_program_date`, date, optional): Enter your training program date.
* **Retail Automation Training Program Time** (`retail_automation_training_program_time`, time, optional): Enter your training program time.
* **Retail Automation Training Program Status** (`retail_automation_training_program_status`, text, optional): Enter your training program status.
* **Retail Automation Training Program Notes** (`retail_automation_training_program_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Additional Notes** (`retail_automation_training_program_additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the training program.
