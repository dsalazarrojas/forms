# learning_and_development_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning and development application form is used to collect application information for learning and development purposes. This form is intended for internal use within the organization and should only be completed by authorized personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal details in the `Applicant Details` page.
2.  List your training requests in the `Training Requests` page.
3.  Select the relevant options in the `Review` and `Submitted By` pages.
4.  Choose the training types in the `Training Type Select` page.
5.  Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Details** (Applicant Details, Text, Optional): This is the first page of the form where you need to provide your personal details.
- **Training Requests** (Training Requests, Text, Optional): In this page, list your training requests, if any.
- **Submission** (Submission, Text, Optional): This page is used to capture any additional information related to your submission.
- **Review** (Review, Select One, Optional): Select one of the options to indicate your review status.
- **Date of Submission** (Date of Submission, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your submission.
- **Submitted By** (Submitted By, Select One, Optional): Select one of the options to indicate who submitted the application.
- **Review Select** (Review Select, Select Multiple, Optional): Select multiple options to indicate your review status.
- **Training Type Select** (Training Type Select, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose multiple training types for your application.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
- Review each field carefully before submitting your application.
- This form should only be completed by authorized personnel.
- If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to the training team for assistance.
