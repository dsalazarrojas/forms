# workplace_learning_plan - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Learning Plan form is designed to gather important information about an employee's training needs, preferred learning methods, and training initiatives. This information is essential for creating a tailored learning plan that meets their individual requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the following questions to the best of your ability. You can skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Needs**: Describe your current training needs, such as skills or knowledge gaps you would like to address. Be as specific as possible to help us understand what you need help with.
* **Preferred Learning Methods**: Select the methods you prefer to learn new skills or knowledge, such as in-person training, online courses, or on-the-job training. You can choose multiple options.
* **Training Initiatives**: Use this text box to describe any specific training initiatives you are interested in or already participating in.
* **Employee ID**: This is an optional field to provide your employee ID number. However, please note that this field is not currently utilized in our system, so you can skip it for now.

## Tips
* Please be honest and as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to your manager or HR team.
* If you have already completed a similar form, please ensure that you are not duplicating the information. This will help us to avoid unnecessary work and ensure that we have the most up-to-date information about your training needs.
