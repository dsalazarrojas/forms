# Voyage Planning Navigation Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Voyage Planning Navigation Quiz is a form designed to help users assess their knowledge on various aspects of voyage planning, including passage planning, vessel speed, course reversal, route planning, chart reading, and position estimation. This quiz is intended to evaluate users' understanding of these essential navigation skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific topic related to voyage planning.
2. For each section, select all applicable answers from the provided options.
3. You may need to refer to the provided instructions or your prior knowledge to determine the correct answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passage Planning** (`passage_planning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable options from "Yes" or "No" to demonstrate your understanding of passage planning concepts.
* **Vessel Speed** (`vessel_speed`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the vessel speed in units of knots or kilometers per hour.
* **Course Reversal** (`course_reversal`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the correct answer from "Yes" or "No" to show your understanding of course reversal.
* **Route Planning** (`route_planning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable options from "Yes" or "No" to demonstrate your knowledge of route planning concepts.
* **Chart Reading** (`chart_reading`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief explanation or description of how you perform chart reading.
* **Position Estimation** (`position_estimation`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the estimated position of the vessel in decimal degrees or degrees, minutes, and seconds.
* **No other fields exist on this form**.

## Tips

- Answer each question carefully, as the quiz is designed to test your knowledge accurately.
- If you're unsure about any question, use your prior knowledge or the provided instructions to aid your decision.
- Review and submit the form once you've completed all the sections to evaluate your performance.
