# job_training_success_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the success of participants in a job training program. It helps to gather feedback on the effectiveness of the training and identifies areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the participant.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), which are:
	* Evaluation Date (\*)
	* Training Hours (\*)
3. Fill in the optional fields as needed:
	* Participant Name (optional)
	* Job Title (optional)
	* Training Program (optional)
	* Training Facilitator (optional)
	* Training Location (optional)
	* Participant Supervisor (optional)
	* Training Session (optional)
	* Evaluation Comments (optional)
	* Evaluation Recommendations (optional)
	* Participant Comments (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation. This should be the date on which the evaluation was conducted.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the participant. If the participant has multiple job titles, enter the title relevant to the training.
* **Training Program**: Enter the name of the training program the participant underwent.
* **Training Facilitator**: Enter the name of the person who facilitated the training session.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training session took place.
* **Participant Supervisor**: Enter the name of the participant's supervisor.
* **Training Session**: Enter a brief description of the training session.
* **Training Hours**: Enter the number of hours the participant spent in the training session. Make sure to include any hours spent in preparatory activities or wrap-up sessions.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Enter any feedback or comments on the participant's performance during the training session.
* **Evaluation Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations for future improvements.
* **Participant Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the participant.
* **Training Session**: This field is not used for input and is for internal reference only.
* **Participant Supervisor**: This field is not used for input and is for internal reference only.
