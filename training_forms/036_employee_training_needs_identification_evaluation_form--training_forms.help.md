# Employee Training Needs Identification Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Training Needs Identification Evaluation Form is designed to help assess an employee's training needs and identify potential development opportunities. This form should be completed to gather information on the current training needs and status of employees within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the relevant information about the employee or employees being evaluated.
2. Review the form sections and questions carefully.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
4. If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Training Needs** (`employee_training_needs_identification_evaluation_form`): This is the main section of the form where you can describe the employee's training needs. Please use this space to provide a detailed and concrete explanation of their requirements and expectations.
* **Organization Employees** (`organization_employees`): This field is intended for listing the number of employees currently in the organization.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`): Answer this question with either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the employee has specific training needs or not.
* **Development Opportunities** (`development_opportunities`): If applicable, describe any development opportunities that can be offered to the employee. Please use the time format to provide a specific date or period when the employee will be able to take advantage of these opportunities.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`): This is a free-text field where you can note any additional information about the employee or their training needs. Please use this space to add any relevant comments or observations.
* **Form Notes** (`employee_training_needs_identification_evaluation_form_notes`): This is a field for adding any additional comments or explanations about the form completion process or any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If unsure, don't hesitate to consult with a supervisor or trainer.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all sections are completed.
* Save a copy of the form for future reference.
