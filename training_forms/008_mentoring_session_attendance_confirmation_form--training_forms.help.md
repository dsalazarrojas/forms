# Mentoring Session Attendance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees after a mentoring session.

## How To Complete This Form
- To fill out this form, please provide the following information:
  - Date of the session.
  - Your name (Attendee Name).
  - The name of the person who mentored you (Mentor).
  - A brief description of the session topic.
  - Your goals and expectations from the session (Session Goals).
  - The duration of the session (Session Duration). This is optional.
  - If you have any comments or notes about the session, please provide them here (Comments).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Date** (`mentoring_session_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the mentoring session.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Mentor** (`mentor_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who mentored you.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the topic of the session.
* **Session Goals** (`session_goals`, `text`, required: false): Enter your goals and expectations from the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `time`, required: false): Enter the duration of the session.
* **Session Outcomes** (`session_outcomes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any notable outcomes or takeaways from the session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the session.
