# Training Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and assessment from individuals who have attended a training session. It is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the training program and gather information on areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand what is being asked.
2. Answer all questions honestly and accurately.
3. Fill out the form completely, but do not worry if you have limited information or cannot answer a question.
4. Use the provided options or select the best answer from the options.
5. Do not worry about grammar or spelling errors when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Assessment Form 1**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or thoughts about the training session.
* **Training Participants**: Please select "Yes" if you have attended this training session, and "No" if you have not.
* **Training Location**: This field is likely for recording the location where the training session was held.
* **Training Facilitator**: This field is likely for recording the name of the facilitator or instructor who led the training session.
* **Feedback**: Please provide any feedback or comments you have about the training session.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have.
* **Rating**: Please select "Good" or "Fair" to rate the overall effectiveness of the training session.
* **Date Completed**: This field is for recording the date the training session was completed.
* **Time Completed**: This field is for recording the time the training session was completed.
* **Training Participants Who Attended**: Please select "Yes" if you have attended this training session, and "No" if you have not.
* **Training Location Who Attended**: Please select "Yes" if the location where you attended the training is the one listed, and "No" if it is not.
* **Training Participants Other Organizations**: Please select "Yes" if you work with other organizations that also attended the training session, and "No" if you do not.
* **Rating Scale Used**: Please select "Good" or "Fair" to rate the overall effectiveness of the training session.
* **Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the trainer or instructor.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone outside of the organization.
* The information collected on this form will be used to improve future training sessions.
