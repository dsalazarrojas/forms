# robotics_training_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the effectiveness of robotics training sessions. It collects information about participant performance, rating, and feedback to help trainers and evaluators monitor progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the training session.
2. Enter the participant's name.
3. Enter the participant's ID.
4. Choose the training evaluator from the dropdown list.
5. Enter the evaluation result as a numerical value.
6. Enter the participant's rating as a numerical value.
7. Provide any additional comments about the participant's performance.
8. Enter the participant's email address (if applicable).
9. Enter the participant's phone number (if applicable).
10. Choose the department(s) that the participant belongs to.
11. Choose the training status from the dropdown list.
12. Choose the training location from the dropdown list.
13. Enter the model of the robot used in the training session (if applicable).
14. Enter the total training hours completed by the participant.
15. Enter the date and time of the training session.
16. Provide feedback from the participant.
17. Enter any additional notes about the training session.
18. Choose the participant's attendance status from the dropdown list.
19. Enter the participant's attendance rating as a numerical value.
20. Enter the training evaluator's rating as a numerical value.
21. Enter any comments from the training evaluator.
22. Enter the participant's feedback rating as a numerical value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, required): The date on which the training session took place.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): The name of the participant who attended the training session.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, number, optional): The ID assigned to the participant for identification purposes.
* **Training Evaluator** (`training_evaluator`, select_one, optional): The person who conducted the training session.
* **Evaluation Result** (`evaluation_result`, number, optional): The outcome of the training session, represented as a numerical value.
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, optional): The participant's rating of their own performance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Any additional comments or feedback from the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email address of the participant (if applicable).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): The phone number of the participant (if applicable).
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): The department(s) that the participant belongs to.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one, optional): The status of the training session (e.g., completed, ongoing).
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_multiple, optional): The location where the training session took place.
* **Robot Model** (`robot_model`, text, optional): The model of the robot used in the training session (if applicable).
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): The total number of hours completed by the participant.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, date, optional): The date and time of the training session.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, optional): Feedback from the participant about their experience.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, text, optional): Additional notes about the training session.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, select_one, optional): The participant's attendance status.
* **Training Evaluator Rating** (`training_evaluator_rating`, number, optional): The training evaluator's rating of the participant's performance.
* **Participant Attendance Rating** (`participant_attendance_rating`, number, optional): The participant's rating of their own attendance.
* **Training Evaluator Comments** (`training_evaluator_comments`, note, optional): Comments from the training evaluator about the participant's performance.
* **Participant Feedback Rating** (`participant_feedback_rating`, number, optional): The participant's rating of their own feedback.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, select_one, optional): The participant's attendance status.
