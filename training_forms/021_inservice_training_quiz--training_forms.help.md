# Inservice Training Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for tracking training completion by instructors and participants in internal educational settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the training in the "training_quiz" field.
2. Select the department from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the date and time of completion in the "date_completed" field.
4. Indicate whether you have participated in the training by selecting "True" or "False" in the "participated" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your understanding of the training in the "completed_understanding" field.
6. Select the reviewers of the training from the list of options in the "review_by" field (Manager, Supervisor, Department Head, or Colleague).
7. If you select "Manager" or "Supervisor" as a reviewer, you will be prompted to provide additional information in the "review_by_other" field.
8. Click the "submit" button to submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **training_quiz**: This field is for entering the title of the training you are completing.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **department**: Select your department from the provided options.
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
- **date_completed**: Enter the date and time of completion.
  * Type: Date
  * Required: No
- **participated**: Indicate whether you have participated in the training by selecting "True" or "False".
  * Type: Select One
  * Required: No
- **completed_understanding**: Provide a brief description of your understanding of the training.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **review_by**: Select the reviewers of the training from the list of options (Manager, Supervisor, Department Head, or Colleague).
  * Type: Select Multiple
  * Required: No
- **review_by_other**: If you select "Manager" or "Supervisor" as a reviewer, provide additional information in this field.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
- **submit**: Click this button to submit your completed form.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: No
