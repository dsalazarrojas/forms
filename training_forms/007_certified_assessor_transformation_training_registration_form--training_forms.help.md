# Certified Assessor Transformation Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Certified Assessor Transformation Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you are not duplicating a previous registration.
2. Fill in your full legal name in the "participant name" field.
3. Enter your employer or affiliation in the "organization" field, if applicable.
4. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field, which will be used for training materials and updates.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Select your preferred training pathway from the "program track" field.
7. Choose your preferred start date from the available session dates.
8. Confirm whether you have completed the required prerequisites.
9. If applicable, enter any food restrictions for in-person sessions.
10. Enter your payment confirmation or invoice reference number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Participant Name (participant_name)
 * **Participant Name** (required): Enter your full legal name.

### 2. Organization (organization)
 * **Organization** (optional): Enter your employer or affiliation.

### 3. Email (email)
 * **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address for training materials and updates.

### 4. Phone (phone)
 * **Phone** (optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### 5. Program Track (program_track)
 * **Program Track** (required): Select your preferred training pathway - Fundamentals, Advanced Assessor, or Refresher.

### 6. Preferred Start Date (start_date)
 * **Preferred Start Date** (required): Choose your preferred start date from the available session dates.

### 7. Prerequisites Completed (prerequisites_completed)
 * **Prerequisites Completed** (required): Confirm whether you have completed the required prerequisites.

### 8. Dietary Requirements (dietary_requirements)
 * **Dietary Requirements** (optional): Enter any food restrictions for in-person sessions.

### 9. Payment Confirmation (payment_confirmation)
 * **Payment Confirmation** (required): Enter your payment method or invoice reference number.

## Tips
- Ensure you are not duplicating a previous registration.
- Use a valid email address for training materials and updates.
- Select the correct payment method or invoice reference number.
- Confirm the completion of required prerequisites.
- If you have any food restrictions, inform us in advance to ensure accommodations can be made.
