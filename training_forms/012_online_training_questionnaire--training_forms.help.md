# Online Training Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about clients' health, fitness, and medical history for training purposes. This data will help trainers and coaches understand clients' needs and goals, allowing for a more personalized and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and the information required.
2. For each field, read the label carefully and provide accurate and honest information.
3. Answer all questions that are marked as required (indicated by a asterisk \*).
4. Take your time to complete the form, and feel free to ask for help if you need assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Client Health
* **Client Health**: This field is a text field where you can describe your current health status. Please provide a brief answer, and if you have any medical conditions, allergies, or medications, please mention them. (Required)
### Page 1: Fitness Level
* **Fitness Level**: Please select the best answer that describes your current fitness level.
	+ Option 1: Beginner
	+ Option 2: Intermediate
	+ Option 3: Advanced
### Page 1: Training Goals
* **Training Goals**: Select all the options that apply to your training goals.
	+ Option 1: Weight loss
	+ Option 2: Muscle gain
	+ Option 3: Endurance
### Page 2: Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in this text field.
### Page 2: Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact**: Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
### Page 2: Relationship with Emergency Contact
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
### Page 3: Allergies
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, please describe them in this text field.
### Page 3: Medications
* **Medications**: If you take any medications, please list them in this text field.
### Page 3: Medical History
* **Medical History**: If you have a medical history, please describe it in this text field.
### Page 4: Client Lifestyle
* **Client Lifestyle**: This field is a text field where you can describe your client lifestyle, including habits, work, or other activities. Please provide a brief answer. (Required)
