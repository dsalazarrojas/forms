# Training Completion Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from trainees about their training completion. Trainees are required to provide details about the organization, their name, training start and end dates, location, training hours, hours completed, trainer's name, evaluation date, and any comments they may have. This information will help us understand their training progress and completion status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your training progress is correctly recorded.
2. Enter your organization name and the name of the trainee for whom you are submitting the form.
3. Select the start and end dates of the training.
4. Specify the location where the training took place.
5. Report the total training hours allocated and the hours completed.
6. Identify the name of the trainer who conducted the training.
7. Provide the date when you completed the evaluation.
8. Add any comments you have about the training if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are submitting this form for.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Name of Trainee**: Enter the full name of the trainee for whom you are submitting this form.
* **Training Start Date**: Enter the date when the training started. Use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Training End Date**: Enter the date when the training ended. Use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Location**: Enter the specific location where the training took place.
* **Training Hours**: Enter the total hours of training allocated to the trainee.
* **Training Hours Completed**: Enter the total hours of training you have completed.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the training.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date when you completed the evaluation. Use the date format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the training. This is an optional field and is not required for completion, but can be helpful for us to know your thoughts and suggestions.
