# RSO Online Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RSO Online Training Registration Form is used to register for online training sessions. Fill in your personal and contact information, details about the training you're interested in, and payment and emergency contact details to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out your personal information**: Enter your name, title, role, and other details as required.
2. **Provide contact information**: Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and emergency contact details.
3. **Select the training session**: Choose the online training session you're interested in and provide details such as session ID, start date, end date, and hours.
4. **Enter payment details**: Enter payment information as required for the training session.
5. **Submit the form**: Once you've filled out all the necessary information, click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Info**
* **User Info** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your personal information here, including your name, title, and other details as required.

### 2. **Contact Info**
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email, phone number, and other contact details.

### 3. **RSO Info**
* **RSO Info** (`rso_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is not clearly defined, but it's likely used to store additional information about the RSO (Responsible Service Owner). Enter the name and email of the RSO, if required.

### 4. **Session Details**
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the details about the online training session you're interested in, including its ID, start date, end date, and hours.

### 5. **Training Details**
* **Training Details** (`training_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the training session, including the topic and location.

### 6. **Certification**
* **Certification** (`certification`, `text`, required: false): This field is likely used to store certification details, but it's not clearly defined. Enter any relevant certification details here, if required.

### 7. **Training Schedule**
* **Training Schedule** (`training_schedule`, `text`, required: false): Enter the schedule of the training session, including start and end dates and hours.

### 8. **Payment Details**
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter payment information as required for the training session, including payment method and amount.

### 9. **Emergency Contact**
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the emergency contact information, including name, phone number, and other contact details.

### 10. **Submit**
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to complete the registration process.

### 11. **RSO Name**
* **RSO Name** (`rso_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the RSO, if required.

### 12. **RSO Email**
* **RSO Email** (`rso_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the email of the RSO, if required.

### 13. **Training Session ID**
* **Training Session ID** (`training_session_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the online training session you're interested in.

### 14. **Training Start Date**
* **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the training session.

### 15. **Training End Date**
* **Training End Date** (`training_end_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the training session.

### 16. **Training Session Hours**
* **Training Session Hours** (`training_session_hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the total hours of the training session.

### 17. **Training Location**
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the online training session.

### 18. **Training Topic**
* **Training Topic** (`training_topic`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the topic of the training session.

### 19. **Training Description**
* **Training Description** (`training_description`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the online training session.

### 20. **RSO Role**
* **RSO Role** (`rso_role`, `text`, required: false): Enter your role in relation to the RSO, if required.

### 21. **RSO Phone**
* **RSO Phone** (`rso_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the RSO, if required.
