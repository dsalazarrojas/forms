# Online Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users to create a personalized training plan based on their interests in specific training areas and topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, which consists of 5 text fields.
2. Enter your answer in the corresponding text field.
3. Review each page to ensure you have completed all required fields.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**
	+ **Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief title for your training request.
* **Page 2**
	+ **Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of your training request.
* **Page 3**
	+ **Title** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your training request.
* **Page 4**
	+ **Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your preferred training method (e.g., in-person, online, or blended).
* **Page 5**
	+ **Title** (Text, Optional): Enter any specific training topics you are interested in or would like to learn about.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when describing your training request.
- If you have any supporting documents or files, such as a resume or certificate, you may attach them to your form submission.
- You will receive a response within 3-5 working days after submitting the form.
