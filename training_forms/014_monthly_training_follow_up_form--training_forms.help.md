# monthly_training_follow_up_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by training organizers or administrators to capture information about each participant who attended a training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a participant from the training session record.
2. Ensure that you are not duplicating an entry for this participant.
3. Fill out each field with the relevant information:
	* **Training Organization**: The name of the organization where the training was held or sponsored.
	* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant who attended the training session.
	* **Participant Contact**: The contact information for the participant (e.g., phone number, email).
	* **Participant Email**: The email address of the participant.
	* **Training Date**: The date of the training session.
	* **Training Topic**: A brief description of the topic covered during the training session.
	* **Session Length**: The length of the training session (e.g., number of hours).
	* **Number of Participants**: The total number of participants in the training session.
	* **Training Goals**: A brief description of the training goals and objectives.
	* **Participant Feedback**: Any feedback or comments from the participant.
