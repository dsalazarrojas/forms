# Personnel Emergency Response Group Training Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps track and document the training status of personnel within an emergency response group.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each person in the emergency response group, ensuring their training status has not already been documented.
2. Select the type of training the individual has received or is due to receive from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the start date of the training, if applicable.
4. Enter the end date of the training, if applicable.
5. Enter the number of personnel trained, if relevant.
6. Provide any additional comments about the personnel's training status.
7. Enter the phone number of the individual, if necessary.
8. Enter the location of the training, if known.
9. Enter the email address of the individual, if necessary.
10. Enter the name of the training provider, if known.
11. Enter the start and end times of the training, if applicable.
12. Select the personnel's training status from the dropdown list, if known.
13. Enter any additional comments about the personnel's training status.
14. Enter the facility address, city, state, and zip of the training location, if known.
15. Enter the name of the person assigned to manage the individual's training status.
16. Enter the country of the training location, if known.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **emergency_response_group_name**: Enter the name of the emergency response group.
* **training_type**: Select the type of training the individual has received or is due to receive. Choose from the dropdown list: First Aid, Disaster Response, Fire Safety, or Other (please specify).
* **start_date**: Enter the date the training started, if applicable.
* **end_date**: Enter the date the training ended, if applicable.
* **personnel_training_count**: Enter the number of personnel trained, if relevant.
* **personnel_in_service**: Enter any additional comments about the personnel's training status.
* **personnel_training_status**: Select the personnel's training status from the dropdown list. Choose from the options: Completed, In Progress, Pending, or Deferred.
* **personnel_notes**: Enter any additional comments about the personnel's training status.
* **emergency_number**: Enter the phone number of the individual, if necessary.
* **training_location**: Enter the location of the training, if known.
* **personnel_email**: Enter the email address of the individual, if necessary.
* **training_provider**: Enter the name of the training provider, if known.
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of the training, if applicable.
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of the training, if applicable.
* **personnel_in_emergency_response_training**: Enter any additional comments about the personnel's training status.
* **personnel_training_hours**: Enter the total number of hours trained, if known.
* **training_facility**: Enter the facility name, if known.
* **personnel_training_status_comments**: Enter any additional comments about the personnel's training status.
* **assigned_to**: Enter the name of the person assigned to manage the individual's training status.
* **training_facility_address**: Enter the address of the training facility, if known.
* **training_facility_city**: Enter the city of the training facility, if known.
* **training_facility_state**: Enter the state of the training facility, if known.
* **training_facility_zip**: Enter the zip code of the training facility, if known.
* **training_facility_country**: Enter the country of the training facility, if known.
* **personnel_training_status**: Select the personnel's training status from the dropdown list. Choose from the options: Completed, In Progress, Pending, or Deferred.
