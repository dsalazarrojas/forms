# software_development_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The software development training form is used to collect information from employees for software development training purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Select the relevant options for the training program, preferred language, and additional comments.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Software Development Training Form Page 1**: This is the first page of the form where you can enter any additional comments or information not covered in other fields.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID or official documents.
* **Training Program**: Select the training program that you are interested in or would like to be enrolled in. Please choose one of the following options:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
	+ option 3
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you are available to attend the training session. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time slot you are available to attend the training session. For example, `09:00` or `10:00`.
* **Employee Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your employee ID.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your work phone number associated with your employee ID.
* **Preferred Language**: Select the languages you are most comfortable with or would like to learn. Please choose one or more of the following options:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
	+ option 3
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information that would be helpful for the training administrators to know.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the training administrators.
* Once you have submitted the form, please allow 24-48 hours for a response.
