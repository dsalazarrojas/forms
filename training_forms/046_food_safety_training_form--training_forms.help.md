# Food Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees or trainers about their food safety training history, areas for improvement, knowledge base, and training status. It helps to track and evaluate the effectiveness of food safety training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2.  Complete the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
3.  Fill in the fields accurately and clearly, using the provided options or entering your own text.
4.  If you are unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or trainer for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training History**: Provide a brief description of your training history, including the date and time of your last food safety training.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Identify areas where you would like to improve your food safety knowledge and skills. Select "New" if you are new to food safety or "Existing" if you are already familiar with food safety practices.
* **Knowledge Base**: Indicate your current level of knowledge about food safety, selecting "Basic," "Intermediate," or "Advanced" from the options.
* **Training Topics**: Select all the topics that you have been trained on, such as "Food Safety," "Food Handling," and "Sanitation."
* **Areas for Improvement 1, 2, 3, 4**: Repeat the "Areas for Improvement" field, providing additional opportunities for you to identify areas where you need improvement.
* **Assessment Knowledge**: Indicate your level of knowledge about food safety, selecting "Basic," "Intermediate," or "Advanced" from the options.
* **Trainer ID, Trainer Name, Employee ID, Employee Name**: Enter your trainer or employee ID and name, as provided by your supervisor or trainer.
* **Food Safety Training Date, Food Safety Training Time**: If you are unsure about the date and time of your last training, ask your supervisor or trainer for clarification.
* **Training Status**: Indicate the completion status of your training, selecting "Complete" or "Incomplete."
* **Trainer ID 1, Trainer ID 1 1**: Repeat the "Trainer ID" field, providing additional opportunities for you to enter your trainer's ID.
* **Training Topics 1, 2, 3**: Select all the training topics that you have been trained on, repeating the "Training Topics" field.
* **Areas for Improvement 1, 2, 3, 4**: Repeat the "Areas for Improvement" field, providing additional opportunities for you to identify areas where you need improvement.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any field or option, ask your supervisor or trainer for clarification.
* Complete the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
* Fill in the fields accurately and clearly, using the provided options or entering your own text.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have completed all the necessary fields.
