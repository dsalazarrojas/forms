# Software Training Seminar Voting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from attendees after a software training seminar. It helps us understand your thoughts, opinions, and preferences about the seminar and its content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the fields below and ensure you have the necessary information before starting to fill in the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields (if any). The required fields will be marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  For fields marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", choose one or multiple options from the list.
4.  For fields marked as "date" and "time", enter dates and times in the correct format.
5.  For fields marked as "text", enter your text response in the field.
6.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.
