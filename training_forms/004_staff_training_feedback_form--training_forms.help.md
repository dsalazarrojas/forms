# Staff Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Training Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from staff members after a training session. This feedback will help us evaluate the effectiveness of the training session, identify areas of improvement, and inform future training initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your honest and constructive feedback.
2. Make sure to provide specific examples or details about your experience.
3. Check that you are not filling out the form in duplicate.
4. Review each field carefully before submitting your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Feedback
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, required): Describe your overall experience of the training session, including any positive or negative aspects.

### Training Effectiveness
* **Training Effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate how effective you found the training session, with options Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, or Not Effective. This will help us understand how well the training achieved its objectives.

### Content Relevance
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, text, required): Describe how relevant the training content was to your needs and expectations.

### Session Length
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required): Rate the session length, providing a numerical value to help us understand how long or short the training was.

### Overall Satisfaction
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): If you wish, rate your overall satisfaction with the session, providing a numerical value.

### Trainer Attentiveness
* **Trainer Attentiveness** (`trainer_attentiveness`, text, optional): Suggest any areas for improvement with the trainer's attentiveness.

### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the training session.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your feedback.
