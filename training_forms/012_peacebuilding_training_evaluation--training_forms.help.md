# Peacebuilding Training Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peacebuilding Training Evaluation form is designed to gather feedback from trainees on the effectiveness of a peacebuilding training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the questions being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing as much detail as you feel necessary.
3. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact information.
5. Provide any additional comments you have about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`): This is a brief introduction to the form.
* **Training Experience** (`2`): How would you rate your overall experience with the training program? You can select from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Training Objectives** (`3`): Which of the following objectives did the training address? You can select one or more of Improve communication, Improve team building, Improve decision-making, or Improve problem-solving.
* **Training Outcomes** (`4`): What positive outcomes did you experience as a result of the training? You can select one or more of Improved communication, Better collaboration, Increased productivity, or Improved problem-solving.
* **Feedback** (`5`): What did you think about the overall quality of the training? Please provide your honest feedback to help us improve.
* **Date** (`6`): What date did you attend the training session?
* **Time** (`7`): What time did you start and end the training session? (Format: HH:MM)
* **Email** (`8`): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
* **Phone** (`9`): Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
* **Comments** (`10`): Is there anything else you'd like to comment on or suggest for future trainings? 

## Tips
* Please answer all questions that apply to you.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the Comments field.
* Make sure to enter your correct email and phone number so we can follow up with you.
