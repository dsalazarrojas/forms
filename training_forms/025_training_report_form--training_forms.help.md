# Training Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Report Form is used to document participant's experiences and outcomes after completing a training or a program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and title as the participant who attended the training.
2. Enter the name of the program or training you attended.
3. Provide the date of the training session.
4. Specify the time it took to complete the training.
5. Enter the location where the training took place.
6. Briefly describe the type of training you attended.
7. Enter the name of the trainer or facilitator who led the training.
8. If applicable, enter the total number of hours you spent on the training.
9. Select whether you achieved a positive training outcome.
10. Enter any comments or feedback you have about the training.
11. Select any areas where you provided feedback to the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the participant who attended the training.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program or training you attended.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): Enter the time it took to complete the training (e.g., 2 hours).
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the training took place.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, text, required): Briefly describe the type of training you attended.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer or facilitator who led the training.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the total number of hours you spent on the training.
* **Training Outcome** (`training_outcome`, select_one, optional): Select whether you achieved a positive training outcome.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the training.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, select_multiple, optional): Select any areas where you provided feedback to the trainer.
	+ Options: Yes, No

Note: This form is for internal use only, and your feedback will be used to improve our training programs.
