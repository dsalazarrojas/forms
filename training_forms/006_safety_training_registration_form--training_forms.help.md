# Safety Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals for safety training. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Check your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone (3)** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Training ID (4)** (`training_id`, `number`, required: false): Enter your Training ID number (if applicable).
* **Date (5)** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to attend training.
* **Time (6)** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to attend training.
* **Category (7)** (`category`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose 'Yes' if you require safety training, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Select Category (8)** (`category_options`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose one or more categories that apply (Safety, Training, Registration).
* **Note (9)** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your training needs.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you're unsure about a field, please ask for help.
* We'll review your submission and contact you with any issues or concerns.
