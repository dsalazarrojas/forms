# Chemical Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track employee chemical safety training, ensuring that trainees have received necessary training and understanding of chemical safety procedures. It is used for record-keeping, certification, and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and employee ID in the first two fields, which are required for identification purposes.
2. Select your department and job title from the provided options to ensure accurate record-keeping.
3. Choose the type of safety training you received (e.g., Initial chemical safety training, Annual refresher, etc.).
4. Provide your supervisor's name and the date you completed the training.
5. Select the format in which you received the training (e.g., In-person classroom, Online course, etc.).
6. Choose all the topics covered during the training that apply to you.
7. If applicable, provide the chemicals covered during the training and the result of any written test (if required).
8. Indicate whether a written test was required and your achieved score (if applicable).
9. Answer questions regarding your understanding of the material and any additional training needed.
10. Confirm with your supervisor that you received proper PPE fitting (if applicable).
11. Confirm that you received training materials (comprehensive or basic).
12. Evaluate your understanding of the material on a scale of excellent to poor.
13. Confirm with your supervisor that additional training is needed (if applicable).
14. Specify the details of additional training required (if applicable).
15. Confirm that a certification was issued to you.
16. Provide the expiry date of the certification (if applicable).
17. Sign to confirm that you completed the training and review the form for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID for identification purposes.
* **Department**: Select your department from the provided options.
* **Job Title**: Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you completed the training.
* **Training Type**: Select the type of training you received (e.g., Initial chemical safety training, Annual refresher, etc.).
* **Trainer Name**: Enter your trainer's name.
* **Trainer Qualifications**: You can provide any additional trainer qualifications if required.
* **Training Duration**: Select the duration of the training (e.g., Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, etc.).
* **Training Format**: Select the format in which you received the training (e.g., In-person classroom, Online course, etc.).
* **Topics Covered**: Select all the topics covered during the training that apply to you.
* **Specific Chemicals Covered**: If applicable, list any specific chemicals discussed during the training.
* **Written Test Required**: Indicate whether a written test was required.
* **Test Score**: Enter your achieved score if a written test was required.
* **Passing Score**: Enter the required passing score if a written test was required.
* **Practical Demonstration Required**: Indicate whether a practical demonstration was required.
* **Demonstration Passed**: Indicate whether you passed the practical demonstration.
* **PPE Fitting Completed**: Confirm whether proper PPE fitting was verified.
* **Training Materials Provided**: Confirm whether you received training materials (comprehensive or basic).
* **Trainee Understanding**: Evaluate your understanding of the material on a scale of excellent to poor.
* **Additional Training Needed**: Indicate whether you require additional training.
* **Additional Training Details**: Specify the additional training required if applicable.
* **Certification Issued**: Confirm whether you received certification.
* **Certification Expiry Date**: Enter the expiry date of the certification if issued.
* **Trainee Signature**: Sign to confirm that you completed the training and review the form for accuracy.
* **Trainer Signature**: Sign to confirm that you completed the training and review the form for accuracy.
* **Record Date**: Enter the date this record was created.
