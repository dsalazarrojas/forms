# Dog Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dog Training Form is used to record and document the training sessions of dogs. It captures essential information about each session, including the dog's name, training details, and contact information of both the client and trainer. This form helps in maintaining accurate records and ensuring that trainers and clients are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Start by entering the dog's name and training details such as date, session notes, and progress level.
* Select the training type (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced) and choose the behavior modification techniques used during the session.
* Enter the trainer's notes and client contact information.
* Provide additional contact information for the client and trainer, including an email address.
* Specify the start and end times of the session, the training location, and the trainer's name and owner's name.
* Finally, enter the number of sessions and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dog Name**: Enter the name of the dog being trained.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Session Notes**: Enter any notes or observations about the session.
* **Progress Level**: Choose the progress level of the dog's training, from Option 1 to Option 3.
* **Behavior Modification**: Select the behavior modification techniques used during the session.
* **Trainer Notes**: Enter any notes or observations from the trainer.
* **Client Contact Information**: Enter the client's contact information, including phone number and address.
* **Trainer Contact Information**: Enter the trainer's contact information.
* **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of the session.
* **Number of Sessions**: Enter the number of sessions scheduled.
* **Training Goals**: Enter any goals or objectives for the training sessions.
* **Client Comments**: Enter any comments from the client.
* **Training Type**: Choose the level of training (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Trainer Comments**: Enter any comments or observations from the trainer.
* **Training Schedule**: Enter the date and time of the training schedule.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the session.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the session.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location of the training session.
* **Trainer**: Enter the trainer's name.
* **Owner**: Enter the owner's name.
* **Dog Breed**: Enter the breed of the dog.
* **Additional Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the client or trainer.
* **Additional Contact Info 2**: Enter any additional email address for the client or trainer.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the client or trainer.
