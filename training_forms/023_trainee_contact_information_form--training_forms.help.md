# Trainee Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trainee Contact Information Form is designed to gather essential details about each trainee. Completing this form will help our trainers reach out to trainees with important updates, notifications, or clarifications about their training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `email` field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Specify your job title in the `job_title` field.
5. Choose the department you belong to by selecting one of the options in the `department` field.
6. Provide your date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field. This will be used to personalize communications.
7. If applicable, choose the training programs you're interested in by selecting multiple options in the `training_program` field.
8. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the `phone_number` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **job_title**: Specify your job title or position in the organization.
* **department**: Choose the department you belong to from the options provided.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **training_program**: If applicable, select one or multiple training programs that interest you.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number if you have one.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out only the relevant fields, as some are not mandatory.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the trainer for clarification.
* Double-check your inputs for accuracy before submitting the form.
