# fundraising_skills_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Skills Training Form is a document used to record information about an individual's fundraising skills training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, email, phone number, and address in the respective fields.
2. Select the level of your fundraising skills (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced) and the training level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
3. Choose the payment method (Online, Offline, or Other) and the payment amount if applicable.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about your training experience.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (participant_email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (participant_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Address** (participant_address, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Fundraising Skills** (fundraising_skills, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of your fundraising skills: Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Training Level** (training_level, select_one, optional): Select the training level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Training Date** (training_date, date, optional): Enter the date of your training.
* **Training Time** (training_time, time, optional): Enter the time of your training.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your training experience.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, optional): Select the payment method: Online, Offline, or Other.
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, optional): Enter the payment amount if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to you for training.
* **Form Submission** (form_submission, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the form submission process.
