# Fitness Performance Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by trainers and coaches to evaluate the fitness performance of their trainees during a training session. It helps them to identify areas of improvement and provide constructive feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the date of the training session.
* Enter the name of the workout or exercise performed.
* Choose the intensity level of the exercise (e.g. Squat, Deadlift, Bench Press, etc.).
* Enter the weight lifted during the exercise.
* Enter the number of sets completed.
* Enter the number of reps performed.
* Enter the rest time taken between exercises.
* Add any additional comments about the session.
* Add any comments or feedback from the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Date (1)**: Enter the date of the training session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Workout Name (2)**: Enter the name of the exercise or workout performed, e.g. Squat, Deadlift, etc.
* **Intensity (3)**: Select the type of exercise performed, e.g. Squat, Deadlift, Bench Press, etc.
* **Exercise Type (4)**: Select one or more types of exercises performed, e.g. Squat, Deadlift, etc.
* **Weight (5)**: Enter the weight lifted during the exercise in pounds or kilograms.
* **Sets (6)**: Enter the number of sets completed during the exercise.
* **Reps (7)**: Enter the number of reps performed during the exercise.
* **Rest Time (8)**: Enter the rest time taken between exercises in minutes.
* **Comments (9)**: Add any additional comments about the session, e.g. notes on form or technique.
* **Trainer Comments (10)**: Add any comments or feedback from the trainer to the trainee, e.g. areas of improvement, etc.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and detailed information to ensure accurate feedback.
* Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY).
* If a field is not applicable to the session, leave it blank.
* Use the comments fields to provide constructive feedback and suggestions for improvement.
