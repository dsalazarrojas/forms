# Fall Protection Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fall Protection Knowledge Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge on fall protection in the workplace. This form will help us evaluate your understanding of fall protection procedures and ensure that you are aware of the necessary precautions to take to prevent falls and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the correct answer to each question. Please read each question carefully and choose the most accurate answer based on your knowledge and experience. If you are unsure of an answer, please refer to your company's safety manual or consult with a supervisor or safety professional.
