# Financial Training Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Training Program Application Form is used to collect information from employees who wish to participate in the company's financial training program. The form helps to gather relevant details such as employee contact information, training preferences, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "First Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
2. Select the type of training you are interested in from the "Training Type" dropdown menu.
3. (Optional) Select multiple training options from the "Select Multiple" dropdown menu.
4. Choose a date and time for the training session.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
6. If applicable, enter the date and time when you submitted your application.
7. Provide your phone number and the tool or system used to submit your application.
8. Finally, enter your comments or feedback about the submitted application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Training Type**: Select the type of financial training you are interested in. Options include:
	+ Financial Planning
	+ Financial Analysis
	+ Financial Modeling
* **Select Multiple**: Select multiple training options. Options include:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Date**: Choose a date for the training session.
* **Time**: Choose a time for the training session.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your application.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the tool or system used to submit your application.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date and time when you submitted your application.
* **Submitted By Phone**: Enter your phone number when submitting your application.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or system assigned to you for the training session.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date when the training session is assigned to you.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the training session.

Note: The "First Name", "Email", "Phone", "Note", and "Comments" fields are optional. The "Training Type", "Select Multiple", "Date", "Time" are required fields.
