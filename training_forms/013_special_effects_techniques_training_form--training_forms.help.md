# Special Effects Techniques Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Effects Techniques Training Form is designed to gather information from users who have enrolled in a training session or have attended a workshop. This information will be used to provide personalized training materials and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Make sure to follow the instructions and guidelines provided for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name (text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address that you use to communicate with us. This will help us to contact you with updates and resources.
- **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date Of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Which Training Session? (select multiple)**: Choose the training session or workshop you have enrolled in. Select all applicable sessions.
- **Workshop Name (text)**: Enter the name of the workshop you have attended.
- **Special Effect (text)**: Enter any information about the special effect you want to achieve.

# Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Select all training sessions you have enrolled in for the "Which Training Session?" field.
- Enter your actual special effect if you are unsure what to enter in the "Special Effect" field.
