# Employee Training Platform Usability Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their experiences with the training platform. Your input will help identify areas where the platform can be improved to better serve you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form thoroughly and accurately, only answering questions that are applicable to your experiences with the training platform.
- Please do not answer questions with "N/A" or "Not Applicable" unless it's truly not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your employee ID badge.
- **Training Experiences** (`training_experiences`, select_multiple, optional):
  • Please select all the types of training you have received or are familiar with on the platform.
  • Examples: instructor-led, online, classroom, certification, etc.
- **Training Platform** (`training_platform`, select_one, optional):
  • Rate the overall usability of the training platform.
  • Excellent: The platform is easy to use and meets all my needs.
  • Good: The platform is mostly easy to use, but I have some minor issues.
  • Neutral: The platform is neutral; it neither impresses nor disappoints.
  • Poor: The platform is difficult to use and does not meet my needs.
- **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional):
  • Rate your overall satisfaction with the training platform.
  • Excellent: I am very satisfied with the platform.
  • Good: I am somewhat satisfied with the platform.
  • Neutral: I am neutral about the platform.
  • Poor: I am dissatisfied with the platform.
- **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving the training platform.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experiences with the training platform.
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, optional): If you have any specific feedback or suggestions for someone or team, please enter their name(s) or department(s).
