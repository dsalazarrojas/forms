# Industrial Reliability Analytics Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration information for the Industrial Reliability Analytics Training program. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

- **Please fill out the form carefully**. Each field is labeled and explained below.
- **Submit your registration information** once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Company
* **Company** (<label: Company, <type: text, required/optional: false>): Enter your company name.
* **Hint**: Please enter your company name.

### First Name
* **First Name** (<label: First Name, <type: text, required/optional: false>): Enter your first name.
* **Hint**: Please enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (<label: Last Name, <type: text, required/optional: false>): Enter your last name.
* **Hint**: Please enter your last name.

### Email
* **Email** (<label: Email, <type: email, required/optional: false>): Enter your email address.
* **Hint**: Please enter a valid email address.

### Role
* **Role** (<label: Role, <type: select_one, required/true>): Select your role as "Training Team Member" or "Training Provider".
* **Hint**: Please select one of the options: "Training Team Member" or "Training Provider".

### Experience Level
* **Experience Level** (<label: Experience Level, <type: select_multiple, required/true>): Select your experience level as "Junior", "Senior", or "Lead".
* **Hint**: Please select one of the options: "Junior", "Senior", "Lead".

### Preferred Date
* **Preferred Date** (<label: Preferred Date, <type: date, required/optional: false>): If you want to be registered for the training, enter a date you prefer.
* **Hint**: Please enter a valid date.

### Start Date Prophets
* **Start Date Prophets** (<label: Start Date Prophets, <type: date, required/optional: false>): If you want to be registered for the training starting from a certain date, enter it here.
* **Hint**: If you want to start the training at a different time, enter that date.

### Time
* **Time** (<label: Time, <type: time, required/optional: false>): If you want to attend the training at a specific time, enter it here.
* **Hint**: Please enter a valid time.

### Note
* **Note** (<label: Note, <type: note, required/optional: false>): If you have any special request or comment, please enter here.
* **Hint**: Please enter any note.

### Phone
* **Phone** (<label: Phone, <type: text, required/optional: false>): Enter your phone number.
* **Hint**: Please enter your phone number.

## Tips

- **Required fields are marked as "required: true". Make sure to fill out these fields to complete the form successfully.
- **Invalid or incomplete submissions will not be processed. Please review your form carefully before submitting.
- **If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the training administrators.**
