# Vision And Goal Setting Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help participants set and track their professional vision and goals during the training program. It is intended to guide participants in reflecting on their current situation, identifying their desired goals, and outlining the next steps for achieving them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name as a participant in the training program.
2.  Enter the name of your company.
3.  Describe your current role or position.
4.  Indicate your current level of proficiency or experience.
5.  Indicate your desired level of proficiency or experience.
6.  Select the status of your current goal (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, On Hold).
7.  Enter the expected date to complete your goal.
8.  Write down the next steps you will take to achieve your goal.
9.  Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant in the training program.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company. This field is used to associate your goals with your organization.
* **Role**: Describe your current role or position in your company.
* **Current Level**: Indicate your current level of proficiency or experience in your field.
* **Desired Level**: Indicate your desired level of proficiency or experience in your field.
* **Current Goal Status**: Select the status of your current goal (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, On Hold).
* **Goal Completion Date**: Enter the expected date to complete your goal. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Next Steps**: Write down the next steps you will take to achieve your goal.
* **Feedback**: Add any comments or feedback about your progress or challenges.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other comments or information that are not covered in the previous fields.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form once and then review it with your trainer during the training sessions.
* Be specific and honest about your current and desired levels.
* Use the provided options for the Current Goal Status field to select the correct status.
* Use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY) for the Goal Completion Date field.
