# performance_review_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for performance reviews of employees. It's used by managers or supervisors to assess an employee's performance, identify training needs, and provide feedback.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options for the "Performance Review" question.
2. Choose your answer for each of the questions on the next pages.
3. Add any additional feedback or comments as needed.
4. Enter the date and time for the performance review.
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact information.
6. Provide any notes or comments as needed.
7. Enter a performance rating score (if applicable).
8. Add any next steps or actions items for the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Review** (Page 1, ID: 1): Select one of the options ("Yes" or "No") to indicate whether the performance review is for an employee who has met or not met their performance goals.
* **Goals** (Page 1, ID: 2): Choose one of the options ("Yes" or "No") to indicate whether the employee has achieved their goals.
* **Performance Rating** (Page 1, ID: 3): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the employee's performance. A higher value indicates better performance.
* **Training Needs** (Page 1, ID: 4): Select one or more options ("Yes" or "No") to indicate what training needs the employee has.
* **Job Satisfaction** (Page 1, ID: 5): Choose one of the options ("Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied") to indicate the employee's level of job satisfaction.
* **Additional Feedback** (Page 1, ID: 6): Enter any additional feedback or comments you have for the employee.
* **Performance Review Comments** (Page 1, ID: 7): Add any comments or feedback on the employee's performance review.
* **Training Needs Comments** (Page 1, ID: 8): Add any comments or feedback on the employee's training needs.
* **Next Steps** (Page 1, ID: 9): Select one or more options ("Yes" or "No") to indicate next steps or actions for the employee.
* **Date** (Page 1, ID: 10): Enter the date of the performance review.
* **Time** (Page 1, ID: 11): Enter the time of the performance review.
* **Email** (Page 1, ID: 12): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (Page 1, ID: 13): Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Note** (Page 1, ID: 14): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Performance Rating Score** (Page 1, ID: 15): Enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 to rate the employee's performance.
* **Performance Improvement** (Page 1, ID: 16): Add any comments or feedback on the employee's performance improvement.

## Tips
* Please complete this form thoroughly and accurately to ensure fair and effective performance reviews.
* Be sure to enter your contact information so the employee can follow up with you.
* Use the form to provide constructive feedback and identify areas for improvement.
* Keep the next steps or actions items clear and actionable.
* Review and discuss the performance review with the employee to ensure understanding and agreement.
