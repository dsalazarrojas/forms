# Product Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request training for a product. It is intended for users who need to request training for a product and its training manager.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information, including Product Title, Requested User, Training Manager, Training Date, Training Time, and Email.
2. You may optionally provide the Product Version, Comments, and Phone numbers.
3. Make sure to submit the form with accurate information as it will be used to plan and execute the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Title**: A text field to enter the product title that requires training.
* **Product Version** (optional): If applicable, enter the version number of the product.
* **Requested User**: The name of the user requesting the training.
* **Training Manager**: The name of the person in charge of providing the training.
* **Training Date**: A date field to schedule the training.
* **Training Time**: A time field to schedule the specific time for the training.
* **Comments** (optional): A field for additional comments or information about the training.
* **Email**: A field to enter the contact email address of the user.
* **Phone** (optional): A field to enter the contact phone number.
* **Submitted By**: A field to enter the person submitting the form.
* **Submission Date**: A date field to record the date the form was submitted.
*   Please note that all required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   Please ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form. 
*   If you are unsure about any of these fields, please contact your training manager or a designated support person.
