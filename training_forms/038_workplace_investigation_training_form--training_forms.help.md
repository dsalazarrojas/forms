# Workplace Investigation Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a workplace investigation for training purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and truthfully.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Use the participant details section to input information about the participant involved in the investigation.
	* **Participant Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): This field is used to input a brief description of the participant.
4. Enter the participant's name in the **Participant Name** field (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false).
5. Enter the participant's position or title in the **Participant Position** field (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false).
6. Enter the participant's email address in the **Participant Email** field (`id: 4`, `type: email`, required: false).
7. Enter the participant's phone number in the **Participant Phone** field (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false).
8. Provide a brief description of the investigation details in the **Investigation Details** field (`id: 6`, `type: text`, required: false).
9. Enter the name of the investigator in the **Investigator** field (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false).
10. Enter the date the investigation started in the **Investigation Start Date** field (`id: 8`, `type: date`, required: false).
11. Enter the time the investigation started in the **Investigation Start Time** field (`id: 9`, `type: time`, required: false).
12. Enter the date the investigation ended in the **Investigation End Date** field (`id: 10`, `type: date`, required: false).
13. Enter the time the investigation ended in the **Investigation End Time** field (`id: 11`, `type: time`, required: false).
14. Enter the duration of the investigation in the **Investigation Duration** field (`id: 12`, `type: number`, required: false).
15. Enter any comments or remarks regarding the investigation in the **Investigation Comments** field (`id: 13`, `type: text`, required: false).
16. Enter any comments or remarks from the participant in the **Participant Comments** field (`id: 14`, `type: text`, required: false).
17. Enter any feedback or comments regarding the form in the **Feedback** field (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false).
18. Select the participant's status in the **Participant Status** field (`id: 16`, `type: select_one`, required: true). Options: Active, Inactive, Pending.
19. Select the tool or software used for the investigation in the **Assigned Tool** field (`id: 17`, `type: select_one`, required: true). Options: Chatjimmy, Other.
20. Enter the name of the user assigned to the investigation in the **Assigned User** field (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false).
21. Enter any participant observations or remarks in the **Participant Observations** field (`id: 19`, `type: text`, required: false).
22. Enter any participant recommendations or suggestions in the **Participant Recommendations** field (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false).
23. Select the participant's agreement to the investigation in the **Participant Agreement** field (`id: 21`, `type: select_one`, required: true). Options: Agree, Disagree, No response.
24. Enter the participant's signature in the **Participant Signature** field (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false).
25. Enter the date the participant completed the form in the **Participant Date** field (`id: 23`, `type: date`, required: false).
26. Enter the time the participant completed the form in the **Participant Time** field (`id: 24`, `type: time`, required: false).
27. Enter any additional comments from the participant in the **Participant Comments 2** field (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false).

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address in the **Participant Email** field.
* Be honest and accurate when providing information in the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Please sign and date the **Participant Signature** field when completed.
* Keep the form for your records and reference in the future.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or superiors.
