# cultural_awareness_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural awareness training registration form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in attending cultural awareness training sessions. This form helps the training organizers to gather necessary details to manage the training sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address to ensure that we can contact you for any updates or notifications.
3. Enter your phone number for any further communication.
4. Indicate your role or position in your organization.
5. Provide the name of your company.
6. Specify the department you belong to.
7. Choose the date you plan to attend the training session.
8. Confirm if you plan to attend the training session.
9. Select the date and time for the training session.
10. Choose the venue for the training session.
11. Provide any additional comments or notes about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official records.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you if needed.
* **Role**: Select your role or position in your organization.
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Department**: Enter the name of the department you belong to.
* **Training Date**: Choose the date you plan to attend the training session.
* **Training Session**: Confirm if you plan to attend the training session.
* **Session Date**: Select the date of the training session you plan to attend.
* **Session Time**: Choose the time slot for the training session you plan to attend.
* **Venue**: Choose the venue for the training session.
* **Location**: Provide the location of the training session.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the training session.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields accurately.
* Check your email for any updates or notifications from us.
* We will contact you if we need any further information to process your registration.
