# soccer_referee_training_participation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The soccer referee training participation form is designed to gather information from referees participating in soccer training sessions. This form helps track training attendance, course selections, and notes for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Referee Details" page to provide your basic information as a referee.
2. Select the training courses you are interested in participating in on the "Training Courses" page.
3. Enter your contact information, including any additional notes or comments, on the "Contact Information" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referee Details** (`referee_details`, text, required: false): This page is used to provide your basic information as a referee, such as your name and contact details.
* **Training Courses** (`training_courses`, select_multiple, required: false): On this page, you can choose which training courses you are interested in participating in by selecting the options provided.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): This page is used to enter your contact information and any additional notes or comments you may have.
* **Referees Training Participation Form Notes** (`referees_training_participation_form_notes`, note, required: false): This is a dedicated space for any notes or comments you may have regarding the training participation form. You can use this section to provide additional context or reminders.
