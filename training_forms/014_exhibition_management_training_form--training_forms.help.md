# Exhibition Management Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to provide information about their training needs and goals related to exhibition management. It helps trainers understand the participants' expectations and requirements for a successful exhibition management experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Exhibition Management Training Form** field with a brief description of the exhibition management topic you would like to focus on or learn about.
2. Enter your **participant name** in the field provided.
3. Enter your **email address** in the correct format (e.g., `[name]@example.com`).
4. Add your **participant phone number** if you want us to contact you directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Management Training Form (text)**:
This field is where you can describe the specific topic of the exhibition management training you would like to receive or improve on. Be as detailed as possible to help us tailor the training experience.

* **participant_name (text)**:
This field is for your full name as the participant or trainee. Please provide it so we can address you personally and keep you updated on any training materials or arrangements.

* **email (email)**:
This field is for your email address where we will send you information about the training schedule, materials, and any updates. Please make sure to enter it in the correct format (e.g., `[name]@example.com`).

* **participant_phone (text)**:
This field is optional and allows you to provide a phone number where we can contact you for any further questions or updates. This helps us keep you informed and ensures we don't miss any crucial calls or messages.
