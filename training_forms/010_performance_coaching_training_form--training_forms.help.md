# Performance Coaching Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect relevant information from participants and coaches for performance coaching training purposes. It aims to ensure that both participants and coaches are properly prepared and equipped with the necessary details for the training session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Ensure you have the necessary information**: Review the form's fields below and gather all the necessary details before proceeding.
2. **Fill out each field**: Complete each field with the required information, making sure to enter the correct format (e.g., email format for participant and coach email fields).
3. **Submit your response**: Once all fields have been filled out, click "Submit" to save your response.
