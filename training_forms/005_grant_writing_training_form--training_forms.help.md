# Grant Writing Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Writing Training Form is used to capture information about grant writing training programs, enabling efficient management and evaluation of these programs.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the workshop.
2. Select the date the workshop will take place.
3. Define the training objective.
4. Specify the workshop venue.
5. Choose the workshop time.
6. Choose the workshop location.
7. Specify the workshop duration.
8. Define the training capacity.
9. Enter the start time of the workshop.
10. Enter the end time of the workshop.
11. Select the status of the workshop (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
12. Select the type of workshop (Online or Offline).
13. Enter the grant amount.
14. Confirm the training capacity.
15. Identify the grant authorized person.
16. Enter the grant authorized contact person's information.
17. Enter the email of the grant authorized person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Grant Writing** (`grant_writing_training_form`, text, optional): 
  A brief description of the grant writing training program.
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, optional): 
  The title of the workshop.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): 
  The date on which the workshop will take place.
* **Training Objective** (`training_objective`, text, optional): 
  A clear statement of the training objective.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`, text, optional): 
  The venue where the workshop will be held.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): 
  The time of the workshop, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, optional): 
  The physical location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, text, optional): 
  The duration of the workshop in terms of hours or minutes.
* **Training Capacity** (`training_capacity`, number, optional): 
  The maximum number of participants the workshop can accommodate.
* **Start Time** (`workshop_start_time`, time, optional): 
  The start time of the workshop, in 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00 for 10:00 AM).
* **End Time** (`workshop_end_time`, time, optional): 
  The end time of the workshop, in 24-hour format (e.g., 15:00 for 3:00 PM).
* **Workshop Status** (`workshop_status`, select_one, optional): 
  The status of the workshop (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional): 
  The type of workshop (Online or Offline).
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number, optional): 
  The amount of the grant.
* **Training Capacity** (`training_capacity`, text, optional): 
  Confirm the training capacity.
* **Grant Authorized** (`grant_authorized`, email, optional): 
  The email of the grant authorized person.
* **Grant Authorized Contact** (`grant_authorized_contact`, text, optional): 
  The name of the grant authorized person.
* **Grant Authorized Email** (`grant_authorized_email`, email, optional): 
  The email of the grant authorized person.
