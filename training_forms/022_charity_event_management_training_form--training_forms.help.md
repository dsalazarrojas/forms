# Charity Event Management Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the training team tailor the content and format of the training session to meet your specific needs and interests as a trainee.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
- Use a pen and paper to note down your answers before typing them into the form.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Trainee Name
**Trainee Name** (`trainee_name`, text, required): Your full name.
### Email Address
**Email Address** (`trainee_email`, email, required): Your email address for contact purposes.
### Phone Number
**Phone Number** (`trainee_phone`, text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
### Organization Name
**Organization Name** (`organization`, text, required): The name of the charity or nonprofit organization you represent.
### Current Role
**Current Role** (`current_role`, text, required): Your current position within the organization.
### Event Management Experience
**Event Management Experience** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of your experience in event management.
### Training Track
**Training Track** (`training_track`, select_one, required): Select the focus area you wish to learn more about.
### Topics of Interest
**Topics of Interest** (`training_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas you would like to learn more about.
### Preferred Training Format
**Preferred Training Format** (`training_format`, select_one, required): Select how you prefer to receive the training.
### Training Availability
**Training Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select when you are available for the training session.
### Learning Goals
**Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, text, required): Describe what you hope to achieve through this training session.
### Types of Events You Manage
**Types of Events You Manage** (`event_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of events you have managed or are currently managing.
### Typical Team Size
**Typical Team Size** (`team_size`, select_one, required): Select the typical size of your team.
### Typical Event Budget
**Typical Event Budget** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select the range of budget you typically manage.
### Interested in Certification
**Interested in Certification** (`certification_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in certification upon completion of the training.
### Additional Training Needs
**Additional Training Needs** (`additional_needs`, text, optional): Provide any other areas you would like to learn more about.

## Tips
- Please be as specific as possible when providing your answers.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will use the information you provide to tailor the content and format of the training to meet your needs.
