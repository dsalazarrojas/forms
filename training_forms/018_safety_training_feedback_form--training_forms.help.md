# Safety Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for users to offer feedback and suggestions on the safety training sessions. It helps to identify areas for improvement and enhance the overall quality of the training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant page by clicking on the corresponding link.
2. Complete the form by filling out the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Form 1** (`training_form_1_label`, text, optional): Please provide a brief summary of the training session you attended.
* **Training Form 2** (`training_form_2_label`, text, optional): Share any notable comments, observations, or thoughts about the training session.
* **Training Form 3** (`training_form_3_label`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate your level of satisfaction with the training session. 
  * Option 1: Highly Satisfactory
  * Option 2: Somewhat Satisfactory
  * Option 3: Not Satisfactory
  * Option 4: Not Satisfactory at all
  * Option 5: Very Dissatisfactory
* **Training Form 4** (`training_form_4_label`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the next session.
* **Training Form 5** (`training_form_5_label`, number, optional): Rate your understanding of the training material. 
* **Training Form 6** (`training_form_6_label`, date, optional): Provide the date and time you attended the training session.
* **Training Form 7** (`training_form_7_label`, time, optional): Provide the duration of the training session. 
* **Training Form 8** (`training_form_8_label`, number, optional): Indicate how many people attended the training session. 

## Tips
* Please provide your feedback in a constructive and respectful manner.
* Ensure all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any concerns or issues, you can report them to your supervisor or trainer.
