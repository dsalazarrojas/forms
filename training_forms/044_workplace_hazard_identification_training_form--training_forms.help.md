# Workplace Hazard Identification Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by employees after a workplace hazard identification training session. It is used to gather information about the training session, including the type of hazards identified, control measures taken, and number of employees trained. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and reported correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the training session and identify any hazards that were discussed or presented.
2. Select the appropriate risk factors that apply to the hazards identified (Risk, Hazard, Injury).
3. Provide a description of each hazard identified.
4. Describe any control measures taken or recommended to mitigate the hazards.
5. Select the reporting procedure that was followed (Reporting, Investigation, Corrective action).
6. Indicate which training materials were used during the session (Video, Presentation, Manual).
7. Enter the location and date of the training session.
8. Enter the number of employees, supervisors, and management staff that were trained.
9. Evaluate the effectiveness of the training session.
10. Enter any comments or additional information about the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can enter any general information about the training session.
* **Risk Factors**: Select all the risk factors that apply to the hazards identified. These are: 
  * Risk: The likelihood and potential impact of the hazard.
  * Hazard: A situation or condition that poses a risk.
  * Injury: An actual or potential harm or risk of harm.
* **Description**: Please provide a description of each hazard identified.
* **Control Measures**: Describe any control measures taken or recommended to mitigate the hazards.
* **Reporting Procedure**: Select the procedure followed for reporting the hazards, these are: 
  * Reporting: Reporting the hazards to the appropriate authorities.
  * Investigation: Investigating the cause and effect of the hazards.
  * Corrective Action: Taking corrective action to mitigate or eliminate the hazards.
* **Training Materials**: Indicate which training materials were used during the session, these are: 
  * Video: A video presentation.
  * Presentation: A slideshow or PowerPoint presentation.
  * Manual: A written guide or handbook.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training session was held.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time of the training session.
* **Number of Employees Trained**: Enter the number of employees trained.
* **Number of Supervisors Trained**: Enter the number of supervisors trained.
* **Number of Management Trained**: Enter the number of management staff trained.
* **Evaluation**: Evaluate the effectiveness of the training session as: 
  * Effective: The session was effective.
  * Not Effective: The session was not effective.
  * Somewhat Effective: The session was somewhat effective.
* **Evaluation Comments**: Enter any comments about the training session.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information about the training session.
* **Training Facilitator**: Enter the name of the person who facilitated the training session.
* **Organization**: Enter the organization where the training session was held.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the person filling out the form.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the training session was held.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the contact name of the person responsible for the training session.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the training session was held.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the training session.
* **Submit**: This is a submit button that saves your entries to the form.
