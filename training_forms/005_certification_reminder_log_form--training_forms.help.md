# Certification Reminder Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Certification Reminder Log Form is designed to help you track and manage upcoming expirations and renewal owners for certifications. Fill in the form to create a new entry and keep yourself updated on the renewal status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **-- Certification Reminder Log --**: This is a header section.
2. **Certification name**: Enter the name of the certification or credential that requires renewal.
3. **Holder name**: Type in the name of the certificate holder, who needs to renew the certification.
4. **Holder email**: Provide the contact email for the certificate holder to receive renewal notifications (optional).
5. **Renewal due date**: Enter the date when the renewal is required.
6. **Reminder owner**: Choose the staff member responsible for sending reminders about the certification renewal.
7. **Renewal status**: Select the current renewal progress from the options: Not started, In progress, Completed, or Overdue.
8. **Required documents**: Check the documents needed for renewal, such as Proof of training, ID, Payment confirmation, or Other.
9. **Notes**: Add any additional context or information about the certification and renewal process.
10. **Last alert sent**: Date when the last reminder was sent (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- Certification Reminder Log --**: This is the header section of the form, which is just an informative header and does not require any input.
* **Certification name**: Enter the name of the certification or credential that requires renewal. This field is required.
* **Holder name**: Type in the name of the certificate holder. This field is required.
* **Holder email**: Provide the contact email for the certificate holder to receive renewal notifications. This field is not required, but it's recommended for efficient communication.
* **Renewal due date**: Enter the date when the renewal is required. This field is required.
* **Reminder owner**: Choose the staff member responsible for sending reminders about the certification renewal. This field is required.
* **Renewal status**: Select the current renewal progress from the options: Not started, In progress, Completed, or Overdue. This field is required.
* **Required documents**: Check the documents needed for renewal. This field is not required, but it's a helpful checklist for the reminder owner.
* **Notes**: Add any additional context or information about the certification and renewal process. This field is not required.
* **Last alert sent**: Date when the last reminder was sent. This field is not required, but it's useful for tracking the reminder history.

## Tips
* Make sure to check the certification renewal status regularly to avoid missed deadlines.
* Update the required documents if any are missing or changed.
* Send reminders to the certificate holder and other relevant staff members.
* Review the notes section for additional information about the certification and renewal process.
* This form is for internal use and does not store any sensitive or confidential information.
