# cross-training-feedback-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from trainees on their experience with the training sessions. Your input will help us understand what worked well and what didn't, so we can make improvements for future trainings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any trouble or questions about a particular question, please let us know so we can help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This is the name that you wish to be displayed in our records.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is also requested for our records.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your feedback, if needed.
* **What did you like most about the training?**: Describe the part of the training that you enjoyed the most or found the most helpful. Be as specific as possible.
* **What did you like least about the training?**: If there was a part of the training that you didn't find useful, please share with us so we can understand where to improve.
* **How would you rate this training?**: Rate the training on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **Which of the following topics did you find most beneficial?**: This question is asking which topics from the options below were most helpful to you. Please select 'Yes' for topics that were beneficial and 'No' for those that were not.
	+ Select One: This question is for selecting one topic that was beneficial.
* **Which of the following topics were you exposed to?**: This question is asking for multiple topics that you were exposed to during the training. Please select 'Yes' for topics that you were exposed to and 'No' for those that you weren't.
* **What is your date of training?**: Enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **Which of the following topics were you exposed to?**: Please select multiple topics that you were exposed to during the training.
	+ Select Multiple: This is for selecting multiple topics you were exposed to.

This form is designed to gather feedback about your experience, so please be as specific and honest as possible in your answers. Your input is greatly appreciated and will help us improve the training sessions.
