# First Aid Training Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and payment details for first aid training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** page with the required details of the participant.
2. Provide the participant's contact information on the **Participant Contact** page.
3. Enter the training details on the **Training Details** page.
4. Confirm the payment details on the **Payment Details** page.
5. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`first_aid_training_licensing_form_participant_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's name and any other relevant information.

### Participant Contact
* **Participant Contact** (`first_aid_training_licensing_form_participant_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's contact information, such as phone number and email.

### Training Details
* **Training Details** (`first_aid_training_licensing_form_training_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the training session, including date, time, and location.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (`first_aid_training_licensing_form_payment_details`, text, required): Enter the payment information, such as amount and payment method.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`first_aid_training_licensing_form_submit`, text, required): Click the submit button to complete the form and process the information.
