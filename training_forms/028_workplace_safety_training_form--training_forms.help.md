# Workplace Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Safety Training Form is used to collect information about workplace safety training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select whether you attended a training session ("Yes" or "No") in the "Training Sessions" field.
2.  If you attended a training session, enter the date you attended in the "Training Date" field.
3.  Enter the start and end times of the training session in the "Training Start Time" and "Training End Time" fields.
4.  Enter the location of the training session in the "Training Location" field.
5.  Indicate whether the training session was supervised ("Yes" or "No") in the "Training Supervisor" field.
6.  Select whether you attended all or part of the training session ("Yes" or "No") in the "Attendance" field.
7.  Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
8.  Enter your email address in the "Employee E-mail" field.
9.  Enter your phone number in the "Employee Phone" field.
10. Select whether your training status is active ("Active") or inactive ("Inactive") in the "Training Status" field.
11. If you have any comments or feedback, enter them in the "Comments" field.
12. Check whether the training has been assigned to someone ("Yes" or "No") in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Sessions**: Select "Yes" if you attended a training session, "No" otherwise.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date you attended the training session in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Training Start Time**: Enter the start time of the training session in 12-hour format (e.g., 9:00 AM).
* **Training End Time**: Enter the end time of the training session in 12-hour format (e.g., 10:30 AM).
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the training session took place.
* **Training Supervisor**: Select "Yes" if you were supervised during the training session, "No" otherwise.
* **Attendance**: Select "Yes" if you attended the entire training session, "No" if you left early or did not attend part of the session.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official company documents.
* **Employee E-mail**: Enter your company email address.
* **Employee Phone**: Enter your company phone number.
* **Training Status**: Select "Active" if you are currently enrolled in the training program, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about the training session.
* **Assigned To**: Select "Yes" if the training has been assigned to someone, "No" otherwise.
