# Training Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Application Form is designed to collect information about participants for a training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which is indicated by an asterisk (\*) on each required field.
2. Enter your name in the **participant_name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **participant_email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **participant_phone** field.
5. Enter your confirmation email address in the **confirm_participant_email** field.
6. Enter your confirmation phone number in the **confirm_participant_phone** field.
7. Enter the name of the training in the **training_name** field.
8. Enter the start date of the training in the **training_start_date** field.
9. Enter the end date of the training in the **training_end_date** field.
10. Enter the number of hours you plan to train in the **participant_hours** field.
11. Enter the duration of the training in the **training_duration** field.
12. Enter any additional information about your training in the **additional_information** field.
13. Confirm your participant information by re-entering it in the confirmation fields.
14. Review your completed form for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **training_application_form** (text): Enter a brief description of your training application.
* **participant_information** (text): Enter your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **training_information** (text): Enter details about the training you are applying for.
* **additional_information** (text): Enter any additional information about your training.
* **confirm** (text): Re-enter your participant information for verification purposes.
* **participant_email** (email): Enter a valid email address.
* **confirm_participant_email** (email): Re-enter a valid email address for verification purposes.
* **participant_phone** (text): Enter a valid phone number.
* **confirm_participant_phone** (text): Re-enter a valid phone number for verification purposes.
* **participant_name** (text): Enter your name.
* **training_name** (text): Enter the name of the training you are applying for.
* **training_start_date** (date): Enter the start date of the training.
* **training_end_date** (date): Enter the end date of the training.
* **participant_hours** (number): Enter the number of hours you plan to train.
* **training_duration** (number): Enter the duration of the training in hours.
* **participant_notes** (text): Enter any notes about your training.
* **training_notes** (text): Enter notes about the training.
* **confirm_participant_name** (text): Re-enter your name for verification purposes.
* **confirm_training_name** (text): Re-enter the name of the training for verification purposes.
* **confirm_participant_hours** (number): Re-enter the number of hours you plan to train for verification purposes.
* **confirm_training_duration** (number): Re-enter the duration of the training for verification purposes.
* **confirm_participant_notes** (text): Re-enter any notes about your training for verification purposes.
* **confirm_training_notes** (text): Re-enter notes about the training for verification purposes.
