# Simple Training Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Simple Training Request Form" is designed to allow employees to submit a request for training or other types of educational activities. This form will be reviewed and processed by the training coordinator or manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the fields marked as "required".
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the fields that ask for a specific type of training or approval.
3. Enter a valid date and time for the training date and start/end times.
4. Provide a clear reason for your training request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your employee ID number as it appears on your ID card.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you need training, "No" otherwise.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to have the training.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the training session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the training session.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, optional): Provide a clear explanation of why you need the training.
* **Manager ID** (`manager_id`, select one, optional): Select "Yes" if you have your manager's approval, "No" otherwise.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address if they approve your training request.
