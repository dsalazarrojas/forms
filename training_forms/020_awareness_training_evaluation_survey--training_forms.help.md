# Awareness Training Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this survey is to evaluate your experience and perceptions about the training you attended. Your input is valuable to us, and your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and provide better training experiences in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the type of training you attended from the options provided.
2.  Enter the date and location of the training.
3.  Fill out the rest of the form with your thoughts and experiences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of training you attended (e.g., Diversity and inclusion, Sexual harassment prevention, etc.).
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Enter the date you attended the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the training was held.
* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Provide your name (if you choose to).
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for certificate delivery (if applicable).
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Job Role** (`job_role`, text, optional): Enter your job role.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Rate your experience level in the topic (e.g., Beginner - new to topic, Some familiarity, etc.).
* **Training Format** (`training_format`, select_one, required): Choose the format in which the training was delivered (e.g., In-person classroom, Virtual live session, etc.).
* **Content Quality** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the training content (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one, required): Rate the relevance of the training content to your job (e.g., Very relevant, Relevant, Somewhat relevant, etc.).
* **Trainer Knowledge** (`trainer_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the trainer's knowledge and expertise (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Trainer Presentation** (`trainer_presentation`, select_one, required): Rate the trainer's presentation skills (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Pacing** (`pacing`, select_one, required): Rate the pace of the training (e.g., Too slow, Just right, Too fast, etc.).
* **Materials Quality** (`materials_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the training materials (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Engagement Level** (`engagement_level`, select_one, required): Rate your engagement level during the training (e.g., Very engaged, Engaged, Neutral, etc.).
* **Interactivity** (`interactivity`, select_one, required): Rate the interactivity opportunities provided (e.g., Excellent, Good, Average, etc.).
* **Knowledge Gained** (`knowledge_gained`, select_one, required): Rate how much you learned (e.g., A great deal, A lot, Some, etc.).
* **Confidence Increase** (`confidence_increase`, select_one, required): Rate your confidence level after the training (e.g., Much more confident, Somewhat more confident, etc.).
* **Will Apply Learning** (`will_apply_learning`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you will apply what you learned (e.g., Definitely will, Probably will, etc.).
* **Most Valuable Topic** (`most_valuable_topic`, text, optional): Specify the most valuable topic covered.
* **Least Valuable Topic** (`least_valuable_topic`, text, optional): Specify the least valuable topic covered (if any).
* **Topics Needing More Time** (`topics_needing_more_time`, text, optional): Suggest topics that need more time or attention.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Suggest improvements for future trainings.
* **Recommend to Others** (`recommend_to_others`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you would recommend this training to others (e.g., Definitely yes, Probably yes, etc.).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the training (e.g., Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, etc.).
* **Additional Training Needs** (`additional_training_needs`, text, optional): Specify any additional training needs you have.
* **Certificate Needed** (`certificate_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you need a certificate for the training completion (e.g., Yes, please, No, thank you, etc.).
