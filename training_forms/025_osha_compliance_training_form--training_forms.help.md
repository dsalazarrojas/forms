# osha_compliance_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OSHA Compliance Training Form is used to record and document employee training sessions, ensuring compliance with OSHA regulations. This form captures essential details about the training session, including the session's ID, date, location, topic, and trainer. It also allows users to track attendance and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the training session ID.
2. Select the training session date.
3. Enter the training facility name.
4. Specify the training topic.
5. Enter the employee ID (if applicable).
6. Enter the employee name (if applicable).
7. Enter the supervisor's name (if applicable).
8. Select the training start date.
9. Select the training end date.
10. Enter the training location details (address, city, state, zip).
11. Enter the trainer's name and phone number.
12. Enter the training hours.
13. Select the training attendance (yes/no).
14. Select whether a training certificate was issued (yes/no).
15. Add comments (if necessary).
16. Enter the trainer's email address.
17. Enter the training session notes.
18. Select the training session status (pending/completed/cancelled).
19. Enter the reason for the status change (if applicable).
20. Sign the form with the trainer's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Training Session ID** (`1`, number, required/optional): Enter the unique ID of the training session.
- **Training Session Date** (`2`, date, required): Select the date when the training session occurred.
- **Training Facility** (`3`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training facility.
- **Training Topic** (`4`, text, optional): Specify the topic of the training session.
- **Employee ID** (`5`, number, optional): Enter the employee's ID (if applicable).
- **Employee Name** (`6`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name (if applicable).
- **Supervisor Name** (`7`, text, optional): Enter the supervisor's name (if applicable).
- **Training Start Date** (`8`, date, required): Select the start date of the training session.
- **Training End Date** (`9`, date, required): Select the end date of the training session.
- **Training Location** (`10`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training session.
- **Trainer Name** (`11`, text, optional): Enter the trainer's name.
- **Training Hours** (`12`, number, required): Enter the total hours of the training session.
- **Training Attendance** (`13`, select_one, required): Select whether the employee attended the training (yes/no).
- **Training Certificate** (`14`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether a training certificate was issued (yes/no).
- **Comments** (`15`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about the training session.
- **Training Location Address** (`16`, text, optional): Enter the address of the training location.
- **Training Location City** (`17`, text, optional): Enter the city of the training location.
- **Training Location State** (`18`, text, optional): Enter the state of the training location.
- **Training Location Zip** (`19`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the training location.
- **Trainer Phone** (`20`, text, optional): Enter the trainer's phone number.
- **Trainer Email** (`21`, email, optional): Enter the trainer's email address.
- **Training Session Notes** (`22`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the training session.
- **Training Session Status** (`23`, select_one, required): Select the status of the training session (pending/completed/cancelled).
- **Training Session Status Reason** (`24`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the status change (if applicable).
- **Trainer Signature** (`25`, text, required): Sign the form with the trainer's signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the training session date and times for accuracy.
- Ensure that the training location address is complete and accurate.
- Use the trainer's signature to confirm that the training session was completed.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled in correctly.
