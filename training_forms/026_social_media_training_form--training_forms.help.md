# social_media_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The social media training form is used to record and organize participant information for social media training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
See the step-by-step instructions provided above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* First Name (text, First Name, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
### Email
* Email (email, Email, required/optional): Enter your email address here.
### Phone
* Phone (text, Phone, required/optional): Enter your phone number here.
### Date
* Date (date, Date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth here.
### Training Needs
* Training Needs (text, Training Needs, required/optional): Specify your training needs here.
### Training Level
* Training Level (select_one, Training Level, required/optional): Choose your training level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced) here.
### Training Focus
* Training Focus (select_multiple, Training Focus, required/optional): Select your training focus (Social Media 101, Content Creation, Analytics) here.
### Participant Information
* Participant Information (text, Participant Information, required/optional): Enter any additional participant information here.
### Assigned Tool
* Assigned Tool (text, Assigned Tool, required/optional): Specify the assigned tool or equipment for the participant here.
### Participant Email
* Participant Email (email, Participant Email, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address here.
### Participant Phone
* Participant Phone (text, Participant Phone, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number here.
### Participant Note
* Participant Note (note, Participant Note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant here.
### Participant Date
* Participant Date (date, Participant Date, required/optional): Enter the participant's date of birth here.
### Participant Information 2
* Participant Information 2 (text, Participant Information 2, required/optional): Enter any other participant information here.

### Participant Email 2

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field to ensure the participant's training experience is tailored to their needs.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the training administrator for guidance.
