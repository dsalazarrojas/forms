# Guest Relations Enhancement Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who require training on guest relations and etiquette, to ensure an exceptional guest experience during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields carefully to guarantee accurate processing of your registration.
- Make sure to review the form thoroughly before submitting your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

**First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false)
- Enter your first name.

**Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false)
- Enter your last name.

**Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
- Enter your email address.

**Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
- Enter your phone number.

**Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false)
- Enter your company name if you are affiliated with one.

**Job Title** (`job_title`, select_multiple, required: false)
- Select one or more of the following job titles: 
  - CEO
  - Manager
  - Director
  - Employee

**Department** (`department`, select_one, required: false)
- Select one of the following departments: 
  - Marketing
  - Sales
  - HR
  - IT

**Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, required: false)
- Enter the date of the event you plan to attend.

**Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, required: false)
- Enter the time of the event you plan to attend.

**Location** (`location`, text, required: false)
- Enter the location of the event.

**Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required: false)
- Add any special requests you may have for the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure that we can process your registration correctly.
- If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
