# Sales Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Onboarding Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool used to evaluate a salesperson's onboarding process, ensuring they have the necessary knowledge, skills, and strategies to succeed in their sales role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or type the requested information for each field. Be as thorough as possible to ensure an accurate assessment. If you are unsure about a particular question, please refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Onboarding Questions
* **Onboarding Questions** (`onboarding_questions`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all questions that you have been asked during your onboarding process. This could include questions about sales policies, product knowledge, or other relevant information.
### 2. Sales Knowledge
* **Sales Knowledge** (`sales_knowledge`, `text`, required): Describe your current level of sales knowledge, including your understanding of sales policies, product features, and customer needs.
### 3. Sales Skills
* **Sales Skills** (`sales_skills`, `select_one`, required): Please select whether you have the following sales skills: Yes or No. This will help us understand your ability to handle sales situations effectively.
### 4. Sales Strategies
* **Sales Strategies** (`sales_strategies`, `text`, required): Describe your approach to sales, including your process for identifying and addressing customer needs, handling objections, and closing deals.
### 5. Sales Product Knowledge
* **Sales Product Knowledge** (`sales_product_knowledge`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all products you have knowledge about. This is essential to understand your product awareness and ability to effectively communicate product features and benefits.
### 6. Onboarding Questions 2
* **Onboarding Questions 2** (`onboarding_questions_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select all questions you have been asked during your onboarding process (if any).
### 7. Sales Training Questions Answers
* **Sales Training Questions Answers** (`sales_training_questions_answers`, `text`, required): Please provide your answers to any sales training questions or exercises you have completed during onboarding.
### 8. Product Training Schedule
* **Product Training Schedule** (`product_training_schedule`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you completed your most recent product training, or if you haven't yet attended training, select "No".
### 9. Next Steps
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select any next steps you would like to take as part of your onboarding process, such as additional training or shadowing a sales colleague.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate assessment.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your manager or trainer for guidance.
* This form is intended to be a comprehensive assessment of your onboarding process, so please provide as much detail as possible.
