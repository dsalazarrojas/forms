# Post Training Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Post Training Risk Assessment" form is used to report potential or actual risks that occurred after completing training. This form helps identify areas for improvement and ensures that necessary steps are taken to mitigate and prevent similar incidents in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Information**: This section is used to provide a brief overview of the training session and the risk that was identified.
2. Select any **Risk Areas** that apply to the identified risk. This will help categorize and prioritize the risks.
3. In the **Specific Incident** field, provide a detailed description of the incident.
4. **Training Completed**: If you have actually completed the training, select "True". If not, select "False" and provide an explanation.
5. Enter any **Comments** you have about the incident, and how it can be improved in the future.
6. Enter your **Submitter Information** including name, position, and email.
7. Enter your **Submitter Name** and **Submitter Position** as they appear on your business card.
8. Enter your **Submitter Email** and **Submitter Phone** in a format that follows your company's email and phone format guidelines.
9. Enter the date and time you are submitting the form as the **Submitter Date** and **Submitter Time** respectively.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   *General Information* (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the training session and the risk that was identified.
*   *Risk Areas* (`risk_areas`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to select multiple risk areas that apply to the identified risk. The options are: Risk of Injury or Illness, Risk of Property Damage, Risk of Financial Loss, Risk of Equipment Damage, Risk of Loss of Productivity, Risk of Loss of Resources, and Risk of Environmental Damage.
*   *Specific Incident* (`specific_incident`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
*   *Training Completed* (`training_completed`, select_one, optional): If you have actually completed the training, select "True". If not, select "False" and provide an explanation.
*   *Comments* (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments you have about the incident.
*   *Submitter Information* (`submitter_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and position as they appear on your business card.
*   *Submitter Name* (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business card.
*   *Submitter Position* (`submitter_position`, text, optional): Enter your position as it appears on your business card.
*   *Submitter Email* (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in a format that follows your company's email format guidelines.
*   *Submitter Phone* (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in a format that follows your company's phone format guidelines.
*   *Submitter Date* (`submitter_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting the form.
*   *Submitter Time* (`submitter_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are submitting the form.
