# Team Collaboration Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track team collaboration training sessions. Complete this form to provide information about the team, session, and participant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter the team leader's name in the "team_leader" field.
3. Enter the team name in the "team_name" field.
4. Enter the name of the training session in the "training_session_name" field.
5. (Optional) Enter the date of the training session in the "training_session_date" field.
6. (Optional) Enter the start and end times of the session in the "session_start_time" and "session_end_time" fields.
7. Enter the duration of the session in minutes in the "session_duration" field.
8. Enter the number of team members in the "team_size" field.
9. (Optional) Provide any additional feedback about the session in the "feedback" field.
10. (Optional) Enter your contact email in the "email" field.
11. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "phone" fields.
12. (Optional) Add any additional comments about the session in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant in this field.
* **team_leader** (`team_leader`, text, required): Enter the name of the team leader.
* **team_name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team.
* **training_session_name** (`training_session_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the training session.
* **training_session_date** (date, optional): (Optional) Enter the date of the training session in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **session_start_time** (time, optional): (Optional) Enter the start time of the session in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:30).
* **session_end_time** (time, optional): (Optional) Enter the end time of the session in the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 16:00).
* **session_duration** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the session in minutes.
* **team_size** (`team_size`, number, required): Enter the number of team members attending the session.
* **feedback** (note, optional): (Optional) Add any feedback or comments about the session.
* **email** (email, optional): (Optional) Enter your contact email.
* **phone** (text, optional): (Optional) Enter your phone number.
* **comments** (text, optional): (Optional) Add any additional comments about the session.
