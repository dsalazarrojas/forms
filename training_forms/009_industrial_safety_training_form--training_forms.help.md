# Industrial Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Industrial Safety Training Form" is a document used to record and track information about industrial safety training sessions. It helps to ensure that participants have received necessary training and are aware of their safety responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the "general_info" field with a brief description of the training session, including the date, time, and location.
2. In the "participant_info" field, enter the name and contact information of the participant.
3. In the "safety_training" field, describe the safety training received during the session, including any notable discussions or topics covered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **general_info**: <small>A brief description of the training session, including date, time, and location.</small>
* **participant_info**: <small>Enter the name and contact information of the participant.</small>
* **safety_training**: <small>Description of the safety training received during the session, including notable discussions or topics covered.</small>

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the "safety_training" field to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* Make sure to proofread your answers for spelling and grammar errors.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your supervisor or trainer for clarification.
