# Restaurant Training Guide Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from employees about their completion of training sessions. It helps to ensure that training records are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Name in the "employee_name" field.
2. Enter your Supervisor's Name in the "supervisor_name" field.
3. Enter the Training Manager's Name in the "training_manager_name" field.
4. Select the date on which you completed the training in the "training_date" field.
5. Enter the date on which you completed the training in the "completion_date" field.
6. Add a note with your employee signature in the "employee_signature" field.
7. Add a note with your supervisor's signature in the "supervisor_signature" field.
8. Add a note with your training manager's signature in the "training_manager_signature" field.
9. Enter the number of training hours completed in the "training_hours_completed" field.
10. Enter the total number of minutes completed in the "training_minutes_completed" field.
11. Enter the total number of seconds completed in the "training_seconds_completed" field.
12. Select the duration of the training in the "training_duration" field.
13. Select the topics that were completed during the training in the "training_topics_completed" field.
14. Confirm that you have completed the training in the "employee_acknowledgment" field by selecting "yes" or "no".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or company records.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the full name of your supervisor as it appears on their ID or company records.
* **Training Manager Name** (`training_manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the full name of your training manager as it appears on their ID or company records.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required: false): Select the date on which you completed the training.
* **Completion Date** (`completion_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which you completed the training.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, note, required: false): Add a note with your employee signature to confirm completion of the training.
* **Supervisor Signature** (`supervisor_signature`, note, required: false): Add a note with your supervisor's signature to confirm completion of the training.
* **Training Manager Signature** (`training_manager_signature`, note, required: false): Add a note with your training manager's signature to confirm completion of the training.
* **Training Hours Completed** (`training_hours_completed`, number, required: false): Enter the number of hours completed during the training.
* **Training Minutes Completed** (`training_minutes_completed`, number, required: false): Enter the total number of minutes completed during the training.
* **Training Seconds Completed** (`training_seconds_completed`, number, required: false): Enter the total number of seconds completed during the training.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, select_one, required: false): Select the duration of the training session from the options provided.
* **Training Topics Completed** (`training_topics_completed`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the topics that were completed during the training session from the options provided.
* **Employee Acknowledgment** (`employee_acknowledgment`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you have completed the training by selecting "yes" or "no".
