# Course Training Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Training Request Form Template is a form used to document and process training requests within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the training request, including the employee's details and training specifics.
2. Fill in the employee's information in the "Employee" field.
3. Select the type of training the employee is requesting in the "Training Type" field.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the training in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Provide a description of the training in the "Description" field (if applicable).
6. Enter any other relevant information in the remaining fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee** (1, number, required/optional): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Training Type** (2, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of training the employee is requesting (e.g., "Yes" for yes, "No" for no).
* **Start Date** (3, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the training.
* **End Date** (4, text, required/optional): Enter the end date of the training.
* **Status** (5, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the training (e.g., "Active" for active, "Inactive" for inactive).
* **Submitted By** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether the request was submitted by the employee or not (e.g., "Yes" for yes, "No" for no).
* **Training Location** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether the training is located at a specific site or not (e.g., "Yes" for yes, "No" for no).
* **Training Duration** (8, number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the training.
* **Description** (9, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the training.
* **Training Request ID** (10, text, required/optional): Enter the training request ID.
* **Form ID** (11, text, required/optional): Enter the form ID.
* **Submitted On** (12, date, required/optional): Enter the date the request was submitted.
* **Submitted By Employees** (13, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether the request was submitted by employees or not (e.g., "Yes" for yes, "No" for no).
* **Assigned Tool** (14, text, required/optional): Enter the assigned tool or software.
* **Created By** (15, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the user who created the form.
* **Created On** (16, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated By** (17, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the user who updated the form.
* **Updated On** (18, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was updated.
* **Deleted By** (19, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the user who deleted the form.
* **Deleted On** (20, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was deleted.
