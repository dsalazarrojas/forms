# Training Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Satisfaction Survey is used to gather feedback from trainees on their experience with a training session. It helps us evaluate and improve the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Select your satisfaction level for "Overall trainee satisfaction" from the options provided (Satisfied, Dissatisfied).
2.  Select your answer for "How effective do you feel the training was?" from the options provided (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3.  Select your satisfaction level for "How satisfied are you with the trainer?" from the options provided (Yes, No).
4.  Select how knowledgeable you think the trainer was from the options provided (Extremely knowledgeable, Somewhat knowledgeable, Not very knowledgeable).
5.  Select whether you found the training material useful (Yes, No).
6.  Select how well the time was managed from the options provided (Very well, Somewhat well, Not very well).
7.  If you have any additional feedback, type it in the "Additional feedback" field.
8.  Enter your email address, so we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall trainee satisfaction** (Select multiple): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the training session. Select the option that best reflects your feelings.
* **How effective do you feel the training was?** (Select one): This question asks about your perception of the training's effectiveness. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **How satisfied are you with the trainer?** (Select multiple): This question asks about your satisfaction level with the trainer. Select the options that best reflect your feelings.
* **How knowledgeable was the trainer?** (Select one): This question asks about how knowledgeable you think the trainer was. Select the option that best describes your opinion.
* **Was the training material useful?** (Select multiple): This question asks about your opinion on the usefulness of the training material. Select the options that best reflect your feelings.
* **How well was the time managed?** (Select one): This question asks about how well the time was managed during the training session. Select the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Additional feedback** (Note): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them here. Please be specific and provide concrete feedback.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you if needed.

Note: All questions are optional, except for entering your email address, which is required.
