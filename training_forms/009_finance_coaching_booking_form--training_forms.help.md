# Finance Coaching Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule sessions with finance coaches. Please fill out the form with your details to book a session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select a coach from our available options, or choose "Other" if you have a specific preference.
3. Choose a session date and time that suits you.
4. Select the session duration and frequency that you need.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or chat).
6. Specify any additional information or topics you would like to discuss during the session.
7. Add any comments or notes for the coach.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as the client.
* **Client Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Coach Name**: Select the coach that suits your needs or choose "Other" if you have a specific preference.
* **Session Date**: Choose a date for your session.
* **Session Time**: Select a time for your session.
* **Session Duration**: Choose the length of your session.
* **Session Frequency**: Select the frequency of your sessions (e.g., one time only, weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Client Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Coach Specialties**: Select the specialty area you would like help with (e.g., Financial Planning, Investment Management, Career Coaching, etc.).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (email, phone, or chat).
* **Session Topic**: Specify any additional topics you would like to discuss during the session.
* **Session Notes**: Add any notes or comments for the coach.
* **Booking Status**: Select the status of your booking (pending, booked, cancelled, etc.).
* **Client Comments**: Add any comments or feedback for the coach.
* **Coach Comments**: Add any comments or feedback for the coach.
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional information you think is relevant.
* **Client Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your booking.
* **Coach Signature**: Sign and date this form to confirm your acceptance of the booking.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure accurate communication.
* Be specific when selecting coach specialties or session topics.
* Review the coach's profile before selecting them to ensure you're comfortable with their expertise.
* If you need to cancel or reschedule, please contact us at least 24 hours prior to the session.
