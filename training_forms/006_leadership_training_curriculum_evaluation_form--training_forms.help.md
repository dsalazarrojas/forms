# leadership_training_curriculum_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of the leadership training program. It helps in gathering feedback from participants on various aspects of the program, including the training title, description, participant feedback, and overall rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Training Program** field with the title of the training program you attended.
2. Provide a brief description of the program in the **Program Description** field.
3. Select the areas where you received feedback from the training program in the **Participant Feedback** field.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the training program in the **Overall Rating** field.
5. If you have any suggestions or comments, please add them in the **Comments and Suggestions** field.
6. If you want to share your email address or contact information, please fill in the **Email Address**, **Contact Name**, and **Supervisor Name** fields.
7. Finally, confirm whether the training program was evaluated using a formal evaluation method and whether this form was completed completely or partially.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Training Program** (`training_program`, `text`, required/optional): The title of the training program you attended.
  • Enter the title of the training program in this field.
  • Please make sure it is accurate and reflects the actual name of the program.
- **Program Title** (`program_title`, `text`, required/optional): A brief title of the training program.

- **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Feedback received from the participant during the program.
  • Select all areas where you received feedback.
