# User Training Materials Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from training participants on their experience with the training materials. It helps us understand how well the materials met their expectations and identify areas for improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
* Please answer the questions on each page as accurately as possible.
* For each question, provide a clear and concise response that will help us understand your thoughts on the session.
* Make sure to answer all pages, even if you're unsure about something.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, note, not required)
	+ Please provide your honest feedback about the recent session you attended. This will help us understand your experience and what we can improve for future sessions.
* **Training Feedback** (`training_feedback`, note, not required)
	+ Share your thoughts about the overall quality and effectiveness of the training materials used during the session. This will help us improve the materials for better learning outcomes.
* **Materials Suggestions** (`materials_suggestions`, note, not required)
	+ If you have any suggestions or ideas for improving the training materials, please let us know. This will help us refine the content to better meet your needs.
* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, note, not required)
	+ Share any comments or feedback about the trainer's performance and delivery style. This will help us improve our trainers' skills for better learning experiences.
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required)
	+ Please provide the date of the session you attended, if possible. This will help us keep track of feedback from different sessions.
## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as they will help us create better training materials and experiences for all participants.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
