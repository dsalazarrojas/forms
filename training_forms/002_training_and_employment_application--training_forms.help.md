# Training And Employment Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for training purposes, such as job training programs or certification processes. It is not intended for general employment applications.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2.  Ensure you have the necessary information ready for each field.
3.  Review the form for any errors before submission.
4.  Submit the form once you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Contact Info
Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.

### 2. Employment History
Describe your previous work experience and job roles.

### 3. Skills
Select all relevant skills from the available options, such as programming languages, certifications, or tools experience.

### 4. Email
Enter your professional email address.

### 5. Phone
Enter your work or contact phone number.

### 6. Resume
Attach your resume, highlighting relevant work experience, skills, and achievements.

## Tips
- Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
- Ensure your resume is up-to-date and relevant to the training or certification process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authority or department.
- If you need to reapply, please follow the procedures for doing so.
