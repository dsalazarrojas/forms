# Training Funds Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Funds Request Form is designed to help employees request training funds for their activities. This form is a crucial tool for managers to review and approve or decline the requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the title of the training activity** (`form-title`): Give a brief and descriptive title to the training activity you are requesting funds for.
2. **Enter your employee name** (`employee-name`): Type your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
3. **Check if you need training** (`training-type`): Select "Yes" if you need the training, otherwise choose "No".
4. **Enter the budget amount required** (`budget-amount`): Specify the amount of funds needed for the training.
5. **Enter the training cost** (`training-cost`): Enter the total cost of the training.
6. **Enter the start date of the training** (`start-date`): Type the start date of the training activity.
7. **Enter the end date of the training** (`end-date`): Type the end date of the training activity.
8. **Enter the amount of training funds allocated** (`training-funds-amount`): Enter the amount of funds allocated for the training.
9. **Select the training status** (`training-status`): Select the status of the training activity as "Active" or "Inactive".
10. **Enter your manager's name** (`manager-name`): Type your manager's name as it appears on their personnel record.
11. **Enter your manager's email** (`manager-email`): Type your manager's email address.
12. **Enter your manager's phone number** (`manager-phone`): Enter your manager's phone number.
13. **Enter a comment for your manager** (`manager-comment`): Add any additional comments for your manager's review.
14. **Enter your comment** (`employee-comment`): Add any additional comments for the training activity.
15. **Enter the manager's approval date** (`manager-approval-date`): If approved, this will be filled in by your manager after reviewing the request.
