# Skill Development Training Feedback Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the training sessions, which will be used to improve and refine the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Overall Rating of the session.
3. Rate the Session Quality.
4. Assess the Instructor's Performance.
5. Indicate if Materials Were Provided.
6. Indicate if the Training Content was satisfactory.
7. Share any Recommendations for improvement.
8. Provide your Participant Comments.
9. Enter your Email address.
10. Enter your Phone number.
11. Provide the Date of your visit (or the date of the training session).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the training session you attended, if any.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): Rate the session overall on a scale, with a higher score indicating a more positive experience.
* **Session Quality** (`session_quality`, select_one, required: false): Rate the session quality using the options provided.
* **Instructor Performance** (`instructor_performance`, select_one, required: false): Rate the instructor's performance using the options provided.
* **Materials Provided** (`materials_provided`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if materials were provided for the session.
* **Training Content** (`training_content`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if the training content was satisfactory.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required: false): Share any recommendations for improvement.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your visit (or the date of the training session).
