This guide will walk you through the "Training Evaluation" form, which is designed to collect feedback and evaluation data from trainees after a training session. The purpose of this form is to help trainers assess their effectiveness and make improvements to future training sessions. This form is not meant to be filled out more than once per training session, so please ensure you only fill it out once per session.

# Training Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Training Evaluation form is a tool used to collect feedback from trainees on their experiences with a training session. The information collected will help trainers evaluate their performance and make improvements for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the options that apply to your experience with the training session.
2. Enter the date you attended the training session in the "Evaluation Date" field.
3. Enter your name as the trainee who attended the session in the "Trainee Name" field.
4. Enter the title of the training session in the "Training Title" field.
5. Answer the questions regarding the overall satisfaction with your experience, whether you were satisfied or not.
6. If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "Evaluation Comments" field.
7. Rate the trainer's performance with a "Trainer Rating" of either "Good" or "Fair".
8. If you have any further comments, please enter them in the "Evaluation Comments 2" field.
9. Select the "Overall Satisfaction Level" as either "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
10. If you have any feedback questions or concerns, please select all that apply in the "Feedback Questions" field.
11. Enter the range of time you spent on the training session in the "Training Hours" field.
12. Enter the time you spent on the training session in the "Evaluation Time" field.
13. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
14. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
15. Select the "Evaluation Status" as either "Active" or "Inactive".
16. Enter the range of time you spent on the evaluation in the "Evaluation Time Range" field.
17. Enter the date you completed the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date 2" field.
18. Enter the time you spent on the evaluation in the "Evaluation Time 2" field.
19. Enter any further comments in the "Evaluation Comment 2" field.
20. Enter the phone number of the trainee who attended the session in the "Trainee Phone Number" field.
21. Select the "Evaluation Status 2" as either "Active" or "Inactive".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Form** (`Training Evaluation Form`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field allows you to select all the options that apply to your experience with the training session.
* **Evaluation Date** (`Evaluation Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you attended the training session.
* **Trainee Name** (`Trainee Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the trainee who attended the session.
* **Training Title** (`Training Title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the training session.
* **Training Session** (`Training Session`, select_one, required/optional): Answer yes or no to indicate whether you participated in the training session.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`Evaluation Comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments, please enter them in this field.
* **Trainer Rating** (`Trainer Rating`, select_one, required/optional): Rate the trainer's performance with either "Good" or "Fair".
* **Evaluation Comments 2** (`Evaluation Comments 2`, text, required/optional): Enter any further comments in this field.
* **Overall Satisfaction Level** (`Overall Satisfaction Level`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you were satisfied or dissatisfied with your experience.
* **Feedback Questions** (`Feedback Questions`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have any feedback questions or concerns, please select all that apply.
* **Training Hours** (`Training Hours`, number, required/optional): Enter the range of time you spent on the training session.
* **Evaluation Time** (`Evaluation Time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you spent on the evaluation.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Evaluation Status** (`Evaluation Status`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the evaluation is active or inactive.
* **Evaluation Time Range** (`Evaluation Time Range`, time, required/optional): Enter the range of time you spent on the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date 2** (`Evaluation Date 2`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time 2** (`Evaluation Time 2`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you spent on the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comment 2** (`Evaluation Comment 2`, text, required/optional): Enter any further comments in this field.
* **Trainee Phone Number** (`Trainee Phone Number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the trainee who attended the session.
* **Evaluation Status 2** (`Evaluation Status 2`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the evaluation is active or inactive.
