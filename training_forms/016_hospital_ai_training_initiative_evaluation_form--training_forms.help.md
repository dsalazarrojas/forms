# Hospital AI Training Initiative Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness and quality of a hospital's AI training initiative. It covers several key areas, including overall impressions, quality of training sessions, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question as thoroughly as possible, providing detailed descriptions where necessary.
2. Choose the most accurate options for questions with specific response choices.
3. Be honest and objective in your evaluations, reflecting on your actual experience with the AI training initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Impressions**:
	+ Label: Overall Impressions
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Describe your overall impression of the AI training initiative. What did you think of the program? Was it useful, confusing, or somewhere in between?
* **Quality of Training Sessions**:
	+ Label: Quality of Training Sessions
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Evaluate the quality of the training sessions you attended. Were they informative, engaging, or did they lack relevant content?
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Label: Additional Comments
	+ Type: note
	+ Description: Share any additional thoughts or observations you have about the AI training initiative. This is a space to provide any extra information that didn't fit in the previous questions.
* **Topics for Future Training Sessions**:
	+ Label: Topics for Future Training Sessions
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Suggest topics or subjects you'd like to see covered in future training sessions. What areas of interest would you like to explore further?
* **Usefulness of Materials and Resources**:
	+ Label: Usefulness of Materials and Resources
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Evaluate the materials and resources provided to support the AI training initiative. Were they helpful, confusing, or lacking?
* **Positive Impact on Patient Care**:
	+ Label: Positive Impact on Patient Care
	+ Type: checkbox
	+ Description: Check one or both of the options if you believe the AI training initiative has had a positive impact on patient care.
* **Page 7**:
	+ Label: Page 7
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: This is a miscellaneous page for any additional thoughts or observations. Be as concise as possible.
* **Suggestions for Improving the Program**:
	+ Label: Suggestions for Improving the Program
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Share any suggestions you have for improving the AI training initiative. What changes would you recommend?
* **Frequency of Training and Updates**:
	+ Label: Frequency of Training and Updates
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Description: Choose how frequently you'd like to see the AI training initiative updated or refreshed. Options are monthly, quarterly, or bi-monthly.
* **Issues Encountered while Using AI System**:
	+ Label: Issues Encountered while Using AI System
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Describe any problems or issues you experienced while using the AI system.
* **Features and Functionalities**:
	+ Label: Features and Functionalities
	+ Type: select_one
	+ Description: Choose one or more features or functionalities you found most useful about the AI system.
* **Satisfaction with the Program**:
	+ Label: Satisfaction with the Program
	+ Type: text
	+ Description: Evaluate your overall satisfaction with the AI training initiative. What did you think of it, overall?
