# flight_crew_training_check_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form assesses the flight crew's performance in critical areas such as emergency procedures, communication, and teamwork, providing an objective evaluation of their training and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step guide below:

1.  Navigate to the "Introduction" page and read through to understand the form's purpose and instructions.
2.  Proceed to the "Crew Rating" section and select all applicable ratings from the provided options for crew performance in emergency situations.
3.  In the "Emergency Procedure Rating" section, enter a numerical value to evaluate the crew's proficiency in emergency procedures.
4.  For "Communication Rating", select the option that best describes the crew's communication style in emergency situations.
5.  Lastly, in the "Teamwork Rating" section, enter a numerical value to evaluate the crew's teamwork and collaboration skills.
