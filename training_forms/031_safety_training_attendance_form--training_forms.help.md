# Safety Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for recording and tracking participant attendance in safety training sessions. It captures relevant details about the session, trainer, participants, and attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your situation.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
4. Use the "Submit" button to complete the form and save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Details
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether the session has occurred or is scheduled for the future. 
  - Select 'Yes' for completed sessions and 'No' for upcoming sessions.
### Trainer Details
* **Trainer Details** (`trainer_details`, select_one, optional): Identify the trainer responsible for the session.
  - Select 'Yes' for the primary trainer and 'No' for any other trainers involved.
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, select_multiple, optional): List all participants in the session.
  - Select 'Yes' for each participant attending the session.
### Training Session Date
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, optional): Record the date of the actual or scheduled training session.
  - Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Training Session Time
* **Training Session Time** (`training_session_time`, time, optional): Record the start and end time of the training session.
  - Enter the time in the format 12:00 AM - 12:00 PM (24-hour format).
### Training Session Duration
* **Training Session Duration** (`training_session_duration`, number, optional): Record the duration of the session in minutes.
  - Enter a whole number value.
### Location
* **Location** (`location_details`, text, optional): Record the location of the training session.
  - Enter a brief description of the location.
### Attendance Status
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, optional): Record the attendance status of the session.
  - Select 'Active' for sessions with active attendance and 'Inactive' otherwise.
### Attendance Confirmation
* **Attendance Confirmation** (`attendance_confirmation`, select_one, optional): Confirm the attendance of the participants.
  - Select 'Yes' for confirmed attendance and 'No' for pending or unknown status.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the session.
  - Enter any relevant notes or observations.
### Submit
* **Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Save and submit the form.
  - Click this button to save and complete the form.
