# Training Records Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to access and view training records for employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if you have a valid reason for accessing training records.
2. Ensure that the training record is not already accessed by another user.
3. Select the relevant options for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee** (`employee`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you are accessing training records for this employee, 'No' otherwise.
* **training_type** (`training_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all applicable training types you are accessing records for. For example, if you are accessing records for both 'Yes' and 'No', select both options.
* **training_date** (`training_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you are accessing training records for. This is typically the date of the training session.
* **training_time** (`training_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you are accessing training records for. This is typically the time of the training session.
* **start_date** (`start_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the start date of the training session.
* **end_date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of the training session.
* **comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have about the training session.
* **notes** (`employee_notes`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have any additional notes about this employee, 'No' otherwise.
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether you submitted this training record, 'Yes' if you did, 'No' otherwise.

Note: This form has been designed to be as simple and straightforward as possible. If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or IT department for assistance.
