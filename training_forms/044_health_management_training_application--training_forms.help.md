# health-management-training-application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals seeking health management training. It collects application information, contact details, medical history, and other relevant data required by the training provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Application Information** on Page 1.
2. Provide your **Contact Information**, including any relevant details.
3. Fill in your **Emergency Contact Information** for the training provider.
4. Report your **Medical History** for the training provider.
5. Enter your **Training Program Information**, as specified by the provider.
6. Complete the **Payment Information** page.
7. Read and confirm the **Declaration of Understanding**.
8. Accept the **Training Provider Agreement**.
9. Confirm the **Emergency Contact Agreement**.
