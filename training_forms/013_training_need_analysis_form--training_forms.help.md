# Training Need Analysis Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Need Analysis Form is a tool used to identify the specific needs of employees or teams in a given department. It helps analyze and document training requirements to ensure that employees have the necessary skills and knowledge to perform their job tasks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the training need.
2. Provide a brief description of the training need.
3. Choose a category for the training need.
4. Enter the assigned tool or resource required for the training need.
5. Enter a unique form ID (if required).
6. Select a file to save the output.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Title** (`title`, text, required: false)
Enter the title of the training need, such as "New Employee Onboarding" or "Data Analysis for Sales Team".

### 2. **Description** (`description`, text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of the training need, including the specific skills or knowledge required.

### 3. **Category** (`category`, text, required: false)
Choose a category for the training need, such as "New Employee Development" or "Sales and Marketing".

### 4. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false)
Enter the tool or resource required for the training need, such as "Excel" or "Salesforce".

### 5. **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false)
Enter a unique ID for the form, if required by your organization.

### 6. **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false)
Select a file to save the output of the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to clearly and concisely describe each training need to ensure accurate analysis and documentation.
- Use a unique title and description for each training need to avoid confusion.
- Choose the most relevant category and tool for the training need.
- Save the output file for further reference and analysis.
