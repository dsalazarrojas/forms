# Training Needs Assessment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Needs Assessment Evaluation Form is designed to help employees assess their training needs and provide feedback for improvement. This form is used to gather information about the employee's training needs, goals, and objectives, as well as their evaluation method and next evaluation date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your employee name, job title, supervisor's name, and supervisor's phone number in the provided fields.
2. Specify your department and location in the designated fields.
3. Identify your training areas of focus and list them in the field provided for this purpose.
4. Describe your training needs and goals in the respective fields.
5. Choose an evaluation method and frequency for your training needs.
6. Provide the date for your next evaluation.
7. Double-check your form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it is currently assigned to you.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your direct supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter your direct supervisor's phone number.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Specify the department you belong to.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify the location where you work.
* **Training Areas of Focus** (`training_areas_of_focus`, text, required): List the specific areas where you need training or improvement.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, text, required): Describe your training needs and goals in detail.
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals_and_objectives`, text, required): Specify your goals and objectives for the training.
* **Evaluation Method** (`evaluation_method`, text, required): Choose a method for evaluating your training needs, such as a performance review or a checklist.
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, number, required): Specify how often you would like to be evaluated (e.g., quarterly, bi-annually, etc.).
* **Next Evaluation** (`next_evaluation`, date, required): Enter the date for your next evaluation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
* This form is for the sole purpose of assessing your training needs and providing feedback for improvement.
