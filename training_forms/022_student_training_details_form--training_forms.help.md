# Student Training Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Training Details Form is used to collect and track student training information, including personal details, training preferences, and session scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field below.
2. Enter your last name in the field below.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Select whether you will be participating in a training program.
7. Select which training sessions you will be attending.
8. Enter the date of your training session.
9. Enter the time of your training session.
10. Enter the location of your training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### Training Program
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you will be participating in a training program, or "No" if not.

### Training Sessions
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the training sessions you will be attending.

### Training Date
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your training session.

### Training Time
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your training session.

### Training Location
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your training session.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and should not be considered a substitute for any official training documentation or support provided by the organization.
