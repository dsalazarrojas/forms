# employee_motivation_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "employee_motivation_training_form" is a tool used to gather information from employees on their motivation and training needs. This form helps managers understand employee motivation levels, identify areas for training, and provide targeted support. It's an essential part of the employee development and growth process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the "employee" question: This question asks whether you are an employee or not. Please choose "Yes" if you are an employee and "No" if you are not.
2. Enter your training needs: In the "training_needs" field, you can enter any specific training needs or areas you require support with.
3. Choose your motivation level: Select your current motivation level by choosing "High", "Low", or your answer.
4. Identify your training initiatives: Check all the training initiatives you are currently involved in or interested in.
5. Rate your employee engagement: This field is not used in the provided YAML, so we'll skip it for now.
6. Provide manager comments: In the "manager_comments" field, enter any comments or suggestions you have for your manager.
7. Enter your manager's name and your own name: These fields are not typically necessary for completion but are included for your manager's and your own reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee**: Please answer "Yes" or "No" to this question to confirm whether you are an employee or not.
* **Training Needs**: If you have specific training needs or requirements, enter them in this field. This will help your manager provide targeted support.
* **Motivation Level**: Choose your current motivation level by selecting "High" for motivated, "Low" for unmotivated, or answer accordingly.
* **Training Initiatives**: Check all the training initiatives you are currently involved in or interested in participating in.
* **Manager Comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions you have for your manager in this field.
* **Manager Name**: This field is typically not required but can be used for your manager's reference.
* **Employee Name**: This field is also not required but can be used for your own reference.
* **Department**: This field is currently not used in the provided YAML but is expected for general employee information.
