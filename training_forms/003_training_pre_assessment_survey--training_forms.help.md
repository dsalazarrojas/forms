# Training Pre Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your current level of training in various areas, including communication, problem-solving, time management, technical skills, soft skills, and job satisfaction. The survey is intended to help us understand your strengths and weaknesses, so we can provide you with personalized training recommendations to help you grow and develop in your role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this survey, simply follow the instructions below:

1. For each question, select all the options that apply to you.
2. Use the rating scale to rate your current level of training in each area (1-5, where 1 is "Not at all" and 5 is "Extremely").
3. For the soft skills questions, select all the options that you possess or are familiar with.
4. In the "Any additional comments" field, provide any additional information or feedback you think will be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Pre Assessment Survey** (Page 1):
	+ This is the title of the survey.
* **How would you rate your current level of training in the following areas?** (Page 2):
	+ This question asks you to rate your current level of training in various areas, using the scale 1-5.
* **Effective Communication** (Page 3):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your effective communication skills, such as:
		- Effective communication with others
		- Understanding of the subject matter
		- Clear and concise writing and presentation
* **Problem Solving and Critical Thinking** (Page 4):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your problem-solving and critical thinking skills, such as:
		- Analytical thinking
		- Critical thinking
		- Problem solving
* **Time Management and Organization** (Page 5):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your time management and organization skills, such as:
		- Time management
		- Goal setting
		- Organization and prioritization
* **Technical Skills and Tools** (Page 6):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your technical skills and tools, such as:
		- Microsoft Office
		- Google Suite
		- Other technical tools and software
* **Soft Skills** (Page 7):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your soft skills, such as:
		- Adaptability
		- Teamwork
		- Leadership
* **Job Satisfaction** (Page 8):
	+ Select all the options that apply to your job satisfaction, such as:
		- Engaging work

* **Career Growth and Development Opportunities** (Page 9):

* **Any Additional Comments** (Page 10):
	+ Provide any additional comments or feedback you think will be helpful for us to know.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when rating your current level of training in each area.
* Select all the options that apply to you, even if it's just one or two.
* Use the "Any additional comments" field to provide any additional information or feedback you think will be helpful for us to know.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
