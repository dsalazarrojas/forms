# Project Management Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Management Training Form is used to collect information from participants in project management training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name.
2. Enter your Participant Email.
3. Select your Department.
4. Choose the training sessions you attended.
5. Select the date and time of the training session you attended.
6. Enter any additional notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Text)** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Participant Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official work email address.
* **Department (Select)** (`department`, select_one, optional): Choose your department from the list below:
	+ Department A
	+ Department B
	+ Department C
* **Training Sessions (Select Multiple)** (`training_sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Select the training sessions you attended, separated by commas:
	+ Session 1
	+ Session 2
	+ Session 3
* **Training Date (Date)** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the training session you attended in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Training Time (Time)** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the training session you attended in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Participant Phone (Text)** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Notes (Note)** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or additional comments about your experience.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly for communication purposes.
* Choose only relevant training sessions that you attended.
* Enter your name and department accurately to ensure correct identification.
* You can add any additional notes to help us improve the training content.
