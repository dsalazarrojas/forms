# OSHA Certification Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The OSHA Certification Training Form is used to capture information about employee OSHA certification status, training details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form only if you have received OSHA certification training.
2. Select your training program from the list provided.
3. Enter the date you received the training.
4. Enter the time you received the training.
5. Enter your attendee name.
6. Select your certificate type.
7. Enter your certificate number (if applicable).
8. Indicate your certification status.
9. Add any additional notes about your training experience.
10. Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number).
11. Select the submitted status of your form.
12. Enter any comments about your training experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your training experience.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, optional): Select the type of OSHA training program you received, e.g., 30 Hour OSHA, General Industry, Other.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you received the training.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you received the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you received the training.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the attendee of the training.
* **Certificate Type** (`certificate_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of certificate you received (e.g., OSHA 30 Hour, OSHA 20 Hour, Other).
* **Certificate Number** (`certificate_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of the certificate you received (if applicable).
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are certified or not certified.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your training experience.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your training experience.
* **Submitted Status** (`submitted_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the form has been completed or is incomplete.
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of certification you received.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the course you took.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the employee who took the course.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of training hours you received.
* **Training Location ID** (`training_location_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the location where you received the training.
* **Training Date ID** (`training_date_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the date you received the training.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to you for the training.
