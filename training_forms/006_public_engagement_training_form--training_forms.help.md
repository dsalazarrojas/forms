# Public Engagement Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Engagement Training Form is used to collect data from participants in public engagement training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information for each participant. The form is divided into several pages, each with a specific set of fields. Start by filling in the participant's name, followed by their organization, email, phone number, and training date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, required: false): Please enter the name of the participant.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, required: false): Please enter the participant's organization or affiliation.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, Email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, Text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, Date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, Time, required: false): Enter the time of the training session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any) to ensure accurate participant information.
* Double-check the participant's email and phone number for accuracy.
* If the participant has a conflict of interest or special requests, please note them in the comments section (not shown in this example).
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the training coordinator for clarification.
* This form should only be used for public engagement training sessions.
