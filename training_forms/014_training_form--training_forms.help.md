# Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users about their training experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Training" field and enter the title of the training session.
2. Enter the date of the training session using the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the training session using the "Time" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the training session using the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training (training_form)**: A text field where you can enter the title of the training session.
* **Date (training_form_date)**: A date field where you can enter the date of the training session.
* **Time (training_form_time)**: A time field where you can enter the time of the training session.
* **Description (training_form_description)**: A text field where you can enter a brief description of the training session.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to complete this form.
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
