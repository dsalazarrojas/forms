## Coordinator Training Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Coordinator Training Evaluation form is designed to collect feedback and evaluations from coordinators about their training experience. This form helps us understand what worked well, what didn't, and areas for improvement in the training program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Select the appropriate options from the drop-down menus.
4. Enter any text or numerical information in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`first_page`, `note`, required/optional): This is the first page of the form where you will find a note section for any comments or feedback about the training program.
* **First Page Section** (`first_page_section`, `select_one`, required/optional): This is a dropdown menu where you can select one of the options provided.
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any text that you think is important about the training program.
* **Second Page Section** (`second_page_section`, `number`, required/optional): Enter a numerical value for this field.
* **Third Page** (`third_page`, `date`, required/optional): Enter a date in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).
