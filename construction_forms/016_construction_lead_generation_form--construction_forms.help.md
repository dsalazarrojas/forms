# Construction Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Lead Generation Form is a tool to gather essential details from customers interested in construction services. By filling out this form, you'll help us understand your project needs, preferences, and expectations, allowing us to better serve you and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the "Inquiry Date" field and choose the date you inquired about our construction services.
3. Choose how you heard about us from the "How Did You Hear About Us" options.
4. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and preferred contact method (Phone Call, Text Message, Email, or Any Method).
5. Select the type of project you're interested in from the "Project Type" options.
6. Enter the address where the project will be located.
7. Choose the type of property you're dealing with from the "Property Type" options.
8. If you have an approximate square footage, select the relevant size from the "Project Size" options.
9. Briefly describe your project in the "Project Description" field.
10. Choose how you'll finance your project from the "Financing Status" options. If you're not sure, you can select "Not Sure".
11. Select when you'd like to start your project from the "When Do You Want to Start" options.
12. Choose when you plan to decide on a contractor from the "When Do You Plan to Decide" options. If you're not sure, you can select "Not Sure".
13. If you're getting bids from other contractors, select the relevant option from "Are You Getting Other Bids".
14. If you've worked on similar projects before, choose the relevant option from "Previous Construction Experience".
15. If you have any special requirements or considerations, type them in the "Special Requirements" field.
16. Choose when you're most available for contact from the "Best Time to Contact" options.
17. If someone referred you to us, type their name in the "Who Referred You" field.
18. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inquiry Date** (`inquiry_date`, date, required): The date of your inquiry about our construction services.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`lead_source`, select_one, required): This field helps us understand how you found out about our company.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the best email address to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Choose how you'd like to be contacted (Phone Call, Text Message, Email, or Any Method).
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of project you're interested in (New Home Construction, Home Addition, Kitchen Remodel, etc.).
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, text, required): Enter the address where the project will be located.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of property (Single Family Home, Townhouse, Condominium, etc.).
* **Project Size** (`project_size`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the approximate square footage of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your project in this field.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select the estimated budget for your project.
* **Financing Status** (`financing_status`, select_one, optional): If applicable, choose how you plan to finance your project.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Select when you'd like to start your project.
* **Decision Timeframe** (`decision_timeframe`, select_one, optional): Choose when you plan to decide on a contractor.
* **Are You Getting Other Bids** (`other_contractors`, select_one, optional): If applicable, indicate if you're getting bids from other contractors.
* **Previous Construction Experience** (`previous_experience`, select_one, optional): If applicable, indicate your experience with similar projects.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If applicable, add any special requirements or considerations for your project.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_contact`, select_one, optional): Choose when you're most available for contact.
* **Who Referred You** (`referral_source`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the person who referred you.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your project.
* **Consent to Contact** (`consent_contact`, select_one, required): Select "True" to agree to be contacted about your project.
