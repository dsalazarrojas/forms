# Construction Lien Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Construction Lien Form is used to document a claim for a construction lien on a property. This form is used by claimants to secure payment for work or materials provided on a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure correctness and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Lien Filing Date** (id: 1, `date`): Date of lien filing.
*   **Claimant Name** (id: 2, `text`, required): Name of party filing the lien.
*   **Claimant Company** (id: 3, `text`, optional): Company name if applicable.
*   **Claimant Address** (id: 4, `text`, required): Full mailing address.
*   **Claimant Phone** (id: 5, `text`, required): Contact phone number.
*   **Claimant Email** (id: 6, `email`, required): Email address.
*   **Claimant Role** (id: 7, `select_one`, required): Your role on the project. Options: General Contractor, Subcontractor, Material Supplier, Equipment Rental Company, Laborer, Professional (Architect/Engineer), Other.
*   **Property Owner Name** (id: 8, `text`, required): Legal owner of the property.
*   **Property Address** (id: 9, `text`, required): Address of property being liened.
*   **Legal Description of Property** (id: 10, `text`, required): From deed or title document.
*   **Parcel Number** (id: 11, `text`, required): County assessor parcel number.
*   **Party You Contracted With** (id: 12, `text`, required): Name of entity you had contract with.
*   **Contract Date** (id: 13, `date`, optional): Date of original agreement.
*   **Work Start Date** (id: 14, `date`, required): When did work begin.
*   **Work End Date** (id: 15, `date`, optional): When was work completed.
*   **Description of Work or Materials** (id: 16, `text`, required): Detailed description of labor or materials provided.
*   **Original Contract Amount** (id: 17, `number`, optional): Total agreed contract price.
*   **Amount Already Paid** (id: 18, `number`, required): Total payments received.
*   **Lien Amount Claimed** (id: 19, `number`, required): Total amount being liened.
*   **Amount Breakdown** (id: 20, `text`, required): Itemize labor, materials, other costs.
*   **Interest Claimed** (id: 21, `select_one`, optional): Is interest included in lien. Options: True, False.
*   **Previous Notices Sent** (id: 22, `text`, optional): List any preliminary notices filed.
*   **Supporting Documents** (id: 23, `text`, optional): List attached documentation.
*   **Verification Statement** (id: 24, `select_one`, required): Statement that information is true. Options: I Verify This Information Is True and Correct, Other.
*   **Claimant Signature** (id: 25, `text`, required): Type full name as signature.
*   **Signature Date** (id: 26, `date`, required): Date signed.

## Tips
*   Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure correctness and completeness.
*   Attach any supporting documentation as required.
