# Construction Change Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a standardized template for documenting changes to construction projects. Its primary purpose is to track and record modifications to a project's scope, timeline, or budget, facilitating transparency, accurate reporting, and informed decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the sequential Change Order Number.
2. Enter the date of the change order.
3. Specify the name of the construction project.
4. Enter the project number or contract reference number.
5. Identify the project owner or client.
6. Enter the contractor's company name.
7. Record the original contract amount.
8. Summarize all previous change orders.
9. Describe the change and its rationale.
10. Specify the reason for the change.
11. Explain the scope impact of the change.
12. Indicate any cost increase or decrease.
13. State the net cost change.
14. Describe the schedule impact of the change.
15. (Optional) Enter the number of days impacted by the change or time saved.
16. (Optional) Enter a revised completion date.
17. (Optional) List materials affected by the change.
18. (Optional) Describe labor hours or staffing changes.
19. (Optional) List subcontractors impacted.
20. (Optional) Attach supporting documentation.
21. Confirm owner, contractor, and architect approval (if applicable).
22. Sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Change Order Number** (`change_order_number`, text, required): Enter the sequential change order number.
* **Change Order Date** (`change_order_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this change order.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the construction project.
* **Project Number** (`project_number`, text, required): Enter the contract or project reference number.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Identify the project owner or client.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, required): Enter the general contractor company name.
* **Original Contract Amount** (`original_contract_amount`, number, required): Record the initial contract value.
* **Total Previous Change Orders** (`previous_change_orders`, number, required): Summarize all prior change orders.
* **Description of Change** (`change_description`, text, required): Describe the detailed description of what is changing.
* **Reason for Change** (`change_reason`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for the change from the options provided.
* **Scope Impact** (`scope_impact`, text, required): Explain how this change affects the project scope.
* **Cost Increase Amount** (`cost_increase`, number, optional): Enter the additional cost if applicable.
* **Cost Decrease Amount** (`cost_decrease`, number, optional): Enter cost reduction if applicable.
* **Net Cost Change** (`net_cost_change`, number, required): Calculate the total change in contract amount.
* **Schedule Impact** (`schedule_impact`, select_one, required): Choose the effect on the project timeline from the options provided.
* **Number of Days Impact** (`days_impact`, number, optional): Enter the positive or negative number of days impacted by the change.
* **Revised Completion Date** (`new_completion_date`, date, optional): Enter the new estimated completion date.
* **Materials Affected** (`materials_affected`, text, optional): List the materials impacted by the change.
* **Labor Impact** (`labor_affected`, text, optional): Describe the labor hours or staffing changes.
* **Subcontractors Affected** (`subcontractors_affected`, text, optional): List the subcontractors impacted.
* **Supporting Documents** (`attachments`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documentation.
* **Owner Approval** (`owner_approval`, select_one, required): Confirm if the owner approves this change.
* **Contractor Approval** (`contractor_approval`, select_one, required): Confirm if the contractor agrees to the change.
* **Architect Approval** (`architect_approval`, select_one, required): Confirm if architect or engineer approval is required/pending (if not required, skip).
* **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as the authorized signature.

Note: This guide assumes that the owner and/or contractor approvals are required, but may not be always necessary for every change. Please review the form's requirements and your project specifics before completing it.
