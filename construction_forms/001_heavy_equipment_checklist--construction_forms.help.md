# Heavy Equipment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heavy Equipment Checklist form is designed to gather essential information about heavy equipment, including its condition, maintenance status, and other relevant details. This form is used to track and manage heavy equipment across various departments, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Machine Name field with the name of the heavy equipment being checked.
2. Enter the Serial Number in the corresponding field.
3. Select the correct options for the Maintenance Status and Condition fields.
4. Choose the correct options for the Machine Type and assigned_to fields.
5. Enter the last Service Date and next Service Date if applicable.
6. Enter the contact information, including Contact Name, Contact Phone, and Contact Email.
7. Add any relevant Remarks or notes.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Machine Name** (`machine_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the heavy equipment being checked.
- **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, required): Enter the serial number of the heavy equipment.
- **Year Manufactured** (`year_manufactured`, number, optional): Enter the year the equipment was manufactured (if known).
- **Hours Used** (`hours_used`, number, optional): Enter the total hours the equipment has been used.
- **Last Service Date** (`last_service_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last service or maintenance performed.
- **Next Service Date** (`next_service_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled service or maintenance.
- **Maintenance Status** (`maintenance_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current maintenance status of the equipment (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
- **Condition** (`condition`, select_multiple, required): Select the current condition of the equipment (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the equipment.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the contact name associated with the equipment.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number associated with the equipment.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email associated with the equipment.
- **Machine Type** (`machine_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of heavy equipment (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
- **Machine Model** (`machine_model`, text, optional): Enter the model number of the heavy equipment (if applicable).
- **Machine Year** (`machine_year`, number, optional): Enter the year the equipment was manufactured (if known).
- **Machine Hours** (`machine_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total hours the equipment has been used.
- **Machine Location** (`machine_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the equipment is currently stored or in use.
- **Machine Image** (`machine_image`, text, optional): Enter the image or photo of the heavy equipment (if available).
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required): Select the person or department responsible for the equipment (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
- **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, select_one, required): Select the person or department that assigned the equipment (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
- **Last Maintenance** (`last_maintenance`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed (if applicable).
- **Last Inspection** (`last_inspection`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last inspection performed (if applicable).
- **Next Maintenance** (`next_maintenance`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance (if applicable).
- **Next Inspection** (`next_inspection`, date, optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection (if applicable).
- **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the equipment.

Note: The form is designed to be completed in a step-by-step manner, and the fields are organized to minimize confusion and errors. Each field's label and explanation have been carefully crafted to provide clear guidance on what is expected.
