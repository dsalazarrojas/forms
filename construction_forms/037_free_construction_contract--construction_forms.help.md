# Free Construction Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a free construction contract between a client and a construction company for a specific project. It gathers information about the project and the parties involved. Fill in the required fields to create a comprehensive contract.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the project and the parties involved.
2. Fill in the contract number, project name, and client name in the corresponding fields.
3. If necessary, provide the client and contractor address, contract start and end dates, payment terms and frequency, and any additional contact information.
4. Select the payment amount, contract type, and status.
5. Choose the contract term frequency and status notes, and add any additional notes if needed.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, number, required): Enter a unique number for this contract.
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the project.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Enter the client's address.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contractor.
* **Contractor Address** (`contractor_address`, text, optional): Enter the contractor's address.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, date, optional): Enter the contract date (not explicitly explained, this field might imply a date of contract signing or agreement initiation).
* **Contract Start Date** (`contract_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the contract started.
* **Contract End Date** (`contract_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the contract ended or expected to end.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Enter the agreed-upon payment terms.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select one, required): Choose how payments are made (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the contract.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email for the client or contractor.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number for the client or contractor.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the contact address for the client or contractor.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Phone Extension** (`client_phone_extension`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone extension.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select one, required): Choose the type of contract (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Contract Status** (`contract_status`, select multiple, required): Choose the status of the contract (Status 1 or Status 2).
* **Contract Term** (`contract_term`, select multiple, optional): Choose the contract term (Term 1 or Term 2).
* **Contract Term Frequency** (`contract_term_frequency`, select multiple, required): Choose how often the contract term is applied (Frequency 1 or Frequency 2).
* **Contract Status Notes** (`contract_status_notes`, note, required): Enter any notes about the contract status.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
