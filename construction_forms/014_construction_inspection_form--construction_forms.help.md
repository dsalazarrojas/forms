# Construction Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the process of conducting a thorough construction inspection. It captures essential information about the inspection date, time, and details of the site, including inspector credentials, project name, and permit number. It also includes sections to evaluate the quality of work, materials, and safety compliance. The form is essential for identifying potential issues and ensuring compliance with building codes and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin the inspection process.
2.  Enter the **Inspection Date** in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
3.  Enter the **Inspection Time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
4.  Enter your **Inspector Name** as it appears on your identification or business card.
5.  Enter your **Inspector Credentials**, such as your license or certification number.
6.  Enter the **Project Name** of the construction project.
7.  Enter the full **Project Address**, including house number and street.
8.  Enter the **Permit Number**, if applicable.
9.  Select the **Inspection Type** from the list, such as Foundation, Framing, etc.
10. Select the **Construction Stage**, including Site Preparation, Foundation, etc.
11. Evaluate the **Work Quality Rating**, selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
12. Determine if **Plans and Specifications** were followed, choosing Yes, With Minor Deviations, No, or Unable to Determine.
13. Assess **Code Compliance**, choosing Fully Compliant, Minor Issues, Non-Compliant, or Not Assessed.
14. Evaluate **Structural Elements Check**, selecting Approved, Approved with Notes, Needs Correction, or Not Applicable.
15. Check for **Safety Compliance**, selecting Fully Compliant, Minor Issues Found, Major Issues Found, or Not Assessed.
16. Assess **Materials Quality**, choosing As Specified, Acceptable Substitutions, Unapproved Substitutions, or Poor Quality.
17. Evaluate the **Workmanship Quality**, selecting Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Needs Improvement, or Poor.
18. Count the number of **Deficiencies** found and describe each one in the corresponding field.
19. If necessary, enter the details of each **Deficiency** found.
20. If necessary, describe the **Corrective Actions** required to address issues.
21. Indicate if a **Reinspection Required**, selecting True or False.
22. If a reinspection is required, enter the **Reinspection Date** in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
23. Select the final **Inspection Result**, such as Approved, Approved with Conditions, Conditional Approval, Failed, or Incomplete.
24. Indicate if any **Photos Were Taken**, selecting True or False.
25. Enter any additional **Inspector Comments** or observations.
26. Finally, enter your **Inspector Signature**, typing your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: The date on which the inspection took place. Format: `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Inspection Time**: The time the inspection started. Format: `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Inspector Name**: The name of the person conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Credentials**: Your identification or business card number.
* **Project Name**: The name of the construction project being inspected.
* **Project Address**: The full address of the project site.
* **Permit Number**: A reference to the building permit for the project.
* **Inspection Type**: The type of inspection being conducted, such as Foundation or Framing.
* **Construction Stage**: The current stage of the construction project, such as Site Preparation or Final Completion.
* **Work Quality Rating**: A subjective assessment of the overall quality of work. Select from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Plans and Specifications Followed**: Determine if the work was conducted according to approved plans.
* **Code Compliance**: Assess whether the work meets building codes and regulations.
* **Structural Elements Check**: Evaluate the quality of the project's structural elements.
* **Safety Compliance**: Assess whether safety standards were followed during the inspection.
* **Materials Quality**: Evaluate the quality of materials used in the project.
* **Workmanship Quality**: Assess the quality of the workmanship, including installation and construction.
* **Number of Deficiencies**: Count and describe each deficiency found during the inspection.
* **Deficiency Details**: Provide a detailed description of each deficiency.
* **Corrective Actions Required**: List any necessary actions to correct identified issues.
* **Reinspection Required**: Indicate if a reinspection is needed.
* **Reinspection Date**: Enter the date for a scheduled reinspection, if applicable.
* **Inspection Result**: Finalize the inspection by selecting Approved, Approved with Conditions, Conditional Approval, Failed, or Incomplete.
* **Photos Taken**: Indicate if photographs were taken during the inspection.
* **Inspector Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations.
* **Inspector Signature**: Type your full name as the inspector.

Note: This help guide is designed to guide users through the process of filling out the construction inspection form. Please ensure that all fields are filled according to the provided information and guidelines.
