# Excavation Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that excavation sites are properly assessed and managed for safety purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, select the relevant options from the provided fields and enter any required information. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Excavation Site** (Required): Select the type of excavation site being assessed.
	+ This field is required as it helps identify the type of site being evaluated.
* **Site Insurance** (Required): Choose whether the site has insurance.
	+ This field helps determine if insurance coverage is in place for the site.
* **Excavation Site Access** (Required): Enter the date of the site's access.
	+ This field is required to track the site's accessibility.
* **Site Access Requirements** (Optional): Enter any specific requirements for accessing the site.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional context for site access.
* **Excavation Site Equipment** (Required): Enter the number of equipment on site.
	+ This field helps track the number of equipment available on the site.
* **Excavation Site Hazard Control** (Required): Enter any hazard control measures in place.
	+ This field is required to document any hazard control measures.
* **Excavation Site Emergency Procedure** (Required): Enter the emergency procedure in place.
	+ This field helps document the emergency procedures.
* **Excavation Site Reviewed** (Required): Enter the date the site was last reviewed.
	+ This field is required to track the date of the last review.
* **Excavation Site Last Updated** (Optional): Enter the date the site was last updated.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional context for site updates.
* **Excavation Supervisor Name** (Required): Enter the name of the supervisor on site.
	+ This field is required to track the supervisor's name.
* **Excavation Supervisor Contact** (Optional): Enter the supervisor's contact information.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional contact information.
* **Excavation Supervisor Phone** (Optional): Enter the supervisor's phone number.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional contact information.
* **Excavation Supervisor Email** (Optional): Enter the supervisor's email address.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional contact information.
* **Excavation Site Responsible Person** (Required): Enter the responsible person's name.
	+ This field is required to track the responsible person.
* **Excavation Site Responsible Person Phone** (Optional): Enter the responsible person's phone number.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional contact information.
* **Excavation Supervisor Company** (Optional): Enter the supervisor's company name.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional context.
* **Excavation Site Location** (Required): Enter the site's location.
	+ This field is required to track the site's location.
* **Excavation Site Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about the site.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional context.
* **Assigned Tool** (Required): Select the tool assigned to the site.
	+ This field is required to track the tool assigned.
* **Form Completed By** (Required): Enter the name of the person completing the form.
	+ This field is required to track the form's completion.
* **Form Completed Date** (Required): Enter the date the form was completed.
	+ This field is required to track the form's completion.
* **Form Updated By** (Optional): Enter the name of the person updating the form.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional context for form updates.
* **Form Updated Date** (Optional): Enter the date the form was updated.
	+ This field is optional but can provide additional context for form updates.

	+ This field is required to determine if the user has completed the form.
 
This guide assumes that the form is being filled out by a user who has basic knowledge of excavation sites and safety protocols. If a field is not understood, please seek guidance from the relevant authorities or supervisors.
