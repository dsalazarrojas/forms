# Incident Injury Reporting Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Incident Injury Reporting Form Template is a tool used to document and manage workplace injuries, ensuring that incidents are reported, investigated, and resolved in a structured and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting it to your supervisor and/or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional)
Employee's name involved in the incident.
### 2. **Job Number** (`job_number`, text, required)
Job number of the employee involved in the incident.
### 3. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional)
Employee's job title at the time of the incident.
### 4. **Date of Occurrence** (`date_of_occurrence`, date, required)
Date the incident occurred.
### 5. **Location** (`location`, text, optional)
Location where the incident occurred.
### 6. **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, text, required)
Detailed description of the injury.
### 7. **Injury Severity** (`injury_severity`, select_one, required)
Severity of the injury (Not Applicable, Minor, Serious, Critical).
### 8. **Body Area Affected** (`body_area_affected`, select_multiple, required)
Areas of the body affected by the injury (Head, Neck, Face, etc.).
### 9. **Medical Care** (`medical_care`, select_multiple, optional)
Required medical care (Medical Care 24/7, Medical Care 9-5, etc.).
### 10. **Incident Narrative** (`incident_narrative`, text, optional)
Additional details or context about the incident.
### 11. **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, optional)
Approval status from the supervisor (Approved, Disapproved, Pending).
### 12. **Manager Approval** (`manager_approval`, select_one, required)
Approval status from the manager (Approved, Disapproved, Pending).
### 13. **Safety Officer Approval** (`safety_officer_approval`, select_one, required)
Approval status from the safety officer (Approved, Disapproved, Pending).
### 14. **Incident Reported** (`incident_reported`, date, required)
Date the incident was reported.
### 15. **Incident Closed** (`incident_closed`, date, optional)
Date the incident was closed.
### 16. **Incident Closed By** (`incident_closed_by`, text, required)
Person who closed the incident.
### 17. **Incident Cause** (`incident_cause`, text, required)
Cause of the incident.
### 18. **Incident Cause Category** (`incident_cause_category`, select_one, required)
Category of the incident cause (Human Error, Machinery, Environmental, etc.).
### 19. **Incident Cause Subcategory** (`incident_cause_subcategory`, select_multiple, optional)
Subcategory of the incident cause (Human Error, Other, etc.).
### 20. **Other (Specify)** (`incident_cause_other`, text, optional)
Specify the cause if it's not listed.
### 21. **Incident Cause Other (Specify)** (`incident_cause_other_description`, text, optional)
Provide a detailed description of the other cause.
### 22. **Incident Cause Hazards** (`incident_cause_hazards`, text, optional)
Hazards involved in the incident.
### 23. **Incident Cause Hazards List** (`incident_cause_hazards_list`, text, optional)
Detailed list of hazards involved.
### 24. **Employer Response** (`employer_response`, text, optional)
Response from the employer regarding the incident.
### 25. **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, optional)
Additional information or follow-up comments on the incident.

Please note that some fields are marked as required, and those must be completed before submitting the form.
