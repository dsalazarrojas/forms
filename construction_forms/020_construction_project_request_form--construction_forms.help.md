# Construction Project Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a construction project request. Please fill out this form to provide your project's details and help us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Start by filling out the required fields**, which include:
	* Request Date
	* Request Type
	* Requester Name
	* Requester Email
	* Requester Phone Number
	* Property Owner Name
	* Property Address
	* Property Type
	* Project Description
	* Scope of Work
	* Budget Range
	* Financing Status
	* Are Plans Available
	* Permits Required
	* Utility Status
2. **Optional fields**:
	* Company/ Organization Name (if applicable)
	* Desired Start Date
	* Desired Completion Date
	* Financing Secured (if applicable)
	* Site Access (if applicable)
	* Special Requirements (if applicable)
	* Timeline Flexibility (if applicable)
	* Decision Authority (if applicable)
	* Other Bids Being Sought (if applicable)
	* How Soon Will You Decide (if applicable)
	* Additional Comments (if applicable)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this request.
* **Request Type**: Choose the type of construction request you are submitting.
* **Requester Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Requester Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Requester Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Property Owner Name**: Enter the name of the property's legal owner.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address where the work will be performed.
* **Property Type**: Choose the type of property.
* **Project Description**: Provide a detailed description of the project.
* **Scope of Work**: Specify the tasks and deliverables for this project.
* **Budget Range**: Choose the estimated budget for this project.
* **Financing Status**: Indicate if funding is secured for this project.
* **Are Plans Available**: Indicate if you have architectural plans or drawings.
* **Permits Required**: Indicate if you know what permits are needed.
* **Utility Status**: Describe the current utility situation.
* **Site Access**: Describe any access limitations to the site.
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any unique needs or constraints.
* **Timeline Flexibility**: Choose your level of flexibility with the project timeline.
* **Decision Authority**: Indicate if you are the final decision maker.
* **Other Bids Being Sought**: Indicate if you are getting other estimates.
* **How Soon Will You Decide**: Choose the timeline for selecting a contractor.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other relevant information.
