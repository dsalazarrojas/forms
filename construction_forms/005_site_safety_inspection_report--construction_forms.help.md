# Site Safety Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Site Safety Inspection Report is a form used by site inspectors to document their findings on site safety during a construction site inspection. The form helps identify potential hazards and ensures site safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Construction Site Details** (Construction Site Details, note): 
  This field is for noting any specific details about the construction site being inspected.
* **Site Inspector** (Site Inspector, text): 
  Enter the name and title of the site inspector who conducted the inspection.
* **Inspection Date** (Inspection Date, date): 
  Record the date of the site inspection.
* **Construction Company** (Construction Company, text): 
  Enter the name of the company responsible for the construction project.
* **Safety Equipment Provided** (Safety Equipment Provided, select multiple): 
  List the safety equipment available on site, such as hard hats, safety harnesses, and first aid kits.
* **Safety Equipment Used** (Safety Equipment Used, select multiple): 
  Select the safety equipment used during the inspection.
* **Hazards Found** (Hazards Found, select multiple): 
  Identify any hazards found on the site, such as electrical, fire, chemical, or structural hazards.
* **Action Taken** (Action Taken, text): 
  Describe any actions taken to address the hazards found on site.
* **Next Inspection Due** (Next Inspection Due, date): 
  Record the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Completed** (Completed, text): 
  Mark the form as completed.
