# Project Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from customers or stakeholders on their experience with a project. Your input will help the project team understand what they did well and what they can improve on for future projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answers that best reflect your experience with the project. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose one or multiple options that apply to you. For select_one and select_multiple fields with the 'Yes' option, select 'Yes' if you strongly agree, and 'No' if you don't agree.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your overall project experience?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_1`, select_one, **Yes**/**No**): This question gauges your general satisfaction with the project outcome.
* **What was the quality of workmanship on the project?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_2`, select_multiple, **Good**/**Poor**): This question evaluates how well the project team delivered on its promises.
* **How satisfied are you with the project timeline?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_3`, date, **mm/dd/yyyy**): This question asks about your experience with project timing and deadlines.
* **How likely are you to recommend this project to others?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_4`, select_one, **Yes**/**No**): This question measures how likely you are to recommend the project to others.
* **What did you find to be the biggest project challenge?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_5`, note, **text**): This field is for you to describe any significant challenges you faced during the project.
* **What could have been done differently on the project?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_6`, note, **text**): This field is for suggestions on how the project team could improve in the future.
* **How satisfied are you with the communication from your project manager?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_7`, select_one, **Yes**/**No**): This question evaluates the effectiveness of project communication.
* **How satisfied are you with the project team?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_8`, select_one, **Yes**/**No**): This question assesses your satisfaction with the project team's performance.
* **How easy was it to get in touch with the project team?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_9`, select_one, **Yes**/**No**): This question measures your experience with project team availability and responsiveness.
* **How satisfied are you with the overall project result?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_10`, select_one, **Yes**/**No**): This question gauges your satisfaction with the project outcome.
* **What do you expect from the project team in future projects?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_11`, select_multiple, **Yes**/**No**): This field is for you to specify any expectations you have for the project team in future projects.
* **How likely are you to work with this project team again?** (`project_satisfaction_survey_12`, select_one, **Yes**/**No**): This question measures your likelihood to collaborate with the project team in the future.
