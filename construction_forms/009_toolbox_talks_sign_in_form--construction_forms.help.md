# Toolbox Talks Sign In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Toolbox Talks Sign In Form" is a form used for collecting information from attendees of a Toolbox Talk, a short training session for employees. This form helps track participant details, attendance, and verification of attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields with the requested information.
2. Make sure to enter your name and other details accurately.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Signed by" field as per your confirmation of attendance.
4. Enter the date and time of the session you attended in the "Signed by (Date)" and "Signed by (Time)" fields.
5. Enter your job title and location in the "Signed by (Job Title)" and "Signed by (Location)" fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_input_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as per your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`user_input_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as per your official identification.
* **Email** (`user_input_3`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`user_input_4`, text, required): Enter your official phone number.
* **Company** (`user_input_5`, text, required): Enter your company name or affiliation.
* **Job Title** (`user_input_6`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department** (`user_input_7`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Location** (`user_input_8`, text, required): Enter the location where you attended the session.
* **Date** (`user_input_9`, date, required): Enter the date of the session you attended.
* **Time** (`user_input_10`, time, required): Enter the time of the session you attended.
* **Reason for Attending** (`user_input_11`, text, required): Enter the reason why you attended the session.
* **Additional Comments** (`user_input_12`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the session.
* **Signed by** (`user_input_13`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you attended the session and "No" if you did not.
* **Signed by (Name)** (`user_input_14`, text, required): Enter your name as verification of attendance.
* **Signed by (Job Title)** (`user_input_15`, text, required): Enter your job title as verification of attendance.
* **Signed by (Date)** (`user_input_16`, date, required): Enter the date you attended the session as verification of attendance.
* **Signed by (Time)** (`user_input_17`, time, required): Enter the time you attended the session as verification of attendance.
* **Signed by (Location)** (`user_input_18`, text, required): Enter the location where you attended the session as verification of attendance.
