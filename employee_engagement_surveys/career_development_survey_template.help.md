# career_development_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Career Development Survey" is designed to gather feedback from employees on their career development and overall satisfaction within the organization, aiming to improve employee experience and growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Answer each question based on your current role and experience at the organization.
2.  Select the relevant options for each question.
3.  Provide any additional comments or suggestions regarding career development at your organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your current role at the organization?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`):
	+ Please select the response that best describes your level of satisfaction with your current role.
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development and career growth?** (`true`, `false`, `Somewhat`):
	+ Select `true` if you feel supported, `false` if you don't, and `Somewhat` if you're unsure.
* **Which of the following professional development opportunities are most important to you?** (`Skill training`, `Mentorship programs`, `Leadership development`, `Conferences/workshops`, `Higher education support`, `Cross-functional projects`):
	+ Select all the opportunities that are most important to you. This will help us understand your priorities.
* **How would you rate the clarity of career paths within the organization?** (`Very clear`, `Clear`, `Neutral`, `Unclear`, `Very unclear`):
	+ Rate the clarity of career paths based on your understanding of the organization's career structure.
* **What initiatives or resources could most improve your career development experience at work?**:
	+ Provide any suggestions or ideas you have for improving your career development experience.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions regarding career development at our organization?**:
	+ Share any further thoughts or ideas you have on this topic.
