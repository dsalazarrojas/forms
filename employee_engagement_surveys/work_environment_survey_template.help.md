# work_environment_survey_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on their work environment, aiming to improve employee engagement and satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer based on your genuine opinions and experiences. Select the best option that reflects your feelings and thoughts. For the multiple-choice question, you can select all applicable options. Do not worry about the length of your answers; we value your honest feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction**: (`Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?`, single, required)
This question asks about your general level of satisfaction with your current role. Please choose the option that best represents your feelings.
* **Communication from Management**: (`How would you rate communication from management?`, single, required)
This question assesses your experience with communication from management. Choose the option that reflects how you perceive their communication.
* **Supported in Professional Development**: (`Do you feel supported in your professional development?`, single, required)
This question checks if you feel empowered to grow and develop in your career. Choose the option that best describes your feelings.
* **Productivity Improvers**: (`Which of these resources would most improve your productivity? (Select all that apply)`, multiple, required)
Choose all the resources that you think would enhance your productivity. You can select multiple options.
*   *Better software tools*: This option suggests that having more efficient and effective software would help you work more efficiently.
*   *More training*: This option implies that receiving training or development opportunities would be beneficial for your productivity.
*   *Quieter workspace*: This option suggests that having a quieter workspace would improve your productivity.
*   *Mentorship program*: This option indicates that having a mentor who guides and supports you in your work would be beneficial.
*   *Flexible hours*: This option suggests that having more flexible work hours would help you work more efficiently.
* **Suggestions**: (`What could improve your experience at work?`, text, optional)
This question invites you to share any additional thoughts or ideas that could make your work experience better.
* **Additional Comments**: (`Any additional comments or suggestions?`, text, optional)
This final question allows you to provide any comments or suggestions that you did not mention earlier, providing us with more detailed feedback.

## Tips
Please answer sincerely and honestly, and do not worry about the length of your answers. Your feedback is valuable for improving our work environment and employee engagement. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
