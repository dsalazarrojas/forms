# employee_morale_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on employee morale and engagement to foster a positive work environment. It will help management understand the overall satisfaction and engagement levels of employees, identify areas of improvement, and take steps to enhance the work experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your answers for each question and provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have. Please answer all questions that apply to you, and provide as much detail as possible in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Overall Satisfaction with Role
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (`Overall Satisfaction`, `single`, required):
This question asks about your general satisfaction with your current role within the company. Choose the option that best describes your feelings.
### How Would You Rate Communication from Management?
* **How would you rate communication from management?** (`communication_from_management`, `single`, required):
This question assesses your perception of the quality of communication from management. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
### Do You Feel Supported in Your Professional Development?
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development?** (`feel_supported`, `single`, required):
This question evaluates whether you feel empowered to grow professionally within the company. Choose "true" if you feel supported, "false" if not.
### Team Collaboration
* **Which of the following best describe your team's collaboration?** (`team_collaboration`, `multiple`, required):
Select all that apply to describe your team's collaboration experience.
	+ **Excellent**
	+ **Good**
	+ **Fair**
	+ **Poor**
### Improving Day-to-Day Experience
* **What could most improve your day-to-day experience at work?** (`improve_experience`, `text`, required):
Provide a brief description of what you would need to see a significant improvement in your day-to-day experience.
### Recognition for Contributions?
* **Do you feel recognized for your contributions?** (`feel_recognized`, `single`, required):
This question evaluates whether you feel acknowledged for your efforts and contributions.
### Belonging at the Organization
* **Do you feel a sense of belonging at our organization?** (`feel_belonging`, `single`, required):
This question explores your sense of connection and belonging within the company.
### Additional Comments or Suggestions?
* **Any additional comments or suggestions?** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional):
Please provide any additional thoughts or ideas you may have to help improve the work environment.

## Tips
Please be honest and objective when answering these questions. Your input will help shape the work experience for everyone.
