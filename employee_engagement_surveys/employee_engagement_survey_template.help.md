# employee_engagement_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Employee Engagement Survey Template** is a confidential tool for employees to provide honest feedback on their work experience and suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read through each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
2. Choose the answer that best reflects your opinion or experience.
3. Select all that apply for the multiple-choice question.
4. Enter your thoughts and suggestions in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Question 1: Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks about your general satisfaction with your job role.
* **How would you rate communication from management?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This question assesses your experience with communication from management, such as email, meetings, or updates.
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development?** (`true`, `false`, `Somewhat`): This question checks whether you feel encouraged to learn and grow in your job.
* **What aspects of our company culture do you value most? (Select all that apply)** (`Collaboration`, `Support`, `Respect`, `Open communication`, `Innovation`, `Work-life balance`): Choose all the aspects of the company culture that you value most.
* **What could most improve your experience at work?**: This open-ended question allows you to provide detailed feedback on what could enhance your job experience.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**: Share any further thoughts or ideas you have for improving your work experience.
