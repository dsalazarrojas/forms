# employee_empowerment_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather insights on employee empowerment and engagement to create a more supportive work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, selecting the response that best reflects your feelings about your role and experiences at work.
2. For question 4, select all the factors that contribute most to your sense of empowerment at work.
3. Use the "Additional Comments" section to provide any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have about your experience with employee empowerment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction** (`Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?`): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your role. Choose the response that best reflects your feelings about your current position and responsibilities.
	+ Very satisfied: You are extremely happy with your role and feel it meets your expectations.
	+ Somewhat satisfied: You are somewhat pleased with your role, but there is room for improvement.
	+ Neutral: You neither feel extremely satisfied nor extremely dissatisfied with your role.
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied: You are somewhat unhappy with your role and feel it does not meet your expectations.
	+ Very dissatisfied: You are extremely unhappy with your role and feel it is not suitable for you.
* **Decision-Making Power** (`To what extent do you feel empowered to make decisions in your role?`): This question assesses your feeling of empowerment to make decisions in your current position. Choose the response that best reflects your level of autonomy.
	+ Fully empowered: You have complete authority to make decisions in your role.
	+ Mostly empowered: You have significant autonomy to make decisions in your role.
	+ Somewhat empowered: You have some decision-making power, but with limitations.
	+ Rarely empowered: You are rarely empowered to make decisions in your role.
	+ Not empowered at all: You have no decision-making power in your role.
* **Support and Development** (`Do you feel supported in your professional development and growth opportunities?`): This question explores your feelings about support for your professional growth. Choose the response that best reflects your level of support.
	+ Strongly agree: You feel very supported in your professional development and growth opportunities.
	+ Agree: You feel somewhat supported in your professional development and growth opportunities.
	+ Neutral: You neither feel extremely supported nor unsupported.
	+ Disagree: You feel somewhat unsupported in your professional development and growth opportunities.
	+ Strongly disagree: You feel very unsupported in your professional development and growth opportunities.
* **Empowerment Factors** (`Which of the following factors contribute most to your sense of empowerment at work? (select all that apply)`): This question allows you to identify the factors that contribute most to your sense of empowerment. Select all the factors that you feel are most important.
	+ Autonomy: Having control over your work and schedule.
	+ Clear goals: Having clear expectations and objectives.
	+ Manager support: Having a supportive manager who encourages your growth.
	+ Team collaboration: Working with a collaborative team.
	+ Recognition: Receiving recognition for your work and achievements.
	+ Access to resources: Having access to necessary resources and tools.
* **Additional Comments** (`What could improve your experience at work regarding empowerment or engagement?`): This question is for any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have about your experience with employee empowerment.
* **Additional Suggestions** (`Do you have any additional comments or suggestions regarding employee empowerment?`): This question is for any further thoughts or suggestions you may have about employee empowerment and support at work.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Select all applicable factors for question 4.
* Use the "Additional Comments" and "Additional Suggestions" sections to provide any further information or suggestions you may have.
