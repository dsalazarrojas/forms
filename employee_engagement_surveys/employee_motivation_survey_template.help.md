# employee_motivation_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from employees on their motivation and engagement within the organization. This form will help identify areas of strength and weakness, allowing the organization to make informed decisions to enhance employee satisfaction and motivation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all questions honestly, selecting the best option that reflects your opinion.
2. For the "What factors would most improve your experience at work and motivation?" question, select all that apply.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the text box at the end of the form.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question asks for your overall satisfaction with your role within the organization.
* **How would you rate communication from management regarding company goals and changes?** (`Excellent`, `Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This question assesses your perception of management's communication on company goals and changes.
* **Do you feel supported in your professional development and career growth?** (`true`, `false`, `Somewhat`): This question determines if you feel supported in your career growth and development within the organization.
* **What factors would most improve your experience at work and motivation?** (`multiple` with options `Better work-life balance`, `More recognition`, `Clearer career paths`, `Improved benefits`, `Better team collaboration`, `More challenging work`): Select all factors that you believe would improve your experience and motivation.
* **Please provide any additional comments or suggestions related to employee motivation or engagement**: This question allows you to provide any additional insights or suggestions that might not be covered in the previous questions.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as they will help the organization better understand your thoughts and feelings.
* If you have any additional suggestions or ideas, use the text box to share them.
* Remember to select all that apply for the "What factors would most improve your experience at work and motivation?" question.
