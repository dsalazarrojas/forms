# Company Satisfaction Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey template is designed to help the company understand employee satisfaction and engagement. It collects feedback on various aspects of the work experience, including communication, support, and company culture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with your role at the organization.
2. Rate your satisfaction with communication from management.
3. Indicate whether you feel supported in your professional development.
4. Choose the aspects of company culture that you value most.
5. If you have any suggestions for improvement, type them in the designated text field.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Satisfaction with Role**: 
  (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)
  This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with your current role. Please select the option that best describes how you feel about your work experience.
* **Communication from Management**: 
  (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor)
  This question assesses your satisfaction with communication from management. Rate your experience using the provided options.
* **Feeling Supported**: 
  (true, false, Somewhat)
  This question is about whether you feel supported in your professional development. Please select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Valued Company Culture Aspects**: 
  (Team collaboration, Work-life balance, Innovation, Learning opportunities, Recognition)
  Choose all the aspects of company culture that you value most. Select as many or as few options as you feel are relevant to your experience.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: 
  (text field)
  If there are any specific areas you think could be improved, please type them in this text field.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions**: 
  (text field)
  This is an optional text field where you can add any additional comments or suggestions that might not have been covered in the previous questions.

## Tips
- Be honest and genuine in your responses.
- Take your time to thoughtfully consider your answers.
- If you need more space, feel free to add additional comments or suggestions.
- Your feedback is valuable and will help us improve the work experience for everyone.
