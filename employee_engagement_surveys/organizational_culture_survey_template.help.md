# Organizational Culture Survey Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather insights on our organizational culture and employee engagement to foster a positive work environment. It aims to understand how employees feel about their role, communication from management, support for professional development, and their values within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the question "Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?" by selecting one of the five options provided.
2. Rate the communication from management by selecting one of the four options from "Excellent" to "Poor".
3. Choose whether you feel supported in your professional development by selecting "true", "false", or "Somewhat".
4. Select all the aspects of our organizational culture that you value most from the list: "Collaboration", "Innovation", "Work-life balance", "Recognition", "Transparency", and "Professional growth".
5. Provide any suggestions or comments on what could improve your experience at work in the text field.
6. Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (`satisfaction`, required): This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your current role in the organization.
- **How would you rate communication from management?** (`communication`, required): This question asks you to rate the quality of communication from management, with options ranging from "Excellent" to "Poor".
- **Do you feel supported in your professional development?** (`support`, required): This question checks whether you feel supported in your professional development, such as training, mentorship, or resources.
- **Which of the following aspects of our organizational culture do you value most?** (`values`, optional): This question allows you to select all the aspects of our organizational culture that you value most, such as "Collaboration", "Innovation", "Work-life balance", "Recognition", "Transparency", and "Professional growth".
- **What could improve your experience at work?** (`feedback`, optional): This question is for you to provide any suggestions or comments on what could improve your experience at work.
- **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, optional): This question is for you to add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
- Select all the aspects of our organizational culture that you value most.
- Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please be constructive and respectful.
