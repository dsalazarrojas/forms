# employee_recognition_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is meant to collect employee feedback on their experience with recognition at work. Your responses will help the organization understand its strengths and weaknesses in this area and implement changes that will enhance employee engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

To provide your honest and constructive feedback, follow these steps:

*   Answer the questions according to your experience and perception of recognition at your organization.
*   Take your time to think carefully before submitting your answers, if needed.
*   If you have any comments or suggestions, use the "Additional Comments" section to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Satisfaction with Role**: 
    How satisfied are you with your role at the company? This question asks about your general feelings towards your position and responsibilities.
*   **Frequency of Recognition**: 
    How often do you feel recognized for your contributions at work? The options range from "Very often" to "Never," giving you a clear idea of how frequently you receive recognition.
*   **Forms of Recognition**: 
    Which forms of recognition are most meaningful to you? You can choose all that apply from a list of options such as verbal praise, written acknowledgment, monetary bonus, gift/voucher, or professional development opportunities.
*   **Manager Communication**: 
    How would you rate the quality of communication from management about your performance and contributions? Use the scale from "Excellent" to "Poor" to express your thoughts.
*   **Initiatives for Improvement**: 
    What specific ideas or changes would you suggest to enhance your experience with recognition at work? This is an open-ended question where you can share any ideas you have.
*   **Additional Comments**: 
    Do you have any other thoughts or suggestions not covered in the previous questions? This is your chance to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

*   Be honest and thorough in your answers.
*   Remember that your feedback is confidential and will be used solely to improve the workplace culture.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the organization.
