# employee_wellness_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The employee wellness survey is designed to gather feedback on employee satisfaction, communication, and areas for improvement in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select one of the five satisfaction levels when answering "Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?" to express your level of satisfaction with your current role.
2.  Choose one of the four rating levels when answering "How would you rate communication from management?" to provide your honest opinion about the organization's communication.
3.  Select "True" if you feel supported in your professional development, or "False" if you do not.
4.  Choose all the initiatives that would improve your overall wellness at work.
5.  Answer "How do you feel about your day-to-day experience at work?" with your honest thoughts and suggestions.
6.  Select one of the five agreement levels when answering "Do you feel a sense of belonging within the organization?" to express your level of agreement or disagreement.
7.  Use the open-ended text field to provide any additional comments or suggestions regarding employee wellness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?** (`Single selection`)
    *   This question helps us understand your feelings towards your role and overall job satisfaction. Choose one of the five satisfaction levels: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
*   **How would you rate communication from management?** (`Single selection`)
    *   This question is about evaluating the effectiveness of communication from management. Choose one of the four rating levels: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
*   **Do you feel supported in your professional development?** (`Single selection`)
    *   This question determines whether you feel supported in your growth and development as a professional. Select "True" if you feel supported, or "False" if you do not.
*   **What initiatives or resources would most improve your overall wellness at work?** (`Multiple selection`)
    *   Here, you can choose all the initiatives that you believe would enhance your overall wellness. Select from Mental health support, Physical fitness programs, Flexible work options, Stress management workshops, Financial wellness guidance, Social activities.
*   **Do you feel a sense of belonging within the organization?** (`Single selection`)
    *   This question helps us gauge your sense of belonging within the organization. Choose one of the five agreement levels: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree.
*   **What could improve your day-to-day experience at work?** (`Open-ended text field`)
    *   This field is for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions about your day-to-day experience at work.
*   **Any additional comments or suggestions regarding employee wellness?** (`Open-ended text field`)
    *   This field is for you to provide any other suggestions or ideas about how to improve employee wellness.
