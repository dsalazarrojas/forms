# Workplace Bullying Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your experiences with bullying and harassment in the workplace, and your opinions on how the organization can improve its safety and culture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Select all options that apply when asked about forms of bullying or harassment.
3. Respond to the question about reporting and resolution satisfaction with the answer that best reflects your experience.
4. Provide detailed responses for the open-ended questions in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Have you personally experienced bullying or harassment at this organization?** (`Have you personally experienced workplace bullying or harassment at this organization?`, Yes/No, required):
This question asks about your personal experiences with bullying or harassment.
* **Have you witnessed bullying or harassment of others at this organization?** (`Have you witnessed bullying or harassment of others at this organization?`, Yes/No, required):
This question asks about your observations of bullying or harassment experienced by others.
* **Forms of bullying or harassment** (`If yes, what forms of bullying or harassment did you experience or witness? (select all that apply)`, Multiple Choice, optional):
Select all forms of bullying or harassment you have experienced or witnessed, such as:
	+ Verbal abuse
	+ Exclusion
	+ Intimidation
	+ Spreading rumors
	+ Sabotage of work
	+ Unfair treatment
* **Did you report the incident(s) of bullying or harassment?** (`Did you report the incident(s) of bullying or harassment?`, Yes/No, required):
This question asks about your experience with reporting incidents of bullying or harassment.
* **Response or resolution satisfaction** (`If reported, how satisfied were you with the response or resolution?`, Single Choice, optional):
Select your level of satisfaction with the response or resolution.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
	+ Not applicable
* **Impact of experience** (`How has your experience of bullying or harassment affected you professionally or personally?`, Text, optional):
Describe how your experience has affected you.
* **Measures for improvement** (`What measures do you think the organization could take to prevent bullying and harassment?`, Text, optional):
Suggest measures that the organization can take to improve its safety and culture.
