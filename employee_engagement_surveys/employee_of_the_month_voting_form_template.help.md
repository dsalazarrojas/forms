# employee_of_the_month_voting_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect nominations from colleagues for the Employee of the Month award. Nominations should be based on outstanding performance and contributions demonstrated this month.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Nominate a Colleague**: Type the name of the colleague you'd like to nominate in the text field.
2. **Choose Nomination Reasons**: Select all the reasons why you're nominating this colleague:
	* Exceeded expectations
	* Demonstrated teamwork
	* Showed leadership
	* Improved processes
	* Helped colleagues
	* Positive attitude
3. **Specific Contributions**: Describe in detail how this colleague contributed outstandingly this month.
4. **Rating**: Assess the overall impact of this colleague's contributions this month:
	* Outstanding
	* Excellent
	* Good
	* Average
5. **Alignment with Company Values**: Indicate whether you believe this colleague embodies the company's values:
	* Strongly agree
	* Agree
	* Neutral
	* Disagree
	* Strongly disagree
6. **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or reasons to support your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which colleague are you nominating for Employee of the Month?** (`name`, `text`, required): Type the name of the colleague you'd like to nominate.
* **Choose Nomination Reasons** (`reasons`, `multiple`, required):
	+ Exceeded expectations
	+ Demonstrated teamwork
	+ Showed leadership
	+ Improved processes
	+ Helped colleagues
	+ Positive attitude
* **Why are you nominating this individual?** (`comments`, `text`, required): Describe in detail why you're nominating this colleague, selecting all that apply.
* **In what specific ways did they contribute outstandingly this month?** (`contributions`, `text`, required): Be specific about the ways in which the nominee contributed outstandingly this month.
* **How would you rate their overall impact this month?** (`rating`, `single`, required): Assess the overall impact of this colleague's contributions this month:
	+ Outstanding
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
* **Do you believe they embody our company values?** (`values`, `single`, required): Indicate whether you believe this colleague embodies the company's values:
	+ Strongly agree
	+ Agree
	+ Neutral
	+ Disagree
	+ Strongly disagree
* **Any additional comments to support your nomination?** (`comments2`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or reasons to support your nomination.

## Tips

* Be specific and provide concrete examples of the nominee's outstanding contributions.
* Ensure the nominee has demonstrated outstanding performance and contributions this month.
* Select the options that best fit the nominee's behavior and performance.
* Provide detailed comments to support your nomination.
