# job_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Job Satisfaction Survey** is a tool to measure employee engagement and help managers identify areas for improvement within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, considering your experiences and opinions about your role at the organization.
2. Choose one or multiple options for questions 1-4, depending on the specific question type (single or multiple).
3. Provide a brief explanation for questions 5 and 6 in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Overall, how satisfied are you with your role at the organization?**
* (`Overall Satisfaction`, single, required): This question assesses your general feeling about your job satisfaction. Please select the option that best describes your level of satisfaction.

### 2. **How would you rate communication from management?**
* (`Communication`, single, required): Evaluate the quality of communication from management, using the provided rating scale (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).

### 3. **Do you feel supported in your professional development?**
* (`Support`, single, required): Indicate whether you feel supported in your professional growth and development within the organization.

### 4. **What aspects of your job do you find most fulfilling?**
* (`Fulfilling Aspects`, multiple, required): Choose all that apply from the list of options provided (Work-life balance, Team collaboration, Challenging tasks, Learning opportunities, Compensation & benefits).

### 5. **What could improve your experience at work?**
* (`Improvement Areas`, text, optional): Share your thoughts on specific areas that could enhance your job satisfaction.

### 6. **Any additional comments or suggestions?**
* (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any further feedback or ideas you have about your job satisfaction or work experience.

## Tips
- Be honest and sincere in your responses.
- If you have trouble with a particular question, feel free to add a comment in the space provided.
- Your feedback is invaluable to us and will help us improve the organization.
