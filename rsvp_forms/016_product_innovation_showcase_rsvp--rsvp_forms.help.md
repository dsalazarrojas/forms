# Product Innovation Showcase RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Product Innovation Showcase RSVP" form is used to collect information from attendees for a showcase event that caters to different interests and needs. It is likely designed to manage RSVPs for a complex event such as a product launch or a conference with multiple sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
* Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
* Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
* If you have a phone number, fill it in the "Phone" field (optional).
* Choose your session interests from the dropdown menu under "Session Interests".
* Select your desired Session Date and Session Time from the respective dropdown menus.
* Choose your preferred Session Location from the dropdown menu.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
* If you have any other comments or questions, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any other notes or questions, enter them in the "Other Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter your email address here. This will be used to contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number here if you have one. This is optional.
* **Session Interests**: Choose one or more from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand which sessions are of interest to you.
* **Session Time**: Select the time for the session you are interested in.
* **Session Date**: Select the date for the session you are interested in.
* **Session Location**: Choose the location for the session you are interested in.
* **Session Preferences**: If you have any preferences or requests, enter them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions Notes**: If you have any notes about your dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions Comments**: If you have any comments about your dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Other**: If you have any other comments or requests, enter them here.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them here.
* **Other Notes**: If you have any other notes or questions, enter them here.
* **Additional Notes**: If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields.
* Make sure to choose your correct session interests and preferences.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event organizers.
