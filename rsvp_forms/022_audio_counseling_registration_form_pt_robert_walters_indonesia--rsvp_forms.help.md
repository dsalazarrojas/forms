# Audio Counseling Registration Form PT. Robert Walters Indonesia - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants interested in audio counseling services provided by PT. Robert Walters Indonesia. The form aims to gather essential details about the participant, including their personal information, session preferences, and background, to schedule an effective audio counseling session.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- For each field, provide the most up-to-date and relevant information.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, as it appears on official documents.
* **Current Job Title**: Provide your current job title and position.
* **Department**: Select the department where you work, such as Finance, Human Resources, Sales and Marketing, IT and Engineering, Operations, or Legal.
* **Professional Email Address**: Enter your official email address associated with your work or educational institution.
* **Preferred Date for Counseling**: Choose a date for the counseling session that suits you best.
* **Preferred Time Slot**: Select a time slot that suits your availability, such as Morning, Mid-day, Afternoon, or Late Afternoon.
* **What is your main goal for this session?**: Describe the primary reason you want to have an audio counseling session.
* **Have you participated in audio counseling before?**: Select 'True' if you have had an audio counseling session previously, 'False' otherwise.
* **Any specific concerns you would like to address?**: If you have any particular issues you would like to discuss during the session, provide them here.
* **I understand that all sessions are strictly confidential**: Confirm your understanding of the confidentiality of the counseling session.
* **Registration Date**: This field will be automatically populated with the current date.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately, as they are necessary for scheduling the audio counseling session. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact our team for assistance.
