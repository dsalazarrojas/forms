# Friendsgiving Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help plan and organize a Friendsgiving potluck dinner. It allows guests to input their name, select the host, choose the type of dishes they will bring, and RSVP with their email address. By filling out this form, you will be able to see who is coming, who will be bringing what, and get in touch with guests to confirm their attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the "Main Info" field with your name and any other relevant details.
2. In the "Guests" field, type in the names of your guests who are attending.
3. In the "Host Info" field, select the person who will be hosting the event from the provided options.
4. In the "Event Info" field, you can add any additional event details.
5. In the "Date" field, select the date of the Friendsgiving dinner.
6. In the "Time" field, select the time of the Friendsgiving dinner.
7. In the "Potluck Info" field, select the type of dishes you will be bringing from the provided options.
8. In the "RSVP" field, enter your email address so we can confirm your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Info**:
	+ This field is for you to input any main information about yourself, such as your name. It's a text field where you can type your name and any other details you'd like to share.
* **Guests**:
	+ This field is for you to list the names of your guests who are attending. Type in the names of those coming, separated by commas.
* **Host Info**:
	+ This field allows you to select the host of the event from the provided options.
* **Event Info**:
	+ This field is for you to add any additional event details such as location, dress code, etc.
* **Date**:
	+ This field is a date field where you can select the date of the Friendsgiving dinner.
* **Time**:
	+ This field is a time field where you can select the time of the Friendsgiving dinner.
* **Potluck Info**:
	+ This field is for you to select the type of dishes you will be bringing from the provided options.
* **RSVP**:
	+ This field is for you to enter your email address so we can confirm your attendance.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your invitation is sent successfully.
* Use commas to separate multiple guests in the "Guests" field.
* If you're unsure about the date or time, use the calendar feature to select a date and time.
* Use the "Potluck Info" field to choose the type of dish you will bring, so we can plan accordingly.
* Enter a valid email address in the "RSVP" field, so we can confirm your attendance.
