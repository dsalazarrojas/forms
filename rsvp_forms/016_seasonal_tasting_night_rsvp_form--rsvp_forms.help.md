# Seasonal Tasting Night RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seasonal Tasting Night RSVP Form is used to register attendees for a seasonal tasting night event. Please fill out the form to let us know if you can attend and provide necessary information for planning and preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on each field to fill out the required information.
- Fill out all the fields marked as "required" to ensure you're registered correctly.
- Select the correct options for your preferences and circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Tasting Night RSVP** (`seasonal_tasting_night_rsvp_form`, text, not required): This is the heading of the form.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional details about the event that you'd like us to know.
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, not required): Enter any relevant information about your guests, such as their names and contact information.
* **Guest Info** (`guest_info`, text, not required): Enter any additional guest information.
* **Guests** (`guests`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" for each guest that will be attending.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, not required): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, text, not required): Enter your dietary preferences, such as vegetarian or gluten-free.
* **Vegetarian** (`vegetarian`, select_multiple, not required): Select your vegetarian options.
* **Vegan** (`vegan`, select_multiple, not required): Select your vegan options.
* **Gluten Free** (`gluten_free`, select_multiple, not required): Select your gluten-free options.
* **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or requests you'd like to communicate.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, not required): Enter the date you're planning to attend.
* **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the time you plan to arrive.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit your RSVP.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, not required): Enter a valid email to confirm your RSVP.
* **Phone Confirmation** (`phone_confirmation`, text, not required): Enter your phone number to confirm your RSVP.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to guarantee correct registration.
Please select the correct options for your guests and preferences.
Please double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
