# Worship Service Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worship Service Availability Form is used to track the availability of volunteers for worship services. It helps the service team schedule volunteers for specific services on specific dates and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required."
2. Select the date and time you are available to serve.
3. Enter your availability in the "Availability" field, selecting all relevant days.
4. Provide your available start and end times, if applicable.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the "Notes" field.
6. Indicate your RSVP preference in the "RSVP" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): Select the date you are available to serve.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required): Choose the time you are available to serve.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you want it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days you are available to serve. Options include Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
* **Available Start Time** (`available_start_time`, time, optional): Enter your available start time, if applicable.
* **Available End Time** (`available_end_time`, time, optional): Enter your available end time, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the service team.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you are available to serve ("True") or not ("False").
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the service team.
