# Admissions VIP SERIES - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Admissions VIP SERIES form is used to collect information from attendees to ensure a smooth and personalized experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each section, and fill in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`attendee_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`organization_affiliation`, `text`, optional): Provide the name of your organization or institution (if applicable).
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your professional title or position.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `select_one`, required): Select a date you would like to attend from the list of available dates.
* **Event Session** (`event_session`, `select_one`, required): Choose which session you would like to attend (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`, `select_one`, required): Select how you plan to attend the event (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Number of Guests** (`guest_count`, `number`, required): Indicate how many people will be attending with you.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify.
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, `text`, optional): If you require any special accommodations, please indicate.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Specify your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **COVID Health Declaration** (`covid_health_declaration`, `select_one`, required): Declare that you are free of COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, `select_one`, optional): If fully vaccinated, select yes; otherwise, select no or prefer not to answer.
* **Previous VIP Events** (`previous_vip_events`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have attended previous VIP series events.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_interest`, `text`, optional): Specify which topics you are most interested in learning about.
* **Networking Interest** (`networking_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select if you are interested in networking opportunities during the event.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required): Date of submitting this RSVP.
* **Attendee Signature** (`attendee_signature`, `text`, required): Provide an electronic signature to confirm your RSVP.
