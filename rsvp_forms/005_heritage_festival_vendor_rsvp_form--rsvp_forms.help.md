# Heritage Festival Vendor RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heritage Festival Vendor RSVP Form is a mandatory registration form for vendors who want to participate in the Heritage Festival. This form collects information about the vendor, their product or service, and their participation status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and business name in the "vendor_name" and "vendor_business_name" fields respectively.
2. Provide the address of your business in the "vendor_address" field.
3. Enter your city in the "vendor_city" field.
4. Enter your state in the "vendor_state" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "vendor_email" field.
6. Fill in the "vendor_info" field with a brief description of your product or service.
7. Select the date you plan to participate in the "rsvp_date" field.
8. Choose the time you plan to participate in the "rsvp_time" field.
9. Select your booth requirements from the "booth_requirements" field. 
10. Choose whether you will participate or not in the "vendor_participation" field.
11. Fill in any additional comments or notes in the "vendor_notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name**: `vendor_name` (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the vendor.
* **Vendor Business Name**: `vendor_business_name` (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the vendor's business.
* **Vendor Address**: `vendor_address` (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the vendor's business.
* **Vendor City**: `vendor_city` (Text, Optional): Enter the city of the vendor's business.
* **Vendor State**: `vendor_state` (Text, Optional): Enter the state of the vendor's business.
* **Vendor Email**: `vendor_email` (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the vendor.
* **Vendor Info**: `vendor_info` (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the vendor's product or service.
* **RSVP Date**: `rsvp_date` (Date, Optional): Select the date the vendor plans to participate in the festival.
* **RSVP Time**: `rsvp_time` (Time, Optional): Select the time the vendor plans to participate in the festival.
* **Booth Requirements**: `booth_requirements` (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any specific booth requirements the vendor may need. 
* **Vendor Participation**: `vendor_participation` (Select One, Optional): Choose whether the vendor will participate in the festival or not.
* **Vendor Contact**: `vendor_contact` (Text, Optional): Enter any additional contact information for the vendor.
* **Vendor Notes**: `vendor_notes` (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the vendor's participation.

## Tips

- Please ensure that all required fields are completed correctly.
- Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the festival organizers.

By following this guide, you should be able to complete the Heritage Festival Vendor RSVP Form accurately and correctly. If you encounter any issues, please contact the festival organizers for assistance.
