# hawaii_event_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants in an event held in Hawaii. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the location where the event will be held in the "Location" field.
2. Fill in the "Event Status" field with the current status of the event.
3. Enter the payment status in the "Payment Status" field.
4. Confirm your participation in the "Confirm Participant" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
• **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the event.
• **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, not required): Enter any relevant information about the participant.
• **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): Enter any contact information for the participant.
• **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, not required): Enter any payment-related information.
• **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, text, not required): Enter any payment confirmation details.
• **Participant Payment Info** (`participant_payment_info`, text, not required): Enter any participant payment information.
• **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, text, not required): Enter the event dates.
• **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
• **Event Status** (`event_status`, text, required): Enter the current status of the event.
• **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, text, required): Enter the current payment status.
• **Confirm Participant** (`confirm_participant`, text, required): Confirm your participation in the event.

## Tips
• Ensure that you fill out all required fields correctly to avoid any issues or errors.
• Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter, especially for fields like "Location" and "Event Status" which are crucial for the event's organization.
