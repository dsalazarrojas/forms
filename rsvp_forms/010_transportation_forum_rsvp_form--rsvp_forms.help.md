# Transportation Forum RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Forum RSVP Form is used to allow users to RSVP for a transportation event. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the event organizers have the correct information about the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Name, which should be the title of the event you plan to attend.
2. Select the Event Date and Start Time and End Time for the event.
3. Provide the Location of the event.
4. Choose the Transportation Type that you will be using to get to the event.
5. Enter the Number of Passengers that will be attending with you.
6. If you are available, enter your Contact Phone number and Contact Email.
7. Finally, provide any Comments you may have about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (Text, Required/Optional)**: Enter the name of the event you plan to attend.
* **Event Date (Date, Required/Optional)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Start Time (Time, Required/Optional)**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time (Time, Required/Optional)**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Location (Text, Required/Optional)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Transportation Type (Select One, Required/Optional)**: Choose the mode of transportation you will use to get to the event.
* **Number of Passengers (Number, Required/Optional)**: Enter the number of passengers attending with you.
* **Contact Phone (Text, Required/Optional)**: If you are available, enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (Email, Required/Optional)**: If you are available, enter your contact email.
* **Comments (Text, Required/Optional)**: Enter any comments you may have about the event.

## Tips
- Be sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizer directly.
- The event organizers will use this information to plan and prepare for the event.
