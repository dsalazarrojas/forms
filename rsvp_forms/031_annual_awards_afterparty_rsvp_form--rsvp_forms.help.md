# Annual Awards Afterparty RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from guests attending the Annual Awards Afterparty. This includes personal details, RSVP status, and event specifics such as date, time, and location. Providing accurate information will help us better organize the event and ensure an enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
2. Provide your email address for event updates.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. If you're attending the afterparty, select "Yes, I will attend" for the RSVP status.
5. Indicate the number of guests accompanying you.
6. If you're bringing guests, provide their names.
7. Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or both).
8. Choose whether you want to receive reminders about the event.
9. If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, list them.
10. Specify any accessibility needs.
11. Select your seating or location preference for the event.
12. Indicate your entertainment interests.
13. Choose your drink preference.
14. If you have any special requests or preferences, please specify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: <full_name> (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: <email_address> (`Email Address`, email, required): Provide your email address for event updates.
* **Phone Number**: <phone_number> (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Awarded Person or Table Name**: <guest_table_name> (`Awarded Person or Table Name`, text, optional): If you're an awarded person or table, enter the name associated with you.
* **Will You be Attending the Afterparty?**: <rsvp_status> (`Will You be Attending the Afterparty?`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I will attend" if you're attending the event.
* **Number of Guests Including Yourself**: <number_of_guests> (`Number of Guests Including Yourself`, number, required): Indicate the number of guests accompanying you.
* **Plus One Details**: <plus_one_details> (`Plus One Details`, text, optional): If you're bringing guests, provide their names.
* **Afterparty Date**: <event_date> (`Afterparty Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Afterparty Time**: <event_time> (`Afterparty Time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location**: <event_location> (`Event Location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the afterparty is held.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: <dietary_restrictions> (`Dietary Restrictions or Allergies`, text, optional): List any food allergies or preferences.
* **Any Accessibility Needs**: <accessibility_needs> (`Any Accessibility Needs`, text, optional): Specify any special accommodations needed.
* **Seating or Location Preference**: <seating_preference> (`Seating or Location Preference`, select_one, optional): Select your seating or location preference (e.g., near the bar, dance floor, or quiet area).
* **Entertainment Interest**: <entertainment_interest> (`Entertainment Interest`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate which types of activities interest you (e.g., dancing, music, games).
* **Drink Preference**: <drink_preference> (`Drink Preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your drink preference (alcoholic, non-alcoholic, or no preference).
* **Preferred Contact Method**: <contact_method> (`Preferred Contact Method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or both).
* **Send me Reminders**: <receive_reminders> (`Send me Reminders`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want event reminders.
* **Names of Additional Guests**: <additional_guests_names> (`Names of Additional Guests`, text, optional): If bringing guests, list their names.
