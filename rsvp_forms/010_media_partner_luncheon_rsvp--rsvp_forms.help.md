# Media Partner Luncheon RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Partner Luncheon RSVP form is designed to collect essential information from media partners attending a luncheon event. This form helps us ensure that we have an accurate headcount and understand each attendee's preferences and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review each field carefully to provide accurate and complete information.
2. Complete all required fields (marked as `true`).
3. If you have any special requests or dietary requirements, please provide detailed information in the relevant fields.
4. Check your email address for accuracy and ensure it is correct.
5. Select your organization and job title correctly from the provided options.
6. If you have any further comments or special requests, please use the `Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp`): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or as you would like it to be printed on our program.
* **Guest Name** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_guest_name`): If you are bringing a guest, please provide their name.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_dietary_preferences`): Select your dietary preferences (e.g., Option 1, Other).
* **Guest Count** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_guest_count`): Enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **Dietary Comment** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_dietary_comment`): If you have specific dietary needs or requests, please provide detailed information here.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_dietary_restrictions`): Select any applicable dietary restrictions (e.g., No Restrictions, Gluten-free, Vegetarian, Vegan).
* **Dietary Restrictions Comment** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_dietary_restrictions_comment`): If you have any additional comments or requests regarding your dietary restrictions, please provide them here.
* **RSVP** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_rsvp`): Select `Accept` to confirm your attendance and `Decline` if you cannot attend.
* **Email** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_email`): Enter your email address accurately, as we will use it to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_phone`): Provide your phone number for any follow-up calls or questions.
* **Guest Email** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_guest_email`): If you are bringing a guest, enter their email address accurately.
* **Organization** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_organization`): Select your organization from the provided options (e.g., None, Company 1, Company 2, Other).
* **Job Title** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_job_title`): Enter your job title accurately.
* **Function** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_function`): Select your function from the provided options (e.g., None, CEO, CFO, CTO, Other).
* **Dietary Special Requests** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_dietary_special_requests`): If you have any special dietary requests, please provide them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions 2** (`media_partner_luncheon_rsvp_dietary_restrictions_2`): This field is currently not used.
