# Dance Festival Performer RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from performers who want to participate in the Dance Festival. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that the festival organizers can plan the event correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact email and phone number.
2. Select your arrival and departure dates.
3. Choose your arrival time.
4. Specify your production needs (e.g., sound equipment, stage setup).
5. Confirm the agreements (e.g., performance schedule, fees).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performer Name** (Performer Name, text, required):
	+ This field is used to enter your name as a performer.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required):
	+ This field is used to enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter your contact phone number. (Note: If you don't have a phone, you can leave this field blank.)
* **Arrival Date** (Arrival Date, date, optional):
	+ This field is used to select the date you plan to arrive at the festival.
* **Departure Date** (Departure Date, date, optional):
	+ This field is used to select the date you plan to depart from the festival.
* **Arrival Time** (Arrival Time, time, optional):
	+ This field is used to select the time you plan to arrive at the festival.
* **Production Needs** (Production Needs, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to list any special production needs you have (e.g., sound equipment, stage setup).
* **Agreements** (Agreements, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to confirm your agreements, such as performance schedule and fees.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the festival organizers can plan the event correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the festival organizers.
* If you have already submitted an RSVP form, please do not resubmit.
