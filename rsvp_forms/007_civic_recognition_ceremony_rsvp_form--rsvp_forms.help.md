## Civic Recognition Ceremony RSVP Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
This form is designed to help us track attendance and prepare for the Civic Recognition Ceremony. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we can properly plan for your participation and any special needs you may have.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. If you have an affiliation or organization, please enter it in the "Organization or Affiliation" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field to receive event updates and confirmations.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field to ensure we can contact you.
5. Select how you will respond to the question "Will You Attend the Ceremony" from the dropdown menu.
6. Choose the number of guests you will bring with you in the "Number of Guests" dropdown menu.
7. If you have additional guests attending, list their names in the "Names of Additional Guests" field.
8. Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have in the "Dietary Restrictions or Allergies" dropdown menu.
9. If you have specific dietary needs, please specify them in the "Please Specify Dietary Requirements" field.
10. If you require accessibility accommodations, select "True" in the "Do You Require Accessibility Accommodations" dropdown menu and describe your needs in the "Please Specify Accommodation Needs" field.
11. Indicate how you will arrive at the event ("Personal vehicle", "Carpool", "Public transportation", "Ride share or taxi", or "Walking") in the "How Will You Arrive at the Event" dropdown menu.
12. Select if you will need parking ("True" or "False") in the "Will You Need Parking" dropdown menu.
13. If you are being honored at the ceremony, select "True" in the "Are You Being Honored at the Ceremony" dropdown menu.
14. Choose how you will participate in the program ("Yes, giving a speech", "Yes, presenting an award", "Yes, other participation", or "No, attending only") in the "Will You Participate in the Program" dropdown menu.
15. Select if you consent to photos and videos ("Yes, I consent to photos and videos", or "No, please do not photograph me") in the "Photo and Video Consent" dropdown menu.
16. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
17. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
18. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Organization or Affiliation (2)**: If you are representing an organization, please enter its name here.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter a valid email address to receive event updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your contact phone number for event coordination.
* **Will You Attend the Ceremony (5)**: Select whether you will attend the ceremony or not.
* **Number of Guests (6)**: Choose how many additional people will be attending with you.
* **Names of Additional Guests (7)**: List the names of all attendees, including yourself.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies (8)**: Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
* **Please Specify Dietary Requirements (9)**: If you have special dietary needs, please provide more information in this field.
* **Do You Require Accessibility Accommodations (10)**: Select if you require any accessibility accommodations.
* **Please Specify Accommodation Needs (11)**: Describe your accessibility needs, if any.
* **How Will You Arrive at the Event (12)**: Choose how you will arrive at the event.
* **Will You Need Parking (13)**: Select if you require parking for your vehicle.
* **Are You Being Honored at the Ceremony (14)**: Select if you are an award recipient.
* **Will You Participate in the Program (15)**: Choose how you will participate in the ceremony's program.
* **Photo and Video Consent (16)**: Select if you consent to photos and videos being taken.
* **Emergency Contact Name (17)**: Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (18)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Additional Comments or Questions (19)**: Use this field to provide any additional information you want to share.

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
