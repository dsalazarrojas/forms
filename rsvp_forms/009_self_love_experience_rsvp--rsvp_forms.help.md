# Self Love Experience RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for RSVPing to a self-love experience event. Please provide the necessary information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2.  Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing to in the "Event Name" field.
3.  Choose the date of the event from the "Event Date" field.
4.  Choose the time of the event from the "Event Time" field.
5.  Enter the name of the person you are RSVPing for (if not yourself) in the "Attendee Name" field.
6.  If applicable, describe any special requests or needs in the "Special Requests" field.
7.  Select any dietary restrictions you have in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
8.  Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
9.  If you are an event host, coordinator, or organizer, please enter your name in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
*  **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
*  **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing to.
*  **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date of the event from the provided options.
*  **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the event from the provided options.
*  **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required: false): If you are RSVPing for someone else, enter their name here.
*  **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about the person you are RSVPing for.
*  **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): Enter any special requests or needs you have.
*  **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., Allergy, Vegan, Gluten-free, Vegetarian, None).
*  **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email.
*  **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
*  **Event Host** (`event_host`, text, required: false): If you are the event host, enter your name here.
*  **Event Coordinator** (`event_coordinator`, text, required: false): If you are the event coordinator, enter your name here.
*  **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required: false): If you are the event organizer, enter your name here.
