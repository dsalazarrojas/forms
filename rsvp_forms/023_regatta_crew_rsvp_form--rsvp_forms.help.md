# Regatta Crew RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about crew members who will be participating in a regatta event. The form is used to gather details such as name, contact information, crew position, boat type, and other relevant information that will help the regatta organizers prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill in your personal information**: Provide your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email**. These fields are required to ensure that we can contact you with important event updates and information.
2. **Specify your crew position**: Select the position you will be holding on the crew, e.g., captain, navigator, etc.
3. **Describe your boat**: If you have a specific type of boat, select it from the options provided. If not, choose "Other" and provide additional details in the "Other (specify)" field.
4. **Provide event details**: Enter the **Start Date**, **End Date**, and **Start Time** of the event.
5. **Crew size and composition**: Specify the number of crew members and select any additional "Other" boat types if applicable.
6. **Experience levels**: Rate your sailing experience, boat experience, and crew experience on a scale of 1-10.
7. **Sailing license**: Indicate whether you hold a valid sailing license or not.
8. **Crew rating**: Rate your overall crew rating, providing notes if necessary.
9. **Rating scale**: Choose the rating scale that best describes your crew's performance.
10. **Any additional comments**: If you have any additional information about your crew, feel free to provide it in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Your contact email address for event updates and information.
* **Crew Position**: The position you will be holding on the crew, e.g., captain, navigator, etc.
* **Boat Type**: Select the type of boat you will be using, or choose "Other" and provide additional details.
* **Regatta Name**: While not required, this field is for informational purposes only.
* **Start Date**: Date of the regatta event.
* **End Date**: Date of the regatta event.
* **Start Time**: Time of the regatta event.
* **End Time**: Time of the regatta event.
* **Crew Size**: Number of crew members participating.
* **Crew Size - Other**: If your crew size is not listed, select "Other" and provide additional details.
* **Boat Length**: Length of your boat, not required.
* **Boat Width**: Width of your boat, not required.
* **Sailing Experience**: Rate your sailing experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Crew Experience**: Rate your crew experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Sailing Boat Experience**: Rate your sailing boat experience on a scale of 1-10.
* **Other Boat Type**: Select any additional boat types not listed, or choose "Other" and provide additional details.
* **Sailing License**: Indicate whether you hold a valid sailing license.
* **Prophets Number**: Not required.
* **Crew Rating**: Rate your crew's overall performance on a scale of 1-10, providing notes if necessary.
* **Rating Scale**: Choose the rating scale that best describes your crew's performance.
* **Comments**: Any additional information about your crew.
