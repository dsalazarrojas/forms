# Senior Citizen Appreciation Lunch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Citizen Appreciation Lunch RSVP Form is a form designed to collect information from senior citizens attending a special lunch event. This form is meant to be filled out by senior citizens who want to attend the lunch event and provide basic contact information and RSVP preferences. The information collected will be used solely for the purpose of accommodating the attendee's needs and preferences at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Select your RSVP Date in the RSVP Date field.
4. Select your RSVP Time in the RSVP Time field.
5. Enter your Contact Phone number in the Contact Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your first name as you would like it to be used for the event.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please fill in your last name as you would like it to be used for the event.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required: false): Please select the date you plan on attending the lunch event.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, `time`, required: false): Please select the time you plan on attending the lunch event.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact phone number so we can reach out to you with any last-minute updates or questions.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure we can accommodate your needs and preferences at the event.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please reach out to us separately to let us know.
* You can select multiple days and times if you plan on attending the event at different dates or times.
