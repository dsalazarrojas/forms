# rsvp_for_homebuyer_discussion - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for potential homebuyers to express their interest in participating in a homebuyer discussion or meeting. Please fill out this form to help us gauge interest and prepare for the discussion or meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name.
2. Choose your RSVP status (Yes, rsvp or No, no rsvp) to express your interest in participating in the homebuyer discussion or meeting.
3. Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further details).
4. Provide your phone number (if you want to be contacted via phone call).
5. Add any additional notes or comments (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name (text)**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **rsvp** (select_multiple): Choose "Yes, rsvp" to express your interest in participating in the homebuyer discussion or meeting, and "No, no rsvp" to decline.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for further details or updates.
* **phone (text)**: Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone call.
* **note (note)**: Add any additional comments or information you think is relevant to the discussion or meeting.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate information to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* If you're unable to attend, please select "No, no rsvp" to help us gauge interest accurately.
* You can use the "note" field to provide any additional details or requests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for further assistance.
