# scrabble_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from participants for a Scramble event. It helps event organizers track attendance, participant details, and event preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** field with a brief description of the event.
2. Select the **Participants** you are registering:
	* Choose the **Name**, **Email**, and **Phone** for each participant.
3. Confirm your **Sign Up** status:
	* Select **True** if you will be attending the event.
4. Provide your **RSVP Date** and **RSVP Time** for the event.
5. Share your **Player Experience**:
	* Choose your level of experience: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Advanced**.
6. Provide details about the **Game Nights** and **Game Nights Frequency** you prefer.
7. Select the type of event you are interested in:
	* Choose between **Tournaments** or **Club Nights**.
8. Choose the **Tournament Type**:
	* Select **Online** or **Offline**.
9. Confirm the **Tournament Size**.
10. Choose the game you want to play:
	* Select between **Boggle**, **Scattergories**, or **Crosswords**.
11. Enter your **Check In** and **Check Out** times.
12. Provide any additional **Notes** about the event or participant preferences.
13. Finally, select your **RSVP Status**:
	* Choose **Pending**, **Confirmed**, or **Cancelled**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`): A short description of the event.
* **Participants** (`participants`): Choose the participant(s) you are registering.
	+ **Name** (`name`): The participant's full name.
	+ **Email** (`email`): The participant's email address.
	+ **Phone** (`phone`): The participant's phone number.
* **Sign Up** (`sign_up`): Confirm if you will be attending the event.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`): The date you plan to attend the event.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`): The time you plan to attend the event.
* **Player Experience** (`player_experience`): Choose your level of experience: **Beginner**, **Intermediate**, or **Advanced**.
* **Game Nights** (`game_nights`): Number of game nights you want to attend.
* **Game Nights Frequency** (`game_nights_frequency`): Frequency of game nights you prefer.
* **Tournament** (`tournament`): Choose between **Tournaments** or **Club Nights**.
* **Tournament Type** (`tournament_type`): Select **Online** or **Offline**.
* **Tournament Size** (`tournament_size`): Confirm the size of the tournament you want to attend.
* **Game** (`game`): Choose the game you want to play: **Boggle**, **Scattergories**, or **Crosswords**.
* **Check In** (`check_in`): Enter your check-in time.
* **Check Out** (`check_out`): Enter your check-out time.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional information about the event or participant preferences.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`): Confirm your RSVP status: **Pending**, **Confirmed**, or **Cancelled**.
* **Name** (`event_name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`player_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Experience** (`player_experience`): Describe your experience.
* **Status** (`rsvp_status_text`): Enter your RSVP status in text form.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`): Enter your check-in time.
* **Event Details** (`event_details_text`): Enter any additional event details.
