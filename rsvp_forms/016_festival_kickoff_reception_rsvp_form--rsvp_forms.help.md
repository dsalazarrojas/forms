# Festival Kickoff Reception RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Kickoff Reception RSVP Form is used to collect information from attendees about their presence at the reception. This helps organizers to prepare for the event and ensure that everyone knows what to expect.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
- Fill out the form by entering your Guest Name
- Enter your Contact Number, which will be your phone number or another contact method
- Enter your Email address, which will be used for follow up and communication
- Choose whether you will be attending the Festival Kickoff Reception (True, False, or Maybe)
- If you are attending, consider leaving a Message for the organizers with any details or requests

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name (Text)**: Enter your name as you would like to be known by the organizers and other attendees.
- **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number or another contact method where you can be reached.
- **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow up and communication.
- **RSVP (Select Multiple)**: Choose whether you will be attending the Festival Kickoff Reception. You can select True if you are attending, False if not, or Maybe if unsure.
- **Message (Text)**: If you are attending, consider leaving a Message for the organizers with any details or requests you may have.

## Tips
- Please enter accurate and honest information in each field.
- The form is designed to be completed by one person per submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask the organizers for clarification. 

Note: The tips section can be adjusted or removed if not necessary, but it's a good practice to include them to provide additional guidance to the users.
