# Annual Fund Drive Kickoff RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from guests who are RSVPing for the annual fund drive kickoff event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. For each section, please provide the requested information in the corresponding fields.
3. If you are unsure about any information, feel free to ask event organizers for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Your full name as it should be used for event communication.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Required
	+ Hint: Your full name
* **Email Address**: Your preferred contact email for event confirmation and updates.
 	* Type: Email
	+ Required
	+ Hint: For event confirmations
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for event-related inquiries.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Required
	+ Hint: Contact number
* **Organization Role**: Your position or title within your organization.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: Your role or title
* **Will You Attend**: Confirm your attendance to the event.
 	* Type: Select One
	+ Required
	+ Hint: Confirm your attendance
* **Number of Guests**: The total number of people in your party.
 	* Type: Number
	+ Required
	+ Hint: Total people in your party
* **Names of Additional Guests**: List the names of any additional guests attending with you.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: If bringing others, list their names
* **Kickoff Event Date**: The date of the event.
 	* Type: Date
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: When is the event
* **Event Time**: The start time of the event.
 	* Type: Time
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: What time does it start
* **Event Location**: The location of the event.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: Where the kickoff will be held
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: Any food restrictions
* **Vegetarian or Vegan Option Needed**: Indicate if you require a vegetarian or vegan option.
 	* Type: Select One
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: Do you need plant-based meals?
* **Interest in Fund Drive Involvement**: Indicate your level of interest in participating in the fund drive.
 	* Type: Select One
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: How involved do you want to be
* **Volunteer or Donor Interests**: Select the roles you are interested in participating in.
 	* Type: Select Multiple
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: What roles interest you
* **Fundraising Ideas or Suggestions**: Share any ideas or suggestions for the fund drive.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: Any ideas for the fund drive
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Indicate your preferred method of contact.
 	* Type: Select One
	+ Required
	+ Hint: How you prefer to be contacted
* **Additional Information or Questions**: Share any additional information or concerns you may have.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: Any special requests or concerns
* **Accessibility or Special Needs**: Indicate any accessibility needs.
 	* Type: Text
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: Any special needs
* **Thank You**: A note of appreciation for your participation.
 	* Type: Note
	+ Optional
	+ Hint: We appreciate your interest in supporting our mission
