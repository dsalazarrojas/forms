# Cocktail Event RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cocktail Event RSVP" form is designed to collect information from guests attending the event, including their contact details, preferences, and RSVP status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Section 1 - Guest Information**: This section is where you will provide your basic information, including your name and email address.
2. **Section 2 - RSVP Response**: Here, you will decide whether you will be attending the event and specify the number of guests who will be accompanying you.
3. **Section 3 - Dietary and Preferences**: If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please specify them here.
4. **Section 4 - Event Details**: Provide your estimated arrival time and indicate if you need transportation assistance.
5. **Section 5 - Networking Information**: If you would like to connect with other attendees, please provide your LinkedIn profile and specify your professional interests.
6. **Section 6 - Communication Preferences**: Choose how you would like to be contacted about the event and future events.
7. **Section 7 - RSVP Date**: Finally, select the date by which you will be able to respond to the invitation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (2): Please provide your full name.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the event.
* **Phone Number** (4): If you would like to be contacted via phone, provide your number. (Optional)
* **Organization or Company** (5): If you are representing a company, provide the name of your organization.
* **Will You Attend?** (7): Select "Yes, I will attend" to confirm your attendance.
* **Number of Guests Attending** (8): Specify the number of guests who will be joining you.
* **Names of Additional Guests** (9): List the names of any guests who will be accompanying you.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (11): If you have any dietary restrictions, specify them here.
* **Alcohol Preference** (12): Choose your preferred drink options.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations** (13): If you have any special requests, specify them here.
* **Estimated Arrival Time** (15): Provide your estimated arrival time.
* **Transportation Assistance Needed** (16): If you need help with transportation, choose the relevant option.
* **Professional Interests or Industry** (18): If you would like to connect with others in your industry, specify your interests.
* **LinkedIn Profile (Optional)** (19): If you would like to connect with other attendees, provide your LinkedIn profile.
* **May We Contact You with Event Updates?** (21): Choose how you would like to be contacted about the event.
* **Interested in Future Cocktail Events?** (22): Specify if you are interested in attending future events.
* **RSVP Date** (23): Select the date by which you will be able to respond to the invitation.
