# trade_delegation_welcome_party_rsvp - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals to RSVP (Respond to an Invitation) to the trade delegation welcome party. It will help us track who is attending, their contact information, and their guest details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your first name.
2. Provide your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Indicate if you will be attending the event (yes/no).
6. If you will be attending, enter the number of guests you will bring.
7. Provide details about your guests (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Will you be attending?** (`attending`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" if you will be attending the event, "No" otherwise.
* **Number of guests** (`guest_count`, `number`, required: false): If you will be attending, enter the number of guests you will bring.
* **Guest details** (`guest_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide details about your guests (e.g., name, age, any special needs). 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any) for a successful submission.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Please note that this form is for the trade delegation welcome party RSVP only.
