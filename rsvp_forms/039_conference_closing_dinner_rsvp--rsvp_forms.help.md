# Conference Closing Dinner RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect attendee information and preferences for the Conference Closing Dinner. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate information is submitted.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name as registered for the conference.
2. Enter your email address to receive confirmation of your RSVP.
3. Choose whether you will attend the closing dinner.
4. If you are bringing guests, enter the number of guests and select their meal preferences.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, list them in the text field.
6. If you have any seating requests or need special accommodations, provide them in the text field.
7. If you will need transportation, select "True" in the field.
8. If you have any special occasions you would like to note, enter them in the text field.
9. Finally, read the confirmation details and ensure you understand your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee name**: Please enter your full name as registered for the conference. This helps us confirm your attendance and ensure we have accurate records.
* **Email address**: Please enter your email address so we can send you a confirmation of your RSVP. This is also how we will communicate with you about any updates or changes.
* **Will you attend the closing dinner**: Choose "True" if you will attend the closing dinner, and "False" if you will not.
* **Number of guests**: If you are bringing guests, enter the number of guests you are bringing. This helps us plan accordingly.
* **Meal choices**: Select the meal you would like for each guest. You can choose from Chicken, Fish, Vegetarian, or Vegan options.
* **Dietary restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, list them here so we can take necessary precautions.
* **Seating requests**: If you have any seating requests or need special accommodations, provide them in this field.
* **Transportation needed**: If you will need transportation, select "True". We will arrange for shuttle or drop off as needed.
* **Special occasions to note**: If you have any special occasions or celebrations you would like to note, such as anniversaries or birthdays, enter them here.
* **Confirmation note**: This is where you will find your confirmation details, including your RSVP status and any other relevant information. Please read carefully before submitting your form.
