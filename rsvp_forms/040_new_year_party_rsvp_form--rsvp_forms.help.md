# new_year_party_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP (Respond to the invitation) for a New Year's party. Please fill in the required information accurately to ensure that the party organizers can plan accordingly and contact you about any updates or changes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and fill in the necessary information.
- Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
- Use the provided options for the "Attending Guests" field to indicate how many guests you will be bringing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Attending Guests** (`attending_guests`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you and at least one guest will be attending the party.
	+ Choose 'No' to indicate that you and all your guests will not be attending.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which you are responding to the invitation. If you're unsure, you can also leave this field blank and provide the date by other means.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number. This will help the party organizers contact you with any updates or changes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Use this field to provide any additional information about your attendance or special requests.
