# Event Seating Arrangement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Seating Arrangement Form is used to collect information about guests' seating preferences and requirements for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
* Fill in the form with all required information. This includes your name, table number, seat type, and any special requests.
* Select your preferred meal choice and chair type.
* Confirm your email address and phone number.
* If you have any special requests, please specify them in the "Special Request" field.
* Finally, confirm your special request in the "Confirm Special Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your name tag.
* **Table Number**: Select the number of the table you have been assigned to.
* **Seat Type**: Select your preferred seat type.
* **Seat Number**: Select the number of the seat you would like to sit in.
* **Row Number**: Select the row number of your seat.
* **Chair Type**: Select the type of chair you would like to sit in.
* **Chair Number**: Select the number of the chair you would like to sit in.
* **Meal Choice**: Select one or more of your preferred meals.
* **Meal Option**: Select one of the meal options.
* **Special Request**: If you have any special requests, please specify them here.
* **Confirm Special Request**: Confirm your special request.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Special Request**: Confirm your special request.
* **Special Arrangement**: Select one or more special arrangements.
* **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3**: Select one or more of the special arrangements.
* **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3**: Select one or more of the special arrangements.

Note: Fields that are not required are not mandatory and can be left blank. Please fill in all required fields to ensure accurate information collection.
