# Union Organizing Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Union Organizing Event Registration form is a tool for collecting information from union members and event organizers to help facilitate successful union events. This form gathers user details, event information, and RSVP responses to streamline the event planning process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your personal details in the **User Information** section.
*   Provide event details in the **Event Details** section.
*   Choose your RSVP preference in the **RSVP** section.
*   Add any additional information you'd like to share in the **Additional Info** section.
*   Click the **Submit** button to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information**: This section is where you can enter your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Event Details**: Please provide a brief description of the event, including the event name, date, time, location, and any other important details.
*   **RSVP**: Indicate whether you plan to attend the event by selecting "Yes, I'll attend" or "No, I won't attend."
*   **Additional Info**: This is an optional section where you can add any additional information about the event or your response.
*   **Submit**: Review and submit your completed form to finalize your registration.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields (although none are required in this form, completeness is still key).
*   Be as detailed as possible in your responses to help event organizers plan effectively.
*   If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to reach out to the form creators at form@gic.mx.
