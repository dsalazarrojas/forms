# Music Artist Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from artists who want to book their music for promotional events, festivals, or other performances. By completing this form, artists can provide their details, which will be reviewed and considered for booking opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your artist booking information in the "artist_booking_info" field.
2. Share your promotional information in the "promotional_info" field.
3. Add any other relevant details in the "other_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Booking Information (artist_booking_info)**:
	+ Label: artist_booking_info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: Please provide a brief introduction about yourself and your music.
* **Promotional Info (promotional_info)**:
	+ Label: promotional_info
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: Share any promotional materials, such as press releases, social media links, or website addresses, that showcase your music.
* **Other Details (other_details)**:
	+ Label: other_details
	+ Type: text
	+ Required: false
	+ Purpose: Add any other relevant details about yourself and your music that you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please be concise and clear in your responses.
* If you have any attachments or links to share, you can include them in the "other_details" field.
* We appreciate your promptness and look forward to reviewing your submission.
