# Public Service Award Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Service Award Night RSVP Form is a tool for attendees to provide their details and preferences for the event. It helps the organizers manage the event and ensure a smooth experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  The form is divided into multiple pages. Each page will have a specific set of questions, and you will need to complete all the questions on each page before proceeding to the next one.
2.  The form will ask you for the following details:
	*   RSVP Date: This is the date you plan to attend the event.
	*   RSVP Time: This is the time you plan to arrive at the event.
	*   Name: This is your name or the name of the group/organization you are representing.
	*   Contact Email: This is your email address or the contact email of your group/organization.
	*   Attending: Please indicate whether you will be attending the event or not. You can select 'True' if you will definitely be attending, 'False' if you will not be attending, or 'May be late' if you are unsure.
	*   Guest Count: This is the number of guests you plan to bring with you.
	*   Special Instructions: If you have any special requests or instructions, please provide them here.
	*   Comments: Any additional comments or remarks you would like to share with the event organizers.
	*   RSVP Type: Please select whether you are representing an individual, a group, or an organization.
	*   Table Number: Please note the table number assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, not required): Please select the date you plan to attend the event.
*   **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, not required): Please select the time you plan to arrive at the event.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name or the name of your group/organization.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address or the contact email of your group/organization.
*   **Attending** (`attending`, select_one, not required): Select whether you will be attending the event or not. You can choose 'True' if you will definitely be attending, 'False' if you will not be attending, or 'May be late' if you are unsure.
*   **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, not required): Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you.
*   **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, not required): Provide any special requests or instructions you have for the event organizers.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Share any additional comments or remarks you would like to make with the event organizers.
*   **RSVP Type** (`rsvp_type`, select_one, not required): Select whether you are representing an individual, a group, or an organization.
*   **Table Number** (`table_number`, number, not required): Note the table number assigned to you.
