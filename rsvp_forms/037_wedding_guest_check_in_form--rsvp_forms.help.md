# Wedding Guest Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and event-specific information from wedding guests to ensure accurate planning and organization for the wedding party.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first and last name for identification purposes.
2. Enter your email address if you'd like to stay updated on event details.
3. Provide your phone number for potential contact.
4. Write any personal message for the wedding party.
5. Choose your RSVP option (yes or no) to indicate your attendance.
6. Select the number of guests you're bringing along.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time in 12-hour format (e.g., 03:30 PM).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for updates and contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for potential contact.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Write any personal message or special request for the wedding party.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the four options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, select_multiple, optional): Select the number of guests you're bringing along from the options provided.
	+ 10 or less
	+ 11-20
	+ 21 or more
