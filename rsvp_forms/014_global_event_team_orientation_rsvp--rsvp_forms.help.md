# Global Event Team Orientation RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Global Event Team Orientation RSVP form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event you are attending. This field is not required.
2. **RSVP Role**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you are RSVPing as a staff member or attendee.
3. **Rsvp Roles Rsvp Role Options**: Select all the roles that apply to you. This field is not required.
4. **Attendee**: Enter the name of the attendee(s) you are RSVPing for. This field is not required.
5. **Rsvp Attendee**: Enter a number to indicate how many attendees you are RSVPing for. This field is not required.
6. **Session Preference**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference for each session. This field is not required.
7. **Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements or requests you may have. This field is not required.
8. **Rsvp Special Reqs 2**: Enter any additional special requirements or requests. This field is not required.
9. **Submit**: Click "Submit" to submit your RSVP.
10. **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and information about the event. This field is not required.
11. **Phone**: Enter your phone number to receive updates and information about the event. This field is not required.
