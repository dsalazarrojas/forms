# Zoom RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Zoom RSVP Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees for a Zoom event. This includes details such as their name, email, whether they will be attending, and specific event details like date, time, and location. The collected data helps organizers to manage the event effectively and ensure a smooth experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Enter your details**: Fill out the form with the required information. You can leave fields like `attendee_name` and `event_location` blank if they are not applicable.
2.  **Choose your attendance**: Select your status as an attendee for the event in the `attendees` field.
3.  **Set event details**: Enter the correct `event_date` and `event_time` of the event. This will help the organizers in planning the event accordingly.
4.  **Submit your response**: Once you have filled out all the required information, click the submit button to send your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **form-data**: This field is not used in this form.
*   **attendee-name**: Enter your name to help the organizers know who you are.
*   **attendee-email**: Enter your email address so that the organizers can contact you with event updates.
*   **attendees**: Select your status as an attendee:
    *   'True': You will be attending the event.
    *   'Maybe': You might attend the event.
    *   'False': You won't be attending the event.
*   **event-time**: Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
*   **event-date**: Enter the date of the event in the format (e.g., 2024-03-12).
*   **event-location**: Enter the location of the event if it's not already specified.
*   **form-submitted-by**: This field is not used in this form.

Note: The fields that have options like `attendees`, `event_date` and `event_time` will have a dropdown menu to select from the given options.
