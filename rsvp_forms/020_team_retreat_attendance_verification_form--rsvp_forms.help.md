# Team Retreat Attendance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from team members about their attendance and preferences for a team retreat. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure that all necessary arrangements can be made for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number.
3. Check your dietary preference (Yes or No).
4. Indicate whether you will attend the retreat (Yes or No).
5. Enter your expected arrival and departure times.
6. Provide any accommodation requests or notes.
7. Enter any comments or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name, as it will be used to identify you in the retreat schedule.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication about the retreat.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used in case of emergencies or last-minute updates.
* **Dietary Preference**: Check "Yes" if you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Will Attend**: Indicate whether you will attend the retreat (Yes or No). If you are unable to attend, please explain in the "Comments" field.
* **Arrival Time**: Enter your expected arrival time at the retreat location.
* **Departure Time**: Enter your expected departure time from the retreat location.
* **Accommodation**: If you require any special accommodation, please enter the details here (e.g., room type or accessibility needs).
* **Accommodation Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your accommodation requirements.
* **Participant Status**: Enter your current status regarding the retreat (e.g., attending, not attending, etc.).
* **Participant Status Notes**: Enter any additional comments or explanations for your participant status.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your attendance or preferences.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the retreat organizers.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a retreat organizer for clarification.
