# Responsive rsvp form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Responsive RSVP form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Select the correct page for your response based on the event details provided.
2. Fill out the required fields with your information, which are denoted by an asterisk (\*) in the form.
3. Choose your RSVP status for the event and select the relevant attendees.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field if necessary.
5. Enter your contact information if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, not required): Enter a short description of the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Description** (`description`, `note`, not required): Enter a description of the event.
* **RSVPed attendees** (`attendees`, `select_one`, not required): Choose your RSVP status for the event.
* **Attending** (`attending`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose which attendees you will be bringing with you.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields for a successful RSVP.
* Double-check your RSVP status and attending list before submitting the form.
* Use the "Note" field to add any additional comments or details about the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer directly.
* Review the event details carefully before filling out the form to ensure accuracy.
