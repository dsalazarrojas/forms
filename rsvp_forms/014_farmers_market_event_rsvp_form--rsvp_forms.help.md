# Farmers' Market Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmers' Market Event RSVP Form is a tool to help event organizers track attendance and gather insights on event attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, select the "Vendor Details" page and enter your name as a vendor if you will be selling at the market.
2. On the "Arrival Time" page, select your estimated arrival time for the market.
3. On the "RSVP" page, select "Yes" if you will be attending the event, and "No" if you will not.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Note" field.
5. On the "Email" page, enter your email address if you would like to receive any follow-up communications.
6. If you would like to provide an estimate of the number of visitors you expect, please enter that number in the "Number of Visitors" field.
7. Similarly, if you will be selling at the market, please enter the number of vendors you expect, in the "Number of Vendors" field.
8. Finally, enter any contact phone number on the "Phone" page.
9. If you have any further comments or updates, enter them on the "Note" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details**: Enter your name as a vendor if you will be selling at the market.
* **Arrival Time**: Select your estimated arrival time for the market.
* **RSVP**: Select "Yes" to indicate that you will attend the event, and "No" otherwise.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your attendance or plans.
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you would like to receive any follow-up communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Number of Visitors**: Enter an estimate of the number of visitors you expect.
* **Number of Vendors**: Enter an estimate of the number of vendors you expect.
