# Public Safety Workshop RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect RSVP information from individuals attending the Public Safety Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before You Begin
Before starting to fill out this form, ensure you're attending the workshop and are aware of the purpose of each section.

### General Instructions

- Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
- Make sure to review each field carefully and provide details according to your needs.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can ask an organizer or leave a comment for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (`first_name`, text, optional):** Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Last Name (`last_name`, text, optional):** Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents, such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Email (`email`, email, optional):** Enter your valid email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, optional):** Enter your primary phone number that you use for contact.
* **Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, optional):** If you're a new attendee, enter your date of birth to ensure we can identify you properly.
* **Workshop Type (`workshop_type`, select_one, optional):** Choose the type of workshop you're attending.
	+ 'Yes' indicates you're attending the workshop.
	+ 'No' indicates you're not attending the workshop.
* **Workshop Date (`workshop_date`, date, optional):** Enter the date of the workshop you're attending (if you're attending).
* **RSVP Status (`rsvp_status`, select_one, optional):** Indicate your RSVP status.
	+ 'Attending' indicates you are attending the workshop.
	+ 'Not Attending' indicates you are not attending the workshop.
* **Comments (`comments`, note, optional):** Enter any additional information or comments about your attendance or any special requests.
* **RSVP By (`rsvp_by`, time, optional):** Enter the time by which you're RSVPing.
* **Created By (`created_by`, select_one, optional):** Choose who created this form entry (only for internal tracking).
	+ 'Jimmy'
	+ 'John'
	+ 'Jane'
* **Updated By (`updated_by`, select_one, optional):** Indicate who last updated this form entry (only for internal tracking).
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Updated At (`updated_at`, select_one, optional):** Indicate whether the form entry was updated (only for internal tracking).
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure efficient tracking and preparation for the workshop.

Note: Some fields might seem redundant, but they're for internal tracking and logging purposes only. Please disregard them unless you're filling out the form as the designated tracker.
