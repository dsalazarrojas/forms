# retirement_party_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The retirement party RSVP form is used to gather information from guests about their attendance and details about their guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Add your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Indicate whether you will attend the party in the "Will Attend" field.
4. Select the type of RSVP in the "RSVP Type" field.
5. Choose the date and time of your RSVP in the "RSVP Date and Time" field.
6. Specify the number of guests attending and their names in the "Guests Attending" and "Guest Names" fields.
7. Add any comments from your guests in the "Guest Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your guest's name.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your phone number or email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Will Attend**: Select 'True' if you will be attending the party.
* **RSVP Type**: Select 'In Person' or 'Online' to indicate how you plan to attend.
* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date of your RSVP.
* **RSVP Time**: Enter the time of your RSVP.
* **RSVP Date and Time**: Select the date and time of your RSVP.
* **Guests Attending**: Enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **Guest Names**: Enter the names of your guests.
* **Guest Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about your guests.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can process your RSVP correctly.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please include them in the "Guest Comments" field.
* If you need to cancel your RSVP, please contact us as soon as possible to let us know.
