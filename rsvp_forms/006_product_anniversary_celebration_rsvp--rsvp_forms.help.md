# Product Anniversary Celebration RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Anniversary Celebration RSVP form is a short and simple form designed to collect information from attendees about their attendance status and guest count for the product anniversary celebration. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event that you are attending.
2. Type the name(s) of the attendee(s) who will be attending.
3. Choose whether you will be attending, not attending, or may be attending the event by selecting one of the options in the "RSVP" field.
4. If you plan to bring guests, enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
5. List the name(s) of the guest(s) who will be attending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (event_name, text): Enter the name of the event that you are attending. This field is not required.
- **Attendee Name** (attendee_name, text): Type the name of the attendee(s) who will be attending. This field is not required, but it's recommended to help the organizers know who will be attending.
- **RSVP** (rsvp, select_multiple): Choose whether you will be attending, not attending, or may be attending the event. This field is not required, but it helps the organizers plan the event.
- **Guest Count** (guest_count, number): If you plan to bring guests, enter the number of guests you will be bringing.
- **Guest Names** (guest_names, text): List the name(s) of the guest(s) who will be attending.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to provide accurate information.
- If you are not sure whether you will be attending, it's better to choose "Maybe" in the RSVP field.
- If you plan to bring guests, list their names in the "Guest Names" field.
