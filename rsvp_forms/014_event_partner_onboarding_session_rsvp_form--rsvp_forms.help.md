# Event Partner Onboarding Session RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from partners about their participation in the event onboarding session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Select your RSVP choice for the onboarding session.
3. If you need to provide additional information about your choice, you can do so in the "RSVP Choice Other" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
5. Enter the name of your company.
6. Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
7. Enter the date and time of the event.
8. Enter the venue of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Partner Onboarding Session RSVP Form** (`text`, required: false): This is the title of the form.
* **partner name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your name as a partner.
* **RSVP Choice** (`select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you plan to participate in the onboarding session, 'No' if you do not plan to participate, or 'Other' if you have a different response. Please see the 'RSVP Choice Other' field if you selected 'Other'.
* **RSVP Choice Other** (`text`, required: false): If you selected 'Other' for your RSVP, please provide more information about your choice.
* **partner email** (`email`, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **partner phone** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **partner company** (`text`, required: false): Please enter the name of your company.
* **event name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **event date** (`date`, required: false): Please enter the date of the event.
* **event time** (`time`, required: false): Please enter the time of the event.
* **event venue** (`text`, required: false): Please enter the venue of the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your RSVP is received.
- If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please contact the event organiser separately.
- If you are unable to participate, please let us know so we can adjust the event schedule accordingly.
- We appreciate your participation and look forward to seeing you at the event!
