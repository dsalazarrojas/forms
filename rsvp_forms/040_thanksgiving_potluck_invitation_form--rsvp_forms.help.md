# Thanksgiving Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about your presence and details at the Thanksgiving potluck event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information and submit it.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
3. If you need to RSVP for multiple guests, you can fill out the form multiple times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Status** (`RSVP Status`, required): Choose whether you will be attending the potluck by selecting "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, required): Enter the name of the person attending the potluck.
* **RSVP Email** (`RSVP Email`, required): Enter a valid email address of the person attending the potluck.
* **RSVP Phone** (`RSVP Phone`, optional): If you have a phone number that you would like to be reached at, enter it here.
* **RSVP Time** (`RSVP Time`, optional): If you have a specific time you plan to arrive at the potluck, enter it here.
* **RSVP Date** (`RSVP Date`, optional): If you have a specific date you plan to attend the potluck, enter it here.
* **Additional Details** (`Additional Details`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share with us.
* **RSVP Message** (`RSVP Message (2)`, optional): Write a message to us if you have any special requests or questions.
* **Guest Name 2** (`Guest Name 2`, optional): If you are attending with someone else, enter their name here.
* **RSVP Message 2** (`Rsvp Message 2 2`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions for your guest, enter them here.
