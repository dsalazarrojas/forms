# Neighborhood Open House RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you let us know if you plan to attend our neighborhood open house event. By filling out this form, you'll be able to let us know how many guests will be coming with you and what time you plan to arrive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Guest Phone" field, so we can contact you with any last-minute updates or changes.
3. Select the number of guests that will be attending with you.
4. Choose whether you will be attending the event in the "Will Attend" field.
5. If you plan to attend, select the date and time you plan to arrive.
6. If you have any additional comments or messages, you can type them in the "Message" field.
7. Finally, confirm your RSVP by selecting the "RSVP By" date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Please enter your name so we can address you by name.
* **Guest Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Number of Guests**: Please let us know how many guests will be coming with you.
* **Will Attend**: Choose "Yes" if you will be attending the event.
* **RSVP Time**: Select the date and time you plan to arrive.
* **RSVP Date**: Select the date you plan to RSVP.
* **Message**: Add any comments or questions you have about the event.
* **RSVP By**: Confirm the date by which you will RSVP.

## Tips
* Please note that this form does not ask for any payment or personal information.
* We will contact you with any updates or changes to the event.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please let us know in the "Message" field.
* You can RSVP at any time, but please try to do so by the date selected in the "RSVP By" field.
* If you're unable to attend, please let us know and we'll be happy to update the event for you.
