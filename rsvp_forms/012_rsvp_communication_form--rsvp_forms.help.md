# RSVP Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your response to an event. Please fill in the required fields to confirm your attendance and provide relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
Before submitting, please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately. If you have any issues or questions, feel free to reach out to the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, select_multiple, not required):
	+ Please select all the options that apply to your event experience. This will help us understand your interest and preferences.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, not required):
	+ Enter the name of the event you are responding to, if you have it.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, text, not required):
	+ List the number of people attending with you, if any.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, not required):
	+ Enter the date you plan to attend the event.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, not required):
	+ Enter the time you plan to arrive or leave the event.
* **RSVP Email** (`rsvp_email`, email, not required):
	+ Enter your email address to allow us to contact you regarding the event.
* **RSVP Phone** (`rsvp_phone`, text, not required):
	+ Enter your phone number for any urgent matters.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required):
	+ Add any additional comments or requests regarding the event.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, not required):
	+ Select your response status: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. This will help us understand your response.
* **RSVP Reason** (`rsvp_reason`, text, not required):
	+ Enter a brief explanation for your response.
