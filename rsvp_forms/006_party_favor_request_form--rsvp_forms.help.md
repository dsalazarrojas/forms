# Party Favor Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from party attendees about their preferences and requests for party favors.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Favor Request** (`Favor Request`, required): Type in your desired party favor here.
* **Message** (`Message`, optional): Type any additional comments or requests you have here (optional).
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Email** (`Email`, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Date** (`Date`, required): Select your preferred date for the party.
* **Time** (`Time`, required): Select your preferred time for the party.
* **Select Favor** (`Select Favor`, required): Select your preferred favor from the list.
* **Multi Select Favor** (`Multi Select Favor`, required): Select multiple favors you would like to receive from the list.
* **Note** (`Note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments here (optional).
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, required): Select the tool assigned to you from the list.
