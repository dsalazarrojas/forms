# travel_partner_appreciation_event_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect RSVP information for a travel partner appreciation event. It helps the event organizers plan and prepare for the guests' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your RSVP information.
2. Make sure to enter your name, email, phone number, and guest type correctly.
3. Provide the travel dates you plan to attend the event.
4. If you have any special instructions or requests, please include them in the "Special Instructions" field.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the event materials.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with event details and updates.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you with any last-minute updates or questions.
* **Guest Type** (Guest Type, select_multiple, required): Select the type of relationship you have with the travel partner, such as "Business Partner", "Client", or "Family Member".
* **Travel Dates** (Travel Dates, date, required): Enter the dates you plan to travel, including start and end dates.
* **Travel Location** (Travel Location, text, optional): Enter the location you will be traveling to, if different from your normal location.
* **Travel Details** (Travel Details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your travel plans, such as flight numbers or hotel reservations.
* **Travel Partner Name** (Travel Partner Name, text, required): Enter the name of the travel partner you are visiting.
* **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions, text, optional): Enter any special requests or instructions you have for the event organizers, such as dietary restrictions or mobility needs.
* **Travel Partner Appreciation Event Details** (Travel Partner Appreciation Event Details, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the event you would like to provide, such as a brief message for the travel partner.
