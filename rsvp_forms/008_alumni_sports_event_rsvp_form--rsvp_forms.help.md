# Alumni Sports Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Sports Event RSVP Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees to plan and execute the event successfully. This guide will walk you through each section and field of the form to help you complete it effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Select the Event Name**: Choose the sports event you plan to attend by selecting the corresponding option in the "Sports Event Name" field.
2. **Enter Event Date**: Provide the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
3. **Confirm Attendance**: Confirm whether you will attend the event by selecting one of the options in the "Will You Attend" field.
4. **Personal Information**: Enter your first and last name, email address, and phone number in their respective fields.
5. **Guest Information**: If attending with guests, list their names and specify their relationship to you in the "Guest Names" and "Relationship to Guests" fields.
6. **Additional Information**: If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.
7. **Event Participation**: If you are an alumnus planning to play in the game, specify your playing status, jersey size, and position preference.
8. **Merchandise and Volunteering**: Indicate your interest in event merchandise and volunteering opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: This section provides an overview of the sports event. Review this section to understand the different event options available.
* **Sports Event Name**: Select the event name that corresponds to the sports event you wish to attend. Examples include Alumni Basketball Game, Alumni Football Game, and so on.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Will You Attend**: Confirm whether you will attend the event by selecting one of the options: Yes - I will attend, No - Unable to attend, or Maybe - Will confirm later.
* **First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number**: Enter your personal details accurately.
* **Graduation Year**: If applicable, enter your graduation year.
* **Guest Information**: If you are attending with guests, list their names and specify their relationship to you.
* **Guest Names**: List the names of all guests attending with you.
* **Relationship to Guests**: Specify the relationship of each guest to you (e.g., spouse, child, friend).
* **Will You Play in the Game, Jersey Size, Position Preference**: If playing in the game, specify your playing status, jersey size, and position preference.
* **Bringing Guests**: Indicate whether you are attending with or without guests.
* **Interested in Volunteering**: Check if you are interested in volunteering for any event activities.
* **Parking Pass Needed**: Confirm whether you will need a parking pass.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accessibility needs, enter them in this field.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Select how you became aware of the event.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Use this field to provide any additional comments or questions you may have about the event.

## Tips

* Complete the form accurately and carefully to ensure that your RSVP is recorded correctly.
* Review each section to ensure that you've provided all necessary information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the event organizers.
* Please note that some fields may not be applicable to all attendees.
