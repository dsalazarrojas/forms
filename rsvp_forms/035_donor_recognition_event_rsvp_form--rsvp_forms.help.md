# Donor Recognition Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Donor Recognition Event RSVP Form. The form is used to collect information from attendees and guests for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the attendee.
2. Select the date of the event.
3. Choose the time of the event.
4. If you are attending with guests, fill in their name and count.
5. Check whether you will be attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. Optionally, you can add any additional notes about your attendance.
7. If you have an email address, fill it in.
8. If you have a phone number, fill it in.
9. If you are representing an organization, fill in the organization name.
10. If you have a job title, fill it in.
