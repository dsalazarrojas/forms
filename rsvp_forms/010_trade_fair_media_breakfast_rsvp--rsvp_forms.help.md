# Trade Fair Media Breakfast RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or teams to RSVP for the Trade Fair Media Breakfast. The purpose of this form is to gather information about attendees, their roles, and preferences for the event. This data will be used to better understand who is attending and to plan for their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Your Name (page 1, name field), Media Role (page 2, role field), and RSVP Date (page 2, rsvp_at field).
2. Select your role at the Trade Fair Media Breakfast from the options provided (page 2, role field).
3. Indicate if you are attending the breakfast (page 3, attending field).
4. Specify any dietary needs or preferences (page 5, dietary_needs field).
5. Enter any additional comments or details (page 6, additional_comments field).
6. Enter your contact information (page 11, contact_name field, contact_email field, and contact_phone field).
7. Enter any additional contact information (page 12-14, contact_company field, contact_job_title field, and contact_role field).
