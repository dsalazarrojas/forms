# Stranger Session Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Stranger Session Registration form is designed to gather information from users for a session with a stranger. This form is used to collect data on user preferences and availability for such sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your user details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2.  Choose a schedule for the session, including the date and time you prefer.
3.  Select whether you are available only in the morning or afternoon.
4.  Indicate if you have a flexible schedule.
5.  Confirm your registration by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Information** (`user_details`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant user details.
*   **Schedule** (`session_date`, date, required): Choose a date for your session.
*   **Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Select a time for your session.
*   **Session Start** (`session_start`, time, required): Confirm the session start time.
*   **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, text, required): Enter the expected duration of your session.
*   **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, required): Describe the topic you want to discuss during your session.
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant you are interacting with.
*   **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the participant you are interacting with.
*   **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant you are interacting with.
*   **Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the participant.
*   **Only in the morning** (`morning_only`, select_one, required): Check 'Yes' if you are available only during the morning.
*   **Only in the afternoon** (`afternoon_only`, select_one, required): Check 'Yes' if you are available only during the afternoon.
*   **Flexible schedule** (`flexible_schedule`, select_multiple, required): Check 'Yes' if you have a flexible schedule.
*   **Confirm** (`confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your registration by selecting 'Yes'.
