# Expo Media Briefing RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expo Media Briefing RSVP Form is a registration form designed for attendees to provide their information and preferences for the Expo Media Briefing event. This form is intended to collect necessary details from attendees, including contact information, team details, and special requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and required fields to ensure you understand what information is being asked.
2. Fill in your media team's information in the `pr_teams` field.
3. Enter the names of the event organizers in the `event_organizers` field.
4. Enter your media organization's name in the `media_name` field.
5. Select your preferred contact method in the `contact_type` field.
6. If applicable, provide any special requirements or requests in the `special_requirements` field.
7. Confirm your attendance and provide any additional information in the `confirmations` field.
8. Review and submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Media Briefing RSVP Form** (`form_media_briefing_rsvp_form`): This is the main form title.
- **PR Teams** (`pr_teams`): Enter the names of your media team members attending the event.
- **Event Organizers** (`event_organizers`): Enter the names of the event organizers.
- **Media Name** (`media_name`): Enter your media organization's name.
- **Contact Type** (`contact_type`): Select your preferred contact method: email, phone, or contact.
- **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`): If you have any special requests or requirements, enter them here.
- **Confirmations** (`confirmations`): Confirm your attendance and provide any additional information.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional details or comments you'd like to share.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, be sure to list them in the `special_requirements` field.
- If you'd like to provide additional information or comments, use the `additional_info` field.
- Review your completed form carefully before submitting to ensure it's accurate and complete.
