# nonprofit_appreciation_gala_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non-Profit Appreciation Gala RSVP Form is designed to collect RSVPs and additional information from guests for a charity event. This form helps hosts manage their event attendance and gather essential details about their guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Guest Name, Email, Phone, and Host Name, Email, and Phone in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the RSVP Method you plan to use (In person, Online, or Phone).
3. Enter the Event Name, Date, Time, and Location.
4. Indicate the number of Guests attending.
5. Select the Event Host's details: Name, Title, and Company.
6. Provide any Special Requests, such as dietary restrictions.
7. Choose any additional options for the event: Guests (Yes or No), Venue (Yes or No).
8. Add any Event Notes.
9. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like to be listed on the event invitation and in the event materials.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to RSVP for the event.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `note`, optional): If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions, please describe them here.
* **RSVP Method** (`rsvp_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you plan to RSVP (In person, Online, or Phone).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about the event.
* **Event Host** (`event_host`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event host.
* **Event Guests** (`event_guests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if guests are allowed (Yes or No).
* **Venue** (`event_venues`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate if the event will be held at a venue (Yes or No).
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional notes about the event.
* **Host Name** (`host_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the event host.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the event host.
* **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the event host.
* **Host Title** (`host_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the event host.
* **Host Company** (`host_company`, `text`, required): Enter the company name of the event host.
* **Host Message** (`host_message`, `note`, optional): Provide any message from the event host.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions (Gluten-free, Vegetarian, Vegan).
