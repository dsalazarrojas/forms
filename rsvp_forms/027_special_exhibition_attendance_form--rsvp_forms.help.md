# Special Exhibition Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Exhibition Attendance Form is designed to collect information from attendees about their interest in or attendance to a special exhibition. This form is used to gather details about the event and the attendee's experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2.  Enter your email address in the email field.
3.  Provide your phone number, if applicable.
4.  Choose a date for the event you are interested in or have attended.
5.  Select a time for the event you are interested in or have attended.
6.  Indicate if you are attending the event.
7.  Choose which event details you would like to provide.
8.  Enter any additional information about the event or your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
    Please note that this field is required.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
    Please note that this field is required.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
    Please note that this field is required.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to provide it.
*   **Visit Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the date of the event you are interested in or have attended.
*   **Visit Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of the event you are interested in or have attended.
*   **Attending** (`attending`, `select_one`, optional): Check if you are attending the event.
*   **Event Details** (`event_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose which event details you would like to provide.
*   **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information about your attendance.
*   **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
*   **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the exhibition.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Choose the date of the event.
*   **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time of the event.
*   **Event Date Range** (`event_date_range`, `date`, optional): Choose the date range of the event.
*   **Event Time Range** (`event_time_range`, `time`, optional): Select the time range of the event.

## Tips

*   If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the exhibition details provided.
*   Make sure to provide accurate and complete information about your attendance and event details.
*   If you are not attending the exhibition, please provide a clear and respectful reason for not attending.
