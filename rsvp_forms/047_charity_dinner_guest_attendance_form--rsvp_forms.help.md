# Charity Dinner Guest Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect RSVPs and record guest meal choices for a charity dinner event. Please use this form to confirm your attendance and provide details about your party's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with your details.
3. For each field, refer to the explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Dinner Guest Attendance --
This is a note field that serves as a header for the form.

### Guest Name 
* **guest name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the primary attendee. This will help us with seating and ticketing arrangements.

### Email 
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address to receive updates and confirmations about the event.

### Number of Guests 
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of guests attending, including yourself.

### Meal Options 
* **Meal Options** (`meal_options`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available meal options:
	+ Chicken
	+ Beef
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan

### Donation Interest 
* **Donation Interest** (`donation_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are interested in making a donation to the charity. You can choose to:
	+ Yes, donate
	+ No thank you

### Seating Preference 
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, `text`, optional): If you have any special seating requests or notes, enter them here.

### Accessibility Notes 
* **Accessibility Notes** (`accessibility_notes`, `text`, optional): Indicate any accessibility needs or requests you have.

### Guest Names List 
* **Guest Names List** (`guest_names_list`, `text`, optional): If there are additional guests in your party, list their names here.

### Confirmation Status 
* **Confirmation Status** (`confirmation_status`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your attendance by selecting one of the following options:
	+ Confirmed
	+ Not attending

## Tips
- Make sure to submit the form to ensure your place at the dinner and to help us with seating and catering arrangements.
- If you have any special requests or needs, use the "Seating Preference" and "Accessibility Notes" fields to let us know.
