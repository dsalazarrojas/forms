# Expo Networking Cocktail RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expo Networking Cocktail RSVP Form is a simple form to help event organizers gather RSVP information from attendees and their guests. It's a quick and easy way to provide details about your attendance and any guest information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct RSVP answer for each guest. If you are attending with guests, you'll need to add their information in the subsequent sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional)
Fill in your name and any other details you'd like to share.

### 2. **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, optional)
Select either True or False to indicate whether you can attend.

### 3. **name** (text, optional)
Enter your name if you wish to share it.

### 4. **email** (email, optional)
Enter your email for communication purposes if you'd like.

### 5. **phone** (text, optional)
If you'd like, add your phone number for event organizers to get in touch with you.

### 6. **attending_event** (select_one, optional)
Indicate if you're attending the event.

### 7. **attending_event_details** (text, optional)
If attending, add any additional details about your attendance.

### 8. **dietary_restrictions** (text, optional)
Share any dietary restrictions if you'd like.

### 9. **networking_interests** (select_multiple, optional)
Tick any interests you'd like to network about: Networking, Business, or Socializing.

### 10. **comments** (note, optional)
Add any comments or questions you'd like to share with event organizers.

### 11. **rsvp_date** (date, optional)
If you're able to RSVP for a specific date, enter it.

### 12. **rsvp_time** (time, optional)
If you have a preferred time of attendance, enter it.

### 13. **guest_count** (number, optional)
Enter the number of guests you're bringing.

### 14. **guest_name** (text, optional)
Enter names of your guests (if any).

### 15. **guest_email** (email, optional)
Enter email of your guests (if any).

### 16. **guest_phone** (text, optional)
Enter phone number of your guests (if any).

### 17. **guest_dietary_restrictions** (text, optional)
Enter any dietary restrictions for your guests.

### 18. **guest_attending_event** (select_one, optional)
Indicate if your guests are attending the event.

### 19. **guest_comments** (note, optional)
Add any comments or questions for your guests to event organizers.

### 20. **guest_rsvp** (select_one, optional)
Select True if your guests can attend.

### 21. **guest_rsvp_date** (date, optional)
If your guests can RSVP for a specific date, enter it.

### 22. **guest_rsvp_time** (time, optional)
If your guests have a preferred time of attendance, enter it.

### 23. **guest_rsvp_note** (text, optional)
Add any notes for your guests' RSVP.

### 24. **host_comments** (note, optional)
Leave any comments or questions for event organizers.
 
Note: This guide covers all fields. However, not all fields may be applicable for each user. Only fill in what's relevant and required for you and your guests.
