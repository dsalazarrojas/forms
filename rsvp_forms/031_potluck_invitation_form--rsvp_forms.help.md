# Potluck Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to invite individuals to a potluck event. Fill in your personal details and specify what you are bringing to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address to help us contact you.
3. If you are able to attend the event, select "Yes" under "What to bring" to specify what you are bringing.
4. Choose "Yes" or "No" under "RSVP" to indicate your attendance.
5. If you are unable to attend, please let us know your expected RSVP date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is for your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is for your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about the potluck event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is not used for this form.
* **What to bring** (`item`, select_multiple, optional): Choose what you are bringing to the potluck event. Select one or more of the options provided.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" if you are attending the potluck, or "No" if you are not.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, optional): If you are unable to attend, please let us know the expected date you will be unavailable.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, optional): If you are unable to attend, please let us know the expected time you will be unavailable.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is for any additional information or comments.
