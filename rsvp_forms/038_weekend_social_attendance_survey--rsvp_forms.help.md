# Weekend Social Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your plans for the weekend social event. Please fill out this form to let us know if you will be attending and what your plans are.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to open it.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, which is marked as "required" next to each field.
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if you don't have the information or don't want to provide it.
4. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekend Social Attendance Survey**: This is the main field for your response to the survey. Please type in your answer to the questions listed below.
* **Contact Information**: If you want to provide your contact information, please fill out this field. This includes your email and phone number.
* **Will you be attending?**: Please select "Yes" if you plan to attend the event, and "No" if you do not.
* **What time would you like to arrive?**: If you're attending, please select the time you plan to arrive.
* **Will you be bringing anything to share?**: Please select "Yes" if you plan to bring something to share, and "No" if you do not.
* **Date**: Please enter the date you would like to arrive. The date should be in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" (year-month-day).
* **Would you like to RSVP for a friend?**: Please select "Yes" if you want to RSVP for someone else, and "No" if you are not.
* **Email**: If you want to provide your email address, please enter it in this field.
* **Phone**: If you want to provide your phone number, please enter it in this format "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
* **Note**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or information you want to provide.
