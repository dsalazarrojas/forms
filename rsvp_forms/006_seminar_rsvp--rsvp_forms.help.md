## Seminar RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar RSVP form is designed for attendees to confirm their attendance and provide necessary contact information for a seminar. It helps the organizers keep track of attendees and ensures accurate headcount for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Seminar RSVP form, follow these steps:

1. **Choose whether you are attending the seminar**
	* Select 'Yes', 'No', or 'Maybe' to indicate your attendance status.
2. **Enter the number of attendees**
	* Type the total number of attendees you plan to bring.
3. **Provide contact information**
	* If you're providing an email address, enter it in the 'Email' field.
	* If you have a phone number, enter it in the 'Phone' field.
4. **Add any comments**
	* Enter any additional information or comments about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is attending seminar** (Yes/No/Maybe)
	* Select the option that best represents your attendance status.
* **Number attending**
	* Enter the number of people who will be attending with you.
* **Contact information**
	* A field for you to enter any additional comments or information about your contact details.
* **Email**
	* Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**
	* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments**
	* Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the seminar.
