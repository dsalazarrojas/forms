# Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP Form is a form used to collect attendance information from sponsors for the sponsor briefing breakfast event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Select your dietary preferences from the "Dietary Preferences" drop-down menu.
3. Choose if you will be attending the event from the "Attendance" drop-down menu.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.
7. Select the date and time of the event from the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. Enter the unique RSVP form ID in the "RSVP Form ID" field.
9. Choose the type of form from the "Form Type" drop-down menu.
10. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP Form** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_title`): This is the title of the form.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_dietary_preferences`): Select your dietary preferences from the drop-down menu.
  • Choose one of the following options: Vegetarian, Vegan, Special Diet, or Other.
* **Attendance** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_attendance`): Choose if you will be attending the event.
  • Choose one of the following options: True or False.
* **Name** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_comments`): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **Date** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_date`): Select the date of the event.
* **Time** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_time`): Select the time of the event.
* **RSVP Form ID** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_rsvp_form_id`): Enter the unique RSVP form ID.
* **Form Type** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_form_type`): Choose the type of form.
  • Choose one of the following options: Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP, Other.
* **Additional Comments** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_additional_comments`): If you have any additional comments, enter them here.
* **Form Name** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_form_name`): Enter the name of the form.
* **Form Version** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_form_version`): Enter the version of the form.
* **Notes** (`sponsor_briefing_breakfast_rsvp_form_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the form.
