# Art Exhibition Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for individuals to RSVP (Reception of a Service/Visit, Please) for an art exhibition. It collects information about the attendees, such as their contact details, event attendance details, and special requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your guest name.
2. Enter your email address for confirmation and to receive tickets.
3. Enter your phone number (optional).
4. Select the date of the exhibition.
5. Indicate the total number of attendees in your party.
6. If applicable, describe any accessibility or special needs for the event.
7. Choose your preferred ticket type (General, VIP, or Donor).
8. If you have dietary restrictions, please specify them for the reception catering.
9. Grant consent for photography (I consent or I do not consent).
10. Add any additional comments for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter a valid email address to receive confirmation and tickets. This will be used for internal communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the exhibition.
* **Number of guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Indicate the total number of people attending with you.
* **Accessibility or special needs** (`access_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific needs or requirements for attending the event, please describe them here.
* **Ticket type** (`ticket_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred ticket type: General, VIP, or Donor.
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them here for the reception catering.
* **Consent for photography** (`hearing_consent`, `select_one`, required): Select if you grant consent for photography or not.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): You may add any additional comments or information for the organizers.
