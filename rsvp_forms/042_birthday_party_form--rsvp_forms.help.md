# Birthday Party Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Birthday Party Form is designed to gather information about you and your guests for the birthday party. Please take a few minutes to review the form carefully to ensure that your responses are accurate and comprehensive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the party details section to ensure you have the correct information.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select your response to "Will You Attend" to indicate your attendance.
4. Specify the number of people and children attending, if any.
5. List the names of all attendees.
6. If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please specify them.
7. Answer the "Can You Bring a Plus One" question to indicate if you are bringing a guest.
8. Provide a special message or note for the birthday person, if desired.
9. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate by checking the "I Confirm" box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Birthday Celebrant Name** (`birthday_celebrant`, `text`, required): Please fill in the name of the person having a birthday.
* **Party Date** (`party_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the party.
* **Party Time** (`party_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the party.
* **Party Location** (`party_location`, `text`, required): Fill in the location of the party.
* **Your Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, `text`, required): Fill in your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Will You Attend** (`rsvp_response`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes, I Will Attend" if you plan to attend the party.
* **Number of People in Your Party** (`number_attending`, `number`, required): Specify the total number of people attending.
* **Number of Children** (`children_attending`, `number`, optional): If you're bringing children, specify their number.
* **Names of People Attending** (`names_of_attendees`, `text`, optional): List the names of all attendees.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any dietary needs or allergies, such as Vegetarian, Vegan, or Gluten-Free.
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`, `text`, optional): Describe specific requirements if you have any.
* **Special Requests or Preferences** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): Specify any other requests or preferences.
* **Allergies to Inform Party Host** (`allergies_warning`, `text`, optional): Inform the party host about any allergies or safety concerns.
* **Gift Registry Information** (`gift_registry`, `text`, optional): Provide information about where gifts can be purchased.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your shirt size for party favors, if applicable.
* **Need Transportation** (`transportation_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you need help with transportation.
* **Can You Bring a Plus One** (`plus_one_allowed`, `select_one`, required): Answer "Yes, With Plus One" if you're bringing a guest.
* **Special Message or Note for Birthday Person** (`special_occasion_note`, `text`, optional): Write a special message or note for the birthday person.
* **I Confirm** (`confirmation_message`, `select_one`, required): Check "I Confirm" to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **May We Contact You to Confirm** (`contact_permission`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes, Contact Me" to allow the party host to confirm your attendance.
