# Employee Appreciation Luncheon Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect RSVP information for the Employee Appreciation Luncheon. It is intended for employees to register their attendance and provide details about their guests, if applicable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any necessary details in the required fields.
2. Select whether you will be attending the luncheon.
3. If you will be attending, provide your RSVP date and time.
4. If you are bringing a guest, enter their name, relation, and dietary needs.
5. Enter any special instructions for your guest.
6. Provide your email and phone number for contact purposes.
7. If you have additional information or special requests, enter them in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the guest attending the luncheon, if any.
* **Attending** (`attending`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you will be attending the luncheon ("Yes") or not ("No").
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, `date`, optional): Enter the date and time you plan to RSVP, if applicable.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of guests you are bringing, if any.
* **Dietary Needs** (`dietary_needs`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the dietary needs of your guest(s), such as vegetarian or special dietary requirements.
* **Prefer** (`prefer_diet`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your personal dietary preferences, if different from your guest's.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, optional): Enter any special instructions or requests for your guest.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `select_multiple`, optional): Enter any additional information or special requests, if necessary.
* **Guest Attendee Name** (`guest_attendee_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the guest's guest, if applicable.
* **Guest Attendee Relation** (`guest_attee_relation`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the relation of the guest's guest, such as spouse or child.
* **Is Plus One** (`is_plus_one`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether the guest is a plus one or not.
* **Guest Attendee Diet** (`guest_attendee_diet`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the dietary needs of the guest's guest.
* **Guest Attendee Special Instructions** (`guest_attendee_special_instruct`, `note`, optional): Enter any special instructions or requests for the guest's guest.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your host email address for contact purposes.
* **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your host phone number for contact purposes.
* **Host Name** (`host_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your host name for reference.
* **Host Title** (`host_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your host title for reference.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as "required".
