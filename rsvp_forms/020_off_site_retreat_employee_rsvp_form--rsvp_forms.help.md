# off_site_retreat_employee_rsvp_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record off-site retreat employee RSVPs, allowing organizers to manage event attendance and communicate with attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Employee ID in the first field.
2. Enter your name in the second field.
3. Enter your email address in the third field.
4. Select your RSVP status from the options provided in the fourth field.
5. If you have any comments, you can add them in the fifth field.
6. Enter the date you plan to attend in the sixth field.
7. Enter the time you plan to attend in the seventh field.
8. Choose the type(s) of attendance you will have in the eighth field.
9. If you have any additional message, you can add it in the ninth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique Employee ID.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, not required): Select one of the options provided to indicate your attendance status.
*   *Option 1*: Indicate you are attending.
*   *Option 2*: Indicate you are not attending.
*   *Option 3*: Indicate you are still unsure.
* **RSVP Comment** (`rsvp_comment`, note, not required): Add any comments or notes about your attendance status if needed.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to attend the off-site retreat.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to attend the off-site retreat.
* **Attendance Type** (`rsvp_attendance_type`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the type(s) of attendance you will have. 
*   *Option 1*: Indicate you will attend in person.
*   *Option 2*: Indicate you will attend remotely.
*   *Option 3*: Indicate you will attend in-person but with remote participation.
* **RSVP Message** (`rsvp_message`, note, not required): Add any additional message if needed.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your information is accurately recorded.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
* Make sure to submit this form only once for each employee.
