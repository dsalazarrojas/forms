# Patient Health Questionnaire PHQ9 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Health Questionnaire PHQ9 (PHQ9) is a standardized tool used to assess the overall health status of patients. It is a comprehensive questionnaire that evaluates the patient's mental health, physical health, and social functioning. By filling out this form, patients can provide their healthcare providers with accurate information about their health status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question based on your current health status.
3. Enter your contact information, including your patient name, age, email (if applicable), phone number (if applicable), and medical provider information.
4. If you have any medical conditions, medications, or allergies, provide that information.
5. Provide the medical record number, date of last visit, time of last visit, and next appointment details (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required): Your full name as it appears on your medical records.
* **Age** (Age, text, required): Your age in years.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date Of Birth, date, required): Your date of birth.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, optional): Any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Medication** (Medication, text, optional): Any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (Allergies, text, optional): Any allergies you have.
* **Medical Record Number** (Medical Record Number, text, optional): Your medical record number, if applicable.
* **Date Of Last Visit** (Date Of Last Visit, date, optional): The date of your most recent visit to your healthcare provider.
* **Time Of Last Visit** (Time Of Last Visit, time, optional): The time of your most recent visit to your healthcare provider.
* **Next Appointment** (Next Appointment, date, optional): The date and time of your next scheduled appointment with your healthcare provider.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Your email address, if you have one.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Your phone number, if you have one.
* **Medical Provider** (Medical Provider, text, optional): Your primary care physician's name and contact information.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Any additional comments or information you'd like to share with your healthcare provider.
* **Questions** (Questions, select_multiple, required): Select the questions that best describe your current health status:
  + 1. Question 1
  + 2. Question 2
  + 3. Question 3
  + 4. Question 4
  + 5. Question 5
  + 6. Question 6
  + 7. Question 7
  + 8. Question 8
  + 9. Question 9

Please note that the questions are multiple choice, and you should select the answer that best describes your current health status. If you're unsure about any question, please select "Not applicable" or "Don't know."
