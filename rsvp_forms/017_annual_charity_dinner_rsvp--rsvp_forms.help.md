# Annual Charity Dinner RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Charity Dinner RSVP form is designed to collect information from attendees to ensure that we can provide the best experience for all guests and make necessary arrangements for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully and ensure you are attending the event
* Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number
* Select your preferred meal option and beverage choice
* Choose whether you are sponsoring a table or not
* Provide your expected number of guests attending with you
* List any guest names if you are attending with a party
* Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you have
* Specify any special dietary details or requests
* Confirm your donation pledge and amount if applicable
* Choose whether you will participate in the auction or not
* Select whether you are interested in volunteering at the event
* Provide any table preferences or special requests
* Check if you require any accessibility accommodations
* Confirm transportation and parking needs
* Give consent for photos to be taken for marketing purposes
* Indicate how you heard about the event
* Provide any special requests or needs
* Enter emergency contact name and phone number if applicable

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it will appear in our records.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and event updates.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your best contact number for communication.
* **Organization or Company** (text, optional): If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Will You Attend?** (select_one, required): Choose whether you will attend the event or not.
* **Table Sponsorship** (select_one, required): Select whether you are sponsoring a full table, a partial table, or not.
* **Number of Guests** (number, required): Enter the total number of guests attending with you.
* **Guest Names** (text, optional): List all the names of attendees in your party.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (select_multiple, required): Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Dietary Details** (text, optional): Specify any special dietary needs or requests.
* **Meal Selection** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred meal option.
* **Beverage Preference** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred beverage choice.
* **Donation Pledge** (select_one, optional): Choose whether you will make a donation and how much.
* **Auction Interest** (select_one, optional): Choose whether you will participate in the auction or not.
* **Volunteer Interest** (select_one, optional): Select whether you are interested in volunteering at the event.
* **Table Preferences** (text, optional): Enter any special table preferences or requests.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (text, optional): Enter any accessibility needs or requests.
* **Transportation Needed** (select_one, optional): Choose whether you need help with transportation.
* **Parking Needed** (select_one, optional): Select whether you need parking at the venue.
* **Photo Consent** (select_one, required): Choose whether you give consent for photos to be taken for marketing purposes.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event?** (select_multiple, optional): Select how you heard about the event.
* **Special Requests** (text, optional): Enter any other special requests or needs.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
