# Wedding Welcome Sign Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests attending the wedding. It's intended for the wedding party or staff to use in welcoming guests and gathering basic contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form has multiple sections with clear labels and instructions. Take your time to fill it out carefully, and don't hesitate to ask for help if needed.
2. Enter your Guest First Name, Guest Last Name, and any other required details.
3. If you're attending, select 'Attending' from the 'RSVP' field. If not, select 'Not Attending'.
4. You can also enter the number of guests attending with you, if applicable.
5. If you'd like to add any additional comments or notes, use the 'Note' section.
6. Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Guest Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **RSVP** (select_one, required): Select 'Attending' if you're planning to join the wedding, or 'Not Attending' if you're unable to attend.
* **Guests** (number, optional): If you're attending with guests, enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Please enter your date of birth if required (this field is usually required for official purposes).
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted before or after the wedding.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted before or after the wedding.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance or anything else you'd like to share with the wedding party.

## Tips

* If you're unsure about any field, don't worry! Just ask someone nearby for help.
* Take your time and be accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
