# Speaker Meet And Greet RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Meet And Greet RSVP Form is designed to collect information from guests attending an event. Please use this form to let us know if you can attend and share your contact information so we can reach you for further details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you can attend the event by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "RSVP" field.
2. Enter your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
3. Provide your guest's email address in the "Guest Email" field.
4. (Optional) Enter your guest's phone number in the "Guest Phone" field.
5. If you or your guest has any special accessibility needs, please select them from the "Accessibility Needs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RSVP (Yes/No)**: Choose whether you or your guest can attend the event.
* **Guest Name**: Enter the name of the guest who will be attending the event.
* **Guest Email**: Enter the email address of the guest who will be attending the event.
* **Guest Phone**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of the guest who will be attending the event.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you or your guest has any special accessibility needs, such as wheelchair accessibility or sign language interpretation, please select them from this field.

## Tips
* Please note that providing your phone number is optional, but it will help us to contact you more efficiently.
* If you have any specific accessibility needs, please be sure to let us know so we can make the necessary arrangements.
