# Wedding Menu Selection Voting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the preferences of wedding guests for the menu selection. You will be asked to choose from various menu options for the main course and dessert, and provide information about your guests' attendance status and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the menu options you prefer for each course by choosing from the options provided for each section.
2. Enter the number of guests you are bringing with you.
3. Provide the contact information of each guest, including their name, email address, and phone number.
4. Confirm your RSVP status (Confirmed or Not Attending) for each guest.
5. Enter any additional comments or information about your guests.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and complete information for each guest to ensure that we can plan for their menu and attendance.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the wedding party to clarify.
