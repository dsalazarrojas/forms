# Winter Social RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to facilitate gathering information for the Winter Social event. It collects attendee details, such as their name, contact information, and attendance status, to help the event organizers prepare for the event. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your information is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **gathering name** (`gathering_name`) - a brief description of the gathering or event.
2. Select your **attendance status** (`attendance_status`) - choose whether you will be actively attending or not attending the gathering.
3. If you are attending, specify **product brought** (`product_brought`) - mention any products you plan to bring to the gathering.
4. Fill in your **date** (`date`) - the date of the event.
5. Enter the **time** (`time`) - the time of the event.
6. Fill in your **RSVP date** (`rsvp_date`) - the date by which you must RSVP.
7. Enter the **RSVP time** (`rsvp_time`) - the time by which you must RSVP.
8. Add any additional **notes** (`note`) - if you have any comments or special requests.
9. Fill in your **email** (`email`) - your contact email address.
10. Enter your **phone** (`phone`) - your contact phone number.
11. Enter your **name** (`name`) - your full name.
12. If assigned, fill in your **assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`) - any tool assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **gathering_name** (`gathering_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in a brief description of the gathering or event.
* **attendance_status** (`attendance_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select whether you will be actively attending or not attending the gathering.
	+ Select "Active" if you will be attending.
	+ Select "Inactive" if you will not be attending.
* **product_brought** (`product_brought`, `text`, required: false): Mention any products you plan to bring to the gathering.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of the event.
* **rsvp_date** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date by which you must RSVP.
* **rsvp_time** (`rsvp_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time by which you must RSVP.
* **note** (`note`, `note`, required: false): Add any comments or special requests.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): If assigned, fill in the tool assigned to you.
