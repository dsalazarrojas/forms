# Party RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect guest information and RSVP responses for a party. It is intended for guests to provide their name, email address, and RSVP response for the event. This form should only be completed by the individual guest themselves, and not by others on their behalf.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name in the "Name" field. Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or as we know you.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us keep you up-to-date on party details and RSVP notifications.
3. Select the correct RSVP response in the "RSVP Response" field. This field allows you to choose between "yes", "no", and "maybe" for the party.
4. (Optional) If you are bringing a guest, enter the number of guests you are attending with in the "Guest" field.
5. Finally, select the date you will be attending the party in the "Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or as we know you.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on party details and RSVP notifications.
* **Guest** (number, optional): If you are bringing guests, enter the number of guests you will be attending with.
* **RSVP Response** (select_multiple, required if filled): Select your response for attending the party: Yes, No, or Maybe.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select the date you will be attending the party in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out this form accurately and truthfully, as it is used for party planning and guest management.
* If you are unsure about your RSVP response, please contact the party host for clarification.
* If you are bringing guests, please make sure to enter the correct number of guests.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please contact the party host separately.
