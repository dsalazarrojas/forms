# Corporate Merger Announcement RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track attendee information and responses to the corporate merger announcement. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your information is correctly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by selecting one of the options in the `Meeting Attendance` field, indicating whether you will attend the meeting or not.
*   If you are attending, enter your name, title, and company in the respective fields.
*   Enter the meeting date and time in the `Meeting Date` and `Meeting Time` fields.
*   Provide the meeting location in the `Meeting Location` field.
*   If you have any questions or comments, use the text fields to enter your thoughts.
*   Finally, select the options for any of the additional questions provided, such as whether you would like to receive more information about the announcement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Meeting Attendance** (`meeting_attendance`): Select the correct attendance status for yourself. Options include `True` (Attending), `False` (Not Attending), and `Not Applicable` (If you are not able to attend, but would like to be informed about the announcement).
*   **Guest Name** (`guest_name`): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
*   **Guest Title** (`guest_title`): Enter your title or position (e.g., CEO, Manager, etc.).
*   **Guest Company** (`guest_company`): Enter the name of your company, if relevant.
*   **Guest Job Title** (`guest_job_title`): Enter your job title within your company.
*   **Guest Function** (`guest_function`): Enter the specific function or department you represent.
*   **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`): Enter the date of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`): Enter the time of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`): Enter the location of the meeting.
*   **Do you have any questions?** (`question1`): Use this field to ask any questions you may have about the announcement.
*   **Do you have any further questions?** (`question2`): Use this field to ask additional questions you may have.
*   **Is there a specific reason for your non-attendance?** (`question3`): If you are unable to attend, provide a brief explanation.
*   **Would you like to receive more information about this announcement?** (`question4`): Select whether you would like to receive more information about the announcement.
*   **Is there anything else you would like to add?** (`question5`): Use this field to include any additional thoughts or comments.
 

## Tips

*   Make sure to complete the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your information is correctly recorded.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the meeting hosts.
*   If you are unable to attend the meeting, please provide a brief explanation for your non-attendance.
