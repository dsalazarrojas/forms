# School Fundraising Auction RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to a school fundraising auction, allowing you to provide information about yourself and your plans to attend the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
*   Enter your name in the "name" field.
*   Provide your email address in the "email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
*   Select a date for the auction in the "rsvp_date" field.
*   Indicate the number of people attending in the "number_attending" field.
*   Choose the time you plan to arrive at the auction in the "rsvp_time" field.
*   Enter any comments or special requests in the "rsvp_comments" field.
*   Choose your preference for a specific activity or service offered at the auction in the "rsvp_select_one" field.
*   Select all the activities or services you are interested in from the options listed in the "rsvp_select_multiple" field.
*   Leave any additional comments about the auction in the "rsvp_comments" field.
*   Indicate whether you will be attending the auction by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "rsvp_submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **rsvp_details (Text)** (`form_1`, `text`, optional): This field is intended for any additional details about your RSVP.
*   **name (Text)** (`form_2`, `text`, required): Please enter your name in this field so we can address you personally.
*   **email (Email)** (`form_3`, `text`, required): This field is for your email address, which will help us contact you with important event information.
*   **phone (Text)** (`form_4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
*   **rsvp_date (Date)** (`form_5`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to attend the auction.
*   **number_attending (Number)** (`form_6`, `number`, required): Indicate the number of people in your party attending the auction.
*   **rsvp_time (Time)** (`form_7`, `time`, required): Choose the time you plan to arrive at the auction.
*   **rsvp_comments (Note)** (`form_8`, `note`, required): Enter any special comments or requests you have for the auction.
*   **rsvp_select_one (Select One)** (`form_9`, `select_one`, optional): This field allows you to select one activity or service you are interested in at the auction.
	+ Option 1: Select this option if it is the one you are interested in.
	+ Other: Select this option if you have a different activity or service in mind.
*   **rsvp_select_multiple (Select Multiple)** (`form_10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all the activities or services you are interested in.
	+ Option 1: Select this option if it is the one you are interested in.
	+ Option 2: Select this option if it is the one you are interested in.
*   **rsvp_comments (Note)** (`form_11`, `note`, required): This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
*   **rsvp_submit (Select One)** (`form_12`, `select_one`, required): Please indicate whether you will be attending the auction by selecting "Yes" or "No".
