# Fashion Buyer Preview RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Buyer Preview RSVP Form is used to collect information from Fashion Buyers who want to attend a specific event or meeting. This form will help us understand who will be attending, their contact details, and their availability.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Select "RSVP Yes" or "RSVP No" in the "RSVP" field to indicate your availability.
6. Enter the date you are available to attend in the "Date" field.
7. Enter the time you are available to attend in the "Time" field.
8. If you have a preferred date to attend, enter it in the "Preferred Date" field.
9. If you have a preferred time to attend, enter it in the "Preferred Time" field.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (full_name): Enter your full name as you would like to be listed on our attendee list.
* **Email** (email): Enter your professional email address.
* **Company** (company): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (job_title): Enter your job title or position in your company.
* **RSVP** (rsvp): Select "RSVP Yes" to indicate you are available to attend, or "RSVP No" if you are not.
* **Date** (date): Enter the date you are available to attend.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time you are available to attend.
* **Preferred Date** (preferred_date): If you have a preferred date to attend, enter it here.
* **Preferred Time** (preferred_time): If you have a preferred time to attend, enter it here.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can contact you correctly.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please include them in the "Notes" field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask us for clarification.
