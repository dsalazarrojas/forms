# Military Appreciation Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from guests for a Military Appreciation Event. It helps event organizers to collect RSVPs, plan the event, and ensure that attendees have all necessary details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information for each field.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
3. Review your submission before submitting it to ensure that it is complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter the full name of the guest.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Military ID** (`military_id`, text, required): Enter the guest's military ID number.
* **Branch** (`branch`, select_one, optional): Select the branch of the military the guest belongs to.
* **Will you be attending?** (`attending`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you will be attending the event, or if you are unsure.
* **Planning Preferences** (`planning_preferences`, text, optional): Enter any special preferences for the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the guest.
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **RSVP Deadline** (`rsvp_deadline`, date, required): Enter the deadline for RSVPs.
* **Venue** (`venue`, select_one, optional): Select the location of the event (online or a physical location).
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the guest.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all required fields are filled accurately and truthfully.
* Enter the date of the event and RSVP deadline in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* Select the correct branch and venue for the guest if applicable.
