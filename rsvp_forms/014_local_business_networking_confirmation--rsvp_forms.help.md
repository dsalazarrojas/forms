# Local Business Networking Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm your attendance and participation in local business networking events. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to enter your correct information in all fields marked as required.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the event organizer for clarification.
- Once you have completed the form, submit it to confirm your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, required): Please enter your email address.
- **Phone** (text, required): Please enter your phone number.
- **Attending** (select_one, required): Select 'True' if you are attending the event, 'False' otherwise.
- **Additional Details** (note, optional): Provide any additional information about your participation or any special requests.
- **Business Name** (text, required): Please enter your business name or the name of your company.
- **Job Title** (text, optional): Please enter your job title or position.
- **Company** (text, required): Please enter the name of your company.
- **Industry** (text, required): Please enter your industry or sector.
- **Location** (text, required): Please enter the location of your business.
- **Date** (date, required): Please enter the date of the event.
- **Time** (time, required): Please enter the time of the event.
- **RSVP Form Submitted At** (date, optional): This field will be automatically populated.
- **User IP** (text, optional): This field is not used for general public use. Please ignore.
- **User Agent** (text, optional): This field is not used for general public use. Please ignore.
- **User Referrer** (text, optional): This field is not used for general public use. Please ignore.

## Tips
- Be accurate and complete in all fields, especially the required ones.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask the event organizer for clarification.
- This form is intended for event management purposes only, and your information will be used for that purpose only.
