# Press Collaboration Meetup RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to a Press Collaboration Meetup and provide information about your attendance and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Event Details section with the relevant information about the event.
2. In the Attendee Details section, provide your name and any other details you wish to share.
3. If you have any accessibility needs, specify them in the Accessibility Needs section.
4. Choose your preferred RSVP option in the RSVP section.
5. In the Discussion Topics section, share any topics you'd like to discuss.
6. Provide your email address in the Email section.
7. Enter your phone number in the Phone section.
8. Optionally, provide additional comments or discussion topics in the Additional Comments section.
9. If you have any additional accessibility needs, specify them in the Accessibility Needs 2 section.
10. Finally, choose your preferred RSVP option in the RSVP 2 section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter the event details, such as date, time, and location.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`): Provide your name and any other details you wish to share.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`): Specify what you need help with for this event.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`): Choose your preferred RSVP option.
* **Discussion Topics** (`discussion_topics`): Share any topics you'd like to discuss.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or discussion topics.
* **Accessibility Needs 2** (`accessibility_needs_2`): Specify what you need help with for this event (if not covered in Accessibility Needs).
* **RSVP 2** (`rsvp_2`): Choose your preferred RSVP option.
* **Discussion Topics 2** (`discussion_topics_2`): Share any additional topics you'd like to discuss.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or discussion topics.
