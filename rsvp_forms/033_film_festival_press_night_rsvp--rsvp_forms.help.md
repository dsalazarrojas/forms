# film_festival_press_night_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The film festival press night RSVP form is a tool for attendees to confirm their participation and provide necessary details for event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Confirm your media confirmation: Select whether you will be attending for media purposes.
2. Provide guest details: Enter the number and name of guests you will be bringing.
3. Choose your photography consent: Select whether you will be okay with being photographed during the event.
4. Contact Information: Enter your contact name, phone number and email address.
5. Specify media company and press passes: Check if you are a member of the press and require a press pass.
6. Select relevant media companies: Confirm which media companies you are representing.
7. Provide additional event details: Enter any additional information about your attendance and requirements.
8. Enter press pass details: If you require a press pass, enter the necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Media Confirmation** (`media_confirmation`, select_multiple, required): Confirm whether you will be attending the event for media purposes.
  - This field asks you to select whether you will be attending the event for media purposes, such as reporting or filming.
  - Please select 'Yes' if you are representing a media company or have media equipment with you.
- **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, required): Enter the number and name of guests you will be bringing.
  - Enter the number and name(s) of your guests, e.g. "1. John Doe, 2. Jane Doe"
- **Photography Consent** (`photography_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you will be okay with being photographed during the event.
  - Choose whether you are comfortable with being photographed during the event. This will help us know if you will be okay with being on camera or not.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your contact name.
  - Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for your attendance and contact information.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
  - Enter your or your representative's contact phone number for easy communication.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
  - Enter your or your representative's email address for easy communication.
- **Media Company** (`media_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the media company you are representing.
  - If you are representing a media company, enter the name of the company here.
- **Press Pass** (`press_pass`, select_multiple, required): Confirm if you require a press pass.
  - Select 'Yes' if you require a press pass for yourself or your representative.
- **Prophet's Press** (`prophets_press`, select_multiple, required): Confirm if you are a member of the press.
  - Select 'Yes' if you are a member of the press or have press privileges.
- **Reed Dawsons Media** (`reed_dawson_media`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media.
  - Select 'Yes' if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media or representing the company.
- **Reed Dawsons Media Press** (`reed_dawson_media_press`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press.
  - Select 'Yes' if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press or representing the company.
- **Reed Dawsons Media Press 1** (`reed_dawson_media_press_1`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press 1.
  - Select 'Yes' if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press 1 or representing the company.
- **Reed Dawsons Media Press 2** (`reed_dawson_media_press_2`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press 2.
  - Select 'Yes' if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press 2 or representing the company.
- **Reed Dawsons Media Press 3** (`reed_dawson_media_press_3`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press 3.
  - Select 'Yes' if you are a member of Reed Dawsons Media Press 3 or representing the company.
- **Press Pass Details** (`press_pass_details`, text, optional): Enter the details for your press pass.
  - Enter any special requirements or instructions for your press pass.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your attendance.
  - Enter any additional information or requests you may have about your attendance.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure your RSVP is processed correctly.
- If you need help or have questions, contact the event organiser at the contact email address provided.
- If you are unsure about any field, try to make an educated guess or ask the event organiser for clarification.
