# Virtual Trick Or Treat Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the virtual trick-or-treat party. Please fill out the form completely to ensure your participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Make sure to provide accurate and complete responses for each field. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes related to the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes during the event.
* **Party Details** (`party_details`, text, optional): Describe the virtual trick-or-treat party you're interested in, including any specific details you'd like to share about your experience.
* **Share Details** (`share_details`, text, optional): If you'd like, share any additional details you'd like to share about yourself or your experience.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, text, optional): Enter your response to the event (e.g., attending, not attending, etc.).

## Tips
Please fill out this form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the event, feel free to ask. The event organizers will be in touch with you via the email provided in this form.
