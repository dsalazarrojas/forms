# event-attendance-and-menu-selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from guests about their attendance and menu selection for an event. This includes RSVP status, date and time of arrival, number of guests, dietary restrictions, and menu choices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name(s) of your guest(s) in the "Guest List" field.
2. Indicate your RSVP status by selecting "True" or "False" in the "RSVP Status" field.
3. If you are coming, select the date of your visit in the "RSVP Date" field.
4. Choose the time of your visit in the "RSVP Time" field.
5. Enter the number of guests attending in the "Number of Guests" field.
6. If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
7. Select your menu choice in the "Menu Choice" field.
8. If you have any additional comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.
9. Select any alternate menu choices in the "Menu Choice Alternate" field.
10. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest List** (text, required/optional): Enter the name(s) of your guest(s).
* **RSVP Status** (select_one, required): Indicate your RSVP status by selecting "True" or "False".
* **RSVP Date** (date, required/optional): Select the date of your visit.
* **RSVP Time** (time, required/optional): Choose the time of your visit.
* **Number of Guests** (number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests attending.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (text, required/optional): Enter any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Menu Choice** (select_one, required/optional): Select your menu choice.
* **Comments** (note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Menu Choice Alternate** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select any alternate menu choices.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to select your RSVP status accurately.
* Enter accurate dates and times.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them so we can accommodate your needs.
* Select menu choices carefully, as they may affect your meal options.
* Enter accurate email and phone numbers to ensure we can contact you.
