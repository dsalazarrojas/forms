# startup_demo_brunch_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The startup demo brunch RSVP form is used to collect information about attendees and their guests, including their names, contact information, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Provide your first name and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
* Enter your email address in the Email field.
* Select your preferred date and time in the Date and Time fields.
* Choose an RSVP option from the RSVP field. Options include Attending, Declining, or Maybe.
* If you are attending with a guest, select the Guest Attending with option and enter the guest's name, email, and phone number, and select their RSVP option.
* You may include additional comments or info in the Comments field.
* Select the Submit button to send your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select your preferred date for the demo brunch event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select your preferred time for the demo brunch event.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, select_one, required): Choose an RSVP option. Options include Attending, Declining, or Maybe.
* **Attending with** (`attending_with`, select_one, optional): If you are attending with a guest, select their relationship (Spouse or Partner, Friend, or Other).
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your guest.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your guest.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your guest.
* **Guest Date** (`guest_date`, date, optional): Select the date of your guest.
* **Guest Time** (`guest_time`, time, optional): Select the time of your guest.
* **Guest RSVP** (`guest_rsvp`, select_one, optional): Choose an RSVP option for your guest. Options include Attending, Declining, or Maybe.
* **Guest Attending with** (`guest_attending_with`, select_one, optional): If your guest is attending with someone, select their relationship (Spouse or Partner, Friend, or Other).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or info.
* **RSVP Notes** (`rsvp_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or info for your RSVP.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or info.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to send your RSVP.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, optional): Click to cancel your RSVP. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are having trouble filling out the form, try reviewing the field-by-field explanation above.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
