# Gender Reveal Event Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect RSVP information from invited individuals for a Gender Reveal Event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the event in the "event_title" field.
2. Select the date of the event in the "event_date" field using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Enter the time of the event in the "event_time" field using the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
4. Enter the location of the event in the "event_location" field.
5. Choose your RSVP status from the options "Yes", "No", or "Maybe" in the "rsvp_status" field.
6. Enter the expected number of guests for the event in the "guest_count" field.
7. Enter your name in the "name" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### event_title (<type> text)
*Enter a brief title of the event.*

### event_date (<type> date)
*Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD.*

### event_time (<type> time)
*Enter the time of the event in the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).*

### event_location (<type> text)
*Enter the location of the event.*

### rsvp_status (<type> select_multiple)
*Choose your RSVP status: "Yes", "No", or "Maybe".*

### guest_count (<type> number)
*Enter the expected number of guests for the event.*

### name (<type> text)
*Enter your name.*

### phone (<type> text)
*Enter your phone number.*
