# St. Patrick's Day Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for individuals to RSVP to the St. Patrick's Day Event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the number of attendees you are bringing in the `Number of Attendees` field.
2. Enter your name in the `Attendee Name` field.
3. Select your event type in the `Event Type` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
5. Select your attendee type in the `Type of Attendee` field.
6. Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
7. Select the payment method in the `Payment Type` field (if applicable).
8. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main**: This field is the main page title, which will be displayed at the top of the form.
* **Number of Attendees**: This field is where you enter the total number of people attending the event. Please enter a whole number only (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Attendee Name**: This field is where you enter your name as the attendee. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **RSVP Choices**: This field is where you select the number of attendees you are bringing. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Event Type**: This field is where you select the type of event you are attending. Please choose from the options provided.
* **Event Date**: This field is not currently active, but will be used for future events. If you have any questions, please contact the event organiser.
* **Event Time**: This field is not currently active, but will be used for future events. If you have any questions, please contact the event organiser.
* **Email**: This field is where you enter your email address. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Type of Attendee**: This field is where you select the type of attendee you are (e.g., Student, Staff, Parent, Faculty/Staff). Please choose from the options provided.
* **Phone Number**: This field is where you enter your contact number. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Payment Type**: This field is where you select the payment method for your attendance. Please choose from the options provided (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that your RSVP is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact the event organiser.
* If you are unsure of your attendee type, please refer to the event organiser for guidance.
