# Sponsor Appreciation Dinner RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us track RSVPs for the Sponsor Appreciation Dinner event. Please fill out this form to let us know if you will be attending and provide us with your guest count, email, and phone number (if applicable). Additionally, you can specify your dietary restrictions and any comments you'd like to make about your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Name**: Your name as registered for the event.
  - Enter your name exactly as it is spelled.
  - This field is for your identification purposes.
  - Required field.
- **Email**: Your contact email address.
  - Enter a valid email address (e.g. johndoe@example.com).
  - This field is for communication purposes.
  - Optional field.
- **Phone Number**: Your phone number (if applicable).
  - Enter a valid phone number (e.g. 123-456-7890).
  - This field is for additional contact information.
  - Optional field.
- **Number of Guests**: The number of guests you plan to bring.
  - Enter a whole number (1-10).
  - This field is to help us prepare for the event.
  - Optional field.
- **Dietary Restrictions**: Specify any dietary restrictions you have (Gluten-free, Vegetarian, Vegan, None).
  - Select all that apply.
  - This field is to ensure we accommodate your needs during the event.
  - Optional field.
- **Attending**: Indicate if you will be attending the event.
  - Choose 'True' if you will be attending.
  - Choose 'False' if you will not be attending.
  - Optional field.
- **RSVP**: Confirm your attendance.
  - Choose 'True' if you will be attending.
  - Choose 'False' if you will not be attending.
  - Required field.
- **Comments**: Any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
  - Enter any text you'd like.
  - This field is for any extra information you'd like to share.
  - Optional field.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
- We appreciate your participation in making this event a success.
