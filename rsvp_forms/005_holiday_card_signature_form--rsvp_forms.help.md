# holiday_card_signature_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to sign a holiday card or message. The form collects basic information from users, such as their name, a message, signature, and email.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **user name** in the provided field.
2. Write a **message** for the holiday card in the text box below.
3. Sign your name in the **signature** field.
4. Provide your **email** address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (`user_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on the holiday card.
  - **Message** (`message`, text, not required): Write a message to accompany the holiday card.
  - **Signature** (`signature`, text, not required): Sign your name as you would on a traditional holiday card.
  - **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you'd like to receive the holiday card digitally, provide your email address.
