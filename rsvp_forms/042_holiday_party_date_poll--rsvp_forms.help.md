# Holiday Party Date Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the details of a holiday party from interested users.

## How To Complete This Form
- Follow the instructions below to fill out the form: 
  1. Review the form fields below and fill out any required information.
  2. Answer all fields as accurately and completely as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **title** (`Holiday Party Date Poll`, text): Enter a title for the party. This will help identify the event and make it easier for users to understand what the form is about.
- **date** (`date`, date): Select the date for the party. This will help set the schedule for the event.
- **start_time** (`start_time`, number): Enter the start time for the party. This will help users know when the event begins.
- **end_time** (`end_time`, text): Enter the end time for the party. This will help users know when the event ends.
- **venue** (`venue`, text): Enter the location for the party. This will help users know where the event will take place.
- **RSVP** (`RSVP`, select_one): Select whether you plan to attend the party. Options are "Yes" or "No".
- **notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional information or comments about the party.
- **email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address. This will help the party organizer contact you if needed.
- **phone** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number. This will help the party organizer contact you if needed.
- **category** (`category`, select_multiple): Select one or more categories that the party will fall under. Options are "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
- **other** (`other`, text): Enter any other relevant information about the party.
- **form_submission** (`form_submission`, text): This field is not currently used, but it may be used for future development.
- **user_input** (`user_input`, text): This field is not currently used, but it may be used for future development.
