# Car Launch Event RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Launch Event RSVP form is a crucial tool for guests and attendees to provide their details and preferences for the upcoming car launch event. By completing this form, you'll help us better plan and prepare for the event, ensuring that you have an enjoyable and comfortable experience. The form collects various information, including your name, contact details, preferences, and interests, which will help us tailor the event to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Car Launch Event RSVP form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Your first name, last name, email address, phone number, attendance status, attendance status for the session, transportation needed, and preferred meal.
2. Select your attendance status for the event, including whether you'll attend, have VIP access, and which session you prefer.
3. Choose your preferred meal and transportation options.
4. Specify your dietary restrictions and notes if applicable.
5. Provide additional requests or needs.
6. Indicate how you heard about the event.
7. Confirm the date of your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information**: This section collects your personal details.
  * **First Name**: Your first name, as requested.
  * **Last Name**: Your last name, as requested.
  * **Email Address**: Your contact email address for event confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for event communication.
* **Company/Organization**: Your company or organization name (optional).
* **Job Title**: Your job title or position (optional).
* **Event Details**: Your event attendance preferences.
* **Attendance Status**: Confirm whether you'll attend the event.
* **Number of Guests**: The number of people, including yourself, attending the event.
* **Guest Names**: List any additional guest names (if applicable).
* **Preferred Session**: Choose your preferred event session.
* **VIP Access**: Confirm whether you have VIP access.
* **Catering Preferences**: Your meal preferences.
* **Meal Preference**: Your meal choice.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Dietary Notes**: Additional details about your dietary restrictions.
* **Beverage Preference**: Choose your drink preference.
* **Transportation**: Note how you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Transportation Needed**: Confirm whether you need shuttle service.
* **Parking Needed**: Confirm whether you need parking.
* **Vehicle Interest**: Specify your vehicle interest.
* **Vehicle Category Interest**: Choose the category that interests you.
* **Purchase Intent**: Confirm your purchase intent.
* **Media Attendance**: Confirm whether you're attending as media.
* **Media Outlet**: Specify the name of your media outlet (if applicable).
* **Additional Requests**: Any special requests or needs.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Specify how you heard about the event.
* **RSVP Date**: Confirm today's date for your RSVP.

Note: This help guide provides a clear and detailed explanation of each field in the Car Launch Event RSVP form, ensuring that users understand what information is required and how to complete it accurately.
