# Fullscreen Event RSVP Responsive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather basic attendee information for events, including guest details, event date, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Guest Details in the "Guest Details" field.
2. Choose your response to "Will You Attend" by selecting one of the options: "Yes" or "No".
3. Enter the event date and time in the "Event Date" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter any additional comments or messages in the "Message" field.
7. Enter any additional event details in the "Event Details" field.
8. Enter any further comments or messages in the "Message 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Details (Guest Details)** (`1`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself as the guest.
* **Will You Attend (rsvp_choice)** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Choose "Yes" if you will attend the event, or "No" if you will not.
* **Event Date (date_and_time)** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the date and time of the event.
* **Email (email)** (`4`, `text`, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone (phone)** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Message (message)** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or messages about the event.
* **Event Details (event_details)** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Message 2 (message2)** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter any further comments or messages about the event.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear in your responses to ensure the organiser can plan for your attendance.
* Ensure all fields that are required are filled out.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, contact the event organiser or refer to this guide for further assistance.
