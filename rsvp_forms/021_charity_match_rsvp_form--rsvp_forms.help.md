# Charity Match RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Match RSVP Form is designed to collect information from attendees and participants in charity events. This form helps event organizers manage their events more efficiently and ensures that attendees' needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Specify the event you are RSVPing for, including the event name and date.
3. Indicate your RSVP status (Yes, I Will Attend, Maybe, or No, I Cannot Attend).
4. Enter the number of guests in your party.
5. If applicable, list the names of other guests in your party.
6. Specify any dietary restrictions, preferences, or accessibility needs.
7. Express interest in volunteering or making a donation.
8. Indicate how you heard about the event (Email, Social Media, Word of Mouth, Website, or Other).
9. Finally, select a date and time to submit your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your given name at birth.
* **Last Name**: Your surname.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email address for event details and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your primary contact number for the event organizers.
* **Event Name**: The name of the event you are RSVPing for.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event.
* **Will You Attend**: Indicate your RSVP status (Yes, Maybe, or No, I Cannot Attend).
* **Number of Guests in Your Party**: How many people will be attending the event with you.
* **Names of Other Guests**: List the names of people in your party (if applicable).
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies for your group.
* **Seating Preference**: Any special seating requests.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed**: Specify any accessibility needs or requests.
* **Interested in Volunteering**: Indicate interest in volunteering for the event.
* **Interested in Making a Donation**: Indicate interest in making a donation.
* **Suggested Donation Amount**: Enter a suggested donation amount (if applicable).
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Specify how you learned about the event (Email, Social Media, Word of Mouth, Website, or Other).
* **Date of RSVP**: Select a date and time to submit your RSVP.
