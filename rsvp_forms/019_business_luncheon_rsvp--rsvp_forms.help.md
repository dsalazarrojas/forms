# Business Luncheon RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect RSVPs from attendees for a business luncheon event. The form aims to gather essential details such as names, contact information, and preferences to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the RSVP process.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as such, to provide accurate and complete information.
3. If unsure about any field, you can use the hints provided for guidance.
4. Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Your Name** (attendee_name): Enter your full name as it should be displayed on your name badge.
- **Email Address** (attendee_email): Provide your email for event confirmation and updates.
- **Phone Number** (attendee_phone): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
- **Company Name** (company_name): Enter your company or organization name.
- **Job Title** (job_title): Type in your current job title.
- **Attendance Status** (attendance_status): Select "Yes - I will attend" if you are planning to attend the event.
- **Number of Guests** (number_of_guests): Enter the total number of attendees, including yourself.
- **Guest Names** (guest_names): List the names of any additional guests attending with you (optional).
- **Meal Preference** (meal_preference): Choose your preferred meal option.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (dietary_restrictions): List any food allergies or dietary needs.
- **Networking Interest** (networking_interest): Indicate your level of interest in structured networking.
- **Table Preference** (table_preference): Select your preferred seating arrangement.
- **Specific Table Requests** (table_requests): If applicable, list names of people you would like to sit with.
- **Business Card Exchange** (business_card_exchange): Indicate if you will bring business cards.
- **Presentation Interest** (presentation_interest): Express your interest in presenting at future events.
- **Topics of Interest** (topics_of_interest): List topics you would like to hear about.
- **Referral Services** (referral_services): Indicate your willingness to receive business referrals.
- **Elevator Pitch** (elevator_pitch): Briefly describe your business in 30 seconds.
- **Special Accommodations** (special_accommodations): Provide any accessibility needs we should be aware of.
- **How Did You Hear About This Event** (how_did_you_hear): Select the source of your event information.
- **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Leave any other comments or questions you may have.
