# Graduation Ceremony RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Ceremony RSVP Form is used to gather information from guests about their attendance status and details at a graduation ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields and fill out the required information for the relevant fields.
2. Provide your RSVP details in the "RSVP Date" field.
3. Enter your name and contact information in the "Guest Name" and "Contact E" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
4. List the names of the guests attending in the "Guests Attending" field.
5. Enter any message or note for the guest(s) attending in the "Message for Guest" field.
6. Select your RSVP status from the options in the "RSVP Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Graduation Ceremony Details**: Enter a brief description of the ceremony details.
* **RSVP Date**: Select the date on which you plan to attend the graduation ceremony.
* **Guest Name**: Enter your name (or the name of the person you are attending with).
* **Contact E**: Enter your email address so that the graduate or their team can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the graduate or their team can contact you for further information.
* **Guests Attending**: Select all the guests that will be attending with you (e.g., Guest 1, Guest 2, Guest 3).
* **Message for Guest**: Enter a message or note for the guest(s) attending, such as a birthday greeting or gift idea.
* **RSVP Status**: Select your RSVP status from the options: Attending, Not Attending, or Maybe.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure that the graduate's family is accurately informed about your attendance.
* If you are unsure about your RSVP status, please select "Maybe" and note the reason in the "Message for Guest" field.
* If you are attending with multiple guests, list all their names in the "Guests Attending" field.
