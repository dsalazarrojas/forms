# Induction Event RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about an induction event. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we have all the necessary details for event organization and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **event_details** section with the relevant information about the event.
2. Provide details about the attendees in the **attendee_details** section.
3. In the **rsvp_details** section, include any additional comments or information about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_details** (event_details): This section is for entering the event details, such as the name, date, time, location, and other relevant information about the event.
* **attendee_details** (attendee_details): This section is for providing details about the attendees, including their names, job titles, and contact information (e.g., email and phone numbers).
* **rsvp_details** (rsvp_details): This section is for including any additional comments or information about your attendance, such as dietary restrictions or special requests.
