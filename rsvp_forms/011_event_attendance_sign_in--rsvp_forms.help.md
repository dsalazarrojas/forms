# Event Attendance Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about event attendance from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Event Name field with the name of the event you are attending.
2. Enter your name in the Attendee Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Attendee Email field. Please ensure it is valid.
4. Enter your phone number in the Attendee Phone field.
5. Select the Checkin Time when you checked in.
6. If you have any comments, please include them in the Comments field.
7. Check the Event Date field to select the date of the event you attended.
8. For the Event Status field, select the status of your attendance (On Time, Late, or No Show).
9. If you wish to be contacted for follow-up, select Yes in the Follow-up field.
10. Confirm your attendance by selecting Yes or No in the Confirm Attendance field.
11. Select your Attendee Status (Confirmed or Declined).
12. Finally, add any follow-up message in the Follow-up Message field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: The name of the event you are attending.
* **Attendee Name**: Your name as it appears on your registration or identification.
* **Attendee Email**: Your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Attendee Phone**: Your phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Checkin Time**: The time when you checked in for the event.
* **Comments**: Any comments or feedback you have about the event.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event you attended.
* **Event Status**: The status of your attendance, select On Time, Late, or No Show.
* **Follow-up**: Select Yes if you want to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Confirm Attendance**: Confirm your attendance by selecting Yes or No.
* **Attendee Status**: Your status, select Confirmed or Declined.
* **Follow-up Message**: Any further messages you want to send to the event organizers.
* **Event ID**: The identifier of the event you attended (not a required field).
* **Attended**: The status of your attendance, select Yes or No. (Note: This field is redundant with Event Status and Confirm Attendance. Please select one of them instead.)
* **Notes**: Any additional notes about your attendance.

Note: The Event ID field is not required for most users, but please fill it in if you need to provide it.
