# Exhibition Opening Soirée RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibition Opening Soirée RSVP Form is designed to help us track attendance and gather guest information for the Soirée. This form is used to inform the event organizers about your plans and preferences for the event.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select whether you will be attending or decline from the "Attending" field.
5. If you are attending, you can provide guest information in the "Guest Information" field.
6. Write any additional message or note in the "Message" field.
7. Click "Submit" in the "Submit RSVP" field to confirm your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RSVP Form**: This field is where you can indicate your intention to attend or not. Please select "I am attending" if you plan to be there.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone**: If you'd like us to have a way to contact you, enter your phone number here.
* **Attending**: Select "I am attending" to confirm your attendance or "Decline" if you won't be able to make it.
* **Guest Information**: If you are attending, please provide information about your guests, such as their names and contact details.
* **Message**: Write any additional message or note you'd like to share with us.
* **Submit RSVP**: Select "Submit" to confirm your RSVP and submit the form.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information or notes you'd like to pass along to the event organizers.
