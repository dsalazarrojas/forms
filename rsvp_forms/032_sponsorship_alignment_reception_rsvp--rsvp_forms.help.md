# Sponsorship Alignment Reception RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals RSVPing to a sponsorship alignment reception event. The event name, attendee name, number of guests, dietary needs, event date, and event time are required fields, while the notes field is optional and allows users to provide any additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event you are RSVPing to in the `Event Name` field.
2. Provide your name in the `Attendee Name` field.
3. Enter the number of guests attending the event in the `Number of Guests` field.
4. Select any dietary needs you have from the `Dietary Needs` dropdown list.
5. Enter the date of the event in the `Event Date` field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
6. Enter the time of the event in the `Event Time` field in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
7. Select your RSVP response from the `RSVP` dropdown list.
8. If you have any additional comments or information, enter them in the `Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: The name of the event you are RSVPing to. This field is required and must be entered in plain text.
* **Attendee Name**: Your name as the person RSVPing to the event. This field is required and must be entered in plain text.
* **Number of Guests**: The number of people attending the event with you. This field is required and must be a whole number.
* **Dietary Needs**: Select any dietary needs you have from the dropdown list. This field is required and allows you to select multiple options.
* **Event Date**: The date of the event. This field is required and must be entered in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Event Time**: The time of the event. This field is required and must be entered in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **RSVP**: Select your response to the event. This field is required and allows you to select one option from the list.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your RSVP. This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
