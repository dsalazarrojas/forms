# Family Visitation Day RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents and guardians for the Family Visitation Day event. It is meant to help ensure a smooth and enjoyable event by collecting necessary details about your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.
2. Fill out the form completely, even if a field is not required.
3. Make sure to select all relevant options for each field.
4. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: RSVP Form
- **rsvp_form** (`rsvp_form`, text, required: false): This field is a text input where you can provide any additional comments or details about your visit.
- **date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to visit.
- **time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you plan to visit.
- **event_details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about your visit, such as any specific activities you plan to participate in.
- **parent_attendance** (`parent_attendance`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you will be attending the event as a parent, 'No' otherwise.
- **student_attendance** (`student_attendance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all applicable students you will be attending the event with, or 'No' if none apply.
- **comment** (`comment`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your visit.
- **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
