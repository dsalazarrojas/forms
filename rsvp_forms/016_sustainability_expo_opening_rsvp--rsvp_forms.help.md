# Sustainability Expo Opening RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Expo Opening RSVP form is designed to help us track attendee information and special requests for the event. Please fill out this form to let us know if you will be attending and if you have any special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Make sure to enter your name and email address correctly.
3. If you have any guests coming with you, enter the number of guests.
4. If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions, please select the relevant option.
5. Choose your arrival time.
6. Click submit to send your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the event badges and lists.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Guest Count**: If you are attending with guests, please enter the number of people in your group.
* **Special Request**: If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or mobility needs, please select the relevant option.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select one of the options to indicate your dietary needs:
	+ Gluten Free: You have a gluten intolerance or require gluten-free options.
	+ Vegan: You do not eat any products that come from animals.
	+ Vegetarian: You eat a diet that includes plant-based foods but excludes meat.
	+ Other: Select "Other" if you have a different dietary restriction not listed above.
* **Arrival Time**: Choose your expected arrival time to help us plan for the event.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your information accurately to ensure that we have the correct information for you and your guests.
