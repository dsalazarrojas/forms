# Language Exchange Meetup RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals interested in attending a language exchange meetup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Indicate your Participant preferences by selecting one or more of the options in the "Participant preferences" field.
5. Choose your Language level from the "Language level" field.
6. Specify your Schedule preferences by selecting one or more of the options in the "Schedule preferences" field.
7. Confirm your Attendance by selecting "Will Attend", "Will Not Attend", or "Maybe" from the "Attendance confirmation" field.
8. Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
9. Choose your Language of interest from the "Language" field.
10. Finally, select a Date and Time for the meetup in the "Meetup date" and "Meetup time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here. This will be used to contact you about the meetup.
* **Participant preferences** (`participant_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate whether you have a specific schedule preference. Select "Yes" if you are interested in specific schedule preferences, such as morning or evening meetings. Select "No" if you do not have any schedule preferences.
* **Language level** (`participant_level`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your language level. Select "Beginner" if you are a beginner, "Intermediate" if you have some experience, or "Advanced" if you are proficient in the language.
* **Schedule preferences** (`schedule_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Specify your schedule preferences. Select "Yes" if you have specific schedule preferences, such as morning or afternoon. Select "No" if you do not have any schedule preferences.
* **Attendance confirmation** (`attendance_confirmation`, `select_one`, required/optional): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Will Attend", "Will Not Attend", or "Maybe".
* **Comments** (`language_exchange_meetup_rsvp_comments`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments you'd like to share with the organizers.
* **Language** (`language`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the language you are interested in learning or practicing.
* **Meetup date** (`meetup_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select a date for the meetup.
* **Meetup time** (`meetup_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select a time for the meetup.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields.
- You can select multiple options for Participant preferences and Schedule preferences.
- Select "Maybe" for Attendance confirmation if you are unsure about your attendance.
- You can choose any language from the options provided, but please ensure that the selected language is one that you are interested in practicing or learning.
