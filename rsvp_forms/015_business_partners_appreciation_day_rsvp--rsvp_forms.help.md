# Business Partners Appreciation Day RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather RSVP information for the Business Partners Appreciation Day event. It will help us know who is attending, their preferences, and any special needs we should be aware of.

## How To Complete This Form
The form is divided into several sections. Follow these steps:

1. Begin with the **Event Information** section, where you will find the event title, date, time, and location. Review the event details to ensure you have them correct.
2. In the **RSVP Confirmation** section, confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes, I will attend" if you plan to join us. If you cannot attend, select "No, I cannot attend" or "Undecided" if you're still unsure.
3. If you will be attending, move on to the **Attendee Details** section, where you will provide your name, company name, email address, and phone number (if applicable). Ensure that your contact information is accurate.
4. In the **Guest Information** section, let us know if you will be bringing any guests, and enter their names if applicable.
5. For your personal preferences, select your preferred meal option and dietary restrictions in the **Event Preferences** section.
6. Indicate if you have any seating preferences in the same section.
7. If you are interested in attending specific activities, select them in the **Engagement Interest** section.
8. Finally, provide any suggestions for future events in the **What We Could Do Better** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`): Enter the title of the appreciation event. You can leave this field blank as it is not required for your RSVP.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the appreciation event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Select the time of the appreciation event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the appreciation event.
* **Will You Be Attending** (`attending_status`): Confirm your attendance for the appreciation event. Select "Yes, I will attend" to confirm.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`): Enter your complete name for the event.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company name (if you have one).
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for event confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`): Provide your phone number (if you have one). This is optional.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`): Enter the total number of guests you will be bringing.
* **Guest Names** (`guest_names_list`): If you are bringing guests, list their names.
* **Meal Preference** (`meal_selection`): Select your preferred meal option from the provided choices.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, list them in this field.
* **Seating Preferences** (`seating_preferences`): Enter any specific seating preferences you may have.
* **Activities of Interest** (`interested_activities`): Select the activities you are interested in attending.
* **What We Could Do Better** (`partner_feedback`): Share any suggestions for future events.
* **Parking Required** (`parking_required`): If you need parking assistance, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".

By following these steps and explanations, you should be able to complete the form accurately and help us make the appreciation event a success!
