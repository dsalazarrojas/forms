# Election Night Watch Party Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Election Night Watch Party Registration form. This form is designed to gather information from individuals planning to attend or host an election night watch party. The purpose of this form is to help hosts and attendees connect with each other and make the most of this special event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** on the first page.
2. Enter your **Email** address to ensure you receive important updates and reminders.
3. Provide your **Phone** number so we can get in touch with you if needed.
4. Indicate the **Number of Guests** you plan to bring with you.
5. Choose **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you will be attending.
6. If you do have a plan to watch the election results, select **At home** or **Public location**.
7. Optionally, add a brief **Message** or comment.
8. Finally, submit your registration form by filling in the **Submit** field and hit the **Submit** button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on the guest list.
* **Email**: This is the email address we will use to send you important updates and reminders about the watch party.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number in case we need to get in touch with you regarding any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of people you plan to bring with you so we can plan accordingly.
* **Will you be attending?** Choose **Yes** if you will be attending, and **No** otherwise.
* **Do you plan on watching the election results at home or at a public location?**: Let us know where you will be watching the election results, so we can connect you with others who will be in the same location.
* **Message**: This is an optional field where you can add any additional comment or information about your watch party.
* **Comment**: This is another optional field where you can add any additional information or comments.
* **Submit**: Fill in your name and hit the **Submit** button to complete your registration.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure we can connect you with other attendees and hosts.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [contact information].
* We are excited to connect you with others who will be watching the election results with you!
