# Employee Christmas Party Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees about their plans to attend the Christmas party.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select whether you will be attending the party.
2. Enter your date of arrival (or absence).
3. If you plan to arrive at a specific time, please select the time of arrival.
4. Confirm your attending status.
5. Select your dietary preference (Meat, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Other).
6. Enter any special requests (e.g. seat arrangement, food preferences).
7. Confirm your RSVP status.
8. Choose how you will RSVP (e.g. phone, email).
9. Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number).
10. If you are bringing guests, select how many will be attending.
11. Enter guest names and comments.
12. Choose whether you would like a gift card for yourself or your guests.
13. If you select "Yes" for a gift card, choose the value.
14. Enter any gift message for your gift card.
15. If you select "Yes" for a gift card for your guests, choose the value.
16. Enter any gift message for your guests' gift cards.
17. Enter any other gift information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attending** (1, Number, Required): Select whether you will be attending the party.
* **Date** (2, Date, Required): Enter your date of arrival (or absence).
* **Time** (3, Time, Required): If you plan to arrive at a specific time, please select the time of arrival.
* **RSVP** (4, Select Multiple, Optional): Confirm your attending status.
* **Dietary Preferences** (5, Select One, Required): Select your dietary preference (Meat, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Other).
* **Special Requests** (6, Note, Optional): Enter any special requests (e.g. seat arrangement, food preferences).
* **RSVP Status** (7, Number, Required): Confirm your RSVP status.
* **RSVP Method** (8, Text, Required): Choose how you will RSVP (e.g. phone, email).
* **RSVP Comments** (9, Text, Optional): Enter any comments regarding your RSVP.
* **Contact Name** (10, Text, Required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (11, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (12, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Message** (13, Text, Optional): Enter any message for the party planning committee.
* **Employee Attendance** (14, Select One, Required): Select whether you will attend the party.
* **Employee Comments** (15, Text, Optional): Enter any comments regarding your attendance.
* **Guest Count** (16, Number, Required): Select how many guests will be attending.
* **Guests** (17, Select Multiple, Required): Select how many guests will be attending.
* **Guest Names** (18, Text, Optional): Enter guest names.
* **Guest Comments** (19, Text, Optional): Enter comments for your guests.
* **Gift Card Preference** (20, Select One, Required): Choose whether you would like a gift card for yourself or your guests.
* **Gift Preferences** (21, Select Multiple, Optional): If you select "Yes" for a gift card, choose the value.
* **Gift Card Value** (22, Number, Optional): Enter any gift message for your gift card.
* **Gift Message** (23, Text, Optional): Enter any gift message for your gift card.
* **Other** (24, Text, Optional): Enter any other gift information.
