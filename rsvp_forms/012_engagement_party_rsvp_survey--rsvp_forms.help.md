# engagement_party_rsvp_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The engagement party RSVP survey form is used to collect information from guests about their attendance and preferences for the party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Confirm your attendance: Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the "Will attend" field to indicate whether you will be able to attend the party.
2. Provide your dietary preferences: Choose your preferred options for the "Dietary preferences" field.
3. Add any additional notes: If you have any additional comments or information you would like to share with the host, enter them in the "Additional notes" field.
4. Enter your RSVP date and time: Select your expected RSVP date and time for the "RSVP date" and "RSVP time" fields.
5. Enter your contact information: Provide your phone number and email address in the "Phone number" and "Email" fields, respectively.
6. Enter guest details: Enter the name and relation of your guest(s) in the "Guest name" and "Guest relation" fields.
7. Leave a message for the host: If you have any messages for the host, enter them in the "Host message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest attendance** (`Will attend`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm whether you will attend the party.
* **Dietary preferences** (`Dietary preferences`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred options for the party.
* **Additional notes** (`Additional notes`, `text`, required): Leave any additional comments or information you would like to share with the host.
* **RSVP date** (`RSVP date`, `date`, required): Select the date you expect to RSVP for the party.
* **RSVP time** (`RSVP time`, `time`, required): Select the time you expect to RSVP for the party.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for the host to contact you.
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address for the host to contact you.
* **Guest name** (`Guest name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your guest(s).
* **Guest relation** (`Guest relation`, `select_one`, required): Select whether your guest is a 'Yes' or 'No' relation.
* **Guest message** (`Guest message`, `text`, required): Leave a message for your guest.
* **Host message** (`Host message`, `text`, required): Leave a message for the host.
