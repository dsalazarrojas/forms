# Youth Coat Pick Up RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents for the youth coat pickup process. It ensures that the correct person is contacted and the coat is handed over to the right person at the correct time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and your child's first name in the relevant fields.
2. Provide your email address so that we can contact you to confirm coat pickup details.
3. Enter the name of the parent or guardian picking up the coat.
4. Select the date and time you will be picking up the coat.
5. Choose the correct AM/PM for the pickup time.
6. Confirm whether you will be picking up the coat.
7. If the coat has already been sent to you, select "True" in the "Email Sent" field.
8. If you have any contact phone number, enter it in the "Contact Phone" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the coat pickup in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person picking up the coat.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person picking up the coat so we can contact you.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian picking up the coat.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the coat pickup.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of the coat pickup.
* **Pickup Time AM/PM** (`pickup_time_am_pm`, select_one, required: false): Choose AM or PM for the pickup time.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select_one, required: false): Confirm whether you will be picking up the coat.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, select_one, required: false): If the coat has already been sent to you, select "True".
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes about the coat pickup.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form correctly to avoid any issues with coat handover.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
- We will contact you using the email address you provide, so make sure it's correct.
- If you are picking up multiple coats, please fill out multiple forms for each coat.
