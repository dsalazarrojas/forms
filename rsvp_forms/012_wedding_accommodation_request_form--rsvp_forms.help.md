# wedding_accommodation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the accommodation preferences and arrival/departure dates of individuals attending a wedding event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, make sure you have all necessary information ready, such as your arrival and departure dates.
2. On the first page, select the accommodation preferences that suit you best.
3. Enter your arrival date in the format MM/DD or MM/DD/YYYY, where "MM" is the month, "DD" is the day, and "YYYY" is the year.
4. Enter your departure date in the same format as your arrival date.
5. Submit your form once you've filled out all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **accommodation_preferences** (optional): Select the type of accommodation you prefer, such as hotel, Airbnb, or other.
* **arrival_date** (optional): Enter your expected arrival date in the format MM/DD or MM/DD/YYYY.
* **departure_date** (optional): Enter your expected departure date in the format MM/DD or MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and correct information to ensure smooth accommodation arrangements.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the event organizer for clarification.
* You can always save your form and come back to it later, but please note that some fields may become required if you've already entered some data.
