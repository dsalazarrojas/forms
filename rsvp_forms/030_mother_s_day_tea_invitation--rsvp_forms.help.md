# Mother's Day Tea Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mother's Day Tea Invitation form is a tool used to collect RSVPs and additional information from guests invited to a Mother's Day tea.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your RSVP status.
2. Enter your name.
3. Choose a date and time to RSVP for.
4. Choose how you will be attending.
5. Select which guests will be attending with you.
6. If you have any special requests, enter them in the text box provided.
7. Optionally, enter any additional comments or message.
8. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable).
9. Enter any additional information about you.
10. Finally, provide the host's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you will be attending the Mother's Day tea. Select 'Active' if you plan to attend.
- **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
- **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you will be RSVPing for.
- **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time you will be attending the Mother's Day tea.
- **Attending** (`attending`, select_one, required): Choose how you will be attending the event. Select from the provided options.
- **Guests Attending** (`guests_attending`, select_multiple, required): Select which guests will be attending with you.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requests, enter them here.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or messages.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Message** (`message`, text, required/optional): Enter a message.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
- **Host Info** (`host_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the host.
- **Form Submitter** (`form_submitter`, text, required/optional): Enter your name or identity.
