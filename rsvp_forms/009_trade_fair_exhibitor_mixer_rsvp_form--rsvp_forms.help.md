# Trade Fair Exhibitor Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from trade fair exhibitors about their attendance and preferences for a mixer event. It helps the event organizers to plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the "Will you be attending?" question with the correct option (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
2. If you will be attending, fill out the "How many people will be attending?" field with the number of people attending.
3. In the "What do you have to offer?" field, briefly describe what you will be offering or presenting at the event.
4. Fill out the "Company Name" field with your company's name.
5. Enter your "Booth Number" if you have one.
6. Fill out the "Contact Name" field with your name.
7. Enter your "Email" address.
8. Enter your "Phone" number.
9. In the "Additional Comments" field, provide any additional information about your company or your presentation.
10. Select the food and beverage options you are interested in from the "What food and beverage options are you interested in?" field.
11. Select the drink options you are interested in from the "What drink options are you interested in?" field.
12. Check the "Do you have any food allergies?" field to indicate if you have any food allergies.
13. Select any dietary restrictions you have from the "Are you on a dietary restriction?" field.
14. In the "What are your preferred food preferences?" field, describe your food preferences.
15. In the "What are your preferred drink preferences?" field, describe your drink preferences.
16. Check the "Do you have any special needs?" field to indicate if you have any special needs.
17. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional comments about your company or presentation.
18. Sign and date the "Your Signature" field.
19. Select the "Date and Time" for the event.
20. Select the "Time" for the event.
21. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Will you be attending?** (Yes/No): Answer this question with the correct option (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **How many people will be attending?**: Enter the number of people attending.
* **What do you have to offer?**: Briefly describe what you will be offering or presenting at the event.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
* **Booth Number**: Enter your booth number if you have one.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information about your company or presentation.
* **What food and beverage options are you interested in?** (Select multiple): Select the food and beverage options you are interested in.
* **What drink options are you interested in?** (Select multiple): Select the drink options you are interested in.
* **Do you have any food allergies?** (Select multiple): Check the option if you have any food allergies.
* **Are you on a dietary restriction?** (Select multiple): Select any dietary restrictions you have.
* **What are your preferred food preferences?**: Describe your food preferences.
* **What are your preferred drink preferences?**: Describe your drink preferences.
* **Do you have any special needs?** (Select multiple): Check the option if you have any special needs.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your company or presentation.
* **Your Signature**: Sign and date the form.
* **Date and Time**: Select the date and time for the event.
* **Time**: Select the time for the event.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the event organizers can plan and prepare adequately.
* If you have any special requests or needs, make sure to indicate them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions, please select the correct options to ensure that the event organizers can accommodate your needs.
* The "Confirmation Code" and "Confirmation Message" fields are for internal use only and should not be filled out by the exhibitor.
