# Thanksgiving Zoom Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from guests to ensure a smooth and enjoyable Thanksgiving Zoom party experience. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form accurately, and don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and details about the party.
3. Provide your Phone number for any last-minute inquiries.
4. Let us know if you can attend or not (RSVP) by selecting the correct option.
5. If attending, specify your Date of Birth for our records.
6. Choose your preferred Time of Arrival for the party.
7. If you have any comments or messages for the host, feel free to add them in the Comments field.
8. If you're bringing a guest, fill out their First Name and Last Name in the Guest Name field. You can also specify their Relationship with you and the host.
9. Select your preferred RSVP Type (Host, Guest, or Both) to indicate your status.
10. If you have any dietary restrictions, choose from the options provided (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, or None).
11. If you have any Special Requests, add them in the Comments field.
12. Fill out the Number of Guests you're bringing to ensure we have enough space and food.
13. If you have any Arrival Time preferences, let us know in the Time of Arrival field.
14. Add a Message for the host to make them happy.
15. Indicate how many Guests will be Attending with you, if any.
16. Finally, select your preferred options for Dietary Restrictions and Special Requests.
17. Don't forget to submit your RSVP By Date to ensure we can finalize the party details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for updates and contact.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for last-minute inquiries.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, `select_one`, required): Let us know if you can attend or not.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): If attending, please specify your Date of Birth for our records.
* **Time of Arrival** (`time_of_arrival`, `time`, required): Choose your preferred Time of Arrival.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Add any comments or messages for the host.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): If bringing a guest, fill out their First Name and Last Name.
* **Relationship** (`guest_relationship`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the Relationship between the guest and the host.
* **RSVP Type** (`rsvp_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred RSVP Type (Host, Guest, or Both).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred options (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, or None).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `note`, required): Add any Special Requests in the Comments field.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Indicate how many Guests will be attending with you.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, required): Choose your preferred Arrival Time.
* **Guest Message** (`guest_message`, `note`, required): Add a Message for the host.
* **Number of Attending** (`number_of_attending`, `number`, required): Indicate how many Guests will be attending with you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required): Add any additional Comments.
* **RSVP By** (`rsvp_by`, `date`, required): Select your preferred RSVP By Date.
* **Guest Attending** (`guest_attending`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify which Guests will be attending with you.
* **Host Message** (`host_message`, `note`, required): Add a Message for the host.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, required): Add any Additional Info.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `date`, not required): This field is automatically populated and not editable.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `date`, not required): This field is automatically populated and not editable.
