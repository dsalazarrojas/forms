# Art Fair Closing Reception RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Fair Closing Reception RSVP Form is designed to gather information from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all. This form helps us plan seating, catering, and other essential details for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your Guest name in the Guest name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field for confirmation purposes.
4. Enter the total number of guests attending in the Number attending field.
5. Select your Ticket preference (General admission, VIP, or Press).
6. If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them in the Dietary restrictions field.
7. If you have any accessibility needs, describe them in the Accessibility needs field.
8. If you have a plus one attending with you, enter their name in the Plus one name field.
9. Check the Photo consent field to give us permission for event photography.
10. If you'd like to leave a message or comment for the organizers, use the Comments field.
11. Optionally, provide your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest name**: Enter your full name as it will appear on the guest list.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Number attending**: Count the total number of guests attending with you.
* **Ticket preference**: Select how you'd like to be seated (General admission, VIP, or Press).
* **Dietary restrictions**: List any food restrictions or preferences (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free).
* **Accessibility needs**: Describe any mobility or sensory needs we should be aware of.
* **Plus one name**: If you've brought an additional guest, enter their name.
* **Photo consent**: Check to grant permission for us to take photos of you and your guests.
* **Contact phone**: Provide your phone number for contact, if you'd like.
* **Comments**: Leave any additional information or comments for our team.

# Tips
* Be accurate and thorough in your responses to help us plan for your guests.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll be in touch with you shortly after submitting your RSVP to confirm your attendance.
