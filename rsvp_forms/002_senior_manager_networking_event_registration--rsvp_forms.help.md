# senior_manager_networking_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for a senior manager networking event. It is designed to collect essential information from attendees, including their details, preferences, and RSVP information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your company name.
4. Select your role (Account Manager, Director, Manager, or Other).
5. Enter your department (if applicable).
6. Specify your preferences (e.g., vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free).
7. Add any additional comments or requests.
8. Indicate if you are attending the event (True or False).
9. Choose a date and time for your RSVP (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional attendees' information (e.g., name, email, company).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details**: Enter your name, email, and company name to identify yourself as an attendee.
* **Name**: This field is for your name, which will be displayed on the event list and in communication with the organizers.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to enable communication with the event organizers and other attendees.
* **Company**: Enter your company name to help the organizers track attendees from your organization.
* **Role**: Select your role as a manager (Account Manager, Director, Manager, or Other) to help the event organizers categorize attendees.
* **Department**: Enter your department name, if applicable, to help the organizers track your department's attendance.
* **Preferences**: Select all that apply from the dietary restrictions list to inform the organizers about your dietary needs.
* **Dietary restrictions**: This field allows you to specify any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free. Select all that apply.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or requests you might have, such as special requests or questions.
* **Is Attending**: Indicate whether you are attending the event (True or False).
* **RSVP Date**: Enter a date for your RSVP, if applicable, to help the organizers track your response.
* **RSVP Time**: Enter a time for your RSVP, if applicable, to help the organizers schedule the event.
* **RSVP Date Time**: This field is for additional information about your RSVP date and time, if applicable.
* **Attendees 1**: Enter the name of additional attendees, if any.
* **Attendees 2**: Enter the email address and company name of additional attendees, if any.

## Tips
- Be accurate when entering your details and RSVP information to ensure proper tracking and communication.
- Review this form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have provided all required information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers or management team for assistance.
