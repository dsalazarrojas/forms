# Corporate Leadership Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for employees to RSVP to the Corporate Leadership Mixer, an event organized by the company leadership. Please fill out this form to let us know if you will be attending and provide us with your details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the required information and submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **RSVP Form**: This is the main form where you can enter your RSVP details.
- **Company Details**: Enter your company name or affiliation if you want to be contacted by your company.
- **Name**: Enter your name as it would be listed in the event attendance list.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you with event updates and information.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be reached in case of any last-minute updates.
- **Will Attend**: Choose whether you will attend the event.
- **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you.
- **Food Preference**: Select your food preference (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, or Omnivore).
- **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have dietary restrictions, check the corresponding box.
- **Comments**: If you have any comments or special requests, enter them here.
- **RSVP by**: Choose a date and time by which you will RSVP.
