# Vendor Appreciation Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees of the Vendor Appreciation Party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Please enter your name. This field is required.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number. This is not required, but it's recommended in case we need to contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is optional.
* **RSVP**: Select one of the three options (Attending, Not Attending, or Maybe) to let us know your response to attending the party. This field is required.
* **Comment**: If you'd like to share any additional information, you can add a comment here. This field is optional.
* **Date Arrived**: If you're attending the party, enter the date you plan to arrive. This field is optional.
* **Time Arrived**: If you're attending the party, enter the time you plan to arrive. This field is optional.
* **Number of Guests Attending**: If you're attending, enter the number of guests you're bringing. This field is optional.
Note: If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for clarification.
