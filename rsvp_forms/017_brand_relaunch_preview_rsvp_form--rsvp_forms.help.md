# Brand Relaunch Preview RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Relaunch Preview RSVP Form is designed to gather essential information from attendees to the brand relaunch preview event. This form helps us confirm your attendance, gather your contact information, and understand your preferences and needs for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it should appear on the invitation.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we will send event details.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, text, required): Provide your contact number for event updates.
* **Company or Organization** (`company_affiliation`, text, optional): If you are representing a company or organization, please enter its name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your professional title.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will be attending, unable to attend, or need to confirm soon.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the relaunch preview event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, select_one, required): Select how many guests, including yourself, will attend.
* **Names of Additional Guests** (`guest_names`, text, optional): Enter the full names of other attendees.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select any dietary requirements or allergies you have.
* **Beverage Preference** (`beverage_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred beverage option.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): Select any accessibility needs you have.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you will get to the event.
* **Parking Required** (`parking_required`, select_one, required): Choose whether you need parking information.
* **Photography Consent** (`media_consent`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to being photographed.
* **Your Relationship to Brand** (`brand_relationship`, select_one, required): Select how you are connected to the brand.
* **Questions for the Brand** (`questions_for_brand`, text, optional): Enter any questions you would like to ask the brand.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any other special requests.
*   **Additional Information** (`questions_for_brand`, text, optional): Enter any other information not covered in the above fields.

Please review your submission carefully before submitting the form to ensure you have provided all necessary information accurately.
