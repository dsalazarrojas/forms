# Conference Pre Networking Session RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form helps us understand who will be attending the pre-networking session, allowing us to plan and prepare for your needs and expectations. By providing your information, you will be able to:

* Participate in the pre-networking session
* Connect with other attendees
* Meet your networking goals

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information ready to go.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
3. Select your answers carefully, as some fields are required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address where you would like to receive session details.
* **Organization/affiliation**: If you are representing a company or institution, enter it here.
* **Job title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Will attend pre networking session**: Choose "Yes" to indicate that you will be attending the pre-networking session.
* **Networking goals**: Describe what you hope to achieve during the session.
* **Dietary requirements**: If you have any food restrictions, enter them here.
* **Accessibility needs**: If you require any accommodations, describe them here.
* **Consent to share contact information**: Choose "Yes" to indicate that you consent to share your name and email with other attendees.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not already RSVPed for this event before completing this form.
* If you're unsure if you've already RSVPed, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can best meet your needs and expectations during the pre-networking session.
