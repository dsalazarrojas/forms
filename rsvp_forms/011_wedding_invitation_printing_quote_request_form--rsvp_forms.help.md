# Wedding Invitation Printing Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Invitation Printing Quote Request Form is designed to help us gather information about your wedding invitation printing needs. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary details for a quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred invitation style.
2. Choose the number of invitations you need.
3. Select the printing style that suits your needs.
4. Choose the quantity of invitations.
5. Select the type of paper and materials used.
6. Choose the font and font sizes.
7. Indicate if color printing is needed.
8. Select the size of the paper.
9. Choose the orientation of the invitation.
10. Select the finish type.
11. Choose the type of ribbon or embellishments.
