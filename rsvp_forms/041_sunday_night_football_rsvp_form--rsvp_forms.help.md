# sunday-night-football-rsvp-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for players to RSVP to Sunday Night Football events, allowing us to get an accurate headcount and plan the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each of the 'Availability' fields, indicating whether you will be available for the respective time slot(s).
2. Enter your name in the 'player-name' field.
3. Enter your email address in the 'email' field.
4. Enter your phone number in the 'phone' field.
5. Click the 'Submit' button to submit your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information** (`player_information`, select_multiple, **Required**): 
  * Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you will be attending the event.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, **Required**):
  * Enter your full name in the text field.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, **Optional**):
  * Choose a date from the calendar to indicate your availability. If you don't have a specific date in mind, you can leave this field blank.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, **Optional**):
  * Choose a specific time slot you are available. Please note that multiple time slots are not supported.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**):
  * Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**):
  * Enter your phone number.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, **Optional**):
  * Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are available for each of the available time slots.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, **Optional**):
  * Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are available for each of the available time slots.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form honestly, as it will help us get an accurate headcount and plan the event accordingly.
* If you need to cancel your RSVP, please contact us as soon as possible so we can make necessary adjustments.
