# Spa Guest Event RSVP Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spa Guest Event RSVP Registration Form is a tool for guests to provide their RSVP information for a spa event. By filling out this form, you will help us manage your attendance, assign a suitable tool for the event, and make sure you are properly confirmed for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Guest Name**, which is your personal name.
2. Provide your **Email**, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. Enter your **Phone**, which will be used for communication purposes.
4. Choose your **RSVP Date** for the event.
5. Select your desired **RSVP Time** for the event.
6. Indicate the number of **Guest Count**, including yourself.
7. If you have any **Special Request**, feel free to provide it.
8. Optionally, you can add any **Comments** you'd like to share.
9. Choose the **Assigned Tool** that suits your needs (chatjimmy or other).
9. Confirm your **RSVP Status** by selecting 'True' or 'False' from the multi-select menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Enter your personal name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **RSVP Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the event.
* **RSVP Time**: Select the time you plan to attend the event.
* **Guest Count**: Indicate the number of guests you will be bringing with you, including yourself.
* **Special Request**: If you have any special requirements or requests, feel free to add them here.
* **Comments**: Optionally, add any comments or additional information about your attendance.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool that suits your needs from the provided options (chatjimmy or other).
* **Confirm**: Confirm your RSVP status by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
