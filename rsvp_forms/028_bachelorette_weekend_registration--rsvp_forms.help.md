# Bachelorette Weekend Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from guests attending the bachelorette weekend event. Please complete the form carefully to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information for all required fields.
2. Fill out each section according to the instructions provided for each field.
3. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name** (guest_name): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email Address** (email): Provide your email address for event updates and communications.
* **Phone Number** (phone): Enter your contact number for coordination purposes.
* **Relationship to Bride** (relationship_to_bride): Choose how you know the bride (e.g., Maid of Honor, Bridesmaid, Sister, Friend, etc.).
* **Will You Attend** (attendance_status): Select your attendance status for the event.
	+ Yes, attending full weekend
	+ Yes, attending select events
	+ No, cannot attend
* **Expected Arrival Date** (arrival_date): If you are traveling from out of town, indicate your expected arrival date. This field is not required.
* **Expected Departure Date** (departure_date): If you are traveling from out of town, indicate your expected departure date. This field is not required.
* **Need Accommodation** (accommodation_needed): Check "Yes" if you need help with lodging; otherwise, select "No" or "Maybe" if you're unsure.
* **Accommodation Preference** (accommodation_preference): If you need accommodation, select your preferred type (Shared rental house, Hotel room, Sharing room with others, No preference).
* **Need Transportation** (transportation_needed): Check "Yes" if you require transportation from the airport or for local transportation; otherwise, select "No" or "Maybe" if you have your own transportation.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (dietary_restrictions): If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, describe them in this field.
* **T-Shirt Size** (t_shirt_size): If you're interested in receiving a party T-shirt, select your size (XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL, Do not want shirt).
* **Activities You Want to Join** (event_activities): Select all activities you are interested in participating in (Welcome dinner, Spa day, Pool or beach day, Night out, Brunch, Games and activities, Farewell breakfast).
* **Budget Range for Weekend** (budget_range): Choose your budget comfort level (Budget friendly, Moderate, No budget concerns).
* **Contributing to Group Gift** (contributing_to_gift): Check "True" if you'll contribute to the bride's gift; otherwise, select "False" or "Maybe".
* **Gift Contribution Amount** (gift_contribution_amount): If you're contributing to the gift, provide an approximate amount in dollars. This field is not required.
* **Special Requests or Needs** (special_requests): If you have any special requests or needs, describe them in this field. This field is not required.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (emergency_contact): In case of an emergency during the event, provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Message for the Bride** (message_for_bride): Share a note or wish for the bride.
* **Date of RSVP** (rsvp_deadline): This field is not a fillable field but a date indicating the deadline to complete the form.

Please note that some fields might seem redundant or have unclear labels, but they have been kept as they are in the original form.
