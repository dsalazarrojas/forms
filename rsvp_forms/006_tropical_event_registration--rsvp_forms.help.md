# Tropical Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a tropical event. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the attendees, including their contact information, event preferences, and dietary restrictions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your attendee information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* **Mobile Phone**: Enter your mobile phone number.
	* **Address**: Enter your address.
2. Choose your event preferences:
	* **RSVP**: Indicate if you will be attending the event.
	* **Event Date Format**: Choose the date format you prefer for the event.
	* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you are attending.
3. Provide your dietary information:
	* **Food Preferences**: Choose your food preferences (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Gluten-Free).
	* **Dietary Restrictions**: Enter any dietary restrictions you have.
4. Enter any special requests or comments:
	* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you may have.
	* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about your attendance.
	* **RSVP Method**: Indicate how you will be attending the event (In-Person, Online).
	* **RSVP Comment**: Enter any additional comments about your RSVP.
5. Provide contact information:
	* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information you would like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Mobile Phone** (Text): Enter your mobile phone number.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address.
* **RSVP** (Select One): Indicate if you will be attending the event.
* **Event Date Format** (Select One): Choose the date format you prefer for the event.
* **Event Type** (Select One): Choose the type of event you are attending.
* **Event Time** (Time): Enter the event time (not required).
* **Note** (Note): Enter any additional notes about your attendance.
* **Consent** (Select Multiple): Indicate if you consent to certain terms.
* **Food Preferences** (Select Multiple): Choose your food preferences (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Gluten-Free).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Text): Enter any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Special Requests** (Text): Enter any special requests you may have.
* **RSVP Method** (Select Multiple): Indicate how you will be attending the event (In-Person, Online).
* **RSVP Comment** (Text): Enter any additional comments about your RSVP.
* **Contact Info** (Text): Enter any additional contact information you would like to provide.
* **Event Details** (Text): Enter any additional event details.
* **Attendee Info** (Text): Enter any information about the attendee (not required).
* **Event Location** (Text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Date Format** (Select One): Choose the date format you prefer for the event.
* **Attendee Attendance** (Select Multiple): Choose who will be attending the event (Attendee 1, Attendee 2).
