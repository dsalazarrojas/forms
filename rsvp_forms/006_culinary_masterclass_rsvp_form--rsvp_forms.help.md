# Culinary Masterclass RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP for a Culinary Masterclass event. Please fill out the form to let us know if you can attend and provide any necessary details about your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **First Name** and **Last Name** (optional but recommended for our records).
2. Enter your **Email** address to receive updates and notifications about the event.
3. If you have a **Phone** number, please provide it for us to contact you.
4. Let us know about any **Dietary Restrictions** you may have, such as food allergies or intolerances.
5. Select your **Payment Method** for the event (if applicable).
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** (if applicable).
7. Choose your **Time of Arrival** (if applicable).
8. Add any **Additional Comments** you'd like to share with us.
9. Finally, **Agree** to the terms and conditions of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification or documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification or documents. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where we can reach you.
* **Dietary Restrictions (Single Select)**: Select any dietary restriction you may have from the list.
* **Dietary Restrictions (Multi Select)**: Select multiple dietary restrictions you may have from the list.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you plan to pay for the event, if applicable.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth if you'd like to share it with us.
* **Time of Arrival**: Choose your expected time of arrival for the event.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Agreement**: Select all options that apply to your participation in the event. 

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid any issues with your RSVP.
