# Kids Christmas Party Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about children attending a Christmas party at school. It helps us understand who will be attending, their contact details, and any special requirements or needs we need to be aware of.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's name in the "Child Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field, in case we need to contact you about any party details.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field, in case we need to contact you about any party updates.
4. Enter your child's date of birth in the "Child Date of Birth" field, using the mm/dd/yyyy format.
5. If your child has any dietary restrictions, such as being gluten-free, lactose-free, or nut-free, select these options in the "Child Dietary Restrictions" field.
6. If your child has any food allergies, select the relevant options in the "Child Food Allergies" field.
7. Enter the number of children attending the party in the "Number of Children" field.
8. Enter your name in the "Guardian Name" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "Guardian Contact Email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Guardian Contact Phone" field.
11. Select your relationship to the child (e.g. Mother, Guardian) in the "Relationship" field.
12. If you have any message for the teacher, enter it in the "Message to Teacher" field.
13. Enter the teacher's name in the "Teacher Name" field.
14. Enter the teacher's email address in the "Teacher Email" field.
15. Enter the teacher's phone number in the "Teacher Phone" field.
16. Select the teacher's relationship to the child (e.g. Other (Specify)).
17. If your child has any special needs or requirements, enter them in the "Special Needs" field.
18. If you would like to sign off on behalf of the teacher, select "None" in the "Teacher Signature" field.
19. Enter the date of the teacher's signature in the "Teacher Signature Date" field.
20. If your child has any additional special needs or requirements, enter them in the "Special Needs 2", "Special Needs 3", "Special Needs 4", "Special Needs 5", "Special Needs 6", or "Special Needs 7" fields.
