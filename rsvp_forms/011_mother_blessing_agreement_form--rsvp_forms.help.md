# Mother Blessing Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and consent for an event. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure that participants provide their consent and agreement for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and consent from participants before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form according to the required fields and options provided.
3. Review and sign the form to confirm your participation in the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Consent
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, text, optional): This field is for the participant to provide their consent for the event. Please type 'True' if they give their consent.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is for the participant's name. Please enter their full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): This field is for the participant's address. Please enter their full address as it appears on their official documents.
* **Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): This field is for the event date. Please select the date of the event as it will occur.
* **Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): This field is for the event time. Please select the time of the event as it will occur.
* **Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): This field is for the event location. Please enter the location of the event where it will take place.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or signatures required by the event organizers.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): This field is for the participant's email address. Please enter their official email address.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the participant's phone number. Please enter their phone number as it appears on their official documents.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional event details or notes. Please enter any relevant information about the event.
* **Participant Consent Date** (`participant_consent_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date of the participant's consent. Please select the date of their consent.
* **Participant Consent Time** (`participant_consent_time`, time, optional): This field is for the time of the participant's consent. Please select the time of their consent.
* **Signature** (`participant_signature`, select_one, optional): This field is for the participant to confirm their agreement to participate. Please select 'True' if they agree.
* **Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date of the participant's signature.
* **Signature Time** (`participant_signature_time`, time, optional): This field is for the time of the participant's signature.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, optional): This field is for the participant to provide any additional agreement or comments. Please enter any relevant information about their participation.
