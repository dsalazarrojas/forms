# Waiver Of Notice Of Meeting Of The Board Of Directors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the attendance status of a board member at a meeting, along with the reason for their absence and their expected return date. It's designed to be a simple and straightforward way to record information about a board member's attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting.
2. **Full Name**: Enter the full name of the board member.
3. **Reason For Absence**: If the board member was absent, explain the reason for their absence.
4. **Expected Return Date**: If the board member is absent, enter their expected return date.
5. **Will Attend**: Select "True" if the board member will attend the meeting, "False" if they will not attend, or "Maybe" if their attendance is uncertain.
6. **Reason For Not Attending**: If the board member will not attend, explain the reason for their absence.
7. **Will Missed Meeting**: Select "True" if the board member will miss the meeting, "False" if they will attend, or "Maybe" if their attendance is uncertain.
8. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the board member's attendance status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Date**: A date field where you can enter the date of the meeting.
  - Type: date
  - Required: No
- **Full Name**: A text field where you can enter the full name of the board member.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
- **Reason For Absence**: A text field where you can enter the reason for the board member's absence.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
- **Expected Return Date**: A date field where you can enter the expected return date of the board member.
  - Type: date
  - Required: No
- **Will Attend**: A dropdown field where you can select "True", "False", or "Maybe" to indicate if the board member will attend the meeting.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: No
- **Reason For Not Attending**: A text field where you can enter the reason for the board member's absence (if they will not attend).
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
- **Will Missed Meeting**: A dropdown field where you can select "True", "False", or "Maybe" to indicate if the board member will miss the meeting.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: No
- **Comments**: A text field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the board member's attendance status.
  - Type: text
  - Required: No
