# Maritime Exhibition Invitation & RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the invitation and RSVP process for a maritime exhibition. It provides a structured way to collect information from attendees, such as exhibition name, date, venue, and RSVP status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Exhibition Name** field with the name of the maritime exhibition you are inviting guests to.
2. Select the **Start Date** and **End Date** fields to specify the duration of the exhibition.
3. Enter the **Venue** where the exhibition will take place.
4. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your personal details.
5. Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you are attending the exhibition by filling in the **Is Attending** field.
6. If you are attending, fill in the **Confirmation Date** field to confirm your attendance.
7. If you have any comments or additional information to share, type it in the **Comment** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Name** (`Exhibition Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the maritime exhibition you are inviting guests to.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required): Select the start date of the exhibition.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required): Select the end date of the exhibition.
* **Venue** (`Venue`, text, required): Enter the venue where the exhibition will take place.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (not required but recommended for contact purposes).
* **Is Attending** (`Is Attending`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you are attending the exhibition.
* **Confirmation Date** (`Confirmation Date`, date, optional): If you are attending, fill in the confirmation date to confirm your attendance.
* **Comment** (`Comment`, text, optional): Type in any comments or additional information you would like to share.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth communication and organization for the exhibition.
* If you are attending, please fill in the **Confirmation Date** field to confirm your attendance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the exhibition organizers directly.
