# Study Leader Appreciation RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to express appreciation to study leaders for their hard work and dedication. It allows guests to send a message of thanks to the study leaders and helps to gather information about the number of guests attending.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the appreciation message in the "Study Leader Appreciation" field.
2. Enter the number of guests attending the event in the "Guest Count" field.
3. Select who you will be attending the event with from the "Who will you be attending the event with?" field.
4. Enter a message of thanks in the "Message of Thanks" field.
5. Click the submit button to send the message to the study leaders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Study Leader Appreciation** (`event_name`, text, required: false): Enter a brief title for your message of appreciation to the study leaders.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, required: false): Enter the number of guests attending the event with you.
* **Who will you be attending the event with?** (`attending`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the person(s) you will be attending the event with from the options provided.
* **Message of Thanks** (`message`, note, required: false): Enter a personal message of thanks to the study leaders.

## Tips
* Please enter a clear and concise message of thanks to the study leaders.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask your event organizer for guidance.
* Make sure to enter accurate information about the number of guests attending the event.
