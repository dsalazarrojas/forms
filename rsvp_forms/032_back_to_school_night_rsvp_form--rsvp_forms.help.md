# Back To School Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Back To School Night RSVP Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their attendance and preferences for the Back To School Night event. This form helps the school administration to gauge the number of attendees and to tailor the event to the needs of students and their families.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
2. Provide your email address for confirmation purposes.
3. Enter your phone number for contact information.
4. Enter your child's name.
5. Select the current grade level of your child.
6. Confirm if you will attend the event.
7. Select the date you plan to attend.
8. Choose a preferred time slot for the event.
9. Enter the number of adults attending.
10. Indicate if both parents or guardians will attend.
11. Confirm if childcare is needed during the event.
12. If childcare is needed, enter the ages of the children.
13. Indicate if a language interpreter is needed.
14. If a language interpreter is needed, select the language required.
15. Select areas you would like to discuss with the teacher.
16. Enter any questions you have for the teacher.
17. Express your interest in volunteering.
18. Select your preferred contact method for communication.
19. Add any additional comments.
20. Set the date of your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for confirmation purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number for future communication.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your child's name.
- **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, required): Select the current grade level of your child.
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, optional): If you know your child's teacher, enter their name. Otherwise, you can leave this blank.
- **Will You Attend** (`attendance_status`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you will attend the event.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to attend.
- **Preferred Time Slot** (`event_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose a time slot for the event.
- **Number of Adults Attending** (`number_attending`, `number`, required): Enter the number of adults attending.
- **Will Both Parents Attend** (`both_parents_attending`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if both parents or guardians will attend.
- **Need Childcare During Event** (`childcare_needed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if childcare is needed.
- **Ages of Children Needing Care** (`children_ages`, `text`, optional): If childcare is needed, list the ages of the children.
- **Need Language Interpreter** (`interpreter_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if a language interpreter is needed.
- **Interpreter Language Needed** (`interpreter_language`, `select_one`, optional): If a language interpreter is needed, select the required language.
- **Topics You Want to Discuss** (`topics_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the areas you would like to discuss with the teacher.
- **Questions for Teacher** (`questions_for_teacher`, `text`, optional): Enter any questions you have for the teacher.
- **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Express your interest in volunteering.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred contact method for communication.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
- **Date of RSVP** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required): Set the date of your RSVP.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your responses are recorded correctly.
* If you need any special accommodations, such as childcare or a language interpreter, please select the corresponding options to help us better serve you and your family.
* You can select more than one topic to discuss with the teacher.
* You can enter multiple questions for the teacher.
* If you select to volunteer, we will contact you separately to discuss further details.
* If you select a contact method other than email, please ensure that we can contact you via that method.
* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us better organize the event.
