# Wedding Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record the details of your attendance at a wedding. It helps the wedding hosts and planners to plan for the event and ensures that everyone who has expressed interest in attending is accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name.
2.  Indicate how many people are planning to attend with you.
3.  Select from the list of guests you are attending with.
4.  Choose your RSVP status.
5.  Enter the date and time you plan to attend.
6.  Add any additional message you would like to include.
7.  Select your preferred RSVP status from the options provided.
8.  Enter the date of your RSVP form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Name** (`<name>`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the wedding invitation and guest lists.
*  **How Many Are Attending?** (`<how_many_attending>`, number, optional): If you are attending alone, enter 1. If you are bringing multiple guests, enter the total number of people in your party.
*  **Name** (`<name>`, text, optional): Enter the name of each guest attending with you. You can select multiple guests from the list provided.
*  **Email** (`<email>`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that the hosts can contact you for any updates or last-minute details.
*  **Phone** (`<phone>`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that the hosts can contact you for any updates or last-minute details.
*  **Date of Attendance** (`<date_of_attendance>`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the wedding.
*  **Time of Attendance** (`<time_of_attendance>`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the wedding.
*  **Message** (`<message>`, note, optional): Write any special message or comment you would like to share with the hosts.
*  **Number of People Attending** (`<number_of_people_attending>`, number, optional): Confirm the total number of people in your party attending the wedding.
*  **Attending Participants** (`<attending_participants>`, select multiple, required): Select the guests you are attending with from the provided list.
*  **RSVP Form Submission Status** (`<rsvp_form_submission_status>`, select one, required): Indicate your response to the invitation.
*  **RSVP Form Submission Date** (`<rsvp_form_submission_date>`, date, optional): Enter the date of your RSVP form submission.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill out all required fields.
*   Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid confusion.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the hosts for clarification.
