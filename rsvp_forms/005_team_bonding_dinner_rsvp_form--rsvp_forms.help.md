# Team Bonding Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect RSVPs from team members for a team bonding dinner event. Please fill out the form with your information to help us plan the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Name.
2. Enter your Email.
3. Select your RSVP status (Attending, Not Attending, or Maybe).
4. If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them in the text field.
5. If you have any preferences, enter them in the text field.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the text field.
7. Enter your Phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, **not required**): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you.
* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, `select_one`, **not required**): Select whether you are Attending, Not Attending, or Maybe.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, `text`, **not required**): If you have any food restrictions, enter them here (e.g. vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Preferences** (`Preferences`, `text`, **not required**): If you have any preferences for the event (e.g. table preference, seat preference, etc.), enter them here.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, **not required**): If you have any additional comments or notes for us, enter them here.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, **not required**): Enter your phone number if you want us to be able to contact you with any last-minute updates or reminders.
