# Event Viewing Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering your interest in or attending an event. It has four pages with various fields to collect relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, select whether you plan to RSVP to the event in person.
2. On the second page, choose the session you would like to attend.
3. On the third page, provide any additional details about your attendance in the note field.
4. On the fourth page, enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Viewing Registration - In Person RSVP**: This field allows you to indicate whether you plan to attend the event in person. Select "Yes" to attend and "No" if you do not.
* **Event Viewing Registration - Session Selection**: This field allows you to choose which session you would like to attend. Select the session that best suits your needs.
* **Event Viewing Registration - Attendee Details (Note)**: This field is for providing any additional information about your attendance. Use it to share your thoughts, questions, or concerns.
* **Event Viewing Registration - Email**: This field requires your email address for communication purposes. Please enter a valid email address to complete your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
- Ensure your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
