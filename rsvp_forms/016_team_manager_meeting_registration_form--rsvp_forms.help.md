# Team Manager Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVP information and session details from team members for team manager meetings. Please fill out this form to register your attendance and provide any necessary comments or feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct RSVP status (Active or Inactive) that applies to you.
2. Choose how you are attending the meeting (Manager or Team Member).
3. Fill out the session details, including date, time, and location.
4. Add any comments or feedback you have for the manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Details**: Select your RSVP status, either Active or Inactive, to indicate your availability to attend the meeting.
* **RSVP Status**: Choose whether you are actively attending the meeting or not.
* **Session Details**: Enter the date of the meeting you are attending.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the meeting you are attending.
* **Session Location**: Enter the location of the meeting you are attending.
* **RSVP Comments**: Add any comments or feedback you have about the meeting.
* **Manager Comments**: Add any comments or feedback you have for the manager.
* **Team Member Availability**: Enter the number of team members you expect to be available to attend the meeting.

Note: The form is not required, and you can fill it out as needed.
