# vip_experience_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vip_experience_registration form is used to collect information from individuals or teams registering for a VIP experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the VIP experience name and details in the `vip_experience` field.
2. Select your RSVP status by choosing one of the options in the `vip_experience_rsvp` field.
3. Enter your contact information, including your phone number, email address, and notes in the `vip_experience_phone`, `vip_experience_email`, `vip_experience_notes`, `vip_experience_rsvp_notes`, `vip_experience_venue_email`, and `vip_experience_rsvp_host_email` fields.
4. Provide additional details about your experience in the `vip_experience_venue`, `vip_experience_venue_other`, `vip_experience_venue_phone`, `vip_experience_venue_address`, `vip_experience_additional_info`, and `vip_experience_additional_info_other` fields.
5. If you have any further questions or concerns, you can provide notes in the `vip_experience_rsvp_host_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vip_experience**: The name and details of the VIP experience you're registering for.
* **rsvp_date**: The date of the VIP experience.
* **vip_experience_time**: The time of the VIP experience.
* **vip_experience_duration**: The duration of the VIP experience.
* **vip_experience_description**: A brief description of the VIP experience.
* **vip_experience_name**: Your name or the name of your team registering for the experience.
* **vip_experience_phone**: Your phone number for the experience organizers to contact you.
* **vip_experience_email**: Your email address for the experience organizers to contact you.
* **vip_experience_notes**: Any additional notes about your experience.
* **vip_experience_availability**: Your availability for the VIP experience, choose from "VIP" or "Other".
* **vip_experience_availability_other**: If you select "Other", provide a brief description of your availability.
* **vip_experience_rsvp**: Your RSVP status, choose from "RSVP Yes", "RSVP No", or "RSVP Maybe".
* **vip_experience_rsvp_notes**: Any additional notes about your RSVP status.
* **vip_experience_additional_info**: Any additional information about your experience.
* **vip_experience_additional_info_other**: If you have any additional information, provide a brief description.
* **vip_experience_venue**: The name of the venue for the VIP experience.
* **vip_experience_venue_other**: If you select "Other", provide a brief description of the venue.
* **vip_experience_venue_email**: The email address of the venue.
* **vip_experience_venue_phone**: The phone number of the venue.
* **vip_experience_venue_notes**: Any additional notes about the venue.
* **vip_experience_rsvp_host**: The name of the person or team hosting the VIP experience.
* **vip_experience_rsvp_host_email**: The email address of the person or team hosting the experience.
* **vip_experience_rsvp_host_phone**: The phone number of the person or team hosting the experience.
* **vip_experience_rsvp_host_notes**: Any additional notes about the host.
* **vip_experience_venue_address**: The address of the venue.
* **vip_experience_venue_address** is not actually defined in the form yaml but was included in the output as a field for the sake of this example.
* **vip_experience_venue_address_other**: If you have any information about the venue address, provide a brief description.

Note: The fields that have the suffix `_other` are used when the "Other" option is selected in the preceding field.
