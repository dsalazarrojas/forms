The Nursing Home Event Registration form is a data collection form used to track events and visitors within a nursing home setting. Its purpose is to gather information about events, attendees, and those responsible for submitting the registration. This form will help the nursing home staff to monitor and prepare for events, ensuring that everyone's needs are catered to and safety is maintained. 

Please review the following sections carefully to ensure that you complete the form correctly:

## Purpose
The Nursing Home Event Registration form is used to register events, track attendees, and collect information from those submitting the event details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all fields with a required status of "True" are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): The title of the event, e.g., "Family Visit" or "Birthday Party".
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): The date of the event, in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): The time of the event, if known.
- **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): The name of the attendee or visitor.
- **Attendee Relationship** (`attendee_relationship`, select_multiple, required): The relationship of the attendee to a resident (Resident, Guest).
- **Attendee DOB** (`attendee_dob`, date, optional): The date of birth of the attendee.
- **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the attendee.
- **RSVP Name** (`rsvp_name`, text, required): The name of the person RSVPing.
- **RSVP Relationship** (`rsvp_relationship`, select_multiple, required): The relationship of the RSVPer to a resident (Resident, Guest).
- **RSVP DOB** (`rsvp_dob`, date, optional): The date of birth of the RSVPer.
- **RSVP Phone** (`rsvp_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the RSVPer.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Additional comments about the event.
- **Submitter** (`submitter_name`, text, required): The name of the person submitting the event details.
- **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): The email of the person submitting the event details.
- **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the person submitting the event details.
- **Submitter Relationship** (`submitter_relationship`, select_multiple, required): The relationship of the submitter to a resident (Resident, Guest).
- **Submitter DOB** (`submitter_dob`, date, optional): The date of birth of the submitter.
- **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, text, optional): Additional notes from the submitter.
- **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required): The address of the nursing home.
- **Facility City** (`facility_city`, text, required): The city of the nursing home.
- **Facility Zip** (`facility_zip`, number, required): The zip code of the nursing home.

Please ensure you review and complete the form accurately and thoroughly. 

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields have accurate and complete information.
- Use the date format of mm/dd/yyyy for date fields.
- Use the correct email format for email fields.
