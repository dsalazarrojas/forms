# strategic_planning_meeting_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Planning Meeting RSVP Form is a tool used to track attendee participation in strategic planning meetings. This form helps meeting organizers collect information on who will attend, their job titles, organizations, and RSVP responses to ensure accurate planning and communication.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the date of the meeting you will attend.
2. Choose the time of the meeting you will be attending.
3. Select whether you will be attending the meeting.
4. Enter your name as it should be displayed on the meeting agenda.
5. Enter your email address to ensure we can reach you with meeting updates.
6. Enter your organization or company name.
7. Enter your job title.
8. Select your RSVP response to the meeting (True/False or Maybe).
9. (Optional) Add a comment to share any additional information with the meeting organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required/optional: false): Enter the date of the meeting you will attend.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required/optional: false): Choose the time of the meeting you will be attending.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: true): Select whether you will be attending the meeting (Yes/No).
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: true): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the meeting agenda.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address to ensure we can reach you with meeting updates.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: true): Enter your organization or company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: true): Enter your job title.
* **RSVP Response** (`rsvp_response`, select_one, required: true): Select your RSVP response to the meeting (True/False or Maybe).
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, required: false): Add a comment to share any additional information with the meeting organizers.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate information to help us plan the meeting efficiently.
* Please note that your RSVP response will be sent to the meeting organizers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
