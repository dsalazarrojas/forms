# Holiday Party Food Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from employees regarding their preferences and requirements for the holiday party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Party Details section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select your response to the question of whether you will be attending the party (Yes/No).
3. If you have dietary restrictions, select the options that apply to you.
4. If you have allergies, select the options that apply to you.
5. Choose your preferred cuisine options.
6. Enter your email address so we can contact you with party updates.
7. Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
8. Select the date and time you plan to attend the party.
9. Add any special requests or notes you have.
10. Enter the number of people in your party group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Party Details** (Party Details, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about your party.
  - **RSVP** (RSVP, select_one, required): Choose whether you will be attending the party (Yes/No).
  - **Dietary Restrictions** (Dietary Restrictions, select_multiple, required): Select the dietary restrictions that apply to you.
  - **Allergies** (Allergies, select_multiple, required): Select any allergies you have.
  - **Cuisine Choices** (Cuisine Choices, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred cuisine options.
  - **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with party updates.
  - **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to reach you.
  - **RSVP Date** (RSVP Date, date, required): Select the date you plan to attend the party.
  - **RSVP Time** (RSVP Time, time, required): Select the time you plan to attend the party.
  - **Note** (Note, note, required): Add any special requests or notes you have.
  - **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): This will automatically be filled in based on the party details, but you can change it if needed.
  - **Event Time** (Event Time, time, required): This will automatically be filled in based on the party details, but you can change it if needed.
  - **Special Requests** (Special Requests, text, required): Add any special requests or notes you have.
  - **Party Size** (Party Size, number, required): Enter the number of people in your party group.
