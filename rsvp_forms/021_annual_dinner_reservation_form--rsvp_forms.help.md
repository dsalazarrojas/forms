# Annual Dinner Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather details about attendees for a special event, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields (marked as "true" in the form).
* Enter your full name, email address, and other requested information accurately.
* Select your preferred options for reservation type, date, seating, table location, meal selection, and payment method.
* If applicable, list all guest names, select any dietary restrictions, and provide dietary details.
* Answer any other questions as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address for confirmation and event updates.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your best contact number.
* **Company/Organization** (4): If applicable, enter your company or organization name.
* **Reservation Type** (5): Select the type of reservation:
	+ Individual Seat
	+ Couple
	+ Full Table
	+ Partial Table
	+ VIP Table
* **Number of Guests** (6): Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **Guest Names** (7): List all attendees (not required).
* **Preferred Date** (8): Select your preferred date.
* **Preferred Seating Time** (9): Select your preferred seating time:
	+ Early Seating
	+ Standard Seating
	+ Late Seating
	+ No Preference
* **Table Location Preference** (10): Select your preferred table location:
	+ No Preference
	+ Near Stage
	+ Near Entrance
	+ Near Window
	+ Quiet Area
	+ Center of Room
* **Dietary Restrictions** (11): Select all applicable dietary restrictions:
	+ None
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Dairy-Free
	+ Nut Allergies
	+ Shellfish Allergies
	+ Kosher
	+ Halal
	+ Other
* **Dietary Details** (12): If any dietary restrictions were selected, provide more information.
* **Meal Selection** (13): Choose your preferred meal entree:
	+ Beef
	+ Chicken
	+ Fish
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Chef's Choice
* **Beverage Package** (14): Select your preferred beverage package:
	+ Open Bar
	+ Wine and Beer
	+ Wine Only
	+ Non-Alcoholic
	+ No Preference
* **Special Occasion** (15): If you're celebrating a special occasion, mark it:
	+ None
	+ Birthday
	+ Anniversary
	+ Promotion
	+ Retirement
	+ Other
* **Accessibility Requirements** (16): If you need special accommodations, enter information here.
* **Parking Needed** (17): Indicate if you need parking:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Transportation Needed** (18): Indicate if you need transportation assistance:
	+ 'True'
	+ 'False'
* **Payment Method** (19): Select how you'll pay:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ Invoice
	+ Check
	+ At Door
* **Special Requests** (20): If you have any other needs or requests, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (21): Enter an emergency contact name.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (22): Enter an emergency contact number.
* **Photo Consent** (23): Indicate if you consent to photos for marketing:
	+ Yes, I Consent
	+ No, Please Do Not
