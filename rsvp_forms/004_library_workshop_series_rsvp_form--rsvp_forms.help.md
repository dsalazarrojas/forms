# Library Workshop Series RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from attendees who are interested in attending the Library Workshop Series. This form will help us confirm their attendance, details such as name, email, and contact information, as well as workshop preferences like date, time, language, and location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you are interested in attending. This will help us track which workshop you are interested in.
2. **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be listed on our records.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with further details.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
5. **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop you are interested in attending. Please use the format mm/dd/yy.
6. **Workshop Time**: Select the time of the workshop you are interested in attending.
7. **Workshop Time Zone**: Select the time zone of the workshop location.
8. **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop venue.
9. **Workshop Description**: Enter a brief description of the workshop to help us better understand your interests.
10. **Workshop Language**: Select the language you prefer the workshop to be conducted in.
11. **Workshop Length**: Enter the length of the workshop you are interested in (in hours).
12. **Minimalist** (optional): If you would like to receive minimal updates and information from us, select this option.
13. **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your workshop preferences.
14. **Additional Comments** (optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
15. **Confirm Workshop**: Confirm your attendance at the workshop by selecting the "True" option.
