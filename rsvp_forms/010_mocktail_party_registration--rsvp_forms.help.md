# Mocktail Party Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from users to help plan and prepare for the mocktail party.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out your User Details.
2. Next, provide Event Details about the party.
3. Then, enter the Headcount (number of guests attending).
4. Select your drink preferences from the options provided.
5. Finally, confirm your attendance with the options below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: This field is for entering your name and any other identifying information. Please enter your first and last name, or the name of the contact person for your group.
* **Event Details**: Please provide a brief description of the party or event, such as the theme or occasion.
* **Headcount**: Enter the total number of guests attending the party. This will help us with party planning and preparations.
* **Preferences**: Choose one or more of the drink options provided. Select "None" if you do not desire any drinks.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm your attendance by selecting "True" if you are attending, "False" if you are not attending, or "Maybe" if your attendance is uncertain.
