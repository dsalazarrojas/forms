# Joint Spiritual Revival Invitation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Spiritual Revival Invitation form is designed to collect RSVPs from guests for a joint spiritual revival event. Please ensure you have not completed this form in the past to provide accurate information and avoid duplication of effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the RSVP Type field with your chosen option.
2.  Enter the number of guests attending in the Guest Count field.
3.  If you are not a guest, you can skip this field.
4.  Enter the name(s) of the guest(s) in the Guest Name field.
5.  Enter your contact email in the Contact Email field.
6.  Enter your contact phone number in the Contact Phone field (optional).
7.  Select the date of your RSVP in the RSVP Date field.
8.  Choose the time of your RSVP in the RSVP Time field.
9.  If you have any additional comments about your RSVP, enter them in the RSVP Comments field (optional).
10.  Enter any additional details about your guest(s) in the Guest Details field (optional).
11.  Select the type of RSVP form submitted in the RSVP Form Type field.
12.  Enter the date and time of form submission in the RSVP Form Submitted At field.
13.  Enter your name in the RSVP Form Submitter field.
14.  Enter your email in the RSVP Form Submitter Email field (optional).
15.  Enter your phone number in the RSVP Form Submitter Phone field (optional).
16.  Enter any additional comments about your form submission in the RSVP Form Submitter Comments field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Type** (`RSVPType`, `select_one`, **required**):
	+ Select the type of RSVP you are submitting.
	+ This field is required to ensure accurate tracking of RSVPs.
* **Guest Count** (`GuestCount`, `number`, **required**):
	+ Enter the number of guests attending the event.
	+ This field is required to ensure accurate headcount for the event.
* **Guest Name** (`GuestName`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ Enter the name(s) of the guest(s) attending the event.
	+ This field is optional and only required if you are a guest.
* **Contact Email** (`ContactEmail`, `email`, **required**):
	+ Enter your contact email address.
	+ This field is required for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`ContactPhone`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
	+ This field is optional and not required for form submission.
* **RSVP Date** (`RSVPDate`, `date`, **required**):
	+ Select the date of your RSVP.
	+ This field is required to track RSVPs by date.
* **RSVP Time** (`RSVPTime`, `time`, **required**):
	+ Choose the time of your RSVP.
	+ This field is required to track RSVPs by time.
* **RSVP Comments** (`RSVPComments`, `note`, **optional**):
	+ Enter any additional comments about your RSVP.
	+ This field is optional and not required for form submission.
* **Guest Details** (`GuestDetails`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ Enter any additional details about your guest(s).
	+ This field is optional and not required for form submission.
* **RSVP Form Type** (`RSVPFormType`, `select_multiple`, **required**):
	+ Select the type of RSVP form submitted.
	+ This field is required to track the type of form submission.
* **RSVP Form Submitted At** (`RSVPFormSubmittedAt`, `date`, **required**):
	+ Enter the date and time of form submission.
	+ This field is required for record-keeping.
* **RSVP Form Submitter** (`RSVPFormSubmitter`, `text`, **required**):
	+ Enter your name or title.
	+ This field is required to track who submitted the form.
* **RSVP Form Submitter Email** (`RSVPFormSubmitterEmail`, `email`, **optional**):
	+ Enter your email address.
	+ This field is optional and not required for form submission.
* **RSVP Form Submitter Phone** (`RSVPFormSubmitterPhone`, `text`, **optional**):
	+ Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is optional and not required for form submission.
* **RSVP Form Submitter Comments** (`RSVPFormSubmitterComments`, `note`, **optional**):
	+ Enter any additional comments about your form submission.
	+ This field is optional and not required for form submission.

## Tips

* Ensure you complete all required fields.
* Be accurate and truthful in your form submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
