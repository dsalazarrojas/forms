# Art Gallery Expansion Launch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Expansion Launch RSVP Form is a simple, user-friendly form designed to collect RSVP information from guests for the Art Gallery Expansion Launch event. This form will help us track attendance, dietary restrictions, and other relevant details for event planning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the form and fill out the required fields.
2. Select your RSVP date.
3. Enter your guest's name.
4. Enter your guest's email address.
5. Fill out the required fields for dietary restrictions and attendance status.
6. List the names of all guests attending.
7. Choose the event date you plan to attend.
8. Select any dietary restrictions or allergies.
9. Specify any additional dietary needs.
10. Indicate if you require any accessibility accommodations.
11. Describe your accessibility needs.
12. Indicate if you need parking and select your transportation method.
13. Check if you need hotel recommendations.
14. Select your areas of interest for the event.
15. Indicate if you're interested in making a donation or joining the gallery membership.
16. Choose how you heard about the event.
17. Add any additional comments or special requests.
18. Click submit to complete your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to submit your RSVP. This date will help us track your response accurately.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the primary guest.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter the email address we'll use for confirmation and event updates.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization or company you represent.
* **Title** (`title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes - Will Attend", "No - Cannot Attend", or "Maybe".
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **Guest Names List** (`guest_names_list`, text, optional): List the names of all guests attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, select_one, required): Select the event date you plan to attend: Opening Night - VIP Reception, Public Opening Day, Weekend Gala Event, or Closing Reception.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Choose any dietary restrictions or allergies you have: None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy, Kosher, Halal, Other.
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`, text, optional): Specify any additional dietary needs.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you need accessibility accommodations.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, text, optional): Describe your accessibility needs.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one, optional): Choose "True" if you need parking assistance.
* **Transportation Method** (`transportation_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you plan to arrive at the event: Personal Vehicle, Rideshare, Public Transit, Taxi, Walking.
* **Hotel Accommodation** (`hotel_accommodation`, select_one, optional): Choose "True" if you need hotel recommendations.
* **Interested In** (`interested_in`, select_multiple, optional): Select your areas of interest for the event: New Gallery Tours, Artist Meet and Greet, Wine and Cheese Reception, Live Music, Art Demonstrations, Silent Auction, Networking.
* **Donation Interest** (`donation_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose "True" if you're interested in making a donation.
* **Membership Interest** (`membership_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose "True" if you're interested in joining the gallery membership.
* **How Heard** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about the event: Email Invitation, Social Media, Website, Friend or Colleague, Press or Media, Other.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or special requests. 

Note: This help guide assumes that the event date and details will be displayed below the form for reference.
