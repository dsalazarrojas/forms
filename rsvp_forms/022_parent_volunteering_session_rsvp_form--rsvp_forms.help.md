# Parent Volunteering Session RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Volunteering Session RSVP Form is a tool used by parents to volunteer their child's session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event for which you wish to volunteer.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Indicate if you are able to volunteer for the session. Please select "Yes" if you are available and "No" if you are not.
6. Indicate your type of volunteering. Please select "Yes" if you can volunteer and "No" if you are not able to volunteer.
7. Choose the date you wish to RSVP by selecting a date from the calendar.
8. If you have any comments or additional information, please provide them in the comments field.
9. Click "Submit" to submit your form and "Cancel" to cancel your form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event** (`event`, `select_one`, required): Select the event for which you wish to volunteer. This is a required field to help the session organizers assign volunteers to the right sessions.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Volunteer Session** (`session`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate if you are able to volunteer for the session. Please select "Yes" if you are available and "No" if you are not.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your type of volunteering. Please select "Yes" if you can volunteer and "No" if you are not able to volunteer.
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you wish to RSVP by selecting a date from the calendar.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): If you have any comments or additional information, please provide them in this field.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, required): Click this button to cancel your form submission.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help the session organizers manage volunteer schedules and assignments efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the session organizers.
* Please note that this form is required for all volunteers.
