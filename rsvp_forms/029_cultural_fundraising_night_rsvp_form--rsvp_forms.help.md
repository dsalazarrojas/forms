# cultural_fundraising_night_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Cultural Fundraising Night RSVP Form" is designed to collect information from attendees for a cultural event or fundraising night. This form will help the organizers to manage the event's attendance and communicate with the attendees effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: The title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Contact Information**: Please enter your name and any additional contact information that may help us reach you.
* **Event Details**: If you have any specific requirements or requests for the event, please describe them here.
* **RSVP Information**: Please select one of the options to confirm if you will attend or not.
* **Message**: If you have any additional comments or messages for us, please enter them here.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address to ensure we can contact you back.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Event Attendance**: Please enter the date and time you plan to attend the event.
* **Event Time**: Please enter the time you plan to attend the event.
* **Name of Guests**: If you are attending with others, please list their names here.
* **Other Guests**: If there are any other guests attending with you, please list them here.
* **Contact Number**: If you would like us to contact you, please enter your contact number.
* **Name of Guests**: If you are attending with others, please list their names here.
* **RSVP Response**: Please select one of the options to confirm if you will attend or not.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided the correct information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* This form will be used to manage the event's attendance and communication process.
