# startup_networking_happy_hour_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from attendees about their RSVP status, preferences, and interests for a networking event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Specify your RSVP status in the "RSVP Status" field. You can choose "True", "False", or "Maybe" to indicate your attendance.
4. If you are attending, select a date and time for your RSVP in the "Date of RSVP" and "Time of RSVP" fields.
5. Enter any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field.
6. Specify your preferred networking style in the "Preferred Networking Style" field.
7. Enter your interests in the "Interests" field.
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
9. Select your preferred t-shirt size and color in the "T-Shirt Size" and "Shirt Color" fields.
10. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
11. Finally, submit the form to complete your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as an attendee.
- **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
- **RSVP Status** (`RSVP Status`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you are attending, "False" if you are not attending, and "Maybe" if you are unsure.
- **Date of RSVP** (`RSVP Date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to RSVP.
- **Time of RSVP** (`RSVP Time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to RSVP.
- **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the event planners.
- **Preferred Networking Style** (`Preferred Networking Style`, text, optional): Describe your preferred networking style (e.g., formal, casual).
- **Interests** (`Interests`, text, optional): Enter any interests or areas of expertise you have.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., vegetarian, non-vegetarian).
- **T-Shirt Size** (`T-Shirt Size`, text, optional): Enter your preferred t-shirt size.
- **Shirt Type** (`Shirt Type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of shirt you prefer (e.g., polo, dress).
- **Shirt Color** (`Shirt Color`, select_one, optional): Select the color of shirt you prefer.
- **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes for the event planners.
- **Shirt Design** (`Shirt Design`, text, optional): Enter any specific design preferences for the t-shirt.
- **Shirt Color 2** (`Shirt Color 2`, select_one, optional): Select the color of the t-shirt (if different from your primary preference).
- **Parking** (`Parking`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you will need parking accommodations.
- **Carpooling** (`Carpooling`, select_multiple, optional): Select if you are open to carpooling or not.
- **Comments 2** (`Comments 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the event planners.
- **Interests 2** (`Interests 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional interests or areas of expertise you have.
- **Food Preferences** (`Food Preferences`, text, optional): Enter any food preferences or dietary restrictions you have.
- **Drink Preferences** (`Drink Preferences`, text, optional): Enter any drink preferences you have.
- **Food Preferences 2** (`Food Preferences 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional food preferences or restrictions.
- **Drink Preferences 2** (`Drink Preferences 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional drink preferences.
- **Car** (`Car`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the event planners.
- **Other 2** (`Other 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the event planners.
