# Free Movie Ticket - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a free movie ticket for a movie showing in a movie house.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the movie house where you are a member.
2. Enter the name of the movie being shown.
3. Enter the number of tickets you wish to allocate.
4. Enter the date of the movie showing (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Enter the time of the movie showing (hh:mm).
6. Enter the duration of the movie in minutes.
7. Enter a brief description of the movie.
8. Provide any notes about the movie or your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Movie House Owner** (`movie_house_owner`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the movie house where you are a member.
* **Movie Name** (`movie_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the movie being shown.
* **Ticket Amount** (`ticket_amount`, number, **required**): Enter the number of tickets you wish to allocate.
* **Movie Date** (`movie_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the movie showing (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Movie Time** (`movie_time`, time, **required**): Enter the time of the movie showing (hh:mm).
* **Movie Duration** (`movie_duration`, number, **required**): Enter the duration of the movie in minutes.
* **Ticket Description** (`ticket_description`, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of the movie.
* **Note** (`note`, note, **optional**): Provide any additional notes about the movie or your request.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields.
* Ensure that you are a member of the movie house to create a free movie ticket.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can ask the movie house staff for assistance.
