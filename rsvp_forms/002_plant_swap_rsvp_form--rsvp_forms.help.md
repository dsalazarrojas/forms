# Plant Swap RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to a plant swap event. Please fill out this form to let us know about your attendance, what plants you'll bring, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "RSVP" field to indicate your attendance.
5. Enter the date and time of the plant swap event in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
6. In the "Message" field, you can enter any other comments or messages you'd like to share.
7. If you're bringing plants, enter how many and what kind of plants you'll bring in the "Plants Brought" field.
8. If you're interested in receiving plants, enter what kind of plants you'd like to receive in the "Plants Wanted" field.
9. If you're sharing plants, enter how many and what kind of plants you'll be sharing in the "Plants Shared" field.
10. If you've received plants, enter how many and what kind of plants you've received in the "Plants Received" field.
11. Finally, select your RSVP status from the "RSVP Status" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your name as you'd like to be known.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you'll be attending the plant swap event.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the plant swap event.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the plant swap event.
* **Message** (`Message`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or messages you'd like to share.
* **Plants Brought** (`Plants Brought`, text): If you're bringing plants, enter how many and what kind of plants you'll be bringing.
* **Plants Wanted** (`Plants Wanted`, text): If you're interested in receiving plants, enter what kind of plants you'd like to receive.
* **Plants Shared** (`Plants Shared`, text): If you're sharing plants, enter how many and what kind of plants you'll be sharing.
* **Plants Received** (`Plants Received`, text): If you've received plants, enter how many and what kind of plants you've received.
* **RSVP Status** (`RSVP Status`, select_one, required): Select your RSVP status from the dropdown menu.

## Tips
* Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that we have a smooth and enjoyable plant swap event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
