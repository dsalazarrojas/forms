# Art Gallery Opening Guest Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register your attendance at an art gallery opening event. Please provide your details to help us plan for your visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (id: 1, required: true): Please enter the date you registered for the event.
* **Guest Name** (id: 2, required: true): Enter your full name.
* **Guest Email** (id: 3, required: true): Enter your email address for confirmation.
* **Guest Phone** (id: 4, required: false): If you need to be contacted, enter your phone number.
* **Organization** (id: 5, required: false): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Title** (id: 6, required: false): If applicable, enter your professional title.
* **RSVP Status** (id: 7, required: true): Indicate whether you will attend the event (Yes - Attending, No - Regretfully Decline, Waitlist).
* **Plus One Name** (id: 8, required: false): Enter the name of the guest accompanying you, if applicable.
* **Plus One Email** (id: 9, required: false): Enter the email address of the guest accompanying you, if applicable.
* **Total Guests** (id: 10, required: true): Enter the total number of people in your party.
* **Opening Event** (id: 11, required: true): Select the event you are attending (VIP Preview Night, Public Opening Reception, Artist Talk and Reception, Curator Tour).
* **Exhibition Name** (id: 12, required: true): Enter the name of the exhibition opening you are attending.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (id: 13, required: true): Select all dietary restrictions that apply (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Shellfish Allergy, Nut Allergy, Kosher, Halal).
* **Dietary Other** (id: 14, required: false): If you have other dietary restrictions, specify them here.
* **Beverage Preference** (id: 15, required: false): Select your preferred beverage type (Wine, Beer, Non-Alcoholic, No Preference).
* **Accessibility Requirements** (id: 16, required: true): Select whether you need accessibility accommodations (True, False).
* **Accessibility Details** (id: 17, required: false): Describe any accessibility needs you have.
* **Parking Required** (id: 18, required: false): If you require parking, select True.
* **Valet Preference** (id: 19, required: false): Select whether you prefer Valet or self-park.
* **Coat Check Needed** (id: 20, required: false): If you need coat check, select True.
* **Interested in Purchasing** (id: 21, required: false): Select whether you are interested in purchasing artwork (True, False, Maybe).
* **Press Attendance** (id: 22, required: false): Select whether you are attending as press (True, False).
* **Media Outlet** (id: 23, required: false): Enter the name of the publication or outlet you represent.
* **How Heard** (id: 24, required: false): Select how you heard about the event (Email Invitation, Social Media, Gallery Website, Friend, Press, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (id: 25, required: false): Add any additional comments or special requests here.

## Tips
* Please ensure accuracy when entering your details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our staff.
* We appreciate your cooperation in helping us plan a successful event.
