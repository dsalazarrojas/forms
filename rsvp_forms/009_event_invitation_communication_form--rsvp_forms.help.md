## Event Invitation Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Invitation Communication Form is designed to collect essential information from event attendees, including their RSVP response, dietary preferences, and any special requests they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with the relevant information about the event, such as the event name, date, and location.
2. Select your **RSVP Response** by choosing from the available options (Yes, No, Maybe).
3. Choose your **Dietary Preferences** by selecting from the available options (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan, Other).
4. If you have any **Special Requests**, you can provide them in the text field.
5. Add any additional **RSVP Notes** you'd like to include in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): This section is for providing details about the event you are attending. Enter the name, date, and location of the event.
* **RSVP Response** (`rsvp_response`, select_multiple, required: false): This is where you indicate your response to the event invitation. Choose from the available options (Yes, No, Maybe) to let the organizer know if you can attend or not.
* **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_preferences`, select_multiple, required: true): Select your dietary preferences from the available options (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan, Other). This will help the organizer plan the food and accommodations for the event.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests or needs, you can mention them in this field. This is an optional field, but it's a good opportunity to let the organizer know if you need anything specific.
* **RSVP Notes** (`rsvp_notes`, text, required: false): You can add any additional notes about your RSVP in this field. This is also optional, but it's a good chance to provide more information about your attendance.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Dietary Preferences) to ensure that the event organizer gets a complete picture of your attendance and needs.
* If you have any special requests, be as specific as possible so that the organizer can plan accordingly.
* If you're unsure about your RSVP response, it's okay to choose "Maybe" and follow up with the organizer to confirm your attendance.
* You can add any additional notes about your attendance in the RSVP Notes field.
