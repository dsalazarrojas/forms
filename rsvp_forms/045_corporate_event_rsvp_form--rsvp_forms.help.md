# corporate_event_rsvp_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect RSVP information from employees for a corporate event.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form with the required and optional fields.
*   Enter the details of the event in the "Event Details" field.
*   Select either "RSVP'd" or "Not RSVP'd" in the "RSVP Status" field.
*   Enter any additional notes about the event in the "Notes" field.
*   Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
*   Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
*   Select the date of the event in the "Date" field.
*   Select the time of the event in the "Time" field.
*   Enter your company name in the "Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Details**: Enter a brief description of the event you are attending.
*   **RSVP**: Select whether you will be attending (RSVP'd) or not attending (Not RSVP'd) the event.
*   **Notes**: Provide any additional information or details about your attendance or special requests.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Date**: Select the date of the event.
*   **Time**: Select the time of the event.
*   **Company**: Enter your company name.
 
## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
*   Use the "Notes" field for any additional details or requests. 
*   Select the correct "RSVP Status" based on your intentions. 
*   Fill out the "Contact Information" fields with accurate information. 
*   Select the correct "Event Schedule" (Date and Time) fields. 
*   Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
