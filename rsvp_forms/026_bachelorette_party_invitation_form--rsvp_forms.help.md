# Bachelorette Party Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your attendance and preferences for the Bachelorette Party celebration. Please take a few minutes to fill it out and let us know about your plans.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by scrolling through the pages and filling out the form one section at a time.
- Make sure to provide the required information in each section.
- If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
- You can also include a message to the Bride-to-be in the "Leave a sweet message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- You're Invited to Celebrate our Bride-to-Be! --** (`invitation_intro`, `note`, not required):  This is the welcome message for the invitation.
* **Your Full Name** (`guest_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name so we can address you correctly.
* **Can you make it?** (`attendance_rsvp`, `select_one`, required): Choose your attendance status (Yes, I'll attend, or Sadly, I won't be able to attend).
* **Total number of guests** (`party_guest_count`, `number`, not required): If you're bringing a party, please include all members attending.
* **Dinner Choice** (`dinner_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred dinner option (Chicken Florentine, Grilled Salmon, Mushroom Risotto, No preference).
* **What's your favorite celebratory drink?** (`drink_of_choice`, `text`, not required): Enter your favorite drink to help with planning the party.
* **Any allergies or dietary restrictions?** (`dietary_restrictions_party`, `text`, not required): Specify any dietary restrictions so we can accommodate you.
* **Must-play song for the dance floor** (`song_request_dance`, `text`, not required): Suggest a song to get the party started.
* **Leave a sweet message for the Bride-to-be** (`message_to_bride`, `text`, not required): Share a kind word or memory with the Bride-to-be.
* **Your Email Address** (`rsvp_confirmation_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can send you the final itinerary and details.
