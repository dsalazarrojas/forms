# Virtual Town Hall Meeting RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from attendees to determine their ability and willingness to participate in the Virtual Town Hall Meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Will you attend the Virtual Town Hall Meeting?" from the dropdown menu and choose "True" if you are able and willing to attend.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the best way to ensure that you receive meeting notifications and important information.
3. If you don't plan to attend, please provide a reason for not attending in the "Reason for not attending" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Will you attend the Virtual Town Hall Meeting? (select_one)**: Select "True" if you are able and willing to attend the meeting.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to receive meeting notifications and important information.
* **Phone number (text)**: Enter your phone number if you would like to receive meeting notifications and important information (optional).
* **Reason for not attending (text)**: If you don't plan to attend, please provide a brief reason for not attending.
* **Comments (text)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them here.
* **Additional comments (text)**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are using the correct email address associated with your account.
* If you are not planning to attend, please provide a valid reason for not attending to help us understand your position.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Comments" and "Additional comments" fields accordingly.
