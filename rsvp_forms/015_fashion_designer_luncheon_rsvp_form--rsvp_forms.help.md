# Fashion Designer Luncheon RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fashion Designer Luncheon RSVP Form is a simple form designed to help us gather information from attendees for the Fashion Designer Luncheon event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field provided.
2. Enter your Last Name in the field provided.
3. Enter your contact email address in the field provided.
4. Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for contact purposes).
5. Choose whether you will be attending or not by selecting one of the options provided.
6. If you are attending, you may add your Guest List (optional).
7. Add any Comments you may have (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Your first name is the first part of your name that you were given at birth.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: true): Your last name is the surname you were given at birth, and it is required to complete this form.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: true): Enter your contact email address so we can get in touch with you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Your contact phone number, optional but recommended for easy communication.
- **Attending** (`attending`, `select_one`, required: true): Choose whether you will be attending the event or not.
- **Guest List** (`guest_list`, `text`, required: false): If you are attending, please add your guest list here.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments you may have.
