# Debate Watch Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us track who will be attending a Debate Watch Party. Please fill out this form to let us know if you plan to join us and if you've invited others. Your input will help us get a headcount for snacks and other party planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the required fields (User Name, Message).
3. Choose if you are attending or not by selecting the corresponding option in the "Attending" field.
4. If you are attending, you can list other guests who will be with you in the "Who else is attending?" field.
5. Finally, click "Submit" again to confirm your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (text, required): Please enter your name to help us identify you at the party.
* **Attending** (number, required): Please select "Yes" if you plan to attend, and "No" if you will not be able to make it.
* **Who else is attending?** (select_multiple, required if "Attending" is "Yes"): If you selected "Yes" above, list the names of your guests here. This can be as few or as many as you like.
