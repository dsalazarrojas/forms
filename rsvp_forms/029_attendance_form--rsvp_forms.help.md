# Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Attendance Form is designed to collect information from attendees about their participation in an event. This form is used to track attendance, gather feedback, and identify areas for improvement for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Event Information section with the name and details of the event you are attending.
2. Enter your personal information in the Attendee Information section, including your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Indicate your attendance status and arrival/departure times in the Attendance Information section.
4. Rate your participation level and purpose of visit/attendance in the Participation Level and Purpose of Visit/Attendance sections.
5. Provide additional information in the Additional Information section, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, required, true): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, required, true): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, required, true): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, required, true): Enter the location of the event.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required, true): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required, true): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required, false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Organization/Department** (`organization`, required, false): Enter your organization or group affiliation (if applicable).
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, required, true): Indicate whether you will be attending the event (Present, Absent, Late, Left Early, or Excused Absence).
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, required, false): Enter the time you arrived at the event.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, required, false): Enter the time you left the event.
* **Duration in Hours** (`duration_hours`, required, false): Enter the number of hours you attended the event.
* **Participation Level** (`participation_level`, required, false): Rate your level of participation (Full Participation, Active Participation, Some Participation, Observer, or Minimal).
* **Purpose of Visit/Attendance** (`purpose_of_visit`, required, false): Select the reason for your visit/attendance (Mandatory, Professional Development, Volunteering, Support, Interest, or Other).
* **Session or Track Attended** (`session_track`, required, false): Enter the session or workshop you attended.
* **Event Rating** (`event_rating`, required, false): Rate the event (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Poor).
* **Comments about the Event** (`event_comments`, required, false): Provide any feedback or suggestions about the event.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_needs`, required, false): Indicate any dietary requirements.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`, required, false): Indicate any special accommodations required.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, required, false): Enter any additional information.
