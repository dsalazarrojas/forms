# Thanksgiving RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals about their plans for attending a Thanksgiving dinner. This includes whether they will be coming, what they will be bringing, and what time they would like to come for dinner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to your plans for the Thanksgiving dinner. You can choose from multiple options for some questions, so feel free to select all that apply. When you have finished selecting your options, click submit to send your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Will you be coming?
* **Will you be coming?** (`thanksgiving_rsvp`, select_one, required/optional): Please select "Yes" if you will be attending the Thanksgiving dinner, and "No" if you will not be attending.

### Will you be bringing?
* **Will you be bringing?** (`will_bringing`, text, optional): If you are bringing something, please enter the name of the food or drink you will be bringing in the text field.

### How many guests will you be bringing?
* **How many guests will you be bringing?** (`guest_count`, number, optional): Please enter the number of guests you will be bringing to the dinner.

### What food will you be bringing?
* **What food will you be bringing?** (`food_item`, text, optional): If you are bringing food, please enter the name of the dish you will be bringing in the text field.

### Will you be bringing drinks?
* **Will you be bringing drinks?** (`drinks`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all that apply from the options "Yes" and "No" if you will be bringing drinks.

### What time would you like to come for dinner?
* **What time would you like to come for dinner?** (`dinner_time`, time, optional): Please enter the time you would like to come for dinner.

### Will you RSVP by?
* **Will you RSVP by?** (`rsvp_by`, time, optional): Please enter the time by which you will RSVP to let us know your plans.

## Tips
* Please make sure to select all that apply for the "Will you be bringing?" and "Will you be bringing drinks?" questions.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* You can only submit this form once, so please make sure to fill it out carefully before submitting.
