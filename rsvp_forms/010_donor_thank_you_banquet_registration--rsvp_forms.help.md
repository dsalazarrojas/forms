# Donor Thank You Banquet Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from donors who are invited to a "Donor Thank You Banquet". The organizers of the event need to know who is attending so that they can arrange the necessary accommodations and arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your RSVP status (Yes or No) in the RSVP Details section.
2. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. Make sure to use your real email address to ensure you receive updates and communication about the event.
4. If you want, you can provide your phone number in the Phone field. This is not required, but it's a useful way for the event organizers to contact you.
5. If you have a meal preference (vegetarian or non-vegetarian), select it from the Meal Preferences options.
6. If you have a specific meal preference that is not listed, you can select multiple meal options in the Meal Preferences Multi field.
7. If you need special accommodations or have any other note, you can provide it in the Additional Notes field.
8. Finally, enter the date you plan to attend the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Details**: Select 'Yes' if you are planning to attend the event, and 'No' if you are unable to attend. This field is required.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Email**: Enter your real email address so that the event organizers can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone**: If you want to be contacted by the event organizers, provide your phone number. This is not required, but it's a useful way for them to reach out to you.
* **Meal Preferences**: Select 'Vegetarian' if you prefer vegetarian meals or 'Non-Vegetarian' if you don't have any dietary restrictions. 
* **Meal Preferences Multi**: If you have a specific meal preference that is not listed, you can select multiple options here. For example, if you prefer fish but not beef, select 'Fish' but not 'Beef'.
* **Accommodations**: If you need any special accommodations or have any other notes, you can provide it in this field.
* **Additional Notes**: You can write any other note that you want to share with the event organizers.
* **Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the event, if you know it already.
