# Quinceañera Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quinceañera Invitation Form is used to track the RSVP information for a Quinceañera party. Please respond to this form to let the host know if you will attend, and provide any necessary details for the host to plan the party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and any other required information.
2. Select whether or not you will attend the party.
3. If you will attend, select whether or not you will bring a companion.
4. Enter your contact number and email (if applicable).
5. Provide any additional information about yourself or your companion.
6. Select whether or not you will send a gift to the host.
7. Enter the time you will arrive at the party.
8. Enter the time you will depart from the party.
9. Select whether or not you will arrange for dinner, lunch, or breakfast.
10. Select whether or not you will arrange for drinks.
11. Enter the time for dinner (if you are arranging for it).
12. Enter the time you will arrive at the party (if you are arranging for it).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it is written on the invitation.
* **Will Attend**: Select 'True' if you will attend the party, 'False' otherwise.
* **Will brings Companion**: Select 'True' if you will bring a companion, 'False' otherwise.
* **Guest Number**: If you are attending with a group, enter the number of people in your group.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for the host to reach you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for the host to contact you (if applicable).
* **Guest Information**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Will Send Present**: Select 'True' if you will send a gift to the host, 'False' otherwise.
* **Companion Name**: If you are bringing a companion, enter their name.
* **Companion Information**: Enter any additional information about your companion.
* **Guest Comments**: Enter any comments or messages for the host.
* **RSVP By**: Enter the date by which you must RSVP to the host.
* **RSVP Before**: Enter the date by which you must RSVP to the host.
* **Will Arrive**: Enter the time you will arrive at the party.
* **Arrival Location**: Enter the location where you will arrive at the party.
* **RSVP Message**: Enter a message for the host.
* **Contact Message**: Enter a message for the host.
* **Will Arrange Dinner**: Select 'True' if you will arrange for dinner, 'False' otherwise.
* **Will Arrange Lunch**: Select 'True' if you will arrange for lunch, 'False' otherwise.
* **Will Arrange Breakfast**: Select 'True' if you will arrange for breakfast, 'False' otherwise.
* **Will Arrange Drinks**: Select 'True' if you will arrange for drinks, 'False' otherwise.
* **Will Arrange Dessert**: Select 'True' if you will arrange for dessert, 'False' otherwise.
* **Dinner Time**: Enter the time for dinner if you are arranging for it.
* **Will Arrive Time**: Enter the time you will arrive at the party if you are arranging for it.
