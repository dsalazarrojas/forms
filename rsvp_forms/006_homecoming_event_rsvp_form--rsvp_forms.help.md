# homecoming_event_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The homecoming_event_rsvp_form is used to gather information from users regarding their intentions to attend a homecoming event and any additional details they may need to share with event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details: Enter your name and contact information (phone and email) accurately.
2. Confirm your event intentions: Select your response to attending the event by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the "will_attend" field.
3. Indicate accessibility needs: Choose your response from the "accessibility_requirements" field according to your needs.
4. Optionally, add more details: You can provide any additional comments or information in the "comments" field if you have any further requirements or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name (event_name)** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you will be attending.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event. This is the date you will be attending the event.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event. This is the time you will be attending the event.
* **Location (location)** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event where it will take place.
* **Description (description)** (`description`, text, optional): You can enter any additional details about the event you'd like to share with organizers.
* **Name (name)** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Number of Guests (number_of_guests)** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): If you're planning to bring guests, enter the number of guests you'll be bringing.
* **Will Attend (will_attend)** (`will_attend`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you will be attending the event or 'No' if you will not be attending.
* **Accessibility Requirements (accessibility_requirements)** (`accessibility_requirements`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any accessibility requirements you have from the options provided.
* **Comments (comments)** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you'd like to share with event organizers.

## Tips
- Be accurate with your contact details to ensure you can be reached by event organizers.
- Clearly indicate your intentions to attend the event to help with planning.
- If you have any special requirements, select them from the options provided to help event organizers accommodate you.
