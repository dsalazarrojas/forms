# Vendor Networking Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Networking Mixer RSVP Form is designed to gather information from vendors and attendees to facilitate connections and networking during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your VENDOR NAME, which is your name as a vendor.
2. Enter your Email to receive updates and communication from the event organizer.
3. Enter your Business Name, which is your company name.
4. Describe your Networking Goals, e.g., looking for partnerships, seeking advice, or exploring opportunities.
5. Select your dietary needs from the available options (gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan, or dairy-free).
6. Choose how you will RSVP (Attendee, Vendor, or Guest).
7. Select the date you plan to RSVP for.
8. Choose the time you plan to arrive and leave the event.
9. Provide any Additional Comments, if needed.
10. Fill out optional fields such as Company Name, Job Title, Job Function, Company Website, Industry, Job Description, and Company Address if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **VENDOR NAME** (Required): Please enter your name as a vendor.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your company email to receive updates and communication from the event organizer.
* **Business Name** (Required): Enter your company name.
* **Networking Goals** (Required): Describe your networking goals, e.g., looking for partnerships, seeking advice, or exploring opportunities.
* **Dietary Needs** (Required): Select your dietary needs from the available options (gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan, or dairy-free).
* **RSVP Type** (Required): Choose how you will attend as Attendee, Vendor, or Guest.
* **RSVP Date** (Required): Select the date you plan to attend the event.
* **RSVP Time** (Required): Choose the time you plan to arrive and leave the event.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Company Name** (Optional): Enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (Optional): Enter your job function.
* **Company Website** (Optional): Enter your company website.
* **Industry** (Optional): Enter your industry.
* **Job Description** (Optional): Enter your job description.
* **Company Address** (Optional): Enter your company address.
* **RSVP Note** (Optional): Provide any additional note for the event organizer.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields to help the event organizer tailor the event experience to your needs.
* If you have any specific dietary needs, please select all applicable options.
* If you are attending as a guest, please provide the name of the guest you are bringing.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the "Additional Comments" field.
