# Pet Birthday Celebration RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to a fun celebration for a pet's birthday, providing information needed for planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and your pet's name in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose the date and time of the celebration.
3. Indicate if you will be attending the celebration (Yes/No).
4. Select how many guests you will be bringing (if any).
5. Choose your favorite food options (select all that apply).
6. Add any additional message or note about your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Owner's Name** (`pet_owners_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Pet's Name** (`pet_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your pet.
* **Celebration Date** (`celebration_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the date of the celebration. Format should be MM/DD.
* **Celebration Time** (`celebration_time`, `time`, required: false): Choose the time of the celebration. Format should be 12-hour clock.
* **Attending RSVP** (`attending_rsvp`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate if you will be attending the celebration (Yes/No).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing. If none, enter 0.
* **Food Preferences** (`food_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all your favorite food options for the celebration. You can choose as many options as you like.
* **RSVP Message** (`rsvp_message`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional message or note about your response.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your name and pet's name as they appear on your identification and pet's identification.
* If you will not be attending, select "No" for the attending RSVP.
* You can select as many food options as you like.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please add them in the RSVP Message field.
