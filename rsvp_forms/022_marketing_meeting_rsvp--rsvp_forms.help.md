# Marketing Meeting RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from attendees about their availability and preferences for a marketing meeting. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to help the meeting organizers prepare for the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Meeting Name**: Enter the name of the marketing meeting you are planning to attend.
- **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the meeting you would like to attend.
- **Meeting Time**: Choose the time of the meeting you prefer.
- **Attendees**: Select the people you would like to meet with (choose as many or as few as needed).
- **RSVP**: Select whether you will be attending or not.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted for further details or updates).
- **Email**: Enter your work email (if you would like to receive updates or reminders).
- **Message**: Enter any comments or questions you have about the meeting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Name (required: no)**: This field is for the meeting name only. You can enter the name of the meeting you would like to attend.
- **Meeting Date (required: no)**: Enter the date of the meeting you would like to attend in standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
- **Meeting Time (required: no)**: Select the time of the meeting you prefer.
- **Attendees (required: no)**: Select the people you would like to meet with. This can be anyone from the company, including yourself.
- **RSVP (required: no)**: Choose 'Yes' if you are attending, 'No' if you are not attending, or 'Maybe' if you are unsure.
- **Phone (required: no)**: If you have a phone number, enter it so the meeting organizer can contact you.
- **Email (required: no)**: If you would like to receive updates or reminders, enter your work email here.
- **Message (required: no)**: Enter any comments or questions you have about the meeting.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to help the meeting organizers prepare for the meeting.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or the meeting organizer.
- Please use your company email and phone number to ensure the meeting organizers can contact you efficiently.
