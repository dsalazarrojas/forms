# nutrition_workshop_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect RSVP (response to an invitation) information from participants for the Nutrition Workshop. This form allows participants to provide their name, email address, phone number, preferred workshop date and time, and whether or not they plan to attend. The form helps us understand who is coming and make necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date and time for the workshop in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.
5. Indicate your plan to attend the workshop by selecting "Yes" or "No" or "Maybe" in the "RSVP" field.
6. Review all fields to ensure accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop you plan to attend.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Participant**: Enter your name.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **email**: Enter your email address.
  - Type: email
  - Required: false
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false
- **Date**: Select the date of the workshop you plan to attend.
  - Type: date
  - Required: false
- **Time**: Select the time of the workshop you plan to attend.
  - Type: time
  - Required: false
- **RSVP**: Indicate your plan to attend the workshop by selecting "Yes", "No", or "Maybe".
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: false
