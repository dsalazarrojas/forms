# Festival Press Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Press Mixer RSVP Form is designed to allow users to RSVP for the Festival Press Mixer event. Please use this form to let us know if you can attend the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Add your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select a date for your RSVP in the "RSVP Date" field.
5. Choose a time for your RSVP in the "RSVP Time" field.
6. Select the type of event you will be attending in the "RSVP Type" field.
7. Add any notes you'd like to include in the "RSVP Notes" field.
8. Optionally, you can upload up to 9 items in the "RSVP Form Uploads" field.
9. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click submit to submit your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email (email)**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you with event updates.
* **Full Name (full_name)**: Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our event list.
* **Phone (phone)**: Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **RSVP Date (rsvp_date)**: Please select a date for your RSVP. This will be used to determine when you plan to attend the event.
* **RSVP Time (rsvp_time)**: Please select a time for your RSVP. This will be used to determine when you plan to arrive at the event.
* **RSVP Type (rsvp_type)**: Please select the type of event you plan to attend. The options are "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
* **RSVP Notes (rsvp_notes)**: Please add any additional notes or comments you'd like to include with your RSVP.
* **RSVP Form Uploads (rsvp_form_uploads)**: Please upload up to 9 items to include with your RSVP.
* **RSVP Form Submitted At (rsvp_form_submitted_at)**: This field is automatically populated with the date and time you submitted your RSVP. Do not fill this out.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information so we can get in touch with you and ensure you have a good experience at the Festival Press Mixer event.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
