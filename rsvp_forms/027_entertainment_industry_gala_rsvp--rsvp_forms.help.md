# entertainment_industry_gala_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "entertainment_industry_gala_rsvp" form is used to collect RSVP information for an industry gala event. It allows attendees to provide their contact details, preferences, and head count information to event planners and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Click on the "Main Page" section to start inputting your information.
3. Fill in the "Contact Details" field with your name and email address.
4. Select your preference for "Guest Preferences" (optional).
5. Select your head count by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Head Count" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
7. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field (optional).
8. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" in the "RSVP" field to confirm your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Page** (Page 1, text): This is the starting point of the form where you can input your general information.
* **Contact Details** (Page 2, select_one): Choose "Yes" to provide your contact details, or "No" if you'd like to remain anonymous.
* **Contact Details 2** (Page 3, select_multiple): If you chose "Yes" in the previous field, select your contact details (e.g., phone, email).
* **Guest Preferences** (Page 4, note): Provide any additional notes or comments about your preferences for the event.
* **RSVP** (Page 5, select_one): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Head Count** (Page 6, select_multiple): Select "Yes" to indicate that you can attend with a head count, or "No" if you cannot.
* **Email** (Page 7, email): Enter your email address (this field is not required, but recommended for communication).
* **Phone** (Page 8, text): Enter your phone number (this field is not required, but recommended for communication).
* **Comments** (Page 9, text): Provide any additional comments or notes about your attendance or preferences.
* **Head Count** (Page 10, select_multiple): This field is used to confirm if you can attend with a head count or not (this field is a duplicate, please disregard this field).
