# Non Profit Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help non-profit organizations RSVP to a dinner event. It collects information about the organization, event details, and guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your non-profit's name in the "Non Profit Name" field.
2. Select the "Event Date" and "Event Time" for the dinner event.
3. Enter the "Event Location" where the dinner will take place.
4. Provide "Event Details" about the dinner event.
5. Specify the "Number of Guests" attending the event.
6. Select your "Dietary Restrictions" from the options provided.
7. Enter any "Dietary Restrictions Notes" if needed.
8. Select how you would like to be contacted for updates, "Send Update" options are "Yes" or "No".
9. If you selected "Yes" for "Send Update", enter any additional "Send Update Notes".
10. Select how you would like to be contacted for updates, "Send Method" options are "Mailchimp", "Email" or "Phone Call".
11. Enter any notes for "Send Method".
12. Enter the "Send Time" for the updates.
13. If you are not attending the event, enter a reason for not attending in "RSVP Form Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Non Profit Name** (`non_profit_name`, text, optional): Enter your non-profit's name.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the dinner event.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of the dinner event.
- **Number of Guests** (`number_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests attending the event.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional event details.
- **Number of Guests Notes** (`number_guests_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the number of guests.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests for the event.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, required): Select from the options: "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3".
- **Dietary Restrictions Notes** (`dietary_restrictions_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about dietary restrictions.
- **Send Update** (`send_update`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to receive updates.
- **Send Update Notes** (`send_update_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional "Send Update" notes.
- **Send Method** (`send_method`, select_one, required): Select from the options: "Mailchimp", "Email", "Phone Call".
- **Send Method Notes** (`send_method_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional "Send Method" notes.
- **Send Time** (`send_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for updates.
- **Event Date Time** (`event_date_time`, date, optional): Select the date and time of the event.
- **Non Profit ID** (`non_profit_id`, text, optional): Enter your non-profit's ID.
- **Event Date Time Notes** (`event_date_time_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional event date and time notes.
- **RSVP Form Notes** (`rsvp_form_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional RSVP form notes.
- **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, optional): Click the submit button to complete the form.
