# Film Crew Appreciation Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Crew Appreciation Party RSVP Form is designed to collect essential information from crew members to ensure they can be accommodated properly for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "Required".
2. Provide accurate information in all fields, including personal details and event-specific information.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, Required): Your name as a crew member.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, Optional): Your job title in the film industry.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, Optional): Your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, Optional): Your phone number for contact.
* **RSVP Type** (`RSVP Type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose how you plan to attend the party (Attending, Unable to Attend, Maybe).
* **Number of Guests** (`Number of Guests`, `number`, Optional): How many guests you are bringing with you.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `note`, Optional): Any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Special Request** (`Special Request`, `text`, Optional): Any special requests or requirements you have.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Other).
* **RSVP By** (`RSVP By`, `date`, Optional): The date you are submitting your RSVP.
* **RSVP Time** (`RSVP Time`, `time`, Optional): The time you are submitting your RSVP.
