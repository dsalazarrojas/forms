# COVID 19 Liability Release WaiverThe Spearman Wedding 1.1.21 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from guests attending the wedding on January 1, 2021, and to obtain a liability release waiver related to COVID-19 risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your guest information, including your full name, phone number, and email address.
2. Indicate the number of guests attending the wedding.
3. If you are bringing a plus one, enter their name.
4. Confirm your event details: date and location.
5. Indicate your attendance status: yes, no, or maybe.
6. Choose your meal preference.
7. Confirm your health status related to COVID-19 symptoms, exposure, and vaccination.
8. Confirm your understanding of the risks associated with attending the wedding.
9. Release the couple from COVID-19 related liability.
10. Take responsibility for your own health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Full Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Party Size (Number of Guests)** (`party_size`, number, required): Enter the number of guests attending.
- **Plus One Name if Applicable** (`plus_one_name`, text, optional): If you are bringing a plus one, enter their name.
- **Event Date - January 1, 2021** (`event_date`, date, required): Confirm the event date.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Confirm the event location.
- **Will You Be Attending** (`attendance`, select_one, required): Indicate yes, no, or maybe.
- **Meal Preference** (`meal_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your meal preference.
- **COVID-19 symptoms in past 14 days** (`symptoms`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have experienced COVID-19 symptoms in the past 14 days.
- **Known COVID-19 exposure** (`exposure`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have had known COVID-19 exposure.
- **Tested positive for COVID-19** (`tested_positive`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have tested positive for COVID-19.
- **Close contact with high-risk individual** (`high_risk_contact`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have had close contact with a high-risk individual.
- **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Confirm your vaccination status.
- **I understand COVID-19 risks** (`understand_risks`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the risks associated with attending the wedding.
- **I accept the risks associated with attendance** (`accept_risks`, select_one, required): Confirm that you accept the risks associated with attending the wedding.
- **I release the couple from COVID-19 related liability** (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you release the couple from COVID-19 related liability.
- **I take responsibility for my own health** (`health_responsibility`, select_one, required): Confirm that you take responsibility for your own health.
- **Date of Declaration** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you sign this form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your RSVP is submitted correctly.
- Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the wedding organizers.
