# party_invitation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for a party invitation. It helps hosts share details with invitees, making it easier for them to prepare and attend the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information about your party.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **party_details** (`party_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the party, such as "Summer BBQ at the park" or "Birthday celebration for John".
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the attendee, such as "John Doe".
* **date** (`date`, required/optional): Select the date of the party in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **time** (`time`, required/optional): Choose the time of the party in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **location** (`location`, required/optional): Enter the address or venue where the party will take place.
* **description** (`description`, required/optional): Provide a short description of the party, including any specific details or instructions.
* **guests** (`guests`, required/optional): Enter the estimated number of guests attending the party.
* **phone** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter a contact phone number for RSVPs or inquiries.
