# thanksgiving_potluck_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs for a Thanksgiving potluck event. It is used to gather information from guests about their attendance, dietary restrictions, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest information in the "Guest Information" section.
2. Indicate if you will be attending the event.
3. Enter the number of guests in your party.
4. Check the types of courses you will bring to the potluck (e.g., main course, side course, dessert).
5. Enter any special dietary restrictions.
6. Leave any special requests or comments for the host.
7. Enter your RSVP comments.
8. Enter your contact information (name, email, phone number, address).
9. Enter any additional potluck details.
10. Enter the date and time of the event.
11. Enter the location of the event.
12. Enter the RSVP deadline and time.
13. Enter the host's name, email, phone number, and address.
14. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information** (`guest_info`, text, not required): Enter your name and any other guest's names that will be attending the event.
* **Attending** (`attending`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if you will be attending the event, "No" otherwise.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`, number, not required): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Main Course** (`main_course`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if you will bring a main course, "No" otherwise.
* **Side Course** (`side_course`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" for each type of side course you will bring (e.g., salad, vegetable, fruit).
* **Dessert** (`dessert`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" for each type of dessert you will bring (e.g., cake, pie, fruit).
* **Drink** (`drink`, select_one, not required): Select "Yes" if you will bring a drink, "No" otherwise.
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, text, not required): Enter a short message to let the host know about your RSVP.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, not required): Leave any additional comments or requests for the host.
* **Special Diet** (`special_diet`, select_multiple, not required): Select "Yes" for each type of special diet you have (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, not required): Enter any special requests or comments for the host.
* **RSVP Comments** (`rsvp_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments about your RSVP.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, not required): Enter your address.
* **Potluck Details** (`potluck_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional details about your contribution to the potluck.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, not required): Enter the date and time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location of the event.
* **RSVP Deadline** (`rsvp_date`, date, not required): Enter the RSVP deadline.
* **RSVP Time** (`rsvp_time`, time, not required): Enter the RSVP time.
* **Host Name** (`host_name`, text, not required): Enter the host's name.
* **Host Email** (`host_email`, email, not required): Enter the host's email address.
* **Host Phone** (`host_phone`, text, not required): Enter the host's phone number.
* **Host Address** (`host_address`, text, not required): Enter the host's address.
