# Respond To An Event Now Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Respond To An Event Now Form is used to collect information from event attendees to help event organizers track and manage event attendance, attendee details, and responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event you wish to RSVP to in the "Event Name" field.
2. Enter your name as you wish to be listed as an attendee in the "Attendee Name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Attendee Email" field.
4. Choose your response to the event in the "RSVP" field.
5. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
6. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
7. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
8. Enter the number of attendees for this event in the "Attendee Number" field.
9. If you would like to include any additional comments or information, type them in the "Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you wish to RSVP to.
* **Attendee Name** (`Attendee Name`, text, required): Enter your name as you wish to be listed as an attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`Attendee Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for the attendee.
* **RSVP** (`RSVP`, select_one, required): Choose your response to the event: True, False, or Maybe.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Event Time** (`Event Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event in 24-hour format.
* **Event Location** (`Event Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Attendee Number** (`Attendee Number`, number, required): Enter the number of people attending this event with you.
* **Comment** (`Comment`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or information, type them here.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when providing your response to the event.
* Ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* Avoid duplicate RSVPs for the same event. If you have already RSVPd, do not submit another response.
* Only include relevant comments and information about the event.
