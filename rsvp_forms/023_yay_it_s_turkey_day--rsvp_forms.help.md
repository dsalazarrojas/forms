# yay_it_s_turkey_day - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information and responses from guests attending a "yay_it_s_turkey_day" event. It allows users to submit details about themselves and what they plan to bring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Follow the instructions provided here to fill out the form in your browser or mobile device.
* Select the number of guests you are bringing and choose what each guest is bringing.
* Choose whether or not you will be "Coming" to the event.
* Optionally, enter any additional details for "Guests List" and "Bringing List" if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main** (Text): A page title, not a field that requires input.
* **Item** (Text): A field for you to enter an item you plan to bring.
* **Another** (Text): Another field for you to enter another item you plan to bring.
* **Guests** (Select One): Choose how many guests you are bringing.
* **Bringing** (Select One): Choose what each guest is bringing. You can select from the options provided or enter your own custom option.
* **Guests List** (Text): A field for you to enter additional details about your guests, if needed.
* **Bringing List** (Text): A field to enter additional details about what you are bringing, if needed.
* **Coming** (Select One): Choose whether you are coming to the event.
* **Name** (Text): A field to enter your name, which is not required but useful for reference. 

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and honest information to help the event organizers prepare for your attendance.
* If you have any special requests or needs, contact the event organizers directly.
* This form is for casual use and is not intended for long-term tracking or sensitive information collection.
