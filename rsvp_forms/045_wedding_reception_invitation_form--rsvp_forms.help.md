# Wedding Reception Invitation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Reception Invitation Form is a tool for guests to communicate their attendance and preferences to the wedding party. It helps the wedding party track RSVPs and plan the reception accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Date, Time, and Venue.
2. Choose your meal preference: Beef, Vegetarian, or Other.
3. Select your attending status: Attending, Declining, or Maybe.
4. If attending, choose your meal attendance: Beef, Vegetarian, Both.
5. If attending, list any special requests or needs in the "Special Requests" field.
6. If you have any other special requests or needs, list them in the "Other Special Requests" field.
7. Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Wedding Details**
`Wedding Details` is the first field to fill out. Enter the date and time of the wedding in the format `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`, and the location of the reception.

### 2. **RSVP**
`RSVP` is where you indicate your attendance status. Select `Attending` if you will be attending the wedding, `Declining` if you will not be attending, or `Maybe` if you are unsure.

### 3. **RSVP Note**
`RSVP Note` is an optional field for any additional information you want to provide regarding your attendance.

### 4. **Number of Guests**
`Number of Guests` is the number of guests you will be bringing to the wedding.

### 5. **Phone Number**
`Phone Number` is a field to enter your phone number, which is not required but can be useful for the wedding party to contact you.

### 6. **Email**
`Email` is a field to enter your email address, which is not required but can be useful for the wedding party to contact you.

### 7. **Special Requests**
`Special Requests` is a field for any special requests or needs you may have, such as dietary restrictions.

### 8. **Meal Preferences**
`Meal Preferences` is where you choose your meal preference. Select `Beef`, `Vegetarian`, or `Other`.

### 9. **Meal Note**
`Meal Note` is an optional field for any additional information about your meal preference.

### 10. **Other Notes**
`Other Notes` is an optional field for any other information you want to provide.

### 11. **Attending**
`Attending` is where you indicate your attending status. Select `Attending`, `Declining`, or `Maybe`.

### 12. **Meal Attendance**
`Meal Attendance` is where you choose whether you will be attending the meal. Select `Beef`, `Vegetarian`, or `Both`.

### 13. **Special Notes**
`Special Notes` is an optional field for any special notes or comments.

### 14. **Date**
`Date` is the date of the wedding, which is not required but can be useful for the wedding party to have in their records.

### 15. **Time**
`Time` is the time of the wedding, which is not required but can be useful for the wedding party to have in their records.

### 16. **Venue**
`Venue` is the location of the reception, which is not required but can be useful for the wedding party to have in their records.

### 17. **Dressing Code**
`Dressing Code` is where you choose your dressing code. Select `Formal` or `Casual`.

### 18. **Dress Code Note**
`Dress Code Note` is an optional field for any additional information about your dressing code.

### 19. **Transportation**
`Transportation` is where you choose how you plan to arrive. Select `By car`, `By bus`, or `By train`.

### 20. **Transportation Note**
`Transportation Note` is an optional field for any additional information about your transportation.

### 21. **Accommodations**
`Accommodations` is where you choose your accommodations for the night. Select `Hotel`, `Motel`, or `None`.

### 22. **Accommodations Note**
`Accommodations Note` is an optional field for any additional information about your accommodations.

### 23. **Special Dietary Needs**
`Special Dietary Needs` is an optional field for any special dietary needs or restrictions you may have.

### 24. **Other Special Requests**
`Other Special Requests` is an optional field for any other special requests or needs you may have.

### 25. **Submit Note**
`Submit Note` is an optional field for any additional comments or notes you want to provide.

## Tips
- Please review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- Make sure to choose the correct options for each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the wedding party directly.
