# seminar_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seminar_rsvp form is used to collect information for both hosts and guests to ensure a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the guest list by providing the names and details of all attending guests.
2. Enter the event details, including the date and time of the event.
3. Choose your RSVP type (e.g., attending, unable to attend, etc.).
4. Fill in your date of birth and other contact details as required.

* **Event details** (`event_details`, `number`, `required`): Enter the date and time of the event.
