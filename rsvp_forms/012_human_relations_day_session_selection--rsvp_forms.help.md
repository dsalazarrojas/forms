# Human Relations Day Session Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Relations Day Session Selection form is used to collect attendee information and session selection preferences for a conference or event with multiple sessions. This guide will walk you through the form's fields and help you complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Sessions**: Choose the sessions you would like to attend by selecting "Yes" for the sessions you're interested in.
2. Fill out the **Name** field with your full name.
3. Enter your **Email** address.
4. If you'd like to provide additional notes or information, type in the **Note** field.
5. Select your desired **Date** and **Time** for the event.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the 10 session options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Sessions**: Select the sessions you would like to attend by choosing "Yes" for each session you're interested in. This field is used to track your session selection preferences.
* **Name**: Enter your full name for registration purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Note**: If you have any additional notes or comments, type them in this field.
* **Date**: Select the date you will be attending the event.
* **Time**: Select the time you will be attending the event.
* **Session 1-10**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the 10 session options, indicating whether you would like to attend each session.
