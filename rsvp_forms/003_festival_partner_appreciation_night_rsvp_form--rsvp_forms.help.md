# Festival Partner Appreciation Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us track which festival partners and their guests will be attending the Festival Partner Appreciation Night event. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Please select the date you will be attending.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Please select the time you will be attending.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): If you are bringing a guest, please enter their name.
* **Guest Relation** (`guest_relation`, select_one, optional): Please select whether your guest is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Please enter any personal message you'd like to share.
* **Attending** (`attending`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you will be attending 'Yes' or 'No', and if so, whether your guest will be attending 'Yes' or 'No'. 

## Tips
* Please only submit one form per partner or guest.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
