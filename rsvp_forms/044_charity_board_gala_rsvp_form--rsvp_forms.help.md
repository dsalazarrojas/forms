# Charity Board Gala RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to manage guest attendance and meal preferences for the Charity Board Gala. It helps us ensure that we have an accurate headcount and can make necessary arrangements for seating and meal preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Step 1:** Start by filling out the initial fields with your information.
2. **Step 2:** Select the meal preferences for each guest by choosing from the available options.
3. **Step 3:** Add any additional information that may be relevant for our staff to prepare for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Gala RSVP --**: This is the form header, which is not editable.
* **guest name**: Please provide the full name of the guest(s) attending.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address so we can send you event updates and confirmations.
* **number of guests**: Indicate how many guests will be attending, including yourself if applicable.
* **meal choice**: Select the meal preference for each guest from the options provided:
	+ **Chicken**: A chicken-based meal is available for selection.
	+ **Fish**: A fish-based meal is available for selection.
	+ **Vegetarian**: A vegetarian meal option is available.
	+ **Vegan**: A vegan meal option is available.
* **dietary restrictions**: If you have any food allergies or special dietary needs, please specify them here.
* **donation option**: If you would like to make a donation during the event, select "Yes please" to indicate your interest.
* **seating requests**: If you have any specific seating requests or preferences, let us know here.
* **accessibility needs**: If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, please specify them here.
* **confirmation**: Finally, confirm your attendance and details by selecting "Confirmed" or "Not attending" from the options provided.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to help us prepare for the event.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
* Thank you for participating in the Charity Board Gala, and we look forward to seeing you there!
