# Business Leadership Breakfast RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Business Leadership Breakfast RSVP form. The form is designed to collect essential information about attendees, including their contact details, attendance preferences, and any special requirements. Your input will help us better organize the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in your **Attendee name** in the "Attendee name" field. This will be used for your name badge.
2.  Enter your **Organization** if you are affiliated with one.
3.  Provide your **Contact email** so we can keep you updated on event details.
4.  Specify the **Number of guests** you will be bringing, including yourself.
5.  If you have any **Dietary requirements**, please list them in the "Dietary requirements" field.
6.  Choose between **In person** or **Virtual** attendance by selecting one of the options in the "Attendance type" dropdown.
7.  If you have specific **Seating preference**, let us know in the "Seating preference" field.
8.  You may optionally provide your **Contact phone number** for any last-minute updates.
9.  Finally, if you have any **Special requests**, please share them with us in the "Special requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it will be displayed on your name badge.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter it here.
- **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): We will use this email to send you updates and event confirmation.
- **Number of guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Please count yourself among the attendees you're bringing.
- **Dietary requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any specific dietary needs or restrictions, list them here.
- **Attendance type** (`attendance_type`, select_one, required): Choose between attending in person or virtually.
- **Seating preference** (`seating_preference`, text, optional): Share your preferred seating arrangement, if any.
- **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Provide an optional phone number for any last-minute updates.
- **Special requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any other requirements, let us know here.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate representation of your intentions.
- If you are unable to attend, please let us know in advance so we can adjust the event accordingly.
- You can update your responses at any time if your needs or preferences change.
