# Campaign Rally RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees and volunteers for a campaign rally. It is used to gather data on who is attending, their contact information, and their interests in volunteering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Provide your street address in the "Street Address" field (optional).
5. Enter your city and state in the "City" and "State" fields (optional).
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field (optional).
7. Select the date and time of the rally in the "Rally Date" and "Rally Time" fields.
8. Enter the location of the rally in the "Rally Location" field.
9. Choose whether you will attend the rally in the "Will you be attending" field.
10. Enter the number of guests attending (including yourself) in the "Number of Guests Attending" field.
11. If interested, select the volunteer roles you are interested in in the "Volunteer roles interested in" field.
12. If you are interested in volunteering, enter the number of hours you can volunteer in the "Hours you can volunteer" field.
13. If you have any special needs or accommodations, enter them in the "Special accommodations needed" field.
14. If you need hearing assistance, select "True" in the "Do you need hearing assistance" field.
15. If you require wheelchair accessibility, select "True" in the "Do you require wheelchair accessibility" field.
16. Finally, select how you heard about the rally in the "How did you hear about this rally" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Text): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (Text): Provide your street address (optional).
* **City** (Text): Enter your city (optional).
* **State** (Text): Enter your state (optional).
* **Zip Code** (Text): Enter your zip code (optional).
* **Rally Date** (Date): Select the date of the rally.
* **Rally Time** (Time): Select the time of the rally.
* **Rally Location** (Text): Enter the location of the rally.
* **Will you be attending** (Select One): Choose whether you will attend the rally.
* **Number of Guests Attending** (Number): Enter the number of guests attending (including yourself).
* **Volunteer roles interested in** (Select Multiple): Select the volunteer roles you are interested in (optional).
* **Hours you can volunteer** (Text): Enter the number of hours you can volunteer (optional).
* **Special accommodations needed** (Text): Enter any special needs or accommodations you have (optional).
* **Do you need hearing assistance** (Select One): Select whether you need hearing assistance.
* **Do you require wheelchair accessibility** (Select One): Select whether you require wheelchair accessibility.
* **How did you hear about this rally** (Select One): Select how you heard about the rally (optional).
