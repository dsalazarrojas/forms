# Travel Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Risk Assessment Form is designed to assess the health status and medical history of an individual before traveling. This form will help you identify potential risks and take necessary precautions to ensure a safe and enjoyable journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
2. For each question, select the appropriate answer from the provided options.
3. If you have any concerns or questions, please contact your travel provider or a medical professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What is your health status?
* **What is your health status?** (`travel_risk_assessment`, text, required: false): This question is asking about your current health status. Please describe your health status in the provided text field.

### Medical history?
* **Medical history?** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Please provide any relevant medical history that may be important for your travel.

### Are you fit for travel?
* **Are you fit for travel?** (`travel_risk_assessment2`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your ability to travel. Select 'True' if you are fit for travel and 'False' if not.

### Are you a diabetic?
* **Are you a diabetic?** (`medical_history2`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is asking if you have diabetes. Select 'True' if you are a diabetic and 'False' otherwise.

### Are you taking any medications?
* **Are you taking any medications?** (`travel_risk_assessment3`, text, required: false): Please list any medications you are currently taking.

### Do you have any allergies?
* **Do you have any allergies?** (`medical_history3`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is asking about any allergies you may have. Select 'True' if you have an allergy and 'False' otherwise.

### Are you a smoker?
* **Are you a smoker?** (`travel_risk_assessment4`, select_one, required: false): This question is asking about your smoking status. Select 'True' if you are a smoker and 'False' otherwise.

### Have you ever had an asthma attack?
* **Have you ever had an asthma attack?** (`medical_history4`, select_multiple, required: false): This question is asking about any past asthma attacks. Select 'True' if you have had an asthma attack and 'False' otherwise.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact your travel provider or a medical professional.
* This form is not intended to replace medical advice or diagnosis.
