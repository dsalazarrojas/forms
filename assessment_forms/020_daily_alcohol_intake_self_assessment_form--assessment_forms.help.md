# Daily Alcohol Intake Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Alcohol Intake Self Assessment Form is a tool to help users evaluate their daily drinking habits and identify potential areas of concern.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
- Read each section carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
- Fill out the form completely, as required fields are marked.
- Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`Personal Info`, note, optional): This section is for your personal details, such as your name, email, or any other identifying information. Please fill this out if you have any relevant personal details.
* **How often do you drink?** (`drink_frequency`, required): Please select how often you drink on a scale, for example, daily, weekly, monthly, or never.
* **Average number of drinks per week** (`drinks_per_week`, required): Enter the average number of drinks you consume per week.
* **What type of alcohol do you usually drink?** (`alcohol_type`, required): Choose the type of alcohol you typically consume, such as beer, wine, or spirits.
* **How often do you drink at social events?** (`drink_frequency_2`, optional): Select how often you drink at social gatherings, such as parties or events.
* **How many drinks do you usually have at social events?** (`drink_quantity`, optional): Enter the typical number of drinks you consume at social events.
* **What time of day do you usually drink?** (`drink_time`, required): Choose the time of day when you usually drink, such as morning, evening, or late night.
* **Where do you usually drink?** (`drink_location`, optional): Provide the location where you usually drink, such as at home or in a bar.
* **How often do you drink at home?** (`drink_frequency_3`, optional): Select how often you drink at home.
* **How much do you usually drink at home?** (`drink_amount`, optional): Enter the typical amount you drink at home.
* **Where do you usually drink?** (`drink_location_2`, optional): Provide another location where you usually drink.
* **How often do you drink with friends?** (`drink_frequency_4`, optional): Select how often you drink with friends.
* **How often do you drink with family?** (`drink_frequency_5`, optional): Choose how often you drink with family.
* **How often do you drink alone?** (`drink_frequency_6`, optional): Select how often you drink by yourself.
* **How often do you drink at bars or clubs?** (`drink_frequency_7`, optional): Choose how often you drink at bars or clubs.
* **How often do you drink at restaurants?** (`drink_frequency_8`, optional): Select how often you drink at restaurants.
* **How often do you drink at sporting events or parties?** (`drink_frequency_9`, optional): Choose how often you drink at sporting events or parties.
* **How often do you drink on weekends?** (`drink_frequency_10`, optional): Select how often you drink on weekends.
* **How often do you drink on weekdays?** (`drink_frequency_11`, optional): Choose how often you drink on weekdays.
* **How often do you drink during lunch?** (`drink_frequency_12`, optional): Select how often you drink during lunch.
* **How often do you drink during dinner?** (`drink_frequency_13`, optional): Choose how often you drink during dinner.
* **How often do you drink before bed?** (`drink_frequency_14`, optional): Select how often you drink before sleep.
* **How often do you drink before exercise or sports?** (`drink_frequency_15`, optional): Choose how often you drink before engaging in physical activities.
* **How often do you drink before medication?** (`drink_frequency_16`, optional): Select how often you drink before taking medication.
* **Submit** (`submit`, required): Click the "Submit" button to finalize your answers and submit the form.
