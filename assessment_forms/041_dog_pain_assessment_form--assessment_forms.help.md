# Dog Pain Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dog Pain Assessment Form is designed to help veterinarians assess a dog's pain level and medical history. This form is a standardized tool to ensure that the evaluation process is thorough and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant pain scale to describe the dog's current pain level from the provided options (1, 2, or 3).
2. Choose the type of pain the dog is experiencing (Acute, Chronic, or Inflammation).
3. Provide a list of the dog's medical history for at least 6 months prior to the current evaluation.
4. Select the frequency of pain from the options provided (Daily, Weekly, or Rarely).
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the dog's condition.
6. Enter the veterinarian's signature and date of assessment.
7. Enter your contact information (email or phone number).
8. Enter the time of assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pain Scale** (`dog_pain_scale`, `select_one`, required): Choose the relevant pain scale from the provided options to describe the dog's current pain level. This field helps to standardize the assessment and ensure consistency across evaluations.
* **Type** (`pain_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of pain the dog is experiencing (Acute, Chronic, or Inflammation). This helps to identify the root cause of the pain.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed list of the dog's medical history for at least 6 months prior to the current evaluation. This includes any previous diagnoses, treatments, or surgeries.
* **Pain Frequency** (`pain_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the frequency of pain from the provided options (Daily, Weekly, or Rarely). This helps to identify patterns in the dog's pain behavior.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the dog's condition. This is an opportunity to provide more detailed information that may be relevant to the assessment.
* **Veterinarian Signature** (`veterinarian_signature`, `number`, required): Enter your veterinary signature to authenticate the assessment. This ensures the form is completed by a qualified expert.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Contact Information** (`contact`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact information (email or phone number) for any follow-up communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.

**Tips**

* Complete the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
* Review and verify the information before submitting.
* Keep the form up-to-date with any changes in the dog's condition or medical history.
* Use the assessment to inform treatment decisions and ensure patient care.
