# Rolled Plate Quality Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rolled Plate Quality Assessment form is used to gather information about the quality of rolled plates in various categories. This form helps inspectors evaluate the plate's material, surface, size, weight, and other relevant details. The assessment results will be used to ensure that the plates meet the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct plate material from the options provided.
2. Choose the type of weld used on the plate.
3. Enter the plate's surface details.
4. Enter the plate's thickness, if applicable.
5. Enter the date and time of inspection.
6. Enter your name and ID as the inspector.
7. Enter the plate's size and weight, if applicable.
8. Enter any notes about the inspection, including the results.
9. If applicable, enter any comments about the plate's quality.
10. Optionally, enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.
11. Optionally, enter the date and time of review and your name and ID as the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Details**: Enter a brief description of the inspection process. (Required)
* **Plate Material**: Select the material of the plate. (Optional)
	+ Select one of the options provided.
* **Weld Type**: Choose the type of weld used on the plate. (Required)
	+ Select multiple options as applicable.
* **Plate Surface**: Enter any notes about the plate's surface. (Required)
* **Thickness**: Enter the plate's thickness, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Date of Inspection**: Enter the date of the inspection. (Optional)
* **Time of Inspection**: Enter the time of inspection. (Optional)
* **Inspector Name**: Enter your name as the inspector. (Required)
* **Inspector ID**: Enter your ID as the inspector. (Required)
* **Location**: Enter the location where the inspection took place. (Optional)
* **Plate Size**: Enter the plate's size. (Required)
* **Plate Weight**: Enter the plate's weight, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Rolled Surface**: Enter any notes about the rolled surface. (Optional)
* **Rolled Thickness**: Enter the rolled thickness, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Inspection Results**: Enter the results of the inspection. (Required)
* **Comments**: Enter any comments about the inspection. (Optional)
* **Email**: Enter your email for contact purposes. (Optional)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes. (Optional)
* **Date of Review**: Enter the date of review, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Time of Review**: Enter the time of review, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter your name as the reviewer. (Optional)
* **Reviewer ID**: Enter your ID as the reviewer. (Optional)
* **Location of Review**: Enter the location of review, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Review Comments**: Enter any comments about the review. (Optional)
* **Approved**: Select whether the plate is approved or not. (Optional)

Note: The form requires specific information and optional fields for additional details. Be accurate and complete in your responses.
