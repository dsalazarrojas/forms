# Skills Assessment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skills Assessment Consent Form is used to obtain the participant's consent and gather relevant information for the assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's information, including their name, address, phone number, and email.
2. Select the skills that will be assessed.
3. Enter the date and time of the evaluation.
4. Provide any additional comments or observations.
5. Check the boxes for "Agreement Status" and "Evaluation Status" based on your understanding of the participant's consent and the evaluation's outcome.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement to the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Participant Information** (`form_participant_info`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Consent** (`form_participant_consent`, text, required: false): Confirm the participant's consent to be evaluated.
* **Evaluator** (`form_evaluator_info`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the evaluator.
* **Organisation** (`form_organisation_info`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's organisation's name and contact information.
* **Evaluation Date** (`form_evaluation_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`form_evaluation_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Skills Assessed** (`form_skills_assessed`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the skills that will be assessed.
* **Observations** (`form_observations`, note, required: false): Provide any observations or comments about the participant.
* **Comments** (`form_comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the participant.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_additional_comments`, note, required: false): Enter any other comments or information.
* **Agree** (`form_agree`, select_one, required: true): Check the box to confirm your agreement to the assessment.
* **Deny** (`form_deny`, select_one, required: true): Check the box to deny agreement to the assessment.
* **Agreement Date** (`form_agreement_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (`form_agreement_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your agreement.
* **Signature** (`form_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Referee** (`form_referee`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the referee.
* **Organisation** (`form_organisation`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's organisation's name and contact information.
* **Position** (`form_position`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's position within their organisation.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Address** (`form_address`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's address.
* **Additional Information** (`form_additional_information`, text, required: false): Enter any other relevant information about the participant.
* **Agreement Status** (`form_agreement_status`, select_one, required: true): Check the box to confirm your agreement status.
* **Evaluation Status** (`form_evaluation_status`, select_one, required: true): Check the box to confirm the evaluation status.
* **Signature Date** (`form_signature_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your signature.
