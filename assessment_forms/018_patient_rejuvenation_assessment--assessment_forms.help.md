# Patient Rejuvenation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Rejuvenation Assessment form is designed to gather comprehensive information about a patient's medical and lifestyle history, which helps healthcare professionals understand the patient's needs and provide accurate and personalized care. This information is essential in developing a treatment plan tailored to the patient's requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Patient Goals**: Describe your primary goals or expectations from the treatment. This can include any physical, mental, or emotional objectives you wish to achieve.
2.  **Past Treatment History**: Provide information about any previous treatments or therapies you have undergone, including the nature of the treatments and their outcomes.
3.  **Medical History**: Share any significant medical events or conditions you have experienced, such as allergies, chronic illnesses, or surgeries.
4.  **Family History**: Describe your family's medical history, including any inherited conditions or genetic predispositions.
5.  **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or environmental substances.
6.  **Surgical History**: Report any past surgeries, including the reasons for the procedures and any complications or outcomes.
7.  **Family Background**: Describe your family's background, including any relevant medical history or genetic predispositions.
8.  **Medication History**: Provide information about any medications you are currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
9.  **Medication Allergies**: List any medications you are allergic to, including dosages and frequencies.
10.  **Medication Side Effects**: Report any side effects you have experienced from medications, including severity and duration.
11.  **Medication Interactions**: Describe any interactions between medications you are taking.
12.  **Medication Dosage**: Record your current medication dosages and frequencies.
