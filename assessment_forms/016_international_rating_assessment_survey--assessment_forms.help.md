# international_rating_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from international customers on their experience with our international products or services. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the survey.
2. Answer the questions according to your experience with our international products or services.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as possible.
4. Once you have completed all the questions, click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (Required): This section is a brief overview of the form. Please take a moment to read it before proceeding.
* **How would you rate this product or service?** (Optional): Please select one of the options (Poor, Average, Good, Very Good, or Excellent) to rate your experience with our product or service.
* **Would you recommend this product or service?** (Optional): Please select one of the options (True, False, or Maybe) to indicate whether you would recommend our product or service to others.
* **Which country do you currently live in?** (Optional): Select your current country of residence from the list to provide location-based insights.
* **How old are you?** (Required): Please enter your age in years.
* **What is your gender?** (Optional): Select one of the options (Female, Male, or Prefer not to say) to indicate your gender.
* **Which contact method do you usually use to contact us?** (Optional): Select all methods that apply to provide information on your preferred contact channel.
* **Please provide your contact details** (Required): Enter your contact information so we can follow-up with you if necessary.
* **Any other comments?** (Optional): If you have any further comments or suggestions, please type them in this field.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Your input will help us improve our international products or services.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
