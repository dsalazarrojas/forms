# Income Contribution Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect income information from users for assessment and decision-making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by a star \* ) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. For fields with a select options, choose one or more relevant options from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter numerical values in the number fields using decimal formatting (e.g., 100.00).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Household Income
* **Household income**: Enter the total household income for the past 12 months. This field is not required but is useful for getting a better understanding of the household's financial situation.

### Household Size
* **Household size**: Enter the number of people living in the household. This field is not required but helps in understanding the size of the household.

### Monthly Income
* **Monthly income**: Enter the total income for the past month. This field is not required but is useful for tracking month-to-month income changes.

### Employment Status
* **Employment status**: Select one or more of the following:
	+ Employed: Currently employed full-time or part-time.
	+ Self-employed: Working for oneself or a business.
	+ Unemployed: Not currently employed.
	+ Pensioner: Receiving a pension or retirement benefits.
	+ Student: Currently studying.
	+ Disability: Receiving disability benefits.

### Annual Income
* **Annual income**: Enter the total annual income for the household. This field is not required but is useful for tracking long-term income trends.

### Monthly Expenses
* **Monthly expenses**: Enter the total monthly expenses for the household. This field is not required but is useful for understanding the household's financial obligations.

### Monthly Allowance
* **Monthly allowance**: Enter the monthly allowance or allowance received by household members. This field is not required but is useful for understanding additional income sources.

### Employment Income
* **Employment income**: Enter the total income received from employment. This field is not required but is useful for tracking employment-based income.

### Social Benefit
* **Social benefit**: Enter the total social benefit or social security received by household members. This field is not required but is useful for understanding social support.

### Other Income
* **Other income**: Enter any other income sources not listed above, such as alimony or inheritances. This field is not required but is useful for collecting additional income sources.

### Employment Income Type
* **Employment income type**: Select one or more of the following:
	+ Wage or salary: Income from a job or salary.
	+ Tips: Income from tips or gratuities.
	+ Investments: Income from investments or dividends.
	+ Rent: Income from rental properties.
	+ Royalties: Income from royalties or intellectual property.
	+ Other: Other employment income sources.
 This field is not required but is useful for understanding the type of employment income.
