# Cognitive Distortions Self Assessment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Cognitive Distortions Self Assessment Checklist is a form designed to help you identify and assess your personal tendencies towards cognitive distortions. Cognitive distortions are irrational or exaggerated mental frameworks that can impact your thought processes and decision-making abilities. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you become more aware of your thought patterns and behaviors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. For each question, select the answer that best describes your behavior.
3. Be as accurate as possible, and don't overthink or second-guess.

* Be honest with yourself.
* Take your time, and don't rush through the form.
