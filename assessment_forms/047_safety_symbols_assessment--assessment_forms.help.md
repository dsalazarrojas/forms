# Safety Symbols Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the presence, absence, or confidence level of safety symbols in various contexts. It helps safety professionals evaluate the effectiveness and usage of safety symbols.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that match your assessment of the safety symbols.
2. For each question, choose one or multiple options from the select type fields.
3. If you are unsure about the answer to a question, feel free to provide feedback in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Symbols** (`safety_symbols`, `select_multiple`, not required): This field asks you to select all the safety symbols that you have observed in your assessment. Choose all the symbols that you think are present in the environment or context being assessed.
* **Confidence Levels** (`confidence_levels`, `select_one`, not required): This field is for you to express your confidence in the presence, absence, or usage of safety symbols. Choose a confidence level that accurately reflects your assessment.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): If you need to provide more information or clarify your assessment, use this field to type in any relevant feedback or comments.
