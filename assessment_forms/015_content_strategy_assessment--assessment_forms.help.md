# Content Strategy Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive assessment tool to help evaluate the current state of your content strategy. It's designed to identify strengths and weaknesses, providing valuable insights to inform and improve your content efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the requested information, and follow the prompts for each field. You will be asked to provide details on your current content goals, publishing frequency, team capacity, and measurement tools in use. This information will help us understand your content strategy and identify areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business/Department Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your business or department.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are conducting this assessment.
* **Clarity of Content Goals** (`goal_clarity_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the clarity of your content goals on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is "No goals" and 5 is "Documented and shared goals".
* **Publishing Frequency** (`publishing_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you publish new content, with options ranging from "Daily" to "Sporadically".
* **Number of Channels** (`channel_diversity`, `number`, required): Enter the number of channels you actively manage (e.g., Website, Social, Email, etc.).
* **Current Team Capacity** (`team_capacity_evaluation`, `select_one`, required): Rate your current team's capacity on a scale from "Overwhelmed - Need more help" to "No dedicated team".
* **Measurement Tools in Use** (`measurement_tools_used`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the measurement tools you use for content performance analysis (e.g., Google Analytics 4, Social Media Native Analytics, etc.).
* **Perceived ROI of Content Marketing** (`content_roi_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the perceived return on investment (ROI) of your content marketing on a scale from "Low ROI - Cost center only" to "High ROI - Core growth driver".
* **Biggest Strategy Gap** (`biggest_strategy_gap`, `text`, required): Describe the main gap in your current strategy.
* **Priority for Strategic Improvement** (`priority_for_improvement`, `select_one`, required): Rate the priority level for improvement on a scale from "High - Need immediate overhaul" to "Low - Small tweaks only".
* **Additional Assessment Notes** (`additional_assessment_notes`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to provide in this assessment.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when rating your current state of content strategy.
- Select all applicable measurement tools used.
- If you have any specific pain points or areas for improvement, describe them in the "Biggest Strategy Gap" field.
- This form is a tool to help you improve your content strategy, so don't hesitate to add any additional comments or notes as needed.
