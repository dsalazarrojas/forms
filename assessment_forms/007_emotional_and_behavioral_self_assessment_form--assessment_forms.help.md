# emotional_and_behavioral_self_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emotional and behavioral self-assessment form is a tool for individuals to evaluate their emotional and behavioral patterns. It is designed to help you understand your feelings, thoughts, and behaviors related to your emotional well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select the options that best describe your emotional state and experiences.
3. Fill in the date and time of your last experience with emotional intensity.
4. Provide any additional comments or notes in the "Additional comments" field.
5. Submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How do you feel about yourself** (`1`, `text`, `yes`): This question is designed to help you reflect on your overall emotional state and how you feel about yourself. Please provide a brief description of your feelings.
* **How often do you experience the following emotions?** (`2`, `select_multiple`, `yes`): Select all the emotions that you frequently experience. This will help us understand your emotional state.
	+ Sadness
	+ Anxiety
	+ Anger
	+ Frustration
	+ Excitement
	+ Happiness
	+ Fear
	+ Guilt
	+ Disgust
* **What is your current emotional state?** (`3`, `select_one`, `yes`): Choose the emotional state that best describes how you feel right now.
	+ Happy
	+ Sad
	+ Anxious
	+ Frustrated
	+ Angry
* **How often do you experience emotional intensity?** (`4`, `select_one`, `yes`): Select how frequently you experience emotional intensity.
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Almost Daily
* **Do you engage in the following behavioral patterns?** (`5`, `select_multiple`, `yes`): Select all the behavioral patterns that you engage in.
	+ Avoiding
	+ Emotional
	+ Behavioral
	+ Social
	+ Interpersonal
	+ Cognitive
* **What is your emotional state when you experience emotional intensity?** (`6`, `select_one`, `yes`): Choose the emotional state that you experience when you are emotionally intense.
	+ Happy
	+ Sad
	+ Anxious
	+ Frustrated
	+ Angry
* **Date of last occurrence** (`7`, `date`, `yes`): Enter the date of the last time you experienced emotional intensity.
* **Time of last occurrence** (`8`, `time`, `yes`): Enter the time of the last time you experienced emotional intensity.
* **Frequency of last occurrence** (`9`, `select_one`, `yes`): Select the frequency of the last time you experienced emotional intensity.
	+ Rarely
	+ Occasionally
	+ Frequently
	+ Almost Daily
* **Additional comments** (`10`, `note`, `no`): Provide any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Submitted by** (`11`, `text`, `yes`): Enter your name or identity.
* **Contact information** (`12`, `email`, `yes`): Enter your email or contact information.

Tips:
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out.
* This form is for personal and non-clinical use only.
