# team_strength_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The team_strength_assessment form is designed to assess the strength of a team in various aspects, including team strengthening, team collaboration, team dynamics, and team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question on the form, providing as much detail as possible for each section.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you have selected the most accurate responses.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your team lead or a team member for guidance.
4. Once you have completed all the sections, click on the "Submit" button to save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Strengthening** (`team_strengthening`, select_multiple, optional): This section allows you to indicate whether your team has strengthened or improved in various ways, such as developing new skills, taking on new challenges, or adapting to changes. Select all the ways in which you feel your team has grown.
* **Team Collaboration** (`team_collaboration`, text, optional): Describe how your team works together to achieve common goals. Provide examples of successful collaborations or initiatives that have improved team performance.
* **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether your team maintains positive team dynamics, such as respect, trust, and open communication. Select "Yes" if you feel that your team has a strong sense of camaraderie and cooperation.
* **Team Performance** (`team_performance`, select_one, optional): Rate your team's performance in recent challenges, such as completing projects on time, meeting expectations, or achieving goals. Select "Yes" if you feel that your team has performed exceptionally well, and "No" if there were areas for improvement.
