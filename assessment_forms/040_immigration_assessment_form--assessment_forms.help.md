# Immigration Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about clients seeking immigration services. It's used to assess their immigration status, gather contact details, and capture any necessary notes for the assessment process. The form is part of the immigration services process and is intended for internal use by our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's purpose and fields to understand what information is required.
2.  Start by filling out the client's information on **Client Info** page.
3.  Move on to the **Assessment Details** page to select the relevant assessment details.
4.  On the **Contact Info** page, enter the client's contact details.
5.  Choose the client's **Immigration Status**.
6.  Select the client's **Country of Residence** from the list.
7.  Fill in the client's **Date of Birth**.
8.  Enter any additional **Assessment Notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Client Info (Page 1)
*   **Client Info (1)** (`Client Info`, `number`, required: false): Enter the client's ID number or other identifying information.

### 2. Assessment Details (Page 2)
*   **Assessment Details (2)** (`Assessment Details`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    *   Select **First Name** to indicate the client's first name.
    *   Select **Last Name** to indicate the client's last name.
    *   Select **Country** to indicate the client's country of origin.
    *   Select **Date of Birth** to enter the client's date of birth.

### 3. Contact Info (Page 3)
*   **Contact Info (3)** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's contact information, such as phone number or email.

### 4. Immigration Status (Page 4)
*   **Immigration Status (4)** (`Immigration Status`, `select_one`, required: false):
    *   Select **Refugee** if the client is a refugee.
    *   Select **Permanent Resident** if the client is a permanent resident.
    *   Select **Temporary Resident** if the client is a temporary resident.

### 5. Country of Residence (Page 5)
*   **Country of Residence (6)** (`Country of Residence`, `select_one`, required: false):
    *   Select **Australia** if the client's country of residence is Australia.
    *   Select **Canada** if the client's country of residence is Canada.
    *   Select **New Zealand** if the client's country of residence is New Zealand.
    *   Select **United Kingdom** if the client's country of residence is the United Kingdom.
    *   Select **United States** if the client's country of residence is the United States.

### 6. Assessment Notes (Page 7)
*   **Assessment Notes (5)** (`Assessment Notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the client's assessment.

### 7. Date of Birth (Page 8)
*   **Date of Birth (7)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter the client's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
