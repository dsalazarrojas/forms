# Ultimate Frisbee Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills of a frisbee player, evaluating their throwing, catching, and other abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions on this form to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the options that most accurately describe your skills in each category.
3. Enter your scores for each skill, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tryout Info** (id: 1): Describe the tryout you are being assessed on, including the date, time, and location.
* **Player Info** (id: 2): Select your playing position, choosing from Forward, Backward, Pivot, and Wing.
* **Position** (id: 3): If your position is not already selected in Player Info, select it here.
* **Score Card** (id: 4): Enter any relevant notes about your performance during the tryout.
* **Throw** (id: 5): Select the type of throw you are most comfortable with, choosing from Forehand, Backhand, and Overhand.
* **Catch** (id: 6): Select the type of catch you are most comfortable with, choosing from Low, High, and Block.
* **Dodge** (id: 7): Enter a numerical score for your dodging ability, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Sprint** (id: 8): Enter a numerical score for your sprinting ability, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Throw Dodge** (id: 9): Enter a numerical score for your throwing and dodging ability, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Distance** (id: 10): Enter a description of your playing distance, such as "short", "medium", or "long".
* **Speed** (id: 11): Enter a description of your playing speed, such as "slow", "average", or "fast".
* **Endurance** (id: 12): Enter a description of your playing endurance, such as "low", "average", or "high".
* **Agility** (id: 13): Enter a description of your playing agility, such as "poor", "average", or "excellent".
* **Vision** (id: 14): Enter a description of your playing vision, such as "poor", "average", or "excellent".
* **Strength** (id: 15): Enter a description of your playing strength, such as "weak", "average", or "strong".
* **Hand-Eye** (id: 16): Enter a description of your playing hand-eye coordination, such as "poor", "average", or "excellent".
* **Footwork** (id: 17): Enter a description of your playing footwork, such as "poor", "average", or "excellent".
* **Endurance Score** (id: 18): Enter a numerical score for your endurance, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Agility Score** (id: 19): Enter a numerical score for your agility, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Hand-Eye Score** (id: 20): Enter a numerical score for your hand-eye coordination, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Footwork Score** (id: 21): Enter a numerical score for your footwork, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Speed Score** (id: 22): Enter a numerical score for your speed, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Stamina Score** (id: 23): Enter a numerical score for your stamina, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Vision Score** (id: 24): Enter a numerical score for your vision, with higher scores indicating better performance.
* **Strength Score** (id: 25): Enter a numerical score for your strength, with higher scores indicating better performance.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to carefully consider each question.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or guidance.
