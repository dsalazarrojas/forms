## Purpose
The Textile Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate your knowledge on various topics related to textiles, including fibers, fabric structures, and textile processes. This form consists of multiple-choice and text-based questions that aim to gauge your understanding of these subjects.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully, ensuring you understand what is being asked.
2. Select the most appropriate option from the choices provided for multiple-choice questions.
3. Enter your answer in the text field for questions that require a written response.
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
