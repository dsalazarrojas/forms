# IT Hardware And Software Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help IT departments assess their current hardware and software needs, and prioritize their upgrade or acquisition plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page of the form, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the options that apply to your department's needs.
3. If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask your IT department for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Hardware Needs
* **Hardware Needs** (`hardware_needs`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the current hardware needs of your department, including any specific equipment or devices needed.
* **Software Needs** (`software_needs`, `text`, required/optional): List any software applications or systems required by your department.
* **Hardware Needs Description** (`hardware_needs_description`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the hardware needs, including any specific requirements or specifications.
* **Software Needs Description** (`software_needs_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the software applications or systems required, including any specific requirements or specifications.
* **Hardware Needs Comments** (`hardware_needs_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or questions about the hardware needs.
* **Upgrades Priorities** (`upgrades_priorities`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the priority level for each type of upgrade, from High to Medium to Low.
* **Budget Impact** (`budget_impact`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the impact level on the budget, from High to Medium to Low.
* **Employee Support** (`employee_support`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of support needed from employees, from Across Departments to Across Locations.
* **Team Needs** (`team_needs`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a description of the team's specific needs.
