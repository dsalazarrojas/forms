# Toulouse Olfactory Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toulouse Olfactory Assessment Questionnaire is a tool used by clinicians to assess a patient's olfactory function and related symptoms. It is essential for the accurate diagnosis and management of various conditions affecting the sense of smell.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please start by reading and understanding the form's purpose and requirements.
2. Answer each question or section of the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required sections to ensure a comprehensive assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**:
This is a brief description of the purpose and scope of the form.
* **Patient Information** (page 2):
This section is for collecting essential information about the patient, including any relevant medical history.
* **Demographics** (page 3):
Select the relevant option from the dropdown list to indicate your demographic information (e.g., 1 or 2).
* **Olfactory Questionnaire** (page 4):
Select all the options that best describe your olfactory symptoms or experiences.
* **Olfactory Screening** (page 5):
If applicable, enter the date you first noticed your olfactory symptoms.
* **Olfactory Symptoms** (page 6):
Select all the options that best describe your olfactory symptoms.
* **Comments** (page 7):
Use this section to provide any additional information about your olfactory symptoms or experiences.
* **Submitted By** (page 8):
Enter your email address or other contact information.
* **Submitted At** (page 9):
Enter the date and time you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Please answer each question accurately and honestly to ensure the most comprehensive assessment of your condition.
* If you are unsure about any section, please ask your clinician for guidance.
* Please do not skip any required sections to ensure a thorough assessment.
