# Hospice Nursing Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hospice Nursing Assessment Form is used to collect information about a patient's status, medical conditions, and care needs to ensure they receive appropriate hospice care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals to access the patient's data.
* Review the patient's medical history and conditions before starting the form.
* Select the relevant options for each field based on the patient's status and needs.
* Complete all required fields marked with * (e.g., `patient_status`, `medical_condition`, `hospice_type`, `primary_care_giver`, `social_status`, `hospice_team`).
* Answer all other fields as necessary to provide a comprehensive assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_status** (`Active`, `Inactive`, required): Select the current status of the patient.
* **medical_condition** (`Yes`, `No`, required): Check if the patient has a medical condition that requires hospice care.
* **pain_level** (`High`, `Low`, optional): Select the patient's pain level, if applicable.
* **medication_allergies** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Check if the patient has any medication allergies.
* **hospice_type** (`Yes`, `No`, required): Confirm if the patient requires hospice care.
* **primary_care_giver** (`Yes`, `No`, required): Confirm the patient's primary care giver.
* **social_status** (`Active`, `Inactive`, required): Select the patient's social status.
* **patient_demeanor** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Check if the patient has a specific demeanor.
* **hospice_medical_history** (`text`, optional): Provide any relevant medical history.
* **medication_side_effects** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Check if the patient experiences any side effects from medications.
* **care_goals** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select the patient's care goals.
* **hospice_team** (`Yes`, `No`, required): Confirm if the patient requires a hospice team.
* **hospice_location** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Check if the patient requires hospice care at a specific location.
* **medical_history** (`text`, optional): Provide any relevant medical history.
* **patient_family** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Check if the patient has a family member involved in their care.
* **hospice_needs** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select any additional hospice needs.
* **hospice_goals** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select any additional hospice goals.
* **hospice_status** (`Active`, `Inactive`, optional): Select the patient's status (note: this field appears to be a duplicate of `patient_status`).
* **hospice_plan** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select if the patient has a hospice plan.
* **hospice_id** (`text`, optional): Provide any relevant patient ID.
* **hospice_referral_source** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Check if the patient's referral source is relevant.
* **hospice_nurse** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select if the patient requires a hospice nurse.
* **hospice_nurse_contact** (`Yes`, `No`, optional): Select if the patient requires contact with a hospice nurse.
* **hospice_nurse_name** (`text`, optional): Provide the name of the hospice nurse.
* **hospice_nurse_phone** (`text`, optional): Provide the contact phone number of the hospice nurse. 

Note: This form is designed to collect comprehensive and accurate information. Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
