# Filter Theory Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Filter Theory Questionnaire is designed to assess your familiarity, understanding, and application of filter theory, as well as your expectations for its results in real-world scenarios. The questionnaire aims to gather insights into your experiences and perspectives on filter theory, which is a concept used to understand and work with data that has been filtered or preprocessed in some way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the questionnaire, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the "Familiarity" and "Understanding" sections with a brief description of your experience and knowledge about filter theory.
2. In the "Application" section, provide a specific example of how you apply filter theory in your work or studies.
3. In the "Submission Results" section, choose one of the options that best describe the results you expect from applying filter theory:
	* Consistent Results
	* Inconsistent Results
	* None
4. In the "Educators", "Trainers", and "Ideal Users" sections, choose one group that you think is most interested in filter theory, most likely to use it, and would benefit most from it, respectively.
5. In the "Submission Results Ideal" and "Consistent Results Ideal" sections, describe the ideal results you expect from applying filter theory.
6. Finally, in the "Real-World Scenarios" section, describe a real-world example of how you would apply filter theory.
