This form is designed to collect data about your shopping experience and loyalty to our brand. Your responses will help us understand your preferences and behaviors, which will inform our business decisions to better serve you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer each question to the best of your ability. You can select one or multiple options for questions with multiple-choice options. If you have any difficulties or need clarification on a question, please don't hesitate to ask.
