# speechTherapyAssessmentForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The speechTherapyAssessmentForm is used to gather information for assessment and evaluation purposes in a speech therapy setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's age in the "Age" field.
3. Select the patient's gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the date of the assessment or evaluation in the "Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the assessment or evaluation in the "Time" field.
6. Enter any relevant notes or comments about the patient in the "Note" field.
7. Enter the patient's email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select the category of the assessment or evaluation from the "Category" dropdown menu.
10. Enter a brief description of the assessment or evaluation in the "Description" field.
10. If applicable, enter the tool assigned to the patient in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patientname`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select the patient's gender.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the patient.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select the category of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assignedtool`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the tool assigned to the patient.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or administrator for clarification.
* Please note that this form is for assessment and evaluation purposes only, and any sensitive or private information should not be entered into this form.
