# Substance Influence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the potential influence of substances on individuals in specific incidents. It's meant for use in medical, social services, or healthcare settings where assessing the impact of substances is crucial.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Read the form carefully**: Before filling out the form, take a moment to review each field and understand its purpose.
2.  **Enter required information**: Fill out the fields marked as "required."
3.  **Select relevant options**: For fields like "Suspected Substance" and "Incident Observation," choose the options that best describe the incident.
4.  **Enter dates and times accurately**: Use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YY) and times (HH:MM AM/PM).
5.  **Enter your contact information**: If required, enter your email and phone number for easy contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Description** (`incident_description`): A brief description of the incident, including the circumstances surrounding the suspected substance influence.
*   **Suspected Substance** (`substance_suspected`): Choose one or more of the substances from the list that best describe the suspected substance involved in the incident.
*   **Assessor Details** (`assessor_details`): A note field for additional information about yourself as the assessor, if needed.
*   **Incident Observation** (`incident_observation`): Select one or more symptoms or effects that you've observed during the incident, such as headache or lack of coordination.
*   **Assessor's Rating** (`assessor_rating`): A rating system for the assessors' opinion on the substance influence, though it's currently not defined, please provide a numerical value as a placeholder.
*   **Date of Incident** (`incident_date`): Enter the date of the incident using the format MM/DD/YY.
*   **Time of Incident** (`incident_time`): Enter the time of the incident using the format HH:MM AM/PM.
*   **Assessor Email** (`assessor_email`): Enter your email address for easy contact.
*   **Assessor Phone** (`assessor_phone`): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Additional information about the incident or substance influence.
*   **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): The location where the incident occurred.

## Tips
*   Always ensure you're using the correct format for dates and times.
*   Use the provided options for "Suspected Substance" and "Incident Observation" to help ensure accuracy and consistency.
*   Keep the "Assessor's Rating" field clear and concise.
*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
*   Use the "Notes" field to add any additional context or information that may be helpful.
*   Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and consistency.

This guide should help you understand the Substance Influence Assessment form and its intended use. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to your role's specific guidelines or reach out to the form owner.
