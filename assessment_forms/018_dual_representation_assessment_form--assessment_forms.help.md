# Dual Representation Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Dual Representation Assessment Form is used to collect participant information and assessment results in an educational or training context. This form is designed to gather data on participants' dual representation, which may include their experiences, perceptions, and outcomes related to training or assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the participant's name.
2. Enter the participant's email address.
3. Provide the participant's phone number (if applicable).
4. Select the relevant formats (format_1, format_2, format_3) that apply to the participant.
5. Fill in any additional participant information.
6. Enter any relevant participant notes.
7. Provide any additional participant information.
8. Enter any training information.
9. Enter any notes about the training.
10. Fill in the assessment results.
11. Compare the results (comparison_results).
12. Select the time period for the assessment.
13. Enter the date of submission.
14. Fill in participant surveys (participant_survey, participant_survey_1, participant_survey_2, participant_survey_3, participant_survey_4, participant_survey_5, participant_survey_6, participant_survey_7, participant_survey_8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (text, optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **participant_email** (email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **participant_phone** (text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number (if applicable).
* **format_1** (select_one, optional): Select the format that applies to the participant. (Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **format_2** (select_one, optional): Select the format that applies to the participant. (Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **format_3** (select_one, optional): Select the format that applies to the participant. (Options: 'Yes', 'No')
* **participant_info** (text, optional): Fill in any additional participant information.
* **participant_notes** (note, optional): Enter any relevant participant notes.
* **participant_additional_info** (text, optional): Provide any additional participant information.
* **training_info** (text, optional): Enter any relevant training information.
* **training_notes** (note, optional): Enter any notes about the training.
* **assessment_results** (text, optional): Fill in the assessment results.
* **comparison_results** (text, optional): Compare the results.
* **time_period** (time, optional): Select the time period for the assessment. (Note: This field is not explicitly defined, so it's likely a placeholder for a date/time picker or a dropdown menu.)
* **submission_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **participant_survey** (text, optional): Fill in participant surveys.
* **participant_survey_1** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 1.
* **participant_survey_2** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 2.
* **participant_survey_3** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 3.
* **participant_survey_4** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 4.
* **participant_survey_5** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 5.
* **participant_survey_6** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 6.
* **participant_survey_7** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 7.
* **participant_survey_8** (text, optional): Fill in participant survey 8.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
