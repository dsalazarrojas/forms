# Social Work Introduction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to provide social workers with a comprehensive overview of a client's social and medical history, current support systems, and ongoing treatment plans. It helps social workers identify areas of concern and develop effective treatment strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information (Client Information)
2. Provide a brief history of the client's social history (Social History)
3. Describe the client's current support system (Support System)
4. Report the client's medical status (Medical Status)
5. Share information about the client's mental status (Mental Status)
6. Indicate any substance use (Substance Use)
7. List the client's current support system (Social Support System)
8. Describe the client's current status (Current Status)
9. Document the client's medical history (Medical History)
10. Report the client's mental health (Mental Health)
11. List the client's current medications (Current Medications)
12. Document any medical procedures (Medical Procedure)
13. Indicate the client's current diagnosis (Current Diagnosis)
14. Describe any medical conditions (Medical Condition)
15. Report the client's mental status (Mental Status 2)
16. Provide information about the social worker's involvement (Social Worker Info)
17. Define the client's goals (Client Goals)
18. List the services currently being provided (Current Services)
19. Indicate the outcome of the assessment (Assessment Outcome)
20. Outline the treatment plan (Treatment Plan)
21. Indicate if follow-up is necessary (Follow-Up)
22. Determine the frequency of assessments (Assessment Frequency)
23. Record the social worker's notes (Social Worker Notes)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (1)**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Social History (2)**: Describe the client's background and any relevant past experiences.
* **Family History (3)**: Provide information about the client's family dynamics and relationships.
* **Support System (4)**: Describe the client's current support system, including family, friends, and support groups.
* **Medical Status (5)**: Report the client's current medical status, including any medical conditions or treatments.
* **Mental Status (6)**: Describe the client's current mental status, including any mental health conditions.
* **Substance Use (7)**: Indicate if the client has any substance use issues.
* **Current Support (8)**: Describe the client's current support system.
* **Social Support System (9)**: Describe the client's current social support system.
* **Current Status (10)**: Report the client's current status, including any relevant details.
* **Medical History (11)**: Document the client's medical history, including any past medical procedures.
* **Mental Health (12)**: Report the client's mental health status.
* **Current Medications (13)**: List the client's current medications.
* **Medical Procedure (14)**: Document any medical procedures the client has undergone.
* **Current Diagnosis (15)**: Indicate the client's current diagnosis.
* **Medical Condition (16)**: Describe any medical conditions the client has.
* **Mental Status 2 (17)**: Describe the client's mental status.
* **Social Worker Info (18)**: Provide information about the social worker's involvement with the client.
* **Client Goals (19)**: Define the client's goals.
* **Current Services (20)**: List the services currently being provided to the client.
* **Assessment Outcome (21)**: Indicate the outcome of the assessment.
* **Treatment Plan (22)**: Outline the treatment plan for the client.
* **Follow-Up (23)**: Indicate if follow-up is necessary.
* **Assessment Frequency (24)**: Determine the frequency of assessments.
* **Social Worker Notes (25)**: Record any notes from the social worker.
