# Family Heritage Division Candidate Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Heritage Division Candidate Assessment Form is used to collect relevant information about candidates for family division assessment. It helps in evaluating candidates' suitability for joining your family division.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Candidate Info section with basic information about the candidate.
2. Select the Assessment Questions that apply to the candidate.
3. Provide answers to the selected assessment questions.
4. Enter the Candidate Score and Family Info.
5. Enter the Family History.
6. Enter the Division Info.
7. Enter the Division Score.
8. If applicable, enter the Assigned Tool used in the assessment.
9. Add any additional Notes if needed.
10. Enter the candidate's Email.
11. Enter the candidate's Phone number.
12. Enter the candidate's Date of Birth.
13. Enter the Time of evaluation.
14. Set the Assessment Form Status as Active, Inactive or Pending.
15. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
16. Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
17. Enter the IP address of the person who submitted the form.
18. Enter the Assessment Form ID.
19. Enter the username of the person who submitted the form.
20. Enter the location from which the form was submitted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Info** (`text`): Enter the basic information about the candidate.
* **Assessment Questions** (`select_multiple`): Select the assessment questions that apply to the candidate.
* **Candidate Answers** (`text`): Provide answers to the selected assessment questions.
* **Candidate Score** (`number`): Enter the score for the candidate.
* **Family Info** (`text`): Enter the family information.
* **Family History** (`text`): Enter the family history.
* **Division Info** (`text`): Enter the division information.
* **Division Score** (`number`): Enter the division score.
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`): If applicable, enter the tool used in the assessment.
* **Notes** (`note`): Enter any additional notes about the candidate.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the candidate's email.
* **Phone** (`text`): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date`): Enter the candidate's date of birth.
* **Time Of Evaluation** (`time`): Enter the time of evaluation.
* **Assessment Form Status** (`select_one`): Set the status of the assessment form as Active, Inactive or Pending.
* **Submitted By** (`text`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submission Date** (`date`): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`time`): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted To** (`text`): Enter the name of the person or system to whom the form was submitted.
* **Submission IP** (`text`): Enter the IP address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Assessment Form ID** (`text`): Enter the ID of the assessment form.
* **Submitted By Tool** (`text`): Enter the name of the tool used to submit the form.
* **Submitted By Username** (`text`): Enter the username of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted From** (`text`): Enter the location from which the form was submitted.
* **Submitted At** (`date`): Enter the date when the form was submitted.
