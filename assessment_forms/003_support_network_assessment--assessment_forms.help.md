# Support Network Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the support network of an individual, evaluating their connections and relationships with different systems that provide emotional support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your organization in the "Organization" field.
4. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
5. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
6. Enter a brief description of your job in the "Job Description" field.
7. Enter the number of years you have been serving in your current role in the "Years of Service" field.
8. Select the systems that provide you with emotional support from the "Support Systems" list.
9. If you have any other support systems not listed, describe them in the "Other Support Systems" field.
10. Select the communication channels you use to communicate with your support systems in the "Communication Channels" list.
11. Indicate how often you communicate with your support systems using the "How often do you communicate with these systems?" options.
12. Rate the quality of communication with your support systems using the "What is the quality of communication with these systems?" options.
13. Describe any barriers you face when communicating with your support systems in the "What are some barriers you face when relying on these systems?" field.
14. Rate your emotional intelligence using the "How would you rate your Emotional Intelligence?" options.
15. Indicate whether you take care of yourself using the "Do you take care of yourself?" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Organization** (`organization`, Text, Optional): Enter your organization.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter your department.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of years you have been serving in your current role.
* **Support Systems** (`support_systems`, Select One, Optional): Select one or more systems that provide you with emotional support.
* **Other Support Systems** (`support_systems_other`, Text, Optional): If you have any other support systems not listed, describe them in this field.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the communication channels you use to communicate with your support systems.
* **How often do you communicate with these systems?** (`communication_frequency`, Select One, Optional): Indicate how often you communicate with your support systems.
* **What is the quality of communication with these systems?** (`communication_quality`, Select One, Optional): Rate the quality of communication with your support systems.
* **What are some barriers you face when relying on these systems?** (`support_systems_barriers`, Note, Optional): Describe any barriers you face when relying on these systems.
* **How would you rate your Emotional Intelligence?** (`emotional_intelligence`, Select One, Optional): Rate your emotional intelligence.
* **Do you take care of yourself?** (`self_care`, Select One, Optional): Indicate whether you take care of yourself.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and thorough when completing this form.
* If a system is not listed, you can describe it in the "Other Support Systems" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrator.
* Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
