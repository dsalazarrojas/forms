# Homework Self Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homework Self Evaluation Form is designed for students to evaluate and submit their homework, providing an overview of their effort, challenge faced, learning outcome, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Select the correct homework type and topic from the corresponding dropdown menus.
* Enter your effort and challenge faced in the respective text fields.
* Choose your self-rating and teacher rating using the rating options.
* Enter any additional feedback or comments to your teacher in the "Feedback to Teacher" and "Teacher Comments to Student" fields.
* Select the submission status and date of submission from the corresponding dropdown menus.
* Upload any relevant submission file (if required).
* Enter your self-assessment and submit any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homework Type** (`homework_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct homework type from the options provided.
* **Topic** (`homework_topic`, `select_one`, required): Choose the relevant topic from the options provided.
* **Homework Effort** (`homework_effort`, `number`, optional): Enter a numerical value to describe the effort you put into the homework.
* **Challenge Faced** (`challenge_faced`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the challenges you faced during the homework process.
* **Learning Outcome** (`learning_outcome`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief description of what you learned from the homework.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Self Rating** (`self_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your own performance using the provided options.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate your teacher's feedback and comments using the provided options.
* **Feedback to Teacher** (`feedback_to_teacher`, `text`, optional): Provide any feedback or comments to your teacher.
* **Submission Comments** (`submission_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments about your submission.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your submission.
* **Submission Status Date** (`submission_status_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the submission status change.
* **Submission Status Change** (`submission_status_change`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments about the submission status change.
* **Submission File** (`submission_file`, `text`, optional): Upload any relevant submission file.
* **Teacher Solutions** (`teacher_solutions`, `note`, optional): View your teacher's solutions and feedback.
* **Teacher Comments to Student** (`teacher_comments_to_student`, `note`, optional): View your teacher's comments and feedback.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, `note`, optional): View your teacher's comments and feedback.
* **Self Reflection** (`self_reflection`, `note`, optional): Enter a brief reflection of your learning process.
* **Student Solutions** (`student_solutions`, `note`, optional): View your own solutions and work.
