## Faculty Promotion Peer Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Faculty Promotion Peer Assessment Form is a tool used by peers to evaluate a faculty member's performance in their department. This form will help assessors provide detailed feedback on a faculty member's strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Department**: Enter the name of the faculty member's department.
2.  **Faculty Member**: Enter the name of the faculty member being assessed.
3.  **Performance**: Select the level of performance you believe the faculty member has demonstrated:
	*   Excellent: The faculty member's performance is exceptional and exceeds expectations.
	*   Good: The faculty member's performance is satisfactory and meets expectations.
	*   Fair: The faculty member's performance is average and requires improvement.
	*   Poor: The faculty member's performance is below expectations.
4.  **Strengths**: Describe the faculty member's strengths, such as research, teaching, service, or administration.
5.  **Recommendations**: Provide specific recommendations for the faculty member's development, such as additional training or mentorship opportunities.
6.  **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions for the faculty member's improvement.
7.  **Email**: Enter the faculty member's email address for communication purposes.
8.  **Phone**: Enter the faculty member's phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips

*   Please be thorough and accurate when completing this form.
*   Avoid duplicating work that has already been completed.
*   Ensure that your assessment is fair and consistent with the faculty member's performance.
*   Keep your comments and recommendations specific and constructive.
