## Infection Control Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Infection Control Assessment Form is a tool to help individuals or organizations evaluate their performance in maintaining infection control and patient safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply provide answers to each question on the pages listed below. Be as detailed and specific as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Infection Control Practice**: Describe your current practice for infection control, including any measures you take to prevent the spread of infections.
* **Compliance with Safety Protocols**: Explain how you comply with safety protocols in place to prevent the spread of infections.
* **Patient Care**: Describe how you ensure patient safety and comfort in your facility.
* **Infection Reporting**: Explain your process for reporting and tracking infections.
* **Incident Reporting**: Describe how you report and respond to incidents that may lead to infection.
* **Patient Safety**: Describe how you maintain patient safety in your facility.
* **Staff Safety**: Explain how you ensure staff safety in your facility.
* **Education and Training**: Describe any education and training programs you have for staff on infection control and patient safety.
* **Policies and Procedures**: Describe your policies and procedures for infection control and patient safety.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you are unsure of the answer to a question, feel free to ask for clarification.
