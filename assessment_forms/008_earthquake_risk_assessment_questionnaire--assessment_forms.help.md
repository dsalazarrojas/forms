# Earthquake Risk Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Earthquake Risk Assessment Questionnaire is a general-purpose tool designed to gather information about the earthquake risk assessment of a specific area.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the provided fields with accurate and relevant information.
- Make sure to answer each field according to the instructions and guidelines provided.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false): This field is for providing any general information about the area being assessed.
- **Building Characteristics** (`building_characteristics`, text, required: false): This field is for describing the building's features, such as its age, size, and structure.
- **Earthquake History** (`earthquake_history`, text, required: false): This field is for recording the history of earthquakes in the area, including past damage or loss of life.
- **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, text, required: false): This field is for listing any risk factors that could increase the likelihood or severity of earthquake damage, such as soil conditions or nearby fault lines.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): This field is for providing the contact information for emergency services or contacts in the area.
- **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, text, required: false): This field is for specifying any preferences for emergency contact, such as language or special needs.
- **Safety Features** (`safety_features`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for indicating if the building has any safety features, such as a safe room or emergency exit.
- **Emergency Preparedness** (`emergency_preparedness`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for declaring if the building has any emergency preparedness measures in place, such as evacuation plans or emergency kits.
- **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is for providing additional information about emergency contacts or services.
- **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, select_one, required: false): This field is for specifying the follow-up plan after the assessment, such as a severe damage likelihood or high probability of damage.
