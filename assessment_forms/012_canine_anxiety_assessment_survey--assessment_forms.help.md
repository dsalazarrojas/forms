# Canine Anxiety Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Canine Anxiety Assessment Survey is a tool designed to help pet owners identify and document their dog's anxiety patterns and behaviors. This form will help you gather essential information about your dog's anxiety triggers, symptoms, and management strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the survey carefully and answer each question based on your observations of your dog's behavior.
2. Select all applicable options for each question.
3. Provide honest and accurate information about your dog's anxiety patterns and behaviors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the owner of the dog.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your contact email address so we can reach out if needed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Dog Name** (`dog_name`, text, required): Enter your dog's name.
* **Dog Age** (`dog_age`, number, required): Enter your dog's age in years.
* **Breed or Mix** (`dog_breed`, text, optional): Enter your dog's breed or mix.
* **How long have you owned this dog?** (`time_owned`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or More than 10 years.
* **Has your dog shown anxiety symptoms before?** (`behavior_history`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Yes, No, or Unsure.
* **Is there a known trauma or trigger in the dog's history?** (`trauma_history`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Yes, No, or Unknown.
* **What situations trigger anxiety in your dog?** (`primary_triggers`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options from the list.
* **How intense are the anxiety responses?** (`trigger_intensity`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Mild - occasional whimpering, Moderate - clear distress behaviors, Severe - significant behavioral changes, or Extreme - panic response.
* **What physical symptoms does your dog show?** (`physical_symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all observed physical symptoms from the list.
* **What behavioral symptoms does your dog show?** (`behavioral_symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all observed behavioral symptoms from the list.
* **How often do anxiety episodes occur?** (`episode_frequency`, select_one, required): Select one of the options: Daily, Several times per week, Weekly, Monthly, Occasionally, or Rarely.
* **How long do episodes typically last?** (`episode_duration`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Minutes, 30 minutes to 1 hour, 1-2 hours, Several hours, or All day.
* **What management strategies do you currently use?** (`current_strategies`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options from the list.
* **How effective are current strategies?** (`strategy_effectiveness`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options: Very Effective, Somewhat Effective, Minimally Effective, Not Effective, or No strategies in place.
