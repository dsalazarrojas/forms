# Strategist Or Sequencer Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategist Or Sequencer Assessment form is designed to evaluate and compare the abilities and skills of individuals in a given team, focusing on their individual and collective performance. This form is intended for team leaders or managers to use when evaluating team members or evaluating team performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have not recently assessed the team member.
2. Choose the team the member belongs to from the "team" field.
3. Evaluate the person's strategist and sequencer skills and abilities by rating them in the "rating" field.
4. Provide any additional comments about the person's performance in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name**: Enter the first name of the team member being assessed.
* **email**: Enter the email address of the team member being assessed.
* **phone**: Enter the phone number of the team member being assessed.
* **team**: Select the team the member belongs to. This field is a multiple-select field, allowing you to choose one or more teams the member belongs to.
* **rating**: Rate the team member's strategist and sequencer skills and abilities on a scale of 1-10.
* **comments**: Provide any additional comments about the person's performance.

## Tips
* Make sure to rate the person's strategist and sequencer skills and abilities honestly and fairly.
* Consider providing constructive feedback to help the team member improve their performance.
* If you need to re-evaluate a team member, make sure to update the previous assessment accordingly.
