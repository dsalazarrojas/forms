# Personal Rights Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Rights Assessment Form is designed to assess an individual's understanding and awareness of personal rights.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly, based on your best understanding.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the answer that best reflects your situation.
3. If you have additional comments or thoughts, please use the free-text fields provided.
4. Make sure to review and understand each question before answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Rights Form** (Note): This section is a note field where you can provide any information you think is relevant to your situation.
* **Rights Awareness** (Number): This question asks you to rate your awareness of your personal rights. Please answer based on your understanding.
* **Confidence Level** (Select Multiple): Choose the level of confidence you have in your understanding of your personal rights.
* **Environment** (Select Multiple): Indicate whether you are in a environment where you feel your rights are respected or not.
* **Educator/Counselor** (Select One): If you have someone who provides guidance and support, please indicate if they are aware of your rights.
* **Community Team** (Select One): If you have a community team that is aware of your rights, please indicate here.
* **Other** (Note): If you have any other information that's relevant to your situation, please provide it here.
* **User Notes** (Note): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts you think are important.
* **Email** (Email): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Text): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Submitter Name** (Text): Please enter your name as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Email** (Email): Please enter your email address as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text): Please enter your phone number as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Comments** (Note): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts from the person submitting this form.
* **Reviewer Name** (Text): Please enter the name of the person reviewing this form.
* **Reviewer Email** (Email): Please enter the email address of the person reviewing this form.
* **Reviewer Phone** (Text): Please enter the phone number of the person reviewing this form.
* **Reviewer Comments** (Note): Please provide any additional comments or thoughts from the reviewer.
* **Submitter Agrees** (Select One): If the submitter agrees with something, please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Reviewer Agrees** (Select One): If the reviewer agrees with something, please select "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitter Agrees Other** (Note): If the submitter has any other comments related to agreeing or not agreeing, please provide them here.
* **Reviewer Agrees Other** (Note): If the reviewer has any other comments related to agreeing or not agreeing, please provide them here.
* **Submitter Comments Other** (Note): If the submitter has any other comments, please provide them here.
* **Reviewer Comments Other** (Note): If the reviewer has any other comments, please provide them here.
* **Date Created** (Date): This field is automatically populated with the date the form was created.

Note: Please make sure to fill out all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
