# gap_analysis_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to identify areas for improvement within an organization by gathering information about the current performance, goals, and stakeholder feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions on each page according to the information you have available.
2. Review each page carefully to ensure you have provided accurate and relevant information.
3. If you're unsure about any questions or need clarification, please refer to the field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Current Performance** (Page 1): 
    Please enter a numerical value between 1-10 to indicate your current performance level, where 1 is "Poor" and 10 is "Excellent".
    
- **Goals** (Page 2): 
    Briefly describe your goals and objectives.
    
- **Stakeholder Feedback** (Page 3): 
    Provide feedback from stakeholders about your performance and goals.
    
- **Performance Gap** (Page 4): 
    Enter a numerical value between 1-10 to indicate your perceived performance gap, where 1 is "Minimal" and 10 is "Significant".
    
- **Strategic Improvements** (Page 5): 
    Select all improvements that you believe are necessary for your performance. Please choose from the available options.
    
- **Improvements** (Page 6): 
    Select one improvement that you believe is the most important for your performance. Please choose from the available options.
