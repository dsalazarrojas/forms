# medical-procedure-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The medical-procedure-assessment-form is designed to collect relevant information about medical procedures performed on patients, ensuring accuracy and adherence to confidentiality and HIPAA standards.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the patient and procedure before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Use the provided options for select fields (e.g., medical condition, medical procedure).
4. Be accurate and detailed in your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Procedure Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the medical procedure performed.
- **Patient Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the patient who underwent the procedure.
- **Medical Record Number** (3, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number (if applicable).
- **Procedure Description** (4, text, required): Describe the procedure that was performed, including any relevant details.
- **Medical Provider Name** (5, text, required): Enter the name of the medical provider who performed the procedure.
- **Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date the procedure was performed.
- **Time** (7, time, required): Enter the time the procedure was performed.
- **Medical Condition** (8, select_one, required): Select the medical condition that led to the procedure.
- **Allergies** (9, select_multiple, required): Select any allergies or sensitivities the patient has.
- **Medical Procedure** (10, select_one, required): Select the medical procedure performed.
- **Medical Test Result** (11, select_multiple, required): Enter any relevant test results related to the procedure.
- **Medical History** (12, text, required): Describe the patient's medical history relevant to the procedure.
- **Medication** (13, text, required): List any medications the patient was taking before or after the procedure.
- **Patient Condition** (14, text, required): Describe the patient's condition before or after the procedure.
- **Surgical Site** (15, select_one, required): Select the surgical site where the procedure was performed.
- **Surgical Site 2** (16, select_multiple, required): Select any additional surgical sites where the procedure was performed.
- **Anesthesia** (17, select_one, required): Select the type of anesthesia used during the procedure.
- **Patient Medical Record** (18, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number or any other relevant information.
- **Procedure Status** (19, select_one, required): Select the status of the procedure (e.g., completed, pending).
- **Surgical Site 3** (20, select_multiple, required): Select any additional surgical sites where the procedure was performed.
- **Surgical Site 4** (21, select_multiple, required): Select any additional surgical sites where the procedure was performed.
- **Medical Condition 2** (22, select_one, required): Select the patient's medical condition (if relevant).
- **Medical Test Result 2** (23, select_multiple, required): Enter any relevant test results.
- **Patient Medication** (24, text, required): List any medications taken by the patient before or after the procedure.
- **Note** (25, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the procedure or patient.
