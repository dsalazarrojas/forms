# risk_assessment_and_income_calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic information from users to calculate a risk score and assess their income level.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information in the "Basic Info" section.
2. Select your risk factors in the "Risk Factors" section.
3. Enter your income details in the "Income Info" section.
4. Calculate your risk score and income level using the "Risk Score" and "Income Score" fields.
5. Select a risk level for yourself from the "Risk Level" section.
6. Review and submit the form to get your calculated risk score and income level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Basic Info (Basic Info)
* **Basic Info** (`Basic Info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your basic information in this field. This includes details such as your name, address, and contact information. Please fill in all the necessary details accurately.
### What Are Your Risk Factors (Risk Factors)
* **What Are Your Risk Factors** (`What Are Your Risk Factors`, `text`, required/optional): This section asks you to list your risk factors. Please select all the risk factors that apply to you from the list.
### Income Info (Income Info)
* **Income Info** (`Income Info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your income details in this field. Please enter your annual income accurately.
### Risk Score (Risk Score)
* **Risk Score** (`Risk Score`, `number`, required/true): This field calculates your risk score based on your risk factors. Please note that this score is for internal use only and not for real-life risk assessment.

### Income Score (Income Score)
