# Health Risk Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Risk Assessment Questionnaire is a form used to collect information about a patient's health risks and medical history. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's health status and provide personalized care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Select any health risks you're currently experiencing from the "Health Risks" dropdown list.
7. Enter any risk factors that may be affecting your health in the "Risk Factors" field.
8. List any medical conditions you're currently experiencing in the "Medical Conditions" field.
9. List any medications you're currently taking in the "Medications" field.
10. List any allergies you have in the "Allergies" field.
11. Enter details about your emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contacts" field.
12. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
13. Enter the contact information (phone number or email) of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" or "Emergency Contact Email" field.
14. Enter the address of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Address" field.
15. Enter any medical history or health concerns in the "Medical History" field.
16. Provide a description of your medical history in the "Medical History Description" field.
17. Enter the date of your medical history in the "Medical History Date" field.
18. Enter the time of your medical history in the "Medical History Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, optional)
	+ Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional)
	+ Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, optional)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Address** (4, text, optional)
	+ Enter your residential address.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Health Risks** (6, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select any health risks you're currently experiencing (e.g., High Blood Pressure, High Cholesterol, etc.).
* **Risk Factors** (7, text, optional)
	+ List any risk factors affecting your health.
* **Medical Conditions** (8, text, optional)
	+ List any medical conditions you're currently experiencing.
* **Medications** (9, text, optional)
	+ List any medications you're currently taking.
* **Allergies** (10, text, optional)
	+ List any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contacts** (11, text, optional)
	+ Enter details about your emergency contacts.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (12, text, optional)
	+ Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (13, text, optional)
	+ Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (14, email, optional)
	+ Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (15, text, optional)
	+ Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical History** (16, note, optional)
	+ Enter any medical history or health concerns.
* **Medical History Description** (17, note, optional)
	+ Provide a description of your medical history.
* **Medical History Date** (18, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of your medical history.
* **Medical History Time** (19, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time of your medical history.
