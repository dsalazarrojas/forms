# Fitness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fitness Assessment Form" is a comprehensive form designed to gather essential information about a client's medical history, physical condition, and goals. This form enables fitness professionals to assess the client's needs and develop a tailored fitness plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide client information, including name, medical history, and any relevant details.
2. Answer questions about your physical condition, including medical history, vital signs, and medications.
3. Select your physical condition and medical history accurately.
4. Indicate your goals and expectations for the fitness program.
5. Provide assessment results, including date and any relevant comments.
6. Develop an exercise plan and nutrition plan (optional).
7. Select your current fitness level and pain level.
8. Add any additional comments or recommendations.
9. Schedule follow-up appointments (optional).
10. Finally, confirm your assessment date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_information`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Describe the client's medical history, including any previous injuries, illnesses, or allergies.
* **Vital Signs** (`vital_signs`, `select_multiple`, required): Check the boxes for your current vital signs, including 'Yes' or 'No' for each condition.
* **Meds** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Physical Condition** (`physical_condition`, `select_one`, required): Select your current physical condition from the options provided.
* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your fitness goals, including any specific objectives or expectations.
* **Assessment Results** (`assessment_results`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Exercise Plan** (`exercise_plan`, `text`, optional): Develop a plan for your exercise routine, including any specific activities or routines.
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current fitness level from the options provided.
* **Pain Level** (`pain_level`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your current pain level, including 'High' or 'Low'.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your condition or progress.
* **Exercise Recommendations** (`exercise_recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide any exercise recommendations for your fitness professional.
* **Nutrition Plan** (`nutrition_plan`, `text`, optional): Develop a plan for your nutrition, including any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Client Outcomes** (`client_outcomes`, `text`, optional): Describe the outcomes or results you expect from the fitness program.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, optional): Schedule follow-up appointments or discuss next steps with your fitness professional.
* **Follow Up Appointments** (`follow_up_appointments`, `text`, optional): Schedule follow-up appointments with your fitness professional.
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, `text`, optional): Describe your long-term fitness goals, including any specific objectives or expectations.
