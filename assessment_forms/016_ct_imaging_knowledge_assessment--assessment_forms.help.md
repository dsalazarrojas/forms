# CT Imaging Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The CT Imaging Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate your understanding of various aspects of CT imaging.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields to provide accurate information.
2. Review each question carefully and select the correct answer based on your knowledge and understanding.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Enter your name and department in the respective fields.
5. Enter the assessment date.
6. Answer questions 1-3 according to the provided options.
7. Enter your estimated score in the designated field (optional).
8. Provide evaluator notes for feedback and remediation suggestions (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for record-keeping purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department, e.g., Radiology or Emergency.
* **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the assessment.
* **Question 1 - Indicate correct windowing technique** (`q1`, select_one, required): Select the correct windowing technique from the options.
* **Question 2 - Identify common artifacts** (`q2`, select_multiple, required): Select all the common artifacts that you are aware of.
* **Question 3 - Typical CT slice thickness for chest** (`q3`, number, required): Enter the typical CT slice thickness for the chest in millimeters.
* **Estimated score** (`score_estimate`, number, optional): Enter your estimated score for the assessment (for instructor use).
* **Evaluator notes** (`evaluator_notes`, text, optional): Provide feedback and remediation suggestions for the instructor.
