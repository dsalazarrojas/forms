# insurance_pre_assessment_tool - Help Guide
## Purpose
The insurance_pre_assessment_tool is a form designed to collect information related to insurance claims in a systematic and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions to access the form.
2. Review the form sections and fields to understand what information is being collected.
3. Fill in the required information for each field, making sure to enter accurate and relevant details.
4. Use the "Submit" button to save your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Contact Information** (`<name: contact_information>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including any relevant details about yourself or your organization.
- **Insurance Type** (`<name: insurance_type>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of insurance related to the claim, such as Home, Automotive, or Business.
- **Insurance Information** (`<name: insurance_info>`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional insurance-related information that is not covered by other fields.
- **Insurance Info Date** (`<name: insurance_info_date>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date related to the insurance claim.
- **Insurance Info Time** (`<name: insurance_info_time>`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time related to the insurance claim.
- **Claim Number** (`<name: claim_number>`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the claim number, if applicable.
- **Policy Number** (`<name: policy_number>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the policy number, if applicable.
- **Claim Date** (`<name: claim_date>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the claim.
- **Claim Time** (`<name: claim_time>`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the claim.
- **Claim Status** (`<name: claim_status>`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the current status of the claim, such as Open or Closed.
- **Claimant Information** (`<name: claimant_info>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Date** (`<name: claimant_info_date>`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date related to the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Time** (`<name: claimant_info_time>`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time related to the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Location** (`<name: claimant_info_location>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location related to the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Phone** (`<name: claimant_info_phone>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Email** (`<name: claimant_info_email>`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Address** (`<name: claimant_info_address>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the claimant.
- **Claimant Info City** (`<name: claimant_info_city>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the city related to the claimant.
- **Claimant Info State** (`<name: claimant_info_state>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the state related to the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Zip** (`<name: claimant_info_zip>`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the zip code related to the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Country** (`<name: claimant_info_country>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the country related to the claimant.
- **Claimant Info Insurance Provider** (`<name: claimant_info_insurance_provider>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the insurance provider related to the claimant.
- **Insurance Provider** (`<name: insurance_provider>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the insurance provider.
- **Submit** (`<name: submit>`, `text`, required/optional): Click the "Submit" button to save your completed form.

This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the insurance_pre_assessment_tool form accurately and efficiently. It covers each field in the form, providing a clear and concise explanation of what information to enter.
