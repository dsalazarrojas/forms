# Correctional Mental Health Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Correctional Mental Health Evaluation Form** is a critical tool for assessing the mental health needs of individuals in correctional facilities. This form helps evaluators identify potential risks, develop effective treatment plans, and make informed decisions to support the well-being of inmates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information about the facility where the evaluation is being conducted.
2. Enter the inmate's number, which can be used to track their mental health history.
3. Choose the date of the evaluation and when the evaluation was conducted.
4. Add any notes about the inmate's clinical findings, such as their mental health status.
5. Assess the risk level of the inmate's behavior, selecting one of the three options: Risk Low, Risk Moderate, or Risk High.
6. Provide a treatment plan to address any identified needs.
7. Suggest recommendations for further action or intervention.
8. Enter any next steps for further actions or follow-up meetings.
9. Provide your name and position as the evaluator.
10. Enter any additional administrative information about the facility, if necessary.
11. Sign and date the evaluation form, confirming your role and responsibilities as the evaluator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Location**: Enter the name and address of the correctional facility where the evaluation is being conducted.
* **Inmate Number**: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the inmate, which helps track their mental health history.
* **Date of Evaluation**: Enter the date when the evaluation was conducted.
* **Evaluation Date**: Enter the date when the evaluation was completed, which may be different from the Date of Evaluation if there are delays or changes.
* **Evaluator Notes**: Add any clinical findings or observations about the inmate's mental health status.
* **Clinical Findings**: Enter any notes or comments about the inmate's behavior, mental health status, or other relevant information.
* **Risk Assessment**: Choose the risk level of the inmate's behavior: Risk Low, Risk Moderate, or Risk High. This will help determine the level of intervention or monitoring required.
* **Treatment Plan**: Provide a written plan to address any identified mental health needs or concerns.
* **Recommendations**: Suggest any next steps or actions for further support or intervention.
* **Next Actions**: Enter any next steps or actions for further meetings or follow-up evaluations.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter your name and title as the evaluator.
* **Evaluator Position**: Enter your position or title within the correctional facility.
* **Evaluator Signature**: Sign and date the form, confirming your role and responsibilities as the evaluator.
* **Facility Administrative Number**: Enter any additional administrative information about the facility, if necessary.
