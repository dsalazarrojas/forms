Staff Certification Assessment is used to record and track staff certification, training and performance. This form is used to ensure that staff members' certifications are up-to-date and relevant to their job responsibilities. It is a critical component of staff development and performance evaluation.

Please note that this form should only be completed for staff members who are directly responsible for certification, training, or performance evaluation. If you are not sure if the form is relevant to your role, please check with your supervisor or HR representative.

## Purpose

The Staff Certification Assessment form is used to collect and record staff members' certifications, training hours, and performance reviews.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in the following fields:
    * First Name
    * Last Name
    * Job Title
    * Department
    * Certification Date
    * Certification Status (select one of: Approved, Not Approved, or Pending)
    * Training Hours (enter a number)
    * Certification ID (enter a number)
    * Certification Type (select one or more: Management, Technical, or Administrative)
    * Performance Review (date)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to.
*   **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your last certification.
*   **Certification Status** (`certification_status`, select_one, required): Choose one of the following options:
    +   **Approved**: Certification has been approved.
    +   **Not Approved**: Certification has not been approved.
    +   **Pending**: Certification is pending review.
*   **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of training hours you have completed.
*   **Certification ID** (`certification_id`, number, required): Enter your certification ID number.
*   **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following:
    +   **Management**: Certification is related to management.
    +   **Technical**: Certification is related to technical skills.
    +   **Administrative**: Certification is related to administration.
*   **Performance Review** (`performance_review`, date, required): Enter the date of your last performance review.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or HR representative.
*   If you need to update your certification or performance review information, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
