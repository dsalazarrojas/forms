# Biotics Research Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Biotics Research Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool designed to gather information about a participant's health and lifestyle habits. This form helps researchers assess the participant's overall health status, identify potential risk factors, and provide personalized recommendations for improvement. It is essential to answer these questions accurately and honestly to get the most valuable insights for the research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information (Section 1: Participant Information)
2. Provide your full name, date of birth, and gender (Section 2)
3. Enter your contact information (Section 3)
4. Answer questions about your health status and lifestyle habits (Sections 4-19)
5. Select your answers from the provided options for each question
6. Review and edit your answers before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Section 2):
	+ This is where you should enter your complete name, including your first and last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Section 2):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender** (Section 2):
	+ Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (Section 3):
	+ Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (Section 3):
	+ Enter your contact phone number.
* **Assessment Date** (Section 4):
	+ Enter today's date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Health History** (Section 4):
	+ This is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of your health background.
* **Current Health Status** (Section 4):
	+ Rate your overall health status from the provided options.
* **Existing Health Conditions** (Section 5):
	+ List any current health conditions you have.
* **Family Medical History** (Section 5):
	+ Provide any relevant family health history.
* **Current Medications** (Section 5):
	+ List all the medications you are currently taking.
* **Number of Daily Medications** (Section 5):
	+ Enter the number of medications you take daily.
* **Allergies** (Section 6):
	+ List any known allergies you have.
* **Lifestyle Factors** (Section 6):
	+ Provide any information about your lifestyle habits.
* **Diet Type** (Section 7):
	+ Select the best description of your diet from the provided options.
* **Daily Servings of Fruits and Vegetables** (Section 7):
	+ Select the number of servings of fruits and vegetables you consume daily.
* **Daily Water Intake** (Section 7):
	+ Select the number of glasses of water you drink daily.
* **Exercise Frequency** (Section 8):
	+ Select how often you exercise from the provided options.
* **Type of Exercise** (Section 8):

* **Sleep Quality** (Section 9):

* **Current Stress Level** (Section 10):
	+ Rate your current stress level from the provided options.
* **Stress Management Techniques** (Section 10):
	+ Select all the stress management techniques you use.
* **Substance Use** (Section 11):

* **Alcohol Consumption** (Section 12):
	+ Select how often you consume alcohol from the provided options.
* **Tobacco Use** (Section 12):

* **Daily Caffeine Intake** (Section 13):

* **Supplement Use** (Section 13):
