# NDIS Training Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The NDIS (National Disability Insurance) Training Assessment form is used to evaluate and document the effectiveness of training sessions provided for participants with disability support needs. This form helps trainers and assessors assess the participant's engagement, understanding, and outcomes from the training session, as well as gather information about the participant's name, date, and training provider.

**Please note that this form is not intended for actual training sessions, but rather as a sample form for demonstration purposes.**

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the NDIS Training Assessment form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant feedback and outcomes for the participant's session.
2. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Choose the session date from the calendar or type it manually in the "Session Date" field.
4. If applicable, enter the name of the training provider in the "Training Provider" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Feedback (Select Multiple)** (`session_feedback`, multiple choice, required: false):
	+ Choose one or more options to provide feedback on the participant's engagement and experience during the session.
* **Session Outcomes (Select One)** (`session_outcomes`, single choice, required: false):
	+ Select the relevant outcome that best describes the participant's progress or success after the session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the participant's name to ensure accurate records and reporting.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Select or enter the date of the training session for tracking and evaluation purposes.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required: false):
	+ If applicable, enter the name of the training provider for further reference and evaluation.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is currently empty and not applicable for this form.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have permission to access and complete this form.
* Review the participant's information carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Save the form regularly to avoid losing your progress.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or support team for guidance.
