# IoT Security Protocol Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate the security protocols of IoT devices. It helps organizations assess the security posture of their IoT devices and ensure they meet specific security requirements and protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the applicable **Security Protocols** that your IoT device uses.
	* You can choose one or more of the options provided.
	* If your device uses none of the listed security protocols, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the notes section (not included in this form).
2. Enter the **Device Name** of the IoT device being assessed.
	* This is a required field, so please enter the actual name of the device.
3. Select the type of **Device Type** used.
	* Choose from the available options: Smart Home Device, Industrial Automation, or Healthcare Device.
	* If your device does not fit into these categories, select "Other".
4. Enter the date of the **Risk Review**.
	* This is a required field, so please enter the actual date of the risk review.
5. Rate the **Security Assessment** level of your IoT device.
	* Choose from the available options: High, Medium, or Low.
6. Enter the **Security Protocol Version** of your IoT device (if applicable).
	* This field is not required.
7. Select the **Compliance Status** of your IoT device.
	* Choose from the available options: Compliant, Non-Compliant, or Unknown.
	* If your device is not applicable to the security protocols, select "Unknown".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Security Protocol** (`Security Protocols`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to select the applicable security protocols used by your IoT device. Choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Device Name** (`Device Name`, `text`, required): Enter the actual name of the IoT device being assessed.
* **Device Type** (`Device Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of IoT device used from the provided options.
* **Risk Review** (`Risk Review`, `date`, required): Enter the actual date of the risk review.
* **Security Assessment** (`Security Assessment`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate the security level of your IoT device. Choose from the available options.
* **Security Protocol Version** (`Security Protocol Version`, `number`, optional): If applicable, enter the version number of the security protocol used.
* **Compliance Status** (`Compliance Status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the compliance status of your IoT device from the available options.
