# workforce_capability_evaluation_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the workforce capability of an individual or team. It is a critical tool for assessing the skills, knowledge, and performance of employees to identify areas for improvement and development.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To complete this form, please ensure you have a clear understanding of the objective and purpose of the assessment.
2.  Fill in the required fields carefully and accurately, as they will be used to gauge the capability of the individual or team.
3.  Select the correct options for each field based on the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Objective** (`Objective`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the objective being assessed.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Enter the size of the team being assessed (1-10 members).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the assessment period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the assessment period.
* **Objective Description** (`objective_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the objective being assessed.
* **Objectives** (`objectives`, text, optional): List the objectives being worked on.
* **Priorities** (`priorities`, text, required): Enter the priorities for the assessment.
* **Timeframes** (`timeframes`, text, optional): Enter the timeframes for the assessment.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, select_one, required): Select "Manager", "Team Lead", or "Departmental" as the requestor.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Select the review frequency options (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, Annually).
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter the name of the submitter.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, select_one, optional): Select "Manager", "Team Lead", or "Departmental" as the reviewer.
* **Reviewee** (`reviewee`, text, required): Enter the name or title of the individual being assessed.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date the assessment was submitted.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Enter the time the assessment was submitted.
* **Submitter Note** (`submitter_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the submitter.
* **Reviewer Note** (`reviewer_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewee Note** (`reviewee_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments from the individual being assessed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the individual being assessed.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the individual being assessed.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the individual being assessed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Select "Approved", "Rejected", or "In Progress".
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Select "Complete", "Partial", "Incomplete".
