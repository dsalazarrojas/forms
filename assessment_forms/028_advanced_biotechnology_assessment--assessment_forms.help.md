# Advanced Biotechnology Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advanced Biotechnology Assessment form is used to collect information about the participant's background and experience in the field of biotechnology. This form is used for evaluation and assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your assessment is processed correctly.
2. Select the correct answers based on your knowledge and experience in the field of biotechnology.
3. If you need help or clarification, please contact your assessor for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Participant name (participant_name)
* Participant name: Participant name (`participant_name`, text, optional) - Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 2. Years of biotechnology experience (years_experience)
* Years of biotechnology experience: Years of biotechnology experience (`years_experience`, number, required) - Enter the total number of years you have spent working in biotechnology.
### 3. Primary field of work (primary_field)
* Primary field of work: Primary field of work (`primary_field`, select_one, required) - Select the primary area of work in biotechnology that best describes your responsibilities and tasks.
### 4. Technical skills proficiency (technical_skills)
* Technical skills proficiency: Technical skills proficiency (`technical_skills`, select_multiple, optional) - Select all the technical skills you have proficiency in, separated by commas.
### 5. Safety training completed (safety_training_completed)
* Safety training completed: Safety training completed (`safety_training_completed`, select_one, required) - Select 'True' if you have received and completed safety training relevant to your biotechnology work.
### 6. Assessment results summary (assessment_results_summary)
* Assessment results summary: Assessment results summary (`assessment_results_summary`, text, optional) - This field is for the assessor's use and should not be filled out by you.
### 7. Recommended training modules (recommended_training)
* Recommended training modules: Recommended training modules (`recommended_training`, text, optional) - This field is for the assessor's use and should not be filled out by you.
### 8. Assessment date (assessment_date)
* Assessment date: Assessment date (`assessment_date`, date, required) - Enter the date of the assessment in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
