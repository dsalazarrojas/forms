# vectors_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and knowledge on a specific topic or category. It is likely to be used in an educational or testing setting to gauge user understanding or opinions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title**: (`title`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the assessment or quiz. Please enter a brief and descriptive title that accurately reflects the topic or subject of the assessment.
* **Description**: (`description`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing a detailed description of the assessment or quiz. Please provide a brief but clear explanation of the topic or subject being assessed.
* **Category**: (`category`, text, required/optional): This field is for selecting the category or subject of the assessment or quiz. Please choose the category that best matches the topic or subject being assessed.
* **Description Long**: (`description_long`, text, required/optional): This field is for providing a more detailed description of the assessment or quiz. Please provide a longer and more in-depth explanation of the topic or subject being assessed.
* **Form ID**: (`form_id`, text, required/optional): This field is for identifying the assessment or quiz form. Please enter a unique identifier or name for the form, if required.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): This field is for specifying the tool or resource associated with the assessment or quiz. Please provide the name or identifier of the tool being used.

## Tips
This form is designed to collect user information and knowledge on a specific topic or category. Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear responses to each field to ensure the assessment or quiz is completed successfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
