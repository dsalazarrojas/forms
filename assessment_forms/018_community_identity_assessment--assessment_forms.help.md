# Community Identity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about the community's identity, values, and priorities to better understand its strengths, challenges, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Select the most suitable response for each question based on your affiliation with the community (Respondent affiliation: Resident, Business owner, Community leader, or Other).
2.  Rate your sense of belonging in the community (Sense of belonging in community: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree).
3.  Choose up to three priority areas for community identity work (Priority areas for community identity work: Youth engagement, Arts and culture, Heritage preservation, Public spaces, or Economic development).
4.  Provide open-ended feedback on barriers to a strong community identity (Barriers to a strong community identity: Open-ended feedback).
5.  Suggest ideas to strengthen community cohesion (Suggestions to strengthen identity: Ideas to improve community cohesion).
6.  Indicate your willingness to help with community initiatives (Willing to help with initiatives: 'True' or 'False').

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Respondent Affiliation** (Respondent affiliation, Select_one, Required): Choose the most accurate description of your relationship with the community. This will help us understand your perspective on community matters.
*   **Sense of Belonging** (Sense of belonging in community, Select_one, Required): This question assesses how much you feel connected to the community. Please respond based on your personal experience and feelings.
*   **Key Community Values** (Key community values, Select_multiple, Optional): Select the top values that are most important to you as a community member. This will help us understand what defines the community's identity.
*   **Cultural Events Participation** (Participation in cultural events, Select_one, Optional): Indicate how frequently you participate in cultural events in the community. This will help us understand engagement patterns.
*   **Priority Areas for Community Identity** (Priority areas for community identity, Select_multiple, Optional): Choose up to three areas that you think are most important for the community's identity. This will help us identify key focus points.
*   **Barriers to Strong Community Identity** (Barriers to a strong community identity, Text, Optional): Share any challenges or obstacles you feel hinder the community's growth and identity.
*   **Suggestions to Strengthen Identity** (Suggestions to strengthen identity, Text, Optional): Suggest any ideas you have to improve community cohesion and identity.
*   **Willing to Help with Initiatives** (Willing to help with initiatives, Select_one, Optional): Indicate your willingness to participate in community initiatives. This will help us reach out to you for support.
*   **Contact Email** (Contact email, Email, Optional): Provide your email address for follow-up communication. This is optional, but it will help us reach out for further discussion.
