# Site Maintenance Team Task Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the risks associated with site maintenance tasks. It is used to identify and evaluate potential risks and their corresponding mitigation strategies for site maintenance tasks. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the task and identify potential risks associated with it.
2. Select the task type (yes or no).
3. Enter the task duration.
4. Choose the risk category (yes or no).
5. Select the assigned team.
6. Choose the assigned tool.
7. Enter any comments.
8. Provide a risk description.
9. Choose the risk level (high or low).
10. Select the task status (active or inactive).
11. Enter the assigned date.
12. Choose the task priority (yes or no).
13. Select the assigned to.
14. Identify the task responsible person.
15. Enter any internal comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Maintenance Team Task Form** (`site_maintenance_team_task_form`): This is the main field where you enter the task description.
* **Task Risk Assessment** (`task_risk_assessment`): This field is used to enter a brief description of the risks associated with the task.
* **Task Type** (`task_type`): This field allows you to select whether the task is yes or no.
* **Task Duration** (`task_duration`): This field is used to enter the duration of the task.
* **Risk Category** (`risk_category`): This field allows you to select yes or no to indicate the presence of a risk.
* **Assigned Team** (`assigned_team`): This field is used to select the team assigned to the task.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): This field allows you to select whether a tool is assigned to the task.
* **Comments** (`comments`): This field is used to enter any comments or notes about the task.
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`): This field is used to enter a detailed description of the risk.
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`): This field allows you to select the level of risk (high or low).
* **Task Status** (`task_status`): This field is used to select the status of the task (active or inactive).
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`): This field is used to enter the date the task was assigned.
* **Task Priority** (`task_priority`): This field allows you to select whether the task has high or low priority.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): This field is used to select whether the task is assigned to someone.
* **Task Responsible** (`task_responsible`): This field is used to identify who is responsible for the task.
* **Comments Internal** (`comments_internal`): This field is used to enter any internal comments or notes about the task.
