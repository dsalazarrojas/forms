## Transport And Logistics Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Transport And Logistics Assessment form is designed to collect information about the transport and logistics capabilities and challenges of an organization. This form is used to gather data about the organization's transport modes, major challenges, and improvement needs. ## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each field carefully and accurately, using the options provided.
