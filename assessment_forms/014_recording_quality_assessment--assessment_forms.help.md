# Recording Quality Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to assess the quality of a recording. It is designed to evaluate the overall quality of a recording, as well as specific aspects such as background noise, clarity, and bitrate. This form is used to ensure that recordings are of a high standard and to identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the overall quality of the recording from the options below.
2. Indicate if there were any issues with background noise.
3. Confirm if the recording is clear to hear.
4. Select whether a recorder was used to capture the recording.
5. Assess the audio quality as Excellent, Good, or Fair.
6. Assess the video quality as Excellent, Good, or Fair.
7. Provide any other comments or feedback you may have about the recording.
8. Enter the date and time the recording was made.
9. Indicate the bitrate of the audio and video recordings.
10. Enter any additional notes or issues with the recording.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recording Quality Assessment (1)**: This field is used to select the overall quality of the recording. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **What was the overall quality of the recording? (2)**: This field is used to assess the overall quality of the recording. Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Were there any issues with background noise? (3)**: This field is used to indicate if there were any issues with background noise during the recording. Choose from the options: True or False.
* **Was the recording clear to hear? (4)**: This field is used to confirm if the recording is clear to hear. Choose from the options: True or False.
* **Recorder Used (5)**: This field is used to indicate if a recorder was used to capture the recording. Choose from the options: True or False.
* **Comments (6)**: This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback about the recording.
* **Date Recorded (7)**: This field is used to enter the date the recording was made.
* **Time Recorded (8)**: This field is used to enter the time the recording was made.
* **Recorder Used (9)**: This field is used to indicate the type of recorder used to capture the recording. Choose from the options: True or False.
* **Audio Quality Assessment (10)**: This field is used to assess the audio quality as Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Video Quality Assessment (11)**: This field is used to assess the video quality as Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Other Issues (12)**: This field is used to provide any other comments or issues with the recording.
* **Other Notes (13)**: This field is used to enter any additional notes about the recording.
* **Audio Bitrate (14)**: This field is used to enter the bitrate of the audio recording.
* **Video Bitrate (15)**: This field is used to enter the bitrate of the video recording.
* **Sample Rate (16)**: This field is used to enter the sample rate of the recording.
* **Sample Rate Bitrate (17)**: This field is used to enter the bitrate of the sample rate.
* **Bitrate 2 (18)**: This field is used to enter additional bitrate information.

Note: Some fields may be optional, as indicated by the "required" field in the form definition.
