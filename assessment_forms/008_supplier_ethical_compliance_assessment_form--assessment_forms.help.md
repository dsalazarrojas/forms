# Supplier Ethical Compliance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Ethical Compliance Assessment Form is used to gather information about a supplier's ethical compliance, including their status, type, industry sector, business activities, certifications, and certification details. This form helps to ensure that suppliers meet the necessary standards for doing business with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*). 
2. Select the correct options for each field from the available options.
3. Ensure that the assessment date is accurate.
4. Enter the correct vendor name, contact person, email, phone number, address, supplier status, supplier type, industry sector, business activities, and certifications.
5. Provide any additional certification details if applicable.
6. Enter the certification date if applicable.
7. Confirm the certification status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the full name of the supplier.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the supplier.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the contact number of the contact person (optional).
* **Address**: Enter the address of the supplier.
* **Supplier Status**: Select the current status of the supplier (e.g. active, inactive, on probation, etc.).
* **Supplier Type**: Select the type of supplier (e.g. material, service, etc.).
* **Industry Sector**: Select the industry sector of the supplier's business.
* **Business Activities**: Describe the supplier's business activities (e.g. what they do, what they produce, etc.).
* **Certifications**: List the certifications the supplier has (e.g. ISO, OHSAS, etc.).
* **Certifications Details**: Provide any additional details about the certifications.
* **Certification Date**: Enter the date of the certification (optional).
* **Certification Status**: Select the status of the certification (e.g. current, expired, etc.).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the supplier.
