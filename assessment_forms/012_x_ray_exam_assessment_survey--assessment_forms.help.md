# X Ray Exam Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from patients about their experience with the X Ray exam.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "Patient Feedback" page to provide your initial feedback about your experience with the X Ray exam.
2. On the "Overall Satisfaction" page, select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the exam.
3. On the "Image Quality" page, select all the options that apply to describe the image quality you experienced during the exam.
4. On the "Communication Quality" page, provide any additional comments or feedback about the communication you received during the exam.
5. On the subsequent pages, select the options that best describe your experience with the radiology team's performance, facility cleanliness, waiting time, exam length, staff professionalism, appointment scheduling, parking and transportation, medical records accessibility, X Ray exam process, and any additional comments you may have.
6. Once you have completed all the pages, click on the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Feedback** (`patient_feedback`, text, required/optional): Please provide your initial feedback about your experience with the X Ray exam. This is a text field where you can describe any positive or negative aspects of your experience.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your overall satisfaction with the exam. You can choose from "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied".
* **Image Quality** (`image_quality`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to describe the image quality you experienced during the exam. You can choose from "Good" or "Poor".
* **Communication Quality** (`communication_quality`, text, required): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the communication you received during the exam. This is a text field where you can describe your experience.
* **Radiology Team Performance** (`radiology_team_performance`, text, required): Please select the options that best describe the performance of the radiology team during your exam. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Staff Friendliness** (`staff_friendlyness`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes your experience with the staff's friendliness. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Facility Cleanliness** (`facility_cleanliness`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to describe the cleanliness of the facility during your exam. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Waiting Time** (`waiting_time`, time, required): This field is designed to collect your feedback about the waiting time during your exam. Please enter the time you waited in the format of "HH:MM".
* **Exam Length** (`exam_length`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the length of the exam. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Staff Professionalism** (`staff_professionalism`, select_multiple, required): Select the options that best describe the professionalism of the staff during your exam. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Appointment Scheduling** (`appointment_scheduling`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the appointment scheduling process. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Parking and Transportation** (`parking_and_transportation`, select_multiple, required): Select all the options that apply to describe the parking and transportation services provided during your visit. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **Medical Records Accessibility** (`medical_records_accessibility`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes the accessibility of your medical records. You can choose from "Yes" or "No".
* **X Ray Exam Process** (`x_ray_exam_process`, text, required): Please describe your experience with the X Ray exam process in detail. This is a text field where you can provide feedback about any aspect of the process.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience. This is a text field where you can describe any concerns or suggestions you may have.
