# sustainable_product_self_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

The sustainable product self-assessment questionnaire is a tool used to collect information about the sustainable characteristics of a product. This form is meant to be used by product owners or stakeholders to evaluate the environmental impact and sustainability of their products.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Navigate to the sustainable product self-assessment questionnaire form.
2.  Fill out the form according to your product’s characteristics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**:
    *   (`id: 1`, `name: introduction`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This section is an opportunity to briefly explain the product and its purpose.
*   **Materials**:
    *   (`id: 2`, `name: materials`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: [Yes, No]`, `required: false`): Select all materials used in the production of the product.
*   **Material Impact**:
    *   (`id: 3`, `name: material_impact`, `type: number`, `options: []`, `required: false`): Estimate the impact of materials on the environment (e.g., 1-5).
*   **Material Impact**:
    *   (`id: 4`, `name: material_impact`, `type: select_one`, `options: [Yes, No]`, `required: false`): Select the correct answer for the material impact question.
*   **Manufacturing**:
    *   (`id: 5`, `name: manufacturing`, `type: number`, `options: []`, `required: false`): Estimate the impact of manufacturing (e.g., 1-5).
*   **Packaging**:
    *   (`id: 6`, `name: packaging`, `type: select_multiple`, `options: [Yes, No]`, `required: false`): Select all materials used in the packaging of the product.
*   **Packaging Impact**:
    *   (`id: 7`, `name: packaging_1`, `type: select_one`, `options: [Yes, No]`, `required: false`): Select the correct answer for the packaging impact question.
*   **End of Life**:
    *   (`id: 8`, `name: end_of_life`, `type: note`, `options: []`, `required: false`): This is a note section where you can add any information regarding the product's end of life.
*   **Notes**:
    *   (`id: 9`, `name: notes`, `type: note`, `options: []`, `required: false`): Add any additional notes about the product.

## Tips

*   Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
*   If unsure about any answer, refer to your product’s documentation or ask your team members.
*   Fill out the form as thoroughly as possible to get the most accurate results.
*   If the form is completed incorrectly or incompletely, it may not be possible to use for sustainability reporting and assessments.
