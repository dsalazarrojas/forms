# Applicant Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for assessing candidates for a specific position within the company. Its purpose is to collect information about the candidate's technical knowledge, problem-solving ability, communication skills, cultural fit, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in one page at a time, starting from the first field on the first page.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before proceeding to the next page.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask the relevant department or team for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Please enter the name of the candidate being assessed.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied_for`, text, required): Please enter the position the candidate is applying for.
* **Interviewer or Assessor Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as the interviewer or assessor.
* **Date of Assessment** (`interview_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of the assessment.
* **Technical Knowledge Score** (`technical_knowledge_score`, select_one, required): Please evaluate the candidate's understanding of role-specific tools and concepts on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Expert).
* **Problem Solving Ability** (`problem_solving_ability`, select_one, required): Please evaluate the candidate's ability to analyze and resolve complex issues on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Outstanding).
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Please evaluate the candidate's clarity of expression and active listening on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 4 (Excellent).
* **Cultural Fit Rating** (`cultural_fit_rating`, select_one, required): Please evaluate the candidate's alignment with company values and team dynamics on a scale from 1 (Potential Conflict) to 4 (Perfect Fit).
* **Soft Skills Observations** (`soft_skills_observations`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or observations about the candidate's teamwork, leadership, adaptability, etc.
* **Overall Assessment Score** (`overall_assessment_score`, number, required): Please enter a score between 1 and 100 that reflects your overall assessment of the candidate.
* **Hiring Recommendation** (`hiring_recommendation`, select_one, required): Please choose one of the four options:
	+ Hire Immediately
	+ Move to Next Round
	+ Keep on File for Future Reference
	+ Do Not Hire
* **Strengths Summary** (`strengths_summary`, text, required): Please provide a brief summary of the candidate's strengths.
* **Areas for Development** (`development_areas`, text, optional): Please provide any areas where the candidate needs development or improvement.
* **Additional Assessor Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Please add any additional comments or observations about the candidate.
* **Soft Skills Observations** (`soft_skills_observations`, text, optional): Please provide any additional comments or observations about the candidate's soft skills.
* **Technical Knowledge Score** (`technical_knowledge_score`, select_one, required): Please evaluate the candidate's understanding of role-specific tools and concepts on a scale from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Expert).
* **Final Recommendation Header** (`final_recommendation_header`, note, optional): Please add any final recommendations or comments.
* **Candidate Information Header** (`candidate_info_header`, note, optional): Please add any additional comments or information about the candidate.
* **Candidate Skills Header** (`candidate_skills_header`, note, optional): Please add any additional comments or information about the candidate's skills.
