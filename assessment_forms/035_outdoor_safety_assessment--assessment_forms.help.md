# outdoor_safety_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outdoor safety assessment form is used to evaluate the safety of an outdoor area. This form helps identify potential hazards, assess risks, and create a plan to address any safety concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by inspecting the outdoor area (page 1).
2. Identify any potential hazards in the area (page 2).
3. Perform a risk assessment to determine the level of risk (page 3).
4. Create an action plan to mitigate any identified risks (page 4).
5. Determine the follow-up actions needed after the assessment (page 5).
6. Report any incidents that occurred during the assessment (page 6).
7. Provide any additional comments or notes about the assessment (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: inspect_area
* **inspect_area** (inspect_area, text, optional): Describe the outdoor area being assessed.

### Page 2: hazards
* **hazards** (hazards, select_multiple, optional): Select all potential hazards found in the area:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Page 3: risk_assessment
* **risk_assessment** (risk_assessment, number, optional): Enter the level of risk (on a scale of 1-5).

### Page 4: action_plan
* **action_plan** (action_plan, text, optional): Describe the actions to be taken to mitigate any identified risks.

### Page 5: follow_up
* **follow_up** (follow_up, select_one, optional): Determine the follow-up actions needed:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

### Page 6: incident_report
* **incident_report** (incident_report, text, optional): Report any incidents that occurred during the assessment.

### Page 7: comments
* **comments** (comments, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the assessment.
