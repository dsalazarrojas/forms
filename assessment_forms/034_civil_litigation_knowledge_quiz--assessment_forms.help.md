# Civil Litigation Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form assesses your knowledge of civil litigation concepts, including discovery methods, pleading types, statute limitations, burden of proof, summary judgment, hearsay rules, jurisdiction, and more. It is essential to complete this form accurately to demonstrate your understanding of these critical topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **Trainee Full Name** in the Trainee Full Name field.
2. Select your **Department or Practice Area** from the provided options.
3. Answer each question in the Litigation Concepts Quiz section, selecting the most accurate option for each question.
4. If you'd like, estimate the time you spent to complete the quiz in minutes and select "Appropriate" as the difficulty level.
5. Finally, indicate whether you'd like to review the correct answers with a supervisor.
