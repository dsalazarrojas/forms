# Psychological APTITUDE ASSESSMENT - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's psychological aptitude. It contains a series of questions that will help gauge their mental fitness, stability, and potential for psychological assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on each page to proceed to the next section of questions.
2. For each question, select or enter the most accurate response from the available options.
3. Make sure to answer all required questions, indicated by a red asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Yes** (page_2): This question asks you to confirm if you are willing to participate in a psychological evaluation. Please select 'Yes' if you are willing and 'No' otherwise.
* **Date** (page_3): This is a date field where you can enter your date of birth or a specific date relevant to your psychological evaluation.
* **Email** (page_4): This is a field for your contact email address. Please enter your email address as you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Yes** (page_5): This question asks you to confirm if you have any medical or psychological conditions that may affect your participation in a psychological evaluation. Please select 'Yes' if you do and 'No' otherwise.
* **Time** (page_6): This is a time field where you can enter your availability for a psychological evaluation.
* **Number 1** (page_7): This question asks you to rate your current level of mental fitness on a scale of 1-10.
* **Number 2** (page_8): This question asks you to rate your current level of mental stability on a scale of 1-10.
* **Text** (page_9): This question asks you to provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your psychological evaluation.

Note to the administrator: Please ensure that the user has provided accurate and relevant information before proceeding with the psychological evaluation.
