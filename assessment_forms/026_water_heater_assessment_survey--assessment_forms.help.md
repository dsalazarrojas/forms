# Water Heater Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the condition and maintenance history of a water heater. It helps gather essential information about the water heater's status, service history, and maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the water heater's details (Page 1: Water Heater Details).
2. Specify the water heater's model (Page 2: Model).
3. Select the current status of the water heater (Page 3: Status).
4. Enter the service history of the water heater (Page 4: Service History).
5. Provide the date of the last service (Page 5: Last Service Date).
6. Specify the next due date for the next service (Page 6: Next Service Due Date).
7. Choose the service frequency (Page 7: Service Frequency).
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the water heater (Page 8: Notes).
9. Provide any additional information about the water heater (Page 9: Additional Information).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Water Heater Details** (Page 1, Text): Describe the water heater's details, including its location, type, and any other relevant information.
* **Model** (Page 2, Text): Enter the brand and model number of the water heater.
* **Status** (Page 3, Select One):
	+ Choose from: Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement.
* **Service History** (Page 4, Text): Enter the service history of the water heater, including any previous maintenance, repairs, or replacements.
* **Last Service Date** (Page 5, Date): Enter the date of the last service in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Next Service Due Date** (Page 6, Date): Enter the date of the next scheduled service in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Service Frequency** (Page 7, Select Multiple):
	+ Choose from: Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-annually, or Annually.
* **Notes** (Page 8, Note): Add any additional comments or notes about the water heater.
* **Additional Information** (Page 9, Text): Provide any other relevant information about the water heater.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure that the information gathered is useful for future maintenance and decision-making processes.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult the owner's manual or contact a professional for guidance.
* This form is designed to be used for water heaters only, so please do not use it for other types of appliances or equipment.
