# Embryonic Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Embryonic Development Assessment form is used to collect data about the developmental stage, gestational age, size, and potential abnormalities of an embryo. This form is likely used in medical or scientific research to track and analyze fetal development. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the Development Stage field by selecting the current stage of the embryo's development.
2. Enter the Gestational Age in the format "mm/dd/yyyy", where mm is the month, dd is the date, and yyyy is the year of the gestation period.
3. Enter the Embryo Size as a numerical value representing its size.
4. Answer the Developmental Abnormalities field by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate if any abnormalities were detected.
5. In the Assessment Outcome field, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the outcome of the assessment.
6. Add any relevant comments in the Comments field.
7. Enter the Researcher's Name and Institution fields with the relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Development Stage**: Enter the current stage of the embryo's development, e.g., "embryonic" or "fetal".
* **Gestational Age**: Enter the gestational age in the format "mm/dd/yyyy", where mm is the month, dd is the date, and yyyy is the year of the gestation period.
* **Embryo Size**: Enter the size of the embryo as a numerical value.
* **Developmental Abnormalities**: Select "Yes" if any abnormalities were detected during the assessment, "No" otherwise.
* **Assessment Outcome**: Select "Yes" if the assessment was successful, "No" otherwise.
* **Comments**: Add any relevant comments about the assessment.
* **Researcher's Name**: Enter the name of the researcher or person conducting the assessment.
* **Institution**: Enter the name of the institution or organization conducting the assessment.

## Tips
- Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields (Development Stage, Gestational Age, and Embryo Size).
- Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- Be as detailed as possible in the Comments field.
