# Service Continuity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Continuity Assessment is a tool to help service providers evaluate their ability to maintain and resume their services in the event of a disaster or major disruption. It is a regular review and improvement process to ensure that the organization's continuity plans are up-to-date and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much information as possible.
2. Review and update your current continuity plans and procedures.
3. Identify potential continuity gaps and prioritize them for improvement.
4. Provide a risk assessment and recovery plan for your organization.
5. Outline your communication planning and training plans.
6. Indicate your testing plans and frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Continuity Assessment Form** (`id: 1`): This is the title of the form. It is not a fillable field.
* **Company** (`id: 2`): Enter the name of your company.
* **Business Owners** (`id: 3`): Select the titles of the individuals who own your business, such as CIO, IT Director, Operations Manager, HR Manager, or Finance Manager.
* **Continuity Teams** (`id: 4`): Select the teams that are responsible for maintaining continuity, such as IT Team, Operations Team, HR Team, or Finance Team.
* **Continuity Gaps** (`id: 5`): Describe any potential gaps in your continuity plan, including any weaknesses or missing elements.
* **Risk Assessment** (`id: 6`): Enter the date of your last risk assessment, or indicate if you are unsure.
* **Recovery Plan** (`id: 7`): Describe your current recovery plan, including procedures for recovery and restoration.
* **Communication Planning** (`id: 8`): Select the methods you use for communication during continuity planning, such as phone, email, text message, or in-person meeting.
* **Training Plan** (`id: 9`): Indicate whether you have a training plan in place, and if so, what type of training your teams receive (yes, no, maybe).
* **Training Frequency** (`id: 10`): Select the frequency of training for your teams, such as monthly, quarterly, biannually, or on-demand.
* **Testing Plan** (`id: 11`): Indicate whether you have a testing plan in place, and if so, what type of testing your teams undergo (yes, no, maybe).
