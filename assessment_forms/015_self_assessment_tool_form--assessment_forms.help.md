# Self Assessment Tool Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a self-assessment tool designed to help you identify your strengths, weaknesses, goals, and areas for personal and professional development. It's meant to be a helpful resource for you to reflect on your skills, motivations, and priorities to achieve a better understanding of yourself and create a path for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To get the most out of this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and rate your answers based on your personal experiences and feelings.
2. For select_multiple questions, choose up to three options that best fit your answer.
3. For number questions, rate your answer on a scale of 1-5 or 1-10, depending on the options provided.
4. Make sure to answer all pages as honestly and accurately as possible.
5. Save your answers for future reference and reflection.
