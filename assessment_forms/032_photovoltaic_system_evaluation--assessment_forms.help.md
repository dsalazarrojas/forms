# Photovoltaic System Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a photovoltaic system's site-specific characteristics. It will guide you through a series of questions to assess the site's suitability for a photovoltaic system installation. This form is not intended to be a comprehensive assessment tool, but rather a quick and easy way to gather basic information about a site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the Site Details section, which gathers information about the site's general characteristics.
2. Provide the site's location, address, and type of property in the Location, Address, and Property Type fields.
3. In the Roof Details section, answer questions about the roof's condition, type, age, and solar readiness. This will help determine if the roof is suitable for a photovoltaic system installation.
4. Answer questions about the site's usage and energy consumption in the Usage and Energy Use sections.
5. Provide information about the site's orientation, elevation, and azimuth in the Site Orientation, Site Elevation, and Site Azimuth sections.
6. Finally, provide information about the site's solar panels, inverter, and any comments you'd like to add in the Solar Panels and Comments fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Details**
	+ **Site Location**: This field is for entering the site's location (e.g., city, state, or region).
* **Address**: Enter the site's address in this field.
* **Property Owner**: This field is for entering the name of the property owner or manager.
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, industrial).
* **Roof Details**: This field is for entering any additional details about the roof (e.g., size, material, condition).
* **Roof Type**: Indicate if the roof is 'Yes' or 'No' for solar readiness.
* **Roof Age**: Enter the age of the roof in years.
* **Roof Condition**: Indicate the condition of the roof (e.g., good, fair, poor).
* **Roof Solar Ready**: Indicate if the roof is 'Yes' or 'No' for solar readiness.
* **Usage Details**: This field is for entering any additional details about the site's usage (e.g., hours of operation, equipment used).
* **Site Usage**: Enter a brief description of the site's usage (e.g., office, residence, warehouse).
* **Site Elevation**: Enter the site's elevation in meters or feet.
* **Site Orientation**: Indicate the site's orientation (e.g., north-facing, south-facing).
* **Site Azimuth**: Enter the site's azimuth (elevation) in degrees.
* **Site Slope**: Enter the site's slope in degrees or percentage.
* **Site Slope Angle**: Enter the site's slope angle in degrees.
* **Usage Hours Per Day**: Enter the number of hours the site operates per day.
* **Usage Peak Hours**: Enter the peak hours of usage per day.
* **Site Capacity**: Enter the site's capacity in kilowatts or megawatts.
* **Site Energy Use**: Enter the site's energy use in kilowatts or megawatts per hour.
* **Solar Panels**: Enter the number of solar panels on site.
* **Inverter**: This field is for entering any details about the inverter (e.g., type, size).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the site.
