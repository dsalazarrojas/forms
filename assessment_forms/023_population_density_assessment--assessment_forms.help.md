# Population Density Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Population Density Assessment form is used to collect data on the population density of different regions. It is essential for administrators to have accurate and up-to-date information on population density to make informed decisions about resource allocation, urban planning, and other regional development projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the demographic information section with the required fields:
	* Population: Enter the total population of the region.
	* Area Size: Enter the total area size of the region.
	* Density: Calculate the population density by dividing the population by the area size.
	* Region: Select the region of the assessment.
	* City/Town: Select whether the region is a city or town.
	* Submission Date: Enter the date of submission.
2. Fill out the contact information section with the required fields:
	* Email: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
	* Phone: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
	* Submission Date: Enter the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Population (1)**: Enter the total population of the region.
* **Area Size (2)**: Enter the total area size of the region in square units (e.g., square kilometers or square meters).
* **Density (3)**: Calculate the population density by dividing the population by the area size. This field is not editable, as it is calculated automatically based on the population and area size fields.
* **Region (4)**: Select the region of the assessment from the list of available options.
* **City/Town (5)**: Select whether the region is a city or town from the list of available options.
* **Notes (6)**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to the assessment. This field is optional.
* **Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form. This field is optional.
* **Submission Date (9)**: Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission Time (10)**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted By (11)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure accurate population density calculations.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the administrator for assistance.
* Please note that the form is designed to be completed by authorized personnel only.
* Submission date and time are automatically recorded for auditing and tracking purposes.
