# Student Social Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help teachers and staff assess a student's social skills, including their strengths and areas of improvement. This information will be used to guide support and guidance provided to the student.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a quiet and private space, where you can focus on writing detailed and thoughtful feedback.
2. Answer each question truthfully and as accurately as possible, using your best judgment and professional expertise.
3. Be specific and provide concrete examples when providing feedback, especially in fields like "What were your observations?" and "What support strategies have you found effective?"
4. Use a clear and concise writing style, avoiding jargon and technical terms that might be unfamiliar to you or the student.
5. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly stated as optional, as missing information might hinder the assessment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student's Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their registration or identification documents.
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, number, required): Choose the student's current grade level, as per their school's or curriculum's classification.
* **Age** (`student_age`, number, required): Enter the student's age in years, to the nearest year.
* **Teacher's Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information, to facilitate follow-up discussions and collaborations.
* **What were your observations?** (`teacher_feedback_1`, text, required): Describe your observations of the student's social behavior, interactions, and communication style. Be specific and provide concrete examples.
* **What are some specific strengths you've noticed?** (`teacher_feedback_2`, text, required): Identify the student's social skills that you have observed, such as empathy, cooperation, or communication skills.
* **What areas do you think the student needs improvement in?** (`teacher_feedback_3`, text, required): Specify areas where the student may need additional support or guidance, such as conflict resolution, self-advocacy, or problem-solving skills.
* **Staff Feedback 1** (`staff_feedback_1`, text, required): Describe your observations or experiences with the student, focusing on their social skills and behavior.
* **Staff Feedback 2** (`staff_feedback_2`, text, required): Continue providing feedback on the student's social skills, building upon your previous observations or sharing new insights.
* **Staff Feedback 3** (`staff_feedback_3`, text, required): Provide additional feedback on the student's social skills, focusing on their strengths and areas for improvement.
* **What support strategies have you found effective?** (`staff_feedback_4`, text, required): Share your most effective strategies for supporting the student's social skills, such as one-on-one coaching, group activities, or adaptive technology.
* **What are some strategies to help the student develop these skills further?** (`teacher_support_strategies`, text, required): Suggest strategies to help the student build upon their strengths and improve in areas identified as needing attention, such as role-playing, feedback, or peer support.
