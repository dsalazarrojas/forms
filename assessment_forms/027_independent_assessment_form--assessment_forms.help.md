# Independent Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Independent Assessment Form is designed to collect information for assessments, evaluations, or judgments made by assessors on a subject. This form captures key details to facilitate feedback and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "User Name" field.
2. Provide your title in the "User Title" field.
3. Enter the name of the project in the "Project Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
5. Enter the time of the assessment in the "Assessment Time" field.
6. Enter the location of the assessment in the "Assessment Location" field.
7. Evaluate the assessment score out of a scale that you feel is appropriate (e.g., 1-5).
8. Enter any comments about the assessment in the "Assessment Comment" field.
9. Determine the follow-up frequency for the assessor in the "Follow-up Frequency" dropdown menu.
10. Select the follow-up date and time in the "Follow-up Date" and "Follow-up Time" fields, respectively.
11. Enter the location for the follow-up assessment in the "Follow-up Location" field.
12. Enter comments about the follow-up assessment in the "Follow-up Comment" field.
13. Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comment" field.
14. Finally, select "Submit" to finalize the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **User Title** (`user_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title to identify you (e.g., student, employee, etc.).
* **Project Name** (`user_project`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the project you are involved with.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the assessment (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the assessment (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Assessment Location** (`assessment_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the assessment.
* **Assessment Score** (`assessment_score`, number, required/optional): Evaluate the assessment score out of a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Assessment Comment** (`assessment_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter comments about the assessment.
* **Recommended Action** (`recommended_action`, text, required/optional): Suggest any necessary action for the subject.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the follow-up assessment (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time for the follow-up assessment (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Follow-up Location** (`follow_up_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location for the follow-up assessment.
* **Follow-up Comment** (`follow_up_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter comments about the follow-up assessment.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the follow-up frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Additional Comment** (`additional_comment`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select "Submit" to finalize the assessment.
* **Cancel** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select "Cancel" to discard the assessment.

Note: Some fields are optional, but they are included for completeness and future reference.
