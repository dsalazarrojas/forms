# Service Plan Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Plan Assessment Form is designed to collect and document a service plan for a client. This form will guide you through the process of completing the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Information**: Start by filling out the client's personal details, such as their name, contact information, and service preferences.
2. **Service Plan Details**: Select the relevant service plan details, including the Data Collection Method and Service Plan Type.
3. **Submit and Track**: Enter the submitter's contact and email information and select the service plan status.
4. **Additional Information**: Finally, add any additional details specific to the client and service provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (id: 1)**: Enter the client's full name.
* **Service Plan ID (id: 2)**: Enter a unique ID for the service plan.
* **Client Need (id: 3)**: Describe the client's specific need or requirement.
* **Service Plan Comparison (id: 4)**: Compare the service plan with other plans, if applicable.
* **Data Collection Method (id: 5)**: Select how the data was collected (In-person, Online, or Phone).
* **Service Plan Type (id: 6)**: Select the type of service plan (Standard, Custom, or Premium).
* **Date (id: 7)**: Enter the date of the service plan.
* **Time (id: 8)**: Enter the time of the service plan.
* **Client Contact Number (id: 9)**: Enter the client's contact number.
* **Client Email (id: 10)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Note (id: 11)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the client.
* **Data Tracking ID (id: 12)**: Enter the data tracking ID, if applicable.
* **Service Provider Name (id: 13)**: Enter the service provider's name.
* **Service Provider Contact Number (id: 14)**: Enter the service provider's contact number.
* **Service Provider Email (id: 15)**: Enter the service provider's email address.
* **Service Plan Status (id: 16)**: Select the status of the service plan (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Submitter Name (id: 17)**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email (id: 18)**: Enter your email address as the submitter.
* **Submitter Contact Number (id: 19)**: Enter your contact number as the submitter.
* **Submitter Note (id: 20)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the submission.
* **Data Collection Method Details (id: 21)**: Provide any additional details about the data collection method.
* **Service Plan Type Details (id: 22)**: Provide any additional details about the service plan type.
* **Service Provider Note (id: 23)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the service provider.
