# Fall Protection Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Protection Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to assess the knowledge of workers on fall protection. It is essential for their safety and the safety of others. The quiz aims to evaluate their understanding of fall protection concepts, such as safety equipment, personal protective equipment, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fall Protection Knowledge Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the available options from the dropdown menu: 
   - Fall Protection
   - Safety Equipment
   - Personal Protective Equipment
   - Fall Prevention
   - Emergency Procedures
   - Other

2. Enter the name of the worker in the text field.
3. Provide the email of the worker (optional).
4. Enter the phone number of the worker (optional).
5. Add any additional safety notes in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fall Protection** (`fall_protection_knowledge_quiz`, select_one, required: false):
    This field requires you to select one of the available options from the dropdown menu. It is a multiple-choice question that aims to evaluate your understanding of fall protection concepts.

* **worker_name** (name, text, required: false):
    This field is for entering the name of the worker. It is an optional field, and you can leave it blank if not required.

* **email** (email, email, required: false):
    This field is for entering the email of the worker. It is an optional field, and you can leave it blank if not required.

* **phone** (phone, text, required: false):
    This field is for entering the phone number of the worker. It is an optional field, and you can leave it blank if not required.

* **notes** (notes, note, required: false):
    This field is for adding any additional safety notes. You can use this field to provide any extra information that is relevant to the worker's safety.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the fields accurately and truthfully.
