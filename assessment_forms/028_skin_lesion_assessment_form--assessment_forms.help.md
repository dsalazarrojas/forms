# Skin Lesion Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skin Lesion Assessment Form is designed to collect detailed information about a patient's skin lesions. This information includes location, description, duration, and any associated symptoms. The form is intended for use in medical settings such as dermatology clinics or hospitals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Skin Lesion Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their location, in the "Patient Information" section.
2. Upload photos of the skin lesions in the "Photo Upload" section.
3. Describe the location of the skin lesions in the "Location Description" section.
4. Enter the duration of the skin lesions in the "Duration" section.
5. Select any symptoms associated with the skin lesions in the "Symptoms" section.
6. Enter any previous treatments the patient has received in the "Previous Treatments" section.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" section.
8. Sign and date the form in the "Patient Signature", "Provider Signature", and "Assessor Signature" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`): Enter the patient's information, including name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Photo Upload** (`photo_upload`): Upload photos of the skin lesions to help the healthcare provider visualize the condition.
* **Location Description** (`location_description`): Describe the location of the skin lesions, including the body part affected and any other relevant details.
* **Duration** (`duration`): Enter the duration of the skin lesions, including the date they first appeared and how long they have lasted.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Select any symptoms associated with the skin lesions, such as redness, scaling, color, shape, size, itching, pain, bleeding, or fever.
* **Previous Treatments** (`previous_treatments`): Enter any previous treatments the patient has received for the skin lesions.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.
* **Patient Signature** (`patient_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm the patient's information is accurate.
* **Provider Signature** (`provider_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm the healthcare provider's review of the patient's information.
* **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm the assessor's review of the patient's information.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`): Enter the name of the healthcare assessor who reviewed the patient's information.
* **Assessor Title** (`assessor_title`): Enter the title of the healthcare assessor who reviewed the patient's information.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time the form was completed.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the form was completed.

Note: The fields marked as `text`, `number`, `select_multiple`, and `date` and `time` types are expected to follow their respective data formats.
