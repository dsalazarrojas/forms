# City Public Services Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from residents on the effectiveness of various city public services. Your input will help the city better understand what services are performing well and where they can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant option for each question that best represents your experience with the city's services.
2. If you wish to provide a specific suggestion for service improvement, type it in the "One specific suggestion for service improvement" field.
3. Fill in the "Assessment Date" field with the date you are completing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident District or Neighborhood** (`neighborhood_district`, text, required): Please enter the name of the neighborhood or district where you reside.
* **How many years have you lived in this city?** (`years_of_residency`, number, required): Please enter the number of years you have lived in this city.
* **Rate Waste Collection and Recycling Services** (`waste_management_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of waste collection and recycling services, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Rate Police and Law Enforcement Services** (`law_enforcement_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of police and law enforcement services, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Rate Fire and Emergency Response Services** (`fire_emergency_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of fire and emergency response services, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Rate Road Maintenance and Snow Removal** (`road_maintenance_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of road maintenance and snow removal services, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Rate City Parks and Recreation Facilities** (`parks_recreation_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of city parks and recreation facilities, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Rate Public Library Services** (`public_library_rating`, number, required): Rate the quality of public library services, from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **How responsive is the city to your inquiries or complaints?** (`service_responsiveness`, select_one, required): Please select how responsive you feel the city is to your inquiries or complaints.
* **Do you feel you receive good value for your local taxes?** (`value_for_taxes`, select_one, required): Please select how you feel about the value of your local taxes.
* **Which city service is most important to you?** (`top_priority_service`, text, optional): If you have a service that you feel is particularly important, please enter it here.
* **One specific suggestion for service improvement** (`improvement_suggestion`, text, optional): If you have a specific suggestion for service improvement, please enter it here.
* **Overall Satisfaction with City Services** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Please select your overall satisfaction with city services.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you are completing this form.

## Tips
* Please be honest and specific when providing feedback, and try to provide specific examples or instances where the city's services have fallen short.
* If you have any suggestions or comments that do not fit in the provided fields, please do not hesitate to reach out to the city through other channels.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help the city improve its services for you and other residents.
