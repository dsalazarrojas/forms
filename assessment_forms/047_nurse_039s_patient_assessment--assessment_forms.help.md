# Nurse's Patient Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect patient data from medical professionals for assessment and decision-making. Please fill it out according to your professional judgment and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the patient's condition carefully and ensure you're collecting accurate information.
2. Fill out the form accordingly, being mindful of the required fields.
3. Use the text fields for detailed, free-form responses, such as medical history or lab results.
4. Choose the patient's outcome from the dropdown options, ensuring it accurately reflects their current status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Assessment
*   **Patient Assessment** (`patient_assessment`, `note`, not required): A free-text field for recording observations about the patient's condition, including any notable symptoms, behaviors, or concerns.

### Vitals Sign
*   **Vitals Sign** (`vital_signs`, `text`, not required): Record the patient's vital signs, such as temperature, pulse, blood pressure, or oxygen saturation.

### Allergies
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any known allergies the patient has, such as Penicillin, Latex, Eggs, etc.

### Medical History
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, not required): Enter any significant medical history, including previous surgeries, chronic conditions, or relevant diagnoses.

### Lab Results
*   **Lab Results** (`lab_results`, `text`, not required): Record any relevant lab results, such as blood work, imaging studies, or biopsy reports.

### Medications
*   **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, not required): List any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.

### Patient Outcome
*   **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, `select_one`, required): Choose the patient's current outcome from the options, indicating whether they have Recovered, Transferred to another facility, Deceased, Discharged, or are In progress.

### Medical Conditions
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, not required): Enter any diagnosed medical conditions or diagnoses related to the patient's current condition.

## Tips
*   Please be thorough but concise in your responses, as the form is intended to facilitate efficient assessment and decision-making.
*   If you're unsure about any information, use your professional judgment and consult relevant medical resources or experts as needed.
*   Remember to document any changes or updates to the patient's condition or outcome.
