# Post-Training Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about potential risks associated with a training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2.  Select the appropriate risk factors, risk level, and risk status relevant to the training program.
3.  Provide a brief description of each risk in 100 characters or less.
4.  Indicate the control measures or prevention methods for each risk.
5.  Specify the frequency of each risk and its severity level.
6.  Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Risk Assessment Form
*   **Risk Assessment Form** (`risk_assessment_form`, `text`, required: false): This is the main section of the form where you can describe the training program's risk assessment form. Please provide a brief overview of the training program and its associated risks.

### Risk Factors
*   **Risk Factors** (`risk_factor`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field asks you to identify the factors that contribute to the risk. You can select one or more of the options provided. Please be specific and choose only the factors that apply to the training program.

### Risk Level
*   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks you to rate the level of risk associated with the training program. You can select one of the three options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. Please choose the level that best describes the risk.

### Risk Description
*   **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, `text`, required: false): In 100 characters or less, provide a brief description of each risk.

### Risk Control
*   **Risk Control** (`risk_control`, `text`, required: false): Describe the control measures or prevention methods to mitigate or manage each risk.

### Risk Prevention
*   **Risk Prevention** (`risk_prevention`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional information on the measures or prevention methods for each risk.

### Risk Frequency
*   **Risk Frequency** (`risk_frequency`, `text`, required: false): Indicate the frequency of each risk.

### Risk Severity
*   **Risk Severity** (`risk_severity`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate the severity of each risk as High, Medium, or Low.

### Risk Status
*   **Risk Status** (`risk_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of each risk as Active, Inactive, or Not Identified.

### Risk Comments
*   **Risk Comments** (`risk_comments`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about each risk.

Please note that this form is a simple and straightforward tool for risk management. It is not intended to be used for complex risk assessments or for any other purpose other than as a basic risk management tool. 

By following these steps and explanations, you should be able to complete the form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
