# Change Management Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Change Management Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate an organization's readiness for change. This form helps identify areas of strength and weakness in an organization's ability to absorb and implement change.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Assessment Date to record the date conducting the assessment.
2. Provide the Organization Name of the organization being assessed.
3. Enter the name of the Assessor conducting the assessment.
4. Describe the Change Initiative Being Assessed, providing a brief overview of the change.
5. Select the Type of Change from the given options (Organizational Restructuring, Technology Implementation, Process Improvement, Merger or Acquisition, Cultural Change, or Strategic Pivot).
6. Select Organizational Readiness for Change from the given options (Very Ready, Ready, Somewhat Ready, Not Ready, Highly Resistant).
7. Select Leadership Commitment Level from the given options (Fully Committed, Mostly Committed, Neutral, Hesitant, Opposed).
8. Choose Employee Buy-in and Support from the given options (Strong Support, Moderate Support, Mixed Reactions, Resistance, Strong Resistance).
9. Determine Resource Availability from the given options (Fully Adequate, Mostly Adequate, Somewhat Adequate, Inadequate, Critical Shortage).
10. Choose whether a Change Communication Plan is Comprehensive, Partial, Minimal, No Plan, or Not Started.
11. Select the Resistance Management Plan from the given options (Well Developed, Partially Developed, Underdeveloped, No Plan).
12. Assess Training and Support Readiness from the given options (Comprehensive, Adequate, Partial, Minimal, None).
13. Identify Key Risks from the free text field (main risks to change success).
14. Describe the Risk Mitigation Strategies to manage identified risks.
15. Define Success Metrics from the free text field (how will success be measured).
16. Assess the Timeline Feasibility from the given options (Very Feasible, Feasible, Challenging, Unrealistic, Not Determined).
17. Determine Stakeholder Alignment from the given options (Highly Aligned, Mostly Aligned, Partially Aligned, Misaligned, Not Aligned).
18. Assess Organizational Capacity for Change from the given options (High Capacity, Moderate Capacity, Limited Capacity, Overwhelmed, Insufficient Capacity).
19. Rate Overall Change Readiness Score from 1-10.
20. Provide Recommendations for Success (Actions to improve readiness).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date (assessment_date)**: Date conducting assessment
* **Organization Name (organization_name)**: Your organization
* **Assessed By (assessed_by)**: Name of assessor
* **Change Initiative Being Assessed (change_initiative)**: Description of the change
* **Type of Change (change_type)**: Category of change
* **Organizational Readiness for Change (organizational_readiness)**: How ready is the organization
* **Leadership Commitment Level (leadership_commitment)**: Executive support for change
* **Employee Buy-in and Support (employee_buy_in)**: Employee acceptance of change
* **Resource Availability (resource_availability)**: Budget, time, people for change
* **Change Communication Plan (communication_plan)**: Is a communication plan in place
* **Resistance Management Plan (resistance_management_plan)**: Strategy for managing resistance
* **Training and Support Readiness (training_readiness)**: Training provided for change
* **Key Risks (key_risks)**: Main risks to change success
* **Risk Mitigation Strategies (risk_mitigation)**: How risks will be managed
* **Success Metrics (success_metrics)**: How will success be measured
* **Timeline Feasibility (timeline_feasibility)**: Is proposed timeline realistic
* **Stakeholder Alignment (stakeholder_alignment)**: Agreement among stakeholders
* **Organizational Capacity for Change (organizational_capacity)**: Ability to absorb change
* **Overall Change Readiness Score (overall_readiness_score)**: Rate readiness 1-10
* **Recommendations for Success (assessment_recommendations)**: Actions to improve readiness
