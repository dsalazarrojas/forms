# Third Party Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the performance and behavior of a third party. It consists of 10 questions that will help you evaluate their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the questions carefully and answer them based on your honest assessment of the third party's performance.
2.  Enter your answers in the text fields provided.
3.  Select the relevant performance area from the options when prompted.
4.  Make sure to complete all required fields.
5.  Submit your assessment once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page**: Enter a brief description of the third party's performance or behavior that you would like to assess.
*  **performance_areas**: Choose one or more areas relevant to the third party's performance, such as "Communication", "Teamwork", or "Adaptability".
*  **first_question**: Provide a detailed answer to the first question, describing the third party's performance or behavior.
*  **second_question**: Answer the second question, providing further details about the third party's performance or behavior.
*  **third_question**: Respond to the third question, continuing to assess the third party's performance or behavior.
*  **fourth_question**: Provide an answer to the fourth question, highlighting specific examples or observations.
*  **fifth_question**: Answer the fifth question, offering additional insights into the third party's performance.
*  **sixth_question**: Respond to the sixth question, continuing to assess the third party's performance or behavior.
*  **seventh_question**: Answer the seventh question, highlighting specific examples or observations.
*  **eighth_question**: Provide an answer to the eighth question, offering additional details.
*  **ninth_question**: Respond to the ninth question, continuing to assess the third party's performance or behavior.
*  **tenth_question**: Answer the tenth question, concluding the assessment of the third party's performance or behavior.

## Tips
- Be honest and objective in your assessment.
- Avoid making personal judgments or biases.
- Provide specific examples or observations to support your answers.
- Review and submit your assessment carefully before finalizing it.
- If you are unsure about any question, please feel free to ask for clarification.
