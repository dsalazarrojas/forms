# Student Screening Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Screening Assessment Form is used to gather information about students, including their personal details and academic requirements. This form is essential for the assessment and identification of students, helping to ensure that they receive the support and resources they need to succeed in their educational journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Student Screening Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.
2. For fields with a drop-down menu, select the relevant option from the list.
3. Enter your answers carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (*) 
First Name is a required field where you should enter the student's first name.

### Last Name (*) 
Last Name is a required field where you should enter the student's last name.

### Date of Birth 
Date of Birth is an optional field where you can enter the student's date of birth.

### Email (*) 
Email is a required field where you should enter the student's email address.

### Phone Number 
Phone Number is an optional field where you can enter the student's phone number.

### Student Identifier 
Student Identifier is a required field where you should enter a unique identifier for the student.

### Academic Needs 
Academic Needs is an optional field where you can select the relevant options for the student's academic needs.

### Support Requirements 
Support Requirements is an optional field where you can enter any additional support requirements for the student.

### Notes 
Notes is an optional field where you can enter any additional information about the student.

### Next Steps 
Next Steps is an optional field where you can enter any next steps for the student's assessment and support.
