# Business Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess your business knowledge, skills, and interests to help the organization identify areas where you need more training or development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and honestly.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, based on your current knowledge and experience within the company.
3. Be sure to review the entire form before submitting it to ensure you have addressed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Your full name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, not required): Your employee number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select your department (e.g., Executive, Operations, Sales, etc.).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Your current job title.
* **Months with Company** (`tenure_months`, number, required): How many months you have worked with the company.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest completed education (e.g., High school, Some college, Associate degree, etc.).
* **Business Fundamentals Knowledge** (`business_fundamentals_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of business fundamentals.
* **Financial Literacy Knowledge** (`financial_literacy_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of financial concepts.
* **Marketing Knowledge** (`marketing_knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your marketing knowledge.
* **Operations Knowledge** (`operations_knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your operations management knowledge.
* **Technology Proficiency** (`technology_proficiency_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your technology skills.
* **Leadership Skills** (`leadership_skills_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your leadership capabilities.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your communication abilities.
* **Problem Solving Skills** (`problem_solving_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your problem-solving abilities.
* **Industry Knowledge** (`industry_knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of our industry.
* **Compliance and Regulatory Knowledge** (`compliance_knowledge_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your understanding of relevant regulations.
* **Training Interests** (`training_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select areas where you would like more training (e.g., Business strategy, Financial analysis, etc.).
* **Preferred Learning Method** (`learning_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method of learning (e.g., Online courses, In-person workshops, etc.).
* **Certification Goals** (`certification_goals`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are actively pursuing or planning to pursue professional certifications.
* **Career Aspirations** (`career_aspirations`, text, not required): State your career goals within the company.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, not required): Provide any other information about your knowledge or skills.
