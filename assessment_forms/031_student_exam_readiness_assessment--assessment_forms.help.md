# Student Exam Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a student's exam readiness. It includes fields for collecting information about the student's ID, name, and grade level, as well as their exam date, time, and duration. The form also includes fields for teacher and student comments, and recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant student ID from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Choose whether the student is ready for the exam (Yes/No).
4. Select the exam date.
5. Choose the exam time.
6. Enter the exam duration.
7. Leave comments for the teacher and student (optional).
8. Choose whether the teacher recommends the student take the exam (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID**: Select the student's ID from the dropdown menu to associate this form with the correct student.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name to identify the student being assessed.
* **Exam Date**: Select the date of the exam.
* **Exam Time**: Select the time of the exam.
* **Exam Duration**: Enter the duration of the exam (in minutes).
* **Teacher Comments**: Leave notes or comments for the teacher to provide feedback.
* **Student Comments**: Leave notes or comments for the student to provide feedback.
* **Teacher Recommendations**: Choose whether the teacher recommends the student take the exam (Yes/No).
* **Student Grade**: Choose whether the student is ready for the exam (Yes/No).
