# Disability Insurance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their disability insurance needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide the required information in each field. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of questions.
- Please answer each question carefully and accurately to ensure that you receive a correct and personalized quote.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Assessment** (`assessment`, `text`, required): This field is for you to describe your current health status and any medical conditions you may have.
  * Please provide a brief description of your current health status and any medical conditions you may have.
  * This information will help us assess your eligibility for disability insurance.
- **Insurance Agent** (`insurance_agent`, `text`, optional): If you have a preferred insurance agent, please provide their name or ID number.
  * This will help us contact your insurance agent and provide you with personalized service.
- **Client** (`client`, `text`, required): This field is for you to provide your name and any other relevant information.
  * Please provide your full name and any other relevant information about yourself.
- **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): This field is for you to provide your age.
  * Please enter your age in numerical format (e.g., 35).
- **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to work, please describe them here.
  * Please provide a brief description of your medical conditions.
  * This information will help us assess your eligibility for disability insurance.
- **Disability Start Date** (`disability_start_date`, `date`, required): This field is for you to provide the date you became disabled.
  * Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
- **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your insurance provider from the list.
  * This will help us determine your coverage options.
- **Other Providers** (`other_providers`, `text`, optional): If you have any other insurance providers, please list them here.
  * This information will help us assess your coverage options.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): This field is for you to describe your medical history.
  * Please provide a brief description of your medical history.
  * This information will help us assess your eligibility for disability insurance.
- **Income** (`income`, `number`, required): This field is for you to provide your income.
  * Please enter your income in numerical format (e.g., 50000).
- **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, required): This field is for you to provide your occupation.
  * Please provide a brief description of your occupation.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select your employment status from the list.
  * This will help us determine your coverage options.
- **Investment** (`investment`, `number`, required): This field is for you to provide the amount you want to invest in disability insurance.
  * Please enter the amount in numerical format (e.g., 500).
- **Frequency** (`investment_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Please select the frequency at which you want to invest in disability insurance.
  * This will help us determine your coverage options.
- **Option** (`investment_option`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select the option for your investment in disability insurance.
  * This will help us determine your coverage options.
- **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Please select your agreement to the terms and conditions.
  * This is a mandatory question to ensure your understanding of the policy.
  * Please read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting this form.
