# Small Business Financial Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather and organize financial data for small businesses. It consists of 8 questions, covering revenue, financial goals, expenses, and other relevant financial details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Revenue Data" page and enter the total revenue for the current year.
2. Choose the financial goals you are currently pursuing (select all that apply).
3. Enter any additional financial data relevant to your business.
4. Select the time period for the data you are providing (e.g., current year, last quarter, etc.).
5. Choose a date for your financial planning (if applicable).
6. (Optional) Drag and drop any relevant files or images to provide context for your financial data.
7. (Optional) Provide any additional comments or context for your form submission.
8. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Revenue Data** (Page 1, Text):
	+ Enter the total revenue for the current year. This field is used to gather information about the business's revenue.
* **Financial Goals** (Page 1, Select Multiple):
	+ Choose all the financial goals you are currently pursuing. This field is used to identify the business's financial priorities.
* **Expense Data** (Page 1, Text):
	+ Enter any additional financial data relevant to your business. This field is used to gather more specific financial information.
* **Time Period** (Page 1, Time):
	+ Select the time period for the data you are providing. This field helps to determine the scope of the financial data.
* **Financial Planning** (Page 1, Date):
	+ Choose a date for your financial planning. This field is used to establish a deadline for financial planning and analysis.
* **Drag and Drop** (Page 1, Text):
	+ (Optional) Upload any relevant files or images to provide context for your financial data.
* **Organized Form Submissions** (Page 1, Text):
	+ (Optional) Provide any additional comments or context for your form submission.
* **No-Code Builder** (Page 1, Text):
	+ (Optional) Enter any details about using a no-code builder in your financial planning and analysis.
