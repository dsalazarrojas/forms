# Online Assessment Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Assessment Consent Form is used to obtain consent from individuals or their authorized representatives for an online assessment. This form ensures that the individual understands the assessment process, their rights, and any potential risks or consequences associated with the assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `contact_person` field.
2. Select your relationship to the individual being assessed from the `relationship` dropdown menu.
3. If you selected "other" as your relationship, please provide more information in the `relationship_other` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_person** (text, required): Enter the name of the person providing consent.
* **relationship** (select_one, required): Select the individual's relationship to the person being assessed. You may choose from "parent," "guardian," "other," or "other" if you are an other relationship such as a legal guardian.
* **Relationship Other** (text, optional): If you selected "other" as your relationship, provide a brief description of your relationship to the individual being assessed.
* **email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the person providing consent. This will be used to notify you of any assessment results or further updates.
