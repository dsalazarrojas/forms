# writing-advisor-feedback-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from users who have interacted with the writing advisor. Your input is valuable in helping us improve the writing advisor service. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form and provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through each page to ensure you have reviewed all sections before submitting your form.
2. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
3. Provide your experience with the writing advisor in the **What was your experience with your writing advisor?** field.
4. In the **Rating** field, select your overall experience level with the advisor, from 1 to 5.
5. Share what you found most helpful about your experience in the **What did you find most helpful about your experience?** field.
6. If you think there's room for improvement, let us know in the **What could be improved about your experience?** field.
7. Answer the question **Have you been able to accomplish your goals as a result of your experience with the writing advisor?** with either "True" or "False".
8. If you would recommend the writing advisor to others, answer **How likely are you to recommend the writing advisor to others?** with either "True" or "False".
9. Add any additional comments about your experience in the **What other comments do you have?** field.
10. Enter your date of interaction with the advisor in the **Date** field.
11. Enter your time of interaction with the advisor in the **Time** field.
12. Provide your email address (if you'd like to be contacted) in the **Email Address** field.
13. Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted) in the **Phone Number** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (Text)**: Enter your name for the feedback submission.
* **What was your experience with your writing advisor? (Text)**: Share your overall experience with the writing advisor.
* **Rating (Select One)**: Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to rate your experience with the writing advisor.
* **What did you find most helpful about your experience? (Text)**: Describe what you found most helpful about your interaction with the advisor.
* **What could be improved about your experience? (Text)**: Share any areas of improvement you think could be made.
* **Have you been able to accomplish your goals as a result of your experience with the writing advisor? (Select One)**: Answer with either "True" or "False".
* **How likely are you to recommend the writing advisor to others? (Select One)**: Answer with either "True" or "False".
* **What other comments do you have? (Text)**: Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of your interaction with the advisor.
* **Time (Time)**: Enter the time of your interaction with the advisor.
* **Email Address (Email)**: Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
* **Phone Number (Text)**: Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).

## Tips

* Please fill out the form completely and honestly to help us improve the writing advisor service.
* You can add any comments or feedback in the **What other comments do you have?** field if you have more thoughts.
* Make sure to enter accurate information in the **Email Address** and **Phone Number** fields if you'd like to be contacted.
* If you have any further questions, don't hesitate to reach out.
