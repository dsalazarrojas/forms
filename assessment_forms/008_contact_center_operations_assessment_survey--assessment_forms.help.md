# Contact Center Operations Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees about their experiences and perceptions of the contact center operations. It aims to gather insights on various aspects of the workplace, including training, communication, tools, and job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most appropriate answer for each question.
3. If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, type them in the "Recommendations for Improvement" field.
4. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `text`, optional): This field is for your employee ID. If you don't have one, you can leave it blank.
- **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select your department from the given options.
- **Your Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role in the contact center from the given options.
- **Tenure at Company** (`tenure`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you have worked in the contact center.
- **Training Program Quality** (`training_quality`, `select_one`, required): Rate the quality of the training programs received.
- **Training Effectiveness** (`training_effectiveness`, `select_one`, required): How well did the training prepare you for your role?
- **Internal Communication Rating** (`communication_rating`, `select_one`, required): Rate the communication within teams.
- **Tools and Technology** (`tools_and_technology`, `select_one`, required): Rate the adequacy of the current tools and technology.
- **Workload Management** (`workload_management`, `select_one`, required): How manageable is your workload?
- **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall job satisfaction.
- **Management Support** (`management_support`, `select_one`, required): Rate the level of support from management.
- **Career Development Opportunities** (`career_development`, `select_one`, required): Rate the opportunities for career development.
- **Work-Life Balance** (`work_life_balance`, `select_one`, required): Rate your work-life balance.
- **Customer Satisfaction Focus** (`customer_satisfaction_focus`, `select_one`, required): Rate the priority given to customer satisfaction.
- **Process Efficiency** (`process_efficiency`, `select_one`, required): Rate the efficiency of current processes.
- **Areas Needing Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all areas that need improvement.
- **Recommendations for Improvement** (`recommendations`, `text`, optional): Share your suggestions for improvement.
- **Would Recommend as Workplace** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, required): Would you recommend working in the contact center?

## Tips

* Take your time to answer each question carefully and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any question, select the option that best represents your opinion.
* Remember to rate your answers based on your personal experience and perception.
* Your feedback is crucial in helping us improve the contact center operations.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the "Recommendations for Improvement" field.
