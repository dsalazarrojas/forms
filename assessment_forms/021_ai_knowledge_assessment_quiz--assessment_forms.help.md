# AI Knowledge Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this AI Knowledge Assessment Quiz is to evaluate the participant's understanding and knowledge of various AI-related topics. The quiz consists of multiple-choice and short-answer questions that cover core AI concepts, modern applications, and ethical considerations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have a clear understanding of each question and its related field.
* Read each question carefully and select or enter your response accordingly.
* Use the provided options or fields to answer each question.
* Fill out all required fields, marked as "true."
* You can leave optional fields blank if you don't have information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the assessment report.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive a copy of your assessment report.
* **Department / Team** (`department_team`, `text`, optional): If you are participating as part of a team or department, enter the name of your team or department.
* **Section 1: Core AI Concepts** (`quiz_section_1_header`, `note`, required): This section covers fundamental principles of AI and machine learning.
	+ **Which of the following best defines Machine Learning?** (`q1_ml_definition`, `select_one`, required): Choose the most accurate description of machine learning.
	+ **Briefly explain the main difference between Supervised and Unsupervised learning.** (`q2_supervised_unsupervised`, `text`, required): Focus on the role of labeled data.
	+ **Which of the following are types of Neural Network architectures? (Select all that apply)** (`q3_neural_network_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable types of neural network architectures.
	+ **What happens when an AI model is 'Overfitting'?** (`q4_overfitting`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct description of overfitting.
* **Section 2: Modern Applications & Ethics** (`quiz_section_2_header`, `note`, optional): This section explores modern applications and ethics of AI.
	+ **What does 'LLM' stand for in the context of AI?** (`q5_llm_meaning`, `text`, required): Enter the correct abbreviation for LLM.
	+ **Which of these are examples of Generative AI models or platforms? (Select all that apply)** (`q6_generative_ai_models`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable examples of Generative AI models or platforms.
	+ **What is a primary source of 'Bias' in AI systems?** (`q7_ai_bias_sources`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct primary source of bias in AI systems.
* **Self-Reflection** (`self_assessment_header`, `note`, optional): This section is for personal reflection on your assessment experience.
* **On a scale of 1-10, how confident do you feel in your overall AI knowledge?** (`self_rating_confidence`, `integer`, required): Rate your confidence level in AI knowledge.
* **Feedback on this assessment** (`quiz_feedback`, `text`, optional): Provide any feedback on the quiz, e.g., too easy, too hard, or just right.
* **Date of Completion** (`completion_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you completed the quiz.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out to complete the form. Optional fields can be left blank if you don't have the necessary information.
