# Cryptic Text Analysis Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cryptic Text Analysis Survey is designed to gather users' thoughts and opinions on certain passages. This form is meant to elicit information about users' perceptions, attitudes, or interpretations of the provided texts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each passage carefully and take your time to understand the content.
2. For the Sentiment Scale field, select the option that best represents your emotional response or attitude towards the passage.
3. In the text fields (First Passage, Second Passage, Third Passage, Interpretation, Second Passage Interpretation, Additional Comments, First Passage Interpretation), provide your thoughts, comments, or insights about the passage.
4. Enter your responses in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Passage** (`first_passage`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your thoughts or comments about the passage.
* **Sentiment Scale** (`sentiment_scale`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose your emotional response or attitude towards the passage. Select one of the options: Positive, Negative, or Neutral.
* **Second Passage** (`second_passage`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your thoughts or comments about the passage.
* **Third Passage** (`third_passage`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your comments or insights about the passage.
* **Interpretation** (`interpretation`, `text`, required/optional): Share your interpretation or understanding of the passage.
* **Second Passage Interpretation** (`second_passage_interpretation`, `text`, required/optional): Explain how you interpreted the second passage.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the passages.
* **First Passage Interpretation** (`first_passage_interpretation`, `text`, required/optional): Share your interpretation or understanding of the first passage.

## Tips
* Please take your time to carefully read each passage before responding.
* Be honest and thorough in your comments.
* If you need help or have questions, feel free to ask the form administrator.
