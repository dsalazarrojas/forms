# Nutritional Assessment Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutritional Assessment Questionnaire Form is designed to gather information about a patient's nutritional status, including their blood sugar, fatty acid, inflammation, and toxicity levels, as well as their eating habits and contact details. This form is not meant to diagnose or treat any medical conditions but rather to collect data for proper care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information about the patient's blood sugar, fatty acid, inflammation, and toxicity levels.
2.  Enter the patient's eating habits in the "Eating Habits" field.
3.  Provide the patient's contact details in the "Patient ID", "Patient Email", "Patient Phone", "Healthcare Provider ID", "Healthcare Provider Email", and "Healthcare Provider Phone" fields.
4.  Enter the date and time of the visit in the "Date of Visit" and "Time of Visit" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Blood Sugar**: A number representing the patient's blood sugar level, indicating how well they are managing their blood sugar. This can be entered as a numerical value.
*   **Fatty Acid**: A number representing the patient's fatty acid levels, indicating how well they are managing their fatty acid levels. This can be entered as a numerical value.
*   **Inflammation**: A number representing the patient's inflammation levels, indicating how well they are managing their inflammation. This can be entered as a numerical value.
*   **Toxicity**: A number representing the patient's toxicity levels, indicating how well they are managing their toxicity. This can be entered as a numerical value.
*   **Eating Habits**: A field for the patient's eating habits. Please describe the patient's eating habits in detail, including their diet, nutritional intake, and any other relevant information.
*   **Patient ID**: A unique identifier for the patient. Enter a number or other unique identifier to distinguish the patient's record.
*   **Patient Email**: A field for the patient's email address. Enter the patient's email address so healthcare providers can contact them.
*   **Patient Phone**: A field for the patient's phone number. Enter the patient's phone number so healthcare providers can contact them.
*   **Healthcare Provider ID**: A unique identifier for the healthcare provider. Enter a number or other unique identifier to distinguish the healthcare provider's record.
*   **Healthcare Provider Email**: A field for the healthcare provider's email address. Enter the healthcare provider's email address so patients can contact them.
*   **Healthcare Provider Phone**: A field for the healthcare provider's phone number. Enter the healthcare provider's phone number so patients can contact them.
*   **Date of Visit**: The date of the visit. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Time of Visit**: The time of the visit. Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure proper care for the patient.
*   Use numerical values for numerical fields.
*   Provide detailed information in the "Eating Habits" field.
*   Use the correct format for date and time entries.
