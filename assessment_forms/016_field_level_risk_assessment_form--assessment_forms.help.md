# Field Level Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Level Risk Assessment Form is used to identify and document risks at the field level, allowing for the tracking and management of potential threats to the project's success. This form helps to provide a clear and concise description of each risk, including its impact severity, likelihood, and mitigation actions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the **Project Name** in the field provided. This is a brief description of the project you are assessing.
*   Select the **Risk Level** (1-5) that best represents the potential impact of the risk on the project.
*   Describe the **Risk Type** as it relates to the project and its context.
*   Provide a short statement of the **Risk Summary** (in one or two sentences) describing the risk.
*   Select the **Impact Severity** (1-5) that best represents the potential impact of the risk on the project.
*   Select the **Likelihood** (1-5) that best represents the likelihood of the risk occurrence.
*   Provide a detailed description of the **Risk Level Description**.
*   Outline the **Mitigation Actions** that can be taken to reduce or eliminate the risk.
*   Describe the **Next Steps** that can be taken to address the risk.
*   Identify the **Risk Owner** who is responsible for the risk.
*   If applicable, assign a specific **Assigned Tool** to the risk.
*   Enter your **Created By** in order to track who created the risk assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project you are assessing.
*   **Risk Level**: Select the risk level that best represents the potential impact of the risk on the project. The risk level is represented on a scale of 1-5, where 1 represents a low risk and 5 represents a high risk.
*   **Risk Type**: Describe the type of risk as it relates to the project and its context.
*   **Risk Summary**: Provide a short statement describing the risk in one or two sentences.
*   **Impact Severity**: Select the impact severity that best represents the potential impact of the risk on the project. The impact severity is represented on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Likelihood**: Select the likelihood that best represents the likelihood of the risk occurrence. The likelihood is represented on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Risk Level Description**: Provide a detailed description of the risk in more detail.
*   **Mitigation Actions**: Outline the actions that can be taken to reduce or eliminate the risk.
*   **Next Steps**: Describe the next steps that can be taken to address the risk.
*   **Risk Owner**: Identify the risk owner who is responsible for the risk.
*   **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, assign a specific tool to the risk.
*   **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the risk assessment.
