# Syllable Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Syllable Evaluation Form is designed to collect user feedback on their Syllable score, which is a measure of their communication skills. This form is intended to gather accurate and relevant information from users to help evaluate their speaking abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the five pages and answer the questions as they appear. Each page will ask for a specific piece of information, which will be used to calculate your Syllable score. Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and truthfully to ensure the best possible evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (<page_1>): Provide a brief description of your background and experience with public speaking. This will help us understand your context and situation.
* **Speech and Language History** (<page_2>): Share your history with speech and language, including any formal training or experience you may have had in this area.
* **Speech Clarity** (<page_3>): Evaluate your own speech clarity, rating it from 1-5, where 1 is difficult to understand and 5 is very clear.
* **Speech Production** (<page_4>): Describe your ability to produce and deliver speech, including your pace, tone, and body language.
* **Additional Comments** (<page_5>): If you have any additional information or comments you'd like to share, please use this field to provide more context.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate in your responses, as your Syllable score will reflect your actual abilities.
* Make sure to fill out all fields as thoroughly as possible to ensure a complete evaluation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
