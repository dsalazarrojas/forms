# Parks Investment Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the condition and quality of parks, including site evaluation, accessibility, maintenance, and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Choose the correct options for each select field.
3. Enter your submission date and time.
4. Ensure that you have accurately and thoroughly filled out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Park Site Evaluation Form** (`site_evaluation_form`, text, optional): A text field for evaluating the site condition.
* **Site Description** (`site_description`, text, optional): A text field for describing the site.
* **Overall Site Score** (`park_score`, number, optional): Enter a numerical score for the site.
* **Site Condition** (`site_condition`, select_one, optional): Choose the condition of the site from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Maintenance Frequencies** (`maintenance_frequencies`, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you visit the park or its surroundings from Often, Occasionally, Rarely, or Never.
* **Park Accessibility** (`park_accessibility`, select_one, optional): Choose the accessibility of the park from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Park Maintenance** (`park_maintenance`, select_one, optional): Choose the maintenance level of the park from Well Maintained, Average, or Poorly Maintained.
* **Park Enjoyment** (`park_enjoyment`, text, required): A text field for evaluating the park's enjoyment.
* **Park Safety** (`park_safety`, select_one, optional): Choose the safety of the park from Very Safe, Average, Somewhat Safe, or Somewhat Not Safe.
* **Scores** (`scores`, note, required): A field for entering overall scores or notes about the park.
* **Site Evaluations** (`site_evaluations`, select_multiple, required): Choose the evaluation of the site from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): A field for entering any additional comments about the park.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): A field for entering the contact person's name.
* **Contact Organisation** (`contact_organisation`, text, optional): A field for entering the contact organisation's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): A field for entering the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): A field for entering the contact person's phone number.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): The date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): The person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, optional): The time when the form was submitted.
* **Park ID** (`park_id`, text, required): Enter the park's ID.
* **Park Name** (`park_name`, text, required): Enter the park's name.
* **Park Address** (`park_address`, text, required): Enter the park's address.
* **Park City State** (`park_city_state`, text, required): Enter the park's city and state.
