# Opinion Article Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from users about articles. It helps us understand users' perceptions and experiences with the article.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the article's title in the **Article Title** field. This is a required field if you want to submit feedback about a specific article.
2. Provide a brief summary of your experience with the article in the **Article Body** field.
3. Rate the article from 1 to 5 in the **Article Rating** field. This will give us an idea of how engaging or useful you found the article.
4. Choose the **Article Recommendation** that best fits your experience with the article.
5. Enter the date you read or interacted with the article in the **Article Date** field.
6. Enter the time you read or interacted with the article in the **Article Time** field.
7. Provide your email address in the **Article E-mail** field.
8. Enter your phone number in the **Article Phone** field.
9. Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the article in the **Article Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Article Title**: Enter the title of the article you're providing feedback about.
* **Article Body**: Provide a brief summary of your experience with the article.
* **Article Rating**: Rate the article from 1 to 5. A higher rating indicates a more positive experience.
* **Article Recommendation**: Choose the option that best fits your experience with the article.
* **Article Date**: Enter the date you read or interacted with the article.
* **Article Time**: Enter the time you read or interacted with the article.
* **Article E-mail**: Enter your email address.
* **Article Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Article Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about the article.
* **User Name**: Enter your name.
* **User Rating**: Rate your experience as a user from 1 to 5.
* **User Recommendation**: Choose the option that best fits your experience as a user.
* **User E-mail**: Enter your email address.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **User Comments**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience as a user.
