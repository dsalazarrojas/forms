# Agricultural Crop Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Agricultural Crop Assessment Form is designed for farmers, agronomists, or field inspectors to record and report on the current state of crops in fields. This form helps in collecting data for crop monitoring, decision-making, and potential interventions to optimize agricultural productivity and crop yields.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you are on the correct page and that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
3.  For fields with options, select the most appropriate choice.
4.  Upload photos to the central server if required or if it's a beneficial addition to your assessment.
5.  Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
*   **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Record the date of the field visit.
*   **Field ID** (`field_id`, text, required): Provide a unique identifier for the field.
*   **Crop Type** (`crop_type`, select_one, required): Choose the primary crop in the field from the list: Corn, Wheat, Soybeans, Cotton, Rice, Specialty Crop.
*   **Growth Stage** (`growth_stage`, select_one, required): Select the current growth stage from: Germination, Vegetative, Flowering, Fruit Development, Maturity, Harvest Ready.
*   **Pest Pressure** (`pest_pressure`, select_one, required): Choose the severity of the pest infestation from: None, Low, Medium, High, Severe.
*   **Weed Presence** (`weed_presence`, select_one, required): Select the density of weeds from: None, Scattered, Moderate, Heavy.
*   **Soil Moisture** (`soil_moisture`, select_one, required): Choose the current soil moisture level from: Dry, Moist, Wet, Saturated.
*   **Estimated Yield** (`estimated_yield`, number, optional): Enter the estimated yield in bushels or tons per acre.
*   **Photos** (`photos`, note, optional): Upload photos of the field for reference.
*   **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, required): Write action items for the grower to implement.

Note:
*   Ensure you provide accurate and comprehensive data in each field.
*   If unsure of a field type, select "Specialty Crop" for the Crop Type.
*   If you need assistance or additional guidance, contact your supervisor or agricultural team leader.

By following this guide, you should be able to accurately and thoroughly complete the Agricultural Crop Assessment Form.
