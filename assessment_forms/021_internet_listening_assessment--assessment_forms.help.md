# internet_listening_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "internet_listening_assessment" form aims to evaluate an individual's internet usage habits, skills, and challenges. It helps identify areas of improvement or provide support for those who struggle with internet usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by filling out the "Introduction" field with a brief statement about your internet usage habits.
2. For the "Internet Use" field, select one of the provided options indicating how often you use the internet:
   - **Never**: You do not use the internet.
   - **Less than 5 hours a day**: You use the internet for less than 5 hours a day.
   - **5-10 hours a day**: You use the internet for 5-10 hours a day.
   - **More than 10 hours a day**: You use the internet for more than 10 hours a day.
3. In the "Internet Skills" field, select the skills you possess that help you use the internet effectively:
   - **Browsing and navigating**: You can navigate and browse online with ease.
   - **Online searching and research**: You can efficiently find and search for online information.
   - **Online communication and interaction**: You can communicate and interact with others online.
   - **Online learning and education**: You can learn new things and improve yourself online.
   - **Other (please specify)**: You possess other skills that are not listed.
4. Identify the challenges you face when using the internet in the "Challenges" field. Select all that apply:
   - **Limited internet access**: You have difficulty accessing the internet due to restrictions or limitations.
   - **Slow internet speed**: Your internet speed is slow, affecting online performance.
   - **Difficulty understanding online content**: You struggle to understand online content or instructions.
   - **Other (please specify)**: You face other challenges not listed here.
5. In "Online Communication" and "Online Learning and Education," you can add any additional information or comments about your online activities.
6. Finally, provide any additional comments or thoughts in the "Additional Comments" field.
