# Answer Sheet Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for students to submit their answers to an assignment, which will then be automatically graded. Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: Student full name, Student email, Assignment id or title, Submission method, Number of answers provided, and Submission date.
2. Choose how you provided your answers (Manual entry, File upload, or Link to document).
3. Optional: Add any comments for the instructor.
4. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student full name (Student full name)** (`student_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student email (Student email)** (`student_email`, email, **required**): Enter your student email address. This will be used to send you confirmation and score messages.
* **Assignment id or title (Assignment id or title)** (`assignment_id`, text, **required**): Enter the title or id of the assignment you are submitting answers for. For example, "Homework 3".
* **Submission method (Submission method)** (`submission_method`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you provided your answers: 
	+ Manual entry: You typed your answers directly into this form.
	+ File upload: You uploaded a file with your answers.
	+ Link to document: You provided a link to your answers online.
* **Number of answers provided (Number of answers provided)** (`number_of_answers`, number, **required**): Enter the total number of answers you provided.
* **Comments for instructor (Comments for instructor)** (`comments_for_instructor`, text, **optional**): You can add any comments you have for the instructor about your submission.
* **Submission date (Submission date)** (`submission_date`, date, **required**): Select the date and time of your submission.
