# Lens Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lens Knowledge Assessment form is a diagnostic tool used to gauge a user's knowledge and understanding of a specific topic or concept related to lenses. The form is designed to evaluate their proficiency level, lens type, and ability to apply their knowledge to real-world scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Lens Knowledge Assessment form, follow these steps:

1.  Please carefully review the form and ensure you understand the questions and the options provided.
2.  For each question, select the most accurate response from the options available, based on your knowledge and experience with lenses.
3.  Answer all questions, as this will help us assess your knowledge and provide a comprehensive evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, optional): The introduction section is a brief background statement about the assessment. Please provide a short statement outlining your understanding of the topic or concept related to the lens.
* **Assessment** (`Assessment`, `select_one`, optional): Please answer "Yes" if you have a good understanding of the assessment topic, and "No" otherwise.
* **Lens Type** (`Lens Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the lens types that apply to your understanding of the assessment topic.
* **Confidence Level** (`Confidence Level`, `number`, optional): Indicate your confidence level in your answer, on a scale from 1-10.
* **Application Examples** (`Application Examples`, `select_one`, optional): Please answer "Yes" if you can apply your knowledge to real-world scenarios related to the lens, and "No" otherwise.
