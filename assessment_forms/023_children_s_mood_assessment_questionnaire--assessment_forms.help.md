# Children Mood Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the current emotional state and well-being of a child, helping to identify potential issues related to their mood. It's a questionnaire for caregivers, parents, or clinicians to gather information about the child's emotional state and any changes that may be occurring.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form for each child you're assessing.
- Start by entering the child's name and your relationship to the child (parent, teacher, caregiver, or clinician).
- Choose how the child is currently feeling, considering the options provided.
- Indicate how often mood changes occur.
- If applicable, report any changes in sleep patterns.
- If applicable, report any significant changes in appetite.
- Assess the impact of their mood on daily functioning, such as school or relationships.
- You may add any additional observations or details in the free text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Name (1)**: Enter the child's full name.
* **Respondent Role (2)**: Select your relationship to the child. This helps us understand who is providing the information.
* **Current Mood (3)**: Choose the best description of the child's current emotional state. This will help us understand their mood's intensity and type.
* **Mood Frequency (4)**: Indicate how often the child's mood changes occur. This helps us identify potential patterns or triggers.
* **Changes in Sleep (5)**: If the child's sleep patterns have changed, select the corresponding option. This can indicate potential issues related to stress or anxiety.
* **Appetite Changes (6)**: If the child's appetite has changed, select the corresponding option. This can be related to emotional state or other factors.
* **Impact on Daily Functioning (7)**: Assess the impact of the child's mood on their daily life, such as school or relationships.
* **Additional Observations (8)**: You may add any additional information or details that might be relevant to the child's emotional state.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when answering the questions to provide accurate information.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, try to answer based on your best observation or understanding.
* Remember, this form is not a substitute for professional medical or psychological evaluation. If you're concerned about a child's emotional state, consult a qualified professional for further assistance.
