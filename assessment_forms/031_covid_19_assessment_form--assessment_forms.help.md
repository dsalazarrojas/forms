# COVID 19 Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Assessment Form is used to evaluate the risk level of patients presenting with COVID-19 symptoms. It helps clinicians to quickly determine the severity of the symptoms and guide further care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information about your symptoms, medical history, and exposure status.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. If you are unsure about any question, select the "Unsure" option or consult with a healthcare professional for guidance.
4. Once you have completed the form, submit it to your healthcare provider for review and guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport. (\*)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (\*)
* **Gender**: Select the correct gender that applies to you. Options are:
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Other
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for follow-up communication. (\*)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes. (\*)
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY. (\*)
* **Current Temperature**: Enter your current body temperature in Fahrenheit. (\*)
* **Fever Present**: Indicate if you currently have a fever over 100.4F. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Current Symptoms**: Select all symptoms you are currently experiencing. Options are:
	+ Cough
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Difficulty breathing
	+ Fatigue
	+ Muscle or body aches
	+ Headache
	+ New loss of taste or smell
	+ Sore throat
	+ Congestion or runny nose
	+ Nausea or vomiting
	+ Diarrhea
	+ Chills
	+ None
* **Symptom Severity**: Select the overall severity of your symptoms. Options are:
	+ Mild - Can manage at home
	+ Moderate - Some limitation
	+ Severe - Significant limitation
	+ Very Severe - Emergency care needed
* **Symptom Onset Date**: Enter the date when your symptoms started, if applicable. (Note: This field is optional)
* **Known COVID-19 Exposure**: Indicate if you have had close contact with a confirmed or suspected COVID-19 case in the past 14 days. Options are:
	+ Yes - Confirmed case
	+ Yes - Suspected case
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Recent Travel History**: Indicate if you have traveled domestically or internationally in the past 14 days. Options are:
	+ Yes - Domestic
	+ Yes - International
	+ No travel
* **Travel Locations**: Enter the locations you have visited, if applicable.
* **High Risk Medical Conditions**: Select all medical conditions that apply to you. Options are:
	+ None
	+ Cancer
	+ Chronic kidney disease
	+ COPD
	+ Heart conditions
	+ Obesity
	+ Pregnancy
	+ Sickle cell disease
	+ Smoking history
	+ Type 2 diabetes
	+ Weakened immune system
* **Age Risk Factor**: Select your age category. Options are:
	+ Under 65
	+ 65 or older
* **Previous COVID-19 Infection**: Indicate if you have had COVID-19 before. Options are:
	+ Yes - Confirmed by test
	+ Yes - Suspected but not tested
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Vaccination Status**: Select your COVID-19 vaccination status. Options are:
	+ Not vaccinated
	+ Partially vaccinated - 1 dose
	+ Fully vaccinated - Primary series
	+ Boosted
* **Emergency Warning Signs**: Select all emergency warning signs you are currently experiencing. Options are:
	+ None
	+ Trouble breathing
	+ Persistent chest pain
	+ New confusion
	+ Inability to wake or stay awake
	+ Bluish lips or face
	+ Other severe symptoms
* **Risk Assessment Result**: This field is completed by the clinician after review of your form. You do not need to fill this out.
* **Testing Recommended**: Indicate if you should undergo testing for COVID-19. Options are:
	+ Yes - PCR test
	+ Yes - Antigen test
	+ No - Not indicated
	+ Already tested
* **Isolation Instructions Given**: Indicate if isolation instructions were given to you. Options are:
	+ Yes - Quarantine at home
	+ Yes - Isolate at home
	+ No - Not indicated
* **Follow-up Plan**: Enter any follow-up plan or instructions given by your clinician.
* **Clinician Name**: Enter the name of the clinician who assessed you, if applicable.
* **Clinician Signature**: Enter the full name of the clinician, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure you answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, consult with a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to your healthcare provider.
* This form is for assessment purposes only and should not be used for medical diagnosis or treatment.
