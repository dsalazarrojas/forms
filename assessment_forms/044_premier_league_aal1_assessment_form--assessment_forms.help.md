# premier-league-aal1-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premier League Assessment Form is a critical tool for evaluating the performance of referees in a sports league. It captures essential information from players, which is used to assess their performance accurately and make informed decisions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Surname in the "Surname" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Specify the Date of Assessment in the "Date of Assessment" field.
5. Enter the Start Time in the "Start Time" field.
6. Enter the Duration of the assessment in the "Duration" field.
7. If applicable, provide the name of your Referees' Club in the "Referees Club" field.
8. If applicable, mention the Referee Assigned to you in the "Referee Assigned" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
  - Example: John
- **Surname** (`surname`, text, required): Please enter your surname.
  - Example: Doe
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `johndoe@example.com`).
  - Example: johndoe@example.com
- **Date of Assessment** (`date_of_assessment`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment in the `YYYY-MM-DD` format (e.g., `2022-01-01`).
  - Example: 2022-01-01
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time in the `HH:MM` format (e.g., `08:00`).
  - Example: 08:00
- **Duration** (`duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the assessment in minutes.
  - Example: 30
- **Referees Club** (`referees_club`, text, optional): If you are a part of a referees club, please enter the name of your club.
  - Example: My Referees Club
- **Referee Assigned** (`referee_assigned`, text, optional): If a referee has been assigned to you, please enter their name.
  - Example: John Smith
