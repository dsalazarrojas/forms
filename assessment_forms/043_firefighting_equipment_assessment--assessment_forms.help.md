# Firefighting Equipment Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the condition and maintenance of fire-fighting equipment in your organization. It's crucial to complete this form to:

* Evaluate the equipment's condition and identify any issues or risks
* Determine if the equipment meets regulatory requirements
* Plan and schedule maintenance and inspections

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the fire-fighting equipment's location and type.
2. Assess the equipment's condition by selecting one of the options for "Firefighting Equipment Last Inspection Result".
3. Provide the date of the last inspection, maintenance, or replacement.
4. Specify the frequency of inspections and maintenance.
5. Identify the person or team responsible for the last inspection.
6. Enter any additional relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firefighting Equipment Location** (`fire_fighting_equipment_location`):
	+ Enter the location where the fire-fighting equipment is installed or stored.
* **Firefighting Equipment Model** (`fire_fighting_equipment_model`):
	+ Enter the model or make of the fire-fighting equipment.
* **Firefighting Equipment Year Of Purchase** (`fire_fighting_equipment_year_of_purchase`):
	+ If known, enter the year the equipment was purchased. If not known, leave this field blank.
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Inspection Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_inspection_date`):
	+ Enter the date of the last inspection or maintenance performed on the equipment.
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Inspection Result** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_inspection_result`):
	+ Select one of the options to describe the equipment's condition after the last inspection:
		+ Firefighting equipment is in good condition
		+ Firefighting equipment is in fair condition
		+ Firefighting equipment is in poor condition
		+ Firefighting equipment is in very poor condition
* **Firefighting Equipment Next Inspection Due Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_next_inspection_due_date`):
	+ If applicable, enter the date when the next inspection is due.
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Replaced Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_replaced_date`):
	+ If applicable, enter the date when the equipment was last replaced or repaired.
* **Firefighting Equipment Inspection Frequency** (`fire_fighting_equipment_inspection_frequency`):
	+ Select the frequency of inspections and maintenance performed on the equipment:
		+ Daily
		+ Weekly
		+ Monthly
		+ Quarterly
		+ Bi-annually
		+ Annually
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Serviced Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_serviced_date`):
	+ If applicable, enter the date when the equipment was last serviced or repaired.
* **Firefighting Equipment Next Due Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_next_due_date`):
	+ If applicable, enter the date when the next maintenance or inspection is due.
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Inspected By** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_inspected_by`):
	+ Select the person or team responsible for the last inspection:
		+ Team Lead
		+ Fire Marshal
		+ Fire Officer
		+ Safety Officer
		+ Other
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Review Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_review_date`):
	+ If applicable, enter the date when the equipment was last reviewed or inspected.
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Test Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_test_date`):
	+ If applicable, enter the date when the equipment was last tested.
* **Firefighting Equipment Last Cleaned Date** (`fire_fighting_equipment_last_cleaned_date`):
	+ If applicable, enter the date when the equipment was last cleaned or maintained.
