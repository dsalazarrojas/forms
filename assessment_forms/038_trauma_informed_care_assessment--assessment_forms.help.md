# Trauma-Informed Care Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trauma-Informed Care Assessment form is designed to collect data on trauma-informed care practices in a specific setting or context. It is used to gather information on the care and services provided, and to identify areas for improvement and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Trauma-Informed Care" as the assessment page.
2. Review the training priorities and select the relevant options that apply to your assessment.
3. Provide information on resource planning and ongoing improvement activities.
4. Add any additional comments or observations.
5. Provide contact information for staff members.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Trauma-Informed Care (1)
* **Trauma-Informed Care** (`trauma_informed_care`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description or summary of the trauma-informed care practices in the setting or context being assessed.

### Training Priorities (2)
* **Training Priorities** (`training_priorities`, select_one, required: false): Select the training priorities that are relevant to the assessment:
	+ Option 1: Trauma-Informed Care
	+ Option 2: Crisis Intervention
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)

### Resource Planning (3)
* **Resource Planning** (`resource_planning`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the resources needed for effective trauma-informed care practices:
	+ Option 1: Trained Staff
	+ Option 2: Equipment and Supplies
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify)

### Ongoing Improvement (4)
* **Ongoing Improvement** (`ongoing_improvement`, text, required: false): Provide information on ongoing activities that support the improvement of trauma-informed care practices.

### Trauma-Informed Practices (5)
* **Trauma-Informed Practices** (`trauma_informed_practices`, time, required: false): Please provide the duration of time spent on trauma-informed practices.

### Additional Comments (6)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or observations.

### Email to Staff (7)
* **Email to Staff** (`email_to_staff`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of staff members to contact.

### Phone to Staff (8)
* **Phone to Staff** (`phone_to_staff`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of staff members to contact.

### Date (9)
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the assessment.

### Tips
- Ensure that each section is completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review each question carefully before providing information.
- If unsure, refer to the original assessment purpose and context.
