# Judge Eval Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users reflect on their experiences and performance within a group, identifying strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer each question in the space provided, being as detailed as possible.
* Use the options provided for the "prioritizing" question to select multiple strengths or areas of improvement.
* For the text fields, provide a written response to each question.
* You are not required to answer every question, but it's recommended to complete all fields to get a comprehensive evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What was your group profile like?
* **What was your group profile like?** (text): A brief description of the group dynamics, including the roles, responsibilities, and relationships among members.

### What were your strengths?
* **What were your strengths?** (select multiple): Select one or more of the options to indicate your strengths within the group. Options:
	+ A
	+ B
	+ C
	+ D

### What was your performance?
* **What was your performance?** (text): A written reflection on your performance within the group, including accomplishments and areas for improvement.

### What is your summary?
* **What is your summary?** (text): A concise summary of your overall experience and takeaways from the group.

### Comments
* **Comments** (text): Any additional thoughts, feedback, or suggestions you have about the group experience.
