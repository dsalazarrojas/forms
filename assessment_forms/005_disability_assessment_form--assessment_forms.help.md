# Disability Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disability Assessment Form is used to collect information from employees about their disabilities and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions accurately and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Please indicate whether you are a student, staff, or non-student in the "Student Status" field.
3. Review and complete each section carefully, as each section is designed to collect specific information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Questions** (Page 1):
	+ **General Questions** (`general_questions`, text, optional):
		- This field is for you to provide any general information or details about your disability or medical condition that you think would be helpful for the organization to know.
* **Medical Conditions** (Page 2):
	+ **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional):
		- Please list any medical conditions you have or are experiencing that may impact your work performance or require accommodations.
* **Student Status** (Page 3):
	+ **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_one, optional):
		- Please select whether you are a student, staff, or non-student. This will help the organization understand your role and provide targeted support.
* **Field 4** (Page 4):
	+ **Field 4** (`field_4`, text, optional):
		- This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your disability or medical condition.
* **Medical History** (Page 5):
	+ **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional):
		- Please provide any relevant medical history that may be helpful for the organization to know.

## Tips
* Please take your time to answer each question carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact your HR representative for guidance.
* The information provided will be kept confidential and used only for internal purposes within the organization.
