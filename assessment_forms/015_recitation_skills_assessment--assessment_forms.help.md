# Recitation Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recitation Skills Assessment form is a tool for evaluating participants' skills in a recitation setting. It helps gather feedback and ratings from both the participant and the evaluator on various aspects of the participant's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and ID in the required fields.
2. Choose your role as a participant (Coach or Student).
3. Select the topic of the recitation you were involved in.
4. Provide your rating and comment on your own performance.
5. If applicable, provide your rating and comment on the topic you were involved in.
6. The evaluator will fill out their rating and comment on your performance.
7. Finally, click "Submit" to submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as a participant.
* **Participant ID**: Enter your unique participant ID.
* **Participant Role**: Choose your role as a participant (Coach or Student).
* **Participant Rating**: Rate your own performance on a scale (to be specified).
* **Participant Comment**: Provide a brief comment on your own performance.
* **Evaluator Feedback**: Enter any feedback you have for the participant.
* **Evaluator Rating**: Rate the participant's performance on a scale (to be specified).
* **Evaluator Comment**: Provide a brief comment on the participant's performance.
* **Recitation Topic**: Select the topic of the recitation you were involved in.
* **Participant Level**: Choose your level of participation (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Recitation Time**: Enter the time of the recitation you were involved in.
* **Participant Feedback**: Provide any feedback you have for the evaluator or other participants.
* **Evaluator Feedback**: Enter any feedback you have for the participant.
* **Recitation Date**: Enter the date of the recitation you were involved in.
* **Participant Comment**: (Same as above, but for the participant to provide feedback to the evaluator)
* **Evaluator Comment**: (Same as above, but for the evaluator to provide feedback to the participant)
