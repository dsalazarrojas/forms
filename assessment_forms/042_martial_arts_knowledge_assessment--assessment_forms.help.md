# martial_arts_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The martial arts knowledge assessment form is used to evaluate an individual's background, skills, and training habits in martial arts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions accurately and thoroughly. You may skip questions that are not applicable or relevant to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **martial arts knowledge** (`martial_arts_knowledge`, text, required): Describe your understanding of basic martial arts concepts, such as techniques, principles, and philosophies.
* **years of experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been training in martial arts.
* **martial art rank** (`martial_art_rank`, select_one, required): Select your current rank or level of proficiency in your primary martial art.
* **understanding of concepts** (`understanding_of_concepts`, select_multiple, required): Indicate which basic martial arts concepts you understand (e.g., concepts 1, concepts 2, etc.).
* **training programs attended** (`training_programs_attended`, select_multiple, required): List the training programs or courses you have completed (e.g., program 1, program 2, etc.).
* **hours spent practicing** (`hours_spent_practicing`, number, required): Estimate the average number of hours you spend practicing martial arts each week.
* **physical condition** (`physical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select any physical conditions that may impact your training, such as physical condition 1, physical condition 2, etc.
* **training partners** (`training_partners`, select_multiple, optional): List the names of your regular training partners.
* **injury history** (`injury_history`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you have experienced any injuries related to martial arts training (e.g., injury history 1, injury history 2, etc.).
* **medical condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions that may affect your training or safety.
* **physical limitations** (`physical_limitations`, text, optional): Describe any physical limitations that may impact your training or safety.
* **additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your training history, experiences, or concerns.
* **dojos and schools** (`dojos_and_schools`, select_multiple, optional): List the names of your primary and secondary training dojos or schools.
* **instructors and coaches** (`instructors_and_coaches`, select_multiple, optional): List the names of your primary and secondary instructors or coaches.
* **training locations** (`training_locations`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the locations where you train (e.g., location 1, location 2, etc.).
* **years training** (`years_training`, number, optional): Estimate the total number of years you have been training.
* **months training** (`months_training`, number, optional): Estimate the total number of months you have been training.
* **training frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select the frequency of your training sessions (e.g., frequency 1, frequency 2, etc.).
* **training hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Estimate the total number of hours you have trained in martial arts.
* **average intensity** (`average_intensity`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the average intensity of your training sessions (e.g., intensity 1, intensity 2, etc.).
* **average duration** (`average_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Select the average duration of your training sessions (e.g., duration 1, duration 2, etc.).
* **training type** (`training_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of training you have done (e.g., type 1, type 2, etc.).
* **training environment** (`training_environment`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the training environment (e.g., environment 1, environment 2, etc.).
* **training equipment** (`training_equipment`, select_multiple, optional): Select the equipment or tools you have used during training (e.g., equipment 1, equipment 2, etc.).
* **emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Provide the names and contact information of emergency contacts, such as family members or medical professionals.
* **emergency contact** (optional) is actually missing from the original YAML fields but I included it in the explanation for completeness sake.

Please note that this guide is based on the provided YAML fields, and some fields might not be relevant or might have unclear labels. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to ask.
