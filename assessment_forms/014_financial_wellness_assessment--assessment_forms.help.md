## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Financial Wellness Assessment, follow these steps:

1. Begin by assessing your annual income.
2. Select the range that best represents your annual income.
3. Report your current savings and monthly savings amounts.
4. Identify your assets, such as checking, savings, investments, and retirement accounts.
5. Select the range that best represents your assets.
6. Answer questions about how you handle financial stress and debt.
7. Indicate whether you have high-interest loans, credit cards, or student loans.
8. Report your risk tolerance and how much financial risk you take.
9. Select the range that best represents your financial independence.
10. Submit your assessment to receive recommendations for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assess Your Finances**: This section is where you report your annual income. Please select the range that best represents your annual income. (Page 1)
* **What Is Your Annual Income**: Enter your annual income. (Page 1)
* **Income Range**: Select the range that best represents your annual income. (Page 1)
* **What Are Your Savings**: Enter your current savings amount. (Page 2)
* **Monthly Savings**: Enter your monthly savings amount. (Page 2)
* **What Are Your Assets**: Identify your assets, such as checking, savings, investments, and retirement accounts. (Page 3)
* **Assets Range**: Select the range that best represents your assets. (Page 3)
* **How Do You Handle Financial Stress?**: This section is a note field for you to describe how you handle financial stress. (Page 4)
* **Do You Have High Interest Loans?**: Indicate whether you have high-interest loans. (Page 4)
* **High Interest Credit Cards**: Indicate whether you have high-interest credit cards. (Page 4)
* **Do You Have High Interest Student Loans?**: Indicate whether you have high-interest student loans. (Page 4)
* **Do You Have Other Debt?**: Indicate whether you have other debt. (Page 4)
* **Financial Independence**: Select the range that best represents your financial independence. (Page 5)
* **What Is Your Risk Tolerance**: Enter your risk tolerance level. (Page 5)
* **How Much Risk Do You Take?**: Enter how much risk you take. (Page 5)
* **How Much Do You Fear Loss?**: Enter how much you fear loss. (Page 5)
* **Financial Independence 2**: Select the range that best represents your financial independence. (Page 5)
