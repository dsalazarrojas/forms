# Pronunciation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pronunciation Evaluation Form is a tool for instructors or evaluators to assess the pronunciation skills of students in language classes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Complete the form for each student only once to avoid creating duplicate records.
3. Ensure that the form is filled out completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Evaluation Date** (`7`, date, required): The date the form is being evaluated.
* **Student Name** (`2`, text, required): The name of the student being evaluated.
* **Audio Upload** (`4`, text, required): A link or path to the audio file uploaded for the student's pronunciation evaluation.
* **Score** (`4`, number, required): A score out of 100 to evaluate the student's pronunciation skills.
* **Score Description** (`5`, text, required): A description of why the score was given.
* **Evaluation Comments** (`6`, note, required): Comments on the student's pronunciation skills, including areas of improvement.
* **Student Audio** (`8`, text, required): A link or path to the student's audio recording.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately to provide the best possible feedback for the students.
* Use the audio upload field to attach a link or path to the student's audio recording.
* The score description field should explain the reasoning behind the score given.
* The evaluation comments field is a good place to provide specific and detailed feedback to the student.
* Remember that the form is for one-time evaluation; if you need to update the form for a student, update the existing record instead of creating a new one.
