# Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Chronic Pain Assessment Questionnaire Form is a comprehensive tool used to evaluate the severity and impact of chronic pain on patients' daily lives. This form is designed for healthcare professionals to gather detailed information about patients' pain experiences, enabling them to provide personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary permissions and authorizations to complete this form.
2.  Read each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient ID Number** (required): Enter the patient's unique identifier number assigned to them by your healthcare system.
*   **Date of Assessment** (required): Enter the date on which you are completing this form.
*   **Main Location of Pain** (required): Provide the specific area where the patient experiences the most pain, being as specific as possible (e.g., back, knee, or head).
*   **How often do you experience this pain?** (required): Select one of the three options:
    *   Constant: Always present
    *   Intermittent: Periodically present
    *   Rare: Occasional
*   **Describe the quality of the pain** (required): Select all that apply to describe the pain experienced:
    *   Aching
    *   Burning
    *   Sharp
    *   Shooting
    *   Throbbing
    *   Numbness
    *   Tingling
*   **Average Pain Intensity (Last 7 Days)** (required): Rate the average pain intensity on a scale of 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst possible).
*   **Worst Pain Intensity (Last 7 Days)** (required): Rate the worst pain intensity on a scale of 0 to 10.
*   **Impact on Daily Life - Sleep Interference** (required): Select one of the four options:
    *   Not at all
    *   Mildly
    *   Moderately
    *   Severely
*   **Impact on Physical Activity (Walking, Exercise)** (required): Select one of the four options:
    *   No Impact
    *   Some Impact
    *   Major Impact
    *   Unable to perform
*   **Has your mood been affected by the pain?** (required): Select one of the three options:
    *   Not at all
    *   Slightly
    *   Significantly
*   **Current Pain Medications and Dosages** (optional): Enter any current medications and dosages being used to manage pain.
*   **What makes the pain worse?** (optional): Provide any factors that exacerbate the pain (e.g., cold weather, sitting, or stress).
*   **What makes the pain better?** (optional): Provide any factors that alleviate the pain (e.g., heat, rest, or massage).
*   **Clinician Name Completing Form** (required): Enter your name as the healthcare professional completing this form.

Note: Please ensure you have all necessary permissions and authorizations to complete this form, and avoid duplicate submissions.
