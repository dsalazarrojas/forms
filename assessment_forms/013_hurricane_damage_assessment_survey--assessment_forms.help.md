# Hurricane Damage Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information related to hurricane damage assessment to help organizations and emergency services respond to the disaster and provide aid to those affected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure that you have access to the requested information and that it is up-to-date.
3. If you're unsure about any question or field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
4. Review each section carefully to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction**: This page is a brief overview of the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
* **Property Details**: Please provide details about the property that was affected by the hurricane.
* **Hurricane Damage Assessment Survey**: This field is for you to describe the extent of the damage caused by the hurricane.
* **Contact Person**: Please provide the name of the contact person for your property or organization.
* **Contact Number**: Please provide the contact number of the contact person.
* **Email**: Please provide the email of the contact person.
* **Address**: Please provide the address of the property.
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about the situation.
* **Additional Information (if needed)**: Please use this field to provide any further details or comments that you think are relevant to the assessment.
