# Agency Ownership Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agency Ownership Assessment form is designed to collect detailed information about the ownership structure of an agency, including the breakdown of ownership percentages, primary owner, and other relevant details. This form is an essential part of the due diligence process for financial institutions to assess the agency's risk profile and ensure accurate reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2.  Provide accurate and complete information for each field as per the hints provided.
3.  Double-check your answers to ensure they are correct and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): This field requires the legal registered name of the agency.
*   **Ownership Structure** (`ownership_structure`, text, required): Describe the breakdown of ownership percentages of the agency.
*   **Primary Owner Name** (`primary_owner`, text, required): Enter the name of the majority stakeholder or primary owner of the agency.
*   **Ownership Percentages** (`ownership_percentages`, text, required): Provide a breakdown of ownership percentages by owner.
*   **Is there a Controlling Interest?** (`controlling_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate if one party has control over the agency, answering either 'True' or 'False'.
*   **Recent Changes in Ownership** (`changes_in_ownership`, text, optional): If there have been any recent changes in ownership, describe them here.
*   **Legal Documents on File** (`legal_documents_on_file`, text, optional): List any relevant legal articles or agreements that are available on file.
*   **Verification Contact Person** (`verification_contact`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and information for ownership verification.
*   **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details about the agency's ownership structure.

**Tips:**

*   Double-check the accuracy of your answers before submission.
*   Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
*   If unsure about any field, seek clarification or support from relevant authorities.
