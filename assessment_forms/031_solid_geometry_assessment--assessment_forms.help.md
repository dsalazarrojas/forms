# Solid Geometry Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's understanding of solid geometry concepts. It is intended to be completed by students who are being assessed on their knowledge of 3D shapes, coordinate systems, graphs and charts, projections, and vectors and rotations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address, which will be used to store your assessment results.
3. Select a date and time for submission.
4. Choose one of the following geometry concepts:
	* 3D Shape Classification
	* 3D Coordinate Systems
	* 3D Graphs and Charts
	* 3D Projection
	* 3D Vector and Rotation
5. In the "Tutor's Name" field, enter your name if you are the tutor who will be assessing the student's work.
6. In the "Assessment Comments" field, provide any feedback or comments about the student's responses.
7. Enter the student's ID in the "Student ID" field.
8. Record the submission time in the "Submission Time" field.
9. For questions 1-4, select the correct options for each question.
10. Enter your answers to questions 1-4 in the "Student Answer 1", "Student Answer 2", "Student Answer 3", and "Student Answer 4" fields.
11. Enter your answers to questions 1-4 in the "Tutor's Answer 1" and "Tutor's Answer 2" fields.
