# Computer Systems Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess an individual's knowledge of computer systems and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring that all required fields are completed before submitting.
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account your level of experience and knowledge in each area.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best represents your level of expertise.
4. For open-ended questions, provide clear and concise answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment ID** (`assessment_id`, text, required): A unique identifier for the assessment.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): The date of the assessment.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): The full name of the candidate.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, email, required): The email address of the candidate.
* **Current Role** (`current_role`, text, required): The candidate's current job title.
* **Years of IT Experience** (`years_experience`, select_one, required): Rate your level of experience in IT. Options: Less than 1 Year, 1 to 3 Years, 3 to 5 Years, 5 to 10 Years, More than 10 Years.
* **Operating Systems Knowledge** (`operating_systems_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your knowledge of operating systems. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Windows Proficiency** (`windows_proficiency`, select_one, required): Rate your level of proficiency with Windows. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Linux Proficiency** (`linux_proficiency`, select_one, required): Rate your level of proficiency with Linux. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **macOS Proficiency** (`macos_proficiency`, select_one, required): Rate your level of proficiency with macOS. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Networking Knowledge** (`networking_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your level of knowledge in networking. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Security Knowledge** (`security_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your level of knowledge in security. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Database Knowledge** (`database_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your level of knowledge in database management. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Cloud Platforms Knowledge** (`cloud_platforms_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your level of knowledge in cloud platforms. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Scripting Knowledge** (`scripting_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your level of scripting skills. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Hardware Troubleshooting** (`hardware_troubleshooting`, select_one, required): Rate your level of hardware troubleshooting skills. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Software Installation** (`software_installation`, select_one, required): Rate your level of software installation skills. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Virtualization Knowledge** (`virtualization_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your level of virtualization knowledge. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Backup and Recovery Knowledge** (`backup_recovery_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate your level of knowledge in backup and recovery. Options: 5 - Expert, 4 - Advanced, 3 - Intermediate, 2 - Basic, 1 - No Knowledge.
* **Certifications Held** (`certifications_held`, select_multiple, required): Select any certifications you hold. Options: CompTIA A Plus, CompTIA Network Plus, CompTIA Security Plus, Microsoft Certified, Cisco CCNA, AWS Certified, Linux Plus, None.
* **Total Score** (`total_score`, number, required): The total assessment score.
* **Proficiency Level** (`proficiency_level`, select_one, required): The overall proficiency level. Options: Expert, Advanced, Intermediate, Basic, Novice.
* **Recommended Training** (`recommended_training`, text, optional): Suggested training areas for improvement.
* **Assessor Comments** (`assessor_comments`, text, optional): Any additional comments from the assessor.

## Tips

* Ensure you answer honestly and accurately to the best of your abilities.
* Take your time to complete the form, as it may be lengthy.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* The form is designed to be completed in a fair and objective manner.
