# Urban Land Use Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the land use of a specific parcel of land. It helps to categorize and assess the land's usage, size, and status. The information collected in this form will be used to support the urban development and planning processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Parcel Details** page with any relevant information about the parcel of land.
2. Select the **Land Use** of the parcel from the options provided.
3. Choose the **Zoning Status** of the parcel by selecting multiple options from the list.
4. If applicable, enter the **Parcel Size** of the land.
5. Provide any **Parcel Observation** comments or notes about the parcel.
6. Enter the **Contact Name** of the person who submitted this form.
7. Enter the **Contact Email** of the person who submitted this form.
8. Enter the **Contact Phone** number of the person who submitted this form.
9. Enter the **Contact Address** of the person who submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parcel Details**: Enter the name and any other relevant information about the parcel, such as its location and size.
* **Land Use**: Select the type of land use (e.g. Urban, Rural, Industrial, Commercial, Residential).
* **Zoning Status**: Choose one or more of the zoning status options (e.g. Approved, Pending, Denied).
* **Parcel Size**: Enter the size of the parcel in numeric format (e.g. 10, 20, etc.).
* **Parcel Observation**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the parcel.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted this form.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the person who submitted this form.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who submitted this form.
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the person who submitted this form.
