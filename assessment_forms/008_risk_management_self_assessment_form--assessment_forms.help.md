# Risk Management Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Management Self Assessment Form is designed to help you identify and assess potential risks associated with your work, projects, or activities. This form will guide you through a series of questions and prompts to collect relevant information and help you understand the likelihood and potential impact of each risk.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have a clear understanding of the questions and prompts.
2. For each page, carefully read the prompt and provide the required information.
3. If a field is marked as "required," ensure you fill it out completely.
4. Use the provided options for select questions, and choose the one that best represents your response.
5. Provide clear and concise answers to open-ended questions.
6. If you have any further comments or suggestions, use the "comments" field to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page: <text input>** (id: 1)
This page is where you will introduce your risk assessment and provide any relevant background information.
* **Risk Level: <select one>** (id: 2)
Choose the level of risk associated with your situation. This can be:
	+ Low: A minor concern with minimal potential impact.
	+ Medium: A moderate concern with potential impact.
	+ High: A significant concern with high potential impact.
* **Assessment Form: <text input>** (id: 3)
This is a space for you to document your risk assessment in more detail. Please use this field to outline the risk, its causes, and its potential consequences.
* **Comments: <text input>** (id: 4)
Use this field to add any additional comments or observations related to your risk assessment.
* **Risk Management Comments: <text input>** (id: 5)
Here, you can add further comments or suggestions on how to mitigate or manage the risk.
