# VOC Control Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VOC Control Assessment Form is designed to collect information about the presence and status of VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) controls on a site. This form will help assess the compliance status of VOC controls, site visit details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant options for each field and enter any required information. The form is divided into the following sections:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **VOC Types** (`voc_types`): Select all VOC types that are present at the site, such as solvents, cleaners, and other hazardous substances.
* **Assessors** (`assessors`): Identify all individuals or teams that have assessed the VOC controls at the site.
* **VOC Sources** (`voc_sources`): Select all sources of VOC emissions at the site, such as emissions from equipment, storage tanks, and other sources.
* **Controls** (`controls`): Select all types of VOC controls present at the site, such as scrubbers, wet scrubbers, and other types of controls.
* **Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`): Select the current compliance status of the VOC controls, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): Enter the date of the last VOC control assessment.
* **Note** (`note`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the site's VOC controls.
* **Facility Manager** (`facility_manager`): Identify the facility manager responsible for the site's VOC controls.
* **Consultant** (`consultant`): Identify any consultants involved in the VOC control assessment.
* **EH&S Team Members** (`ehs_team_members`): Select all EH&S team members who were present during the site visit.
* **Site Visit Details** (`site_visit_details`): Enter any relevant details about the site visit, such as observations or notes.
* **Site Visit Date** (`site_visit_date`): Enter the date of the site visit.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of the site visit.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the individual responsible for the site's VOC controls.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact email of the individual responsible for the site's VOC controls.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter the name of the individual responsible for the site's VOC controls.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the facility being assessed.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`): Enter the type of facility being assessed.
* **Site Visit Frequency** (`site_visit_frequency`): Select how often the site is visited for VOC control assessments.
* **Compliance Frequency** (`compliance_frequency`): Select how often the compliance status of the VOC controls is checked.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`): Enter the type of assessment performed (e.g., routine, special, or follow-up).
* **Assessment Frequency** (`assessment_frequency`): Enter how often the VOC control assessment is performed.
* **Site Visit Comments** (`site_visit_comments`): Enter any comments or observations about the site visit.
* **Site Visit Comments 2** (`site_visit_comments_2`): Enter any additional comments or observations about the site visit.
* **Site Visit Comments 3** (`site_visit_comments_3`): Enter any further comments or observations about the site visit.

Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
