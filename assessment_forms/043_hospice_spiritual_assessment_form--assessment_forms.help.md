# hospice_spiritual_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess the spiritual needs and concerns of patients who are near the end of life. The purpose of this form is to gather information about a patient's spiritual values, beliefs, and practices that can inform their care and support their well-being during this critical period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability. If you are unsure or unable to answer a question, please explain your reasons.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each question.
3. If necessary, provide additional information in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_info** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): This section asks for general information about the patient, including their medical team, primary caregivers, and relevant medical conditions.
* **patient_spirituality** (`patient_spirituality`, text, required: false): Please describe the patient's spiritual beliefs and practices, including any spiritual or religious affiliations.
* **spirituality_level** (`spirituality_level`, number, required: false): On a scale of 1-10, rate the patient's level of spirituality, with 1 being not spiritual at all and 10 being very spiritual.
* **spiritual_values** (`spiritual_values`, number, required: false): Please list the patient's spiritual values and priorities.
* **spiritual_concerns** (`spiritual_concerns`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's spiritual concerns and fears.
* **spiritual_strengths** (`spiritual_strengths`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's spiritual strengths and abilities.
* **spiritual_needs** (`spiritual_needs`, text, required: false): Identify the patient's spiritual needs and how they can be met.
* **spiritual_principles** (`spiritual_principles`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's spiritual principles and practices.
* **spiritual_practice** (`spiritual_practice`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's spiritual practices and activities.
* **spiritual_rituals** (`spiritual_rituals`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's spiritual rituals and practices.
* **spiritual_friends** (`spiritual_friends`, text, required: false): Identify the patient's spiritual friends and support system.
* **spiritual_support** (`spiritual_support`, text, required: false): Describe how the patient would like to be spiritually supported.
* **spiritual_clergy** (`spiritual_clergy`, text, required: false): Identify the patient's spiritual leader or cleric.

* **spiritual_reflection** (`spiritual_reflection`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's spiritual reflection and introspection practices.

* **spiritual_other** (`spiritual_other`, text, required: false): Describe any other spiritual practices or activities that are important to the patient.

## Tips

* Please answer these questions in a way that reflects the patient's own voice and experiences.
* If you are unsure or unable to answer a question, please explain your reasons.
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each question.
* This form is designed to be completed by a healthcare professional, but it is also a tool for the patient to express their own spiritual needs and concerns.
* Please review the patient's answers carefully and provide support and guidance as needed.
