# Assessment Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Assessment Booking Form is designed to collect information from applicants for the purpose of booking an assessment. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of the assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your personal details in the first section, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2.  In the second section, select the type of assessment you require (Academic, Skills, Health, Career, Aptitude Test, or Other).
3.  Indicate the level of assessment you prefer (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional).
4.  Choose your preferred assessment date and time from the available options.
5.  Select the location where you'd like to take the assessment (On-site, Online, At Testing Center, or No Preference).
6.  If you have any special accommodations or requirements, provide details in the "Special Accommodations Needed" field.
7.  Confirm that you've reviewed the assessment documents.
8.  Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Check, or Online Payment).
9.  Enter the email address to which you'd like to receive confirmation details.
10.  Finally, provide any additional notes or questions in the "Additional Notes or Questions" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your primary email contact.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Residential Address** (6, text, required): Enter your full street address.
* **City** (7, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (8, text, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (9, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Type of Assessment** (10, select_one, required): Select the type of assessment you require.
* **Assessment Level** (11, select_one, required): Choose the level of assessment you prefer.
* **First Preferred Assessment Date** (12, date, required): Enter your first choice date for the assessment.
* **Second Preferred Assessment Date** (13, date, optional): Enter a backup date option for the assessment. (Leave blank if not needed).
* **Preferred Assessment Time** (14, select_one, required): Select the time slot for your assessment.
* **Assessment Location Preference** (15, select_one, required): Choose the location for your assessment.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (16, text, optional): Enter any accessibility needs or special requirements.
* **I have reviewed the assessment documents** (17, select_one, required): Confirm that you've reviewed the assessment documents.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (18, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Send confirmation to this email** (19, email, required): Enter the email address to receive confirmation details.
* **Additional Notes or Questions** (20, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or questions (if applicable).

## Tips

*  Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
*  If you have any special requirements or accommodations, please mention them in the "Special Accommodations Needed" field.
*  Review the assessment documents carefully before confirming that you have done so.
*  Choose your preferred assessment time and location carefully, as these may impact the assessment process.
