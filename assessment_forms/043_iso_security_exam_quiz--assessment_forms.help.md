# iso_security_exam_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge users' knowledge and understanding of security controls and policies related to the ISO 27001 standard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Please ensure you are the intended user of this form and that your completion is necessary.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required.
3. Provide thoughtful and accurate information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input (Text)
• **User Input** (`user_input`, Text, Required): This field is for users to enter any comments or answers related to their understanding of security controls and policies.

### ISO 27001 Knowledge (Text)
• **ISO 27001 Knowledge** (`iso_27001_knowledge`, Text, Required): In this field, please express your understanding of the ISO 27001 standard. This is an essential part of the assessment.

### Iso Policies (Note)
• **Iso Policies** (`isopolicies`, Note, Required): This field is for taking notes on policies related to security within your organization.

### User Comments (Note)
• **User Comments** (`user_comments`, Note, Required): Please use this field to add any comments or thoughts on the submitted form.

### Security Controls (Text)
• **Security Controls** (`security_controls`, Text, Required): In this field, report on the security controls you have in place within your organization.

### Date Submitted (Date)
• **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, Date, Required): This field is automatically populated when you submit the form, but you can also enter the date manually if needed.

### Submitter (Text)
• **Submitter** (`submitter`, Text, Required): Please enter your name or identity to help us track who submitted this form.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Ensure that each field is filled out according to its type and requirements.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the form creator or administrator for clarification.
- Remember that this form is an assessment tool and should be completed seriously.
