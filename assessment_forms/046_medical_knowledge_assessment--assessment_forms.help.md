# Medical Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a medical professional's current level of medical knowledge, highest level of medical degree earned, birth year, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
* For questions with multiple options, select all the relevant options.
* For date fields, use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* For text fields, enter your current information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your current level of medical knowledge?** (Medical Knowledge, text, required)
    This question is to assess your current level of medical knowledge.
* **Medical Degree Earned** (Medical Degree, text, required)
    This question is to determine your highest level of medical degree earned.
* **Year of Birth** (Year of Birth, date, required)
    This question is to determine your birth year.
* **Medical License Expiration Date** (Medical License Expiration Date, date, required)
    This question is to determine your medical license expiration date.
* **Section A: Anatomy and Physiology**
    This section is to assess your knowledge of anatomy and physiology.
    * **Circulatory System Function** (Select Multiple, required)
        What are the main functions of the circulatory system? Choose all that apply.
        * Heart
        * Lungs
        * Blood Vessels
    * **Cellular Respiration Process** (Select One, required)
        What is the process of cellular respiration?
        * Aerobic
        * Anaerobic
        * Fermentation
    * **Respiratory System Organs** (Select Multiple, required)
        What are the main organs in the respiratory system? Choose all that apply.
        * Lungs
        * Trachea
        * Esophagus
    * **Photosynthesis Process** (Select Multiple, required)
        What is the process of photosynthesis?
        * Light dependent
        * Light independent
        * Both
* **Section B: Medical Knowledge**
    This section is to assess your medical knowledge of various medical conditions and their treatments.
    * **Hypoglycemia Definition** (Select One, required)
        What is the definition of hypoglycemia?
        * Low blood sugar
        * High blood sugar
        * Both
    * **Type 2 Diabetes Treatment** (Select Multiple, required)
        What is the treatment for type 2 diabetes? Choose all that apply.
        * Insulin
        * Exercise
        * Diet
    * **Hypertension Definition** (Select One, required)
        What is the definition of hypertension?
        * High blood pressure
        * Low blood pressure
        * Normal blood pressure
    * **Pneumonia Treatment** (Select Multiple, required)
        What is the treatment for pneumonia? Choose all that apply.
        * Antibiotics
        * Rest
        * Fluids
* **Section C: Medical Ethics**
    This section is to assess your knowledge of medical ethics.
    * **Informed Consent Definition** (Select One, required)
        What is the definition of informed consent?
        * Permission given
        * Permission not given
        * Both
    * **Confidentiality Definition** (Select One, required)
        What is the definition of confidentiality?
        * Patient information kept secret
        * Patient information shared
        * Both
    * **Medical Malpractice Definition** (Select One, required)
        What is the definition of medical malpractice?
        * Unprofessional behavior
        * Professional behavior
        * Both
    * **Patient Autonomy Definition** (Select One, required)
        What is the definition of patient autonomy?
        * Patient decision making power
        * Doctor decision making power
        * Both
* **Medical License** (Medical License, text, required)
    This question is to determine your medical license type.
* **Medical Residency** (Medical Residency, text, required)
    This question is to determine your medical residency type.
* **Medical License Number** (Medical License Number, text, required)
    This question is to determine your medical license number.
* **Medical License Status** (Medical License Status, Select One, required)
    What is your current medical license status?
    * Active
    * Expired
    * Suspended
* **Medical Degree** (Medical Degree, text, required)
    This question is to determine your highest level of medical degree earned.

## Tips
* Please double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure of any question, please ask for clarification.
* If you need help with any section, please contact your supervisor or support staff.
