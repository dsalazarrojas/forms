# Training Pre Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Training Pre Assessment Survey" form is designed to gather information about the trainee's self-evaluation and training needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the multiple-choice questions in the format provided.
2. Enter your text responses in the provided text fields.
3. Date and time stamps will be filled in automatically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Training Pre-Assessment**: 
  Select whether you have completed the training course ("Yes" or "No").
- **Training Level**: 
  Enter your current level of training, as an integer.
- **What level of training do you have?**: 
  Enter a brief text describing your current level of training.
- **Self-Evaluation**: 
  Enter a note about your self-evaluation.
- **How would you evaluate your current level of training?**: 
  Select the options that best describe your current level of training.
- **Areas Of Growth**: 
  Select the areas where you would like to grow.
- **What areas would you like to grow in?**: 
  Enter a text response about areas where you would like to grow.
- **Action Plans**: 
  Select whether you have action plans to improve your skills ("Yes" or "No").
- **What plans do you have to improve your skills?**: 
  Enter a text response about your action plans to improve your skills.
- **Support System**: 
  Select whether you would need a support system to improve your skills ("Yes" or "No").
- **What support system would you need to improve your skills?**: 
  Enter a text response about the support system you would need.
- **Training Needs**: 
  Select whether you feel you need additional training ("Yes" or "No").
- **Do you feel you need additional training?**: 
  Enter a text response about your training needs.
- **Training Recommendation**: 
  Select whether you would recommend additional training ("Yes" or "No").
- **Who would you recommend for training?**: 
  Enter a text response about who you would recommend for training.
- **Additional Comments**: 
  Enter any additional comments or concerns.
- **Please enter any additional comments -**: 
  Enter a text response about additional comments or concerns.
- **Date Completed**: 
  Will be filled in automatically.
- **Time Completed**: 
  Will be filled in automatically.
- **Completed By**: 
  Enter your email address.
- **Who completed this survey?**: 
  Enter a text response about who completed the survey.
- **Completed Date**: 
  Will be filled in automatically.
- **Completed By Date**: 
  Will be filled in automatically.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions as accurately and honestly as possible.
* Be as concise and clear as possible in your text responses.
* This form should take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
