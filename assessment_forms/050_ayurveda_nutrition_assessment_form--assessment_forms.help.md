# Ayurveda Nutrition Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to guide the user through a comprehensive Ayurvedic nutrition assessment. It is designed to collect information about the user's profile, physical metrics, lifestyle habits, and health concerns, which will be used by practitioners to create a personalized Ayurvedic diet plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Patient Profile section, providing your personal details such as name, date of birth, and email address.
2. Enter your current height and weight measurements in the Physical Metrics section.
3. In the Health Concern section, describe your primary health concern or reason for seeking a nutrition assessment.
4. In the Lifestyle and Nutrition Habits section, describe your typical eating habits, sleep quality, and exercise habits.
5. In the Clinical and Assessment Notes section, note any relevant medical conditions, medications, or supplements you are currently taking.
6. Leave the Recommended Ayurvedic Diet Plan section blank for the practitioner to fill out with personalized advice.
7. Sign the Practitioner Digital Signature section with your digital signature when the form is complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Full Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication with the practitioner.
* **Height**: Enter your current height in centimeters (cm).
* **Current Weight**: Enter your current weight in kilograms (kg).
* **Primary Health Concern**: Describe your primary health concern or reason for seeking a nutrition assessment.
* **Dominant Dosha (Prakriti)**: Select your dominant dosha type from the options provided (e.g., Vata, Pitta, Kapha, Bi-Doshic, etc.).
* **Quality of Digestion (Agni)**: Choose the most accurate description of your digestion quality from the options provided (e.g., Tikshnagni, Vishamagni, Mandagni, Samagni).
* **Typical Breakfast**: Describe what you typically eat for breakfast and at what time of day.
* **Typical Lunch**: Describe what you typically eat for lunch and at what time of day.
* **Typical Dinner**: Describe what you typically eat for dinner and at what time of day.
* **Snacks and Frequent Cravings**: Describe any snacks you reach for between meals and any frequent cravings you may have.
* **Average Energy Level**: Rate your average energy level from 1 (Low) to 5 (High).
* **Sleep Quality Rating**: Rate your sleep quality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **Exercise and Activity Habits**: Select your current exercise and activity level from the options provided (e.g., Sedentary, Moderate, Active).
* **Current Medications or Supplements**: List any medications or supplements you are currently taking.
* **Recommended Ayurvedic Diet Plan**: This section will be filled out by the practitioner with personalized dietary advice.
* **Practitioner Digital Signature**: Sign with your digital signature when the form is complete.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, discuss with the practitioner.
* Complete the form carefully and thoroughly to ensure the practitioner has the necessary information to create a personalized Ayurvedic diet plan.
