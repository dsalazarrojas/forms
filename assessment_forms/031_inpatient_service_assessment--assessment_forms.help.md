# Inpatient Service Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inpatient Service Assessment form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of care provided to patients in an inpatient setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select your response for each question using the provided options or type in your comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Experience**: How would you rate the overall experience of your inpatient stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Physician Performance**: How would you rate the performance of your physician during your inpatient stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Team Performance**: How would you rate the performance of the healthcare team that cared for you during your stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Nurse Performance**: How would you rate the performance of the nurses who cared for you during your stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Communication with Physician**: How would you rate the communication with your physician during your stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Communication with Team**: How would you rate the communication with your healthcare team during your stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Teamwork**: How would you rate the teamwork among the healthcare staff during your stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Patient Safety**: How safe do you feel you were during your stay? Choose one of the options below:
	+ Very Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns about your inpatient stay, please feel free to type them in here.
* **Overall Assessment**: Please provide a brief summary of your overall inpatient experience.
