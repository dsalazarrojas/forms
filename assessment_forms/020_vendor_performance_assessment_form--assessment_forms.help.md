# Vendor Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Performance Assessment Form is used to collect feedback and ratings from assessors on various aspects of a vendor's performance. It helps to evaluate their strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and make informed decisions about future collaborations or partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
* Ensure you have a good understanding of the vendor's work and performance.
* Select the relevant pages and fields to fill out, based on the specific areas you want to assess.
* For the `vendor_rating` field, select the ratings that apply to your assessment.
* Enter your comments and notes about the vendor's performance in the `vendor_comments` and `vendor_notes` fields.
* If applicable, provide a numerical score for the vendor's performance using the `vendor_score` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Rating** (`assessment_form_vendor_rating`): Select one or more of the following ratings: Good, Fair. This field allows you to evaluate the vendor's performance based on their overall quality of work.
* **Vendor Comments** (`assessment_form_vendor_comments`): Please provide any comments or feedback you have about the vendor's performance. This can include specific examples or anecdotes about their strengths and weaknesses.
* **Vendor Notes** (`assessment_form_vendor_notes`): Add any additional notes or information you want to include about the vendor's performance. This can be helpful for future reference or for passing along to others.
* **Vendor Score** (`assessment_form_vendor_score`): If applicable, provide a numerical score between 1 and 10 to indicate the vendor's performance. This score can be a general assessment of their overall performance.

## Tips
* Be specific and concrete when providing feedback and ratings.
* Focus on the vendor's performance, rather than personal opinions or biases.
* Use this form to identify areas for improvement and opportunities for growth.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the relevant team leader or project manager for guidance.
