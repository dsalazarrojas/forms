## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please read the introduction carefully and answer the questions honestly to the best of your knowledge.
2.  Choose the correct answers for each question based on your current health status.
3.  Make sure to answer all required questions, indicated by an asterisk \*.
4.  Enter your contact phone number and email address if you're willing to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **-- COVID-19 Guest Self Assessment --** (`assessment_intro`, note, not required): This is the introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and importance. Please read it carefully before answering the questions.
* **Full name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
* **Appointment date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your scheduled appointment.
* **Service scheduled** (`appointment_service`, text, required): Describe the service you have scheduled at our salon.
* **Do you currently have a fever or elevated body temperature?** (`fever_symptoms`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you currently have a fever or elevated body temperature, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Do you have a new or worsening cough?** (`cough_symptoms`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have a new or worsening cough, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Do you have difficulty breathing or shortness of breath?** (`breathing_difficulty`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have difficulty breathing or shortness of breath, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Have you experienced loss of taste or smell?** (`loss_taste_smell`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have experienced loss of taste or smell, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Do you have fatigue, chills, or body aches?** (`fatigue_body_aches`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have fatigue, chills, or body aches, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Do you have a sore throat?** (`sore_throat`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have a sore throat, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Do you have a headache?** (`headache`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have a headache, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Have you been in close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days?** (`covid_exposure`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have been in close contact with someone who tested positive for COVID-19, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days?** (`covid_tested_positive`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you have tested positive for COVID-19, 'False' if you don't, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Are you currently under quarantine or isolation?** (`quarantine_status`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you're currently under quarantine or isolation, 'False' if you're not, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **Vaccination status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, not required): Choose your current vaccination status: 'Fully vaccinated', 'Partially vaccinated', 'Not vaccinated', or 'Prefer not to answer'.
* **Are you willing to wear a mask during your appointment?** (`mask_requirement`, select_one, required): Answer 'True' if you're willing to wear a mask, 'False' if you're not, or 'Unsure' if you're unsure.
* **I certify that I have answered the above questions honestly and to the best of my knowledge** (`health_declaration`, select_one, required): Answer 'I agree' if you have answered the questions honestly, 'I do not agree' if you haven't.
* **Contact phone number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact email address** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter your contact email address if you're willing to provide it.
