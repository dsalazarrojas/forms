# Alcohol Use Self Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool designed to help individuals evaluate their alcohol use and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question honestly, taking your time to reflect on your behaviors and feelings related to alcohol consumption.
2. Select the most accurate response for each question based on your experiences in the past year.
3. Be as specific as possible when answering questions, especially when providing numerical values (e.g., average drinks per week).
4. If you have any concerns or questions, feel free to use the "Additional Comments" field to provide further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment ID** (`assessment_id`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier for this assessment (e.g., your name or initials).
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are taking this assessment.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Provide your current age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Relationship Status** (`relationship_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current relationship status.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Have you felt you should cut down on drinking?** (`question_1`, select_one, required): Choose how often you have felt the need to cut down on drinking in the past year.
* **Have people annoyed you by criticizing drinking?** (`question_2`, select_one, required): Select how often people have criticized your drinking in the past year.
* **Have you felt bad or guilty about drinking?** (`question_3`, select_one, required): Choose how often you have felt bad or guilty about drinking in the past year.
* **Have you had a drink first thing in the morning to steady nerves?** (`question_4`, select_one, required): Select how often you have had an eye-opener drink in the past year.
* **Average Drinks Per Week** (`drinks_per_week`, select_one, required): Select your typical weekly consumption of drinks.
* **Maximum Drinks in One Day** (`max_drinks_single_day`, select_one, required): Choose the highest number of drinks you have had in a single day in the past month.
* **Drinking Frequency** (`drinking_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you drink.
* **Unable to Stop Once Started** (`inability_to_stop`, select_one, required): Choose how often you have struggled to stop drinking once started.
* **Failed to Meet Expectations** (`failed_expectations`, select_one, required): Select how often you have failed to meet expectations due to drinking in the past year.
* **Need to Have a Morning Drink** (`morning_drinking`, select_one, required): Select how often you have needed a morning drink to get going in the past year.
* **Felt Guilt After Drinking** (`guilt_after_drinking`, select_one, required): Choose how often you have felt guilty after drinking in the past year.
* **Memory Blackouts** (`memory_blackouts`, select_one, required): Select how often you have experienced memory blackouts due to drinking.
* **Injured While/After Drinking** (`injury_while_drinking`, select_one, required): Select if you have ever been injured while or after drinking in the past year.
* **Concerned by Others** (`concerned_by_others`, select_one, optional): Choose if anyone has expressed concern about your drinking habits.
* **Withdrawal Symptoms** (`withdrawal_symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply from the list of possible withdrawal symptoms.
* **Tolerance Level** (`tolerance_level`, select_one, required): Choose how your tolerance to alcohol has increased.
* **Impact on Relationships** (`impact_on_relationships`, select_one, required): Select the impact of your drinking habits on your relationships.
* **Impact on Work or School** (`impact_on_work`, select_one, required): Choose the impact of your drinking habits on your work or school performance.
* **Readiness to Change** (`readiness_to_change`, select_one, required): Choose how ready you are to make changes to your drinking habits.
* **Support System Available** (`support_system`, select_one, optional): Choose the level of support you have available to make changes.
* **Previous Attempts to Cut Down** (`previous_attempts`, select_one, optional): Select how many times you have previously tried to cut down on drinking.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any further comments or concerns you have.

**Tips**

* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as this form is intended to provide a comprehensive evaluation of your drinking habits.
* If you are unsure about any question, it's okay to ask for clarification or guidance.
* Remember that this form is a self-assessment tool, and the information collected will help you identify areas for improvement.
