# Corporate Team Communication Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the effectiveness of corporate team communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form with accurate information to get a clear picture of team communication.
2. Answer each question based on the frequency or effectiveness of team communication.
3. Be consistent with the unit of time (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly) when answering questions that ask for frequency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are assessing.
* **Department** (Department, text, optional): Enter the department name, if applicable.
* **Supervisor** (Supervisor, text, required): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Team Members** (Team Members, select multiple, required): Check all that apply if team members have good communication with each other.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (Communication Effectiveness, select one, required): Check if communication is effective, ineffective, or neutral.
* **Frequency of Meetings** (Frequency of Meetings, number, optional): Enter the frequency of meetings (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
* **Meeting Length** (Meeting Length, number, optional): Enter the length of each meeting (e.g., 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour).
* **Meeting Frequency** (Meeting Frequency, select one, required): Check if meetings are held regularly, occasionally, or not at all.
* **Communication Channels** (Communication Channels, select multiple, required): Check all that apply for communication channels used (e.g., email, phone, in-person).
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments about team communication.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address for feedback or communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Comments** (Comments, email, optional): Add any additional comments or questions.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, optional): Add any further comments about team communication.
* **Company Size** (Company Size, select one, optional): Check if the company has 1-10, 11-50, 51-100, or 101+ employees.
* **Company Type** (Company Type, select one, optional): Check if the company is private, public, or non-profit.
* **Industry** (Industry, select one, optional): Check if the company is in the tech, finance, or other industry.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments about team communication.
* **Team Size** (Team Size, number, optional): Enter the number of team members.
* **Team Location** (Team Location, select one, optional): Check if the team is located in the same building, different building, or remote.
* **Manager Location** (Manager Location, select one, optional): Check if the manager is located in the same building, different building, or remote.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Add any additional comments about team communication.
* **Submission Date** (Submission Date, date, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, optional): Add any final comments or questions.
