# Antigone Literature Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge of the classic Greek tragedy Antigone, a play by Sophocles. The quiz tests your understanding of the plot, characters, and themes of the play. It's a self-assessment tool to gauge your comprehension and help you better understand this ancient Greek literature.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the provided questions carefully and select the correct answer from the options provided.
2. Fill in the required fields: Student Name, Course Name, Instructor Name, Quiz Date, and your answers to each question.
3. You may also choose to provide your confidence level in your answers and add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your school record.
* **Student ID** (2, text, optional): If applicable, enter your ID number.
* **Course Name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the class or course.
* **Instructor Name** (4, text, required): Enter the name of your teacher or professor.
* **Quiz Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the quiz.
* **Who is the author of Antigone** (6, select_one, required): Choose from: Sophocles, Euripides, Aeschylus, Aristophanes.
* **In which ancient city is Antigone set** (7, select_one, required): Choose from: Athens, Thebes, Sparta, Corinth.
* **Who is Antigone's father** (8, select_one, required): Choose from: Creon, Oedipus, Polynices, Haemon.
* **What does Antigone want to do that is forbidden** (9, select_one, required): Choose from: Marry Creon, Bury her brother Polynices, Leave Thebes, Kill Haemon.
* **Who is the ruler of Thebes at the start of the play** (10, select_one, required): Choose from: Oedipus, Polynices, Creon, Eteocles.
* **What is Antigone's relationship to Ismene** (11, select_one, required): Choose from: Mother and daughter, Sisters, Cousins, Friends.
* **Who is Haemon** (12, select_one, required): Choose from: Antigone's brother, Creon's son, The prophet, The guard.
* **What punishment does Creon decree for burying Polynices** (13, select_one, required): Choose from: Exile, Death by stoning, Imprisonment, Fine.
* **Who tries to persuade Creon to change his mind** (14, select_one, required): Choose from: Ismene only, Haemon and Tiresias, The Chorus only, No one.
* **What happens to Antigone at the end of the play** (15, select_one, required): Choose from: She escapes, She is pardoned, She hangs herself, She marries Haemon.
* **Who is Tiresias** (16, select_one, required): Choose from: A soldier, A blind prophet, A priest, A messenger.
* **What theme is central to Antigone** (17, select_one, required): Choose from: Love and romance, Duty vs personal conscience, War and peace, Wealth and poverty.
* **What happens to Haemon** (18, select_one, required): Choose from: He exiles himself, He kills himself, He kills Creon, He marries Ismene.
* **What happens to Creon's wife Eurydice** (19, select_one, required): Choose from: She exiles herself, She kills herself, She forgives Creon, She becomes ruler.
* **What literary device is used extensively in Antigone** (20, select_one, required): Choose from: Metaphor, Dramatic irony, Simile, Alliteration.
* **Essay Question - Discuss the conflict between divine law and human law in Antigone** (21, text, required): Write a short essay response.
* **Confidence Level** (22, select_one, required): Choose from: Very confident, Confident, Somewhat confident, Not confident.
* **Time Spent on Quiz** (23, number, optional): Enter approximate minutes.
* **Additional Comments** (24, text, optional): Enter any thoughts on the quiz.
