# Hiring Personality Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for a job opening. The purpose of this form is to gather detailed information about the candidate, including their personality, work experience, and job preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and honest information about yourself.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering the personality questions.
4. Attach any relevant images or videos to support your answers.
5. Proofread your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Candidate First Name)** (`candidate_first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Candidate Last Name)** (`candidate_last_name`, `text`, required/true): Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email Address)** (`email_address`, `email`, required/true): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number (Phone)** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/true): Enter your valid phone number.
* **Job Title (Job Title)** (`job_title`, `text`, required/true): Enter the job title you are applying for.
* **Work Experience (Work Experience)** (`work_experience`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select the length of your work experience from 1-2 years, 2-5 years, 5-10 years, or 10+ years.
* **Personality Question 1 (Personality Question 1)** (`personality_question_1`, `select_one`, required/true): Select a trait that best describes you: Agreeable, Ambitious, Confident, or Outgoing.
* **Personality Question 2 (Personality Question 2)** (`personality_question_2`, `select_one`, required/true): Select a trait that best describes you: Adaptable, Analytical, Assertive, or Diplomatic.
* **Personality Question 3 (Personality Question 3)** (`personality_question_3`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select up to two traits that best describe you: Creative, Diplomatic, Outgoing, or Pragmatic.
* **Personality Question 4 (Personality Question 4)** (`personality_question_4`, `select_one`, required/true): Select a trait that best describes you: Analytical, Assertive, Diplomatic, or Outgoing.
* **Personality Question 5 (Personality Question 5)** (`personality_question_5`, `select_multiple`, required/true): Select up to four traits that best describe you: Agreeable, Analytical, Assertive, or Diplomatic.
* **Job Description (Job Description)** (`job_description`, `note`, optional/true): Write a brief description of the job you are applying for.
* **Candidate Essay (Candidate Essay)** (`candidate_essay`, `note`, optional/true): Write a brief essay about yourself.
* **Manager Notes (Manager Notes)** (`manager_notes`, `note`, optional/true): Leave any notes for the hiring team.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/true): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Job Title ID (Job Title ID)** (`job_title_id`, `select_one`, optional/true): Select Yes or No if you have a job title ID.
* **Team Member (Team Member)** (`team_member`, `select_multiple`, optional/true): Select Yes or No if you have experience working in a team.
* **Candidate Image (Candidate Image)** (`candidate_image`, `text`, optional/true): Attach an image that best represents you.
* **Additional Files (Additional Files)** (`additional_files`, `text`, optional/true): Attach any additional files that support your application.
* **Candidate Video (Candidate Video)** (`candidate_video`, `text`, optional/true): Attach a video that best represents you.
* **Manager Video (Manager Video)** (`manager_video`, `text`, optional/true): Attach a video of your manager or colleague.
* **Job Video (Job Video)** (`job_video`, `text`, optional/true): Attach a video that best represents the job.
* **Job Posting URL (Job Posting URL)** (`job_posting_url`, `text`, optional/true): Attach the job posting URL.
* **Submission Date (Submission Date)** (`submission_date`, `date`, required/true): Enter the date and time of submission in the format MM/DD/YYYY 00:00.

## Tips
1. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Proofread your form before submitting it.
4. Attach any relevant images or videos to support your answers.
5. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the hiring team.
