# Lumbar Extension Rotation Test Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lumbar Extension Rotation Test Assessment form is used to collect information from healthcare professionals about the assessment of the patient's lumbar extension rotation. This form is essential for gathering data on patient demographics, pain intensity, and treatment options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter patient demographics such as name, date of birth, or any other relevant information.
2. **Lumbar Rotation Test** (`lumbar_rotation_test`, number, optional): Record the results of the lumbar rotation test, using a numerical value to represent the degree of rotation.
3. **Pain Location** (`pain_location`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the location of the patient's pain, selecting one or more options: Left, Right, or Bilateral.
4. **Pain Intensity** (`pain_intensity`, number, optional): Report the intensity of the patient's pain, using a numerical value to represent the level of discomfort.
5. **Pain Duration** (`pain_duration`, number, optional): Record the duration of the patient's pain, in hours or days.
6. **Treatment Options** (`treatment_options`, select_one, optional): Select one or more treatment options that the patient has undergone or would like to undergo, such as Rest, Massage, Physical therapy, Heat, or Cold.
7. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Briefly describe the patient's medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments.
8. **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
9. **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): Record any medications the patient is currently taking or has taken in the past.
10. **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Provide additional comments or notes about the patient's case.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide the necessary information for diagnosis and treatment planning.
- If any field is not applicable, please leave it blank or indicate "N/A" in the field.
- Please save the form after completing it, as any unsaved changes may be lost upon navigation away from the form.
