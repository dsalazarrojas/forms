# Patient Wait Time Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a patient's wait time experience at the healthcare facility. Specifically, it aims to assess the patient's satisfaction level regarding their waiting time and schedule their wait in minutes. This form will help to understand how efficiently the facility is managing patient flow and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "wait-time-experience" page.
2. Enter the actual waiting time in the "waiting_time_schedule" field in minutes.
3. Choose your satisfaction level regarding the waiting time in the "satisfaction_level" field.
4. Optionally, enter any additional comments or feedback about your waiting experience in the "waiting_experience" field.
5. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Wait Time Experience (wait-time-experience)**: Enter the actual waiting time you experienced at the healthcare facility in minutes. This field is not required.
* **Satisfaction Level (satisfaction-level)**: Choose one of the following options to express your satisfaction level regarding your waiting time:
	+ Very High
	+ High
	+ Neutral
	+ Low
	+ Very Low
* **Waiting Time Schedule (waiting-time-schedule)**: Enter the waiting time you scheduled in minutes. This field is not required.
* **Waiting Experience (waiting-experience)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your waiting experience. This field is not required and optional.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate waiting time in the "waiting_time_schedule" field for better data analysis.
- Choose the most accurate satisfaction level that reflects your actual experience.
- If you have any concerns or suggestions, feel free to provide them in the "waiting_experience" field.
