# Adulthood Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adulthood Development Assessment is a tool used to evaluate an individual's level of development in various aspects of adulthood, including social relationships, financial management, and career goals. This assessment is designed to help assessors and individuals identify strengths and areas for improvement in their personal and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with the information requested.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Fill in the text fields as needed.
4. Complete the assessment by answering all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Assessed Individual Name
* **Assessed Individual Name** (`assessed_individual_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person being assessed.
### Assessor Name
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the professional conducting the assessment.
### Assessment Date
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the assessment is being conducted.
### Age Group
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select the age range of the individual being assessed from the options provided: 
  * Early adulthood (18-25)
  * Young adulthood (26-35)
  * Middle adulthood (36-50)
  * Late middle adulthood (51-65)
  * Late adulthood (65+)
### Education Level
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select the highest level of education completed from the options provided: 
  * High school diploma
  * Some college
  * Bachelor's degree
  * Master's degree
  * Doctoral degree
  * Other
### Employment Status
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select the current employment situation of the individual from the options provided: 
  * Employed full-time
  * Employed part-time
  * Self-employed
  * Unemployed
  * Student
  * Retired
### Financial Management
* **Financial Management** (`financial_management`, select_one, required): Select the level of financial management ability from the options provided: 
  * Completely independent
  * Mostly independent
  * Needs occasional support
  * Needs frequent support
  * Dependent on others
### Household Management
* **Household Management** (`household_management`, select_one, required): Select the level of household management ability from the options provided: 
  * Completely independent
  * Mostly independent
  * Needs occasional support
  * Needs frequent support
  * Dependent on others
### Transportation Independence
* **Transportation Independence** (`transportation_independence`, select_one, required): Select the level of transportation management ability from the options provided: 
  * Completely independent
  * Mostly independent
  * Needs occasional support
  * Needs frequent support
  * Dependent on others
### Relationship Building
* **Relationship Building** (`relationship_building`, select_one, required): Select the level of relationship building ability from the options provided: 
  * Strong abilities
  * Good abilities
  * Developing abilities
  * Needs support
  * Significant challenges
### Communication Skills
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Select the level of communication skills from the options provided: 
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Adequate
  * Needs improvement
  * Poor
### Emotional Regulation
* **Emotional Regulation** (`emotional_regulation`, select_one, required): Select the level of emotional regulation from the options provided: 
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Adequate
  * Needs improvement
  * Poor
### Career Goal Setting
* **Career Goal Setting** (`career_goal_setting`, select_one, required): Select the level of career goal setting from the options provided: 
  * Highly developed
  * Well developed
  * Developing
  * Needs support
  * Underdeveloped
### Professional Skills
* **Professional Skills** (`professional_skills`, select_one, required): Select the level of professional skills from the options provided: 
  * Advanced
  * Proficient
  * Competent
  * Developing
  * Needs significant development
### Assessment Summary
* **Assessment Summary** (`assessment_summary`, text, optional): Enter a summary of the assessment and any recommendations from the assessor.

Note: This help guide is for the Adulthood Development Assessment form. Please refer to the form's instructions and fields for further clarification.
