# Investment Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients determine their investment strategy based on various factors such as their risk tolerance, investment objectives, timeframes, amounts, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by answering all the required questions (marked with `required: true`) in each section.
- If you have answers that do not fit into the provided options, please use the "Other" sections (e.g., `risk_tolerance_other`, `investment_frequency_other`, etc.) for your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your risk tolerance?** (`risk_tolerance`, `text`, required `true`): This is the level of risk you are willing to take with your investments. Answer accordingly to what you are comfortable with.
* **What are your investment objectives?** (`investment_objectives`, `select_multiple`, required `true`): Select all the objectives that best describe your investment goals. For example, growth, income, preservation, etc.
* **What is your investment timeframe?** (`investment_timeframe`, `time`, required `true`): Enter the timeframe in which you expect to achieve your investment goals. This could be short-term or long-term.
* **Investment amount?** (`investment_amount`, `number`, required `true`): Enter the amount of money you are willing to invest.
* **What is your investment horizon?** (`investment_horizon`, `select_one`, required `true`): Select the timeframe in which you expect to achieve your investment goals.
* **What are your investment preferences?** (`investment_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required `true`): Select all the preferences that best describe your investment style. For example, conservative, aggressive, etc.
* **What is your risk acceptance level?** (`risk_acceptance`, `select_one`, required `true`): Select the level of risk you are willing to take with your investments.
* **What is your expected investment return?** (`investment_return`, `select_multiple`, required `true`): Select the returns you expect from your investments.
* **How often do you invest?** (`investment_frequency`, `select_one`, required `true`): Select how often you invest.
* **Please specify frequency** (`investment_frequency_other`, `text`, required `true`): If your investment frequency is not among the provided options, please specify it here.
* **Please specify investment amount** (`investment_amount_other`, `text`, required `false`): If your investment amount is not among the provided options, please specify it here.
* **Other investment objectives** (`investment_objectives_other`, `text`, required `false`): If your investment objectives are not among the provided options, please specify them here.
* **Other risk tolerance** (`risk_tolerance_other`, `text`, required `false`): If your risk tolerance is not among the provided options, please specify it here.
* **Other investment timeframe** (`investment_timeframe_other`, `text`, required `false`): If your investment timeframe is not among the provided options, please specify it here.
* **Other investment horizon** (`investment_horizon_other`, `select_one`, required `false`): If your investment horizon is not among the provided options, please specify it here.
* **Other investment preferences** (`investment_preferences_other`, `select_multiple`, required `false`): If your investment preferences are not among the provided options, please select them here.
* **Other risk acceptance level** (`risk_acceptance_other`, `select_one`, required `false`): If your risk acceptance level is not among the provided options, please select it here.
* **Other expected investment return** (`investment_return_other`, `select_multiple`, required `false`): If your expected investment return is not among the provided options, please select it here.
* **Other frequency** (`investment_frequency_other`, `select_one`, required `false`): If your investment frequency is not among the provided options, please select it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required `false`): Add any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required `true`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required `false`): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required `false`): Add any additional comments or thoughts (optional).
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required `false`): You may add your name here (optional).
* **Submitted date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required `true`): The date the form was submitted.

Please note that the required fields should not be left blank.
