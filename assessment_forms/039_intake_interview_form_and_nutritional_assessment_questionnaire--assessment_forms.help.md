# Intake Interview Form And Nutritional Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information and nutritional assessment data from patients or clients.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete all the required fields. The fields marked as required are necessary for us to process your data accurately.
2. If a field is marked as optional, you can choose to provide information if it's relevant to your situation.
3. Ensure that you answer all questions accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as name, phone number, and email. This will help us to keep in touch with you and ensure that we have the right contact details.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your full name. Please enter your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your contact phone number. Please ensure that it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your email address. This will help us to communicate with you via email.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your contact address. Please ensure that it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter your date of birth. Please enter it in the correct format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): This field is for you to enter the time of your visit or appointment. Please ensure that it's accurate.
* **NAQ Scores** (`naq_scores`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is for you to select the relevant NAQ scores from the available options.
* **NAQ Score 1-16** (`naq_score_1-16`, `text`, required/optional): These fields are for you to enter your NAQ score 1-16. Please enter each score separately and accurately.

Note: Please ensure that you fill in all the fields accurately and completely. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your healthcare provider for guidance.
