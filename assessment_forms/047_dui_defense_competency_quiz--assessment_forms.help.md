# DUI Defense Competency Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool for DUI defense attorneys to evaluate their skills and knowledge in a simulated DUI defense scenario.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and provide thoughtful responses.
2. Answer all questions that apply to your experience and expertise.
3. Select the correct option for each question.
4. Complete all fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your license or ID.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a defense attorney.
* **Attorney Office** (`attorney_name_office`, text, required: false): Enter your law office or firm name.
* **Quiz Completed** (`quiz_completed`, select_one, required: false): Confirm that you have finished the quiz.
* **Quiz Duration** (`quiz_duration`, number, required: false): Record the time it took you to complete the quiz (in minutes).
* **Quiz Difficulty** (`quiz_difficulty`, number, required: false): Rate the difficulty level of the quiz (1-10).
* **Case Brief** (`case_brief`, text, required: false): Describe a recent or hypothetical DUI case you have handled.
* **Jury Instructions** (`jury_instructions`, text, required: false): Write instructions you would provide to the jury in a typical DUI case.
* **Defense Tactics** (`defense_tactics`, text, required: false): List any defense strategies you commonly use in DUI cases.
* **Voir Dire** (`voir_dire`, text, required: false): Describe how you would handle voir dire in a DUI case.
* **Direct Examination** (`direct_examination`, text, required: false): Explain how you would conduct a direct examination in a DUI case.
* **Cross Examination** (`cross_examination`, text, required: false): Describe how you would conduct a cross-examination in a DUI case.
* **Evidence** (`evidence`, text, required: false): List any evidence you commonly use in DUI cases.
