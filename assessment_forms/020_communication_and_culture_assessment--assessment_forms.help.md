# Communication And Culture Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees about their experiences and perceptions of communication and culture within the organization. The feedback collected will help the organization understand areas of improvement and make informed decisions to enhance communication and culture.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name, employee ID, and department in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer the rating questions (e.g., Communication Effectiveness, Information Flow, Leadership Communication) by selecting the most accurate answer for each.
3. Identify areas that need improvement by selecting the options in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
4. Provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Suggestions" field.
5. If you are willing, participate in follow-up discussions by selecting "Yes" in the "Willing to Participate" field.
6. Finally, select a date to record the date you completed this assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you work in.
* **Tenure** (`tenure`, select_one, required): Select how long you have been with the company.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required): Select where you primarily work.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the overall communication effectiveness.
* **Information Flow** (`information_flow`, select_one, required): Rate how well information flows through the organization.
* **Leadership Communication** (`leadership_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of leadership communication.
* **Team Communication** (`team_communication`, select_one, required): Rate communication within your team.
* **Cross-Department Communication** (`cross_department_communication`, select_one, required): Rate communication between departments.
* **Feedback Culture** (`feedback_culture`, select_one, required): Rate how comfortable you are giving feedback.
* **Feedback Reception** (`feedback_reception`, select_one, required): Rate how well feedback is received and acted upon.
* **Meeting Effectiveness** (`meeting_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of meetings.
* **Communication Tools** (`communication_tools`, select_one, required): Rate the adequacy of current communication tools.
* **Culture - Belonging** (`culture_belonging`, select_one, required): Rate how much you feel you belong in the organization.
* **Culture - Values** (`culture_values`, select_one, required): Rate how well company values align with your personal values.
* **Culture - Inclusion** (`culture_inclusion`, select_one, required): Rate how included you feel in decision-making processes.
* **Culture - Recognition** (`culture_recognition`, select_one, required): Rate how well your contributions are recognized.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select areas that need improvement.
* **Biggest Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Willing to Participate** (`willing_to_participate`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are willing to participate in follow-up discussions.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Select the date you completed this assessment.

## Tips
* Please answer the rating questions based on your honest opinion and perception of the organization.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting areas for improvement.
* If you have any suggestions, provide them in the "Suggestions" field.
* If you are willing, participate in follow-up discussions to help the organization improve communication and culture.
