# Fiction And Structure Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and assess submitted manuscripts for publication. It helps authors, editors, and reviewers track manuscript details, submit and review manuscripts, and provide feedback and ratings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the relevant options for manuscript type and genre.
3. Enter feedback, ratings, and comments as applicable.
4. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manuscript Title** (1, text, optional): Enter the title of the submitted manuscript.
* **Manuscript Author** (2, text, optional): Enter the author's name.
* **Manuscript Genre** (3, select_one, optional): Select the genre of the manuscript (e.g., Fantasy, Science Fiction, Romance, etc.).
* **Manuscript Rating** (4, number, optional): Enter a rating for the manuscript (e.g., 1-5).
* **Feedback Rating** (5, number, optional): Enter a feedback rating for the manuscript (e.g., 1-5).
* **Feedback Prophet** (6, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the feedback.
* **Feedback Comment** (7, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments.
* **Review Date** (8, date, optional): Enter the date of the review.
* **Submission Date** (9, date, optional): Enter the date the manuscript was submitted.
* **Manuscript Status** (10, select_one, optional): Select the status of the manuscript (e.g., Accepted, Rejected, Pending).
* **Manuscript Type** (11, select_one, optional): Select the type of the manuscript (e.g., Short Story, Novel).
* **Submission Email** (12, email, optional): Enter the email address of the submitter.
* **Submission Phone** (13, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Submission Address** (14, text, optional): Enter the address of the submitter.
* **Reviewer Email** (15, email, optional): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (16, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Address** (17, text, optional): Enter the address of the reviewer.
* **Submission Date** (18, date, optional): Enter the date the manuscript was submitted (Note: Duplicate field, not recommended for fill-in).
* **Manuscript Status Note** (19, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for the manuscript status.
* **Review Notes** (20, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for the review.
* **Manuscript Pages** (21, number, optional): Enter the number of pages in the manuscript.
* **Manuscript Words** (22, number, optional): Enter the number of words in the manuscript.
* **Manuscript File** (23, text, optional): Enter the file name or URL of the manuscript.
* **Manuscript Comments** (24, text, optional): Enter any additional comments for the manuscript.
* **Submission Date** (25, date, optional): Enter the date the manuscript was submitted (Note: Duplicate field, not recommended for fill-in).
