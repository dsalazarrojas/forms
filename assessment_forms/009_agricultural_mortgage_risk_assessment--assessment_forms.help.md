# Agricultural Mortgage Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the risk involved in lending to an agricultural operation. It collects essential details about the operation, including the applicant's name, farm address, loan amount, revenue, and risk levels to help determine the feasibility and terms of the loan.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and clear information for each field.
3. Review the help text for each field to understand its purpose and what is expected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant name** (Required): Enter the name of the borrower, the person responsible for the agricultural operation.
* **Farm address** (Required): Provide the primary location of the agricultural operation.
* **Loan amount requested** (Required): Enter the total amount of the loan being requested in local currency. This should be a whole number.
* **Annual farm revenue** (Optional): Estimate the total annual gross revenue of the agricultural operation. This helps to assess the operation's financial stability.
* **Collateral description** (Optional): Describe the assets you are offering as security for the loan, such as land, equipment, or livestock.
* **Credit history summary** (Optional): Note your past loan and payment experiences to demonstrate your ability to repay the loan.
* **Production risk level** (Required): Assess your production risks, such as climate and pest exposure.
*   *Select Low, Medium, or High.*
* **Market risk level** (Required): Evaluate the market risks, such as price volatility and buyer availability.
*   *Select Low, Medium, or High.*
* **Recommended risk mitigation actions** (Optional): Suggest steps to reduce the loan risk, such as diversification or insurance.
* **Assessor name** (Required): Enter the name of the loan officer or assessor who is evaluating your loan application.

Note: All required fields must be completed for the application to be processed.
 
**Tips**

* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the help text or consult with the loan officer.
* Ensure that all information provided is accurate and complete to avoid delays in processing your application.
* Keep in mind that providing clear and detailed information will assist the loan officer in evaluating your loan request. 
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to the loan officer.
