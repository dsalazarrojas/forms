# Delivery Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Delivery Impact Assessment Form is used to evaluate the impact of a service on the environment, community, and other stakeholders. It helps to identify areas of improvement in service delivery and ensures that the service aligns with business goals and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and its purpose.
* Complete each section with accurate and honest information.
* Make sure to answer all required fields, indicated by the asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Quality Feedback** (`service_quality`, number, *: 1*): Rate the quality of service, from 1 (low) to 10 (high).
* **Environmental Impact** (`environmental_impact`, select_multiple, *: 2*): Select all the environmental impacts that apply. Options: True, False.
* **Community Impact** (`community_impact`, select_one, *: 3*): Select one of the following:
	+ True: The service has a positive impact on the community.
	+ False: The service has a negative or no impact on the community.
* **Incident Reporting** (`incident_reporting`, date, *: 4*): Enter the date of the last reported incident.
* **How often do deliveries occur?** (`delivery_frequency`, select_one, *: 5*): Select one of the following:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, *: 6*): Enter the delivery time, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, *: 7*): Select all the types of service that apply. Options: True, False.
* **Provide contact information** (`contact_details`, text, *: 8*): Enter the contact information of the person responsible for the service.
* **Where do deliveries occur?** (`delivery_location`, select_one, *: 9*): Select one of the following:
	+ Warehouse
	+ Field
	+ Client Site
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, *: 10*): Select one of the following:
	+ True: There is a contact person assigned to the service.
	+ False: There is no contact person assigned to the service.
* **How would you rate the service quality?** (`service_quality_2`, select_one, *: 11*): Select one of the following:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
* **Delivery Frequency 2** (`delivery_frequency_2`, select_multiple, *: 12*): Select all the delivery frequencies that apply. Options: True, False.
* **Delivery Time 2** (`delivery_time_2`, date, *: 13*): Enter the delivery time, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
