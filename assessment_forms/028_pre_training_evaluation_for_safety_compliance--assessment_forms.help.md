# pre-training-evaluation-for-safety-compliance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre-training-evaluation-for-safety-compliance" form is used to collect information about pre-training evaluations for safety compliance. It is used to assess the status of pre-training evaluations and ensure that employees have received the necessary training to perform their job tasks safely.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Enter your information in the required fields.
3. Select the correct options for each multiple-choice field.
4. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Intro-Page (Page 1)
* **Intro-page**: This page is an introduction to the form.
### Training Date (Page 2)
* **training-date**: Enter the date of the training event. This field is optional.
### Safety Topic (Page 3)
* **safety-topic: ** Select all the safety topics that apply to this training event. Options: Safety, Security, Compliance.
### Training Status (Page 4)
* **training-status: ** Select the current status of the training event. Options: Not Started, Completed, In Progress, Scheduled.
### Training Duration (Page 5)
* **training-duration**: Enter the duration of the training event. This field is optional.
### Training Hours (Page 6)
* **training-hours**: Enter the number of hours spent in training. This field is optional.
### Employee Name (Page 7)
* **employee-name**: Enter the name of the employee who received the training.
### Manager Review Status (Page 8)
* **manager-review-status: ** Select the status of the manager's review. Options: Completed, Not Started, In Progress.
### Notes for Manager (Page 9)
* **notes-for-manager: ** Enter any additional notes for the manager's review.
### Manager Notes (Page 10)
* **manager-notes: ** Enter any notes for the manager's review.
### Completed Date (Page 11)
* **completed-date**: Enter the date when the training was completed. This field is optional.
### Training Locations (Page 12)
* **training-locations: ** Select all locations where the training event was conducted. Options: Office, Onsite, Offsite, Online.
### Training Locations Additional (Page 13)
* **training-locations-additional: ** Enter any additional location information.
### Training Locations Geo Coordinates (Page 14)
* **training-locations-geo-coordinates: ** Enter the geographic coordinates of the training location.
### Location Verified (Page 15)
* **location-verified: ** Select whether the location was verified. Options: True, False, Maybe.
### Training Completion Status (Page 16)
* **training-completion-status: ** Select the status of the training completion. Options: Completed, Not Started, In Progress, Scheduled.
### Training Completion Date (Page 17)
* **training-completion-date: ** Enter the date when the training was completed. This field is optional.
### Manager Completion Status (Page 18)
* **manager-completion-status: ** Select the status of the manager's completion. Options: Completed, Not Started, In Progress, Scheduled.
### Manager Completion Date (Page 19)
* **manager-completion-date: ** Enter the date when the manager completed the review. This field is optional.
