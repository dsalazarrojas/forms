# Sales Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Onboarding Assessment Form is a crucial tool to evaluate a new sales representative's readiness to join the sales team. It helps to gather essential information about their sales onboarding program, training, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct Sales Representative from the dropdown list.
2. Choose the appropriate Sales Onboarding Program from the list.
3. If applicable, select the Sales Manager and Territory from the dropdown lists.
4. Enter the Sales Representative's Name, Email, and Phone number in the text fields.
5. Enter the Onboarding Start and End Dates in the date fields.
6. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Sales Representative**: Choose the correct Sales Representative from the dropdown list.
* **Sales Onboarding Program**: Select the Sales Onboarding Program that the representative is currently enrolled in.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date the representative completed their training program.
* **Sales Manager**: Choose the Sales Manager who will be responsible for the representative.
* **Territory**: Select 'Yes' if the representative will be working in a specific territory, or 'No' if they will not.
* **Sales Rep Name**: Enter the name of the Sales Representative.
* **Sales Rep Email**: Enter the email address of the Sales Representative.
* **Sales Rep Phone**: Enter the phone number of the Sales Representative.
* **Onboarding Start Date**: Enter the date the Sales Representative started their onboarding process.
* **Onboarding End Date**: Enter the date the Sales Representative completed their onboarding process.
