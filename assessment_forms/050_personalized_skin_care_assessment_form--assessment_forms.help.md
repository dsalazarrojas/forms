# Personalized Skin Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your skin care needs and concerns. The data collected will be used to provide you with personalized recommendations and advice for your skin care routine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the questions thoroughly and honestly.
2. Enter your response in the corresponding text fields.
3. If you have a choice of options for a question, select the one that best describes your skin care need or concern.
4. Submit your completed form for personalized recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_1_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **form_1_label** (Text, Optional): This field will be used as a placeholder for your response. Please enter your thoughts or concerns about your skin care.
* **form_2_label** (Select, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you have experienced any skin issues or concerns in the past, or 'No' if you have not.
* **form_3_label** (Text, Optional): Enter any relevant details about your skin care routine, such as products or habits you follow.
