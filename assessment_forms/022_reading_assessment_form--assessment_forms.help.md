# reading_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Reading Assessment Form" is used to gather information about your reading habits and preferences. Please fill out this form to help us better understand your reading experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have been asked to fill this form. This form is not meant for general access.
2. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
3. Fill out all required fields (marked as `true`).
4. Use the provided options in the `select` and `select_multiple` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **demographic_info** (`demographic_info`, `text`, required): This field is for providing demographic information about yourself, such as name, age, or contact details.
* **reading_comprehension** (`reading_comprehension`, `text`, required): Describe your reading comprehension, or how well you understand and interpret written text.
* **reading_preferences** (`reading_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate your preferences for reading, such as reading in groups or alone, or during different time of the day.
* **reading_frequency** (`reading_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer whether you read regularly or occasionally.
* **reading_format** (`reading_format`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you prefer reading physical books, digital books, or other formats.
* **reading_difficulty** (`reading_difficulty`, `number`, optional): Rate the difficulty level of your reading experience, with 1 being the most challenging and 10 being the least challenging.
* **reading_speed** (`reading_speed`, `number`, optional): Rate your reading speed, with 1 being the slowest and 10 being the fastest.
* **reading_time** (`reading_time`, `time`, optional): Provide the total time you spend on reading each day or week.
* **reading_date** (`reading_date`, `date`, optional): Record the date of your last reading experience or the last time you read.
* **reading_time_of_day** (`reading_time_of_day`, `time`, optional): Choose the time of day when you usually read.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any further information or comments about your reading experience.
