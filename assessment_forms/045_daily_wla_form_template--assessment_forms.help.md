# Daily WLA Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily WLA Form Template is designed to collect information about your daily work habits, team collaboration, and project progress. This form will help gather data on various aspects of your work, including time management, resource allocation, and project completion rates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to answer all questions marked as "required."
3. If a question doesn't apply to your situation, skip it or explain why in the provided text field.
4. Submit the form to save your progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What time do you usually arrive at work?** (`resource_allocation`, `time`, required): This question is asking about your daily routine, specifically your arrival time at work.
- **How many hours do you work each day?** (`time_management`, `number`, required): This question is about your work hours, please provide the actual number of hours you work per day.
- **How would you rate your team's productivity?** (`team_productivity`, `select_multiple`, required): Rate your team's productivity as High, Medium, or Low.
- **What is the current project status?** (`project_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from In Progress, On Hold, or Completed.
- **What resources are available to you on Monday?** (`resource_availability`, `text`, required): List the resources you have access to on Monday.
- **How do you prioritize tasks?** (`task_completion`, `text`, required): Describe how you prioritize tasks on a daily basis.
- **How often do you have meetings?** (`meeting_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you have meetings - Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
- **Do you collaborate with other teams?** (`team_collaboration`, `checkbox`, required): Check if you collaborate with other teams.
- **How do you manage tasks?** (`task_management`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you manage tasks - To-Do List, Task Manager, or Project Management.
- **Where do you work?** (`work_location`, `text`, required): Provide the location where you work.
- **How do you plan your schedule for the day?** (`time_management_2`, `text`, required): Explain how you plan your schedule.
- **How do you update project status?** (`project_status_update`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how often you update project status - Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
- **How many resources do you have at your disposal?** (`resource_allocation_2`, `number`, required): Provide the number of resources you have access to.
- **How many resources do you have for this project?** (`resource_allocation_3`, `number`, required): Provide the number of resources specifically for this project.
- **What resources do you have available during work hours?** (`resource_availability_2`, `text`, required): List the resources you have access to during work hours.
- **What is your work hours?** (`time_management_2_2`, `time`, required): Provide the specific hours you work.
- **What factors affect team productivity?** (`team_productivity_2`, `text`, required): Describe the factors that affect your team's productivity.
- **What is your project completion rate?** (`project_completion_rate`, `number`, required): Provide the rate of your project completion.

Please answer these questions accurately and thoroughly to help us understand your work habits and experiences. If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
