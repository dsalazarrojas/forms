# Exercise Accuracy Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a user's understanding of their responses to a set of exercises. It is used to determine the accuracy of their answers and provide feedback.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the exercise you are completing.
2. Describe the exercise in detail.
3. Enter the questions asked in the exercise.
4. Enter the answer choices for each question.
5. Identify the correct answer for each question.
6. Enter the total score for the exercise.
7. Enter the total score for the assessment form.
8. Provide any additional comments about your completion of the exercise.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exercise Accuracy Quiz** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz`, note, required): Enter the name of the exercise you are completing.
* **Assessment Forms** (`assessment_forms`, text, required): Describe the exercise in detail.
* **Description** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the exercise.
* **Questions** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_questions`, text, required): Enter the questions asked in the exercise.
* **Answer Choices** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_answer_choices`, text, required): Enter the answer choices for each question.
* **Correct Answer** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_correct_answer`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer for each question.
* **Correct Answer Choice** (`assessment_forms_correct_answer`, select_one, required): Select the correct answer for the assessment form.
* **Score** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_score`, select_multiple, required): Enter the total score for the exercise.
* **Assessment Forms Score** (`assessment_forms_score`, select_multiple, required): Enter the total score for the assessment form.
* **Comments** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_comments`, text, required): Provide any additional comments about your completion of the exercise.
* **Exercise** (`exercise_prophets`, number, required): Enter the number of exercises completed.
* **Assessment Forms** (`assessment_forms_prophets`, number, required): Enter the number of assessment forms completed.
* **Email** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Time** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_time`, time, optional): Enter the time spent on the exercise.
* **Date** (`exercise_accuracy_quiz_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of completion.
* **Exercise Date** (`assessment_forms_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of completion of the assessment form.
* **Assessment Forms Date** (`assessment_forms_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of completion of the assessment form.
