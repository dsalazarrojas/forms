# External Worker Training Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The External Worker Training Assessment form is designed to collect information about external workers' training status, including their name, contact information, training hours, and categories.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Provide your Email address, which is a required field for communication purposes.
3. Enter the number of Training Hours you've completed.
4. Select Yes or No for Training Program.
5. Enter the date of the last training session.
6. Enter the time of the last training session.
7. Add any additional Notes about your training experience.
8. Enter your Phone Number for contact purposes.
9. Choose any applicable Category(ies) for your training.
10. Enter any relevant information for Category Option 1 to 10.
11. Enter the date and time when you submitted this assessment.
12. Enter the name of the tool used to submit this form.
13. Finally, review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Training Hours**: Enter the number of hours you've completed for the training program.
* **Training Program**: Select Yes if you've completed the training program, or No if you haven't.
* **Training Date**: Enter the date of your last training session.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time of your last training session.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about your training experience.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool used for the training program (if applicable).
* **Category**: Select any applicable categories for your training (if available).
* **Category Option 1** to **Category Option 10**: Enter any relevant information for each selected category.
* **Submitted**: Enter the date when you submitted this assessment.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting this assessment.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the time when you submitted this assessment.
* **Submitted By Tool**: Enter the tool used to submit this assessment (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or training administrator.
* Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
