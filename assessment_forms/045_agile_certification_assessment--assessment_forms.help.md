# Agile Certification Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agile Certification Assessment is designed to evaluate the knowledge and skills of candidates for the Agile certification. It collects information on the candidate's personal details, assessment performance, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the candidate's full name as registered
*   Enter the candidate's email address for results and certification purposes
*   Provide the date of assessment
*   Enter the knowledge score out of 100 for the written exam
*   Enter the score for the practical exercises
*   Select the outcome of the portfolio review (Accepted, Needs improvement, or Rejected)
*   Choose the final certification outcome (Certified, Not certified, or Conditional certification)
*   (Optional) Add any assessor comments or remedial actions

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the candidate as registered.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the candidate for results and certification purposes.
*   **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Provide the date of the assessment.
*   **Knowledge score** (`knowledge_score`, number, required): Enter the written exam score out of 100.
*   **Practical exercise score** (`practical_score`, number, required): Enter the score for the practical exercises.
*   **Portfolio review outcome** (`portfolio_review`, select_one, required): Select the outcome of the portfolio review (Accepted, Needs improvement, or Rejected).
*   **Overall result** (`overall_result`, select_one, required): Choose the final certification outcome (Certified, Not certified, or Conditional certification).
*   **Assessor comments** (`assessor_comments`, text, optional): Add any notes or remedial actions for the assessor.
*   **Certificate ID** (`certificate_id`, text, optional): Enter the assigned certificate reference (if applicable).
