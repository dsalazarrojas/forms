# Process Change Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Change Assessment Form is used to document and assess changes to business processes, ensuring that changes are properly tracked, reviewed, and communicated across the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in the designated format, providing clear and concise information about the process change.
2. Answer questions in a way that accurately represents the impact, risk, and benefits of the change.
3. Include relevant details and dates for review and tracking purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Process Change Form** (`process_change_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter a brief description of the process change, providing an overview of what has changed.
* **Change Details** (`change_details`, text, optional): Here, you can provide more in-depth information about the process change, including any relevant context or justification.
* **Impact** (`impact`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable options that best describe the impact of the change, such as:
	+ Yes: The change has a positive impact on the organization.
	+ No: The change has no impact on the organization.
* **Risk** (`risk`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the change introduces new risks or potential problems, or "No" if the change is risk-free.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all applicable options that best describe the benefits of the change, such as:
	+ Yes: The change introduces new benefits or opportunities.
	+ No: The change has no benefits.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Describe any next steps or actions that need to be taken as a result of the process change.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the process change was assessed or reviewed.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the process change was reviewed or revised.
* **Reviewed By** (`reviewed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who reviewed or revised the process change. 

Note that this form is not a substitute for actual review and approval processes. It's essential to follow the organization's formal review and approval procedures.
