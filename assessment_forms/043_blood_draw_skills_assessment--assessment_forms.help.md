# Blood Draw Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the skills of a candidate in performing a blood draw, which is essential for phlebotomy competency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Candidate Name**, which is the name of the person being assessed.
2. Enter the **Employee ID**, which is the staff ID number of the person being assessed (if applicable).
3. Enter your **Name**, which is your evaluator's name.
4. Enter the **Assessment Date**, which is the date of the evaluation.
5. Select the **Candidate Experience Level**, which indicates the candidate's training status:
	* **Student**: The candidate is in training.
	* **New Hire**: The candidate is new to the position.
	* **Less than 1 Year**: The candidate has less than 1 year of experience.
	* **1-3 Years**: The candidate has 1-3 years of experience.
	* **Over 3 Years**: The candidate has over 3 years of experience.
6. Evaluate the candidate's skills in each of the following areas:
	* **Hand Hygiene**: Assess the candidate's hand washing technique.
	* **PPE Usage**: Assess the candidate's use of personal protective equipment.
	* **Patient Identification**: Assess the candidate's ID verification process.
	* **Vein Selection**: Assess the candidate's ability to locate a suitable vein.
	* **Site Preparation**: Assess the candidate's cleaning technique.
	* **Equipment Setup**: Assess the candidate's preparation of supplies.
	* **Needle Insertion**: Assess the candidate's technique and angle.
	* **Tube Filling**: Assess the candidate's filling of the tube.
	* **Needle Removal**: Assess the candidate's safe withdrawal technique.
	* **Post-Draw Care**: Assess the candidate's pressure and bandaging technique.
	* **Sample Labeling**: Assess the candidate's accurate tube labeling.
	* **Safety Compliance**: Assess the candidate's adherence to safety protocols.
	* **Patient Communication**: Assess the candidate's bedside manner and explanation.
7. Provide feedback on the candidate's **Overall Performance**, which indicates their general assessment:
	* **Competent**: The candidate performed well.
	* **Needs Practice**: The candidate needs more practice.
	* **Not Competent**: The candidate is not competent in performing blood draws.
8. Provide feedback on the candidate's **Areas of Strength**, which are their positive qualities.
9. Provide feedback on the candidate's **Areas for Improvement**, which are areas where they need to improve.
10. Select **Reassessment Needed**, which indicates whether follow-up is required.
11. Sign your **Assessor Signature** and the candidate's **Candidate Signature**, acknowledging the results.
