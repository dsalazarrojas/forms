# Speech Language Pathology Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients undergoing speech and language evaluations to ensure accurate and thorough documentation of their assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above and enter accurate and relevant information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details**: <client_details>: Enter the client's basic information, such as their name.
* **History**: <history>: Document the client's history and relevant medical information.
* **Consent**: <consent>: Indicate whether the client has provided consent for the evaluation.
* **Consent Date**: <consent_date>: Record the date the client provided consent.
* **Speech & Language Evaluations**: <speech_language_evaluations>: Check off any speech and language evaluations that need to be conducted.
* **Client Consent Status**: <client_consent_status>: Confirm whether the client has given their consent.
* **Evaluation Date**: <evaluation_date>: Record the date the evaluation was conducted.
* **Evaluator**: <evaluator_name>: Enter the name of the person conducting the evaluation.
* **Client Name**: <client_name>: Enter the name of the client.
* **Date of Birth**: <client_birthdate>: Record the client's date of birth.
* **Contact Information**: <client_contact>: Enter any necessary contact information for the client.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check the client's consent status to ensure it is accurate.
* Document any evaluations and assessments that need to be conducted.
* Keep the client's contact information up-to-date.
* Ensure that all necessary information is entered for the client's file.
