# Home Safety Assessment Consent Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather consent for the home safety assessment process. The assessment is to evaluate the safety of a resident's home environment. Please ensure you are filling out this form for the correct purpose and not duplicating efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Resident Information` page with the resident's personal details.
2. Provide contact information on the `Contact Information` page.
3. Describe the safety concerns or issues you have identified on the `Safety Assessment` page.
4. Indicate your consent status on the `Consent Status` page.
5. Sign and date the form on the `Signature` page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Information** (`resident_info`, text, required: false): Enter the resident's name, address, and any other relevant information that may be required for the assessment.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide the contact information of the person completing this form, such as their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Safety Assessment** (`safety_assessment`, text, required: false): Describe the safety concerns or issues you have identified in the resident's home environment. Be as detailed as possible to help the assessor understand the situation.
* **Consent Status** (`consent_status`, select_one, required: false): Select 'True' if you consent to the home safety assessment, 'False' if you do not consent, or 'Not Applicable' if you are not able to consent (e.g., minor).
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it accurately and truthfully.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have obtained the resident's consent before proceeding with the home safety assessment.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities for guidance.
