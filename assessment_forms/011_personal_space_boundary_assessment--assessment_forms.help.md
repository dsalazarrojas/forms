The "Personal Space Boundary Assessment" form is designed to evaluate an individual's comfort and preferences when it comes to personal space and boundaries in a training or workshop setting. The form will help identify how an individual likes to perceive and maintain their personal space, how comfortable they are with their environment, and what they prefer when it comes to training or workshop norms. This form is not a one-time assessment, and answers may be subject to change based on individual preferences and circumstances. Please be aware that answers to this form will not affect your standing or standing in the training or workshop.

## Purpose
This form is designed to gauge your comfort level and preferences regarding personal space and boundaries in a training or workshop setting. Your responses will help us understand your individual needs and provide a more tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Personal Space Boundary Assessment form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. You can provide as much or as little information as you feel comfortable sharing.
