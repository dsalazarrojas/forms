# unit_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the knowledge and confidence of units in a specific topic or subject area. It is designed to gather information about how well individuals understand and are confident about the unit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the form as accurately as possible.
2. Select the most appropriate option for each multiple-choice question.
3. Provide a reflection on what you learned about the unit (if applicable).
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **What is your confidence about this unit?** (1) (`select_one`, required: false)
* This question asks about your level of confidence in understanding the unit.

### **How well do you understand the unit?** (2) (`select_multiple`, required: false)
* This question assesses how well you understand the unit, choose all that apply.

### **Can you reflect on what you learned about this unit?** (3) (`note`, required: false)
* Provide a brief reflection on what you learned about the unit, if applicable.

### **What would you do if you were in charge?** (4) (`select_multiple`, required: false)
* This question is designed to understand your approach or strategy if you were in charge of the unit.

### **How confident are you about this unit?** (5) (`number`, required: false)
* This question asks you to rate your level of confidence on a scale from 0 to 10.
