# Cyber Security Risk Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cyber Security Risk Assessment Checklist is designed to help IT professionals and risk analysts assess and prioritize potential cyber security risks in an organization or system. This form guides users through a series of questions to identify, evaluate, and document risks, providing a structured approach to risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form, which includes questions about risk management, identification, assessment, and mitigation.
2. Answer each question truthfully, using the options provided.
3. Use the provided options for each field to select the most accurate response.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: `false`): This page provides a brief overview and context for the form. It is not a mandatory field but provides a good starting point for understanding the form's purpose.
* **Risk Management** (`Risk Management`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field assesses the organization's risk management practices and policies.
	+ Choose all that apply to describe your current risk management practices:
		- Yes: The organization has a risk management process in place.
		- No: The organization does not have a risk management process in place.
* **Risk Identification** (`Risk Identification`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field identifies potential risks in the organization or system.
	+ Choose all that apply to describe the types of risks that exist:
		- Yes: Identifies potential risks.
		- No: No potential risks are identified.
* **Risk Assessment** (`Risk Assessment`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field evaluates the likelihood and potential impact of each identified risk.
	+ Choose all that apply to describe the assessment:
		- Yes: The risk has been assessed.
		- No: The risk has not been assessed.
* **Risk Priority** (`Risk Priority`, `select_one`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field prioritizes the risks based on their potential impact and likelihood.
	+ Choose the most accurate priority level for each risk:
		- Yes: High priority.
		- No: Low priority.
* **Risk Category** (`Risk Category`, `select_multiple`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field categorizes the risks into different types (e.g., confidentiality, integrity, availability).
	+ Choose all that apply to describe the risk category:
		- Yes: The risk falls under the category.
		- No: The risk does not fall under the category.
* **Risk Frequency** (`Risk Frequency`, `select_one`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field evaluates the frequency of the risk.
	+ Choose the most accurate frequency level for each risk:
		- Yes: Continuous.
		- No: Occasional.
* **Risk Description** (`Risk Description`, `text`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field provides a detailed description of the risk.
	+ Describe each risk in as much detail as possible.
* **Risk Impact** (`Risk Impact`, `select_one`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field evaluates the potential impact of the risk.
	+ Choose the most accurate impact level for each risk:
		- Yes: Critical.
		- No: Minimal.
* **Risk Probability** (`Risk Probability`, `number`, required: `true`): 
	+ This field estimates the likelihood of the risk.
	+ Enter a numerical value between 0 and 100 to describe the probability.
## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure accurate risk assessment.
* Review each page carefully before proceeding to the next.
* Use the provided options to guide your answers.
* If you are unsure about a question, use the 'No' or 'None of the above' option and provide additional comments.
