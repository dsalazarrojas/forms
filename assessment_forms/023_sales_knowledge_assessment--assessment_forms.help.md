# Sales Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate an employee's sales knowledge and experience. It helps to identify areas of strength and weakness in an employee's sales performance and provides a basis for targeted training and development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
* Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your current job title in the "Job Title" field.
* Enter your current manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
* Enter your department in the "Department" field.
* Enter a brief description of your job function in the "Job Function" field.
* Enter the name of your current company in the "Company" field.
* Enter the number of years you have been working in sales in the "Years In Sales" field.
* Select your level of sales performance from the options provided in the "Sales Performance" field.
* Indicate whether any additional training is needed for you to improve your sales performance in the "Training Needed" field.
* Finally, select the date you completed this assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your work account.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current department.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, `text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your job function.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your current company.
* **Years In Sales** (`years_in_sales`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have been working in sales.
* **Sales Performance** (`sales_performance`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your level of sales performance:
  + High: You consistently meet or exceed sales targets.
  + Medium: You generally meet sales targets, but with some room for improvement.
  + Low: You frequently struggle to meet sales targets.
* **Training Needed** (`training_needed`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether any additional training is needed:
  + True: Yes, you need additional training.
  + False: No, you do not need additional training.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you completed this assessment.
