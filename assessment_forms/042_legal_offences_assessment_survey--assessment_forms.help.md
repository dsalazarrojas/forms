# Legal Offences Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Offences Assessment Survey is a form designed to collect information about legal offensives, such as assaults, harassments, and vandalisms. This form is used to gather data and track incidents, and may be completed by law enforcement, authorities, or individuals reporting crimes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction page, which provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and importance.
2.  Select the relevant offence type from the list provided (Assault, Harassment, or Vandalism).
3.  Enter the location where the offence occurred.
4.  Provide the date of the offence.
5.  Enter the name(s) of the person involved, if applicable.
6.  Enter the contact information (phone number or email) of the person reporting the offence.
7.  Provide a detailed description of the offence.
8.  Select the name of the person submitting the form (Submitter).
9.  Enter the email of the person submitting the form (Submitter Email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction (page 1)**: This page provides a brief overview of the form's purpose and importance.
*   **Offence Type (page 2)**: Select the type of offence that occurred (Assault, Harassment, or Vandalism).
*   **Location (page 3)**: Enter the exact location where the offence occurred.
*   **Date of Offence (page 4)**: Enter the date when the offence took place.
*   **Contact Person (page 5)**: Enter the name(s) of the person(s) involved in the offence, if applicable.
*   **Contact Number/Contact Email (page 6 & 7)**: Enter the contact information of the person reporting the offence (phone number or email).
*   **Offence Details (page 8)**: Provide a detailed description of the offence.
*   **Submitter (page 9)**: Select the name of the person submitting the form.
*   **Submitter Email (page 10)**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.

Note: The fields with a type of `select_one` or `select_multiple` imply a dropdown list of options, whereas fields with a type of `text` or `date` imply free-text entry.
