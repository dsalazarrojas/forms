# Mini Mental State Examination (MMSE) Score Recording - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mini Mental State Examination (MMSE) Score Recording form is designed to record the MMSE scores for patients who have undergone the examination. This form helps medical professionals assess the cognitive status of patients and provides essential information for their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Form Title**, a brief description of the patient's case or examination.
2. Enter the **Patient Name**, the name of the patient being examined.
3. Enter the **Age** of the patient, a numerical value.
4. Select the **Gender** of the patient, one of the three options: Female, Male, or Other.
5. Select the **Marital Status** of the patient, one of the five options: Married, Divorced, Single, Widow, or Other.
6. Select the **Education Level** of the patient, one of the four options: Primary school, High school, College/University, or University/Postgraduate.
7. Enter the **Cognitive Screening**, the date of the examination or screening.
8. Enter the **Score 1**, **Score 2**, **Score 3**, **Score 4**, and **Score 5**, five numerical values representing the MMSE scores.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title**: A brief description of the patient's case or examination.
* **Patient Name**: The name of the patient being examined.
* **Age**: A numerical value representing the patient's age.
* **Gender**: The gender of the patient, selecting one of the three options: Female, Male, or Other.
* **Marital Status**: The marital status of the patient, selecting one of the five options: Married, Divorced, Single, Widow, or Other.
* **Education Level**: The education level of the patient, selecting one of the four options: Primary school, High school, College/University, or University/Postgraduate.
* **Cognitive Screening**: The date of the examination or screening.
* **Score 1**, **Score 2**, **Score 3**, **Score 4**, and **Score 5**: Five numerical values representing the MMSE scores.

## Tips
- Ensure that each field is completed accurately and truthfully.
- Use the same format for recording scores, if possible.
- If any field is left blank, please provide a clear explanation for the omission.
