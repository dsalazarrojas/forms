# Performance And Attention Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance And Attention Evaluation form is used to assess the performance and attention areas of an individual or team. It helps evaluators to evaluate the individual or team's strengths and weaknesses, identify areas for improvement, and provide a comprehensive picture of their capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the relevant options for "Focus Areas" that are applicable to the individual or team being evaluated.
2.  Choose the corresponding performance level for each of the selected focus areas.
3.  Fill in the required contact information (Date, Time, Email, and Phone) and submission status information (Submission Status, Submission Date, Submission Time, and Submission Email).
4.  Optionally, provide additional comments or insights about the evaluation.
