# Business Client Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Client Assessment Form is a comprehensive guide designed to collect information about your business needs, goals, and expectations for our services. This form will help us tailor our solutions to meet your specific requirements and provide the best possible results.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your basic client details.
2.  Answer each question in the best way possible, using the space provided.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed, as these are marked with an asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, `note`, not required): Please enter your basic company details in a brief paragraph.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your company or organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact for your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry your business operates in.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Choose the number of employees your company has.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, `select_one`, not required): Select your company's approximate annual revenue.
* **Business History** (`business_history`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long your company has been in business.
* **Needs Assessment** (`needs_assessment`, `note`, not required): Enter a brief description of your business needs.
* **Primary Need** (`primary_need`, `select_one`, required): Select the main reason for our engagement.
* **Specific Challenges** (`specific_challenges`, `text`, required): Describe the specific challenges you're facing.
* **Current Pain Points** (`current_pain_points`, `text`, required): Describe what keeps you up at night.
* **Goals and Objectives** (`goals`, `text`, required): Enter your goals and objectives for our engagement.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, `select_one`, required): Choose the timeline for when you need results.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `select_one`, not required): Select your available budget for our services.
* **Decision Makers** (`decision_makers`, `text`, required): Enter the names of those who make final decisions.
* **Key Stakeholders** (`stakeholders`, `text`, not required): Enter the names of key stakeholders involved in your business.
* **Current Service Providers** (`current_providers`, `text`, not required): List any current service providers you're working with.
* **Previous Initiatives** (`previous_initiatives`, `text`, not required): Describe any previous initiatives to solve similar problems.
* **Success Criteria** (`success_criteria`, `text`, required): Enter how success will be measured.
* **Risks** (`risks`, `text`, not required): Describe any risks you perceive.
* **Competitive Landscape** (`competition`, `text`, not required): Describe your main competitors.
* **Unique Value Proposition** (`unique_value`, `text`, not required): Enter what makes your company different.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, required): Choose where you found us.
* **Preferred Engagement Type** (`engagement_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred type of engagement.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any other relevant information.

Please ensure that you fill out this form accurately and completely. If you need any clarification or have questions about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us.
