# Sustainability Initiatives Implementation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the implementation of sustainability initiatives in a company or organization. It is meant to be used to track and evaluate the progress of these initiatives, ensuring that they are being implemented effectively and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name and email address to identify yourself as the person filling out the form.
2. Select the sustainability initiative you are assessing from the dropdown list.
3. Choose the current status of the initiative (Not implemented, In Progress, or Completed).
4. Enter the implementation date (optional).
5. Enter the completion date (optional).
6. Provide a brief description of the initiative's impact.
7. Enter any justification or justification for the initiative.
8. Enter the budget allocated for the initiative (optional).
9. Enter any comments or feedback on the initiative.
10. Upload any attachments related to the initiative (optional).
11. Enter a description of the attachment.
12. Enter the URL of the attachment (optional).
13. Enter any comments or feedback from the user.
14. Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
15. Select the review status of the initiative (Not reviewed, Under review, or Approved).
16. Enter the review date (optional).
17. Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
18. Enter the submission date (optional).
19. Enter the submitter ID (optional).
20. Enter the submitter name (optional).
21. Enter the reviewer ID (optional).
22. Enter the reviewer name (optional).
23. Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter your name**: Your name is required to identify yourself as the person filling out the form.
* **Enter your email**: Your email address is required to contact you for any further information.
* **Select initiative**: This field allows you to choose the sustainability initiative you are assessing.
* **Select current status**: Choose the current status of the initiative (Not implemented, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Implementation date**: Enter the date the initiative was implemented (optional).
* **Completion date**: Enter the date the initiative was completed (optional).
* **Enter impact**: Provide a brief description of the initiative's impact.
* **Enter justification**: Enter any justification or reasoning for the initiative.
* **Enter budget**: Enter the budget allocated for the initiative (optional).
* **Enter comments**: Enter any comments or feedback on the initiative.
* **Attachment**: This is an optional field to upload any attachments related to the initiative.
* **Attachment description**: Enter a description of the attachment.
* **Enter attachment URL**: Enter the URL of the attachment (optional).
* **Enter user comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the user.
* **Enter reviewer comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Select review status**: Choose the review status of the initiative (Not reviewed, Under review, or Approved).
* **Review date**: Enter the date the initiative was reviewed (optional).
* **Enter reviewer comments**: Enter any comments or feedback from the reviewer.
* **Submission date**: Enter the date the initiative was submitted (optional).
* **Submitter ID**: Enter the ID of the person who submitted the initiative (optional).
* **Submitter name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the initiative (optional).
* **Reviewer ID**: Enter the ID of the reviewer (optional).
* **Reviewer name**: Enter the name of the reviewer (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are filling out the form for an initiative that has not been previously assessed or reviewed.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Please fill out the form in a clear and concise manner to ensure that the information is accurate and easy to read.
