# Public Service Program Efficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields of the Public Service Program Efficiency Assessment form and explain their purpose and how to fill them out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Program Name field, which is the name of the public service program you are assessing.
2. Enter your Contact Number, which is your phone number or other contact information.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Rate the Efficiency, Effectiveness, and Satisfaction of the program.
5. Provide any Suggestion for improvement.
6. Identify the Department responsible for the program.
7. Rate the Service Level (High, Medium, or Low).
8. Enter the date and time you provided your feedback.
9. Enter any Feedback Note, which is an opportunity for you to add any additional comments.
10. Finally, rate the Contact Method (Phone, Email, or In-person) and the Contact Location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the public service program you are assessing.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number or other contact information. This field is optional.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is required.
* **Rating (Efficiency, Effectiveness, Satisfaction)**: Rate the program using the provided options (Efficiency, Effectiveness, Satisfaction).
* **Suggestion**: Provide any suggestions for improvement. This field is optional.
* **Department**: Identify the department responsible for the program (Administration, Finance, Human Resources).
* **Service Level**: Rate the service level of the program (High, Medium, Low). This field is required.
* **Feedback Date**: Enter the date you provided your feedback. This field is required.
* **Feedback Time**: Enter the time you provided your feedback. This field is required.
* **Feedback Note**: Enter any additional comments or explanations. This field is optional.
* **Contact Method**: Rate the contact method you used (Phone, Email, In-person). This field is required.
* **Contact Location**: Enter the location where you provided your feedback. This field is optional.
* **Follow-up**: Enter any additional comments or explanations. This field is optional.

Please note that all required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and must be filled out. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or contact the relevant department.
