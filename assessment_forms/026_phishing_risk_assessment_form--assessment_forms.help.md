# Phishing Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phishing Risk Assessment Form is designed to help security and IT teams evaluate and document the likelihood and potential impact of phishing threats on their organization. This form will guide you through assessing the risk factors, identifying recommended controls, and documenting mitigation plans to minimize the risk.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief description of the phishing risk, including any relevant details (field: `phishing_risk_description`).
2. Select all applicable risk factors that contribute to the risk (field: `risk_factors`).
3. Choose the recommended control to mitigate the risk (field: `recommended_controls`).
4. Document any mitigation plans or actions taken to address the risk (field: `mitigation_plans`).
5. Evaluate the risk's impact on your organization on a scale of 1-10 (field: `phishing_risk_impact`).
6. Assess the likelihood of the risk occurring on a scale of 1-10 (field: `phishing_risk_probability`).
7. If applicable, specify the expected time frame for the impact of the risk (field: `phishing_risk_impact_time`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Phishing Risk Description** (`phishing_risk_description`): A brief description of the phishing risk, including any relevant details. This field is not required, but it helps to provide context for the assessment.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`): Select all applicable risk factors that contribute to the risk. This field allows you to identify and prioritize the factors that increase the likelihood and potential impact of the phishing risk.
* **Recommended Controls** (`recommended_controls`): Choose the recommended control to mitigate the risk. This field helps to implement measures to minimize the risk's impact.
* **Mitigation Plans** (`mitigation_plans`): Document any mitigation plans or actions taken to address the risk. This field is not required, but it helps to keep track of your organization's response to the risk.
* **Phishing Risk Impact** (`phishing_risk_impact`): Evaluate the risk's impact on your organization on a scale of 1-10. This field helps to prioritize the risk's severity.
* **Phishing Risk Probability** (`phishing_risk_probability`): Assess the likelihood of the risk occurring on a scale of 1-10. This field helps to prioritize the risk's likelihood.
* **Phishing Risk Impact Time** (`phishing_risk_impact_time`): If applicable, specify the expected time frame for the impact of the risk. This field helps to plan and prepare for potential consequences.

Note: This form is designed to be flexible and adaptable to your organization's needs. Please feel free to add or modify fields as necessary to suit your organization's specific requirements.
