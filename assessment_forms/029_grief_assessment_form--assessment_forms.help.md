# Grief Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grief Assessment Form is designed to provide a structured and comprehensive evaluation of your grief reactions and feelings. This form is typically completed after experiencing a significant loss, such as the loss of a loved one. It helps healthcare professionals and others understand your emotional state, enabling them to offer appropriate support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Answer the physical reactions questions (questions 1-4) as honestly as possible, rating your experiences on a scale (e.g., 0-10, where 0 means no experience and 10 means very severe).
2. Answer the cognitive reactions questions (questions 5-8) as honestly as possible, rating your experiences on the same scale.
3. Provide a written description of your thoughts and feelings in the text fields (questions 9-10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Physical Reactions
* **Physical Reactions (1)** (`physical_reactions`, number, required: false): Rate your level of physical reactions such as pain, fatigue, or changes in appetite since the loss. Answer on the scale of 0-10, where 0 means no experience and 10 means very severe.

### Cognitive Reactions
* **Cognitive Reactions (2)** (`cognitive_reactions`, number, required: false): Rate your level of cognitive reactions such as confusion, difficulty concentrating, or memory loss since the loss. Answer on the scale of 0-10, where 0 means no experience and 10 means very severe.

### Emotional Reactions
* **Emotional Reactions (3)** (`emotional_reactions`, number, required: false): Rate your level of emotional reactions such as sadness, guilt, or anxiety since the loss. Answer on the scale of 0-10, where 0 means no experience and 10 means very severe.

### Behavioral Reactions
* **Behavioral Reactions (4)** (`behavioral_reactions`, number, required: false): Rate your level of behavioral reactions such as changes in sleep, appetite, or energy since the loss. Answer on the scale of 0-10, where 0 means no experience and 10 means very severe.

### Thoughts
* **Thoughts (5)** (`thoughts`, text, required: false): Describe your thoughts and feelings related to the loss. Be as detailed as possible, and provide any specific thoughts, worries, or concerns that have been occurring since the loss.

### Consider Grief
* **Consider Grief (6)** (`consider_grief`, text, required: false): Describe any specific thoughts or considerations you have about the loss and its impact on your life. Be as detailed as possible, and provide any specific concerns or feelings that have been occurring since the loss.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and accurately, without overthinking or underestimating your experiences.
* Rate your reactions on the scale as instructed, where 0 means no experience and 10 means very severe.
* Provide detailed descriptions in the text fields to help healthcare professionals and others better understand your emotional state.
