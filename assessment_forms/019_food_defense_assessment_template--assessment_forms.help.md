# Food Defense Assessment Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for assessing the effectiveness and progress of a food defense program. It helps to identify areas for improvement and ensures that the program is aligned with industry standards and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the program's goals and objectives.
2. List the program's strengths, such as effective procedures and protocols in place.
3. Identify the program's weaknesses, such as areas that need improvement.
4. Assess the risk factors, such as potential contaminants and allergens.
5. Determine the next steps for improvement.
6. Evaluate the program's effectiveness and progress.
7. Set a follow-up date and frequency.
