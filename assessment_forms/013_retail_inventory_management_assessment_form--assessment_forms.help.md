# Retail Inventory Management Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from retail stores about their inventory management practices. It helps assess the efficiency and effectiveness of inventory management systems, allowing stakeholders to identify areas for improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and honest answers to the best of your ability.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled and the information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **store_info** (1, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your store, including its name, location, and relevant details.
* **location** (2, select_one, optional): Select the location of your store from the available options.
* **inventory** (3, number, optional): Enter the current inventory level or quantity of your store.
* **store_hours** (4, time, optional): Enter the store hours of operation, including start and end times.
* **contact** (5, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name and position/title.
* **feedback** (6, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your store's inventory management practices.
* **email** (7, email, optional): Enter your store's email address.
* **phone** (8, text, optional): Enter your store's phone number.
* **submit** (9, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your submission purpose (e.g., assessment, evaluation, survey).
* **store_name** (10, text, optional): Enter the name of your store.
