# Recognition Of Prior Learning (RPL) Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recognition Of Prior Learning (RPL) Assessment Form is used to assess the recognition of prior learning in educational and career contexts. This form is designed to help individuals demonstrate their acquired skills and knowledge gained through non-traditional means, such as workshops, training programs, or online courses. The assessment is used to determine whether these skills and knowledge can be recognized for professional advancement or certification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields have been filled out.
2. For each field, enter the information as prompted, taking care to provide accurate and relevant details.
3. If you are unsure about any field or have questions, contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a designated contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **rpl_applications** (`rpl_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for submitting this RPL assessment form.
* **application_id** (`rpl_application_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for this application.
* **application_date** (`rpl_application_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the prior learning activity.
* **training_provider** (`training_provider`, select_one, optional): Select the name of the organization or institution that provided the training.
* **assessors_name** (`assessors_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the individual who assessed your prior learning.
* **other_assessors_name** (`other_assessors_name`, text, optional): Enter the names of any other individuals who assisted with the assessment.

## Tips
- Be sure to provide clear and accurate information in each field to ensure a successful RPL assessment.
- If you are unsure about the assessment process or have questions, contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a designated contact person.
- This form is intended for individuals who have acquired new skills or knowledge outside of traditional educational settings; if you have questions about traditional educational recognition, contact relevant authorities.
