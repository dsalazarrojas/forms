# Aerial Rescue Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aerial Rescue Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate the skills and performance of a candidate in aerial rescue operations. The form assesses the candidate's ability to identify hazards, prepare for the rescue, and execute the rescue safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the rescue scenario from the options provided in the "Rescue Scenario" field.
2. Evaluate the candidate's performance in each section, marking "Pass" or "Fail" as necessary.
3. Provide a brief explanation for each assessment, if possible.
4. Enter any feedback or comments in the "Assessor Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the candidate being assessed.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the assessor conducting the evaluation.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Rescue Scenario** (`scenario_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the specific rescue scenario being tested.
* **Hazard Assessment** (`hazard_assessment`, select_one, **required**): Evaluate the candidate's ability to identify hazards, marking "Pass" or "Fail".
* **Equipment Check** (`equipment_check`, select_one, **required**): Evaluate the candidate's ability to check equipment properly, marking "Pass" or "Fail".
* **Anchor Building** (`anchor_building`, select_one, **required**): Evaluate the candidate's ability to use secure and appropriate anchors, marking "Pass" or "Fail".
* **Patient Access** (`patient_access`, select_one, **required**): Evaluate the candidate's safe approach to the patient, marking "Pass" or "Fail".
* **Patient Handling** (`patient_handling`, select_one, **required**): Evaluate the candidate's ability to handle the patient safely, marking "Pass" or "Fail".
* **System Operation** (`system_operation`, select_one, **required**): Evaluate the candidate's ability to operate the system effectively, marking "Pass" or "Fail".
* **Completed Within Time Limit** (`time_limit`, select_one, **required**): Evaluate whether the rescue was completed within the time limit, marking "True" or "False".
* **Overall Result** (`overall_result`, select_one, **required**): Provide a final determination of the candidate's skill level, marking "Competent" or "Not Yet Competent".
* **Assessor Feedback** (`feedback`, text, **required**): Provide any feedback or comments for the candidate, including areas for improvement.
