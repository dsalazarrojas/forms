# Boxing and Fitness Client Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boxing and Fitness Client Assessment Form is a comprehensive form used to gather information about the client's health, fitness level, and goals. The form is designed to help trainers create a personalized workout program tailored to the client's needs and abilities. This form should only be completed by the client after consulting with a medical professional if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information (First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, etc.)
2. Choose your gender and select any medical conditions that apply to you.
3. Enter your phone number, email address, and address.
4. List your emergency contact's name and phone number.
5. Set your primary fitness goals (e.g., weight loss, muscle building, stress relief, etc.)
6. Select your boxing experience level.
7. Indicate how often you currently exercise.
8. Choose how you would rate your stress level.
9. If you take medications, list them.
10. Answer questions related to your physical limitations and previous injuries (if applicable).
11. Select your smoking status and drinking habits.
12. Indicate your sleep patterns.
13. Enter your resting heart rate (if known).
14. Record your height and weight in feet/inches or cm/kg.
15. Provide information about your body fat percentage (if known).
16. Enter any medical conditions that you have been diagnosed with.
17. If applicable, provide any results from previous cardiovascular, strength, or flexibility assessments.
18. Enter any additional comments or notes for the trainer.
19. Review and agree to the liability waiver.
20. Confirm that you have received medical clearance to exercise (if required).
21. Sign and date the form as the client.
22. Sign and date the form as the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Client First Name, Text, Required): Please enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (Client Last Name, Text, Required): Please enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (Select One, Required): Select your gender.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your complete home address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Assessment Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Primary Fitness Goals** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all of your primary fitness goals (e.g., weight loss, muscle building, stress relief, etc.).
* **Boxing Experience Level** (Select One, Required): Select your level of boxing experience (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced, etc.).
* **Exercise Frequency** (Select One, Required): Choose how often you currently exercise.
* **Medical Conditions** (Select Multiple, Required): Select any medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Current Medications** (Text, Required): List all medications you are currently taking.
* **Previous Injuries** (Text, Optional): If applicable, list any previous injuries.
* **Physical Limitations** (Text, Optional): If applicable, list any physical limitations.
* **Smoking Status** (Select One, Required): Select your smoking status.
* **Alcohol Consumption** (Select One, Optional): Choose how often you drink alcohol.
* **Sleep Patterns** (Select One, Required): Choose your sleep patterns.
* **Resting Heart Rate** (Number, Optional): If known, enter your resting heart rate.
* **Height** (Text, Required): Enter your height in feet/inches or cm.
* **Weight** (Number, Required): Enter your weight in pounds or kg.
* **Body Fat Percentage** (Number, Optional): If known, enter your body fat percentage.
* **Cardiovascular Assessment** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the results of your cardiovascular assessment.
* **Strength Assessment** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the results of your strength assessment.
* **Flexibility Assessment** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the results of your flexibility assessment.
* **Trainer Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes for the trainer.
* **Program Recommendations** (Text, Optional): If applicable, provide any suggested training programs.
* **Liability Waiver** (Select One, Required): Agree or disagree to the liability waiver.
* **Medical Clearance** (Select One, Required): Confirm if you have received medical clearance to exercise.
* **Client Signature** (Text, Required): Sign your name as the client.
* **Signature Date** (Date, Required): Date of signing.
* **Trainer Signature** (Text, Required): Sign as the trainer.
* **Program Recommendations** (Text, Optional): Suggested training program.

Note that some fields are marked as "Optional", but please fill them out only if necessary or applicable.
