# Service Tiering Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the tiering of a service. It is used to evaluate the support level and priority of a service, as well as make tiering decisions and document next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as prompted. The "Next Steps" field is required and must be selected.
2. Choose the Support Level (High, Medium, or Low) and Priority (Low, Medium, or High) of the service.
3. Make a tiering decision and enter it in the "Tiering Decision" field.
4. Add any comments or notes about the service in the "Comments" field.
5. Select the next steps to be taken for the service from the list in the "Next Steps" field.
6. Enter the date reviewed, date approved, and date completed (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Name** (`service_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the service being assessed.
* **Support Level** (`support_level`, select_one, optional): Select the level of support required for the service (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Select the priority of the service (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Tiering Decision** (`tiering_decision`, text, optional): Enter a decision about the tiering of the service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about the service.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_multiple, required): Select the next steps to be taken for the service.
	+ Create a plan to improve service performance
	+ Monitor and review tiering decisions
	+ Document and store tiering data
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date the service was reviewed.
* **Date Approved** (`date_approved`, date, optional): Enter the date the service was approved.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date the service was completed.
* **Date Due** (`date_due`, date, optional): Enter the date the service is due to be reviewed again.
