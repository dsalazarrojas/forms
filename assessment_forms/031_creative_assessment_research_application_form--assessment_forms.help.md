# Creative Assessment Research Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Creative Assessment Research Application Form is a tool used to collect information from potential applicants for a research project. The form helps researchers gather essential details about the applicants, including their background, contact information, and project specifics. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and an explanation of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Application Form" section (page 1). This section is not required, but it's a good place to begin.
2. Proceed to the "Application Background" section (page 2). Here, you can provide a brief description of your research background and experience.
3. In the "Consent" section (page 3), you will be asked to confirm your consent for the project. Please select "Yes" if you agree to participate.
4. Next, fill out your "Phone Number" and "Email Address" (pages 4-5). These are important contact details for the research team.
5. In the "Candidate Background" (page 6) and "Researcher Background" (page 7) sections, please provide a brief description of your background and experience related to the project.
6. The "University Name" (page 8) and "Lab Name" (page 9) sections are optional but can help the research team understand your affiliation.
7. Finally, provide a detailed description of the project in the "Project Details" (page 10) section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (page 1, optional):
	+ This section is a basic contact information block where you can input your name, title, or any other relevant details.
* **Application Background** (page 2, optional):
	+ This field is a text box for you to describe your background and experience related to the project.
* **Consent** (page 3, optional):
	+ Please select "Yes" to confirm your consent for the project. If you are unsure or have questions, contact the research team.
* **Phone Number** (page 4, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number for the research team to contact you.
* **Email Address** (page 5, optional):
	+ Enter your email address for the research team to contact you.
* **Candidate Background** (page 6, optional):
	+ This field is a text box for you to describe your background and experience related to the project.
* **Researcher Background** (page 7, optional):
	+ This field is a text box for you to describe your background and experience related to the project.
* **University Name** (page 8, optional):
	+ If you are affiliated with a university, please enter the name of your institution.
* **Lab Name** (page 9, optional):
	+ If you are affiliated with a lab, please enter the name of your lab.
* **Project Details** (page 10, optional):
	+ Please provide a detailed description of the project, including objectives, methods, and expected outcomes.
