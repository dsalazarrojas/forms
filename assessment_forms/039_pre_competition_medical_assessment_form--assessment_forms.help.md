# Pre Competition Medical Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Competition Medical Assessment Form is used to gather essential medical information from individuals before participating in a competition, ensuring their health and fitness for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately and completely.
2. Ensure you have the necessary medical clearance before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the competition organizers or medical team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Medical Assessment Form
* **Medical Assessment Form** (`medical_assessment_form`, text, optional): This is the main form section where you can provide detailed information about your current health and medical status.

### Injury History
* **Injury History** (`injury_history`, text, optional): Describe any previous injuries or medical conditions that may impact your participation in the competition.

### Injury Form
* **Injury Form** (`injury_form`, text, optional): Provide more detailed information about any past injuries or medical conditions that require attention.

### Health Declaration
* **Health Declaration** (`health_declaration`, text, optional): Declare any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the competition.

### Medical Certifications
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, text, optional): Attach any relevant medical certificates or documents that support your health declaration.

### Medication List
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking that may interact with the competition environment.

### Allergy List
* **Allergy List** (`allergy_list`, text, optional): Describe any allergies or sensitivities that may impact your participation in the competition.

### Medical History
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a detailed medical history, including any past medical conditions, surgeries, or hospitalizations.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer all questions accurately and completely.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the competition organizers or medical team.
* This form is confidential and only used for medical assessment purposes.
