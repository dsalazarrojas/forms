# digital_skills_self_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your digital skills and provide details about your proficiency in various areas. This includes listing the skills you possess, your level of proficiency in those skills, any additional notes you'd like to share, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Introduction** (`intro`, note, required): This is an optional field where you can provide any background information or context about your digital skills.
2. **List of Skills** (`skills_list`, select_multiple, required): Select all the digital skills you possess. You can choose one or more options from the list provided.
3. **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, required): Choose your level of proficiency in the skills you selected. Options include Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced.
4. **Notes** (`skill_notes`, text, required): Provide any additional notes or comments about your skills.
5. **Knowledge Areas** (`knowledge_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional knowledge areas you'd like to highlight, but this is not required.
6. **Performance Goals** (`performance_goals`, select_one, required): Choose your top performance goal for the digital skills you've selected. This helps us understand your priorities.
7. **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
8. **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, required): Provide a numerical rating out of a certain scale (e.g., 1-10) for your performance.
9. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This is an optional field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
10. **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you submitted this assessment form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This field is for background information or context about your digital skills.
* **List of Skills**: Select all the digital skills you possess from the given options.
* **Skill Level**: Choose your level of proficiency in the skills you selected.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your skills.
* **Knowledge Areas**: Select any additional knowledge areas you'd like to highlight, but this is not required.
* **Performance Goals**: Choose your top performance goal for the digital skills you've selected.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Performance Rating**: Provide a numerical rating out of a certain scale for your performance.
* **Comments**: This field is optional, providing any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when you submitted this assessment form.
