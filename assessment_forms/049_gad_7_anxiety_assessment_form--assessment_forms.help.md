The GAD 7 Anxiety Assessment Form is a tool used by mental health professionals to assess the severity of anxiety in individuals. This form helps to identify the presence and impact of anxiety symptoms, as well as the patient's need for further evaluation or treatment. By using this form, healthcare providers can quickly and efficiently determine the need for anxiety management plans, referrals to specialized care, or follow-up appointments.

This form is used for both initial and follow-up assessments. It is designed to be completed by healthcare providers, but may also be used by patients or caregivers who can provide valuable input on the patient's symptoms and treatment needs.

# GAD 7 Anxiety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to assess the severity of anxiety in an individual, determine the need for further evaluation or treatment, and guide the development of anxiety management plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the patient's medical history and any ongoing treatments or interventions.
2. Complete the form using the guidelines below.
3. Enter your findings and recommendations in the designated fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Anxiety Scale**: This scale assesses the severity of anxiety symptoms over the past two weeks. Rate the patient's symptoms using the scale below:
	+ 0: No anxiety symptoms
	+ 1: Mild anxiety
	+ 2: Moderate anxiety
	+ 3: Severe anxiety
* **Item 1-7**: These questions ask about specific anxiety symptoms. Choose the answer that best describes the patient's symptoms over the past two weeks.
* **Total Score**: The total score is calculated by summing the scores from the anxiety scale and item questions.
* **Clinical Significance**: Provide a brief explanation of the clinical significance of the patient's symptoms.
* **Anxiety Diagnosis**: Determine whether the patient has anxiety or not.
* **Anxiety Referral**: Determine whether the patient requires referral to specialized care.
* **Follow-up Scheduled**: Determine whether a follow-up appointment is scheduled.
* **Anxiety Referral Frequency**: Rate the frequency of anxiety referrals.
* **Anxiety Cause**: Provide a brief explanation of the cause of the patient's anxiety.
* **Anxiety Management Plans**: Provide a brief explanation of the patient's management plans.
* **Follow-up Notes**: Record any notes from the patient's follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Status**: Determine the status of the patient's follow-up appointment.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are using the most up-to-date version of the form.
* Be aware of the patient's medical history and any ongoing treatments or interventions.
* Complete the form using clear and concise language.
* Use the guidelines above to determine the severity of anxiety symptoms and the need for further evaluation or treatment.
