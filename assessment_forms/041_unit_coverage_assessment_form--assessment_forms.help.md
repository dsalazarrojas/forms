# Unit Coverage Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unit Coverage Assessment Form is used to assess the coverage of a unit, gather information about its condition, and track the assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Unit Coverage Assessment Form** field with a brief description of the unit being assessed.
2. In the **Unit Details** field, provide a detailed description of the unit, including its type, size, and any notable features.
3. Select the **Coverage Status** field to indicate whether the unit has coverage or not.
4. In the **Notes** field, add any additional comments or observations about the unit.
5. If required, upload relevant files, such as documents or images, in the **File Upload 1** and **File Upload 2** fields.
6. Enter the relevant dates in the **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 1**, **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 2**, and **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 3** fields.
7. Select multiple options from the **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 4** field to indicate the unit's coverage status.
8. If applicable, enter a valid email address in the **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 5** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Unit Coverage Assessment Form** (Text): A brief description of the unit being assessed.
* **Unit Details** (Text): A detailed description of the unit, including its type, size, and any notable features.
* **Coverage Status** (Select One): Select "True" if the unit has coverage, "False" otherwise.
* **Notes** (Text): Additional comments or observations about the unit.
* **File Upload 1** (Text): Upload a file, such as a document or image, related to the unit's coverage.
* **File Upload 2** (Text): Upload another file, such as a supporting document or image.
* **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 1** (Date): Enter the relevant date for the unit's coverage assessment.
* **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 2** (Time): Enter the relevant time for the unit's coverage assessment.
* **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 3** (Text): Enter any additional information related to the unit's coverage assessment.
* **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 4** (Select Multiple): Select multiple options to indicate the unit's coverage status.
* **Unit Coverage Assessment Form 5** (Email): Enter a valid email address, if applicable.
