# vehicle_safety_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The vehicle_safety_assessment_form is a tool used to collect and document information about the safety features and condition of a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions to the best of your abilities, based on your knowledge of the vehicle's safety features and condition.
2. Fill out the form on one page at a time, using the information provided in each section.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled out and that the information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the vehicle, including its make, model, and year.
* **Safety Standards** (`safety_standards`, select_multiple, required/optional): In this field, select all the safety standards that the vehicle meets. The options are 'Yes' and 'No'. 
* **Condition** (`condition`, text, required/optional): Describe the current condition of the vehicle. This could include any issues or problems that affect its safety.
* **Vehicle Safety Assessment** (`vehicle_safety_assessment`, text, required/optional): This field is used to document any assessments or inspections that have been performed on the vehicle, including any findings or recommendations.
* **Vehicle Inspection Results** (`vehicle_inspection_results`, text, required/optional): In this field, describe the results of any inspections or tests that have been performed on the vehicle.
