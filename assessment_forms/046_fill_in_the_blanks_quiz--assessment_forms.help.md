# fill_in_the_blanks_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fill-in-the-blanks quiz form collects personal preferences and interests from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully and enter the desired information.
2. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (marked as required).
3. Enter multiple selections for fields that allow multiple selections (e.g., Favorite Color, Favorite Band2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (5, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Of Birth** (6, time, optional): Enter your time of birth in the format hh:mm am/pm.
* **Favorite Food** (7, select_one, optional): Select your favorite food from the options apple, banana, or orange.
* **Favorite Color** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple favorite colors from the options red, blue, and green.
* **Favorite Animal** (9, select_one, required): Select your favorite animal from the options lion, elephant, or giraffe.
* **Favorite Hobby** (10, text, optional): Enter your favorite hobby.
* **Favorite Language** (11, select_one, required): Select your favorite language from the options English, Spanish, or French.
* **Favorite Food 1** (12, select_one, optional): Select your favorite food from the options pizza, sushi, or tacos.
* **Favorite Food 2** (13, select_multiple, required): Select multiple favorite foods from the options ice cream, cake, and donut.
* **Favorite Music** (14, text, optional): Enter your favorite music.
* **Favorite Book** (15, text, optional): Enter your favorite book.
* **Favorite Movie** (16, text, optional): Enter your favorite movie.
* **Favorite Game** (17, text, optional): Enter your favorite game.
* **Favorite Band** (18, text, optional): Enter your favorite band.
* **Favorite Band1** (19, select_one, required): Select your favorite band from the options Beatles, Rolling Stones, or Queen.
* **Favorite Band2** (20, select_multiple, required): Select multiple favorite bands from the options Metallica, Led Zeppelin, or AC/DC.
* **Favorite Band3** (21, select_one, optional): Select your favorite band from the options Pink, Maroon 5, or Adele.
* **Favorite Song** (22, text, optional): Enter your favorite song.
* **Favorite Artist** (23, text, optional): Enter your favorite artist.
* **Favorite Band4** (24, text, optional): Enter your favorite band.
* **Favorite Band5** (25, text, optional): Enter your favorite band.

Note: Please ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
