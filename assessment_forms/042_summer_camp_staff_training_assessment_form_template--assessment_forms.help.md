# Summer Camp Staff Training Assessment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the training and performance of summer camp staff. It helps to evaluate the effectiveness of the training program and identify areas for improvement. Complete this form to provide valuable feedback on staff training.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Staff Training Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter a brief description of the training session. Please type in your answers in the text field provided.
2. **Assessment Scale**: This is an optional field to rate the training session on a scale from 1 to 5. If you don't think the session was effective, please leave this field blank.
3. **Topics Covered**: Select all the topics that were covered during the training session from the provided options. If none of the topics were covered, select "No".
4. **Training Hours**: If you have information on the total number of hours spent on training, please enter it here. This field is optional.
5. **Evaluation Questions**: Select all the evaluation questions that were asked during the training session from the provided options. If none were asked, select "No".
6. **Evaluation Scale**: This is an optional field to rate the overall evaluation of the training session. Select from the provided options.
7. **Feedback Comments**: This is an optional field for providing additional feedback or comments on the training session. Please type your answer in the text field provided.
8. **Evaluation Notes**: Select all the evaluation notes that were made during the training session from the provided options. If none were made, select "No".
9. **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field for providing any additional comments or suggestions on the training session. Please type your answer in the text field provided.
10. **Topics Covered Scale**: This is an optional field to rate the topics covered during the training session on a scale from 1 to 5. If you don't think the topics were covered effectively, please leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please answer all the required fields to ensure you provide a comprehensive assessment of the training session.
* You can skip optional fields if you don't think they are relevant to your assessment.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field to provide them.
* Make sure to rate the "Topics Covered Scale" only if you have a clear understanding of the topics covered during the training session.
* Remember to provide specific examples or reasons for your answers to make your assessment more effective.
