# Long Covid Care Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Long Covid Care Assessment form is designed to help identify an individual's care needs, assess their symptoms, and provide support and resources tailored to their situation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select the relevant options for each field according to your situation.
3. Provide dates for follow-up interactions or referrals when applicable.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Long Covid Care Assessment** (`1`, text, required: false)
Please provide a brief description of your long Covid care needs.

### **Long Covid Symptoms** (`2`, text, required: false)
Describe your long Covid symptoms.

### **Long Covid Symptom Frequency** (`3`, select_one, required: false)
Select the frequency of your long Covid symptoms:
* Less than 1 day
* 1-2 days
* 3-7 days
* More than 7 days

### **Long Covid Symptom Severity** (`4`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the severity of your long Covid symptoms:
* Mild
* Moderate
* Severe

### **Long Covid Care Needs** (`5`, text, required: false)
Describe your long Covid care needs.

### **Long Covid Medication Use** (`6`, number, required: false)
Please enter the number of medications you are currently taking.

### **Long Covid Vaccine Status** (`7`, select_one, required: false)
Select your current vaccination status:
* Not yet vaccinated
* Fully vaccinated
* Booster dose received
* Partially vaccinated

### **Long Covid Test Result** (`8`, select_one, required: false)
Select your test result:
* Negative
* Positive

### **Long Covid Care Service Needs** (`9`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the care services you need:
* Mental health support
* Medical care
* Home care
* Rehabilitation programs
* Others

### **Long Covid Medication Referral** (`10`, select_one, required: false)
Select if you've received a medication referral:
* 'Yes'
* 'No'

### **Long Covid Medication Referral Date** (`11`, date, required: false)
Provide the date of medication referral if applicable.

### **Long Covid Care Referral** (`12`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the location and provider of your long Covid care referral:
* None
* Clinic
* Rehabilitation program
* Other health services

### **Long Covid Care Referral Date** (`13`, date, required: false)
Provide the date of your long Covid care referral if applicable.

### **Long Covid Support Services Referral** (`14`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the support services you need:
* Mental health support
* Medical care
* Home care
* Rehabilitation programs
* Others

### **Long Covid Support Services Referral Date** (`15`, date, required: false)
Provide the date of your long Covid support services referral if applicable.

### **Long Covid Rehabilitation Program** (`16`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select the type of rehabilitation program you are or have been in:
* In-person
* Remote
* Both

### **Long Covid Referral Location** (`17`, select_one, required: false)
Select the location of your long Covid referral:
* Hospital
* Clinic
* Rehabilitation program
* Other

### **Long Covid Referral Provider** (`18`, select_one, required: false)
Select the provider of your long Covid referral:
* Doctor
* Nurse
* Social worker
* Other

### **Long Covid Follow-up Date** (`19`, date, required: false)
Provide the date of your last follow-up interaction if applicable.

### **Long Covid Last Interaction** (`20`, select_one, required: false)
Select if you have been in contact with healthcare services:
* 'True'
* 'False'

### **Long Covid Last Interaction Date** (`21`, date, required: false)
Provide the date of your last interaction with healthcare services if applicable.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider or a relevant support service.
* This form is intended for individuals who have experienced prolonged Covid-19 symptoms or have been impacted by the virus in some way.
* The information gathered will be used to provide support and resources tailored to your situation.
