# Fleet Insurance Driver Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a driver and their vehicle insurance policy for risk assessment and coverage determination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Enter your name and contact details in the **Full Name** field.
3. Provide your vehicle's make, model, and registration information in the **Vehicle Information** field.
4. If applicable, describe your vehicle's maintenance history and any existing insurance policies in the **Vehicle Maintenance** and **Vehicle Insurance** fields.
5. List your previous employers and their relevant details in the **Previous Employers** field.
6. Evaluate your risk level by selecting one of the options provided in the **Risk Evaluation** field.
7. Specify how you use your vehicle (e.g. personal, commercial, etc.) in the **Vehicle Use** field.
8. In the **Vehicle Insurance** field, describe any existing insurance policies you have for your vehicle.
9. Provide details about any incidents or accidents involving your vehicle in the **Incident Details** field.
10. Select your current driver's license status from the options provided in the **Driver's License Status** field.
11. Enter any additional information relevant to your driving history or insurance needs in the **Additional Information 1**, **Additional Information 2**, and **Additional Information 3** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): This field collects your name and contact details.
* **Vehicle Information** (Optional): Enter your vehicle's make, model, and registration information.
* **Vehicle Maintenance** (Optional): Describe your vehicle's maintenance history.
* **Previous Employers** (Optional): List your previous employers and their relevant details.
* **Risk Evaluation** (Required): Evaluate your risk level by selecting one of the options provided.
* **Vehicle Use** (Optional): Specify how you use your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (Optional): Describe any existing insurance policies you have for your vehicle.
* **Incident Details** (Required): Provide details about any incidents or accidents involving your vehicle.
* **Driver's License Status** (Required): Select your current driver's license status from the options provided.
* **Additional Information 1**, **Additional Information 2**, and **Additional Information 3** (Optional): Enter any additional information relevant to your driving history or insurance needs.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and truthful as possible when completing this form.
* If you're unsure about any information, please consult your vehicle's registration or speak with your insurance provider.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your fleet manager or insurance representative.
