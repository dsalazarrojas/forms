# office_software_proficiency_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The office software proficiency assessment form is used to evaluate an individual's proficiency in office software, including Microsoft Office, to provide feedback or training recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User ID, which is required for tracking and referencing your assessment results.
2. Answer the office software proficiency assessment questions, which will help us understand your current level of expertise.
3. Select your proficiency level in office software, which will help us understand your strengths and areas for improvement.
4. Identify your office software skills, which will help us understand what you're proficient in.
5. Enter the date of your assessment, which will help us track your progress over time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (`user_id`, `number`, required): Enter your unique User ID, which is required for tracking and referencing your assessment results.
* **Office Software Proficiency Assessment** (`office_software_proficiency_assessment`, `text`, required): This field is for providing additional context or comments about your office software proficiency assessment.
* **Software Proficiency Assessment** (`software_proficiency_assessment`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your assessment, which will help us track your progress over time.
* **Office Software Proficiency** (`office_software_proficiency`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you are proficient in office software or "No" to indicate you are not proficient.
* **Office Software Skills** (`office_software_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select the office software skills you are proficient in, such as "Software", "Office", "Application", "Skills", "Microsoft", "Word", "Excel", "Outlook", or "Access".
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your assessment, which will help us track your progress over time.
* **Software Skills Assessment** (`software_skills_assessment`, `select_one`, required): Select "Office", "Software", "Application", "Skills", "Proficiency".
* **Office Software Proficiency 2** (`office_software_proficiency_2`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" to indicate you are proficient in office software or "No" to indicate you are not proficient.
* **Office Software Proficiency 3** (`office_software_proficiency_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the office software skills you are proficient in, such as "Yes" for "Software", "Office", "Application", "Skills", "Microsoft", "Word", "Excel", "Outlook", or "Access".
