# self_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-assessment questionnaire is a form used to gather information from users about their self-assessment process. It helps us understand your current situation, goals, and next steps to improve the system and provide better support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the purpose of this form.
2. Answer the questions and provide as much detail as possible about your current situation, goals, and next steps.
3. Fill in your email and phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select the "submit" button to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Welcome to the self-assessment questionnaire
This page is just an introduction to the form. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### 2. What motivates you
This question is meant to understand what drives you to complete this self-assessment questionnaire. Try to be as specific as possible and provide details about your goals and aspirations.

### 3. What do you want to self-assess
This is a multiple-choice question where you can select one or more options that apply to your situation. Please choose all that apply.

* 'Yes'
* 'No'

### 4. Goals
This question is meant to help us understand what you're trying to achieve. Please provide as much detail as possible about your goals.

### 5. Current Situation
This field is a space for you to describe your current situation in as much detail as possible.

### 6. Needs
This is a text field where you can list any needs or challenges you're currently facing.

### 7. Next steps
This field is for you to outline any next steps you plan to take or actions you'll take to address your current situation.

### 8. Additional comments
This is an optional field for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the system.

### 9. Email
This is your email address where we'll contact you for follow-up questions or to provide support.

### 10. Phone
This is your phone number where we'll contact you for follow-up questions or to provide support.

### 11. Date (optional)
If you're completing this form in person, this is the date of the self-assessment.

### 12. Time (optional)
If you're completing this form in person, this is the time of the self-assessment.

### 13. Submit
This is the final button to submit your feedback and complete the form. Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting.
