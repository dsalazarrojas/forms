# ICH Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ICH Risk Assessment Form is a tool used by medical professionals to evaluate patients' risk profiles based on various factors. It gathers information about the patient's medical history, symptoms, and treatment outcomes to support treatment decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name and condition.
2. Evaluate the patient's ICH-VM3, ICH-VM4, and ICH-VM5 scores based on their medical history and symptoms.
3. Select the patient's condition from the dropdown list.
4. List the patient's risk factors.
5. Enter the patient's dose and weight information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their medical record.
* **ICH-VM3 Score** (`icv3_score`): Enter a number value for the patient's ICH-VM3 score, which is used to assess the severity of their condition.
* **ICH-VM3 Score Hint** (`icv3_score_hint`): Enter any relevant information or notes about the patient's ICH-VM3 score.
* **ICH-VM4 Score** (`icv4_score`): Enter a number value for the patient's ICH-VM4 score, which is used to assess the severity of their condition.
* **ICH-VM4 Score Hint** (`icv4_score_hint`): Enter any relevant information or notes about the patient's ICH-VM4 score.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`): Select the patient's condition from the list provided.
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`): Select all risk factors that apply to the patient, such as Age, History of Stroke, Family History, or Other.
* **ICH-VM5 Score** (`icv5_score`): Enter a number value for the patient's ICH-VM5 score, which is used to assess the severity of their condition.
* **ICH-VM5 Score Hint** (`icv5_score_hint`): Enter any relevant information or notes about the patient's ICH-VM5 score.
* **Patient Dose** (`patient_dose`): Enter the patient's dose information, including any relevant medications or treatments.
* **Patient Weight** (`patient_weight`): Enter the patient's weight information, including any relevant medical history or treatments.
* **Patient Dose Hint** (`patient_dose_hint`): Enter any relevant information or notes about the patient's dose.
* **Patient Weight Hint** (`patient_weight_hint`): Enter any relevant information or notes about the patient's weight.
