**Form Design and Purpose Review**
This form is intended to collect information on risk assessments for ISO 27001 compliance. The form is designed to guide users in identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks to the organization's assets, data, and operations. The form's primary purpose is to provide a structured approach to risk assessment, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and documented.

**ISO 27001 Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The ISO 27001 Risk Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool designed to help you identify, assess, and mitigate risks to your organization's assets, data, and operations. This form guides you through a structured risk assessment process, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and documented.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the General Information section, which includes your name, email, and phone number.
2. In the Risk Assessment section, select the priority of the risk (High, Medium, Low).
3. Provide a brief description of the risk.
4. Determine the risk level (High, Medium, Low) and type (Physical, Cyber, Data).
5. Specify the risk frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
6. Assess the risk probability (High, Medium, Low).
7. Identify the risk owner and any relevant risk action or review date.
8. Add any additional comments or notes.
9. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: This section is for your contact information. Please fill in your name, email, and phone number.
* **Risk Assessment (2)**: Select the priority of the risk from the options provided (High, Medium, Low).
* **Priority (3)**: This field is also part of the risk assessment section. Select the priority of the risk from the options provided (High, Medium, Low).
* **Description (4)**: Provide a brief description of the risk.
* **Risk Level (5)**: Determine the risk level (High, Medium, Low).
* **Risk Type (6)**: Identify the type of risk (Physical, Cyber, Data).
* **Risk Frequency (7)**: Specify how often the risk occurs (Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Risk Probability (8)**: Assess the risk probability (High, Medium, Low).
* **Risk Owner (9)**: Identify who owns the risk.
* **Risk Action (10)**: If applicable, specify any risk action or review date.
* **Review Date (11)**: If applicable, specify the review date.
* **Review By (12)**: If applicable, specify the review by date.
* **Note (13)**: Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Email (14)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (15)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Category (16)**: Identify the category of the risk.
* **Subcategory (17)**: Identify the subcategory of the risk.
* **Asset Tag (18)**: Enter any relevant asset tag.
* **Risk Rating (19)**: Enter a risk rating from 0 to 5.
* **Risk Rating Scale (20)**: Enter a description of the risk rating.
* **Risk Rating Description (21)**: Enter any additional information about the risk rating.
* **Control (22)**: Identify any relevant control measures.
* **Control Type (23)**: Identify the type of control measures.
* **Control Effectiveness (24)**: Describe the effectiveness of the control measures.
* **Comments (25)**: Add any additional comments or notes.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the ISO 27001 Risk Assessment Form effectively and accurately, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and documented.
