# reverse_osmosis_plant_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reverse Osmosis Plant Assessment form is used to gather information about the status, performance, and maintenance of the Reverse Osmosis Plant. This form is essential for the facility team to monitor and maintain the plant's performance and for operators to document their findings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information about the Reverse Osmosis Plant.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Choose the correct options for each field based on your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RO Plant Summary** (`ro_plant_summary`, text, required): A brief summary of the Reverse Osmosis Plant's status and performance.
* **Water Temperature** (`water_temperature`, number, optional): The current water temperature reading.
* **Osmosis Plant Performance** (`osmosis_plant_performance`, select_one, required): Select the current performance of the plant (Plant 1, Plant 2, or Plant 3).
* **Maintenance Findings** (`maintenance_findings`, text, required): Any notable maintenance issues or concerns.
* **Assessment Data** (`assessment_data`, select_multiple, required): Select any relevant assessment data (Value 1, Value 2, or Value 3).
* **RO Plant Status** (`ro_plant_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of the plant (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Any additional comments or notes about the plant.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Choose who submitted this assessment (Operator 1, Operator 2, or Operator 3).
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, required): The date this assessment was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, required): The time this assessment was submitted.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Operator Signature** (`operator_signature`, select_one, required): Select your signature (Signature 1, Signature 2, or Signature 3).
* **Operator Comment** (`operator_comment`, text, optional): Any comments from the operator.
* **Engineer Signature** (`engineer_signature`, select_one, optional): Select the engineer's signature (Signature 1, Signature 2, or Signature 3).
* **Engineer Comment** (`engineer_comment`, text, optional): Any comments from the engineer.
* **Facility Team Signature** (`facility_team_signature`, select_one, optional): Select the facility team's signature (Signature 1, Signature 2, or Signature 3).
* **Facility Team Comment** (`facility_team_comment`, text, optional): Any comments from the facility team.
* **RO Plant Equipment** (`ro_plant_equipment`, select_one, required): Select the equipment type (Equipment 1, Equipment 2, or Equipment 3).
* **RO Plant Location** (`ro_plant_location`, select_one, required): Select the location (Location 1, Location 2, or Location 3).
* **RO Plant Description** (`ro_plant_description`, text, required): A brief description of the Reverse Osmosis Plant.
* **Osmosis Plant Capacity** (`osmosis_plant_capacity`, number, required): The current capacity of the plant.
* **Osmosis Plant Age** (`osmosis_plant_age`, number, required): The age of the plant.
* **Osmosis Plant Maintenance Schedule** (`osmosis_plant_maintenance_schedule`, date, required): The scheduled maintenance date.
* **Osmosis Plant Maintenance Type** (`osmosis_plant_maintenance_type`, select_one, required): Select the maintenance type (Maintenance 1, Maintenance 2, or Maintenance 3).
