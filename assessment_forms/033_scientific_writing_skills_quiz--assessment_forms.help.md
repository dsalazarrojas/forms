# Scientific Writing Skills Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scientific Writing Skills Quiz is designed to assess your skills in scientific writing. This quiz is meant to evaluate your ability to understand and apply scientific writing principles. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the questions carefully and choose the answers that best reflect your skills.
2. For the select_multiple and select_one type questions, select one or more options that apply to you.
3. For the select_one type questions, select only one option that best reflects your skills.
4. Fill in the required fields (e.g., section6 and section9) with numerical values.
5. Submit your answers by clicking the "Submit" button.
