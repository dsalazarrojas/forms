# Financial Planning Client Assessment Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Financial Planning Client Assessment Form Template is designed to help financial advisors and planners gather detailed information about a client's financial situation, goals, and objectives. This form will be used to create a comprehensive financial plan tailored to the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each section of the form in order, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Do not skip any sections, as each section is essential to creating a complete financial plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Financial Planning Form** (`financial_planning_form`, Text, Required):
  + This section is the introduction to the form and asks the client to provide their name and contact information.
  + Please enter your full name and contact details to ensure that we can reach out to you with any further questions or follow-ups.
* **Income Details** (`income_details`, Text, Required):
  + In this section, please provide a detailed breakdown of your annual income, including all sources of income such as salary, investments, and any other regular income sources.
* **Assets Owned** (`assets_owned`, Text, Required):
  + In this section, please list all your assets, including savings, investments, retirement accounts, and any other assets of value.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`, Text, Required):
  + This section is where you can specify your short-term and long-term financial goals, such as saving for a down payment on a house or retirement.
  + Be as specific as possible when stating your financial goals.
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`, Text, Required):
  + Please provide an account of your financial history, including any major transactions or changes that may impact your financial situation.
* **Investment Goals** (`investment_goals`, Text, Required):
  + In this section, please specify your investment goals, such as growing your wealth over time or generating passive income.
* **Investment Objects** (`investment_objects`, Text, Required):
  + In this section, please list any specific investment objects you may be considering or have invested in, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
* **Financial Vehicles** (`financial_vehicles`, Text, Required):
  + Please list any financial vehicles, such as cars or boats, that you own or are considering purchasing.
* **Financial Partners** (`financial_partners`, Text, Required):
  + In this section, please identify any financial partners or co-owners you may have.
* **Financial Dependents** (`financial_dependents`, Text, Required):
  + Please list any financial dependents, such as children or family members, that rely on you financially.
* **Financial Planning Recommendations** (`financial_planning_recommendations`, Note, Not Required):
  + This section is where you can provide any additional comments or insights about your financial situation.
  + Be as detailed as possible, as this will help us better understand your needs.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, Note, Not Required):
  + In this section, please provide any further steps you would like to take to move forward with your financial plan.
  + Be specific and detailed in your suggestions.
* **Financial Plan** (`financial_plan`, Note, Not Required):
  + This section is where you can outline your current financial plan and any specific financial objectives you have.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, Note, Not Required):
  + Please use this section to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about your financial plan.
* **Review Actions** (`review_actions`, Select, Not Required):
  + In this section, you can specify the actions you would like us to take next with your financial plan.
  + Please select one of the options provided.
