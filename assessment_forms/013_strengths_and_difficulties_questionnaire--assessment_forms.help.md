# Strengths And Difficulties Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strengths And Difficulties Questionnaire is a tool to help assess an individual's personality, behavior, and social interactions. It is designed to identify an individual's strengths and difficulties in various areas, providing valuable insights for personal and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully, and select the options that best describe your personality, behavior, and social interactions.
2. For select_multiple fields, you can choose multiple options that apply to you.
3. For select_one fields, choose one option that best fits your description.
4. Enter your answers in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Social behaviors**: Describe how you generally interact with others in social situations. 
* **Interpersonal distance**: Rate the distance you maintain with others, on a scale (e.g., 0-10).
* **Interpersonality**: Select how you generally interact with others: Agreeable, Assertive, or Conscientious.
* **Intrapersonal (strengths)**: Choose the strengths you have, such as Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.
* **Intrapersonal (difficulties)**: Select the difficulties you face, such as Agreeable, Conscientious, or Assertive (Note: This field is not the same as interpersonal distance).
* **Interpersonal (strengths)**: Select how you generally interact with others: Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.
* **Intrapersonal (difficulties)**: Select the difficulties you face, such as Agreeable, Conscientious, or Assertive (Note: This field is not the same as interpersonal distance).
* **Social behaviors (strengths)**: Choose the strengths you have in social interactions, such as Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.
* **Social behaviors (difficulties)**: Select the difficulties you face, such as Agreeable, Conscientious, or Assertive.
* **Emotional regulation**: This field is currently not applicable as it's a note type.
* **Self-concept**: Describe how you view yourself.
* **Self-concept (strengths)**: Select how you view yourself, such as Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.
* **Self-concept (difficulties)**: Select the difficulties you face in how you view yourself, such as Agreeable, Conscientious, or Assertive.
* **Self-image prophets**: Enter a description of how you see yourself.
* **Self-image (strengths)**: Select how you see yourself, such as Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.
* **Self-image (difficulties)**: Select the difficulties you face in how you see yourself, such as Agreeable, Conscientious, or Assertive.
* **Social skills**: Describe your social skills.
* **Social skills (strengths)**: Choose your social skills, such as Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.
* **Reported behavioral skills**: Rate your behavioral skills on a scale (e.g., 0-10).
* **Reported behavioral skills (strengths)**: Select your reported behavioral skills, such as Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.
* **Reported behavioral skills (difficulties)**: Select the difficulties you face in your reported behavioral skills, such as Agreeable, Conscientious, or Assertive.
* **Behavioral skills**: This field is currently not applicable.
* **Behavioral skills (strengths)**: Choose your behavioral skills, such as Active, Ambitious, or Adventurous.

Please note that the following fields are not applicable or currently empty: Emotional regulation and behavioral skills fields.

Tips:
* Be honest and accurate when responding to each question.
* Take your time to think carefully before selecting each option.
* If you're unsure about an option, choose the one that best fits your description.

This guide is intended to help you complete the Strengths And Difficulties Questionnaire form accurately and effectively. If you have any further questions or need clarification, please contact your administrator or support team.
