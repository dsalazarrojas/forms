# Expectations Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expectations Assessment Survey is designed to assess an individual's expectations and experiences. This form gathers information about expectations, suggestions, and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form in the following sequence:
	* First page: 
		- **First Name** (text input): Enter your first name.
		- **Last Name** (text input): Enter your last name.
		- **Email** (email input): Enter your email address.
		- **Phone Number** (text input): Enter your phone number.
	* Second page: 
		- **Date of Birth** (date input): Choose your date of birth.
		- **Time of Birth** (time input): Choose your time of birth.
		- **Comments** (text input): Enter any comments.
	* Third page: 
		- **Expectations** (multiple select input): Select all the expectations that apply.
		- **Suggestions** (text input): Enter any suggestions.
	* Fourth page: 
		- **Assigned Survey** (select one input): Select the survey assigned to you.
		- **Expected Outcome** (text input): Enter the expected outcome.
		- **Improvement Plan** (text input): Enter an improvement plan.
		- **Feedback** (text input): Enter any feedback.
	* Fifth page: 
		- **Submit** (note input): Review your answers and submit the form.
		- **Category** (select one input): Select the category for the form.
		- **Form ID** (number input): Enter the form ID.
		- **Description** (text input): Enter a description.
		- **Output File** (text input): Enter the output file.
		- **Assigned Tool** (text input): Enter the assigned tool.
		- **Form Category** (text input): Enter the form category.
		- **Form Description** (text input): Enter the form description.
		- **Form Title** (text input): Enter the form title.
		- **Form ID** (text input): Enter the form ID.
		- **Form Version** (text input): Enter the form version.
		- **Form Status** (select one input): Select the form status.
* Submit the form after filling out all the pages.
