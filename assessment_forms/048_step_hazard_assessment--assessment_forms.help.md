# Step Hazard Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Step Hazard Assessment form is designed to identify and document hazards on a specific step of the process. This form helps in identifying potential safety risks and creating a plan to mitigate them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Evaluate the step of the process you are assessing and identify any potential hazards.
2. Complete each field as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hazard Rating** (1. Hazard rating): This field allows you to rate the severity of the hazard on a scale of 1 (least severe) to a high value (most severe).
* **Stairway Condition** (2. Stairway condition): Select the condition of the stairway from the options provided (Good, Fair, Poor). This helps in understanding the current state of the stairway.
* **Hazard Description** (3. Hazard description): Provide a detailed description of the hazard present on the step.
* **Recommendations** (4. Recommendations): List any recommendations for mitigation or corrective actions to address the hazard.
* **Action Plan** (5. Action Plan): Outline any plans or actions to be taken to address the hazard.
* **Facility Location** (6. Facility Location): Provide the location of the facility where the hazard was identified.
* **Date Completed** (7. Date completed): Enter the date when the assessment was completed.
* **Completed by** (8. Completed by): Enter the name of the person who completed the assessment.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you complete all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
