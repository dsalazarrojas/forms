# Nutrition And Lifestyle Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user information and data on various health-related topics such as diet preferences, medical conditions, and physical activity. This data will help health professionals provide personalized advice and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your accurate and honest information.
2. For each field, please provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Information** (Text, Optional)
User Information is a field where you can provide any additional details about yourself that you feel is relevant to your health status.

### 2. **Diet Preferences** (Select Multiple, Optional)
Diet Preferences is a field where you can select your preferred diet from the options provided: Apple, Pear, Cherry, Apricot, and Peach.

### 3. **Medical Conditions** (Text, Optional)
Medical Conditions is a field where you can provide any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.

### 4. **Medications** (Text, Optional)
Medications is a field where you can list any medications you are currently taking or have taken in the past.

### 5. **Physical Activity** (Text, Optional)
Physical Activity is a field where you can describe your current physical activity level or any physical activities you are currently participating in.

### 6. **Sleep Patterns** (Text, Optional)
Sleep Patterns is a field where you can describe your current sleep patterns, including your sleep schedule and any sleep disorders you may have.

### 7. **Nutrition Goals** (Text, Optional)
Nutrition Goals is a field where you can describe your short-term and long-term nutrition goals, such as weight loss or weight gain.

### 8. **Allergies** (Text, Optional)
Allergies is a field where you can list any allergies or sensitivities you have.

### 9. **Medical History** (Text, Optional)
Medical History is a field where you can describe any significant medical events or surgeries you have undergone in the past.

### 10. **Family History** (Text, Optional)
Family History is a field where you can provide information about any notable medical history among your family members.

### 11. **Social History** (Text, Optional)
Social History is a field where you can describe any social factors that may impact your health status.

### 12. **Lab Results** (Text, Optional)
Lab Results is a field where you can upload any relevant lab results, such as blood test results or medical imaging reports.

### 13. **Physical Measurements** (Text, Optional)
Physical Measurements is a field where you can report any relevant physical measurements, such as blood pressure or body mass index.

### 14. **Health Insurance** (Text, Optional)
Health Insurance is a field where you can provide information about your current health insurance coverage.

### 15. **Contact Information** (Text, Optional)
Contact Information is a field where you can provide any relevant contact information, such as email or phone number.
