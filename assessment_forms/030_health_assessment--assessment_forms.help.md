# Health Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your current health status, covering physical health, sleep habits, and personal details. It helps us understand your overall well-being and provides valuable insights for further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current physical health status from the options provided for each question.
2. Answer the questions about your sleep habits and select the most accurate option.
3. If required, enter your date of birth and select your bedtime.
4. If required, provide your email address.
5. You can add any additional comments or information you'd like to discuss in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Physical Health** (`page_1`, select_one, required): Rate your current physical health status as Good, Fair, or Poor.
- **Sleep Habits** (`page_2`, select_one, required): Select the option that best describes how often you get enough sleep.
- **Date of Birth** (`page_3`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Time** (`page_4`, time, required): Select your usual bedtime in 12-hour format (e.g., 8:00 PM).
- **Medical History** (`page_5`, note, optional): If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please provide details.
- **Email** (`page_6`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Contact Information** (`page_7`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please share them here.
- **Additional Comments** (`page_8`, text, optional): Is there anything else you'd like to add or discuss?
