# Muscle Action Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Muscle Action Assessment Form is designed to collect information about a participant's muscle action assessment, including participant demographics, session type, and assessment type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Info" section, providing the participant's details.
2. Choose the type of session (New Participant/Returning Participant).
3. Provide the assessment date (mm/dd/yy).
4. Select the type of assessment (Muscle Action, Range of Motion, Posture, Strength, or Endurance).
5. If necessary, provide additional notes or signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info (2)**: This section requires demographic information about the participant.
* **Session Type (4)**: Choose whether the participant is a New Participant or a Returning Participant.
* **Assessment Date (3)**: Enter the date of the assessment in mm/dd/yy format.
* **Assessment Type (7)**: Select the type of assessment to be performed (Muscle Action, Range of Motion, Posture, Strength, or Endurance).
* **Notes (8)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the assessment.
* **Participant Signature (9)**: Sign and date the form as the participant.
* **Trainer Signature (10)**: Sign and date the form as the trainer.
* **Date Signed (11)**: Record the date the form was signed.
