# Cyst Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cyst Assessment Form is designed for medical professionals to collect and record patient information related to cysts. This form helps gather essential details for diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete all required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2.  Ensure accurate and up-to-date information is entered for each field.
3.  Use the "Select One" or "Select Multiple" fields as per the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Name** (`patient_name`): Enter the patient's full name as recorded in their medical records.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the patient's date of birth in the standard format (dd/mm/yyyy or mm/dd/yyyy).
*   **Gender** (`gender`): Select the patient's gender from the provided options.
*   **Age** (`age`): Enter the patient's age in numbers (e.g., 25).
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the patient's primary contact number.
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter the patient's primary email address.
*   **Address** (`address`): Enter the patient's current address.
*   **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`): Enter the patient's medical record number.
*   **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`): Enter the primary treating doctor's name.
*   **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`): Enter the doctor's contact number.
*   **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`): Enter the doctor's email address.
*   **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`): Enter the doctor's address.
*   **Cyst Type** (`cyst_type`): Select the type of cyst from the provided options (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
*   **Cyst Location** (`cyst_location`): Select the location of the cyst from the provided options (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
*   **Cyst Description** (`cyst_description`): Enter a brief description of the cyst, including size, shape, and color.
*   **Cyst Diagnosis** (`cyst_diagnosis`): Enter the diagnosis or suspected diagnosis.
*   **Cyst Treatment** (`cyst_treatment`): Enter the treatment options or plans for the cyst.
*   **Cyst Outcomes** (`cyst_outcomes`): Record the treatment outcomes or results.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Add any relevant comments or notes about the patient's condition.
