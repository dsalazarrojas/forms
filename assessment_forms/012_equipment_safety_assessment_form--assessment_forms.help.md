# Equipment Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the safety status of a piece of equipment in an organizational setting. It helps in evaluating the equipment's condition, potential safety risks, and overall safety rating. The form is a critical tool for ensuring the safety and well-being of employees and stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment being assessed. Choose from "None", "Equipment", "Machinery", or "Tools".
2. **Location**: Select the location where the equipment is currently situated. Choose from "Office", "Warehouse", "Factory", or "Other".
3. **Description**: Briefly describe the equipment being assessed.
4. **Condition**: Evaluate the condition of the equipment by selecting one or more of the following options: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
5. **Safety Risks**: Identify any potential safety risks associated with the equipment.
6. **Corrective Actions**: If any corrective actions are necessary, provide them in this field.
7. **Certification**: Indicate whether the equipment has been certified or not.
8. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or observations about the equipment.
9. **Date Completed**: Record the date when the assessment was completed.
10. **Completed By**: Enter the name of the person who completed the assessment.
11. **Reviewer ID**: Enter the ID of the reviewer who conducted the assessment.
12. **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
13. **Equipment Status**: Select the current status of the equipment: "In Use", "Not In Use", "Decommissioned", or "Removed".
14. **Review Date**: Record the date when the review was completed.
15. **Reviewer Department**: Enter the department of the reviewer.
16. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or observations about the equipment.
17. **Last Equipment Inspection**: Record the date of the last equipment inspection.
18. **Next Equipment Inspection**: Schedule the date of the next equipment inspection.
19. **Last Equipment Maintenance**: Record the date of the last equipment maintenance.
20. **Next Equipment Maintenance**: Schedule the date of the next equipment maintenance.
21. **Next Equipment Overhaul**: Schedule the date of the next equipment overhaul.
22. **Next Equipment Retirement**: Schedule the date of the next equipment retirement.
23. **Equipment Location**: Provide the location of the equipment.
24. **Safety Rating**: Evaluate the overall safety rating of the equipment by selecting one of the following: "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
