# Study Data Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Study Data Quality Assessment Form is used to assess the quality of a study. This form helps the reviewer evaluate the study's data and provide feedback to the researchers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Please fill in the required fields as best as you can.
*   Review each field to ensure you are providing accurate information.
*   You may not need to fill out all the fields, but please ensure you complete all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is the study title?** (`study_title`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the title of the study.
    Please provide the title of the study.
*   **Who is the researcher?** (`researcher`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the name of the researcher.
    Please provide the name of the researcher.
*   **What is the researcher's name?** (`researcher_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the name of the researcher.
    Please provide the name of the researcher.
*   **What is the reviewer's name?** (`reviewer`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the name of the reviewer.
    Please provide the name of the reviewer.
*   **What is the review duration?** (`review_duration`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to record the duration of the review.
    Please select the duration of the review from the options provided.
*   **What is the review type?** (`review_type`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to record the type of review.
    Please select the type of review from the options provided.
*   **Is the study active or inactive?** (`study_status`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to record the status of the study.
    Please select the status of the study from the options provided.
*   **What is the study status?** (`study_status_value`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the status of the study.
    Please provide the status of the study.
*   **What is the review period?** (`review_period`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the period of the review.
    Please select the period of the review from the calendar.
*   **What is the reviewer score?** (`reviewer_score`, number, required: false): This field is used to record the score of the reviewer.
    Please provide the score of the reviewer.
*   **Is the study type "yes" or "no"?** (`study_type`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to record the type of the study.
    Please select the type of the study from the options provided.
*   **What is the review type value?** (`review_type_value`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the value of the type of review.
    Please provide the value of the type of review.
*   **What are the review comments?** (`review_comments`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the comments of the review.
    Please provide any additional comments about the review.
*   **What is the study ID?** (`study_id`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the ID of the study.
    Please provide the ID of the study.
*   **Review Period Value** (`review_period_value`, date, required: false): This field is used to record the value of the review period.
    Please select the period of the review from the calendar.
*   **What are the reviewer comments?** (`reviewer_comments`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the comments of the reviewer.
    Please provide any additional comments about the reviewer.
*   **What is the reviewer contact?** (`reviewer_contact`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the contact of the reviewer.
    Please provide the contact information of the reviewer.
*   **What is the reviewer email?** (`reviewer_email`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the email of the reviewer.
    Please provide the email of the reviewer.
*   **What is the reviewer phone?** (`reviewer_phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to record the phone of the reviewer.
    Please provide the phone number of the reviewer.
