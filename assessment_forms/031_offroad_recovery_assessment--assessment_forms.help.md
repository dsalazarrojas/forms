# Offroad Recovery Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Offroad Recovery Assessment form is designed to collect information about offroad recovery incidents. It helps in assessing the situation and making decisions regarding recovery efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions and provide information about the offroad recovery incident.
2. Select the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
3. Upload photos of the incident if necessary.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the location where the offroad recovery incident occurred.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the vehicle involved in the offroad recovery incident.
* **Hazards** (`hazard_description`, text, optional): This field is for providing a list of hazards present during the offroad recovery incident.
* **Photos** (`photos`, multiple select, optional): This field allows you to upload up to three photos of the offroad recovery incident. Please select the relevant photo(s) to accompany your assessment.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, optional): This field is for providing a brief description of the vehicle's condition after the offroad recovery incident.
* **Recovery Services** (`recovery_services`, multiple select, optional): This field allows you to select the relevant recovery services that were provided during the offroad recovery incident.
* **Offroad Type** (`offroad_type`, select one, optional): This field is for selecting the type of offroad recovery incident that occurred. Please choose from the available options.
* **Vehicle Condition** can be used to describe the condition of the vehicle before or after the offroad recovery incident, depending on the context of the assessment.
Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult the provided contact information for assistance.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of each field.
- Upload only relevant and clear photos of the offroad recovery incident.
- If you need help or have questions, please contact [insert contact information].
