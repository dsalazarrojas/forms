# gas_turbine_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gas_turbine_knowledge_assessment form is designed to gather information from users about their knowledge on gas turbines.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the gas_turbine_knowledge_assessment form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. If you are unsure about a question, you can skip it or ask for clarification.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields, as indicated by the "required" label.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.
