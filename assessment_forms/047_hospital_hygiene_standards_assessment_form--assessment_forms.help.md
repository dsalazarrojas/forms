# Hospital Hygiene Standards Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Hygiene Standards Assessment Form is designed to assess the cleanliness and maintenance of a hospital's facilities, equipment, and personnel. This form helps identify areas of improvement and ensures that the hospital meets or exceeds established hygiene standards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the hospital or facility being assessed.
2. **Department Name**: Enter the name of the department or unit within the hospital being assessed (e.g., ICU, ER, etc.).
3. **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the assessment visit.
4. **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the assessment visit.
5. **Facilities Score**: Assess the cleanliness and maintenance of the hospital's facilities, such as corridors, bathrooms, and patient rooms. Rate the score from 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
6. **Personnel Score**: Assess the performance of hospital personnel, including their attitude, knowledge, and adherence to hygiene protocols. Rate the score from 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
7. **Equipment Score**: Assess the condition and functionality of hospital equipment, such as medical devices, furniture, and machinery. Rate the score from 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
8. **Facilities Score Note**: Provide additional comments on the facilities score, if necessary.
9. **Personnel Score Note**: Provide additional comments on the personnel score, if necessary.
10. **Equipment Score Note**: Provide additional comments on the equipment score, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`facilities_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital or facility being assessed.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the department or unit within the hospital being assessed (e.g., ICU, ER, etc.).
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment visit.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, required): Enter the time of the assessment visit.
* **Facilities Score** (`facilities_score`, text, required): Assess the cleanliness and maintenance of the hospital's facilities, rate from 1-5.
* **Personnel Score** (`personnel_score`, text, required): Assess the performance of hospital personnel, rate from 1-5.
* **Equipment Score** (`equipment_score`, text, required): Assess the condition and functionality of hospital equipment, rate from 1-5.
* **Facilities Score Note** (`facilities_score_note`, text, optional): Provide additional comments on the facilities score, if necessary.
* **Personnel Score Note** (`personnel_score_note`, text, optional): Provide additional comments on the personnel score, if necessary.
* **Equipment Score Note** (`equipment_score_note`, text, optional): Provide additional comments on the equipment score, if necessary.
