# Ear Infection Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ear Infection Risk Assessment Form is a tool used by healthcare providers to evaluate the risk of ear infection in patients. It consists of multiple sections that cover various aspects of the patient's condition, including medical history, risk factors, and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form for each patient, taking care to answer questions accurately and honestly.
2.  Review the patient's medical history and symptoms to ensure accurate assessment.
3.  Evaluate the risk level of the patient based on their responses.
4.  Document the follow-up schedule and any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ear Infection Risk Assessment** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_1`, text, **required**): This field is used to gather a brief description of the patient's ear infection risk assessment.
* **Medical History** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_2`, text, **required**): Enter the patient's relevant medical history.
* **Ear Infection Risk Factors** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_3`, text, **required**): Provide a list of factors that contribute to the patient's ear infection risk.
* **Patient Provided Risk Assessment** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_4`, text, **optional**): This field is used to capture the patient's own risk assessment.
* **Healthcare Provider Risk Assessment** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_5`, text, **required**): Enter the healthcare provider's risk assessment of the patient.
* **Risk Level** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_6`, select, **required**): Select the patient's risk level (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Date** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_7`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the risk assessment.
* **Time** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_8`, time, **required**): Enter the time of the risk assessment.
* **Note** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_9`, note, **required**): Add any additional notes about the patient's condition.
* **Email** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_10`, email, **required**): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_11`, text, **required**): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Follow-up Date** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_12`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Time** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_13`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of the follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_14`, note, **optional**): Add any additional follow-up notes.
* **Healthcare Provider** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_15`, text, **required**): Enter the healthcare provider's name and title.
* **Patient Signature** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_16`, text, **required**): The patient must sign this field.
* **Healthcare Provider Signature** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_17`, text, **required**): The healthcare provider must sign this field.
* **Date Signed** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_18`, date, **required**): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_19`, time, **required**): Enter the time of signing.
* **Note Signed** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_20`, note, **required**): Add any additional notes about the signing.
* **Follow-up Date Signed** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_21`, date, **optional**): Enter the date of the follow-up appointment after the form was signed.
* **Follow-up Time Signed** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_22`, time, **optional**): Enter the time of the follow-up appointment after the form was signed.
* **Follow-up Notes Signed** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_23`, note, **optional**): Add any additional follow-up notes after the form was signed.
* **Follow-up Email** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_24`, email, **optional**): Enter the patient's email address for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Phone** (`ear_infection_risk_assessment_form_25`, text, **optional**): Enter the patient's phone number for follow-up.
