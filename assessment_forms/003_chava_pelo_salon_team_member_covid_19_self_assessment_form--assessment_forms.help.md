# Chava' Pelo Salon Team Member COVID 19 Self Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to ensure that team members are healthy and clear to work before starting their shift. It is a quick and effective screening tool to identify potential risks associated with COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer each question carefully, indicating "True" or "False" for health-related questions.
2. If you answer "No" or "False" to all health-related questions, select "Yes, I am clear to work".
3. If you answer "Yes" to any health-related question, select "No, I need to take time off" for question 18, and fill out the supervisor's name.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team member name** (required, text): This is where you enter your name as a team member.
* **Today's date** (required, date): Please enter the current date.
* **Shift start time** (required, time): Please enter your scheduled shift start time.
* **Your position at the salon** (required, select_one): Select your role within the salon.
* **Do you have a fever or elevated temperature this morning?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have a fever or elevated temperature.
	+ "False" if you do not have a fever or elevated temperature.
	+ "Did not check" if you have not checked your temperature.
* **Do you have a new or worsening cough?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have a new or worsening cough.
	+ "False" if you do not have a cough.
* **Do you have difficulty breathing?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have difficulty breathing.
	+ "False" if you do not have difficulty breathing.
* **Have you lost sense of taste or smell?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have lost your sense of taste or smell.
	+ "False" if you do not have lost your sense of taste or smell.
* **Do you have fatigue or body aches?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have fatigue or body aches.
	+ "False" if you do not have fatigue or body aches.
* **Do you have a sore throat?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have a sore throat.
	+ "False" if you do not have a sore throat.
* **Has anyone in your household tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if someone in your household has tested positive.
	+ "False" if no one in your household has tested positive.
* **Have you had close contact with someone who tested positive?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have had close contact.
	+ "False" if you have not had close contact.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the past 14 days?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you have tested positive.
	+ "False" if you have not tested positive.
* **Vaccination and booster status** (optional, select_one):
	+ "Fully vaccinated with booster".
	+ "Fully vaccinated without booster".
	+ "Partially vaccinated".
	+ "Not vaccinated".
* **Are you currently under quarantine or isolation?** (required, select_one):
	+ "True" if you are under quarantine or isolation.
	+ "False" if you are not under quarantine or isolation.
* **I certify that I am healthy and clear to work today** (required, select_one):
	+ "Yes, I am clear to work".
	+ "No, I need to take time off".
	+ "Unsure".
* **Supervisor name (if not clear to work)** (optional, text): If you are not clear to work, enter your supervisor's name.
* **I agree to notify management immediately if I develop COVID-19 symptoms during my shift** (required, select_one):
	+ "I agree".
	+ "I do not agree".
