# Pre Employment Assessment Practice Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from a candidate after a pre-employment assessment. It's essential to provide accurate and helpful information to recruiters and hiring managers to improve the assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form to provide your thoughts and experiences from the pre-employment assessment.
2. Be as descriptive and honest as possible, as this feedback will help improve the assessment process.
3. You can fill out this form at your convenience, but it's recommended that you do so while the experience is still fresh.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Practice Feedback (<practice_feedback>)
* This field is where you can provide a general impression of the assessment experience. Please describe what you felt about the questions, the time allocated, and any other relevant thoughts.

### Recruiter Name (<recruiter_name>)
* This field is for you to provide the name of the recruiter who conducted the assessment. If you're unsure, you can check your communication with the recruiter or ask someone who might know.

### Feedback (<feedback>)
* This field is where you can elaborate on your thoughts about the assessment questions, the questions themselves, or any other aspects of the experience. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your perspective.

### Date Given (<date_given>)
* This field is to record the date when the pre-employment assessment took place. If you're unsure, you can check your calendar or ask someone who was present.

### Comments (<comments>)
* This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you might have about the assessment process. If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to mention them here.

## Tips
* Be as honest and descriptive as possible in your feedback.
* If you're unsure about any information, it's okay to ask for clarification.
* Your feedback is essential in helping us improve the pre-employment assessment process.
