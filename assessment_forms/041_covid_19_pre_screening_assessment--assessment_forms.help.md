# COVID 19 Pre Screening Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Pre Screening Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's risk level and provide recommendations for their safety and well-being in the context of COVID-19.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, using the options provided.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information and resources to complete the form.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`subject_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself and allow the assessor to know who you are.
* **ID Number or Employee ID** (`subject_id`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your ID number or employee ID to provide additional context.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth to determine your age and potential risk factors.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to contact you with results and recommendations.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to allow the assessor to contact you with results and recommendations.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your assessment to keep track of your risk level and recommendations.
* **Do you have a fever (above 100.4F)?** (`fever`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it indicates potential symptoms of COVID-19.
* **Do you have a cough?** (`cough`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it is a symptom of COVID-19.
* **Do you have difficulty breathing or shortness of breath?** (`dyspnea`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it indicates potential respiratory issues.
* **Do you have a sore throat?** (`sore_throat`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it is a common symptom of COVID-19.
* **Do you have loss of taste or smell?** (`loss_taste_smell`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it is a symptom of COVID-19.
* **Do you feel fatigued or have body aches?** (`fatigue`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it indicates potential underlying health issues.
* **Have you been diagnosed with COVID-19 in the past 14 days?** (`diagnosed_covid`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it determines your current risk level.
* **Have you been in close contact with someone positive for COVID-19?** (`close_contact_covid`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, considering close contact with someone in the past 14 days.
* **Have you traveled internationally in the past 14 days?** (`international_travel`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it may indicate potential exposure risks.
* **Do you have chronic health conditions?** (`chronic_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it affects your overall health risk.
* **Are you immunocompromised or on immunosuppressive therapy?** (`immunocompromised`, `select_one`, required): Answer this question truthfully, as it affects your immune system.
* **Risk Assessment Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required): This question determines your risk level, based on your answers to the previous questions.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, required): Based on your risk level, you will receive a recommendation for your safety and well-being.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, optional): This field is for the assessor's information and identification.

## Tips

* If you are unsure of any question or answer, choose the "Unsure" option.
* If you have any underlying medical conditions or concerns, please discuss them with the assessor before completing the form.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the assessor for assistance.
