# Military Entrance Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess applicants' suitability for military service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your information.
2. Select your military status and service branch (if applicable).
3. Provide any additional details or comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page (Page 1)** (`first_page`, text, required: false): Please fill out the first page with your information.
* **Applicant Details (Page 2)** (`applicant_details`, select_one, required: false): Select your option for this field.
	+ Option 1: Select this option if you are a...
	+ Option 2: Select this option if you are a...
	+ Option 3: Select this option if you are a...
* **Readiness Insights (Page 3)** (`readiness_insights`, text, required: false): Provide any additional insights or information about your readiness for military service.
* **First Name (Page 4)** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Page 5)** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth (Page 6)** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Email (Page 7)** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (Page 8)** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Next Steps (Page 9)** (`next_steps`, text, required: false): Describe any next steps you'd like to take regarding your military service.
* **Additional Comments (Page 10)** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Military Status (Page 11)** (`military_status`, select_one, required: false): Select your military status.
	+ Option 1: Active Duty
	+ Option 2: Reservist
	+ Option 3: Other
* **Service Branch (Page 12)** (`service_branch`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your service branch.
	+ Option 1: Army
	+ Option 2: Navy
	+ Option 3: Airforce
	+ Option 4: Marine Corps
	+ Option 5: Coast Guard
* **Service Location (Page 13)** (`service_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you served or are currently serving.
* **Military Occupation (Page 14)** (`military_occupation`, text, required: false): Enter your military occupation or job title.
* **Discharge Date (Page 15)** (`discharge_date`, date, required: false): Enter your discharge date (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* This form is intended to be a comprehensive assessment tool, so please provide as much information as possible.
