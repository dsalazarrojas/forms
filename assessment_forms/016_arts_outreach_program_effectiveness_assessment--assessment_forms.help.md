## Purpose
The Arts Outreach Program Effectiveness Assessment is a form designed to gauge the success of the outreach program. This assessment will help us evaluate how well the program met its goals and identify areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions based on your personal experience with the arts outreach program.
2. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "Required" in the form) are completed.
3. Select your answer choices carefully, as they will help us understand your perspective.
4. For fields with hints, please refer to the hint for guidance on how to answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Outreach Program Name** (`program_identification`, text, Required):
    Enter the name of the outreach program you participated in.
* **Date of Participation** (`participation_date`, date, Required):
    Enter the date you participated in the program.
* **I participated as a** (`participant_type`, select_one, Required):
    Select the category that best describes how you participated in the program:
    - Student
    - Parent/Guardian
    - Educator
    - Community Member
    - Volunteer
* **Satisfaction with Program Activities** (`activity_satisfaction`, number, Required):
    Rate your satisfaction with the program activities from 1 (very dissatisfied) to 5 (very satisfied).
* **Impact on your artistic skills or knowledge** (`skill_impact`, number, Required):
    Rate the impact of the program on your artistic skills or knowledge from 1 (no impact) to 5 (significant impact).
* **Impact on your connection to the community** (`community_impact`, number, Required):
    Rate the impact of the program on your connection to the community from 1 (no impact) to 5 (significant impact).
* **What was the most memorable part of the program** (`highlights`, text, Optional):
    Describe the most memorable part of the program that stood out to you.
* **How can we improve this outreach program** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, Optional):
    Share your thoughts on how we can improve the outreach program.
* **How likely are you to recommend this program to others** (`recommendation_score`, number, Required):
    Rate your likelihood of recommending the program to others from 0 (not likely) to 10 (very likely).
* **May we contact you for a follow-up interview** (`follow_up_permission`, select_one, Optional):
    Select "True" if you are willing to be contacted for a follow-up interview.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional):
    If you selected "True" for the above option, enter your email address so we can contact you.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and as accurately as possible.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, we welcome your feedback.
- Thank you for taking the time to participate in this assessment!
