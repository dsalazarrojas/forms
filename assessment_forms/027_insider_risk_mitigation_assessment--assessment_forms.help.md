# Insider Risk Mitigation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insider Risk Mitigation Assessment is a form designed to help organizations identify and assess the risks associated with insider threats, which are malicious or unauthorized actions taken by employees, contractors, or other insiders that can compromise the organization's security and integrity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and questions carefully to understand what information is being collected.
2. Read each section's instructions and label to determine the required fields and available options.
3. Fill in the form with the relevant information, using the available options to help guide your answers.
4. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form's purpose and guidelines for completion.
* **Incident Reporting**: This section asks about past incidents that may have occurred within your organization.
  * **Incident 1-3**: Select all incidents that apply. For example, if you reported an incident last week, select "Incident 1".
* **Threat Controls**: This section asks about the controls in place to mitigate the risk of insider threats.
  * **Control 1-3**: Select all controls that apply. For example, if you have a control that monitors employee activity, select "Control 1".
* **Incident Reporting Frequency**: This section asks about how often incidents are reported within your organization.
  * **Weekly**: If incidents are reported weekly, select "Weekly".
* **Incident Reporting Channels**: This section asks about the methods used to report incidents within your organization.
  * **Channel 1-3**: Select all channels that apply. For example, if you report incidents via email, select "Channel 1".
* **Other Incident Reporting Channels**: If your organization has other channels not listed here, enter them in the text field.
* **Incident Reporting Frequency Other**: If your organization has a different reporting frequency than listed, enter it in the text field.
* **Incident Reporting Who**: This section asks about who reports incidents within your organization.
  * **Reporter 1-3**: Select all reporters that apply. For example, if you report incidents to the IT department, select "Reporter 1".
* **Other Incident Reporters**: If your organization has other reporters not listed here, enter them in the text field.
* **Incident Reporting Reason**: This section asks why incidents are reported within your organization.
  * **Text field**: Enter a brief explanation of why incidents are reported.
* **Other Incident Report Reason**: If your organization has other reasons not listed here, enter them in the text field.
* **Incident Reporting Else**: This section is for entering any additional information about incident reporting not captured in previous sections.
  * **Other 1-3**: Select all others that apply. For example, if you report incidents using a different method, select "Other 1".
  * **Text field**: Enter any additional information about incident reporting.

**Tips**

* Make sure to answer all required fields.
* Use the available options to help guide your answers.
* If unsure about a field, leave it blank and explain in the "Other" text field.
* Review your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your organization's security team.
 
Note: This guide is a general guide and may not cover all specific scenarios or questions.
