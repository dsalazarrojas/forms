# Mold Symptom Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mold Symptom Assessment Survey is a form used to collect information about potential mold-related symptoms exhibited by individuals or facilities. This form helps assess the severity of mold symptoms and create a follow-up plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page and ensure that all required fields are filled out.
2. For the "Respiratory Symptoms", "Eye Symptoms", "Skin Symptoms", "Muscle and Joint Symptoms", and "Other Symptoms" sections, select all applicable symptoms from the provided options. Use commas to separate multiple selections if necessary.
3. For the "Exposure Concerns" field, provide a brief description of any concerns or suspicions about mold exposure.
4. Enter the follow-up plan, including the date and any relevant details, in the "Follow-up Plan" field.
5. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Number" and "Email Address" fields for follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Facility Name
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the facility or location being assessed for mold symptoms.

### Respiratory Symptoms
* **Respiratory Symptoms** (`symptom_1`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable respiratory symptoms, separated by commas:
	+ Shortness of breath
	+ Coughing
	+ Sore throat
	+ Runny nose

### Eye Symptoms
* **Eye Symptoms** (`symptom_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable eye symptoms, separated by commas:
	+ Eye irritation
	+ Redness of the eye
	+ Itching of the eye
	+ Vision problems

### Skin Symptoms
* **Skin Symptoms** (`symptom_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable skin symptoms, separated by commas:
	+ Skin irritation
	+ Rashes
	+ Blisters
	+ Burning sensations

### Exposure Concerns
* **Exposure Concerns** (`exposure_concerns`, text, optional): Describe any concerns or suspicions about mold exposure.

### Follow-up Plan
* **Follow-up Plan** (`follow_up_plan`, date, optional): Enter the follow-up plan, including the date and any relevant details.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.

### Email Address
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.

### Headache
* **Headache** (`symptom_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable headache symptoms, separated by commas:
	+ Headache
	+ Migraine
	+ Fever
	+ Chest pain

### Muscle and Joint Symptoms
* **Muscle and Joint Symptoms** (`symptom_5`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable muscle and joint symptoms, separated by commas:
	+ Muscle weakness
	+ Joint pain
	+ Fatigue
	+ Muscle cramps

### Other Symptoms
* **Other Symptoms** (`symptom_6`, text, optional): Describe any other applicable symptoms not listed above.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* Review each section carefully to ensure that all relevant information is provided.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please consult the relevant authorities or experts.
* Please use commas to separate multiple selections in the symptom sections.
