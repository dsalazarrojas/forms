# Adolescence And Puberty Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adolescence And Puberty Assessment form is designed to collect information about an individual's experiences and feelings during the transition into puberty. This form aims to help individuals understand and navigate this significant life change.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, age, and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer the questions about your experiences with physical changes during puberty, your level of knowledge, and your comfort discussing topics.
3. Select all that apply to the "Physical Changes Noticed" field regarding your experiences with physical changes.
4. If you have any concerns or questions, please type them in the "Concerns or Questions" field.
5. Indicate how comfortable you are discussing topics related to puberty in the "Comfort Discussing Topics" field.
6. Select the primary person you usually talk to about personal changes in the "Primary Support Person" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or information to share, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your current age.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
* **Physical Changes Noticed** (`physical_changes_noticed`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to you:
	+ Growth spurt
	+ Skin changes
	+ Voice deepening
	+ Body hair growth
	+ Mood changes
* **Knowledge of Puberty** (`knowledge_of_puberty`, select_one, required): How much do you know about the changes happening during puberty?
	+ Very knowledgeable
	+ Somewhat knowledgeable
	+ Neutral
	+ Little knowledge
	+ Not knowledgeable at all
* **Concerns or Questions** (`concerns_or_questions`, text, optional): Is there anything you are worried about or want to ask?
* **Comfort Discussing Topics** (`comfort_discussing_topics`, select_one, required): How comfortable are you talking about these changes?
	+ Very comfortable
	+ Somewhat comfortable
	+ Neutral
	+ Uncomfortable
	+ Very uncomfortable
* **Primary Support Person** (`primary_support_person`, select_one, optional): Who do you usually talk to about personal changes?
	+ Parent
	+ Teacher
	+ Counselor
	+ Friend
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other information you would like to share?
