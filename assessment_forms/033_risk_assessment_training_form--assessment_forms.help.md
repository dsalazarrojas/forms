# Risk Assessment Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Training Form is used for risk assessment training purposes only. It is not intended for other uses such as risk assessment reporting or management. Please complete the form accurately and honestly to support risk assessment training efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant training program(s) from the "Training Programs" dropdown menu, if applicable.
2. Enter relevant information in the text fields for "Safety Protocols", "Safety Procedures", and "Risk Management".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Assessment Training Form** (`risk_assessment_training_form`, text, optional): This field is a free-text area for entering any relevant information related to the risk assessment training form. You can enter your name, date, or any other information that may be required to fill this form accurately.
* **Training Programs** (`training_programs`, select_one, optional): This dropdown menu allows you to select one training program that is relevant to the risk assessment training form. Please select the program that best matches your training topic. 
* **Safety Protocols** (`safety_protocols`, text, optional): Enter any relevant safety protocols that you have learned or are familiar with during the risk assessment training form. This can include standard operating procedures, guidelines, or best practices.
* **Safety Procedures** (`safety_procedures`, text, optional): Enter any relevant safety procedures that you have learned or are familiar with during the risk assessment training form. This can include step-by-step guides, protocols, or processes.
* **Risk Management** (`risk_management`, text, optional): Enter any relevant risk management information that you have learned or are familiar with during the risk assessment training form. This can include risk mitigation strategies, risk assessment tools, or other risk management practices.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you enter is accurate and relevant to the risk assessment training form.
* Please fill out the form in its entirety to support risk assessment training efforts.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
