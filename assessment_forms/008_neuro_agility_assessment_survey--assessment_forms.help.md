# Neuro Agility Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your learning preferences, habits, and characteristics. Your responses will help us create more effective learning materials tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer based on your real experiences.
2. Select all options that apply to each question.
3. Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
