# Patient Health Outcome Improvement Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Patient Health Outcome Improvement Report form, explaining each section and field to help you complete it effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Patient Health Outcome Improvement Report**: Enter a brief summary of the patient's current health outcomes.
2. **Patient Progress**: Briefly describe the patient's current progress towards their goals.
3. **Patient Goals**: Indicate whether the patient has goals for their care.
	* Select 'Yes' if the patient has set goals.
	* Select 'No' if the patient does not have set goals.
4. **Current Status**: Indicate the patient's current status.
	* Select 'Active' if the patient is actively receiving care.
	* Select 'Inactive' if the patient is not actively receiving care.
5. **Next Steps**: Describe any next steps the patient needs to take to improve their health outcome.
6. **Outcome Measures**: Indicate what measures are being used to assess the patient's outcome.
	* Select 'Yes' if outcome measures are being used.
	* Select 'No' if not using outcome measures.
7. **Assessment Frequency**: Record the date of the last assessment.
8. **Last Assessment Date**: Record the date of the last assessment.
9. **Care Team Members**: Indicate if the care team members are involved.
	* Select 'Yes' if involved.
	* Select 'No' if not involved.
10. **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the patient's progress.
11. **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who can be contacted for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Health Outcome Improvement Report**: A brief summary of the patient's current health outcomes.
* **Patient Progress**: A brief description of the patient's current progress towards their goals.
* **Patient Goals**: A selection indicating whether the patient has set goals.
* **Current Status**: A selection indicating the patient's current status.
* **Next Steps**: A description of any next steps the patient needs to take.
* **Outcome Measures**: A selection indicating what measures are being used to assess the patient's outcome.
* **Assessment Frequency**: The date of the last assessment.
* **Last Assessment Date**: The date of the last assessment.
* **Care Team Members**: A selection indicating if care team members are involved.
* **Comments**: A note field for any comments or notes about the patient's progress.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person who can be contacted for further information.

## Tips
* Ensure you are familiar with the patient's care plan and goals before completing this form.
* Be as concise as possible while providing sufficient information for the patient's care team.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered, especially regarding assessment dates and frequencies.
