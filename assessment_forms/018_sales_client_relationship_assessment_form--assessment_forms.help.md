# Sales Client Relationship Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the relationship between sales teams and their clients. It helps to evaluate how well sales teams are meeting the needs of their clients, providing a basis for feedback and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form questions and provide honest and detailed responses.
2.  Answer each question to the best of your ability, using the provided response options.
3.  Ensure that all required questions are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required: false): Please rate the overall quality of the relationship with the client, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest.
*   **Satisfaction with Sales** (`satisfaction_with_sales`, number, required: false): How satisfied are you with the sales team's communication and sales process?
*   **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, number, required: false): How would you rate the communication skills of the sales team?
*   **Product Knowledge** (`product_knowledge`, number, required: false): How well does the sales team demonstrate knowledge of the product or service?
*   **Sales Support** (`sales_support`, number, required: false): How well does the sales team provide support to you as a client?
*   **Client Feedback** (`client_feedback`, text, required: false): Please provide any feedback you have about your experience with the sales team.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Additional comments or any further feedback.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Any other comments or feedback you would like to provide.

## Tips
- Be honest and provide detailed responses to help the sales team improve their performance.
- Make sure to answer all required questions to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to the sales team.
