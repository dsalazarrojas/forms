# online_assessment_tool_support_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online assessment tool support request form is designed to collect user feedback and information for support and troubleshooting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask us for clarification.
3. Make sure to submit one form per issue to avoid duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This field is for your name and contact information. Please provide your name, email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you to discuss your issue.
* **Issue Description**: In this field, please describe the issue you're experiencing with our online assessment tool. Be as detailed as possible so we can understand the problem.
* **Urgency Level**: Please select the level of urgency for your issue. This will help us prioritize our support efforts.
* **Platform Used**: Please indicate which platform you were using when you encountered the issue (e.g., Online Testing, In-House Testing, or Other).
* **Issue Details**: This field is for any additional information you'd like to share about the issue.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address so we can contact you to discuss your issue.
* **Phone**: Please provide your phone number if you'd like us to call you to discuss your issue.
* **Note**: This field is for any additional comments or notes you'd like to provide about your issue.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your descriptions to help us understand your issue better.
* If you're experiencing a critical issue, please select "High" for the Urgency Level.
* If you have any attachments or screenshots that can help us diagnose the issue, please include them with your form submission.
