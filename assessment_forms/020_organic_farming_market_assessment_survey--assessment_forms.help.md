# Organic Farming Market Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for collecting information about the organic farming market. It consists of several sections that gather data on the current state and trends of the market. Your input will help stakeholders assess the market's dynamics and identify areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the instructions and each field explanation below.
2. Scroll through the form's pages, taking note of each field's label and type.
3. Fill in the fields with the requested information, making sure to follow the instructions for each field.
4. Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completion.

* **Organic Practices Details (Page 5)** (`organic_farming_market_assessment_survey_page_5`, text, false): If you have any additional information or details about your organic farming practices, please provide them in this section.
