# Digital Culture Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help assess your understanding and perception of digital culture within the organization. It is meant to be completed by employees to gather information about their attitudes and behaviors regarding technology adoption, communication, leadership, and other aspects of digital culture.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Select the correct answer for each question, taking into account your personal experience and understanding of digital culture within the organization.
3. Be concise and clear in your answers, avoiding vague or ambiguous statements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Engagement** (Employee Engagement, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your level of engagement with digital tools and resources, and how you feel about your role in the digital landscape of the organization.
* **Technology Adoption** (Technology Adoption, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question is asking whether you have adopted and successfully integrated technology into your daily work or personal life within the organization.
* **Communication** (Communication, text, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your thoughts on the communication channels and methods used within the organization. Please provide your honest and constructive feedback.
* **Leadership** (Leadership, text, required: false)
	+ This question inquires about your perception of leadership's approach to digital culture and innovation within the organization.
* **Innovation** (Innovation, number, required: false)
	+ This question is asking you to rate your level of innovation within the organization, considering the tools, processes, and ideas implemented.
* **Feedback** (Feedback, date, required: false)
	+ This question is asking about your experience with receiving feedback, either positive or constructive, regarding your digital work or actions within the organization.
* **Time** (Time, time, required: false)
	+ Please enter the amount of time you typically spend on digital activities or tasks within the organization.
* **Note** (Note, note, required: false)
	+ This section is for any additional comments or feedback you would like to provide regarding digital culture within the organization.
* **E-mail** (E-mail, email, required: false)
	+ Please enter your organizational email address for any follow-up communications.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false)
	+ Please enter your organizational phone number for any follow-up communications.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* If unsure or uncomfortable answering a question, you can skip it or provide a comment in the Note section.
* The form is designed to be anonymous, so your responses will not be linked to your identity.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR department.
