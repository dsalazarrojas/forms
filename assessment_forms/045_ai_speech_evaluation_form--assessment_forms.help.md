# AI Speech Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The AI Speech Evaluation Form is designed to assess and evaluate a speaker's performance in a comprehensive manner, covering their vocal performance, content structure, and overall impact. This form helps evaluators provide feedback and suggestions for improvement to speakers, ensuring they can refine their skills and deliver more effective presentations or speeches.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Complete each section carefully**: Fill out each section one by one, starting from "Speech Details" and moving on to "Overall Impact" and finally "Final Feedback & Recommendations".
2. **Use the options provided**: Select the options that best fit your evaluation for each field. If a field doesn't fit any option, choose 'N/A' (Not Applicable).
3. **Provide specific feedback and suggestions**: In "Final Feedback & Recommendations", describe specific steps the speaker can take to improve their performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Name (id: 2)**: Provide the speaker's name as it appears on their ID badge or introduction.
* **Speech Topic / Title (id: 3)**: Enter the title or topic of the speech or presentation.
* **Date of Evaluation (id: 4)**: Enter the date when the speech was delivered or recorded.
* **Pronunciation & Enunciation (id: 6)**: Assess the speaker's pronunciation and enunciation using the scale:
	+ 5 - Excellent (No errors)
	+ 4 - Good (Few minor errors)
	+ 3 - Average (Understandable but accented)
	+ 2 - Fair (Difficult to understand at times)
	+ 1 - Poor (Inaudible/Inaccurate)
* **Pacing & Fluency (id: 7)**: Evaluate the speaker's pace and flow:
	+ Too Fast
	+ Steady & Natural (Good)
	+ Too Slow / Hesitant
	+ Inconsistent
* **Tone & Intonation (id: 8)**: Assess the speaker's tone and intonation:
	+ Expressive & Dynamic
	+ Professional & Balanced
	+ Somewhat Monotone
	+ Overly Critical/Inappropriate
* **Volume & Projection (id: 9)**: Evaluate the speaker's volume and projection:
	+ Strong & Clear
	+ Adequate
	+ Weak / Hard to hear
* **Clarity of Message (id: 11)**: Assess how well the main point was conveyed:
	+ Highly Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Mostly Clear
	+ Neutral / Ambiguous
	+ Confusing
* **Logical Flow & Organization (id: 12)**: Evaluate the speaker's logical flow and organization:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4
	+ 5
* **Use of Language & Vocabulary (id: 13)**: Assess the speaker's use of language and vocabulary:
	+ Advanced & Sophisticated
	+ Professional & Precise
	+ Appropriate / Standard
	+ Basic / Repetitive
	+ Poor / Inaccurate
* **Overall Impact (id: 15)**: Evaluate the speaker's overall impact:
	+ 5 - Very Engaging
	+ 4 - Engaging
	+ 3 - Average
	+ 2 - Lacks Impact
	+ 1 - Unengaging
* **Areas of Strength (id: 16)**: Select all areas where the speaker excelled:
	+ Confidence & Presence
	+ Subject Knowledge
	+ Vocal Clarity
	+ Strong Opening/Closing
	+ Logical Structure
	+ Effective Examples
* **Areas for Improvement (id: 17)**: Select all areas where the speaker needs improvement:
	+ Filler Words (um, ah, like)
	+ Speech Speed
	+ Eye Contact / Body Language
	+ Lack of Conclusion
	+ Monotone Delivery
	+ Complex Language
* **Overall Score (id: 19)**: Provide a numerical assessment of the speech, out of 100.
* **Final Feedback & Recommendations (id: 20)**: Provide specific feedback and suggestions for the speaker to improve.
