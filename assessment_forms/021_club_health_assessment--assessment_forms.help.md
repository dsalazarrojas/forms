# Club Health Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club Health Assessment is a tool used by club administrators to gather feedback from members on various aspects of the club's performance. This form is designed to be completed by club members to provide valuable insights for club improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Select the date you completed this assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
4. Choose your level of engagement in the club by selecting one of the options in the "Overall Engagement Level" field.
5. Rate the club's communication effectiveness by selecting one of the options in the "Club Communication Effectiveness" field.
6. Rate your sense of belonging and inclusion in the club by selecting one of the options in the "Sense of Belonging and Inclusion" field.
7. Evaluate the leadership effectiveness by selecting one of the options in the "Leadership Effectiveness" field (if applicable).
8. Rate the diversity and inclusion in the club by selecting one of the options in the "Diversity and Inclusion in Club" field.
9. Indicate how safe you feel in the club activities by selecting one of the options in the "Feel Safe and Respected" field.
10. Rate the quality of club events by selecting one of the options in the "Quality of Club Events" field (if applicable).
11. Indicate your likelihood of continuing membership by selecting one of the options in the "Likely to Continue Membership" field.
12. Choose whether you would recommend the club to others by selecting one of the options in the "Would You Recommend to Others" field.
13. Enter any biggest concerns or issues with the club in the "Biggest Concerns or Issues" field (if applicable).
14. Provide any suggestions for club improvement in the "Suggestions for Club Improvement" field (if applicable).

* **Assessment Date** (`3`, `date`, required): Select the date you completed this assessment.

	+ 3-6 Months: Between 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 Months: Between 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 Years: Between 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 Years: Between 2-5 years
	+ 5+ Years: More than 5 years
* **Overall Engagement Level** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Choose how engaged you are in club activities.
