# Risk Assessment Communication Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Risk Assessment Communication Form is used to document and communicate risk assessments and their associated information. This form is used to identify, categorize, and prioritize risks that may affect our organization's operations, people, or assets. Completing this form helps ensure that risks are evaluated and addressed in a consistent and comprehensive manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Enter the title of the risk assessment in the "risk_assessment_form_name" field.
3. Select the risk level from the "risk_level" field, using the options provided.
4. Choose the type of risk from the "risk_type" field, using the options provided.
5. Enter the risk probability in the "risk_probability" field.
6. Select the potential impact of the risk from the "risk_impact" field, using the options provided.
7. Choose the potential actions to be taken from the "risk_action" field, using the options provided.
8. Select the current status of the risk from the "risk_status" field, using the options provided.
9. Enter the date of the risk in the "risk_date" field.
10. Enter the time of the day when the risk was assessed in the "risk_time" field.
11. Enter the location of the risk in the "risk_location" field.
12. Enter any phone numbers associated with the risk in the "risk_phone" field.
13. Enter any email addresses associated with the risk in the "risk_email" field.
14. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "risk_notes" field.
15. Finally, select the type of form from the "risk_form" field, using the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **risk_assessment_form_name (Text, Optional)**: Enter a brief description of the risk assessment.
* **risk_level (Number, Optional)**: Select the level of risk, with options: High, Medium, Low.
* **risk_type (Select One, Optional)**: Choose the type of risk, with options: High, Medium, Low.
* **risk_probability (Number, Optional)**: Enter the probability of the risk occurring.
* **risk_impact (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the potential impact of the risk, with options: High, Medium, Low.
* **risk_action (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Choose the actions to be taken in response to the risk, with options: High, Medium, Low.
* **risk_status (Select One, Optional)**: Select the current status of the risk, with options: Active, Resolved, Inactive.
* **risk_date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of the risk assessment.
* **risk_time (Time, Optional)**: Enter the time of the day when the risk was assessed.
* **risk_location (Text, Optional)**: Enter the location of the risk.
* **risk_phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter any phone numbers associated with the risk.
* **risk_email (Email, Optional)**: Enter any email addresses associated with the risk.
* **risk_notes (Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the risk.
* **risk_form (Select One, Optional)**: Select the type of form, with options: Risk Assessment Communication Form, Other.
