# Personal Independence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Independence Assessment form gathers information about an individual's level of independence in various aspects of their daily life.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you provide accurate information about the individual.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or support.
4. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have completed it accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, **not required**): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please provide a short explanation of what the form is used for.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, **not required**): Enter the individual's name.
* **Caregiver** (`Caregiver`, `text`, **not required**): If the individual has a caregiver, provide their name and contact information.
* **Contact** (`Contact`, `text`, **not required**): Enter the individual's contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, **not required**): Enter the individual's address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, **not required**): Enter the individual's phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, **not required**): Enter the individual's email address.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `text`, **not required**): Describe the individual's medical condition or any health issues they are experiencing.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, **not required**): Describe the individual's medical history, including any previous conditions or treatments.
* **Medication** (`Medication`, `text`, **not required**): List any medication the individual is currently taking.
* **Independence Level** (`Independence Level`, `select_one`, **not required**): Choose the individual's level of independence (High, Low, Medium).
* **Daily Activities** (`Daily Activities`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select all activities the individual is able to perform independently (Bathing, Dressing, Grooming, Eating, Toileting, Transferring, Walking).
* **Mobility** (`Mobility`, `select_one`, **not required**): Choose the individual's level of mobility (Independent, Limited, Unable).
* **Communication** (`Communication`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select the individual's communication skills (Verbal, Non-verbal, None).
* **Cognitive** (`Cognitive`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select the individual's cognitive abilities (Normal, Impaired, None).
* **Emotional** (`Emotional`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select the individual's emotional state (Stable, Stable with anxiety, Stable with depression, Stable with other, Stable with trauma, Unstable).
* **Social** (`Social`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select the individual's social skills (High, Moderate, Low).
* **Safety** (`Safety`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select the individual's level of safety (High, Moderate, Low).
* **Financial** (`Financial`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select the individual's financial situation (High, Moderate, Low).
* **Employment** (`Employment`, `select_multiple`, **not required**): Select the individual's employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Disabled, Retired, Student).
* **Education** (`Education`, `text`, **not required**): Describe the individual's education background.
* **Caregiver Info** (`Caregiver Info`, `text`, **not required**): Provide additional information about the individual's caregiver (e.g., their name, contact information).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, **not required**): Provide any additional information or notes about the individual.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, **not required**): Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have completed it accurately.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, **not required**): This is a placeholder for submission or next steps.

## Tips
* Please take your time to complete the form accurately.
* Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the best information possible.
* If unsure about any question, please ask for clarification or support.
* Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have completed it accurately.
