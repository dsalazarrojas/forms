# Warehouse Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the safety and emergency procedures of a warehouse. It is designed to collect information about the warehouse's name, location, contact person, warehouse type, size, layout, safety hazards, hazard prevention measures, emergency exit information, first aid kit, emergency contact, phone number, email, date and time of the assessment, and any additional notes. This form helps ensure that all warehouses are assessed consistently and that any safety concerns are addressed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the warehouse's name in the "Warehouse Name" field.
2. Provide the location of the warehouse in the "Location" field.
3. Select the contact person responsible for the warehouse in the "Contact Person" field. This can be multiple people.
4. Choose the type of warehouse from the "Warehouse Type" field.
5. Enter the size of the warehouse in the "Warehouse Size" field.
6. Describe the warehouse's layout in the "Warehouse Layout" field.
7. Identify any safety hazards present in the warehouse in the "Safety Hazard" field.
8. List any measures taken to prevent these hazards in the "Hazard Prevention" field.
9. Select the emergency exit locations in the "Emergency Exit" field. This can be multiple locations.
10. Choose the first aid kit type that is available in the warehouse in the "First Aid Kit" field.
11. Select the emergency contact person(s) in the "Emergency Contact" field. This can be multiple people.
12. Enter the phone number of the warehouse in the "Phone Number" field.
13. Enter the email address of the warehouse in the "Email" field.
14. Record the date and time of the assessment in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
15. Enter any additional notes about the warehouse in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warehouse Name (text, required)**: Enter the name of the warehouse.
* **Location (text, required)**: Provide the address or location of the warehouse.
* **Contact Person (select_multiple, optional)**: Select the person(s) responsible for the warehouse. This can be multiple people.
* **Warehouse Type (select_one, optional)**: Choose the type of warehouse (Dry, Cold, Frozen, or Ambient).
* **Warehouse Size (number, optional)**: Enter the size of the warehouse.
* **Warehouse Layout (text, optional)**: Describe the layout of the warehouse.
* **Safety Hazard (text, optional)**: Identify any safety hazards present in the warehouse.
* **Hazard Prevention (text, optional)**: List any measures taken to prevent these hazards.
* **Emergency Exit (select_multiple, optional)**: Select the emergency exit locations in the warehouse. This can be multiple locations.
* **First Aid Kit (select_one, optional)**: Choose the first aid kit type that is available in the warehouse (Red Cross or Other).
* **Emergency Contact (select_multiple, optional)**: Select the emergency contact person(s).
* **Phone Number (text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the warehouse.
* **Email (email, optional)**: Enter the email address of the warehouse.
* **Date (date, optional)**: Record the date of the assessment.
* **Time (time, optional)**: Record the time of the assessment.
* **Note (note, optional)**: Enter any additional notes about the warehouse.

Note: This form does not require completion of all fields. Fill out as much information as you have available, and leave out any fields that are not applicable.
