# Post Test On Safety And Performance - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Test On Safety And Performance form is designed to evaluate an employee's safety awareness and performance during a post-test assessment. It helps in identifying areas of improvement and ensures that the employee is aware of their safety responsibilities and performance levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form page by page, using the navigation buttons to move forward or backward.
2. For select fields, choose the correct option from the list.
3. Use the "Save Draft" function to save your progress and return later.
4. Review each field before submitting to ensure accurate and complete information.
5. Once you have completed the form, you can submit or save your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Post Test On Safety And Performance**: This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description or summary of the assessment.
* **Employee Safety Knowledge**: Please select "Yes" if you have a good understanding of safety procedures, or "No" if you are unsure.
* **Employee Confidence**: Select "Yes" if you feel confident in your ability to follow safety procedures, or "No" if you do not.
* **Safety Concerns**: If you have safety concerns, select "Yes", or "No" if you do not.
* **Safety Procedure**: Please select "Yes" if you follow safety procedures, or "No" if you do not.
* **Safety Equipment**: If you have access to safety equipment, please select "Yes", or "No" if you do not.
* **Safety Training**: Select "Yes" if you have received safety training, or "No" if you have not.
* **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or concerns you may have regarding your safety and performance.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address if you would like to receive updates or notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted for further information.
* **Submit**: This is the final step to submit your assessment. Make sure to review each field before submitting.
* **Cancel**: Use this option if you do not want to submit your assessment. However, your progress will not be saved.
* **Back**: Navigate back to the previous page.
* **Next**: Move forward to the next page.
* **Save Draft**: Save your progress and return later.
* **Clear Answers**: Delete all your answers and start over.
* **Save and Submit**: Save your answers and submit the form.
* **Submit and Save**: Submit your answers and save your progress.
* **Save and Clear**: Save your answers and clear all fields.
* **Save**: Save your answers without submitting or saving progress.
* **Cancel/Submit**: Use this option to cancel or submit your assessment.
* **Save/Submit**: Save and submit your answers.
* **Save and Exit**: Save your answers and exit the form.
* **Save**: Save your answers without submitting or saving progress.

Note: Please review each field carefully before submitting to ensure accurate and complete information. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
