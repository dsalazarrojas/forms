# health_assessment_team_lamar - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic information from individuals for health assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth in their respective fields.
2. Enter your Contact Number and Email Address.
3. Select your Gender from the options provided.
4. Choose your Marital Status (Single, Married, Widowed, or Divorced).
5. Enter your Address Line 1 and Address Line 2 (if applicable).
6. Enter your City and State.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)** (`first_name`, Required): This is your personal name, the one you prefer to be called.
* **Last Name (Text)** (`last_name`, Required): This is the second part of your personal name.
* **Date of Birth (Date)** (`date_of_birth`, Optional): The date of your birth, which is your actual date of birth.
* **Contact Number (Text)** (`contact_number`, Optional): Your mobile or other contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address (Email)** (`email_address`, Optional): The email address you use for communication.
* **Gender (Select One)** (`gender`, Optional): Select your gender, which can be Male, Female, or Other.
* **Marital Status (Select Multiple)** (`marital_status`, Optional): Choose all that apply from Single, Married, Widowed, or Divorced.
* **Address Line 1 (Text)** (`address_line_1`, Optional): Enter the first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2 (Text)** (`address_line_2`, Optional): Enter the second line of your address, if applicable.
* **City (Text)** (`city`, Optional): Enter your city where you reside.
* **State (Text)** (`state`, Optional): Enter your state of residence.
