# hospitality_knowledge_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospitality Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate the knowledge and skills of hotel staff in various areas. This form is essential for ensuring that staff members have the necessary expertise to provide excellent service to guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have reviewed the form carefully to understand the questions and answers.
2. Select the relevant answers for each field, considering the options provided.
3. Fill in the required fields, and leave the optional fields blank if not applicable.
4. Review and submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  This field is used to determine if you are in contact with guests, such as during check-in or check-out.
  Please select 'Yes' if you have interacted with guests in this manner, or 'No' otherwise.
### Staff Skills
* **Staff Skills** (`staff_skills`, `select_one`, optional):
  This field asks if you have received training on specific skills, which may be relevant to your role.
  Select 'Yes' if you have received such training, or 'No' if not.
### Procedures
* **Procedures** (`procedures`, `select_multiple`, optional):
  This field is used to gauge your knowledge of hotel procedures, such as check-in and check-out processes.
  Select 'Yes' for each procedure you are familiar with, or leave blank if not.
### Training Needs
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, `select_one`, optional):
  This field assesses your need for additional training or support in specific areas.
  Select 'Yes' if you require further training, or 'No' if not.
### Overall Experience
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `note`, optional):
  This field is for you to describe your overall experience in the hospitality industry.
  Please provide a brief but thorough description of your experience, including relevant details.
### Staff Procedures
* **Staff Procedures** (`staff_procedures`, `select_one`, optional):
  This field checks your knowledge of hotel staff procedures, such as handling customer complaints.
  Select 'Yes' if you are familiar with these procedures, or 'No' if not.
### Feedback
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional):
  This field is for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
  Please be as detailed as possible to help us improve our services.
### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional):
  This field is for any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
  Please use this space to elaborate on any points you would like to make.
### Submission Date
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional):
  This field records the date on which you submitted your answers.
  This will be automatically populated based on your submission.
### Submission Time
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional):
  This field captures the time of your submission.
  This will be automatically populated based on your submission.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate information to ensure the assessment is comprehensive and informative.
* Review each field carefully before providing an answer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
