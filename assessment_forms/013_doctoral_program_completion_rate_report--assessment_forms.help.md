# Doctoral Program Completion Rate Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctoral Program Completion Rate Report is used to collect data on the completion rate of doctoral programs across various universities. This report is essential for evaluating and improving the effectiveness of doctoral programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **University Name** to identify the university for which you are reporting the completion rate.
2. Select the **Academic Program** that the report is for.
3. Choose the **Completion Year** for which you are reporting the completion rate.
4. Indicate if students were **Active** or **Inactive** for the selected **Completion Year**.
5. Enter the **Student Count** for the selected **Completion Year**.
6. Check if the program has **Program Goals**.
7. Select the **Graduation Date** for the selected **Graduation Year**.
8. Choose the **Completion Status** (Active or Inactive) for the selected **Graduation Year**.
9. Enter the **Email Address** of the person filling out this form.
10. Enter the **Email Address** of the **Department**.
11. Enter the **Departmental Website** for the department.
12. Confirm if the program has a **University Logo** and choose accordingly.
13. Confirm if the **Program Director** is available and choose accordingly.
14. Confirm if the **Contact Person** is available and choose accordingly.
15. Enter the **Contact Phone** of the contact person.
16. Enter the **University Website**.
17. Confirm if the **Departmental Phone** is available and choose accordingly.
18. Enter the **Departmental Email**.
19. Confirm if the **Departmental Website** is available and choose accordingly.
20. Enter the **Program Goals**.
21. Confirm if the selected **Program Goals** are available.
22. Confirm the **Departmental Phone** is available and choose accordingly.
23. Enter the **Program Goals Goals**.
24. Enter the **Department**.
25. Select **Submit** to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the university for which you are reporting the completion rate.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, select_one, required): Select the academic program that the report is for.
* **Completion Year** (`completion_year`, select_multiple, required): Choose the completion year for which you are reporting the completion rate.
* **Student Count** (`student_count`, number, required): Enter the number of students in the selected **Completion Year**.
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, select_one, required): Select if the program has program goals.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the graduation date for the selected **Graduation Year**.
* **Complete Status** (`completion_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the completion status (Active or Inactive) for the selected **Graduation Year**.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person filling out this form.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **University Website** (`university_website`, text, required): Enter the university website.
* **University Logo** (`university_logo`, select_one, optional): Confirm if the program has a university logo.
* **Program Director** (`program_director`, select_one, optional): Confirm if the program director is available.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, select_one, optional): Confirm if the contact person is available.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the contact email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone of the contact person.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department that the program belongs to.
* **Departmental E-mail** (`departmental_email`, email, optional): Enter the departmental email.
* **Departmental Phone** (`departmental_phone`, text, optional): Enter the departmental phone number.
* **Departmental Website** (`departmental_website`, text, required): Enter the departmental website.
* **Prog Goals Goals** (`program_goals_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm if the program has program goals.
* **Grad Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter the graduation date for the selected **Graduation Year**.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, required): Select **Submit** to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the completion rate report is reliable.
* If you are not sure about any field, refer to your internal university records or contact the relevant departmental staff for assistance.
* Please note that some fields are not required but are still important for providing additional context to the report.
* Before submitting the form, ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* You can refer to your university's internal records or contact the relevant departmental staff if you need help in filling out this form.
