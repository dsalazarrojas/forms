# Academic Department Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate the performance of an academic department. It assesses various aspects of the department's activities, including teaching quality, research productivity, student support, and overall performance. This form is meant to be used by department heads, evaluation committees, or similar stakeholders to provide a comprehensive assessment of the department's performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the "Department name" field with the name of the academic department being evaluated.
2.  Enter the name of the person completing the evaluation in the "Evaluator name" field.
3.  Specify the evaluation period in the "Evaluation period" field.
4.  For each section (Teaching Quality, Research Productivity, and Student Support), select the rating that best describes the department's performance.
5.  In the "Department strengths" field, list the key strengths observed during the evaluation.
6.  In the "Areas for improvement" field, suggest recommended actions or focus areas for the department.
7.  In the "Overall recommendation" field, provide a summary recommendation for the department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Department name** (`Department name`, text, required): Enter the name of the academic department being evaluated.
*   **Evaluator name** (`Evaluator name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing the evaluation.
*   **Evaluation period** (`Evaluation period`, text, required): Specify the term or academic year under review.
*   **Teaching Quality Rating** (`Teaching quality rating`, select_one, required): Rate the department's overall teaching effectiveness as:
    +   Excellent
    +   Good
    +   Satisfactory
    +   Needs improvement
    +   Unsatisfactory
*   **Research Productivity Rating** (`Research productivity rating`, select_one, required): Rate the departmental research output as:
    +   Excellent
    +   Good
    +   Satisfactory
    +   Needs improvement
    +   Unsatisfactory
*   **Student Support and Advising Rating** (`Student support and advising rating`, select_one, required): Rate the support services for students as:
    +   Excellent
    +   Good
    +   Satisfactory
    +   Needs improvement
    +   Unsatisfactory
*   **Department Strengths** (`Department strengths`, text, optional): List the key strengths observed during the evaluation.
*   **Areas for Improvement** (`Areas for improvement`, text, optional): Suggest recommended actions or focus areas for the department.
*   **Overall Recommendation** (`Overall recommendation`, text, required): Provide a summary recommendation for the department.

## Tips

*   Ensure you fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Be specific with your ratings and recommendations.
*   Provide constructive and actionable suggestions for improvement.
*   Proofread the form before submitting it.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
