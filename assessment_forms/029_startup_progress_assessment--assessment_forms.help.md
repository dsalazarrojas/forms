# Startup Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Progress Assessment form is designed to help startups track and evaluate their progress in various areas of development. This information will be used to identify areas of improvement and provide guidance and support where needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field from the dropdown menus or enter your text response in the text fields.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Stage** (startup_stage):
	+ This field asks for the current stage of your startup, which is a measure of your startup's development stage.
	+ Select the option that best describes your startup's current stage: Early Stage, Growth Stage, Established Stage, or Mature Stage.
* **Milestones Achieved** (milestones_achieved):
	+ This field asks for a list of milestones you have achieved in your startup, such as Product-Market Fit, Revenue Runway Established, Traction, Regulatory Approval, Funding, User Acquisition, Market Share, or Product Development.
	+ Select all the milestones that apply to your startup.
* **Challenges Faced** (challenges):
	+ This field allows you to enter any challenges or issues you have faced in your startup journey.
	+ Provide a brief description of the challenges you have faced.
* **Short-Term Goals** (short_term_goals):
	+ This field asks for your short-term goals for your startup, such as Product Development, Revenue Growth, Team Building, Marketing, Sales, or Customer Acquisition.
	+ Select all the goals that apply to your startup.
* **Self-Assessment of Progress** (startup_progress):
	+ This field is an opportunity for you to self-assess your progress in your startup, providing any additional information or insights you feel are relevant.
	+ Provide a detailed description of your startup's progress.
* **Review Frequency** (review_frequency):
	+ This field asks for how frequently you would like to be reviewed, which is a measure of how often you would like guidance and support from us.
	+ Select the review frequency that best suits your needs: Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Biannually, or Annually.
* **Mentoring Needs** (mentoring_needed):
	+ This field asks for any mentoring or guidance you may need in specific areas such as Strategy, Operations, Product Development, Funding, Regulatory, Team Building, Marketing, or Sales.
	+ Select all the areas where you feel you need mentoring or guidance.
* **Review Comments** (review_comments):
	+ This field is a space for you to provide any additional comments or feedback about your startup.
	+ Enter any relevant information or insights you feel are important.
* **Reviewer Comments** (reviewer_comments):
	+ This field is a space for our reviewer to provide feedback and guidance based on your submitted information.
	+ Please do not enter any information in this field.
