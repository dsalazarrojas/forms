# Sentence Clarity Evaluation Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Sentence Clarity Evaluation Questionnaire" form is used to assess the clarity of a given sentence. It consists of five fields that help you evaluate how well the sentence communicates its meaning to different audiences. This form is meant to be a quick and easy way to get feedback on the clarity of your writing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, select the option that best answers "Is the sentence clear?" from the dropdown menu on page 1. You can choose multiple options, so select all that apply.
2. On page 2, choose which audience you are evaluating the sentence for. You can choose from three options:
	* General Public: The sentence should be easy to understand for people without a specialized background in the subject matter.
	* Academic: The sentence should be clear for people with a background in the subject matter, but may require some technical knowledge.
	* Academic and General Public: The sentence should be clear for both general and academic audiences.
3. On page 3, rate the sentence's clarity on a scale from 1 to 5, where 1 is not clear at all and 5 is very clear.
4. In the "Additional comments" section, you can provide any extra feedback you have on the sentence. This can include specific words or phrases that are unclear.
5. Finally, on page 5, simply type in the sentence you would like to evaluate for clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is the sentence clear?** (`sentence_clarity`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all options that apply to describe the sentence's clarity.
* **Which audience** (`audience`, `select_one`, required): Choose which audience you are evaluating the sentence for.
* **How clear?** (`score`, `number`, required): Rate the sentence's clarity on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required): Provide any extra feedback you have on the sentence.
* **The sentence** (`sentence`, `text`, required): Type in the sentence you would like to evaluate for clarity.

## Tips

* Be honest and objective when answering the first three questions.
* Use clear and concise language when providing additional comments.
* Remember that clarity can vary depending on the context and purpose of the sentence.
