# Lead Scoring Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lead Scoring Assessment Form is used to evaluate the potential of a lead to be a good fit for a business opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For questions with a time or date field, enter the exact value in the format specified in the field.
4. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Lead Scoring Form 1** (`text`, optional)
This field is a text input field where you can provide any additional information about the lead that is not covered in the other questions.

### **Lead Scoring Form 2** (`select_multiple`, optional)
This field allows you to choose all that apply from the options provided. Select 'Yes' if the lead has demonstrated the desired behavior or characteristic.

### **Lead Scoring Form 3** (`text`, optional)
This field is another text input field for any further details about the lead.

### **Lead Scoring Form 4** (`time`, optional)
Enter the exact time in the format specified in the field. This will help in assessing the lead's availability for a project.

### **Lead Scoring Form 5** (`date`, optional)
Enter the exact date in the format specified in the field. This will help in assessing the lead's availability for a project.

### **Lead Scoring Form 6** (`select_one`, optional)
This field allows you to choose only one option that best describes the lead's behavior or characteristic. Select 'Yes' if the lead has demonstrated the desired behavior.

### **Lead Scoring Form 7** (`select_one`, optional)
This field is similar to Lead Scoring Form 6. Select 'Yes' if the lead has demonstrated the desired behavior.

### **Lead Scoring Form 8** (`text`, optional)
This field is another text input field for any further details about the lead.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers to ensure accurate scoring.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact your sales representative.
