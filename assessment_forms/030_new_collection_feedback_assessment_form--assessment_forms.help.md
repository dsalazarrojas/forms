# New Collection Feedback Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The New Collection Feedback Assessment Form is used to collect feedback on a collection's quality, usability, and overall performance. This form is designed to help assessors evaluate and improve the collection's content, design, and overall user experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing feedback on each field.
2. Be as detailed and specific as possible with your feedback.
3. Make sure to only select options that accurately reflect your opinions and experiences with the collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Collection Name** (2): Enter the name of the collection being assessed.
* **Collection Image** (3): Upload or enter a link to the collection's image or screenshot.
* **Collection Description** (4): Provide a brief description of the collection, including any relevant context or background information.
* **Collection Price Range** (5): Enter the collection's price range, if applicable.
* **Collection Release Date** (6): Enter the date the collection was released or is expected to be released.
* **Collection Sentiment** (7): Choose how you feel about the collection, selecting from Positive, Neutral, or Negative.
* **Collection Urgency** (8): Choose how urgent you feel the collection is, selecting from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Collection Frequency** (9): Choose how often you use or interact with the collection, selecting from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Collection Importance** (10): Choose how important you think the collection is, selecting from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Collection Next Steps** (11): Choose any next steps or actions that can be taken to improve the collection, selecting from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Collection Feedback** (12): Enter any general feedback or comments about the collection.
* **Collection Additional Feedback** (13): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the collection.
* **What Did You Like** (14): Enter what you liked about the collection.
* **What Did You Not Like** (15): Enter what you did not like about the collection.
* **What Should Improve Next Time** (16): Enter any suggestions for improvement for the collection.
* **Next Steps for Improvement** (17): Enter any specific actions that can be taken to improve the collection.
* **What to Improve** (18): Enter any specific areas of the collection that need improvement.
* **Improvement Plan** (19): Enter a plan for how the collection can be improved.
* **Improvement Status** (20): Choose the status of the collection's improvement plan, selecting from Completed, Ongoing, or Not Started.
* **Improvement Priority** (21): Choose the priority of the collection's improvement plan, selecting from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Who to Invite** (22): Enter any specific people or teams that can help with the collection's improvement plan.
* **Next Release Date** (23): Enter the expected date for the collection's next release or update.
* **Last Reviewed Date** (24): Enter the date the collection was last reviewed.
* **Last Reviewed By** (25): Enter the name of the person who last reviewed the collection.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form fields and may not cover every possible scenario or option. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please reach out to the collection owner or moderator for guidance.
