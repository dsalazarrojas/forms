# Head To Toe Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Head To Toe Assessment Form is a medical assessment tool used to evaluate a patient's overall condition and identify potential areas of concern. This form is used to gather information about a patient's physical status, including vital signs, muscle strength, and sensory and motor functions. It is an essential tool for healthcare professionals to gather comprehensive data and inform treatment decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's ID number in the "Patient ID" field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
3. Select the date of the visit from the "Date of Visit" field.
4. Select the time of the visit from the "Time of Visit" field.
5. Enter the reason for the visit in the "Reason for Visit" field.
6. Select the patient's current condition from the "Patient Condition" field.
7. Select the patient's vital signs from the "Vital Signs" field.
8. Select the patient's muscle strength from the "Muscle Strength" field.
9. Select the patient's muscle tone from the "Muscle Tone" field.
10. Select the patient's current muscle atrophy status from the "Muscle Atrophy" field.
11. Select the patient's sensory functions from the "Sensory" field.
12. Select the patient's motor functions from the "Motor Function" field.
13. Select the patient's cognitive function from the "Cognitive Function" field.
14. Enter the patient's current status in the "Patient Status" field.
15. Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition in the "Physician Notes" field.
16. Sign and date the form by entering your name and signature in the "Physician Signature" field.
17. Enter the date and time of the next visit in the "Date of Next Visit" field.
18. Select the tentative date and time of the next visit from the "Date of Next Visit (Tentative)" field.
19. Select the time of the next visit from the "Time of Next Visit" field.
20. Enter any next visit comments or notes in the "Next Visit Comments" field.
21. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Patient History" field.
22. Enter any additional comments or notes from the physician in the "Physician Comments" field.
23. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Head to Toe Notes" field.
