# AI Maturity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Maturity Assessment Form is designed to evaluate your organization's current state of AI implementation and identify areas for improvement. The form assesses four dimensions: Data Maturity, Infrastructure & Technology, Talent & Culture, and Strategy & Governance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions as accurately and completely as possible based on your current understanding of your organization's AI efforts.
2. Be sure to answer all required questions, but feel free to skip optional questions if you're unsure or lack information.
3. If you're unsure about the relevance of a question, provide your best effort and reach out to us if you need clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization / Department Name**: Enter your organization's name or department name.
* **Respondent's Role**: Select the role of the person completing this form (e.g., CTO, Head of AI, Operations Manager).
* **Industry Sector**: Enter your organization's industry sector (e.g., Finance, Healthcare, Retail).
* **Data Accessibility & Silos (1-5)**: Rate your organization's data accessibility and silos, with 1 being highly siloed/inaccessible and 5 being centrally accessible data lake.
* **Data Quality & Governance (1-5)**: Rate your organization's data quality and governance, with 1 being poor/noisy and 5 being high accuracy/standardized.
* **MLOps & Lifecycle Management (1-5)**: Rate your organization's MLOps and lifecycle management, with 1 being no MLOps (manual only) and 5 being advanced/self-healing systems.
* **In-house AI Expertise**: Select the level of in-house AI expertise, with None (Outsourced only), Small/Incubating team, Growing/Core capabilities, Strong/Distributed expertise, or World-class/Industry leading.
* **Executive AI Literacy & Support**: Rate your organization's executive AI literacy and support, with 1 being low/skeptical and 5 being visionary/core to identity.
* **AI Vision and Roadmap (1-3)**: Select the level of AI roadmap existence, with No roadmap exists, Short-term/Project-based only, or Defined 1-3 year roadmap.
* **Ethical AI & Compliance Framework**: Select the level of ethical AI and compliance framework, with None, Informally followed, Formally documented & training provided, or Strict enforcement & auditing in place.
* **Current Number of AI Models in Production**: Enter the current number of AI models in production.
* **Primary Bottleneck to Scaling AI**: Select the primary bottleneck to scaling AI, with Data Quality / Availability, Budget / Funding, Talent / Skill Gap, Legacy Infrastructure, Culture / Resistance to Change, or Regulatory / Legal Concerns.
* **Additional Comments on Organizational Readiness**: Provide any additional comments on your organization's AI readiness.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Email for Detailed Maturity Report**: Enter the email address to receive a detailed AI maturity report.

**Tips**:

* Be honest and accurate in your answers, as they will help us provide a more accurate assessment.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* The assessment results will be used to identify areas of improvement and provide recommendations for your organization.
* Please keep in mind that this form is a starting point for further discussion and discovery, and your feedback is valuable to us.
