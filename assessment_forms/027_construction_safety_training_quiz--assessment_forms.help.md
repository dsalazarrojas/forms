# Construction Safety Training Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Safety Training Quiz is designed to assess your knowledge and understanding of construction safety procedures and regulations. This quiz covers various topics related to construction safety, including fall protection, scaffolding, and confined space safety. The quiz is intended to ensure that participants have a good grasp of safety protocols and procedures to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Construction Safety Training Quiz, follow these steps:

1.  Select the **Quiz Date** field and enter the date of assessment.
2.  Choose the **Training Program** that this quiz is for (e.g., General Safety Orientation, Fall Protection, etc.).
3.  Enter your **Participant Name** in the text field.
4.  Enter your **Employee ID** (if applicable).
5.  Enter your **Company Name**.
6.  Enter your **Job Title**.
7.  Answer the **questions** (e.g., **What Is the Leading Cause of Death in Construction**?).
8.  Click the **Submit** button to review and submit your answers.
