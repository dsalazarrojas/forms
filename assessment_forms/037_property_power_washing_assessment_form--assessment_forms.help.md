# Property Power Washing Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess the condition and needs of a property's exterior for power washing. It will help property managers, owners, or maintenance teams determine the best course of action for power washing and maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each field as accurately as possible based on your assessment of the property's condition.
2. If a field is not applicable to your property, leave it blank.
3. Use the options in each field to select the most accurate description of the property's condition or features.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Details** (`property_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the property, including its address, size, and any notable features.
* **Service Needs** (`service_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the services that the property needs, such as power washing, repair, or replacement.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
* **Site Layout** (`site_layout`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property layout, such as single-story or double-story.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, note, optional): Provide a note about the person you are contacting, such as their name and role.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person you are contacting.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person you are contacting.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the property.
* **Site Accessibility** (`site_accessibility`, select_one, optional): Select the level of difficulty in accessing the property, such as easy, moderate, or difficult.
* **Roof Type** (`roof_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of roof, such as flat, gable, or hip.
* **Roof Condition** (`roof_condition`, select_one, optional): Select the condition of the roof, such as excellent, good, fair, or poor.
* **Gutter Condition** (`gutter_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the conditions that apply to the gutters, such as good, fair.
* **Down Spout Condition** (`downspout_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the conditions that apply to the downspouts, such as good, fair, bad.
* **Deck Condition** (`deck_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the conditions that apply to the deck, such as good, fair.
* **Door Condition** (`door_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the conditions that apply to the doors, such as good, fair.
* **Door Type** (`door_type`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the types that apply to the doors, such as hinged, pivoting.
* **Siding Type** (`siding_type`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the types that apply to the siding, such as vinyl, fiber cement, stucco.
* **Siding Color** (`siding_color`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the colors that apply to the siding, such as white, off-white, light blue, dark blue, brown.
* **Windows Condition** (`windows_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the conditions that apply to the windows, such as good, fair, bad.
* **Window Type** (`window_type`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the types that apply to the windows, such as double-hung, hopper, single-hung, casemented, sliding.
* **Door Locks** (`door_locks`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the conditions that apply to the door locks, such as good, fair, bad.
