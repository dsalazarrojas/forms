# Audiovisual Culture Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audiovisual Culture Assessment form aims to gather information about your audiovisual culture, including demographic characteristics, viewing habits, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select your answer for each field based on your personal experiences and attitudes.
2.  Be accurate and honest in your responses, as they will be used to create a comprehensive understanding of your audiovisual culture.
3.  Make sure to complete all required fields to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Age Range** (`respondent_age`): This question asks for your age range to understand your generational perspective in the audiovisual culture.
* **Gender** (`respondent_gender`): This question is to gather information on your gender identity, which can influence your viewing preferences.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`): This question aims to understand your educational background and how it may impact your viewing habits.
* **Location Type** (`location_type`): This question helps to identify your living situation and how it may relate to your audiovisual preferences.
* **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`): This is a required field to identify your country of residence.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): Please select the date of this assessment.
* **Weekly Screen Time** (`weekly_screen_time`): This question is to gather information on how many hours you spend on screens per week.
* **Primary Viewing Device** (`primary_viewing_device`): This question asks which device you use most for viewing.
* **Streaming Services Used** (`streaming_services_used`): Select all the streaming services you use.
* **Content Preferences** (`content_preferences`): Choose the types of content you prefer to watch.
* **Genre Preferences** (`genre_preferences`): Select your favorite genres of content.
* **Viewing Habits** (`viewing_habits`): This question is to understand when you typically watch audiovisual content.
* **Viewing Style** (`viewing_style`): Choose how you usually watch audiovisual content.
* **Binge Watching Frequency** (`binge_watching_frequency`): This question is to understand how often you binge-watch content.
* **Social Media Influence** (`social_media_influence`): This question is to gather information on how social media influences your viewing decisions.
* **Subtitle Usage** (`subtitle_usage`): This question is to understand how often you use subtitles.
* **International Content Interest** (`international_content_interest`): This question is to gauge your interest in foreign language content.
* **Advertising Tolerance** (`advertising_tolerance`): This question is to understand your attitude towards advertising in audiovisual content.
* **Content Discovery** (`content_discovery`): Select how you find new content.
* **Future Viewing Trends** (`future_viewing_trends`): Choose the trends you expect to follow in the audiovisual culture.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, refer to the explanations above.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses to provide valuable insights into your audiovisual culture.
