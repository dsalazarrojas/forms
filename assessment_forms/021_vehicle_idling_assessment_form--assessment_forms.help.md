# Vehicle Idling Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about vehicle idling habits. It's used to identify areas for improvement and optimize vehicle performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Review the options for each field and choose the most accurate answer for your situation.
* Answer each field thoroughly and accurately.
* Submit the form when you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Idling Assessment** (`vehicle_idling_assessment_form_page_one`, Select Multiple, Optional): 
  This field asks whether you've observed any idling habits in the vehicle. Choose 'Yes' if you've noticed idling, and 'No' if not.
* **Vehicle Idling Habits** (idling_habits, Note, Optional): 
  This field is for providing additional information about the vehicle's idling habits. Type any relevant details, such as frequency, duration, or specific circumstances where idling occurs.
* **Fleet Management** (fleet_management, Text, Optional): 
  Use this field to explain any fleet management practices or decisions related to idling. Describe any actions you've taken to improve vehicle idling or plans to implement in the future.

## Tips
* Ensure you answer each field honestly and accurately to provide reliable data.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Submit the form only when you're confident in your answers.
