# Quarterly Food Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quarterly Food Safety Assessment Form is designed to help food service teams conduct a thorough assessment of their safety protocols on a quarterly basis. This form guides them in documenting their review results, corrective actions, follow-up actions, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each quarter.
2. Enter the review date in the `Review Date` field.
3. Select the review type from the options provided in the `Review Type` field.
4. Enter the assessment results in the `Assessment Results` field.
5. Identify the food safety risks in the `Food Safety Risks` field.
6. Document the corrective actions taken in the `Corrective Actions` field.
7. Enter any follow-up actions in the `Follow-up Actions` field.
8. Provide the name and title of the person who conducted the review in the `Review Conducted By` field.
9. Enter your name and contact information in the `Form Submitted By` field.
10. Select the review status from the options provided in the `Review Status` field.
11. Enter any notes regarding the review status in the `Review Status Note` field.
12. Record the submission status in the `Submission Status` field.
13. Enter any submission status notes in the `Submission Status Note` field.
14. Enter any follow-up status notes in the `Follow-up Status Note` field.
15. Document the person responsible for follow-up actions in the `Follow-up Status Note Who` field.
16. Record the follow-up status date in the `Follow-up Status Note When` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Service Team**: Enter the name of your food service team.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Type**: Select the type of review (Quarterly or Monthly).
* **Assessment Results**: Enter the results of your assessment.
* **Food Safety Risks**: List any food safety risks identified.
* **Corrective Actions**: Document any actions taken to address food safety risks.
* **Follow-up Actions**: Document any follow-up actions taken.
* **Review Conducted By**: Enter the name and title of the person who conducted the review.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Review Status**: Select the current status of the review (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **Review Status Note**: Enter any notes regarding the review status.
* **Submission Status**: Select the submission status of the form (Complete, In Progress, or Not Complete).
* **Submission Status Note**: Enter any submission status notes.
* **Submission Status Note Who**: Enter the person responsible for submission.
* **Submission Status Note When**: Record the submission status date.
* **Follow-up Status Note**: Enter any follow-up status notes.
* **Follow-up Status Note Who**: Enter the person responsible for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Status Note When**: Record the follow-up status date.
