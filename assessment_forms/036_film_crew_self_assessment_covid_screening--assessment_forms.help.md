# Film Crew Self Assessment COVID Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Crew Self Assessment COVID Screening form is designed to help film crew members assess their risk level and report their health status in relation to COVID-19. This form will help identify potential risks and ensure a safe working environment on set.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answer for each question based on your most recent health status and circumstances.
2. Answer all questions thoroughly, but only answer questions that apply to your situation.
3. Use the options provided in each question to select your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant health information that you believe is important to share about your health status.
* **What is your risk level?** (`risk_level`, select_one, optional):
	+ High: You have been in close contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19, or you have traveled to a high-risk area.
	+ Low: You have not been in close contact with someone diagnosed with COVID-19, and you have not traveled to a high-risk area.
* **Have you traveled internationally in the past 14 days?** (`travel_info`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: Yes, traveled internationally in the past 14 days.
	+ Option 2: No, not traveled internationally in the past 14 days.
	+ Option 3: Unable to answer due to lack of knowledge.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms_info`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: Fever
	+ Option 2: Cough
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify in General Information)
* **Have you been exposed to COVID-19?** (`exposure_info`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: Yes, have been exposed to someone diagnosed with COVID-19.
	+ Option 2: No, not exposed to someone diagnosed with COVID-19.
	+ Option 3: Unable to answer due to lack of knowledge.
* **Testing** (`testing_info`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: Tested positive for COVID-19.
	+ Option 2: Tested negative for COVID-19.
	+ Option 3: Unable to answer due to lack of knowledge.
* **Vaccination status** (`vaccination_info`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Option 1: Fully vaccinated.
	+ Option 2: Partially vaccinated.
	+ Option 3: Not vaccinated.
* **Other Information** (`other_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional health information that you believe is relevant to your health status.

## Tips
- Answer all questions thoroughly, but only answer questions that apply to your situation.
- If you are unsure about any question, select "Unable to answer due to lack of knowledge" and include the reason in the General Information field.
- If you have any health concerns or questions, contact your production team or a medical professional for guidance.
