# Go To Market Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to walk you through the Go To Market Strategy Assessment form, which is used to gather essential information about your go-to-market strategy.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please review the form and ensure that you have all the necessary information before beginning.
2.  Complete each section carefully, providing accurate and complete information where required.
3.  Use the hints provided as a guide to ensure that you are capturing the right information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Market Strategy** (`market_strategy`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the overall market strategy for your product or service. Please provide a brief description of your market approach.
*   **Product Launch Plan** (`product_launch_plan`, text, required): This field is used to outline the plan for launching your product or service. Please provide a detailed description of your launch plan, including key milestones and timelines.
*   **Target Market** (`target_market`, text, required): This field is used to identify your target audience. Please describe the demographics, needs, and preferences of your ideal customer.
*   **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the marketing channels you plan to use. Please choose one or more from the provided options: Social Media, Email, Print, or Digital.
*   **Product Features** (`product_features`, text, required): This field is used to list the key features of your product or service. Please provide a comprehensive list of features that will appeal to your target market.
*   **Sales Funnel** (`sales_funnel`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your sales process. Please outline the key stages of the sales process, from lead generation to conversion.
*   **Revenue Streams** (`revenue_streams`, text, optional): This field is used to describe the revenue streams for your product or service. Please provide a detailed description of the revenue models you plan to use.
*   **Competitor Analysis** (`competitor_analysis`, text, optional): This field is used to research your competitors. Please analyze the strengths and weaknesses of your competitors and how they compare to your product or service.
*   **Pricing Model** (`pricing_model`, select_one, required): This field is used to define your pricing model. Please choose one from the provided options: Tiered Pricing or Tiered Plus.
*   **Sales Model** (`sales_model`, select_one, required): This field is used to define your sales model. Please choose one from the provided options: Direct or Partnership.
*   **Customer Acquisition** (`customer_acquisition`, text, required): This field is used to define your customer acquisition strategy. Please describe the tactics you plan to use to attract new customers.
*   **Customer Retention** (`customer_retention`, text, required): This field is used to define your customer retention strategy. Please describe the tactics you plan to use to retain customers.
*   **Revenue Growth** (`revenue_growth`, number, optional): This field is used to track revenue growth. Please provide a number indicating the expected revenue growth rate.
*   **Go To Market Plan** (`go_to_market_plan`, text, required): This field is used to describe your go-to-market plan. Please provide a comprehensive plan outlining key activities and timelines.
*   **Key Performance Indicators** (`key_performance_indicators`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to track KPIs. Please choose one or more from the provided options: Website Traffic, Social Media Engagement, or Customer Lifetime Value.
*   **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`, number, optional): This field is used to define your marketing budget. Please provide a number indicating the allocated budget.
*   **Sales and Revenue** (`sales_and_revenue`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your sales and revenue metrics. Please provide a detailed description of your sales and revenue performance.
*   **Product Launch Date** (`product_launch_date`, date, required): This field is used to define your product launch date. Please provide a date indicating the launch date.
*   **Sales Team** (`sales_team`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your sales team. Please provide a list of team members and their roles.
*   **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, text, optional): This field is used to describe your customer satisfaction metrics. Please provide a detailed description of how you measure customer satisfaction.
*   **Customer Satisfaction Score** (`customer_satisfaction_score`, number, optional): This field is used to define your customer satisfaction target. Please provide a number indicating the target score.
*   **Customer Satisfaction Target** (`customer_satisfaction_target`, number, optional): This field is used to define your customer satisfaction target. Please provide a number indicating the target score.
*   **Market Share** (`market_share`, number, optional): This field is used to define your market share. Please provide a number indicating the market share.
*   **Go To Market Cost** (`go_to_market_cost`, number, optional): This field is used to define your go-to-market cost. Please provide a number indicating the cost.
*   **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): This field is used to describe next steps. Please provide a list of key activities and timelines.

Please note that this form is a dynamic document and is subject to change as the go-to-market strategy evolves. It is recommended that you review and update the form regularly to ensure that the information remains accurate and relevant.
