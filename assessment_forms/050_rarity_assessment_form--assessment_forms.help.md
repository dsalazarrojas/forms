# Rarity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rarity Assessment Form is used to evaluate the rarity of various items. The form asks for specific information about the item to help our team provide an accurate assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out all required fields**, including the item's name, type, condition, year made, and item description.
2. **Choose the correct options** from the dropdown menus for the item's type, collector, appraiser, and reseller status.
3. **Select the correct rarity** from the dropdown menu.
4. **Enter the item price and year made**.
5. **Upload or reference an image** of the item, if applicable.
6. **Submit the form** once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Item Name** (`item_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the item being assessed.
* **Item Type** (`item_type`, select_one, **not required**): Choose the type of item from the dropdown menu.
	+ If the item is a collectible, select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
* **Condition** (`condition`, select_one, **not required**): Choose the condition of the item from the dropdown menu.
	+ "Yes" indicates the item is in good condition.
	+ "No" indicates the item is not in good condition.
* **Rarity** (`rarity_rare`, select_one, **not required**): Choose the rarity of the item from the dropdown menu.
	+ "Rare" indicates the item is rare.
	+ "Uncommon" indicates the item is uncommon.
	+ "Common" indicates the item is common.
* **Rarity Moderate** (`rarity_moderate`, select_one, **not required**): Choose the moderate rarity of the item from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields to ensure accurate assessment.
* Choose the correct options from the dropdown menus to help our team evaluate the item's rarity accurately.
