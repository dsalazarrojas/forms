# outcome_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to conduct an outcome assessment, which is a process to evaluate the effectiveness and impact of a particular outcome or program. The form helps gather information on various aspects of the outcome, including its effectiveness, achievements, impact, benefits, and adverse effects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and understand the questions and fields provided.
2. Answer all the questions truthfully and accurately, based on the outcome being assessed.
3. Fill in the relevant fields with the required information.
4. If a field is not applicable, you can indicate it with "N/A" (Not Applicable).
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **introduction** (1, text, required): This is the introduction section where you can provide a brief overview of the outcome being assessed.
- **outcome assessment** (2, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if you agree that the outcome has been achieved, and "No" otherwise.
- **program name** (3, text, required): Enter the name of the program being assessed.
- **start date** (4, date, required): Enter the start date of the program or outcome being assessed.
- **end date** (5, date, required): Enter the end date of the program or outcome being assessed.
- **outcome effectiveness** (6, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the outcome on a scale from "Very effective" to "Not at all effective".
- **outcome achievements** (7, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the outcome achieved any of the following: "True" or "False".
- **other (if applicable)** (8, text, required): If none of the above options apply, you can provide a brief description of the outcome achievements.
- **outcome impact** (9, text, required): Describe the impact of the outcome, in a few words.
- **outcome benefits** (10, text, required): List the benefits of the outcome.
- **outcome adverse effects** (11, text, required): List any adverse effects of the outcome.
- **outcome recommendations** (12, text, required): Suggest any recommendations for future outcomes.
- **outcome sustainability** (13, select_one, required): Select "Sustained" if the outcome has been sustained, or "Not sustained" otherwise.
- **other (if applicable)** (14, text, required): If none of the above options apply, you can provide a brief description of the outcome sustainability.
- **outcome further actions** (15, text, required): Describe the next steps for the outcome.
- **outcome next steps** (16, text, required): Describe the next steps for the outcome.
- **outcome notes** (17, note, required): Add any additional notes about the outcome.
- **outcome additional info** (18, note, required): Add any additional information about the outcome.
- **other (if applicable)** (19, text, required): If none of the above options apply, you can provide a brief description of the outcome additional info.
- **outcome assessment rating** (20, number, required): Rate the outcome on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent".
- **outcome impact rating** (21, number, required): Rate the impact of the outcome on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Low" and 5 is "Very High".
- **outcome effectiveness rating** (22, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of the outcome on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Low" and 5 is "Very High".
- **outcome achievment** (23, text, required): Describe the outcome achievements in a few words.
- **outcome beneficial rating** (24, number, required): Rate the beneficial aspects of the outcome on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very Low" and 5 is "Very High".
- **outcome adverse effects rating** (25, number, required): Rate the adverse effects of the outcome on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very High" and 5 is "Very Low".
