# extracurricular_activity_success_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **extracurricular_activity_success_assessment** form is a self-assessment tool used by students to evaluate their experience in an extracurricular program or activity. It gathers feedback on various aspects of the activity, helping program administrators or teachers to understand student performance and make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the introduction to understand the purpose and context of the form.
2. Answer each question according to your experience and involvement in the extracurricular activity.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses to provide valuable feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section provides a brief background on the form's purpose and context.
* **Student Info**: This question asks if you, as a student, found the activity to be a positive experience.
  * If "Yes", you can proceed to further questions.
  * If "No", you might want to provide feedback on why you didn't find it positive.
* **Student Grade Level**: Rate your grade level of engagement in the activity, considering how much time and effort you invested.
* **Teacher Recommendation**: Rate the level of support and guidance you received from teachers or instructors.
* **Staff Support**: Rate the level of support you received from staff members or program administrators.
* **Student Feedback**: Share any comments or suggestions you have about your experience in the activity.
* **Staff Feedback**: If you received feedback from staff, you can record it here.
* **Student Progress**: Record your perception of your progress or growth during the activity.
* **Program Participation**: Rate the level of participation you had in the activity (high, moderate, or low).
