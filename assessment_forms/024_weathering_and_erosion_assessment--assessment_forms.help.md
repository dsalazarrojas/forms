# Weathering And Erosion Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the site's weathering and erosion status after a visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter a brief description of the site conditions in the `Site Conditions` field.
2. Provide a text-based description of your weathering observation in the `Weathering Observation` field.
3. Enter your observation of the erosion condition in the `Erosion Observation` field.
4. For both weathering and erosion, rate the condition using the numeric rating system.
5. In the `Recommended Actions` field, suggest potential actions to address any issues you observed.
6. Enter the date of your site visit in the `Site Visit Date` field.
7. Enter the time of your site visit in the `Site Visit Time` field.
8. Add any additional comments about your visit in the `User Comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Conditions** (`site_conditions`, text, optional): Describe the overall condition of the site.
* **Weathering Observation** (`weathering_observation`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your observation regarding weathering.
* **Erosion Observation** (`erosion_observation`, text, optional): Describe your observation of erosion.
* **Weathering Rating** (`weathering_rating`, number, optional): Use a numeric rating from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent) to rate the weathering condition.
* **Erosion Rating** (`erosion_rating`, number, optional): Use a numeric rating from 1 (poor) to 10 (excellent) to rate the erosion condition.
* **Recommended Actions** (`recommended_actions`, text, optional): Suggest potential actions to address any issues observed.
* **Site Visit Date** (`site_visit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your site visit.
* **Site Visit Time** (`site_visit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your site visit.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your visit.
