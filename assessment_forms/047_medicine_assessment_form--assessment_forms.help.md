# Medicine Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medicine Assessment Form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical assessment. This form captures essential details such as patient demographics, medical history, and submission specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Start on the "Patient Info" page**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other identifying information. This page is where you can enter basic patient data.
2. **Enter the patient's medical information** on the "Medical Info" page. This includes details about the patient's medical history, allergies, and medications.
3. **Assess the patient's condition** on the "Assessment" page. Provide a summary of the patient's medical status and any relevant medical findings.
4. **Enter submission details** on the "Submission Info" page. This includes information about when and how the assessment was submitted.
5. **Enter the submission date and time** on the "Submission Date" page. This is the date and time the assessment was submitted.
6. **Enter your contact information** on the "Submitter" page. This includes your name, email, and phone number.
7. **Enter any additional notes** on the "Submitter Notes" page. This is an optional field for any additional comments or details you'd like to include.
8. **Select your role** on the "Submitter Role" page. Choose from the dropdown menu to indicate your role as a healthcare provider (Clinic Staff, Pharmacist, or Provider).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info (Patient Info)** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other identifying information.
* **Medical Info (Medical Info)** (`text`, required: false): Enter details about the patient's medical history, allergies, and medications.
* **Assessment (Assessment)** (`text`, required: false): Provide a summary of the patient's medical status and any relevant medical findings.
* **Submission Info (Submission Info)** (`text`, required: false): Enter information about when and how the assessment was submitted.
* **Submission Date (Submission Date)** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date the assessment was submitted.
* **Submission Time (Submission Time)** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time the assessment was submitted.
* **Submitter (Submitter)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Submitter Email (Submitter Email)** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Phone (Submitter Phone)** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submitter Notes (Submitter Notes)** (`note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or details about the submission.
* **Submitter Role (Submitter Role)** (`select_one`, required: true): Select your role as a healthcare provider (Clinic Staff, Pharmacist, or Provider).
