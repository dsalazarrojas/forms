# JumpStart Personal Training Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form collects essential details from clients or patients to inform the creation of their personal training plan. It is intended to be completed once per individual and should not be shared or used for repeated assessments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please fill in all required fields and answer each question to the best of your ability. Required fields are marked as "required" and must be completed for the form to be submitted. Optional fields are not marked as "required" and can be left blank if you do not have the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last name**: (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address associated with your account, please enter it here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Contact phone**: (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
* **Date of birth**: (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Height**: (`height`, `number`, required): Please enter your height in centimeters (cm).
* **Medical conditions**: (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your training, please enter them here.
* **Medications**: (`medications`, `text`, optional): If you are taking any medications, please list them here.
* **Emergency contact**: (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Please enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency phone**: (`emergency_phone`, `text`, optional): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical history**: (`medical_history`, `note`, optional): If you have any relevant medical history, please provide a brief description.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed to submit the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your trainer or healthcare professional for guidance.
* Please keep your answers accurate and up-to-date.
* This form is intended for internal use only and should not be shared or used for repeated assessments.
