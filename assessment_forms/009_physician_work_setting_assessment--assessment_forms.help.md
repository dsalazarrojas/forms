# Physician Work Setting Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a physician's work setting, including work hours, work environment, and patient flow, to help healthcare stakeholders understand and improve the work of physicians in different settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the work setting where the physician works (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Ambulatory Surgery, Office, or Other).
2. Enter the physician's work hours per day for each day of the week (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday).
3. Assess the physician's work environment (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
4. Evaluate the physician's patient flow (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
5. If necessary, provide additional information about the physician's work setting and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Physician Information** (Page 1): This is the first page of the form, and it's used to identify the physician who is being assessed.
* **Work Setting** (Page 2): This field is required and asks you to select the work setting where the physician works (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Ambulatory Surgery, Office, or Other).
* **Work Hours** (Page 2): This field is required and asks you to enter the number of hours the physician works per day.
* **Work Hours on Holidays** (Page 2): This field is required and asks you to enter the number of hours the physician works on holidays.
* **Work Settings Urgency** (Page 2): This field is not required and asks you to select the level of urgency in the physician's work setting (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Other Work Settings** (Page 2): This field is not required and allows you to select additional work settings that are not listed (e.g., Other 1, Other 2, Other 3).
* **Other Work Setting Description** (Page 2): This field is not required and allows you to provide a description of the other work setting.
* **Physician Work Setting Comments** (Page 2): This field is not required and allows you to provide comments about the physician's work setting.
* **Work Environment** (Page 2): This field is required and asks you to select the level of the physician's work environment (e.g., Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Patient Flow** (Page 2): This field is required and asks you to select the level of the physician's patient flow (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Physician Workload** (Page 2): This field is required and asks you to select the level of the physician's workload (e.g., High, Medium, Low).

## Tips

* Please answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* This form is meant to provide an additional layer of information for decision-making purposes and is not meant to replace other evaluations or assessments.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
