# Financial Advisory Risk Tolerance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Advisory Risk Tolerance Assessment is a tool used to understand your risk tolerance and investment preferences. It helps us to provide personalized financial advice and investment strategies that suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the risk tolerance options that apply to you.
2. Choose your investment horizon for the assessment.
3. Select your risk level for investments.
4. Choose your preferred investment return goal.
5. Select your tax status.
6. (Optional) Enter the value of your investment assets.
7. Select your level of risk awareness.
8. Choose your level of investment experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Financial Risk Tolerance** (`financial_risk_tolerance`, select_multiple): Please select all the risk tolerance options that apply to you. These options include:
	+ Yes: You are willing to take on some level of risk in your investments.
	+ No: You are not willing to take on any level of risk in your investments.
* **Investment Horizon** (`investment_horizon`, select_one): This question helps us understand how far ahead you are looking to plan your investments. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Less than 5 years
	+ 5-10 years
	+ 10-15 years
	+ More than 15 years
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one): Please select the risk level that best describes your investment strategy:
	+ Conservative: Low risk, stable returns.
	+ Moderate: Moderate risk, moderate returns.
	+ Aggressive: High risk, potential for higher returns.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`, select_one): Please select the primary goal for your investments:
	+ Income: Focus on generating regular income.
	+ Capital preservation: Focus on preserving your capital.
	+ Growth: Focus on growing your wealth over time.
* **Tax Status** (`tax_status`, select_one): Please select the tax status that applies to you:
	+ Individual: You are a solo investor.
	+ Joint: You are an investor with a joint account or partnership.
	+ Trust: You are an investor with a trust account.
* **Investment Assets** (`investment_assets`, number): (Optional) Please enter the value of your investment assets. This will help us understand your current investment position.
* **Risk Awareness** (`risk_awareness`, select_one): Please select the level of risk awareness that best describes your investment experience:
	+ Low: Limited investment experience or knowledge.
	+ Medium: Moderate investment experience or knowledge.
	+ High: Advanced investment experience or knowledge.
* **Investment Experience** (`investment_experience`, select_one): Please select the level of investment experience that best describes your current situation:
	+ Beginner: Limited investment experience.
	+ Intermediate: Moderate investment experience.
	+ Advanced: Extensive investment experience.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when answering these questions, as they will help us provide you with personalized financial advice.
* If you are unsure about any of the options, please contact us for guidance.
* This assessment is not a substitute for professional investment advice, but rather a tool to help us understand your risk tolerance and investment preferences.
