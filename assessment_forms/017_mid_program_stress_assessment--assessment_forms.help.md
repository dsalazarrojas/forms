# Mid-Program Stress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mid-Program Stress Assessment is a self-assessment tool designed to help individuals evaluate their stress levels and experiences within a program. It is meant to be a personal reflection tool, and responses will be kept confidential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer questions honestly.
2. Provide accurate and clear responses to each question.
3. Make sure to review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `note`, optional): 
This field is a free-text area where you can provide any thoughts, comments, or feedback about your experiences within the program.
* **Participant** (`participant`, `email`, optional): 
This field is where you can enter your email address. However, please note that this field is not required for any specific functionality.
* **Program support** (`program_support`, `select_one`, optional): 
Choose the level of support you have received from the program. Select from the options: Program support 1, Program support 2, Program support 3.
  - Program support 1: You have received some support from the program.
  - Program support 2: You have received moderate support from the program.
  - Program support 3: You have received strong support from the program.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Take your time to review each question carefully.
- If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
- This form is intended for personal reflection and is not intended to be a formal evaluation.
