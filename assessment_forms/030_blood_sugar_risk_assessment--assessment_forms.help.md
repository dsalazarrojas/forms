# Blood Sugar Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Blood Sugar Risk Assessment form is designed to help individuals understand their risk factors for developing high blood sugar levels. It collects information on demographic and lifestyle factors that contribute to the risk of high blood sugar levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Select your gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your ethnicity from the "Ethnicity" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your height in centimeters in the "Height (cm)" field.
8. Enter your weight in kilograms in the "Weight (kg)" field.
9. Select your BMI category from the "BMI Category" dropdown menu.
10. Answer "Yes", "No", or "Unknown" to indicate if you have a family history of diabetes in the "Family history of diabetes?" field.
11. If you have had a previous fasting glucose test, enter the result in mg/dL in the "Previous fasting glucose test result?" field.
12. Select "Yes", "No", or "Not sure" to indicate if you have been diagnosed with pre-diabetes in the "Ever diagnosed with pre-diabetes" field.
13. Select "Yes", "No", or "Not applicable" to indicate if you have a history of gestational diabetes in the "History of gestational diabetes?" field.
14. Choose how often you engage in physical activity from the "Physical activity frequency" dropdown menu.
15. Select the quality of your diet from the "Diet quality" dropdown menu.
16. Enter your stress level on a scale of 1-10 in the "Stress level" field.
17. Enter the number of hours you sleep per night in the "Average sleep hours per night" field.
18. Select "Never used", "Former user", or "Current user" to indicate your tobacco use in the "Tobacco use" field.
19. Select "None", "Moderate", or "Heavy" to indicate your alcohol consumption in the "Alcohol consumption" field.
20. Answer "Yes", "No", or "Not sure" to indicate if you have high blood pressure in the "Do you have high blood pressure?" field.
21. Answer "Yes", "No", or "Not sure" to indicate if you have high cholesterol in the "High cholesterol?" field.
22. Finally, select a date for the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
