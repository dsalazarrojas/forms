# Media Content Distribution Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Content Distribution Assessment Form is used to evaluate the media content distribution channels, performance, and other factors related to customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus for questions with options.
3. Provide text answers for questions that require a written response.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media content distribution channels**: This field is used to select the channels used for media content distribution. Choose the channels that apply to your media content distribution process.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Performance**: Evaluate the performance of your media content distribution process. Choose from Excellent, Average, or Poor.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Marketing teams involved**: Select all marketing teams involved in the media content distribution process. Multiple selections are allowed.
	+ Required: No
* **Publishing teams involved**: Select all publishing teams involved in the media content distribution process. Multiple selections are allowed.
	+ Required: No
* **How do you communicate to your customers?**: Describe how you communicate with your customers.
	+ Required: Yes
* **What is your satisfaction level of your customers?**: Evaluate your customer satisfaction level. Choose from High, Average, or Low.
	+ Required: Yes
* **What is your customer satisfaction level?**: This is a duplicate question of the previous one. Please answer accordingly.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Target audience**: Describe your target audience.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Media content performance**: Evaluate the performance of your media content. Choose from Excellent, Average, or Poor.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Publishing performance**: This field is not required, but it is related to the media content distribution process. Please evaluate if applicable.
	+ Required: No
* **Target audience age**: Select the age of your target audience.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Target audience sex**: Select all sex of your target audience. Multiple selections are allowed.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Target audience geographic**: Describe the geographic location of your target audience.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Target audience language**: Describe the language spoken by your target audience.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Target audience education**: Describe the education level of your target audience.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Customer engagement**: Select all customer engagement levels. Multiple selections are allowed.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Communication approach**: Select all communication approaches. Multiple selections are allowed.
	+ Required: Yes
* **What sources do you use for customer satisfaction surveys?**: Describe the sources used for customer satisfaction surveys.
	+ Required: Yes
* **Which measures do you use for customer satisfaction?**: Describe the measures used for customer satisfaction.
	+ Required: Yes
* **What methods do you use for customer satisfaction?**: Describe the methods used for customer satisfaction.
	+ Required: Yes
* **How often do you conduct customer satisfaction surveys?**: Select how often you conduct customer satisfaction surveys.
	+ Required: Yes
* **What criteria do you use for customer satisfaction?**: Describe the criteria used for customer satisfaction.
	+ Required: Yes
* **What criteria do you use for customer satisfaction list?**: This field is not necessary but is part of the customer satisfaction process. Please describe accordingly.
	+ Required: No
* **What criteria do you use for customer satisfaction detail?**: Describe the criteria used for customer satisfaction.
	+ Required: Yes
* **What criteria do you use for customer satisfaction comment?**: Describe the comment used for customer satisfaction.
	+ Required: Yes

This form is designed to gather information about the media content distribution channels, performance, and other factors related to customer satisfaction. Please answer each question accurately and completely to get a thorough evaluation of the media content distribution process.
