# Mental Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Assessment Form is a tool designed to gather information about an individual's mental health status. It is intended to help identify potential mental health concerns and provide a baseline for further evaluation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page of the form to understand the questions and prompts.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the provided options for fields with dropdown menus.
4. Enter your answers in the text fields.
5. Submit the completed form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Mood Tracker
* **Mood Tracker** (`mood_tracker`, text, optional): Use this field to describe your current mood. Write a brief statement summarizing how you are feeling.
### Mental State
* **Mental State** (`mental_state`, text, optional): Describe your current mental state. For example, you might be feeling anxious, calm, or overwhelmed.
### Anxiety Level
* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`, number, optional): Rate your current level of anxiety on a scale of 0-10.
### Depression Scale
* **Depression Scale** (`depression_scale`, number, optional): Rate your current level of depression on a scale of 0-10.
### Sleep Patterns
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patterns`, text, optional): Describe your typical sleep patterns. For example, do you have trouble falling asleep or staying asleep?
### Medication Usage
* **Medication Usage** (`medication_usage`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Anxiety
	+ Depressed
	+ None
### Medical Condition
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions you have that may be affecting your mental health.
### Treatment Plan
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Share any treatment plans or therapies you are currently undergoing.
### Physical Activity
* **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, text, optional): Describe your current level of physical activity. For example, do you regularly exercise or engage in physical activity?

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing information about your mental health status.
* If you are unsure about any question or field, please reach out to a healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is intended for internal use only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice or diagnosis.
