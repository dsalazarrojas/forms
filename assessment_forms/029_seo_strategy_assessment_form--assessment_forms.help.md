# SEO strategy assessment form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your current SEO strategy and provide insights on how to improve it. This form is a self-assessment tool that helps you evaluate your website's or entity's online presence and identify areas that need attention to optimize your SEO.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Enter your current performance or status in each field, considering the options provided for each field type.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, as indicated by the "required" status in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Current Performance** (id: 1, number, required: false)
	* This field asks for your current performance score, which is a measure of how well your website or entity is performing in terms of SEO. Please enter a number between 1 and 10, where 1 indicates the lowest performance and 10 indicates the highest performance.
### 2. **Keywords** (id: 2, text, required: false)
	* Enter a list of relevant keywords associated with your website or entity. These are the words that users would search for when looking for your website or entity.
### 3. **Meta Tags** (id: 3, text, required: false)
	* Meta tags are HTML tags used to provide additional information about a web page. Enter a list of meta tags used on your website or entity.
### 4. **Descriptions** (id: 4, text, required: false)
	* Enter a brief description of your website or entity, highlighting its unique value proposition.
### 5. **Backlinks** (id: 5, text, required: false)
	* Backlinks are links from other websites that point to your website or entity. Enter a list of notable backlinks, if any.
### 6. **Content** (id: 6, text, required: false)
	* This field asks for your current content strategy, including any content types (e.g., blog posts, articles, videos, etc.).
### 7. **Alt Tag** (id: 7, text, required: false)
	* Alt tags are alternative text versions of images on your website or entity. Enter a brief description of how you use alt tags.
### 8. **Sitemap** (id: 8, text, required: false)
	* Enter your website or entity's sitemap, including any notable sections or areas.

Note: The required status of fields is based on the form's design and may vary depending on your actual needs. Please fill out all fields as thoroughly as possible to get the most accurate assessment results.
