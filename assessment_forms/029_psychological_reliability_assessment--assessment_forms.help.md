# Psychological Reliability Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the psychological reliability of an individual, evaluating their emotional stability and coping mechanisms. The form is used to gauge their ability to manage stress and anxiety, and to provide a score indicating their overall emotional state.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the response that best matches your current situation.
2. Answer each question honestly, and to the best of your ability.
3. Enter all required information, including your name, email, phone number, and submitted date and time.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all questions.
