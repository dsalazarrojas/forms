# Disaster Recovery Plan Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Disaster Recovery Plan Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess the organization's disaster recovery plan. It helps identify potential weaknesses and areas for improvement, ensuring that the plan is effective and ready to respond to disasters or major disruptions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address (optional) in the designated fields.
2. Provide the name of the organization being assessed.
3. Select the recovery strategy currently employed by the organization (e.g., Good, Fair).
4. Enter any assessment notes, providing specific details about the plan's strengths and weaknesses.
5. Share feedback from stakeholders, if applicable.
6. Add evaluation comments for the plan, highlighting any areas for improvement.
7. Indicate the current status of the recovery plan (Active or Inactive).
8. Select the next steps required for the plan's improvement.
9. Enter the date and time of the last review.
10. Indicate whether the organization has had a disaster (Yes or No).
11. Identify the current status of the disaster (Active or Inactive).
12. Select the status of the review time (Active or Inactive).
13. Leave any additional notes for reference.
14. List the reviewers involved in the review process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1): Enter your name to ensure accurate tracking and accountability.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address for contact purposes (optional).
* **Organization Name** (3): Type the name of the organization being assessed.
* **Recovery Strategy** (4): Select the strategy currently employed by the organization (Good, Fair).
* **Assessment Notes** (5): Provide specific details about the plan's strengths and weaknesses.
* **Stakeholder Feedback** (6): Share feedback from stakeholders, if applicable.
* **Evaluation Comments** (7): Add comments about the plan's evaluation, highlighting areas for improvement.
* **Recovery Plan Status** (8): Indicate the current status of the recovery plan (Active or Inactive).
* **Next Steps** (9): Enter the next steps required for plan improvement.
* **Review Date** (10): Enter the date of the last review.
* **Review Time** (11): Enter the time of the last review.
* **Disaster Type** (12): Indicate whether the organization has had a disaster (Yes or No).
* **Disaster Status** (13): Select the current status of the disaster (Active or Inactive).
* **Review Time Status** (14): Select the status of the review time (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (15): Leave any additional notes for reference.
* **Reviewers** (16): List the reviewers involved in the review process.
