## Purpose
The "Appearance Assessment Survey" form is designed for healthcare professionals to collect data on a client's appearance concerns, assess the severity of their issues, and gather information on any previous treatments or desired outcomes. This form helps healthcare professionals provide effective care and create a treatment plan tailored to the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the "Client Information" section. If the client is anonymous, you can leave this field blank. 
2.  Enter the "Assessment Date" in the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
3.  In the "Main Appearance Concern" section, select the primary area of concern from the provided options. If none of the options match your concern, you can select "Other" and provide a brief description.
4.  Rate the severity of your concern using the number from 1 to 10, with 1 being minimal and 10 being severe.
5.  If you've had any previous treatments or procedures related to your appearance, list them in the "Previous Treatments" section.
6.  If you have a desired outcome or expected result from treatment, provide a brief description in the "Desired Outcome" section.
7.  Check the box to consent for before and after photos for clinical records and treatment planning purposes.
