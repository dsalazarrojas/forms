# Phone Interaction Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Phone Interaction Assessment Form is used to collect and record information about customer interactions with our Customer Service Representatives (CSRs). This form helps CSRs document customer calls, assess the outcome of each interaction, and track customer feedback. By filling out this form, CSRs can provide valuable insights to improve customer service quality and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of interaction (e.g., phone call, email, chat, etc.).
2. Select the outcome of the interaction (e.g., resolved, unresolved, etc.).
3. Choose the relevant Customer Service Representative(s) involved in the interaction.
4. Provide the date and time of the interaction.
5. Enter the duration of the interaction.
6. Enter any feedback or comments from the customer.
7. Indicate if the customer has a complaint status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
8. Set the priority of the customer complaint (e.g., low, medium, high).
9. Enter any additional comments or information about the interaction.
10. Enter the name and contact information of the Customer Service Representative who handled the interaction.
11. Enter the customer's name and contact information (if applicable).
12. Enter the reason for the call.
13. Document the outcome of the interaction.
14. Describe the customer's complaint, if applicable.
15. Rate the customer's satisfaction with the CSR (1-5).
16. Rate the interaction (1-5).
17. Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interaction Type (1)**: Choose the type of interaction (e.g., phone call, email, chat, etc.).
* **Interaction Outcome (2)**: Select the outcome of the interaction (e.g., resolved, unresolved, etc.).
* **Customer Service Representative (3)**: Choose the Customer Service Representative involved in the interaction.
* **Date and Time (4)**: Enter the date and time of the interaction.
* **Duration (5)**: Enter the duration of the interaction.
* **Feedback (6)**: Enter any feedback or comments from the customer.
* **Customer Feedback (7)**: Indicate if the customer has a complaint status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Customer Complaint Status (8)**: Set the priority of the customer complaint (e.g., low, medium, high).
* **Additional Comments (9)**: Enter any additional comments or information about the interaction.
* **Customer Service Representative Name (10)**: Enter the name of the Customer Service Representative who handled the interaction.
* **Customer Service Representative Phone (11)**: Enter the contact information of the Customer Service Representative.
* **Customer Name (12)**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Email (13)**: Enter the customer's email.
* **Customer Phone (14)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Service Representative Comments (15)**: Enter any comments from the Customer Service Representative.
* **Customer Interaction Notes (16)**: Document any notes about the interaction.
* **Customer Interaction Status (17)**: Select the status of the customer interaction (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Customer Interaction Status Reason (18)**: Enter the reason for the customer interaction status.
* **Reason For Call (19)**: Enter the reason for the call.
* **Call Outcome (20)**: Document the outcome of the interaction.
* **Customer Complaint Description (21)**: Describe the customer's complaint, if applicable.
* **Customer Service Representative Rating (22)**: Rate the customer's satisfaction with the CSR (1-5).
* **Customer Interaction Rating (23)**: Rate the interaction (1-5).
* **Customer Interaction Comments (24)**: Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.
