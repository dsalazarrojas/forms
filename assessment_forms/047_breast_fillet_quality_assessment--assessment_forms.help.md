# Breast Fillet Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Breast Fillet Quality Assessment" form is used to evaluate the quality of breast fillet products. The form assesses multiple aspects of the product, including visual appearance, color, texture, and more, to provide a comprehensive evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Assessor's full name in the "Assessor Full Name" field.
2. Enter the Assessor's ID in the "Assessor ID" field.
3. Enter the date of assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
4. Enter the time of assessment in the "Assessment Time" field.
5. Enter the batch or lot number in the "Batch or Lot Number" field.
6. Enter the supplier's name in the "Supplier Name" field.
7. Select the type of breast fillet in the "Product Type" field.
8. Enter the number of fillets assessed in the "Sample Size" field.
9. Rate the visual appearance of the fillet in the "Visual Appearance" field.
10. Rate the color quality of the fillet in the "Color Assessment" field.
11. Rate the texture of the fillet in the "Texture Assessment" field.
12. Rate the odor of the fillet in the "Odor Assessment" field.
13. Rate the size consistency of the fillet in the "Size Uniformity" field.
14. Rate the trimming quality of the fillet in the "Trim Quality" field.
15. Select any defects found on the fillet in the "Defects Found" field.
16. Enter the product temperature in degrees in the "Temperature Check" field.
17. Rate the packaging condition in the "Packaging Condition" field.
18. Check if the labeling is accurate in the "Labeling Accuracy" field.
19. Select the final quality grade in the "Overall Quality Grade" field.
20. Decide on the product disposition in the "Product Disposition" field.
21. Provide any corrective actions needed in the "Corrective Actions Needed" field.
22. Sign your name as the assessor in the "Assessor Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessor Full Name (1)**: Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor ID (2)**: Enter the ID of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessment Date (3)**: Enter the date of the assessment in "MM/DD/YY" format.
* **Assessment Time (4)**: Enter the time of the assessment in 24-hour format.
* **Batch or Lot Number (5)**: Enter the batch or lot number of the product.
* **Supplier Name (6)**: Enter the name of the supplier.
* **Product Type (7)**: Select the type of breast fillet (e.g. Chicken Breast, Turkey Breast, etc.).
* **Sample Size (8)**: Enter the number of fillets assessed.
* **Visual Appearance (9)**: Rate the visual appearance of the fillet (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Below Standard, Unacceptable).
* **Color Assessment (10)**: Rate the color quality of the fillet (Uniform Pink-White, Slightly Varied, Some Discoloration, Significant Discoloration, Abnormal Color).
* **Texture Assessment (11)**: Rate the texture of the fillet (Firm and Elastic, Slightly Soft, Soft, Mushy, Slimy).
* **Odor Assessment (12)**: Rate the odor of the fillet (Fresh, No Odor, Mild, Acceptable, Strong, Unacceptable, Putrid).
* **Size Uniformity (13)**: Rate the size consistency of the fillet (Very Uniform, Mostly Uniform, Somewhat Varied, Highly Varied, Not Uniform).
* **Trim Quality (14)**: Rate the trimming quality of the fillet (Excellent Trim, Good Trim, Acceptable, Poor Trim, Unacceptable).
* **Defects Found (15)**: Select any defects observed on the fillet.
* **Temperature Check (16)**: Enter the product temperature in degrees.
* **Packaging Condition (17)**: Rate the packaging condition (Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Damaged, Severely Damaged).
* **Labeling Accuracy (18)**: Check if the labeling is accurate (Accurate, Minor Errors, Major Errors, Missing Labels).
* **Overall Quality Grade (19)**: Select the final quality grade (Grade A Premium, Grade A, Grade B, Grade C, Reject).
* **Product Disposition (20)**: Decide on the product disposition (Approved for Sale, Approved with Restrictions, Requires Rework, Hold for Review, Rejected).
* **Corrective Actions Needed (21)**: Provide any corrective actions needed.
* **Assessor Signature (22)**: Sign your name as the assessor.

Tips:
* Be thorough and accurate when assessing the fillet quality.
* Ensure that you have all necessary information before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully to avoid mistakes.
* Sign your name as the assessor to validate your assessment.
