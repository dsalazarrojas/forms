# Work Structure Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Structure Assessment Form is designed to gather information about a team's structure and dynamics. This form helps assess the team's performance and provides insights for potential improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name, role, manager name, manager email, and any other required information under their respective fields.
2. Indicate whether you have team members or not under the "Team Members" field.
3. Select the level of collaboration and communication between team members.
4. Provide any feedback or comments about the team's structure and dynamics under the "Feedback" field.
5. Note any additional feedback or observations under the "Feedback Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company you are working for.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required: false): Describe your role within the company.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of your manager.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have team members and 'No' if you don't.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about the team's structure and dynamics.
* **Collaboration** (`collaboration`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the level of collaboration between team members. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Communication** (`communication`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the level of communication between team members. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Feedback Notes** (`feedback_notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional feedback or observations.
* **Role Influence** (`role_influence`, text, required: false): Describe the influence of your role within the team.
