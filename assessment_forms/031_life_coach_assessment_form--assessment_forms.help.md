# Life Coach Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Coach Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool designed to help coaches and professionals in the industry evaluate their own skills, experience, and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Work through each page, answering questions and providing detailed information about your skills, experience, and abilities.
3. Use the "Save Progress" button to save your work and come back later if needed.
4. Once you've completed all pages, click "Submit" to review and finalize your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required/optional): This section is for providing general information about yourself, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant details.

### Skills and Abilities (Page 2)
* **Skills and Abilities** (`skills_and_abilities`, text, required/optional): In this section, describe your skills and abilities as a coach, including any relevant training or certifications you've received.

### Experience (Page 3)
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required/optional): Share your work experience, including any coaching certifications, training, or other relevant experience.

### Education and Certifications (Page 4)
* **Education and Certifications** (`education_and_certifications`, text, required/optional): Provide details about your educational background, any relevant certifications, or other qualifications you've received.

### Personal Info (Page 5)
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, required/optional): This section is for providing any personal details that may be relevant to your coaching practice.

### Coaching Skills (Page 6)
* **Coaching Skills** (`coaching_skills`, text, required/optional): Describe your coaching skills, including any relevant experience, training, or certifications.

### Professional Info (Page 7)
* **Professional Info** (`professional_info`, text, required/optional): Provide details about your professional background, including any relevant certifications, training, or experience.

### Professional History (Page 8)
* **Professional History** (`professional_history`, text, required/optional): Share your work history, including any relevant experience, certifications, or training.

### Professional Certifications (Page 9)

### Education (Page 10)
