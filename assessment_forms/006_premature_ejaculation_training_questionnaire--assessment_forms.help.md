# Premature Ejaculation Training Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about your experiences with premature ejaculation, feelings, and medical background to help professionals in the field provide better guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all the required fields (marked as "true" in the form). You can skip optional fields if you don't have any information about them.
2. For each field, provide your honest and accurate information.
3. Be as detailed as possible in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (id: 1)**: This is a text field where you can provide any general information about yourself.
* **Symptoms (id: 2)**: Please select all the symptoms that you have experienced related to premature ejaculation.
* **Feelings (id: 3)**: Select all the feelings that you are experiencing related to premature ejaculation. These feelings can include Anxiety, Frustration, Boredom, Anger, or Sadness.
* **Triggers (id: 4)**: Select all the triggers that you have experienced related to premature ejaculation. These can include Emotional State, Physical State, Social Situations, or Other.
* **Frequency (id: 5)**: Please describe how often you experience premature ejaculation.
* **Duration (id: 6)**: If you have any information, please describe how long you experience premature ejaculation. Otherwise, you can leave this field blank.
* **Past Experiences (id: 7)**: Select the number of times you have experienced premature ejaculation in the past.
* **Medical Background (id: 8)**: Provide any relevant medical history or information in this text field.
* **Medications (id: 9)**: If you are taking any medications, select them from the list. This includes Antidepressants, Anxiolytics, Anti-androgens, or Other.
* **Medical Procedures (id: 10)**: If you have undergone any medical procedures, select them from the list. This includes Surgery, Counseling, Therapy, or Other.
* **Training Goals (id: 11)**: If you have any specific training goals, please describe them in this text field.
* **Support System (id: 12)**: Select if you have a support system in place.
* **Support People (id: 13)**: Please list the people who are part of your support system.
* **Contact Information (id: 14)**: Provide your email address so that we can contact you for further information.
* **Emergency Contact (id: 15)**: Please list your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Authorities (id: 16)**: If you have any medical authorities you'd like to contact, select them from the list. This includes Doctor, Therapist, Specialist, or Other.
* **Medical Condition (id: 17)**: Describe your medical condition in this text field.
* **Comments (id: 18)**: If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them in this text field.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Please do not use this form for commercial purposes.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact us at: form@gic.mx
