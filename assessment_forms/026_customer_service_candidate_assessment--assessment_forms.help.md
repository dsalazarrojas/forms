# Customer Service Candidate Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from candidates applying for a Customer Service role. It includes questions about the candidate's experience, education, language proficiency, and job satisfaction. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used as your contact information.
3. Select one or more roles that you have applied for or are interested in.
4. Choose the length of time you have worked in a support role, as well as your previous role experience.
5. Select your highest level of education that you have completed.
6. Indicate your level of language proficiency.
7. Choose the type(s) of work you do or have experience with.
8. Select your preferred work environment.
9. If you are currently employed, indicate how satisfied you are with your job and provide a brief reason why you may be dissatisfied.
10. Answer questions about your current work schedule and experience with your previous manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (`name: first_name, type: text, required: false`)
Enter your first name.

### Last name (`name: last_name, type: text, required: false`)
Enter your last name.

### Email (`name: email, type: email, required: false`)
Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Role (`name: role_applicant, type: select_one, required: true`)
Select one or more roles that you have applied for or are interested in.

### Phone number (`name: phone_number, type: text, required: false`)
Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Job title (`name: job_title, type: text, required: false`)
Enter a brief description of your current or previous job title.

### Work experience (`name: work_experience, type: select_multiple, required: true`)
Choose the length of time you have worked in a support role.

### Previous work experience (`name: previous_work_experience, type: text, required: false`)
Describe your previous role in support.

### Education (`name: education, type: select_one, required: true`)
Select your highest level of education completed.

### Language proficiency (`name: language, type: select_multiple, required: true`)
Choose your level of language proficiency.

### Work type (`name: work_type, type: select_multiple, required: false`)
Select the type(s) of work you do or have experience with.

### Work environment (`name: work_environment, type: select_one, required: false`)
Choose the type of work environment you prefer.

### Job satisfaction (`name: job_satisfaction, type: select_one, required: false`)
Indicate your level of satisfaction with your current job.

### Reason for job dissatisfaction (`name: job_satisfaction_why, type: text, required: false`)
Explain why you are not very satisfied with your current job.

### Additional comments (`name: job_satisfaction_comments, type: text, required: false`)
Add any additional comments about your current job.

### Job satisfaction with manager (`name: job_satisfaction_2, type: select_one, required: false`)
Indicate your level of satisfaction with your current manager.

### Reason for manager dissatisfaction (`name: job_satisfaction_3, type: text, required: false`)
Explain why you are not very satisfied with your current manager.

### Work hours (`name: job_satisfaction_4, type: number, required: true`)
Enter the number of hours you work per week.

### Previous manager experience (`name: previous_manager_experience, type: text, required: false`)
Describe your experience with your previous manager. 

Note: This help guide is a general explanation and may not cover all possible scenarios or fields. Always review the form with the candidate before submission to ensure understanding of each field.
