# Skin Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Skin Care Assessment Form** is a comprehensive form to assess the skin care needs and habits of an individual. This form is used to collect information about an individual's skin care practices, including their skin types, concerns, and self-care routines. The information collected will help in providing personalized recommendations and guidance for improving skin health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Skin Care Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question.
3. Fill in the required fields.
4. Review and submit the form for evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **skin_care_assessment_form_label** (1, text, required): This is a text box where you can enter a brief description or introduction for your skin care assessment.
* **skin_care_question_1** (2, text, required): What is your skin type? (e.g., Normal, Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive)
* **skin_care_question_2** (3, select_one, required): Do you have any skin concerns? (e.g., Acne, Eczema, Psoriasis, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_3** (4, text, required): What is your current skin care routine? (e.g., Daily skincare, Exfoliating, Toner, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_4** (5, select_multiple, required): Which skin care products do you use? (Select all that apply)
	+ Yes: Facial wash
	+ Yes: Moisturizer
	+ Yes: Sunscreen
	+ Yes: Exfoliant
* **skin_care_question_5** (6, select_one, required): Do you have any skin allergies or sensitivities? (e.g., Fragrances, dyes, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_6** (7, date, required): When was your last skin care appointment or check-up?
* **skin_care_question_7** (8, time, required): How many minutes do you typically spend on your daily skin care routine?
* **skin_care_question_8** (9, select_one, required): Do you have a skincare routine for your hands? (e.g., Washing, Moisturizing, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_9** (10, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up skin care advice.
* **skin_care_question_10** (11, text, required): Do you have any skin-related medical conditions or concerns? (e.g., Acne, Rosacea, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_11** (12, text, required): What is your preferred method of skincare product packaging? (e.g., Tube, bottle, jar, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_12** (13, select_one, required): Do you have a skincare routine for your face? (e.g., Cleansing, Toning, Moisturizing, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_12_1** (14, date, required): If yes, when was your last facial skincare routine check-up?
* **skin_care_question_13** (15, time, required): How many minutes do you typically spend on your facial skin care routine?
* **skin_care_question_13_1** (16, select_multiple, required): Which of the following skin care products do you have at home? (Select all that apply)
	+ Yes: Cleanser
	+ Yes: Toner
	+ Yes: Moisturizer
	+ Yes: Exfoliant
* **skin_care_question_14** (17, select_one, required): Do you have a skincare routine for your body? (e.g., Washing, Moisturizing, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_15** (18, number, required): How many times do you exfoliate your skin per week?
* **skin_care_question_15_1** (19, number, required): How many minutes do you typically spend on your body skin care routine?
* **skin_care_question_16** (20, text, required): Do you have a preference for natural or organic skincare products? (e.g., Yes, No)
* **skin_care_question_17** (21, text, required): Do you have any skin care concerns? (e.g., Acne, Eczema, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_18** (22, text, required): What is your preferred method of payment for skincare products? (e.g., Credit card, Cash, etc.)
* **skin_care_question_19** (23, number, required): How many skin care products do you own? 
* **skin_care_question_20** (24, text, required): Do you have any skin care tips or advice to share? (e.g., sunscreen daily, etc.)

## Tips
* Be honest when answering each question to ensure accurate assessment.
* Fill in all required fields.
* Review and submit the form carefully before submission.
* Contact us for any questions or concerns.
