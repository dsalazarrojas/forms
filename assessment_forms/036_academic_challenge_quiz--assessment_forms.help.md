# Academic Challenge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test your knowledge in various subjects, including scientific method, world history, mathematics, literature, geography, logic, biology, chemistry, and critical thinking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information in the section labeled "-- Participant Information --".
2. Answer the multiple-choice questions in the sections labeled "-- General Knowledge --" and "-- Critical Thinking --". Choose the best answer from the options provided.
3. For question 9, "Explain in your own words why critical thinking is important in academic study". Write a brief explanation in 2-3 sentences.
4. Rate your overall academic preparation in the section labeled "How would you rate your overall academic preparation".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information
* **Full name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Grade level or course** (`grade_or_level`, `text`, required): Enter your current grade level or course.

### General Knowledge
* **What is the correct order of the scientific method** (`q1_scientific_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct sequence of steps for the scientific method.
* **The Renaissance period originated in which country** (`q2_world_history`, `select_one`, required): Select the country where the Renaissance period began.
* **What is the value of the square root of 144** (`q3_mathematics`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct answer from the options provided.
* **Who wrote the novel '1984'** (`q4_literature`, `select_one`, required): Select the author who wrote the novel '1984'.
* **Which is the longest river in the world** (`q5_geography`, `select_one`, required): Choose the longest river in the world.

### Critical Thinking
* **If all roses are flowers and some flowers fade quickly, which statement must be true** (`q6_logic`, `select_one`, required): Select the statement that must be true.
* **Which organelle is responsible for producing energy in a cell** (`q7_biology`, `select_one`, required): Choose the organelle responsible for energy production.
* **What is the chemical symbol for gold** (`q8_chemistry`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct chemical symbol for gold.
* **Explain in your own words why critical thinking is important in academic study** (`q9_open_response`, `text`, required): Write a brief explanation in 2-3 sentences.
* **How would you rate your overall academic preparation** (`q10_self_assessment`, `select_one`, required): Rate your overall academic preparation.

## Tips
* Answer the questions to the best of your ability, but do not worry about making mistakes. This form is for assessment and educational purposes.
* Be honest and accurate in your answers.
* If you have any doubts or questions, contact the form author at form@gic.mx.
