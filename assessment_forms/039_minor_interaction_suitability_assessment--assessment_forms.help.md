# Minor Interaction Suitability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the suitability for minors to interact with adults in various situations. The form helps to identify potential risks and ensure that adults are aware of and address them before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of interaction that you plan (e.g., planned interaction).
2. Provide information about the adult participant involved, including their name, age, and gender.
3. Provide information about the minor participant involved, including their name, age, and gender.
4. Describe the interaction details, including any safety concerns.
5. Indicate if a plan is in place for the interaction (e.g., planned action).
6. Select the risk level of the interaction (e.g., low, medium, high).
7. Choose the review date and who reviewed the interaction (e.g., supervisor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Planned Interaction (1)
* **Planned Interaction** (`Yes/No`, `type`: select_multiple): Indicate if the interaction is premeditated or not.
### Adult Participant (2)
* **Adult Participant** (`text`, `required`: false): Enter the name of the adult participant.
### Adult Participant Age (3)
* **Adult Participant Age** (`number`, `required`: false): Enter the age of the adult participant.
### Adult Participant Gender (4)
* **Adult Participant Gender** (`Male/Female/Unknown`, `required`: false): Select the gender of the adult participant.
### Minor Participant (5)
* **Minor Participant** (`text`, `required`: false): Enter the name of the minor participant.
### Minor Participant Age (6)
* **Minor Participant Age** (`number`, `required`: false): Enter the age of the minor participant.
### Minor Participant Gender (7)
* **Minor Participant Gender** (`Male/Female/Non-Binary`, `required`: false): Select the gender of the minor participant.
### Interaction Details (8)
* **Interaction Details** (`text`, `required`: false): Describe the details of the interaction, including any safety concerns.
### Safety Concerns (9)
* **Safety Concerns** (`Yes/No`, `required`: false): Identify if there are any safety concerns with the interaction.
### Action Planned (10)
* **Action Planned** (`Yes/No`, `required`: false): Indicate if a plan is in place for the interaction.
### Risk Level (11)
* **Risk Level** (`Low/Medium/High`, `required`: false): Select the risk level of the interaction.
### Review Date (12)
* **Review Date** (`date`, `required`: false): Select the date of the review.
### Reviewed By (13)
* **Reviewed By** (`text`, `required`: false): Choose the person who reviewed the interaction.
