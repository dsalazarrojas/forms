# planning_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect metadata about knowledge quizzes, allowing for easy organization and categorization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your quiz in the "Quiz Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your quiz in the "Quiz Description" field.
3. Select a category for your quiz from the list of options in the "Quiz Category" field.
4. Add comma-separated tags for your quiz in the "Quiz Tags" field.
5. Enter the questions for your quiz in the "Quiz Questions" field.
6. Add individual questions for your quiz in the "Question 1", "Question 2", "Question 3", "Question 4", and "Question 5" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Quiz Name** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your quiz that will be displayed as the main title.
  - **Quiz Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your quiz to help users understand what it covers.
  - **Quiz Category** (`category`, text, required/optional): Select a category for your quiz to help with organization and searching.
  - **Quiz Tags** (`tags`, text, required/optional): Add comma-separated tags for your quiz to help with searching and filtering.
  - **Quiz Questions** (`questions`, text, required/optional): Enter the questions for your quiz, one per line.
  - **Question 1** (`question_1`, text, required/optional), **Question 2** (`question_2`, text, required/optional), **Question 3** (`question_3`, text, required/optional), **Question 4** (`question_4`, text, required/optional), **Question 5** (`question_5`, text, required/optional): Enter individual questions for your quiz, one per field.
  - Note: Questions will be displayed as they are entered in the corresponding fields.
