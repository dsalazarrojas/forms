# Strategies For Managing Weight Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Strategies for Managing Weight Assessment form aims to help users manage their weight by setting goals, evaluating their daily habits, and scoring their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and its sections to understand the different areas of assessment.
2. Fill in the goals section with a brief description of your weight management goals.
3. In the habits section, describe your daily habits related to weight management.
4. In the readiness section, provide a brief explanation of your current state of readiness for weight loss.
5. In the habits score and goals score sections, provide a numeric value representing your progress towards your goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required: false): Describe your weight management goals. This is a text field where you can write down your objectives, such as losing a certain amount of weight or maintaining a healthy diet.
* **Habits** (`habits`, `text`, required: false): Describe your daily habits that affect your weight, such as exercise frequency or meal planning.
* **Readiness** (`readiness`, `text`, required: false): Assess your current state of readiness for weight loss, such as your motivation or confidence level.
* **Habits Score** (`habits_score`, `number`, required: false): Score your progress towards your habits, with higher numbers indicating better habits.
* **Goals Score** (`goals_score`, `number`, required: false): Score your progress towards your goals, with higher numbers indicating closer to achieving your objectives.

## Tips
Remember to be as specific as possible when filling in each field, and don't hesitate to review your answers before submitting the form.
