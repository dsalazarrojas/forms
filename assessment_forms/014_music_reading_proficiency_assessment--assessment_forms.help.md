# Music Reading Proficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form assesses an individual's or group's music reading proficiency, focusing on their skills, confidence, and habits related to reading musical notation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your music reading skills, confidence, and habits for each question.
2. For select_multiple and select_one fields, you can only choose one or multiple options from the available choices.
3. Enter numerical values for total time spent reading and practicing in the corresponding fields.
4. Answer questions truthfully, and remember that there are no right or wrong answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music Notation Skills** (`notation_skills`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the options that best describe your skills in reading music notation, such as 0-4 or Other.
* **Reading Confidence** (`reading_confidence`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select High, Medium, or Low to indicate how confident you are in reading music notation.
* **Notation Systems** (`notation_systems`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose the notation systems you are familiar with, like Staff, Tablature, Braille, or Other.
* **Notation Readability** (`notation_readability`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the levels of difficulty you find most readable, from Very Easy to Very Difficult.
* **Notation Speed** (`notation_speed`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the average speed at which you can read music notation.
* **Total Time Spent Reading** (`total_time_spent_reading`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the total time you have spent reading music notation in the past [time period].
* **Total Time Spent Practicing** (`total_time_spent_practicing`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the total time you have spent practicing music reading in the past [time period].
* **Music Background** (`music_background`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose how you acquired your music reading skills, such as through formal training, self-taught, or both.
