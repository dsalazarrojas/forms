# professional-risk-assessment-questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Risk Assessment Questionnaire is a comprehensive tool used to evaluate and document potential risks associated with a specific workplace or process. This form is part of a broader risk assessment process that identifies and prioritizes risks to ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. For select multiple and select one fields, choose one or more relevant options from the list.
3. Enter dates in the date fields in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification document.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **What are the main areas of your workplace?** (`risk_areas`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more relevant options from the list (Area 1 to 8).
* **Training Procedure** (`training_procedure`, `select_one`, required): Choose one relevant procedure from the list (Procedure 1 to 6).
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of prophets or relevant information.
* **Risk management process** (`risk_management_process`, `text`, required): Describe the risk management process used in the assessment.
* **Hazard Areas** (`hazard_areas`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose one or more relevant options from the list (Hazard 1 to 6).
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `number`, required): Enter a numerical risk level from 1 to 10.
* **Date of risk assessment** (`risk_assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the risk assessment in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Number of employees** (`risk_assessment_employee_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of employees involved in the risk assessment.
* **Comments** (`risk_assessment_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Signoff/Approval** (`risk_assessment_signoff_approval`, `select_one`, required): Choose one relevant option from the list (Approved or Not Approved).
* **Review Date** (`risk_assessment_review_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the review in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Reviewer Signature** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_signature`, `select_one`, required): Choose one relevant option from the list (Yes or No).
* **Reviewer Name** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Location** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Remarks** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_remarks`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments from the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Signature Date** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_signature_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the reviewer's signature in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Reviewer Signature Title** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_signature_title`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the reviewer's signature.
* **Reviewer Signature Name** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_signature_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer's signature.
* **Reviewer Signature Remark** (`risk_assessment_reviewer_signature_remark`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments from the reviewer's signature.
