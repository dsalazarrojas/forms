# knowledge-assessment-on-religious-figures - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on end users' opinions or attitudes towards specific religious figures.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions in the "How To Complete This Form" section above.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form-questions**: This is a text field where users can provide their thoughts or opinions about religious figures.
* **religious-leaders**: This field asks users to select whether they agree or disagree with the statement.
* **form-sections**: This is a text field where users can provide additional information or context about their assessment.
* **section-one**: This field is likely a placeholder for section one of a longer form, but in this simplified version, it's a text field.
* **question-one**: This field asks users to answer a specific question about a religious figure.
* **correct-answer**: This field requires users to select the correct answer from the provided options.
* **score**: This is an optional field for users to input their own score or assessment.

**Tips**: Please answer all required fields (denoted by an asterisk \*) and provide as much information as possible to help with your assessment.
