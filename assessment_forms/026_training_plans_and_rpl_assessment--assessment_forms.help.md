# Training Plans And RPL Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess an employee's training plans and RPL evidence. It is used to evaluate an employee's training history, including their training status, and ensure that they have completed all required training hours.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Training plans** field with a brief description of your training plans, including any relevant details such as training dates, locations, and hours completed.
2. Select **Yes** or **No** in the **RPL Evidence** field, indicating whether or not you have completed RPL evidence for your training.
3. Enter the **Date of Training** field with the date you completed your training.
4. Enter the **Training Location** field with the location where you completed your training.
5. Enter the **Trainer Name** field with the name of your trainer.
6. Enter the **Training Hours Completed** field with the number of hours you have completed for your training.
7. Select **Yes** or **No** for the **Training Type** field, indicating the type of training you completed.
8. Enter the **Contact Information** field with your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
9. Enter a valid email address in the **Email** field.
10. Select **Active** or **Inactive** in the **Training Status** field, indicating your training status.
11. If applicable, enter the **RPL Evidence Link** field with a link to your RPL evidence.
12. Enter the **Additional Training Location** field with any additional training locations you have completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training plans**: A brief description of your training plans, including any relevant details such as training dates, locations, and hours completed.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **RPL Evidence**: Select **Yes** if you have completed RPL evidence for your training, otherwise select **No**.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: true
* **Date of Training**: The date you completed your training.
  * Type: date
  * Required: true
* **Training Location**: The location where you completed your training.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **Trainer Name**: The name of your trainer.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **Training Hours Completed**: The number of hours you have completed for your training.
  * Type: number
  * Required: true
* **Training Type**: Select **Yes** if you completed the training, otherwise select **No**.
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: true
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: true
* **Training Status**: Select **Active** if your training is active, otherwise select **Inactive**.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: true
* **RPL Evidence Link**: If you have completed RPL evidence, enter a link to it.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
* **Additional Training Location**: Enter any additional training locations you have completed.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Please enter valid email addresses in the **Email** field.
