# Education Assessment Method Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Assessment Method Audit Form is a tool used to assess and audit the methods used in education settings. It helps to evaluate the effectiveness of various assessment methods used in educational institutions. This form is used to gather information on the assessment practices, frequency, and methods used in education, and to collect feedback from assessors and reviewers. The form also allows users to rate the overall performance of the education setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Assessment Practices**: This page asks about the assessment practices used in the education setting.
   - Enter the assessment method used (Yes/No).
   - Enter the frequency of assessment (High/Medium/Low).
   - Enter the date of the most recent assessment.
   - Enter the time of the most recent assessment.
   - Enter the length of the assessment.

2. **Assessment Method Used**: Choose one of the following options for the assessment method used:
   - Yes
   - No

3. **Assessment Frequency**: Choose one of the following options for the frequency of assessment:
   - High
   - Medium
   - Low

4. **Date of Most Recent Assessment**: Enter the date of the most recent assessment.

5. **Time of Most Recent Assessment**: Enter the time of the most recent assessment.

6. **Length of Assessment**: Enter the length of the assessment.

7. **Assessor**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the assessment.

8. **Assessor Email**: Enter the email of the person who conducted the assessment.

9. **Assessor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who conducted the assessment.

10. **Feedback Frequency**: Choose one of the following options for the frequency of feedback:
    - Frequently
    - Occasionally
    - Rarely

11. **Source of Feedback**: Choose one of the following options for the source of feedback:
    - Internal
    - External
    - Other
    - None

12. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments on feedback.

13. **Review Frequency**: Choose one of the following options for the frequency of review:
    - Frequently
    - Occasionally
    - Rarely
    - Never

14. **Review Comments**: Enter any additional comments on review.

15. **Overall Rating**: Choose one of the following options for the overall rating:
    - Excellent
    - Good
    - Fair
    - Poor

16. **Type of Assessment**: Choose one of the following options for the type of assessment:
    - Summative
    - Formative
    - Diagnostic
    - Other
    - None

17. **Other Assessment Method**: Enter any other assessment methods used.

18. **Other Assessed Method Description**: Enter a description of the other assessment method used.

19. **Assessor Comments**: Enter any comments from the assessor.

20. **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments from the reviewer.

21. **Other Reviewer Comments**: Enter any other comments from the reviewer.

22. **Overall Assessment Score**: Enter the overall score of the assessment.

23. **Overall Comments**: Enter any additional comments on the overall assessment.

24. **Other Comments**: Enter any other comments.
