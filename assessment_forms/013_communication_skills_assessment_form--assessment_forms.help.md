# Communication Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Communication Skills Assessment form is used to evaluate an individual's verbal and non-verbal communication skills, providing a consistent and structured way to assess their overall communication effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Participant name: Enter the name of the person being assessed.
	* Assessor name: Enter your name as the person completing the assessment.
2. Evaluate each section using the rating scale:
	* Verbal communication clarity: Rate from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
	* Listening skills: Rate from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
	* Written communication quality: Rate from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
	* Non-verbal communication: Rate from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
3. Provide any additional comments or recommendations for improvement:
	* Overall comments and recommendations: This field is optional but can be used to provide any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant name
**Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This field is for entering the name of the person being assessed.

### Role or position
**Role or position** (`role`, text, optional): This field is for entering the job title or student role of the participant.

### Verbal communication clarity
**Verbal communication clarity** (`verbal_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the participant's verbal communication clarity, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### Listening skills
**Listening skills** (`listening_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the participant's listening skills, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### Written communication quality
**Written communication quality** (`written_communication`, select_one, required): Rate the participant's written communication quality, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### Non-verbal communication
**Non-verbal communication** (`non_verbal_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the participant's non-verbal communication, from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).

### Overall comments and recommendations
**Overall comments and recommendations** (`overall_comments`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional feedback or suggestions for improvement.

### Assessor name
**Assessor name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the person completing the assessment.

## Tips
* Be honest and objective when evaluating the participant's communication skills.
* Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your ratings.
* Keep your comments constructive and actionable, focusing on specific behaviors or skills that the participant can improve on.
* Use this form to provide regular feedback to participants and help them grow and develop their communication skills.
