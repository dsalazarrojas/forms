# Labor And Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labor and Care Assessment Form is used to document and assess the labor and care provided by laborers on a project. This form is meant to gather information on the labor and care observations, overall rating, and other relevant details about the labor and care provided. It is used to evaluate the quality of care and labor provided and to ensure consistency and accuracy in reporting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select multiple options for Labor and Care Assessment from the available choices.
2. Provide notes on the Labor and Care Observations.
3. Enter your Overall Rating.
4. Enter your Supervisor Comments (if applicable).
5. Select the Submit To option.
6. Enter the Date Observed and Time Observed.
7. Enter your Email address in the Submitter field.
8. Enter your Phone Number.
9. Enter the Date Submitted.
10. Enter the Email address of the Submitter.
11. Enter the name of the Supervisor.
12. Enter the Email address of the Supervisor.
13. Enter the phone number of the Supervisor.
14. Enter the name of the Laborer.
15. Enter the Email address of the Laborer.
16. Enter the phone number of the Laborer.
17. Enter the name of the Laborer's Supervisor.
18. Enter the name of the Care Team.
19. Enter the name of the Care Team's Supervisor (if applicable).
20. Enter the Submitter's Department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Labor and Care Assessment** (`labor_and_care_assessment`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options that best describe the labor and care provided.
* **Labor and Care Observations** (`labor_and_care_observations`, note, required): Provide notes on the labor and care observations.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, required): Enter a number between 1 and 5 that represents your overall rating of the labor and care provided.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback from the supervisor.
* **Submit To** (`submit_to`, select_one, optional): Select the person or department to submit the form to.
* **Date Observed** (`date_observed`, date, required): Enter the date the labor and care was observed.
* **Time Observed** (`time_observed`, time, required): Enter the time the labor and care was observed.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the supervisor.
* **Laborer Name** (`laborer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the laborer.
* **Laborer Email** (`laborer_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the laborer.
* **Laborer Phone** (`laborer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the laborer.
* **Laborer Supervisor** (`laborer_supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the laborer's supervisor (if applicable).
* **Care Team Name** (`care_team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the care team.
* **Care Team Supervisor** (`care_team_supervisor`, text, optional): Enter the name of the care team's supervisor (if applicable).
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department submitting the form.
