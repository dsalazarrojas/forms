# Paragraph Writing Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students on their paragraph writing exercises, allowing them to reflect on their writing progress and receive feedback on their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as needed.
2. For text fields, type in your response, and for date and time fields, select the correct values.
3. Make sure to enter your email address to facilitate communication and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paragraph Draft** (`paragraph_draft`, text, optional): Write a brief summary of the paragraph you've written, or describe your writing process.
* **Self-Reflection** (`self_reflection`, text, optional): Reflect on what you've learned from the paragraph writing exercise, or what you would do differently next time.
* **Feedback Request** (`feedback_request`, text, optional): Ask the instructor or peers for specific feedback on your paragraph writing.
* **Feedback Comment** (`feedback_comment`, text, optional): Provide additional comments or insights on your paragraph writing.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted the paragraph writing exercise.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`, time, optional): Enter the time you submitted the paragraph writing exercise.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to facilitate communication and feedback.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure meaningful feedback.
* If unsure about any field, refer to the field's label for guidance.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
