# AI Reading Comprehension Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your reading comprehension skills by asking you to answer a series of questions based on a provided passage. The passage is a brief text about the evolution of neural networks, and you will be asked questions that range from identifying the main idea of the passage to providing a summary of the content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Read the passage provided.
*   Answer each question based on your reading of the passage.
*   Select the correct option for each question.
*   Fill out any optional fields, such as providing feedback or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **--- Reading Comprehension Assessment ---**: This is the header for the form, providing a brief introduction to the assessment.
*   **Student Name**: *`student_name`* (Required): Please provide your name as you would like it to be recorded in the assessment results.
*   **Class / Section**: *`class_section`* (Required): Enter the class or section you are taking the assessment in.
*   **Reading Passage: The Evolution of Neural Networks**: *`reading_passage_note`* (Optional): This is the passage you will be reading and answering questions about. Please note that this field is for notes, not answers.
*   **Text to Read**: *`passage_text`* (Optional): This is the passage itself, which you will read and answer questions about.
*   **What is the primary focus of the passage?**: *`q1_main_idea`* (Required): Choose the option that best describes the main idea of the passage.
*   **According to the text, why was backpropagation important?**: *`q2_backpropagation_role`* (Required): Select the option that best describes the significance of backpropagation.
*   **In the context of the passage, what does the term "hallucinations" refer to?**: *`q3_vocabulary`* (Required): Choose the option that best defines "hallucinations" in the context of the passage.
*   **Based on the text, what can be inferred about modern language models?**: *`q4_inference`* (Required): Select the option that best describes what can be inferred about modern language models.
*   **Summarize the difference between the 1950s models and modern Transformers in your own words**: *`q5_summary`* (Required): Provide a brief summary of the difference between the two.
*   **According to the text, how do modern models generate text?**: *`q6_token_prediction`* (Required): Enter your answer to this question based on the passage.
*   **How many minutes did you spend reading the passage?**: *`time_spent_reading`* (Optional): Enter the time you spent reading the passage.
*   **Rate the difficulty of this passage**: *`difficulty_rating`* (Required): Rate the difficulty of the passage on a scale of 1-5.
*   **Any comments or questions about the quiz?**: *`feedback_comments`* (Optional): Enter any feedback or questions you have about the assessment.
*   **Date**: *`date_of_quiz`* (Required): Enter the date you took the assessment.
