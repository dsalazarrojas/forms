# Fit For Work Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fit For Work Assessment Form is a document used to evaluate an employee's fitness for work-related activities. This form aims to assess their current fitness level, readiness for specific work areas, and any additional comments or next steps required for their continued employability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fit For Work Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling in the **Assessment Page** section, which is a text area where you can provide any relevant information about the assessment.
2.  In the **Assessors Name** field, enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
3.  For the **Date Completed** field, select the date on which the assessment was completed.
4.  In the **Work Area** field, choose the specific area of work being assessed (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
5.  In the **Fitness Rating** field, select all the ratings that apply to the individual's fitness level (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3").
6.  For the **Readiness** field, choose the level of readiness of the individual for the selected work area (e.g., "Option 1" or "Option 2").
7.  In the **Comments** field, enter any additional comments or observations about the individual's fitness for work.
8.  The **Assessor Comments** field is for the person conducting the assessment to provide their comments or feedback.
9.  Finally, in the **Assessor Name** field, confirm the name of the person conducting the assessment.
