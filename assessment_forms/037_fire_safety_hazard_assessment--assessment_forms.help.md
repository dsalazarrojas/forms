# Fire_Safety_Hazard_Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fire Safety Hazard Assessment is used to identify potential fire hazards in a specific area or environment. It's a crucial tool for regulatory compliance and risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the sections and fields listed below, making sure to review the fields carefully to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Safety Hazard Assessment** (`fire_safety_hazard_assessment`): This is the main title of the form, where you can enter a brief description of the area or environment being assessed.
* **Section 1** (`section_1`): This section is used to identify specific fire hazards in the area or environment. Select all applicable hazards from the options provided.
*   * **Section 1.1** (`section_1_1`): Select all fire hazards that apply from the options provided.
*   * **Section 1.2** (`section_1_2`): Select one fire hazard that best describes the main concern in this area or environment.
*   * **Section 1.3** (`section_1_3`): Select one fire hazard that best describes the main concern in this area or environment.
* **Section 2** (`section_2`): This section is used to identify specific fire hazards in the area or environment. Select all applicable hazards from the options provided.
*   * **Section 3** (`section_3`): Select all fire hazards that apply from the options provided.
*   * **Section 4** (`section_4`): Select all fire hazards that apply from the options provided.
*   * **Section 5 Goals** (`section_5_goals`): Select one fire hazard that best describes the main concern in this area or environment, then select multiple other hazards that contribute to this main concern.
*   * **Section 5 Goals** (`section_5_goals`): This field is used to select multiple fire hazards that contribute to the main concern selected in the previous field.
* **Signature** (`signature`): This is the field where you can enter your name or contact information for contact purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`): This is the submit button to submit your assessment.

## Tips
*   Review the options carefully to ensure accurate identification of fire hazards.
*   If you're unsure about a hazard, select "None" or "Unknown" to indicate that it's not applicable.
*   Make sure to submit the form only for the correct purpose and area/environment.
