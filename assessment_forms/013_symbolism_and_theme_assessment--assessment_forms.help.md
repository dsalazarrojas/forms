# Symbolism And Theme Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Symbolism And Theme Assessment form is a comprehensive tool used to evaluate the symbolism and theme elements in a text or work of art. This form helps assessors analyze and evaluate the symbolism and theme used, providing a detailed understanding of the work's meaning and significance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your student's name and ID to identify the work being assessed.
2. Provide a title for the text or work being evaluated.
3. Describe the theme of the work in the Theme Description field.
4. Select the type of symbolism used (e.g., symbolism, alliteration, metaphor, simile).
5. Evaluate the theme and provide a justification for your assessment.
6. Analyze the theme and provide a conclusion to your evaluation.
7. Identify the theme's relevance, development, ideas, relationships, supports, oppositions, and implications.
8. Complete any additional fields as required, such as analyzing and concluding on the symbolism.
