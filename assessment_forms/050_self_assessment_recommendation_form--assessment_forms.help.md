# Self Assessment Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool used by employees to evaluate their own performance and progress over a specified time period. It collects relevant information about the employee, such as name, job title, and department, as well as evaluation details like evaluation date and period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure you have a valid email address associated with your company account.
2. Fill out the form with your own information, providing accurate details about your performance and goals.
3. Be honest and thorough in your self-assessment, as it will help your internal evaluators better understand your progress and areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on official company documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on official company documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address associated with your account.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title as recognized by the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your current department as recognized by the company.
* **Years of Service** (`years_of_service`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation period you are assessing.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of the evaluation period (Six Months, Twelve Months, Sixty Months, or Ninety Days).
* **Evaluation Comments** (`evaluation_comments`, text, optional): Provide feedback on your performance and goals for the next evaluation period.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out the form honestly and accurately, as it will be used for internal evaluation and development purposes.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your internal evaluator or HR representative for assistance.
