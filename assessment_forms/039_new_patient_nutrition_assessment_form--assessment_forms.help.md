# new_patient_nutrition_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new patient nutrition assessment form is designed to collect relevant information about new patients, including their demographic details, medical history, and emergency contact information. This form helps healthcare professionals gather necessary data for personalized care and treatment planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's **First name** in the first field.
2. Provide the patient's **Email** address.
3. Enter the patient's **Phone** number.
4. Enter the patient's **Date of birth** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Select the patient's **Time zone** from the available options.
6. Select the patient’s **Gender** from the provided options.
7. Enter the patient’s **Height** in centimeters or meters.
8. Enter the patient’s **Weight** in kilograms.
9. Record the patient’s **Blood Pressure** in mmHg.
10. Mark if the patient is taking **Medication** and select the type.
11. Write any relevant **Medical history** in the provided text field.
12. Select any **Allergies** the patient has from the provided options.
13. Enter the **Emergency contact name**.
14. Enter the **Emergency contact phone** number.
15. Specify the **Relationship** with the emergency contact.
16. Add any **Additional notes** about the patient in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Email**: Provide the patient's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time zone**: Select the time zone the patient is in.
* **Gender**: Select the patient's gender.
* **Height**: Enter the patient's height in centimeters or meters.
* **Weight**: Enter the patient's weight in kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure**: Record the patient's blood pressure in mmHg.
* **Medication**: Mark if the patient is taking medication.
* **Medical history**: Write a brief description of the patient's medical history.
* **Allergies**: Select any allergies the patient has.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Specify the relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Additional notes**: Add any additional notes about the patient.
