# Aerial Lift Safety Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to conduct a safety assessment on aerial lifts to ensure their safe operation. It is essential for inspectors to accurately evaluate the lift's condition and identify potential hazards. This form will help you to systematically evaluate and document the safety of aerial lifts and their components.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure that you have the necessary information about the lift, including its ID/serial number.
2. Fill in the inspector's name and date/time of the assessment.
3. Provide the work location where the assessment was conducted.
4. Perform a visual inspection and select the correct response for each category.
	* "Pass" means the lift passed the inspection without any issues.
	* "Fail" means the lift failed the inspection due to a specific issue.
	* "N/A" means Not Applicable or Not Available (if you cannot perform the inspection).
5. Answer "Pass" or "Fail" for the controls and safety devices.
6. Check for overhead and ground hazards and select the correct response.
7. Verify the Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) of the personnel working with the lift.
8. Provide comments or corrective actions if any issues were found.
9. Complete the inspector's signature and certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name** (2, text, required): Enter your name as the inspector performing the assessment.
* **Date and Time** (3, text, required): Record the date and time of the assessment.
* **Lift ID / Serial Number** (4, text, required): Enter the lift's ID or serial number for reference.
* **Work Location** (5, text, required): Record the specific location where the assessment was conducted.
* **-- Pre-Start Inspection --** (6, note, required): This section is for noting any pre-start inspection results.
* **Visual Inspection Passed?** (7, select_one, required): If the lift's visual inspection passed without any issues.
* **Controls Function Check** (8, select_one, required): If the controls functioned correctly.
* **Safety Devices Check** (9, select_one, required): If the safety devices, such as horns and alarms, functioned correctly.
* **-- Work Zone Assessment --** (10, note, required): This section is for noting any work zone hazards.
* **Overhead Hazards Check** (11, select_one, required): If there were any overhead hazards, such as power lines.
* **Ground Conditions** (12, select_one, required): If the ground conditions were safe for the lift to operate.
* **PPE Verification** (13, select_one, required): If all necessary PPE is present and in good condition.
* **Comments / Corrective Actions** (14, text, required): This section is for describing any issues found during the assessment and any corrective actions taken.
* **Inspector Sign-off** (15, select_one, required): Confirm that you, as the inspector, certify the lift is safe to operate.
