# Work Order System Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Work Order System Assessment form is designed to gather feedback from end-users about their experience with the work order system. This form helps us to identify areas of improvement and make necessary changes to the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select your Department from the "Department" field.
4. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your rating of the system from the "Rating" field.
7. Enter any comments or feedback in the "Comment" field.
8. Describe any issues you have experienced with the system in the "Issues 1" field.
9. Rate your satisfaction with the system in the "Satisfaction 1" field.
10. Provide an improvement idea for the system in the "Improvement Idea 1" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: false): This is the field for your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): This is the field for your last name.
* **Department** (select one, required: false): This field asks you to select your department.
* **Email** (email, required: false): This is the field for your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): This is the field for your phone number.
* **Rating** (select one, required: false): This is the field to rate your experience with the system. You can choose between "Good" and "Fair".
* **Comment** (text, required: false): This is the field for any comments or feedback about the system.
* **Issues 1** (text, required: false): This is the field to describe any issues you have experienced with the system.
* **Satisfaction 1** (time, required: false): This field is not a standard field type and is likely meant to be a scale for rating your satisfaction with the system. You can enter your satisfaction level in any format (e.g., 1-10).
* **Improvement Idea 1** (text, required: false): This is the field to provide an improvement idea for the system.
* **Issues 1** (text, required: false): This is the same field as "Issues 1". Please note that this field is marked as "id: 8" in the schema, but has the same label as "Issues 1" with id: 9. It's likely a duplicate field and should be removed or renumbered to avoid confusion.
