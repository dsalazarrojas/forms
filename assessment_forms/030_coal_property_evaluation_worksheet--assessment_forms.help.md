# Coal Property Evaluation Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and evaluate data on properties related to coal, including their development potential, geological conditions, and economic viability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Property ID to uniquely identify the property.
2. Provide the Property Name and its Location.
3. Enter the Property Acreage in total acres.
4. Specify the Evaluation Date.
5. Enter the Evaluator's Name.
6. For the Coal Seam Information section:
   - Enter the Coal Seam Name.
   - Measure the Coal Seam Thickness in feet.
   - Determine the Average Depth of Seam in feet below surface.
7. Assess the Coal Quality:
   - Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
8. Evaluate the Ground Stability:
   - Choose one of the options: Very stable, Stable, Moderate concerns, Significant concerns, or Unstable.
9. Assess the Water Conditions:
   - Provide a brief description of any water issues present.
10. For the Access and Infrastructure section:
    - Assess the Road Access:
        - Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or None.
    - Evaluate the Proximity to Utilities:
        - Enter the distance to power, water, etc.
11. For the Reserve Estimation section:
    - Enter the Estimated Coal Reserves in tons.
    - Calculate the Estimated Recovery Percentage.
12. For the Financial Assessment section:
    - Enter the Estimated Property Value.
13. For the Overall Assessment section:
    - Assess the Development Potential:
        - Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Limited, or Not viable.
    - Enter the Evaluator's Notes and Recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property ID (2)**: A unique identifier for this property.
* **Property Name (3)**: The name of the property.
* **Property Location (4)**: The county, state, and coordinates of the property.
* **Property Acreage (5)**: The total acreage of the property.
* **Evaluation Date (6)**: The date the evaluation was conducted.
* **Evaluator Name (7)**: The person conducting the evaluation.
* **Coal Seam Name (9)**: The primary coal seam present.
* **Coal Seam Thickness (10)**: The thickness of the coal seam in feet.
* **Average Depth of Seam (11)**: The average depth of the coal seam in feet below surface.
* **Coal Quality Assessment (12)**: An evaluation of the coal quality, with options: Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, or Poor.
* **Overburden Thickness (14)**: The thickness of the overburden in feet.
* **Ground Stability Assessment (15)**: An evaluation of the ground stability, with options: Very stable, Stable, Moderate concerns, Significant concerns, or Unstable.
* **Water Conditions (16)**: A brief description of any water issues present.
* **Road Access Assessment (18)**: An evaluation of the road access, with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or None.
* **Proximity to Utilities (19)**: The distance to power, water, etc.
* **Estimated Coal Reserves (22)**: The estimated coal reserves in tons.
* **Estimated Recovery Percentage (23)**: The percentage that can be recovered.
* **Estimated Property Value (25)**: The estimated property value.
* **Development Potential (28)**: An evaluation of the development potential, with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Limited, or Not viable.
* **Evaluator Notes and Recommendations (29)**: A detailed assessment comments from the evaluator.

Note: This form is designed for data collection and evaluation, and answers to the sections should be provided based on the specific property's attributes.
