# Vessel Weather Capability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vessel Weather Capability Assessment form is designed to evaluate the weather capability of a vessel. This assessment helps to determine the vessel's readiness for various weather conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the vessel assessment in the "Vessel Weather Capability Assessment" field.
2. Choose the type of vessel (Offshore, Inshore, or Other) in the "Vessel Type" field.
3. Indicate whether the vessel is capable of navigating in various weather conditions (Yes/No) in the "Weather Condition" field.
4. Evaluate the sea state of the vessel and select the corresponding number (1-5) in the "Sea State" field.
5. Record your assessment of the wave height of the vessel in the "Wave Height" field.
6. Provide any additional comments about the sea state in the "Sea State Comment" field.
7. Assess the condition of the vessel (Good, Fair, Poor) in the "Vessel Condition" field.
8. Evaluate the experience level of the crew (Low, Medium, High) in the "Crew Experience" field.
9. Indicate the level of training of the crew (None, In progress, Completed) in the "Crew Training" field.
10. Select the level of certification of the crew (None, In progress, Completed) in the "Crew Certification" field.
11. Assess the condition of the vessel equipment (Good, Fair, Needs attention) in the "Vessel Equipment" field.
12. Evaluate the level of weather studies (None, In progress, Completed) in the "Weather Studies" field.
13. Check if the weather radio is used for navigation (None, In progress, Completed) in the "Weather Radio Check" field.
14. Check if emergency procedures are in place (None, In progress, Completed) in the "Emergency Procedures" field.
15. Assess the condition of emergency equipment (None, In progress, Completed) in the "Emergency Equipment" field.
16. Indicate if emergency communication is available (None, In progress, Completed) in the "Emergency Communication" field.
17. Record the number of first assistance missions (1-5) in the "First Assistance Mission" field.
18. Record the number of personnel on the first assistance mission in the "First Assistance Personnel" field.
19. Record the speed of the vessel (1-10) in the "Vessel Speed" field.
20. Record the size of the vessel crew (1-10) in the "Vessel Crew Size" field.
21. Provide an emergency contact name in the "Emergency Contact" field.
22. Record any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
