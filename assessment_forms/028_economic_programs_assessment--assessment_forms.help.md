# Economic Programs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economic Programs Assessment is designed to collect feedback from participants about the effectiveness of economic programs implemented in your organization. This feedback will help in evaluating the impact and effectiveness of these programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Describe the outcomes you have achieved or experienced through the economic program in the "program_outcomes" field.
3. Indicate your level of satisfaction with the program in the "satisfaction" field.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the program in the "additional_comments" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field to allow us to follow up with you if needed.
6. If you participated in the program recently, please enter the date and time of participation in the "date_of_participation" and "time_of_participation" fields.
7. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
8. If you have any additional contact information, please enter it in the "participant_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it is known to us.
* **program_outcomes** (`program_outcomes`, text, required: false): Describe the outcomes you have achieved or experienced through the economic program.
* **satisfaction** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select "satisfied" if you are satisfied with the program.
	+ Select "not satisfied" if you are not satisfied with the program.
* **additional_comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the program.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to follow up with you if needed.
* **date_of_participation** (`date_of_participation`, date, required: false): If you participated in the program recently, please enter the date of participation.
* **time_of_participation** (`time_of_participation`, time, required: false): If you participated in the program recently, please enter the time of participation.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your participant phone number.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as accurately and honestly as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
