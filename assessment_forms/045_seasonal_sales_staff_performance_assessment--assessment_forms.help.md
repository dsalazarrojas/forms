# Seasonal Sales Staff Performance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the performance of seasonal sales staff members. The form collects data on their sales performance, job title, location, start and end dates, sales amount, commission, commission percentage, performance score, and any comments about their performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Seasonal Sales Staff Name** in the field provided.
2. Enter the **Job Title** of the seasonal sales staff member.
3. Enter the **Location** where the seasonal sales staff member is based.
4. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the seasonal sales staff member's term or contract.
5. Enter the **Sales Amount** achieved by the seasonal sales staff member.
6. Enter the **Commission** earned by the seasonal sales staff member.
7. Enter the **Commission Percentage** earned by the seasonal sales staff member.
8. Enter the **Performance Score** out of 100.
9. Provide any **Comments** about the seasonal sales staff member's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Sales Staff Name**: Enter the name of the seasonal sales staff member being assessed.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the seasonal sales staff member.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the seasonal sales staff member is based.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the seasonal sales staff member's term or contract.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the seasonal sales staff member's term or contract.
* **Sales Amount**: Enter the amount of sales achieved by the seasonal sales staff member.
* **Commission**: Enter the commission earned by the seasonal sales staff member.
* **Commission Percentage**: Enter the commission percentage earned by the seasonal sales staff member.
* **Performance Score**: Enter the performance score out of 100.
* **Comments**: Provide any comments or feedback about the seasonal sales staff member's performance.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the assessment is complete and accurate.
- Double-check the dates entered to ensure they are correct.
- Enter the correct commission percentage to ensure accurate calculations.
- Use clear and concise comments to provide useful feedback to the seasonal sales staff member.
