# Preliminary Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preliminary Risk Assessment Form is a tool used to assess potential risks to patient safety. It is used within a medical or healthcare context.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow the steps outlined in the previous section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* 1. **Patient Safety** (1): Enter a brief description of the patient's safety status.
* 2. **Medical Equipment** (2): Select the risks associated with the medical equipment used:
	+ Risk of Mechanical Failure
	+ Risk of Electrical Shock
* 3. **Emergency Procedures** (3): Select all applicable risks related to emergency response or preparedness:
	+ Risk of Delayed Response
	+ Insufficient Training
* 4. **Medical Conditions** (4): Enter a detailed description of the patient's medical condition(s).
* 5. **Risk Factors** (5): Select all applicable factors that may affect the patient's safety:
	+ Age
	+ Health Status
* 6. **Patient Information** (6): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the patient information is available or not.
* 7. **Additional Comments** (7): Provide any additional notes or comments about the patient's safety status.
* 8. **Contact Information** (8): Enter the contact details of the person filling out this form or the team member responsible for the patient's care.
* 9. **Date of Birth** (9): Enter the patient's date of birth if required or relevant.
* 10. **Date of Last Update** (10): Enter the last update or revision date of this form, if applicable.
* 11. **Note** (11): This field is for any additional comments or notes. Please use it as needed.
* 12. **Email** (12): Enter a contact email address if you need to be contacted regarding updates or changes to the patient's safety status.
