# Allergy Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help healthcare providers assess the risk of allergies in patients. It collects information on patient demographics, medical history, and allergy risk factors to determine the level of risk for allergic reactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's first and last name, date of birth, and assessment date.
2. **Allergies and Medical History**: List any known allergies, medical conditions, and family history of allergies or asthma.
3. **Risk Level**: Rate the risk level for food, drug, and environmental allergies.
4. **Personal and Family History**: Answer questions about personal and family history of allergies, asthma, and eczema.
5. **Previous Reactions and Treatment**: Describe any previous allergic reactions and emergency treatment required.
6. **Allergy Testing and Medications**: Answer questions about allergy testing history and current allergy medications.
7. **Risk Factors**: Select any additional risk factors that may be relevant to the patient's allergy risk.
8. **Overall Risk Assessment**: Rate the overall risk level of the patient's allergies.
9. **Recommendations**: Provide any recommended actions or treatments for the patient.
10. **Assessor's Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments from the assessor.
11. **Certification and Follow-Up**: Complete the form by indicating the assessor's signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (ID 1): Enter the patient's first name. This is required information.
* **Last Name** (ID 2): Enter the patient's last name. This is required information.
* **Date of Birth** (ID 3): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format. This is required information.
* **Assessment Date** (ID 4): Enter the date the assessment was completed. This is required information.
* **Assessor Name** (ID 5): Enter the name of the healthcare provider completing the form. This is required information.
* **Assessor Title** (ID 6): Enter the title of the healthcare provider (e.g., MD, NP, RN). This is required information.
* **Known Allergies** (ID 7): List any known allergies the patient has. This is required information.
* **Allergy Categories** (ID 8): Select any categories of allergies that apply to the patient. For example, food, drug, environmental, etc. This is required information.
* **Food Allergy Risk Level** (ID 9): Rate the risk level of the patient's food allergy. Select one of the five options: No known risk, Low risk, Moderate risk, High risk, or Confirmed allergy. This is required information.
* **Drug Allergy Risk Level** (ID 10): Rate the risk level of the patient's drug allergy. Select one of the five options: No known risk, Low risk, Moderate risk, High risk, or Confirmed allergy. This is required information.
* **Environmental Allergy Risk Level** (ID 11): Rate the risk level of the patient's environmental allergy. Select one of the five options: No known risk, Low risk, Moderate risk, High risk, or Confirmed allergy. This is required information.
* **Family History of Allergies** (ID 12): Indicate if the patient has a family history of allergies. Select one of the four options: No known family history, One parent with allergies, Both parents with allergies, Sibling with allergies, or Multiple family members. This is required information.
* **Family History of Asthma** (ID 13): Indicate if the patient has a family history of asthma. Select one of the three options: False, Yes, one family member, or Yes, multiple family members. This is required information.
* **Family History of Eczema** (ID 14): Indicate if the patient has a family history of eczema. Select one of two options: False or True. This is required information.
* **Personal History of Asthma** (ID 15): Indicate if the patient has a personal history of asthma. Select one of four options: False, Yes, mild, Yes, moderate, or Yes, severe. This is required information.
* **Personal History of Eczema** (ID 16): Indicate if the patient has a personal history of eczema. Select one of three options: False, Yes, past, or Yes, current. This is required information.
* **Personal History of Allergic Rhinitis** (ID 17): Indicate if the patient has a personal history of allergic rhinitis (hay fever). Select one of four options: False, Yes, seasonal, Yes, perennial, or Yes, both. This is required information.
* **Previous Allergic Reactions** (ID 18): Describe any previous allergic reactions the patient has experienced. Select one of five options: Never, Once, 2-5 times, More than 5 times, or Frequent reactions. This is required information.
* **Most Severe Reaction** (ID 19): Describe the worst allergic reaction the patient has experienced. This is an optional field.
* **History of Anaphylaxis** (ID 20): Indicate if the patient has a history of anaphylaxis. Select one of four options: False, Yes, once, Yes, multiple times, or Unknown. This is required information.
* **Anaphylaxis Trigger** (ID 21): Describe any triggers for anaphylaxis. This is an optional field.
* **Emergency Treatment Required** (ID 22): Indicate if the patient has ever required emergency treatment for an allergic reaction. Select one of four options: Never, Once, Multiple times, or Unknown. This is required information.
* **Epinephrine Prescription** (ID 23): Indicate if the patient has a current epinephrine prescription. Select one of four options: Yes, current prescription, Yes, but expired, No, never prescribed, or No, but should have one. This is required information.
* **Current Allergy Medications** (ID 24): List any current allergy medications the patient is taking. This is required information.
* **Allergy Testing History** (ID 25): Indicate if the patient has ever been tested for allergies. Select one of five options: Never tested, Skin prick test, Blood test (RAST), Patch test, or Food challenge. This is required information.
* **Testing Results** (ID 26): Summarize any previous testing results. This is an optional field.
* **Additional Risk Factors** (ID 27): Select any additional risk factors that may be relevant to the patient's allergy risk. This is an optional field.
* **Overall Risk Assessment** (ID 28): Rate the overall risk level of the patient's allergies. Select one of five options: Minimal risk, Low risk, Moderate risk, High risk, or Very high risk. This is required information.
* **Risk Factors Summary** (ID 29): Provide a summary of any additional risk factors. This is required information.
* **Recommendations** (ID 30): Provide any recommendations for the patient. This is required information.
* **Epinephrine Recommendation** (ID 31): Indicate if the patient should carry an epinephrine injector. Select one of four options: Yes, strongly recommended, Yes, recommended, Optional, or Not necessary. This is required information.
* **Allergy Action Plan Needed** (ID 32): Indicate if a plan is needed for the patient's allergies. Select one of four options: Yes, create immediately, Yes, at next visit, No, existing plan adequate, or No plan needed. This is required information.
* **Follow-Up Required** (ID 33): Indicate when the patient should return for follow-up care. Select one of five options: Urgent - within 1 week, Soon - within 1 month, Routine - within 3 months, Annual follow-up, or No follow-up needed. This is required information.
* **Specialist Referral Needed** (ID 34): Indicate if the patient needs to be referred to a specialist. Select one of three options: Yes, urgent, Yes, routine, or No, continue current care. This is required information.
* **Patient Education Provided** (ID 35): Indicate if any education was provided to the patient. Select one of four options: Allergen avoidance, Medication use, Epinephrine administration, Emergency action plan, Symptom recognition, or None provided. This is required information.
* **Additional Notes** (ID 36): Add any additional notes or comments from the assessor. This is an optional field.
* **Assessor Signature** (ID 37): Complete by indicating the assessor's signature. Select one of two options: I certify this assessment is accurate, or Not yet signed.
* **Assessment Date** (ID 38): Complete by indicating the date the assessment was signed. This is required information.

## Tips

* Always complete the form with the patient's best interest in mind.
* Be thorough and accurate when assessing the patient's allergy risk.
* Consider any other relevant medical history or risk factors that may not be listed in the form.
* Follow any recommended guidelines or protocols for allergy risk assessment.
