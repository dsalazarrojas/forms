# Productivity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Productivity Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate an employee's productivity, work habits, and satisfaction. It helps to identify areas of improvement and facilitates regular feedback and reviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name.
2. Enter your weekly work hours.
3. Rate your job satisfaction (if required).
4. Select your work life balance (if required).
5. Enter your productivity level.
6. Add any additional comments about your productivity factors (if required).
7. Enter your email.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Provide your employee ID.
10. (Optional) Enter your manager ID.
11. Select the date of your last review.
12. Select the time of your last review.
13. Add any review notes (if required).
14. Indicate the status of your assessment.
15. Enter the date you completed this assessment.
16. Enter the name of who last reviewed you.
17. Select the date you were last reviewed.
18. (Optional) Choose the frequency of reviews.
19. Enter your productivity barometer (a note field).
20. (Optional) Add a hint for your productivity barometer.
21. Enter the name of who completed your assessment.
22. (Optional) Enter your productivity score.
23. (Optional) Add a hint for your productivity score.
24. (Optional) Select the frequency of reviews.
25. (Optional) Enter the name of who last reviewed you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Work Hours Per Week**: Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Job Satisfaction**: If required, rate how satisfied you are with your job, with options: Highly Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Not Satisfied.
* **Work Life Balance**: If required, select how well your work and personal life are balanced, with options: Highly Balanced, Somewhat Balanced, Not Balanced, Not At All Balanced.
* **Time Management Skills**: Add any comments about your time management skills, if required.
* **Productivity Level**: Enter your self-assessment of your productivity level.
* **Productivity Factors**: Add any comments about factors that impact your productivity, if required.
* **Productivity Barometer**: Enter any relevant information, as it is a note field.
* **Productivity Barometer Hint**: Add any hint for the productivity barometer field, if required.
* **Email**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID as it appears on your ID card or personnel records.
* **Manager ID**: If applicable, enter your manager's ID, if required.
* **Date Last Review**: Enter the date of your last review.
* **Time Last Review**: Enter the time of your last review.
* **Review Notes**: Add any comments or feedback from your last review, if required.
* **Assessment Status**: If required, select the status of your current assessment: In Progress, Complete, Not Started.
* **Completed At**: If completed, enter the date you completed this assessment.
* **Completed By**: If applicable, enter the name of who completed this assessment, if required.
* **Productivity Score**: Enter your self-assessed productivity score, if required.
* **Productivity Score Hint**: Add any hint for your productivity score, if required.
* **Review Frequency**: If required, select the frequency of reviews you receive: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly.
* **Last Reviewed By**: If applicable, enter the name of who last reviewed you, if required.
* **Last Reviewed At**: If applicable, enter the date you were last reviewed, if required.
