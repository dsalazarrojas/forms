# Trainee Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Trainee Assessment Form is used to evaluate and provide feedback to trainees in an educational or professional setting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* First Name
	* Email
	* Supervisor
	* Evaluation Date
	* Score
2. Optionally, provide additional information:
	* Department (if applicable)
	* Comments (if you have any feedback or suggestions)
3. Select the Trainee you are evaluating from the list:
	* Trainee 1
	* Trainee 2

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`trainee_1`, text, required): Enter the trainee's first name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the trainee's email address.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the department the trainee belongs to.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainee's supervisor or evaluator.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date the evaluation took place.
* **Score** (`score`, number, required): Enter a numerical score between 1-10 to evaluate the trainee's performance.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional feedback or suggestions for the trainee.
* **Trainee Name** (`trainee_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the trainee being evaluated (only if it's not the same as the first name).
* **Trainee** (`trainee_3`, select_one, required): Select which trainee you are evaluating from the list.
