# Potential Hire Cultural Fit Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the cultural fit of potential hires, helping you make more informed hiring decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Complete each page thoroughly**: Each page is designed to cover a specific topic, so make sure to answer every question to the best of your ability.
2. **Use the options provided**: Select the options that best fit your answer for each question.
3. **Be honest and accurate**: Answer truthfully, and make sure your answers are as accurate as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details**: This is the first page, where you will enter general information about the candidate. Enter their name, and any other relevant details you have about them.
* **Cultural Fit**: Assess the candidate's cultural fit by selecting one of three options: High, Medium, or Low. Think about how well the candidate aligns with your team's or company's cultural values.
* **Interviewer Details**: Enter any relevant information about the person doing the interview, such as their name and title.
* **Cultural Alignment**: Select all the cultural values that the candidate aligns with.
* **Evaluation**: Provide your overall evaluation of the candidate's cultural fit.
* **Recommendation**: Decide whether you recommend the candidate for the role based on their cultural fit.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the candidate's cultural fit.
* **Cultural Values**: Select all the cultural values that the candidate's align with.
* **Candidate Experience**: Assess the candidate's experience level, from 1-3 months to 6-12 months.
* **Interviewer Agreement**: Indicate whether you agree or disagree with the candidate's cultural fit assessment.
* **Cultural Alignment Notes**: Enter any notes about the candidate's cultural alignment.
* **Evaluation Notes**: Enter any notes about your evaluation of the candidate's cultural fit.
* **Recommendation Notes**: Enter any notes about your recommendation of the candidate.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the candidate.
* **Cultural Values Notes**: Enter any notes about the candidate's cultural values.

Note: If a field is not marked as required, it is optional. However, try to fill it out if you have any information to add.
