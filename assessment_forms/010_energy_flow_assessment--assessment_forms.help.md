> # energy_flow_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The energy_flow_assessment form is used to evaluate the energy flow in a facility, including the sources, flow, and goals of energy generation and consumption. This form will help you provide a comprehensive overview of your facility's energy performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answers that best describe your facility's energy sources and flow:
   * Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your facility uses each energy source.
   * Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if each energy flow is present.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **flow_sources_1** (`flow_sources_1`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to determine if your facility uses natural gas as an energy source. Choose 'Yes' if natural gas is used, 'No' otherwise.
* **facility_type_1** (`facility_type_1`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select which type of facility your energy flow assessment applies to. Choose 'Yes' if this field applies to your facility, 'No' otherwise.
* **energy_sources_1** (`energy_sources_1`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to determine if your facility uses each energy source. Choose 'Yes' for each energy source you use, 'No' for each energy source not used.
* **energy_flow_1** (`energy_flow_1`, number, required: false): Enter the amount of energy flow measured in this field.
* **energy_flow_2** (`energy_flow_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the energy flow.
* **energy_flow_3** (`energy_flow_3`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to determine if your facility uses a specific energy flow type. Choose 'Yes' if this energy flow type is used, 'No' otherwise.
* **notes_1** (`notes_1`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your energy flow assessment.
* **energy_flow_4** (`energy_flow_4`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select each energy flow type present. Choose 'Yes' if each energy flow type is present, 'No' otherwise.
* **energy_flow_5** (`energy_flow_5`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to determine if your facility has a specific energy flow characteristic. Choose 'Yes' if this energy flow characteristic is present, 'No' otherwise.
* **energy_flow_6** (`energy_flow_6`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the energy flow measurement.
* **energy_flow_7** (`energy_flow_7`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the energy flow measurement.
* **energy_flow_8** (`energy_flow_8`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select each energy flow characteristic. Choose 'Yes' if each energy flow characteristic is present, 'No' otherwise.
* **energy_flow_9** (`energy_flow_9`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to determine if your facility has a specific energy flow status. Choose 'Yes' if this energy flow status is present, 'No' otherwise.
* **energy_flow_10** (`energy_flow_10`, number, required: false): Enter the energy flow amount measured.
* **energy_flow_11** (`energy_flow_11`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to determine if your facility has a specific energy flow quality. Choose 'Yes' if this energy flow quality is present, 'No' otherwise.
* **energy_flow_12** (`energy_flow_12`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the energy flow.

Note: Some fields are required to be filled only under specific conditions or are not required at all. Ensure you understand the conditions before completing this form.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer each field honestly and accurately to ensure the form reflects your facility's energy flow performance.
* Review and update your energy flow assessment periodically to track changes over time.
* Use this form in conjunction with other assessment forms to gain a comprehensive understanding of your facility's performance and identify areas for improvement.
