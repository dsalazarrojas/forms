# Training Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback on the training provided to participants. It is designed for trainers and assessors to give ratings and comments on the effectiveness of the training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's rating of the training program on the "rating" field.
2. Provide your rating of the trainer's performance on the "trainer_rating" field.
3. Choose a rating for the topic being trained on the "topic" field.
4. If you have any additional comments about the participant's experience, type them in the "comments" field.
5. If you have any further comments, add them in the "additional_comments" field.
6. Enter the participant's email address in the "email" field.
7. If you need to, enter the participant's phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **rating** (1, number, required: false): This field is where you rate the participant's experience of the training program. Rate it from 1 to 5.
* **trainer_rating** (3, number, required: false): This field is where you rate the trainer's performance. Rate it from 1 to 5.
* **topic** (4, select_one, required: false): This field is where you rate the topic being trained. Choose one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or Not Rated.
* **comments** (5, note, required: false): This field is for any additional comments you want to add about the participant's experience.
* **additional_comments** (6, note, required: false): This field is for any further comments you have about the participant's experience.
* **email** (7, email, required: false): This field is where you enter the participant's email address.
* **phone** (8, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the participant's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest and thorough in your ratings and comments.
* If you need to contact the participant, use the "email" field.
* If you have any doubts about how to fill out this form, consult the relevant personnel.
