# fhd_agency_overview_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an agency, including its services, performance, and other relevant details. It is designed to be completed by the agency itself or by someone evaluating the agency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each section of the form carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Fill in the required fields with the correct information about the agency.
3. Use the available options for select fields to choose the most accurate answer.
4. Review and complete all sections of the form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agency Info** (`agency_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the agency, including its name, purpose, or a brief overview.
* **Recruiter Info** (`recruiter_info`, text, required: false): Provide information about the person or team responsible for the agency's operations.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the contact information for the agency, including email, phone number, or physical address.
* **Assessment Info** (`assessment_info`, text, required: false): Write a brief summary of the assessment or evaluation process used by the agency.
* **Agency Services** (`agency_services`, text, required: false): List the services offered by the agency, including any additional services or products.
* **Assessment Questions** (`assessment_questions`, text, required: false): Enter any additional questions or comments related to the agency's performance or services.
* **Agency Services Provided** (`agency_services_provided`, text, required: false): List the services or products offered by the agency, including any additional details.
* **Assessment Questions 2** (`assessment_questions_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or questions related to the agency's performance or services.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the agency.
* **Assessment Completed** (`assessment_completed`, text, required: false): Confirm that the assessment or evaluation is completed.
* **Review and Next Steps** (`review_and_next_steps`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for future evaluation or action.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the assessment or evaluation was completed.
* **Completed Time** (`completed_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the assessment or evaluation was completed.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, select_one, required: false): Choose the person or team responsible for the assessment or evaluation.
* **Reviewer Note** (`reviewer_note`, text, required: false): Enter any notes or comments from the reviewer.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the agency.
* **Assessment Status** (`assessment_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the status of the assessment or evaluation, such as Active, Inactive, or Completed.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, select_one, required: false): Choose the person or team responsible for completing the assessment or evaluation.
* **Agency Status** (`agency_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the status of the agency, such as Active, Inactive, or Completed.
* **Completed Date 2** (`completed_date_2`, date, required: false): Enter the second completed date.
* **Completed Time 2** (`completed_time_2`, time, required: false): Enter the second completed time.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the agency.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Completed By 2** (`completed_by_2`, text, required: false): Enter the person or team responsible for completing the second assessment or evaluation.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the agency.
