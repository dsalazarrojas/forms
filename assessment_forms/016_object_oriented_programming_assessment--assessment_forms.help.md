# Object Oriented Programming Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect your personal information for an Object Oriented Programming Assessment. Please answer the questions truthfully to provide accurate results.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name, which are the only required fields.
2. Enter your Email and Phone number (if you want to be contacted).
3. If applicable, provide your Date of Birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your given name, e.g., John.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your surname, e.g., Smith.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address, e.g., john.smith@example.com.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): If you want to be contacted, enter your phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 1990-02-12.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill in all required fields.
* Use a valid email address.
* Format your date of birth correctly (YYYY-MM-DD).
