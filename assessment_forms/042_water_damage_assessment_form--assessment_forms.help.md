# Water Damage Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Damage Assessment Form is a tool used to collect information about incidents of water damage that have occurred within a building or facility. It helps assess the impact and severity of the damage, and can be used to track progress and status of the repair process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the details of the incident, including the incident description, location, date, and time.
2. Select the type of incident (e.g. Water Damage, Other Damage, or Not Applicable).
3. Choose the status of the incident (e.g. Not Started, In Progress, Complete, or Not Applicable).
4. Select the priority level of the incident (if applicable).
5. Provide the number of employees affected by the incident.
6. Enter the email address of the incident manager (if applicable).
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details about the damage.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, required: false): Provide a more detailed description of the incident, including any relevant background information.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the incident occurred, including the room or building number if applicable.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Photo** (`incident_photo`, note, required: false): Upload any photos or images that may help document the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of incident that occurred (e.g. Water Damage, Other Damage, or Not Applicable).
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the status of the incident (e.g. Not Started, In Progress, Complete, or Not Applicable).
* **Incident Priority** (`incident_priority`, number, required: false): Select a number value to represent the priority level of the incident (e.g. 1, 2, 3).
* **Employees Affected** (`incident_employees_affected`, text, required: false): Provide an estimate of the number of employees affected by the incident.
* **Incident Manager** (`incident_manager`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person managing the incident.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or incident manager for guidance.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only, and any information provided will only be used to track and manage water damage incidents within our facility.
