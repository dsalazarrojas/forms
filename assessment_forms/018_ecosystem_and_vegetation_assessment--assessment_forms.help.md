# Ecosystem And Vegetation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ecosystem And Vegetation Assessment form is used to collect data on the species observed, their abundance, and other relevant details in a specific location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by answering the "Species Observed" question. If you observed any species during your visit, select "Yes" from the options.
2.  If you did not observe any species, select "No" from the options.
3.  Fill in the abundance and coverage fields with numerical values representing the number of individuals or square meters observed.
4.  Enter the Species ID in the "Species ID" field, which is likely a unique identifier for the species.
5.  Enter the common name and scientific name of the species in the respective fields.
6.  Add any additional notes or observations in the "Notes" field.
7.  If you observed a species multiple times, you can add multiple entries in the "Notes" field.
8.  Finally, add any conservation status and if the species is invasive or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Species Observed** (`species_observed`, select_one, required: false): Check if any species were observed during your visit.
* **Abundance** (`abundance`, number, required: false): Enter the number of individuals of the species observed.
* **Coverage** (`coverage`, number, required: false): Enter the square meters of the species' coverage.
* **Species ID** (`species_id`, text, required: false): Enter the Species ID of the species observed.
* **Common Name** (`common_name`, text, required: false): Enter the common name of the species observed.
* **Scientific Name** (`scientific_name`, text, required: false): Enter the scientific name of the species observed.
* **Genus** (`genus`, text, required: false): Enter the genus of the species observed.
* **Species Notes** (`species_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or observations about the species.
* **Habitat** (`habitat`, text, required: false): Enter the habitat where the species was observed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or observations about the species.
* **Is Species Common** (`species_common`, select_one, required: false): Check if the species is common or not.
* **Conservation Status** (`conservation_status`, select_one, required: false): Enter the conservation status of the species.
* **Is Invasive** (`invasive`, select_one, required: true): Check if the species is invasive or not.
* **Date Observed** (`date_observed`, date, required: false): Enter the date when the species was observed.
* **Time Observed** (`time_observed`, time, required: false): Enter the time when the species was observed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the species was observed.
* **Latitude** (`latitude`, number, required: false): Enter the latitude of the location.
* **Longitude** (`longitude`, number, required: false): Enter the longitude of the location.
* **Altitude** (`altitude`, number, required: false): Enter the altitude of the location.
* **Notes Terrain** (`notes_terrain`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes or observations about the terrain.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy.
* Use the "Notes" field to add any additional details or observations.
* If you're unsure about a field, leave it blank or contact the relevant authority for guidance.
