# Constructive Feedback And Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for participants to provide constructive feedback and self-assessment on their performance and professional development. It's designed for individuals to reflect on their accomplishments, areas for improvement, and goals for the next period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by reviewing the form sections and answering the questions relevant to your role and context.
2. Ensure that you are providing honest and accurate information about your performance and development.
3. Answer each question carefully, taking into account your experiences and reflections during the assessment period.
4. Review and revise your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): This field is used to capture your full name as a participant in the assessment.
* **Current Job Title and Department** (`participant_job_title`, text, required): Please enter your current job title and department as it reflects your role and responsibilities during the assessment period.
* **Assessment Period** (`assessment_period_ident`, text, required): Specify the assessment period, e.g., Q1 2024, Annual Review.
* **Type of Assessment** (`assessment_type_ident`, select_one, required): Select the type of assessment, e.g., Self-Assessment, Peer Review, Supervisor Feedback, 360-degree Feedback.
* **What are your three most significant professional achievements this period?** (`achievement_summary_desc`, text, required): Share your three most significant professional achievements during the assessment period. 
* **Describe a major challenge you faced and how you overcame it** (`challenge_handling_reflection`, text, required): Share a specific example of a challenge you faced and how you successfully handled it.
* **Current Level of Job Satisfaction (1-10)** (`job_satisfaction_rating`, number, required): Rate your current level of job satisfaction, with 1 being very dissatisfied and 10 being highly satisfied.
* **Communication and Interpersonal Skills** (`communication_rating`, number, required): Rate your communication and interpersonal skills, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration** (`team_collaboration_rating`, number, required): Rate your ability to work with teams and collaborate with others, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.
* **Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving** (`problem_solving_rating`, number, required): Rate your critical thinking and problem-solving skills, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.
* **Time Management and Prioritization** (`time_management_rating`, number, required): Rate your time management and prioritization skills, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.
* **Job-Specific Technical Knowledge** (`technical_skills_rating`, number, required): Rate your job-specific technical skills, with 1 being poor and 5 being exceptional.
* **What are your primary areas for professional growth/improvement?** (`improvement_areas_ident`, text, required): Share specific areas you need to improve or grow professionally.
* **What additional training or resources would support your development?** (`training_needs_summary`, text, required): Suggest any training or resources that could help you grow professionally.
* **Primary Professional Goals for the Next Period** (`next_period_goals_ident`, text, required): Set specific goals for the next assessment period, up to 1000 characters.
* **Constructive Feedback for Others** (`section_constructive_feedback`, note, optional): This section allows you to provide constructive feedback to your supervisor or team members.
* **What could your supervisor do to better support your work?** (`supervisor_feedback_desc`, text, optional): Share specific suggestions for your supervisor's support.
* **One suggestion to improve the overall team/company culture** (`company_improvement_feedback`, text, optional): Suggest ways to improve the overall team or company culture.
* **Date of Assessment Completion** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the assessment.
* **I confirm the accuracy and honesty of this assessment** (`final_ack_ident`, select_one, required): Select "I Acknowledge and Confirm" to confirm the accuracy and honesty of your assessment.
