# Speaking Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaking Evaluation Form is a tool used to assess a speaker's performance in a speaking evaluation exercise. This form is designed to provide a comprehensive evaluation of the candidate's communication skills, content knowledge, and overall rating.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, carefully read the candidate's speaking performance during the exercise and answer the questions based on your observation. For each question, select the appropriate rating based on your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **introduction** (text, optional): A brief description of the introduction made by the candidate at the beginning of the speaking evaluation exercise.
* **candidate id** (number, optional): The unique identifier for the candidate being evaluated.
* **evaluation criteria** (select multiple, optional): Select all criteria that apply to the candidate's performance.
	+ Communication skills
	+ Content knowledge
	+ Pronunciation
	+ Confidence
	+ Other (please specify)
* **communication skills** (select one, optional): Rate the candidate's communication skills as:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
* **content knowledge** (select one, optional): Rate the candidate's content knowledge as:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
* **pronunciation** (select one, optional): Rate the candidate's pronunciation as:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
* **body language** (select one, optional): Rate the candidate's body language as:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
* **eye contact** (select one, optional): Rate the candidate's eye contact as:
	+ Strong
	+ Fair
* **feedback** (text, optional): Write comments about the candidate's performance.
* **overall rating** (select one, optional): Rate the candidate's overall performance as:
	+ 5
	+ 6
	+ 7
	+ 8
	+ 9

## Tips
* Be objective and unbiased in your assessment.
* Consider the candidate's strengths and weaknesses when evaluating their performance.
* Use the evaluation criteria selected in the "evaluation criteria" field as a guide for your assessment.
* Take your time to complete the form carefully and accurately.
