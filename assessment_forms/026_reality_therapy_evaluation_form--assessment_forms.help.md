# Reality Therapy Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reality Therapy Evaluation Form is a tool for licensed professionals to assess the effectiveness of reality therapy sessions. It helps practitioners collect notes from the session, rate the client's progress, and plan follow-up activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the session notes (Page 1, Field 1).
2. Enter the Therapist Name (Page 1, Field 2).
3. Enter the Client Name (Page 1, Field 3).
4. Select the Client Relationship (Page 2, Field 4).
5. Rate the client's performance on the Rating Scales (Page 2, Fields 6, 7, 8).
6. Enter any Recommendations (Page 2, Field 9).
7. Plan follow-up activities in the Follow-up Planning field (Page 2, Field 10).
8. Enter Next Steps for the client (Page 2, Field 11).
9. Enter the Evaluation Date and Time (Page 3, Fields 12 and 13).
10. Choose who submitted the evaluation (Page 3, Field 14).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, optional): Write down any important notes from the session.
* **Therapist Name** (`therapist_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the therapist or practitioner who conducted the session.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client who received the therapy.
* **Client Relationship** (`client_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select the nature of the client's relationship (Parent-Child, Friend, Partner, etc.).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the session (e.g., 30 minutes).
* **Rating Scale 1** (`rating_scale_1`, number, optional): Rate the client's performance on a 1-5 scale. The rating indicates their current level of progress.
* **Rating Scale 2** (`rating_scale_2`, number, optional): Rate the client's performance on a 1-5 scale. The rating indicates their expected growth.
* **Rating Scale 3** (`rating_scale_3`, number, optional): Rate the client's performance on a 1-5 scale. The rating indicates their overall progress.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text, optional): Provide any recommendations or suggestions for the client's future sessions.
* **Follow-up Planning** (`follow_up_planning`, text, optional): Plan any follow-up activities or sessions for the client.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Enter any next steps or instructions for the client.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the evaluation (User 1, User 2, User 3, etc.).
