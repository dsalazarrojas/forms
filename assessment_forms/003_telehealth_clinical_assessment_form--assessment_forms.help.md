# Telehealth Clinical Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telehealth Clinical Assessment Form is used to collect and record clinical information about patients, including demographic data, medical conditions, and health status. This form helps healthcare professionals assess the patient's situation accurately and provide appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Provide the patient's age in the **patient_age** field.
3. List the patient's current medical condition in the **medical_condition** field.
4. Indicate the patient's current health status:
	* Select **Active** for patients who are currently healthy.
	* Select **Inactive** for patients who are not currently healthy.
5. Enter the patient's contact information:
	* Enter the patient's phone number in the **patient_phone** field.
	* Enter the patient's email address in the **email** field.
6. Provide the patient's medical history in the **medical_history** field.
7. Add any additional notes about the patient in the **assessment_form_fields_10** field.
8. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the **medication** field.
9. Enter the name of the medical specialist the patient is under the care of in the **medical_specialist** field.
10. Enter the date of the patient's last medical check-up in the **assessment_form_fields_13** field.
11. Enter the time of the patient's last medical check-up in the **assessment_form_fields_14** field.
12. List any allergies the patient has in the **patient_allergies** field.
13. Add any additional comments or notes about the patient in the **assessment_form_fields_20** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **patient_name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **patient_age** (number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **medical_condition** (text, optional): List the patient's current medical condition.
* **health_status** (select_one, required): Indicate the patient's current health status:
	+ **Active**: The patient is currently healthy.
	+ **Inactive**: The patient is not currently healthy.
* **patient_phone** (text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **medical_history** (text, optional): Provide the patient's medical history.
* **assessment_form_fields_10** (text, optional): Add any additional notes about the patient.
* **medication** (text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **medical_specialist** (text, optional): Enter the name of the medical specialist the patient is under the care of.
* **assessment_form_fields_13** (date, optional): Enter the date of the patient's last medical check-up.
* **assessment_form_fields_14** (time, optional): Enter the time of the patient’s last medical check-up.
* **patient_allergies** (text, optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **assessment_form_fields_20** (text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient.

Note: Please be sure to complete all required fields.
