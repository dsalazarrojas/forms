# Service Proposal Value Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Proposal Value Assessment form is designed to evaluate the value of service proposals submitted to assess their feasibility and potential impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. **Service Proposal**: Enter a brief description of the service proposal you are evaluating. This will help us understand the proposal's scope and objectives.
2. **Review Service**: Select the level of service quality you believe this proposal will provide (High, Medium, Low). This will help us assess the proposal's potential impact.
3. **Benefits**: List the benefits of the proposal as per your understanding.
4. **Costs**: Provide an estimated cost breakdown of the proposal.
5. **Overall Value**: Rate the overall value of the proposal from 1 to 10.
6. **Overall Value Justification**: Explain your reasoning behind the overall value rating.
7. **Stakeholders**: List all stakeholders involved in the proposal.
8. **Stakeholder Relationships**: Describe the relationships between stakeholders.
9. **Decision Making Process**: Describe the decision-making process used to evaluate the proposal.
10. **Review Date**: Enter the date the proposal was reviewed.
11. **Review Time**: Enter the time spent reviewing the proposal.
12. **Next Steps**: Specify any next steps or actions required.
13. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the proposal.
14. **Rating**: Rate the proposal from 1 to 10, based on your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Proposal**: A brief description of the service proposal.
* **Review Service**: A scale of High, Medium, or Low, indicating the quality of service provided.
* **Benefits**: An estimated list of benefits as per the proposal.
* **Costs**: An estimated cost breakdown of the proposal.
* **Overall Value**: A rating from 1 to 10 indicating the overall value of the proposal.
* **Overall Value Justification**: A brief explanation of the reasoning behind the overall value rating.
* **Stakeholders**: A list of all stakeholders involved in the proposal.
* **Stakeholder Relationships**: A description of the relationships between stakeholders.
* **Decision Making Process**: A description of the decision-making process used to evaluate the proposal.
* **Review Date**: The date the proposal was reviewed.
* **Review Time**: The time spent reviewing the proposal.
* **Next Steps**: A brief description of any next steps or actions required.
* **Comments**: Additional comments or observations about the proposal.
* **Rating**: A final rating from 1 to 10, based on the evaluation.
