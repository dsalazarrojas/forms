This guide is intended for end-users who need to complete the Brand Risk Assessment Form. The form is designed to assess the risk level and potential impact of a brand's operations, products, or services. The form will help identify areas of risk and provide a framework for mitigation actions to reduce those risks.

Please note that this form is a one-time assessment and not a continuous monitoring tool. It's essential to fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure the assessment is comprehensive.

Before starting, review the form structure and fields to understand what information is required and how it should be filled out.

## Brand Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Risk Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the potential risks associated with a brand's operations, products, or services. It helps assessors identify areas of risk and provide recommendations for mitigation actions to reduce those risks.

## How To Complete This Form
### To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  **Assessment Date**: Fill in the date of the risk assessment. This will help track the progress and follow-up on any identified risks.
2.  **Assessor Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
3.  **Risk Area**: Select the area of the brand's operation that is being assessed. This could be Reputation, Compliance, Financial, or Operational.
4.  **Likelihood**: Choose the likelihood of the risk occurring, which can be High, Medium, or Low.
5.  **Impact**: Select the potential impact if the risk occurs, which can be Severe, Moderate, or Minor.
6.  **Mitigation Actions** (Optional): If you have identified actions to reduce the risk, enter them here.
7.  **Risk Owner**: Enter the name of the person responsible for mitigating the risk.
8.  **Review Date** (Optional): If you want to schedule a follow-up review, enter the date here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, Required): Enter the date of the risk assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Risk Area** (`risk_area`, `select_one`, Required): Select the area of the brand's operation that is being assessed (Reputation, Compliance, Financial, Operational).
* **Likelihood** (`likelihood`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the likelihood of the risk occurring (High, Medium, Low).
* **Impact** (`impact`, `select_one`, Required): Select the potential impact if the risk occurs (Severe, Moderate, Minor).
* **Mitigation Actions** (`mitigation_actions`, `text`, Optional): Enter any actions to reduce the risk, if applicable.
* **Risk Owner** (`owner`, `text`, Required): Enter the name of the person responsible for mitigating the risk.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, Optional): If you want to schedule a follow-up review, enter the date here.

## Tips

* Be thorough when filling out the form to ensure a comprehensive risk assessment.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Use the form as a one-time assessment, and consider revising it if necessary based on new information.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the form instructions or contact your supervisor for guidance.
