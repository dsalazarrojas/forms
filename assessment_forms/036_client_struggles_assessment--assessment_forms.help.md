# Client Struggles Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you identify and assess your client's key challenges and goals. By providing detailed information about their struggles, you will be able to better understand their business needs and develop targeted solutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring you provide accurate and honest information about your client's struggles and goals.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, and feel free to elaborate on your responses if needed.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please provide the best answer you can based on your knowledge of the client's situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Client Challenges Assessment --** (`assessment_intro`, note, not required): This section is a brief introduction to the assessment. It's a note field where you can provide any additional context or information about the client's challenges.
* **Your Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification or business card.
* **Company or Organization** (`client_company`, text, not required): If applicable, please enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Your Role or Title** (`client_role`, text, not required): Enter your current role or title within the company or organization.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with any questions or follow-up information.
* **Primary Business Struggle** (`primary_struggle`, text, required): Describe your biggest challenge or struggle you are currently facing in your business.
* **Impact of This Struggle** (`struggle_impact`, text, required): Explain how this challenge affects your business and what consequences it has on your operations.
* **How Long Have You Faced This Challenge?** (`struggle_duration`, select_one, required): Choose how long you have been facing this challenge from the options provided:
	+ Less than 3 months
	+ 3-6 months
	+ 6-12 months
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2+ years
* **Secondary Struggles or Challenges** (`secondary_struggles`, text, not required): List any other significant challenges you are facing.
* **Solutions You Have Already Tried** (`previous_solutions_tried`, text, not required): Describe any solutions or methods you have tried to address these issues.
* **Barriers Preventing Solution** (`barriers_to_solution`, text, not required): Explain what prevents you from solving this challenge.
* **Top Business Priorities** (`business_priorities`, text, required): Describe your top business goals and objectives.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Choose how urgent it is to resolve this challenge:
	+ Critical - needs immediate attention
	+ High - within 1 month
	+ Medium - within 3 months
	+ Low - can wait
* **Ideal Outcome or Solution** (`ideal_outcome`, text, required): Describe the ideal solution or outcome you would like to achieve.
* **How Would You Measure Success?** (`success_metrics`, text, not required): Explain how you would measure success for this challenge or solution.
* **Available Resources or Budget** (`available_resources`, text, not required): List any resources or budget you can allocate to solve this challenge.
* **Team Buy-in or Support** (`team_support`, select_one, not required): Choose the level of support from your team:
	+ Strong support
	+ Good support
	+ Moderate support
	+ Limited support
	+ No support
* **Additional Context or Information** (`additional_context`, text, not required): Provide any additional context or information that might be helpful in understanding your client's challenges.
