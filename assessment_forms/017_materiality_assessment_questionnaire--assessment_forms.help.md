# Materiality Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Materiality Assessment Questionnaire form, which is used to report and monitor an organization's sustainability and ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Organization field with your company's name and other relevant details.
2. Indicate whether your organization has sustainability and ESG issues by selecting all applicable options from the Sustainability and ESG Issues field.
3. Choose your organization's materiality level from the Materiality Level field.
4. Select the impact categories that apply to your organization in the Impact Category field.
5. If necessary, specify other impact categories in the Other (specify) field.
6. Choose the time frame for your assessment in the Time Frame field.
7. Select the reporting period for your assessment in the Reporting Period field.
8. Choose how frequently you will report your assessment in the Reporting Frequency field.
9. If necessary, specify a different reporting frequency in the Other (specify) field.
10. Enter the date your assessment was submitted.
11. Enter the date your assessment was reviewed.
12. Enter the date your assessment was approved.
13. Choose who submitted the assessment in the Submitted By field.
14. Enter the name of the reviewer who reviewed your assessment.
15. Enter the name of the person who approved your assessment.
16. Choose who submitted the assessment to in the Submitted To field.
17. If necessary, specify a different submitted to in the Other (specify) field.
18. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the assessment.
19. Select the department that received the assessment in the Department field.
20. If necessary, specify a different department in the Other (specify) field.
21. Enter the position of the person who submitted the assessment.
22. Enter the title of the person who submitted the assessment.
23. Enter any additional comments about the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization** (Text, Required): Enter your company's name and other relevant details.
- **Sustainability and ESG Issues** (Select Multiple, Required): Check all applicable issues related to sustainability and ESG.
- **Materiality Level** (Select One, Required): Choose your organization's materiality level.
- **Impact Category** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable impact categories.
- **Other (specify)** (Text, Optional): If necessary, specify other impact categories not listed.
- **Time Frame** (Time, Required): Choose the time frame for your assessment.
- **Reporting Period** (Time, Required): Select the reporting period for your assessment.
- **Reporting Frequency** (Select One, Required): Choose how frequently you will report your assessment.
- **Other (specify)** (Text, Optional): If necessary, specify a different reporting frequency.
- **Reporting Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date you submitted the assessment.
- **Reporting Deadline** (Time, Required): Enter the deadline for your assessment submission.
- **Submitted By** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who submitted the assessment.
- **Reviewer** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the assessment.
- **Approved Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the assessment was approved.
- **Approved By** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person who approved the assessment.
- **Submitted To** (Select One, Required): Choose who submitted the assessment to.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the assessment.
- **Department** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the department that received the assessment.
- **Other (specify)** (Text, Optional): If necessary, specify a different department.
- **Position** (Text, Required): Enter the position of the person who submitted the assessment.
- **Title** (Text, Required): Enter the title of the person who submitted the assessment.
- **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the assessment.
