# Kinetic Chain Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kinetic Chain Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect and analyze data from clients regarding their physical condition. It's used to evaluate their mobility, strength, and overall fitness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Trained By field with the name of the person who trained you.
2. Enter your Client Name and Date of Birth accurately.
3. Provide your Phone number and Email for contact purposes.
4. Describe your Movement Observations in the provided text field.
5. Set a Follow-up Date if necessary.
6. Add any additional Note if required or if you have any comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trained By**: The name of the person who trained you.
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Your email address for communication.
* **Movement Observations**: Describe any notable movements or actions that are relevant to your assessment.
* **Follow-up Date**: If you need to be contacted again, provide a date for follow-up.
* **Note**: Any additional comments or information about your condition.
