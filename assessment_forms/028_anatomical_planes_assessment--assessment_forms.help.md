# Anatomical Planes Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Anatomical Planes Assessment" form is a critical tool used to evaluate a student's knowledge and understanding of the anatomical planes in the human body. This form is designed to assess a student's ability to identify and describe the sagittal, transverse, and coronal planes, and their applications in clinical settings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your name and the course ID/number as prompted.
2. Review the assessment questions and provide an example of a cut along the transverse plane.
3. Select the correct answer for the sagittal plane description and coronal plane alternative name.
4. Rate your confidence level in identifying anatomical planes.
5. Write a reflection on the importance of anatomical orientation in clinical settings.
6. If you are an instructor, provide feedback on the student's answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`): This is the field where you need to enter your full name as prompted.
* **Course ID/Number** (`course_id`): Enter the course ID/number as provided.
* **Sagittal Plane Description** (`sagittal_plane_description`): Choose the description that best describes the sagittal plane. The options are:
	+ Divides body into left and right
	+ Divides body into front and back
	+ Divides body into top and bottom
* **Transverse Plane Example** (`transverse_plane_example`): Provide an example of a cut along the transverse plane. Choose one of the provided options:
	+ A horizontal cut through the waist
	+ A vertical cut through the ears
	+ A vertical cut from nose to back
* **Coronal Plane Alternative Name** (`coronal_plane_alternative_name`): Select the alternative name for the coronal plane. The options are:
	+ Frontal Plane
	+ Horizontal Plane
	+ Median Plane
* **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`): Rate your confidence level in identifying anatomical planes. Choose one of the provided options:
	+ Very Confident
	+ Confident
	+ Somewhat Confident
	+ Not Confident
* **Written Reflection** (`written_reflection_on_anatomical_orientation`): Write a brief reflection on the importance of anatomical orientation in clinical settings. This is an opportunity to demonstrate your understanding of the subject.
* **Instructor Feedback** (`instructor_feedback`): If you are an instructor, provide feedback on the student's answers in this field.
* **Total Questions Answered** (`total_questions_answered`): This field displays the total number of questions answered.
* **Practical Application** (`section_practical_application`): This is a note field for instructor use only.
* **Grading Information** (`section_grading_info`): This is a note field for instructor use only.
