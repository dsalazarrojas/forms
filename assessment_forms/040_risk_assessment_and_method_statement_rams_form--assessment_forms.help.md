# Risk Assessment And Method Statement (RAMS) Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Assessment And Method Statement (RAMS) Form is used to identify, document, and analyze potential hazards on a job site. By filling out this form, users can ensure that all relevant risks are accounted for and control measures are in place to minimize danger.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the introduction page, which serves as a brief overview of the job details.
2.  Move on to the hazards page, where you will select all potential hazards associated with the job.
3.  Evaluate the risk level of each selected hazard using the risk level options provided.
4.  Outline control measures to mitigate the identified risks.
5.  Provide any action items or comments relevant to the risk assessment.
6.  Indicate the review status and review details, including the person assigned to review it and the date of review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): A brief overview of the job details. Please provide a short description of the job, including any relevant information about the location, equipment, or materials involved.
- **Job Details** (`2`, `text`, required: true): Enter a detailed description of the job, including any relevant information about the location, equipment, or materials involved.
- **Hazards** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all potential hazards associated with the job. Please choose from the options provided, or add any other hazards not listed.
- **Risk Level** (`4`, `number`, required: true): Evaluate the risk level of each selected hazard using the provided options: High, Low, Moderate.
- **Control Measures** (`5`, `text`, required: true): Outline all control measures to mitigate the identified risks. This may include training, personal protective equipment, or specific work procedures.
- **Risk Factors** (`6`, `select_one`, required: true): Indicate the risk factors associated with the job, using the provided options: High, Low, Moderate.
- **Risk Control Measures** (`7`, `text`, required: true): Provide a detailed explanation of the risk control measures implemented.
- **Action Items** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Enter any action items or comments relevant to the risk assessment.
- **Review Status** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Indicate the review status of the risk assessment. Choose from the options: Approved, Rejected, In review, Awaiting approval.
- **Comments** (`10`, `text`, required: true): Enter any comments or feedback on the risk assessment.
- **Assigned to** (`11`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the person or team assigned to review the risk assessment.
- **Reviewed by** (`12`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the person or team that reviewed the risk assessment.
- **Reviewed on** (`13`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date the risk assessment was reviewed.
