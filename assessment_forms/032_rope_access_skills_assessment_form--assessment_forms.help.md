# Rope Access Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to evaluate your expertise in rope access techniques by assessing your skills in various areas, including safety protocols, rigging practices, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer all required fields.
2. Choose the correct options for each section, taking into account your relevant experience and expertise.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **First Page** (`title`, `text`, not required): Please enter a brief description of your relevant work experience, including the number of years you've worked as a rope access technician.

* **Section 1** (`select_multiple`, not required):
	+ Option 1: Describe your experience working with rope systems in confined spaces.
	+ Option 2: Explain your experience with rigging procedures for suspended access operations.
	+ Option 3: List your experience with rope inspections and maintenance.

* **Section 2** (`number`, not required): Enter the number of years you've completed rope access certifications and training.

* **Section 3** (`select_one`, not required):
	+ Option A: Describe your experience working with rope systems for construction projects.
	+ Option B: Explain your experience with rope access techniques for inspection and maintenance.
	+ Option C: List your experience with rope systems for rescue operations.

* **Section 4** (`time`, not required): Enter the average duration of your rope access work shifts.

* **Section 5** (`note`, not required): Add any additional comments, notes, or relevant information about your rope access experience.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any question, please consult your supervisor or the designated assessors.
- Be thorough and honest when answering the questions to ensure accurate assessment of your expertise.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
