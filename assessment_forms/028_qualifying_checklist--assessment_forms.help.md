# qualifying_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The qualifying checklist is a form used to assess an individual's employment history and current employment status to determine their qualification for certain benefits or programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and honest reporting.
* For each field, select the correct response based on your current situation.
* If you are unsure about a particular field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **has been hired elsewhere** (`has_been_hired_elsewhere`, select_one, required: false): Check if you have been hired or employed by another company in the past, if so, select "Yes".
* **has other sources of income** (`has_other_sources_of_income`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate if you have other sources of income besides employment, such as self-employment or investments.
* **Employment Start Date** (`employment_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you started your current or most recent employment.
* **Employment End Date** (`employment_end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date your current or most recent employment ended.
* **reason for leaving last employment** (`reason_for_leaving_last_employment`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for why you left your last job.
* **current employment status** (`current_employment_status`, select_one, required: false): Check your current employment status:
	+ Select "Active" if you are currently employed.
	+ Select "Inactive" if you are not currently employed.
* **prophets employment history length** (`employment_history_length`, number, required: false): Enter the length of time you have been employed.
* **Employment History Start Date** (`employment_history_start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you started your employment history.

Note: All fields are optional, but please ensure you complete all fields as accurately as possible to ensure proper assessment.
