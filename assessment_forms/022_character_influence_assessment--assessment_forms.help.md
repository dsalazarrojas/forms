# Character Influence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Character Influence Assessment form is used to evaluate the influence of a character on the audience, cultural significance, and narrative arc. It gathers information about the character's role in the story, their cultural influence, and their emotional impact on the audience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the character's role in the story and choose the most accurate description from the provided options.
2. Rate the character's cultural influence on a scale from 0 to 10.
3. Rate the character's influence on the narrative arc on a scale from 0 to 10.
4. Rate the character's emotional impact on the audience on a scale from 0 to 10.
5. Choose the type of behavioral role modeling presented by the character.
6. Rate the character's moral complexity on a scale from 0 to 10.
7. Rate the character's influence on other characters in the story on a scale from 0 to 10.
8. Provide a brief summary of the character's major influence on their world.
9. Consider the potential real-world consequences of the character's portrayal.
10. Confirm that the assessment is based on a thorough analysis.
11. Enter the date of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor Full Name**: Please enter your full name as the assessor.
* **Media Title (Movie, Book, Series, etc.)**: Enter the title of the media being analyzed.
* **Character Being Assessed**: Enter the name of the character being analyzed.
* **Character Role in the Story**: Select the most accurate description of the character's role in the story:
	+ Protagonist: The main character or protagonist of the story.
	+ Antagonist: The main opponent or antagonist of the story.
	+ Supporting Character: A character that supports the protagonist or antagonist.
	+ Mentor: A character that guides or advises the protagonist.
	+ Foil: A character that contrasts with the protagonist or antagonist.
	+ Narrative Device: A character that serves a literary function, such as a plot device or a symbol.
* **Cultural Influence Rating**: Rate the character's cultural influence on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Influence on the Narrative Arc**: Rate the character's influence on the story's plot on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Emotional Impact on the Audience**: Rate the character's emotional impact on the audience on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Behavioral Role Modeling Type**: Select the type of behavioral role modeling presented by the character:
	+ Positive Role Model (Aspirational): A character that inspires positive behavior.
	+ Negative Role Model (Cautionary): A character that warns against negative behavior.
	+ Ambiguous (Complex): A character that presents complex or nuanced behavior.
	+ No Significant Impact: The character has little to no influence on the audience.
* **Moral Complexity Rating**: Rate the character's moral complexity on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Impact on Other Characters**: Rate the character's influence on other characters in the story on a scale from 0 to 10.
* **Summary of Major Influence**: Provide a brief summary of the character's major influence on their world.
* **Potential Real-World Consequences**: Consider the potential real-world impact of the character's portrayal.
* **Confirmation of Influence Assessment**: Confirm that the assessment is based on a thorough analysis.
* **Date of Assessment**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Additional Influence Comments**: Provide any additional relevant information or context.
