# Clinic Service Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Clinic Service Assessment" form is designed to collect feedback on a patient's experience at a clinic. The form aims to evaluate the quality of care, communication, and overall satisfaction. This assessment helps clinics identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Physician Name in the designated field (field 2).
2. Enter the Clinic Name in the designated field (field 3).
3. Select the Assessment Date from the calendar picker or enter it manually.
4. For the "Type of Visit" field (field 8), choose the relevant option from the dropdown menu:
	* New patient
	* Follow-up
	* Routine check-up
	* Urgent care
	* Preventive care
5. For the remaining fields, select the corresponding answer based on your experience:
	* Access to care was easy: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree
	* Wait time was acceptable: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree
	* Quality of care received: Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent
	* Communication quality: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree
	* Patient involvement: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree
	* Questions were answered thoroughly: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree
	* Professionalism: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree
	* Cultural sensitivity: Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly Agree
	* Overall service satisfaction: Very Poor, Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Assessor Information
* Physician Name (`field 2`, text, required): Please enter your name as the assessor.
* Clinic Name (`field 3`, text, required): Enter the name of the clinic where the service was provided.

### Patient Information
* Patient Name (`field 6`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* Medical Record Number (`field 7`, text, optional): If available, enter the patient's medical record number.
* Type of Visit (`field 8`, select_one, required): Choose the type of visit from the dropdown menu.

### Service Assessment
* Assessment Date (`field 4`, date, required): Select the date of the assessment.
* Access to care was easy (`field 10`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with access to care.
* Wait time was acceptable (`field 11`, select_one, required): Rate your experience with wait time.
* Quality of care received (`field 12`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of care provided.
* Communication quality (`field 14`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of communication with the physician.
* Patient involvement (`field 15`, select_one, required): Rate the patient's involvement in decision-making.
* Questions were answered thoroughly (`field 16`, select_one, required): Rate the thoroughness of questions answered.
* Professionalism (`field 18`, select_one, required): Rate the physician's professionalism and respect.
* Cultural sensitivity (`field 19`, select_one, required): Rate the physician's cultural sensitivity.
* Overall service satisfaction (`field 21`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.
* Areas for improvement (`field 22`, text, optional): Optional field for providing additional feedback.

### Tips

* Answer honestly and accurately based on your experience.
* Please select the options that best describe your experience.
* If you have any specific feedback or concerns, include them in the "Areas for improvement" field.
