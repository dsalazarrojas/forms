# Fitness Coaching Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Coaching Assessment Form is a tool for fitness professionals to gather essential information from clients to create tailored coaching plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Take your time to fill out this form accurately and honestly. The more information you provide, the better the coach can understand your needs.
2.  Start by providing your client's health history in the `Client Health History` field. Describe your current health status, including any medical conditions, medications, or previous injuries.
3.  In the `Goals` field, specify your personal and fitness goals. What are you looking to achieve in your fitness journey? Be as specific as possible.
4.  If you have any other goals that are not listed in the `Goals` field, please describe them in the `Other` field. This helps the coach understand your broader objectives.
5.  In the `Lifestyle Data` field, select a date that represents your current lifestyle data, such as your last menstrual cycle, your last menstrual period, or your last workout session.
6.  For `Exercise Frequency`, choose a time that represents how often you exercise per week. This will help the coach understand your current physical activity level.
7.  In the `Medical Conditions` field, describe any medical conditions you are currently managing or have had in the past.
8.  If you take any medications, list them in the `Medications` field. This is an optional field, but it's essential to provide this information for the coach to create a safe and effective coaching plan.
9.  Finally, select the contact person the coach should reach out to in case of an emergency in the `Emergency Contacts` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Health History**: This field is where you'll describe your current health status, including any medical conditions, medications, or previous injuries.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Goals**: Specify your personal and fitness goals. What are you looking to achieve in your fitness journey?
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Goals Goals**: Select the specific goals you have among the listed options (Goal 1, Goal 2, Goal 3). Choose all that apply.
    *   Type: Select One
    *   Required: No
*   **Other**: Describe any other goals not listed in the `Goals` field.
    *   Type: Text
    *   Required: No
*   **Lifestyle Data**: Select a date that represents your current lifestyle data, such as your last menstrual cycle, your last menstrual period, or your last workout session.
    *   Type: Date
    *   Required: No
*   **Exercise Frequency**: Choose a time that represents how often you exercise per week.
    *   Type: Time
    *   Required: No
*   **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions you are currently managing or have had in the past.
    *   Type: Note
    *   Required: No
*   **Medications**: List any medications you're currently taking.
    *   Type: Select Multiple
    *   Required: No
*   **Emergency Contacts**: Select the contact person the coach should reach out to in case of an emergency.
    *   Type: Select One
    *   Required: No

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure the coach can create an effective plan.
*   If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask your coach for guidance.
*   Remember to review the form before submitting it to ensure you've provided all necessary information.
