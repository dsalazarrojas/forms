# Opportunities Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opportunities Assessment Checklist is a tool used to evaluate opportunities based on various factors, providing a systematic and objective assessment of potential opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the list of potential opportunities and select all that apply.
2. Evaluate each selected opportunity based on the provided criteria, selecting the most fitting response for each factor.
3. Fill in the Opportunity Score, if applicable.
4. Provide a brief Business Rationale for each selected opportunity.
5. Answer questions regarding Financial Impact, Strategic Fit, Operational Risk, Potential for Growth, Business Impact, Customer Satisfaction, and Market Trends, selecting the most fitting response for each factor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Potential Opportunities
* **Potential Opportunities** (`potential_opportunities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all potential opportunities that you want to assess.

### Risk Level
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the risk level of each selected opportunity, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.

### Opportunity Score
* **Opportunity Score** (`opportunity_score`, `number`, required: false): If applicable, fill in a numerical score for each selected opportunity.

### Business Rationale
* **Business Rationale** (`business_rationale`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief rationale for each selected opportunity, explaining why it's a potential opportunity.

### Financial Impact
* **Financial Impact** (`financial_impact`, `number`, required: false): Evaluate the financial impact of each selected opportunity, choosing from a numerical value.

### Strategic Fit
* **Strategic Fit** (`strategic_fit`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the strategic fit of each selected opportunity, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.

### Operational Risk
* **Operational Risk** (`operational_risk`, `select_one`, required: false): Assess the operational risk of each selected opportunity, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.

### Potential for Growth
* **Potential for Growth** (`potential_for_growth`, `select_one`, required: false): Determine the potential for growth of each selected opportunity, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.

### Business Impact
* **Business Impact** (`business_impact`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the business impact of each selected opportunity, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.

### Customer Satisfaction
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): Assess the customer satisfaction of each selected opportunity, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.

### Market Trends
* **Market Trends** (`market_trends`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the market trends of each selected opportunity, choosing from High, Medium, or Low.
