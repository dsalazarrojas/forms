# Pharmaceutical Compliance Employment Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your employment status and job characteristics related to pharmaceutical compliance. Please read this guide carefully to ensure you complete the form accurately and without duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and requirements.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a supervisor or HR representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_1_1`, text, required): Enter your first name.
	+ Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`form_1_2`, text, required): Enter your last name.
	+ Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Job Title** (`form_1_3`, text, required): Enter your job title.
	+ Please enter your job title as it appears in official records.
* **Date of Birth** (`form_1_4`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
	+ Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Social Security Number** (`form_1_5`, text, required): Enter your social security number.
	+ Please enter your social security number as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`form_1_6`, email, required): Enter your email address.
	+ Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`form_1_7`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number including area code.
* **Address** (`form_1_8`, text, required): Enter your address.
	+ Please enter your current address as it appears on your official documents.
* **Employment Status** (`form_1_9`, select_multiple, required): Select your employment status.
	+ Please select one or more of the following options: Active, Inactive.
* **Years of Experience** (`form_1_10`, number, required): Enter your years of experience.
	+ Please enter a whole number (e.g., 5, 10, etc.).
* **Job Duration** (`form_1_11`, time, required): Enter your job duration.
	+ Please enter the duration of your job in months or years (e.g., 5 months, 3 years, etc.).
* **Job Type** (`form_1_12`, select_one, required): Select your job type.
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Training History** (`form_1_13`, text, required): Enter your training history.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your past training or experience relevant to this form.
* **Compliance Risk** (`form_1_14`, note, required): Enter your compliance risk assessment.
	+ Please provide a brief description of any compliance risks or issues you have faced.
* **Department** (`form_1_15`, select_one, required): Select your department.
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Job Classification** (`form_1_16`, select_one, required): Select your job classification.
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Job Category** (`form_1_17`, select_one, required): Select your job category.
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Job Description** (`form_1_18`, text, required): Enter your job description.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your job role and responsibilities.
* **Job Location** (`form_1_19`, select_one, required): Select your job location.
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Manager Name** (`form_1_20`, text, required): Enter your manager's name.
	+ Please enter your manager's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Manager Title** (`form_1_21`, text, required): Enter your manager's title.
	+ Please enter your manager's title as it appears on official documents.
* **Manager Email** (`form_1_22`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
	+ Please enter your manager's email address that they check regularly.
* **Manager Phone** (`form_1_23`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
	+ Please enter your manager's phone number including area code.
* **Manager Department** (`form_1_24`, select_one, required): Select your manager's department.
	+ Please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submission Date** (`form_1_25`, date, required): Enter the submission date.
	+ Please enter the date this form was submitted.

## Tips

* Please ensure you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a supervisor or HR representative.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please report them to the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a supervisor or HR representative.
