# Pre Task Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Task Safety Assessment form is designed to ensure that all necessary safety measures are taken before starting a task. It gathers information about the task's description, potential hazards, control measures, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the task description and identify any potential hazards.
2. Select all relevant hazards from the list.
3. Describe any control measures that will be taken to mitigate these hazards.
4. Enter the supervisor's name and date and time started.
5. Enter the field team's name (if applicable).
6. Enter the total hours worked on the task.
7. Sign and date the form as the supervisor and field team leader (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Task Description** (Text)
* Label: Task Description
* Type: Text
* Required: No
* Description: Enter a brief description of the task to be performed.

### 2. **Hazards** (Select Multiple)
* Label: Hazards
* Type: Select Multiple
* Required: No
* Description: Select all potential hazards associated with the task.

### 3. **Control Measures** (Text)
* Label: Control Measures
* Type: Text
* Required: No
* Description: Describe any control measures that will be taken to mitigate the hazards.

### 4. **Safety Briefings** (Text)
* Label: Safety Briefings
* Type: Text
* Required: No
* Description: Enter any additional safety briefings or information relevant to the task.

### 5. **Supervisor Name** (Text)
* Label: Supervisor Name
* Type: Text
* Required: No
* Description: Enter the name of the supervisor responsible for ensuring safety on this task.

### 6. **Field Team Name** (Text)
* Label: Field Team Name
* Type: Text
* Required: No
* Description: Enter the name of the field team performing the task (if applicable).

### 7. **Date Started** (Date)
* Label: Date Started
* Type: Date
* Required: No
* Description: Enter the date the task was started.

### 8. **Time Started** (Time)
* Label: Time Started
* Type: Time
* Required: No
* Description: Enter the time the task was started.

### 9. **Time Finished** (Time)
* Label: Time Finished
* Type: Time
* Required: No
* Description: Enter the time the task was finished.

### 10. **Hours Worked** (Number)
* Label: Hours Worked
* Type: Number
* Required: No
* Description: Enter the total hours worked on the task.

### 11. **Supervisor Signature** (Select One)
* Label: Supervisor Signature
* Type: Select One
* Required: No
* Description: Choose one of the provided options as the supervisor's signature.

### 12. **Field Team Signature** (Select One)
* Label: Field Team Signature
* Type: Select One
* Required: No
* Description: Choose one of the provided options as the field team leader's signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand your organization's safety policies and procedures before completing this form.
* Be thorough when selecting potential hazards and describing control measures.
* Complete all required fields and add any additional relevant information as needed.
* Ensure the supervisor and field team leader sign and date the form to confirm their review and approval.
