# Army Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess various aspects of a soldier's performance, including behavior, interpersonal skills, and mental and emotional status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
3. If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with your supervisor or relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Required): Enter the name of the soldier being assessed.
* **Rank** (Required): Enter the soldier's rank.
* **Unit Assignment** (Required): Enter the soldier's unit assignment.
* **Years of Service** (Required): Enter the number of years the soldier has served.
* **Assessment Date** (Required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessing Officer Name** (Required): Enter your name as the assessing officer.
* **Overall behavior in unit** (Required): Select the overall behavior of the soldier in the unit.
* **Discipline or conduct incidents** (Required): Select the number of discipline or conduct incidents the soldier has had.
* **If yes, describe incidents** (Optional): If the soldier has had incidents, describe them in detail.
* **Insubordination or defiance** (Required): Select the frequency of insubordination or defiance exhibited by the soldier.
* **Relationships with peers** (Required): Select the nature of the soldier's relationships with peers.
* **Relationship with authority** (Required): Select the nature of the soldier's relationship with authority.
* **History of conflicts with others** (Required): Select the severity of the soldier's history of conflicts with others.
* **Emotional stability** (Required): Select the soldier's emotional stability.
* **Anger management and impulse control** (Required): Select the soldier's ability to manage their anger and impulses.
* **Identified risk factors** (Optional): Select any risk factors you have identified for the soldier (e.g. substance use, mental health issues, etc.).
* **Risk of violence toward others** (Required): Select the risk of the soldier being violent toward others.
* **Recommended monitoring level** (Required): Select the recommended level of monitoring for the soldier (e.g. standard, increased, close monitoring).
* **Intervention or counseling needed** (Required): Select whether the soldier needs intervention or counseling.
* **Additional comments or observations** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the soldier.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible.
* Use clear and concise language when describing incidents or observations.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with your supervisor or relevant authorities.
