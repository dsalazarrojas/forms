# Third Party Risk Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess the risk associated with third-party vendors. It collects information about the vendor's name, risk factors, contact person's details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Start by filling out the form title.
Fill in the title of the third-party risk assessment survey.

### Step 2: Provide the vendor's name and other details.
Enter the name of the vendor and other relevant details such as risk factors, contact person's name, email, phone number, job title, and address.

### Step 3: Identify risk factors.
Select all relevant risk factors that you think are associated with the vendor. You can choose from the following options: Vendor's business practices, Vendor's financial stability, Vendor's management structure, Vendor's IT systems, Vendor's data security, Vendor's compliance, or Other.

### Step 4: Specify other risk factors if necessary.
If you selected "Other" in the previous step, provide a brief description of the other risk factors.

### Step 5: Enter contact person's details.
Enter the name, email address, phone number, and job title of the contact person who can best answer questions about the vendor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Third Party Risk Assessment Survey**: This is the title of the form.
* **Vendor Name**: Enter the name of the third-party vendor.
* **Risk Rating**: Enter a numerical value to rate the risk associated with the vendor.
* **Risk Factors**: Select all relevant risk factors that you think are associated with the vendor. These factors include: Vendor's business practices, Vendor's financial stability, Vendor's management structure, Vendor's IT systems, Vendor's data security, Vendor's compliance, or Other.
* **If Other, please specify**: If you selected "Other" in the previous field, provide a brief description of the other risk factors.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the contact person who can best answer questions about the vendor.
* **Contact Person Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Job Title**: Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Vendor's Address**: Enter the address of the vendor.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible when answering questions about risk factors.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your supervisor or IT department for guidance.
