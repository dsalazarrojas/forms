# Cable Hauling Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to assist in the completion of the Cable Hauling Safety Assessment Form. The form is designed to assess the safety of cable hauling operations on a job site, ensuring that all necessary precautions and controls are in place to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working environment for personnel.
## How To Complete This Form
- Start with the project identification section: 
  - Enter the project ID or number in the "Project ID or Number" field.
  - Enter the work site location in the "Work Site Location" field.
  - Identify the crew leader or supervisor in charge of the project in the "Crew Lead / Supervisor" field.
  - Date of assessment should be filled in the "Date of Assessment" field.
- Move on to the hazard identification section: 
  - Answer "True" or "False" for the status of the traffic management plan in the "Traffic Management Plan in Place" field.
  - Identify if confined space entry is necessary and select the correct option in the "Confined Space Entry Required" field.
  - Confirm if underground services have been verified or if there are any pending checks in the "Dial-Before-You-Dig or Underground Services Verified" field.
  - Select the weather conditions in the "Weather Conditions Assessment" field according to your observations.
- Proceed to the equipment and PPE section: 
  - Answer "Pass" or "Fail" for the inspection of the winch and hauling equipment in the "Winch and Hauling Equipment Inspected" field.
  - Confirm if two-way communication is functional between the hauling and feeding ends in the "Two-way Communication Functional" field.
  - Identify if all personnel are using the required PPE by selecting the correct option in the "All Personnel Wearing Required PPE" field.
- Lastly, assess the risk and mitigation: 
  - Select the residual risk rating based on your assessment in the "Residual Risk Rating" field.
  - Provide a description of any additional controls implemented in the "Description of Additional Controls Implemented" field if applicable.
  - Finally, confirm if the site is safe to commence work in the "I certify the site is safe to commence work" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project ID or Number** (`Project ID/Number`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the project ID or number assigned by your organization.
* **Work Site Location** (`Work Site Location`, `text`, required): 
  Enter the exact location of the job site where cable hauling operations will take place.
* **Crew Lead / Supervisor** (`Crew Lead/Supervisor`, `text`, required): 
  Identify the crew leader or supervisor in charge of the project.
* **Date of Assessment** (`Date of Assessment`, `date`, required): 
  Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Traffic Management Plan in Place** (`Traffic Management Plan in Place`, `select_one`, required): 
  Confirm if the traffic management plan is in place.
* **Confined Space Entry Required** (`Confined Space Entry Required`, `select_one`, required): 
  Identify if confined space entry is necessary.
* **Dial-Before-You-Dig or Underground Services Verified** (`Dial-Before-You-Dig or Underground Services Verified`, `select_one`, required): 
  Confirm the status of underground services checks.
* **Weather Conditions Assessment** (`Weather Conditions Assessment`, `select_one`, required): 
  Assess the weather conditions on site.
* **Winch and Hauling Equipment Inspected** (`Winch and Hauling Equipment Inspected`, `select_one`, required): 
  Confirm if the winch and hauling equipment has been inspected.
* **Two-way Communication Functional** (`Two-way Communication Functional`, `select_one`, required): 
  Confirm if two-way communication is functional between the hauling and feeding ends.
* **All Personnel Wearing Required PPE** (`All Personnel Wearing Required PPE`, `select_one`, required): 
  Confirm if all personnel are using the required PPE.
* **Residual Risk Rating** (`Residual Risk Rating`, `select_one`, required): 
  Select the residual risk rating based on your assessment.
* **Description of Additional Controls Implemented** (`Description of Additional Controls Implemented`, `text`, optional): 
  Provide a description of any additional controls implemented.
* **I certify the site is safe to commence work** (`I certify the site is safe to commence work`, `select_one`, required): 
  Confirm if the site is safe to commence work.
