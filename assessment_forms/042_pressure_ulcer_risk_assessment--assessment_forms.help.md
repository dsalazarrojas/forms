# Pressure Ulcer Risk Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a patient's risk of developing pressure ulcers. The goal of this form is to gather information about the patient's medical history, potential risk factors, and clinical goals to help healthcare providers evaluate their risk of developing pressure ulcers and create personalized plans for prevention and care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill in the Patient Information section with the patient's details.
*   Select all applicable risk factors from the Risk Factors section.
*   Provide a description of the patient's observation for Pressure Injury Observation.
*   Enter any relevant medical history in the Medical History section.
*   Choose the patient's risk score from the options provided.
*   Choose the clinical risk score from the options provided.
*   Describe the prevention measures that will be taken for the patient.
*   Outline the prevention planning for the patient.
*   State the patient's goals.
*   State the clinical goals for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Information**: This section is where you will enter the patient's basic information such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
*   **Risk Factors**: This section lists potential risk factors for developing pressure ulcers. Select all the risk factors that apply to the patient to indicate their relevance.
*   **Pressure Injury Observation**: This section is for describing any observations or concerns related to pressure injury.
*   **Medical History**: This section is for recording any relevant medical history of the patient.
*   **Patient Risk Score**: Choose the risk score that best describes the patient's level of risk for developing pressure ulcers.
*   **Clinical Risk Score**: Choose the clinical risk score that best describes the patient's level of clinical risk.
*   **Prevention Measures**: Describe the measures that will be taken to prevent pressure ulcers.
*   **Prevention Planning**: Outline the specific actions that will be taken to prevent pressure ulcers.
*   **Patient Goals**: State the patient's personal goals for treatment and care.
*   **Clinical Goals**: State the clinical goals for the patient's care and treatment.
