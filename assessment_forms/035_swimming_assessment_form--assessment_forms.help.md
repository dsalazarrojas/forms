# Swimming Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The swimming assessment form is used to collect information about an individual's swimming experience, abilities, and medical status in order to assess their eligibility for a swim program or event. This form is typically completed by individuals applying for the program or event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information:
    * First Name: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
    * Last Name: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
    * Birthday: Enter your birth date in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
    * Email: Enter your email address.
    * Phone: Enter your phone number.
2.  Choose the swim event(s) that you are participating in.
3.  Provide medical information:
    * Medical Condition: Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
4.  Answer questions about swimming habits:
    * Swim Event: Select the type of swim event you are participating in.
    * Strokes: Select the stroke you use most often.
    * Swimming Frequency: Select how often you swim.
    * Swimming Distance: Enter the distance you swim most often.
5.  Choose your swim style and water surface:
    * Swimming Style: Select the type of swim style you use most often.
    * Water Surface: Select the surface you prefer for your swim training.
6.  Provide assessment results:
    * Assessment Results: Select any of the assessment options provided.
    * Assessment Outcome: Select the outcome of your assessment.
7.  Add additional comments:
    * Assessment Comments: Enter any comments about your assessment.
8.  Add contact information:
    * Emergency Contact Name: Enter your emergency contact person's name.
    * Emergency Contact Phone: Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
    * Emergency Contact Email: Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
9.  Provide testimonial:
    * Swim Testimonial: Enter a testimonial about your swimming experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birthday** (3, date, required): Enter your birth date in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Swim Event** (4, select_multiple, required): Select the type of swim event you are participating in.
* **Date of Assessment** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Age** (6, number, required): Enter your age.
* **Height** (7, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight** (8, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Type of Swim** (9, select_one, required): Select the type of swim you are doing most often.
* **Strokes** (10, select_multiple, required): Select the strokes you use most often.
* **Assessment Results** (11, select_multiple, required): Select any of the assessment options provided.
* **Assessment Outcome** (12, select_one, required): Select the outcome of your assessment.
* **Assessment Comments** (13, text, required): Enter any comments about your assessment.
* **Email** (14, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (15, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (16, text, required): Enter your emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (17, text, required): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (18, email, required): Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
* **Medical Condition** (19, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Swimming Frequency** (20, select_multiple, required): Select how often you swim.
* **Swimming Distance** (21, text, required): Enter the distance you swim most often.
* **Swimming Style** (22, select_multiple, required): Select the type of swim style you use most often.
* **What kind of water surface** (23, select_multiple, required): Select the surface you prefer for your swim training.
* **Assessment Status** (24, select_one, required): Select the status of your assessment.
* **Testimonial** (25, text, required): Enter a testimonial about your swimming experience.
