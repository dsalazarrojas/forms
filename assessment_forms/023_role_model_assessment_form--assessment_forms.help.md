# Role Model Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Role Model Assessment Form is designed to evaluate a leader's qualities, skills, and traits from the perspective of someone who has worked with or observed them. This form will help you gather specific feedback and insights about the leader's behavior, communication style, and ability to lead.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and answer them based on your actual experience or observation.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
3. Select the options that best fit your perception of the leader's characteristics.
4. You can choose to skip any questions that you feel are not relevant or do not apply to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro Question 1: What motivates you to work with a particular leader?** This question is an icebreaker to help you get started and focus on the qualities that drew you to this leader. Answer in as much detail as possible.
* **Intro Question 2: What characteristics do you value most in a leader?** Think about the essential qualities you look for in a leader, such as fairness, communication, or accountability. Answer with specific traits or skills you value.
* **Intro Question 3: Can you tell us more about this leader's example-setting skills?** Share an example or story about a time when the leader demonstrated their ability to set a good example.
* **Intro Question 4: Can you describe a specific example where this leader demonstrated their example-setting skills?** Choose a specific instance where you saw the leader's example-setting skills in action.
* **Intro Question 5: Can you tell us about this leader's ability to give feedback?** Reflect on a time when the leader provided you feedback or coaching.
* **Intro Question 6: How do you think this leader handles conflicts?** Consider a situation where the leader handled a difficult or contentious issue.
* **Intro Question 7: Can you describe a situation where this leader had to make a difficult decision?** Share an example of when the leader had to make a challenging decision.
* **Intro Question 8: How does this leader handle stress and pressure?** Think about how the leader managed their own stress and pressure in challenging situations.
* **Intro Question 9: How does this leader balance their own priorities with the priorities of the team or organization?** Reflect on how the leader managed their own priorities in relation to the team's or organization's goals.
* **Intro Question 10: Can you tell us about this leader's sense of humor and ability to have fun?** Think about how the leader's personality and humor contributed to the team's morale and well-being.
* **Intro Question 11: How would you rate this leader's level of approachability and willingness to listen?** Choose from the options: Very approachable, Somewhat approachable, Not very approachable, Not at all approachable.
* **Intro Question 12: Can you describe a situation where this leader was approachable?** Share a specific example of when the leader was approachable.
* **Intro Question 13: How does this leader handle mistakes and setbacks?** Think about how the leader reacted to errors or setbacks.
* **Intro Question 14: Can you describe a time when this leader helped you or a team?** Reflect on a situation where the leader provided support or assistance.
* **Intro Question 15: How would you rate this leader's level of empathy?** Choose from the options: Very empathetic, Somewhat empathetic, Not very empathetic, Not at all empathetic.
* **Intro Question 16: Can you describe a situation where this leader showed empathy?** Share an example of when the leader demonstrated empathy.
* **Intro Question 17: How would you rate this leader's ability to give feedback and coaching?** Choose from the options: Very effective, Somewhat effective, Not very effective, Not at all effective.
* **Intro Question 18: Can you describe a time when the leader gave you feedback or coaching?** Reflect on a specific example of when the leader provided feedback or coaching.
* **Intro Question 19: How would you rate this leader's level of communication?** Choose from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Intro Question 20: Can you describe how the leader communicates their expectations?** Think about how the leader expressed their expectations and goals.
* **Intro Question 21: How would you rate this leader's level of trustworthiness?** Choose from the options: Very trustworthy, Somewhat trustworthy, Not very trustworthy, Not at all trustworthy.
* **Intro Question 22: Can you describe a situation where you trusted this leader?** Share a specific example of when you trusted the leader.
* **Intro Question 23: Would you like to work with this leader again?** Choose from the options: Very likely, Somewhat likely, Not very likely, Not at all likely.
* **Summary:** Summarize your answers and insights about the leader. Provide any additional comments or reflections you'd like to share.
