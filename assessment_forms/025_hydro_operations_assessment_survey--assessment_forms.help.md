# Hydro Operations Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hydro Operations Assessment Survey is used to assess the status and performance of hydroelectric power plants during site visits or interactions. It helps ensure data consistency and quality by providing a standardized way for field staff to document their observations and interactions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are submitting the form for a single site visit.
2. Review each field carefully to provide accurate and truthful information.
3. Enter all required fields, even if they are optional (e.g., Contact Name, Contact Email).
4. Use the provided options for select_multiple and select_one fields, or enter a custom value if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Info (2)**: Enter the name of the contact person and their email address.
*   **Site Visit (3)**: Enter a brief description of the site visit, including any notable events or issues.
*   **Hydro Operations (4)**: Select all hydroelectric power plants that were visited or interacted with.
*   **Operational Status (5)**: Select the current operational status of the plant (e.g., Running, Under Maintenance, Shutdown).
*   **Site Visit Date (6)**: Enter the date of the site visit.
*   **Comments (7)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the site visit.
*   **Contact Name (8)**: Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone (10)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Contact Notes (11)**: Enter any additional notes about the contact person.

**Tips**

*   Use proper formatting for date and phone numbers.
*   If you need to add or remove options from the drop-down menus, please contact your supervisor.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
