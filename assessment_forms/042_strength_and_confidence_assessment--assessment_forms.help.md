# Strength And Confidence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strength And Confidence Assessment form is designed to help users evaluate their level of self-assurance in various areas, providing a better understanding of their personal strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
- Fill in your responses in the provided text fields.
- For select multiple and select one options, choose the answer that best describes your self-assurance level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Self Assurance** (`self_assurance`, text, optional): A text field where you can describe your self-assurance level in your own words.
* **Strength** (`strength`, number, optional): A numerical field where you can input your self-assurance level, with no numerical scale provided (please use your best judgment).
* **Confidence** (`confidence`, number, optional): Similar to the **Strength** field, this is a numerical field where you can input your confidence level, with no numerical scale provided (please use your best judgment).
* **Self Assurance 2** (`self_assurance_2`, text, optional): Another text field for further description of your self-assurance level.
* **Self Assurance 3** (`self_assurance_3`, select_one, optional, options: High, Medium, Low): Choose the option that best describes your self-assurance level.
* **Self Assurance 4** (`self_assurance_4`, select_one, optional, options: High, Medium, Low): Another select-one option for your self-assurance level.
* **Self Assurance 5** (`self_assurance_5`, select_multiple, optional, options: High, Medium, Low): Choose multiple options that best describe your self-assurance level.
* **Self Assurance 6** (`self_assurance_6`, select_multiple, optional, options: High, Medium, Low): Another select multiple option for your self-assurance level.
* **Self Assurance 7** (`self_assurance_7`, select_multiple, optional, options: High, Medium, Low): Another select multiple option for your self-assurance level.
* **Self Assurance 8** (`self_assurance_8`, select_multiple, optional, options: High, Medium, Low): Another select multiple option for your self-assurance level.
* **Strength 2** (`strength_2`, text, optional): A text field for further description of your self-assurance level.
* **Confidence 2** (`confidence_2`, number, optional): A numerical field where you can input your confidence level, with no numerical scale provided (please use your best judgment).
* **Self Assurance 9** (`self_assurance_9`, select_multiple, optional, options: High, Medium, Low): Another select multiple option for your self-assurance level.

## Tips
- Be honest with your answers, as this form is designed to provide a genuine assessment of your self-assurance level.
- There is no right or wrong answer, the goal is to understand your self-assurance level.
- If you're unsure about any question, use your best judgment and describe your answer in the text fields.
- If you need further clarification, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
