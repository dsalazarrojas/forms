# Cardiorespiratory Fitness Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's cardiorespiratory fitness level. It collects data on resting and exercise-related physiological parameters, such as heart rate, blood pressure, and VO2 max estimate. The form is used to evaluate an individual's fitness level and provide recommendations for future assessments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's full name in the "Client Full Name" field.
2. Select the date the assessment was performed in the "Date of Assessment" field.
3. Enter the name of the fitness professional conducting the test in the "Assessor/Fitness Professional" field.
4. Measure the resting heart rate after 5 minutes of quiet rest and enter it in the "Resting Heart Rate (BPM)" field.
5. Record the resting blood pressure values (systolic and diastolic) in the "Resting Blood Pressure (Systolic)" and "Resting Blood Pressure (Diastolic)" fields.
6. Enter the estimated VO2 max value from the submaximal or maximal testing in the "VO2 Max Estimate (ml/kg/min)" field.
7. Record the heart rate recovery 1 minute after stopping exercise in the "Heart Rate Recovery (1 min)" field.
8. Select the exercise mode used for the test from the "Exercise Mode Selected" dropdown menu.
9. Rate the rate of perceived exertion (RPE) from 6 (no effort) to 20 (max effort) in the "RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)" field.
10. Select the fitness level category based on the VO2 max results in the "Fitness Level Category" dropdown menu.
11. If applicable, enter the recommended date for the next assessment or retest in the "Next Assessment/Retest Date" field.
12. Enter any observations or comments from the fitness professional in the "Assessor Comments and Summary" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name (Text)**: Enter the full name of the client being assessed.
* **Date of Assessment (Date)**: Select the date the assessment was performed.
* **Assessor/Fitness Professional (Text)**: Enter the name of the fitness professional conducting the test.
* **Resting Heart Rate (BPM) (Number)**: Measure the resting heart rate after 5 minutes of quiet rest.
* **Resting Blood Pressure (Systolic) (Number)**: Record the top number (mmHg) of the resting blood pressure.
* **Resting Blood Pressure (Diastolic) (Number)**: Record the bottom number (mmHg) of the resting blood pressure.
* **VO2 Max Estimate (ml/kg/min) (Number)**: Enter the estimated VO2 max value from submaximal or maximal testing.
* **Heart Rate Recovery (1 min) (Number)**: Record the heart rate recovery 1 minute after stopping exercise.
* **Exercise Mode Selected (Select One)**: Choose the testing method used.
* **RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) (Number)**: Rate the RPE from 6 (no effort) to 20 (max effort).
* **Fitness Level Category (Select One)**: Select the fitness level category based on VO2 max results.
* **Next Assessment/Retest Date (Date)**: Enter the recommended date for the next assessment or retest.
* **Assessor Comments and Summary (Text)**: Enter any observations or comments from the fitness professional.
