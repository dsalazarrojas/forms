# Metamorphosis Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metamorphosis Assessment is a tool used to evaluate an individual's progress and identify areas where they need help or guidance. It is a self-assessment form that helps individuals reflect on their current state and provide insights for future improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly to the best of your ability.
2. For the "Current State" field, provide a brief description of your current situation.
3. In the "Goals" field, select all the goals that apply to your current state.
4. In the "Obstacles" field, describe any challenges or obstacles you are currently facing.
5. For "Self-Rated Progress", rate your progress based on your own self-assessment.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "Coach Assistance Needed" based on whether you feel you need guidance.
7. In "Next Steps", describe the actions you plan to take to improve your current state.
8. Finally, select all the follow-up meetings you require or would like to schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current State (1)**: This field is a text area where you can describe your current situation or state. Please provide a brief but clear answer that reflects your current status.
* **Goals (2)**: Select all the goals that apply to your current state. These goals are options for you to choose from, and by selecting them, you will help the assessor understand what you are trying to achieve.
* **Obstacles (3)**: This field is also a text area where you can describe any challenges or obstacles you are currently facing. Be as specific as possible so that the assessor can better understand your situation.
* **Self-Rated Progress (4)**: This field is a text area where you can rate your progress based on your own assessment. Use the space to describe how far you have come and how you feel about your progress.
* **Coach Assistance Needed (5)**: Select "Yes" if you feel you need guidance or coaching to help you progress further. If not, select "No".
* **Next Steps (6)**: This field is a text area where you can describe the actions you plan to take to improve your current state.
* **Follow-up Meetings (7)**: Select all the follow-up meetings you require or would like to schedule. This will help the assessor understand when you would like to meet again to review your progress.
