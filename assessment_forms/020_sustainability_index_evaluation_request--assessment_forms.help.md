# Sustainability Index Evaluation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Index Evaluation Request form is designed to help us assess and evaluate your company's sustainability performance. This form is used to gather essential information about your request context, focus areas, company information, documentation, contact email, contact phone, evaluator comments, and evaluator name. By filling out this form, you'll be providing us with valuable insights to better understand your company's sustainability posture and support your evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant focus areas for your sustainability index evaluation (e.g., ESG, Environmental, Social, and Governance).
2. Provide a brief description of the context for your request (e.g., "We are seeking sustainability index evaluation for our company's environmental performance").
3. Enter your company's information, including any relevant documentation (e.g., financial reports, sustainability reports).
4. Enter your contact email and phone number for easy communication.
5. Add any additional comments or feedback from the evaluator.
6. Finally, enter the name of the evaluator who will be performing the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request context** (`request_context`, text, optional): A brief description of the context for your request.
* **Focus areas** (`focus_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the focus areas relevant to your sustainability index evaluation (e.g., ESG, Environmental, Social, and Governance).
* **Company information** (`company_info`, text, optional): Enter your company's information, such as its name and address.
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, select_one, optional): Select the type of documentation you are providing (e.g., financial reports, sustainability reports, other).
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email for easy communication.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Evaluator comments** (`evaluator_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback from the evaluator.
* **Evaluator name** (`evaluator_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the evaluator who will be performing the evaluation.

## Tips

Please note that some fields are optional, but we recommend filling out as much information as possible to ensure a thorough evaluation. Ensure you fill out the required fields and follow the submission guidelines to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
