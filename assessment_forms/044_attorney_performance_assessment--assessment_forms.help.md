# Attorney Performance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Attorney Performance Assessment form is designed to evaluate an attorney's performance within an organization. It assesses their skills, behavior, and overall performance across various areas, providing a comprehensive picture of their strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To begin, ensure you are logged in and have permission to access the form.
2.  Click on the "Start Assessment" button to begin the evaluation process.
3.  Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
4.  For each field, choose the relevant rating from the provided options (Exceptional, Strong, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory).
5.  Ensure you provide detailed comments for the "Key Strengths" and "Areas for Development" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Evaluation Date** (id: 2, required/true, date): Enter the date of this assessment to ensure accurate tracking of evaluations over time.
*   **Evaluation Period Start** (id: 3, required/true, date): Enter the beginning of the review period for this assessment.
*   **Evaluation Period End** (id: 4, required/true, date): Enter the end of the review period for this assessment.
*   **Reviewer Name** (id: 5, required/true, text): Enter the name of the evaluating partner or manager.
*   **Attorney Name** (id: 7, required/true, text): Enter the full name of the attorney being reviewed.
*   **Bar Number** (id: 8, required/true, text): Enter the state bar number for the attorney.
*   **Position Title** (id: 9, required/true, text): Enter the current role and level of the attorney.
*   **Years With Firm** (id: 10, required/false, number): If applicable, enter the number of years the attorney has been employed at the firm.
*   **Practice Areas** (id: 11, required/true, text): Select the primary areas of practice for the attorney.
*   **Legal Knowledge and Expertise** (id: 13, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's legal knowledge and expertise.
*   **Legal Research and Analysis Skills** (id: 14, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's legal research and analysis skills.
*   **Writing Quality** (id: 15, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's writing quality.
*   **Client Service and Communication** (id: 17, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's client service and communication skills.
*   **Client Satisfaction** (id: 18, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for client satisfaction based on feedback received.
*   **Professional Conduct and Ethics** (id: 19, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's professional conduct and ethics.
*   **Business Development** (id: 20, required/false, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's business development and rainmaking skills. 
*   **Case and Matter Management** (id: 21, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's case and matter management skills.
*   **Teamwork and Collaboration** (id: 22, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the attorney's teamwork and collaboration skills.
*   **Key Strengths** (id: 23, required/true, text): Enter the primary strengths demonstrated by the attorney.
*   **Areas for Development** (id: 24, required/true, text): Enter the areas where the attorney requires improvement.
*   **Overall Performance Rating** (id: 25, required/true, select_one): Choose the highest applicable rating from the provided options for the overall performance of the attorney.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and consistency when filling out the form.
- Keep the assessments focused on the attorney's performance within the organization, rather than external factors.
- Review and follow up on progress regularly to help the attorney grow and improve.
