# Mental Capacity Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mental Capacity Assessment Form is designed to help healthcare professionals evaluate a patient's mental capacity, which is a crucial aspect of patient care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:
	1. Review the patient's medical history and current mental state.
	2. Complete the Patient Identification page with the patient's demographic information.
	3. Answer the Mental State questions based on the patient's current mental state.
	4. Indicate the patient's capacity for decision-making using the Mental Capacity Determination field.
	5. Record any risks associated with the patient's mental health using the Risk Assessment field.
	6. Select the patient's mental capacity status using the Mental Capacity Status field.
	7. Record the date and time of the assessment using the Assessment Date field.
	8. Select the healthcare professional who completed the assessment using the Assessed By field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Identification (Page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, age, and date of birth to identify the individual.
* **Mental State (Page 2)**: Answer the questions about the patient's current mental state, such as their mood, behavior, and any notable symptoms.
* **Mental State Questions (Page 3)**: Select all the questions that apply to the patient's mental state.
* **Capacity Determination (Page 4)**: Indicate the patient's capacity for decision-making, with options ranging from "Full" to "None".
* **Decision Making (Page 5)**: Record any observations or comments about the patient's capacity for decision-making.
* **Risk Assessment (Page 6)**: Indicate the level of risk associated with the patient's mental health, with options ranging from "Low" to "High".
* **Mental Capacity (Page 7)**: Select the patient's mental capacity level, with options such as "Full", "Moderate", or "Limited".
* **Mental Capacity Determination (Page 8)**: Select all the factors that contribute to the patient's mental capacity, such as "Cognitive Impairment" or "Medication Side Effects".
* **Treatment Plan (Page 9)**: Record any treatment plans or interventions that may be necessary to support the patient's mental health.
* **Medical Notes (Page 10)**: Record any medical notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **Mental Capacity Status (Page 11)**: Select the patient's mental capacity status, with options such as "Full Capacity", "Limited Capacity", or "No Capacity".
* **Assessment Date (Page 12)**: Record the date and time of the assessment.
* **Assessed By (Page 13)**: Select the healthcare professional who completed the assessment.

## Tips

* Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the patient receives the best possible care.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with a colleague or supervisor.
* Please ensure that the form is completed in a timely manner to ensure that the patient receives the necessary care and support.
