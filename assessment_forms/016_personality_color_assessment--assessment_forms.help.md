# personality_color_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The personality color assessment form is designed to help you determine your personal preferences and characteristics based on color choices. This assessment is an essential part of the process to categorize yourself accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully to understand the questions and choices provided.
2. Answer each question according to your personal preferences and feelings.
3. Select the corresponding options for each question based on your choices.
4. Ensure that you answer all required fields.

- **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the colors that you personally prefer or find appealing. This is not about your favorite color in general, but rather the colors that evoke a particular feeling or mood.
