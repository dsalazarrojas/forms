# refresher_pre_test_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "refresher pre-test assessment" form is designed to gauge your current knowledge and understanding of a given topic or subject matter. It helps to evaluate your baseline level of confidence, identify areas where you need improvement, and provide a foundation for further learning and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the most accurate options for each question, taking into account your current level of understanding.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Confidence Level (1)** (`pre_test_question_1`, text, required): Please describe your current level of confidence in the subject matter. You can express your confidence as "high", "medium", or "low".
* **Years of Experience (2)** (`pre_test_question_2`, number, required): Please enter the number of years you have been working with or studying the subject matter.
* **Primary Source of Information (3)** (`pre_test_question_3`, select_one, required): Select the primary source that you rely on for information on this topic. You can choose from the options provided.
* **Frequency of Use (4)** (`pre_test_question_4`, select_multiple, required): Select how often you use or work with this topic. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Expected Outcomes (5)** (`pre_test_question_5`, text, required): Please describe the outcomes you expect to achieve or accomplish related to this topic.
* **Expected Skills or Knowledge (6)** (`pre_test_question_6`, text, required): List the skills or knowledge you expect to acquire or improve related to this topic.
* **Hours of Instruction Needed (7)** (`pre_test_question_7`, number, required): Please enter the number of hours you estimate you need to complete the instruction or training.
* **Expected Date of Completion (8)** (`pre_test_question_8`, date, required): Please enter the date you expect to complete the instruction or training in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Expected Completion Status (9)** (`pre_test_question_9`, select_one, required): Select the status you expect to achieve upon completion.
* **Expected Date of Completion Status (10)** (`pre_test_question_10`, date, required): Please enter the date you expect to complete the instruction or training in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Pre Test Question 11 (11)** (`pre_test_question_11`, select_multiple, required): Please select the skills or knowledge you expect to acquire or improve related to this topic.
* **Expected Duration (12)** (`pre_test_question_12`, number, required): Please enter the number of weeks or months you estimate you need to complete the instruction or training.
* **Expected Budget (13)** (`pre_test_question_13`, number, required): Please enter the budget in dollars you expect to spend on the instruction or training.
* **Expected Location (14)** (`pre_test_question_14`, text, required): Please describe the location where you expect to complete the instruction or training.
* **Expected Start Date (15)** (`pre_test_question_15`, date, required): Please enter the date you expect to start the instruction or training in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Expected Completion Date (16)** (`pre_test_question_16`, date, required): Please enter the date you expect to complete the instruction or training in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Expected Method of Completion (17)** (`pre_test_question_17`, select_multiple, required): Please select the method you expect to use to complete the instruction or training.
* **Expected Outcome (18)** (`pre_test_question_18`, select_one, required): Please select the outcome you expect to achieve upon completion.
