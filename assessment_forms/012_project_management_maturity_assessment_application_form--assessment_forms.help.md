# Project Management Maturity Assessment Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the project management maturity of an organization. It helps to evaluate the current state of project management practices, identify areas of improvement, and provide recommendations for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most appropriate response to each question based on your organization's current state.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields.
