# Station Stock Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the current state of a station's stock.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you are filling it out for the correct station.
2. Enter the station's name in the "Station name" field.
3. Enter the item ID in the "Item id" field.
4. Enter the item name in the "Item name" field.
5. Enter the current quantity of the item in the "Current quantity" field.
6. Enter the low stock level for the item in the "Low stock level" field.
7. Check "Yes" or "No" for "Damaged item" field if the item is damaged.
8. Enter any additional comments about the item's condition in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Station name** (`station_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the station being assessed.
* **Item id** (`item_id`, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the item being assessed.
* **Item name** (`item_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the item being assessed.
* **Current quantity** (`current_quantity`, number, required): Enter the current quantity of the item.
* **Low stock level** (`low_stock_level`, number, required): Enter the low stock level for the item.
* **Damaged item** (`damaged_item`, select_one, required): Check "Yes" if the item is damaged, "No" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the item's condition.

## Tips
* Ensure you're filling this form for a specific station only once.
* Be as accurate as possible when entering the current quantity and low stock level.
* Clearly state whether an item is damaged to help with future decision-making.
