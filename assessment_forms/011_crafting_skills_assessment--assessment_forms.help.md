# Crafting Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crafting Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's crafting skills, experience, and completion of a crafting-related activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by reading the form instructions and understanding the purpose.
2.  Answer each question according to your experience and knowledge.
3.  Provide detailed answers to the text fields (Participant Info, Craft Name, Skill Experience, Review Sample Work, Instructor Feedback, Additional Comments).
4.  Select the appropriate response for the select-one field (Participant Level).
5.  Select all relevant options for the select_multiple field (Class/Program Placement).
6.  Enter dates and times accurately in the Date Completed and Time Completed fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Introduction**: A brief welcome and introduction to the form. It is an optional note field.
*   **Participant Info**: A text field where you can provide information about yourself, such as your name, role, or any relevant details.
*   **Craft Name**: A text field to input the name of your craft.
*   **Skill Experience**: A text field to describe your experience and skill level in the craft.
*   **Review Sample Work**: A text field to provide an example or sample of your work, such as a picture or description.
*   **Participant Level**: A select-one field to choose your level of experience or proficiency in the craft.
	+ Options:
		- '1': First-level experience or beginner.
		- '2': Intermediate experience or skill level.
		- '3': Advanced experience or skill level.
*   **Class/Program Placement**: A select_multiple field to choose all relevant classes or programs you've participated in.
	+ Options:
		- Class 1.
		- Class 2.
		- Class 3.
		- Class 4.
		- Class 5.
*   **Date Completed**: A date field to record the date you completed a crafting-related activity.
*   **Time Completed**: A time field to record the time it took to complete the activity.
*   **Instructor Feedback**: A text field to provide feedback or comments from an instructor, if applicable.
*   **Additional Comments**: A text field for any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Provide detailed answers for text fields.
- Select all relevant options for select_multiple fields.
- Enter dates and times accurately for the Date Completed and Time Completed fields.
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx.
