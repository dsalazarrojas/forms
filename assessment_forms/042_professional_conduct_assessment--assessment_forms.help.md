# Professional Conduct Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and record incidents or issues within the organization, providing a structured way to collect information and assess the situation. It is used for internal purposes, such as HR or personnel assessments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Review each section carefully and provide accurate information.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Professional Conduct Assessment** (`2`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether the incident occurred or not.
* **Professional Conduct Description** (`3`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Date of Incident** (`4`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident** (`5`, `time`, optional): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location of Incident** (`6`, `text`, optional): Describe the location where the incident occurred.
* **Employee Name** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the employee involved.
* **Department** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): Select the department the employee belongs to.
* **Supervisor Name** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the employee's supervisor.
* **Employee Supervisor Relationship** (`10`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the nature of the employee's relationship with their supervisor.
* **Incident Description** (`11`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Action Taken** (`12`, `text`, optional): Describe any actions taken in response to the incident.
* **Recommendation** (`13`, `text`, optional): Offer any recommendations for improvement or next steps.
* **Review Date** (`14`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Reviewed By** (`15`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Title** (`16`, `text`, optional): Enter the title of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Department** (`17`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Organization** (`18`, `text`, optional): Enter the organization of the reviewer.
* **Form Completion Status** (`19`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate the status of form completion.

Note: Required fields are marked as "required: false" and are not necessarily required for completion, but their presence and accuracy are still crucial for the form's purpose.
