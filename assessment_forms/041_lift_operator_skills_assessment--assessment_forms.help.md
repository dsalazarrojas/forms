# Lift Operator Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from lift operators, including personal details, medical and safety certifications, experience, work status, and supervisory relationships. The gathered data will be used for evaluation and certification purposes, ensuring compliance with industry standards and regulations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
2. For text fields, enter your first name, last name, address, contact number, supervisor name, supervisor relationship, and any necessary training records or hours.
3. For date fields, enter the date of your medical certifications, safety equipment used, training programs completed, and any other dates required.
4. For select_multiple fields, choose your current work status, medical certifications, first aid and CPR training completion, safety equipment used, and training programs completed.
5. Review your answers carefully and submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Operator License Number** (`operator_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your lift operator license number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working as a lift operator.
* **Previous Employer(s)** (`previous_employers`, text, optional): List any previous employers.
* **Current Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, required): Choose your current work status from the options: Active, Retired, On Leave, or On Layoff.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, select_multiple, required): Select your medical certifications from the options: None, Class A, Class B, Class C.
* **First Aid and CPR Training** (`first_aids_training_completed`, select_multiple, required): Select your first aid and CPR training completion status from the options: Completed, Not Completed, N/A.
* **Safety Equipment Used** (`safety_equipment_used`, select_multiple, required): Select your safety equipment used from the options: True, False, N/A.
* **Training Programs Completed** (`training_programs_completed`, select_multiple, required): Select your training programs completed from the options: Completed, Not Completed, N/A.
* **Medical Certifications Date** (`medical_certifications_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your medical certifications.
* **Safety Equipment Used Date** (`safety_equipment_used_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started using safety equipment.
* **Training Programs Completed Date** (`training_programs_completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed training programs.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Relationship** (`supervisor_relationship`, select_multiple, required): Select your supervisor relationship from the options: Direct Supervisor, Indirect Supervisor, No Supervisor.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of your supervisor.
* **Training Records** (`training_records`, text, optional): Enter any additional training records.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, text, optional): Enter any training hours completed.
* **Training Hours Date** (`training_hours_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your training hours completion.

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all required fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to make changes, contact the form administrator for assistance.
