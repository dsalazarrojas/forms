# monthly_audit_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly_audit_assessment form is used to record and track the results of regular audits and assessments, helping to identify risks and opportunities for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, such as compliance areas, risk identification, corrective actions, audit frequency, and assigned user.
2. Select the relevant compliance areas from the options provided.
3. Provide a risk identification and corrective actions in the respective fields.
4. Choose the audit frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually).
5. If applicable, provide the audit date and start and end times.
6. Enter any comments and submit the form.
7. Optionally, select whether to send an email notification and enter the phone number and email address (if applicable).
8. Assign a user and tool (if applicable).
9. Select the status of the assessment (e.g., new, in progress, completed).
10. Choose who completed the assessment and select the completed date and time (if applicable).
11. Enter comments for completion and provide a review status and comments (if applicable).
12. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Compliance Areas**: Select the areas of compliance that were assessed.
* **Risk Identification**: Provide a description of the risk identified.
* **Corrective Actions**: Select the corrective actions to be taken.
* **Audit Frequency**: Select the frequency of the audit (e.g., monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **Audit Date**: Enter the date of the audit (if applicable).
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the audit (if applicable).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the audit (if applicable).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **Email Notification**: Select whether to send an email notification (if applicable).
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number (if applicable).
* **Assigned User**: Select the user assigned to the audit.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the audit (if applicable).
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the assigned date (if applicable).
* **Assigned Time**: Enter the assigned time (if applicable).
* **Status**: Select the status of the assessment (e.g., new, in progress, completed).
* **Completed By**: Select who completed the assessment.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the completion date (if applicable).
* **Completed Time**: Enter the completion time (if applicable).
* **Comments Completed**: Enter comments for completion.
* **Review**: Select whether the assessment is to be reviewed.
* **Review Comments**: Enter review comments (if applicable).
* **Reviewed By**: Select who reviewed the assessment.
* **Review Date**: Enter the review date (if applicable).

Note: The fields marked as required must be filled out. The fields marked as optional can be skipped, but it is recommended to fill out as much information as possible for better tracking and record-keeping.
