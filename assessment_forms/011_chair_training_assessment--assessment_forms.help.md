# Chair Training Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chair Training Assessment form is used to evaluate the outcomes and confidence level of users after chair safety training sessions. This form helps assessors (trainers) to identify areas of improvement and provide feedback to trainees, ensuring that they have the necessary knowledge and skills to use chairs safely and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Trainee Name** field with the full name of the person being assessed.
2. Select the **Department** (work unit or team) where the trainee is working.
3. Enter the **Assessment Date** when the assessment was conducted.
4. Rate the trainee's **Competence** level after training using the select_one options.
5. Rate the trainee's **Comfort Level** with using the chair safely using the select_one options.
6. Provide notes on **Observed Issues** if any concerns were observed during the assessment.
7. Suggest **Corrective Actions** to improve competency.
8. Enter your name as the **Trainer Name** who performed the assessment.
9. Sign the form with your **Assessment Signature** to confirm the assessment is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Trainee Name** (required): Enter the full name of the person being assessed.
*   **Trainee Name** (`trainee_name`, text, required): Provide the full name of the person assessed.

### 2. **Department** (optional): Enter the work unit or team the trainee is part of.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or team where the trainee works.

### 3. **Assessment Date** (required): Enter the date the assessment was conducted.
*   **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Provide the date the assessment took place.

### 4. **Competence** (required): Rate the trainee's overall readiness after training.
*   **Competence** (`competence_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the trainee's overall readiness after training using the options: Not competent, Partially competent, Competent.

### 5. **Comfort Level** (required): Rate the trainee's comfort level with using the chair safely.
*   **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, select_one, required): Rate the trainee's comfort level with using the chair safely using the options: Uncomfortable, Somewhat comfortable, Comfortable.

### 6. **Observed Issues** (optional): Enter any notes on observed issues during the assessment.
*   **Observed Issues** (`observed_issues`, text, optional): Provide notes on any concerns or follow-up actions needed.

### 7. **Corrective Actions** (optional): Suggest steps to improve competency.
*   **Corrective Actions** (`corrective_actions`, text, optional): Suggest steps to improve competency.

### 8. **Trainer Name** (required): Enter the name of the assessor or instructor.
*   **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the assessor or instructor.

### 9. **Assessment Signature** (required): Sign the form to confirm the assessment is accurate.
*   **Assessment Signature** (`assessment_signature`, text, required): Sign the form with your name to confirm the assessment is accurate.
