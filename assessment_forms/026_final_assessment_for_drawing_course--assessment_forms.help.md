# Final Assessment For Drawing Course - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and assessment data for students who have completed a drawing course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the student's final artwork and their overall performance in the course.
2. Answer each question in the form according to your assessment of the student's work and conduct.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (*).
4. Click "Submit" to save your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Reflections**: Please provide a brief summary of your assessment of the student's final artwork and performance in the course. This can include any notable strengths or weaknesses you observed during the course.
* **Instructor Evaluation**: Please select "Yes" if the student met the course requirements and demonstrated satisfactory understanding of the material, or "No" if the student did not meet the course requirements.
* **Final Artwork**: Please describe the student's final artwork, including any notable characteristics or areas for improvement you observed.
* **Final Drawing Course Assessment**: Please provide a brief description of your overall assessment of the student's performance in the course.
* **Student Conduct**: Please report any instances of misconduct or issues with the student's behavior during the course.
* **Final Artwork Upload**: Please upload the student's final artwork for evaluation.
* **Student Name**: Please enter the student's name as it appears on their course enrollment record.
* **Instructor Name**: Please enter your name and title as the instructor of the course.
