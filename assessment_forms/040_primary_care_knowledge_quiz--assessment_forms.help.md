## Purpose
The primary_care_knowledge_quiz form is used to assess the knowledge of primary care, a medical field that focuses on providing comprehensive care to patients with a variety of health needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and select the correct answer.
2. Answer all questions, even if you are unsure.
3. Fill out the demographic information (questions 2-4).
4. Answer the questions related to primary care knowledge (questions 5-8).
5. Enter the date and time of completion.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your answers.
