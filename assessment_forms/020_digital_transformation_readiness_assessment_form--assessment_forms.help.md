# Digital Transformation Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess a company's readiness for digital transformation, a process of changing traditional business practices to be more efficient, cost-effective, and responsive to changing market conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Read through each page carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Each page is designed to collect specific information about your company's current state and goals related to digital transformation.
3. Answer each question truthfully, providing as much detail as necessary to help us understand your company's readiness for digital transformation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, id: 1)
	+ A brief introduction to your company and its goals for digital transformation.
* **Company Information** (Page 1, id: 2)
	+ Provide information about your company, including its size, industry, and location.
* **Digital Transformation** (Page 2, id: 3)
	+ Describe your current stance on digital transformation, including any initiatives or projects currently underway.
* **Assessment Goals** (Page 2, id: 4)
	+ Outline your specific goals and objectives for digital transformation, including timelines and key performance indicators (KPIs).
* **Current Stance** (Page 3, id: 5)
	+ Explain your current approach to digital transformation, including any challenges or obstacles you're facing.
* **Leadership Support** (Page 3, id: 6)
	+ Describe the level of support you receive from leadership for digital transformation initiatives.
* **Resources** (Page 4, id: 7)
	+ List the resources available to support digital transformation, including budget, personnel, and technology.
* **Change Management** (Page 4, id: 8)
	+ Explain your current change management processes, including how you handle resistance to change.
* **Communication** (Page 5, id: 9)
	+ Describe your communication strategies for promoting digital transformation, including internal and external stakeholders.
* **Training** (Page 5, id: 10)
	+ Outline your employee training and development plans for digital transformation.
* **Metrics** (Page 6, id: 11)
	+ Explain the metrics you use to measure progress towards digital transformation, including KPIs and dashboards.
* **Technology Stack** (Page 6, id: 12)
	+ List the technologies and tools used to support digital transformation.
* **Innovation Capacity** (Page 7, id: 13)
	+ Assess your company's innovation capacity, including its ability to adapt to changing market conditions.
* **Innovation Adoption** (Page 7, id: 14)
	+ Describe your approach to innovation adoption, including how you encourage experimentation and learning from failure.

## Tips

* Answer each question truthfully and as thoroughly as possible.
* Be specific with your answers, providing examples or examples of metrics, processes, or technologies.
* If you're unsure about a particular question, feel free to provide a brief explanation or example.
* Remember to review your answers carefully before submission, ensuring they accurately reflect your company's current state and goals.
