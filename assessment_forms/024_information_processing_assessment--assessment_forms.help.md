# Information Processing Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users to assess their experience with the information processing process. This assessment aims to gather data on the user's age, email address, phone number, ratings, and reflective responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct answer for each field. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field. This is a numerical field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. For "Rating 1" and "Rating 2", select one of the two options: "Good" or "Fair".
6. For "Rating 3", select one of the two options: "Good" or "Fair".
7. In the "Reflective Responses" field, enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the information processing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Age** (Required): Enter your age.
	+ This field is used to determine your demographic information.
* **Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
	+ This field is used to contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is used to contact you with further information.
* **Rating 1** (Required): Select "Good" if you have a positive experience with the information processing process, or "Fair" if it was average.
* **Rating 2** (Required): Select "Good" if you have a positive experience with the information processing process, or "Fair" if it was average.
* **Rating 3** (Required): Select "Good" if you have a positive experience with the information processing process, or "Fair" if it was average.
* **Reflective Responses** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or thoughts you have about the information processing process.
	+ This field is used to capture any additional feedback or suggestions you may have.
