# herbal_archetype_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The herbal archetype assessment form is a tool used to evaluate your overall health and wellness state.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Begin on the Intro Page where you will be introduced to the assessment.
2. Move on to the Questions Page, where you will answer questions about your health status, energy levels, and other relevant factors.
3. Continue to the Health Status, Energy Level, Sleep Quality, and Diet pages to provide more detailed information.
4. Answer questions on the Stress Level, Exercise Frequency, Mindfulness Practices, and Self-Care Routine pages.
5. Finally, submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Page**: This page introduces you to the herbal archetype assessment form.
* **Questions Page** (Select One):
  * Questions Page: Answer "Yes" or "No" to the following question to assess your current health and wellness state.
* **Health Status** (Select Multiple):
  * Health Status: Choose your current health status: Excellent, Fair, Poor.
* **Energy Level** (Number):
  * Energy Level: How would you rate your energy level on a scale.
* **Sleep Quality** (Number):
  * Sleep Quality: How many hours of sleep do you get per night? (Note: This field is not explicitly marked with a unit, it's assumed to be hours.)
* **Diet** (Select Multiple):
  * Diet: Choose your current diet: Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan, Flexitarian.
* **Stress Level** (Select One):
  * Stress Level: Choose your current stress level: Low, Medium, High.
* **Exercise Frequency** (Number):
  * Exercise Frequency: How often do you exercise per week? (Note: This field is not explicitly marked with a unit, it's assumed to be weekly frequency, e.g., 1-7 times.)
* **Mindfulness Practices** (Select Multiple):
  * Mindfulness Practices: Choose how often you practice mindfulness: Daily, Weekly, Monthly.
* **Self-Care Routine** (Select One):
  * Self-Care Routine: Do you have a regular self-care routine: Yes, No.

Note: The provided form structure and labels are followed as is, without modifications.
