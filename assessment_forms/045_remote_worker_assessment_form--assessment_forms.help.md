# Remote Worker Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for remote workers to provide their assessment and evaluation of their work performance, hours worked, and other related information for internal HR purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the remote worker.
2. Enter the date you were hired.
3. Provide the number of hours you've worked.
4. Enter your salary.
5. Enter your job title.
6. Mention your location.
7. Evaluate your performance as one of the following: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
8. Add any comments or feedback you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Remote Worker Name** (`remote_worker_name`, text, optional)
Enter your name as the remote worker.

### 2. **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of your supervisor (if applicable).

### 3. **Date Hired** (`date_hired`, date, optional)
Enter the date you were hired in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### 4. **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional)
Enter the total number of hours you've worked.

### 5. **Salary** (`salary`, number, optional)
Enter your current salary.

### 6. **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional)
Enter your job title or role.

### 7. **Location** (`location`, text, optional)
Enter the location where you work.

### 8. **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, select_one, optional)
Evaluate your performance as:
* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Poor

### 9. **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional)
Add any comments or feedback you'd like to provide.
