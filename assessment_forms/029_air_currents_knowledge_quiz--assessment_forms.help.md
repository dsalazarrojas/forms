# Air Currents Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Air Currents Knowledge Quiz is a tool used to assess a person's knowledge on air currents and related topics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all the questions to the best of your knowledge.
2. For question 2, 3, and 4, select the best answer from the available options.
3. For question 5, choose the correct effect of wind on pollination.
4. You can optionally report your estimated score after completing the quiz.
5. If you have any feedback or comments, please use the text field for comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name or identifier for tracking purposes.
* **What causes local air currents** (`question_1`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best answer. Options: Temperature differences, Moon phases, Soil type, None.
* **Effect of urban heat islands on air movement** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required): Pick the correct effect. Options: Stronger updrafts, No effect, Reduce wind, Increase humidity only.
* **Recognize sea breeze timing** (`question_3`, `select_one`, required): Select typical timing. Options: Afternoon, Early morning, Midnight, Dawn.
* **Importance of wind for pollination** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required): Correct role of wind. Options: Distributes pollen, Blocks sunlight, Increases pests, Decreases soil fertility.
* **Estimated score** (`score`, `number`, optional): Optionally report your self-assessed score.
* **Feedback about the quiz** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Use this field to provide comments or suggestions about the quiz.

## Tips
* Be honest in your answers, as the quiz relies on your knowledge.
* Make sure to choose only one option per question.
* If you're not sure about an answer, choose the option that seems most plausible.
* This quiz is meant to be a self-assessment tool, so take your time and answer carefully.
