# Social Dynamics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Dynamics Assessment form is a tool used to evaluate the dynamics and relationships within a group or team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Group Communication" section with a brief description of how members of your group or team interact with each other.
2.  In the "Group Roles" section, select the roles that each person in your group or team plays.
3.  In the "Conflict Patterns" section, describe any patterns of conflict or tension that you have observed within your group or team.
