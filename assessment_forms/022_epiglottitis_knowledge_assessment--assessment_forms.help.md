# Epiglottitis Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Epiglottitis Knowledge Assessment form is designed to help healthcare professionals evaluate their knowledge and confidence in managing patients with epiglottitis. This form assesses clinical knowledge and confidence in various aspects of care, including diagnosis, treatment, and patient management. It helps healthcare providers identify areas where they need additional education or training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. For questions that are not applicable or not relevant to your experience, please mark "No" or leave them blank.
3. Take as much time as you need to complete the form, and do not rush.
