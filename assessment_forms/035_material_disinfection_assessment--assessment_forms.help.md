# Material Disinfection Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the effectiveness of material disinfection in a given facility. It captures information about the facility's name, the date of evaluation, and various other relevant details such as ratings and follow-up information. The form is used to track and record assessment data, enabling facilities to evaluate their disinfection protocols and make necessary improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the facility's name.
2. Select the date of evaluation.
3. Fill out the ratings for the material disinfection assessment. (Select 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 for each rating.)
4. If necessary, provide follow-up information, including the follow-up date and status.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **facility_name**: Enter the name of the facility being assessed.
* **evaluated_date**: Select the date on which the assessment was performed.
* **rating_1**: Rate the effectiveness of the material disinfection on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **follow_up_action**: Describe the action taken for follow-up, if any.
* **follow_up_date**: Select the date of the next follow-up, if any.
* **follow_up_status**: If a follow-up is not needed, select "No Follow-up Needed". Otherwise, select "Completed" or "Due Soon" to indicate the status of the follow-up.
* **note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the assessment.
* **email**: If you have an email address for the facility, enter it here (optional).
* **phone_number**: If you have a phone number for the facility, enter it here (optional).
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee responsible for the assessment (optional).
* **employee_title**: Enter the title or position of the employee responsible for the assessment (optional).
* **employee_department**: Enter the department of the employee responsible for the assessment (optional).
* **team_member**: Enter the team member involved in the assessment (optional).
* **facility_address**: Enter the address of the facility (optional).
* **facility_phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility (optional).
* **facility_notes**: Enter any additional notes about the facility (optional).
* **follow_up_notes**: Enter any follow-up notes or comments (optional).
* **rating_2**: Rate the effectiveness of the cleaning protocols on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
* **rating_3**: Rate the effectiveness of the disinfectant usage on a scale of 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent).
