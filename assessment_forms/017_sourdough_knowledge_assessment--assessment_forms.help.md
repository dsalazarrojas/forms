# Sourdough Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's knowledge and familiarity with sourdough, a type of bread dough that requires careful preparation and handling. The form is intended to gauge the respondent's understanding of various aspects of sourdough, including their experience, knowledge areas, learning needs, and baking skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, choosing the best option that reflects your understanding and experience with sourdough.
2. Select all applicable options for questions that allow multiple responses.
3. Ensure that you complete all required questions (marked as "Required: true") before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`title`, `text`, Required: false): This is a brief introductory question where you can provide any relevant information about your background or experience with sourdough. Please answer this question as a brief statement.
* **Sourdough Experience** (`sourdough_experience`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Indicate your level of experience with sourdough. Select all options that apply to you:
	+ 'Yes' - You have experience with sourdough.
	+ 'No' - You do not have experience with sourdough.
* **Knowledge Areas** (`knowledge_areas`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Choose all areas of knowledge you have related to sourdough:
	+ 'Yes' - You are familiar with this area.
	+ 'No' - You are not familiar with this area.
* **Learning Needs** (`learning_needs`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Indicate your areas of learning need related to sourdough. Select all options that apply:
	+ 'Yes' - You need to learn about this area.
	+ 'No' - You do not need to learn about this area.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, `select_one`, Required: false): Select the evaluation scale that best represents your understanding of sourdough:
	+ 'Yes' - You are very familiar with sourdough.
	+ 'No' - You are not very familiar with sourdough.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Indicate the details you are submitting related to sourdough. Select all options that apply:
	+ 'Yes' - You are submitting this information.
	+ 'No' - You are not submitting this information.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, Required: false): Provide any additional comments or questions you have about sourdough.
* **Sourdough Knowledge** (`sourdough_knowledge`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Choose all areas of sourdough knowledge you possess:
	+ 'Yes' - You have knowledge about this area.
	+ 'No' - You do not have knowledge about this area.
* **Baking Skills** (`baking_skills`, `select_multiple`, Required: false): Select all baking skills you possess related to sourdough:
	+ 'Yes' - You have this baking skill.
	+ 'No' - You do not have this baking skill.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `date`, Required: true): Please select the date you submitted this form (or leave blank for current date).

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest when answering each question.
- Take your time to review each question carefully before selecting your response.
- If you are unsure about any question, it is okay to ask for clarification or guidance.
- Remember to complete all required questions before submitting the form.
