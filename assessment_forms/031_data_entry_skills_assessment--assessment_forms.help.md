# Data Entry Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your data entry skills. It consists of several questions and fields that assess your ability to accurately and quickly complete data entry tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the fields on each page as instructed.
2. Enter your answers in the provided text boxes or select the corresponding options.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page** (`first_page`, Text, Optional): Fill out this field with any information you'd like to provide.
* **Speed Test** (`speed_test`, Number, Optional): Enter a number to represent your speed in data entry tasks.
* **Accuracy Test** (`accuracy_test`, Number, Optional): Enter a number to represent your accuracy in data entry tasks.
* **Speed Results** (`speed_results`, Text, Optional): Describe your speed in data entry tasks.
* **Accuracy Results** (`accuracy_results`, Text, Optional): Describe your accuracy in data entry tasks.
* **Assessment Results** (`assessment_results`, Text, Optional): Provide a general description of your assessment results.
* **Assessment Results Comment** (`assessment_results_comment`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or details about your assessment results.
* **Speed Score** (`speed_score`, Number, Optional): Enter a number representing your speed score.
* **Accuracy Score** (`accuracy_score`, Number, Optional): Enter a number representing your accuracy score.
* **Assessment Comment** (`assessment_comment`, Text, Required): This is a required field. Enter your comment on the assessment results.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Assessment Results** (`assessment_results`, Text, Optional): This field is not clearly labeled, so it's assumed to be a field for entering the results of the assessment.

Note: The required field "Assessment Comment" is a mandatory field and must be filled out. The other fields are optional and can be filled out or left blank.
