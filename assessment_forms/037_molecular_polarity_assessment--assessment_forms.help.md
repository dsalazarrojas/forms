# Molecular Polarity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Molecular Polarity Assessment form is designed to gather information about a molecule's properties. This form will help researchers and scientists assess the molecular polarity of a molecule.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked with an *.
2. For text fields, type in a clear and concise manner.
3. For date and time fields, format the input according to the required date and time format.
4. For selection fields, choose all applicable options that apply to the molecule.
5. Click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact information** (question8, text, required: false): Please enter your name and institution to provide contact information.
* **Note field** (question9, text, required: false): This field is for any additional notes or comments about the molecule.
* **Molecular weight** (question10, number, required: true): Enter the molecular weight of the molecule.
* **Time** (question11, time, required: true): When did you first discover this molecule? Please format the date and time accordingly.
* **Nonpolar** (question12, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all applicable types of nonpolar molecules that apply to the molecule.
	+ Hydrocarbon (C-C)
	+ Halogen
	+ Sulfuric
* **Polar** (question13, select_multiple, required: true): Choose all applicable types of polar molecules that apply to the molecule.
	+ Hydroxyl (OH)
	+ Alkyl (R-group)
	+ Amino (-NH2)
* **Email** (question14, email, required: true): Enter your email address.
* **Question15** (question15, text, required: false): This field is for any additional notes or comments about the molecule.
* **Molecular weight** (question16, number, required: true): Enter the molecular weight of the molecule.
* **Time** (question17, time, required: true): When did you first discover this molecule? Please format the date and time accordingly.
