# English Grammar And Vocabulary Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the English language skills of learners, covering grammar and vocabulary. It is meant to collect information from users about their learner details, English grammar level, vocabulary level, assessment date, submission time, note, email address, phone number, and school name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Learner Information** section with your details.
2. Select the level of your English **Grammar** skills.
3. Choose the level of your **Vocabulary** skills.
4. Enter the date you completed this **Assessment Date** in the given format.
5. Indicate the time you submitted this form in the **Submission Time** field.
6. Provide any additional comments or notes in the **Note** field.
7. Enter your **Email Address** for correspondence.
8. Type in your **Phone Number** for quick contact.
9. Enter the name of your **School Name**, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner Information**: Please provide your personal details, including your name or identifier.
* **English Grammar**: Select the level of your English grammar skills, ranging from 1 to 10.
* **Vocabulary**: Choose the level of your English vocabulary, also ranging from 1 to 10.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you completed this assessment in the format DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 25/02/2024).
* **Submission Time**: Indicate the time you submitted this form in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes you want to be considered.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for correspondence and communication.
* **Phone Number**: Type in your phone number for easy contact.
* **School Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your school or institution.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
- Ensure all date and time fields are in the correct format.
