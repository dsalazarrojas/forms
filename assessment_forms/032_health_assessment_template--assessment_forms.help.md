# health_assessment_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a health assessment template used by healthcare professionals to collect information about a patient's health. It's essential to complete this form accurately and honestly to help healthcare providers make informed decisions about your care.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field and its label before proceeding.
- Fill in your information accurately and completely.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask the healthcare professional assisting you for clarification.
- Complete as many fields as possible to provide a comprehensive view of your health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Patient Information** (Patient Information, Text, Optional)
- This field is for you to provide your name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.

### 2. **Medical History** (Medical History, Text, Required)
- Please provide a brief description of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or conditions that may impact your health.

### 3. **Meds** (Meds, Select Multiple, Optional)
- Check 'Yes' if you take any prescription medications or have any medical devices implanted or used.
- Check 'No' if you do not take any prescription medications or have any medical devices.

### 4. **Allergies** (Allergies, Select Multiple, Optional)
- Check 'Yes' if you have any allergies or sensitivities to foods, substances, or environments.
- Check 'No' if you do not have any allergies or sensitivities.

### 5. **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, Select Multiple, Optional)
- Check 'Yes' if you have any chronic or acute medical conditions, such as diabetes or a cold.
- Check 'No' if you do not have any medical conditions.

### 6. **Medical Conditions List** (Medical Conditions List, Text, Optional)
- Provide a list of any medical conditions you have, if not listed in the previous field.

### 7. **Health Assessment** (Health Assessment, Select One, Optional)
- Check 'Yes' if you're willing to participate in a health assessment.
- Check 'No' if you're not willing or would rather not participate.

### 8. **Assessment Results** (Assessment Results, Select Multiple, Optional)
- If you've undergone a health assessment, select the outcome (e.g., 'Yes', 'No', or 'Partial').
- If you have not undergone a health assessment, select 'Not applicable'.

# Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Ask the healthcare professional assisting you if you're unsure or have questions.
- Completing this form will aid in providing you with better care and treatment.
