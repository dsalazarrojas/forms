# Barber Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Barber Skills Assessment form, which is used to evaluate a barber's performance and skills in a professional setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you are the designated assessor/evaluator for the barber being assessed.
* Review the form and understand what each field represents.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately, using your observations and assessment of the barber's skills and performance.
