# Linking Words Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the use of linking words in a given text. Linking words are words or phrases that connect ideas, concepts, or thoughts within a piece of writing. This form helps you to evaluate and identify the correct and incorrect use of linking words in a text.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the text for "What are linking words" (Page 1) to get an idea of the linking words used in the text.
2. Select the correct linking words from the list "transition_types" (Page 2).
3. Identify the linking words that are used correctly (Page 3) and incorrectly (Pages 4-6) using the "correct_linking_words" and "transition_words_used" options.
4. Provide additional information about the linking words used correctly and incorrectly using the "linking_words_used_other" and "linking_words_used_incorrectly_other" options (Pages 7-12).
5. Enter any other linking words that are not listed in the options (Pages 13-15).
6. Confirm your answers and submit the form.
