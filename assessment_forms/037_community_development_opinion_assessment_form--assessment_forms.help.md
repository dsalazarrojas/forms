# Development Opinion Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather opinions from stakeholders about a proposed development project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your respondent role from the options provided.
2. Choose the perceived level of impact the project will have on you.
3. Select any anticipated benefits you expect from the project.
4. List any main concerns you have about the development project.
5. (Optional) Provide a short summary of your overall opinion of the project.
6. If you want to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent role** (`respondent_role`, select_one, required): Select the role you play in the development project:
	+ Resident: If you are a resident in the area affected by the project.
	+ Business owner: If you own a business that will be impacted by the project.
	+ Community leader: If you are a community leader or representative.
	+ Other: If none of the above applies.
* **Perceived level of impact** (`level_of_impact`, select_one, required): Choose how much the project will affect you:
	+ High: The project will have a significant impact on me.
	+ Moderate: The project will have a moderate impact on me.
	+ Low: The project will have a minimal impact on me.
	+ None: The project will have no impact on me.
* **Anticipated benefits** (`anticipated_benefits`, select_multiple, required): Select any benefits you expect from the project:
	+ Job creation: The project will create new job opportunities.
	+ Improved infrastructure: The project will improve local infrastructure.
	+ More housing: The project will result in more housing options.
	+ Increased services: The project will provide increased access to services.
* **Main concerns** (`main_concerns`, select_multiple, required): List any concerns you have about the development project:
	+ Displacement: The project may displace existing residents or businesses.
	+ Traffic congestion: The project may increase traffic congestion.
	+ Environmental impact: The project may have a negative environmental impact.
	+ Noise or disruption: The project may cause noise or disruption.
* **Overall opinion** (`overall_opinion`, text, optional): Provide a short summary of your overall opinion of the project.
* **Contact for follow-up** (`contact_for_follow_up`, email, optional): If you want to be contacted for follow-up, provide your email address.
