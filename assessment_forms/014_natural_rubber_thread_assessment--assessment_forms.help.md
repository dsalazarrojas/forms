# Natural Rubber Thread Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess the condition and quality of natural rubber thread. It is intended to capture detailed information about the thread's properties, performance, and potential defects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
2. Provide clear and concise answers to each question.
3. Ensure that your answers accurately reflect the actual condition of the natural rubber thread.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ratings** (`ratings_form`): Rate the thread's quality and performance on a scale. Your answer should be a numerical value.
* **Defect Check** (`defect_check_form`): Check if the thread has any defects or flaws. Select 'True' if a defect is present, or 'False' otherwise.
* **Defect Description** (`defect_description`): Describe the defect(s) you detected in the thread, if any.
* **Signed Evaluation** (`signed_evaluation`): Provide a signed evaluation of the thread's condition, either 'Good' or 'Bad'.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the thread's condition.
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`): Enter the date of the inspection in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Inspection** (`time_of_inspection`): Enter the time of the inspection in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter your name and role as the inspector or quality control personnel.
* **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`): Provide your signature as verification of your identity.
* **Manufacturer Name** (`manufacturer_name`): Enter the name of the thread's manufacturer.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the manufacturer or supplier.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the email address of the manufacturer or supplier.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter the name of the company or organization responsible for the thread's production or supply.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Provide any additional comments or observations about the thread's condition.

## Tips

* Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Use clear and concise language when providing answers and comments.
* If you are unsure about any field or aspect of the form, please ask for guidance or clarification.
