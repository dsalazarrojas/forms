# Verification Specialist Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Verification Specialist Assessment is a form used to assess a verification specialist's skills and knowledge. It appears to be a structured assessment with multiple fields that collect various information about the verification specialist. Each field is optional, allowing for flexibility in different user scenarios or situations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form structure and understand the purpose of each field.
2. Go through each field and provide the required information, or simply leave it blank if it's not required.
3. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Scenario-questions** (Text, Optional)
Enter a scenario that you would like the verification specialist to assess.
### 2. **Candidate-details** (Number, Optional)
Provide the candidate's details, if applicable.
### 3. **Assessment-date** (Date, Optional)
Enter the date of the assessment.
### 4. **Assessment-time** (Time, Optional)
Enter the time of the assessment.
### 5. **Scenario-screening** (Select One, Optional)
Select one of the scenario screening questions.
### 6. **Verification specialist name** (Text, Optional)
Enter the name of the verification specialist.
### 7. **Verification specialist designation** (Text, Optional)
Enter the designation of the verification specialist.
### 8. **Verification specialist phone** (Text, Optional)
Enter the phone number of the verification specialist.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information in each field.
- If you're unsure about a field or its purpose, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section for clarification.
- If a field is optional, feel free to leave it blank if it's not applicable to your situation.
