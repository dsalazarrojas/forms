# Support Staff Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess support staff performance, identify areas for improvement, and document progress toward meeting goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections to ensure they are relevant to your role and responsibilities.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information and materials required for the form.
3. Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, making sure to provide enough details to support your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a general information section where you can provide any relevant context or details about the assessment.
* **Employee Name**: Please enter the name of the employee being assessed.
* **Role**: Select the specific role of the employee (e.g., Support Staff, Other).
* **Department**: Enter the department the employee belongs to.
* **Date Hired**: Enter the date the employee was hired.
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the employee's manager.
* **Performance Issues**: Check any performance issues that the employee is currently facing (e.g., Poor Communication, Lack of Initiative, Inflexibility).
* **Training Needed**: Select any training areas that the employee needs improvement in (e.g., Communication Skills, Time Management, Problem Solving).
* **Action Planned**: Enter any actions planned to address the performance issues.
* **Next Steps**: Enter any next steps that need to be taken to address the performance issues.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the performance review.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the performance.
* **Review Completed**: Enter the date the review was completed.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the employee's performance.
* **Performance Meeting**: Enter the date of the performance meeting.
* **Performance Meeting Employees Attending**: Enter the names of employees attending the performance meeting.
* **Performance Meeting Outcome**: Enter the outcome of the performance meeting.
* **Performance Meeting Notes**: Add any additional notes about the performance meeting.
* **Follow Up Actions**: Enter any follow-up actions planned.
* **Next Action**: Select the method to follow up on the actions (e.g., Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Next Action Date**: Enter the date for the next action.
* **Next Action Due**: Enter the due date for the next action.
* **Next Action Completed**: Enter the date the next action was completed.

Note: This form is designed to help you document and track progress toward meeting goals and objectives. Please ensure that you complete it accurately and thoroughly to support effective performance assessment and improvement.
