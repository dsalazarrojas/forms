# Lead Acid Battery Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the safety of lead acid batteries in a facility. It is used to identify potential risks and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure that all fields that are marked as "required" are filled out accurately.
- Use the available options in the drop-down menus to select the correct information.
- For fields that are not marked as "required", you may choose to fill them out if you have additional information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <label> Lead Acid Battery Safety Assessment
This is the title of the form, which cannot be edited.

### <label> Facility Name
*   **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the batteries are stored or handled.
*   (Required, text)

### <label> Facility Address
*   **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility where the batteries are stored or handled.
*   (Required, text)

### <label> Battery Type
*   **Battery Type**: Select the type of battery from the available options: 12V, 6V, or Other.
*   (Required, select_one)

### <label> Training Status
*   **Training Status**: Select the current training status of personnel handling batteries from the available options: Not Started, Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, or N/A.
*   (Required, select_multiple)

### <label> Storage Location
*   **Storage Location**: Select the storage location for the batteries from the available options: Basement, Warehouse, or Other.
*   (Required, select_multiple)

### <label> Battery Handling
*   **Battery Handling**: Select how the batteries are handled from the available options: Correctly, Incorrectly, or Other.
*   (Required, select_multiple)

### <label> Observations
*   **Observations**: This field is for any additional information or observations related to the battery storage or handling practices.
*   (Optional, note)

### <label> Notes
*   **Notes**: This field is for any additional information or comments related to the assessment.
*   (Optional, note)

### <label> Facility Signature
*   **Facility Signature**: Sign and date to confirm completion of the assessment.
*   (Facility text)

### <label> Reviewer Name
*   **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
*   (Optional, text)

### <label> Reviewer Signature
*   **Reviewer Signature**: Sign and date to confirm review of the assessment.
*   (Reviewer text)
