# Story Themes Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the story themes of a narrative. It will help evaluate the presence and quality of different themes in your story.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, navigate through the 22 story themes questions, selecting the options that best suit your assessment.
- Each theme is represented by multiple or single choice questions.
- Answer the questions with the options that best match your assessment.
- You can choose only one or multiple options for each question.
