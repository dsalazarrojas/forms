# EuroPassport Eligibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EuroPassport Eligibility Assessment form is designed to evaluate the eligibility of individuals for a EuroPassport. This form helps to collect necessary information to verify the applicant's details and assess their eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the User Input section with your personal details.
2.  Select your nationality from the list in the Nationality section.
3.  Provide your Passport Type (e.g., Full, Biometric, etc.).
4.  Choose the countries you have resided in from the EU Country of Residence section.
5.  Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
6.  Fill in the Address fields (Address 1 and Address 2) with your current address, if applicable.
7.  Provide any additional Contact Info, if necessary.
8.  In the Follow-up section, indicate if you need any follow-up actions (e.g., phone call, email).
9.  Choose the method for follow-up actions and indicate the follow-up date and time, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Nationality**: Select your country of nationality from the list.
    *   Options: Irish, British, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Belgian, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Greek, Cypriot, Maltese, Cypriot (Dual), British Overseas, Other, Other (please specify)
*   **Passport Type**: Choose the type of passport you possess.
    *   Options: Full, Biometric, Biometric (with residence permit), Other, Other (please specify)
*   **EU Country of Residence**: Choose the countries you have resided in.
    *   Options: Ireland, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland, Greece, Cyprus, Malta
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the dd/mm/yyyy format.
*   **Address 1**: Provide your current address (if applicable).
*   **Address 2**: Provide any additional address information (if applicable).
*   **Contact Info**: Fill in any additional contact information (if necessary).
*   **Follow Up**: Indicate if you require any follow-up actions.
    *   Options: True, False, Not applicable
*   **Follow Up Method**: Choose the method for follow-up actions (e.g., phone call, email).
    *   Options: Phone Call, Email, Other, Other (please specify)
*   **Follow Up Date**: Choose the follow-up date.
*   **Follow Up Time**: Choose the follow-up time.
*   **Follow Up Remarks**: Provide any additional comments for follow-up actions.
*   **Follow Up Actions**: Describe the follow-up actions to be taken.
*   **Follow Up Comments**: Provide any additional comments for follow-up actions.
