# neuro_rehabilitation_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The neurorehabilitation assessment form is designed to collect comprehensive data from patients undergoing neurorehabilitation. This includes general information, medical history, treatment plans, and patient data. The form is intended to be used as a reference for healthcare providers to assess the patient's condition and develop an appropriate treatment plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information about the patient, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Report any neurological symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as numbness, weakness, or tremors.
3. Share the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
4. Outline the patient's treatment plan, including goals and objectives.
5. Enter patient data, such as vital signs and medical test results.
6. Document the patient's neurological status, including any relevant test results or examination findings.
7. Report on the patient's cognitive status, including any memory or cognitive impairments.
8. Document any results from recent neuroimaging studies (e.g., MRI or CT scans).
9. Describe the implementation of the treatment plan, including any progress or challenges.
10. Note any follow-up appointments or check-ups scheduled.
11. List any medications currently prescribed to the patient.
12. Enter any information about patient counseling or education provided.
13. Document any discharge plans or next steps for the patient.
14. Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition.
15. Finally, sign the form with the patient's signature and that of the provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (1, text, required): Enter general information about the patient, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Neurological Symptoms** (2, select_multiple, optional): Report any neurological symptoms the patient is experiencing, such as numbness, weakness, or tremors.
* **Medical History** (3, text, optional): Share the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions or treatments.
* **Treatment Plan** (4, text, optional): Outline the patient's treatment plan, including goals and objectives.
* **Patient Data** (5, text, optional): Enter patient data, such as vital signs and medical test results.
* **Neurological Status** (6, text, optional): Document the patient's neurological status, including any relevant test results or examination findings.
* **Cognitive Status** (7, text, optional): Report on the patient's cognitive status, including any memory or cognitive impairments.
* **Neurological Examination** (8, text, optional): Document any results from recent neuroimaging studies (e.g., MRI or CT scans).
* **Physical Examination** (9, text, optional): Describe the patient's physical examination, including any relevant findings.
* **Laboratory Examinations** (10, text, optional): Enter results from laboratory examinations or tests.
* **Imaging Studies** (11, text, optional): Describe the patient's imaging studies, including any relevant findings.
* **Treatment Plan Implementation** (12, text, optional): Describe the implementation of the treatment plan, including any progress or challenges.
* **Follow Up** (13, text, optional): Note any follow-up appointments or check-ups scheduled.
* **Medications** (14, text, optional): List any medications currently prescribed to the patient.
* **Patient Counseling** (15, text, optional): Enter any information about patient counseling or education provided.
* **Patient Education** (16, text, optional): Document any patient education provided.
* **Discharge Plans** (17, text, optional): Document any discharge plans or next steps for the patient.
* **Other Notes** (18, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the patient's condition.
* **Patient Signature** (19, note, optional): Sign the form with the patient's signature.
* **Provider Signature** (20, note, optional): Sign the form with the provider's signature.
