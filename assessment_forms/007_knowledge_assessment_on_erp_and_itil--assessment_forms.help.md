# knowledge_assessment_on_erp_and_itil - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to test a user's knowledge on Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) and Information Technology Infrastructure Library (ITIL) concepts.
It is intended to help identify areas where the user needs more training or education on these subjects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question or field in the form.
2. Answer each question based on your actual knowledge of ERP and ITIL.
3. For select-type questions, choose the option that best describes your answer.
4. For text or date/time fields, type your answer in the correct format.
5. Do not leave any fields blank without a valid answer.
6. Review your answers before submitting the form.
