# Suicide Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Suicide Risk Assessment Form is a self-report assessment designed to help identify potential risks and provide a basis for further evaluation and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly, without rushing through the assessment.
2.  Select the options that best apply to your situation.
3.  If you're unsure about any question, answer 'True' to indicate that you need help or support.
4.  Please review each question carefully, as the answers will be used to guide further evaluation and support.
