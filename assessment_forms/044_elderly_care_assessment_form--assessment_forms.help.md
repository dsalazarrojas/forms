# Elderly Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
 
## Purpose
The Elderly Care Assessment Form is a tool used to gather information about an elderly person's health, medical conditions, and living arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form pages below and ensure you have understood each section.
2. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any question, please ask for help or clarification.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
 
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `note`, optional):
This section is for recording any previous medical history that may be relevant to the individual's care.
 
- **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional):
This section is for listing any medications the individual is currently taking.
 
- **Physical Functioning** (`physical_functioning`, `select_one`, optional):
This section is for rating the individual's physical functioning on a scale of Very Good, Fair, or Poor.
 
- **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional):
This section is for selecting any medical conditions that the individual has, such as Arthritis, Diabetes, High Blood Pressure, Heart Disease, or Stroke.
 
- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `number`, optional):
This section is for recording the individual's contact information, including their phone number or other relevant details.
 
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `select_one`, optional):
This section is for indicating whether the individual has an emergency contact person.
 
- **Caregiver Support System** (`caregiver_support_system`, `select_multiple`, optional):
This section is for selecting the types of caregiver support systems in place, such as Formal or Informal.
 
- **Home Environment** (`home_environment`, `select_one`, optional):
This section is for indicating whether the individual lives in a home environment.
 
- **Living Arrangements** (`living_arrangements`, `select_multiple`, optional):
This section is for selecting the individual's living arrangements, such as Live with Family, Live Alone, or Live in Facility.
