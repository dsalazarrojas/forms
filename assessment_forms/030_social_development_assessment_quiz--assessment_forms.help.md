# Social Development Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Development Assessment Quiz is designed to assess the social development of students, helping educators or administrators understand their strengths and weaknesses in various areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant answer for each question.
2. Answer all questions, even if you're unsure or not sure.
3. Choose one option for select-one fields and multiple options for select-multiple fields.
4. Save your answers to review and submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assess The Student** (`assess_the_student`, select_multiple, optional): 
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student exhibits strong social skills, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Good At** (`social_skills`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student excels in social skills, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Struggling With** (`communication_skills`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" for skills the student is currently struggling with, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Emotionally Mature** (`emotional_maturity`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student exhibits emotional maturity, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Able To Collaborate** (`collaboration_skills`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Answer "Good" or "Fair" for collaboration skills you observe in the student.
* **What Is The Student Self Controlled** (`self_control`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student demonstrates self-control, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Problem Solving** (`problem_solving`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" for skills the student excels in problem-solving, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Able To Manage Time** (`time_management`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student manages time effectively, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Organized** (`organization`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" for skills the student excels in organization, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Takes Responsibility** (`responsibility`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student takes responsibility for their actions, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Adapted To Changes** (`adaptability`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student adapts to changes, and "No" otherwise.
* **What Is The Student Has Self Confidence** (`self_confidence`, select_one, optional):
	+ Answer "Yes" if you believe the student has self-confidence, and "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and objectively based on your observations and experiences with the student.
* If you're unsure or not sure, choose the option that best describes your impression.
* Complete the form as thoroughly as possible to provide a comprehensive assessment.
