# Aircraft Fuel Systems Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aircraft Fuel Systems Assessment form is used to evaluate the status of an aircraft's fuel systems. It gathers information about the assessor, the date of the assessment, and key details about the aircraft's fuel system, including any issues detected during the assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information as the assessor.
2. Enter the date of the assessment.
3. Provide the tail number or registration ID of the aircraft.
4. Describe the type of fuel system present (e.g., type of tank, fuel type, etc.).
5. Check if any visual leaks were detected during the assessment.
6. Report the condition of the fuel filter and note its last replacement date.
7. Provide notes on the performance of the fuel pump and any pressures observed.
8. Report the results of the contamination check (e.g., water or particulate presence).
9. Describe any corrective actions required to address any issues detected.
10. Summarize your final assessment and make recommendations for next steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor name** (`assessor_name`): Enter your name and contact information for reference.
* **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`): Enter the date of the assessment in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Aircraft registration** (`aircraft_registration`): Enter the tail number or registration ID of the aircraft.
* **Fuel system type** (`fuel_system_type`): Describe the type of fuel system present (e.g., type of tank, fuel type, etc.).
* **Visual leaks detected** (`visual_leaks`): Check the box if visual leaks were detected during the assessment.
* **Filter condition** (`filter_condition`): Report the condition of the fuel filter and note its last replacement date.
* **Fuel pump performance** (`pump_performance`): Provide notes on the performance of the fuel pump and any pressures observed.
* **Contamination check results** (`contamination_check`): Report the results of the contamination check (e.g., water or particulate presence).
* **Corrective actions required** (`corrective_actions`): Describe any corrective actions needed to address issues detected.
* **Final assessment and recommendations** (`final_assessment`): Summarize your final assessment and make recommendations for next steps.

## Tips
* Be thorough in your assessment and report any issues detected.
* Ensure accurate and clear reporting of conditions and performance.
* Follow up with maintenance or repairs as needed.
* Keep the form updated and accessible for future reference.
