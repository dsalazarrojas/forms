# Patient Rehabilitation Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's rehabilitation progress, allowing healthcare professionals to assess and monitor their treatment and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the `Patient Name` field.
2. Select the type of treatment the patient is undergoing (`Treatment Type` field).
3. Check the current status of the patient's treatment in the `Current Status` field.
4. Enter the date of the patient's visit in the `Date of Visit` field (if required).
5. Add any additional notes in the `Notes` field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (1)**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Treatment Type (2)**: Select the type of treatment the patient is undergoing, such as Physical, Occupational, or Psychological.
* **Current Status (3)**: Check all the statuses that apply to the patient's treatment progress, such as Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
* **Date of Visit (4)**: Enter the date of the patient's visit (if required).
* **Notes (5)**: Add any additional comments or observations about the patient's progress.
