# Pre Training Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Training Skills Assessment form is designed to gather information about an individual's background, skills, and training needs to help the training department understand their current situation and tailor the training program to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Select the most suitable options from the dropdown menus.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.
4. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or training coordinator for clarification.
