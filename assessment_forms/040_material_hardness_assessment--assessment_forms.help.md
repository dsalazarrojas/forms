The Material Hardness Assessment form is used to gather information about the hardness of a material, particularly the test method used, the test value, and any observations or notes about the assessment. This form is likely used in industries such as manufacturing, materials science, or quality control, where evaluating material hardness is crucial for product development or quality assessment. 

The form is designed to be completed by users who have conducted a material hardness test and want to record the results and any relevant details.

### Material Hardness Assessment - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Material Hardness Assessment form is used to collect information about the hardness of a material, allowing users to record and document test results.

### How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by selecting the test method used**. From the drop-down menu, choose either "Rockwell", "Brinell", or "Shore" to indicate the method used for the test.
2.  **Enter the test value**. This is the actual hardness value obtained from the test.
3.  **Add any observations or notes**. Provide any relevant details about the assessment, such as any issues or anomalies that occurred during the test.
4.  **Specify who the test was assigned to**. If applicable, indicate whether the test was assigned to anyone or if it's a general assessment.
5.  **Enter the date and time of submission**. Record the date and time you submitted the form.
6.  **Enter your name and any additional user input**. This is an optional field for recording your name and any additional information about the assessment.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Test Method (id: 2)**: This field allows you to select the test method used to determine the material's hardness. Select one of the available options, which are "Rockwell", "Brinell", or "Shore".
*   **Test Value (id: 3)**: Enter the actual hardness value obtained from the test.
*   **Observation (id: 4)**: Provide any notes or observations about the assessment, including any issues that occurred during the test.
*   **Assigned To (id: 6)**: If the assessment was assigned to someone, select "Yes" to indicate it was assigned to them. Otherwise, select "No".
*   **Date (id: 7)**: Record the date on which you submitted the form.
*   **Time (id: 8)**: Record the time at which you submitted the form.
*   **User Input (id: 9)**: Enter any additional information or comments about the assessment.
*   **Submitted By (id: 10)**: Enter your name and title or position, as relevant.
*   **Submitted On (id: 11)**: Record the date on which you submitted the form.

### Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all fields as accurately as possible for a thorough assessment of the material's hardness.
*   Use the correct test method and value to ensure accurate results.
*   Keep your observations and notes clear and concise to facilitate understanding.
*   Enter your name and title/position to facilitate tracking of who submitted the form and for whom.

Note to self: This guide assumes that all required fields are optional except for "Test Value". The "Test Value" field, although not strictly required, is likely essential for a meaningful assessment.
