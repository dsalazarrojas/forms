# Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate a candidate's performance in a specific context. This form gathers information about the candidate's name, identification number, assessment date, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Candidate's Name in the "Candidate Name" field (id: 1).
2. Enter the Candidate's ID number in the "Candidate ID" field (id: 2).
3. Select the Assessment Date in the "Assessment Date" field (id: 3).
4. Enter the Assessor's Name in the "Assessor Name" field (id: 4).
5. Select the Assessment Category (id: 5).
6. Select the Assessment Method (id: 6).
7. Enter the Time Allowed in Minutes for the assessment (id: 7).
8. Enter the Time Taken in Minutes for the assessment, if applicable (id: 8).
9. Enter the Total Possible Score for the assessment (id: 9).
10. Enter the Score Achieved by the candidate (id: 10).
11. Calculate the Percentage Score (id: 11).
12. Select the Pass or Fail Status (id: 12).
13. Select the Grade Level (id: 13).
14. Enter the Strengths Observed (id: 14).
15. Enter the Areas for Improvement (id: 15).
16. Enter the Assessor Comments, if applicable (id: 16).
17. Select whether the Candidate needs to Retake (id: 17).
18. Enter the Retake Date, if applicable (id: 18).
19. Enter the Recommendations for the candidate (id: 19).
20. Enter your name to confirm completion of the assessment, in the "Candidate Signature" field (id: 20).
21. Enter your name to sign the assessment, in the "Assessor Signature" field (id: 21).
22. Confirm that the assessment is complete by selecting "Yes" in the "Assessment Complete" field (id: 22).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the candidate.
* **Candidate ID** (id: 2, text, required): Enter the candidate's identification number.
* **Assessment Date** (id: 3, date, required): Select the date of the assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (id: 4, text, required): Enter the name of the assessor.
* **Assessment Category** (id: 5, select_one, required): Select from Knowledge, Skills, Abilities, Performance, Competency, or Behavior.
* **Assessment Method** (id: 6, select_one, required): Select from Written Test, Practical Exam, Interview, Observation, Portfolio Review, or Simulation.
* **Time Allowed in Minutes** (id: 7, number, required): Enter the time allowed for the assessment.
* **Time Taken in Minutes** (id: 8, number, optional): Enter the actual time taken for the assessment, if applicable.
* **Total Possible Score** (id: 9, number, required): Enter the maximum points possible.
* **Score Achieved** (id: 10, number, required): Enter the points earned by the candidate.
* **Percentage Score** (id: 11, number, required): Calculate the percentage score.
* **Pass or Fail Status** (id: 12, select_one, required): Select Pass, Fail, or Pending Review.
* **Grade Level** (id: 13, select_one, required): Select from Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory.
* **Strengths Observed** (id: 14, text, required): Enter what went well during the assessment.
* **Areas for Improvement** (id: 15, text, required): Enter areas for improvement for the candidate.
* **Assessor Comments** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter additional comments, if applicable.
* **Is Retake Required** (id: 17, select_one, required): Select Yes or No, if the candidate needs to retake.
* **Retake Date** (id: 18, date, optional): Enter the scheduled retake date, if applicable.
* **Recommendations** (id: 19, text, optional): Enter recommendations for the candidate.
* **Candidate Signature** (id: 20, text, required): Enter your name to confirm completion of the assessment.
* **Assessor Signature** (id: 21, text, required): Enter your name to sign the assessment.
* **Assessment Complete** (id: 22, select_one, required): Select Yes to confirm assessment completion.
