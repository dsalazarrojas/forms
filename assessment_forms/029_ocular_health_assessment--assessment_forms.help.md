# ocular_health_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ocular health assessment form is used to collect and document a patient's ocular health assessment. The form gathers information about the patient's vision, eye exam results, and any underlying medical conditions that may be affecting their eye health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the patient's medical history and any relevant documentation.
2. Fill out the patient's identification information, including any relevant medical ID numbers.
3. Select the appropriate options for the patient's ocular systems and any other relevant information.
4. Complete the eye exam section with details of any notable findings.
5. Fill out the patient's vision test results.
6. Document any patient status information, such as their current condition or treatment status.
7. Note any medical conditions that may be affecting the patient's eye health.
8. Provide a comprehensive treatment plan for the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Identification** (`patient_identification`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identification information.
* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, select multiple, required: false): Select all applicable options from the list of available choices for the patient's information.
* **Ocular Systems** (`ocular_systems`, text, required: false): Describe the patient's ocular systems, including their eye movement, vision, and any other relevant details.
* **Eye Exam** (`eye_exam`, text, required: false): Document the results of the eye exam, including any notable findings.
* **Vision Test** (`vision_test`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the vision test results.
* **Eye Procedure** (`eye_procedure`, text, required: false): Note any procedures performed on the patient's eyes, including surgery or other treatments.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select one, required: false): Select the current status of the patient, such as "stable" or "treatment-in-progress."
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false): Document any underlying medical conditions that may be affecting the patient's eye health.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false): Provide a comprehensive treatment plan for the patient, including any recommendations or instructions for follow-up care.
