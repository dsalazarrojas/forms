# Situational Judgment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Situational Judgment Quiz form with ease. The form is designed to assess your critical thinking and decision-making skills in real-world scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each scenario carefully and understand the situation described.
2. Respond with your judgment or action for each scenario, being as specific as possible.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **intro**
*   `intro` (`note`, optional)

This field is a brief introduction to the form, providing context and background information about the Situational Judgment Quiz. You can read it to understand the purpose and objectives of the quiz.

### **scenario1**, **scenario2**, ..., **scenario24**
*   `scenario1`, `scenario2`, ..., `scenario24` (`text`, optional)

Each of these fields represents a hypothetical scenario where you will be asked to respond with your judgment or action. Read each scenario carefully and answer truthfully, being as specific as possible. Your responses will help assess your critical thinking and decision-making skills in real-world situations.

### **scenario25**
*   `scenario25` (`text`, optional)

This field is not currently used in this form. Please ignore it.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any scenario, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Your responses will help assess your critical thinking and decision-making skills in real-world situations.
