# Ethics Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethics Knowledge Assessment is designed to evaluate an individual's knowledge and application of ethics in professional settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each question, ensuring you understand the scenario and the available options.
2. Select the most accurate response for each question based on your knowledge and experiences.
3. Answer all required fields (indicated by a *), if any.
4. Double-check your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
