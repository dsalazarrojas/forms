# Dyadic Communication Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dyadic Communication Assessment Form is designed to help participants in a dyadic relationship (e.g., two colleagues, friends, or romantic partners) assess their communication dynamics. The form will help you identify areas where you need to improve your communication to strengthen your relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the participants involved in the dyadic relationship.
2. Answer the questions about your relationship, such as your level of support, conflict, and satisfaction with each other.
3. Provide any comments or suggestions you have for improving your communication.
4. Submit your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dyadic participants**: Select the names of the two participants involved in the dyadic relationship.
* **Participant 1 name**: Enter the name of the first participant.
* **Participant 2 name**: Enter the name of the second participant.
* **Participant 1's relationship**: Describe your relationship with the first participant (e.g., colleague, friend, romantic partner).
* **Participant 2's relationship**: Describe your relationship with the second participant (e.g., colleague, friend, romantic partner).
* **Participant 1's support**: Indicate whether you generally provide support to the first participant.
* **Participant 2's support**: Indicate whether you generally provide support to the second participant.
* **Communication frequency**: Describe how often you communicate with the other participant (e.g., daily, weekly, rarely).
* **Participant 1's conflict**: Indicate if you have conflicts with the first participant.
* **Participant 2's conflict**: Indicate if you have conflicts with the second participant.
* **Participant 1's satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your relationship with the first participant (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Participant 2's satisfaction**: Rate your satisfaction with your relationship with the second participant (e.g., High, Medium, Low).
* **Participant 1's suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving your communication with the first participant.
* **Participant 2's suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you have for improving your communication with the second participant.
* **Participant 1's comments**: Provide any additional comments about your relationship with the first participant.
* **Participant 2's comments**: Provide any additional comments about your relationship with the second participant.
* **Participant 1's total score**: Enter a total score for your relationship with the first participant (e.g., 1-10).
* **Participant 2's total score**: Enter a total score for your relationship with the second participant (e.g., 1-10).
* **Communication score**: Enter a score representing the overall quality of your communication.
* **Total score**: Enter a score representing the overall quality of your dyadic relationship.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about your relationship.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions about your relationship.
