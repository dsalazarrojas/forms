# Behavior Based Psychometric Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your personality traits, behaviors, and preferences. The results will help you gain insights into your strengths and weaknesses, and provide suggestions for personal and professional development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your honest and accurate responses.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. You may remain anonymous if you choose to do so.
4. Please answer all required questions, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name, but feel free to remain anonymous if you prefer.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for results delivery, but feel free to leave it blank.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment, which is today's date.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Enter your current occupation or student status.
* **Purpose of Assessment** (`assessment_purpose`, select_one, required): Select the reason you are taking this assessment.
* **Social Interaction Preference** (`social_interaction`, select_one, required): Select how you typically behave in social situations.
* **Decision Making Style** (`decision_making`, select_one, required): Select how you typically make decisions.
* **Response to Stress** (`stress_response`, select_one, required): Select how you respond to stress.
* **Preferred Work Pace** (`work_pace`, select_one, required): Select how you prefer to work.
* **Adaptability to Change** (`change_adaptability`, select_one, required): Select how you adapt to changes.
* **Conflict Handling Style** (`conflict_handling`, select_one, required): Select how you handle conflicts.
* **Attention to Detail** (`attention_to_detail`, select_one, required): Select your level of attention to detail.
* **Primary Motivation Source** (`motivation_source`, select_one, required): Select what motivates you most.
* **Communication Style** (`communication_style`, select_one, required): Select your communication style.
* **Preferred Team Role** (`team_role`, select_one, required): Select your preferred team role.
* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`, select_one, required): Select how you approach risk.
* **Response to Feedback** (`feedback_reception`, select_one, required): Select how you respond to feedback.
* **Time Management Style** (`time_management`, select_one, required): Select how you manage time.
* **Creativity Level** (`creativity_level`, select_one, required): Select how creative you are.
* **Emotional Expression** (`emotional_expression`, select_one, required): Select how you express emotions.
* **Independence Level** (`independence`, select_one, required): Select how independent you are.
* **Persistence Level** (`persistence`, select_one, required): Select how you persist in the face of obstacles.
* **Empathy Level** (`empathy_level`, select_one, required): Select how empathetic you are.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself.
