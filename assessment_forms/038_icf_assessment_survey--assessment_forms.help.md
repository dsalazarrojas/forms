# icf_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The icf_assessment_survey form aims to gather information about your current health status, habits, and preferences for care. This information will help your care provider better understand your needs and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read each question and answer truthfully.
2. Select the correct answer for each question based on your current situation.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields to provide accurate and comprehensive information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to the ICF Assessment Survey**: This is a brief introduction to the form.
* **What is your current pain score (1-10)?**: Please rate your current pain level from 1 (no pain) to 10 (extreme pain).
* **How often do you engage in exercise or physical activity?**: Choose your frequency of physical activity from:
	+ Every day
	+ Several times a week
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **How would you rate your current stress levels?**: Please rate your current stress level.
* **Which of the following best describes your current sleep quality?**: Choose your sleep quality from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How would you rate your current overall well-being?**: Choose your well-being from:
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Do you have any chronic health conditions?**: Answer:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Do you take any medications?**: Answer:
	+ True
	+ False
* **What is your height (in inches)?**: Enter your height in inches.
* **What is your weight (in pounds)?**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **What is your age?**: Enter your age.
* **What is your zip code?**: Enter your zip code.
* **Do you have any allergies or sensitivities?**: Answer:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Do you have any medical devices or implants?**: Answer:
	+ True
	+ False
* **What is your primary care provider contact info?**: Enter your primary care provider's contact information.
* **What is your insurance information?**: Enter your insurance information.
* **Do you have any medical conditions that require immediate attention?**: Answer:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Do you have any concerns that you would like to report?**: Enter any concerns you may have.
* **Is there anything else you would like to share with your care provider?**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.
* **Do you have any questions for your care provider?**: Enter any questions you have for your care provider.
* **Would you like to receive any follow-up communications?**: Answer:
	+ True
	+ False
* **How would you like to be contacted for follow-up?**: Choose your preferred contact method from:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Mail
* **Do you have a care provider that you would like to contact?**: Enter your care provider's information.
* **Do you have any emergency contact information for someone close to you?**: Enter your emergency contact's information.
* **Is there anything else we can help you with?**: Enter any additional comments or requests for support.
