# Typing Speed Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Typing Speed Assessment form is designed to evaluate your typing speed and accuracy over a short period. It's essential to fill out the form correctly to obtain accurate results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your typing speed in the "Typing Speed" field. This field measures how many words you can type per minute (WPM).
2. Fill out the "Accuracy" field with the number of errors you made while typing.
3. Enter the time it took you to complete the typing test in the "Time" field.
4. Choose one of the three options for the "Typing Speed Assessment Result" field to indicate your level of typing speed and accuracy.
5. Enter your average words per minute (WPM) in the "Average WPM" field.
6. Calculate your accuracy percentage by dividing your accuracy by the number of total words typed and multiply it by 100.
7. Enter the number of errors you made in the "Errors" field.
8. Calculate your words per minute (WPM) by dividing the total number of words you typed by the time it took you to complete the test.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Typing Speed** (1): Enter your average words per minute typing speed. This is a measure of how fast you can type.
* **Accuracy** (2): Enter the number of errors you made while typing. This measures how accurate you are.
* **Time** (3): Enter the time it took you to complete the typing test. This field is used to calculate your typing speed and accuracy.
* **Typing Speed Assessment Result** (4): Choose one of the three options to indicate your level of typing speed and accuracy. The options are:
	+ A: Excellent - You have a high typing speed and accuracy.
	+ B: Average - You have an average typing speed and accuracy.
	+ C: Low - You have a low typing speed and accuracy.
* **Average WPM** (5): Enter your average words per minute typing speed.
* **Accuracy Percentage** (6): This field is calculated by dividing your accuracy by the number of total words typed and multiplying it by 100. This field is not editable.
* **Errors** (7): Enter the number of errors you made while typing. This field is used to calculate your accuracy percentage.
* **WPM** (8): This field is calculated by dividing the total number of words you typed by the time it took you to complete the test. This field is not editable.
