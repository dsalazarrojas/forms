# Cryptocurrency Eligibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the suitability of a user for a particular program or service related to cryptocurrencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

1. Select the answers for each field accordingly, based on your understanding and information about yourself.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Make sure to read each field's label carefully and understand what it's asking for before answering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Suitability** (<code>user_suitability</code>): Choose "Yes" if you have the necessary skills, experience, and knowledge to participate in the program or service. Choose "No" otherwise.
* **User Experience** (<code>user_experience</code>): Select "None" if you have no experience with cryptocurrencies, or "Other" if you have other types of experience.
* **Risk Awareness** (<code>risk_awareness</code>): Choose "Yes" if you are aware of the risks associated with cryptocurrencies, or "No" otherwise.
* **Exchange Registration** (<code>exchange_registration</code>): Enter the number of your exchange registration, if applicable.
* **Exchange Commission** (<code>exchange_commission</code>): Enter the commission paid at the exchange, if applicable.
* **Fintech Registration** (<code>fintech_registration</code>): Enter the number of your fintech registration, if applicable.
* **Advisor Registration** (<code>advisor_registration</code>): Select "None" if you are not registered as an advisor, or "Other" if you have other types of registration.
* **Risk Tolerable Assets** (<code>risk_tolerable_assets</code>): Enter the number of risk-tolerable assets you have.
* **Risk Unacceptable Assets** (<code>risk_unacceptable_assets</code>): Enter the number of risk-unacceptable assets you have.
* **Assets in USD** (<code>assets_in_usd</code>): Enter the total value of your assets in USD.
* **Net Worth** (<code>net_worth</code>): Enter your total net worth.
* **Investment Experience** (<code>investment_experience</code>): Select "None" if you have no investment experience, or "Other" if you have other types of investment experience.
* **Risk Tolerable Investments** (<code>risk_tolerable_investments</code>): Enter the number of risk-tolerable investments you have.
* **Risk Unacceptable Investments** (<code>risk_unacceptable_investments</code>): Enter the number of risk-unacceptable investments you have.
* **Has Tax ID** (<code>has_tax_id</code>): Choose "None" if you do not have a tax ID, or "Other" if you have other types of tax ID.
* **Tax Residence** (<code>tax_residence</code>): Select "None" if you are not a tax resident, or "Other" if you have other types of tax residence.
* **Citizenship** (<code>citizenship</code>): Select "None" if you are not a citizen, or "Other" if you have other types of citizenship.
* **Employment Status** (<code>employment_status</code>): Select "None" if you are not employed, or "Other" if you have other types of employment.
* **Occupation** (<code>occupation</code>): Enter your occupation.
* **Annual Income** (<code>annual_income</code>): Enter your annual income.
* **Employment Length** (<code>employment_length</code>): Enter the length of your employment.
* **Employment Status Description** (<code>employment_status_description</code>): Enter a description of your employment status.
* **Address** (<code>address</code>): Enter your address.
* **Has Exchange** (<code>has_exchange</code>): Choose "None" if you do not have an exchange, or "Other" if you have other types of exchanges.
* **Note** (<code>note</code>): Enter any additional notes about yourself, if applicable.
