# Risk And Opportunity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about risks and opportunities in a structured and organized manner. It helps to identify potential risks and opportunities, assess their impact and likelihood, and document associated actions and comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Category field to specify the type of risk or opportunity being assessed.
2. Provide a brief Description of the risk or opportunity.
3. Assess the Impact on your organization by selecting High, Moderate, or Low from the dropdown list.
4. Evaluate the Likelihood of the risk or opportunity by selecting High, Moderate, or Low from the dropdown list.
5. Document any relevant Actions taken or planned to address the risk or opportunity.
6. Identify the Owner responsible for managing or mitigating the risk or opportunity.
7. Add Comments about the risk or opportunity, if any.
