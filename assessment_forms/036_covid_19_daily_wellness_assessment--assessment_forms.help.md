# COVID 19 Daily Wellness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID 19 Daily Wellness Assessment form is designed to help track and record the overall, physical, and mental well-being status of an individual on a daily basis. This information is used to inform decisions about access to work, school, or other activities and to provide support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you fill out the form on the current day, as it will help track any changes in your well-being status over time.
* Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly. The more accurate information you provide, the better healthcare professionals and administrators will be able to make informed decisions.
* Review the options for each question carefully and select the one that best describes your current state.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date (Page 1)** (`assessment_date`, `date`, **Required**): The date you are completing this form.
* **Time of Assessment (Page 1)** (`time_of_assessment`, `time`, **Optional**): The time you are completing this form.
* **Name (Page 1)** (`participant_name`, `text`, **Required**): Your full name.
* **ID Number (Page 1)** (`participant_id`, `text`, **Optional**): Employee, student, or member ID number if applicable.
* **Department or Location (Page 1)** (`department_location`, `text`, **Required**): Where you are currently located (e.g., home, school, office).
* **Overall Wellness (Page 1)** (`overall_wellness`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate your overall well-being on a scale of 1-10.
* **Physical Wellness Status (Page 1)** (`physical_wellness`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select how well you are feeling physically.
* **Mental/Emotional Wellness (Page 1)** (`mental_wellness`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select how well you are feeling mentally/emotionally.
* **Body Temperature (Page 1)** (`temperature_check`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter your body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Do you have a fever? (Page 1)** (`fever`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select yes or no.
* **Respiratory Symptoms (Page 1)** (`respiratory_symptoms`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the severity of your respiratory symptoms.
* **Gastrointestinal Symptoms (Page 2)** (`gastrointestinal_symptoms`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select the severity of your gastrointestinal symptoms.
* **General Symptoms of Illness (Page 2)** (`general_malaise`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select the severity of your general symptoms.
* **Sleep Quality Last Night (Page 2)** (`sleep_quality`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select how well you slept last night.
* **Stress Level (Page 2)** (`stress_level`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select how stressed you are feeling.
* **Anxiety Level (Page 2)** (`anxiety_level`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select how anxious you are feeling.
* **Mood Today (Page 2)** (`mood`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select how your mood is today.
* **Energy Level (Page 3)** (`energy_level`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select how much energy you have today.
* **COVID-19 Risk Factors Present (Page 3)** (`covid_risk_factors`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select all applicable COVID-19 risk factors.
* **Activities Planned for Today (Page 3)** (`activities_today`, `select_multiple`, **Optional**): Select any activities planned for today.
* **Do you need wellness support or resources? (Page 3)** (`wellness_support_needed`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select yes for any support.
* **Cleared to Attend Activities Today (Page 3)** (`cleared_to_attend`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select based on your wellness assessment.
* **Additional Notes or Concerns (Page 3)** (`additional_notes`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any additional information.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and thoroughly.
* Review each question carefully and select the best option for your current state.
* Ensure you fill out the form for each day to track any changes in your well-being status over time.
