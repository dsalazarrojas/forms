# Specialist Referral Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialist Referral Assessment form is used to collect information about patients referred to specialists for further care. This form ensures that relevant details are captured about the patient's medical condition, specialist involved, and other essential healthcare information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's basic information, including their name, contact phone number, and email address.
2. Select the specialist to whom the patient is being referred.
3. Enter the patient's medical record number and other relevant medical history.
4. Provide a brief summary of the patient's condition and treatment plan.
5. Enter the dates of admission and discharge (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes or follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`Patient Info`, text, required): Fill in the patient's basic information, including their name, contact phone number, and email address.
* **Specialty** (`Specialty`, select_one, required): Select the specialist to whom the patient is being referred.
* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter the patient's address.
* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, text, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, text, required): Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, text, required): Describe the patient's current medical condition.
* **Treatment Plan** (`Treatment Plan`, text, required): Outline the proposed treatment plan for the patient.
* **Specialist Name** (`Specialist Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the specialist to whom the patient is being referred.
* **Doctor Name** (`Doctor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the referring doctor.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`Doctor Specialty`, select_one, required): Select the specialty of the referring doctor.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`Patient Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Date of Admission** (`Patient Date of Admission`, date, required): Enter the date the patient was admitted (if applicable).
* **Patient Date of Discharge** (`Patient Date of Discharge`, date, optional): Enter the date the patient was discharged (if applicable).
* **Follow Up Date** (`Follow Up Date`, date, required): Enter the date for follow-up (if applicable).
* **Hospital Name** (`Hospital Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the hospital where the patient is being referred.
* **Doctor Department** (`Doctor Department`, text, required): Enter the department of the hospital where the patient is being treated.
* **Doctor Designation** (`Doctor Designation`, text, required): Enter the doctor's designation.
* **Doctor Designation 2** (`Doctor Designation 2`, text, required): Enter the doctor's designation 2.
* **Doctor Specialty 2** (`Doctor Specialty 2`, text, required): Enter the doctor's specialty 2.
* **Specialist Name 2** (`Specialist Name 2`, text, required): Enter the name of the specialist 2.
* **Medical Record Number 2** (`Medical Record Number 2`, text, required): Enter the second medical record number.
* **Follow Up Note** (`Follow Up Note`, text, required): Enter any additional follow-up information.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the patient's information for accuracy.
* Use the correct specialty and doctor specialty for the referral.
* Provide clear and concise information for the treatment plan and follow-up note.
* Save the form before submitting it for review.
