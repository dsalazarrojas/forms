# employment_reference_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employment_reference_assessment_form is used to gather detailed information about an individual's professional references, which is vital for employment-related purposes. This form ensures that you have all the necessary details about your references, making it easier to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submission.
3. Review each field's description below to understand its purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Section 1** (ID: 1, Required: True): This field is the first section of the form where you provide the basic details about your employment history.
* **Contact Details** (ID: 2, Required: True): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email, accurately and completely.
* **Referee Name** (ID: 3, Required: True): Provide the name of your professional reference.
* **Relationship** (ID: 4, Required: True): Specify your relationship with the reference, such as colleague, manager, or mentor.
* **Contact Title** (ID: 5, Required: True): Enter the title or position of your reference.
* **Email** (ID: 6, Required: True): Enter your reference's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (ID: 7, Required: True): Enter your reference's phone number.
* **Rating** (ID: 8, Required: True): Rate your reference's performance, using the scale provided (excellent, average, or below average).
* **Comments** (ID: 9, Optional): Add any additional comments about your reference.
* **Recommendations** (ID: 10, Required: True): List any recommendations for your reference.
* **Additional Comments** (ID: 11, Optional): Add any further comments about your reference.
* **Signature** (ID: 12, Required: True): Sign your name and date to confirm your submission.
* **Referee Occupation** (ID: 13, Required: True): Enter your reference's occupation or job title.
* **Referee Qualification** (ID: 14, Required: True): Provide details about your reference's qualifications or certifications.
* **Referee Address** (ID: 15, Required: True): Enter your reference's address.
* **Referee City** (ID: 16, Required: True): Enter your reference's city or location.
* **Referee State** (ID: 17, Required: True): Enter your reference's state or province.
* **Referee Country** (ID: 18, Required: True): Enter your reference's country.
* **Referee Zip** (ID: 19, Required: True): Enter your reference's zip code.
* **Referee Email** (ID: 20, Required: True): Enter your reference's email address.
* **Referee Phone** (ID: 21, Required: True): Enter your reference's phone number.
* **Referee Date of Last Contact** (ID: 22, Optional): Enter the date of your last contact with your reference.
* **Referee Date of Last Contact Other** (ID: 23, Optional): Add any additional information about your last contact with your reference.
* **Referee Last Contact Method** (ID: 24, Optional): Specify the method of your last contact with your reference.
* **Referee Last Contact Details** (ID: 25, Optional): Add any additional details about your last contact with your reference.
