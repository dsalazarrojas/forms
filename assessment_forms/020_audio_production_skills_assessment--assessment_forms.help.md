# Audio Production Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Audio Production Skills Assessment form is designed to evaluate the skills, experience, and expertise of audio production professionals. This comprehensive assessment will help identify strengths and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

* Start by entering your full name, email address, phone number, current location, and date of this assessment in the corresponding fields.
* Select your years of experience in audio production, primary role in the industry, and level of experience in recording, mixing, and mastering.
* Rate your proficiency in various DAWs, knowledge of audio plugins, and skills in microphone techniques, signal flow, and acoustic treatment.
* Indicate your level of experience in live sound, post-production, and other relevant areas.
* Specify if you hold any audio certifications and provide a link to your portfolio or work samples if applicable.
* Choose your current availability and describe your career goals briefly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Full Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address (Email, Required)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number (Text, Required)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Current Location (Text, Required)**: Enter your city and state or country.
* **Assessment Date (Date, Required)**: Enter the date of this assessment.
* **Years of Experience (Select One, Required)**: Select your total years of experience in audio production.
* **Primary Role (Select One, Required)**: Select your main role in audio production, such as Audio Engineer, Music Producer, etc.
* **Recording Experience (Select One, Required)**: Rate your level of experience in recording, from Expert to No experience.
* **Mixing Experience (Select One, Required)**: Rate your level of experience in mixing, from Expert to No experience.
* **Mastering Experience (Select One, Required)**: Rate your level of experience in mastering, from Expert to No experience.
* **DAW Proficiency (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select the DAWs you are proficient in, such as Pro Tools, Logic Pro, etc.
* **Plugin Knowledge (Select One, Required)**: Rate your knowledge of audio plugins, from Expert to No experience.
* **Microphone Techniques (Select One, Required)**: Rate your knowledge of microphone techniques, from Expert to No experience.
* **Signal Flow Understanding (Select One, Required)**: Rate your understanding of signal flow, from Expert to No experience.
* **Acoustic Treatment Knowledge (Select One, Required)**: Rate your knowledge of acoustic treatment, from Expert to No experience.
* **Live Sound Experience (Select One, Required)**: Rate your experience in live sound, from Expert to No experience.
* **Post-Production Experience (Select One, Required)**: Rate your experience in post-production, from Expert to No experience.
* **Certifications Held (Select One, Required)**: Indicate if you hold any audio certifications, such as Pro Tools certified.
* **Portfolio Available (Select One, Required)**: Indicate if you have a portfolio of work, and if so, provide a link or attach samples.
* **Availability (Select One, Required)**: Select your current availability, such as Full-time, Part-time, etc.
* **Career Goals (Text, Optional)**: Briefly describe your career goals.
