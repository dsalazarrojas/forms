> ## Oral Exammation Feedback Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
The Oral Exammation Feedback Form is used to provide feedback on oral examinations. This form is designed to gather information about the examiner, candidate, and examination details. The form helps evaluate the examination process and identify areas for improvement.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name or ID number of the candidate.
2. Choose the examiner's title and department.
3. Enter the date and time of the examination.
4. Enter the examiner's score and select the score range from the dropdown menu.
5. Provide a brief comment from the examiner.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback.
7. Select the type of oral examination (Internal or External).
8. Enter the evaluation date and time.
9. Enter the location of the evaluation.
10. Add any notes about the oral examination.
11. Select the status of the evaluation (Completed or In progress).
12. Enter any assessment comments.
13. Enter the submission ID.
14. Enter the name of the person who created the form.
15. Enter the date the form was created.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Feedback Form** (`oral_exam`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the oral examination.
- **Examiner** (`examiner`, text, required): Enter the name of the examiner who gave the oral examination.
- **Examiner Title** (`examiner_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the examiner.
- **Examiner Department** (`examiner_department`, text, required): Enter the department of the examiner.
- **Candidate** (`candidate`, text, required): Enter the name or ID number of the candidate.
- **Date of Examination** (`candidate_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the oral examination.
- **Time of Examination** (`candidate_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the oral examination.
- **Examiner Score** (`examiner_score`, number, required): Enter the score given by the examiner.
- **Examiner Score Range** (`examiner_score_range`, select_one, required): Select a score range from the dropdown menu.
- **Examiner Comment** (`examiner_comment`, text, required): Enter a brief comment from the examiner.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
- **Oral Exam Type** (`oral_exam_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of oral examination (Internal or External).
- **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
- **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the evaluation.
- **Evaluation Location** (`evaluation_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the evaluation.
- **Oral Examination Notes** (`oral_exam_notes`, text, required): Enter any notes about the oral examination.
- **Evaluation Status** (`evaluation_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the evaluation (Completed or In progress).
- **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, text, required): Enter any assessment comments.
- **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, text, required): Enter the submission ID.
- **Created By** (`created_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
- **Created Date** (`created_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was created.
- **Modified By** (`modified_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who modified the form.
- **Modified Date** (`modified_date`, date, required): Enter the date the form was last modified.

### Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Use the dropdown menus to select the correct options.
- Enter dates in the format YYYY-MM-DD for dates and HH:MM:SS for times.
- Proofread the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
