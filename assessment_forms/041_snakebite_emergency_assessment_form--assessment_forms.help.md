# Snakebite Emergency Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snakebite Emergency Assessment Form is designed to collect information in cases of a snakebite emergency. It helps to quickly gather essential details about the incident, the patient's medical history, and any treatment provided. This form is intended for use by medical professionals or emergency responders to assess the situation and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Emergency Contact Info** section with the patient's contact information, including their name, phone number, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter the **Patient Info** section with the patient's name, age, and any other relevant medical history.
3. Provide **Incident Info** about the snakebite, including the location and time of the incident.
4. Document the **Medical History** of the patient, including any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies.
5. Record the **Treatment** provided to the patient.
6. Add any **Follow-up Info** regarding the patient's condition and any follow-up actions taken.
7. Include any **Note** about the case, such as observations or actions taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the person who was bitten by the snake.
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Enter the name, age, and any other relevant medical history of the patient.
* **Incident Info** (`incident_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Describe the location and time of the snakebite incident.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required):
	+ Record the patient's pre-existing medical conditions or allergies.
* **Treatment** (`treatment`, `text`, required):
	+ Document the medical treatment provided to the patient.
* **Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_info`, `text`, required):
	+ Record any follow-up actions taken or the patient's current status.
* **Note** (`note`, `text`, required):
	+ Include any additional comments or observations about the case.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the patient's contact information for accurate and up-to-date details.
* Keep the **Note** field for any additional comments or observations.
