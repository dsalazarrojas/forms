# COVID 19 Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the risk level of a site or location, considering factors such as social distancing, hygiene, PPE, and overall safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the assessor.
2. Enter the site or location name.
3. Choose the date of the assessment.
4. Answer each question regarding the site's safety measures:
	* Social Distancing Measures in Place: Select the level of social distancing measures observed.
	* Hygiene Facilities Available: Select whether hygiene facilities are available and adequate.
	* PPE Available for Staff: Select whether staff has access to Personal Protective Equipment.
	* Ventilation System Checked: Select whether the ventilation system is functioning correctly.
	* Signage for Public: Select whether public signage is posted.
	* Cleaning Schedule Maintained: Select whether the cleaning schedule is kept.
5. Determine the Overall Risk Level based on the site's safety measures.
6. Provide recommended actions for improvement, if necessary.
7. Choose a follow-up inspection date, if applicable.
8. Certify that the assessment was conducted according to CDC and local guidelines.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor Name (`assessor_name_full`):** Enter your name as the assessor.
* **Site or Location Inspected (`site_location_text`):** Enter the name of the site or location being assessed.
* **Date of Assessment (`assessment_date_val`):** Select the date of the assessment.
* **Social Distancing Measures in Place (`social_dist_yn`):** Select the level of social distancing measures observed:
	+ True: Measures are strictly observed and enforced.
	+ False: Measures are not observed or enforced.
	+ Partially: Measures are partially observed or enforced.
	+ N/A: Not Applicable.
* **Hygiene Facilities Available (`hygiene_fac_yn`):** Select whether hygiene facilities are available and adequate:
	+ True: Facilities are available and sufficient.
	+ False: Facilities are not available or insufficient.
	+ Inadequate: Facilities are available but not adequate.
* **PPE Available for Staff (`ppe_avail_yn`):** Select whether staff has access to PPE:
	+ True: PPE is available and sufficient.
	+ False: PPE is not available or insufficient.
	+ Low Stock: PPE is available but low in stock.
* **Ventilation System Checked (`ventilation_check_yn`):** Select whether the ventilation system is functioning correctly:
	+ True: The system is functioning as expected.
	+ False: The system is not functioning or is malfunctioning.
	+ Ongoing: The system is functioning but requires ongoing maintenance.
* **Signage for Public (`signage_posted_yn`):** Select whether public signage is posted:
	+ True: Signage is present and visible.
	+ False: Signage is not present or is missing.
	+ Needs improvement: Signage is present but requires improvement.
* **Cleaning Schedule Maintained (`cleaning_sched_yn`):** Select whether the cleaning schedule is kept:
	+ True: Schedule is maintained and adhered to.
	+ False: Schedule is not maintained or irregular.
	+ Irregular: Schedule is not maintained or irregular.
* **Overall Risk Level (`overall_risk_cat`):** Select the overall risk level based on the assessment:
	+ Low Risk: The site or location poses a low risk.
	+ Moderate Risk: The site or location poses a moderate risk.
	+ High Risk: The site or location poses a high risk.
	+ Critical Need: The site or location poses a critical need for improvement.
* **Recommended Actions (`recommended_actions_text`):** Provide recommended actions for improvement, if necessary.
* **Follow-up Inspection Date (`follow_up_date_val`):** Select a follow-up inspection date, if applicable.
* **Certify this assessment (`assessment_cert_yn`):** Select whether the assessment was conducted according to CDC and local guidelines:
	+ True: The assessment was conducted as required.
	+ I certify: The assessor is certifying that the assessment was conducted as required.
Note: The last question is a certification statement that the assessor is confirming that the assessment was conducted according to guidelines.
