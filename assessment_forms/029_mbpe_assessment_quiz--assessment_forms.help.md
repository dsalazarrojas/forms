# MBPE Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MBPE Assessment Quiz is a form used to collect information from end-users for assessments and evaluations. It is designed to gather data in a variety of fields, including personal details and skills evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the MBPE Assessment Quiz, follow these steps:

* Please fill out each field carefully and accurately, taking care to ensure that the information is up-to-date and relevant to your situation.
* Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the required text fields.
* Enter your Phone number, Date of Birth, and Years of Experience in the required text or numeric fields.
* Select your Confidence Level (High, Medium, or Low) and Knowledge Area (Leadership and Strategy, Operations, Finance, or Marketing) from the drop-down menus.
* Select your Education Level (Bachelors, Masters, or PHD) and Training Area of Interest (Leadership and Strategy, Operations, Finance, or Marketing) from the drop-down menus (note: this field is optional).
* If you have completed the assessment, please select 'True' from the 'Assessment Completed' drop-down menu. If you have not completed the assessment, please select 'False'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number without any special characters or spaces.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Confidence Level** (Confidence Level, select_one, required): Select your confidence level in the assessment (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Knowledge Area** (Knowledge Area, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas in which you have knowledge (Leadership and Strategy, Operations, Finance, or Marketing).
* **Years of Experience** (Years of Experience, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Education Level** (Education Level, select_one, optional): Select your highest education level (Bachelors, Masters, PHD, or Other).
* **Training Area of Interest** (Training Area of Interest, select_multiple, optional): Select areas you are interested in for training (Leadership and Strategy, Operations, Finance, or Marketing).
* **Assessment Completed** (Assessment Completed, select_one, optional): If you have completed the assessment, select 'True', otherwise select 'False'.
* **Training Area of Interest** (Training Area of Interest, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas you are interested in for training (Leadership and Strategy, Operations, Finance, or Marketing).
