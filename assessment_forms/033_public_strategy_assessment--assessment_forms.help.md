# Public Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your public strategy, which is a critical component of your overall business strategy. It's a series of questions and fields meant to guide you in evaluating your public strategy, from setting a clear public strategy to creating a timeline and securing resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing each section carefully and answering the questions truthfully.**
2. **Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each field.**
3. **If you're unsure about a field, try to provide the best possible answer you can.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Public strategy** (`public_strategy`, text, required): 
  This field is meant to be a brief description of your public strategy. Think of a one-sentence summary that captures the essence of your strategy.
* **Mission** (`mission`, text, optional): 
  This field is where you can elaborate on your company's or organization's mission statement. This can be a statement that reflects your values and overall purpose.
* **Vision** (`vision`, text, optional): 
  In this field, you can outline your company's or organization's vision for the future. This can be a statement that describes your desired outcome.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): 
  List the specific, measurable goals you aim to achieve with your public strategy. These goals should be SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, and Time-bound).
* **Target audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): 
  Identify who your public strategy is meant to serve. This could be customers, stakeholders, or a specific market segment.
* **Strategy** (`strategy`, text, optional): 
  Outline the steps and actions you'll take to achieve your public strategy. This can include specific tactics, marketing channels, and communication plans.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, text, optional): 
  Set a realistic timeline for your public strategy. This can be a specific date or a period of time (e.g., quarterly, annually) when you expect to see progress.
* **Resources** (`resources`, text, optional): 
  List the necessary resources (people, budget, tools, etc.) to successfully execute your public strategy.

## Tips
* Be honest and realistic when answering each question.
* Remember that this form is a starting point, not a final product. Use it as a tool to guide your thought process and help you develop a solid public strategy.
* If you're unsure or need more information, refer back to your company's or organization's overall business strategy.
