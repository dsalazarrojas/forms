# Logistics Delivery Personnel Efficiency Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the efficiency of logistics delivery personnel. It will be used to evaluate their performance in delivering logistics, including meeting deadlines, managing inventory, and coordinating with other teams.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select "Yes" or "No" for each question, as applicable.
2. Please enter your User Name if you are a logistics delivery personnel.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the presence of delivery personnel.
4. Enter the date of the assessment.
5. Enter the time of the assessment.
6. Enter your email for any necessary communication.
7. Please provide a brief description of your role and responsibilities.
8. Please confirm if a specific tool was assigned to you.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any other delivery personnel with you during the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Logistics Delivery Personnel Efficiency Assessment**: This is the title of the form.
* **Category**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have been assigned to a category (e.g., team, project, etc.).
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of your role and responsibilities.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm if you have been assigned a specific tool.
* **User Name**: Enter your name as a logistics delivery personnel.
* **Delivery Personnel**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any other delivery personnel with you during the assessment.
* **Delivery Personnel 2**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any other delivery personnel with you during the assessment.
* **Delivery Personnel 3**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you have any other delivery personnel with you during the assessment.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the assessment in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time**: Enter the time of the assessment in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Email**: Enter your email address for any necessary communication.
* **Yes** and **No** options: These are used to provide a simple "yes" or "no" answer for each question.
