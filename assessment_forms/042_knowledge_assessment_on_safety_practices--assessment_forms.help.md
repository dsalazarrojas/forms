# Knowledge Assessment On Safety Practices - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your safety practices and knowledge in a workplace setting. It will help assess your understanding and adherence to safety procedures, guidelines, and protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the appropriate options for each field based on your actual practices and knowledge.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please answer based on your best judgment or seek clarification if necessary.
4. Please be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of equipment do you use on the job?** (`first_page`, Select One, Required): This field asks about the types of safety equipment you use during work hours.
	+ Select one of the provided options: Hard hat, Safety glasses, or Safety boots.
* **Job type** (`job_type`, Select One, Required): This field asks about the nature of your job.
	+ Select one of the provided options: Production, Maintenance, or Office.
* **First aid kit available on site** (`first_aids`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about the availability of first aid kits on site.
	+ Select all that apply: Yes, No.
* **Safety equipment available** (`safety_equipment`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about the types of safety equipment available on site.
	+ Select all that apply: Hard hat, Safety glasses, or Safety harness.
* **How many hours of safety training have you had?** (`training_hours`, Number, Required): This field asks about your total hours of safety training.
	+ Enter a numerical value for the number of hours.
* **Safety training topics covered** (`training_topics`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about the topics covered in your safety training.
	+ Select all that apply: Hazardous Materials, Confined spaces, or Fire suppression systems.
* **Have you attended any safety conferences or workshops?** (`safety_conferences`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your participation in safety-related events.
	+ Select all that apply: Yes, No.
* **Years of experience in current job** (`years_of_experience`, Number, Required): This field asks about your work experience in your current role.
	+ Enter a numerical value for the number of years.
* **What safety equipment have you used?** (`safety_equipment_used`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your personal experience with safety equipment.
	+ Select all that apply: Hard hat, Safety glasses, or Safety harness.
* **First aid certification** (`first_aids_certification`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your status of first aid certification.
	+ Select all that apply: Yes, No.
* **Safety equipment providers** (`safety_equipment_providers`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your knowledge of safety equipment suppliers.
	+ Select all that apply: ABC Safety, Miller, or Deere.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, Email, Required): This field asks for your emergency contact information.
	+ Enter a valid email address.
* **First aid supplied on site** (`first_aids_supplied`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about the availability of first aid supplies on site.
	+ Select all that apply: Yes, No.
* **Total hours of safety training received in the past 6 months** (`safety_training_hours`, Number, Required): This field asks about your recent safety training hours.
	+ Enter a numerical value for the number of hours.
* **Safety equipment inspection record kept** (`safety_equipment_inspection`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your practice of recording safety equipment inspections.
	+ Select all that apply: Yes, No.
* **What emergency equipment do you have on hand?** (`emergency_equipment`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your emergency equipment availability.
	+ Select all that apply: First aid kit, Fire extinguisher, or Emergency phone.

	+ Select all that apply: OSHA, Local, or Manufacturer.
* **Where are first aid locations on site?** (`first_aids_location`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about the location of first aid kits.
	+ Select all that apply: Near entrance, Near exit, or Central area.
* **Safety training topics** (`safety_training_topics`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about the topics of your safety training.
	+ Select all that apply: Hazardous Materials, Confined spaces, or Fire suppression systems.

* **When is regular safety equipment maintenance performed?** (`safety_equipment_maintenance`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about the frequency of safety equipment maintenance.
	+ Select all that apply: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **How often do you report hazards on site?** (`hazard_reporting`, Select Multiple, Required): This field asks about your reporting frequency for hazards.
	+ Select all that apply: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

	+ Select all that apply: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
