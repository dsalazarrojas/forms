# 4D Lifestyle Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for coaching clients to assess their lifestyle habits across four dimensions: physical activity, nutrition, mind-set, and finances. The goal is to gain insights into areas that may need attention and support.
## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your **Full name**, which should include both first and last names.
2. Enter your **Date of birth** using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select a value for **Age** in years.
4. Provide your **Phone number** including country code, if applicable.
5. Enter your **Email address** for communication.
6. Record the **Date of assessment** for reference.
7. Choose the level of **Physical activity**, from Sedentary to Athlete.
8. Select the average hours you **sleep per night** with decimal precision.
9. Assess the quality of your **Nutrition** as Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
10. List any current **medications** you are taking, or type 'None'.
11. Identify your **stress level**, from 0 to 10.
12. Choose your **Mood overview**, from Stable, Fluctuating, Low, High, or Mixed.
13. Select all sources of **support network** you have, such as Family, Friends, Partner, or Professional Support.
14. Select your perceived level of **Financial security**, from Secure to Insecure.
15. Briefly describe your **Top financial goals**.
16. Determine the **primary area to address**, from Body, Mindset, Relationships, or Finances.
17. Choose your **readiness to change**, from Not Ready, Considering, Ready, or Actively Changing.
18. Leave any **additional comments** or coach notes in the text field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information**: This section is a header for the following fields, providing a brief overview of the client's contact information.
* **Full name**: Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age**: Record your age in years.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address for communication.
* **Date of assessment**: Record the date you completed this assessment for reference.
* **Physical activity level**: Choose how active you are, ranging from Sedentary to Athlete.
*   *Physical activity level* is used to gauge an individual's physical activity level and how it affects their health and well-being.
*   *Sedentary* means having a lifestyle that is largely inactive or not physically active.
*   *Lightly active* means being moderately physically active, but not as much as someone who is moderately active.
*   *Moderately active* means being physically active for about 30 minutes a day for at least 5 days of the week.
*   *Very active* means being physically active for more than 30 minutes a day for about 6-7 days of the week.
*   *Athlete* means being extremely physically active, either through professional sports or intense exercise.
* **Average sleep per night**: Record the average hours you sleep per night, with decimal precision.
*   *Average sleep per night* is used to assess an individual's sleep quality and how it affects their mental and physical health.
*   *Average sleep per night* should be recorded with decimal precision to ensure accurate data collection.
* **Nutrition quality**: Choose how you rate the quality of your diet, from Poor to Excellent.
*   *Nutrition quality* is used to assess an individual's dietary habits and how they affect their overall health.
*   *Poor* means having a poor diet with little to no fruits and vegetables.
*   *Fair* means having a fair diet, but with some room for improvement.
*   *Good* means having a good diet with some balance of fruits, vegetables, and lean proteins.
*   *Very good* means having a very good diet with a balanced mix of foods.
*   *Excellent* means having an excellent diet with a variety of fruits, vegetables, and lean proteins.
* **Current medications**: List any medications you are currently taking, or type 'None' if none.
*   *Current medications* is used to collect information about any ongoing medical treatment or health issues.
*   *List medications* or type 'None' to ensure accurate data collection.
*   *Current medications* is an important field for the coach to understand any potential health implications.
*   If you are taking medications, please list them in the format: 'Medication 1, Medication 2, etc.'
*   If you are not taking any medications, type 'None'.
* **Stress level**: Record your perceived level of stress, from 0 to 10.
*   *Stress level* is used to gauge an individual's stress levels and how it affects their mental health.
*   *Stress level* should be recorded with a number from 0 to 10.
*   A score of 0 means having no stress at all.
*   A score of 10 means experiencing extreme stress.
*   *Stress level* is an important field for the coach to understand an individual's mental health.
*   Please be honest when recording your stress levels.
*   Record your stress levels based on how you feel currently.
*   If you are feeling stressed, please take a moment to reflect on why and how you are feeling this way.
* **Mood overview**: Choose how you feel about your current mood, from Stable to Mixed.
*   *Mood overview* is used to assess an individual's emotional state and how it affects their overall well-being.
*   *Stable* means feeling calm and stable.
*   *Fluctuating* means feeling unstable or emotional.
*   *Low* means feeling sad or depressed.
*   *High* means feeling energetic or happy.
*   *Mixed* means feeling a mix of emotions.
*   *Mood overview* is an important field for the coach to understand an individual's emotional state.
*   Please be honest when recording your mood.
*   Record your mood based on how you feel currently.
*   If you are feeling emotional, please take a moment to reflect on why and how you are feeling this way.
* **Support network**: Select all sources of support you have, such as Family, Friends, Partner, or Professional Support.
*   *Support network* is used to collect information about an individual's support system and how it affects their mental health.
*   *Select all* that apply to ensure accurate data collection.
*   Please be honest when selecting your support network.
*   Record your support network based on your actual relationships and connections.
*   *Support network* is an important field for the coach to understand an individual's social connections.
*   If you have multiple sources of support, please select all that apply.
*   If you have no support network, please select 'None'.
*   Please take a moment to reflect on why and how you are feeling this way.
* **Financial security**: Choose your perceived level of financial security, from Secure to Insecure.
*   *Financial security* is used to assess an individual's financial stability and how it affects their overall well-being.
*   *Secure* means feeling financially stable and secure.
*   *Mostly secure* means feeling mostly secure, but with some concerns.
*   *Neutral* means feeling neutral about your finances.
*   *Somewhat insecure* means feeling somewhat insecure about your finances.
*   *Insecure* means feeling extremely insecure about your finances.
*   *Financial security* is an important field for the coach to understand an individual's financial stability.
*   Please be honest when recording your financial security.
*   Record your financial security based on how you feel currently.
*   If you are feeling financially insecure, please take a moment to reflect on why and how you are feeling this way.
*   Please take a moment to reflect on how you can work to improve your financial stability.
* **Top financial goals**: Briefly describe your top financial goals.
*   *Top financial goals* is used to collect information about an individual's financial aspirations.
*   *Briefly describe* your top financial goals to ensure accurate data collection.
*   Please be honest when recording your financial goals.
*   Record your financial goals based on your actual goals and priorities.
*   *Top financial goals* is an important field for the coach to understand an individual's financial aspirations.
*   If you have multiple financial goals, please describe all that apply.
*   If you have no financial goals, type 'None'.
*   Please take a moment to reflect on why and how you are feeling this way.
* **Primary area to address**: Choose the dimension that is highest priority for you, from Body to Finances.
*   *Primary area to address* is used to identify the most critical dimension to focus on for coaching.
*   *Body* means focusing on physical health and well-being.
*   *Mindset* means focusing on mental health and well-being.
*   *Relationships* means focusing on personal relationships and connections.
*   *Finances* means focusing on financial stability and security.
*   *Primary area to address* is an important field for the coach to understand the individual's priorities.
*   Please be honest when recording your primary area.
*   Record your primary area based on your actual priorities.
*   Please take a moment to reflect on why and how you are feeling this way.
*   *Readiness to change*: Choose how ready you are to start making changes, from Not Ready to Actively Changing.
*   *Readiness to change* is used to gauge an individual's willingness to start making changes.
*   *Not ready* means not feeling ready to start making changes.
*   *Considering* means considering making changes, but not ready yet.
*   *Ready* means feeling ready to start making changes.
*   *Actively changing* means actively making changes.
*   *Readiness to change* is an important field for the coach to understand an individual's willingness to start making changes.
*   Please be honest when recording your readiness to change.
*   Record your readiness to change based on how you feel currently.
*   If you are not feeling ready to change, please take a moment to reflect on why and how you are feeling this way.
*   Please take a moment to reflect on how you can start making changes.
*   *Additional comments*: Leave any additional comments or coach notes for further discussion.
*   *Additional comments* is an open-ended field for coaches to gather more information.
*   Please be honest when recording your comments.
*   Record your comments based on your actual observations and feelings.
*   *Additional comments* is an important field for the coach to understand the individual's thoughts and feelings.
*   If you have additional comments, please type them in the text field.
*   If you have no comments, type 'None'.
