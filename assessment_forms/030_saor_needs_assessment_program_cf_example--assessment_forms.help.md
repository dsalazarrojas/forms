# saor_needs_assessment_program_cf_example - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and assessment information from clients who have completed the program. It includes fields for personal details and program completion information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone Number".
5. Answer the assessment questions in the field labeled "Needs Assessment Questions".
6. Provide your answers to the questions in the field labeled "Questions and Answers".
7. If applicable, enter the Date Completed for when you finished the program.
8. If applicable, enter the Time Completed for when you finished the program.
9. If applicable, enter the name of who completed the program in the field labeled "Completed by".
10. Provide any comments about your experience in the field labeled "Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Needs Assessment Questions** (`text`, required/optional): Answer the assessment questions provided.
* **Questions and Answers** (`text`, required/optional): Provide answers to the questions posed.
* **Date Completed** (`date`, required/optional): If you've completed the program, enter the date you finished.
* **Time Completed** (`time`, required/optional): If you've completed the program, enter the time you finished.
* **Completed by** (`text`, required/optional): If you've completed the program, enter the name of who finished the program.
* **Comments** (`text`, required/optional): Any comments or feedback about your experience with the program.

Note: Some fields are required while others are optional, depending on your experience with the program.
