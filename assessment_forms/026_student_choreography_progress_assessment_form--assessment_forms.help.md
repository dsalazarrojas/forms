# Student Choreography Progress Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the progress of a student's choreography skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID to identify the student being assessed.
2. Choose the level of proficiency the student has reached in the current dance session.
3. Select the dance skills the student has demonstrated during the assessment.
4. (Optional) Provide any evaluation criteria that the student meets or exceeds.
5. (Optional) Enter any comments or feedback about the student's performance.
6. (Optional) Enter any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
7. Choose the date the assessment was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (student_id, number, Required)**: Enter the unique student ID to identify the student being assessed.
* **Level (level, text, Required)**: Choose the level of proficiency the student has reached in the current dance session. This could be a description, such as "Beginner", "Intermediate", "Advanced", etc.
* **Dance Skills (dance_skills, select_multiple, Required)**: Select the dance skills the student has demonstrated during the assessment. These will be specific dance styles or techniques that the student has mastered.
* **Evaluation Criteria (evaluation_criteria, text, Optional)**: (Optional) Enter any evaluation criteria that the student meets or exceeds. For example, "Good balance", "Good rhythm", "Good technique", etc.
* **Comments (comments, text, Optional)**: (Optional) Enter any comments or feedback about the student's performance.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments, text, Optional)**: (Optional) Enter any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Assessment Date (assessment_date, date, Required)**: Choose the date the assessment was completed.

## Tips
* Ensure that the student's ID is unique and accurate.
* Be clear and concise in the comments and evaluation criteria.
* Use specific dance skills and techniques when selecting from the options.
* Make sure to choose the correct date the assessment was completed.
