# Stock Valuation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Valuation Assessment form is used to estimate the current value of a stock based on multiple criteria. This form is designed to help users assess and evaluate the value of a stock.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Company Name field with the name of the stock you are assessing.
2. Select the Risk Level of the stock from the options provided (High, Medium, Low).
3. Choose the Valuation Method to be used (Financial, Operational, Strategic, Technical).
4. Enter the Stock Price of the stock.
5. Enter the Expected Return of the stock.
6. Enter the Risk Free Rate of the stock.
7. Enter the Dividend Yield of the stock.
8. Enter the Beta Factor of the stock.
9. Enter the Debt Equity Ratio of the stock.
10. Enter the Financial Leverage of the stock.
11. Enter the Operational Leverage of the stock.
12. Enter the Management Efficiency of the stock.
13. Enter the Growth Potential of the stock.
14. Enter the Stock Value on a specific date.
15. Select the Valuation Period (Month, Quarter, Six-Month, One-Year).
16. Select the Valuation Frequency (Quarterly, Semiannually, Annually).
17. Select the Valuation Basis (Market, Book, Net Asset).
18. Enter the Stock Symbol.
19. Enter the Valuation Basis Date.
20. Enter the Stock Identifier.
21. Select the Investment Objective (Speculative, Growth, Income, Balanced).
22. Select the Investment Horizon (Short-Term, Mid-Term, Long-Term).
23. Select the Investment Risk Tolerance (High, Medium, Low).
24. Select whether Risk Reversal is True or False.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the stock you are assessing.
* **Risk Level**: Select the risk level of the stock (High, Medium, Low).
* **Valuation Method**: Choose the method to be used for the valuation (Financial, Operational, Strategic, Technical).
* **Stock Price**: Enter the current stock price.
* **Expected Return**: Enter the expected return of the stock.
* **Risk Free Rate**: Enter the risk-free rate of the stock.
* **Dividend Yield**: Enter the dividend yield of the stock.
* **Beta Factor**: Enter the beta factor of the stock.
* **Debt Equity Ratio**: Enter the debt equity ratio of the stock.
* **Financial Leverage**: Enter the financial leverage of the stock.
* **Operational Leverage**: Enter the operational leverage of the stock.
* **Management Efficiency**: Enter the management efficiency of the stock.
* **Growth Potential**: Enter the growth potential of the stock.
* **Stock Value**: Enter the stock value on a specific date.
* **Valuation Period**: Select the valuation period (Month, Quarter, Six-Month, One-Year).
* **Valuation Frequency**: Select the valuation frequency (Quarterly, Semiannually, Annually).
* **Valuation Basis**: Select the valuation basis (Market, Book, Net Asset).
* **Stock Symbol**: Enter the stock symbol.
* **Valuation Basis Date**: Enter the date for the valuation basis.
* **Stock Identifier**: Enter the stock identifier.
* **Investment Objective**: Select the investment objective (Speculative, Growth, Income, Balanced).
* **Investment Horizon**: Select the investment horizon (Short-Term, Mid-Term, Long-Term).
* **Investment Risk Tolerance**: Select the investment risk tolerance (High, Medium, Low).
* **Risk Reversal**: Select whether risk reversal is true or false.
