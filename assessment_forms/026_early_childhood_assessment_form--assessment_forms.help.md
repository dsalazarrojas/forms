# Early Childhood Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Early Childhood Assessment Form is used to collect information about a child's developmental status and assessment details.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
2.  Enter the parent's contact information in the "Parent Contact Information" field.
3.  Enter the child's birth date in the "Birth Date" field.
4.  Select the assessment date from the calendar.
5.  Choose the child's developmental stage (e.g., Infant, Toddler, Preschool, School Aged) in the "Developmental Stage" field.
6.  Select the area of observation (e.g., Cognitive, Motor, Language, Social) in the "Observation Area" field.
7.  Enter any additional observation notes in the "Observation Notes" field.
8.  Choose the developmental status (e.g., Normal, Delayed, Advanced) in the "Developmental Status" field.
9.  Select the action planned for the child's development in the "Action Planned" field.
10. Enter the name of the person who conducted the assessment in the "Assessment By" field.
11. Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
12. Enter the child's ID number.
13. Enter the parent's name.
14. Enter the parent's email address.
15. Enter the parent's phone number.
16. Enter the assessor's name.
17. Enter the assessor's phone number.
18-25. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Child Name** (1): Enter the child's name to identify them in our database.
*   **Parent Contact Information** (2): Enter the parent's contact information so we can get in touch with them.
*   **Birth Date** (3): Enter the child's birth date to ensure accurate assessment.
*   **Assessment Date** (4): Select the date when the assessment was conducted.
*   **Developmental Stage** (5): Choose the child's developmental stage to understand their current level of development.
*   **Observation Area** (6): Select the area of observation to focus on specific aspects of the child's development.
*   **Observation Notes** (7): Enter any additional notes about the observation.
*   **Developmental Status** (8): Choose the child's developmental status to understand their current level of development.
*   **Action Planned** (9): Select the action planned for the child's development.
*   **Assessment By** (10): Enter the name of the person who conducted the assessment.
*   **Notes** (11): Enter any additional notes about the assessment.
*   **Child ID** (12): Enter the child's ID number for accurate identification.
*   **Parent Name** (13): Enter the parent's name.
*   **Parent Email** (14): Enter the parent's email address.
*   **Parent Phone** (15): Enter the parent's phone number.
*   **Assessor Name** (16): Enter the assessor's name.
*   **Assessor Phone** (17): Enter the assessor's phone number.
*   **Notes1-8** (18-25): Enter any additional notes about the assessment or child's development.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Please complete the form for the correct child.
*   Please check for duplicate entries before submitting the form.
*   Please contact our team if you have any questions or need further assistance.
