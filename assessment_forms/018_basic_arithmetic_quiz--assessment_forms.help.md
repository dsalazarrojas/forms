# Basic Arithmetic Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Basic Arithmetic Quiz is a form designed to assess a student's basic arithmetic skills. It covers addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division operations, as well as word problems. The form is divided into six sections: student information, addition problems, subtraction problems, multiplication problems, division problems, and word problems. The quiz is meant to help identify areas where a student needs improvement in their basic arithmetic skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Basic Arithmetic Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information section with your name and ID (if applicable).
2. Answer each arithmetic problem in the corresponding section (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division).
3. For each problem, enter the answer to the question.
4. Provide additional notes or comments in the "Additional notes or comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID, if applicable.
* **Class section** (`class_section`, text, optional): Enter your class section, if applicable.
* **Quiz date** (`quiz_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the quiz.
* **What is 7 + 5?** (`addition_q1`, number, required): Add 7 and 5.
* **What is 23 + 15?** (`addition_q2`, number, required): Add 23 and 15.
* **What is 156 + 243?** (`addition_q3`, number, required): Add 156 and 243.
* **What is 12 - 3?** (`subtraction_q1`, number, required): Subtract 3 from 12.
* **What is 45 - 17?** (`subtraction_q2`, number, required): Subtract 17 from 45.
* **What is 500 - 237?** (`subtraction_q3`, number, required): Subtract 237 from 500.
* **What is 6 x 4?** (`multiplication_q1`, number, required): Multiply 6 by 4.
* **What is 9 x 7?** (`multiplication_q2`, number, required): Multiply 9 by 7.
* **What is 12 x 15?** (`multiplication_q3`, number, required): Multiply 12 by 15.
* **What is 20 divided by 4?** (`division_q1`, number, required): Divide 20 by 4.
* **What is 56 divided by 8?** (`division_q2`, number, required): Divide 56 by 8.
* **What is 144 divided by 12?** (`division_q3`, number, required): Divide 144 by 12.
* **If Sarah has 15 apples and gives away 6, how many does she have left?** (`word_problem_1`, number, required): Solve the word problem as described.
* **A book costs $12 and a pen costs $3. What is the total cost?** (`word_problem_2`, number, required): Solve the word problem as described.
* **Additional notes or comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your answers.
