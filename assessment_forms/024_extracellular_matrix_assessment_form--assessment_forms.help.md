# Extracellular Matrix Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Extracellular Matrix Assessment Form is used to assess the status of extracellular matrices in various environments. This form helps to evaluate the presence, condition, and frequency of different matrices, and to track their impact on the environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the assessment location, date, and time to ensure they are accurate.
2. Select the primary goal of the assessment (if applicable).
3. List the key performance indicators (KPIs) to be evaluated (Invasive Species, Biodiversity, Ecosystem Health).
4. Choose the frequency at which the assessment will be performed (optional).
5. Indicate the status of the assessment (Active, Passive).
6. Identify the assessor (User, Admin).
7. Note any additional information about the assessment.
8. Enter the email contact information (if applicable).
9. Enter any notes about the assessment.
10. Specify the next action to be taken.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Primary Goal of the Assessment (1)**: The primary goal of the assessment. Enter a brief description of the reason for the assessment.
- **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) (2)**: Choose one or more of the KPIs that will be evaluated during this assessment.
  - Invasive Species: This indicator monitors the presence of non-native species that can harm native species or ecosystems.
  - Biodiversity: This indicator measures the variety and health of native species present in the assessment area.
  - Ecosystem Health: This indicator assesses the overall health of the ecosystem, including factors like water quality and soil health.
- **Assessment Location (3)**: The location where the assessment will take place.
- **Assessment Date (4)**: The date on which the assessment will be performed.
- **Assessment Time (5)**: The time at which the assessment will be performed.
- **Contact Information (6)**: Your name and contact information (phone number, email, or other contact method).
- **Email (7)**: Your email address for follow-up contact.
- **Note (8)**: Any additional comments or notes about the assessment.
- **Assessment Status (9)**: Select the status of the assessment (Active, Passive).
  - Active: The assessment is ongoing and actively monitoring the extracellular matrix.
  - Passive: The assessment is not ongoing, but rather a historical or one-time evaluation.
- **Assessor (10)**: The person or group responsible for the assessment (User, Admin).
- **Assessment Frequency (11)**: The frequency at which the assessment will be performed. Enter the interval (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).
- **Next Action (12)**: Any additional action that needs to be taken as a result of this assessment.
