# Productive Systems Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Productive Systems Assessment is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of your organization's productivity systems, tools, and processes. It helps to identify areas of improvement, track progress, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Introduction
Start by reading through the form carefully to understand the questions and fields that follow. 

### Step 2: Fill out the form fields according to the instructions and guidelines provided.

### Step 3: Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your company.
- **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of team members involved in your team, such as Team Lead, Project Manager, or Team Member.
- **Productivity Tools** (`productivity_tools`, select_one, optional): Select a productivity tool that you use in your work, like Excel, Google Drive, or Microsoft Office.
- **Tool Satisfaction** (`tool_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your satisfaction with the tools used in your work. 
  - Scale: 
    - 1-10
  - Description: Rate your satisfaction level with the tool, with 1 being the least satisfied and 10 being the most satisfied.
- **Process Satisfaction** (`process_satisfaction`, text, optional): Describe your satisfaction with the process.
- **Department Satisfaction** (`department_satisfaction`, select_multiple, optional): Choose departments where you feel the most satisfied, such as Marketing, Sales, or Customer Service.
- **Continuous Improvement** (`continuous_improvement`, note, optional): Write down any areas for improvement or feedback.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the submission date and time when you complete this form.
  - Format: MM/DD/YYYY
- **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the submission time when you complete this form.
  - Format: HH:MM AM/PM
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you, like Chatjimmy or Other.
- **Company Rating** (`company_rating`, number, optional): Rate your company performance on a scale of 1-10.
- **Team Rating** (`team_rating`, number, optional): Rate your team performance on a scale of 1-10.
- **Department Rating** (`department_rating`, number, optional): Rate your department performance on a scale of 1-10.
- **Team Lead Rating** (`team_lead_rating`, number, optional): Rate your team lead performance on a scale of 1-10.
- **Team Member Rating** (`team_member_rating`, number, optional): Rate your team member performance on a scale of 1-10.
