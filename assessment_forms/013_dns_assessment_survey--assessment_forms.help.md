# DNS Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form aims to assess your organization's current status in the realm of DNS management practices. It helps us understand your team's level of DNS security readiness, satisfaction with DNS infrastructure, and procedures for managing DNS changes. This information will be used to identify areas for improvement and provide recommendations for enhancing your organization's DNS practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each page carefully and answer the questions according to your organization's current practices.
* Select the options that best describe your answers for each question.
* Enter any additional information or details in the text fields provided.
* Submit the form once you have completed all the pages.
