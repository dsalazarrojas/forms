# Rigging Supervisor Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rigging Supervisor Assessment form is designed to evaluate the expertise and qualifications of a rigging supervisor, capturing their safety performance, certifications, skills, experience, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Page** (`rigging_supervisor_form_1`): Please enter your brief overview of your work experience and qualifications as a rigging supervisor.
2. **Rigging Certifications** (`rigging_certifications`): Select one of the following certifications:
	* Certification 1
	* Certification 2
	* Certification 3
	* Certification 4
3. **Safety Performance** (`safety_performance`): Rate your safety performance from 1 to 5, where 1 is "Needs improvement" and 5 is "Excellent".
4. **Rigging Skills** (`rigging_skills`): Select all the skills that apply to your experience as a rigging supervisor:
	* Skill 1
	* Skill 2
	* Skill 3
5. **Supervisor Experience** (`supervisor_experience`): Enter the number of years of experience you have as a rigging supervisor.
6. **Rigging Qualifications** (`rigging_quals`): Select one of the following qualifications:
	* Qual 1
	* Qual 2
	* Qual 3
7. **First Aid Certification** (`first_aid`): Enter your first aid certification number.
8. **Note** (`rigging_supervisor_form_8`): Please provide any additional information or comments about your experience and qualifications.
