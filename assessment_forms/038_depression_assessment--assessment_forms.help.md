# Depression Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The depression assessment form is a tool used to gather information from patients about their mental health status, particularly regarding depression. This form helps healthcare professionals assess and document a patient's symptoms, medical history, and treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the patient's identifier from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the patient's name.
3. Enter the patient's date of birth.
4. Enter the date of the last visit.
5. Describe the reason for the visit.
6. Describe the patient's chief complaint.
7. Record the patient's history of illness.
8. Record the patient's physical examination results.
9. Note any laboratory results.
10. List the patient's current medication.
11. Describe the patient's mental status.
12. Record the patient's depression assessment.
13. Document the patient's suicidal tendency, plans, and status.
14. Note any hospitalization.
15. Create a treatment plan for the patient.
16. Enter the follow-up date.
17. Sign and date the form as the patient.
18. Sign and date the form as the healthcare provider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_identifier**: Select the patient's identifier from the dropdown menu. This field is not required.
* **name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **date_of_last_visit**: Enter the date of the last visit in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **reason_for_visit**: Describe the reason for the current visit.
* **chief_complaint**: Describe the patient's chief complaint.
* **history_of_illness**: Record the patient's history of illness.
* **physical_exam**: Record the results of the patient's physical examination.
* **laboratory**: Note any laboratory results.
* **medication**: List the patient's current medication.
* **mental_status**: Describe the patient's mental status.
* **depression_assessment**: Record the patient's depression assessment.
* **suicidal_tendency**: Describe the patient's suicidal tendency.
* **suicidal_plans**: Describe the patient's suicidal plans.
* **suicidal_attempts**: Describe the patient's suicidal attempts.
* **suicidal_status**: Describe the patient's suicidal status.
* **hospitalization**: Note if the patient has been hospitalized.
* **treatment_plan**: Create a treatment plan for the patient.
* **follow_up_date**: Enter the follow-up date in the MM/DD/YY format.
* **signature**: Sign and date the form as the patient.
* **provider_signature**: Sign and date the form as the healthcare provider.

Note: All dates should be entered in the MM/DD/YY format.
