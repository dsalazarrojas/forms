# Relationship Self Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your romantic relationship, including your relationship type, status, and dynamics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of relationship you are in or have been in.
2. Choose your relationship status.
3. Enter your partner's name.
4. Select your partner's gender.
5. Enter the duration of your relationship.
6. Enter your partner's age.
7. Select your partner's employment status.
8. Choose your relationship satisfaction level.
9. Select your relationship expectations.
10. Enter your partner's employment education level.
11. Enter other relationship expectations.
12. Select the type of conflict that has occurred in your relationship.
13. Enter the frequency of conflicts.
14. Enter the duration of conflicts.
15. Select the method of conflict resolution.
16. Enter other conflict resolution methods.
17. Choose your relationship health.
18. Select your relationship expectations health.
19. Select your partner's employment status health.
20. Select your partner's employment education health.
21. Select the frequency of conflicts health.
22. Select the resolution health.
23. Select your relationship expectations health 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Relationship Type**: Select the type of relationship you are in or have been in.
	+ Relationship: Romantic relationship.
	+ Friendship: Friendships are close relationships between two people.
	+ Family: Family relationships.
	+ Romantic: Romantic relationships.
	+ Other: Other.
* **Relationship Status**: Choose your relationship status.
	+ Single: Not in a relationship.
	+ In a relationship: In a romantic relationship.
	+ Engaged: Engaged to be married.
	+ Married: Married.
	+ In a long-term partnership: In a long-term partnership.
	+ Separated: Separated from your partner.
	+ Divorced: Divorced.
	+ Widowed: Widowed.
	+ Other: Other.
* **Partner Name**: Enter your partner's name.
* **Partner Gender**: Select your partner's gender.
	+ Male: Male partner.
	+ Female: Female partner.
	+ Other: Other (e.g. non-binary, transgender).
* **Relationship Duration**: Enter the duration of your relationship.
* **Partner Age**: Enter your partner's age.
* **Partner Employment Status**: Select your partner's employment status.
	+ Employed: Employed.
	+ Unemployed: Unemployed.
	+ Student: Student.
	+ Retired: Retired.
	+ Disabled: Disabled.
	+ Other: Other.
* **Relationship Satisfaction**: Choose your relationship satisfaction level.
	+ Satisfied: Satisfied with your relationship.
	+ Neutral: Neutral about your relationship.
	+ Unsatisfied: Unsatisfied with your relationship.
* **Relationship Expectations**: Select your relationship expectations.
	+ Communication: Communication is important in your relationship.
	+ Emotional Support: Emotional support is important in your relationship.
	+ Financial Support: Financial support is important in your relationship.
	+ Intimacy: Intimacy is important in your relationship.
	+ Other: Other expectations.
* **Partner Employment Education**: Select your partner's employment education level.
	+ High School: High school graduate.
	+ College: College graduate.
	+ Some College: Some college education.
	+ Bachelor's: Bachelor's degree.
	+ Master's: Master's degree.
	+ Doctoral: Doctoral degree.
	+ Other: Other.
* **Other Expectations**: Enter other relationship expectations.
* **Conflict Type**: Select the type of conflict that has occurred in your relationship.
	+ Communication: Communication conflict.
	+ Financial: Financial conflict.
	+ Intimacy: Intimacy conflict.
	+ Emotional: Emotional conflict.
	+ Physical: Physical conflict.
	+ Other: Other.
* **Conflict Frequency**: Enter the frequency of conflicts.
* **Conflict Duration**: Enter the duration of conflicts.
* **Conflict Resolution**: Select the method of conflict resolution.
	+ Resolved: Conflicts were resolved.
	+ Pending: Conflicts are still pending resolution.
	+ Not Resolved: Conflicts were not resolved.
* **Conflict Resolution Method**: Select the method of conflict resolution.
	+ Talking: Talking to resolve conflicts.
	+ Writing: Writing to resolve conflicts.
	+ Other: Other methods of conflict resolution.
* **Other Conflict Resolution Method**: Enter other conflict resolution methods.
* **Relationship Health**: Choose your relationship health.
	+ Good: Healthy relationship.
	+ Fair: Fair relationship.
	+ Poor: Poor relationship.
* **Relationship Expectations Health**: Select your relationship expectations health.
	+ Satisfied: Healthy relationship expectations.
	+ Neutral: Neutral relationship expectations.
	+ Unsatisfied: Unhealthy relationship expectations.
* **Partner Employment Status Health**: Select your partner's employment status health.
	+ Employed: Healthy employment status.
	+ Unemployed: Unhealthy employment status.
	+ Student: Healthy employment status as a student.
	+ Retired: Healthy employment status as retired.
	+ Disabled: Healthy employment status as disabled.
	+ Other: Other employment status.
* **Partner Employment Education Health**: Select your partner's employment education health.
	+ High School: Healthy employment education as a high school graduate.
	+ College: Healthy employment education as a college graduate.
	+ Some College: Healthy employment education as some college graduate.
	+ Bachelor's: Healthy employment education as a bachelor's degree holder.
	+ Master's: Healthy employment education as a master's degree holder.
	+ Doctoral: Healthy employment education as a doctoral degree holder.
	+ Other: Other employment education.
* **Partner Employment Education Health 2**: Select your partner's employment education health 2.
* **Conflict Frequency Health**: Select the frequency of conflicts health.
	+ Less than once a month: Healthy conflict frequency.
	+ Once a month: Unhealthy conflict frequency.
	+ Several times a month: Unhealthy conflict frequency.
	+ Once a week: Unhealthy conflict frequency.
	+ More than once a week: Unhealthy conflict frequency.
* **Conflict Resolution Health**: Select the resolution health.
	+ Resolved: Conflicts were resolved.
	+ Pending: Conflicts are still pending resolution.
	+ Not Resolved: Conflicts were not resolved.
* **Relationship Expectations Health 2**: Select your relationship expectations health 2.
* **Tips**

Remember to answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability. This form is designed to help you assess your relationship dynamics and identify areas for improvement.
