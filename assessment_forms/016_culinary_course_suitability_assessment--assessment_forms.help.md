# Culinary Course Suitability Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us understand your background, experience, and preferences to determine the best culinary course for you. Please complete this form to ensure you get the most suitable and enjoyable learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form sections one by one.
2. Answer each question truthfully, and make sure to provide as much detail as you can.
3. When asked to choose from multiple options, select all that apply.
4. If you have any additional information that is not covered in this form, use the `Notes` section to provide more details.
5. When you have completed the form, you will have the opportunity to review your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospective Student**: Enter a brief description of yourself as a prospective student.
* **Experience Level**: Choose the level of experience you have with cooking or food preparation:
	+ None: No prior experience with cooking or food preparation.
	+ Basic: Some basic cooking skills, but not much experience.
	+ Intermediate: Moderate experience with cooking and food preparation.
	+ Advanced: Significant experience with cooking and food preparation.
	+ Master: Extensive experience with cooking and food preparation.
* **Learning Needs**: Choose the topics you are interested in learning about or need improvement:
	+ Cooking Techniques: Cooking techniques such as knife skills, cooking methods, etc.
	+ Knife Skills: Developing skills in using knives in the kitchen.
	+ Food Safety: Food safety and handling techniques.
* **Availability**: Estimate how many hours per week you have available for studying.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Notes**: Any additional information you would like to share about your experience, skills, or preferences.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter your assigned tool or equipment (if applicable).
* **Form ID**: This field is used for internal reference purposes.
* **Category**: Enter the category of culinary course you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Description**: Enter a short description of what you hope to achieve or learn through this course.
* **Output File**: This field is used for internal purposes, please do not fill it.
