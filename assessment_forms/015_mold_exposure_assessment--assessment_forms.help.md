# Mold Exposure Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about mold exposure in a property. It helps assess the presence and severity of mold growth in a given property, and guides you through the process of gathering essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Property Details, including any relevant information about the property.
2. Provide the Property Location where the property is situated.
3. Describe any concerns or issues related to the occupants of the property, such as health problems or complaints.
4. Select the Property Type (Apartment, House, or Office) and confirm the property's age.
5. Enter the occupant's details, including Last Name, First Name, and Email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details (1)**: This field is optional and allows you to enter a brief description of the property.
* **Property Location (2)**: Enter the address or location of the property.
* **Occupant Concerns (3)**: Describe any health concerns or issues related to the property occupants.
* **Property Type (4)**: Select the type of property (Apartment, House, or Office) from the dropdown list. Note that this field is optional.
* **Occupant Details (5)**, **Last Name (6)**, **First Name (7)**, and **Email (8)**: These fields are optional and allow you to enter the occupant's personal information.
* **Property Type (9)**: This field is a duplicate of field 4 and allows you to select the type of property.
* **Property Age (10)**: Enter the age of the property, which is optional.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information to facilitate a thorough assessment.
* Double-check the fields for completeness and correctness before submitting.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, do not hesitate to seek assistance.
