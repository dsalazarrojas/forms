# Business Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Business Needs Assessment Form, designed to help us better understand your organization's needs and challenges. Please follow these steps to ensure accurate and helpful information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing accurate information about your business.
2. Be specific when answering questions, as this will help us better understand your organization's context.
3. Review each question carefully to ensure you're providing the most relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required)

* This field is for your business's legal name.

### **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required)

* This field is for the primary contact person for your business.

### **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required)

* Enter your primary contact email address.

### **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required)

* Enter your primary contact phone number.

### **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required)

* Select the primary category your business falls under. Options include: Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Hospitality, Professional services, Education, Non-profit, and Other.

### **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required)

* Enter the number of years your business has been operating.

### **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, number, required)

* Enter the total number of employees in your business.

### **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue`, select_one, required)

* Select your approximate gross revenue range. Options include: Under $500K, $500K to $1M, $1M to $5M, $5M to $10M, $10M to $50M, and Over $50M.

### **Areas Needing Assessment** (`assessment_areas`, select_multiple, required)

* Select all areas you would like to have assessed. Options include: Strategy and planning, Operations, Finance, Marketing and sales, Human resources, Technology, Customer service, Supply chain, and Other.

### **Primary Business Challenges** (`primary_challenges`, select_multiple, required)

* Select your biggest challenges, such as Revenue growth, Profitability, Cash flow, Customer acquisition, Customer retention, Employee retention, Competition, Technology adoption, Process efficiency, and Market expansion.

### **Challenge Description** (`challenge_description`, text, required)

* Provide a detailed description of your main challenge.

### **Business Goals and Objectives** (`goals_objectives`, text, required)

* Define your main business goals and objectives.

### **Timeline for Achieving Goals** (`timeline_for_goals`, select_one, required)

* Select when you aim to achieve these goals. Options include: Within 3 months, Within 6 months, Within 1 year, Within 2 years, and Long-term - 3 plus years.

### **Previous Improvement Efforts** (`previous_improvement_efforts`, select_one, required)

* Select if you have previously tried to address these issues. Options include: Yes - multiple attempts, Yes - one attempt, No - first time addressing.

### **Previous Efforts Description** (`previous_efforts_description`, text, not required)

* Provide a description of any previous efforts you've made to address these issues.

### **Budget for Improvement Initiatives** (`budget_for_improvement`, select_one, not required)

* Select your available budget for improvements. Options include: Under $10K, $10K to $25K, $25K to $50K, $50K to $100K, $100K to $250K, and Over $250K.

### **Stakeholder Buy-in Level** (`stakeholder_buy_in`, select_one, required)

* Select how supportive your leadership is. Options include: Fully committed, Mostly supportive, Somewhat supportive, Neutral, and Resistant.

### **Organizational Change Readiness** (`change_readiness`, select_one, required)

* Select how ready your organization is for change. Options include: Very ready - eager for change, Ready - willing to change, Neutral - open to discussion, Hesitant - prefer status quo, and Resistant - against change.

### **How Will You Measure Success** (`success_metrics`, text, required)

* Define how you will measure the success of these improvements.

### **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, not required)

* Share any other relevant details.
