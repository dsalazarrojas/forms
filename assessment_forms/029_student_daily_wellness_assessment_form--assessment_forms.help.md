# student_daily_wellness_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student daily well-being assessment form is designed to gather information about a student's daily experiences, including their emotional state, physical comfort, and habits. This form helps track and monitor the student's well-being over time, enabling the identification of trends and patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your current emotional state from the list of options on page 1.
2. Choose your current pain level from the list of options on page 2.
3. Indicate your current stress level on page 3.
4. Report your current sleep duration on page 4.
5. Enter your current water intake level on page 5.
6. Answer the question about whether you have eaten today on page 6.
7. Select the number of meals you have eaten on page 7.
8. Indicate whether you have consumed caffeine or alcohol on pages 8 and 9, respectively.
9. Check if you have exercised today on page 10.
10. Enter the time you went to bed and woke up on pages 11 and 12, respectively.
11. Add any additional comments on page 13.
12. Enter your name and email address on pages 14 and 15, respectively.
13. Enter any additional comments on pages 14 and 23-25.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How Are You Feeling**: This question helps track your current emotional state, selecting from: I am feeling great, I am feeling okay, I am feeling not so good, I am feeling miserable.
* **What is your current pain level**: Choose from 0-10, where 0 is low and 10 is high.
* **What is your current stress level**: Select from Low, Medium, High to indicate your stress level.
* **What is your current sleep duration**: Enter the time you went to bed and wake up.
* **What is your current water intake level**: Enter the amount of water you have consumed today.
* **Have you eaten today**: Select True or False.
* **How many meals have you eaten**: Choose from 0-10, the number of meals you have eaten.
* **Have you consumed caffeine today**: Select True or False.
* **Have you consumed alcohol today**: Select True or False.
* **Have you exercised today**: Select True or False.
* **What time did you go to bed**: Enter the time you went to bed.
* **What time did you wake up**: Enter the time you woke up.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or observations.
* **Student name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Student daily well-being assessment form phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or observations.
* **Agreed**: Select True or False if you agree with the assessment.
* **Conducted by**: Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Additional comments 2-5**: Add any additional comments or observations.
