# Clerical Skills Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your clerical skills and experience to provide a comprehensive understanding of your administrative abilities. The form is meant to be completed by individuals seeking to demonstrate their skills and knowledge in various clerical areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate completion, please fill out the form in its entirety. If you are unable to fill out a field, you may leave it blank, and we will make necessary adjustments. Please enter your responses as accurately as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Full Name** (`candidate_name`, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Current Position/Job Title** (`current_position`, optional): Please enter your current position or job title.
- **Date** (`date_of_assessment`, required): Enter the date of this assessment in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Word Processing (e.g. MS Word, Google Docs)** (`word_processing_rating`, required): Rate your proficiency in word processing from 1 (Novice) to 5 (Expert).
- **Spreadsheets (e.g. MS Excel, Google Sheets)** (`spreadsheet_rating`, required): Rate your proficiency in spreadsheets from 1 (Novice) to 5 (Expert).
- **Database Management (e.g. CRM, Access)** (`database_rating`, required): Rate your proficiency in database management from 1 (Novice) to 5 (Expert).
- **Email and Calendar Management** (`email_management_rating`, required): Rate your proficiency in email and calendar management from 1 (Novice) to 5 (Expert).
- **Approximate Typing Speed (WPM)** (`typing_speed_wpm`, required): Enter your approximate typing speed in words per minute (WPM).
- **Data Entry Accuracy** (`data_entry_accuracy`, required): Select the level of accuracy you achieve in data entry: High (99-100%), Moderate (95-98%), or Low (Less than 95%).
- **Professional Phone Etiquette** (`phone_etiquette`, required): Select the level of professionalism you exhibit in phone interactions: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Improvement.
- **Filing and Document Management** (`file_organization`, required): Select the level of competence you have in filing and document management: Expert in digital and physical filing, Competent, or Basic knowledge.
- **Meeting Coordination and Minutes** (`meeting_coordination`, required): Select the level of experience you have in meeting coordination and minutes: Very Experienced, Some Experience, or No Experience.
- **Primary Language** (`primary_language`, required): Enter your primary language.
- **Additional Languages Spoken** (`additional_languages`, optional): Enter any additional languages you speak.
- **Write a Writing Sample or Take a Skills Test?** (`writing_sample_required`, required): Select if you are willing to provide a writing sample or take a skills test.
- **Additional Training Interest** (`additional_training_interest`, optional): Enter areas where you would like more training.
- **Additional Comments** (`comments_footer`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
