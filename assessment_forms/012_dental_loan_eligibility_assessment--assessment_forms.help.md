# Dental Loan Eligibility Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist patients in understanding the Dental Loan Eligibility Assessment form, which is used to gather information necessary for assessing their eligibility for a dental loan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal and contact information (patient_info, email, phone, contact_address, emergency_contact)
2. Share your loan details (loan_details)
3. Choose your financing options (financing_options)
4. Answer questions regarding your loan eligibility (loan_eligibility)
5. Provide any additional information or answers to questions (additional_questions)
6. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have provided accurate and complete information (patient_signature)
7. Provide your signature date and time

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Please provide your personal details including name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
* **Loan Details**: Enter details about the loan you are applying for, including loan amount and term.
* **Financing Options**: Choose your preferred financing options.
* **Loan Eligibility**: Answer questions regarding your eligibility for the loan.
* **Additional Questions**: Provide any additional information or answers to questions that may be relevant to your loan application.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have provided accurate and complete information.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your contact address.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Info**: Provide any insurance information related to your loan.
* **Employment Details**: Enter your employment details, including job title and employer.
* **Income Info**: Enter your income information, including income source and amount.
* **Credit Score**: Enter your credit score.
* **Employment Status**: Choose your employment status.
* **Credit History**: Choose your credit history.
* **Income Source**: Choose your income source.
* **Loan Type**: Choose whether the loan is for a specific purpose (e.g. emergency loan).
* **Loan Amount**: Enter the loan amount.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Loan Term**: Choose your loan term.
* **Credit Inquiries**: Choose whether you have made credit inquiries.
* **Employment Verification**: Choose whether your employment has been verified.
* **Credit History**: Choose whether you have a good or bad credit history.

Note: Some fields may not be required to complete, depending on your situation. Please answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
