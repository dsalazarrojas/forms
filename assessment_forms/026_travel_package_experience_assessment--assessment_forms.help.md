# Travel Package Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from users on their travel package experience. It helps us evaluate and improve our services to provide a better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and sections.
2. Select the appropriate options for each question or provide text responses where required.
3. Submit your feedback at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (rating_page, Select One, Required): Rate your overall experience from "Poor" to "Excellent".
* **What did you enjoy most about the trip?** (section_1, Text, Required): Provide a brief description of what you enjoyed most about the trip.
* **What did you enjoy?** (section_1_1, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the activities you enjoyed most, such as Guided Hikes, Kayak or Canoe Trips, Scenic Flights, or Wildlife Tours. You can also select "Other (please specify)" to provide a different option.
* **What did you least enjoy about the trip?** (section_2, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of what you least enjoyed about the trip.
* **What did you least enjoy?** (section_2_1, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the activities you least enjoyed, similar to the previous field.
* **What type of transportation did you use during the trip?** (section_3, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the transportation methods you used, such as Guided Transportation, Self-Guided Transportation, or Other (please specify).
* **Additional Comments** (comments, Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving the experience.
* **Submission** (submission_page, Text, Optional): You can add any additional information or comments here.
* **Confirmation** (confirmation_page, Text, Optional): This field is for internal use and is not visible to the user.
