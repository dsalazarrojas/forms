# Animation Career Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animation Career Readiness Assessment is a tool to help you assess your readiness for a career in animation. It will guide you through a series of questions to determine your level of proficiency in various aspects of animation, including your primary skills, software proficiency, education, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Select your primary animation skill from the list provided.
3. Check all the industry software you are proficient in.
4. Choose your highest education level achieved.
5. Enter the URL of your online portfolio or demo reel, if applicable.
6. Enter the number of professional projects you have completed.
7. Choose your long-term career goal.
8. Indicate your willingness to relocate for a career in animation.
9. Self-assess your portfolio rating (from 1 to 10).
10. Enter your preferred internship or role start date.
11. (Optional) Enter the name of a professional reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name to help us identify you accurately.
* **Primary Animation Skill**: Select the area of animation that you specialize in, such as 2D Hand-drawn Animation or 3D Modeling and Texturing.
* **Proficiency in Industry Software**: Check all the software you are proficient in, such as Maya, Toon Boom Harmony, or Blender. This will help us understand your technical skills.
* **Highest Education Level**: Choose your highest education level achieved, such as Self-taught, Associate Degree, or Master Degree or PhD.
* **Link to Online Portfolio or Demo Reel**: If you have an online portfolio or demo reel, enter the URL here. This will help us evaluate your creative work.
* **Number of Completed Professional Projects**: Enter the number of professional projects you have completed. This will give us an idea of your experience.
* **Long-term Career Goal**: Choose where you see yourself in 5 years, such as Feature Film Studio or Game Development Studio.
* **Willingness to Relocate**: Indicate your willingness to relocate for a career in animation.
* **Portfolio Rating - Self-assessment**: Self-assess your portfolio rating (from 1 to 10). This will help us evaluate your self-assessment of your skills.
* **Preferred Internship or Role Start Date**: Enter your preferred start date for an internship or role.
* **Professional Reference Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of a professional reference, such as a colleague or mentor.

Tips:

* Be honest in your responses, as the information provided will be used to give you accurate feedback on your readiness for an animation career.
* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and carefully to get the most relevant results.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
