# Special Education Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Needs Assessment Form is used to gather information about a student's special education needs. This form helps identify areas where a student may require additional support to access their education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's ID number.
2. Provide the student's name.
3. Select the student's grade level.
4. Select any special education needs the student may have (speech, learning, emotional, or other).
5. Choose the student's educational goal (reading, writing, math, communication, socialization, or other).
6. Enter the date of the assessment.
7. Select any educational support services the student may require (speech, reading, writing, math, communication, socialization, or other).
8. Enter the name of the teacher who will be working with the student.
9. (Optional) Enter the name of the parent/guardian who will be involved in the student's education.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID (1)**: Enter the student's ID number, as assigned by the school or education authority.
* **Student Name (2)**: Provide the student's full name.
* **Grade Level (3)**: Select the student's current grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Special Education Needs (4)**: Select all applicable special education needs from the list (speech, learning, emotional, or other).
* **Educational Goal (5)**: Choose the area where the student requires extra support (reading, writing, math, communication, socialization, or other).
* **Assessment Date (6)**: Enter the date when the assessment was conducted.
* **Teacher Name (8)**: Enter the name of the teacher who will be working with the student.
* **Parent Name (9)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the parent/guardian who will be involved in the student's education.
* **Student Comments (10)**: Provide any additional comments or information about the student's needs.
