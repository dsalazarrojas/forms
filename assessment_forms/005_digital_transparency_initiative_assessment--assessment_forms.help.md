# Digital Transparency Initiative Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Transparency Initiative Assessment is designed to evaluate your organization's current level of digital transparency and identify areas for improvement. This form helps us understand your organization's practices, gaps, and progress in achieving digital transparency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. For each field, provide a clear and concise answer that accurately reflects your organization's current state.
3. If you're unsure about any question or field, contact your point of contact for guidance.
4. Submit the completed form to proceed with the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are the digital transparency practices your organization uses? (digital_transparency_practices)**: This field is used to gather information about the digital transparency practices your organization currently employs. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
	+ **Yes**: Your organization has implemented this practice.
	+ **No**: Your organization has not implemented this practice.
* **What are the digital transparency gaps your organization needs to address? (digital_transparency_gaps)**: This field is used to identify areas where your organization could improve its digital transparency practices. Please select all that apply from the options provided.
	+ **Yes**: Your organization has this gap and needs to address it.
	+ **No**: Your organization does not have this gap.
* **How would you rate your organization's current progress on digital transparency? (digital_transparency_progress)**: This field is used to rate your organization's current progress on digital transparency, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest.
* **What are your top 3 priorities for digital transparency? (digital_transparency_priority)**: This field is used to identify your organization's top 3 priorities for digital transparency. Please select up to 3 options from the provided list.
	+ **Yes**: This is one of your top 3 priorities.
* **Additional comments (digital_transparency_comments)**: This field is for providing any additional information or comments about your organization's digital transparency practices, gaps, or progress.
* **Your top 3 priorities for digital transparency (digital_transparency_priority)**: This field is used to rate your organization's current progress on digital transparency.

**Tips**
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any question or field, contact your point of contact for guidance.
* Please note that this form is designed to assess your organization's current state and not to assign blame or ratings. The goal is to identify areas for improvement and provide guidance on how to enhance digital transparency.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
