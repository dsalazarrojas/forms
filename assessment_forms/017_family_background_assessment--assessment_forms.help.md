# Family Background Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Background Assessment form is used to collect information about an individual's family background. This information may be used for research or assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form, one page at a time.
2. For each section, read the label and hint carefully to understand what information is being requested.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
4. Use the options or input fields provided for each section to complete the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household members**: A list of names and relationships of individuals living in your household. (text)
* **Family History**: A brief description of your family medical history, including any relevant health conditions or genetic risks. (text)
* **Relationship Status**: The relationship between you and your current or previous partners, including marital status. (text)
* **Sibling Order**: The order of birth among your siblings, with 1 being the eldest. (select_multiple)
* **Sibling or Spouse**: The relationship between you and your sibling or spouse, with options for brother, sister, spouse, or partner. (select_multiple)
* **Family Income**: The total income of your household, including all sources of income. (number)
* **Family Employment**: A description of the employment status of each household member, including occupation, job title, and industry. (text)
* **Family Vehicles**: A list of vehicles owned or used by your household, including make, model, and year. (text)
