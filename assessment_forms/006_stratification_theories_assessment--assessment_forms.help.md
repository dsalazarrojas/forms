# Stratification Theories Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stratification Theories Assessment form is designed to collect information from users about their assessment of stratification theories. The data collected will help us improve our services and understand user perspectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Input your Phone number.
5. Check your Social Media presence.
6. Provide your assessment of Social Stratification Theories.
7. Optionally, add any Comments if you have further thoughts.
8. Set the Submission Date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, false): Check if you have a social media presence.
* **Social Stratification Theories** (`theories`, text, required): Provide your assessment of social stratification theories.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, false): Add any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Set the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required): Set the time of submission.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, false): Optionally, provide a rating (1-10).
