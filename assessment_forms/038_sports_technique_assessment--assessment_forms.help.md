# Sports Technique Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Technique Assessment is a form used to evaluate an athlete's sports technique in a specific sport. It is meant to be used by coaches, instructors, or evaluators to assess an athlete's skills and performance in a structured and comprehensive way.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: athlete's **Athlete Name**, **Technique**, **Sport**, **Level**, **Score**, **Date**, and **Time**.
2. Enter the athlete's **Email** (if applicable).
3. Leave the **Note** field for any additional comments or observations.
4. Leave the **Phone** field if not applicable (only required if the athlete has a phone number).
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter the athlete's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Technique** (`technique`, text, required): Describe the specific sport technique being assessed.
* **Sport** (`sport`, text, required): Select the sport being evaluated (e.g., tennis, basketball, swimming, etc.).
* **Level** (`level`, text, required): Describe the athlete's level of proficiency in the evaluated technique (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced, etc.).
* **Score** (`score`, number, required): Enter a numerical score for the athlete's performance.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or observations about the athlete's performance.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the athlete's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the athlete's phone number if applicable.
