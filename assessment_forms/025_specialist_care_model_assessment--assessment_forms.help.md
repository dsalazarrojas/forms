# Specialist Care Model Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specialist Care Model Assessment form helps evaluate the quality and effectiveness of specialist care services, gathering feedback and ratings on various aspects of care delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

* Review the questions carefully and answer them based on your genuine experiences with specialist care services.
* Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest responses.
* Select multiple options when applicable, using the options provided for each question.
* Leave additional comments if you have any further feedback or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Feedback Given** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Provide any feedback or comments you have received from others about your experiences with specialist care services.
* **Feedback Received** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Describe any feedback or comments you have given to others about their experiences with specialist care services.
* **Care Plan In Use** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often specialist care plans are used during your practice:
	+ `Yes`: The care plan is frequently used.
	+ `No`: The care plan is not used.
* **Assessment Time** (`4`, `time`, required: true): Enter the actual time taken to complete this assessment.
* **Implementation Progress** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Evaluate the progress of implementing specialist care services:
	+ `Excellent`
	+ `Good`
	+ `Fair`
	+ `Poor`
* **Staff Support** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you receive adequate support from staff:
	+ `Excellent`
	+ `Good`
	+ `Fair`
	+ `Poor`
* **Training Provided** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check if specialist care services have provided adequate training:
	+ `Yes`
	+ `No`
* **Staff Involved** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required: false): List the types of staff involved with specialist care services:
	+ `Lead Clinician`
	+ `Care Co-ordinator`
	+ `Other`
* **Communication With Team** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the quality of communication with your team when using specialist care services:
	+ `Excellent`
	+ `Good`
	+ `Fair`
	+ `Poor`
* **Collaboration With Others** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Evaluate the level of collaboration with others when using specialist care services:
	+ `Excellent`
	+ `Good`
	+ `Fair`
	+ `Poor`
* **Evaluation and Improvement** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate the effectiveness of the evaluation process and opportunities for improvement:
	+ `Excellent`
	+ `Good`
	+ `Fair`
	+ `Poor`
* **Additional Comments** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Provide any extra feedback or suggestions about specialist care services.
* **Follow Up** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Mention any follow-up actions or activities related to specialist care services.
