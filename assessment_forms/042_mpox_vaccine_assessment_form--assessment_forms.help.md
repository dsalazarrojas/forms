# mpox_vaccine_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect medical information related to the mpox vaccine, including patient demographics, medical history, and vaccine administration details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the fields carefully to ensure you are completing the form for the correct purpose and not duplicating information.
2. Fill out each field in the following order: patient demographics, medical history, vaccine administration details, vaccine recommendations, and additional information.
3. Make sure to review the field-by-field explanations below for more information on each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Info** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and other relevant demographic information.
- **mpox_vaccine_history** (2, text, required/optional): Record the patient's mpox vaccine history, including previous vaccinations and any adverse reactions.
- **Symptoms** (3, text, required/optional): List any symptoms the patient is experiencing related to the mpox vaccine.
- **Medical Conditions** (4, select_one, required/optional): Select any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact the patient's ability to receive the mpox vaccine.
  - Idiopathic: A condition that is not caused by a known factor.
  - Immunocompromised: A condition that weakens the immune system.
  - Pregnancy: The patient is pregnant.
  - Other: Select this option if the patient has a different medical condition.
- **Vaccine Availability** (5, number, required/optional): Enter the availability of the mpox vaccine, including the number of doses and schedule for administration.
- **Patient Phone** (6, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Patient Email** (7, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
- **Physician Info** (8, text, required/optional): Record any relevant information from the patient's physician, including medical history and treatment plans.
- **Medical History** (9, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical history, including allergies and previous medical conditions.
- **Allergies** (10, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any allergies the patient has, including Aspirin and Penicillin.
  - Aspirin: The patient is allergic to Aspirin.
  - Penicillin: The patient is allergic to Penicillin.
  - Other: Select this option if the patient has a different allergy.
- **Medical Tests** (11, text, required/optional): Record any relevant medical tests, including lab results and imaging studies.
- **Vaccine Recommendation** (12, select_one, required/optional): Select whether the patient should receive the mpox vaccine.
  - True: The patient should receive the mpox vaccine.
  - False: The patient should not receive the mpox vaccine.
  - Maybe: The decision is uncertain.
- **Vaccine Administered** (13, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether the mpox vaccine has been administered to the patient.
  - True: The mpox vaccine has been administered.
  - False: The mpox vaccine has not been administered.
  - Maybe: The decision is uncertain.
- **Vaccine Adverse Reactions** (14, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any adverse reactions the patient has experienced after receiving the mpox vaccine.
  - True: The patient has experienced an adverse reaction.
  - False: The patient has not experienced an adverse reaction.
  - Maybe: The decision is uncertain.
- **Vaccine Dosage** (15, number, required/optional): Enter the dose of the mpox vaccine administered to the patient.
- **Physician Notes** (16, text, required/optional): Record any relevant notes from the patient's physician.
- **Patient Signature** (17, note, required/optional): The patient's signature is required for this field.
- **Date Completed** (18, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was completed.
- **Time Completed** (19, time, required/optional): Enter the time the form was completed.
- **Date Received** (20, date, required/optional): Enter the date the mpox vaccine was received.
- **Time Received** (21, time, required/optional): Enter the time the mpox vaccine was received.
- **Vaccine Provider** (22, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the vaccine provider.
- **Patient Address** (23, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
- **Patient Phone** (24, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
- **Patient Email** (25, email, required/optional): Enter the patient's email address.
