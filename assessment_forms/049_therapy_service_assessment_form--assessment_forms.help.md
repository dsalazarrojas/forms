# Therapy Service Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a client's mental health and treatment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each question carefully and answer as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2.  Select the correct option from the dropdown menus for questions with options.
3.  Enter a clear and concise answer in the text fields.
4.  If a question is not applicable, select "N/A" or "Not Applicable" to indicate that.
5.  If you have any issues or concerns, please let us know and we will do our best to accommodate your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is your relationship status?**: This question is about your current relationship status. Please select one of the options provided:
    \*   Option 1
    \*   Option 2
    \*   Option 3
*   **How do you feel about your mental health?**: Please provide a clear and concise answer about your mental health situation.
*   **What issues would you like help with?**: This question is about the specific issues you would like to address through therapy. Please provide a brief description of your concerns.
*   **What treatment options have you considered?**: Please select the treatment options you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
*   **How long have you been dealing with this issue?**: This question is about how long you have been experiencing the issue you would like to address.
*   **What days are you available to begin treatment?**: Please select the days you are available to start treatment from the dropdown menu.
*   **Do you have any medical insurance?**: This question is about whether you have medical insurance that covers mental health services.
*   **Do you have an employer that will cover this treatment?**: Please confirm if your employer will cover the cost of treatment.
*   **Would you like to receive follow-up calls?**: This question is about your preference for follow-up calls or communication.
*   **Would you like to be contacted about future services?**: Please let us know if you are interested in being contacted about future services.
*   **How often would you like to receive communication from us?**: Please select from the options provided:
    \*   Daily
    \*   Weekly
    \*   Monthly
*   **What is your preferred contact method?**: This question is about how you would like to be contacted.
*   **Do you have any emergency contacts?**: Please confirm if you have emergency contacts that we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
*   **What are your emergency contacts?**: If you have emergency contacts, please provide their names and numbers.
*   **What is your preferred appointment time?**: This question is about your preferred appointment time.
*   **What day would you like to have your first appointment?**: Please select the day you prefer for your first appointment from the dropdown menu.
*   **Would you like to receive any medication or supplements?**: This question is about whether you are interested in receiving medication or supplements.
*   **What kind of medication or supplements would you like to have?**: If you are interested in receiving medication or supplements, please specify what kind.
*   **What is your preferred method of payment?**: Please select from the options provided:
    \*   Cash
    \*   Credit
    \*   Insurance
*   **What is your estimated annual income?**: This question is about your estimated annual income.
*   **Have you taken any medications in the past 6 months?**: Please confirm if you have taken any medications in the past 6 months.
*   **If yes, what medications are you currently taking?**: If you have taken medications in the past 6 months, please list them.
*   **How many years have you been taking these medications?**: This question is about how long you have been taking the medications.
*   **What is the reason you are taking these medications?**: Please provide a brief description of why you are taking the medications.
*   **Do you take these medications as directed?**: This question is about whether you take the medications as directed by your healthcare professional.
*   **Have you taken any other medications in the past 6 months?**: This question is about whether you have taken other medications in the past 6 months.
*   **If yes, what medications are you currently taking?**: If you have taken other medications, please list them.
*   **How many years have you been taking these medications?**: This question is about how long you have been taking the medications.
*   **What is the reason you are taking these medications?**: Please provide a brief description of why you are taking the medications.
*   **Do you take these medications as directed?**: This question is about whether you take the medications as directed by your healthcare professional.

## Tips

*   Please take your time to answer each question carefully and accurately.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask.
*   We are here to support you and provide personalized care.

This guide will help ensure that you complete the form correctly and provide the necessary information to the service provider. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
