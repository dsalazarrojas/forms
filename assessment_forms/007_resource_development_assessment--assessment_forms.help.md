# Resource Development Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resource Development Assessment form is designed to evaluate the effectiveness and efficiency of resources within a team or organization. It assesses the challenges faced by team and individual members, their responses to these challenges, and the adequacy of resources to address them.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, start by filling out the form fields as instructed. Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
2.  Begin with section 1, "team lead response", and answer the questions as prompted.
3.  Move on to section 2, "team challenges", and enter your team's current challenges.
4.  Section 3, "individual challenges", is where you enter individual team members' personal challenges.
5.  In section 4, "team priorities", list the current priorities of your team.
6.  Section 5, "individual priorities", lists individual priorities.
7.  In section 6, "resource adequacy", select the most relevant response from the provided options or specify other.
8.  Section 7, "resource adequacy other", is for additional information about your response.
9.  Section 8, "improvement plan", outlines actions or measures to address team and individual challenges.
10.  Finally, section 9, "team response", and section 10, "individual response", are open fields for any additional comments or responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team Lead Response (1)
*   **Team Lead Response**: Enter your response as the team leader, explaining the team's current situation and challenges.
*   **Hint**: A brief description of the team's current state and any challenges faced.

### Team Challenges (2)
*   **Team Challenges**: List the team's current challenges and difficulties faced.
*   **Hint**: Be as detailed as possible about the team's problems and obstacles.

### Individual Challenges (3)
*   **Individual Challenges**: List individual team members' personal challenges and difficulties faced.
*   **Hint**: Be as detailed as possible about each member's problems and obstacles.

### Team Priorities (4)
*   **Team Priorities**: Enter the current priorities of your team.
*   **Hint**: List the team's top priorities and any challenges associated with them.

### Individual Priorities (5)
*   **Individual Priorities**: List individual team members' personal priorities.
*   **Hint**: Be as detailed as possible about each member's priorities.

### Resource Adequacy (6)
*   **Resource Adequacy**: Select one of the provided options or specify "Other (please specify)" and provide additional information in the "Resource Adequacy Other" field.
*   **Hint**: Assess the adequacy of resources to address team and individual challenges.

### Resource Adequacy Other (7)
*   **Other (please specify)**: Provide additional information about your response in the "Resource Adequacy" field.
*   **Hint**: Be as detailed as possible about the resource adequacy.

### Improvement Plan (8)
*   **Improvement Plan**: Outline actions or measures to address team and individual challenges.
*   **Hint**: Be as specific and detailed as possible about the improvement plan.

### Team Response (9)
*   **Team Response**: Enter any additional comments or responses.
*   **Hint**: This is an open field for any additional comments or remarks.

### Individual Response (10)
*   **Individual Response**: Enter any additional comments or responses.
*   **Hint**: This is an open field for any additional comments or remarks.
