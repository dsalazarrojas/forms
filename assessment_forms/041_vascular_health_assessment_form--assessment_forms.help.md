# Vascular Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vascular Health Assessment Form is a tool used to collect information from patients about their health status, medical conditions, and symptoms. This information will help healthcare professionals provide personalized care and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your patient's name and date of birth.
2. Answer the questions regarding your medical condition, symptoms, and other relevant health information.
3. Provide your phone number and email (if applicable).
4. Check the box indicating your consent to share data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Medical Condition**: Select 'Yes' if you have a current medical condition. If you select 'Yes', you will be asked to specify which condition.
* **Symptom 1**: Select the symptom that best describes your current health issue.
* **Symptom 2**: Select the second symptom that you are experiencing.
* **Symptoms 3 (Select All That Apply)**: Select all the symptoms that you are currently experiencing.
* **Consent to Share Data**: Check this box if you consent to share your data with healthcare professionals.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to ask your healthcare professional for clarification.
* The information collected on this form is for the purpose of providing personalized care and will not be shared with any third parties without your consent.
