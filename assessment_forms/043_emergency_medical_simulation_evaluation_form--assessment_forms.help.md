# Emergency Medical Simulation Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide feedback and assessment on individual or team performance in medical simulations, helping identify areas of improvement and recognition of strengths.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. For each field, choose the most accurate option that reflects your assessment of the performance.
3. If you are unsure of any options, use the "Other" or "Note" fields to provide more information.
4. Be thorough in your assessment, but concise in your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Medical Simulation Evaluation Form** (`emergency_medical_simulation_evaluation_form`, text, required: false): This is the title of the form, and should not be filled out by the evaluator. It is a reference field for internal use.
* **Simulation Details** (`simulation_details`, select_one, required: false): Answer "Yes" if the simulation was conducted as part of a training program or "No" if it was a regular simulation. 
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose all team members who were part of the simulation, including doctors, nurses, and other medical professionals.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose how the simulation was assessed, whether verbally, in writing, or practically.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_one, required: false): Choose how well the simulation was performed on a scale of "Pass", "Fail", or "Needs Improvement".
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the simulation.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the simulation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the simulation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email of the person being evaluated, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person being evaluated, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or organization who should be contacted in case of an emergency.

This form is designed to be easy to understand and fill out. If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to your supervisor or instructor for guidance.
