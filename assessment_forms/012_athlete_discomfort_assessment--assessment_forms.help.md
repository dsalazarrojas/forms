# Athlete Discomfort Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your current physical condition, specifically focusing on areas of discomfort or pain you may be experiencing. This will help our team provide you with the most accurate and effective guidance on how to manage and alleviate your symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your full name and date of assessment.
2. Identify your primary sport or activity you are currently participating in.
3. Select the most affected area of your body where you are experiencing discomfort.
4. Describe the specific location and any other details about your discomfort.
5. Indicate which side of your body is affected.
6. Rate your pain intensity on a scale from 0 to 10.
7. Select the types of pain sensation(s) you are experiencing.
8. Choose when your discomfort is most noticeable.
9. If applicable, select whether your discomfort has a sudden or gradual onset.
10. Specify how long you have been experiencing this discomfort.
11. Indicate if you have had a previous injury to the affected area.
12. Select how this discomfort affects your training.
13. Provide any treatments you have already tried.
14. Indicate if you need a follow-up with medical staff.
15. Add any additional comments or information about your condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Record the date you are completing this form.
* **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`, text, required): Identify the sport or activity you are currently participating in.
* **Primary Area of Discomfort** (`discomfort_area`, select_one, required): Select the most affected area of your body experiencing discomfort.
* **Specific Location Description** (`specific_location`, text, required): Describe the exact location and any other details about your discomfort.
* **Side of Body** (`body_side`, select_one, required): Indicate which side of your body is affected.
* **Pain Intensity Score** (`pain_intensity`, select_one, required): Rate your pain intensity on a scale from 0 (no pain) to 10 (worst possible pain).
* **Type of Pain Sensation** (`pain_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all pain types you are experiencing (e.g., sharp or stabbing, dull or aching, etc.).
* **When is the Discomfort Most Noticeable** (`pain_timing`, select_multiple, required): Choose when your discomfort is most noticeable (e.g., during physical activity, immediately after, at rest, etc.).
* **Onset of Discomfort** (`onset_type`, select_one, required): Select whether your discomfort had a sudden or gradual onset.
* **Duration of Discomfort** (`duration_present`, text, required): Specify how long you have been experiencing this discomfort.
* **Previous Injury** (`previous_history`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had a previous injury to the affected area.
* **Impact on Training** (`training_impact`, select_one, required): Select how your discomfort affects your training (e.g., no impact, minor reduction, etc.).
* **Treatments Tried** (`treatments_tried`, text, optional): Describe any treatments or remedies you have already tried to alleviate your discomfort.
* **Follow-up Needed** (`followup_needed`, select_one, required): Indicate if you feel you need a follow-up with medical staff.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your condition.
