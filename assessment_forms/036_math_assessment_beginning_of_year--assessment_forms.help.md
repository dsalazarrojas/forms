# Math Assessment Beginning Of Year - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Math Assessment Beginning Of Year form is a tool used to evaluate students' understanding of math facts at the beginning of the year. It helps teachers identify areas where students need additional support and tracks progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each student at the beginning of the year.
2. Enter the student's name, grade level, and date of birth.
3. Assess the student's understanding of addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division facts.
4. Record the number of correct answers and number of problems answered correctly.
5. Indicate if the student is new or not.
6. Record the time it took to complete the test (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student's Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their official record.
* **Number of Correct Answers**: Enter the total number of correct answers out of the number of questions answered.
* **Does student understand addition facts?** (Yes/No): Check "Yes" if the student demonstrates understanding of addition facts.
* **Does student understand subtraction facts?** (Yes/No): Check "Yes" if the student demonstrates understanding of subtraction facts.
* **Grade Level**: Enter the student's current grade level.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Current Grade Level**: Enter the student's current grade level.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the student's contact information (email address).
* **Number of problems answered correctly**: Enter the total number of problems answered correctly.
* **Time of Test Completion**: Record the time it took to complete the test (if applicable).
* **Does student understand multiplication facts?** (Yes/No): Check "Yes" if the student demonstrates understanding of multiplication facts.
* **Does student understand division facts?** (Yes/No): Check "Yes" if the student demonstrates understanding of division facts.
* **Is new student?** (Yes/No): Check "Yes" if the student is new to your institution.
