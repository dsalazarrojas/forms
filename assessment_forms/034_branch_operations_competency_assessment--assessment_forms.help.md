# Branch Operations Competency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Branch Operations Competency Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the skills and proficiency of employees in various areas of branch operations. It helps to identify strengths and weaknesses, provide feedback, and support professional development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest answers.
2. Choose the corresponding level of proficiency for each section from the provided options.
3. Provide specific examples or details for training needs, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the employee being assessed.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Provide a personnel identification number for the employee.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required): Enter the current role or job title of the employee.
* **Branch Location** (`branch_location`, text, required): Specify the branch where the employee works.
* **Years in Current Role** (`years_in_role`, number, required): Enter the duration the employee has been in their current position.
* **Years with Company** (`years_with_company`, number, required): Enter the total tenure of the employee with the company.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the assessment.
* **Knowledge of Branch Processes** (`process_knowledge`, select_one, required): Choose the level of proficiency in understanding procedures: 
  * **Expert**: Demonstrates exceptional knowledge of branch processes.
  * **Proficient**: Has a good understanding of branch processes.
  * **Developing**: Needs improvement in understanding branch processes.
  * **Basic**: Basic knowledge of branch processes.
  * **Minimal**: Lack of understanding in branch processes.
* **Compliance and Policy Adherence** (`compliance_adherence`, select_one, required): Choose the level of adherence to company standards:
  * **Excellent**: Consistently follows company standards.
  * **Good**: Mostly follows company standards.
  * **Satisfactory**: Meets company standards.
  * **Below average**: Fails to meet company standards.
  * **Poor**: Frequently disregards company standards.
* **Customer Service Skills** (`customer_service`, select_one, required): Choose the level of customer service skills:
  * **Excellent**: Demonstrates exceptional customer service skills.
  * **Good**: Has good customer service skills.
  * **Satisfactory**: Meets customer service expectations.
  * **Below average**: Fails to meet customer service expectations.
  * **Poor**: Frequently fails to meet customer service expectations.
* **System and Software Proficiency** (`system_proficiency`, select_one, required): Choose the level of proficiency in technology:
  * **Expert**: Demonstrates exceptional proficiency in technology.
  * **Proficient**: Has good proficiency in technology.
  * **Developing**: Needs improvement in technology.
  * **Basic**: Basic knowledge of technology.
  * **Minimal**: Lack of proficiency in technology.
* **Data Entry and Accuracy** (`data_accuracy`, select_one, required): Choose the level of accuracy in record keeping:
  * **Excellent**: Demonstrates exceptional accuracy in record keeping.
  * **Good**: Has good accuracy in record keeping.
  * **Satisfactory**: Meets accuracy expectations.
  * **Below average**: Fails to meet accuracy expectations.
  * **Poor**: Frequently fails to meet accuracy expectations.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration** (`teamwork`, select_one, required): Choose the level of teamwork and collaboration skills:
  * **Excellent**: Demonstrates exceptional teamwork skills.
  * **Good**: Has good teamwork skills.
  * **Satisfactory**: Meets teamwork expectations.
  * **Below average**: Fails to meet teamwork expectations.
  * **Poor**: Frequently fails to meet teamwork expectations.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication`, select_one, required): Choose the level of communication skills:
  * **Excellent**: Demonstrates exceptional communication skills.
  * **Good**: Has good communication skills.
  * **Satisfactory**: Meets communication expectations.
  * **Below average**: Fails to meet communication expectations.
  * **Poor**: Frequently fails to meet communication expectations.
* **Identified Training Needs** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Provide specific areas for improvement or development. 

## Tips
* Answer questions honestly and accurately to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
* Review the assessment for completeness before submitting.
* Use specific examples to support your answers, if possible.
* This form is for evaluation purposes only; your responses will not be used for any other reason.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [support contact].
