# Course Creator Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Creator Readiness Assessment form is designed to evaluate a course creator's readiness in terms of years of experience, marketing strategy, target audience, course creation process, course launch strategy, lead qualification, and overall readiness score. This form helps course creators assess their strengths and weaknesses, providing a comprehensive evaluation to inform their growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing each field and selecting the most accurate response**. Ensure you answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. **Complete all the required fields**, which are indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
3. **Choose your responses carefully**, as they will directly impact your readiness score.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Creator Readiness Assessment Form**: This field is the title of the form, indicating that this is the main assessment form.
* **Years of Experience**: Please select the number of years you have been creating courses. This field helps assess your experience level.
* **Marketing Strategy**: Please choose the most suitable marketing strategy for your courses:
	+ option 1: Focus on social media advertising
	+ option 2: Leverage email marketing
	+ option 3: Use influencer partnerships
* **Target Audience**: Please select all the target audience types that apply to your courses:
	+ option 1: Professionals
	+ option 2: Students
	+ option 3: Entrepreneurs
* **Course Creation Process**: Please choose the course creation process that best describes your workflow:
	+ option 1: Linear process (create a content calendar)
	+ option 2: Agile process (iterative and flexible)
	+ option 3: Hybrid process (mix of linear and agile)
* **Course Launch Strategy**: Please choose the course launch strategy that best suits your needs:
	+ option 1: Simultaneous launch (launch all at once)
	+ option 2: Phased launch (launch in batches)
	+ option 3: Dynamic launch (launch as needed)
* **Lead Qualification**: If you have a lead qualification process, please select the date of your last successful lead qualification. Otherwise, select "N/A".
* **Course Creator Readiness Score**: This field displays your calculated readiness score based on your responses. You will not be able to edit this field.

Note: The options in each field are not exhaustive, so choose the most accurate response for your situation. 

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as they will directly impact your readiness score.
* Review the form carefully before submitting, ensuring all fields are completed accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification.
