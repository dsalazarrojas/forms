# Respiratory Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the respiratory system of a patient, gathering information on their medical history, symptoms, vital signs, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide a brief medical history in the "Medical History" field.
3. Describe the patient's current symptoms in the "Current Symptoms" field.
4. Enter the patient's respiratory rate in the "Respiratory Rate" field.
5. Enter the patient's oxygen saturation percentage in the "Oxygen Saturation" field.
6. Select the patient's vital signs from the options provided in the "Vital Signs" field.
7. Describe the results of the lung exam in the "Lung Exam" field.
8. If applicable, list the patient's current medication in the "Medication List" field.
9. If applicable, list the patient's allergies in the "Patient Allergies" field.
10. Describe the patient's respiratory system in the "Respiratory System" field.
11. Enter the date of the last dose of any medication in the "Date of Last Dose" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required):
Enter the patient's name.
* **Medical History** (text, required):
Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, treatments, or allergies.
* **Current Symptoms** (text, required):
Describe the patient's current symptoms, including any respiratory-related issues.
* **Respiratory Rate** (number, required):
Enter the patient's respiratory rate, usually measured in breaths per minute.
* **Oxygen Saturation** (number, required):
Enter the patient's oxygen saturation percentage, usually measured in a range from 0 to 100%.
* **Vital Signs** (select_one, required):
Select the patient's vital signs from the options provided:
	+ Normal
	+ Tachycardic (elevated heart rate)
	+ Tachypneic (elevated respiratory rate)
	+ Bradycardic (low heart rate)
	+ Bradypneic (low respiratory rate)
* **Lung Exam** (text, required):
Describe the results of the lung exam, including any abnormalities or concerns.
* **Patient Condition** (select_multiple, optional):
Select the patient's condition from the options provided:
	+ Well
	+ Stable
	+ Unwell
	+ Critical
* **Medication List** (text, optional):
List the patient's current medication, including any allergies or concerns.
* **Patient Allergies** (text, optional):
List the patient's allergies, including any medication or substance allergies.
* **Respiratory System** (text, required):
Describe the patient's respiratory system, including any concerns or issues.
* **Date of Last Dose** (date, required):
Enter the date of the last dose of any medication.

## Tips
Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, as it will help healthcare professionals make informed decisions about the patient's care. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for guidance or clarification.
