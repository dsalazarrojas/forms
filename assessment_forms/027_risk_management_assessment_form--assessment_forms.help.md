# Risk Management Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Risk Management Assessment Form is used to identify, assess, and manage risks that may impact your organization's objectives and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and relevance of each field.
2. Fill out the form page by page, ensuring accuracy and consistency in your responses.
3. For multiple-choice fields, select only one option that best describes your answer.
4. For text fields, provide clear and concise answers that accurately reflect your risk assessment.
5. Ensure you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Risk Management Risk Level Scale
* **Risk Management Risk Level Scale** (`risk_management_risk_level_scale`, select_one, **Required**): Select the level of risk that best describes the risk.
	+ **Low**: The risk is minimal and unlikely to occur.
	+ **Medium**: The risk is moderate and has some potential for impact.
	+ **High**: The risk is significant and has a potential for serious impact.
	+ **Critical**: The risk has severe and potentially catastrophic consequences.

### Risk Management Review Frequency
* **Risk Management Review Frequency** (`risk_management_review_frequency`, select_one, **True**): Select how frequently the risk should be reviewed.
	+ **Monthly**: Review the risk monthly.
	+ **Quarterly**: Review the risk every three months.
	+ **Semi-Annually**: Review the risk every six months.
	+ **Annually**: Review the risk once a year.

### Risk Management Risk Probability Scale
* **Risk Management Risk Probability Scale** (`risk_management_risk_probability_scale`, select_one, **Required**): Select the probability of the risk occurring.
	+ **Low**: The risk is unlikely to occur.
	+ **Medium**: The risk is moderately probable.
	+ **High**: The risk is highly probable.
	+ **Critical**: The risk is almost certain to occur.

### Risk Management Risk Impact Scale
* **Risk Management Risk Impact Scale** (`risk_management_risk_impact_scale`, select_one, **Required**): Select the potential impact of the risk.
	+ **Low**: The impact is minimal.
	+ **Medium**: The impact is moderate.
	+ **High**: The impact is significant.
	+ **Critical**: The impact is severe and far-reaching.

### Risk Management Review Comment
* **Risk Management Review Comment** (`risk_management_review_comment`, text, **False**): Provide comments or notes about the risk review process.
