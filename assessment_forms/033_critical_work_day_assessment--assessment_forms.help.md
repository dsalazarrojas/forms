# Critical Work Day Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Work Day Assessment form is designed to collect information about the critical aspects of a workday, including risk factors, impact factors, and resource requirements. This information helps identify and mitigate risks, allocate resources effectively, and provide support where needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter Work Day Details**: In the "Work Day Details" field, describe the key events, tasks, and activities that occurred during the workday.
2. **Select Date**: Choose the date for which you are assessing the workday.
3. **Assess Risk Factors**: For each risk factor (High, Medium, Low), select all that apply to the workday.
4. **Calculate Impact Factor**: Enter a numerical value to represent the impact of the risk factors on the workday.
5. **Specify Resource Requirements**: If additional resources are needed for the workday, enter the number of resources required.
6. **Specify Support Requirements**: If additional support is needed, select the method of support required (e.g., None, Email, Phone, In Person).
7. **Criticality Rating**: Enter a numerical value to represent the criticality of the workday.
8. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the workday.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Work Day Details**: Enter a brief description of the key events, tasks, and activities that occurred during the workday.
* **Date**: Enter the date for which you are assessing the workday.
* **Risk Factor**: Select all risk factors that apply to the workday, where:
	+ High: High-risk activities or events occurred during the workday.
	+ Medium: Medium-risk activities or events occurred during the workday.
	+ Low: Low-risk activities or events occurred during the workday.
* **Impact Factor**: Enter a numerical value to represent the impact of the risk factors on the workday.
* **Resource Requirements**: If additional resources are needed for the workday, enter the number required.
* **Support Requirements**: If additional support is needed, select the method of support required.
* **Criticality Rating**: Enter a numerical value to represent the criticality of the workday.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the workday.
