# Sleep And Stress Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you track your daily sleep patterns and stress levels. By completing this form regularly, you can gain insights into your sleep habits and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. For the questions about sleep frequency, quality, stress levels, and wake times, please answer using the numbers provided.
3. If you have trouble with the time fields, please format the time as 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
4. Fill out the form every day to track changes in your sleep patterns and stress levels over time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **how_often_do_you_sleep** (sleep_frequency, number, required: false)
	+ How often do you sleep in a typical day? Please answer using the numbers provided.
* **on_a_scale_of_10** (stress_level, number, required: false)
	+ On a scale of 1-10, with 1 being not at all stressed and 10 being extremely stressed, how would you rate your stress level today?
* **how_well_do_you_sleep** (sleep_quality, number, required: false)
	+ On a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very poorly and 10 being very well, how would you rate the quality of your sleep tonight?
* **how_long_do_you_slept** (sleep_duration, number, required: false)
	+ How many hours did you sleep tonight?
* **what_time_do_you_wake_up** (wake_time, time, required: false)
	+ What time did you wake up this morning?
* **how_long_have_you_felt_stressed** (stress_duration, number, required: false)
	+ How many days have you felt stressed?
* **how_often_do_you_feel_stressed** (stress_frequency, number, required: false)
	+ How often do you feel stressed?
* **how_stressed_did_you_feel_yesterday** (stress_level_1, number, required: false)
	+ On a scale of 1-10, with 1 being not at all stressed and 10 being extremely stressed, how stressed did you feel yesterday?
* **how_well_did_you_sleep_last_night** (sleep_quality_1, number, required: false)
	+ On a scale of 1-10, with 1 being very poorly and 10 being very well, how well did you sleep last night?
* **what_time_did_you_wake_up_last_night** (wake_time_1, time, required: false)
	+ What time did you wake up last night?
