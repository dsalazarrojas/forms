# Childhood ADHD Collateral Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childhood ADHD Collateral Questionnaire is a tool used to gather information about a child's behavior, performance, and potential symptoms of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD). This form is completed by caregivers or teachers who have had regular contact with the child.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each section and question carefully before answering.
- Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- Use the provided options for select fields or type your response if it's not listed.
- Complete the form for each child being evaluated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the child being evaluated.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the caregiver or teacher completing this form.
* **Relationship to Child** (`relationship_to_child`, select_one, required): Choose the relationship you have with the child (e.g., Parent, Teacher, Guardian, Other).
* **Attention Concerns** (`attention_concerns`, text, required): Describe any difficulties the child has with attention or focus.
* **Hyperactivity Observations** (`hyperactivity_observations`, text, required): Note any observed hyperactivity or impulsivity in the child.
* **Behavior in Class** (`behavior_in_class`, select_one, required): Choose how the child typically behaves during lessons and group activities (Appropriate, Occasionally Distracted, Frequently Distracted, Disruptive).
* **School Performance** (`school_performance`, select_one, required for completion, optional for others): Choose the child's academic performance (Above Average, Average, Below Average, Significantly Below Average).
* **Interventions Tried** (`interventions_tried`, text, optional): List any strategies or supports already used for the child.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other notes relevant to the assessment.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when describing the child's symptoms or behaviors.
- If you're unsure about a particular question or response, choose the option that best fits your situation.
- You can add any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.
