# kitchen_style_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a kitchen project's style and design preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the project before starting.
2. Review each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
3. Select the options that best fit your project's needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (Page 1, Not Required): This page provides a brief overview of your project.
* **Project Details** (Page 2, Required):
	+ **Project Details**: Select one of the available options to describe your project.
* **Project Budget** (Page 3, Optional): If applicable, enter the budget for your project.
* **Preferred Layouts** (Page 4, Required):
	+ **Preferred Layouts**: Select the layouts that best fit your project's needs.
* **Preferred Furniture Styles** (Page 5, Required):
	+ **Preferred Furniture Styles**: Select the styles that best fit your project's needs.
* **Kitchen Size** (Page 6, Optional): If known, enter the size of your kitchen.
* **Budget** (Page 7, Optional): If applicable, enter the budget for your project.
* **Email** (Page 8, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Page 9, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Project Start Date** (Page 10, Optional): If known, enter the start date for your project.
* **Expected Project Completion Date** (Page 11, Optional): If known, enter the expected completion date for your project.
* **Contact Person** (Page 12, Optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Showroom Location** (Page 13, Optional): Enter the showroom location.
* **Showroom Phone Number** (Page 14, Optional): Enter the showroom phone number.
* **Showroom Email** (Page 15, Optional): Enter the showroom email.
* **Showroom Website** (Page 16, Optional): Enter the showroom website.
