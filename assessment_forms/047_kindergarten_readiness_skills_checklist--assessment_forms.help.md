# Kindergarten Readiness Skills Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help teachers and administrators assess a child's readiness for kindergarten. It gathers information on the child's developmental skills, such as language, social, and cognitive abilities, to determine their suitability for kindergarten.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the observation date from the calendar.
2. Enter the child's name.
3. Enter the child's age in years.
4. Answer whether the child's parent is present during the observation.
5. Answer whether the teacher observed the child's skills.
6. Enter the name of the child's school or preschool program.
7. Enter the teacher's name and contact information.
8. Enter the classroom name.
9. Answer whether the child's teacher provided input on their readiness.
10. Enter the child's kindergarten readiness level and score.
11. Enter any additional comments or notes about the child's readiness.
12. Answer whether the child is ready for kindergarten.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Observation Date** (`observation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the child's name.
* **Age** (`age_in_years`, number, required): Enter the child's age in years.
* **Parent Given** (`parent_given`, select_one, required): Answer whether the child's parent is present during the observation.
* **Teacher Observed** (`teacher_observed`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether the child's teacher observed their skills.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the child's school or preschool program name.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Classroom Name** (`classroom_name`, text, required): Enter the classroom name.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Classroom Phone** (`classroom_phone`, text, optional): Enter the classroom phone number.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Given** (`teacher_given`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether the child's teacher provided input on their readiness.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the child's parent email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter the child's parent phone number.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the child's readiness.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the teacher or administrator.
* **School Comments** (`school_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the school.
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the teacher.
* **Kindergarten Readiness** (`kindergarten_readiness`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the child's readiness.
* **Kindergarten Readiness Level** (`kindergarten_readiness_level`, select_one, required): Select the child's readiness level (High or Low).
* **Kindergarten Readiness Score** (`kindergarten_readiness_score`, number, required): Enter the child's readiness score.
* **Kindergarten Readiness Date** (`kindergarten_readiness_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the child's readiness assessment.
* **Kindergarten Readiness Comments** (`kindergarten_readiness_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the child's readiness.
* **Kindergarten Readiness Score Scale** (`kindergarten_readiness_score_scale`, select_one, required): Select the child's readiness score scale (Yes or No).
