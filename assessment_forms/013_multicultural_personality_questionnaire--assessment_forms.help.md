# Multicultural Personality Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multicultural Personality Questionnaire is a tool designed to assess an individual's cultural background, experience, and personality characteristics in a multicultural context. The form aims to gather information on a respondent's cultural identity, social norms, and personality traits to provide insights into their cultural background and behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page carefully.
2. Answer the questions in the text fields by providing detailed descriptions or short answers.
3. Select the option that best fits your cultural background and experience.
4. Submit your answers by selecting one of the options at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the Multicultural Personality Questionnaire?** (`intro_question`, text, required): This is the introduction to the form, providing a brief explanation of what the questionnaire is and what it aims to achieve.
* **Cultural Background** (`cultural_background`, text, required): This field is designed to collect information about your cultural background, such as your country of origin, cultural identity, or cultural influences.
* **Experience Abroad** (`experience_abroad`, text, required): This field is intended to gather information about your experiences abroad, including travel, education, work, or other significant events that have shaped your cultural background.
* **Cultural Identity** (`cultural_identity`, text, required): This field is meant to explore your self-identification as a member of a particular culture or cultural group, and how you perceive your cultural identity.
* **Social Norms** (`social_norms`, text, required): This field is focused on understanding your perceptions of social norms and values within your cultural context, including expectations and behaviors.
* **Personality Insights** (`personality_insights`, text, required): This field is designed to collect information about your personality traits, such as your values, attitudes, and behaviors.
* **Multicultural Personality Questionnaire** (`multicultural_personality_questionnaire`, text, required): This is a repeat of the previous question, intended to ensure that your answers are consistent and accurate.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, select_one, required): Select one of the options provided to submit your answers and complete the form.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* Ensure that your answers are accurate and consistent throughout the form.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
