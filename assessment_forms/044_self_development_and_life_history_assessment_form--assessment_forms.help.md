# self_development_and_life_history_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and personal details for assessment purposes. It will help us better understand your background and experiences, which will aid in providing tailored support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with accurate and relevant information. If you need help with any field or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1, text, optional): A field where you can enter your name as it is written in official documents, such as your passport or ID.
* **Birth Date** (2, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Gender** (3, select_one, optional): Choose your gender from the provided options: Female, Male.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any contact purposes.
* **Address** (5, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **Country** (6, text, optional): Enter the country where you reside.
* **Region** (7, text, optional): Enter the region or state where you reside.
* **City** (8, text, optional): Enter the city where you live.
* **Postal Code** (9, text, optional): Enter your postal or zip code.
* **Email** (10, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields carefully to ensure the most accurate assessment.
- If any field is blank, please provide any available information about it.
- Double-check the provided information before submission to avoid mistakes.
