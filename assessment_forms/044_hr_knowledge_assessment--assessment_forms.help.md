# HR Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Knowledge Assessment form is used to evaluate the knowledge and skills of employees or team members regarding HR-related topics. Completing this form ensures that users have the necessary knowledge to perform their job tasks effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number in the respective fields.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the assessment questions (Questions 1, 2, and 3).
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Assigned to" and "Assigned Tool" questions.
4. Optionally, you can add notes in the "Notes" field.
5. Set the date and time when the assessment was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name**: Enter your first name.

### Last Name
• **Last Name**: Enter your last name.

### Email
• **Email**: Enter your email address.

### Phone Number
• **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.

### HR Knowledge Assessment
• **HR Knowledge Assessment**: Answer "Yes" if you have the necessary HR knowledge, otherwise select "No".

### Question 1
• **Question 1**: Answer "Yes" if you are familiar with the relevant HR topic, otherwise select "No".

### Question 2
• **Question 2**: Select all the relevant answers you are familiar with for the HR topic.

### Question 3
• **Question 3**: Select all the relevant answers you are familiar with for the HR topic.

### Score
• **Score**: Enter your score out of 10.

### Notes
• **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your assessment.

### Date
• **Date**: Select the date the assessment was completed.

### Time
• **Time**: Select the time the assessment was completed.

### Assigned to
• **Assigned to**: Answer "Yes" if you are the correct person assigned to this assessment, otherwise select "No".

### Assigned Tool
• **Assigned Tool**: Answer "Yes" if you used the correct tool for this assessment, otherwise select "No".

Note: This guide is a general guidance and the actual fields may vary based on your company's requirements.
