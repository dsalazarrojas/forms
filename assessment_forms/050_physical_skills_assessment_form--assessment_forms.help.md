# Physical Skills Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the physical skills of a participant. It collects information about the participant's details, their rating of their own skills, the date of the assessment, and any additional notes that need to be considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's details in the "Participant Details" section.
2. Assess the participant's skills by selecting one of the provided ratings in the "Participant Skill Assessment" section.
3. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" section.
4. If necessary, provide additional comments or notes in the "Assessment Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): Enter the participant's name and any other relevant details.

### Participant Skill Assessment
* **Participant Skill Assessment** (`participant_rating`, select_multiple, required): Rate the participant's skills by selecting one of the provided options. The options are:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Needs Improvement
	+ Unsatisfactory

### Assessment Date
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.

### Assessment Notes
* **Assessment Notes** (`assessment_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes that need to be considered.
