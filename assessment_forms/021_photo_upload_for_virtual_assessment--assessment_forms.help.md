## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Upload a photo of yourself
* This field requires a high-quality image of the person being assessed. The photo should clearly show the person's face and any relevant features.

### Item or Property Name
* This field asks for the full name of the item or property being assessed. This could be a medical condition, an item of clothing, or any other relevant attribute.

### Photo Date
* This field asks for the date the photo was taken. This helps ensure that the assessment is based on a specific moment in time.

### Assessment Date
* This field asks for the date the assessment is being made. This helps track the progress or changes over time.

### Upload more photos
* This field allows uploading additional photos that are relevant to the assessment.

### Description
* This field is a brief description of the item or property being assessed. If you upload additional photos, this field helps to explain the significance of each photo.
