# new_coaching_clients_initial_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_coaching_clients_initial_assessment_form is a form used to gather essential information from new clients to create a strong foundation for the coaching relationship. It is not mandatory, and its completion is at the client's discretion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the New Client Assessment section with a brief description of the client's background, goals, and expectations.
2.  Describe your coach experience in the Coach Experience section.
3.  Provide information about yourself in the New Client Info section.
4.  Select your preferred communication methods in the Communication Preferences section.
5.  Choose your coaching goals in the New Coaching Goals section.
6.  Select the coaching style that best suits your preferences in the Coaching Style section.
7.  Specify your availability in the Client Availability section.
8.  Specify the coach's availability in the Coach Availability section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Client Assessment**: A brief description of the client's background, goals, and expectations.
    + Type: text
    + Required: false
* **Coach Experience**: A description of your coaching experience and qualifications.
    + Type: text
    + Required: false
* **New Client Info**: A description of your personal and professional details.
    + Type: text
    + Required: false
* **Communication Preferences**: Select all your preferred communication methods (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
    + Type: select_multiple
    + Required: false
* **New Coaching Goals**: A description of your coaching goals and objectives.
    + Type: text
    + Required: false
* **Coaching Style**: Select the coaching style that best suits your preferences (e.g., goal-based, process-based, or transformational).
    + Type: select_one
    + Required: false
* **Client Availability**: Specify your availability for coaching sessions.
    + Type: number
    + Required: false
* **Coach Availability**: Specify the coach's availability for coaching sessions.
    + Type: number
    + Required: false

## Tips
* Take your time to complete this form accurately and thoroughly.
* Ensure that you have reviewed the client's consent before sharing this information with the coach.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your coach or support staff.
