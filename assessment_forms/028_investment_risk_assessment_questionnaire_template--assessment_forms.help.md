# Investment Risk Assessment Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand your investment risk tolerance and other key factors that impact your investment strategy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the answer that best fits each question, and move on to the next. You can review and modify your answers as needed at any time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Risk Tolerance** (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`): Select how much risk you are willing to take on with your investments. This will help your investment advisor understand the level of volatility you can handle.

  - Select `High` if you are comfortable with investments that may fluctuate significantly and are willing to take on more risk for potentially higher returns.
  - Select `Medium` if you prefer investments with moderate returns and are willing to take on some risk.
  - Select `Low` if you prioritize stability and predictability over potential returns.

- **Investment Experience** (`text`): Describe your level of experience in investing, including any relevant education or experience.

- **Financial Goals** (`Financial independence`, `Comfortable retirement`, `Financial security`, `Wealth accumulation`): Select all financial goals that apply to you. These goals will help your investment advisor understand your priorities and create a tailored strategy.

  - `Financial independence` - You aim to break free from financial worries and achieve a sense of liberation.
  - `Comfortable retirement` - You want to ensure a steady income stream during your golden years.
  - `Financial security` - You prioritize having a stable and secure financial situation.
  - `Wealth accumulation` - You aim to grow your wealth over time.

- **Time Horizon** (`Short term`, `Medium term`, `Long term`): Select the length of time you expect to hold onto your investments.

  - `Short term` - You plan to sell your investments within a year or less.
  - `Medium term` - You plan to hold onto your investments for several years.
  - `Long term` - You expect to hold onto your investments for an extended period.

- **Income Source** (`Salary`, `Investments`, `Business`, `Other`): Select how you earn your income.

  - `Salary` - You earn a steady income from employment.
  - `Investments` - Your income comes from existing investments.
  - `Business` - Your income comes from a business venture.
  - `Other` - You earn income from another source.

- **Asset Base** (`Stocks`, `Bonds`, `Real estate`, `Other`): Select the type of investments you currently hold.

  - `Stocks` - You own stocks or shares in companies.
  - `Bonds` - You hold bonds or other fixed-income securities.
  - `Real estate` - You own real estate or property.
  - `Other` - You hold other types of investments.

- **Risk Perception** (`number`): Enter a number between 1-10 to rate how sensitive you are to investment risk. The higher the number, the more risk averse you are. Please note that this is a subjective scale and not a precise measurement.

- **Financial History** (`text`): Describe your financial history, including any bankruptcies, foreclosures, or other significant financial events. This will help your investment advisor understand your financial context.

- **Family Status** (`Single`, `Married`, `Divorced`, `Widowed`): Select your family situation.

  - `Single` - You are single and do not have dependents.
  - `Married` - You are married and have a spouse.
  - `Divorced` - You are divorced and do not have dependents.
  - `Widowed` - You are a widow(er) and do not have dependents.

- **Children Status** (`Single`, `Married`, `Divorced`, `Widowed`): Select the status of your children.

  - `Single` - Your children are single and independent.
  - `Married` - Your children are married or in a long-term relationship.
  - `Divorced` - Your children are divorced and do not have dependents.
  - `Widowed` - Your children are widowed and do not have dependents.

## Tips
- Answer the questions as honestly as possible to ensure accurate investment recommendations.
- Be prepared to discuss your answers with your investment advisor.
- This form is not a substitute for professional advice, but rather a tool to help you understand your investment needs.
- You can modify your answers as your financial situation changes over time.
