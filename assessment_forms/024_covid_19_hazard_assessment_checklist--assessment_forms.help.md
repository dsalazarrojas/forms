# Covid 19 Hazard Assessment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the readiness and preparedness of an organization to respond to COVID-19-related incidents in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions according to your knowledge.
2. Select all that apply for each question.
3. Ensure you complete the form in its entirety before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workplace Risks**: Check all areas where potential hazards related to COVID-19 may be present in the workplace. Please select all that apply.
* **Prevention Measures**: Describe any measures in place to prevent the spread of COVID-19, such as hand hygiene practices, distancing protocols, etc.
* **Emergency Response**: Identify the plan in place for responding to a COVID-19-related incident.
* **Personal Risks**: List any personal risks or vulnerabilities you are aware of that may impact your ability to respond to a COVID-19-related incident.
* **Health Status**: Select the current health status of the employee, as it may impact their ability to respond to a COVID-19-related incident.
* **Employee PPE**: Check if personal protective equipment (PPE) is provided and accessible to employees.
* **Workplace PPE**: Check if the workplace is equipped with necessary PPE to reduce the spread of COVID-19.
* **Emergency Contact**: Identify who to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Employee Status**: Select the current status of the employee, as it may impact their ability to respond to a COVID-19-related incident.
* **Emergency Status**: Select all that apply regarding any emergency situations or incidents that have occurred.
* **Location**: Describe the location where the employee is currently working or will be working during a potential COVID-19-related incident.

Note: The fields marked as "select_multiple" and "select_one" require selecting one or multiple options from the list provided. The fields marked as "text" require descriptive answers. Ensure to provide as much detail as needed for each field.
