# Angles and Scales Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Angles and Scales Assessment is designed to evaluate your understanding of angles and scales concepts. This form is a self-assessment tool for students to gauge their knowledge in this subject area.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and select the correct answer for each question.
2. For questions with multiple answers, choose the most accurate answer from the options provided.
3. If you're unsure about any question, you can describe your reasoning in the "Show Your Work" section.
4. Finally, select all areas where you need more practice or review.
