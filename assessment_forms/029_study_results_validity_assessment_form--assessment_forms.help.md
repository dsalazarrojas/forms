# Study Results Validity Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the validity of study results. It is designed to gather information about the quality and accuracy of the study outcomes. The form is divided into multiple sections, each focusing on different aspects of study results validity. It is essential to complete this form carefully and thoroughly to ensure accurate assessment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully and answer all the questions truthfully.
2. For each question, select the appropriate response based on your understanding of the study results.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please refer to the study protocol or consult with the study author.
4. Fill in all the required fields, as indicated by the * symbol.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
