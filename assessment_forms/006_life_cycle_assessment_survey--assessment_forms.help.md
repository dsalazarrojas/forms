# Life Cycle Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your company's products and services across different stages of their life cycle. The goal is to assess your company's processes, products, and services from development to disposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your survey in the "Survey Title" field.
2. Select the category of your product or service.
3. Choose the subcategories related to your product or service.
4. Enter your name and any relevant comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Survey Title (1)
* **Survey Title**: Enter a brief and descriptive title for your survey.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### Category (2)
* **Category**: Select the main category of your product or service (e.g., Category 1).
* Type: Select One
* Required: No

### Subcategory (3)
* **Subcategory**: Choose the subcategories related to your product or service (e.g., Subcategory 1, Subcategory 2, Subcategory 3).
* Type: Select Multiple
* Required: No

### Name (4)
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### Process (5)
* **Process**: Describe the process related to your product or service.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### Product (6)
* **Product**: Enter your product name.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### Location (7)
* **Location**: Enter the location where your product or service will be used.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

### Comments (8)
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or feedback you have about your product or service.
* Type: Text
* Required: No

## Tips

* Ensure that the answers provided are accurate and up-to-date.
* Be specific when describing your product or service.
* If you need help or clarification, contact our support team at form@gic.mx.
