# Onboard Supervision Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Onboard Supervision Assessment is a form designed to evaluate the effectiveness of the onboarding process for new hires under the supervision of a manager/supervisor. It aims to gather feedback on key aspects of the onboarding experience, including new hire experience, supervisor support, communication, training, support resources, overall satisfaction, and feedback on the supervisor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best reflects your experience or feelings.
3. For text fields, provide a brief but clear answer.
4. Make sure to answer all questions, as they are all required.
5. If you need help or clarification on any question, do not hesitate to reach out to the supervisor or contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Introduction
* **Introduction**: This section is a brief overview of your experience with the onboarding process. Please share any relevant details that might help us understand your experience.

*   2. New Hire Experience
*   **New Hire Experience**: How would you describe your overall experience as a new hire in your current role? Please select one of the provided options:
*   Option 1: The onboarding process was clear and well-structured.
*   Option 2: The onboarding process was somewhat unclear, but I managed to figure things out.
*   Option 3: The onboarding process was difficult, and I struggled to understand what was expected of me.
*   Option 4: I was not provided with any onboarding process, and I'm still figuring things out.

*   3. Supervisor Support
*   **Supervisor Support**: How often do you feel supported by your supervisor during the onboarding process? Please select one of the provided options:
*   Option 1: Very often.
*   Option 2: Sometimes.
*   Option 3: Rarely.

*   4. Feedback
*   **Feedback**: Please provide any feedback or comments you may have on the onboarding process, your supervisor, or any other aspect of your experience.

*   5. Onboarding Process
*   **Onboarding Process**: Which of the following best describes your experience with the onboarding process? Please select all that apply:
*   Option 1: Clear and well-structured.
*   Option 2: Somewhat unclear, but I managed to figure things out.
*   Option 3: Difficult, and I struggled to understand what was expected of me.
*   Option 4: There was no onboarding process.

*   6. Communication
*   **Communication**: How would you rate the communication from your supervisor during the onboarding process? Please select one of the provided options:
*   Option 1: Effective and regular.
*   Option 2: Sometimes effective and sometimes irregular.
*   Option 3: Ineffective, but I managed to communicate effectively.
*   Option 4: I received no communication.

*   7. Training
*   **Training**: Please provide any feedback or comments on the training provided to you during the onboarding process.

*   8. Support Resources
*   **Support Resources**: Which of the following support resources were provided to you during the onboarding process? Please select all that apply:

*   9. Overall Satisfaction
*   **Overall Satisfaction**: How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the onboarding process? Please select one of the provided options:
*   Option 1: Very satisfied.
*   Option 2: Somewhat satisfied.
*   Option 3: Neutral.
*   Option 4: Somewhat dissatisfied.
*   Option 5: Very dissatisfied.

*   10. Feedback on Supervisor
*   **Feedback on Supervisor**: Please provide any feedback or comments on your supervisor's performance and how they supported you during the onboarding process.
*   **Tip**: Be as specific and detailed as possible when providing feedback, and include any positive or negative comments.
