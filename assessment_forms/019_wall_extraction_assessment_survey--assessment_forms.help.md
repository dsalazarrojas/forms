# Wall Extraction Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wall Extraction Assessment Survey is a form designed to collect information about the condition and feasibility of removing a wall in a construction or building assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions truthfully to the best of your ability.
2.  Fill out the form with the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **wall_extraction_assessment_survey_form** (1, text, required): This field is the title of the form, and it is required to be filled out. Please provide a brief description of the assessment.
* **wall_condition** (2, select_one, required): This field asks about the condition of the wall you are assessing. Choose one of the available options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **wall_removal_feasibility** (3, select_one, required): This field inquires about the feasibility of removing the wall. Choose one of the available options:
	+ True
	+ False
* **site_conditions** (4, note, required): This field is a note field where you can provide additional information about the site conditions that may affect the wall extraction process.
* **wall_height** (5, number, required): Enter the height of the wall in meters.
* **wall_depth** (6, number, required): Enter the depth of the wall in meters.
* **wall_width** (7, number, required): Enter the width of the wall in meters.
* **wall_length** (8, number, required): Enter the length of the wall in meters.
* **wall_angle** (9, number, required): Enter the angle of the wall in degrees.
* **notes** (10, note, optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments you may have about the wall extraction assessment.
* **wall_removal_method** (11, select_multiple, optional): This field asks about the method of wall removal you would prefer or use. Choose one or more of the available options:
	+ None
	+ Hand
	+ Machine
	+ Both

## Tips
* Please answer the questions accurately and truthfully.
* Make sure to provide the required information for each field.
* You can add additional comments or notes in the "notes" field if you have any further information or concerns.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant experts or team members before submitting the form.
