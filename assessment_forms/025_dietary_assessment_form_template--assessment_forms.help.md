# dietary_assessment_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's dietary habits and medical history to aid medical professionals in assessing their nutritional needs and potential health risks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and ensure you understand each section's purpose and required fields.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please consult with the patient or relevant medical records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (1)** (`patient_information`, text, optional): This section is for providing basic information about the patient, such as name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Dietary History (2)** (`dietary_history`, text, optional): Please describe the patient's dietary history, including any significant changes or patterns.
* **Dietary Restrictions (3)** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): List any dietary restrictions or allergies the patient has, such as vegetarian or vegan diet.
* **Medical Conditions (4)** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions the patient has, such as diabetes or heart disease.
* **Medication List (5)** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies (6)** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any food allergies the patient has, such as peanut allergy.
* **Dietary Intake (7)** (`dietary_intake`, number, optional): Please provide the daily intake of food for the last 3 months.
* **Food Allergies (8)** (`food_allergies`, text, optional): List any food allergies the patient has, such as lactose intolerance.
* **Food Restrictions (9)** (`food_restrictions`, text, optional): List any food restrictions or preferences the patient has, such as gluten-free diet.
* **Medical Conditions List (10)** (`medical_conditions_list`, text, optional): List any medical conditions the patient has, such as high blood pressure.
* **Food Frequency (11)** (`food_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of food intake:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Frequency of Eating (12)** (`frequency_of_eating`, select_one, optional): Choose how often the patient eats:
	+ Always
	+ Sometimes
	+ Never
* **Food Intakes Frequency (13)** (`food_intakes_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose the frequency of food intake:
	+ Once a week
	+ More than once a week
	+ Twice a day
