# Statistical Analysis Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statistical Analysis Report Form is designed to collect information about your research question, data source, and analysis method. This information will help us understand your research objectives and provide better support for your statistical analysis needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the research question in the "What is your research question?" field.
2. Select the type of data you are working with from the "Data type" field.
3. Specify the data source and location from the "Data source" and "Data location" fields.
4. Choose the data format from the "Data format" field.
5. Provide details about the data structure, size, period, and other relevant information using the "Data structure", "Data size", and "Data period" fields.
6. Select the analysis method you will be using from the "Analysis method" field.
7. Choose the statistical analysis type from the "Statistical analysis" field.
8. Select the results format from the "Results format" field.
9. Specify the output format from the "Output format" field.
10. Provide your contact information, including your email, phone number, and address.
11. If applicable, attach a file containing your data.
12. Add any additional comments or notes about your project.
13. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your research question?
* **Research question** (`research_question`, text, optional): This is the main question or problem you are trying to solve with your statistical analysis.

### Data type
* **Data type** (`data_type`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided, or select "Other" to provide a custom option.

### Data source
* **Data source** (`data_source`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable data sources for your research, such as "Data" and/or "Other".

### Data location
* **Data location** (`data_location`, text, optional): Provide the location of your data, such as "Local" or "Cloud".

### Data format
* **Data format** (`data_format`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: CSV, Excel, or "Other".

### Data structure
* **Data structure** (`data_structure`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Matrix, Array, or "Other".

### Data size
* **Data size** (`data_size`, number, optional): Enter the size of your data in the provided field.

### Data period
* **Data period** (`data_period`, date, optional): Enter the period of your data, including date and time.

### Analysis method
* **Analysis method** (`analysis_method`, text, optional): Describe the analysis method you will be using.

### Statistical analysis
* **Statistical analysis** (`statistical_analysis`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Descriptive, Inferential, or "Other".

### Results format
* **Results format** (`results_format`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Table, Chart, or "Other".

### Output format
* **Output format** (`output_format`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: PDF, Excel, Word Document, or "Other".

### Submitter
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter your name or identity.

### Contact info
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.

### Phone number
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Address
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.

### File upload
* **File upload** (`file_upload`, text, optional): If applicable, upload a file containing your data.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your project.

### Form submitted
* **Form submitted** (`form_submitted`, time, optional): This field is automatically populated upon form submission.

### Data description
* **Data description** (`data_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your data.

### Data category
* **Data category** (`data_category`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Research, Business, or "Other".

### Data security
* **Data security** (`data_security`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Secure, Insecure, or "Other".

### Data quality
* **Data quality** (`data_quality`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Good, Poor, or "Other".

### Data completeness
* **Data completeness** (`data_completeness`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Complete, Incomplete, or "Other".

### Data validity
* **Data validity** (`data_validity`, select_one, optional): Choose from the options provided: Valid, Invalid, or "Other".
