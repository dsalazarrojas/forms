# internal_wall_insulation_risk_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the internal wall insulation of a building or property, identifying any potential risks associated with it. The assessment includes details about the surveyor, property, risks, and recommendations for mitigation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the surveyor details (page 1)
2. Add property details (page 2)
3. Choose risk areas (page 3)
4. Provide other risk areas if necessary (page 4)
5. Enter risks and recommendations (page 5)
6. Add comments (page 6)
7. Assign to a team member (page 7)
8. Enter submission details (page 8)
9. Set status and add notes (page 9)
10. Update status and notes (page 10)
11. Record creation details (page 11)
12. Set property type (page 12)
13. Choose building type (page 13)
14. Determine property size (page 14)
15. Select wall construction type (page 15)
16. Choose wall construction materials (page 16)
17. Add surveyor notes (page 17)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Surveyor Details (1)**: This section collects information about the surveyor who is completing the assessment. Enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
* **Property Details (2)**: Provide a brief description of the property being assessed. This will help identify the specific risks associated with internal wall insulation.
* **Risk Areas (3)**: Select the potential risks associated with internal wall insulation from the available options:
	+ Insulation gaps
	+ Poorly fitted or non-existent insulation
	+ Lack of vapour control
	+ Poorly sealed
	+ Dampness or condensation
* **Other Risk Areas (4)**: If there are any additional risk areas not listed above, you can provide them here. This field is optional.
* **Risks (5)**: Describe any other risks you've identified during the assessment.
* **Recommendations (6)**: Provide advice or recommendations for mitigating or addressing the identified risks.
* **Comments (7)**: Add any additional comments or observations about the assessment.
* **Assigned To (8)**: Select the team member who will be assigned to address the risks identified.
* **Submission Details (9)**: Enter your email address and the date and time you submitted the assessment.
* **Status (10)**: Set the status of the assessment as "Incomplete", "Complete", or "In Progress".
* **Notes (11)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the assessment.
* **Risk Assessment Status (12)**: Set the status of the risk assessment as "Incomplete", "Complete", or "In Progress".
* **Property Type (13)**: Select the type of property being assessed (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
* **Building Type (14)**: Choose the type of building being assessed (Flat, Detached, Semi-Detached, Terraced, or Other).
* **Property Size (15)**: Determine the size of the property being assessed (Small, Medium, or Large).
* **Wall Construction Type (16)**: Select the type of wall construction for the property (Solid, Hollow, Composite, or Unknown).
* **Wall Construction Materials (17)**: Choose the materials used in the wall construction of the property (Brick, Block, Concrete, Plasterboard, or Other).
* **Surveyor Notes (18)**: Add any additional notes or comments from the surveyor about the assessment.

Note: All fields marked as "optional" can be left blank unless required for the specific assessment.
