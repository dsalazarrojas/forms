# Software And Device Needs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from teams on their software and device needs to support team performance and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Review each page carefully and ensure you have answered all the relevant questions.
*   Fill out the form with your team's details.
*   If you need help with any section, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, not required): This is a brief introduction to the form. Please take a moment to read it carefully before proceeding.
*   **software** (`software`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to describe any existing software that your team is currently using.
*   **devices** (`devices`, `text`, not required): This field is for you to list any devices your team currently uses.
*   **operating_systems** (`operating_systems`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please choose which operating systems your team uses from the options provided: Good or Fair. This will help us understand your team's operating systems.
*   **hardware** (`hardware`, `text`, not required): If your team has any specific hardware requirements, describe them here.
*   **software_requirements** (`software_requirements`, `text`, not required): If your team has any additional software requirements, describe them here.
*   **hardware_requirements** (`hardware_requirements`, `text`, not required): If your team has any additional hardware requirements, describe them here.
*   **comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any comments or additional information, enter them here.
*   **Department** (`department_0`, `select_one`, not required): This field is for selecting your team's department. Choose the department that best matches your team's area of work.
*   **Department** (`department_1`, `select_one`, not required): This field is for selecting your team's department. Choose the department that best matches your team's area of work.
*   **Team size** (`team_size`, `number`, not required): If you know your team size, enter it here. If not, you can describe your team size in the next field.
*   **Team size** (`team_size_other`, `text`, not required): If you don't know your team size, describe it here.
*   **Manager** (`manager`, `select_one`, not required): Are you the team's manager? If so, mark 'Yes'. If not, mark 'No'.
*   **Manager's name** (`manager_other`, `text`, not required): If you're not the team's manager, enter the name of your manager here.
*   **Location** (`location`, `text`, not required): Enter your work location here.
*   **Location other** (`location_other`, `text`, not required): If your location is not listed, describe your location here.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact information here, if you'd like to be contacted about your submission.
