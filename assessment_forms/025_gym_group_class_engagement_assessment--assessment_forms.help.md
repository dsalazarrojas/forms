# Gym Group Class Engagement Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Group Class Engagement Assessment is designed to evaluate the engagement level of group class participants. This form helps instructors and administrators assess the level of engagement, difficulty, and overall effectiveness of each class. It's a valuable tool for improving group class offerings and providing better experiences for students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Gym Group Class Engagement Assessment, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the `instructor_name` field.
2. Rate the engagement level of the class by selecting one of the following numbers: 1-5
   - 1: Very low engagement
   - 2: Low engagement
   - 3: Medium engagement
   - 4: High engagement
   - 5: Very high engagement
3. Choose the difficulty level of the class by selecting one of the following numbers: 1-5
   - 1: Very easy
   - 2: Easy
   - 3: Medium
   - 4: Hard
   - 5: Very hard
4. Select the class level from the following options: Aerobics, Cardio, Strengthen, Stretching, Balance, Core
5. Choose if equipment is required for the class
6. Enter any additional comments about the class in the `comments` field
7. Enter your email address in the `email` field

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **instructor_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as the instructor of the class.
* **engagement_rating** (Number, Optional): Rate the engagement level of the class (1-5).
* **difficulty_level** (Number, Optional): Rate the difficulty level of the class (1-5).
* **class_level** (Select One, Optional): Choose the class type (Aerobics, Cardio, Strengthen, Stretching, Balance, Core).
* **equipment_required** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose if equipment is required for the class (Yes, No).
* **comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments about the class.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **attendance** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of attendees in the class.
* **attendance_frequency** (Number, Optional): Enter the frequency of attendance in the class.
* **attendance_average** (Number, Optional): Enter the average attendance for the class.
* **engagement_score** (Number, Optional): Enter the engagement score.
* **engagement_frequency** (Number, Optional): Enter the engagement frequency.
* **engagement_average** (Number, Optional): Enter the average engagement for the class.
* **class_type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the class type (Aerobics, Cardio, Strengthen, Stretching, Balance, Core).
* **instructor** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **user_input** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the class.
* **feedback_comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any feedback comments about the class.

## Tips
* Please be honest and objective when answering each question.
* Use numbers to rate the engagement and difficulty levels.
* Choose the class type and whether equipment is required for the class.
* Enter accurate information about the class and your experience as an instructor.
