# Group Class Popularity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences and expectations regarding group classes. This data will help instructors and staff assess the popularity of classes and tailor their offerings to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with your true preferences and expectations.
2. Make sure to answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. If a field is not applicable or you do not have an opinion, please select the "Other" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **User Type** (`user_type`, select_one, optional): Select your user type, either Member, Non Member, or Staff.
* **Class Frequency** (`fitness_class_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select how often you attend classes, either "Yes", "No", or "Other".
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time_of_day`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred time of day to attend classes, either "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Evening".
* **Preferred Day of Week** (`preferred_day_of_week`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred day of the week to attend classes, either "Monday", "Tuesday", "Wednesday", "Thursday", "Friday", "Saturday", or "Sunday".
* **Preferred Duration** (`preferred_duration`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred class duration, either "30min", "45min", "60min", or "90min".
* **Class Demand** (`class_demand`, select_one, optional): Select how you perceive the demand for classes, either "High", "Medium", or "Low".
* **Class Suggested** (`class_suggested`, select_one, optional): Select whether you think the class is suggested, either "True" or "False".
* **Class Revenue** (`class_revenue`, number, optional): Enter your estimate of class revenue.
* **Class Capacity** (`class_capacity`, number, optional): Enter your estimate of class capacity.
* **Member Satisfaction** (`member_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your satisfaction level as a member, either "Very satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **Staff Satisfaction** (`staff_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your satisfaction level as staff, either "Very satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **Class Time of Day** (`class_time_of_day`, select_one, optional): Select the time of day of the class, either "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Evening".
* **Class Day of Week** (`class_day_of_week`, select_one, optional): Select the day of the week the class is held, either "Monday", "Tuesday", "Wednesday", "Thursday", "Friday", "Saturday", or "Sunday".
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, select_one, optional): Select whether the class has a fixed duration, either "Yes", "No", or "Other".
* **Class Frequency** (`class_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select whether the class has a fixed frequency, either "Yes", "No", or "Other".
* **Class Revenue 2** (`class_revenue_2`, number, optional): Enter your estimate of class revenue for the second class.
* **Class Capacity 2** (`class_capacity_2`, number, optional): Enter your estimate of class capacity for the second class.
* **Member Satisfaction 2** (`member_satisfaction_2`, select_one, optional): Select your satisfaction level as a member for the second class, either "Very satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **Staff Satisfaction 2** (`staff_satisfaction_2`, select_one, optional): Select your satisfaction level as staff for the second class, either "Very satisfied", "Neutral", or "Dissatisfied".
* **Member Preferences** (`member_preferences`, note, optional): Enter any additional preferences or expectations as a member.
* **Staff Preferences** (`staff_preferences`, note, optional): Enter any additional preferences or expectations as staff.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
