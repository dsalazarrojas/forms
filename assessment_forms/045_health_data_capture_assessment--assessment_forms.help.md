# Health Data Capture Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form captures basic health information and consent from users in a straightforward manner, aiming to support medical professionals in understanding users' health status and history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's sections carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and relevance of the information requested.
2. Fill in the provided fields with accurate and truthful information. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Health Intake** (health_data_capture_assessment, text, optional): This field allows you to share your overall health status, providing a general overview of your well-being.
* **Lifestyle Insights** (health_lifestyle_insights, text, optional): Share any relevant lifestyle habits, medical history, or other factors that might impact your health.
* **Consent** (health_consent, text, optional): Confirm that you have read and agree with the terms of this assessment, allowing medical professionals to use your data in accordance with their standard practices and guidelines.

## Tips
- Be truthful and accurate when filling out the form to ensure medical professionals have a clear understanding of your health status.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to discuss them with the medical professional administering the assessment.
- This form is intended to support health assessments; if you are completing it for other reasons, please ensure you understand the context and purpose of the form.
