# Narcissistic Behavior Assessment Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Narcissistic Behavior Assessment Quiz aims to assess a person's level of narcissistic behavior. Narcissistic behavior is a personality trait that is characterized by an excessive love for oneself or egocentric tendency. It can manifest in different forms, such as grandiosity, entitlement, and lack of empathy. This quiz is designed to identify and score a person's level of narcissistic behavior based on their tendencies, emotional reactivity, self-concept, social dominance, assertiveness, empathy, self-objectivity, personal growth, self-realism, intimacy, self-regard, and actualization.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Narcissistic Behavior Assessment Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer each one honestly.
2. Choose the options that best describe your tendencies for each question.
3. For select multiple and select one questions, you can choose more than one or just one option respectively.
4. If you're unsure or have questions, ask your administrator for help.

## Tips

* Answer questions honestly and accurately.
* If you're unsure or have questions, ask your administrator for help.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the quiz.
* This quiz is designed to provide an assessment of your narcissistic behavior, not a diagnosis. If you're concerned about your behavior or need professional help, please seek the advice of a mental health professional.
