# Comprehensive Education Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a comprehensive assessment of a student's learning performance in a specific subject area. It captures basic student information, their name, and other relevant details such as their school or program identifier, assessment date, and subject area. Additionally, it evaluates competency levels and provides space for teachers to collect evidence and make comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student name" field.
2. Enter the student's school or program identifier in the "Student id" field.
3. Select the date of the assessment from the calendar in the "Assessment date" field.
4. Choose the subject area being assessed from the provided options in the "Subject area" field.
5. Rate the student's competency levels using the provided scale in the "Competency levels" field.
6. Describe any evidence collected or attach links to supporting materials in the "Evidence collected" field.
7. Enter any observations and recommendations from the teacher in the "Teacher comments" field.
8. Suggest any interventions or resources to support the student's learning in the "Recommended support" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Information
* **-- Student Information --**: This section provides a brief introduction to the student's basic details.
### Student name (Student name)
* **Student name**: Enter the full name of the learner.
### Student id (Student id)
* **Student id**: Enter the school or program identifier for the student.
### Assessment date (Assessment date)
* **Assessment date**: Select the date of the assessment using the calendar.
### Subject area (Subject area)
* **Subject area**: Choose the subject area being assessed from the provided options.
### Competency levels (Competency levels)
* **Competency levels**: Rate the student's competency levels using the provided scale: Advanced, Proficient, Basic, or Below basic.
### Evidence collected (Evidence collected)
* **Evidence collected**: Describe or attach any evidence collected during the assessment.
### Teacher comments (Teacher comments)
* **Teacher comments**: Enter any observations and recommendations from the teacher.
### Recommended support (Recommended support)
* **Recommended support**: Suggest any interventions or resources to support the student's learning.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the provided scale to rate the student's competency levels.
* Attach any supporting evidence to demonstrate the student's performance.
* Keep the "Teacher comments" field concise and focused on suggestions for improvement.
* Consider suggesting relevant resources to support the student's learning in the "Recommended support" field.
