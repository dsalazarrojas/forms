# Academic Exchange Program Impact Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Exchange Program Impact Assessment is a form designed to gather feedback from participants of the exchange program on their experiences, perceptions, and opinions on the program's impact on their academic and personal development. This assessment aims to help improve the program and provide valuable insights for future participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the required fields with your personal information and experiences during the exchange program.
2.  For each field, please provide honest and accurate feedback based on your experiences.
3.  Make sure to select all relevant challenges faced during the exchange program if applicable.
4.  Once you've filled in all the required fields, review your answers for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): This field is for you to provide your full name as a participant of the exchange program. Please make sure to fill it in accurately.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): This field is for you to provide your contact email address for follow-up purposes. Please ensure it's correct and up-to-date.
*   **Home Institution** (`home_institution`, `text`, required): In this field, please enter the name of your university or institution where you are enrolled.
*   **Host Institution** (`host_institution`, `text`, required): Fill in the name of the university or institution where you completed the exchange program.
*   **Duration of Exchange** (`exchange_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your exchange program from the given options (One semester, Two semesters, One academic year, Summer program, Winter program, Other).
*   **Academic Discipline** (`academic_discipline`, `text`, required): In this field, please describe your field of study during the exchange program.
*   **Cultural Integration Experience** (`cultural_integration_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your experience of integrating into the host culture on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means it was very difficult and 5 means it was easy.
*   **Academic Quality** (`academic_quality_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of your academic experience during the exchange on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means it was very poor and 5 means it was excellent.
*   **Language Skill Improvement** (`language_improvement_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the improvement in your language skills during the exchange program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means no improvement and 5 means significant improvement.
*   **Professional Networking** (`professional_networking_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the opportunities for professional networking during the exchange program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means very few opportunities and 5 means many opportunities.
*   **Personal Growth** (`personal_growth_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the extent of personal growth experienced during the exchange program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means very little growth and 5 means significant growth.
*   **Career Impact** (`career_impact_rating`, `number`, required): Rate how the exchange program impacted your career prospects on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means no impact and 5 means significant impact.
*   **Would You Recommend This Program?** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `number`, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend this exchange program to others on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means not likely and 5 means very likely.
*   **Major Challenges Faced** (`challenges_faced`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the challenges faced during the exchange program that apply to you (Language barrier, Cultural differences, Academic system differences, Housing issues, Financial constraints, Homesickness, Academic workload, None).
*   **Support Services Quality** (`support_services_rating`, `number`, required): Rate the quality of support services provided on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means very poor and 5 means excellent.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, `number`, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the exchange program on a scale of 1-5, where 1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.
*   **Major Challenges** (`major_challenges`, `select_multiple`, required): This is a list of challenges you faced, select all that apply.

Note: Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and honestly for the assessment to be complete.
