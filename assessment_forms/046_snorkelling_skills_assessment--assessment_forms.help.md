# Snorkelling Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snorkelling Skills Assessment is designed to evaluate the snorkelling skills of participants in the snorkelling program. This form is used to collect relevant information about each participant, including their name, contact email, snorkelling level, ability, and frequency of snorkelling activities. The form also includes additional fields for any other snorkelling-related information that needs to be captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Select your snorkelling level in the "Snorkelling Level" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your snorkelling ability and frequency in the respective fields.
5. Optionally, provide any additional snorkelling-related information in the "Snorkelling Other 1" to "Snorkelling Other 20" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email** (participant_email, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Snorkelling Level** (snorkelling_level, select_one, required): Select the level of your snorkelling skills (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Snorkelling Ability** (snorkelling_ability, text, required): Describe your snorkelling ability and experience.
* **Snorkelling Frequency** (snorkelling_frequency, number, optional): Enter the frequency of your snorkelling activities (optional).
* **Snorkelling Comments** (snorkelling_comments, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your snorkelling activities.
* **Snorkelling Other 1** (snorkelling_other_1, text, optional): Enter any other snorkelling-related information.
* **Snorkelling Other 2** (snorkelling_other_2, text, optional): Enter any other snorkelling-related information.
* ... (Snorkelling Other 3 to Snorkelling Other 20)
 
The Snorkelling Skills Assessment form is designed to gather information about your snorkelling skills and experience. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
