# Jewelry Expertise Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Jewelry Expertise Assessment form is a tool used to evaluate an individual's expertise in the field of jewelry. It gathers information about the user's experience, education, and skills related to jewelry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required fields.
2. Fill in the "Last Name", "Email Addr", "Expertise Level", "Experience Years", and "Education Background" fields as accurately as possible.
3. Select the relevant options for "Jewelry Type" and "Knowledge Areas" based on your expertise.
4. If applicable, list your relevant certifications in the "Certification Info" field.
5. Provide any additional relevant information in the "Jewelry Related Experience" and "Additional Info" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Addr** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Num** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Expertise Level** (select_one, required): Choose the level of expertise that best describes your skills and experience. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Experience Years** (number, required): Enter the number of years of experience you have in the field of jewelry.
* **Jewelry Type** (select_multiple, optional): Select the types of jewelry you have expertise in (e.g., Diamond, Gold, Silver).
* **Knowledge Areas** (select_multiple, required): Select the areas of knowledge you have, such as Jewelry Design, Gemology, or Marketing.
* **Education Background** (note, required): Describe your education background related to jewelry, e.g., degree, diploma, certification, or courses.
* **Certification Info** (select_multiple, optional): If you hold any certifications relevant to jewelry, select them from the options provided.
* **Jewelry Related Experience** (text, optional): Provide any relevant experience or skills you have in the field of jewelry.
* **Additional Info** (text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your expertise in jewelry.
