# roots evaluation survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The roots evaluation survey is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about the knowledge, health, and management of roots. It is intended to collect data from participants to better understand their perspectives and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question in the provided text fields.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the knowledge, health, and management of roots.
3. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please use the "participant feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophets roots knowledge** (Roots knowledge survey - Page 1):
	+ This field asks for information about the knowledge related to roots.
* **Roots health observations** (Roots health survey - Page 1):
	+ This field collects information about the health of roots.
* **Challenges with root management** (Roots management survey - Page 1):
	+ This field is for any challenges or difficulties you have with managing roots.
* **Participant feedback** (Roots evaluation survey - Page 1):
	+ This field is for any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the form or the subject matter.

## Tips
Make sure to provide clear and concise answers for each question. If you have any questions or concerns about the form, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.
