# supply_chain_industry_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supply chain industry assessment form is designed to collect information about a specific business or company's supply chain practices and operations. It is used to evaluate the industry's overall assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an *.
2. Provide the business name, location, and contact information (name, phone number, and email).
3. Select the industry sector and type (Yes/No) as it relates to the supply chain.
4. Choose the industry type (Yes/No) that applies to the company.
5. Enter the company size (Yes/No) based on the number of employees.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supply_chain_industry_assessment** (`supply_chain_industry_assessment`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the company's supply chain practices and operations.
* **business_name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company being assessed.
* **supply_chain_industry_sector** (`supply_chain_industry_sector`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the company's primary industry sector is related to the supply chain, otherwise select "No".
* **location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the company's operations, including the country, state or province, and city.
* **assessment_date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **contact_person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the assessment.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person (leave blank if not applicable).
* **industry_type** (`industry_type`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if the company's operations are related to the industry, otherwise select "No".
* **company_description** (`company_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the company and its operations.
* **company_size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the company has a size that is applicable for the industry.
* **assessment_comments** (`assessment_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the assessment.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes about the form (e.g. any issues or concerns). 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the information you enter, as accuracy is important for a thorough assessment.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the relevant authorities.
