# Chapter Self Assessment Form Undergraduate Chapter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chapter Self Assessment Form Undergraduate Chapter is a form used to evaluate the chapter's performance and progress in various areas such as academics, governance, and community involvement. This form serves as a means of self-assessment for the chapter's leadership and members to identify strengths and weaknesses and make informed decisions for future growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and field.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately based on your chapter's current status.
3. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*). These fields are essential for a complete assessment.
4. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Undergraduate Chapter Full Name** (`undergraduate_chapter_full_name`, text, *): Enter the formal name of your chapter as registered with the national organization.
* **College or University Affiliation** (`college_university_affiliation`, text, *): Provide the name of the institution where your chapter is located.
* **Chapter Region** (`chapter_region_undergraduate`, select_one, *): Select the region assigned to your chapter.
* **Current Chapter Basileus (President) Name** (`chapter_basileus_president_name`, text, *): Enter the name of your chapter's current Basileus (President).
* **Graduate Advisor Name** (`graduate_advisor_name_contact`, text, *): Provide the name of the graduate advisor supervising your chapter.
* **Cumulative Chapter GPA Score** (`cumulative_chapter_gpa_score`, number, *): Enter the combined GPA of all chapter members.
* **Academic Support Programs Implemented** (`academic_support_programs_implemented`, select_one, *): Indicate whether your chapter has formal study sessions or academic workshops.
* **Recognition of Academic Achievement** (`recognition_of_academic_achievement`, select_one, *): Answer whether your chapter recognizes high-performing members.
* **University Compliance and Standing** (`university_compliance_rating`, number, *): Rate your chapter's standing with the host university.
* **Tимeliness of Financial Dues Payments** (`timeliness_of_financial_dues_payments`, select_one, *): Indicate how often member and chapter dues are paid.
* **Average Chapter Meeting Attendance Rate** (`chapter_meeting_attendance_rate`, number, *): Provide the percentage of members attending meetings.
* **Service Project Impact Rating** (`chapter_service_project_impact_rating`, number, *): Rate the effectiveness of campus service projects.
* **Collaboration with Other Organizations** (`collaboration_with_other_organizations`, select_one, *): Indicate how often your chapter co-sponsors events.
* **Confirmation of Undergraduate Assessment** (`accuracy_confirmation_undergraduate`, select_one, *): Confirm that this assessment accurately reflects your chapter's status.
* **Date of Completion** (`date_of_undergraduate_assessment_log`, date, *): Enter today's date.
* **Additional Assessment Comments** (`additional_undergraduate_comments`, text, ): Provide any other relevant information or context.

# Tips

- Take your time to answer each question carefully and thoroughly.
- Be honest and accurate in your assessment to ensure the best possible outcome.
- If you're unsure about any field, refer to this guide or contact your chapter's leadership for assistance.
