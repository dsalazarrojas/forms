# Neurotransmitter Effects Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Neurotransmitter Effects Assessment is a comprehensive form used to evaluate the effects of neurotransmitters on an individual's mood, stress levels, and overall well-being. This form helps assessors understand how different neurotransmitters influence various aspects of a person's daily life, such as sleep patterns, motivation, and social interaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate response for each field.
2. Ensure you've answered all required fields. While some fields are optional, it's essential to fill out every question to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
3. Select the options that best match your experiences or current state.
