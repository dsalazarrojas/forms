# Pharmaceutical Career Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your career in the pharmaceutical industry. It will guide you through various sections to gather information about your education, skills, experience, career interests, and personal background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Select the most appropriate options for each field, even if you are not sure.
3. Use the hint provided for each field to help you understand what to expect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Education**:
  This field is for you to describe your educational background, including the institutions you attended and any relevant courses or degrees you have completed.
* **Skills**:
  Select all the skills you have acquired or are interested in acquiring in the pharmaceutical industry. For example, you can choose skills like data analysis, project management, or communication.
* **Experience**:
  Describe your work experience in the pharmaceutical industry, including your job titles, duration of employment, and job responsibilities.
* **Career Interests**:
  Select all the career interests you have in the pharmaceutical industry. For example, you can choose interests like clinical trials, regulatory affairs, or drug development.
* **Career Goals**:
  Write a brief statement describing your career goals and aspirations in the pharmaceutical industry.
* **Personal Statement**:
  Write a brief statement about your personal background and motivations for pursuing a career in the pharmaceutical industry.
* **Education Background**:
  Select the highest level of education you have achieved, for example, Bachelor's, Master's, or Ph.D.
* **Work Background**:
  Select all your work experience in the pharmaceutical industry, including job titles and duration of employment.
* **Education Level**:
