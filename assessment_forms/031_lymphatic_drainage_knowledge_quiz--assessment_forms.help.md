# lymphatic_drainage_knowledge_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the knowledge of medical professionals on lymphatic drainage, a crucial process in the human body. It consists of 8 questions, each covering a different aspect of the topic.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question to the best of your knowledge and with confidence.
- Make sure to choose the correct option for each select questions.
- For text fields, provide a clear and concise explanation.
- Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Lymphatic System** (`lymphatic_system`, select_one, required): This question asks you to choose the correct type of lymphatic system that is responsible for the transport of lymph fluid throughout the body.
  - Select the correct option from the list, for example, "a", "b", or "c".
- **Lymphatic Drainage** (`lymphatic_drainage`, text, required): This question asks you to describe the process of lymphatic drainage, including the movement of lymph fluid and its role in immune function.
  - Provide a clear and concise explanation of lymphatic drainage.
- **Lymphatic Drainage Pathways** (`lymphatic_drainage_pathways`, text, required): This question asks you to describe the pathways through which lymph fluid moves throughout the body, including the lymph nodes and the lymphatic vessels.
  - Provide a clear and concise explanation of the lymphatic drainage pathways.
- **Lymphatic Circulation** (`lymphatic_circulation`, text, required): This question asks you to describe the circulation of lymph fluid, including its connection to the blood circulation.
  - Provide a clear and concise explanation of lymphatic circulation.
- **Lymphatic Drainage System Dysfunction** (`lymphatic_drainage_system_dysfunction`, select_one, required): This question asks you to choose the correct type of dysfunction in the lymphatic drainage system, for example, "a", "b", or "c".
  - Select the correct option from the list.
- **Assessment** (`assessment`, select_multiple, required): This question asks you to choose multiple options that accurately describe the assessment methods used for evaluating the lymphatic drainage system, for example, "Option 1", "Option 2".
  - Select multiple options that you consider most relevant and accurate.
- **Submit** (`submit`, time, required): This is the final step to complete the form. Make sure to review your answers before submitting.
  - Double-check your answers and click the submit button to complete the form.
- **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This is a free-text field for you to provide any additional information or comments about the lymphatic drainage system.
  - You may choose to provide an introduction to provide context or additional information about your answers.
- **Note**: This field is not required, but it's an opportunity to provide more details.
