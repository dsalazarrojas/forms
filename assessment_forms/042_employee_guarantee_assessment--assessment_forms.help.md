# Employee Guarantee Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's guarantee status, including the type of guarantee and period of guarantee. It's essential to provide accurate and complete answers to help ensure the correct processing and management of employment-related data.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee's name and job title in the respective fields.
2.  Select the guarantee type and period that apply to your employee from the provided options.
3.  Ensure you've filled out all required fields, even if they seem optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the employee in question. This field is required.
*   **Guarantee Type** (`guarantee_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of guarantee that applies to your employee from the options provided. This field is required.
*   **Guarantee Period** (`guarantee_period`, Select Multiple, Required): Select all the periods that apply to your employee's guarantee. This field is required.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief description of your employee's job title, if applicable.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`, Text, Optional): Enter a detailed description of your employee's job, if required.
*   **Work Location** (`work_location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where your employee works, if applicable.
