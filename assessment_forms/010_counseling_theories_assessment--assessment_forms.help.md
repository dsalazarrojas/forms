# Counseling Theories Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Counseling Theories Assessment form is designed to gauge your level of education and your preferences regarding different counseling theories. Your responses will help assess your familiarity with various theories and their application in practice.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start the assessment, select your current education level from the dropdown list on page 1.
2.  On page 1, choose from the available options the most relevant education levels that best describe your educational background.
3.  On page 1, select only one theory that you prefer among the available options under "theory_preferences."
4.  On page 2-3, provide short descriptions for each given case scenario under "case\_response\_1" to "case\_response\_4."
5.  For each case scenario, answer the questions as concisely as possible, but ensure your answer provides the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Education Level** (`education_level`): Select your current education level from the following options:
    *   High school
    *   College/University
    *   Master's
    *   Doctoral

    This section asks about your current education level, which will help tailor the assessment to your background.
*   **Theory Preferences** (`theory_preferences`): Choose the theory that best describes your approach to counseling:
    *   Cognitive
    *   Humanistic
    *   Existential

    This option will help us understand your theoretical orientation and guide the assessment accordingly.
*   **Case Response 1** (`case_response_1`): Briefly describe how you would approach the following case scenario:
    *   This field is intended for you to respond to a case scenario.
*   **Case Response 2** (`case_response_2`): Briefly describe how you would approach the following case scenario:
    *   This field is intended for you to respond to a second case scenario.
*   **Case Response 3** (`case_response_3`): Briefly describe how you would approach the following case scenario:
    *   This field is intended for you to respond to a third case scenario.
*   **Level of Education** (`education_level_2`): Select your level of education from the following options:
    *   High school
    *   College/University
    *   Master's
    *   Doctoral

    This option will help us understand your educational background better.
*   **Case Response 4** (`case_response_4`): Briefly describe how you would approach the following case scenario:
    *   This field is intended for you to respond to a fourth case scenario.

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate in your responses as they will help in understanding your background and preferences.
*   Ensure that your responses are concise and relevant to the case scenarios provided.
*   If unsure, choose the option that best describes your approach.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Counseling Theories Assessment form effectively and provide valuable insights into your education level and theoretical preferences.
