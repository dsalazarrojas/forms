# Project Safety Protocol Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Safety Protocol Assessment Form is used to evaluate the safety protocols of a project site, ensuring that all necessary safety measures are in place to prevent accidents and ensure a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the project.
2. Provide detailed descriptions for each field as needed.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly, as incomplete or incorrect information may lead to inaccurate assessments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the project.
* **Site Location** (`site_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the project site.
* **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, required): Enter the date you visited the project site.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the supervisor on-site.
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the supervisor.
* **Safety Officer Name** (`safety_officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the safety officer.
* **Safety Officer Phone** (`safety_officer_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the safety officer.
* **Safety Officer Email** (`safety_officer_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the safety officer.
* **Observation Start Time** (`observation_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the observation.
* **Observation End Time** (`observation_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the observation.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`, text, required): Describe the weather conditions during the observation.
* **Observation Area** (`observation_area`, text, required): Describe the area of the observation.
* **Observation Notes** (`observation_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the observation.
* **Safety Procedure Rating** (`safety_procedure_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the safety procedures as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Safety Equipment Rating** (`safety_equipment_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the safety equipment as Satisfactory or Unsatisfactory.
* **Safety Procedure Improvement** (`safety_procedure_improvement`, text, required): Suggest any improvements to the safety procedures.
* **Safety Equipment Improvement** (`safety_equipment_improvement`, text, required): Suggest any improvements to the safety equipment.
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the supervisor.
* **Safety Officer Comments** (`safety_officer_comments`, text, required): Enter any comments from the safety officer.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of the project (In Progress, Complete, Delayed, Cancelled).
* **Safety Protocol Review Date** (`safety_protocol_review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the safety protocol review.
* **Safety Protocol Revision Date** (`safety_protocol_revision_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the safety protocol revision.
* **Safety Protocol Reviewer** (`safety_protocol_reviewer`, text, required): Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, note, required): Sign and date the form.

Note: Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately, as this form is crucial for assessing project safety protocols.
