# Conditions Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conditions Assessment Form is a tool used to assess the condition of a site or building. This form helps to gather information about the type of condition being assessed, its severity rating, and compliance status. The purpose of this form is to provide a consistent and structured way to document and report on the conditions found during an assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the Assessor's name and title of the form.
2. Select the Assessment date.
3. Choose the Location of the assessment (e.g. a site or building name).
4. Select the Condition category (e.g. Structural, Mechanical, Electrical, or Cosmetic).
5. Rate the Severity rating (e.g. Low, Medium, or High).
6. Enter any Measured values or readings (e.g. measurement details).
7. Report the Compliance status (e.g. Compliant, Non-compliant, or Conditional).
8. Provide a Recommended action (e.g. repairs, monitoring, or replacement).
9. Enter an Estimated cost (if applicable).
10. Add any Additional notes or observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Assessor name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter your name or title as the person completing the assessment.
- **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the assessment.
- **Location of assessment** (`location`, text, required): Enter the site or building name where the assessment was conducted.
- **Condition category** (`condition_category`, select_one, required): Choose the type of condition being assessed (e.g. Structural, Mechanical, Electrical, or Cosmetic).
- **Severity rating** (`severity_rating`, select_one, required): Select the scale of severity (e.g. Low, Medium, or High).
- **Measued values or readings** (`measured_values`, text, optional): Enter any measurement details or readings taken during the assessment.
- **Compliance status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, required): Choose whether the item meets the standards (e.g. Compliant, Non-compliant, or Conditional).
- **Recommended action** (`recommended_action`, text, optional): Suggest any repairs, monitoring, or replacement that should be done.
- **Estimated cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, optional): Enter a rough cost estimate for any recommended actions.
- **Additional notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any observations or photos that can support the assessment.
