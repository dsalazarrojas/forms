# COVID 19 Employee Health Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the overall health and work readiness of employees during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, please read and understand this guide carefully. This form has 23 fields divided into several sections. Please answer each question accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department name as it appears on your official documentation.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as it appears on your official documentation.
*   **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are completing this form.
*   **Overall Health Status** (`overall_health`, select_one, required): Choose your current overall health status from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
*   **Any COVID-19 symptoms currently** (`covid_symptoms`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you are experiencing COVID-19 symptoms, and 'False' otherwise.
*   **Symptoms present** (`symptom_list`, text, optional): If you are experiencing COVID-19 symptoms, describe them in this field.
*   **Known COVID-19 exposure in past 14 days** (`covid_exposure`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you have been exposed to someone with COVID-19 in the past 14 days, 'False' otherwise, or 'Unknown' if you are unsure.
*   **Have you been tested for COVID-19** (`covid_testing`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes, Positive', 'Yes, Negative', 'Yes, Results Pending', or 'False' to indicate your testing status.
*   **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, required): Choose 'Fully Vaccinated', 'Partially Vaccinated', or 'Not Vaccinated' to indicate your current vaccination status.
*   **Pre-existing Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you have any pre-existing medical conditions, 'False' otherwise.
*   **Describe medical conditions if applicable** (`condition_details`, text, optional): If you have any pre-existing medical conditions, describe them in this field.
*   **Employee ready to work in-person** (`ready_to_work`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you are ready to work in-person, 'Conditional' otherwise.
*   **Requiring work-from-home accommodation** (`work_from_home`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you require work-from-home accommodation, 'False' otherwise.
*   **Reason for accommodation if applicable** (`accommodation_reason`, text, optional): If you require work-from-home accommodation, describe the reason in this field.
*   **Stress Level** (`stress_level`, number, required): Rate your current stress level from 1-10.
*   **Support or resources needed** (`support_needed`, select_one, required): Choose 'True' if you need support or resources, 'False' otherwise.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please enter them in this field.
