# Retail Customer Service Effectiveness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the effectiveness of the customer service received at our store. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and provide us with your honest feedback. Your input will help us improve our service to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- To get started, select the options that best describe your experience on the day of your visit. You can only answer each question once.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. Was there a staff member present at the location?**
	+ This field checks if a staff member was available and present at the store.
- **2. Was this location available at the time of your visit?**
	+ This field checks if the store was open and ready for customers at the time of your visit.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
- **3. Date of Visit**
	+ The date of your visit to the store.
- **4. Name of Staff Member Interacted With**
	+ Enter the name of the staff member you interacted with during your visit.
- **5. Rating of Staff Member (1-5)**
	+ Rate the staff member's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **6. Service Quality (1-5)**
	+ Rate the quality of service provided during your visit on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
- **7. How Friendly Was the Staff?**
	+ Select the level of friendliness you experienced from the staff member. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4
- **8. Resolution of Issue (Multiple Select)**
	+ Select all that apply if the staff member resolved any issues you had during your visit. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4
- **9. Follow-up (Select One)**
	+ Select how you were followed up on after the issue was resolved. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4
- **10. Customer Satisfaction (Select One)**
	+ Select how satisfied you are with the service you received. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4
- **11. Recommendation**
	+ Select if you would recommend this store based on your experience. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4
