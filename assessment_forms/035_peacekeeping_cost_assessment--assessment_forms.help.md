# Peacekeeping Cost Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about peacekeeping costs, allowing for a detailed analysis and comparison of estimated and actual costs. This data will help in assessing the effectiveness and efficiency of peacekeeping operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by providing a brief description of the peacekeeping mission.
2. Estimate the costs involved in the mission.
3. Provide actual costs incurred during the mission.
4. Describe any cost variances between estimated and actual costs.
5. Outline the mission objectives and how they relate to the costs.
6. Provide explanations for any cost variances.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the mission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Peacekeeping Cost Assessment** (`peacekeeping_cost_assessment_form`): A brief description of the peacekeeping mission.
* **Mission Details** (`mission_details`): A more detailed explanation of the mission's objectives, scope, and any relevant context.
* **Estimated Costs** (`estimated_costs`): The costs that were initially estimated or budgeted for the mission.
* **Actual Costs** (`actual_costs`): The actual costs incurred during the mission, including any additional expenses or adjustments.
* **Cost Variance** (`cost_variances`): A description of any difference between estimated and actual costs, including reasons for the variance.
* **Mission Objectives** (`mission_objectives`): A list of the mission's main objectives and how they relate to the costs involved.
* **Actual Cost Variance** (`actual_cost_variances`): An explanation of any variances between estimated and actual costs.
* **Explanations** (`explanations`): A detailed explanation of any cost variances or discrepancies.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Any additional comments or observations about the mission.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when estimating and reporting actual costs.
- Use clear and concise language when explaining cost variances.
- If there are significant cost variances, provide a breakdown of the reasons behind them.
- Make sure to keep records of mission objectives and how they relate to costs for future reference.
