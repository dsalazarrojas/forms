# Rejection Sensitivity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rejection Sensitivity Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's sensitivity to rejection and rejection experiences. It aims to help understand how someone feels about rejection and their self-perception.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Introduction page, where you can read more about the assessment.
2. On the "Feelings about others" page, select all the options that apply to you.
3. On the "Feelings about self" page, select all the options that apply to you.
4. Review the Self-Reflection page and choose the option that best describes your current state.
5. Enter your score on the "Score 0-3" page.
6. On the "Self-Reflection 2" page, select the option that best describes your current state.
7. In the "Comments" field, provide any additional information you would like to share.
8. Finally, fill in the "Form ID" field with your unique identifier, and the "Submitted By" field with your name. Note that these fields are not required, but they will help us understand who submitted the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro_page`, note, optional): This page provides a brief overview of the assessment and its purpose.
* **Feelings about others** (`feelings_about_others`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to you regarding how you feel about others.
	+ "Yes": You feel a strong emotional response when others reject or criticize you.
	+ "No": You do not feel any emotional response when others reject or criticize you.
* **Feelings about self** (`feelings_about_self`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to you regarding how you feel about yourself.
	+ "Yes": You feel a strong emotional response when you are rejected or criticized.
	+ "No": You do not feel any emotional response when you are rejected or criticized.
* **Self-Reflection** (`self_reflection`, select_one, optional): This page asks you to reflect on your current state.
	+ "Yes": You are currently experiencing strong feelings of rejection sensitivity.
	+ "No": You are not experiencing strong feelings of rejection sensitivity.
* **Score 0-3** (`score_0`, number, optional): Enter a score between 0 and 3 that describes your current level of rejection sensitivity.
* **Self-Reflection 2** (`self_reflection_2`, select_one, optional): This page asks you to reflect on your current state again.
	+ "Yes": You are currently experiencing strong feelings of rejection sensitivity.
	+ "No": You are not experiencing strong feelings of rejection sensitivity.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share about your feelings and experiences.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for your assessment.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as a reference for your submitted assessment.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all the questions honestly and accurately, as this will help us understand your feelings and experiences better.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for support.
