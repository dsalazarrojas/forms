# Connection Skills Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Connection Skills Assessment is a form used to evaluate an employee's connection skills, which are essential for building strong relationships and working effectively within a team. The assessment helps to identify areas of strength and areas for improvement in communication, interpersonal skills, conflict resolution, and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest answers.
2. Choose the correct options for each rating question.
3. Enter any relevant information or suggestions for improvement in the text fields.
4. Provide a signature and date for the reviewer to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the name of the person being assessed.
* **Department** (`department`): Select the department the employee belongs to.
* **Type of Assessment** (`assessment_type`): Choose the type of assessment (Self-Assessment, Peer Review, Managerial Review, or Annual Performance Check).
* **Clarity and Conciseness in Communication** (`clarity_of_expression`): Rate how effectively the employee conveys ideas using a scale of 5 (Exceptional) to 1 (Not Observed).
* **Active Listening Skills** (`active_listening`): Rate how well the employee demonstrates understanding and attention using a scale of 5 (Exceptional) to 1 (Not Observed).
* **Empathy and Building Rapport** (`empathy_and_rapport`): Rate how effectively the employee builds trust and understanding with others using a scale of 5 (Exceptional) to 1 (Not Observed).
* **Conflict Resolution Ability** (`conflict_resolution_skill`): Rate how well the employee handles disagreements using a scale of 5 (Exceptional) to 1 (Not Observed).
* **Collaboration and Teamwork** (`collaboration_teamwork`): Rate how well the employee works effectively within a team using a scale of 5 (Exceptional) to 1 (Not Observed).
* **Primary Connection Strength** (`strength_summary`): Enter a brief description of the employee's primary strength.
* **Areas for Skill Development** (`improvement_areas`): Provide suggestions for areas where the employee can improve.
* **Interest in Connection Skills Training** (`training_interest`): Indicate if the employee is interested in connection skills training.
* **Reviewer Signature (Full Name)** (`reviewer_signature`): Enter the reviewer's full name.
* **Assessment Date** (`review_date`): Enter the date of the assessment.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* Provide specific examples when answering the rating questions.
* Be constructive when offering suggestions for improvement.
* Review and discuss the results with the employee to help them grow in their connection skills.
