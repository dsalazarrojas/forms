# Risk Assessment For Educational Institutions - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help educational institutions identify and assess potential risks that may impact their operations, students, staff, or the institution as a whole.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **What Are Your Concerns**: Describe any concerns or issues you are currently facing or have identified as potential risks for your educational institution.
2. **Potential Risks Identified**: Select all applicable potential risks that you have identified from the provided options. This will help us understand the types of risks you are dealing with.
3. **Risk Assessment Process**: Provide a detailed description of the risk assessment process you have in place or would like to have in place to address the identified risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What Are Your Concerns**: A text field where you can describe any concerns or issues that you are currently facing or have identified as potential risks for your educational institution. This is an open-ended field, so feel free to provide as much detail as you need.
* **Potential Risks Identified**: A multiple-select field where you can choose from the provided options that best match the risks you have identified. The options are:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Risk Assessment Process**: A note field where you can describe the process you have in place or would like to have in place to address the identified risks. This is an open-ended field, so feel free to provide as much detail as you need.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing your concerns or risks.
* Select all applicable risks from the provided options.
* When describing your risk assessment process, provide enough detail to help us understand how you will address the identified risks.
