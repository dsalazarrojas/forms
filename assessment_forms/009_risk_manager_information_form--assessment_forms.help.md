# risk_manager_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The risk_manager_information_form is used to gather information about a Risk Manager's background, experience, and job details. This form is designed to capture essential data to facilitate effective risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Fill in the Risk Manager's name in the "Risk Manager Name" field.
- Enter the Risk Manager's job title in the "Job Title" field.
- Provide the name of the Reporting Manager in the "Reporting Manager" field.
- Enter the Risk Manager's experience in the "Risk Management Experience" field.
- Select the Risk Manager's relevant certifications in the "Risk Management Certifications" field.
- Specify the Risk Manager's education level in the "Risk Management Education" field.
- Enter the Risk Manager's relevant training dates in the "Risk Management Training" field.
- Select the Risk Manager's preferred books for risk management in the "Risk Management Books" field.
- Enter the job location in the "Job Location" field.
- Provide a detailed description of the job in the "Job Description" field.
- Specify the Risk Manager's responsibilities in the "Risk Management Responsibilities" field.
- Select the Risk Manager's preferred tools for risk management in the "Risk Management Tools" field.
- Enter the Risk Manager's budget for risk management in the "Risk Management Budget" field.
- Select the Risk Manager's preferred process for risk management in the "Risk Management Process" field.
- Enter the date the Risk Manager started their job in the "Job Start Date" field.
- Enter the date the Risk Manager ended their job in the "Job End Date" field.
- Enter the duration of the Risk Manager's job in the "Job Duration" field.
- Enter the number of hours the Risk Manager worked in the "Job Hours" field.
- Specify the Risk Manager's job classification in the "Job Classification" field.
- Enter the Risk Manager's job department in the "Job Department" field.
- Specify the Risk Manager's job reporting level in the "Job Reporting Level" field.
- Enter the Risk Manager's job function in the "Job Function" field.
- Enter the Risk Manager's supervisor's name in the "Job Supervisor" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Risk Manager Name** (`risk_manager_name`): Enter the Risk Manager's name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the Risk Manager's job title.
- **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`): Enter the name of the Risk Manager's reporting manager.
- **Risk Management Experience** (`risk_management_experience`): Enter the Risk Manager's experience in risk management.
- **Risk Management Certifications** (`risk_management_certifications`): Select the Risk Manager's relevant certifications.
- **Risk Management Education** (`risk_management_education`): Select the Risk Manager's education level (e.g., Bachelor's, Master's, PhD).
- **Risk Management Training** (`risk_management_training`): Enter the dates of the Risk Manager's relevant training.
- **Risk Management Books** (`risk_management_books`): Select the Risk Manager's preferred books for risk management.
- **Job Location** (`job_location`): Enter the location of the Risk Manager's job.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`): Provide a detailed description of the Risk Manager's job.
- **Risk Management Responsibilities** (`risk_management_responsibilities`): Specify the Risk Manager's responsibilities.
- **Risk Management Tools** (`risk_management_tools`): Select the Risk Manager's preferred tools for risk management.
- **Risk Management Budget** (`risk_management_budget`): Enter the Risk Manager's budget for risk management.
- **Risk Management Process** (`risk_management_process`): Select the Risk Manager's preferred process for risk management.
- **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`): Enter the date the Risk Manager started their job.
- **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`): Enter the date the Risk Manager ended their job.
- **Job Duration** (`job_duration`): Enter the duration of the Risk Manager's job.
- **Job Hours** (`job_hours`): Enter the number of hours the Risk Manager worked.
- **Job Classification** (`job_classification`): Specify the Risk Manager's job classification.
- **Job Department** (`job_department`): Enter the Risk Manager's job department.
- **Job Reporting Level** (`job_reporting_level`): Specify the Risk Manager's job reporting level.
- **Job Function** (`job_function`): Enter the Risk Manager's job function.
- **Job Supervisor** (`job_supervisor`): Enter the name of the Risk Manager's supervisor.
