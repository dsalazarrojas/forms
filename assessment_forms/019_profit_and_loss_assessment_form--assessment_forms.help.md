# Profit and Loss Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help businesses assess their profit and loss performance. It collects information on business name, year, revenue streams, revenue, cost of goods sold, operating expenses, and other key metrics to calculate the net profit.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the business name and year of assessment.
2. Select whether the business has multiple revenue streams.
3. Enter the revenue generated by the business.
4. Choose the level of operating expenses.
5. Enter the cost of goods sold.
6. Choose whether the business has tax expenses.
7. Calculate the gross profit by summing the revenue and subtracting the cost of goods sold.
8. Calculate the net profit by subtracting operating expenses from the gross profit.
9. Enter any additional comments if needed.
10. Submit the form or navigate through other options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the business.
* **Year** (Number, Required): Enter the year of assessment.
* **Revenue Streams** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose whether the business has multiple revenue streams (Yes/No).
	+ Note: This field indicates whether the business has multiple sources of income.
* **Revenue** (Number, Required): Enter the total revenue generated by the business.
* **Cost of Goods Sold** (Select One, Required): Choose whether the business has cost of goods sold (Yes/No).
	+ Note: This field indicates whether the business has cost of goods sold or not.
* **Operating Expenses Level** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the level of operating expenses (Good/Fair).
	+ Note: This field indicates the level of operating expenses as Low, Moderate, or High.
* **Operating Expenses Breakdown** (Text, Optional): Enter a detailed breakdown of operating expenses if necessary.
* **Tax Expenses** (Select One, Required): Choose whether the business has tax expenses (Yes/No).
* **Net Profit** (Number, Required): This is the summary of the gross profit and operating expenses.
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Submit** (Select One, Required): Submit the form once all fields are filled.
* **Back** (Select One, Optional): Navigate back to the previous page.
* **Save** (Select One, Optional): Save the form for later editing.
* **Cancel** (Select One, Optional): Cancel the form and exit.
* **Help** (Select One, Optional): Access help resources for further assistance.
* **Reset** (Select One, Optional): Reset the form to its initial state.
