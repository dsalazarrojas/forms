# Regulatory Compliance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an organization's regulatory compliance in various aspects, including awareness, training, documentation, implementation, and review. It helps you evaluate your organization's level of compliance and identify areas that need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the assessment type from the list provided (e.g., Compliance Assessment, Regulatory Risk Assessment, or Compliance Review).
2. Choose the risk level of your organization, from Low, Medium, to High.
3. Fill in your organization's name, which is a required field for identification purposes.
4. Answer questions about your organization's regulatory requirements, awareness, training, documentation, and implementation. Select the options that best match your current state.
5. Provide dates for assessment, if applicable.
6. Answer questions about the frequency of regulatory requirements revisions, reviews, and documentation, and select the options that best match your current practices.
7. Enter comments or any additional information you want to provide.
8. Review and submit the form for final assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name for identification purposes.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of assessment you want to perform (e.g., Compliance Assessment, Regulatory Risk Assessment, or Compliance Review).
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_multiple, required): Select the risk level of your organization, from Low, Medium, to High.
* **Regulatory Requirements Awareness** (`regulatory_requirements_awareness`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization is aware of the regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Training** (`regulatory_requirements_training`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization provides training on regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Review** (`regulatory_requirements_review`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization regularly reviews regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Documentation** (`regulatory_requirements_documentation`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization maintains regulatory documentation (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Implementation** (`regulatory_requirements_implementation`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization implements regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Compliance** (`regulatory_requirements_compliance`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization complies with regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Monitoring** (`regulatory_requirements_monitoring`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization monitors regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Reporting** (`regulatory_requirements_reporting`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization reports on regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Revisions** (`regulatory_requirements_revisions`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization revises regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Reviews** (`regulatory_requirements_reviews`, select_multiple, required): Answer whether your organization reviews regulatory requirements (True or False).
* **Regulatory Requirements Revision Frequency** (`regulatory_requirements_revision_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of regulatory revisions (e.g., Quarterly, Semi-Annually, or Annually).
* **Regulatory Requirements Documentation Frequency** (`regulatory_requirements_documentation_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency of regulatory documentation (e.g., Daily, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Regulatory Requirements Training Frequency** (`regulatory_requirements_training_frequency`, select_one, optional): Answer the frequency of regulatory training (e.g., As Needed, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Regulatory Requirements Review Frequency** (`regulatory_requirements_review_frequency`, select_one, optional): Answer the frequency of regulatory reviews (e.g., As Needed, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Regulatory Requirements Revision Content** (`regulatory_requirements_revision_content`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about regulatory revisions.
* **Regulatory Requirements Revision Frequency Justification** (`regulatory_requirements_revision_frequency_justification`, text, optional): Enter any additional justification for regulatory revisions frequency.
* **Regulatory Requirements Revision Frequency Justification 2** (`regulatory_requirements_revision_frequency_justification_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional justification for regulatory revisions frequency 2.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you want to provide.

## Tips
* Answer questions honestly and accurately to ensure a comprehensive assessment of your organization's regulatory compliance.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult your organization's regulatory experts or seek guidance from relevant authorities.
* This form is for informational purposes only and is not intended to be used as a substitute for professional regulatory advice.
