# store_compliance_assessment_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The store_compliance_assessment_registration form is used to store compliance assessments for a store. This form helps track and record compliance data for various areas and audits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are completing this form for the correct store and contact.
3. Answer the questions based on your knowledge and understanding of the store's compliance status.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store_compliance_assessment_registration** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the form title.
* **scheduled_audits** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the scheduled audits that apply to the store. You can select multiple audits if necessary.
* **compliance_areas** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the compliance areas that the store is in compliance with.
* **contact_details** (`4`, `note`, required: false): This field is for additional contact details. You can enter any relevant information about the contact person.
* **first_name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's first name.
* **last_name** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's last name.
* **email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **submit** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Submit the form once you have completed it.
* **scheduled_audits_0** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Another scheduled audit option, similar to scheduled_audits.
