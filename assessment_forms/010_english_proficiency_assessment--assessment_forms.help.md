# English Proficiency Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The English Proficiency Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's language skills, reading comprehension, writing expression, listening comprehension, speaking expression, grammar, vocabulary, and overall English proficiency level.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the Language Skills Assessment page, which consists of a single note field where you can provide information about your language skills.
2. Proceed to the Reading Comprehension page, where you will find three options. Select the option that best describes your reading comprehension abilities.
3. The Writing Expression page is a text field where you can type your writing expression.
4. On the Listening Comprehension page, choose all that apply from the options, which will determine your listening comprehension abilities.
5. The Speaking Expression page is another text field where you can type your speaking expression.
6. Move on to the Grammar page, where you will select one option that best describes your grammar skills.
7. The Vocabulary page consists of multiple options, and choose all that apply to describe your vocabulary skills.
8. On the English Proficiency Score page, enter a numerical value that represents your English proficiency level.
9. Finally, on the Overall English Proficiency Level page, select one option that best describes your overall language proficiency level.
10. End by selecting all that apply from the Recommended Actions page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language Skills Assessment** (`language_skills_assessment`, `note`, optional): Describe your language skills, if applicable.
* **Reading Comprehension** (`reading_comprehension_assessment`, `select_one`, optional): Select the option that best describes your reading comprehension abilities.
* **Writing Expression** (`writing_expression_assessment`, `text`, optional): Type your writing expression in the text field.
* **Listening Comprehension** (`listening_comprehension_assessment`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from the options to determine your listening comprehension abilities.
* **Speaking Expression** (`speaking_expression_assessment`, `text`, optional): Type your speaking expression in the text field.
* **Grammar** (`grammar_assessment`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option that best describes your grammar skills.
* **Vocabulary** (`vocabulary_assessment`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose all that apply from the options to describe your vocabulary skills.
* **English Proficiency Score** (`english_proficiency_score`, `number`, optional): Enter a numerical value that represents your English proficiency level.
* **Overall English Proficiency Level** (`overall_english_proficiency_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option that best describes your overall language proficiency level.
* **Recommended Actions** (`recommended_actions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from the options to determine recommended actions for improvement.

## Tips
* Please answer all applicable fields honestly and accurately to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
* If you are unsure about any field or option, please contact your language skills trainer or assessor for guidance.
* Complete the form carefully, as incorrect information may affect the assessment results.
