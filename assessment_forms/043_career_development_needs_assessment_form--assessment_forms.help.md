# Career Development Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Development Needs Assessment Form is a tool used to assess the career development needs of an employee. It helps to identify areas where the employee requires improvement and provides a framework for creating a plan for their career growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Employee name" field.
2. Rate your current skill level as either Advanced, Intermediate, or Beginner in the "Current skill level" field.
3. Describe the skills you want to develop or improve in the "Target skills for development" field.
4. Choose a preferred learning format from the options provided in the "Preferred learning format" field (optional).
5. Specify what success looks like in terms of outcome for your career development in the "Expected outcome" field.
6. Choose a timeline for when you want to improve your skills in the "Timeline for development" field (optional).
7. Describe the available resources you have for development, including training budget and mentors, in the "Available resources" field (optional).
8. If necessary, describe the support you need from your manager in the "Manager support needed" field (optional).
9. Provide any additional comments or information you think would be helpful in the "Additional comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Current skill level**: Rate your current skill level as:
	+ **Advanced**: You have a strong proficiency in this skill.
	+ **Intermediate**: You have some experience, but room for improvement.
	+ **Beginner**: You are new to this skill.
* **Target skills for development**: Describe the skills you want to develop or improve.
* **Preferred learning format**: Choose how you prefer to learn:
	+ **Workshops**: In-person training sessions.
	+ **Online courses**: Online tutorials or courses.
	+ **Mentoring**: Guidance from an experienced professional.
	+ **On the job training**: Practical experience under the guidance of a mentor.
* **Expected outcome**: Specify what success looks like in terms of outcome for your career development.
* **Timeline for development**: Choose a timeline for when you want to improve your skills:
	+ **3 months**: Short-term goal.
	+ **6 months**: Mid-term goal.
	+ **12 months**: Long-term goal.
* **Available resources**: Describe the resources you have available for development, including training budget and mentors.
* **Manager support needed**: Describe the support you need from your manager.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful.
