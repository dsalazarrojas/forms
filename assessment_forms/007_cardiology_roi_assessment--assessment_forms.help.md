# Cardiology ROI Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardiology ROI Assessment form is used to collect data for evaluating the value and effectiveness of a cardiology program or intervention.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Assessment Context**: A brief description of the assessment, including the program or intervention being evaluated and the assessment period.
2. **Facility Name**: The name of the organization performing the assessment.
3. **Assessment Period Start**: The start date of the measurement period for the assessment.
4. **Assessment Period End**: The end date of the measurement period for the assessment.
5. **Intervention Description**: A brief summary of the cardiology program or intervention being assessed.
6. **Cost of Program**: The total cost of the program in local currency.
7. **Measurable Outcomes**: Key metrics used to evaluate the value of the program.
8. **Estimated Savings**: (Optional) Estimated financial benefit over the assessment period.
9. **ROI Calculation Method**: (Optional) A brief description of the method used for ROI calculation.
10. **Recommendations**: (Optional) Actionable next steps based on the findings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Assessment Context**: The context in which the assessment was performed, to provide a clear understanding of the program or intervention being evaluated.
- **Facility Name**: The name of the organization performing the assessment, to track the assessing authority.
- **Assessment Period Start**: The date marking the beginning of the measurement period for the assessment, to calculate the duration of the program or intervention.
- **Assessment Period End**: The date marking the end of the measurement period for the assessment, to calculate the duration of the program or intervention.
- **Intervention Description**: A brief summary of the cardiology program or intervention being assessed, to provide clarity on what was evaluated.
- **Cost of Program**: The total cost of the program in local currency, to understand the financial investment.
- **Measurable Outcomes**: Key metrics used to evaluate the value of the program, to provide a clear understanding of its effectiveness.
- **Estimated Savings**: (Optional) The estimated financial benefit over the assessment period, to understand potential return on investment.
- **ROI Calculation Method**: (Optional) A brief description of the method used for ROI calculation, to provide transparency on how the results were derived.
- **Recommendations**: (Optional) Actionable next steps based on the findings, to facilitate implementation of program improvements.
 
 ## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Use a clear and concise format for each field.
- If you need help or have questions, please reach out to the assessment authority.
