# Forensic Chemistry Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forensic Chemistry Assessment form is used to collect information related to forensic chemistry assessments. It covers various aspects such as assessment form, knowledge checks, technique ratings, integrity confirmation, technique confidence, comments, submitted by, submission date, submitted time, email, and phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **AssessmentForm**: This field allows you to fill out the assessment form with any relevant information.
*   **KnowledgeChecks**: This field asks you to confirm whether certain knowledge checks are applicable.
*   **TechniqueRatings**: This field asks you to rate the technique using available options.
*   **IntegrityConfirmed**: This field confirms the integrity of the assessment.
*   **TechniqueConfidence**: This field asks you to enter a confidence level for the technique.
*   **Comments**: This field allows you to provide any additional comments.
*   **SubmittedBy**: This field asks you whether the assessment was submitted by someone else.
*   **SubmissionDate**: This field asks you to enter the submission date.
*   **SubmittedTime**: This field asks you to enter the submitted time.
*   **Email**: This field asks you to enter your email address.
*   **Phone**: This field asks you to enter your phone number.
