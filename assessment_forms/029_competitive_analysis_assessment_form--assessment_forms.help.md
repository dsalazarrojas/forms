This form is designed to assess the competitive landscape by providing a thorough evaluation of a competitor's strengths and weaknesses. By filling out this form, you will be able to identify areas where you can improve and create effective strategies to stay ahead of the competition.

3.  **Department**: Select the department within your organization that is responsible for the analysis.
4.  **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
5.  **Industry**: Select the industry sector your company operates in.
6.  **Competitor Name**: Enter the name of the competitor you're analyzing.
