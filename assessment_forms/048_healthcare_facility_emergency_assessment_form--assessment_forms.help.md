# Healthcare Facility Emergency Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare Facility Emergency Assessment Form is used to assess the emergency status of a facility during a healthcare emergency. This form helps healthcare professionals identify and manage emergency situations effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the emergency contact name in the **emergency contact name** field (Field 1).
2. Enter the location where the emergency occurred in the **location** field (Field 2).
3. Select the date of the incident from the calendar in the **incident date** field (Field 3).
4. Check the box next to each service that was needed in response to the emergency, such as medical treatment or transportation, in the **emergency services needed** field (Field 4).
5. Choose the current status of the patient in the **patient status** field (Field 5).
6. Enter a description of the patient's medical condition, if applicable, in the **medical condition** field (Field 6).
7. Enter a description of the injury or incident in the **injury description** field (Field 7).
8. Select the current status of the facility in the **facility status** field (Field 8).
9. Choose the current status of the emergency in the **emergency status** field (Field 9).
10. Enter a brief description of the services received in response to the emergency in the **emergency services** field (Field 10).
11. Enter the name of the patient involved in the **patient name** field (Field 11).
12. Enter the person who reported the incident in the **incident reported by** field (Field 12).
13. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the **contact number** field (Field 13).
14. Indicate whether the facility is a hospital or not in the **facility type** field (Field 14).
15. (Optional) Enter the email address of the contact person in the **contact email** field (Field 15).
16. (Optional) Enter the name of the facility in the **contact facility** field (Field 16).
17. (Optional) Select the type of incident that occurred, if applicable, in the **incident type** field (Field 17).
18. Enter the date and time the incident occurred in the **incident date and time** field (Field 18).
19. Enter the location of the facility in the **facility location** field (Field 19).
20. Choose the current status of the emergency in the **emergency status 2** field (Field 20).
21. (Optional) Enter a brief narrative description of the incident in the **incident narrative** field (Field 21).
22. (Optional) Choose the current status of the facility in the **facility status 2** field (Field 22).
23. (Optional) Enter the name of the emergency contact in the **emergency contact name** field (Field 23).
24. (Optional) Enter the email address of the emergency contact in the **emergency contact email** field (Field 24).
25. (Optional) Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the **emergency contact phone** field (Field 25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency contact name (1)**: Enter the name of the person who can provide emergency contact information.
* **location (2)**: Enter the location where the emergency occurred.
* **incident date (3)**: Select the date of the incident using the calendar.
* **emergency services needed (4)**: Check the boxes of services that were needed in response to the emergency. Options include medical treatment and transportation.
* **patient status (5)**: Choose the current status of the patient (e.g., stable, critical, deceased).
* **medical condition (6)**: Enter a description of the patient's medical condition, if applicable.
* **injury description (7)**: Enter a description of the injury or incident.
* **facility status (8)**: Select the current status of the facility (e.g., closed, restricted, open).
* **emergency status (9)**: Choose the current status of the emergency.
* **emergency services (10)**: Enter a brief description of the services received in response to the emergency.
* **patient name (11)**: Enter the name of the patient involved.
* **incident reported by (12)**: Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
* **contact number (13)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **facility type (14)**: Indicate whether the facility is a hospital or not.
* **contact email (15)**: (Optional) Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact facility (16)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the facility.
* **incident type (17)**: (Optional) Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **incident date and time (18)**: Enter the date and time of the incident.
* **facility location (19)**: Enter the location of the facility.
* **emergency status 2 (20)**: Choose the current status of the emergency.
* **incident narrative (21)**: (Optional) Enter a brief narrative description of the incident.
* **facility status 2 (22)**: (Optional) Choose the current status of the facility.
* **emergency contact name (23)**: (Optional) Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **emergency contact email (24)**: (Optional) Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
* **emergency contact phone (25)**: (Optional) Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact the emergency contact or the facility's administration for clarification.
* This form is for emergency assessment purposes only.
