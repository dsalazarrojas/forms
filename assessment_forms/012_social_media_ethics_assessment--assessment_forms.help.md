# Social Media Ethics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Media Ethics Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's understanding and adherence to social media ethics, including responsible social media usage, respect for privacy, no harassment, no bullying, reporting procedures, consequences of breach of ethics, data sharing, online safety, digital rights, online community standards, digital identity, digital conduct, and social media etiquette. This form helps ensure that individuals maintain a positive and respectful online presence, protecting themselves and others from potential harm.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Social Media Ethics Assessment form, follow these steps:

* Please answer all questions on each page.
* Select all relevant options that apply to your social media usage and online behavior.
* Make sure to answer honestly and thoroughly, as this assessment is designed to help you develop healthy online habits.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Social media usage** (`social_media_usage`, text, required): Please describe your current social media usage, including the platforms you use and your typical online activities.
* **Online Identity** (`online_identity`, number, required): Please rate your comfort level with creating and sharing online content that represents yourself, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Social Media Ethics** (`social_media_ethics`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for responsible social media usage, such as "Responsible social media usage", "Respect for privacy", "No harassment", and "No bullying".
* **Reporting procedures** (`reporting_procedures`, select_one, required): Choose the reporting procedure you would use in case of a social media breach of ethics.
* **Consequences of breach of ethics** (`consequences_of_breach`, number, optional): Please rate the consequences you face when there is a breach of ethics, from 1 (low) to 5 (high).
* **Data sharing** (`data_sharing`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for data sharing, such as "Option 1" and "Option 2".
* **Online safety** (`online_safety`, time, required): Please report your online safety procedures, including how often you update your passwords and which security measures you take.
* **Digital rights** (`digital_rights`, select_one, required): Choose the digital right you prioritize, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
* **Online community standards** (`online_community_standards`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for online community standards, such as "Option 1" and "Option 2".
* **Digital identity** (`digital_identity`, text, required): Describe your digital identity, including how you present yourself online and your level of self-awareness.
* **Digital conduct** (`digital_conduct`, select_one, required): Choose the digital conduct you practice online, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2".
* **Social media etiquette** (`social_media_etiquette`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable options for social media etiquette, such as "Option 1" and "Option 2".

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any question, consult with a colleague or supervisor.
* This assessment is designed to help you develop healthy online habits; please take it seriously.
