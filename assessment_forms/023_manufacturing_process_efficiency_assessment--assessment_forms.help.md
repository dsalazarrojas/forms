# Manufacturing Process Efficiency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the efficiency of a manufacturing process. This assessment is used to evaluate the process's current state and provide a score and rating to identify areas for improvement. This form is designed to be completed by process owners, supervisors, or other relevant personnel to provide a comprehensive evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the manufacturing process and gathering relevant information about its current state.
2. Answer the questions on this form based on your knowledge and observations.
3. Enter your assessment score and rating for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Manufacturing Process Efficiency Assessment
* **Manufacturing Process Efficiency Assessment** (`manufacturing_process_efficiency_assessment`, text, required: false): This is the main question where you need to provide a brief description of the current state of the manufacturing process.
* **Score** (`manufacturing_process_efficiency_score`, number, required: false): Enter a score out of 5 for the current state of the manufacturing process. Higher scores indicate better efficiency.
* **Rating** (`manufacturing_process_efficiency_rating`, number, required: false): Enter a rating out of 10 for the current state of the manufacturing process. This rating is used to provide a more subjective evaluation of the process's efficiency.

### Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
* Use your best judgment when entering scores and ratings.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please contact your supervisor or process owner for guidance.
