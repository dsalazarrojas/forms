# tree species knowledge assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a participant's knowledge of tree species. It is likely used in a research study or educational setting to collect data on participants' understanding of tree species.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own set of questions or fields. Please answer each section carefully.
2. For the `Tree Species Quiz` section, select all the correct answers that apply from the provided options.
3. In the `Reflection` section, provide a written note about your thought process or reasoning behind your quiz answers.
4. For the `Participant Details` section, enter the required number of participants or individuals being assessed.
5. Enter the quiz score achieved and the time taken to complete the quiz in the respective fields.
6. In the `Score Interpretation` section, select the option that best describes your understanding of your quiz score.
7. Finally, in the `Score Rationale` section, select all the reasons that justify your score interpretation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tree Species Quiz** (`tree_species_quiz`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ This section presents a list of multiple-choice questions related to tree species. Select all the correct answers that apply to your understanding of tree species.
* **Reflection** (`reflection`, `note`, optional):
	+ Please provide a written note explaining your thought process or reasoning behind your quiz answers.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `number`, optional):
	+ If applicable, enter the number of participants or individuals being assessed.
* **Quiz Score** (`quiz_score`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the score you achieved in the quiz.
* **Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date you completed the quiz.
* **Time Taken** (`time_taken`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time it took you to complete the quiz.
* **Score Interpretation** (`score_interpretation`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the option that best describes your understanding of your quiz score.
* **Score Rationale** (`score_rationale`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all the reasons that justify your score interpretation.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Make sure to enter accurate and truthful information for each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification or help.
