# pre_job_technical_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate's suitability for a job, particularly their technical skills and availability for various shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your available technical skills from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your available shifts for each shift type (morning, afternoon, evening).
4. Enter any additional notes or comments if needed.
5. Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Candidate Info** (`candidate_info`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about yourself (e.g., job title, company).
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary technical skill from the dropdown menu.
* **Technical Skills Other** (`technical_skills_other`, `text`, required): If not listed, enter your technical skill here.
* **Available For Shift** (`available_for_shift`, `note`, required): Enter the shift type(s) you are available for.
* **Available From Shift** (`available_from_shift`, `note`, required): Enter the start time you are available for the shift.
* **Available To Shift** (`available_to_shift`, `note`, required): Enter the end time you are available for the shift.
* **Available For Shift 3** (`available_for_shift_3`, `note`, required): Enter the shift type(s) you are available for.
* **Available From Shift 3** (`available_from_shift_3`, `note`, required): Enter the start time you are available for the shift.
* **Available To Shift 3** (`available_to_shift_3`, `note`, required): Enter the end time you are available for the shift.
* **Shift 1** (`shift_1`, `select_one`, required): Select your available shift type for the first shift.
* **Shift 2** (`shift_2`, `select_one`, required): Select your available shift type for the second shift.
* **Available Shift 1** (`available_shift_1`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the shift type(s) you are available for.
* **Available Shift 2** (`available_shift_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the shift type(s) you are available for.
* **Available Shift 3** (`available_shift_3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the shift type(s) you are available for.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date you submitted the form.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by candidates, and the fields are intended to be filled out with their availability and technical skills. The form is not meant to be used for internal or administrative purposes.
