# Retail Security Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Security Assessment Survey is designed to help retailers evaluate and improve their store's security measures, policies, and procedures. This form will help you identify potential security gaps and weaknesses, and provide a comprehensive assessment of your store's safety and security protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Be sure to provide specific examples or details when possible.
3. Use the provided options as a guide, but feel free to provide additional information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store_location (ID 1)**: The name of your store or location.
* **store_address (ID 2)**: The address of your store or location.
* **store_hours_of_operation (ID 3)**: The hours of operation for your store, including start and end times.
* **number_of_employees (ID 4)**: The number of employees working at your store at any given time.
* **manager_name (ID 5)**: The name of your store's manager or supervisor.
* **security_camera_system (ID 6)**: A list of cameras installed in your store, including their locations and types.
* **alarm_system (ID 7)**: A list of alarm systems installed in your store, including their types and locations.
* **emergency_plan (ID 8)**: A detailed description of your store's emergency plan, including procedures and protocols.
* **security_training (ID 9)**: Information about security training programs or courses completed by employees, including frequency and duration.
* **store_locks (ID 10)**: A list of locks installed in your store, including their types and locations.
* **store_security_policy (ID 11)**: A detailed description of your store's security policy, including protocols and procedures.
* **loss_prevention_measures (ID 12)**: Information about loss prevention measures in place, including types and effectiveness.
* **incident_reporting (ID 13)**: A description of your store's incident reporting procedures, including frequency and protocols.
* **incident_response (ID 14)**: A detailed description of your store's incident response procedures, including protocols and procedures.
* **employee_background_check (ID 15)**: Information about employee background checks, including types and frequency.
* **visitor_log_book (ID 16)**: A description of your store's visitor log book, including types and frequency of entries.
* **security_policy (ID 17)**: A detailed description of your store's security policy, including protocols and procedures.
* **emergency_exit (ID 18)**: A list of emergency exits in your store, including locations and types.
* **security_training_program (ID 19)**: Information about security training programs or courses completed by employees, including frequency and duration.
* **store_security_measures (ID 20)**: A list of security measures in place, including types and locations.
* **store_safety_measures (ID 21)**: A list of safety measures in place, including types and locations.
* **incident_reporting_frequency (ID 22)**: The frequency of incident reporting at your store.
* **store_safety_measures_frequency (ID 23)**: The frequency of safety measures implementation at your store.
* **employee_conduct (ID 24)**: A description of employee conduct, including policies and procedures.
* **store_security_measures_effectiveness (ID 25)**: A description of the effectiveness of store safety measures, including impact on store operations.

Note: The provided explanations are based on the form structure and field labels. If you are unsure about a specific field or option, please contact your security team or manager for guidance.
