# Extraordinary Exam Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Extraordinary Exam Assessment" form is designed to collect feedback and comments from students about their exam experience. This form is meant to help instructors and educators identify areas of improvement and provide insights to enhance future exam development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
2. Provide specific examples or details to support your answers.
3. Use the "Comments" fields to add any additional information that may be relevant to your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was the most challenging part of the exam?** (`exam_questionnaire`, text, required): This is the first question on the form. Please describe the most challenging part of the exam you experienced. Be as specific as possible so that we can understand the difficulties you faced.
* **Student progress** (`student_progress`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe your progress during the exam. You can mention any areas where you felt you were doing well or struggling.
* **Comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Here, you can add any additional comments or thoughts about your exam experience. This is a good place to provide feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **Comments from educator** (`educator_comments`, text, optional): This field is for the educator/instructor to provide feedback about your performance during the exam. Please ask them to be as specific as possible so that we can understand their perspective.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as your feedback is valuable for improving the exam.
* If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them in the "Comments" fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
