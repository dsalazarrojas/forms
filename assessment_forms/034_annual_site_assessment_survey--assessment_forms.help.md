# Annual Site Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Annual Site Assessment Survey is a tool to evaluate the overall condition and compliance of a facility.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your observations and findings.
3. Provide any additional notes or observations that may be relevant to the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Site Name or Location** (`site_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility or site being assessed.
* **Site Address** (`site_address`, text, required): Enter the full address of the facility or site.
* **Site ID or Code** (`site_id`, text, optional): Enter the internal identification number of the facility or site, if applicable.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Role** (`assessor_role`, text, optional): Enter the position or expertise of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Type of Assessment** (`assessment_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of assessment being conducted (e.g., Compliance, Safety, Operational, etc.).
* **Overall Site Condition** (`overall_condition`, select_one, required): Assess the general condition of the facility (e.g., Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Critical).
* **Safety Standards Compliance** (`safety_standards`, select_one, required): Assess the compliance with safety regulations (e.g., Fully compliant, Mostly compliant with minor issues, Non-compliant in some areas, etc.).
* **Cleanliness and Sanitation** (`cleanliness`, select_one, required): Assess the overall cleanliness of the facility (e.g., Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Unacceptable).
* **Maintenance Condition** (`maintenance`, select_one, required): Assess the condition of facility equipment and maintenance (e.g., Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Poor, Needs urgent repairs).
* **Equipment Functionality** (`equipment_functionality`, select_one, required): Assess the operational status of equipment (e.g., All equipment operational, Minor issues only, Some equipment non-functional, etc.).
* **Pest Control Evidence** (`pest_control`, select_one, optional): Assess the presence of pest control measures (e.g., No pest evidence, Minor signs observed, Moderate infestation, Severe infestation).
* **Security Systems and Access** (`security`, select_one, required): Assess the functionality of security systems and access control (e.g., Excellent - all systems functional, Good - mostly functional, Satisfactory - some issues, etc.).
* **Compliance Issues Identified** (`compliance_issues`, text, optional): List any identified compliance issues or regulations non-compliant.
* **Safety Hazards Identified** (`hazards_identified`, text, optional): Identify any specific safety hazards or risks found.
* **Maintenance or Repairs Needed** (`maintenance_needed`, text, optional): List any required maintenance or repairs needed.
* **Priority Actions Required** (`priority_actions`, text, optional): Identify any priority actions required to address issues.
* **Estimated Cost of Repairs** (`estimated_repairs_cost`, number, optional): Estimate the cost of necessary improvements or repairs.
* **Timeline to Achieve Compliance** (`timeline_compliance`, text, optional): Provide a timeline for achieving compliance.
* **Recommended Next Assessment Date** (`next_assessment_date`, date, required): Select the recommended next assessment date.
* **Additional Notes or Observations** (`assessor_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes or observations about the assessment.
