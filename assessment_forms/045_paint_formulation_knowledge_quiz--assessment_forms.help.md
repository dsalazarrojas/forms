# Paint Formulation Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paint Formulation Knowledge Quiz is designed to test your understanding of paint formulation, a process that involves mixing and blending various materials to create different colors and finishes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps outlined in the instructions above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on your results.
* **What is Paint** (`what_is_paint`, `text`, required): Briefly describe what you think "paint" is.
* **What is Paint Color** (`paint_formulation_knowledge_quiz_color`, `text`, required): Describe what you believe "paint color" is.
* **Paint Formulation** (`paint_formulation`, `text`, required): Explain your understanding of paint formulation.
* **Application Methods** (`application_methods`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the application methods you are familiar with: Spray, Roll, Brush, or Pour.
* **Finish** (`finish`, `text`, required): Mention the finish you typically achieve with paint.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required): Specify your goals in completing this form.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your results.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required): Select the date you submitted your answers.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`, required): Choose the time you submitted your answers.

## Tips
Be as specific as possible when answering each question, and don't worry if you're not sure of some answers. Your results will help us gauge your understanding of paint formulation.
