# Pharmacology MCQ Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Pharmacology MCQ Quiz form is designed to collect data from users who have completed a pharmacology quiz. This form captures essential information such as user details, quiz completion status, scores, and times spent. The form is used to track and analyze user engagement with the quiz.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Pharmacology MCQ Quiz form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the first field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email** if you'd like to receive any updates or communications. This field is optional.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for contact purposes. This is a required field.
4. Select the status of your quiz completion from the **Quiz Completed** dropdown menu.
5. Enter your **Score** achieved in the quiz.
6. Indicate the **Time Spent** on the quiz by selecting from the given options.
7. Enter the **Date** and **Start Time** and **End Time** of your quiz completion.
8. Select the option for **Submitted By** to indicate your role.
9. Choose the option for **Submitted At** to indicate the time of submission.
10. Add any **Comments** about your experience with the quiz.
11. Select any other relevant **Quiz ID** or **User Info**, **Other Info**, **Score Info**, **Other Info 2**, **Other Score**, **Other Time**, **Other Date**, **Other Start Time**, **Other End Time**, **Other Submitted By**, **Other Submitted At**, or **Other Comments** fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name (User Name, text, required)**: Please enter your name as it will be displayed on your quiz results.
* **Email (Email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address if you'd like to receive any updates or communications.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Quiz Completed (Select One, not required)**: Select the status of your quiz completion.
* **Score (Number, required)**: Enter your achieved score in the quiz.
* **Time Spent (Select One, not required)**: Indicate the time spent on the quiz by selecting from the given options.
* **Date (Date, not required)**: Enter the date of your quiz completion.
* **Start Time (Time, not required)**: Enter the start time of your quiz completion.
* **End Time (Time, not required)**: Enter the end time of your quiz completion.
* **Submitted By (Select One, not required)**: Select your role in completing the quiz.
* **Submitted At (Select One, not required)**: Choose the time of submission.
* **Comments (Note, not required)**: Add any comments about your experience with the quiz.
* **Quiz ID (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any relevant quiz IDs.
* **User Info (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any relevant user info.
* **Other Info (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other info relevant to your experience.
* **Score Info (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any score info relevant to your experience.
* **Other Info 2 (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other info 2 relevant to your experience.
* **Other Score (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other score info relevant to your experience.
* **Other Time (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other time info relevant to your experience.
* **Other Date (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other date info relevant to your experience.
* **Other Start Time (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other start time info relevant to your experience.
* **Other End Time (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other end time info relevant to your experience.
* **Other Submitted By (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other submitted by info relevant to your experience.
* **Other Submitted At (Select Multiple, not required)**: Select any other submitted at info relevant to your experience.
