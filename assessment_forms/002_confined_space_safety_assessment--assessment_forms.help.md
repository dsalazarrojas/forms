# Confined Space Safety Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you assess the safety of confined spaces. Fill out this form to identify and control hazards, evaluate risks, and take necessary precautions for safe entry into confined spaces.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct answers to each question based on the information about the confined space and the work to be performed.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible, including any relevant notes or comments.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date**: The date when this safety assessment is conducted.
* **Assessor Name**: Name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Qualification**: The certification or qualification of the assessor.
* **Facility Name**: The name of the facility or worksite.
* **Confined Space Identifier**: A unique ID or tag for the confined space.
* **Type of Confined Space**: Select the category of confined space, such as Storage Tank, Silo, Vessel, etc.
* **Space Dimensions**: Approximate size of the confined space.
* **Number of Entry Points**: How many access points does the space have.
* **Previous Incidents**: Indicate if there have been any prior incidents or near misses.
* **Incident Details**: Provide a description of any previous incidents if applicable.
* **Potential Atmospheric Hazards**: Select all potential atmospheric hazards present, such as Oxygen Deficiency, Oxygen Enrichment, etc.
* **Physical Hazards**: Select all physical hazards present, such as Engulfment Risk, Falling Objects, etc.
* **Biological Hazards**: Indicate if there are potential biological hazards, such as Bacteria or Mold, Viruses, etc.
* **Type of Work to Be Performed**: Describe the nature of work inside the space, such as Inspection, Cleaning, etc.
* **Expected Duration of Work**: Indicate how long workers will be in the space.
* **Hot Work Required**: Indicate if welding or cutting will be performed.
* **Chemicals Used**: Indicate if chemicals will be used.
* **Chemical Details**: List chemicals and provide any SDS if applicable.
* **On-Site Rescue Capability**: Indicate if the facility has trained rescue personnel.
* **Expected Rescue Response Time**: Indicate how quickly rescue can be initiated.
* **Overall Risk Level**: Assess the overall risk for entry into the space.
* **Control Measures**: Select all measures needed to control hazards, such as Continuous Atmospheric Monitoring, etc.
* **Additional Safety Recommendations**: Provide any other safety measures or notes.
* **Assessment Conclusion**: Determine if it's safe to enter the space, and if so, under what conditions.
* **Assessor Signature**: The name of the assessor confirming the assessment.

## Tips

- Always ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information.
- Use this form in conjunction with other safety protocols and guidelines.
- Review and update this form regularly to ensure it remains relevant and effective.
