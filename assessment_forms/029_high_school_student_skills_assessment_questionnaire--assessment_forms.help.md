# high_school_student_skills_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills and qualifications of a high school student.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your student ID in the **student_id** field.
2. Enter your name in the **student_name** field.
3. Enter your grades for math, English, science, social science, and foreign languages.
4. Select one or more career aspirations that you have.
5. Enter your graduation year in the **graduation_year** field.
6. Select your graduation status from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_id** (required): Enter a unique identifier for yourself.
* **student_name** (required): Enter your full name.
* **math_grade** (required): Enter your grade in math subject (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
* **math_subjects** (not required): Select one math subject that you excel at (Algebra, Calculus, Geometry, Trigonometry, Statistics).
* **english_grade** (required): Enter your grade in English subject (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
* **science_grade** (required): Enter your grade in science subject (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
* **social_science_grade** (required): Enter your grade in social science subject (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
* **foreign_language_grade** (required): Enter your grade in foreign language subject (e.g., 9, 10, 11, 12).
* **social_skills** (required): Describe your social skills (e.g., teamwork, leadership, communication).
* **career_aspiration** (not required): Select one or more career aspirations that you have (e.g., Business/Entrepreneurship, Art/Design, Engineering, Social Science).
* **graduation_year** (required): Enter the year you plan to graduate.
* **graduation_status** (not required): Select your current graduation status (e.g., High School, Currently in high school, Some college, Associates, Bachelor, Master, Doctoral).
