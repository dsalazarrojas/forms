# Pre Op Anesthetic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Op Anesthetic Assessment Form is designed to collect information about a patient's preoperative and postoperative condition. This form is used by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's risks and needs before and after surgery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information (page 1)
	* Fill in the patient's details, including name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Provide medical history (page 2)
	* Answer the questions regarding the patient's medical history, including allergies, medications, and past medical conditions.
3. Choose the type of anesthesia (page 3)
	* Select the type of anesthesia that will be used during the procedure.
4. List any allergies and past medications (page 4)
	* Enter any allergies or past medications that the patient has.
5. List any preoperative findings (page 5)
	* Enter any findings from previous medical tests or evaluations.
6. Choose any general anesthetics (page 7)
	* Select any general anesthetics that will be used during the procedure.
7. List any monitoring required (page 8)
	* Enter any continuous monitoring required for the patient.
8. List any other procedures (page 9)
	* Enter any other procedures that will be performed during the surgery.
9. Enter the anesthetic plan (page 10)
	* Describe the anesthetic plan for the patient.
10. List any post-anesthesia concerns (page 11)
	* Enter any concerns about the patient's post-anesthesia recovery.
11. Enter the name of the anesthesiologist (page 12)
	* Enter the name of the anesthesiologist who will be administering the anesthetic.
12. Enter the date and time of the last anesthetic (page 13)
	* Enter the date and time of the last anesthetic administered to the patient.
13. Enter the date and time of the pre-op assessment (page 14)
	* Enter the date and time of the pre-op assessment.
14. Enter the name of the pre-op assessment provider (page 15)
	* Enter the name of the healthcare professional who performed the pre-op assessment.
15. Enter any pre-op notes (page 16)
	* Enter any notes or comments about the pre-op assessment.
16. List any post-op notes (page 17)
	* Enter any notes or comments about the post-op recovery.
17. List any other procedures (page 18)
	* Enter any other procedures that will be performed during the surgery.
18. Choose any patient concerns (page 20)
	* Select any concerns about the patient's condition.
19. List any pre-op findings (page 21)
	* Enter any findings from previous medical tests or evaluations.
20. List any post-op findings (page 22)
	* Enter any findings from post-op medical tests or evaluations.
21. List any pre-op recommendations (page 23)
	* Enter any recommendations for the patient's pre-op care.
22. List any post-op recommendations (page 24)
	* Enter any recommendations for the patient's post-op care.
23. List any post-op comments (page 25)
	* Enter any comments about the patient's post-op recovery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information (page 1)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical History (page 2)**: Answer the questions regarding the patient's medical history, including allergies, medications, and past medical conditions.
* **Anesthesia Medication (page 3)**: Select the type of anesthesia that will be used during the procedure.
* **Allergies (page 4)**: List any allergies the patient has.
* **Past Medications (page 4)**: List any past medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Surgical Site (page 5)**: List any preoperative findings from previous medical tests or evaluations.
* **General Anesthetic (page 7)**: Select any general anesthetics that will be used during the procedure.
* **Monitoring (page 8)**: List any continuous monitoring required for the patient.
* **Other Procedure (page 9)**: List any other procedures that will be performed during the surgery.
* **Anesthetic Plan (page 10)**: Describe the anesthetic plan for the patient.
* **Post Anesthesia (page 11)**: List any concerns about the patient's post-anesthesia recovery.
* **Anesthesia Provider (page 12)**: Enter the name of the anesthesiologist who will be administering the anesthetic.
* **Anesthesia Date (page 13)**: Enter the date of the last anesthetic administered to the patient.
* **Anesthesia Time (page 13)**: Enter the time of the last anesthetic administered to the patient.
* **Pre-Op Assessment Date (page 14)**: Enter the date of the pre-op assessment.
* **Pre-Op Assessment Provider (page 15)**: Enter the name of the healthcare professional who performed the pre-op assessment.
* **Pre-Op Notes (page 16)**: Enter any notes or comments about the pre-op assessment.
* **Post-Op Notes (page 17)**: Enter any notes or comments about the post-op recovery.
* **Other (page 18)**: List any other procedures that will be performed during the surgery.
* **Patient Concerns (page 20)**: Select any concerns about the patient's condition.
* **Pre-Op Findings (page 21)**: Enter any findings from previous medical tests or evaluations.
* **Post-Op Findings (page 22)**: Enter any findings from post-op medical tests or evaluations.
* **Pre-Op Recommendations (page 23)**: Enter any recommendations for the patient's pre-op care.
* **Post-Op Recommendations (page 24)**: Enter any recommendations for the patient's post-op care.
* **Post-Op Comments (page 25)**: Enter any comments about the patient's post-op recovery.
