# DSM 5 Level 1 Cross Cutting Symptom Measure Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess an individual's mental health symptoms and functioning across various domains, as described in the DSM-5 criteria. The assessment includes sections on symptom areas, symptom severity, and overall functioning.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select one or multiple answers for each question. For select multiple questions, choose all that apply.
2. Fill out the form carefully, as the information collected will help healthcare providers make informed decisions about your treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Symptom Areas** (`symptom_areas`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field asks whether you experience any symptoms from the following areas: 
    * Depression Symptoms
    * Anxiety
    * Psychosis
    * Goal-oriented behavior
* **Depression Symptoms** (`depression_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all the depression symptoms that you experience.
* **Anxiety** (`anxiety`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check the anxiety symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Psychosis** (`psychosis`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all the psychosis symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Goal** (`goal`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check if you have any goal-oriented behavior.
* **Somatic Symptoms** (`somatic_symptoms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate all somatic symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Sleep Disturbances** (`sleep_disturbances`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check any sleep disturbances you are experiencing.
* **Eating Disturbances** (`eating_disturbances`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all eating disturbances you are experiencing.
* **Mood** (`mood`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your mood.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your motivation level.
* **Concentration** (`concentration`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your concentration level.
* **Cognitive Function** (`cognitive_function`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your cognitive function level.
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your current sleep quality.
* **Sexual Functioning** (`sexual_functioning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check your current sexual functioning.
* **Physical Functioning** (`physical_functioning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your current physical functioning level.
* **Social Functioning** (`social_functioning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check your current social functioning level.
* **Self Care** (`self_care`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all self-care practices you are following.
* **Interpersonal Relationships** (`interpersonal_relationships`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your current interpersonal relationships.
* **Occupational Functioning** (`occupational_functioning`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check your current occupational functioning level.
* **Total Score** (`total_score`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check the total score.
* **Total Score 1** (`total_score_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check the total score 1.
* **Symptoms Severity** (`symptoms_severity`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check the symptoms severity.
* **Severity of Symptoms** (`severity_of_symptoms`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check the severity of symptoms.
* **Severity of Symptoms 1** (`severity_of_symptoms_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check the severity of symptoms 1.
* **Symptoms Severity 1** (`symptoms_severity_1`, `select_one`, required/optional): Check the symptoms severity 1.

**Tips**

* Be honest when answering questions about your symptoms and functioning.
* If you are unsure about a particular symptom or functioning area, try to answer based on how you have been feeling in the past week.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your healthcare provider.
