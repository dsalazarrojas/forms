# Business Vision Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form assesses your understanding and alignment with your organization's vision, values, and strategic priorities. It's a self-assessment tool to help you evaluate your own awareness, understanding, and commitment to the company's vision and values.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by providing your answers to each question or field.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
3. Review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant Name (<name>: <participant_name>)
*   *Participant Name* is a required field that asks for your full name.
*   Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.

### 2. Title or Position (<name>: <participant_title>)
*   *Title or Position* is a required field that asks for your title or position within the organization.
*   Enter your current title or position within the organization.

### 3. Organization Name (<name>: <organization_name>)
*   *Organization Name* is a required field that asks for the name of the organization you are associated with.
*   Enter the name of the organization you are currently working with.

### 4. Department or Division (<name>: <department>)
*   *Department or Division* is an optional field that asks for your department or division within the organization.
*   If you are unsure, you can leave this field blank.

### 5. Years with Organization (<name>: <tenure_years>)
*   *Years with Organization* is a required field that asks for the number of years you have been with the organization.
*   Enter the number of years you have been working with the organization.

### 6. Clarity of Organization Vision (<name>: <vision_clarity>)
*   *Clarity of Organization Vision* is a required field that asks for your perception of the organization's vision clarity.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your understanding of the company's vision.

### 7. Personal Alignment with Vision (<name>: <vision_alignment>)
*   *Personal Alignment with Vision* is a required field that asks for your level of alignment with the company's vision.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your level of alignment with the company's vision.

### 8. Understanding of Company Values (<name>: <values_understanding>)
*   *Understanding of Company Values* is a required field that asks for your level of understanding of the company's values.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your level of understanding of the company's values.

### 9. Values Practiced in Organization (<name>: <values_practice>)
*   *Values Practiced in Organization* is a required field that asks for your perception of how well the company's values are practiced within the organization.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your perception of the company's values practice.

### 10. Awareness of Strategic Priorities (<name>: <strategic_priorities>)
*   *Awareness of Strategic Priorities* is a required field that asks for your level of awareness of the company's strategic priorities.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your level of awareness of the company's strategic priorities.

### 11. Ability to Contribute to Strategy (<name>: <strategy_contribution>)
*   *Ability to Contribute to Strategy* is a required field that asks for your ability to contribute to achieving the company's strategic priorities.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your ability to contribute to the company's strategy.

### 12. Confidence in Leadership Direction (<name>: <leadership_confidence>)
*   *Confidence in Leadership Direction* is a required field that asks for your level of confidence in the company's leadership direction.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your level of confidence in the company's leadership direction.

### 13. Effectiveness of Vision Communication (<name>: <communication_effectiveness>)
*   *Effectiveness of Vision Communication* is a required field that asks for your perception of how well the company's vision is communicated.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your perception of the company's vision communication.

### 14. Suggestions for Improvement (<name>: <vision_improvements>)
*   *Suggestions for Improvement* is an optional field that asks for any suggestions you may have for improving the company's vision.
*   If you have any suggestions, please provide them here.

### 15. Commitment Level to Vision Success (<name>: <commitment_level>)
*   *Commitment Level to Vision Success* is a required field that asks for your overall commitment level to the company's vision success.
*   Choose one of the options provided to best describe your level of commitment to the company's vision success.

## Tips

*   Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please choose the option that best describes your current situation.
*   Please review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the form administrators or support team for assistance.
