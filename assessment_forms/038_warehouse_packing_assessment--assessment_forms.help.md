# warehouse_packing_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the packing procedure, materials used, and quality of packing in a warehouse. It collects information on the packing process, including the time taken, comments, and observations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the general information (Page 1: General).
2. Describe the packing procedure and materials used (Page 2: Packing Procedure).
3. Indicate the materials used for packing (Page 3: Materials Used).
4. Perform a quality check (Page 4: Quality Check).
5. Record the time taken for packing (Page 5: Packing Time).
6. Add any comments or observations (Page 6: Comments and Page 7: Observations).
7. Sign off (Page 8: Signature).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General** (`1`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the purpose of the packing process.
* **Packing Procedure** (`2`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the steps taken during the packing process.
* **Materials Used** (`3`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more materials used during packing (e.g., Box, Belt, Label).
* **Quality Check** (`4`, `note`, optional): Record any issues or concerns identified during the quality check.
* **Packing Time** (`5`, `time`, required): Record the total time taken for packing.
* **Comments** (`6`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the packing process.
* **Observations** (`7`, `text`, optional): Record any observations or notes about the packing process.
* **Signature** (`8`, `text`, required): Sign off to confirm completion of the assessment.
