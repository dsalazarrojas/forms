# Hairdressing Assessment Summary - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record the details of a client's hair assessment, including their personal details, hair condition, and treatment history.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's details in the "Client Details" field.
2. Select the client's hair condition from the "Hair Condition" dropdown menu.
3. Enter any relevant treatment history in the "Treatment History" field.
4. Enter any stylist recommendations in the "Stylist Recommendations" fields.
5. Upload any relevant images, videos, or audio files using the "Client Image", "Client Video", "Client Audio", and "Client File" fields.
6. Enter any additional client notes in the "Client Notes" field.
7. Enter the stylist's name in the "Stylist Name" field.
8. Enter the stylist's recommendations in the "Stylist Recommendations 2" to "Stylist Recommendations 6" fields.
9. Enter the stylist's notes in the "Stylist Notes" field.
10. Enter the client's dosage in the "Client Dosage" field.
11. Enter the stylist's dosage in the "Stylist Dosage" field.
12. Enter the assessment date and time in the "Assessment Date" and "Assessment Time" fields.
13. Sign the form with the stylist's signature in the "Stylist Signature" field.
14. Sign the form with the client's signature in the "Client Signature" field.
15. Enter the stylist's title in the "Stylist Title" field.
16. Enter the form ID in the "Form ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details**: Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Hair Condition**: Select the client's current hair condition, such as Damaged, Normal, Frizzy, or Dry.
* **Treatment History**: Enter any relevant treatment history, such as previous hair treatments or products used.
* **Stylist Recommendations 1-6**: Enter any recommendations or suggestions for the client's hair care.
* **Client Image**: Upload a relevant image of the client's hair.
* **Client Video**: Upload a relevant video of the client's hair.
* **Client Audio**: Upload a relevant audio file related to the client's hair.
* **Client File**: Upload any relevant files related to the client's hair.
* **Client Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the client's hair.
* **Stylist Name**: Enter the stylist's name and title.
* **Stylist Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the stylist.
* **Client Dosage**: Enter the client's dosage information.
* **Stylist Dosage**: Enter the stylist's dosage information.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time**: Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Stylist Signature**: Sign the form with the stylist's signature.
* **Client Signature**: Sign the form with the client's signature.
* **Stylist Title**: Enter the stylist's title.
* **Form ID**: Enter the unique ID for the form.
