# Personalized Weight Loss Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a personalized weight loss assessment. It will help us understand your health status, medical history, and current weight loss goals, which will enable us to provide a tailored plan for your weight loss journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Answer questions about your health information, including any medical conditions you may have.
3. List any medications you are currently taking.
4. Indicate if you exercise regularly or not.
5. Answer if you are following a dietary plan.
6. Describe your weight loss goals and what motivates you.
7. Answer if you have a weight loss plan in place.
8. Add any notes about your weight loss progress.
9. Finally, provide information about your medical history.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, optional): A brief description of yourself and your health status.
* **Health Information** (`health_information`, text, optional): List any health conditions you may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions you may have that could impact your weight loss journey.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose if you exercise regularly or not:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Dietary Plan** (`diet_plan`, select_one, optional): Choose if you follow a dietary plan:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Weight Loss Goals** (`weight_loss_goals`, text, optional): Describe your weight loss goals and what motivates you.
* **Weight Loss Plan** (`weight_loss_plan`, select_one, optional): Choose if you have a weight loss plan in place:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'
* **Weight Loss Progress** (`weight_loss_progress`, note, optional): Add any notes about your weight loss progress.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide information about your medical history. 

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out to get a comprehensive understanding of your situation.
- Be as detailed as possible when answering each question.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
- We appreciate your honesty in providing accurate information, which will help us create a personalized plan for your weight loss journey.
