# Training Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about training needs, specifically for the purpose of assessing and identifying areas that require development. It is used to collect feedback and input from individuals or organizations on their training requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the training needs assessment survey in the "Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the training needs assessment survey in the "Description" field.
3. In the main field, provide your detailed input on the training needs and requirements. This can include but is not limited to:
* What are the skills or knowledge gaps you have in your current role or organization?
* What training or professional development opportunities would you like to receive?
* Are there any specific industries or fields you are looking to develop skills in?
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, required/optional): Enter a concise title that captures the essence of the training needs assessment survey.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the training needs assessment survey.
* **Training Needs Assessment Survey** (`training_needs_assessment_survey`, text, required/optional): This is the main field for providing detailed input on training needs and requirements. Use this space to describe the specific skills or knowledge gaps you have, and suggest relevant training or professional development opportunities.
