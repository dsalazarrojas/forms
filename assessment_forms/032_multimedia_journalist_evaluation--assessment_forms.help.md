# Multimedia Journalist Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Multimedia Journalist Evaluation form is designed to assess the work and performance of multimedia journalists in various media and formats, such as video, audio, image, social media, and text. This form helps to evaluate their ability to create engaging and informative content, covering story length, type, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of media used for the story (e.g., video, audio, image, social media, or text).
2. Choose the story length in hours or days (if applicable).
3. Select the type of story (e.g., feature, news, blog, or other).
4. Enter the name of the journalist responsible for the story.
5. Choose the department or team the journalist belongs to (if applicable).
6. Select the supervisor of the journalist (e.g., manager, editor, or both).
7. Enter the date and time of the event (if applicable).
8. Enter the location of the story (if applicable).
9. Enter the URL of the story (if applicable).
10. Enter a title for the story.
11. Enter a summary of the story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Used** (`media_used`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the media types that were used to create the story, such as video, audio, image, social media, or text.
