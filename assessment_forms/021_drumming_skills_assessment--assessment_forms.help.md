# drumming_skills_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a student's drumming skills and provide valuable information for instructors and teachers to evaluate their progress and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the number of years the student has been playing drums in the "years_playing" field.
3. Select the student's current level of skills from the "skills_level" dropdown menu.
4. Choose the student's performance style from the "performance_style" dropdown menu.
5. If desired, add any additional comments or notes in the "comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name** (Text, Required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their record.
* **years_playing** (Number, Required): Enter the number of years the student has been playing drums.
* **skills_level** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose one or more skills levels that apply to the student's abilities:
	+ Basic: The student is just starting to learn drumming basics.
	+ Intermediate: The student has some experience and knowledge of drumming techniques.
	+ Advanced: The student has a high level of skill and proficiency in drumming.
* **performance_style** (Select One, Required): Choose the student's primary performance style:
	+ Rock: The student performs primarily in a rock music genre.
	+ Jazz: The student performs primarily in a jazz music genre.
	+ Pop: The student performs primarily in a pop music genre.
	+ Classical: The student performs primarily in a classical music genre.
	+ Folk: The student performs primarily in a folk music genre.
	+ Other: The student performs in a different genre not listed above.
* **comments** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the student's skills or progress.
