# Signal Words Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signal Words Assessment form is used to evaluate a student's performance in reading and understanding during a specific lesson or learning activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Student Name** field with the student's name you are assessing.
2. Indicate the student's response to **Signal Words** and select all the signal words that apply from the dropdown list.
3. Select all the signal words that apply for **Reading Transitions** from the dropdown list.
4. Select all the signal words that apply for **Student Understanding** from the dropdown list.
5. Type any comments or feedback you have for the student in the **Teacher Comments** field.
6. Enter the date you completed the assessment in the **Assessment Date** field.
7. Enter the time you completed the assessment in the **Assessment Time** field.
8. Enter your name in the **Submitted By** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official records.
* **Signal Words**: Check all the signal words that the student has used during the lesson or learning activity to indicate understanding.
* **Reading Transitions**: Select all the reading transitions that the student has demonstrated during the lesson or learning activity.
* **Student Understanding**: Check all the signal words that indicate the student's level of understanding during the assessment.
* **Teacher Comments**: Type any comments or feedback you have for the student, such as strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you completed the assessment in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Assessment Time**: Enter the time you completed the assessment in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name as it appears on your official records.
