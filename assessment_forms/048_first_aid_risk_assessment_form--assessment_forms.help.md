# First Aid Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Aid Risk Assessment Form is designed to assess the risk factors and first aid facilities in a workplace, school, or any other facility, ensuring that emergency situations can be handled effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form completely, as it requires all fields to be filled out accurately.
- Answer all questions truthfully, as this will help identify potential risks and areas for improvement.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it, to ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information.
- Once you have filled out the form, submit it to the reviewed_by_facility field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Aid Risk Form**: Enter a brief description of the first aid facilities and equipment available in the facility.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
- **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where the form is being completed.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **First Aid Supplies**: Enter a list of first aid supplies available in the facility.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Accident Prevention**: Describe any measures taken to prevent accidents in the facility.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Emergency Contact**: Provide information about the emergency contact person or department.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Medical Facilities**: Indicate if the facility has access to medical facilities.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Required:** true
  - **Options:** Yes/No
- **Emergency Plan**: Describe the emergency plan in place in the facility.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Site Characteristics**: Describe any site-specific characteristics that could affect emergency response.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **First Aid Risks**: Indicate if the facility has first aid risks.
  - **Type:** select_multiple
  - **Required:** true
  - **Options:** Yes/No
- **Risk Factors**: Describe any risk factors that could affect emergency response.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Action Plan**: Describe the action plan in place in the facility.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Follow-up**: Describe any follow-up actions taken after an emergency.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Reviewed Date**: Enter the date when the form was reviewed.
  - **Type:** date
  - **Required:** true
- **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Reviewed By Title**: Enter the title of the person who reviewed the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Comments**: Enter any comments or suggestions from the reviewer.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
- **Reviewed By Position**: Enter the position of the person who reviewed the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
- **Reviewed By Email**: Enter the email of the person who reviewed the form.
  - **Type:** email
  - **Required:** false
- **Reviewed By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person who reviewed the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
- **Facility Signature**: Enter the signature of the facility.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Reviewed By Facility**: Enter the name of the facility that reviewed the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** true
- **Approved Date**: Enter the date when the form was approved.
  - **Type:** date
  - **Required:** false
- **Approved By**: Enter the name of the person who approved the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
- **Approved By Title**: Enter the title of the person who approved the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
- **Approved By Position**: Enter the position of the person who approved the form.
  - **Type:** text
  - **Required:** false
