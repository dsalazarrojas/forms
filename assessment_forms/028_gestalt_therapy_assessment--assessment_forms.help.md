# Gestalt Therapy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather client reflections, emotions, and feelings in the context of Gestalt Therapy. It is designed to help therapists understand the client's mental state and provide a comprehensive assessment of their well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your reflections on your current mental state. Please be as detailed and honest as possible in your response.
2. Next, select any applicable emotions that you are currently experiencing. Choose one or more of the options provided.
3. Finally, provide a description of your current feelings in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client reflections** (`client_reflections`, text, required: false): This field is for you to express your thoughts and feelings about your current situation. Please provide a detailed description of your reflections, including any concerns, fears, or hopes you have.
- **Client emotions** (`client_emotions`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select any emotions you are currently experiencing. Choose one or more of the options provided to help the therapist understand your emotional state.
- **Client feelings** (`client_feelings`, text, required: false): This field is for you to describe your current feelings in more detail. Please be as specific as possible in your response.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and thoroughly to provide the best possible assessment.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for clarification from the therapist.
- Remember, this form is a tool to help the therapist understand you better, so please be as detailed and open as possible.
