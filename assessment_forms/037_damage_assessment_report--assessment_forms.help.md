# Damage Assessment Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Damage Assessment Report is used to document and track damage to a property, including the date and time it occurred, the cause of the damage, and any relevant details or attachments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the following fields with the required information:
	* Assessor Name: Enter the name of the individual or organization conducting the assessment.
	* Damage Type: Select the type of damage that occurred (Physical, Property, or Financial Loss).
	* Damage Date: Enter the date the damage occurred.
2. Provide details about the damage:
	* Cause of Damage: Describe the cause of the damage.
	* Description of Damage: Enter a brief description of the damage.
	* Damage Location: Enter the location where the damage occurred.
3. Add any relevant attachments:
	* Damage Pictures: Upload any photos or images related to the damage.
	* Damage Videos: Upload any videos related to the damage.
	* Damage Audio: Upload any audio recordings related to the damage.
	* Damage Documents: Upload any supporting documents related to the damage.
4. Enter any other relevant information:
	* Damage Notes: Enter any notes or comments about the damage.
	* Damage Attachments: Upload any other attachments related to the damage.
	* Damage Certifications: Upload any certifications related to the damage.
	* Damage Agreements: Enter any agreements related to the damage.
	* Damage Denials: Enter any denials related to the damage.
	* Damage Comments: Enter any comments related to the damage.
	* Agreed Amount: Enter the agreed amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessor Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the individual or organization conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email address of the Assessor.
* **Assessor Phone** (4, text, required): Enter the phone number of the Assessor.
* **Damage Type** (5, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Physical Damage
	+ Property Damage
	+ Financial Loss
* **Damage Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date the damage occurred.
* **Damage Time** (7, time, required): Enter the time the damage occurred.
* **Cause of Damage** (8, text, required): Describe the cause of the damage.
* **Description of Damage** (9, text, required): Enter a brief description of the damage.
* **Damage Location** (10, text, required): Enter the location where the damage occurred.
* **Estimated Cost** (11, number, required): Enter the estimated cost of the damage.
* **Actual Cost** (12, number, required): Enter the actual cost of the damage.
* **Recommended Action** (13, text, required): Enter any recommended action.
* **Action Taken** (14, text, required): Enter any action taken.
* **Damage Pictures** (15, text, required): Upload any photos or images related to the damage.
* **Damage Videos** (16, text, required): Upload any videos related to the damage.
* **Damage Audio** (17, text, required): Upload any audio recordings related to the damage.
* **Damage Documents** (18, text, required): Upload any supporting documents related to the damage.
* **Damage Notes** (19, text, required): Enter any notes or comments about the damage.
* **Damage Attachments** (20, text, required): Upload any other attachments related to the damage.
* **Damage Certifications** (21, text, required): Upload any certifications related to the damage.
* **Damage Agreements** (22, text, required): Enter any agreements related to the damage.
* **Damage Denials** (23, text, required): Enter any denials related to the damage.
* **Damage Comments** (24, text, required): Enter any comments related to the damage.
* **Agreed Amount** (25, number, required): Enter the agreed amount.
