# Linguistics And Literature Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Linguistics And Literature Assessment form is designed to evaluate your background in languages and literature across various genres and styles. This information will help us better understand your demographics and preferences, enabling us to tailor our services and content to meet your needs more effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each field accurately, based on your personal experiences and preferences.
2. Select the languages you are familiar with and the literature genres you enjoy reading or studying.
3. Indicate your proficiency level in each language, even if it's just a basic understanding.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language Background** (`language_background`, `text`, required: false): Describe your background in languages other than English, including any languages you have learned or studied.
* Example: If you have experience with Mandarin, you would type "Mandarin" in this field.
