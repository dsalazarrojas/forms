# Warehouse Management Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your knowledge and skills in warehouse management. It will help us evaluate your experience, knowledge, and attitude in managing a warehouse setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question carefully and to the best of your ability.
2. Use the provided options for select questions to select the correct answer(s).
3. Enter any required fields with accurate and complete information.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

* **user_id** (`user_id`, Number, Required: No): Please enter a unique user ID to identify you.
* **What_is_your_name** (`question_1`, Text, Required: No): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or business card.
* **question_2** (`question_2`, Text, Required: No): This question is asking you to explain your experience in warehouse management. Please provide a short description of your background and experience.
* **question_3** (`question_3`, Date, Required: No): This question is asking for the date of your last warehouse management training or certification. If you do not have one, please enter 'N/A'.

* **question_5** (`question_5`, Select Multiple, Required: No): Please select all options that apply to your experience in warehouse management (e.g., inventory management, shipping and receiving, etc.).
* **question_6** (`question_6`, Select One, Required: No): Please select the option that best describes your role in warehouse management (e.g., supervisor, coordinator, etc.).
* **question_7** (`question_7`, Email, Required: No): This question is asking for your work email address. Please enter your work email address.
* **question_8** (`question_8`, Text, Required: No): This question is asking for any relevant certifications or licenses you hold. Please provide a list of your certifications or licenses.
* **question_9** (`question_9`, Note, Required: No): This question is asking for any additional comments or information about your experience. Please use this field to provide any additional context.
