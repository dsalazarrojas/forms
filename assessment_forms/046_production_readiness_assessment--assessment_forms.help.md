# Production Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Production Readiness Assessment form is designed to evaluate the readiness of a product or feature for launch. It helps assess the production readiness of a product by considering various factors such as launch risks, IT teams involved, and the launch review process. This form is used to gather information to determine if the product or feature is ready for production and if any additional steps are needed to ensure its successful launch.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the production readiness checklist to identify potential issues.
2. Select the correct response for each section, and provide any necessary comments.
3. Enter the launch date and any relevant comments.
4. Confirm IT involvement and review status.
5. Review the results and take necessary actions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Production Readiness Checklist** (`production_readiness_checklist`, note, optional)
* A list of questions to help you assess production readiness.
* Review the checklist and identify any items that need attention.
### 2. **Launch Risks** (`launch_risks`, select_multiple, optional)
* Identify potential launch risks that may impact the product or feature.
* Select all risks that apply.
### 3. **IT Teams** (`it_teams`, select_one, optional)
* Select the IT teams involved in the launch process.
* Choose the team most relevant to the launch process.
### 4. **Launch Review Process** (`launch_review_process`, note, optional)
* Describe the launch review process used for this product or feature.
* Provide any necessary details about the review process.
### 5. **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, note, optional)
* Enter any relevant risk assessments or comments.
* Provide any risk-related information.
### 6. **Production Readiness Score** (`production_readiness_score`, select_one, optional)
* Evaluate the production readiness of this product or feature.
* Select the correct score.
### 7. **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, optional)
* Enter the launch date for this product or feature.
* Ensure it's a date in the correct format.
### 8. **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional)
* Enter any additional comments or notes about the production readiness assessment.
* Provide any further information.
### 9. **IT Lead Review** (`it_lead_review`, note, optional)
* Enter any relevant information about the IT lead review process.
* Provide any comments or notes.
### 10. **Launch Review Process Owners** (`launch_review_process_owners`, select_one, optional)
* Identify the owner of the launch review process.
* Select the correct owner.
### 11. **Launch Review Process Status** (`launch_review_process_status`, select_one, optional)
* Evaluate the status of the launch review process.
* Select the correct status.
### 12. **Launch Review Deadline** (`launch_review_deadline`, date, optional)
* Enter the launch review deadline.
* Ensure it's a date in the correct format.

## Tips
* Complete this form carefully, as the results will impact the production readiness assessment.
* Ensure you provide accurate information to ensure the assessment is reliable.
* If unsure, seek clarification from the relevant teams or stakeholders.
* Review the results and take necessary actions to ensure production readiness.
