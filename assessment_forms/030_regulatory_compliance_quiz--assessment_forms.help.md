# Regulatory Compliance Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regulatory Compliance Quiz is designed to gather information on various aspects of a user's preferences and habits, helping us better understand their needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding text fields.
2. Enter your email address to receive any updates or notifications.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select your department from the provided list.
5. Choose your role from the provided list.
6. Enter your job title.
7. Answer the questions about your favorite colors, foods, animals, eating habits, and exercise frequency by selecting the corresponding options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (user_input, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last name** (last_name, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Department** (department, text, required): Select your department.
* **Role** (role, text, required): Choose your role.
* **Job title** (job_title, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **What is your favorite color?** (quiz_questions, select_one, optional): Select your favorite color (Blue, Red, Green).
* **What is your favorite food?** (quiz_questions_2, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred food options (Pizza, Burger, Sushi).
* **What is your favorite animal?** (quiz_question_3, select_one, optional): Select your favorite animal (Dog, Cat, ?).
* **Additional information** (note, note, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks.
* **When do you like to eat?** (quiz_question_4, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred eating times (Yes, No).
* **Morning** (quiz_question_4_1, select_multiple, optional): Select if you eat during the morning.
* **Afternoon** (quiz_question_4_2, select_multiple, optional): Select if you eat during the afternoon.
* **Evening** (quiz_question_4_3, select_multiple, optional): Select if you eat during the evening.
* **How often do you like to exercise?** (quiz_question_5, select_multiple, optional): Select your exercise frequency (Yes, No).
* **Daily** (quiz_question_5_1, select_multiple, optional): Select if you exercise daily.
* **Weekly** (quiz_question_5_2, select_multiple, optional): Select if you exercise weekly.
* **Additional information** (note_2, note, optional): Add any additional comments or remarks.
