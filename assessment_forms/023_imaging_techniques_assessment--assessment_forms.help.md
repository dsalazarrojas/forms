# Imaging Techniques Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Imaging Techniques Assessment" form is used to evaluate a user's knowledge, skills, and experience in various imaging techniques.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer the questions truthfully and accurately based on your expertise and experience.
2.  Select the appropriate response for each question, considering the options provided.
3.  Provide additional comments and insights, if any, for each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This section is a brief overview of the assessment. Please review the assessment criteria and objectives before proceeding.
* **Imaging Techniques**: This section assesses your knowledge and experience in various imaging techniques. Select "Yes" if you have a good understanding and can perform the technique accurately. Select "No" if you do not have the necessary knowledge or experience.
* **Imaging Knowledge**: This section evaluates your understanding of imaging concepts and protocols. Please provide a detailed answer in the text box, explaining your understanding of the subject matter.
* **Decision Making**: This section assesses your ability to make informed decisions while performing imaging procedures. Select "Yes" if you can make decisions based on your knowledge and experience. Select "No" if you are unsure or lack the necessary confidence.
* **Imaging Proficiency**: This section evaluates your level of proficiency in imaging procedures. Please select the appropriate level of proficiency based on your experience and training.
* **Image Quality**: This section assesses your ability to identify and interpret image quality. Select "Good" if you can accurately identify image quality issues. Select "Poor" if you are not confident in your ability to identify image quality issues.
* **Imaging Decision Making**: This section assesses your ability to make decisions based on imaging results. Select "Yes" if you can make informed decisions. Select "No" if you are not confident in your ability to make decisions.
* **Training Teams**: This section assesses your experience in working with training teams. Select "Yes" if you have experience in team-based training. Select "No" if you do not have the necessary experience.
* **Additional Comments**: This section allows you to provide any additional comments or insights that may be relevant to the assessment.
