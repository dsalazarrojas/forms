# Compliance Therapy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect comprehensive data about your mental health and progress with therapy. Please fill it out carefully and honestly to ensure accurate assessment and effective support from your therapist.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and truthfully, one field at a time.
2. Enter your complete name and identification number.
3. Select your current mood, anxiety, and depression levels.
4. Rate your sleep quality, medication compliance, and progress with therapy goals.
5. Indicate any barriers to engagement, coping strategies used, and support system quality.
6. Disclose any suicidal ideations, substance use, and significant events that occurred this week.
7. Rate your satisfaction with therapy and consent for follow-up contact.
8. Add any additional comments or information you want to share with your therapist.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (1): Your complete name for accurate identification.
* **Client ID Number** (2): Your unique client identification number.
* **Assessment Date** (3): The date of this assessment.
* **Therapist Name** (4): The name of your assigned therapist.
* **Session Number** (5): The number of this therapy session.
* **Current Mood Rating** (6): Rate your overall mood since the last session using the scale: 1 - Very Poor, 2 - Poor, 3 - Fair, 4 - Good, 5 - Very Good.
* **Anxiety Level** (7): Rate your anxiety level over the past week using the scale: None, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Extreme.
* **Depression Level** (8): Rate your depression level over the past week using the scale: None, Mild, Moderate, Severe, Extreme.
* **Sleep Quality** (9): Rate your sleep quality using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor.
* **Medication Compliance** (10): Indicate if you have been taking your medications as prescribed: Yes, always, Most of the time, Sometimes, Rarely, Never.
* **Progress on Therapy Goals** (11): Rate your progress with your therapy goals using the scale: Significant Progress, Moderate Progress, Some Progress, Little Progress, No Progress.
* **Barriers to Engagement** (12): Select any barriers affecting your therapy engagement (transportation issues, financial constraints, time conflicts, lack of motivation, anxiety about sessions, family responsibilities, work schedule, health issues, or none).
* **Support System Quality** (13): Rate your current support system using the scale: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, No Support System.
* **Coping Strategies Used** (14): Select coping strategies you have used this week (deep breathing exercises, meditation, physical exercise, journaling, talking to friends or family, hobbies, professional support, or none).
* **Suicidal Ideations** (15): Disclose if you have experienced thoughts of self-harm or suicide: False, Yes, passive thoughts, yes, with a plan, yes, with intent.
* **Substance Use** (16): Disclose if you've used alcohol or recreational drugs this week: False, Yes, alcohol only, Yes, drugs only, Yes, both.
* **Significant Events** (17): Describe any significant events or changes this week.
* **Satisfaction with Therapy** (18): Rate your satisfaction with therapy using the scale: Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied.
* **Consent for Follow Up Contact** (19): Indicate if you consent to be contacted between sessions: Yes, I consent, No, I do not consent.
* **Additional Comments** (20): Add any other information you'd like to share with your therapist.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when filling out the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- If you have questions or concerns, please talk to your therapist.
- This form is for assessment and monitoring of your mental health progress.
