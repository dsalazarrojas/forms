# Generator Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generator Assessment Survey is used to collect feedback and assessments from users regarding the generator's performance, efficiency, and condition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form for each generator that you are assessing.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible.
3. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Generator Performance
* **Generator Performance** (`generator_performance`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the generator's performance during the assessment period.
* **Efficiency** (`efficiency`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the generator's efficiency during the assessment period.
* **Time To Fill Out** (`time_to_fill_out`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the time it took to fill out this form.
* **Completed** (`date_completed`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you completed this assessment.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you completed this assessment.
* **Generator Type** (`generator_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of generator being assessed (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Generator Condition** (`generator_condition`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the condition of the generator during the assessment period.
* **Operating Conditions** (`operating_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the operating conditions of the generator during the assessment period.
* **Generator Status** (`generator_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the generator (e.g. "Active" or "Inactive").
* **Generator Notes** (`generator_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about the generator.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or organization.
* **Contact Department** (`contact_department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the department of the contact person or organization.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the generator.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the category of the generator (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the generator.
* **Form Id** (`form_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the unique ID of this form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the output file related to this form.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of this assessment.
* **Category Id** (`category_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the category.
* **Form Ids** (`form_ids`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the IDs of related forms.
* **Assigned Tool Id** (`assigned_tool_id`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the ID of the tool or equipment assigned.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the person who created this assessment.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest answers to each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or department representative for guidance.
* Please fill out the form for each generator being assessed.
