# Individual Self Assessment Form For Program Management Office - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees assess their skills and strengths, as well as identify areas for improvement. The form is used by the Program Management Office to collect self-assessment information from employees for program evaluation and development purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name and Employee ID.
2. List your goals and objectives.
3. Select the skills you feel you are strong in.
4. Identify your strengths.
5. Identify your weaknesses that you would like to improve on.
6. Evaluate your skills based on the provided criteria.
7. Choose an evaluation score out of 10.
8. Add any evaluation comments.
9. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: This field is required and should be filled in with your name.
* **Employee ID**: This field is required and should be filled in with your employee ID.
* **Manager Name**: This field is optional but may be required by your manager or supervisor.
* **Goals**: This field is optional but helps us understand your goals and objectives.
* **Skills**: This field is required and should be filled in with skills you feel you are strong in.
* **Strengths**: This field is required and should be filled in with one strength you feel you possess.
* **Weaknesses**: This field is required and should be filled in with weaknesses you would like to improve on.
* **Evaluation Criteria**: This field is required and should be selected based on the provided criteria.
* **Evaluation Score**: This field is required and should be selected based on your evaluation score out of 10.
* **Evaluation Comments**: This field is optional but may be helpful to provide additional comments on your evaluation.
* **Signature**: This field is required and should be signed with your digital signature.
* **Timestamp**: This field is optional but may be filled in with the current timestamp.
* **Submitted By**: This field is optional but may be filled in with your name.
* **Submitted On**: This field is optional but may be filled in with the date and time you submit the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Be honest and realistic when evaluating your skills and weaknesses.
* Use the provided criteria to evaluate your skills accurately.
* Proofread your form before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
