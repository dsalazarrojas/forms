# Cell Decontamination Training Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess an employee's understanding and competencies in cell decontamination procedures. It is used to evaluate their knowledge, skills, and adherence to safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your employee details in the "Employee Information" section.
2. Select the correct answers to the assessment questions in the "Training Attendance" and "Knowledge Assessment" sections.
3. Rate your confidence and understanding in the "Procedure Competency" section.
4. Provide feedback on the training quality and clarity of materials in the "Training Feedback" section.
5. If needed, provide additional support or training required in the "Areas Needing Improvement" and "Additional Support Needed" sections.
6. Finally, confirm your understanding of cell decontamination procedures by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Certification" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, **required**): Enter your ID number as per your official ID.
* **Department or Section** (`department_section`, text, **required**): Enter the department or section where you are working.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date on which you are taking this assessment.
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the training session you attended.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Training Attendance** (`training_attendance`, select_one, **required**): Select the attendance status (Full attendance, Most of session, Partial attendance, or Absent).
* **What is the primary purpose of cell decontamination?** (`question_1_purpose`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ To eliminate pathogenic organisms and prevent contamination
	+ To improve cell growth rates
	+ To change cell morphology
	+ To increase cell viability
	+ Unsure
* **Which of the following is a key step in proper cell decontamination?** (`question_2_protocol`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Complete media replacement and cleaning equipment
	+ Adding antibiotics only
	+ Changing incubator temperature
	+ Transferring cells to new dishes
	+ Unsure
* **Common contaminants in cell cultures include** (`question_3_contamination_types`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all that apply:
	+ Bacteria
	+ Fungi
	+ Mycoplasma
	+ Viruses
	+ Precipitation salts
* **Which practice helps prevent cell culture contamination?** (`question_4_prevention`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Proper aseptic technique and biosafety hood use
	+ Keeping cultures at room temperature
	+ Sharing equipment without cleaning
	+ Working quickly without safety protocols
	+ Unsure
* **How can contamination be detected?** (`question_5_detection`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Visual inspection for clarity, color changes, and microscopic observation
	+ By cell count alone
	+ Only through genetic testing
	+ Cultures must be discarded first
	+ Unsure
* **Confidence with decontamination equipment** (`equipment_handling`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Confident
	+ Confident
	+ Somewhat Confident
	+ Not Confident
	+ No experience
* **Understanding of aseptic technique** (`aseptic_technique`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor
	+ Needs Improvement
* **Adherence to safety protocols** (`safety_protocol`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Always follows protocols
	+ Usually follows protocols
	+ Sometimes follows protocols
	+ Rarely follows protocols
	+ Never follows protocols
* **Overall Training Quality** (`training_quality`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Satisfactory
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Were training materials clear and comprehensive?** (`material_clarity`, select_one, **not required**): Select one of the following options:
	+ Very Clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat Clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very Unclear
* **Areas Needing Improvement in Training** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, **not required**): If you have any areas that need improvement, please describe them in this section.
* **Additional Support or Training Needed** (`additional_support_needed`, text, **not required**): If you require additional support or training, please describe it in this section.
* **I certify understanding of cell decontamination procedures** (`certification`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm your understanding.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any question, select "Unsure" as the answer.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Areas Needing Improvement" and "Additional Support Needed" sections.
