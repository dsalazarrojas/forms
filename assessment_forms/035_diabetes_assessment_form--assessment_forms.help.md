# Diabetes Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Diabetes Assessment Form is designed to collect information about patients undergoing diabetes treatment. This form aims to capture essential details about the patient, including their name, medical record number, and medical history. The data collected will be used to assess the patient's current condition and provide guidance for treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out the Diabetes Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the "Patient Name" field with the patient's name as it is listed on their medical records.
2. Enter the patient's "Medical Record Number" in the corresponding field.
3. In the "Medical History" section, provide a brief description of the patient's medical background, including any previous treatments, allergies, or medications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's full name as it appears on their medical records.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, number, optional): Enter the patient's unique medical record number for identification purposes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous treatments, allergies, or medications that are relevant to their current condition.

## Tips

- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the assessment is accurate.
- If you are unsure about any information, please seek guidance from a medical professional.
- Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
