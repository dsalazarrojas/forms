# Behavioral Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Behavioral Assessment Quiz form. The form is designed to assess an individual's behavioral style based on their answers to a series of questions. By the end of this guide, you'll be able to navigate the form with ease and gain valuable insights into your dominant behavioral style.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by answering each question with the corresponding option that best represents your agreement or preference**. You can choose from the options provided for each question.
2. **Ensure you complete all required fields**. These fields are marked as "required" and are essential for the assessment.
3. **Use the space provided for any additional comments** if you'd like to elaborate on your answers or share any other information.
