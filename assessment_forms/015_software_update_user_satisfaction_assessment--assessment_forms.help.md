# Software Update User Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on the recent software update. The goal is to understand your overall satisfaction, ease of use, performance, and the impact of the update on your productivity, work efficiency, and accuracy. Your responses will help us understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction page to understand the questions we're asking.
2. Answer each question according to your honest feelings about the update.
3. For questions with rating scales (e.g., how satisfied, how easy), select the option that best reflects your experience.
4. For open-ended questions (e.g., have you experienced any issues), provide as much detail as you can.
5. Finally, indicate whether you would recommend this software to others and provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): This is a short message introducing the form and the purpose of the survey.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the update?** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to rate your overall satisfaction with the update on a scale from Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
* **How would you rate the ease of use of the update?** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to rate how easy or hard it was to use the update.
* **How would you rate the performance of the update?** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to rate the performance of the update on a scale from Very Good to Very Bad.
* **How would you rate the update's impact on your productivity?** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to rate how much the update has improved or declined your productivity.
* **How would you rate the update's impact on your work efficiency?** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to rate how much the update has improved or declined your work efficiency.
* **How would you rate the update's impact on your work accuracy?** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks you to rate how much the update has improved or declined your work accuracy.
* **Have you experienced any issues with the update?** (`8`, `text`, required: false): This question allows you to provide details about any issues you have experienced with the update.
* **Would you recommend this software to others?** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): This question asks whether you would recommend this software to others.
* **Comments** (`10`, `note`, required: false): This is an open-ended field for you to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have about the update.
