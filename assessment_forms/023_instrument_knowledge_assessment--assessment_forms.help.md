# Instrument Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instrument Knowledge Assessment form is designed to measure an individual's knowledge and skills in playing a music instrument. This form is used to evaluate the user's level of proficiency in various aspects of music, including instrument-specific knowledge, practice habits, and performance level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the name of the music instrument you wish to assess (e.g., Violin, Cello, Trumpet).
2. Choose your experience level in playing the instrument (e.g., Intermediate, Advanced, Beginner).
3. Indicate whether you have ensemble experience (e.g., True or False).
4. Select the types of musical reading you are comfortable with (e.g., Sight reading, Ear, Score).
5. Choose the types of instrument techniques you are proficient with (e.g., Bowing, Finger, Mouthpiece).
6. Select your level of music theory knowledge (e.g., Scales, Chord, Harmony).
7. Select your performance level (e.g., Professional, Intermediate, Novice).
8. Enter the amount of time you typically practice the instrument (e.g., 1-2 hours, 2-5 hours, etc.).
9. Enter your practice frequency (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
10. Enter your music reading speed (e.g., Slow, Average, Fast).
11. Enter your practice effectiveness (e.g., Effective, Ineffective, Somewhat effective).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Music Instrument** (Music Instrument): Select the name of the music instrument you wish to assess.

* **Ensemble Experience** (Ensemble Experience): Indicate whether you have experience playing in ensembles (e.g., True or False).
* **Musical Reading** (Musical Reading): Select the types of musical reading you are comfortable with, such as Sight reading, Ear, or Score.
* **Instrument Technique** (Instrument Technique): Choose the types of instrument techniques you are proficient with, such as Bowing, Finger, or Mouthpiece.
* **Music Theory** (Music Theory): Select your level of music theory knowledge, such as Scales, Chord, or Harmony.
* **Performance Level** (Performance Level): Select your level of performance, from Professional to Novice.
* **Practice Time** (Practice Time): Enter the amount of time you typically practice the instrument.
* **Practice Frequency** (Practice Frequency): Enter your practice frequency, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Music Reading Speed** (Music Reading Speed): Enter your music reading speed, such as Slow, Average, or Fast.
* **Practice Effectiveness** (Practice Effectiveness): Enter your practice effectiveness, such as Effective, Ineffective, or Somewhat effective.
