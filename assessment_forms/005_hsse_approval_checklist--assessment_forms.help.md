# Hsse_approval_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hsse_approval_checklist form is used to gather information and approvals for specific standards and guidelines related to health, safety, and security. This form ensures that all relevant approvals and documentation are in place before proceeding with any new project or proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the relevant fields, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to check the "Approved_by" field to indicate if the necessary approvals have been received.
4. Date and time of approval are also required.
5. Submit the form once completed and reviewed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (`General_info`, text, optional): This section is for any additional information or comments relevant to the project or proposal.
  - Please provide any relevant details that may affect the assessment process.
- **Health Standards** (`Health_standards`, text, optional): This field is for any health-related standards or guidelines that need to be met.
  - Please provide any relevant health-related information or documentation.
- **Safety Standards** (`Safety_standards`, text, optional): This field is for any safety-related standards or guidelines that need to be met.
  - Please provide any relevant safety-related information or documentation.
- **Security Standards** (`Security_standards`, text, optional): This field is for any security-related standards or guidelines that need to be met.
  - Please provide any relevant security-related information or documentation.
- **Environmental Standards** (`Environmental_standards`, text, optional): This field is for any environmental-related standards or guidelines that need to be met.
  - Please provide any relevant environmental-related information or documentation.
- **Approved By** (`Approved_by`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if the necessary approvals have been received.
  - This will indicate that the necessary approvals have been obtained.
- **Date Approved** (`Date_approved`, date, optional): Provide the date of approval.
  - Please enter the date when approvals were received.
- **Time Approved** (`Time_approved`, time, optional): Provide the time of approval.
  - Please enter the time of approval.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all relevant fields, providing as much detail as possible.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any mistakes.
- Duplicate submissions will be automatically rejected.
- If any field is left blank, it may cause delays or affect the assessment process.
