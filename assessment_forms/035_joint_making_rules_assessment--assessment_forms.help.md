# Joint Making Rules Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Making Rules Assessment form is designed to collect information about joint making rules in a given context. This form is typically used for assessing and providing feedback on joint making rules in specific scenarios.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and sections to understand what information is required.
2. Complete each page and section according to the instructions and options provided.
3. Make sure to answer all questions and select all relevant options.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Joint Making Rules Assessment Form 1 (first_page)
* **first_page** (text, optional): This is the first page of the form where you can provide a brief explanation or description of the context in which the joint making rules assessment is being performed.

### Section 1 (section_1)
* **section_1** (select_multiple, optional): Select all the options that apply to the joint making rules assessment.

### Joint Making Rules Assessment Form 2 (second_page)
* **second_page** (text, optional): This is the second page of the form where you can provide additional information or context about the joint making rules assessment.

### Joint Making Rules Assessment Form 3 (third_page)
* **third_page** (select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes the joint making rules assessment.

### Joint Making Rules Assessment Form 4 (fourth_page)
* **fourth_page** (number, optional): This is the fourth page of the form where you can provide a numerical value or rating for the joint making rules assessment.

### Joint Making Rules Assessment Form 4 (written_explanations)
* **written_explanations** (note, optional): This is a field for providing additional written explanations or comments about the joint making rules assessment.

### Joint Making Rules Assessment Form 4 (confidence_level_1)
* **confidence_level_1** (number, optional): This is the first confidence level for the joint making rules assessment.

### Joint Making Rules Assessment Form 4 (confidence_level_2)
* **confidence_level_2** (number, optional): This is the second confidence level for the joint making rules assessment.
