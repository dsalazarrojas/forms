# Comfort Zone Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Comfort Zone Assessment form is designed to help you understand your comfort level in various areas, identify potential limitations, and set growth goals. Take a few minutes to complete this form and gain valuable insights into your comfort zone and areas where you feel limited.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read each question carefully**: Each question is designed to help you assess your comfort level in different areas.
2. **Choose your responses**: Select the options that best fit your feelings and experiences.
3. **Be honest**: Answer honestly, and don't worry about what others might think.
4. **Take your time**: You can review and save your progress if needed.
5. **Submit and review your results**: Once you've completed the form, you'll be able to review your results and identify areas where you feel limited.

* **Email Address (if desired)**: Your email address for receiving assessment results, if you choose to share them.
