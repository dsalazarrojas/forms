# Fitness Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's fitness needs, including their personal details, health information, and physical activity level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as needed. Please ensure that you have permission to collect and submit the information of the individual completing this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (Required)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the individual's first name.

### Last Name (Required)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the individual's last name.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the individual's email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., example@email.com).

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the individual's contact number. This can be a phone number or any other contact method.

### Fitness Goals
* **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goal`, text, optional): Describe the individual's fitness goals. For example, "Improve overall health" or "Lose weight."

### Height
* **Height** (`height`, number, optional): Enter the individual's height in inches.

### Weight
* **Weight** (`weight`, number, optional): Enter the individual's weight in pounds.

### Age
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the individual's age.

### Physical Activity Level
* **Physical Activity Level** (`physical_activity_level`, select_one, optional): Select the individual's current physical activity level:
	+ sedentary (little or no exercise)
	+ lightly active (light exercise/sports 1-2 days a week)
	+ moderately active (moderate exercise/sports 3-5 days a week)
	+ highly active (hard exercise/sports 6-7 days a week)

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or health concerns the individual may have.

### Health Insurance Provider
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`health_insurance_provider`, text, optional): Specify the individual's health insurance provider.

Note: Each field will be displayed on a separate page, but all fields can be filled out on the same page. Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
