# level_of_care_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Level of Care Assessment Form is used to assess the level of care required for a patient's rehabilitation program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient Dob" field.
3. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
4. Select the appropriate care level from the "Care Level" field.
5. Select any additional care level options required from the "Care Level Options" field.
6. Indicate whether the patient is enrolled in a rehabilitation program from the "Rehabilitation Program" field.
7. Select any additional rehabilitation program options required from the "Rehabilitation Program Options" field.
8. Enter the facility's name, address, city, state, zip, telephone number, healthcare provider's name, title, and license in their respective fields.
9. Select the level of care from the "Level Of Care" field.
10. Enter the discharge status and date in their respective fields.
11. Sign the "Signature" field to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name (`patient_name`, text, required)**: Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Dob (`patient_dob`, date, required)**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Condition (`patient_condition`, text, required)**: Enter a brief description of the patient's condition.
* **Medical History (`medical_history`, text, required)**: Enter the patient's medical history, including any relevant details.
* **Care Level (`care_level`, select_one, required)**: Select the appropriate level of care required for the patient.
* **Care Level Options (`care_level_options`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any additional care level options required for the patient.
* **Rehabilitation Program (`rehabilitation_program`, select_one, required)**: Indicate whether the patient is enrolled in a rehabilitation program.
* **Rehabilitation Program Options (`rehabilitation_program_options`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any additional rehabilitation program options required for the patient.
* **Discharge Date (`discharge_date`, date, required)**: Enter the patient's discharge date.
* **Discharge Status (`discharge_status`, text, required)**: Enter the patient's discharge status.
* **Signature (`signature`, text, required)**: Sign the form to complete it.
* **Facility Name (`facility_name`, text, required)**: Enter the facility's name.
* **Facility Address (`facility_address`, text, required)**: Enter the facility's address.
* **Facility City (`facility_city`, text, required)**: Enter the facility's city.
* **Facility State (`facility_state`, text, required)**: Enter the facility's state.
* **Facility Zip (`facility_zip`, text, required)**: Enter the facility's zip code.
* **Facility Telephone (`facility_telephone`, text, required)**: Enter the facility's telephone number.
* **Healthcare Provider Name (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, required)**: Enter the healthcare provider's name.
* **Healthcare Provider Title (`healthcare_provider_title`, text, required)**: Enter the healthcare provider's title.
* **Healthcare Provider License (`healthcare_provider_license`, text, required)**: Enter the healthcare provider's license number.
* **Healthcare Provider Facility (`healthcare_provider_facility`, text, required)**: Enter the facility's name where the healthcare provider works.
* **Healthcare Provider Telephone (`healthcare_provider_telephone`, text, required)**: Enter the healthcare provider's telephone number.
* **Level Of Care (`level_of_care`, select_one, required)**: Select the level of care required for the patient.
* **Level Of Care Options (`level_of_care_options`, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any additional care level options required for the patient.
