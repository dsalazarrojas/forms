# Use Of Force Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and assess the use of force during incidents. It captures relevant information about the suspect, incident, and responding officer's experiences. The form helps to evaluate the appropriateness and justification of the force used and identify potential injuries sustained by the suspect or responding officer. This data will help in training, policy development, and future incident reviews.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the suspect's name** (`suspect_name`): Enter the suspect's name as it appears on their identification or records.
2. **Enter the incident date** (`incident_date`): Record the date the incident occurred.
3. **Enter the incident time** (`incident_time`): Record the time the incident occurred.
4. **Enter the suspect's location** (`suspect_location`): Describe the location where the incident took place.
5. **Enter the responding officer's name** (`responding_officer_name`): Record the name of the officer who responded to the incident.
6. **Select the type of force used** (`use_of_force_justified`): Choose from the options:
	* Force: Physical force was used by the officer.
	* Chemical Agent: Chemical agents were used by the officer.
	* Physical Restraint: Physical restraint was used by the officer.
	* Other: Choose this option if other types of force were used.
7. **Assess the suspect's injuries** (`suspect_injuries`): Select from the options:
	* No Injuries
	* Minor Injuries
	* Moderate Injuries
	* Severe Injuries
8. **Assess the officer's injuries** (`officer_injuries`): Select from the options:
	* No Injuries
	* Minor Injuries
	* Moderate Injuries
	* Severe Injuries
9. **Provide details about the officer's injuries** (`officer_injuries_details`): If the officer sustained injuries, describe them briefly.
10. **Specify the location of the officer's injuries** (`officer_injuries_location`): If the officer sustained injuries, provide the location where they occurred.
11. **Enter incident notes** (`incident_notes`): Record any additional relevant details about the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Suspect's Name** (`suspect_name`): The suspect's name as it appears on their identification or records.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): The date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): The time the incident occurred.
* **Suspect Location** (`suspect_location`): The location where the incident took place.
* **Responding Officer's Name** (`responding_officer_name`): The name of the officer who responded to the incident.
* **Type of Force Used** (`use_of_force_justified`): The type of force used by the responding officer.
* **Suspect's Injuries** (`suspect_injuries`): The severity of the suspect's injuries (if any).
* **Officer's Injuries** (`officer_injuries`): The severity of the responding officer's injuries (if any).
* **Officer's Injuries Details** (`officer_injuries_details`): A brief description of the responding officer's injuries (if any).
* **Officer's Injuries Location** (`officer_injuries_location`): The location where the responding officer's injuries occurred (if any).
* **Incident Notes** (`incident_notes`): Any additional relevant details about the incident.
