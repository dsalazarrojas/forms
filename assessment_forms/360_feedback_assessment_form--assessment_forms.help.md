# 45 Days Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants in the 45 Days Challenge. It is a crucial part of the registration process, and your accuracy and completeness will ensure that you get matched with a suitable workout plan and trainer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant full name** in the "Participant full name" field (Field 2).
2. Enter your **Email address** in the "Email address" field (Field 3).
3. Enter your **Phone number** in the "Phone number" field (Field 4) (including the country code if applicable).
4. Select your **Date of birth** in the "Date of birth" field (Field 5).
5. Enter the **Emergency contact name** in the "Emergency contact name" field (Field 6).
6. Enter the **Emergency contact phone number** in the "Emergency contact phone" field (Field 7).
7. Select your **Current fitness level** from the options in the "Current fitness level" field (Field 8).
8. Select all relevant **Primary goals** from the list in the "Primary goals" field (Field 9).
9. Select your **Preferred session time** (if applicable) in the "Preferred session time" field (Field 10).
10. Choose the **Desired challenge start date** (Field 11).
11. Enter the **Registration payment amount** in the "Registration payment amount (USD)" field (Field 12).
12. If applicable, enter any **Medical conditions or limitations** in the "Medical conditions or limitations" field (Field 13).
13. If applicable, enter the name of your preferred **Trainer or coach** in the "Preferred trainer or coach" field (Field 14).
14. Select "True" to confirm that you accept the **Waiver and terms** (Field 15).
15. If you have a **Promotional code**, enter it in the "Promotional code (optional)" field (Field 16).
16. Use the "Anything else we should know?" field (Field 17) to provide any additional information about your goals, constraints, or accessibility needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant full name** (`Participant full name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
- **Email address** (`Email address`, Email, Required): Enter your email address used for schedules and updates.
- **Phone number** (`Phone number`, Phone, Optional): Enter your phone number, including the country code if applicable.
- **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth for age-based classes.
- **Emergency contact name** (`Emergency contact name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in an emergency.
- **Emergency contact phone number** (`Emergency contact phone number`, Phone, Required): Enter the best number to reach your emergency contact.
- **Current fitness level** (`Current fitness level`, Select One, Required): Select your current fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
- **Primary goals** (`Primary goals`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all relevant goals (Weight loss, Strength building, Endurance / cardio, Flexibility / mobility, Habit formation).
- **Preferred session time** (`Preferred session time`, Time, Optional): Select a preferred session time (if applicable).
- **Desired challenge start date** (`Desired challenge start date`, Date, Required): Choose the weekend that you would like to start the challenge.
- **Registration payment amount (USD)** (`Registration payment amount (USD)`, Decimal, Required): Enter the total or negotiated fee for the challenge.
- **Medical conditions or limitations** (`Medical conditions or limitations`, Text, Optional): If you have any medical conditions or limitations, list them here.
- **Preferred trainer or coach** (`Preferred trainer or coach`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your preferred trainer or coach (or leave blank for assignment).
- **Waiver and terms** (`Waiver and terms`, Select One, Required): Select "True" to confirm that you accept the participant waiver and terms.
- **Promotional code (optional)** (`Promotional code (optional)`, Text, Optional): Enter any promotional code if you have one.
- **Anything else we should know?** (`Anything else we should know?`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your goals, constraints, or accessibility needs.
