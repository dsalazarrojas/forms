# Efficacy And Safety Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Efficacy And Safety Assessment Survey is designed to gather feedback on the effectiveness and safety of a product or service. The survey aims to gather information on the user's experience, including their ratings of efficacy and safety, as well as any safety concerns they may have encountered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form, and make sure you have a clear understanding of each field's purpose.
2. Fill in the "Product" field with the name of the product or service being assessed.
3. Rate your experience with the "Experience Rating" field, using a numeric scale (e.g., 1-5).
4. Rate the efficacy of the product or service with the "Efficacy Rating" field, using a numeric scale (e.g., 1-5).
5. Rate the safety of the product or service with the "Safety Rating" field, using a numeric scale (e.g., 1-5).
6. If you have any safety concerns, please list them in the "Safety Concerns" field.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the product or service being assessed.
* **Experience Rating** (`experience_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate your overall experience with the product or service, using a numeric scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Efficacy Rating** (`efficacy_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the effectiveness of the product or service, using a numeric scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Safety Rating** (`safety_rating`, number, required/optional): Rate the safety of the product or service, using a numeric scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, text, required/optional): If you have any safety concerns, please list them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback on the product or service.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback is valuable in helping us improve the product or service.
* Use the provided numeric scales to rate your experience and efficacy/safety ratings.
* If you have any specific safety concerns, please describe them in detail to help us address them.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, please share them with us.
