## Museum Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Museum Knowledge Assessment form is designed to measure your knowledge about museums. It consists of a series of questions and prompts that will help us understand your thoughts, opinions, and experiences regarding museums.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question or prompt in the space provided.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the option that best answers or describes your response.
3. For open-ended questions, provide as much detail as you can.
4. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the main goal of museums?** (`quiz_question`, `select_one`, required)
This question helps us understand what you think is the primary purpose of a museum. Select the option that best fits your response.
* **What do you think is the most important part of a museum visit?** (`reflection_question`, `select_multiple`, required)
This question assesses your opinion on what is most valuable during a museum visit. Choose the options that best describe your perspective.
* **Your answer** (`quiz_answer`, `text`, optional)
Provide a brief response to the previous question, describing your thoughts.
* **Your reflection** (`reflection_answer`, `text`, optional)
Share your thoughts on what you think is the most important part of a museum visit.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
Please enter your phone number for our records.
* **Submit** (`submit_page`, `time`, optional)
Please note that this is not an actual submit button, but rather a placeholder page with a time field that is currently not used.
