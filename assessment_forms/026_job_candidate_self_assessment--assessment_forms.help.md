# job_candidate_self_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a job candidate's self-assessment, including their job title, job type, job status, work experience, and years of experience. This data is used for internal purposes to track and analyze candidate information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Review the form fields below and provide information as requested.
2. Fill out the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Enter your answers accurately and concisely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Job Candidiate Info
* **Job Candidiate Info** (`job_candidiate_info`, `note`, **not required**): Please provide any additional information about yourself that is not already covered in the other fields.

### Job Candidate Self Assessment
* **Job Candidate Self Assessment** (`job_candidate_self_assessment`, `text`, **not required**): Briefly describe why you are a good fit for this job position. You may want to mention your relevant skills, experience, and qualifications.

### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, **not required**): Do you consider yourself a: 
  - Yes
  - No

### Job Type
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_multiple`, **required**): What type of job do you consider yourself a?
  - Job Title
  - Manager
  - Team Leader
  - Team Member

### Job Status
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, **required**): What is your current job status?
  - Active
  - Inactive
  - Pending

### Work Experience
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `number`, **not required**): How many years of work experience do you have?

### Years of Experience
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, **not required**): How many years of experience do you have in the field of this job title?

### Job Description
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, **not required**): Briefly describe your job responsibilities and tasks. 

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and should not be considered as a substitute for the actual form instructions.
