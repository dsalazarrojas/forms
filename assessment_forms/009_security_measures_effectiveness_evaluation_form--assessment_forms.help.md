# Security Measures Effectiveness Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the security measures and risk assessments of your organization. It is essential to understand the current state of your organization's security measures and risk assessments to identify areas for improvement and ensure the highest level of security. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

*   Evaluate the effectiveness of your existing security measures
*   Identify potential risks and weaknesses
*   Document your risk assessment and measures to improve security

## How To Complete This Form
### Follow the instructions below to complete the form.

1.  **Answer each question carefully and thoroughly.**
2.  **Use the options provided for each question to guide your answers.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Security Measures Effectiveness Evaluation**
    *   **Security Measures Effectiveness** (`security_measures_effectiveness_evaluation`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe the effectiveness of your organization's current security measures. Please provide a brief description of each security measure's effectiveness.
    *   **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select the level of risk associated with your organization's current security measures. The options are:
        *   Low
        *   Medium
        *   High
    *   **Risk Control Measures** (`risk_control_measures`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe any additional risk control measures taken by your organization to mitigate risk.
    *   **Risk Control Measures Other** (`risk_control_measures_other`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about any other risk control measures taken by your organization.
    *   **Risk Assessment** (`risk_assessment`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe the current state of your organization's risk assessment process.
    *   **Risk Assessment Other** (`risk_assessment_other`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide additional information about your organization's risk assessment process.
    *   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter your organization's name.

**Tips:**

*   Be sure to answer each question thoroughly and accurately.
*   If you have any additional information that does not fit into the provided options, use the "Other" fields to provide more detail.
*   If you are unsure about any question, please contact your security team for guidance.
