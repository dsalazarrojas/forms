# Youth Empowerment Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Empowerment Assessment form is a tool used to collect feedback and assessment data from participants in the youth empowerment program. This data will help the program's administrators to better understand the effectiveness of the program and identify areas where improvements can be made.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are eligible to take this assessment. You should have participated in the youth empowerment program and have not been specifically invited to take this assessment.
2. Fill out the form with your responses to the questions and fields provided.
3. Take your time to answer the questions carefully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the school or organization that participated in the program.
  - Please enter the name of the school or organization that you participated in.
  - This field is optional, but providing your school name will help us to better understand the context of your participation.

- **School Email** (`school_email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect the email address of the school or organization that participated in the program.
  - If you have access to the school's email address, please enter it.
  - This field is optional, but providing your school email will help us to better understand the context of your participation.

- **School Contact Person** (`school_contact_person`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name and contact information of the person who represents the school or organization that participated in the program.
  - Please enter the name and contact information of the person who represents the school or organization.
  - This field is optional, but providing your school contact person will help us to better understand the context of your participation.

- **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the program that you participated in.
  - Please enter the name of the program that you participated in.
  - This field is optional, but providing your program name will help us to better understand the context of your participation.

- **Program Contact Person** (`program_contact_person`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name and contact information of the person who represents the program that you participated in.
  - Please enter the name and contact information of the person who represents the program.
  - This field is optional, but providing your program contact person will help us to better understand the context of your participation.

- **How long have you participated in this program?** (`how_long`, select_one, optional): This field is used to ask you about the duration of your participation in the program.
  - Please select one of the options: 1-3 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5+ years.
  - This field is optional, but providing your participation duration will help us to better understand the context of your participation.

- **On a scale from 1-5, how would you rate your sense of empowerment?** (`empowerment_level`, number, optional): This field is used to ask you to rate your sense of empowerment on a scale from 1 to 5.
  - Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 means "not at all empowered" and 5 means "very empowered".
  - This field is optional, but providing your empowerment level will help us to better understand your experience in the program.

- **On a scale from 1-5, how would you rate your level of participation?** (`participation_level`, number, optional): This field is used to ask you to rate your level of participation in the program on a scale from 1 to 5.
  - Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 means "not at all participating" and 5 means "very participating".
  - This field is optional, but providing your participation level will help us to better understand your experience in the program.

- **Overall satisfaction with your experience in the program** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): This field is used to ask you to rate your overall satisfaction with your experience in the program on a scale from 1 to 5.
  - Please enter a number from 1 to 5, where 1 means "very dissatisfied" and 5 means "very satisfied".
  - This field is optional, but providing your overall satisfaction will help us to better understand your experience in the program.

- **Additional feedback or suggestions** (`program_feedback`, note, optional): This field is used to provide us with additional feedback or suggestions about the program.
  - Please enter any additional thoughts or suggestions you may have about the program.
  - This field is optional, but providing your feedback will help us to better understand your experience in the program.

- **Demographic Information** (`demographic_info`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to collect demographic information about you.
  - Please select all that apply: Age, Sex, Ethnicity, Language, Disability Status, Geographic Location, or Other (please specify).
  - This field is optional, but providing your demographic information will help us to better understand your experience in the program.
