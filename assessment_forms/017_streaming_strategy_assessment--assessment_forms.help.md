# Streaming Strategy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streaming Strategy Assessment is a form designed to help content creators gain insights into their streaming habits and identify areas for improvement. This assessment will ask you questions about your streaming frequency, time, goals, challenges, content planning, and performance. By completing this form, you'll be able to understand your strengths and weaknesses, and make informed decisions about your content and streaming schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the first page of the form, titled "Streaming Strategy Assessment".
2. Start by filling out the required fields:
	* **Streaming Frequency**: Choose how often you stream: Less than weekly, Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly.
	* **Streaming Frequency Time**: Select the time of day you most often stream (check all that apply): Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Late Night.
3. If applicable, answer the optional fields:
	* **Streamer Name**: Enter your streamer name, if you'd like to identify yourself.
	* **Streamer Email**: Enter your email address, if you'd like to contact you about your assessment.
	* **Streamer Phone**: Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted.
4. Continue to the next page:
	* **Streaming Goals**: Describe what you're trying to achieve through streaming.
	* **Streaming Challenges**: Describe any challenges you face while streaming.
5. Complete the final questions:
	* **Content Planning**: Choose how you plan your content: Thorough or Spontaneous.
	* **Performance Improvements**: Describe any improvements you'd like to see in your streaming performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Streaming Frequency**: This question helps us understand how often you stream, which can affect your viewership and engagement.
* **Streamer Name**: If you'd like to be identified by your streamer name, enter it here.
* **Streamer Email**: If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion, enter your email address.
* **Streamer Phone**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
* **Streaming Frequency Time**: This question helps us understand your streaming schedule, which can impact your audience's viewing habits.
* **Streaming Goals**: Describe your goals for streaming, whether they're related to growing your audience, promoting products, or simply entertaining.
* **Streaming Challenges**: Be honest about any difficulties you face while streaming, such as maintaining focus or dealing with technical issues.
* **Content Planning**: Choose how you typically plan your content: thoroughly or spontaneously.
* **Performance Improvements**: Describe any areas where you'd like to improve your streaming performance, such as video production, sound quality, or engagement with your audience.
* **Performance Improvements** (again, but this time with a note that this field is for suggestions, not a criticism): This question is meant to help you identify areas where you'd like to improve, and we're not judging your performance.
