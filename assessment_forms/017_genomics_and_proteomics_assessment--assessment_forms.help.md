# Genomics And Proteomics Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Genomics And Proteomics Assessment form, which is designed to evaluate your knowledge levels, training needs, and other relevant factors in genomics and proteomics. The form is essential for researchers and professionals working with genomics and proteomics data to ensure they have the necessary skills and support to excel in their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to your knowledge levels, training needs, workshop planning, and research support. Be as specific as possible and select only the options that accurately describe your situation.
2. Review each field carefully, as the form is designed to gather detailed information about your knowledge levels, training needs, and other relevant factors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Knowledge Levels** (`knowledge_levels`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select all the knowledge levels that apply to you. Choose 'High' if you have a deep understanding of genomics and proteomics concepts, or 'Low' if you need more training or support.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to select all the training needs that apply to you. Choose 'Yes' if you need training on specific topics, or 'No' if you don't need additional training.
* **Workshop Planning** (`workshop_planning`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to indicate if you are planning to attend a workshop. Choose 'Yes' if you are planning to attend, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Research Support** (`research_support`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to indicate if you need research support. Choose 'Yes' if you need research support, or 'No' if you don't need it.

## Tips
* Take your time to carefully review each field, as accuracy is essential.
* If you're unsure about a field, please reach out to your supervisor or team lead for clarification.
* Be specific when selecting options, as it will help us provide you with more tailored support.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
