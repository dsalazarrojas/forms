# Human Respiration Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Respiration Assessment form gathers information on a user's health status regarding respiration symptoms, risk factors, medical history, and interactions with healthcare professionals. This form aims to provide a comprehensive picture of the user's situation to aid in diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for each field based on your actual experiences or conditions.
2. Enter the required information for each field as prompted.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information**: Enter your basic contact information to help healthcare professionals reach out to you for further evaluation or treatment.
- **Select Respiratory Symptoms**: Choose all symptoms that you have experienced, such as Dyspnea (difficulty breathing), Cough, Wheezing, Chest pain, Shortness of breath, Chest tightness, or Coughing up blood.
	+ If you select multiple symptoms, please ensure the selections are accurate to the best of your ability.
- **Select Risk Factors**: Identify any risk factors that may contribute to your current condition, such as Family history of respiratory disease, Environmental exposure, Smoking or vaping, or Other ( specify in the Medical History field).
- **Date Symptoms Became Symptomatic**: Enter the date when you first experienced respiratory symptoms in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Doctor Information (Optional)**: If you have a doctor, provide their name and specialty for further evaluation or treatment.
- **Medical History**: Briefly describe any pre-existing medical conditions that may be relevant to your current situation.
- **Are you taking any medication? (Optional)**: Indicate if you are currently taking any medication, and if so, what is its name.
- **What is the name of your medication? (Optional)**: If you selected "Yes" for the previous question, provide the name of the medication.
- **Doctor Name (Optional)**: If you have a doctor, provide their name for further evaluation or treatment.
- **Doctor Specialty (Optional)**: If you have a doctor, specify their specialty for further evaluation or treatment.

Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that healthcare professionals receive a clear picture of your situation. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask.
