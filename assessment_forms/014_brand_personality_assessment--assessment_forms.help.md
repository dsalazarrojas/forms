# Brand Personality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to assist users in understanding and completing the Brand Personality Assessment form. This form is used to evaluate the personality traits of a brand, providing insights for improvement and alignment with target audiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill out your personal details**: 
	* Enter your name and role in the "Your Name" and "Your Role" fields, respectively. These fields are optional, but help us track your responses for better insights.
2. **Select your role and relationship to the brand**: 
	* Choose your relationship to the brand from the "Your Role" field, which can be Customer, Employee, Partner, Industry Expert, General Public, or other options not listed.
3. **Assess the brand's personality traits**:
	* Rate the brand's sincerity, excitement, competence, sophistication, ruggedness, and overall brand voice using the provided scale.
	* Choose the brand archetype that best fits the brand from the available options.
	* Describe the brand's personality as if it were a person.
4. **Evaluate the brand's alignment with your lifestyle and values**:
	* Determine how well the brand aligns with your lifestyle and values.
5. **Assess the brand's consistency and differentiation**:
	* Rate the brand's consistency across touchpoints and how well it stands out from others.
6. **Provide an overall assessment**:
	* Summarize your overall assessment of the brand's personality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name**: Enter your name for tracking purposes.
* **Your Role**: Select your relationship to the brand (Customer, Employee, Partner, Industry Expert, General Public, or other options).
* **Brand Name**: Enter the name of the brand being assessed.
* **Sincerity Rating**: Rate how sincere you find the brand to be.
* **Excitement Rating**: Rate how exciting the brand appears.
* **Competence Rating**: Rate how competent the brand seems.
* **Sophistication Rating**: Rate how sophisticated the brand appears.
* **Ruggedness Rating**: Rate how rugged or tough the brand seems.
* **Brand Voice**: Select the brand voice that best describes the brand's communication style.
* **Brand Archetype**: Select the brand archetype that best fits the brand from the given options.
* **Human Characteristics**: If this brand were a person, describe its human-like qualities.
* **Lifestyle Alignment**: Determine how well this brand fits your lifestyle.
* **Values Alignment**: Determine how well the brand's values align with yours.
* **Consistency Perception**: Rate how consistent the brand's personality is across touchpoints.
* **Differentiation**: Rate how well the brand stands out from others.
* **Personality Improvement Areas**: If you have any suggestions for improvement in the brand's personality, describe them.
* **Overall Assessment**: Provide a summary of your assessment of the brand's personality.

## Tips
* This form is a comprehensive assessment tool, so please take your time to carefully evaluate each trait.
* If you need help with any field, feel free to contact the form creators.
* Remember to be honest and sincere in your assessment, as this will help ensure accurate results.
