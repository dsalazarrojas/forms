# Behavioral Concepts Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Behavioral Concepts Assessment form is designed to evaluate a learner's understanding and confidence in applying various behavioral concepts. It's a crucial tool to gauge the effectiveness of training programs and help learners identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Learner Name** field (Field 1).
2. Enter the date you completed the training or assessment in the **Assessment Date** field (Field 2).
3. Enter the title of the course or training you completed in the **Course or Training Title** field (Field 3).
4. Answer each question in the subsequent fields, using the scale provided for each question.
	* For fields **4-9**, select one of the options for "Not at All," "Minimal," "Moderate," "Good," "Excellent" or "Not at All Confident," "Slightly Confident," "Moderately Confident," "Very Confident," "Extremely Confident."
	* For field **14**, select one of the options for "Not Applicable," "Not Likely," "Possibly," "Likely," or "Very Likely."
5. If applicable, enter your response in the **Most Valuable Concept** field (Field 15).
6. If you need clarification on any of the concepts, type it in the **Concepts That Need Clarification** field (Field 16).
7. Check the box next to **Would You Like Additional Resources** (Field 17).
8. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the **Additional Comments** field (Field 18).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Learner Name** (Field 1): Please enter your name as it appears on your official record.
* **Assessment Date** (Field 2): Enter the date you completed the training or assessment.
* **Course or Training Title** (Field 3): Enter the title of the course or training you completed.
* **Understanding of Habit Loops** (Field 4): How well do you understand the concept of habit loops?
* **Confidence in Applying Habit Loops** (Field 5): How confident are you in applying habit loops?
* **Understanding of Behavioral Triggers** (Field 6): How well do you understand behavioral triggers?
* **Confidence in Identifying Triggers** (Field 7): How confident are you in identifying triggers?
* **Understanding of Reinforcement and Rewards** (Field 8): How well do you understand reinforcement and rewards?
* **Confidence in Using Reinforcement and Rewards Effectively** (Field 9): How confident are you in using reinforcement and rewards effectively?
* **Understanding of Motivation Theories** (Field 10): How well do you understand motivation theories?
* **Confidence in Applying Motivation Strategies** (Field 11): How confident are you in applying motivation theories?
* **Understanding of Change Resistance** (Field 12): How well do you understand change resistance?
* **Confidence in Overcoming Resistance** (Field 13): How confident are you in overcoming resistance?
* **Can You Apply These Concepts in Your Work** (Field 14): How likely are you to apply these concepts in your work?
* **Most Valuable Concept** (Field 15): If applicable, please enter the concept that was most valuable to you.
* **Concepts That Need Clarification** (Field 16): If you're not clear about any of the concepts, please provide more information.
* **Would You Like Additional Resources** (Field 17): Check the box next to "True" if you'd like additional resources.
* **Additional Comments** (Field 18): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
