# Healthcare Staff Competency Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare Staff Competency Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the competencies of healthcare staff members to determine their suitability for specific roles or positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose your response for each question, indicating whether you have the relevant experience or skills.
2. Review each question carefully to ensure you're answering accurately.
3. Make sure to select all applicable options for each field where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Member** (`staff_member`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check if this staff member is the one being assessed.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select the role that this staff member is applying for or currently holds.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose all applicable departments where this staff member works or has experience in.
* **Competency Areas** (`competency_areas`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Indicate the competency areas where this staff member has demonstrated proficiency.
* **Skills And Knowledge** (`skills_and_knowledge`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check if this staff member possesses the necessary skills and knowledge for their role.
* **Clinical Experience** (`clinical_experience`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all applicable clinical experiences this staff member has had.
* **Education And Training** (`education_and_training`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose all applicable education and training received by this staff member.
* **Evaluations** (`evaluations`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check if this staff member has received evaluations or reviews.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all applicable recommendations received by this staff member.
* **Evaluations 2** (`evaluations_2`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Check if this staff member has received further evaluations or reviews.
