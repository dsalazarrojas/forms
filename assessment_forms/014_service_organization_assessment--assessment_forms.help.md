# Service Organization Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess the performance of a service organization. It consists of six questions that cover various aspects of their service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and think about your experience with the service organization.
2. For each question, select the answer that best reflects your experience.
3. If you're unsure or don't have experience with a particular service, leave the field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Organization** (`service_organization`): Are you satisfied with the service organization's service level agreements (SLAs)?
	+ 'Yes' if the service organization has met or exceeded their SLAs.
	+ 'No' if the service organization has not met their SLAs.
* **Customer Satisfaction** (`customer_satisfaction`): How satisfied are you with the service organization's services?
	+ Satisfied if you're very satisfied with their services.
	+ Dissatisfied if you're not very satisfied with their services.
* **Service Delivery** (`service_delivery`): Does the service organization deliver services as expected?
	+ 'Yes' if the service organization delivers services as expected.
	+ 'No' if the service organization does not deliver services as expected.
* **Communication** (`communication`): How would you rate the level of communication between the service organization and you?
	+ 'Yes' if you feel the communication is good.
	+ 'No' if you feel the communication is not good.
* **Improvement Plan** (`improvement_plan`): Does the service organization have an improvement plan in place?
	+ 'Yes' if they have an improvement plan.
	+ 'No' if they do not have an improvement plan.
* **Internal Evaluation** (`internal_evaluation`): Are the service organization's internal processes and systems well-structured and effective?
	+ 'Yes' if their internal processes and systems are well-structured and effective.
	+ 'No' if their internal processes and systems are not well-structured and effective.

## Tips

- Please be honest and accurate in your assessment.
- If you're unsure or don't have experience with a particular service, try to answer based on what you know.
- This form is for gathering feedback, so feel free to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
