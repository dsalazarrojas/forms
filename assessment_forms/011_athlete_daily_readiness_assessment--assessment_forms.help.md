# Athlete Daily Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The athlete daily readiness assessment is a tool used to evaluate the athlete's state of readiness for training. It ensures that the athlete's physical and mental state are in optimal condition for a productive and safe training session.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name.
2. **Date of Assessment**: Select the date of the assessment.
3. **Time of Submission**: Enter the time of submission.
4. **Sleep Quality**: Rate your sleep quality from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
5. **Sleep Duration**: Enter the number of hours you slept last night.
6. **Current Energy Level**: Rate your current energy level from 1 (Very Low) to 5 (Very High).
7. **Muscle Soreness Level**: Rate your muscle soreness level from 1 (None) to 5 (Extreme).
8. **Hydration Status**: Rate your hydration status from 1 (Dehydrated) to 5 (Well Hydrated).
9. **Mental Readiness**: Rate your mental state from 1 (Very Negative) to 5 (Very Positive).
10. **Current Stress Level**: Rate your stress level from 1 (No Stress) to 5 (High Stress).
11. **Readiness to Train**: Rate your readiness to train from 1 (Not Ready) to 5 (Fully Ready).
12. **Any new Injuries or Niggles?**: Answer if you have any new injuries or niggles to report.
13. **Injury or Niggle Details**: Enter a description of any new injuries or niggles.
14. **Any other comments for the coach?**: Provide any additional comments or information for the coach.
## Tips
- Complete the form honestly and accurately to ensure the coach receives a true representation of your current state.
- Rate your answers based on your actual experience and feeling, rather than guessing.
- Be specific when describing injuries or niggles in the "Injury or Niggle Details" field.
- Use clear and concise language when providing comments for the coach.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
