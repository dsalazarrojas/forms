# Archiving Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Archiving Knowledge Quiz is a form designed to test your knowledge and understanding of digital archiving concepts, techniques, and best practices. It assesses your skills in identifying key aspects of digital preservation, metadata management, and file formats suitable for long-term archiving.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the quiz, follow these steps:

* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately based on your knowledge and experience.
* Select the correct options for each question, and make sure to read each question carefully before answering.
* If you are unsure or have questions about any of the topics, provide brief explanations in the text fields provided.
* Finally, indicate your confidence level about your answers and suggest topics you would like to receive more training on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for scoring purposes.
* **Participant ID or Employee Number** (`participant_id`, `text`, optional): If you have a participant ID or employee number, enter it here. This is not required, but it helps with scoring and identification.
* **Date of Quiz** (`test_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you are taking the quiz.
* **What is digital archiving** (`question_1_definition`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best description of digital archiving from the options provided.
* **Key to digital preservation** (`question_2_preservation`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that are key to digital preservation.
* **Metadata describes what aspect of digital records** (`question_3_metadata`, `select_one`, required): Choose the option that best describes what metadata describes in digital records.
* **File formats most suitable for long-term preservation** (`question_4_formats`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the file formats that are most suitable for long-term preservation.
* **Format migration in digital archiving** (`question_5_migration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best description of format migration in digital archiving.
* **Name two major risks to digital preservation** (`question_6_risks`, `text`, required): Provide brief explanations of two major risks to digital preservation.
* **ISO standard for digital records management** (`question_7_standards`, `select_one`, required): Choose the correct ISO standard for digital records management.
* **How does digital archiving differ from digital access** (`question_8_access`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief explanation of the difference between digital archiving and digital access, if you wish.
* **Confidence level about these answers** (`confidence_level`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your confidence level about your answers, from Very confident to Very uncertain.
* **Topics for review** (`topics_for_review`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the topics you would like to receive more training on.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Share any additional comments or questions you have about the quiz.

Note that fields 1, 5, 10, 14, and 15 are required and need to be completed before submitting the form. Fields 9 and 16 are optional but still important for a comprehensive understanding.
