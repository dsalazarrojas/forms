# Position Specific Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
3. Enter your experience and skills in the "Experience" section.
4. Select your level of education from the available options.
5. Select your relevant skills from the available options.
6. Enter any relevant qualifications or certificates you have earned.
7. Select your references from the available options.
8. Enter your work history prophets (number of years).
9. Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
10. Enter your date of start (HH:MM AM/PM).
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Add any additional notes or comments.
14. Enter your first name.
15. Enter your middle name (if applicable).
16. Enter your last name.
17. Enter your address 1.
18. Enter your address 2 goals.
19. Enter your address 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Experience** (id: 2, type: select_multiple)
	+ This field is used to determine your level of experience. Please select "Yes" if you have relevant experience, and "No" if you do not.
* **Education** (id: 3, type: select_one)
	+ This field is used to determine your level of education. Please select your highest level of education completed.
* **Skills** (id: 4, type: select_multiple)
	+ This field is used to determine your relevant skills. Please select "Yes" for each skill you possess.
* **Qualifications** (id: 5, type: text)
	+ This field is used to enter any relevant qualifications or certificates you have earned.
* **References** (id: 6, type: select_multiple)
	+ This field is used to select your references. Please select "Yes" for each reference.
* **Work History Prophets** (id: 8, type: number)
	+ This field is used to enter your number of years of work history.
