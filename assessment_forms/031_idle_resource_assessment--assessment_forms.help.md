# Idle Resource Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Idle Resource Assessment" form is designed to collect information about idle resources, including their condition, status, and maintenance history. This form helps identify which resources need attention and schedule next maintenance actions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **User Input**: Enter any relevant information about the resource, such as its current status or location.
2. **location**: Provide the location where the resource is currently stored or located.
3. **asset_type**: Enter the type of asset or resource being assessed.
4. **asset_status**: Describe the current status of the resource.
5. **priority**: Indicate if the resource requires immediate attention ("Yes") or not ("No").
6. **resource_id**: Enter a unique identifier for the resource.
7. **assigned_tool**: Specify if the resource is assigned to a specific tool or personnel ("Yes") or not ("No").
8. **follow_up_date**: Enter the date for the next scheduled maintenance or follow-up.
9. **follow_up_by**: Enter the person or organization responsible for the follow-up.
10. **follow_up_status**: Select if the follow-up is active or inactive.
11. **asset_tag**: Enter any additional tags or labels associated with the resource.
12. **asset_tag_status**: Describe the status of the asset tag.
13. **comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the resource.
14. **asset_condition**: Indicate if the resource is in good condition ("Yes") or not ("No").
15. **asset_value**: Enter the value or monetary worth of the resource.
16. **asset_last_maint**: Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the resource.
17. **notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the resource.
18. **next_maintenance**: Enter the date for the next scheduled maintenance.
19. **follow_up_frequency**: Enter the frequency of follow-up actions.
20. **follow_up_interval**: Enter the interval between follow-up actions.
21. **follow_up_status_2**: Select if the follow-up is active or inactive.
22. **asset_last_maint_status**: Describe the status of the last maintenance performed.
23. **last_maintenance_frequency**: Enter the frequency of the last maintenance performed.
24. **follow_up_frequency_2**: Enter the frequency of follow-up actions.
25. **assigned_by**: Enter the person or organization responsible for the resource.
