# University Subject Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on university courses, allowing students to rate the quality of various aspects of the course. The form is meant to provide an evaluation of the course's overall quality, teaching staff, course materials, organization, and overall value.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide general information (page 1) by entering your name and any other relevant details in the text field.
2. For each question, select the answer that best matches your opinion. You can choose from three options for each question.
3. Answer question 1 about the overall quality of the course.
4. Answer question 2 about the quality of the teaching staff.
5. Answer question 3 about the relevance of the course materials.
6. Answer question 4 about the organization of the course.
7. Answer question 5 about the overall value of the course.
8. Provide any additional comments you have about the course in the "Comments" field.
9. Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (page 1)** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This field is for you to provide any general information about yourself, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **How would you rate the overall quality of this course? (page 2)** (`How would you rate the overall quality of this course?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Rate the overall quality of the course on a scale of 1 to 3, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **How would you rate the quality of the teaching staff? (page 2)** (`How would you rate the quality of the teaching staff?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Rate the quality of the teaching staff on a scale of 1 to 3, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **How would you rate the relevance of the course materials? (page 2)** (`How would you rate the relevance of the course materials?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Rate the relevance of the course materials on a scale of 1 to 3, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **What did you think of the course organization? (page 3)** (`What did you think of the course organization?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Rate the organization of the course on a scale of 1 to 3, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **What do you think of the overall value of this course? (page 3)** (`What do you think of the overall value of this course?`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Rate the overall value of the course on a scale of 1 to 3, where 1 is the lowest and 3 is the highest.
* **Comments (page 4)** (`Comments`, `text`, required: true):
	+ Type any additional comments you have about the course in this text field.
* **Email (page 4)** (`Email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* You can choose any one of the three options for each question.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please type them in the "Comments" field.
* If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or clarification, please enter your email address in the "Email" field.
