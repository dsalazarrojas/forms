# Thrive Sport And Fitness Solutions Client Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather general information, medical history, contact details, assessment questions, medical questions, and physical activity information from clients to better understand their needs and provide personalized recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the **General Information** section with your name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Next, move on to the **Medical History** section and provide any relevant medical information that may be helpful for us to understand your health status.
3. In the **Contact Information** section, please provide your contact details such as phone number and address.
4. The **Assessment Questions** section is where you can provide any additional information or concerns you may have.
5. In the **Medical Questions** section, please answer any medical-related questions that may be relevant to your assessment.
6. Finally, in the **Physical Activity** section, describe your current physical activity level and any exercise or sports-related activities you engage in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (General Information, text, not required): This is the starting point of the form where you can provide your basic contact information.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, not required): Please provide any medical history or conditions that may be relevant to your assessment.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, not required): Enter your phone number and address in this field.
* **Assessment Questions** (Assessment Questions, text, not required): Describe any questions or concerns you may have.
* **Medical Questions** (Medical Questions, text, not required): Answer medical-related questions that may be helpful for your assessment.
* **Physical Activity** (Physical Activity, text, not required): Describe your current physical activity level and any sports-related activities you engage in.
