# Account Manager Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an account manager's performance in various key areas, including client satisfaction, revenue growth, and critical skills.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the assessed employee's information, such as their name, employee ID, and department.
2. Choose the assessment date and period being assessed.
3. Evaluate the account manager's performance in each section, selecting the corresponding rating for each question.
4. Provide specific feedback and suggestions for improvement in the "Development Activities" and "Improvement Areas" sections.
5. Rate the account manager's overall performance at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assessed Employee Name**: Enter the full name of the account manager being assessed.
* **Assessor Name**: Enter the full name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessment Date**: Select the date when the assessment is being conducted.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's identification number.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the account manager works.
* **Assessment Period**: Enter the time period being assessed.
* **Client Satisfaction Rating**: Rate the account manager's client satisfaction scores as:

	+ Unsatisfactory
