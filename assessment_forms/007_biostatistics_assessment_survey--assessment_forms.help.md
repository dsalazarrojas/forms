# Biostatistics Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your biostatistics expertise and identify areas where you need improvement. It will help tailor training programs to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read each section carefully**: The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of biostatistics.
2. **Answer honestly and accurately**: Provide truthful and accurate information about your background, experience, and skills.
3. **Select relevant options**: Choose the options that best describe your situation or preferences.
4. **Rate your confidence and proficiency**: Use the provided scale to rate your confidence and proficiency in various biostatistics skills.
5. **Provide additional comments**: Share any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional Comments or Suggestions" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Respondent Information**: This section collects your contact information and background details.
	+ **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on official documents.
	+ **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up communication.
	+ **Institution or University**: Your current or affiliated institution.
	+ **Your Role**: Select your role from the provided options (Faculty Member, Graduate Student, Undergraduate Student, Researcher, Postdoctoral Fellow, Other).
	+ **Department or Field**: The department or area of study you are involved with.
* **2. Biostatistics Experience**: This section assesses your experience with biostatistics.
	+ **Biostatistics Experience Level**: Select your level of experience with biostatistics from the provided options (No Experience, Minimal, Some Experience, Moderate, Advanced, Expert).
	+ **Years of Experience with Biostatistics**: Approximately how many years you have been working with biostatistics.
* **3. Formal Biostatistics Training**: This section evaluates your formal training in biostatistics.
	+ **Formal Biostatistics Training**: Select your level of formal training in biostatistics from the provided options (None, Workshop Only, Course, Certificate, Degree).
* **4. Confidence and Competency**: This section assesses your confidence and competency in biostatistics skills.
	+ **Confidence in Statistical Analysis**: Rate your confidence in statistical analysis from 1-10.
	+ **Confidence in Research Design**: Rate your confidence in designing studies from 1-10.
	+ **Confidence in Data Interpretation**: Rate your confidence in interpreting results from 1-10.
	+ **Confidence in Using Statistical Software**: Rate your confidence in using statistical software from 1-10.
* **5. Skills Assessment**: This section evaluates your proficiency in various biostatistics skills.
	+ **Proficiency in Descriptive Statistics**: Rate your proficiency in descriptive statistics from the provided options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent).
	+ **Proficiency in Inferential Statistics**: Rate your proficiency in inferential statistics from the provided options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent).
	+ **Proficiency in Hypothesis Testing**: Rate your proficiency in hypothesis testing from the provided options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent).
	+ **Proficiency in Regression Analysis**: Rate your proficiency in regression analysis from the provided options (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, Excellent).
* **6. Training Needs and Interests**: This section identifies your training needs and interests.
	+ **Topics You Want to Learn**: Select the topics you are interested in learning more about from the provided options (Advanced Regression, Experimental Design, Survival Analysis, Bayesian Methods, Machine Learning, Data Visualization, Software Training).
* **7. Training Preferences**: This section determines your preferred learning format.
	+ **Preferred Learning Format**: Select your preferred way of learning from the provided options (In-Person Workshop, Online Course, Self-Paced Tutorial, Mentoring, Textbook Study).
* **8. Available Time for Training**: This section assesses your available time for training.
	+ **Available Time for Training**: Select the amount of time you can commit to training from the provided options (Less than 5 hours, 5-10 hours, 10-20 hours, 20-40 hours, 40+ hours).
* **9. Resource Access**: This section evaluates your access to resources.
	+ **Statistical Software Access**: Select the statistical software you have access to from the provided options (R or RStudio, SAS, SPSS, Stata, Python, Other).
	+ **Computational Resources Available**: Rate your access to computational resources from 1-5, with 1 being limited and 5 being excellent.

By following these steps and explanations, you will be able to complete the biostatistics assessment survey effectively and accurately.
