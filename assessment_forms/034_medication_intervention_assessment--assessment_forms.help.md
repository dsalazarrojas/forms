# Medication Intervention Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and document information about a patient's medication regimen, allowing healthcare providers to make informed decisions about their care.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate options for the "Patient Information" section, including patient name, date of birth, and gender.
2. Choose the type of medication the patient is taking using the "Medications" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the start and stop dates for the medication in the "Start Date" and "Stop Date" fields.
4. Provide a reason for the medication in the "Reason" field.
5. Add any additional notes about the patient's medication regimen in the "Notes" field.
6. Indicate if follow-ups are needed using the "Follow-ups" dropdown menu.
7. Select the follow-up dates for the medication using the "Follow-up Dates" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the medication name and dosage in the "Medication Name" and "Dose" fields.
9. Provide instructions for the medication in the "Instructions" field.
10. Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown) and follow-up status (Completed, Incomplete, or Unknown) using the "Patient Status" and "Follow-up Status" dropdown menus.
11. Enter the follow-up next and last dates using the "Follow-up Next" and "Follow-up Last" fields.
12. Provide additional follow-up notes in the "Follow-up Notes" field.
13. Enter the name, phone number, and email of the pharmacist involved in the patient's care.
14. Enter the patient's name and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (text): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and gender.
* **Medications** (select_multiple): Choose the type of medication the patient is taking.
* **Dosage Form** (select_one): Select the dosage form of the medication (e.g., Liquid, Tablet, etc.).
* **Start Date** (date): Enter the start date for the medication.
* **Stop Date** (date): Enter the stop date for the medication.
* **Reason** (text): Provide a reason for the medication.
* **Notes** (text): Add any additional notes about the patient's medication regimen.
* **Follow-ups** (select_multiple): Indicate if follow-ups are needed for the medication.
* **Follow-up Dates** (select_multiple): Select the follow-up dates for the medication.
* **Medication Name** (text): Enter the name of the medication.
* **Frequency** (text): Enter the frequency of the medication.
* **Dose** (text): Enter the dose of the medication.
* **Instructions** (text): Provide instructions for the medication.
* **Patient Status** (select_one): Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Follow-up Status** (select_one): Select the follow-up status (Completed, Incomplete, or Unknown).
* **Follow-up Next** (date): Enter the next follow-up date for the medication.
* **Follow-up Last** (date): Enter the last follow-up date for the medication.
* **Follow-up Interval** (text): Enter the follow-up interval for the medication.
* **Follow-up Notes** (text): Provide additional follow-up notes.
* **Pharmacist Name** (text): Enter the name of the pharmacist involved in the patient's care.
* **Pharmacist Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the pharmacist.
* **Pharmacist Email** (text): Enter the email of the pharmacist.
* **Patient Name** (text): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Gender** (select_one): Select the patient's gender (Male, Female, or Unknown).
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Status** (select_one): Select the patient's status (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
