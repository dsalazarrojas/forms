# Remote Work Ergonomic Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to assess the ergonomic setup and potential issues of employees who work from home or remotely. It helps to gather information about the employee's workstation, equipment, and working habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the form carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Provide accurate information about your workstation, including your height, the height of your chair, monitor, and other input devices.
3. Indicate your working habits, such as whether you have a standing desk and the location of your mouse.
4. List any other input devices you use.
5. Specify any ergonomic issues you have or have noticed.
6. Provide any recommendations or suggestions for improvement.
7. Enter the date of submission.
8. Enter any comments or observations you have about your workspace.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official personnel record.
* **Workstation Location** (`workstation_location`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' if your work-from-home workstation is located in a dedicated workspace away from distractions, or 'No' if it is in a shared living space.
* **Monitor Height** (`monitor_height`, `number`, required): Enter the height of your monitor from the floor in inches.
* **Input Device** (`input_device`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all input devices you use, such as 'Yes' for keyboard, 'Yes' for mouse, 'Yes' for other (specify).
* **Chair Height** (`chair_height`, `number`, required): Enter the height of your chair from the floor in inches.
* **Standing Desk Height** (`standing_desk_height`, `number`, optional): Enter the height of your standing desk from the floor in inches (only applicable if you use a standing desk).
* **Keyboard Height** (`keyboard_height`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all keyboard heights you use, such as 'Yes' for standard height, 'Yes' for high-rise.
* **Mouse Location** (`mouse_location`, `select_one`, optional): Select the location of your mouse, such as 'Yes' for left, 'Yes' for right.
* **Other Input Devices** (`other_input_devices`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all other input devices you use, such as 'Yes' for other (specify).
* **Ergonomic Issues** (`ergonomic_issues`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all ergonomic issues you experience, such as 'Yes' for discomfort, 'Yes' for pain.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improving your workspace.
* **Submission Date** (`submission`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or observations you have about your workspace.
* **Reviewer** (`reviewer`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the reviewer.
