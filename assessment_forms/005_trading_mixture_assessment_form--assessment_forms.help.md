# Trading Mixture Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the trading mixture of a product to identify potential hazards and ensure safe and proper handling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the intended use of the trading mixture.
2. Identify the components of the trading mixture.
3. Evaluate the potential hazards associated with the mixture.
4. Provide details about the laboratory that conducted the testing and evaluation.
5. Complete all required fields and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Introduction
* **trading_mixture_assessment_form_label** (Text, Required): This field is where you describe the trading mixture and its purpose.

### Intended Use
* **trading_mixture_assessment** (Text, Required): This field is where you describe the intended use of the trading mixture.

### Components
* **components** (Text, Required): List all the components of the trading mixture.

### Hazards
* **hazards** (Text, Required): Identify any potential hazards associated with the trading mixture.

### Document Details
* **document_details** (Text, Required): Provide any relevant documentation or reports related to the trading mixture.

### Internal Review
* **internal_review** (Text, Required): Describe the internal review process for the trading mixture.

### Trading Decisions
* **trading_decisions** (Text, Required): Outline the decisions made regarding the trading mixture.

### Components List
* **components_list** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate whether each component is present in the trading mixture.
	+ Yes/No

### Hazard List
* **hazard_list** (Select Multiple, Required): Identify any hazards associated with each component.
	+ Yes/No

### Laboratory Details
* **lab_details** (Text, Required): Provide details about the laboratory that conducted the testing and evaluation.

### Laboratory ID
* **lab_id** (Number, Required): Enter the ID of the laboratory that conducted the testing and evaluation.

### Laboratory Location
* **lab_location** (Text, Required): Enter the location of the laboratory that conducted the testing and evaluation.

### Laboratory Phone
* **lab_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the laboratory that conducted the testing and evaluation.

### Laboratory Email
* **lab_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the laboratory that conducted the testing and evaluation.

### Distributor Details
* **distributor_details** (Text, Required): Provide details about the distributor of the trading mixture.

### Distributor ID
* **distributor_id** (Number, Required): Enter the ID of the distributor of the trading mixture.

### Distributor Location
* **distributor_location** (Text, Required): Enter the location of the distributor of the trading mixture.

### Distributor Phone
* **distributor_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the distributor of the trading mixture.

### Distributor Email
* **distributor_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the distributor of the trading mixture.

### Manufacturer Details
* **manufacturer_details** (Text, Required): Provide details about the manufacturer of the trading mixture.

### Manufacturer ID
* **manufacturer_id** (Number, Required): Enter the ID of the manufacturer of the trading mixture.

### Manufacturer Location
* **manufacturer_location** (Text, Required): Enter the location of the manufacturer of the trading mixture.

### Manufacturer Phone
* **manufacturer_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the manufacturer of the trading mixture.

### Manufacturer Email
* **manufacturer_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the manufacturer of the trading mixture.

### Review Date
* **review_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the review of the trading mixture.

Please note that all fields are required, and the information provided must be accurate and complete.
