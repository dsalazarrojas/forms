# Facility Features Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Facility Features Assessment form is designed to collect detailed information about a facility's features and characteristics. This form helps assessors evaluate the facility's safety, security, and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to the best of your ability.
2. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access to fill out this form.
3. Review each field carefully and enter the relevant information for your facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Address**:
	+ Enter the physical address of the facility (e.g., 123 Main St, Anytown, USA).
* **Building Name**:
	+ Enter the name of the building or facility (e.g., Smith Building, Anytown).
* **Floor Area**:
	+ Enter the total floor area of the facility (e.g., 10,000 square feet).
* **Building Type**:
	+ Select the type of building (e.g., Residential, Commercial, Industrial).
* **Facility Features**:
	+ Select all features that apply to your facility (e.g., Parking Lot, Restroom, Elevator).
* **Total Floors**:
	+ Enter the total number of floors in the facility.
* **Building Age**:
	+ Enter the age of the building (e.g., 5 years).
* **Building Materials**:
	+ Select all materials used in the construction of the building (e.g., Brick, Concrete, Steel).
* **Electrical Systems**:
	+ Select all electrical systems used in the facility (e.g., Circuit Breaker, Generator, Solar Panel).
* **Plumbing Systems**:
	+ Select all plumbing systems used in the facility (e.g., Water Heater, Fire Hydrant, Sump Pump).
* **Heating and Cooling**:
	+ Select all heating and cooling systems used in the facility (e.g., Central AC, Radiant Heat, Heat Pump).
* **Safety Systems**:
	+ Select all safety systems used in the facility (e.g., Fire Alarm, Fire Suppression, Security Camera).
* **Fire Safety Procedures**:
	+ Enter any fire safety procedures or protocols in place (e.g., fire drills, emergency exits).
* **Evacuation Plans**:
	+ Select all evacuation plans or procedures in place (e.g., Fire Drill, Emergency Exit, Evacuation Route).
* **Emergency Contact**:
	+ Enter the emergency contact person's name, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Phone**:
	+ Enter the contact phone number for the facility.
* **Contact Email**:
	+ Enter the contact email for the facility.
* **Contact Fax**:
	+ Enter the contact fax number for the facility.
* **Contact Address**:
	+ Enter the contact address for the facility.
* **Building Owner**:
	+ Enter the name of the building owner.
* **Building Manager**:
	+ Enter the name of the building manager.
* **Emergency Contact Person**:
	+ Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Building Supervisor**:
	+ Enter the name of the building supervisor.
* **Facility Inspector**:
	+ Enter the name of the facility inspector.
* **Submit**:
	+ Review and submit your completed form to complete the assessment.
