# extracurricular_activities_impact_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extracurricular Activities Impact Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the effects of extracurricular activities on students' lives, including their education, career, social, physical, emotional, and spiritual development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of extracurricular activity your student has participated in (e.g., High School Club, School Team, etc.).
2. Enter the name of the activity.
3. If the activity is of a type not listed, please describe it in the "Program Type Other" field.
4. Describe your student's experience in the "Student Experience" field.
5. For each of the remaining fields (Impact on Education, Impact on Career, Impact on Social, Impact on Physical, Impact on Emotional, and Impact on Spiritual), describe how the extracurricular activity affected your student in a positive or negative way. For example, "My student's participation in the school club improved their focus and time management skills."
6. If you have any additional comments or information about the extracurricular activity, please enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Type**: Choose the type of extracurricular activity your student participated in (e.g., High School Club, School Team, etc.).
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the activity.
* **Program Type Other**: If the activity is not listed, describe it in this field.
* **Student Experience**: Describe your student's experience in the activity.
* **Impact on Education**: Describe how the activity affected your student's education.
* **Impact on Career**: Describe how the activity affected your student's career.
* **Impact on Social**: Describe how the activity affected your student's social life.
* **Impact on Physical**: Describe how the activity affected your student's physical health or well-being.
* **Impact on Emotional**: Describe how the activity affected your student's emotional well-being.
* **Impact on Spiritual**: Describe how the activity affected your student's spiritual or moral development.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or information about the activity.

Note: This form is for assessment purposes only and should not be used to gather information that is already well-documented or easily accessible.
