# Metabolic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help calculate an individual's metabolic rate and daily energy expenditure based on provided information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answers for each field based on your real-life circumstances.
2. Enter your weight in the "Weight (kg)" field.
3. Enter your height in the "Height (cm)" field.
4. Enter your age in the "Age" field.
5. Choose your gender from the "Gender" field.
6. Choose your height unit from the "Height Unit" field (either meters or centimeters).
7. Choose your weight unit from the "Weight Unit" field (either kilograms or pounds).
8. Select your daily activity level from the "Daily Activity Level" field.
9. Enter your resting metabolic rate (RMR) or basal metabolic rate (BMR) if you know it.
10. Optionally, enter the formulas used for your resting metabolic rate and total daily energy expenditure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Initial Assessment** (`Initial Assessment`, `metabolic_assessment_form`, Optional): This field is a text box where you can provide any additional information that might be relevant to your metabolic assessment.
*   **Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR)** (`Resting Metabolic Rate (RMR)`, `resting_metabolic_rate`, Optional): This field is used to enter your resting metabolic rate, which is the number of calories your body burns at rest.
*   **Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)** (`Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)`, `basal_metabolic_rate`, Optional): This field is used to enter your basal metabolic rate, which is the number of calories your body burns at rest.
*   **Daily Activity Level** (`Daily Activity Level`, `daily_activity_level`, Required): This field asks you to select your level of physical activity. Please choose the best fit from the provided options.
*   **Weight (kg)** (`Weight (kg)`, `weight_kg`, Required): Please enter your weight in kilograms.
*   **Height (cm)** (`Height (cm)`, `height_cm`, Required): Please enter your height in centimeters.
*   **Age** (`Age`, `age`, Required): Please enter your age.
*   **Gender** (`Gender`, `gender`, Required): Please choose your gender from the provided options.
*   **Height Unit** (`Height Unit`, `height_unit`, Required): Please choose your height unit from the provided options.
*   **Weight Unit** (`Weight Unit`, `weight_unit`, Required): Please choose your weight unit from the provided options.
*   **Activity Level** (`Activity Level`, `activity_level`, Required): This field asks you to select your level of physical activity. Please choose the best fit from the provided options.
*   **Basal Metabolic Rate Formula** (`Basal Metabolic Rate Formula`, `basal_metabolic_rate_formula`, Optional): This field is used to enter the formula for your basal metabolic rate, which is an optional field and not necessary for the calculation.
*   **Resting Metabolic Rate Formula** (`Resting Metabolic Rate Formula`, `resting_metabolic_rate_formula`, Optional): This field is used to enter the formula for your resting metabolic rate, which is an optional field and not necessary for the calculation.
*   **Total Daily Energy Expenditure Formula** (`Total Daily Energy Expenditure Formula`, `total_daily_energy_expenditure_formula`, Optional): This field is used to enter the formula for your total daily energy expenditure, which is an optional field and not necessary for the calculation.

Note: This form requires all required fields to be completed before the calculation can be performed.
