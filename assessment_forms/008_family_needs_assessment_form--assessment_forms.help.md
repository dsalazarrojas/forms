# Family Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a family's needs and circumstances. It is intended for use by social workers, counselors, or other professionals working with families to assess their needs and develop a plan to support them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the parent's name in the "Enter Parent Name" field.
2. List the names of all children in the "Enter Children Names" field using the provided options.
3. If applicable, check if the family is currently in counseling service in the "In Counseling Service" field.
4. Enter any requests from the service provider in the "Enter Requests" field.
5. Provide parent and child contact information in the "Parent Contact Info" and "Children Contact Info" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the number of children in the "Enter Children Count" field.
7. Add any notes for the service provider in the "Service Provider Notes" field.
8. Select the level of need for the family in the "Family Needs Assessment" field.
9. Indicate how often the family needs assessment is needed in the "Family Needs Assessment Frequency" field.
10. Enter the date and time of the family needs assessment in the "Family Needs Assessment Date" and "Family Needs Assessment Time" fields, respectively.
11. Specify the location of the family needs assessment in the "Family Needs Assessment Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Parent Name**: This field is used to record the name of the parent or guardian who is completing the form.
* **Enter Children Names**: This field allows you to list the names of all children in the family. Select all children that apply from the provided options.
* **In Counseling Service**: Check this field if the family is currently receiving counseling services.
* **Enter Requests**: This field is for any requests or concerns the family has regarding their needs.
* **Parent Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for the parent or guardian.
* **Children Contact Info**: Enter the contact information for the children.
* **Enter Children Count**: Enter the number of children in the family.
* **Service Provider Notes**: Add any notes or comments for the service provider.
* **Family Needs Assessment**: Select the level of need for the family (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Family Needs Assessment Frequency**: Indicate how often the family needs assessment is required (Often, Occasionally, or Rarely).
* **Family Needs Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the family needs assessment.
* **Family Needs Assessment Time**: Enter the time of the family needs assessment.
* **Family Needs Assessment Location**: Specify the location where the family needs assessment took place.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible support for the family.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with a supervisor or colleague for guidance.
