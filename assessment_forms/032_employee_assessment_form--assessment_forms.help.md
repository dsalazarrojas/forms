# Employee Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Assessment Form is a tool used by our organization to evaluate an employee's performance and skills. This form helps us to better understand an employee's strengths and weaknesses and provides a basis for development and improvement plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Employee Assessment Form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible to help us better understand your performance and skills.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Information** (Text, Optional): This field is for you to describe your current work performance, including any accomplishments or challenges you've faced recently.
* **Employee Type** (Select One, Optional): Please select one of the following options to indicate your employment status:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Remote
* **Skills and Experience** (Text, Optional): Here, you can describe your relevant skills and experience, including any specialized training or certifications you've acquired.
* **Experience** (Time, Optional): Please enter the length of time you've been working in your current position or in a similar role to provide context for your skills and experience.
