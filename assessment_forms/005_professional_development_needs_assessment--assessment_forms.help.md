# Professional Development Needs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Needs Assessment form is used to gather information about your professional development needs. This form will help us better understand your current job role, work environment, and professional skills, ultimately providing valuable insights into areas where you need support for growth and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and honestly, providing accurate information about yourself.
2. Answer each question carefully, taking into account your current situation and needs.
3. Review your answers to ensure they are accurate before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Job Category** (`job_category`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe your current or desired job category (e.g., 'Yes' for multiple categories).
- **Professional Skills** (`professional_skills`, text, optional): Describe your current or desired professional skills.
- **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Please list any relevant education or training you've received.
- **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
- **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, optional): State your short-term and long-term career goals.
- **Job Search Status** (`job_search_status`, text, optional): Describe your current job search status (e.g., actively searching, on the fence, etc.).
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current or desired job title.
- **Work Environment** (`work_environment`, select_one, optional): Select the option that best describes your current or desired work environment.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Describe the type of job you're currently in or seeking.
- **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, text, optional): Provide the industry you're currently in or seeking.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Write a brief description of your current or desired job role.
- **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your current or desired job.
- **Job Salary** (`job_salary`, text, optional): Mention your current or desired salary range.
- **Job Requirements** (`job_requirements`, text, optional): List the skills or qualifications you're looking for in a job.
- **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status that best describes your current work situation.
- **Job Title Status** (`job_title_status`, text, optional): Describe the status of your current or desired job title.
- **Job Type Status** (`job_type_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired job type.
- **Job Industry Status** (`job_industry_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired job industry.
- **Job Description Status** (`job_description_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired job description.
- **Job Location Status** (`job_location_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired job location.
- **Job Salary Status** (`job_salary_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired salary range.
- **Job Requirements Status** (`job_requirements_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired job requirements.
- **Work Environment Status** (`work_environment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired work environment.
- **Job Category Status** (`job_category_status`, select_multiple, optional): Check all that apply to describe the status of your current or desired job category.
