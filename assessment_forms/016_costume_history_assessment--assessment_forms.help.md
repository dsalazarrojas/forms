# Costume History Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Costume History Assessment form is designed to evaluate your understanding of the history of costume design. It will test your knowledge of different historical periods, art styles, notable artists, materials, and techniques used in costume design. The form is meant to be a quick and engaging assessment of your knowledge, and it is not a comprehensive or exhaustive resource.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select the correct options for each question, taking into account the information you have learned.
3. Be as specific as possible when providing answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, not required): Introduce yourself and tell us a bit about your background and experience related to costume design. Be brief and concise.
* **Historical Period** (`historical_period`, select_one, not required): Choose the historical period you are most knowledgeable about, such as Ancient Greece, Renaissance, etc.
* **Art Styles** (`art_styles`, select_multiple, not required): Select the art styles that you are familiar with, such as Art Deco, Gothic, etc.
* **Notable Artists** (`artists`, select_one, not required): Choose a notable artist who is well-known for their work in costume design, such as Mary Quant or Oscar Wilde.
* **Materials and Techniques** (`materials_and_techniques`, text, not required): Describe some of the materials and techniques used in costume design, such as silk, velvet, or beading.
* **Textiles** (`textiles`, text, not required): Mention some examples of textiles commonly used in costume design, such as cotton, lace, or leather.
* **Fashion Trends** (`fashion_trends`, text, not required): Describe some notable fashion trends that you are familiar with, such as the influence of cultural movements or historical events.
* **Cultural Influences** (`cultural_influences`, text, not required): Explain how cultural influences have shaped costume design throughout history.
* **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, text, not required): Summarize your knowledge and experience in costume design, and reflect on what you have learned.

## Tips

* Be honest about your knowledge and experience.
* Provide specific examples to support your answers.
* Take your time, but be as concise as possible.
* This form is meant to be a quick assessment, so don't worry about making mistakes.
* Have fun and enjoy learning!
