# fat_malabsorption_assessment - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient Dob" field in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Describe the patient's symptoms in the "Symptoms" field.
4. Indicate the frequency of the patient's symptoms in the "Symptom Frequency" field.
5. Provide any relevant medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medications" field.
7. Enter any relevant laboratory results in the "Laboratory Results" field.
8. Describe the patient's malabsorption symptoms in the "Malabsorption Symptoms" field.
9. Indicate the patient's gastrointestinal system health in the "Gastrointestinal System" field.
10. Describe any weight changes experienced by the patient in the "Weight Change" field.
11. Enter any relevant malabsorption diagnosis in the "Malabsorption Diagnosis" field.
12. Describe the patient's clinical status in the "Clinical Status" field.
13. Enter any laboratory diagnosis in the "Laboratory Diagnosis" field.
14. Create a treatment plan for the patient in the "Treatment Plan" field.
15. Enter the patient's outcome in the "Patient Outcome" field.
16. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false)
* Enter the patient's full name as they would like to be addressed.

### 2. **Patient Dob** (`patient_dob`, date, required: false)
* Enter the patient's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### 3. **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, required: false)
* Describe the patient's malabsorption symptoms, such as bloating, gas, or abdominal pain.

### 4. **Symptom Frequency** (`symptoms_frequency`, text, required: false)
* Indicate how often the patient experiences their symptoms, e.g., daily, weekly, or occasionally.

### 5. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false)
* Provide any relevant medical history that may be related to malabsorption, e.g., previous surgeries or chronic conditions.

### 6. **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false)
* List any medications the patient is currently taking that may be related to malabsorption.

### 7. **Laboratory Results** (`laboratory_results`, text, required: false)
* Enter any relevant laboratory results, such as blood tests or imaging studies.

### 8. **Malabsorption Symptoms** (`malabsorption_symptoms`, text, required: false)
* Describe the patient's specific malabsorption symptoms, e.g., bloating, gas, or abdominal pain.

### 9. **Gastrointestinal System** (`gastrointestinal_system`, text, required: false)
* Indicate any issues with the patient's gastrointestinal system, such as constipation or diarrhea.

### 10. **Weight Change** (`weight_change`, text, required: false)
* Describe any weight changes the patient has experienced, e.g., weight loss or gain.

### 11. **Malabsorption Diagnosis** (`malabsorption_diagnosis`, text, required: false)
* Enter any relevant malabsorption diagnosis, such as celiac disease or lactose intolerance.

### 12. **Clinical Status** (`clinical_status`, text, required: false)
* Describe the patient's current clinical status, e.g., stable or unstable.

### 13. **Laboratory Diagnosis** (`laboratory_diagnosis`, text, required: false)
* Enter any laboratory-based diagnosis for malabsorption, e.g., low stomach acid levels.

### 14. **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, required: false)
* Create a plan of action to address the patient's malabsorption, including any necessary lifestyle changes or medications.

### 15. **Patient Outcome** (`patient_outcome`, text, required: false)
* Enter any information about the patient's outcome, such as improved symptoms or reduced weight loss.

### 16. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false)
* Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's malabsorption assessment.

## Tips
* Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and completely to ensure accurate assessment and treatment.
* Review and update the form regularly to track patient progress and adjust the treatment plan as needed.
* Use the information collected from this form to inform further testing, monitoring, or treatment decisions.
