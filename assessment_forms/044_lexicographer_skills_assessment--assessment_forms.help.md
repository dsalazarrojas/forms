# Lexicographer Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lexicographer Skills Assessment form is designed to collect essential information about individuals with lexicographic skills. This form is used for assessment and evaluation purposes, ensuring accurate and thorough data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. Provide your Job Title.
5. Type your Work Experience in the provided field.
6. Enter your Email Address.
7. Enter your Phone Number (optional).
8. Select your language skills from the list (e.g., English, French, Mandarin, Spanish).
9. Select your level of lexicographic skills (e.g., Elementary, Intermediate, Advanced).
10. If applicable, list any other skills you possess.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (First Name)
* **First Name**: (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name (Last Name)
* **Last Name**: (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Date of Birth (Date of Birth)
* **Date of Birth**: (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

### Job Title (Job Title)
* **Job Title**: (text, required): Enter your job title or position you hold.

### Work Experience (Work Experience)
* **Work Experience**: (text, required): Describe your work experience in detail, including relevant job roles and duration.

### Email (Email)
* **Email**: (email, required): Enter your email address.

### Phone (Phone)
* **Phone**: (text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to share it).

### Skills (Skills)
* **Skills**: (select_one, required): Select your languages from the list:
	+ English
	+ French
	+ Mandarin
	+ Spanish

### Native Tongue (Native Tongue)
* **Native Tongue**: (select_multiple, optional): Select your native language(s) from the list:
	+ English
	+ French
	+ Mandarin
	+ Spanish

### Lexicographic Skill Level (Lexicographic Skill Level)
* **Lexicographic Skill Level**: (select_multiple, required): Select your level of lexicographic skills:
	+ Elementary
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced

### Other Skills (Other Skills)
* **Other Skills**: (text, optional): List any additional skills you possess, if not already included in the previous field.

### Comments (Comments)
* **Comments**: (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes, if needed.
