# Event Environmental Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Event Environmental Impact Assessment Form is used to record and analyze the potential environmental impact of a specific event, such as a conference or festival, on the surrounding area.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Complete each section carefully, as the data collected will be used to inform decisions related to the environmental impact of the event.**
2.  Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with accurate and detailed information about the event.
3.  For optional fields, consider the specific needs and circumstances of the event and answer accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **event_name** (text, optional): *Enter the name of the event, for example, "Annual Music Festival" or "Company Conference".*
*   **event_location** (text, optional): *Enter the location where the event will take place, including any relevant details such as the city, state, and country.*
*   **event_date** (date, optional): *Enter the date of the event, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.*
*   **event_time** (time, optional): *Enter the start time of the event, in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00.*
*   **number_of_people** (number, optional): *Estimate the total number of attendees or participants.*
*   **event_duration** (number, optional): *Enter the length of the event, in hours.*
*   **event_type** (select one, optional):
	+ *small*: The event has a small or localized impact.
	+ *medium*: The event has a medium impact, requiring some special arrangements.
	+ *large*: The event has a large or extensive impact.
*   **waste_management** (select one, optional):
	+ *no*: No special waste management is required.
	+ *yes*: Special waste management arrangements are necessary.
*   **recycling_programs** (select multiple, optional):
	+ *no*: No recycling programs are in place.
	+ *yes*: Recycling programs are implemented.
*   **energy_efficiency** (select one, optional):
	+ *low*: The event has low energy efficiency.
	+ *moderate*: The event has moderate energy efficiency.
	+ *high*: The event has high energy efficiency.
*   **transportation** (select multiple, optional):
	+ *car*: Private vehicles are expected to be used for transportation.
	+ *bus*: Public buses or shuttles will be used for transportation.
	+ *train*: Train is an expected mode of transportation.
	+ *flight*: Flight is an expected mode of transportation.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
*   If unsure about any field, consult with the event organizers or relevant authorities.
*   This form must not be used for events without an environmental impact.
