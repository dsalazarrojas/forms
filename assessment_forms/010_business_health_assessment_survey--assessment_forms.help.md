# Business Health Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Health Assessment Survey is designed to evaluate a company's overall well-being by gathering information on various aspects of its performance. The survey includes questions about financial stability, operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, major challenges, strategic priorities, readiness for change, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Respond to each question carefully**, providing as much detail as possible.
2. **Answer questions based on your current knowledge** about the company.
3. **Save your progress** and come back to it later if needed.
4. **Review and submit your answers** when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization name**: This field is used to collect the name of the company being assessed.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Hint: Enter the official name of the company (e.g., "ABC Corporation")

* **Respondent role**: This field is used to identify your role within the company.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Hint: Enter your job title or position

* **Financial stability**: This field asks you to rate the company's current financial stability.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent

* **Operational efficiency**: This field asks you to rate the company's operational efficiency.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Options: Poor, Fair, Good, Excellent

* **Customer satisfaction**: This field asks you to rate the company's customer satisfaction levels.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Options: Low, Moderate, High

* **Major challenges**: This field allows you to list the main challenges facing the company.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Provide specific challenges, e.g., "Managing supply chain disruptions"

* **Strategic priorities**: This field allows you to select up to three strategic priorities for the company.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No
	+ Options: Revenue growth, Cost reduction, Talent development, Digital transformation, Market expansion

* **Readiness for change**: This field asks you to rate the company's readiness for change.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Required: Yes
	+ Options: Not ready, Somewhat ready, Ready, Very ready

* **Additional comments**: This field is for any additional notes or comments you'd like to provide to the consultant.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Use this field to provide further context or details about the company's situation.

## Tips
* Please answer honestly and thoroughly, as the results will help guide the consultant in providing valuable insights and recommendations.
* If you're unsure about any of the questions, you can always save your progress and come back to it later.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
