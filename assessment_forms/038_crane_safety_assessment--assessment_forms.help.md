# Crane Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Crane Safety Assessment" form is used to assess the safety of cranes within the organization. It helps identify and document potential hazards and the corrective actions taken to mitigate them. This form should only be used for assessing cranes and not for any other purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, please ensure you have all the necessary information and resources required to complete this form accurately.
2.  Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields. Note that all fields are optional unless specifically marked as required.
3.  If you are unsure about any field or the purpose of this form, please seek guidance from your supervisor or relevant personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, **required: false**): This section is for a brief introduction or overview of the crane being assessed. You can use it to provide context or background information about the crane.
*  **Crane Details** (`Crane Details`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the details of the crane being assessed. This could include its location, type, or any other relevant information.
*  **Inspection Date** (`Inspection Date`, `date`, **required: false**): Enter the date of the crane inspection. This should match the date of the actual inspection.
*  **Inspector Name** (`Inspector Name`, `text`, **required: false**): Enter the name of the person conducting the crane safety assessment.
*  **Hazard Found** (`Hazard Found`, `select_multiple`, **required: false**):
    *   'Yes'
    *   'No'
    *   Select 'Yes' if any hazards were found during the inspection.
*  **Hazard Description** (`Hazard Description`, `text`, **required: false**): Provide a detailed description of the hazard found during the assessment.
*  **Risk Level** (`Risk Level`, `select_one`, **required: false`):
    *   'Low'
    *   'Moderate'
    *   'High'
    *   Indicate the level of risk posed by the hazard found.
*  **Corrective Action** (`Corrective Action`, `text`, **required: false`): Describe the corrective actions taken to mitigate the hazard found.
*  **Inspector Comments** (`Inspector Comments`, `note`, **required: false`): Leave any additional comments or notes about the assessment or the corrective actions.
*  **Safety Meeting** (`Safety Meeting`, `select_one`, **required: false`):
    *   'Yes'
    *   'No'
    *   Indicate whether a safety meeting was held as a result of the assessment.
*  **Next Safety Inspection** (`Next Safety Inspection`, `date`, **required: false`): Enter the date of the next safety inspection for the crane.

## Tips

*  Be thorough and accurate when completing this form to ensure that all potential hazards are identified and documented.
*  If any questions or concerns arise during the completion of this form, please seek guidance from your supervisor or relevant personnel.
*  This form is meant to aid in ensuring crane safety; if you have any questions or doubts, please don't hesitate to ask.
