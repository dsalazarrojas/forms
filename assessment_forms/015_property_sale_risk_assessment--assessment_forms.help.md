# Property Sale Risk Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Sale Risk Assessment form is designed to identify and document any potential risks associated with a property sale. This process helps buyers and sellers make informed decisions and be prepared to address any concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these general steps:

1.  Review the property details, including its address and status.
2.  Assess the legal risks, such as fines, court cases, or other legal issues.
3.  Identify any structural risks, such as foundation or roof issues.
4.  Check for safety risks, including asbestos exposure or lead paint concerns.
5.  Record your risk assessment score and comments.
6.  Update the risk assessment status and notes as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Risk Assessment Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This is the main section where you will document your risk assessment findings.
*   **Property Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the property, including its address.
*   **Legal Risks** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check for any outstanding fines, court cases, or other legal issues that may affect the property.
    *   Select all applicable options from the dropdown list.
*   **Structural Risks** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify any foundation issues or roof problems that may impact the property.
    *   Choose the most accurate option from the list.
*   **Safety Risks** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Check for any existing or potential safety hazards, such as asbestos exposure or lead paint concerns.
    *   Select the most relevant option from the list.
*   **Property Sale Risk Score** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Enter a numerical score to indicate the level of risk associated with the property.
*   **Risk Assessment Comments** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional comments or notes about the risk assessment process.
*   **Risk Assessment Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Record the date the risk assessment was completed.
*   **Property Address** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Enter the property address.
*   **Property Status** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate the current status of the property.
    *   Choose from the available options.
*   **Risk Assessment Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false): Update the risk assessment status as necessary.
    *   Select from the available options.
*   **Risk Assessment Notes** (`12`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes about the risk assessment process.
*   **Property Type** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): Identify the type of property.
    *   Choose from the available options.
*   **Sales Price** (`14`, `number`, required: false): Enter the sales price of the property.

Note: This help guide is intended to be a general guide. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or relevant authorities for guidance.
