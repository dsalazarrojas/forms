# Syntactic Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate your knowledge on syntax, specifically:
- Identifying correct grammatical structures
- Verb tenses
- Sentence agreements
- Sentence forms
- Punctuation
- Clause and phrase structures
- Modal and auxiliary verb forms

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by reading the introduction on page 1.
2. Answer the select-type questions by choosing the correct option from the dropdown list.
3. Answer the text question by writing a short paragraph on page 7.
4. Click the "Submission" button to submit your answers.
