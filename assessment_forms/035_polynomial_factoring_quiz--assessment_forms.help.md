# Polynomial Factoring Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Polynomial Factoring Quiz" is designed to test the understanding of polynomial factoring among users, likely as part of an educational or internal assessment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the instructions and guidelines provided carefully before starting the quiz.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Fill out each field carefully, considering the type of input required for each.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Polynomial** (`polynomial`, number, optional): Please enter the polynomial for which you want to factor, or leave it blank if you're not providing one for factoring.
* **Coefficients** (`coefficients`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to factor the polynomial (Yes/No).
* **Factoring** (`factoring`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the methods you would like to use for factoring (Yes/No for each method).
* **Check** (`check`, date, optional): Please enter the date you would like to use for this assessment (if applicable).
* **Verify** (`verify`, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to use for this assessment (if applicable).
* **Result** (`result`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or results from your factoring attempt.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive any feedback or results via email.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to receive a call back with your results (or leave blank if not applicable).
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to submit your assessment and 'No' if you would like to cancel or edit.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest with your responses to ensure the best outcome.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, please contact your instructor or supervisor for assistance.
