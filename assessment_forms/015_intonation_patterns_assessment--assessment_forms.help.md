# Intonation Patterns Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's intonation patterns, which refers to the pattern of pitch variations used while speaking. This assessment aims to evaluate your ability to identify and respond to intonation patterns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page carefully, as each page focuses on a different aspect of intonation patterns.
2. For each page, select the correct response according to the options provided.
3. Ensure that you complete all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Introduction

* **Introduction** (`title`, optional): This is the first page, which is just an introduction to the form.
    It is not a required field and can be skipped.

### Page 2: Pronunciation

* **Pronunciation** (`Pronunciation`, optional, select_one): Answer the question "Yes" or "No" according to your pronunciation habits.
    This field is used to assess your awareness of your pronunciation patterns.

### Page 3: Pitch

* **Pitch** (`Pitch`, optional, number): Enter the number of pitch variations you have observed. 
    This field is used to evaluate your accuracy in identifying pitch patterns.

### Page 4: Assessment

* **Assessment** (`Assessment`, optional, select_multiple): Select "Yes" or "No" according to your assessment of the intonation patterns. 
    This field is used to gauge your understanding of intonation patterns.

### Page 5: Results

* **Results** (`Results`, optional, time): Fill in the time taken to complete the assessment. 
    This field is used to measure the time taken to complete the form.
