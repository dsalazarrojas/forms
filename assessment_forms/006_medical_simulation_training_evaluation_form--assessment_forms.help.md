# Medical Simulation Training Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from trainees after a medical simulation training session. It helps to assess the effectiveness of the simulation and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your personal experience and perceptions during the simulation.
2. Be honest and objective in your feedback.
3. Take your time and fill out each page carefully.
4. Review each page carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page**: This is the first page of the form. Please enter any relevant comments or feedback about your experience with the simulation.
* **Second Page**: This is the second page of the form. Please enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the simulation.
* **Third Page**: This page asks if you found the simulation to be an effective way to learn about medical skills. Please select "Yes" if you think it was effective, and "No" if you did not.
* **Fourth Page**: This page asks if you think the simulation was helpful in improving your medical skills. Please enter "Yes" if you think it was helpful, and "No" if you did not.
* **Fifth Page**: This page asks if you selected any skills that you think were not well simulated or needed improvement. Please select all applicable options from the list.
* **Sixth Page**: This page asks for the date you completed the simulation. Please enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Seventh Page**: This page asks for the time you spent on the simulation. Please enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Eighth Page**: This is a text field for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the simulation.
* **Ninth Page**: This is an email field where you can enter any contact information if you would like to receive updates or follow-up information about future simulations.
* **Tenth Page**: This page is for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the simulation.
* **Eleventh Page**: This page is for any additional comments or feedback about your experience with the simulation.
* **Twelfth Page**: This page is for any final comments or feedback about your experience with the simulation.
