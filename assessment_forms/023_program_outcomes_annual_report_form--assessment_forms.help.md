# Program Outcomes Annual Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for program administrators or managers to document and report on the progress of their programs for the given reporting period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter the name of the program in the **program_name** field. This is the title of the program for which you are reporting.
2.  Describe the achievement of the program outcomes in the **outcome_achievement** field. This field is for the summary of what the program has achieved or accomplished.
3.  In the **assessment_findings** field, provide your assessment of the program's progress and any findings. This could include both positive and negative aspects of the program.
4.  If there are any specific details or insights you'd like to share about your assessment, enter them in the **assessment_findings_description** field. This can be a good space to provide examples or context.
5.  Finally, enter the year for which you are reporting in the **year** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **program_name** (text, optional): Enter the title of the program for which you are reporting.
*   **outcome_achievement** (text, optional): Summarize what the program has achieved or accomplished in the reporting period.
*   **assessment_findings** (text, optional): Provide your assessment of the program's progress and any notable findings.
*   **assessment_findings_description** (text, optional): If you have specific details or insights to share about your assessment, enter them here.
*   **year** (number, optional): Enter the year for which you are reporting on the program's progress.

## Tips
*   Make sure to complete the form honestly and accurately to ensure the report reflects the true status of the program.
*   If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, contact your supervisor or the relevant department for assistance.
