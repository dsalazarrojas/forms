# Non Profit Volunteer Engagement Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the engagement and satisfaction of volunteers within a non-profit organization. It aims to help organizations understand areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the volunteer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Select all that apply from the given options.
3. Fill in any relevant text boxes with your thoughts and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Feedback**: This section asks about your overall feedback on your experience as a volunteer. Please share any positive or negative comments about your experience.
* **Volunteer Commitment**: This section asks about your level of commitment to the non-profit organization. Please select all that apply.
* **Program Satisfaction**: This section asks about your satisfaction with the non-profit program. Please select one option.
* **Volunteer Retention**: This section asks about your likelihood of continuing to volunteer with the organization. Please select all that apply.
* **Communication**: This section asks about the communication you receive from the organization. Please select one option.
* **Volunteer Recognition**: This section asks about whether you feel recognized for your volunteer work. Please select one option.
* **Inclusivity**: This section asks about how inclusive you feel the organization's environment is. Please select all that apply.
* **Support**: This section asks about the level of support you receive from the organization. Please select all that apply.
* **Program Participation**: This section asks about your participation in the non-profit program. Please select one option.
* **Volunteer Leadership**: This section asks about whether you feel empowered as a volunteer leader. Please select all that apply.
* **Volunteer Engagement**: This section asks about your level of engagement with the organization. Please select one option.
* **Program Satisfaction Level**: This section asks about your overall satisfaction with the program. Please select all that apply.
* **Volunteer Satisfaction Level**: This section asks about your overall satisfaction with your volunteer experience. Please select all that apply.
* **Feedback Frequency**: This section asks about how often you receive feedback from the organization. Please select one option.
* **Program Updates**: This section asks about how often you receive updates about the organization's programs. Please select all that apply.
* **Volunteer Updates**: This section asks about how often you receive updates about your volunteer tasks. Please select all that apply.
* **Feedback Channels**: This section asks about the channels you use to provide feedback. Please select one option.
* **Feedback Frequency Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to receive feedback on a regular basis. Please type your answer in the text box.
* **Feedback Channels Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to have multiple channels for providing feedback. Please type your answer in the text box.
* **Program Updates Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to receive regular program updates. Please type your answer in the text box.
* **Volunteer Updates Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to receive regular updates about your volunteer tasks. Please type your answer in the text box.
* **Volunteer Satisfaction Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to feel satisfied with your volunteer experience. Please type your answer in the text box.
* **Program Satisfaction Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to feel satisfied with the program. Please type your answer in the text box.
* **Volunteer Engagement Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to be engaged with the organization. Please type your answer in the text box.
* **Volunteer Retention Importance**: This section asks about how important it is for you to be retained as a volunteer. Please type your answer in the text box.
