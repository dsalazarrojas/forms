# Scouts COVID 19 Self Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from event organizers, parents or guardians, and scouts for COVID-19 self-assessment and contact tracing purposes. It helps to identify potential risks and ensure the safety of all attendees during the scouting event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the Event Organizer, Parent or Guardian, and Child.
2. Provide the name of the scout and any relevant symptoms start and end dates.
3. If applicable, provide the dates and details of any travel history.
4. Answer questions about contact with an infected person, including start and end dates of symptoms.
5. Add any additional information as needed.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, required): Enter the name and details of the event organizer.
* **Parent or Guardian** (`parent_or_guardian`, text, required): Enter the name and details of the parent or guardian.
* **Name of Child** (`name_of_child`, text, required): Enter the child's name.
* **Name of Scout** (`name_of_scout`, text, required): Enter the scout's name.
* **Scout Camp Date** (`scout_camp_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the scout camp.
* **Scout Location** (`scout_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the scout camp.
* **Symptoms Start Date** (`symptoms_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of any symptoms.
* **Symptoms End Date** (`symptoms_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of any symptoms.
* **Travel History Start Date** (`travel_history_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of any travel history.
* **Travel History End Date** (`travel_history_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of any travel history.
* **Contact with Infect Person** (`contact_with_infect_person`, text, required): Answer yes or no if there was contact with an infected person.
* **Contact with Infect Person Symptoms Start Date** (`contact_infect_person_symptoms_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of any symptoms after contact with an infected person.
* **Contact with Infect Person Symptoms End Date** (`contact_infect_person_symptoms_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of any symptoms after contact with an infected person.
* **Self-Assessment Questionnaire** (`self_assessment_questionnaire`, note, required): Add any additional information or comments.
* **Scout Camp Location Name** (`scout_camp_location_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Address** (`scout_camp_location_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location City** (`scout_camp_location_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Zip/Zip+4** (`scout_camp_location_zip_code`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Phone** (`scout_camp_location_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Email** (`scout_camp_location_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Latitude** (`scout_camp_location_latitude`, text, required): Enter the latitude of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Longitude** (`scout_camp_location_longitude`, text, required): Enter the longitude of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Elevation** (`scout_camp_location_elevation`, text, required): Enter the elevation of the scout camp location.
* **Scout Camp Location Altitude** (`scout_camp_location_altitude`, text, required): Enter the altitude of the scout camp location.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required): Add any additional information or comments.

Note: Required fields are marked as required in the explanation.
