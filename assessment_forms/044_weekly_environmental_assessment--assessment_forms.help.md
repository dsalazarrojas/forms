# Weekly Environmental Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect weekly assessments of environmental conditions at various sites. It is meant to be filled out by personnel who have been assigned to specific sites.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the site conditions and answer the questions accordingly.
2. Check the hazards that exist at the site, selecting all that apply.
3. Enter the site's sustainability metrics, if applicable.
4. Provide a brief description of the site.
5. Assess the environmental impact of the site, selecting the correct option.
6. Add any additional comments about the site.
7. Enter the email and phone contact information of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Site Conditions** (`site_conditions`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the site conditions, including any notable features or concerns.
* **Hazards** (`hazards`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify any hazards present at the site, selecting all that apply (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Sustainability Metrics** (`sustainability_metrics`, number, required: false): Enter any relevant sustainability metrics for the site, if applicable.
* **Site Description** (`site_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the site, including its location and any notable features.
* **Environmental Impact** (`environmental_impact`, select_one, required: false): Assess the environmental impact of the site, selecting the correct option (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any additional comments about the site.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* Be thorough when describing site conditions and hazards.
* If you are unsure about a field, please ask for guidance.
* This form is meant to be a regular and ongoing process, please submit it on a weekly basis.
