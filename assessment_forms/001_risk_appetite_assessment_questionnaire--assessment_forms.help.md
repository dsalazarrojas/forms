# Risk Appétit Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess various aspects of risk tolerance and financial goals to provide a comprehensive understanding of an individual's or organization's risk appetite.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, selecting the options that best represent your risk tolerance and financial goals.
3. Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions, especially when selecting options like "Yes" or "No".
