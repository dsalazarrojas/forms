# Email Routing Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Routing Assessment Survey is designed to assess your current email routing process, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations for enhancing your email management. This survey will guide you through a series of questions to help you evaluate your email routing process and provide insights for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your current email routing process on **page 2**.
2. Choose the options that apply to your email frequency on **page 4** and **page 16**.
3. Enter your satisfaction rating for email routing and sensitivity on **page 5**, **page 7**, and **page 17**.
4. List any additional comments or pain points on **page 6**, **page 12**, **page 20**, and **page 22**.
5. Select your improvement priorities on **page 9**, **page 13**, **page 15**, **page 18**, and **page 19**.
6. Finally, select the time spent on email routing on **page 7** and **page 21**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Routing Process**: On **page 2**, choose the options that best describe your current email routing process.
* **Email Frequency**: On **page 4** and **page 16**, select the options that apply to your email frequency.
* **Satisfaction**: On **page 5** and **page 17**, enter your satisfaction rating for email routing and sensitivity, respectively.
* **Additional Comments**: On **page 6**, **page 12**, **page 20**, and **page 22**, list any additional comments or pain points you have regarding email routing and sensitivity.
* **Improvement Priorities**: On **page 9**, **page 13**, **page 15**, **page 18**, and **page 19**, select your improvement priorities for email routing and sensitivity.
* **Time Spent**: On **page 7** and **page 21**, select the time spent on email routing and sensitivity, respectively.
