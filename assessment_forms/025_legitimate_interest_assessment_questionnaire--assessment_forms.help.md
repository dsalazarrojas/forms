# Legitimate Interest Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's legitimate interest in a particular opportunity or project, and is used to determine their eligibility and suitability for certain benefits or services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page and fill in the required fields.
2. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information, as this will help us process your assessment efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First name** (1, text, optional)
First name: Enter your given name.

### **Last name** (2, text, optional)
Last name: Enter your surname.

### **Email** (3, email, optional)
Email: Enter your email address.

### **Phone number** (4, text, optional)
Phone number: Enter your contact phone number.

### **Date of birth** (5, date, optional)
Date of birth: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### **Employment status** (6, select_one, optional)
Select your current employment status.

*   Employed
*   Self-employed
*   Unemployed
*   Student

### **Address** (7, note, optional)
Address: Enter any additional information about your address if necessary.

### **Nationality** (8, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply:

*   US Citizen
*   Non-US Citizen
*   International

### **Employment type** (9, select_one, optional)
Select your employment type.

*   Full-time
*   Part-time
*   Contract
*   Freelance

### **Employment length** (10, number, optional)
Enter the length of your current employment.

### **Date of hire** (11, date, optional)
Enter the date you were hired.

### **Employment history** (12, select_multiple, optional)
Select all that apply:

*   10 years
*   5 years
*   1-5 years
*   Less than 1 year
