# Signal Quality Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signal Quality Assessment Form is used to collect descriptive data from users about their experiences with signals. This form aims to gather information about signal quality, location and time of signal reception, signal performance, any issues encountered, and additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions thoroughly, but feel free to skip any that are not relevant to your experience.
2. Use the "Notes" field to add any additional comments or details that may be helpful in assessing the signal quality.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Signal Quality
* **Signal Quality** (1, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the signal's quality, e.g., clear, distorted, weak, or strong.

### Location and Time
* **Location and Time** (2, text, required/optional): Describe the location where you received the signal and the time of reception.

### Signal Performance
* **Signal Performance** (3, text, required/optional): Enter details about the signal's performance, e.g., signal strength, clarity, or frequency.

### Issue Capture
* **Issue Capture** (4, text, required/optional): If any issues were encountered while receiving the signal, describe them here.

### Notes
* **Notes** (5, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details that may be helpful in assessing the signal quality.
