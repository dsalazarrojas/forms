# Workshop Knowledge Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Knowledge Assessment Form is designed to gather feedback from workshop attendees about their experiences and perceptions of the workshop. This feedback will be used to improve future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the best option that reflects your feelings or opinions.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for any open-ended questions.
4. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **how_would_you_rate_your_knowledge_on_the_topic (1)** (`number`, optional): Rate your current knowledge level on the topic, on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **do_you_feel_more_confident_about_the_topic (2)** (`select_one`, optional): How confident do you feel about the topic? Select an option that best describes your feelings.
* **did_you_feel_the_workshop_was_engaging (3)** (`select_multiple`, optional): How engaging did you find the workshop? Select all options that apply.
* **how_long_was_the_workshop_too_long (4)** (`select_one`, optional): How long did you feel the workshop was? Select an option that best describes your experience.
* **do_you_recommend_this_workshop (5)** (`select_one`, optional): Do you recommend this workshop? Select an option that best describes your opinion.
* **what_did_you_find_most_helpful (6)** (`select_multiple`, required): What aspect of the workshop did you find most helpful? Select all applicable options.
* **what_did_you_find_most_difficult (7)** (`select_multiple`, required): What aspect of the workshop did you find most difficult? Select all applicable options.
* **do_you_have_any_other_comments (8)** (`text`, optional): Do you have any other comments or suggestions? Type them in the space provided.
* **email (9)** (`email`, optional): Your contact email address. Please enter a valid email address so that we can follow up with you.
* **phone (10)** (`text`, optional): Your contact phone number. Please enter a valid phone number so that we can follow up with you.
