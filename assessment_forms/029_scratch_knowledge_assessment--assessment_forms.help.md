# Scratch Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scratch Knowledge Assessment form is designed to evaluate a student's understanding of a specific subject. It is a self-assessment tool used to gauge student progress and identify areas where improvement is needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by carefully reading the instructions on the Intro Page.
2. On the Quiz Page, select one or multiple answers to the questions presented.
3. On the Feedback Page, provide feedback on your performance.
4. On the Project Page, evaluate your experience with the project.
5. On the Student Information and Teacher Information pages, enter your basic student and teacher details.
6. On the Score Page, select the level of your assessment (Excellent, Good, or Fair).
7. On the Submit Button page, confirm your submission.
8. Review your progress on the Review Page.
9. Add any additional comments on the Comments Page.
10. On the Project Experience page, indicate your level of experience with the project.
11. On the Confidence Level page, select your confidence level (High, Medium, or Low).
12. Finally, enter your name and surname on the Student Name and Student Surname pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Intro Page** (Intro Page, note, optional)
* This page introduces the assessment and provides instructions to the student.

### 2. **Quiz Page** (Quiz, select_multiple, required)
* Select one or multiple answers to the questions presented on this page.

### 3. **Feedback Page** (Feedback, note, optional)
* Provide feedback on your performance.

### 4. **Project Page** (Project, select_multiple, required)
* Evaluate your experience with the project.

### 5. **Student Name** (Student Name, text, optional)
* Enter your name.

### 6. **Student Surname** (Student Surname, text, optional)
* Enter your surname.

### 7. **Submit Page** (Submit, note, optional)
* Confirm your submission.

### 8. **Review Page** (Review, time, optional)
* Review your progress.

### 9. **Comments Page** (Comments, note, optional)
* Add any additional comments.

### 10. **Project Experience** (Project Experience, number, optional)
* Indicate your level of experience with the project.

### 11. **Confidence Level** (Confidence Level, select_one, required)
* Select your confidence level (High, Medium, or Low).

### 12. **Score Page** (Score, select_one, required)
* Select the level of your assessment (Excellent, Good, or Fair).

### 13. **Student Information** (Student Information, text, optional)
* Enter basic student information.

### 14. **Teacher Information** (Teacher Information, text, optional)
* Enter teacher information.

### 15. **Email** (Email, email, optional)
* Enter your email address.

### 16. **Score** (Score, select_one, required)
* Select your score (Excellent, Good, or Fair).

## Tips

* Please ensure you answer all required fields accurately.
* If you need help, review the provided instructions on each page.
* Double-check your submission before confirming it.
