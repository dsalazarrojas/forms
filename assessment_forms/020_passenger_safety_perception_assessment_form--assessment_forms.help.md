# Passenger Safety Perception Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from passengers on their safety experience during a ride or travel. It will help us understand their overall perception of safety and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. On Page 1, enter any additional comments you may have about your experience.
3. On Page 2, select your response to the question about your overall safety rating.
4. Select all that apply to describe your experience with the ride.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience.
* **Rider Experience** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the following options to describe your overall experience: 
  * Very Dissatisfied
  * Somewhat Dissatisfied
  * Neutral
  * Somewhat Satisfied
  * Very Satisfied
* **Overall Safety Rating** (`number`, optional): Enter a number between 0 and 100 to rate your overall safety experience.
* **Ride Safety** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply to describe your experience with the ride.
  * Safe
  * Somewhat Safe
  * Neither Safe nor Dangerous
  * Somewhat Dangerous
  * Dangerous
* **Page 6** (`select_one`, optional): Have you ever had any issues with the ride?
  * Yes
  * No
* **Additional Information** (`note`, optional): Please add any additional information about your experience.
* **Date** (`date`, optional): Enter the date of your experience.
* **Time** (`time`, optional): Enter the time of your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your experience.
