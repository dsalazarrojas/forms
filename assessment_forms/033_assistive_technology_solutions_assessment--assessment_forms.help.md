# Assistive Technology Solutions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's assistive technology needs, including referral date, demographic information, disability details, and technology usage habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing information about the individual referred for assessment.
2.  Ensure that you are providing detailed information about the person's needs and circumstances.
3.  If you are unsure about any section, please refer to the field explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, Date, required): The date the assessment request was made.
    *   Enter the date the individual was referred for assessment.
*   **Individual First Name** (`individual_first_name`, Text, required): The person needing assessment.
    *   Enter the individual's first name.
*   **Individual Last Name** (`individual_last_name`, Text, required): Last name.
    *   Enter the individual's last name.
*   **Individual ID Number** (`individual_id`, Text, optional): Medical record or ID.
    *   Enter any available medical record or ID number for the individual.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, required): Birth date.
    *   Enter the individual's birth date.
*   **Referring Agency or Professional** (`referring_agency`, Text, required): Who referred them?
    *   Enter the name of the referring agency or professional.
*   **Assessment Environment** (`assessment_environment`, Select One, required): Where do they need technology?
    *   Choose the location where the individual requires assistive technology support.
*   **Primary Disability or Condition** (`primary_disability`, Select One, required): Main condition needing support.
    *   Select the primary disability or condition the individual is experiencing.
*   **Details of Disability** (`disability_details`, Text, required): More specific information.
    *   Enter a detailed description of the individual's disability or condition.
*   **Functional Limitations** (`functional_limitations`, Text, required): What activities are limited?
    *   Enter the specific activities the individual is limited in due to their condition.
*   **Current Assistive Technology in Use** (`current_technology`, Text, optional): What AT do they use now?
    *   Enter any current assistive technology used by the individual.
*   **Effectiveness of Current Technology** (`technology_effectiveness`, Select One, required): How well does current tech work?
    *   Choose how well the current technology used by the individual meets their needs.
*   **Identified Technology Needs** (`technology_needs`, Select Multiple, required): What areas need AT support?
    *   Select all the areas that require assistive technology support.
*   **Mobility and Positioning Needs** (`mobility_needs`, Text, optional): Specific mobility requirements.
    *   Enter any specific mobility needs of the individual.
*   **Communication and Speech Needs** (`communication_needs`, Text, optional): Communication support needed?
    *   Enter any communication and speech needs of the individual.
*   **Computer and Digital Access Needs** (`computer_access_needs`, Text, optional): How do they access computers?
    *   Enter how the individual accesses computers.
*   **Independent Living Goals** (`independent_living_goals`, Text, required): What do they want to achieve?
    *   Enter the individual's goals for independent living.
*   **Work Capacity Assessment** (`work_capacity_assessment`, Text, optional): Employment potential and goals.
    *   Enter any information about employment potential and goals.
*   **Learning Style and Technology Comfort** (`learning_style`, Text, required): How do they learn best?
    *   Enter how the individual learns best in terms of technology usage.
*   **Budget Constraints** (`budget_constraints`, Text, optional): Funding available or limitations?
    *   Enter any budget constraints or limitations.
*   **Home Environment Evaluation** (`home_environment`, Text, optional): Details about living space.
    *   Enter any details about the individual's living space.
*   **Family and Support System** (`support_system`, Text, required): Who helps them?
    *   Enter the names of family members or support systems that help the individual.
*   **Additional Assessment Notes** (`assessment_notes`, Text, optional): Any other relevant information.
    *   Enter any other information that may be relevant to the assessment.
*   **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, Text, required): Who conducted this assessment?
    *   Enter the name of the assessor.
*   **Assessment Completion Date** (`assessment_completion_date`, Date, required): Date assessment was finished.
    *   Enter the date the assessment was completed.

**Tips**

*   Ensure that you are providing accurate and detailed information.
*   If unsure, please refer to the field explanations above for guidance.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrators.
