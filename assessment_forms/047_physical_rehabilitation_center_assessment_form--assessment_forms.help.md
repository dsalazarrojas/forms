# Physical Rehabilitation Center Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Rehabilitation Center Assessment Form is a comprehensive assessment tool used by the rehabilitation center staff to collect essential information about each patient.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please provide accurate and truthful information about yourself.
- Fill out the form according to the sections below.
- If you need help, do not hesitate to ask the staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: This section is for providing basic information about yourself, such as your name, contact details, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Medical History**: Please describe your medical history, including any previous treatments, allergies, or medical conditions that may affect your rehabilitation.
* **Physical Assessment**: Report any physical limitations or challenges you experience, such as mobility issues, pain levels, or other physical concerns.
* **Functional Status**: Describe your current functional abilities, such as daily activities, work capacity, or ability to perform daily living tasks.
* **Rehabilitation Plans**: Share your goals and expectations for your rehabilitation, including any specific objectives or outcomes you desire.
* **Patient Goals**: Identify your personal goals, such as regaining independence, improving mobility, or recovering from an injury.
* **Treatment Options**: List any specific treatment options or therapies you are interested in or have already tried.
* **Family History**: Provide information about your family medical history, including any genetic conditions, allergies, or medical concerns that may impact your rehabilitation.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any question, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- This form is for internal use only; your data will not be shared or distributed without your consent.
