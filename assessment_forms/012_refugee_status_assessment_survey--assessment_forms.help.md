# Refugee Status Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refugee Status Assessment Survey is a tool used to gather information about an individual's refugee status. It is designed to collect essential details that aid in the assessment and support process. Please ensure you have reviewed the survey's purpose to understand its relevance to your situation before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For select-type questions (e.g., gender, country of origin), choose the relevant option from the provided list.
3. For text or note fields (e.g., name, address, safety concerns), enter your information in the designated field.
4. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Refugee Status Assessment** (`refugeeStatusAssessment`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of the individual's refugee status. Please enter a concise statement that summarizes your current situation.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Arrival** (`date_arrival`, date, optional): Provide the date of your arrival in the country you are currently in.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select the gender that best identifies you (Male, Female, Non-Binary).
* **Country of Origin** (`country_of_origin`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the countries where you have a connection (e.g., birth country, country of citizenship).
* **Contact Number** (`contactNumber`, text, optional): If available, provide a contact number that can be used to reach you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address (if you have one).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your current address or the address you are currently using.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safetyConcerns`, note, optional): Describe any safety concerns or risks you are currently facing that may impact your well-being.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed voluntarily, and you are not obligated to provide any information that you do not feel comfortable sharing. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the support team for assistance.
