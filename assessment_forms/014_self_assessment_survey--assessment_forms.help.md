# self_assessment_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-assessment survey is a tool for users to evaluate their own performance in a specific assessment area. It helps users reflect on their progress and identify areas where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Assessment Area where you want to self-assess.
2. Enter your Self Assessment score out of 10.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments in the Notes field (optional).
4. Enter your Email (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input (1)
 * **User Input** (`text`, required: `false`): This is a text field where you can enter any information or comments about yourself. It is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have any additional comments.

### Assessment Area (2)
 * **Assessment Area** (`select_one`, required: `true`): Choose the assessment area where you are self-assessing. Select from the options: Area 1, Area 2, or Area 3.

### Self Assessment (3)
 * **Self Assessment** (`number`, required: `true`): Enter a score out of 10 for how you think you performed in the selected assessment area. For example, if you scored 8 out of 10.

### Notes (4)
 * **Notes** (`note`, required: `false`): This is a field where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your self-assessment. It is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have any notes.

### Email (5)
 * **Email** (`email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted about your self-assessment or any follow-up questions). This field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't want to be contacted.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct Assessment Area where you are self-assessing.
* Enter your Self Assessment score honestly and accurately, out of 10.
* Keep your notes clear and concise if you choose to provide them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
