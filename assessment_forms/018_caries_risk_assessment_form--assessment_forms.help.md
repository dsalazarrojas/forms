# Caries Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Caries Risk Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the risk of caries (cavities) in patients. This form helps dental professionals to assess the risk factors and provide personalized treatment plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please enter the patient's name and age in the respective fields.
2. Enter the date of the assessment.
3. Report any relevant medical history.
4. Answer the questions regarding dietary and hygiene habits, including frequency of sugary snacks and beverages, brushing and flossing frequency, use of fluoride toothpaste or mouthwash, and presence of orthodontic appliances or partial dentures.
5. Answer the clinical findings questions, including presence of new cavities, white spot lesions, visible plaque, dry mouth symptoms, and other relevant indicators.
6. Evaluate the overall caries risk level based on all indicators.
7. Identify the primary risk factors contributing to the patient's caries risk level.
8. Select the recommended recall interval for professional cleaning and exam.
9. Choose the prescribed preventive treatment interventions for the patient.
10. Add any additional clinician notes for specific instructions to the patient.
11. Sign the form with your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Age**: Enter the patient's current age.
* **Date of Assessment**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Relevant Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or medications that may affect the patient's oral health.
* **Frequency of Sugary Snacks or Beverages**: Select the frequency of sugary snacks or beverages consumed per day.
* **Brushing Frequency**: Select the frequency of brushing per day.
* **Flossing Frequency**: Select the frequency of flossing per day.
* **Fluoride Exposure**: Select whether the patient uses fluoride toothpaste or mouthwash.
* **Overall Caries Risk Level**: Select the overall caries risk level based on all indicators.
* **Primary Risk Factors**: Select all risk factors that contribute to the patient's caries risk level.
* **Recommended Recall Interval**: Select the recommended interval for professional cleaning and exam.
* **Prescribed Preventive Treatment**: Select the preventive treatment interventions for the patient.
* **Additional Clinician Notes**: Add any specific instructions for the patient.
* **Clinician Signature**: Type your full name to sign the form.

Please note that this form is intended to guide dental professionals in assessing caries risk and providing personalized treatment plans. Completing this form accurately and truthfully will help ensure optimal patient care.
