# Stage Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Progress Assessment is a form used to evaluate the progress of participants in a stage of a project or process. It helps assessors to provide a clear and objective evaluation of the participant's progress, which is crucial for making informed decisions and ensuring the participant meets the expected standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant assessment stage from the options below.
2. Evaluate the participant's progress in this stage, using the scale provided.
3. Provide any additional feedback you have for the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Initial Assessment** (`Stage Progress Assessment 1`, **Note**, Required):
    This is the first stage of the assessment, where you will evaluate the participant's progress.
* **Follow-up Assessment** (`Stage Progress Assessment 2`, **Select One**, Required):
    This field is used to re-evaluate the participant's progress after a period of time.
    Use the scale to select the level of progress you think the participant has made: Very Low, Low, Moderate, High, Very High.
* **Participant Progress** (`Participant Progress`, **Number**, Required):
    Rate the participant's progress on a scale.
* **Feedback** (`Feedback`, **Text**, Required):
    Provide any additional feedback you have for the participant. This could include suggestions for improvement or areas of concern.
