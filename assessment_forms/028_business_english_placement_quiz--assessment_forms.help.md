# Business English Placement Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business English Placement Quiz is a tool to assess your English language skills in a business context. It will help identify your strengths and weaknesses in grammar, vocabulary, listening, and speaking skills, providing you with a baseline assessment to inform your learning goals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant information (full name, email address, job title, and company name).
2. Answer each question according to your language skills and knowledge.
3. Indicate your years of experience speaking business English.
4. Answer the grammar and vocabulary questions.
5. Choose the correct sentence or option for each question.
6. Select all areas for improvement from the list.
7. Indicate your confidence level in presenting in English and your experience with giving business presentations.
8. Select all areas where you need improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Company Name** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **English Language Background** (`english_background`, select_one, required): Indicate if English is your native language or if you learned it as a second language.
* **Years Speaking Business English** (`years_speaking`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have spoken business English.
* **Grammar Question 1** (`grammar_question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the correct sentence that completes the sentence.
* **Grammar Question 2** (`grammar_question_2`, select_one, required): Choose the correct word or option for the sentence.
* **Grammar Question 3** (`grammar_question_3`, select_one, required): Choose the correct word or option for the sentence.
* **Vocabulary Question 1** (`vocab_question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the correct definition or option for the word.
* **Vocabulary Question 2** (`vocab_question_2`, select_one, required): Choose the correct definition or option for the word.
* **Listening Question 1** (`listening_question_1`, select_one, required): Choose the correct answer or option for the question.
* **Speaking Confidence** (`speaking_confidence`, select_one, required): Choose your level of confidence when presenting in English.
* **Presentation Experience** (`presentation_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate your experience with giving business presentations.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas where you need improvement.
* **My Confidence Presenting in English** (`speaking_confidence`, select_one, required): Choose your level of confidence when presenting in English.
