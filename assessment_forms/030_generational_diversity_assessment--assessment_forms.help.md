# Generational Diversity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generational Diversity Assessment is a tool to evaluate the diversity and inclusion climate within an organization. It consists of various sections that cover different aspects of diversity and inclusion, such as generational identity, communication style, inclusion practices, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the provided instructions and options carefully before starting.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your abilities.
3. For select multiple and select one options, choose the answer that best describes your current situation or practices.
4. For numerical fields, enter the actual numbers as prompted.
5. Complete all sections, even if you are unsure about some options.
6. Make sure to save your progress or submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Job Title** (4, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (5, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Years of Experience** (6, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working in your current role.
* **Generational Identity** (7, select_one, optional): Choose the generational identity that best describes you:
	+ **Boomers**: Born between 1946 and 1964.
	+ **Gen X**: Born between 1965 and 1980.
	+ **Gen Z**: Born between 1997 and 2003.
	+ **Millennials**: Born between 1981 and 1996.
* **Communication Style** (8, select_multiple, optional): Choose the communication style that best describes you:
	+ **Open**: Open and collaborative communication style.
	+ **Closed**: Closed and not open to feedback.
	+ **Collaborative**: Collaborative and open to feedback.
	+ **Avoidant**: Avoidant and not open to feedback.
* **Inclusion Practices** (9, select_multiple, optional): Check the inclusion practices you currently have in place:
	+ **High**: High inclusion practices.
	+ **Medium**: Medium inclusion practices.
	+ **Low**: Low inclusion practices.
* **Diversity and Inclusion Commitment** (10, select_multiple, optional): Check the level of diversity and inclusion commitment:
	+ **Strong**: Strong commitment to diversity and inclusion.
	+ **Somewhat**: Somewhat committed to diversity and inclusion.
	+ **Not at all**: Not committed to diversity and inclusion.
* **Manager Diversity Knowledge** (11, select_multiple, optional): Check the level of diversity knowledge of your manager:
	+ **High**: High diversity knowledge.
	+ **Somewhat**: Somewhat diversity knowledge.
	+ **Not at all**: Not diversity knowledge.
* **Team Diversity Knowledge** (12, select_multiple, optional): Check the level of diversity knowledge of your team:
	+ **High**: High diversity knowledge.
	+ **Somewhat**: Somewhat diversity knowledge.
	+ **Not at all**: Not diversity knowledge.
* **Workplace Climate** (13, select_multiple, optional): Describe the current workplace climate:
	+ **Open**: Open and inclusive climate.
	+ **Closed**: Closed and not inclusive.
	+ **Neutral**: Neutral climate.
* **Inclusion Strategies** (14, select_multiple, optional): Check the inclusion strategies you have in place:
	+ **True**: Have inclusion strategies.
	+ **False**: Do not have inclusion strategies.
* **Feedback** (15, select_multiple, optional): Describe your feedback culture:
	+ **Regular**: Regular feedback.
	+ **Ad-hoc**: Ad-hoc feedback.
* **Diversity and Inclusion Initiatives** (16, select_multiple, optional): Check the diversity and inclusion initiatives you have in place:
	+ **Active**: Active diversity and inclusion initiatives.
	+ **Passive**: Passive diversity and inclusion initiatives.
* **Manager Support** (17, select_multiple, optional): Check the level of support from your manager:
	+ **High**: High manager support.
	+ **Somewhat**: Somewhat manager support.
	+ **Not at all**: Not manager support.
* **Team Support** (18, select_multiple, optional): Check the level of support from your team:
	+ **High**: High team support.
	+ **Somewhat**: Somewhat team support.
	+ **Not at all**: Not team support.
* **Communication Channels** (19, select_multiple, optional): Describe the communication channels you use:
	+ **Email**: Email communication.
	+ **Phone**: Phone communication.
	+ **Video**: Video communication.
* **Diversity and Inclusion Challenges** (20, select_multiple, optional): Check the diversity and inclusion challenges you face:
	+ **True**: Face diversity and inclusion challenges.
	+ **False**: Do not face diversity and inclusion challenges.
* **Inclusion Action Plan** (21, select_multiple, optional): Check the level of inclusion action plan:
	+ **High**: High inclusion action plan.
	+ **Somewhat**: Somewhat inclusion action plan.
	+ **Not at all**: Not inclusion action plan.
* **Employee Resource Allocations** (22, select_multiple, optional): Check the employee resource allocations:
	+ **True**: Have employee resource allocations.
	+ **False**: Do not have employee resource allocations.
* **Inclusion Funds** (23, select_multiple, optional): Check the inclusion funds:
	+ **True**: Have inclusion funds.
	+ **False**: Do not have inclusion funds.
* **Workplace Diversity Score** (24, number, optional): Enter a score between 0 and 100 that describes your current workplace diversity climate.
* **Employee Diversity Score** (25, number, optional): Enter a score between 0 and 100 that describes your current employee diversity climate.

## Tips
* Answer honestly and to the best of your abilities.
* Take your time and review each question carefully.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your HR or diversity and inclusion team.
* This assessment is intended to be a self-assessment tool, not a mandatory requirement.
* The results will help you identify areas for improvement and provide insights for your organization's diversity and inclusion efforts.
