# Creative Design Risk Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Design Risk Assessment form is used to identify and assess potential risks associated with creative design projects. It gathers information about project details, design team composition, and risk factors to help project managers and team leaders make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the project category that best describes the project type.
2. List the members of the design team involved in the project.
3. Identify potential risk factors and score each factor based on its severity.
4. Enter the corresponding risk scores for each factor.
5. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (1)** (`project_details`, number): This field is used to enter a brief description or number that represents project details.
* **Project Type (3)** (`project_type`, select_one): This field asks you to select the category that best describes the project type, such as Risk Assessment, Project Management, Design, or Other.
* **Design Team (4)** (`design_team`, select_multiple): This field allows you to list the members of the design team involved in the project.
* **Risk Factors (5)** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple): This field presents a list of potential risk factors, and you should select "Yes" or "No" for each factor to indicate its presence.
* **Risk Factor Scores 1-10 (17-26)** (`risk_factor_scores_1-risk_factor_scores_10`, number): These fields are used to score each risk factor based on its severity, with higher scores indicating higher risks.
* **Risk Scores (25)** (`risk_scores`, number): This field is used to calculate the overall risk score based on the risk factor scores.
* **Risk Factors 1-10 (6-15)** (`risk_factors_1-risk_factors_10`, select_multiple): These fields ask you to select "Yes" or "No" for each risk factor to identify its presence in the project.
* **Risk Factor Scores 1-10 (17-20)** (`risk_factor_scores_1-risk_factor_scores_10`, number): These fields are used to score each risk factor based on its severity, with higher scores indicating higher risks.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully review each risk factor and score it accurately based on its potential impact on the project.
* Use the project details field to provide a brief description or number that represents project details.
* Select the project type that best describes the project category.
* Ensure that all design team members are listed and their roles are clear.
* Review and approve the form before submitting it for final review.
* Regularly review and update risk assessments to ensure they remain accurate and relevant.
* Use the risk scores to inform decision-making and risk mitigation strategies.
