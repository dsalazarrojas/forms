# Ocean Alkalinity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ocean Alkalinity Assessment Form is a tool for collecting and documenting ocean alkalinity data from sampling locations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose one of the sampling locations provided (Location A, Location B, or Location C).
2. Select the date of the ocean sampling (in the format mm/dd/yyyy or any other format supported by the date type field).
3. Record the time of the sampling (in the format hh:mm).
4. Measure and input the water temperature in degree Celsius or other supported unit.
5. Choose one of the provided salinity levels (Low, Medium, or High).
6. Select all applicable alkalinity levels from the options (Alkalinity 1, Alkalinity 2, or Alkalinity 3).
7. Add any additional comments or remarks regarding the sampling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location** (`page_1`, `select_one`, not required): Choose the location where the sampling occurred, such as Location A, Location B, or Location C.
* **Sampling Date** (`page_2`, `date`, not required): Provide the date of the sampling in the supported format, including month, day, and year.
* **Sampling Time** (`page_3`, `time`, not required): Record the time of the sampling, including hour and minute.
* **Water Temperature** (`page_5`, `number`, not required): Measure and input the water temperature.
* **Salinity** (`page_6`, `select_one`, not required): Select one of the provided salinity levels (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Alkalinity** (`page_7`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all applicable alkalinity levels (Alkalinity 1, Alkalinity 2, or Alkalinity 3).
* **Other Comments** (`page_8`, `note`, not required): Provide any additional comments or remarks about the sampling.

## Tips

- Make sure to choose one option for each field to ensure accurate data collection.
- If unsure about the salinity or alkalinity levels, refer to your field guidelines or protocols.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid errors in the data collected.
- If any field is not applicable, you may leave it blank or provide a brief explanation in the "Other Comments" field.
