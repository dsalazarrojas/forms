# nursing-assessment-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The <code>nursing-assessment-form</code> is designed to collect essential information from patients, healthcare providers, or caregivers. This form aims to streamline the assessment process, gathering crucial details such as patient info, vital signs, allergies, medications, medical history, physical conditions, and mental health. The data collected will help healthcare professionals understand the patient's overall well-being and provide targeted care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the <code>nursing-assessment-form</code> accurately and efficiently:

1. Review the form and its sections carefully.
2. Enter the patient's information in the designated fields.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for each field, especially for medical history and allergies.
4. Be thorough when reporting any allergies or medical conditions.
5. Ensure you answer all required questions truthfully and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Patient Info (page 1)
* **Patient Info** (<code>patient_info</code>, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, contact information, or any other relevant details.
* **Vital Signs** (<code>vital_signs</code>, text, optional): Report the patient's vital signs, such as blood pressure, temperature, or oxygen saturation level.
* **Allergies** (<code>Allergies</code>, text, optional): List any allergies, sensitivities, or adverse reactions to medications or substances.
* **Medications** (<code>medication</code>, text, optional): Record the patient's current medications, including dosage and frequency.
* **Medical History** (<code>medical_history</code>, text, optional): Provide a brief medical history, including significant medical events, surgeries, or conditions.
* **Physical Conditions** (<code>physical_conditions</code>, text, optional): Document any current or chronic physical conditions, such as mobility issues or chronic pain.
* **Mental Health** (<code>mental_health</code>, text, optional): Report any mental health concerns, such as anxiety or depression.
* **Medical-Social Service** (<code>medical_social_service</code>, text, optional): Discuss any relevant social services or support systems in place for the patient.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If unsure about any information, leave it blank and consult with medical staff.
- Remember to update the form as changes occur or new information becomes available.
- If this form is used for multiple patients, ensure each is filled out separately and accurately.
- Review and confirm information with patients before submitting.
- Keep this form for reference and record-keeping purposes.
