# Self Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect self-reported health data from individuals in a healthcare setting. Please ensure that the information collected will be used for its intended purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, please follow these steps:
    1.  Review the form carefully before starting to complete it.
    2.  Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your current health status.
    3.  Ensure that you have provided all required information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Self Health Assessment** (Self Health Assessment, note, optional): This is a text box where you can describe your current health status. Please provide a brief explanation of your health condition or any concerns you have.
*   **Health Data** (Health Data, text, optional): This is a text field where you can provide any relevant medical history or health information.
*   **Wellness Goal** (Wellness Goal, select multiple, optional): Please select all applicable wellness goals that are relevant to your current health status.
*   **Health Risk** (Health Risk, select one, optional): Please select the level of health risk you currently face.
*   **Self-Assessment** (Self-Assessment, date, optional): Please provide the date you completed this form for self-assessment.
*   **Assessment Date** (Assessment Date, date, optional): Please provide the date this form was assessed by a healthcare provider.
*   **Healthcare Provider** (Healthcare Provider, select one, optional): Please select your healthcare provider's professional designation (MD, DO, or NP).
*   **Contact Number** (Contact Number, number, optional): This is a field for entering a contact number, likely for follow-up purposes.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant healthcare provider or contact number provided.
*   Please keep in mind that this form is for self-reported health data, and accuracy is essential for proper evaluation.
