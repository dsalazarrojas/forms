# Interview Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a candidate's interview experience and to aid in the decision-making process for hiring or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's information, including their name, email, and any other relevant details.
2. Answer the interview questions as honestly and thoroughly as possible.
3. Provide feedback about the candidate's performance during the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`):
	+ A text field where you can fill in the candidate's name and other relevant details.
* **Interview Details** (`interview_details`):
	+ A text field where you can describe the interview, including the date, time, and location.
* **Candidate Questionnaire** (`candidate_questionnaire`):
	+ A text field where you can ask the candidate a series of questions and have them answer in detail.
* **Interviewer Feedback** (`interviewer_feedback`):
	+ A text field where you can provide feedback about the candidate's performance during the interview.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`):
	+ A text field where you can provide the candidate's contact information, such as email and phone number.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Do not hesitate to ask follow-up questions to clarify any answers or concerns.
* This form is meant to be used as a tool to aid in the decision-making process, not as a replacement for a thorough evaluation process.
