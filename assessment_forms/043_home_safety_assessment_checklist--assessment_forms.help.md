# Home Safety Assessment Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help ensure the safety and well-being of individuals in the workplace or home setting. It assesses the presence or absence of home safety features and emergency contact information. The purpose is to identify potential hazards and take corrective actions to maintain a safe environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant information for each page.
2. Fill in the text fields with your information or location details.
3. Ensure you have selected all required options and fields.
4. Review your completed form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Floor** (Select One):
  • This field helps identify the location of the home or workplace.
  • Select "Ground Floor" if your home or workplace is on the ground floor.
  • Select "Upper Floors" if your home or workplace is on the upper floors.
* **Emergency Contacts** (Select One):
  • This field asks about emergency contact information.
  • Select "John Doe", "Jane Doe", or "Jim Smith" from the list.
* **Home Address** (Text Field):
  • Enter your home address here.
  • Ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Date of Birth** (Date Field):
  • Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
  • Ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (Select One):
  • This field asks about your relationship to the emergency contact.
  • Select "Spouse", "Child", or "Parent" from the list.
* **Contact Phone** (Text Field):

* **Contact Email** (Email Field):
