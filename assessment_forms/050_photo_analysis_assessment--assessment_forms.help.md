# photo_analysis_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photo analysis assessment form is used to collect and store information about a photo analysis process. This form is used to gather details about the photo, including user information, image review, and analysis requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details in the "User Details" field.
2. Provide a brief review of the image in the "Image Review" field.
3. Enter any additional image details in the "Image Details" field.
4. Specify whether the analysis is required or not in the "Analysis" field.
5. If uploading a photo, use the "Photo Upload" field to add the file.
6. Alternatively, you can provide a URL to the photo in the "Photo URL" field.
7. Enter the date the photo was taken in the "Photo Date" field.
8. Enter the time the photo was taken in the "Photo Time" field.
9. Specify the location where the photo was taken in the "Photo Location" field.
10. Enter any additional details about the image size in the "Image Size" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: A field to enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Image Review**: A field to provide a brief review of the image being analyzed.
* **Image Details**: A field to enter any additional details about the image.
* **Analysis**: A field to specify whether the analysis is required or not.
* **Photo Upload**: A field to upload a photo file.
* **Photo URL**: A field to enter a URL to an online photo.
* **Photo Date**: A field to enter the date the photo was taken.
* **Photo Time**: A field to enter the time the photo was taken.
* **Photo Location**: A field to enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Image Size**: A field to enter any additional details about the image size.
