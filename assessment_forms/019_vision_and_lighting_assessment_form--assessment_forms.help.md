# vision_and_lighting_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the lighting conditions in a given area. It will help us understand the lighting conditions, fixture status, and any issues or concerns related to lighting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing a brief introduction to the area being assessed.
*   Describe the location where the assessment is taking place.
*   Choose the type of lighting conditions you see in the area (LED, CFL, Halogen, or Other).
*   Select the status of the fixtures in the area (Good, Fair, Poor).
*   Choose the lighting level in the area (Very Low, Low, Medium, High).
*   Select the color temperature of the light (Warm, Neutral, Cool).
*   Provide a description of any lighting issues or concerns.
*   Indicate the priority of the issue (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
*   Sign and date the form to indicate completion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, `false`):
    *   This field is for a brief introduction to the area being assessed. Please provide a short description of the area and the purpose of the assessment.
*   **Location** (`Location`, `text`, `false`):
    *   In this field, please describe the location where the assessment is taking place.
*   **Lighting Conditions** (`Lighting Conditions`, `text`, `false`):
    *   Please provide a description of the lighting conditions in the area.
*   **Lighting Type** (`Lighting Type`, `select_one`, `false`):
    *   This field allows you to select the type of lighting you see in the area (LED, CFL, Halogen, or Other).
*   **Fixture Status** (`Fixture Status`, `select_multiple`, `false`):
    *   This field gives you the option to select the status of the fixtures in the area (Good, Fair, Poor).
*   **Lighting Level** (`Lighting Level`, `select_one`, `false`):
    *   Please select the lighting level in the area (Very Low, Low, Medium, High).
*   **Color Temperature** (`Color Temperature`, `select_one`, `false`):
    *   Choose the color temperature of the light (Warm, Neutral, Cool).
*   **Issue Description** (`Issue Description`, `text`, `false`):
    *   If there are any lighting issues or concerns, please describe them in this field.
*   **Issue Priority** (`Issue Priority`, `select_one`, `false`):
    *   Indicate the priority of the issue (Critical, High, Medium, Low).
*   **Signature** (`Signature`, `date`, `false`):
    *   Sign and date the form to indicate completion.
