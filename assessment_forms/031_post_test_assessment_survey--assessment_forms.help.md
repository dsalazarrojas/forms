# Post Test Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Post Test Assessment Survey is a form designed to gather feedback from participants after a training session. This form helps us evaluate the effectiveness of the session, identify areas for improvement, and gather valuable insights for future program development. Please take a few minutes to complete this survey to help us better understand your experiences and thoughts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Select your responses for each question by choosing from the available options or entering a value in the text fields.
3. Ensure you have filled out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
4. Submit your responses by clicking the "Submit" button at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How was the training session?** (`post_test_assessment_survey`, select_one, required: false)
Select one option that best describes your experience with the training session.
	- Option 1: It was excellent, and I gained valuable knowledge.
	- Option 2: It was good, and I learned something new.
	- Option 3: It was average, and I did not gain much from it.
* **What were your participant experiences?** (`participant_experiences`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all the options that apply to your experience with the participant, such as:
	- Option 1: Engaging and interactive.
	- Option 2: Well-organized and structured.
	- Option 3: Difficult to follow and disorganized.
* **How can we improve future programs?** (`future_programs`, text, required: false)
Enter any suggestions or ideas you have for improving future programs, such as new topics, more hands-on activities, or additional resources.
* **How engaged did you feel during the session?** (`participant_engagement`, number, required: false)
Choose a number between 1 and 10 to indicate how engaged you felt during the session, with 1 being "not at all engaged" and 10 being "very engaged."
* **How would you evaluate this program?** (`program_evaluation`, number, required: false)
Choose a number between 1 and 10 to evaluate the program overall, with 1 being "very poor" and 10 being "excellent."
* **How would you evaluate this session?** (`session_evaluation`, date, required: false)
Please enter the date you would suggest to hold the session in the future, taking into account your schedule and preferences.
* **How long should this session be?** (`session_length`, time, required: false)
Choose a time duration, such as "30 minutes" or "2 hours", to suggest the ideal length for future sessions.
* **How long should this session be?** (Duplicate of `session_length`, note, required: false)
This field is a duplicate of the previous one, and you can disregard it.
* **Do you have any other feedback?** (`feedback`, text, required: false)
Please enter any additional comments or suggestions you have for us.
* **Email** (`email_address`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for further discussion or updates.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone for further discussion or updates.
