# Colonization Impact Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is intended to assist users in understanding the purpose and requirements of the Colonization Impact Assessment form. The form aims to collect data on the impact of colonization on communities, specifically in regards to cultural, economic, social, and health indicators.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the assessment date and geographic location of the impact.
2. Enter the name of the lead researcher or organization.
3. Select the correct impact categories and severity of cultural, economic, social, and health impacts.
4. Provide detailed descriptions of economic impact and resource extraction, disruption to social and political structures, and any ongoing mitigation or restorative efforts.
5. Assess the recovery and revitalization potential of affected communities.
6. Record any additional field observations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Researcher or Organization Name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the research team or organization conducting the assessment.
* **Assessment Date (Date, Required)**: Enter the date on which the assessment was conducted.
* **Geographic Location of Assessment (Text, Required)**: Enter the country, region, or territory where the assessment was conducted.
* **Primarily Affected Communities or Indigenous Groups (Text, Required)**: Enter the name(s) of the primarily affected community or indigenous group.
* **Impact Categories (Note, Required)**: Select all categories that apply to the assessment.
* **Cultural Impact Severity (Select One, Required)**: Choose the level of cultural impact, from Minimal to Critical.
* **Land and Territory Displacement Status (Select One, Required)**: Select the status of land and territory displacement, from No Displacement to Forced Relocation to Reservation.
* **Economic Impact and Resource Extraction (Text, Optional)**: Provide a detailed description of economic impact and resource extraction.
* **Disruption to Social and Political Structures (Text, Optional)**: Describe any breakdown of traditional governance systems.
* **Health and Well-being Indicators (Note, Optional)**: Select the health disparities indicator that applies to the assessment.
* **Evidence of Intergenerational Trauma (Select One, Required)**: Choose the level of intergenerational trauma, from Low to High.
* **Ongoing Mitigation or Restorative Efforts (Text, Optional)**: Describe any revitalization or reparations programs.
* **Recovery and Revitalization Potential (Select One, Optional)**: Select the recovery and revitalization potential of affected communities.
* **Additional Field Observations (Text, Optional)**: Record any additional observations about the assessment.
