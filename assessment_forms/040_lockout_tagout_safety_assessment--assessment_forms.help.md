# Lockout Tagout Safety Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document and track the safety procedures and status of equipment that needs to be locked out for maintenance or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the equipment that needs to be locked out.
2. Describe the lockout procedure that will be followed.
3. Check if there are any hazards associated with the equipment.
4. Determine if the equipment needs to be locked out due to lockout conditions.
5. Select the lockout device that will be used.
6. Enter the current lockout status of the equipment.
7. Enter the date and time the equipment was locked out.
8. Enter the name of the employee performing the lockout.
9. Enter the name of the supervisor or manager reviewing the lockout procedure.
10. Enter the date and time the lockout procedure was reviewed.
11. Select if the lockout procedure was reviewed by the employee.
12. Select if the equipment has been properly closed out.
13. Enter any comments or remarks about the lockout.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lockout tagout procedure**: Describe the specific steps that will be taken to lock out the equipment safely.
* **Procedure Description**: Enter a brief description of the procedure that was followed.
* **Equipment**: Select all the equipment that needs to be locked out.
* **Hazards**: Check if there are any hazards associated with the equipment.
* **Lockout Conditions**: Describe the conditions that require the equipment to be locked out.
* **Lockout Device**: Enter the specific device used to lock out the equipment.
* **Lockout/Tagout Status**: Select the current status of the lockout (Active or Inactive).
* **Date**: Enter the date the equipment was locked out.
* **Time**: Enter the time the equipment was locked out.
* **Employee**: Enter the name of the employee performing the lockout.
* **Supervisor**: Enter the name of the supervisor or manager reviewing the lockout procedure.
* **Procedure Review By**: Enter the date and time the lockout procedure was reviewed.
* **Procedure Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the procedure.
* **Lockout/Tagout Closure**: Select if the equipment has been properly closed out.
* **Lockout/Tagout Closure Status**: Select the status of the closure (Active or Inactive).
* **Remarks**: Enter any comments or remarks about the lockout.
* **Review By**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewer**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Email**: Enter the email of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Verified**: Select if the reviewer was verified.
* **Lockout/Tagout Status**: Select the status of the lockout (Active or Inactive).
* **Closure**: Select if the equipment has been properly closed out.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or remarks about the lockout.
