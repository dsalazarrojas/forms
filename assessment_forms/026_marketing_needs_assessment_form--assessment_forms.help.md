## Purpose
The Marketing Needs Assessment Form is used to gather information about the current marketing position, channels, budget, expected and actual outcomes, and recommended actions for marketing campaigns. This form helps in identifying marketing opportunities and strategies that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the form fields with the required information.
