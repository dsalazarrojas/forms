# self_report_measures_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect self-reported data from users on a set of specific topics or domains.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each section carefully and select the options that apply to you.
2. For sections where you can select multiple options, choose as many as apply.
3. For sections where you can select only one option, choose the option that best describes your experience or behavior.
4. Make sure to fill out every section completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background and Identifying Info** (`background_and_identifying_info`, text, required): This section is meant to gather some basic background information and identifying details about you. Please provide a brief description of your background and any identifying characteristics that might be relevant to the assessment.
* **Demographics** (`demographics`, number, required): This section asks about your demographic details. Please answer with numbers as per the options provided.
* **Self Report Measures Section 1** (`self_report_measures_section_1`, select_multiple, required): This section consists of multiple-choice questions. Choose as many options as apply to you from the list provided.
* **Self Report Measures Section 2** (`self_report_measures_section_2`, select_one, required): This section is similar to Section 1 but consists of single-choice questions. Choose the option that best describes your experience or behavior.
* **Self Report Measures Section 3-21** (`self_report_measures_section_3-21`, select_multiple/one, required/optional): These sections 3-21 consist of multiple-choice questions similar to Sections 1 and 2. Choose as many options as apply or select the best option for your experience or behavior.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_one, required): This is the final section. Please make sure to submit your form after reading this guide carefully.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need help or clarification, contact us for assistance.
