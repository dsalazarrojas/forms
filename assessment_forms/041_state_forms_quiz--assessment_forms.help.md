# State Forms Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The State Forms Quiz is a form used to assess users' knowledge and understanding of a particular subject matter. It is not a required form and users can choose to complete it at their discretion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the State Forms Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the intro_page section, which is a general introduction to the form.
2. Proceed to the question_page section, where you will be presented with a multiple-choice question.
3. Select the answer that best matches your thoughts, feelings, or experiences related to the question.
4. Once you have completed the question_page section, click "Submit" to view your results on the results_page section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Intro Page**: 
	+ *Label*: intro_page
	+ *Type*: text
	+ *Required*: False
	+ This is the first section of the form where you can type in any information you want to provide as a brief introduction to your answers.
* **Question**:
	+ *Label*: question_page
	+ *Type*: select_one
	+ *Required*: False
	+ This is where you will be presented with a multiple-choice question. Choose the answer that best matches your thoughts, feelings, or experiences related to the question.
* **Results**:
	+ *Label*: results_page
	+ *Type*: note
	+ *Required*: False
	+ This is where you will see your results after completing the question_page section.

## Tips
- Make sure to read each question carefully before selecting an answer.
- Choose the answer that best reflects your true thoughts, feelings, or experiences.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask for help.
