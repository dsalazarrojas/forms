# Training Program Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Program Audit Form is used to assess the effectiveness and relevance of a training program. It helps us evaluate how well the program met its objectives, whether it was useful for the participants, and if it led to continuous improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. For each question or field, choose the most accurate response from the provided options or enter a numerical value.
2. Answer each question truthfully, as they will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of the program.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, which are marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. How Effective (Program Effectiveness)
* **How Effective** (`program_effectiveness`, number, required: false): This field asks you to rate the overall effectiveness of the training program. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5, where 1 is not effective at all and 5 is very effective.

### 2. Relevance
* **Relevance** (`program_relevance`, select_multiple, required: false): This field asks you to indicate whether the training program was relevant to your work. Please select all the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 3. Participant Satisfaction
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to rate your satisfaction with the training program. Please select the option that best describes your feelings:
	+ Satisfied
	+ Dissatisfied

### 4. Continuous Improvement
* **Continuous Improvement** (`continuous_improvement`, note, required: false): This field is a text area where you can provide any suggestions or comments on how the training program could be improved in the future.

Tips:

* Please answer honestly and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any suggestions or comments, use the "Continuous Improvement" field to provide them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for help.
