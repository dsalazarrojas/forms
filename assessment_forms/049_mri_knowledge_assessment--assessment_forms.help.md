# MRI Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The MRI Knowledge Assessment is designed to evaluate your knowledge and understanding of MRI procedures, safety risks, and other essential aspects of MRI operations. This form helps to ensure that you have a solid foundation in MRI-related knowledge and skills, which is crucial for providing quality patient care and maintaining a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select the correct answers based on your knowledge and experience.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") and provide any additional comments or information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Knowledge** (`general_knowledge`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your overall knowledge and understanding of MRI procedures. Please provide a brief description of your knowledge in this field.
* **Safety Risks** (`safety_risks`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to assess your awareness of safety risks associated with MRI operations. Select all the risks that you are familiar with.
* **Equipment Handling** (`equipment_handling`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to evaluate your ability to handle MRI equipment. Please select the correct answer based on your experience and knowledge.
* **Emergency Procedures** (`emergency_procedures`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is used to test your understanding of emergency procedures in an MRI environment. Select all the procedures that you are familiar with.
* **Radiation Safety** (`radiation_safety`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to assess your knowledge of radiation safety. Please select the correct answer based on your understanding.
* **Patient Concerns** (`patient_concerns`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture any patient concerns or issues that you have encountered in an MRI environment. Please provide a brief description of your experience.
* **Case Study** (`case_study`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to test your understanding of case studies related to MRI operations. Select all the cases that you are familiar with.
* **Staff Education** (`staff_education`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture any staff education or training that you have received related to MRI operations. Please provide a brief description of your education.
* **Staff Education 2** (`staff_education_2`, `text`, optional): This field is used to capture any additional staff education or training that you have received related to MRI operations. Please provide a brief description of your education.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to test your understanding of training programs related to MRI operations. Select all the programs that you are familiar with.
* **Training Program 2** (`training_program_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to test your understanding of additional training programs related to MRI operations. Select all the programs that you are familiar with.
* **Assessment Results** (`assessment_results`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to capture your understanding of assessment results related to MRI operations. Please select the correct answer based on your knowledge.
* **Assessment Results 2** (`assessment_results_2`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to capture your understanding of additional assessment results related to MRI operations. Please select the correct answer based on your knowledge.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): This field is used to provide any additional comments or information that you think would be helpful to understand your knowledge and experience with MRI operations. Please fill in this field with a brief description of your thoughts.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields carefully and honestly.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your supervisor or trainer for guidance.
