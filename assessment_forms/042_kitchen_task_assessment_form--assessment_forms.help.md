# Kitchen Task Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a person's skills and experience in a kitchen setting. It's intended for employees who have been working in the kitchen and is used to gather feedback on their performance. The assessment will help managers identify areas where the employee excels and where they need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for further communication.
4. Indicate whether you were assigned to a specific task or not.
5. Enter the date and time of the assessment.
6. Enter any comments or feedback about your performance.
7. Choose the skills you possess under the following categories:
	* Kitchen Tasks
	* Kitchen Equipment
	* Culinary Skills
	* Food Safety
	* Food Handling
	* Knife Skills
	* Teamwork
	* Cleanliness
	* Food Styling
	* Knife Care

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select one, optional): Indicate whether you were assigned to a specific task or not.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about your performance.
* **Kitchen Tasks** (`kitchen_tasks`, select multiple, optional): Choose the kitchen tasks you possess.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have the skill or not.
* **Kitchen Equipment** (`kitchen_equipment`, select multiple, optional): Choose the kitchen equipment you are familiar with.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have experience with the equipment or not.
* **Culinary Skills** (`culinary_skills`, select multiple, optional): Choose the culinary skills you possess.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have the skill or not.
* **Food Safety** (`food_safety`, select multiple, optional): Choose the food safety practices you follow.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you follow the practice or not.
* **Food Handling** (`food_handling`, select multiple, optional): Choose the food handling skills you possess.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have the skill or not.
* **Knife Skills** (`knife_skills`, select multiple, optional): Choose the knife handling skills you possess.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have the skill or not.
* **Teamwork** (`teamwork`, select multiple, optional): Choose the teamwork skills you possess.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have the skill or not.
* **Cleanliness** (`kitchen_cleanliness`, select multiple, optional): Choose the cleanliness practices you follow in the kitchen.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you follow the practice or not.
* **Food Styling** (`food_styling`, select multiple, optional): Choose the food styling skills you possess.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have the skill or not.
* **Knife Care** (`knife_care`, select multiple, optional): Choose the knife care practices you follow.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you follow the practice or not.
