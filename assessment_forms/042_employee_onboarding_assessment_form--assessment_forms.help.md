# Employee Onboarding Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Onboarding Assessment Form is a comprehensive tool used to track the onboarding progress of newly hired employees, providing a centralised platform for collecting data on various aspects of their onboarding journey.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

- Review the form sections to understand what information is requested.
- Select the correct options for each field, as specified below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Onboarding Details (ID 1)**: Please enter a brief description of the employee's onboarding details, such as their job title, department, and role.
* **New Hire Manager (ID 2)**: Select 'Yes' if the new hire has a manager, 'No' otherwise.
* **Onboarding Process (ID 3)**: Select all processes the new hire is currently undergoing or has completed.
* **Date Started Onboarding (ID 4)**: Enter the date the new hire started onboarding, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Date Completed Onboarding (ID 5)**: Enter the date the new hire completed onboarding, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Feedback (ID 6)**: Select 'Yes' if you want to provide feedback, 'No' otherwise.
* **New Hire Survey (ID 7)**: Select all surveys the new hire has completed.
* **New Hire Survey Answer (ID 8)**: Please enter the employee's answer to the new hire survey.
* **Employee Feedback (ID 9)**: Please provide any feedback or comments about the new hire.
* **Date Last Contacted (ID 10)**: Enter the date you last contacted the new hire, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Last Contact Method (ID 11)**: Please select the method used to contact the new hire.
* **Last Contact Notes (ID 12)**: Provide any notes on the last contact with the new hire.
* **Date Next Step (ID 13)**: Enter the date of the next step in the onboarding process, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Next Step (ID 14)**: Select the next step in the onboarding process.
* **Next Step Reason (ID 15)**: Please enter the reason for the next step.
* **Notes (ID 16)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the new hire.
* **Onboarding Complete Status (ID 17)**: Select the status of the new hire's onboarding as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Onboarding Complete Date (ID 18)**: Enter the date the new hire completed onboarding, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **New Hire Manager (ID 19)**: Please enter the new hire manager's name.
* **New Hire Manager Email (ID 20)**: Enter the new hire manager's email address.
* **New Hire Manager Phone (ID 21)**: Enter the new hire manager's phone number.
* **New Hire Manager Job Title (ID 22)**: Please enter the new hire manager's job title.
* **New Hire Manager Department (ID 23)**: Enter the department of the new hire manager.
* **New Hire Manager Location (ID 24)**: Enter the location of the new hire manager.
* **Date Last Onboarding Survey (ID 25)**: Enter the date the last onboarding survey was completed, in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Completed Onboarding Surveys (ID 26)**: Select all completed onboarding surveys. 

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct options for each field to avoid incomplete or incorrect data.
* Ensure that dates entered are in the correct format.
* Complete as many fields as applicable to provide a comprehensive picture of the new hire's onboarding journey.
