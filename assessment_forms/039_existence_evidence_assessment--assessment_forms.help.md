# Existence Evidence Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Existence Evidence Assessment form is used to gather information about the existence of entities. This form helps users to document and verify the presence of entities in a specific location or context. It is essential for assessing the accuracy and completeness of the information provided. The form serves as a tool to collect and review data, which can be used for various purposes such as research, analysis, or decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the relevant entity type from the provided options in the "Entity Type" field.
2.  Enter the location where the entity was found in the "Location" field.
3.  Provide a brief description of the entity in the "Description" field.
4.  Rate the entity's existence in the "Rating" field.
5.  Select the category for the entity in the "Category" field.
6.  Enter the date when the evidence was found or observed in the "Evidence Date" field.
7.  Enter the name of the reviewer who assessed the entity in the "Review Name" field.
8.  Select the type of evidence being assessed in the "Evidence Type" field.
9.  Choose the status of the evidence in the "Evidence Status" field.
10.  Enter the date when the review was performed in the "Review Date" field.
11.  Select the time when the review was performed in the "Review Time" field.
12.  Enter any additional notes about the review in the "Review Notes" field.
13.  Rate the reviewer's overall satisfaction with the review in the "Reviewer Rating" field.
14.  Enter the unique identifier for the entity in the "Entity ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Entity Type** (`entity_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of entity that was assessed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the entity was found.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the entity.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate the entity's existence on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Select a category for the entity.
* **Evidence Date** (`evidence_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the evidence was found or observed.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the reviewer who assessed the entity.
* **Evidence Type** (`evidence_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of evidence being assessed.
* **Evidence Status** (`evidence_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the evidence.
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the review was performed.
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, optional): Select the time when the review was performed.
* **Review Notes** (`review_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the review.
* **Reviewer Rating** (`reviewer_rating`, number, optional): Rate the reviewer's overall satisfaction with the review.
* **Entity ID** (`entity_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier for the entity.

Note: The fields with a "required" flag are mandatory and must be filled in by the user. The optional fields can be left blank if not applicable.
