# Educational Unit Understanding Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and assessment from teachers and students on the effectiveness of an educational unit. It aims to gather information on the student's understanding of the unit, their feedback, and the teacher's feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the form name**: This is a text field where you can provide the name of the educational unit being assessed.
2. **Enter student ID**: This is a text field where you can enter the student's ID number or any other identifying information.
3. **Enter lesson ID**: This is a text field where you can enter the lesson ID number or any other identifying information.
4. **Enter unit topic**: This is a text field where you can provide the topic of the unit being assessed.
5. **Enter teacher name**: This is a text field where you can enter your name as the teacher.
6. **Enter student feedback**: This is a text field where the student can provide their feedback on the unit.
7. **Enter lesson length**: This is a text field where you can enter the length of the lesson.
8. **Enter teacher feedback**: This is a text field where the teacher can provide their feedback on the unit.
9. **Enter date given**: This is a date field where you can enter the date the lesson was given.
10. **Enter time given**: This is a time field where you can enter the time the lesson was given.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the educational unit being assessed.
* **student_id** (text, optional): Enter the student's ID number or any other identifying information.
* **lesson_id** (text, optional): Enter the lesson ID number or any other identifying information.
* **unit_topic** (text, optional): Enter the topic of the unit being assessed.
* **teacher_name** (text, optional): Enter your name as the teacher.
* **student_feedback** (text, optional): Provide feedback from the student on the unit.
* **lesson_length** (text, optional): Enter the length of the lesson.
* **teacher_feedback** (text, optional): Provide feedback from the teacher on the unit.
* **date_given** (date, optional): Enter the date the lesson was given.
* **time_given** (time, optional): Enter the time the lesson was given.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field.
- If you are a teacher, ensure to provide constructive feedback that is helpful to the student.
- If you are a student, ensure to provide honest and specific feedback on your understanding of the unit.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
