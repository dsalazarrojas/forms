# Curriculum Outcome Review Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Curriculum Outcome Review Report Form is designed to collect data and insights on student performance and progress over multiple terms or semesters. This form is intended for educators, instructors, or program administrators to evaluate and assess the effectiveness of a curriculum or educational program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each term or semester listed in the form, one by one.
2. For each term or semester, fill out the corresponding fields with the requested information.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and relevant data for each field.
4. Complete all required fields, as indicated by the required label next to each field.
5. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
