# Digital Assessment Analytics Collection Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Assessment Analytics Collection Consent Form is designed to collect participant information for the purpose of analytics collection. The form requires participants to provide their consent to collect and analyze their assessment data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Participant Information section with the required details.
2.  Provide your contact details in the Contact Details section.
3.  Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4.  Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format "mm/dd/yy".
5.  Choose your consent option in the Consent field.
6.  Add any additional comments in the Additional Comments field (optional).
7.  Enter your Phone number (optional).
8.  Enter any other Participant IDs (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (1)**: Enter your name and other identifying details.
* **Contact Details (2)**: Provide your contact information for any follow-up communication.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your Email address.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: Enter your Date of Birth in the format "mm/dd/yy".
* **Consent (5)**: Choose to consent or not to collect and analyze your assessment data.
	+ **Consent to Collection**: Agree to the collection and analysis of your assessment data.
	+ **Do Not Consent to Collection**: Refuse the collection and analysis of your assessment data.
* **Additional Comments (6)**: Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Phone (7)**: Enter your Phone number (optional).
* **Participant ID (8-10)**: Enter your Participant ID (optional, multiple fields are provided for different IDs).
* **Time of Day (11-14)**: Enter the time of day in the format "HH:MM:SS" (optional).
* **Time of Day 2-7 (15-17)**: Enter the time of day in the format "HH:MM:SS" (optional, multiple fields are provided for different times).

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Digital Assessment Analytics Collection Consent Form accurately and ensure that your data is collected and analyzed correctly.
