# Salm Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Salm Knowledge Assessment form is used to collect and document user knowledge, skills, and experiences in a structured and standardized way. This form helps assess the user's understanding and capabilities in various areas.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please review the form carefully and complete it accurately.
2. Enter your information in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3. For select fields, choose the most accurate options from the provided list.
4. If a field is not applicable or not relevant, please leave it blank or provide a suitable explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the user's input or response.
* **Assessment Questions** (`assessment_questions`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the assessment questions that apply to the user's situation. Select all relevant options.
* **Selected Answers** (`selected_answers`, select_one, optional): Choose the answer that best reflects the user's response to the selected assessment questions.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the assessment or evaluation.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter the user's email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter the user's phone number.
* **Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter the user's name.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution or organization related to the user.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user's department or role.
* **Program** (`program`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user's program or course.
* **Level** (`user_level`, text, optional): Enter the user's level or grade.
* **Role** (`user_role`, text, optional): Enter the user's role or position.
* **Result** (`assessment_result`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the result or outcome of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Score** (`score`, number, optional): Enter the score or rating of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of assessment or evaluation.
* **Assessment Category** (`assessment_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category of the assessment or evaluation.
* **Assessment Tags** (`assessment_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional tags or keywords related to the assessment or evaluation.
* **User Feedback** (`user_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments from the user.
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the user.
* **Rating** (`user_rating`, select_one, optional): Choose the user's rating or evaluation of the assessment or experience.
* **Status** (`user_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the user's status or position.
* **User Tags** (`user_tags`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional tags or keywords related to the user.
* **Notes** (`user_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the user.

## Tips

* Please review and double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
* If you are unsure of any information, please seek clarification or assistance.
* This form is designed to be completed carefully and thoughtfully. If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
