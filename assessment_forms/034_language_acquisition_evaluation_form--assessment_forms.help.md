# Language Acquisition Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess an individual's language acquisition level and progress. It gathers information about the user, their language skills, and the evaluation process. The goal is to evaluate the user's language acquisition level, identify areas for improvement, and provide recommendations for further development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the required fields**: Please answer the questions marked as "required" in the form.
2. **Enter your name and email**: In fields 1 and 2, enter your name and email address.
3. **Provide school year information (if applicable)**: If you are a student, enter the year you are currently attending school in field 4.
4. **Select your language level**: In field 5, choose your current language level from the options provided.
5. **Assess your progress**: In field 6, select the levels that describe your progress in language acquisition.
6. **Provide placement recommendation (optional)**: In field 7, enter any comments or suggestions for placement in language classes.
7. **Enter evaluation comments (optional)**: In field 8, write any comments or suggestions about the evaluation process.
8. **Set the assessment date and time**: In fields 9 and 10, enter the date and time of the assessment.
9. **Set the assessment time zone**: In field 11, select your time zone.
10. **Enter language acquisition level**: In field 12, enter your overall language acquisition level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the evaluation results.
* **User email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further evaluation or feedback.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): If you are a student, enter your parent's name for parental consent and involvement in the evaluation process.
* **School year** (`school_year`, time, optional): If you are a student, enter the year you are currently attending school.
* **Language level** (`language_level`, select_one, required): Choose your current level of language acquisition:
	+ Level 1: Beginner
	+ Level 2: Intermediate
	+ Level 3: Advanced
* **Progress level** (`progress_level`, select_multiple, required): Select all the levels that describe your progress in language acquisition:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Proficient
* **Placement recommendation** (`placement_recommendation`, text, optional): Enter any comments or suggestions for placement in language classes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Write any comments or suggestions about the evaluation process.
* **Assessment date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Assessment time zone** (`assessment_time_zone`, select_one, required): Select your time zone:
	+ UTC
	+ EST
	+ CST
	+ PST
* **Language acquisition level** (`language_acquisition_level`, text, optional): Enter your overall language acquisition level.
