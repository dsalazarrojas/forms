# Creative Direction Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Creative Direction Self Assessment form is designed to evaluate an individual's creative direction and goals, providing a comprehensive understanding of their objectives and priorities. This assessment will help in identifying strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement, enabling better decision-making and resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select the relevant sections**: Choose the sections that align with your creative direction and goals.
2. **Fill out each field**: Complete all required and optional fields as instructed.
3. **Review and update**: Review your responses for accuracy and update them if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you want to assess? (first_page_fields)** (`first_page`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to specify the aspects of your creative direction you want to assess. Select all that apply.
* **What is your current creative direction? (creative_direction)** (`creative_direction`, `text`, required: false): Describe your current creative direction, focusing on your overall vision and goals.
* **What are your goals for the next quarter? (goals)** (`goals`, `text`, required: false): Outline your objectives for the next quarter, including specific targets and outcomes.
* **Goals for the next quarter (goals_for_the_next_quarter)** (`goals_for_the_next_quarter`, `text`, required: false): Define your specific goals for the next quarter, breaking down larger objectives into smaller, actionable tasks.
* **Next Quarter (next_quarter)** (`next_quarter`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the quarter you are planning to focus on in your creative direction.
* **Goals for Next Quarter (next_quarter_goals)** (`next_quarter_goals`, `text`, required: false): Outline your specific goals for the chosen quarter, including targets and outcomes.
* **Feedback (feedback)** (`feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share any feedback or insights about your current creative direction and goals.
* **Next Quarter Feedback (next_quarter_feedback)** (`next_quarter_feedback`, `text`, required: false): Share feedback or insights about your goals for the next quarter.
* **Creative Directional Skills (creative_directional_skills)** (`creative_directional_skills`, `text`, required: false): Describe your creative skills and abilities, including strengths and areas for improvement.
* **Next Quarter Creative Directional Skills (next_quarter_creative_directional_skills)** (`next_quarter_creative_directional_skills`, `text`, required: false): Outline your planned skills and abilities for the next quarter.
* **Next Quarter Creative (next_quarter_creative)** (`next_quarter_creative`, `text`, required: false): Describe your creative direction and vision for the next quarter.
* **Quarter (quarter)** (`quarter`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current quarter of the year.
* **Next Quarter Quarter (next_quarter_quarter)** (`next_quarter_quarter`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the quarter you are planning to focus on for your creative direction.
* **Next Quarter Date (next_quarter_date)** (`next_quarter_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date for the next quarter.
* **Current Quarter Quarter (current_quarter_quarter)** (`current_quarter_quarter`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current quarter of the year.
* **Next Quarter Time (next_quarter_time)** (`next_quarter_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the planned time for the next quarter.
* **Current Quarter Time (current_quarter_time)** (`current_quarter_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the current time for the quarter.
* **Next Quarter Rating (next_quarter_rating)** (`next_quarter_rating`, `number`, required: false): Rate your progress in the next quarter, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Next Quarter Rating Scale (next_quarter_rating_scale)** (`next_quarter_rating_scale`, `text`, required: false): Describe the rating scale you used for your progress.
* **Next Quarter Agenda (next_quarter_agenda)** (`next_quarter_agenda`, `text`, required: false): Outline your agenda for the next quarter.
* **Next Quarter Agenda Item (next_quarter_agenda_item)** (`next_quarter_agenda_item`, `text`, required: false): Describe each item on your agenda.
* **Next Quarter Agenda Item Description (next_quarter_agenda_item_description)** (`next_quarter_agenda_item_description`, `text`, required: false): Provide a detailed description of each agenda item.

## Tips

* Be thorough and honest when answering each field.
* Review and update your responses as needed.
* Use clear and concise language when describing your creative direction and goals.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
