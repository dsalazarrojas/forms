# Ligature Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ligature Risk Assessment Form is designed to collect data on the risk level and other relevant details of a ligature used in medical procedures. This form helps to assess the risks associated with a ligature and ensure that patients receive proper care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Provide the patient's contact information in the "Patient Contact Info" field.
3. Select the type of ligature used from the "Ligature Type" field.
4. Describe the patient's status regarding the ligature in the "Patient Status" field.
5. Select the risk level (High or Low) in the "Risk Level" field.
6. Describe the risk associated with the ligature in the "Risk Description" field.
7. Select all relevant prevention measures from the "Prevention Measures" field.
8. Enter the date of the review in the "Review Date" field.
9. Select the review time in the "Review Time" field.
10. Enter the name of the reviewer in the "Reviewed By" field.
11. Provide any additional reviewer notes in the "Reviewer Notes" field.
12. Describe the prevention measures comments in the "Prevention Measures Comments" field.
13. Enter the status of the prevention measures in the "Prevention Measures Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Please enter the name of the patient being assessed.
* **Patient Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number.
* **Ligature Type**: Select the type of ligature used in the medical procedure.
* **Patient Status**: Describe the patient's status regarding the ligature, such as whether they are at risk of complications or not.
* **Risk Level**: Select the risk level associated with the ligature (High or Low).
* **Risk Description**: Briefly describe the risk associated with the ligature.
* **Prevention Measures**: Select all relevant prevention measures that can be taken to mitigate the risk.
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the review.
* **Review Time**: Select the review time.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Notes**: Provide any additional notes from the reviewer.
* **Prevention Measures Comments**: Describe any comments related to the prevention measures.
* **Prevention Measures Status**: Enter the status of the prevention measures.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
* This form is for updating or adding new information to the patient's medical records. If the patient's ligature information is already documented, please do not fill out this form.
