# Telemetry Technician Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from telemetry technicians to assess their qualifications and experience in the medical field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Provide your Phone Number.
3. Select your Years of Experience.
4. Choose your Skills (select multiple).
5. Indicate your Shift Readiness (select one).
6. Enter your Emergency Contact Information (optional).
7. Select your Work Location (select one).
8. Choose your Education Background (select one).
9. Select your Medical License Status (select multiple).
10. Enter your Certification and Issuer (if applicable).
11. Enter your License Number (if applicable).
12. Select your State (select one).
13. Select your Issuer State (select one).
14. Enter your Certification Number (required).
15. Enter your Other Work Experience (optional).
16. Select your Medical Technical Skills (select multiple).
17. Enter your Other Work Experience (optional).
18. Select your Medical Technical Skills 2 (select multiple).
19. Enter your Secondary Emergency Contact (optional).
20. Select your Medical Technical Skills 3 (select multiple).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on any medical records related to you.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on any medical records related to you.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Years of Experience**: Select how many years of experience you have as a telemetry technician.
* **Skills**: Choose multiple skills you possess as a telemetry technician (e.g., Communication skills, Leadership skills, etc.).
* **Shift Readiness**: Indicate how ready you are to work shifts on short notice.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter your emergency contact's name and relationship to you.
* **Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Work Location**: Select where you would like to work (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, etc.).
* **Education Background**: Select your highest educational background (e.g., High School, College, etc.).
* **Medical License**: Select the status of your medical license (if applicable).
* **Certification**: Enter your certification name and number (if applicable).
* **Issuer**: Enter your certification issuer's name (if applicable).
* **License Number**: Enter your medical license number (if applicable).
* **State**: Select the state where you are licensed (e.g., Alabama, California, etc.).
* **Issuer State**: Select the state where your certification or license was issued (if applicable).
* **Number**: Enter your certification or license number.
* **Other Work Experience**: Enter any other work experience you have as a telemetry technician.
* **Medical Technical Skills**: Choose multiple medical technical skills you possess (e.g., ECG interpretation, etc.).
* **Other Work Experience**: Enter any other work experience you have as a telemetry technician.
* **Medical Technical Skills 2**: Choose multiple medical technical skills you possess.
* **Secondary Emergency Contact**: Enter your secondary emergency contact's name and relationship to you.
* **Medical Technical Skills 3**: Choose multiple medical technical skills you possess.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us using the email address provided.
* This form is intended to be a helpful tool to assess your qualifications and experience as a telemetry technician. Please take a few minutes to carefully complete the form accurately.
* If you have any other work experience that is not listed, please use the "Other Work Experience" field to describe it in detail.
* If you have any other medical technical skills that are not listed, please use the "Medical Technical Skills" field to select them.
