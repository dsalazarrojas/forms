# Wealth Preservation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wealth Preservation Assessment is a form designed to help you understand your financial goals, risk tolerance, and investment preferences. This assessment is a valuable tool for creating a comprehensive picture of your wealth preservation needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions thoroughly and honestly to ensure accurate results.
2. Use the provided options for select fields, or enter your custom answer if no options are available.
3. Review your answers for completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Goals**
* **Client Goals**: `client_goals` (text, required: false)
	+ A brief description of your primary investment goals and objectives.
### 2. **Risk Tolerance**
* **Risk Tolerance**: `risk_tolerance` (number, required: false)
	+ Your comfort level with risk, measured on a scale of 0-100. Higher values indicate a higher risk tolerance.
### 3. **Asset Details**
* **Asset Details**: `asset_details` (text, required: false)
	+ A brief description of your current financial assets, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
### 4. **Investment Goals**
* **Investment Goals**: `investment_goals` (select_one, required: false)
	+ Select one of the following options:
		+ Value A
		+ Value B
		+ Value C
### 5. **Financial Objects**
* **Financial Objects**: `financial_objects` (select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select all applicable financial objects, such as stocks, bonds, or real estate.
	+ Options:
		+ Option 1
		+ Option 2
		+ Option 3
### 6. **Risk Level**
* **Risk Level**: `risk_level` (number, required: false)
	+ Your current risk level, measured on a scale of 0-100. Higher values indicate a higher risk level.
### 7. **Investment Horizon**
* **Investment Horizon**: `investment_horizon` (date, required: false)
	+ The expected duration of your investment plan, e.g., 1 year, 5 years, or 10 years.
### 8. **Investment Frequency**
* **Investment Frequency**: `investment_frequency` (time, required: false)
	+ The frequency at which you plan to invest, e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually.
### 9. **Wealth Objectives**
* **Wealth Objectives**: `wealth_objectives` (note, required: false)
	+ A brief description of your long-term wealth objectives, such as retirement or wealth transfer.
### 10. **Investment Risk**
* **Investment Risk**: `investment_risk` (number, required: false)
	+ Your risk tolerance for investments, measured on a scale of 0-100. Higher values indicate a higher risk tolerance.
### 11. **Investment Return**
* **Investment Return**: `investment_return` (number, required: false)
	+ Your expected investment return, measured on a scale of 0-100. Higher values indicate a higher expected return.

## Tips
- Please answer each question honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate results.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the provided options or consult with a financial advisor.
- Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.
