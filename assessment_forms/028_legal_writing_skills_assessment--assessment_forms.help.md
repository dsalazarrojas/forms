# Legal Writing Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to help you self-assess your legal writing skills and provide feedback on the quality of your submitted documents. This assessment is an opportunity for you to reflect on your writing abilities and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions in the form honestly and accurately based on your self-assessment of your writing skills.
2. For each section, select the corresponding answer from the provided options or upload a sample document.
3. Make sure to provide feedback on the quality of each submitted document based on your assessment.
4. If you have uploaded additional files, make sure to provide feedback on their quality as well.
