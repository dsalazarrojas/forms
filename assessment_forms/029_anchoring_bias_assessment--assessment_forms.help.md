# Anchoring Bias Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the anchoring bias, a cognitive bias that occurs when people's judgments are influenced by a reference point or anchor. The form aims to collect numerical responses from participants to understand how they estimate values or quantities after being presented with an initial anchor. This can help researchers and analysts to study and mitigate the effects of anchoring bias in decision-making processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the assessment instructions on the first page.
2. Enter your Participant ID (a code for anonymous tracking).
3. Enter the Initial Anchor (a numeric value provided in the scenario).
4. Estimate the value or quantity after the anchor.
5. Provide a brief reasoning for your estimate.
6. If you have demographic information (e.g., age, gender), you can optionally provide it in the Demographic Notes field.
7. Select whether you consent to use your data for research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Assessment Instructions**
*   **Assessment Instructions** (`-- Assessment Instructions --`, `note`, required: false)

    This page explains the assessment scenario and the task you are about to perform. Read it carefully before proceeding.

### 2. **Participant ID**
*   **Participant ID** (`Participant ID`, `text`, required: false)

    Enter a code for anonymous tracking. This will help us to identify your responses but keep them private.

### 3. **Initial Anchor**
*   **Initial Anchor** (`Initial anchor presented`, `number`, required: true)

    Enter the numeric anchor provided in the scenario.

### 4. **Participant estimate after anchor**
*   **Participant estimate after anchor** (`Participant estimate after anchor`, `number`, required: true)

    Estimate the value or quantity after the anchor.

### 5. **Brief reasoning for estimate**
*   **Brief reasoning for estimate** (`Brief reasoning for estimate`, `text`, required: false)

    Provide a short explanation for your estimate.

### 6. **Confidence level**
*   **Confidence level** (`Confidence level`, `select_one`, required: false)

    Select a confidence level from 1 to 5 for your estimate.

### 7. **Demographic notes**
*   **Demographic notes** (`Demographic notes`, `text`, required: false)

    Enter any demographic information you'd like to provide for analysis.

### 8. **Consent**
*   **Consent** (`Consent to use data for research`, `select_one`, required: true)

    Select "Yes" to agree to use your data for research purposes.

### Tips

*   Take your time to carefully estimate the value or quantity after the anchor.
*   Provide a clear and concise reasoning for your estimate.
*   Select a confidence level that reflects your confidence in your estimate.
*   If you have demographic information, feel free to provide it for analysis.
*   Read the consent carefully and select "Yes" to agree to use your data for research.
