# Competency Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess an employee's competencies and identify areas of strength and improvement. It helps assessors evaluate an employee's technical skills, quality of work, and behavioral competencies, and provide recommendations for development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name and the name of the person conducting the assessment.
2. Select the date of the assessment.
3. Evaluate the employee's technical skills, quality of work, and behavioral competencies using the ratings provided.
4. Identify the employee's key strengths and areas for development.
5. Provide recommendations for training or actions that can help the employee improve.
6. Select the overall assessment result based on the evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1.  Employee Name (required)

* Employee Name: This field is for entering the name of the person being assessed.
* Hint: Enter the employee's name as it appears on their identification documents.

### 2. Assessor Name (required)

* Assessor Name: This field is for entering the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* Hint: Enter your name and position or title.

### 3. Date of Assessment (required)

* Date of Assessment: This field is for entering the date the evaluation was completed.
* Hint: Use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### 4. Technical Knowledge and Skills (required)

* Technical Knowledge and Skills: This field is for evaluating the employee's proficiency in job-specific tools and processes.
* Hint: Select one of the following ratings:
	+ 1 - Beginner: Has little to no experience with the tool or process.
	+ 2 - Developing: Has some experience but requires guidance.
	+ 3 - Proficient: Has a good understanding and can perform tasks with minimal supervision.
	+ 4 - Advanced: Has a deep understanding and can perform tasks with confidence.
	+ 5 - Expert: Has exceptional knowledge and can mentor others.

### 5. Quality and Accuracy of Work (required)

* Quality and Accuracy of Work: This field is for evaluating the employee's attention to detail and error rates.
* Hint: Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Exceeds Standards: Consistently delivers high-quality work.
	+ Meets Standards: Meets expectations and follows guidelines.
	+ Needs Improvement: Needs to improve attention to detail.
	+ Unsatisfactory: Does not meet expectations.

### 6. Communication Skills (required)

* Communication Skills: This field is for evaluating the employee's verbal, written, and interpersonal interaction.
* Hint: Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Excellent: Communicates effectively and clearly.
	+ Effective: Communicates effectively but sometimes struggles with clarity.
	+ Developing: Struggles to communicate effectively.
	+ Poor: Struggles to communicate at all.

### 7. Problem Solving and Decision Making (required)

* Problem Solving and Decision Making: This field is for evaluating the employee's ability to analyze situations and find solutions.
* Hint: Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Highly Proactive: Actively looks for solutions.
	+ Capable: Can analyze situations and find solutions.
	+ Reactive: Struggles to find solutions.
	+ Struggles to decide: Struggles to analyze situations.

### 8. Teamwork and Collaboration (required)

* Teamwork and Collaboration: This field is for evaluating the employee's ability to work with others towards a common goal.
* Hint: Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Exceptional Team Player: Consistently demonstrates exceptional teamwork.
	+ Collaborative: Works collaboratively with others.
	+ Independent Worker: Prefers to work independently.
	+ Difficult to work with: Struggles to work with others.

### 9. Key Strengths (required)

* Key Strengths: This field is for identifying areas where the employee excels.
* Hint: Enter specific strengths or skills.

### 10. Areas for Development (required)

* Areas for Development: This field is for identifying specific skills or behaviors that the employee needs to improve.
* Hint: Enter specific areas for improvement or development.

### 11. Recommended Training or Actions (optional)

* Recommended Training or Actions: This field is for suggesting specific courses, mentoring, or project assignments that can help the employee improve.
* Hint: Enter specific recommendations.

### 12. Overall Assessment Result (required)

* Overall Assessment Result: This field is for selecting the final summary rating.
* Hint: Select one of the following ratings:
	+ Exceptional: Demonstrates exceptional performance.
	+ Successful: Meets expectations.
	+ Partially Successful: Meets some expectations.
	+ Not Successful: Does not meet expectations.
