# Financial Behavior Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate your financial behavior, habits, and attitudes towards money management. It will help you identify areas of improvement, develop a better understanding of your financial habits, gain insight into your financial progress, and develop a plan to improve your financial well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly.
2. Choose the options that best describe your financial behavior.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by the *Required* label).
4. If you're unsure about any question, try to make an informed guess based on your best understanding.
5. Once you've completed the form, review your answers to identify areas that need improvement and consider seeking professional financial advice if needed.

## Tips

* This form is a self-assessment tool. Be honest with yourself, and take the time to reflect on your financial habits and goals.
* This form is not a substitute for professional financial advice. If you're unsure about any question, consider seeking guidance from a financial expert.
* Review your answers carefully and use this assessment as a basis for your financial planning and decision-making.
* Keep this form as a reference for future financial reviews and progress tracking.
