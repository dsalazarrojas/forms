# SCBA Safety Assessment Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The SCBA Safety Assessment Survey is designed to assess the safety of Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA) equipment used in various settings. This form helps ensure that the equipment is in good working condition, meets safety standards, and is properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read each section carefully, and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2.  Enter your assessor's details, including their name and signature.
3.  For each field, enter the relevant information about the equipment being assessed.
4.  Answer "yes" or "no" to indicate whether the equipment has undergone inspections, and select the inspection date and time if applicable.
5.  Provide a brief description of the equipment, its current status, and the date and time of the last maintenance or service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Equipment Readiness** (`equipment_readiness`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the equipment's current state, including any issues or concerns.
*   **Assessor Details** (`assessor_details`, text, not required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the assessor.
*   **Inspections** (`inspections`, select_multiple, not required): Check "yes" if the equipment has undergone inspections, and select the dates and times of these inspections.
*   **SCBA Unit** (`scba_unit`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the SCBA unit, including its type or model.
*   **Equipment Description** (`equipment_description`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of the equipment, including any distinctive features or characteristics.
*   **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the person completing this form.
*   **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`, text, not required): Enter your signature, which will be used to confirm your assessment.
*   **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, text, not required): Enter the current status of the equipment, including any maintenance or service history.
*   **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the inspection, if one has been conducted.
*   **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the inspection, if one has been conducted.
*   **Equipment Last Service** (`equipment_last_service`, text, not required): Enter the date and time of the last maintenance or service.

**Tips**

*   This form should be completed accurately and honestly, taking into account all the information available.
*   Ensure that you have access to the equipment and can provide accurate information about its condition.
*   If you are unsure about any field or information, please contact your supervisor or seek guidance from a relevant expert.
*   This guide is intended to assist you in completing the form. If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Ensure to save or print this guide for future reference, should you need to complete this form again.
