# Automotive Troubleshooting Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess the diagnostic skills of automotive professionals, specifically mechanics, technicians, or enthusiasts, by asking them to identify the most likely causes of common automotive issues.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the participant information section, which includes:
	* Name: Enter your full name.
	* Email Address: Enter your work email address.
	* Employee ID (optional): If you have an employee ID, enter it.
2. Go through the troubleshooting questions section, and for each question:
	* Read the description carefully.
	* Select the most likely cause from the provided options.
3. After completing the quiz, provide feedback on your experience:
	* Confidence in troubleshooting abilities.
	* Areas where you need more training.
	* Difficulty level of the quiz.
	* Approximate time spent.
	* Feedback on quiz content.
4. Finally, indicate if you need additional training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Participant Information --**
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name to help us understand who you are.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your work email address so we can contact you with your results.
	+ **Employee ID (optional)**: If you have an employee ID, enter it for reference purposes.
* **Question 1 - Engine Will Not Start**
	+ This question tests your ability to identify the first diagnostic step when an engine won't start.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Check for fuel, Check battery voltage, Check spark plugs, Replace starter.
* **Question 2 - Check Engine Light Is On**
	+ This question assesses your understanding of the first action to take when an engine light is on.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Replace the oxygen sensor, Read the diagnostic trouble codes, Clear the fault codes, Replace the engine.
* **Question 3 - Brake Pedal Is Soft**
	+ This question gauges your ability to identify the likely cause of a soft brake pedal.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Air in brake lines, Worn brake pads, Weak springs, Low coolant.
* **Question 4 - Vehicle Overheating**
	+ This question evaluates your first step in diagnosing a vehicle overheating.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Coolant level, Fan belt tension, Radiator cap, Engine thermostat.
* **Question 5 - Strange Noise from Engine**
	+ This question assesses your understanding of potential causes for a knocking sound from the engine.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Worn bearings, Low oil level, Worn timing belt, Clogged air filter.
* **Question 6 - Transmission Slipping**
	+ This question evaluates your understanding of the most common cause of transmission slipping.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Low transmission fluid, Worn transmission fluid, Failed solenoid, Mechanical wear.
* **Question 7 - Power Steering Not Working**
	+ This question gauges your first diagnostic step when power steering is not working.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Check belt condition, Check fluid level, Check pump pressure, Replace pump.
* **Question 8 - Battery Draining Quickly**
	+ This question assesses your understanding of what to test when a battery drains quickly.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Alternator output, Battery terminals, Parasitic draw, Cable resistance.
* **Question 9 - Vibration at High Speed**
	+ This question evaluates your understanding of a common cause of vibration at high speed.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Unbalanced wheels, Worn suspension, Engine misfire, Low tire pressure.
* **Question 10 - No Air Conditioning Cooling**
	+ This question gauges your first check when air conditioning is not cooling.
	+ Select the most likely cause from: Refrigerant level, Compressor operation, Fan motor, Expansion valve.
* **Confidence in Troubleshooting Abilities**
	+ Rate your confidence level in your troubleshooting skills from: Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, Not Confident.
* **Areas Where You Need More Training**
	+ Briefly describe areas where you feel you need more training or practice.
* **Quiz Difficulty Level**

* **Approximate Time Spent**

* **Feedback on Quiz Content**
	+ Provide feedback on the quiz content and structure.
