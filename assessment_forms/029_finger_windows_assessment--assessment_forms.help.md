# Finger Windows Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Finger Windows Assessment is a form used to evaluate a participant's performance and skills, including their motor and cognitive abilities. This form is typically used to assess an individual's finger dexterity, coordination, and other related skills. The assessment results are used to identify areas for improvement and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. Fill in your answers according to the type of question (text or numerical input).
3. Make sure to provide detailed information for the text fields.
4. If you have any additional comments or notes, use the appropriate text fields.
5. Review your answers carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_performance** (`participant_performance`, text, optional): This field is used to record the participant's performance during the assessment. Please provide a brief description of their actions and any notable events that occurred during the assessment.
* **evaluator_notes** (`evaluator_notes`, note, optional): This field is for the evaluator to record any additional comments or notes about the participant's performance.
* **participant_comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): This field is for the participant to provide any comments or feedback about their experience during the assessment.
* **motor_skills** (`motor_skills`, text, optional): This field is used to record the participant's motor skills, such as hand-eye coordination, finger dexterity, and other related abilities.
* **cognitive_skills** (`cognitive_skills`, text, optional): This field is used to record the participant's cognitive skills, such as attention, memory, and problem-solving abilities.
* **participant_info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional information about the participant, such as their name, age, or any other relevant details.
* **participant_performance_score** (`participant_performance_score`, number, optional): This field is used to record the participant's overall performance score.
* **motor_sensitivity** (`motor_sensitivity`, number, optional): This field is used to record the participant's motor sensitivity.
* **motor_speed** (`motor_speed`, number, optional): This field is used to record the participant's motor speed.
* **motor_distance** (`motor_distance`, number, optional): This field is used to record the participant's motor distance.

## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the evaluator for clarification.
* This form is for your use only, please do not share it with others without permission.
