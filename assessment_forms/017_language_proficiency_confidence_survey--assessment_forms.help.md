# Language Proficiency Confidence Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gauge your self-assessment of your language proficiency levels, skills, and areas that need improvement.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Select your responses using the provided options.
3. Ensure all required questions are completed before submitting the form.
4. Be honest and accurate in your assessments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`intro`, `text`, optional): A brief introduction that explains the purpose and importance of this assessment.
* **What are your language abilities?** (`lang_abilities`, `text`, optional): Describe your overall language abilities, including reading, writing, speaking, and listening skills.
* **How well do you feel about your language skills?** (`lang_skills_report`, `text`, optional): Evaluate your current language skills in a specific area, such as grammar, vocabulary, or pronunciation.
* **What do you feel confident about your language level?** (`confidence_levels`, `text`, optional): Specify the aspects of language proficiency where you feel confident, such as reading comprehension or writing skills.
* **Which skills do you feel confident in?** (`lang_skills_assessment`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas where you feel confident in your language skills, using the provided options.
* **Which skills do you feel need improvement?** (`lang_skills_improvement`, `select_multiple`, required): Identify the areas where you feel you need improvement in language skills.
* **What skills do you feel are difficult for you?** (`lang_skills_difficult`, `select_multiple`, optional): Specify the areas of language skills that you find challenging.
* **What areas do you feel need improvement?** (`lang_skills_improvement_areas`, `select_multiple`, optional): Describe the specific areas of language skills that you feel need improvement.
## Tips
* Answer honestly and accurately, as this assessment will help identify areas of improvement.
* Ensure you select all required questions before submitting the form.
* Review your responses before submitting to ensure accuracy.
