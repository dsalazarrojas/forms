# Life Insurance Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Insurance Risk Assessment Form is used to collect information that helps our underwriters determine the risk associated with insuring an individual. This form is a critical tool in the underwriting process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided correct and relevant details.
3. Do not duplicate information; each form should only be filled out once per client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address, including street address and apartment or suite number, if applicable.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter your zip code (e.g., 12345).
* **Income** (`income`, number, optional): Enter your current annual income.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, text, optional): Describe your occupation in a few words (e.g., Software Engineer, Teacher).
* **Risk Level** (`risk_level`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following risk levels:
	+ Low
	+ Medium
	+ High
* **Insurance Purpose** (`insurance_purpose`, text, optional): Explain why you are seeking life insurance (e.g., to cover funeral expenses, to protect beneficiaries).
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about your family's medical history or any existing conditions that may impact your risk assessment.

## Tips
* Please ensure accuracy and completeness when filling out this form to avoid delays in the underwriting process.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, please contact our underwriter for assistance.
* Duplicate submissions may lead to incorrect or incomplete assessments. Only submit this form once per client.
