# fruit_juice_quality_assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The fruit_juice_quality_assessment form is used to record the quality assessment of a fruit juice sample. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the fruit juice name in the "fruit_juice_name" field.
2. Select the color of the fruit juice from the "color" options.
3. Enter a numerical value for the aroma, texture, and taste of the fruit juice using the "aroma", "texture", and "taste" fields respectively.
4. Select the appearance of the fruit juice from the "appearance" options.
5. Enter any comments you may have in the "comments" field.
6. Enter the date and time the fruit juice sample was collected using the "fruit_juice_date" and "fruit_juice_time" fields.
7. Enter the email and phone number of the person submitting the form.
8. Select "Submitted" from the "submitted" options to mark the form as submitted.
9. Enter a rating for the fruit juice if desired using the "fruit_juice_rating" field.
10. Enter any additional comments if desired in the "comments_2" field.
11. Finally, select "Submitted" from the "submitted" options and enter the name, email, and phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **fruit_juice_name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the fruit juice sample being assessed.
* **color (Select One, Optional)**: Select the color of the fruit juice from the options provided (Apple, Banana, Orange).
* **aroma (Number, Optional)**: Enter a numerical value to describe the aroma of the fruit juice, with higher values indicating better aroma.
* **taste (Number, Optional)**: Enter a numerical value to describe the taste of the fruit juice, with higher values indicating better taste.
* **appearance (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the appearance of the fruit juice from the options provided (Clean, Cloudy, Dirty).
* **texture (Text, Optional)**: Enter a description of the texture of the fruit juice.
* **consistency (Select One, Required)**: Select the consistency of the fruit juice from the options provided (Firm, Soft).
* **quality (Text, Optional)**: Enter any comments regarding the quality of the fruit juice.
* **comments (Text, Optional)**: Enter any comments regarding the fruit juice.
* **fruit_juice_date (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date the fruit juice sample was collected.
* **fruit_juice_time (Time, Optional)**: Enter the time the fruit juice sample was collected.
* **fruit_juice_email (Email, Optional)**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **fruit_juice_phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **email_sent (Text, Optional)**: This field is not intended for user input.
* **phone_sent (Text, Optional)**: This field is not intended for user input.
* **submitted_by (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted_at (Date, Required)**: Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **comments (Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments.
* **submited_by_email (Email, Required)**: Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **submited_by_phone (Text, Required)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **submited_by_name (Text, Required)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted (Select One, Required)**: Select "Submitted" to mark the form as submitted.
* **fruit_juice_rating (Number, Optional)**: Enter a rating for the fruit juice if desired.
* **comments_2 (Text, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments.
