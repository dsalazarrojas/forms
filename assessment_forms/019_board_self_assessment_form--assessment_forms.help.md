# Board Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Self Assessment Form is a tool for board members to evaluate their own performance and effectiveness as a governing body. This form helps to identify areas of strength and weakness, and to prioritize areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please select the correct answers for each field, taking into consideration your experiences and observations as a board member.
2. Please answer the questions honestly and accurately, based on your own perceptions and experiences.
3. If you're unsure about any of the answers, you can skip or leave them blank, but please make sure to indicate so in the "Board Strengths" and "Board Weaknesses" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent Name** (Page 1, Field 1)
	+ Your name (optional for anonymous completion).
* **Your Role** (Page 1, Field 2)
	+ Select the role you hold on the board.
* **Assessment Date** (Page 1, Field 3)
	+ The date on which you are completing this assessment.
* **Years of Service** (Page 1, Field 4)
	+ The length of time you have served on the board.
* **Board Composition** (Page 2, Field 5)
	+ Rate the diversity and skills of the board as a whole.
* **Board Leadership** (Page 2, Field 6)
	+ Rate the effectiveness of board leadership.
* **Strategic Direction** (Page 2, Field 7)
	+ Rate the clarity of strategic direction.
* **Financial Oversight** (Page 2, Field 8)
	+ Rate financial monitoring and oversight.
* **Risk Management** (Page 2, Field 9)
	+ Rate risk identification and management.
* **Stakeholder Engagement** (Page 2, Field 10)
	+ Rate engagement with stakeholders.
* **Board-Staff Relationships** (Page 3, Field 11)
	+ Rate working relationship with staff.
* **Meeting Effectiveness** (Page 3, Field 12)
	+ Rate overall meeting effectiveness.
* **Decision Making Process** (Page 3, Field 13)
	+ Rate the quality of decision-making process.
* **Board Communication** (Page 3, Field 14)
	+ Rate communication among board members.
* **Governance Policies** (Page 3, Field 15)
	+ Rate the adequacy of governance policies.
* **Board Development Opportunities** (Page 3, Field 16)
	+ Rate opportunities for board development.
* **Overall Board Effectiveness** (Page 4, Field 17)
	+ Rate the board as a whole.
* **Board Strengths** (Page 4, Field 18)
	+ Describe what the board does well.
* **Board Weaknesses** (Page 4, Field 19)
	+ Identify areas that need improvement.
* **Priorities for Improvement** (Page 4, Field 20)
	+ Suggest areas to focus on.
* **Additional Recommendations** (Page 4, Field 21)
	+ Any other suggestions you have.

Note: The answers will not be visible to the public, and the board members are encouraged to provide honest and constructive feedback.
