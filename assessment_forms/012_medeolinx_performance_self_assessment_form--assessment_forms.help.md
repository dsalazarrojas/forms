# MedeoLinx Performance Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess your performance and provide feedback for improvement. It's an essential tool for evaluating your progress and setting goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your performance level: Choose the best description of your current performance level: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Improvement.
2. Choose your review period: Select the date and time of your last performance review.
3. Enter your employee name: Type your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
4. Check your title status: Indicate whether you have a title or not.
5. Enter your manager's name: Type your manager's full name.
6. Select the review date: Choose the date of your last performance review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Performance Level** (`performance_level_rating`, `select_one`, required): Choose the best description of your current performance level: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Improvement. This will help you identify areas where you need improvement.
- **Review Period** (`review_period`, `date`, required): Select the date and time of your last performance review. This helps us understand when you last had a performance review.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Type your full name as it appears on your identification documents. Make sure to use the correct spelling and capitalization.
- **Title** (`employee_title`, `select_one`, required): Check whether you have a title or not. This is an important part of your employee information.
- **Manager** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Type your manager's full name. Double-check their title and last name for accuracy.
- **Review Date** (`review_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of your last performance review. Be precise with the date to ensure accurate tracking.
