# Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This assessment form is designed to help you gather information about your current work-related pressures and challenges. The form is meant to be a starting point for reflection and discussion between you and your manager or HR representative. It's a tool to help identify areas where support and resources can be provided to improve work life balance and overall job satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the current page you are on by clicking on the corresponding "Page 1: <page_name>" button.
2. For each question, provide your honest and detailed responses in the text box provided. You can write as much or as little as you need.
3. If you need to add more text or clarify your response, you can use the extra space provided at the end of each page.
4. When you've finished all pages, click "Submit" to review and submit your responses.
