# Anti Static Workstation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help assess the condition and status of anti-static workstations. It will help you evaluate the overall health and readiness of the workstations for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Record the details of the workstation you are assessing, including the id and location.
2. Enter the name of the person completing the assessment.
3. Answer the questions regarding the ESD mat condition, wrist strap status, and grounding verification status.
4. Provide any additional comments or observations about the workstation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workstation id** (`workstation_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique designation or serial number of the workstation being assessed.
* **Assessor name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person completing this assessment.
* **ESD mat condition** (`esd_mat_condition`, `select_one`, required): Choose the condition of the ESD mat from the options:
	+ Good
	+ Worn
	+ Damaged
* **Wrist strap status** (`wrist_strap_status`, `select_one`, required): Check the status of the wrist strap:
	+ Present and functional
	+ Present but faulty
	+ Not present
* **Grounding verification status** (`grounding_verification`, `select_one`, required): Verify the grounding status:
	+ Verified
	+ Not verified
	+ Not applicable
* **Corrective actions** (`corrective_actions`, `text`, optional): Provide any needed repairs or replacements for the workstation.
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any other observations or comments about the workstation.

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately report the condition and status of the workstation.
* Use this assessment form to monitor and track the condition of workstations over time.
* Complete this form as thoroughly and honestly as possible.
