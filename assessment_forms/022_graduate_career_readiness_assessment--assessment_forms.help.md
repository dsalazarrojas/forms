# Graduate Career Readiness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a student's career readiness, career goals, and areas where they may need additional support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field. Please enter a valid email address where we can reach you for any follow-up questions.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Select your current job status from the list:
	* High School
	* Working
	* Looking for a job
	* Other
5. In the "What are your career goals?" field, enter a brief description of what you hope to achieve in your career.
6. Select all the career fields or industries that interest you, from the list:
	* Arts and Design
	* Engineering
	* Healthcare
	* Technology
	* Other
7. If you have any specific needs or areas where you'd like additional support, enter a date in the "Is there anything else the student might need support with?" field. If not, leave this field blank.
8. Select all the career services you'd like to receive support with, from the list:
	* Resume and CV development
	* Interview preparation
	* Networking events
	* Other
9. Answer "True" or "False" to the question: "Is the student considering graduate school?"
10. Click "Submit" to submit your assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you for any follow-up questions.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
- **What is your current job status?** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your current employment status from the list:
	* High School
	* Working
	* Looking for a job
	* Other
- **What are your career goals?** (`career_goals`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your career goals.
- **What career fields or industries interest you?** (`career_fields`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the career fields or industries that interest you, from the list:
	* Arts and Design
	* Engineering
	* Healthcare
	* Technology
	* Other
- **Is there anything else the student might need support with?** (`support_needed`, `date`, optional): If you have any specific needs or areas where you'd like additional support, enter a date. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
- **What career services would you like to receive?** (`career_services`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the career services you'd like to receive support with, from the list:
	* Resume and CV development
	* Interview preparation
	* Networking events
	* Other
- **Is the student considering graduate school?** (`graduate_programs`, `select_multiple`, required): Answer "True" or "False" to this question.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields. If you're unsure, ask a career counselor for guidance.
* Be as specific and detailed as possible when answering the questions.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us for support.
