# Position And Pay Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about employee's position and pay details for internal tracking and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by entering the position and pay details for the employee. This can be done by filling in the "What is the position" field with a brief description of the position.
*   Enter the pay details, if applicable, in the "what_is_the_pay" field. Please note that this field is a number field and should be entered as a numerical value.
*   For the "Performance Rating" field, select a number from 1 to 5 using the dropdown options provided.
*   Enter the employee's job title in the "job_title" field.
*   Enter the employee's department in the "department" field.
*   Enter the name of the employee's manager in the "Manager Name" field.
*   Enter the email of the manager in the "Manager email" field.
*   Enter the phone number of the manager in the "Manager phone" field.
*   Select the job level of the employee from the dropdown options provided in the "job_level" field.
*   If applicable, enter any additional job type details in the "job_type" field.
*   Enter any job function information in the "Job function" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is the position
*   **What is the position** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the position.
### 2. what_is_the_pay
*   **what_is_the_pay** (number, required): Enter the pay details as a numerical value.
### 3. Performance Rating
*   **Performance Rating** (select_one, required): Select a number from 1 to 5 from the dropdown options provided.
### 4. job_title
*   **job_title** (text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
### 5. department
*   **department** (text, required): Enter the employee's department.
### 6. Manager Name
*   **Manager Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
### 7. manager_email
*   **manager_email** (email, required): Enter the email of the manager.
### 8. manager_phone
*   **manager_phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the manager.
### 9. job_level
*   **job_level** (select_multiple, required): Select the job level of the employee from the dropdown options provided.
### 10. job_type
*   **job_type** (text, optional): Enter any additional job type details, if applicable.
### 11. Job function
*   **Job function** (text, optional): Enter any job function information, if applicable.
