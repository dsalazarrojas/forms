# Online Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect assessment data from users in an educational or testing context. It helps to evaluate a user's performance, track their progress, or provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **first name** in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your **email address** in the "email address" field. Make sure to use a valid email address.
3. Enter your **last name** in the "last name" field.
4. Choose a valid **date** for your assessment.
5. Choose a valid **time** for your assessment.
6. Enter your **test score** in the "test score" field.
7. Select your **test type** from the options provided.
8. If applicable, add **submission notes** in the "submission notes" field.
9. If applicable, select the type of **submission** you have made (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
10. If applicable, select the status of your **submission** from the options provided.
11. Enter your **submission date** and **submission time** if necessary.
12. Enter the **test duration** if applicable.
13. Enter your **phone number** in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (1): Enter your first name.
* **email address** (2): Enter your email address.
* **last name** (3): Enter your last name.
* **date** (4): Choose a valid date for your assessment.
* **time** (5): Choose a valid time for your assessment.
* **test score** (6): Enter your test score.
* **test type** (7): Select your test type from the options provided.
* **submission notes** (8): If applicable, add notes about your submission.
* **submission file** (9): If applicable, select whether you have made a submission.
* **submission type** (10): If applicable, select the type of submission you have made.
* **submission date** (11): If applicable, enter the date of your submission.
* **submission time** (12): If applicable, enter the time of your submission.
* **score range** (13): Enter any relevant information about your score range.
* **score** (14): Enter your score.
* **notes** (15): Add any additional notes about your submission.
* **email** (16): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (17): Enter your phone number.
* **test duration** (18): Enter the duration of your test.
* **submission status** (19-30): Select the status of your submission from the options provided.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure it's correct.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your instructor or administrator for guidance.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
