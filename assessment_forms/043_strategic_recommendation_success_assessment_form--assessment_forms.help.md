# Strategic Recommendation Success Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Strategic Recommendation Success Assessment Form is a tool used to measure the outcome and impact of strategic recommendations made to clients. This form helps to gather feedback, identify areas of improvement, and recognize successful initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions and fields below.
2. For each field, enter the relevant information as prompted.
3. Fill in all required fields. Note that some fields are not required, but filling them out can provide valuable contextual information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Strategic Recommendation Success Assessment Form 1 (Page 1)**
  • **Strategic Recommendation Success Assessment Form 1** (`text`, optional): This field is for entering your overall assessment of the strategic recommendation's success. Enter your evaluation of the recommendation's effectiveness in a brief and concise manner.

- **Select One (Page 2)**
  • **Strategic Recommendation Success Assessment Form 2** (`select_one`, optional): Select one of the options provided to evaluate the recommendation's success. This field allows you to choose from multiple possible outcomes.

- **Strategic Recommendation Success Assessment Form 3 (Page 3)**
  • **Strategic Recommendation Success Assessment Form 3** (`date`, optional): Enter the date you assessed the strategic recommendation's success. This field helps track when the assessment was conducted.

## Tips
Please note that some fields are optional, but filling them out can provide valuable contextual information. Ensure that the form is completed carefully and accurately to provide useful feedback.
