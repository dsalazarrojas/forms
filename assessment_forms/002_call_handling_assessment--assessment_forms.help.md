# Call Handling Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Call Handling Assessment form evaluates the performance of Customer Service Representatives (CSRs) in handling calls. It is used to provide insights into their skills and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the name of the Customer Service Representative (CSR) being assessed in the "Customer Service Representative Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment in the "Evaluator Name" field.
3. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
4. Enter the Call Reference ID of the call being assessed in the "Call Reference ID" field.
5. Answer the evaluation questions in the "Evaluation Criteria" section.
6. Provide overall comments and feedback in the "Overall Evaluator Comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Service Representative Name**: Enter the name of the CSR being assessed.
* **Evaluator Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Call Reference ID**: Enter the unique ID of the recorded call.
* **Opening and Greeting (select one)**:
	+ 1 - Poor
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 3 - Good
	+ 4 - Very Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **Tone and Empathy (select one)**:
	+ 1 - Poor
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 3 - Good
	+ 4 - Very Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **Active Listening (select one)**:
	+ 1 - Poor
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 3 - Good
	+ 4 - Very Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **Problem Solving and Technical Knowledge (select one)**:
	+ 1 - Poor
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 3 - Good
	+ 4 - Very Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **Closing Etiquette (select one)**:
	+ 1 - Poor
	+ 2 - Fair
	+ 3 - Good
	+ 4 - Very Good
	+ 5 - Excellent
* **Was the issue resolved during the call? (select one)**:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Partially
	+ Escalated
* **Overall Evaluator Comments**: Enter any general feedback or comments about the CSR's performance.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Provide specific feedback for the CSR to improve.
* **Call Duration (Minutes)**: If desired, enter the duration of the call.
* **Was the issue resolved during the call? (select one)**:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Partially
	+ Escalated

Note: If you are not sure about any of the options, choose "False" or "Partially" to indicate that there is room for improvement.
