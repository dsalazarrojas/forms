# Salon Staff COVID 19 Self-Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Salon Staff COVID 19 Self-Assessment Form is a tool designed to help salon staff assess their own health status in relation to COVID-19. This form is meant to be completed by salon staff to determine if they are fit to work and interact with clients and other staff members in a safe and healthy environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and accurately, considering the current state of your health.
2. Choose the correct options for each question, selecting 'True' or 'False' for symptoms or close contact, and providing a brief description for text fields.
3. Fill out all required fields. Note that all fields are currently optional, but completing them will help provide more comprehensive information.
4. Enter the current date and time using the date field.
5. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter your name as it appears on your employee records.
* **Has Temperature** (`has_temperature`): Check 'True' if you have taken your temperature and it is above 100.4°F (38°C) or check 'False' otherwise.
* **Has Cough** (`has_cough`): Check 'True' if you have a cough or check 'False' otherwise.
* **Has Sore Throat** (`has_sore_throat`): Check 'True' if you have a sore throat or check 'False' otherwise.
* **Recent Travel** (`recent_travel`): Check 'True' if you have traveled recently or check 'Other' if you have other recent travel history not listed.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`): Describe any symptoms you are currently experiencing, such as fever, chills, or difficulty breathing.
* **Contact** (`contact`): Provide the name of any individuals or organizations you have come into contact with recently.
* **Other Symptoms** (`other_symptoms`): Describe any other symptoms or health concerns you are experiencing.
* **Have Any Other Symptoms** (`have_any_other_symptoms`): Check 'True' if you have any other symptoms not listed above, or check 'False' otherwise.
* **Recent Close Contact** (`recent_close_contact`): Check 'True' if you have had close contact with someone recently or check 'Other' if you have other close contact history not listed.
* **Have Any Other Close Contact** (`have_any_other_close_contact`): Check 'True' if you have any other close contact not listed above, or check 'False' otherwise.
* **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`): Provide a brief description of your current health status and any concerns you may have.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`): Enter the current date and time using the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM:SS AM/PM.

Note: This form is intended to be a starting point for further assessment and guidance from management or HR. If you are unsure about any of the questions or have concerns about your health, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
