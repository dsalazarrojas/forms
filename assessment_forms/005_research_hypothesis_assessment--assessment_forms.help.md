# Research Hypothesis Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Research Hypothesis Assessment form is designed to collect information about a research hypothesis. It is intended for users who have a clear research question and hypothesis in mind and want to document their thoughts and ideas.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and make sure you have a valid research question and hypothesis.
2. Fill out the form by providing your research hypothesis statement, selecting the correct form type, category, and submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a note field where you can provide a brief description of your research project. 
* **Research Hypothesis Statement**: A clear and concise statement of your research question or hypothesis. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Submitted**: Select the correct option to indicate when the research hypothesis form was submitted. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Type**: Choose the correct type for your research hypothesis. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Category**: Select all relevant categories for your research hypothesis. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Date**: Enter the date when the research hypothesis form was submitted. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Time**: Enter the time when the research hypothesis form was submitted. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Email**: Enter your email address. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Phone**: Enter your phone number. 
* **Research Hypothesis Form Note**: Additional note field for any other information you want to add. 

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise research hypothesis statement. 
* Double-check the form type and category selections. 
* Enter the correct date and time of submission.
