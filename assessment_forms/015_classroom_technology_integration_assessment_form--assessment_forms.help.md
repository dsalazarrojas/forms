# Classroom Technology Integration Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you assess your current technology integration practices and identify areas where you may need support or training to improve. The form will ask you to provide information about your teaching context, comfort level with technology, and professional development needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each section of the form based on your current practices and comfort level with technology.
2. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
3. For questions with a "text" type, provide a brief answer in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a teacher.
* **Teacher ID Number** (`teacher_id`, text, required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your school email address.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_multiple, required): Select all grade levels you teach.
* **Subject Area** (`subject_area`, select_one, required): Select your primary subject area taught.
* **Years of Teaching Experience** (`years_teaching`, select_one, required): Select how long you have been teaching.
* **Technology Devices Available** (`devices_available`, select_multiple, required): Select all technology devices you have access to in the classroom.
* **Condition of Available Technology** (`device_condition`, select_one, required): Rate the condition of your available technology.
* **Frequency of Technology Use in Instruction** (`technology_usage_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you use technology for instruction.
* **Level of Technology Integration** (`technology_integration_level`, select_one, required): Select how technology is integrated into your teaching.
* **Software or Applications Commonly Used** (`software_applications_used`, select_multiple, required): Select all software or applications you commonly use.
* **Overall Comfort Level with Technology** (`overall_comfort_level`, select_one, required): Select your comfort level with using technology for instruction.
* **Specific Technology Skills Rating** (`specific_skills_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency in specific technology areas.
* **Student Device Management Comfort** (`student_device_management`, select_one, required): Select your comfort level managing student devices.
* **Primary Barriers to Technology Integration** (`primary_barriers`, select_multiple, required): Select all barriers to technology integration you face.
* **Frequency of Technical Issues** (`technical_issues_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you experience technical issues.
* **Satisfaction with Technical Support** (`technical_support_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Select your satisfaction with available technical support.
* **Training Topics of Interest** (`training_topics_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all topics you would like to receive training on.
* **Preferred Training Format** (`preferred_training_format`, select_one, required): Select your preferred format for professional development.
* **Preferred Training Time** (`training_time_preference`, select_one, required): Select when you would prefer to attend training.
* **Your Technology Integration Goals** (`technology_goals`, text, required): Describe your goals for technology use this year.
* **Support Needed to Achieve Goals** (`support_needed`, text, required): Describe the support you need to achieve your goals.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any other feedback about technology integration.

Note: You can provide additional comments or suggestions in this section.
