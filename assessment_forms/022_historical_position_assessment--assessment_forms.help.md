# Historical Position Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to complete the Historical Position Assessment form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, select the "Historical Position" page and fill in the following fields:
	* **Historical Position**: Enter a brief description of your past job title.
	* **roles**: Select the roles you held in your past position (e.g., Accountant, Manager, Engineer).
2. If applicable, fill in the dates of your past employment:
	* **start_date**: Enter the start date of your past job.
	* **end_date**: Enter the end date of your past job.
3. Describe your past job responsibilities:
	* **responsibilities**: Provide a detailed account of your job responsibilities.
4. Evaluate your past performance:
	* **performance_rating**: Enter a numerical rating (e.g., 1-5) to assess your performance during your past job.
	* **status**: Indicate the status of your past job (e.g., Active, Inactive).
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your past job:
	* **notes**: Enter any relevant comments or observations about your past job.
6. If applicable, fill in the following fields:
	* **position**: Select the type of position you held (e.g., Full Time, Part Time, Consultant).
	* **job_description**: Provide a detailed description of your job.
	* **organisation**: Enter the name of your past employer or organization.
	* **job_title**: Enter the title of your past job.
	* **supervision**: Select the types of supervision you received (e.g., Accountant, Manager, Engineer).
	* **salary**: Enter your past salary.
	* **hours_worked**: Enter the number of hours you worked per week.
	* **start_time**: Enter the time you started your past job.
	* **end_time**: Enter the time you ended your past job.
	* **job_type**: Select the type of job you held (e.g., Full Time, Part Time, Consultant).
	* **job_status**: Indicate the status of your past job (e.g., Active, Inactive).
7. Review and submit the form.
