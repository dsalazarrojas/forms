# Dimensionality Reduction Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dimensionality Reduction Quiz is a form designed to assess your skills or knowledge on dimensionality reduction. It is intended to help users evaluate their understanding of this concept.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Dimensionality Reduction Quiz, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you have experience with dimensionality reduction.
2. Choose the dimensions you have experience with.
3. Enter the scorecard score, if applicable.
4. Indicate whether you have completed the quiz.
5. Submit your submission details, including your name, email, phone number, and any additional comments.
