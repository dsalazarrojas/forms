# Palliative Care Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's palliative care status, including their medical history, symptoms, pain level, emotional status, and other relevant factors. The form is likely used by healthcare professionals to assess the patient's condition and provide appropriate care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by reviewing the patient's introduction page.
2. Answer each question truthfully and concisely.
3. Select the correct options for each field.
4. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Patient Information
* **patient_info (required)**: Enter the patient's demographic information, such as age, sex, and date of birth.

### 2. Medical History
* **medical_history (optional)**: Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses or conditions.

### 3. Type of Care
* **care_type (required)**: Select the type of care the patient is currently receiving, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 4. Symptoms
* **symptoms (required)**: Select all symptoms the patient is currently experiencing, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 5. Pain Level
* **pain (required)**: Rate the patient's pain level, from 0 to 10.

### 6. Emotional Status
* **emotional_status (required)**: Select the patient's emotional status, such as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

### 7. Medical Team
* **medical_team (required)**: Select all healthcare professionals involved in the patient's care, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 8. Medication
* **medication (optional)**: Provide any current medications the patient is taking.

### 9. Discharge Status
* **discharge (required)**: Select the patient's current discharge status, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 10. Medical Conditions
* **medical_conditions (optional)**: Provide a list of the patient's current medical conditions.

### 11. Patient Care Status
* **patient_care_status (required)**: Select the patient's current care status, such as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

### 12. Hospice Information
* **hospice_info (optional)**: Provide any information about the patient's hospice care.

### 13. Social Support
* **social_support (optional)**: Select all social support services the patient is receiving, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 14. Spiritual Status
* **spiritual_status (optional)**: Provide any information about the patient's spiritual status.

### 15. End-of-Life Information
* **end_of_life_info (optional)**: Provide any information about the patient's end-of-life wishes.

### 16. Family Involvement
* **family_involvement (optional)**: Select all family members involved in the patient's care, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 17. Care Location
* **care_location (required)**: Select the location where the patient is receiving care.

### 18. Medical Facility
* **medical_facility (optional)**: Provide the name of the medical facility the patient is visiting.

### 19. Medical Staff
* **medical_staff (required)**: Select all medical staff members involved in the patient's care, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 20. Discharge Status
* **discharge_status (required)**: Select the patient's current discharge status, such as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

### 21. Hospice Location
* **hospice_location (optional)**: Provide the name of the hospice facility the patient is visiting.

### 22. Care Team Members
* **care_team_members (required)**: Select all care team members involved in the patient's care, such as 'Yes' or 'No'.

### 23. Care Level
* **care_level (required)**: Rate the level of care the patient is receiving, from 1 to 10.

### 24. Other Comments
* **other_comments (optional)**: Provide any additional comments about the patient's care.

## Tips
* Be thorough when completing each field to ensure accurate information.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Use clear and concise language when providing information.
* Avoid using jargon or technical terms that may confuse others.
* If unsure about any field, ask for clarification from the healthcare professional completing the form.
* Ensure accurate and up-to-date information is provided.
* Use the form as a guide for ongoing care decisions.
