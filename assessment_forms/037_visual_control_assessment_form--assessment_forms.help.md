# Visual Control Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visual Control Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the safety and efficiency of a workplace. It gathers information about the presence and effectiveness of visual controls and safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively:

*   Read each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
*   Answer all required fields truthfully and to the best of your ability.
*   If a field is marked as optional, feel free to provide as much information as you need to provide context, but be aware that it may not be required for the form to be considered complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Workplace
*   **Workplace (Text)**: Enter a description of your current workplace or work environment.

### Safety Risks
*   **Safety Risks (Select Multiple)**: Choose all safety risks that you believe are present in your current workplace. These might include:
    *   **Falls**: Slip, trip, or fall hazards
    *   **Cuts**: Sharp objects or edges, potentially hazardous materials
    *   **Burns**: Heat, fire, or chemical burns
    *   **Other**: Specify other types of safety risks not mentioned above

### Workspaces
*   **Workspaces (Number)**: Enter the number of different workspaces or areas where you work.

### Visual Controls
*   **Visual Controls (Text)**: Describe any visual controls you have in place to prevent or mitigate safety risks.

### Workstations
*   **Workstations (Number)**: Enter the number of workstations or work areas where you work.

### Control Measures
*   **Control Measures (Text)**: Describe any measures you take to prevent or mitigate safety risks.

### Control Measures Effectiveness
*   **Control Measures Effectiveness (Number)**: Rate the effectiveness of your control measures on a scale of 0 to 10.

### Safety Training
*   **Safety Training (Text)**: Describe any safety training or education you have received related to workplace safety.

### Safety Risks Frequency
*   **Safety Risks Frequency (Text)**: Describe how often you encounter safety risks in your workplace. Be as specific as possible.

### Safety Risks Consequences
*   **Safety Risks Consequences (Select One)**: Rate the severity of safety risks you encounter, choosing from:
    *   **High**: Safety risks are severe and potentially life-threatening.
    *   **Moderate**: Safety risks are present but not severe.
    *   **Low**: Safety risks are relatively low or rare.

### Safety Risks Description
*   **Safety Risks Description (Text)**: Provide a detailed description of the safety risks you encounter in your workplace.
