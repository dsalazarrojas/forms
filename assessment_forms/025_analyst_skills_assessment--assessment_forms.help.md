# Analyst Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills and abilities of an individual in an analytical role, specifically their proficiency in Microsoft Excel, SQL, data visualization tools, and their analytical and communication skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please provide your full name and email address in the required fields.
2.  Select your proficiency level in Microsoft Excel (Expert, Advanced, Intermediate, Basic) in the "Microsoft Excel Proficiency Level" field.
3.  Choose your level of SQL knowledge (Advanced Joins / Optimization, Basic Queries, No Knowledge) in the "SQL Knowledge" field.
4.  Select all the data visualization tools you have experience with (Tableau, Power BI, Google Data Studio, Python / Matplotlib, R / ggplot2) in the "Experience with Data Visualization Tools" field.
5.  Describe your approach to a scenario where there is a 10% drop in revenue.
6.  Choose your level of experience with statistical modeling (Extensive, Some, None) in the "Statistical Modeling Experience" field.
7.  Enter the number of years you have spent in professional analytical roles.
8.  Select how clear you are in communicating complex data to non-technical audiences (Very Clear, Clear, Needs Work) in the "Communication of Complex Data to Non-Technical Audiences" field.
9.  Describe a recent complex problem you solved.
10.  Enter the date of this assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate Full Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Candidate Email Address** (`candidate_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Microsoft Excel Proficiency Level** (`excel_proficiency_level`, `select_one`, required): This field measures your level of expertise in Microsoft Excel, from Basic to Expert.
*   **SQL Knowledge** (`sql_knowledge`, `select_one`, required): This field evaluates your level of SQL knowledge, from no knowledge to advanced joins and optimization.
*   **Experience with Data Visualization Tools** (`data_visualization_tools`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the data visualization tools you have experience with.
*   **Scenario Analysis Response** (`scenario_analysis_response`, `text`, required): Describe how you would approach a 10% drop in revenue.
*   **Statistical Modeling Experience** (`statistical_modeling_experience`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience with statistical modeling, from extensive to none.
*   **Years of Analytical Experience** (`years_of_analytical_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have spent in professional analytical roles.
*   **Communication of Complex Data to Non-Technical Audiences** (`communication_of_complex_data`, `select_one`, required): Choose how clear you are in communicating complex data to non-technical audiences.
*   **Describe a recent Complex Problem Solved** (`example_of_problem_solving`, `text`, required): Describe a recent complex problem you solved.
*   **Date of Assessment** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this assessment.
