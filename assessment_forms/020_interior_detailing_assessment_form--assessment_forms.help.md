# interior_detailing_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interior_detailing_assessment_form is used to assess the interior condition of vehicles.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, provide accurate and complete information.
3. Use the "Other" option for assigned detailer if not listed.
4. Select all applicable contact methods for communication.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle's interior condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Condition**: A text field to describe the vehicle's interior condition.
* **Interior Details**: A text field for any additional interior details or notes.
* **Interior Condition Rating**: A numeric field to rate the interior condition (likely on a scale, e.g., 1-5).
* **Interior Condition Score**: A numeric field to score the interior condition (likely out of 10).
* **Comments**: A text field for any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
* **Date of Last Detailing**: A date field to record the date of the last interior detailing.
* **Assigned Detailer**: A select-one field to choose the detailer assigned to the vehicle.
* **Contact Method**: A select-multiple field to choose contact methods for communication (e.g., Phone, Email, Text).
* **Field 9**: A text field for any additional information.
