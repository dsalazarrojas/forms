# Parade Safety Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parade Safety Assessment Form is used to collect information about a parade event, including contact information, emergency contacts, and event details. This form helps ensure that all necessary safety measures are taken into account before the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Contact Information**: Select the most relevant contact information for the event from the provided options.
2. **Emergency Contact Person**: Provide the name and information of a person who will be available in case of an emergency.
3. **Emergency Phone Number**: Select a contact method that will be available in case of an emergency.
4. **Emergency Address**: If the event is held at a private residence or a specific location, enter the address of the emergency contact person's location.
5. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event, following the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
6. **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event, using 24-hour format.
7. **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event will take place.
8. **Event Notes**: Add any additional information about the event that might be relevant for safety purposes.
9. **Event Risk Factors**: Select all relevant risk factors that might affect the event, such as inclement weather, large crowds, or security concerns.
