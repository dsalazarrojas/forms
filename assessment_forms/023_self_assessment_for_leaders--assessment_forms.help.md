## Purpose
This form is designed to help leaders reflect on their skills and performance in various areas, aiding in self-awareness and self-improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
2. Be specific in your answers, providing details and examples when possible.
3. Review each question carefully and take note of any areas where you'd like to improve or focus on.
