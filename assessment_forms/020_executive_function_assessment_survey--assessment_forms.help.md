# Executive Function Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Function Assessment Survey is designed to assess an individual's executive function skills, including planning, organization, time management, and self-regulation. This form is used to identify strengths and weaknesses in these areas, which can inform treatment planning, goal setting, and progress monitoring.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form by providing the requested information for each section. Each section is labeled with a brief description of what that section is designed to assess.
2. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. You may skip fields that are not applicable or relevant to your situation.
4. Please enter your start and end times in the format of HH:MM (hours and minutes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`personal_info`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal information that you feel is necessary to provide context.
* **Rating for self** (`ratings`, `text`, optional): This section is designed to assess your self-regulation skills. Please rate your ability to regulate your emotions, thoughts, and behaviors on a scale of 1-5.
* **Selected item 0** (`select_item_0`, `text`, optional): This section is designed to assess your ability to make selections and decisions. Please enter the item you selected, if any.
* **Selected item 1** (`select_item_1`, `text`, optional): This section is designed to assess your ability to make selections and decisions. Please enter the item you selected, if any.
* **Start time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Please enter the start time of a relevant task or activity in the format of HH:MM (hours and minutes).
* **End time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Please enter the end time of a relevant task or activity in the format of HH:MM (hours and minutes).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Please provide any additional information or context that you feel is relevant to your assessment.

## Tips
- Please answer all required fields (Start Time and End Time) accurately and truthfully.
- You may skip fields that are not applicable or relevant to your situation.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with the administrator or support team.

Note: The form structure and field names were used as a basis for this help guide. The explanations are based on the provided YAML fields and attempt to provide a clear and concise understanding of each field's purpose.
