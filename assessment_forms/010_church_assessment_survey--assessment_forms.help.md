# Church Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from church members, attenders, or visitors to help the church improve its services, leadership, and overall experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question based on your personal experience with the church.
2. Choose the most suitable response for each question, considering your honest opinion.
3. Provide any specific feedback or suggestions in the free-text field at the end of the form.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name (Optional)**: This field is optional and allows you to provide your name for any follow-up communication or for the church to acknowledge your feedback.
* **Your Role in the Church**: This field helps us understand your position or level of involvement with the church. Please select the most accurate option.
* **How long have you been involved with this church**: This question helps us understand your level of experience with the church. Select the time frame that best fits your involvement.
* **How would you rate the quality of worship services**: This question assesses your opinion on the quality of worship services. Choose the rating that best reflects your experience.
* **How effective are the sermons in your spiritual growth**: This question evaluates the effectiveness of sermons in your spiritual growth. Select the rating that best describes your experience.
* **How satisfied are you with the liturgy and music**: This question rates your satisfaction with the liturgy and music in the church. Choose the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Do you feel a sense of community and belonging**: This question assesses your sense of community and belonging in the church. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Are there adequate fellowship and social opportunities**: This question evaluates the availability of fellowship and social opportunities in the church. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Satisfaction with Outreach and Mission Work**: This question rates your satisfaction with the church's outreach and mission work. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **How effective is the church leadership**: This question evaluates the effectiveness of the church leadership. Select the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Is church communication clear and timely**: This question assesses the effectiveness of the church's communication. Choose the option that best describes your experience.
* **Is the church transparent in its decision-making**: This question evaluates the level of transparency in the church's decision-making process. Select the option that best reflects your opinion.
* **Condition and Cleanliness of Facilities**: This question rates the condition and cleanliness of the church's facilities. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
* **Accessibility for People with Disabilities**: This question evaluates the accessibility of the church's facilities for people with disabilities. Select the option that best describes your experience.
* **Areas Where the Church Could Improve (Select all that apply)**: This question allows you to select areas where the church could improve. Choose all that apply.
* **Suggestions for Church Improvement**: This question provides an opportunity to share specific recommendations for improvement.
* **Overall Satisfaction with the Church**: This question rates your overall satisfaction with the church. Choose the option that best reflects your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend this church to others**: This question evaluates your likelihood of recommending the church to others. Select the option that best describes your opinion.
