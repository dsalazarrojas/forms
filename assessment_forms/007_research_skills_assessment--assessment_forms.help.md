# Research Skills Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to evaluate your research skills in various areas, such as question answering, data analysis, and communication. It's a comprehensive assessment to help us understand your strengths and weaknesses and inform our training programs and resources to support your growth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read each question carefully and answer them accurately and honestly.
2. For questions with multiple options, select the one that best fits your situation.
3. You can skip questions that are not applicable or relevant to your situation.
4. Use the space provided for questions that require a written answer.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Skills Assessment**: This is a free-text field for you to describe your research skills and experience.
  * Type your response in the space provided.
  * Be as detailed as possible, but concise and clear.
* 
* **Question 1:** This question assesses your ability to ask insightful questions. Choose 'Yes' if you usually get the right answer, or 'No' if not.
* **Question 2:** This question measures your ability to identify and extract relevant information. Select all applicable options that describe your approach.
* 
* **Question 3:** This question evaluates your ability to analyze data. Choose 'Yes' if you usually do it correctly, or 'No' if not.
* 
* **Question 4-12:** These questions assess your ability to communicate complex ideas and findings. Choose 'Yes' if you usually do it effectively, or 'No' if not. Please note that these questions are designed to be more specific and context-dependent.

**Tips:**

* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you're unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any comments or suggestions, please use the space provided for each question to add them.
