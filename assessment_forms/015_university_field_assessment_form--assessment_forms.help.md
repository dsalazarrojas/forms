# university_field_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The university field assessment form is used to gather information about students' performance, behavior, and progress during field visits.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter the student's ID number (if applicable).
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Field Visit Date** (`field_visit_date`, date, required): Select the date of the field visit.
* **Field Visit Notes** (`field_visit_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the field visit.
* **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the faculty member accompanying the student.
* **Faculty ID** (`faculty_id`, number, optional): Enter the faculty member's ID number (if applicable).
* **Faculty Email** (`faculty_email`, email, optional): Enter the faculty member's email address (if applicable).
* **Challenge Description** (`challenge_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the challenge the student faced during the field visit.
* **Observation Description** (`observation_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the observations made during the field visit.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, text, optional): Enter any recommendations for the student's progress.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the staff member who conducted the field visit.
* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`, number, optional): Enter the staff member's ID number (if applicable).
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, email, optional): Enter the staff member's email address (if applicable).
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Select the date on which the assessment was completed.
* **Assessment Comments** (`assessment_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the assessment.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Enter the next steps to be taken by the student.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted On** (`form_submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted Time** (`form_submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Assessment Complete** (`assessment_complete`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if the assessment is complete, otherwise select "No".
* **Assessment Form Status** (`assessment_form_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the assessment (Complete, Incomplete, or Pending).
* **Field Visit Location** (`field_visit_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the field visit.
* **Field Visit Duration** (`field_visit_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the field visit.
* **Field Visit Comments** (`field_visit_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the field visit.
* **Assessment Complete** (`assessment_complete`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the assessment is complete.
* **Assessment Complete** (`assessment_form_status`, select_one, optional): Select "Complete", "Incomplete", or "Pending" to indicate the status of the assessment.
