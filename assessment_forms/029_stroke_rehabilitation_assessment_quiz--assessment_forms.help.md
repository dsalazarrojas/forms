# Stroke Rehabilitation Assessment Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stroke Rehabilitation Assessment Quiz is designed to collect information about a patient's stroke recovery and rehabilitation progress. This quiz helps healthcare professionals assess the impact of a stroke on different aspects of a patient's life, such as motor, language, cognitive, and behavioral functions. The data collected will be used to monitor the patient's recovery and adjust their treatment plan accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the following questions about the patient's stroke:
	* Stroke side affected (left, right, or bilateral)?
	* Type of stroke (ischemic, hemorrhagic, or other)?
	* Locations affected by the stroke (cerebral cortex, cerebellum, brainstem, or other)?
2. Assess the patient's motor, language, cognitive, and behavioral impact after the stroke:
	* Has the patient experienced motor impact after the stroke? (Yes or No)
	* Has the patient experienced language impact after the stroke? (Yes or No)
	* Has the patient experienced cognitive impact after the stroke? (Yes or No)
	* Has the patient experienced behavioral impact after the stroke? (Yes or No)
3. Specify the frequency and duration of follow-up appointments:
	* How often do you need follow-up appointments? (1 week, 2 weeks, 3 weeks, 4 weeks, 6 weeks, monthly)
	* How long do you want each follow-up appointment to last? (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 60 minutes)
4. Provide any additional notes about the patient's condition:
	* Enter any relevant notes about the patient's condition or treatment plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stroke Side Affected**: Select the side of the patient's body affected by the stroke. This will help us understand which hemisphere is impacted.
* **Stroke Type**: Choose the type of stroke the patient experienced. This will help us understand the underlying cause of the stroke.
* **Stroke Location**: Select the areas of the brain affected by the stroke. This will help us understand the extent of the damage.
* **Motor Impact**: Indicate if the patient has experienced motor impact after the stroke. This will help us understand the patient's motor function recovery.
* **Language Impact**: Indicate if the patient has experienced language impact after the stroke. This will help us understand the patient's language function recovery.
* **Cognitive Impact**: Indicate if the patient has experienced cognitive impact after the stroke. This will help us understand the patient's cognitive function recovery.
* **Behavioral Impact**: Indicate if the patient has experienced behavioral impact after the stroke. This will help us understand the patient's behavioral function recovery.
* **Rehabilitation Focus**: Select the areas where the patient needs rehabilitation. This will help us understand the patient's rehabilitation needs.
* **Follow-up Frequency**: Specify how often you want follow-up appointments. This will help us understand your schedule and adjust the patient's treatment plan accordingly.
* **Follow-up Duration**: Specify how long each follow-up appointment should last. This will help us adjust the patient's treatment plan accordingly.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the patient's condition or treatment plan. This will help us provide better care and support.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for further communication. This will help us contact you with updates and information about the patient's condition.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further communication. This will help us contact you with updates and information about the patient's condition.
* **Follow-up Date**: Specify the date of the next follow-up appointment. This will help us schedule the appointment and ensure we have the necessary information.
