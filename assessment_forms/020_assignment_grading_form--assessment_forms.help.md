# Assignment Grading Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Assignment Grading Form is used to collect feedback and grading information from instructors on student assignments. It's a detailed form designed to help you provide a thorough review, offering constructive comments and scores for each criterion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Grader Name (Field 1) and Grading Date (Field 2).
2. Enter the Course Name (Field 3), Course Number (Field 4), and Instructor Name (Field 5).
3. Identify the Student Name (Field 6) and Student ID Number (Field 7).
4. Enter the Assignment Name (Field 8) and Assignment Number (Field 9).
5. Record the Submission Date (Field 10).
6. Evaluate the submission by answering the Was Submission Late? (Field 11) question.
7. Provide scores for each criterion, including Completeness (Field 12), Accuracy (Field 13), Organization (Field 14), Clarity of Communication (Field 15), Originality (Field 16), and Effort and Quality (Field 17).
8. Calculate the Overall Score or Grade (Field 18) based on the scores from the previous step.
9. Enter the Letter Grade (Field 19).
10. Provide feedback for the student with the Key Strengths (Field 20) and Areas for Improvement (Field 21).
11. Offer detailed Feedback Comments (Field 22) and Recommendations for the student (Field 23), if applicable.
12. Decide on Resubmission (Field 24) and provide internal notes in Grader Notes (Field 25), if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Grader Name** (Field 1, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as the grader.
* **Grading Date** (Field 2, date, required): Record the date you're grading the assignment.
* **Course Name** (Field 3, text, required): Enter the name of the course where the assignment was submitted.
* **Course Number** (Field 4, text, required): Enter the course identification number.
* **Instructor Name** (Field 5, text, required): Enter your name as the instructor.
* **Student Name** (Field 6, text, required): Enter the name of the student being graded.
* **Student ID Number** (Field 7, text, required): Enter the student's identification number.
* **Assignment Name** (Field 8, text, required): Enter the title or name of the assignment.
* **Assignment Number** (Field 9, text, required): Enter the sequence number of the assignment.
* **Submission Date** (Field 10, date, required): Record the date the assignment was submitted.
* **Was Submission Late?** (Field 11, select_one, required): Answer whether the submission was late.
* **Completeness - Score 1-10** (Field 12, number, required): Rate the completeness of the work.
* **Accuracy - Score 1-10** (Field 13, number, required): Rate the accuracy of the work.
* **Organization - Score 1-10** (Field 14, number, required): Rate the organization of the work.
* **Clarity of Communication - Score 1-10** (Field 15, number, required): Rate the clarity of communication in the work.
* **Originality - Score 1-10** (Field 16, number, required): Rate the originality of the work.
* **Effort and Quality - Score 1-10** (Field 17, number, required): Rate the overall effort and quality of the work.
* **Overall Score or Grade** (Field 18, number, required): Calculate the overall score or grade.
* **Letter Grade** (Field 19, text, required): Enter the letter grade (A, B, C, D, F, etc.).
* **Key Strengths** (Field 20, text, required): Identify the key strengths of the student's work.
* **Areas for Improvement** (Field 21, text, required): Identify areas that need improvement.
* **Detailed Feedback Comments** (Field 22, text, required): Provide detailed feedback comments for the student.
* **Recommendations for Student** (Field 23, text, optional): Offer suggestions for next steps.
* **Allow Resubmission?** (Field 24, select_one, required): Decide if the student can resubmit.
* **Grader Notes** (Field 25, text, optional): Enter internal notes for the grader.
