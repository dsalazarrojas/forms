# onsite_dynamic_risk_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The onsite dynamic risk assessment form is a tool used to evaluate and document potential risks associated with a site or location. This form helps assessors gather and record relevant information about the risks present at the site, enabling informed decision-making and risk management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and the site's specific context before starting.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and resources readily available.
3. Complete the form by answering each question accurately and thoroughly.
4. Select the relevant risk level, probability, and consequence for each identified risk.
5. Provide clear and concise text explanations for the risk prevention and onsite dynamic risk fields.
6. Review and submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **onsite_header** (`onsite_header`, `note`, required: false): This is a placeholder note field to add any additional context or information about the site.
* **onsite_dynamic_risk_assessment_form_header** (`onsite_risk_assessment_form_header`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to add a header or title for the risk assessment form.
* **onsite_risk_level** (`onsite_risk_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the three options: Low, Medium, or High, to indicate the level of risk associated with the site.
* **onsite_risk_probability** (`onsite_risk_probability`, `number`, required: false): Enter a numeric value between 0 and 100 to indicate the probability of the risk occurring.
* **onsite_risk_probability_frequency** (`onsite_risk_probability_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or multiple options (Low, Medium, High) to indicate how often the risk is expected to occur.
* **onsite_risk_consequence** (`onsite_risk_consequence`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one of the three options: Low, Medium, or High, to indicate the severity of the risk's consequence.
* **onsite_risk_prevention** (`onsite_risk_prevention`, `text`, required: false): Provide a clear and concise explanation of the measures that can be taken to prevent the risk from occurring.
* **onsite_dynamic_risk** (`onsite_dynamic_risk`, `note`, required: false): This field is used to provide a summary of the identified risks and their associated details.
* **onsite_recommendations** (`onsite_recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Enter any recommendations or suggested actions to mitigate or address the identified risks.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when assessing and documenting risks.
- Use clear and concise language in the risk prevention and onsite dynamic risk fields.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes or updates in the site's risk profile.
