# Climbing Skills Risk Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess the climbing skills and risk factors of a climber. This form will help identify potential risks and provide a rating to guide climbing leaders and assessors in their decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Full Name
	* Date of Assessment
	* Assessor Name
	* Type of climbing experience (Select all that apply)
	* Years of climbing experience
	* Self-assessed experience level
	* Belay competency level
	* Grip strength assessment
	* Core strength assessment
	* Flexibility and mobility assessment
	* Cardiovascular endurance assessment
	* Fear and anxiety management
	* Risk awareness and judgment
	* Decision-making under pressure
	* Overall risk assessment rating
2. For the medical history section, please note any past climbing-related injuries, current physical limitations, and overall health status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the full name of the climber.
* **Date of Assessment** (`date_of_assessment`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the assessor or climber's name.
* **Type of climbing experience** (`climbing_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of climbing experience you have, such as Bouldering, Top-rope, Sport/Lead climbing, etc.
* **Years of climbing experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years you have been climbing.
* **Self-assessed experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your self-assessed experience level, which includes Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Belay competency level** (`belay_competency`, `select_one`, required): Choose your belay competency level, which includes Not competent, Basic competency, Competent, or Highly competent.
* **Rope management skills** (`rope_management`, `select_one`, required): Choose your rope management skills, which include Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Anchor placement and building knowledge** (`anchor_knowledge`, `select_one`, not required): This option is not required, but indicates your level of experience with anchor placement and building, which includes None, Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
* **Grip strength assessment** (`grip_strength`, `select_one`, required): Choose your grip strength assessment, which includes Below average, Average, Above average, or Excellent.
* **Core strength** (`core_strength`, `select_one`, required): Choose your core strength assessment, which includes Below average, Average, Above average, or Excellent.
* **Flexibility and mobility** (`flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Choose your flexibility and mobility assessment, which includes Below average, Average, Above average, or Excellent.
* **Cardiovascular endurance** (`endurance`, `select_one`, required): Choose your cardiovascular endurance assessment, which includes Below average, Average, Above average, or Excellent.
* **Fear and anxiety management** (`fear_management`, `select_one`, required): Choose your fear and anxiety management assessment, which includes Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Risk awareness and judgment** (`risk_awareness`, `select_one`, required): Choose your risk awareness and judgment assessment, which includes Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Decision-making under pressure** (`decision_making`, `select_one`, required): Choose your decision-making under pressure assessment, which includes Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent.
* **Past climbing-related injuries** (`past_injuries`, `text`, not required): If you have experienced any climbing-related injuries, please describe them here.
* **Current physical limitations** (`current_limitations`, `text`, not required): If you have any current physical limitations, please describe them.
* **Overall risk assessment rating** (`overall_risk`, `select_one`, required): Choose your overall risk assessment rating, which includes Low risk, Moderate risk, High risk, or Very high risk.

## Tips
* Please answer all required fields honestly and thoroughly.
* Be as specific as possible when describing your climbing experience and medical history.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your assessor for guidance.
* This form is intended to be used by climbing leaders and assessors to guide their decision-making process. The results of this form should not be used as a substitute for professional judgment.
