## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Mental Status
• **Mental Status**: This section is used to assess the individual's current mental status.

### Personal History
• **Personal History**: This section is used to gather information about your personal background, including your medical history, work experience, and any other relevant details.

### Family Background
• **Family Background**: This section is used to gather information about your family's medical history, including any genetic disorders, medical conditions, or any other relevant details.

### Medical Status
• **Medical Status**: This section is used to assess your current medical status, including any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant health information.

### Treatment Goals
• **Treatment Goals**: This section is used to identify your goals and priorities for treatment and care.

### Assessment Questions
• **Assessment Questions**: This section is used to answer a series of questions that will help healthcare professionals and counselors understand your current situation and needs.
