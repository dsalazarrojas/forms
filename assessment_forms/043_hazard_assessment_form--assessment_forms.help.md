# Hazard Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hazard Assessment Form is designed to identify and assess potential hazards and risks in a work area. It provides a structured approach to documenting and managing these hazards, ensuring a safe working environment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Main work area from the provided options.
2. Indicate whether hazards have been identified for the selected area.
3. Rate the level of risk associated with the identified hazards.
4. Document any planned mitigation actions for the identified hazards.
5. Record the control measures taken to mitigate the identified hazards.
6. Schedule a follow-up review or action.
7. Specify the next review date or follow-up date.
8. Identify the individual or team responsible for the assigned task.
9. Record the review status and frequency.
10. Document follow-up comments or actions.
