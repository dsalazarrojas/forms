# Retail Seasonal Promotion Impact Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Seasonal Promotion Impact Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the impact of your seasonal promotional efforts on your retail business. It will help you assess the effectiveness of your promotional strategy and identify areas for improvement to enhance future seasonal promotions.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the assessment questions and provide an honest evaluation of your seasonal promotion impact.
2. Choose the correct option that best describes the impact of your seasonal promotion.
3. Select all relevant regions that were impacted by your seasonal promotion.
4. Enter specific notes about the promotional impact.
5. Select the level of merchandising satisfaction.
6. Choose the level of seasonal promotion awareness.
7. Review your answers and ensure they accurately reflect your seasonal promotion's impact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seasonal Promotion Impact** (`seasonal_promotion_impact`, select_one, required): Assess the overall impact of your seasonal promotion. Choose Highly Impactful, Moderately Impactful, or Not Impactful.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, required): Select all regions where your seasonal promotion took place.
* **Merchandising Issues** (`merchandising_issues`, note, required): Provide specific merchandising issues that affected your seasonal promotion. This could include issues with product availability, pricing, or inventory management.
* **Merchandising Suggestions** (`merchandising_suggestions`, text, required): Suggest specific products or product lines that were impacted by merchandising issues.
* **Marketing Issues** (`marketing_issues`, select_multiple, required): Identify the marketing channels that were affected by your seasonal promotion. Choose Print, Digital, or In-Store.
* **Marketing Suggestions** (`marketing_suggestions`, text, required): Suggest specific marketing campaigns or campaigns lines that were impacted by marketing issues.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date when your seasonal promotion took place.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of day or period when your seasonal promotion took place.
* **Season** (`season`, select_one, required): Choose the season when your seasonal promotion took place. Select Spring, Summer, Fall, or Winter.
* **Region of Interest** (`region_of_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select all regions that were interested in your seasonal promotion.
* **Seasonal Promotion Impact Notes** (`seasonal_promotion_impact_notes`, note, required): Provide specific notes about the promotional impact.
* **Merchandising Satisfaction** (`merchandising_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Assess the level of merchandising satisfaction. Choose Highly Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, or Not Satisfied.
* **Seasonal Promotion Awareness** (`seasonal_promotion_awareness`, select_one, required): Assess the level of seasonal promotion awareness. Choose Highly Aware, Moderately Aware, or Not Aware.
