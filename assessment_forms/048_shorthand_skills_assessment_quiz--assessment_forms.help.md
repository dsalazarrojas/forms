# shorthand_skills_assessment_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Shorthand Skills Assessment Quiz is designed to evaluate a user's skills in shorthand writing, including their proficiency in shorthand systems, transcription ability, and real-world usage. The form collects information about the user's shorthand proficiency, transcription ability, and real-world usage, as well as their evaluation of their skills in various aspects. The form also includes a note-taking section for any additional comments or notes the user may want to add.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the form's sections and understanding the questions asked.
2. Read each question carefully and fill in the answers as needed.
3. Select the correct options for the questions that require multiple selections.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the note-taking section.
5. Once you have filled out all the sections, review your answers to ensure you have completed everything accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intro** (`intro_section`, note, required: false): This section is for any initial comments or notes before beginning the assessment.
* **Shorthand Proficiency** (`shorthand_proficiency`, number, required: false): Rate your proficiency in shorthand writing from 1 to 10.
* **Transcription Ability** (`transcription_ability`, number, required: false): Rate your ability to transcribe shorthand writing from 1 to 10.
* **Real-World Usage** (`real_world_usage`, number, required: false): Rate your real-world usage of shorthand writing from 1 to 10.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, select_one, required: false): Select how you typically use shorthand writing:
	+ 1. Shorthand
	+ 2. Shorthand with a small hand-held device
	+ 3. Shorthand with a digital device
* **Evaluation Form 2** (`evaluation_form_2`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
* **Evaluation Form 3** (`evaluation_form_3`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ 1
	+ 2
* **Evaluation Form 4** (`evaluation_form_4`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Evaluation Form 5** (`evaluation_form_5`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Evaluation Form 6** (`evaluation_form_6`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all that apply:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last shorthand writing assessment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of day when you last used shorthand writing.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your assessment.
