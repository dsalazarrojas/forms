# Strategic Capabilities Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Strategic Capabilities Assessment form is used to gather information on an entity's strategic position, including its strengths, challenges, and priorities. This form provides a snapshot of the entity's current capabilities and identifies areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible.
2. For the "strategic_strengths", "strategic_challenges", and "strategic_priorities" sections, select all that apply from the available options.
3. For the "upload_*" sections, provide any supporting documents or information that you think will help in the assessment.
4. Enter the date of the assessment in the "results" field.
5. For the "first_page_uploads", "upload_first_page", "upload_first_page_documents", and "upload_first_page_additional" fields, provide any additional information that you think will be helpful.
