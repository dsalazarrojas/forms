# Understandability_Assessment_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the understandability of a document.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions as accurately as possible based on your experience with the document.
2. For each question, provide a response in the corresponding field.
3. You can skip any optional fields if you are not able to provide the information.
4. If you have any additional comments or feedback, please enter them in the "Form Submission" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the primary purpose of the document?** (`user_input_1`, `text`, optional): This question asks you to briefly describe the main objective of the document.
* **Document Name** (`document_name`, `text`, optional): This is the title of the document.
* **On a scale of 10, how easy was it for you to understand?** (`user_score`, `number`, optional): Rate how easy or difficult it was to understand the document on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Comments** (`user_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about the document.
* **Owner of the Document** (`document_owner`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the owner of the document from the options provided.
* **Document Version** (`document_version`, `date`, optional): Enter the version number of the document.
* **Estimated Time Spent** (`time_spent`, `number`, optional): Estimate the time it took you to understand the document.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the type of document from the options provided.
* **Rate Your Overall Understanding** (`user_rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall understanding of the document on a scale of 1 to 10.
* **Document Link** (`document_link`, `text`, optional): Enter the link to the document.
* **Additional Notes** (`user_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or feedback about the document.
* **Author of the Document** (`document_author`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the author of the document from the options provided.
* **Reviewers** (`document_reviewers`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the reviewers of the document from the options provided.
* **Reviewers Notes** (`document_reviewers_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes or feedback from the reviewers.
* **Date** (`document_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the document.
* **Document Status** (`document_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the status of the document from the options provided.
* **Category** (`document_category`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the category of the document from the options provided.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **File** (`document_file`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional files related to the document.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Title** (`user_title`, `text`, optional): Enter a title.
* **Document ID** (`document_id`, `text`, optional): Enter the ID of the document.
* **Version History** (`document_version_history`, `text`, optional): Enter the version history of the document.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the submission.

Note: This guide is designed to be comprehensive, but please refer back to the form if there are any discrepancies.
