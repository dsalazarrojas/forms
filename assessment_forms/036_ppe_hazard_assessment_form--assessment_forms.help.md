# PPE Hazard Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PPE Hazard Assessment Form is designed to assess the presence and effectiveness of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) used by employees in a work environment. This form helps to identify potential hazards, assess the level of risk, and provide feedback for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the name of the workplace where the assessment is being conducted.
2.  Check if a hazard is present in the location.
3.  Provide a risk assessment of the hazard.
4.  Select the type of Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) provided and used.
5.  Confirm if PPE training has been received.
6.  Confirm if supervision is provided.
7.  Enter the frequency of training received.
8.  Enter the number of training hours received.
9.  Enter the date and time of the last review of the form.
10. Enter your name and position as the reviewer.
11. Enter any comments from the reviewer.
12. Enter your name and position as the employee being assessed.
13. Enter your phone number (optional).
14. Enter your email address (optional).
15. Enter your work location.
16. Enter your job title.
17. Enter your comments as the employee (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workplace Name** (`Workplace Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the workplace where the assessment is being conducted.
*   **Location** (`Location`, text, required): Enter the location within the workplace where the assessment is being conducted.
*   **Is Hazard Present?** (`Is hazard present`, select_one, required): Indicate if a hazard is present in the location by selecting "Yes" or "No".
*   **Risk Assessment** (`Risk Assessment`, text, required): Provide a description of the risk level present.
*   **Personal Protective Equipment Provided** (`Personal Protective Equipment Provided`, select_multiple, required): Select all the equipment used to mitigate the hazard.
*   **PPE Training** (`PPE Training`, select_one, required): Confirm if PPE training has been received.
*   **Supervision** (`Supervision`, select_one, optional): Confirm if supervision is provided.
*   **Training Frequency** (`Training Frequency`, number, required): Enter the frequency of training received.
*   **Training Hours** (`Training Hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours of training received.
*   **Last Updated** (`Last Updated`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last review of the form.
*   **Last Updated By** (`Last Updated By`, text, optional): Enter your name as the reviewer.
*   **Assigned Form** (`Assigned Form`, text, optional): Enter the form assigned to you.
*   **Reviewer Comments** (`Reviewer Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the reviewer.
*   **Reviewer Name** (`Reviewer Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the reviewer.
*   **Reviewer Position** (`Reviewer Position`, text, optional): Enter your position as the reviewer.
*   **Date Reviewed** (`Date Reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last review.
*   **Time Reviewed** (`Time Reviewed`, time, optional): Enter the time of the last review.
*   **Reviewer Notes** (`Reviewer Notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes from the reviewer.
*   **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required): Enter your name as the employee being assessed.
*   **Employee Position** (`Employee Position`, text, required): Enter your position as the employee.
*   **Employee Phone** (`Employee Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Employee Email** (`Employee Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Employee Work Location** (`Employee's Work Location`, text, optional): Enter your work location.
*   **Employee Job Title** (`Employee's Job Title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
*   **Employee Comments** (`Employee Comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from the employee.

Note: This guide assumes the reader has access to the form and its fields. If any field is not understood, please seek clarification from the form creator.
