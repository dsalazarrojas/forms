# New Business Venture Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Business Venture Risk Assessment Form is a tool designed to gauge the feasibility and potential success of a new business venture. It assesses the risk associated with key factors such as risk factors, business model, revenue models, risk assessments, risk level, financial performance, market share, market position, management team, customer base, customer acquisition, competition, market trends, regulatory framework, regulatory environment, technology infrastructure, intellectual property, business model rationale, financial prosperity, financial stability, and financial vulnerability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with the relevant information about your new business venture.
2. Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
3. Select the correct options for each field from the provided options.
4. Review the assessment results to understand the overall risk level of your new business venture.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Factor**: This field is used to identify and describe the risk factors associated with your new business venture. Please enter a brief description of the risk factors, such as market competition, customer acquisition, or regulatory framework.
* **Business Model**: This field is used to describe the business model or business strategy of your new venture. Please enter a short description of your business model, including any relevant details about your product or service offerings.
* **Business Model Type**: This field is used to categorize your business model into one of the provided options.
* **Revenue Model**: This field is used to describe the revenue model of your new business venture. Please enter a short description of how you plan to generate revenue, including any relevant details about your pricing strategy or sales channels.
* **Revenue Model Type**: This field is used to categorize your revenue model into one of the provided options.
* **Risk Assessment**: This field is used to describe your risk assessment, including any mitigating or exacerbating factors. Please enter a brief description of your risk assessment, including any relevant details about your risk management strategy.
* **Risk Level**: This field is used to select the risk level of your new business venture from the provided options: low, medium, or high.
* **Financial Performance**: This field is used to select the financial performance of your new business venture from the provided options: financial prosperity, financial stability, or financial vulnerability.
* **Market Share**: This field is used to select the market share of your new business venture from the provided options (e.g., number).
* **Market Position**: This field is used to select the market position of your new business venture from the provided options: strong, weak, or neutral.
* **Management Team**: This field is used to select the experience level of your management team from the provided options: experienced, inexperienced, or neutral.
* **Customer Base**: This field is used to select the customer base of your new business venture from the provided options (e.g., number).
* **Customer Acquisition**: This field is used to select the customer acquisition strategy of your new business venture from the provided options (e.g., number).
* **Competition**: This field is used to select the level of competition in your market from the provided options: strong, weak, or neutral.
* **Market Trends**: This field is used to select the market trends affecting your new business venture from the provided options: positive, negative, or neutral.
* **Regulatory Framework**: This field is used to describe the regulatory framework or laws and regulations that apply to your new business venture. Please enter a brief description of the regulatory framework, including any relevant details about government agencies or industry-specific regulations.
* **Regulatory Environment**: This field is used to describe the regulatory environment or regulatory climate in your industry. Please enter a brief description of the regulatory environment, including any relevant details about industry associations or self-regulatory organizations.
* **Technology Infrastructure**: This field is used to describe the technology infrastructure or IT systems used by your new business venture. Please enter a brief description of the technology infrastructure, including any relevant details about hardware, software, or data storage.
* **Intellectual Property**: This field is used to describe the intellectual property or IP used by your new business venture. Please enter a brief description of the IP, including any relevant details about patents, trademarks, or copyrights.
* **Business Model Rationale**: This field is used to select whether your business model is based on a proven model or not from the provided options: Yes or No.
* **Financial Prosperity**: This field is used to select whether your financial performance is prosperous or not from the provided options: Yes or No.
* **Financial Stability**: This field is used to select whether your financial performance is stable or not from the provided options: Yes or No.
* **Financial Vulnerability**: This field is used to select whether your financial performance is vulnerable or not from the provided options: Yes or No.

Please note that this guide is intended to help you understand each field and complete the form accurately. However, the actual risk assessment results may vary based on the specific details and context of your new business venture.
