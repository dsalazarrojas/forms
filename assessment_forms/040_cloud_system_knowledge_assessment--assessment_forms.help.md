# Cloud System Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to assess your knowledge and skills in cloud computing. This assessment will help us to identify areas where additional training or support may be needed to aid in your development and growth within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out each section completely and accurately.
* Please answer questions honestly, and provide as much detail as possible.
* For select-one questions, please select the option that best describes your level of expertise.
* For select_multiple questions, please select all that apply.
* For text fields, please provide a brief description or answer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate or Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Assessment Date**: Enter the date you completed this assessment.
* **Experience Level**: Briefly describe your experience level in IT.
* **Years of IT Experience**: Enter the total number of years you have been working in the IT field.
* **Years of Cloud Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working with cloud platforms.
* **Current Role or Position**: Enter your current job title.
* **Cloud Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in Amazon Web Services (AWS).
* **Azure Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in Microsoft Azure.
* **GCP Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
* **Virtualization Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in virtualization technologies (VMware, Hyper-V).
* **Container Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in container technology (Docker, Kubernetes).
* **Scripting Languages Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in scripting languages (PowerShell, Bash).
* **Infrastructure as Code Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in infrastructure as code (Terraform, CloudFormation).
* **CI/CD Pipelines Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in CI/CD pipelines (Jenkins, GitLab).
* **Cloud Security Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in cloud security.
* **Cloud Networking Knowledge**: Select your level of knowledge in cloud networking.
* **Cloud Certifications**: Select any cloud-related certifications you hold.
* **Recent Training**: Describe any recent cloud-related training or courses you have completed.
* **Development Goals**: Describe the areas you want to develop or improve in cloud computing.
* **Additional Comments or Notes**: Provide any additional relevant information.

## Tips

* Please answer honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select the option that best describes your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out.
