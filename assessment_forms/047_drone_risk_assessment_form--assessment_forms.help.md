# Drone Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drone Risk Assessment Form aims to identify potential hazards or risks associated with drones, ensuring safe and responsible drone operations.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1.  Enter your input in the "User Input" field with any relevant details or comments.
2.  Select the risk level that best describes the drone operation.
3.  Enter the drone's serial number.
4.  Select the pilot's training level and experience.
5.  Choose the flight area from the available options.
6.  Enter the height of the drone.
7.  Select the pilot's experience level.
8.  Enter the weight of the drone.

    •   Select one or more of the following options: basic, advanced, refresher.
