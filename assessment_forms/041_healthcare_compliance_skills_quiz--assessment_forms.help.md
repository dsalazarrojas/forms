# Healthcare Compliance Skills Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess a user's level of knowledge and understanding of healthcare compliance, including their frequency of training, understanding of policies, and participation in training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the answer that best matches your current situation for each question.
2. Be sure to answer all required fields (indicated by "required: true").
3. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How often do you receive regular training on healthcare compliance?** (`training_frequency`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your frequency of receiving regular training on healthcare compliance.
	+ Please select from the options: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Rarely.
* **What is your level of understanding of healthcare policies?** (`policy_understanding`, number, required: false)
	+ This question assesses your understanding of healthcare policies.
	+ Please respond with a numerical value to indicate your understanding level.
* **Do you regularly check in with training providers?** (`training_checkin`, checkbox, required: false)
	+ This question asks if you regularly check in with your training providers.
	+ Please select "True" if you regularly check in, or "False" if you do not.
* **What is your compliance status?** (`compliance_status`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your current compliance status.
	+ Please select from the options: Active or Inactive.
* **How many training sessions do you have within the past 6 months?** (`training_sessions`, number, required: true)
	+ This question asks about the number of training sessions you have participated in within the past 6 months.
	+ Please enter a numerical value.
* **What is the date of your most recent training session?** (`training_date`, date, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the date of your most recent training session.
	+ Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **How many hours do you typically spend on training per week?** (`training_time`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the amount of time you spend on training per week.
	+ Please enter a numerical value.
* **Which training topics do you have received recently?** (`training_topics`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the training topics you have received recently.
	+ Please select from the options: Compliance, Clinical, Administrative, or Other.
* **What is the name of your most recent training provider?** (`training_provider`, text, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the name of your most recent training provider.
	+ Please enter the name of the provider.
* **What is your current compliance score?** (`compliance_score`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your current compliance score.
	+ Please enter a numerical value.
* **Do you have active or inactive training status?** (`training_status`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about your training status.
	+ Please select from the options: Active or Inactive.
* **What is the date of your next training session?** (`next_session_date`, date, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the date of your next training session.
	+ Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **What time is your next training session?** (`next_session_time`, time, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the time of your next training session.
	+ Please enter the time in the format hh:mm.
* **Where is the location of your next training session?** (`next_session_location`, text, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the location of your next training session.
	+ Please enter the location.
* **What is the topic of your next training session?** (`next_session_topic`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the topic of your next training session.
	+ Please select from the options: Compliance, Clinical, Administrative, or Other.
* **Who is your next training provider?** (`next_session_provider`, text, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the name of your next training provider.
	+ Please enter the name of the provider.
* **How many hours will you spend on the next training session?** (`next_session_hours`, number, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the amount of time you will spend on the next training session.
	+ Please enter a numerical value.
* **What is the status of your next training session?** (`next_session_status`, select_one, required: false)
	+ This question asks about the status of your next training session.
	+ Please select from the options: Active or Inactive.
