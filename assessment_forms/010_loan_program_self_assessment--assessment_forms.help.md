# Loan Program Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Loan Program Self Assessment is a form designed to help individuals evaluate their eligibility for a loan program. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you determine your loan program eligibility. Please note that this is not an application for a loan, but rather a tool to help you assess your potential eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the contact information section with your personal details.
2. Choose your employment status (Active or Inactive).
3. Select your income source (Business, Salary, or Other).
4. Indicate your income frequency (Yes or No).
5. Enter your income amount (e.g., 5000.00).
6. Enter your employment information (e.g., job title, employer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This section asks for your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you with any further questions or to discuss your loan program options.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please choose whether you are actively employed or not. This will help us determine your eligibility for the loan program.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how you earn your income. Do you earn it from a business, a salary, or some other source?
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check "Yes" if you receive income regularly, and "No" if you don't.
* **Employment Information** (`employment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant employment information, such as your job title or employer.
* **Income Amount** (`income_amount`, `number`, required: false): Enter your current income amount. This is an important factor in determining your eligibility for the loan program.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form. Inaccurate information may affect your loan program eligibility.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, you can always contact us for guidance.
