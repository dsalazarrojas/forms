# Shaft Design Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Shaft Design Quiz" form is used to collect parameters related to shaft design.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each field carefully and provide accurate information.
2. Answer each question in the relevant field.
3. Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name** (`shaft_design_quiz_form`): Enter your name.
* **What are the calculations** (`shaft_calculations`): Enter relevant calculations related to the shaft design.
* **What is the shaft design concept** (`shaft_concept_quiz`): Select one of the available options: Shaft diameter 1, Shaft diameter 2, or Shaft diameter 3.
* **What is the shaft material** (`shaft_materials`): Select one or more of the available options: Material 1, Material 2, or Material 3.
* **What is the shaft length** (`shaft_length`): Enter the length of the shaft.
* **What is the shaft diameter** (`shaft_diameter`): Enter the diameter of the shaft.
* **What is the shaft pitch** (`shaft_pitch`): Enter the pitch of the shaft.
* **What is the shaft angle** (`shaft_angle`): Select one of the available options: 45°, 90°, or -90°.
* **What is the shaft type** (`shaft_type`): Select one or more of the available options: Shaft type 1, Shaft type 2, or Shaft type 3.
* **What are the notes** (`shaft_notes`): Enter any additional notes related to the shaft design.
* **What is the email** (`shaft_email`): Enter your email address.
* **What is the phone** (`shaft_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Who is the submitter** (`shaft_submitter`): Select one of the available options: Submitter 1, Submitter 2, or Submitter 3.
* **What are the comments** (`shaft_comments`): Enter any comments related to the shaft design.
* **What is the submitter email** (`shaft_submitter_email`): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **How many shafts** (`shafts`): Enter the number of shafts.
* **What is the submitter phone** (`shaft_submitter_phone`): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **What is the submitter location** (`submitter_location`): Enter the submitter's location.
* **What are the shaft comments** (`shaft_comments`): Enter any comments related to the shaft design.
* **What are the submitter notes** (`submitter_notes`): Enter any additional notes related to the submitter.
* **What are the comments for the submitter** (`shaft_submitter_comments`): Enter any comments related to the submitter.
* **What are the submitter comments** (`submitter_comments`): Enter any comments related to the submitter.
* **What is the submitter phone** (`shaft_submitter_phone`): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **What is the submitter email** (`shaft_submitter_email`): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Where is the submitter** (`submitter_location`): Enter the submitter's location.
