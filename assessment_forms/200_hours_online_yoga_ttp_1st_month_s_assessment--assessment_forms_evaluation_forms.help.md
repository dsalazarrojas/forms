# 200 Hours Online Yoga TTP 1st Month's Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback and assessment from participants after the first month of the 200 Hours Online Yoga Training Program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete all the sections carefully and honestly.
2. Answer the questions as thoroughly as possible.
3. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
4. Make sure to review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that everything is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (2): Enter your full name as used for registration.
* **Email address** (3): Enter your preferred contact email address for course updates.
* **Phone number** (4): Include your country code if you're outside your local area (optional).
* **Date of assessment** (5): Enter the date you're completing this evaluation.
* **Age** (6): Enter your age in years.
* **Years of practice** (7): Enter the number of whole years you've practiced yoga consistently.
* **Current fitness level** (8): Choose the option that best matches your typical capacity.
* **Attendance percentage** (9): Enter the percentage of sessions you attended this month, rounded to the nearest whole percent.
* **Practice sessions per week** (10): Enter the average number of sessions you practice per week, including self-practice and live sessions.
* **Preferred practice time** (11): Enter the time of day you most often practice.
* **Primary focus areas in self-practice** (12): Select all areas you actively work on in your self-practice.
* **Primary goal for the training** (13): Choose the single most important goal for you in this course.
* **Practical technique rating** (14): Rate your execution and alignment as 1-10.
* **Understanding of theory and concepts** (15): Rate your knowledge from lectures and readings as excellent, good, satisfactory, or needs improvement.
* **Comfort with pranayama practices** (16): Rate how comfortable you are with guided breathwork.
* **Injuries, medical conditions, or contraindications** (17): List any injuries, chronic conditions, or limitations you have; leave blank if none.
* **Instructor observations and feedback** (18): Note strengths, areas to improve, and immediate next steps for instructors.
* **Additional comments or personal reflection** (19): Open feedback about the month, suggestions, or questions.
