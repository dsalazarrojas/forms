# Risk Assessment Approval Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather risk assessment and approval information for risks within your organization. It is used by authorized personnel to evaluate and approve risks, ensuring that they are properly managed and mitigated.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the risk assessment form with all required and optional fields as necessary.
2. Ensure that you are filling out the form for a risk that requires approval.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Risk Assessment Form
* **Risk Assessment Form** (`risk_assessment_form`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a description of the risk that needs to be assessed and approved. Provide a clear and concise description of the risk.

### 2. Owner
* **Owner** (`owner`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to specify the owner of the risk. This could be yourself or another authorized personnel.

### 3. Risk Severity
* **Risk Severity** (`severity`, number, required/optional): This field is for you to select the level of risk severity. Please choose a number that best represents the risk severity.

### 4. Mitigation
* **Mitigation** (`mitigation`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a description of the mitigation plan for the risk.

### 5. Mitigation Plan
* **Mitigation Plan** (`mitigation_plan`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a detailed description of the mitigation plan for the risk.

### 6. Assignee
* **Assignee** (`assignee`, select_one, required/optional): This field is for you to select the person or team that will be responsible for mitigating the risk. Choose from the list of available options.

### 7. Approve
* **Approve** (`approve`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is for you to select the approval status for the risk. Please choose from the options: Approve, Decline, or Hold.

### 8. Risk Description
* **Risk Description** (`risk_description`, note, required/optional): This field is for you to provide a detailed description of the risk.

### 9. Risk ID
* **Risk ID** (`risk_id`, number, required/optional): This field is for you to enter a unique identifier for the risk.

### 10. Risk Severity ID
* **Risk Severity ID** (`risk_severity_id`, number, required/optional): This field is for you to enter a unique identifier for the risk severity.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled out, while "optional" fields are not mandatory. However, providing as much information as possible will help in the risk assessment and approval process.
