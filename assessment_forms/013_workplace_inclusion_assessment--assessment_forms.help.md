# Workplace Inclusion Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and observations of workplace inclusion. Your responses will help us better understand the workplace environment and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Review each question carefully and provide your honest response.
2.  Answer each question in the space provided.
3.  Be as specific as possible when providing your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Assessment Purpose 
* **Assessment Purpose** (`assessment_purpose`, `text`, required): Please take a moment to read the purpose of this assessment. This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and observations of workplace inclusion.

### 1. How do you feel about your workplace environment? 
* **How do you feel about your workplace environment?** (`feeling_at_work`, `number`, required): Please rate your feelings on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Very dissatisfied" and 5 is "Very satisfied".

### 2. Describe any physical cues you observe in the workplace related to body language 
* **Describe any physical cues you observe in the workplace related to body language** (`physical_cues_body_language`, `text`, required): Please provide specific examples of behaviors or body language you have observed that make you feel included or excluded in the workplace.

### 3. Describe any physical cues you observe in the workplace related to nonverbal communications 
* **Describe any physical cues you observe in the workplace related to nonverbal communications** (`physical_cues_nonverbal_communications`, `text`, required): Please provide specific examples of nonverbal cues you have observed that affect how you feel included or excluded in the workplace.

### 4. Describe any psychological cues you observe in the workplace related to body language 
* **Describe any psychological cues you observe in the workplace related to body language** (`psychological_cues_body_language`, `text`, required): Please provide specific examples of how body language contributes to feelings of inclusion or exclusion in the workplace.

### 5. Describe any psychological cues you observe in the workplace related to nonverbal communications 
* **Describe any psychological cues you observe in the workplace related to nonverbal communications** (`psychological_cues_nonverbal_communications`, `text`, required): Please provide specific examples of how nonverbal cues affect feelings of inclusion or exclusion in the workplace.

### 6. Please provide feedback on this assessment 
* **Please provide feedback on this assessment** (`feedback`, `text`, required): Do you have any suggestions or comments about the assessment process or the questions asked?

### 7. What are your next steps after completing this assessment? 
* **What are your next steps after completing this assessment?** (`next_steps`, `text`, required): Please describe any actions you plan to take or changes you hope to see in the workplace as a result of this assessment.

### 8. Create an action plan to address any issues you've identified 
* **Create an action plan to address any issues you've identified** (`action_plan`, `text`, required): Based on your experiences and observations, please suggest specific actions that can be taken to improve the workplace environment.
