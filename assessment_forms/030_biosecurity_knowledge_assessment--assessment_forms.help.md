# Biosecurity Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Biosecurity Knowledge Assessment is a tool used to evaluate an individual's knowledge and confidence in biosecurity principles and practices. It is designed to be completed by employees in biosecure environments and is used to identify areas where additional training is required.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each question carefully and select the best answer based on your knowledge and experience.
2. Answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the "None - Feel Fully Trained" option when you are confident in your knowledge of a particular topic.
4. Select all relevant areas where you require additional training.
