# Gym Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a client's health and fitness goals, medical conditions, and contact details. It's intended for use in a gym or fitness center to assess a client's readiness for a workout program or to gather information for a trainer-client consultation. Please ensure you're completing this form accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the options that apply to your current health and fitness status.
2. Answer all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) or indicated as required.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` (e.g., 06/12/1999).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Health
* **Client Health** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have any health concerns or conditions that might impact your ability to exercise.
	+ Choose "No" if you don't have any health concerns or conditions.

### Fitness Level
* **Fitness Level** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Select "High" if you're an experienced athlete or have a high level of fitness.
	+ Select "Low" if you're a beginner or have a lower level of fitness.

### Goals
* **Goals** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" for each goal that applies to you, such as:
		- Weight loss
		- Weight gain
		- Increased strength
		- Increased flexibility
		- Improved overall fitness

### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" for each medical condition you're experiencing or have a history of, such as:
		- Diabetes
		- Hypertension
		- Heart conditions
		- Other

### Height
* **Height** (number, required: false):
	+ Enter your height in centimeters (cm).

### Weight
* **Weight** (number, required: false):
	+ Enter your weight in kilograms (kg).

### Age
* **Age** (number, required: false):
	+ Enter your age.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (date, required: false):
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

### Medical Notes
* **Medical Notes** (note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional medical notes or concerns that might impact your fitness program.

### Medical Notes 2
* **Medical Notes 2** (note, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional medical notes or concerns that might impact your fitness program.

### Client Goals
* **Client Goals** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" for each goal you have for your fitness program, such as:
		- Weight loss
		- Weight gain
		- Increased strength
		- Improved overall fitness

### Trainer Goals
* **Trainer Goals** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" for each goal you have for your trainer, such as:
		- To help with weight loss
		- To improve overall fitness

### Email
* **Email** (email, required: false):
	+ Enter your email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required: false):
	+ Enter your phone number.

This form is designed to be completed by clients or prospective clients of a gym or fitness center. Please ensure you complete all required fields accurately and honestly to help your trainer understand your needs and goals for your fitness program.
