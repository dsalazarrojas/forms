# Automated Test Creation and Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help create and evaluate assessment tests. It collects information about the test, such as its name, description, and type, as well as the test settings and assessment criteria. The form also collects information about the creator and test taker, including their name, email, and organization. The form is used to generate reports and track evaluation metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the test details: **Test Name**, **Test Description**, **Test Type**, and **Subject Area**.
2.  Enter the **Creator Information**, including your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**.
3.  Set the test settings: **Total Questions**, **Time Limit**, and **Passing Score**.
4.  Choose the **Test Type** from the available options (e.g., Knowledge Assessment, Skills Test, Certification Exam, etc.).
5.  Decide on the **Randomize Questions** and **Show Correct Answers** settings.
6.  Select the types of **Question Types** to include in the test.
7.  Choose the **Difficulty Level** of the test.
8.  Set the **Topic Distribution** (optional).
9.  Fill in the **Test Content** (questions and answers) for up to 5 questions.
10. Choose the **Grading Method** and **Feedback Type**.
11. Set the **Retake Policy** and **Maximum Attempts** (if applicable).
12. Choose the **Test Availability** dates.
13. Enter the **Target Audience** and **Prerequisites** (if applicable).
14. Select the **Evaluation Metrics** to track.
15. Choose the report type (e.g., Individual, Summary, or None).
16. Add any **Additional Instructions** or **Creator Signature**.
17. Finally, set the **Creation Date**.
