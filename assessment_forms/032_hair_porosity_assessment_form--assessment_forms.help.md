# Hair Porosity Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Porosity Assessment Form is a tool used to evaluate the porosity of a client's hair. Porosity refers to the size and distribution of hair follicles on the scalp. This form is designed to collect information on various aspects of a client's hair, including hair type, porosity level, length, color, condition, and follicle characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the first page to begin the assessment process.
2. On the first page, select the relevant information for your hair assessment, including whether you have client information (Client Info), your hair type (Hair Type), and porosity level (Porosity Level).
3. On the second page, select your hair length (Hair Length) and describe the condition of your hair (Hair Condition).
4. On the third page, select the size of your hair follicles (Hair Follicle Size) and their distribution (Hair Follicle Distribution).
5. On the fourth page, assess your porosity score (Porosity Score) and select the porosity scale (Porosity Scale).
6. On the final page, enter any additional comments or observations about your hair (Hair Breakage and Form End).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Start** (`form_start`, text, optional): This is the first page of the form, where you can begin the assessment process.
- **Client Info** (`client_info`, select multiple, optional): If you have client information, please select it here. This can include details such as the client's name, address, and contact information.
- **Hair Type** (`hair_type`, select one, optional): Please select your hair type from the provided options: Fine, Medium, or Coarse.
- **Porosity Level** (`porosity_level`, select one, optional): Please select your porosity level from the provided options: 0, 10, 20, 30, 40, or 50.
- **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, select one, optional): Please select your hair length from the provided options: 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4.
- **Hair Color** (`hair_color`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide more specific information about your hair color, please enter it here.
- **Porosity Location** (`porosity_location`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide more specific information about the location of your porosity, please enter it here.
- **Hair Condition** (`hair_condition`, text, optional): Please describe the condition of your hair, such as its texture, shine, or any specific characteristics.
- **Hair Porosity** (`hair_porosity`, number, optional): If you have a specific measurement or value for your hair porosity, you can enter it here.
- **Porosity Scale** (`porosity_scale`, select one, optional): Please select a porosity scale from the provided options: Low, Medium, or High.
- **Porosity Score** (`porosity_score`, number, optional): If you have a specific score or measurement for your porosity, you can enter it here.
- **Hair Follicle Size** (`hair_follicle_size`, select one, optional): Please select the size of your hair follicles from the provided options: Small, Medium, or Large.
- **Hair Follicle Distribution** (`hair_follicle_distribution`, select one, optional): Please select the distribution of your hair follicles from the provided options: Even, Uneven, or Mixed.
- **Hair Breakage** (`hair_breakage`, number, optional): If you have information about hair breakage or shedding, you can enter it here.
- **Form End** (`form_end`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or observations about your hair, please enter them here.
