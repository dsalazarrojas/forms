# Supplier Credit Assessment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Credit Assessment Request Form is used to assess the creditworthiness of a supplier and determine the terms and payment schedule for a potential partnership or business deal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title**: This is the title of the form.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you are assessing for credit.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the company.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the company.
* **City**: Enter the city where the company is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where the company is located.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the company.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Method**: Select how the contact person would like to be contacted (phone, email, or in person).
* **Payment Terms**: Choose the payment terms (30 days, 45 days, 60 days, or 90 days).
* **Payment Frequency**: Determine how often payments will be made (weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannually, or annually).
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method (credit card, bank transfer, cash, or check).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **Credit Limit**: Enter the credit limit.
* **Credit Term**: Choose the credit term (30 days, 60 days, 90 days, or 180 days).
* **Credit Frequency**: Determine how often credits will be issued (weekly, biweekly, monthly, quarterly, semiannually, or annually).
* **Payment Schedule**: Set the due date for payment.
* **Due Date**: Set the due date for payment.
* **Payment Terms Notes**: Enter any notes or comments regarding the payment terms.
* **Credit Status**: Determine the credit status (active, inactive, or unknown).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or notes.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
