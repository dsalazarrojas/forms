# Professional Development Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals evaluate their professional development progress, identify areas of improvement, and set goals for personal and professional growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your job title in the job title field.
4. Enter your manager's name in the manager's name field.
5. Evaluate your skills in the skills evaluation field by selecting from the provided options.
6. Describe your strengths in the strengths field.
7. Identify areas for improvement in the areas of improvement field.
8. Set goals and objectives in the goals and objectives field.
9. Specify your training needs in the training needs field.
10. Describe your next steps for improvement in the next steps field.
11. Enter the review date in the review date field.
11. Enter the review by field, which is the name of the person who conducted the review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (`first_name`, Text, required: false):**
Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

* **Last Name (`last_name`, Text, required: false):**
Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

* **Job Title (`job_title`, Text, required: false):**
Enter your current job title or position.

* **Manager Name (`manager_name`, Text, required: false):**
Enter the name of your manager or supervisor.

* **Skills Evaluation (`skills_evaluation`, Select Multiple, required: false):**
Choose how you evaluate your skills in the following areas:
	+ Strong
	+ Weak
	+ Average
	+ Below Average

* **Strengths (`strengths`, Text, required: false):**
Describe your strengths and areas where you excel.

* **Areas of Improvement (`areas_of_improvement`, Text, required: false):**
Identify areas where you need to improve.

* **Goals and Objectives (`goals_and_objectives`, Text, required: false):**
Set specific and achievable goals and objectives for yourself.

* **Training Needs (`training_needs`, Text, required: false):**
Specify what you need to improve your skills and performance.

* **Next Steps (`next_steps`, Text, required: false):**
Describe your next steps for improvement and growth.

* **Review Date (`review_date`, Date, required: false):**
Enter the date of the review.

* **Review By (`review_by`, Text, required: false):**
Enter the name of the person who conducted the review.

## Tips
* This form is for personal and professional development purposes only, and the information you provide will be used for evaluation and goal setting.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Take your time to reflect on your strengths, weaknesses, and areas of improvement.
* This form is not a performance evaluation or evaluation of your worth as an employee.
