# Medical Fitness For Air Travel Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess a traveler's medical fitness for air travel. It gathers information on travel details, medical clearance, and any potential health risks associated with the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

*   Flight details: Enter the flight number, departure and arrival cities, and departure date.
*   Travel date: Enter the date of your travel.
*   Travel destination: Select the city you are traveling to.
*   Travel reason: Enter the purpose of your trip.
*   Medical counsel: Indicate if you have any medical condition that requires special attention during travel.
*   Physician clearance: Check if you have received clearance from your physician.
*   Travel companion: Enter the name and relationship of the person accompanying you.
*   Emergency contact name: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   Emergency contact relationship: Describe the relationship with the emergency contact person.
*   Patient medical info: Provide any medical information related to your condition.
*   Air travel clearance: Check if you are cleared for air travel.
*   Travel clearance note: Enter any additional notes or comments related to travel clearance.
*   Medical notes: Enter any additional medical information or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the flight number, departure and arrival cities, and departure date.
*   **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your travel.
*   **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the city you are traveling to.
*   **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the purpose of your trip.
*   **Medical Counsel** (`medical_counsel`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate if you have any medical condition that requires special attention during travel.
*   **Physician Clearance** (`physician_clearance`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if you have received clearance from your physician.
*   **Travel Companion** (`travel_companion`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and relationship of the person accompanying you.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the relationship with the emergency contact person.
*   **Patient Medical Info** (`patient_medical_info`, `note`, required/optional): Provide any medical information related to your condition.
*   **Air Travel Clearance** (`airtravel_clearance`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Check if you are cleared for air travel.
*   **Travel Clearance Note** (`travel_clearance_note`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to travel clearance.
*   **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional medical information or comments.

## Tips

*   Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Consult local medical guidelines and travel regulations for more detailed information.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting.
