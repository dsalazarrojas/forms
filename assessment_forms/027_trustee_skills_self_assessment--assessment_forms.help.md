# Trustee Skills Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trustee Skills Self Assessment form is designed to help trustees evaluate their skills and competencies in order to identify areas where they may need additional training or support. This form is meant to be completed by the trustee themselves and will help them identify their own strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, taking into account your own skills and competencies.
3. Provide any additional information or comments in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trustee Skills Self Assessment Form** (`trustee_skills_self_assessment_form`, text, optional): This field is the main question for this form, where you can provide a brief description of your skills and competencies.
* **Board Development Insights** (`board_development_insights`, text, optional): In this field, describe your insights on board development, including any challenges you've faced and how you've overcome them.
* **Self-Assessment** (`self_assessment`, select_multiple, optional): Select the self-assessment options that apply to you, such as "Self-Assessment 1", "Self-Assessment 2", or "Self-Assessment 3".
* **Training Priorities** (`training_priorities`, select_one, optional): Choose the priority that best describes your training needs, such as "Priority 1", "Priority 2", or "Priority 3".
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, time, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about your evaluation process, but it's not a formal evaluation tool.
* **Competencies** (`competencies`, text, optional): Describe your competencies, including any relevant experience or qualifications.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes that you'd like to add.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have any questions or need further support, you can provide your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have any questions or need further support, you can provide your phone number here.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* Take your time to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for clarification or additional support.
* Remember, this form is for your personal development and improvement, so don't worry about being perfect.
