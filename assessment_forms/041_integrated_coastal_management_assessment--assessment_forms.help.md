# Integrated Coastal Management Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on coastal management assessments. Your input will help us improve this process and better serve your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields, marked with *.
2. Choose your options from the select_one, select_multiple, and select_one_numeric fields.
3. Select multiple options from the select_multiple fields.
4. Enter your email and phone number to help us contact you if needed.
5. Provide any additional comments in the text area.
6. Submit your feedback when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Integrated Coastal Management Assessment**: This is the title of the form, feel free to enter any information you'd like.
* **What is this form for**: Briefly explain why you are using this form.
* **Title**: Enter a title for your assessment.
* **Description**: Provide a short description of your assessment.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are completing this form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are completing this form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your assessment.
* **Select One**: Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Select One Numeric**: Choose one numeric option from the list.
* **Select Multiple Numeric**: Choose multiple numeric options from the list.
* **Checkbox**: Choose one or multiple options from the list.
* **Checkbox Multiple**: Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Yes/No**: Choose yes or no from the list.
* **Yes/No Multiple**: Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Checkbox Multiple Yes/No**: Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your feedback.
