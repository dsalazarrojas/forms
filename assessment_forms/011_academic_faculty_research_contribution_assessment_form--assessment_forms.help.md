# Academic Faculty Research Contribution Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Faculty Research Contribution Assessment Form is designed to collect information on the research contributions of academic faculty members, including their publications, grant activities, impact assessment, collaborative projects, supervision of graduate students, research development needs, and assessor comments.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
2. **Department/Unit**: Enter your departmental affiliation.
3. **Number of Publications**: Enter the total number of publications you have made during the specified period.
4. **Grant Activity and Funding Secured**: Provide details on any grants you have secured, including their totals and major awards.
5. **Impact Assessment of Research**: Summarize the impact of your research, including any citations, policy influence, or uptake.
6. **Collaborative Projects and Partnerships**: List any key collaborations you have undertaken.
7. **Supervision of Graduate Students**: Enter the number of graduate students you have supervised during the specified period.
8. **Research Development Needs**: Identify any training, infrastructure, or support you require for your research.
9. **Assessor Comments**: Provide any comments or recommendations from assessors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Name** (`faculty_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Department or Unit** (`department`, text, optional): Provide your departmental affiliation.
* **Number of Publications** (`research_output_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of publications you have made during the specified period.
* **Grant Activity and Funding Secured** (`grant_activity`, text, optional): Provide details on any grants you have secured, including their totals and major awards.
* **Impact Assessment of Research** (`impact_assessment`, text, required): Summarize the impact of your research, including any citations, policy influence, or uptake.
* **Collaborative Projects and Partnerships** (`collaborative_projects`, text, optional): List any key collaborations you have undertaken.
* **Supervision of Graduate Students** (`supervision_activity`, number, optional): Enter the number of graduate students you have supervised during the specified period.
* **Research Development Needs** (`development_needs`, text, optional): Identify any training, infrastructure, or support you require for your research.
* **Assessor Comments** (`assessor_comments`, text, required): Provide any comments or recommendations from assessors.
