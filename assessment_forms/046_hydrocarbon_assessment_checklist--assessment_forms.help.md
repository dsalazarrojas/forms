# Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hydrocarbon Assessment Checklist is a comprehensive tool used to evaluate the hydrocarbon management practices and procedures of a storage facility. This form ensures that the facility is meeting industry standards and guidelines for hydrocarbon storage and management.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the storage facility's information and hydrocarbon storage type.
2. Record the spill response plan and procedures in place.
3. Evaluate the staff training and frequency of training sessions.
4. Note any observations or comments about the facility's hydrocarbon management practices.
5. Record any corrective actions taken or planned.
6. Schedule site visits and record the frequency of site visits.
7. Record the training frequency for facility staff.
8. Record the review date and the date the form was reviewed.
9. Record the name and position of the person who reviewed the form.
10. Record the phone number and email of the person who reviewed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storage Facility Info** (Storage Facility Info, text, required): This section is for entering the storage facility's information, including name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Hydrocarbon Storage** (Hydrocarbon Storage, select_one, required): Select the type of hydrocarbon stored at the facility, such as Oil, Gas, or Both.
* **Spill Response** (Spill Response, text, required): Describe the spill response plan and procedures in place at the facility.
* **Spill Response Plan** (Spill Response Plan, text, required): Describe any specific actions or procedures to be taken in the event of a spill.
* **Staff Training** (Staff Training, select_multiple, required): Select the types of training provided to facility staff, such as Emergency Response and Regular Training.
* **Observations** (Observations, note, required): Note any observations or comments about the facility's hydrocarbon management practices.
* **Corrective Actions** (Corrective Actions, text, required): Record any corrective actions taken or planned to address any identified issues.
* **Site Visits** (Site Visits, date, required): Schedule and record site visits for the facility.
* **Site Visit Frequency** (Site Visit Frequency, number, required): Record the frequency of site visits.
* **Training Frequency** (Training Frequency, number, required): Record the frequency of training sessions for facility staff.
* **Review Date** (Review Date, date, required): Record the date the form was reviewed.
* **Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By** (Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By, text, required): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form.
* **Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed On** (Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed On, time, required): Record the date and time the form was reviewed.
* **Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Name** (Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Name, text, required): Enter the name of the person who reviewed the form.
* **Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Position** (Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Position, text, required): Enter the position of the person who reviewed the form.
* **Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Phone** (Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person who reviewed the form.
* **Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Email** (Hydrocarbon Assessment Form Reviewed By Email, email, required): Enter the email of the person who reviewed the form.

Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a comprehensive review of your facility's hydrocarbon management practices.
