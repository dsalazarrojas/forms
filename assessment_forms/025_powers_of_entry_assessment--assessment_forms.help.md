# powersOfEntryAssessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The powersOfEntryAssessment form is designed to assess the power of entry, which is the process of evaluating an entry in a given site or location. This form is used to gather information about the entry process, including the date and time of entry, decision to enter, reasons for entering or not, and any issues encountered during the entry process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "site_info" field with any relevant details about the site or location.
2. Enter the date and time of entry in the "site_entry_date" and "site_entry_time" fields, respectively.
3. Select the "entry_decision" based on your decision to enter, which can be Decision to Enter, Decision to Not Enter, or Decision to Enter but with Restrictions.
4. Provide any justification or reason for your decision in the "entry_justification" field, if applicable.
5. If there are any issues encountered during the entry process, select them from the list in the "site_issues" field.
6. Enter any comments from the inspector in the "inspector_comments" field, if applicable.
7. Enter any comments from the reviewer in the "reviewer_comments" field, if applicable.
8. Enter the date and time of the entry decision in the "entry_decision_date" and "entry_decision_time" fields, respectively.
9. Provide any additional comments or justification for the entry decision in the "entry_justification_comments" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **site_info** (text, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the site or location.
- **site_entry_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date of entry.
- **site_entry_time** (time, required: false): Enter the time of entry.
- **entry_decision** (select_one, required: false): Select your decision to enter, which can be Decision to Enter, Decision to Not Enter, or Decision to Enter but with Restrictions.
- **entry_justification** (note, required: false): Provide any justification or reason for your decision in this field, if applicable.
- **site_issues** (select_multiple, required: false): If there are any issues encountered during the entry process, select them from the list.
- **inspector_comments** (text, required: false): Enter any comments from the inspector, if applicable.
- **reviewer_comments** (text, required: false): Enter any comments from the reviewer, if applicable.
- **entry_decision_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the entry decision.
- **entry_decision_time** (time, required: false): Enter the time of the entry decision.
- **entry_justification_comments** (note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or justification for the entry decision, if applicable.

## Tips
- Be as specific and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, select them from the list in the "site_issues" field.
- If you have any comments or justification, provide them in the corresponding fields.
- Make sure to enter the date and time of entry and entry decision accurately.
