# Individual Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Individual Self Assessment Form is a tool used to collect personal information and performance evaluations from individuals. It helps users gauge their progress and performance over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Job Title.
6. Enter your Department name.
7. Enter your Years of Service.
8. Select your Performance Rating 1.
9. Select your Performance Rating 2.
10. Select your Performance Rating 3.
11. Enter the Date of evaluation.
12. Enter the Time of evaluation.
13. Add any additional comments or notes about your performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. 
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your department.
* **Years of Service**: Enter the number of years you have been with the company.
* **Performance Rating 1**: Select your performance rating for one of your tasks or objectives.
* **Performance Rating 2**: Select your performance rating for another one of your tasks or objectives.
* **Performance Rating 3**: Select your performance rating for another aspect of your performance.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your evaluation.
* **Time**: Enter the time of your evaluation.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your performance.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field.
* Select the correct performance ratings that reflect your actual performance.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
