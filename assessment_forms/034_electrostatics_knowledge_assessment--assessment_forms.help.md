# Electrostatics Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess knowledge in electrostatics, providing a comprehensive evaluation of the student or instructor's understanding of the subject. The assessment includes multiple questions, including questions on the primary difference between conductors and insulators, the force between charged particles, and the difference between insulators and conductors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all the questions on the first page, which includes electrostatics_question_1, electrostatics_question_2, and electrostatics_question_3.
2. Enter your details on the second page, including your name, email address, institution (if applicable), and course details.
3. On the third page, enter your instructor's details, including their name, email address, and title.
4. Review and submit the form to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Electrostatics Knowledge Assessment** (`electrostatics_knowledge_assessment`): A text field where you can enter any comments or notes about the assessment.
* **What is the primary difference between conductors and insulators?** (`electrostatics_question_1`): Describe the main difference between conductors and insulators.
* **What is the force between two charged particles?** (`electrostatics_question_2`): Describe the force between two charged particles.
* **How do insulators and conductors differ?** (`electrostatics_question_3`): Describe the difference between insulators and conductors.
* **Electrostatics Knowledge Assessment Score** (`electrostatics_knowledge_assessment_score`): Enter a score out of 10 to evaluate your knowledge in electrostatics.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`): Enter your name as an instructor.
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`): Enter your email address as an instructor.
* **Instructor Institution** (`instructor_institution`): Enter your institution's name (if applicable).
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`): Enter your email address as a student.
* **Student Institution** (`student_institution`): Enter your institution's name (if applicable).
* **Student Major** (`student_major`): Enter your major or field of study.
* **Student Year** (`student_year`): Enter your current year of study.
* **Course** (`student_course`): Enter the name of the course you are taking.
* **Electrostatics Course** (`electrostatics_course`): Enter the name of the electrostatics course you are taking.
* **Instructor Title** (`instructor_title`): Enter your title as an instructor.
* **Comments** (`electrostatics_comments`): Enter any comments or notes about the assessment.

Note that fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be completed before submitting the form.
