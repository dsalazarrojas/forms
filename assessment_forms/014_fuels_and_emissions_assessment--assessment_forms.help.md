# Fuels And Emissions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuels And Emissions Assessment form is used to evaluate the fuel consumption and emissions of a vehicle or equipment. It is likely used by environmentalists, researchers, or regulatory bodies to assess the impact of different types of fuel on the environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The form is divided into several pages, each with its own set of questions.
2. Please select the relevant page (or question group) for the information you are reporting.
3. Answer each question accurately and honestly.
4. Ensure that you fill in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Fuel Consumption (Page 1)
* **Fuel Consumption** (`fuel_consumption`, text, required: false): Please report the amount of fuel consumed by the vehicle or equipment.

* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, select_one, required: false): Please select the type of fuel used by the vehicle or equipment:
	+ 'Yes'
	+ 'No'

* **Emissions Rating** (`emissions_rating`, select_multiple, required: false): Please select the level of emissions produced by the vehicle or equipment:
	+ Good
	+ Fair

* **CO2 Emissions** (`co2_emissions`, number, required: false): Please report the CO2 emissions produced by the vehicle or equipment.

* **CO2 Emissions per Unit** (`co2_emissions_per_unit`, number, required: false): Please report the CO2 emissions per unit of fuel consumed by the vehicle or equipment.

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult the relevant documentation or experts in the field.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or support staff.
