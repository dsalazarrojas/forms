# weekly_spelling_quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect student performance data for weekly spelling quizzes. It is meant to be used to assess the spelling proficiency of students in a class, providing a snapshot of their performance on each quiz question. The data collected will aid in tracking progress, identifying areas of improvement, and informing instruction to aid in the development of students' spelling skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are on the correct form, "weekly_spelling_quiz".
2. Fill in your name and class name as per the "Student Name" and "Class Name" fields. These fields are **text** input fields.
3. Enter the quiz date in the "Quiz Date" field. Select the date when the quiz was administered to the students.
4. Enter the scores for each student in the corresponding "Score 1 1", "Score 1 2", "Score 2 1", "Score 2 2", "Score 3 1", and "Score 3 2" fields. These fields are **number** input fields.
5. Finally, ensure to calculate the "Total Score" by adding up all the individual scores entered in the previous fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the student for whom you are recording the spelling performance data.
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the class where the quiz was administered.
* **Quiz Date** (`quiz_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the quiz was administered to the students. The format should be in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Score 1 1** (`score_1_1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the score of student for question 1.
* **Score 1 2** (`score_1_2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the score of student for question 1.
* **Score 2 1** (`score_2_1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the score of student for question 2.
* **Score 2 2** (`score_2_2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the score of student for question 2.
* **Score 3 1** (`score_3_1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the score of student for question 3.
* **Score 3 2** (`score_3_2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the score of student for question 3.
* **Total Score** (`total`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the sum of all individual scores.

## Tips
- Ensure to calculate the "Total Score" accurately by adding up all individual scores.
- Verify that the "Quiz Date" is entered in the correct format, i.e., YYYY-MM-DD.
