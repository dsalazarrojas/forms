# Tech Product Development Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess various aspects of a tech product development project, aiming to evaluate its overall health and effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select all the project initiatives that apply to your project.
2.  Enter a brief description of your project's scope.
3.  Choose your project's readiness level (optional).
4.  Indicate the level of collaboration within your team (optional).
5.  Select the project lead and manager (if applicable).
6.  Choose the current project status (optional).
7.  Enter the expected submission time for your project.
8.  Enter your project's name and description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Initiatives**: Select all the initiatives that apply to your project from the list below.
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Project Scope**: Enter a brief description of your project's goals and objectives.
* **Readiness Level**: Choose the level of readiness for your project. This is an optional field, but it can help to better understand your project's progress.
	+ 1-5
	+ 6-10
	+ 11-20
	+ 21+
* **Team Collaboration**: Indicate the level of collaboration within your team. This is an optional field, but it can help to better understand how your team works together.
	+ None
	+ High
	+ Medium
	+ Low
* **Project Lead**: Select the person leading your project (if applicable).
	+ None
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Other
* **Project Manager**: Select the project manager (if applicable).
	+ None
	+ John
	+ Jane
	+ Other
* **Project Status**: Choose the current status of your project (optional).
	+ In Progress
	+ On Hold
	+ Closed
	+ Cancelled
* **Submission Time**: Enter the expected time for your project's submission.
* **Project Name**: Enter your project's name.
* **Project Description**: Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Team Size**: Select the size of your team.
	+ 1-5
	+ 6-10
	+ 11-20
	+ 21+
