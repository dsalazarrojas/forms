# Business Structure Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the current state of your organization's structure, helping you to identify areas that need improvement and suggest potential changes to enhance your organization's overall performance and employee satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer all questions as honestly as possible, using the best information available.
2. Select one answer for select-one questions.
3. Select all that apply for select-multiple questions.
4. Use the provided hints for guidance.
5. Reach out to your supervisor or HR representative if you need clarification on any question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (1): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your official contact email.
* **Company Name** (3): Enter your organization or department name.
* **Your Role or Title** (4): Enter your position in the organization.
* **How long have you been with the company?** (5): Enter the number of years you've been with the company.
* **Your Organizational Level** (6): Choose your current position within the organization:
	+ Executive or C-suite
	+ Senior management
	+ Middle management
	+ Team lead or supervisor
	+ Individual contributor
	+ Other
* **How clearly defined is the current organizational structure?** (7): Choose the level of clarity:
	+ Very clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Very unclear
* **Are reporting relationships well understood?** (8): Choose the level of understanding:
	+ Completely understood
	+ Mostly understood
	+ Partially understood
	+ Poorly understood
	+ Not understood at all
* **How effective is the current structure?** (9): Choose the level of effectiveness:
	+ Very effective
	+ Effective
	+ Neutral
	+ Ineffective
	+ Very ineffective
* **What are the main structural issues?** (10): Select all that apply:
	+ Unclear roles and responsibilities
	+ Inefficient decision-making
	+ Silos between departments
	+ Overlapping responsibilities
	+ Too many management layers
	+ Lack of accountability
	+ Poor communication channels
	+ No significant issues
* **How quickly are decisions made?** (11): Choose the speed:
	+ Too fast - lacks proper review
	+ Appropriately fast
	+ Too slow - bureaucratic
	+ Varies widely
* **How effective is internal communication?** (12): Choose the level of effectiveness:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **How well do teams collaborate across departments?** (13): Choose the level of collaboration:
	+ Excellent collaboration
	+ Good collaboration
	+ Some collaboration
	+ Limited collaboration
	+ No collaboration
* **Are you appropriately managed?** (14): Choose the level of management:
	+ Over-managed
	+ Appropriately managed
	+ Under-managed
	+ No clear management
* **Is accountability clear in your area?** (15): Choose the level of accountability:
	+ Very clear
	+ Clear
	+ Somewhat clear
	+ Unclear
	+ Not at all clear
* **What structural improvements would you recommend?** (16): Enter any suggestions for improvement.
* **What concerns do you have about potential restructuring?** (17): Enter any concerns.
* **How ready is the organization for change?** (18): Choose the level of readiness:
	+ Very ready
	+ Ready
	+ Somewhat ready
	+ Not very ready
	+ Not ready
* **How would you rate current employee morale?** (19): Choose the level of morale:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ Very poor
* **Any additional feedback or suggestions?** (20): Enter any final thoughts or recommendations.
