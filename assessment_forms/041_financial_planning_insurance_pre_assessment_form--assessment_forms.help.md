# financial_planning_insurance_pre_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers for financial planning and insurance assessment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form** (`form_header`, text, optional): Enter the title of the form, usually "Financial Planning Insurance Pre-Assessment Form".
* **Customer** (`customer_info`, text, optional): Provide your name or the name of the customer.
* **Contact** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Insurance** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): Provide information about the customer's insurance needs or concerns.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment, usually the date you plan to conduct the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators.
