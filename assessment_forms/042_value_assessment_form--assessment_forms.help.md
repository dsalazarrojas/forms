# Value Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Value Assessment Form is designed to gather information on the value of a rental property. It's essential to use this form for accurate and fair assessment of the property's worth.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form for each property separately.
2.  Ensure you've selected all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
3.  Fill out fields with accurate and relevant information.
4.  Review and edit your answers before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rent Period** (`rent_period`, number, required/optional): Select the period of rent, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Rent Type** (`rent_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of rent, such as monthly or weekly.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of rent for the selected period.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often the rent is paid, such as daily or yearly.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of property, such as House or Apartment.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the property, such as Active or Inactive.
* **Unit ID** (`unit_id`, number, optional): Enter the identification number for the property unit.
* **Unit Value** (`unit_value`, select_multiple, optional): Select the value of the unit, such as True or False.
* **Value Change** (`value_change`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how the unit value has changed, such as Increase or Decrease.
* **Reason Change** (`reason_change`, select_multiple, optional): Select the reason for the change, such as Rent increase or Market change.
* **Value Amount** (`value_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of value change.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the property.
* **Date of Assessment** (`date_assessment`, date, optional): Enter the date of assessment.
* **Time of Assessment** (`time_of_assessment`, time, optional): Select the time of assessment.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or concerns from the user.
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or concerns from the manager.
* **Unit Status** (`unit_status`, note, optional): Add any additional information about the unit status.
* **Unit Address** (`unit_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the unit.
