# Skill Assessment Form PDS - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for skill assessment and evaluation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Candidate Information section with the candidate's details.
2. Select all relevant skills from the Skills List in the Skills Assessed section.
3. Complete the Work Experience section with the candidate's work experience details.
4. Fill out the Reviewer Information section with the reviewer's details.
5. Provide any additional comments in the Assessment Comments section.
6. Finally, submit the form to complete the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`Candidate Information`, text, required): Enter the candidate's name, title, and other relevant contact information.
* **Skills List** (`Skills List`, select_multiple, optional): Select all skills that the candidate has demonstrated or possesses.
* **Assessment Scale** (`Assessment Scale`, number, required): Rate the candidate's skills on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Hands-on Assessment Scale** (`Hands-on Assessment Scale`, number, required): Rate the candidate's hands-on skills on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being low and 10 being high.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the candidate's skills and qualifications.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, date, required): Enter the candidate's start and end dates of work experience.
* **Work Experience To** (`Work Experience To`, date, required): Enter the end date of the work experience.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter the candidate's contact number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the candidate's email.
* **Skills Assessed** (`Skills Assessed`, select_one, required): Indicate if the candidate's skills were assessed during the evaluation.
* **Skills Assessed List** (`Skills Assessed List`, select_multiple, required): Select all skills that were assessed during the evaluation.
* **Assessment Comments** (`Assessment Comments`, text, required): Provide comments about the candidate's skills and qualifications.
* **Assessment Scale Averaged** (`Assessment Scale Averaged`, number, required): Enter the average rating of the candidate's skills.
* **Hands-on Assessment Scale Averaged** (`Hands-on Assessment Scale Averaged`, number, required): Enter the average rating of the candidate's hands-on skills.
* **Skills Assessed Averaged** (`Skills Assessed Averaged`, select_one, required): Indicate if the candidate's skills were averaged during the evaluation.
* **Skills Assessed List Averaged** (`Skills Assessed List Averaged`, select_multiple, required): Select all skills that were averaged during the evaluation.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`Additional Comments 2`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the candidate's skills and qualifications.
* **Review Date** (`Review Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
* **Reviewer** (`Reviewer`, text, required): Enter the reviewer's name.
* **Reviewer Title** (`Reviewer Title`, text, required): Enter the reviewer's title.
* **Reviewer Department** (`Reviewer Department`, text, required): Enter the reviewer's department.
* **Additional Comments 2 2** (`Additional Comments 2 2`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the candidate's skills and qualifications.
* **Skills Assessment Form PDS** (`Skills Assessment Form PDS`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the candidate's skills and qualifications.
* **Work Experience From** (`Work Experience From`, date, required): Enter the start date of the work experience.
* **Work Experience To 2** (`Work Experience To 2`, date, required): Enter the end date of the work experience.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and honest information about the candidate's skills and qualifications.
- Use the skills assessment scale to rate the candidate's skills fairly and consistently.
- Provide detailed comments about the candidate's skills and qualifications in the Assessment Comments section.
- Use the averaged ratings to get an overall assessment of the candidate's skills.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
