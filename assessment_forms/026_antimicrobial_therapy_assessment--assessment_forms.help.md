# Antimicrobial Therapy Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Antimicrobial Therapy Assessment form is designed to collect information about a patient's antimicrobial therapy, including details about the patient's demographics, infection type, therapy regimen, and clinical outcomes. This form is typically used by healthcare professionals to assess the appropriateness and effectiveness of antimicrobial therapy.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's medical record number in the Patient ID field.
2. Enter the patient's age in years in the Patient Age field.
3. Select the patient's gender in the Patient Gender field.
4. Enter the assessment date in the Assessment Date field.
5. Enter the facility name (e.g., hospital or clinic) in the Facility Name field.
6. Select the unit or department where the patient is located in the Unit or Department field.
7. Enter the admitting diagnosis in the Admitting Diagnosis field.
8. Select the type of infection in the Type of Infection field.
9. Enter the specific infection site (if applicable) in the Specific Infection Site field.
10. List the current antibiotics regimen in the Current Antibiotic Regimen field.
11. Enter the date therapy started in the Therapy Start Date field.
12. Select whether the indication is documented in the Indication Documented field.
13. Select whether cultures were obtained before starting antibiotics in the Cultures Obtained field.
14. Select whether culture results are available in the Culture Results field.
15. Enter the name of the pathogen (if known) in the Organism Identified field.
16. Select whether susceptibility testing was done in the Susceptibility Testing Done field.
17. Select whether the therapy is appropriate based on guidelines in the Drug Selection Appropriateness field.
18. Select whether the dosing is appropriate in the Dosing Appropriateness field.
19. Select whether renal dose adjustment was made (if needed) in the Renal Dose Adjustment field.
20. Select whether the route of therapy is appropriate in the Route Appropriateness field.
21. Select whether the patient can switch from IV to oral therapy in the IV to Oral Candidate field.
22. Select whether the duration of therapy is appropriate in the Duration Assessment field.
23. Enter the recommended duration of therapy in the Recommended Duration field (if applicable).
24. Select whether there is an opportunity for de-escalation in the De-escalation Opportunity field.
25. Enter the recommendation for de-escalation (if applicable) in the De-escalation Recommendation field.
26. Select whether there are any drug interactions in the Drug Interactions field.
27. Select whether any adverse effects have been observed in the Adverse Effects Observed field.
28. Enter a description of any adverse effects in the Adverse Effects Description field (if applicable).
29. Select whether any stewardship interventions are recommended in the Intervention Recommended field.
30. Select the type of intervention(s) recommended in the Type of Intervention field.
31. Enter a description of the recommended intervention(s) in the Intervention Details field (if applicable).
32. Select whether the provider accepted the recommended intervention(s) in the Provider Response field.
33. Select whether follow-up is needed in the Follow-up Needed field.
34. Select the clinical outcome of the patient in the Clinical Outcome field.
35. Enter your name in the Reviewer Name field.
36. Enter your professional credentials in the Reviewer Credentials field.
37. Enter the date the assessment was completed in the Assessment Completion Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`): Enter the patient's medical record number.
  \* **Patient Age** (`patient_age`): Enter the patient's age in years.
  \* **Patient Gender** (`patient_gender`): Select the patient's gender.
  \* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`): Enter the date of the assessment.
  \* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Enter the name of the hospital or clinic where the patient is being treated.
  \* **Unit or Department** (`unit_or_department`): Select the unit or department where the patient is located.
  \* **Admitting Diagnosis** (`admitting_diagnosis`): Enter the primary diagnosis on admission.
  \* **Type of Infection** (`infection_type`): Select the type of infection.
  \* **Specific Infection Site** (`infection_site`): Enter the specific location of the infection (if applicable).
  \* **Current Antibiotic Regimen** (`current_antibiotics`): List all antibiotics currently being used.
  \* **Therapy Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date therapy was started.
  \* **Indication Documented** (`indication_documented`): Select whether the indication is documented.
  \* **Cultures Obtained** (`culture_obtained`): Select whether cultures were obtained before starting antibiotics.
  \* **Culture Results** (`culture_results`): Select whether culture results are available.
  \* **Organism Identified** (`organism_identified`): Enter the name of the pathogen (if known).
  \* **Susceptibility Testing Done** (`susceptibility_testing`): Select whether susceptibility testing was done.
  \* **Drug Selection Appropriateness** (`drug_selection`): Select whether the drug selection is appropriate based on guidelines.
  \* **Dosing Appropriateness** (`dosing`): Select whether the dosing is appropriate.
  \* **Renal Dose Adjustment** (`renal_adjustment`): Select whether renal dose adjustment was made (if needed).
  \* **Route Appropriateness** (`route`): Select whether the route of therapy is appropriate.
  \* **IV to Oral Candidate** (`iv_to_oral`): Select whether the patient can switch from IV to oral therapy.
  \* **Duration Assessment** (`duration_assessment`): Select whether the duration of therapy is appropriate.
  \* **Recommended Duration** (`recommended_duration`): Enter the recommended duration of therapy (if applicable).
  \* **De-escalation Opportunity** (`de_escalation_opportunity`): Select whether there is an opportunity for de-escalation.
  \* **De-escalation Recommendation** (`de_escalation_recommendation`): Enter the recommendation for de-escalation (if applicable).
  \* **Drug Interactions** (`drug_interactions`): Select whether there are any drug interactions.
  \* **Adverse Effects Observed** (`adverse_effects`): Select whether any adverse effects have been observed.
  \* **Adverse Effects Description** (`adverse_effects_description`): Enter a description of any adverse effects (if applicable).
  \* **Stewardship Interventions** (`stewardship_interventions`): Select whether any stewardship interventions are recommended.
  \* **Intervention Recommended** (`intervention_recommended`): Select whether an intervention is recommended.
  \* **Type of Intervention** (`intervention_type`): Select the type of intervention(s) recommended.
  \* **Intervention Details** (`intervention_details`): Enter a description of the recommended intervention(s) (if applicable).
  \* **Provider Response** (`provider_response`): Select whether the provider accepted the recommended intervention(s).
  \* **Follow-up Needed** (`follow_up_needed`): Select whether follow-up is needed.
  \* **Clinical Outcome** (`clinical_outcome`): Select the clinical outcome of the patient.
  \* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`): Enter your name.
  \* **Reviewer Credentials** (`reviewer_credentials`): Enter your professional credentials.
  \* **Assessment Completion Date** (`assessment_complete_date`): Enter the date the assessment was completed.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please consult with the patient's healthcare team.
* The assessment should be completed as soon as possible after the patient's admission or change in condition.
* Please make sure to review and update the assessment regularly to ensure that the information remains accurate.
