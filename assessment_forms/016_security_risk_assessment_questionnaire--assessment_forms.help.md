# Security Risk Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help users identify and document security risks and their corresponding mitigation plans for a specific process or project.

## How To Complete This Form

### General Steps

*   Click on the form and review each page, filling out all required fields.
*   Review the form pages to ensure you are filling out the correct information.
*   Ensure that you answer all questions truthfully, accurately and thoroughly.
*   Double-check your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **General Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter a brief description of the process or project being assessed.
*   **Security Controls** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Describe the security controls currently in place for the process or project.
*   **Risk Factors** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    List any risk factors that could affect the process or project.
*   **Risk Scores** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter a risk score for the process or project.
*   **Mitigation Plans** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Describe the plans in place to mitigate the risks identified.
*   **Risk Level** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, required: false): 
    Select a risk level for the process or project.
*   **Risk Acceptance** (`id: 7`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Indicate if the risk is acceptable, not acceptable or if there are no risks to accept.
*   **Risk Priority** (`id: 8`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Indicate the risk priority.
*   **Risk Summary** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter a brief summary of the risk.
*   **Mitigation Actions Planned** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Describe the mitigation actions planned for the risk.
*   **Mitigation Actions Taken** (`id: 11`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Describe any mitigation actions taken.
*   **Mitigation Actions Remaining** (`id: 12`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Describe any remaining mitigation actions.
*   **Risk Assessment** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required: false): 
    Indicate if the risk assessment is complete.
*   **Risk Score** (`id: 14`, `type: number`, required: false): 
    Enter a risk score for the process or project.
*   **Date Completed** (`id: 15`, `type: date`, required: false): 
    Enter the date the risk assessment was completed.
*   **Completed By** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter the name of who completed the risk assessment.
*   **Comments** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter any comments about the risk assessment.
*   **Attachments** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Attach any relevant documents.
*   **Review Status** (`id: 19`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Indicate the review status.
*   **Notes** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter any additional notes.
*   **Review Date** (`id: 21`, `type: date`, required: false): 
    Enter the review date.
*   **Reviewer** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter the name of who performed the review.
*   **Reviewer Comments** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter any comments from the reviewer.
*   **Review Notes** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter any review notes.
*   **Review Recommendations** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false): 
    Enter any review recommendations.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
*   If you are unsure about a field, it is best to leave it blank.
*   Make sure to save and submit the form once completed.

This form is designed to be completed in a way that will help users understand and document their security risks and mitigation plans accurately. Each field has been created to be easy to understand, and the help guide is designed to make it as easy as possible to complete. If you are unsure about how to fill out a specific field, it's always best to leave it blank and seek clarification before proceeding. The form is there to help you identify and document your security risks and mitigation plans accurately and efficiently.
