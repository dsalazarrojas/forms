# Proportional Reasoning Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Proportional Reasoning Assessment is a tool designed to evaluate an individual's ability to understand and apply proportional relationships in different contexts. This assessment covers various types of proportional relationships, including equivalent, greater than, and less than.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and respond accordingly.
2.  For multiple-choice questions, select the correct answer from the options provided.
3.  For open-ended questions, provide a clear and concise answer that demonstrates your understanding of the concept.
4.  Make sure to provide explanations and justifications for your answers where possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What is a ratio?** (`title`, `text`, required): This question is designed to gauge your understanding of what a ratio is. Please provide a short answer that defines a ratio.
*   **Types of Proportional Relationships** (`proportional_relationships`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the types of proportional relationships that you are familiar with.
*   **Example of Proportion 1** (`example_proportion_1`, `select_one`, required): This question is designed to test your ability to apply proportional relationships in real-world scenarios. Please select the correct answer for this example.
*   **Another Example of Proportion 2** (`example_proportion_2`, `select_one`, required): This is another opportunity for you to apply proportional relationships in a different context. Select the correct answer.
*   **Ratio Interpretation** (`ratio_interpretation`, `text`, required): This question asks you to interpret the meaning of a given ratio. Please provide a short answer that explains the ratio.
*   **Answer 1** (`answer1`, `text`, required): Provide a short answer that applies a proportional relationship in a real-world scenario.
*   **Answer 2** (`answer2`, `text`, required): Similar to Answer 1, provide a short answer that applies a proportional relationship in a different context.
*   **Answer 3** (`answer3`, `text`, required): This is another opportunity for you to apply a proportional relationship in a new scenario. Provide a short answer.
*   **Conclusion** (`conclusion`, `text`, required): Summarize your understanding of proportional relationships in a short conclusion.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): This is the final step. Please review your answers and submit the form.

## Tips
