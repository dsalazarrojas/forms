# Mathematics Diagnostic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mathematics Diagnostic Assessment Form is a tool used to collect and track student information and assessment results. This form helps teachers and administrators to get a comprehensive overview of a student's math readiness and areas of improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's information in the "Student Information" section.
2. Select the student's math subject of focus in the "Math Subject" dropdown menu.
3. Input the student's grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
4. Choose the student's level of math readiness in the "Math Readiness" dropdown menu.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Math Needs" field.
6. Enter the date of submission in the "Assessment Date" field.
7. Input any additional comments or notes in the "Additional Comments" field.
8. Record the date and time of submission in the "Submit Date" field.
9. Enter the person who submitted this form in the "Submitted By" field.
10. Enter the student's email address in the "Email Address" field.
11. Enter any school information or notes in the "School Information" field.
12. Enter any teacher information or notes in the "Teacher Information" field.
13. Enter the assessment result in the "Assessment Result" field.
14. Enter the student's ID in the "Student ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (1)**: Enter the student's name, grade, and any other relevant details.
* **Math Subject (2)**: Select the specific math subject the student is working with (e.g., Algebra, Geometry, etc.).
* **Student Grade (3)**: Enter the student's current grade level (e.g., 6th, 7th, etc.).
* **Math Readiness (4)**: Choose the level of math readiness the student has, based on their current skills and abilities.
* **Math Needs (6)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's math needs or areas of improvement.
* **Assessment Date (5)**: Enter the date the assessment was conducted.
* **Additional Comments (8)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the student's assessment.
* **Submit Date (9)**: Record the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By (10)**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Email Address (11)**: Enter the student's email address.
* **School Information (12)**: Enter any school-specific information or notes.
* **Teacher Information (13)**: Enter any teacher-specific information or notes.
* **Assessment Result (14)**: Enter the outcome of the assessment.
* **Student ID (15)**: Enter the student's ID number.
