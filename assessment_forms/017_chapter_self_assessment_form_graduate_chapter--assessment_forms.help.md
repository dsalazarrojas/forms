# Chapter Self Assessment Form Graduate Chapter - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help graduate chapters evaluate their performance and identify areas for improvement. It is essential to provide accurate and honest answers to ensure that the national organization can support and guide the chapter effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the corresponding page and section for each section of the form (Chapter Basics, Operational Efficiency, etc.).
2. Answer each question based on your chapter's actual performance and circumstances.
3. Use the available options for numerical questions (e.g., select one from the list for officer transition and training).
4. Be sure to answer each question accurately and provide supporting information where possible.
5. Review and proofread your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduate Chapter Full Name** (`graduate_chapter_full_name`, text, required): Enter the formal name of your chapter as registered with the national organization.
* **Chapter Location** (`chapter_location_city_state`, text, required): Enter the city and state of your chapter.
* **Chapter Region** (`chapter_region_selected`, select_one, required): Select the region assigned to your chapter.
* **Current Chapter President Name** (`current_chapter_president_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your chapter's current president.
* **Assessment Period Year** (`assessment_period_year`, number, required): Enter the year of the assessment being reported.
* **Adherence to National Bylaws** (`adherence_to_national_bylaws`, number, required): Rate the chapter's compliance with governing documents (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Frequency of Chapter Meetings** (`frequency_of_chapter_meetings`, select_one, required): Indicate whether regular meetings are held as required (Monthly, Bi-Monthly, Quarterly, or Irregularly).
* **Financial Reporting Compliance** (`financial_reporting_compliance`, select_one, required): Indicate whether all financial reports are submitted on time and accurately (Yes, Mostly, or No).
* **Membership Growth and Retention Rating** (`membership_growth_and_retention_rating`, number, required): Rate the chapter's effectiveness in recruitment and retention (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Implementation of National Programs** (`success_of_national_programs_implementation`, number, required): Rate how effectively national targets are being met (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Community Service Hours Logged** (`community_service_hours_logged`, number, required): Enter the total number of community service hours logged for the assessment period.
* **Local Community Impact Summary** (`local_community_impact_summary`, text, required): Briefly describe the chapter's most significant achievement.
* **Scholarship and Awards Distribution** (`scholarship_awards_distribution_rating`, number, required): Rate the effectiveness of the scholarship program (1-5, with 5 being excellent).
* **Officer Transition and Training** (`officer_transition_and_training_adequacy`, select_one, required): Indicate how well new officers are prepared (Excellent, Adequate, Minimal, or Poor).
* **Committee Activity and Reporting** (`committee_activity_and_reporting`, select_one, required): Indicate whether all required committees are active and reporting (Yes, Mostly, or No).
* **Confirmation of Assessment Accuracy** (`accuracy_confirmation_chapter`, select_one, required): Confirm that the assessment accurately reflects the chapter's status (I Confirm and Submit, or Other).
* **Date of Self-Assessment Completion** (`date_of_self_assessment_completion`, date, required): Enter the date of completion.
* **Additional Assessment Comments** (`additional_assessment_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information or context.
