# Supply Chain Operations Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide helps you complete the Supply Chain Operations Assessment form to provide a comprehensive view of your supply chain operations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Overall Risk of Supply Chain Operations: Describe the overall risk of your supply chain operations. This field is used to identify potential threats and weaknesses in your supply chain.
2. Reporting Team: Enter the name of your reporting team.
3. Forecasting Team: Select the names of the members of your forecasting team. These are the people responsible for predicting and managing future demand and supply.
4. Execution Team: Enter the name of your execution team.
5. Communication Frequency: Select the frequency of your communication with your suppliers. This can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
6. Review Frequency: Select the frequency of your review of your supply chain operations plan. This can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
7. Overall Risk Score: Enter the overall risk score of your supply chain operations. A higher score indicates a higher risk.
8. Current Status: Describe the current status of your supply chain operations.
9. Primary Contact: Enter the name and title of the primary contact person for your supply chain operations.
10. Meeting Frequency: Select the frequency of your meetings with your supply chain team. This can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
11. Overall Score: Enter the overall score of your supply chain operations. A higher score indicates better performance.
12. Inventory Management Team: Enter the name of your inventory management team.
13. Planning Team: Enter the name of your planning team.
14. Monitoring Team: Enter the name of your monitoring team.
15. Execution Team 2: Enter the name of your execution team.
16. Inventory Management Team: Enter the name of your inventory management team.
17. Current Status 2: Describe the current status of your supply chain operations score.
18. Overall Score 2: Enter the overall score of your inventory management. A higher score indicates better performance.
