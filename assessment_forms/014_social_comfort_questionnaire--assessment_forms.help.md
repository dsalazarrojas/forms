# social-comfort-questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "social-comfort-questionnaire" form is designed to assess your level of social comfort in various situations. It is meant to help you identify areas where you feel most or least comfortable socially. Please answer the questions honestly, and you will receive a score at the end of the form that indicates your overall social comfort level.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For questions 1-20, select a number from 1 to 10, where 1 is "not at all" and 10 is "very much."
3. For questions 21, "how-to-use-this-form," simply read the instructions and do not enter any information.
4. Click "Submit" to review your answers and score.
