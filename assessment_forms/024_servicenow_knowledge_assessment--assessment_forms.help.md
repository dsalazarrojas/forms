# ServiceNow Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ServiceNow Knowledge Assessment form is designed to gather information from users about their ServiceNow knowledge and experience. This form will help us understand who has knowledge and skills in ServiceNow, which areas they have experience with, and what their level of knowledge is.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Choose the relevant options for your level of experience and knowledge.
3. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Provide any additional feedback or comments in the "Additional Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. What is ServiceNow
* **What is ServiceNow** (text): This is a short text field where you can describe ServiceNow in a few words.

### 2. adoption_level
* **adoption_level** (select one):
	+ **Basic**: You have a basic understanding of ServiceNow.
	+ **Advanced**: You have an advanced understanding of ServiceNow.
	+ **Expert**: You are an expert in ServiceNow.

### 3. What is your skill level with ServiceNow
* **skill_level** (select one):
	+ **Beginner**: You are new to ServiceNow.
	+ **Intermediate**: You have some experience with ServiceNow.
	+ **Advanced**: You have a good understanding of ServiceNow.

### 4. Which ServiceNow knowledge area do you have experience with?
* **knowledge_area** (select multiple):
	+ **ITSM**: You have experience with IT Service Management.
	+ **ITNM**: You have experience with IT Nuclear Medicine (this option seems unrelated, but we will keep it for the sake of this exercise).
	+ **ITOM**: You have experience with IT Operations Management.

### 5. How many hours have you spent in ServiceNow?
* **time_spent** (number): This is not a select field, so simply type in the number of hours you have spent.

### 6. How many hours have you spent on ServiceNow training?
* **training_hours** (number): This is not a select field, so simply type in the number of hours.

### 7. Feedback
* **Feedback** (note): This is a text field where you can provide any feedback or comments about ServiceNow.

### 8. Do you have any additional feedback about ServiceNow?
* **additional_feedback** (text): This is a text field where you can provide any additional feedback or comments about ServiceNow.

### 9. Email Address
* **email_address** (email): This is a required field where you need to type your email address.

### 10. Phone Number
* **phone_number** (text): This is a required field where you need to type your phone number.

### 11. Submission Date
* **submission_date** (date): This is a required field where you need to type the date of submission.

Please note that I've kept the field with the unrelated "ITNM" option to follow the provided YAML structure, but please review and adjust accordingly to remove or modify it if necessary.
