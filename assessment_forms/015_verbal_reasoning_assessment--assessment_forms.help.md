# Verbal Reasoning Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to assess your verbal reasoning, communication skills, and self-awareness. It consists of four questions that aim to understand your background, motivations, and work style. Your responses will help us evaluate your ability to think critically and express yourself effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Provide detailed responses for all required questions (marked as "required" in the form).
3. Choose one option for questions that have a "select_one" type (marked as "options").
4. Leave blank any question that does not apply to you or is not relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Background Information** (`Background Information`, `text`, required): This is the first question where you can provide a brief description of your background, such as your education, work experience, or relevant skills.
* **Question 1 - What is your current role?** (`Role`, `select_one`, required): Please choose one of the options:
	+ Manager
	+ Director
	+ CEO
* **Question 1 - Other** (`Role - Other`, `select_one`, not required): If "Role - Executive" doesn't apply to you, select "Other" and describe your role.
* **Question 2 - What motivates you?** (`Motivation`, `select_one`, not required): Please choose one of the options:
	+ Personal wealth
	+ Career advancement
	+ Social recognition
* **Question 2 - Other** (`Motivation - Other`, `select_one`, not required): If "Motivation - Financial" doesn't apply to you, select "Other" and describe your motivation.
* **Question 3 - What is your work style?** (`Style`, `select_one`, not required): Please choose one of the options:
	+ Data-driven
	+ Strategic
	+ Advisory
* **Question 3 - Other** (`Style - Other`, `select_one`, not required): If "Style - Analytical" doesn't apply to you, select "Other" and describe your style.

## Tips

* Be honest and truthful in your responses.
* Provide detailed and specific examples whenever possible.
* You can use as much text space as you need for each question.
* Take your time and answer carefully, as this form assesses your verbal reasoning and communication skills.
