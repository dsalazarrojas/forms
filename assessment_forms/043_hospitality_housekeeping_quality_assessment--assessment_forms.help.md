# Hospitality Housekeeping Quality Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the quality of housekeeping services in hospitality settings. It is designed to collect information about the condition and cleanliness of rooms, bathrooms, and other areas of the hotel, as well as overall satisfaction with the housekeeping services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have access to the relevant areas of the hotel that you wish to assess.
* Fill out the "Area Inspected" field with the areas you have inspected, selecting all that apply.
* Rate the condition of each area using the "Condition Rating" field.
* Provide any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Enter your name and contact information in the "Inspector Name", "Inspector Email", and "Hotel Name" fields.
* Date and time of the assessment can be entered in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
* Finally, click "Submit" to complete the form.
