# Compliance Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compliance Knowledge Assessment is a tool used to evaluate an employee's understanding of compliance policies and procedures. It is an essential part of maintaining a culture of integrity and accountability within the organization. This assessment helps to identify knowledge gaps and areas for improvement, ensuring that employees are equipped to make informed decisions and take the right actions to prevent compliance issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Compliance Knowledge Assessment, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide your full name and employee ID number.
2.  Select your department from the list provided.
3.  Choose your job role from the list of options.
4.  Indicate your tenure with the company (less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 3 years, 4 to 6 years, or over 6 years).
5.  Answer each of the 14 questions in Sections A, B, and C.
6.  Rate your confidence level in your answers.
7.  Evaluate the helpfulness of your previous compliance training.
8.  Indicate what additional training you require (select all that apply).
9.  Provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID badge.
*   **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, `number`, required): Enter your employee ID number.
*   **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, `date`, required): The date on which you are taking the assessment.
*   **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required): Select the department you belong to (Human Resources, Finance, Operations, IT, Legal, Compliance, Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, or Other).
*   **Job Role** (`job_role`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or position.
*   **Tenure** (`tenure`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you have worked for the company (Less than 6 months, 6 months to 1 year, 1 to 3 years, 4 to 6 years, or Over 6 years).
*   **Previous Compliance Training** (`previous_training`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have had compliance training (Yes - within past year, Yes - 1 to 2 years ago, Yes - over 2 years ago, or No - never).
*   **Section A - General Compliance Knowledge** (`section_a_intro`, `note`, required): This section is an introduction to Section A, where you will answer questions about general compliance principles.
*   **What is Compliance** (`q1_compliance_definition`, `select_one`, required): Select the best definition of compliance (Following all laws and regulations, Following company policies only, Avoiding legal trouble, or Meeting minimum requirements).
*   **Reporting Violations** (`q2_reporting_violations`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct procedure for reporting violations (Through designated reporting channels, Only to direct supervisor, Only if certain, or Do not report).
*   **Confidentiality** (`q3_confidentiality`, `select_one`, required): Select the best definition of confidential information (Any non-public business information, Only customer data, Only financial data, or Only employee records).
*   **Conflict of Interest** (`q4_conflict_of_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct definition of a conflict of interest (When personal interests conflict with duty, When you disagree with policy, When work is difficult, or When you have multiple tasks).
*   **Retaliation** (`q5_retaliation`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct definition of retaliation (Punishing someone for reporting concerns, Disagreeing with a report, Investigating a complaint, or Filing a counter-complaint).
*   **Section B - Policy Knowledge** (`section_b_intro`, `note`, required): This section is an introduction to Section B, where you will answer questions about specific company policies.
*   **Code of Conduct Applies To** (`q6_code_of_conduct`, `select_one`, required): Select who must follow the code (All employees and contractors, Only management, Only full-time employees, or Only customer-facing staff).
*   **Data Protection Requirements** (`q7_data_protection`, `select_one`, required): Select what data must be protected (All personal and business data, Only customer data, Only financial data, or Only electronic data).
*   **Gifts Policy** (`q8_gifts_policy`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct guideline for gifts (Follow company gift guidelines, Accept all gifts, Reject all gifts, or Only accept cash gifts).
*   **Social Media Guidelines** (`q9_social_media`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct requirement for social media (Professional and respectful conduct, No social media use, Only personal opinions, or No company mentions ever).
*   **Workplace Safety** (`q10_workplace_safety`, `select_one`, required): Select who is responsible for workplace safety (Everyone in the organization, Only safety officers, Only management, or Only facilities team).
*   **Section C - Scenario-Based Questions** (`section_c_intro`, `note`, required): This section is an introduction to Section C, where you will apply your knowledge to practical scenarios.
*   **Gift Scenario** (`q11_scenario_gift`, `select_one`, required): Select what you would do if a vendor offers you an expensive gift (Decline and report per policy, Accept and keep it private, Accept and tell your manager, or Accept and share with team).
*   **Data Scenario** (`q12_scenario_data`, `select_one`, required): Select what you would do if you find customer data on a shared drive (Report to IT or compliance, Download for your use, Ignore it, or Delete it immediately).
*   **Conflict of Interest Scenario** (`q13_scenario_conflict`, `select_one`, required): Select what you would do if you have a potential conflict of interest (Disclose it per policy, Handle it yourself, Ignore it if minor, or Wait to be asked).
*   **Harassment Scenario** (`q14_scenario_harassment`, `select_one`, required): Select what you would do if you witness potential harassment (Report through proper channels, Confront the person directly, Ignore if not your business, or Tell coworkers only).
*   **Deadline Scenario** (`q15_scenario_deadline`, `select_one`, required): Select what you would do if meeting a deadline requires cutting corners (Follow proper procedures regardless, Cut corners to meet deadline, Ask manager for guidance, or Skip documentation only).
*   **Confidence Level** (`confidence_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your confidence level in your answers (Very confident, Somewhat confident, Neutral, Somewhat unsure, or Very unsure).
*   **Previous Training Helpfulness** (`training_helpful`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the helpfulness of your previous compliance training (Very helpful, Somewhat helpful, Neutral, Not very helpful, or Not applicable).
*   **Additional Training Needed** (`additional_training_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select what additional training you need (Data privacy, Anti-harassment, Ethics and conduct, Industry regulations, Reporting procedures, or Other).
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
