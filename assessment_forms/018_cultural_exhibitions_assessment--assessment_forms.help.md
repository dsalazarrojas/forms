# cultural_exhibitions_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **cultural_exhibitions_assessment** form is used by curators to gather feedback on exhibitions in a museum or gallery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **exhibition name** in the text field provided.
2. Select the **exhibition date** from the date picker or enter it manually.
3. Rate the exhibition using the **rating** field, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, type them in the **comments** field.
5. Enter your **email** address so we can follow up on your feedback.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the **phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Name**: The name of the exhibition you are assessing.
* **Exhibition Date**: The date of the exhibition.
* **Rating**: Rate the exhibition from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Comments**: Share any feedback or suggestions you may have.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can follow up on your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you have any.

## Tips
* Make sure to rate the exhibition fairly based on your experience.
* Use the **comments** field to provide specific feedback.
* If you are unable to attend the exhibition, use the **comments** field to explain why.
* If you enter your email address, be aware that it will be used for follow-up purposes.
