# Survey Techniques Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survey Techniques Assessment form is designed to collect feedback from participants about their experience with survey techniques, helping organizers to evaluate and improve training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form to provide your honest assessment of the survey techniques used during the training session you participated in.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability, and choose the options that best describe your experience.
- If a question does not apply to you, you can select "Not applicable" or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Survey Techniques Assessment Form** (`survey_techniques_assessment_form`): The main form that contains the questions for this assessment.
  * This is the main section where you will answer the questions about your experience with survey techniques.
  * Please complete this section to provide a comprehensive feedback.
- **Training Methods** (`training_methods`): Select the methods you were trained with.
  * Choose one or more options that best describe the methods used during the training session.
  * Options include Multiple Choice, True or False, and Likert Scale.
- **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`): A numerical value indicating your level of experience.
  * Please enter a numerical value to indicate your level of experience with survey techniques.
  * The higher the number, the more experienced you are.
- **Internal Training** (`internal_training`): Select the internal training methods you have experience with.
  * Choose one or more options that best describe the internal training methods you have experience with.
  * Options include Workshops, Courses, and Other.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`): A note field for any additional comments or preferences.
  * Please use this field to provide any additional comments or preferences you have about survey techniques.
  * This is a free-text field, so you can write as much or as little as you need.

## Tips
- Please answer honestly and accurately, as your feedback is valuable for improving survey techniques.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the organizers.
- Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and providing your feedback!
