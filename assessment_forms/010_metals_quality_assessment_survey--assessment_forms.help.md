# Metals Quality Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metals Quality Assessment Survey form is used to collect information about a sample's characteristics, including its chemical composition, weight, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the information relevant to the sample being assessed.
2. For fields that are not applicable, leave them blank or note "Not Applicable" (NA).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Sample Information (Page 1)
* **Sample Information** (`sample_information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the sample, including its name, date received, and location.

* **Material Id** (`material_id`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier for the sample's material.

* **Chemical Composition** (`chemical_composition`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the chemical composition of the sample.

* **Sample Weight** (`sample_weight`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the weight of the sample.

* **Sample Weight Unit** (`sample_weight_unit`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the unit of measurement for the sample weight (e.g., kg, g, mg, lb).

* **Sample Weight Instrumental Method** (`sample_weight_instrumental`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the method used to measure the sample weight.

* **Sample Id** (`sample_id`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more of the sample ids from the list provided.

* **Sample Type** (`sample_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of sample (e.g., Metal, Non-metal, Other).

* **Sample Description** (`sample_description`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a detailed description of the sample.

* **Sample Date Received** (`sample_date_received`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date the sample was received.

* **Sample Time Received** (`sample_time_received`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time the sample was received.

* **Sample Location** (`sample_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where the sample was received.

* **Sample Collector** (`sample_collector`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who collected the sample.

* **Sample Notes** (`sample_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the sample.

* **Lab Technician** (`lab_technician`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who will be working with the sample.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If a field is not applicable, leave it blank or note "Not Applicable" (NA).
- Review the form carefully to ensure that you are providing the correct information for the sample being assessed.
