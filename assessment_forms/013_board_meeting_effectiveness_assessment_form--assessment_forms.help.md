# Board Meeting Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a tool for assessing the effectiveness of board meetings, helping you evaluate your experiences and provide feedback for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing honest and detailed feedback.
2. Select the correct response for each question based on your experience with the meeting.
3. Provide suggestions for improvements in the "Suggested Improvements" field, if applicable.
4. Fill out the "Topics for Next Meeting" field with ideas for future discussions, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the meeting being assessed.
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the type of board meeting (e.g., Regular Board Meeting, Special Board Meeting, etc.).
* **Respondent Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your name (if you wish to provide feedback anonymously, leave this field blank).
* **Respondent Role** (`respondent_role`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your position on the board (e.g., Board Chair, Vice Chair, Board Member, etc.).
* **Agenda Clarity** (`agenda_clarity`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the clarity of the agenda (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Agenda Distribution Timing** (`agenda_distribution_timing`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the timing of agenda distribution (Yes, well in advance, Yes, but too late, No, not sent in advance).
* **Meeting Materials Quality** (`meeting_materials_quality`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the quality of pre-meeting materials (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Start Time Punctuality** (`start_time_punctuality`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the start time of the meeting (Yes, on time, Slightly late, Moderately late, Significantly late).
* **End Time Punctuality** (`end_time_punctuality`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the end time of the meeting (Yes, on time, Slightly late, Moderately late, Significantly late).
* **Discussion Participation** (`discussion_participation`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the level of member participation (Excellent - All members engaged, Good - Most members engaged, Average - Some members engaged, Poor - Limited participation).
* **Decision Making Effectiveness** (`decision_making_effectiveness`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the effectiveness of the decision-making process (Very Effective, Effective, Average, Ineffective, Very Ineffective).
* **Chair Facilitation** (`chair_facilitation`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the chair's facilitation (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the time management (Excellent, Good, Average, Below Average, Poor).
* **Action Items Clarity** (`action_items_clarity`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the clarity of action items (Very Clear, Clear, Somewhat Clear, Unclear, Not Defined).
* **Meeting Objectives Met** (`meeting_objectives_met`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the meeting objectives (All objectives met, Most objectives met, Some objectives met, Few objectives met, No objectives met).
* **Overall Effectiveness Rating** (`overall_effectiveness_rating`, `select_one`, **Required**): Rate the overall effectiveness of the meeting (5 - Excellent, 4 - Good, 3 - Average, 2 - Below Average, 1 - Poor).
* **Suggested Improvements** (`improvements_suggested`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide suggestions for improvements, if applicable.
* **Topics for Next Meeting** (`topics_for_next_meeting`, `text`, **Optional**): Suggest topics for the next meeting, if applicable.
