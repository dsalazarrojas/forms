# Marriage Readiness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals assess their readiness for marriage, covering various aspects of their current relationship status, communication styles, conflict resolution, and expectations for the future.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. For questions that require a numerical rating (e.g., 1-10), rate your answer accordingly.
3. For questions that require a text response, provide a brief description of your thoughts and feelings.
4. For questions that require multiple selection, choose all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Relationship Status** (relationship_status)
 Select your current relationship status: Single, In a relationship, or Married.

### **How long have you been with your current partner?** (relationship_duration)
Provide the number of months or years you've been with your current partner.

### **What are your expectations for marriage?** (expectations_marriage)
Describe your hopes and dreams for marriage in a brief text response.

### **How do you handle conflicts in your current relationship?** (conflict_resolution)
Choose all that apply: Avoiding, Discussing, Listening, or Compromising.

### **What are your communication styles in your current relationship?** (communication_styles)
Describe your communication style in a brief text response.

### **How do you feel about intimacy in your current relationship?** (intimacy)
Rate your level of intimacy from 1 to 10.

### **What are your thoughts on marriage as a concept?** (marriage_concept)
Describe your thoughts on marriage in a brief text response.

### **How do you see your future with your partner?** (future_plans)
Describe your plans and expectations for your future together.

### **What are your expectations for resolving conflicts in your future marriage?** (conflict_resolution_future)
Choose all that apply: Avoiding, Discussing, Listening, or Compromising.

### **What are your communication styles in your future marriage?** (communication_styles_future)
Describe your communication style in a brief text response.

### **How do you envision your partner's role in your life?** (partner_role)
Describe your expectations for your partner's role in a brief text response.

### **How do you plan to maintain intimacy in your future relationship?** (intimacy_plans)
Rate your level of intimacy maintenance from 1 to 10.

### **Final Questions** (final_questions)
Choose all that apply:

*   I am ready to have children in the next 5 years
*   I am willing to relocate for my partner
*   I am prepared for financial stability
*   I am committed to long-term commitment

### **Relationship Reflections** (relationship_reflections)
Rate your level of reflection on your relationship from 1 to 10.

### **Additional Comments** (additional_comments)
Provide any additional comments or thoughts about your relationship in a text response.
