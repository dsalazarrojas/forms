# Catering Equipment Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the condition of catering equipment in a kitchen setting. It helps assessors evaluate the cleanliness, physical and functional condition, and safety compliance of the equipment, and provide a rating and recommendation for any necessary actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Assessor Name field with your name.
2. Enter the Date of Assessment in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Select the Site/Kitchen Location where the assessment was performed.
4. Fill out the Equipment Asset ID field with the corresponding ID from the internal inventory system.
5. Choose the correct Equipment Category from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the Manufacturer and Model Number of the equipment.
7. Evaluate the Cleanliness and Sanitation Status by selecting one of the provided options.
8. Rate the Physical and Functional Condition by selecting one of the provided options.
9. Check if the equipment passed or failed the Safety Compliance (Wiring, Seals, Valves) assessment.
10. If necessary, describe any maintenance or repairs required.
11. Select the priority level of the action to be taken.
12. If known, estimate the repair cost.
13. Enter the recommended Date for the next assessment.
14. Provide your digital signature at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessor Name**: Enter your name as the person performing the assessment.
* **Date of Assessment**: Enter the date the assessment was performed in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Site/Kitchen Location**: Enter the location where the assessment was performed (e.g. "Kitchen 1", "Dining Hall", etc.).
* **Equipment Asset ID**: Enter the corresponding ID from the internal inventory system.
* **Equipment Category**: Select the type of equipment being assessed (e.g. "Oven/Range", "Refrigerator/Chiller", etc.).
* **Manufacturer and Model Number**: Enter the manufacturer and model number of the equipment.
* **Cleanliness and Sanitation Status**: Rate the cleanliness and sanitation status of the equipment by selecting one of the provided options.
* **Physical and Functional Condition**: Rate the physical and functional condition of the equipment by selecting one of the provided options.
* **Safety Compliance (Wiring, Seals, Valves)**: Check if the equipment passed or failed the safety assessment.
* **Describe any necessary maintenance or repairs**: If necessary, describe any maintenance or repairs required for the equipment.
* **Action Priority**: Select the priority level of the action to be taken (e.g. Immediate - Safety Hazard, High - Affects Food Quality, etc.).
* **Estimated Repair Cost**: If known, estimate the repair cost.
* **Recommended Date for Next Assessment**: Enter the recommended date for the next assessment.
* **Assessor Digital Signature**: Provide your digital signature at the end of the form.

Note to Admins: Please ensure that the internal inventory system is updated with the new asset ID for the equipment.
