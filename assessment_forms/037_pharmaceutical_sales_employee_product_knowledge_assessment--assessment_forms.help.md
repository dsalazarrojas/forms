# Pharmaceutical Sales Employee Product Knowledge Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the product knowledge of a pharmaceutical sales employee. It will be used to evaluate their understanding of various aspects of the pharmaceutical products they represent, such as product names, sales territories, and training needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk \*.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as instructed.
3. Enter any additional information as needed in the provided fields.
4. Once you have completed all fields, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Knowledge** (`product_knowledge`, text, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your product knowledge, including any relevant details about the pharmaceutical products you represent.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, text, required: false): Describe any additional training or support you require to improve your product knowledge and sales skills.
* **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`, select_one, required: false): Choose the sales team you represent:
	+ Sales Team A
	+ Sales Team B
	+ Sales Team C
* **Sales Territory** (`sales_territory`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all sales territories you are responsible for:
	+ North
	+ South
	+ East
	+ West
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your product knowledge or training needs.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of a specific product you are familiar with.
* **Product Family** (`product_family`, text, required: false): Describe the product family of the product you are familiar with.
