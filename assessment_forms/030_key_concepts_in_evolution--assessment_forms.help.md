# Key Concepts In Evolution - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Key Concepts In Evolution" form is designed to collect data from students about their understanding of key concepts in evolution. This form is used to gather information on their grasp of fundamental principles such as natural selection, genetic drift, mutation, and gene flow. The data collected will help instructors assess students' knowledge and understanding of these concepts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as you would like it to appear in the form.
2. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your phone number if you want to be contacted about your submission.
4. Select the characteristics that best describe you as a student.
5. Choose the level of understanding you have for the key concepts in evolution.
6. Provide the date of a natural selection event that you are aware of.
7. Specify the timeframe for an evolutionary process.
8. Select the principles that you have learned about evolution.
9. Provide an example of natural selection that you have come across.
10. List the processes that you have learned about evolutionary principles.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (1)** (`student_name`, text, required: false): This is the first field where you should enter your name as you would like it to appear in the form.
* **Email (2)** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address here for contact purposes. This can be a personal or institutional email address.
* **Phone (3)** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): If you want to be contacted about your submission, enter your phone number here.
* **Selected Characteristics (4)** (`selected_characteristics`, select_one, required: false): Select the characteristics that best describe you as a student. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Understanding Level (5)** (`understanding_level`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate the level of understanding you have for the key concepts in evolution. You can choose more than one option if you have a mix of levels.
* **Natural Selection Event (6)** (`natural_selection_event`, date, required: false): If you are aware of a natural selection event, provide the date of when it occurred.
* **Evolutionary Timeframe (7)** (`evolutionary_timeframe`, time, required: false): Specify the timeframe for an evolutionary process.
* **Evolution Principles (8)** (`evolution_principles`, select_one, required: false): Select the principles that you have learned about evolution. You can choose one or more of the options provided.
* **Natural Selection Example (9)** (`natural_selection_example`, text, required: false): Provide an example of natural selection that you have come across.
* **Evolutionary Process (10)** (`evolutionary_process`, select_multiple, required: false): List the processes that you have learned about evolutionary principles. You can choose more than one option if you have a mix of processes.
