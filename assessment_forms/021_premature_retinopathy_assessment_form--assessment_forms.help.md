# Premature Retinopathy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Premature Retinopathy Assessment Form is used to collect information on premature infants' medical history and condition to aid in diagnosis and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter the infant's data in the "Infant Data" field.
3. Describe the infant's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
4. Record any examination notes in the "Examination Notes" field.
5. Obtain the parents' consent in the "Parents Consent" field.
6. Indicate the screening results in the "Screening Results" field (select all applicable options).
7. Develop a treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Born Check** (`new_born_check`, number, required): Enter the result of the new born check.
* **Infant Data** (`infant_data`, text, optional): Enter the infant's date of birth, weight, length, and any other relevant information.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe the infant's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or sensitivities.
* **Examination Notes** (`examination_notes`, text, optional): Record any notes from previous examinations or tests.
* **Parents Consent** (`parents_consent`, note, required): Obtain the parents' consent for treatment, including any questions or concerns they may have.
* **Screening Results** (`screening_results`, select multiple, optional): Select all applicable screening results (Yes or No).
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, note, required): Develop a treatment plan for the infant, including any necessary medical interventions or follow-up appointments.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with a healthcare professional before completing it.
