# AAA Self Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AAA Self-Assessment form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with the AAA Self-Assessment process. Your input will help us identify areas for improvement and enhance the overall quality of the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Personal Information, including Full name, Employee ID, and Email address.
2. Answer questions about your department and job title.
3. Select an assessment period that covers the time you're being asked to provide feedback on.
4. Rate your overall satisfaction with the AAA Self-Assessment process.
5. Answer questions about your experience with different aspects of the process.
6. Choose whether you would recommend additional training on the AAA Self-Assessment process.
7. If you've chosen to recommend training, select the topics you think would be most helpful.
8. Provide any improvement suggestions or additional comments about the process.
9. Indicate your level of confidence in completing the assessment accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Your company-assigned identification number.
* **Email address** (`email_address`, email, required): Used for follow-up communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, phone, optional): Optional, for urgent updates.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): For HR records only.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Select the department you belong to.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your current position title.
* **Assessment period** (`assessment_period`, date, required): The month and year the assessment covers.
* **How satisfied are you with the AAA Self-Assessment process?** (`satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction.
* **Overall rating** (`overall_rating`, integer, required): Rate your experience with the AAA Self-Assessment process (1-10).
* **Which aspects applied to your experience with AAA Self-Assessment?** (`experience_aspects`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple aspects that applied to your experience.
* **Would you recommend additional training on AAA Self-Assessment?** (`recommend_training`, select_one, required): Choose the option that best reflects your view.
* **If yes, which training topics would you find most helpful?** (`training_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple topics you think would be most helpful.
* **What would you improve or change about AAA Self-Assessment?** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, optional): Be specific about features or processes.
* **Additional comments or notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any other feedback you wish to share.
* **How confident are you in completing the assessment accurately?** (`submitter_confidence`, select_one, required): Reflect on your comfort level.
