# Retail Merchandising Effectiveness Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess the effectiveness of retail stores in meeting customer needs and expectations. It will help store managers and regional teams to evaluate store performance and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the location of the store you are assessing.
2. Enter the Store ID and Store Name of the store being assessed.
3. Enter the Manager's Name and any relevant information.
4. Select the region where the store is located.
5. Enter the date and time of the assessment.
6. Enter the store's operational hours.
7. Write a brief executive summary of your assessment.
8. Enter your assessment score for the store and region.
9. Enter the average score for the store and region.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Location** (Location, select_one, required): Select the location of the store you are assessing.
* **Store ID** (Store ID, number, optional): Enter the unique identifier of the store.
* **Store Name** (Store Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the store.
* **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the store manager or contact person.
* **Region** (Region, select_one, optional): Select the region where the store is located.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Store Hours** (Store Hours, number, optional): Enter the store's operational hours.
* **Store Executive Summary** (Store Executive Summary, text, optional): Write a brief summary of your assessment.
* **Store Score** (Store Score, number, optional): Enter your assessment score for the store (1-5).
* **Region Score** (Region Score, number, optional): Enter your assessment score for the region (1-5).
* **Average Score** (Average Score, number, optional): Enter the average score for the store and region (1-5).

Note: All scores are on a scale of 1-5. The scores are used to evaluate the overall performance of the store and region.
