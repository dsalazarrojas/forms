# Dissociation Screening Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dissociation Screening Assessment Form is used to screen for dissociation and its impact on a client's daily life. It is designed to gather information about a person's experiences and behaviors that may indicate dissociation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. For each question, select the answer that best describes your experiences or behaviors.
3. If you are unsure about any question, please note your uncertainty.
4. Please complete all questions, even if you are unsure or have difficulty answering a particular question.
