# FIA Suitability Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FIA Suitability Assessment Form is designed to help us evaluate the suitability of a particular investment or financial product for your needs and goals. It's a set of questions that will help us assess your risk tolerance, time horizon, and investment objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose the option that best describes your situation.
3. For text fields (goals and financial_goals), provide a brief description of your goals and financial objectives.
4. If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Risk Tolerance** (`risk_tolerance`): This question is meant to gauge your comfort level with taking risks in your investments. Select the options that best describe your risk tolerance.
* **Time Horizon** (`time_horizon`): This question is about your time frame for making the investment or achieving your financial goals. Choose the option that best describes your time horizon.
* **Goals** (`goals`): Describe your short-term, medium-term, and long-term goals. This could include career goals, education, or personal objectives.
* **Financial Goals** (`financial_goals`): Describe your financial objectives, such as saving for a specific purpose, paying off debt, or building wealth.
* **Investment Goal** (`investment_goal`): Describe the specific investment or financial product you're interested in.
* **Investment Return** (`investment_return`): This question is about your expectations for the investment's return. Do you expect a high, medium, or low return?
* **Risk Tolerances** (`risk_tolerances`): Similar to risk tolerance, this question is meant to gauge your comfort level with taking risks in your investments. Select the options that best describe your risk tolerances.
* **Time Horizons** (`time_horizons`): This question is about your time frame for making the investment or achieving your financial goals. Choose the option that best describes your time horizons.

## Tips
- Take your time to complete the form carefully, and don't hesitate to ask if you're unsure about any question.
- Remember to answer honestly and accurately to ensure we get a clear understanding of your needs and goals.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
