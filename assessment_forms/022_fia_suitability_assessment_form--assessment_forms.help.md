# FIA Suitability Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The FIA Suitability Assessment Form is designed to help us evaluate the suitability of a particular investment or financial product for your needs and goals. It's a set of questions that will help us assess your risk tolerance, time horizon, and investment objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer each question honestly.
2. For select_multiple and select_one fields, choose the option that best describes your situation.
3. For text fields (goals and financial_goals), provide a brief description of your goals and financial objectives.
4. If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
