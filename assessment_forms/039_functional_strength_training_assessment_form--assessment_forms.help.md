# Functional Strength Training Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Functional Strength Training Assessment Form is designed to help users assess their current fitness level and set realistic goals for their functional strength training program. It collects information about the user's demographics, fitness level, experience, and preferences to provide a tailored plan for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: This page is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
2. **Age**: Please enter your age, as this will help us determine your current fitness level and potential for improvement.
3. **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters (cm) to help us understand your body proportions and potential for exercise.
4. **Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms (kg) to help us understand your body composition and potential for exercise.
5. **Fitness goal**: Briefly describe your fitness goals, such as building strength, losing weight, or improving overall health.
6. **Training frequency**: Select how often you currently train per week, from less than 1 time to 5 or more times.
7. **Exercise experience**: Choose your current level of exercise experience, from Beginner to Advanced.
8. **Injury history**: Indicate if you have ever had any injuries, and select the types of injuries you have experienced.
9. **Current level**: Choose your current level of fitness, from Beginner to Elite.
10. **Goal intensity**: Describe the intensity of your fitness goals, such as low, moderate, or high.
11. **Goal duration**: Describe the duration of your fitness goals, such as short-term (less than 6 months) or long-term (more than 6 months).
12. **Goal frequency**: Describe the frequency of your fitness goals, such as daily, weekly, or occasional.
13. **Current program**: Describe the fitness program you are currently participating in.
14. **Goal program**: Choose the fitness program you hope to join after completing this assessment.
15. **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments about your current fitness state and goals.

## Tips
* Please answer all fields honestly and thoroughly to ensure accurate results.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask your fitness coach or trainer for guidance.
