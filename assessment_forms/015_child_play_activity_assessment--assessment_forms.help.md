# Child Play Activity Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to assess a child's social, emotional, cognitive, and motor skills during play activity observations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question based on your observation during the play activity.
2. Select the relevant options for each question.
3. Fill in any additional information as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date**: <label> (1, Assessment Date, required): The date of the play activity assessment.
* **Assessment Time**: <label> (2, Assessment Time, required): The time of assessment observation.
* **Child First Name**: <label> (3, Child First Name, required): Legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: <label> (4, Child Last Name, required): Legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth**: <label> (5, Child Date of Birth, required): Please provide the child's birth date.
* **Child Age**: <label> (6, Child Age, required): Current age of the child in years.
* **Assessor Name**: <label> (7, Assessor Name, required): Name of the person conducting the assessment.
* **Assessor Role**: <label> (8, Assessor Role, required): Role or title of the assessor.
* **Play Setting**: <label> (9, Play Setting, required): Where the play activity is taking place.
* **Type of Play Observed**: <label> (10, Type of Play Observed, required): What type of play is being assessed? Select one of the following options: Free play, Structured play, Guided play, Cooperative play, Parallel play, Solitary play, Dramatic or pretend play.
* **Play Duration in Minutes**: <label> (11, Play Duration in Minutes, required): How long was the play observation?
* **Child Mood at Start**: <label> (12, Child Mood at Start, required): What was the child's mood when play began? Select one of the following options: Happy and excited, Calm and relaxed, Anxious or nervous, Sad or withdrawn, Agitated or restless, Neutral.
* **Engagement Level**: <label> (13, Engagement Level, required): How engaged was the child in the play activity? Select one of the following options: Highly engaged throughout, Mostly engaged, Somewhat engaged, Minimally engaged, Not engaged.
* **Social Interaction**: <label> (14, Social Interaction, required): How did the child interact with others during play? Select one of the following options: Actively interacted with peers, Interacted primarily with adults, Played alongside peers without interaction, Played alone, Avoided interaction.
* **Communication Skills Displayed**: <label> (15, Communication Skills Displayed, required): Rate the child's communication during play. Select one of the following options: Excellent verbal communication, Good verbal communication, Limited verbal communication, Non-verbal communication only, Minimal communication.
* **Motor Skills Observed**: <label> (16, Motor Skills Observed, required): What motor skills were demonstrated? Select multiple options from: Fine motor skills (grasping, manipulating objects), Gross motor skills (running, jumping), Hand-eye coordination, Balance and coordination, Bilateral coordination, Limited motor skills observed.
* **Cognitive Skills Observed**: <label> (17, Cognitive Skills Observed, required): What cognitive skills were demonstrated? Select multiple options from: Problem-solving, Creative thinking, Memory recall, Attention and focus, Sequencing abilities, Symbolic thinking, Limited cognitive engagement.
* **Emotional Regulation**: <label> (18, Emotional Regulation, required): How well did the child regulate emotions during play? Select one of the following options: Excellent self-regulation, Good self-regulation, Some difficulty regulating, Significant difficulty regulating, Unable to assess.
* **Play Preferences Observed**: <label> (19, Play Preferences Observed, required): What types of play did the child prefer? Type in your observations.
* **Strengths Observed**: <label> (20, Strengths Observed, required): What strengths did the child demonstrate during play? Type in your observations.
* **Challenges Observed (optional)**: <label> (21, Challenges Observed, optional): What challenges or areas of concern were noted? Type in your observations.
* **Play Materials Used**: <label> (22, Play Materials Used, required): List the toys, games, or materials used during play.
* **Recommendations**: <label> (23, Recommendations, optional): Any recommendations for future play activities or interventions. Type in your suggestions.
* **Follow-Up Needed**: <label> (24, Follow-Up Needed, required): Is a follow-up assessment recommended? Select one of the following options: Yes, within 1 week, Yes, within 1 month, Yes, within 3 months, No follow-up needed at this time, Refer to specialist.
