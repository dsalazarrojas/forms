# Player Assessment And Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Assessment And Certification Form is designed to evaluate a player's performance and certification in a sports-related context. This form collects information on different aspects of a player's skills, physical ability, teamwork, communication, sportsmanship, coach and parent interactions, and overall attitude.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page to understand the specific questions and fields.
2. For each field, provide detailed and honest answers about the player's performance and experiences.
3. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by a *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Assessment (Field 1)
* **Player Assessment** (`Player Assessment`, text, optional): This field is a brief description of the player's performance, highlighting their strengths and weaknesses.

### Player Certification (Field 2)
* **Player Certification** (`Player Certification`, text, optional): This field is for documenting any relevant certifications or qualifications the player has achieved.

### Overall Performance (Field 3)
* **Overall Performance** (`Overall Performance`, text, optional): Describe the player's overall performance, including any notable achievements or areas for improvement.

### Skills Assessment (Field 4)
* **Skills Assessment** (`Skills Assessment`, text, optional): Evaluate the player's skills in specific areas, such as communication, teamwork, or problem-solving.

### Physical Ability (Field 5)
* **Physical Ability** (`Physical Ability`, text, optional): Assess the player's physical ability, including any notable strengths or weaknesses.

### Teamwork (Field 6)
* **Teamwork** (`Teamwork`, text, optional): Describe the player's ability to work collaboratively with others, including any notable successes or challenges.

### Communication (Field 7)
* **Communication** (`Communication`, text, optional): Evaluate the player's communication skills, including verbal and non-verbal communication.

### Sportsmanship (Field 8)
* **Sportsmanship** (`Sportsmanship`, text, optional): Assess the player's sportsmanship, including their behavior and conduct during games and practices.

### Coach Sightings (Field 9)
* **Coach Sightings** (`Coach Sightings`, text, optional): Document any notable interactions or observations the coach has with the player, including any positive or negative comments.

### Parent Sightings (Field 10)
* **Parent Sightings** (`Parent Sightings`, text, optional): Record any notable interactions or observations parents have with the player, including any positive or negative comments.

### Overall Attitude (Field 11)
* **Overall Attitude** (`Overall Attitude`, text, optional): Assess the player's overall attitude, including their positive or negative traits.
