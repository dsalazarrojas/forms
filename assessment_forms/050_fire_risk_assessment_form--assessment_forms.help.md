# Fire Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fire Risk Assessment Form is designed to collect information about fire hazards and risks in your workplace or environment. This form helps assess the potential fire risks and identify areas that need improvement to ensure a safe working condition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with your contact details and location.
2. Describe the type of substance used or stored at the location.
3. Identify the risk factors present in the area, such as electrical issues or poor ventilation.
4. Choose the possible outcome in case of a fire, such as "Fire" or "Explosion".
5. Provide details on prevention measures taken to mitigate fire risks.
6. Describe any control measures in place to prevent or contain fires.
7. List any fire extinguishers or equipment available to combat fires.
8. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to fire safety.
9. Enter your name and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Please provide your contact details, such as your name and location.
  - Enter your name and contact information in this section.
  - This information will help us contact you for further clarification if needed.
  
- **Substance** (`substance`, text, optional): Describe the type of substance used or stored at the location.
  - Be as specific as possible about the substance, its quantity, and location.
  - This information will help us assess the fire risk associated with the substance.
  
- **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple, required): Identify the risk factors present in the area.
  - Choose all that apply from the options: Low, Medium, High.
  - Be honest about the risks present in the area to ensure accurate assessment.
  
- **Possible Outcome** (`outcome_info`, select_one, optional): Choose the possible outcome in case of a fire.
  - Select "Fire" or "Explosion" depending on the potential risk.
  
- **Prevention Measures** (`prevention`, text, optional): Provide details on prevention measures taken to mitigate fire risks.
  - Be specific about the measures, such as regular cleaning schedules or regular inspections.
  
- **Control Measures** (`control_measures`, text, optional): Describe any control measures in place to prevent or contain fires.
  - Mention any systems, equipment, or procedures that help control fires.
  
- **Fire Extinguishers** (`fire_extinguishers`, text, optional): List any fire extinguishers or equipment available to combat fires.
  - Be specific about the types and locations of the extinguishers.
  
- **Other Information** (`other_info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant to fire safety.
  - Be honest and detailed about any other relevant information.
  
- **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name.
  - This is your name for contact purposes.
  
- **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
  - This will help track the submission time.
  
- **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the form was submitted.
  - This will help track the submission time.
  
- **Submitted Location** (`submitted_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the form was submitted.
  - This will help track the submission location.
  
- **Fire Suppression System** (`fire_suppression_system`, text, optional): Describe any fire suppression systems in place.
  - Be specific about the system and its location.
  
- **Fire Extinguisher Equipment** (`fire_extinguishers_equipment`, text, optional): List any fire extinguishers or equipment available.
  - Be specific about the equipment and its location.
  
- **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_safety_equipment`, text, optional): Describe any fire safety equipment available.
  - Be specific about the equipment and its location.
  
- **Fire Alarm System** (`fire_alarm_system`, text, optional): Describe any fire alarm system in place.
  - Be specific about the system and its location.
  
- **Alarm Test Result** (`alarm_test_result`, text, optional): Enter the result of the alarm test.
  - This will help assess the system's functionality.
  
- **Alarm Test Frequency** (`alarm_test_frequency`, text, optional): Enter the frequency of alarm tests.
  - This will help assess the system's maintenance schedule.
  
- **Fire Safety Plan** (`fire_safety_plan`, text, optional): Describe the fire safety plan in place.
  - Be specific about the plan and its implementation.
  
- **Training Program** (`training_program`, text, optional): Describe any training program related to fire safety.
  - Be specific about the program and its frequency.
