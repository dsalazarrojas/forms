# ESL Placement Test Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ESL Placement Test Quiz is a form designed to assess students' language skills in English, including listening, speaking, reading, and writing. The quiz aims to evaluate a student's language proficiency and provide a snapshot of their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the general information page and fill in the required fields, including the student's email and phone number.
2. Move on to the student details page, where you can provide additional context about the student.
3. On the skills pages, rate the student's skills in listening, speaking, reading, and writing using the provided scales.
4. Provide comments and notes for each skill, if necessary.
5. Upload any relevant files, such as audio or video recordings, to support your assessment.
6. Finally, select a submission date and time for the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Page 1, Text): This page collects basic information about the student, including their email and phone number.
* **Student Details** (Page 2, Text): Provide additional context about the student, such as their name, grade level, or any special needs.
* **Listening Skills** (Page 3, Number): Rate the student's listening skills using a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Speaking Skills** (Page 4, Number): Rate the student's speaking skills using a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Reading Skills** (Page 5, Number): Rate the student's reading skills using a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Writing Skills** (Page 6, Number): Rate the student's writing skills using a scale (e.g., 1-10).
* **Listening Score** (Page 7, Number): Enter a score for the student's listening skills.
* **Speaking Score** (Page 8, Number): Enter a score for the student's speaking skills.
* **Reading Score** (Page 9, Number): Enter a score for the student's reading skills.
* **Writing Score** (Page 10, Number): Enter a score for the student's writing skills.
* **Listening Notes** (Page 11, Note): Provide any comments or notes about the student's listening skills.
* **Speaking Notes** (Page 12, Note): Provide any comments or notes about the student's speaking skills.
* **Reading Notes** (Page 13, Note): Provide any comments or notes about the student's reading skills.
* **Writing Notes** (Page 14, Note): Provide any comments or notes about the student's writing skills.
* **Listening Comments** (Page 15, Text): Enter any comments or feedback about the student's listening skills.
* **Speaking Comments** (Page 16, Text): Enter any comments or feedback about the student's speaking skills.
* **Reading Comments** (Page 17, Text): Enter any comments or feedback about the student's reading skills.
* **Writing Comments** (Page 18, Text): Enter any comments or feedback about the student's writing skills.
* **Email** (Page 19, Email): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (Page 20, Text): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Listening File** (Page 21, Text): Upload any relevant files, such as audio recordings, to support the assessment of the student's listening skills.
* **Speaking File** (Page 22, Text): Upload any relevant files, such as video recordings, to support the assessment of the student's speaking skills.
* **Reading File** (Page 23, Text): Upload any relevant files, such as text documents, to support the assessment of the student's reading skills.
* **Writing File** (Page 24, Text): Upload any relevant files, such as text documents, to support the assessment of the student's writing skills.
* **Submission Date** (Page 25, Date): Select a date and time for the submission of the assessment.
