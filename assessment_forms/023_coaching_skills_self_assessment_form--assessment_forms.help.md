# Coaching Skills Self Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Skills Self Assessment Form is a tool designed to evaluate a coach's skills and abilities in various areas, including communication, leadership, technical, and personal skills. This form is intended to help coaches assess their strengths and weaknesses, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name, email address, coaching position, and organization or team name.
2. Rate your abilities in each of the skills listed under the 'Communication Skills' section, using the scale provided.
3. Rate your technical expertise under the 'Technical and Domain Knowledge' section.
4. Answer the questions under the 'Professional Development' section, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name**: This field is for your full name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address, which we may use to contact you if needed. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Coaching Position**: This field is for your coaching role or title within your organization or team.
* **Organization or Team**: This is where you enter the name of your organization or team you are a part of.
* **Assessment Date**: This field is for the date you completed this assessment. Please ensure it is in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
