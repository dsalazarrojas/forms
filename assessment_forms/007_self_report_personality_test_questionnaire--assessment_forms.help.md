# Self Report Personality Test Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your personality, preferences, and goals. The data collected will help guide coaching or educational programs tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please fill out this form completely and honestly.
* Answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your coach or program administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. personality_test_1 (Number)**: This question is designed to assess your personality traits. Please answer with a number between 1 and 5.
* **2. name (Text)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **3. email (Email)**: Enter your email address to allow for communication and updates.
* **4. phone (Text)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it to enable easy communication.
* **5. select_one_1 (Select One)**: Choose one of the options provided.
* **6. select_multiple_1 (Select Multiple)**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **7. select_one_2 (Select One)**: Choose one of the options provided.
* **8. select_multiple_2 (Select Multiple)**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **9. date (Date)**: Please enter your date of birth or a significant date relevant to your coaching or education journey.
* **10. time (Time)**: If applicable, please enter a specific time related to your coaching or education goals.
* **11. note (Note)**: If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them here.
* **12. notes (Note)**: Please enter any other additional comments or notes.
* **13. coaching_goal (Text)**: Describe your coaching goals.
* **14. education_goal (Text)**: Describe your education goals.
* **15. wellbeing_goal (Text)**: Describe your well-being goals.
* **16. coaching_programs (Text)**: If you are interested in any coaching programs, please describe them.
* **17. education_programs (Text)**: If you are interested in any educational programs, please describe them.
* **18. wellbeing_programs (Text)**: If you are interested in any well-being programs, please describe them.
* **19. submission_notes (Note)**: If you have any additional comments about your submission, please enter them here.
* **20. submit (Select One)**: Choose to submit your form.
* **21. save_and_exit (Select One)**: Choose to save and exit.
* **22. cancel (Select One)**: Cancel your submission.
* **23. delete_submission (Select One)**: Choose to delete your submission.
* **24. submission_status (Select Multiple)**: Select the status of your submission (Draft, Pending, Published).
* **25. submission_id (Text)**: Please leave this field blank. It will be populated automatically.
* **Save** or **Submit** to complete and submit the form.
