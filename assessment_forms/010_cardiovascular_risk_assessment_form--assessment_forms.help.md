# Cardiovascular Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the cardiovascular risk of patients in a clinical setting. It is used to gather information on patient demographics, medical history, and lifestyle factors that contribute to cardiovascular risk. The clinician uses this information to estimate the patient's risk level and recommend relevant actions or follow-ups.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the patient's name**: Please enter the patient's full legal name in the field labeled "Patient name".
2. **Enter the date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth using the format "year month day" in the field labeled "Date of birth".
3. **Indicate smoking status**: Select the current smoking status from the options provided in the field labeled "Smoking status".
4. **Confirm diabetes diagnosis**: Mark the field "Diabetes diagnosis" as "True" if the patient has a known diagnosis, otherwise mark it as "False".
5. **Enter systolic blood pressure (optional)**: If known, enter the most recent systolic blood pressure value in the field labeled "Systolic blood pressure".
6. **Enter total cholesterol (optional)**: If known, enter the total cholesterol value in milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL) in the field labeled "Total cholesterol".
7. **Report family history of CVD (optional)**: If any first-degree relatives have a history of cardiovascular disease, mark the field "Family history of cardiovascular disease" as "True".
8. **Estimate risk level**: Select the estimated risk level from the options provided in the field labeled "Estimated risk level".
9. **Add recommended actions or follow-ups**: Provide relevant lifestyle medication or referral recommendations based on the estimated risk level in the field labeled "Recommended actions or follow up".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient name**: Please enter the patient's full legal name.
* **Date of birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth using the format "year month day", e.g., "1980 07 24".
* **Smoking status**: Select the current smoking status from the options:
	+ Current smoker
	+ Former smoker
	+ Never smoked
* **Diabetes diagnosis**: If the patient has a known diagnosis of diabetes, mark this field as "True". Otherwise, mark it as "False".
* **Systolic blood pressure (optional)**: If known, enter the most recent systolic blood pressure value.
* **Total cholesterol (optional)**: If known, enter the total cholesterol value in milligrams per deciliter (mg/dL).
* **Family history of CVD (optional)**: If any first-degree relatives have a history of cardiovascular disease, mark this field as "True". Otherwise, mark it as "False".
* **Estimated risk level**: Select the estimated risk level from the options:
	+ Low
	+ Moderate
	+ High
* **Recommended actions or follow up (optional)**: Provide relevant lifestyle medication or referral recommendations based on the estimated risk level.
