# Self Assessment Form For Students In Preparation For School - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Assessment Form For Students In Preparation For School is a tool used by students to report their health and wellness status, which will help the school staff prepare and provide necessary services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
2. If you're unsure about any question, ask your parents or school staff for guidance.
3. Fill in all the required fields (marked as 'required').

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student (1)**: This is an identifier for the student. Please fill in your school ID or student number.
* **Body Temperature (2)**: Please report your current body temperature. This will help the school staff assess your health.
* **Symptoms (3)**: Check 'Yes' if you are experiencing any of the following symptoms:
	+ Headache
	+ Fatigue
	+ Cough
	+ Sore throat
	+ Runny nose
	+ Muscle pain
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Date (4)**: Report the current date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **School ID (5)**: If applicable, please fill in your school ID or student number.
* **Email (6)**: If you have an email address, please fill it in so that the school staff can contact you for more information.

## Tips
- Be accurate and honest when filling in this form.
- If you're unsure about any question, ask your parents or school staff for guidance.
- This form is a confidential tool, and your information will not be shared with anyone without your consent.
