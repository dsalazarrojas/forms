This guide will help you understand how to complete the Logistics Incident Report Form, which is used to report incidents related to transportation issues, warehouse problems, fleet issues, and other logistics-related events. This form ensures that incidents are documented accurately and consistently, providing valuable information for incident management and resolution.

## Logistics Incident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Logistics Incident Report Form is a standardized report to document and track logistics incidents, ensuring that they are reported accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing details about the incident, including:
	* Shipping issues: Describe any problems that occurred during shipping.
	* Warehouse issues: Report any problems that occurred in the warehouse.
	* Fleet issues: Report any problems with vehicles or equipment.
	* Description: Provide a detailed description of the incident.
	* Incident Date: Enter the date the incident occurred.
2. Select the correct assignee from the list:
	* Choose the person responsible for investigating and resolving the incident.
3. Select the correct follow-up result:
	* Choose whether the incident was resolved or not.
	* Choose whether the incident is pending further investigation.
4. Select the correct submitter from the list:
	* Choose the person who submitted the incident report.
5. Fill out the remaining fields:
	* Follow-up Date: Enter the date the follow-up is scheduled.
	* Follow-up By: Enter the name of the person responsible for the follow-up.
	* Follow-up Result: Enter the result of the follow-up.
	* Email: Enter the submitter's email address.
	* Phone: Enter the submitter's phone number.
	* Incident ID: Enter a unique ID for the incident.
	* Incident Number: Enter a unique number for the incident.
	* Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
	* Attachments: Upload any relevant attachments or supporting documents.
	* Submitted: Enter the date and time the incident was submitted.
	* Submitted By: Enter the name of the person who submitted the incident.
	* Submitted To: Choose the person or team responsible for reviewing and acting on the incident.
	* Submitted To: Enter the name of the person or team responsible for reviewing and acting on the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Issue** (Shipping Issue, text, required): Describe any problems that occurred during shipping.
* **Warehouse Issue** (Warehouse Issue, text, required): Report any problems that occurred in the warehouse.
* **Fleet Issue** (Fleet Issue, text, required): Report any problems with vehicles or equipment.
* **Description** (Description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (Incident Date, date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (Incident Time, time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (Incident Location, text, optional): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (Incident Type, text, optional): Select the type of incident (e.g., delivery failure, equipment damage).
* **Incident Description** (Incident Description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, select_multiple, required): Choose the person responsible for investigating and resolving the incident.
* **Follow-up Date** (Follow-up Date, date, optional): Enter the date the follow-up is scheduled.
* **Follow-up By** (Follow-up By, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Result** (Follow-up Result, select_one, required): Choose whether the incident was resolved or not.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, select_one, required): Choose the person who submitted the incident report.
* **Submitter's Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the submitter's email address.
* **Submitter's Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the submitter's phone number.
* **Incident ID** (Incident ID, text, optional): Enter a unique ID for the incident.
* **Incident Number** (Incident Number, text, optional): Enter a unique number for the incident.
* **Notes** (Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the incident.
* **Attachments** (Attachments, note, optional): Upload any relevant attachments or supporting documents.
* **Submitted** (Submitted, date, required): Enter the date and time the incident was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who submitted the incident.
* **Submitted To** (Submitted To, select_multiple, required): Choose the person or team responsible for reviewing and acting on the incident.
* **Submitted To** (Submitted To, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or team responsible for reviewing and acting on the incident.
