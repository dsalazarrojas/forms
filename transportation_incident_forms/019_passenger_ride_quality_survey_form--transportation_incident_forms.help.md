# Passenger Ride Quality Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from passengers about their ride experience. It helps us understand how we can improve our service to better serve our passengers.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select one of the options for each question to rate your experience.
- You can also provide any additional comments or improvement areas in the "Improvement Areas" field.
- If you would like to provide your contact information, please select the "Email" or "Phone Number" options in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Feelings** (rating_1): Rate your overall feelings about your trip. Select one of the options: Very Dissatisfied, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied.
* **Service Quality** (service_quality): Rate the service quality of your trip. Select one of the options: Very Poor, Somewhat Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **Cleanliness** (cleanliness): Rate the cleanliness of your trip. Select one of the options: Very Poor, Somewhat Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **Communication** (communication): Rate the communication of your trip. Select one of the options: Very Poor, Somewhat Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **On-time Service** (on_time): Rate the on-time service of your trip. Select one of the options: Very Poor, Somewhat Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **Overall Experience** (overall_experience): Rate your overall experience with your trip. Select one of the options: Very Poor, Somewhat Poor, Neutral, Good, or Excellent.
* **Will You Recommend** (will_recommend): Would you recommend this service? Select one of the options: True or False.
* **Improvement Areas** (improvement_areas): If you have any comments or suggestions on how we can improve our service, please provide them here.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info): If you would like to provide your contact information, please select one of the options: Email or Phone Number.
* **Email** (email): If you selected to provide your email address, please enter your email here.
* **Phone Number** (phone): If you selected to provide your phone number, please enter your phone number here.
