# Airline Disability Accommodation Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airline Disability Accommodation Complaint Form is a tool for travelers to report incidents where they experienced difficulties or dissatisfaction with disability accommodations during their flight. This form is used by the airline to gather information and address the concerns raised by the traveler.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
3. If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Name** (1, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Email** (3, Email, Required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Phone Number** (4, Text, Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Booking Reference Number** (5, Text, Required): Enter the booking reference number for your flight.
* **Flight Number** (7, Text, Required): Enter the flight number that you took.
* **Date of Incident** (8, Date, Required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Airport or Location of Incident** (9, Text, Required): Enter the airport or location where the incident occurred (e.g. Gate B12, Check-in Counter).
* **Type of Accommodation Requested** (10, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type of accommodation that you requested and was not met (e.g. Wheelchair Assistance, Priority Boarding).
* **Detailed Description of the Complaint** (11, Text, Required): Describe what happened and how the accommodation was not met.
* **Personnel Involved** (12, Text, Optional): If applicable, list the names or descriptions of staff members involved in the incident.
* **Witnesses** (14, Text, Optional): If applicable, list the names and contact information of any witnesses.
* **Preferred Resolution** (15, Text, Optional): Suggest a preferred resolution or outcome for the incident.

Note: Please ensure that you have provided accurate and truthful information in the form. Any false or misleading information may be subject to further investigation.
