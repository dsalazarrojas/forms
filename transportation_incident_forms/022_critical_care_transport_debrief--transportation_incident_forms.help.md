# Critical Care Transport Debrief - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Critical Care Transport Debrief form is used to record and analyze details of critical care transport incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are authorized to access and report transport incidents.
2. Check if a similar report has been submitted before and, if so, reference it.
3. Start by filling in the Transport Time field with the actual time of the transport.
4. Enter the date of the transport in the Transport Date field.
5. Select the Critical Care Team involved from the available options.
6. Choose whether team members were available or not during the transport.
7. Provide details of the patient's condition during the transport in the Patient Condition field.
8. Describe any critical care procedure performed during the transport in the Critical Care Procedure field.
9. Enter any comments or observations relevant to the transport in the Comments field.
10. Enter the contact email and phone number of the submitter.
11. Add any additional notes or comments about the transport in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **transport_time**: Enter the actual time of the critical care transport in hours and minutes.
* **transport_date**: Enter the date of the critical care transport in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **critical_care_team**: Select the Critical Care Team involved in the transport from the available options.
* **team_member**: Choose whether team members were available or not during the transport.
* **patient_condition**: Describe the patient's condition during the transport, e.g., stable, unstable, critical.
* **critical_care_procedure**: Describe any critical care procedure performed during the transport, e.g., intubation, fluid resuscitation.
* **comments**: Enter any comments or observations relevant to the transport.
* **email**: Enter the contact email of the submitter.
* **phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the submitter.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the transport.
