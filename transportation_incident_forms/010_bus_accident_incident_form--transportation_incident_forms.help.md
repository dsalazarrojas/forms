# Bus Accident Incident Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bus Accident Incident Form is a tool for reporting and documenting bus accidents. It helps transportation staff to gather information that will assist in making informed decisions and taking necessary actions to prevent future accidents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are reporting a genuine incident and have not already submitted a report for this incident.
2. Select the relevant pages and fields that apply to your incident.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields.
4. Enter the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
5. Select the weather conditions at the time of the incident from the options provided.
6. Select the road conditions at the time of the incident from the options provided.
7. Enter the details about the bus involved, including its number and route.
8. Enter the name and employee ID of the driver.
9. Enter the number of passengers on board at the time of the incident.
10. Select the type of accident from the options provided.
11. Indicate if anyone was injured and the number of injuries sustained.
12. If emergency services were called, select the services that responded.
13. Indicate if any passengers were transported to the hospital.
14. Enter the number of witnesses and their contact information.
15. Provide a detailed description of the incident.
16. Select any contributing factors that may have led to the accident.
17. Indicate if photos were taken at the scene.
18. Indicate if a police report has been filed.
19. Enter the police report number (if applicable).
20. Enter your name and role/relationship to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Basics** (`incident_basics`): A brief summary of the incident.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`): The date when the accident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`): The time when the accident occurred.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`): The full address or location description where the accident occurred.
* **Weather Conditions** (`weather_conditions`): The weather conditions at the time of the incident (e.g. Clear, Cloudy, Rain, etc.).
* **Road Conditions** (`road_conditions`): The condition of the road surface at the time of the incident (e.g. Dry, Wet, Icy, etc.).
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`): Details about the bus involved (optional).
* **Bus Number** (`bus_number`): The bus identification number.
* **Bus Route** (`bus_route`): The bus route number or name.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`): The full name of the driver.
* **Driver ID** (`driver_id`): The driver's employee ID.
* **Driver License Number** (`driver_license`): The driver's license number.
* **Passengers Onboard** (`passengers_onboard`): The number of passengers at the time of the incident.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`): The type of accident (e.g. Collision with Vehicle, Pedestrian, etc.).
* **Injuries** (`injuries`): Information about injuries sustained (optional).
* **Were There Injuries** (`injuries_reported`): Indicate if anyone was injured.
* **Number of Injured** (`number_injured`): The total number of injured persons (if applicable).
* **Fatalities** (`fatalities`): The number of fatalities.
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`): A description of the injuries sustained (if applicable).
* **Emergency Response** (`emergency_response`): Emergency services information (optional).
* **Emergency Services Called** (`emergency_services_called`): The services that responded to the incident (e.g. Police, Ambulance, etc.).
* **Hospital Transport** (`hospital_transport`): Indicate if any passengers were transported to the hospital.
* **Witnesses** (`witnesses`): Witness information (optional).
* **Number of Witnesses** (`witness_count`): The number of witnesses.
* **Witness Contact Information** (`witness_contacts`): Names and contact details of witnesses (if applicable).
* **Detailed Incident Description** (`incident_description`): A detailed account of what happened.
* **Contributing Factors** (`contributing_factors`): Any factors that may have contributed to the accident.
* **Were Photos Taken** (`photos_taken`): Indicate if photos were taken at the scene.
* **Police Report Filed** (`police_report_filed`): Indicate if a police report has been filed.
* **Police Report Number** (`police_report_number`): The police report reference number (if applicable).
* **Reporter Name** (`reporter_name`): The name of the person filing the report.
* **Reporter Role** (`reporter_role`): The position or relationship to the incident.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`): The date this report is filed.

## Tips

* Ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear and concise account of the incident.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant transportation staff for guidance.
* If you have already reported this incident, please do not submit a duplicate report.
* This form is designed to help transportation staff make informed decisions and take necessary actions to prevent future accidents.
