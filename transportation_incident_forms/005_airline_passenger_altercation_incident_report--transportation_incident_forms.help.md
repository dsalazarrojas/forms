# Airline Passenger Altercation Incident Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report an altercation incident that occurred on an airline or at a transportation location. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the incident, including details about the parties involved, the description of what happened, and any immediate actions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the **Reporter name** field. This is your contact information for follow-up purposes.
2. Select your role in the incident by choosing the relevant option from the **Reporter role** dropdown menu. This will help us understand your relationship to the incident.
3. Enter the date of the incident in the **Incident date** field. This will help us track the timing of the incident.
4. Provide the flight number or airport location where the incident occurred in the **Flight or location** field.
5. Describe the parties involved in the incident in the **Parties involved** field. This can include names or descriptions of individuals, or other relevant details.
6. Enter a detailed narrative of what happened in the **Incident description** field. This will help us understand the circumstances surrounding the incident.
7. Select the immediate actions taken during or after the incident from the **Immediate actions taken** dropdown menu. This will help us understand the response to the incident.
8. Indicate if anyone was injured in the **Injuries reported** field. Choose from the options: None, Minor, or Serious.
9. Enter any witness details in the **Witnesses** field, if applicable.
10. Choose whether follow-up is required by selecting the relevant option from the **Follow up required** dropdown menu.
11. Enter the team or staff member responsible for follow-up in the **Assigned to** field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reporter name**: This field allows you to enter your name for contact information purposes. Please enter your name to ensure that we can contact you if additional information is needed.
* **Reporter role**: This field helps us understand your relationship to the incident. Please choose the option that best describes your role (Passenger, Crew, Ground staff, or Other).
* **Incident date**: This field is used to track the date of the incident. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Flight or location**: This field is used to provide the flight number or airport location where the incident occurred.
* **Parties involved**: This field is used to describe the individuals or entities involved in the incident. Please enter names, descriptions, or other relevant details.
* **Incident description**: This field is used to provide a detailed narrative of what happened during the incident. Please provide as much information as possible.
* **Immediate actions taken**: This field allows you to select the immediate actions taken during or after the incident. Choose from the options: Separated parties, Crew intervention, Police notified, Medical assistance, or Other.
* **Injuries reported**: This field is used to indicate if anyone was injured during the incident. Choose from the options: None, Minor, or Serious.
* **Witnesses**: This field is used to provide witness details, if applicable.
* **Follow up required**: This field is used to choose whether follow-up is required. Select "True" if follow-up is needed, or "False" otherwise.
* **Assigned to**: This field is used to enter the team or staff member responsible for follow-up, if applicable.
