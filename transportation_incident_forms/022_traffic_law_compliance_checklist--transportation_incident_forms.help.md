# Traffic Law Compliance Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data for traffic law compliance checks on vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all that apply from the **Vehicle Checks** section.
2. If necessary, select one record compliance item from the **Record Compliance Items** section.
3. Provide a detailed description of the vehicle's condition in the **Observation Notes** section.
4. Review and update the **Date Created** and **Last Updated** sections as needed.
5. Select the person who performed the vehicle check in the **Vehicle Checked By** section.
6. Confirm whether the safety manager has verified the vehicle check in the **Safety Manager Verified** section.
7. Select all that apply from the **Vehicle Inspections Performed** section.
8. Select the status of the observation from the **Observation Status** section.
9. Provide any additional comments about the vehicle in the **Comments** section.
10. Provide URLs of attachments related to the vehicle in the **Attachment URLs** section.
11. Provide any relevant vehicle identifiers in the **Vehicle Identifiers** section.
12. Provide additional notes about the vehicle in the **Notes** section.
13. Select the make of the vehicle from the **Vehicle Make** section.
14. Select the year of the vehicle from the **Vehicle Year** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Checks**: Select all that apply from a list of predefined vehicle checks.
* **Record Compliance Items**: Select one record compliance item from a list of predefined items.
* **Observation Notes**: Provide a detailed description of the vehicle's condition.
* **Date Created**: This field automatically populates with the date the form was created and is not editable.
* **Vehicle Checked By**: Select the person who performed the vehicle check.
* **Safety Manager Verified**: Confirm whether the safety manager has verified the vehicle check.
* **Vehicle Inspections Performed**: Select all that apply from a list of predefined vehicle inspections.
* **Observation Status**: Select the status of the observation.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments about the vehicle.
* **Attachment URLs**: Provide URLs of attachments related to the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identifiers**: Provide any relevant vehicle identifiers.
* **Notes**: Provide additional notes about the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Select the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year**: Select the year of the vehicle.
* **Last Updated**: This field automatically populates with the date the form was last updated and is not editable.
* **Last Updated By**: This field automatically populates with the person who last updated the form and is not editable.

## Tips
- Make sure to select all that apply from the **Vehicle Checks** and **Vehicle Inspections Performed** sections.
- Ensure that the **Safety Manager Verified** section is confirmed after completing the vehicle check.
- Review and update the **Date Created** and **Last Updated** sections as needed.
- Enter a detailed description of the vehicle's condition in the **Observation Notes** section.
- Provide accurate and relevant information in all optional fields.
- If unsure about any field, seek assistance from a supervisor or colleague.
- Save a copy of the completed form for future reference.
