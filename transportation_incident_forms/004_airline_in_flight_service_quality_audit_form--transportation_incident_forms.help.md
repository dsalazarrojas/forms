# Airline In Flight Service Quality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the quality of service provided on airline flights. It covers various aspects of service, including crew presentation, service delivery, and overall experience. The information collected will help airlines identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments to enhance their services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name as the auditor in the "Auditor Name" field.
2. Enter the date of the audit in the "Date of Audit" field (MM/DD/YYYY format).
3. Enter the flight number in the "Flight Number" field.
4. Select the type of aircraft flown from the "Aircraft Type" field.
5. Enter the sector flown (e.g., LHR-JFK) in the "Sector Flown" field.
6. Select the cabin class audited (e.g., Economy, Premium Economy, Business, First Class) in the "Cabin Class Audited" field.
7. Answer the questions in the "Service Quality" section, rating the level of service received from 1 (Below Standard) to 5 (Excellent).
8. In the "Summary and Remarks" field, provide a brief summary of your experience and any notable comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Auditor Name**: Please enter your name as the auditor.
* **Date of Audit**: Enter the date of the audit in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number as per your ticket receipt.
* **Aircraft Type**: Select the type of aircraft flown.
* **Sector Flown**: Enter the sector flown (e.g., LHR-JFK).
* **Cabin Class Audited**: Select the cabin class audited (e.g., Economy, Premium Economy, Business, First Class).
* **Crew Grooming and Presentation**: Rate the standard of the crew uniform from 1-5, indicating the level of standard it met.
* **Crew Professionalism**: Rate the level of professionalism displayed by the crew from 1-5.
* **Greeting at Boarding**: Select whether the greeting at boarding was Excellent, Standard, Below Standard, or Not Observed.
* **Pre-departure Service Execution**: Select whether the pre-departure service was Completed Correctly, Completed with Errors, or Not Completed.
* **Meal Presentation Quality**: Rate the quality of meal presentation from 1-5.
* **Frequency of Beverage Service**: Select whether the service was Proactive, On Request Only, or Insufficient.
* **Lavatory Cleanliness**: Rate the cleanliness of lavatories throughout the flight from 1-5.
* **Clarity and Professionalism of Safety Announcements**: Rate the clarity and professionalism of safety announcements from 1-5.
* **Auditor Summary and Remarks**: Provide a brief summary of your experience and any notable comments or suggestions.
* **Is Corrective Action Required?**: Select whether corrective action is required.

Note: Please answer the questions truthfully and accurately, providing specific instances or examples to support your ratings. The ratings will help airlines identify areas of improvement and make necessary adjustments to enhance their services.
