# flight_damage_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flight_damage_report_form is used to report damage to baggage during flights. This form helps identify and document incidents, allowing airline staff to investigate and follow up on the issues effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Select the type of incident that occurred (Accidental damage, Force Majeure, Natural Disaster, etc.)
3. Describe the incident in as much detail as possible.
4. Enter the date and time of the incident.
5. Indicate if the incident was reported by someone (and if so, who).
6. Describe the damage and include photos or videos if possible.
7. Provide additional information about the damage, such as the damage amount.
8. Indicate the status of the claim (Claim submitted, Claim not submitted, Awaiting response, Closed).
9. Confirm if this is a completed form.
10. Confirm if the form was submitted.
11. Select the reason for the damage.
12. Enter details about the baggage, including the flight number, date, and contents.
13. Describe the damage severity.
14. Identify the cause of the damage.
15. Describe the impact of the damage.
16. Finally, submit the form via email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of incident occurred?** (form_1_question_2): This field asks for the type of incident that happened to the baggage. Select from the available options.
* **Description of incident** (form_1_question_3): Please describe what happened to the baggage in as much detail as possible.
* **Date of incident** (form_1_question_4): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Time of incident** (form_1_question_5): Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Reported by** (form_1_question_6): If someone reported the incident, enter their name or title.
* **Damage description** (form_1_question_7): Describe the damage to the baggage in detail.
* **Damage photos** (form_1_question_8): If possible, attach photos of the damaged baggage.
* **Damage videos** (form_1_question_9): If possible, attach videos of the damaged baggage.
* **Damage notes** (form_1_question_10): Provide any additional information about the damage.
* **Damage amount** (form_1_question_11): Enter the estimated damage amount.
* **Damage status** (form_1_question_12): Indicate the status of the claim, such as Claim submitted, Claim not submitted, Awaiting response, Closed.
* **Form completed** (form_1_question_13): Confirm if this is a completed form.
* **Form submitted** (form_1_question_16): Confirm if the form was submitted.
* **Flight number** (form_1_question_14): Enter the flight number of the incident.
* **Flight date** (form_1_question_15): Enter the date of the flight.
* **Damage reason** (form_1_question_17): Select the reason for the damage (Accidental damage, Force majeure, etc.).
* **Baggage claim** (form_1_question_18): Enter the baggage claim reference number.
* **Baggage type** (form_1_question_19): Enter the type of baggage.
* **Baggage weight** (form_1_question_20): Enter the weight of the baggage.
* **Baggage contents** (form_1_question_21): Describe the contents of the baggage.
* **Damage severity** (form_1_question_22): Describe the severity of the damage.
* **Damage cause** (form_1_question_23): Describe the cause of the damage.
* **Damage impact** (form_1_question_24): Describe the impact of the damage.
* **Submit form** (form_1_question_25): Submit the form via email.

**Tips**

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the incident is properly documented.
* If possible, attach photos or videos to support the report.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for assistance.
* The form will be reviewed and processed by airline staff to identify and follow up on the incident.
