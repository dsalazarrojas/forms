# Cruise Line Health Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to report and document health-related incidents that occurred on a cruise line. It is essential to provide accurate and detailed information to help the cruise line's management and medical teams to assess and respond to incidents effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the incident type (Medical Issue, Injury, or Other).
2. Enter the date of the incident.
3. Provide the location where the incident occurred.
4. Specify the time of the incident.
5. Choose the affected area.
6. Enter the names of any witnesses.
7. Specify the positions and relationships of the witnesses.
8. Provide any witness details.
9. Describe any treatment given to the affected individual.
10. Enter any comments or additional information about the incident.
11. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident type** (Select One, required): Choose the category that best describes the incident (Medical Issue, Injury, or Other).
* **Incident date** (Date, required): Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident location** (Text, required): Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident time** (Time, required): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Time of day** (Select One, optional): Specify the time of day (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Affected area** (Text, optional): Enter the area affected by the incident (e.g., Cabin, Deck, etc.).
* **Incident reporter** (Text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Incident reporter position** (Text, optional): Enter your position or role.
* **Incident reporter relationship** (Select One, optional): Specify your relationship (Passenger, Crew, or Other).
* **Witness names** (Text, optional): Enter the names of any witnesses.
* **Witness positions** (Text, optional): Enter the positions or roles of the witnesses.
* **Witness relationships** (Text, optional): Enter the relationships between you and the witnesses.
* **Witness details** (Text, optional): Provide any details about the witnesses.
* **Treatment given** (Text, optional): Describe any treatment given to the affected individual.
* **Treatment given details** (Text, optional): Enter any details about the treatment given.
* **Comments** (Note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Incident reporter signature** (Text, optional): Sign your name.
* **Incident reporter date** (Date, optional): Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Incident reporter time** (Time, optional): Enter the time you signed the form.
* **Incident reporter relationship** (Select One, optional): Specify your relationship with the cruise line (Passenger, Crew, or Other).
* **Staff comments** (Note, optional): Enter any comments or information from the staff.
* **Staff signature** (Text, optional): Sign the staff member's name.
* **Staff date** (Date, optional): Enter the date the staff signed the form.
* **Staff time** (Time, optional): Enter the time the staff signed the form.
