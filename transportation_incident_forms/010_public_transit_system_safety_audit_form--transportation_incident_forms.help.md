# public_transit_system_safety_audit_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "public_transit_system_safety_audit_form" is used to report safety issues and incidents within the public transit system. This form helps safety inspectors gather information about the location, type of vehicle involved, and the actions needed to address safety concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Station Name where the incident occurred.
2. Enter the Station Address where the incident occurred.
3. Select the Station Location type:
	* Station: A fixed location where passengers board or alight.
	* Stop: A designated area where passengers wait for transit vehicles.
	* Other: A non-standard location.
4. Select the type(s) of Transit Vehicle involved:
	* Bus
	* Train
	* Other
5. Describe the Safety Issues observed.
6. Provide a brief description of the Action Needed to address the safety concerns.
7. Add any additional Comments.
8. Enter the Safety Inspector's Name who conducted the audit.
9. Select the date the audit was conducted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Station Name** (text): Enter the name of the station where the incident occurred.
* **Station Address** (text): Enter the address of the station where the incident occurred.
* **Station Location** (select_one, required):
	+ Select the type of station location where the incident occurred.
* **Transit Vehicle Type** (select_multiple, required):
	+ Select the type(s) of transit vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Safety Issues** (text): Describe the safety issues observed at the station.
* **Action Needed** (text): Provide a brief description of the action needed to address the safety concerns.
* **Comments** (text): Add any additional comments or information about the incident.
* **Safety Inspector Name** (text): Enter the name of the safety inspector who conducted the audit.
* **Safety Inspector Date** (date, required): Select the date the audit was conducted.
