# Aviation Baggage Handling Incident Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and document baggage handling incidents that occurred during the handling of passenger baggage. The information collected will be used to analyze and identify the root cause of the incident, and to guide corrective actions to prevent similar incidents from happening in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the type of incident from the options provided.
2.  Enter the date the incident occurred.
3.  Enter the approximate time the incident occurred (if applicable).
4.  Enter the three-letter airport code where the incident took place.
5.  Enter the flight number associated with the incident.
6.  Enter the two-letter airline code.
7.  Enter your full name.
8.  Enter your employee ID.
9.  Select the department you belong to from the options provided.
10. Enter your work email address.
11. Enter your contact phone number.
12. Enter the name of the affected passenger (if known).
13. Enter the baggage claim tag or tracking number.
14. Enter a detailed description of the baggage's condition (color, size, brand, etc.).
15. Enter the current condition of the baggage.
16. Select where on the baggage the damage is located (if applicable).
17. Select the specific location where the incident occurred.
18. Enter a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
19. Indicate whether anyone witnessed the incident (if applicable).
20. Enter the names of any witnesses (if applicable).
21. Indicate whether photos were taken of the incident.
22. Enter any immediate actions taken after the incident.
23. Indicate whether the passenger has been informed of the incident.
24. Rate the severity level of the incident based on the impact on the passenger.
25. Enter any corrective actions needed (if applicable).
26. Select the current status of this report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Type**: Select the type of baggage handling incident that occurred.
* **Incident Date**: Enter the date the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time**: Enter the approximate time the incident occurred (if applicable).
* **Airport Code**: Enter the three-letter airport code where the incident took place.
* **Flight Number**: Enter the flight number associated with the incident.
* **Airline Code**: Enter the two-letter airline code.
* **Reporter Full Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID (if applicable).
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to from the options provided.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Passenger Name**: Enter the name of the affected passenger (if known).
* **Baggage Tag Number**: Enter the baggage claim tag or tracking number.
* **Baggage Description**: Enter a detailed description of the baggage's condition (color, size, brand, etc.).
* **Baggage Condition**: Select the current condition of the baggage.
* **Damage Location**: Select where on the baggage the damage is located (if applicable).
* **Specific Incident Location**: Select the specific location where the incident occurred.
* **Detailed Incident Description**: Enter a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Were There Witnesses**: Indicate whether anyone witnessed the incident (if applicable).
* **Witness Names**: Enter the names of any witnesses (if applicable).
* **Were Photos Taken**: Indicate whether photos were taken of the incident.
* **Immediate Action Taken**: Enter any immediate actions taken after the incident.
* **Was Passenger Notified**: Indicate whether the passenger has been informed of the incident.
* **Severity Level**: Rate the severity level of the incident based on the impact on the passenger.
* **Corrective Actions Needed**: Enter any corrective actions needed (if applicable).
* **Report Status**: Select the current status of this report.
