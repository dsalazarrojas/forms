# Vehicle Stop Procedures Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Stop Procedures Quiz is designed to collect data on vehicle stop incidents that occur on the transportation network. This data will help improve safety and efficiency on the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Choose the correct options from the drop-down menus for each question.
3. Answer all questions thoroughly and truthfully to provide valuable insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Driver Information** (driver, note, required: false)
* This field is optional and not required for the form submission. It is intended for any additional comments or details about the driver that may be relevant to the incident.

### 2. **Vehicle Type** (vehicle_type, select_one, required: true)
* Select the type of vehicle that was involved in the incident from the given options.

### 3. **Vehicle Color** (vehicle_color, select_multiple, required: false)
* Select the color of the vehicle that was involved in the incident from the given options.

### 4. **Vehicle Make** (vehicle_make, text, required: false)
* Enter the make of the vehicle that was involved in the incident.

### 5. **Driver Experience** (driver_experience, number, required: false)
* Enter the driver's level of experience with the vehicle or the specific route where the incident occurred.

### 6. **Traffic Stop Condition** (traffic_stop_condition, select_multiple, required: false)
* Select the condition that led to the traffic stop from the given options.

### 7. **Vehicle Weight** (vehicle_weight, number, required: false)
* Enter the weight of the vehicle that was involved in the incident.

### 8. **Stop Sign** (stop_sign, select_one, required: false)
* Select whether a stop sign was present at the location where the incident occurred.

### 9. **Vehicle Speed** (vehicle_speed, number, required: false)
* Enter the speed of the vehicle when the incident occurred.

### 10. **Stop Light** (stop_light, select_one, required: false)
* Select whether a stop light was present at the location where the incident occurred.

### 11. **Driver Attention** (driver_attention, select_multiple, required: false)
* Select whether the driver was paying attention to the road or not at the time of the incident.

### 12. **Traffic Stop Type** (traffic_stop_type, select_multiple, required: false)
* Select the type of traffic stop that occurred from the given options.

### 13. **Vehicle Height** (vehicle_height, number, required: false)
* Enter the height of the vehicle that was involved in the incident.

### 14. **Stop Duration** (stop_duration, number, required: false)
* Enter the duration of the traffic stop in seconds.

### 15. **Driver Distance** (driver_distance, number, required: false)
* Enter the distance traveled by the vehicle before and after the incident.

### 16. **Traffic Stop Condition 2** (traffic_stop_condition_2, select_multiple, required: false)
* Select the condition that led to the second traffic stop from the given options.

### 17. **Vehicle Length** (vehicle_length, number, required: false)
* Enter the length of the vehicle that was involved in the incident.

### 18. **Stop Frequency** (stop_frequency, select_one, required: false)
* Select how often traffic stops occur at the location where the incident occurred.

### 19. **Vehicle Weight Rating** (vehicle_weight_rating, number, required: false)
* Enter the weight rating of the vehicle that was involved in the incident.

### 20. **Driver Weight** (driver_weight, number, required: false)
* Enter the weight of the driver involved in the incident.

### 21. **Traffic Stop Type 2** (traffic_stop_type_2, select_multiple, required: false)
* Select the type of traffic stop 2 that occurred from the given options.

### 22. **Stop Distance** (stop_distance, number, required: false)
* Enter the distance traveled by the vehicle during the traffic stop.

### 23. **Traffic Stop Duration** (traffic_stop_duration, number, required: false)
* Enter the duration of the second traffic stop in seconds.

### 24. **Traffic Stop Position** (traffic_stop_position, select_multiple, required: false)
* Select the position of the traffic stop from the given options.

### 25. **Submit** (submit, text, required: true)
* Enter a brief description of the incident and any additional comments or information.
* Make sure to proofread for accuracy before submitting.
