# Pet Monitoring Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pet Monitoring Checklist form is used to keep track of a pet's status over time, gathering information about its name, breed, weight, condition, and other relevant details. This form is typically used by veterinarians or pet owners to ensure the pet's health and well-being are being monitored consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the pet's name in the "pet_name" field.
2. Select the pet's breed from the "pet_breed" field.
3. Enter the pet's weight in the "pet_weight" field.
4. Select any conditions that apply to the pet's current state from the "pet_condition" field.
5. Enter the time of the last monitoring session in the "pet_monitoring_time" field.
6. Enter any notes or comments about the pet's condition in the "pet_monitoring_notes" field.
7. Select the frequency of monitoring for this pet (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly) from the "pet_monitoring_frequency" field.
8. Enter the email address of the contact person for follow-up actions in the "contact_email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "contact_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_name** (`pet_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the pet being monitored.
* **pet_breed** (`pet_breed`, select_one, required): Select the breed of the pet from the available options.
* **pet_weight** (`pet_weight`, number, required): Enter the current weight of the pet.
* **pet_condition** (`pet_condition`, select_multiple, required): Select any conditions that apply to the pet's current state.
* **pet_monitoring_time** (`pet_monitoring_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the last monitoring session.
* **pet_monitoring_notes** (`pet_monitoring_notes`, note, required): Enter any notes or comments about the pet's condition.
* **pet_monitoring_frequency** (`pet_monitoring_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of monitoring for this pet.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person for follow-up actions.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Tips
- Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure all relevant information is recorded accurately.
- If you need to report a new condition, update the "pet_condition" field accordingly.
- If the pet's monitoring frequency has changed, update the "pet_monitoring_frequency" field.
- Ensure the contact information is up-to-date in case of any follow-up actions.
