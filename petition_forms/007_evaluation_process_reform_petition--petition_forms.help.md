# Evaluation Process Reform Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who want to express their support or feedback on the evaluation process. If you are a member of the public, a stakeholder, or an employee, you can use this form to share your thoughts and opinions on how to reform the evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Name, Email) if you want to be contacted for further discussions.
2. Provide your supporter details (Name, Email) if you are supporting someone else.
3. Add a message to explain your support or feedback.
4. Add your signature and date/time to confirm your submission.
5. If you are supporting someone else, add their signature and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information**: This is where you can enter your name and contact email (if you want to be contacted for further discussions).
* **Name**: If you are supporting someone else, enter their name here.
* **Email**: If you are supporting someone else, enter their email here.
* **Supporter Details**: This is where you can add more details about why you are supporting the petition.
* **Signature**: This is a required field to confirm that you are a real person and have actually submitted this form.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Feedback**: This is where you can add any additional comments or suggestions on how to reform the evaluation process.
* **Supporter Signature**: If you are supporting someone else, this is where they should sign to confirm their support.
* **Supporter Name**: If you are supporting someone else, enter their name here.
* **Supporter Email**: If you are supporting someone else, enter their email here.
* **Supporter Message**: This is where you can add a message from the supporter, explaining their reasons for supporting the petition.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully, as it will be used to express your genuine support or feedback.
* If you are supporting someone else, make sure they know about this form and have given their consent.
* If you have any concerns or doubts, contact: form@gic.mx
