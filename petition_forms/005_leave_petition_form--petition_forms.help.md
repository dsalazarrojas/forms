# leave_petition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leave Petition Form is used to collect information from employees about their leave requests. This form helps managers and HR teams track and manage employee leaves in an organized and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete data for each field, as this will help us process your leave request efficiently.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (1, required): Enter your unique employee ID number to identify yourself in our system.
* **Start Date** (2, required): Select the date you want your leave to start from the calendar. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date** (3, required): Select the date you want your leave to end. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Reason** (4, required): Briefly describe the reason for your leave. Please be as concise as possible while still providing sufficient information.
* **Duration** (5, required): Specify the number of days you will be taking for your leave.
* **Status** (6, required): Select the status of your leave request from the options: Approved, Rejected, or Pending.
* **Assigned To** (7, required): Choose the person or team that will be handling your leave request from the options: chatjimmy or Other.
* **Comments** (8, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your leave request. This is optional, but it can help us understand your request better.
