# Online Petition Signing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to sign a petition. The form gathers basic contact information, including first name, last name, email address, and date signed. It also asks for a confirmation of the email address and a signature. Additionally, the form includes fields for departmental and faculty affiliations, as well as comments and date/time signed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
5. Enter the date you signed the petition in the "Date Signed" field.
6. Enter your signature in the "Signature" field.
7. (Optional) Add any contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
8. (Optional) Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
9. Select your departmental affiliation(s) from the "Department" field. You can select multiple departments.
10. Indicate if you are a faculty member from the "Faculty Member" field. You can select multiple faculty statuses.
11. (Optional) Confirm your departmental affiliation(s) in the "Department Agreed" field.
12. (Optional) Confirm your faculty status in the "Faculty Agreed" field.
13. Select whether you agreed to the petition from the "Agreed" field.
14. (Optional) Select your departmental affiliation for a second petition from the "Department 2" field.
15. (Optional) Indicate if you are a faculty member for a second petition from the "Faculty 2" field.
16. (Optional) Confirm your departmental affiliation for a second petition in the "Department 2" field.
17. (Optional) Confirm your faculty status for a second petition in the "Faculty 2" field.
18. (Optional) Select whether you agreed to a second petition from the "Agreed 2" field.
19. (Optional) Select your departmental affiliation for a third petition from the "Department 3" field.
20. (Optional) Indicate if you are a faculty member for a third petition from the "Faculty 3" field.
21. (Optional) Confirm your departmental affiliation for a third petition in the "Department 3" field.
22. (Optional) Confirm your faculty status for a third petition in the "Faculty 3" field.
23. (Optional) Select whether you agreed to a third petition from the "Agreed 3" field.
24. (Optional) Select your departmental affiliation for a fourth petition from the "Department 4" field.
25. (Optional) Indicate if you are a faculty member for a fourth petition from the "Faculty 4" field.
26. (Optional) Confirm your departmental affiliation for a fourth petition in the "Department 4" field.
27. (Optional) Confirm your faculty status for a fourth petition in the "Faculty 4" field.
28. (Optional) Select whether you agreed to a fourth petition from the "Agreed 2" field.
29. (Optional) Select your departmental affiliation for a fifth petition from the "Department 5" field.
30. (Optional) Indicate if you are a faculty member for a fifth petition from the "Faculty 5" field.
31. (Optional) Confirm your departmental affiliation for a fifth petition in the "Department 5" field.
32. (Optional) Confirm your faculty status for a fifth petition in the "Faculty 5" field.
33. (Optional) Select whether you agreed to a fifth petition from the "Agreed 2" field.
34. (Optional) Enter any additional comments for a second petition in the "Comments 2" field.
35. (Optional) Enter the date and time you signed the petition in the "Date" and "Time" fields.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly, as it will be used for future communications.
* Ensure that you select the correct departmental affiliation(s) and faculty status.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
* If you have any further questions, please contact us.
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you need to sign multiple petitions, please fill out the form for each petition separately.
