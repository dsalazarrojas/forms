# Noise Ordinance Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for petitioning against or in support of noise-related issues in the community. It collects information from petitioners and their neighbors to gather relevant data for noise ordinance enforcement and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number, but this is optional.
3. Sign and date your petition.
4. Indicate whether or not your neighbors support the petition.
5. Note any comments or issues you have regarding the noise ordinance.
6. Specify the level of noise impact you are experiencing.
7. Choose the resolution method you believe is most effective.
8. Indicate the success of the resolution method.
9. Provide a follow-up date for future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner Name (1)**: Enter your name as the person submitting this petition.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Petitioner Phone Number (3)**: Enter your phone number, if you choose to provide it. This is optional.
* **Signature (4)**: Date and sign this petition to confirm your identity.
* **Neighbor's Names (6)**: Check if your neighbors support the petition.
* **Neighbor's Email (7)**: Check if your neighbors have provided an email address for contact.
* **Neighbor's Phone (8)**: Check if your neighbors have provided a phone number for contact.
* **Issues (9)**: Check if the noise ordinance is being broken.
* **Comments (10)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the noise issue.
* **Issues Comments (11)**: Enter any additional comments about the issues being addressed.
* **Noise Level (12)**: Enter a numerical value indicating the level of noise impact you are experiencing.
* **Impact (13)**: Choose the level of noise impact you are experiencing.
* **Resolution Method (14)**: Choose the method you believe will best resolve the issue.
* **Resolution Success (15)**: Rate the success of the resolution method you chose.
* **Follow Up (16)**: Enter a date for follow-up purposes.

Note: The fields labeled as "select_multiple" and "select_one" require you to choose one or more options from the provided list.
