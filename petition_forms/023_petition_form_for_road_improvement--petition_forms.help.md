# Petition Form for Road Improvement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Petition Form for Road Improvement is a form used to collect information from users about their opinions or suggestions regarding road improvements in their area.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "user_name" field.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "email" field to allow us to contact you if needed.
3. Enter your address in the "address" field.
4. Add a comment about the road improvement you are suggesting in the "comment" field.
5. Indicate whether you are in favor of ("Yes") or not ("No") the road improvement by selecting an option in the "signature" field.
6. If "Yes", enter the number of people who support the idea in the "signature_count" field.
7. If you have a specific category for your petition, please select it from the "category" field. We have three options: "Petition", "Road", and "Improvement". If your category does not match any of these, you can enter the category in the "category_other" field.
8. If relevant to your petition, please enter the date of the event or issue you are addressing in the "date" field.
9. If relevant to your petition, please enter the time of the day of the event or issue you are addressing in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **email**: A valid email address where we can contact you if needed.
*   **address**: Your full address for reference.
*   **comment**: Provide a brief description of the road improvement you are suggesting.
*   **signature**: Indicate whether you support the road improvement. If you do, please select "Yes".
*   **signature_count**: If you selected "Yes", enter the number of people who support the idea.
*   **category**: Select the category that best describes your petition. We have three options: "Petition", "Road", and "Improvement".
*   **category_other**: If your category does not match any of the three options, enter it here.
*   **date**: If relevant to your petition, enter the date of the event or issue you are addressing.
*   **time**: If relevant to your petition, enter the time of the day of the event or issue you are addressing.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all fields with accurate and relevant information.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us.
* We will review and verify the submitted petitions and respond accordingly.
* This form is intended for constructive feedback and suggestions only.
