# Alumni Chapter Reactivation Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Chapter Reactivation Petition is a form to be completed by alumni chapters that wish to re-activate their chapter. This form will help the national organization understand the chapter's plans and goals for reactivation, ensuring that they meet the necessary requirements and have the support needed for a successful reactivation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have all the necessary information and supporting documents ready before starting.
2.  Complete each section of the form carefully, providing accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Review your answers for completeness and clarity before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Chapter Location (1)**: Enter the city and state of your chapter.
* **-- Petition Purpose and Rationale -- (2)**: Briefly explain why your chapter should be reactivated.
* **Reason for Reactivation Request (3)**: Describe the reasons why you want to re-activate your chapter and what benefits it will bring to the alumni community.
* **Estimated Alumni Population in Area (4)**: Estimate how many alumni live in your region.
* **Brief Chapter History (5)**: Provide a brief history of your chapter, including when it was last active and why it closed.
* **Proposed Activities and Events (6)**: Describe the events and activities your chapter plans to host.
* **Leadership Team Available (7)**: List the names and roles of your core leaders.
* **-- Primary Contact Information -- (8)**: Provide contact information for the person initiating the petition.
* **First Name (9)**: Enter the first name of the primary contact.
* **Last Name (10)**: Enter the last name of the primary contact.
* **Email Address (11)**: Enter the email address of the primary contact.
* **Phone Number (12)**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact.
* **Class Year (13)**: Enter your graduation year.
* **-- Supporting Signatories -- (14)**: List the names of alumni who support the re-activation of your chapter. You may include their titles and positions.
* **Minimum Signatures Required (15)**: Estimate the minimum number of signatures needed to support your chapter's re-activation.
* **Current Number of Signatures (16)**: Estimate the current number of signatures collected.
* **-- Budget and Resources -- (17)**: Explain your financial plans and resources needed for re-activation.
* **Anticipated Annual Budget (18)**: Estimate the annual budget needed for re-activation.
* **Proposed Funding Sources (19)**: Describe the proposed funding sources.
* **Resources Needed from National (20)**: Describe any resources needed from the national organization.
* **-- Additional Information -- (21)**: Provide any additional relevant information that supports your re-activation request.
* **Supporting Documentation Available (22)**: If you have supporting documentation, such as a contact list or meeting minutes, indicate that it is available.
* **Expected Timeline for Reactivation (23)**: Provide an estimated timeline for when you plan to re-activate your chapter.
* **Commitment to Sustained Activity (24)**: Commit to maintaining chapter operations after re-activation.
* **Additional Comments (25)**: Provide any additional information that may support your request.

By following these steps and completing this form carefully, you will ensure that your re-activation petition is well-received by the national organization.
