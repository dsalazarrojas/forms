# Support Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Petition Form is a simple way to collect information from individuals who want to express their support for a cause or issue. This form is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from any form platform and is an original adaptation created by AI.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Support Petition Form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the text fields. If you prefer not to provide this information, you can leave these fields blank.
- Enter your Email Address in the format of a typical email address. This is a required field to allow us to contact you if needed.
- Select one or more options from the Support Expression field to indicate your level of support. Options are: Yes, Maybe, No.
- If you choose to provide a reason for your support, enter a brief explanation in the text field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format of: `name@email.com`.
- **Support Expression** (`support_expression`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu to express your level of support: Yes, Maybe, No.
  - Choosing "Yes" means you strongly agree with the cause.
  - Choosing "Maybe" means you are neutral or unsure.
  - Choosing "No" means you do not support the cause.
- **Reason for Support** (`reason_for_support`, text, optional): If you want to provide more context, enter a brief explanation of why you support the cause.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your information is recorded correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
