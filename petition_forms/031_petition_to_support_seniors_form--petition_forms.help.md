# petition_to_support_seniors_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The petition_to_support_seniors_form is designed to collect information from seniors and caregivers about their needs, concerns, and issues. This data will be used to provide more effective support and services to seniors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Senior Name field with the name of the senior or individual who needs support.
2. Enter the Contact Number of the senior or individual in the Contact Number field.
3. Enter the senior's Email address in the Email field.
4. Provide a detailed description of the issue or concern in the Issue Description field.
5. Select all the organizations and leaders that can support the senior or individual from the Supporting Organizations and Supporting Leaders fields respectively.
6. Enter the date and time of when the issue started or became a concern in the Date and Time fields respectively.
7. Enter the location where the issue is happening or affecting the senior or individual in the Location field.
8. Add any additional comments or information about the issue in the Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Senior Name** (`senior_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the senior or individual who needs support.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter the contact number of the senior or individual.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the senior or individual.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the issue or concern.
* **Supporting Organizations** (`supporting_orgs`, select_multiple, required): Select all the organizations that can support the senior or individual.
* **Supporting Leaders** (`supporting_leaders`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the leaders that can support the senior or individual (optional).
* **Senior Signature** (`senior_signature`, date, required): Enter the date of when the issue started or became a concern.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of when the issue is happening or affecting the senior or individual.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of when the issue is happening or affecting the senior or individual.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the issue is happening or affecting the senior or individual.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the issue.
