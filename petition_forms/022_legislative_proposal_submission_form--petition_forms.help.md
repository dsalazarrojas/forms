# legislative_proposal_submission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for legislative proposal submissions. It is intended to help individuals or organizations submit their ideas, proposals, and suggestions to be considered for implementation or consideration by a governing body or decision-maker.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields (if any) with the requested information.
* For fields that are not required, you can leave them blank if they do not apply to your submission.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legislative Proposal Submission Form**
	+ **Legislative Proposal** (id: 1)
		- This is the main idea or proposal you wish to submit.
* **Name** (id: 2)
	+ Please provide your name as the submitter of this proposal.
* **Organization** (id: 3)
	+ If you are submitting on behalf of an organization, please provide its name.
* **Idea Title** (id: 4)
	+ A brief title that summarizes your proposal.
* **Proposal** (id: 5)
	+ A detailed description of your proposal, including any supporting information or evidence.
* **Category** (id: 6)
	+ Select one or more categories that best describe the nature of your proposal, such as Legislative or Petition.
* **Date Submitted** (id: 7)
	+ The date you are submitting this proposal.
* **Contact Name** (id: 8)
	+ Your name and contact information for follow-up or further discussion.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your proposal is clear, concise, and well-written.
* Review your submission before submitting it to ensure it accurately represents your idea.
* If you have any doubts or need clarification on any field, do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
