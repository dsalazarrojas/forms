# Sheriff Candidate Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for collecting support for a sheriff candidate. It's a petition that allows users to express their endorsement of a specific candidate by providing their name, contact information, and selection of the candidate and party they support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your support for the candidate.
2. Enter the name of the candidate you are supporting.
3. Enter your contact information (this will be used by the campaign to get in touch with you).
4. Choose the party you are supporting from the given options.
5. Click 'Submit' to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Support the Candidate**: 
  Select 'Yes' to express your support for the candidate. This indicates your endorsement and willingness to be contacted by the campaign.
* **Candidate Name**:
  Enter the name of the candidate you are supporting. This will help the campaign identify you as an endorser.
* **Contact Info**:
  Enter your name, address, phone number, and email address (if applicable) to let the campaign get in touch with you.
* **Party Name**:
  Select the party you are supporting from the given options. This helps the campaign understand who is backing the candidate.
* **Party**:
  Select who you are supporting from the given options. This will help the campaign understand the breadth of support for the candidate.
