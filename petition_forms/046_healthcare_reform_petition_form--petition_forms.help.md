# healthcare_reform_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a petition tool for healthcare reform. It's designed to collect signatures and information from individuals and organizations in support of policy changes related to healthcare.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address and Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Enter your Zip Code for geographical information.
4. Select your preferred Policy Change from the dropdown options.
5. Choose whether you're signing as an Individual or an Organization.
6. Sign and date your signature (mm/dd/yyyy format).
7. Sign and time your signature (HH:MM AM/PM format).
8. Add any additional Note if required.
9. Select the Category of the petition (Petition Forms, Healthcare, Reform).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Zip Code** (text, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Policy Change** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Single-Payer System
	+ Multi-Payer System
	+ Medicare for All
	+ Universal Coverage
* **Signature Type** (select_one, required: false):
	+ Individual
	+ Organization
* **Signature Date** (date, required: false): Enter the date of your signature in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Signature Time** (time, required: false): Enter the time of your signature in HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Note** (note, required: false): Add any additional note if required.
* **Category** (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Petition Forms
	+ Healthcare
	+ Reform

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
* Use the correct format for date and time entries (mm/dd/yyyy and HH:MM AM/PM respectively).
* Select the correct category for your petition.
