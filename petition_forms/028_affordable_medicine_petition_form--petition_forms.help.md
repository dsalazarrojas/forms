# Affordable Medicine Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect stories and experiences of individuals affected by high medication costs, providing a platform for supporters to voice their concerns and advocate for policy change. By sharing your story, you'll help raise awareness and build momentum for more affordable medicine initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with accurate and truthful information about your experience with high medication costs. Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise specified. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Your date of birth.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, optional): Email for updates on petition progress.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): Phone number for petition updates (if you'd like to be contacted directly).
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Your residential address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (`City, State, ZIP Code`, text, required): City, state, and ZIP code of your address.
* **Personal Experience with Medication Costs** (`Personal Experience with Medication Costs`, text, required): Describe your personal experience with high medication costs.
* **Affected Family Members** (`Affected Family Members`, select one, required): Check if family members are affected by high medication costs.
* **Details of Affected Family Members** (`Details of Affected Family Members`, text, optional): If yes, please describe how family members are affected.
* **Specific Medications Affected** (`Specific Medications Affected`, text, optional): Which medications are too expensive for you or your family?
* **Impact of Medication Costs** (`Impact of Medication Costs`, select multiple, required): How do high medication costs affect your access to necessary medications? 
	+ Skipping doses
	+ Taking less than prescribed
	+ Delaying filling prescriptions
	+ Choosing between medications
	+ Forgoing medications entirely
	+ Switching to cheaper alternatives
	+ Using international pharmacies
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Financial Impact** (`Financial Impact`, text, required): How do high medication costs impact your finances?
* **Health Consequences** (`Health Consequences`, text, optional): What health consequences have resulted from your inability to afford medications?
* **Insurance Coverage** (`Insurance Coverage`, select one, required): What type of insurance coverage do you have?
	+ Private Insurance
	+ Medicare
	+ Medicaid
	+ Uninsured
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Reason for Supporting the Petition** (`Reason for Supporting the Petition`, text, required): Why do you support affordable medicine initiatives?
* **Desired Policy Change** (`Desired Policy Change`, text, required): What specific policy change do you hope to achieve?
* **Pharmaceutical Industry View** (`Pharmaceutical Industry View`, text, optional): What is your view on pharmaceutical pricing practices?
* **Awareness of Alternative Solutions** (`Awareness of Alternative Solutions`, text, optional): Are you aware of any alternative solutions to high medication costs?
* **Advocacy History** (`Advocacy History`, select one, optional): Have you previously advocated for healthcare or medication access issues?
* **Details of Previous Advocacy** (`Details of Previous Advocacy`, text, optional): If yes, please describe your previous advocacy efforts.
* **Willingness to Participate** (`Willingness to Participate`, select one, required): Are you willing to participate in further advocacy efforts?
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe
* **Preferred Method of Participation** (`Preferred Method of Participation`, select multiple, optional): How would you prefer to participate in advocacy efforts?
	+ Email updates
	+ Phone calls to representatives
	+ Attending rallies
	+ Writing letters
	+ Social media advocacy
	+ Volunteering
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Any other comments about affordable medicine access.
* **Electronic Signature** (`Electronic Signature`, text, required): Sign to add your support to the petition.
