# Faculty Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Petition Form is a tool designed to help faculty members submit petitions to the university administration. This form is used to gather information about the petition, the submitter, and any supporting members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for the petition in the "Faculty Petition" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the petition in the "Petition Details" field.
3. Specify the reason for the petition in the "Reason for Petition" field.
4. List any supporting faculty members in the "Supporting Members" field, separated by commas.
5. Select the supporting members from the list in the "Supporting Members List" field.
6. Describe the proposed action in the "Proposed Action" field.
7. Provide additional details about the proposed action in the "Proposed Action Details" field.
8. Enter a brief statement supporting the petition in the "Supporting Statement" field.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes about the petition in the "Faculty Petition Form Submitter Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Petition** (`faculty_petition_form`): A brief title for the petition.
* **Petition Details** (`petition_details`): A brief description of the petition.
* **Reason for Petition** (`reason_for_petition`): The reason why the petition is being submitted.
* **Supporting Members** (`supporting_members`): A list of faculty members supporting the petition, separated by commas.
* **Supporting Members List** (`supporting_members_list`): A list of supporting faculty members to select from.
* **Proposed Action** (`proposed_action`): A description of the proposed action.
* **Proposed Action Details** (`proposed_action_details`): Additional details about the proposed action.
* **Supporting Statement** (`supporting_statement`): A brief statement supporting the petition.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter`): The name and title of the faculty member submitting the petition.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Details** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_details`): Additional details about the submitter, including department, position, affiliation, and institutional unit.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Email** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_email`): The email address of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Phone** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_phone`): The phone number of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Department** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_department`): The department of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Position** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_position`): The position of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Affiliation** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_affiliation`): The affiliation of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Institutional Role** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_institutional_role`): The institutional role of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Institutional Unit** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_institutional_unit`): The institutional unit of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Institutional Group** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_institutional_group`): The institutional group of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Institutional Level** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_institutional_level`): The institutional level of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Institutional Title** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_institutional_title`): The institutional title of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Notes** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_notes`): Additional comments or notes about the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Additional Comments** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_additional_comments`): Additional comments about the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Signature** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_signature`): The signature of the submitter.
* **Faculty Petition Form Submitter Agreement** (`faculty_petition_form_submitter_agreement`): A statement of agreement from the submitter.

Note: The form is designed to guide users through the submission process and provide clear explanations of each field's purpose.
