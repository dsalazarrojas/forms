# Agricultural Advisory Council Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Advisory Council Petition Form is a tool for petitioners to communicate their concerns and requests to the council. It helps the council understand the scope and urgency of the issue, which informs their decision-making process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Petitioner Name** field.
2. If you are representing an organization or farm, enter their name in the **Organization or Farm Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field (optional).
5. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
6. Select the **Petition Topic** that best describes your concern.
7. Provide a clear explanation of the issue in the **Petition Details** field.
8. Suggest a proposed solution in the **Proposed Solution** field.
9. Estimate the number of supporters you have in the **Number of Supporters** field (optional).
10. Rate the **Impact Assessment** of the issue (High, Medium, or Low).
11. Indicate the **Urgency Level** needed to address the issue (Immediate, Short-term, or Long-term).
12. Check the **Agreement to Terms** field to confirm you have filled out the form accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Petitioner Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Organization or Farm Name**: If you are representing an organization or farm, enter their name.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where the council can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further communication (optional).
* **Address**: Enter your address for the council's reference.
* **Petition Topic**: Select the primary subject of your concern.
* **Petition Details**: Explain the issue clearly and provide context.
* **Proposed Solution**: Suggest a specific action the council can take to address the issue.
* **Number of Supporters**: Estimate the number of people supporting your concern (optional).
* **Impact Assessment**: Rate the significance of the issue (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Urgency Level**: Indicate how quickly the council needs to address the issue (Immediate, Short-term, or Long-term).
* **Agreement to Terms**: Confirm that you have filled out the form accurately.
