# Community Action Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Action Petition Form is a tool for users to create and manage online petitions, allowing them to gather support and signatures for their cause. The form is divided into several sections, each designed to capture specific information about the petition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Petition Information**: Enter a brief description of the petition, including details about the cause and reason for creating it.
2. **Petition Title**: Provide a title for the petition, summarizing the main issue or cause.
3. **Petition ID**: Enter a unique reference number for the petition, to track its progress and status.
4. **Petition Date**: Choose the date when the petition was started.
5. **Organizer Information**: Enter details about the person organizing the petition.
6. **Organizer Name**: Enter the full name of the person or organization behind the petition.
7. **Organizer Email**: Enter the email address of the person or organization responsible for the petition.
8. **Organizer Phone**: Enter a contact phone number for the person or organization.
9. **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization behind the petition.
10. **Petition Details**: Enter a brief description of the petition cause or issue.
11. **Petition Purpose**: State the specific purpose of the petition.
12. **Petition Description**: Enter a detailed description of the issue or cause being addressed.
13. **Target Audience**: Identify who the petition is directed to.
14. **Action Requested**: Specify what action the supporters are being asked to take.
15. **Petition Category**: Select a category that best describes the petition's focus (e.g., Environmental, Social Justice, Education, etc.).
16. **Goal Signature Count**: Enter the target number of signatures the petition hopes to achieve.
17. **Deadline Date**: Choose a date when the petition will be delivered or considered complete.
18. **Supporter Information**: Enter details about the person signing the petition.
19. **Supporter Name**: Enter the full name of the person signing the petition.
20. **Supporter Email**: Enter the email address of the person signing the petition.
21. **Supporter Address**: Enter the street address of the person signing the petition.
22. **Supporter City**: Enter the city of the person signing the petition.
23. **Supporter State**: Enter the state or province of the person signing the petition.
24. **Supporter ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP or postal code of the person signing the petition.
25. **Supporter Country**: Enter the country of the person signing the petition.
26. **Signature Declaration**: Select whether the supporter agrees to support the petition.
27. **Signature Date**: Enter the date the signature was made.
28. **Willing to Volunteer**: Choose whether the supporter is willing to help promote the petition.
29. **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about why the supporter is signing the petition.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your petition title and category accurately reflect the cause or issue being addressed.
* Be specific when stating the purpose and action requested by the petition.
* Remember to enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
* Review the petition category options carefully to select the best fit.
* If you are not sure about any information, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
* Consider sharing the petition link with others to increase support and signature counts.
