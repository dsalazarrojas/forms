# Workplace Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Petition Form is designed to collect information from employees on various aspects of their workplace experience. This form will help management understand the needs and concerns of employees and provide a platform for employees to express their opinions on various aspects of their workplace.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your general information, including your name and contact details.
2. Describe your concerns and suggestions for improving your workplace experience in the space provided.
3. Answer questions about labor unions, employee advocacy groups, and employee assistance programs to help management better understand your needs.
4. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`title`, `required/optional`: Not required): This field is for you to provide your name and any other general information that you think is relevant to your petition.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `required/optional`: Not required): This field is for you to provide your contact information, such as your email address and phone number, so that management can contact you if they need to follow up on your petition.
### Contact Details (Page 2)
* **Concerns** (`concerns`, `required/optional`: Not required): This field is for you to describe your concerns and suggestions for improving your workplace experience.
* **Management Response** (`management_response`, `required/optional`: Not required): This field is for management to respond to your concerns and suggestions, if they choose to do so.
### Additional Options (Page 3)
* **Labor Unions** (`labor_unions`, `required/optional`: Not required): This field is for you to indicate whether you are part of a labor union. This will help management understand your perspective on workplace issues.
* **Employee Advocacy Groups** (`employee_advocacy_groups`, `required/optional`: Not required): This field is for you to indicate whether you are part of an employee advocacy group. This will help management understand your perspective on workplace issues.
* **Employee Assistance Programs** (`employee_assistance_programs`, `required/optional`: Not required): This field is for you to indicate whether you are aware of or have access to employee assistance programs, such as EAPs or employee wellness programs. This will help management understand your perspective on workplace support systems.

## Tips
* Please be specific and clear in your concerns and suggestions.
* Try to keep your responses concise and focused on the specific issue or suggestion.
* Management will review and respond to your petition, and will take your concerns and suggestions into consideration when making decisions about the workplace.
