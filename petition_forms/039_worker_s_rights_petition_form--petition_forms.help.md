# Worker's Rights Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker's Rights Petition Form is used to gather information from workers about their working conditions, rights, and grievances. This form is intended for workers who want to report issues or concerns related to their workplace or industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, including your first name, last name, email address, and company name (if applicable).
2. Describe the issue or grievance you are experiencing in the "Issue Description" field.
3. Specify your demands or requests for resolution in the "Demands" field.
4. If you have attached any documents, files, or images to support your petition, select the relevant options in the "Uploaded Documents", "Uploaded Files", "Uploaded Images", and "Uploaded Videos" fields.
5. Indicate whether you submitted this form voluntarily or were asked to do so by a supervisor or manager.
6. Finally, review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that is actively checked by you.
* **Company Name** (if applicable): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Role at Work**: Describe your role or position within your company or organization.
* **Workplace Name**: Enter the name of your workplace or organization.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue or grievance you are experiencing in detail.
* **Demands**: Specify the demands or requests you have for resolution.
* **Signatures**: Indicate whether you have attached documents or files to support your petition. Select "Yes" if you have attached files or images.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Uploaded Documents**: Select this option if you have attached documents to support your petition.
* **Uploaded Files**: Select this option if you have attached files to support your petition.
* **Uploaded Images**: Select this option if you have attached images to support your petition.
* **Uploaded Videos**: Select this option if you have attached videos to support your petition.
* **Uploaded Audio**: Select this option if you have attached audio recordings to support your petition.
* **Upload**: This is not a field for input, but a placeholder for uploading attached files.
* **Other Files**: Select this option if you have attached other types of files to support your petition.
* **Submitted By**: Indicate whether you submitted this form voluntarily or were asked to do so by a supervisor or manager.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your petition is considered.
