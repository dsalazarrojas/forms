# Online Petition Form With E Signature - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create online petitions. Petitions can be categorized under "Petition Forms" or other forms, and each petition has a unique name and description. You can also specify a start and end date for the petition, as well as a category for embedded reports and assigned tools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the name and description of your petition.
2. Choose the category under which the petition will be listed ("Petition Forms" or other forms).
3. Select the start and end dates for the petition.
4. You can add a signature if you want to sign the petition.
5. If necessary, add embedded reports to provide more information about the petition.
6. Assign a tool or resource to the petition if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, required: false): Enter a unique name for your petition.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the category under which your petition will be listed. Select "Petition Forms" or other forms.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Provide a description of your petition to give context and details.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Choose the start date for your petition.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Choose the end date for your petition.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Add your signature to show your support for the petition.
* **Embedded Reports** (`embedded_reports`, text, required: false): Provide additional reports or information about your petition.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Choose a tool or resource to be assigned to the petition.
