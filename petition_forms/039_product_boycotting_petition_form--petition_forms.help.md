# product_bycotting_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product bycotting petition form is a tool for customers to express their opposition to a certain product or company. This form allows you to provide feedback and concerns about products or businesses that are causing harm or negative impacts on the environment, animals, or human rights.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "first and last name" field.
2. Provide your mailing address in the "mailing address" field.
3. Describe the reason for your concern in the "reason for signing" field.
4. Select all applicable concerns from the list, such as "animal cruelty", "environmental harm", "human rights violations", or "labor abuses".
5. If you are representing a business, enter the name of the business and its address in the corresponding fields.
6. Finally, click the submit button to send your concerns and signatures to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first and last name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first and last name to associate your signature with your identity.
* **mailing address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Provide your full mailing address to help us contact you if needed.
* **reason_for_signing** (`reason_petition`, `text`, required: false): Describe the reason for your concern or opposition to the product or company.
* **concerns** (`concerns`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all applicable concerns that apply to your situation, such as "animal cruelty", "environmental harm", "human rights violations", or "labor abuses".
* **your_signature** (`signature`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you are signing this petition.
* **name_of_business** (`name_of_business`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing a business, enter the name of the business.
* **business_mailing_address** (`business_address`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing a business, provide the business's mailing address.
* **city** (`business_city`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing a business, enter the city of the business's location.
* **state** (`business_state`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing a business, enter the state of the business's location.
* **zip/postal code** (`business_zip`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing a business, enter the business's postal code.

## Tips
* Please be specific when describing your concerns in the "reason for signing" field.
* If you are representing a business, ensure that the address and contact information for the business is accurate and up-to-date.
* We appreciate your feedback and concerns, and we will review and consider them carefully.
