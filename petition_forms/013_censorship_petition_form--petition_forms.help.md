# Censorship Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect signatures and statements of support for the protection of diverse voices and freedom of expression against censorship.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read the **Statement of Purpose** to understand the underlying concerns and goals of the petition.
2.  Enter your **Full Name** as a show of support for the petition.
3.  Provide your **Email Address** for signature verification and to receive updates on the petition.
4.  If you wish, enter your **Phone Number** to further contact you for updates or other purposes.
5.  Specify your **City** where you reside.
6.  Choose your **Country** of residence to help understand the geographical scope of the petition.
7.  Select up to three **Areas of Concern** that you are most worried about in terms of censorship.
8.  Optionally, share your personal **Reason for Signing** to help others understand why you support the petition.
9.  Choose whether you want your **Name to be displayed publicly** for others to see your support.
10. Select whether you want to **Receive updates on this petition** via email.
11. Finally, enter the date you sign the petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name**: Your real name that will be associated with your support for the petition. (Required)
*   **Email Address**: Your contact email, which will be used for verification of your signature. (Required)
*   **Phone Number**: An optional number to contact you about the petition. (Optional)
*   **City**: Where you reside in the world. (Required)
*   **Country**: The country of your residence. (Required)
*   **Areas of Concern**: Choose up to three areas where you feel censorship is most concerning to you: 
     + Internet and Social Media
     + Literature and Books
     + Art and Creative Expression
     + Journalism and Media
     + Academic and Research Freedom
*   **Why are you signing this petition?**: Share a personal thought or experience that motivates your support. (Optional)
*   **Allow name to be displayed publicly**: Choose if you want to show your support publicly. (Required)
*   **Receive updates on this petition**: Select if you wish to stay updated on the progress and news of the petition. (Required)
*   **Date of Signature**: The date when you sign the petition, automatically generated. (Required)

This guide is created to help users understand and navigate the Censorship Petition Form, ensuring that users provide accurate and valid information to support the collective voice against censorship.
