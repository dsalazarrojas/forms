# Petition For Music Program - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Petition For Music Program" form is designed to collect information from schools and individuals to support music programs. We aim to gather support for music programs in schools and use this data to advocate for music education.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by selecting the corresponding section for your school's music program. Section 1 is for the main school's information, and Section 2 is for additional details.
2. Fill out the required fields for each section, making sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3. If you have a supporting member, please include their name and contact information.
4. You can also sign up for updates to stay informed about our efforts to support music programs.
5. Finally, please provide your signature and contact method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Be specific and provide accurate information about your school and the music program.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your support for music programs in schools, and we look forward to hearing your story!
