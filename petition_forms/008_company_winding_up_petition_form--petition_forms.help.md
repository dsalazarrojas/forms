# Company Winding Up Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Company Winding Up Petition Form is designed to be completed by a company's creditor, member, or other authorized person to initiate the winding up process of a company. This process involves submitting evidence of the company's insolvency or other reasons for winding up, which will then be reviewed by the court.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Review the form sections and ensure that all required fields are completed.
2.  Enter the Petitioner Details, including the Petitioner Full Name or Entity Name, Petitioner Type, and Petitioner Contact Email.
3.  Select the grounds for winding up from the provided options.
4.  Enter the company details, including the Name of the Company to be Wound Up, Company Registration Number, and Registered Office Address.
5.  Provide a summary of evidence supporting the grounds for winding up.
6.  Enter the date of the petition and the verification statement.
7.  Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner Full Name or Entity Name**: Enter the full name of the person or entity bringing the petition, as it appears on official documents.
* **Petitioner Type**: Select the type of relationship between the petitioner and the company, such as Creditor, Contributory (Shareholder), Director, Secretary of State, or Other Authorized Person.
* **Petitioner Contact Email**: Enter the official email address used for correspondence with the petitioner.
* **Name of Company to be Wound Up**: Enter the full legal name of the company as registered with the national authorities.
* **Company Registration Number**: Enter the unique identifier assigned by the national registry.
* **Registered Office Address**: Enter the physical address of the company's registered office.
* **Grounds for Winding Up**: Select the primary reason for winding up the company from the provided options, such as Company is unable to pay its debts or Just and Equitable Ground.
* **Summary of Evidence**: Briefly describe the evidence supporting the grounds for winding up.
* **Legal Representative Name**: Enter the name of the solicitor or firm representing the petitioner.
* **Date of Petition**: Enter the official date of the petition.
* **I verify that the facts stated in this petition are true**: Select the verification statement to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Total Debt Amount**: If applicable, enter the total amount owed by the company.
* **Total Debt Amount**: Note: This field is optional and only required if the grounds for winding up includes debt.
* **Other**: This field is optional and should only be used if the verification statement is not "I so verify".
