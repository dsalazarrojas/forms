This form is a Generation Recognition Petition, designed to collect information and support from individuals for a specific cause or issue. The purpose of this form is to gather first names, last names, email addresses, supporter messages, and responses from those who wish to show support. This form is meant to be a simple and efficient way to collect data from a diverse group of people.

Before filling out this form, please make sure you're not duplicating efforts and that you're not filling out a duplicate petition. If you're unsure, you can try searching for similar petitions using your email address or name.

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **User First Name** in the first field. This will be used to identify you as a supporter.
2. Enter your **User Last Name** in the second field. This will be used to further identify you as a supporter.
3. Enter your **Email** (if you choose to) in the third field. This will allow us to contact you regarding the petition, but it is not required.
4. In the **Supporter Message** field, briefly share your thoughts, feelings, or reasons for supporting this petition. This will help us understand your perspective and show your support.
5. In the **Supporter Response** field, provide any additional information or context that you think would be helpful in understanding your support. This will help us tailor our approach to address the issue at hand.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name**: The first name of the person filling out the form. This is a required field.
* **User Last Name**: The last name of the person filling out the form. This is a required field.
* **Email**: An optional field for entering your email address, which we can use to contact you regarding the petition.
* **Supporter Message**: A field for briefly sharing your thoughts, feelings, or reasons for supporting this petition.
* **Supporter Response**: A field for providing any additional information or context that would help us understand your support.

## Tips

* Be honest and sincere in your responses, as we value your authenticity and commitment to the cause.
* Keep your responses brief and clear, as this will help us get a better understanding of your support.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

Please use this form to make your voice heard and show your support for this important issue. Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form and for being a part of this generation's recognition movement!
