# Human Rights Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Human Rights Petition Form is a tool to gather information and support for a specific human rights issue or cause. The form helps the organization to better understand the scope and severity of the issue, which can inform advocacy efforts and support services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false)
	* Please enter a brief title summarizing the purpose of your petition.
2. **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false)
	* Select all applicable categories that best describe the human rights issue or cause you are petitioning for. You can choose multiple categories.
	* For example, if you are petitioning for "Refugee Rights," you would select both "Refugee Rights" and "Immigration Rights."
3. **Description** (`description`, text, required: false)
	* Provide a detailed description of the issue or cause you are petitioning for. Be as clear and concise as possible.
4. **Your Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
	* Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates and follow-up information.
5. **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
	* If you have a phone number, please enter it so that we can reach you for further discussion or clarification.
6. **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, required: false)
	* Enter a unique identifier for your petition, such as a name or code. This will help us track your submission.
7. **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false)
	* If you are assigned a specific tool or group to work with, please select "Yes" and enter the tool's name.
8. **Output File** (`output_file`, select_one, required: false)
	* If you would like to save a copy of the form as a file for your records, select "Yes."
9. **Category (2)** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false)
	* Please select all applicable categories for your issue or cause.

## Tips

* Please be honest and clear in your submission to ensure we can better understand and support your cause.
* Make sure to proofread your submission before submitting it for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure or have questions, please contact your team lead or support staff for guidance.
