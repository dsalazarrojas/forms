# Academic Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Petition Form is designed to collect information from students who are seeking academic accommodations or exceptions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all necessary information and attachments before starting.
2. Fill out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
3. Use the provided options to select the correct answers.
4. Attach supporting documentation if requested.
5. Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Full Name** (`student_name`, *required*): Enter your legal name as it appears on your student ID or university records.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, *required*): Enter your institutional student ID number.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, *required*): Enter your university email address.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Academic Information** (`academic_info_section`, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any relevant academic information.
* **Class Year** (`class_year`, *required*): Select your current class year from the provided options.
* **Major or Program** (`major_program`, *required*): Enter your major or academic program.
* **Petition Information** (`petition_info_section`, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any relevant petition information.
* **Type of Petition** (`petition_type`, *required*): Select the type of petition you are submitting from the provided options.
* **Relevant Course** (`relevant_course`, optional): Enter the relevant course number and name.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_involved`, optional): Enter the instructor's name.
* **Petition Details** (`petition_details_section`, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional details about your petition.
* **Detailed Explanation** (`detailed_explanation`, *required*): Provide a clear and concise explanation of your request.
* **Justification** (`justification`, *required*): Explain why your petition should be approved.
* **Have You Discussed This With Your Advisor?** (`precedent_inquiry`, *required*): Select the option that best describes your interaction with your advisor.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, optional): Select the supporting documentation you are attaching, if applicable.
* **Special Circumstances** (`special_circumstances`, optional): Select the option that best describes your special circumstances.
* **Describe Special Circumstances** (`circumstances_detail`, optional): If you selected yes for special circumstances, provide a detailed explanation.
* **Academic Standing** (`academic_standing`, *required*): Select your current academic standing from the provided options.
* **Current GPA** (`current_gpa`, optional): Enter your current GPA.
* **Previous Petitions Filed** (`previous_petitions`, optional): Select the option that best describes your previous petitions.
* **Urgency and Deadline** (`urgency_section`, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any relevant urgency and deadline information.
* **Date Petition Decision is Needed By** (`needed_by_date`, optional): Enter the date by which you need the petition decision.
* **Date Petition Decision is Needed By**: Enter the date by which you need the petition decision.
* **Date**: Enter the date by which you need the petition decision.

Note: This guide is designed to be a general help guide for the form. However, it is recommended that you review the form options and fields carefully before completing it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
