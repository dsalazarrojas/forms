# Parking Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Petition Form is designed to collect information and feedback from individuals who have concerns or suggestions related to parking in the area.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete the form, follow these steps:
    *   Start by filling out the "Parking Petition Form Input" field, providing a clear and concise statement of your concern or suggestion.
    *   Then, enter the parking location related to your concern or suggestion in the "Parking Location" field.
    *   Finally, add any additional comments or details in the "Comments" field.
2.  Please note that some fields are optional and can be left blank if you don't have any information to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parking Petition Form Input**: (`petition_form_input`, Text, optional) Enter a clear and concise statement of your concern or suggestion related to parking.
*   **Parking Location**: (`parking_location`, Text, optional) Enter the specific location related to your concern or suggestion, such as a street, block, or specific parking lot.
*   **Comments**: (`comments`, Text, optional) Add any additional comments or details you'd like to provide about your concern or suggestion.

## Tips
*   Please be clear and concise in your submissions to help us better understand and address your concerns.
*   If you have any supporting materials or documentation, feel free to attach them to your form submission.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
