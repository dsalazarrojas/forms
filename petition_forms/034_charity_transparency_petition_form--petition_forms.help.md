# Charity Transparency Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who want to express their concerns and demands regarding the transparency of charitable organizations. This form will help charitable organizations understand their weaknesses and improve their practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the "Your Information" section.
2. Provide your name, email address, phone number (optional), and address in the required fields.
3. In the "Petition Details" section, describe the specific transparency issue you are concerned about and what action you are requesting the charity organization to take.
4. Choose how strongly you support this petition and whether you are willing to help promote it.
5. Finally, confirm that you agree with the petition goals and statement, and confirm that your name can be published with the petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (required): Your complete name.
- **Email Address** (required): Your email address for petition communications.
- **Phone Number** (optional): Your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Address** (optional): Your physical address.
- **City** (optional): The city where you reside.
- **State or Province** (optional): The state or province where you reside.
- **Country** (optional): Your country of residence.
- **Petition Title** (required): A brief description of the transparency issue you are concerned about.
- **Charity Organization Name** (required): The name of the charitable organization you are concerned about.
- **Specific Transparency Issue** (required): Choose the specific transparency issue you are concerned about.
  - **Financial Disclosure**: Lack of financial information.
  - **Executive Compensation**: Unfair executive compensation.
  - **Program Impact Data**: Lack of information on program impact.
  - **Board Composition**: Unbalanced board composition.
  - **Conflict of Interest**: Conflicts of interest among board members.
  - **Fundraising Practices**: Unfair fundraising practices.
  - **Other**: Other concerns not listed above.
- **Detailed Description of Concern** (required): Explain the transparency issue in detail.
- **What Action Are You Requesting** (required): Describe the specific action you are requesting the charity to take.
- **Specific Reforms Requested** (optional): List specific changes you would like the charity to implement.
- **Level of Support** (required): How strongly do you support this petition.
  - **Strongly Support**: Fully support the petition.
  - **Support**: Support the petition.
  - **Somewhat Support**: Somewhat support the petition.
  - **Neutral**: Do not support the petition.
  - **Somewhat Oppose**: Oppose the petition.
- **Willing to Help Promote This Petition** (optional): Are you willing to help promote the petition.
  - **Yes**: Yes, you are willing to help.
  - **Maybe**: Maybe, you are not sure.
  - **No**: No, you are not willing to help.
- **My Name Can Be Published with This Petition** (required): Choose whether your name should be published with the petition.
  - **Yes, Publish My Name**: Publish your name with the petition.
  - **No, Keep Anonymous**: Keep your name anonymous.
- **I Agree with the Petition Goals and Statement** (required): Confirm that you agree with the petition goals and statement.
  - **Yes, I Agree**: Agree with the petition goals and statement.
  - **No, I Do Not Agree**: Do not agree with the petition goals and statement.
- **Date of Signature** (required): The current date.
