# Refugee Rights Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from refugees and asylum seekers to help them exercise their rights and receive support. Please fill out this form if you are a refugee or asylum seeker looking to exercise your rights or if you are an organization or individual seeking to support them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your full name in the `Full Name` field.
*   Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
*   Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
*   Enter a message in the `Message` field to explain your reason for filling out the form.
*   Select your country of origin in the `Country` field.
*   If you are not a refugee or asylum seeker, please leave the `State or Province` field blank.
*   Select your current refugee status in the `What is your current refugee status?` field.
*   You can choose to be aware or not aware of your refugee rights in the `I am aware of my rights as a refugee` field.
*   Select the language you prefer to communicate in the `Language` field.
*   Enter your signature in the `Signature` field.
*   Enter the current date and time in the `Date` and `Time` fields.
*   Enter the location where you currently reside in the `Location` field.
*   Enter any additional notes you have in the `Note` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Full Name** (Required, Text): Enter your full name.
*   **Email** (Required, Email): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (Required, Text): Enter your phone number.
*   **Message** (Required, Note): Enter a brief message explaining why you are filling out this form.
*   **Country** (Required, Select One): Select the country where you are from.
*   **State or Province** (Optional, Select Multiple): This field is not required but can be filled out if you want to provide additional information.
*   **I am aware of my rights as a refugee** (Optional, Select One): Select "I am aware" if you know your rights as a refugee, "I do not know" if you do not, or "Refused to answer" if you do not want to answer.
*   **What is your current refugee status?** (Optional, Select One): Select your current status as a refugee or asylum seeker.
*   **Language** (Optional, Select One): Select the language you prefer to communicate in.
*   **Signature** (Required, Text): Enter your signature.
*   **Date** (Required, Date): Enter the current date.
*   **Time** (Required, Time): Enter the current time.
*   **Location** (Required, Text): Enter the location where you currently reside.
*   **Note** (Optional, Note): Enter any additional notes you have.
