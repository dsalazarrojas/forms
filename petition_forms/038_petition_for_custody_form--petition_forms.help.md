# Petition For Custody Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Petition for Custody Form is used to gather information from a petitioner who wishes to file a petition for custody. This form helps the petitioner provide essential details about their situation, including their relationship status, residency, and reason for filing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Petition for Custody Form, follow these steps:

1. **Petition for Custody** (Text): Enter a brief description of the circumstances that led you to file for custody.
2. **Petitioner Information** (Text): Provide your name, address, phone number, and email address.
3. **Petitioning Party** (Select One): Choose who is petitioning for custody (you as the parent, the other parent, or both parents).
4. **Custody Status** (Select Multiple): Select the custody status you are seeking (Sole Custody, Joint Custody, Legal Custody, or Physical Custody).
5. **Residency** (Select Multiple): Choose your current and intended residence locations.
6. **Child's Age** (Text): Enter the age of the child/children involved.
7. **Date** (Date): Select a date for the events leading up to the petition.
8. **Time** (Time): Select the time for the events leading up to the petition.
9. **Address 1** (Text): Enter your current address.
10. **Address 2** (Text): Enter the address of the other parent (if applicable).
11. **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
12. **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
13. **Notes** (Note): Enter any additional information or details about the circumstances.
14. **Reason** (Text): Provide a brief explanation of the reason for filing the petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Petition for Custody**: A brief description of the circumstances that led you to file for custody. This is a required field.
* **Petitioner Information**: This section is used to gather your contact information. Please provide your name, address, phone number, and email address.
* **Petitioning Party**: Choose who is petitioning for custody. You can select yourself, the other parent, or both parents.
* **Custody Status**: This field allows you to select the custody status you are seeking. You can choose Sole Custody, Joint Custody, Legal Custody, or Physical Custody.
* **Residency**: Choose your current and intended residence locations.
* **Child's Age**: Enter the age of the child/children involved.
* **Date**: Select a date for the events leading up to the petition.
* **Time**: Select the time for the events leading up to the petition.
* **Address 1**: Enter your current address.
* **Address 2**: Enter the address of the other parent (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or details about the circumstances.
* **Reason**: Provide a brief explanation of the reason for filing the petition.
