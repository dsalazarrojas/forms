# Divorce Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Divorce Petition Form is a legal document used to initiate a divorce case in a court of law. It requires information from the petitioner (spouse) to establish the grounds for divorce, the dates of marriage and divorce, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which include:
	* Spouse Name: Enter the name of the spouse you are petitioning for divorce.
	* Spouse Address: Enter the address of the spouse you are petitioning for divorce.
	* Grounds for Divorce: Specify the reason for the divorce.
	* Court Order: Enter any relevant court orders related to the divorce case.
	* Signature Date: Enter the date of your signature.
	* Signature Judge: Enter the name of the judge who signed the document.
	* Signature Clerk: Enter the name of the clerk who signed the document.
	* Divorce Attorney: Enter the name and contact information of the divorce attorney.
	* Case Number: Enter the case number assigned by the court.
	* Case Status: Enter the current status of the case.
	* Court File Number: Enter the court file number assigned to the case.
	* Court Location: Select the court location where the case is being handled.
2. Fill out the non-required fields as needed:
	* Date of Marriage: Enter the date of marriage (optional).
	* Divorce Date: Enter the date of divorce (optional).

* **Spouse Name**: Enter the name of the spouse you are petitioning for divorce.
* **Spouse Address**: Enter the address of the spouse you are petitioning for divorce.
* **Grounds for Divorce**: Specify the reason for the divorce, such as "irreconcilable differences" or "irreconcilable differences due to infidelity."
* **Date of Marriage**: If applicable, enter the date of marriage.
* **Divorce Date**: If applicable, enter the date of divorce.
* **Court Order**: Enter any relevant court orders related to the divorce case.
* **Divorce Date**: Enter the date of the divorce, if applicable.
