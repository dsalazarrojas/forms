# Petition Submission Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Petition Submission Tracking Form is used to track and update the status of petitions submitted for review. Petitioners can use this form to submit new petitions, track the progress of their submissions, and receive information on the status of their requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Petition Name**: Enter the name of your petition.
	* **Petitioner Name**: Enter your name as the person submitting the petition.
	* **Status**: Select the current status of your petition (New, Pending, Accepted, or Rejected).
	* **Follow-up Status**: Select the follow-up status of your petition (New, Pending, Accepted, or Rejected).
2. Optional fields:
	* **Form Title**: Enter a title for your petition, if desired.
	* **Date Received**: Enter the date your petition was received.
	* **Date Reviewed**: Enter the date your petition was reviewed.
	* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information related to your petition.
	* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the user assigned to review your petition.
	* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the petition.
	* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the petition.
	* **Created At**: Enter the date and time the petition was created.
	* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the petition was last updated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a title for your petition to give it a clear and concise identity.
* **Petition Name** (`petition_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your petition, a clear and brief description of the request being made.
* **Petitioner Name** (`petitioner_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person submitting the petition.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, **required**): Select the current status of your petition from the options: New, Pending, Accepted, or Rejected.
* **Date Received** (`date_received`, date, optional): Enter the date your petition was received.
* **Date Reviewed** (`date_reviewed`, date, optional): Enter the date your petition was reviewed.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information related to your petition.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow_up_status`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the follow-up status of your petition from the options: New, Pending, Accepted, or Rejected.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, optional): Enter the name of the user assigned to review your petition.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the petition.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who last updated the petition.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, time, optional): Enter the date and time the petition was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, optional): Enter the date and time the petition was last updated.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your petition can be properly reviewed and tracked.
* You can add any additional comments or information related to your petition in the **Comments** field.
* If you are unsure about the status or follow-up status of your petition, please contact the assigned user or the petition team for clarification.
