# Petition To Save Teacher Shortages Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Petition To Save Teacher Shortages Form is designed to collect insights and ideas from various stakeholders on how to address the issue of teacher shortages in the education sector. Your responses will help us understand the challenges and propose solutions to ensure that all students receive quality education from qualified and effective teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much information as you can.
2. If you are representing an organization or institution, ensure that you are not duplicating efforts or information already underway.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields or their requirements, please ask for clarification before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petition To Save Teacher Shortages Form** (`title`, text): This field is where you enter the title of the form. It's the first thing people see, so make it clear and concise. Enter the title of your petition, and make sure it accurately represents the purpose of your form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): This field categorizes the type of form you are filling out. Please select "Petition Forms" to indicate that this is indeed a petition form.
* **description** (`description`, text): This field is where you enter a brief description of your petition. Be as detailed and specific as possible, explaining the challenges you face with teacher shortages and the solutions you propose.
* **A Petition to Save Teacher Shortages Form is a form template designed** (`teacher_shortages_form_description`, text): This field is a description of the form itself, not a part of your petition. Please ignore it or leave it blank.
* **Form ID** (`form_ids`, number): This field is not required, but if you want to track your form's unique ID, you can enter a number here. It's not necessary for your petition, so feel free to leave it blank.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): This field is not required, but if you are using a specific tool or platform to fill out this form, please select it from the options provided. This helps us understand how you are accessing our form.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text): This field is not required, but if you want to save a copy of your completed form as a file, you can enter the file path here. It's not necessary for your petition, so feel free to leave it blank.
* **Assigned Tool 2**,  **Assigned Tool 3**, and **Assigned Tool 4** (`assigned_tool_2`, `assigned_tool_3`, `assigned_tool_4`, text): These fields are not required, and you can leave them blank. They are intended for internal use only, and you won't need to worry about them for your petition.

## Tips
* Be as specific and detailed as possible in your description and answers.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or their requirements, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* Please do not duplicate efforts or information already underway in your institution or organization.
* Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your contributions to addressing the issue of teacher shortages.
