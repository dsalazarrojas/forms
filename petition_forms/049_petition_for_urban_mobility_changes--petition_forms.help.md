# Petition For Urban Mobility Changes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to submit petitions for urban mobility changes, which can include proposals for new or modified transportation systems, public services, or infrastructure projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  In the **Petition For Urban Mobility Changes** field, enter a brief title for your petition.
2.  In the **Description** field, provide a detailed explanation of your proposal, including the reasons why you think it's necessary and beneficial.
3.  Choose a category for your petition from the options provided (e.g., Petition Forms or Other).
4.  If you've selected the "Other" category, type a brief description of your category in the **Category Other** field.
5.  Enter your contact email and phone number in the **Email** and **Phone** fields, respectively.
6.  Check the box if you agree with the petition's content.
7.  Add any additional comments or explanations in the **Comments** field.
8.  If you have supporting documents or files, upload them as attachments in the **Attachments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Petition For Urban Mobility Changes** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for your petition that summarizes the main idea or proposal.
*   **Description** (`form_description`, note, optional): Provide a detailed explanation of your petition, including the reasons why you think it's necessary and beneficial.
*   **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category for your petition from the options provided. The categories will help us understand the scope and focus of your proposal.
*   **Category Other** (`form_category_other`, text, optional): If you've selected the "Other" category, type a brief description of your category here.
*   **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address, so we can get in touch with you for further discussion or clarification.
*   **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, so we can reach out to you for further discussion or clarification.
*   **Agreed** (`form_agreed`, select_one, optional): Check the box if you agree with the petition's content.
*   **Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations that support your proposal.
*   **Attachments** (`form_attachments`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents or files that help demonstrate your proposal.
*   **Attachments** (`form_attachments_note`, note, optional): Use this field to provide additional context or explanation about your attachments.
*   **Attachment 1** (`form_attachments_file_0`, text, optional): This field is where you can upload the first supporting document or file for your proposal.
