# environmental_regulations_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals or organizations seeking to submit a petition related to environmental regulations. The collected data will be used to review and potentially support or oppose environmental regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and other contact information in the respective fields.
2. Fill out the zip code field with your postal code.
3. Select your state from the provided options.
4. Enter your county, address, and any additional comments or information about your petition.
5. Optionally, provide details about your organization and job title (if applicable).
6. Finally, sign and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents. This will be used to identify you and communicate with you about your petition.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information about your petition.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **zip_code**: Enter your postal zip code for location identification.
* **city**: Enter the name of your city.
* **state**: Select your state from the provided options. This will help us understand which state's regulations you are petitioning about.
* **county**: Enter the name of your county.
* **address**: Enter your full address for location identification.
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your petition. This can be a description of your concerns or requests.
* **organization**: Enter the name of your organization, if applicable.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title or position within your organization.
* **department**: Enter the name of your department within your organization.
* **department2**: This field is for additional department information, if necessary.
* **date**: Enter the date you are submitting your petition.
* **signature**: Enter your signature, which signifies your commitment to submitting this petition.

Note: Some fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help us better understand and review your petition.
