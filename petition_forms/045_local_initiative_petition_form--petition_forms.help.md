# local_initiative_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to create a local initiative petition to be submitted to an organization for a specific policy change or initiative. Fill out this form to provide necessary information about the petitioners, the petition details, and the signature information required for the submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the required fields**: The form has several required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). Please ensure that you complete these fields to ensure that the form can be properly processed.
2. **Enter accurate information**: Enter accurate and detailed information in each field to ensure that the form is complete and can be properly submitted.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click on the "submit_button" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **petitioner_info**: * (text, required) - This field is for providing general information about the petitioners, such as their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **petitioner_name**: * (text, required) - Enter the name of the individual or group signing the petition.
* **petitioner_email**: * (email, required) - Enter the email address of the individual or group signing the petition.
* **petitioner_phone**: * (text, required) - Enter the phone number of the individual or group signing the petition.
* **petition_details**: * (text, required) - Provide detailed information about the petition, including the proposed policy change or initiative.
* **target_department**: * (text, required) - Specify the department or agency to which the petition is being submitted.
* **petition_goal**: * (text, required) - Enter the specific goal or objective of the petition.
* **petition_objectives**: * (text, required) - Provide a detailed description of the petition objectives.
* **petition_slogan**: (text, optional) - Enter any optional slogan or catchphrase for the petition.
* **petition_signature**: * (text, required) - Sign your name as the petitioner, confirming that you have read and understand the petition content.
* **petition_signature_image**: * (text, required) - Upload a digital image of your signature, if applicable.
* **petition_signature_note**: (note, optional) - Enter any additional notes or comments about your signature.
* **petition_signature_date**: * (date, required) - Enter the date on which you signed the petition.
* **petition_signature_time**: * (time, required) - Enter the time at which you signed the petition.
* **petition_signature_count**: * (number, required) - Enter the number of signatures collected so far.
* **submit_button**: * (text, required) - Click this button to submit the completed form.
* **cancel_button**: (text, optional) - Click this button to cancel the form submission.
* **petitioner_notes**: (note, optional) - Enter any additional notes or comments about the petition.
* **petition_details_notes**: (note, optional) - Enter any additional notes or comments about the petition details.
* **petition_signature_date_time**: * (time, required) - Enter the date and time at which you signed the petition.
* **petitioner_agreement**: * (text, required) - Check this box to confirm that you have read and understood the petition content.
* **petitioner_agreement_date**: * (date, required) - Enter the date on which you agreed to the terms of the petition.
* **petitioner_agreement_time**: * (time, required) - Enter the time at which you agreed to the terms of the petition.
* **petitioner_agreement_note**: (note, optional) - Enter any additional notes or comments about your agreement.
