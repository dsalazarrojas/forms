# Gun Control Measures Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gun Control Measures Petition Form is a collection of demographic and interest-based information from individuals who are interested in supporting gun control measures. The information collected will be used for internal purposes only and will not be shared publicly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. First, fill out your personal details:
	* Enter your first name
	* Enter your last name
	* Enter your email address
	* Enter your street address
	* Enter your city
	* Enter your state
	* Enter your zip code
2. If you represent an organization, enter the name of the organization (optional)
3. Enter your contact phone number (optional)
4. If you'd like to be in touch with us, enter your preferred contact date and time (optional)
5. Select your level of interest in this campaign (required)
6. Choose the issues you are most interested in (required)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you use to receive updates from us.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address where you can be reached.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Organization (Optional)**: If you represent an organization, enter its name here.
* **Phone (Optional)**: Enter your contact phone number if you want us to reach out to you.
* **Date (Optional)**: Enter a date and time when you would like to be contacted.
* **Time (Optional)**: Enter a time when you would like to be contacted.
* **Which of the following best describes your level of interest in this campaign? (Required)**: Select one of the options provided:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Which of the following issues are you most interested in? (Required)**: Select multiple options provided:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have here. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will not share your information publicly.
