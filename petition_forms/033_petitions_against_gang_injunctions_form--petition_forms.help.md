# Petitions Against Gang Injunctions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage information related to petitions against gang injunctions, allowing users to create or update existing petitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* **Form Title**: Enter the title of the petition.
	* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the petition.
	* **Category**: Select the category that best describes the petition (e.g., Petition Forms).
2. Optional information (if applicable):
	* **Category (select_one)**: Select a specific category for the petition.
	* **Form IDs**: Enter any relevant form IDs.
	* **Assigned to**: Enter the name of the person or organization assigned to the petition.
	* **Created At**: Enter the date the petition was created.
	* **Updated At**: Enter the date the petition was last updated.
	* **Description (text)**: Enter a detailed description of the petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, not required): Enter a brief title for the petition.
* **Description** (`form_description`, note, not required): Provide a short description of the petition.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required): Select a category that best describes the petition (e.g., Petition Forms).
* **Category (select_multiple)** (`form_category`, select_multiple, not required): Select multiple categories for the petition.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, not required): Enter any relevant form IDs.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person or organization assigned to the petition.
* **Created At** (`form_created_at`, date, not required): Enter the date the petition was created.
* **Updated At** (`form_updated_at`, date, not required): Enter the date the petition was last updated.
* **Description (text)** (`description`, text, not required): Enter a detailed description of the petition.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to ensure that the petition is properly processed.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities or the form administrator.
* This form is intended for legitimate purposes only, and any misuse may result in consequences.
