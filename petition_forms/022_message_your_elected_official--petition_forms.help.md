# Message Your Elected Official - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Message Your Elected Official" form is a tool for constituents to communicate with their elected officials, enabling them to express their thoughts, suggestions, or concerns. This form helps elected officials stay informed about the needs and interests of their constituents, facilitating more effective representation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the elected official you wish to contact from the "Elected Official" dropdown menu.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
3. Write your message in the "Message Body" field, detailing your thoughts, suggestions, or concerns.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your message to the elected official.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Elected Official** (required): Select the elected official you wish to contact, such as Senator, Congressman, or Mayor.
* **Name** (optional): Enter your name, which will help the elected official's office understand who is contacting them.
* **Organization** (optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name to provide context.
* **Email** (optional): Provide your email address to facilitate communication and allow the elected official's office to follow up with you.
* **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Message Body** (optional): Type in the details of your message, including any thoughts, suggestions, or concerns you'd like to share.
* **Category** (optional): If you have a specific category in mind for your message, such as budget or transportation, enter it here.

## Tips

* Be clear and concise when writing your message, as elected officials and their staff will be reading your input.
* Proofread your message for spelling and grammar errors before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about which elected official to contact or have questions about the form, reach out to their office directly for guidance.
* Remember to check the "Message Your Elected Official" form for updates and revisions, as forms are subject to change.
