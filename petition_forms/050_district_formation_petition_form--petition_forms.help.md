# District Formation Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The District Formation Petition Form is a tool for submitting a formal request to establish or modify a local government district. This form is intended to collect relevant information to support the petition process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Base Information section with your name, organization, and contact details.
2. Provide the address related to the district formation petition.
3. Enter your contact information to facilitate communication.
4. Describe the district boundaries as clearly as possible.
5. Attach any supporting documents relevant to the petition.
6. Clearly state your petition justification.
7. If necessary, list any additional signatures or endorsements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Base Information** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and organization to identify yourself and the entity submitting the petition.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Provide the address related to the district formation petition. This could be a physical location or a digital address.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, such as email and phone number, to facilitate communication.
* **District Boundaries** (`district_boundaries`, `text`, optional): Clearly describe the boundaries of the proposed district.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `text`, optional): Attach any relevant documents or files that support the petition.
* **Petition Justification** (`petition_justification`, `text`, optional): Clearly state the reason or justification for the district formation petition.
* **Additional Signatures** (`additional_signatures`, `text`, optional): List any additional signatures or endorsements for the petition.

## Tips
* Be clear and concise when filling out the form.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Attach relevant supporting documents to strengthen your petition.
* Clearly justify your petition reason to help the reviewing authority understand your request.
* Proofread your form before submission to avoid mistakes.
