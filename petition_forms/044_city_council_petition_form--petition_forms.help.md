# City Council Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide citizens in submitting petitions to the city council, providing all the necessary information to help the council understand and address community concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief title and detailed description of your petition.
2. Identify the target city council members or departments that should address this issue.
3. Choose a category for your petition from the list provided.
4. Estimate the number of people affected by the issue.
5. Attach any supporting documents or evidence to help the council understand your concerns.
6. Describe any previous attempts to address the issue.
7. Specify the exact action you request the city council to take.
8. Explain how your proposed action would solve the problem.
9. Set a timeline for the city council to address this issue.
10. Indicate whether you would like the city council to follow up with you about your petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Petition Title** (Required): A brief and clear title of your petition that summarizes the issue you are addressing.
* **Detailed Description of Petition** (Required): A detailed explanation of the issue, including what you are asking the city council to do.
* **Target City Council Members or Departments** (Optional): Identify the council members or departments that should address this issue.
* **Petition Category** (Required): Select the category that best describes your petition from the list provided.
* **Community or Neighborhoods Affected** (Required): Estimate the number of people affected by this issue.
* **Estimated Beneficiaries** (Optional): Choose the estimated number of people affected from the list provided.
* **Supporting Documents** (Optional): Attach any studies, data, or other supporting materials that can help the city council understand your concerns.
* **Previous Attempts to Address This Issue** (Optional): Describe any previous attempts to address this issue.
* **Specific Action Requested from Council** (Required): Clearly state what action you are asking the city council to take.
* **Proposed Action Details** (Optional): Explain how your proposed action would solve the problem.
* **Timeline for Implementation** (Optional): Set a timeline for the city council to address this issue.
* **Follow-up Contact** (Required): Indicate whether you would like the city council to follow up with you about your petition.
* **Permission to Follow Up** (Required): Select whether you want the city council to follow up with you.
* **Organizer Name** (Required): Provide your name and contact information as the person leading the petition.
* **Organizer Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Organizer Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Organizer Address** (Required): Confirm your community residency.
* **Number of Current Supporters** (Optional): Enter the number of current supporters.
* **Support Goal (Target Signatures)** (Optional): Enter the target number of signatures you are seeking.
* **Partner Organizations or Allies** (Optional): List any partner organizations or allies supporting this petition.
* **Funding Needed for Implementation** (Optional): Estimate any funding needed for implementation.
* **Permission to Follow Up About This Petition** (Required): Select whether you want the city council to follow up with you about your petition.
