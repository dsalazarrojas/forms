# Campaign Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Campaign Petition Form is a tool for collecting support and contact information from individuals who agree with the campaign's cause. The information collected on this form will be used to verify the authenticity of signatures and for communication with supporters.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Signer Name" field with your full name.
2. Provide your address in the "Address" field (optional).
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field (optional).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
5. Write a short statement explaining why you support the campaign in the "Statement of Support" field.
6. Select the date you signed the petition in the "Date of Signature" field.
7. Choose whether to consent to publishing your name publicly in the "Consent to Publish Name" field (optional).
8. If applicable, enter your affiliation with the campaign in the "Affiliation" field (optional).
9. Enter a verification code for verification purposes (if provided by the campaign).
10. Add any additional comments or messages for the petition organizers in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Signer Name (Signer Name, text, required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Address (Address, text, optional)**: Enter your city or postal area for verification purposes.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, optional)**: Enter your contact email address for communication with the campaign.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Statement of Support (Statement of Support, text, required)**: Briefly explain why you support the campaign.
* **Date of Signature (Date of Signature, date, required)**: Select the date you signed the petition.
* **Consent to Publish Name (Consent to Publish Name, select_one, optional)**: Choose whether to consent to publishing your name publicly.
* **Affiliation (Affiliation, text, optional)**: If you have an affiliation with the campaign, such as an organization, enter it here.
* **Verification Code (Verification Code, text, optional)**: Enter a verification code if provided by the campaign for verification purposes.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or messages for the petition organizers.
