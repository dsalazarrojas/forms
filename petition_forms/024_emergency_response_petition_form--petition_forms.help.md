# emergency_response_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The emergency response petition form is a tool for providing critical information to aid in emergency response efforts. Users are requested to provide accurate and relevant details to support response teams in making informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form to ensure you are providing information for an emergency response.
2.  Fill in the User Information page with your details.
3.  Answer the Signature question with either 'Yes' or 'No'.
4.  Provide your Email and Phone numbers.
5.  Enter your Organization Name (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information

*   **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, required: false): This page is for providing basic information about yourself. Enter your name and any other relevant personal details you think are necessary for the emergency response team to know.

### Signature

*   **Signature** (`signature`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether you can confirm that the information you are providing is accurate. Please answer with either 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Email

*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so the emergency response team can reach out to you with updates or further questions.

### Phone

*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number if you would like to be contacted personally or if the response team requires it for their operations.

### Organization Name

*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required: false): If you are representing an organization, please enter the name here. This can be your workplace or any other organization you are affiliated with.

## Tips

*   Only use this form for emergency response efforts.
*   Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
*   Do not submit duplicate or irrelevant responses.
*   If you have any further questions, contact the emergency response team directly.
