# Candidate Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registered voters to express support for a specific candidate in an election. It requires accurate and truthful information to verify the authenticity of the petition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the full name of the candidate you are supporting.
2. Specify the position or office the candidate is running for.
3. Provide your full name as a registered voter.
4. Enter your registered voter address.
5. Enter your email address for campaign contact.
6. Choose how you want to be contacted by the campaign (email, SMS, or both).
7. Certify that you are a registered voter in the district.
8. Sign the petition with your electronic signature.
9. Select the date you are signing the petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Nominee Full Name** (`nominee_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the candidate you are supporting.
- **Position or Office Sought** (`position_sought`, `text`, required): Specify the position or office the candidate is running for.
- **Petitioner Full Name** (`petitioner_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as a registered voter.
- **Registered Voter Address** (`voter_address`, `text`, required): Enter your registered voter address.
- **Email Address** (`petitioner_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for campaign contact.
- **Consent to be Contacted** (`campaign_contact_consent`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to be contacted by the campaign (email, SMS, or both).
- **I Certify I am a Registered Voter** (`registered_voter_certify`, `select_one`, required): Verify that you are a registered voter in the district.
- **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature_text`, `text`, required): Type your full name to sign the petition.
- **Date of Signing** (`signing_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you are signing the petition.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are accurate and truthful.
- Enter your registered voter address correctly.
- Choose your preferred contact method for campaign communication.
- Verify your certification as a registered voter.
- Sign the petition with your electronic signature.
- Date the petition correctly.
