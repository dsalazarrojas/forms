# Social Media Age Verification Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the age of a social media user and gather information about their social media presence.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the User Input section with your social media username.
2. Select whether you have signed up for the following social media platforms: Facebook, Twitter, TikTok, Snapchat, Instagram, Twitch, YouTube, or Reddit.
3. Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text, or Other).
4. If you have not already, confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (user_input, text, required): Enter your social media username or handle.
* **Signatures** (signature_input, select_multiple, optional): Select all the social media platforms you have accounts on.
* **Social Media Platforms** (social_media_platforms, select_one, required): Select all the social media platforms you have accounts on.
* **Age Verification** (age_verification, date, optional): Enter your date of birth, if you choose to verify your age.
* **Contact Info** (contact_info, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, Text, or Other).
* **Message** (message_input, text, optional): Enter a message to provide additional information (optional).
* **Name** (name_input, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Organization** (organization, text, optional): Enter your organization or affiliation (if applicable).
* **Position** (position, text, optional): Enter your position or title (if applicable).
* **Signature Confirmation** (signature_confirmation, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
