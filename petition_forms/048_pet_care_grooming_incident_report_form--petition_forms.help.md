# Pet Care Grooming Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for pet owners to report incidents that occurred during or after a pet grooming visit. It's used to gather information to help improve the services of the salon or service provider.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field completely and accurately.
3. Select the correct option for each select_one field (e.g., "Pet Grooming Incident" for the "Incident Type" field).
4. Enter your pet's name, age, and breed in the corresponding fields.
5. Describe the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
6. Choose the correct outcome for the "Incident Outcome" field.
7. If applicable, select a follow-up date for the "Follow-up" field.
8. Add any additional notes about the incident in the "Notes" field.
9. Click on "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Incident Date** (`1`, `date`, required: false):
    Enter the date the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Time** (`2`, `time`, required: false):
    Enter the time the incident occurred.
*   **Incident Location** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the location where the incident occurred (e.g., salon, home, etc.).
*   **Incident Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Choose the type of incident that occurred (e.g., "Pet Grooming Incident" or "Other").
*   **Incident Description** (`5`, `text`, required: false):
    Provide a detailed description of what happened during the incident.
*   **Pet Name** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the name of the pet involved in the incident.
*   **Pet Age** (`7`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the age of the pet involved in the incident.
*   **Pet Breed** (`8`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the breed of the pet involved in the incident.
*   **Salon Name** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the name of the salon where the incident occurred.
*   **Salon Staff** (`10`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the name of the salon staff member involved in the incident.
*   **Incident Outcome** (`11`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Choose the outcome of the incident (e.g., "Resolved", "Pending", or "Closed").
*   **Follow-up** (`12`, `date`, required: false):
    Enter the date for any follow-up actions or appointments.
*   **Notes** (`13`, `text`, required: false):
    Add any additional notes about the incident.

## Tips

*   Please provide as much detail as possible to help the salon or service provider understand what happened.
*   If you have any supporting evidence (e.g., photos, videos), consider attaching them to the report.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
