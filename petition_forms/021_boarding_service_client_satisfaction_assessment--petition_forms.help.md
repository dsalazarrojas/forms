# Boarding Service Client Satisfaction Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback and ratings about your experience with our boarding service. Your input will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for future clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, enter the requested information as prompted.
3. For select fields, choose the rating that best describes your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Client Name** (*)
• Client Name: Enter the client's name who made the booking.

### 2. **Client Email** (*)
• Client Email: Enter the contact email address of the client.

### 3. **Client Phone** (*)
• Client Phone: Enter the client's phone number.

### 4. **Pet Name**
• Pet Name: Enter the name of your pet.

### 5. **Pet Type**
• Pet Type: Select the type of pet you boarded with us.

### 6. **Service Dates**
• Service Dates: Enter the dates of the boarding service.

### 7. **Service Type**
• Service Type: Select the type of boarding service you received.

### 8. **Overall Satisfaction**
• Overall Satisfaction: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service.

### 9. **Facility Cleanliness**
• Facility Cleanliness: Rate the cleanliness of our facilities.

### 10. **Staff Friendliness**
• Staff Friendliness: Rate the friendliness and courtesy of our staff.

### 11. **Staff Knowledge**
• Staff Knowledge: Rate the expertise and knowledge of our staff regarding pets.

### 12. **Communication Quality**
• Communication Quality: Rate the quality of updates and communication from our staff.

### 13. **Pet Comfort Level**
• Pet Comfort Level: Rate how comfortable your pet seemed during their stay.

### 14. **Pet Health Upon Return**
• Pet Health Upon Return: Rate how your pet's health was upon return.

### 15. **Value for Money**
• Value for Money: Rate the value you received for the service.

### 16. **Likelihood to Recommend**
• Likelihood to Recommend: Indicate how likely you are to recommend our services to others.

### 17. **Will You Return**
• Will You Return: Indicate if you are likely to return to our services.

### 18. **Best Aspect of Service**
• Best Aspect of Service: Enter the aspect of our service that you enjoyed most.

### 19. **Areas for Improvement**
• Areas for Improvement: Enter any areas that you feel we could improve.

### 20. **Additional Comments**
• Additional Comments: Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.

By following these steps and completing this form, you will be providing valuable feedback that will help us improve our services and provide a better experience for future clients.
