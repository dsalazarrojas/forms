# Petition To Stop Construction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Petition To Stop Construction Form" is designed to provide concerned citizens with a formal way to express their opposition to ongoing or proposed construction projects. This form helps to collect and document the concerns and objections of the community, which can be presented to relevant authorities for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Project Details" field with a brief description of the construction project you are concerned about.
2. In the "Concerns" field, provide a detailed description of your concerns related to the project.
3. If applicable, select the relevant "Select Option" checkboxes to indicate your stance on specific issues.
4. Enter your contact information, including your Email Address and Phone Number, for us to get in touch with you.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or information about the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Provide a brief description of the construction project you are concerned about. Please keep it concise and focused on the main issues you have with the project.
* **Concerns**: Describe your concerns in detail, explaining how the project affects you or your community.
* **Signatures** (Number): This is not currently used; however, please provide your signature to show your support for the petition.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you to discuss your concerns.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted directly.
* **Date**: This field is not currently used; however, please provide the date you became aware of the project for context.
* **Time**: Like "Date", this field is not currently used; please ignore it.
* **Address**: If the project is located in a specific area, please provide the address for reference.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the project.
* **Select Option** (Multiple): Choose all that apply from the provided options to indicate your stance on specific issues related to the project.
	+ Option 1: Concerned about noise and pollution
	+ Option 2: Concerned about traffic and congestion
	+ Option 3: Concerned about property value decrease
* **Regulatory Compliance**: Check 'Yes' if you believe the project is not following relevant regulations.
* **Public Safety**: Check 'Yes' if you think the project poses a public safety risk.
* **Environmental Conservation**: Check 'Yes' if you are concerned about the project's impact on the environment.
* **Historic Preservation**: Check 'Yes' if you think the project affects historic or cultural significance.
