# Racial Equality Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from individuals or organizations expressing support for racial equality. It is intended to help track and analyze the feedback and support received for racial equality causes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your zip code in the **Zip Code** field.
4. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
5. Enter your city in the **City** field.
6. Enter your state in the **State** field.
7. Enter your county in the **County** field.
8. Enter your organization (if applicable) in the **Organization** field.
9. Sign your name in the **Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text): Enter your name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Zip Code** (Text): Enter your zip code for location tracking.
* **Address** (Text): Enter your address for location tracking.
* **City** (Text): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (Text): Enter the state where you are located.
* **County** (Text): Enter the county where you are located.
* **Organization** (Text): Enter your organization's name (if you are submitting on behalf of an organization).
* **Signature** (Note): Sign your name to confirm your support for racial equality.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields to help track and analyze the feedback accurately.
- If you are representing an organization, please ensure that you have the necessary permission to submit on behalf of the organization.
