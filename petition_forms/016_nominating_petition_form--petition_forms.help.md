# nominating_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from nominees and nominators for the purpose of nominating someone for a position or recognition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all relevant information is captured.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions provided for that field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
4. Review the form for completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Nomination Information (id: 1)
* **Nomination Information** (`Nomination Information`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to provide a brief description of the nomination.

### Nominator Information (id: 2)
* **Nominator Information** (`Nominator Information`, `text`, required: false): In this field, please enter the details about the person nominating.

### Nominators (id: 3)
* **Nominators** (`Nominators`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the people who have agreed to be nominators for this nomination.

### Endorsers (id: 4)
* **Endorsers** (`Endorsers`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the people who have endorsed this nomination.

### Nominator Statement (id: 5)

### Nominator Phone (id: 6)
