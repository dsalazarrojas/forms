# Petition For Presidency Of Advocacy Group Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your interest in becoming the president of the advocacy group. Your response will be reviewed by the group's administration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact details in the "name", "contact_details", "contact_details_first_name", and "contact_details_last_name" fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field.
3. If you have a phone number, please provide it in the "phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (text): Your full name that will be associated with your petition.
* **contact_details** (text): A brief description of your contact details. This can include any relevant information about yourself that the group should know.
* **contact_details_first_name** (text): Your first name, as a part of your contact details.
* **contact_details_last_name** (text): Your last name, as a part of your contact details.
* **email** (email): A valid email address where you can be reached.
* **phone** (text): Your phone number, if applicable.
* **address** (text): Your address for official communication.
