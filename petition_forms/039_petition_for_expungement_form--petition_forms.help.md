# Petition For Expungement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to assist individuals who have committed a crime and are seeking expungement (dismissal) of their record. Expungement is a process that removes or reduces the penalties associated with a crime, often for individuals who have shown remorse, completed community service, or have met specific conditions set by the court. This form helps you provide necessary information to support your expungement petition, increasing the likelihood of a successful outcome.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out this form accurately and completely, including all required and optional fields.
2.  Enter your Petitioner name in the "Petitioner name" field. This is your name as it appears on your identification documents.
3.  Enter the date of your crime in the "Crime date" field. This is the date you committed the crime.
4.  Describe the location where the crime occurred in the "Location" field.
5.  Specify the type of crime you committed in the "Crime type" field. This helps the court understand your situation.
6.  List the court case number associated with your case in the "Case number" field.
7.  Select the current status of your expungement application from the options provided in the "Expungement status" field.
8.  Sign and date the form in the "Signature" field.
9.  Finally, enter the date you completed the form in the "Date completed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Petitioner name
**Petitioner name** (`petitioner_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents to ensure accurate processing of your expungement application.

### Crime date
**Crime date** (`crime_date`, Date): Enter the date you committed the crime to provide a clear timeline for your expungement application.

### Location
**Location** (`location`, Text, Required): Provide the exact location where the crime was committed to help the court understand the circumstances surrounding the offense.

### Crime type
**Crime type** (`crime_type`, Text, Required): Describe the type of crime you committed to facilitate the expungement process.

### Court
**Court** (`court`, Text, Required): Enter the court associated with your case to ensure correct processing of your expungement application.

### Case number
**Case number** (`case_number`, Text, Required): List the case number for the court case associated with your application.

### Expungement status
**Expungement status** (`expungement_status`, Select one, Optional): Select the current status of your expungement application from the provided options. This field helps the court understand the progress of your expungement process.

### Signature
**Signature** (`signature`, Text, Required): Sign and date the form to indicate your acknowledgment of the information provided and your commitment to the terms of the expungement application.

### Date completed
**Date completed** (`date_completed`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you completed this form to record the completion of your expungement application.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
*   Double-check the information entered to avoid errors.
*   If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
*   Follow the instructions provided by the court for the expungement process.
