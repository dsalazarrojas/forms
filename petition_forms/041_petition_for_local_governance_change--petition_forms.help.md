# Petition For Local Governance Change - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from citizens expressing their support or opposition to a local governance change. Your input will help shape public opinion on the matter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **first name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **last name** in the second field.
3. Enter your valid **email address** in the third field to help us contact you if needed.
4. Enter your **phone number** (optional) in the fourth field.
5. Provide a clear and concise **message** expressing your viewpoint in the fifth field.
6. Select a **date** (optional) when you became aware of the issue or when you want to be contacted about it in the sixth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name**: Enter your given or personal name.
* **last name**: Enter your surname or family name.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted if needed.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number (optional) if you want to be contacted by phone.
* **message**: Provide a clear and concise statement expressing your viewpoint on the local governance change. Be respectful and follow the guidelines for constructive feedback.
* **date**: Select a date when you became aware of the issue or when you want to be contacted about it (optional).
