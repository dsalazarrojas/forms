# Legal Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for a legal petition, which is a written request to a court for some kind of relief or redress. A legal petition typically involves a specific legal case and is used to initiate or continue a legal process. This form helps gather all necessary details about the case and the petitioner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information: name, address, phone, and email.
2. Provide details about the case: case number, court name, case type, case status, and jurisdiction area.
3. Specify the parties involved: parties involved, parties contact info (if applicable).
4. Explain the reason and relief sought: reason, relief sought.
5. Choose the correct court level: lower, middle, higher, or supreme.
6. Select the court location.
7. Attach any necessary documents or files (if applicable).
8. Add any additional information, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Address** (2, text, required): Enter your complete address.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (4, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted).
* **Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the legal issue.
* **Time** (6, time, required): Enter the time of the legal issue.
* **Details** (7, text, required): Provide a detailed explanation of the issue.
* **Attachment** (8, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents or files (if applicable).
* **Case Number** (9, text, required): Enter the case number assigned by the court or other relevant authorities.
* **Court Name** (10, text, required): Enter the name of the court where the case is being filed.
* **Case Type** (11, select_one, required): Choose the type of case (civil or criminal).
* **Case Status** (12, select_multiple, required): Choose the current status of the case (pending, active, resolved).
* **Jurisdiction** (13, text, required): Specify the jurisdiction area where the case is being filed.
* **Location** (14, text, required): Enter the location where the case is being filed.
* **Reason** (15, text, required): Provide a clear explanation of the reason for the petition.
* **Relief Sought** (16, text, required): Specify the relief you are seeking from the court.
* **Parties Involved** (17, text, required): List all parties involved in the case.
* **Parties Contact Info** (18, text, optional): Provide contact information for the parties involved (if applicable).
* **Court Level** (19, select_one, required): Choose the level of the court (lower, middle, higher, supreme).
* **Court Location** (20, text, required): Enter the location of the court.
* **Jurisdiction Area** (21, text, required): Specify the jurisdiction area where the case is being filed.
* **Signature** (22, note, optional): Add your signature (if required or applicable).
* **Date of Service** (23, date, optional): Enter the date of service (if applicable).
* **Time of Service** (24, time, optional): Enter the time of service (if applicable).
* **Service Type** (25, text, required): Choose the type of service you are seeking.

Note that some fields have specific requirements based on the selected options, so ensure you fill out the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
