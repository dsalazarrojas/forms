# public_composting_petition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in public composting initiatives. It may be used by local government, non-profit, or community organizations to gather signatures or expressions of support for such initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin on the "introduction" page, where you can briefly describe your interest in public composting.
2. On the "name" page, provide your or your organization's name.
3. On the "email" page, enter your email address (if you wish to be contacted regarding your response).
4. On the "organization" page, if applicable, provide the name of your organization or affiliation.
5. Finally, on the "contact_name" page, provide a name and title for the person submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **introduction** (Page 1): This is the initial page where you can provide a brief description of your interest in public composting. You can simply type in a few sentences about why you're supporting this initiative.
* **name** (Page 2): Enter your or your organization's name as it appears on official documents.
* **email** (Page 2): Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted regarding your response or if you're interested in learning more about public composting initiatives.
* **organization** (Page 3): If applicable, provide the name of your organization, as your response may be attributed to it.
* **contact_name** (Page 4): Enter a name and title for the person submitting this form, ideally the person responsible for your or your organization's interest in public composting.
