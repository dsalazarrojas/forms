# running_for_student_council_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The running for Student Council petition form is used by students to submit their petitions to run for a position in the Student Council. This form collects essential information about the student's name, position, contact details, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **User Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or official documents.
2. **Position Running For (2)**: Select the position for which you are running (e.g., Student Council, Student Council Treasurer, etc.).
3. **Student ID (3)**: Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
4. **Email (4)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
5. **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
6. **Signature (6)**: Sign your name to confirm your submission.
7. **Reasons For Running (7)**: Provide a brief explanation of why you are running for this position.
8. **Supporting Students (8)**: List the names of students who support your candidacy.
9. **Date Signed (9)**: Enter the date you signed this form.
10. **Time Signed (10)**: Enter the time you signed this form.
11. **Time Zone (11)**: Select your time zone (EST, CST, MST, PST, UTC).
12. **Time Zone Offset (12)** (Optional): If you are in a time zone not listed, please enter the offset from UTC.
13. **Time Zone Description (13)** (Optional): Provide any additional information about your time zone.
14. **Signature Image (14)**: Decide whether to upload or not upload a signature image.
15. **Student Grade (15)**: Check the box to indicate if you are a grade-level student.
16. **Student Grade Description (16)** (Optional): Provide a brief explanation of your grade-level status.
17. **Student Section (17)**: Check the box to indicate if you are in a specific section.
18. **Student Subsection (18)**: Check the box to indicate if you are in a specific subsection.
19. **Student Subsection (19)**: Check the box to indicate if you are in a specific subsection.
20. **Grade Level (20)**: Select the grade level you are currently in (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
21. **Student Year (21)**: Select the year you are in (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, etc.).
22. **Student Year Description (22)** (Optional): Provide a brief explanation of your year status.
23. **Student Grade Period (23)**: Check the box to indicate if you are currently in a specific grade period.

Note: Fields with an asterisk (*) are required fields. Please ensure you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
