# Name Change Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist individuals in preparing a petition to change their name. It collects essential information needed for a formal name change petition, which is typically submitted to a competent court.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "User Input Form" section with your personal details.
2. In the "Name Change Petition" section, write a brief explanation of why you are requesting a name change.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input Form (<name>, <type>, required/optional)
This section is where you can input any additional information you feel is relevant to your name change petition. It's an open-ended field where you can provide context or supporting details.

### Name Change Petition (<name>, <type>, required/optional)
This section is a free-text field where you can explain the reason for your name change request. Keep it concise, but make sure to provide a clear and coherent statement.

### Date Of Birth (<name>, <type>, required/optional)
Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help us process your request efficiently.
