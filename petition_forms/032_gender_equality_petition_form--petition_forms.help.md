# Gender Equality Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gender Equality Petition Form is a platform for individuals to express their support or opposition to gender equality. It's a way to gather public opinion and facilitate communication with those who want to take action on this topic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these simple steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Petition" button.
2. Fill in your name in the text field provided.
3. Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further discussion.
4. If you'd like to include an additional comment or message, you can type it in the "comments" text field.
5. Finally, click on the "Submit" button to send your message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **gender_equality_petition_form** (Text, Optional): A space where you can write your thoughts, ideas, and views on gender equality.
* **name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in public records.
* **email** (Email, Optional): A valid email address is required so we can contact you regarding your submission.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): If you have any additional comments or concerns that you'd like to share, you can provide your phone number. This is not required for processing, but it will aid in further communication.
* **comments** (Text, Optional): This field is for any additional, lengthy comments or messages you'd like to share with us.
