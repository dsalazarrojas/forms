# Support SB 224 HB701 For Texas Seniors - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather support for a bill (SB 224 HB701) aimed at helping Texas seniors. The bill's goal is to provide benefits and protections for seniors in the state of Texas.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in the order of the pages.
- Each page should take around 1-2 minutes to complete, depending on the number of fields you need to fill out.
- Make sure to review each field carefully before moving on to the next page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Advocacy Form For Texas Seniors
* **Advocacy Form For Texas Seniors** (`support_sb_224_hb701_for_texas_seniors_page`, text, optional): This page is a text input field where you can provide a message of support or opposition to the bill. You can write a brief statement explaining your position and why you support or oppose the bill.

### 2. Submit Letter of Support
* **Submit Letter of Support** (`submit_letter_of_support`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to submit a letter of support for the bill, or "No" if you do not support the bill.

### 3. Submit Support For Who
* **Submit Support For Who** (`submit_support_for_who`, select_multiple, optional): Choose who you support the bill for. You can select multiple options if applicable.

### 4. Submit Support Letter To House Member
* **Submit Support Letter To House Member** (`submit_support_letter_to_house_member`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether you want to submit a letter to a house member.

### 5. Submit Letter To Email
* **Submit Letter To Email** (`submit_letter_to_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted for any updates or follow-up information about the bill.

### 6. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for any updates or follow-up information about the bill.

### 7. Submit Support Letter
* **Submit Support Letter** (`submit_support_letter`, select_one, optional): Choose "Yes" to submit a support letter for the bill, or "No" if you do not want to submit a letter.

### 8. Name of Supporter
* **Name of Supporter** (`name_of_supporter`, text, optional): Enter your name as a supporter of the bill, if applicable.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to help us better understand your position on the bill.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
- You can submit the form even if you are not a Texas resident.
- Please note that by submitting this form, you are agreeing to be contacted for any updates or follow-up information about the bill.
