# good_cause_petition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for a petition, which is a formal request made to an entity, usually a government official or organization, to take some action or make a decision regarding a particular cause or issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Reason For Petition** field, which is a required field. This is where you'll explain the reason behind your petition.
2. If you have any **Supporting Documentation** that can help support your petition, you can upload it in the **Supporting Documentation** field.
3. In the **Petition Details** field, provide a clear and concise description of the specific details and relief you're seeking.
4. If there have been any **Adverse Decisions** made regarding your petition, please describe them in this field.
5. Finally, in the **Relief Sought** field, specify what relief you're asking for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reason For Petition** (`reason_for_petition`, text, required):
	+ This field is a required field where you'll explain the reason behind your petition.
* **Supporting Documentation** (`supporting_documentation`, text, optional):
	+ This field is optional but if you have any documents, files, or other supporting evidence that can help support your petition, you can upload it here.
* **Petition Details** (`petition_details`, text, optional):
	+ In this field, provide a clear and concise description of the specific details and relief you're seeking.
* **Adverse Decisions** (`adverse_decisions`, text, optional):
	+ If there have been any adverse decisions made regarding your petition, please describe them in this field.
* **Relief Sought** (`relief_sought`, text, optional):
	+ Specify what relief you're asking for in this field.

## Tips
* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling in the fields, especially the **Reason For Petition** field, as this is a crucial part of your petition.
* If you have any supporting documentation, please ensure it's relevant and directly related to your petition.
* If you're seeking relief, be specific about what you're asking for.
* If there have been any adverse decisions, please provide a clear explanation and context.
