# Political Reform Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Reform Petition Form is used to collect information from users who wish to submit a petition for political reform. The form will help us understand the concerns and demands of the users and help us to create meaningful change.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Petition Details" section with a brief description of your petition.
3. In the "Signature" section, select "Yes" to sign the petition or "No" if you do not wish to sign.
4. Enter your contact details, including your name and address, in the "Contact Details" section.
5. Provide your email address in the "Email" section.
6. In the "Address" field, enter your home address or the address where you want to be contacted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Petition Details** (`Petition Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your petition, stating its purpose and goals.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `select_multiple`, options: ['Yes', 'No'], required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate that you wish to sign the petition, or "No" if you do not wish to sign.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on official documents (e.g., license, passport, etc.).
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your home address or the address where you would like to be contacted.
