# petition_letter_to_town_council - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a petition to the town council, allowing citizens to express their concerns, suggestions, or proposals to the local government. It is designed to be a clear and concise way to communicate with the town council, helping to foster transparency and accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your petition in the "Petition Title" field. This should be a brief and descriptive title that summarizes your request or concern.
2. Select the relevant category for your petition from the "Category" dropdown menu. This will help the town council understand the nature of your request.
3. In the "Requesting Action" field, describe the specific action or outcome you are seeking from the town council.
4. If you want to be contacted regarding your petition, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a phone number where you can be reached, enter it in the "Phone" field.
6. Finally, use the "Comments" field to add any additional details or context about your petition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Petition Title** (form_title, text, required): A brief and descriptive title that summarizes your request or concern.
* **Category** (form_category, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more relevant categories to help the town council understand the nature of your request.
* **Requesting Action** (form_description, text, required): Describe the specific action or outcome you are seeking from the town council.
* **Email** (form_email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted regarding your petition.
* **Phone** (form_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted regarding your petition.
* **Comments** (form_comments, text, optional): Add any additional details or context about your petition.
