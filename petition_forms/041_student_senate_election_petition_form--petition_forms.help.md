# Student Senate Election Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Senate Election Petition Form is a tool for students to express their support for a particular candidate in an election. This form collects information about the student candidate, the reason for petitioning, and the date of the election.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Student Candidate field with the name of the student running for election.
* Enter a brief explanation for the Reason for Petition field, describing why you support this candidate.
* Select a date for the Election Date field, which should be the date of the student senate election.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Candidate**: Enter the name of the student who is running for election. 
* **Reason for Petition**: Provide a brief explanation for why you support this candidate. 
* **Election Date**: Enter the date of the student senate election.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the election date to ensure you are petitioning for the correct election.
* Review the student senate's guidelines and rules before submitting your petition to understand the process and any specific requirements.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact <form@gic.mx>.
