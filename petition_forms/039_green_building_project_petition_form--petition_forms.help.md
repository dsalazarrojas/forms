# green_building_project_petition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in participating in the Green Building project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First name and Last name in the "First name" and "Last name" fields respectively.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the "Phone number" field.
4. Enter the name of the project you are interested in participating in in the "Project name" field.
5. Specify the location of the project in the "Location" field.
6. Enter the date on which you sign the form in the "Signature date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the time on which you sign the form in the "Signature time" field in the format 12:XX PM/AM.
8. Optionally, add a brief Message in the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (text, First name): Enter your First name.
- **Last name** (text, Last name): Enter your Last name.
- **Email** (email, Email): Enter your Email address.
- **Phone number** (text, Phone number): Enter your Phone number.
- **Project name** (text, Project name): Enter the name of the project you are interested in participating in.
- **Location** (text, Location): Specify the location of the project you are interested in participating in.
- **Signature date** (date, Signature date): Enter the date on which you sign the form in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Signature time** (time, Signature time): Enter the time on which you sign the form in the format 12:XX PM/AM.
- **Message** (text, Message): Optionally, add a brief message about your interest in the project.
