# Petition For Regulatory Review - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Petition For Regulatory Review" form is a formal request to regulatory bodies for review and approval of a particular action or decision. It is intended to gather information about the request, contact details of the submitter, and any additional supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Pet request** field with a detailed and clear description of the action or decision you are requesting.
2. Provide your **contact information**, including your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Enter the **date of request**, the date on which you are submitting this request.
4. In the **description of request** field, provide a detailed and concise explanation of the request.
5. Select one of the options from the **requested action** field, which will help us understand the nature of your request.
6. If applicable, select the **regulatory body** that has jurisdiction over your request.
7. Upload any additional supporting documentation in the **file upload** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **email** field, which will be used to contact you regarding your request.
9. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
10. Enter the **date signed**, the date on which you signed the petition.
11. Enter the **date signed** in the **date signed** field, which should match the date you actually signed the petition.
12. Enter your **signature** in the **signature** field, which should be your actual handwritten signature.
13. Provide any additional **comments** regarding your request.
14. If you have any supporting documentation, enter the **file url** where it can be accessed.
