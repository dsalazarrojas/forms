# Campus Vehicle Policy Petition - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect signatures and supporting information from students, faculty, and staff on a particular issue related to vehicle policy on campus. The goal is to gather insights and data to inform decision-making regarding vehicle-related policies and practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Ensure that you have read and understand the form's purpose as explained above.
2. Fill in your name in the **Supporter Name** field and ensure it's accurately represented.
3. Enter your email address (if you want to receive updates) in the **Supporter Email** field.
4. Choose your affiliation with the campus (e.g., Student, Faculty, Staff, or Community member) in the **Affiliation** field.
5. If you wish to provide a location, enter it in the **Signature Location** field.
6. If you wish to provide a brief statement or reason for signing, do so in the **Position Statement** field. This is optional.
7. Choose whether or not to consent to have your name published with petition results in the **Consent to Publish Name** field.
8. Optionally provide your postal code for demographic tracking purposes in the **Postal Code** field.
9. Choose whether or not to opt-in for updates in the **Opt in for updates** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supporter Name (Required)**: A field where you enter your name to associate it with your petition signature.
* **Supporter Email (Optional)**: An optional field to provide your email address for updates and information about the petition.
* **Affiliation (Required)**: Select your affiliation with the campus (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Community member).
* **Signature Location (Optional)**: A field to provide your location, e.g., city or campus.
* **Position Statement (Optional)**: A brief statement or reason for signing the petition.
* **Consent to Publish Name (Required)**: Decide whether or not to have your name published with petition results.
* **Postal Code (Optional)**: Provide your postal code for demographic tracking purposes.
* **Opt in for updates (Optional)**: Choose whether or not to receive updates about the petition's progress.
