# Consumer Rights Petition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for consumers to submit their petitions, grievances, or concerns about a specific company or organization. It gathers information about the issue, the consumer's contact details, and their desired outcome. This data will be reviewed and addressed by the relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form carefully and accurately.
2. Select the relevant category and industry for the petition.
3. Provide your contact information and the details about the issue.
4. Estimate the number of people affected and describe the impact on you.
5. Specify what changes you are seeking and list your demands.
6. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
7. Sign and date the form to submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Petition Title** (1): Enter a brief title of the petition.
  - A clear and concise title helps identify the petition.
  - Keep it short and relevant to the issue.

* **Petition Category** (2): Select the category that best matches your petition.
  - This helps us understand the nature of the issue.
  - Choose the one that best describes your concern.

* **Target Company or Organization** (3): Enter the name of the company being petitioned.
  - This is the company or organization you are addressing your concerns to.
  - Make sure to spell it correctly.

* **Company Industry** (4): If known, select the industry sector of the company.
  - This helps us understand the context of the issue.
  - If not sure, leave it blank.

* **First Name** (5), **Last Name** (6), **Email Address** (7), **Phone Number** (8), **Street Address** (9), **City** (10), **State** (11), **ZIP Code** (12), **Country** (13): Fill in your contact information.
  - These are your personal details and will be used to contact you.
  - Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.

* **Issue Description** (14): Describe the issue or grievance in detail.
  - Be as specific as possible about the problem.
  - Include relevant dates and facts.

* **Incident Date** (15): If applicable, enter the date when the issue occurred.
  - Helps us understand the timeline of the issue.
  - If not known, leave it blank.

* **Number of Consumers Affected** (16): Estimate the number of people affected.
  - Helps us understand the scope of the issue.
  - Be honest and accurate.

* **How Has This Affected You** (17): Describe the impact on you personally.
  - Helps us understand the personal effect of the issue.
  - Be as detailed as possible.

* **Desired Outcome** (18): Specify what change or outcome you are seeking.
  - Clearly state your demands or desired resolution.
  - Keep it specific and realistic.

* **Specific Demands** (19): List your specific demands or actions you are requesting.
  - Be as detailed as possible about what you are asking for.
  - Keep it clear and concise.

* **Have You Contacted the Company** (20): Select the option that applies to you.
  - Helps us understand if you have already reached out to the company.
  - Be honest about your previous contact.

* **Company Response** (21): If applicable, describe any response from the company.
  - Helps us understand their reaction.
  - Be factual and accurate.

* **Supporting Documents** (22): If applicable, list any supporting documents.
  - Helps us understand the supporting evidence.
  - Be as detailed as possible.

* **Witnesses or Supporting Parties** (23): If applicable, list anyone who can support your claim.
  - Helps us understand who is backing your petition.
  - Be honest and accurate.

* **Are You Willing to Speak to Media** (24): Select the option that applies to you.
  - Helps us understand if you are willing to go public.
  - Be honest about your willingness.

* **Interest in Legal Action** (25): Select the option that applies to you.
  - Helps us understand if you are considering legal action.
  - Be honest and accurate.

* **Will You Share This Petition** (26): Select the option that applies to you.
  - Helps us understand if you will help gather signatures.
  - Be honest and willing to participate.

* **Additional Comments** (27): If applicable, add any other information.
  - Helps us understand any other relevant information.
  - Be as detailed as possible.

* **Signature Confirmation** (28): Confirm that the information is accurate.
  - Helps us understand that you have reviewed the form.
  - Be honest and accurate.

* **Petition Date** (29): Date and sign the form.
  - Helps us understand when you submitted the petition.
  - Sign it accurately and honestly.
