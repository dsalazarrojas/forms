# Lake Pleasant Road Improvement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lake Pleasant Road Improvement form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from the community on how to improve the road. This form is intended to gather ideas and suggestions from residents, visitors, and stakeholders to enhance the road's usability and safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a title for your suggestion in the "Lake Pleasant Road Improvement" field.
2. Select the category of the form (e.g., Petition Forms, Other Forms).
3. Provide a brief description of your suggestion in the "Description" field.
4. If applicable, specify the tool or department assigned to handle your suggestion in the "Assigned to" field.
5. Specify the file name you would like to use for the output file in the "Output File" field.
6. Enter a unique identifier for the form in the "Form ID" field (this is for internal purposes only).
7. Enter a title for your suggestion in the "Title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Lake Pleasant Road Improvement** (`petition_title`, text, not required): Enter a title that summarizes your suggestion or idea for improving the road.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required): Select the type of form you are submitting (e.g., Petition Forms, Other Forms).
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): Provide a brief description of your suggestion or idea.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, not required): If applicable, select one or more tools or departments that should handle your suggestion.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, not required): Specify the file name you would like to use for the output file.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, not required): Enter a unique identifier for the form (this is for internal purposes only).
* **Title** (`title`, text, not required): Enter a title that summarizes your suggestion or idea for improving the road.
