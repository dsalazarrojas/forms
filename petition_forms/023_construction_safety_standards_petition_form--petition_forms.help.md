# Construction Safety Standards Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Construction Safety Standards Petition Form is a tool for individuals to petition for changes to the construction industry's safety standards. This form will allow you to submit your suggestions and support for changes to the existing safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information as the petitioner, including your full name, and your organization/affiliation (if applicable).
2. Provide a valid contact email address so that we can reach out to you for updates about your petition.
3. Enter your support statement, explaining why you support the change to the safety standard.
4. If this petition pertains to a specific region, enter the relevant jurisdiction or area.
5. Choose whether you want your name to be published with the petition signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Signer name
* **Signer name**: <code>Signer name</code> (text, required)
	+ The full name of the person submitting this petition.
### Organization
* **Organization**: <code>Organization</code> (text, optional)
	+ The employer or affiliation of the person submitting this petition, if applicable.
### Contact email
* **Contact email**: <code>Contact email</code> (email, optional)
	+ A valid email address to reach out to you for updates about the petition.
### Support statement
* **Support statement**: <code>Support statement</code> (text, required)
	+ A clear explanation of why you support the change to the safety standard.
### Region
* **Region**: <code>Region</code> (text, optional)
	+ The jurisdiction or area this petition pertains to.
### Permission to publish
* **Permission to publish name**: <code>Permission to publish</code> (select_one, optional)
	+ Whether you want your name to be published with the petition signatures.
