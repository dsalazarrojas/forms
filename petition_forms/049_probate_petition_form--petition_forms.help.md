# Probate Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Probate Petition Form is used to collect information from individuals or representatives of the estate who wish to file a petition in the probate court.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field. Please ensure that this is a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field. This can be a phone number where you can be reached.
5. Enter the Case Number in the "Case Number" field. This is the unique identifier for the case.
6. Select your County from the list of options in the "County" field.
7. Enter your State in the "State" field.
8. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text Field)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (Text Field)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (Email Field)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number (Text Field)**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Case Number (Text Field)**: Enter the unique identifier for the case.
* **County (Select One Field)**: Select your county from the list of options.
* **State (Text Field)**: Enter your state.
* **Date of Birth (Date Field)**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have all necessary information available before starting the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the probate court or your legal representative for assistance.
