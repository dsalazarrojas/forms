This form, "Event Catering Satisfaction Questionnaire", is designed to collect feedback from event organizers, attendees, or their guests who have utilized the catering services. It will help assess the overall quality and satisfaction of the catering services, identifying areas of improvement and opportunities to enhance customer experience. This form ensures that the feedback is consistent and comparable across events, allowing for data-driven analysis to inform decision-making and drive future service development. Before filling this form, please make sure you haven't already submitted a review or feedback for the same event.

# Event Catering Satisfaction Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Catering Satisfaction Questionnaire is designed to gauge the satisfaction level of customers with our catering services. It helps identify areas of improvement and enhances our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Rate your overall experience and the quality of food, service, and value for money for the event.
2. Provide your feedback on the event details such as event date, event time, event location, number of attendees, and the name of the event.
3. Rate the communication and the quality of the catering team, if applicable.
4. Share your recommendations and suggestions on how to improve our services.
5. Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `required=true`): Rate your overall experience with our catering services on a scale of 1-5.
* **Food Quality** (`food`, `required=true`): Rate the quality of the food served at the event.
* **Service** (`service`, `required=false`): Rate the quality of the service provided during the event. (Optional)
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `required=true`): Rate the value for money of the catering services.
* **Event Team** (`event_team`, `required=true`): Rate the quality of the event team.
* **Food Quality Rating** (`food_quality_rating`, `required=true`): Rate the quality of the food.
* **Service Quality Rating** (`service_quality_rating`, `required=true`): Rate the quality of service.
* **Value for Money Rating** (`value_for_money_rating`, `required=true`): Rate the value for money.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `required=false`): Rate the level of communication provided by our team. (Optional)
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `required=true`): Provide the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `required=true`): Provide the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, `required=true`): Provide the location of the event.
* **Number of Attendees** (`attendees`, `required=true`): Provide the number of attendees at the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `required=true`): Provide the name of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `required=true`): Provide the type of event.
* **Catering Service** (`event_catering_service`, `required=true`): Provide the type of catering service.
* **Catering Team** (`event_catering_team`, `required=false`): Provide the name of the catering team members. (Optional)
* **Catering Notes** (`event_catering_note`, `required=false`): Provide any additional comments about the event catering services.
* **Recommendation** (`event_catering_recommendation`, `required=true`): Indicate if you would recommend our services to colleagues, friends, or family.
* **Suggestion** (`event_catering_suggestion`, `required=false`): If you would recommend our services, provide your suggestions on how to improve our services.
* **Email** (`event_catering_email`, `required=false`): Provide your email address for any future communication.
* **Phone** (`event_catering_phone`, `required=false`): Provide your phone number for any future communication.
* **Website** (`event_catering_website`, `required=false`): Provide your website for any future communication.
* **Additional Comments** (`event_catering_additional_comments`, `required=false`): Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
