# Conference Feedback Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help us understand your experience and perceptions of the conference, and to improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please answer the questions based on your actual experience with the conference.
2.  Provide as much detail as you are comfortable sharing.
3.  If you have any additional thoughts or comments, please use the "Open Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Conference Attended** (`conference_attended`, `text`, required): Please type the name of the conference you attended.
*   **Your Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, optional): If you want to share your name, please provide it for follow-up purposes.
*   **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, optional): This will help us contact you for follow-up.
*   **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, `date`, required): Please indicate when you attended the conference.
*   **Attendee Profile** (`attendee_profile`, `note`, optional): Please describe your background in brief.
*   **Job Role** (`job_role`, `select_one`, required): This will help us gauge how our content applies to your professional life.
*   **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Please select the industry your work belongs to.
*   **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, optional): If you wish to share your experience level, please choose one of the provided options.
*   **Satisfaction Survey** (`satisfaction_survey`, `note`, optional): This will allow us to gauge your satisfaction with the conference overall.
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required): This helps us know if you were pleased or unhappy with the event.
*   **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, `select_one`, required): How relevant was the content for you?
*   **Speaker Expertise** (`speaker_expertise`, `select_one`, required): Please rate the speaker's knowledge on the subject matter.
*   **Presentation Quality** (`presentation_quality`, `select_one`, optional): How good was the presentation?
*   **Logistics Rating** (`logistics_rating`, `select_one`, required): How well was the conference organized?
*   **Value for Time** (`value_for_time`, `select_one`, required): How well was your time spent at the conference?
*   **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, `select_one`, required): Was the cost justified for your experience?
*   **Learning Outcomes** (`learning_outcomes`, `select_one`, required): What did you gain from the event?
*   **Will Apply Learning** (`will_apply_learning`, `select_one`, optional): Will you apply the knowledge gained from this conference?
*   **Recommendation Likelihood** (`recommendation_likelihood`, `number`, required): How likely are you to recommend the conference?
*   **Would Return** (`would_return`, `select_one`, required): Will you attend the conference again?
*   **Open Feedback** (`open_feedback`, `note`, optional): This is your space for any comments or suggestions you may have.
*   **What Worked Well** (`what_worked_well`, `text`, optional): What did we do well in organizing the conference?
*   **What Needs Improvement** (`what_needs_improvement`, `text`, optional): What could we improve on in future conferences?
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Anything you'd like to add about your experience.

**Tips**

*   Try not to spend more than 5-7 minutes answering the form.
*   Be as honest and genuine as possible with your answers.
*   If you have any further comments or suggestions, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

Please fill out the form according to the guidelines above and submit it once completed. Your feedback is crucial for us to improve and better serve our attendees in future events.
