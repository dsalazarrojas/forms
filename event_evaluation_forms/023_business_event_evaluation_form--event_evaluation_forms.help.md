# Business Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Event Evaluation Form is designed to collect feedback and evaluation from attendees about their experience at an event. This form is meant to assess the overall quality of the event, the effectiveness of the content and sessions, the organization and logistics, and the networking opportunities provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions and sections below to understand what is being asked.
2. Answer each question honestly and accurately based on your experience at the event.
3. Use the provided options or text fields to provide feedback and suggestions.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Your Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an attendee.
* **Your Company** (`attendee_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you attended the event (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (Very Unsatisfied, Unsatisfied, Neutral, Satisfied, or Very Satisfied).
* **Would Recommend** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): State if you would recommend this event to others (Definitely Not, Probably Not, Neutral, Probably Yes, or Definitely Yes).
* **Likelihood to Attend Again** (`likelihood_attend_again`, select_one, required): Rate your likelihood of attending future events (Very Unlikely, Unlikely, Neutral, Likely, or Very Likely).
* **Number of Sessions Attended** (`sessions_attended`, number, optional): Enter the number of sessions you attended.
* **Quality of Session Content** (`content_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the session content (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Quality of Speakers and Presenters** (`speaker_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the speakers and presenters (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Learning Objectives** (`learning_outcomes`, select_one, required): State if you achieved your learning objectives (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree).
* **Venue and Facilities** (`venue_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the venue and facilities (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Registration and Check-in Process** (`registration_process`, select_one, required): Rate the registration and check-in process (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Event Timing and Schedule** (`timing`, select_one, required): Rate the event timing and schedule (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Meals and Refreshments** (`catering`, select_one, optional): Rate the meals and refreshments (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Quality of Networking Opportunities** (`networking_opportunities`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the networking opportunities (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Valuable Connections Made** (`connections_made`, select_one, optional): State if you made valuable professional connections (Strongly Disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, or Strongly Agree).
* **Most Valuable Aspect of the Event** (`most_valuable`, text, optional): Describe the most valuable aspect of the event.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvements`, text, optional): Suggest areas for improvement for future events.
* **Topics for Future Events** (`future_topics`, text, optional): Suggest topics for future events.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Use the provided options or fields to provide clear and concise feedback.
- If you have any suggestions or comments, please include them in the areas for improvement or topics for future events fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers or administrators.
