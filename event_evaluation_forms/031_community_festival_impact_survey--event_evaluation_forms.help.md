## Community Festival Impact Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Festival Impact Survey aims to gather insights from attendees on their experience with the festival, including their attendance, spending habits, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement. Your input is valuable in helping us create a better festival experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Name (optional), which we'll use for follow-up purposes only if you're willing to share.
2. Enter your Email Address to allow us to get in touch with you about the festival and other future events.
3. Select your ZIP Code, as it is a required piece of information to ensure we can understand the festival's local impact.
4. Choose how you attended the festival: In Person, Virtual, or Both.
5. Indicate the number of times you visited the festival: Once, 2-3 Times, 4-5 Times, or More than 5 Times.
6. Select how far you traveled to attend the festival: Local Resident, Less than 10 Miles, 10-25 Miles, 25-50 Miles, or More than 50 Miles.
7. Enter the number of people in your party that attended.
8. Estimate how much you spent at the festival, selecting from options Less than $25, $25-$50, $51-$100, $101-$200, or More than $200.
9. Choose what categories you spent money on during the festival: Food and Beverages, Merchandise, Tickets or Entry, Parking, Accommodations, or Other.
10. Rate your overall satisfaction with the festival on a scale of Very Satisfied to Very Dissatisfied.
11. Indicate your perceived economic impact on local businesses, choosing from Significant Benefit, Some Benefit, Neutral, Little Benefit, or No Benefit.
12. Answer if the festival boosted your community pride, selecting from Significant Boost, Some Boost, Neutral, Little Impact, or No Impact.
13. If you wish, describe your favorite aspects of the festival.
14. If you wish, mention any least favorite aspects of the festival that you'd like to see improved.
15. Confirm if you would attend the festival again, choosing from Definitely Yes, Probably Yes, Maybe, Probably No, or Definitely No.
16. Indicate how likely you are to recommend the festival to others, choosing from Very Likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Very Unlikely.
17. Share any suggestions for future festival improvements.
