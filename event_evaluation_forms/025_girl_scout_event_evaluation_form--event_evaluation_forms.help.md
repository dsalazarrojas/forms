# Girl Scout Event Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Girl Scout Event Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from Girl Scouts who have attended events hosted by their organization. Your input is invaluable in helping us improve future events and better serve our community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a red asterisk \*).
3. Choose your answers thoughtfully, as they will help inform future event planning.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Information
* **Event Information**: This section is for you to provide some basic information about the event you attended. Please fill in the text box with a brief description of the event, including its title, date, and any other relevant details that might be helpful for us to know.

### How did you feel about the event?
* **How did you feel about the event?**: This question is an open-ended opportunity for you to share your thoughts and feelings about the event. Be as detailed as possible, and don't hesitate to express your opinions or concerns.

### Would you attend this event again?
* **Would you attend this event again?**: Choose one of the two options provided: 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us gauge the overall satisfaction with the event.

### Why or why not?
* **Why or why not?**: This field is for you to elaborate on your previous answer. If you chose 'Yes', please explain why you would attend again. If you chose 'No', please explain why you wouldn't attend again. Be as specific as possible in your answer.

### How would you rate the event?
* **How would you rate the event?**: This is a numerical rating system to help us measure your overall satisfaction with the event. Use the scale provided to indicate how satisfied you were with the event. The more stars you give, the higher your satisfaction.

## Tips
* Make sure to be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you have any suggestions or feedback that didn't fit in any of the provided fields, please don't hesitate to add them in the 'Why or why not?' field.
* Your input is invaluable to us, and we appreciate your time and effort in helping us improve our events!
