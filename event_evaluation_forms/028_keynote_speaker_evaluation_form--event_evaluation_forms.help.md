# Keynote Speaker Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Keynote Speaker Evaluation Form is a tool used to gather feedback from attendees on the performance of the keynote speaker at an event. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to help the speaker and organizers improve their future performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the first page with your name and presentation title.
2.  Evaluate the speaker's performance by selecting one of the options for each of the evaluation questions.
3.  Provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about the speaker's performance.
4.  Make sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. speaker_name (Speaker Name)
*   This is the field where you will enter your name as an attendee of the event.

### 2. presentation_title (Presentation Title)
*   This is the field where you will enter the title of the presentation you attended.

### 3. evaluation_scale (How would you rate the presentation?)
*   This is the field where you will select one of the options to rate the speaker's performance. The options are:
    + Excellent
    + Very Good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor

### 4. content (Content)
*   This is the field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the speaker's performance.

### 5. organization (Organization)
*   This is the field where you can enter the name of the organization you are associated with, if any.

### 6. relevance (Relevance)
*   This is the field where you will select multiple options to rate the relevance of the presentation to your work or interests. The options are:
    + Extremely Relevant
    + Somewhat Relevant
    + Not Relevant
    + Not at All Relevant

### 7. engagement (Engagement)
*   This is the field where you will select multiple options to rate the engagement level of the presentation. The options are:
    + Excellent
    + Very Good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor

### 8. speaker_presentation_skills (Speaker Presentation Skills)
*   This is the field where you will select multiple options to rate the speaker's presentation skills. The options are:
    + Excellent
    + Very Good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor

### 9. overall (Overall)
*   This is the field where you will select one of the options to rate the overall performance of the speaker. The options are:
    + Excellent
    + Very Good
    + Good
    + Fair
    + Poor

### 10. comments (Comments)
*   This is the field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback about the speaker's performance.

## Tips
*   Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help the speaker and organizers improve.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Use clear and concise language in the comments field.
*   If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
