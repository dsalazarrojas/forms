# corporate_team_building_activity_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate team building activity evaluation form is used to gather feedback from participants about their experience with the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly, taking into account your experience with the activity.
3. Please answer all questions honestly and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**:
	+ **Label: Participant Information**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Please enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Activity Participation**:
	+ **Label: Activity Participation**
	+ **Type: Number**
	+ **Description:** Indicate how much you participated in the activity, with 1 being "did not participate" and 10 being "completely participated".
* **Evaluation Scale**:
	+ **Label: Evaluation Scale**
	+ **Type: Select One**
	+ **Description:** Select a number from 1 to 10 to evaluate how much you enjoyed the activity.
* **Participant Experience**:
	+ **Label: Participant Experience**
	+ **Type: Select Multiple**
	+ **Description:** Choose all that apply to describe your experience with the activity.
* **Participant Satisfaction**:
	+ **Label: Participant Satisfaction**
	+ **Type: Select One**
	+ **Description:** Select a number from 1 to 5 to indicate how satisfied you are with the activity.
* **Participant Comments**:
	+ **Label: Participant Comments**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Add any comments or feedback about your experience with the activity.
* **Participant Photo**:
	+ **Label: Participant Photo**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Please take a photo of yourself during the activity. (optional)
* **Participant Phone**:
	+ **Label: Participant Phone**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Participant E-mail**:
	+ **Label: Participant E-mail**
	+ **Type: Email**
	+ **Description:** Enter your email address for emergency contact purposes.
* **Participant Address**:
	+ **Label: Participant Address**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Enter your address for emergency contact purposes.
* **Participant Notes**:
	+ **Label: Participant Notes**
	+ **Type: Note**
	+ **Description:** Add any additional notes about your experience with the activity.
* **Participant Diet**:
	+ **Label: Participant Diet**
	+ **Type: Select One**
	+ **Description:** Choose your dietary preference.
* **Participant Special Instructions**:
	+ **Label: Participant Special Instructions**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Enter any special instructions or requests you have.
* **Participant Allergies**:
	+ **Label: Participant Allergies**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** List any allergies you have.
* **Participant Health Condition**:
	+ **Label: Participant Health Condition**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Describe any health conditions you have.
* **Participant Medical Notes**:
	+ **Label: Participant Medical Notes**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Enter any medical notes or information.
* **Participant Emergency Contact**:
	+ **Label: Participant Emergency Contact**
	+ **Type: Select One**
	+ **Description:** Indicate whether or not you have an emergency contact.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Name**:
	+ **Label: Participant Emergency Contact Name**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Relationship**:
	+ **Label: Participant Emergency Contact Relationship**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Phone**:
	+ **Label: Participant Emergency Contact Phone**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Email**:
	+ **Label: Participant Emergency Contact Email**
	+ **Type: Email**
	+ **Description:** Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Address**:
	+ **Label: Participant Emergency Contact Address**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Emergency Contact Note**:
	+ **Label: Participant Emergency Contact Note**
	+ **Type: Text**
	+ **Description:** Add any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Participant Signature**:
	+ **Label: Participant Signature**
	+ **Type: Select One**
	+ **Description:** Indicate whether or not you signed an agreement.
* **Participant Agreement**:
	+ **Label: Participant Agreement**
	+ **Type: Select One**
	+ **Description:** Indicate whether or not you agreed to participate in the activity.

Note: Please review and sign the agreement before submitting the form.
