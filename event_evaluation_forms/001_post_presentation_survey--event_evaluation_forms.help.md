# postPresentationSurvey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and ratings from the audience after a presentation. It is meant to gather valuable insights on the presentation's quality, content, and overall impact.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Choose the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **post_presentation_survey**: A text field for entering any additional comments or feedback about the presentation.
* **presentation_feedback**: A select multiple option field with two options: "Yes" or "No". Choose "Yes" if you found the presentation engaging, informative, and effective. Choose "No" otherwise.
* **presenter_name**: A text field for entering the presenter's name.
* **presentation_date**: A date field for entering the date of the presentation.
* **presentation_time**: A time field for entering the time of the presentation.
* **rating**: A select one option field with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. Choose the rating that best reflects your opinion of the presentation's quality.
* **comments**: A note field for entering any additional comments or feedback about the presentation.
* **email**: An email field for entering your email address (if you wish to be contacted for further discussion).
* **phone**: A text field for entering your phone number.
* **rating_1**: A select one option field with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. (Note: This field is similar to the "rating" field, but with slightly different options. Choose the rating that best reflects your opinion of the presentation's quality.)
* **comments_1**: A note field for entering any additional comments or feedback about the presentation.
* **rating_2**: A select one option field with options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor. (Note: This field is similar to the "rating" field, but with slightly different options. Choose the rating that best reflects your opinion of the presentation's quality.)
* **comments_2**: A note field for entering any additional comments or feedback about the presentation.

Note: The fields with the same name but different labels are intended to be similar in purpose, but with slightly different context or focus.
