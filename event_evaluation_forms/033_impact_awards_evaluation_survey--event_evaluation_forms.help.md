## Purpose
This form evaluates the impact and effectiveness of an award or recognition given to participants in an event or program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by reading the introduction page carefully.
2. On the second page, provide your name and any additional details relevant to the participant.
3. In the third page, evaluate the overall scale of the impact of the award or recognition, using the provided scale.
4. Answer the questions about your attendance at the event and how well you understood the award or recognition purpose and significance.
5. On the last page, indicate whether you would recommend the award or recognition to others.
6. If you have any additional comments or ratings, please include them in the final page.
