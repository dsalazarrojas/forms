# Hallowfest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects feedback and opinions from users about their experiences with Hallowfest, a hypothetical festival. It's designed to gauge user engagement, satisfaction, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully to understand what it's asking.
2. Select the best answer for each question based on your experience with Hallowfest.
3. Fill out all the required fields (none are required in this case).
4. Submit the form to share your opinions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How often do you attend** (`festival_frequency`, select_one, optional): This question asks about your frequency of attending Hallowfest. You can select from the options: Halloween Festival, Summer Festival, Never, Rarely, Occasionally, or Always.
* **Favorite Halloween character** (`favorite_halloween_charachter`, text, optional): This field is for you to mention your favorite Halloween character, if you have one.
* **Best Halloween activity** (`best_halloween_activity`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more activities that you enjoy doing during Hallowfest, such as Pumpkin Carving, Trick or Treat, Costume Party, or Attending a Horror Movie.
* **Attended Hallowfest** (`attended_hallowfest_event`, select_one, optional): This question asks if you've attended Hallowfest in the past. You can select 'True' or 'False'.
* **Best food at Hallowfest** (`best_hallowfest_food`, select_one, optional): Choose the food that you think is the best at Hallowfest, from options like Candy, Donuts, Pumpkin Pie, or Sausage.
* **How would you describe the ambiance** (`hallowfest_ambiance`, select_one, optional): Describe the ambiance of Hallowfest using one of the provided options: Scary, Fun, Exciting, Peaceful, or Calming.
* **Suggestion for Hallowfest** (`hallowfest_suggestion`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or ideas you have for improving Hallowfest.
* **Rate Hallowfest** (`hallowfest_rating`, number, optional): Rate Hallowfest on a scale of 1-10.
* **Comments** (`hallowfest_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts you have about Hallowfest.
* **Attended other Hallowfest event** (`attended_other_event`, select_one, optional): This question asks if you've attended another Hallowfest event. You can select 'True' or 'False'.
