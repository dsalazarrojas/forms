# Online Seminar Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Seminar Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback from attendees after an online seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title of the seminar you attended.
2. Provide the date of the seminar.
3. Choose a time slot for the seminar.
4. Enter the location of the seminar.
5. Specify the speaker of the seminar.
6. Rate the overall quality of the seminar (1-5 stars).
7. Rate the seminar in four categories: 
    - 1.  (required)
    - 2.  (required)
    - 3.  (required)
    - 4.  (required)
8. Add any additional comments or feedback about the seminar.
9. Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
11. Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Title** (`seminar_title`, text, optional): A brief title of the seminar you attended.
* **Event Date** (`seminar_date`, date, optional): The date of the seminar you attended.
* **Event Time** (`seminar_time`, time, optional): A time slot for the seminar you attended.
* **Event Location** (`seminar_location`, text, optional): The location of the seminar you attended.
* **Speaker** (`speaker`, text, optional): The name of the speaker who conducted the seminar.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number, optional): Rate the seminar's overall quality (1-5 stars).
* **Rating 1** (`rating_1`, select_one, required): Rate the seminar's content quality (1-5 stars).
* **Rating 2** (`rating_2`, select_one, required): Rate the seminar's presentation quality (1-5 stars).
* **Rating 3** (`rating_3`, select_one, required): Rate the seminar's interaction quality (1-5 stars).
* **Rating 4** (`rating_4`, select_one, required): Rate the seminar's organization quality (1-5 stars).
* **Rating 5** (`rating_5`, select_one, required): Rate the seminar's overall quality (1-5 stars).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the seminar.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Add any other comments or feedback about the seminar.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any other comments or feedback about the seminar.
