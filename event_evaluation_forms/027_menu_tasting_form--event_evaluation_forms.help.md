# Menu Tasting Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback on the menu tasting experience from customers. It will help us to evaluate the presentation, flavor, freshness, and other aspects of our menu items. By providing your feedback, you'll be contributing to the improvement of our menu offerings.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Rate the presentation of the dish on a scale of 1-5.
3. Rate the flavor of the dish on a scale of 1-5.
4. Rate the freshness of the produce on a scale of 1-5.
5. Select the type of cuisine the dish belongs to from the available options (American, Italian, Chinese, Indian).
6. Rate how much the dish appeals to you on a scale of 1-5.
7. Rate the portion size of the dish on a scale of 1-5.
8. Indicate how much you would like to have the dish again on a scale of 1-5.
9. Select a price range you are willing to pay for the dish (e.g., $5-$10, $10-$15, $15-$20).
10. Rate how likely you are to recommend the dish to friends on a scale of 1-5.
11. Check off any options you would like to have a discount on the dish.
12. Rate the overall experience on a scale of 1-5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`form_name`, text, required): Please enter your name so we can know who provided the feedback.
* **How would you rate the presentation of the dish?** (`presentation_rating`, number, required): Rate the presentation of the dish on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How good was the flavor of the dish?** (`flavor_rating`, number, required): Rate the flavor of the dish on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **How fresh is the produce?** (`produce_freshness`, number, required): Rate the freshness of the produce on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **What type of cuisine does the dish belong to?** (`cuisine_type`, select one, required): Select the type of cuisine the dish belongs to from the available options (American, Italian, Chinese, Indian).
* **How much does the dish appeal to you?** (`appeal_rating`, number, required): Rate how much the dish appeals to you on a scale of 1-5.
* **How would you rate the portion size?** (`portion_size_rating`, number, required): Rate the portion size of the dish on a scale of 1-5.
* **How much would you like to have it again?** (`have_again_rating`, number, required): Rate how much you would like to have the dish again on a scale of 1-5.
* **What is the price range you are willing to pay for this dish?** (`price_range`, select multiple, required): Select a price range you are willing to pay for the dish (e.g., $5-$10, $10-$15, $15-$20).
* **How likely are you to recommend this dish to friends?** (`recommendation`, number, required): Rate how likely you are to recommend the dish to friends on a scale of 1-5.
* **Would you like to have a discount on this dish?** (`discount_request`, checkbox, required): Check off any options you would like to have a discount on the dish.
* **How would you rate the overall experience?** (`overall_experience`, number, required): Rate the overall experience on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips

* Be honest and rate the dish fairly.
* Make sure to complete all the required fields.
* If you have any comments or suggestions about the dish, please provide them in the space provided.
