# Social Impact Film Festival Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Impact Film Festival Evaluation Form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from festival attendees on the films shown during the festival. This form helps festival organizers understand which films resonated with the audience and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** of the film you just watched.
2. Select the **Event Date** when you attended the film.
3. Enter the **Film Title** of the film you watched.
4. Select the genres you felt the film belonged to (e.g., **Drama**, **Romance**, **Thriller**).
5. Enter the **Film Director's** name, if you remember it.
6. If you'd like, enter your **Rating** of the film out of 10.
7. Provide any additional **Feedback** on the film, such as what you liked or disliked about it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the film you watched. This will help us know which film you're evaluating.
* **Event Date**: Choose the date when you attended the film. This ensures we match your feedback with the correct film screening.
* **Film Title**: Enter the title of the film as it appears on the screen or in the festival's program.
* **Film Genre**: Select all genres that you think the film belongs to. You can choose multiple genres if the film blends elements of different styles.
* **Film Director**: If you remember the film's director, please enter their name. This will help us understand who created the film.
* **Rating**: If you'd like, you can rate the film out of 10, where 1 is the lowest and 10 is the highest.
* **Feedback**: Share any additional thoughts or comments about the film. This is your chance to tell us what you thought of the film and why.

## Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and honestly to help us improve the festival and provide better experiences for future attendees.
