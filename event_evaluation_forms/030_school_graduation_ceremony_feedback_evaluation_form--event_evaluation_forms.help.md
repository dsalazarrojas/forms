# School Graduation Ceremony Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of a school graduation ceremony. It will help us understand how well the ceremony was organized, how many attendees were present, and what suggestions you have for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as instructed. Please note that all fields are not required, but filling out as many as possible will help us gather more comprehensive feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Graduation Ceremony** (`graduation_ceremony`, text, optional): Please describe the graduation ceremony you attended, including any notable events or moments that stood out to you.
- **Graduation Ceremony Date** (`graduation_ceremony_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the graduation ceremony you attended.
- **Graduation Ceremony Time** (`graduation_ceremony_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the graduation ceremony you attended.
- **Graduation Ceremony Location** (`graduation_ceremony_location`, text, optional): Describe the location where the graduation ceremony took place.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any general feedback you have about the ceremony, including what went well and what didn't.
- **Suggestion for improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, text, optional): Share any ideas you have for how we can improve future graduation ceremonies.
- **Suggested for** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): If applicable, please let us know who you would suggest be in charge of future graduation ceremonies (options: Graduation Committee, Event Coordinator, Other).
- **Graduation Ceremony Attendee Feedback** (`graduation_ceremony_attendee_feedback`, text, optional): Share any feedback about the ceremony from your perspective as an attendee.
- **Graduation Ceremony Attendee Suggestion** (`graduation_ceremony_attendee_suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for future graduation ceremonies from the perspective of an attendee, please share them.
- **Graduation Ceremony Additional Comments** (`graduation_ceremony_additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the ceremony that didn't fit in the other fields, please share them here.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- All fields are optional, but filling out as many as possible will help us gather more comprehensive feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
