# summit_attendee_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from attendees of the summit or conference on various aspects of the event. Your input will help organizers understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review the form and ensure you have completed all fields that apply to your experience at the summit.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, or provide a brief text description if a field allows it.
3. Don't hesitate to provide any additional comments or suggestions in the "Additional comments" field.

* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback, as it will help us better understand your experience.
* You can submit the form without selecting any options if you're not comfortable rating or commenting on certain aspects of the event.
* If you have any technical difficulties or questions, feel free to contact us.
