# event_check_in_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience at an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Answer the question "What was your experience like?" in the "event_check_in_experience" field. This field is required and should provide a brief description of your experience.
2.  Select your level of satisfaction with the event by answering the "Satisfaction" question. You can select from "Very satisfied", "Somewhat satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat dissatisfied", and "Very dissatisfied". This field is optional but helps us understand your general feeling about the event.
3.  If you selected "Satisfaction", you will be asked to provide a reason for your satisfaction in the "Reason for satisfaction" field. This field is optional and allows you to provide more detailed feedback about what you liked.
4.  In the "What could we improve?" field, provide any suggestions on how we could improve the event. This field is optional and helps us to identify areas for improvement.
5.  Finally, let us know if you would like to be contacted for follow-up by selecting "Would you like to be contacted?" in the "event_check_in_contact" field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### event_check_in_experience
*   **What was your experience like?** (`event_check_in_experience`, text, required): This field asks you to provide a brief description of your experience at the event. Be as specific as possible to help us understand your experience.

### Satisfaction
*   **Satisfaction** (`Satisfaction`, select_one, optional): This field asks you to select your level of satisfaction with the event. You can select from "Very satisfied", "Somewhat satisfied", "Neutral", "Somewhat dissatisfied", and "Very dissatisfied". Your selection will help us understand your general feeling about the event.

### Reason for satisfaction
*   **Reason for satisfaction** (`event_check_in_satisfaction_reason`, text, optional): If you selected "Satisfaction", you will be asked to provide a reason for your satisfaction in this field. This field helps us to understand what specifically you liked about the event.

### What could we improve?
*   **What could we improve?** (`event_check_in_improvement`, text, optional): This field asks you to provide any suggestions on how we could improve the event. Your feedback will help us identify areas for improvement and make the event better in the future.

### Would you like to be contacted?
*   **Would you like to be contacted?** (`event_check_in_contact`, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you if you would like to be contacted for follow-up. If you select "True", we will contact you for further discussion or to share event updates.
