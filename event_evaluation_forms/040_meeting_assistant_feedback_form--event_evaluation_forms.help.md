# Meeting Assistant Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Assistant Feedback Form is designed to help us gather feedback about meetings. By filling out this form, you'll provide valuable input to improve future meeting planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Select the "Meeting Assistant" page.
2.  On this page, rate your overall experience and provide a brief description of how you felt about the meeting.
3.  Choose one of the three options for the "Meeting Effectiveness" field:
	*   Positive: You had a clear understanding of the meeting's goals and outcomes.
	*   Neutral: You had some understanding, but there's room for improvement.
	*   Negative: You didn't understand the meeting's goals or outcomes.
4.  For "Meeting Outcome," select the outcome that best describes the meeting:
	*   Positive: The meeting met or exceeded your expectations.
	*   Neutral: The meeting was okay, but not outstanding.
	*   Negative: The meeting did not meet your expectations.
5.  In the "Meeting Facilities" field, choose the options that apply to your experience:
	*   Good: The facilities were suitable for the meeting.
	*   Fair: The facilities were somewhat suitable, but not ideal.
	*   Not Available: The facilities were not available or not suitable for the meeting.
6.  If you have any additional suggestions or comments, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
7.  Fill out the "Name," "Email," "Phone," "Date," "Time," "Meeting Duration," "Meeting Date," and "Meeting Location" fields with your relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Meeting Assistant (`meeting_assistant_feedback_form_page`, text, not required):** This field is where you provide a brief description of your experience with the meeting assistant.
*   **Overall Experience (overall_experience, number, not required):** Rate your overall experience with the meeting, on a scale from 1 to 5.
*   **Meeting Effectiveness (meeting_effectiveness, number, not required):** Choose how well you understood the meeting's goals and outcomes.
*   **Meeting Outcome (meeting_outcome, select_one, not required):** Select the meeting outcome that best describes your experience.
*   **Meeting Facilities (meeting_facilities, select_multiple, not required):** Choose the facilities that applied to your experience.
*   **Suggestion (suggestion, text, not required):** Type in any suggestions you have for future meetings.
*   **Additional Comments (additional_comments, text, not required):** Provide any additional comments or feedback about the meeting.
*   **Name (name, text, not required):** Type in your name.
*   **Email (email, email, not required):** Type in your email address.
*   **Phone (phone, text, not required):** Type in your phone number.
*   **Date (date, date, not required):** Type in the date of the meeting.
*   **Time (time, time, not required):** Type in the time of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Duration (meeting_duration, number, not required):** Type in the length of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Date (meeting_date, date, not required):** Type in the date of the meeting.
*   **Meeting Location (meeting_location, text, not required):** Type in the location of the meeting.

## Tips

*   Try to keep your answers brief and to the point.
*   Be honest and provide constructive feedback.
*   If you're unsure about any of the fields, just leave them blank.
