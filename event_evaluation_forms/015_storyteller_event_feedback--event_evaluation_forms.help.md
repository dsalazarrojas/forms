# Storyteller Event Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Storyteller Event Feedback" form is designed to gather feedback from attendees on events organized by our organization. This form helps us evaluate the success and effectiveness of our events, identify areas for improvement, and better serve our attendees in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the relevant feedback option for each question by using the select or select multiple options provided.
2.  Provide your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
3.  Enter the date and time of the event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
4.  Choose the type of event that you attended (Event, Workshop, Seminar, Conference, or Other) in the "Event Type" field.
5.  Indicate whether or not you found the session topic relevant by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Session Topic" field.
6.  Rate the event on a scale (though the "Rating" field is not required, it's optional).
7.  Leave comments about your experience in the "Comments" field (optional).
8.  Enter any additional feedback you'd like to provide in the "Feedback" field (optional).
9.  Enter your contact information (email and phone number) in the "Email" and "Phone" fields (optional).
10.  Finally, submit your feedback by clicking on the "Submit" button. If you want to go back, you can use the "Back" button, and to return to the home page, click on the "Home" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Feedback Form** (Select Multiple):
    This field allows you to select one or more feedback options for the event. Select the relevant options that best describe your experience.
*   **Attendee Name** (Text):
    Please enter your name in this field.
*   **Event Date** (Date):
    Enter the date when the event took place.
*   **Event Time** (Time):
    Enter the time when the event occurred.
*   **Event Type** (Select One):
    Choose the type of event that you attended from the provided options (Event, Workshop, Seminar, Conference, or Other).
*   **Session Topic** (Select Multiple):
    Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not you found the session topic relevant.
*   **Rating** (Number):
    This field is optional, and you can rate the event on a scale if you choose to.
*   **Comments** (Text):
    Provide any comments you'd like to share about your experience.
*   **Feedback** (Text):
    Enter any additional feedback you'd like to provide.
*   **Email** (Email):
    Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (Text):
    Enter your phone number.
*   **Submit** (Text):
    Click this button to submit your feedback.
*   **Back** (Text):
    Click this button to return to a previous page.
*   **Home** (Text):
    Click this button to return to the home page.
