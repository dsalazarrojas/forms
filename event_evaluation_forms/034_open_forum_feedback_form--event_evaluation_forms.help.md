# open_forum_feedback_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Open Forum Feedback Form is used to gather participant feedback on events, speakers, and venues. It allows participants to rate their experience and provide comments on various aspects of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Open Forum Feedback Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name.
2. Rate your overall feedback rating (1-5).
3. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
4. Enter your email address (optional).
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Select the category you want to provide feedback on (event, speaker, venue).
7. Select the date of the event you're providing feedback on.
8. Select the time of the event you're providing feedback on.
9. Select any additional categories you'd like to provide feedback on (event, feedback, evaluation).
10. Rate your participant rating (1-5).
11. Select your level of participant satisfaction (1-3).
12. Enter your rating (optional, can be any value).
13. Provide any additional feedback.
14. Select the name of the event you're providing feedback on (1, 2, or 3).
15. Enter the name of the speaker (optional).
16. Rate your participant rating (1-5).
17. Select your level of participant satisfaction (1-3).
18. Select the category you'd like to provide feedback on (event, feedback, evaluation).
19. Select the name of the event you're providing feedback on (1, 2, or 3).
20. Enter the name of the speaker (optional).
21. Rate your participant rating (1-5).
22. Select any additional categories you'd like to provide feedback on (event, feedback, evaluation).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, select_one, required): Rate your overall feedback rating (1-5).
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Feedback Category** (`feedback_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category you'd like to provide feedback on (event, speaker, venue).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event you're providing feedback on.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of the event you're providing feedback on.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional categories you'd like to provide feedback on (event, feedback, evaluation).
* **Participant Rating** (`participant_rating`, number, optional): Rate your participant rating (1-5).
* **Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Select your level of participant satisfaction (1-3).
* **Rating** (`rating`, text, optional): Enter your rating (if you want to).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, note, optional): Provide any additional feedback.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, select_one, optional): Select the name of the event you're providing feedback on (1, 2, or 3).
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the speaker (if you have one).
* **Participant Rating 2** (`participant_rating_2`, number, optional): Rate your participant rating (1-5).
* **Participant Satisfaction 2** (`participant_satisfaction_2`, select_one, optional): Select your level of participant satisfaction (1-3).
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional categories you'd like to provide feedback on (event, feedback, evaluation).
* **Event Name 2** (`event_name_2`, select_one, optional): Select the name of the event you're providing feedback on (1, 2, or 3).
* **Speaker Name 2** (`speaker_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the speaker (if you have one).
* **Participant Rating 3** (`participant_rating_3`, number, optional): Rate your participant rating (1-5).
* **Category 3** (`category_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional categories you'd like to provide feedback on (event, feedback, evaluation).
