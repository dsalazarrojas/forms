# Healthcare Team Collaboration Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to evaluate the success of a healthcare team collaboration workshop. It is used to gather feedback from participants on various aspects of the workshop, including workshop quality, collaboration effectiveness, overall satisfaction, facilitation skills, team dynamics, and communication. This feedback will help in identifying areas for improvement and refining future workshops.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the relevant options for each question based on your experience and observation.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback, and use clear and concise language.
3. Ensure you are providing honest and objective feedback.
4. Do not leave any fields blank if possible.
5. If you are unsure about any field, please refer back to the workshop's original documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Quality (1)** (number, required: false): Rate the quality of the workshop on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Collaboration Effectiveness (2)** (number, required: false): Rate the collaboration among team members on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Improvement Ideas (3)** (text, required: false): Provide any suggestions or ideas for improving the workshop.
* **Participant Satisfaction (4)** (number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Workshop Duration (5)** (number, required: false): Rate the duration of the workshop, considering whether it was too short or too long.
* **Content Relevance (6)** (number, required: false): Rate the relevance of the workshop content to the team's needs.
* **Overall Satisfaction (7)** (number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop on a scale of 1-5.
* **Facilitation Skills (8)** (number, required: false): Rate the skills of the workshop facilitator.
* **Team Dynamics (9)** (number, required: false): Rate the team dynamics, considering how well team members worked together.
* **Communication (10)** (number, required: false): Rate the communication during the workshop.
* **Feedback Comments (11)** (text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about the workshop.
* **Email (12)** (email, required: false): Enter your email address for future contact.
* **Phone (13)** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for future contact.
* **Department (14)** (text, required: false): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Team Leadership (15)** (number, required: false): Rate the team leadership during the workshop.
* **Workshop Attendance (16)** (number, required: false): Rate the number of attendees at the workshop.
* **Workshop Facilitator (17)** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop facilitator.
* **Date (18)** (date, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Time (19)** (time, required: false): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Location (20)** (text, required: false): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Attendees (21)** (text, required: false): Enter the names of the attendees, if possible.
* **Comments (22)** (text, required: false): Provide any comments about the workshop.
* **Evaluation Date (23)** (date, required: false): Enter the date of evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time (24)** (time, required: false): Enter the time of evaluation.
* **Evaluation By (25)** (select_one, required: false): Select who evaluated the workshop, e.g., team member, facilitator, or participant.
