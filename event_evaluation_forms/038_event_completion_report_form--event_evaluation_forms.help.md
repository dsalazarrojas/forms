# Event Completion Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture essential details and feedback about events, helping event organizers and attendees provide a comprehensive report on event outcomes, feedback, and areas for improvement. It's crucial to ensure that each field is filled out accurately and thoroughly to guarantee a clear and actionable report.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Event Outcomes**: Describe the primary outcome of the event, selecting the most relevant option from the list.
2.  **Event Feedback**: Provide feedback about the event, selecting one or more options that best describe your thoughts.
3.  **Areas for Improvement**: Share specific areas where the event could be improved, writing as much detail as you can about each point.
4.  **Event Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of the event.
5.  **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
6.  **Event Description**: Give a brief description of the event.
7.  **Event Notes**: Add any additional notes about the event.
8.  **Event Outcome Details**: Provide further details about the event outcome.
9.  **Event Feedback Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the event.
10. **Event Evaluation Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the event evaluation form (not required but recommended for tracking).
11.  **Evaluation Date and Time**: Enter the date and time of evaluation.
12.  **Evaluation Status**: Select the status of the evaluation (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
13.  **Evaluation Form Completion Date**: Enter the date of form completion.
14.  **Evaluation Form Completion Time**: Enter the time of form completion.
15.  **Evaluation Form Completion Status 2**: Select the status of the form completion (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
16.  **Event Completion Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the event completion form (not required but recommended for tracking).
17.  **Evaluation Form Last Updated**: Enter the last update date of the form.
18.  **Evaluation Form Version**: Enter the version of the form being used.
