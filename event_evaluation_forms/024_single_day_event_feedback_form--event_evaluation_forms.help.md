# Single Day Event Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback on single-day events held at our organization. The goal is to collect comments, ratings, and other information about each event. Feedback will help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Feedback** section with your thoughts and comments about the event.
2. Select the **Rating** that best describes your overall experience with the event.
3. Choose the **Event Type** that best categorizes the event (e.g., conference, workshop, webinar, etc.).
4. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, type them in the **Additional Comment** field.
5. Enter your **Event Name** if you want to specify which event this feedback is about.
6. If you want to provide more information about yourself, enter your **User**, **User Email**, and **User Phone**.
7. Select the **Event Date** and **Event Time** of the event.
8. Enter your scores for each of the **Score 1** to **Score 7** fields, which will help us understand your experience with different aspects of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Feedback**: Enter a brief description of your experience with the event.
* **Event Details**: Enter any additional information about the event, if necessary.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Score**: Rate your experience with the event from 1 to 5.
* **Event Type**: Select all the types of event that this event was a part of (e.g., conference, workshop, webinar, etc.).
* **Additional Comment**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have about the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event, if desired.
* **User**: Enter a description of yourself.
* **User Email**: Enter your email address, if desired.
* **User Phone**: Enter your phone number, if desired.
* **Rating**: Rate your overall experience with the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Score 1**: Rate your experience with one aspect of the event (e.g., content, organization, etc.).
* **Score 2**: Rate another aspect of the event.
* **Score 3**: Rate another aspect of the event.
* **Score 4**: Rate another aspect of the event.
* **Score 5**: Rate another aspect of the event.
* **Score 6**: Rate another aspect of the event.
* **Score 7**: Rate another aspect of the event.
* **Event Type**: Select all types of events that this event was a part of.
