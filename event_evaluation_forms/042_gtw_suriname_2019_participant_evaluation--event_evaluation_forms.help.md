# GTW Suriname 2019 Participant Evaluation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and opinions from participants about their experience at the GTW Surinine 2019 event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your honest opinions and feedback.
2. Answer each question as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. If you need help with a particular field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_evaluation_form_1** (Text, Optional): Please share your name.
* **participant_evaluation_form_2** (Text, Optional): Describe your role at the event (e.g., speaker, attendee, sponsor).
* **participant_evaluation_form_3** (Text, Optional): What did you find most enjoyable about the event?
* **participant_evaluation_form_4** (Text, Optional): What did you find less enjoyable about the event?
* **participant_evaluation_form_5** (Text, Optional): Do you have any suggestions for future events?
* **participant_evaluation_form_6** (Text, Optional): Would you attend another event like this in the future?
* **participant_evaluation_form_7** (Text, Optional): If yes, which aspects of the event made you want to attend again?
* **participant_evaluation_form_8** (Text, Optional): If no, why not?
* **participant_evaluation_form_9** (Text, Optional): How can we improve the event in your opinion?
* **participant_evaluation_form_10** (Text, Optional): Do you have any other comments or suggestions for us?

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this evaluation form! Your feedback is greatly appreciated.
