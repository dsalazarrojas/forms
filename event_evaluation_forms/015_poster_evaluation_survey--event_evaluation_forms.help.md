# Poster Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poster Evaluation Survey is a tool used to gather feedback from users about a presentation or poster.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the ratings and provide any comments that you think would be useful to improve the presentation. Please fill out all required fields (Rating Scale 1-5, Comments, and Content).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rating scale 1-5** (Rating scale 1-5, select_one): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to express your opinion on the presentation's clarity. The rating scale is designed to measure how clear the presentation is.
* **Comments** (Comments, text): Enter any comments or suggestions you have about the presentation. This will help the presenter improve their communication skills.
* **Poster Clarity** (Poster Clarity, number): If you think the poster is clear, enter a number between 1 (low) and 5 (high). This will help evaluate the overall clarity of the poster.
* **Presentation Clarity** (Presentation Clarity, number): If you think the presentation is clear, enter a number between 1 (low) and 5 (high). This will help evaluate the overall clarity of the presentation.
* **Overall Clarity** (Overall Clarity, number): If you think the overall presentation is clear, enter a number between 1 (low) and 5 (high). This will help evaluate the overall clarity of the presentation.
* **Communication Effectiveness** (Communication Effectiveness, number): If you think the presenter's communication is effective, enter a number between 1 (low) and 5 (high). This will help evaluate how well the presenter communicates their message.
* **Content** (Content, text): Enter any additional thoughts or comments about the presentation's content. This can help the presenter improve their content and delivery. 

**Tips**
* Please be honest and objective when answering the questions.
* Use numbers between 1 and 5 for rating fields.
* You can enter any additional comments or thoughts in the Content field.
* Please fill out all required fields.
