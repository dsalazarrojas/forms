# Hospital Ethics Committee Workshop Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluation from attendees after a hospital ethics committee workshop. It aims to assess the quality and effectiveness of the workshop, including its purpose, organization, and overall impression.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Workshop Name** field with the actual name of the workshop attended.
2. Enter the **Event Date** to specify the date of the workshop.
3. Provide your evaluation scores for each of the **Evaluation Scales** (1-5), which assess the quality of the workshop in different areas.
4. Enter your **Overall Score** (1-5) to summarize your evaluation of the workshop.
5. Share any **Comments** or **Additional Comments** about the workshop.
6. Fill in your **Submitter** information, including your **Name**, **Department**, **Contact**, **Email**, and **Job Title**, to ensure accountability and follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the workshop attended.
* **Event Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Time of Event** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the start and end time of the workshop (if applicable).
* **Venue** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the venue or location of the workshop (if not already specified).
* **Objective** (`purpose`, text, required): Enter the workshop's objective or purpose.
* **Evaluation Scale 1** (`evaluation_scale_1`, number, optional): Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5 (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Scale 2** (`evaluation_scale_2`, number, optional): Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5 (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Scale 3** (`evaluation_scale_3`, number, optional): Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5 (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Scale 4** (`evaluation_scale_4`, number, required): Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5.
* **Evaluation Scale 5** (`evaluation_scale_5`, number, required): Rate the workshop on a scale of 1-5.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, required): Enter your overall score for the workshop (1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any comments or feedback about the workshop.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`, text, required): Enter your department or affiliation.
* **Submitter Contact** (`submitter_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information (phone number or email).
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your institutional email.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`submitter_jobtitle`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, optional): Enter your institutional affiliation.
* **Institution** (`submitter_institution`, text, optional): Enter your institution's name.
* **Faculty** (`submitter_faculty`, text, optional): Enter your faculty or academic affiliation.
* **Department Name** (`submitter_department_name`, text, optional): Enter your department's name.
* **Room Number** (`submitter_room_number`, text, optional): Enter the room number where the workshop was held.
* **Floor Number** (`submitter_floor_number`, text, optional): Enter the floor number where the workshop was held.
