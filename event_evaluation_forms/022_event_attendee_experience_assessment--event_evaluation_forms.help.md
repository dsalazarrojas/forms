# Event Attendee Experience Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Attendee Experience Assessment form is used to gather feedback from event attendees after the event has taken place. This feedback is essential for event organizers to evaluate the event's success and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Rate your overall experience of the event in the "Rating" field.
4. Describe your experience of the event in the "Event Experience" field.
5. Share any improvement ideas you may have for future events in the "Improvement Ideas" field.
6. If you have any additional comments, please use the "Comments" field.
7. Select the correct team lead who was associated with the event from the "Team Lead" dropdown menu.
8. Choose any applicable feedback for the event in the "Attendee Feedback" dropdown menu.
9. Select the tool that was used to support the event from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
10. Enter the name of the person who created the form in the "Created By" field.
11. If this is your first time filling out the form, enter the current date in the "Created At" field. If you've filled out the form before, you will not need to enter this field.
12. Finally, ensure that the form has been properly submitted by entering the current date in the "Updated At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name**: This is the field to fill in your name as an attendee of the event.
* **Email**: This is the field to enter your email address so that we can contact you for any further information.
* **Rating**: This is the field to rate your overall experience of the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Event Experience**: This is the field to describe your experience of the event in as much detail as possible.
* **Improvement Ideas**: This is the field to share any suggestions you may have for improving future events.
* **Comments**: This is the field to enter any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Team Lead**: This is the field to select the team lead who was associated with the event.
* **Attendee Feedback**: This is the field to select any feedback that you experienced during the event.
* **Assigned Tool**: This is the field to select the tool that was used to support the event.
* **Created By**: This is the field to enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created At**: This is the field to enter the date and time when the form was created.
* **Updated At**: This is the field to ensure that the form has been properly updated and submitted.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out the form correctly and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your team lead or the event organizer for clarification.
* Please submit the form only after attending the event and having a genuine experience.
* The form will only be available to fill out after the event has concluded.
