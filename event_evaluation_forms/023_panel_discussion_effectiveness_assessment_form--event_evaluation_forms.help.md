# Panel Discussion Effectiveness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panel Discussion Effectiveness Assessment Form is a tool used by event organizers and attendees to evaluate the performance of speakers and provide feedback on the overall event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Read through the form carefully and answer all the questions honestly.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields.
3. Provide clear and concise feedback in the text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, optional): This field is for any general comments or notes about the event. You can type any information you feel is relevant to the effectiveness of the panel discussion.
* **Speaker Presentation** (`2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Rate the speaker's presentation by selecting all the options that apply. You can choose multiple options if the speaker effectively presented multiple points.
* **Speaker Clarification** (`3`, `number`, optional): Evaluate the speaker's ability to clearly explain and answer questions. Rate the speaker's clarification on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Audience Questions** (`4`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the quality of questions asked by the audience. Choose the option that best describes the level of engagement and interest from the audience.
* **Content** (`5`, `number`, optional): Evaluate the relevance and interest of the content presented by the speaker. Rate the content on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "not engaging" and 5 is "highly engaging".
* **Follow-up** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Rate the speaker's ability to address questions and follow up on topics discussed. Choose the option that best describes the speaker's follow-up.
* **Overall Effectiveness** (`7`, `number`, optional): Rate the overall effectiveness of the speaker's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "poor" and 5 is "excellent".
* **Recommendations** (`8`, `note`, optional): Provide any suggestions or recommendations for improvement for future events or speaker performances.
* **Comments** (`9`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you have about the speaker or event.
* **Assigned Tool** (`10`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide the name of the tool or resource that was assigned to the speaker or audience.
* **Event Evaluation Form Comments** (`11`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the event or form itself.
