# Cultural Event Audience Satisfaction Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from cultural event attendees about their experience, satisfaction, and suggestions for improvement. Your input will help event organizers understand what worked well and what didn't, allowing them to make necessary adjustments for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question as accurately and honestly as possible.
2. Select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the prompt.
3. Provide detailed comments and suggestions in the text fields, if applicable.
4. Make sure to submit your responses before leaving the event, so your feedback can be considered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: false): This is a note field where you can take a moment to read and understand the event's objective and the purpose of this form. Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the questions and options.
* **How do you feel about the cultural event?** (`evaluation`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event, selecting all options that apply from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What was the quality of the program?** (`program`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the option that best describes your experience with the program. You can select Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What was the level of entertainment?** (`entertainment`, `select_one`, required: false): Evaluate the entertainment aspect of the event, choosing from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, `select_one`, required: false): Rate your overall experience at the event, selecting Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Event Satisfaction** (`event_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate your level of satisfaction with the event, choosing from Very Good, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **What do you recommend to improve the cultural event?** (`recommendations`, `text`, required: false): Share your suggestions on how the event can be improved. Be as specific as possible, and provide any details that can help organizers enhance future events.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional thoughts, ideas, or comments about the event.
