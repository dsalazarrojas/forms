# Tournament Media Coverage Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the media coverage of a tournament, allowing users to provide their opinions on the quality, reach, and overall score of the coverage, as well as suggest areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Media Coverage Quality**: In this section, please provide an honest evaluation of the media coverage's quality. Rate your experience from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

2. **Audience Reach**: Estimate the number of people you think the media coverage reached.

3. **Coverage Scope**: Indicate which of the following apply to the media coverage: 
* **Yes** (if you think the coverage was comprehensive and covered all necessary aspects)
* **No** (if you think the coverage was lacking or incomplete)

4. **Improvement Ideas**: If you have any suggestions or ideas for improvement, please share them here.

5. **Overall Score**: Rate the media coverage's overall score from 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

6. **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or thoughts on the media coverage, please share them here. 

7. **Email Address**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, provide your email address.

8. **Phone Number**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up questions or discussions, provide your phone number.

9. **Event Name**: Please provide the name of the event you are evaluating.

10. **Media Type**: Indicate the type of media coverage you received, such as print, digital, or social media.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Media Coverage Quality** (`media_coverage_quality`, text, required: false): A rating system to evaluate the quality of the media coverage.
* **Audience Reach** (`audience_reach`, number, required: false): An estimate of the number of people the media coverage reached.
* **Coverage Scope** (`coverage_scope`, select_multiple, required: false): Indicate which of the following apply to the media coverage.
* **Improvement Ideas** (`improvement_ideas`, text, required: false): A place to share suggestions or ideas for improvement.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, number, required: false): A rating system to evaluate the overall media coverage score.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): A place to share additional comments or thoughts.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required: false): A field to provide an email address for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): A field to provide a phone number for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): The name of the event being evaluated.
* **Media Type** (`media_type`, select_one, required: false): The type of media coverage received.
