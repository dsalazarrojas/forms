# Feedback On Recent Engagement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from participants on a recent event or engagement. It allows you to provide a subjective assessment of the event's quality, suggest improvements, and offer comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Name** field with the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
2. Enter the **Event Start Date** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
3. Select the rating of your experience by choosing a number from 1-5.
4. Write a brief **Participant Opinion** of your experience.
5. Provide a **Participant Suggestion** for how to improve the event.
6. Enter any additional **Participant Comment** about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the date when the event started.
* **Rating**: Rate your experience from 1 (lowest) to 5 (highest).
* **Participant Opinion**: Give a brief description of your experience.
* **Participant Suggestion**: Suggest ways to improve the event.
* **Participant Comment**: Enter any additional comments about the event.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
* Use the rating system to give a clear indication of your experience.
* Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time and input.
