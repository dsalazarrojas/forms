# Information Session Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Information Session Feedback Form is designed to gather feedback on the overall quality of the information session, including the speaker's performance, content relevance, and overall experience. This form helps us understand what we can do better and improve future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information, including your email address and phone number.
2. Choose the speaker for your session.
3. Rate the speaker's feedback on content relevance, presentation skills, communication skills, time management, and overall experience.
4. Provide any additional suggestions or comments about the session.
5. Choose any facilitators who were present during the session.
6. Select the category of the session (Event, Training, or Workshop).
