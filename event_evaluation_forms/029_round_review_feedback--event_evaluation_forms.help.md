# Round Review Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from event attendees to help event organizers improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on in the `event_name` field.
2. Enter your name in the `participant_name` field.
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5 in the `overall_satisfaction` field.
4. Provide your rating for the event in the `rating` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are providing feedback on.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name to associate your feedback with your identity (optional but recommended for tracking purposes).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the event on a scale of 1-5.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, optional): Provide your rating for the event. This field is not clearly defined, so it's assumed to be a separate rating score for the event, potentially on a similar scale to `overall_satisfaction`. 

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback to help event organizers improve their events.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, feel free to include them in the space provided.
