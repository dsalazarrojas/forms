# Cultural Exhibitions Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and ratings from users after visiting a cultural exhibition. It helps us understand their overall experience and identify areas of improvement for future exhibitions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the exhibition name in the "Exhibition Name" field.
2. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date Visit" field.
3. Rate your experience from 1 to 5 stars in the "Rating" field.
4. Share any feedback or comments about your experience in the "Feedback" field.
5. Provide any suggestions for improvement in the "Suggestions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibition Name (Exhibition Name)**: Enter the name of the cultural exhibition you visited.
* **Date Visit (Date Visit)**: Enter the date you visited the exhibition in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Rating (Rating)**: Rate your experience from 1 to 5 stars, where 1 is poor and 5 is excellent.
* **Feedback (Feedback)**: Share any comments or thoughts about your experience. 
* **Suggestions (Suggestions)**: Provide any ideas or suggestions for improving future exhibitions.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback and suggestions.
* If you have any concerns or issues, feel free to share them with us. 
* Your input helps us make cultural exhibitions more enjoyable and informative for everyone.
