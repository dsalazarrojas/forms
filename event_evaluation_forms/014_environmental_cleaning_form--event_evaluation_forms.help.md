# Environmental Cleaning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and record environmental cleaning events. It captures relevant information about the event, including event details, personnel involved, and tasks completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the introduction page with a brief description of the event.
2. Enter the event details, including start and end times.
3. Provide information about the crew and their work hours.
4. Add any supervisor comments or notes.
5. Indicate whether the cleaning work was completed.
6. Enter any relevant information about the clean area.
7. Add any additional notes.
8. Finally, enter information about the supervisor, including their contact details and any notes they may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Introduction
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `text`, not required): Briefly describe the event.

### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, not required): Enter any details about the event.

### Start Time
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the start time of the event.

### End Time
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the end time of the event.

### Crew Size
* **Crew Size** (`crew_size`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of people in the crew.

### Hours Worked
* **Hours Worked** (`crew_hours_worked`, `number`, not required): Enter the total hours worked by the crew.

### Supervisor Comments
* **Supervisor Comments** (`supervisor_comments`, `text`, not required): Enter any comments from the supervisor.

### Work Completed
* **Work Completed** (`work_completed`, `select_one`, not required): Indicate whether the cleaning work was completed.

### Clean Area
* **Clean Area** (`clean_area`, `text`, not required): Enter any relevant information about the clean area.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, not required): Add any additional notes about the event.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the person in charge of the event.

### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the company involved.

### Supervisor Name
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the supervisor.

### Supervisor Title
* **Supervisor Title** (`supervisor_title`, `text`, not required): Enter the title of the supervisor.

### Supervisor Email
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, `email`, not required): Enter the email of the supervisor.

### Supervisor Phone
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter the phone number of the supervisor.

### Supervisor Address
* **Supervisor Address** (`supervisor_address`, `text`, not required): Enter the address of the supervisor.

### Supervisor Note
* **Supervisor Note** (`supervisor_note`, `text`, not required): Enter any notes from the supervisor.

### Clean Date
* **Clean Date** (`clean_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the clean event.

### Clean Time
* **Clean Time** (`clean_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time of the clean event.

### Work Start Date
* **Work Start Date** (`work_start_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the start date of the work event.

### Work End Date
* **Work End Date** (`work_end_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the end date of the work event.

### Work Date
* **Work Date** (`work_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the work event.

### Notes 2
* **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, `text`, not required): Add any additional notes about the event.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill out as much information as possible.
* Enter accurate information for the supervisor fields.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Save the form regularly to avoid losing your progress.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or the person who assigned you the task.
