# workshop_participant_outcomes_evaluation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback and outcomes from workshop participants to improve future workshop planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Workshop Participant Outcomes Evaluation Form**: This page is the main form where you will provide your feedback and ratings.
2. **Participant Outcome Rating**: Select one option from the dropdown menu to rate the participant's outcome from the workshop.
3. **Workshop Effectiveness Rating**: Select multiple options from the dropdown menu to rate the effectiveness of the workshop.
4. **Suggestions for Improvement**: Type any suggestions you have for improving future workshops.
5. **Participant Feedback**: Enter your overall feedback about the workshop.
6. **Email**: Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for follow-up communication).
7. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for follow-up communication).
8. **Assigned Tool**: Select one option from the dropdown menu to assign a tool or resource to the participant.
9. **Output File**: Enter any relevant file or link related to the workshop.
10. **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID number for the form (optional but recommended for tracking).
11. **Category**: Enter a category or label for the form (optional but recommended for categorization).
12. **Description**: Enter a brief description of the form (optional but recommended for context).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Participant Outcomes Evaluation Form**: This is the main form page where you will provide your feedback and ratings.
* **Participant Outcome Rating**: Select one option from the dropdown menu to rate your outcome from the workshop. For example, you might rate how well you achieved your goals or objectives from the workshop.
* **Workshop Effectiveness Rating**: Select multiple options from the dropdown menu to rate the effectiveness of the workshop. For example, you might rate how engaging or informative the workshop was.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Type any suggestions you have for improving future workshops. This is a free-form text field where you can provide any feedback or ideas you think would be helpful.
* **Participant Feedback**: Enter your overall feedback about the workshop. This could include your thoughts on the content, the instructor, or any other aspect of the workshop.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional, but recommended for follow-up communication). This will help us contact you if we need more information or have questions about your feedback.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for follow-up communication). This will help us contact you if we need to discuss your feedback.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select one option from the dropdown menu to assign a tool or resource to the participant. This might be a tool or resource that was used during the workshop.
* **Output File**: Enter any relevant file or link related to the workshop. This could be a document, a video, or any other file that you think would be helpful.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID number for the form (optional but recommended for tracking). This will help us identify your feedback and connect it to the workshop.
* **Category**: Enter a category or label for the form (optional but recommended for categorization). This will help us group your feedback with other similar feedback.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the form (optional but recommended for context). This will provide context for your feedback and help us understand your comments.

## Tips

* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback, as it will help us improve future workshops.
* If you have any questions or need more information, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for taking the time to provide your feedback!
