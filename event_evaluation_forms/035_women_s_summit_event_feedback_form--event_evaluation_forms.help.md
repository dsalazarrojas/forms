# Women's Summit Event Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Summit Event Feedback Form is designed to collect feedback from participants on their experience at the summit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your First Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Company name.
4. Enter your Department name.
5. Enter your Job Title.
6. Select which session you attended (you can select multiple sessions).
7. Provide feedback on the session(s) you attended.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (on a scale of 1 to 5).
9. Indicate whether you would recommend the event.
10. If you have attended previous summits, select yes or no.
11. If you have attended previous summits, enter the year(s) you attended.
12. Add any additional comments.
13. Provide suggestions for the next summit.
14. Optional: Add any comments.
15. Optional: Add any further suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Email**: Enter your participant's email address.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Session Attended**: Select which session(s) you attended (you can select multiple sessions).
* **Session Feedback**: Provide feedback on the session(s) you attended.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the event (on a scale of 1 to 5).
* **Rating**: Rate your experience with the event.
* **Recommend**: Indicate whether you would recommend the event.
* **Attended Previous Summit**: Select yes or no if you have attended a previous summit.
* **Previous Summit Years**: If you have attended previous summits, enter the year(s) you attended.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments.
* **Suggestions**: Provide suggestions for the next summit.
* **Overall Satisfaction Scale**: Rate your overall satisfaction on a scale of 1 to 5. 
* **Session Attendance Scale**: If you attended multiple sessions, indicate the number of sessions you attended.
* **Comments** (optional): Add any further comments.
* **Suggestions** (optional): Add any further suggestions.
