# Orange Challenge Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback and insights from event participants to understand their experience, satisfaction, and preferences for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them honestly.
2. For questions with select_one type, choose the response that best fits your experience or opinion.
3. For questions with text type, provide a brief and clear answer in a few sentences.
4. You can skip questions that don't apply to you or your experience.
5. Make sure to provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How did you hear about the Orange Challenge event?** (`event_evaluation_22`, `select_one`): This question helps us understand how you learned about the event. Please select the option that best describes how you found out about the event.
* **How long did you participate in the event?** (`event_evaluation_2`, `number`): This question is about the duration of your participation in the event.
* **How would you rate your overall satisfaction with the event?** (`event_evaluation_3`, `select_one`): Rate your satisfaction with the event on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **What did you find most valuable about the event?** (`event_evaluation_4`, `text`): Share what you valued most about the event.
* **Was this event a good use of your time?** (`event_evaluation_5`, `select_one`): Answer yes or no on whether you found the event time-well spent.
* **Do you have any suggestions or comments about the event or future events?** (`participant_suggestions`, `note`): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or comments you have about the event or future events.
* **How likely are you to recommend this event to a friend or colleague?** (`event_evaluation_7`, `select_one`): Rate your likelihood of recommending the event to others.
* **How valuable was the event to you?** (`event_value`, `select_one`): Rate the event's value to you on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How much impact did the event have on you?** (`event_impact`, `select_one`): Rate the event's impact on you on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **How often would you like to participate in similar events in the future?** (`event_frequency`, `select_one`): Choose how often you'd like to attend similar events.
* **What is your age?** (`participant_demographics`, `number`): Please provide your age.
* **Do you work or have worked in the wellness industry?** (`event_evaluation_20`, `select_one`): Answer yes or no on whether you're involved in the wellness industry.
* **Do you know someone who participated in this event?** (`participant_relationship`, `select_one`): Answer yes or no on whether you know someone who attended the event.
* **How did you hear about the Orange Challenge event?** (`event_evaluation_22`, `select_one`): Repeat the question from the beginning, as it's an important one.
* **Do you have any suggestions for this event or future events?** (`participant_suggestions_1`, `text`): Share any thoughts, suggestions, or comments you have.
* **Would you like to receive updates and information about future events from the Orange Challenge?** (`event_evaluation_24`, `select_one`): Answer yes or no on whether you'd like to stay updated.
* **Would you like to participate in future Orange Challenge events?** (`event_evaluation_25`, `select_one`): Answer yes or no on whether you'd like to attend future events.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions for future events?** (`participant_suggestions_2`, `text`): Share any final thoughts or suggestions you may have.
