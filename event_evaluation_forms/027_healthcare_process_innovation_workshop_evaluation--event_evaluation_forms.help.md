# Healthcare Process Innovation Workshop Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and evaluations from participants of a Healthcare Process Innovation Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have completed the workshop and have a good understanding of the content covered during the event.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing specific examples or details when possible.
3. Please be honest and thorough in your responses, as your feedback is invaluable to us.
4. If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the text fields provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Evaluation** (`workshop_evaluation`, `text`, required): This is the main evaluation section where you can provide a general overview of your experience with the workshop.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, `number`, required): Please enter the unique ID of the event you participated in.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, not required): If you have a specific date in mind, please enter it. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, not required): If you have a specific time in mind, please enter it. Otherwise, you can leave it blank.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_multiple`, not required): Please indicate if you attended the workshop as an individual or as part of a team. If you attended with a team, please list the members of your team.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `select_one`, required): Please rate your overall experience with the workshop on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, not required): If you have any specific feedback or comments about the workshop, please add them here.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, not required): If you have any suggestions for future workshops, please add them here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, not required): If you have any general comments about the workshop, please add them here.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Team** (`team`, `text`, not required): If you attended the workshop as part of a team, please enter the names of your team members here.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your department.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, `text`, required): Please enter your job title or position.
* **Workshop Rating** (`workshop_rating`, `select_one`, not required): Please rate your experience with the workshop on a scale of Good or Fair.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, not required): Please rate your overall satisfaction with the workshop on a scale of Satisfied or Dissatisfied.
* **Quality of Presenters** (`quality_of_presenters`, `select_one`, not required): Please rate the quality of the presenters on a scale of Good or Poor.
* **Quality of Content** (`quality_of_content`, `select_one`, not required): Please rate the quality of the content on a scale of Good or Poor.
* **Quality of Materials** (`quality_of_materials`, `select_one`, not required): Please rate the quality of the materials provided on a scale of Good or Poor.
* **Relevance to Topic** (`relevance_to_topic`, `select_one`, not required): Please indicate if the workshop was relevant to the topic on a scale of Yes or No.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, `select_one`, not required): Please indicate if the workshop's time management was effective on a scale of Yes or No.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `select_one`, not required): Please indicate if the workshop's organization was effective on a scale of Yes or No.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, `select_one`, not required): Please indicate if you would recommend this workshop on a scale of Yes or No.
* **Submission** (`submission`, `select_one`, not required): Please indicate if you would submit your work or ideas for a future workshop on a scale of Yes or No.

## Tips

* Please be as thorough and honest as possible in your responses.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your time and feedback, and we'll use it to improve our workshops in the future.
