# teacher_appreciation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to express appreciation and evaluation of a teacher, allowing students to provide feedback and recognition for the teacher's work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the teacher's **name** in the "Teacher Name" field.
2. Enter the teacher's **title** in the "Teaching Title" field.
3. Select the **date** of the appreciation using the date picker.
4. Enter the **time** of the appreciation using the time picker.
5. Enter the **reason for appreciation** in the text field.
6. Enter your **message to teacher** in the text field.
7. Enter your **message to student** in the text field.
8. If desired, provide an optional **Teacher Evaluation**, **Student Evaluation**, and **School Evaluation** by entering a number.
9. Finally, indicate the **Appreciation Amount** and choose the **Appreciation Method** for the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name who deserves appreciation.
* **Teaching Title** (`teacher_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the teacher's position or role.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the appreciation in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time of the appreciation in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Reason for Appreciation** (`reason_for_appreciation`, text, required): Explain the reason why the teacher deserves appreciation.
* **Message to Teacher** (`message_to_teacher`, text, required): Write a message or note to the teacher expressing your appreciation.
* **Message to Student** (`message_to_student`, text, required): Write a message or note to the student who is appreciating the teacher.
* **Teacher Evaluation** (`teacher_evaluation`, number, optional): Provide a numerical evaluation or score for the teacher, if desired.
* **Student Evaluation** (`student_evaluation`, number, optional): Provide a numerical evaluation or score for the student, if desired.
* **School Evaluation** (`school_evaluation`, number, optional): Provide a numerical evaluation or score for the school, if desired.
* **Appreciation Amount** (`appreciation_amount`, number, required): Indicate the amount of appreciation you want to express.
* **Appreciation Method** (`appreciation_method`, text, required): Choose the method of appreciation, such as a card, gift, or words of encouragement.
