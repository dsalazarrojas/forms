# Corporate Volunteer Program Feedback Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from participants who have engaged with the corporate volunteer program. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us to refine the program for future sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Be as specific as possible when providing feedback.
3. You may select multiple answers for some questions if applicable.
4. Please submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Corporate Volunteer Program Feedback Evaluation** (page 1):
	+ This section is a free-text area where you can provide general feedback on your experience with the corporate volunteer program.
* **Program Evaluation Form** (page 2):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you believe the corporate volunteer program met your expectations and was effective.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not believe the corporate volunteer program met your expectations.
* **Volunteer Evaluation Form** (page 3):
	+ Select all that apply if the volunteers you interacted with were helpful, friendly, and made you feel valued.
	+ You may select multiple answers for this question, as the volunteers may have made a positive impact in different ways.
