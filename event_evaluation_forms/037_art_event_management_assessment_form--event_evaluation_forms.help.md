# Art Event Management Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the management and execution of art events, providing an opportunity for event organizers to reflect on their performance and identify areas for improvement. The information collected will help inform future event planning and decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the best option that describes your experience with the event.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields (e.g., challenges encountered, suggestions for improvement).
4. Ensure you answer all questions in the context of the event you are evaluating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Information
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the event being assessed.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, *): Enter the date of the event.
* **Organizer or Event Manager Name** (`organizer_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the person responsible for organizing or managing the event.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, *): Enter the date when this assessment is being completed.

### Logistics Assessment
* **Venue Suitability for Event** (`venue_suitability`, select_one, *): Rate the suitability of the venue for the event.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Space Adequacy for Attendees** (`space_adequacy`, select_one, *): Rate the space adequacy for attendees.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Parking and Accessibility** (`parking_accessibility`, select_one, *): Rate the parking and accessibility for the event.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Planning and Organization
* **Timeline Adherence** (`timeline_adherence`, select_one, *): Rate the event timeline adherence.
	+ On schedule
	+ Mostly on schedule
	+ Some delays
	+ Significant delays
* **Budget Management** (`budget_management`, select_one, *): Rate the budget management for the event.
	+ Well managed
	+ Adequately managed
	+ Somewhat over budget
	+ Significantly over budget
* **Effectiveness of Communication with Participants** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, *): Rate the effectiveness of communication with participants.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Event Execution
* **Quality of Program or Activities** (`program_quality`, select_one, *): Rate the quality of the program or activities.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Artist or Presenter Engagement** (`artist_engagement`, select_one, *): Rate the artist or presenter engagement.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Audience Engagement Level** (`audience_engagement`, select_one, *): Rate the audience engagement level.
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

### Outcomes and Impact
* **Actual Attendance** (`attendance`, number, *): Enter the actual attendance number.
* **Attendance Compared to Goal** (`attendance_vs_goal`, select_one, *): Compare the actual attendance to the goal.
	+ Exceeded expectations
	+ Met expectations
	+ Below expectations
	+ Significantly below
* **Overall Participant Satisfaction** (`participant_satisfaction`, select_one, *): Rate the overall participant satisfaction.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied

### Areas for Improvement
* **Challenges Encountered** (`challenges_encountered`, text, *): Provide details about the challenges encountered during the event.
* **Suggestions for Future Events** (`improvement_suggestions`, text, *): Suggest improvements for future events.

### Overall Assessment
* **Overall Event Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, *): Rate the overall event.
	+ Excellent
	+ Very good
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when answering questions, providing examples or details when necessary.
* Ensure accuracy and honesty when assessing the event performance.
* Complete all required fields to ensure a comprehensive assessment.
* Use the suggestions and feedback to improve future event planning and execution.
