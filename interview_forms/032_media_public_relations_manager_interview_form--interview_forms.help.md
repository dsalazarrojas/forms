# Media Public Relations Manager Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Public Relations Manager Interview Form is designed to gather information from potential candidates about their experience, skills, and preferences as a Public Relations Manager. This form is used in the hiring process to determine the candidate's fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Candidate Name" field.
4. If applicable, provide a brief written interview answer in the "Written Interview" field.
5. Indicate your experience as a Public Relations Manager with the "PR Manager Skills" field.
6. Choose whether you have experience as a Public Relations Manager with the "PR Manager Experience" field.
7. List any relevant achievements in the "PR Manager Achievements" field.
8. Choose whether you would like to be contacted by this company with the "Why This Company" field.
9. Indicate whether you can provide professional references with the "PR Manager References" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Email (`email`, email, optional)
This field is for your email address. Enter the email address that is currently in use for contact purposes.

### 2. Phone (`phone`, text, optional)
This field is for your phone number. Enter your current phone number so we can contact you.

### 3. Candidate Name (`candidate_name`, text, optional)
This field is for your name. Enter your full name as it should be displayed on any official documentation.

### 4. Written Interview (`written_interview`, note, optional)
This field is for any additional information you would like to provide in written format. It's an opportunity to elaborate on your answers or provide context.

### 5. PR Manager Skills (`pr_manager_skills`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose from the following options whether you have skills in:
	* Yes
	* No

### 6. PR Manager Experience (`pr_manager_experience`, select_one, optional)
Choose from the following options:
	* Yes
	* No

### 7. PR Manager Achievements (`pr_manager_achievements`, text, optional)
List any relevant achievements or accomplishments in your career as a Public Relations Manager.

### 8. Why This Company (`why_this_company`, select_one, optional)
Choose from the following options:
	* Yes
	* No

### 9. PR Manager References (`pr_manager_references`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose from the following options whether you can provide references:
	* Yes
	* No

### 10. (no label, id: 10, text, optional)
No label is provided for this field, which could indicate that it's not intended to be filled or used. Please fill in the information as needed.
