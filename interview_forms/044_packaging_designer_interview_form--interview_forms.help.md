# Packaging Designer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your background, experience, and expectations as a packaging designer. Please fill out this form to the best of your ability to help us get to know you better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details such as your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Provide your phone number and work experience.
3. Share your educational background.
4. If you have a portfolio or a link to your LinkedIn profile, please provide it.
5. Add any relevant attachments like resume or other documents that you think would be helpful for us to get to know you.
6. Indicate your availability and preferred work location.
7. Specify your expected salary range and work schedule.
8. Let us know your preferred work type and your current job status.
