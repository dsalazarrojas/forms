# Automotive Interview Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automotive Interview Guide is a comprehensive evaluation tool used by automotive employers and hiring managers to assess a candidate's qualifications, experience, and skills for a position in the automotive industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the candidate's contact information, including their name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the interview details, including the date and time of the interview, the type of interview (e.g. phone screen, in-person, video interview), and the interviewer's name.
3. Assess the candidate's background and experience, including their years of industry experience, previous automotive roles, and relevant certifications.
4. Evaluate the candidate's technical skills, such as their mechanical knowledge, diagnostic skills, and familiarity with automotive tools and equipment.
5. Assess the candidate's soft skills, including their communication skills, problem-solving ability, and teamwork and collaboration skills.
6. Summarize your overall impression and make a hiring recommendation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the candidate's name.
* **Email Address** (`candidate_email`, email, required): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`candidate_phone`, text, required): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, text, required): Enter the position the candidate is applying for.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time** (`interview_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the interview.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person conducting the interview.
* **Type of Interview** (`interview_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of interview (e.g. phone screen, in-person, video interview).
* **Years of Industry Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the candidate's years of experience in the automotive industry.
* **Previous Automotive Roles** (`previous_roles`, text, required): List the candidate's previous automotive roles or positions held.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant certifications the candidate holds.
* **Mechanical Knowledge Rating** (`mechanical_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's mechanical knowledge as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Needs Development.
* **Diagnostic Skills Rating** (`diagnostic_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's diagnostic skills as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Needs Development.
* **Knowledge of Automotive Tools and Equipment** (`tools_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's knowledge of automotive tools and equipment as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Limited.
* **Communication Skills Rating** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's communication skills as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Needs Development.
* **Problem-Solving Ability Rating** (`problem_solving`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's problem-solving ability as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Needs Development.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration Rating** (`teamwork`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's teamwork and collaboration skills as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, or Needs Development.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, text, required): Summarize your overall impression of the candidate.
* **Hiring Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_one, required): Select a hiring recommendation, such as Highly Recommend, Recommend, Consider with Caution, or Do Not Recommend.
