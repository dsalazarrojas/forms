# Home Buyer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Buyer Interview Form is designed to gather information from potential home buyers about their preferences, needs, and expectations when searching for a new home. This form is used to ensure a smooth and stress-free experience for the buyer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the options that best match your preferences and needs.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date contact information for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What's your current budget?** (`what_is_your_current_budget`, number, required: false)
	+ Please enter your current budget, including any monthly income or expenses that may impact your ability to afford a home.
* **What location are you interested in?** (`what_location_are_you_preferred`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Choose the location(s) you are interested in or willing to consider.
* **What type of property are you looking for?** (`what_type_of_property`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the type(s) of property that interests you, such as single-family home, condo, or townhouse.
* **What details about the property are you looking for?** (`what_details_about_property`, note, required: false)
	+ Provide any specific details about the property you are looking for, such as size, amenities, or condition.
* **When do you want to move in?** (`when_do_you_want_to_move_in`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate when you expect to move into your new home.
* **How do you want to be contacted?** (`how_do_you_want_to_be_contacted`, text, required: false)
	+ Provide your preferred method of contact, such as email, phone number, or address.
* **What is your budget breakdown?** (`what_is_your_budget`, number, required: false)
	+ Enter your budget breakdown, including any financial constraints or priorities.
* **What questions do you have for the agent?** (`what_do_you_want_to_ask_the_agent`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Indicate any questions or concerns you have for the real estate agent.
* **What features are you looking for in a property?** (`what_features_are_you_looking_for`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select the features you are interested in or expect to find in a property, such as backyard, pool, or parking.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to ensure the agent can provide the best possible guidance and support.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask the agent for clarification or advice.
* This form is a collaborative and conversational tool, so don't hesitate to provide additional information or clarification as needed.
