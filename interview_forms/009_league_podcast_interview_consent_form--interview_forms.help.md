# League Podcast Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The League Podcast Interview Consent Form is a crucial document that ensures the comfort and consent of the individuals being interviewed. It collects essential information about the individual, their role, and their preferences regarding recording and publication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and email address in the designated fields.
2. Choose your role in the interview (e.g. guest, host, etc.).
3. Indicate your preference for recording and publication by selecting the relevant options.
4. Provide any additional notes or details about the interview.
5. Confirm your email address for verification purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name (id: 2)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for verification and other communication purposes.
* **Role (id: 4)**: Select the role you will be playing in the interview (e.g. guest, host, etc.).
* **Consent (id: 5)**: Please indicate if you are willing to be recorded and published.
* **Recording (id: 6)**: Specify your preference for recording (e.g. audio only, video and audio, etc.).
* **Published (id: 7)**: Choose how you wish to be published (e.g. with permission, without permission, etc.).
* **Recording Date (id: 8)**: If applicable, enter the date you were recorded.
* **Recording Time (id: 9)**: If applicable, enter the time the recording took place.
* **Published Date (id: 10)**: If applicable, enter the date you were published.
* **Published Time (id: 11)**: If applicable, enter the time you were published.
* **Note (id: 12)**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the interview.
* **Email Confirmation (id: 13)**: Please confirm that the email address you provided is accurate.
* **Phone (id: 14)**: Optional phone number for contact purposes.
* **Recording Notes (id: 15)**: Additional notes or details about the recording.
* **Published Notes (id: 16)**: Any notes or details about the publication.
* **Interview Details (id: 17)**: Any other information you think is relevant to the interview.
