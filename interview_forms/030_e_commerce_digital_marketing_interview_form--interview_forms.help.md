# E Commerce Digital Marketing Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The E Commerce Digital Marketing Interview Form is designed to gather information about your background, work experience, and skills as a candidate for an e-commerce digital marketing job. This form helps us understand your qualifications and how you can contribute to our team.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in your basic details, such as your name and contact information.
2.  Describe your work experience, including any relevant positions you've held in the past.
3.  Share your marketing background, including any degrees or certifications you've earned.
4.  List your digital skills, including any programming languages or software you're proficient in.
5.  Select your level of e-commerce experience, as well as the skills and tools you've used in the past.
6.  Choose the e-commerce platforms you have experience with and the job title and job status that best fit your current situation.
7.  Indicate your job location, job level, job category, job type, and job schedule.
8.  Finally, confirm your availability and terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
    *   Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
    *   Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
    *   Provide your email address for any follow-up communication.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Enter your address, city, and state.
    *   Enter your physical address to ensure we can contact you in case of an interview.
    *   Enter your city and state for geographical location purposes.
*   **Work Experience** (`work_experience`): Describe any work experience you've had.
    *   This is your opportunity to showcase your past work experience in the digital marketing field.
*   **Marketing Background** (`marketing_background`): Describe any marketing background you have.
    *   Share any degrees or certifications you've earned in marketing or related fields.
*   **Digital Skills** (`digital_skills`): List any digital skills you have.
    *   Include any programming languages or software you're proficient in, such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, or Adobe Creative Cloud.
*   **Ecom Experience** (`ecom_experience`): Select your level of e-commerce experience.
    *   Choose the number of years you've spent working in e-commerce.
*   **Ecom Skills** (`ecom_skills`): Choose your e-commerce skills.
    *   Select the e-commerce development, integration, or management skills you've acquired.
*   **Ecom Tools** (`ecom_tools`): Choose the e-commerce platforms you're familiar with.
    *   Select from the list of e-commerce platforms like Shopify, Magento, or BigCommerce.
*   **Ecom Platform** (`ecom_platform`): Choose the e-commerce platforms you have experience with.
    *   Select from the list of e-commerce platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, or BigCommerce.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`): Select the job title that best fits your current role.
    *   Choose from the list of job titles like Marketing Manager, E-commerce Manager, or Digital Marketing Manager.
*   **Job Description** (`job_description`): Briefly describe the job you're applying for.
    *   Write a brief description of the job responsibilities and requirements.
*   **Job Status** (`job_status`): Select the status of the job you're applying for.
    *   Choose whether the job is Open, Closed, or Pending.
*   **Ecom Industry** (`ecom_industry`): Choose the e-commerce industry you have experience with.
    *   Select from the list of e-commerce industries like Fashion, Beauty, or Electronics.
*   **Ecom Channel** (`ecom_channel`): Choose the e-commerce channels you've used.
    *   Select from the list of e-commerce channels like Social Media, Email, or Phone.
*   **Job Location** (`job_location`): Select your job location.
    *   Choose whether you're working remotely, in an office, or a hybrid setup.
*   **Job Level** (`job_level`): Select the level of your job role.
    *   Choose from the list of job levels like Individual Contributor, Team Member, or Manager.
*   **Job Category** (`job_category`): Select the job category that best fits your role.
    *   Choose from the list of job categories like Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`): Select the type of job you're applying for.
    *   Choose from the list of job types like Permanent, Contract, or Freelance.
*   **Job Schedule** (`job_schedule`): Select the job schedule that best fits your needs.
    *   Choose from the list of job schedules like Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
*   **Job Terminology** (`job_terminology`): Briefly describe any unique job terminology.
    *   Write a brief explanation of any technical terms or jargon you use regularly.
*   **Candidate References** (`candidate_references`): Indicate whether references are available.
    *   Choose whether you have available references or not.
*   **Candidate Availability** (`candidate_availability`): Indicate your availability.
    *   Choose whether you're available or not available for an interview.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Agree to the terms and conditions.
    *   Read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting your application.
