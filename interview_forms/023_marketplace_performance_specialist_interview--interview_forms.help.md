# Marketplace Performance Specialist Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your expertise, experience, and availability as a Marketplace Performance Specialist. Please provide accurate and relevant information to increase your chances of being selected for an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide all required information.
2. Fill in the General Info section with your name, email, and any additional comments.
3. In the Contact Details section, provide your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
4. In the Marketplace Expertise section, select your relevant expertise.
5. In the Scheduling section, select your preferred scheduling options.
6. In the E-commerce Experience section, select your level of experience in e-commerce.
7. In the E-commerce Sales section, provide your sales figures or numbers of sales.
8. In the E-commerce Customer Service section, select your relevant customer service skills.
9. In the E-commerce Expertise Level section, select your level of expertise in e-commerce.
10. In the Available to Work section, select your availability for the interview.
11. In the Schedule Interview section, select a date for the interview.
12. In the Availability section, select your availability for the interview.
13. In the Interview Location section, select your preferred location for the interview.
14. In the Interviewers section, select the interviewers you are comfortable working with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (Text, Required): This is the main contact information section, including your name, email, and any additional comments about yourself.
- **Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Provide your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
- **Marketplace Expertise** (Text, Optional): Select your expertise related to marketplaces.
- **Scheduling** (Text, Optional): Select your preferred scheduling options.
- **E-commerce Experience** (Select One, Required): Choose your level of experience in e-commerce.
- **E-commerce Sales** (Number, Required): Enter your sales figures or numbers of sales.
- **E-commerce Customer Service** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your relevant customer service skills.
- **E-commerce Expertise Level** (Select One, Required): Choose your level of expertise in e-commerce.
- **Available to Work** (Select One, Required): Select your availability for the interview.
- **Schedule Interview** (Date, Required): Select a date for the interview.
- **Availability** (Time, Required): Select your availability for the interview.
- **Interview Location** (Select Multiple, Required): Select your preferred location for the interview.
- **Interviewers** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the interviewers you are comfortable working with.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to increase your chances of being selected for an interview.
- If you are unsure about a field, contact us for clarification.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
