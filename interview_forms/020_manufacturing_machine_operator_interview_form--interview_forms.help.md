# Manufacturing Machine Operator Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from manufacturing machine operators, including personal details, qualifications, and availability for work.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed, even if they are not marked as "required".
3. If a field is marked as "select_one", select only one option that best fits your situation.
4. If a field is marked as "select_multiple", select all options that apply to you.
5. For fields marked as "date", enter the date in the standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
6. For fields marked as "number", enter a whole number without decimal points or commas.
7. For fields marked as "text", enter a brief description or answer.
8. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Work Experience** (text): Describe your work experience, including any relevant experience as a manufacturing machine operator.
* **Email** (email): Enter your professional email address.
* **Manufacturing Machine Operators License** (select_one): Check "Yes" if you hold a manufacturing machine operator's license, otherwise check "No".
* **Available to Travel** (select_multiple): Indicate which shifts and times of day you are available to travel for work (Available, Not Available).
* **Qualifications** (text): List any relevant qualifications or certifications you have.
* **References** (text): Provide the names and contact information of two professional references.
* **Emergency Contact** (text): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Job Title** (select_one): Select the job title that best fits your role (Production Line Operator, Maintenance Manager, Shift Leader).
* **Available to Work** (select_multiple): Indicate which shifts and times of day you are available to work (Weekends, Weekdays, Flexible).
* **Work Availability** (date): Enter the date when you are available to start work.
* **Available Shifts** (select_multiple): Select the shifts you are available to work (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Work Hours** (number): Enter the number of hours you are available to work per week.
* **Available to Work Days** (select_multiple): Select the days of the week you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Work Hours Available Shifts** (select_multiple): Select the shifts you are available to work during the week (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
* **Emergency Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Job Title 2** (select_one): Select the job title that best fits your role (Production Line Operator, Maintenance Manager, Shift Leader).
* **Job Title 3** (select_one): Select the job title that best fits your role (Production Line Operator, Maintenance Manager, Shift Leader).
* **Job Title 4** (select_one): Select the job title that best fits your role (Production Line Operator, Maintenance Manager, Shift Leader).
* **Job Title 5** (select_one): Select the job title that best fits your role (Production Line Operator, Maintenance Manager, Shift Leader).
