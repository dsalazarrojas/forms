# Restaurant Manager Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from a restaurant manager for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field accepts only standard email formats (e.g., @domain.com or @example.org).
6. Enter the date on which the interview took place in the "Date" field using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
7. Enter the time on the day of the interview in the "Time" field using the 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is commonly known to others.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is commonly known to others.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your current home or work address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the standard format (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., @domain.com or @example.org).
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the interview took place using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time on the day of the interview using the 24-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your information is up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the person responsible for this form for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only and should not be shared publicly.
