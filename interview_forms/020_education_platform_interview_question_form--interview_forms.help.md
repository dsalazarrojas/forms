# Education Platform Interview Question Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form helps to gather information about the user's education background, availability, and preferred communication time zones and days.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the role that best describes you from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your years of experience in the education platform.
3. Select your highest education level from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose the times of day you are available for communication (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
5. Select your preferred time zone for communication.
6. Choose your preferred days for communication (you can select multiple).
7. Optionally, select your availability ending time.
8. Select your preferred time slots for communication (you can select multiple).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Role** (role, select_one, required): Choose a role that best describes you from the dropdown menu. Options: Teacher, Instructor, Administrator.
* **Years of Experience** (years_of_experience, number, required): Enter your years of experience on the education platform.
* **Highest Education Level** (highest_education_level, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education from the dropdown menu. Options: High School, Bachelor's, Master's, Ph.D..
* **Available Dates** (available_dates, time, required): Not applicable in this form. This field seems to be a leftover from the schema and should be removed or corrected.
* **Availability** (availability, select_one, required): Choose a time of day you are available for communication (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Availability Ending** (availability_ending, time, required): Not applicable in this form. This field seems to be a leftover from the schema and should be removed or corrected.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (preferred_contact_time, select_multiple, required): Not applicable in this form. This field seems to be a leftover from the schema and should be removed or corrected.
* **Preferred Contact Days** (preferred_contact_days, select_multiple, required): Not applicable in this form. This field seems to be a leftover from the schema and should be removed or corrected.
* **Preferred Time Zone** (preferred_time_zone, select_one, required): Choose a time zone for communication. Options: Pacific, Eastern, Central, Mountain, GMT-05, GMT-08, GMT-11, GMT-14, GMT-16, GMT-18.
* **Preferred Contact Days** (preferred_contact_days, select_multiple, required): Choose days of the week for communication. Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
