# Sales Employment Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates for sales employment opportunities. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the below steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Job Title and Phone number in the respective fields.
3. Provide your work experience in years.
4. Choose your Job Type and Salary Range from the respective drop-down menus.
5. Select the Job Type 2 you are interested in.
6. Fill in the available work hours and available days you are able to work.
7. Add any additional notes you would like to include with your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you regularly check.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your resume or LinkedIn profile.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Experience**: Enter the number of years you have work experience in sales.
* **Prophets**: Please select one of the options: Apple or Orange. (Note: We are not looking for actual prophets, just a demonstration of the field type.)
* **Job Type**: Select one of the following options: Full-time, Part-time. (Note: This field is mandatory.)
* **Salary Range**: Select one or more of the following options: 20,000-50,000, 50,000-80,000. (Note: This field is mandatory.)
* **Job Type 2**: Select one or more of the following options: Full-time, Part-time. (Note: This field is not mandatory.)
* **Available**: Unfortunately, this field is not currently available. (Note: This field is not mandatory and will be removed in future versions.)
* **Work Hours**: Select one or more of the following options: 8, 12, 24. (Note: This field is mandatory.)
* **Available Days**: Select one or more of the following options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday. (Note: This field is mandatory.)
* **Note**: Add any additional notes you would like to include with your application. 

Note: The fields marked with * are required and must be filled in for your application to be considered complete.
