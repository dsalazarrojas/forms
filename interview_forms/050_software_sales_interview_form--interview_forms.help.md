# Software Sales Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback and assessment from the sales team members after an interview with a sales candidate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the candidate's interview performance and choose the relevant options for each field.
2. Enter the candidate's details such as job position, job category, job title, and job location.
3. Select the relevant skills and qualifications the candidate possesses.
4. Choose the relevant interview feedback and assessment scores.
5. Enter any other comments or observations about the candidate.
6. Enter the contact information for the candidate, including their email and phone number.
7. Select the status of the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, not required): This is a free-text field for any notes or comments about the candidate.
* **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, not required): Enter the candidate's details, such as their name, contact information, or any other relevant information.
* **Candidate Experience** (`candidate_experience`, number, not required): Select the candidate's experience level, e.g., low, high, or medium.
* **Candidate Qualifications** (`candidate_qualifications`, select_one, not required): Choose whether the candidate has the required qualifications for the job.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, not required): Select the skills the candidate possesses, e.g., leadership, communication, or problem-solving.
* **Interview Feedback** (`interview_feedback`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the feedback for the candidate's performance during the interview, e.g., positive, negative, or neutral.
* **Final Assessment** (`final_assessment`, select_one, not required): Choose the final assessment score, e.g., yes or no.
* **Scorecard** (`scorecard`, select_multiple, not required): Select the scorecard, e.g., yes or no.
* **Other Comments** (`other_comments`, text, not required): Enter any additional comments or observations about the candidate.
* **Candidate Reference Number** (`candidate_reference_number`, email, not required): Enter the candidate's reference number, if applicable.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the contact person's name and contact information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, text, not required): Enter the company name.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, not required): Enter the job position.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, not required): Enter the job type.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_multiple, not required): Select the job level, e.g., high, low, or medium.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter the job title.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, text, not required): Enter the job category.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, not required): Enter the job location.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, not required): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Interviewers** (`interviewers`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the interviewers who were present during the interview.
* **Assessment Score** (`assessment_score`, select_one, not required): Choose the assessment score, e.g., yes or no.
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, not required): Choose the submission status, e.g., active or inactive.

Please make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly, and do not hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need further guidance.
