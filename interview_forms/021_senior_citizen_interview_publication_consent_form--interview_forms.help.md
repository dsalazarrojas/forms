# Senior Citizen Interview Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Citizen Interview Publication Consent Form is designed to gather consent from senior citizens for the publication of their interview details. This form ensures that interviewees are aware of and agree to the publication of their information.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Start by reviewing the introduction, which explains the purpose of the form and the information that will be published.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the introduction for guidance.
*   Proceed to the "Interview Details" field to provide any additional information about the interview you are participating in.
*   In the "Do you consent to the publication of your interview details" field, select "I do" if you agree to the publication of your interview details.
*   Next, confirm your agreement by signing the "Interviewee Signature" field with a "Yes" selection.
*   Sign the "Signature" field with your name to confirm your identity and authority.
*   Date and time of signing can be filled out in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
*   Finally, include any additional information or comments in the "Note" field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction**: This is a non-editable text area where a brief explanation of the form's purpose and the information that will be published is provided.
*   **Interview Details**: This field is for any additional information about the interview you are participating in. It is optional and can be left blank if no additional information is needed.
*   **Do you consent to the publication of your interview details**: This field is a select-one dropdown where you must choose whether to agree or disagree to the publication of your interview details. Please select "I do" to agree or "False" to disagree.
*   **Interviewee Signature**: This field is a select-one dropdown where you must confirm your agreement by selecting "Yes".
*   **Signature**: This field is a text area where you must sign your name to confirm your identity and authority.
*   **Date**: This field is a date picker where you must enter the date of signing.
*   **Time**: This field is a time picker where you must enter the time of signing.
*   **Location**: This field is a text area where you can enter the location where the form is being signed.
*   **Note**: This field is a text area where you can enter any additional comments or information about the form.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough when filling out this form to ensure that your interview details are published correctly.
*   Review the introduction for any guidance or clarification needed before proceeding.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please refer to the contact information provided in the introduction.
