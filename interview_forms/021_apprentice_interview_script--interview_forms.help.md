# Apprentice Interview Script - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate's background, skills, and fit for an apprenticeship position. It is meant to be used by interviewers to gather essential details about the candidate's qualifications, experiences, and approach to learning and teamwork.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Candidate Information**: This page requires a brief introduction about the candidate. Please fill in the candidate's name and select the correct interview date.
2. **Interview Questions and Responses**: On this page, you will find the answers to several interview questions, including questions about the candidate's motivations, experience, skills, and approach to learning and teamwork.
3. **Assessment**: On the final page, you will find the space to provide a brief overall impression of the candidate and an assessment of their fit for the apprenticeship position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (Candidate Information): Enter the candidate's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Interview Date** (Candidate Information): Select the date of the interview.
* **Interviewer Name** (Candidate Information): Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Interview Questions and Responses** (Interview Questions and Responses): Respond to each question by providing a brief answer. For example:
	+ **What motivates you to pursue this apprenticeship?**: Describe the reasons behind the candidate's motivation to pursue the apprenticeship.
	+ **Describe your relevant experience or education**: Share relevant work or educational experiences that align with the apprenticeship position.
	+ **What technical skills do you already possess?**: List any existing technical skills that would be valuable to the apprenticeship.
	+ **How do you handle challenging situations?**: Provide an example of a challenging situation you faced and how you handled it.
	+ **Tell us about a time you worked effectively in a team**: Share a specific example of a team project or collaboration you were part of.
	+ **How do you approach learning new skills?**: Describe your approach to learning new skills and technologies.
* **Overall Impression** (Interviewer Assessment): Choose the best description for your overall impression of the candidate:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Assessment of Fit** (Interviewer Assessment): Choose the best description for your assessment of the candidate's fit for the apprenticeship:
	+ Highly Recommended
	+ Recommended
	+ Undecided
	+ Not Recommended
* **Notes and Next Steps** (Interviewer Assessment): Add any additional comments or notes about the candidate, and suggest any next steps for the candidate.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each question carefully and provide thoughtful answers.
* Take notes during the interview and use them to write your assessment.
* Consider providing feedback to the candidate after the interview.
