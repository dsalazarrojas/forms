# Art Gallery Curator Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from candidates applying for curator positions in an art gallery. It helps the curator team assess their background, experience, and cultural fit, enabling informed hiring decisions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in the candidate's full name.
2. Select the position the candidate is applying for.
3. Enter the URL of the candidate's online portfolio (if applicable).
4. Describe the candidate's relevant experience and key roles/responsibilities.
5. Schedule the interview date.
6. List the candidate's strengths for the role.
7. Identify areas where the candidate needs professional development.
8. Assess the candidate's cultural fit with the gallery's culture.
9. Add any additional notes from the interview discussion.
10. Provide a hiring recommendation based on the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the candidate.
* **Position applied for** (`position`, text, required): Select the position the candidate is applying for, such as Curator Assistant, Curator, or Head Curator.
* **Portfolio link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Enter a URL to the online portfolio of the candidate, if applicable.
* **Relevant experience** (`relevant_experience`, text, required): Describe the candidate's relevant experience and key roles/responsibilities.
* **Interview date** (`interview_date`, date, required): Enter the scheduled interview date.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, text, optional): List the candidate's strengths for the role.
* **Areas for development** (`areas_for_development`, text, optional): Identify areas where the candidate needs professional development.
* **Culture fit** (`culture_fit`, select_one, required): Assess the candidate's cultural fit with the gallery's culture using the options Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **Interviewer notes** (`interviewer_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes from the interview discussion.
* **Recommendation** (`recommendation`, select_one, required): Provide a hiring recommendation using the options Strongly recommend, Recommend, Consider, Do not recommend.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in fields.
* Use the interview date as scheduled, not actual.
* Be thorough when assessing cultural fit.
* Use the provided options for the recommendation, or add additional information to support your decision.
* Keep additional notes from the interview discussion brief but informative.
