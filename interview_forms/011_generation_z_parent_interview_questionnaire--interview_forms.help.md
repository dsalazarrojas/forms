# Generation Z Parent Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the role of technology in the life of Generation Z parents and their children. The goal is to understand the parent's perspective on their child's technology habits, their relationship with their child, and how they model responsible technology use in the home. This will help us better understand how to support parents in raising responsible digital citizens.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Answer all the questions as thoroughly as possible. You can add as much detail as you feel is necessary.
2. For multiple-choice questions like "digital_habits", select all that apply.
3. Be honest and open in your response, as all answers are confidential.
4. Take your time and answer each question to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think is the role of technology in your child's life?** (`gen_z_parent_survey`, text, optional): This question is an opportunity for you to share your thoughts on how technology influences your child's life. Please provide a detailed answer, and feel free to elaborate on any specific experiences or concerns you have.
* **What digital habits do you think are most challenging for your child?** (`digital_habits`, select_multiple, optional): For this question, select all the digital habits that you think are challenging for your child. These may include excessive screen time, social media use, online gaming, or online safety concerns.
* **How would you describe your relationship with your child?** (`parent_child_relationship`, text, optional): This question is a chance to reflect on your relationship with your child and how you support them in their technology use. Be as specific as possible about your interactions, communication, and boundaries.
* **How many hours do you typically spend on technology with your child?** (`tech_spend`, number, optional): Please provide an exact number of hours you spend on technology with your child. This will help us better understand your involvement in their digital lives.
* **How often do you talk to your child about the responsible use of technology?** (`digital_role_modeling`, text, optional): For this question, share how often you discuss responsible technology use with your child. This may be weekly, daily, or as needed, depending on their needs and concerns.
