# Company Selection For Interviews - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information from candidates being considered for an interview, allowing hiring teams to make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as the person being considered for the interview.
2. Enter the position you applied for and are being considered for.
3. If applicable, provide the names of the interviewer panel members.
4. Choose your preferred interview time slot, considering your availability.
5. Select your preferred interview format: In person, Video call, or Telephone.
6. If necessary, list any required documents for the interview, such as references or a portfolio.
7. Finally, provide the name and email of the HR contact responsible for scheduling the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate full name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as the person being considered for the interview.
* **Position applied for** (`position_applied`, `text`, **required**): Select the job title you are being considered for.
* **Interviewer panel members** (`interviewer_panel`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, provide the names of those invited to interview you.
* **Preferred interview time slot** (`preferred_time_slot`, `text`, **optional**): Choose your availability for the interview.
* **Interview mode** (`interview_mode`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred format for the interview: In person, Video call, or Telephone.
* **Required documents for interview** (`required_documents`, `text`, **optional**): If necessary, list any required documents, such as references or a portfolio.
* **HR contact for scheduling** (`hr_contact`, `text`, **required**): Provide the name and email of the HR contact responsible for scheduling the interview.
