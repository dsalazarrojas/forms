# Web Design Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for customers to provide us with information about their web design project. Please fill out the form below to help us understand their requirements and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
2. For fields with multiple options, select the relevant choice that best describes your project.
3. For fields with text boxes, enter a clear and concise description of your project.
4. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the correct format (e.g., [name@example.com](mailto:example@example.com)).
5. If any field is not applicable to your project, please select "N/A" or leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your full name, including your first and last name. This will help us identify you and your project.
* **Company Name**: This field is used to collect the name of your company or organization. Enter the name as it appears on your business cards or stationery.
* **Project Description**: This field is used to collect a brief description of your project. Please enter a concise description of your project goals and objectives.
* **Target Audience**: This field is used to collect information about the people who will be using your website. Please enter a list or description of your target audience.
* **Project Budget**: This field is used to collect the budget for your project. Please enter a valid number, if applicable. If you're not sure about the budget, please leave it blank.
* **Expected Launch Date**: This field is used to collect the date when you expect your project to be launched. Please enter a valid date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Primary Color Scheme**: This field is used to collect the primary color scheme for your website. Please select one of the available options: blue, red, or green.
* **Secondary Color Scheme**: This field is used to collect the secondary color scheme for your website. Please select one of the available options: blue, red, or green.
* **Font Size**: This field is used to collect the font size for your website. Please enter a valid number, if applicable. If you're not sure about the font size, please leave it blank.
* **Design File Type**: This field is used to collect the type of design files you have for your project. Please select one or more of the available options: PSD, PDF, or JPG.
* **Design Platform**: This field is used to collect the design platform for your website. Please select one of the available options: desktop, mobile, or tablet.
* **Email Address**: This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address in the format [name@example.com](mailto:example@example.com).
* **Contact Number**: This field is used to collect your contact number. Please enter a valid phone number or leave it blank if not applicable.
* **Project Manager**: This field is used to collect the name of your project manager. Please enter the name of the person responsible for your project.
* **Project Status**: This field is used to collect the current status of your project. Please select one of the available options: pending, active, or completed.

Note: If any field has a hint, please refer to it for additional guidance.
