# Operations Manager Interview Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Operations Manager Interview Assessment form is used to collect essential information about a candidate's work experience, job history, and other relevant details to assess their suitability for an operations management role.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** in the first section. Make sure to use your real name and a valid email address.
2. In the **Job Title** field, enter your current or last job title in your previous role(s).
3. Select your **Department** from the available options (Marketing, Finance, or HR).
4. Enter your **Experience (years)** in the number of years you have worked in your current or last role(s).
5. In the **Notice Period** field, select the notice period you would provide if you were to leave your current or last role(s).
6. Choose your **Job Status** (Full-time or Part-time).
7. Select your **Work Hours** (if applicable, e.g., 8 hours a day).
8. Choose your **Work Status** (Remote or On-site).
9. Select your **Work Location** (if applicable, e.g., Home Office or On-site).
10. Choose your **Work Level** (Manager, Supervisor, or Team Member).
11. Select your **Job Type** (Part-time, Full-time, Permanent, or Contract).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Job Title**: Describe your job title in your current or last role(s).
* **Department**: Select your department (Marketing, Finance, or HR).
* **Experience (years)**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your current or last role(s).
* **Notice Period**: Select the notice period you would provide if you were to leave your current or last role(s).
* **Job Status**: Choose your job status (Full-time or Part-time).
* **Work Hours**: Enter your work hours (if applicable).
* **Work Status**: Select your work status (Remote or On-site).
* **Work Location**: Enter your work location (if applicable).
* **Work Level**: Choose your work level (Manager, Supervisor, or Team Member).
* **Job Type**: Select your job type (Part-time, Full-time, Permanent, or Contract).

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* If you have gaps in your work history, explain them in the "Experience (years)" field.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
