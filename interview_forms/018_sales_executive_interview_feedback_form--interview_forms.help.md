# Sales Executive Interview Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from sales executives after an interview, ensuring accurate and fair evaluation of candidates. It should be filled out by the sales executive who conducted the interview, and should only be completed after an interview has taken place. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of candidate interviewed.
2. Fill out the candidate's name and title.
3. Input the date of the interview.
4. Record the start and end times of the interview.
5. Choose an overall rating out of 5.
6. Provide comments on the interview.
7. Decide if you recommend proceeding with the interview process.
8. Provide additional comments if necessary.
9. Sign off with your signature, title, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### What type of candidate is this interview for?
* **What type of candidate is this interview for** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_type_of_candidate_interviewed`): This is the type of candidate being interviewed, e.g. Executive or Team Member.

* **Candidate Name** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_candidate_name`): This is the name of the candidate being interviewed. Please enter their full name in the text box.

* **Candidate Title** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_candidate_title`): This is the title of the candidate being interviewed, e.g. CEO or Manager.

* **Date of Interview** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_date_of_interview`): This is the date of the interview, which is when the interview took place. Enter it in the format MM/DD/YY.

* **Start of Interview** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_start_of_interview`): This is the time the interview began, which should be entered in the format HH:MM. Please use the 24-hour clock.

* **Overall Interview Rating** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_rating`): This is a numerical rating of the interview out of 5, where 5 is excellent and 1 is poor. Choose the number that best reflects your opinion.

* **Comments** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_comments`): Write any comments or observations you have about the interview that are not captured elsewhere in this form. These might include anything from the tone of the conversation to red flags or positives.

* **Recommendation** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_recommendation`): Choose whether you recommend proceeding with the interview process, or not. This will help us understand which candidates should be moved forward and which may not be the right fit for the role.

* **Additional Comments** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_additional_comments`): If you have any other comments or concerns that are not captured elsewhere in this form, enter them here.

* **Signature** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_signature`): This is where you sign off with your name, title, and contact information so we know who to contact if needed.

* **Title** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_title`): This is your title, e.g. Mr., Ms., etc.

* **First Name** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_first_name`): This is your first name.

* **Last Name** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_last_name`): This is your last name.

* **Job Title** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_job_title`): This is your job title.

* **Company** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_company`): This is the name of your company.

* **Department** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_department`): This is the department you work in.

* **Phone** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_phone`): This is your phone number.

* **Email** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_email`): This is your email address.

* **Job Function** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_job_function`): This is your job function.

* **Reporting Managers** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_reporting_managers`): If you have a reporting manager, this is their name.

* **Other** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_other`): If you have any other information that's not captured elsewhere, enter it here.

* **Submitter's Name** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_submitter_name`): This is your name, the person completing this form.

* **Submitter's Email** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_submitter_email`): This is your email address.

* **Submitter's Department** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_submitter_department`): This is the department you belong to.

* **Submitter's Phone** (`sales_executive_interview_feedback_form_submitter_phone`): This is your phone number.

## Tips
* Please fill this form after each interview to help us evaluate candidates fairly and consistently.
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the 24-hour clock for the start and end times.
* If you have any questions or issues with this form, contact your supervisor or IT for assistance.
