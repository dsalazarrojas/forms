# Non Profit Administrative Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Non Profit Administrative Interview Form is designed to evaluate the administrative skills and experience of a candidate for a non-profit organization. It is used to assess the candidate's overall score based on their interview performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the candidate's name and other required information in the "candidate" field (ID 1).
3. Evaluate the candidate's administrative skills and experience, and enter their overall score in the "overall_score" field (ID 2).
4. Provide feedback and comments about the candidate's performance in the "feedback" field (ID 3).
5. Enter the date of the interview in the "date_of_interview" field (ID 4).
6. Evaluate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest, by selecting an option in the "submit_review" field (ID 5).
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "non_profit_administrative_interview_form_comments" field (ID 6).
8. Enter the name of the reviewer in the "reviewer" field (ID 7).
9. Enter the name of the reviewed candidate in the "reviewee" field (ID 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Candidate
* **Name** (ID 1, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the candidate being evaluated.
### 2. Overall Score
* **Score** (ID 2, number, required/optional): Evaluate the candidate's overall score, on a scale of 1-5.
### 3. Feedback
* **Comments** (ID 3, text, required/optional): Provide feedback and comments about the candidate's performance.
### 4. Date of Interview
* **Date** (ID 4, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the interview.
### 5. Submit Review
* **Rating** (ID 5, select_one, required): Evaluate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-5.
### 6. Non Profit Administrative Interview Form Comments
* **Comments** (ID 6, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback.
### 7. Reviewer
* **Name** (ID 7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the reviewer.
### 8. Reviewee
* **Name** (ID 8, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the reviewed candidate.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete the form in a fair and objective manner, without bias or conflict of interest.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure accurate and complete information is collected.
* If unsure about any field, review the form instructions and fields carefully before proceeding.
* If you need help or clarification, contact the form administrators.
