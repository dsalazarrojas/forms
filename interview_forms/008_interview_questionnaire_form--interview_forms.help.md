# Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from potential interviewees to ensure a successful and efficient interview process. It covers various aspects, including contact information, availability, work experience, job preferences, personal questions, education, work experience, skills questions, references, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand each question's purpose.
2.  Fill in all required fields marked with a red asterisk (\*).
3.  Provide accurate and detailed information for each field.
4.  Ensure that you have not duplicated any existing records or forms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`id: 1`): This is a non-editable note field that provides a general introduction to the form.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 2`): \*Enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number for us to get in touch with you easily.
* **Availability** (`id: 3`): \*Please select your availability in terms of how long you would like to consider for a job: 6 months, 1 year, or 2 years.
* **Work Experience** (`id: 4`): \*Provide a detailed description of your work experience in the relevant field, including your role, duration of employment, and relevant skills used.
* **Job Preferences** (`id: 5`): Select all the job preferences that apply to you, e.g., working with children, working with seniors, working as part of a team.
* **Personal Questions** (`id: 6`): \*Select all the personal questions that you would like to be asked during the interview, e.g., salary range, work hours, job security.
* **Education** (`id: 7`): \*Indicate your level of education: High or Low, depending on the level of your education.
* **Work Experience 2** (`id: 8`): \*Provide another description of your work experience, if applicable.
* **Skills Questions** (`id: 9`): \*Select all the relevant skills you possess, e.g., data analysis, problem-solving, leadership skills.
* **References** (`id: 10`): \*Indicate if you would like to be contacted by any of your professional references.
* **References** (`id: 11`): \*Provide any other information that you feel is essential for us to know about you.
* **Skills Levels** (`id: 12`): \*Select your level of proficiency for each of the skills listed, e.g., High, Low.
