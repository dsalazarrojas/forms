# Phone Interview - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for scheduling and recording phone interviews. It captures the contact details, call type, schedule, and any additional note-taking information.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, fill out the form with the required information, including your contact details (phone number, name, email) and schedule (select a time slot).
- Select the type of call you wish to have.
- Add any additional comments or notes about the interview.
- Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Phone Number (1)**: A text field where you will input your phone number.
* **Name (2)**: A text field where you will enter your name.
* **Email (3)**: A text field where you will enter your email address.
* **Type of Call (4)**: Select the type of call you wish to have, from the provided options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Schedule (5)**: Select the schedule for the phone interview:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Date (6)**: A date field where you will select the date of the phone interview.
* **Time (7)**: A time field where you will select the time of the phone interview.
* **Note (8)**: A text field where you can add any additional comments or notes about the interview.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have entered accurate and complete information.
- If you need to resubmit the form, please do not overwrite your previous submission.
- If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, please contact your support team.
