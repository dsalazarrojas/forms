# Online Interview Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and work-related information from users. Please fill it out accurately and thoroughly to ensure you get the most out of this interaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin on the first page, where you'll find the contact information section.
2. Answer each question thoroughly, and select the relevant options for your situation.
3. Review each page's fields to ensure you're answering all required questions.
4. Submit the completed form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (id: 1)**: Provide your name, email, and phone number to get in touch with you if needed.
* **Work Experience (id: 2)**: Answer yes or no to indicate if you have work experience or not.
* **Education Background (id: 3)**: Select multiple options to indicate your education level and background (e.g., yes to "Bachelor's degree" if you have one).
* **Interview Details (id: 4)**: Type in a brief description of the interview or meeting you're applying for or inquiring about.
* **Job History (id: 5)**: Select multiple options to indicate your job status and history (e.g., yes to "Current employee" if you have a job).
* **Skills (id: 6)**: Choose skills you have or are interested in, such as yes to "Programming" if you have programming experience).
* **Availability (id: 7)**: Select a date for when you're available to work or meet.
* **Work Type (id: 8)**: Choose a work type that suits your situation (e.g., yes to "Full-time" if you're looking for full-time work).
* **Job Status (id: 9)**: Select the current job status (e.g., yes to "Active" if you're actively employed).
* **Contact Information 2 (id: 10)**: Provide additional contact information for follow-up, such as a second email or phone number.
* **Job Location (id: 11)**: Select multiple options to indicate where you're willing to work or travel to for a job.
* **Work Hours (id: 12)**: Choose the work hours that suit your situation (e.g., yes to "Flexible" if you need flexible hours).

Please remember that this form is for information gathering purposes only, and you'll be contacted if your answers are of interest to the relevant parties.
