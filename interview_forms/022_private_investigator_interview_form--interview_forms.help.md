# Private Investigator Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from private investigators, including their personal details, experience, and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your basic contact information, including first and last name, phone number, email, and address.
2. Next, provide your date of birth and employment status.
3. Indicate your years of experience in your field and your occupation.
4. Select the services you provide, such as surveillance, background checks, or forensic services.
5. If applicable, select the agency you represent or work with.
6. Confirm whether you are a private investigator by selecting the correct answer.
7. If necessary, add any additional services you offer.
8. Finally, provide any relevant notes or comments about your services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Please enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Please enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Address** (address, text, optional): Enter your address in the format `Street Name, Apt Number, City, State, Zip Code`.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Employment Status** (employment_status, select_one, required): Indicate your current employment status:
	+ Active
	+ Retired
	+ Disabled
* **Occupation** (occupation, text, optional): Briefly describe your occupation.
* **Years in Field** (years_in_field, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working in this field.
* **Experience** (experience, text, optional): Describe your relevant experience.
* **Investigative Services** (investigative_services, select_multiple, required): Select the services you provide:
	+ Surveillance
	+ Background Check
	+ Forensic Services
	+ Digital Forensics
* **Investigative Agency** (investigative_agency, text, optional): If applicable, enter the agency you represent or work with.
* **Private Investigator** (private_investigator, select_one, required): Confirm whether you are a private investigator:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Agency** (agency, text, optional): If applicable, enter the agency you represent or work with.
* **Services** (services, text, optional): Briefly describe any additional services you offer.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about your services.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date of the interview or service (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time of the interview or service (HH:MM AM/PM).
