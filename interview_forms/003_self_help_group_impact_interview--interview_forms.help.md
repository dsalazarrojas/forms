## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data on the impact of self-help groups on participants' outcomes, satisfaction, and engagement.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

	* **Impact measured**: This section evaluates the level of impact of the self-help group. Select 'Yes' if impact was measured, and 'No' if it was not.
