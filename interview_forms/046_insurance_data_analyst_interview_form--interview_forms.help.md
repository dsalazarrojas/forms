# Insurance Data Analyst Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for the Insurance Data Analyst position. It will help the hiring team assess their qualifications and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Candidate Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Attach your resume in the "Resume" field.
5. Answer the question of whether you have applied for the job ("Job Applied For") by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter the number of years of experience you have in the field ("Years of Experience").
7. List your relevant skills in the "Skills" field.
8. Enter your available start date and end date in the "Available Start Date" and "Available End Date" fields respectively.
9. Select your available frequency ("Available Frequency") as full-time, part-time, contract, or freelance.
10. Answer the question of why you are applying for this position ("Why are you applying for this position?").
11. Indicate whether you have the type of experience required for the job ("What type of experience do you have?") by selecting "Yes" or "No".
12. Select the level of education you have ("What level of education do you have?") as High or Low.
13. Provide a description of the type of work experience you have ("Please specify what kind of work experience do you have?").
14. Mention where you did most of your work experience ("Where did you do most of your work experience?").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your valid phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Resume**: Upload your resume in PDF or DOC format for review.
* **Job Applied For**: Indicate whether you have actually applied for the position.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in the field.
* **Skills**: List your relevant skills and experience that align with the position requirements.
* **Available Start Date**: Enter your earliest start date you are available to begin work.
* **Available End Date**: Enter your latest end date you are available to begin work.
* **Available Frequency**: Select your preferred work frequency as full-time, part-time, contract, or freelance.
* **Why are you applying for this position?**: Explain your reason for applying for the Insurance Data Analyst position.
* **What type of experience do you have?**: Indicate whether you have the type of experience required for the job.
* **What level of education do you have?**: Select your level of education, either High or Low.
* **Please specify what kind of work experience do you have?**: Provide a detailed description of your work experience.
* **Where did you do most of your work experience?**: Describe the location where you gained most of your work experience.
