# Independent Party Interview Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Independent Party Interview Evaluation form is used to collect feedback from an independent party about a candidate during an interview. This feedback is crucial in making informed decisions about the candidate's suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Interview Form** (`interview_form`):
    This field is a text input where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the interview.
2.  **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`):
    Enter the candidate's name as it was provided during the interview.
3.  **Candidate Contact Information** (`candidate_contact_info`):
    Enter any contact information you have for the candidate, such as their email or phone number.
4.  **Candidate Experience** (`candidate_experience`):
    Describe the candidate's relevant work experience, highlighting their skills and qualifications.
5.  **Candidate Skills** (`candidate_skills`):
    List the candidate's relevant skills, such as programming languages, tools, or software proficiency.
6.  **Candidate Education** (`candidate_education`):
    Enter any relevant education or qualifications the candidate holds.
7.  **Candidate Work History** (`candidate_work_history`):
    Describe the candidate's work history, highlighting any relevant positions or experiences.
8.  **Candidate Salary** (`candidate_salary`):
    Enter the candidate's current or proposed salary range.
9.  **Candidate Availability** (`candidate_availability`):
    Enter the candidate's availability for work, including any potential start date.
10. **Interviewer ID** (`interviewer_id`): (Note: This field was removed as it is not a field from the input schema)
11. **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`):
    Enter your name as the interviewer.
12.  **Interview Location** (`interview_location`):
    Enter the location of the interview, if applicable.
13.  **Interview Type** (`interview_type`):
    Select the type of interview that took place (Phone, Video, In-person).
14.  **Candidate Feedback** (`candidate_feedback`):
    Provide feedback on the candidate's performance, using the options Strongly agree, Agree, Neither agree nor disagree, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
15.  **Candidate Recommendation** (`candidate_recommendation`):
    Select whether you recommend the candidate for the position, using the options Recommended, Not recommended, or Neutral.
16.  **Candidate Comments** (`candidate_comments`):
    Enter any additional comments or notes about the candidate.
17.  **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`):
    Enter any additional comments or feedback about the candidate, if needed.
