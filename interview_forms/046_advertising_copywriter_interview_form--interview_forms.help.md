# Advertising Copywriter Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advertising Copywriter Interview Form is designed to gather information about your experience, skills, and qualifications as a copywriter. This form will help us assess your fit for potential future collaboration opportunities with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each section carefully and thoroughly.
2. Answer all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, contact us.
4. Once you've completed the form, you will be considered for potential future collaboration opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, \*): Please enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, \*): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, \*): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, `text`, \*): Provide a link to your writing portfolio.
* **Years of Copywriting Experience** (`years_copywriting_experience`, `number`, \*): Enter the number of years you have experience in copywriting.
* **Previous Employers** (`previous_employers`, `text`, \*): List the names of your previous employers or clients.
* **Industries Worked** (`industries_worked`, `select_multiple`, \*): Select the industries you have written copy for, such as Technology, Healthcare, etc.
* **Writing Styles Familiar With** (`writing_styles_familiar_with`, `select_multiple`, \*): Choose the writing styles you are experienced in, such as Brand Storytelling, Direct Response, etc.
* **Brand Voice Adaptation** (`brand_voice_adaptation`, `select_one`, \*): Rate your ability to adapt to different brand voices.
* **Creative Brief Interpretation** (`creative_brief_interpretation`, `select_one`, \*): Rate your ability to interpret creative briefs.
* **Product to Write About** (`product_to_write_about`, `text`, \*): Write a compelling headline for a new eco-friendly cleaning product.
* **Call to Action Sample** (`call_to_action_sample`, `text`, \*): Write a persuasive call-to-action for a free trial.
* **SEO Keywords Integration** (`seo_keywords_integration`, `text`, \*): Explain how you incorporate keywords into your copy naturally.
* **Availability for Interview** (`availability_for_interview`, `text`, \*): Indicate your availability for an interview.
* **Questions for the Team** (`questions_for_the_team`, `text`): This is an opportunity to ask us questions or express your thoughts.

Please note that some fields have specific options or instructions that must be followed. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your application.
