# Engineering Candidate Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about engineering candidates during the interview process. It helps assess the candidate's fit and suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Candidate Details**: Fill in the candidate's basic details, including any other information that might be relevant to the position.
2. **Contact Info**: Provide the candidate's contact information, including their email and phone number.
3. **Technical Skills**: Select the relevant technical skills the candidate possesses. This will help you assess their suitability for the job.
4. **Communication Style**: Choose the best description of the candidate's communication style.
5. **Overall Fit**: Select the options that best describe the candidate's overall fit for the position.
6. **Feedback**: Write any additional feedback or comments about the candidate.
7. **Interview Panel**: Select the members of the interview panel who participated in the interview.
8. **Hiring Manager**: Choose the person who was present during the interview.
9. **Date Interviewed**: Select the date of the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (1): Fill in the candidate's name, job title, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (2): Enter the candidate's email and phone number for easy communication.
* **Technical Skills (3)**: Select the technical skills the candidate demonstrated during the interview.
*   *Option 1*: The candidate possesses excellent skills in programming languages like C++ or Java.
*   *Option 2*: The candidate is proficient in data analysis and visualization tools like Tableau or Power BI.
*   *Option 3*: The candidate is skilled in cloud infrastructure, including AWS or Azure.
* **Communication Style (4)**: Choose how the candidate communicates, such as:
*   *Option 1*: The candidate is clear and concise in their communication.
*   *Option 2*: The candidate is able to adapt to technical jargon and complex concepts.
* **Overall Fit (5)**: Select the options that best describe the candidate's overall fit for the position.
*   *Option 1*: The candidate is an excellent fit for the role.
*   *Option 2*: The candidate is a good fit, but some adjustments might be needed.
* **Feedback (6)**: Write any additional comments or feedback about the candidate.
* **Interview Panel (7)**: Select the members of the interview panel who participated in the interview.
* **Hiring Manager (8)**: Choose the person who was present during the interview.
* **Date Interviewed (9)**: Select the date of the interview.
