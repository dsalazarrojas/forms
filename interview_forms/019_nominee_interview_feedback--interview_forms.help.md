# Nominee Interview Feedback - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback on nominees who have gone through an interview process. The information collected will help hiring managers and interviewers make informed decisions about the candidate's suitability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the Interview Form**: Ensure you have access to the relevant information and have a clear understanding of the interview process.
2. **Complete the Required Fields**: Fill in the required fields, marked with *Required*.
3. **Provide Feedback**: Enter your honest feedback on the nominee's performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Interview Form Rating
**Rating** (`interview_form_rating`, Number, Not Required)
Enter a numerical rating of the nominee's performance during the interview.

### Overall Rating
**Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, Number, Not Required)
Enter a numerical rating of the overall fit of the nominee for the role.

### Strengths
**Strengths** (`strengths`, Text, Not Required)
List the nominee's strengths and qualifications that made a positive impact during the interview.

### Areas for Improvement
**Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, Text, Not Required)
Identify areas where the nominee could improve or grow in, based on the interview experience.

### Recommendations
**Recommendations** (`recommendations`, Text, Not Required)
Share any recommendations you have for the nominee, such as additional training or support.

### Candidate Score
**Candidate Score** (`candidate_score`, Number, Not Required)
Enter a numerical score of the nominee's overall performance.

### Final Decision
**Final Decision** (`final_decision`, Select One, Not Required)
Mark 'Yes' if the nominee is recommended for the role, or 'No' if they are not.

### Date Interviewed
**Date Interviewed** (`date_interviewed`, Date, Not Required)
Enter the date of the interview.

### Time Interviewed
**Time Interviewed** (`time_interviewed`, Time, Not Required)
Enter the time of the interview.

### Comments
**Comments** (`comments`, Text, Not Required)
Enter any additional comments or feedback about the nominee's interview experience.

Note: The fields marked with *Required* are mandatory, while others are optional and meant for additional feedback and insights.
