# E Learning Specialist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate applying for an E Learning Specialist position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Click on the "Get Started" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your name, job title, and contact information on the "Candidate Details" page.
3. Provide a brief description of your work experience on the "Work Experience" page.
4. List your technical skills and relevant qualifications on the "Technical Skills" page.
5. Describe your education background on the "Education Background" page.
6. Provide information about your references on the "References" page.
7. Share any other relevant experience you have on the "Other Experience" page.
8. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details**:
	+ (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
	+ (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title or profession.
	+ (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address.
	+ (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Work Experience**:
	+ (`text`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your work experience.
* **Technical Skills**:
	+ (`text`, `text`, required): List your technical skills and relevant qualifications.
* **Education Background**:
	+ (`text`, `text`, required): Describe your education background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **References**:
	+ (`text`, `text`, required): Provide information about your references, including names, job titles, and contact information.
* **Other Experience**:
	+ (`text`, `text`, required): Share any other relevant experience you have that is not included in your work experience.
* **Tips**:
	+ Please be as specific as possible when providing information about your work experience, technical skills, education background, and references.
	+ Proofread your application carefully to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
