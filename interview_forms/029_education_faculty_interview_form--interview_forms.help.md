# Education Faculty Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Faculty Interview Form is used to collect feedback from faculty members regarding a student's performance after an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you have interviewed the student.
2. Enter your faculty name.
3. Enter the date and time of the interview.
4. Evaluate the student's performance by selecting a score and rank.
5. Enter any notes regarding the interview.
6. Enter any comments regarding the student's performance.
7. Enter the department name and location.
8. Select which faculty member you submitted the evaluation to.
9. Select which faculty member reviewed the evaluation.
10. Enter any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Faculty Interview Form** (`id: 1`): Select all that apply if you have interviewed the student. The options are:
	+ Select all that apply
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **Faculty Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your faculty name in this field.
* **Evaluation Date** (`id: 3`): Enter the date of the interview in this field.
* **Evaluation Time** (`id: 4`): Enter the time of the interview in this field.
* **Candidate Score** (`id: 5`): Evaluate the student's performance by selecting a score from 1 to 5.
* **Candidate Rank** (`id: 6`): Evaluate the student's performance by selecting a rank from High, Medium, or Low.
* **Interview Notes** (`id: 7`): Enter any notes regarding the interview in this field.
* **Faculty Comments** (`id: 8`): Enter any comments regarding the student's performance in this field.
* **Department Name** (`id: 9`): Enter the department name in this field.
* **Department Location** (`id: 10`): Enter the department location in this field.
* **Submitted By** (`id: 11`): Select which faculty member you submitted the evaluation to.
* **Submitted Date** (`id: 12`): Enter the date you submitted the evaluation in this field.
* **Submitted Time** (`id: 13`): Enter the time you submitted the evaluation in this field.
* **Submitted Comments** (`id: 14`): Enter any comments regarding submitting the evaluation in this field.
* **Department Comments** (`id: 15`): Enter any comments regarding the department in this field.
* **Review Status** (`id: 16`): Select the review status of the evaluation from Completed, In Progress, or Pending.
* **Review Notes** (`id: 17`): Enter any notes regarding the review in this field.
* **Review Date** (`id: 18`): Enter the date of the review in this field.
* **Review Time** (`id: 19`): Enter the time of the review in this field.
* **Review By** (`id: 20`): Select which faculty member reviewed the evaluation.
* **Review Comments** (`id: 21`): Enter any comments regarding the review in this field.
* **Submitted To** (`id: 22`): Select which faculty member the evaluation was submitted to.
* **Submitted To Comments** (`id: 23`): Enter any comments regarding submitting to in this field.
* **Review To** (`id: 24`): Select which faculty member the review was submitted to.
* **Review To Comments** (`id: 25`): Enter any comments regarding reviewing to in this field.

## Tips
Before filling out this form, please ensure you have conducted an interview with the student and are submitting a unique evaluation. If you have already submitted an evaluation, do not submit another.
