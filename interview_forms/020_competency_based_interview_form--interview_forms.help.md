# Competency Based Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to evaluate a candidate's competencies in various areas during an interview.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the candidate's interview and take notes during the conversation.
2. Complete the form fields as you go through the interview, using the following pages as a guide:
	* Page 1: Gather basic candidate information
	* Page 2: Evaluate communication and problem-solving competencies
	* Page 3: Evaluate teamwork and leadership competencies
	* Page 4: Evaluate technical competencies
3. Answer each field as accurately as possible based on your observations during the interview.
4. Use the "Overall Impression" field to summarize your assessment of the candidate.
5. Finally, provide a final recommendation for hiring and any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, Date, required): The date of the interview.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, Text, required): The full name of the candidate.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, Email, required): The candidate's email address.
* **Candidate Phone** (`candidate_phone`, Text, required): The candidate's phone number.
* **Position Applied For** (`position_applied`, Text, required): The job title the candidate is applying for.
* **Department** (`department`, Select One, required): The hiring department.
* **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, Text, required): The name of the interviewer.
* **Interviewer Title** (`interviewer_title`, Text, required): The job title of the interviewer.
* **Interview Round** (`interview_round`, Select One, required): The round of the interview (e.g. phone screen, first round, etc.).
* **Interview Format** (`interview_format`, Select One, required): The format of the interview (e.g. in-person, video call, etc.).
* **Communication Competency** (`competency_communication`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's communication skills.
* **Communication Notes** (`competency_communication_notes`, Text, optional): Specific observations about the candidate's communication skills.
* **Teamwork Competency** (`competency_teamwork`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's teamwork skills.
* **Teamwork Notes** (`competency_teamwork_notes`, Text, optional): Specific observations about the candidate's teamwork skills.
* **Problem Solving Competency** (`competency_problem_solving`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's problem-solving skills.
* **Problem Solving Notes** (`competency_problem_solving_notes`, Text, optional): Specific observations about the candidate's problem-solving skills.
* **Leadership Competency** (`competency_leadership`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's leadership potential.
* **Leadership Notes** (`competency_leadership_notes`, Text, optional): Specific observations about the candidate's leadership potential.
* **Adaptability Competency** (`competency_adaptability`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's adaptability.
* **Adaptability Notes** (`competency_adaptability_notes`, Text, optional): Specific observations about the candidate's adaptability.
* **Technical Competency** (`competency_technical`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's technical skills.
* **Technical Notes** (`competency_technical_notes`, Text, optional): Specific observations about the candidate's technical skills.
* **Behavioral Question 1 Response** (`behavioral_question_1`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's response to the first behavioral question.
* **Behavioral Question 2 Response** (`behavioral_question_2`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's response to the second behavioral question.
* **Behavioral Question 3 Response** (`behavioral_question_3`, Select One, required): A rating of the candidate's response to the third behavioral question.
* **Overall Impression** (`overall_impression`, Select One, required): A summary of your assessment of the candidate.
* **Hire Recommendation** (`hire_recommendation`, Select One, required): A final recommendation for hiring.
* **Salary Expectations Discussed** (`salary_expectations`, Text, optional): Specific observations about the candidate's salary expectations.
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date_availability`, Date, optional): The date the candidate can start working.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, optional): Any other observations or feedback about the candidate.

Note: Some fields are marked as optional, but it's highly recommended that you complete them as they provide valuable information for the hiring process.
