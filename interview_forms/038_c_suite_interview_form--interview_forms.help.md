# C Suite Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The C Suite Interview Form is designed to collect information from candidates during the executive interview process. This form helps gather essential details about the candidate's background, experience, and qualifications for a C-Suite position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by selecting the "Interview Date" field** (`interview_date`, date). Enter the date of the interview you're filling out the form for.
2. **Enter the candidate's full name** (`candidate_name`, text). This field requires a candidate's complete name.
3. **Enter the candidate's email address** (`candidate_email`, email). This is a required field, ensure you enter a valid email address.
4. **Enter the candidate's phone number** (`candidate_phone`, text). This will be used as the contact number for the candidate.
5. **Select the position the candidate is applying for** (`position_applied`, text). Choose from the list of executive role titles provided.
6. **Choose the executive level the candidate is applying for** (`position_type`, select_one). Select from the list of options (CEO, COO, CFO, CTO, CMO, President, Vice President, Other C-Suite).
7. **Enter the names of the interviewing panel members** (`interview_panel`, text). This will be used for internal reference.
8. **Provide an update on the candidate's current employment status** (`current_employment`, text). Describe the candidate's current situation.
9. **Enter the number of years the candidate has executive experience** (`years_experience`, number). This field is mandatory.
10. **Describe the candidate's previous executive positions** (`previous_positions`, text). Summarize the candidate's career history.
11. **List the industries the candidate has led** (`industry_background`, text). Describe the various industries the candidate has led.
12. **Highlight the candidate's key accomplishments** (`key_accomplishments`, text). Share the candidate's most notable achievements.
13. **Describe the candidate's leadership style** (`leadership_style`, text). Explain how the candidate approaches leadership.
14. **Share the candidate's strategic vision for the role** (`strategic_vision`, text). Outline their thoughts on the position.
15. **Identify potential obstacles or challenges** (`biggest_challenge`, text). Discuss any anticipated difficulties.
16. **Describe the candidate's salary expectations** (`salary_expectations`, text). Specify the expected compensation range.
17. **List any benefits or perks the candidate is interested in** (`benefits_interests`, text). Indicate what matters most to the candidate.
18. **Enter the earliest start date available** (`start_date`, date). Choose a date when the candidate can begin.
19. **Make any relevant comments or observations during the interview** (`interview_notes`, text). Record any notable interactions or insights.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview.
* **Candidate Name**: Enter the candidate's full name.
* **Candidate Email**: Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Candidate Phone Number**: Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Position Applied**: Select the executive role the candidate is applying for.
* **Position Type**: Choose the executive level the candidate is applying for.
* **Interviewing Panel Members**: List the names of the interviewing panel members.
* **Current Employment Status**: Describe the candidate's current employment situation.
* **Years of Executive Experience**: Enter the number of years the candidate has executive experience.
* **Previous Executive Positions**: Summarize the candidate's previous executive positions.
* **Industry Background**: Describe the industries the candidate has led.
* **Key Accomplishments**: Highlight the candidate's key accomplishments.
* **Leadership Style**: Describe the candidate's leadership approach.
* **Strategic Vision**: Outline the candidate's vision for the position.
* **Biggest Challenge**: Discuss potential obstacles or challenges.
* **Salary Expectations**: Specify the expected compensation range.
* **Interested Benefits or Perks**: Indicate what matters most to the candidate.
* **Earliest Start Date Available**: Enter the earliest start date available.
* **Interview Notes and Observations**: Record any notable comments or observations during the interview.
