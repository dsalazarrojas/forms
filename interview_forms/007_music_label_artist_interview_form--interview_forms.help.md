# Music Label Artist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about artists for a music label.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question accurately and thoroughly. Make sure to fill out all the required fields (indicated by a asterisk *). If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Artist Details** (*id: 1*)
 Artist Details: This is a free-text field where you can enter any relevant information about the artist.
*Required: No*

### **Interview** (*id: 2*)
 Interview: This field is meant for any additional comments or observations about the artist.
*Required: No*

### **Artist Info** (*id: 3*)
 Artist Info: This field can be used to provide background information about the artist.
*Required: No*
