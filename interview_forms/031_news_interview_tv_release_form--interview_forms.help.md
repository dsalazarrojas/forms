# news_interview_tv_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "news_interview_tv_release_form" is used to collect participant details for TV news releases. This includes gathering information on the participant's details, shoot schedule, and consents for release.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below. 

1. Fill out the participant details section, which includes:
   - participant_details: Provide the participant's details as per your records.
   - participant_name: Enter the participant's name.
   - participant_phone: Enter the participant's phone number.
   - participant_email: Enter the participant's email address. 
2. Enter the release details, which includes:
   - release_date: Enter the date of the release, following the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
   - release_time: Enter the time of the release, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
   - shoot_details: Provide any additional details about the shoot.
3. Fill out the consent section:
   - consent: Confirm whether the participant has given consent for release.
4. Finally, enter any other relevant details:
   - location: Provide the location where the shoot took place.
   - production_company: Enter the name of the production company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details**: (text, optional): Enter any relevant participant details, such as their role or any other identifying information.
* **participant_name**: (text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **participant_phone**: (text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **participant_email**: (email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **release_date**: (date, optional): Enter the date of the release, following the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **release_time**: (time, optional): Enter the time of the release, in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **shoot_details**: (text, optional): Provide any additional details about the shoot, such as location or other specifics.
* **participant_notes**: (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.
* **consent**: (select_one, required): Confirm whether the participant has given consent for release.
* **location**: (text, optional): Provide the location where the shoot took place.
* **production_company**: (text, optional): Enter the name of the production company.

## Tips

- Double-check the participant's details for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure the release date and time are correctly formatted.
- Confirm the participant's consent before releasing any information.
