# Academic Expert Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Expert Interview Form is a tool used to collect information from academic experts for the purpose of conducting interviews. The form helps to gather essential details about the expert, their background, and their preferences for the interview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Expert Name" field with the full name of the academic expert being interviewed.
2. Enter the expert's professional email address in the "Expert Email" field.
3. Provide the institution where the expert is affiliated in the "Institution" field.
4. Specify the expert's current position or academic title in the "Position/Title" field.
5. Identify the expert's primary area of research or field of expertise in the "Area of Expertise" field.
6. Explain the main topic or focus of the interview in the "Purpose of Interview" field.
7. Choose the preferred interview format (in-person, video conference, phone call, email exchange, or written responses) in the "Preferred Interview Format" field.
8. Specify the available times for the interview in the "Available Times" field.
9. Choose the expected duration of the interview from the options provided in the "Expected Duration" field.
10. Indicate your consent to recording the interview in the "Consent to Recording" field.
11. Specify your consent to publication of your responses, either fully or with editorial review, in the "Consent to Publication" field.
12. Choose the desired level of confidentiality for the interview in the "Confidentiality Level" field.
13. If applicable, provide any special accommodations required for the interview in the "Special Accommodations" field.
14. If you have been interviewed about this topic before, indicate the nature of your previous experience in the "Previous Interviews" field.
15. If you want to highlight any key points to cover during the interview, provide them in the "Key Points to Cover" field.
16. Finally, specify your preferred method of contact for scheduling the interview in the "Contact Preference" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expert Name** (`expert_name`, text, required): Fill out the full name of the academic expert being interviewed.
* **Expert Email** (`expert_email`, email, required): Enter the expert's professional email address for scheduling purposes.
* **Institution** (`expert_institution`, text, required): Provide the university or organization where the expert is affiliated.
* **Position/Title** (`expert_position`, text, required): Specify the expert’s current position or academic title.
* **Area of Expertise** (`research_area`, text, required): Identify the expert’s primary area of research or field of expertise.
* **Purpose of Interview** (`interview_purpose`, text, required): Explain the main topic or focus of the interview.
* **Preferred Interview Format** (`interview_format`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred format for the interview (in-person, video conference, phone call, email exchange, or written responses).
* **Available Times** (`available_times`, text, required): Specify the available times for the interview.
* **Expected Duration** (`interview_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the expected duration of the interview (15-30 minutes, 30-45 minutes, 45-60 minutes, 60-90 minutes, or over 90 minutes).
* **Consent to Recording** (`consent_to_recording`, select_one, required): Indicate your consent to recording the interview (yes, I consent, yes, with restrictions, or no, I do not consent).
* **Consent to Publication** (`consent_to_publication`, select_one, required): Specify your consent to publication (yes, I consent, yes, with editorial review, no, not for publication, or conditional on anonymity).
* **Confidentiality Level** (`confidentiality_level`, select_one, required): Choose the desired level of confidentiality (public, academic community only, limited distribution, or strict confidentiality).
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any special accommodations required for the interview.
* **Previous Interviews** (`previous_interviews`, select_one, optional): If you have been interviewed about this topic before, indicate the nature of your previous experience (yes, recently, yes, but some time ago, no, this is new, or not sure).
* **Key Points to Cover** (`key_points_to_cover`, text, optional): If you want to highlight any key points to cover during the interview, provide them here.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_one, required): Specify your preferred method of contact for scheduling the interview (email, phone, video call, text message, or other).

## Tips
