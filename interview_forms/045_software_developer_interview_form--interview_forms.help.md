# Software Developer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Developer Interview Form is a collection of questions and fields to help us better understand your qualifications and interests for the software developer position. Please fill in the form accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and contact information, including your email address and phone number.
2.  If you have a LinkedIn profile, GitHub profile, or a resume, please share the link with us.
3.  List the programming languages, frameworks, and tools you are proficient in.
4.  Specify the operating systems you have experience with.
5.  Share your level of experience in years.
6.  Indicate your availability for the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (text): Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text): Provide your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **LinkedIn Profile** (text): If you have a LinkedIn profile, share the link with us so we can review it.
* **Resume** (text): Share your resume link, if you have one.
* **GitHub Profile** (text): If you have a GitHub profile, share the link with us so we can review it.
* **Programming Languages** (text): List the programming languages you have experience with, separated by commas (e.g., Java, Python, C++, etc.).
* **Frameworks and Tools** (text): List the frameworks and tools you have experience with, separated by commas (e.g., React, Angular, Docker, etc.).
* **Operating Systems** (text): List the operating systems you have experience with, separated by commas (e.g., Windows, Linux, Mac, etc.).
* **Experience Years** (number): Enter the number of years you have experience as a software developer.
* **Availability** (select one): Choose how you are available for an interview, available, not available, or undetermined.
