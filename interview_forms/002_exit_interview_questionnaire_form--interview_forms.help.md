# exit_interview_questionnaire_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from employees about their experiences, reasons for departure, and feedback about the company. This data will help us understand the reasons for employee departures and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the "Reason for Departing" and "Feedback" fields.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4. Use the "Contact Information" page to provide your email address and phone number.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Information (page 1)**: Provide general information about your employer, such as company name and industry.
* **Position**: Describe your role and position within the company.
* **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor.
* **Department**: Enter the department you were a part of.
* **Reason for Departing**: Explain why you are leaving the company. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Feedback**: Share any feedback or comments you have about your experience with the company.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, enter them here.
* **Contact Information (page 2)**: Provide your email address and phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
* Submit the form once you have completed all pages.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
