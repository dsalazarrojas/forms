# Special Education Teacher Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a Special Education Teacher's background, experience, and qualifications for hiring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your general information (e.g., name, contact details).
2. Share your education background.
3. List any teaching certifications you hold (check the relevant boxes).
4. Describe your special education experience.
5. Select your relevant work history (number of years).
6. Identify your available job references (yes/no preference).
7. Describe any other relevant experience you have.
8. Upload your resume for review (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, `false`): Provide your basic contact information and other relevant details.
- **Education Background** (`Education Background`, `text`, `false`): Share your educational background and qualifications.
- **Teaching Certifications** (`Taching Certifications`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select any teaching certifications you hold, such as American Sign Language (ASL), Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA), and Certified Special Education Teacher.
- **Special Education Experience** (`Special Education Experience`, `text`, `false`): Describe your experience working with special education students.
- **Work History** (`Work History`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select the number of years you have worked in special education.
- **Job References** (`Job References`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Identify your available job references (yes/available, no/unavailable, no preference).
- **Other Experience** (`Other Experience`, `text`, `false`): Share any other relevant experience you have.
- **Resume Upload** (`Resume Upload`, `text`, `false`): Upload your resume for review (optional).
