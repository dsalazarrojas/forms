# Retail Sales Assistant Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about candidates applying for a retail sales assistant position. It helps us assess their background and qualifications for the role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the **candidates_name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **candidates_phone** field.
4. Indicate your availability to work shifts by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **shifts** field.
5. Enter your availability start date in the **availability_start** field.
6. Enter your availability end date in the **availability_end** field.
7. Indicate if you have **retail experience** by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **retail_experience** field.
8. Indicate if you have **sales experience** by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **sales_experience** field.
9. Indicate if you are skilled in **customer service** by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **customer_service** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **candidates_name**: Enter your full name.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **candidates_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **shifts**: Indicate if you are available to work shifts by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
* **availability_start**: Enter your availability start date.
* **availability_end**: Enter your availability end date.
* **retail_experience**: Indicate if you have retail experience by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
* **sales_experience**: Indicate if you have sales experience by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
* **customer_service**: Indicate if you are skilled in customer service by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
