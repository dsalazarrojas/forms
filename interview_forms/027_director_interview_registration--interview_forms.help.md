# director_interview_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from candidates during the director interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Candidate Details** fields with the candidate's personal information.
2. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of the interview.
3. Indicate if there will be **Interviewers** present during the interview.
4. Upload your **CV/Portfolio** if required.
5. Click on the **Submit** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details**: Enter the candidate's name, contact information, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Candidate Details 2**: Enter additional details about the candidate, if required.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the interview in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the interview in 12-hour clock format (e.g., 10:00 AM).
* **Interviewers**: Select 'Yes' if there will be interviewers present during the interview, or 'No' otherwise.
* **CV/Portfolio Uploader**: Upload your CV or portfolio if it's required to be submitted with the form.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form after filling in all the required fields.
