# job_interview_appointment_checklist_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for job interviews, ensuring that all necessary details are captured in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the candidate's details in the "candidate info" section.
3. Provide contact details for the candidate in the "contact details" section.
4. Enter the interview details, including the job title, description, requirements, and qualifications.
5. Select the interviewers and job type.
6. Add any additional notes or comments as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Info (candidate info)**: Enter the candidate's name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details (contact details)**: Enter the candidate's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Contact Person (contact person)**: Enter the name and title of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (contact phone)**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (contact email)**: Enter the contact email address.
* **Contact Note (contact note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the contact person.
* **Interview Details (interview details)**: Enter the interview date, time, and location.
* **Job Title (job title)**: Enter the title of the job being interviewed for.
* **Job Description (job description)**: Enter a brief description of the job.
* **Job Requirements (job requirements)**: Enter the necessary requirements for the job.
* **Job Qualifications (job qualifications)**: Enter the necessary qualifications for the job.
* **References (job references)**: Enter any relevant references related to the job.
* **Interviewers (interviewers)**: Select the interviewers for the job.
* **Other Interviewers (other interviewers)**: Select any additional interviewers.
* **Job Type (job type)**: Select the type of job (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Interview Notes (interview notes)**: Add any additional notes or comments about the interview.
* **Job Status (job status)**: Select the current status of the job.
* **Interview Date (interview date)**: Enter the date of the interview.
* **Interview Time (interview time)**: Enter the time of the interview.
* **Interview Location (interview location)**: Enter the location of the interview.
* **Interview Duration (interview duration)**: Enter the expected duration of the interview.
* **Other (other)**: Add any additional information not covered in other fields.
