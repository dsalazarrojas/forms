# Embassy Official Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Embassy Official Interview Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information from applicants for official purposes. This form is intended to capture essential details about the applicant's personal and professional background, as well as their job history and visa application status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each section carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. Make sure to select the correct options from the drop-down menus, if applicable.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant Information
* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, `text`, required): Enter your personal details, including your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your personal background and qualifications.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your current address.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `text`, required): Describe your current employment status.

### Work History
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required): List your previous work experience, including job titles and dates of employment.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required): Describe your educational background and qualifications.
* **Language Skills** (`language_skills`, `text`, required): List your language skills and proficiency levels.
* **Visa Application** (`visa_application`, `text`, required): Describe your current visa application status.
* **Visa Status** (`visa_status`, `text`, required): Enter your current visa status.
* **Visa Sponsor** (`visa_sponsor`, `text`, required): Identify your visa sponsor.
* **Work Authorization** (`work_authorization`, `text`, required): Explain your work authorization status.
* **Work Authorization Type** (`work_authorization_type`, `text`, required): Specify your work authorization type.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `text`, required): Enter your work location.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, `time`, required): Enter your work hours.
* **Work Type** (`work_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of work you do (Part-time, Full-time, Freelance).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required): Describe your job duties and responsibilities.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `text`, required): Choose a category for your job.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter your job start date.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter your job end date.
* **Salary Range** (`salary_range`, `text`, required): Enter your salary range.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the status of your job (Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Add any additional comments or information.

Note: If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
