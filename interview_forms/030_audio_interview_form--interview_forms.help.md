# Audio Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Audio Interview Form is designed to collect information about an audio interview, including the interviewee's name, the interviewer's name, the date of the interview, the topic or subject discussed, and other relevant details such as consent and technical notes. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and documented for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the interviewee's full name in the "Interviewee Full Name" field.
2. Enter the interviewer's name in the "Interviewer Name" field.
3. Select the date of the interview in the "Date of Interview" field.
4. Specify the interview subject or topic in the "Interview Subject or Topic" field.
5. In the "Do you consent to this interview being recorded?" field, select whether the interview is recorded only or also recorded with video.
6. For the "Permission to Publish/Broadcast" field, choose whether the interview can be fully published, requires review, or is private.
7. In the "Primary Questions Asked" field, list the questions that were asked during the interview for indexing purposes.
8. If necessary, provide a brief summary of the key takeaways or main points of the interview in the "Key Takeaways or Summary" field.
9. In the "Technical Notes" section, add any relevant technical notes about the recording.
10. Enter the filename of the recording in the "Recording Filename Reference" field.
11. Indicate whether a full transcript of the interview is needed in the "Is a full transcript needed?" field.
12. Evaluate the audio quality by selecting from the options provided in the "Audio Quality Check" field.
13. Sign and date the form with your name and title in the "Interviewer Signature (Type Name)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interviewee Full Name (Text, Required)**: Please enter the full name of the person being interviewed.
* **Interviewer Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as the interviewer.
* **Date of Interview (Date, Required)**: Select the date when the interview was conducted.
* **Interview Subject or Topic (Text, Required)**: Briefly describe the subject or topic of the interview.
* **Do you consent to this interview being recorded? (Select One, Required)**: Choose whether the interview is recorded only or also recorded with video.
* **Permission to Publish/Broadcast (Select One, Required)**: Choose whether the interview can be fully published, requires review, or is private.
* **Primary Questions Asked (Text, Optional)**: List the questions asked during the interview for indexing purposes.
* **Key Takeaways or Summary (Text, Required)**: Provide a brief summary of the main points or takeaways from the interview.
* **Technical Notes (Note, Optional)**: Add any relevant technical notes about the recording.
* **Recording Filename Reference (Text, Required)**: Enter the filename of the recording.
* **Is a full transcript needed? (Select One, Required)**: Indicate whether a full transcript of the interview is required.
* **Audio Quality Check (Select One, Required)**: Evaluate the audio quality of the recording.
* **Interviewer Signature (Type Name) (Text, Required)**: Sign and date the form with your name and title.
