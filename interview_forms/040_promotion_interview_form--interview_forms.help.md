# Promotion Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Promotion Interview Form is used to collect information about a candidate's promotion interview, ensuring consistency and accuracy in our promotion evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out each field with the corresponding information about the candidate's promotion interview.
3. Check the required fields to ensure they are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Promotion Interview Form First Page (1)**: Start by selecting the first page of the form.
* **Candidate Name (2)**: Enter the candidate's full name.
* **Reporting Manager (3)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if the candidate has a reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Department (4)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if the reporting manager has a department.
* **Candidate Job Title (5)**: Enter the candidate's job title.
* **Job Family ID (6)**: Enter the job family ID.
* **Job Family (7)**: Enter the job family name.
* **Reporting Line Manager ID (8)**: Enter the reporting line manager ID.
* **Reporting Line Manager (9)**: Enter the reporting line manager's name.
* **Candidate Manager (10)**: Enter the candidate's manager's name.
* **Candidate Job Title History (11)**: Enter any previous job titles held by the candidate.
* **Reporting Manager Title History (12)**: Enter any previous titles held by the reporting manager.
* **Performance Review (13)**: Enter the date of the performance review.
* **Performance Rating (14)**: Select the performance rating (Good or Fair).
* **Performance Rating Scale (15)**: Select the performance rating scale (Good or Fair).
* **Evaluation Date (16)**: Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Score (17)**: Enter the evaluation score.
* **Evaluation Type (18)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if the evaluation type is relevant.
* **Candidate Salary (19)**: Enter the candidate's salary.
* **Candidate Salary Currency (20)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if the candidate's salary is in a specific currency.
* **Candidate Currency Symbol (21)**: Enter the candidate's currency symbol.
* **Promotion Decision Date (22)**: Enter the date of the promotion decision.
* **Promotion Decision Date Time (23)**: Enter the time of the promotion decision.
* **Promotion Decision Date Time Comment (24)**: Enter any comments about the promotion decision date and time.
* **Promotion Decision (25)**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' if the promotion decision was made.
