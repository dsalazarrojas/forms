# Online Magazine Content Writer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Magazine Content Writer Interview Form is designed to collect applications from writers interested in writing for our online magazine. Please fill out this form to provide us with your details and writing experience.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Provide Your Information

1. **Writer Application** (`writer_application`): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself and how you heard about our magazine.
2. **Writer Name** (`writer_name`): Enter your full name as you would like it to be published in our magazine.
3. **Writer Email** (`writer_email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
4. **Writer Phone** (`writer_phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
5. **Sample Link** (`sample_link`): Provide a link to one of your writing samples or a portfolio that showcases your writing style.
6. **Resume Attachment** (`resume_attachment`): Upload your resume to provide more information about your experience and background.
7. **Cover Letter Attachment** (`cover_letter_attachment`): Upload a cover letter to express your interest in writing for our magazine and your qualifications.
8. **Writer Experience** (`writer_experience`): Describe your experience as a writer, including any relevant courses, training, or certifications.
9. **Writer Education** (`writer_education`): Provide your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
10. **Writer Specialties** (`writer_specialties`): List any areas of specialization, such as fiction, non-fiction, or copywriting.

### Step 3: Date and Category

1. **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of your availability to start writing for us.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out each field accurately and honestly.
* You can attach multiple files for your resume and cover letter if needed.
* Review and compare samples are an essential part of our evaluation process, so make sure to answer these questions carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in writing for our online magazine, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
