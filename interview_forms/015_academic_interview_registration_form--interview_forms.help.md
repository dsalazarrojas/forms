# Academic Interview Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather important information from applicants for academic interviews. The data collected will be used to schedule and coordinate the interview.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the preferred options for the type of interview, interview format, and interview duration.
4. If you have any additional information or questions for the interviewers, provide them in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Academic Interview Registration**: A brief introduction to the form.
* **Applicant Name**: Your full name as it appears on official documents (\*)
* **Email Address**: Your contact email for interview coordination (\*)
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for interview scheduling (\*)
* **Position or Role**: The position or role you are being interviewed for (\*)
* **Department or Unit**: The department or academic unit conducting the interview (\*)
* **Type of Interview**: Select the type of interview (e.g., Job, Admission, Research, Evaluation, Informational, or Other)
* **Interview Format**: Choose the format of the interview (e.g., In-person, Video conference, Phone call, Panel, Multiple rounds, or Other)
* **Preferred Dates**: Select the preferred dates for the interview
* **Preferred Time Slots**: Choose the preferred time slots for the interview (optional)
* **Interview Duration Preference**: Select the desired duration of the interview (e.g., 15-30 minutes, 30-45 minutes, 45-60 minutes, 60-90 minutes, or Over 90 minutes)
* **Availability Notes**: Provide any special considerations for scheduling
* **Preparation Materials Needed**: Indicate if you require any materials to be sent in advance
* **Special Accommodations**: Describe any special accommodations required during the interview
* **Interview Experience Level**: Choose your level of experience with this type of interview (optional)
* **Questions for Interviewers**: Provide specific questions you'd like to ask during the interview
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other information relevant to your interview
