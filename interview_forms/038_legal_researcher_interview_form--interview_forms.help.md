# Legal Researcher Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Researcher Interview Form is a tool used to gather information from potential legal researchers about their background, experience, and availability. The form helps us understand the qualifications and fit of candidates for various legal research positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your Candidate Details:
	* Fill in your name and any other relevant details in the Candidate Details field.
2.  Share your research experience:
	* Enter the number of years you have had legal research experience.
3.  Select your research skills:
	* Choose the skills you have used in your previous roles (Legal Research, Document Review, Document Analysis).
4.  Share your education background:
	* Enter any relevant details about your education background.
5.  Specify your degree in law:
	* Choose the degree you hold in law (LL.B, LLM, LLM and Barrister, LL.B and Barrister, or Other).
6.  Indicate your employment status:
	* Choose your current employment status (Employed, Freelance, Self-Employed, Retired, or Unemployed).
7.  Enter your available start date:
	* If you are available to start immediately, enter the current date or a future date. If you are not available to start yet, indicate when you will be available.
8.  Provide your email address:
	* Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
9.  Enter your phone number:
	* Enter your phone number, including the country code and area code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (`Candidate Details`, text): 
	+ Enter your name and any other details about yourself.
* **Candidate Experience** (`years of legal research experience`, number): 
	+ Enter the number of years you have had legal research experience.
* **Research Skills** (`select multiple`): 
	+ Select the skills you have used in your previous roles. Choose from Legal Research, Document Review, and Document Analysis.
* **Education Background** (`Education Background`, text): 
	+ Enter any details about your education background.
* **Degree in Law** (`select one`): 
	+ Choose the degree you hold in law (LL.B, LLM, LLM and Barrister, LL.B and Barrister, or Other).
* **Employment Status** (`select one`): 
	+ Choose your current employment status (Employed, Freelance, Self-Employed, Retired, or Unemployed).
* **Available to Start** (`date`): 
	+ If available, enter the current date or a future date. If not available, indicate when you will be available.
* **Email** (`email`, email): 
	+ Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text): 
	+ Enter your phone number, including the country code and area code.

## Tips

* Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
* If you have any supporting documentation or references, please attach them to your application.
