# Software Developer Project Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your background, experience, and skills as a software developer for a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as possible, but only for the requested information.
3. Review your form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email**: Your professional email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone**: Your work phone number or a number where you can be reached.
* **Project**: The name of the project you are applying for or interested in.
* **Role**: Your expected role in the project (e.g., developer, team lead, etc.).
* **Duration**: The expected duration of the project in months or years.
* **Date**: The date you started and ended (if applicable) working on the project.
* **Programming Languages**: List the programming languages you are proficient in (e.g., Java, Python, C++).
* **Frameworks and Libraries**: List any relevant frameworks or libraries you have experience with (e.g., React, Node.js, TensorFlow).
* **Databases**: List the databases you have experience with (e.g., MySQL, MongoDB, PostgreSQL).
* **Operating Systems**: List the operating systems you are proficient in (e.g., Windows, Linux, macOS).
* **Cloud Services**: List any cloud services you have experience with (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).
