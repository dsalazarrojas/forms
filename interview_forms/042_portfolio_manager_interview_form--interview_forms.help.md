# portfolio_manager_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential candidates for a portfolio management position. It consists of three sections: Candidate Information, Contact Details, and Education Background.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by providing the candidate's personal details in the "Candidate Information" section.
- Next, provide the candidate's contact information in the "Contact Details" section.
- Finally, describe the candidate's education background in the "Education Background" section.
- Make sure to provide all required information for each section as indicated by the required status of each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, **Required: False**): This section is for gathering basic information about the candidate.
* **Contact Details** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, **Required: False**): Provide the candidate's contact information, including their email address and phone number.
* **Education Background** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, **Required: False**): Describe the candidate's educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
- Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the email address provided at the top of this help guide.
