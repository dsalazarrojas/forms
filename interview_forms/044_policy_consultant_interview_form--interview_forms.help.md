# Policy Consultant Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from policy consultants during the interview process. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your *Full Name* in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your *Email* address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your *Phone* number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter the *Contact Time* you are available to be contacted in the "Contact Time" field.
5. Select your *Preferred Contact Method* from the dropdown list.
6. Enter your *Job Title* in the "Job Title" field.
7. Select your *Employment Status* from the dropdown list.
8. Enter your *Years of Experience* in the "Years of Experience" field.
9. Enter your *Highest Degree* obtained in the "Highest Degree" field.
10. Enter the name of your *University* in the "University" field.
11. Enter your *Graduation Date* in the "Graduation Date" field.
12. Enter your *First Name* in the "First Name" field.
13. Enter your *Last Name* in the "Last Name" field.
14. If you have any *Additional Comments*, you can enter them in the "Additional comments" field.
15. Finally, select *I have read and understand the terms and conditions* from the dropdown list and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Time** (`contact_time`, time, required): Enter the time you are available to be contacted during the day.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_communication`, select_one, required): Select how you prefer to be contacted (Yes or No).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your employment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working as a policy consultant.
* **Highest Degree** (`highest_degree`, text, required): Enter the highest degree you have obtained.
* **University** (`university`, text, required): Enter the name of your university.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you graduated.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, note, optional): This field is for uploading any relevant documents you may have.
* **I have read and understand the terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Select Yes to indicate that you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, text, optional): This field is for submitting the form.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you may have.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields to complete the form accurately.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
