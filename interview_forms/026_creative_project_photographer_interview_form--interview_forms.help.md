# Creative Project Photographer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather essential information from photographers interested in participating in a creative project. It is used to evaluate their suitability for the project and to ensure they have a clear understanding of the project's guidelines and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the submission guidelines before filling out this form to ensure you understand the project's requirements and expectations.
2.  Complete each field thoroughly and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
3.  Save your progress frequently to avoid losing any entered information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Application (Text)**: Enter a brief introduction about yourself, including your name, contact information, and a short description of your experience and qualifications.
* **Review Portfolio (Text)**: Describe your portfolio, including any relevant projects or works that showcase your photography skills and experience.
* **Review Criteria (Select Multiple)**: Choose the criteria that best describe what you value most in your photography, such as artistic value, technical skill, creativity, or relevant experience.
* **Notes (Note)**: Add any additional comments or thoughts that you feel are relevant to your application.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your professional email address where we can contact you for further discussions or project details.
* **Phone (Text)**: Provide your phone number where we can contact you for further discussions or project details.
* **Submission Agreement (Select One)**: Choose whether you have read and agreed to the submission guidelines, or not.
