# Nonprofit Fundraising Manager Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from nonprofit fundraising managers about their experience, skills, and challenges, and to help identify potential candidates for a position that requires strong fundraising and management skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your name and contact information.
2. Choose your years of experience.
3. Indicate your highest level of education.
4. Select the skills you have experience with.
5. Choose your preferred method of communication.
6. List the types of fundraising campaigns you are familiar with.
7. Indicate who your primary contact person is.
8. Choose how often you would like to be contacted for communication.
9. (Optional) Check any additional challenges you face in your role.
10. Provide any specific measures of success you have achieved.
10. (Optional) Name your favorite resource for nonprofit fundraising.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information**: This field is for your name and contact information.
* **Years of Experience**: This field asks for your years of experience in nonprofit fundraising management.
* **Education**: This field asks for your highest level of education completed.
* **Skills**: This field is for selecting the skills you have experience with, such as grant writing, event planning, and volunteer management.
* **Preferred Communication**: This field asks for your preferred method of communication, such as phone, email, or in-person meeting.
* **Fundraising Campaigns**: This field is for selecting the types of fundraising campaigns you are familiar with, such as awareness, annual, and social media campaigns.
* **Primary Contact**: This field is for indicating who your primary contact person is, such as the CEO or Development Director.
* **Communication Frequency**: This field asks for how often you would like to be contacted for communication, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.
* **Challenges**: This field is for checking any additional challenges you face in your role, such as budgeting, communication, or donor engagement.
* **Success Measures**: This field is for providing any specific measures of success you have achieved in your role.
* **Favorite Resource**: This field is for naming your favorite resource for nonprofit fundraising, such as a book or website.
* 
Note: All fields are required unless otherwise specified. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the best possible outcome for your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form is designed to help identify potential candidates for a position, so please be honest about your experience and skills.
