# Meteorologist Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meteorologist Interview Form is designed to collect information from candidates for a meteorologist position. This form helps interviewers gather necessary details about each candidate, including their contact information, qualifications, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Candidate Name**, which should be your full name.
2. Enter your **Job Title**, if applicable.
3. Provide your **Contact Email Address** and **Contact Phone Number** to ensure we can get in touch with you for further discussions.
4. Select whether you are an **Interviewer** or not (this is an optional field).
5. You can add any notes or questions you have for the interviewer in the **Questions** field.
6. If applicable, share your **Qualifications**, such as education and skills.
7. Describe your **Experience** relevant to the position.
8. If you have a preferred **Salary Range**, please specify it.
9. Provide your available start date and end date in the **Available Start Date** and **Available End Date** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
  - **Job Title** (2, text, optional): Enter your job title, if applicable.
  - **Contact Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address where we can reach you.
  - **Contact Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
  - **Date of Interview** (5, date, optional): Enter the date of the interview, if applicable.
  - **Start Time** (6, time, required): Enter the start time of the interview.
  - **End Time** (7, time, optional): Enter the end time of the interview, if applicable.
  - **Interviewers** (8, select_multiple, required): Select whether you are an interviewer or not.
  - **Questions** (9, note, optional): Add any questions or notes for the interviewer.
  - **Qualifications** (10, text, optional): Describe your education, skills, or qualifications.
  - **Experience** (11, text, optional): Share your relevant experience.
  - **Salary Range** (12, number, optional): Specify your expected salary range.
  - **Available Start Date** (13, date, optional): Enter your available start date.
  - **Available End Date** (14, date, optional): Enter your available end date.

Note: The fields marked as optional should only be filled if the information is relevant to your application.
