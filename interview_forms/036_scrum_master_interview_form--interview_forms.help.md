# scrum_master_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The scrum_master_interview_form is designed to collect information about an individual's background and experience as a Scrum Master. This form helps to evaluate a candidate's suitability for a Scrum Master role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Choose the correct options for each field based on your experience and background.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Experience** (2/ Yes/No): This field asks if you have experience working as a Scrum Master. Answer 'Yes' if you have prior experience in this role.
* **Scrum Values** (3, Scrum Values/Yes/No): This field asks if you value certain principles in the Scrum framework. Answer 'Yes' for each value you adhere to.
* **Leadership** (4, Leadership/Yes/No): This field asks if you have leadership experience or aspire to be a leader. Answer 'Yes' if you have or aspire to have leadership roles.
* **Problem Solving** (5, Problem Solving/Yes/No): This field asks if you have experience solving problems effectively. Answer 'Yes' if you have experience solving problems in a team setting.
* **Soft Skills** (6, Soft Skills/Yes/No): This field asks about your soft skills, such as communication, teamwork, or time management. Answer 'Yes' for each skill you possess.
* **Personal Values** (7, Personal Values/Yes/No): This field asks about your personal values that align with the Scrum values. Answer 'Yes' for each value you uphold.
* **Availability** (8, Availability/Yes/No): This field asks about your work availability. Answer 'Yes' if you are available to work as a Scrum Master.
* **Education Background** (9, Education Background/Yes/No): This field asks if you have a relevant educational background. Answer 'Yes' if you have a degree or training in a related field.
* **Certification** (10, Certification/Yes/No): This field asks if you have a relevant certification. Answer 'Yes' if you have a certification in a related field.

## Tips

* Answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for clarification.
* This form should take approximately 10-15 minutes to complete.

Note: The form will be reviewed and discussed during the interview process. Please be prepared to elaborate on your answers during the interview.
