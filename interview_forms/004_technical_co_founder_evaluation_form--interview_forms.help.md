# Technical Co-Founder Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills and characteristics of potential co-founders for a tech project or startup.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please start by filling out the Introduction page, which serves as a background information page.
2. On the next page, provide your candidate's name and email address.
3. Then, enter the name of the company or organization they represent.
4. Next, describe the project or venture they are applying for.
5. For the skills section, rate the candidate's technical ability, leadership ability, communication skills, problem-solving skills, time management, adaptability, and team fit using the provided scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the co-founder candidate.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the co-founder's email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company or organization the candidate represents.
* **Project** (`project_description`, text, required): Describe the project or venture the candidate is applying for.
* **Technical** (`technical_ability`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's technical ability using the provided scale: Strong, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Answer.
* **Leadership** (`leadership_ability`, select_multiple, required): Rate the candidate's leadership ability using the provided scale: Strong, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Answer.
* **Communication** (`communication_skills`, text, required): Describe the candidate's communication skills.
* **Problem** (`problem_solving`, text, required): Describe the candidate's problem-solving skills.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one, required): Rate the candidate's time management using the provided scale: Strong, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Answer.
* **Adaptability** (`adaptability`, select_multiple, required): Rate the candidate's adaptability using the provided scale: Strong, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Answer.
* **Team Fit** (`fit_with_team`, select_multiple, required): Rate the candidate's fit with the team using the provided scale: Strong, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Answer.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Please provide as much detail as possible for each field.
* If a question doesn't apply to you, please select "No Answer" and provide a brief explanation.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it with anyone outside our organization.
* You are free to use, modify, and distribute this form as needed.
