# retail_supplier_interview_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about retail suppliers, specifically their contact details, store information, supplier information, and product information. The form helps us understand the suppliers' capabilities and products they can offer to our company. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your actual situation.
2. Make sure to answer all fields that are marked as "required".
3. Click "Submit" to finish and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Choose the relevant options for our contact with your company. 
  * "Yes" - You have contact with our company.
  * "No" - No contact with our company.
* **store_information**: Choose the relevant option for your store.
  * "Yes" - Your store is relevant to our business.
  * "No" - Your store is not relevant.
* **supplier_information**: Choose the relevant options about our supplier information.
  * "Yes" - You have supplier information that we can use.
  * "No" - No supplier information available.
* **product_information**: Choose the relevant options for your products. 
  * "Yes" - You have products that we can use.
  * "No" - No products available.
