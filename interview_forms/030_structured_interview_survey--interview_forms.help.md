# Structured Interview Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect structured interview data from candidates during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form as instructed, providing all required information.
2. The fields are self-explanatory, so please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.
3. If you are unsure about a field, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant department.

### 1. **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional)
Enter the name of the candidate being interviewed.
