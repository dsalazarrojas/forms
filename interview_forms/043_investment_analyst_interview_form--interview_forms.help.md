# Investment Analyst Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to capture information and feedback from Investment Analyst interviews, including candidate details, evaluation, ratings, and comments. The form is intended to aid in the evaluation and assessment of investment analysts during the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Investment Analyst's name.
2. Enter the name of the company they represent or work for.
3. Enter the job title of the Investment Analyst.
4. Enter the contact number of the Investment Analyst.
5. Enter the email address of the Investment Analyst.
6. Provide the date of the interview.
7. Enter the evaluation comments or feedback on the candidate.
8. Select all applicable behavioral questions that you found the candidate to exhibit.
9. If applicable, select all technical questions that the candidate was able to answer correctly.
10. Enter any additional comments about the candidate.
11. Provide your rating on the candidate's performance on a rating scale.
12. Enter your Interviewer ID, so we can contact you for further information.
13. Enter any comments about the interviewee.
14. Enter any comments about the interview.
15. Enter the rating score on the candidate's behavioral performance.
16. Enter the rating score on the candidate's technical performance.
17. Enter the overall rating score on the candidate.
18. Enter the time taken for the interview.
19. Enter the date of the interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investment Analyst Name** (`investment_analyst_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the Investment Analyst who was interviewed.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the company represented by the Investment Analyst.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, `required`): Enter the job title of the Investment Analyst.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, `required`): Enter the contact number of the Investment Analyst.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, `required`): Enter the email address of the Investment Analyst.
* **Date Of Interview** (`date_of_interview`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the interview in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, `text`, `required`): Provide any comments or feedback on the candidate's performance.
* **Behavioral Questions** (`behavioral_questions`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all applicable behavioral questions that the candidate demonstrated during the interview.
* **Technical Questions** (`technical_questions`, `select_multiple`, `required`): If applicable, select all technical questions that the candidate was able to answer correctly.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, `required`): Enter any additional comments about the candidate's performance.
* **Ratings** (`ratings`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select your rating on the candidate's performance on a rating scale.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select all recommendations for the candidate.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `required`): Enter any additional comments about the candidate.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the rating scale for the candidate's performance.
* **Interviewer ID** (`interviewer_id`, `text`, `required`): Enter your Interviewer ID so we can contact you for further information.
* **Comments On Interviewer** (`comments_on_interviewer`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any comments about the interviewer.
* **Comments On Interviewee** (`comments_on_interviewee`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any comments about the interviewee.
* **Rating** (`rating`, `select_one`, `required`): Enter your rating on the candidate's performance on a rating scale.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, `required`): Enter the time taken for the interview.
* **Date Interviewed** (`date_interviewed`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of the interview.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any additional comments about the interview.
* **Evaluation Form** (`evaluation_form`, `text`, `required`): Enter the overall evaluation form.
* **Behavioral Score** (`behavioral_score`, `number`, `required`): Enter the rating score on the candidate's behavioral performance.
* **Technical Score** (`technical_score`, `number`, `required`): Enter the rating score on the candidate's technical performance.
* **Overall Score** (`overall_score`, `number`, `required`): Enter the overall rating score on the candidate.
