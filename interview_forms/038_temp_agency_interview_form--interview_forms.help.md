# Temp Agency Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from candidates for temporary agency positions, allowing for a thorough and efficient screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you're providing the most relevant details.
3. If you're unsure about a field, refer back to this guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`id: 2`, `name: job_title`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the title of the job you're applying for.
* **Job Description** (`id: 3`, `name: job_description`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Provide a brief description of the job, including its responsibilities and requirements.
* **Job Reference** (`id: 4`, `name: job_reference`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter a reference number or ID for the job (if applicable).
* **Job Status** (`id: 5`, `name: job_status`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the status of the job: Active, Inactive, or Closed.
* **Job Type** (`id: 6`, `name: job_type`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: true`): Choose the type of job: Full-Time, Part-Time, or Contract.
* **Job Category** (`id: 7`, `name: job_category`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the category of the job: Sales, Marketing, or Engineering.
* **Job Skills** (`id: 8`, `name: job_skills`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any specific skills required for the job (optional).
* **Candidate Name** (`id: 9`, `name: candidate_name`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the candidate applying for the job.
* **Candidate Email** (`id: 10`, `name: candidate_email`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the candidate's email address (if applicable).
* **Candidate Phone** (`id: 11`, `name: candidate_phone`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the candidate's phone number (if applicable).
* **Candidate Availability** (`id: 12`, `name: candidate_availability`, `type: date`, `required: true`): Enter the candidate's availability for the job (in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD').
* **Availability** (`id: 13`, `name: candidate_availability_2`, `type: time`, `required: false`): Enter the desired availability for the job (in the format 'HH:MM' or 'HH:MM AM/PM') (optional).
* **Role Fitting** (`id: 14`, `name: role_fitting`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter how the candidate fits the job's requirements.
* **Candidate Fit** (`id: 15`, `name: candidate_fit`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select whether the candidate is a good fit for the job: True or False.
* **Screening Outcome** (`id: 16`, `name: screening_outcome`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the outcome of the screening process: True or False.
* **Notes** (`id: 17`, `name: notes`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Add any additional notes about the candidate or job (optional).
* **Screening Comments** (`id: 18`, `name: screening_comments`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any comments about the screening process (optional).
* **Screening Outcome Comments** (`id: 19`, `name: screening_outcome_comments`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter any comments about the screening outcome (optional).
* **Screening Outcome Notes** (`id: 20`, `name: screening_outcome_notes`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Add any additional notes about the screening outcome (optional).
* **Screening Status** (`id: 21`, `name: screening_status`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the status of the screening process: Open, Screened, or Declined.
* **Screening Outcome 2** (`id: 22`, `name: screening_outcome_2`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the outcome of the second screening: True or False.
* **Screening Outcome 3** (`id: 23`, `name: screening_outcome_3`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the outcome of the third screening: True or False.
* **Screening Outcome 4** (`id: 24`, `name: screening_outcome_4`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the outcome of the fourth screening: True or False.
* **Screening Outcome 5** (`id: 25`, `name: screening_outcome_5`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the outcome of the fifth screening: True or False.

## Tips

* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Use this form to gather information from candidates for temporary agency positions.
