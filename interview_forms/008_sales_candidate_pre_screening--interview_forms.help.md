# Sales Candidate Pre Screening - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from potential sales candidates during the pre-screening process. It gathers contact details, sales experience, and schedule interview options for further evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select all the relevant contact information: This includes your phone number, email, and preferred contact methods such as phone or email.
2. Choose your sales experience: Select the category that best describes your sales experience in the industry. This helps the evaluator get a better understanding of your background and skills.
3. Schedule an interview: Choose a time that suits you best for an interview.
4. Add any additional comments: You can provide any other information that you think is relevant for the screening process.
5. Enter your email and phone number: You need to provide a valid email and phone number to enable communication for the next steps in the process.
6. Specify the type of form you are submitting: This helps in case the form is not used for the intended purpose of Sales Candidate Pre Screening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Phone, Email, Phone number)**:
	+ Enter your valid phone number and email.
* **Sales Experience (Years of Experience)**:
	+ Select the category that best fits your sales experience in the industry.
* **Schedule Interview (Time)**:
	+ Choose a date and time that suits you best for the interview.
* **Additional Comments**:
	+ Enter any other relevant information that you think will help in the screening process.
* **Email (Valid Email Address)**:
	+ Enter your valid email to ensure we can contact you for the next steps in the process.
* **Phone (Valid Phone Number)**:
	+ Enter your valid phone number to enable easy communication.
* **LinkedIn Profile (Valid URL or Username)**:
	+ Enter your valid LinkedIn profile URL or username if you have one.
* **What is this form for (Form Type)**:
	+ Select the intended use of the form from the options provided.
* **Sales Candidate Pre Screening Form 2**:
	+ This field is currently not used; please do not fill this in.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Provide any additional comments that might be helpful for the evaluation process. 

Note: Please keep in mind that this is a help guide, not a formal instruction manual, if you need further clarification or have specific questions, please ask the form owner or support team.
