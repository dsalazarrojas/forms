# Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from employees who are leaving the company. It helps us understand your reasons for departure and gather feedback on how we can improve our working environment and processes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible. If you need help or have questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.

1. Enter your full name to help us identify you in our system.
2. Briefly explain your reason for leaving the company.
3. If you're moving on to another job, please enter your new job title and the name of your new company.
4. We're interested in knowing how you felt about your job while you were here. Choose "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied" from the options.
5. How would you rate your experience with our internal communication?
6. Please provide any feedback or suggestions for improvement you think we should know about.
7. Share any specific comments or observations about your time at the company.
8. Finally, provide the date you officially left the company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Reason for Departure**: Briefly explain why you're leaving the company. Be as honest and specific as possible.
* **New Job Title**: If you're moving on to another job, enter your new job title here.
* **New Company**: If you're moving on to another job, enter the name of your new company here.
* **Job Satisfaction**: Choose "Satisfied" if you're generally happy with your job, or "Dissatisfied" if you had issues.
* **Communication**: How would you rate your experience with our internal communication? Choose "Yes" or "No".
* **Feedback**: Any specific thoughts or suggestions you have about your time at the company.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Any ideas you have on how we can improve our working environment and processes.
* **Comments**: Any additional comments or observations you'd like to share about your time at the company.
* **Date of Departure**: Enter the date you officially left the company.
