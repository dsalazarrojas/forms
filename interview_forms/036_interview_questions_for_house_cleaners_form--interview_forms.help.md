# Interview Questions For House Cleaners Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interview Questions For House Cleaners Form is used to gather information from potential house cleaners who are interested in working as house cleaners. The form helps the hiring manager to assess the candidate's qualifications, availability, and preferences to find the best fit for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number.
2. Enter your Work Experience, Availability, and Notice Period.
3. Select your preferred Job Title, Available Shifts, and Job Types.
4. Specify your available days and time.
5. Choose your preferred language and emergency contact details.
6. Confirm your availability on weekends and holidays.
7. Rate your job satisfaction level.
8. Select your benefits preferences.
9. Add any additional comments about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number that we can contact you on.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the title of the house cleaning position you're applying for.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you're available to start work.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your years of experience in house cleaning.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the notice period you're expecting from your current employer.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the days you're available to work.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time you're available to work.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the languages you're proficient in.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact details of your emergency contact.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact details of the person to contact you through.
* **Available Shifts** (`available_shifts`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the shifts you're available to work.
* **Notice Period 2** (`notice_period_2`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the additional notice period you're expecting from your current employer.
* **Job Types** (`job_types`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the types of jobs you're interested in.
* **Work Schedule** (`work_schedule`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your work schedule.
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your expected salary.
* **Notice Period 3** (`notice_period_3`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the third notice period you're expecting from your current employer.
* **Available Hours** (`available_hours`, `number`, required/optional): Enter your available hours per week.
* **Available Weekends** (`available_weekends`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you're available to work on weekends.
* **Available Holidays** (`available_holidays`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you're available to work on holidays.
* **Job Satisfaction** (`job_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required/optional): Rate your job satisfaction level.
* **Benefits** (`benefits`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the benefits you're entitled to.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments about your application.
