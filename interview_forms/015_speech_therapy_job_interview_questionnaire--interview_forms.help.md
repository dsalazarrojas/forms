# Speech Therapy Job Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your job preferences and requirements for speech therapy positions. It will help us understand your goals, qualifications, and availability to provide you with potential job opportunities that suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select the page with the correct information for each field.
2. Fill in the required information for each field.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speech Therapy Job Interview** (Page 1)
	+ **Job Title**: Enter the name of the job you are interested in (e.g., "Speech-Language Pathologist").
	+ **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job you are interested in (e.g., "Working with children with autism").
* **Job Title** (Page 2)
	+ **Job Title**: Enter the actual job title of the position you are interested in (e.g., "Speech-Language Pathologist").
	+ **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job you are interested in (e.g., "Providing speech therapy services to children with autism").
* **Job Status** (Page 3)
	+ **Job Status**: Select the type of employment you are looking for:
		- Full-Time: Working full-time for the company.
		- Part-Time: Working part-time for the company.
		- Contract: Temporary or contract work for the company.
* **Job Type** (Page 3)
	+ **Job Type**: Select the type of work environment you are interested in:
		- School: Working in a school setting.
		- Clinic: Working in a medical or clinic setting.
		- Private: Working in a private practice or home environment.
* **Years of Experience** (Page 4)
	+ **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years of experience you have in the field.
* **Education Level** (Page 4)
	+ **Education Level**: Select the level of education you have completed:
		- Bachelor's: Completed a bachelor's degree.
		- Master's: Completed a master's degree.
		- Doctorate: Completed a doctorate degree.
* **Job Location** (Page 5)
	+ **Job Location**: Enter the city, state, or region you are willing to work in.
* **Availability** (Page 5)
	+ **Availability**: Select the times of the day you are available to work:
		- Morning: Working morning shifts.
		- Afternoon: Working afternoon shifts.
		- Evening: Working evening shifts.
		- Weekend: Working weekend shifts.
* **Salary Range** (Page 6)
	+ **Salary Range**: Enter the salary range you are looking for (e.g., "$40,000 - $60,000").
* **Desired Salary** (Page 6)
	+ **Desired Salary**: Enter the salary you are looking for (e.g., "$50,000").
* **Benefits** (Page 7)
	+ **Benefits**: Select the benefits you are looking for:
		- Paid Vacation: Paid vacation time.
		- Paid Sick Leave: Paid sick leave.
		- Health Insurance: Health insurance benefits.
		- Retirement Plan: Retirement plan benefits.
* **Professional Certifications** (Page 7)
	+ **Professional Certifications**: Enter any relevant professional certifications you have.
* **Job References** (Page 8)
	+ **Job References**: Enter any job references you have (e.g., previous employers).
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Page 9)
	+ **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter any emergency contact information (e.g., parent's contact info).
* **Date of Birth** (Page 9)
	+ **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., "1990-01-01").
* **Email Address** (Page 10)
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your work email address (e.g., "john.doe@example.com").
* **Phone Number** (Page 10)
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number (e.g., "123-456-7890").
* **Work Experience** (Page 11)
	+ **Work Experience**: Briefly describe your work experience in the field.
* **Skills** (Page 11)
	+ **Skills**: List any relevant skills you have (e.g., language skills, computer skills).
* **Certifications** (Page 12)
	+ **Certifications**: List any relevant certifications you have (e.g., ASHA certification).
* **Job Requirements** (Page 13)
	+ **Job Requirements**: Briefly describe the requirements for the job you are looking for (e.g., "Must be able to work with children").
* **Availability Schedule** (Page 13)
	+ **Availability Schedule**: Enter your availability schedule (e.g., "Monday to Friday, 9am-5pm").
* **Work Status** (Page 14)
	+ **Work Status**: Select your work status:
		- Available: Available to work.
		- Unavailable: Unavailable to work.
* **Job Description** (Page 15)
	+ **Job Description**: Briefly describe the job you are interested in.

Note: This help guide is for general guidance only and is not intended to be comprehensive or exhaustive. It is recommended that you review the form carefully and ask for clarification if you are unsure about any section.
