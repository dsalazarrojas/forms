# Data Stream Job Interview Questions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job candidates for a job interview. The data collected will be used by the hiring team to assess the candidate's qualifications and fit for the available position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out each section with your responses to the questions asked.
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   You can select multiple options for certain questions if applicable.
*   Enter your availability date for the availability question.
*   Finally, select your preferred job reference type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Round Questions (First_Round_Questions)** (`First_Round_Questions`, `text`, optional): Answer this section with your responses to the questions asked. The fields in this section are designed to be free form text input.
*   **Skills Questions (Skills_Questions)** (`Skills_Questions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more relevant skills that you posses related to the job.
*   **Technical Questions (Technical_Questions)** (`Technical_Questions`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one relevant technical question that you have experience with.
*   **Experience Questions (Experience_Questions)** (`Experience_Questions`, `text`, optional): Describe your relevant experience in this field. Please provide a brief but detailed description.
*   **Availability (Availability)** (`Availability`, `date`, optional): Enter your availability date for the job. Please enter the date you are available to start working.
*   **Job References (Job_References)** (`Job_References`, `select_one`, optional): Select one relevant job reference type that you are looking for in your next job.
