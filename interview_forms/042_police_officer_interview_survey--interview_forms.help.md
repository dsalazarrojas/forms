# police_officer_interview_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The police officer interview survey form is designed to collect information about police officers' performance and feedback during their interviews. It helps evaluate their skills, behavior, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the guidelines provided by your supervisor or team lead to ensure that you are completing the form for the right purpose.
2. Ensure that you are not duplicating efforts, i.e., if you have already completed a similar form for the same officer, do not complete this form again.
3. Fill in the form with the required information, starting from the first page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, `text`, optional)
This field is for entering the police officer's name, badge number, or any other identifying information.
 
### 2. **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, `date`, optional)
Enter the date of the interview.

### 3. **Job Posting** (`job_posting`, `select_one`, optional)
Select one of the following job postings: 60951, 60952, or 60953.

### 4. **Interviewer Name** (`interviewer_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name of the officer conducting the interview.

### 5. **Interview Questions** (`interview_questions`, `text`, optional)
This field is for recording any questions or topics discussed during the interview.

### 6. **Candidate Comments** (`candidate_comments`, `text`, optional)
Enter any comments or feedback from the candidate.

### 7. **Interviewer Feedback** (`interviewer_feedback`, `text`, optional)
Enter your feedback or observations about the candidate's performance.

### 8. **Rating Score** (`rating_score`, `number`, optional)
Enter a rating score out of 100 for the candidate's performance.

## Tips
- Be concise and clear in your entries.
- Use specific examples or anecdotes to support your feedback.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
