# ER Nurse Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates about their experience and qualifications for a position as an ER nurse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Provide detailed answers to each question, using the space provided for each field.
3. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the "required" label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details** (`Candidate Details`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter the candidate's name and any other relevant information about the candidate.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your experience in emergency room nursing, including any relevant certifications, training, or work experience.
* **Scenario Responses** (`Scenario Responses`, `text`, required/optional): In this section, you will be presented with hypothetical emergency scenarios. Please respond to each scenario by describing how you would handle the situation.

## Tips

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Provide specific examples from your experience when possible.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, it's okay to ask for clarification.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your qualifications and experience.
