# Culture Fit Interview Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culture Fit Interview Questionnaire is a form used to collect information about an individual's work history and contact details. It is intended for individuals responding to job postings or filling out the form at the request of their employer or HR representative.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Ensure you have been authorized to fill out this form.
2. Select the correct options for each field as accurately as possible.
3. Fill out all required fields.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (2)**: Select all of your contact information that apply. This includes True or False for whether you would like to be contacted by email, phone, or other means.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Work Phone (4)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Cell Phone (5)**: Enter your cell phone number.
* **Work Address (6)**: Enter your physical work address.
* **City (7)**: Enter the city of your workplace.
* **State (8)**: Select the state where you work.
* **Country (9)**: Enter the country of your workplace.
* **Zip (10)**: Enter the zip code of your workplace.
* **Company (11)**: Enter the name of your current or previous company.
* **Job Title (12)**: Enter your current or previous job title.
* **Job Description (13)**: Provide a brief description of your current or previous job duties.
* **Start Date (14)**: Enter the date you started working at your current or previous company.
* **Month/Year (15)**: Enter the month and year you started working at your current or previous company.
* **End Date (16)**: Enter the date you ended your employment at your current or previous company.
* **Month/Year (17)**: Enter the month and year you ended employment at your current or previous company.
* **Hours (18)**: Enter the number of hours you worked per week.
* **Salary (19)**: Enter your current or previous salary.
* **Status (20)**: Select your current employment status.
* **Employment Status (21)**: Select your type of employment.
* **Shift (22)**: Select your shift or schedule.
* **Employment Type (23)**: Select the type of employment you have.
* **Contract Type (24)**: Select the type of contract you have.
* **English Level (25)**: Select your level of English proficiency.

Note: This form has been designed to be completed by authorized individuals only. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your employer or HR representative for clarification.
