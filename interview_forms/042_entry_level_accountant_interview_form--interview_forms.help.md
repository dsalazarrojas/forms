# Entry Level Accountant Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better as a candidate for an entry-level accountant position. We will use the information you provide to assess your qualifications and fit for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each field as accurately and truthfully as possible.
2. Some fields are required to fill in, while others are optional.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Candidate Information
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information you want to share about yourself.

### Personal Information
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Your first name or given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Your last name or surname.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Your work or personal phone number.

### Additional Information
* **Upload Resume** (`resume_upload`, `text`, optional): You can upload your resume to this form for us to review.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): Please provide your work experience in detail, using bullet points or paragraphs.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, optional): This is for any educational background you have.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, optional): List your technical skills and software proficiency.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Any professional references you have.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, optional): This is for your availability to start work.
* **Salary Requirements** (`salary_requirements`, `text`, optional): Please specify your desired salary and any other benefits you are expecting, using commas to separate each benefit.
* **Why Us** (`why_us`, `text`, optional): Tell us about a time when you overcame a difficult situation or challenge.
* **Why Us Other** (`why_us_other`, `text`, optional): Describe any other qualities or qualifications you think would make you a good fit for this role.
* **Certification** (`certification`, `text`, optional): This is for any relevant certification you have.
* **Other Certification** (`certification_other`, `text`, optional): This is for any other relevant certification you have.
* **Professional License** (`professional_license`, `text`, optional): This is for any professional license you have.
* **Other Professional License** (`professional_license_other`, `text`, optional): This is for any other professional license you have.
* **Language** (`language`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary language you are proficient in.
* **Availability Date Range** (`availability_date_range`, `date`, optional): Please specify the earliest and latest date you are available to start.
* **Availability Time Range** (`availability_time_range`, `time`, optional): This is for your availability time range.
* **Other Work Experience** (`work_experience_other`, `text`, optional): Please describe any other work experiences you have that is not in the previous question.
* **Work Experience 2** (`work_experience_2`, `text`, optional): This is for another work experience.
* **Certification 2** (`certification_2`, `text`, optional): This is for any other certification you have.
* **Professional License 2** (`professional_license_2`, `text`, optional): This is for any other professional license you have.
* **Work Experience 3** (`work_experience_3`, `text`, optional): This is for another work experience.
