# Digital Marketing SEO Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from job candidates for digital marketing and SEO positions. It consists of eight sections that cover different aspects of the candidate's background, skills, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your skills and experience from the available options in Section 4.
3. Choose your availability for workdays in Section 6.
4. Ensure you provide a valid email address in Section 7.
5. Double-check your contact information in Section 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Section 1 - Candidate Information
* **title** (`title`, `text`, optional): Please enter a brief description of this form, which is already filled in as "Digital Marketing SEO Interview Form".

### Section 2 - Experience
* **label** (`label`, `text`, optional): Describe your experience in the field of digital marketing and SEO. This can include previous work, internships, or projects you've completed.

### Section 3 - Communication
* **label** (`label`, `text`, optional): In this section, please provide a few sentences explaining how you communicate with team members, managers, or clients. This can include your approach to email, phone, or in-person conversations.

### Section 4 - Skills
* **Google Analytics** (`options`, `select_one`, optional): If you have experience with Google Analytics, select this option.
* **Content Writing** (`options`, `select_one`, optional): If you have experience with content writing, select this option.
* **Social Media Marketing** (`options`, `select_one`, optional): If you have experience with social media marketing, select this option.

### Section 5 - Experience (Years)
* **label** (`label`, `number`, optional): Estimate the number of years you've worked in digital marketing and SEO. This can be a range (e.g., 2-5 years).

### Section 6 - Availability
* **Available on Monday** (`options`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you are available to work. This can be one or multiple days.
* **Available on Tuesday** (`options`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Available on Wednesday** (`options`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Available on Thursday** (`options`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you are available to work.
* **Available on Friday** (`options`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you are available to work.

### Section 7 - Email
* **label** (`label`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address. This should be a valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### Section 8 - Phone
* **label** (`label`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number. This can be a work or personal phone number.
