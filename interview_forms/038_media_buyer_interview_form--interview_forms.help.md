# Media Buyer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential media buyers to facilitate the interview process for job positions within the company. It gathers necessary contact and personal details to create a comprehensive profile for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Media Buyer Interview Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide detailed answers for optional fields, if applicable.
3. Review your submission before clicking the submit button to ensure all fields are correctly filled and no information is missing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* <label>First Name</label> (`first_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your given name.
### Last Name
* <label>Last Name</label> (`last_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your surname.
### Email
* <label>Email</label> (`email`, `email`, optional):
	+ Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
### Phone
* <label>Phone</label> (`phone`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number.
### Experience Years
* <label>Experience Years</label> (`experience_years`, `number`, optional):
	+ Enter the number of years you have experience in your field.
### Company Name
* <label>Company Name</label> (`company_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your current or previous company.
### Job Title
* <label>Job Title</label> (`job_title`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your job title at the company.
### Available Date
* <label>Available Date</label> (`available_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Select the date when you will be available for an interview.
### Preferred Contact Time
* <label>Preferred Contact Time</label> (`preferred_contact_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Choose a time of day when you are most available for contact.
### Preferred Contact Method
* <label>Preferred Contact Method</label> (`preferred_contact_method`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or chat).
### LinkedIn
* <label>LinkedIn</label> (`linkedin`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter your LinkedIn URL or profile name.
### Resume
* <label>Resume</label> (`resume`, `text`, optional):
	+ Upload or paste a link to your resume for easier reference.
