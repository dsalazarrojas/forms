# press_interview_slot_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The press_interview_slot_registration form is a tool for managing and scheduling press interviews. It's used to register specific interview slots for journalists, companies, and other stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the interview slot you'd like to schedule (Yes or No) in the **Interview Slot** field.
2. Enter the **Journalist Name** if the interview is with a journalist.
3. Enter the **Email** of the journalist or representative.
4. Enter the name of the **Company** associated with the interview.
5. Select the **Role** of the journalist or representative (Yes or No).
6. Enter any additional **Media Contact** information if necessary.
7. Enter the **Contact Number** of the representative.
8. Select the interview **Event** type (Yes or No).
9. Choose the **Interview Date** for the scheduled meeting.
10. Set the **Interview Time** for the meeting.
11. Set the **Interview Length** in minutes.
12. Select the **Language** for the interview.
13. Enter any **Questions** that you'd like to discuss during the interview.
14. Upload any relevant **File** if necessary.
15. Add any **Additional Info** regarding the interview.
16. Enter your **Submitter** name.
17. Enter your **Submitter Email**.
18. Enter your **Submitter Phone** number.
19. Enter your **Submitter Company**.
20. Select whether the form has been assigned to a specific **User** (Yes or No).
21. Set the **Status** of the form (Active or Inactive).
22. Add any **Comments** about the form.
23. Select whether the form has been assigned to a specific **Tool** (Yes or No).
24. Set the date the form was **Created At**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule Interview**: Select Yes or No to schedule an interview slot.
* **Journalist Name**: Enter the name of the journalist you're interviewing.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the journalist or representative.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company associated with the interview.
* **Role**: Select Yes or No to confirm the representative's role.
* **Media Contact**: Enter any additional media contact information if necessary.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the representative.
* **Event**: Select Yes or No to mark the interview as an event.
* **Interview Date**: Choose the date of the scheduled interview.
* **Interview Time**: Set the time of the interview.
* **Interview Length**: Set the length of the interview in minutes.
* **Language**: Select the language of the interview.
* **Questions**: Add any questions you'd like to discuss during the interview.
* **File**: Upload any relevant file for the interview.
* **Additional Info**: Add any additional information about the interview.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Assigned User**: Select Yes or No to mark the form as assigned to a specific user.
* **Status**: Set the status of the form to Active or Inactive.
* **Comments**: Add any comments about the form.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select Yes or No to mark the form as assigned to a specific tool.
* **Created At**: Set the date the form was created.
