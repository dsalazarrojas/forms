# Marketing Intern Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Intern Interview Form is a tool used to collect information from potential candidates for marketing internship positions. This form will help us understand your qualifications, interests, and availability, which will be used to determine your suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete each section carefully and thoroughly.
2. If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to ask.
3. Make sure to double-check the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Field 1)
* **General Information** (`text`, required/optional): This section is for you to provide any relevant information about yourself that you feel is important for us to know.

### Contact Details (Field 2)
* **Contact Details** (`text`, required/optional): Please provide your current contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.

### Education History (Field 3)
* **Education History** (`text`, required/optional): This is the section to list your education history, including institutions attended, degrees earned, and relevant courses.

### Work Experience (Field 4)
* **Work Experience** (`text`, required/optional): Share your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.

### Skills and Abilities (Field 5)
* **Skills and Abilities** (`text`, required/optional): List your relevant skills and abilities that you think are essential for the marketing internship position.

### Availability (Field 6)
* **Availability** (`text`, required/optional): This section is for you to describe your availability for the internship, including any conflicts or limitations you may have.

### Availability Date (Field 7)
* **Availability Date** (`date`, required/optional): If you have a preferred date for the internship, please specify it here.

### Availability Time (Field 8)
* **Availability Time** (`time`, required/optional): If you have a preferred time for the internship, please specify it here.

### Availability Period (Field 9)
* **Availability Period** (`number`, required/optional): Please indicate how many months you are available for the internship.

### Job Preferred (Field 10)
* **Job Preferred** (`select_one`, required/true): Please select the marketing job that you are most interested in:
	+ Marketing
	+ Sales
	+ Support

### Work Arrangement (Field 11)
* **Work Arrangement** (`select_one`, required/true): Please select your preferred work arrangement:
	+ Remote
	+ Onsite

### Work Arrangement Shift (Field 12)
* **Work Arrangement Shift** (`select_multiple`, required/true): Please select your available work shifts:
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening

### Work Arrangement Hours (Field 13)
* **Work Arrangement Hours** (`select_multiple`, required/true): Please select your available work hours:
	+ Part-time
	+ Full-time

### Job Title (Field 14)
* **Job Title** (`select_one`, required/true): Are you interested in a job title?
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Job Type (Field 15)
* **Job Type** (`select_one`, required/true): Are you interested in a job type?
	+ Yes
	+ No

### Employment Status (Field 16)
* **Employment Status** (`select_one`, required/true): What is your employment status?
	+ Active
	+ Inactive

### Job Location (Field 17)
* **Job Location** (`text`, required/true): Please provide your preferred job location.

### Job Description (Field 18)
* **Job Description** (`text`, required/true): Please provide a brief description of the job you are interested in.

### Job Salary (Field 19)
* **Job Salary** (`number`, required/true): Please provide your expected salary for the internship.

### Job Requirements (Field 20)
* **Job Requirements** (`text`, required/true): Please list any job requirements you have, such as skills or qualifications.

### Job Benefits (Field 21)
* **Job Benefits** (`text`, required/true): Please list any job benefits you are expecting, such as health insurance or paid time off.

### Job Qualifications (Field 22)
* **Job Qualifications** (`text`, required/true): Please list any qualifications you have that align with the job requirements.

### Job Notes (Field 23)
* **Job Notes** (`text`, required/true): Please provide any additional information about the job you are interested in.

### Job References (Field 24)
* **Job References** (`text`, required/true): Please provide the names and contact information of any professional references you have.

### Job Agreement (Field 25)
* **Job Agreement** (`text`, required/true): Please agree to the terms and conditions of the internship.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when providing information.
* Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you need help or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to ask.
