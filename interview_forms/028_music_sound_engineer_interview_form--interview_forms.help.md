# Music Sound Engineer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from music sound engineers, including their experience, equipment, and availability. It's meant to be completed by music sound engineers to provide their details for internal purposes, such as research or recruitment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options for each field. The form has multiple pages, each with its own set of fields. Please make sure to fill out all required information to ensure that your details are accurately recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Music Sound Engineer Form (`music_sound_engineer_form`, `select_multiple`)
This field is a select multiple field that asks you to choose your answer. Please select the options that best apply to you.
* **Yes**: This option is selected if you have the specified quality or characteristic.
* **No**: This option is selected if you do not have the specified quality or characteristic.

### Name (`name`, `text`)
This field is a text field where you can enter your full name.
* Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* Do not enter any additional information or explanations in this field.

### Job Title (`job_title`, `text`)
This field is a text field where you can enter your job title or current position.
* Please enter the title of your current or recent job as a music sound engineer.
* Do not enter any additional information or explanations in this field.

### Contact Info (`contact_info`, `email`)
This field is an email field where you can enter your email address.
* Please enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* Ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.

### Experience (`experience`, `text`)
This field is a text field where you can enter your experience as a music sound engineer.
* Please provide a brief description of your experience, including years and any relevant skills or qualifications.
* Do not enter any additional information or explanations in this field.

### Availability (`availability`, `time`)
This field is a time field where you can select your availability.
* Please select the time of day you are most available to work or meet.
* Be as specific as possible to ensure accurate scheduling.

### Equipment (`equipment`, `text`)
This field is a text field where you can enter equipment you have experience with.
* Please list any relevant equipment you have experience with, including software or hardware.
* Do not enter any additional information or explanations in this field.

### References (`references`, `select_one`)
This field is a select one field that asks you to choose your answer.
* **Yes**: This option is selected if you have references or recommendations from previous clients or employers.
* **No**: This option is selected if you do not have references or recommendations from previous clients or employers.

### Portfolio (`portfolio`, `select_multiple`)
This field is a select multiple field that asks you to choose your answer.
* **Yes**: This option is selected if you have a portfolio or a website showcasing your work.
* **No**: This option is selected if you do not have a portfolio or website showcasing your work.
