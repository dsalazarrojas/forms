# Tech Startup Data Analyst Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Startup Data Analyst Interview Form is a tool used by recruiters, hiring managers, or interviewers to collect and document information about candidate interactions during the interview process for a data analyst position in a tech startup.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form for each candidate interviewed. Make sure to provide detailed and accurate information in each field.
*   Enter the candidate's name and other relevant details under "Candidate Details".
*   Describe the role's relevance to the candidate under "Role Relevant Screening".
*   Record the candidate's contact information for future reference.
*   Indicate the candidate's availability for the position under "Availability".
*   List the candidate's skills and qualifications under "Candidate Skills".
*   Provide a brief description of the role under "Role Description".
*   Outline the role's requirements under "Role Requirements".
*   Ask and record screening questions and answers under "Screening Questions" and "Screening Answers".
*   Record a reference from the candidate under "Candidate Reference".
*   Leave any additional comments or information under "Additional Comments".
*   Mark the candidate's current status under "Candidate Status".
*   Update the screening status under "Screening Status".
*   Record the final status of the candidate under "Final Status".
*   Record feedback or comments on the candidate under "Feedback Status".
*   Schedule and record the interview under "Interview Schedule".
*   Record the outcome of the interview under "Interview Result".
*   Identify the candidate's source under "Candidate Source".
*   Record any additional notes about the candidate under "Candidate Notes".
*   Describe the interview details under "Interview Details".
*   Specify the interview location under "Interview Location".
*   Record the candidate's interview experience under "Candidate Interview Experience".
*   Add any recruiter's notes under "Recruiter Notes".
*   Outline the next steps for the candidate under "Candidate Next Steps".
*   Leave any final notes under "Final Notes".

*   Make sure to keep the information up-to-date.
