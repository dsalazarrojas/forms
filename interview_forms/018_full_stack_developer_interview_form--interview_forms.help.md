# Full Stack Developer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from candidates interested in working with the company as Full Stack Developers. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to provide the best possible opportunity for us to review your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully for any required information.
3. Use the options in the "Technical Stack" field to select all applicable technologies.
4. Use the options in the "Technical Skills" field to select all programming languages and tools you are familiar with.
5. Enter your availability for an interview in the "Availability" field.
6. Select your preferred method of interview in the "Interview Preferences" field.
7. Enter your work history in the "Work History" field.
8. Enter your education background in the "Education" field.
9. Enter any relevant references in the "References" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Candidate Information
* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, text, optional): This field is for providing any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with us.

### Candidate Input
* **Candidate Input** (`candidate_info_input`, text, required): This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information about yourself.

### Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Indicate the number of years of experience you have in the field of full stack development.

### Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required): List your experience with programming languages and technologies in the field of full stack development.

### Technical Stack
* **Technical Stack** (`technical_stack`, select_multiple, required): Select all technologies with which you have experience in the field of full stack development.

### Technical Skills
* **Technical Skills** (`technical_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all programming languages and tools you are familiar with in the field of full stack development.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): Enter your availability for an interview.

### Interview Preferences
* **Interview Preferences** (`interview_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select the methods by which you prefer your interviews to be conducted.

### Work History
* **Work History** (`work_history`, text, required): Enter your work history in the field of full stack development.

### Work History Input
