# Project Management Interview Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Management Interview Schedule form is designed to help project teams manage and schedule interviews with candidates. This includes collecting information about the interview date, time, location, and attendees, as well as the interviewers' preferences and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Interview Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Choose the **Interview Time** in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30 for 2:30 PM).
3. Select the **Project Leader** responsible for the interview.
4. List the **Team Members** who will be attending the interview (yes or no).
5. Enter the **Candidate's Name and Contact Information** for reference.
6. Select the **Interview Duration** in minutes.
7. Provide the **Interview Location** where the interview will take place.
8. List the **Interviewers** (yes or no).
9. Choose the **Scheduling Preference** (in-person, virtual, or phone).
10. Add any **Notes** for the interviewer.
11. Enter the **Confirmation Date** when the confirmation was sent to the candidate (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Interview Date**: Enter the date of the interview in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Interview Time**: Choose the time of the interview in 24-hour format.
* **Project Leader**: Select the person responsible for leading the interview.
* **Team Members**: List the team members who will be attending the interview. Select 'yes' if they will be attending, and 'no' if they won't.
* **Candidate Details**: Enter the candidate's name and contact information for reference.
* **Interview Duration**: Select the length of the interview in minutes.
* **Interview Location**: Provide the exact location where the interview will take place.
* **Interviewers**: List the names of the interviewers who will be conducting the interview. Select 'yes' if they will be conducting the interview, and 'no' if they won't.
* **Scheduling Preferences**: Choose how the interview will be conducted (in-person, virtual, or phone).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or instructions for the interviewer.
* **Confirmation Date**: Enter the date when the confirmation was sent to the candidate (if applicable).
