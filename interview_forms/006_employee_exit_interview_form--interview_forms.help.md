# Employee Exit Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a mandatory exit procedure for departing employees. It helps us understand the reasons behind their decision to leave and gather any suggestions or insights on how we can improve our company culture and operations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out your contact information: 
    - **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
    - **Email**: Enter your company email address.
    - **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
2. Provide details about your departure:
    - **Job Title**: Enter the title of your position in the company.
    - **Exit Date**: Enter the date you are leaving the company.
3. Share your reasons for leaving:
    - **Reasons for Leaving**: Explain in detail why you are leaving the company. This will help us understand your perspective and possibly identify areas for improvement.
4. Offer suggestions for improvement:
    - **Suggestions for Improvement**: Share any ideas or suggestions you have on how we can improve our company culture, operations, or any other aspect that you believe is important.
5. If you are moving to another company, provide the details of your new job:
    - **New Job Title**: If you have a new job, enter the title of your position in the new company.

* **Exit Interview Form**: This field is not required and will be filled out by all departing employees.
* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* This field is optional and only required to provide additional details.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
*   This field is optional and only required for contact.
* **Phone**: Enter your primary phone number.
*   This field is optional and only required for future contact.
* **Department**: This field is optional and not currently used in this form.
*   Please do not fill it out.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of your position in the company.
*   This field helps us understand your role in the company.
* **Exit Date**: Enter the date you are leaving the company.
*   This field is optional, but it helps us track your exit date.
* **Reasons for Leaving**: Explain in detail why you are leaving the company.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative.
- We appreciate your feedback and suggestions to help us improve our company.
