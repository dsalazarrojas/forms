# educational_workshop_partner_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational workshop partner interview form is designed to collect information from internal workshop partners for internal review and collaboration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide partner information in the "Partner Information" field.
2. Select the contact information for the partner in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Choose the type of workshop information you want to share with the partner in the "Workshop Information" field.
4. Enter any additional review information in the "Review Information" field.
5. Provide collaboration details in the "Collaboration Details" field.
6. Enter any communication information in the "Communication Information" field.
7. Choose the follow-up information for the partner in the "Follow-up Information" field.
8. Enter the submission information for the partner in the "Submission Information" field.
9. Choose the time zone of the partner in the "Time Zone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Information** (`partner_info`, text, optional):
  This field is for entering the partner's information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple, optional):
  This field is for selecting the partner's contact information, such as phone, email, or website.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, select_one, optional):
  This field is for choosing the type of workshop information to share with the partner, such as partner workshop, workshop location, or workshop dates.
* **Review Information** (`review_info`, note, optional):
  This field is for entering any additional review information.
* **Collaboration Details** (`collaboration_details`, text, optional):
  This field is for providing any collaboration details with the partner.
* **Communication Information** (`communication_info`, text, optional):
  This field is for entering any communication information with the partner.
* **Follow-up Information** (`follow_up_info`, select_multiple, optional):
  This field is for choosing the follow-up information for the partner, such as phone, email, or website.
* **Submission Information** (`submission_info`, date, optional):
  This field is for entering the submission information for the partner.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone_info`, time, optional):
  This field is for choosing the partner's time zone.
