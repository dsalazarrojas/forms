# Social Responsibility Research Interview Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect consent from participants in a social responsibility research interview. It is intended for use by researchers to gather information from participants about their participation in the study.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your Participant Information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your Contact Information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Check the boxes that apply to give your consent for sharing data, results, and contact information.
4. Sign the Signature field with your electronic signature.
5. Enter your Researcher Information, including the name and title of the researcher.
6. Provide the dates of the interview and contact dates (if applicable).
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Page 1):
	+ This section is where you will enter your participant information, including your name and any other relevant details. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Contact Information** (Page 1):
	+ This section is where you will enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number. Please ensure that this information is accurate and current.
* **Participant Permissions** (Page 2):
	+ This section is where you will give your consent for sharing data, results, and contact information. Check the boxes that apply to give your consent.
* **Signature** (Page 2):
	+ This section is where you will sign the form with your electronic signature.
* **Researcher Information** (Page 3):
	+ This section is where you will enter the name and title of the researcher conducting the study.
* **Interview Dates** (Page 3):
	+ This section is where you will enter the dates of the interview.
* **Contact Dates** (Page 4):
	+ This section is where you will enter the dates of contact (if applicable).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can ask for clarification.
