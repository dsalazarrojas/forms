# Medical Science Liaison Interview Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of a medical science liaison during an interview. The evaluation is used to assess the individual's skills and knowledge in a specific field of medicine.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Interviewee Name" field with the name of the individual being interviewed.
2. Enter the "Evaluation Date" of the interview in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).
3. Select the "Evaluation Type" from the dropdown list, either "Medical Science Liaison" or "Other".
4. Enter a score for each of the specific evaluation questions, using the numeric value from 1 to 5.
5. Enter any additional comments about the interviewee's performance in the "Comments" field.
6. Enter your "Evaluator Name" and "Evaluator Email" and "Evaluator Phone" in the respective fields. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Interviewee Name** (`name`, `text`, required)
Enter the name of the individual being interviewed.

### 2. **Evaluation Date** (`date`, `required`)
Enter the date of the interview in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).

### 3. **Score** (`number`, `required`)
Select a score for the individual's performance, using a numeric value from 1 to 5.

### 4. **Comments** (`text`, `optional`)
Enter any additional comments about the interviewee's performance.

### 5. **Evaluation Type** (`select_one`, `required`)
Select the type of evaluation from the dropdown list, either "Medical Science Liaison" or "Other".

### 6-9 **Evaluation Score 1.x** (`number`, `required`)
Select a score for each of the specific evaluation questions, using a numeric value from 1 to 5.

### 10 **Field 10** (`text`, `optional`)
Enter any additional comments about the interviewee's performance.

### 11. **Evaluator Name** (`text`, `required`)
Enter your name as the evaluator.

### 12 **Comments** (`text`, `optional`)
Enter any additional comments about the interviewee's performance.

### 13 **Evaluator Email** (`email`, `optional`)
Enter your email address.

### 14 **Evaluator Phone** (`text`, `optional`)
Enter your phone number.

### 15 **Comments** (`text`, `optional`)
Enter any additional comments about the evaluation process.
