# Graduate School Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Graduate School Interview Form is a tool used to collect information about graduate school interviews. This form helps to gather details about the interview schedule, evaluations, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with your name, email, and other contact details.
2. Provide information about your interview schedule, including the date and time.
3. Enter any evaluations or feedback you received during the interview.
4. Add your contact information, including phone and email.
5. If applicable, provide details about your work experience.
6. Select the type of interview (phone, video, in-person) and choose the interviewers who conducted the interview.
7. Enter the evaluation type and provide an evaluation score (if applicable).
8. Provide notes about the interview.
