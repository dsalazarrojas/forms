# data_scientist_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The data scientist interview form is designed to collect information from candidates for data science roles. This form helps us to evaluate a candidate's suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's details in the "candidate_details" field.
2. Provide the candidate's contact information in the "contact_info" field.
3. Describe the candidate's work experience in the "work_experience" field.
4. Select the candidate's technical skills from the options provided in the "technical_skills" field.
5. Enter the candidate's educational background in the "education" field.
6. Write a personal statement in the "personal_statement" field.
7. Enter any relevant references in the "references" field.
8. If applicable, upload the candidate's resume in the "upload_resume" field.
9. If applicable, provide a link to the candidate's portfolio in the "upload_portfolio" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Details (candidate_details)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Enter the candidate's name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Enter the candidate's contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Work Experience (work_experience)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Describe the candidate's relevant work experience.
* **Technical Skills (technical_skills)** (`<name>`, `select_one`, `false`): Select the candidate's technical skills from the options provided:
	+ **Programming Languages**: e.g. Python, Java, C++
	+ **Machine Learning**: e.g. supervised, unsupervised, deep learning
	+ **Data Visualization**: e.g. Tableau, Power BI, D3.js
	+ **Data Mining**: e.g. data wrangling, data transformation
* **Education (education)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Enter the candidate's educational background, including degrees and institutions.
* **Personal Statement (personal_statement)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Write a personal statement explaining why you are suitable for the data science role.
* **References (references)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Enter any relevant references, including names and contact information.
* **Additional Comments (additional_comments)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): If you have any additional comments, you can provide them here.
* **Resume Upload (upload_resume)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): If applicable, upload the candidate's resume.
* **Portfolio Link (upload_portfolio)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): If applicable, provide a link to the candidate's portfolio.
* **Upload Resume (upload_resume)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Upload your resume as a PDF or JPEG file.
* **Upload Portfolio (upload_portfolio)** (`<name>`, `text`, `false`): Upload a link to your portfolio.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and attachments before submitting the form.
* If you have already completed this form for the same role, you may not need to complete it again.
* Please review the form carefully to avoid duplication of effort.
