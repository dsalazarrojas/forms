# Video Interview Appearance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for users who want to provide feedback on video interview appearances. It helps interviewers evaluate the appearance and demeanor of interviewees during video interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully.
2. Select your response for each field based on your observation.
3. Use the scale provided for numerical fields (e.g., Video Resolution, Audio Level).
4. Use the space provided for any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **On-Camera Presence** (`1 - Excellent`, `2 - Good`, `3 - Average`, `4 - Poor`): This field assesses the level of confidence and composure displayed by the interviewee during the video interview.
* **Confidence** (`Confident`, `Somewhat Confident`, `Not Confident`): This field evaluates the level of self-assurance and conviction displayed by the interviewee while being interviewed.
* **Formattedness** (note: this seems to be a typo in the original YAML, and it was corrected to `Formalness`): This field assesses the level of formality displayed by the interviewee during the video interview.
* **Body Language** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This field evaluates the nonverbal cues, such as posture, gestures, and facial expressions, displayed by the interviewee.
* **Facial Expression** (`Friendly`, `Somewhat Friendly`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat Unfriendly`, `Ungentlemanly`): This field assesses the overall facial expression and demeanor displayed by the interviewee during the video interview.
* **Eye Contact** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This field evaluates the level of eye contact maintained during the video interview.
* **Smile** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This field assesses the presence and quality of a smile during the video interview.
* **Posture** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This field evaluates the level of good posture and body alignment maintained during the video interview.
* **Voice Quality** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This field assesses the clarity, tone, and overall quality of the interviewee's voice during the video interview.
* **Audio Level** (`Good`, `Fair`, `Poor`): This field evaluates the overall level of audio quality during the video interview.
* **Overall Impression** (`Strong`, `Neutral`, `Weak`): This field assesses the general impression left by the interviewee after completing the video interview.
* **Additional Comments** (`free-form text`): This field is for any additional feedback or comments you may have about the interviewee.
* **Video Resolution** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the video resolution of the interview. A higher value indicates a clearer and more stable video feed.
* **Audio Quality** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the audio quality of the interview. A higher value indicates a clearer and more stable audio feed.
* **Video Duration** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the length of time spent during the video interview.
* **Audio Duration** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the length of time spent during the video interview.
* **Time Spent On-Boarding** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the time spent preparing for the video interview.
* **Time Spent Interview** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the time spent actually interviewing.
* **Time Spent On-Camera** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the time spent being interviewed.
* **Total Time** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the total time spent on the video interview.
* **Total Video Time** (`integer value`): This field is for rating the total video time spent during the interview.
* **Interviewer's Feedback 1**, **Interviewer's Feedback 2**, **Interviewer's Feedback 3** (`free-form text`): These fields are for any additional feedback or comments from the interviewer.
