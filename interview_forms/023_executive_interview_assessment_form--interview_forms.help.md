# Executive Interview Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential details about the candidate for executive positions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information about the candidate.
2. Fill in the form with the candidate's details, taking care to answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. Make sure to submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the candidate's full name.
* **What is the candidate's previous work experience?** (`question1`, `text`, Optional): Describe the candidate's previous work experience.
* **What is the candidate's salary expectation?** (`question3`, `number`, Optional): Enter the candidate's expected salary range.
* **What is the candidate's current location?** (`question5`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose the candidate's current location from the provided options.
* **How many hours can the candidate work per week?** (`question6`, `number`, Optional): Enter the number of hours the candidate is available to work per week.
* **What time can the candidate start on the first day of work?** (`question7`, `time`, Optional): Choose the time the candidate is available to start work.
* **What time can the candidate start on the first day of work (end of shift)?** (`question8`, `time`, Optional): Choose the end of shift time the candidate is available to start work.
* **What is the candidate's preferred email?** (`question9`, `email`, Optional): Enter the candidate's preferred email address.
* **What is the candidate's preferred phone number?** (`question10`, `text`, Optional): Enter the candidate's preferred phone number.
* **What is the company's website?** (`question11`, `text`, Optional): Enter the company's website URL.
* **What is the company's address?** (`question12`, `text`, Optional): Enter the company's address.
* **What is the company's email?** (`question13`, `text`, Optional): Enter the company's email address.
* **What is the company's phone number?** (`question14`, `text`, Optional): Enter the company's phone number.
* **What is the company's social media link?** (`question15`, `text`, Optional): Enter the company's social media link.
* **What is the company's description?** (`question16`, `text`, Optional): Enter a brief description of the company.
* **What is the company's manager's name?** (`question17`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's name.
* **What is the company's manager's email?** (`question18`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's email address.
* **What is the company's manager's phone number?** (`question19`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's phone number.
* **What is the company's manager's job title?** (`question20`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's job title.
* **What is the company's manager's department?** (`question21`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's department.
* **What is the company's manager's location?** (`question22`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's location.
* **What is the company's manager's job description?** (`question23`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's job description.
* **What is the company's manager's photo?** (`question24`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's photo.
* **What is the company's manager's bio?** (`question25`, `text`, Optional): Enter the manager's bio.

Note: Please keep in mind that the fields marked as Optional are only required if necessary and relevant to the candidate's profile.
