# Corporate Management Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Management Interview Form is designed to collect information about corporate management interviews. This information is used to assess an individual's qualifications, performance, or fit for a management role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete each page of the form, one page at a time.
3. For each field, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Corporate Management Interview Form 1** (`text`, required: false): This field is a text input where you can provide a brief description of the interview.
* **Category** (`select_one`, required: false): Select one of the categories: Category 1, Category 2, or Category 3. This field helps to categorize the interview in terms of its purpose or type.
* **Description** (`text`, required: false): This field is a text input where you can provide a detailed description of the interview.
* **Assigned Tool** (`text`, required: false): Provide the name of the tool or software used during the interview.
* **Corporate Management Interview Form 6** (`text`, required: false): This field is a text input where you can provide additional notes or comments about the interview.
* **Corporate Management Interview Form 7** (`text`, required: false): This field is a text input where you can provide further notes or comments about the interview.
* **Corporate Management Interview Form 9** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more of the options: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. This field allows you to select multiple relevant options for the interview.
* **Corporate Management Interview Form 10** (`date`, required: false): This field is a date input where you can provide the date of the interview.
