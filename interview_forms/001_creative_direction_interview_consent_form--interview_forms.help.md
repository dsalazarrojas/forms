# Creative Direction Interview Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the consent of participants in a creative direction interview, which is a critical part of the research process for understanding user needs and preferences in design projects. It helps ensure that participants understand and agree to the terms of the interview, including the use of recordings, and it provides a clear understanding of their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Read the form carefully**: Before filling out the form, take a moment to review the questions and understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out the **interview_form** field: This is where you can enter any additional information that may be relevant to the interview, such as your name, email, or other identifying information.
3. Provide **participant_information**: This is the section where you can enter any personal details that are relevant to your participation, such as your age, job title, or any other relevant background information.
4. Confirm **agreement**: Please read and agree to the terms of the interview, including the use of recordings, by typing "yes" or "I agree" in the **agreement** field.
5. Indicate **use_of_recordings**: Choose from the options how you would like to use the recordings of the interview. You can choose "Option 1" or type "Other" if none of the provided options meet your needs.
6. Enter your **participant_agreement**: Please enter the number of the option that best represents your agreement to the terms of the interview.
7. Date and sign the **signature_date**: Enter the date and sign the form in the **signature** field to confirm your agreement.
8. Finally, confirm that you have **form_complete**: Enter "yes" or "I agree" in the last field to indicate that you have read and understood the terms of the form.
