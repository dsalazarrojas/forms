# Interview Schedule Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and interview information from individuals. It is essential for the interviewer to gather accurate data for communication and scheduling purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Contact Details section with the individual's name and contact information.
2. Move on to the Interview Schedule section and select the interview location and date.
3. Add any additional comments if needed.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): This is the section where you will enter the individual's name and contact information.
* **Interview Location** (`interview_location`, text, required): This is the location where the interview will take place. Select the correct location from the available options or enter a new location if it is not listed.
* **Interview Date** (`interview_date`, date, required): This is the date when the interview will take place. Select the correct date from the available options or enter a new date if it is not listed.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): This is an opportunity to add any additional information about the interview or individual.
