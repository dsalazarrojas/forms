# Aviation Flight Crew Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Aviation Flight Crew Interview Form" is a comprehensive guide for interviewers to assess a candidate's qualifications, experience, and fit for an aviation flight crew position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and effectively, follow the steps outlined in this guide. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate and Interviewer Information**: This is the starting point for the form. Fill in the candidate's and interviewer's names and contact information.
* **Candidate Full Name**: Enter the candidate's full name.
* **Date of Interview**: Enter the date of the interview. 
* **Position Applied For**: Choose the primary role being interviewed for from the dropdown menu. Select from Captain, First Officer, Flight Attendant, Flight Engineer, or Cabin Crew Supervisor.
* **Flight Experience and Qualifications**: This is a note field where you can add any relevant information about the candidate's flight experience and qualifications.
* **Total Flight Hours**: Enter the overall flight time for the candidate.
* **Pilot-in-Command (PIC) Hours**: Enter the total time the candidate has spent as a pilot-in-command.
* **Aircraft Type Ratings**: List all current and valid type ratings held by the candidate.
* **Competency Assessment**: Rate the candidate's performance on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "Poor" and 5 is "Excellent." This includes Communication Skills, Technical Knowledge, and Crew Resource Management.
* **Situational Scenario Management**: Write a brief description of the candidate's response to an emergency scenario.
* **Final Recommendation**: Choose one of three options: "Hire Immediately," "Consider for Pool," or "Not Recommended for Hire."
* **Final Interview Notes**: Add any additional comments about the candidate's performance during the interview.
* **Expected Start Date**: Enter the earliest available start date for training, if applicable.
