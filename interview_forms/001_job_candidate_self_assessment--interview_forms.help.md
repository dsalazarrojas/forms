# Job Candidate Self Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Job Candidate Self Assessment" form is designed to gather critical information from job applicants about their suitability for available positions at the company.

## How To Complete This Form

### First Page

*   **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.
*   **Resume** (`Resume`, `text`, **optional**): Upload your resume for us to review.

### Personal Details

*   **Job Title** (`Job Title`, `text`, **required**): Select the job title you are applying for.
*   **Years of Experience** (`Years of Experience`, `number`, **required**): Enter the number of years of experience you have.
*   **Available for what** (`Available for what`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the job type you are interested in.
*   **Available for how long** (`Available for how long`, `date`, **required**): Enter how long you are available for the position.

### Availability and Preferences

*   **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select your preferred language.
*   **Preferred Location** (`Preferred Location`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select your preferred location (e.g., North America, Europe, Asia, Australia, South America, Africa).

### Availability for Interview

*   **Available for Interview** (`Available for Interview`, `select_one`, **required**): Select if you are available for an interview.
*   **Available for Interview Time** (`Available for Interview Time`, `time`, **required**): Enter your preferred interview time.
*   **Available for Interview Date** (`Available for Interview Date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your preferred interview date.
*   **Available for Interview Location** (`Available for Interview Location`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred interview location (e.g., In-person, Virtual, Flexible).
*   **Preferred Interviewer** (`Preferred Interviewer`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select your preferred interviewer (e.g., Team Lead, Manager, Director, Other).
*   **Interview Experience** (`Interview Experience`, `time`, **required**): Enter your interview experience (in hours).
*   **Interview Location** (`Interview Location`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred interview location (e.g., In-person, Virtual, Flexible).
*   **Preferred Interview Time** (`Preferred Interview Time`, `time`, **required**): Enter your preferred interview time.
*   **Preferred Interview Date** (`Preferred Interview Date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your preferred interview date.
*   **Preferred Interview Location** (`Preferred Interview Location`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your preferred interview location (e.g., In-person, Virtual, Flexible).

### Submission

*   **Submission** (`Submission`, `note`, **optional**): If required, provide a brief description of your submission.

**Tips:**

*   Please ensure you answer all questions accurately and honestly.
*   Ensure your resume is relevant to the job position you are applying for.
*   Provide detailed descriptions in text fields.
*   Select options that best fit your situation.
