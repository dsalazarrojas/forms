# Employment Lawyer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employment Lawyer Interview Form is designed to collect detailed information from candidates or employees for assessment or interview purposes. This form will help you gather crucial data for making informed decisions about employment-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the form in a linear manner, from left to right, and from top to bottom.
2.  Ensure you fill out each field accurately and completely before proceeding to the next one.
3.  For multiple-choice fields, select all applicable options by clicking on each relevant choice.
4.  For text fields, type in the required information in the space provided.
5.  Ensure you submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.
