# Career Fair Interview Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for candidates to book interviews with companies at career fairs. The information collected will help the company assess your qualifications and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the career fair event and its name from the first field.
2. Choose an available interview date and time slot that suits you from the second field.
3. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number to facilitate communication.
4. Enter the company you are interviewing with and the job title or role you are applying for.
5. Select the format of the interview you prefer.
6. If in-person, provide the location of the interview.
7. Enter your education level and major field of study.
8. If applicable, provide your expected graduation date and years of relevant experience.
9. Indicate if you have a resume attached and if you are prepared for the interview.
10. Select your preferred method of communication for confirmation.
11. Answer any additional questions as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Career Fair Name**: Event you are interviewing at
	+ Enter the name of the career fair event you are attending.
* **Interview Date**: Select an available date for the interview
	+ Choose a date that suits you from the available options.
* **Candidate Name**: Full name
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Email Address
	+ Enter your email address for communication and confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Phone Number
	+ Include your area code for easy contact.
* **Company Name**: Which company are you interviewing with?
	+ Enter the name of the company you are applying to.
* **Position Title or Role**: Job title you are interviewing for
	+ Enter the job title or role you are applying for.
* **Interviewer or Recruiter Name**: Name of person conducting interview
	+ If you have a preferred interviewer, enter their name here (optional).
* **Preferred Interview Time**: Select an available time slot
	+ Choose a time slot that suits you from the available options.
* **Interview Format**: How will the interview be conducted?
	+ Select the format you prefer, such as in-person or phone/video.
* **Interview Location**: If in-person, where is the interview location?
	+ Enter the location of the interview (if in-person).
* **Education Level**: Current or completed education
	+ Select your current education level or completed education.
* **Major or Field of Study**: Major or Field of Study
	+ Enter your major or field of study.
* **Expected Graduation Date**: Expected graduation date
	+ Enter your expected graduation date (if applicable).
* **Years of Relevant Experience**: Professional work experience in this field
	+ Enter your years of experience in the field.
* **Resume Attached**: Have you attached your resume?
	+ Select if you have attached your resume to the form.
* **Do You Have a Cover Letter Ready?**: Yes or No
	+ Select if you have a cover letter ready.
* **Are You Prepared for the Interview?**: Yes, Mostly, Somewhat, or No
	+ Select your level of preparation for the interview.
* **Questions You Want to Ask**: What would you like to know about the role?
	+ Enter any questions you have for the interviewer.
* **Can You Pass a Background Check?**: Yes, No, or Unsure
	+ Select if you are able to pass a background check.
* **When Can You Start?**: When would you be available to start?
	+ Select when you would be available to start work.
* **Special Accommodations Needed?**: Accessibility, dietary, or other needs
	+ Enter any special accommodations you require.
* **How Should We Confirm Your Interview?**: Select preferred method of communication
	+ Choose how you would like to be confirmed about the interview.

## Tips

* Be accurate with date and phone number formatting.
* Choose one option for each select field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
