# Field Interview Card Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Interview Card Log is a form designed to document field interviews, providing a structured way to capture essential details about each interview, including the event's title and description, participant's information, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1.  Enter a title that describes the event and its purpose.
2.  Provide a brief description of the event.
3.  Select a date for the event.
4.  Enter a time for the event.
5.  Identify the location of the event.
6.  Fill in the name of the participant.
7.  Select the gender of the participant.
8.  Enter the age of the participant.
9.  Enter the contact number and email of the participant.
10. Add any additional notes about the participant or the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (1): Enter a title that describes the event and its purpose. This will help identify the event in the database.
* **Description** (2): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Date of Event** (3): Select the date of the event. Format as `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Event** (4): Enter the time of the event. Use `HH:MM` format.
* **Location of Event** (5): Identify the location of the event, including any relevant details.
* **Name of Participant** (6): Fill in the name of the participant.
* **Gender of Participant** (7): Select the gender of the participant. Options are Female, Male, and Other.
* **Age of Participant** (8): Enter the age of the participant.
* **Contact Number** (9): Enter the contact number of the participant.
* **Email** (10): Enter the email of the participant.
* **Notes** (11): Add any additional notes about the participant or the event.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to provide valuable information for future reference and analysis.
* Ensure that the form is completed thoroughly and accurately to maintain data quality.
* Check the database or previous submissions to avoid creating duplicate entries for the same event and participant.
