# Remote Job Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for remote job interviews.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Email, Availability, Preferred Language, Time Zone, Job Type, and Job Status.
2. Select your availability from the options provided in the Availability field.
3. Select your preferred language from the options provided in the Preferred Language field.
4. Choose the correct time zone from the options provided in the Time Zone field.
5. Select the correct job type from the options provided in the Job Type field.
6. Select the correct job status from the options provided in the Job Status field.
7. For the Job Location field, select 'Remote' if you are interviewing remotely or 'Other' if you will be interviewing in person.
7. If you select 'Other' in the Job Location field, enter the address in the Job Location Address field.
8. For the Skills field, select 'Yes' if you have the required skills or 'No' if you do not.
9. For the Experience field, select 'Yes' if you have the required experience or 'No' if you do not.
10. If you are a recruiter or hiring manager, please ensure that you only use this form for its intended purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your available morning, afternoon, or evening time slots.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred language (English, French, Spanish, or Other).
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `select_one`, required): Select your time zone (EST, PST, CST, GMT, or Other).
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `select_multiple`, required): Select where you will be working (Remote, Home, or Other).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional): Enter your job description (if applicable).
* **Notice** (`notice`, `note`, optional): Enter any special notice (if applicable).
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether you have the required skills or not.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select whether you have the required experience or not.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of job you are interviewing for (Remote or On-site).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the job (Open, Closed, or Archived).
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who created this job (if applicable).
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the person who updated this job (if applicable).
* **Job URL** (`job_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the job (if applicable).
* **Job ID** (`job_id`, `number`, required): Enter the unique ID of the job.
* **Job Description URL** (`job_description_url`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the job description (if applicable).
* **Created Date** (`created_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this job was created.
* **Updated Date** (`updated_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date this job was updated (if applicable).
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the location of the job (Remote, Other).
* **Job Location Address** (`job_location_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the job location (if applicable).
* **Job Location Map** (`job_location_map`, `text`, optional): Enter the map URL of the job location (if applicable).
* **Job Tags** (`job_tags`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any job tags (if applicable).
* **Job Status Notes** (`job_status_notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any notes regarding the job status (if applicable).
