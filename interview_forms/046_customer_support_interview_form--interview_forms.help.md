# customer_support_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer support interview form is designed to gather information about a person's experience, skills, and availability for potential job opportunities within a customer support role.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each page and question carefully before proceeding.
- Enter your general information (page 1) to help us get to know you better.
- In the Job Application section, provide a brief description of why you're interested in the customer support role and why you're a good fit.
- For Work Experience, enter your relevant work experiences in reverse order, starting with the most recent first.
- Select your educational background from the options provided in the Education section.
- Choose relevant skills and certifications that apply to you in the Skills section.
- Select your preferred communication methods and availability in the Preferred Communication and Availability sections.
- Provide names and details of your references in the References and References 2 sections.
- Enter any additional relevant skills and languages you possess in the Skills and Languages section.
- If applicable, provide details about your work experiences and history in the Work Experience and Work History sections.
- Enter your preferred salary in the Salary section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`General Info`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Job Application** (`Job Application`, text, optional): Briefly describe why you're interested in the customer support role and why you think you're a good fit.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, text, optional): Enter your work experience in reverse order, starting with the most recent first.
* **Education** (`Education`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your level of education:
		- High School
		- Some College
		- Bachelors
		- Masters
		- PhD
* **Skills** (`Skills`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select relevant skills:
		- Communication
		- Problem Solving
		- Time Management
		- Team Management
		- Leadership
* **Certifications** (`Certifications`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose relevant certifications:
		- First Aid
		- Basic Life Support
		- Crisis Management
		- Data Analysis
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your available days:
		- Monday
		- Tuesday
		- Wednesday
		- Thursday
		- Friday
		- Saturday
		- Sunday
* **Preferred Communication** (`Preferred Communication`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Select your preferred communication methods:
		- Email
		- Phone
		- In-person
		- Video call
* **Preferred Contact** (`Preferred Contact`, text, optional): Enter any additional preferred contact information.
* **Preferred Language** (`Preferred Language`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your preferred languages:
		- English
		- Spanish
		- French
		- German
		- Other
* **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, text, optional): Enter your preferred work hours.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact information.
* **References** (`References`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your references:
		- John Doe
		- Jane Doe
* **References 2** (`References 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional reference information.
* **Skills and Languages** (`Skills and Languages`, text, optional): Enter any additional skills and languages you possess.
* **Work Experience 1** (`Work Experience 1`, text, optional): Enter any additional work experience information.
* **Work Experience 2** (`Work Experience 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional work experience information.
* **Work History** (`Work History`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose your work status:
		- Currently Working
		- Formerly Working
		- Not Employed
* **Salary** (`Salary`, text, optional): Enter your expected or preferred salary.
