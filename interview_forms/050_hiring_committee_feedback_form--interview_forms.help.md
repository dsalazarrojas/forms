# Hiring Committee Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from committee members on a candidate or nominee. Each field corresponds to a different committee member, and their input will help in the overall evaluation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for each committee member listed.
2. Provide a title for each committee member, if desired.
3. Enter a rating (1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high) for each committee member.
4. Enter feedback for each committee member, explaining your reasons for your rating.
5. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **committee_member** (Select One, Required: False): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are a member of the hiring committee.
* **committee_member_title** (Text, Required: False): Enter a title for yourself, if desired.
* **rating** (Number, Required: False): Enter a rating from 1 to 5 to rate the candidate.
* **feedback** (Note, Required: False): Explain your reasons for your rating in the provided text field.
* **committee_member_2**, **committee_member_3**, **committee_member_4**, **committee_member_5**, **committee_member_6** (Select Multiple, Required: False): Select "Yes" or "No" for each member of the hiring committee.
* **committee_member_2_title**, **committee_member_3_title**, **committee_member_4_title**, **committee_member_5_title**, **committee_member_6_title** (Text, Required: False): Enter a title for the corresponding committee member, if desired.
* **rating_2, rating_3, rating_4, rating_5, rating_6** (Number, Required: False): Enter a rating from 1 to 5 for each committee member.
* **feedback_2, feedback_3, feedback_4, feedback_5, feedback_6** (Note, Required: False): Enter feedback for each committee member, explaining your reasons for your rating.
* **submission** (Select One, Required: False): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have submitted feedback for all members of the committee.
