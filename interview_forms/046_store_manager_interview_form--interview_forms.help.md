# store_manager_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from store managers about their job openings. The form is intended to be used as a starting point for conducting interviews and gathering relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and understand the questions and fields presented.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary information and resources readily available to complete the form.
3. Begin by filling in the first field: **store_manager_interview_form**.
4. Move on to the remaining fields, filling in the required and optional information as instructed.
5. Review your completed form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **store_manager_interview_form** (`store_manager_interview_form`, text, optional): A brief description of the job opening, including job title, job summary, and any other relevant details.
- **job_opening_details** (`job_opening_details`, text, optional): Additional information about the job opening, such as job description, requirements, and qualifications.
- **candidate_information** (`candidate_information`, text, optional): Information about the candidate, including name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
- **communication_skills** (`communication_skills`, text, optional): A description of the communication skills required for the job, including verbal and written communication, presentation, and teamwork skills.
- **problem_solving** (`problem_solving`, text, optional): A description of the problem-solving skills required for the job, including analytical, decision-making, and adaptability skills.
- **candidate_experience** (`candidate_experience`, text, optional): Information about the candidate's relevant experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
- **availability** (`availability`, text, optional): A description of the candidate's availability for the job, including work schedule, location, and any other relevant details.
- **work_history** (`work_history`, text, optional): A brief description of the candidate's work history, including previous job roles, job duration, and reasons for leaving.

## Tips
- Be as detailed and concise as possible when filling in the fields.
- Use the provided space to provide as much information as necessary.
- Remember to review and proofread your completed form before submitting.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the form creators at form@gic.mx.
