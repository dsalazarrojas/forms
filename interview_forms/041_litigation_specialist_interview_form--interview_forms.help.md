# Litigation Specialist Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from Litigation Specialists about their personal and professional background, skills, and availability. The purpose of this form is to assist in the recruitment process and ensure that applicants are matched with suitable job opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the form with your honest and accurate information.
2. Ensure that you complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. This will be used to address you in any communication.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your professional email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Resume** (Note): Attach a copy of your resume or a brief summary of your experience and qualifications.
* **Job Title** (Select One):
	+ Partner
	+ Senior Associate
	+ Associate
	+ Other (Please specify)
* **Years of Experience** (Number): Enter the number of years you have been working as a Litigation Specialist.
* **Areas of Specialty** (Select Multiple):
	+ Corporate Law
	+ Family Law
	+ Personal Injury
* **Work History** (Text): Briefly describe your previous work experience and any relevant job responsibilities.
* **Education Background** (Text): Describe your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
* **Certifications** (Text): List any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
* **Languages Spoken** (Text): List any languages you are proficient in.
* **Available Dates** (Date): Enter the dates you are available to start work.
* **Desired Salary** (Number): Enter your desired salary range.
* **Job References** (Select Multiple):
	+ True (You are willing to provide job references)
	+ False (You are not willing to provide job references)
* **Time Zone** (Select One):
	+ Eastern Time Zone
	+ Central Time Zone
	+ Pacific Time Zone
* **Job Status** (Select One):
	+ Currently Working
	+ Not Currently Working
	+ Retired
* **Job Type** (Select One):
	+ Full-Time
	+ Part-Time
* **Available Start Date** (Date): Enter the date you are available to start work.
* **Available End Date** (Date): Enter the date you are no longer available to work.
* **Work Location** (Text): Specify the location you are willing to work in.
* **Job Description** (Text): Briefly describe the type of work you are looking for.
* **Required Skills** (Select Multiple):
	+ Yes (You have the required skills)
	+ No (You do not have the required skills)
* **Preferred Skills** (Select Multiple):
	+ Yes (You have preferred skills)
	+ No (You do not have preferred skills)
* **Required Tools** (Select Multiple):
	+ Yes (You have the required tools)
	+ No (You do not have the required tools)
* **Preferred Tools** (Select Multiple):
	+ Yes (You have preferred tools)
	+ No (You do not have preferred tools)

Note: Please ensure that you are honest and accurate in your responses. Incomplete or misleading information may impact your application process.
