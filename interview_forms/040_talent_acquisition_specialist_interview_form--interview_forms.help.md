# Talent Acquisition Specialist Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Talent Acquisition Specialist Interview Form is designed to gather essential information about candidates to support the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:
1. Fill out all the required fields marked as "required" to ensure that your application is complete.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3. Review your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information (Page 1)**: This section is for providing basic candidate information, such as their name, contact details, and other identifying features.
    * **Candidate Information (text)**: Enter the candidate's name and contact details accurately.
* **Work Experience (Page 2)**: Select all relevant work experiences from the provided options.
* **Skills (Page 3)**: Select the most relevant skills that you possess from the options provided.
* **Education (Page 4)**: Select all relevant educational qualifications you have.
* **Feedback (Page 5)**: Provide any feedback or comments about the candidate.
* **Contact Information (Page 6)**: Enter any additional contact information for the candidate.
* **Candidate Preferences (Page 7)**: Specify any special requests or preferences you have.
* **Interview Details (Page 8)**: Provide details about the interview, such as date, time, location.
* **Other Information (Page 9)**: Provide any additional information that is not included in the above sections.
* **Candidate Availability (Page 10)**: Specify your availability for the interview.
