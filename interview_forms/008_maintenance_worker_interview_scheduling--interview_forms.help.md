# Maintenance Worker Interview Scheduling - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to schedule interviews for maintenance workers. It is used to collect information about the candidate, interview details, and other relevant details for the interview process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the candidate(s) you want to schedule an interview for from the "Candidate Information" dropdown list.
2. Choose the interview details (if applicable) from the available options.
3. Select the facility location where the interview will take place (if applicable).
4. Choose the interview type and status (if applicable).
5. Enter any comments or notes about the interview (if applicable).
6. Enter the candidate's contact information (if applicable).
7. Schedule the interview date and time (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Interview**: (`schedule_interview`, date, false) This field allows you to select the date for the interview. You can select a date from the calendar and it will be displayed in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Candidate Information**: (`candidate_info`, select_multiple, true) Please select one or more candidates you want to schedule an interview for. The available options are "Candidate 1", "Candidate 2", "Candidate 3", and "Candidate 4".
* **Interview Details**: (`interview_details`, number, false) This field is not used in this form.
* **Work Order Number**: (`work_order_number`, select_one, false) You can select one of the available work order numbers: "1", "2", "3", "4", or "5".
* **Interview Time**: (`interview_time`, time, false) This field is not used in this form. You can enter the interview time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Facility Location**: (`facility_location`, select_multiple, false) You can select one or more facilities where the interview will take place. The available options are "HQ", "Location 1", "Location 2", and "Location 3", and "Location 4".
* **Interview Date**: (`interview_date`, date, false) This field is not used in this form. You can enter the interview date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Interview Time**: (`interview_time`, time, false) This field is not used in this form. You can enter the interview time in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Candidate Status**: (`candidate_status`, select_one, false) You can select the candidate's status: "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Candidate Rating**: (`candidate_rating`, number, false) This field is not used in this form. You can enter a number between 1 and 10 to rate the candidate.
* **Comments**: (`comments`, note, false) Enter any comments or notes about the interview.
* **Candidate Contact Info**: (`candidate_contact_info`, text, false) Enter the candidate's contact information.
* **Interview Type**: (`interview_type`, select_one, false) You can select the interview type: "On-site" or "Off-site".
* **Schedule Interview**: (`schedule_interview`, date, false) This field is not used in this form. You can select the interview date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Candidate Contact Info**: (`candidate_contact_info`, text, false) This field is not used in this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct candidate(s) and work order number for the interview.
* Choose the correct facility location for the interview.
* Enter accurate interview details and candidate contact information.
* Schedule the interview date and time correctly.
