# social_media_manager_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a candidate for a Social Media Manager position. It helps in getting a better understanding of the candidate's background, experience, and skills required for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal and professional information, following the instructions below for each section.
2. Be as detailed as possible while answering each question, as this will help the interviewer assess your suitability for the position.
3. If you're applying for multiple positions, please fill out the form separately for each position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: 
  • (ID 1, Introduction)
  • A brief introduction to the form.

* **Name**: 
  • (ID 2, Name)
  • Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

* **Email**: 
  • (ID 3, Email)
  • Enter your professional email address that you use for work or related purposes.

* **Job Title**: 
  • (ID 4, Job Title)
  • Enter the position you are applying for in our company.

* **Company**: 
  • (ID 5, Company)
  • List your current or previous company where you gained experience relevant to this position.

* **LinkedIn**: 
  • (ID 6, LinkedIn)
  • If you have a LinkedIn profile, please provide the link to it. If not, leave this blank.

* **Location**: 
  • (ID 7, Location)
  • Enter the location where you are based or available to work.

* **Experience**: 
  • (ID 8, Experience)
  • Enter the number of years of experience you have in the field of social media management.

* **Salary**: 
  • (ID 9, Salary)
  • Enter your expected or current salary for this position.

* **Education**: 
  • (ID 10, Education)
  • Briefly describe your education background, including any relevant courses or qualifications.

* **About**: 
  • (ID 11, About)
  • Write a short paragraph about your background and interests.

* **Skills**: 
  • (ID 12, Skills)
  • Select all the skills you have that match the job requirements, such as content creation, analytics, etc.

* **Available to Start**: 
  • (ID 13, Available to Start)
  • Enter the date when you are available to start work in this position.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure a smooth evaluation process.
* Use bullet points or numbered lists where applicable, especially for the "About" and "Skills" fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the interviewer for clarification.
