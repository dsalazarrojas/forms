# Freelancer Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freelancer Interview Form is designed to gather essential information about your availability, experience, and payment preferences as a freelancer. This information will help us understand your qualifications and determine the best fit for potential projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your **First Name** field with your given name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Email** address for communication purposes.
4. Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you have a **Job Title**.
5. Choose a **Job Title** from the dropdown menu if you selected **Yes** in the previous question.
6. Select your **Available Start Date** and **Available End Date** using the date picker.
7. Provide a brief description of your **Experience** in the text field.
8. Describe your **Availability** in the text field.
9. Enter your **Rate** per hour or project (optional).
10. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Payment Terms** and choose multiple options if applicable.
11. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Payment Frequency**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Job Title**: Select **Yes** if you have a job title and choose from the dropdown menu if selected.
* **Available Start Date**: Select a date you are available to start work on a project.
* **Available End Date**: Select a date you will be available to finish work on a project.
* **Experience**: Describe your relevant experience and skills in a few sentences.
* **Availability**: Describe your availability for the project duration in a few sentences.
* **Rate**: Enter your rate per hour or project (optional).
* **Payment Terms**: Select **Yes** if you agree to the payment terms and choose multiple options if applicable.
* **Payment Frequency**: Select **Yes** if you are willing to accept payment on a regular frequency.
