# Semi Structured Interview Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
This semi-structured interview guide is designed to help gather information about a product or service through a combination of open-ended and closed-ended questions. It's a tool for data collection, possibly for customer feedback or satisfaction surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Make sure to select all the required options for each page.
3. Provide clear and concise answers to all the questions.
4. Use the notes fields to jot down any additional information that may not be covered in the other fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### first_page (Page 1)

* **first_page** (text): This is the first page of the interview guide. Please fill in this field with the relevant information.

### questions (Page 2)

* **questions** (select_multiple): Please select all the relevant questions that apply to the topic of the interview. You can select multiple options.

### notes (Page 3)

* **notes** (note): This field is for the interviewer to note down any additional information that may not be covered in the other fields.

### date (Page 4)

* **date** (date): Please fill in the date of the interview.

### time (Page 5)

* **time** (time): Please fill in the time of the interview.

### location (Page 6)

* **location** (text): This field is for the location of the interview.

### contact (Page 7)

* **contact** (text): This field is for any contact information related to the interview.

### email (Page 8)

* **email** (email): Please fill in the email of the contact person.

### interviewer_notes (Page 9)

* **interviewer_notes** (note): This field is for the interviewer to note down any additional information that may not be covered in the other fields.

### additional_notes (Page 10)

* **additional_notes** (note): This field is for the interviewer to note down any other additional information that may not be covered in the other fields.

### other (Page 11)

* **other** (text): This field is for any other information that may not be covered in the other fields.

### other_notes (Page 12)

* **other_notes** (note): This field is for the interviewer to note down any other additional information that may not be covered in the other fields.

### interviewer (Page 13)

* **interviewer** (text): This field is for the name of the interviewer.

### interviewer_title (Page 14)

* **interviewer_title** (text): This field is for the title of the interviewer.

### interviewer_phone (Page 15)

* **interviewer_phone** (text): This field is for the phone number of the interviewer.

### interviewer_email (Page 16)

* **interviewer_email** (email): This field is for the email of the interviewer.

### interview_location (Page 17)

* **interview_location** (text): This field is for the location of the interview.

### interview_date (Page 18)

* **interview_date** (date): Please fill in the date of the interview.

### interview_time (Page 19)

* **interview_time** (time): Please fill in the time of the interview.

### interviewers (Page 20)

* **interviewers** (select_multiple): Please select all the interviewers who will be conducting the interview.

### other_interviewers (Page 21)

* **other_interviewers** (select_multiple): This field is for selecting any other interviewers who may be conducting the interview.
