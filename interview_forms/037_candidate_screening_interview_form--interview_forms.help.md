# Candidate Screening Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candidate Screening Interview Form is a critical part of the hiring process that helps determine a candidate's suitability for a position. It assesses their qualifications, experience, and fit for the role, ensuring that the hiring process is efficient and effective.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately.
4. Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly stated as optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`screened_candidate_name`, Text, Required): Enter the candidate's full name as on their application.
* **Position Applied For** (`screening_position_title`, Text, Required): Specify the title of the position the candidate is applying for.
* **Interviewer Name** (`screening_interviewer_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as the screener.
* **Date of Screening** (`screening_interview_date`, Date, Required): Record the date of the screening interview.
* **Basic Qualifications Met** (`basic_qualifications_check`, Select One, Required): Assess if the candidate meets the basic qualifications for the position.
* **Relevant Background** (`background_relevance_rating`, Select One, Required): Evaluate how well the candidate's previous experience matches the role.
* **Salary Expectations** (`screening_salary_expectations`, Number, Optional): Record the candidate's salary expectations.
* **Expected Start Date** (`screening_start_date_pref`, Date, Optional): Note the candidate's expected start date.
* **Willingness to Work Schedule** (`schedule_willingness_check`, Select One, Required): Assess the candidate's willingness to work the required schedule.
* **Current Employment Status** (`screening_current_employment`, Text, Required): Indicate if the candidate is currently working or looking for work.
* **Reason for Seeking a New Role** (`search_reason_text`, Text, Required): Ask the candidate about their reason for seeking a new role.
* **Communication Proficiency** (`screening_communication_rating`, Select One, Required): Evaluate the candidate's communication skills during the interview.
* **Technical Proficiency Level** (`screening_technical_proficiency`, Select One, Required): Assess the candidate's technical skills and experience (if applicable).
* **Cultural Alignment Potential** (`screening_cultural_alignment`, Select One, Required): Assess the candidate's cultural fit with the team.
* **Move to Next Round Check** (`move_to_next_round_check`, Select One, Required): Indicate if the candidate should proceed to the next round.
* **Screening Notes** (`screening_notes_field`, Text, Optional): Add any additional notes for the hiring manager.
* **Submission Date** (`screening_submission_date`, Date, Optional): Record the date the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Always complete the form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to the hiring manager.
