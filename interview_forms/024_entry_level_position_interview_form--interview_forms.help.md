# Entry Level Position Interview Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Entry Level Position Interview Form is used to collect information from candidates applying for an entry-level position. This form helps the hiring team to have a better understanding of the candidate's background, skills, and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Start by providing information about yourself as a candidate, including your name and any other relevant details.
2.  Enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number.
3.  Describe your previous employment history, including any relevant work experience or projects you have been involved in.
4.  List your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
5.  Specify your skills, such as programming languages, tools, or software proficiency.
6.  Share your relevant experience, including any internships, volunteer work, or projects you have completed.
7.  List any relevant certifications or licenses you hold.
8.  Provide references, including their name, title, and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Candidate Information
*   **Candidate Information** (`candidate_information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief introduction about yourself as a candidate. Please enter a short description of your background, qualifications, and interests.

### Contact Information
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your email address and phone number. Please ensure that your email address is up-to-date and your phone number is accurate.

### Employment History
*   **Employment History** (`employment_history`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to describe your previous work experience, including any relevant projects or internships you have completed. Please provide a clear and concise description of your work experience.

### Education
*   **Education** (`education`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to list your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications. Please specify the name of the institution, degree, and relevant courses or specializations.

### Skills
*   **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to specify your skills, including programming languages, tools, or software proficiency. Please be specific about your skills and experience.

### Experience
*   **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to share your relevant experience, including any internships, volunteer work, or projects you have completed. Please provide a clear and concise description of your experience.

### Certifications
*   **Certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to list any relevant certifications or licenses you hold. Please specify the name of the certification, issuing organization, and relevant details.

### References
*   **References** (`references`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to provide references, including their name, title, and contact information. Please ensure that you have permission from your references before sharing their contact information.

## Tips
*   Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
*   Use clear and concise language when describing your background, qualifications, and experience.
*   Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
*   Ensure that you have permission from your references before sharing their contact information.
