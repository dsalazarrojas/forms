# Permaculture Design Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register for the Permaculture Design Course. Fill out this form to provide your registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the "Registration Details" field with any additional information you wish to provide.
2.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3.  Enter your email in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select your preferred course session from the "Course Session" dropdown menu.
6.  Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
7.  Enter your payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
8.  Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Registration Details**: A text field to collect any additional details about your registration.
*   **Name**: A field to collect your name, which will likely be used for course materials or contact purposes.
*   **Email**: A field to collect your email, probably for communication or notifications about the course.
*   **Phone**: A field to collect your phone number, which will be used for communication or emergency contact purposes.
*   **Course Session**: A dropdown menu where you can select which course session you wish to attend.
*   **Payment Method**: A dropdown menu where you can choose how you wish to pay for the course, likely between "PayPal" and "Bank Transfer".
*   **Payment Amount**: A field to collect the payment amount, which will likely be used for course fee purposes.
*   **Notes**: A field for any additional notes or comments you might have about your registration.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful course registration.
*   Double-check your selected course session and payment method to ensure accuracy.
*   Be sure to enter the correct payment amount for your selected course session.
