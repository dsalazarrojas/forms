# Gymnastics Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by users who are interested in enrolling in gymnastics classes. It collects relevant information to ensure that you are matched with the best class for your needs and abilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure that you have all the necessary information required in the form.
* Enter your user information, including your name and any relevant details.
* Enter your contact information, including any contact person's name, phone number, and email address.
* Select the class schedule that best fits your needs.
* Describe your gymnastics experience and any relevant skills or training.
* Choose your class times and ensure that they align with the schedule you selected.
* Enter the contact information of your emergency contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name, age, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your contact person's full name, phone number, and email address.
* **Class Schedule** (`class_schedule`, `text`, required: false): Please select the class schedule that best fits your needs, such as morning, afternoon, or evening classes.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, required: false): Describe your gymnastics experience, including any relevant skills or training you have.
* **Class Times** (`class_times`, `text`, required: false): Choose your class times, ensuring they align with the schedule you selected.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name, phone number, and email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to submit your enrollment application.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* We will review your submission and contact you to confirm your enrollment details.
