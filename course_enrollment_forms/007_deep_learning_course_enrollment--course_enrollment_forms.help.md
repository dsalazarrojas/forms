# Deep Learning Course Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect application information from individuals interested in enrolling in the Deep Learning course. The form helps us understand their names, contact details, and course preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to contact you if needed.
3. Provide your Phone Number for communication purposes.
4. Enter the name of the course you are interested in enrolling in (Course Name).
5. Select your Applicant Readiness Level, which indicates your current level of knowledge and experience in the course topic.
6. Enter the name of the team you are applying to join (Team Name), if applicable.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the Review Note field, if needed.
8. Enter the name of the cohort you are applying to join (Cohort Name).
9. If desired, provide a Follow-up Message to us about any additional information you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name, e.g., John.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname, e.g., Doe.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address, e.g., johndoe@example.com. This field is optional but recommended for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number, e.g., 123-456-7890.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you are interested in enrolling in, e.g., Deep Learning Course.
* **Applicant Readiness Level**: Select the level that best describes your current level of knowledge and experience in the course topic. Please choose from the options:
	+ Level 1: Beginner
	+ Level 2: Intermediate
	+ Level 3: Advanced
* **Team Name**: If you are applying to join a team, enter the name of the team you are interested in joining.
* **Review Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Cohort Name**: Enter the name of the cohort you are applying to join.
* **Follow-up Message**: If you have any additional information to share, enter it here.
