# Pediatrics Virtual Course Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users interested in enrolling in the Pediatrics Virtual Course. It is meant to be completed by the individual enrolling in the course. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. The form is divided into multiple pages, each with a set of fields. Fill in the required fields accurately, and you will be able to submit the form at the end of the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (Page 1)
* **User Info** (text): 
  This field is for you to enter any relevant information about yourself. This information will help us understand your needs and provide a more personalized experience.

### Name (Page 1)
* **Name** (text): 
  Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents. This is the name we will use to address you in our communication.

### Age (Page 1)
* **Age** (number): 
  Enter your age, which will help us determine your eligibility for the course.

### Contact Info (Page 2)
* **Contact Info** (select multiple): 
  Select "Yes" if you are willing to provide your contact information for us to reach out to you regarding course updates and notifications. Select "No" if you would like to receive no contact information.

### Email (Page 2)
* **Email** (email): 
  Enter your email address that we can use to send you course updates and notifications. Please make sure it is a valid email address that you regularly check.

### Phone (Page 2)
* **Phone** (text): 
  Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you in case of emergencies or further communication.
