# Lifeguard Certification Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather participant information and course details for the Lifeguard Certification Course. Fill out this form to enroll in the course and receive more information on the course schedule, price, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out all required fields.
*   Answer all questions clearly and accurately.
*   Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Info (Page 1)
*   **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of your interest in the Lifeguard Certification Course. This can include any relevant details about your qualifications, experience, or motivation for enrolling in the course.

### Course Selection (Page 1)
*   **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in enrolling in the Lifeguard Certification Course. Select 'No' otherwise.

### Payment Info (Page 2)
*   **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional payment information, such as payment method or payment status, if applicable.

### Participant Name (Page 2)
*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a participant in the Lifeguard Certification Course.

### Participant Email (Page 3)
*   **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used to communicate course details and updates to you.

### Participant Phone (Page 3)
*   **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number, which will be used to contact you in case of course updates or changes.

### Course Name (Page 4)
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in, which will be used to identify you as a participant in the course.

### Start Date (Page 4)
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to start the course. This will help us schedule the course accordingly.

### End Date (Page 5)
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to complete the course. This will help us track your course progress.

### Price (Page 5)
*   **Price** (`price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the course. This will help us determine the course fee for you.
