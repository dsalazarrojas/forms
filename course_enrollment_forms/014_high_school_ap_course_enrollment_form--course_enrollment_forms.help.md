# High School AP Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for Advanced Placement (AP) course enrollment. It includes student, teacher, and parent information to ensure a seamless enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required information for the student:
	* **Student ID**: Enter the unique student ID.
	* **Course ID**: Enter the ID for the course the student wishes to enroll in.
	* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
	* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number (if applicable).
	* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
	* **Student Grade**: Select the student's grade level (high school or middle school).
	* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name.
	* **Parent Contact**: Enter the parent's contact information.
	* **Student Signature**: Have the student sign the form to confirm their enrollment.
2. For the teacher:
	* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
	* **Teacher Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
	* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
3. Course information:
	* **Course Section**: Select whether the course is part of a section.
	* **Course Request Approved**: Select whether the course request has been approved.
	* **Course Request Denied**: Select whether the course request has been denied.
	* **Course Request Pending**: Select whether the course request is pending.
	* **Course Request Comments**: Enter any comments related to the course request.
	* **School Year**: Select the school year the student is enrolling in.
4. Date and time information:
	* **Enrollment Date**: Enter the date the student enrolled in the course.
	* **Enrollment Time**: Enter the time the student enrolled in the course.
