# Coaching Course Assignment Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow participants to submit their assignments for a coaching course. It is used to gather necessary information about the participant and their assignment submission.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, email address, and student ID or participant ID.
2. Enter the course name and instructor name.
3. Provide details about the assignment you are submitting, including the assignment name or title and due date.
4. Upload your completed assignment in PDF, Word, or text format.
5. Confirm that your submission is original work and acknowledge the academic integrity policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where the course instructor can contact you.
* **Student ID or Participant ID**: Enter your student ID or participant ID as required by your institution.
* **Phone Number**: If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number (optional).
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the coaching course you are enrolled in.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of your course instructor.
* **Assignment Name or Title**: Enter the name or title of the assignment you are submitting.
* **Assignment Number**: If applicable, enter the assignment number as provided by your instructor.
* **Due Date**: Enter the date by which the assignment was due.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting the assignment.
* **Upload Assignment File**: Attach your completed assignment in PDF, Word, or text format.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or explanations about your assignment (optional).
* **Is this a late submission?**: If the assignment was submitted late, select "Yes, late" and provide an explanation for the late submission.
* **If late, please explain why**: Enter a brief explanation for why the assignment was submitted late.
* **I confirm this is my original work**: Select "I Confirm" to declare that this is your original work.
* **I acknowledge the academic integrity policy**: Select "I Acknowledge" to confirm that you have read and understand the academic integrity policy.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your submission carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the course instructor or administrator.
* Ensure that your submission is original work and complies with the academic integrity policy.
