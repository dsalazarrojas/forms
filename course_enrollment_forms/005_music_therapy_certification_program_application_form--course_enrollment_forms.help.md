# Music Therapy Certification Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Therapy Certification Program Application Form is used to collect information from individuals who want to apply for the music therapy certification program. This form helps the certification committee evaluate applications and make informed decisions about admission to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields to provide your personal details.
2. Enter your Email address to allow us to contact you with updates.
3. Provide your Phone Number so we can get in touch with you.
4. Enter your Date of Birth for verification purposes.
5. Describe your Music Therapy Background, including any relevant experience or qualifications.
6. Specify your Education, including any diplomas or certifications you've earned.
7. Check the Music Therapy Certification box to confirm whether you hold a certification or not.
8. Select the Submit button to send your application.
9. Confirm that you have submitted the application by selecting the Confirm Submission option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Music Therapy Background** (`music_therapy_background`, `text`, required): Describe your music therapy background, including any relevant experience or qualifications.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required): List your education background, including any diplomas or certifications you've earned.
* **Music Therapy Certification** (`music_therapy_certification`, `select_one`, required): Check the box next to "Certified Music Therapist" if you hold a music therapy certification; otherwise, select "Not Certified".
* **Submit** (`submit`, `note`, required): Click the Submit button to send your application.
* **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" to confirm that you have submitted the application and agree to the application process.

## Tips
Before submitting the form, ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly. Double-check your Music Therapy Certification status to ensure it accurately reflects your qualifications.
