# Course Substitution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Substitution Form is used to request a course substitution in your academic program. Please use this form to provide all necessary information and details about the course substitution you are requesting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Choose the current and new courses from the dropdown menus.
3. Provide a brief reason for the substitution in the "Reason" field.
4. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).
5. Enter your email and phone number for contact information.
6. Select the date and time of the substitution request.
7. If necessary, specify the tool assigned to you for the new course.
8. Finally, click on "Submit" to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (User Name, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID or transcript.
* **Current Course** (current_course, select_one, required/optional): Select the current course that you would like to substitute.
* **New Course** (new_course, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more new courses that you would like to take in place of the current course.
* **Reason** (reason, text, required/optional): Provide a brief explanation for the course substitution request. This should include any relevant information about your academic goals or needs.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your course substitution request.
* **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be reached for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for contact purposes.
* **Date** (Date, date, required/optional): Select the date when you would like to start the new course.
* **Time** (Time, time, required/optional): Select the time when you would like to start the new course.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool, text, required/optional): If you have been assigned a tool for the new course, specify it here.
* **Submitted By** (submitted_by, text, required/optional): This field will be populated automatically with your user name.

Note: Please ensure that you have reviewed and understand the submission requirements and deadlines before submitting this form. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your academic department or support team.
