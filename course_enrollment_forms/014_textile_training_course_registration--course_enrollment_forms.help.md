# Textile Training Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to enroll in the Textile Training Course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the course enrollments you are interested in.
2. Provide your participant details by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each option.
3. Enter your participant name, email, phone number, and course name.
4. Select the start and end dates for the course.
5. Enter the location and course provider for the course.
6. Provide your consent for general participant information and course-specific details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Enrollments** (1, Select Multiple, Required: No): Select which courses you are interested in enrolling in. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Participant Details** (2, Select Multiple, Required: No): Select "Yes" or "No" for general participant information. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Consent** (3, Select One, Required: No): Select "Yes" or "No" for course-specific details. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Participant Name** (4, Text, Required: Yes): Enter your name as the participant.
* **Email** (5, Email, Required: Yes): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (6, Text, Required: Yes): Enter your phone number.
* **Course Name** (7, Text, Required: Yes): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Start Date** (8, Date, Required: Yes): Select the start date for the course.
* **End Date** (9, Date, Required: Yes): Select the end date for the course.
* **Location** (10, Text, Required: Yes): Enter the location of the course.
* **Course Provider** (11, Text, Required: Yes): Enter the name of the course provider.
* **Consent 1** (12, Select One, Required: No): Select "Yes" or "No" for general participant information. Options are "Yes" and "No".
