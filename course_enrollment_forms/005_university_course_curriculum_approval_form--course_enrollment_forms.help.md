# University Course Curriculum Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Course Curriculum Approval Form is used to review and approve university courses. This form helps ensure that the curriculum meets the required standards and is consistent with the university's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate and detailed information about the course.
2. Ensure that you have reviewed the course details carefully and are aware of the university's curriculum approval process.
3. Complete all required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*).
4. Select all relevant options for Faculty Approval and Committee Approval 2 as required.
5. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **University Course Title** (*Required*): Enter the title of the course to be reviewed.
* **Faculty Approval** (Select multiple):
	+ Check all applicable options for faculty approval.
* **Course Changes** (Required):
	+ Enter any changes made to the course since the last review.
* **Curriculum Information** (Optional):
	+ Enter any relevant details about the curriculum.
* **Committee Feedback** (Optional):
	+ Enter any feedback or comments from the committee.
* **Approval Decision** (Required):
	+ Select 'Yes' to approve the course.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional):
	+ Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Faculty Approval 2** (Optional):
	+ Check all applicable options for faculty approval.
* **Page 10** (Optional):
	+ Enter any additional information.
* **Page 11** (Optional):
	+ Enter any additional information.
* **Committee Approval 2** (Optional):
	+ Check all applicable options for committee approval.
