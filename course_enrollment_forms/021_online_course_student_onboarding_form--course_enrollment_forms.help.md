# Online Course Student Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students about their online course onboarding experience. Please follow the instructions below to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your course code in the "course_code" field.
2. Fill in your name in the "student_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field (optional).
5. Enter any relevant information about your address in the "student_address" field (optional).
6. Select the date you enrolled in the "enrollment_date" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Code (1)** (`course_code`, text, optional): Enter the code for the online course you are enrolled in.
- **Student Name (2)** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email (3)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
- **Contact Number (4)** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a phone number that the institution can contact you on if needed.
- **Student Address (5)** (`student_address`, note, optional): Enter any relevant information about your address, such as your physical location or other relevant details.
- **Enrollment Date (6)** (`enrollment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you enrolled in the online course.

## Tips
- Please fill out as much information as you can, as this will help the institution provide you with better service.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the institution for clarification.
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
