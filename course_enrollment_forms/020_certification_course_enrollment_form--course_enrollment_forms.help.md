# Certification Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students enrolling in certification courses. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all necessary details are collected for administrative and educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Review and understand the terms and conditions before signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Enrollment Date** (Enrollment Date): The date you are enrolling in the course.
- **Student Name** (Student Name): Your full legal name.
- **Email Address** (Email Address): Your email for course communications.
- **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Your contact phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth): Your student date of birth.
- **Mailing Address** (Mailing Address): Your full mailing address.
- **Certification Program** (Certification Program): Select the certification program you are enrolling in.
- **Course Level** (Course Level): Choose the level of the certification course you wish to attend.
- **Preferred Start Date** (Preferred Start Date): The date you would like to begin the course.
- **Learning Format** (Learning Format): Choose your preferred method of instruction.
- **Prior Experience** (Prior Experience): Indicate your level of prior experience in the field.
- **Educational Background** (Educational Background): Select your highest level of education completed.
- **Employer Name** (Employer Name): If applicable, provide your current employer's name.
- **Employer Sponsored** (Employer Sponsored): Indicate if your employer is sponsoring your enrollment.
- **Payment Method** (Payment Method): Choose how you will pay for the course.
- **Special Accommodations** (Special Accommodations): Indicate if you require any special accommodations.
- **Accommodation Details** (Accommodation Details): If you require special accommodations, specify your needs.
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (How Did You Hear About Us): Indicate the source of your program awareness.
- **Career Goals** (Career Goals): Describe how this certification will help your career.
- **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions): Confirm that you have read and agree to the enrollment terms.
- **Refund Policy Acknowledgment** (Refund Policy Acknowledgment): Confirm that you understand the refund policy.
- **Student Signature** (Student Signature): Type your full legal name as a signature.
- **Signature Date** (Signature Date): Date of enrollment.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are completed to successfully submit the form.
