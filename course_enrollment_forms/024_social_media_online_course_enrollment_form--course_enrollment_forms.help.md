# Social Media Online Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for enrolling in an online course on social media platforms.

<thinking>
This form is intended to be used for internal purposes only. Please ensure that you are authorized to access this form and that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your password for the online course in the `Password` field.
4. Confirm your email address in the `Confirm E-mail` field.
5. Select the desired enrollment date and time in the `Enrollment Date` and `Enrollment Time` fields.
6. Choose one option from the `Select Option 1` dropdown menu.
7. Select multiple options from the `Select Option 2` dropdown menu.
8. Add any additional notes about your enrollment in the `Note` field.
9. Enter your phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
10. If you want to receive email notifications, add your email address in the `Email List` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollment Form** (`enrollment_form`, text, optional): This is the main form title that you will see on the screen.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the online course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you will receive notifications and updates about the online course.
* **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Enter your password for the online course. Make sure it's strong and secure!
* **Confirm E-mail** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Confirm your email address to ensure correct enrollment information.
* **Enrollment Date** (`enrollment_date`, date, optional): Choose the date you would like to start the online course.
* **Enrollment Time** (`enrollment_time`, time, optional): Choose the time you would like to start the online course.
* **Select Option 1** (`select_option_1`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list to customize your online course experience.
* **Select Option 2** (`select_option_2`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list to customize your online course experience.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your enrollment.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Email List** (`email_list`, email, optional): If you want to receive email notifications, add your email address in this field.
