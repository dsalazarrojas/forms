# Digital Learning Platform Beta Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in a beta testing phase for a digital learning platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Information**:
	* This section is optional, but it helps us understand who you are.
	* Please fill in your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Tell us about your **Education**:
	* Share with us your academic background, relevant courses, or certifications.
	* This will help us tailor our platform to your learning needs.
3. Describe your **Devices**:
	* Please list the devices you use to access the internet, such as smartphones, laptops, or tablets.
4. Share your **Motivation**:
	* Tell us why you want to use our platform, what you hope to achieve, or what features you're looking for.
5. Provide your **Consent**:
	* Please read and agree to our terms of service before proceeding.
6. Submit your application:
	* Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (optional): Your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Education** (optional): Share your academic background, relevant courses, or certifications.
* **Devices** (optional): List the devices you use to access the internet.
* **Motivation** (optional): Share your reasons for wanting to use our platform.
* **Consent** (required): Read and agree to our terms of service before proceeding.
* **Consent** is required to complete this form. 

## Tips
* Please note that this form is for beta testing purposes only and we will not use your data for any other purpose.
* We will only process one application per user.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
* Your data will be handled in accordance with our terms of service.
