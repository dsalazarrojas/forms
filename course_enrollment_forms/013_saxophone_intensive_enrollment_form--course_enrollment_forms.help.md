# Saxophone Intensive Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to provide their details and preferences for enrolling in the Saxophone Intensive course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required to fill out the form correctly.
2. Fill out the form in the provided format, with each section dedicated to a specific topic.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure you have provided accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (1, text, optional): This section asks for your name and any other relevant details about you as a participant.
* **Participant Name** (2, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in course materials and communication.
* **Participant Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication about course updates and schedules.
* **Participant Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication that may be required.
* **Music Style** (5, select_one, optional): Choose the type of music you are most interested in: Jazz, Classical, Pop, Rock, Folk, or Country.
* **Preferred Session** (6, select_one, optional): Select the time slot you prefer for your session: 8:00am - 9:00am, 9:00am - 10:00am, and so on.
* **Preferred Date** (7, date, optional): Select a date for your session, using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Preferred Time** (8, time, optional): Choose a time within your selected session that suits you.
* **Participant Notes** (9, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Course Enrollment** (10, select_one, optional): Choose which course you are enrolling in: Saxophone Intensive, Music Theory, Music Performance, Ensemble, Music History, or Other (Please specify).
* **Follow-up** (11, date, optional): Enter a date for any follow-up communication.
* **Submission** (12, date, optional): Enter a date for the submission of the form.
* **Follow-up Email** (13, email, optional): Enter an email address for follow-up communication.
* **Submission Status** (14, select_one, optional): Specify the status of your submission: Pending, Confirmed, Declined, or Rescheduled.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any errors.
* Ensure that you have entered all required information.
* If you have any issues or need clarification, contact the course organizers.
* Please be considerate of other participants and course organizers when filling out the form.
* This form will be used to plan and organize the course, so accurate information is crucial.
