# Customer Journey Optimization Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in the Customer Journey Optimization course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details, such as name, email, and phone number.
2. Share your learning goals and preferences for the course session.
3. Provide your contact information and participant notes (if any).
4. Select the desired session date and time.
5. Choose the duration of the session.
6. Specify if you need a session in a specific time zone.
7. Select the location of the session.
8. Provide any additional session notes.
9. Sign the participant agreement form.
10. Confirm the participant agreement status.
11. Select the assigned tool for the course.
12. Enter the participant agreement status reason (if applicable).
13. Enter the form version and ID.
14. Enter the submitted by field (likely for tracking purposes).
15. Enter the submission date.
