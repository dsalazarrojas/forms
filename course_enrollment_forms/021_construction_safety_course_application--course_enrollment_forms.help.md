# Construction Safety Course Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for the Construction Safety Course, a training program aimed at providing essential safety knowledge to construction industry professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. Provide your contact email address for course confirmation and materials.
3. Enter your mobile phone number for day-of-course contact.
4. If applicable, enter your company's name.
5. Select your preferred course session date from the provided options.
6. List any prior relevant safety training you have completed.
7. If necessary, note any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
8. Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **Required**): Enter the email address to be used for course confirmation and materials.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter your mobile phone number for day-of-course contact. This is an optional field, but it's recommended to ensure smooth communication.
* **Employer** (`employer`, text, **Optional**): If you are an employee of a company, enter the company name to help us better understand your affiliation.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, **Required**): Select your preferred session date for the course.
* **Prior Training** (`prior_training`, text, **Optional**): List any prior relevant safety training you have completed to help us tailor the course content.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, **Optional**): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the course, please note them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, **Required**): Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are completing this form as a representative of your company and have permission to do so.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at your company's administration office.
* We will review and confirm your course enrollment shortly after submitting this form.
