# Course Enrollment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students for course enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Student Name** in the text field.
2. Type your **Student Email** in the email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
3. Enter your **Course Code**, which is likely a unique identifier for the course you are enrolling in.
4. Enter your **Institution ID**, which identifies your institution or academic organization.
5. Provide your **Student Phone** number.
6. Select the **Declaration Type** from the dropdown menu (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
7. If you have any comments or additional information to provide, type them in the **Institution Comments** and **Student Comments** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (text): Enter your full name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Student Email** (email): Type your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Course Code** (text): Enter the unique code for the course you are enrolling in.
* **Institution ID** (text): Enter your institution's ID or unique identifier.
* **Student Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Enrollment Date** (date): This field is currently not required, but if filled, it would be the date of your enrollment.
* **Declaration Type** (select_one): Select one of the three options from the dropdown menu to categorize your enrollment.
* **Institution Comments** (note): Provide any additional comments or information about your institution or enrollment.
* **Student Comments** (note): Provide any additional comments or information about yourself or enrollment.
