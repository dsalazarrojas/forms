This form is intended to be a tool for instructors to record the withdrawal of students from a course, allowing them to track student engagement and provide accurate headcounts for resource allocation. To ensure that the form is completed accurately and efficiently, please ensure that you are using it for its intended purpose and not duplicating effort elsewhere in your process.

This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form and explain each field in detail to help you do so effectively.

**Purpose**
-----------

The Course Withdrawal Form is designed to collect information on students who are withdrawing from a course. This data will be used to update course rosters and track student engagement.

**How To Complete This Form**
-------------------------

1. Determine whether the student has withdrawn from the course by selecting "Yes" or "No" on the "course_withdrawal_form_course" field.
2. If the student has withdrawn, provide a clear reason for their withdrawal in the "reason_for_withdrawal" field.
3. If applicable, record the date and time of their last attendance in the "date_of_last_attendance" and "time_of_last_attendance" fields.

**Field-by-Field Explanation**
---------------------------

* **course_withdrawal_form_course (Yes/No)**: Select "Yes" to indicate that the student has withdrawn from the course, or "No" to indicate that they have not.
* **reason_for_withdrawal (text)**: Enter a clear and concise reason for the student's withdrawal. This will help instructors understand the circumstances surrounding their departure.
* **date_of_last_attendance (date)**: If you are aware of the date of the student's last attendance, enter it here. This helps instructors track student engagement and update rosters accurately.
* **time_of_last_attendance (time)**: If you have recorded the time of the student's last attendance, enter it here. This information is useful for instructors to review attendance patterns.

**Tips**
----

* Always use this form for student withdrawals to maintain accurate and up-to-date records.
* Ensure you are using the correct date and time for the student's last attendance.
* If the student's status changes, update this form accordingly to reflect the new status.

Please note that this form is a tool to support instructors in tracking student engagement and updating course rosters. If you have any questions or concerns about using this form, please reach out to your support team for assistance.
