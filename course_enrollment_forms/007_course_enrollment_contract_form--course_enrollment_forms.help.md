# course_enrollment_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for course enrollment contracts. This includes student name, course name, contract date, contract time, student signature, course instructor name, and contract term length.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the course in the "Course Name" field.
3. Select the contract date in the "Contract Date" field.
4. Choose the contract time in the "Contract Time" field.
5. Sign and date the "Student Signature" field as the student's agreement to the contract terms.
6. Enter the name of the course instructor in the "Course Instructor Name" field.
7. Enter the length of the contract term in the "Contract Term Length" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, `text`, required/optional: no): Enter the name of the student who is enrolling in the course.
* **Course Name** (`Course Name`, `text`, required/optional: no): Enter the name of the course being enrolled in.
* **Contract Date** (`Contract Date`, `date`, required/optional: no): Select the date of the contract.
* **Contract Time** (`Contract Time`, `time`, required/optional: no): Choose the time of the contract.
* **Student Signature** (`Student Signature`, `note`, required/optional: no): Sign and date the contract as the student's agreement to the terms.
* **Course Instructor Name** (`Course Instructor Name`, `text`, required/optional: no): Enter the name of the instructor for the course.
* **Contract Term Length** (`Contract Term Length`, `number`, required/optional: no): Enter the length of the contract term.

## Tips
This form is designed to be completed in conjunction with other relevant information. Make sure to check the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
