# Electronics Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for an electronics course. Fill out this form to provide your details and choose a payment method to secure your spot in the course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your user details in the **User Details** field.
2. Choose your desired course from the **Course Choice** field.
3. Provide your payment details in the **Payment Details** field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the **Payment Method** field.
5. Enter the payment amount in the **Payment Amount** field.
6. Provide any additional student details in the **Student Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (User Details, text, required: false): This field is for entering your personal details such as name, email, and password.
* **Course Choice** (Course Choice, select_one, required: false): Choose the electronics course you want to enroll in. Please select one of the available options: Course 1, Course 2, or Course 3.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, required: false): Enter your payment information such as credit card number, expiration date, and security code.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required: false): Select how you want to pay for the course. You can choose from Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Amount** (Payment Amount, number, required: false): Enter the amount you want to pay for the course.
* **Student Details** (Student Details, text, required: false): This field is for providing any additional information about yourself, such as a student ID number or any other relevant details.
