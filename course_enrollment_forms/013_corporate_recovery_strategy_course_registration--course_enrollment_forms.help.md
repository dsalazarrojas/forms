# Corporate Recovery Strategy Course Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Corporate Recovery Strategy course. It collects information about the participant, company, and course details to enable efficient and organized course management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter the company's name in the "Company" field.
3. Enter the participant's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
6. Select the start and end dates of the course in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
7. Add any additional details about the participant in the "Participant Details" field.
8. Choose the participant's type (Full-time Employee, Part-time Employee, or Contractor) in the "Participant Type" field.
9. Enter the department of the participant in the "Department" field.
10. Enter the participant's role in the "Role" field.
11. Enter the location of the participant in the "Location" field.
12. Enter the participant's job function in the "Job Function" field.
13. Enter the participant's job title in the "Job Title" field.
14. Select the participant's status (Active, On Leave, or Not Active) in the "Participant Status" field.
15. Enter the team name in the "Team" field.
16. Enter the team lead's name in the "Team Lead" field.
17. Enter the team member's name in the "Team Member" field.
18. Select the team lead's status (Active, On Leave, or Not Active) in the "Team Lead Status" field.
19. Choose the company size (1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-1000, or More than 1000) in the "Company Size" field.
20. Enter the company type in the "Company Type" field.
21. Enter the company address in the "Company Address" field.
22. Enter the contact person's name in the "Contact Person" field.
23. Add any additional notes about the participant in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course the participant is enrolling in.
* **Start Date**: Select the start date of the course. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date**: Select the end date of the course. Please use the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Participant Details**: Add any additional details about the participant.
* **Participant Type**: Choose the type of participant (Full-time Employee, Part-time Employee, or Contractor).
* **Department**: Enter the department of the participant.
* **Role**: Enter the role of the participant.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the participant.
* **Job Function**: Enter the job function of the participant.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the participant.
* **Participant Status**: Select the status of the participant (Active, On Leave, or Not Active).
* **Team**: Enter the team name of the participant.
* **Team Lead**: Enter the team lead's name.
* **Team Member**: Enter the team member's name.
* **Team Lead Status**: Select the status of the team lead (Active, On Leave, or Not Active).
* **Company Size**: Choose the size of the company (1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-1000, or More than 1000).
* **Company Type**: Enter the type of company.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the participant.
