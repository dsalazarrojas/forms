# Mentorship And Investment Course Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentorship And Investment Course Signup form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in enrolling in the course. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: This section collects general information about you. Please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant details that might help us understand who you are and what you do.
* **Interests**: Please select all that apply from the provided options that best represent your interests.
* **Consent**: Please indicate your consent to receive communication and updates about the course.
* **Program Details**: This field is where you can provide any specific details about your program of interest.
* **Contact Info**: Please provide a valid contact phone number or email address so we can reach out to you for course updates and information.
* **Upload Documents**: This field is not currently used, but you may use it to upload any relevant documents or files for review.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
* Only one form per user will be accepted. If you have previously submitted a form, please do not resubmit.
