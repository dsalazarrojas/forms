# Instrument Flight Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instrument Flight Training Enrollment Form is designed to help students enroll in an instrument flight training session with a chosen flight school and instructor. This form collects personal details and preferences to streamline the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select your instructor from the drop-down menu in the "instructor name" field.
5. Choose your preferred start and end dates for the training session in the "start date" and "end date" fields.
6. Select your preferred schedule for the training session from the drop-down menu in the "schedule" field.
7. Fill in your student name in the "student name" field (optional).
8. Fill in your student email address in the "student email" field (optional).
9. Fill in your student phone number in the "student phone" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Instructor name** (`instructor`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select the name of your instructor from a list of available instructors. Select the name of your preferred instructor.
- **Email** (`email`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your email address in this field.
- **Flight School** (`flight_school`, `text`, required: false): This field is currently not in use, and no options are listed.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required: false): This field allows you to set your preferred start date for the training session.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required: false): This field allows you to set your preferred end date for the training session.
- **Schedule** (`schedule`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select your preferred schedule for the training session. Choose between "Morning", "Afternoon", and "Evening" for your preferred schedule.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to enter your student name.
- **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to enter your student email address.
- **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is optional and allows you to enter your student phone number.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is the first name of the student.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): This field is the last name of the student.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the fields are filled in correctly to avoid any delays or errors in the enrollment process.
- If you encounter any issues during the enrollment process, please contact the flight school or instructor for assistance.
