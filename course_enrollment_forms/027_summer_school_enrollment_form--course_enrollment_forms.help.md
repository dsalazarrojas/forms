# Summer School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from students who are interested in enrolling in summer school programs. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the courses you want to enroll in.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Review your submission to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Info**: (`Student Info`, `text`, **required**): Fill in your student's details for summer school enrollment.
* **Select a course**: (`Select a course`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose one course from the given options to enroll.
* **Program Info**: (`Program Info`, `text`, **required**): Enter relevant details about your program of interest.
* **Summer School Info**: (`Summer School Info`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any additional information about the summer school program.
* **Select an additional course**: (`Select an additional course`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple courses if you want to enroll in more than one course.
* **Contact Info**: (`Contact Info`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
* **Submission**: (`Submission`, `text`, **optional**): You can add any comments or notes about your submission if needed.
* **Program Info 2**: (`Program Info 2`, `text`, **optional**): Enter more details about your program (e.g., course level, location).
* **Program Info 3**: (`Program Info 3`, `text`, **optional**): Add any additional program details if needed.
* **Select a course**: (`Select a course`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose multiple courses from the given options to enroll in more than one course.
*   **Program Info 3**: (`Program Info 3`, `text`, **optional**): Enter more details about the courses you selected (e.g., section number, time slot).
