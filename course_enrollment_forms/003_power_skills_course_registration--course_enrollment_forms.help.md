# Power Skills Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Power Skills Course Registration form is used to collect information from participants registering for the Power Skills course. This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to fill out the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by selecting the participant type: individual or group.
2.  Enter the required participant details and contact information.
3.  Enter a valid email address and phone number.
4.  Describe any specific learning needs or preferences.
5.  Enter the name of the course you wish to enroll in.
6.  Choose the start and end dates for your enrollment period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Type** (`Participant Type`, `select_one`, required): Select your participant type: `Individual` or `Group` to ensure accurate course planning.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant participant details.
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, optional): Provide your organization's contact information for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for course notifications and updates.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Learning Needs** (`Learning Needs`, `text`, optional): Describe any specific learning needs or preferences you have.
* **Course Name** (`Course Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the course you wish to enroll in.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, optional): Choose the start date for your enrollment period.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, optional): Choose the end date for your enrollment period.

## Tips
* Double-check your participant type selection to ensure accurate course planning.
* Ensure you enter a valid email address and phone number for course communication.
* Be specific when describing your learning needs for course customization.
* Confirm your course name and dates to avoid errors.
* If you are registering as a group, please ensure all members have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
