# Diesel Engine Wiring Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diesel Engine Wiring Course Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from students who want to enroll in the diesel engine wiring course. This form will help us to verify your enrollment details and keep you updated on the course schedule, instructor, and any other important course information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information to ensure we have accurate and up-to-date records about you.
2. Double-check the fields as you fill them out to ensure you enter correct details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`Email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address to help us contact you regarding course updates and information.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number to enable us to contact you in case of any course-related updates.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`Date Of Birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required: false): Enter your residential or business address to help us contact you.
* **City** (`City`, text, required: false): Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State** (`State`, text, required: false): Enter the state or province where you reside or work.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required: false): Enter your postal code or ZIP code.
* **Enrollment Date** (`Enrollment Date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to enroll in the course.
* **Course Name** (`Course Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you want to enroll in.
* **Class Schedule** (`Class Schedule`, text, required: false): Enter the schedule of the class you want to enroll in.
* **Instructor Name** (`Instructor Name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the instructor who will be teaching the course.
* **Course Description** (`Course Description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the course you want to enroll in.
* **Course Length** (`Course Length`, text, required: false): Enter the duration of the course in terms of weeks or months.
* **Course Price** (`Course Price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the course you want to enroll in.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, text, required: false): Choose the payment method you want to use for the course fees.
* **Enrollment Status** (`Enrollment Status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the enrollment status (Active, Pending, or Inactive) of the course.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, text, required: false): Enter your student ID number if applicable.
* **Form Submitted By** (`Form Submitted By`, text, required: false): Enter your name or title to help us verify the form submission.
* **Form Submitted On** (`Form Submitted On`, date, required: false): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Course ID** (`Course ID`, number, required: false): Enter the ID number of the course you want to enroll in.
* **Form Version** (`Form Version`, number, required: false): Enter the version number of the form you are using.
* **Form Last Updated** (`Form Last Updated`, date, required: false): Enter the date this form was last updated.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out if you want to submit the form.
- If you have any doubts or issues with the form, contact us for assistance.
