# Peer Learning Exchange Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Learning Exchange Program Application form is designed to collect relevant information from individuals interested in participating in our Peer Learning Exchange Program. This program aims to connect people with shared learning interests and goals, promoting knowledge sharing, collaboration, and skill development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by carefully reading the purpose and instructions above.
2. Fill in your **Name** in the "Name" field (ID: 1). This is a required field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field (ID: 2). While not required, this field is useful for us to contact you regarding your application.
4. Select your **Learning Interests** from the dropdown list in the "Learning Interests" field (ID: 3). You can select multiple interests if applicable. This is a required field.
5. Enter your **Age** in the "Age" field (ID: 4). This field is required.
6. If applicable, provide your **Location** in the "Location" field (ID: 5). This field is not required.
7. List your **Skills** in the "Skills" field (ID: 6). You can enter multiple skills separated by commas. This field is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (ID: 1)**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **Email (ID: 2)**: Your email address for us to contact you regarding your application.
* **Learning Interests (ID: 3)**: Choose one or multiple interests that best match your learning goals.
* **Age (ID: 4)**: Enter your age for demographic purposes.
* **Location (ID: 5)**: Enter your location (city, state, etc.) if you wish to participate in local peer groups.
* **Skills (ID: 6)**: List your skills, separated by commas, that you're willing to share or learn from others.

## Tips
- Please ensure your answers are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We value your honesty and respect in filling out this form.
