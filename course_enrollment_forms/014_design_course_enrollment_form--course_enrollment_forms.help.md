# Design Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Design Course Enrollment Form is a simple application process for students to enroll in a course. Fill out this form to submit your interest in a course, select your chosen courses, and provide your payment information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a new tab or window.
2. Select the courses you are interested in from the "Select Courses" field.
3. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Student Contact" field.
5. Click "Submit Enrollment" to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Application** (`student_application`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief application essay or statement explaining why you are interested in this course.
* **Select Courses** (`course_selection`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or multiple courses you are interested in enrolling in. 
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, `number`, optional): Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card number, bank transfer, etc.). 
* **Student Contact** (`student_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, phone number, or email address so we can contact you.
* **Submit Enrollment** (`submit_enrollment`, `note`, optional): This is a confirmation button to submit your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Be specific about your course interests to increase your chances of being accepted.
* Double-check your payment information before submitting.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us at [your contact email or info].
