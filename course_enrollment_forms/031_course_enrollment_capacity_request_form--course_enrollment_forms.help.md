# course_enrollment_capacity_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request course enrollment capacity for a specific course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: Enter your user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Course Information**: Enter details about the course you are requesting enrollment capacity for.
* **Request Details**: Enter the start and end dates for the requested capacity.
* **Requested Capacities**: List the capacities you are requesting.
* **Current Capacities**: List any existing capacities that are currently available.
* **Requested Start Date**: Enter the start date for the requested capacity.
* **Requested End Date**: Enter the end date for the requested capacity.
* **Reasons for Request**: Provide any reasons or explanations for the requested capacity.
* **Justification**: Enter any additional justification or comments for the request.
* **Supporting Documents**: Attach any supporting documents or files that are relevant to the request.
* **Approver**: Choose whether the approver has agreed or disagreed to the request.
* **Approver Email**: Enter the approver's email address.
* **Approver Phone**: Enter the approver's phone number.
* **Approver Note**: Capture any notes or comments from the approver.
* **Approver Comments**: Capture any additional comments from the approver.
* **Approver Approvals**: Choose whether the approver has approved or rejected the request.
* **Approver Signature**: Capture the approver's signature for the approval.
* **Approver Name**: Enter the approver's name.
* **Approver ID**: Enter the approver's ID.
* **Approver Department**: Enter the approver's department.
* **Approver Title**: Enter the approver's title.
* **Approver Note**: Capture any additional notes from the approver.
* **Approver Comments**: Capture any additional comments from the approver.
* **Approver Status**: Choose the status of the approval.
* **Approver Status Date**: Enter the date of the approver's status.
