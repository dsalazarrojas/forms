# Clean Energy Transition Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the registration process for the Clean Energy Transition Course. The form is designed to be completed by individuals interested in registering for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant sections of the form that apply to you.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your completed form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required)
Your first name as it appears on official documents.

### **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required)
Your last name as it appears on official documents.

### **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required)
Your contact email address.

### **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required)
Your phone number for contact purposes.

### **Organization or Institution** (`organization`, `text`, optional)
If you are affiliated with an organization or institution, please provide the name here.

### **Job Title or Position** (`job_title`, `text`, optional)
Your current job title or position.

### **Country** (`country`, `text`, required)
Your country of residence.

### **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, `select_one`, required)
Select the course that you would like to register for from the available options.

### **Course Format Preference** (`course_format_preference`, `select_one`, required)
Choose your preferred course format: Online - Self-paced, Online - Live virtual sessions, In-person - On campus, or Hybrid - Combination of online and in-person.

### **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, required)
Choose the date when you would like to begin the course.

### **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `text`, required)
Your time zone.

### **Highest Level of Education** (`highest_education`, `select_one`, required)
Select your highest level of education from the available options: High School Diploma, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Doctorate or PhD, Professional Certification, or Other.

### **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, `text`, optional)
If you have completed a field of study, please provide it here.

### **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `select_one`, required)
Select your level of experience: Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 4-7 years, 8-15 years, or Over 15 years.

### **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, `select_one`, required)
Choose your industry sector: Energy or Utilities, Engineering or Construction, Government or Public Sector, Education or Research, Finance or Investment, Consulting, Manufacturing, Technology, Nonprofit or NGO, or Other, or Not Currently Employed.

### **Clean Energy Experience** (`clean_energy_experience`, `select_one`, required)
Select your level of experience in clean energy: Extensive - Work in clean energy sector, Moderate - Some clean energy projects, Limited - Basic knowledge only, or None - New to clean energy.

### **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, required)
Choose your registration category: Individual - Full Price, Individual - Early Bird Discount, Student - With Verification, Nonprofit or Government - Discounted, Corporate Group - 3 or More, or Scholarship Recipient.

### **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required)
Select your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Purchase Order, Invoice Request, or Scholarship or Sponsorship.

### **Employer Sponsorship** (`employer_sponsorship`, `select_one`, optional)
If your employer is sponsoring your course, please select the appropriate option.

### **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, `text`, required)
Provide a brief explanation of what you hope to achieve from the course.

### **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional)
If you require any accessibility accommodations, please provide your needs here.

### **How Did You Hear About This Course** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional)
Provide a brief explanation of how you learned about this course.

### **Marketing Communications Consent** (`marketing_consent`, `select_one`, required)
Select whether you want to receive marketing communications from us: Yes - I would like to receive updates, or No - Do not send marketing communications.

### **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, `select_one`, required)
Please select whether you accept the terms and conditions: Yes - I accept the terms and conditions, or No - I do not accept.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have read and understand the terms and conditions before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the course administrators for clarification.
* Review your completed form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
