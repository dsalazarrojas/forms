# Advanced Course Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit a referral for a candidate to a course or program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit Referral" button to access the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields, marked with \*.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Referrer name
*   \* **Referrer name** (`referrer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the staff member referring the candidate.

### 2. Candidate name
*   \* **Candidate full name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person being referred.

### 3. Candidate email
*   \* **Candidate email** (`candidate_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person being referred, which will be used for course contact.

### 4. Program of interest
*   \* **Program of interest** (`program_of_interest`, text, required): Select the specific course or program the candidate is interested in.

### 5. Referral date
*   \* **Referral date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date the candidate was referred to the course or program.

### 6. Consent to contact
*   \* **Candidate consent to be contacted** (`consent_to_contact`, select_one, required): Confirm if the candidate has given permission to be contacted by the course staff.

### 7. Reason for referral
*   \* **Reason for referral** (`reason_for_referral`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for why the candidate is suitable for the course or program.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all the required fields marked with \*.
*   Only internal staff members can access and submit this form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, refer to your supervisor or course coordinator for guidance.
*   Before submitting, review the form for accuracy and completeness.
*   The system will automatically prevent submissions for the same candidate within 30 days of the last submission, ensuring that you will not be able to submit duplicates for the same candidate.
