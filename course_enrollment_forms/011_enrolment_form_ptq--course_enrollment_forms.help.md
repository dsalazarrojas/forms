# Enrolment Form PTQ - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on trainee enrollments for the "PTQ" training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Training name**: Enter the name of the training program you are enrolling in.
3. Select the start date and time for the training.
4. Select the end date for the training.
5. Enter the location where the training will take place.
6. Choose the courses you are enrolling in by selecting all applicable options.
7. Enter your name and any other details as requested.
8. Enter your contact phone number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training name** (`training_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training program you are enrolling in.
* **Training start date** (`training_start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of the training.
* **Training start time** (`training_start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of the training.
* **Training end date** (`training_end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of the training.
* **Training location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the training will take place.
* **Course enrolled** (`course_enrolled`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the courses you are enrolling in from the list.
* **Trainee name** (`trainee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Trainee phone** (`trainee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Trainee email** (`trainee_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Training venue** (`training_venue`, text, optional): Enter the location where the training will take place.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Ensure you have saved a copy of the form for your records.
* If you need to make changes to your enrollment, please contact the relevant authorities to initiate the process.
