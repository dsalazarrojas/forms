# Course Overview Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify the course overview for a student. It asks for information about the student, parent, and the course director, as well as the student's and parent's addresses and acknowledgement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Course Title.
2. Enter the Student Information.
3. Enter the Parent Information.
4. Enter the Course Director.
5. Enter the Student's Address.
6. Enter the Parent's Address.
7. Enter the Acknowledgement (a brief statement acknowledging the course overview).
8. Enter your signature.
9. Enter the Submission Date and Time.
10. Enter the Acknowledgement Date and Time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Title**: Enter the title of the course for which you are acknowledging the overview.
* **Student Information**: Enter details of the student such as name, date of birth, student ID, or any other relevant student information.
* **Parent Information**: Enter details of the parent such as name, address, phone number, or any other relevant parent information.
* **Course Director**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the course.
* **Student's Address**: Enter the address of the student.
* **Parent's Address**: Enter the address of the parent.
* **Acknowledge**: Enter a brief statement acknowledging the course overview.
* **Signature**: Your electronic or printed signature.
* **Submission Date and Time**: The date and time of submission.
* **Acknowledge Date and Time**: The date and time when the acknowledgement was made.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission. 
* **Acknowledge Time**: Enter the time of acknowledgement.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Acknowledgement Date**: Enter the date of acknowledgement.

This form is meant to be completed by a student or parent and should be submitted along with other required documents to complete the course enrollment process. It is recommended to complete the form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues related to course enrollment.
