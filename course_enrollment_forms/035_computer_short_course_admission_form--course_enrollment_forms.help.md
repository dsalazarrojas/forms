# Computer Short Course Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential students who want to enroll in computer science short courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read each question carefully and provide accurate information.
* Enter your application ID, which is a unique identifier for your application.
* Provide your date of application, which is the date and time when you submit this form.
* Enter your complete legal name, date of birth, and email address.
* Select your gender and country of residence.
* Choose your highest education level and current occupation.
* Select the course you want to enroll in and the preferred format (in-person, online live, online self-paced, or hybrid).
* Indicate your level of programming experience and select any programming languages you know.
* Write a motivation statement explaining why you want to take the course.
* Provide your career goals, if any.
* Indicate the number of hours per week you can dedicate to the course.
* Choose a start date preference for the course.
* Select a payment method for the course fees.
* Indicate if you need a scholarship.
* Select the source where you heard about the course.
* Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the course.
* Finally, indicate the current status of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application ID** (`application_id`, text, required): A unique identifier for your application.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): The date and time when you submit this form.
* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Your complete legal name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Your birth date.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender (Male, Female, Non-Binary, Prefer Not to Say, Other).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Your complete address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city where you reside.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): The state or province where you reside.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Your ZIP or postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Select the country where you reside (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, Other).
* **Highest Education Level** (`highest_education`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education (High School, Some College, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, Master Degree, Doctorate, Other).
* **Current Occupation** (`current_occupation`, select_one, required): Select your current occupation (Employed Full Time, Employed Part Time, Self Employed, Student, Unemployed, Retired).
* **Course Selection** (`course_selection`, select_one, required): Select the course you want to enroll in (Introduction to Programming, Web Development Bootcamp, Python for Data Science, Cybersecurity Fundamentals, Cloud Computing Basics, Mobile App Development, Database Management, Artificial Intelligence Intro, Other).
* **Course Format** (`course_format`, select_one, required): Select your preferred course format (In-Person, Online Live, Online Self-Paced, Hybrid).
* **Programming Experience** (`programming_experience`, select_one, required): Select your level of programming experience (No Experience, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Programming Languages Known** (`programming_languages_known`, select_multiple, optional): Select any programming languages you know (None, Python, Java, JavaScript, C Plus Plus, C Sharp, Ruby, Other).
* **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`, text, required): Write a statement explaining why you want to take this course.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, text, optional): Write a statement explaining your career goals.
* **Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, select_one, required): Select the number of hours per week you can dedicate to the course (Less than 5 Hours, 5 to 10 Hours, 10 to 20 Hours, More than 20 Hours).
* **Start Date Preference** (`start_date_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred start date for the course (Next Available, Within 1 Month, Within 3 Months, Specific Date).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you prefer (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, Employer Sponsorship, Student Loan, Other).
* **Scholarship Needed** (`scholarship_needed`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you need a scholarship.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select the source where you heard about the course (Website, Social Media, Friend or Family, Advertisement, School Counselor, Other).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Accept or decline the terms and conditions of the course.
* **Application Status** (`application_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your application (Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, Waitlisted, Rejected).
