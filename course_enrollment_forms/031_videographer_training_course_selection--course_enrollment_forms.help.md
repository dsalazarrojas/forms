# Videographer Training Course Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants interested in video production courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Applicant Details" section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. In the "Course Interest" section, select the course that interests you the most from the available options.
3. If you are available for training, provide a date in the "Training Availability" section.
4. In the "School Details" section, provide information about the school you are currently attending or have attended.
5. If applicable, provide any additional details about the workshop you are interested in in the "Workshop Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details (Applicant Details)**: This is the first section of the form. It is optional and should only be filled out by people who have not already enrolled in a video production course. Enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Course Interest (course_interest)**: Select the course that you are interested in from the available options. The options are:
	+ Basic 101
	+ Intermediate 201
	+ Advanced 301
* **Training Availability (training_availability)**: If you are available for training, provide a date here. The date should be in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **School Details (School Details)**: This is an optional section for providing information about the school you are currently attending or have attended.
* **Workshop Details (Workshop Details)**: This is an optional text field for providing additional details about the workshop you are interested in.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form only if you have not already enrolled in a video production course.
- If you are available for training, please provide a specific date to increase your chances of being selected for training. 
- If you have any questions or need more information, contact form@gic.mx
