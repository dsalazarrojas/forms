1.  Enter the Course Name in the "Course Name" field.
2.  Provide your Student Name in the "Student Name" field.
3.  Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6.  Choose the Enrollment Date from the calendar.
7.  Select the type of course you are enrolling in from the "Course Type" options.
8.  Choose your Student Status as Active or Inactive.
9.  Select your Student Type as Yes or No.
10. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
11. Enter your Tax ID number.
12. Select the Class ID and Course ID from the available options.
