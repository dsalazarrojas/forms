# programming_course_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for course enrollment, allowing users to enroll in a programming course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **first name** in the "first name" field.
2. Fill in your **last name** in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your **email** in the "email" field.
4. Enter your **birth date** in the "birth date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. (Optional) Enter your **phone number** in the "phone number" field.
6. Select the **course ID** for which you are enrolling.
7. (Optional) Enter the **enrolled date** for which you are enrolling.
8. Enter the **course name** for which you are enrolling.
9. (Optional) Select the **student grade** for which you are enrolling.
10. (Optional) Select the **enrolled by** option for which you are enrolling.
11. Enter the **program year** for which you are enrolling.
12. (Optional) Enter the **program name** for which you are enrolling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: This field is for your first name. Please fill in your first name.
* **Last name**: This field is for your last name. Please fill in your last name.
* **Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Birth date**: This field is for your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone number**: This field is optional and is for your phone number.
* **Course ID**: This field is for selecting the ID of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Enrolled date**: This field is optional and is for the date you are enrolling.
* **Course name**: This field is for the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Student grade**: This field is optional and is for your student grade level.
* **Enrolled by**: This field is optional and is for selecting who you are enrolling with or by.
* **Program year**: This field is required and is for the year of the program you are enrolling in.
* **Program name**: This field is optional and is for the name of the program you are enrolling in.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can refer to the course or program's documentation for more information.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
