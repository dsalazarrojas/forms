# Training Enrolment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information for training enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Confirm your email address by selecting the correct option in the "Confirm Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number (optional).
4. Specify the course title, start date, end date, training location, duration, cost, and type.
5. Select the course level and topic.
6. Enter any additional notes about the training session (optional).
7. Choose a submission option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Required)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Required)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your email address as used for communication.
* **Confirm Email (Required)**: Select "Email confirmed" if your email address is accurate.
* **Phone (Optional)**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Course Title (Required)**: Enter the title of the training course you are enrolling in.
* **Course Start Date (Required)**: Enter the date when the training course begins.
* **Course End Date (Required)**: Enter the date when the training course ends.
* **Training Location (Required)**: Enter the location of the training session.
* **Training Duration (Required)**: Enter the duration of the training course.
* **Training Cost (Required)**: Enter the cost of the training course.
* **Training Type (Required)**: Select "Full-time" or "Part-time" to specify the type of training.
* **Training Status (Required)**: Select "Active" or "Inactive" to specify the status of the training.
* **Training Status Note (Optional)**: Enter any notes about the training status.
* **Training Instructor (Required)**: Enter the name of the instructor leading the training session.
* **Training Instructor Phone (Optional)**: Enter the phone number of the instructor.
* **Training Instructor Email (Optional)**: Enter the email address of the instructor.
* **Training Location Note (Optional)**: Enter any notes about the training location.
* **Training Notes (Optional)**: Enter any additional notes about the training session.
* **Course Level (Required)**: Select "Foundation" or "Intermediate" to specify the course level.
* **Course Topic (Optional)**: Enter the topic of the course.
* **Course Topic Note (Optional)**: Enter any notes about the course topic.
* **Course Topic Link (Optional)**: Enter a link related to the course topic.
* **Course Topic Link Note (Optional)**: Enter any notes about the course topic link.
* **Submit (Optional)**: Select "Submit" to finalize your enrollment request, or "Other" if you need to provide additional information.
