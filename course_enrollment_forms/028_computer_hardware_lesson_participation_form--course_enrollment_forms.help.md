# Computer Hardware Lesson Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Computer Hardware Lesson Participation Form is used to register participants for computer hardware lessons. It helps instructors to better understand who is participating in the lesson, what their experience level is, and how they can tailor the lesson to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your **Participant name** (full name) in the field labeled "Participant name".
2. Enter your **Email address** in the field labeled "Email address" (this will be used for lesson confirmations).
3. Select your **Current experience level** from the options "Beginner", "Intermediate", or "Advanced".
4. Enter your **Topics of interest** (optional).
5. Enter your **Availability** (e.g., specific days or times you are available).
6. Confirm that you **Consent to lesson terms** by selecting "I agree".
7. If you have any **Special requirements** (e.g., accessibility needs), enter them in the field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is your full name that will be used to identify you in the lesson.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address that we will use to send you confirmations and important updates about the lesson.
* **Current experience level** (`current_experience_level`, select_one, required): This is your self-assessed level of experience with computer hardware, from "Beginner", "Intermediate", or "Advanced".
* **Topics of interest** (`topics_of_interest`, text, optional): If you have any specific topics you'd like to focus on during the lesson, enter them here (e.g., Assembly, Troubleshooting, Upgrades).
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): Enter your preferred days or times for the lesson.
* **Consent to lesson terms** (`consent_to_terms`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to confirm that you've read and understand the lesson terms and safety rules.
* **Special requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs or special materials required for the lesson, enter them here.
