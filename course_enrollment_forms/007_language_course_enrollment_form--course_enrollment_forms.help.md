# Language Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather user information and preferences for a language course enrollment process. It collects details about the user's language choices, availability, and preferred course terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your details accurately and truthfully.
2. Select your preferred languages from the list provided.
3. Choose your language level from beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
4. Select the duration of the course you would like to take.
5. Choose your preferred term to start the course.
6. Select the type of course you are interested in (group or private).
7. Indicate your available class days.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (User Information, text, required: false): This section is for you to input your details, such as your name and contact information.
* **Course Details** (Course Details, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the course you are interested in.
* **Student Availability** (Student Availability, text, required: false): Share your availability for classes.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Language Choices** (Language Choices, select multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose the languages you would like to learn (e.g., English, French, Spanish, etc.).
* **Language Level** (Language Level, select one, required: false):
	+ Select your current level in the language (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
* **Course Duration** (Course Duration, select one, required: false):
	+ Choose the duration of the course you would like to take (1 month, 3 months, or 6 months).
* **Preferred Terms** (Preferred Terms, select one, required: false):
	+ Select the term you would like to start the course (Spring, Summer, or Fall).
* **Course Type** (Course Type, select one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of course you are interested in (group or private).
* **Class Days** (Class Days, select one, required: false):
	+ Indicate which class days you are available (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).

## Tips

- Be honest when filling out your preferences and details.
- If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Please double-check your selections before submitting the form.
- If you'd like to make changes, you can do so before submitting the form.
- We'll contact you to confirm your enrollment once we receive your submission.
