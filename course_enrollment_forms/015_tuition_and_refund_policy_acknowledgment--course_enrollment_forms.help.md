# Tuition And Refund Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a Tuition And Refund Policy Acknowledgment form, designed to collect acknowledgment from users about the institution's tuition and refund policy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the provided information about the tuition and refund policy.
2. If you understand the terms and conditions, select "Tuition and refund terms".
3. If you do not understand the terms and conditions, select "I do not understand these terms".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tuition And Refund Policy Acknowledgment Form**: This field is the main heading of the form and provides an introduction to the acknowledgment process.
* **Date**: Please enter the current date to acknowledge that you have reviewed the tuition and refund policy on the specified date.
* **Tuition And Refund Terms**: This is a note field to provide detailed information about the institution's tuition and refund policy.
* **Confirm Tuition And Refund Terms**: This field is used to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the tuition and refund policy. Select "Tuition and refund terms" if you understand, or "I do not understand these terms" if you need further clarification.

## Tips
* Please take the time to carefully review the tuition and refund policy before providing your acknowledgment.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, select "I do not understand these terms" to initiate further communication with the institution.
