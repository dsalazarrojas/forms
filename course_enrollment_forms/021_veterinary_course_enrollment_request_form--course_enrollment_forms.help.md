# Veterinary Course Enrollment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential students to facilitate enrollment in veterinary courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Select relevant options for your course interests.
3. Provide accurate background details.
4. Enter your contact information.
5. Confirm your preferred enrollment option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (contact_details, Text, Required): Enter your name, address, email, and phone number to help us get in touch with you.
* **Course Interests** (course_interests, Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the course options that interest you. Please select as many options as apply.
* **Background Details** (background_details, Text, Required): Share any relevant background information about yourself, including your experience, qualifications, or anything else that may be helpful for us to know.
* **Contact Information** (contact_information, Text, Required): Enter your full contact details, including your name, address, email, and phone number.
* **Confirm Enrollment** (confirm_enrollment, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred enrollment option to confirm your interest in enrolling in the veterinary course.
