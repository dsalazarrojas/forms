# Traffic Control Supervisor Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Traffic Control Supervisor course registration form is used to collect information from individuals who want to enroll in the course. This form helps course administrators track enrollment and course progress.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your experience, including your years and months of experience.
3. Select the course name you're enrolling in.
4. Enter the session date and time for the course.
5. Choose the location where the course will be held.
6. Specify the session length and capacity.
7. Enter the start and end time of the session.
8. Choose the start date of the course.
9. Select the end date of the course.
10. Provide a description of the course.
11. Enter the name of the training firm providing the course.
12. Enter the name of the agency offering the course.
13. Select your enrollment status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
14. Add any additional notes about your enrollment.
15. Select your course enrollment status (Enrolled, Not Enrolled, or Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`course_enrollment_form`): This is the main registration section of the form. Enter your registration information here.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`): Enter the number of years you have experience in the field.
* **Experience Months** (`experience_months`): Enter the number of months you have experience in the field.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`): Select the name of the course you're enrolling in.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`): Enter the name of the training provider.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`): Enter the date of the course session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`): Enter the time of the course session.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location where the course will be held.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`): Enter the length of the course session.
* **Session Capacity** (`session_capacity`): Enter the maximum number of participants for the course session.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`): Enter the start time of the course session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`): Enter the end time of the course session.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Training Firm** (`training_firm`): Enter the name of the firm providing the training.
* **Agency** (`agency`): Enter the name of the agency offering the course.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`): Select your current enrollment status (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Enrollment Notes** (`enrollment_notes`): Add any additional notes about your enrollment.
* **Course Enrollment Status** (`course_enroll_status`): Select your current course enrollment status (Enrolled, Not Enrolled, or Pending).
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes about your enrollment.
