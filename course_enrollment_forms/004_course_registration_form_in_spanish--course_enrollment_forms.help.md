# Course Registration Form In Spanish - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Registration Form In Spanish is a form designed to collect essential information for students registering for courses in Spanish. The form is used to record course details, student data, and inscription time zone information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the desired Course from the list provided.
2. Enter the Student name.
3. Choose the Inscription Date and Time Zone.
4. Provide the Inscription Duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course** (course_registration_form_in_spanish_course): Select the course you would like to register for from the list provided.
- **Student** (course_registration_form_in_spanish_student): Enter the name of the student registering for the course.
- **Inscription Date** (course_registration_form_in_spanish_inscription_date): Select the date on which the student will be inscribing for the course.
- **Inscription Time** (course_registration_form_in_spanish_inscription_time): Choose the time of inscription for the course.
- **Inscription Time Zone** (course_registration_form_in_spanish_inscription_time_zone): Select the time zone relevant to the inscription time you chose.
- **Inscription Duration** (course_registration_form_in_spanish_inscription_duration): Provide the duration of inscription for the course (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).

## Tips
- Please ensure you select the correct course, student, inscription date, and time zone to avoid errors.
- For inscription duration, enter the actual duration the student will be inscribing for the course.
