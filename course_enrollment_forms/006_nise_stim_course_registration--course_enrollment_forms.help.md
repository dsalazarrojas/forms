# Nise Stiim Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nise Stiim Course Registration form is used to collect user and course information for course registration purposes. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure correct course enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the user information section and choose the relevant option for your user information.
2. Select the course details section and choose the relevant option for your course information.
3. Fill in the date and time of the course.
4. Select the course duration.
5. Choose the status of the course.
6. Select the type of the course.
7. Fill in any additional course comments.
8. Choose the payment method.
9. Fill in the payment details.
10. Select the date and time of payment.
11. Choose the payment status.
12. Fill in the amount paid.
13. Fill in any follow-up comments.
14. Choose the follow-up option.
15. Select the follow-up option 2.
16. Fill in any additional notes.
17. Choose the enrollment status.
18. Select the enrollment status options.
19. Fill in the enrollment status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info (prophets)**: Select the relevant option for your user information.
* **course_details (course code)**: Select the relevant option for your course information.
* **course_date**: Enter the date of the course.
* **course_time**: Enter the time of the course.
* **course_duration**: Select the duration of the course.
* **course_status (Status 1)**: Choose the status of the course.
* **course_type (type1)**: Select the type of the course.
* **course_comments**: Fill in any additional course comments.
* **payment_method (method1)**: Choose the payment method.
* **payment_details (detail1)**: Fill in the payment details.
* **payment_date**: Enter the date of payment.
* **payment_time**: Enter the time of payment.
* **payment_status (Status 1)**: Choose the payment status.
* **amount_paid**: Fill in the amount paid.
* **followup_comments**: Fill in any follow-up comments.
* **followup (up1)**: Choose the follow-up option.
* **followup2 (up2 1)**: Select the follow-up option 2.
* **notes**: Fill in any additional notes.
* **enrollment_status (Status 1)**: Choose the enrollment status.
* **enroll_status (Status 1)**: Select the enrollment status options.
