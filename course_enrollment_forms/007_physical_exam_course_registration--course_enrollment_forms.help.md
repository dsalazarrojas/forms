# Physical Exam Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Physical Exam Course Registration" form is used to register or manage courses with various details, including instructor and student information, course settings, and exam schedules.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form.
2.  Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required.
3.  For non-required fields, use your best judgment to provide relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Course Category** (`course_category`, number, required): Choose a category for the course from the available options. This helps categorize and filter courses for easier management.
*   **Tags** (`course_tags`, select multiple, required): Select relevant tags for the course, such as "Online" or "Offline." This helps identify the course type and adds metadata for searching and filtering.
*   **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the course, providing a brief and descriptive title.
*   **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the course instructor or presenter.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the course student or attendee.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the exam date, if applicable.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, not required): Enter the exam time, if applicable.
*   **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, not required): Enter a unique identifier for the course, which might be provided by the course provider or creator.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter the contact email for the course, such as instructor or student email.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter the contact phone number for the course, such as instructor or student phone number.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes about the course, such as exam location or requirements.
*   **Course Status** (`course_status`, select multiple, not required): Select the status of the course, which can be pending, approved, or rejected.
*   **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, not required): Confirm the completion of the form by entering a message, which helps in tracking progress and confirmation of form submission.
*   **Error Message** (`error_message`, text, not required): Enter any error messages or issues encountered during the course registration process, which helps in troubleshooting and improving the form's functionality.
*   **Location** (`location`, select multiple, not required): Indicate whether the course is held online or offline.
*   **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of the exam, if applicable.
*   **Course Level** (`course_level`, number, not required): Enter the level of the course, such as basic, advanced, or expert.
*   **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of the exam, if applicable.
*   **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, text, not required): Enter the duration of the course, such as hours or minutes.
*   **Course Type** (`course_type`, number, not required): Enter the type of the course, such as theoretical or practical.
*   **Instructor** (`instructor`, text, not required): Enter the name of the instructor or presenter.
*   **Student** (`student`, text, not required): Enter the name of the student or attendee.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Confirm the form submission and review the information entered.
*   **Submit2** (`submit2`, text, not required): This field appears to be redundant with the 'Submit' field and can be ignored.

**Tips**

*   Always review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   Use the provided hints for guidance on filling in the required fields.
*   For non-required fields, use your best judgment based on the provided information and context.
