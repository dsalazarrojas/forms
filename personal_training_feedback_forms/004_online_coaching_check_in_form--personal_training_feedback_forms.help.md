# Online Coaching Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track information after each coaching session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter your name, the date of the session, and the session length in the respective fields.
3. Describe the session goals and any comments you'd like to share with your coach.
4. If you have any comments or feedback for your coach, please share those in the coach comments field.
5. Select the days you are available for the next session.
6. Choose the days your coach is available for the next session.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, false): Enter your name as the client.
* **Date** (`date`, date, false): Enter the date of the current session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, false): Enter the time of the current session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, false): Enter the length of the current session.
* **Session Goals** (`session_goals`, text, false): Describe the goals you set for this session.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, text, false): Share any comments or feedback you'd like to give your coach.
* **Coach Comments** (`coach_comments`, text, false): Share any comments or feedback you'd like to receive from your coach.
* **Next Session** (`next_session`, select_multiple, false): Select the days you are available for the next session.
* **Coach Next Session** (`coach_next_session`, select_multiple, false): Choose the days your coach is available for the next session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Be as specific as possible with your session goals and comments.
* Use the options provided for next session availability to ensure a smooth and convenient scheduling process.
