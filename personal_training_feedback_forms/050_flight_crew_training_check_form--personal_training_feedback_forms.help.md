# Flight Crew Training Check Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to check on the training status of flight crew members. It gathers information about the crew member's name, position, and training date, as well as their performance in various areas such as Crew Resource Management, Safety Management, Communication, Leadership, and Teamwork.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the crew member's name in the "Crew Member Name" field.
2. Select the crew member's position from the "Crew Member Position" field.
3. Enter the date of the training check in the "Training Date" field.
4. Answer the questions about the crew member's performance in the various areas listed in the "Training Area" fields (Crew Resource Management, Safety Management, Communication, Leadership, and Teamwork).
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about the crew member's performance in the "Crew Member Notes" field.
6. Finally, sign your name as the assessor in the "Assessor Name" field and add your signature in the "Assessor Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Crew Member Name** (`crew_member_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the crew member being assessed.
* **Crew Member Position** (`crew_member_position`, `text`, required): Enter the position of the crew member, e.g., pilot, co-pilot, etc.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the training check, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Crew Resource Management** (`flight_crew_training_check_form_1_1`, `text`, required): Rate the crew member's performance in Crew Resource Management.
* **Safety Management** (`flight_crew_training_check_form_1_2`, `text`, required): Rate the crew member's performance in Safety Management.
* **Communication** (`flight_crew_training_check_form_1_3`, `text`, required): Rate the crew member's performance in Communication.
* **Leadership** (`flight_crew_training_check_form_1_4`, `text`, required): Rate the crew member's performance in Leadership.
* **Teamwork** (`flight_crew_training_check_form_1_5`, `text`, required): Rate the crew member's performance in Teamwork.
* **Crew Member Notes** (`crew_member_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the crew member's performance.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the assessor.
* **Assessor Signature** (`assessor_signature`, `text`, required): Add your signature as the assessor.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Check the training date to ensure it is up-to-date.
* Be specific and detailed when rating the crew member's performance in each area.
* Use the "Crew Member Notes" field to provide any additional information that may not be captured in the rating fields.
* Finally, make sure to sign your name and add your signature as the assessor to authenticate the form.
