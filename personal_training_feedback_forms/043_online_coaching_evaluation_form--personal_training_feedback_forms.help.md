# online_coaching_evaluation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form aims to gather feedback and evaluations from students who have received online coaching sessions, helping the coaching service provider to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please go through each page of the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. The form has multiple pages with fields that need to be filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Info** (student_info, note, optional): This field is for providing a brief background information about yourself as a student, if you wish to do so. You may add any relevant details that might be helpful for the coaching service provider.
* **Feedback** (feedback, select_multiple, optional): This field asks you to provide your evaluation of the online coaching sessions. Choose all the options that best describe your experience.
	+ Yes: You had a positive experience with the coaching sessions.
	+ No: You did not have a positive experience with the coaching sessions.
* **Rating** (rating, select_one, optional): Here, you are asked to rate your overall experience with the online coaching sessions on a scale of 1 to 5.
* **Comments** (comments, text, optional): This is a text area where you can provide additional comments or suggestions about your experience.
* **Date** (date, date, optional): Enter the date when you received the online coaching sessions.
* **Time** (time, time, optional): Enter the time when you received the online coaching sessions.

## Tips
- Be specific and honest while filling out the form to provide the coaching service provider valuable insights.
- If you have any comments or feedback, please provide as much detail as possible.
- If you have any suggestions for improvement, don't hesitate to add them in the comments field.

Note: Please fill out all the required fields and be as detailed as possible in the optional fields to provide the coaching service provider with the most valuable feedback.
