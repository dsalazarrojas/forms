# personal-training-consultation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients for personal training consultations. It is intended for clients who are ready to schedule a session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Choose a session schedule that works best for you.
2. Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you.
3. Select your preferred session duration.
4. Share any additional information or notes for the trainer.
5. Select your available dates and time slots.
6. Add any additional notes or feedback.
7. Confirm your availability to schedule a session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Schedule** (`session_schedule`, `text`, optional): Please select the day of the week you are available to schedule a session.
* **Client Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, or any other contact information we can use to reach out to you.
* **Available Sessions** (`available_sessions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you are available to schedule a session.
* **Preferred Session Duration** (`preferred_session_duration`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the duration of your preferred session.
* **Trainer Preference** (`trainer_preference`, `text`, optional): Share any specific trainer preferences you may have.
* **Client Notes** (`client_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or information about yourself.
* **Trainer Availability** (`trainer_availability`, `date`, optional): Select the date of the available time slot.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, `time`, optional): Select the start time of your preferred session.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Trainer Notes** (`trainer_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or feedback for the trainer.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, optional): Share any next steps or information for the trainer.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if you are ready to schedule a session.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Add any feedback or comments.
* **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Add any additional feedback or comments.
* **Available Dates** (`trainer_availability_date`, `date`, optional): Select the available dates for the session.
* **Available Time Slots** (`trainer_availability_time`, `time`, optional): Select the available time slots.
* **Trainer Special Requests** (`trainer_special_requests`, `text`, optional): Share any special requests you may have for the trainer.
* **Confirmation Time** (`confirmation_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time for the confirmation.
* **Confirmation Date** (`confirmation_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date for the confirmation.
* **Client Name** (`confirmation_client_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
* **Client Email** (`confirmation_client_email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone Number** (`confirmation_client_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Are You Ready To Schedule A Session** (`confirmation`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if you are ready to schedule a session.
