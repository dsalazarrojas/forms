# Fitness App Workout Plan Effectiveness Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback on the effectiveness of a fitness app's workout plans. This feedback will help trainers assess whether their plans are meeting the users' needs and make necessary adjustments to improve future workouts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and the information you want to provide.
2. Review the form's fields and fill in the required information. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. For non-required fields, feel free to provide any additional information you deem relevant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (page 1): Enter a brief general description of your workout plan.
* **Workout Details** (page 1): Provide a number indicating the level of progress or effectiveness of your workout plan (e.g., 1-10).
* **Progress Insights** (page 2): Select the level of progress you've seen in your workouts ("Yes" or "No").
* **Feedback** (page 2): Choose any applicable points from the list to provide feedback on your workout plan.
* **Trainer Name** (page 3): Enter your name as the trainer responsible for creating the workout plan.
* **Trainer Email** (page 3): Enter your email address for communication with the trainer.
* **Trainer Phone** (page 4): Enter your phone number for communication with the trainer.
* **Comments** (page 4): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improving the workout plan.
* **Email** (page 4): Enter your email address for communication with the app (optional).
* **Phone** (page 4): Enter your phone number for communication with the app (optional).

Please note that all fields marked with an asterisk (*) are required. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact your trainer for assistance.
