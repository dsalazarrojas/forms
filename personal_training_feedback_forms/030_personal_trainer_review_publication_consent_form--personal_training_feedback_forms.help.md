# Personal Trainer Review Publication Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for personal trainers to review and provide feedback on the publication of testimonials from their clients. It is a crucial part of our process to ensure that we are sharing the right information and to get valuable insights from trainers about their experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including your name and any relevant contact details.
2. Indicate your consent to publish your testimonial on various platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube).
3. Provide a rating of your experience with the trainer (on a scale).
4. Specify the date you had your session with the trainer.
5. Share any additional information about your experience with the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (1)** (`user_information`, `text`, required): Please fill in your name and any relevant contact details so we can reach out to you for any further questions or concerns.
* **Consent (2)** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select which websites and social media platforms you'd like to publish your testimonial on. For example, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, or YouTube.
* **Testimonial (3)** (`testimonial`, `note`, optional): Share your experience with the trainer in a short paragraph or sentence.
* **Rating (4)** (`rating`, `number`, required): Rate your experience with the trainer on a scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Publish on Websites (5)** (`publish_on_websites`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select which websites you'd like to share your testimonial on.
* **Publish on Social Media (6)** (`publish_on_social_media`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select which social media platforms you'd like to share your testimonial on.
* **Date Published (7)** (`date_published`, `date`, required): Specify the date you had your session with the trainer.
* **Trainer Info (8)** (`trainer_info`, `text`, required): Share any additional information about your experience with the trainer, such as what made the experience great, what the trainer did well, or areas for improvement.
