# Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from users about their experience in a training session. It helps the training staff to evaluate the session's quality, identify areas for improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future trainings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately, as the information will be used to make data-driven decisions.
2. Ensure that you are filling out this form for a session you attended, and not for someone else.
3. Please do not submit multiple entries for the same session.
4. Be honest and provide constructive feedback to help improve the training program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, text, required/optional): Please provide a brief description of your overall experience in the session. This could include comments about the content, instructor, or any other relevant details.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the date of the training session you are providing feedback about.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): Please enter the time of the training session you are providing feedback about.
* **Trainer's Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the trainer who led the session.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Please provide detailed feedback about your experience in the session, including any suggestions or recommendations.
* **Rating** (`rating`, select_one, required/optional): Please select one of the options: Good, Fair, or Bad, to describe your overall rating of the session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Please provide any additional comments or suggestions about the session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address so that we can contact you if we need further information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your phone number so that we can contact you if we need further information.
* **Trainer Feedback** (`trainer_feedback`, text, required/optional): Please provide feedback about the trainer's performance and areas for improvement.
