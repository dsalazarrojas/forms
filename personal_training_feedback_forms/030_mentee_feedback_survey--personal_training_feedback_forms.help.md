# mentee_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from mentees about their experience with their mentor and the program. The feedback will help us to improve the mentorship program and make it more effective for future mentees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide the name of your Mentor.
4. Share feedback about your mentor in the Feedback About Mentor field. This is an optional field.
5. Give your feedback about the program in the Feedback About Program field. This is also optional.
6. Rate your overall experience with the program on the Overall Experience scale (from 1 to 10).
7. Give a rating to your mentor based on your overall experience.
8. If you have any additional comments, you can write them in the Comments field. This is optional.
9. Finally, provide any feedback about the form itself in the Feedback About Form field. This is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address that is associated with your account.
* **Mentor Name (text)**: Enter the name of your mentor.
* **Feedback About Mentor (text)**: Share any feedback about your mentor, if you have any.
* **Feedback About Program (text)**: Share any feedback about the program, if you have any.
* **Overall Experience (number)**: Rate your overall experience with the program on the scale of 1 to 10.
* **Rating (number)**: Rate your mentor based on your overall experience.
* **Comments (text)**: If you have any additional comments, you can write them here.
* **Feedback About Form (text)**: Share any feedback about the form, if you have any.
