# Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Trainer Evaluation Survey is a tool used to evaluate the professionalism and communication skills of gym trainers. This form will help us understand how well our trainers are performing and provide valuable feedback to help them grow in their role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you are rating the trainer's professionalism and communication based on your recent experience with them.
2. Select your ratings and feedback for each section.
3. Fill out any additional feedback you have about the trainer in the "Feedback" field.
4. Choose if the trainer was able to get you to "Say Yes" to their training.
5. Choose which gym you were working with when you had your session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Professionalism
*   **Professionalism** (`<type>: number`, required: false`): Please rate the trainer's professionalism on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is 'Not At All Professional' and 5 is 'Highly Professional'.

### Communication
*   **Communication** (`<type>: select_one`, required: false`): Rate the trainer's communication style as: Very Good, Good, Neutral, Poor, or Very Poor.

### Motivation
*   **Motivation** (`<type>: select_multiple`, required: false`): Choose the levels of motivation you experienced with the trainer: Very Motivating, Somewhat Motivating, Neutral, Somewhat Demotivating, or Very Demotivating.

### Overall Satisfaction
*   **Overall Satisfaction** (`<type>: number`, required: false`): Rate your overall satisfaction with the trainer on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is 'Not At All Satisfied' and 5 is 'Extremely Satisfied'.

### Trainer Professionalism
*   **Trainer Professionalism** (`<type>: select_multiple`, required: false`): Choose the level of professionalism you experienced with the trainer: Highly Professional, Somewhat Professional, Not Very Professional, or Not At All Professional.

### Trainer Communication
*   **Trainer Communication** (`<type>: select_multiple`, required: false`): Choose the level of communication you experienced with the trainer: Highly Communicative, Somewhat Communicative, Not Very Communicative, or Not At All Communicative.

### Feedback
*   **Feedback** (`<type>: note`, required: false`): Share any additional feedback about the trainer that you think would be helpful for them to know.

### Trainer Name
*   **Trainer Name** (`<type>: select_one`, required: false`): Let us know if you were able to say "yes" to the trainer after your session.

### Gym Name
*   **Gym Name** (`<type>: select_one`, required: false`): Let us know which gym you were at for your session.
