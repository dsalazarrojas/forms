# WISE Mentor Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The WISE Mentor Feedback Form is a tool to collect feedback from mentees about their experiences with their mentors. It helps us to understand what our mentors are doing well and what areas they can improve on.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name in the "Mentor Name" field.
2. Select the program you are associated with from the "What program" dropdown menu.
3. Write your feedback about your mentor in the "Feedback" field.
4. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
5. If you would like to provide a way for us to contact you, please enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. If you would like to provide your phone number, please enter it in the "Phone" field.
7. Finally, indicate the date and time you submitted this form in the "Submitted At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Mentor Name (Text Field)
* **Mentor Name**: Enter your mentor's name in this field.

### What program (Select One)
* **What program**: Select the program you are associated with from the dropdown menu.

### Feedback (Text Field)
* **Feedback**: Write your thoughts and insights about your mentor's performance and guidance. Be specific about what they did well and what areas they can improve on.

### Comments (Note Field)
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or thoughts you have about your mentor.

### Submitted By (Text Field)
* **Submitted By**: Please enter your name for record-keeping purposes.

### Email (Email Field)
* **Email**: If you would like to provide a way for us to contact you, enter your email address.

### Phone (Text Field)
* **Phone**: If you would like to provide your phone number, please enter it.

### Submitted At (Date Field)
* **Submitted At**: Indicate the date and time you submitted this form.

## Tips
* Please be honest and constructive in your feedback.
* Remember to provide specific examples and insights about your mentor's performance.
* If you have any trouble completing this form, don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
