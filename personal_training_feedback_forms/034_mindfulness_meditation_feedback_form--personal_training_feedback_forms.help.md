# Mindfulness Meditation Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect feedback from individuals who have attended a mindfulness meditation session. It aims to gather information about their experience, what they would like to focus on, and their overall satisfaction with the session. The information gathered will help in making the sessions more effective and enjoyable for future participants.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for each field based on your experience.
2. Fill in the fields as prompted and click "Submit".
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Id** (`session_id`, `number`, optional): This is a unique identifier for the session you attended. If you know it, please enter it.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, optional): Please enter the date you attended the meditation session in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts and experiences about the session. You can write as much or as little as you want.
* **What would you like to focus on** (`what_would_you_like_to_focus_on`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one or multiple options to indicate what you would like to focus on in future sessions:
	+ Mindfulness
	+ Relaxation
	+ Stress Management
	+ Concentration
	+ Other
* **What did you find most challenging** (`what_did_you_find_most_challenging`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all options that apply to what you found most challenging:
	+ Mindfulness
	+ Concentration
	+ Stress Management
	+ Relaxation
	+ Other
* **Rating** (`rating`, `number`, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with the session on a scale of 0-5.
* **Comment** (`comment`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please enter them here.
* **Would you return** (`would_you_return`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you would like to return for another session:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you would like to be contacted for future sessions, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted for future sessions, please enter your phone number.
* **Would you like to schedule next session** (`would_like_to_schedule_next_session`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you would like to schedule a next session:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Maybe

Note: All fields are optional, so feel free to fill in the information you are comfortable sharing. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
