# Little League Season Feedback Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information about a Little League season. It is used to gather data on player attendance, performance, and suggestions for improvement.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, you'll need to provide information about the league and players involved in the game. Please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the league and players from the options provided in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the name of the league, players, feedback, and any other required information.
3. Select the outcome of the game (win, loss, or draw).
4. Enter any notes or comments about the game.
5. Select the players who attended the game.
6. Select the players who were absent from the game.
7. Enter the date and time of the game.
8. Enter any game notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **League Name**: Enter the name of the league you are providing feedback for.
* **Player Name**: Enter the name of the player you are providing feedback for.
* **Feedback**: Enter any feedback or comments you have about the player's performance.
* **Communication**: Enter any communication or notes about the player's behavior or performance.
* **Suggestions**: Enter any suggestions for improvement or changes for the player.
* **Player Attendance**: Select the players who attended the game.
* **Player Absent**: Select the players who were absent from the game.
* **Game Day**: Enter the date of the game.
* **Game Time**: Enter the time of the game.
* **Game Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about the game.
* **Game Outcome**: Select the outcome of the game (win, loss, or draw).
* **Coach Name**: Enter the name of the coach or manager of the team.
* **Parent Contact**: Enter the contact information for the player's parent or guardian.
* **Player Contact**: Enter the contact information for the player.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team.
* **Team League**: Enter the league of the team.
* **Game Location**: Enter the location of the game.
* **Game Date**: Enter the date of the game.

Note: Fields marked as required (*) are mandatory and must be filled out. Other fields are optional and can be completed if desired.
