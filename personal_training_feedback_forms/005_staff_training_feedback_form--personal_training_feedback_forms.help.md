# Staff Training Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from staff members about their recent training sessions. It helps trainers and training program administrators to understand the effectiveness of the training, identify areas of improvement, and make data-driven decisions for future training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the staff member who attended the training session from the list provided.
2. Choose whether you attended the training session or not.
3. Provide feedback on the training session, including what you learned, and any additional comments you may have.
4. Rate the staff's attitude and the availability of training materials during the session.
5. Provide overall ratings for the session and a brief rating comment.
6. If necessary, suggest improvements for future training sessions.
7. Finally, submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session**: This field is used to record the details of the training session you attended. This may include the session's topic, date, time, and other relevant information.
* **Staff Member**: Select the staff member who led the training session. This helps to associate feedback with the specific staff member.
* **Session Date**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the training session.
* **Training Goals**: This field allows you to describe what you learned during the session. Be specific about the topics or skills covered.
* **What did you learn?**: In this field, describe the key takeaways or skills you acquired during the session.
* **Feedback**: Share any additional comments or insights you may have about the session.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other information that may be helpful in understanding your experience with the session.
* **Session Attendance**: Indicate whether you attended the training session or not.
* **Staff Attitude**: Rate the staff member's attitude during the session. Choose the ratings provided.
* **Training Materials**: Rate the availability of training materials during the session. Choose the ratings provided.
* **Overall Rating**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the session.
* **Rating Comment**: Add a brief comment to your rating.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Suggest any improvements for future training sessions.
* **Session Length**: If applicable, enter the length of the training session.
* **Session Duration**: If applicable, enter the total duration of the session.
* **Session Topic**: If applicable, enter the topic of the training session.
* **Session Outcome**: If applicable, describe the outcome of the session.
* **Training Evaluated By**: Identify the staff member who evaluated the session.
* **Training Program**: If applicable, enter the name of the training program.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are providing this feedback.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are providing this feedback.
* **Staff**: Select the staff member who provided the training.
* **Training Session Attended**: Indicate whether you attended the training session.
* **Submit**: Submit your feedback for review and analysis.
