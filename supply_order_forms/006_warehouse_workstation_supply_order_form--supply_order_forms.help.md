# Warehouse Workstation Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about supplies needed for warehouse operations. It helps warehouse staff submit requests for supplies, stock management, and track supply orders. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supply Order Form**: A brief description of the purpose and scope of the order.
* **Item Number** (`item_number`): Enter the unique identifier for the item needed (e.g., 123).
* **Item Description** (`item_description`): Briefly describe the item to be ordered (e.g., "Office supplies").
* **Unit of Measurement** (`unit_of_measurement`): Specify the measurement unit (e.g., "units", "kilograms").
* **Quantity Needed** (`quantity_needed`): Enter the quantity of the item required (e.g., 5).
* **Supply Type** (`supply_type`): Choose the type of supply requested (e.g., "Office Supplies", "Hardware").
* **Supply Location** (`supply_location`): Select the location where the supply will be used (e.g., " Warehouse A", " Warehouse B").
* **Order Date** (`order_date`): Enter the date the supply is needed by.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`): Enter the specific time the supply is needed (e.g., 9:00 AM).
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional notes or comments (e.g., "For urgent use").
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the contact email for follow-up or updates (e.g., "[john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the contact phone number for further communication (e.g., "555-123-4567").
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Choose the person or team responsible for the order (e.g., "John Doe").
* **Workstation** (`workstation`): Select the workstation or department that will use the supply (e.g., "Workstation A", "Workstation B").
* **Warehouse** (`warehouse`): Choose the warehouse location where the supply will be stocked (e.g., "Warehouse A", "Warehouse B").
* **Supply Status** (`supply_status`): Choose the status of the supply request (e.g., "Ordered", "Shipped", "Delayed").
* **Supply Priority** (`supply_priority`): Choose the priority level of the supply request (e.g., "High", "Medium", "Low").
* **Category** (`category`): Enter the category of the supply (e.g., "Office supplies", "Hardware", "Inventory").
* **Sub Category** (`sub_category`): Enter the subcategory of the supply (e.g., "Pens", "Pencils", "Markers").
* **Item Status** (`item_status`): Choose the status of the item (e.g., "Available", "Out of stock", "Backordered").
* **Supply Requester** (`supply_requester`): Choose the person or team that requested the supply (e.g., "John Doe", "Jane Doe").
* **Supply Manager** (`supply_manager`): Choose the person or team that will manage the supply (e.g., "John Doe", "Jane Doe").
* **Note**: A place to add any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Please fill in as much or as little information as needed, depending on the specific supply order.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all necessary information is included.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the supply manager or workstation supervisor.
