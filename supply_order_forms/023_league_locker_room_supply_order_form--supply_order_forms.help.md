# league_locker_room_supply_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **league_locker_room_supply_order_form** is used to order supplies and manage the inventory of a locker room in an e-commerce or business setting. This form collects information about the user, supply request, delivery method, delivery date and time, and other relevant details to facilitate the supply ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the **user_info** field. This is the contact information for the person requesting the supplies.
2. Provide a detailed **supply_request** describing the required supplies.
3. Select the **delivery_method** that best suits your needs from the available options.
4. Choose a specific **delivery_date** and **delivery_time** for the supply delivery.
5. Select the current **order_status** of the supply order from the available options.
6. Provide any additional **notes** related to the supply order.
7. Enter your **email** address for contact purposes.
8. Enter your **phone_number** for easy contact.
9. Enter the **order_id** if it's available.
10. Finally, select the **order_status** to track the progress of your supply order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info (text)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **supply_request (text)**: Describe the supplies you need to order, including quantity and any specific requirements.
* **delivery_method (select_one)**: Choose from the available delivery methods, such as in-person pickup or shipping.
* **delivery_date (date)**: Select the date you would like to receive the supplies.
* **delivery_time (time)**: Select the time you would like to receive the supplies.
* **order_status (select_multiple)**: Select the current status of your supply order, such as Active or Inactive.
* **notes (note)**: Add any additional comments or notes related to your supply order.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone_number (text)**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **order_id (number)**: Enter the order ID if it's available.
* **created_at (date)**: This field is automatically populated with the date the supply order was created.
* **updated_at (date)**: This field is automatically populated with the date the supply order was last updated.
