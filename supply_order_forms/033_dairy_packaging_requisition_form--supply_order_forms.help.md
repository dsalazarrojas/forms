# dairy_packaging_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dairy packaging requisition form is designed to collect information from customers about their packaging requests. This form aims to gather essential details about the customer's product needs, including the type of packaging they require, the desired delivery date, and the delivery time. The collected data will help the dairy packaging team to efficiently process and fulfill the customer's packaging needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Fill in the customer's information, including their name and any other relevant details.
3. Specify the product(s) you would like to order packaging for.
4. Choose a delivery date that suits your needs.
5. Select a delivery time that works best for you.
6. Review your form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_info (Customer Information)**: Enter your name, company, and any other relevant details.
* **product_request (Product Request)**: Describe the product(s) you would like to order packaging for, including the quantity needed.
* **delivery_date (Delivery Date)**: Select a date for when you would like to receive your packaged products.
* **delivery_time (Delivery Time)**: Choose a time that suits your schedule for delivery.
 

## Tips
* Double-check your form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
