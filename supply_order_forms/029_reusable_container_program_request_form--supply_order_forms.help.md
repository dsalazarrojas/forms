# reusable_container_program_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a reusable container program from the campus. It collects information about the container request, including details about the organization, container type, and other related information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form completely and accurately with your organization's details.
- Make sure to include all required fields and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting it to the campus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Container Request** (`container_request`, text, required/optional): This field is not displayed as it is not a field in the form.
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your organization, if you are not sure, you can leave it blank.
- **Campus Location** (`campus_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your campus, if you are not sure, you can leave it blank.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that the campus can use to contact you.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required/optional): Enter your phone number that the campus can use to contact you.
- **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to pick up the container.
- **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to pick up the container.
- **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you would like to pick up the container.
- **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where you would like the container to be delivered.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to have the container delivered.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to have the container delivered.
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of containers you are requesting.
- **Reuse Initiative** (`reuse_initiative`, text, required/optional): Enter the initiative you are reusing the container for.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your container request.
- **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person who can be contacted.
- **Container Type** (`container_type`, text, required/optional): Enter the type of container you are requesting.
- **Container Size** (`container_size`, text, required/optional): Enter the size of the container you are requesting.
- **Reuse Justification** (`reuse_justification`, note, required/optional): Enter the justification for reusing the container.
- **Container Condition** (`container_condition`, text, required/optional): Enter the condition of the container you are requesting.
- **Campus Name** (`campus_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the campus.
- **Campus Location** (`campus_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the campus.
- **Campus Email** (`campus_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the campus.
- **Campus Phone** (`campus_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the campus.
- **Campus Comments** (`campus_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the campus.
