# Sports Facility Restroom Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Facility Restroom Supply Order Form is used to order restocking and maintenance supplies for sports facility restrooms. 
This form will help the facility manager to efficiently and accurately request the necessary supplies, and ensure that the order is delivered to the correct location.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your facility address in the "Facility Address" field.
2. Select the date and time you would like the order to be processed in the "Order Date" and "Order Time" fields.
3. Enter the quantity of each supply item you need, in the corresponding fields:
   - Tissue: "Quantity Tissue"
   - Paper Towels: "Quantity Paper Towels"
   - Soap: "Quantity Soap"
   - Sham Poo: "Quantity Sham Poo"
   - Towel: "Quantity Towel"
4. Select the type of tissue you need in the "Quantity Tissue Type" field:
   - Toilet Tissue
   - Hand Towel
   - Other
5. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the order in the "Contact Name", "Contact E-mail", and "Contact Phone" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Facility Address** (Facility Address): Enter the address of the sports facility where the supplies are needed.
- **Order Date** (Order Date): Enter the date you would like the order to be processed. This field is a date picker.
- **Order Time** (Order Time): Enter the time you would like the order to be processed. This field is a time picker.
- **Quantity Tissue** (Quantity Tissue): Enter the quantity of tissue you need. This is a numerical field.
- **Quantity Paper Towels** (Quantity Paper Towels): Enter the quantity of paper towels you need. This is a numerical field.
- **Quantity Soap** (Quantity Soap): Enter the quantity of soap you need. This is a numerical field.
- **Quantity Sham Poo** (Quantity Sham Poo): Enter the quantity of sham poo you need. This is a numerical field.
- **Quantity Towel** (Quantity Towel): Enter the quantity of towel you need. This is a numerical field.
- **Quantity Tissue Type** (Quantity Tissue Type): Select the type of tissue you need:
   - Toilet Tissue
   - Hand Towel
   - Other
- **Contact Name** (Contact Name): Enter the name of the person responsible for the order.
- **Contact E-mail** (Contact E-mail): Enter the e-mail of the person responsible for the order. 
- **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the order.
- **Submit** (Submit): Click to submit the order.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
- Ensure that you have provided accurate contact information to facilitate follow-up communication.
- If you have any special requests or questions, contact the relevant department for assistance.
