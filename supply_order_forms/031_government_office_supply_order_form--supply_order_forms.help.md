# Government Office Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a new supply order for the government office. It is intended to be completed by office staff to request supplies from the facilities or procurement department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Order ID, a unique identifier for the order.
2. Enter the date of the order.
3. Provide a brief description of the order.
4. List the item(s) you are ordering.
5. Specify the quantity of each item ordered.
6. Enter the delivery address for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order ID
*   **Order ID** (`order_id`, `number`, required): Enter a unique identifier for the order, which is used to track and identify the order in the system.

### Order Date
*   **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which you are making the order.

### Description
*   **Description** (`order_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the order, including any specific details that will help the facilities or procurement department process the order.

### Order Item
*   **Order Item** (`order_item`, `text`, optional): List the item(s) you are ordering. This field is not required but is useful for additional information or clarification.

### Quantity
*   **Quantity** (`order_item_quantity`, `number`, required): Specify the quantity of each item ordered. This field is required to ensure accurate ordering and stock management.

### Delivery Address
*   **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Enter the delivery address for the order, which is where the supplies will be shipped to.

## Tips
*   Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, contact the facilities or procurement department for assistance.
*   Complete the form carefully to ensure timely and efficient processing of your order.
