# Factory Emergency Exit Signage Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to order emergency exit signage for a factory setting. Please provide accurate information to ensure proper completion of the order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: 
   * Contact Name: Enter the name of the person or department responsible for placing the order.
   * Contact Email: Enter the email address of the person or department responsible for placing the order.
   * Number of Signs: Enter the number of emergency exit signs required.
   * Sign Type: Select the type of sign desired (e.g., "Yes" for a standard sign or select multiple options if necessary).
2. Provide additional information as needed:
   * Address: Enter the address where the signs will be installed.
   * Message: Enter any special requests or instructions for the signs.
3. Submit the form to place the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the person or department responsible for placing the order.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of the person or department responsible for placing the order.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the person or department responsible for placing the order.
* **Address** (text): Enter the address where the signs will be installed.
* **Number of Signs** (number): Enter the number of emergency exit signs required.
* **Sign Type** (select_one): Select the type of sign desired (e.g., "Yes" for a standard sign or select multiple options if necessary).
* **Options Sign Type** (select_multiple): Select multiple types of signs as needed.
* **Message** (text): Enter any special requests or instructions for the signs.
* **Submit** (text): Click the submit button to complete and submit the order.
