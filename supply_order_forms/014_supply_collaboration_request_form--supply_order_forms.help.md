# Supply Collaboration Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supply Collaboration Request Form is a tool designed to facilitate communication and collaboration between stakeholders for requesting supplies or items. It helps ensure that all necessary information is captured efficiently, reducing the chance of errors and enhancing the overall supply ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Page 1**: This page is a general overview of your request.
    *   Enter your name to let us know who is making the request.
    *   Select the date when you need the supplies.
    *   Choose how urgent this request is.
    *   Decide whether or not a supplier is involved in this request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name**: Your name will help us identify who is making the request. Please fill this out with your correct name.
*   **Request Date**: This is when you need the supplies. Please select a date or enter it manually.
*   **Request Type**: Select 'Yes' if this request is urgent, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Requester**: If you are requesting from a supplier, select 'Yes', otherwise, select 'No'.
*   **Items Needed**: If you are requesting from a supplier, please list the items needed.
*   **Items Description**: Describe the items you are requesting.
*   **Requested By**: This is your contact information for follow-ups or questions.
*   **Items Status**: Please select if the items are active or inactive.
*   **Comment**: Add any additional comments or details about your request.
*   **Email**: Provide your email address for further communication.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick calls or updates.
*   **Additional Comments**: Add any other details about your request here.

## Tips
*   Be as specific as possible when describing the items needed.
*   Make sure to enter accurate contact information for follow-ups.
*   If you are requesting from a supplier, make sure to involve them in the process.
*   Keep the 'Request Type' field accurate to ensure timely attention to your request.
*   If the items are not needed, mark them as inactive to avoid confusion.
*   Use the 'Additional Comments' field to add any details that may not fit into other fields.
*   Review your form before submitting to ensure all necessary information is captured.
