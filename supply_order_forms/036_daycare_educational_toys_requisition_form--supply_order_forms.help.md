# Daycare Educational Toys Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request educational toys from the daycare department. Fill out the form to provide necessary information for the toys' delivery and processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email in the "Submitter Name" and "Submitter Email" fields, respectively.
2. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
3. Choose the type(s) of educational toy(s) you would like to request from the "Toy Type" dropdown menu. You can select one or multiple options.
4. Enter the quantity of toys you would like to request in the "Requested Quantity" field.
5. Enter the date when you would like to receive the toys in the "Date Requested" field.
6. Enter your comments or any additional information about the request in the "Comments" field.
7. Finally, select the date when you would like to receive the delivery of the toys in the "Delivery Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your department from the dropdown menu. This helps us process the request correctly.
* **Toy Type** (`toy_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose the type(s) of educational toy(s) you would like to request.
* **Requested Quantity** (`requested_quantity`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the quantity of toys you would like to request.
* **Date Requested** (`date_requested`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when you would like to receive the toys.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date when you would like to receive the delivery of the toys.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your request. 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to ensure timely and correct processing of your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
