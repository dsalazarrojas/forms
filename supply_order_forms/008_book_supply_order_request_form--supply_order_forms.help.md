# Book Supply Order Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to process book supply orders from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the customer information fields:
	* Enter the **Customer Name**, **Business or Organization Name**, and **Email Address**.
	* Enter the **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
2. Enter the **Billing Address** and **Shipping Address**.
3. Select the **Order Date** for this order.
4. If you have more than 3 books to order, fill out the **Book 1 - Title**, **Book 1 - ISBN**, **Book 1 - Quantity**, **Book 1 - Unit Price** fields, and so on for each book.
5. Choose the **Payment Terms** (e.g., **Net 30**, **Net 60**, **Credit Card**, **Check**, or **Bank Transfer**).
6. Add any **Special Requests** or **Additional Books or Details**.
7. If applicable, enter the **Purchase Order Number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the full name of the customer.
* **Business or Organization Name**: Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you will receive the order confirmation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the contact number for order purposes.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the full billing address.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter the full delivery address.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of this order.
* **Book 1 - Title**: Enter the title of the first book.
* **Book 1 - ISBN**: Enter the ISBN number for the first book.
* **Book 1 - Quantity**: Enter the number of copies for the first book.
* **Book 1 - Unit Price**: Enter the price per copy for the first book.
* **Book 2 - Title**: Enter the title of the second book.
* **Book 2 - Quantity**: Enter the number of copies for the second book.
* **Book 2 - Unit Price**: Enter the price per copy for the second book.
* **Book 3 - Title**: Enter the title of the third book.
* **Book 3 - Quantity**: Enter the number of copies for the third book.
* **Book 3 - Unit Price**: Enter the price per copy for the third book.
* **Additional Books or Details**: Enter any additional books not shown above.
* **Order Total**: Enter the total amount for this order.
* **Payment Terms**: Choose the payment method from the options provided.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special handling or delivery instructions.
* **Purchase Order Number**: Enter the PO number if applicable.
