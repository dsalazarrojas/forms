# Material Requisition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Material Requisition Form is designed to collect information about material requisitions from users. This form is intended for users who need to request materials for a specific purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are requesting any supply item** 
   * This field helps us understand your request better and prioritize it accordingly.
2. **Select the type of equipment or material you are requesting** 
   * This field ensures that we understand the type of equipment or material you need.
3. **Enter the quantity of the material you are requesting** 
   * This field is used to determine the amount of material needed.
4. **Provide a brief description of the material you are requesting** 
   * This field helps us understand the purpose of your request.
5. **Enter your name and email address** 
   * This field ensures that we can contact you regarding your request.
6. **Enter your phone number** 
   * This field allows us to contact you if needed.
7. **Date and time you expect the material** 
   * This field helps us plan and manage our resources accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **supply_item_1**: Select "Yes" if you are requesting any equipment or material. This field helps us determine if you have a genuine need for the material.
* **equipment_type_1**: Select all relevant equipment or material types that apply to your request.
* **quantity_1**: Enter the quantity of the material you are requesting.
* **description_1**: Provide a brief description of the material you are requesting.
* **requisitioner_1**: Enter your name as the person requesting the material.
* **email_1**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your request.
* **phone_1**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **date_1**: Select the date and time you expect the material. 

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when filling out this form.
* Make sure to include all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
