# homecare_field_nurse_supply_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the supply needs of patients, ensuring accurate and structured data collection for efficient communication with relevant personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Patient Info" section with the relevant details about the patient you are caring for.
2. In the "Supply Needed" section, select all the supplies that you think the patient needs. You can choose multiple items from the list.
3. Move on to the "Order Details" section, where you can provide any additional information about the order.
4. The "Date" and "Time" fields are not required, but they can be filled out if relevant to the supply request.
5. You can also provide the patient's "Phone" number and "Address" if they are relevant to the order.
6. If you have any additional comments or instructions, use the "Notes" field.
7. Finally, select the "Assigned Tool" that will be used to fulfill the supply request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Supply Needed**: Select all the supplies that you think the patient needs.
* **Order Details**: Provide any additional information about the order, such as quantities or special requests.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the supply is needed (if applicable).
* **Time**: Enter the time when the supply is needed (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number (if relevant).
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address (if relevant).
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or instructions about the order.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that will be used to fulfill the supply request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Double-check the information you provide to ensure it is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
