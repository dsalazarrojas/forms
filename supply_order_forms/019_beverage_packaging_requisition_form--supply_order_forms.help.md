# Beverage Packaging Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request beverage packaging supplies from a designated team. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the requester information section.
2. Select the type of packaging required.
3. Provide packaging specifications and product details.
4. Specify if the packaging requires customization or printing.
5. Confirm delivery and logistics information.
6. Confirm budget and approval information.
7. Provide any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Requester** (`requester_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the person submitting this request.
* **Department** (`requester_department`, `text`, required): Please enter the department this request is from.
* **Email Address** (`requester_email`, `email`, required): Please enter the email address of the person submitting this request.
* **Phone Number** (`requester_phone`, `text`, required): Please enter the phone number of the person submitting this request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date this request is being submitted.
* **Type of Packaging Required** (`packaging_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of packaging required.
* **Packaging Size or Specifications** (`packaging_size`, `text`, required): Please enter the size or specifications of the packaging required.
* **Product Name or Brand** (`product_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name or brand of the beverage product.
* **Quantity Required** (`quantity_needed`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of units needed.
* **Unit of Measurement** (`quantity_unit`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measurement for the quantity required.
* **Does This Require Custom Printing** (`custom_printing`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if this packaging requires custom printing.
* **Design or Printing Specifications** (`design_specifications`, `text`, required): Please enter the design or printing specifications.
* **Is Artwork Already Provided** (`artwork_provided`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if artwork is already provided.
* **Has This Design Been Approved** (`approved_design`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the design has been approved.
* **Date Needed By** (`needed_by_date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date by which the packaging is needed.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, `text`, required): Please enter the delivery location.
* **Complete Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, required): Please enter the complete delivery address.
* **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred delivery method.
* **Estimated Cost or Budget** (`estimated_cost`, `text`, required): Please enter the estimated cost or budget.
* **Cost Center or Account Code** (`cost_center`, `text`, required): Please enter the cost center or account code.
* **Is This Within Approved Budget** (`budget_approved`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if this is within the approved budget.
* **Manager Approval (if required)** (`approval_manager`, `text`, required): Please enter the approver name if required.
* **Special Instructions or Requirements** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required): Please enter any special instructions or requirements.
* **Quality Standards Required** (`quality_standards`, `text`, required): Please enter any quality standards required.
* **Does This Require Custom Printing** (`custom_printing`, `select_one`, required): Repeat of `Does This Require Custom Printing`.
