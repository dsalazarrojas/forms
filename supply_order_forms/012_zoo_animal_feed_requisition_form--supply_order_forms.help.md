# Zoo Animal Feed Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request feed for zoo animals. It helps staff to quickly and accurately gather the necessary information to fulfill the needs of each animal in the zoo.

## How To Complete This Form
- First, select all the species of the animals that need feed from the "species" field.
- Choose the habitat where the feed will be delivered, or select "Other" and specify the location.
- Enter the quantity of feed required for each species.
- Choose the date when the feed is needed, or select "None" if not applicable.
- If the feed is needed by a specific date, choose the date.
- Add any additional notes about the request in the "notes" field.
- Enter the name of the tool or equipment assigned to the request in the "assigned_tool" field.
- Enter the name of the person submitting the request in the "submitted_by" field.
- Enter the date and time the request was submitted in the "submitted_at" field.
- Enter the name of the person making the changes to the request in the "updated_by" field.
- Enter the date and time of the last update in the "updated_at" field.
- If the request was submitted to someone, enter their name in the "submitted_to" field.
- Enter the email of the person submitting the request in the "submitted_by_email" field.
- Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request in the "submitted_by_phone" field.
- If the request was submitted to someone, enter their email in the "submitted_to_email" field.
- If the request was submitted to someone, enter their phone number in the "submitted_to_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Species**: Choose all the species of animals that need feed, or select "None" if none are applicable.
* **Habitat**: Select the location where the feed will be delivered, or select "Other" and specify the location.
* **Quantity**: Enter the amount of feed required for each species.
* **Date**: Choose the date when the feed is needed, or select "None" if not applicable.
* **Needed By Date**: Choose the date when the feed is needed, if applicable.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the request.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool or equipment assigned to the request.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time the request was submitted.
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person making the changes to the request.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time of the last update.
* **Submitted To**: Enter the name of the person or department the request was submitted to.
* **Submitted By Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitted To Email**: Enter the email of the person or department the request was submitted to.
* **Submitted To Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person or department the request was submitted to.
