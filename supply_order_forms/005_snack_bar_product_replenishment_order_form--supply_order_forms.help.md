# Snack Bar Product Replenishment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Snack Bar Product Replenishment Order Form is used to submit a new stock replenishment order. This form is intended for employees of the organization who need to order supplies for the snack bar.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Choose the location that the order is intended for: School, Office, Gym, or Venue.
2. Provide the delivery details in the delivery_details field.
3. Select a delivery date.
4. Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
5. Select the locations where the order needs to be delivered to.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the delivery.
7. Enter any additional notes or instructions for the delivery.
8. Select the status of the order: Pending, In Progress, or Completed.
9. Enter the name of the supplier.
10. Enter the supplier's phone number.
11. Enter the supplier's email address.
12. Select the method of delivery.
13. Enter the date the order was placed.
14. Enter the quantity of the order.
15. Enter the price of the order.
16. Select the status of the order: Pending, In Progress, or Completed.
17. Select the type of customer: School, Office, or Gym.
18. Enter the address of the customer.
19. Enter the customer's phone number.
20. Enter the customer's email address.
21. Enter any notes or comments about the customer.
22. Select the status of the order: Pending, In Progress, or Completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_or_office** (Select One, Required): Choose the location the order is intended for. This could be a School, Office, Gym, or Venue.
* **delivery_details** (Text, Required): Enter any additional details about the delivery, such as special instructions or specific delivery location.
* **delivery_date** (Date, Required): Select the date the order is due to be delivered.
* **quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **delivery_location** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the locations where the order needs to be delivered to.
* **contact_person** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the delivery.
* **contact_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the delivery.
* **contact_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the delivery.
* **notes** (Note, Required): Enter any additional notes or comments for the delivery.
* **delivery_instructions** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional delivery instructions.
* **delivery_notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the delivery.
* **delivery_status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the order, such as Pending, In Progress, or Completed.
* **supplier** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the supplier.
* **supplier_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the supplier.
* **supplier_email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the supplier.
* **delivery_method** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the method of delivery, such as yes or no.
* **order_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date the order was placed.
* **order_quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the quantity of the order.
* **order_price** (Number, Required): Enter the price of the order.
* **order_status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the order, such as Pending, In Progress, or Completed.
* **customer** (Select One, Required): Select the type of customer, such as School, Office, or Gym.
* **customer_address** (Text, Required): Enter the address of the customer.
* **customer_phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the customer.
* **customer_email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the customer.
* **customer_note** (Note, Required): Enter any notes or comments about the customer.
* **delivery_status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the order, such as Pending, In Progress, or Completed.
