# Specialty Oil Procurement Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for gathering information about specialty oil procurement orders for internal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Review each section to ensure that all relevant information is captured.
4. For fields with multiple options, select the correct option that applies to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your first name as it appears on official records.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your last name as it appears on official records.
* **Address** (`3`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full address as it is registered in official records.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, `required`): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Date** (`6`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date when the order is required.
* **Order Type** (`7`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of order (Yes/No).
* **Quantity** (`8`, `number`, `required`): Fill in the amount of oil required.
* **Special Oil** (`9`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the type of special oil required (Yes/No).
* **Notes** (`10`, `note`, `optional`): Add any additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Confirm** (`11`, `select_one`, `optional`): Confirm the order (Yes/No).
* **Confirm Message** (`12`, `text`, `optional`): Add a confirmatory message about the order.
* **Specialty Oil Type** (`13`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the type of specialty oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Type** (`14`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the type of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Sub Type** (`15`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the sub-type of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Category** (`16`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the category of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Brand** (`17`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the brand of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Viscosity** (`18`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the viscosity of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Capacity** (`19`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the capacity of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Temperature** (`20`, `number`, `optional`): Fill in the oil temperature required.
* **Oil Sulfur** (`21`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the sulfur level of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Viscosity Type** (`22`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the viscosity type of oil required (Yes/No).
* **Oil Sulfur Level** (`23`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the sulfur level of oil required (High/Low).
* **Order Status** (`24`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the status of order (Active/Inactive).
* **Assigned To** (`25`, `select_one`, `required`): Select who the order is assigned to (Yes/No).

## Tips

1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the procurement officer or manager for guidance.
4. The form is designed for internal use only.
