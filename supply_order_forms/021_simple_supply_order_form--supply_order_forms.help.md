# simple_supply_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for creating a simple supply order. It collects user information and order details to facilitate the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your user information, including any relevant details.
2. Select the items you want to order from the list provided.
3. Enter the quantity of each item you selected.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
5. Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (Text)**: Enter your name and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Item List (Select Multiple)**: Choose the items you'd like to order from the list below.
	+ Select one or more items by clicking on each one to add it to your order.
* **Quantity (Number)**: Enter the quantity of each item you've selected.
* **Notes (Text)**: Any additional comments or instructions you'd like to add about your order.
* **Email Address (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
