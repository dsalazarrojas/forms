# Worker Ear Protection Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Ear Protection Kit Order Form is a form for workers to order a worker ear protection kit, a safety device used to protect workers' ears from harmful sounds and environments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **supply_order_form** field with a brief description of the order.
2. Provide **delivery_details**, including any special delivery instructions.
3. Specify the **employee_requests**, any specific requests or concerns from the employee.
4. Confirm the **order_confirmation**, to ensure the order is accurate.
5. Select the **kit_selection** you would like to order, choosing from 'Yes' or 'No'.
6. Choose the **order_status**, selecting from 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
7. Enter your **employee_name**.
8. Enter your **phone_number**.
9. Enter your **email_address**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **supply_order_form** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the order.
* **delivery_details** (Text, Optional): Special delivery instructions, if any.
* **employee_requests** (Text, Optional): Specific requests or concerns from the employee.
* **order_confirmation** (Text, Optional): Confirmation that the order is accurate.
* **kit_selection** (Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ 'Yes': Select this option if you would like to order a kit.
	+ 'No': Do not select this option if you do not want to order a kit.
* **order_status** (Select One, Optional):
	+ 'Active': Select this option if the kit is currently active.
	+ 'Inactive': Select this option if the kit is currently inactive.
* **employee_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **phone_number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **email_address** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately, as this will help us process your order correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
