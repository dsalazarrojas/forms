# Art Gallery Exhibition Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Exhibition Supplies Order Form is a tool used to collect necessary information for the procurement and delivery of exhibition supplies. This form is a crucial part of the internal process to ensure accurate tracking and accounting of supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your order ID, which is an internal reference number for the order.
2. Provide your name and title as the person placing the order.
3. Enter the delivery address where the supplies should be delivered.
4. List the required supply items and quantities accurately.
5. Specify the required date for the supplies to be delivered.
6. If preferred, select a vendor for the supplies.
7. Enter the internal budget code for accounting purposes.
8. Update the approval status of the order (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
9. If necessary, enter the name of the approving manager.
10. Provide special delivery instructions if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order ID** (`order_id`, `text`, **required**): Enter your internal order reference number to track the order accurately.
* **Requestor name** (`requestor`, `text`, **required**): Provide your name and title as the person placing the order.
* **Delivery address** (`delivery_address`, `text`, **required**): Enter the exact address where the supplies should be delivered.
* **Supply items required** (`supply_items`, `text`, **required**): List the specific supply items and quantities needed for the exhibition.
* **Required by date** (`required_by_date`, `date`, **required**): Specify the date by which the supplies are required.
* **Vendor preference** (`vendor_preference`, `text`, **optional**): If you have a preferred vendor for the supplies, enter their name.
* **Budget code** (`budget_code`, `text`, **required**): Enter the internal accounting code for the budget.
* **Approval status** (`approval_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Update the approval status of the order to (Pending, Approved, or Denied).
* **Approver name** (`approver_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the name of the approving manager if necessary.
* **Delivery instructions** (`delivery_instructions`, `text`, **optional**): Provide any special delivery notes or instructions if required.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields to ensure smooth delivery and proper accounting.
* Double-check the required date for the supplies delivery to avoid delays.
* If you have a preferred vendor, make sure to select them for the order.
* Update the approval status regularly to track the order's progress.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the exhibition coordinator or the procurement team for guidance.
