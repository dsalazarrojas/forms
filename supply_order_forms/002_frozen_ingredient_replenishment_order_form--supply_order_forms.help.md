# Frozen Ingredient Replenishment Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Frozen Ingredient Replenishment Order Form is a tool used by suppliers to order frozen ingredients and maintain inventory levels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of the order you would like to place in the `order_details` field.
2. Select the frequency of your order from the `order_frequency` field. This will help us determine how often you need to receive your replenishment.
3. Choose your preferred delivery frequency from the `delivery_frequency` field. This will help us ensure that your goods are delivered at the right time.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your order in the `order_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **order_details**: Enter a brief description of the order you are placing. This can include the product code, quantity, and any other relevant details.
* **order_frequency**: Select how often you would like to receive your replenishment. Options include:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **delivery_frequency**: Choose your preferred delivery frequency. Options include:
	+ Every 1 week
	+ Every 2 weeks
	+ Every 3 weeks
* **order_notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your order. This can include special requests, delivery instructions, or any other relevant information.
