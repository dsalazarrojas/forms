# material_handling_crane_component_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a material handling crane component order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the relevant information for the crane part you wish to order.
2. Specify the quantity of the component you need.
3. Select the order and delivery dates.
4. Provide your contact details to ensure smooth communication during the order process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crane Part** (crane_part, text, required): Enter the specific crane component you wish to order, for example, 'Hook 5000'.
* **Quantity** (quantity, number, required): Enter the number of crane components you need to order.
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, required): Enter the date you need to receive the ordered components.
* **Delivery Date** (delivery_date, date, required): Enter the date you expect the ordered components to be delivered.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for the order.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address or the email address of the person responsible for the order.
