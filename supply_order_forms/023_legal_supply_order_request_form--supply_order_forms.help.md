# Legal Supply Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Supply Order Request Form is designed to collect information for official legal supply orders. This form helps staff members who need to request supplies to ensure that all necessary details are provided for processing and fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Order Type** from the available options.
2. Choose the **Category** of the supply order (e.g., Office, Janitorial, etc.).
3. Enter the **Order Description** in the provided text field.
4. If necessary, provide additional details in the **Additional Information** field.
5. Review the form for completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **Order Type**: (Required) The type of order you are requesting (e.g., Routine, Special, or Urgent).
* **Category**: (Optional) The category of the supply order (e.g., Office, Janitorial, etc.).

### Second Page
* **Order Description**: (Required) A detailed description of the supply order.
* **Additional Information**: (Optional) Any other details that would be helpful for processing the order.

### Third Page
No fields are present on this page.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact your supply chain representative.
* Double-check the form for accuracy before submission to avoid errors or delays in processing.
