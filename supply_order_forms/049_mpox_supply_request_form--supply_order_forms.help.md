# Mpox Supply Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Supply Request Form is designed for users to submit supply requests to their team or department. This form is used to gather information for the purpose of ordering supplies and ensuring they are delivered on time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first name.
2. Enter your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the date you need the supply by.
6. Select the time you need the supply by.
7. Provide a brief description of the supply you are requesting.
8. Choose the type of Mpox you are requesting (Mpox, Mpox2, or Mpox3).
9. Enter a justification for your request.
10. Choose who requested and ordered the supply (Alice, Bob, or Charlie).
11. Choose the status of the request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
12. Enter the location of the Mpox supply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or business card.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or business card.
- **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your work account.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
- **Date**: Enter the date you need the supply by in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time**: Enter the time you need the supply by in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- **Description**: Briefly describe the supply you are requesting, including any specific details.
- **Mpox Type**: Choose the type of Mpox you are requesting (Mpox, Mpox2, or Mpox3).
- **Justification**: Enter a brief explanation for why the supply is needed.
- **Requested By**: Choose who requested the supply (Alice, Bob, or Charlie).
- **Ordered By**: Choose who ordered the supply (Alice, Bob, or Charlie).
- **Date Ordered**: Enter the date the supply was ordered in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Status**: Choose the current status of the request (Pending, Approved, or Rejected).
- **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the supply request (optional).
- **Mpox Location**: Enter the location of the Mpox supply, which may impact delivery.

# Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, as they are marked with an asterisk (\*).
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the team lead or department responsible for supply ordering.
- If the status of your request is not what you expected, please review your request and contact the team lead or department responsible for supply ordering.
