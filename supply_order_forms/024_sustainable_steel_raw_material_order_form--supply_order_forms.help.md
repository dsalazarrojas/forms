# Sustainable Steel Raw Material Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers for sustainable steel raw materials. It helps ensure that suppliers provide accurate and detailed information about their materials, making it easier for buyers to place orders and receive their products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier information section with your company name and contact details.
2. Select the type of order you are placing (Raw Material Order or Steel Order).
3. Choose the type of steel grade you require (High-Strength Low-Alloy Steel, Low-Carbon Steel, or Stainless Steel).
4. Describe the material you are ordering, including its type and condition.
5. Provide any additional order details, such as special requests or specifications.
6. Specify your delivery instructions, if any.
7. Enter your delivery address, which will be used for shipping purposes.
8. Select a delivery date and time that suits your needs.
9. Choose a contact method for the supplier (Phone, Email, or Text Message).
10. If you have any special requests or notes, you can add them in the procurement request field.
11. Finally, provide any additional delivery instructions if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier** (`Supplier`, text, required: false): 
  Enter your company name, which will be used to identify you as a supplier.

* **Order Type** (`Order Type`, select_one, required: false):
  Select the type of order you are placing (Raw Material Order or Steel Order).

* **Steel Grade** (`Steel Grade`, select_multiple, required: false):
  Choose the type of steel grade you require (High-Strength Low-Alloy Steel, Low-Carbon Steel, or Stainless Steel).

* **Material Description** (`Material Description`, text, required: false):
  Briefly describe the type and condition of the material you are requesting.

* **Order Details** (`Order Details`, text, required: false):
  Provide any additional details about the order, such as special requests or specifications.

* **Delivery Instructions** (`Delivery Instructions`, text, required: false):
  Describe any specific delivery needs, if applicable.

* **Delivery Address** (`Delivery Address`, text, required: false):
  Enter your delivery address, which will be used for shipping purposes.

* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, date, required: false):
  Select a delivery date that suits your needs.

* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, time, required: false):
  Choose a delivery time that suits your needs.

* **Supplier Contact** (`Supplier Contact`, select_one, required: false):
  Select a contact method for the supplier (Phone, Email, or Text Message).

* **Procurement Request** (`Procurement Request`, note, required: false):
  Add any special requests or notes about the order.

* **Delivery Instructions 2** (`Delivery Instructions 2`, text, required: false):
  Provide any additional delivery instructions, if necessary.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information about the material you are ordering.
- Double-check the delivery date and time to ensure it meets your needs.
- If you have any special requests or notes, use the procurement request field to communicate them to the supplier.
- Use the delivery instructions fields to provide specific delivery needs, if applicable.
