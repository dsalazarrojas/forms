# Daycare Supplies Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Supplies Order Form is designed to help daycare administrators or staff order supplies for their center. This form collects information about the supplies needed and their delivery preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you want to have your supplies delivered or picked up at the daycare center.
2. Add as many items as you need by clicking the "Add Item" button.
3. For each item, fill in the name, quantity, and price of the item.
4. Provide a brief description of each item.
5. Select the delivery method for your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery or Pickup** (`delivery_or_pickup`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether you want to have your supplies delivered or picked up at the daycare center.
* **Items Needed** (`items_needed`, `text`, optional): This is where you can provide a list of items you need. You can enter as many items as you need, and we will help you order them.
* **Item 1 Name** (`item1_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the first item you need.
* **Item 1 Quantity** (`item1_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the first item you need.
* **Item 1 Description** (`item1_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the first item you need.
* **Item 1 Price** (`item1_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the first item you need.
* **Item 2 Name** (`item2_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the second item you need.
* **Item 2 Quantity** (`item2_quantity`, `number`, optional): Enter the quantity of the second item you need.
* **Item 2 Description** (`item2_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the second item you need.
* **Item 2 Price** (`item2_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price of the second item you need.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, `select_one`, optional): Select the delivery method for your order: shipping or pickup.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all required information for each item.
* Double-check the quantity and price of each item to ensure accuracy.
* If you need help or have any questions, please contact our support team.
