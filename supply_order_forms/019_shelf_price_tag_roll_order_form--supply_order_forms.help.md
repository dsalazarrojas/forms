# Shelf Price Tag Roll Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shelf Price Tag Roll Order Form is used to submit roll orders for shelf price tags. This form is required for all roll orders to ensure accurate and prompt processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Select the correct payment method.
4. Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (customer_name, text, required): Enter the customer's name as it appears on their order confirmation.
* **Customer Email** (customer_email, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order Date** (order_date, date, required): Enter the date of the order in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Order Quantity** (order_quantity, number, required): Enter the number of rolls ordered.
* **Order Description** (order_description, text, required): Enter a brief description of the order.
* **Order Total** (order_total, number, required): Enter the total cost of the order.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required): Select the payment method used for the order:
	+ Credit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Bank Transfer

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure payment method is selected correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the order department for assistance.
