# School Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Supply Order Form is designed to allow students to submit orders for school supplies. This form aims to gather information necessary for the school administration to fulfill student requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as a student.
2. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
3. Select the school supplies you would like to order (e.g., Pencil, Pencils, Notebooks, Binders).
4. Add any additional order notes or instructions.
5. Choose a date for the order (if required).
6. (Optional) Provide a specific time for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_form** (`order_form`, text, required: false): Enter your order details in this text area.
* **student_name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your name as a student.
* **student_email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **school_supplies** (`school_supplies`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the school supplies you would like to order. Available options include Pencil, Pencils, Notebooks, and Binders.
* **order_notes** (`order_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional order notes or instructions.
* **order_date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for the order.
* **order_time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): (Optional) Provide a specific time for the order.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (if any).
- If you need help or have questions, contact the form owner at form@gic.mx.
