# Safety Floor Marking Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Floor Marking Supply Order Form is used to collect information for the ordering of safety floor marking supplies. This form ensures that all necessary details are provided to ensure the correct and timely delivery of the supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Order Details** field with a brief description of the order.
2. Provide the **Facility Name** where the safety floor marking supplies are to be delivered.
3. Enter the **Contact Person** responsible for the order.
4. Enter the **Contact Email** of the **Contact Person**.
5. Enter the **Contact Phone** number of the **Contact Person**.
6. Select the **Delivery Date** when the supplies are to be delivered.
7. Choose the **Delivery Time** for the supplies.
8. Select whether you would like to **Supply Items** (Yes/No).
9. Choose the **Payment Method** (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
10. Enter the **Payment Amount** for the order.
11. Enter the **Payment Terms** if any special payment conditions apply.
12. Add any additional **Delivery Address** details for the supplier.
13. Enter any **Delivery Instructions** to ensure proper handling and delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (order_details)**: A brief description of the order, such as the quantity of supplies needed.
* **Facility Name (facility_name)**: The name of the facility where the safety floor marking supplies are to be delivered.
* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: The person responsible for the order.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date)**: The date when the supplies are to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time)**: The time when the supplies are to be delivered.
* **Supply Items (supply_items)**: Select whether you would like to supply items (Yes/No).
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose the payment method for the order (Stripe, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount (payment_amount)**: The amount to be paid for the order.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms)**: Any special payment conditions that apply.
* **Delivery Address (delivery_address)**: Any additional details for the delivery address.
* **Delivery Instructions (delivery_instructions)**: Any special instructions for the delivery, such as handling instructions.
