# Cinema Concession Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request concessions such as popcorn kernels, popcorn oil, butter topping, and other snack and beverage items for a cinema or movie theater.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the location where the concession items will be used.
2. Enter the location code for the selected location.
3. Enter the request date and the name of the person requesting the concessions.
4. Enter the quantity of each concession item needed.
5. Add any special or limited-time items if necessary.
6. Enter any delivery or special instructions for the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cinema Location**: Select the location where the concession items will be used, such as Downtown theater, Mall location, or Suburban complex.
* **Location Code**: Enter the unique code for the selected location.
* **Request Date**: Enter the date when the concessions are needed.
* **Requested By (Name)**: Enter the name of the person requesting the concessions.
* **Position Title**: Enter your title or role in the cinema, such as Concession Manager.
* **Required by Date**: Enter the date when the concessions are needed by.
* **Popcorn Kernels (pounds)**: Enter the quantity of popcorn kernels needed.
* **Popcorn Oil (gallons)**: Enter the quantity of popcorn oil needed.
* **Butter Topping (gallons)**: Enter the quantity of butter topping needed.
* **Popcorn Bags or Containers (units)**: Enter the quantity of popcorn bags or containers needed.
* **Soda Syrup (units)**: Enter the quantity of soda syrup needed.
* **Soda Cups - Various Sizes (units)**: Enter the quantity of soda cups needed.
* **Bottled Water (cases)**: Enter the quantity of bottled water needed.
* **Juice or Other Beverages (units)**: Enter the quantity of juice or other beverages needed.
* **Candy Boxes (select types)**: Enter the quantity and type of candy boxes needed.
* **Nachos (units or cases)**: Enter the quantity of nachos needed.
* **Hot Dogs or Sausages (units)**: Enter the quantity of hot dogs or sausages needed.
* **Pretzels (units or cases)**: Enter the quantity of pretzels needed.
* **Napkins (cases)**: Enter the quantity of napkins needed.
* **Straws (boxes)**: Enter the quantity of straws needed.
* **Condiments - Ketchup, Mustard, etc (units)**: Enter the quantity of condiments needed.
* **Cleaning Supplies (specify items)**: Enter the type and quantity of cleaning supplies needed.
* **Special or Limited-Time Items**: Enter any special or limited-time items needed.
* **Delivery or Special Instructions**: Enter any delivery or special instructions for the order.
