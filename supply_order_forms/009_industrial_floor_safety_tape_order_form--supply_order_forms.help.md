# industrial_floor_safety_tape_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for industrial floor safety tape.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the desired quantity of safety tape you would like to order.
2. Select the type of tape you would like to order (yellow, blue, or red).
3. Choose the material of the tape (PVC, PVC with adhesive, or Paper-based).
4. If you have any special instructions for the order, enter them in the "Special Instructions" field.
5. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with order updates and confirmation.
6. Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
7. Enter your name to confirm your identity and order details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Tape** (Order Tape, text, required): Enter the desired quantity of safety tape you would like to order. Please make sure to include the correct quantity to avoid any discrepancies with our warehouse.
* **Tape Type** (Tape Type, select_one, optional): Select the type of tape you would like to order. The available options are yellow, blue, and red. You can choose one of these options or leave it blank if you're not sure.
* **Quantity** (Quantity, number, required): Enter the quantity of tape you would like to order. Make sure to enter a numerical value.
* **Material** (Material, select_multiple, optional): Choose the material of the tape you would like to order. The available options are PVC, PVC with adhesive, and Paper-based.
* **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions, note, optional): If you have any special instructions or requests for the order, enter them here. This is an optional field, but it's a good idea to include any relevant details to ensure your order is fulfilled correctly.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with order updates and confirmation.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your name to confirm your identity and order details.
