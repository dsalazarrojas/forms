# Client Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about a client's supply order. Please use this form to provide the necessary details about your order.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Complete all required fields. The required fields are marked as such in this guide.
2. Fill out each section in its entirety before proceeding to the next one.
3. Review your order before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (3, Customer Name, **required**): Please enter the name of the customer.
* **Company Name** (4, Company Name, **required**): Please enter the name of the company the order is for.
* **Email Address** (5, Email Address, **required**): Please enter the email address of the customer.
* **Phone Number** (6, Phone Number, **required**): Please enter the phone number of the customer.
* **Customer ID** (7, Customer ID, **optional**): If applicable, please enter the customer's account number.
* **Order Date** (8, Order Date, **required**): Please enter the date of the order.
* **Street Address** (10, Street Address, **required**): Please enter the street address of the billing address.
* **City** (11, City, **required**): Please enter the city of the billing address.
* **State or Province** (12, State or Province, **required**): Please enter the state or province of the billing address.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (13, ZIP or Postal Code, **required**): Please enter the ZIP or postal code of the billing address.
* **Country** (14, Country, **required**): Please enter the country of the billing address.
* **Shipping address same as billing?** (16, Shipping address same as billing?, **required**): Check if the shipping address is the same as the billing address.
* **Shipping Street** (17, Shipping Street, **optional**): If the shipping address is different from the billing address, please enter the street address of the shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (18, Shipping City, **optional**): If the shipping address is different from the billing address, please enter the city of the shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (19, Shipping State, **optional**): If the shipping address is different from the billing address, please enter the state or province of the shipping address.
* **Shipping ZIP or Postal Code** (20, Shipping ZIP or Postal Code, **optional**): If the shipping address is different from the billing address, please enter the ZIP or postal code of the shipping address.
* **Item 1 - Product Name** (22, Item 1 - Product Name, **required**): Please enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Item 1 - SKU or Product Code** (23, Item 1 - SKU or Product Code, **optional**): If applicable, please enter the SKU or product code of the product being ordered.
* **Item 1 - Quantity** (24, Item 1 - Quantity, **required**): Please enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Item 1 - Unit Price** (25, Item 1 - Unit Price, **required**): Please enter the unit price of the product being ordered.
* **Item 1 - Total Price** (26, Item 1 - Total Price, **optional**): This field will be automatically calculated and should not be filled out.
* **Item 2 - Product Name** (27, Item 2 - Product Name, **optional**): If you are ordering multiple items, please enter the name of the second item.
* **Item 2 - Quantity** (28, Item 2 - Quantity, **optional**): If you are ordering multiple items, please enter the quantity of the second item.
* **Item 2 - Unit Price** (29, Item 2 - Unit Price, **optional**): If you are ordering multiple items, please enter the unit price of the second item.
* **Item 2 - Total Price** (30, Item 2 - Total Price, **optional**): This field will be automatically calculated and should not be filled out.
