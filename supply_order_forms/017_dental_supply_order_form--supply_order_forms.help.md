# Dental Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help dental professionals order dental supplies in an efficient and user-friendly manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields on **Page 1: Patient Information**. Enter the patient's details as needed.
2. Proceed to **Page 2: Product Selection** and select the required dental supplies from the list.
3. On **Page 3: Order Details**, provide any additional information or comments about the order, if necessary.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it for final review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their dental chart or medical records.
* **Other** (`hint`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the patient or order, if needed.

### Product Selection (Page 2)
* **Product Options** (`options`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the dental supplies required for the patient's treatment. Choose 'Yes' for the products needed and 'No' for those not needed.

### Order Details (Page 3)
* **Order Notes** (`label`, `text`, optional): If there are any specific instructions or details regarding the order, enter them here.
