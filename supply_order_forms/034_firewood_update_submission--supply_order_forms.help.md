# Firewood Update Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Firewood Update Submission** form is used to submit updates regarding firewood availability. This form is intended for suppliers to provide accurate and timely updates on their current status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the **Firewood Update Submission** link to access the form.
2. Select the applicable firewood type from the **firewood_type** dropdown menu, or choose "No" if the firewood type is not applicable.
3. Optionally, enter the quantity of firewood available for update. If no quantity is provided, it will be assumed that no change has occurred.
4. Select the **status** of the firewood, either **Active** or **Inactive**.
5. If you have any additional comments regarding the status, enter them in the **status_notes** field.
6. Choose whether photos are **Yes** or **No** if you need to provide additional documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **firewood_type**: Select the type of firewood being updated.
  - This field is used to identify the type of firewood being reported, whether it's a specific species, type, or size.
* **quantity**: Enter the number of units of firewood available for update. (Optional)
  - This field is used to indicate if you are updating the quantity of firewood available.
* **status**: Select the current status of the firewood.
  - Choose between **Active** or **Inactive** to indicate whether the firewood is available or not.
* **status_notes**: Provide any additional comments regarding the status of the firewood.
  - Enter any additional information you'd like to include with your update.
* **photos**: Choose whether you need to upload photos with your submission.
  - If you've taken additional photos for reference, select **Yes** to include them with your submission.
