# Daily Truck Inventory Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Truck Inventory Checklist Form is used to track and maintain accurate and up-to-date information about the trucks, including their serial number, model, mileage, and other key attributes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Navigate to the first page of the form.
2. Enter the **Truck Serial Number**. This is a unique identifier for the truck.
3. Enter the **Truck Model**. This is the type of truck you are tracking.
4. Enter the **Mileage**. The current mileage of the truck.
5. Select the **Truck Color** from the available options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
6. Select the **Truck Type** from the available options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
7. Enter the **Fuel Level**. This indicates the level of fuel in the truck.
8. Select the **Engine Oil** from the available options: Regular, Synthetic, or Synthetic Blend.
9. Select the **Transmission Fluid** from the available options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
10. Select the **Tire Pressure** from the available options: Low, Medium, or High.
11. Enter the **Driver Name**. This is the name of the driver who last drove the truck.
12. Select the **Vehicle Status** from the available options: Active, Inactive, or On Order.
13. Add any **Notes** about the truck.
14. Enter the **Contact Email**. This is the contact information for the user.
15. Enter the **Contact Phone**. This is the contact information for the user.
16. Select the **Created Date** and **Updated Date**.
17. Select the **Assigned To** from the available options: chatjimmy or Other.
18. Enter the **Assigned Date**.
19. Enter the **Due Date**.
20. Select the **Completed By** from the available options: chatjimmy or Other.
21. Select the **Submitted By** from the available options: chatjimmy or Other.
22. Select the **Submitted Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Truck Serial Number** (1, number, required): Enter the unique identifier for the truck.
* **Truck Model** (2, text, required): Enter the type of truck you are tracking.
* **Truck Year** (3, number, required): Enter the year of the truck.
* **Mileage** (4, number, required): Enter the current mileage of the truck.
* **Truck Color** (5, text, required): Select the color of the truck from the available options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **Truck Type** (6, select_one, required): Select the type of truck from the available options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **Fuel Level** (7, number, required): Enter the fuel level in the truck.
* **Engine Oil** (8, select_multiple, required): Select the type of engine oil used in the truck from the available options: Regular, Synthetic, or Synthetic Blend.
* **Transmission Fluid** (9, select_multiple, required): Select the type of transmission fluid used in the truck from the available options: Red, Blue, Green, or Yellow.
* **Tire Pressure** (10, select_one, required): Select the tire pressure from the available options: Low, Medium, or High.
* **Driver Name** (11, text, required): Enter the name of the driver who last drove the truck.
* **Vehicle Status** (12, select_one, required): Select the status of the vehicle from the available options: Active, Inactive, or On Order.
* **Notes** (13, note, required): Enter any notes about the truck.
* **Contact Email** (14, email, required): Enter the contact information for the user.
* **Contact Phone** (15, text, required): Enter the contact information for the user.
* **Created At** (16, date, required): Select the date the form was created.
* **Updated At** (17, time, required): Select the time the form was updated.
* **Assigned To** (18, select_one, required): Select the person assigned to the truck from the available options: chatjimmy or Other.
* **Assigned Date** (19, date, required): Enter the date the truck was assigned.
* **Due Date** (20, date, required): Enter the due date for the truck.
* **Completed At** (21, time, required): Select the time the truck was completed.
* **Completed By** (22, select_one, required): Select the person who completed the form from the available options: chatjimmy or Other.
* **Submitted By** (23, select_one, required): Select the person who submitted the form from the available options: chatjimmy or Other.
* **Submitted Date** (24, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation above for more information.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, please contact your supervisor or support team.
