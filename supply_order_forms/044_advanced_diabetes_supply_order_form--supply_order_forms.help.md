### Advanced Diabetes Supply Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Advanced Diabetes Supply Order Form is designed for healthcare professionals to order advanced diabetes supplies on behalf of a patient. This form gathers essential information to ensure that the ordered supplies meet the patient's needs and are delivered correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the facility's information.
2.  Select the required supplies for the patient.
3.  Specify the quantity of each selected item.
4.  Choose the urgency of the order.
5.  Enter the shipping and billing information.
6.  Confirm that the order is under valid medical direction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Facility Name** (id: 2): Enter the name of the facility where the order is being placed.
*   **Department Ordering** (id: 3): Enter the department within the facility where the order is being placed.
*   **Authorized Purchaser Name** (id: 4): Enter the name of the authorized person responsible for the order.
*   **Facility Phone Number** (id: 5): Enter the phone number of the facility.
*   **Patient Identifier - Internal** (id: 7): Enter the patient's internal identifier, which is their medical record number or ID.
*   **Glucose Monitoring Supplies** (id: 9): Select all required glucose monitoring items:
    *   **CGM Sensors**
    *   **CGM Transmitters**
    *   **Test Strips**
    *   **Lancets**
    *   **Control Solution**
*   **Insulin Delivery Supplies** (id: 10): Select all required insulin delivery items:
    *   **Insulin Pump Reservoirs**
    *   **Infusion Sets**
    *   **Insulin Pens**
    *   **Pen Needles**
    *   **Syringes**
*   **Quantity per Item Description** (id: 11): Describe the quantity of each selected item, e.g., "3 boxes of Test Strips" or "2 CGM Sensors".
*   **Order Urgency** (id: 12): Choose the urgency of the order:
    *   **Routine Stock**
    *   **Patient Discharge**
    *   **Emergency Need**
*   **Shipping Address** (id: 14): Enter the shipping address for the ordered supplies.
*   **Billing Account Number** (id: 15): Enter the billing account number registered with the supply company.
*   **Confirm Prescriptive Authority** (id: 17): Confirm that the ordered supplies are under valid medical direction.
    *   **True** 
    *   **False**
