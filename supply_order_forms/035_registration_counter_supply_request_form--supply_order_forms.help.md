# Registration Counter Supply Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request supplies for the registration counter. Please ensure that you only submit new or updated requests to avoid duplicating existing ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **registration_counter_id** (optional) to help identify your request.
2. Enter your **first_name** and **last_name** to provide personal identification.
3. Select the supplies you are requesting for the registration counter in the **supply_requested** field.
4. Provide a brief description of the items you are requesting in the **item_description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **registration_counter_id**: A unique identifier for your request (optional). Please enter your registration counter ID if you have one.
- **first_name**: Your first name.
- **last_name**: Your last name.
- **supply_requested**: Select the supplies you are requesting from the list of available options (e.g. Option 1, Option 2).
- **item_description**: A brief description of the items you are requesting.
