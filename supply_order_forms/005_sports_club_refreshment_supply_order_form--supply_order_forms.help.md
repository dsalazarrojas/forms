# sports_club_refreshment_supply_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports club refreshment supply order form is used to collect information about orders for sports club supplies. This form helps the staff process orders correctly and ensure timely delivery of the supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the order information in the "Order info" field.
2. Enter the quantity of the item being ordered.
3. Add a brief description of the item being ordered.
4. Enter the price of the item.
5. Enter the date and time of the order.
6. Add any additional notes about the order.
7. Enter the contact email for the person placing the order.
8. Enter the contact phone number for the person placing the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Order info**: A field for entering order-related information (e.g., order number, order status).
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the item being ordered.
	+ Type: Number field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Item description**: A field for entering a brief description of the item being ordered.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Item price**: Enter the price of the item being ordered.
	+ Type: Number field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Order date**: Enter the date of the order.
	+ Type: Date field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Order time**: Enter the time of the order.
	+ Type: Time field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Order notes**: Add any additional notes about the order.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Contact email**: Enter the email address of the person placing the order.
	+ Type: Email field.
	+ Required: No.
- **Contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the person placing the order.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: No.

Note: Fields marked as "No" for required do not necessarily mean they are optional, but rather that they are not required to be filled out for the form to be submitted.
