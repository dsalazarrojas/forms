# Shelf Accessory Hardware Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information, order details, and payment information for shelf accessory hardware orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information on the first page, including any relevant details that will help us understand your order.
2. Provide order details, including any necessary information about the order you are placing.
3. Select any additional order options that apply to your order.
4. (Optional) If you have payment information, you can provide it to speed up the order process. Otherwise, you can confirm your order without payment information.
5. Confirm your order by selecting either "confirm" or "decline".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (Page 1)** (`user_info`, text, required): This field is where you provide your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Order Details (Page 1)** (`order_details`, text, required): This field is where you provide details about the order you are placing, including any necessary specifications and requirements.
* **Order Options (Page 1)** (`order_options`, text, required): This field is where you select any additional options for your order, such as delivery speed or packaging preferences.
* **Payment Information (Page 1)** (`payment_info`, text, optional): (Optional) If you have payment information, you can provide it here to speed up the order process.
* **Confirm (Page 2)** (`confirm`, select_one, required): This field is where you confirm your order by selecting either "confirm" or "decline".
