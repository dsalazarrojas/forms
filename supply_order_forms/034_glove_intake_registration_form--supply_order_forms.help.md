# Glove Intake Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Glove Intake Registration Form. The form is used to register a glove intake, gathering information about the department, time, and quantity of the glove.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Department (1)**:
	* Enter the department where the glove was taken from. This is a required field.
2. **Intake Time (2)**:
	* Enter the date and time when the glove was taken in.
3. **Intake Quantity (3)**:
	* Enter the number of gloves that were taken in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department (1)**: Enter the department where the glove was taken from. This is a text field where you can enter the name of the department.
* **Intake Time (2)**: Enter the date and time when the glove was taken in. This field is for entering dates in a standard format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Intake Quantity (3)**: Enter the number of gloves that were taken in. This is a numeric field where you can enter a whole number.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for each field to ensure accurate tracking and inventory management.
* If you have trouble with the form, feel free to ask for help or contact the relevant team for assistance.
