# goods_movement_record_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Goods Movement Record Form" is a tool used to record and track goods movements within the organization. It ensures that all necessary information is accurately documented for future reference and efficient management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the "Goods Movement" page to understand the context of the goods movement.
2. Fill out the "Goods Type" field by selecting the relevant option that best describes the type of goods being moved.
3. Choose the "Movement Type" that best describes the movement (e.g., "Yes" for a valid movement).
4. Select all applicable "Warehouse" locations where the goods were or will be stored.
5. Choose all applicable "Location" points where the goods were or will be transported.
6. Enter the "Movement Date" when the goods movement occurred.
7. Enter the "Delivery Date" when the goods were delivered.
8. Provide the "Shipment ID" if available.
9. Enter a brief description of the goods being moved in the "Goods Description" field.
10. Enter the quantity of goods moved in the "Movement Quantity" field.
11. Enter the weight of the goods in the "Goods Weight" field.
12. Select all applicable "Goods Size" options (e.g., "Yes" for a specific size).
13. Select all applicable "Goods Color" options (e.g., "Yes" for a specific color).
14. Select all applicable "Supplier" options (e.g., "Yes" for the supplier involved).
15. Select all applicable "Goods Status" options (e.g., "Active" for goods currently in stock).
16. Enter any additional remarks regarding the goods movement in the "Remarks" field.
17. Choose the personnel assigned to the goods movement (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Goods Movement**: This field serves as a page heading to provide context for the form.
* **Goods Type**: Select the type of goods being moved (e.g., "Yes" for a specific type).
* **Movement Type**: Choose the type of movement (e.g., "Yes" for a valid movement).
* **Warehouse**: Select all locations where the goods were or will be stored.
* **Location**: Choose all points where the goods were or will be transported.
* **Movement Date**: Enter the date of the goods movement.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date when the goods were delivered.
* **Shipment ID**: Enter the ID of the shipment (if available).
* **Goods Description**: Provide a brief description of the goods being moved.
* **Movement Quantity**: Enter the quantity of goods moved.
* **Goods Weight**: Enter the weight of the goods.
* **Goods Size**: Select all sizes that apply (e.g., "Yes" for a specific size).
* **Goods Color**: Select all colors that apply (e.g., "Yes" for a specific color).
* **Supplier**: Select all suppliers involved in the movement (e.g., "Yes" for the supplier).
* **Goods Status**: Choose the status of the goods (e.g., "Active" for currently in stock).
* **Remarks**: Enter any additional remarks regarding the goods movement.
* **Assigned To**: Choose the personnel assigned to the goods movement (if applicable).
