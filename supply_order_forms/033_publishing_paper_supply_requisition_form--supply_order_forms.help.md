# Publishing Paper Supply Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information on publishing paper supply requests from employees, allowing for accurate and efficient management of paper supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Employee Name` field with your name as it appears on your employee ID.
2. Select your department from the `Department` dropdown list.
3. Enter the `Location` of where you would like the paper supply to be delivered.
4. Choose the type of paper you need from the `Paper Type` dropdown list.
5. Enter the quantity of paper you require.
6. Enter the date you would like to order the paper supply (`Date Ordered`).
7. Enter the date you received the paper supply (`Date Received`).
8. If applicable, add any additional comments about the order in the `Comments` field.
9. Select the `Approver` who will be responsible for verifying the order (this will likely be an administrator or supervisor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_header**: This is a header section that provides information about the form and its purpose.
* **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your employee ID.
* **Department**: Select your department from the dropdown list to help us track supplies specific to your department.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you would like the paper supply to be delivered.
* **Paper Type**: Choose the type of paper you need from the list. This will help us ensure that we provide the correct type of paper for your order.
* **Quantity**: Enter the quantity of paper you require for your needs.
* **Date Ordered**: Enter the date you would like to order the paper supply.
* **Date Received**: Enter the date you received the paper supply. This will help us track the timing of your order.
* **Comments**: If there are any additional comments or special requests, please enter them here.
* **Approver**: Select the person who will be responsible for verifying the order, this will likely be an administrator or supervisor.
