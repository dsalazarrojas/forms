# Vineyard Barrels Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vineyard Barrels Requisition Form is used by the winery's operations team to request and manage wine deliveries. This form serves as a centralized platform for submitting and tracking barrel requests.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, refer to the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
- Review your completed form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: winery_request_form
*   **Winery Request Form** (`winery_request_form`, text, required): This field is a text input where you can describe the nature of the request, including any specific details or requirements for the wine delivery.
*   **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Enter the specific date you need the wine to be delivered on. Please use the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Enter the specific time you need the wine to be delivered on. Please use the 24-hour clock format `HH:MM`.
*   **Internal Approval** (`internal_approval`, note, required): Provide any additional notes or comments for the internal operations team to review and approve your request.
*   **Winery Manager Request** (`winery_manager_request`, email, required): Enter your email address for the winery manager or operations team to contact you with updates or questions regarding your request.

## Tips
- Be specific and accurate when filling out the form to ensure timely and effective delivery of the requested wine.
- If you need to cancel or modify a request, please contact the form maintainer at form@gic.mx.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out to the form maintainer for help.
