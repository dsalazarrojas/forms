# Supplier Credit Refill Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Supplier Credit Refill Order Form" is a tool for suppliers to request a credit refill. This form helps us track and process your credit refill requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the fields below. Note that some fields are not required, but providing them can help us process your request faster.
2. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Credit Refill Order** (`supplier_credit_refill_order_form`): Provide a brief description of your credit refill request.
* **Select One** (`select_one`): Choose 'Yes' if you are requesting a credit refill, 'No' otherwise.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`): Choose 'Yes' if you have any additional requirements or requests for your credit refill, 'No' otherwise.
* **Date** (`supplier_credit_refill_date`): Select the date you are requesting the credit refill.
* **Time** (`supplier_credit_refill_time`): Select the time you are available to discuss your credit refill request.
* **Note** (`supplier_credit_refill_note`): Provide any additional information or notes about your credit refill request.
* **Email** (`supplier_credit_refill_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your credit refill request.
* **Phone** (`supplier_credit_refill_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your credit refill request.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.

* We will review your submission and respond to you as soon as possible.
