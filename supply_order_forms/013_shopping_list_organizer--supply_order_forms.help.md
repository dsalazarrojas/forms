# Shopping List Organizer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shopping List Organizer form is designed to help you prepare for your next shopping trip. Fill out this form to gather all the necessary information for a successful and stress-free shopping experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the personal details: First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Enter the items you want to buy in the "Items to buy" field, separating each item with a comma.
3. Set a budget for your trip in the "Budget" field.
4. If you have any additional details to note about your trip, you can add them in the "Trip details" field.
5. Select the store where you plan to shop from the "Store Name" dropdown menu.
6. If you have multiple store addresses, select all of them in the "Store Address" field. Note that this field is optional.
7. Lastly, select the date and time for your trip in the "Date" and "Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Items to Buy** (`shopping_info_1`, required): A comma-separated list of items you want to buy. For example, "apples, bananas, milk, bread".
* **Budget** (`shopping_info_2`, required): Enter the budget for your trip.
* **Trip details** (`shopping_info_3`, optional): Any additional notes or details about your trip.
* **Email** (`general_info_6`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`general_info_7`, optional): This field is optional and not currently used. Please ignore this field.
* **Store Name** (`shopping_info_4`, required): Select the store where you plan to shop from the dropdown menu.
* **Store Address** (`shopping_info_5`, optional): If you have multiple store addresses, select all of them in this field.
* **Date** (`general_info_6`, required): Select the date for your trip.
* **Time** (`general_info_7`, required): Select the time for your trip.
