# Kitchen Ventilation Filter Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Ventilation Filter Order Form is designed to gather information for ordering kitchen ventilation filters. It is intended for use by individuals or businesses that require filters for their kitchen ventilation systems. This form should only be used for ordering purposes and not for other uses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Ensure that all relevant information is provided to ensure proper ordering and installation of the filters.
3. Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (Page 1):
	+ **General Information** (General Information, Text, Not required): This is a free-text field for any additional information you might need to provide about your order. Please use it to share any context or details that might be relevant to your order.
* **Filter Informations** (Page 2):
	+ **Filter Informations** (Filter Informations, Text, Required): Please provide detailed and accurate information about the type and quantity of filters you need. This field is crucial for ensuring that the correct filters are ordered for your kitchen ventilation system.
* **Order Summary** (Page 3):
	+ **Order Summary** (Order Summary, Text, Not required): This field is intended for you to provide a brief summary of your order, if you feel it is necessary. You can use it to confirm the details of your order or add any additional notes for our reference.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* We strive to provide the best possible service and support for your kitchen ventilation filter order.
