# Requisition Of Office Maintenance Supplies Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a request for office maintenance supplies. Fill out the form with the required information to ensure your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the date when you would like to submit the requisition.
2. Choose the items you would like to requisition by selecting all applicable options from the list.
3. Enter the quantity of each selected item.
4. Select the supplier for the requested items.
5. Provide any additional remarks or comments about the requisition.
6. Select the person who approved or rejected the requisition.
7. Enter the total cost of the requisition.
8. Select the status of the requisition (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
9. Enter the submitted date and time.
10. Select the person who submitted the requisition.
11. Select the person who updated the requisition status.
12. Enter the date and time the requisition was updated.
13. Enter the requisition number.
14. Select the category and subcategory of the items being requisitioned.
15. Enter a brief description of the item(s) being requisitioned.
16. Enter the supplier's address.
17. Provide any additional notes about the requisition.
18. Enter the date the requisition was approved.
19. Enter the reason for rejection (if applicable).
20. Enter the title of the person who approved the requisition.
21. Enter the form ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requisition Date** (1): Select the date when you would like to submit the requisition.
* **Item** (2): Choose the items you would like to requisition by selecting all applicable options from the list.
* **Quantity** (3): Enter the quantity of each selected item.
* **Unit Cost** (4): Enter the cost per unit of each selected item.
* **Total Cost** (5): Enter the total cost of all selected items.
* **Supplier** (6): Select the supplier for the requested items.
* **Remarks** (7): Provide any additional remarks or comments about the requisition.
* **Approved By** (8): Select the person who approved or rejected the requisition.
* **Requisitioner** (9): Select the person who submitted the requisition.
* **Status** (10): Select the status of the requisition (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Submitted Date** (11): Select the date and time you submitted the requisition.
* **Submitted By** (12): Select the person who submitted the requisition.
* **Updated By** (13): Select the person who updated the requisition status.
* **Updated Date** (14): Select the date and time the requisition was updated.
* **Requisition No** (15): Enter the requisition number.
* **Item Category** (16): Select the category of the items being requisitioned.
* **Item Subcategory** (17): Select the subcategory of the items being requisitioned.
* **Item Description** (18): Enter a brief description of the item(s) being requisitioned.
* **Supplier Address** (19): Enter the supplier's address.
* **Notes** (20): Provide any additional notes about the requisition.
* **Approved Date** (21): Select the date the requisition was approved.
* **Rejected Reason** (22): Enter the reason for rejection (if applicable).
* **Approved By Title** (23): Enter the title of the person who approved the requisition.
* **Form ID** (24): Enter the form ID (if applicable).
