# food_supply_cold_storage_audit_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record requests for food supply chain cold storage audits. It is used to gather information about the storage condition, storage status, and audit request details to maintain accurate inventory and ensure the safety of stored food items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Audit Request" section with the details of the audit request, if necessary.
2. Provide the storage location in the "Storage Location" field.
3. Enter the storage capacity and storage temperature in the respective fields.
4. Select the storage status and conditions from the options provided in the "Storage Status" and "Storage Condition" fields.
5. Choose the audit frequency and date and time of the audit from the "Audit Frequency", "Audit Date", and "Audit Time" fields.
6. Add any additional notes about the audit in the "Audit Note" field.
7. If you are not the submitter, fill in their details in the "Submitter" and their contact information fields.
8. Finally, select the audit by person's details in the "Audit By" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Audit Request
* **Audit Request** (`audit_request`, text, optional): Enter the details of the audit request, if necessary.
### Storage Location
* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, text, optional): Enter the storage location where the audit is requested.
### Storage Capacity
* **Storage Capacity** (`storage_capacity`, number, optional): Enter the storage capacity in a numerical value.
### Storage Temperature
* **Storage Temperature** (`storage_temp`, number, optional): Enter the storage temperature in a numerical value.
### Storage Condition
* **Storage Condition** (`storage_condition`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the storage conditions that apply. 
*   Options: Active, Inactive, etc.
### Audit Frequency
* **Audit Frequency** (`audit_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the audit frequency. 
*   Options: Yes, No, etc.
### Audit Date
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the audit.
### Audit Time
* **Audit Time** (`audit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the audit.
### Storage Status
* **Storage Status** (`storage_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the storage status.
