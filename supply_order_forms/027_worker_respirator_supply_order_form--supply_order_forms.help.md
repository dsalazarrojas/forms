# Worker Respirator Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Respirator Supply Order Form is used to collect information for a worker respirator supply order. This form is intended for workers who need to order respirators for their work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your respirator request.
2. Select the type of respirator you need.
3. Enter the order quantity.
4. Select the shipping information.
5. Enter your contact details.
6. Add any order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respirator Request** (`respirator_request`, text, required): Enter the type of respirator you need. This will help us understand your requirements.
* **Respirator Type** (`respirator_type`, select_one, optional): If you select a type, please be specific about which model you need.
* **Order Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of respirators you need to order.
* **Ship To** (`ship_to`, select_multiple, optional): Select the warehouse where you want to receive the respirators.
* **Ship Date** (`ship_date`, date, required): Enter the expected ship date for the respirators.
* **Ship Time** (`ship_time`, time, required): Enter the expected ship time for the respirators.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter your name or contact person responsible for the order.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your order.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your order.
* **Respirator Price** (`respirator_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the respirators. This will be calculated automatically.
* **Respirator Cost** (`respirator_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the respirators. This will be calculated automatically.
* **Total Price** (`respirator_total`, number, required): The total price of the order will be calculated automatically.
* **Ship Address** (`ship_address`, text, optional): Enter the shipping address for the respirators.
* **Ship City** (`ship_city`, text, optional): Enter the city for the shipping address.
* **Ship State** (`ship_state`, text, optional): Enter the state for the shipping address.
* **Ship Zip** (`ship_zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code for the shipping address.
* **Status** (`respirator_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of your order.
* **Order Number** (`order_number`, text, optional): Enter the order number you receive from us.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter your customer ID.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your customer name.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your customer address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`, text, optional): Enter your customer city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`, text, optional): Enter your customer state.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled out.
* If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank and we will contact you for clarification.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us at [support email or phone number].
