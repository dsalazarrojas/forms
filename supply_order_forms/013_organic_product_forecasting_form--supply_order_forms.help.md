# Organic Product Forecasting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organic Product Forecasting Form is used by sales and purchasing teams to forecast and prepare for upcoming organic product orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the Product Name**: Enter the name of the organic product being ordered.
2. **Choose the Product Type**: Select whether the product is 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate its type.
3. **Enter the Quantity**: Enter the total quantity of the product being ordered.
4. **Enter the Unit Price**: Enter the price of the product per unit.
5. **Set the Production Date**: Enter the date on which the product is to be produced.
6. **Set the Delivery Date**: Enter the date on which the product is to be delivered.
7. **Optional: Set the Delivery Window**: If you need to specify a range of dates for delivery, enter the start and end dates of the delivery window.
8. **Add any Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the organic product being ordered.
* **Product Type** (select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the product is a type that requires special handling or has specific requirements.
* **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the total quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Unit Price** (number, required): Enter the price of the product per unit.
* **Production Date** (date, required): Enter the date on which the product is to be produced.
* **Delivery Date** (date, required): Enter the date on which the product is to be delivered.
* **Delivery Window** (date, optional): If you need to specify a range of dates for delivery, enter the start and end dates of the delivery window.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the order.
