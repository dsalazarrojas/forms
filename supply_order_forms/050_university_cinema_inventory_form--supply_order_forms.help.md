# University Cinema Inventory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Cinema Inventory Form is used to update the university cinema's inventory data, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information is available for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form for each item you need to add or update in the inventory.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **University Cinema Inventory Form** (`title`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is already pre-filled and serves as a heading.
* **form_page_1_label** (`form_page_1`, text, required/optional): This page is for general information about the item being inventoried. Enter a brief description of the item.
* **form_page_2** (`form_page_2`, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the item currently in inventory.
* **form_submit_label** (`form_submit`, text, required/optional): This is the final page where you submit the form. Please enter a brief confirmation message to ensure you've reviewed and submitted the form correctly.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, contact your supervisor for clarification.
* If you need to make multiple submissions, ensure you fill out the form completely for each item before proceeding.
* Save a copy of the submitted form for your records, if needed.
