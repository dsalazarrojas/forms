# Clone Of Nail Wishlist Southern Beil Nails - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a personalized nail wishlist from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Requester name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email used for notifications" field. This will ensure you receive updates and notifications regarding your order.
3. Provide the name or code of the nail product you're interested in.
4. Enter the desired color or design for your nails.
5. Enter the quantity of nails you wish to order.
6. Select your preferred delivery method for the order.
7. If you have a specific budget per unit, enter it in the "Optional price limit per item" field.
8. Add any special requests or notes for the stylist in the "Special requests" field.
9. Decide if you'd like to receive product updates when they become available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name** (Text, required): Enter your full name to be used for order communication.
* **Email used for notifications** (Email, required): Enter your email address where you'll receive order updates and notifications.
* **Product name or code** (Text, required): Enter the name or code of the nail product you wish to order.
* **Desired color or design** (Text, optional): Enter the color or design you'd like to have on your nails.
* **Quantity desired** (Number, required): Enter the number of nail units you'd like to order.
* **Preferred delivery method** (Select One, optional): Choose between Standard, Express, or Pickup as your delivery preference.
* **Optional price limit per item** (Number, optional): Enter a budget limit for each nail unit, if applicable.
* **Special requests** (Text, optional): Provide any special notes or requests for the stylist.
* **Subscribe for product updates** (Select One, optional): Decide if you'd like to receive updates when the product becomes available.
