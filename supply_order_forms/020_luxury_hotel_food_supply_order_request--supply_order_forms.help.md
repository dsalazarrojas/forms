# Luxury Hotel Food Supply Order Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Hotel Food Supply Order Request form is used by hotel staff to request food supplies and other materials from the purchasing team. This form helps to ensure that supplies are properly ordered, and that the hotel's purchasing team is aware of the requested items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the order from the provided options.
2. Enter a brief description of the order.
3. Choose the delivery preference for the order (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4. Select the date and time for the order.
5. Select who the order is requested by (Hotel Purchasing Team, Hotel Staff, or Supplier).
6. Select who the order is requested to (Hotel or Supplier).
7. Enter any additional supply details if necessary.
8. Enter any order notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Supply Order Forms`, required: false): Select the category for the order, such as food, cleaning supplies, or other materials.
* **Description** (`Description`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the order, such as "Fresh fruit and vegetables" or "Bottled water".
* **Delivery Preference** (`Delivery Preference`, required: false): Choose when you would like to receive the order, such as Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Order Date** (`Order Date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Order Time** (`Order Time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to receive the order.
* **Requested by** (`Requested by`, required: false): Select who is requesting the order, such as Hotel Purchasing Team, Hotel Staff, or Supplier.
* **Requested to** (`Requested to`, required: false): Select who the order is requested to, such as Hotel or Supplier.
* **Supply Details** (`Supply Details`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the supply, such as quantity or brand.
* **Order Notes** (`Order Notes`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the order, such as special instructions or preferences.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the purchasing team.
