# Sports Facility Hand Sanitizer Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Facility Hand Sanitizer Supply Order Form is used to collect information for hand sanitizer supply orders from sports facilities. It captures relevant details about the order, such as gym name, delivery date, quantity, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the sports facility from which you are placing the order.
2. Enter the name of the facility or gym where you will be delivering the hand sanitizers.
3. Specify the date you would like to receive the delivery (if known).
4. Enter the quantity of hand sanitizers you would like to order.
5. Provide any additional comments or information about the order.
6. Choose the type of hand sanitizer you would like to order.
7. Select the size of the hand sanitizer you would like to order.
8. Enter any additional comments or information about the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gym Name** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_order_form_gym_name`): Enter the name of the facility or gym where you will be delivering the hand sanitizers.
* **Delivery Date** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_order_form_delivery_date`): Specify the date you would like to receive the delivery (if known).
* **Quantity** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_order_form_quantity`): Enter the quantity of hand sanitizers you would like to order.
* **Shipping Information** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_order_form_shipping_info`): Provide any additional comments or information about the delivery.
* **Sanitizer Type** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_form_sanitizer_type`): Choose the type of hand sanitizer you would like to order.
* **Sanitizer Size** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_form_sanitizer_size`): Select the size of the hand sanitizer you would like to order.
* **Delivery Address** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_form_delivery_address`): Enter the address where you would like to have the hand sanitizers delivered.
* **Additional Comments** (`sports_facility_hand_sanitizer_supply_form_additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or information about the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure proper delivery and ordering.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your internal contact person for clarification.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please specify them in the "Additional Comments" field.
