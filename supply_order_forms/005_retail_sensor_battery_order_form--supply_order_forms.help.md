# Retail Sensor Battery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Sensor Battery Order Form is designed to collect information from retailers who need to place orders for sensor batteries. This form helps streamline the ordering process and ensures that all necessary information is collected accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have the required information ready. This form requires some basic contact and shipping information from you as the retailer.
2.  Select your sensor battery type from the "Sensor Battery Type" dropdown list. Choose from the options provided.
3.  Enter the quantity of sensor batteries you require.
4.  Choose your shipping method from the "Shipping Method" dropdown list.
5.  Enter your shipping address and phone number.
6.  Add any additional order comments if needed.
7.  Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Retailer Information
*  **Retailer Information** (`retail_sensor_battery_order_form_1`, text, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you will enter your store's information, including its name and contact details.

### Shipping Details
*  **Shipping Details** (`retail_sensor_battery_order_form_2`, text, required: false): This is the second section of the form where you will enter your shipping details, including address and phone number.

### Sensor Battery Type
*  **Sensor Battery Type** (`sensor_battery_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the type of sensor battery you need from the dropdown list.

### Quantity
*  **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the number of sensor batteries you require.

### Shipping Method
*  **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you want your sensor batteries shipped, such as "Yes" for yes, "No" for no, or other options may be available.

### Shipping Address
*  **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter your shipping address where the sensor batteries will be sent.

### Shipping Phone
*  **Shipping Phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number associated with your shipping address.

### Order Comments
*  **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, text, required: false): If there's anything else you'd like to add or comment on your order, enter it here. This can include special instructions or requests.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, and double-check your information before submitting the form. If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to contact us.
