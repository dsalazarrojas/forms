# fitness_studio_floor_repair_material_order_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used by fitness studios to order materials needed for floor repairs. It is intended to collect important information about the gym's details, delivery and shipping details, and payment information. Please make sure this form is filled out correctly to avoid any errors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the "gym_info" page, which is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your gym.
2. Next, move on to the "delivery_details" page, which is also a text field for any delivery-related information.
3. Then, fill out the "shipping_details" page, another text field for shipping information.
4. Finally, complete the "payment_details" page, which is another text field for payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **gym_info** (`gym_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about your gym.
* **delivery_details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Here, you can provide any delivery-related information.
* **shipping_details** (`shipping_details`, text, optional): Enter your shipping information in this field.
* **payment_details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Use this field to provide your payment information.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all fields that apply to your specific situation.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at `form@gic.mx` for assistance.
