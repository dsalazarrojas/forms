# Eco Friendly Bag Restock Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Friendly Bag Restock Order Form is used to request a restock of eco-friendly bags from a supplier. This form is meant for customers who want to reorder their bags on a regular basis. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your customer information (Name, Phone, and Email) in the **Customer Information** section.
2.  Enter your shipping information in the **Shipping Information** section.
3.  Provide details about your order in the **Order Details** section.
4.  Choose the type of order you are placing in the **Order Type** section.
5.  Enter the quantity of bags you are ordering.
6.  Choose the frequency of your order (e.g., how often you want to receive the bags).
7.  If applicable, select a specific date for your order to be fulfilled.
8.  Add any additional notes about your order in the **Reorder Note** section.
9.  Enter your email address and phone number so the supplier can contact you about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information**: This section is where you enter your name and other identifying information. This will help the supplier identify you and fulfill your order.
*   **Shipping Information**: In this section, you will enter the details about how you want to receive your order (e.g., address, shipping method).
*   **Order Details**: Describe the specific bags you want to order. If you are unsure about what bags are available, contact the supplier for more information.
*   **Order Type**: Choose the type of order you are placing (e.g., new order, reorder, replacement). 
*   **Quantity Ordered**: Enter the number of bags you want to order.
*   **Order Frequency**: Choose the frequency at which you would like to receive your order (e.g., every month, every 2 months).
*   **Reorder Interval**: Enter the time interval between each order (e.g., 1 month, 2 months).
*   **Reorder Date**: Choose a specific date you would like to receive your order.
*   **Reorder Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your order.
*   **Customer Email**: Enter your email address so the supplier can contact you about your order.
*   **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number in case the supplier needs to reach you about your order.
*   **Supplier Email**: This field is used to enter the supplier's email address, though this might not be necessary if the supplier is contacting you directly.
*   **Supplier Phone**: You might not need to enter the supplier's phone number as they will likely contact you directly.
 
## Tips

*   Please ensure that you are providing accurate information, especially if you have previously ordered from the supplier before.
*   If you are unsure about any field, contact the supplier for clarification.
*   Be sure to enter your email address and phone number so the supplier can contact you about your order.
*   Check your order details carefully to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.
