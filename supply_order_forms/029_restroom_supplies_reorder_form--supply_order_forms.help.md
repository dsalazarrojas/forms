# Restroom Supplies Reorder Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restroom Supplies Reorder Form is used to collect information about restroom supplies reorders. Please use this form to reorder restroom supplies for a specific location, and provide your delivery preference and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the location where the supplies are needed.
2. Choose your delivery preference (same day, next business day, or unspecified).
3. Enter the quantity of supplies needed.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments.
5. Enter your name, as the staff member reordering the supplies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location where the supplies are needed.
- **Delivery Preference** (`delivery_preference`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you want the supplies to be delivered (same day, next business day, or no preference).
- **Quantity** (`quantity`, `number`, required: false): Enter the amount of supplies needed.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the reordering.
- **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the staff member reordering the supplies.
