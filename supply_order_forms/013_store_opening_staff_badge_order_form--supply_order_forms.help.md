# Store Opening Staff Badge Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Store Opening Staff Badge Order Form is a document used to place an order for store opening staff badges. This form is used to collect information about the staff member who will be receiving the badge, including their details, shipping and billing information, payment method, and badge preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the staff member's details, including their name, email, phone number, and employee ID.
2. Select the roles the staff member will be assigned to.
3. Choose the payment method for the order (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
4. Enter the shipping and billing information, including street, city, state, and zip code.
5. Select the badge type and color.
6. Specify the quantity of badges needed.
7. Enter the order date and time.
8. Add any notes about the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Details**: This field is used to enter the staff member's details, including their name, email, phone number, and employee ID.
* **Shipping Info**: This field is used to enter the shipping address of the staff member, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Roles**: Select the roles the staff member will be assigned to (Manager, Owner, or Staff).
* **Payments**: This field is used to enter any payment information for the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method for the order (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Info**: This field is used to enter any additional payment information.
* **Shipping Street**: Enter the street address of the shipping address.
* **Shipping City**: Enter the city of the shipping address.
* **Shipping Zip**: Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **Shipping State**: Enter the state of the shipping address.
* **Billing Street**: Enter the street address of the billing address.
* **Billing City**: Enter the city of the billing address.
* **Billing Zip**: Enter the zip code of the billing address.
* **Billing State**: Enter the state of the billing address.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the staff member.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the staff member.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the staff member.
* **Employee ID**: Enter the ID of the staff member.
* **Role**: Enter the role of the staff member.
* **Badge Type**: Enter the type of badge to be ordered (e.g., "Staff Badge").
* **Badge Color**: Enter the color of the badge to be ordered.
* **Badge Quantity**: Enter the number of badges to be ordered.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order.
* **Order Time**: Enter the time of the order.
* **Notes**: Add any notes about the order.

Note: This form is for ordering staff badges for store opening purposes. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to avoid delays in processing the order.
