# Shelf Label Adhesive Refill Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for suppliers to submit their refill orders for adhesive labels. The form helps suppliers to provide necessary information for order fulfillment, including supplier details, order details, shipping information, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Supplier Information section** with your business name and contact details.
2. **Enter your order details** in the Order Details section, including the quantity of adhesive labels you need.
3. **Enter your shipping information** in the Shipping Information section, including your address and any additional notes for the shipment.
4. **Add your payment information** in the Payment Information section, including payment method and any relevant payment references.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Information (Page 1)**: This section is used to identify your business and contact information. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Supplier Name**: Enter your business name as it appears on your invoices or marketing materials.
	+ **Contact Name**: Enter your name and title (e.g., "Jane Doe, Sales Manager").
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter your main office phone number.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter your main office email address.
* **Order Details (Page 1)**: This section is used to provide details about your adhesive label order. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Order Quantity**: Enter the number of adhesive labels you need.
* **Shipping Information (Page 1)**: This section is used to provide shipping details for your order. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Ship to Address**: Enter your shipping address for the adhesive labels.
	+ **Additional Notes**: Enter any special requests or instructions for the shipment.
* **Payment Information (Page 1)**: This section is used to provide payment details for your order. Please fill in the following:
	+ **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you will use (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
	+ **Payment Reference**: Enter any relevant payment references or invoices related to this order.
