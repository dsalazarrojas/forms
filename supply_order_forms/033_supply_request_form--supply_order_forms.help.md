# Supply Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supply Request Form is designed for customers to submit their supply requests, providing essential information for processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter a title for your supply request.
2.  Provide information about the customer in the corresponding fields.
3.  Enter the requested supply date and delivery date.
4.  Enter the detailed description of the supply required.
5.  Choose the unit of measurement for the supply.
6.  Enter the quantity of the supply needed.
7.  Enter the price of the supply.
8.  Sign the form to confirm the request is from the customer.
9.  Select the manager assigned to handle the supply request.
10. Confirm the manager's approval status.
11.  Enter any additional comments or comments from the manager.
12.  Select the current status of the supply.
13.  Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.
14.  Enter any comments or feedback from the assigned manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Supply Request Form (`supply_request_form`)**: Enter a title for your supply request.
*   **Customer Information (`customer_info`)**: Enter the customer information in this field.
*   **Customer Name (`customer_name`)**: Enter the customer's name.
*   **Customer Email (`customer_email`)**: Enter the customer's email address.
*   **Customer Phone (`customer_phone`)**: Enter the customer's phone number.
*   **Customer Order Date (`customer_order_date`)**: Enter the date the customer would like to receive the supply.
*   **Customer Delivery Date (`customer_delivery_date`)**: Enter the date the supply will be delivered.
*   **Supply Details (`supply_details`)**: Enter the detailed description of the supply required.
*   **Supply Description (`supply_description`)**: Enter any additional details about the supply.
*   **Supply Unit (`supply_unit`)**: Select the unit of measurement for the supply (e.g., "unit" or "Other").
*   **Supply Quantity (`supply_quantity`)**: Enter the quantity of the supply needed.
*   **Supply Price (`supply_price`)**: Enter the price of the supply.
*   **Customer Signature (`customer_signature`)**: Sign the form to confirm the request is from the customer.
*   **Assign Supply Manager (`assign_supply_manager`)**: Select the manager assigned to handle the supply request.
*   **Manager Confirmation (`manager_confirmation`)**: Confirm the manager's approval status.
*   **Manager Comments (`assign_manager_comments`)**: Enter any comments or feedback from the manager.
*   **Supply Status (`supply_status`)**: Select the current status of the supply.
*   **Customer Comments (`customer_comments`)**: Enter any comments or feedback from the customer.
*   **Assign Manager Comments (`assign_manager_comments`)**: Enter any comments or feedback from the manager.
*   **Customer Approval (`customer_approval`)**: Confirm the customer's approval status.
