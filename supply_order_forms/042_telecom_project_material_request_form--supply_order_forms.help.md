# telecom_project_material_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `telecom_project_material_request_form` is used to request materials for a specific telecom project. This form is used to gather information about the project ID, project name, materials requested, type of material, quantity, unit price, total cost, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project ID of the telecom project you are requesting materials for.
2. Enter the name of the project you are requesting materials for.
3. Describe the material(s) you are requesting.
4. Select the type of material you are requesting.
5. Enter the quantity of material you are requesting.
6. Enter the unit price of the material.
7. Enter the total cost of the material.
8. Provide a brief description of the material.
9. Enter any additional supplier information.
10. Enter the name of the contact person for the supplier.
11. Enter the email address of the contact person.
12. Enter the phone number of the contact person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **project_id** (1, number, required): Enter the project ID of the telecom project you are requesting materials for.
* **project_name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the project you are requesting materials for.
* **material_requested** (3, text, required): Describe the material(s) you are requesting.
* **material_type** (4, select_one, required): Select the type of material you are requesting.
	+ 'Yes' or 'No'
* **quantity** (5, number, required): Enter the quantity of material you are requesting.
* **unit_price** (6, number, required): Enter the unit price of the material.
* **total_cost** (7, number, required): Enter the total cost of the material.
* **description** (8, text, required): Provide a brief description of the material.
* **material_description** (9, text, required): Enter any additional details about the material.
* **supplier_info** (10, text, required): Enter any additional supplier information.
* **contact_person** (11, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for the supplier.
* **contact_email** (12, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone** (13, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
