# Reusable Carton Program Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reusable Carton Program Order Form is used by customers to place orders for custom cartons. This form collects information about the customer, their contact details, order details, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name of the customer.
2. Fill in the first name and last name of the customer.
3. Enter the customer's email address.
4. Fill in the customer's phone number.
5. Add any additional contact notes for the customer.
6. Enter the company address.
7. Fill in the city of the customer.
8. Select the state from the list.
9. Enter the customer's zip code.
10. Fill in the quantity of cartons ordered.
11. Enter the unit price of the cartons.
12. Calculate the subtotal.
13. Enter the tax amount.
14. Calculate the total.
15. Select the delivery date.
16. Select the delivery time.
17. Add any delivery notes for the customer.
18. Select the payment method (credit card, check, PayPal).
19. Fill in the payment terms.
20. Add any customer notes.
21. Enter the order date.
22. Add any order notes.
23. Fill in the sales representative's name.
24. Select the assigned tool.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Additional Contact Notes** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Add any additional contact notes for the customer.
* **Company Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the company address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of the customer.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select the state from the list.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Fill in the quantity of cartons ordered.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required): Enter the unit price of the cartons.
* **Subtotal** (`subtotal`, number, required): Calculate the subtotal based on quantity and unit price.
* **Tax** (`tax`, number, required): Calculate the tax amount.
* **Total** (`total`, number, required): Calculate the total based on subtotal and tax.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, required): Select the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, required): Select the delivery time.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, note, optional): Add any delivery notes for the customer.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method (credit card, check, PayPal).
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Fill in the payment terms.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, text, optional): Add any customer notes.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the order date.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, note, optional): Add any order notes.
* **Sales Rep** (`sales_rep`, text, optional): Fill in the sales representative's name.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Select the assigned tool.
