# Retail Safety Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to place orders for retail safety equipment. It is intended for internal use within the company, and it's used by customers to provide their information and preferences for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of customer you are (Company or Individual).
2. Select your delivery preference (Standard, Expedited, or Express).
3. Enter your purchasing details (this field is for any additional comments or information about the order).
4. Add any additional notes about the order.
5. Provide your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your address.
8. Select the date and time you would like to place the order.
9. Enter the name of the user who will be handling the order.
10. Enter the name of the tool or equipment being ordered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info** (required): Choose between Company and Individual to indicate who the customer is.
* **Delivery Preferences** (required): Select from Standard, Expedited, or Express to choose how you would like to receive your order.
* **Purchasing Details** (required): Enter any additional comments or information about the order.
* **Notes** (required): Add any additional notes or comments about the order.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Address** (required): Enter your address where your order will be shipped to.
* **Order Date** (required): Select the date you would like to place the order.
* **Order Time** (required): Select the time you would like to place the order.
* **Assigned User** (required): Enter the name of the user who will be handling your order.
* **Assigned Tool** (required): Enter the name of the tool or equipment being ordered.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your order is processed correctly.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, please add them in the "Purchasing Details" field.
* If you would like to track your order, please make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly.
