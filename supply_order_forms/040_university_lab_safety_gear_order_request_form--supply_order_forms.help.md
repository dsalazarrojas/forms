# University Lab Safety Gear Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to request lab safety gear from the university. It is intended for staff, faculty, and students to submit requests for necessary equipment and supplies. Please use this form to ensure accurate and timely delivery of lab safety gear.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your department from the list in the "User Department" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
3. Choose the correct lab location for your request in the "Lab Location" field.
4. In the "Lab Supplies Needed" field, provide a detailed list of the equipment or supplies you need.
5. Select who requested the gear in the "Requested By" field.
6. If needed, enter a specific date and time for delivery in the "Date Needed" and "Time Needed" fields.
7. Select the users who should receive the gear in the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Department** (`user_department`, text, required): Select your department from the list to ensure accurate delivery of lab safety gear.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates on your order.
* **Lab Location** (`lab_location`, select_one, required): Choose the correct lab location for your request.
* **Lab Supplies Needed** (`lab_supplies_needed`, text, required): Provide a detailed list of the lab safety gear you need.
* **Requested By** (`requested_by`, select_multiple, required): Select who requested the gear (Staff, Faculty, or Student).
* **Date Needed** (`date_needed`, date, optional): If you have a specific date you need the gear by, enter it here.
* **Time Needed** (`time_needed`, time, optional): If you have a specific time you need the gear by, enter it here.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your request.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Select who submitted the form (John, Jane, or Joe).
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, required): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, required): Select who created the form (System, Staff, or User).
* **Created On** (`created_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was created.
