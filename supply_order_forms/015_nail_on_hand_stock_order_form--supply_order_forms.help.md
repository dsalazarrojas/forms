# Nail On Hand Stock Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for stock orders from customers. It gathers details about the customer, delivery, order, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section.
2. Provide the delivery details.
3. Enter the order information.
4. Add any additional details about the order.
5. Enter payment information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, optional):
  Enter the customer's name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information. This will help us process the order efficiently.
- **Delivery Info** (`delivery_info`, text, optional):
  Enter the delivery address, contact person's name, and any special instructions for delivery. This will help us ensure correct delivery of the ordered stock.
- **Order Info** (`order_info`, text, optional):
  Enter the product or service ordered, quantity, and any other relevant details. Be as specific as possible to help us fulfill your order.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional):
  Add any additional information about the order, such as special requests or comments.
- **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, optional):
  Enter the payment method and any relevant payment details. This will help us process the payment correctly.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields for accurate processing.
- Double-check all entered information for correctness.
- If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
