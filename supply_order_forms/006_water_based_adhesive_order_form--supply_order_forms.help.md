# Water Based Adhesive Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Water Based Adhesive Order Form is designed to collect information necessary for processing orders of water-based adhesives from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide your details as the customer, including your contact name, phone number and email address.
2. Select the type of adhesive you would like to order.
3. Enter the quantity of adhesive you would like to order.
4. Ensure that the total price is calculated correctly based on the quantity and unit price.
5. Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
6. Enter any additional notes or instructions related to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Water Based Adhesive Order** (text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the order, including any specific details about the adhesive or the order requirements.
* **Customer Information** (text, required): Enter your name, company name, and contact details.
* **Delivery Details** (text, required/optional): Enter any specific delivery instructions, such as a delivery address or any special handling requirements.
* **Adhesive Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of adhesive you are ordering from the options provided.
* **Quantity Ordered** (number, required): Enter the quantity of adhesive you would like to order.
* **Unit Price** (number, required): Enter the unit price of the adhesive.
* **Total Price** (number, required): The total price will be automatically calculated based on the quantity and unit price.
* **Order Date** (date, required): Enter the date you would like to receive the order.
* **Contact Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the contact person for the order.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your company address.
* **Notes** (note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or instructions related to the order.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please seek clarification from the relevant personnel before submitting the form.
