# Stock Transfer Coordination Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stock Transfer Coordination Request form is used to request stock transfers between warehouses. It captures essential information about the transfer, such as the stock item, quantity, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Stock Transfer Date.
2. Enter the Stock Transfer Location From.
3. Enter the Stock Transfer Location To.
4. Enter the Stock Item Transfer ID.
5. Enter the Stock Transfer Quantity.
6. Select the Stock Transfer Status.
7. Enter Stock Transfer Notes (optional).
8. Enter the Stock Transfer Requester's Name.
9. Enter the Stock Transfer Requester's Email.
10. Enter the Stock Transfer Requester's Phone Number.
11. Enter the Stock Transfer Warehouse Teams.
12. Enter the Stock Transfer Warehouse Teams Email.
13. Enter the Stock Transfer Warehouse Teams Phone Number.
14. Enter the Stock Transfer Warehouse.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Stock Transfer Date**: Enter the date when the stock transfer should take place.
- **Stock Transfer Location From**: Enter the name of the warehouse where the stock item is currently located.
- **Stock Transfer Location To**: Enter the name of the warehouse where the stock item will be transferred to.
- **Stock Item Transfer ID**: Enter the ID number of the stock item to be transferred.
- **Stock Transfer Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the stock item to be transferred.
- **Stock Transfer Status**: Select the status of the stock transfer (e.g., "Pending", "In Progress", or "Completed").
- **Stock Transfer Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the stock transfer.
- **Stock Transfer Requester**: Enter the name of the person requesting the stock transfer.
- **Stock Transfer Requester Email**: Enter the email address of the person requesting the stock transfer.
- **Stock Transfer Requester Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person requesting the stock transfer.
- **Stock Transfer Warehouse Teams**: Enter the name of the warehouse team responsible for the stock transfer.
- **Stock Transfer Warehouse Teams Email**: Enter the email address of the warehouse team.
- **Stock Transfer Warehouse Teams Phone**: Enter the phone number of the warehouse team.
- **Stock Transfer Warehouse**: Enter the name of the warehouse where the stock item will be stored after transfer.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form.
