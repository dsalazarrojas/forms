# Material Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Material Order Form is used to collect information related to material orders. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details for your material order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Material Type, Quantity, and Unit Cost.
2. Fill out any other fields that you feel are relevant to your order, but note that they are not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you regarding your order.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number that can be reached at for order updates.
* **Material Type** (Select One, Required): Choose the type of material you are ordering.
* **Quantity** (Number, Required): Enter the quantity of the material you are ordering.
* **Unit Cost** (Number, Optional): Enter the unit cost of the material you are ordering.
* **Total Cost** (Number, Optional): This field is calculated automatically based on the quantity and unit cost. You do not need to fill this out.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
* **Contractor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the contractor or person placing the order.
* **Contractor Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the contact information for the contractor or person placing the order.
* **Contractor Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number for the contractor or person placing the order.
* **Contractor Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address for the contractor or person placing the order.
* **Job Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where the material will be used.
* **Job Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the job or project that the material will be used for.
* **Date Ordered** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the order was placed.
* **Time Ordered** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the order was placed.
* **Date Received** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the material was received.
* **Time Received** (Time, Optional): Enter the time the material was received.
* **Material Ordered** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the material that was ordered.
* **Supplier Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the supplier of the material.
* **Supplier Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number for the supplier.
* **Supplier Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address for the supplier.
