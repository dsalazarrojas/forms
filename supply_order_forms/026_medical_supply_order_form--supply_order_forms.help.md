# Medical Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Supply Order Form is used to order medical supplies from suppliers. It captures essential information about the patient, order details, and payment method. This form ensures accurate and efficient communication with suppliers, facilitating a smooth order fulfillment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Medical Supply Order Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Provide the patient's address in the **patient_address** field.
3. Select the order date in the **order_date** field.
4. Choose the type of medical supplies from the **supply_type** field (multiple selections are allowed).
5. Enter the quantity of the ordered supplies in the **quantity_ordered** field.
6. Provide your contact information for the supplier in the **supplier_contact** field.
7. Enter the supplier's name in the **supplier_name** field.
8. Enter any additional order details in the **order_details** field.
9. Select the payment method from the **payment_method** field.
10. Enter the delivery date and time in the **delivery_date** and **delivery_time** fields, respectively.
11. Enter the delivery address in the **delivery_address** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **supply_order_form_1**: This is the title of the form, which you should not fill out as it is a heading.
* **patient_name**: Enter the name of the patient for whom the medical supplies are being ordered.
* **patient_address**: Provide the patient's address where the supplies will be delivered.
* **order_date**: Select the date when the order was placed.
* **supply_type**: Choose the type of medical supplies (e.g., gloves, bandages, etc.) being ordered. Select multiple options as needed.
* **quantity_ordered**: Enter the quantity of each item ordered.
* **supplier_contact**: Enter your contact information for the supplier, including phone number and other relevant details.
* **supplier_name**: Enter the supplier's name.
* **order_details**: Enter any additional details or instructions for the supplier.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method used for the order (e.g., cash, credit card, etc.).
* **delivery_date**: Enter the date when the supplies are expected to be delivered.
* **delivery_time**: Enter the time when the supplies are expected to be delivered.
* **delivery_address**: Enter the address where the supplies will be delivered.
* **signature**: This field is not required for the form but is for recording the patient's or supplier's signature for verification purposes.

Note: This form is designed for internal use and is not intended for external users. Please ensure you have permission to access and use this form.
