# Farm Harvest Equipment Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect order and contact information from customers interested in purchasing farm harvest equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section, including name, address, email, phone number, and any additional customer notes.
2. Provide details about the order, including equipment type, delivery schedule, and order notes.
3. Select a delivery method (in-person, delivery, or pickup).
4. Enter the delivery address and date.
5. Confirm the order status and any additional information.
