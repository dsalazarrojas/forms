# Concession Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Concession Supply Order Form is designed to collect essential details for ordering 
supplies. Please fill out this form with accurate and relevant information to 
ensure timely and proper supply delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, as indicated by the required fields.
- Ensure that each field is correctly filled in accordance with your needs and 
organization's requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Requester name** (requester_name)
* **Type**: text field
* **Required**: true
* This field is for the contact person's name for the order.

### 2. **Department or vendor** (department)
* **Type**: text field
* **Required**: false
* This is where you specify the department or vendor that will receive the supplies.

### 3. **Contact phone** (contact_phone)
* **Type**: text field
* **Required**: true
* Enter the phone number that the requester can be contacted on for order 
coordinating purposes.

### 4. **Items requested** (item_list)
* **Type**: text field
* **Required**: true
* List the specific items and quantities needed for the order.

### 5. **Preferred delivery date** (delivery_date)
* **Type**: date field
* **Required**: true
* Choose the date you would like the supplies delivered by.

### 6. **Delivery location** (delivery_location)
* **Type**: text field
* **Required**: true
* Specify the exact building and room or dock where the supplies will be 
received.

### 7. **Special instructions** (special_instructions)
* **Type**: text field
* **Required**: false
* Provide any special instructions or handling requirements for the supplies.

### 8. **Budget code** (budget_code)
* **Type**: text field
* **Required**: false
* Enter the budget code to charge the order to.

### 9. **Approval status** (approval_status)
* **Type**: select_one field
* **Required**: false
* This field shows the current status of the order for internal use.

### 10. **Order summary** (order_summary)
* **Type**: note field
* **Required**: false
* This is a read-only summary of the submitted order.
