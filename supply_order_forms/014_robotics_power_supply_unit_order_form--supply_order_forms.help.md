# Robotics Power Supply Unit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers to process a request for a robotics power supply unit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields. The form is divided into several sections:
	* Customer Details: This section includes fields for customer name, shipping info, and contact information.
	* Shipping Details: This section includes fields for shipping address, city, and country.
	* Order Details: This section includes fields for PSU type, quantity, and delivery date and time.
	* Payment and Terms: This section includes fields for payment terms and shipping terms.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

* **Customer Details** (`1`: Customer Details)

		- Enter the customer's name.
	+ **Shipping Info** (required)
		- Enter the customer's shipping information (e.g., address, phone number, etc.).

	+ **Shipping Country** (required)

		- Enter the customer's email address.
