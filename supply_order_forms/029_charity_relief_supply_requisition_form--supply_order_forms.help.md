# Charity Relief Supply Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Charity Relief Supply Requisition Form is used by charity requesters to submit requests for supplies needed in emergency situations. This form helps our team understand the scope of the request, including the location and quantity of supplies needed, to ensure accurate and timely delivery of essential items.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select the date you are requesting supplies, as this is a required field.
2.  Enter a unique identifier for your request.
3.  Provide your name and contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you for updates.
4.  Specify the location that needs supplies, which is your organization or a specific location that needs aid.
5.  Choose the urgency level of your request, and provide a brief justification for urgent requests.
6.  Select the category of supplies needed from the provided options.
7.  Add each item needed, including the product name, quantity, and unit of measurement.
8.  Specify the delivery location, including the full address, contact person, and phone number.
9.  Add any special handling or storage instructions for the supplies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Date of Requisition**: The date when this request is being made, which is a required field to help track request timelines.
*   **Requisition Number**: A unique identifier for your request, which can help us distinguish between multiple requests.
*   **Requester Name**: The name of the person making the request, which is used for contact and follow-up purposes.
*   **Requester Contact Information**: Your contact details, including phone and email, to ensure we can get back to you about your request.
*   **Organization or Location**: Specify the location that needs the supplies, which is your organization or a specific location that requires aid.
*   **Urgency Level**: Choose how critical the supplies are needed, which will help us prioritize requests.
*   **Justification if Urgent**: Provide a brief explanation for why urgent action is needed for this request.
*   **Date Needed By**: The date by which supplies are needed, which is a required field for accurate planning.
*   **Category of Supplies**: Select the type of supplies you need from the provided options.
*   **Item 1 - Product Name**: Enter the specific item you need, which is a required field for accurate supply sourcing.
*   **Item 1 - Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the item you need.
*   **Item 1 - Unit**: Specify the unit of measurement for the item (e.g., boxes, cases, units).
*   **Item 2 - Product Name**: This is an optional field for second item, if needed.
*   **Item 2 - Quantity**: Enter the quantity of the second item, if applicable.
*   **Item 2 - Unit**: Specify the unit of measurement for the second item.
*   **Delivery Location**: Enter the full address where supplies will be delivered, which is a required field.
*   **Delivery Contact Person**: Specify the person at the delivery location who should receive the supplies.
*   **Delivery Location Phone**: Enter the contact phone number at the delivery location.
*   **Special Handling or Storage Instructions**: Provide any special requirements for handling or storing the supplies.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields and provide as much detail as possible to help us serve your request efficiently.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form or the process, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   Duplicate requests or non-emergency requests may be denied, and we appreciate your understanding in prioritizing genuine emergency needs.
