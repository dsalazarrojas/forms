# Paypal Furniture Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paypal Furniture Order Form is a collection of fields that gather essential information from customers for a smooth ordering and payment process. This form is meant to be filled out by customers to provide their order details and payment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer information, including your name and contact details in the `customer_info` field.
2. Provide your order details in the `order_details` field.
3. Choose your preferred payment method (Paypal or Stripe) in the `payment_method` field.
4. Enter your shipping address in the `shipping_address` field.
5. Enter your phone number in the `customer_phone` field.
6. Enter your email in the `customer_email` field.
7. Provide any additional customer notes in the `customer_notes` field.
8. Enter any additional information in the `additional_info` field.
9. Enter additional fields in `additional_fields_1`, `additional_fields_2`, ..., `additional_fields_16` as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info** (`customer_info`): Enter your customer name and contact information.
- **Order Details** (`order_details`): Provide a description of the order.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose either Paypal or Stripe as your preferred payment method.
- **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`): Enter your shipping address.
- **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number.
- **Customer Email** (`customer_email`): Enter your email address.
- **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Provide any additional customer notes.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Enter any additional information.
- **Additional Fields 1-16** (`additional_fields_1`, `additional_fields_2`, ..., `additional_fields_16`): Enter any additional information as needed.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
