# Beef Cut Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beef Cut Order Form is designed to collect order information for beef cuts from customers. This guide will walk you through each field of the form to help you fill it out accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Order Date**: Enter the date you want to pick up or have your order delivered. The date should be in a format that your system accepts (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
2. **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person placing the order, as it should appear on your records.
3. **Customer Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person placing the order, so we can contact them if needed.
4. **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the person placing the order. This will help us keep you informed about your order status.
5. **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you want the order delivered. Make sure to include the apartment or suite number if applicable.
6. **Desired Delivery Date**: Enter the date you want to receive your order. If this is different from the order date, please make sure to include it so we can ensure timely delivery.
7. **Beef Grade**: Select the quality grade of beef you prefer:
	* **Prime**: High-quality beef from young, grass-fed cattle.
	* **Choice**: Beef from older, grain-fed cattle.
	* **Select**: Beef from older, grain-fed cattle with less marbling (fat content).
	* **Standard**: Beef from older, grain-fed cattle with moderate marbling.
8. **First Cut Type**: Select the type of beef cut you want for your first order:
	* **Ribeye Steak**: A tender and lean cut from the rib section.
	* **New York Strip**: A rich and tender cut from the strip loin.
	* **Filet Mignon**: A delicate and tender cut from the tenderloin.
	* **T-Bone Steak**: A rich and tender cut from the rear section.
	* **Sirloin Steak**: A lean and flavorful cut from the sirloin section.
	* **Chuck Roast**: A flavorful and tender cut from the chuck section.
	* **Brisket**: A lean and savory cut from the breast or plate section.
	* **Ground Beef**: Ground meat from a variety of cuts.
	* **Short Ribs**: Meaty and fall-off-the-bone tender.
	* **Other**: Specify a different type of cut not listed here.
9. **Quantity of First Cut**: Enter the quantity of the first cut you want in pounds.
10. **Second Cut Type**: Select the type of beef cut you want for your second order (if applicable):
	* **Ribeye Steak**: A tender and lean cut from the rib section.
	* **New York Strip**: A rich and tender cut from the strip loin.
	* **Filet Mignon**: A delicate and tender cut from the tenderloin.
	* **T-Bone Steak**: A rich and tender cut from the rear section.
	* **Sirloin Steak**: A lean and flavorful cut from the sirloin section.
	* **Chuck Roast**: A flavorful and tender cut from the chuck section.
	* **Brisket**: A lean and savory cut from the breast or plate section.
	* **Ground Beef**: Ground meat from a variety of cuts.
	* **Short Ribs**: Meaty and fall-off-the-bone tender.
	* **Other**: Specify a different type of cut not listed here.
11. **Quantity of Second Cut**: Enter the quantity of the second cut you want in pounds (if applicable).
12. **Third Cut Type**: Select the type of beef cut you want for your third order (if applicable):
	* **Ribeye Steak**: A tender and lean cut from the rib section.
	* **New York Strip**: A rich and tender cut from the strip loin.
	* **Filet Mignon**: A delicate and tender cut from the tenderloin.
	* **T-Bone Steak**: A rich and tender cut from the rear section.
	* **Sirloin Steak**: A lean and flavorful cut from the sirloin section.
	* **Chuck Roast**: A flavorful and tender cut from the chuck section.
	* **Brisket**: A lean and savory cut from the breast or plate section.
	* **Ground Beef**: Ground meat from a variety of cuts.
	* **Short Ribs**: Meaty and fall-off-the-bone tender.
	* **Other**: Specify a different type of cut not listed here.
13. **Quantity of Third Cut**: Enter the quantity of the third cut you want in pounds (if applicable).
14. **Special Requests or Instructions**: If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, such as custom cuts or thickness preferences, enter them here.
15. **Packaging Preference**: Select how you want your order packaged:
	* **Vacuum Sealed**: The order will be sealed in a vacuum packaging for freshness.
	* **Paper Wrapped**: The order will be wrapped in paper for a more natural presentation.
	* **Individual Steaks**: Each steak will be individually wrapped for easier handling.
	* **Bulk Package**: The order will be packaged in bulk for easier handling.
16. **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment you prefer:
	* **Cash**: Pay with cash at pickup or delivery.
	* **Credit Card**: Pay with a credit card.
	* **Debit Card**: Pay with a debit card.
	* **Check**: Pay with a check.
	* **Bank Transfer**: Pay via bank transfer.
17. **Estimated Total Cost**: Enter the estimated total cost of your order. This will help us ensure accuracy and inform you of the total cost.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your order before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We appreciate your understanding and cooperation in filling out this form accurately and efficiently.
