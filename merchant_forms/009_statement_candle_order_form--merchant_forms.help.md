# Statement Candle Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Statement Candle Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for placing orders with a merchant or store that sells candles. This form is intended for customers to provide their details and preferences for the order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the required fields.
* Choose the city and state where the order is to be delivered.
* Select the number of candles you wish to order.
* Choose the color and size of the candle that suits your preferences.
* Add any special instructions or notes for the order.
* Set a date and time for the order.
* Provide the total price of the order.
* Select the order status and payment type.
* Add any comments or notes for the order.
* Enter the merchant ID, store name, location, and order source details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name for the order.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter the address where the order is to be delivered.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Choose the city for the order delivery.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Choose the state for the order delivery from the provided options.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code for the order delivery.
* **Number of Candles** (`number_of_candles`, `number`, required): Enter the number of candles you wish to order.
* **Candle Color** (`candle_color`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the color of the candle that suits your preferences.
* **Candle Size** (`candle_size`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the size of the candle that suits your preferences.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `text`, required): Add any special notes or instructions for the order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required): Set a date for the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required): Set a time for the order.
* **Order Amount** (`order_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Total Price** (`total_price`, `text`, required): Enter the total price of the order.
* **Order Status** (`order_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the order status from the provided options.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the payment type from the provided options.
* **Order Comments** (`order_comments`, `text`, required): Add any comments or notes for the order.
* **Merchant ID** (`merchant_id`, `text`, required): Enter the merchant ID.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required): Enter the store name.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter the location.
* **Order Source** (`order_source`, `select_one`, required): Choose the order source from the provided options.
* **Other Order Source** (`order_source_other`, `text`, required): Enter any other order source information.
