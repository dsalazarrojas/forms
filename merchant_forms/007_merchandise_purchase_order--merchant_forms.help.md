# merchandise_purchase_order - Help Guide
## Purpose
The merchandise purchase order form is used by merchants to collect information from customers for processing purchase orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** field with the specific details of the order.
2. Provide your **Contact Info** in the relevant fields.
3. Enter your **Payment Info** to ensure smooth payment processing.
4. Specify the **Delivery Details** for the order.
5. List the required **Merchandise Details** for the order.
6. Enter any **Vendor Details** relevant to the order.
7. Select the **Merchandise Services** that apply to the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details (1)**: Enter the specific details about the order, such as date, time, or order number.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to contact you for any order updates or questions.
* **Payment Info (3)**: Enter your payment method, such as bank transfer or credit card information.
* **Delivery Details (4)**: Specify the delivery address, date, and any other relevant delivery information.
* **Merchandise Details (5)**: Enter a brief description of the merchandise ordered, including quantity and color.
* **Vendor Details (6)**: Enter any relevant information about the vendor or supplier of the merchandise.
* **Merchandise Services (7)**: Select all services that apply to the merchandise, such as installation or customization.
