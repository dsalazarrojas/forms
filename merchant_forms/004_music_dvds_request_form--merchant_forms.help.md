# Music DVDs Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Music DVDs Request Form is used by customers to request music DVDs from a merchant. The form will be reviewed by the merchant to fulfill the customer's music DVD requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Merchant Name field with the name of the merchant you are requesting from.
2. In the Contact Number field, enter the merchant's phone number so we can contact them to confirm the request.
3. Answer "True" or "False" in the Music Store Acceptance field to indicate whether the merchant accepts music DVD requests.
4. Select one or multiple options in the Music & DVDs field to specify the type of music DVDs you are requesting.
5. In the Music & DVDs List field, describe the specific music DVDs you are requesting.
6. Enter the Customer Name in the Customer Name field.
7. Enter the Customer Email in the Customer Email field.
8. Enter the Customer Phone number in the Customer Phone field.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes in the Order Note field for the merchant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Merchant Name** (`merchant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the merchant you are requesting from.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the merchant's phone number so we can contact them to confirm the request.
* **Music Store Acceptance** (`music_store_acceptance`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if the merchant accepts music DVD requests or "False" if not.
* **Music & DVDs** (`music_dvds`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple options for the type of music DVDs you are requesting (e.g., Music, DVDs).
* **Music & DVDs List** (`music_dvds_list`, text, required): Describe the specific music DVDs you are requesting.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the Customer's name.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the Customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required): Enter the Customer's phone number.
* **Order Note** (`order_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the merchant to fulfill the order.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be specific when listing the music DVDs you are requesting in the Music & DVDs List field.
* The merchant will review your request and contact you to confirm.
* The merchant may contact you to request additional information or clarification if needed.
