# online_shopping_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place orders online, collecting customer and payment information for order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Customer Name** in the field provided.
2.  Enter your **Order Total** in the field, this is the amount you are willing to spend on the order.
3.  Enter your **Shipping Address** in the field, this is where your order will be delivered to.
4.  Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the options provided, this is how you want to pay for your order.
5.  Enter your **Email** in the field, this is where you can be contacted regarding your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount you are willing to spend on the order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address where you want your order to be delivered to.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: false): Choose one of the provided payment methods, such as Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address, this is where you can be contacted about your order.

## Tips

- Make sure to enter accurate information in each field.
- Double-check your order total before submitting.
- Verify your email address is correct to receive order updates.
