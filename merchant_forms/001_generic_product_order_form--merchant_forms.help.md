# Generic Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a product order from a customer. It includes fields for product details and customer contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
2. Enter the product price in the "Product Price" field. If the price is a special promotion or discount, make sure to note this in the field.
3. Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
4. Provide a brief description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
5. Enter your name and title in the "Customer Name" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
8. Enter the order date in the "Order Date" field. This is the date you would like to have the product delivered or available for pickup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product being ordered. This is a required field, as it is essential to identify the product.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the product being ordered. This field is optional, but it is helpful to know the price in case of any promotions or discounts.
* **Quantity** (`product_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the product being ordered to help with order fulfillment and communication.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name and title as the person placing the order.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications about your order.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to receive calls or messages about your order.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you would like to have the product delivered or available for pickup.
