# Christmas Market Stallholder Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Christmas Market Stallholder Application Form is designed to gather necessary information from merchants who want to participate in the Christmas market event. Please fill out the form with your details and requirements to help the organizers make an informed decision about your application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions.
3. If you have any additional notes or information, use the "Additional Notes" field to provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the legal or trading name of your business or stall.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, text, required): This is the primary contact person for the application.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for the organizers to send updates and information about your application.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required): This is your mobile phone number, preferred.
* **Product category** (`product_category`, select_one, required): Choose the main category of products you plan to sell at the market.
	+ Food and beverage
	+ Crafts and gifts
	+ Clothing and accessories
	+ Services
* **Stall size requested** (`stall_size_requested`, select_one, required): Choose the standard stall size you need.
	+ 3x3 meters
	+ 6x3 meters
	+ Outdoor pitch
* **Insurance details** (`insurance_details`, text, required): If you have public liability insurance, please provide the name of the insurer and policy number.
* **Electricity required** (`electricity_required`, select_one, required): Indicate if you need access to electricity at your stall.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Product image links** (`product_images`, text, required): Please provide URLs to sample images or portfolio of your products.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, required): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information about your application.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* We will review your application and contact you with further instructions.
