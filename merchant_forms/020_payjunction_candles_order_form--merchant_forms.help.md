# Payjunction Candles Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for Payjunction Candles. To place an order, fill out the form with the required information. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter your **customer name** in the customer name field. 
*   Enter your **customer email** in the customer email field. 
*   Enter your **customer phone** in the customer phone field. 
*   If you have any **customer note**, enter it in the customer note field. 
*   Select your **payment method** from the list of available options. 
*   Enter any **order notes** in the order notes field. 
*   Enter the **order quantity** and **order total price** as per your order. 
*   Enter your **shipping details** in the shipping details field. 
*   Enter any **customer instructions** in the customer instructions field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the customer's name. 
*   **customer email** (`customer_email`, email, required/optional): This field is used to collect the customer's email address. 
*   **customer phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect the customer's phone number. 
*   **customer note** (`customer_note`, note, required/optional): This field is used to collect any note from the customer. 
*   **payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): This field is used to select the payment method for the order. 
    +   Options: 
        -   Cash
        -   Check
        -   Credit Card
        -   PayPal
        -   Apple Pay
        -   Google Pay
        -   Amazon Pay
        -   Stripe
*   **order notes** (`order_notes`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect any notes related to the order. 
*   **order quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, required/optional): This field is used to enter the quantity of the order. 
*   **order total price** (`order_total_price`, number, required/optional): This field is used to enter the total price of the order. 
*   **shipping details** (`shipping_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to enter any shipping details. 
*   **customer instructions** (`customer_instructions`, text, required/optional): This field is used to collect any instructions from the customer.
