# paypal_book_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Book Order Form is a tool for merchants to place orders with PayPal. This form helps to collect essential information for a successful transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Navigate to the form and start by filling out the first page.
2.  On the **book_order_form_page** page, you can add any additional comments or information about the book.
3.  On the **book_details** page, select the relevant options for the book you are ordering.
4.  On the **billing_info** page, choose the billing information for the order.
5.  On the **book_image** page, add the image of the book.
6.  On the **order_total** page, enter the total cost of the order.
7.  On the **payment_method** page, select the payment method for the order.
8.  On the **order_notes** page, add any additional notes about the order.
9.  On the **email_address** page, enter your email address.
10.  On the **phone_number** page, enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **book_order_form_page**: Enter any additional comments or information about the book.
* **book_details**: Select the relevant options for the book you are ordering. Please choose the correct option from the list to accurately describe the book.
* **billing_info**: Choose the billing information for the order. This will help the recipient know who to bill and how to contact you.
* **book_image**: Add the image of the book to help the recipient identify it correctly.
* **order_total**: Enter the total cost of the order. This will be used to calculate the payment amount.
* **payment_method**: Select the payment method for the order. This will determine how the payment is processed.
* **order_notes**: Add any additional notes about the order. This can be used to communicate with the recipient.
* **email_address**: Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you about the order.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number. This will be used in case of any issues with the order.

Note: The fields that are marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple" require you to choose one or multiple options from the list. The fields that are marked as "text" or "email" require you to enter a string of text. The fields that are marked as "number" require you to enter a numerical value.
