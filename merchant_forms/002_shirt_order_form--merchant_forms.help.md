# shirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shirt_order_form is a tool used to gather and manage information related to shirt orders, providing a structured format for tracking customer and payment details, shipping information, and order status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** section, providing a brief description of the order.
2. Enter the **Order Total** and **Payment Details** sections, specifying the amount to be paid and the payment method used.
3. Enter the **Shipping Details** section, providing information about the shipping method and cost.
4. Select the **Payment Method** and **Order Status** from the respective dropdown menus.
5. Enter the **Customer Details** section, including the customer's name, email, and phone number.
6. Enter the **Shipping Address** and **Customer Address** sections, providing the customer's address information.
7. Enter the **Payment Amount** and **Tax Amount** fields, specifying the payment and tax amounts.
8. Add any additional **Order Comments** or **Order Notes** to provide further context.
9. Review and submit the form to complete the order process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: A brief description of the order, providing context for the order process.
* **Order Total**: The total amount to be paid for the order.
* **Payment Details**: Information about the payment method used, such as PayPal or Credit Card.
* **Shipping Details**: Information about the shipping method and cost.
* **Payment Method**: Select one of the available payment methods (PayPal or Credit Card).
* **Order Status**: Select the status of the order (Pending, Delivered, or Cancelled).
* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.
* **Customer Email**: The customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: The customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address**: The customer's address information.
* **Shipping Address**: The shipping address information.
* **Payment Amount**: The payment amount for the order.
* **Tax Amount**: The tax amount for the order.
* **Total Cost**: The total cost of the order, including tax and other charges.
* **Payment Type**: Select the type of payment used (Credit Card or PayPal).
* **Shipping Method**: Select one or more shipping methods (Ground or Express).
* **Order Date**: The date the order was placed.
* **Order ID**: A unique identifier for the order.
* **Order Comments**: Additional comments or notes about the order.
* **Order Status Notes**: Additional notes about the order status.
* **Order Notes**: Additional notes about the order.
* **Tax Notes**: Additional notes about the tax amount.
* **Customer Notes**: Additional notes about the customer.
* **Shipping Cost**: The cost of shipping the order.
* **Customer Notes**: Additional notes about the customer.
