# Food Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields and instructions for completing the Food Vendor Application Form for our platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Submit your application by filling out all the required fields. Required fields are marked as such.
3. Please do not duplicate or submit incomplete applications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Vendor Information (Page 1)
* **Vendor Information**: Please provide basic information about your business, including the vendor name.
* **Business Information**: Describe your business, including its type and nature.
* **Contact Information**: This includes your personal name, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Name**: The name of the person who will be the primary contact.
* **Contact Phone**: Phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Email address of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Number**: Phone number of the primary contact person.

### Business and Food Information (Page 2)
* **Menu Information**: Describe your menu and the types of food you will be serving.
* **Products Served**: List the specific products you will be offering.
* **Hours Of Operation**: Schedule of your business hours.
* **Target Event**: Date and time you plan to start serving food.
* **Menu Price Range**: The range of prices for your menu items.
* **Additional Menu Price Range**: Second range of prices for menu items.
* **Food License Number**: Food service license number.
* **Business License Number**: Business license number.

### Insurance and Tax Information (Page 3)
* **Insurance Information**: Describe your insurance coverage and providers.
* **Tax ID**: Tax identification number or ID.
* **Tax ID**: This field is intended for secondary tax ID information. Please provide if applicable.

### Additional Information (Page 4)
* **Insurance Information**: Describe your insurance coverage and providers.
* **Equipment and Services**: List the equipment and services you will be providing.
* **Target Date**: Date and time you plan to start serving food.
* **Start Date**: Start date of your business.
* **End Date**: End date of your business.
* **Vendor Signature**: Your signature, confirming you have read and understood the guide.
* **Date Submitted**: Date and time the application was submitted.
* **Date and Time**: Date and time the application was submitted.
* **Insurance Information**: This field is for additional insurance information. Please provide if applicable.
* **Date and Time**: Date and time the application was submitted.
