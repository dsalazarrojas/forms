# Simple Wine Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information and wine order details from merchants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and other contact information in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Enter your wine order in the "Wine Order" field.
3. Add any additional details about your order in the "Wine Order Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: This section is where you provide your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Wine Order**: Enter a brief description of the wine you're ordering, including the type and quantity.
* **Wine Order Details**: Add any additional details or instructions about your order, such as the occasion or preferred delivery date.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and accurate information to ensure timely delivery of your order.
* If you have any special requests or instructions, use the "Wine Order Details" field to communicate them to the merchant.
* Review your order carefully before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
