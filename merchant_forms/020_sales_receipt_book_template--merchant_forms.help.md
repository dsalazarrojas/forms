# Sales Receipt Book Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales receipt book template is used to create a standardized document for capturing sales receipt details. This template helps in maintaining accurate and organized records of transactions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields, marked by an asterisk (\*), and make sure they are completed accurately and thoroughly.
2. Enter the company's name, address, and contact number to identify the seller.
3. Select or input the date of the transaction.
4. Type the customer's name accurately.
5. Enter the amount paid or received.
6. Provide a brief reason for the payment or transaction.
7. Input the receipt number for easy tracking and reference.
8. Fill in the name of the person receiving the payment or goods.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company making the sale.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the company making the sale.
* **Company Contact Number** (`company_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the company for any further communication.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the transaction.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer accurately.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Input the amount paid or received.
* **Reason for Payment** (`reason_for_payment`, text, required): Briefly describe the reason for the payment or transaction.
* **Receipt Number** (`receipt_number`, number, required): Enter the unique number assigned to the receipt.
* **Receiver Name** (`receiver_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the person receiving the payment or goods.
