# Fabric Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fabric Order Form is designed to collect essential information about fabric orders from customers or users. This form ensures that all relevant details are gathered accurately and efficiently, facilitating smooth processing and fulfillment of orders.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Order Type" relevant to your order (e.g., Custom, Wholesale, or Sample).
2. Choose the "Fabric Type" that suits your requirements (e.g., Cotton, Polyester, Linen, or Rayon).
3. Select the desired "Fabric Color" for your order.
4. Enter the quantity and weight of fabric you need in the respective fields.
5. Enter your "Shipping Address" for accurate delivery.
6. Add any "Additional Comments" that might be relevant to your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Type** (Order Type, select_multiple, Optional):
	+ Select the type of order you are making (e.g., Custom, Wholesale, or Sample).
	+ This will help us understand the nature of your order.
* **Fabric Type** (Fabric Type, select_one, Optional):
	+ Choose from the available fabric types (e.g., Cotton, Polyester, Linen, or Rayon).
	+ Select the fabric type that best matches your order.
* **Fabric Color** (Fabric Color, select_one, Optional):
	+ Pick the fabric color you require for your order.
	+ If you don't see your color listed, please choose "Other" and specify in the "Additional Comments".
* **Fabric Quantity** (Fabric Quantity, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the exact quantity of fabric you need.
	+ Be as precise as possible to ensure accurate processing.
* **Fabric Weight** (Fabric Weight, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the weight of fabric you require.
	+ Ensure to enter the correct weight for accurate calculations.
* **Fabric Width** (Fabric Width, number, Optional):
	+ Enter the width of fabric you need.
	+ This will help us provide correct calculations for your order.
* **Shipping Address** (Shipping Address, text, Optional):
	+ Enter your shipping address for accurate delivery.
	+ Please ensure to include all relevant address details.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, Optional):
	+ Add any further information that might be relevant to your order.
	+ This will help us understand your specific needs and preferences.
* **Additional Comments (if applicable)** (Additional Comments, note, Optional):
	+ If you need to specify a custom color not listed, add it here.
	+ This will ensure we understand your specific requirements for processing your order.
