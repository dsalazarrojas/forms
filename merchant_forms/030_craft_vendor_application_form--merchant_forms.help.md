# Craft Vendor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information from merchants applying to become vendors on your platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each page of the form in a structured and organized manner.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
3. Review your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (1)**: This is the name of your event or business name as it will be displayed on the platform.
* **Vendor Name (2)**: This is the name of your vendor or business name as it will be displayed on the platform.
* **Contact Name (3)**: This is the name of the contact person responsible for your business.
* **Email (4)**: This is the email address where you can be reached for any inquiries or updates.
* **Phone (5)**: This is the phone number where you can be reached for any emergencies or updates.
* **Website (6)**: This is the URL of your business website or online presence.
* **Business Hours (7)**: This is the time range when your business is available to operate.
* **Vendor Category (8)**: Select the category that best describes your business (e.g. Handmade Products, Home Decor, Food, etc.).
* **Business Type (9)**: Choose the type of business you operate (e.g. Online Business, Local Business, Wholesale, Retail, etc.).
* **Years of Experience (10)**: This is the number of years you have been running your business.
* **Event Frequency (11)**: Select how often you host events or operate your business (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Monthly, etc.).
* **Product List (12)**: List the products or services you offer (separate with commas).
* **Photo List (13)**: List any photos of your products or business (separate with commas).
* **Vendor License (14)**: Check if you have a vendor license.
* **Vendor License Number (15)**: If you have a vendor license, enter the license number.
* **Business License (16)**: Check if you have a business license.
* **Business License Number (17)**: If you have a business license, enter the license number.
* **Insurance (18)**: Enter your business insurance information.
* **If other, please specify (19)**: If your event frequency is not listed, please specify it in this field.
* **Additional Questions (20)**: This is an open note field for any additional questions or comments you may have.
* **Vendor Requirements (21)**: This is an open note field for any vendor requirements you may have.
* **Vendor Requirements List (22)**: List any vendor requirements you have (separate with commas).
* **Contact Person (23)**: Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Contact Information (24)**: This is an open note field for any additional contact information.
* **Vendor Agreement (25)**: This is a field for you to agree or disagree with the terms and conditions of the platform.
