# vendor_maintenance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vendor_maintenance_form is designed to collect basic vendor contact information for general maintenance and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vendor's name in the "name" field.
2. Enter the vendor's address in the "address" field.
3. Provide the vendor's email in the "email" field.
4. Add any relevant phone number for the vendor in the "phone" field.
5. Include any additional notes about the vendor in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name**: This field collects the vendor's name for identification and reference purposes.
* **address**: This field is for the vendor's physical address.
* **email**: This is the vendor's email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **phone**: This field is for the vendor's phone number for easy contact.
* **notes**: This is an optional field for any additional information or comments about the vendor.

## Tips
- Please ensure the email address is formatted correctly (e.g., user@domain.com).
- Use a format consistent with the vendor's actual address (e.g., Street Address, City, State, ZIP).
- Keep the notes field concise and relevant to the vendor information.
