# Cigar Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about your demographics, cigar-smoking habits, preferences, and purchasing behavior.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Follow the instructions on each page carefully.
2. Read each question carefully before answering.
3. Fill in the required fields (marked as required).
4. Select only one option for multiple-choice questions (marked as type: select_one).
5. Select all that apply for questions marked as type: select_multiple.
6. Type in your answers for text questions (marked as type: text).
7. If you have any doubts or concerns, refer to this help guide for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Respondent Information**
*   **Your Name** (text): Enter your full name.
*   **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address.
*   **Age Group** (select_one): Select your age group from 18-25, 26-35, 36-45, 46-55, 56-65, 65+.
*   **Gender** (select_one): Select your gender from Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.

### 2. **Cigar Experience**
*   **How Long Have You Been Smoking Cigars** (select_one): Select how long you have been smoking cigars from Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years.
*   **How Often Do You Smoke Cigars** (select_one): Select how often you smoke cigars from Daily, Several times a week, Weekly, Monthly, Occasionally, Special occasions only.
*   **Experience Level** (select_one): Select your experience level with cigars from Novice, Intermediate, Advanced, Connoisseur.

### 3. **Cigar Preferences**
*   **Favorite Cigar Type** (select_one): Select your favorite cigar type from Full-bodied, Medium-bodied, Light-bodied, No preference.
*   **Favorite Cigar Origin Country** (select_one): Select your favorite cigar origin country from Cuba, Dominican Republic, Nicaragua, Honduras, Brazil, Other.
*   **Preferred Cigar Size** (select_one): Select your preferred cigar size from Panetela, Corona, Robusto, Toro, Churchill, Double Corona, No preference.
*   **Typical Price Range per Cigar** (select_one): Select your typical price range per cigar from Under $5, $5-$10, $10-$15, $15-$25, $25-$50, Over $50.

### 4. **Brand Preferences**
*   **Your Top Three Favorite Cigar Brands** (text): Enter your top three favorite cigar brands.
*   **How Loyal Are You to Your Favorite Brands** (select_one): Select your level of loyalty to your favorite brands from Very loyal, Somewhat loyal, Neutral, Open to trying new brands, Always trying new brands.

### 5. **Purchase Behavior**
*   **Where Do You Typically Purchase Cigars** (select_one): Select where you typically purchase cigars from Local cigar shop, Online retailer, Tobacco shop, Premium retailer, Multiple sources.
*   **Factors That Influence Your Purchase** (select_multiple): Select all that apply from Price, Brand reputation, Origin or blend, Size or shape, Reviews or recommendations, Availability, Packaging.

### 6. **Satisfaction and Feedback**
*   **Overall Satisfaction with Cigars You Smoke** (select_one): Select your overall satisfaction with cigars from Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
*   **What Improvements Would You Like to See in Cigar Products** (text): Enter any improvements you would like to see in cigar products.
*   **Interested in Receiving Cigar News and Promotions** (select_one): Select whether you are interested in receiving cigar news and promotions from Yes, No.
