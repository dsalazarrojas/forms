# Elementary School T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elementary School T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect order information from Elementary School students, parents, or staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **user_info (User Information)**: Enter your name and contact information (e.g., email address).
	* **order_info (Order Information)**: Provide details about the T-shirt you want to order (size, style, color).
	* **payment_info (Payment Information)**: Enter your payment method and payment amount.
2. You can leave the **shirt_info (Shirt Information)** field empty, as it's optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info**: Enter your name and contact information to help us keep you updated on your order status.
* **shirt_info (optional)**: If you want to specify the type of T-shirt you want to order (e.g., short sleeve, long sleeve), enter the details here.
* **order_info**: Enter the type of T-shirt you want to order (e.g., logo, plain).
* **payment_info**: Enter the payment method and amount for your order. Make sure to include your payment reference number for our records.
* **order_summary (optional)**: If you have any special requests or comments, enter them here.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate contact information and payment details to avoid any issues with your order.
* If you're experiencing issues with your order, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
