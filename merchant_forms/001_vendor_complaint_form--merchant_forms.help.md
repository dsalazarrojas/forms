# Vendor Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Complaint Form is designed to collect information from customers about their complaints against a vendor. This form helps us understand the issue and take necessary actions to resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the vendor you are complaining about in the "Vendor Name" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your complaint in the "Complaint Description" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the complaint in the "Complaint Date" and "Complaint Time" fields.
4. If applicable, enter the contact number of the vendor in the "Contact Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor you are complaining about.
* **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your complaint.
* **Complaint Date** (`complaint_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the complaint.
* **Complaint Time** (`complaint_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the complaint.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the contact number of the vendor.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you have already submitted a complaint about the same issue, please do not fill out this form again.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
