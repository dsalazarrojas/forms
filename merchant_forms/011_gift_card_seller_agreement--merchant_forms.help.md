# Gift Card Seller Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the purpose and requirements for the Gift Card Seller Agreement form. This form is used for merchants to onboard with the gift card platform, agreeing to the terms and conditions of selling gift cards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the Seller Details section** with your company's basic information, such as your name and address.
2. **Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions** by selecting "Accept" in the Terms and Conditions Signature field.
3. **Choose your gift card seller programs** by selecting the programs you wish to participate in from the dropdown menu.
4. **Provide your contact details** so we can get in touch with you.
5. **Enter your payment details** so we can process your payments.
6. **Agree to the Program Terms Acceptance** by selecting "Accept" in the Program Terms Acceptance field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Details** (`seller_details`, text, optional): This section is for you to enter your company's basic information, such as your name and address.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): This is where you agree to the terms and conditions of selling gift cards on the platform. Please read the terms and conditions carefully before selecting "Accept".
* **Participation Agreement** (`participation_agreement`, number, optional): This field is currently not used in this form, so it's best to leave it empty.
* **Gift Card Seller Programs** (`gift_card_seller_programs`, select multiple, optional): Choose the gift card programs you wish to participate in from the dropdown menu.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): This field is not used in this form, so it's best to leave it empty.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): This field is not used in this form, so it's best to leave it empty.
* **Gift Card Programs Details** (`gift_card_programs_details`, text, optional): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information about your gift card programs.
* **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, note, optional): This is a note field where you can provide any additional information or notes about your onboarding process.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, email, optional): This is where you enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): This is where you enter your payment details so we can process your payments.
* **Terms and Conditions Signature** (`terms_and_conditions_signature`, select one, optional): This is where you agree to the terms and conditions of selling gift cards on the platform by selecting "Accept".
* **Terms and Conditions Date** (`terms_and_conditions_date`, date, optional): This field is not used in this form, so it's best to leave it empty.
* **Program Terms Acceptance** (`program_terms_acceptance`, select one, optional): This is where you agree to the program terms by selecting "Accept".

Note: The fields marked as optional can be left empty or skipped.
