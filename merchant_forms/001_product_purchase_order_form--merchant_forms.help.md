# Product Purchase Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Product Purchase Order Form is used to collect information from customers when a purchase is made from a merchant. The form captures essential details about the customer, product, order, shipping, and payment. By filling out this form, customers can provide the necessary information for the merchant to process their order accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Customer Info form**: Enter the customer's name, contact information, and any additional details as required.
2. **Provide Product Info**: Enter the product's description, quantity, and any other relevant details.
3. **Enter Order Details**: Specify the order's date, quantity, and any other relevant details.
4. **Enter Shipping Info**: Provide the shipping address, shipping method, and any other relevant details.
5. **Enter Payment Info**: Enter the payment method and amount.
6. **Enter Order Total**: Enter the total cost of the order.
7. **Add a Customer Order Note**: If necessary, add any additional comments or instructions for the merchant.
8. **Enter Merchant Info**: Enter any additional details about the merchant or the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (customer_info)**: The customer's name, contact information, and any additional details.
* **Product Info (product_info)**: A brief description of the product being purchased, including quantity.
* **Order Details (order_details)**: Specifics about the order, such as date and quantity.
* **Shipping Info (shipping_info)**: The shipping address and method.
* **Payment Info (payment_info)**: The payment method and amount.
* **Order Total (order_total)**: The total cost of the order.
* **Customer Order Note (customer_order_note)**: Any additional comments or instructions for the merchant.
* **Merchant Info (merchant_info)**: Any additional details about the merchant or the order.
