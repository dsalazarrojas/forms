# stripe_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The stripe_registration_form is used to register merchants with Stripe, a payment gateway service, to start accepting payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your User Name.
* Enter your Email address.
* Enter your Phone Number.
* Enter your Date of birth.
* Enter your Team name.
* Enter your League name.
* Enter your Address.
* Enter your Zip Code.
* Enter your City name.
* Select your Team type from the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address associated with your merchant account.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of birth** (`birth_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Team name** (`team_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your team name (e.g., football, hockey, basketball, etc.).
* **League name** (`league_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your league name (e.g., local, national, etc.).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your merchant's physical address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required/optional): Enter your merchant's zip code.
* **City** (`city_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your city name.
* **Team type** (`team_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select your team type from the given options (football, hockey, basketball, baseball, soccer).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure successful registration.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
