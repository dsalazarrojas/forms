# PayJunction T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayJunction T Shirt Order Form is designed to collect information from customers who want to purchase a t-shirt. It's essential to fill out this form correctly to ensure smooth and secure transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field.
4. Select your date of birth in the Date of Birth field.
5. Choose your preferred t-shirt size in the T Shirt Size field.
6. Select your preferred t-shirt color in the T Shirt Color field.
7. Enter any additional order notes in the Order Notes field.
8. Fill in your shipping address in the Shipping Address, Shipping City, Shipping State, Shipping Zip, and Shipping Country fields.
9. Select your payment method in the Payment Method field.
10. Enter the total amount of your order in the Total Amount field.
11. Choose your currency in the Currency field.
12. Fill in your billing address in the Billing Address, Billing City, Billing State, Billing Zip, and Billing Country fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, optional): Select your date of birth in MM/dd/yyyy format.
* **T Shirt Size** (6, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred t-shirt size.
* **T Shirt Color** (7, select_one, optional): Select your preferred t-shirt color.
* **Order Details** (8, note, required): Enter any additional order notes or instructions.
* **Payment Method** (9, select_one, required): Choose your payment method.
* **Total Amount** (10, number, required): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Currency** (11, select_one, required): Choose your currency.
* **Shipping Address** (12, text, required): Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City** (13, text, required): Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State** (14, text, required): Enter your shipping state.
* **Shipping Zip** (15, text, required): Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Shipping Country** (16, text, optional): Select your shipping country.
* **Billing Address** (17, text, required): Enter your billing address.
* **Billing City** (18, text, required): Enter your billing city.
* **Billing State** (19, text, required): Enter your billing state.
* **Billing Zip** (20, text, required): Enter your billing zip code.
* **Billing Country** (21, text, optional): Select your billing country.
* **Order Notes** (22, text, optional): Enter any additional order notes or instructions.
* **T Shirt Designer** (23, text, optional): Enter the name of the t-shirt designer.
* **Order Total** (24, number, required): Enter the total amount of your order.
* **Shipping Country** and **Billing Country** fields are only visible if shipping country and billing country are not set as required.
* **T Shirt Size** and **T Shirt Color** fields are only visible if they are enabled in the settings.

Note: Fields marked as optional are not required for the form submission, but filling them out can provide more information or details about the order. 
*   **Order Notes** and **T Shirt Designer** fields are only visible to the store admin and are not required for form submission.
