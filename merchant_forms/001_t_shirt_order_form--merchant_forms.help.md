# T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "T Shirt Order Form" is a collection of fields used to gather information for processing a t-shirt order. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Select your preferred T-Shirt color in the "T-Shirt Details" field.
3. Select your desired T-Shirt size in the "Shirt Size" field.
4. Enter your Shipping Address in the "Shipping Address" field. This will help us ship your order to the correct location.
5. Enter your Phone Number for further communication and order updates.
6. Enter your Email to allow us to send order updates and notifications.
7. Choose your preferred Payment Method from the "Payment Method" field.

* **First Name** (`t_shirt_order_form_page_1_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`t_shirt_order_form_page_1_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **T-Shirt Details** (`t_shirt_order_form_page_2_t_shirt_details`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferred T-Shirt color.
* **Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_order_form_page_3_shirt_size`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your desired T-Shirt size.
* **Color** (`t_shirt_order_form_page_2_t_shirt_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your preferred T-Shirt color. Note: This field might be redundant with the "T-Shirt Details" field.

* **Phone Number** (`t_shirt_order_form_page_5_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for further communication and order updates.
* **Email** (`t_shirt_order_form_page_4_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email to receive order updates and notifications.
