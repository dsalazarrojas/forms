2. Indicate the quantity of the product you would like to order.
3. Select the desired color for your sweatshirt.

8. Enter your shipping city.

10. Enter your shipping zip code.

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product you would like to order. This can be any name you think is funny or meaningful for your dad.

* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address where we can deliver your order.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, text, optional): Enter the city for your shipping address.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, text, optional): Enter the state for your shipping address.
