# Gun Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Gun Transfer Form is designed for gun owners to transfer ownership of their firearms to a new owner. This form is used to facilitate a safe and compliant transfer process, ensuring that the necessary information is collected for both the transferor and transferee.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the first name of the current owner.
3. Enter the last name of the current owner.
4. Specify the type of gun being transferred.
5. Enter the model of the gun being transferred.
6. Select the recipient's transfer method (Friend or Family).
7. Enter the recipient's address.
8. Enter the recipient's contact phone number.
9. Choose the transfer date.
10. If necessary, include any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter the first name of the current owner.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter the last name of the current owner.
* **Gun Type** (Text): Specify the type of gun being transferred (e.g. Pistol, Rifle, Shotgun).
* **Gun Model** (Text): Enter the model of the gun being transferred.
* **Transfer To** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the transfer recipient type (Friend or Family).
* **Address** (Text, Required for Select Multiple): Enter the address of the recipient if Friend is selected; otherwise, leave blank.
* **Contact Phone** (Text): Enter the contact phone number of the recipient.
* **Transfer Date** (Date, Required): Choose the date of the transfer.
* **Signature** (Note): Include any additional comments or notes about the transfer.
* **Signature of Transfer Recipient** (Note): The recipient's signature of acceptance of the transfer.
