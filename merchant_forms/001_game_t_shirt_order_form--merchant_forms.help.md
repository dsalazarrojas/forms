# Game T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information for a game t-shirt order, including customer details and order specifics. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your order is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the Order Details page, which includes any additional comments or notes about your order.
- Move on to the Contact Information page, where you will enter your name and contact details.
- On the Order Details page, select the correct Shirt Size and Shirt Color for your order.
- Finally, review and complete the order by entering the Order Date and Order Time, and selecting the Payment Method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Order Details
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, optional): This field is for any additional comments or notes about your order. If you have any specific requests or requirements, please include them here.

### Contact Information
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your order.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you would like to receive order updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you with order updates and questions.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date for your event or occasion, please enter it here.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time for your event or occasion, please enter it here.

### Order Options
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, required): Select the size of the t-shirt you ordered from the options provided.
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_multiple, required): Select the color of the t-shirt you ordered from the options provided.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`, number, required): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment you will use for this order.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure it is correct.
- If you have any questions or concerns about your order, please contact us.
