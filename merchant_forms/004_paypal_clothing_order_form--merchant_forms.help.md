# paypal_clothing_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information necessary for processing clothing orders. It captures customer details and payment information to facilitate smooth transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your shipping address, including your name, address line 1, and address line 2.
2. Enter the total cost of your order.
3. Select your preferred payment method from the list provided (Credit, Debit, or PayPal).
4. Enter your email address.
5. Optionally, you can select multiple payment methods for this transaction.
6. Enter a unique transaction ID (if applicable).
7. Choose the status of your payment (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shipping Address (text)**: Enter your shipping address to ensure timely and correct delivery of your order.
* **Order Total (number)**: Enter the total cost of your order.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Select your preferred payment method (Credit, Debit, or PayPal).
* **Customer Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your order.
* **Payment Method (select_multiple)**: This field is optional and allows you to select multiple payment methods for this transaction.
* **Transaction ID (text)**: Enter a unique transaction ID (if applicable).
* **Payment Status (select_one)**: Choose the status of your payment (Paid, Pending, or Refunded).

## Tips
- Double-check your shipping address for accuracy to avoid delays.
- Ensure you select a payment method that suits your needs.
- Enter a unique transaction ID if you have one.
- Confirm the payment status to track your order's progress.
