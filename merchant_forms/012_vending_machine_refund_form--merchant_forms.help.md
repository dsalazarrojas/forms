# vending_machine_refund_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect details about a refund for a vending machine transaction. It is designed to be filled out by merchants or customers who need to initiate a refund for a specific transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for each field as prompted.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Review your submission before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### customer_info (customer_info)
• **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the customer or individual requesting the refund.

### transaction_details (transaction_details)
• **transaction_details** (`transaction_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the transaction for which you're requesting a refund. This includes the date and time of the transaction, the item purchased, and any other relevant information.

### refund_amount (refund_amount)
• **refund_amount** (`refund_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the refund you are requesting.

### merchant_info (merchant_info)
• **merchant_info** (`merchant_info`, text, required/optional): Enter information about the merchant who handled the transaction, including their name and contact details.

### merchant_contact_info (merchant_contact_info)
• **merchant_contact_info** (`merchant_contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information for the merchant, if applicable.

### notes (notes)
• **notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations for the refund request, if necessary.

### email (email)
• **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the merchant or customer requesting the refund.

### phone (phone)
• **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter any phone number related to the refund request, if applicable.

### date (date)
• **date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the transaction for which you're requesting a refund.

### time (time)
• **time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the transaction for which you're requesting a refund.

## Tips
- Double-check all the information you provide for accuracy to ensure a smooth refund process.
- If you need more information or support, contact the relevant authorities before or after submitting this form.
- This form is designed to be user-friendly, so take your time and fill it out carefully.
