# HandMade Bag Order Form Worldpay UK Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture order details and customer information for HandMade Bag orders. It includes fields for customer contact and delivery details, order total, payment methods, and order status. Fill out the form completely to submit your order.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the fields as required, starting with the order details.
2. Provide your customer details, including email address, phone number, first name, last name, and other relevant information.
3. Specify the delivery details, such as delivery name, street, city, state, postcode, and country.
4. Choose your preferred payment method from the options provided.
5. Select the current order status.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`): Enter a brief description of your order.
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`): Fill out the customer details, including email address, phone number, first name, last name, etc.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Order Total** (`order_total`): Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Customer First Name** (`customer_first_name`): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Customer Last Name** (`customer_last_name`): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Customer Street** (`customer_street`): Enter the customer's street address.
* **Customer City** (`customer_city`): Enter the customer's city.
* **Customer State** (`customer_state`): Enter the customer's state or region.
* **Customer Postcode** (`customer_postcode`): Enter the customer's postcode.
* **Customer Country** (`customer_country`): Enter the customer's country.
* **Customer Company** (`customer_company`): Enter the customer's company name.
* **Delivery Name** (`delivery_name`): Enter the delivery name (if different from customer name).
* **Delivery Street** (`delivery_street`): Enter the delivery street address.
* **Delivery City** (`delivery_city`): Enter the delivery city.
* **Delivery State** (`delivery_state`): Enter the delivery state or region.
* **Delivery Postcode** (`delivery_postcode`): Enter the delivery postcode.
* **Delivery Country** (`delivery_country`): Enter the delivery country.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`delivery_instructions`): Enter any special delivery instructions.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Add any additional customer notes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Order Status** (`order_status`): Select the current order status (pending, paid, shipped, cancelled, on hold, refunded, or failed).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
* Use the correct payment method to avoid any processing issues.
* Enter accurate customer and delivery details to ensure smooth processing.
