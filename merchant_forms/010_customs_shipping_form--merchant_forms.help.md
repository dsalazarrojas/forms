# Customs Shipping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customs Shipping Form is designed to collect information about a shipment to facilitate smooth customs clearance and shipping processes. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your details as the sender.
2. Enter the recipient's details.
3. Provide the shipment details, including weight and value.
4. Confirm customs clearance and shipping method.
5. Add any additional information if necessary.
6. Provide notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sender Details** (Sender Details): Enter your name as the sender of the shipment.
* **Sender Name** (sender_name): Enter your name as the sender of the shipment.
* **Sender Email** (sender_email): Enter your email address as the sender of the shipment.
* **Recipient Details** (Recipient Details): Enter the recipient's name and address.
* **Recipient Name** (recipient_name): Enter the recipient's name.
* **Recipient Email** (recipient_email): Enter the recipient's email address.
* **Shipment Details** (Shipment Details): Enter a brief description of the shipment.
* **Shipment Description** (shipment_description): Enter a detailed description of the shipment (if necessary).
* **Shipment Weight** (shipment_weight): Enter the weight of the shipment. Please ensure this is accurate.
* **Shipment Value** (shipment_value): Enter the value of the shipment. Please ensure this is accurate.
* **Customs Clearance** (Customs Clearance): Confirm if the shipment requires customs clearance.
* **Shipping Method** (shipping_method): Confirm the shipping method used.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info): Add any additional information about the shipment if necessary.
* **Notes** (Notes): Add any additional comments or notes about the shipment.

Please note that any field marked as 'required' must be completed accurately for the form to be processed correctly. If you need help or have further questions, please contact the shipping team for assistance.
