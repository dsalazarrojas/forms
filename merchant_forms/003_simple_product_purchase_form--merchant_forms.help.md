# Simple Product Purchase Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a simple product purchase. It is meant to gather basic details from the customer, including their personal information and the product they wish to purchase. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it matches the format of your actual email address.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Select the product you wish to purchase from the drop-down menu in the "Product" field.
6. Enter the quantity of the product you wish to purchase in the "Quantity" field.
7. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the product in the "Price" field. This will be used as a reference price for your purchase.
8. Enter your address in the "Address" field. You can provide additional details in the "Address" field if necessary.
9. Fill in the city where you are located in the "City" field.
10. Enter the state where you are located in the "State" field.
11. Enter the zip code for the address in the "Zip" field.
12. Select the date you wish to pick up or have the product delivered on in the "Date" field.
13. Choose the time of your desired pickup or delivery time in the "Time" field.
14. If you have any comments or special requests, type them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Product** (`product`, select_one, optional): Select the product you wish to purchase from the drop-down menu.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Enter how many of the selected product you wish to purchase.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the product.
* **Address** (`address`, note, optional): Enter your address where you want the product delivered or picked up.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where you are located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where you are located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code for the address.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date you wish to pick up or have the product delivered on.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your desired pickup or delivery time.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any comments or special requests, type them here.
