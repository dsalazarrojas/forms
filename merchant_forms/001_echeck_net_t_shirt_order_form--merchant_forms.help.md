# echeck_net_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for an online order form, specifically for ECheck.Net.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form's pages and identifying the fields you need to fill out.
2. Fill out the ECheck.Net field, which is a text field that serves as a note.
3. Provide your order information in the Order Form field, which is a text field.
4. Enter payment details in the Payment field, which is also a text field.
5. Fill out Merchant Info, which is a text field containing information about the merchant.
6. Confirm your payment information in the Confirm Payment Info field, which is a text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **ECheck.Net** (`id: 1`, `type: note`): This field serves as a note and is meant to be filled out with information about ECheck.Net.
* **Order Form** (`id: 2`, `type: text`): Fill out this field with your order information, providing details about what you are ordering.
* **Payment** (`id: 3`, `type: text`): Enter your payment details in this field, such as payment amount or method.
* **Merchant Info** (`id: 4`, `type: text`): Fill out this field with information about the merchant, such as their name or contact information.
* **Confirm Payment Info** (`id: 5`, `type: text`): Verify your payment information in this field, ensuring accuracy.

## Tips
* Please double-check your payment details to avoid errors.
* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately to ensure a smooth order process.
