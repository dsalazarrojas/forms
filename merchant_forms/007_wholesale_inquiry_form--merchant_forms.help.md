# Wholesale Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wholesale Inquiry Form is a tool used to collect information from potential wholesale customers to facilitate business arrangements. This form will help us understand your company's needs and preferences to provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Make sure to check the "How to Complete This Form" section carefully to understand what each field is used for.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name (company_name)** (`company name`, Optional): Provide the name of your company, which will be displayed on our records.
* **Contact Name (contact_name)** (`Contact name`, Optional): Enter your contact person's name.
* **Contact Email (contact_email)** (`contact_email`, Optional): Enter your contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)** (`Contact phone`, Optional): Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Company Address (company_address)** (`Company address`, Optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Company Website (company_website)** (`Company website`, Optional): Enter your company's website.
* **Product Name (product_name)** (`Product name`, Optional): Enter the name of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Product Description (product_description)** (`Product description`, Optional): Enter a brief description of the product you are inquiring about.
* **Quantity (quantity)** (`Quantity`, Optional): Enter the quantity of the product you are interested in purchasing.
* **Unit Price (unit_price)** (`Unit price`, Optional): Enter the price per unit of the product you are interested in purchasing.
* **Total Price (total_price)** (`Total price`, Optional): Enter the total price for the quantity of the product you are interested in purchasing.
* **Terms (terms)** (`Terms`, Optional): Enter any specific terms or conditions related to your inquiry.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any).
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to contact our support team.

Note: Required fields are denoted by "Required" in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.
