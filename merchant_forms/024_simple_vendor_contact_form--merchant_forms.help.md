# Simple Vendor Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow customers or users to contact a specific vendor or merchant. It is intended to collect contact information and other relevant details from the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and details ready before filling out the form.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
- **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
- **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: `false`): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Address** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your physical address where you can be contacted or visited.
- **Vendor Name** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the vendor or merchant you are contacting.
