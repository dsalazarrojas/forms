# Payment Cancellation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Payment Cancellation Form is used by merchants to cancel payments that have been initiated but not yet processed or completed. This form is designed to gather necessary details to facilitate the cancellation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate to the Payment Cancellation Form page.
2. Select the "Cancel Payment" option for the specific payment you want to cancel.
3. Fill out each field with the required information:
	* "Cancel Payment Details": A brief description of the reason for cancellation.
	* "Cancel Date": The date you want to cancel the payment (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
	* "Payment Cancellation Time": The time you want to cancel the payment (in 24-hour format).
	* "Reason for Cancellation": A detailed description of the reason for the payment cancellation.
	* "Cancellation Amount": The amount of the payment you want to cancel.
4. Select "Agree" to confirm that you understand and agree to the cancellation.
5. If necessary, add any additional notes about the cancellation in the "Notes" field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your merchant support team for assistance.
* Double-check that all information is accurate before submitting the form.
