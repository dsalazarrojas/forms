# Freight Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for merchants to report freight claims to the company, providing essential information about their shipment and claim details. It is used to process freight claims efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Make sure to provide all required fields (indicated by *).
* For each field, please provide as much detail as possible.
* If you need to attach additional documents, please make sure they are relevant to your claim.
* If you need help, please contact form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Merchant Form Data** (`merchant_form_data`, `text`, required): This field is for merchants to provide any relevant information about their merchant account or store.
* **Shipment Details** (`shipment_details`, `text`, required): Here, merchants can enter details about their shipment, including any relevant tracking numbers, dates, or times.
* **Carrier Information** (`carrier_info`, `text`, required): In this field, merchants can provide information about the carrier or shipping company involved in the shipment.
* **Claim Information** (`claim_info`, `text`, required): Merchants can describe their claim in this field, including any relevant circumstances surrounding the claim.
* **Expense Details** (`expense_details`, `text`, required): Here, merchants can report any expenses related to the shipment, including any applicable fees, taxes, or charges.
* **Upload Receipt** (`upload_receipt`, `text`, optional): If merchants have attached receipts or other supporting documents, they can upload them here.
* **Attach Additional Documents** (`attach_additional_docs`, `text`, optional): Merchants can attach additional documents, such as invoices or proof of delivery, to support their claim.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, optional): This field is for merchants to sign their claim, indicating their acceptance of its content. 

# Tips
* Please make sure all fields are complete and accurate before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact form@gic.mx.
