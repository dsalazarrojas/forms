# Supplier Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from suppliers to register them and agree to the terms of use. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter the required information under "first_page".
2. On the second page, provide details about the supplier, including their contact information.
3. On the following pages, select the corresponding options for payment terms, contact details, financial details, agreement, and terms of use. Ensure that you select the correct options for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page** (text, required/optional): This field is the first part of the supplier registration form. Enter the necessary information to proceed with the registration process.
* **supplier_details** (text, required/optional): Provide accurate contact information for the supplier, including their address, phone number, and email.
* **payment_terms** (select multiple, required): Select whether the supplier agrees to the payment terms. Choose 'Yes' to agree or 'No' to decline.
* **contact_details** (select multiple, required): Select whether the supplier is willing to share their contact details. Choose 'Yes' to share or 'No' to decline.
* **financial_details** (select multiple, required): Select whether the supplier is willing to share their financial details. Choose 'Yes' to share or 'No' to decline.
* **agreement** (select multiple, required): Select whether the supplier agrees to the terms of use. Choose 'Yes' to agree or 'No' to decline.
* **merchant_details** (text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the merchant, if necessary.
* **terms_of_use** (select multiple, required): Select whether the supplier agrees to the terms of use. Choose 'Yes' to agree or 'No' to decline.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and how it contributes to the overall registration process.
* Double-check your selections for payment terms, contact details, financial details, agreement, and terms of use to ensure correct registration.
