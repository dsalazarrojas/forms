# Marketplace Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Marketplace Consent Form is a document that is designed to be filled out by individuals who wish to use a marketplace platform for buying and selling goods and services. The form is used to gather the user's consent for the collection and use of their data, as well as to provide information about the marketplace's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read and understand the form carefully** before proceeding.
2. **Answer all questions truthfully and accurately**.
3. **Sign and date the form** at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Marketplace Overview
* **Marketplace** (text): This is a brief description of what the marketplace is and what it offers.
* **Yes/No/Other** (select multiple): Please select the type of marketplace that best describes our platform.
* **What is a Marketplace?** (text): Please provide a brief description of what you think a marketplace is.
* **Types of Marketplaces** (select multiple): Please select the types of marketplaces that are relevant to our platform.

### Types of Service Providers
* **Types of Service Providers** (select multiple): Please select the types of service providers that are relevant to our platform.
* **What is a Service Provider?** (text): Please provide a brief description of what you think a service provider is.

### Data Protection
* **How do we make money?** (text): This is a brief description of how the marketplace makes money.
* **How do we protect your data?** (text): This is a description of the policies and procedures in place to protect your data.

### Consent
* **Do you consent to share your data with us?** (select one): Please select "I agree" or "I do not agree" to share your data with us.

### Marketplace Agreements
* **Marketplace Service Agreement** (text): This is a detailed description of the terms and conditions of using the platform.
* **Marketplace Termination** (text): This is a description of the conditions under which the platform may be terminated.
* **Terms & Conditions** (text): This is a detailed description of the terms and conditions of using the platform.
* **Privacy & Security** (text): This is a description of the policies and procedures in place to protect your data and ensure security.
* **Dispute Resolution** (text): This is a description of the process for resolving disputes or issues that may arise.
* **Governing Law and Jurisdiction** (text): This is a description of the laws and jurisdictions that govern the platform.
* **Entire Agreement** (text): This is a declaration that this form is a complete and final agreement between the user and the platform.
* **Contact Us** (text): This is a contact form for users to get in touch with the platform.
* **Additional Comments** (text): This is an optional field for users to provide additional comments or information.
* **Marketplace Consent Form Section 21-24** (text): These fields are reserved for potential future development and are not currently used.
