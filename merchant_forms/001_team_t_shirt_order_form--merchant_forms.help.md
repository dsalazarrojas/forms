# Team T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for team members to order t-shirts for team events, activities, or personal use. It collects information on t-shirt style, size, color, and quantity, as well as contact information for order tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your team name and order information in the "Team T Shirt Order Form" text field.
2. Select your desired t-shirt details:
	* Select a style (V, H, A).
	* Choose your t-shirt size (S, M, L, XL).
	* Choose one or more colors (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green).
3. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you want to order.
4. If you have a message to be printed on the t-shirts, enter it in the "T-Shirt Message" text field.
5. Enter your email address and phone number for order tracking.

* **Team T Shirt Order Form**: Enter a brief description of your order.
* **T-Shirt Details**:
	+ **T-shirt Style**: Choose the style of t-shirt you want to order (V, H, A).

* **T-Shirt Color**: Choose one or more colors for your t-shirt (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green).
* **T-Shirt Quantity**: Enter the number of t-shirts you want to order.
* **T-Shirt Message**: If you have a message to be printed on your t-shirt, enter it here.
