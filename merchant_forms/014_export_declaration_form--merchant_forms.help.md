# Export Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Export Declaration Form is a document used by merchants to declare their exported goods, providing necessary information for the export process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the base information (page 1).
2. Provide details about the goods being exported (page 1).
3. Enter declaration information (page 1).
4. Fill out the contact information (page 1).
5. Enter shipping information (page 1).
6. Complete the payment information (page 1).
7. Add any additional notes (optional, page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Base Information (page 1)

* **Base Information** (`Base Information`, text, required): This section captures the fundamental details of the export declaration.
* **Goods Information** (`Goods Information`, text, required): Describe the goods being exported, including their type, quantity, and other relevant details.
* **Declaration Information** (`Declaration Information`, text, required): Enter the declaration information for the exported goods, following the format specified by the export regulations.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Fill in the contact details of the person responsible for the export, including name, address, and other relevant contact information.
* **Shipping Information** (`Shipping Information`, text, required): Enter the shipping details, including shipping date, carrier name, and tracking number.
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, text, required): Provide payment details, including payment method and amount.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the export declaration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check the declaration information to ensure it follows the export regulations.
* Keep the contact information up to date and accurate.
* Be thorough in providing shipping information to avoid delays.
* Review the payment information carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use the notes section for any additional comments or questions.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
