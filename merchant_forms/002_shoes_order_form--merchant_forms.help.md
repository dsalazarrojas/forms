# Shoes Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shoes Order Form is a vital document for capturing essential information for a customer's shoe order. It ensures that all necessary details are collected to process the order smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired shoe design from the provided options (Sneakers, Loafers, Boots).
2. Enter the size of the shoes you would like to order.
3. Specify the quantity of shoes you would like to purchase.
4. Choose the color of the shoes from the available options (Red, Blue, Green).
5. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
6. Select a delivery date and time for your order.
7. Choose your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, PayPal).
8. Enter the payment amount for your order.
9. Provide any additional payment details, if necessary.
10. Enter an order note or special requests, if any.
11. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
12. Specify if you have any special requests for your order.
13. Enter your shipping details, including address, city, state, and zip code.
14. Enter your signature as a customer, indicating that you are agreeing to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Design (design, select_one, required)**: Select the type of shoes you would like to order from the available options (Sneakers, Loafers, Boots).
* **Size (size, number, required)**: Enter the size of the shoes you would like to order.
* **Quantity (quantity, number, required)**: Specify the number of shoes you would like to purchase.
* **Color (color, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the color of the shoes from the available options (Red, Blue, Green).
* **Contact Name (contact_name, text, optional)**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email (contact_email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Delivery Date (delivery_date, date, required)**: Select a delivery date and time for your order.
* **Delivery Time (delivery_time, time, optional)**: Enter the delivery time for your order (if applicable).
* **Further Requests (further_requests, text, optional)**: Enter any additional requests or comments you have.
* **Payment Method (payment_method, select_one, required)**: Choose your preferred payment method (Bank Transfer, PayPal).
* **Payment Amount (payment_amount, number, required)**: Enter the payment amount for your order.
* **Payment Details (payment_details, text, required)**: Enter any additional payment details, if necessary.
* **Order Note (order_note, text, required)**: Enter an order note or special requests.
* **Terms and Conditions (terms_and_conditions, text, required)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Special Requests (special_requests, select_multiple, optional)**: Specify if you have any special requests for your order.
* **Shipping Details (shipping_details, text, required)**: Enter your shipping details, including address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Shipping Address (shipping_address, text, required)**: Enter your shipping address.
* **Shipping City (shipping_city, text, required)**: Enter your shipping city.
* **Shipping State (shipping_state, text, required)**: Enter your shipping state.
* **Shipping Zip (shipping_zip, number, required)**: Enter your shipping zip code.
* **Shipping Country (shipping_country, text, required)**: Enter your shipping country.
* **Customer Signature (customer_signature, select_one, required)**: Enter your signature as a customer, indicating that you are agreeing to the terms and conditions.
* **Merchant Signature (merchant_signature, select_one, required)**: Enter the merchant's signature, confirming that the order has been accepted.
* **Further Requests 2 (further_requests_2, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or requests.
