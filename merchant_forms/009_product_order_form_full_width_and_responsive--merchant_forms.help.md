# Product Order Form Full Width And Responsive - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect customer information for in-store or delivered product orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Product Name** of the product you wish to purchase.
2. Enter the **Quantity** of the product you wish to purchase.
3. Enter your **Email** address for our records.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** for communication.
5. Select your **Delivery Date** for in-store pickup or delivery.
6. Select your **Delivery Time** for in-store pickup or delivery.
7. If you have any **Additional Notes** about your order, enter them here.
8. Check if you have any **Special Requests** (not required).
9. Select the **Delivery Method**: choose between "In-store pickup", "Delivery", or "Pickup".
10. If applicable, **Confirm Select Multiple Options** (e.g., "Yes", "No" or "Other").
11. If you have **Select Multiple Options**, choose from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product you wish to purchase. (Text, required)
* **Quantity**: Enter the number of products you wish to purchase. (Number, required)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for our records. (Email, required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication. (Text, required)
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date for in-store pickup or delivery. (Date, required)
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time for in-store pickup or delivery. (Time, required)
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your order if you have any. (Text, optional)
* **Special Requests**: Check if you have any special requests (not required). (Text, optional)
* **Delivery Method**: Select the method for your order: "In-store pickup", "Delivery", or "Pickup". (Select, required)
* **Select Multiple Options**: Choose your options (Yes, No, or Other). This is not required. (Select, optional)
* **Confirm Select Multiple Options**: Confirm your selected options. (Select, required)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or questions. (Note, optional)

Note: This field-by-field explanation will guide you through each field on the form, providing a clear understanding of what each field is for and what is expected of you.
