# Rain Coat Recommendation Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rain Coat Recommendation Quiz is designed to help you find the best rain coat that suits your needs and preferences. Please answer the following questions to get a personalized recommendation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full name** in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your **Preferences** for the rain coat you're looking for (check all that apply).
5. Choose your **Location** where you'll be using the rain coat (select one option).
6. Select the **Activities** you engage in (check all that apply).
7. Indicate your **Budget** for the rain coat (select one option).
8. Choose how often you encounter **Rain Frequency** (select one option).
9. Select the **Desired Features** you'd like in your rain coat (check all that apply).
10. Indicate your **Purchase Intent** (select one option).
11. Select your **Preferred Color** for the rain coat (select one option).
12. Choose your preferred **Size** for the rain coat (select one option).
13. Select where you'd like to **Purchase** the rain coat (select one option).
14. Choose how you'd like to **Purchase** the rain coat (select one option).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name, which will be used to address the recommendation.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to ensure you receive the recommendation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for further communication.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the preferences you'd like in your rain coat (e.g., Waterproof, Breathable, Insulated).
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the location where you'll be using the rain coat (e.g., City, Hike, Mountain, Beach, Urban).
* **Activities** (`activities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check all the activities you engage in (e.g., Hiking, Climbing, Kayaking, Cycling, Traveling, Running, Camping, Surfing).
* **Budget** (`budget`, `number`, required: false): Select your budget for the rain coat.
* **Rain Frequency** (`rain_frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how often you encounter rain (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Rarely).
* **Desired Features** (`desired_features`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the desired features you'd like in your rain coat (e.g., Waterproof, Breathable, Insulated, Lightweight, Water-repellent).
* **Purchase Intent** (`purchase_intent`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your purchase intent (e.g., Gift, Personal use, Business use).
* **Preferred Color** (`preferred_color`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred color for the rain coat.
* **Size** (`size`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred size for the rain coat.
* **Purchase Location** (`purchase_location`, `select_one`, required: false): Select where you'd like to purchase the rain coat (e.g., Online, Offline).
* **Purchase Method** (`purchase_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you'd like to purchase the rain coat (e.g., In-store, Online).

**Tips**

* Please answer all fields to get an accurate recommendation.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact our customer support team.
* The recommended rain coat will be based on your preferences and needs.
* We'll do our best to provide you with the best possible recommendation.
