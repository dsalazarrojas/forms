# Generic T Shirt Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Generic T Shirt Order Form is used to collect and manage information for T-Shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Fill out the customer information**:
   * Enter your name and contact information.
   * This information will be used as part of the order details.

2. **Choose your T-Shirt size**:
   * Select the size of the T-Shirt from the drop-down list.

3. **Select your T-Shirt color**:
   * Choose the color of the T-Shirt you want to order.
   * This field is multiple-selectable, allowing you to pick more than one color.

4. **Enter your T-Shirt size and color**:
   * Enter the size of the T-Shirt you want to order.
   * Enter the color of the T-Shirt you want to order.

5. **Enter order details**:
   * Enter any additional information about your order.
   * This field is optional and not required.

6. **Enter shipping details**:
   * Enter shipping information for your order.
   * This field is optional and not required.

7. **Enter customer notes**:
   * Leave any additional notes or comments about your order.
   * This field is optional and not required.

8. **Enter payment details**:
   * Enter payment information for your order.
   * This field is optional and not required.

9. **Enter order total**:
   * This field is not editable and is used to display the total cost of the order.

10. **Select payment method**:
    * Choose how you want to pay for your order.
    * Options include credit card, PayPal, and Stripe.

11. **Select order status**:
    * Choose the status of your order.
    * Options include pending, shipped, and delivered.

12. **Enter customer ID**:
    * Enter a customer ID if applicable.
    * This field is optional and not required.

13. **Enter order ID**:
    * Enter an order ID if applicable.
    * This field is optional and not required.

14. **Enter date ordered**:
    * Enter the date you ordered the T-Shirt.
    * This field is required.

15. *Enter time ordered*:
    * Enter the time you ordered the T-Shirt.
    * This field is required.

16. **Enter time shipped**:
    * Enter the time the T-Shirt was shipped.
    * This field is optional and not required.

17. **Enter date shipped**:
    * Enter the date the T-Shirt was shipped.
    * This field is optional and not required.

18. **Enter order comments**:
    * Leave any additional comments about your order.
    * This field is optional and not required.

19. **Enter order created**:
    * This field is not editable and is used to display the date the order was created.

20. **Enter order updated**:
    * This field is not editable and is used to display the last time the order was updated.

21. **Enter order closed**:
    * This field is not editable and is used to display the date the order was closed.

22. **Enter order opened**:
    * This field is not editable and is used to display the date the order was opened.

23. **Select payment status**:
    * Choose the status of your payment.
    * Options include pending, paid, and failed.

24. **Enter notes**:
    * Leave any additional notes about your order.
    * This field is optional and not required.
