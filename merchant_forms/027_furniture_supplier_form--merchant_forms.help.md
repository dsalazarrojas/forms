# Furniture Supplier Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Furniture Supplier Form is a form designed to collect information about a furniture supplier. It helps merchants gather essential details about the supplier, including contact information, supplier profile, and business terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the contact details section with the supplier's address, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the supplier's name and website (if applicable).
3. List the products offered by the supplier.
4. Specify the supplier's experience in the industry since when they started supplying furniture.
5. Outline the payment terms and conditions agreed upon by the supplier.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (contact_details): Enter the supplier's address where they can be contacted.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for communicating with the supplier.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email): Enter the email address of the supplier for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone): Enter the phone number of the supplier's representative.
* **Supplier Name** (supplier_name): Enter the name of the supplier, which will be used for official purposes.
* **Supplier Address** (supplier_address): Enter the physical address of the supplier.
* **Supplier Website** (supplier_website): Enter the URL of the supplier's website (if they have one).
* **Supplier Phone** (supplier_phone): Enter the phone number of the supplier, which can be used for urgent or out-of-hours communications.
* **Products Offered** (products_offered): List the type of furniture products that the supplier offers.
* **Supplier Since** (supplier_since): Enter the date when the supplier started offering furniture services or products.
* **Payment Terms** (payment_terms): Specify the payment terms and conditions agreed upon by the supplier, including any specific requirements or deadlines.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the contact details to ensure effective communication.
- Be precise when listing the products offered to ensure accurate categorization.
- Verify the supplier's website and phone number for authenticity.
- Clearly outline the payment terms to avoid any confusion.
