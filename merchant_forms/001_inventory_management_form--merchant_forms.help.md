# Inventory Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help store employees manage the store's inventory. It allows them to update and record product details, including store location, product id, quantity, price, and sales history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field as instructed below.
2. Fill out the required fields.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Location** (store_location): Enter the store location where the product is currently held.
* **Store Name** (store_name): Enter the name of the store where the product is currently held.
* **Product Id** (product_id): Enter the id of the product being managed.
* **Quantity** (quantity): Enter the current quantity of the product in stock.
* **Product Name** (product_name): Enter the name of the product being managed.
* **Product Category** (category): Select the category of the product (e.g. Electronics, Clothing, Toys).
* **Subcategory** (subcategory): Select the subcategory of the product (e.g. TVs, Laptops, Tablets).
* **Price** (price): Enter the current price of the product.
* **Date Received** (date_received): Enter the date the product was received.
* **Date Sold** (date_sold): Enter the date the product was sold.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the product.
* **Email** (email): Enter the email of the store employee managing the product.
* **Phone** (phone): Enter the phone number of the store employee managing the product.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name): Enter the name of the store employee managing the product.
* **Position** (employee_position): Enter the position of the store employee managing the product.
