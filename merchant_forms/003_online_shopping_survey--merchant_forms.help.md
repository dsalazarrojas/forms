# Online Shopping Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your shopping experience. We want to understand your thoughts, preferences, and satisfaction with our online store. Your responses will help us improve our services and better serve you in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, simply fill out each field below with as much or as little information as you like.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as they will help us understand your shopping experience better.
3. You can type as much or as little as you need to answer each question.
4. If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us at [contact email].

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **order_details (Required: False)**: Describe your recent or upcoming purchase, including the product(s) you're interested in or have purchased.
* **product_info (Required: False)**: If you've purchased a product, tell us about your experience with it.
* **purchase_info (Required: False)**: Share your purchase history or any relevant information about your shopping behavior.
* **satisfaction (Required: False)**: Rate your satisfaction with our online store, product, or service on a scale of 1-5.
* **demographic (Required: False)**: Share your demographics, such as your location, occupation, or any other relevant information.
* **store_info (Required: False)**: Tell us about your shopping experience with our store, including any feedback or suggestions.
