# state_football_championship_t_shirt_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The state_football_championship_t_shirt_order_form is designed for team members to submit their order details for customized t-shirts. This form collects essential information such as name, contact details, and order specifics to ensure accurate and timely order fulfillment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email address in the Email field in the correct format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Team Name as it appears on the t-shirt design.
6. Select the Championship Year when your team won the state championship.
7. Choose your T Shirt Size from the available options (S, M, L, XL, XXL).
8. Select the desired Color for your t-shirt order (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, White).
9. Enter the quantity of t-shirts you'd like to order in the Quantity field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number without symbols or spaces.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter your team's name as it appears on the t-shirt design.
* **Championship Year** (`team_championship_year`, number, required): Select the year when your team won the championship.
* **T Shirt Size** (`team_tshirt_size`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred t-shirt size from the available options (S, M, L, XL, XXL).
* **Color** (`order_color`, select_multiple, required): Select the color for your t-shirt order (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, White).
* **Quantity** (`order_quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of t-shirts you'd like to order.
