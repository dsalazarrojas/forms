# paypal_meal_kit_delivery_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users regarding their meal kit delivery subscription preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* User Email
	* Meal Type (Select one or more options)
	* Delivery Frequency (Select one option)
	* Subscription Type (Select one option)
	* Payment Method (Select one option)
	* Payment Cycle (Select one option)
	* Payment Frequency (Select one option)
	* Subscription Status (Select one option)
2. Optionally, fill in the following fields:
	* User Phone
	* User Address
	* User City
	* User State
	* User Zip
	* User Country
	* Cancellation Reason (if applicable)
	* Cancellation Date (if applicable)
	* Notes (if applicable)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`1`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **User Phone** (`2`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **User Address** (`3`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **User City** (`4`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **User State** (`5`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **User Zip** (`6`, text, optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **User Country** (`7`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **Meal Type** (`8`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more meal types you are interested in (Meal Kit, Food Box, Grocery Box, Other).
* **Delivery Frequency** (`9`, select_one, optional): Select how often you want to receive your meal kit delivery (Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Start Date** (`10`, date, optional): Select the start date of your subscription.
* **End Date** (`11`, date, optional): Select the end date of your subscription.
* **Subscription Type** (`12`, select_one, optional): Select the type of subscription you want to have (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, Weekly).
* **Payment Method** (`13`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method (PayPal, Credit/Debit, Cash).
* **Payment Cycle** (`14`, select_one, optional): Select the payment cycle for your subscription (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Payment Frequency** (`15`, select_one, optional): Select the payment frequency for your subscription (One Time, Monthly, Bi-Monthly).
* **Subscription Status** (`16`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of your subscription (Active, Inactive, Trial).
* **Cancellation Reason** (`17`, text, optional): Provide a reason for cancellation if applicable.
* **Cancellation Date** (`18`, date, optional): Enter the date of cancellation if applicable.
* **Payment Status** (`19`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your payment (Paid, Pending, Unpaid).
* **Notes** (`20`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your subscription.
