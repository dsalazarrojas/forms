# KYC Verification Form For Payment Processing - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect Know Your Customer (KYC) information from merchants to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and to facilitate smooth onboarding onto the payment platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions carefully.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Merchant Business Details (optional)**: This section is meant to provide business information. Please fill out this section if you are a business owner or representative.
* **Address (optional)**: This is the address of your business. Please provide the exact address where your business is located.
* **Business Documents (optional)**: Upload scanned copies of your business documents here. Please ensure the documents are clear and readable.
* **Business License (optional)**: Upload a photo or scan of your business license here. Please ensure the license is clear and readable.
* **Tax ID (optional)**: Upload a copy of your tax ID or passport here. Please ensure the ID is clear and readable.
* **Bank Details (optional)**: This section is meant to provide banking information. Please fill out this section if you are using a bank account for payment processing.
* **Bank Name (optional)**: This is the name of your bank. Please provide the exact name of your bank.
* **Bank Account Number (optional)**: This is the account number of your bank account. Please provide the exact account number.
* **Account Holder Name (optional)**: This is the name of the account holder. Please provide the exact name of the account holder.
* **Account Holder Phone (optional)**: This is the phone number of the account holder. Please provide the exact phone number.
* **Account Holder Email (optional)**: This is the email of the account holder. Please provide the exact email address.
* **Account Holder Address (optional)**: This is the address of the account holder. Please provide the exact address.
* **Account Holder ID (optional)**: This is the ID of the account holder. Please provide the exact ID number.
* **Account Holder Date Of Birth (optional)**: This is the date of birth of the account holder. Please provide the exact date of birth.
* **Account Holder Date Of Termination (optional)**: This is the date of termination of the account holder. Please provide the exact date of termination.
* **Account Holder Date Of Employment (optional)**: This is the date of employment of the account holder. Please provide the exact date of employment.
* **Account Holder Date Of Termination Ending (optional)**: This is the date of termination ending of the account holder. Please provide the exact date of termination ending.
* **Account Holder Date Of Last Transaction (optional)**: This is the date of last transaction of the account holder. Please provide the exact date of last transaction.

## Tips

- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Use clear and readable scans or photos for business documents and IDs.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
- The information collected will be used for payment processing purposes only.
- Any incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to delays or rejections in the onboarding process.
