# Merchant Services Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for merchant services application. It captures essential details about the merchant, their contact information, services requested, and submission data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Merchant Address**: This field is for the merchant's address information.
* **Contact Name**: This field is for the contact name of the merchant.
* **Email**: This field is for the merchant's email address.
* **Fax**: This field is for the merchant's fax number (optional).
* **Date**: This field is for the date of submission.
* **Time**: This field is for the time of submission.
* **Merchant Services**: This field is for selecting the services the merchant is applying for.
* **Merchant Services Description**: This field is for selecting additional services the merchant is applying for.
* **Submitted By**: This field is for the contact email of the submitter.
* **Submitted Date**: This field is for the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time**: This field is for the time of submission.
* **Merchant**: This field is for selecting the merchant type.
* **Merchant Address 2**: This field is for the merchant's address.
* **Contact Phone**: This field is for the contact phone number of the merchant.
* **Contact Email**: This field is for the contact email of the merchant.
* **Submitted Fax**: This field is for the submitted fax number (optional).
* **Notes**: This field is for any additional comments or notes.
* **Submitted**: This field is for selecting whether the form has been submitted.
* **Submitted By 2**: This field is for the contact email of the submitter.
* **Submitted Date 2**: This field is for the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time 2**: This field is for the time of submission.
* **Merchant Services 2**: This field is for selecting additional services the merchant is applying for.

Note: This form requires accurate and complete information. Ensure you review each field carefully before submitting the form.
