# Online Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the "Online Order Form" for sending orders or messages to a receiver. The form is designed for sending orders or messages to a specific person or address, and allows you to select options and add a personal message.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your sender's name in the "Senders Name" field.
2. Enter your sender's email address in the "Sender Email" field.
3. Enter the receiver's name in the "Receiver Name" field.
4. Enter the receiver's address in the "Receiver Address" field.
5. Enter the receiver's phone number in the "Receiver Phone" field.
6. Select the delivery date for the order or message in the "Delivery Date" field.
7. Choose any applicable options for the order or message from the "Flowers" field.
8. Enter a personal message in the "Personal Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Senders Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the sender of the order or message.
- **Sender Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that will be used as the sender's contact information.
- **Receiver Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or entity receiving the order or message.
- **Receiver Address** (text, required): Enter the address of the person or entity receiving the order or message.
- **Receiver Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of the person or entity receiving the order or message.
- **Delivery Date** (date, required): Select the date on which the order or message is to be delivered.
- **Flowers** (select_multiple, optional): Choose any applicable options for the order or message (e.g., "Option 1", "Option 2", "Option 3").
- **Personal Message** (text, required): Enter a personal message or note to be included with the order or message.
