# Custom T Shirt Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for custom t-shirt orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the customer's information on the **Customer Info** page.
2. Choose the order details on the **Order Details** page.
3. Select the t-shirt information on the **T-Shirt Info** page.
4. Choose the payment method on the **Payment Method** page.
5. Select the design style on the **T-Shirt Design** page.
6. Choose the t-shirt size on the **T-Shirt Size** page.
7. Select the t-shirt color on the **T-Shirt Color** page.
8. Choose the t-shirt style on the **T-Shirt Style** page.
9. Choose the t-shirt material on the **T-Shirt Material** page.
10. Enter any customer notes on the **Customer Notes** page.
11. Enter any order notes on the **Order Notes** page.
12. Enter the total order amount on the **Order Total** page.
13. Enter the t-shirt price on the **T-Shirt Price** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Info (customer_info)**: Enter the customer's name and other contact information.
- **Order Details (order_details)**: Choose the order type (e.g., gift, personal use).
- **T-Shirt Info (tshirt_info)**: Enter the t-shirt information, including design, style, size, and color.
- **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
- **T-Shirt Design (tshirt_design)**: Choose the t-shirt design (e.g., Red T-Shirt, Blue T-Shirt).
- **T-Shirt Size (tshirt_size)**: Choose the t-shirt size (e.g., Small, Medium, Large).
- **T-Shirt Color (tshirt_color)**: Choose the t-shirt color (e.g., Red, Blue, White).
- **T-Shirt Style (tshirt_style)**: Choose the t-shirt style (e.g., Classic, Crew, Hoodie).
- **T-Shirt Material (tshirt_material)**: Choose the t-shirt material (e.g., Cotton, Polyester, Blend).
- **Customer Notes (customer_notes)**: Enter any additional notes from the customer.
- **Order Notes (order_notes)**: Enter any notes for the order.
- **Order Total (order_total)**: Enter the total order amount.
- **T-Shirt Price (tshirt_price)**: Enter the price of the t-shirt.
