# E Wallet Merchant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The E Wallet Merchant Application Form is designed for merchants to apply for services or partnerships with E Wallet. This form collects basic contact information to facilitate the application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your merchant name in the "Merchant Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your password (note: this field might not be necessary, but please fill it in for consistency).
4. Enter your address line 1 in the "Address Line 1" field.
5. Enter your city in the "City" field.
6. Enter your state in the "State" field.
7. Select your country from the "Country" dropdown list.
8. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Merchant Name (Merchant Name)**: Enter your business name as it appears on your website or other official documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your official email address associated with your merchant business.
* **Password (Password)**: We're not sure why this field is included, as it's not necessary for merchant applications. Please feel free to fill it in, but it's not a required field.
* **Address Line 1 (Address Line 1)**: Enter your business address, including the number and street name.
* **City (City)**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State (State)**: Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **Country (Country)**: Select the country where your business is based.
* **Postal Code (Postal Code)**: Enter your business's postal or zip code.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out this form, contact E Wallet's support team.
- Please review your application carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
