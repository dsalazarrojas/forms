# PayPal Business Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a business product order from customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the product name in the **Product Name** field.
2. Enter the product price in the **Product Price** field. Please use a numerical value only.
3. Enter the purchase quantity in the **Purchase Quantity** field.
4. Select the order date in the **Order Date** field. Use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Select the order time in the **Order Time** field. Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
6. Enter the customer's email address in the **Customer Email** field.
7. Enter the customer's phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
8. Enter any additional order notes in the **Order Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the product being ordered.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required: false): Enter the price of the product.
* **Purchase Quantity** (`purchase_quantity`, number, required: false): Enter the quantity of the product being ordered.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the order in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the order in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Order Notes** (`order_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the order.
