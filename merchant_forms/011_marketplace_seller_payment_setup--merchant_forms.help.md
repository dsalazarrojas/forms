# Marketplace Seller Payment Setup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketplace Seller Payment Setup form is used to configure payment settings for marketplace sellers. It collects information about the seller's preferred payout frequency, payment methods, bank account details, and agreement options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form's pages and sections to understand the different categories of information being requested.
2. Fill out the required fields and select your preferred payout frequency, payment methods, and agreement options.
3. Enter your bank account details, including account holder name, account number, and routing number.
4. Verify your email and phone numbers for communication purposes.
5. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the setup process.
