# craft_fair_vendor_survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from vendors about their experience with your craft fair, including their name, email address, phone number, and feedback about the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your experience with our craft fair, including any feedback or suggestions you may have.
3. Share why you are interested in participating in our craft fair.
4. Share any improvements or changes you would like to see in the event.
5. Outline your expectations for our next event.
6. Provide feedback and suggestions for future events.
7. Recommend any events or activities that you think would be beneficial to our attendees.
8. Share your next event plan or follow-up schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Vendor Experience** (`vendor_experience`, text, required: false): Share your experience with our craft fair.
* **Vendor Interest** (`vendor_interest`, text, required: false): Share why you are interested in participating in our craft fair.
* **Why Craft Fair** (`why_craft_fair`, text, required: false): This field is for providing additional information about why you are interested in participating in our craft fair.
* **Vendor Improvement** (`vendor_improvement`, text, required: false): Share any improvements or suggestions you may have for the event.
* **Vendor Expectations** (`vendor_expectations`, text, required: false): Outline your expectations for our next event.
* **Vendor Feedback** (`vendor_feedback`, text, required: false): Provide feedback about the event.
* **Vendor Suggestions** (`vendor_suggestions`, text, required: false): Suggest any events or activities that you think would benefit our attendees.
* **Vendor Recommendation** (`vendor_recommend`, text, required: false): Recommend any events or activities that you think would benefit our attendees.
* **Vendor Implementation** (`vendor_implement`, text, required: false): This field is for sharing information about how you plan to implement your idea or suggestion.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, text, required: false): Provide a follow-up schedule or plan for next event.
* **Next Event** (`next_event`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your next event or meeting.
