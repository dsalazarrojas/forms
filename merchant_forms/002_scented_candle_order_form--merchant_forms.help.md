# scented_candle_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users for a scented candle order. It includes fields for the user's first name, last name, address, email address, phone number, desired scents, quantity of scented candles, and total cost.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field. Please use a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
6. Select one or more scents from the **Scent** dropdown menu.
7. Enter the quantity of scented candles you wish to order in the **Quantity** field.
8. Enter the total cost of your order in the **Total cost** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Scent** (`scents`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more scents from the menu:
	+ Lavender
	+ Vanilla
	+ Eucalyptus
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the number of scented candles you wish to order.
* **Total cost** (`total_cost`, number, required): Enter the total cost of your order.
