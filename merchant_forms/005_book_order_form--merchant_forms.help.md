## Purpose

The Book Order Form is designed to gather essential information from customers to process book orders efficiently. It includes fields for customer contact details, book specifications, shipping, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter your Customer Information**: Fill in your full name and email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
2. **Select the Book Details**: Choose the title and author of the book you wish to order.
3. **Specify the Book Edition and Quantity**: Select the edition type (Hardcover, Paperback, E-book, or Audiobook) and the quantity of copies you desire.
4. **Set Shipping Preferences**: Choose your preferred shipping method (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight Delivery, or Local Pickup).
5. **Choose a Payment Method**: Select how you plan to pay for your order (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the buyer.
* **Email Address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive order updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Provide a contact number for any further order updates or inquiries.
* **Book Title** (`book_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the book you wish to order.
* **Author Name** (`book_author`, text, required): Enter the author of the book you have chosen.
* **ISBN Number** (`isbn_number`, text, optional): If known, provide the ISBN to ensure correct edition selection.
* **Edition Type** (`edition_type`, select_one, required): Select the book edition type (Hardcover, Paperback, E-book, or Audiobook).
* **Quantity to Order** (`quantity`, number, required): Specify the number of copies you wish to order.
* **Unit Price** (`unit_price`, number, required): Enter the price per copy of the book.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Provide your full shipping address for accurate delivery.
* **Shipping Method** (`shipping_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred shipping method (Standard Shipping, Express Shipping, Overnight Delivery, or Local Pickup).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, optional): Include any special requests or notes about your order.
