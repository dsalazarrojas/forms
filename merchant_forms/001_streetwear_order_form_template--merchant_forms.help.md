# Streetwear Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Streetwear Order Form Template is a structured form designed for merchants to process streetwear orders efficiently. This template collects essential customer and order information, including customer details, order date and time, order items, payment method, and more.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Customer Details** section with the customer's name and other relevant information.
2. Provide the customer's address in the **Customer Address** section.
3. Enter the order date and time in the **Order Date** and **Order Time** sections, respectively.
4. Select the ordered items from the list in the **Order Items** section.
5. Enter the customer's phone number and email in the **Customer Phone** and **Customer Email** sections, respectively.
6. Add any additional notes about the order in the **Order Notes** section.
7. Enter the merchant's notes about the order in the **Merchant Notes** section.
8. Select the payment method from the **Payment Method** section.
9. Enter the total amount of the order in the **Total Amount** section.
10. Select the assigned staff member to handle the order from the **Assigned To** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Details**: Enter the customer's name, ensuring it is spelled correctly.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's full address.
* **Order Date**: Select the date of the order.
* **Order Time**: Select the time of the order.
* **Order Items**: Select the items ordered from the list.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Order Notes**: Add any additional notes about the order.
* **Merchant Notes**: Add any notes from the merchant about the order.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the order (Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount of the order.
* **Assigned To**: Select the staff member assigned to handle the order.

## Tips
* Double-check the customer details and address for accuracy.
* Ensure the order date and time are correct.
* Be thorough when selecting order items.
* Verify the customer's phone number and email address.
* Add any relevant merchant notes for reference.
* Select the correct payment method and total amount.
* Assign the order to the correct staff member.
