# Tea Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help customers order their chosen tea kits from our business. It collects necessary information to process and fulfill orders accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your details.
2. Select your preferred tea kit options from the tea kit preferences section.
3. Choose your shipping preferences to ensure timely delivery.
4. Provide your payment information to complete the order.
5. Review your order to ensure accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Info (id: 1)** (`<type>: text`): Enter your name and contact details in this section.
* **Tea Kit Preferences (id: 2)** (`<type>: text`): Select your preferred tea kit type, quantity, and any additional options.
* **Shipping Preferences (id: 3)** (`<type>: text`): Choose your shipping method, delivery date, and any specific instructions.
* **Payment Info (id: 4)** (`<type>: text`): Enter your payment method and details to complete the order.
* **Order Review (id: 5)** (`<type>: text`): Review your order to ensure accuracy before submitting it.
* **Tea Kits Ordered (id: 6)** (`<type>: text`): This section is not applicable as it's currently a required field but doesn't serve a practical purpose; please ignore it.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in processing your order.
- Double-check your order review section before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
