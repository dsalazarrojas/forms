# Paymentwall Product Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Paymentwall Product Order Form is a tool for merchants to provide necessary information about their products, users, and payment methods. This form is required for listing products on the Paymentwall platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all the required fields (none required for this form, but please fill in all relevant fields).
2. Provide accurate and complete information about your product, user, shipping details, payment method, order details, total cost, payment amount, and merchant info.
3. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Paymentwall Product Info** (`product_info`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide detailed information about your product, including its name, description, and any other relevant details. Please type in a clear and concise description of your product.
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide information about the user, including their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Shipping Info** (`shipping_info`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide information about the shipping method, cost, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to specify whether or not your product will have a payment method associated with it. Please select "Yes" if you plan to have a payment method, or "No" if you do not.
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide more detailed information about your order, such as the product quantity and any specific instructions.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, `number`, optional): This field is used to specify the total cost of your product.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, optional): This field is used to specify the payment amount for your product.
* **Merchant Info** (`merchant_info`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide information about your merchant business, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language in your descriptions.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
