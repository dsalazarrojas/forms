# Candy Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a candy order, allowing customers to provide their details and preferences for ordering candies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**.
2. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
3. Enter your **Billing Address**, including **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code**.
4. Select the candy items you'd like to order, choosing the desired quantity for each item.
5. Choose your **Shipping Address**, including **City**, **State**, and **ZIP Code**.
6. Select the **Shipping Method** that best suits your needs.
7. Choose your **Payment Method**.
8. If applicable, enter a **Promo Code** for any discount.
9. If you have any **Special Requests**, feel free to mention them.
10. Accept the **Terms and Conditions**.
11. Finally, choose an **Order Date** for your candy order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (`customer_first_name`)

* This is the form's first field, where you should enter your given name.

### Last Name (`customer_last_name`)

* Enter your family name here.

### Email Address (`customer_email`)

* Provide your email address for order confirmation purposes.

### Phone Number (`customer_phone`)

* Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Billing Address (`billing_address`)

* Enter your full billing address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.

### City (`billing_city`)

* Enter the city of your billing address.

### State (`billing_state`)

* Enter the state or province of your billing address.

### ZIP Code (`billing_zip`)

* Enter the postal code of your billing address.

### Shipping Address (`shipping_address`)

* Enter your full shipping address, including street, city, state, and ZIP code.

### Shipping City (`shipping_city`)

* Enter the city of your shipping address.

### Shipping State (`shipping_state`)

* Enter the state or province of your shipping address.

### Shipping ZIP Code (`shipping_zip`)

* Enter the postal code of your shipping address.

### First Candy Item (`candy_item_1`)

* Select the first candy item you'd like to order from the given options.

### Quantity for First Item (`quantity_item_1`)

* Enter the quantity of the first candy item you'd like to order.

### Second Candy Item (`candy_item_2`)

* Select the second candy item you'd like to order from the given options. If no preference, select "None".

### Quantity for Second Item (`quantity_item_2`)

* Enter the quantity of the second candy item you'd like to order. This field is optional if no second item is selected.

### Third Candy Item (`candy_item_3`)

* Select the third candy item you'd like to order from the given options. If no preference, select "None".

### Quantity for Third Item (`quantity_item_3`)

* Enter the quantity of the third candy item you'd like to order. This field is optional if no third item is selected.

### Gift Wrapping Option (`gift_wrapping`)

* Choose whether you'd like to have your order gift-wrapped (standard or premium box).

### Gift Message (`gift_message`)

* If you'd like to include a gift message, enter it here.

### Shipping Method (`shipping_method`)

* Select your preferred shipping method (Standard, Expedited, or Express).

### Delivery Instructions (`delivery_instructions`)

* If you have any special delivery instructions, mention them here.

### Payment Method (`payment_method`)

* Choose your preferred payment method (Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, or Mobile Pay).

### Promo Code (`promo_code`)

* If you have a promo code, enter it here.

### Special Requests (`special_requests`)

* If you have any special requests, feel free to mention them here.

### Terms and Conditions Accepted (`terms_accepted`)

* Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

### Order Date (`order_date`)

* Select the date of your order.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your details for accuracy.
* Be specific with your shipping and billing addresses.
* Choose your preferred shipping method and payment method.
* If you have any special requests, be detailed and clear.
