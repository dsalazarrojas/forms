# Refund Receipt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Refund Receipt Form is used by merchants to process refunds for customers. It captures key information about the customer, merchant, refund amount, and refund status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's email and phone number to ensure accurate communication.
2. Enter the refund amount and select the refund method.
3. Choose the refund status and select the payment status.
4. Enter any relevant merchant comments or customer feedback.
5. Enter the customer's order ID for reference.
6. Select the payment gateway used.
7. Enter the customer's IP address (if available).
8. Enter the merchant's name and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Refund Receipt Form** (`merchant_forms_refund_receipt_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Customer Refund Amount** (`customer_refund_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount to be refunded to the customer.
* **Merchant Refund Amount** (`merchant_refund_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount refunded to the customer.
* **Refund Date** (`refund_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the refund.
* **Refund Method** (`customer_refund_method`, select_one, optional): Select whether the refund was made by the customer or not.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the refund reason.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Merchant Notes** (`merchant_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the merchant.
* **Refund Status** (`refund_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the refund (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Refund Amount** (`refund_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total refund amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select whether the payment was made (Yes or No).
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the payment (Active or Inactive).
* **Merchant Comments** (`merchant_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments from the merchant.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address.
* **Refund ID** (`refund_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique refund ID.
* **Merchant Name** (`merchant_name`, text, optional): Enter the merchant's name.
* **Customer Order ID** (`customer_order_id`, number, optional): Enter the customer's order ID.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, optional): Select the payment gateway used (Yes or No).
* **Customer IP** (`customer_ip`, text, optional): Enter the customer's IP address.
* **Refund ID** (`refund_id`, number, optional): Enter a unique refund ID.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if they are not applicable.
