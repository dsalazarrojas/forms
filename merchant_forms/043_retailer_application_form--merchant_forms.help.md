# Retailer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from retailers for various business purposes. It is intended for individual use and may create a record in your account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have the necessary information to provide accurate and complete answers.
2. For fields marked as `text`, type in your answer using the provided field.
3. For the `Category A`, `Category B`, and `Category C` field (id: 13), select your products from the dropdown options.
4. Answer any remaining fields (id: 14-25) as needed and according to the provided instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Details (id: 1)**: This section is for providing basic information about your application.
* **Company Details (id: 2)**: Enter your company's name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Info (id: 3)**: Provide your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Products (id: 4)**: Describe the products you offer or wish to offer.
* **Shipping (id: 5)**: Explain your shipping policies and procedures.
* **Payment Info (id: 6)**: Describe your payment terms and procedures.
* **Tax Info (id: 7)**: Provide your tax information and any necessary tax-related details.
* **Store Info (id: 8)**: Enter your store's name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Store Policies (id: 9)**: Outline your store's policies and procedures.
* **Store Setup (id: 10)**: Describe your store's setup and layout.
* **Review (id: 11)**: Provide a review of your store's performance or any other relevant information.
* **Terms & Conditions (id: 12)**: Check the box to agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Category A, B, C (id: 13)**: Select your products from the dropdown options.
* **Store Address (id: 14)**: Enter your store's address.
* **Store Hours (id: 15)**: Enter your store's operating hours.
* **Store Phone (id: 16)**: Enter your store's phone number.
* **Store Email (id: 17)**: Enter your store's email address.
* **Store Url (id: 18)**: Enter your store's website URL.
* **Store Description (id: 19)**: Describe your store and its offerings.
* **Store Facebook (id: 20)**: Enter your store's Facebook URL.
* **Store Instagram (id: 21)**: Enter your store's Instagram URL.
* **Store Twitter (id: 22)**: Enter your store's Twitter URL.
* **Store YouTube (id: 23)**: Enter your store's YouTube URL.
* **Store Pinterest (id: 24)**: Enter your store's Pinterest URL.
* **Store Notes (id: 25)**: Enter any additional notes about your store.

## Tips

* Be accurate and truthful in your responses.
* Double-check your answers for spelling and formatting errors.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, please contact us for clarification.
* Please do not submit incomplete or inaccurate information.
