# Printing Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Printing Quotation Request Form is a tool used by merchants to submit their printing needs. It helps our team in understanding their requirements and provide a quotation for the requested printing job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `First Name` and `Last Name` fields with your contact details.
2. Enter your `Email` address to ensure we can contact you with the quotation.
3. Provide your `Address` to ensure we can deliver the printed materials to you or to the specified location.
4. Choose the desired `Paper Size` (e.g., A4, Letter, Legal, or Tabloid) from the options provided.
5. Select the desired `Paper Orientation` (e.g., Portrait or Landscape) from the options provided.
6. Enter the `Quantity` of printed materials required.
7. Select the color(s) needed for the printing (Black, White, or Color).
8. Provide `Additional Notes` about the printing job, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name here.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name here.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with the quotation.
- **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address for delivery purposes, if applicable.
- **Paper Size** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following: A4, Letter, Legal, or Tabloid.
- **Paper Orientation** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following: Portrait or Landscape.
- **Quantity** (number, required): Enter the quantity of printed materials needed.
- **Color** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following: Black, White, or Color.
- **Additional Notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the printing job.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information and printing specifics for accuracy.
- Ensure the correct paper size and orientation are selected.
- Provide a valid quantity of printed materials.
- Clarify any special requirements or conditions for the printing job.
