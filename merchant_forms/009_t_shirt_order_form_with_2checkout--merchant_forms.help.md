# t_shirt_order_form_with_2checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to place an order for a t-shirt. It gathers information needed to fulfill the order, including personal details, product preferences, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as prompted.
2. Review and submit the form to complete your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name for your order details.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name for your order details.
* **Address** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address so we can ship your order to the correct location.
* **Shirt Size** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the size of t-shirt you would like to purchase (S, M, L, or XL).
* **Shirt Color** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the color of the t-shirt you would like to purchase (red, blue, white, or gray).
* **Quantity** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of t-shirts you would like to order. Please note that this field is optional, as the default is one t-shirt.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you with order updates.
* **Phone Number** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any issues with your order.
* **T Shirt Image** (`9`, `text`, required: false): This field is currently not applicable. It might be used for a future development.
* **T Shirt Type** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of t-shirt you would like to purchase (graphic, athletic, crew, or v-neck).
* **Payment Method** (`11`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your preferred payment method (credit card, PayPal, bank transfer, or Apple Pay).
* **Date of Birth** (`12`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy for identification purposes.
* **Time of Birth** (`13`, `time`, required: false): This field is currently not applicable. It might be used for a future development.
* **Order Comments** (`14`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes for your order.
