# sample_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from merchants for a sample request. It is not a mandatory form, but it helps us understand the merchant's needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Business Name field with the name of your business.
2. Enter your contact email in the Contact Email field.
3. Select one or more options from the Product or Service field.
4. Enter the Request Date in the Request Date field.
5. Select the Request Time in the Request Time field.
6. Provide a short message in the Message field.
7. Choose who the request should be assigned to in the Assigned To field.
8. Optionally, you can fill in the Merchant ID field with a number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Fill in the name of your business.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email.
* **Product or Service** (`product_or_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more from the options provided:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the request.
* **Request Time** (`request_time`, time, optional): Select the time for the request.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Provide a short message.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose who the request should be assigned to:
	+ Chatjimmy
	+ John
	+ Jane
* **Merchant ID** (`merchant_id`, number, optional): Optionally, fill in a number for the Merchant ID.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is filled out correctly and completely.
* If you need help or clarification, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
