# laundry_service_online_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "laundry_service_online_order_form" is designed to collect detailed information from customers to process their online orders efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by selecting the type of service required: **Service Type**. Choose from **Wash & Fold**, **Dry Clean**, or **Hand Wash**.
2.  Enter the **Laundry Day** for the service. This is the date when the customer wants the laundry service to be executed.
3.  Select a **Pickup Service** option, if applicable.
4.  Choose a **Delivery Service** option, if applicable.
5.  Enter any **Special Inquiries** you may have.
6.  Fill out the contact information: **Contact Person**, **Contact Email**, and **Contact Phone**.
7.  Enter the customer's information: **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone**.
8.  Fill out the **Service Address** field with the location where the laundry service will be performed.
9.  Enter any **Customer Instructions** if required.
10. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and efficiency in processing the order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`id: 1`): A brief description of the order for reference. This field is not visible on the form.
* **Service Type** (`id: 2`): Select the type of laundry service required: **Wash & Fold**, **Dry Clean**, or **Hand Wash**.
* **Laundry Day** (`id: 3`): Enter the date for the laundry service.
* **Laundry Time** (`id: 4`): Enter the time for the laundry service. This field is not required.
* **Laundry Address** (`id: 5`): Enter the location where the laundry service will be performed.
* **Order Comments** (`id: 6`): Enter any additional comments about the order.
* **Pickup Service** (`id: 7`): Select if you want to arrange for a pickup service.
* **Delivery Service** (`id: 8`): Select if you want to arrange for a delivery service.
* **Special Inquiries** (`id: 9`): Enter any special inquiries or requests.
* **Contact Person** (`id: 10`): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 11`): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 12`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Customer Name** (`id: 13`): Enter your name.
* **Customer Email** (`id: 14`): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (`id: 15`): Enter your phone number.
* **Service Address** (`id: 16`): Enter the address where the service will be performed.
* **Customer Instructions** (`id: 17`): Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Customer Notes** (`id: 18`): Enter any additional notes about the order.
* **Laundry Service** (`id: 19`): Enter the laundry service required.
* **Laundry Type** (`id: 20`): Enter the type of laundry required.
* **Delivery Note** (`id: 21`): Enter any delivery notes.
* **Special Instructions** (`id: 22`): Enter any special instructions for the order.
* **Pickup Location Note** (`id: 23`): Enter any pickup location notes.
* **Delivery Date** (`id: 24`): Enter the date for the delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (`id: 25`): Enter the time for the delivery.
