# healthy-and-affordable-food-product-order-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from customers for healthy and affordable food product orders. This form will help the customer to choose their preferred items and make any special requests, as well as provide their contact information and delivery date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer name in the "customer_name" field.
2. Select the items you are interested in purchasing from the "select_an_item" field.
3. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "special_request" field.
4. Choose the delivery date for your order in the "delivery_date" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Customer Name (`customer_name`)
Customer name is the name of the person placing the order. Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.

### 2. Select an Item (order_items)
Select the items you would like to purchase from the available options:

* Item 1
* Item 2
* Item 3

### 3. Special Request (special_request)
If you have any special requests, such as modifications to your order, or instructions for delivery, please enter them here. 

### 4. Delivery Date (delivery_date)
Choose the date you would like to have the order delivered. This will help us to arrange delivery on your preferred date.

## Tips

- Please select your items carefully as you will be purchasing only what you have selected.
- If you have any special requests, please be as specific as possible to ensure they are met.
- Delivery dates must be a valid date in the future.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
