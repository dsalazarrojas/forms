# Menu Tasting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Menu Tasting Form is designed to collect feedback from users on a specific menu item. This form is intended to be completed by users who have tasted the menu item and want to provide their feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the menu item you wish to provide feedback on from the "menu_item" field.
2. Rate the menu item by selecting one or multiple options from the "rating" field.
3. Provide any additional comments about your experience in the "comments" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Select the date and time of the event where you tasted the menu item from the "event_date" and "event_time" fields, if applicable.
7. Enter the location of the event where you tasted the menu item in the "event_location" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **menu_item** (select_one): Select the menu item you wish to provide feedback on from the dropdown menu.
* **rating** (select_multiple): Rate the menu item by selecting one or multiple options from the list.
* **comments** (note): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **event_date** (date): Select the date you tasted the menu item. This field is only required if you are providing feedback for an event.
* **event_time** (time): Select the time you tasted the menu item. This field is only required if you are providing feedback for an event.
* **event_location** (text): Enter the location where you tasted the menu item. This field is only required if you are providing feedback for an event.
