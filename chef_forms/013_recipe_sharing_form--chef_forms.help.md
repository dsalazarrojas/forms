# Recipe Sharing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to share their recipes, including title, ingredients, cooking method, preparation time, cooking time, and servings. The form also allows users to add tags to make the recipe more discoverable.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter a title for your recipe in the "Recipe Title" field.
2. Select the ingredients used in your recipe from the "List of Ingredients" field.
3. Enter the cooking instructions for your recipe in the "Cooking Instructions" field.
4. Choose the cooking method for your recipe from the "Cooking Method" field.
5. Enter the preparation time for your recipe in the "Prep Time (minutes)" field.
6. Enter the cooking time for your recipe in the "Cook Time (minutes)" field.
7. Enter the number of servings for your recipe in the "Servings" field.
8. Enter the serving size for your recipe in the "Serving Size" field.
9. You can optionally select tags for your recipe from the "Tags" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Recipe Title** (1, text, required): Enter a title for your recipe that accurately describes it.
* **List of Ingredients** (2, select_multiple, required): Select all the ingredients used in your recipe from the provided options.
* **Cooking Instructions** (3, text, required): Enter a detailed step-by-step guide on how to cook your recipe.
* **Cooking Method** (4, select_one, required): Choose the method used to cook your recipe, e.g., baking, boiling, grilling, etc.
* **Prep Time (minutes)** (5, number, required): Enter the preparation time for your recipe in minutes.
* **Cook Time (minutes)** (6, number, required): Enter the cooking time for your recipe in minutes.
* **Servings** (7, number, required): Enter the number of servings for your recipe.
* **Serving Size** (8, text, required): Enter the serving size for your recipe, e.g., per serving.
* **Tags** (9, select_multiple, optional): Select tags for your recipe to make it more discoverable, e.g., dessert, vegan, gluten-free, etc.
* **Tags** (10, select_one, optional): This field is a duplicate of "Tags" field (9) and is likely a leftover from a previous design iteration.
* **Tags 0, Tags 1, Tags 2, Tags 3** (11, 12, 13, 14, select_multiple, optional): These fields are also duplicates of "Tags" field (9) and are likely a leftover from a previous design iteration.

Note that fields 10, 11, 12, and 13 are redundant and can be removed or merged into a single "Tags" field.
