# Cooking Workshop Registration Form PayPal Standard - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users register for a cooking workshop. Fill out the form with your details to sign up for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the workshop date from the "Workshop Date" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your payment method by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
7. Specify the workshop duration in the "Workshop Duration" field.
8. Choose the workshop time in the "Workshop Time" field.
9. Select the workshop location by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Workshop Location" field.
10. Optionally, enter any additional notes in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Workshop Date** (date, required): Select the date of the workshop you are interested in.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Standard** (select one, required): Choose the standard payment option.
* **Payment Method** (select multiple, required): Choose how you would like to pay for the workshop ("Yes" or "No").
* **Note** (note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your workshop registration.
* **Payment Amount** (number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Workshop Duration** (number, required): Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (time, required): Choose the time for the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (select one, required): Choose the workshop location.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information to ensure we can contact you about the workshop.
* If you have any special requests or needs, feel free to enter them in the "Note" field.
* Please note that you can only register for one workshop at a time.
* We will contact you about payment information and workshop details once you have submitted the form.
