# Culinary Technique Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Technique Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the performance of a specific culinary technique.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Enter the technique name in the "Technique Name" field.
* Provide a brief description of the technique in the "Technique Description" field.
* Rate the technique's score out of an unspecified range (likely 1-10 or 1-9) in the "Score" field.
* Evaluate the performance level of the technique for 9 different aspects ( Performance Levels 1-9) using the "Performance Level" fields.
* Add any additional comments about the technique in the "Technique Comment" field.
* Enter the date and time of the evaluation in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which is likely for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technique Name**: Enter the name of the culinary technique being evaluated.
* **Technique Description**: Provide a brief description of the technique, including its purpose and any relevant details.
* **Score**: Rate the technique's performance out of an unspecified range, likely between 1 and 10.
* **Performance Level 1-9**: Evaluate the performance level of the technique for each of the 9 different aspects, using the options "High" and "Low".
* **Technique Comment**: Add any additional comments or feedback about the technique.
* **Chef Comment**: Add any comments or feedback from the person evaluating the technique.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the evaluation in the format MM/dd/yyyy.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the evaluation in the format HH:mm.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
