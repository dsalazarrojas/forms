# Daily Food Temperature Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Daily Food Temperature Form is designed for food handlers and handlers of food items stored in a facility to record the temperature of food items stored in the facility. This form ensures that food temperature monitoring and logging are done consistently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Choose the relevant Food Group from the drop-down list. This will help in tracking which category of food items is being stored and monitored for temperature.
2.  Check the Food Item checkbox for every type of food being stored. Select only the items relevant to your monitoring.
3.  Enter the temperature of the food item before storage using the Temperature Before Storage field. This is the temperature of the item when it was initially stored in the facility.
4.  Record the temperature of the food item during storage in the Temperature During Storage field. This will show how the temperature has changed while the food is stored.
5.  Enter the duration for which the food item has been stored using the Storage Time field in the number of days.
6.  Record how frequently the food item's temperature has been monitored using the Monitoring Frequency field in the number of times per day.
7.  Enter any additional remarks or comments about the food item's storage conditions, using the Note field.
8.  Enter the email address for further contact in the Email field.
9.  Enter the phone number for further communication in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Food Group** (`1`): This field asks you to select the type of food being stored, to help in tracking different categories of food items.
    \- Select your relevant food group from the drop-down list.
*   **Food Item** (`2`): Check the checkboxes for all types of food items being stored and being monitored.
    \- Select only the relevant food items for the batch.
*   **Temperature Before Storage** (`3`): Enter the initial storage temperature of the food item.
    \- This will be the temperature of the food item when it was first stored.
*   **Temperature During Storage** (`4`): Record the temperature of the food item during storage.
    \- This will show the change in temperature over storage duration.
*   **Storage Time** (`5`): Enter the duration for which the food item has been stored in the number of days.
    \- Enter the number of days since the item was stored.
*   **Monitoring Frequency** (`6`): Record the frequency at which the food item's temperature has been monitored in times per day.
    \- Enter the number of times temperature checks have been done per day.
*   **Note** (`7`): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the food storage conditions.
    \- Enter any observations or comments that are relevant to the food storage process.
*   **Email** (`8`): Enter the email address for further contact or information sharing.
    \- Enter a valid email address for further communication or sharing information.
*   **Phone** (`9`): Enter the phone number for further communication.
    \- Enter a valid phone number for further contact or communication.

This guide helps users understand what to do in each step to fill the form properly.
