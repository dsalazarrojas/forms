# kids_cooking_class_allergy_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand the dietary needs of children attending the cooking class. It collects information on the child's first name, last name, date of birth, food allergies, and any medical conditions they may have. Please note that this form is required for registration, and it is intended to be filled out by a parent or guardian on behalf of the child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with the child's details.
2. Select any applicable food allergies for the child.
3. If there are any medical conditions, please specify them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the child's date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Food Allergies** (`food_allergies`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select any food allergies the child may have from the list:
	+ Peanuts
	+ Tree nuts
	+ Shellfish
	+ Milk
	+ Eggs
	+ Fish
	+ Crustacean
	+ Soy
	+ Wheat
	+ Sesame
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_condition`, `text`, **optional**): Specify any medical conditions the child may have, if any.
