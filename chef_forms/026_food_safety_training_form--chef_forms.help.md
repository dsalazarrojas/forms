# Food Safety Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Food Safety Training Form is a tool used to collect and track food safety training data for trainees. This form gathers information on trainees' food safety knowledge, areas for improvement, training hours, certification dates, and other relevant details. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and regularly to ensure the highest level of food safety standards.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the necessary information and resources before starting to fill out the form.
2.  Begin by filling out the required fields, such as trainer and trainee names, email addresses, phone numbers, and training location.
3.  Move on to collecting training data, including food safety knowledge, areas for improvement, training hours, certification dates, and training frequency.
4.  Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Trainer Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the food safety training.
    *   **Trainer Email (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter the email address of the trainer.
    *   **Trainer Phone (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter the phone number of the trainer.
*   **Trainee Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter the name of the trainee.
    *   **Trainee Email (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter the email address of the trainee.
    *   **Trainee Phone (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** Enter the phone number of the trainee.
*   **Food Safety Training History (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** A brief description of the trainee's food safety training history, including dates and topics.
    *   **Food Safety Training Form Last Filled Out (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The date of the last time the form was filled out.
    *   **Food Safety Training Form Filled Out Count (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The total number of times the form has been filled out.
*   **Food Safety Training Form Version (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The current version of the food safety training form.
    *   **Food Safety Training Form Version History (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** A history of previous versions of the food safety training form.
*   **Food Safety Knowledge (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** A score indicating the trainee's current food safety knowledge level.
*   **Areas for Improvement (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** A list of areas where the trainee needs improvement.
    *   **Areas for Improvement Last Date (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The date of the last time the trainee received feedback on areas for improvement.
*   **Date of Last Training (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The date of the last food safety training session.
*   **Time of Last Training (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The time of the last food safety training session.
*   **Training Hours (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The number of hours the trainee has completed in food safety training.
*   **Food Safety Certification Date (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The date of the trainee's food safety certification.
*   **Food Safety Training Frequency (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The frequency of the trainee's food safety training sessions.
    *   **Food Safety Training Last Date (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The date of the last food safety training session.
    *   **Food Safety Training Frequency Last Date (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The date of the last time the trainee received feedback on their food safety training frequency.
*   **Training Location (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The location of the food safety training session.
*   **Food Safety Training Form Version (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** The current version of the food safety training form.
    *   **Food Safety Training Form Version History (<name>, <type>, required/optional):** A history of previous versions of the food safety training form.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are essential to complete the form, while those marked as "optional" can be filled out at your discretion. Always check the relevant fields for more specific instructions or help tips.
