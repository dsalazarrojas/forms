# Kitchen Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kitchen Requisition Form is used to document and track kitchen requisitions, including ingredient lists, supplier information, and financial details. This form ensures accurate and efficient management of kitchen orders and helps to prevent errors in the ordering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the ingredient list for the order.
2.  Provide the name of the supplier.
3.  Enter the quantity ordered.
4.  Enter the unit price of the item.
5.  Enter any comments or notes related to the order (optional).
6.  Calculate the item total by multiplying the quantity ordered by the unit price.
7.  Enter the tax rate (e.g., 0.08 for 8% tax).
8.  Calculate the total cost by adding the item total and tax.
9.  Enter the date the order was requisitioned.
10.  Enter the date the order was received.
11.  Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
12.  Enter your email address (optional).
13.  Enter your phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ingredient List** (1, text, required): Enter the list of ingredients for the order.
* **Supplier Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the supplier from whom the items are being ordered.
* **Quantity Ordered** (3, number, required): Enter the quantity of each item being ordered.
* **Unit Price** (4, number, required): Enter the unit price of each item.
* **Comments** (5, text, optional): Add any comments or notes related to the order.
* **Item Total** (6, number, required): Calculate the item total by multiplying the quantity ordered by the unit price.
* **Tax** (7, number, required): Enter the tax rate (e.g., 0.08 for 8% tax).
* **Total Cost** (8, number, required): Calculate the total cost by adding the item total and tax.
* **Date Requisitioned** (9, date, required): Enter the date the order was requisitioned.
* **Date Received** (10, date, required): Enter the date the order was received.
* **Notes** (11, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (12, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (13, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
