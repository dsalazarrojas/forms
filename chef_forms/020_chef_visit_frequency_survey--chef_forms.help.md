# Chef Visit Frequency Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experiences with personal chef services. It will help us understand how often you visit a chef, what services you use, and how satisfied you are with the service. This feedback will help us improve our service and provide better experiences for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your details, including your name (optional) and email address (optional).
2. Select your customer type from the provided options.
3. Choose when you first hired a personal chef and how often they visit you.
4. Select how often the chef visits and how long the visit typically lasts.
5. Choose the services you have used and the types of cuisine you enjoy.
6. Rate your satisfaction with the service, food quality, and chef's professionalism.
7. Provide any additional comments or suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Your full name, which is optional.
* **Customer Type**: Choose from Homeowner, Restaurant owner, Event planner, Corporate client, Private chef client, Other, to describe the type of customer you are.
* **Email Address**: Your email address, which is optional for follow-up communications.
* **When Did Chef First Visit**: Select from options: Within the last month, 1-3 months ago, 3-6 months ago, 6-12 months ago, 1-2 years ago, Over 2 years ago.
* **How Often Does Chef Visit**: Select from options: Daily, Several times a week, Once a week, Twice a month, Once a month, Occasionally, One-time service.
* **Typical Visit Duration**: Select from options: 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, Full day, Multiple days.
* **Meals Prepared Per Visit**: Select from options: 1 meal, 2-3 meals, 4-6 meals, 7-10 meals, 10+ meals.
* **Services Used**: Select all services you have used from Meal preparation, Menu planning, Grocery shopping, Kitchen organization, Cooking lessons, Special event catering, Dietary consultation, Other.
* **Cuisine Types Requested**: Select all types of cuisine you enjoy from Italian, French, American, Asian, Mexican, Mediterranean, Indian, Fusion, Other.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Select from options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
* **Food Quality Rating**: Rate the quality of food prepared from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor.
* **Professionalism Rating**: Rate the chef's professionalism from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor.
* **Value for Money**: Select from options: Excellent value, Good value, Fair value, Poor value, Very poor value.
* **Reliability Rating**: Select from options: Always on time, Usually on time, Sometimes late, Often late, Unreliable.
* **Cleanliness Rating**: Rate the cleanliness of the chef's workspace from Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, Very poor.
* **Flexibility Rating**: Select from options: Very flexible, Somewhat flexible, Neutral, Inflexible, Very inflexible.
* **Would Recommend**: Select from options: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no.
* **Plan to Continue Service**: Select from options: Definitely yes, Probably yes, Not sure, Probably no, Definitely no.
* **Frequency Change**: Select from options: Increase frequency, Keep same, Decrease frequency, Discontinue service.
* **Best Part of the Service**: Describe what you enjoy most about the service.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Provide any feedback on how the service can be improved.
* **Additional Services Interested In**: Describe any other services you would like to see offered.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any other feedback or comments about the service.
