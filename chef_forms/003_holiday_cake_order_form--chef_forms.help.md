# holiday_cake_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers about their holiday cake order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Holiday Cake Order (1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your holiday cake order.
* **Customer Name (2)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Order Date (3)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the holiday cake order.
* **Order Time (4)** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the holiday cake order.
* **Cake Type (5)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you want a cake or not.
* **Cake Size (6)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the size of the cake you want.
* **Delivery Address (7)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the address where you want the cake to be delivered.
* **Payment Type (8)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Choose your payment method.
* **Payment Method (9)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your payment method.
* **Payment Amount (10)** (`number`, required/optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Email (11)** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (12)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please fill in the relevant information for your holiday cake order.
