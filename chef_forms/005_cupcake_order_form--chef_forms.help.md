# Cupcake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cupcake Order Form is a simple form to order a cupcake, cake, or pastry from our bakery. Please fill out the form with your order details and we will process your order as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section of the form carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Make sure to submit the form after you have filled out all the required fields.
3. If you have any questions, don't hesitate to contact our customer service team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: <label>Customer Name</label> (`<name>`, `text`, required: false) 
  This is where you can enter your name, which will be used for order confirmation and communication.

* **Order Type**: <label>Order Type</label> (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  Select the type of item you would like to order: Cupcake, Cake, or Pastry. 

* **Number of Cupcakes**: <label>Number of Cupcakes</label> (`<name>`, `number`, required: false)
  Please enter the number of cupcake you would like to order. This can be any whole number (e.g. 1, 2, 3, etc.).

* **Order Date**: <label>Order Date</label> (`<name>`, `date`, required: false)
  Please enter the date you would like to order your item. Our bakery will process the order on this date.

* **Order Time**: <label>Order Time</label> (`<name>`, `time`, required: false)
  Select the time you would like to order your item. Please note that this field is not strictly enforced, but it helps us to know when to prepare your order.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields, if any.
* If you have any special requests or instructions for your order, please let us know in the notes section.
* Our customer service team is available to help with any questions or concerns.
