# theme_cake_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The theme_cake_order_form is a form used to gather information from customers for a custom cake order. This form helps the bakery or restaurant to understand the customer's preferences and requirements for their cake order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name as a reference for your order.
2.  Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.
3.  Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.
4.  Select the date you'd like to pickup or have your order delivered.
5.  Choose the time for pickup or delivery.
6.  Add any additional notes about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as a reference for your order. This will help us identify you as the customer for your order.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your order. This will help us communicate with you about your order status and any updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you if needed.
* **Date** (`order_date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you'd like to pickup or have your order delivered.
* **Time** (`order_time`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time for pickup or delivery.
* **Notes** (`order_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your order that might help us fulfill it correctly.

## Tips

- Make sure to enter accurate information for your name and contact details.
- If you have any special requirements or requests for your order, use the "Notes" field to provide more information.
- If you have any further questions or need assistance with your order, contact us after submitting this form.
