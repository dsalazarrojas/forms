# Square Cake Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect order details from customers who want to purchase a square cake. Fill out this form to submit your order request, and our team will contact you to confirm and prepare your cake according to your specifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name** in the first field. This is where you'll enter your name as the person placing the order.
2. Enter your **Contact Email** address in the second field. This is the email we'll use to contact you if we need more information or to confirm your order details.
3. Choose a **Order Date** in the third field when you'd like to pick up or receive your square cake.
4. Select a **Order Time** for your pick-up or delivery in the fourth field.
5. Select your preferred **Cake Type** from the dropdown menu (5 options are available).
6. Choose your preferred **Cake Size** from the dropdown menu (2 options available).
7. Optionally, add a **Message** for us to know any special requirements or requests you might have.
8. For our production team, you can optionally specify the **Assigned Tool** they will use to prepare your cake.
9. You can also provide your **Customer Phone** number for us to contact you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the person placing the order.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for order details or updates.
* **Order Date** (date, optional): Choose a date when you'd like to pick up or receive your square cake.
* **Order Time** (time, optional): Select a time slot for pick-up or delivery.
* **Cake Type** (select_one, optional): Select your preferred cake type from the dropdown menu.
* **Cake Size** (select_multiple, optional): Choose your desired cake size from the dropdown menu.
* **Message** (note, optional): Add any special message or notes for our production team.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Provide the tool name assigned to your cake production.
* **Customer Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
