# Food Taste Testing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect information on a food taste test. The form is designed to gather data on whether a food product is liked or disliked, and the level of satisfaction with the taste.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Review each page of the form to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately.
3. For questions with multiple options, select all that apply.
4. Enter your email and phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further information or follow-up questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (Page 1, <type:text>, Optional): Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to the taste test.
* **Taste Test** (Page 2, <type:select_multiple>, Required): Select whether the food product was liked or disliked.
* **Rating** (Page 3, <type:select_one>, Required): Choose a rating from 1 to 5 to express your level of satisfaction with the taste.
* **Comment** (Page 4, <type:note>, Optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about the food product.
* **Email** (Page 5, <type:email>, Optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further information or follow-up questions.
* **Phone** (Page 6, <type:text>, Optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted for further information or follow-up questions.
* **First Page 2** (Page 7, <type:text>, Optional): Enter any additional information that you feel is relevant to the taste test.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when selecting the options for the taste test and rating.
* Use the comment field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the taste test.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrators for further assistance.
