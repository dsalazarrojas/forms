# Halloween Cookie Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Halloween Cookie Order Form is designed to collect customer information and preferences for a Halloween cookie order. This form helps customers choose their cookie type, quantity, and payment method, ensuring accurate and efficient order processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "customer name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "customer email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "customer phone" field.
4. Enter the customer's address in the "customer address" field.
5. Choose the desired cookie type from the "cookie type" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the desired quantity of cookies in the "cookie quantity" field.
7. Enter the total cost of the order in the "order total" field.
8. Select the preferred payment method from the "payment method" dropdown menu.
9. Enter any additional payment information in the "payment info" field.
10. Enter the delivery date in the "order date" field.
11. Enter the delivery time in the "delivery time" field.
12. Enter the delivery address in the "delivery address" field.
13. Enter the delivery city in the "delivery city" field.
14. Select the delivery state from the list of options.
15. Enter the delivery zip code in the "delivery zip" field.
16. Enter any additional delivery notes in the "delivery note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### customer_info
* **customer_info** (Text, optional): Enter any additional information about the customer.

### cookie_order
* **cookie_order** (Text, optional): Enter any additional information about the order.

### delivery_details
* **delivery_details** (Text, optional): Enter any additional information about delivery.

### payment_info
* **payment_info** (Text, optional): Enter any additional payment information.

### customer_name
* **customer name** (`customer name`, Text, optional): Enter the customer's name.

### customer_email
* **customer email** (`customer email`, Email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.

### customer_phone
* **customer phone** (Text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.

### customer_address
* **customer address** (Text, optional): Enter the customer's address.

### order_date
* **order date** (Date, optional): Enter the date the order was placed.

### delivery_date
* **delivery date** (Date, optional): Enter the date for delivery.

### delivery_time
* **delivery time** (Time, optional): Enter the time for delivery.

### cookie_type
* **cookie type** (Select One, required): Choose the type of cookie from the list.

### cookie_quantity
* **cookie quantity** (Number, optional): Enter the quantity of cookies.

### order_total
* **order total** (Number, optional): Enter the total cost of the order.

### payment_method
* **payment method** (Select One, required): Choose the payment method from the list.

### payment_info
* **payment info** (Text, optional): Enter any additional payment information.

### delivery_address
* **delivery address** (Text, optional): Enter the delivery address.

### delivery_city
* **delivery city** (Text, optional): Enter the city for delivery.

### delivery_state
* **delivery state** (Select Multiple, required): Choose the delivery state from the list.

### delivery_zip
* **delivery zip** (Text, optional): Enter the delivery zip code.

### delivery_note
* **delivery note** (Text, optional): Enter any additional delivery notes.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional, but providing the required information will help ensure accurate order processing.
