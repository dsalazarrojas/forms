# Kitchen Milk Delivery Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to request milk delivery from the kitchen.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose a delivery date and time that suits you.
2. Select the type of milk you would like to order (e.g., Whole Milk, 2% Milk, etc.).
3. Provide your full name and address so we know who to deliver the milk to.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about your order.
5. Submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Date** (id: 1, type: date): Choose the date you would like to receive your milk delivery.
* **Delivery Time** (id: 2, type: time): Select the time you would like to receive your milk delivery. 
* **Milk Type** (id: 3, type: select_one): Choose the type of milk you would like to order (e.g., Whole Milk, 2% Milk, etc.). 
* **Customer Name** (id: 4, type: text): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or driver's license. 
* **Customer Address** (id: 5, type: text): Type your address where you would like to receive the delivery. 
* **Order Details** (id: 6, type: text): Add any additional comments or instructions about your order. 
* **Customer Phone** (id: 7, type: text): Enter your contact phone number so we can confirm your delivery. 
* **Customer Email** (id: 8, type: email): Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your order. 
* **Notes** (id: 9, type: note): Add any additional notes or comments about your order. 
* **Submit** (id: 10, type: text): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click this button to submit your request. 

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields (marked as "required" in the field description).
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- We'll review your request and get back to you as soon as possible.
