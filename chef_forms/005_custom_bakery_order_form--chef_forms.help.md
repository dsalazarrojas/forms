# Custom Bakery Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Custom Bakery Order Form. The form is designed to help customers place custom orders for baked goods at a bakery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details in the "Order Details" section.
2. Select the items you want to order in the "Items Ordered" section. You can choose one or multiple items.
3. For each item, select the quantity you want to order.
4. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Request" field.
5. Choose a delivery time in the "Delivery Time" section.
6. If you have any additional comments or information about delivery, enter it in the "Delivery Time" section.
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
8. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
9. Finally, enter your name in the "Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details (1, text, optional):** This is the main details section where you can enter any information about your custom order.
* **Items Ordered (2, select multiple, optional):** Select one or multiple items you want to order from the options provided.
* **Bagel (3, select one, optional):** Select the type of bagel you want to order.
* **Quantity (4, number, optional):** Enter the quantity you want to order for each item.
* **Special Request (5, note, optional):** This is an optional field to enter any special requests you may have for your order.
* **Delivery Time (6, date, optional):** Select the delivery time for your order.
* **Delivery Time Note (7, note, optional):** This is an optional field to enter any additional comments about delivery.
* **Contact Number (8, text, optional):** Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Email (9, email, optional):** Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Name (10, text, optional):** Enter your name for the order.

## Tips

* Please make sure to select the correct items and quantities for your order.
* If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Request" field.
* Double-check your contact information is accurate.
* The "Delivery Time" field is optional but recommended to ensure timely delivery.
* Make sure to enter your name for the order so we can contact you if needed.
