# Custom Cake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers for custom cake orders, which can be used by bakeries or food establishments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the customer's name.
2. Enter the customer's email address.
3. Enter the customer's phone number.
4. Select the desired date for the cake order.
5. Select the desired time for the cake order.
6. Choose one of the options for the flavor of cake you would like to order.
7. Choose one or more of the options for the type of cake you would like to order.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about your order.
9. Select the payment method you would like to use for the order.
10. Review and submit the form.
