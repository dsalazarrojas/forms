## Bread Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to place orders for bread from a bakery. It collects essential information such as bakery name, contact details, delivery preferences, and order specifics to ensure accurate and timely delivery of bread products.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Bakery Name" field and enter the name of your bakery.
2. Enter the "Contact Name" of the primary contact person responsible for communication.
3. Enter the "Contact Email" of the primary contact person.
4. Enter the "Contact Phone" number of the primary contact person.
5. Enter the "Delivery Address" where the bread should be delivered.
6. Select the "Delivery Frequency" (e.g., Daily, 3 Times Per Week, etc.).
7. Select the "Preferred Delivery Day" from the available options (Monday to Sunday).
8. Choose the "Preferred Delivery Time" for your convenience.
9. Select the types of bread you wish to order from the "Bread Types" options.
10. Specify the quantity of each bread type you selected.
11. Choose the "Slice Preference" (e.g., Unsliced, Thin Sliced, etc.).
12. Select the "Packaging Preference" (e.g., Individual Bags, Bulk Packaging, etc.).
13. Enter any specific "Quality Specifications" or requirements.
14. Select whether you need "Custom Labels" or not.
15. Enter any special "Storage Instructions" if needed.
16. Choose the "Payment Terms" (e.g., Pay on Delivery, Net 7, etc.).
17. Set the "Start Date" for your deliveries.
18. Finally, add any "Special Notes" or comments about your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Bakery Name**: Enter the name of your bakery to help us identify your business and ensure accurate communication.
* **Contact Name**: This is the primary contact person for order communications.
* **Contact Email**: Use this email to receive order updates and communications.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide your contact person's phone number for any questions or issues.
* **Delivery Address**: Enter the address where you want the bread delivered.
* **Delivery Frequency**: Choose how often you need deliveries (e.g., Daily, 3 Times Per Week, etc.).
* **Preferred Delivery Day**: Select the day you prefer for deliveries.
* **Preferred Delivery Time**: Choose a time that suits you best.
* **Bread Types**: Select the types of bread you wish to order from the available options.
* **Quantities**: Specify the quantity of each bread type selected.
* **Slice Preference**: Choose whether you want bread sliced or not.
* **Packaging Preference**: Select the packaging preference (e.g., Individual Bags, Bulk Packaging, etc.).
* **Quality Specifications**: Enter any specific quality requirements you have.
* **Labeling Requirements**: Choose whether you need custom labels or not.
* **Storage Instructions**: Provide any special storage instructions if required.
* **Payment Terms**: Select your preferred payment arrangement (e.g., Pay on Delivery, Net 7, etc.).
* **Start Date**: Set the start date for your deliveries.
* **Special Notes**: Enter any other information or special requests you may have about your order.
