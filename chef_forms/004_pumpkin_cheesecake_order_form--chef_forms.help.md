# Pumpkin Cheesecake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details, customer information, delivery information, and order confirmation from customers who want to place an order for a pumpkin cheesecake.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the order details in the "Order Details" section, which could include the type of cheesecake, size, and any special requests.
2. Provide your customer information in the "Customer Info" section, including your name, contact number, and any other relevant contact details.
3. Enter your delivery information in the "Delivery Info" section, which could include your address, delivery date and time, and any special instructions for the delivery process.
4. In the "Order Confirmation" section, add any additional comments or notes about your order, such as any changes to the order or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Order Details
* **Order Details**: This field is where you can specify the type of pumpkin cheesecake you want to order, including the size and any special requests. Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure we can fulfill your order accurately.
* **Customer Info**
	+ **<name>**: Please enter your name as it should be displayed on the order and any packaging.
	+ **<contact number>**: Enter your contact number so we can reach you for any updates or questions.
* **Delivery Info**
	+ **<address>**: Enter the address where you would like the cheesecake to be delivered.
	+ **<delivery date and time>**: Enter the date and time you would like to receive your order.
* **Order Confirmation**
	+ **<text>**: This is a free-text field where you can add any additional comments or notes about your order.
