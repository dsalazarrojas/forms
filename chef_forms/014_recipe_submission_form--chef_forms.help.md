# Recipe Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who want to submit their recipes to the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Recipe Submission Form** (`recipe_submission_form`): Enter the title of your recipe in this text field.
2.  **Category** (`category`): Choose a category for your recipe from the dropdown list: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, or Snack.
3.  **Cuisine** (`cuisine`): Select one or multiple cuisines that your recipe belongs to from the dropdown list: Italian, Chinese, Indian, or Thai.
4.  **Ingredients** (`ingredients`): Enter the ingredients required for your recipe in this text field.
5.  **Cooking Method** (`cooking_method`): Describe the steps to make your recipe in this text field.
6.  **Cooking Time** (`cooking_time`): Enter the cooking time for your recipe in minutes.
7.  **Serving Size** (`serving_size`): Enter the number of people this recipe will serve.
8.  **Serving Size Units** (`serving_size_units`): Choose the unit of measurement for your serving size from the dropdown list: Person, Cup, Tablespoon, or Teaspoon.
9.  **Recipe Image** (`recipe_image`): Enter the URL of the image associated with your recipe.

*   **Recipe Submission Form** (`recipe_submission_form`): Required.

*   **Ingredients** (`ingredients`): Enter the ingredients required for your recipe in this text field.
*   **Cooking Method** (`cooking_method`): Describe the steps to make your recipe in this text field.

*   **Serving Size** (`serving_size`): Enter the number of people this recipe will serve.
*   **Serving Size Units** (`serving_size_units`): Choose the unit of measurement for your serving size from the dropdown list: Person, Cup, Tablespoon, or Teaspoon.
*   **Recipe Image** (`recipe_image`): Not required, but recommended for a better user experience.
