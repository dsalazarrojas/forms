# stripe_cake_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "stripe_cake_order_form" is used to collect information from customers for ordering a custom cake with a stripe effect. This includes details about the order, customer information, payment method, and the type of cake desired.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your order details in the first field, including any special requests or instructions you may have.
2. Provide your customer information in the second field, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.
3. Enter your payment information in the third field, using the format specified.
4. Select the type of cake you would like to order from the options provided in the fourth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details** (`order_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a description of your order, including any special requests or instructions you may have.
* **customer_info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment method and details using the format specified.
* **Cake Type** (`cake_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of cake you would like to order from the options provided, which are "Yes" and "No".

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and clear information in each field to ensure efficient processing of your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our customer support team.
* Our team will review and process your order promptly, and we will contact you with any further questions or requests.
* Please note that our cake types are subject to availability, and we will inform you of any limitations or alternatives during the ordering process.
