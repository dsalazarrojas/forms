# Weekly Menu Selections Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect menu information from participating restaurants for weekly planning purposes. It helps restaurants to effectively communicate their menu offerings, enabling efficient planning and preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Review each field carefully and ensure you provide accurate information.
2. Select the correct options for each menu item.
3. Enter the restaurant's name, phone number, and email address.
4. Add any comments, if necessary.
5. Double-check your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant Name (1)**: Enter the name of your restaurant.
* **Select Menu Item 1 (2)**: Choose 'Yes' if the menu item is available and 'No' if not.
* **Select Menu Item 2 (3)**: Choose 'Yes' if the menu item is available and 'No' if not.
* **Select Menu Item 3 (4)**: Choose 'Yes' if the menu item is available and 'No' if not.
* **Comments (5)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your restaurant's menu.
* **Restaurant Phone (6)**: Enter your restaurant's phone number.
* **Restaurant E-Mail (7)**: Enter your restaurant's email address.

## Tips
* Ensure accurate and up-to-date information is provided for each menu item.
* Double-check your form before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant person for clarification.
