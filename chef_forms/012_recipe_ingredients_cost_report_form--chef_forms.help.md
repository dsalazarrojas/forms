# recipe_ingredients_cost_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a recipe's ingredients, their costs, and yield. This form is likely used to report on recipe ingredients costs for internal usage, such as inventory management or pricing decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Recipe Title**, a brief description of the recipe.
2. Select all the **Ingredients** involved in the recipe from the list.
3. Enter the **Cost** of each selected ingredient.
4. Enter the **Recipe Yield**, the total amount of the recipe's output.
5. Enter the **Cost Per Unit**, the cost per unit of each ingredient.
6. Calculate the **Total Cost**, by entering the cost of each ingredient multiplied by its unit cost.
7. Enter the **Ingredient Weight**, the weight of each ingredient used in the recipe.
8. Calculate the **Recipe Cost**, by summing up the total cost of all ingredients.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipe Title** (text, optional): Enter a brief title for the recipe.
* **Ingredients** (select multiple, optional): Select all the ingredients involved in the recipe from the list.
* **Cost** (number, optional): Enter the cost of each selected ingredient.
* **Recipe Yield** (number, optional): Enter the total amount of the recipe's output.
* **Cost Per Unit** (number, optional): Enter the cost per unit of each ingredient.
* **Total Cost** (number, optional): Calculate the total cost by multiplying the cost of each ingredient by its unit cost.
* **Ingredient Weight** (number, optional): Enter the weight of each ingredient used in the recipe.
* **Recipe Cost** (number, optional): Calculate the recipe cost by summing up the total cost of all ingredients.
* **Ingredient Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about each ingredient.
* **Submit** (select one, optional): Choose between **Submit** or **Cancel** to finalize or cancel the form.
