# Bread & Pastry Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bread & Pastry Order Form is designed to collect order information for our bread and pastry products. This form is intended for both personal and business use cases, such as catering, events, or personal consumption.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** as required contact information.
2. Provide your **Delivery Address**, including **City** and **Postal Code**, which will be used for delivery purposes.
3. Specify your **Desired Delivery Date** and **Preferred Delivery Time**.
4. Select your preferred **Bread Types** from the list.
5. Enter the **Total Bread Quantity** you would like to order.
6. If you would like to order **Pastry Types**, select them from the list. (Note: This field is optional)
7. If you have any **Custom Requests** or **Dietary Restrictions**, please include them in the text field.
8. Specify the **Order Occasion**, if applicable.
9. Choose your **Packaging Preference**.
10. Select your preferred **Payment Method**.
11. Finally, add any **Special Instructions**, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Full name of the person ordering.
* **Email Address**: For order confirmation and any further communication.
* **Phone Number**: Contact number for delivery and any additional information.
* **Delivery Address**: Where we should deliver your order.
* **City**: City of delivery.
* **Postal Code**: Delivery postal code.
* **Desired Delivery Date**: Date of desired delivery.
* **Preferred Delivery Time**: Time of desired delivery.
* **Bread Types**: Select the type of bread you would like to order.
* **Total Bread Quantity**: Number of loaves or items you would like to order.
* **Pastry Types**: Select the type of pastry you would like to order. (Optional)
* **Total Pastry Quantity**: Number of pastries you would like to order. (Optional)
* **Custom Requests**: Any special requests or allergies.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary requirements.
* **Order Occasion**: What is this order for (e.g. party, event, personal consumption).
* **Packaging Preference**: How would you like items packaged.
* **Payment Method**: How you will pay for your order.
* **Special Instructions**: Any additional notes or information.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out, while optional fields are not required but can be used to provide additional information.
