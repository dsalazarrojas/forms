# kitchen_task_assessment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data for assessing the skills and performance of kitchen staff members.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete **Task Assessment Form 1** (Page 1): This is the first page of the form. Enter any relevant information or comments related to the task assessment.
2.  Complete **Task Assessment Form 2** (Page 2): This page is similar to the first page, where you can enter any additional comments or information related to the task assessment.
3.  Provide **Staff Evaluation 1** (Page 3): Evaluate whether the staff member has the necessary skills to complete tasks. Select either 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown options.
4.  Provide **Staff Evaluation 2** (Page 4): Evaluate which specific tasks the staff member needs help or improvement with. Select multiple options as necessary.
5.  Enter **Cooking Skills 1** (Page 5): Enter the amount of time the staff member has spent honing their cooking skills, in hours or minutes.
6.  Enter **Cooking Skills 2** (Page 6): Enter the number of tasks or skills the staff member has demonstrated proficiency with, up to 100.
7.  Enter **Date Assessed** (Page 7): Enter the date the assessment was completed.
8.  Enter **Time Assessed** (Page 8): Enter the time the assessment was completed.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately enter all relevant information for each page.
- Enter dates and times in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM).
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the staff evaluation questions to provide clear feedback.
- For cooking skills, use a standard unit of time (hours or minutes) to ensure consistency.
- Review each page carefully before completing the assessment to ensure accuracy.
