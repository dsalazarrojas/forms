# Cheesecake Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect order details for a cheesecake purchase. It ensures that customers provide accurate and relevant information to place their orders successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). This includes:
	* Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Order Date
	* Delivery/Pickup Preference
	* Quantity
	* Crust Type
	* Payment Method
2. Choose your preferred flavor, size, and toppings for your cheesecake.
3. Select the occasion and specify any dietary requirements or special instructions (if applicable).
4. Choose a payment method and whether you want gift wrapping or a special message.
5. Add any additional order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name* (1)**: Your full name for order confirmation.
* **Email Address* (2)**: Email address for order confirmation.
* **Phone Number* (3)**: Contact number for order inquiries.
* **Order Date* (4)**: Date of this order.
* **Pickup or Delivery* (5)**: Choose between store pickup, home delivery, or event delivery.
* **Delivery Date* (6)**: When you need your order delivered.
* **Delivery Address** (7): Full delivery address if applicable.
* **Cheesecake Flavor* (8)**: Select your preferred flavor.
* **Cheesecake Size* (9)**: Choose the size of your cheesecake.
* **Quantity* (10)**: Number of cheesecakes to order.
* **Crust Type* (11)**: Select the type of crust for your cheesecake.
* **Toppings (12)**: Select desired toppings for your cheesecake.
* **Dietary Requirements (13)**: Specify any dietary restrictions.
* **Special Message (14)**: Message for cake decoration if applicable.
* **Gift Wrapping (15)**: Choose whether you need gift wrapping.
* **Gift Message (16)**: Message for gift card if applicable.
* **Occasion (17)**: Choose the occasion for your cheesecake.
* **Special Instructions (18)**: Any special requests or allergies.
* **Payment Method* (19)**: Choose how you will pay.
* **Order Notes (20)**: Additional information about your order.

**Tips**:

- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately to facilitate efficient order processing.
- Select options carefully, as they may affect cheesecake preparation or delivery.
- If you have any special requests, please specify in the "Special Instructions" field.
