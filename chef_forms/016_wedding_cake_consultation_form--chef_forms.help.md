# wedding_cake_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Cake Consultation Form is used to gather information from clients about their wedding cake preferences. This form helps wedding planners or cake designers create a custom wedding cake that fits the clients' needs and style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of cake you prefer.
2. Choose the size of the cake you want.
3. Provide any additional flavor profile notes.
4. Specify the number of tiers you want.
5. Provide the wedding date.
6. Choose the reception time.
7. Select the reception location.
8. Provide any special requests.
9. Choose how you want to receive your cake (pick-up or delivery).
10. Enter your contact information.
11. Select the payment method.
12. Provide any additional notes.
13. Choose any cake designs you prefer.
14. Select any cake toppings you like.
15. Select any cake decorations you prefer.
16. Select any cake messages you want on the cake.
17. Choose the shape of the cake.
18. Enter any additional cake size information.
19. Enter the cake color.
20. Enter the cake theme.
21. Provide any order notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cake Type**: Select the type of cake you prefer. (Vanilla, Chocolate, Red Velvet)
* **Cake Size**: Choose the size of the cake you want. (Small, Medium, Large)
* **Flavor Profile**: Provide any additional flavor profile notes.
* **Tier Count**: Specify the number of tiers you want.
* **Wedding Date**: Provide the wedding date.
* **Reception Time**: Choose the reception time.
* **Reception Location**: Select the reception location.
* **Special Requests**: Provide any special requests.
* **Delivery Method**: Choose how you want to receive your cake (Pick-up, Delivery, Pickup and Delivery)
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Address**: Enter your address.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method. (Cash, Check, Credit)
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes.
* **Cake Designs**: Choose any cake designs you prefer. (Buttercream, Fresh Flower, Fondant)
* **Cake Toppings**: Select any cake toppings you like. (Fruit, Candy, Sprinkles)
* **Cake Decorations**: Choose any cake decorations you prefer. (Flowers, Fruits, Ribbons)
* **Cake Messages**: Select any cake messages you want on the cake. (Happy Birthday, Congratulations, Love You)
* **Cake Shape**: Choose the shape of the cake. (Round, Rectangular, Hexagonal)
* **Cake Size 2**: Enter any additional cake size information.
* **Cake Color**: Enter the cake color.
* **Cake Theme**: Enter the cake theme.
* **Order Notes**: Provide any order notes.
