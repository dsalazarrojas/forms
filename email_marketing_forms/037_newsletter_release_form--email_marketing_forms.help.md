# Newsletter Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create and schedule a new newsletter release, specifying the title, issue details, publishing channels, and approval status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the title of the newsletter in the `Newsletters Title` field.
2.  In the `Issue Details` field, select the issue date or time of the newsletter.
3.  Choose all applicable publishing channels for the newsletter in the `Publishing Details` field.
4.  Select all the approvers who need to approve the newsletter in the `Approvals` field.
5.  Enter any additional comments or notes in the `Notes` field.
6.  Specify the release date and time in the `Release Date` and `Release Time` fields, respectively.
7.  Confirm whether the newsletter has been published or not in the `Published` field.
8.  Enter any additional comments or notes in the `Notes 2` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Newsletters Title** (`title`, required): Enter a brief and descriptive title of the newsletter.
- **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, optional): Choose the issue date or time of the newsletter from the options provided.
- **Publishing Details** (`publishing_details`, optional): Select all applicable channels where the newsletter should be published (e.g., Email, Social Media, Website, Blog).
- **Approvals** (`approval`, optional): Select all the approvers who need to approve the newsletter (e.g., Marketing Manager, Communications Officer, Creative Director, Editor, Publisher).
- **Notes** (`notes`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the newsletter.
- **Release Date** (`release_date`, required): Specify the date on which the newsletter should be released.
- **Release Time** (`release_time`, required): Specify the time of day when the newsletter should be released.
- **Published** (`published`, optional): Confirm whether the newsletter has been published or not.
- **Notes 2** (`notes_2`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the newsletter's release.
