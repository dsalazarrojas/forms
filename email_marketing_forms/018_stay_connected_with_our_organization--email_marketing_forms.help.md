# Stay Connected With Our Organization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you stay connected with our organization. It captures user information, contact details, and preferences for our email marketing activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address and phone number.
3. Choose your email preferences (No email, Weekly, or Daily).
4. Select your notification preferences (True or False for Updates and Events).
5. Add any additional comments or notes.
6. If applicable, select the category (Email Marketing Forms or Other).
7. If applicable, add any additional information (form ID, form title, assigned tool, etc.).
