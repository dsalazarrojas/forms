# Real Estate Marketing Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Marketing Intake Form is designed to collect information from potential customers or leads about their real estate marketing preferences and sources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the basic contact information:
	* Enter your name in the `name` field.
	* Enter your email address in the `email` field.
	* Enter your phone number in the `phone` field.
2. Answer the property-related questions:
	* Select whether you are interested in a specific type of property (e.g., residential, commercial) in the `real_estate_type` field.
	* If you have a different property type, enter it in the `property_type_other` field.
3. Identify your lead source:
	* Select whether you found us through a specific marketing channel or source (e.g., online ads, social media) in the `lead_source` field.
	* If you have a different lead source, enter it in the `lead_source_other` field.
4. Select your marketing preferences:
	* Choose the marketing channels you are currently using or interested in (e.g., email, social media, print ads) in the `marketing_channel` field.
	* If you have another marketing channel, enter it in the `marketing_channels_other` field.
5. Provide feedback on your real estate agent:
	* Select whether you are currently working with a real estate agent or have a preference for one in the `real_estate_agent` field.
	* If you have a different preference, enter it in the `real_estate_agents` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Real Estate Type** (`real_estate_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property you are interested in (e.g., residential, commercial).
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, required): Select whether you are interested in a specific type of property (e.g., single-family home, condo).
* **Other Property Type** (`property_type_other`, text): If you have a different type of property, enter it here.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, required): Select how you found us (e.g., online ads, social media, referral).
* **Other Lead Source** (`lead_source_other`, text): If you have a different lead source, enter it here.
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channel`, select_multiple, required): Select the marketing channels you are currently using or interested in (e.g., email, social media, print ads).
* **Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels`, text): If you have another marketing channel, enter it here.
* **Other Marketing Channels** (`marketing_channels_other`, text): If you have a different marketing channel, enter it here.
* **Real Estate Agent** (`real_estate_agent`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you are currently working with a real estate agent or have a preference for one.
* **Real Estate Agents** (`real_estate_agents`, text): If you have a different preference, enter it here.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when answering the questions to ensure we can better understand your needs and preferences.
* If you have any additional comments or feedback, feel free to include them in the "Other" fields.
* Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to submit your information.
