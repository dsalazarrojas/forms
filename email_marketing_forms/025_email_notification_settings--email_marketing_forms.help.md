# email_notification_settings - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email notification settings form is designed to provide a way to configure email notifications for various applications or services. This form helps users set up their email notification preferences, ensuring they receive important updates, reminders, and notifications from their email accounts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Email Notification Settings" field on the form. This is the main section of the form where you'll configure your email notification settings.
2. In this field, you can enter your email address and other relevant details to set up your email notification settings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email Notification Settings** (`title`, `text`, **required**): This is the main field where you can enter your email address and other details to set up your email notification settings. Please ensure you fill in this field accurately and completely, as it is required to save your email notification settings.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address and other details before saving your email notification settings.
- If you're unsure about any part of the form, feel free to contact our support team for assistance.
- You can adjust your email notification settings at any time by revisiting this form and making the necessary changes.
