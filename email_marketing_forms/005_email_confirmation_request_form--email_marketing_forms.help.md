# email_confirmation_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a confirmation of email address and optional additional details from the user. It is intended to be used for email marketing purposes, allowing the user to subscribe or unsubscribe from email communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
2. If you wish to subscribe to email communications, select "True" in the "Subscribe" field. If you do not wish to subscribe, select "False".
3. If applicable, provide your preferred date in the "Date" field.
4. If applicable, select your preferred time in the "Time" field.
5. Add any additional note or comment in the "Note" field.
6. Optionally, provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
7. Confirm your information by filling in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Email (email)**: Enter your email address that you wish to confirm.
- **Subscribe (select_one)**: Select "True" to subscribe to email communications, or "False" to unsubscribe.
- **Date (date)**: Enter a date if you wish to schedule a specific date for email communication.
- **Time (time)**: Select a time if you wish to schedule a specific time for email communication.
- **Note (note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your email communication request.
- **Phone Number (text)**: Optionally, enter your phone number for further contact.
- **Confirm (text)**: Fill in this field to confirm your email address and other details.
