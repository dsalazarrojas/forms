# Email Subscription Incentive - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Subscription Incentive form is used to collect demographic information from email subscribers, which will allow us to tailor our communication and offer incentives that match their interests.

## How To Complete This Form

The form is divided into several sections, each requiring one or more fields. Please fill out each field in the following order:

1. **First Name (<name>, text, false)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Last Name (<name>, text, false)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email (<name>, email, false)**: Enter your valid email address.
4. **Phone (<name>, text, false)**: Enter your phone number. Please format it in the standard international format (XXX-XXX-XXXX).
5. **Country (<name>, text, false)**: Enter the country where you are currently residing.
6. **Region (<name>, text, false)**: Enter the region where you currently reside.
7. **City (<name>, text, false)**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
8. **Postal Code (<name>, text, false)**: Enter your postal code.
9. **Company (<name>, text, false)**: If you represent a company, enter its name.
10. **Job Title (<name>, text, false)**: Enter your current job title.
11. **Age (<name>, number, false)**: Enter your age in years.
12. **Date of Birth (<name>, date, false)**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
