# Email-Campaign-Plan-Form-Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email-Campaign-Plan-Form-Template is a tool to help you plan and organize email marketing campaigns. It guides you through the essential steps to create a well-structured campaign that meets your objectives.


## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the form title and category to provide context to your campaign.
2.  Provide a clear description of your campaign, including its objective and key aspects.
3.  Define the target audience for your campaign, including their type (e.g., email marketing, social media, content).
4.  Choose the marketing channel(s) you will use to reach your target audience (e.g., email, mobile, social media, offline).
5.  Determine the send timeframe and frequency of your campaign, including the send duration and send frequency period.
6.  Select the send days and times of the day for your campaign.
7.  Define the campaign goal and budget, including send and send lists size.
8.  Set a send frequency limit for your campaign.
9.  Craft a clear and compelling email subject and body, including a footer with any necessary disclaimers or links.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Campaign Plan** (`form_title`, text, required): A brief description of your campaign that will serve as a reference for the team.
* **Category** (`form_category`, text, required): The category of your campaign (e.g., promotional, informational, or transactional).
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required): A unique name for your campaign that will help identify it in the future.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required): The people you are trying to reach with your campaign (e.g., customers, subscribers, or prospects).
* **Target Audience Type** (`target_audience_type`, select_one, required): The type of audience you are targeting (e.g., email marketing, social media, content).
* **Marketing Channel** (`marketing_channel`, select_one, required): The channel(s) you will use to reach your target audience (e.g., email, mobile, social media, offline).
* **Send Timeframe** (`send_timeframe`, time, optional): The timeframe during which you will send your campaign (e.g., morning, afternoon, or evening).
* **Send Frequency** (`send_frequency`, number, optional): The frequency at which you will send your campaign (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly).
* **Send Day** (`send_day`, date, optional): The specific day(s) of the week you will send your campaign (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
* **Send Time** (`send_time`, time, optional): The specific time of the day you will send your campaign (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Send Duration** (`send_duration`, select_multiple, required): The number of days to send your campaign (e.g., 30, 60, 90).
* **Send Frequency Period** (`send_frequency_period`, select_one, required): The period at which you will send your campaign (e.g., day, week, month).
* **Send Day of Week** (`send_day_of_week`, select_multiple, optional): The specific days of the week you will send your campaign (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
* **Send Time of Day** (`send_time_of_day`, select_multiple, optional): The specific time of the day you will send your campaign (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Campaign Goal** (`campaign_goal`, text, required): A clear statement of what you want to achieve with your campaign.
* **Send List** (`send_list`, text, optional): A list of recipients or addresses you will send your campaign to.
* **Campaign Budget** (`campaign_budget`, number, required): The budget allocated for your campaign.
* **Send Budget** (`send_budget`, number, required): The cost to send your campaign to the target audience.
* **Send List Size** (`send_list_size`, text, optional): The size of the list you will send your campaign to.
* **Send Frequency Cap** (`send_frequency_cap`, number, optional): The maximum number of times you will send your campaign.
* **Send Frequency Limit** (`send_frequency_limit`, select_multiple, optional): The maximum frequency at which you can send your campaign (e.g., unlimited, 10000, 50000).
* **Email Subject** (`email_subject`, text, required): A clear and compelling subject line for your email campaign.
* **Email Body** (`email_body`, text, required): The content of your email campaign, including a clear and concise message.
* **Email Footer** (`email_footer`, text, optional): A footer with any necessary disclaimers or links.
