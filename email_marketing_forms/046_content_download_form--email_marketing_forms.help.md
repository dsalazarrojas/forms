# Content Download Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide users with a straightforward way to request free content from the company. By filling out this form, users can specify their contact information, content preferences, and marketing preferences, ensuring they receive the right material and stay updated on marketing efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Your Full Name** field with your name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter your **Your Email Address** in the format of a valid email address.
3. Select the **Free Content Requested** option that best fits your needs.
4. Choose your **Your Industry** from the provided list.
5. Select your **Your Company Size** from the provided list.
6. Choose whether to **Sign up for our marketing updates?** (True or False).
7. Confirm that you have read and agree to the **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (True or False).
8. Select the **Date of Download Request** using the provided date format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Full Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Fill out this field with your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Your Email Address** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Free Content Requested** (`content_requested`, select_one, required): Select the type of free content you are interested in receiving.
* **Your Industry** (`industry_type`, select_one, required): Choose your industry from the provided list.
* **Your Company Size** (`company_size_range`, select_one, required): Select your company size from the provided list.
* **Sign up for our marketing updates?** (`marketing_opt_in`, select_one, required): Choose whether to opt-in for marketing updates.
* **I have read and agree to the privacy policy regarding my data** (`privacy_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy.
* **Date of Download Request** (`download_request_date`, date, required): Select the date of your download request using the provided format.

## Tips
- Ensure your email address is valid and correctly formatted.
- Select the correct industry and company size to receive relevant content.
- Confirm your opt-in choice for marketing updates carefully.
- Review and agree to the privacy policy before proceeding.
- Enter the date accurately to track your download request.
- Fill out the form once to avoid duplicate submissions.
