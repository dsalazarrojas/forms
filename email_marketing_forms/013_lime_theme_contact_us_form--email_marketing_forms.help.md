## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and messages from visitors or customers interested in LimeTheme. It's a simple and efficient way to gather the essential details needed for communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Contact Us" page to begin.
2. Fill in your Email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Type your Message in the "Message" field.
5. Click the "Send" button to submit your contact information and message.
