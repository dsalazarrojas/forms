# Newsletter Subscription Preferences - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your newsletter preferences, including the frequency, topics, and channel of delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the "How often do you want to receive newsletters?" field by selecting one of the options: Every day, Weekly, or Monthly.
- Select the topics you're interested in learning more about from the "What topics are you interested in learning more about?" field. You can choose multiple topics.
- Choose how you want to receive our newsletter from the "How do you want to receive our newsletter?" field. You can select Email, Social Media, or Phone.
- Enter your email address in the "What is your email address?" field. This is a required field.
- Optionally, you can enter your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, you can write them in the "Additional comments or questions" field.
- Confirm your email address in the "Confirm email address" field to ensure we have the correct contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### How often do you want to receive newsletters?
* **How often do you want to receive newsletters? (newsletter_frequency, select_one, required)**: Choose how often you'd like to receive our newsletters. Options include Every day, Weekly, or Monthly.

### What topics are you interested in learning more about?
* **What topics are you interested in learning more about? (newsletter_content, select_multiple, required)**: Select the topics that interest you from the list provided. You can choose multiple topics.

### How do you want to receive our newsletter?
* **How do you want to receive our newsletter? (newsletter_channel, select_one, required)**: Choose how you'd like to receive our newsletter. Options include Email, Social Media, or Phone.

### What is your email address?
* **What is your email address? (email, email, required)**: Enter your email address where we'll send our newsletters.

### What is your phone number?
* **What is your phone number? (phone, text, optional)**: Optionally, enter your phone number to receive our newsletter.

### Additional comments or questions?
* **Additional comments or questions (note, note, optional)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, this is the place to write them.

### Confirm email address
* **Confirm email address (confirm_email, email, required)**: Confirm your email address to ensure we have the correct contact information.

### Confirm phone number
* **Confirm phone number (confirm_phone, text, optional)**: If you entered a phone number, confirm it here to ensure accuracy.
