# youth_forum_mailing_list_signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to create a mailing list subscription for the youth forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information, including your first name, last name, email, and any additional details as required.
2. Select your interests from the provided list.
3. Add any notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (id: 3, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Select Interests** (id: 4, select multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the following interests:
  • Football
  • Basketball
  • Baseball
  • Soccer
  • Tennis
  • Volleyball
  • Cricket
  • Golf
* **Age** (id: 5, number, required: false): Enter your age.
* **Time** (id: 6, time, required: false): Select a time for any specific reason or appointment.
* **Phone** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (id: 8, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or details.
