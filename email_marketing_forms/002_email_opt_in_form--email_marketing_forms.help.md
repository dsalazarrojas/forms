# EMail Opt In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The EMail Opt In Form is used for collecting email addresses and other information from users for marketing and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit. If you encounter any issues, you can ask for help from our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **EMail Address (form_email)**: This field is used to capture the user's email address. Please enter your email address in the format `username@domain.com`.
* **Phone Number (form_phone)**: This field is used to capture the user's phone number.
* **Agree (form_agree)**: This field allows you to agree to terms and conditions. Select one of the two options: "I Agree" or "I Don't Agree".
* **Terms and Conditions (form_terms)**: This field allows you to select which terms and conditions you agree to. Select all that apply.
* **Additional Information (form_note)**: This field is a free text field for entering any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Category (form_category)**: This field allows you to select a category for your information. Choose one of the two options: "Category 1" or "Category 2".
* **Tags (form_tags)**: This field allows you to select one or more tags for your information. Choose one or more of the two options: "Tag 1" or "Tag 2".
* **Date (form_date)**: This field is used for entering a date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time (form_time)**: This field is used for entering a time in 24-hour format.
* **More Information (form_note_2)**: This field is a free text field for entering any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Another Email (form_email_2)**: This field is used to capture another email address.
* **Another Phone Number (form_phone_2)**: This field is used to capture another phone number.
* **Agree Again (form_agree_2)**: This field allows you to agree to terms and conditions again. Select one of the two options: "I Agree Again" or "I Don't Agree Again".
* **Another Terms (form_terms_2)**: This field allows you to select which terms and conditions you agree to again. Select all that apply.
* **Even More Information (form_note_3)**: This field is a free text field for entering any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Yet Another Phone Number (form_phone_3)**: This field is used to capture another phone number.
* **Agree Yet Again (form_agree_3)**: This field allows you to agree to terms and conditions yet again. Select one of the two options: "I Agree Yet Again" or "I Don't Agree Yet Again".
* **More Terms (form_terms_3)**: This field allows you to select which terms and conditions you agree to yet again. Select all that apply.
* **Still More Information (form_note_4)**: This field is a free text field for entering any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Still Another Phone Number (form_phone_4)**: This field is used to capture another phone number.
* **Agree Still Again (form_agree_4)**: This field allows you to agree to terms and conditions still again. Select one of the two options: "I Agree Still Again" or "I Don't Agree Still Again".
* **Still More Terms (form_terms_4)**: This field allows you to select which terms and conditions you agree to still again. Select all that apply.
* **Last Information (form_note_5)**: This field is a free text field for entering any additional information you'd like to provide.

Please note that not all fields are required. You can enter any relevant information you'd like to provide.
