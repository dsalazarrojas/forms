# Fancy Envelope Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fancy Envelope Contact Form is a simple form designed to collect contact information from users, allowing them to easily send a message or inquire about services. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number you'd like to share, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Type in your message in the "Message" field.
6. Click the "Send" or "Submit" button to forward your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your first name here, for example, John.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your last name here, for example, Doe.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address here, for example, [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): If you have a phone number you'd like to share, enter it here.
* **Message** (`message`, text, **optional**): Type in your message here, describing the purpose of your inquiry.
