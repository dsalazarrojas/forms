# Email Design Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Email Design Questionnaire is a form designed to gather essential information from clients about their preferences and expectations for an email design project. The gathered data will be used to create a tailored and effective email design that meets the client's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all required questions and provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
2. For the customer expectations field, select the option that best describes your expectations for the email design project.
3. You can add as many lines to the email content field as needed to describe your expectations for the email content.
4. For the design preferences field, provide any specific design preferences or requirements you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, Text, Optional): This field is for your personal contact information, such as your name and email address.
* **Customer Expectations** (`customer_expectations`, Select One, Required): This field is where you specify what you expect from the email design project. Please select the option that best describes your expectations.
* **Business Details** (`business_details`, Text, Optional): This field is for any relevant business information, such as your company's name or industry.
* **Email Content** (`email_content`, Note, Optional): This field is for describing any specific content requirements or expectations for the email. You can add as many lines as needed to cover all your expectations.
* **Design Preferences** (`design_preferences`, Text, Optional): This field is for specifying any specific design preferences or requirements you have for the email. 

## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields carefully and answer all required questions to ensure the most accurate and effective email design.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
* This form should not be filled out unless it's the first time we're working with a client.
