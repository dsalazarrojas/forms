# Email Campaign Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect contact information and permission from users to receive marketing materials from us.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the designated text field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the designated email field.
3. Select the type of permission you grant us for marketing purposes from the **Permission** dropdown menu.
4. Optionally, provide your **Phone** number for further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, **required**): Enter your name as you prefer to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, **required**): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Permission** (`permission`, Select One, **required**): Choose which type of marketing materials you want to receive:
	+ Receive occasional updates on industry news
	+ Receive promotional offers
	+ Receive marketing information
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for further communication (not required for submission, but recommended for personalization).

## Tips

* Ensure your email address is entered correctly to ensure you receive updates and promotions.
* Choose the permission that best fits your preferences to avoid overwhelming your inbox.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [support email].
