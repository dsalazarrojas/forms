# Master Class Tour Email Signup - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us learn more about your interests and preferences for the Master Class Tour. By filling out this form, you will be able to express your interest in the tour and provide us with your contact information so we can follow up with you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you and fill in your contact details. Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tour_preferences**: 
  * Select 'Yes' if you are interested in the Master Class Tour, or 'No' if you are not.
* **name**: 
  * Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed when we contact you.
* **email**: 
  * Enter the email address where you would like us to contact you.
* **phone**: 
  * Enter your phone number where you would like us to contact you.
* **tour_date**: 
  * Select the date you are interested in attending the Master Class Tour.
* **tour_time**: 
  * Select the time you are interested in attending the Master Class Tour.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us and we will be happy to help.
