# Newsletter Update Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an update to the email marketing newsletter, allowing users to subscribe or unsubscribe from the newsletter and provide a reason for unsubscribing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose whether you would like to subscribe to the newsletter (`Subscribe to Newsletter`) by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
2. If you would like to unsubscribe from the newsletter, choose "Unsubscribe" from the dropdown menu.
3. If you are unsubscribing, provide a reason for unsubscribing in the text field (`Unsubscription Reason`).
4. Enter your email address in the `Email Address` field.
5. Confirm your email address in the `Email Confirmation` field.
