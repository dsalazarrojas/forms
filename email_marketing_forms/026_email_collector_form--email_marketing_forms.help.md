# email_collector_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email collector form is designed to collect contact information from users. The form is typically used on a website or landing page to gather email addresses from visitors. This form can be used for various purposes such as newsletter sign-ups, event registrations, or survey responses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the email collector form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the Email field.
2. Confirm your email address in the Confirm Email field.
3. If you wish to provide additional contact information, enter your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
4. If you need to be contacted through phone, enter your Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email**: Enter the address you check and use to send and receive email. This field is required for sending you newsletters or other communications.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter the email address you entered in the previous field to help us confirm it's correct.
* **First name**: If you'd like to provide your first name for personalization or for us to address you by name.
* **Last name**: If you'd like to provide your last name for personalization or for us to address you by name.
* **Phone number**: If you'd like to provide a phone number for us to contact you in case we need to reach out for additional information or to confirm a response.
