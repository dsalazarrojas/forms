# Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Opt In Form is used to collect email addresses and phone numbers from users who wish to opt-in for email marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Opt In Form, simply fill out the fields as follows:

1. **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used as the primary contact method.
2. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional). If you choose to enter your phone number, it will be used for additional contact options.
3. **Name**: Enter your name. This will help us personalize communications with you.
4. **Message** (1): This is an optional field for additional comments or notes. Feel free to use it if you have any questions or have specific requests.
5. **Message** (2): This is another optional field for further comments or notes. It serves as a second note field, allowing you to elaborate on your previous message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email (email)**: A valid email address is required for this field. Please enter a working email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone (text)**: An optional phone number field for additional contact options. Please format your phone number in the international format (e.g., +1-800-123-4567).
* **Name (text)**: Enter your name to help us personalize communications with you.
* **Message (1/2, note)**: These fields are optional and can be used to provide additional comments or notes. They are not required for submission. 

Note: These fields are not validated for length, so please use them as needed to provide any relevant information. 

Tips:
* Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number if you want to be contacted via these methods.
* Use the optional fields for any further comments or requests you may have.
