# general-inquiry-contact-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to send general inquiries to the organization, such as asking questions, providing feedback, or requesting information. It is not a survey or a form for specific, tracked responses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter a brief description of the subject of your inquiry in the "subject" field.
4. Type your message in the "message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (<name>)
* **name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Email (<email>)
* **email**: Enter a valid email address that you use to receive messages from the organization.

### Subject (<subject>)
* **subject**: Briefly describe the topic or purpose of your inquiry.

### Message (<message>)
* **message**: Type a clear and concise message describing your inquiry or request. 

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is accurate to receive any responses to your inquiry.
* If you have already made an inquiry, please do not fill out this form again.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
