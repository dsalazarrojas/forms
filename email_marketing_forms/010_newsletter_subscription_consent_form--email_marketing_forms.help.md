# Newsletter Subscription Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect email addresses and other contact information from users who wish to subscribe to a newsletter or promotional email list. It serves as a legally binding consent mechanism, indicating that the user has agreed to receive emails from the sender.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it's a valid email address that you own or have access to.
4. Select "Yes" in the "I Accept" field to indicate that you've reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
• **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here. This field is used to collect your first name for address and communication purposes.

### Last Name
• **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/true): Enter your last name here. This field is required as part of the contact information for the newsletter or promotional email list.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, email, required/true): Enter a valid email address that you own or have access to. This email address will be used to send you newsletters or promotional emails.

### I Accept
• **I Accept** (`accept_terms`, select_one, required/true): Select "Yes" to indicate that you've reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of the newsletter or promotional email list. This is a critical component of the form, as it ensures that you have provided consent before receiving emails.
