# Black Friday Opt In Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture user information and preferences for a Black Friday opt-in campaign, with the goal of sending exclusive deals and promotional emails.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Opt-in Header
The "Join Our Black Friday Email List" section serves as a brief introduction to the opt-in process.

### Step 2: First Name and Last Name
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you would like to be referred to.

### Step 3: Email Address
- **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address to receive exclusive deals and promotions.

### Step 4: Email Frequency Preference
- **Email Frequency Preference**: Select how often you would like to receive emails from us:
  - Daily During Black Friday Week: Receive daily emails during the Black Friday period.
  - Weekly: Receive weekly emails.
  - Only Major Deals: Receive emails only for major deals.
  - One Time Black Friday Only: Receive only one email on Black Friday.

### Step 5: Product Interests
- **Product Interests**: Select the categories that interest you:
  - Electronics and Gadgets
  - Fashion and Accessories
  - Home and Living
  - Beauty and Wellness
  - Sports and Outdoors
  - Kids and Family
  - Food and Gourmet
  - Travel and Experiences

### Step 6: Shopping Behavior
- **Shopping Behavior**: Select how you describe your shopping style:
  - Early Bird Shopper: You like to shop early for deals.
  - Last Minute Shopper: You shop at the last minute.
  - Research First Shopper: You research before making a purchase.
  - Impulse Buyer: You tend to buy on impulse.
  - Deal Hunter: You're looking for discounts and deals.
  - (Optional) Not specified: Leave this field blank if not applicable.

### Step 7: Preferred Shopping Device
- **Preferred Shopping Device**: Select the device you shop on most:
  - Desktop Computer
  - Laptop
  - Smartphone
  - Tablet

### Step 8: SMS Notifications
- **SMS Notifications**: Select whether you want to receive SMS alerts for flash sales:
  - Yes Add My Number: Receive SMS alerts.
  - No Thanks: Do not receive SMS alerts.

### Step 9: Mobile Number (Optional)
- **Mobile Number**: Enter your mobile number for SMS alerts (optional).

### Step 10: Birthday Month (Optional)
- **Birthday Month**: Select your birth month for potential birthday offers:
  - January
  - February
  - March
  - April
  - May
  - June
  - July
  - August
  - September
  - October
  - November
  - December
  - Prefer Not to Say: Leave this field blank if you don't want to share your birthday.

### Step 11: How Did You Find Us (Optional)
- **How Did You Find Us**: Select where you discovered this offer:
  - Social Media
  - Search Engine
  - Friend Referral
  - Email Forward
  - Website Popup
  - Advertisement
  - Other

### Step 12: Privacy Consent
- **Privacy Consent**: Agree to our privacy policy and data handling:
  - I Consent: Accept our privacy policy.
  - I Do Not Consent: Decline our privacy policy.

### Step 13: Marketing Consent
- **Marketing Consent**: Agree to receive promotional emails:
  - Yes I Agree: Accept promotional emails.
  - No Thanks: Decline promotional emails.

### Step 14: Additional Preferences (Optional)
- **Additional Preferences**: If you have any other preferences or comments, enter them here.
- Note: This field is optional and for users who want to provide extra information.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure that your preferences are correctly captured.
- Be honest about your interests and shopping behavior to help us tailor our marketing efforts to you.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
- Review our privacy policy to understand how we handle your data.
