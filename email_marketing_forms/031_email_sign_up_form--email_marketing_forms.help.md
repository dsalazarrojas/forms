# Email Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Email Sign Up Form is designed to allow users to subscribe to our email marketing campaigns. It helps us gather essential information about each user to create targeted and personalized content.

## How To Complete This Form

- To complete this form, simply fill in your details in the required fields.
- Make sure to enter a valid email address in both the "Email address" and "Confirm Email address" fields.
- Confirm your email address by entering the same email address in the "Confirm Email address" field again.
- Choose your job title, role, department, and industry from the respective fields.
- Click the "Sign Up" button to submit your details and start receiving our email marketing campaigns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address (Email)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to send you targeted and relevant content.
* **Confirm Email address (Email)**: Confirm your email address to ensure we have the correct address on file.
* **Job title (Text)**: Enter your current job title or profession.
* **Role (Select Multiple)**: Choose your current role, whether it's Yes or No.
* **Department (Select Multiple)**: Choose your department or area of work, whether it's Yes or No.
* **Industry (Text)**: Enter your industry or area of work.

## Tips

- Be cautious with your email address as we use it for targeted and personalized content.
- Double-check your job title, role, and department to ensure accuracy.
- You can change your subscription preferences at any time by <a href='https://example.com/unsubscribe'>unsubscribing</a> from our emails.
