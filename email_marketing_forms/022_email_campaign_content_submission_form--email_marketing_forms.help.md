# Email Campaign Content Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Campaign Content Submission Form is designed for submitting campaign content, such as subjects and bodies, for email marketing campaigns. This form allows users to select the list(s) to which the campaign will be sent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for the campaign in the "Campaign Subject" field.
2. Type the campaign body in the "Campaign Body" field.
3. Select the list(s) to which the campaign will be sent by choosing from the "Send to" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Campaign Subject (Campaign Subject, text, required: false)
* This field is for entering a brief title of the campaign. Please provide a clear and concise subject that will grab the attention of the intended audience.

### Campaign Body (Campaign Body, text, required: false)
* This field is for typing the main content of the campaign. Please provide a detailed and engaging body that will convey the message of the campaign.

### Send to (Send to, select_one, required: false)
* This field is for selecting the list(s) to which the campaign will be sent. Please choose the list(s) that correspond to the intended audience of the campaign.

### Email Address (Email Address, email, required: false)
* This field is for entering the email address that will be used to send the campaign. Please ensure that the email address is valid and correct.

## Tips
* Double-check the campaign subject and body for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.
* Ensure that the selected list(s) correspond to the intended audience of the campaign.
* Review the submitted campaign content for clarity and effectiveness before sending.

Note: Since the `required` field is set to `false` for all fields, it is not explicitly mentioned in the guide. However, it is still a good practice to fill in all fields accurately and comprehensively to ensure the form submission is complete and correct.
