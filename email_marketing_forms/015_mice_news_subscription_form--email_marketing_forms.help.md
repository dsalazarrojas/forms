# MICE News Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The MICE News Subscription Form is designed to gather user information for our internal records. We will use this information to keep in touch with users who have shown interest in specific topics.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in your First Name and Last Name if you would like to be contacted personally.
- Enter your Email address to receive our news and updates.
- Choose the topics that interest you the most by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
- Select how you would like to receive our email consents using the dropdown menu.
- You can optionally provide your Phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the Form Comment field.
- The form will automatically collect the date and time of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your First Name.
  - **Last Name**: Enter your Last Name.
    - **Email**: Enter a valid Email address.
    - **Topic Interests**: Select one or multiple topics that interest you the most.
    - **Email Consent**: Select how you would like to receive our email consents.
    - **Phone**: If you would like to be contacted via phone, enter your Phone number.
    - **Form Comment**: Provide any comments or suggestions.
    - **Created At**: This field will automatically be populated with the date and time of submission.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your Email address is valid and up-to-date.
- You can select one or multiple topics from the dropdown menu.
- Please note that the information collected will be used solely for internal purposes.
