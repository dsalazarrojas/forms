# Email Listing Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a person's job listing or job posting. It will be used to populate an email marketing campaign or job board.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. (This field is required.)
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. (This field is required.)
4. Optionally, enter your **Company** name in the "Company" field.
5. Optionally, enter your **Job Title** and **Job Description** in their respective fields.
6. Choose the **Job Type** that best describes your job listing or posting.
7. Select **Yes** to submit your job listing or **No** to cancel.
