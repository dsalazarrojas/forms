# paypal_sponsor_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `paypal_sponsor_donation_form` is a form used to collect information from potential sponsors who want to support a specific project or initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Sponsor Name** in the first field. This is the name of the organization or individual sponsoring the project.
2. Provide your **Sponsor Email** in the second field. This is the email address associated with the sponsor's account.
3. Enter your **Sponsor Phone** in the third field. This is the phone number of the sponsor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sponsor Name** (`sponsor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization or individual sponsoring the project.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address associated with the sponsor's account.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the sponsor.
