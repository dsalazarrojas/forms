# paypal_book_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "paypal_book_order_form" is a form used to collect information from customers about their book orders for processing through PayPal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the book you wish to order in the "book_title" field.
2. Enter the author of the book you wish to order in the "book_author" field.
3. Enter the price of the book you wish to order in the "book_price" field. Please note that this field requires a numeric value (e.g., 10.99 for $10.99).
4. Enter the quantity of the book you wish to order in the "book_quantity" field.
5. The system will automatically calculate the "book_total" based on the book price and quantity.
6. Select the shipping option for your order from the "book_shipping" field.
7. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can enter them in the "book_comments" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "book_email" field. Please note that this field requires a valid email format (e.g., username@domain.com).
9. Enter your phone number in the "book_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **book_title**: Enter the title of the book you wish to order.
* **book_author**: Enter the author of the book you wish to order.
* **book_price**: Enter the price of the book you wish to order (a numeric value, e.g., 10.99 for $10.99).
* **book_quantity**: Enter the quantity of the book you wish to order.
* **book_total**: The system will automatically calculate this field based on the book price and quantity.
* **book_shipping**: Select the shipping option for your order.
* **book_comments**: If you have any additional comments or requests, you can enter them here.
* **book_email**: Enter your email address in a valid format (e.g., username@domain.com).
* **book_phone**: Enter your phone number.
