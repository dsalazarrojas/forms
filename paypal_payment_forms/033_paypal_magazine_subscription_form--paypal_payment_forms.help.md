# paypal_magazine_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to subscribe to a magazine or publication. It is intended for both internal and external use, depending on the implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred subscription duration from the "Subscription duration" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your subscription frequency from the "Subscription frequency" dropdown menu.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
8. (Optional) Enter the date and time when you would like to be subscribed (created at), and (optional) select the time of the last update.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name (First name, text, Optional)**: Enter your first name, as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Last name (Last name, text, Optional)**: Enter your last name, as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Email (Email, email, Optional)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone (Phone, text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number, if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Subscription duration (Subscription duration, select_one, Optional)**: Select the duration of your desired subscription.
* **Subscription frequency (Subscription frequency, select_multiple, Optional)**: Choose how often you would like to receive our magazine or publication.
* **Note (Note, note, Optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your subscription.
* **Created at (Created at, date, Optional)**: If you know the exact date and time when you would like to be subscribed, you can enter it here.
* **Updated at (Updated at, time, Optional)**: If you know the exact time of the last update, you can select it here.

Note: The "Created at" and "Updated at" fields are optional and are likely to be used for internal purposes, such as tracking user activity.
