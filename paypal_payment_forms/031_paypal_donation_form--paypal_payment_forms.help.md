# PayPal Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Donation Form is used to accept donations from individuals, specifying the frequency and amount of their donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name in the **Donator Name** field.
2. Enter the amount of your donation in the **Donation Amount** field.
3. Select the frequency of your donation in the **Donation Frequency** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field (if applicable).
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field (if applicable).
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can include them in the **Note** field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donator Name (Donator Name, text, optional)**: This field is where you enter your name as the donor.
* **Donation Amount (Donation Amount, number, optional)**: Enter the amount of your donation. Please ensure that the amount is correct and accurate.
* **Donation Frequency (Donation Frequency, select_one, optional)**: Select the frequency of your donation. You can choose from One-time, Recurring, or Monthly.
* **Email (Email, email, optional)**: If you want to be contacted for follow-up or updates, please enter your email address. This field is optional.
* **Phone (Phone, text, optional)**: If you want to be contacted via phone for follow-up or updates, please enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **Note (Note, note, optional)**: If you have any additional comments or notes, you can include them here. This field is optional.
