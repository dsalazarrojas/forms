# Camp Registration Form PayPal Checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and pay for a summer camp program. It collects camper and parent/guardian information, payment details, and other relevant data to secure the payment and finalize the registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), for each section.
2. Select the correct options for the camper's information and payment details.
3. Review and confirm your selections before proceeding to the next step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper First Name** (`camper_first_name`, text, *): Enter the first name of the camper.
* **Camper Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, text, *): Enter the last name of the camper.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Enter the camper's birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`, number): Enter the camper's current age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one): Select the gender of the camper (Male, Female, Non-binary, Prefer not to say, Other).
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, *): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, *): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, *): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, *): Enter the camper's home address.
* **City** (`city`, text, *): Enter the city or town where the camper resides.
* **State** (`state`, text, *): Enter the state or province where the camper resides.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, *): Enter the postal code of the camper's location.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, *): Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, *): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, *): List any health conditions that the camper has.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, *): List any food, insect, or medication allergies that the camper has.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple): Select all that apply from the list of dietary restrictions (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut allergy, Kosher, Halal, Other).
* **Camp Session Selection** (`camp_session`, select_one): Select the desired camp session.
* **Before Camp Care** (`before_camp_care`, select_one): Choose the before-camp care option (No, Yes - some days, Yes - all days, $25, $50).
* **After Camp Care** (`after_camp_care`, select_one): Choose the after-camp care option (No, Yes - some days, Yes - all days, $25, $50).
* **Transportation Service** (`transportation`, select_one): Select the transportation option (No, Yes - morning only, Yes - afternoon only, Yes - both ways, $40, $75).
* **Camp T-Shirt** (`camp_tshirt`, select_one): Select an optional camp T-shirt (No thanks, Yes - Youth Small, Yes - Youth Medium, Yes - Youth Large, Yes - Adult Small, Yes - Adult Medium, Yes - Adult Large, Yes - Adult XL).
* **Total Amount Due** (`total_amount`, number, *): This field will be calculated automatically.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one): Select the payment method (PayPal, Credit Card via PayPal, Debit Card via PayPal).
* **PayPal Email Address** (`paypal_email`, email, *): Enter the PayPal email address associated with the payment method.
* **Promo Code** (`promo_code`, text, ): Enter any promo code if applicable.
* **Payment Authorization** (`payment_authorization`, select_one): Confirm that you authorize the payment (Yes - I authorize payment, False).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, select_one): Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions (Yes - I accept, False).
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledged** (`cancellation_policy_acknowledged`, select_one): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy (Yes - I acknowledge, False).
* **How Did You Hear About This Camp** (`how_heard_about_camp`, select_multiple): Select how you heard about this camp (School flyer, Friend or family, Social media, Website, Previous attendee, Community event, Advertisement, Other).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (*).
* Review your selections carefully before proceeding.
* Use a valid PayPal email address for payment processing.
* Enter a valid promo code if applicable.
* Confirm your payment authorization and acceptance of terms and conditions.
* Understand the cancellation policy before proceeding.
