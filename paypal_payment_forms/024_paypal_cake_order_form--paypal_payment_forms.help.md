# paypal_cake_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The paypal_cake_order_form is a form used to collect order details, customer information, and other relevant details for managing cake orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Order Details** field with the necessary information about the order.
2. Provide your **Customer Info** in the next field.
3. Select the desired **Delivery Date** for the order.
4. Choose the delivery time that suits you best in the **Delivery Time** field.
5. If you want to place an order for a cake, select **Cake Type** as "Yes". Otherwise, select "No".
6. Choose your preferred **Flavor** from the list of available options.
7. Add any additional **Message** to the order if needed.
8. Provide a valid **Email** address and **Phone** number for contact.
9. Enter your **Address**, including the location where you want to receive the cake.
10. Enter the **Delivery Address** where the cake should be delivered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Details**: A field for providing detailed information about the order.
* **Customer Info**: A field for inputting your contact information.
* **Delivery Date**: A field for choosing the desired date for delivery.
* **Delivery Time**: A field for selecting the desired time for delivery.
* **Cake Type**: A field to determine whether you want to order a cake or not.
* **Flavor**: A field for choosing your preferred flavor for the cake.
* **Message**: A field for adding any additional message to the order.
* **Email**: A field for inputting your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: A field for inputting your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: A field for entering your address where you want to receive the cake.
* **Delivery Address**: A field for inputting the address where the cake should be delivered.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the entered information before submitting the form.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for contact purposes.
- Enter your address carefully to ensure accurate delivery.
