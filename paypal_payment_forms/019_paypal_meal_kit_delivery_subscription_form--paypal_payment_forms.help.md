# PayPal Meal Kit Delivery Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to sign up for a meal kit delivery subscription service. By filling out this form, you will be able to choose your subscription plan, payment method, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field. This is the name that will be used for communication purposes.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field. This is also for communication purposes.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used for subscription notifications and updates.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This will be used for contact and delivery updates.
5. Enter your password in the "Password" field. This is a required field for securing your account.
6. Select your subscription plan in the "Subscription Plan" field. You can choose between Monthly, Quarterly, and Bi-Monthly.
7. Choose your payment method in the "Payment Method" field. You can select from Credit Card and PayPal.
8. Enter your payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field. This will be the amount you are willing to pay per subscription period.
9. Enter your address in the "Address" field. This will be used for delivery purposes.
10. Enter your city in the "City" field. This will help us identify your location.
11. Enter your state in the "State" field. This will help us identify your location.
12. Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field. This will help us confirm your address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: Enter your given name here. (Required)
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your surname here. (Required)
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address here. This will be used for subscription notifications and updates. (Optional)
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number here. This will be used for contact and delivery updates. (Optional)
* **Password (text)**: Enter your password here. This is a required field for securing your account. (Required)
* **Subscription Plan (select_one)**: Choose between Monthly, Quarterly, and Bi-Monthly subscription plans.
	+ **Monthly**: A month-by-month subscription plan.
	+ **Quarterly**: A quarter-by-quarter subscription plan.
	+ **Bi-Monthly**: A bi-monthly subscription plan.
* **Start Date (date)**: Enter your start date for the subscription here. 
* **Payment Method (select_multiple)**: Choose between Credit Card and PayPal as your payment method.
	+ **Credit Card**: Pay with your credit card.
	+ **PayPal**: Pay with PayPal.
* **Payment Amount (number)**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay per subscription period.
* **Address (text)**: Enter your address here. This will be used for delivery purposes. 
* **City (text)**: Enter your city here. This will help us identify your location.
* **State (text)**: Enter your state here. This will help us identify your location.
* **Zip Code (text)**: Enter your zip code here. This will help us confirm your address.
