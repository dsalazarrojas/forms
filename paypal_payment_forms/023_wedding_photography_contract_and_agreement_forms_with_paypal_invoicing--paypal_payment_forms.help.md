# Wedding Photography Contract And Agreement Forms With Paypal Invoicing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and store client and payment information for wedding photography services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client's contract and agreement details on the first page.
2. Provide the client's billing details on the second page.
3. Enter the wedding date and time on the third page.
4. Provide the client's contact information, including name, phone, and email.
5. Enter the payment amount, method, and date on the fourth page.
6. Enter the PayPal token, email, shipping name, address, city, state, and zip on the fifth page.
7. Enter any additional PayPal note, tax rate, and currency on the sixth page.
8. Sign the client's signature on the last page.

* **Photography Contract** (Page 1, id: 1): Enter the client's contract and agreement details.
* **Photography Agreement** (Page 1, id: 2): Enter any additional agreement terms and conditions.
* **Billing Details** (Page 2, id: 3): Enter the billing details for the client, including billing name, date, and payment method.

* **Client Phone** (Page 2, id: 5): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (Page 2, id: 6): Enter the client's email address.
* **Wedding Date** (Page 3, id: 7): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Wedding Time** (Page 3, id: 8): Enter the time of the wedding.
* **Client Address** (Page 3, id: 9): Enter the client's address.
* **Payment Amount** (Page 4, id: 10): Enter the payment amount for the services.
* **Payment Method** (Page 4, id: 11): Enter the payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Date** (Page 4, id: 12): Enter the date of the payment.
* **PayPal Token** (Page 5, id: 13): Enter the PayPal token for the payment.
* **PayPal Email** (Page 5, id: 14): Enter the PayPal email for the payment.
