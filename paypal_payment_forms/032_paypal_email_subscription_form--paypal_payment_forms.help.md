# PayPal Email Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to subscribe to a newsletter and optionally receive updates. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select whether or not to subscribe to the newsletter.
2.  Enter your email address.
3.  Confirm your email address.
4.  Select whether or not to receive updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscribe to Newsletter**: This field allows you to choose whether or not to subscribe to the newsletter. You can select one or more options.
* **Email Address**: This field is for entering your email address. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Confirm Email Address**: This field is for confirming your email address. Select 'Yes' if your email address is correct.
* **Subscribe to Updates**: This field allows you to choose whether or not to receive updates. You can select one or more options.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* If you have any issues, contact us for assistance.
