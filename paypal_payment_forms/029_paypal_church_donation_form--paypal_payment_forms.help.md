# PayPal Church Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for one-time or recurring donations to a church, allowing individuals and organizations to make easy and secure online donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information on the first page.
2. Select the donation frequency on the second page.
3. Choose your currency on the third page.
4. Optionally provide additional information on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information
* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required/optional): This page is used to collect your name and other user details. Please enter your name, email address and any other relevant information.

### Donation Frequency
* **Donation Frequency** (`Frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of donation you wish to make. Choose from one-time, regular, or subscription options.

### Currency
* **Currency** (`Currency`, `select_one`, required): Select the currency you wish to use for your donation. This will be used to process your donation. Choose from USD, EUR, or GBP options.

### Other Currency
* **Other** (`Other`, `text`, optional): If you do not see your currency listed, please specify the alternative currency you wish to use in this field. Note that this option will not be displayed in the dropdown menu.

### Frequency (Other)
* **Other** (`Other`, `text`, optional): If you do not see your frequency of giving listed, please specify your preferred frequency in this field. Note that this option will not be displayed in the dropdown menu.

### Purpose
* **Purpose** (`Purpose`, `text`, optional): Optionally, enter a brief description of the purpose of your donation. This will help the church understand how to utilize the funds.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or additional information about your donation. This is a text field for any additional notes or details.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`Comments`, `text`, optional): This field is identical to the previous one and serves as an additional text box for any comments or additional information.
