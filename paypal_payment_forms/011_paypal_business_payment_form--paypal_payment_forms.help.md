# PayPal Business Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about PayPal payment forms for business use, allowing merchants to provide payment options to their customers. This form helps in creating a smooth transaction experience by collecting necessary details about the form title, description, category, price, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the form title, which will be displayed on the form for customer reference.
2. Write a brief description of the form, providing customers with more information about what they can expect.
3. Select a category for the form from the options provided, such as "PayPal Payment Forms" or "Other Forms".
4. If desired, provide a short description of the form for quick reference.
5. Enter the price of the form.
6. Choose the currency for the price from the options provided.
7. Select the currency symbol to be used for the price.
8. Decide if you want to include buttons on the form for customers to easily access payment options.
9. If desired, provide a hint for the customer to understand the buttons' purpose.
10. Write any additional terms related to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title for the form that customers will see.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Describe the form in a few sentences to provide customers with more information.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, select_one, optional): Choose a category for the form, such as "PayPal Payment Forms" or "Other Forms".
* **Category Hint** (`form_category_hint`, text, optional): Provide a hint to help customers understand the selected category.
* **Form Description (Short)** (`form_description_short`, text, optional): Enter a short description of the form for quick reference.
* **Form Description (Long)** (`form_description_long`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the form.
* **Form Price** (`form_price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the form.
* **Currency** (`form_price_currency`, select_one, optional): Choose the currency for the price from the options provided.
* **Currency Symbol** (`form_currency_symbol`, select_one, optional): Select the currency symbol to be used for the price.
* **Buttons** (`form_buttons`, select_multiple, optional): Decide if you want to include buttons on the form for customers to easily access payment options.
* **Buttons Hint** (`form_buttons_hint`, text, optional): If desired, provide a hint to help customers understand the buttons' purpose.
* **Terms** (`form_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms related to the form.
