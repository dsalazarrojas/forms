# payPal_Clothing_Order_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal_Clothing_Order_Form is a tool used to collect customer information, product details, shipping information, payment details, and order summary from customers who want to place an order for clothing online or in-store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the clothing product(s) you wish to order, including any specific styles, sizes, and colors.
3. Enter your shipping information, including your address, city, state, and zip code.
4. Fill in your payment information, including your payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal) and payment amount.
5. Summarize your order in the final section, including any notes or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number to help us process your order quickly and efficiently.
* **Product Information (product_info)**: Describe the clothing product(s) you wish to order, including the style, size, and color.
* **Shipping Information (shipping_info)**: Enter your address, city, state, and zip code to ensure accurate shipping of your order.
* **Payment Information (payment_info)**: Provide your payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal) and payment amount to complete the payment process.
* **Order Summary (order_summary)**: Summarize your order, including any notes or special requests, to help us fulfill your order accurately.
