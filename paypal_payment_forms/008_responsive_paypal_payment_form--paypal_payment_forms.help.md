# Responsive Paypal Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Paypal Payment Form is designed to facilitate secure and convenient online payments to a store.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your User Information, including your name and email address.
2.  Enter your Product Information, including the product you are purchasing.
3.  Provide your payment details, including the payment method (PayPal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer), payment email address, and payment amount.
4.  If you are making a payment, confirm the product price.
5.  Add any additional notes you'd like to include with your payment.
6.  Review your information carefully and select "Submit" to complete the form.
7.  If you need to cancel, select "Cancel".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and email address in this field.
*   **Product Information** (`product_info`, text, required/optional): Provide the product you are purchasing in this field.
*   **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment details in this field.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the payment method you would like to use for this payment (PayPal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
*   **Email** (`paypal_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in this field.
*   **Number** (`paypal_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in this field.
*   **Amount** (`paypal_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the payment amount in this field.
*   **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the product you are purchasing.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your payment in this field.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Submit" to complete the payment or "Cancel" to cancel the payment.
