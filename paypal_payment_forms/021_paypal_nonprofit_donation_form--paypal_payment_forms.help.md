# PayPal Nonprofit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Nonprofit Donation Form is designed to collect donations from individuals and organizations to support your nonprofit's causes. It's a secure and efficient way to receive donations and track donor information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine the donation amount you wish to make.
2.  Select the payment method for your donation (Yes or No).
3.  Enter your name, email, phone number, street address, city, state, and zip code.
4.  Provide a comment about your donation (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, **Required**): This field is for you to input the amount of your donation. Please enter a numerical value in the box.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, **Optional**): This field allows you to choose how you'd like to make your donation. Select "Yes" to proceed with a payment or "No" to donate in other ways.
*   **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, **Optional**): This field is for you to input your name as the donor.
*   **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, `email`, **Optional**): Enter your email address here. This will help us contact you with updates and information about your donation.
*   **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, `text`, **Optional**): If you prefer to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number here.
*   **Donor Street** (`donor_street`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the street address where you'd like to receive mail or other communications from us.
*   **Donor City** (`donor_city`, `text`, **Optional**): Input the city where you live.
*   **Donor State** (`donor_state`, `text`, **Optional**): Input the state where you live.
*   **Donor Zip Code** (`donor_zip_code`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your zip code for mailing purposes.
*   **Donation Comment** (`donation_comment`, `text`, **Optional**): This is an optional field where you can provide any comments or messages about your donation.
