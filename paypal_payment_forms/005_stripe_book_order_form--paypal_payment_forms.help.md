# Stripe Book Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Book Order Form is used to collect information from customers about a book order. Please fill out the form to help us process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, marked as required.
2. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
3. Submit the form to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you about your order.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Note (4)**: Add any additional comments or questions about your order.
