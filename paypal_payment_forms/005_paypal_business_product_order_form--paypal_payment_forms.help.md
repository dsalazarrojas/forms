# PayPal Business Product Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Business Product Order Form is a user-facing form designed to help business clients place orders for products or services with their PayPal business account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. For optional fields, provide the information if it's applicable to your order.
3. Review your order for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the product or service you are ordering.
* **Product Price** (`product_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the product or service.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, required): Enter the quantity of the product or service you are ordering.
* **Order Date** (`order_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to place the order.
* **Order Time** (`order_time`, time, required): Enter the time you want to place the order.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address if it's different from your billing address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your order details before submitting to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your PayPal business account representative for assistance.
* This form is only for business clients; if you are a personal customer, please use the personal order form.
