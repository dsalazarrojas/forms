# PayPal Webinar Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from users interested in attending a webinar. The gathered data will help us confirm attendance and provide personalized communication for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully complete this form, follow the instructions below:

- Ensure your email address is correct, as it will be used to send you webinar-related updates and communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you would like to receive webinar updates and communication.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone for any last-minute updates or confirmations.
* **Webinar Date** (`webinar_date`, date, optional): Choose the date you'd like to attend the webinar. Please note that this field is not used for scheduling purposes.
* **Webinar Time** (`webinar_time`, time, optional): Select the time slot you would like to attend the webinar. Please note that this field is not used for scheduling purposes.
* **Webinar Type** (`webinar_type`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or multiple options for your preferred webinar topics:
		- **Webinar Title**: Select to receive information about titles.
		- **Webinar Description**: Select to receive information about descriptions.
		- **Speaker Bio**: Select to receive information about the speaker.
		- **Webinar Agenda**: Select to receive information about the agenda.
		- **Webinar Location**: Select to receive information about locations.
		- **Webinar Language**: Select to receive information about languages.
		- **Additional Details**: Select to receive any additional information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional details you'd like to provide about your attendance or preferences.
* **Confirm Webinar** (`confirm_webinar`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have confirmed your attendance.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not confirmed your attendance.
* **Confirm Webinar Time** (`confirm_webinar_time`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have confirmed the time of your attendance.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not confirmed the time of your attendance.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the email address you provide is correct and active, as this is the primary channel for communication regarding the webinar.
- If you select multiple options for Webinar Type, please be prepared to receive separate emails with information relevant to each selected topic.
- For any issues or concerns, contact us at your preferred phone number or email address.
- If you have already signed up for this webinar, please do not fill out this form again.
