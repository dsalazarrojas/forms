# paypal_purchase_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `paypal_purchase_order_form` is a form designed to collect user details and shipping information for a purchase order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. User Details
* **User Details** (text): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details to identify yourself.

### 2. Shipping Address
* **Shipping Address** (text): Enter your shipping address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.

### 3. Payment Info
* **Payment Info** (text): Enter your payment information, including payment method and payment details.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to avoid any issues with your purchase order.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to our support team for assistance.
