# ประกийlaypal_newsletter_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for subscribers to personalize their experience with PayPal's newsletter by providing personal details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
3. Select your preferred format for the newsletter in the **Preferred Format** field (optional).
4. Choose your desired subscription duration in the **Subscription Duration** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you want to receive the newsletter.
* **Preferred Format**: Select "Yes" if you want to receive the newsletter in a specific format (e.g., HTML or plain text). Select "No" if you don't have a preference.
* **Subscription Duration**: Choose your desired subscription duration (e.g., "Yes" for a one-time subscription or "No" for no subscription duration).
