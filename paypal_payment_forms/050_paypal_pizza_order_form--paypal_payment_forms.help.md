# paypal_pizza_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal pizza order form is a simple form used to collect order details, payment method and amount, and delivery information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form in its entirety**. 
2. Make sure to select a valid payment method (Paypal, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer).
3. Enter the correct payment amount.
4. Select a valid delivery date and time.
5. Fill in any special instructions or comments if desired.
6. Enter your email address (if available).
7. Enter your phone number (if available).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Order Details**: Enter the details of your order, such as type of pizza and any other relevant information.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you wish to use for your order. You may select from three options: **Paypal**, **Credit Card**, or **Bank Transfer**.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to pay for your order. This must be a numeric value.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any additional information you have regarding your order that is not specified in the previous fields.
* **Order Comments**: Enter any comments you have about your order.
* **Delivery Date**: Select the date on which you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Delivery Time**: Select the time on which you would like your order to be delivered.
* **Order Notes**: Enter any additional notes you have about your order.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you wish to receive any communications from us).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you wish to receive any communications from us).

Note: Fields marked as required will be highlighted in bold in the actual form. 

**Tips:**
- Always make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your payment method and amount before submitting.
- Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number if you wish to receive communications.
- If you have any special instructions or comments, please enter them in the corresponding fields.
