# Special Education Teacher Interview Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from special education teachers applying for a position in our organization. It collects their educational background, work experience, and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Candidate Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide your work experience in the Experience section.
3. List your educational background in the Educational Background section.
4. Choose the relevant certifications from the Certifications section.
5. Upload required documents in the Upload Documents section.
6. Provide additional information in the Additional Information section (optional).
