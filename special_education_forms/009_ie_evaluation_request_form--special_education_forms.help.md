# IE Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IE Evaluation Request Form is used to collect information for the evaluation of students for potential Individualized Education Program (IEP) eligibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter a brief description in the text field for the title of your evaluation request.
2. Enter the student's name in the text field provided.
3. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the school team's involvement in the evaluation process.
4. Enter the evaluation date in the date field.
5. Enter the evaluation time in the time field.
6. Describe the student's concerns, if any, in the text field.
7. If follow-up is necessary, enter the follow-up date and time in the date and time fields, respectively.
8. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for who submitted the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Form Title** (`title`): Enter a brief description of your evaluation request.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the name of the student being evaluated.
* **School Team Involvement** (`school_team`): Check 'Yes' if the school team is involved in the evaluation process; otherwise, check 'No'.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`): Enter the time of the evaluation.
* **Concerns** (`concerns`): Describe any concerns related to the student's evaluation.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`): Enter the follow-up date, if applicable.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`): Enter the follow-up time, if applicable.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Check 'Yes' if the request was submitted by the school team; otherwise, check 'No'.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Only submit one form per student.
* If you are unsure about the evaluation process or have questions, please consult with the evaluation team.
* The evaluation team will review and process your request accordingly.
