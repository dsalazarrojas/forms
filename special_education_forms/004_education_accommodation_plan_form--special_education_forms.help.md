# Education Accommodation Plan Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Accommodation Plan Form is a vital tool for educators to gather information about a student's educational needs and accommodations required for their education. This form helps to identify individualized support and ensure that each student receives the necessary resources to succeed academically.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and ID number.
2. Enter the student's birthdate.
3. Indicate whether the student requires support needs.
4. Confirm parent consent for the accommodation plan.
5. Enter the school's name and address.
6. Provide the teacher's name and title.
7. Specify the accommodation requests and plans for the student.
8. Enter any additional student notes.
9. Confirm parent consent for the accommodation plan (if applicable).
10. Enter the student's email and phone number.
11. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Education Accommodation Plan Form** (`education_accommodation_plan_form`): This is the form title, which should be filled in accurately.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id_number`): Enter the student's unique ID number assigned by the school or institution.
* **Student Birthdate** (`student_birthdate`): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Support Needs** (`support_needs`): Check the boxes to indicate what support the student requires, such as accommodations, modifications, or other educational needs.
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`): Check the box indicating that you have obtained parent consent for the accommodation plan. If applicable, also confirm parent consent for the second instance.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the school's name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`): Enter the school's address.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`): Enter the teacher's name and title.
* **Teacher Title** (`teacher_title`): Enter the teacher's title.
* **Accommodation Requests** (`accommodation_requests`): Specify the specific accommodations or modifications required for the student's education.
* **Accommodation Plans** (`accommodation_plans`): Describe the plans for implementing the student's accommodations and modifications.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the student's educational needs.
* **Parent Notes** (`parent_notes`): Enter any additional notes from parents or guardians.
* **School Notes** (`school_notes`): Enter any additional notes from the school staff.
* **School Contacts** (`school_contacts`): Enter contact information for the school staff.
* **Parent Consent 2** (`parent_consent_2`): Check the box to confirm parent consent for the accommodation plan (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Submit the form with all required fields filled in.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Time Submitted** (`time_submitted`): Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Date Submitted** (`submitted_at`): Enter the date the form was submitted (this field is automatically populated).

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in before submission.
