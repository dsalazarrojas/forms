# Child Speech Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child's speech and language development, concerns, and history. It is used to gather data for research and to identify potential needs of the child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
2. Ensure that you are completing the form for the right child and that you have access to their medical and educational records.
3. Answer each question honestly and accurately, based on your knowledge and observations about the child.
4. For date fields, use the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. For questions with select_one options, choose one of the listed options that best describes your experience.
6. You can leave the "Additional Comments" field blank if you do not have any additional information to provide.
7. Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Tips
