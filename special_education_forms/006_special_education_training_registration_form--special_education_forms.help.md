# Special Education Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Training Registration Form is a registration form used to collect information from trainees for special education training programs. This form is typically used to register for special needs training, regular training, or other types of training. The form is designed to be completed by trainees to provide the necessary information for registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of training you are registering for from the "What type of training are you registering for?" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Select the date of the training from the "Training Date" field.
7. Select the duration of the training from the "Training Time" field.
8. Choose the payment method for the training from the "Payment Method" field. You can select one or multiple payment methods.
9. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of training are you registering for?**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the type of training you are registering for. Please select one of the options:
	+ Special Needs
	+ Regular Training
	+ Other
* **First Name**: This is a text field where you can enter your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: This is a text field where you can enter your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: This is an email field where you can enter your email address. Please enter a valid email address where you can receive notifications and updates.
* **Phone Number**: This is a text field where you can enter your phone number. Please enter your phone number as it appears on your official documents.
* **Training Date**: This is a date field where you can select the date of the training. Please select the date on which you plan to attend the training.
* **Training Time**: This is a time field where you can select the duration of the training. Please select the start and end time of the training.
* **Payment Method**: This is a dropdown menu where you can select the payment method for the training. Please select one or multiple payment methods from the options:
	+ Cash
	+ Check
	+ Bank Transfer
* **Comments**: This is a note field where you can enter any additional comments or information about your registration. Please use this field to provide any relevant details about your training needs or preferences.
* **Submit**: This is a text field that you must click to complete and submit the form. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
