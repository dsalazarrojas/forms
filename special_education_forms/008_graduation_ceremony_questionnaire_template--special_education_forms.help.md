# Graduation Ceremony Questionnaire Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graduation Ceremony Questionnaire Template is a document used to collect detailed information about a graduation ceremony for future reference and planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required information.
2. Gather all necessary details about the graduation ceremony before starting to fill out the form.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Graduation ceremony details** (`graduation_ceremony_details`, `text`, required): This field is for entering the details of the graduation ceremony, including any notable events or activities that took place during the ceremony.
* **Graduation date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the graduation ceremony in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Graduation time** (`graduation_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation location** (`graduation_location`, `text`, required): Enter the location where the graduation ceremony took place.
* **Graduation attire** (`graduation_attire`, `text`, required): Enter a description of the graduation attire, if any, including color or style.
* **Graduation attendees** (`graduation_attendees`, `text`, required): List the names of attendees of the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation speaker** (`graduation_speaker`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the graduation speaker, if applicable.
* **Graduation notes** (`graduation_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation email** (`graduation_email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address for the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation phone** (`graduation_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number for the graduation ceremony.
* **Graduation survey** (`graduation_survey`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the types of feedback that were gathered during the graduation ceremony, such as Alumni feedback, Parent feedback, Student feedback, Faculty feedback, Staff feedback, or Other.
* **Graduation other** (`graduation_other`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information about the graduation ceremony that did not fit into any of the other fields.
