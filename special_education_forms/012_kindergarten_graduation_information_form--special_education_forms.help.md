# Kindergarten Graduation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Graduation Information Form is designed to gather information about your kindergarten experience. This form will help us document your progress and achievements and provide a record of your time in kindergarten.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and other personal details on the "Student Information" page.
2. Provide your parent's name and contact information on the "Parent Information" page.
3. Write your teacher's name and contact information on the "Teacher Information" page.
4. Enter the date of your graduation on the "Graduation Date" page.
5. Check your certification type (Kindergarten Graduation Certification, High School Diploma, or Other) on the "Graduation Certification" page.
6. Add any comments or additional information on the "Comments" page.
7. Confirm that you have completed the form by selecting "True" on the "Form Complete" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information (1)** (`student_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and other personal details.
* **Parent Information (2)** (`parent_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your parent's name and contact information.
* **Teacher Information (3)** (`teacher_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your teacher's name and contact information.
* **Graduation Date (4)** (`graduation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your graduation in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Graduation Certification (5)** (`graduation_certification`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your certification type (Kindergarten Graduation Certification, High School Diploma, or Other).
* **Teacher Signature (6)** (`teacher_signature`, note, required/optional): Please sign your name to confirm that you have completed this form.
* **Comments (7)** (`comments`, note, required/optional): Add any comments or additional information about your kindergarten experience.
* **Form Complete (8)** (`form_complete`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check that you have completed this form.
