# Classroom Support Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Classroom Support Agreement is a form used to document the support services provided to a student in a classroom setting. It outlines the specific services and accommodations needed for the student, as well as the support team members and parents/guardians involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name, student ID number, date of birth, and grade level.
2. Select the type(s) of support services needed for the student.
3. Choose the frequency and duration of support services.
4. Specify the start and end dates for the support services.
5. List any accommodations or modifications needed for the student.
6. Enter the name and contact information of the case manager.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the paraprofessional (if applicable).
8. Select the related service providers involved.
9. Enter your own name and signature (as the classroom teacher, administrator, and parent/guardian).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, required): The full name of the student, as it appears on their records.
* **Student ID Number** (`Student ID Number`, required): The student's unique identification number.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, required): The student's date of birth.
* **Grade Level** (`Grade Level`, required): The student's current grade level.
* **Classroom Teacher** (`Classroom Teacher`, required): The name of the primary classroom teacher.
* **Classroom Number** (`Classroom Number`, required): The room number or location of the classroom.
* **Type of Support Needed** (`Type of Support Needed`, required): Select all applicable support services, such as:
	+ One-on-One Paraprofessional Support
	+ Small Group Instruction
	+ Speech Therapy
	+ Occupational Therapy
	+ Physical Therapy
	+ Behavioral Support
	+ Academic Intervention
	+ Assistive Technology Support
	+ Transportation Assistance
	+ Other
* **Support Frequency** (`Support Frequency`, required): Choose the frequency of support services, such as:
	+ Daily - Full day
	+ Daily - Partial day
	+ Several times per week
	+ Once per week
	+ As needed
	+ Other schedule
* **Support Duration** (`Support Duration`, required): Specify the duration of support services.
* **Support Start Date** (`Support Start Date`, required): The start date for support services.
* **Support End Date or Review Date** (`Support End Date or Review Date`, optional): The date support services will end or be reviewed.
* **Support Goals and Objectives** (`Support Goals and Objectives`, required): Specify the specific goals for this support.
* **Accommodations Needed** (`Accommodations Needed`, required): List any classroom accommodations required for the student.
* **Modifications Needed** (`Modifications Needed`, optional): List any curriculum modifications required for the student.
* **Case Manager Name** (`Case Manager Name`, required): The name of the case manager coordinating services.
* **Case Manager Email** (`Case Manager Email`, required): The contact email address for the case manager.
* **Case Manager Phone** (`Case Manager Phone`, required): The contact phone number for the case manager.
* **Paraprofessional Assigned** (`Paraprofessional Assigned`, optional): The name of assigned paraprofessional (if applicable).
* **Related Service Providers** (`Related Service Providers`, optional): The names of therapists or specialists involved.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`Parent or Guardian Name`, required): The full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`Parent or Guardian Email`, required): The contact email address for the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`Parent or Guardian Phone`, required): The contact phone number for the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Consent** (`Parent or Guardian Consent`, required): Select one of the options:
	+ Yes - I consent to all services listed
	+ Yes - I consent with modifications
	+ No - I need to discuss further
* **Parent or Guardian Comments** (`Parent or Guardian Comments`, optional): Any questions or concerns from parents.
* **Classroom Teacher Signature** (`Classroom Teacher Signature`, required): The teacher's electronic signature.
* **Classroom Teacher Signature Date** (`Classroom Teacher Signature Date`, required): The date of the teacher's signature.
* **Administrator Signature** (`Administrator Signature`, required): The administrator's electronic signature.
* **Administrator Signature Date** (`Administrator Signature Date`, required): The date of the administrator's signature.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`Parent or Guardian Signature`, required): The parent's electronic signature.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature Date** (`Parent or Guardian Signature Date`, required): The date of the parent's signature.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
- Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
- If there are any questions or concerns, please contact the support team.
