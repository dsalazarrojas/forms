# special_education_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from special education professionals to assess the needs and challenges of individuals with special needs, providing a comprehensive checklist for reference and supporting the creation of tailored support plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand its purpose and ensure you're not duplicating any existing work.
2. Answer each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible, providing as much detail as necessary.
3. Use the provided fields to gather information on the individual's needs and progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **section-1** (`section-1`, `text`, required/optional): This section is a free-text field where you can provide a brief description of the individual's special needs or challenges.
* **section-2** (`section-2`, `text`, required/optional): This section is another free-text field where you can elaborate on the individual's needs or progress.
* **page-2** (`page-2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a text input where you can provide more information about the individual's needs or progress.
* **page-3** (`page-3`, `text`, required/optional): This field is a text input where you can provide additional information on the individual's needs or progress.

## Tips
* Be as thorough and accurate as possible when providing information in each field.
* Use clear and concise language to ensure understanding.
* If you're unsure about a field, contact your supervisor or colleague for guidance.
* Please note that this form is intended for internal use within your organization, and the information collected will help support the creation of tailored support plans for individuals with special needs.
