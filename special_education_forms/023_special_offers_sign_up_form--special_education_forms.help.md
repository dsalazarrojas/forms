# Special Offers Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Offers Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in special offers. This form helps the organization understand the users' preferences and provide targeted promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal details.
2. Select the type of newsletter you would like to receive.
3. Confirm your personal details and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Special Offers Sign Up**: Enter your name in this field.
* **Newsletter**: Choose the type of newsletter you would like to receive.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Special Offers**: Choose the type of special offer you are interested in.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your interest in the special offer.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or details.
* **Confirm First Name**: Confirm your first name.
* **Confirm Last Name**: Confirm your last name.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Special Offers**: Confirm your interest in the special offer.
* **Confirm Address**: Confirm your address.
* **Confirm Newsletter Selection**: Confirm your newsletter subscription.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or details.

## Tips
* Please enter your details accurately to ensure that you receive the correct offers and news.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.
