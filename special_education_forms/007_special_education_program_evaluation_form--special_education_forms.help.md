# Special Education Program Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Program Evaluation Form is designed to help educators and administrators assess the effectiveness of special education programs. It collects information on student progress, program goals, and school settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the relevant information about the program being evaluated.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* "progress_assessment" (assessing the student's progress)
	* "school_level" (the level of the school where the student attends)
	* "school_size" (the size of the school where the student attends)
	* "grade_level" (the grade level of the student)
3. Provide additional relevant information for the student, school, and program:
	+ "student_name" (student's name)
	+ "school_name" (school name)
	+ "program_name" (program name)
	+ "program_goals" (program goals)
	+ "program_implementation" (program implementation details)
	+ "district_name" (district name)
	+ "school_building" (school building name)
4. Select the submission method:
	* "form_submission_workflow" (how the form will be submitted, e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox, or Email)
5. Provide any additional comments:
	+ "form_comments" (comments about the form completion process)
	+ "additional_comments" (additional comments about the program)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_name**: The student's name to be used for reference.
* **school_name**: The name of the school where the student attends.
* **program_name**: The name of the program being evaluated.
* **program_goals**: A description of the program's goals.
* **program_implementation**: Details about how the program is implemented.
* **progress_assessment**: Assessment of the student's progress.
* **form_submission_workflow**: Select the method for submitting the form (e.g., Google Drive, Dropbox, or Email).
* **form_comments**: Comments about the form completion process.
* **additional_comments**: Additional comments about the program.
* **date**: The date the form is completed.
* **time**: The time the form is completed.
* **email**: The email address to contact for further information.
* **phone**: The phone number to contact for further information.
* **district_name**: The name of the district the school is part of.
* **school_district**: The district of the school.
* **school_building**: The name of the school building.
* **school_level**: The level of the school (e.g., elementary, middle, high).
* **school_size**: The size of the school (e.g., small, medium, large).
* **grade_level**: The grade level of the student.
* **program_year**: The year the program was implemented.
* **student_participation**: The student's participation level.
* **teacher_participation**: The teacher's participation level.
* **form_completion_date**: The date the form is completed.
* **additional_comments**: Additional comments about the program.
