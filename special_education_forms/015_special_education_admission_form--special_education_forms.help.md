# Special Education Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's personal details, educational background, and any additional needs or services required for special education purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
* Provide accurate and thorough information about the student's details.
* Choose the relevant options for the student's educational background and any additional needs or services required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, optional)
Provide a brief description of the student.

### 2. **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required)
Enter the student's name.

### 3. **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, optional)
Enter the student's age.

### 4. **Special Education Programs** (`special_education_programs`, select_one, optional)
Choose from the available special education programs:
	* Special Education Programs
	* Inclusion and Transition
	* Gifted and Talented
	* Hearing Impairment
	* Multiple Disabilities
	* Visual Impairment
	* Learning Disability
	* Physical Disability

### 5. **Student Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`, text, optional)
Provide contact information for the student.

### 6. **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional)
Enter the parent's name.

### 7. **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional)
Enter the contact number.

### 8. **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter the email address.

### 9. **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional)
Provide the student's address.

### 10. **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, select_one, optional)
Choose from the available guardian relationships:
	* Guardian - Mother
	* Guardian - Father
	* Guardian - Other
	* Guardian - Not Specified
	* Guardian - Both

### 11. **Student Special Education Services** (`student_special_education_services`, select_one, optional)
Choose from the available options:
	* True
	* False

### 12. **Student Language** (`student_language`, select_multiple, optional)
Choose from the available languages:
