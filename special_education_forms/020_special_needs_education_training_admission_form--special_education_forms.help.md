# Special Needs Education Training Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the Special Needs Education Training Admission process. It helps the admission team to gather necessary details about the student, their category, special needs, and other relevant information to streamline the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category that best describes the student's needs.
2. Provide a brief description of the student's needs.
3. Select the special needs assistance required for the student.
4. Enter the student's date of birth.
5. Enter the student's email address.
6. Enter the date for the Special Needs Education Training Admission Form submission.
7. If applicable, select the assigned tool and output file for the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Category 1, Category 2, Category 3): Select the category that best describes the student's needs. This will help the admission team to better understand the student's requirements.
* **Description** (Description): Briefly describe the student's needs or requirements. This will help the admission team to have a better understanding of the student's situation.
* **Student Info** (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3): Select the student's information as it relates to their special needs. This will help the admission team to identify the student's needs and requirements.
* **Special Needs Assistance** (Needs 1, Needs 2, Needs 3): Select the special needs assistance required for the student. This will help the admission team to provide the necessary support for the student.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth. This is a mandatory field to ensure that the student's age and eligibility are verified.
* **Email**: Enter the student's email address. This will help the admission team to communicate with the student or their representative.
* **Special Needs Education Training Admission Form Date**: Enter the date for the Special Needs Education Training Admission Form submission. This is a mandatory field to ensure that the form is submitted on the correct date.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter the student's phone number. This will help the admission team to contact the student or their representative for further information or clarification.
* **Assigned Tool** (Tool 1, Tool 2, Tool 3): If applicable, select the assigned tool for the student's special needs education training. This will help the admission team to provide the necessary tool and support for the student.
* **Output File** (File 1, File 2, File 3): If applicable, select the output file for the student's special needs education training. This will help the admission team to track the student's progress and provide feedback.
