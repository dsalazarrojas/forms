# Self Help Skills Progress Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Help Skills Progress Report form is designed to track a student's progress in special education services. It is used to record and report observations, goals, and reviews of a student's skills over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **User Input**: Please enter your unique identification number.
2. **Observations**: Record your observations about your skills over time. Be as detailed as possible, and include any notable progress or challenges you've faced.
3. **Goals**: Set goals for skills you would like to learn or improve.
4. **Review**: Review and update your progress regularly. Check off any goals you've achieved and add new ones as needed.
5. **Date**: Enter the date of your most recent progress review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Input** (Number)
Enter your unique student identification number.

### 2. **Observations** (Text)
Record any notable observations about your skills over time. This field is used to track your progress and note any changes or challenges you've faced.

### 3. **Goals** (Text)
Set specific goals for skills you would like to learn or improve. Be clear and concise, and try to specify exactly what you aim to accomplish.

### 4. **Review** (Text)
Review and update your progress regularly. Check off any goals you've achieved and add new ones as needed. This field is used to track your progress and identify areas where you need improvement.

### 5. **Date** (Date)
Enter the date of your most recent progress review.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when recording your observations and goals.
* Review your progress regularly to ensure you're on track to meet your goals.
* Use this form to track your progress and identify areas where you need improvement.
