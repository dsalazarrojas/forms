# Special Education Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students who require special education services. It will help you understand their specific needs and determine their eligibility for such services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Student Name** field with the student's name.
2. Enter the student's **Birthdate** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Provide the student's **Contact Info**, including their contact number and email address.
4. Select the student's **Student Type** from the provided options (Student with disability, Student with medical exception, or Student with other disability).
5. Check the box next to the relevant **Student Status** options (Currently enrolled, New transfer student, or Returning student) that apply to the student.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name**: Enter the student's name in the format First Name Last Name.
- **Birthdate**: Enter the student's birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Contact Info**: Enter the student's contact number and email address.
  * Contact Number: Enter the student's phone number.
  * Email Address: Enter the student's email address.
- **Student Type**: Select the category that best describes the student's situation.
  * Student with disability: The student has a disability that requires special education services.
  * Student with medical exception: The student has a medical condition that requires special education services.
  * Student with other disability: The student has another type of disability that requires special education services.
- **Student Status**: Check the boxes for the options that apply to the student's current situation.
  * Currently enrolled: The student is currently enrolled in the school.
  * New transfer student: The student is transferring to the school.
  * Returning student: The student is returning to the school.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If unsure about any field, please contact the special education team for assistance.
* This form is a template and may need to be adapted to fit the specific needs of your institution.
* Keep in mind that the purpose of this form is to gather information and determine eligibility for special education services.
