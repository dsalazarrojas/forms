# IEP Scheduling Notes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "IEP Scheduling Notes" form is used to document and track meeting information, scheduling details, and follow-up actions for students with Individualized Education Plans (IEPs).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Meeting Info" field with details about the meeting, including the date and location.
2. Enter the "Meeting Date" and "Meeting Time" fields with the date and time of the scheduled meeting.
3. In the "Attendees" field, select the relevant staff members and students who will be attending the meeting.
4. In the "Follow-up" field, describe any follow-up actions that are needed after the meeting.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
6. Finally, enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Info**: Enter a brief description of the meeting, including the purpose and details.
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date of the scheduled meeting.
* **Meeting Time**: Enter the time of the scheduled meeting.
* **Meeting Location**: Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Summary**: Enter a brief summary of the meeting.
* **Attendees**:
	+ Select "Yes" if the relevant staff members and students will be attending the meeting.
* **Attendees**: Select "Yes" for any staff members and students who will be attending the meeting.
* **Follow-up**: Enter any follow-up actions that are needed after the meeting.
* **Follow-up Actions**: Enter any additional follow-up actions that are needed.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the meeting.

# Tips
* Ensure that you are completing this form for the correct meeting and student.
* If you have already completed a previous form for the same student, please update the existing form instead of submitting a new one.
* Use the "Attendees" field to select relevant staff members and students who will be attending the meeting.
* Use the "Follow-up" field to describe any follow-up actions that are needed after the meeting.
* Keep the "Comments" and "Notes" fields for any additional comments or notes about the meeting.
* Submit this form as soon as possible after the meeting to ensure that the information is up-to-date.
