# Engagement Story Session Booking - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients book an engagement story session with a service provider. It collects client details, session preferences, payment information, and other relevant data to confirm the booking.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Details**: Please enter your name, contact person, and other relevant details about yourself or your group (if booking for multiple people).
2. **Date And Time**: Select the desired date and time for your engagement story session. Please choose from the available time slots (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM, 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM, 3:00 PM - 6:00 PM).
3. **Session Details**: Provide additional information about your session, such as any specific needs or requests you may have.
4. **Booking Status**: Indicate your booking status (Active, Inactive) to confirm your availability.
5. **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (Bank, Credit Card, PayPal).
6. **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment information required for your booking.
7. **Session Schedule**: Confirm your session schedule based on the selected date and time.
8. **Session Status**: Indicate the status of your session (Pending, Active, Completed).
9. **Session Type**: Select the type of session you are booking (Couple, Individual, Family).
10. **Follow Up**: Enter any follow-up information or additional details about your session.
11. **Session Details 2**: Provide any additional information about your session that may not have been covered in the previous fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`): Enter your name and contact person's name to ensure we can reach out to you about your session.
* **Date And Time** (`date_and_time`): Select the date and time that works best for your engagement story session.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`): Share any specific needs or requests you may have for your session.
* **Booking Status** (`booking_status`): Confirm your availability by selecting Active if you are ready to book your session.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose your preferred payment method for the session.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): Enter any additional payment information required.
* **Session Schedule** (`session_schedule`): Confirm your session schedule based on your selected date and time.
* **Session Status** (`session_status`): Update your session status to reflect the current status (Pending, Active, Completed).
* **Session Type** (`session_type`): Select the type of session you are booking (Couple, Individual, Family).
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`): Enter any follow-up information or additional details about your session.
* **Session Details 2** (`session_details_2`): Provide any additional information about your session that may not have been covered in previous fields.

This guide should provide a clear overview of the form and help clients navigate the booking process for an engagement story session.
