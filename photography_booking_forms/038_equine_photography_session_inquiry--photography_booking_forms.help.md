# Equine Photography Session Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a platform for potential clients to inquire about an equine photography session with a professional photographer. It helps the photographer understand the client's preferences and provide a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and details in the **Photographer** field.
2. Select the **Equine Barn** where the photography session will take place.
3. Describe the **Session Details** in the text area.
4. Choose your **Preferred Dates** for the photography session.
5. Select the start and end dates for the session.
6. Choose a **Package Selection** from the available options.
7. Select your **Preferred Time** for the session.
8. Choose multiple time slots for your **Preferred Time Range**.
9. Enter your **Contact Information** in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photographer**: Enter your name and any relevant details about yourself.
* **Equine Barn**: Select the barn where you would like to have the photography session.
* **Session Details**: Provide a brief description of what you are looking for during the photography session.
* **Preferred Dates**: Choose a date for the photography session that suits you best.
* **Start Date** and **End Date**: Select the start and end dates for the session.
* **Package Selection**: Choose a package that suits your needs and budget.
* **Preferred Time**: Select a time slot for the session.
* **Preferred Time Range**: Select multiple time slots for the session.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details so the photographer can get in touch with you.

## Tips
- Ensure that you are clear and concise in your **Session Details** field.
- Select a package that suits your needs and budget.
- Be flexible with your **Preferred Dates** and time slots to accommodate the photographer's schedule.
- Proofread your **Contact Information** for accuracy. 

Note: The form is designed to be user-friendly and adaptable to your specific needs.
