# Event Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Event Photography Contract form, which is used to collect essential information for creating a comprehensive contract between a client and a photographer for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the event date by clicking on the calendar icon next to the "Event Date" field.
2.  Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3.  Enter your own name as the photographer in the "Photographer Name" field.
4.  If applicable, enter the service date (date when you will provide photography services) in the "Service Date" field.
5.  Provide a brief description of the event in the "Event Details" field.
6.  Describe any specific terms or conditions related to the contract in the "Contract Terms" field.
7.  Indicate your acceptance of the contract terms by selecting either "True" or "False" in the "Accept Contract Terms" field. This confirms that you have read and understand the contract terms.
8.  Specify any details related to payment terms and conditions in the "Payment Terms" field.
9.  Enter the event location where the photography service will take place in the "Event Location" field.
10.  Provide any additional information about the event location in the "Event Location Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event for which you are providing photography services.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client or event organizer.
*   **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your own name as the person providing the photography services.
*   **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required: false): If you will be providing photography services on a date other than the event date, specify it here.
*   **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Give a brief description of the event, such as the occasion, type of event, or any other relevant details.
*   **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, required: false): List any specific terms or conditions you have for the contract, such as cancellation policies or payment terms.
*   **Accept Contract Terms** (`contract_terms_accept`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm whether you agree to the contract terms.
*   **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required: false): Specify any payment terms or conditions, such as payment schedules or methods.
*   **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the physical location of the event.
*   **Event Location Details** (`event_details_location`, text, required: false): Provide additional details about the event location, such as the venue or address.
