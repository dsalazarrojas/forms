# Wedding Photography Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Schedule form is a tool used by photographers to collect and record details about a wedding they've been booked for. This includes information about the couple, the photographer, and the wedding specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Ensure you're filling in the form for a specific wedding booking.
- Enter the correct wedding date.
- Enter the start and end times for the wedding shoot.
- Enter your name and experience as a photographer.
- Select the package that best fits your needs.
- Enter any special requests for the wedding shoot.
- Enter the couple's name.
- Enter their contact number.
- Enter their email address.
- Enter the location of the wedding shoot.
- Enter any additional special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the wedding shoot.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the wedding shoot.
* **Photographer Name** (`photographer_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as the photographer.
* **Photographer Experience** (`photographer_experience`, number, required: false): Enter how many years you have of experience as a photographer.
* **Package Name** (`package_name`, select_one, required: false): Select the package that best fits your needs, such as Wedding Package, Elopement Package, Family Package, etc.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, required: false): Enter any special requests you have for the wedding shoot.
* **Couple Name** (`couple_name`, text, required: false): Enter the couple's name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the couple's contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the couple's email address.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the wedding shoot.
* **Special Request 2** (`special_request_2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional special requests.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields for accurate record-keeping.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the photographer for clarification.
