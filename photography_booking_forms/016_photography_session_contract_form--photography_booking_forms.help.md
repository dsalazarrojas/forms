# photography_session_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a photography session and create a contract between the photographer and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your name and contact information in the **Client Details** field.
* Provide details about the session you are booking, such as the date, time, location, and type of session (e.g. portrait, event, wedding) in the **Session Details** field.
* Read and agree to the terms of the **Photography Session Contract** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (client_details): This field is for you to provide your name and contact information. Please enter your name and any relevant contact details so the photographer can reach out to you.
* **Session Details** (session_details): This field is for you to provide information about the photography session you are booking. Please include the date, time, location, and type of session (e.g. portrait, event, wedding).
* **Photography Session Contract** (photography_session_contract): This field is a standard contract that outlines the terms of the photography session, including payment, cancellation policies, and expectations. Read and agree to the terms before submitting the form.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to carefully read and understand the terms of the **Photography Session Contract** before agreeing to it.
* Double-check your contact information in the **Client Details** field to ensure it is accurate.
* Be specific and detailed in the **Session Details** field to help the photographer prepare for your session.
