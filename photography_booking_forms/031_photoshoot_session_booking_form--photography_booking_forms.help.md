# photoshoot_session_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photoshoot session booking form is a crucial tool for photographers and agencies to manage their booking requests with clients. It is used to collect information about the photoshoot session, model, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you would like to book a photoshoot session by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the "Photoshoot Session" dropdown.
2. If you've selected 'Yes', fill out your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Read and agree to the "Model Consent" form by typing your consent in the "Model Consent" field.
6. Sign your contract by typing your signature in the "Contract" field.
7. Enter the name of the photographer you are working with in the "Name" field.
8. Provide any additional contact information in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photoshoot Session** (`photoshoot_session_booking`, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' to book a photoshoot session or 'No' if you do not want to book.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Model Consent** (`model_consent`, text, required: false): Read and agree to the model consent form.
* **Contract** (`contract`, text, required: false): Please sign your contract here to confirm your booking.
* **Name** (`photographer`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the photographer you are working with.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional contact information for us to reach you.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that some fields are not required, but it is recommended to fill them out for a smoother communication process.
