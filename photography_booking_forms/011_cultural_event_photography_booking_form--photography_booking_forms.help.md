# cultural_event_photography_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Event Photography Booking Form is designed to help clients book photography services for their events. It gathers essential details from clients, ensuring a smooth and efficient photography experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Provide the necessary information for the "Client Info" and "Event Details" sections.
3. Select the photography package and payment method for your event.
4. Enter the payment details.
5. Provide contact information for yourself and your event.
6. Specify the event date and time, as well as the photography date and time.
7. Complete the "Event Message" section with any additional event details.
8. Select the event venue.
9. Review and submit your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info (Client Info)** (`client_info`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, company, or organization name.
* **Event Details (Event Details)** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Describe the event, its purpose, and any other relevant details.
* **Photo Package (Photo Package)** (`photo_package`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of photography package you would like to book.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Enter the payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Photo Delivery (Photo Delivery)** (`photo_delivery`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you would like to receive your photos (e.g., email, USB drive).
* **Message (Message)** (`message`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments about your event.
* **Photographer's Email (Email)** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone (Phone Number)** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Photo Date (Photo Date)** (`photo_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the photography shoot.
* **Photographer's Time (Photographer's Time)** (`photo_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the photography shoot.
* **Photographer Info (Photographer Info)** (`photographer_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your photographer's name and contact information.
* **Photographer Phone (Photographer Phone)** (`photographer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your photographer's phone number.
* **Package Price (Package Price)** (`photo_package_price`, `number`, required): Enter the total price for your photography package.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method of payment (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Payment Status (Payment Status)** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment status of your event.
* **Event Date (Event Date)** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time (Event Time)** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the event.
* **Venue (Venue)** (`venue`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the venue where the event will take place.
* **Event Message (Event Message)** (`event_message`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your event.
