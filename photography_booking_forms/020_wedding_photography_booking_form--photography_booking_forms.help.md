# Wedding Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers interested in booking a wedding photographer for their special day. It helps us understand their requirements and preferences to provide a tailored and efficient service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
2. Provide details about the event, such as the date and time of the wedding.
3. Choose the services you would like us to offer, like photo shooting or video recording.
4. Enter any additional comments or messages you have for us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Form**: A text field where you can provide any additional information about your wedding.
* **Photographer Details**: This field is not used in this form as it only has `title` and `hint` fields with no specific input fields.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Event Details**: Enter details about your wedding event, such as the date and time.
* **Services Offered**: Select one or more services you would like us to offer, such as photo shooting or video recording.
* **Date Event**: Enter the date of your wedding event.
* **Time Event**: Enter the time of your wedding event.
* **Location Event**: Enter the location of your wedding event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Message**: Enter any additional comments or messages you have for us.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we get the necessary information to process your booking.
* For the "Services Offered" field, select the services you are interested in, and we will be happy to discuss and confirm with you.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the "Message" field.
