# Wedding Photoshoot Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture the necessary details and agreement for a wedding photoshoot, ensuring that both the photographer and client are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client's details, including their name, contact information, and any other required fields.
2. Provide your own details as the photographer, including your name, contact information, and any other required fields.
3. Specify the details of the photoshoot, including the location, date, and any other relevant information.
4. Select any special add-ons that you would like to include in the photoshoot.
5. Choose the payment terms that apply to the photoshoot.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the photoshoot.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, text, required: false):

* **Photoshoot Details** (`photoshoot_details`, text, required: false):

* **Special Add-ons** (`special_addons`, select_multiple, required: false):
