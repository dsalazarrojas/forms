# haley_barnhill_photography_session_contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a photography session with Haley Barnhill. Fill out this form to schedule a photography session that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of photography session you are interested in (e.g., Landscape, Portrait, etc.) on the "Photo Types" page.
2. Choose the date and time for your session on the "Date" and "Time" pages.
3. Enter the location where you would like to have your session on the "Location" page.
4. Provide your contact information (phone number and email) on the "Contact Number" and "Email" pages.
5. Review and agree to the contract terms on the "Contract Terms" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Session**: Choose a session type from the drop-down menu to begin booking your photography session.
* **Photo Types**: Select the type of photography session you are interested in, such as Landscape, Portrait, City, Night, or Studio.
* **Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule your photography session. The date should be in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: Choose the time you would like to schedule your photography session. Be aware that some time slots may not be available.
* **Location**: Enter the location where you would like to have your photography session.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number so the photographer or studio can contact you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the photographer or studio can contact you.
* **Contract Terms**: Review and agree to the contract terms before submitting your form. This includes important details about the session and any necessary agreements. 

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your selections before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer or studio directly.
- Review and understand the contract terms before agreeing to them.
