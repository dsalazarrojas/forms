# photography_session_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography session booking form is for clients to book a photography session with a photographer. It gathers the necessary information from the client, including contact details and session specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the contact information section with the required details.
2. Choose the number of people attending the session.
3. Select the date of the session.
4. Choose the time of the session.
5. Enter the location of the session.
6. Select the session type(s) desired.
7. Read and agree to the contact agreement terms.
8. Enter the deposit amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (contact_information, text, optional): This field is for entering your contact details such as name and email address.
* **Number of People** (number_of_people, number, optional): This field is for selecting the number of people attending the session.
* **Session Date** (session_date, date, optional): This field is for selecting the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (session_time, time, optional): This field is for selecting the time of the session.
* **Location** (location, text, optional): This field is for entering the location of the session.
* **Session Type** (session_type, multiple select, optional): This field is for selecting the type of session desired. Choose one or more from the options: Option A, Option B, and Option C.
* **Contact Agreement** (contact_agreement, note, optional): This is a note field for you to read and agree to the terms of contact.
* **Deposit** (deposit, number, optional): This field is for entering the deposit amount for the session.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful booking.
* Select the correct session type(s) for your needs.
* Read the contact agreement carefully before agreeing to it.
* Enter the correct deposit amount for the session.
