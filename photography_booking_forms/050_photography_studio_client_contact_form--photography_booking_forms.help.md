# photography_studio_client_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The photography studio client contact form is designed to collect information from clients interested in booking a session with the studio. The form helps the studio to understand the client's requirements and preferences to schedule and prepare for a successful session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your title.
2.  Select a contact person.
3.  Enter your email address.
4.  Enter your phone number.
5.  Enter a message to the studio.
6.  Choose your preferred session schedule.
7.  Choose your session preferences.
8.  Enter any photographer details.
9.  Enter any studio details.
10.  Enter any special requests.
11.  Enter a message to the photographer.
12.  Enter a message to the studio.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Inquiry** (`client_inquiry`, text, optional): This field is for the client to enter a brief description of their inquiry or reason for booking a session.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): In this field, please enter the name of the contact person responsible for the session.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further information or updates.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further information or updates.
* **Client Message** (`client_message`, text, optional): Use this field to enter any additional information or messages for the studio.
* **Schedule Session** (`schedule_session`, date, optional): Choose a date for the session. Please note that this date is not binding and may be subject to change based on the studio's availability.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select any preferences for your session, such as:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Photographer Details** (`photographer_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific details about the photographer you would like to work with.
* **Studio Details** (`studio_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific details about the studio you would like to work with.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Use this field to enter any special requests or requirements for your session.
* **Message to Photographer** (`message_to_photographer`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or messages for the photographer.
* **Message to Studio** (`message_to_studio`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or messages for the studio.
