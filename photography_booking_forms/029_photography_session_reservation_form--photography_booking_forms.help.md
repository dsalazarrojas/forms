# Photography Session Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Session Reservation Form is designed to collect essential information from clients about their photography session requirements and preferences. This form helps us understand their needs and schedule the session accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Client Info with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select the photography services you're interested in (e.g., "Yes" for yes, "No" for no).
3. Provide your preferred schedule for the photography session in the Schedule Booking field.
4. Enter your contact details, including your address and any other relevant information.
5. Describe your photo session requirements and preferences in the Photo Session Details field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Info**: Please enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about your session.
* **Photography Services**: Select "Yes" if you're interested in any of our photography services, or "No" if you're not interested.
* **Schedule Booking**: Enter your preferred schedule for the photography session, including the date and time you're available.
* **Contact Details**: Please provide your address and any other relevant contact information.
* **Photo Session Details**: Briefly describe your photo session requirements and preferences, such as location, style, or any specific requests you may have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to ensure a successful session.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the Photo Session Details field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your earliest convenience.
