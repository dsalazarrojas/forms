# Wedding Photography Quote Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Quote Template is a form used to gather client information and preferences for a wedding photography quote. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Information section with your details.
2. Select the services you are interested in.
3. Choose the price quote option that suits your needs.
4. Enter the date and time of service.
5. Provide the photographer's name, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (`client_info`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Services Offered (`services_offered`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select the services you are interested in, such as "Yes" for each service you want to book.
* **Price Quote (`price_quote`, `select_one`, required: false)**: Choose the price quote option that suits your needs, such as "Full" for a full-service quote.
* **Date of Service (`date_of_service`, `date`, required: false)**: Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Time of Service (`time_of_service`, `time`, required: false)**: Enter the time of the wedding.
* **Photographer's Name (`photographer_name`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the name of the photographer.
* **Photographer's Phone (`photographer_phone`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter the phone number of the photographer.
* **Photographer's Email (`photographer_email`, `email`, required: false)**: Enter the email of the photographer.
* **Additional Services (`services`, `select_multiple`, required: false)**: Select any additional services you want to book, such as "Yes" for each service you want to add.
* **Payment Terms (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false)**: Enter any special payment terms or instructions.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Check the options for each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the photographer for assistance.
