# Projection Mapping Setup Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Projection Mapping Setup Booking Form is used to collect information about a specific event's projection mapping requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Event Type" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the "Event Name" of the event.
3. Choose a date for the event in the "Event Date" field.
4. Select a time for the event in the "Event Time" field.
5. Enter the "Venue" where the event will take place.
6. Enter the "Client" who is responsible for the event.
7. List any technical requirements, such as video format, resolution, screen size, in the "Technical Requirements" field.
8. Describe your booking requests in the "Booking Requests" field.
9. Select the type of "Projection Mapping Setup" required for the event (Projection Mapping, Projection Mapping with 360, Projection Mapping with Video Mapping, or Projection Mapping without 360).
10. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
11. Enter your email in the "Email" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
13. Click the "Submit Form" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select the type of event you are planning (Event, Projection Mapping Event, or Installation).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Choose a date for the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Choose a time for the event.
* **Venue** (`venue`): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Client** (`client`): Enter the client's name.
* **Technical Requirements** (`tech_requirements`): List any technical requirements, such as video format, resolution, screen size.
* **Booking Requests** (`booking_requests`): Describe your booking requests.
* **Projection Mapping Setup** (`projection_mapping_setup`): Select the type of projection mapping required for the event (Projection Mapping, Projection Mapping with 360, Projection Mapping with Video Mapping, or Projection Mapping without 360).
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`): Click to submit the form.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the event details and technical requirements before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the support team.
