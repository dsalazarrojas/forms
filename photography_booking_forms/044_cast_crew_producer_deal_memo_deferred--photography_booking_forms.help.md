# Cast/Crew/Producer Deal Memo Deferred - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains the Cast/Crew/Producer Deal Memo Deferred form, used to record and manage agreements between cast, crew, and production staff on film or television projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Production Title** and **Production Type**.
2. Provide **Memo Date**, which should be in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
3. Fill out the **Party Information** section with the following:
	* **Name of Cast/Crew/Producer Member**.
	* **Role in Production** (choose from options: Actor, Director, Producer, Cinematographer, Sound Designer, Crew, Other).
	* **Social Security Number or Tax ID** (last 4 digits).
	* **Address**.
	* **Phone Number**.
	* **Email Address**.
4. In the **Compensation Terms** section:
	* Enter the **Compensation Amount**.
	* Choose the **Compensation Type** (from options: Flat Fee, Daily Rate, Weekly Rate, Percentage of Budget, Back-End Participation, Deferred, Other).
	* Enter the **Payment Schedule**.
5. Provide **Deferment Terms**:
	* **Deferred Amount**.
	* Choose the **Deferment Payment Due Date** in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
	* Describe the **Conditions for Deferment Payment**.
6. Enter the **Engagement Details**:
	* **Production Start Date** in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
	* **Expected End Date** in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
	* **Expected Number of Work Days**.
7. Confirm your **Agreement Acceptance** by selecting **Yes, I accept**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Production Title**: Enter the name of the film, project, or production.
* **Production Type**: Choose the type of production from options: Feature Film, Television, Web Series, Commercial, Music Video, Photography, Other.
* **Memo Date**: Enter the date of the memo in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
* **Party Information**: Enter the name, role, social security number or tax ID, address, phone number, and email of the cast/crew/producer member.
* **Party Name**: Enter the name of the cast/crew/producer member.
* **Role in Production**: Choose the role from options: Actor, Director, Producer, Cinematographer, Sound Designer, Crew, Other.
* **Social Security Number or Tax ID**: Enter the last 4 digits of the SSN or tax ID.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the cast/crew/producer member.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the cast/crew/producer member.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the cast/crew/producer member.
* **Compensation Terms**: Enter the total payment amount and choose the compensation type from options: Flat Fee, Daily Rate, Weekly Rate, Percentage of Budget, Back-End Participation, Deferred, Other.
* **Compensation Amount**: Enter the total payment amount.
* **Compensation Type**: Choose the type of compensation from options: Flat Fee, Daily Rate, Weekly Rate, Percentage of Budget, Back-End Participation, Deferred, Other.
* **Payment Schedule**: Enter when the payment will be made.
* **Deferment Terms**: Enter the amount to be deferred and choose the payment due date in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
* **Deferred Amount**: Enter the amount to be deferred.
* **Deferment Payment Due Date**: Choose the date when the deferred payment is due in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
* **Conditions for Deferment Payment**: Describe the conditions and triggers for deferment payment.
* **Engagement Details**: Enter the production start date and end date in the format **MM/DD/YYYY** and the expected number of work days.
* **Production Start Date**: Enter the start date of the production in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
* **Expected End Date**: Enter the expected end date of the production in the format **MM/DD/YYYY**.
* **Expected Number of Work Days**: Enter the expected number of work days.
* **Agreement Acknowledgment**: Confirm your acceptance by selecting **Yes, I accept**.
