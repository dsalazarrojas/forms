# Wedding Photography Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Photography Survey form is designed to collect essential information about a wedding. It helps in capturing the photographer's details, wedding day information, and any notes or comments. This form is crucial for ensuring accuracy and preventing duplication of information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide the title of the wedding photography survey to identify the event.
2. Select the category of the wedding (Wedding, Engagement, Event, or Other).
3. Enter a brief description of the wedding photography survey.
4. Provide the name of the photographer.
5. Enter the date of the wedding (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
6. Enter the start and end time of the wedding (in 12:00 AM format).
7. Enter the location of the wedding.
8. Enter the number of guests attending the wedding.
9. Provide the contact information for the wedding.
10. Enter the email of the photographer.
11. Add any additional notes or comments regarding the wedding.
12. Confirm if you have reviewed and confirmed your wedding details.
13. Confirm if you have reviewed and confirmed your photographer's details.
14. Provide any additional comments or notes about the wedding.
15. Select to submit or cancel the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Photography Survey (Text):** A short description of the wedding, up to 255 characters.
* **Category (Select One):** Choose the category of the wedding (Wedding, Engagement, Event, or Other).
* **Wedding Photography Survey Description (Text):** A brief description of the wedding, up to 255 characters.
* **Photographer Name (Text):** The name of the person or organization responsible for the wedding photography.
* **Wedding Date (Date):** Enter the date of the wedding in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Start Time (Time):** Enter the start time of the wedding in 12:00 AM format.
* **End Time (Time):** Enter the end time of the wedding in 12:00 AM format.
* **Wedding Location (Text):** The location where the wedding will take place.
* **Number of Guests (Number):** The estimated number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Contact Information (Text):** Any relevant contact information for the wedding.
* **Photographer Email (Email):** The email address of the photographer.
* **Note (Note):** Any additional notes or comments about the wedding.
* **Photographer Note (Note):** Any additional notes or comments from the photographer.
* **Confirm Your Wedding Details (Select Multiple):** Confirm that you have reviewed and confirmed your wedding details.
* **Confirm Your Wedding Photographer Details (Select Multiple):** Confirm that you have reviewed and confirmed your photographer's details.
* **Comments (Text):** Any additional comments or notes about the wedding.
* **Submit/Cancel (Select One):** Select to submit or cancel the form.
