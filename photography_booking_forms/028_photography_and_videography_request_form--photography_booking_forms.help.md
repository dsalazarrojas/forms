# Photography And Videography Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details about a photography and videography request from clients. It helps us understand their specific needs, preferences, and requirements for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details:
	* **Name**: Provide your name as it should be displayed for the photographer and videographer.
	* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your email address for us to contact you about your request.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to reach you for any further questions or updates.
	* **Company Name (if applicable)**: If you are representing a company, please enter your company name.
2. Choose your service type:
	* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you require - Photography, Videography, or Both.
3. Provide event details:
	* **Number of Guests**: Enter the estimated number of guests you expect for the event.
	* **Date Request**: Choose a date for the event.
	* **Time Request**: Choose a time for the event.
	* **Location**: Enter the exact location of the event.
	* **Specific Location Note (optional)**: If you have any additional notes or specific requirements for the location.
4. Equipment preferences:
	* **Video Equipment**: Check which video equipment you need - Drones, Cameras, Lenses, Lighting, or Sound.
	* **Photography Equipment**: Check which photography equipment you need - Camera, Lens, Tripod, Lighting, or Drone.
