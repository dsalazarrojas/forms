# Seasonal Portrait Special Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture leads for seasonal portrait photography packages. It's designed to collect contact information and details about a potential client's needs, so that our photography team can follow up and provide more information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If applicable, enter your location in the "Location" field.
6. Select a date for your photo shoot in the "Date" field.
7. Choose a time for your photo shoot in the "Time" field.
8. Choose the type of photo shoot you are interested in from the "Photo Type" field.
9. Select the style of photography you prefer from the "Photo Style" field.
10. Choose the specific photo session you are interested in from the "Photo Session" field.
11. Select a package that suits your needs from the "Package" field.
12. Enter the price you are willing to pay for the package in the "Price" field.
13. Provide any additional package details in the "Package Details" field.
14. Mention if there is any promotion or discount you are aware of in the "Promotion" field.
15. Enter the name of the photographer you are interested in working with in the "Photographer" field.
16. Provide a follow-up method in case we need to contact you again in the "Follow-up Method" field.
17. Mention the frequency of follow-up in "Follow-up Frequency" field.
18. Enter any follow-up message you'd like to be communicated with you in "Follow-up Message" field.
19. Select the status of your lead in the "Status" field.
20. Mention the source of your lead in the "Lead Source" field.
21. If applicable, enter the URL of the package you are interested in in the "Package URL" field.
22. Provide a submission URL in the "Submission URL" field (not required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first-name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last-name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If you're willing to share, enter your location.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose a date for your photo shoot.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose a time for your photo shoot.
* **Photo Type** (`photo-type`, text, required): Select the type of photo shoot you are interested in (e.g. family, couple, etc.).
* **Photo Style** (`photo-style`, text, required): Select the style of photography you prefer (e.g. natural light, studio, etc.).
* **Photo Session** (`photo-session`, text, required): Choose the specific photo session you are interested in.
* **Package** (`package`, text, required): Select a package that suits your needs.
* **Price** (`price`, number, required): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the package.
* **Package Details** (`package-details`, text, required): Provide any additional details about the package you are interested in.
* **Promotion** (`promotion`, text, required): Mention if there is any promotion or discount you are aware of.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, required): Enter the name of the photographer you are interested in working with.
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow-up-method`, text, optional): Provide a follow-up method in case we need to contact you again.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow-up-frequency`, text, optional): Mention the frequency of follow-up.
* **Follow-up Message** (`follow-up-message`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up message you'd like to be communicated with you.
* **Follow-up Status** (`follow-up-status`, text, optional): Select the status of your follow-up.
* **Status** (`status`, text, required): Select the status of your lead.
* **Lead Source** (`lead-source`, text, required): Mention the source of your lead.
* **Package URL** (`package-url`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the URL of the package you are interested in.
* **Submission URL** (`submission-url`, text, optional): Provide a submission URL (not required).
