# Pet Photography Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and preferences from clients for pet photography sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and contact information in the "contact_form" field.
2.  Provide a brief description of your pet in the "pet_form" field.
3.  Specify your photography session preferences in the "photo_session_preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Form** (`contact_form`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact information in this field.
*   **Pet Form** (`pet_form`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of your pet, including its age, breed, and any relevant characteristics that will help the photographer understand the session.
*   **Photo Session Preferences** (`photo_session_preferences`, `text`, required): Specify your preferred photography session date and time, location, and any specific preferences or requests you have for the session.

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in the "contact_form" field.
*   Be as descriptive as possible in the "pet_form" field to help the photographer capture the best images.
*   Clearly communicate your photography session preferences in the "photo_session_preferences" field to ensure a smooth and successful session.
