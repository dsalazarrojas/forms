# portrait_questionnaire_for_women - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire for women who are booking a photography session. It is designed to collect accurate and complete information to provide the best possible experience and results for our clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your height in centimeters in the "Height (in centimeters)" field.
5. Enter your weight in kilograms in the "Weight (kg)" field.
6. Describe your hair length in the "Hair Length" field.
7. Specify your shoe size in the "Shoe Size" field.
8. Choose your dress size from the options provided in the "Dress Size" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for further details or updates.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can receive updates and photos from our photographer.
* **Height (in centimeters)** (`height`, number, required): Enter your height in centimeters to help our photographer prepare for the session.
* **Weight (kg)** (`weight`, number, required): Enter your weight in kilograms to help our photographer prepare for the session.
* **Hair Length** (`hair_length`, text, required): Describe your hair length, e.g., "long", "short", "chin-length", etc.
* **Shoe Size** (`shoe_size`, text, required): Specify your shoe size to ensure comfortable and proper footwear for the session.
* **Dress Size** (`dress_size`, select_one, required): Choose your dress size from the provided options to help our photographer prepare for the session.
