# photo_session_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect booking information for a photo session. It is intended for users to input their details for a photo shoot or session with a photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the information on each page, and proceed to the next page when ready.
2. Start on [photo_session_booking_form_page_1](page_1) with the booking details.
3. Enter the necessary information on this page and click "Continue" to proceed to the next page.
4. On [photo_session_booking_form_page_2](page_2), enter the photographer's details.
5. On [photo_session_booking_form_page_3](page_3), enter the photo session details.
6. On [photo_session_booking_form_page_4](page_4), enter a summary of the booking.
7. On [photo_session_booking_form_page_5](page_5), enter any additional information as needed.
8. On [photo_session_booking_form_page_6](page_6), enter any further details.
9. On [photo_session_booking_form_page_7](page_7), enter any final information.
10. On [photo_session_booking_form_page_8](page_8), review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **photo_session_booking_form_page_1: booking_details**: Enter your booking details, such as date, time, or session type.
*   **photo_session_booking_form_page_2: photographer_details**: Enter the photographer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
*   **photo_session_booking_form_page_3: photo_session_details**: Describe the photo session details, such as location, theme, or number of photos to be taken.
*   **photo_session_booking_form_page_4: booking_summary**: Summarize the booking details in a few sentences.
*   **photo_session_booking_form_page_5: photo_session_booking_form_page_5**: Enter any additional information as needed.
*   **photo_session_booking_form_page_6: photo_session_booking_form_page_6**: Enter further details about the photo session.
*   **photo_session_booking_form_page_7: photo_session_booking_form_page_7**: Enter any final information or comments.
*   **photo_session_booking_form_page_8: photo_session_booking_form_page_8**: Review and submit the form after entering all the necessary information.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate information is collected.
- Review each page carefully before proceeding to ensure all necessary information is entered.
- If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
