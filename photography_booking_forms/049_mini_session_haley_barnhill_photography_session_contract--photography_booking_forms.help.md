# Mini Session Haley Barnhill Photography Session Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a mini or full photography session with Haley Barnhill. The information provided in this form will help Haley Barnhill Photography to understand your needs and preferences for the session, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for both you and your photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the required information and options as accurately as possible.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are completed and accurate.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact Haley Barnhill Photography directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to schedule the photography session. This will help Haley Barnhill Photography to plan and prepare for your session.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and name of any co-clients (such as a partner or child's name) as you would like them to appear on your photos.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, optional): Provide a valid phone number where you can be reached for any further questions or updates.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for any updates or reminders about your session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose between Mini and Full session options to determine the type of session you'd like to book. A Mini session is typically 20 minutes, while a Full session is typically longer.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, `number`, optional): If you've selected a Full session, you can specify the desired length. Please note that this will be confirmed with Haley Barnhill Photography directly.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional): If you've selected a Full session, you can specify your preferred start time. Please note that this will be confirmed with Haley Barnhill Photography directly.
