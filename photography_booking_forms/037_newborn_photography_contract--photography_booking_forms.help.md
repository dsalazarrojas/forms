# Newborn Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the terms and conditions of a photography session, including the session date, customer information, and contract terms. It is intended to be completed by the customer before or after the photography session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your session date on the "Session Date" field.
2. Enter your name on the "Customer Name" field.
3. Enter your email address on the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number on the "Customer Phone" field.
5. Select the correct options for "We will not use the images for any purpose other than as described on this contract." and "We will not use the images on any other social media or platform."
6. Select the correct option for "We will not use the images for any other purpose without the customer's consent."
7. Sign the "Signature" field with your signature.
8. Sign the "Customer Signature" field with your name.
9. Enter the date you sign the contract on the "Signature Date" field.
10. Sign the "Photographer Signature" field with the photographer's name.
11. Enter the date the photographer signs the contract on the "Photographer Signature Date" field.
12. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
13. Select the correct option for "I understand that my contract and signature must be returned to the photographer within 24 hours of the session."
14. Select the correct option for "I understand that I am free to terminate this contract at any time without cost."
15. Select the correct option for "I understand that the photographer has the right to refuse the contract."
16. Select the correct option for "I understand that the photographer is not responsible for any damage or loss of property."
17. Select the correct option for "I understand that I must provide valid contact information for my emergency contact."

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date (id: 1)**: Enter the date of the photography session.
* **Customer Name (id: 2)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contract Start (id: 5)**: Enter the start time of the photography session.
* **Consent 1 (id: 6)**: Select "I agree" if you agree to not use images for any purpose other than as described on this contract.
* **Consent 2 (id: 7)**: Select "I agree" if you agree to not use images on any other social media or platform.
* **Consent 3 (id: 8)**: Select "I agree" if you agree to not use images for any other purpose without your consent.
* **Signature (id: 9)**: Sign your contract with your signature.
* **Customer Signature (id: 10)**: Sign your name as the customer.
* **Signature Date (id: 11)**: Enter the date you sign the contract.
* **Photographer Signature (id: 12)**: Sign the contract with your name as the photographer.
* **Photographer Signature Date (id: 13)**: Enter the date the photographer signs the contract.
* **Notes (id: 14)**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Customer Signature Image (id: 15)**: Select "I agree" if you agree to provide a signature image.
* **Photographer Signature Image (id: 16)**: Select "I agree" if you agree to provide a signature image.
* **Session Notes (id: 17)**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the session.
* **Session Start Time (id: 18)**: Enter the start time of the photography session.
* **Session End Time (id: 19)**: Enter the end time of the photography session.
* **Session Date (id: 20)**: Enter the date of the photography session.
* **Consent 4 (id: 21)**: Select "I agree" if you understand that your contract and signature must be returned to the photographer within 24 hours of the session.
* **Consent 5 (id: 22)**: Select "I agree" if you understand that you are free to terminate this contract at any time without cost.
* **Consent 6 (id: 23)**: Select "I agree" if you understand that the photographer has the right to refuse the contract.
* **Consent 7 (id: 24)**: Select "I agree" if you understand that the photographer is not responsible for any damage or loss of property.
* **Consent 8 (id: 25)**: Select "I agree" if you understand that you must provide valid contact information for your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you understand the terms and conditions outlined in this contract before signing.
* Review each field carefully and select the correct options to ensure you are in agreement with the photographer.
* Ensure that you sign both your signature and the photographer's signature to finalize the contract.
* Return the contract to the photographer within 24 hours of the session.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the photographer to clarify any doubts.
