## online_photo_shoot_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "online_photo_shoot_booking_form" is used for customers to book an online photo shoot session with our team. This form captures essential customer information and session details to ensure a smooth and efficient booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Details**.
2. Enter your **Contact Number**.
3. Select your preferred **Session Details** (choose one or more from the options).
4. Optionally, provide a brief description of the purpose of your session in the **Session Purpose** field.
5. If you have any questions or comments, use the **Questions Comments** field to share them with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details**: This field is required and is used to collect your basic contact information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Number**: This field is required and should include your phone number or other contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Session Details**: Choose one or more sessions from the options available to determine the type of photo shoot you'd like to book. This is a required field and will help us prepare for your session.
* **Session Purpose**: This is an optional field where you can briefly describe the reason or topic you'd like to focus on during your session.
* **Questions Comments**: This is an optional field where you can share any questions or comments you may have with our team, and we'll be happy to assist you.
