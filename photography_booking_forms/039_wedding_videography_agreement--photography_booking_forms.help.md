# Wedding Videography Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Videography Agreement form collects essential information about the wedding videography project, including details about the photographer, contact information, wedding details, services required, equipment, rates, payment, and client contact.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary details and information before filling out this form.
2.  Review each field carefully to understand its purpose and required or optional nature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Videographer Details (<code>wedding_videographer_details</code>):** This field is an optional text field where you can provide information about the wedding videographer, including their name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information (<code>contact_information</code>):** This field is an optional text field where you can provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number. This will be helpful for communication and payment purposes.
* **Wedding Details (<code>wedding_details</code>):** This field is an optional text field where you can provide information about the wedding, including the wedding date, location, and other relevant details.
* **Wedding Services (<code>wedding_services</code>):** This field is a selection of services that you can choose from, including:
	+ Wedding Videography
	+ Videography Coverage
	+ Videography Package
* **Equipment List (<code>equipment_list</code>):** This field is a selection of equipment options that you can choose from, including:
	+ Drone Coverage
	+ Drone Coverage + Sound
	+ Videography Package
*   <code>rates</code> and <code>payment</code> are optional number fields that should reflect the agreed-upon rates and payment terms for the services.
* **Client Name (<code>client_name</code>):** This field is an optional text field where you can provide your name or the name of the person responsible for the agreement.
* **Signature (<code>signature</code>):** This field is not applicable for digital forms and is for internal use only.
* **Contact Person (<code>contact_person</code>):** This field is an optional email field where you can provide a contact email for further communication.

## Tips
