# Photo Shoot Session Questionnaire And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients or subjects about their photo shoot session preferences and details. It ensures that the photographer has all the necessary information before proceeding with the photo shoot.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the desired date and time for your photo shoot session.
2. Choose the location for your session (Studio, Outdoor, or On Location).
3. Select the length of your photo shoot session (30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours).
4. Choose the subject of your photo shoot (Family, Couples, Individual, or Child).
5. Select the style of your photo shoot (Formal, Casual, Editorial, or Documentary).
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
7. Choose the release form you would like to sign (Model Release or Client Release).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (`photo_shoot_session_questionnaire_and_release_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your photo shoot session.
* **Session Date and Time** (`photo_shoot_date_and_time`, select_one, required/optional): Select a date and time that is available for your photoshoot session. The options available are 600, 660, and 720.
* **Session Location** (`photo_shoot_location`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the location for your session. You can select one or multiple options (Studio, Outdoor, or On Location).
* **Session Length** (`photo_shoot_length`, select_one, required/optional): Select the length of your photoshoot session. The options available are 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours.
* **Session Subject** (`photo_shoot_subject`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the subject of your photo shoot. You can select one or multiple options (Family, Couples, Individual, or Child).
* **Session Style** (`photo_shoot_style`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the style of your photo shoot. You can choose one or multiple options (Formal, Casual, Editorial, or Documentary).
* **Session Notes** (`photo_shoot_notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
* **Release Form** (`photo_shoot_release`, select_one, required/optional): Select the release form you would like to sign. You can choose either Model Release or Client Release.
* **Additional Comments** (`photo_shoot_additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth and efficient photo shoot session.
* Be as specific as possible when selecting the date and time, location, and style to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the additional comments field.
* Review the release form carefully before signing it, ensuring you understand the terms and conditions.
