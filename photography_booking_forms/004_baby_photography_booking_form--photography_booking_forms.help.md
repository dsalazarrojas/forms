# Baby Photography Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients to book a baby photography session. It ensures that all the necessary details are gathered to provide a great experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with your details, ensuring to provide accurate information.
2. Select the type of photography session that suits your needs.
3. Enter your baby's name and age at the time of the session.
4. Choose your preferred session date and time.
5. Select a location for the session.
6. Choose the package that best fits your needs.
7. If you have any special requests or props, let us know.
8. Tell us how you found our photography services.
9. Finally, confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Baby Photography Session Booking --** (`booking_header`, note, required: false): This is the introduction section of the form. It serves as a header and is not required to be filled out.
* **Parent or Client Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required: true): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required: true): Enter your email address, as we will use it to send booking confirmations and gallery delivery instructions.
* **Mobile Number** (`client_phone`, text, required: true): Please enter your mobile phone number for contact and confirmation purposes.
* **Type of Photography Session** (`session_type`, select_one, required: true): Choose from the options: 
	+ Maternity
	+ Newborn (Hospital/Fresh 48)
	+ Newborn (Studio)
	+ Milestone (6 months)
	+ Cake Smash (1 year)
	+ Family Portrait
* **Baby's Name and Age (at time of session)** (`baby_name_age`, text, required: true): Enter your baby's name and age at the time of the session.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_session_date`, date, required: true): Choose a date for your session, subject to availability.
* **Alternative Date Preference** (`alternative_date`, date, required: false): Enter an alternative date if the preferred date is not available.
* **Preferred Time of Day** (`preferred_time`, time, required: false): Choose a time of day for your session, ideally morning for baby-friendly shoots.
* **Desired Location** (`session_location`, select_one, required: true): Select from the options:
	+ My Studio
	+ At Your Home
	+ Outdoor Location
* **Choose Your Package** (`photography_package`, select_one, required: true): Choose from the options:
	+ Basic - 10 Digital Images
	+ Essential - 25 Digital Images
	+ Premium - All Digitals + Album
	+ Mini Session
* **Special Requests or Props to Include** (`special_requests`, text, required: false): If you have any special requests, such as themes or heirlooms, please enter them here.
* **How did you find my photography?** (`referral_source`, text, required: false): Share how you discovered our photography services.
* **I agree to the photography service terms and conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required: true): Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions, including the requirement of a deposit to secure your date.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* Be specific with your preferences for the session, including date and time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* A deposit is required to secure your date.
* We will be in touch with you regarding your session details and confirmation.
* Please allow adequate time for your session, and be prepared to follow our instructions for optimal results.
