# f_b_wedding_photography_booking_payments - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking photography services for a wedding. It is used to collect client information, payment details, and other relevant data to facilitate the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your wedding client information on Page 1.
2.  Provide payment details on Page 2.
3.  Add contact information on Page 3.
4.  If applicable, list any additional services you would like to book on Page 4.
5.  Confirm your payment on Page 5.
6.  Confirm your payment method on Page 6.
7.  Make any special requests on Page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Wedding Client Information** (`wedding_client_information`, text, required): Enter your name, contact information, and other relevant details about your wedding.
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter your payment information, including the amount and method of payment.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
- **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, text, required): If you have any additional services you would like to book, enter them here.
- **Payment Confirmation** (`payment_confirmation`, text, required): Confirm your payment details to ensure accuracy.
- **Payment Method Confirmation** (`payment_method_confirmation`, text, required): Confirm your payment method to ensure accuracy.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required): If you have any special requests, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your payment details to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the photographer for assistance.
