# Photography Session Booking Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a photography session for a client. It collects client information, contract details, and session specifics to ensure a smooth and secure booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the contract status in the "Contract Status" field.
5. Optionally, enter notes in the "Notes" field.
6. Sign the "Client Signature" field if you agree to the contract terms.
7. Select the photographer's name in the "Photographer" field.
8. Enter the photo delivery date in the "Photo Delivery Date" field.
9. Choose the photo session duration in the "Photo Session Duration" field.
10. Enter the photo session details in the "Photo Session Details" field.
11. Specify the photo session location in the "Photo Session Location" field.
12. Select the photo session time in the "Photo Session Time" field.
13. Enter the photo session date in the "Photo Session Date" field.
14. Choose the photo session type in the "Photo Session Type" field.
15. Enter the photo session price in the "Photo Session Price" field.
16. Enter the photo session discount in the "Photo Session Discount" field.
17. Enter the photo session terms in the "Photo Session Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Contract Date**: Enter the contract date.
* **Client Type**: Choose whether this is a new or existing client.
* **Photographer**: Choose the photographer assigned to this session.
* **Session Details**: Enter the details of the photo session.
* **Contract Status**: Select the status of the contract.
* **Notes**: Optionally, enter any additional notes about the session.
* **Client Signature**: Sign this field to confirm you have read and agree to the contract terms.
* **Photographer Signature**: Sign this field to confirm the photographer has read and agrees to the contract terms.
* **Client Agreement**: Enter the client's agreement to the contract terms.
* **Contract Terms**: Enter the contract terms.
* **Contract Conditions**: Enter the contract conditions.
* **Photo Delivery Date**: Enter the date you want the photos delivered.
* **Photo Session Duration**: Choose the duration of the photo session.
* **Photo Session Details**: Enter the details of the photo session.
* **Photo Session Location**: Specify the location of the photo session.
* **Photo Session Time**: Choose the time of the photo session.
* **Photo Session Date**: Enter the date of the photo session.
* **Photo Session Type**: Choose the type of photo session.
* **Photo Session Price**: Enter the price of the photo session.
* **Photo Session Discount**: Enter any applicable discount.
* **Photo Session Terms**: Enter any terms related to the photo session.
* **Notes**: Optionally, enter any additional notes about the session.
* **Contract Terms**: Enter the contract terms.
* **Contract Conditions**: Enter the contract conditions.
* **Client Agreement**: Enter the client's agreement to the contract terms.
