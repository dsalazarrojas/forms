# property_photography_service_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from photographers who wish to offer their services to a business or organization for property photography. It captures essential details about the photographer, the property, and the contact information of the person submitting the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, begin by filling out the **Photographers Details** field.
2.  Provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a photographer.
3.  Move on to the **Property Details** field.
4.  Enter the information about the property where the photography services will be provided.
5.  Lastly, fill out the **Contact Details** field with your contact information.
6.  If you have any specific **Appointment Preferences**, you can select a date for the photography service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Photographers Details** (`Photographer's name`, `type: text`, required/optional: **required**): Enter your name and any other details about yourself as a photographer.
*   **Property Details** (`Property name`, `type: text`, required/optional: **required**): Enter the name of the property where the photography services will be provided.
*   **Contact Details** (`Your name`, `type: text`, required/optional: **required**): Enter your name and contact information for communication.
*   **Appointment Preferences** (`type: date`, required/optional: **optional**): Select a specific date for the photography service if you have any preferences.

## Tips

*   Ensure that the details you provide are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Be specific when entering the property name to ensure it's correctly associated with the photography service.
*   If you have any specific date preferences for the photography service, use the **Appointment Preferences** field to select a suitable date.
