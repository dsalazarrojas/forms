# Wedding Videography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for couples to book a videographer for their wedding day. It collects information from the couple regarding their event details and service preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information section by providing your name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter the contact information of the person or business handling the booking.
3. Provide details about the wedding, including date and location.
4. Select the video package that suits your needs.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in the clauses.
6. Sign the form electronically to confirm your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details that you would like to share with the videographer.
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person or business handling the booking. This could be the videographer or a representative from your agency.
- **Wedding Information** (`wedding_info`, text, required: false): Provide details about the wedding date, location, and other relevant details that will help the videographer understand the event.
- **Video Package** (`video_package`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose from the available video packages offered by the videographer. The Basic Package might be a shorter coverage period, while the Premium Package includes additional features such as longer coverage, extra footage, and more. The Deluxe Package may include the highest level of coverage, additional features, and services. 
  - Basic Package: A shorter coverage period, typically 2-4 hours.
  - Premium Package: A longer coverage period, typically 6-8 hours.
  - Deluxe Package: The longest coverage period, typically 10 hours.
- **Clause 1, 2, 3** (`clause_1`, `clause_2`, `clause_3`, note, required: false): These fields are meant to serve as notes for the videographer. They are meant to be used as a reference for the videographer to understand the specifics of your wedding day.
- **E-Signature** (`e_signature`, text, required: false): Enter your electronic signature to confirm your booking.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully review the video package options to ensure you select the best fit for your needs.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any specific requests or requirements for the videographer, include them in the wedding information section or in one of the clauses.
