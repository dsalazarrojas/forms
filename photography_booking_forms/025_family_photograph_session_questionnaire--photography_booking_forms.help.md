# Family Photograph Session Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Photograph Session Questionnaire is a document that guides you through the essential details that you need to share with us before your family photography session. This form will help us understand your preferences, session requirements, and expectations, ensuring that we provide a tailored and enjoyable experience for you and your loved ones.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, providing all the necessary information requested in each section. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us. We are here to guide you through the process and answer any queries you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required): This section is where you can provide us with your preferences regarding your family photography session. Be as specific as possible when describing what you're looking for, and we'll do our best to make it happen.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Please provide us with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Photographer** (`photographer_details`, text, required): Share with us the name and contact information of your selected photographer.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Let us know the location where you'd like the session to take place.
* **Contact** (`contact_preferences`, text, required): Provide us with your preferred method of contact (phone, email, or in-person) and any other contact information you're comfortable sharing.
* **Session Requirements** (`session_requirements`, text, required): Describe your expectations and requirements for the session, including any specific poses, angles, or themes you'd like to focus on.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, text, required): Review our cancellation policy and indicate your understanding of the terms and conditions.
* **Image Rights** (`image_rights`, text, required): Please acknowledge that you understand the image rights agreement and that you're willing to sign the contract.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Provide us with payment information, including the amount and method of payment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Leave any additional comments, questions, or concerns you may have for us to consider.
* **Session Terms** (`session_terms`, text, optional): If you have any additional terms or conditions you'd like to discuss or add to the session, please let us know.
* **Image Rights Agreement** (`image_rights_agreement`, text, optional): If you have any specific image rights requirements or agreements, please provide them to us.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): If you have any special payment terms or conditions, please share them with us.
* **Client Consent** (`client_consent`, text, optional): If you have any special consent requirements or agreements, please provide them to us.
* **Client Declaration** (`client_declaration`, text, optional): If you have any declarations or statements you'd like to make regarding the session, please share them with us.
* **Client Confirmation** (`client_confirmation`, text, optional): If you have any confirmation requirements or agreements, please provide them to us.
* **Photographer Confirmation** (`photographer_confirmation`, text, optional): If you have any confirmation requirements or agreements from your selected photographer, please share them with us.
* **Image Delivery** (`image_delivery`, text, optional): If you have any specific image delivery requirements or agreements, please provide them to us.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes regarding the session, please share them with us.
* **Image Storage** (`image_storage`, text, optional): If you have any requirements or agreements regarding image storage, please provide them to us.
* **Image Release** (`image_release`, text, optional): If you have any image release requirements or agreements, please share them with us.
* **Session Fees** (`session_fees`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns regarding session fees, please reach out to us.
* **Image Fees** (`image_fees`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns regarding image fees, please reach out to us.
* **Payment Terms Agreement** (`payment_terms_agreement`, text, optional): If you have any payment terms or agreements, please provide them to us.

Note: The following sections are not mandatory but are optional and can be filled out if you have any additional comments or concerns. Please be aware that not providing the optional information will not affect the session, but it's recommended to provide as much detail as possible to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.
