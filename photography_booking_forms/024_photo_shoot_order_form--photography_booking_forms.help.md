# Photo Shoot Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Shoot Order Form is a crucial tool for capturing and organizing essential details related to photography shoots. It helps in ensuring that all necessary information is recorded accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Photo Shoot Date** (field 1) in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
2.  Select the **Start Time** (field 2) for the photo shoot in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
3.  Select the **End Time** (field 3) for the photo shoot in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4.  Enter the **Location** (field 4) of the photo shoot.
5.  Choose the type of **Photo Shoot** (field 5) from the available options: Landscape, Portrait, Wedding, or Event.
6.  Provide any additional **Photo Shoot Details** (field 6) in the text field.
7.  Enter the **Client Name** (field 7) as it appears on the contract or agreement.
8.  Enter the **Contact Email** (field 8) of the client or contact person.
9.  Select the **Package Option** (field 9) from the available choices: Package1, Package2, Package3, or Package4.
10. Provide any additional **Package Details** (field 10) in the text field.
11. Select the **Payment Method** (field 11) from the available options: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal.
12. Enter the **Payment Amount** (field 12) as per the selected package.
13. Select the **Payment Status** (field 13) as Paid or Unpaid.
14. Provide any additional **Payment Method Details** (field 14) in the text field.
15. Enter the **Order Number** (field 15) if available.
16. Provide the **Client Signature** (field 16) if required.
17. Provide the **Photographer Signature** (field 17) if required.
18. Provide any **Client Notes** (field 18) in the text field.
19. Provide any **Photographer Notes** (field 19) in the text field.
20. Enter the **Date of Submission** (field 20) in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
21. Select the **Time of Submission** (field 21) in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
22. Select the **Photo Shoot Status** (field 22) from the available options: Completed, Pending, or Cancelled.
23. Select the **Submission Status** (field 23) from the available options: Submitted, Not Submitted, or Pending.
24. Provide any additional **Submission Details** (field 24) in the text field.
25. Enter the **Submission Date** (field 25) in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Photo Shoot Date** (field 1): Enter the date for the scheduled photo shoot.
*   **Start Time** (field 2): Select the start time for the photo shoot in 24-hour format.
*   **End Time** (field 3): Select the end time for the photo shoot in 24-hour format.
*   **Location** (field 4): Enter the location of the photo shoot.
*   **Photo Shoot Type** (field 5): Choose the type of photo shoot from the available options.
*   **Photo Shoot Details** (field 6): Provide any additional details about the photo shoot.
*   **Client Name** (field 7): Enter the name of the client or contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (field 8): Enter the email address of the client or contact person.
*   **Package Option** (field 9): Select the package option for the photo shoot.
*   **Package Details** (field 10): Provide any additional details about the selected package.
*   **Payment Method** (field 11): Choose the payment method from the available options.
*   **Payment Amount** (field 12): Enter the amount for the selected payment method.
*   **Payment Status** (field 13): Select the payment status as Paid or Unpaid.
*   **Payment Method Details** (field 14): Provide any additional details about the payment method.
*   **Order Number** (field 15): Enter the order number if available.
*   **Client Signature** (field 16): Provide the client's signature if required.
*   **Photographer Signature** (field 17): Provide the photographer's signature if required.
*   **Client Notes** (field 18): Provide any additional notes from the client.
*   **Photographer Notes** (field 19): Provide any additional notes from the photographer.
*   **Date of Submission** (field 20): Enter the date of submission in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Time of Submission** (field 21): Select the time of submission in 24-hour format.
*   **Photo Shoot Status** (field 22): Select the status of the photo shoot from the available options.
*   **Submission Status** (field 23): Select the submission status from the available options.
*   **Submission Details** (field 24): Provide any additional details about submission.
*   **Submission Date** (field 25): Enter the date of submission in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Tips

*   Ensure that the photo shoot date is accurate and in the correct format.
*   Double-check the start and end times for the photo shoot.
*   Enter the location accurately to avoid any confusion.
*   Choose the correct package option and enter the corresponding payment amount.
*   Verify the payment status before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that the client signature and photographer signature are provided if required.
*   Enter the order number if available.
*   Provide clear and concise notes for the client and photographer.
*   Enter the date and time of submission accurately.
*   Select the correct status for the photo shoot and submission.
