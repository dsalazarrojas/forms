# Creative Portfolio Review Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Creative Portfolio Review Booking Form is designed for clients or users to provide information about their creative portfolio. This form is used to schedule a review session, share the portfolio link, and collect contact information for review.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, providing all required and optional information.
2. Ensure that all fields with a label are filled out according to their type.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Schedule A Session** (`id: 1`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date and time for the review session.
* **Portfolio Link** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Share the link to your portfolio. Please ensure it's publicly accessible.
* **Client Name** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your name or the name of the client.
* **Review Date** (`id: 4`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date of the review session.
* **Review Time** (`id: 5`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time of the review session.
* **Notes** (`id: 6`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any relevant notes about the review session.
* **Email** (`id: 7`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address or the email of the client.
* **Phone** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the client.
* **Client Portfolio** (`id: 9`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of portfolio you'd like to share.
* **Client Portfolio URL** (`id: 10`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the URL of your portfolio.
* **Reviewer** (`id: 11`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select the reviewers for the review session.
* **Review Type** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the type of review you'd like to have.
* **Review Status** (`id: 13`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the status of the review.
* **Reviewer Email** (`id: 14`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`id: 15`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the reviewer.
* **Review Notes** (`id: 16`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any notes about the review session.
* **Schedule Time** (`id: 17`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time for the review session.
* **Schedule Date** (`id: 18`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date of the review session.
* **Reviewer Notes** (`id: 19`, `type: note`, required: false): Add any notes about the review session.
* **Form Submission** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the form submission.
