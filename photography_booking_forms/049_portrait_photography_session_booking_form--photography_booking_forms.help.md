# Portrait Photography Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the booking of a portrait photography session. It allows clients to provide their information, session details, and preferences for a meeting with a photographer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely to ensure that the photographer has all necessary information for the session.
2. Ensure that the correct format for date and time is mm/dd/yyyy and 12:00pm or 1:00pm for the time respectively.
3. Choose "Yes" for the Photographer field if you are a photographer or will be accompanied by one.
4. Enter the desired session length in minutes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: A required field for the client's name.
* **Email**: A field to input the client's email address.
* **Phone**: A field to input the client's phone number.
* **Date**: A field to input the desired date for the session in mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Time**: A field to input the desired time for the session in 12:00pm or 1:00pm format.
* **Location**: A field to input the location where the session will take place.
* **Photographer**: A field to choose whether you are a photographer or accompanied by one. Select "Yes" if you meet this condition.
* **Session Length**: A field to input the desired length of the session in minutes.
* **Comments**: A field to provide any additional comments or requests for the session.
* **Booking Form**: A required field to confirm that the form is being submitted correctly.
