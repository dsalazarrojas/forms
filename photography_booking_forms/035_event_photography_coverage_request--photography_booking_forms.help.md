# Event Photography Coverage Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request and confirm event photography coverage. You will need to fill out the form with your event details so that we can provide accurate and sufficient coverage for your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the type of event you are requesting coverage for (e.g., Wedding, Party, Concert, Conference, or Other).
2.  Enter the start date and end date of your event.
3.  Provide the location of your event.
4.  Specify the type of coverage you require (e.g., photographer, videographer, or both).
5.  Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
6.  Describe your schedule and any specific requirements for the event.
7.  Add any additional notes about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type** (Event Type, Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose the type of event you are requesting coverage for. This will help us determine the best approach for your photography and videography needs.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the start date of your event in a YYYY-MM-DD format. This will help us schedule your coverage correctly.
* **End Date** (End Date, Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the end date of your event in a YYYY-MM-DD format. This will help us schedule your coverage for the entire event.
* **Location** (Location, Text, Optional):
	+ Provide the address or venue name where your event will take place. This will help us ensure we can reach the location on time.
* **Coverage** (Coverage, Text, Optional):
	+ Specify the type of coverage you require. For example, you can request photography, videography, or both.
* **Contact** (Contact, Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your name, phone number, or email address so we can get in touch with you in case of any questions or updates.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Optional):
	+ Enter your email address so we can send you event updates, reminders, or confirmation emails.
* **Schedule** (Schedule, Text, Optional):
	+ Describe your event schedule, including any specific requirements or requests you have for our coverage.
* **Notes** (Notes, Text, Optional):
	+ Add any additional notes or details about your event that might be helpful for our team to know.
