# photo_model_release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "photo_model_release" form is used to collect information from models, photographers, and agencies involved in a photo shoot. This form helps establish a release agreement between the parties, outlining their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Choose the correct options for each field, such as "Yes" or "No".
3. Ensure you have filled in all required fields, which are marked as such in the form.

* **Model Name (`prophets`):** Enter the model's name.
* **Date of Capture (`date_of_capture`):** Enter the date the photos were taken.
* **Location of Capture (`location_of_capture`):** Enter the location where the photos were taken.
* **Photographer Credit (`photographer_credit`):** Choose "Yes" if the photographer is credited for the shoot, or "No" if not.
* **Agency Name (`agency_name`):** Choose "Yes" if the agency is involved in the shoot, or "No" if not.
* **Contact Person (`contact_person`):** Choose "Yes" if there is a contact person for the agency, or "No" if not.
* **Agency Address (`agency_address`):** Enter the agency's address, if applicable.

* **Email Address (`email_address`):** Enter the agency's email address, if applicable.
* **Signature Date (`signature_date`):** Enter the date the release agreement was signed.
* **Model Signature (`model_signature`):** Sign and date the model's signature.
* **Photographer Signature (`photographer_signature`):** Sign and date the photographer's signature.
* **Release (`release`):** Choose "Yes" if the release agreement is accepted, or "No" if not.
* **Agreement Signed (`agreement_signed`):** Choose "Yes" if the agreement is signed, or "No" if not.
* **Client (`client`):** Choose "Yes" if the client is involved in the shoot, or "No" if not.
