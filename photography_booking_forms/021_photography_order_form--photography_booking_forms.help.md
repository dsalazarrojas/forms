# Photography Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Photography Order Form" is designed to collect information from clients for ordering photography sessions. This form is used by photographers to receive and process requests from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the relevant fields.
2. Enter your Email and Contact Number to allow us to contact you.
3. Select the Session Package that you are interested in.
4. Choose a Session Date and Session Time for your session.
5. Add any additional Notes about your session.
6. Confirm your Session Package selection.
7. Enter the Session Start Time and Session End Time for your session.
8. Specify the Session Duration and Session Location of your session.
9. Provide your Contact Address if you'd like to receive any notifications or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name, as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Last Name**: Your last name, as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email**: A valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number**: A phone number where we can contact you.
* **Contact Email**: A secondary email address where we can contact you.
* **Session Packages**: Choose one of the available session packages to book your photography session (e.g., Session 1, Session 2, Session 3).
* **Session Date**: The date of your photography session.
* **Session Time**: The time of your photography session.
* **Notes**: Any additional information about your session that we should know.
* **Session Package**: Confirm your selected session package.
* **Session Start Time**: The start time of your photography session.
* **Session End Time**: The end time of your photography session.
* **Session Duration**: The length of your photography session.
* **Session Location**: The location of your photography session.
* **Session Date**: The date of your photography session (this field is for your convenience, please use the Session Date field above for the session date).
* **Contact Address**: If you have a preferred address for notifications or updates, please provide it here.
* **Package Price**: The price of your selected session package (this field is for internal use only, please do not fill it in).

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields to avoid any issues with your order.
- If you need to make any changes to your order, please contact us directly.
- We will be in touch with you shortly after submitting your order to confirm the details.
