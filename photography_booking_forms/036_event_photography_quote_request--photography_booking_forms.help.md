# Event Photography Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for event photography services. Please fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information about your event so that we can better understand your needs and provide an accurate quote.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of your event.
2. Choose the time of your event.
3. Enter the name of your event.
4. Select the type of event you are hosting (e.g. Wedding, Party, Corporate).
5. Enter the location of your event.
6. Add any additional details about your event.
7. Enter your name.
8. Enter your email address.
9. Enter your phone number.
10. Enter any contact message or additional information about your event.
11. Enter your quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of your event in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your event (e.g. Wedding Reception, Birthday Party).
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of event you are hosting (e.g. Wedding, Party, Corporate).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your event (e.g. Venue X, Address Y).
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, note, required/optional): Add any additional details about your event that may help us understand your needs.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a reference point of contact.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your quote.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with your quote.
* **Contact Message** (`contact_message`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about your event or message to us.
* **Quote Request** (`quote_request`, text, required/optional): Enter any specific quote request you may have.
