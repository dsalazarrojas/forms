# Vehicle Registration Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect vehicle registration information from users. It gathers essential details about a vehicle's owner and its status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your vehicle's details such as the vehicle registration number, type, and registration date.
2. Select the status of your vehicle (Active, Suspended, or Cancelled).
3. Enter your owner information, including your address.
4. Select your owner status (Active, Suspended, or Cancelled).
5. Provide your vehicle status (Active, Suspended, or Cancelled).
6. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Details** (Vehicle Details, text, required/optional): This field is for entering your vehicle's details such as the vehicle registration number and type.
- **Owner Information** (Owner Information, text, required/optional): Enter your owner information, including your address.
- **Vehicle Registration Number** (Vehicle Registration Number, text, required/optional): Enter your vehicle's registration number.
- **Vehicle Type** (Vehicle Type, text, required/optional): Select your vehicle's type from the provided options.
- **Owner Status** (Owner Status, select one, required/optional): Select your owner status (Active, Suspended, or Cancelled).
- **Owner Address** (Owner Address, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
- **Vehicle Status** (Vehicle Status, select one, required/optional): Select your vehicle status (Active, Suspended, or Cancelled).
- **Vehicle Registration Date** (Vehicle Registration Date, date, required/optional): Enter your vehicle's registration date.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields that are marked as required are filled out.
- Make sure to select the correct vehicle type from the provided options.
- Be accurate when entering dates and addresses.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
