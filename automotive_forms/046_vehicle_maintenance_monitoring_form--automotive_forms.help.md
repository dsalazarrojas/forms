# vehicle_maintenance_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle_maintenance_monitoring_form is used to monitor and manage vehicle maintenance schedules, service history, and other important details. This form helps users track and record vehicle-related information in a structured and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by providing the required vehicle number and vehicle model information.
- Enter the maintenance tasks, service history, and repair schedules for the vehicle.
- Enter the last service date, next service date, and vehicle mileage for the vehicle.
- Optional fields include vehicle condition and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Number**: Enter the vehicle's unique identification number or registration number (required).
- **Vehicle Model**: Enter the make and model of the vehicle (required).
- **Maintenance Tasks**: List any maintenance tasks that need to be performed on the vehicle (required).
- **Service History**: Provide a brief description of the vehicle's service history (required).
- **Repair Schedules**: List any upcoming or scheduled repairs for the vehicle (required).
- **Last Service Date**: Enter the date of the last service or maintenance performed on the vehicle (required).
- **Next Service Date**: Enter the date of the next scheduled service or maintenance for the vehicle (required).
- **Vehicle Mileage**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle (required).
- **Vehicle Condition**: Optionally, enter a brief description of the vehicle's current condition (optional).
- **Maintenance Frequency**: Select how often the vehicle is maintained (Daily, Weekly, Monthly) (required).
- **Maintenance Type**: Select the type of maintenance performed on the vehicle (Oil Change, Tire Rotation, Brake Check) (required).
- **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the vehicle's maintenance (optional).
- **Email**: Enter a contact email (optional).
- **Phone**: Enter a contact phone number (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and monitoring of vehicle maintenance.
- Use the form to schedule and record upcoming maintenance tasks and appointments.
- Review and update the form regularly to reflect any changes in the vehicle's maintenance status.
