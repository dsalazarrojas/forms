# vehicle detailing booking form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to book a vehicle detailing service for their vehicles. Please fill out the form with the required and optional information to ensure that we can provide the best service for your vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Customer Name field with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter the Make of your vehicle in the Vehicle Make field.
3. Enter the Model of your vehicle in the Vehicle Model field.
4. Enter the Year of your vehicle in the Vehicle Year field.
5. Choose the type of service you require in the Service Type field. Our service types include:
	* Vehicle Inspection
	* Detailing
	* Polishing
	* Other (please specify in the Notes field)
6. Select the date and time for the service in the Service Date and Service Time fields respectively.
7. Enter your phone number in the Customer Phone field.
8. Enter your email address in the Customer Email field.
9. Add any additional notes about your vehicle or the service in the Notes field.
10. Select the assigned user who will be performing the service from the Assigned User field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of your vehicle, e.g. Toyota, Honda, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of your vehicle, e.g. Corolla, Civic, etc.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of service you require.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to receive the service.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): Select the time you would like to receive the service.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your vehicle or the service.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_multiple, optional): Select the assigned user who will be performing the service.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
