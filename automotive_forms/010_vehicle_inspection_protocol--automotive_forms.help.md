# vehicle_inspection_protocol - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle_inspection_protocol form is used to record and document the status of a vehicle's inspection, including details about the vehicle make, model, year, and registration number.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Choose the vehicle make from the drop-down menu on Page 1.
2. Select the vehicle model from the drop-down menu on Page 2.
3. Enter the vehicle year on Page 3.
4. Enter the vehicle registration number on Page 4.
5. Choose the vehicle inspection status on Page 5.
6. Choose the vehicle inspection result on Page 6.
7. Provide comments about the vehicle inspection on Page 7.
8. Select the date of the inspection on Page 8.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_1`, `select_one`, required):
  This field is used to select the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_2`, `select_one`, required):
  This field is used to select the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_3`, `number`, required):
  This field is used to enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_4`, `text`, required):
  This field is used to enter the registration number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Inspection Status** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_5`, `select_one`, required):
  This field is used to select the status of the vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Result** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_6`, `select_one`, required):
  This field is used to select the result of the vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Comments** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_7`, `text`, required):
  This field is used to enter comments about the vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date** (`vehicle_inspection_protocol_page_8`, `date`, required):
  This field is used to enter the date of the vehicle inspection.
## Tips
- Please ensure that the vehicle make, model, and year match the details provided for the vehicle registration number.
- Please ensure that the vehicle inspection status and result are accurately selected.
- Please enter accurate comments and date for the vehicle inspection.
