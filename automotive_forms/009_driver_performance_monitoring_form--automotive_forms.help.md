# Driver Performance Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Driver Performance Monitoring Form is used to track and analyze the performance of drivers in vehicles. This form helps to collect data on various parameters, such as vehicle ID, driver ID, monitoring date, driving time, average speed, maximum speed, and acceleration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing each page carefully to understand the required information.
2. Enter the correct vehicle ID in the `vehicle_id` field (page 1).
3. Enter the correct driver ID in the `driver_id` field (page 2).
4. Select the monitoring date from the calendar picker for the `monitoring_date` field (page 3).
5. Enter the driving time spent on the road in the `driving_time` field (page 4).
6. Report the average speed achieved during the monitoring period in the `average_speed` field (page 5).
7. Record the maximum speed reached during the monitoring period in the `max_speed` field (page 6).
8. Enter the acceleration value achieved during the monitoring period in the `acceleration` field (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **vehicle_id** (`vehicle_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier of the vehicle being monitored.
- **driver_id** (`driver_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier of the driver being monitored.
- **monitoring_date** (`monitoring_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date when the monitoring took place.
- **driving_time** (`driving_time`, number, required/optional): Enter the total time spent driving during the monitoring period.
- **average_speed** (`average_speed`, number, required/optional): Report the average speed achieved during the monitoring period.
- **max_speed** (`max_speed`, number, required/optional): Record the highest speed reached during the monitoring period.
- **acceleration** (`acceleration`, number, required/optional): Enter the rate of acceleration achieved during the monitoring period.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you enter accurate and consistent data to ensure reliable results.
- If you need help or clarification on any field, please refer to this guide or contact your supervisor/instructor.
- Double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
