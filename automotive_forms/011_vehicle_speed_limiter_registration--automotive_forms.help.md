# Vehicle Speed Limiter Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register speed limiter installations on vehicles. It collects information on the owner, vehicle, and installation details to ensure accurate and efficient documentation.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form sections to understand what information is being requested.
* Fill in your vehicle's owner details in the "Owner Details" section.
* Enter your vehicle's registration number in the "Vehicle Registration Number" field.
* Select the correct "Installation Location" from the given options.
* Choose the status of the speed limiter from the options provided.
* Enter the speed limiter's serial number if available.
* Add any relevant notes or comments as needed.
* Review the form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details**: Enter your name and contact information as the vehicle owner or operator.
* **Vehicle Data**: Enter your vehicle's make, model, and year of manufacture.
* **Speed Limiter Installation**: Describe the installation process and any relevant details.
* **Speed Limiter Type**: Select the type of speed limiter used (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3, Type 4).
* **Speed Limiter Certificate**: Attach any relevant certification documents or provide a description.
* **Owner Vehicle Relationship**: Enter your relationship with the vehicle (e.g., owner, operator, or driver).
* **Owner Vehicle Number**: Enter your vehicle's number plate or VIN.
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Date Installed**: Enter the date the speed limiter was installed.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date the speed limiter was installed.
* **Installation Status**: Select whether the speed limiter is installed, not installed, or unknown.
* **Installation Location**: Select the workshop or location where the speed limiter was installed.
* **Installation Details**: Provide any additional details about the installation.
* **Notes**: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the installation.
* **Speed Limiter Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the speed limiter.
* **Speed Limiter Serial Number**: Enter the speed limiter's serial number.
* **Speed Limiter Manufacturer**: Enter the speed limiter's manufacturer.
* **Speed Limiter Model**: Enter the speed limiter's model.
* **Speed Limiter Size**: Enter the speed limiter's size.
* **Speed Limiter Capacity**: Enter the speed limiter's capacity.
* **Speed Limiter Installed By**: Enter the person who installed the speed limiter.
* **Installation Cost**: Enter the cost of installation.
* **Speed Limiter Status**: Select the status of the speed limiter (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
* **Vehicle Insurance**: Select whether the vehicle is insured or not.
* **Speed Limiter Notes 2**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the speed limiter.
