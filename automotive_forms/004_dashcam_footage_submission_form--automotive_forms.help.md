# Dashcam Footage Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dashcam Footage Submission Form is designed to collect information about a dashcam footage submission related to a specific incident. This form will help you provide clear and accurate information about the incident, including the time and location it occurred, the vehicle involved, and any damage caused.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the incident.
* If you are uploading a video file, make sure it is a valid dashcam footage.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details** (`incident_details`): Please describe the incident in a concise and accurate manner.
* **Video** (`incident_video`): Please upload your dashcam footage related to the incident.
* **Time** (`incident_time`): Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **Location** (`incident_location`): Enter the location where the incident occurred.
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`): Enter any relevant information about the driver involved.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`): Enter any information about the vehicle involved, such as make, model, and license plate number.
* **Damage Details** (`damage_details`): Describe any damage caused by the incident.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`): If you have any supporting files or attachments, you can upload them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Double-check your entries and submit your report.
