# Auto Repair Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on your experience with our auto repair service. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers in the future.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each section carefully and answer every question as accurately as possible.
2. Select the appropriate option for each question, and provide a brief explanation if necessary.
3. For open-ended questions, please provide detailed comments to help us understand your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief welcome note from us.
* **Customer Information** (Optional): If you'd like to provide your contact details, please do so here. This will help us get in touch with you if needed.
* **First Name** (Optional): If you're willing to share your first name, please enter it here.
* **Last Name** (Optional): If you're willing to share your last name, please enter it here.
* **Email Address** (Optional): If you'd like us to follow up with you, please enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): If you'd like us to contact you via phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **Service Information**: This section is to provide details about the service you received. Please describe the service you had done and any related issues.
* **Date of Service**: This is a required field where you need to enter the date of the service you received.
* **Type of Service**: Please select the type of service you received from the options provided.
* **Vehicle Year**: If you'd like to share the year of your vehicle, please enter it here.
* **Vehicle Make**: If you'd like to share the make of your vehicle, please enter it here.
* **Vehicle Model**: If you'd like to share the model of your vehicle, please enter it here.
* **Technician Name**: If you remember the name of the technician who worked on your vehicle, please enter it here.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Please select how satisfied you were with the overall service you received.
* **Service Quality Rating**: Please rate the quality of work you received.
* **Staff Friendliness**: Please rate how friendly the staff was during your visit.
* **Staff Knowledge**: Please rate how knowledgeable the staff was during your visit.
* **Wait Time**: Please select how long you waited during the service.
* **Wait Time Satisfaction**: Please rate your satisfaction with the wait time.
* **Facility Cleanliness**: Please rate the cleanliness of our facility.
* **Communication Rating**: Please rate how well we communicated with you.
* **Explanation of Work Done**: Please select how well the work was explained to you.
* **Pricing Fairness**: Please rate how fair the pricing was.
* **Value for Money**: Please rate how good of a value you felt you received.
* **Timeliness of Service**: Please select how on-time the service was completed.
* **Was Problem Resolved**: Please select whether the issue you had was resolved.
* **Likelihood to Return**: Please select how likely you are to return to our service.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: Please select how likely you are to recommend our service.
* **Best Aspect of Service**: This is an optional field where you can describe what we did well.
* **Areas for Improvement**: This is an optional field where you can suggest areas we could improve.
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field where you can share any additional comments or feedback. 

Note: Please answer every question as accurately as possible, and provide detailed comments where necessary. This will help us improve our services and provide better experiences for our customers.
