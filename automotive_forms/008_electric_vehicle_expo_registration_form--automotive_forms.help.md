# electric_vehicle_expo_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electric_vehicle_expo_registration_form is used for registering electric vehicles at an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address if you have one.
3. Enter your Phone number if you have one.
4. Select the year of your vehicle from the list of options.
5. Select the Make of your vehicle from the list of options.
6. Enter the Model of your vehicle.
7. Select the Type of your vehicle from the list of options.
8. Enter the name of the event you are attending.
9. Enter the date of the event.
10. Select the time of the event.
11. Add any additional Notes about your vehicle.
12. Select the Assigned Tool for your vehicle.
13. Enter the Category of your vehicle.
14. Enter the Form ID.
15. Enter the Output File name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you have one.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Year** (Year, select_one, required): Select the year of your vehicle from the list of options.
* **Make** (Make, select_multiple, required): Select the make of your vehicle from the list of options.
* **Model** (Model, text, optional): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Type** (Type, select_multiple, required): Select the type of your vehicle from the list of options.
* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Select the time of the event.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your vehicle.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to you for your vehicle.
* **Category** (Category, text, optional): Enter the category of your vehicle.
* **Form ID** (Form ID, text, optional): Enter the form ID.
* **Output File** (Output File, text, optional): Enter the output file name.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for help.
