# Digital Title Transfer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the transfer of a vehicle's title from one owner to another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information for each party involved in the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. seller_name (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the name of the seller.
### 2. seller_address (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the address of the seller.
### 3. seller_phone_number (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the phone number of the seller.
### 4. vehicle_year (number, optional)
*   This field is used to record the year of the vehicle.
### 5. vehicle_make (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the make of the vehicle.
### 6. vehicle_model (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the model of the vehicle.
### 7. vehicle_vin (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
### 8. buyer_name (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the name of the buyer.
### 9. buyer_phone_number (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the phone number of the buyer.
### 10. buyer_address (text, optional)
*   This field is used to record the address of the buyer.
### 11. transfer_date (date, optional)
*   This field is used to record the date of the transfer.
### 12. transfer_price (number, optional)
*   This field is used to record the price of the transaction.
### 13. seller_signature (note, optional)
*   This field is used to record the seller's signature.
### 14. buyer_signature (note, optional)
*   This field is used to record the buyer's signature.

Note: All fields are optional, but it's recommended that all fields be filled in for accurate and complete documentation of the transfer.
