# Auto Detailing Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from customers to provide a quote for an auto detailing service. This form collects customer contact and vehicle information, service preferences, and detailing package details to create a personalized quote for the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer information: Please provide your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your vehicle information: Describe your vehicle, including its type, year, make, and model.
3. Select your preferred services: Choose the detailing packages and services you're interested in.
4. Choose your service location: Decide where you'd like the detailing service to take place.
5. Provide any special requests or concerns: If you have any specific areas of concern or requests, please let us know.
6. Submit your form: Once you've completed all the fields, click the submit button to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Page 1): Please provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
	+ **First Name**: Your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: Your last name.
	+ **Email Address**: Where we'll send your quote.
* **Vehicle Information** (Page 2): Tell us about your vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you have.
	+ **Vehicle Year**: The year of manufacture.
	+ **Vehicle Make**: Manufacturer name.
	+ **Vehicle Model**: Model name.
* **Vehicle Color** (Page 3): The primary exterior color of your vehicle.
* **Current Vehicle Condition** (Page 3): Describe the condition of your vehicle.
* **Service Packages** (Page 4): Select the service package that interests you.
* **Interior Services** (Page 4): Select any desired interior services.
	+ **Vacuum and Dusting**: Vacuum and dust interior surfaces.
	+ **Leather Cleaning**: Clean and condition leather surfaces.
	+ **Carpet Shampoo**: Shampoo and clean interior carpets.
	+ **Seat Stain Removal**: Remove stains from seats.
	+ **Odor Treatment**: Eliminate odors.
	+ **Dashboard Conditioning**: Condition and protect dashboard surfaces.
	+ **Window Cleaning**: Clean windows.
* **Exterior Services** (Page 5): Select any desired exterior services.
	+ **Hand Wash**: Wash the exterior of your vehicle.
	+ **Clay Bar Treatment**: Remove contaminants from paint.
	+ **Wax Application**: Apply a layer of wax to protect the paint.
	+ **Polish and Buff**: Polish and buff exterior surfaces.
	+ **Scratch Removal**: Remove minor scratches.
	+ **Tire Dressing**: Condition and protect tires.
	+ **Engine Bay Cleaning**: Clean the engine bay.
* **Add-On Services** (Page 6): Select any additional services you'd like.
	+ **Headlight Restoration**: Restore headlight clarity.
	+ **Pet Hair Removal**: Remove pet hair.
	+ **Child Seat Cleaning**: Clean child seats.
	+ **Trunk Organization**: Organize your trunk.
	+ **Paint Protection Film**: Apply paint protection film.
	+ **Undercarriage Wash**: Wash the undercarriage.
* **Service Location Preference** (Page 6): Choose where you'd like the service to take place.
* **Preferred Service Date** (Page 7): Select a date for the service.
* **Preferred Time** (Page 7): Select a time for the service.
* **Special Requests or Concerns** (Page 7): If you have any specific areas of concern or requests, please let us know.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (Page 8): Tell us how you found our services.
	+ **Google Search**: Found us through Google.
	+ **Social Media**: Found us through social media.
	+ **Friend or Family**: A friend or family member recommended us.
	+ **Previous Customer**: You were a previous customer.
	+ **Advertisement**: Saw our advertisement.
	+ **Other**: Other
