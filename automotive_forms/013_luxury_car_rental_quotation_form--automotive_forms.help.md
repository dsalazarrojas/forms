# Luxury Car Rental Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to generate a quotation for luxury car rental. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your First Name.
2. Provide your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Select the date you want to rent the car on.
6. Choose a rental time.
7. Specify the desired length of rental.
8. Choose the type of car you want to rent (Luxury Car, Economy Car, or SUV).
9. Choose the type of insurance you want (Optional, Required, or Not Applicable).
10. Add any comments about your rental preferences.
11. If applicable, select a category (Luxury Car, Economy Car, or SUV).
12. Enter any other relevant information (Form ID).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, **optional**): 
    Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, **optional**): 
    Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, **optional**): 
    Enter your email address in this format: `username@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, **optional**): 
    Enter your phone number in this format: `123-456-7890`.
* **Rental Date** (`Rental Date`, date, **optional**): 
    Select the date you want to rent the car on.
* **Rental Time** (`Rental Time`, time, **optional**): 
    Choose the rental time.
* **Desired Rental Length** (`Desired Rental Length`, number, **optional**): 
    Specify the length of time you want to rent the car for.
* **Car Type** (`Car Type`, select_one, **optional**): 
    Select the type of car you want to rent (Luxury Car, Economy Car, or SUV).
* **Insurance Type** (`Insurance Type`, select_multiple, **optional**): 
    Choose the type of insurance you want (Optional, Required, or Not Applicable).
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, **optional**): 
    Add any comments about your rental preferences.
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, **optional**): 
    If applicable, select a category for your rental (Luxury Car, Economy Car, or SUV).
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, text, **optional**): 
    You can enter any other relevant information about your rental.
