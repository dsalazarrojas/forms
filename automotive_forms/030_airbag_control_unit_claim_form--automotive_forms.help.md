**Airbag Control Unit Claim Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

The Airbag Control Unit Claim Form is designed to gather information about incidents involving airbag control units in vehicles. This form helps us understand the incident's details, allowing us to assess the issue and provide the best possible resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and supporting documents before starting.
2. Fill in your claimant information, including your name, email address, phone number, and address.
3. Enter your vehicle's details, such as its year, make, model, and Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
4. Provide the date and location of the incident, as well as the type of incident that occurred.
5. Describe the symptoms and diagnostic codes you observed, if any.
6. Report any injuries sustained and provide a detailed description if necessary.
7. Indicate the extent of vehicle damage and whether a police report was filed.
8. Specify the insurance company and claim number, if applicable.
9. Select the type of claim you're submitting.
10. Choose the desired resolution outcome and provide a detailed incident description.
11. Indicate whether you have supporting documents and select the types you're submitting.
12. Sign and date the form to confirm your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claimant Name** (`claimant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`claimant_email`, `email`, required): Provide your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`claimant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`claimant_address`, `text`, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Claim Date** (`claim_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you're submitting this claim.
* **Claim Number** (`claim_number`, `text`, optional): Provide the reference number if available.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's manufacturer.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's model name.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`vin`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's full VIN.
* **Current Mileage** (`mileage`, `number`, required): Enter your odometer reading.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, `text`, required): Enter where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of incident that occurred.
* **Airbag Deployment Status** (`airbag_deployment`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether airbags deployed as expected.
* **ACU Part Number** (`acu_part_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the control unit part number if known.
* **ACU Serial Number** (`acu_serial_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the serial number if available.
* **Symptoms Observed** (`symptoms_observed`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all symptoms you observed.
* **Diagnostic Codes** (`diagnostic_codes`, `text`, optional): Enter any diagnostic codes retrieved.
* **Injuries Sustained** (`injuries_sustained`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the nature of injuries, if any.
* **Injury Description** (`injury_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of injuries, if necessary.
* **Vehicle Damage Extent** (`vehicle_damage`, `select_one`, required): Report the extent of vehicle damage.
* **Police Report Filed** (`police_report`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether a police report was filed.
* **Police Report Number** (`police_report_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the report number if available.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your insurer.
* **Insurance Claim Number** (`insurance_claim_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the claim number if available.
* **Repair Facility** (`repair_facility`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the vehicle is being repaired.
* **Claim Type** (`claim_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the type of claim you're submitting.
* **Desired Resolution** (`desired_resolution`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the desired outcome.
* **Detailed Incident Description** (`incident_description`, `text`, required): Describe what happened.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have documents to submit.
* **Document Types** (`document_types`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of documents you're submitting.
* **Claimant Signature** (`claimant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm your claim.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Date and time of signature.
