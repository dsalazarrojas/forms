# Dealership Maintenance Service Feedback Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide feedback about your recent service visit at our dealership. Your input will help us improve our services and provide better experience for our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title.
2. Enter your dealership name.
3. Select the type of service you received.
4. Rate the service you received.
5. Provide any additional comments about the service.
6. Rate the service staff.
7. Provide any additional comments about the staff.
8. Select the service advisor(s) who assisted you.
9. Enter the date of your visit.
10. Enter the time of your visit.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter your email address.
13. Enter the phone number of the service advisor.
14. Enter the email address of the service advisor.
15. Provide any follow-up comments.
16. Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up.
17. Provide a message for follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your visit, e.g., "Oil change and tire rotation".
* **Dealership Name** (`dealership_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of our dealership where you visited.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you received, such as "Service Visit" or "Oil Change".
* **Rating of Service** (`rating_service`, number, optional): Rate your service experience on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being excellent.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about your service experience.
* **Rating of Staff** (`rating_staff`, number, optional): Rate the staff's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being excellent.
* **Comments about Staff** (`comments_staff`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the staff's performance.
* **Service Advisor** (`service_advisor`, select_multiple, optional): Select the service advisor(s) who assisted you during your visit.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of your visit in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time of your visit.
* **Phone Number** (`dealership_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Service Advisor Phone Number** (`service_advisor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the service advisor.
* **Service Advisor Email** (`service_advisor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the service advisor.
* **Follow-up Comments** (`follow_up_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Contact Method** (`follow_up_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted for follow-up, such as "Call" or "Email".
* **Follow-up Message** (`follow_up_message`, text, optional): Provide a message for follow-up.

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
