# Roadside Assistance Service Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help our roadside assistance service team understand your specific needs and preferences for roadside assistance services. Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure we can tailor our services to your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on the "Customer Info" page.
2. On the "Roadside Assistance" page, select one or multiple services that best fit your needs.
3. On the "Contact Info" page, enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
4. On the "Vehicle Info" page, provide details about your vehicle.
5. On the "Location" page, enter the location where you need the roadside assistance service.
6. On the "Service Providers" page, select one or multiple service providers that interest you.
7. On the "Notes" page, add any additional information or comments about your service requirements.
8. On the "Phone" page, enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Info
* **Customer Name** (`customer_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Roadside Assistance
* **Roadside Assistance** (`roadside_service`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one or multiple services that best fit your needs. Options: Service 1, Service 2, Service 3.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
### Vehicle Info
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about your vehicle, including make, model, and year.
### Location
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location where you need the roadside assistance service.
### Service Providers
* **Service Providers** (`service_providers`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple service providers that interest you. Options: Service Provider 1, Service Provider 2, Service Provider 3.
### Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional information or comments about your service requirements.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your service.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
* We will review and respond to your submission within 24 hours.
