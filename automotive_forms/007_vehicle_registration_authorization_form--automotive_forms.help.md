# vehicle_registration_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle registration authorization form is designed to gather information about a vehicle, its owner, and the person authorizing the registration. This form is part of the vehicle registration process, helping to ensure accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle details:
	* Select the vehicle's make from the provided options or type 'Other' if the make is not listed.
	* Choose the vehicle's model from the provided options or type 'Other' if the model is not listed.
	* Select the vehicle's year from the provided options or type 'Other' if the year is not listed.
	* Choose the vehicle's color from the provided options or type 'Other' if the color is not listed.
	* Select the vehicle type (Car, Bike, Truck, Boat, or Other).
	* Enter the vehicle's engine size.
	* Choose the vehicle's engine type (Gas, Diesel, Electric, Hybrid, or Other).
	* Select the vehicle's transmission type (Automatic, Manual, Semi-Automatic, Continuously Variable, or Other).
	* Choose the vehicle's status (Registered, Unregistered, Pending, Cancelled, or Other).
2. Fill in the owner's information:
	* Enter the owner's name.
	* Enter the owner's address.
	* Enter the owner's phone number.
3. Provide authorization details:
	* Select who authorized the registration (Vehicle Dealership, Vehicle Registration Center, or Owner).
	* Choose the reason for the registration (Dealer, Repair, Insurance, or Other).
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Verify the submitted information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vehicle Details (1)**: Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Nissan).
- **Owner (2)**: Enter the owner's identifying number.
- **Name (3)**: Enter the owner's name.
- **Address (4)**: Enter the owner's address.
- **Phone (5)**: Enter the owner's phone number.
- **Signature (6)**: Enter your digital signature, as proof of authorization.
- **Registration Date (7)**: Enter the date of the vehicle registration.
- **Authorized By (8)**: Select who authorized the registration (e.g., Vehicle Dealership, Vehicle Registration Center, Owner).
- **Reason (9)**: Choose the reason for the registration (e.g., Dealer, Repair, Insurance).
- **Notes (10)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the registration.
- **Make (11)**: Enter the vehicle's make.
- **Model (12)**: Enter the vehicle's model.
- **Year (13)**: Enter the vehicle's year.
- **Color (14)**: Enter the vehicle's color.
- **Vehicle Type (15)**: Select the vehicle type (e.g., Car, Bike, Truck, Boat).
- **Engine Size (16)**: Enter the vehicle's engine size.
- **Engine Type (17)**: Enter the vehicle's engine type.
- **Transmission (18)**: Enter the vehicle's transmission type.
- **Vehicle Status (19)**: Enter the vehicle's status (e.g., Registered, Unregistered, Pending, Cancelled).
- **VIN (20)**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
- **Signature 2 (21)**: Enter your digital signature, as proof of authorization.
- **Date 2 (22)**: Enter the date of the vehicle registration.
- **Name 2 (23)**: Enter the owner's name.
- **Address 2 (24)**: Enter the owner's address.
- **Phone 2 (25)**: Enter the owner's phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Use the provided options for select fields to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about a field, consult the form's creator or supervisor.
- Double-check your submissions for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary authorization to complete this form.
