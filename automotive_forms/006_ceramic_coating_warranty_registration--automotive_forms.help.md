# Ceramic Coating Warranty Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ceramic Coating Warranty Registration form is used to collect and record important warranty information about your vehicle. This form helps ensure that your warranty is properly processed and that you are informed about your warranty coverage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the **Registration Date**, which is the date you registered for the warranty.
2. Enter your **Vehicle Owner Name**, which is your full legal name.
3. Enter your **Email Address**, which is the email address you want to use for warranty communications.
4. Enter your **Phone Number**, which is your contact phone number.
5. Enter your **Mailing Address**, which is your full address for warranty documents.
6. Enter the **Vehicle Year**, which is the year of your vehicle.
7. Enter the **Vehicle Make**, which is the manufacturer of your vehicle.
8. Enter the **Vehicle Model**, which is the model of your vehicle.
9. Enter the **Vehicle Color**, which is the primary exterior color of your vehicle.
10. Enter the **Vehicle VIN**, which is the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number.
11. Enter the **Current Mileage**, which is the odometer reading at the time of coating.
12. Enter the **Installer Company Name**, which is the name of the installation facility.
13. Enter the **Installer Location**, which is the city and state of the installation.
14. Enter the **Installation Date**, which is the date the ceramic coating was applied.
15. Enter the **Coating Product Name**, which is the specific ceramic coating product used.
16. Select the **Coating Package Level**, which is the tier or level of coating applied (e.g., Entry level coating, Standard coating, etc.).
17. Select the **Warranty Duration**, which is the length of warranty coverage (e.g., 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, etc.).
18. Choose the **Proof of Installation**, which indicates whether proof of installation has been provided (e.g., yes, yes with invoice, yes with certificate, etc.).
19. Select **Additional Services Performed**, which is a list of services provided (e.g., paint correction, clay bar treatment, etc.).
20. Choose whether you are enrolled in a **Maintenance Plan**, which is an annual or bi-annual maintenance program.
21. Select the **How Did You Hear About Us**, which is the source of your referral (e.g., internet search, social media, etc.).
22. Accept the **Terms and Conditions**, which is the agreement to the warranty terms.
23. Agree to **Marketing Communications Consent**, which is the agreement to receive product updates and maintenance reminders.
