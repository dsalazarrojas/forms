# Car Rental Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have rented a car from our company. It will help us improve our services and better serve our customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer it based on your experience with our car rental services. You can select one of the provided options for each question, unless otherwise specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: This is your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: This is your contact email address. If we need to follow up with you, we'll use this email address.
* **Phone Number**: This is your contact phone number. If we need to contact you, we'll call you at this number.
* **Rental Date**: This is the date you rented the vehicle. Please enter the date using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Return Date**: This is the date you returned the vehicle. If you didn't return the vehicle, please enter the original rental date.
* **Pickup Location**: This is the location where you picked up the vehicle. Please enter the location as it appears on your rental agreement.
* **Vehicle Category**: This is the type of vehicle you rented. If you're unsure, please select "Other".
* **Rental Duration**: This is how many days you rented the vehicle. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Purpose of Rental**: This is why you rented the vehicle. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: This is your overall satisfaction with our car rental services. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Vehicle Rating**: This is your rating of the vehicle you rented. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Vehicle Cleanliness**: This is your rating of the vehicle's cleanliness. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Vehicle Condition**: This is your rating of the vehicle's condition. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Vehicle Performance**: This is your rating of the vehicle's performance. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Vehicle Match to Reservation**: This is whether the vehicle you received matched what you reserved. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Pickup Experience**: This is your rating of the pickup experience. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Wait Time at Pickup**: This is how long you waited at pickup. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Staff Friendliness**: This is your rating of the staff friendliness. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Staff Professionalism**: This is your rating of the staff professionalism. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Staff Knowledge**: This is your rating of the staff knowledge. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Return Experience**: This is your rating of the return experience. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Wait Time at Return**: This is how long you waited at return. Please select one of the provided options, unless the field is not required.
* **Billing Accuracy**: This is whether the billing was accurate. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Value for Money**: This is whether you thought our car rental services were a good value. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Additional Services Used**: This is if you used any additional services during your rental. Please select all that apply.
* **Additional Services Rating**: This is your rating of the additional services. Please select one of the provided options, unless the field is not required.
* **Likelihood to Recommend**: This is whether you would recommend our car rental services to others. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Likelihood to Return**: This is whether you would rent from us again. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Best Aspect**: This is what we did well during your rental experience. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
* **Areas for Improvement**: This is what we could improve during your rental experience. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
* **Vehicle Suggestions**: This is any vehicle recommendations you have. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
* **Service Suggestions**: This is any service recommendations you have. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: This is how you heard about our car rental services. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Additional Comments**: This is any other feedback you have. Please enter your answer in the provided text field.
* **Contact Permission**: This is whether we can contact you for follow-up purposes. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Feedback Date**: This is the current date. Please do not enter an answer, as it's automatically populated.

Note: Some fields are not required, and you can skip them if they do not apply to your experience.
