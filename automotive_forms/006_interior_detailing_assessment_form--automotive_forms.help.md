# interiorDetailingAssessmentForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interiorDetailingAssessmentForm is designed to assess the interior of a vehicle. It asks for information on various aspects of the vehicle's interior, including its condition and features.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vehicle's interior carefully, taking note of any notable features or conditions.
2. For each field, select the most accurate answer based on your evaluation.
3. Ensure that you have a clear understanding of each field's purpose and the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_multiple, optional): Select the make of the vehicle being assessed. For example, Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle being assessed. For example, Corolla, Mustang, etc.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle's manufacture. Please ensure the format is consistent (e.g., 2009, 2010, etc.).
* **Vehicle Trim** (`vehicle_trim`, text, optional): Enter the trim level of the vehicle being assessed. For example, LX, EX, etc.
* **Vehicle Interior** (`vehicle_interior`, select_multiple, optional): Select the interior features or conditions of the vehicle being assessed. For example, leather, cloth, etc.
* **Exterior Damage** (`exterior_damage`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of exterior damage on the vehicle.
* **Interior Damage** (`interior_damage`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of interior damage on the vehicle.
* **Upholstery** (`upholstery`, select_one, optional): Select the type of upholstery used on the vehicle.
* **Odor** (`odor`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of a noticeable odor within the vehicle.
* **Stains** (`stains`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of stains on any interior surfaces.
* **Smoke Odor** (`smoke_odor`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of a smoke smell within the vehicle.
* **Excessive Wear** (`excessive_wear`, select_multiple, optional): Select the presence or absence of excessive wear on various components of the vehicle's interior.
* **Vehicle Odor** (`vehicle_odor`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of a specific vehicle-related odor.
* **Headliner** (`headliner`, select_one, optional): Select the condition and type of headliner in the vehicle.
* **Carpet** (`carpet`, select_one, optional): Select the condition and type of carpet in the vehicle.
* **Upholstery Stains** (`upholstery_stains`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of stains on the upholstery.
* **Floor Matting** (`floor_matting`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of floor matting in the vehicle.
* **Trunk** (`trunk`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of notable features or conditions in the trunk area.
* **Glove Box** (`glove_box`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of notable features or conditions in the glove box.
* **Console** (`console`, select_one, optional): Select the presence or absence of notable features or conditions on the console.
