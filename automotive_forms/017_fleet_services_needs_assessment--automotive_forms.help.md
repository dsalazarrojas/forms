# Fleet Services Needs Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for fleet services needs assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct answers for each field, ensuring that you have the necessary information and permissions to do so.
2. Review your answers carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Provide detailed answers for text fields and select the correct options for dropdown fields.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the provided explanations or contact your supervisor for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Ownership** (`vehicle_ownership`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of vehicle ownership, e.g., owned, leased, or rented.
* **Vehicle Usage** (`vehicle_usage`, date, required: false): Enter the date of vehicle usage, e.g., the date of the last vehicle usage.
* **Vehicle Maintenance** (`vehicle_maintenance`, time, required: false): Enter the last vehicle maintenance date and time, e.g., oil change, tire rotation, etc.
* **Fuel Consumption** (`fuel_consumption`, number, required: false): Enter the current fuel consumption rate.
* **Vehicle Location** (`vehicle_location`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current vehicle location, e.g., on-site, off-site, etc.
* **Vehicle Driver** (`vehicle_driver`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the drivers of the vehicle, e.g., employee, manager, etc.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of vehicle insurance, e.g., comprehensive, collision, etc.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, number, required: false): Enter the current vehicle mileage.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_one, required: false): Choose the vehicle make, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the vehicle model, e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the vehicle year, e.g., 2010, 2015, etc.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the vehicle type, e.g., sedan, SUV, etc.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): Enter the contact name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required: false): Enter the contact email.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter the company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required: false): Enter the job description.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): Enter the department.
* **Manager** (`manager`, text, required: false): Enter the manager's name.
* **Fleet Type** (`fleet_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the fleet type, e.g., commercial, personal, etc.
* **Fleet Size** (`fleet_size`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the fleet size, e.g., small, medium, large, etc.
* **Fleet Location** (`fleet_location`, select_one, required: false): Choose the fleet location, e.g., on-site, off-site, etc.
* **Fleet Frequency** (`fleet_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Choose the fleet frequency, e.g., daily, weekly, etc.
* **Fleet Status** (`fleet_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the fleet status, e.g., active, inactive, etc.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the submission date.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the submission time.
