# dealership_inventory_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering a vehicle's inventory information in an automotive dealership setting. It helps dealerships to catalog and manage their vehicle inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle information:
	* Fill in the vehicle's make, model, year, price, color, and other relevant details.
2. Select the vehicle status:
	* Choose the current status of the vehicle (Active, Available, Sold, Reserved, or Unknown).
3. Enter dealership information:
	* Enter the dealership name, address, contact, and other relevant details.
4. Enter customer information (optional):
	* If a customer owns the vehicle, provide their name, phone, email, and other relevant details.
5. Provide vehicle image (optional):
	* Upload or paste the vehicle's image.
6. Add notes and tags (optional):
	* Add any additional notes or tags related to the vehicle.
