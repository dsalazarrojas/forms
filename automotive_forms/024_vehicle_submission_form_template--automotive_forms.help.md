# Vehicle Submission Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about vehicles for use in a vehicle database or inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the vehicle submission form template.
2. Select the type of vehicle from the list of options.
3. Choose the make of the vehicle from the list of options.
4. Enter the model of the vehicle.
5. Enter the year of the vehicle.
6. Enter the color of the vehicle.
7. Enter the mileage of the vehicle.
8. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
9. Enter the license plate number of the vehicle.
10. Select the type of transmission of the vehicle.
11. Choose the type of engine of the vehicle.
12. Enter the interior color of the vehicle.
13. Enter the exterior color of the vehicle.
14. Choose the body style of the vehicle.
15. Enter the engine size of the vehicle.
16. Enter the type of fuel of the vehicle.
17. Enter the drive type of the vehicle.
18. Enter the type of transmission of the vehicle.
19. Enter the status of the vehicle.
20. Choose the condition of the vehicle.
21. Enter the location where the vehicle is located.
22. Enter a URL for an image of the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
23. Upload an image of the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
24. Enter any additional comments about the vehicle.
25. Enter your name and title.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_submission_form_template**: This is a text field where you can enter a title for the vehicle submission.
* **vehicle_type**: This field allows you to choose the type of vehicle you are submitting (e.g. Car, Truck, SUV).
* **vehicle_make**: This field allows you to select the make of the vehicle from a list of options.
* **vehicle_model**: This is a text field where you can enter the model of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_year**: This field is for entering the year of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_color**: This is a text field where you can enter the color of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_mileage**: This field is for entering the mileage of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_vin**: This is a text field where you can enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **license_plate**: This field is for entering the license plate number of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_transmission**: This field allows you to select the type of transmission of the vehicle (e.g. Manual, Automatic, Semi-Automatic).
* **vehicle_engine_type**: This field allows you to choose the type of engine of the vehicle (e.g. Gasoline, Diesel, Hybrid).
* **vehicle_interior_color**: This is a text field where you can enter the interior color of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_exterior_color**: This is a text field where you can enter the exterior color of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_body_style**: This field allows you to choose the body style of the vehicle (e.g. Sedan, Truck, SUV).
* **vehicle_engine_size**: This is a text field where you can enter the engine size of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_fuel_type**: This field is for entering the type of fuel of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_drive_type**: This is a text field where you can enter the drive type of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_transmission_type**: This field is for entering the type of transmission of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_status**: This field is for entering the status of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_condition**: This field allows you to choose the condition of the vehicle (e.g. New, Used, Refurbished).
* **vehicle_location**: This is a text field where you can enter the location where the vehicle is located.
* **vin_image_url**: This field is for entering a URL of an image of the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **vin_image**: This field allows you to upload an image of the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **vehicle_comments**: This is a text field where you can enter any additional comments about the vehicle.
* **submitted_by**: This field is for entering your name and title.
