# Vehicle Ownership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Ownership Application form is designed to collect information about vehicle ownership. It is intended for use by dealerships and other relevant authorities to gather data about vehicles and their owners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **dealer_info** field with the name and address of the dealership where the vehicle was purchased or sold.
2. Enter the vehicle's **VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)** in the **vin** field. This is a unique identifier assigned to each vehicle.
3. Enter the owner's **name** and **address** in the **name_and_address** field. This is where the vehicle is registered.
4. Enter the owner's **name** and **date of birth** (in the format **mm/dd/yyyy**) in the **owner_name** and **owner_dob** fields, respectively.
5. Enter the owner's phone number and email address in the **owner_phone** and **owner_email** fields, respectively.
6. Enter the **vehicle_year**, **vehicle_type**, and **vehicle_license_state**, **vehicle_license_number** fields with details about the vehicle.
7. Enter the type of **document_type** and any relevant documents in the **documents** field.
8. Select either **Yes** or **No** for **dealer_acknowledge** to confirm that the information is accurate.
9. Repeat steps 1-8 for the second vehicle if necessary.
10. Finally, click the **submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **dealer_info**: Enter the name and address of the dealership where the vehicle was purchased or sold.
- **vin**: Enter the vehicle's unique VIN number.
- **name_and_address**: Enter the owner's name and address.
- **owner_name**: Enter the owner's name.
- **owner_dob**: Enter the owner's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **owner_phone**: Enter the owner's phone number.
- **owner_email**: Enter the owner's email address.
- **vehicle_year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
- **vehicle_type**: Enter the type of vehicle (e.g., car, truck, etc.).
- **document_type**: Enter the type of document related to the vehicle (e.g., title, registration, etc.).
- **documents**: Enter any relevant documents related to the vehicle (optional).
- **dealer_acknowledge**: Confirm that the information is accurate.
- **vehicle_license_state**: Enter the state where the vehicle is registered.
- **vehicle_license_number**: Enter the vehicle's license number.
- **vehicle_license_state_2**: (Optional) Enter the state where the second vehicle is registered.
- **vehicle_license_number_2**: (Optional) Enter the license number of the second vehicle.
- **submit**: Click this button to complete the form.

Note: Some fields are optional and are only required for additional vehicles.
