# Auto Transport Carrier Contract Termination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to terminate the contract between your company and the carrier. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth termination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary information and authorization to fill out this form.
2. Choose the correct reason for termination from the provided options.
3. Fill out the required fields with the correct information.
4. Review and double-check all the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Carrier Contract Termination** (id: 1): This is the introduction to the form. Please read the instructions provided before proceeding.
* **Company Information** (id: 2): This section is for carrier company details. You can skip it if not applicable.
* **Company Name** (id: 3): Enter the legal name of your company.
* **Company Address** (id: 4): Type in the business address of your company.
* **City** (id: 5): Enter the city of your business.
* **State** (id: 6): Select the state where your business is located.
* **ZIP Code** (id: 7): Enter the 5-digit ZIP code of your business.
* **Contact Person** (id: 8): Provide the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title** (id: 9): Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (id: 10): Provide the business email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 11): Enter the business phone number.
* **Contract Information** (id: 12): This section is for providing details about the contract. You can skip it if not applicable.
* **Contract Number** (id: 13): Enter the contract ID number.
* **Contract Start Date** (id: 14): Choose the date when the contract began.
* **Contract Type** (id: 15): Select the type of agreement your company had with us.
* **MC Number** (id: 16): Enter the Motor Carrier number.
* **DOT Number** (id: 17): Enter the Department of Transportation number.
* **Termination Information** (id: 18): This section is for providing details about the termination. You can skip it if not applicable.
* **Requested Termination Date** (id: 19): Choose the date when you want the contract to end.
* **Reason for Termination** (id: 20): Select the reason for terminating the contract.
* **Reason Details** (id: 21): Provide more information about the reason for termination.
* **Notice Period** (id: 22): Choose the notice period you're providing.
* **Pending Loads** (id: 23): Select if you have active shipments and how many.
* **Pending Loads Details** (id: 24): List the load numbers if you have active shipments.
* **Completion Plan** (id: 25): Choose how you will finish any pending loads.
* **Company Equipment** (id: 26): Select if you have company equipment.
* **Equipment List** (id: 27): If you have company equipment, list them here.
* **Equipment Return Date** (id: 28): Choose the date when you will return the equipment.
* **Financial Information** (id: 29): This section is for providing payment details.
* **Outstanding Payments Due** (id: 30): Select if you're owed money or if you owe money.
* **Payments Owed to Company** (id: 31): If you're owed money, select the amount.
* **Final Payment Method** (id: 32): Choose how you want to receive your final payment.
* **Bank Name** (id: 33): If you want direct deposit, enter the bank name.
* **Account Number** (id: 34): Enter the last 4 digits of your account number.
* **Access and Credentials** (id: 35): This section is for providing system access information.
* **System Access** (id: 36): Select if you have system logins.
* **Credentials Returned** (id: 37): If you have system logins, select if you returned or will return them.
* **Additional Information** (id: 38): Provide any other relevant information.
* **Future Relationship** (id: 39): Select if you'd like to work with us again in the future.
* **Feedback** (id: 40): Provide any comments about your experience with us.
* **Authorization** (id: 41): Select if you agree and authorize the termination.
* **Authorized Signature** (id: 42): Type your digital signature.
* **Date of Signature** (id: 43): Enter the current date.
* **Printed Name** (id: 44): Enter your name as it should appear on the documents.

Please review the form and your contract before filling it out, ensuring you have the necessary information and authorization. If you have any questions, contact your representative or the company for clarification.
