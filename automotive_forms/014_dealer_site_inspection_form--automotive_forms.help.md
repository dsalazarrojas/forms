# Dealer Site Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dealer Site Inspection Form is a comprehensive inspection form for dealerships to report their findings on a vehicle's condition. This form captures various details about the vehicle, including its make, model, year, and condition, as well as the inspector's notes and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields for the vehicle's details (Vehicle Make, Vehicle Model, Vehicle Year, Dealer License, Dealer Address, Vehicle Condition, Vehicle Mileage, Vehicle Odometer, Vehicle Color, Vehicle Year Model, Vehicle Engine Size, and Vehicle Transmission).
2. Select the Inspection Type from the provided options.
3. Upload any relevant photos of the vehicle.
4. Enter your inspector's name and signature.
5. Enter the Inspection Date.
6. Add any relevant inspection notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dealer Site Inspection Form** (Page 1): This is the title of the form.
* **Inspection Details** (Page 2): This section is not currently used and may be removed or repurposed.
* **Inspection Findings** (Page 3): This section is not currently used and may be removed or repurposed.
* **Vehicle Details** (Page 4): 
  * **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle.
  * **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
  * **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Photos** (Page 5): Upload any relevant photos of the vehicle.
* **Inspection Notes** (Page 6): Add any relevant notes about the inspection.
* **Signature** (Page 7): Sign and date the form.
* **File Upload** (Page 8): Upload any additional files or documents related to the inspection.
* **Vehicle VIN** (Page 9): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **Inspection Date** (Page 10): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Type** (Page 11): Select the type of inspection (e.g. Pre-Purchase, Routine, etc.).
* **Vehicle Make** (Page 12): 
  * **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (Page 13): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (Page 14): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Dealer License** (Page 15): Enter your dealership's license number.
* **Inspector Name** (Page 16): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector Signature** (Page 17): Sign and date the form.
* **Vehicle Condition** (Page 18): Select the condition of the vehicle (e.g. Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Vehicle Mileage** (Page 19): Enter the mileage of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Odometer** (Page 20): Enter the odometer reading of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (Page 21): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Dealer Address** (Page 22): Enter your dealership's address.
* **Vehicle Year Model** (Page 23): Enter the year and model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Engine Size** (Page 24): Enter the engine size of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Transmission** (Page 25): Enter the type of transmission of the vehicle.

Note: This explanation assumes that the pages have not been merged or re-arranged.
