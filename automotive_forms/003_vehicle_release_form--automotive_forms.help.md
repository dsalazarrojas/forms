# Vehicle Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle's release from your company or facility. This includes details about the owner, vehicle details, transfer information, and vehicle status at the time of release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the vehicle's owner's name and any other relevant contact information.
2.  Provide details about the vehicle, such as its make, model, and year.
3.  Enter the date the vehicle is being transferred to a new party.
4.  Enter the details of the party the vehicle is being transferred to.
5.  Enter the vehicle's registration information.
6.  Enter the vehicle's current mileage reading.
7.  Enter the vehicle's odometer reading.
8.  Describe the vehicle's condition at the time of release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **vehicle_owner** (`vehicle_owner`, text, optional): The name and contact information of the vehicle's owner or current custodian.
*   **vehicle_details** (`vehicle_details`, text, optional): A brief description of the vehicle, including its make, model, and year.
*   **transfer_to** (`transfer_to`, text, optional): The name and contact information of the party the vehicle is being transferred to.
*   **transfer_date** (`transfer_date`, date, optional): The date the vehicle is being transferred to the new party.
*   **vehicle_registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, optional): The vehicle's registration information, including the license plate number.
*   **mileage_reading** (`mileage_reading`, number, optional): The vehicle's current mileage reading.
*   **odometer_reading** (`odometer_reading`, number, optional): The vehicle's current odometer reading.
*   **vehicle_condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, optional): A description of the vehicle's condition at the time of release, including any notable issues or concerns.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when providing vehicle information.
*   Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals to release the vehicle to the new party.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
