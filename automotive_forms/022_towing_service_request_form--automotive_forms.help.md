# Towing Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Towing Service Request Form is used to gather vehicle information for towing service. Fill out the form to provide essential details about the vehicle, its driver, and service requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the vehicle make to help us identify the vehicle make.
2. Provide the vehicle model for more accurate service planning.
3. Enter the vehicle year to determine the vehicle's age and specifications.
4. Enter the vehicle's license plate number to verify its identity.
5. Specify the vehicle's weight to help us plan the necessary equipment.
6. Enter the driver's name to ensure that we can communicate with them efficiently.
7. Enter the driver's phone number in case we need to contact them.
8. Enter the location where the vehicle is stranded.
9. Select the date of service to schedule the towing service.
10. Choose the time of service to plan the logistics.
11. Enter the name of the towing company providing the service (if applicable).
12. If there are any specific requests or instructions, enter them in the "Special Request" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Ford, Toyota, etc.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Mustang, Corolla, etc.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter the year of the vehicle, e.g., 2015, 2020, etc.
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's license plate number to identify the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, number, optional): Enter the approximate weight of the vehicle to help us plan the necessary equipment.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, optional): Enter the driver's name to ensure that we can communicate with them efficiently.
* **Driver Phone** (`driver_phone`, text, optional): Enter the driver's phone number in case we need to contact them.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the vehicle is stranded, e.g., highway, parking lot, etc.
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, date, optional): Select the date of service to schedule the towing service.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, time, optional): Choose the time of service to plan the logistics.
* **Towing Company** (`towing_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the towing company providing the service (if applicable).
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Enter any specific requests or instructions for the service.

Note: If a field is not required, it is not marked as such in the help guide to avoid confusing the user.
