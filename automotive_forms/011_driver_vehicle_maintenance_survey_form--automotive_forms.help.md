# driver_vehicle_maintenance_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The driver_vehicle_maintenance_survey_form is a form designed to gather information on vehicle maintenance practices, condition, frequency, and cost. This information will help the organization to better understand the needs and habits of vehicle owners, and to make informed decisions for future maintenance and service plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure the vehicle in question is relevant to the organization's database. This form should not be used for vehicles that have already been surveyed or maintained.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
3. Be honest and thorough in your responses to provide the best possible data for the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Maintenance Practices** (`vehicle_maintenance_practices`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the vehicle's maintenance practices, such as regular check-ups, oil changes, and tire rotations.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, select_one, required): Evaluate the overall condition of the vehicle, selecting one of the options: Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Maintenance Frequency** (`maintenance_frequency`, number, optional): Indicate how often the vehicle is maintained, in months or years.
* **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, number, optional): Estimate the average cost of maintenance for the vehicle, in dollars.
* **Service Advice** (`service_advice`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or advice for future service or maintenance.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any contact information for the vehicle owner or driver.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the vehicle owner or driver.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about the vehicle.

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* If unsure about any field, please consult with a supervisor or colleague.
* This form is for the purpose of gathering information only, and not for soliciting business or promotional activities.
