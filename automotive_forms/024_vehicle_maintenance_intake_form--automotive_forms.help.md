# Vehicle Maintenance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vehicle Maintenance Intake Form is used to report any issues or concerns with your vehicle. This form helps us assess and prioritize maintenance tasks to keep your vehicle in good condition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as "Required") and any optional fields as needed.
2. Answer each question accurately and to the best of your ability.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Info (1)**: Provide a brief description of your vehicle, including its make, model, and any relevant details.
* **Make And Model (2)**: Enter the vehicle's make and model, e.g., "Toyota Camry" or "Ford F-150".
* **Year (3)**: Enter the vehicle's model year.
* **Mileage (4)**: Enter the current mileage of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Problem (5)**: Describe the issue or concern you're experiencing with your vehicle.
* **Maintenance Needs (6)**: Check all options that apply to the maintenance needs of your vehicle, e.g., oil change, tire rotation, etc.
* **Vehicle Type (7)**: Select the type of vehicle you're reporting on, e.g., passenger, commercial, or agricultural.
* **Location (8)**: Provide the location where the issue occurred or is being reported.
* **Contact Number (9)**: Enter a contact number where you can be reached for follow-up questions or updates.
* **Email (10)**: Enter your email address for further communication.
* **Notes (11)**: Add any additional comments or information about the vehicle issue.
* **Vehicle Status (12)**: Select the current status of your vehicle, e.g., Active, Inactive, or Sold.
* **Vehicle Condition (13)**: Indicate whether the vehicle is in good or poor condition.
* **Vehicle Condition Notes (14)**: Provide more details about the vehicle's condition, if necessary.
* **Mechanic (15)**: Enter the name of the mechanic or repair shop handling the vehicle issue.
* **Mechanic Notes (16)**: Add any notes or comments about the mechanic's work or recommendations.
* **Vehicle Insurance (17)**: Check if the vehicle is insured, e.g., "Yes, through XYZ Insurance".
* **Vehicle Insurance Provider (18)**: Enter the name of the vehicle insurance provider, if applicable.
* **Vehicle Insurance Status (19)**: Select the current status of your vehicle insurance, e.g., Active, Inactive, or Expired.

Note: Fields marked as "Required" must be filled out for the form to be submitted.
