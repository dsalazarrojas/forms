# Vehicle Valuation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vehicle Valuation Form is used to capture details about a vehicle for valuation purposes. This information helps dealerships, sales teams, and other stakeholders to quickly and accurately assess the vehicle's value.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of vehicle from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the year of the vehicle.
3. Enter the make of the vehicle.
4. Enter the model of the vehicle.
5. Choose the trim level of the vehicle (High or Low).
6. Indicate if the vehicle has an engine (yes or no).
7. Describe any damage to the vehicle.
8. Indicate the level of damage to the vehicle (enter a number).
9. Specify if the vehicle has a transmission (yes or no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand what kind of vehicle you are valuing.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle. This will help us understand the age of the vehicle.
* **Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle. This will help us understand the brand of the vehicle.
* **Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle. This will help us understand the specific model of the vehicle.
* **Trim Level**: Choose the trim level of the vehicle (High or Low). This will help us understand the level of features and options on the vehicle.
* **Engine Size**: Indicate if the vehicle has an engine (yes or no). If the vehicle has an engine, you may be asked to provide additional information.
* **Damage Description**: Describe any damage to the vehicle. This will help us understand the extent of any repairs or maintenance needed.
* **Damage Level**: Enter a number that indicates the level of damage to the vehicle. Please note that this is a subjective measure and may not always reflect the true extent of damage.
* **Transmission**: Indicate if the vehicle has a transmission (yes or no). If the vehicle has a transmission, you may be asked to provide additional information.

## Tips

* Please make sure to answer all fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure of any information, you may want to ask for assistance from a knowledgeable party.
* Remember that this form is used for valuation purposes, and the information provided will help us make an informed decision about the vehicle's value.
