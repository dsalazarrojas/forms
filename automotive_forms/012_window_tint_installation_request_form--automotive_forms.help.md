# Window Tint Installation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request window tint installation services for your vehicle. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that we have all the necessary information to provide you with an accurate quote and schedule an appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Select the correct options for your vehicle preferences.
3. Choose a suitable appointment time that works for you.
4. Provide any additional comments or requests in the "Customer Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Details**: This is the section where you will provide your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
  - **Customer Details** (text, required/optional): Your name and contact information. Please enter your full name and any other relevant details that will help us contact you.
- **Vehicle Information**: This is the section where you will provide details about your vehicle.
  - **Vehicle Information** (text, required/optional): Your vehicle's make, model, and year. Please enter the accurate information about your vehicle to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible service.
- **Tint Preferences**: This is the section where you can choose your preferred type of tint.
  - **Tint Preferences** (select multiple, required/optional): Please select the type of tint you prefer: Light, Dark, or Medium.
- **Appointment Time**: This is the section where you can choose a suitable appointment time.
  - **Appointment Time** (date, required/optional): Please select a date and time that works for you. We'll do our best to accommodate your schedule.
- **Vehicle Year** : This is the section where you can enter the year of your vehicle.
  - **Vehicle Year** (number, required/optional): Please enter the year of your vehicle.
- **Vehicle Make**: This is the section where you can enter your vehicle's make.
  - **Vehicle Make** (text, required/optional): Please enter your vehicle's make.
- **Customer Message**: This is the section where you can provide any additional comments or requests.
  - **Customer Message** (text, required/optional): Please use this field to provide any additional comments or requests you may have. This will help us understand your needs better.
