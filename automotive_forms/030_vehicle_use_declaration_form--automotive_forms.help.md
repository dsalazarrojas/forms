# vehicle_use_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare the use of vehicles, gather information about employees, vehicle types, and their usage details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the form title: "Vehicle Use Declaration".
2.  Start filling out the form page by page, section by section.
3.  Begin with the first page, "Employee Details", where you will find a text field. Fill out the employee's details.
4.  Proceed to the next page, "Vehicle Information", and fill out the vehicle information section.
5.  Continue to the subsequent page "Usage Details", fill out the usage details section.
6.  Move on to the "Insurance Information" page, and fill out the insurance information.
7.  Next, fill out the vehicle registration information on the "Vehicle Registration" page.
8.  Then fill out the employee's vehicle information on the "Employee Vehicle Info" page.
9.  Fill out the vehicle use information on the "Vehicle Use" page.
10. Finally, proceed to the "Vehicle Driver Info" page, and fill out the vehicle driver information.
11.  Once you've completed all the pages, click submit to finalize your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details
*   **Employee Details** (`Employee Details`, text, required/optional): This field is where you will be filling out the employee's name and other details.

### Vehicle Information
*   **Vehicle Information** (`Vehicle Information`, text, required/optional): This field gathers information about the vehicle being declared.

### Usage Details
*   **Usage Details** (`Usage Details`, text, required/optional): This section gathers details about the vehicle's usage.

### Insurance Information
*   **Insurance Information** (`Insurance Information`, text, required/optional): This field gathers information about the vehicle's insurance status.

### Vehicle Registration
*   **Vehicle Registration** (`Vehicle Registration`, text, required/optional): This field gathers registration details of the declared vehicle.

### Employee Vehicle Info
*   **Employee Vehicle Info** (`Employee Vehicle Info`, text, required/optional): This section gathers employee's vehicle information.

### Vehicle Use
*   **Vehicle Use** (`Vehicle Use`, text, required/optional): This field declares the use of the vehicle.

### Vehicle Driver Info
*   **Vehicle Driver Info** (`Vehicle Driver Info`, text, required/optional): This field gathers driver information for the declared vehicle.

Note: The above fields are for information purposes only and should not be considered as legal or regulatory advice. This form is for general guidance purposes only. For accurate and up-to-date information regarding vehicle declarations and insurance, consult a legal professional.
