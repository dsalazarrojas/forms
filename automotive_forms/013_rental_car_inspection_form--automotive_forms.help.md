# Rental Car Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rental Car Inspection Form is a tool used to inspect and document the condition of rental cars. This form helps users to report on any damages or issues found during the inspection process, providing a clear and concise record of the vehicle's status. It is a crucial tool for rental car agencies to maintain accurate records and ensure that vehicles are properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Photos" page to upload any photos of the vehicle that may be relevant to the inspection.
2. On the "Vehicle Status" page, select the current status of the vehicle from the dropdown list (e.g., good, fair, poor).
3. In the "Notes" section, describe any issues or damages found during the inspection.
4. If applicable, provide your email address in the "Email" field, so the agency can contact you for further information or clarification.
5. Finally, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, in case the agency needs to contact you with questions or concerns.
6. In the "Address" field, enter the address where the inspection took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Photos (Text)**: Upload any relevant photos of the vehicle to provide visual evidence of its condition.
* **Vehicle Status (Number)**: Select the current status of the vehicle from the dropdown list. This will help the agency understand the level of maintenance or repairs required.
* **Notes (Note)**: Describe any issues or damages found during the inspection. Be as detailed as possible to help the agency understand the situation.
* **Email (Email)**: Provide your email address if you'd like to be contacted for further information or clarification.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number in case the agency needs to contact you with questions or concerns.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter the address where the inspection took place. This will help the agency locate the vehicle if necessary.
