# Fuel Pump Control Module Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the fuel pump control module, its type, control module type, version, and other related details. It will help us understand your experiences and gather feedback for improving our products and services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you need help or clarification on any field, feel free to ask.
3. Submit your responses and comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fuel Pump Type** (`fuel_pump_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of fuel pump you are using.
* **Control Module Type** (`control_module_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of control module used with your fuel pump.
* **Control Module Version** (`control_module_version`, text, required: false): Enter the version of the control module you are using.
* **Pump Control Status** (`pump_control_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all statuses that apply to your experience with the pump control module.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your contact email (if you want to receive follow-up communications).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number (if you want to receive follow-up calls).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date when you last submitted this form.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time when you last submitted this form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): If you were assigned a tool, enter the tool name here.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, text, required: false): If you were assigned a user, enter their name here.

**Tips**

* Take your time and provide accurate information to help us understand your experience.
* If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask.
* Please submit the form and let us know if you have any other comments or feedback.
