# Injector Service Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Injector Service Feedback Form is a tool designed to collect feedback from customers about their recent visit or experience with the injector service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information:
	* Enter your **Customer Name** in the field provided.
	* Provide your **Contact Number**, if available.
	* Enter your **Email**, if available.
2. Select the details of your visit:
	* Choose the **Service Date** of your visit.
	* Enter the **Service Time** of your visit.
3. Rate your experience:
	* Rate the **Service Quality** (out of 5) and explain why in the **Comments** field.
	* Rate the **Technician** that served you (out of 5) and provide any additional comments.
4. Submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Customer Name** (Text, Optional)
* This field is used to collect your name. Please enter your name as you would like it to be recorded in our system.
### 2. **Contact Number** (Text, Optional)
* This field is used to collect your contact number, if you have one.
### 3. **Email** (Email, Optional)
* This field is used to collect your email address, if you have one.
### 4. **Service Date** (Date, Optional)
* This field is used to select the date of your service visit. Please choose the date from the calendar or select it manually.
### 5. **Service Time** (Time, Optional)
* This field is used to select the time of your service visit. Please choose the time from the available options.
### 6. **Rating** (Number, Optional)
* This field is currently not used in this form. Please ignore this field.
### 7. **Comments** (Note, Optional)
* This field is used to provide any additional comments or feedback about your visit.
### 8. **Service Quality** (Number, Optional)
* This field is used to rate the quality of service you received. Please rate it out of 5 and provide any explanations in the **Comments** field.
### 9. **Technician** (Number, Optional)
* This field is used to rate the technician that served you. Please rate it out of 5 and provide any additional comments.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our service team directly.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated and will help us improve our service.
