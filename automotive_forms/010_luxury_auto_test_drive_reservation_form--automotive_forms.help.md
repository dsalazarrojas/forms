# Luxury Auto Test Drive Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Auto Test Drive Reservation Form is a tool used to schedule test drives for luxury vehicles. This form helps customers reserve a test drive at a designated location and duration, and allows customers to communicate with agents involved in the test drive process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your reservation date in the `Reservation Date` field.
2. Select the preferred test drive time from the `Test Drive Time` field.
3. Fill in your `Contact First Name` and `Contact Last Name` fields with your personal details.
4. Enter your `Contact Email` and `Contact Phone` fields with your contact information.
5. Select the `Vehicle Make` field with the luxury vehicle you are interested in.
6. Enter the `Test Drive Location` field with the preferred location for the test drive.
7. Select the `Test Drive Duration` field with the desired duration of the test drive from 15, 30, or 1 hour.
8. Add any additional `Customer Message` in the note field if needed.
9. Finally, submit the form to schedule your test drive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the test drive.
* **Test Drive Time**: Select the time you would like to schedule the test drive.
* **Contact First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Vehicle Make**: Select the make of the luxury vehicle you are interested in.
* **Vehicle Model**: Not applicable for this form. You can provide the model in the `Customer Message` field if needed.
* **Test Drive Location**: Select the preferred location for the test drive.
* **Test Drive Duration**: Select the duration of the test drive from 15, 30, or 1 hour.
* **Customer Message**: Add any additional information or comments for the test drive.
* **Test Drive Status**: Not applicable for this form. This field is used internally to track the test drive status.
* **Assigned Agent**: Not applicable for this form. This field is used internally to assign agents to the test drive.
* **Agent Message**: Not applicable for this form. This field is used internally to communicate with agents.
* **Test Drive Status Update**: Not applicable for this form. This field is used internally to update the test drive status.
