# Garage Door Opener Installation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garage Door Opener Installation Survey is designed to gather information from customers about their garage door opener installation needs. The form is used to capture essential details about the customer's preferences, door type, and installation requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your Customer Details (Section 1).
2. Select your Garage Door Type (Section 2).
3. Specify your Feature Preferences (Section 3).
4. Choose your Preferred Installation Time (Section 4).
5. Add any Additional Installation Notes (Section 5).
6. Select your Preferred Contact Method (Section 6).
7. Provide any Additional Notes or Comments (Section 7).
8. Enter your Contact Information (Section 8).
9. Enter your Installation Details (Section 9).
10. Specify your Payment Terms (Section 10).
11. Enter any Additional Notes or Comments (Section 11).
12. Enter your Service Company Details (Section 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Section 1: Customer Details
* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or Both).

### Section 2: Garage Door Details
* **Door Type** (`door_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of garage door you have (Roller, Swing, Slide, Panel, Sectional, or Other).
* **Door Type Description** (`door_type_description`, text, required): Describe the type of garage door you have.
* **Garage Details** (`garage_details`, text, required): Provide details about your garage.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the job.

### Section 3: Installation Preferences
* **Feature Preferences** (`feature_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which features you would like to have in your garage door opener installation (Opener type, Remote control, Safety features, Battery backup, or Other).
* **Preferred Installation Time** (`preferred_installation_time`, select_one, required): Select the time of day you prefer for the installation (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Weekend, or Flexible).

### Section 4: Installation and Payment
* **Installation Notes** (`installation_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the installation.
* **Service Company** (`service_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the service company.
* **Service Company Address** (`service_company_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the service company.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to pay for the installation (Payment on installation, Payment plan, or Other).
* **Payment Terms Details** (`payment_terms_details`, text, required): Enter the payment terms details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to make the payment (Credit card, Bank transfer, or Other).
* **Install Date** (`install_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the installation.
* **Install Time** (`install_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the installation.
* **Installation Status** (`installation_status`, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of the installation (Completed, Ongoing, Scheduled, or Canceled).
* **Installation Comments** (`installation_comments`, note, optional): Enter any comments about the installation.
* **Install Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, required): Enter your address.

Please note that the fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out before proceeding with the form.
