# Auto Repair Services Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential customers about their automotive needs, allowing the auto repair services team to provide accurate services and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each field carefully and understand what is being asked.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as "true" under the "required" section.
3. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Auto Repair Services**: (Note) This is an introduction to the form and a brief explanation of the services offered.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, note, false): This field is not required but helps us understand how to contact you.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, true): Enter your first name for our records.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, true): Enter your last name for our records.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, true): Enter the email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, true): Enter your contact phone number for us to reach you.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method, select_one, true): Choose how you would like us to contact you (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, or Any Method).
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, false): Enter your street address if you would like us to mail you something.
* **City** (City, text, false): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (State, select_one, false): Choose your state of residence from the list.
* **Zip Code** (ZIP Code, text, false): Enter your 5-digit zip code.
* **Vehicle Information** (Vehicle Information, note, false): This field is not required but provides information about your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year** (Vehicle Year, number, true): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (Vehicle Make, text, true): Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (Vehicle Model, text, true): Enter the model name of your vehicle.
* **Current Mileage** (Current Mileage, number, true): Enter the current odometer reading of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (Vehicle Condition, select_one, false): Choose the overall condition of your vehicle (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Service Needs** (Service Needs, note, false): This field is not required but provides information about your service needs.
* **Primary Service Needed** (Primary Service Needed, select_one, true): Choose the main reason you contacted us (Oil Change, Brake Service, Engine Repair, etc.).
* **Additional Services** (Additional Services, select_multiple, false): Choose any other services you may need (Oil Change, Tire Rotation, etc.).
* **Problem Description** (Problem Description, text, true): Describe the issue with your vehicle.
* **Service Urgency** (Service Urgency, select_one, true): Choose how soon you need service (Emergency, Within 24 hours, Within 3 days, etc.).
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (Preferred Appointment Date, date, false): Choose a date and time for the appointment if you have one in mind.
* **Preferred Time** (Preferred Time, time, false): Choose a time for the appointment if you have one.
* **Appointment Flexibility** (Appointment Flexibility, select_one, false): Choose how flexible you are with scheduling.
* **Service History** (Service History, note, false): This field is not required but provides information about your service history.
* **Last Service** (Last Service, date, false): Enter the date of your last service.
* **Do You Have a Regular Mechanic** (Do You Have a Regular Mechanic, select_one, false): Choose if you have a regular mechanic.
* **Reason for Switching** (Reason for Switching, text, false): Enter why you are looking for a new service provider.
* **Customer Preferences** (Customer Preferences, note, false): This field is not required but provides information about your preferences.
* **Parts Preference** (Parts Preference, select_one, false): Choose the type of parts you prefer (OEM Only, Aftermarket OK, Cheapest Option, etc.).
* **Budget Concerns** (Budget Concerns, select_one, false): Choose if budget is a primary concern.
* **Warranty Importance** (Warranty Importance, select_one, false): Choose how important warranty is to you.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, false): Enter any other information we should know.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (How Did You Hear About Us, select_one, true): Choose how you found us (Google Search, Social Media, Friend or Family Referral, etc.).
* **Referral Name** (Referral Name, text, false): Enter the name of the person who referred you if it's not you.
* **Marketing Consent** (Marketing Consent, select_one, true): Choose if you consent to marketing communications.
* **Best Time to Call** (Best Time to Call, select_one, false): Choose the best time to contact you.

## Tips
- Fill out this form only once to save time and prevent unnecessary work.
- Be thorough with your answers to ensure we understand your needs accurately.
- If you are not sure about any field, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
