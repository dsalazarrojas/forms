# Vehicle Advertisement Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle for advertisement purposes. It is used by sales teams to create advertisements for vehicles that are being sold or showcased.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Advertisement Submission Form" text field.
2. Enter the year of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Year" field.
3. Enter the make of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Make" field.
4. Enter the model of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Model" field.
5. Enter the trim of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Trim" field.
6. Enter the color of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Color" field.
7. Enter the year the vehicle was released in the "Vehicle Year Released" field.
8. If applicable, enter the price of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Price" field.
9. If applicable, enter a URL to an image of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Image URL" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Advertisement Submission Form** (Text): Enter a brief title or description of the vehicle being advertised.
* **Vehicle Year** (Text): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (Text): Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Honda, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (Text): Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Civic, etc.).
* **Vehicle Trim** (Text): Enter the trim level of the vehicle (e.g., GT, Sport, etc.).
* **Vehicle Color** (Text): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year Released** (Text): Enter the year the vehicle was released.
* **Vehicle Price** (Number): Enter the price of the vehicle (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Image URL** (Text): Enter a URL to an image of the vehicle (if applicable).
