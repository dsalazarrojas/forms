# Vehicle Design Style Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects opinions on various design aspects of vehicles to help stakeholders understand how different elements impact the overall perception and functionality of a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select all that apply for **vehicle style preferences** (page 1).
2. Type your overall impression of the exterior design (page 2).
3. Type your overall impression of the interior design (page 3).
4. Select your overall impression of the vehicle's design (page 4).
5. Select all that apply for the **color scheme** (page 5).
6. Choose a **trim level** for the vehicle (page 6).
7. Select the **body style** (page 7).
8. Select all that apply for the **wheel type** (page 8).
9. Choose a **transmission type** for the vehicle (page 9).
10. Select a **fuel type** for the vehicle (page 10).
11. Select all that apply for the **drivetrain type** (page 11).
12. Choose an **engine size** for the vehicle (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **vehicle style preferences** (select multiple)
Select all that apply:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the design style.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the design style.

### 2. **exterior design impressions** (text)
Type your overall impression of the exterior design.

### 3. **interior design impressions** (text)
Type your overall impression of the interior design.

### 4. **overall design impressions** (select one)
Select your overall impression:
	* **Yes**: You like the overall design.
	* **No**: You do not like the overall design.

### 5. **color scheme** (select multiple)
Select all that apply:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the color scheme.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the color scheme.

### 6. **trim level** (select one)
Choose a trim level:
	* High
	* Low

### 7. **body style** (select one)
Select the body style:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the body style.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the body style.

### 8. **wheel type** (select multiple)
Select all that apply:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the wheel type.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the wheel type.

### 9. **transmission type** (select one)
Choose a transmission type:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the transmission type.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the transmission type.

### 10. **fuel type** (select one)
Choose a fuel type:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the fuel type.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the fuel type.

### 11. **drivetrain type** (select multiple)
Select all that apply:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the drivetrain type.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the drivetrain type.

### 12. **engine size** (select one)
Choose an engine size:
	* **Yes**: You prefer the engine size.
	* **No**: You do not prefer the engine size.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide detailed feedback on each design aspect.
* Ensure you're providing unique and constructive comments for the design team.
* If unsure, use the space for comments at the end to elaborate on your feedback.
