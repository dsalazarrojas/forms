# Car Buying Decision Factors Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

<thinking>

This form is designed to help you decide on the key factors to consider when buying a new vehicle. It covers various aspects of your vehicle preferences, including personal and vehicle specifications, safety features, technology needs, and financial requirements. By filling out this form, you will be able to identify your priorities and make an informed decision when purchasing a car.

</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing your personal information, including your full name and email address.
2.  Select the type of vehicle you are looking for, such as sedan, SUV, truck, or electric/hybrid.
3.  Choose your maximum budget range for the vehicle.
4.  Specify how you plan to use the vehicle most often, such as for daily commute or family use.
5.  Indicate the most important factors to you when deciding on a vehicle, such as fuel efficiency, safety ratings, or performance/power.
6.  Rate the importance of safety features on a scale of 1 to 5.
7.  Select the key technology features you must have, such as Apple CarPlay or a backup camera.
8.  Specify the number of passengers you typically transport and the importance of cargo space to you.
9.  Indicate whether you plan to trade in a vehicle and how you intend to finance the purchase.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (2, text, required): Enter your first and last name for follow-up purposes.
*   **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
*   **Preferred Vehicle Type** (5, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle you are looking for, such as sedan, SUV, truck, or electric/hybrid.
*   **Budget Range** (6, select_one, required): Select your maximum budget for the vehicle.
*   **Primary Use for Vehicle** (7, select_one, required): Specify how you plan to use the vehicle most often.
*   **Most Important Decision Factors** (8, select_multiple, required): Select all the most important factors to you when deciding on a vehicle.
*   **Safety Features Importance** (9, number, required): Rate the importance of safety features on a scale of 1 to 5.
*   **Key Technology Features Needed** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select the key technology features you must have in the vehicle.
*   **Required Seating Capacity** (11, number, required): Specify the number of passengers you typically transport.
*   **Cargo Space Importance** (12, select_one, required): Select the importance of cargo space to you.
*   **Do you Plan to Trade in a Vehicle?** (13, select_one, required): Indicate whether you plan to trade in a vehicle.
*   **Preferred Financing Option** (14, select_one, required): Specify how you intend to finance the purchase of the vehicle.
*   **Any Additional Requirements or Comments?** (15, text, optional): Share any additional requirements or comments you have.
