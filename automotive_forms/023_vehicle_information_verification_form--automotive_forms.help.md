# Vehicle Information Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and verify information about a vehicle for various purposes such as insurance claims, vehicle sales, and rentals. It helps ensure accurate and efficient processing of the associated transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle registration number, which is a unique identifier assigned to the vehicle.
2. Enter the make of the vehicle, such as Toyota or Ford.
3. Provide the year of the vehicle, which can be found on the vehicle's ID plate or documentation.
4. Describe the color of the vehicle, including the exact color name or a color code.
5. Enter the model of the vehicle, such as a specific model number or name.
6. If applicable, enter the name of the vehicle's insurance provider.
7. Enter the owner's name and any other relevant details about the owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Registration Number** (Vehicle Registration Number, text, required): Enter the vehicle's unique registration number, which can be found on the vehicle's ID plate or documentation.
* **Vehicle Make** (Vehicle Make, text, not required): Enter the make of the vehicle, such as Toyota or Ford.
* **Vehicle Year** (Vehicle Year, number, not required): Enter the year of the vehicle, which can be found on the vehicle's ID plate or documentation.
* **Vehicle Color** (Vehicle Color, text, not required): Describe the color of the vehicle, including the exact color name or a color code.
* **Vehicle Model** (Vehicle Model, text, not required): Enter the model of the vehicle, such as a specific model number or name.
* **Vehicle Insurance Provider** (Vehicle Insurance Provider, text, not required): If applicable, enter the name of the vehicle's insurance provider.
* **Vehicle Owner** (Vehicle Owner, text, not required): Enter the owner's name and any other relevant details about the owner.
