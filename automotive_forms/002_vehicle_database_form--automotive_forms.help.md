# Vehicle Database Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about vehicles and their owners. The goal is to gather data that helps maintain a comprehensive vehicle database for various uses, such as inventory management, maintenance scheduling, or insurance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Vehicle Information" page to start filling out the form.
2.  Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year of manufacture.
3.  Select the vehicle's color from the available options (if any).
4.  Enter the vehicle's mileage.
5.  Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) for the vehicle.
6.  Fill out the "Owner Information" page.
7.  Enter the owner's name.
8.  Enter the owner's address.
9.  Enter the owner's phone number.
10.  Enter the owner's email address (if applicable).
11.  Add any additional notes about the owner or vehicle (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the vehicle to help identify it.
*   **Make** (`make`, text, required): Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, or Honda.
*   **Model** (`model`, text, required): Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Camry, Mustang, or Accord.
*   **Year** (`year`, number, required): Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
*   **Color** (`color`, text, optional): Select the color of the vehicle from the available options (if any).
*   **Mileage** (`mileage`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's mileage for reference.
*   **VIN** (`vin`, text, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) for the vehicle.
*   **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the owner to help identify them.
*   **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, text, required): Enter the owner's full name.
*   **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, required): Enter the owner's address.
*   **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
*   **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, text, optional): Enter the owner's email address (if applicable).
*   **Notes** (`owner_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the owner or vehicle (if necessary).
