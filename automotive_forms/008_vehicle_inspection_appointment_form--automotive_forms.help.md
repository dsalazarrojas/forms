# vehicle_inspection_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a vehicle inspection appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information in the "Client Information" field.
2. Select your preferred time for the inspection appointment in the "Preferred Time" field.
3. Provide your available time for the inspection appointment in the "Available Time" field.
4. Choose the service provider you would like to conduct the inspection from the "Service Provider" field.
5. Enter the details of the inspection appointment in the "Appointment Details" field.
6. Select the type of inspection you would like to schedule (e.g. Vehicle Inspection, Service, Repair) from the "Vehicle Inspection" field.
7. Choose the type of inspection you would like to conduct (e.g. Full Inspection, Quick Inspection, Express Inspection) from the "Vehicle Inspection Type" field.
8. Answer whether the vehicle is a "Yes" or "No" for having a specific feature in the "Vehicle Make" field.
9. Enter your vehicle's model in the "Vehicle Model" field.
10. Enter your vehicle's year in the "Vehicle Year" field.
11. Enter your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the "VIN" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: This field is where you should enter your name, contact information, and any relevant details about yourself as the client.
* **Preferred Time**: This field is where you should select your preferred time for the inspection appointment.
* **Available Time**: This field is where you should provide your available time for the inspection appointment.
* **Service Provider**: This field is where you should choose the service provider you would like to conduct the inspection.
* **Appointment Details**: This field is where you should enter any additional details about the inspection appointment.
* **Vehicle Inspection**: This field is where you should select the type of inspection you would like to schedule (e.g. Vehicle Inspection, Service, Repair).
* **Vehicle Inspection Type**: This field is where you should choose the type of inspection you would like to conduct (e.g. Full Inspection, Quick Inspection, Express Inspection).
* **Vehicle Make**: This field is where you should answer whether your vehicle has a specific feature or not (e.g. "Yes" or "No").
* **Vehicle Model**: This field is where you should enter your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year**: This field is where you should enter your vehicle's year.
* **VIN**: This field is where you should enter your Vehicle Identification Number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your inspection appointment is scheduled correctly.
* Double-check your preferred and available time to ensure that they match the service provider's schedule.
* If you have any special requests or requirements for the inspection, please enter them in the "Appointment Details" field.
