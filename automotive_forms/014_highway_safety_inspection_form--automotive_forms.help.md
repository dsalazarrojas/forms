# Highway Safety Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Highway Safety Inspection Form is used to collect data on the highway safety of vehicles. The form is used to identify potential safety risks and provide recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Vehicle Status** field: Choose whether the vehicle is "Active" or "Inactive".
2. Select the **Vehicle Condition** field: Choose all options that describe the vehicle's condition, such as "Yes" if the vehicle is in good condition, or select "No" if it is not.
3. Enter **Driver Info**: Enter any relevant information about the driver, such as their name and contact information.
4. Select the **Vehicle Year** field: Choose the year the vehicle was manufactured.
5. Enter the **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection.
6. Select the **Safety Risks Identified** field: Choose all options that describe any safety risks that were identified during the inspection.
7. Enter **Vehicle Weight**: Enter the weight of the vehicle in pounds.
8. Enter **Vehicle Color**: Enter the color of the vehicle.
9. Select the **Vehicle Make** field: Choose the make of the vehicle.
10. Select the **Vehicle Model** field: Choose the model of the vehicle.
11. Enter **Vehicle Registration**: Enter the vehicle's registration number.
12. Select the **Vehicle Year** field again: Choose the year the vehicle was last registered.
13. Select the **Safety Rating** field: Choose a rating for the vehicle's safety, such as "Good" or "Fair".
14. Enter **Safety Comments**: Enter any comments or notes about the vehicle's safety.
15. Select the **Safety Recommendations** field: Choose any recommended safety improvements for the vehicle.
