# Luxury Sports Car Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Luxury Sports Car Pre Order Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in purchasing a luxury sports car. This form is not intended for internal use and is meant to be filled out by potential customers who want to express their interest in a specific model.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields.
2. Select the options that best match your preferences.
3. If you have any additional comments or messages, use the "Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Please fill out your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Please fill out your last name.
* **Email** (email): Please enter a valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (text): Please enter your phone number.
* **Car Model** (select_one): Choose the luxury sports car model that interests you.
	+ Options: Audi, Ferrari, Lamborghini, Porsche, Tesla
* **Preferred Color** (select_multiple): Select the color(s) you prefer for your vehicle.
	+ Options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Black
* **Preferred Color 2** (select_multiple): Select the color(s) you prefer for your vehicle (if different from the above).
	+ Options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Black
* **Interior** (select_one): Choose the interior type you prefer.
	+ Options: Leather, Synthetic, Hybrid, Other
* **Exterior** (select_one): Choose the exterior type you prefer.
	+ Options: Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Black
* **Engine Type** (select_one): Choose the engine type you prefer.
	+ Options: Gasoline, Diesel, Electric
* **Transmission** (select_one): Choose the transmission type you prefer.
	+ Options: Automatic, Manual
* **MSRP** (number): Enter the MSRP (Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price) of the vehicle you are interested in.
* **Deposit** (number): Enter the deposit amount you are willing to pay.
* **Total Price** (number): Enter the total price you are willing to pay.
* **Is Ready to Drive** (select_one): Check if you are ready to drive the vehicle.
	+ Options: True, False
* **Is Insurance** (select_one): Check if you have insurance.
	+ Options: True, False
* **Is Fleet Owned** (select_one): Check if you own a fleet.
	+ Options: True, False
* **Date** (date): Enter the date you would like to take delivery of the vehicle.
* **Time** (time): Enter the time you would like to take delivery of the vehicle.
* **Message** (note): Enter any additional comments or messages you may have.
* **First Name Other** (text): Enter your first name if you have multiple names.
* **Last Name Other** (text): Enter your last name if you have multiple names.
* **Email Other** (email): Enter an email address for your other contact information.
* **Phone Other** (text): Enter your other phone number.
* **Preferred Color Other** (select_multiple): Select the color(s) you prefer for your vehicle (if different from the above).
	+ Options: Yes, No

Note: This form is not intended for actual sales or order processing purposes.
