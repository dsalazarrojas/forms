# Car Service Appointment Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to provide feedback on their car service experience, including their satisfaction with the service, staff professionalism, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out this form after completing your car service visit.
2. Answer the questions truthfully to help us improve our services.
3. Use the fields as described below for more guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your driver's license or identification card.
* **Email address** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you for follow-up.
* **Phone number** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you via phone.
* **Date of service visit** (`visit_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your service visit.
* **Type of service received** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service you received, such as oil change or brake service.
* **Was your service completed as promised?** (`service_completed`, select_one, required): Choose whether your service was completed as scheduled.
* **Service completed on scheduled date?** (`completion_timeline`, select_one, optional): Choose whether your service was completed on the expected date.
* **Final cost matched the estimate?** (`cost_expectations`, select_one, optional): Choose whether the final cost of your service was as estimated.
* **Satisfaction rating** (`satisfaction_rating`, select_one, required): Choose your overall satisfaction with the service from very satisfied to very dissatisfied.
* **Staff professionalism and courtesy** (`staff_professionalism`, select_one, required): Choose your assessment of the staff's professionalism and courtesy.
* **Quality of communication** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required): Choose your assessment of the quality of communication you received from our staff.
* **Waiting time for service** (`waiting_time`, select_one, optional): Choose how you felt about the wait time for your service.
* **Facility cleanliness and condition** (`facility_cleanliness`, select_one, optional): Choose your assessment of the cleanliness and condition of our facility.
* **Quality of work performed** (`quality_of_work`, select_one, required): Choose your assessment of the quality of work performed by our technicians.
* **What went well during your visit?** (`what_went_well`, text, optional): Provide specific details about what went well during your visit.
* **Areas for improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Provide specific details about areas that could be improved.
* **Name of technician who served you** (`technician_name`, text, optional): Provide the name of the technician who served you.
* **Would you return for future service?** (`return_likelihood`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would return to us for future service.
* **Would you recommend us to others?** (`recommend_likelihood`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend us to friends and family.
* **May we contact you with follow-up?** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted for follow-up.
* **Additional comments or suggestions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for improvement.
