# Vehicle Weight Rating Correction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Weight Rating Correction Form is used to correct the weight rating of a vehicle. This form is for making adjustments to the weight rating of a vehicle for which a correction is needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the relevant fields with the correct information for the vehicle in question.
* Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the 'Vehicle GVWR' field, indicating if the weight rating correction is being made for this vehicle.
* Enter the VIN (Vehicle Identification Number) of the vehicle being corrected.
* Enter the make of the vehicle being corrected.
* Enter the year of the vehicle being corrected.
* Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the 'Correction Reason' field, indicating if the correction is being made for this vehicle.
* Enter the amount of the correction.
* Enter any additional comments about the weight rating correction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle GVWR**: 
    * This field is for selecting whether or not the weight rating correction is being made.
    * Select 'Yes' if the correction is being made, and 'No' otherwise.
* **VIN**:
    * This field is for entering the Vehicle Identification Number of the vehicle.
* **Make**:
    * This field is for entering the make of the vehicle being corrected.
* **Year**:
    * This field is for entering the year of the vehicle being corrected.
* **Correction Reason**: 
    * This field is for selecting the reason for the weight rating correction.
    * Select 'Yes' if this is the reason for the correction, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Correction Amount**: 
    * This field is for entering the amount of the correction.
* **Vehicle Weight Rating Correction Form Comments**:
    * This field is for entering any additional comments about the weight rating correction.

## Tips
* Please double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter the correct Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) for the vehicle being corrected.
* If unsure about any of the fields, contact the relevant department or authority for further guidance.
* This form is intended for corrections only, do not use for new ratings.
