# Auto Insurance Online Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to enable policyholders to make online payments for their insurance policies. It's a fast and secure way to manage your insurance payments and maintain your policy.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill in all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review and confirm your payment details before submitting the form.
4. Submit the form to complete your payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required/true): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required/true): Enter your legal last name.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, required/true): Enter your insurance policy number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required/true): Enter your email address for receipt confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required/true): Enter your contact number for payment confirmation.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, required/true): Enter the amount you wish to pay.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, required/true): Select the type of payment you are making (Full, Partial, Deductible, Late, Reinstatement).
* **Billing Period** (`billing_period`, required/true): Select the period you are paying for (Current, Next, Previous, Quarterly, Semi-Annual, Annual).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, required/true): Choose how you would like to pay (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Account, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay).
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, optional/false): If paying by card, select your card type (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Not Applicable).
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, optional/true): If paying by card, enter the card number.
* **Cardholder Name** (`cardholder_name`, optional/true): Enter the name as it appears on the card.
* **Expiration Month** (`expiration_month`, optional/true): Enter the expiration month of the card.
* **Expiration Year** (`expiration_year`, optional/true): Enter the expiration year of the card.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, optional/true): Enter the 3-digit security code.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, optional/true): Enter the billing address associated with the payment method.
* **Billing ZIP Code** (`billing_zip`, optional/true): Enter the billing ZIP code.
* **Save Payment Method** (`save_payment_method`, required/true): Save the payment method for future payments.
* **Enable AutoPay** (`autopay_option`, required/true): Choose to enable automatic payments.
* **Email Receipt** (`email_receipt`, required/true): Choose to receive email confirmation of payment.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, optional/true): Enter any comments or notes about the payment.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, required/true): Agree to the terms and conditions of the payment.
* **Payment Authorization** (`authorization`, required/true): Authorize the payment to your payment method.

Please fill out the form carefully and review all fields before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your insurance provider.
