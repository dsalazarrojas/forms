# vehicle_safety_inspection_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document and store data about vehicle safety inspections. It is designed to ensure vehicles meet safety standards and reduce the risk of accidents and injuries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2. Ensure the vehicle's current ownership status is verified.
3. Enter the correct information for the vehicle year.
4. Enter the correct information for the vehicle make.
5. Enter the correct information for the vehicle model.
6. Select the date of the inspection.
7. Select the time of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **vehicle_year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the year of the vehicle to be inspected.
* **vehicle_make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the make of the vehicle to be inspected.
* **vehicle_model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required/optional): Enter the model of the vehicle to be inspected.
* **inspection_date** (`inspection_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the vehicle safety inspection.
* **inspection_time** (`inspection_time`, time, required/optional): Select the time of the vehicle safety inspection.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
* Use this form for vehicle safety inspections only.
* Do not create duplicate entries.
