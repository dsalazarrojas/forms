# Fleet Maintenance Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleet Maintenance Declaration Form is used by fleet administrators to record and document vehicle maintenance activities for their fleet of vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the vehicle information (Vehicle Info page) with the vehicle's details such as make, model, year, and registration number.
2. Provide a brief description of the vehicle's maintenance history (Maintenance History page).
3. Submit the form with a plan for future vehicle maintenance (Maintenance Plan page).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Info
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's details such as make, model, year, and registration number to identify the vehicle.

### Maintenance History
* **Maintenance History** (`maintenance_history`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the vehicle's maintenance history, including any previous repairs or services performed.

### Maintenance Plan
* **Maintenance Plan** (`maintenance_plan`, `text`, required): Submit a plan for future vehicle maintenance, including any scheduled services or repairs due.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are completing the form accurately and consistently to avoid errors or duplication of effort.
* Review and submit the form once for each vehicle to ensure that the data is accurate and up-to-date.
* This form is designed for one-time submission and should not be used for continuous or repetitive data collection.
