# Hybrid Battery Maintenance Cost Estimate Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to estimate the cost of hybrid battery maintenance. Fill out this form to get an initial quote for maintenance or repair work on your hybrid vehicle's battery.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please provide basic vehicle information on the first page.
* Choose whether or not your vehicle has a hybrid battery, and what type of maintenance it needs.
* Enter your vehicle's mileage to give us a better understanding of your vehicle's condition.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to add them in the "Notes" section.
* Make sure to enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you to discuss your estimate.
* Please review your form to ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, required: false): This is the first page of the form where you can enter your vehicle's basic information. This includes details such as vehicle make, model, and year.
* **Battery Type** (`battery_type`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to indicate whether or not your vehicle has a hybrid battery. If you select "Yes", it will help us tailor the estimate to your vehicle's specific needs.
* **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to choose the type of maintenance you are requesting. Options include Oil Change, Service, Inspect, and Repair.
* **Mileage** (`mileage`, number, required: false): Please enter your vehicle's current mileage to help us provide a more accurate estimate.
* **Estimated Cost** (`estimated_cost`, number, required: false): This field is not currently required, but feel free to enter any previous estimate or quote you may have received for this maintenance work.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you to discuss your estimate.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you with your estimated cost and any follow-up questions or instructions.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any last-minute questions or to confirm your appointment.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please feel free to add them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): This field is not currently used. Please leave blank.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: true): This is your unique form ID. Please enter this value to identify your form submission.
* **Form Version** (`form_version`, number, required: false): This field is not currently used. Please leave blank.

## Tips

* Make sure to check your estimate carefully before submitting the form to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're here to help.
* Please review our estimated cost and let us know if you have any further questions or need clarification on any charges.
