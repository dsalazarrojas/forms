# Vehicle Air Intake Boot Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vehicle Air Intake Boot Order Form" is designed to collect relevant information about vehicles for maintenance or repair services. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that the correct details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by selecting the vehicle make from the options provided in the "Vehicle Make" field.
2. Enter the vehicle model in the "Vehicle Model" field.
3. If applicable, select the engine size from the options in the "Engine Size" field.
4. Choose the transmission type from the options in the "Transmission Type" field.
5. Select the type of air filter needed from the options in the "Air Filter Type" field.
6. Choose the order date for the air intake from the "Order Date" field.
7. Select the order time for the service from the "Order Time" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the vehicle year from the options in the "Vehicle Year" field.
10. Enter the order total in the "Order Total" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Make (Select One, required):** Select the make of your vehicle from the given options. This will help identify the correct vehicle model and ensure the correct maintenance or repair.
* **Vehicle Model (Text, optional):** Enter your vehicle's model number for accurate identification purposes.
* **Engine Size (Number, optional):** If your vehicle has a larger or smaller engine size, select the correct option. This information will help our staff to prepare for the service.
* **Transmission Type (Select One, optional):** Choose the type of transmission you need help with. This will aid our technicians in the service process.
* **Air Filter Type (Select Multiple, optional):** Select all the types of air filters needed for your vehicle.
* **Order Date (Date, optional):** Select the date you wish to schedule the service. Our staff will schedule the appointment according to your chosen date.
* **Order Time (Time, optional):** Choose the time slot you prefer for the service. This will help us allocate our resources efficiently.
* **Notes (Note, optional):** Add any additional comments or notes about your vehicle or service needs. This will help our technicians understand your requirements better.
* **Vehicle Year (Number, optional):** Enter the year of your vehicle for accurate identification purposes.
* **Order Total (Number, optional):** Enter the total cost for the service. This will help us prepare for the payment process.
* **Tips:** Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
