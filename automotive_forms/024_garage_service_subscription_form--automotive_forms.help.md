# Garage Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garage Service Subscription Form is designed to collect information from customers who wish to subscribe to your garage services. This form will help us understand their vehicle details, contact information, and subscription preferences. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure you receive the right services for your vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter your vehicle year of manufacture in the "Vehicle Year" field.
3. Select whether your vehicle is "Yes" or "No" for "Vehicle Make".
4. Enter your vehicle model in the "Vehicle Model" field.
5. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
7. Select your preferred subscription frequency from the options provided in the "Subscription Frequency" field.
8. Select whether you need the service for your vehicle from the options provided in the "Service Type" field.
9. Enter the service schedule date in the "Service Schedule" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Vehicle Year (Vehicle Year, number, required: false)**: Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make (vehicle_make, select_one, required: false)**: Select "Yes" if you want to subscribe to garage services for your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (vehicle_model, text, required: false)**: Enter your vehicle's model number.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Subscription Frequency (Subscription Frequency, select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose how often you want to receive garage services for your vehicle (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Service Type (Service Type, select_one, required: false)**: Select the type of service you need for your vehicle.
* **Service Schedule (Service Schedule, date, required: false)**: If you've selected "Yes" for any service type, enter the date for the scheduled service.

Note: Fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be left blank if not applicable. Please make sure to fill out all applicable fields accurately to help us provide the best service for your vehicle. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information so we can reach you for any updates or services.
* If you have any special requirements or requests, please let us know in the "Additional Comments" field (not shown in this form).
* We'll review your subscription and provide you with personalized services accordingly.
