# vehicle_repair_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle repair form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect and document vehicle repair information. It is intended for use by mechanics, service managers, and other automotive professionals to ensure accurate and complete repair reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to understand the purpose of the corresponding section.
2. Fill out the fields as instructed, and don't hesitate to ask if you are unsure about any field.
3. Make sure to review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Page 1: Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_1`, text, required):
	* This page is for capturing the basic vehicle information, such as make, model, and year.
- **Page 2: Problem Description** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_2`, text, required):
	* Describe the problem with the vehicle, including symptoms and any error messages.
- **Page 3: Repair Work Done** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_3`, text, required):
	* Document the work done to repair the vehicle, including parts replaced and labor hours spent.
- **Page 4: Labor Hours** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_4`, text, required):
	* Record the total labor hours spent on the repair.
- **Page 5: Parts Installed** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_5`, text, required):
	* List and describe the parts installed or replaced during the repair.
- **Page 6: Additional Information** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_6`, text, optional):
	* Add any additional information relevant to the repair, such as notes or observations.
- **Page 7: Repair Summary** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_7`, text, required):
	* Summarize the repair work done, including the problem addressed and the outcome.
- **Page 8: Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_repair_form_page_8`, text, required):
