# electric_vehicle_maintenance_program_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from applicants for the electric vehicle maintenance program. The form is meant to collect relevant details about the applicant, their contact and professional information, vehicle specifics, and any maintenance certifications or work experience they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Applicant Information** on page 1. Enter your name, and any other relevant details.
2. On page 2, enter your **Contact Information**. This should include any relevant contact details, such as phone number or email.
3. On page 3, provide your **Professional Experience**. Describe your relevant work experience, especially in the electric vehicle maintenance industry.
4. On page 4, provide **Vehicle Information**. Describe your vehicle and its electric components, including any relevant details about the model or make.
5. On page 5, fill out **Electric Vehicle Details**. Provide any additional information about your electric vehicle, such as its battery type or charging capabilities.
6. If applicable, list your **Maintenance Certifications** on page 6. Choose from the options provided, or type "Other" if your certification is not listed.
7. On page 7, select your **Work Experience**. Choose from the options provided, or type "Other" if your experience is not listed.
8. Finally, on page 8, select your **Education**. Choose from the options provided, or type "Other" if your education level is not listed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Information (Page 1)**: Enter your name as the primary applicant.
* **Contact Information (Page 2)**: Enter any relevant contact details such as email or phone number.
* **Professional Experience (Page 3)**: Describe relevant work experience, especially in the electric vehicle maintenance field.
* **Vehicle Information (Page 4)**: Describe your vehicle, including its model, make, or any other relevant details.
* **Electric Vehicle Details (Page 5)**: Provide any additional information about your electric vehicle.
* **Maintenance Certifications (Page 6)**: Select any maintenance certifications you hold, or type "Other" if your certification is not listed.
* **Work Experience (Page 7)**: Select your level of work experience in the electric vehicle maintenance industry, or type "Other" if your experience is not listed.
* **Education (Page 8)**: Select your level of education, or type "Other" if your education level is not listed.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form, especially in areas that are required.
- If unsure about any section, refer to the provided options.
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
