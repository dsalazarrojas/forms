# RV Service Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RV Service Check In Form is designed to collect information from RV owners when they arrive at the dealership for service. This form gathers essential details to ensure efficient and personalized service.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your owner details in the "Owner Details" field.
2. Provide information about your vehicle in the "Vehicle Info" field.
3. Specify any service requests you may have in the "Service Requests" field.
4. Select your preferred check-in time from the "Preferred Checkin Time" field.
5. If you have any additional comments or instructions for the service team, enter them in the "Owner Notes" field.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details** (`Owner Details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information as the RV owner.
* **Vehicle Info** (`Vehicle Info`, text, required: false): Please provide the make, model, and any other relevant details about your vehicle.
* **Service Requests** (`Service Requests`, text, required: false): Describe the services you would like to have performed on your vehicle, such as oil change, tire rotation, etc.
* **Preferred Checkin Time** (`Preferred Checkin Time`, number, required: false): Select a number representing your preferred check-in time, which will help us to schedule your service efficiently.
* **Owner Notes** (`Owner Notes`, note, required: false): If you have any special requests or additional comments for the service team, please enter them here.
