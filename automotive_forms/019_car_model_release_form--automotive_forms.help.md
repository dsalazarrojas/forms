# Car Model Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by vehicle owners, photographers, or representatives to release and grant permission for the use of images of a vehicle. The form is used to document the terms and conditions of this usage, including the intended use, duration, and any restrictions or requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are authorized to submit this form, either as the vehicle owner or as an authorized representative.
2. Review and understand the terms and conditions outlined in the form.
3. Answer all required fields to provide detailed information about the vehicle and the usage of the images.
4. Verify that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully to ensure correct documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner Information**: Provide your contact information and details about yourself, the vehicle owner.
	+ First Name: Enter your first name.
	+ Last Name: Enter your last name.
	+ Email Address: Enter your email address for communications.
	+ Phone Number: Enter your contact phone number.
	+ Street Address: Enter your street address.
	+ City: Enter the city where you reside.
	+ State: Enter the state where you reside.
	+ ZIP Code: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Vehicle Information**: This section is optional and provides additional details about the vehicle, if desired.
* **Vehicle Year, Make, Model, Color, VIN, License Plate, Distinguishing Features**: Enter accurate and up-to-date information about the vehicle.
* **Release Details**: This section is optional and provides information about the usage permissions.
* **Photographer/Requester Name, Company**: Enter the name and company of the photographer or requester.
* **Intended Use, Usage Duration, Usage Territory**: Select the intended use of the images, the duration of the usage, and the territory where the images will be used.
* **Compensation, Compensation Amount**: Select whether you will be compensated and enter the amount, if applicable.
* **Exclusive Rights, Image Modifications, Attribution Required**: Select whether you grant exclusive rights, allow image modifications, and require attribution.
* **Shoot Details**: This section is optional and provides information about the photo session.
* **Shoot Date, Shoot Location, Shoot Time, Vehicle Condition at Shoot**: Enter accurate information about the photo session.
* **Additional Vehicles**: Select whether other vehicles were included in the shoot.
* **Additional Vehicle Details**: Enter information about the other vehicles, if applicable.
* **Usage Restrictions, Restriction Details**: Select whether there are any restrictions and provide a description if needed.
* **Legal Agreement, Ownership Confirmation, Authorization Documentation, Release Agreement, Owner Signature Confirmation, Witness Name, Release Date**: Review, agree, confirm, and sign the terms and conditions. If not the owner, you are also required to confirm authorization.
* **Additional Documentation**: If applicable, attach any additional documentation, such as a letter of authorization or a release agreement.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Attach any required documentation, if applicable.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
