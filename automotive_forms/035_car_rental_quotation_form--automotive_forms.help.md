# Car Rental Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Rental Quotation Form is designed to gather information from customers about their car rental requirements, allowing the rental company to create an accurate quote for the customer's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the customer information page (pages 1-6) with your contact details (first and last name, email, phone number) and any additional information (company name, address).
2. Go to the rental details page (pages 7-15) and fill out the required fields:
   * Pickup location and date
   * Return location and date
   * Vehicle preferences (type, transmission, fuel type, passenger capacity)
   * Additional services (GPS navigation, child safety seats)
   * Driver information (age, driver experience)
   * Special requests (any additional information)
3. Choose how to receive the quote (email, phone call, text message, fax)
4. Select the quote validity period (24 hours, 48 hours, 1 week, 2 weeks)
5. Provide any additional comments or information

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (2, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (3, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter your email address for quote delivery.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Company Name** (6, text, optional): If you are a business customer, enter your company name.
* **Street Address** (7, text, optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (8, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (9, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (10, text, optional): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Rental Details** (11, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your rental needs.
* **Pickup Location** (12, select_one, required): Choose where you want to pick up the vehicle:
	+ Airport Location
	+ Downtown Location
	+ Suburban Location
	+ Hotel Delivery
	+ Other
* **Pickup Address** (13, text, optional): Enter a specific address for pickup.
* **Pickup Date** (14, date, required): Choose the date you want to pick up the vehicle.
* **Pickup Time** (15, time, required): Choose the time you want to pick up the vehicle.
* **Return Location** (16, select_one, required): Choose where you want to return the vehicle:
	+ Same as pickup
	+ Different location
* **Return Address** (17, text, optional): Enter a specific address for return.
* **Return Date** (18, date, required): Choose the date you want to return the vehicle.
* **Return Time** (19, time, required): Choose the time you want to return the vehicle.
* **Rental Duration** (20, text, optional): This field is automatically calculated based on the pickup and return dates and times.
* **Vehicle Preferences** (21, note, optional): Enter any specific vehicle preferences.
* **Vehicle Category** (22, select_one, required): Choose the class of vehicle you need:
	+ Economy
	+ Compact
	+ Mid-size
	+ Full-size
	+ SUV
	+ Minivan
	+ Luxury
	+ Sports car
	+ Truck
	+ No preference
* **Specific Vehicle Request** (23, text, optional): If you have any specific vehicle requests, enter them here.
* **Transmission Type** (24, select_one, required): Choose your transmission type preference:
	+ Automatic
	+ Manual
	+ No preference
* **Fuel Type** (25, select_one, required): Choose your fuel type preference:
	+ Gasoline
	+ Diesel
	+ Hybrid
	+ Electric
	+ No preference
* **Passenger Capacity** (26, select_one, required): Choose the number of passengers you need to transport:
	+ 1-2
	+ 3-4
	+ 5-6
	+ 7-8
	+ 9+
* **Luggage Capacity** (27, select_one, required): Choose the number of bags you need to transport:
	+ 1-2 bags
	+ 3-4 bags
	+ 5-6 bags
	+ 7+ bags
* **Additional Services** (28, select_multiple, optional): Choose any additional services you need:
	+ GPS Navigation
	+ Child Safety Seat
	+ Booster Seat
	+ Additional Driver
	+ Ski Rack
	+ Bike Rack
	+ Toll Pass
	+ Mobile WiFi
	+ Car Seat for Infant
	+ None
* **Insurance Preferences** (29, select_multiple, optional): Choose your insurance preferences:
	+ Collision Damage Waiver
	+ Liability Insurance
	+ Personal Accident Insurance
	+ Personal Effects Coverage
	+ Roadside Assistance Plus
	+ Decline all coverage
	+ Undecided
* **Estimated Mileage** (30, select_one, optional): Choose the estimated mileage you expect:
	+ Under 100 miles
	+ 100-300 miles
	+ 300-500 miles
	+ Over 500 miles
* **Driver Information** (31, note, optional): Enter any additional driver information.
* **Primary Driver Age** (32, select_one, required): Choose the age range of the primary driver:
	+ 21-24
	+ 25-64
	+ 65-74
	+ 75+
* **Additional Drivers** (33, select_one, required): Choose if there will be other drivers:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Number of Additional Drivers** (34, select_one, optional): Choose the number of additional drivers:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3
	+ 4+
* **Rental Purpose** (35, select_one, required): Choose why you are renting:
	+ Business
	+ Leisure/Vacation
	+ Personal vehicle repair
	+ Special event
	+ Test drive
	+ Other
* **Corporate Rate** (36, select_one, optional): Choose if you qualify for corporate rates:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Corporate ID** (37, text, optional): Enter your corporate account number.
* **Loyalty Program** (38, select_one, optional): Choose if you are a loyalty program member:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Want to join
* **Loyalty Number** (39, text, optional): Enter your loyalty program number.
* **Special Requests** (40, text, optional): Enter any additional special requests.
* **Quote Delivery** (41, select_one, required): Choose how you want to receive the quote:
	+ Email
	+ Phone call
	+ Text message
	+ Fax
* **Quote Validity** (42, select_one, optional): Choose the quote validity period:
	+ 24 hours
	+ 48 hours
	+ 1 week
	+ 2 weeks
* **Decision Timeframe** (43, select_one, optional): Choose when you want to make your decision:
	+ Immediately
	+ Within 24 hours
	+ Within 1 week
	+ Still shopping
	+ Just browsing
* **Competing Quotes** (44, select_one, optional): Choose if you have other quotes:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Prefer not to say
* **Budget Range** (45, select_one, optional): Choose your budget range:
	+ Under $50/day
	+ $50-$100/day
	+ $100-$150/day
	+ $150-$200/day
	+ Over $200/day
	+ No budget limit
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (46, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the rental company:
	+ Website
	+ Mobile app
	+ Search engine
	+ Social media
	+ Friend/Family
	+ Travel agent
	+ Advertisement
	+ Previous customer
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments** (47, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Quote Request Date** (48, date, required): This field is automatically filled in with the current date.

Note: This guide assumes that the form is divided into two pages. If the form is not divided into two pages, the page numbers will not match with the field labels.
