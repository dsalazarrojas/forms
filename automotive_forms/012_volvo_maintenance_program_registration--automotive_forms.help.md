# volvo_maintenance_program_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Volvo Maintenance Program registration form is designed to allow customers to register their vehicles and select their preferred service center for maintenance and repair services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Vehicle Type" field and select all relevant options that apply to your vehicle from the dropdown list:
	* Volvo
	* SUV
	* Car
	* Truck
2. Enter your Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) in the "VIN" field, if you are aware of it. This will help us better understand your vehicle.
3. Select the year of your vehicle in the "Year" field.
4. Choose your preferred service center from the "Service Center" dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of vehicle you own, such as Volvo, SUV, Car, or Truck.
* **VIN** (`vin`, text, required): Enter your Vehicle Identification Number if you are aware of it. This will help us better understand your vehicle.
* **Year** (`year`, number, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Service Center** (`service_center`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred service center for maintenance and repair services.

## Tips
* Ensure that you select all relevant vehicle types that apply to your vehicle.
* If you are unsure about your vehicle's VIN, please contact us and we will assist you in finding it.
* Please note that not providing a VIN may result in incomplete or inaccurate information about your vehicle.
