# vintage-car-exhibit-reservation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about participants' vintage cars and their contact details for an event or exhibit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields:
    - Event ID: Enter the unique identifier for the event.
    - Participant Name: Enter your name.
    - Vehicle Year: Enter the year of your vintage car.
    - Vehicle Make: Select the make of your vehicle from the list (e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, etc.).
    - Vehicle Model: Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g., Mustang, Camaro, etc.).
    - Event Date: Enter the date of the event.
    - Event Start Time: Enter the start time of the event.
    - Event End Time: Enter the end time of the event.
    - Event Location: Enter the location of the event.
    - Participant Notes: Enter any additional notes about yourself.
2. Fill out the optional fields if you wish:
    - Participant Contact Info: Enter any additional contact information for yourself.
    - Participant Email: Enter your email address.
    - Participant Phone: Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID (1)**: Enter the unique identifier for the event.
* **Participant Name (2)**: Enter your name.
* **Vehicle Year (3)**: Enter the year of your vintage car.
* **Vehicle Make (4)**: Select the make of your vehicle from the list (e.g., Ford, Chevrolet, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model (5)**: Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g., Mustang, Camaro, etc.).
* **Participant Contact Info (6)**: Enter any additional contact information for yourself.
* **Participant Email (7)**: Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone (8)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date (9)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time (10)**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time (11)**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Location (12)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Participant Notes (13)**: Enter any additional notes about yourself.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Use the correct format for dates and times (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD for dates, HH:MM:SS for times).
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers.
