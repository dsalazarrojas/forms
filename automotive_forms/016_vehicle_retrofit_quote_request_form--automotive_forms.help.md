# Vehicle Retrofit Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Vehicle Retrofit Quote Request Form is used to gather information from customers interested in retrofitting their vehicles with specific modifications or features. This form will be used by the sales team to provide a quote to the customer based on their requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Vehicle Retrofits" dropdown menu and select "Yes" if you are interested in retrofitting your vehicle.
2. Select the type of vehicle you own by choosing from the available options.
3. Enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle in the "Vehicle Make Model Year" field.
4. Enter the trim level of your vehicle in the "Vehicle Trim" field.
5. Enter the current mileage of your vehicle in the "Vehicle Km" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for each of the following:
	* Engine Type
	* Transmission
	* Drive Type
7. Upload any relevant attachments, such as photographs or specifications, in the "Attachment 1" and "Attachment 2" fields.
8. Enter your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields.
9. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
10. Select your contact preferences for future communication.
11. Enter any additional message or notes in the "Customer Message" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Retrofits** (select_multiple): 
    Select "Yes" if you are interested in retrofitting your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (select_one): Choose the type of vehicle you own.
* **Vehicle Make Model Year** (text): Enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Trim** (text): Enter the trim level of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Km** (number): Enter the current mileage of your vehicle.
* **Engine Type** (select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" if you have a specific engine type.
* **Transmission** (select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" if you have a specific transmission type.
* **Drive Type** (select_one): Select "Yes" or "No" if you have a specific drive type.
* **Attachment 1** (text): Upload any relevant attachments, such as photographs or specifications.
* **Attachment 2** (text): Upload any additional attachments.
* **Customer Name** (text): Enter your name as the customer.
* **Customer Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Preferences** (select_multiple): Select how you would like to be contacted.
* **Customer Message** (text): Enter any additional message or notes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to get the best possible quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Our sales team will review your submission and provide a quote based on your requirements.
