# Vehicle Inspection Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Inspection Declaration Form is used to document the results of a vehicle inspection, including any issues found and recommendations for maintenance or repair.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture in the "Vehicle Year" field.
2. Enter the vehicle's make in the "Vehicle Make" field.
3. Enter the vehicle's model in the "Vehicle Model" field.
4. Enter the date of the vehicle inspection in the "Vehicle Inspection Date" field.
5. Select any relevant vehicle inspection items that were identified during the inspection.
6. Enter any recommendations or comments in the "Vehicle Inspection Recommendations" field.
7. Select any relevant vehicle parts that were inspected or noted during the inspection.
8. Enter the cost of any repairs or maintenance recommended.
9. Enter the date the vehicle inspection was completed.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured. 
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle, e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date**: Enter the date the vehicle inspection was conducted.
* **Vehicle Inspection Item 1**: Select any relevant vehicle inspection items that were identified during the inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Item 2**: Select any additional relevant vehicle inspection items.
* **Vehicle Inspection Item 3**: Select any other relevant vehicle inspection items.
* **Vehicle Inspection Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the vehicle inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Recommendations**: Enter any recommendations or comments for maintenance or repairs.
* **Relevant Vehicle Parts**: Select any vehicle parts that were inspected or noted during the inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Cost**: Enter the cost of any repairs or maintenance recommended.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date Completed**: Enter the date the vehicle inspection was completed.
