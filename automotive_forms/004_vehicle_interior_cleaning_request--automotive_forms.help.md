# Vehicle Interior Cleaning Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Vehicle Interior Cleaning Request" form is designed to gather information from customers for a vehicle interior cleaning service. This form will help you communicate your needs and preferences for the service.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle you own (Vehicle, SUV, or Truck).
2. Choose the services you require (Interior Cleaning, Exterior Cleaning, or Interior Detailing).
3. Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or In-person).
4. Enter your contact information (phone number and email).
5. Describe your vehicle and any additional comments or concerns.
6. Choose the frequency of the cleaning service (Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
7. Select a convenient service location (Local or Mobile).
8. Optionally, select a specific date and time for the service.
9. Confirm the next available date and time for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle you own (Vehicle, SUV, or Truck).
* **Service Preferences** (`service_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose the services you require (Interior Cleaning, Exterior Cleaning, or Interior Detailing).
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date for the appointment (leave blank for a future date).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time for the appointment (leave blank for a default time).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time for the appointment (leave blank for a default time).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Vehicle Description** (`vehicle_description`, text, optional): Describe your vehicle.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or concerns.
* **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or In-person).
* **Preferred Contact Info** (`preferred_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your preferred contact information.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, select_one, optional): Select a convenient service location (Local or Mobile).
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): Enter a specific time for the service (leave blank for default time).
* **Next Available Date** (`next_available_date`, date, optional): Confirm the next available date for the service.
* **Service Frequency** (`service_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of the cleaning service (Weekly, Bi-weekly, or Monthly).
* **Service Notes** (`service_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional service notes or instructions.
