# Vehicle Insurance Excess Refund Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the vehicle insurance excess refund process. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure the efficient processing of your claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure all fields are filled in completely before submitting the form.
3. Review your information for accuracy before pressing the 'Submit' button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Insured Name** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_insured_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of the insured who is making the claim.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_vehicle_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of vehicle that was involved in the claim (e.g. Vehicle, Motorhome, Motorbike, Van).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of the vehicle involved in the claim.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Please enter the make of the vehicle involved in the claim (e.g. Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the vehicle involved in the claim (e.g. Corolla, Ranger, Civic).
* **Insured Date of Birth** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_insured_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter the date of birth of the insured who is making the claim.
* **Insured Address** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_insured_address`, `text`, required): Enter the address of the insured who is making the claim.
* **Vehicle Reg Number** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_vehicle_reg_number`, `text`, required): Please enter the registration number of the vehicle involved in the claim.
* **Vehicle Reg Date** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_vehicle_reg_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of registration of the vehicle involved in the claim.
* **Claim Date** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_claim_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the claim incident.
* **Claim Description** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_claim_description`, `text`, required): Please describe the circumstances surrounding the claim.
* **Claim Details** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_claim_details`, `text`, required): Provide any additional details about the claim.
* **Claim Amount** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_claim_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Insurer Contact** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_insurer_contact`, `text`, required): Please enter your contact details for the insurer.
* **Insurer E-mail** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_insurer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address of the insurer.
* **Insurer Phone** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_insurer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the insurer.
* **Refund Status** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_refund_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of your refund (e.g. Pending, Approved, Rejected).
* **Refund Reason** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_refund_reason`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason for the refund (e.g. Refund Due to Excess Excess, Refund Due to Other).
* **Additional Info** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_additional_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information you deem relevant to your claim.
* **Submit** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_submit`, `text`, required): Press this button to submit your claim.
* **Back** (`vehicle_insurance_excess_refund_form_back_button`, `text`, required): Press this button to return to the previous page.
