# Catalytic Converter Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about catalytic converters submitted for recycling or sale. It helps the processing team evaluate and process your submission accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your details.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you need help with any field, click on the hint for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Full Name** (1, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company Name** (2, optional): If you represent a business, enter your company name here.
* **Email Address** (3, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (4, required): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Business Address** (5, required): Enter your business address for processing and evaluation.
* **Submission Date** (6, required): Enter the date you are submitting the catalytic converters.
* **Converter Type** (7, required): Select the type of catalytic converter you are submitting.
* **Manufacturer** (8, optional): If available, enter the brand or manufacturer of the converter.
* **Serial Number** (9, required): Enter the EPA or manufacturer serial number.
* **Part Number** (10, optional): If available, enter the part number of the converter.
* **Vehicle Make** (11, optional): If relevant, enter the vehicle make.
* **Vehicle Model** (12, optional): If relevant, enter the vehicle model.
* **Vehicle Year** (13, optional): If relevant, enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Quantity** (14, required): Enter the number of converters you are submitting.
* **Total Weight** (15, optional): If known, enter the total weight of the converters.
* **Condition** (16, required): Select the physical condition of the converters.
* **Photos Attached** (17, required): Select if you have included photos with your submission.
* **Number of Photos** (18, optional): If photos are attached, enter the number of photos.
* **Origin Verified** (19, required): Select if you can verify the origin of the converters.
* **Documentation Provided** (20, optional): Select the documents included with your submission.
* **Pickup or Delivery Preference** (21, required): Select your preferred method of transfer.
* **Expected Price Range** (22, optional): If known, enter your expected price range per unit.
* **Additional Notes** (23, optional): Add any additional information or notes about your submission.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to help the processing team evaluate and process your submission efficiently.
