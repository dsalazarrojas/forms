# Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about vehicle accidents, allowing users to report incidents and provide relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting your response.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately based on the incident you experienced or witnessed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Weather** (`weather`, `text`, required): Describe the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if the accident occurred on a public or private road.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of what happened during the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Injuries** (`injuries`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if any injuries were sustained during the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the type of vehicle involved in the accident (e.g. car, truck, motorcycle, etc.).
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of the vehicle involved in the accident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate the color of the vehicle involved in the accident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, `text`, required): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle involved in the accident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, `text`, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Vehicle Mileage** (`vehicle_mileage`, `number`, optional): Enter the mileage of the vehicle involved in the accident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Road Condition** (`road_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the road conditions at the time of the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Weather Condition** (`weather_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Traffic Condition** (`traffic_condition`, `text`, optional): Describe the traffic conditions at the time of the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Other Factors** (`other_factors`, `text`, optional): Provide any other relevant factors that contributed to the incident.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Vehicle Accident Image** (`vehicle_accident_image`, `text`, required): Attach a photo of the accident scene.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Roadside Image** (`roadside_image`, `text`, required): Attach a photo of the road where the accident occurred.
* **Vehicle Accident Incident Form - Other Images** (`other_images`, `text`, required): Attach any other photos related to the incident.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting your response.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or your supervisor for guidance.
