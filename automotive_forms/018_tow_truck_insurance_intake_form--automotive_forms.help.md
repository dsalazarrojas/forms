# Tow Truck Insurance Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tow Truck Insurance Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from tow truck operators about their vehicles and insurance providers. This form helps us understand their insurance coverage and ensures that we can provide you with accurate information about your insurance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information (Company Name, Phone Number, and Email).
2. Provide details about your vehicle (Vehicle Year, Vehicle Make, Vehicle Model, and Vehicle License Plate).
3. Enter your tow truck registration details (Tow Truck Registration).
4. List your vehicle's insurance provider (Vehicle Insurance Provider).
5. Enter your insurance provider's contact information (Insurance Provider, Insurance Provider Address, Insurance Provider Phone, and Insurance Provider Email).
6. Enter your insurance provider's website (Insurance Provider Website).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): This is the name of your company that owns the tow truck. Enter your company's name to identify your business.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): This is the phone number of your company where you can be reached.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): This is the email address of your company where you can be contacted.
* **Vehicle Year** (Vehicle Year, number, optional): This is the year your vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make** (Vehicle Make, text, optional): This is the make of your vehicle (e.g., Ford, Toyota, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (Vehicle Model, text, optional): This is the model of your vehicle (e.g., Mustang, Camry, etc.).
* **Vehicle License Plate** (Vehicle License Plate, text, optional): This is the plate number of your vehicle.
* **Tow Truck Registration** (Tow Truck Registration, text, optional): This is the registration information of your tow truck.
* **Vehicle Insurance Provider** (Vehicle Insurance Provider, text, optional): This is the provider of your vehicle's insurance (e.g., Geico, Allstate, etc.).
* **Insurance Provider** (Insurance Provider, text, optional): This is the name of your insurance provider (e.g., Geico, Allstate, etc.).
* **Insurance Provider Address** (Insurance Provider Address, text, optional): This is the address of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Phone** (Insurance Provider Phone, text, optional): This is the phone number of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Email** (Insurance Provider Email, email, optional): This is the email address of your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Provider Website** (Insurance Provider Website, text, optional): This is the website of your insurance provider.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
* Please note that some fields may be required for insurance purposes, and it's essential to fill them out correctly.
