# Roadside Assistance Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from customers for road assistance services. It is designed to help the service provider understand the customer's needs and provide the best possible assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name field with your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email address field. Make sure it is in the correct format (e.g., [username]@domain.com).
4. Enter your phone number in the phone number field. If you don't have a phone, you can leave this field blank.
5. Enter the city or state where you need assistance in the location field. If you are unsure, you can search for "city, state" in the field.
6. Enter the year of your vehicle in the vehicle year field.
7. Enter the make of your vehicle in the vehicle make field.
8. Enter the model of your vehicle in the vehicle model field.
9. Enter the color of your vehicle in the vehicle color field.
10. Choose the type of assistance you need from the assistance type field. Select one option only.
11. Select the locations where assistance was provided from the assistance location field. Select all applicable locations.
12. Add any additional comments in the additional comments field.
13. If you have a specific date for the assistance, enter it in the date of assistance field.
14. If you have a specific time for the assistance, enter it in the time of assistance field.
15. If you have a specific date and time for the assistance, enter it in the date and time of assistance field. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Location** (text, required): Enter the city or state where you need assistance.
* **Vehicle year** (number, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle make** (text, required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle model** (text, required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle color** (text, required): Enter the color of your vehicle.
* **Assistance type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of assistance you need.
* **Assistance location** (select_multiple, required): Select the locations where assistance was provided.
* **Additional comments** (note, required): Add any additional comments about the assistance.
* **Date of assistance** (date, required): Enter the specific date of assistance.
* **Time of assistance** (time, required): Enter the specific time of assistance.
* **Date and time of assistance** (text, required): Enter the specific date and time of assistance.
* **Type of assistance 2** (select_one, required): Choose the type of assistance you need again.
* **Location 2** (text, required): Enter the city or state where you need assistance.
* **Phone 2** (text, required): Enter your phone number again.
* **Assistance type** (select_one, required): This field is redundant, please do not fill it out.
* **Location** (text, required): This field is redundant, please do not fill it out.
* **Phone** (text, required): This field is redundant, please do not fill it out.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out only once. If you have already provided this information, please do not fill it out again. 

Please contact us if you have any questions or concerns about this form.
