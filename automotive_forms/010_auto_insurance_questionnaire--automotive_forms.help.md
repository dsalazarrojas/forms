# Auto Insurance Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Insurance Questionnaire form is designed to collect information from users to provide them with accurate quotes for auto insurance. Please answer the questions below to help us provide you with the best possible quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each page carefully and enter your information accurately.
2.  Enter your contact information in the Personal Information section.
3.  Provide details about your vehicle in the Vehicle Questions section.
4.  Answer the driving history and insurance questions in the Driving History Questions section.
5.  Choose your desired coverage options in the Insurance Questions section.
6.  Review and confirm your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM-DD-YYYY.
* **Gender** (Select One, Required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Marital Status** (Select One, Required): Select your current marital status from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive your quote.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your best contact number.
* **Street Address** (Text, Required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (Select One, Required): Select the state where you reside from the provided options.
* **ZIP Code** (Text, Required): Enter your 5-digit ZIP code.
* **Vehicle Year** (Number, Required): Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make** (Text, Required): Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (Text, Required): Enter the model name of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of your vehicle from the provided options.
* **Estimated Vehicle Value** (Select One, Required): Select the current market value of your vehicle from the provided options.
* **Ownership Status** (Select One, Required): Select whether you own or lease your vehicle.
* **Years Licensed** (Select One, Required): Select how long you have been licensed.
* **Accidents in Past 5 Years** (Select One, Required): Select the number of at-fault accidents in the past 5 years.
* **Violations in Past 3 Years** (Select One, Required): Select the number of speeding tickets or violations in the past 3 years.
* **License Suspension History** (Select One, Required): Select whether your license has ever been suspended.
* **Current Insurance Coverage** (Select One, Required): Select your current insurance coverage.
* **Desired Coverage** (Select One, Required): Select your desired insurance coverage.
* **Additional Drivers** (Select One, Required): Select whether you have additional drivers.
* **Number of Vehicles to Insure** (Select One, Required): Select how many vehicles you need insurance for.
* **When Do You Need Coverage** (Select One, Required): Select your timeline for insurance.
* **Current Insurance Provider** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of your current insurance provider.
* **Discounts of Interest** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any discounts that might apply to you.

Tips:

* Please answer questions accurately and honestly.
* Ensure you provide your best contact information for communication.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us directly.
* Review your information carefully before submitting.
* If you've already submitted a quote request, you may not need to fill out this form.
* This form is for initial quote requests only, not for policy changes or updates.
