# Auto Mechanic Recommendation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect reviews and recommendations from customers who have had positive experiences with a specific auto mechanic or shop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and email address as the person making the recommendation.
2. Select the relationship you have with the person you are recommending (e.g., friend, family member, etc.).
3. Enter the name of the auto shop or mechanic you are recommending.
4. Enter the address or city of the shop, if available.
5. Enter the phone number of the shop, if available.
6. Select the services you received at the shop (e.g., oil change, brake repair, etc.).
7. Rate the shop's pricing as "Fairly Priced," "Slightly Expensive," or "Budget Friendly."
8. Rate the overall quality of work as "Excellent," "Good," or "Average."
9. Share a brief reason why you recommend the shop or mechanic.
10. If applicable, indicate the duration of your professional relationship with the mechanic.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Please enter your name as the person making the recommendation.
* **Your Email**: Enter your email address for follow-up questions or communication.
* **Relationship to Recipient**: Select how you know the person seeking help (e.g., friend, family member, etc.).
* **Auto Shop Name**: Enter the name of the business or mechanic you are recommending.
* **Shop Address or City**: If available, enter the address or city of the shop.
* **Shop Phone Number**: Enter the professional contact number of the shop, if available.
* **Website Link**: If available, enter the URL of the shop's website.
* **Services Performed**: Select the services you received at the shop (e.g., oil change, brake repair, etc.).
* **Pricing Rating**: Rate the shop's pricing as "Fairly Priced," "Slightly Expensive," or "Budget Friendly."
* **Quality of Work**: Rate the overall quality of work as "Excellent," "Good," or "Average."
* **Why do you recommend them?**: Share a brief reason why you recommend the shop or mechanic.
* **Years Using This Mechanic**: If applicable, indicate the duration of your professional relationship with the mechanic.

## Tips

Please be as detailed and honest as possible when filling out the form, but be respectful and professional in your recommendation. This will help us better understand your experience and provide accurate feedback for others who may be looking for a mechanic or shop. If you have any concerns or issues with the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
