# vehicle_entry_and_exit_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle_entry_and_exit_log form is used to record the entry and exit details of vehicles, including dates, times, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the date and time for vehicle entry and exit.
2. Enter the vehicle license number, vehicle number, and vehicle make.
3. Provide the driver's name and the reason for the entry and exit.
4. Select whether the vehicle is in a category (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
5. Select the person or group assigned to the vehicle.
6. Submit the form to create a new log entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the vehicle's entry. This field is optional, but it is essential for accurate tracking.
* **Vehicle License Number** (`vehicle_license_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's license number for identification purposes.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the vehicle's entry.
* **Exit Date** (`exit_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the vehicle's exit. This field is optional, but it is essential for accurate tracking.
* **Exit Time** (`exit_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the vehicle's exit.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the driver associated with the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, text, required/optional): Enter a unique identifier for the vehicle.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the reason for the entry and exit.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the vehicle is in a specific category.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, text, required/optional): Select the person or group responsible for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the make of the vehicle for identification purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure correct tracking and reporting.
* If a vehicle has been previously logged, do not create a new log entry.
* Use this form to track vehicle usage and movement for administrative or maintenance purposes.
