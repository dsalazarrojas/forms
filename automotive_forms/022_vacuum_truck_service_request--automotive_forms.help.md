# Vacuum Truck Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request services such as maintenance or repair for a vehicle. It will help us understand the job details, customer information, and service requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the job information, including a brief description of the service needed.
2. Enter the customer's details, including their name, contact information, and email.
3. Enter the vehicle's details, including its make, model, and any other relevant information.
4. Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
5. Enter the service type and date you require.
6. Select the service status and provide any additional service comments or details.
7. Enter your signature, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Information** (`job_information`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the service needed for the vehicle.
* **Customer Info** (`customer_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's make, model, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address related to the vehicle or service.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of service required (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional service comments or details.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you require the service.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you require the service.
* **Service Status** (`service_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the service status (e.g., 'Active' or 'Inactive').
* **Service Comments** (`service_comments`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional service comments or details.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any job-specific notes.
* **Technician Notes** (`technician_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any technician-specific notes.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Enter your signature.
