# Car Rental Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Rental Authorization Form is used to collect essential information from end-users before authorizing a car rental. This form ensures that all necessary details are collected and that the renter is aware of and agrees to the rental terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and the terms and conditions.
2. Fill in your primary renter information, including your first name, last name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and street address.
3. Fill in your driver's license information, including your license number, license state, and license country.
4. Select your payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, corporate account, or cash deposit).
5. Choose how you would like to pay (e.g., full rental amount plus deposit, deposit only, estimated total, or custom amount).
6. Select any additional insurance coverage options you would like to add to your rental.
7. Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the terms and conditions of the rental.
8. Sign the authorization agreement to confirm your rental agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Primary Renter Information** (Primary renter information): Your personal details.
* **First Name** (First name): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last name): Your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of birth): Your date of birth, which must be 21 or older.
* **Email Address** (Email address): Your email address for communications.
* **Phone Number** (Phone number): Your contact number.
* **Street Address** (Street address): Your address.
* **City** (City): Your city.
* **State** (State): Your state.
* **ZIP Code** (ZIP code): Your ZIP code.
* **Driver's License Number** (Driver's license number): Your driver's license number.
* **License State** (License state): State that issued your license.
* **License Country** (License country): Country of issuance.
* **License Expiration** (License expiration): Expiration date of your license.
* **Reservation Number** (Reservation number): If applicable.
* **Rental Start Date** (Rental start date): Pickup date.
* **Rental End Date** (Rental end date): Return date.
* **Pickup Location** (Pickup location): Where to pick up the vehicle.
* **Return Location** (Return location): Where to return the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Description** (Vehicle description): Vehicle details.
* **Vehicle VIN** (Vehicle VIN): Vehicle Identification Number.
* **Authorized Amount** (Authorized amount): Amount to authorize.
* **Insurance Selection** (Insurance selection): Coverage options.
* **Authorization Agreement** (Authorization agreement): Do you authorize this rental and agree to all terms.
* **Signature Confirmation** (Signature confirmation): Confirm your authorization.
* **Authorization Date** (Authorization date): Today's date.
* **Payment Method** (Payment method): How you will pay (e.g., credit card, debit card, corporate account, or cash deposit).
* **Card Type** (Card type): Type of card (e.g., Visa, MasterCard, American Express, or Discover).
* **Cardholder Name** (Cardholder name): Name on card.
* **Card Number** (Card number): Card number.
* **CVV** (CVV): Security code.
* **Billing Address** (Billing address): Address on card.
* **Additional Driver 1 Name** (Additional driver 1 name): Full name of additional driver 1.
* **Additional Driver 1 License** (Additional driver 1 license): License number of additional driver 1.
* **Additional Driver 1 State** (Additional driver 1 state): State that issued license of additional driver 1.
* **Additional Driver 1 DOB** (Additional driver 1 DOB): Date of birth of additional driver 1.
* **Additional Driver 2 Name** (Additional driver 2 name): Full name of additional driver 2.
* **Additional Driver 2 License** (Additional driver 2 license): License number of additional driver 2.
* **Additional Driver 2 State** (Additional driver 2 state): State that issued license of additional driver 2.
* **Additional Driver 2 DOB** (Additional driver 2 DOB): Date of birth of additional driver 2.
* **Payment Authorization** (Payment authorization): Payment details.
* **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and conditions): Agreement terms.
* **Vehicle Inspection** (Vehicle inspection): Do you want to inspect the vehicle.
* **Fuel Policy** (Fuel policy): Do you understand the fuel policy.
* **Mileage Policy** (Mileage policy): Do you understand the mileage policy.
* **Late Return Policy** (Late return policy): Do you understand late fees.
* **Prohibited Uses** (Prohibited uses): Do you agree to restrictions.
* **Authorization Agreement** (Authorization agreement): Do you authorize this rental and agree to all terms.
* **Signature Confirmation** (Signature confirmation): Confirm your authorization.
* **Authorization Date** (Authorization date): Today's date.
