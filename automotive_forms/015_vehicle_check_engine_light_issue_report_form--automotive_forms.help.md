# vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report engine light issues on vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the vehicle's registration number or any other relevant vehicle information ready.
2. Select the page that corresponds to your issue (there are 10 pages in total).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_1** (Text)
	+ A text input field where you can provide a brief description of your issue.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_2** (Number)
	+ A numerical input field where you can enter a specific number related to your issue.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_3** (Number)
	+ A numerical input field where you can enter another specific number related to your issue.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_4** (Select One)
	+ A dropdown select field where you can choose one of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_5** (Select Multiple)
	+ A multi-select dropdown select field where you can choose multiple of the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_6** (Date)
	+ A date input field where you can enter a specific date related to your issue.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_7** (Time)
	+ A time input field where you can enter a specific time related to your issue.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_8** (Note)
	+ A text area where you can provide additional notes about your issue.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_9** (Email)
	+ An email input field where you can enter an email address related to your issue.
* **vehicle_check_engine_light_issue_report_form_10** (Text)
	+ A text input field where you can provide any additional information or comments about your issue.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when providing information in each field.
* Use the dropdown select fields to choose the most accurate options.
* Make sure to provide any relevant vehicle registration numbers or details.
