# Vehicle Inspection Verification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to verify the inspection details of a vehicle. It is used for tracking and recording the vehicle's inspection status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the vehicle's year of manufacture.
2. Indicate whether the vehicle's make is correct or incorrect.
3. Select the vehicle's model(s) from the options provided.
4. Enter the year the vehicle was made.
5. Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
6. Select the date of the inspection.
7. Choose the result of the inspection (Pass or Fail).
8. Enter any comments about the inspection.
9. List any parts that were repaired during the inspection.
10. Enter any costs associated with the repairs.
11. Enter the name of the person who performed the inspection.
12. Enter the phone number of the person who performed the inspection.
13. Enter the email of the person who performed the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Inspection Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is the title of the form, which should be filled out with the same text as shown.
* **Year** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false): Enter the year of manufacture of the vehicle.
* **Make** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate whether the vehicle's make is correct or incorrect.
* **Model** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the model(s) of the vehicle from the provided options.
* **Year Made** (`5`, `number`, required: false): Enter the year the vehicle was made.
* **License Plate Number** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Inspection Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Result** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the result of the inspection (Pass or Fail).
* **Comments** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Enter any comments about the inspection.
* **Repaired Parts** (`10`, `select_multiple`, required: false): If repairs were made, list the parts that were repaired.
* **Repaired Costs** (`11`, `number`, required: false): Enter any costs associated with the repairs.
* **Inspector Name** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person who performed the inspection.
* **Phone** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who performed the inspection.
* **Email** (`14`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the person who performed the inspection.
