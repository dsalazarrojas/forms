# In Car Communication Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The In Car Communication Feedback Form is designed to collect subjective feedback about the clarity and usefulness of in-car communications. This form is used to help the organization understand how well the communication experience met your needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and respond thoughtfully.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the open-ended fields.
4. Make sure to answer all required questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **In Car Communication Form**: This is the title of the form, which is not a question to be answered. Please leave this field blank.
* **Communication Clarity** (`communication_clarity`, number, required): How clear was the communication? Please respond on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being Not Very Clarifying and 5 being Very Clarifying.
* **How Well Was The Communication** (`communication_clarity_2`, number, required): How well was the communication about the car? Please respond on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being Not At All Useful and 5 being Very Useful.
* **Clarity 3** (`communication_clarity_3`, text, required): What was your overall impression of the communication? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **Comfort Level** (`comfort_level`, number, required): How comfortable were you during the communication? Please respond on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being Not Comfortable At All and 5 being Very Comfortable.
* **Comfort Level 2** (`comfort_level_2`, text, required): What specifically made you feel uncomfortable during the communication? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **Comfort Clarity Level** (`communication_clarity_4`, time, required): How much time did you need to understand the communication? Please respond in hours, minutes, and seconds.
* **How Clarifying Was It** (`communication_clarity_5`, select_one, required): How clarifying was the communication? Please respond with one of the following options: Very Clarifying, Rather Clarifying, Not Very Clarifying, or Not At All Clarifying.
* **How Clarifying Was It 2** (`communication_clarity_6`, select_multiple, required): How clarifying was the communication? Please respond with one or more of the following options: Very Clarifying, Rather Clarifying, Not Very Clarifying, or Not At All Clarifying.
* **How Clarifying Was It 3** (`communication_clarity_7`, select_multiple, required): How clarifying was the communication? Please respond with one or more of the following options: Very Clarifying, Rather Clarifying, Not Very Clarifying, or Not At All Clarifying.
* **How Clarifying Was It 4** (`communication_clarity_8`, text, required): How clarifying was the communication? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **What Did You Learn** (`communication_clarity_9`, text, required): What did you learn from the communication? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **Comments** (`communication_clarity_10`, note, required): Is there anything else you would like to share about the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **What Did You Learn 2** (`communication_clarity_11`, text, required): What else did you learn from the communication? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **Comments** (`communication_clarity_12`, text, required): Is there anything else you would like to share about the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **What Was Most Helpful** (`communication_clarity_13`, text, required): What was most helpful in the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **What Would You Like** (`communication_clarity_14`, text, required): What would you like to see changed or improved in the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **What Is Most Useful** (`communication_clarity_15`, select_one, required): What is most useful in the communication experience? Please respond with one of the following options: Very Useful, Rather Useful, Not Very Useful, or Not At All Useful.
* **How Would You Like 2** (`communication_clarity_16`, select_multiple, required): How would you like to see the communication experience improved? Please respond with one or more of the following options: Very Useful, Rather Useful, Not Very Useful, or Not At All Useful.
* **What Is Most Useful 2** (`communication_clarity_17`, text, required): What else is most useful in the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **What Is Most Useful 3** (`communication_clarity_18`, text, required): What else is most useful in the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **What Is Most Useful 4** (`communication_clarity_19`, text, required): What else is most useful in the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **Communication Clarity 19** (`communication_clarity_20`, text, required): Is there anything else you would like to share about the communication experience? Please respond with your thoughts.
* **Submission** (`submission`, email, required): Please enter your email address to submit your feedback. This will help us contact you with any follow-up questions or to thank you for your feedback. Please respond with your email address in the format `email@example.com`.
