# New Resident Vehicle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new vehicles for residents of the community. Please fill out this form to provide accurate information about your vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of vehicle you are registering (Car, Motorcycle, or Boat).
2. Provide the make of your vehicle.
3. Enter your vehicle's model.
4. Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured.
5. Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
6. Enter the date of your vehicle's registration expiration.
7. Indicate whether your vehicle has on-site, off-site, or no parking available.
8. Upload any additional documents required for vehicle registration (e.g. vehicle registration documents).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_registration_type`): Select the type of vehicle you are registering. This will help us to categorize and manage vehicle registrations efficiently.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the make of your vehicle. This is an important field as it helps us to verify the vehicle's identity.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter your vehicle's model. This helps us to have accurate records of your vehicle's specifications.
* **Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year your vehicle was manufactured. This ensures that we have a complete record of your vehicle's history.
* **License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`): Enter your vehicle's license plate number. This is a required field as it helps us to identify your vehicle uniquely.
* **Registration Expiration Date** (`registration_expiration_date`): Enter the date of your vehicle's registration expiration. This helps us to keep track of the registration's validity.
* **Parking Needs** (`parking_needs`): Indicate whether your vehicle has on-site, off-site, or no parking available. This helps us to manage parking arrangements for our community.
* **Vehicle Registration Document 1** (`documentation_upload1`): Upload any additional documents required for vehicle registration. You may upload up to 3 documents.
* **Vehicle Registration Document 2** (`documentation_upload2`): Upload any additional documents required for vehicle registration. You may upload up to 3 documents.
* **Vehicle Registration Document 3** (`documentation_upload3`): Upload any additional documents required for vehicle registration. You may upload up to 3 documents.
