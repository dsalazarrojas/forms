# Used Car Monthly Payment Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Used Car Monthly Payment Inquiry Form is designed to gather information from users who are interested in financing their vehicle. This form helps the user to provide relevant details about their financing needs and schedule a follow-up appointment to discuss their options.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of financing you are interested in by choosing from the options "Used Car Financing Options" or "Used Car Lease Options" or "Other".
2. Choose the vehicle you are inquiring about from the list of available options.
3. Enter the year of the vehicle you are inquiring about.
4. Enter the mileage of the vehicle you are inquiring about.
5. Enter the price of the vehicle you are inquiring about.
6. Choose your monthly payment preference by selecting "Lowest Price First", "Highest Price First", or "Best Interest Rate".
7. Enter your contact email address.
8. Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your inquiry.
9. Choose a follow-up appointment option by selecting "Phone", "Email", "In Person", or "Other".
10. Schedule a follow-up appointment time.
11. If desired, enter the location for the follow-up appointment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Used Car Financing Options/Used Car Lease Options/Other (Select One)**:
	+ This field is where you select the type of financing you are interested in.
* **Vehicle Type (Select Multiple)**:
	+ Select the type of vehicle you are inquiring about (e.g. Toyota, Honda, Volkswagen, Other).
* **Vehicle Year (Number)**:
	+ Enter the year of the vehicle you are inquiring about.
* **Vehicle Mileage (Number)**:
	+ Enter the mileage of the vehicle you are inquiring about.
* **Vehicle Price (Number)**:
	+ Enter the price of the vehicle you are inquiring about.
* **Monthly Payment Preference (Select One)**:
	+ Choose your preference for monthly payment options (e.g. "Lowest Price First", "Highest Price First", "Best Interest Rate").
* **Contact Information (Email)**:
	+ Enter your contact email address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Information (Text)**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your inquiry.
* **Follow-up Appointment (Select One)**:
	+ Choose your preferred method for your follow-up appointment (e.g. "Phone", "Email", "In Person", "Other").
* **Follow-up Appointment Time (Time)**:
	+ Schedule a time for your follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Appointment Location (Text)**:
	+ If desired, enter the location for your follow-up appointment.
* **Comments (Note)**:
	+ Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide about your inquiry.

## Tips

* Please make sure to select the correct vehicle type and year to ensure accurate financing options.
* Enter the correct vehicle price to ensure accurate payment estimates.
* Choose a follow-up appointment time and location that works best for you.
* You can enter additional comments or information in the "Contact Information" field to help us better understand your needs.
