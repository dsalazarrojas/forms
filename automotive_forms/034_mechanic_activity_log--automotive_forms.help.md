# Mechanic Activity Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanic Activity Log form is designed to record and document activities, such as services, repairs, and inspections, for customer vehicles. This form helps maintain accurate records, track progress, and provide a clear overview of the vehicle's history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information for the vehicle, such as vehicle make, model, and year.
2. Choose the activity type, such as Service, Repair, or Inspection.
3. Enter a brief description of the activity performed.
4. Select the assigned tool or equipment used.
5. Enter the date and time of the activity.
6. Add any additional comments or notes.
7. Finally, confirm that the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the customer whose vehicle is being serviced.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, Select One, Optional): Choose the make of the vehicle, such as Toyota, Ford, or Honda.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, Number, Optional): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Activity Type** (`activity_type`, Select One, Optional): Select the type of activity performed, such as Service, Repair, or Inspection.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the service performed.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the service.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the start time of the activity.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the end time of the activity.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, Note, Optional): Obtain the customer's signature to confirm the accuracy of the information.
* **Mechanic Signature** (`mechanic_signature`, Note, Optional): Obtain the mechanic's signature to confirm the accuracy of the information.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Optional): Choose the tool or equipment assigned to the activity.
* **Category** (`category`, Select One, Optional): Select the category of the activity, such as Automotive Forms or Other.
* **Description** (`description`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or descriptions.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any notes or comments from the customer.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, Select One, Optional): Choose whether the activity is assigned to a specific user.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, Text, Optional): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, Select One, Optional): Select the status of the job, such as Completed, Pending, or In Progress.
* **Job Status Description** (`job_status_description`, Text, Optional): Enter a description of the job status.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, Text, Optional): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, Text, Optional): Enter the condition of the vehicle.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Start Time H** (`start_time_h`, Time, Optional): Enter the start time of the activity in 24-hour format.
* **End Time H** (`end_time_h`, Time, Optional): Enter the end time of the activity in 24-hour format.
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
