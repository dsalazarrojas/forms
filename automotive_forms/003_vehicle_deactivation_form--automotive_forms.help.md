# vehicle_deactivation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for vehicle deactivation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the vehicle is deactivated or not.
2. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
3. Choose the reason for deactivation from the list of options provided.
4. Enter the date of deactivation.
5. Enter the odometer reading of the vehicle.
6. Enter the mileage reading of the vehicle.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
8. Enter the vehicle make and model.
9. Enter the vehicle year.
10. Enter the vehicle color.
11. Enter the vehicle type.
12. Enter the location of the vehicle.
13. Enter the name of the vehicle's manager.
14. Enter the manager's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Info**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the vehicle is deactivated or not.
* **vin_number**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **date_deactivated**: Enter the date of deactivation.
* **odometer_reading**: Enter the odometer reading of the vehicle.
* **mileage_reading**: Enter the mileage reading of the vehicle.
* **reason_for_deactivation**: Choose the reason for deactivation from the list of options provided.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle.
* **vehicle_make**: Enter the make of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_color**: Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **vehicle_type**: Enter the type of vehicle.
* **location**: Enter the location of the vehicle.
* **manager_name**: Enter the name of the vehicle's manager.
* **manager_email**: Enter the email address of the vehicle's manager.
