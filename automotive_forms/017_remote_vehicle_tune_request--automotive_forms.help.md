# Remote Vehicle Tune Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Remote Vehicle Tune Request" form is designed to collect information from customers about their vehicle needs for remote tune-up or maintenance services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your vehicle details, including make and model.
2. Select the tuning shops and technicians you prefer for the service.
3. Provide any additional notes or comments about your vehicle.
4. Enter your contact information for scheduling and communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`): Enter a brief description of your vehicle, including its make and model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Select the make of your vehicle from the provided options (e.g., Honda, Ford, Toyota).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Select the model of your vehicle (e.g., Civic, Mustang, Corolla).
* **Customer Goals** (`prophets_goals`): Enter any specific goals or requirements you have for the tune-up service.
* **Tuning Shops** (`tuning_shops`): Select one or more preferred tuning shops for the service from the provided options.
* **Technicians** (`technicians`): Select one or more preferred technicians from the provided options.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or information about your vehicle.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`): Enter your phone number for scheduling and communication purposes.
* **Customer Email** (`prophets_email`): Enter your email address for scheduling and communication purposes.
* **Customer Notes** (`customer_notes`): Enter any additional comments or information about your vehicle, if needed.
* **Scheduling Info** (`scheduling_info`): Enter the date and time you prefer for the service.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are filled out to ensure timely and efficient service.
