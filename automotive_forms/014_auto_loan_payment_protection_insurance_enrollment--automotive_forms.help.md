# Auto Loan Payment Protection Insurance Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the borrower for Auto Loan Payment Protection Insurance Enrollment. It gathers details about the borrower's personal information, loan details, and coverage options for the protection insurance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your personal information:
    *   First Name
    *   Last Name
    *   Date of Birth (MM-DD-YYYY)
    *   Social Security Number (XXX-XX-XXXX)
    *   Email Address (Primary email address)
    *   Phone Number (Best contact number)
    *   Street Address
    *   City
    *   State
    *   ZIP Code (5-digit)
2.  Enter loan information:
    *   Loan Account Number
    *   Original Loan Amount
    *   Loan Term (36, 48, 60, 72, or 84 months)
    *   Monthly Payment Amount
3.  Select coverage options:
    *   Unemployment Coverage: Choose to cover payments if you lose your job
    *   Disability Coverage: Choose to cover payments if you become disabled
    *   Life Coverage: Choose to pay off the loan in case of death
    *   Critical Illness Coverage: Choose to cover payments during critical illness
4.  Select premium payment method:
    *   Monthly with Loan Payment
    *   Single Upfront Payment
    *   Financed into Loan
5.  Review and sign the form:
    *   Please review the information you have entered carefully
    *   Acknowledge that you understand the terms and conditions of the insurance coverage
    *   Sign the form digitally

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your legal first name.
*  **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your legal last name.
*  **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*  **Social Security Number** (`ssn`, Text, Required): Enter your Social Security Number in XXX-XX-XXXX format.
*  **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your primary email address.
*  **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your best contact number.
*  **Street Address** (`address`, Text, Required): Enter your current street address.
*  **City** (`city`, Text, Required): Enter the city of your residence.
*  **State** (`state`, Select One, Required): Select your state of residence.
*  **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, Text, Required): Enter your 5-digit ZIP code.
*  **Co-Applicant Information** (`co_applicant_info`, Note, Optional): Enter optional co-applicant details.
*  **Co-Applicant First Name** (`co_applicant_first`, Text, Optional): Enter the co-applicant's first name if applicable.
*  **Co-Applicant Last Name** (`co_applicant_last`, Text, Optional): Enter the co-applicant's last name if applicable.
*  **Co-Applicant Date of Birth** (`co_applicant_dob`, Date, Optional): Enter the co-applicant's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format if applicable.
*  **Co-Applicant SSN** (`co_applicant_ssn`, Text, Optional): Enter the co-applicant's Social Security Number in XXX-XX-XXXX format if applicable.
*  **Loan Information** (`loan_info`, Note, Optional): Enter details about your auto loan.
*  **Loan Account Number** (`loan_number`, Text, Required): Enter your loan account number.
*  **Original Loan Amount** (`loan_amount`, Number, Required): Enter the total loan amount.
*  **Loan Term** (`loan_term`, Select One, Required): Select the loan term in months (36, 48, 60, 72, or 84 months).
*  **Monthly Payment Amount** (`monthly_payment`, Number, Required): Enter your monthly payment amount.
*  **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, Note, Optional): Enter details about the financed vehicle.
*  **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, Number, Required): Enter the year of the vehicle.
*  **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, Text, Required): Enter the vehicle manufacturer.
*  **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, Text, Required): Enter the vehicle model name.
*  **VIN** (`vin`, Text, Required): Enter the vehicle identification number.
*  **Coverage Selection** (`coverage_selection`, Note, Optional): Choose your coverage options.
*  **Unemployment Coverage** (`unemployment_coverage`, Select One, Required): Choose to cover payments if you lose your job.
*  **Disability Coverage** (`disability_coverage`, Select One, Required): Choose to cover payments if you become disabled.
*  **Life Coverage** (`life_coverage`, Select One, Required): Choose to pay off the loan in case of death.
*  **Critical Illness Coverage** (`critical_illness_coverage`, Select One, Required): Choose to cover payments during critical illness.
*  **Coverage Amount Selection** (`coverage_amount`, Select One, Optional): Choose how much coverage you want (Full Loan Balance, 12 months of payments, 24 months of payments, 36 months of payments).
*  **Beneficiary Information** (`beneficiary_info`, Note, Optional): Enter information about the beneficiary.
*  **Primary Beneficiary Name** (`beneficiary_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the full name of the beneficiary.
*  **Beneficiary Relationship** (`beneficiary_relationship`, Select One, Optional): Choose how the beneficiary is related to you (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling, Other).
*  **Premium Information** (`premium_info`, Note, Optional): Enter details about the premium.
*  **Premium Payment Method** (`premium_payment`, Select One, Required): Choose how you will pay the premium (Monthly with Loan Payment, Single Upfront Payment, Financed into Loan).
*  **Estimated Monthly Premium** (`monthly_premium`, Number, Optional): Enter the estimated monthly premium.
*  **Eligibility Questions** (`eligibility`, Note, Optional): Answer questions about your employment status and pre-existing medical conditions.
*  **Are You Currently Employed** (`currently_employed`, Select One, Required): Select your employment status.
*  **Pre-Existing Medical Conditions** (`pre_existing_conditions`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you have any pre-existing medical conditions.
*  **Conditions Description** (`conditions_description`, Text, Optional): Please describe the conditions if applicable.
*  **Acknowledgments and Disclosures** (`acknowledgments`, Note, Optional): Please read the insurance disclosure document carefully.
*  **Received Insurance Disclosure** (`received_disclosure`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you have received the insurance disclosure document.
*  **Understand Terms and Conditions** (`understand_terms`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you understand the terms and conditions of the insurance coverage.
*  **Voluntary Purchase Acknowledgment** (`voluntary_purchase`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you understand that this insurance is optional.
*  **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** (`cancellation_policy`, Select One, Required): Choose whether you understand that you can cancel at any time.
*  **Borrower Signature** (`borrower_signature`, Text, Required): Sign the form digitally.
*  **Borrower Signature Date** (`borrower_signature_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date signed.

## Tips

*  Please review the form carefully before proceeding.
*  Enter accurate and complete information in each field.
*  Choose the correct coverage and premium payment method according to your needs.
*  Sign the form digitally and date it correctly.
*  This form is for informational purposes only. The information gathered will be used for the Auto Loan Payment Protection Insurance Enrollment process.
