# Engine Performance Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Engine Performance Assessment" form is designed to evaluate the condition and performance of an automotive engine. It helps identify potential issues and document the engine's condition for future maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the vehicle's engine and its documentation to ensure you have the necessary information.
2. Answer each question accurately and truthfully based on your knowledge and experience.
3. If you are unsure or lack experience, do not complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter the vehicle's make, model, and any relevant identification details.
* **Engine Condition** (`engine_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select the current condition of the engine (Bad, Fair, Good, or Excellent).
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter a numerical value to indicate the engine's performance (e.g., 0-10).
* **Oil Quality** (`oil_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the current quality of the engine oil (Low, Medium, High, or Excellent).
* **Fuel Level** (`fuel_level`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select the current fuel level (Empty, Less than 1/4, 1/4 to 1/2, 1/2 to 3/4, or More than 3/4).
* **Engine Problem** (`engine_problem`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Choose any engine problems (Low Oil Level, High Water Temperature, Leaking Oil, Overheating, or Other (please specify)).
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select the type of fuel used (Regular, Mid-Grade, Premium, Diesel, or Other (please specify)).
* **Engine Size** (`engine_size`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter the engine's size.
* **Engine Type** (`engine_type`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the engine type (Gas, Diesel, Hybrid, or Other (please specify)).
* **Fuel Efficiency** (`fuel_efficiency`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter a numerical value to indicate the engine's fuel efficiency (e.g., 0-10).
* **Engine Year** (`engine_year`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter the engine's year of manufacture.
* **Engine Oil Filter** (`engine_oil_filter`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the condition of the engine's oil filter (Clean, Dirty, Not Installed, or Other (please specify)).
* **Engine Air Filter** (`engine_air_filter`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the condition of the engine's air filter (Clean, Dirty, Not Installed, or Other (please specify)).
* **Transmission Type** (`transmission_type`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select the transmission type (Automatic, Manual, Semi-Automatic, CVT, or Other (please specify)).
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter the vehicle's weight.
* **Transmission Oil Quality** (`transmission_oil_quality`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the quality of the transmission oil (Low, Medium, High, or Excellent).
* **Transmission Oil Filter** (`transmission_oil_filter`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the condition of the transmission's oil filter (Clean, Dirty, Not Installed, or Other (please specify)).
* **Drive Position** (`drive_position`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
Select the vehicle's drive position (Front-Wheel Drive, Rear-Wheel Drive, All-Wheel Drive, Four-Wheel Drive, or Other (please specify)).
* **Fuel Mileage** (`fuel_mileage`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter the vehicle's fuel mileage.
* **Fuel Maintenance** (`fuel_maintenance`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter a numerical value to indicate the fuel maintenance (e.g., 0-10).
* **Engine Oil Leak** (`engine_oil_leak`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Select whether an engine oil leak is present (True or False).
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `select_one`, required: false): 
Choose the vehicle's condition (New, Average, Used, or Other (please specify)).
* **Vehicle Age** (`vehicle_age`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter the vehicle's age.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required: false): 
Enter the vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): 
Enter any additional comments or information about the engine's condition or performance.
