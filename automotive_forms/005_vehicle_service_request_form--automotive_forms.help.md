# Vehicle Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about vehicles requiring service. It's intended to be used by customers or vehicle owners to request services for their vehicles. Fill out this form to provide detailed information about your vehicle, the service requested, and any specific needs you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form on a desktop or mobile device with a web browser.
2.  Click on the "Vehicle Information" page to enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
3.  On the "Vehicle Details" page, provide a brief description of your vehicle, including any additional details that may help the service provider understand your needs.
4.  In the "Service Request" page, describe the specific service you are requesting for your vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Information (Vehicle Service Request Form 1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your vehicle's make, model, and year here. This information will help us understand the type of vehicle you are requesting service for.
*   **Vehicle Details (Vehicle Details)** (`text`, required/optional): Briefly describe your vehicle, including any additional details that may help us understand your needs. This could include the vehicle's condition, any previous issues, or recent maintenance history.
*   **Service Request (Service Request Form 3)** (`text`, required/optional): Describe the specific service you are requesting for your vehicle. Be as detailed as possible about what needs to be done or checked.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate information about your vehicle to ensure efficient service.
- Be as detailed as possible when describing your vehicle and the service requested.
- If you have any additional requirements or concerns, you can provide them in the space provided.
