# Auto Parts Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Parts Verification Form is a critical tool for verifying the accuracy and compatibility of auto parts. It ensures that the parts received are correct and meet the vehicle's specifications, reducing errors and streamlining the overall process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your name and contact information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the vehicle information, including the year, make, model, and vehicle identification number (VIN).
3. Specify the part information, including the name, number, brand, and type of the part.
4. Choose the correct compatibility options, such as transmission type, engine type, and part condition.
5. Select the urgency level and preferred supplier, if applicable.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments, if necessary.
7. Confirm your verification request.
