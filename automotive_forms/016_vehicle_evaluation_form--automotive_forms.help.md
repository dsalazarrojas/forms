# Vehicle Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Evaluation Form is a tool for evaluating vehicles. Use this form to collect and record information about a vehicle's details such as its name, type, make, model, year, condition, mileage, price, rating, and evaluation date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "Vehicle Name" field with the specific name of the vehicle being evaluated.
2. In the "Vehicle Type" field, select the correct type of vehicle from the available options (car, truck, SUV, van, motorcycle).
3. Enter the make of the vehicle in the "Make" field.
4. In the "Model" field, enter the model number or name of the vehicle.
5. For the "Year" field, enter the vehicle's production year.
6. Select the condition of the vehicle from the list (new, used, certified pre-owned, lease) in the "Condition" field.
7. Enter the vehicle's mileage (in kilometers) in the "Mileage" field.
8. Enter the vehicle's price (in currency of choice) in the "Price" field.
9. In the "Rating" field, add notes about the vehicle's performance, appearance, or condition (this field type is a free text area and will not be evaluated).
10. If applicable, select the evaluation date from the calendar in the "Evaluation Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Name** (Text): Enter the name given to the vehicle or a nickname.
* **Vehicle Type** (Select One): Choose the type of vehicle you are evaluating (car, truck, SUV, van, motorcycle).
* **Make** (Text): Enter the vehicle's make or manufacturer (e.g., Toyota, Ford, BMW).
* **Model** (Text): Enter the model number or name of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Mustang, Golf).
* **Year** (Number): Enter the vehicle's production year.
* **Condition** (Select Multiple): Select the current condition of the vehicle (new, used, certified pre-owned, lease).
* **Mileage** (Number): Enter the vehicle's mileage (in kilometers).
* **Price** (Number): Enter the vehicle's price (in the currency of choice).
* **Rating** (Note): Add notes about the vehicle's performance, appearance, or condition.
* **Evaluation Date** (Date): Select the date the evaluation was completed or is scheduled to be completed.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered, especially for fields that require specific values (Year, Mileage, Price).
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure form submission success.
* Use the "Rating" field for any notes or comments about the vehicle that will aid in its evaluation.
* If the vehicle's condition is "used", you may also want to include notes about its maintenance history or any known issues.
