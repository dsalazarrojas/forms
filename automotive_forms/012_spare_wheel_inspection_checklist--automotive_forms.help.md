# Spare Wheel Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spare Wheel Inspection Checklist is a form to record the results of a spare wheel inspection. This is an essential maintenance task to ensure the safe and reliable operation of a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the inspection date by choosing the date you conducted the inspection.
2. Choose the type of wheel being inspected from the available options.
3. Select any applicable conditions of the wheel, such as "Clean", "Dirty", "Damaged", or "Missing".
4. Enter the mileage of the vehicle at the time of inspection.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
6. Enter your email address for contact information.
7. Enter your phone number for contact information.
8. Select the tool used for the inspection from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Inspection Date**: Enter the date you conducted the inspection.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
- **Wheel Type**: Choose the type of wheel being inspected from the options provided.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
- **Condition**: Select any applicable conditions of the wheel, such as "Clean", "Dirty", "Damaged", or "Missing".
  * Type: select_multiple
  * Required: false
- **Mileage**: Enter the mileage of the vehicle at the time of inspection.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
- **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
  * Type: note
  * Required: false
- **Email**: Enter your email address for contact information.
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool used for the inspection from the available options.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct options for each field to ensure accurate and useful information for future reference.
- If you need to contact the user, use the email or phone number provided.
- This form is designed to be flexible, so fill out as much or as little information as needed.
