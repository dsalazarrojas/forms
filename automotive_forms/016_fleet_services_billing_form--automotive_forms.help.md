**Fleet Services Billing Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Fleet Services Billing Form is used to collect information about a client's payment details for services rendered to their fleet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Prophets**: Select one or more prophets from the provided list.
	* **Email**: Enter the client's email address.
	* **Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
	* **Date**: Enter the date of the service.
	* **Time**: Enter the time of the service.
	* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status (paid, pending, or unpaid).
	* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
	* **Payment Amount**: Enter the payment amount.
	* **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address.
	* **Invoice Number**: Enter the invoice number.
	* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (cash, paypal, visa).
	* **Account Number**: Enter the account number.
	* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address (again, this is a duplicate field).
	* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number (again, this is a duplicate field).
	* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms (net10, net20, or net30).
2. Optionally, fill in the following fields:
	* **Note**: Add any additional notes about the payment or service.
	* **Payment Notes**: Add notes about the payment.
	* **Account Notes**: Add notes about the account.
	* **Payment Status Notes**: Add notes about the payment status.
	* **Payment Terms Notes**: Add notes about the payment terms.
3. Review and submit the form.
