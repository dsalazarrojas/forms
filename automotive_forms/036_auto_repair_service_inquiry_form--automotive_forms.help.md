# Auto Repair Service Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential customers to better serve their needs. It will guide them in providing the necessary details about their vehicle repair or maintenance service request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about the vehicle you would like to repair, including its make, model, and year.
3. Describe the issue or service you are inquiring about, including any specific details about the symptoms or services needed.
4. Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or text).
5. Indicate your urgency level for the service, such as emergency, within 24 hours, or just inquiring.
6. If you have any additional questions or comments, you can enter them in the provided text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (Required)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow up communication.
* **Phone Number (Required)**: Enter your best phone number where you can be reached.
* **Vehicle Make (Required)**: Enter the make of your vehicle, e.g. Toyota.
* **Vehicle Model (Required)**: Enter the model of your vehicle, e.g. Camry.
* **Vehicle Year (Required)**: Enter the year of your vehicle, e.g. 2015.
* **Inquiry Type (Required)**: Select the category of your request, such as Routine Maintenance or Specific Repair.
* **Description of Issue (Required)**: Describe the symptoms or services you are inquiring about.
* **Preferred Contact Method (Required)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted, e.g. phone, email, or text.
* **Urgency Level (Required)**: Choose how soon you need the service, e.g. emergency, within 24 hours, or just inquiring.
* **Additional Questions (Optional)**: If you have any additional information or questions, you can enter it here.
