# Car Insurance Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify vehicle insurance information for the purpose of verifying insurance coverage, checking for accuracy, and updating records. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the verification process is successful.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately**. Each field is marked as required, and your responses will be reviewed for accuracy.
2. **Provide all necessary documents and information**. This includes insurance cards, policy numbers, and other relevant details.
3. **Review and sign the form**. Ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
4. **Submit the form**. Once reviewed, your submission will be processed, and you will receive updates on the verification process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner Information**
	+ **First Name**: Provide the first name of the vehicle owner.
	+ **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the vehicle owner.
	+ **Email Address**: Enter the contact email address of the vehicle owner.
	+ **Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number of the vehicle owner.
	+ **Street Address**: Enter the vehicle owner's mailing address.
	+ **City**: Enter the city of the vehicle owner's address.
	+ **State**: Enter the state of the vehicle owner's address.
	+ **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of the vehicle owner's address.
* **Vehicle Information**
	+ **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle Make**: Enter the manufacturer of the vehicle.
	+ **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
	+ **VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
	+ **License Plate Number**: Enter the plate number of the vehicle.
	+ **Plate State**: Enter the state that issued the plate number.
	+ **Registration Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the vehicle registration.
* **Insurance Information**
	+ **Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the insurance company.
	+ **Policy Number**: Enter the policy number of the vehicle.
	+ **Policy Effective Date**: Enter the start date of the policy coverage.
	+ **Policy Expiration Date**: Enter the end date of the policy coverage.
	+ **Policy Status**: Select the current status of the policy (Active, Pending, Cancelled, Expired, or Unknown).
	+ **Coverage Types**: Select the coverages included in the policy (Liability, Collision, Comprehensive, Uninsured Motorist, Personal Injury Protection, Medical Payments, Roadside Assistance, or Rental Reimbursement).
	+ **Liability Limits**: Select the coverage limits (State minimum, 25/50/25, 50/100/50, 100/300/100, 250/500/250, or Other).
	+ **Deductible Amount**: Select the deductible amount ($0, $250, $500, $1000, $2000, or Other).
	+ **Named Drivers**: Select the number of drivers on the policy (1, 2, 3, or 4 or more).
	+ **Additional Driver Names**: If there are more than 4 drivers, provide their names.
* **Verification Details**
	+ **Verification Purpose**: Select the reason for the verification (Vehicle registration, Accident claim, Traffic citation, Employment requirement, Personal records, Loan/Lease requirement, or Other).
	+ **Requesting Entity**: Enter the name of the entity requesting the verification.
	+ **Verification Deadline**: Enter the deadline for the verification process.
	+ **Proof of Insurance Card**: Select whether you have the insurance card (Yes - will submit, Yes - already submitted, No - need replacement).
	+ **Prior Insurance**: Select whether you had prior coverage (Yes - continuous coverage, Yes - with gap, No - first time insuring).
	+ **Prior Carrier**: Enter the name of the previous insurance carrier.
	+ **Additional Comments**: Provide any other relevant information.
	+ **Signature Confirmation**: Confirm that the information is accurate and complete.
	+ **Verification Date**: Enter the current date.

Note: This form is designed to gather accurate and complete information. Please fill it out carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
