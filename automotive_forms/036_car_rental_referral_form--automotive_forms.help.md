# Car Rental Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Car Rental Referral Form is used to refer potential customers to our car rental service. This form captures information about the referrer, the referred customer, and the rental details to help us process the referral and follow up with the customer.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, and company (if applicable).
2. Select the type of referrer you are (e.g. travel agent, hotel concierge, corporate partner, etc.).
3. Enter the referred customer's full name, email address, and phone number.
4. Select the type of vehicle category needed.
5. Enter the expected pickup and return dates, if applicable.
6. Select the referral source (e.g. travel agent recommendation, hotel recommendation, etc.).
7. Provide any additional comments or notes about the referral.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referrer Information (1)**: Your contact details.
* 	**First Name (2)**: Enter your first name.
* 	**Last Name (3)**: Enter your last name.
* 	**Email Address (4)**: Enter your email address for referral communications.
* 	**Phone Number (5)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* 	**Company Name (6)**: If you represent a company, enter it here.
* 	**Referrer Type (7)**: Select the type of referrer you are.
* 	**Referral Information (8)**: Provide details about the referred customer.
* 	**Referred Customer Name (9)**: Enter the full name of the customer.
* 	**Referred Customer Email (10)**: Enter the customer's email address.
* 	**Referred Customer Phone (11)**: Enter the customer's contact phone number.
* 	**Referral Date (12)**: Enter the current date.
* 	**Rental Details (13)**: Enter any additional information about the rental.
* 	**Expected Pickup Date (14)**: Enter the date the customer expects to pick up the car.
* 	**Expected Return Date (15)**: Enter the date the customer expects to return the car.
* 	**Vehicle Category Needed (16)**: Select the type of vehicle category needed.
* 	**How Did Customer Hear About Us (17)**: Select the source of the referral.
* 	**Referral Notes (18)**: Provide any special notes about this referral.
* 	**Customer Preferences (19)**: Enter any known preferences of the customer.
* 	**Special Requirements (20)**: Enter any special requirements or needs of the customer.
* 	**Referral Tracking (21)**: This field is for internal use.
* 	**Referral ID (22)**: Enter an internal tracking number for the referral.
* 	**Commission Eligible (23)**: Select whether the referral is commission-eligible.
* 	**Commission Rate (24)**: Select the commission rate (if applicable).
* 	**Follow-Up Required (25)**: Select whether follow-up is required.
* 	**Follow-Up Notes (26)**: Enter instructions for follow-up.
* 	**Referral Status (27)**: Select the current status of the referral.
* 	**Additional Comments (28)**: Enter any other information about the referral.
* 	**Submission Date (29)**: Enter the current date.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out completely for the form to be successfully submitted. Fields that are marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple" will have specific options provided for your selection.
