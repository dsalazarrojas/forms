# Vehicle Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Order Form is designed to collect information from users for a vehicle order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill out the form by providing the required and optional information as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. vehicle_order_form_1 (Text) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_1_label**: Enter a brief description of your vehicle preference.

### 2. vehicle_order_form_2 (Select One) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_2_label**: Choose your preferred type of vehicle:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
	+ option 3

### 3. vehicle_order_form_3 (Number) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_3_label**: Enter the number of seats you prefer.

### 4. vehicle_order_form_4 (Date) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_4_label**: Enter the desired delivery date.

### 5. vehicle_order_form_5 (Time) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_5_label**: Enter the desired delivery time.

### 6. vehicle_order_form_6 (Email) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_6_label**: Enter your contact email address.

### 7. vehicle_order_form_7 (Text) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_7_label**: Enter any additional information about your vehicle preference.

### 8. vehicle_order_form_8 (Select Multiple) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_8_label**: Choose your preferred color and features:
	+ option 1
	+ option 2
	+ option 3

### 9. vehicle_order_form_9 (Note) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_9_label**: Add any additional notes or comments about your vehicle preference.

### 10. vehicle_order_form_10 (Number) (Optional)
* **vehicle_order_form_10_label**: Enter the desired mileage of your vehicle.
