# Mechanical Maintenance Material Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and track information about the maintenance materials used on vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Maintenance Log** field with a brief description of the maintenance activity.
2. Select the **Material Used** field to specify the type of material used for the maintenance operation.
3. Choose the **Maintenance Operation** field to select the type of maintenance activity performed.
4. Enter the **Material Cost** field with the actual cost of the material used.
5. In the **Material Supplier** field, select all relevant suppliers from whom the material was purchased.
6. If applicable, enter the **Purchase Date** and **Purchase Time** fields with the date and time when the material was purchased.
7. Enter the quantity of material **Purchased** and record the remaining quantity.
8. Choose the **Purchase Location** field to select where the material was purchased from.
9. If applicable, select whether the material was supplied by a **Vendor** (Yes/No) and enter any additional **Notes** about the transaction.
10. Enter the **Email**, **Phone Number**, **Contact Name**, **Address**, **City**, **State**, and **Zip** fields if they are relevant to the purchase or supplier.

* **Maintenance Log**: Describe the maintenance activity, e.g., "Oil change" or "Tire replacement".
* **Material Used**: Specify the type of material used for the maintenance activity.
* **Maintenance Operation**: Select the type of maintenance activity performed (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5).
* **Material Cost**: Record the actual cost of the material used.

* **Email**: Enter the contact email of the supplier (if applicable).
