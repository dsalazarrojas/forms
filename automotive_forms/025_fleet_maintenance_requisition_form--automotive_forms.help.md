# Fleet Maintenance Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleet Maintenance Requisition Form is used to submit requests for vehicle maintenance services. This form collects information about the maintenance service requested, the vehicle involved, and relevant details such as estimated costs and due dates. The goal is to gather all necessary information for the maintenance process, ensuring efficient service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form pages and ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill in the vehicle number to identify the vehicle requiring maintenance.
3. In the service request field, describe the specific maintenance or service needed for the vehicle.
4. Enter the start date and service request date as relevant.
5. Provide an estimate of the cost for the service.
6. Choose whether the vehicle is active or inactive.
7. Choose whether the service is assigned to anyone or if it's open.
8. Add any comments for the service request, if necessary.
9. Enter your email to identify who submitted the request.
10. Choose the department or team responsible for the service request.
11. Provide the due date for the service request.
12. Enter any phone number for contact purposes.
13. Select whether the vehicle is in active or inactive status.
14. If the vehicle is in inactive status, enter any other details about its status.
15. Choose whether the service location is relevant or not.
16. If the service location is not relevant, enter any other details about its location.
17. Enter any additional comments for the service request.
18. Select the type of maintenance service needed for the vehicle.
19. If the type of maintenance service is not listed, enter any other details about the service.
20. Finally, submit the form to initiate the maintenance process.
