# engine_intake_manifold_torque_sequence_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information related to engine intake manifold torque sequences. It's designed for mechanics or technicians to document and track procedures, types, and results related to engine intake manifold maintenance or repair work.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and ensure you understand the purpose of each field.
2. Answer each field accurately and completely, using the provided options or entering free-form text as necessary.
3. Verify the form's required fields (indicated by the "required" keyword) before submitting.
4. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Engine Intake Torque Procedures** (Field 1): This text field is for documenting engine intake manifold procedures performed.
* **Manifold Type** (Field 2): Select the type of engine intake manifold used (standard, tuned, or custom).
* **Sequence Torque** (Field 3): Enter the torque sequence value for the intake manifold.
* **Torque Sequence Notes** (Field 4): Provide any additional notes or comments about the torque sequence.
* **Date Performed** (Field 5): Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Technician's Name** (Field 6): Enter your name as the person completing the form.
* **Shop Name** (Field 7): Enter the name of the shop or organization where the form was completed.
* **Vehicle Number** (Field 8): Enter the vehicle's identification number.
* **Serial Number** (Field 9): Enter the engine's serial number.
* **Engine Type** (Field 10): Enter the type of engine used.
* **Comments** (Field 11): Provide any additional comments or notes about the engine intake manifold.
* **Form Submitted** (Field 12): Indicate whether the form has been submitted (True or False).

## Tips

* Double-check the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are complete.
* Review and update the form as necessary to reflect changes in engine intake manifold procedures or types.
* This form is meant for internal use within your organization. Ensure all users understand the form's purpose and fields.
