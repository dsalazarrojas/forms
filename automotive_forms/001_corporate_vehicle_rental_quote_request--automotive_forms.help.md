# corporate_vehicle_rental_quote_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a quote for corporate vehicle rentals. It helps us to understand your rental needs and provide you with a quote for your corporate vehicle rental requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start and end dates for your rental period.
2. Choose the type of rental that suits your needs (One-Way, Return, or Both).
3. Select the type of vehicle you need (Car, Van, or Truck).
4. Choose the location for your rental.
5. Provide your contact details including name, company name, email, and phone number.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your rental request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rental Date** (`rental_date`, date, optional): Select the start and end dates for your rental period.
* **Type of Rental** (`rental_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of rental that suits your needs (One-Way, Return, or Both).
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of vehicle you need (Car, Van, or Truck).
* **Rental To** (`rental_start_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your rental period.
* **Location** (`rental_location`, select_one, optional): Choose the location for your rental.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Provide your first and last name as the contact person.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, optional): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your rental request.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any assigned tools or equipment needed for the rental.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
