# Towing Service Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a notification for towing services, used to inform the user of the vehicle's towing status. It is used to gather information from the user and provide options for confirmation of authorization, pickup, dropoff, and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the request details in the first field.
2. Select "Authorized" or "Not Authorized" in the second field to confirm your authorization for the towing service.
3. Enter the pickup details in the third field.
4. Confirm whether the pickup is confirmed or not in the fourth field.
5. Confirm whether the dropoff is confirmed or not in the fifth field.
6. Choose the services you need from the options in the sixth field.
7. Enter the vehicle details in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details**: This is where you can enter the details of your towing request, such as the date, time, and location of the incident. Please enter the information as accurately as possible.
* **Confirm Authorization**: Select "Authorized" if you are confirming that the driver has authorized the towing service, or "Not Authorized" if you are not confirming. This field is optional, but it helps us provide better service.
* **Pickup Details**: This is where you can enter any additional information about the pickup process, such as the type of vehicle or any specific instructions.
* **Confirm Pickup**: Select "Confirmed" if the pickup was successful, or "Not Confirmed" if it was not. This field helps us know whether we need to send a technician to the location again.
* **Confirm Dropoff**: Select "Confirmed" if the dropoff was successful, or "Not Confirmed" if it was not. This field helps us know whether we need to check on the status of the vehicle again.
* **Confirm Services**: Choose the services you need from the options provided, such as "Towing" or "Tow and Go".
* **Vehicle Details**: This is where you can enter any additional information about the vehicle, such as its make, model, and license plate number. Please enter the information as accurately as possible.

# Tips

* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form, as it will help us provide better service.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* Make sure to review your submission before submitting, as it is easier to correct any mistakes before we process the information.
