# International Motorcycle Shipping Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information for international motorcycle shipping applications. It's meant for users who need to ship motorcycles across borders, whether for business or personal reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal or company details in the "user_input" field.
2. In the "transport_details" field, describe the transport method you'll be using, such as air or sea freight.
3. Enter your shipping information in the "shipping_info" field, including any relevant contact details.
4. If applicable, provide your broker's contact information in the "broker_details" field.
5. Finally, include any additional information you'd like to be considered in the "additional_info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_input (Text)** (`name`, `optional`): Please enter your name or company name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **transport_details (Text)** (`name`, `optional`): Describe the transport method you will be using for shipping (e.g., air or sea freight).
* **shipping_info (Text)** (`name`, `optional`): Enter your shipping information, including your contact details and any relevant shipping addresses.
* **broker_details (Text)** (`name`, `optional`): If you're using a broker, provide their contact information.
* **additional_info (Text)** (`name`, `optional`): Include any additional information that might aid in the shipping process.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to review the shipping costs and terms carefully to avoid any last-minute surprises.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the shipping process, feel free to ask for clarification before submitting the form.

This form is a tool designed to help ensure a smooth and secure international motorcycle shipping process. Please use it with care to avoid any issues during the shipping process.
