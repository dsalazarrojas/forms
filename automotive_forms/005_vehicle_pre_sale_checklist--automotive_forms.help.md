# Vehicle Pre Sale Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is a crucial tool for automotive professionals to gather and document information about a vehicle before it's sold. The "Vehicle Pre Sale Checklist" form is designed to streamline the process of evaluating and documenting a vehicle's condition, history, and sales details. This form will help ensure that all relevant information is captured accurately and consistently, which is essential for making informed decisions and avoiding potential issues down the line.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the vehicle's condition and history, and answer the questions accurately.
2.  Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year in the "Vehicle Details" section.
3.  Record any pre-sale activities or events in the "Pre-Sale Activities" section.
4.  Add any additional notes or comments in the "Condition Notes" section.
5.  Provide information about the vehicle's past, including any major repairs or servicing, in the "Vehicle History" section.
6.  Document any damage or issues found on the vehicle in the "Damage Notes" section.
7.  Identify any known issues with the vehicle before it's sold in the "Pre-Sale Issues" section.
8.  Update the vehicle's sales status in the "Sales Status" section.
9.  Enter the sale date and time in the "Sale Date" and "Sale Time" sections.
10. Record the sale and purchase prices in the "Sales Price" and "Purchase Price" sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the vehicle's overall condition, including any notable issues or defects.
*   **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the vehicle's make, model, and year.
*   **Pre-Sale Activities** (`pre_sale_activities`, `text`, optional): Record any relevant activities or events related to the vehicle before it's sold.
*   **Condition Notes** (`condition_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the vehicle's condition.
*   **Vehicle History** (`vehicle_history`, `text`, optional): Provide information about the vehicle's past, including any major repairs or servicing.
*   **Damage Notes** (`damage_notes`, `text`, optional): Document any damage or issues found on the vehicle.
*   **Pre-Sale Issues** (`pre_sale_issues`, `select_multiple`, optional): Identify any known issues with the vehicle before it's sold.
*   **Sales Status** (`sales_status`, `text`, optional): Update the vehicle's sales status, including any pending or cancelled sales.
*   **Sale Date** (`sale_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the sale date of the vehicle.
*   **Sale Time** (`sale_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the sale time of the vehicle.
*   **Sales Price** (`sales_price`, `number`, optional): Record the sale price of the vehicle.
*   **Purchase Price** (`purchase_price`, `number`, optional): Record the purchase price of the vehicle.

## Tips

*   Always review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   Update the vehicle's information as soon as possible to prevent data inaccuracies.
*   Use the form in conjunction with other tools and documentation to ensure a complete understanding of the vehicle's condition and history.
*   Consult with the vehicle's owner or manager to gather more information when needed.
*   Keep the form organized and easily accessible for future reference.
