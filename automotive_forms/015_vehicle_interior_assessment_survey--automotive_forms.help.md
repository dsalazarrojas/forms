# Vehicle Interior Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Interior Assessment Survey is a tool used to evaluate the interior condition of a vehicle. It helps assessors document their findings and provides a structured approach to identifying potential issues with the vehicle's interior.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information about the vehicle, including its make, model, year, and other relevant details.
2. Fill out the "Vehicle Info" section with the vehicle's basic information (make, model, year, etc.).
3. Evaluate the interior condition of the vehicle and select the corresponding options for each section (e.g., "Excellent", "Fair", "Poor" for the "Interior Condition" section).
4. Document any notable issues or observations in the "Comments" field.
5. Take photos of the vehicle's interior to provide evidence of any issues.
6. Fill out any other required fields (e.g., "Vehicle ID", "Submission Date", etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Info**: Enter the vehicle's basic information, including make, model, year, and other relevant details.
* **Interior Condition**: Evaluate the interior condition of the vehicle and select the corresponding option (Excellent, Fair, Poor).
* **Wear and Tear**: Identify any notable wear and tear issues with the vehicle's interior.
* **Damage**: Document any damage to the vehicle's interior.
* **Odors**: Record any notable odors or issues with the vehicle's odors.
* **Stains**: Document any stains or issues with the vehicle's interior.
* **Wear on Leathers**: Evaluate the wear on the vehicle's leather seats.
* **Wear on Upholstries**: Evaluate the wear on the vehicle's upholstery.
* **Wear on Plastics**: Evaluate the wear on the vehicle's plastic components.
* **Wear on Flooring**: Evaluate the wear on the vehicle's flooring.
* **Wear on Headliner**: Evaluate the wear on the vehicle's headliner.
* **Wear on Steering Wheel**: Evaluate the wear on the vehicle's steering wheel.
* **Cleanliness**: Evaluate the overall cleanliness of the vehicle's interior.
* **Odor Level**: Record the level of odor present in the vehicle's interior.
* **Comments**: Document any notable issues or observations.
* **Photos**: Take photos of the vehicle's interior to provide evidence of any issues.
* **Vehicle ID**: Enter the vehicle's ID number.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submission By**: Enter the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form used.
* **User Info**: Enter any additional user information.
* **Submission Type**: Enter the type of submission (e.g., assessment, inspection, etc.).

Please note that each field has specific options or requirements based on its type. Ensure you understand the specific requirements for each field before completing the form.
