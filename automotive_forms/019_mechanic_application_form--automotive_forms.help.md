# Mechanic Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your mechanical experience, skills, and availability for potential mechanic positions at our shop. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to help us get started.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling out the required fields (marked with \*\*).
2. Enter your Shop Name, which is the name of your current or previous workplace.
3. Enter your Contact Name and valid Email to help us contact you.
4. Provide your phone number, including the country code.
5. If you have any mechanical experience, enter the number of years you've been working in the trade.
6. Enter your job title at your current or previous workplace.
7. (Optionally) Briefly describe your job responsibilities.
8. Choose your highest level of education completed.
9. List any relevant mechanical skills you have.
10. (Optionally) Select your availability for work (if available).
11. (Optionally) If applicable, select your work hours.
12. (Optionally) Choose where you'd like to work, either on-site or remotely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shop Name** (`shop_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current or previous workplace.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents or your preferred name to be contacted by.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number, including country code.
* **Years of Experience** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you've been working in the trade if you have any experience.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your title at your current or previous workplace.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your job responsibilities.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Choose your highest level of education completed, such as High School, Some College, College, Associate, Bachelor, or Masters.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant mechanical skills you have.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): Select your availability for work if you have a specific date or period you're available.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, optional): If you have a fixed work schedule, select your work hours.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, select_one, required): Choose where you'd like to work: on-site or remotely.
