# motorcycle_license_application_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The motorcycle license application form is designed to gather information from individuals who wish to obtain a motorcycle license. This form will be reviewed by licensing authorities to process the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field.
5. Choose your **City** from the list of options.
6. Choose your **State** from the list of options.
7. Enter your **Zip Code** in the "Zip Code" field.
8. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
9. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
10. Enter your **Rider License Number** in the "Rider License Number" field.
11. Choose your **Motorcycle License Type** from the list of options.
12. Choose your **Vehicle Class** from the list of options.
13. Enter your **Motorcycle License Number** in the "Motorcycle License Number" field.
14. Enter your **Motorcycle License Issue Date** in the "Motorcycle License Issue Date" field.
15. Enter your **Motorcycle License Expiration Date** in the "Motorcycle License Expiration Date" field.
16. Choose your **Motorcycle License Status** from the list of options.
17. Choose your **Insurance Policy Status** from the list of options.
18. Enter your **Insurance Policy Issue Date** in the "Insurance Policy Issue Date" field.
19. Enter your **Insurance Policy Expiration Date** in the "Insurance Policy Expiration Date" field.
20. Enter your **Vehicle Registration Date** in the "Vehicle Registration Date" field.
21. Choose your **Vehicle Registration Status** from the list of options.
22. Choose your **Vehicle License Renewal Status** from the list of options.
23. Choose your **Vehicle License Cancellation Status** from the list of options.
24. Enter your **Vehicle License Cancellation Date** in the "Vehicle License Cancellation Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year.
* **Address** (id: 4, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **City** (id: 5, text, required): Enter the city of your address.
* **State** (id: 6, select_one, required): Choose the state of your address.
* **Zip Code** (id: 7, number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone Number** (id: 8, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (id: 9, email, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Rider License Number** (id: 10, text, required): Enter your rider's license number.
* **Motorcycle License Type** (id: 11, select_one, required): Choose the type of motorcycle license you are applying for.
* **Vehicle Class** (id: 12, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable vehicle classes.
* **Motorcycle License Number** (id: 13, text, required): Enter your motorcycle license number.
* **Motorcycle License Issue Date** (id: 14, date, required): Enter the date the license was issued.
* **Motorcycle License Expiration Date** (id: 15, date, required): Enter the date the license is set to expire.
* **Motorcycle License Status** (id: 16, select_multiple, required): Choose your license status.
* **Insurance Policy Status** (id: 17, select_multiple, required): Choose your insurance policy status.
* **Insurance Policy Issue Date** (id: 18, date, required): Enter the date the insurance policy was issued.
* **Insurance Policy Expiration Date** (id: 19, date, required): Enter the date the insurance policy is set to expire.
* **Vehicle Registration Date** (id: 20, date, required): Enter the date the vehicle was registered.
* **Vehicle Registration Status** (id: 21, select_multiple, required): Choose your vehicle registration status.
* **Vehicle License Renewal Status** (id: 22, select_multiple, required): Choose your vehicle license renewal status.
* **Vehicle License Cancellation Status** (id: 23, select_multiple, required): Choose your vehicle license cancellation status.
* **Vehicle License Cancellation Date** (id: 24, date, required): Enter the date the vehicle license was cancelled.
