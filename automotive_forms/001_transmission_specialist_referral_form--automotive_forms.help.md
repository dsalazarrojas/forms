# Transmission Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transmission Specialist Referral Form is used to refer a vehicle to a transmission specialist for repair or maintenance. This form captures essential information about the vehicle and the issue being referred. The form helps transmission specialists assess and provide accurate estimates and schedules for repairs. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields. 
2.  Enter your email address and phone number in the Email and Phone fields, respectively.
3.  Enter your address in the Address field.
4.  Select the type of transmission problem you are experiencing in the Transmission Type field.
5.  Enter the year of your vehicle in the Vehicle Year field.
6.  Enter the make and model of your vehicle in the Vehicle Make and Vehicle Model fields, respectively.
7.  Briefly describe the transmission problem in the Transmission Problem field.
8.  Select the type of repair or maintenance needed in the Repair Type field.
9.  Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or both) in the Contact Method field.
10. Enter any additional comments or notes in the Comments field.
11. Choose a technician to assign this referral to in the Assigned Technician field.
12. Set the completed date and due date for the referral in the Completed and Due Date fields, respectively. 
13. Select the tool or equipment to be used in the Assigned Tool field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (Text, Required): This field is for your first name, please enter your first name here.
*   **Last Name** (Text, Required): This field is for your last name, please enter your last name here.
*   **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address here so we can keep in touch with you.
*   **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number here so we can contact you easily.
*   **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address, this will help us to locate you for repair.
*   **Transmission Type** (Text, Required): Select the type of transmission issue you are facing.
*   **Vehicle Year** (Number, Required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Make** (Text, Required): Enter the make of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Model** (Text, Required): Enter the model of your vehicle.
*   **Transmission Problem** (Text, Optional): Briefly describe the transmission problem you are facing.
*   **Repair Type** (Text, Required): Select the type of repair or maintenance you are seeking.
*   **Referral** (Text, Required): Please provide any relevant information about the referral.
*   **Contact Method** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
*   **Comments** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
*   **Assigned Technician** (Text, Optional): Choose a technician to assign this referral to.
*   **Completed** (Time, Optional): Set the completed date for the referral.
*   **Due Date** (Date, Optional): Set the due date for the referral.
*   **Submitted By** (Text, Required): Enter your name here.
*   **Assigned Tool** (Text, Required): Select the tool or equipment to be used.
*   **Created At** (Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated when the form is created.
*   **Updated At** (Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated when the form is updated.
*   **Completed At** (Date, Optional): This field is populated when the referral is completed.
*   **Submitted At** (Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated when the form is submitted.
*   **Submitted By ID** (Text, Optional): This field is automatically populated when the form is submitted.
*   **Assigned To** (Text, Optional): This field is automatically populated when the form is assigned to a technician. 

## Tips
*   Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
*   Double-check your information before submitting.
*   Make sure to provide any relevant information to help us better assist you.
*   If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
