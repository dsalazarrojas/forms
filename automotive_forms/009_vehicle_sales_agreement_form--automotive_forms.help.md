# vehicle-sales-agreement-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle-sales-agreement-form is a form used to collect information from buyers and sellers involved in a vehicle sale. This includes contact details, vehicle information, and payment terms. The form is likely used for internal record-keeping in an automotive sales department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the buyer's contact information in the "Buyer Information" field.
2. Provide the vehicle's identification details in the "Vehicle Identification" field.
3. Describe the vehicle's make, model, and year in the "Vehicle Make and Model" field.
4. Enter the seller's contact information in the "Seller Information" field.
5. Check and select the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
6. Enter the vehicle's price in the "Vehicle Price" field.
7. List any additional features of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Features" field.
8. Sign the form with the buyer's signature in the "Buyer Signature" field.
9. Sign the form with the seller's signature in the "Seller Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Buyer Information** (`Buyer Information`, text, required: false): Enter your contact details here, including your name, address, and phone number.
* **Vehicle Identification** (`vehicle_identification`, number, required: false): This field is likely for the vehicle's unique identifier (e.g., VIN).
* **Vehicle Make and Model** (`vehicle_make_model_year`, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of the vehicle, including make and model year.
* **Seller Information** (`seller_information`, text, required: false): Enter the seller's contact details here, including their name, address, and phone number.
* **Buyer Agreement** (`buyer_agreement`, number, required: false): This field is not well-defined, but it may be for a buyer agreement or a checkbox to indicate the buyer's consent.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the payment term options (e.g., cash, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Vehicle Price** (`vehicle_price`, number, required: false): Enter the vehicle's price here.
* **Vehicle Features** (`vehicle_features`, text, required: false): List any additional features of the vehicle (e.g., sunroof, GPS, etc.).
* **Buyer Signature** (`buyer_signature`, time, required: false): Sign this field with your signature as the buyer.
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, time, required: false): Sign this field with your signature as the seller.
