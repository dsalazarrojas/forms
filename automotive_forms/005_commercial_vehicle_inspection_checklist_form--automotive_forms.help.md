# Commercial Vehicle Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Commercial Vehicle Inspection Checklist Form is used to document the condition and status of commercial vehicles during regular inspections. This form helps inspectors and drivers keep track of the vehicle's condition and ensures that any issues are reported and addressed in a timely manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Review the vehicle's information (vehicle ID number, make, model, year, and mileage) to ensure it is accurate.
* Record the date and time of the inspection.
* Enter the name and ID of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* Check the vehicle's exterior and interior for any issues or damage.
* Evaluate the condition of the vehicle's lights, mirrors, tires, and brake system.
* Check the engine performance and fluid levels.
* Record your overall inspection result and any additional notes or comments.
* Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Inspection Information
* **Inspection Information**: This section captures basic details about the inspection, including the inspector's contact information and vehicle information.
* **Date of inspection**: This field is used to record the date of the inspection.
* **Time of inspection**: This field is used to record the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name**: This field is used to record the full name of the inspector.
* **Inspector ID**: This field is used to record the inspector's ID number.

### 2. Vehicle Information
* **Vehicle ID Number**: This field is used to record the vehicle's VIN or fleet ID number.
* **License Plate**: This field is used to record the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Make**: This field is used to record the manufacturer of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: This field is used to record the model of the vehicle.
* **Year**: This field is used to record the year of manufacture.
* **Mileage**: This field is used to record the current mileage.

### 3. Vehicle Status
* **Vehicle Type**: This field is used to categorize the type of commercial vehicle (e.g. Box Truck, Semi-Truck, etc.).
* **Exterior Inspection**: This section captures a note on the external vehicle condition.
* **Body Condition**: This field is used to evaluate the overall body and paint condition of the vehicle.
* **Tires Condition**: This field is used to check the tire tread and condition.
* **Lights Functional**: This field is used to check if all lights are working properly.
* **Mirrors Condition**: This field is used to evaluate the condition and adjustment of the mirrors.
* **Interior Inspection**: This section captures a note on the internal vehicle condition.
* **Dashboard Warnings**: This field is used to check for any warning lights on the dashboard.
* **Seatbelts Condition**: This field is used to check the seatbelt functionality.
* **Mechanical Inspection**: This section captures a note on the mechanical systems check.
* **Engine Condition**: This field is used to evaluate the engine performance and leaks.
* **Brake System**: This field is used to evaluate the brake condition and performance.
* **Fluid Levels**: This field is used to check all fluid levels.
* **Overall Inspection Result**: This field is used to determine the final inspection result.

### 4. Additional Information
* **Additional Notes**: This field is used to capture any additional comments or observations.
* **Next Inspection Due**: This field is used to record the date of the next scheduled inspection.

## Tips
* Make sure to thoroughly inspect the vehicle to ensure accurate and detailed information.
* Use the "Overall Inspection Result" field to summarize the findings and determine the next course of action.
* Keep the "Additional Notes" field for any additional comments or observations that may be helpful for future reference.
