# Pump Prestart Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pump Prestart Checklist is used to ensure that all necessary steps are taken before starting the pump, reducing the risk of accidents and errors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand the questions and instructions.
2. Answer all required fields (indicated by a ***) to ensure you have completed all necessary steps.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Page 1: pump_prestart_checklist_page_1**: Read and understand the prestart checklist instructions.
* **Page 2: pump_prestart_checklist_page_2**: Enter any relevant information or comments about the pump.
* **Page 3: pump_prestart_checklist_page_3**: Select the correct option for your specific situation:
	+ Option 1: This option is for situations where the pump is being started for the first time.
	+ Option 2: This option is for situations where the pump is being restarted after a shutdown.
	+ Option 3: This option is for situations where the pump is being operated in a specific mode.
* **Page 4: pump_prestart_checklist_page_4**: Enter the date and time you started the pump.
* **Page 5: pump_prestart_checklist_page_5**: Enter the time you started the pump (in 24-hour format).
* **Page 6: pump_prestart_checklist_page_6**: Select the relevant options for your specific situation:
	+ Option 1: This option is for situations where certain features are enabled.
	+ Option 2: This option is for situations where certain features are disabled.
	+ Option 3: This option is for situations where the pump is being operated in a specific mode.
* **Page 7: pump_prestart_checklist_page_7**: Enter a number to indicate the pump's operating condition.
* **Page 8: pump_prestart_checklist_page_8**: Enter a number to indicate the pump's operating condition (same as page 7).
* **Page 9: pump_prestart_checklist_page_9**: Enter any additional information about the pump.
* **Page 10: pump_prestart_checklist_page_10**: Enter the email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency. 

## Tips

* Make sure to review each page carefully before moving on to the next one.
* Use the available options to choose the most accurate answers for your situation.
* If you're unsure about any of the options, consult with your supervisor or colleague.
