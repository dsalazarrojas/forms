# vehicle-maintenance-and-repair-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request maintenance or repair for a vehicle. It is designed to collect relevant information about the vehicle and the repair request, including the vehicle's make, year, and type, as well as the date and time of the scheduled maintenance or repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the vehicle make from the drop-down menu or enter it manually.
2. Enter the vehicle year.
3. Enter the vehicle model.
4. Provide a description of the issue you are experiencing with your vehicle.
5. If you are a repair technician, indicate whether you are actively working on the vehicle.
6. Select the date and time for the scheduled maintenance or repair.
7. Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
8. Enter your vehicle's address, city, and state.
9. Select the status of your vehicle.
10. Select the status of the repair technician.
11. Select the status of the vehicle.
12. Provide any additional notes about the vehicle or repair request.
13. Confirm the vehicle type and subtype.
14. Confirm the vehicle make and color.
15. Enter the vehicle year for any additional maintenance or repair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Make** (`1`, `vehicle_make`, required): Enter the make of your vehicle, such as Toyota or Ford.
* **Vehicle Year** (`2`, `vehicle_year`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`3`, `vehicle_model`, required): Enter the model of your vehicle, such as Camry or Mustang.
* **Vehicle Year 2** (`4`, `vehicle_year_2`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle, to confirm the year of your vehicle.
* **Issue Description** (`5`, `issue_description`, required): Describe the issue you are experiencing with your vehicle.
* **Repair Technician** (`6`, `repair_technician`, required): If you are a repair technician, indicate whether you are actively working on the vehicle.
* **Schedule Date** (`7`, `schedule_date`, required): Select the date for the scheduled maintenance or repair.
* **Schedule Time** (`8`, `schedule_time`, required): Select the time for the scheduled maintenance or repair.
* **Vehicle Owner Name** (`9`, `vehicle_owner_name`, required): Enter your name as the owner of the vehicle.
* **Contact Email** (`10`, `contact_email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`11`, `contact_phone`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`12`, `address`, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`13`, `city`, required): Enter the city where the vehicle is located.
* **State** (`14`, `state`, required): Select the state where the vehicle is located.
* **Zip** (`15`, `zip`, required): Enter the zip code of your location.
* **Notes** (`16`, `notes`, required): Provide any additional notes about the vehicle or repair request.
* **Vehicle Status** (`17`, `vehicle_status`, required): Select the status of your vehicle.
* **Service Technician Status** (`18`, `service_technician_status`, required): Select the status of the repair technician.
* **Service Status Vehicle** (`19`, `service_status_vehicle`, required): Select the status of the vehicle.
* **Notes 2** (`20`, `notes_2`, required): Provide any additional notes about the vehicle or repair request.
* **Vehicle Type** (`21`, `vehicle_type`, required): Confirm the type of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Subtype** (`22`, `vehicle_subtype`, required): Confirm the subtype of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make 2** (`23`, `vehicle_make_2`, required): Confirm the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`24`, `vehicle_color`, required): Confirm the color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year 3** (`25`, `vehicle_year_3`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle for any additional maintenance or repair.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or assistance.
* Review and submit the form carefully before submitting it.
* Double-check the vehicle information to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
