# Aftermarket Information Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aftermarket Information Disclosure Form is a crucial document used to collect information about a company's or individual's request for aftermarket products or services. It helps us understand your requirements and ensure that we provide accurate and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately.**
2. **Review each field to ensure it's filled out correctly.**
3. **Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required)
* Enter the name of your company or business.

### 2. **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_name`, text, required)
* Provide the full name of the person to contact regarding this disclosure.

### 3. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required)
* Enter the business email address for correspondence.

### 4. **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required)
* Enter the business phone number for follow-up.

### 5. **Purpose of Information Request** (`disclosure_purpose_header`, note, not required)
* This field is not required but provides a brief description of the purpose of your request.

### 6. **Purpose of Request** (`request_purpose`, text, required)
* Briefly describe why you need this aftermarket information.

### 7. **Product Category** (`product_category`, select_one, required)
* Select the category of aftermarket products you're interested in from the options:
	+ Performance Parts
	+ Interior Accessories
	+ Exterior Modifications
	+ Electronics
	+ Maintenance Items
	+ Other

### 8. **Vehicle Make and Model** (`vehicle_make_model`, text, required)
* Specify the make and model of vehicles this information applies to.

### 9. **Intended Product Usage** (`product_usage_type`, select_multiple, optional)
* Choose how you plan to use the disclosed information:
	+ Product Development
	+ Marketing Research
	+ Competitive Analysis
	+ Quality Improvement
	+ Regulatory Compliance

### 10. **Estimated Annual Volume** (`estimated_annual_volume`, number, optional)
* Approximate quantity of products you expect to purchase annually (if applicable).

### 11. **Compliance Acknowledgment** (`compliance_acknowledgment`, select_one, required)
* Confirm that you will comply with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the use of this information:
	+ Yes
	+ No

### 12. **Information Confidentiality Agreement** (`information_confidentiality_agreement`, select_one, required)
* Confirm that you will maintain the confidentiality of any proprietary information shared:
	+ Agree
	+ Do Not Agree

### 13. **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional)
* Provide any other relevant information about your request.

### 14. **Authorized Signature** (`authorized_signature`, text, optional)
* Enter the digital signature of the authorized representative.

By following these steps, you'll be able to complete the Aftermarket Information Disclosure Form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
