# Vehicle Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle maintenance checklist form is designed to track and report on vehicle maintenance activities. It helps users document and prioritize maintenance tasks, identify potential issues, and schedule inspections. This form is intended for use in an organizational or industrial setting where vehicle maintenance is a regular concern.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Navigate to the form**: Locate the vehicle maintenance checklist form in your system or application.
2.  **Select the correct form page**: Depending on the form implementation, you may need to select the "Vehicle Maintenance Form" page from the available options.
3.  **Fill in the required and optional fields**: Complete all required fields and select from available options for optional fields.
4.  **Submit the form**: Once all fields are filled, submit the form to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vehicle Maintenance Form** (`id: 1`): This is the first page of the form, providing an overview of the vehicle maintenance activities.
*   **Vehicle Year** (`id: 2`): Enter the year of the vehicle to be maintained. This field helps in tracking maintenance activities for specific model years.
*   **Maintenance Frequency** (`id: 3`): Select the frequency of maintenance (New, Regular, or Annual) to schedule regular checks and repairs.
*   **Maintenance Status** (`id: 4`): Choose the maintenance status (Scheduled, Completed, or Due) to track the current state of maintenance activities.
*   **Maintenance Tasks** (`id: 5`): Enter a list of tasks that need to be performed for the vehicle maintenance.
*   **Maintenance Repairs** (`id: 6`): Document any repairs or issues identified during maintenance activities.
*   **Maintenance Cost** (`id: 7`): Record the cost associated with the maintenance tasks and repairs. This field is optional but helps with budgeting and cost tracking.
*   **Maintenance Description** (`id: 8`): Add any additional comments or descriptions about the maintenance activities.

## Tips
*   Be sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
*   Use the "New" option for newly purchased or newly maintained vehicles.
*   For regular or annual maintenance, select the corresponding frequency option.
*   Document all repairs and issues to track potential problems and plan for future maintenance.
