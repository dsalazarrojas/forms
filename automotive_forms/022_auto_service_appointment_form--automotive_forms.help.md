# Auto Service Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The auto service appointment form is designed to collect information from customers to schedule a service appointment at an auto service center. This form gathers details about the customer's vehicle, service requirements, and personal preferences to ensure a smooth and efficient service experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information about your vehicle, including year, make, model, and mileage.
3. Select the type of service you need, such as oil change, brake service, or tire rotation.
4. Choose your preferred appointment date and time.
5. Indicate your time flexibility and preferred service method.
6. Specify your communication preference and estimate approval.
7. Check the terms and conditions box to agree to the service terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for appointment confirmation.
- **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for appointment confirmation.
- **Vehicle Year** (`Year of vehicle`, `number`, required): Enter your vehicle's year of manufacture.
- **Vehicle Make** (`Manufacturer`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's make.
- **Vehicle Model** (`Model name`, `text`, required): Enter your vehicle's model name.
- **Vehicle Trim** (`Trim level`, `text`, optional): Enter your vehicle's trim level, if applicable.
- **Current Mileage** (`Odometer reading`, `number`, required): Enter your vehicle's current mileage.
- **Service Type** (`Type of Service`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you need, such as oil change, brake service, or tire rotation.
- **Type of Service** (`Select the service needed`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you need.
- **Additional Services** (`Any other services`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional services you may require.
- **Problem Description** (`Describe the issue`, `text`, optional): Describe any issues you are experiencing with your vehicle.
- **Symptoms** (`What are you experiencing`, `text`, optional): Describe the symptoms you are experiencing with your vehicle.
- **Preferred Date** (`First choice date`, `date`, required): Select your preferred appointment date.
- **Preferred Time** (`What time works best`, `time`, required): Select your preferred appointment time.
- **Alternate Date** (`Second choice`, `date`, optional): Select an alternate appointment date, if desired.
- **Time Flexibility** (`How flexible is your schedule`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how flexible you are with your appointment time.
- **Service Method** (`How will service be done`, `select_one`, required): Select the service method you prefer, such as drop off at the shop or wait in lobby.
- **Shuttle Service Needed** (`Do you need a ride`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you need a shuttle service.
- **Loaner Vehicle Request** (`Would you like a loaner`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you would like a loaner vehicle.
- **Priority Level** (`How urgent is this`, `select_one`, required): Select the priority level of your service appointment.
- **Customer History** (`Have you been here before`, `note`, optional): Describe your previous experience with the service center.
- **Are You a Previous Customer** (`Have you visited before`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have visited the service center before.
- **Customer ID** (`If you have one`, `text`, optional): Enter any customer ID you may have.
- **Date of Last Visit** (`When was your last service`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your last service visit.
- **Preferences** (`Your service preferences`, `note`, optional): Describe your service preferences.
- **Parts Preference** (`What type of parts`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of parts you prefer.
- **Communication Preference** (`How should we contact you`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be contacted.
- **Estimate Approval** (`How should we handle estimates`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to handle estimates.
- **Additional Notes** (`Anything else we should know`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information you feel is necessary.
- **How Did You Hear About Us** (`How did you find us`, `select_one`, optional): Describe how you found the service center.
- **Service Reminders** (`Would you like reminders`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to be reminded about your appointment.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`I agree to the terms`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the terms and conditions of the service center.
