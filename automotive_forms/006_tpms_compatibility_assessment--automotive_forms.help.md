# tpms_compatibility_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tpms_compatibility_assessment form is designed to help assess the compatibility of a vehicle with a specific TPMS product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct vehicle details (page 1).
2. Choose the correct TPMS product (id 3).
3. Select the relevant services for your vehicle (id 4).
4. Enter the vehicle's year (id 5).
5. Enter the TPMS version (id 6).
6. Enter the service date for your vehicle (id 7).
7. Input the vehicle's mileage (id 8).
8. Add any additional notes about the vehicle (id 9).
9. Enter your contact information (id 10, id 11).
10. Choose the relevant category for your vehicle (id 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (id 1, text, required: false): This field is for entering the vehicle's make, model, and other essential details.
* **Vehicle Model** (id 2, text, required: false): Enter the specific model of the vehicle, e.g., Toyota Camry or Honda Civic.
* **TPMS Product** (id 3, select_one, required: false): Select the correct TPMS product that you want to assess the compatibility for.
* **Service Type** (id 4, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the services that apply to your vehicle, such as oil change or tire rotation.
* **Vehicle Year** (id 5, number, required: false): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **TPMS Version** (id 6, text, required: false): Enter the version of the TPMS product.
* **Service Date** (id 7, date, required: false): Enter the date of the last service for your vehicle.
* **Mileage** (id 8, text, required: false): Enter the current mileage of your vehicle.
* **Notes** (id 9, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the vehicle.
* **Email** (id 10, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (id 11, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Category** (id 12, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the category that best describes your vehicle.
