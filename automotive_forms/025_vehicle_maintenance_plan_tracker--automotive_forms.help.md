# Vehicle Maintenance Plan Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record key information about a vehicle's maintenance plan and history. It helps users keep track of a vehicle's condition, upcoming maintenance needs, and service history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle's basic information:
	* Vehicle Maintenance: Enter a brief description of the vehicle's maintenance history and needs.
	* Service History: Provide a short summary of the vehicle's service history.
2. Schedule Upcoming Maintenance:
	* Upcoming Maintenance: Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance or service.
3. Report and Track:
	* Report: Enter any relevant notes or comments about the vehicle's condition or maintenance.
4. Identify the Vehicle:
	* Year: Enter the vehicle's model year.
	* Make: Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
	* Model: Enter the vehicle's model (e.g., Camry, F-150, etc.).
	* Registration Number: Enter the vehicle's registration number.
5. Set Vehicle Status:
	* Ownership Status: Select the status of the vehicle (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Maintenance** (`vehicle_maintenance`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the vehicle's maintenance history and needs.
* **Service History** (`service_history`, text, required/optional): Provide a short summary of the vehicle's service history.
* **Upcoming Maintenance** (`upcoming_maintenance`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance or service.
* **Report** (`report`, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments about the vehicle's condition or maintenance.
* **Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's model year.
* **Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's model (e.g., Camry, F-150, etc.).
* **Registration Number** (`vehicle_registration_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Ownership Status** (`vehicle_ownership_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select the status of the vehicle (Active or Inactive).

## Tips
* This form should only be completed by authorized personnel.
* If the vehicle's maintenance history is not well-documented, consider providing a clear and concise summary.
* Ensure that all relevant notes and comments are entered in the Report field.

Please note that this form is intended to be simple and easy to use. If you are unsure about any field or require further clarification, please refer to the form's creator or the relevant documentation.
