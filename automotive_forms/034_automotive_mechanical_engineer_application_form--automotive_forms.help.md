# Automotive Mechanical Engineer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from applicants to support the hiring process for an Automotive Mechanical Engineer position. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the best evaluation of your qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please ensure that you have received an invitation or have been granted access to this form before starting your application. 
2.  Carefully review each section of the form, and complete all required fields marked as "True" in the form schema.
3.  Double-check that your email address, phone number, and other contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
4.  Be honest and precise in your descriptions of your education, experience, technical skills, and areas of specialization.
5.  Use specific examples to illustrate your experience and areas of specialization.
6.  Proofread your application before submitting to avoid errors and ensure clarity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in your application.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your current and active email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your active phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current address for mailing and other communication purposes.
* **Highest Degree** (`highest_degree`, select_one, required): Select your highest degree earned (Bachelor of Science, Master of Science, PhD, or Professional Degree).
* **Major/Field of Study** (`major`, text, required): Enter the specific major or field of study you pursued in your highest degree.
* **University or Institution** (`university`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution where you earned your highest degree.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, required): Enter the year you graduated with your highest degree.
* **GPA (if above 3.0)** (`gpa`, number, optional): Enter your grade point average if it's above 3.0.
* **Years of Mechanical Engineering Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the total years of experience in mechanical engineering.
* **Years of Automotive Industry Experience** (`automotive_years`, number, required): Enter the total years of experience in the automotive industry.
* **Current or Most Recent Employer** (`current_employer`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or most recent employer.
* **CAD Software Proficiency** (`cad_software`, select_multiple, required): Select all CAD software you are proficient in (e.g., AutoCAD, SolidWorks, CATIA, Pro/ENGINEER, Fusion 360, NX, Other).
* **Simulation and Analysis Tools** (`simulation_tools`, text, optional): Enter any simulation and analysis tools you are proficient in (e.g., ANSYS, CFX, etc.).
* **Programming Languages Known** (`programming_languages`, text, optional): Enter any programming languages you are proficient in (e.g., C++, Python, MATLAB, etc.).
* **Areas of Specialization** (`specializations`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas of specialization you are interested in (e.g., Vehicle Dynamics, Powertrain, Chassis, Thermal Management, Emissions, Electric Vehicles, Autonomous Systems, Safety Systems, Manufacturing).
* **Professional Licenses or Certifications** (`professional_licenses`, text, optional): Enter any professional licenses or certifications you hold (e.g., PE, CSWA, etc.).
* **Why Are You Interested in This Position** (`interest_statement`, text, required): Enter a brief statement explaining why you are interested in this Automotive Mechanical Engineer position.

Please note that this help guide is a general reference and might need adjustments based on specific company or job requirements. Review the form carefully and follow the provided instructions for a successful application.
