# Vehicle Maintenance Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Maintenance Contract Form is a generic template designed to collect information about a vehicle's maintenance needs. This form is intended to be used in various contexts, including dealerships, repair shops, and maintenance facilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section, providing details about the vehicle's owner, such as name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2. In the vehicle information section, enter the vehicle's details, including make, model, year, and any other relevant information.
3. In the maintenance plan section, describe the maintenance needs of the vehicle, including the frequency and type of maintenance required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information (customer_info)**: Enter the details of the vehicle's owner, such as name, contact information, and any other relevant details. This field is optional.
* **Vehicle Information (vehicle_info)**: Provide details about the vehicle, including make, model, year, and any other relevant information. This field is optional.
* **Maintenance Plan (maintenance_plan)**: Describe the maintenance needs of the vehicle, including the frequency and type of maintenance required. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to provide accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a professional.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary information is provided.
