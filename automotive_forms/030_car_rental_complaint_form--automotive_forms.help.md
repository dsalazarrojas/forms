# Car Rental Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information about your experience with our car rental service, including any complaints or issues you experienced during your rental. Please fill out this form to help us understand what went wrong and how we can improve our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions in the following sections:

*   Customer Information
*   Rental Information
*   Complaint Details
*   Resolution and Desired Resolution
*   Follow-up Information

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information** (`first_name`, `text`, `true`): Fill in your first name as requested.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `true`): Enter your last name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `true`): Provide your email address for follow-up on your complaint.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `true`): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, `false`): Provide your street address, but this is optional.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, `false`): Enter your city of residence.
*   **State** (`state`, `text`, `false`): Enter your state or region.
*   **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, `false`): Enter your ZIP code.
*   **Rental Information** (`rental_information`, `note`, `false`): Describe your rental experience.
*   **Reservation Number** (`reservation_number`, `text`, `true`): Enter your reservation number.
*   **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, `date`, `true`): Choose the date you picked up the vehicle.
*   **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, `date`, `true`): Choose the date you returned the vehicle.
*   **Pickup Location** (`pickup_location`, `text`, `true`): Enter where you picked up the vehicle.
*   **Return Location** (`return_location`, `text`, `false`): Enter where you returned the vehicle (if applicable).
*   **Vehicle Category** (`vehicle_category`, `text`, `false`): If applicable, select the category of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Description** (`vehicle_description`, `text`, `false`): Enter the make and model of your vehicle.
*   **License Plate** (`license_plate`, `text`, `false`): Enter your license plate number (if available).
*   **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, `note`, `false`): Describe your complaint.
*   **Date of Incident** (`complaint_date`, `date`, `true`): Choose the date of your complaint.
*   **Time of Incident** (`complaint_time`, `time`, `false`): Enter the time of your complaint.
*   **Complaint Category** (`complaint_category`, `select_one`, `true`): Select the type of issue you experienced.
*   **Complaint Description** (`complaint_description`, `text`, `true`): Provide detailed description of your complaint.
*   **Location of Incident** (`issue_location`, `select_one`, `true`): Select where your issue occurred.
*   **Was Staff Involved** (`staff_involved`, `select_one`, `true`): Indicate if a staff member was involved.
*   **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of the staff member (if known).
*   **Staff Description** (`staff_description`, `text`, `false`): Describe the staff member's role in the incident.
*   **Vehicle Issues** (`vehicle_issues`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select all vehicle issues you experienced.
*   **Vehicle Issue Details** (`vehicle_issue_details`, `text`, `false`): Describe the vehicle issues you experienced.
*   **Service Issues** (`service_issues`, `select_multiple`, `false`): Select all service issues you experienced.
*   **Service Issue Details** (`service_issue_details`, `text`, `false`): Describe the service issues you experienced.
*   **Impact Level** (`impact_level`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose how your issue affected you.
*   **Was Resolution Attempted** (`resolution_attempted`, `select_one`, `true`): Indicate if you tried to resolve the issue.
*   **Resolution Details** (`resolution_details`, `text`, `false`): Describe how your issue was resolved.
*   **Desired Resolution** (`desired_resolution`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select the resolution you desire.
*   **Desired Resolution Details** (`desired_resolution_details`, `text`, `false`): Describe the resolution you desire.
*   **Financial Impact** (`financial_impact`, `number`, `false`): Enter any financial loss you experienced.
*   **Evidence Available** (`evidence_available`, `select_one`, `false`): Indicate if you have evidence.
*   **Previous Complaints** (`previous_complaints`, `select_one`, `false`): Indicate if you have made complaints before.
*   **Customer Status** (`customer_status`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose your relationship with us.
*   **Would Rent Again** (`would_rent_again`, `select_one`, `true`): Indicate if you would rent from us again.
*   **Recommend to Others** (`would_recommend`, `select_one`, `true`): Indicate if you would recommend us to others.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, `false`): Enter any other information.
*   **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose your preferred contact method.
*   **Expected Response Timeframe** (`response_timeframe`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose when you expect a response.
*   **Complaint Priority** (`complaint_priority`, `select_one`, `true`): Choose the priority level of your complaint.
*   **Signature Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm that the information is accurate.
*   **Complaint Submission Date** (`complaint_date_submitted`, `date`, `true`): This date is automatically populated and should be confirmed.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form.
*   Attach any relevant evidence (photos, videos, or documents) that can support your complaint.
*   Please review all fields carefully before submitting.
