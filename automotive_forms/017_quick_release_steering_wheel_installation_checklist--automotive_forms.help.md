# quick_release_steering_wheel_installation_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The quick release steering wheel installation checklist is used to guide mechanics and customers through the process of installing a quick release steering wheel on a vehicle. This form captures essential details for accurate records, ensuring correct installation and any potential issues are addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the "Make" of the vehicle from the available options.
2. Enter the "Model Year" of the vehicle.
3. Choose the vehicle's "Vehicle Type" (e.g., 4WD, FWD, RWD, AWD, AT, MT).
4. Select the "Vehicle Condition" of the vehicle (e.g., new, used, certified preowned).
5. Choose the type of "Steering Wheel Type" (e.g., quick release, power locking, manual).
6. Select the "Installation Method" (e.g., bolt on, press fit, snap on).
7. Specify the "Installation Location" date.
8. Add any additional "Installation Notes" if necessary.
9. If not required, enter your "Customer Signature" (usually for verification or confirmation purposes).
10. Enter the "Mechanic Signature" for validation and confirmation.
11. Enter the "Date Installed" for accuracy and record-keeping.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Make**: The make of the vehicle. This is an essential field to ensure the correct steering wheel is selected and installed.
* **Model Year**: The model year of the vehicle, which helps in identifying the correct specifications and compatibility.
* **Vehicle Type**: The type of vehicle (e.g., 4WD, FWD, RWD, AWD, AT, MT). This selection will guide the installation process for the correct steering wheel configuration.
* **Vehicle Condition**: The condition of the vehicle (e.g., new, used, certified preowned). This information affects the warranty and maintenance of the vehicle.
* **Steering Wheel Type**: The type of steering wheel to be installed (e.g., quick release, power locking, manual). This selection will impact the vehicle's overall performance and handling.
* **Installation Method**: The method of installing the steering wheel (e.g., bolt on, press fit, snap on). This information is crucial for ensuring accurate installation procedures.
* **Installation Location**: The date of installation, which ensures accurate record-keeping and future reference.
* **Installation Notes**: Any additional notes or comments about the installation process or any issues encountered.
* **Customer Signature**: An optional field for customers to provide their signature, usually for verification or confirmation purposes.
* **Mechanic Signature**: The required field for the mechanic to provide their signature, validating their involvement in the installation process.
* **Date Installed**: The date of installation, which is essential for maintaining accurate records and future reference.
Note: Please ensure all fields are filled as they are required for accurate and correct installation records. If unsure about any field, please consult the relevant documentation or seek guidance from the relevant authorities.
