# Parking Cost Estimator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Cost Estimator form is designed to help users estimate the cost of parking for a given scenario. This form will collect information about the parking start and end times, vehicle type, parking location, and other relevant details to provide an estimated total cost.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the parking start and end times from the provided time input fields.
2. Choose the type of vehicle from the dropdown menu.
3. Select the parking location from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the number of hours parked.
5. Enter the hourly rate for parking.
6. Select the estimated total cost based on the provided options.
7. Provide any additional comments or information about the parking scenario.
8. Enter your contact details to allow us to follow up on your estimated cost.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **start_time**: The time you started parking. This is a required field to accurately estimate the total parking cost.
* **end_time**: The time you ended parking. This is a required field to accurately estimate the total parking cost.
* **vehicle_type**: Select the type of vehicle you were driving or riding. This will help us estimate the correct parking rate for your vehicle.
* **parking_location**: Select the location where you parked. This will help us estimate the correct parking rates for that location.
* **hours_parked**: Enter the number of hours you parked. This field is required to calculate the total parking cost.
* **hourly_rate**: Enter the hourly rate for parking. This is a required field to calculate the total parking cost.
* **total_cost**: This field is automatically calculated based on the parking duration and hourly rate. You do not need to fill this in.
* **estimated_cost**: This is the estimated cost based on the parking duration and hourly rate. It is automatically calculated.
* **parking_fees**: Select the estimated parking fees based on the options provided. This field is required.
* **comments**: Any additional comments or information about your parking experience.
* **contact_name**: Enter your name to allow us to follow up on your estimated cost.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with any follow-up questions or estimates.
