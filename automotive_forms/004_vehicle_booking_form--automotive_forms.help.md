# Vehicle Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the user's vehicle and personal details for the purpose of booking a vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your vehicle's details in the **Vehicle Details** section.
2. Enter your personal contact information in the **Customer Info** section.
3. Provide any additional booking details in the **Booking Details** section.
4. Enter your payment information in the **Payment Info** section.
5. If you have any feedback or comments, please leave it in the **Review** section.
6. Enter your phone number and email in the **Customer Phone and Email** sections.
7. Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** section.
8. Finally, enter the vehicle ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (text): Enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
* **Customer Info** (text): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Booking Details** (text): Enter any additional information about your booking, such as the purpose of your trip or any special requests.
* **Payment Info** (text): Enter your payment method and any relevant payment details.
* **Review** (text): Leave any feedback or comments about your experience with our service.
* **Customer Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Customer Email** (email): This is a duplicate field for your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Vehicle ID** (number): Enter the vehicle ID number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth booking process.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team.
* Our service may require additional information or verification processes to confirm your booking.
