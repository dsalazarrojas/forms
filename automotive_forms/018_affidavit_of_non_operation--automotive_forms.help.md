# Affidavit Of Non Operation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to declare that a vehicle is not being operated on public roads due to various reasons such as mechanical issues, storage, financial reasons, or other reasons. The affiant is required to provide detailed information about the vehicle and make declarations about its status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your full name, address, and contact information in the "Affiant Information" section.
2. Enter the year, make, model, VIN, and license plate number of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Information" section.
3. Select the reason for non-operation (mechanical issues, storage/seasonal, financial reasons, replacement vehicle, inactive military duty, extended travel, insurance lapse, registration expired, or other) in the "Reason for Non-Operation" section.
4. If you selected "Other" as the reason for non-operation, provide more details in the "Details of Reason (Other)" field.
5. Enter the storage location of the vehicle in the "Storage Location" section.
6. Select whether the vehicle is currently insured or not in the "Insurance Status" section.
7. Select whether the vehicle's registration is current or not in the "Registration Status" section.
8. Declare that the vehicle has not been operated on public roads during the specified period and that no claims have been made regarding this vehicle during the non-operation period in the "Statement - Vehicle Not Operated" and "Statement - No Claims" sections, respectively.
9. Declare your intent to resume operation of the vehicle when the non-operation period ends in the "Statement - Intent to Resume Operation" section.
10. Declare that all information provided is true and accurate to the best of your knowledge in the "Statement - Accuracy Declaration" section.
11. Select whether this affidavit requires notarization in your jurisdiction in the "Notarization Required" section.
11. Provide your electronic signature in the "Affiant Signature" section.
12. Enter the date of signature in the "Date of Signature" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Affiant Information** (Full Name, Address, Phone Number, Email Address): Enter your name, address, and contact information.
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
* **Address**: Enter your current residential or mailing address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication (optional).
* **Vehicle Information** (Year, Make, Model, VIN, License Plate Number): Enter the year, make, model, VIN, and license plate number of the vehicle.
* **Year, Make, and Model**: Enter the year, make, and model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: Enter the 17-character VIN of the vehicle.
* **License Plate Number**: Enter the current license plate number of the vehicle.
* **State of Registration**: Select the state where the vehicle is registered.
* **Reason for Non-Operation**: Select the reason why the vehicle is not being operated.
* **Details of Reason (Other)**: If you selected "Other" as the reason for non-operation, provide more details.
* **Storage Location**: Enter the location where the vehicle is currently stored.
* **Insurance Status**: Select whether the vehicle is currently insured or not.
* **Registration Status**: Select whether the vehicle's registration is current or not.
* **Statement - Vehicle Not Operated**: Declare that the vehicle has not been operated on public roads during the specified period.
* **Statement - No Claims**: Declare that no claims have been made regarding this vehicle during the non-operation period.
* **Statement - Intent to Resume Operation**: Declare your intent to resume operation of the vehicle when the non-operation period ends.
* **Statement - Accuracy Declaration**: Declare that all information provided is true and accurate to the best of your knowledge.
* **Notarization Required**: Select whether this affidavit requires notarization in your jurisdiction.
* **Affiant Signature**: Provide your electronic signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of signature.
