# vehicle-insurance-extension-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "vehicle-insurance-extension-form" is designed to gather information about vehicle insurance policies and extend their coverage. This form helps users provide essential details to ensure accurate and efficient policy extension.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether you want to extend your vehicle insurance policy.
2.  Enter your vehicle's **Vehicle Identification** number to ensure accurate identification.
3.  Specify the **Policy Coverage Period** you want to extend your policy for.
4.  Enter the **New Coverage Start Date** for the extended policy period.
5.  Enter the **Policy Start Date** for the new coverage period.
6.  Enter the **Policy End Date** for the new coverage period.
7.  Select the **New Policy Coverage** type from the available options.
8.  Select the **Coverage Type** for the policy extension.
9.  Enter your **Vehicle Insurance Policy Number** for reference.
10. Enter the **Insurance Agent's Name** who will handle the policy extension.
11. If required, upload a **Vehicle Identification Document** for verification.
12. Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether your vehicle is **Active** or **Inactive**.
13. Enter the **Vehicle's Make** for accurate identification.
14. Enter the **Vehicle Model** for accurate identification.
15. Enter the **Vehicle Year of Manufacture** for accurate identification.
16. Select the **Vehicle Ownership Status** as **Active** or **Inactive**.
17. Enter the **Vehicle Ownership Duration** in years for accurate tracking.
18. Select the **Vehicle Usage Status** as **Active** or **Inactive**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Insurance Extension Form**: (`vehicle-insurance-extension-form`, select_one, required): Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether you want to extend your vehicle insurance policy.
*   **Vehicle Identification**: (`vehicle_insurance_vehicle_identification`, number, required): Enter your vehicle's unique identification number for accurate identification.
*   **Policy Coverage Period**: (`policy_coverage_period`, text, required): Specify the length of time you want to extend your policy for.
*   **New Coverage Start Date**: (`new_coverage_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the new coverage period.
*   **Policy Start Date**: (`policy_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the current policy period.
*   **Policy End Date**: (`policy_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the current policy period.
*   **New Policy Coverage**: (`new_policy_coverage`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of coverage you want for the extended policy period.
*   **Coverage Type**: (`coverage_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of coverage you want for the policy extension.
*   **Vehicle Insurance Policy Number**: (`vehicle_insurance_policy_number`, text, required): Enter your vehicle insurance policy number for reference.
*   **Insurance Agent's Name**: (`insurance_agents_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the insurance agent handling your policy extension.
*   **Vehicle Identification Document**: (`vehicle_identification_document`, note, required): If required, upload a document for vehicle identification.
*   **Vehicle Make**: (`vehicle_make`, select_one, required): Select the make of your vehicle for accurate identification.
*   **Vehicle Model**: (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's model for accurate identification.
*   **Vehicle Year of Manufacture**: (`vehicle_year_of_manufacture`, number, required): Enter your vehicle's year of manufacture for accurate identification.
*   **Vehicle Ownership Status**: (`vehicle_ownership_status`, select_one, required): Select whether your vehicle is **Active** or **Inactive**.
*   **Vehicle Ownership Duration**: (`vehicle_ownership_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of vehicle ownership in years.
*   **Vehicle Usage Status**: (`vehicle_usage_status`, select_one, required): Select whether your vehicle is **Active** or **Inactive**.
