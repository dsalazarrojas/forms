# Vehicle Maintenance Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vehicle Maintenance Subscription Form is designed to gather information from customers about their vehicle maintenance needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, select the "Vehicle Info" page and enter the vehicle's make, model, and year.
2. Next, select the "Customer Info" page and enter the customer's name, email, and phone number.
3. Finally, select the "Service Preferences" page and enter the customer's preferred service options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Info (Vehicle Info, text, required: false)**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, and year. This information is used to identify the vehicle for maintenance purposes.
* **Customer Info (Customer Info, text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's name, email, and phone number. This information is used to contact the customer and provide them with updates about their vehicle's maintenance.
* **Service Preferences (Service Preferences, text, required: false)**: Enter the customer's preferred service options. This information is used to determine the type of service the customer is interested in.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure all fields are complete and correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
