# Rally Video Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rally Video Upload Form is a tool for users to upload a video from their computer or mobile device, providing details about the video, and optionally selecting an event it's related to. This form helps ensure accurate and efficient video upload and metadata collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Upload Video**: Enter a brief title for your video in the "Upload Video" field.
2. **Duration**: If you know the video length, enter it in the "Duration" field.
3. **Upload Date**: Select the date your video was uploaded.
4. **Name**: Enter your name as it would appear on your profile.
5. **Email**: Enter your email address to help us contact you about your video.
6. **Event**: If your video is related to a specific event, enter the event name.
7. **Video File**: Click to select the video file from your computer or mobile device. Ensure it's a valid video file (MP4, MOV, AVI, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upload Video** (`user_data`, `text`, required/optional: optional): Enter the title of your rally video.
* **Duration** (`video_duration`, `number`, required/optional: optional): Enter the length of your rally video in minutes.
* **Upload Date** (`upload_date`, `date`, required/optional: optional): Select the date you uploaded your rally video.
* **Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required/optional: optional): Enter your name as it appears on your profile.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required/optional: optional): Enter your email address.
* **Event** (`event_name`, `text`, required/optional: optional): Select the event your rally video is related to.
* **Video File** (`video_file`, `text`, required/optional: optional): Click to select the video file from your computer or mobile device.
 

## Tips
- Ensure you have the required permission to upload videos.
- Only upload valid video files (MP4, MOV, AVI, etc.).
- Be aware that uploaded videos are subject to review and may be removed if they don't follow our guidelines.
