# electric_vehicle_test_drive_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electric_vehicle_test_drive_reservation_form is designed for customers to reserve a test drive for an electric vehicle. This form gathers relevant information from the customer, including their name, contact details, vehicle preferences, and other important details. It serves as a pre-registration step for potential customers who want to experience an electric vehicle before making a purchase.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Customer Email" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred vehicle make from the "Test Vehicle Make" dropdown list.
5. Choose your preferred vehicle model from the "Test Vehicle Model" dropdown list.
6. Schedule a test drive date and time in the "Test Drive Date" and "Test Drive Time" fields.
7. Select a test drive location from a list of available options.
8. Add any additional customer notes in the "Customer Notes" field.
9. Choose your preferred sales representative from the "Sales Rep" dropdown list.
10. Select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown list.
11. Schedule the test drive timeframe in the "Test Drive Timeframe" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Customer Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your personal email address that you want us to use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Test Vehicle Make**: Select the make of the electric vehicle you would like to test drive.
	+ Options: Audi, BMW, Mercedes, Toyota, Honda, Ford, Dodge, Tesla
* **Test Vehicle Model**: Select the model of the electric vehicle you would like to test drive.
	+ Options: A4, 5 series, GLE, CR-V, Accord, Focus, Charger, Model 3
* **Test Drive Date**: Choose a date for the test drive.
* **Test Drive Time**: Select a time for the test drive.
* **Test Drive Location**: Choose a location for the test drive.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional notes about your test drive preferences.
* **Sales Rep**: Choose your preferred sales representative.
	+ Options: Sales Rep 1, Sales Rep 2, Sales Rep 3, Sales Rep 4, Sales Rep 5
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact.
	+ Options: Phone, Email, In Person
* **Test Drive Timeframe**: Schedule the test drive timeframe.
