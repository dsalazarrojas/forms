# Auto Sales Customer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from customers who are interested in purchasing a vehicle from our dealership. It is used to qualify potential customers for financing, insurance, and other automotive services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide honest and truthful information.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (3, First Name, required): Your legal first name.
* **Middle Name** (4, Middle Name, optional): Your middle name (if applicable).
* **Last Name** (5, Last Name, required): Your legal last name.
* **Suffix** (6, Suffix, optional): Your suffix (e.g., Jr., Sr., etc.).
* **Date of Birth** (7, Date, required): Your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Social Security Number** (8, Text, required): Your social security number in XXX-XX-XXXX format.
* **Driver License Number** (9, Text, required): Your driver's license number.
* **License State** (10, Select One, required): The state that issued your driver's license.
* **License Expiration** (11, Date, required): When your license expires.
* **Email Address** (12, Email, required): Your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (13, Text, required): Your best contact number.
* **Street Address** (14, Text, required): Your current street address.
* **City** (15, Text, required): The city of your residence.
* **State** (16, Select One, required): The state of your residence.
* **ZIP Code** (17, Text, required): Your 5-digit ZIP code.
* **Time at Current Address** (18, Select One, required): How long you have lived at your current address.
* **Residence Type** (19, Select One, required): Your housing situation.
* **Monthly Housing Payment** (20, Number, required): Your monthly housing payment.
* **Employment Information** (21, Note, optional): Information about your employment details.
* **Employer Name** (22, Text, required): Your current employer's name.
* **Employer Address** (23, Text, optional): Your employer's address.
* **Job Title** (24, Text, required): Your job title.
* **Work Phone** (25, Text, optional): Your employer's phone number.
* **Length of Employment** (26, Select One, required): How long you have been employed.
* **Gross Annual Income** (27, Number, required): Your total income before taxes.
* **Other Income** (28, Number, optional): Additional income.
* **Vehicle Interest** (29, Note, optional): Information about the vehicle you are interested in.
* **Vehicle of Interest** (30, Text, required): The year, make, and model of the vehicle you are interested in.
* **Vehicle Type** (31, Select One, required): The type of vehicle you are interested in.
* **New or Used** (32, Select One, required): Whether you are looking for a new or used vehicle.
* **Budget Range** (33, Select One, required): Your price range for the vehicle.
* **Payment Method** (34, Select One, required): How you plan to pay for the vehicle.
* **Available Down Payment** (35, Select One, optional): The amount you can afford to pay as a down payment.
* **Do You Have a Trade-In** (36, Select One, required): Whether you have a trade-in vehicle.
* **Trade-In Details** (37, Text, optional): Information about the trade-in vehicle.
* **Credit Information** (38, Note, optional): Information about your credit status.
* **Estimated Credit Rating** (39, Select One, required): Your estimated credit score range.
* **Any Bankruptcies** (40, Select One, required): Whether you have filed for bankruptcy in the past 7 years.
* **Any Repossessions** (41, Select One, required): Whether you have had a repossessed vehicle in the past 5 years.
* **Personal References** (42, Text, optional): Name and phone number of your personal references.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (43, Select One, optional): How you found our dealership.
* **Best Time to Contact** (44, Select One, required): When we should contact you for follow-up.
* **Authorization** (45, Select One, required): Whether you authorize verification of your information.
* **Date** (46, Date, required): Today's date.

Please note that the form is subject to change, and fields may be added or removed as necessary. It is your responsibility to ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
