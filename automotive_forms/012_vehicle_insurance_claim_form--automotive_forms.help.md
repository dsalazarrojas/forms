# vehicle_insurance_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to submit a vehicle insurance claim. It collects details about the incident, policy holder, and vehicle involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle involved (Vehicle, Motorcycle, Boat, or Other).
2. Enter the date of the incident in the "Claim Date" field.
3. Provide the policy holder's name in the "Policy Holder Name" field.
4. Enter the policy holder's contact information in the "Policy Holder Contact" field.
5. Enter the policy number in the "Policy Number" field.
6. Enter the vehicle make in the "Vehicle Make" field.
7. Enter the vehicle model in the "Vehicle Model" field.
8. Provide the location of the incident in the "Incident Location" field.
9. Describe the vehicle damage in the "Vehicle Damage" field.
10. Enter the name of the insurance company in the "Insurance Company" field.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the adjuster in the "Adjuster Name" and "Adjuster Contact" fields.
12. Enter the year of the vehicle in the "Vehicle Year" field.
13. Enter the vehicle's mileage in the "Vehicle Mileage" field.
14. Enter the vehicle's license plate number in the "Vehicle License Plate" field.
15. Enter the total claim amount in the "Claim Amount" field.
16. Enter the policy holder's address in the "Policy Holder Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (<code>Vehicle Type</code>): Select the type of vehicle involved in the incident (Vehicle, Motorcycle, Boat, or Other).
* **Claim Date** (<code>Claim Date</code>): Enter the date when the incident occurred.
* **Policy Holder Name** (<code>Policy Holder Name</code>): Enter the name of the policy holder.
* **Policy Holder Contact** (<code>Policy Holder Contact</code>): Enter the contact information of the policy holder.
* **Policy Number** (<code>Policy Number</code>): Enter the policy number associated with the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (<code>Vehicle Make</code>): Enter the make of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Model** (<code>Vehicle Model</code>): Enter the model of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Incident Location** (<code>Incident Location</code>): Provide the location where the incident occurred.
* **Vehicle Damage** (<code>Vehicle Damage</code>): Describe the damage sustained by the vehicle.
* **Insurance Company** (<code>Insurance Company</code>): Enter the name of the insurance company.
* **Adjuster Name** (<code>Adjuster Name</code>): Enter the name of the adjuster handling the claim.
* **Adjuster Contact** (<code>Adjuster Contact</code>): Enter the contact information of the adjuster.
* **Vehicle Year** (<code>Vehicle Year</code>): Enter the year of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Mileage** (<code>Vehicle Mileage</code>): Enter the mileage of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (<code>Vehicle License Plate</code>): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Claim Amount** (<code>Claim Amount</code>): Enter the total amount of the claim.
* **Policy Holder Address** (<code>Policy Holder Address</code>): Enter the address of the policy holder.
