## Vehicle Accident Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a vehicle accident, including details about the vehicle involved, the accident itself, and any witnesses. The information collected will be used to aid in insurance claims and other relevant purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of vehicle involved in the accident using the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the type of accident that occurred using the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the location of the accident in the provided text field.
4. Select a date and time to report the accident.
5. Enter the color of the vehicle, if applicable.
6. Enter the year of the vehicle.
7. Enter the make and model of the vehicle involved.
8. Enter the name and contact information of any witnesses.
9. Indicate if the witness has a relationship with the driver or vehicle.
10. Enter the name of the insurance company and policy number, if applicable.
11. Enter any additional information or notes about the accident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`): Select the type of vehicle involved in the accident from the dropdown menu.
* **Accident Type** (`accident_type`): Select the type of accident that occurred from the dropdown menu.
* **Location** (`location`): Enter the location of the accident in this text field.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the accident using the date picker.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the accident using the time picker.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`): Enter the color of the vehicle, if applicable.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Enter the make of the vehicle involved.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Enter the model of the vehicle involved.
* **Witness** (`witness`): Enter the name and contact information of any witnesses.
* **Witness Relationship** (`witness_relationship`): Indicate if the witness has a relationship with the driver or vehicle.
* **Insurance Company** (`insurance_company`): Enter the name of the insurance company and policy number, if applicable.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`): Enter the policy number of the insurance company.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`): Select a date to report the accident using the date picker.
* **Report Time** (`report_time`): Select a time to report the accident using the time picker.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`): Enter the license plate number of the vehicle involved.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of the accident.
* **Damage Description** (`damage_description`): Enter a description of the damage caused by the accident.
* **Witness Contact Number** (`witness_contact_number`): Enter the contact number of the witness.
* **Witness Contact Email** (`witness_contact_email`): Enter the contact email of the witness, if applicable.
* **Witness Position** (`witness_position`): Enter the position of the witness, if applicable.
* **Witness Relationship to Driver** (`witness_relationship_to_driver`): Indicate if the witness has a relationship with the driver.
* **Witness Relationship to Vehicle** (`witness_relationship_to_vehicle`): Indicate if the witness has a relationship with the vehicle.
* **Police Report Number** (`police_report_number`): Enter the police report number, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional information or notes about the accident.
