# Vehicle Lease Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a lease extension for a leased vehicle. It is used to collect necessary information to update the lease agreement and ensure a smooth lease extension process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Vehicle Lease Date**: This field is for the date when the original vehicle lease began.
2. **Lease ID**: This is the unique identifier for your lease agreement. Please ensure you have this correct to avoid any issues.
3. **Customer Name**: This is your name as it appears on your lease agreement.
4. **Vehicle Year**: The year of the leased vehicle.
5. **Vehicle Make**: The manufacturer of the leased vehicle.
6. **Vehicle Model**: The specific model of the leased vehicle.
7. **Reason for Extension**: A brief explanation for your lease extension request.
8. **Customer Email**: Your email address as it appears on your lease agreement.
9. **Customer Phone**: Your phone number as it appears on your lease agreement.
10. **Lease Term Length**: The length of the lease extension you are requesting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Lease Date**: Enter the date when the original lease began. This will help us verify your lease history.
* **Lease ID**: A unique identifier for your lease agreement, ensure you have this correct to avoid any issues.
* **Customer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your lease agreement.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the leased vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the manufacturer of the leased vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the specific model of the leased vehicle.
* **Reason for Extension**: Provide a brief explanation for your lease extension request.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your lease agreement.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number as it appears on your lease agreement.
* **Lease Term Length**: Enter the length of the lease extension you are requesting.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any information, please double-check with your leasing representative or refer to your original lease agreement.
