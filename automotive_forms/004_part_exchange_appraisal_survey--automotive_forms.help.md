# Part Exchange Appraisal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Part Exchange Appraisal Survey" form is a tool designed to gather information from customers about a vehicle part exchange. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are collected to facilitate the appraisal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the basic vehicle details (step 1, vehicle_details)
2. Enter your details as an owner (step 2, owner_details)
3. Select the condition of the vehicle (step 3, vehicle_condition)
4. Choose the reason for the trade-in (step 4, trade_in_reason)
5. If applicable, add any additional details from the appraiser (step 5, appraiser_details)
6. Provide any submission comments or questions (step 6, submission_details)
7. Add any notes regarding the vehicle condition (step 7, vehicle_condition_note)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`):
	+ This field is where you enter the make, model, and year of your vehicle.
* **Owner Details** (`owner_details`):
	+ This field is where you enter your personal details as the vehicle owner.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`):
	+ Select the current condition of your vehicle, which can be Excellent, Good, or Fair.
* **Trade-in Reason** (`trade_in_reason`):
	+ Choose one or more options from the list to explain the reason for trading in your vehicle.
* **Additional Appraiser Details** (`appraiser_details`):
	+ If you have any additional information from the appraiser, enter it here.
* **Submission Details** (`submission_details`):
	+ Provide any comments or questions you have regarding the submission.
* **Vehicle Condition Note** (`vehicle_condition_note`):
	+ Add any notes about the vehicle condition, such as any damage or issues.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), to ensure that your appraisal is processed efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
