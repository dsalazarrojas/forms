# Vehicle Maintenance Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a vehicle maintenance claim for a vehicle. Please provide the required information to facilitate the maintenance process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle details, such as make, model, year, etc.
2. Select the type of maintenance claim.
3. Provide any additional comments or details regarding the maintenance claim.
4. Attach any relevant documents or images to support your claim.
5. Select the status of the claim from the options provided.
6. Provide your contact information, including phone number and email address.
7. Add any additional notes or comments to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Maintenance Claim Form Type**: Select the type of vehicle maintenance claim you are submitting. 
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Claimed At**: Enter the date when the vehicle maintenance was claimed.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Claimed By**: Enter your name or the name of the person claiming the maintenance.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Claimed By Phone**: Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person claiming the maintenance.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Claimed By Email**: Enter your email address or the email address of the person claiming the maintenance.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Claim Form Comments**: Add any additional comments or details regarding the maintenance claim.
* **Vehicle Maintenance Claim Form Attachment**: Select up to three attachments to support your maintenance claim.
