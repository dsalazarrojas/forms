# Utility Trailer Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from renters for a utility trailer rental. It helps to establish a rental agreement between the renter and the trailer provider.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name as the renter in the "Renter's Name" field.
2. Enter the start date of the rental in the "Start Date of Rental" field.
3. If applicable, enter the end date of the rental in the "End Date of Rental" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Renter's Name** (`renter_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as the renter who will be using the utility trailer.
- **Start Date of Rental** (`rental_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the rental starts. This is the date when you will take possession of the trailer.
- **End Date of Rental** (`rental_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the rental ends. If you don't know the exact end date, you can leave this field blank for now.
