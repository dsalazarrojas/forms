# Vehicle Transport Quote Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information to generate a quote for vehicle transport. Please fill in the required fields to provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of vehicle you are transporting: 
   - Please choose the type of vehicle from the select one dropdown menu. This will help us determine the size and weight of the vehicle to be transported.
2. Enter the origin state:
   - Type in the state from which the vehicle is being picked up or transported from.
3. Enter the origin city:
   - Type in the city from which the vehicle is being picked up or transported from.
4. Enter the destination state:
   - Type in the state to which the vehicle is being transported.
5. Enter the destination city:
   - Type in the city to which the vehicle is being transported.
6. Select the shipping date:
   - Choose the date on which the vehicle is to be shipped.
7. Select the shipping time:
   - Choose the time of day on which the vehicle is to be shipped.
8. Enter your contact name:
   - Please type in your name, this is for contact purposes.
9. Enter your contact email:
   - Please type in your email address, this will be used to contact you for further information.
10. Enter your contact phone number:
   - Please type in your phone number, this will be used to contact you for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle you are transporting (e.g. car, truck, van).
* **Origin State** (`origin_state`, text, optional): Type in the state from which the vehicle is being picked up or transported from.
* **Origin City** (`origin_city`, text, optional): Type in the city from which the vehicle is being picked up or transported from.
* **Destination State** (`destination_state`, text, optional): Type in the state to which the vehicle is being transported.
* **Destination City** (`destination_city`, text, optional): Type in the city to which the vehicle is being transported.
* **Ship Date** (`ship_date`, date, optional): Choose the date on which the vehicle is to be shipped.
* **Ship Time** (`ship_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of day on which the vehicle is to be shipped.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Type in your name, this is for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Type in your email address, this will be used to contact you for further information.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Type in your phone number, this will be used to contact you for further information.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out completely to get an accurate quote.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know through the contact information provided. 
- We strive to deliver accurate quotes, please contact us if there is any discrepancy.
