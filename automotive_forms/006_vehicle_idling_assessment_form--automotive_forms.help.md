# vehicle_idling_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle idling assessment form is designed to help assess the impact of vehicle idling on various environmental and health factors. This form helps to gather information on vehicle idling frequency, reason, and its effects on fuel consumption, air pollution, noise, vibration, heat, CO2 emissions, particulate matter, and NOx emissions. This form is typically used by fleet managers, fleet owners, or maintenance teams to assess and improve vehicle idling practices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Select a vehicle idling frequency**:
	* Choose one or more of the options to indicate how frequently the vehicle idles (e.g., idling a few minutes each day, idling an hour or more each day, idling several hours each day, or idling most of the day).
2. **Choose an idling reason**:
	* Select the reason for vehicle idling (e.g., Personal reasons, Work-related reasons, or Other reasons).
3. **Enter idling duration in minutes**:
	* If you've selected "idling a few minutes each day" or "idling an hour or more each day", estimate the average duration of idling in minutes.
4. **Assess idling impacts**:
	* For each question, select one or more of the options to indicate the impact of vehicle idling on:
		+ Fuel consumption (Low, Medium, High)
		+ Air pollution (Low, Medium, High)
		+ Noise (Low, Medium, High)
		+ Vibration (Low, Medium, High)
		+ Heat (Low, Medium, High)
		+ CO2 emissions (Low, Medium, High)
		+ Particulate matter (Low, Medium, High)
		+ NOx emissions (Low, Medium, High)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **vehicle idling frequency** (`vehicle_idling_frequency`): This question helps to determine how often a vehicle idles. Select one or more of the options to indicate the frequency of idling.
* **idling reason** (`idling_reason`): This question helps to determine the reason for vehicle idling. Select the reason that best describes the idling scenario.
* **idling duration** (`idling_duration`): This question helps to estimate the average duration of idling in minutes. Enter a numerical value for this field.
* **idling fuel consumption impact** (`idling_fuel_consumption_impact`): This question helps to assess the impact of vehicle idling on fuel consumption. Select one or more of the options to indicate the level of impact.
* **idling air pollution impact** (`idling_air_pollution_impact`): This question helps to assess the impact of vehicle idling on air pollution. Select one or more of the options to indicate the level of impact.
* **idling noise impact** (`idling_noise_impact`): This question helps to assess the impact of vehicle idling on noise. Select one or more of the options to indicate the level of impact.
* **idling vibration impact** (`idling_vibration_impact`): This question helps to assess the impact of vehicle idling on vibration. Select one or more of the options to indicate the level of impact.
* **idling heat impact** (`idling_heat_impact`): This question helps to assess the impact of vehicle idling on heat. Select one or more of the options to indicate the level of impact.
* **idling CO2 emissions** (`idling_co2_emissions`): This question helps to estimate the average CO2 emissions produced during vehicle idling in units of g/minute.
* **idling particulate matter** (`idling_particulate_matter`): This question helps to estimate the average particulate matter produced during vehicle idling in units of μg/m3.
* **idling NOx emissions** (`idling_nox_nox_emissions`): This question helps to estimate the average NOx emissions produced during vehicle idling in units of mg/Nm3.
* **idling PM2.5 emissions** (`idling_pm2_5_emissions`): This question helps to estimate the average PM2.5 emissions produced during vehicle idling in units of μg/m3.

## Tips

* Be accurate when estimating idling duration and impact values.
* Select all relevant impacts when assessing the effects of vehicle idling.
* Use this form to identify areas of improvement and optimize vehicle idling practices.
