# Rental Car Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rental Car Incident Form is designed to collect information about incidents or accidents involving rental cars. This form aims to gather details about the incident, including the date, location, and circumstances surrounding it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the incident in the **accident_date** field. This is a date field, so you can select a date from the calendar or enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2. In the **vehicle_registration_number** field, enter the vehicle's registration number. This is typically the license plate number of the rental car.
3. Describe the vehicle involved in the **vehicle_description** field. Please provide a brief description of the vehicle, including its make, model, and color.
4. Enter the location of the incident in the **accident_location** field. This is where the incident occurred.
5. Enter the time of the incident in the **accident_time** field. This is the time the incident occurred.
6. Provide a brief description of the incident in the **accident_description** field. This should include any relevant details about the incident.
7. Describe any damage to the vehicle in the **damage_description** field. If there was no damage, please indicate this in the field.
8. If there were witnesses, provide a brief statement about what they witnessed in the **witness_statement** field.
9. If you have any witness information, please provide it in the **witness_details** field.
10. If the witness is a person known to you, please enter their name in the **witness_name** field.
11. If the witness is a person you know, select their relation to you (e.g., driver, passenger, witness) in the **witness_relation** field.
12. If you have the witness's phone number, please enter it in the **witness_phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **accident_date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **vehicle_registration_number**: Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **vehicle_description**: Describe the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **accident_location**: Enter the location of the incident.
* **accident_time**: Enter the time the incident occurred.
* **accident_description**: Provide a brief description of the incident.
* **damage_description**: Describe any damage to the vehicle.
* **witness_statement**: If there were witnesses, provide a brief statement about what they witnessed.
* **witness_details**: If you have any witness information, please provide it.
* **witness_name**: If the witness is a person known to you, please enter their name.
* **witness_relation**: If the witness is a person you know, select their relation to you.
* **witness_phone**: If you have the witness's phone number, please enter it.

## Tips

* Ensure that you are not duplicating an existing report or incident.
* If you are unsure whether the incident has already been reported, please contact the relevant authorities or parties involved.
* Please provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
