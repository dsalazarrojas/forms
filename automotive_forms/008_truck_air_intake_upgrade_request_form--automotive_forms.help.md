# Truck Air Intake Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request upgrades to a truck's air intake system. It is a critical form that requires accurate and detailed information to ensure proper processing and fulfillment of the upgrade request. Please complete the form carefully and accurately to avoid delays or errors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Upgrade Request**: Enter a brief description of the upgrade request, including the type and purpose of the upgrade.
2. **Shop**: Select the shop that will be performing the upgrade from the dropdown menu.
3. **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle that requires the upgrade from the dropdown menu.
4. **Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the upgrade.
5. **Upload File**: Upload any relevant files or images related to the upgrade request.
6. **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the upgrade request (optional).
7. **Service Type**: Select the type of service required for the upgrade from the dropdown menu.
8. **Service Date**: Enter the date you would like to schedule the service.
9. **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about the service (optional).
10. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
11. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
12. **Service Team**: Select the service team that will be performing the upgrade from the dropdown menu.
13. **Fleet Operator**: Select the fleet operator that owns the vehicle from the dropdown menu.
14. **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle that requires the upgrade.
15. **Vehicle Make**: Select the make of the vehicle that requires the upgrade from the dropdown menu.
16. **Vehicle Model**: Select the model of the vehicle that requires the upgrade from the dropdown menu.
17. **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle that requires the upgrade.
18. **Shop Address**: Enter the address of the shop that will be performing the upgrade.
19. **Shop City**: Enter the city of the shop that will be performing the upgrade.
20. **Shop Postal**: Enter the postal code of the shop that will be performing the upgrade.
21. **Shop Phone**: Enter the phone number of the shop that will be performing the upgrade.
22. **Shop Country**: Select the country of the shop that will be performing the upgrade from the dropdown menu.
23. **Customer Signature**: Enter your signature as confirmation of the upgrade request.
24. **Shop Signature**: Enter the shop's signature as confirmation of the upgrade request.
25. **Upload Additional File**: Upload any additional files or images related to the upgrade request (optional).

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid delays or errors.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authorities or shop for guidance.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure everything is accurate.

Note: This help guide assumes the user is familiar with the concept of a truck's air intake system and the purpose of the form.
