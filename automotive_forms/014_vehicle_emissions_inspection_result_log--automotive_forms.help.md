# Vehicle Emissions Inspection Result Log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Emissions Inspection Result Log form is used to record the results of a vehicle emissions inspection, which is a mandatory process in the automotive industry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct vehicle year from the list.
2. Choose the vehicle make from the options listed.
3. Select the correct vehicle model from the list of options.
4. Enter the number of miles the vehicle has been driven.
5. Enter the inspection date.
6. Select whether the vehicle was inspected at a station or not.
7. Record the results of the inspection.
8. Enter the date the vehicle was reinspected.
9. Sign off on the inspection.
10. Select the status of the vehicle inspection.
11. If necessary, enter the reason for reinspection.
12. Enter the date of retest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Emissions Inspection Result Log** (`vehicle_emissions_inspection_result_log`): A brief description of the form's purpose and the vehicle being inspected.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`): The year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`): Select the make of the vehicle from the list of options.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`): Select the model of the vehicle from the list of options.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`): This field is not essential, but it's good to have this information on record.
* **Vehicle Year Miles** (`vehicle_year_miles`): The number of miles the vehicle has been driven.
* **Vehicle Inspection Date** (`vehicle_inspection_date`): The date the vehicle was inspected.
* **Vehicle Inspection Station** (`vehicle_inspection_station`): Select whether the vehicle was inspected at a station or not.
* **Vehicle Inspection Results** (`vehicle_inspection_results`): Record the results of the inspection.
* **Remarks** (`vehicle_remarks`): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle.
* **Remarks 2** (`vehicle_remarks2`): Enter any additional comments or remarks about the vehicle.
* **Emissions Test Result 1** (`vehicle_emissions_test_result1`): Record the result of the first emissions test.
* **Emissions Test Result 2** (`vehicle_emissions_test_result2`): Record the result of the second emissions test.
* **Emissions Test Result 3** (`vehicle_emissions_test_result3`): Record the result of the third emissions test.
* **Emissions Test Result 4** (`vehicle_emissions_test_result4`): Record the result of the fourth emissions test.
* **Inspection By** (`vehicle_inspection_by`): Select who conducted the inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Signature** (`vehicle_inspection_signature`): Sign off on the inspection.
* **Vehicle Inspection Status** (`vehicle_inspection_status`): Select the status of the vehicle inspection.
* **Reinspection Reason** (`vehicle_reinspection_reason`): Enter the reason for reinspection if necessary.
* **Reinspection Date** (`vehicle_reinspection_date`): Enter the date of retest.
* **Retest Date** (`vehicle_retest_date`): Enter the date of retest.
* **Test Cycle** (`vehicle_test_cycle`): Enter any additional information about the test cycle.
* **Test Cycle 2** (`vehicle_test_cycle2`): Enter any additional information about the test cycle.
* **Test Cycle 3** (`vehicle_test_cycle3`): Enter any additional information about the test cycle.
* **Test Cycle 4** (`vehicle_test_cycle4`): Enter any additional information about the test cycle.
