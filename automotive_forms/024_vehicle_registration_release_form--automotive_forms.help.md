# Vehicle Registration Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Registration Release Form is used by automotive businesses to document the release of vehicles from their inventory. This form provides a standardized way to report vehicle information, ownership status, lien status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you're filling this form for a vehicle that is being released from your inventory.
2. Enter the vehicle's details, including its make, model, and any relevant information.
3. Choose the correct ownership status: Active or Inactive.
4. Select the correct lien status: Active or Inactive.
5. Enter the release amount, if applicable.
6. Enter the release date.
7. Add any additional notes about the vehicle's release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_registration_release_form_vehicle_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the vehicle's make, model, and any other relevant details.
* **Vehicle Ownership Status** (`vehicle_registration_release_form_ownership_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether the vehicle is Active or Inactive.
* **Lien Status** (`vehicle_registration_release_form_lien_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select whether the vehicle has an Active or Inactive lien.
* **Release Amount** (`vehicle_registration_release_form_release_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of money released for the vehicle.
* **Release Date** (`vehicle_registration_release_form_release_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the vehicle was released from your inventory.
* **Notes** (`vehicle_registration_release_form_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the vehicle's release.
