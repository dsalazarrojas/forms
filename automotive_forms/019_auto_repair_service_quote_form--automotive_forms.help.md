# Auto Repair Service Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers for an auto repair service quote. It will help us provide an estimated cost for the service requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your vehicle, such as the year, make, model, and trim level.
3. Select the type of service you need (e.g., oil change, brake service, etc.).
4. Describe the issue you're experiencing with your vehicle.
5. Choose your preferred method of quote delivery and any additional services you'd like to include.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments about your service request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you'd like us to send the quote.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your street address (optional).
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your residence (optional).
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select your state of residence from the list.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your 5-digit ZIP code (optional).
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, required): Enter the manufacturer of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, required): Enter the model name of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Trim** (`vehicle_trim`, text, optional): Enter the trim level of your vehicle (optional).
* **Current Mileage** (`mileage`, number, required): Enter the current odometer reading of your vehicle.
* **VIN** (`vin`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (optional).
* **Service Information** (`service_info`, note, optional): Describe the service you need (optional).
* **Type of Service Needed** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of service you need (e.g., oil change, brake service, etc.).
* **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, text, required): Describe the issue you're experiencing with your vehicle.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any symptoms you're experiencing (optional).
* **Warning Lights On** (`warning_lights`, select_multiple, optional): Select any dashboard warning lights that are currently on.
* **Unusual Noises** (`noises`, select_multiple, optional): Select any unusual noises you're experiencing.
* **When Does Problem Occur** (`when_occurs`, select_one, optional): Select when the problem occurs (e.g., at startup, while idling, etc.).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Select how soon you need the service.
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Select a preferred date for the service.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select a preferred time for the service.
* **Service Location Preference** (`service_location`, select_one, optional): Select where you'd like the service to be performed.
* **Quote Preferences** (`quote_preferences`, note, optional): Indicate any preferences for the quote delivery method.
* **Quote Delivery Method** (`quote_delivery`, select_one, required): Select how you'd like to receive the quote.
* **Parts Preference** (`parts_preference`, select_one, optional): Select the type of parts you'd like to use (e.g., OEM, aftermarket, etc.).
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Select your expected budget range for the service.
* **Additional Services of Interest** (`additional_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional services you'd like to include.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Select how you found us (e.g., Google search, social media, etc.).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or notes about your service request.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we can provide an accurate quote.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to ask us.
* We will respond to your quote request as soon as possible.
* If you have any issues with your vehicle, be as detailed as possible when describing the problem.
