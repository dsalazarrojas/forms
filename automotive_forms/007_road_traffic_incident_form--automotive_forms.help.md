# Road Traffic Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Road Traffic Incident Form is used to collect information about road traffic incidents that have occurred on roads. This form helps gather data that will be used to improve road safety and identify areas where improvements can be made to reduce the occurrence of such incidents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Incident Date" and "Incident Time" fields, respectively.
3. Provide information about the vehicle involved in the incident, including its make, color, model, license plate, and year.
4. Describe the location where the incident occurred.
5. Enter the number of injuries and deaths resulting from the incident, if any.
6. Select the type of involvement (e.g., collision, pedestrian collision, etc.) that occurred.
7. Indicate whether the vehicle was damaged in any way.
8. Select the number of witnesses present during the incident.
9. Provide information about the weather conditions at the time of the incident.
10. Indicate whether it was night or day when the incident occurred.
11. Select the amount of sunlight available at the time of the incident.
12. Indicate whether there was street lighting available at the time of the incident.
13. Provide information about the vehicle license holder, including their name, phone number, and insurance status.
14. Enter the address of the vehicle license holder.
15. If a witness was present, enter their name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (date): The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Incident Time** (time): The time at which the incident occurred.
* **Vehicle Make** (text): The make of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Color** (text): The color of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Model** (text): The model of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (text): The license plate number of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Vehicle Year** (number): The year of the vehicle involved in the incident.
* **Incident Location** (text): The location where the incident occurred.
* **Incident Description** (text): A brief description of what happened.
* **Number of Injuries** (number): The number of people injured as a result of the incident.
* **Number of Deaths** (number): The number of people who died as a result of the incident.
* **Involvement Type** (select_one): The type of involvement (e.g., collision, pedestrian collision, etc.).
* **Vehicle Damage** (select_multiple): The parts of the vehicle that were damaged as a result of the incident.
* **Reported By** (select_one): Who reported the incident (e.g., driver, passenger, etc.).
* **Witnesses** (select_multiple): The number of people who witnessed the incident.
* **Weather** (select_one): The weather conditions at the time of the incident.
* **Night** (text): Whether it was night or day when the incident occurred.
* **Sunlight** (select_one): The amount of sunlight available at the time of the incident.
* **Street Lighting** (select_one): Whether there was street lighting available at the time of the incident.
* **Vehicle License Holder** (text): The name of the vehicle's license holder.
* **Vehicle License Holder Phone** (text): The phone number of the vehicle's license holder.
* **Vehicle License Holder Insurance** (select_multiple): Whether the vehicle's license holder has insurance.
* **Vehicle License Holder Address** (text): The address of the vehicle's license holder.
* **Witness Name** (text): The name of the witness, if applicable.
* **Witness Phone** (text): The phone number of the witness, if applicable.
