# Rock Chip Repair Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rock Chip Repair Acknowledgment Form is used to document the repair of a vehicle's "rock chip" (a minor scratch or blemish). This form is essential for the customer to acknowledge the repair work, provide additional information about their vehicle, and sign off on the work completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer information section (id: 1) with the customer's contact details.
2. Provide details about the appointment (id: 2), including the date, time, and location of the appointment.
3. Enter the repair details (id: 3) of the "rock chip" being addressed, including its location and any additional notes.
4. Enter your contact information (id: 4), so we can get in touch with you if needed.
5. Add any notes (id: 5) you'd like to include about the repair.
6. Confirm that you've seen the repair work (id: 6) and sign off (id: 9) with your signature.
7. Confirm your signature (id: 10).
8. Add any customer notes (id: 11) about the repair or your vehicle.
9. Enter any technician notes (id: 12) about the repair.
10. Add any repair notes (id: 13) about the work completed.
11. Enter your vehicle's license (id: 14), make (id: 15), model (id: 16), year (id: 17), color (id: 19), and VIN (id: 18).
12. Enter your odometer reading (id: 20), insurance company (id: 21), claim number (id: 22), and adjuster's name (id: 23) if applicable.
13. Enter the insurance date (id: 24) and signature date (id: 25) for the repair work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information (id: 1)**: Enter your full name, phone number, and email address.
* **Appointment Details (id: 2)**: Provide the date, time, and location of the appointment.
* **Repair Details (id: 3)**: Describe the "rock chip" and any additional notes.
* **Contact Information (id: 4)**: Enter your contact details so we can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Notes (id: 5)**: Add any additional notes about the repair or your vehicle.
* **Confirmation (id: 6)**: Confirm that you've seen the repair work.
* **Signature (id: 7)**: Sign with your name to acknowledge the repair.
* **Confirm Signature (id: 10)**: Confirm your signature.
* **Customer Notes (id: 11)**: Add any notes about the repair or your vehicle.
* **Technician Notes (id: 12)**: Add any notes from the technician about the repair.
* **Repair Notes (id: 13)**: Add any notes about the repair work completed.
* **Vehicle License (id: 14)**: Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Make (id: 15)**: Enter your vehicle's make (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model (id: 16)**: Enter your vehicle's model (e.g., Camry, Mustang).
* **Vehicle Year (id: 17)**: Enter your vehicle's year of manufacture.
* **VIN (id: 18)**: Enter your vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Vehicle Color (id: 19)**: Enter your vehicle's color.
* **Odometer Reading (id: 20)**: Enter your vehicle's odometer reading.
* **Insurance Company (id: 21)**: Enter your insurance company's name.
* **Claim Number (id: 22)**: Enter your claim number.
* **Adjuster's Name (id: 23)**: Enter the adjuster's name.
* **Insurance Date (id: 24)**: Enter the insurance date (if applicable).
* **Signature Date (id: 25)**: Enter the date of your signature.

Please take a few minutes to review each field carefully and fill out the form accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
