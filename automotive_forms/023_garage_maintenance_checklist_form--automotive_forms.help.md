# Garage Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and track maintenance tasks and inspections for your vehicle. It helps ensure that your vehicle receives regular check-ups and stays in good condition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand what each field represents.
2. Select the applicable maintenance tasks for your vehicle's current status.
3. Choose the inspection points to be checked.
4. Input the date of the next scheduled maintenance appointment.
5. Record any important notes or comments about the vehicle's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Maintenance Tasks** (`Engine Oil`, `Brake Pads`, `Tire Pressure`, `Brake Fluid`): Select all applicable maintenance tasks that your vehicle requires. This field allows you to keep track of necessary repairs and replacements for your vehicle.
* **Inspection Points** (`Headlights`, `Taillights`, `Turn Signals`, `Brake Lights`, `Reverse Lights`): Choose the areas to be inspected to identify any issues or problems that need attention.
* **Maintenance Schedule** (`Maintenance Schedule`): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance appointment for your vehicle. This helps you stay organized and on track.
* **Vehicle Information**: This field is for any relevant information about your vehicle that you want to record, such as model number, mileage, or recent repairs.
* **Notes**: Record any additional comments or notes about your vehicle's condition or maintenance history. This is a good place to document any issues or concerns you've encountered.

## Tips
* Make sure to update this form regularly to keep track of your vehicle's maintenance and inspection history.
* Use this form to identify potential issues before they become major problems.
* Keep a copy of this form as a reference for future maintenance appointments.
