# vehicle_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle registration form is used to collect and manage vehicle information, including owner details, registration status, and financial data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Vehicle Owner" field with the owner's name and contact information.
2. Enter the "Owner Name" field with the owner's full name.
3. Enter the "Vehicle License Number" field with the vehicle's license plate number.
4. Enter the "Vehicle Year Of Manufacture" field with the year the vehicle was manufactured.
5. Enter the "Vehicle Year Of Registration" field with the year the vehicle was registered.
6. Select the "Vehicle Registration Type" field with the type of registration (Registration, Re-registration, or Renewal).
7. Enter the "Vehicle Model" field with the vehicle's make and model.
8. Enter the "Vehicle Color" field with the vehicle's color.
9. Enter the "Vehicle Year Of Purchase" field with the year the vehicle was purchased.
10. Enter the "Vehicle Purchase Date" field with the date of purchase.
11. Select the "Vehicle Registration Status" field with the current registration status (Active, Inactive, Suspended, or Cancelled).
12. Enter the "Vehicle Last Registration Date" field with the last registration date.
13. Enter the "Vehicle Registration Fee" field with the registration fee amount.
14. Select the "Vehicle Registration Fee Currency" field with the currency of the registration fee (reported, USD, GBP, or CAD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Owner** (text, optional): Enter the owner's name and contact information.
* **Owner Name** (text, optional): Enter the owner's full name.
* **Vehicle License Number** (text, optional): Enter the vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Year Of Manufacture** (number, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Year Of Registration** (number, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was registered.
* **Vehicle Registration Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of registration (Registration, Re-registration, or Renewal).
* **Vehicle Model** (text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make and model.
* **Vehicle Color** (text, optional): Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle Year Of Purchase** (number, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
* **Vehicle Purchase Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of purchase.
* **Vehicle Registration Status** (select_one, optional): Select the current registration status (Active, Inactive, Suspended, or Cancelled).
* **Vehicle Last Registration Date** (date, optional): Enter the last registration date.
* **Vehicle Registration Fee** (number, optional): Enter the registration fee amount.
* **Vehicle Registration Fee Currency** (select_one, optional): Select the currency of the registration fee (reported, USD, GBP, or CAD).
