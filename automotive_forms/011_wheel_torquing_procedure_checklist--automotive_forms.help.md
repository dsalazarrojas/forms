# Wheel Torquing Procedure Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wheel Torquing Procedure Checklist is a form used to collect data on the wheel torquing process for a vehicle. This form helps ensure that all relevant details are recorded accurately, which aids in maintaining vehicle safety and preventing wheel-related issues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter the required user information (first name, job title, email, and phone number).
3. Select the correct vehicle type, year, make, and model.
4. Indicate whether the wheel is type (yes or no).
5. Specify the manufacturer and verify its correctness.
6. Enter the torque specification and value (if applicable).
7. Enter any relevant vehicle details (weight, body type).
8. Record the notes and verification details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Job Title** (`user_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, text, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Manufacturer** (`manufacturer`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's manufacturer (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Torque Specification** (`torque_specification`, number, optional): Enter the recommended torque value for the wheel.
* **Wheel Type** (`wheel_type`, select_one, optional): Select 'yes' if the wheel is type, 'no' otherwise.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make (e.g., Camry, Mustang, etc.).
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's model (e.g., Accord, Civic, etc.).
* **Vehicle Body Type** (`vehicle_body_type`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's body type (e.g., sedan, SUV, etc.).
* **Year Verified** (`vehicle_year_verified`, text, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle's verification (if applicable).
* **Tightening Method** (`wheel_tightening_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select 'yes' or 'no' for the tightening method.
* **Torque Value** (`torque_value`, number, optional): Enter the torque value (if applicable).
* **Vehicle Weight** (`vehicle_weight`, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's weight.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's type (e.g., car, truck, etc.).
* **Manufacturer Verified** (`manufacturer_verified`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's manufacturer verification (if applicable).
* **Year Verified** (`vehicle_year_verified`, text, optional): Enter the year of your vehicle's verification (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Notes Verified** (`notes_verified`, note, optional): Enter any additional verified notes or comments.

## Tips

* Double-check your entries for accuracy to ensure the correct data is captured.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the relevant expert or refer to the user manual.
* Complete the form as thoroughly as possible to ensure a comprehensive wheel torquing process.
* Review the form carefully before submission to avoid missing any critical information.
