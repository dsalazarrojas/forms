# Vehicle Registration Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vehicle Registration Renewal Form is used to gather information for vehicle registration renewal. This includes selecting the type of vehicle, providing the license plate number, choosing the renewal method, and inputting address, city, state, zip code, email, and phone number details. The form is designed to be completed online.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vehicle Registration Renewal Form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of vehicle you are registering.
2.  Enter your license plate number.
3.  Choose your preferred renewal method.
4.  Input your address.
5.  Enter your city.
6.  Enter your state.
7.  Enter your zip code.
8.  Enter your email address.
9.  Enter your phone number.
10.  Upload your signature for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vehicle you are registering. This will help us determine the correct renewal process for your vehicle.
*   **License Plate Number** (`license_plate_number`, text, required): Enter your vehicle's license plate number. This is a required field and must be accurate.
*   **Renewal Method** (`renewal_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to renew your vehicle registration. You can select one or multiple methods.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method by which you want to pay for your renewal. This will help us process your payment efficiently.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full address.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you are located.
*   **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you are located.
*   **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required): Enter your zip code.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates and notifications.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Upload your signature for verification purposes. This will help us confirm your identity.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact our support team for assistance.
