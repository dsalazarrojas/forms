# Automotive Service Advisor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automotive Service Advisor Application Form is used to gather information from applicants for the Automotive Service Advisor position. This form is designed to help us understand your qualifications, experience, and background.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your current address.
3. Answer questions about your professional background, including your years of service experience and current employment status.
4. Select your level of expertise in various areas, including vehicle knowledge, diagnostic tools, and software systems.
5. Choose your soft skills, such as communication and customer service skills.
6. Select all areas of experience you have worked on as a service advisor.
7. Provide any relevant certifications or recent training completed.
8. Indicate your availability to start work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your work email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your work phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Please enter your current address for our records.
* **Years of Service Experience** (`years_service_experience`, number, required): Please enter the number of years you have been working as a service advisor.
* **Previous Employers** (`previous_employers`, text, optional): If you have any previous employment, please list them.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment`, select_one, required): Please select your current employment status from the options provided.
* **General Vehicle Knowledge** (`vehicle_knowledge`, select_one, required): Please select your level of expertise in vehicle knowledge from the options provided.
* **Familiarity with Diagnostic Tools** (`diagnostic_tools`, select_one, required): Please select your level of familiarity with diagnostic tools from the options provided.
* **Computer System and Software Knowledge** (`software_systems`, select_one, required): Please select your level of expertise in computer systems and software from the options provided.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, required): Please select your level of expertise in communication skills from the options provided.
* **Customer Service Orientation** (`customer_service`, select_one, required): Please select your level of expertise in customer service from the options provided.
* **Organization and Time Management** (`organization_skills`, select_one, required): Please select your level of expertise in organization and time management from the options provided.
* **Service Areas of Experience** (`service_areas`, select_multiple, required): Please select all areas of experience you have as a service advisor.
* **Relevant Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): If you have any relevant certifications, please list them.
* **Recent Training Completed** (`training_completed`, text, optional): If you have any recent training completed, please list them.
* **Specific Position Interests** (`position_interest`, text, optional): If you are interested in any specific position, please list them.
* **Available Start Date** (`availability`, date, required): Please enter the date you are available to start work.
