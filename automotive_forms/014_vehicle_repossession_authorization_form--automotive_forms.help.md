# vehicle_repossession_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and manage information about vehicles that are currently repossessed or are at risk of repossession.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vehicle year.
2. Indicate whether the vehicle make is 'Yes' or 'No'.
3. Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
4. Enter the vehicle model.
5. Enter the vehicle color.
6. Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
7. Enter the current value of the vehicle.
8. Enter the reason for repossession.
9. Enter the date of repossession.
10. Select the current repossession status: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
11. Enter the lender's name.
12. Enter the lender's phone number.
13. Enter any additional notes about the vehicle or repossession.
14. Indicate whether repo service is required: 'Yes' or 'No'.
15. Enter the name of the person assigned to handle the repossession.
16. Enter the date the repossession was assigned to this person.
17. (Optional) Enter the time the repossession was assigned to this person.
18. Enter the lender's email address.
19. (Optional) Select whether to report the repossession: 'Yes' or 'No'.
20. Select the current status of the repossession: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
21. (Optional) Enter any additional notes from the lender.
22. (Optional) Select whether comments are required: 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Indicate whether the vehicle make is 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color**: Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Year Purchased**: Enter the year the vehicle was purchased.
* **Vehicle Current Value**: Enter the current value of the vehicle.
* **Repossession Reason**: Enter the reason for repossession.
* **Repossession Date**: Enter the date of repossession.
* **Repo Status**: Select the current status of the repossession: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Lender Name**: Enter the name of the lender.
* **Lender Phone**: Enter the lender's phone number.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the vehicle or repossession.
* **Repo Service**: Indicate whether repo service is required: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to handle the repossession.
* **Assigned Date**: Enter the date the repossession was assigned to this person.
* **Assigned Time**: (Optional) Enter the time the repossession was assigned to this person.
* **Lender Email**: Enter the lender's email address.
* **Report**: (Optional) Select whether to report the repossession: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Repo Status 2**: Select the current status of the repossession: 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Lender Notes**: (Optional) Enter any additional notes from the lender.
* **Comments**: (Optional) Select whether comments are required: 'Yes' or 'No'.
