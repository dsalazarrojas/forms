# Fleet Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help administrators track and monitor the maintenance and status of vehicles in the fleet.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct owner from the dropdown menu.
2. Select the correct driver(s) from the dropdown menu.
3. Select the correct vehicle status from the dropdown menu.
4. Enter the correct last service date and mileage.
5. Enter the correct last service cost.
6. Enter the correct next service date and mileage.
7. Select the correct next service frequency from the dropdown menu.
8. Select the correct last inspection date and frequency.
9. Enter the correct last inspection cost and mileage.
10. Enter the correct last mileage reading.
11. Enter the correct last maintenance date and frequency.
12. Enter the correct next maintenance date and frequency.
13. Select the correct vehicle condition and damage type from the dropdown menus.
14. Enter the correct remarks in the text area.
15. Enter any additional remarks or comments in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Information
• **Vehicle Information** (Vehicle Information, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and other relevant details.
### Vehicle Condition Report
• **Condition Report** (Condition Report, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the vehicle's condition.
### Vehicle Mileage
• **Mileage** (Mileage, number, optional): Enter the vehicle's current mileage.
### Last Service Date
• **Last Service Date** (Last Service Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the last maintenance or service performed on the vehicle.
### Next Service Date
• **Next Service Date** (Next Service Date, date, optional): Enter the scheduled date for the next maintenance or service of the vehicle.
### Last Service Mileage
• **Last Service Mileage** (Last Service Mileage, number, optional): Enter the mileage of the vehicle at the last service.
### Last Service Cost
• **Last Service Cost** (Last Service Cost, number, optional): Enter the cost of the last service.
### Next Service Cost
• **Next Service Cost** (Next Service Cost, number, optional): Enter the estimated cost of the next service.
### Owner
• **Owner Name** (Owner Name, select_one, optional): Select the owner of the vehicle from the dropdown menu.
### Driver
• **Driver** (Driver, select_multiple, optional): Select the driver(s) of the vehicle from the dropdown menu.
### Vehicle Status
• **Vehicle Status** (Vehicle Status, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the vehicle from the dropdown menu.
### Remarks
• **Remarks** (Remarks, note, optional): Enter any remarks or comments about the vehicle.
### Vehicle Condition
• **Vehicle Condition** (Vehicle Condition, select_one, optional): Select the current condition of the vehicle from the dropdown menu.
### Damage Type
• **Damage Type** (Damage Type, select_multiple, optional): Select any damage type(s) from the dropdown menu.
### Last Maintenance Date
• **Last Maintenance Date** (Last Maintenance Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the last maintenance.
### Inspection Date
• **Inspection Date** (Inspection Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the last inspection.
### Inspection Frequency
• **Inspection Frequency** (Inspection Frequency, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of the inspections from the dropdown menu.
### Last Inspection Cost
• **Last Inspection Cost** (Last Inspection Cost, number, optional): Enter the cost of the last inspection.
### Last Inspection Mileage
• **Last Inspection Mileage** (Last Inspection Mileage, number, optional): Enter the mileage of the vehicle at the last inspection.
### Last Mileage Reading
• **Last Mileage Reading** (Last Mileage Reading, number, optional): Enter the last mileage reading.
### Remarks
• **Remarks** (Remarks, note, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the vehicle.
### Next Maintenance Date
• **Next Maintenance Date** (Next Maintenance Date, date, optional): Enter the scheduled date for the next maintenance.
### Next Maintenance Frequency
• **Next Maintenance Frequency** (Next Maintenance Frequency, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of the next maintenance from the dropdown menu.
### Next Remarks
• **Next Remarks** (Next Remarks, note, optional): Enter any remarks or comments about the next maintenance.
### Vehicle Remarks 2
• **Remarks** (Remarks, note, optional): Enter any additional remarks or comments about the vehicle.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and thoroughly to provide a clear picture of the vehicle's status.
* Please select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
* If any field is not applicable, please select "None" or enter "N/A" as the value.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact the fleet manager or administrator.
