# windshield_claim_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The windshields_claim_form is a form for users to submit claims related to their windshields. This form is used to gather information about the issue with the windshields and help resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out page 1 of the form.
2.  Enter your vehicle make.
3.  Select the type of claim you are making.
4.  Enter any additional details about your claim.
5.  Enter the date and time of the issue with your windshields.
6.  Enter the time of day when the issue occurred.
7.  Select if the issue is still ongoing.
8.  Select if you have any other issues with your windshields.
9.  Select the type of claim you are making.
10. Enter any additional details about your claim.
11.  Enter the date and time of your windshields issue.
12.  Enter the time of day when your windshields issue occurred.
13.  Select if your windshields issue is still ongoing.
14.  Select if you have any other issues with your windshields.
15.  Select the type of claim you are making.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **windshield_claim_form_1 (Text, Optional)**: This field is for entering any additional details about your claim.
*   **vehicle_make (Select One, Required)**: Select the make of your vehicle to help us understand which windshields we are dealing with.
*   **windshield_claim_form_3 (Text, Optional)**: This field is for entering any other details about your claim.
*   **windshield_claim_form_4 (Text, Optional)**: This field is for entering any additional details about your claim.
*   **windshield_claim_form_5 (Number, Optional)**: Enter the number of days since the issue has been ongoing.
*   **windshield_claim_form_6 (Number, Optional)**: Enter the time of day when the issue occurred.
*   **windshield_claim_form_7 (Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select if you have any other issues with your windshields.
*   **windshield_claim_form_8 (Note, Optional)**: Enter any additional details about your claim.
*   **windshield_claim_form_9 (Select One, Optional)**: Select the type of claim you are making.
*   **windshield_claim_form_10 (Date, Optional)**: Enter the date of your windshields issue.
*   **windshield_claim_form_11 (Time, Optional)**: Enter the time of day when your windshields issue occurred.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting your form.
*   Use extra space if you need to enter more than one line of text.
*   If you have any other issues with your windshields, select the options for each issue.
*   If you are unsure about any field, ask for help.
*   Submit your form when you have finished filling out all required fields.
*   Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.
