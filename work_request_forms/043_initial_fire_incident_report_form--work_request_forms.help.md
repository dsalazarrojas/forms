# Initial Fire Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Initial Fire Incident Report Form is used to report incidents that have occurred on site. The form collects information about the incident, including its type, description, location, and status. This information helps the management team to quickly assess and respond to the incident. Please ensure that you only submit reports for incidents that are not already known to the management team and that you are not duplicating a report that has already been submitted.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct incident type from the options provided in the "Incident Type" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the incident in the "Incident Description" field.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident in the "Date of Incident" and "Time of Incident" fields.
4. Specify the location where the incident occurred in the "Incident Location" field.
5. Check the "Incident Status" field to indicate whether the incident is open, closed, or in progress.
6. If applicable, select "Yes" from the "Escalation" and "Responders" fields to indicate that the incident requires escalation or response from a specific team or individual.
7. Specify any injuries, damage, or deaths that occurred as a result of the incident in the "Injuries", "Damage", and "Deaths" fields.
8. Enter any fund allocation for the incident in the "Fund Allocation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Details**: This field is where you should provide a brief description of the incident. Try to be as specific as possible about what happened.
* **Incident Type**: Choose the option that best describes the type of incident that occurred.
* **Date of Incident**: Enter the date on which the incident occurred.
* **Time of Incident**: Enter the time when the incident occurred.
* **Incident Location**: Specify the exact location where the incident took place.
* **Investigation**: This field is for any additional information about the incident that you would like to add.
* **Incident Status**: Choose the status that best describes the incident's current state.
* **Escalation**: Check "Yes" if the incident requires escalation to a higher authority.
* **Responders**: Check "Yes" if a response from a specific team or individual is required.
* **Injuries**: Check "Yes" if any injuries occurred as a result of the incident.
* **Damage**: Check "Yes" if any damage was caused by the incident.
* **Deaths**: Check "Yes" if any deaths occurred as a result of the incident.
* **Fund Allocation**: Enter the amount of funds allocated for the incident.
