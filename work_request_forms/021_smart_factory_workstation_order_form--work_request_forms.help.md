# Smart Factory Workstation Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Factory Workstation Order Form is designed to collect information from customers or operators about their workstation orders. This form is used for internal purposes and will not be shared or sold, so you can focus on providing accurate and relevant details without worrying about external visibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **General Information** page with your contact information and any other relevant details.
2. Move on to the **Workstation Details** page and provide information about the workstation you are ordering, such as its type, model, or specific features.
3. If you have any additional information or comments, you can add them in the **Additional Information** page.
4. Finally, click the **Submit** button to send your form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information
* **Title: Smart Factory Workstation Order Form**
*   *General Information* (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can provide your name, email address, or any other contact information that might be relevant to your workstation order. Please fill in your name and any other necessary details.

### Workstation Details
* **Workstation Details** (`workstation_details`, `text`, required: false): This is where you can describe the specific workstation you are ordering, including its model, features, or any other relevant details.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): If you have any additional comments or information about your order, please add it here.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to send your form for processing. Once you've filled out all the required and optional fields, this button will submit your form.
