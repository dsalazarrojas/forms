# Sewer Maintenance Workflow Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide field technicians through a thorough and structured workflow for maintaining sewers. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information for efficient sewer maintenance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required details for the work order in the first field.
2. Perform a site check, indicating whether the site meets or does not meet specific requirements.
3. Conduct a safety review, determining whether the safety standards for the site are met.
4. Enter the dates for each stage of the process: work order date, site check date, and safety review date.
5. Enter the completed date and indicate whether the site has been assigned to someone.
6. Indicate the priority level of the site.
7. Enter the status of the site, either active or inactive.
8. Add any additional notes about the site.
9. Enter the email address and phone number of the person responsible for the site.
10. Specify the location and type of site.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Order** (1, text, required): Enter the details of the work order for the site.
* **Site Check** (2, select_multiple, required): Perform a site check by selecting 'Yes' for each item that is present or 'No' for each item that is not.
* **Safety Review** (3, select_one, required): Indicate whether the site meets the required safety standards by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Work Order Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the initial work order.
* **Site Check Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the site check.
* **Safety Review Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date of the safety review.
* **Completed Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date of completion of the maintenance work.
* **Assigned To** (8, select_one, required): Indicate whether the site is assigned to someone by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Assigned By** (9, select_one, required): Indicate whether the person responsible for the site is assigned by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Priority** (10, select_one, required): Select the priority level of the site, either High, Low, or Medium.
* **Status** (11, select_one, required): Enter the status of the site, either Active or Inactive.
* **Notes** (12, text, required): Add any additional notes about the site.
* **Email** (13, email, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for the site.
* **Phone** (14, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the site.
* **Location** (15, select_multiple, required): Specify the location of the site, selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for each option.
* **Type** (16, text, required): Enter the type of site, e.g., residential, commercial, industrial.

## Tips
- Be thorough in filling out each field to ensure accurate and complete information.
- Review each field carefully to ensure that you have included all necessary details.
