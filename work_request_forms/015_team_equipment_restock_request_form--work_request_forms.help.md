# Team Equipment Restock Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request restocking of team equipment. You can use this form to submit a request for equipment that is currently out of stock or low in supply.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the Category of the equipment you are requesting.
2. Enter the title of the form, if desired.
3. Provide a brief description of the equipment you are requesting.
4. Enter a clear reason for your request.
5. Specify the type of equipment you are requesting.
6. Enter the quantity of equipment you need.
7. Select a date for when you would like to receive the requested equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (id: 2)**: Select the category that best describes the type of equipment you are requesting. Options are: "Work Request Forms", "Other Prophets", or "Other Forms".
* **Title (id: 3)**: If you want to give your request a title, enter it here.
* **Description (id: 4)**: Briefly describe the equipment you are requesting. This will help the team understand what you need.
* **Request Reason (id: 5)**: Provide a clear and concise explanation for your request.
* **Item Type (id: 6)**: Enter the type of equipment you are requesting.
* **Equipment Type (id: 7)**: Enter the specific type of equipment you are requesting.
* **Quantity (id: 8)**: Enter the amount of equipment you need.
* **Request Date (id: 9)**: Select the date when you would like to receive the requested equipment.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is processed correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
- Please review your request carefully before submitting it to ensure it is accurate and complete.
