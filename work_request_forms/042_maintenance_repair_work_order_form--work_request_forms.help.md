# Maintenance Repair Work Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this Maintenance Repair Work Order Form is to document and report the status of maintenance and repair work orders. This form helps track and manage the maintenance and repair work process, ensuring that all relevant information is collected and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Device Description" field with a brief description of the device that requires maintenance or repair.
2. Enter the "Device Model" of the device, if applicable.
3. Provide the "Device Serial Number" of the device, if available.
4. Record the "Date Received" when the device was received for maintenance or repair.
5. Enter the "Date Returned" when the device was returned after maintenance or repair.
6. Choose the "Maintenance Type" from the options: "Regular", "Urgent", or "Critical". If other, select "Other" and provide a description in the "Other" field.
7. Add any additional remarks or comments in the "Remarks" field.
8. Sign and date the "Signed" field to confirm that the form was signed.
9. Sign the "Signed By" field with your name and title.
10. Finally, enter the "Signed Date" when the form was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Device Description** (Text, Optional): A brief description of the device that requires maintenance or repair.
* **Device Model** (Text, Optional): The model number of the device, if applicable.
* **Device Serial Number** (Text, Optional): The serial number of the device, if available.
* **Date Received** (Date, Optional): The date the device was received for maintenance or repair.
* **Date Returned** (Date, Optional): The date the device was returned after maintenance or repair.
* **Maintenance Type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of maintenance or repair required, from "Regular", "Urgent", or "Critical". If other, select "Other" and provide a description in the "Other" field.
* **Other** (Text, Optional): A description of the maintenance or repair type if "Other" is selected in the "Maintenance Type" field.
* **Remarks** (Text, Optional): Any additional remarks or comments about the maintenance or repair work order.
* **Signed** (Text, Optional): A confirmation that the form was signed.
* **Signed By** (Text, Optional): The name and title of the person who signed the form.
* **Signed Date** (Date, Optional): The date when the form was signed.
