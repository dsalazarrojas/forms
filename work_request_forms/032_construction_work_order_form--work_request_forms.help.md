# Construction Work Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture and track work orders for construction projects. It includes sections for client contact information, work order details, labor and financial information, and authorization and confirmation. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is collected and that work orders are properly managed and tracked.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the client contact information section:
	* **Client Full Name or Organization**: Enter the name of the client or organization.
	* **Billing Email Address**: Enter the email address where invoices and receipts will be sent.
	* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the contact phone number for the client.
2. Enter the site address for the work order:
	* **Site Address for Work Order**: Enter the address of the location where the work will be performed.
3. Fill in the work order details section:
	* **Work Order Reference (Internal ID)**: Enter a unique identifier for the work order.
	* **Project Name**: Enter the name of the project.
	* **Scheduled Start Date**: Enter the date when the work is scheduled to start.
	* **Estimated Completion Date**: Enter the date when the work is estimated to be completed.
	* **Primary Work Category**: Select the main trade or category of work (e.g., General Carpentry/Framing).
	* **Detailed Scope of Work**: Describe the specific tasks that will be performed.
4. Enter labor and financial information:
	* **Service Rate Per Hour (\$)**: Enter the rate per hour for the work.
	* **Estimated Total Hours for Completion**: Enter the estimated total hours required for completion.
	* **Estimated Total Labor Cost (\$)**: This will be automatically calculated based on the service rate and estimated hours.
	* **Estimated Material Cost (\$)**: Enter the estimated cost of materials (excluding labor).
	* **Total Work Order Amount (\$)**: This will be automatically calculated as the sum of labor and material costs.
5. Confirm authorization and submit:
	* **I Authorize and Agree**: Select this option to confirm that you have authorized and agreed to the work order.
	* **Additional Instructions or Notes**: Enter any additional comments or instructions.
6. Complete the submission date and finalize:
	* **Date of Work Order**: Enter the date when the work order was submitted.
	* **Supervisor Approval (Name)**: Enter the name of the supervisor who approved the work order.
	* **Invoice Already Issued?**: Select whether an invoice has already been issued for the work order.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete work order information.
* Double-check all calculations and estimates to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that you have authorized and agreed to the work order before submitting it.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
