# Creative Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Design Request Form is designed to collect necessary information from clients about their creative design request. This form will help us understand their project requirements and preferences to provide an excellent outcome.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled in.
2. Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure that we can understand your request effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required): This field is where you can provide us with your company information, including your name, address, and other contact details.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required): Please describe your project requirements and objectives. Be as specific as possible to help us create an excellent design.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): If you have any relevant files, such as project documents or images, you can attach them here.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This is a unique identifier for your form submission. You can leave this blank if you don't know what it is.
* **Project Details 2** (`project_details_2`, text, optional): This field is an optional additional project description. If you have any further information, please add it here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you have any attachments, such as images or documents, please make sure they are relevant to your project.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your form submission and get back to you as soon as possible.
