The purpose of the "milton_keynes_handyman_quotation" form is to gather information for a quotation request from customers. This form is designed to be filled out by customers who require a handyman service quotation. The form collects details about the customer and their quotation request, including whether they have a previous quotation, their name, and address information. This form is meant to be completed by customers, not internal staff.

## Milton Keynes Handyman Quotation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Milton Keynes Handyman Quotation" form is used by customers to provide details for a quotation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Quotation Request** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the work you require a quotation for.
2. **Customer Details** (`2`, `select_one`, required: false):
	* Select "Yes" if you have a previous quotation for this job.
	* Select "No" if you do not have a previous quotation.
3. **Customer Name** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
4. **Address Line 1** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address line 1.
5. **Address Line 2** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your address line 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quotation Request**: A brief description of the work you require a quotation for.
* **Customer Details**:
	+ Select "Yes" to indicate you have a previous quotation for this job. If you have already submitted a quotation request, this will allow the handyman service to access your previous details and provide a revised quotation.
	+ Select "No" if you do not have a previous quotation.
* **Customer Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your first line of address.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your second line of address.

## Tips
* Please provide as much detail as possible to ensure the handyman service can provide an accurate quotation.
* If you have any additional requests or comments, please include them in the "Quotation Request" field.
