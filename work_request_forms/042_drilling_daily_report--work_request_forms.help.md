# Drilling Daily Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about drilling operations for record-keeping and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Drilling Daily Report form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the date of the drilling operation.
2. Enter the location where the drilling took place.
3. Indicate whether drilling equipment was used or not.
4. Choose the type of drilling that occurred (drilling or non-drilling).
5. Select the status of the drilling operation (Active or Inactive).
6. Enter the priority level of the drilling operation (numeric value).
7. Enter the duration of the drilling operation (numeric value).
8. Provide any comments or notes about the drilling activity.
9. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
10. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
11. Enter the address where the drilling took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date Field**: Select the date of the drilling operation using the calendar input field.
* **Time Field**: Enter the time of the drilling operation.
* **Drilling Location**: Enter the location where the drilling took place.
* **Drilling Equipment**: Indicate whether drilling equipment was used or not.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool assigned to the drilling operation.
* **Drilling Type**: Choose the type of drilling that occurred (drilling or non-drilling).
* **Drilling Status**: Select the status of the drilling operation (Active or Inactive).
* **Drilling Priority**: Enter a numeric value indicating the priority level of the drilling operation.
* **Drilling Duration**: Enter a numeric value indicating the duration of the drilling operation.
* **Drilling Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the drilling activity.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter the address where the drilling took place.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure accurate record-keeping.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult your supervisor or refer to the project guidelines.
