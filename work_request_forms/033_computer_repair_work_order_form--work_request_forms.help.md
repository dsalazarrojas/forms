# Computer Repair Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit repair requests for computer-related issues. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient processing and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
2. Select the correct "Device type" from the options provided.
3. Enter a detailed description of the problem.
4. Choose the appropriate "Priority" for your repair request.
5. Provide your "Contact email" for updates about your repair status.
6. If you wish, provide the "Location" where the device is located.
7. If necessary, enter the work performed by the technician.
8. Finally, select the "Resolution status" of your repair request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Requester name**: This field is used to identify the person making the repair request. Please enter your full name.
* **Contact email**: This field is used to keep you informed about the status of your repair request. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Device type**: This field is used to determine the type of device being repaired. Select from the options: Laptop, Desktop, Tablet, Mobile, or Other.
* **Problem description**: This field is used to describe the issue with your device. Please provide a detailed account of the problem.
* **Priority**: This field is used to determine the order in which repair requests are processed. Select from the options: Low, Normal, High, or Urgent.
* **Location**: This field is used to identify the location where the device is being repaired. If you wish to provide this information, please enter the location.
* **Work performed**: This field is used to record the actions taken by the technician to resolve your issue. If you feel this is necessary, please enter the work performed.
* **Resolution status**: This field is used to track the progress of your repair request. Select from the options: Open, In progress, or Completed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately to avoid delays in processing your repair request.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* We appreciate your patience and understanding as we work to resolve your repair issue efficiently and effectively.
