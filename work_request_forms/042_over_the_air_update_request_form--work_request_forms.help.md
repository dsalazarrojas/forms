# over-the-air-update-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an over-the-air update for a device. It is intended for internal use within an organization and should only be used when an over-the-air update is required for a specific device.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information for the device in question.
2. Select the type of update required (Device Firmware or Device Software).
3. Choose who requested the update (IT Team, Service Provider, or Other).
4. Enter the date and time for the scheduled update.
5. Provide any additional information or comments as needed.
6. Review your submission and click the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (`form_id`): A unique identifier for this form submission.
* **Device ID** (`device_id`): Enter the device ID number.
* **Update Type** (`update_type`): Choose the type of update required - Device Firmware or Device Software.
* **Scheduled Date** (`scheduled_date`): Select the date for the over-the-air update.
* **Scheduled Time** (`scheduled_time`): Select the time for the over-the-air update.
* **Description** (`description`): A brief description of the update reason.
* **Status** (`status`): A status update on the progress of the update.
* **Requestor** (`requestor`): Select who requested the update (IT Team, Service Provider, or Other).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the contact email address.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Additional information or comments about the update.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): Select who submitted the form (IT Team, Service Provider, or Other).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`): Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Assigned** (`assigned`): A field for additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`): Enter the date the update was assigned.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`): Enter the date the update was completed.
* **Submitted By ID** (`submitted_by_id`): Enter the ID of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Email** (`submitted_by_email`): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`submitted_by_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form.
* **Assigned By ID** (`assigned_by_id`): Enter the ID of the person assigned to work on the update.
* **Assigned By Email** (`assigned_by_email`): Enter the email address of the person assigned to work on the update.
* **Assigned By Phone** (`assigned_by_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person assigned to work on the update.
* **Submitted Status** (`submitted_status`): Select the status of the submission (New, In Progress, or Complete).

Note: This form requires careful and accurate submission to ensure proper processing of over-the-air updates. Please double-check all fields before submitting the form.
