# Window Frame Repair Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information from users who need repair services for their window frames.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Details" field. This is where you can enter your name, address, or any other relevant contact information.
2. Describe the issue with your window frame in the "Issue Description" field. This will help our team understand the problem and provide the best possible solution.
3. If you have any photos of the affected window frame, upload them in the "Photos" field. This will help our team assess the issue more efficiently.
4. Schedule an appointment time for our team to visit and repair your window frame. Enter the desired appointment time in the "Appointment Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Details (Text)**: This field is where you can enter your contact information, such as your name, address, or phone number.
* **Issue Description (Text)**: Describe the problem with your window frame here. Be as detailed as possible so that our team can provide the best possible solution.
* **Photos (Text)**: If you have photos of the affected window frame, upload them here. This will help our team assess the issue more efficiently.
* **Appointment Time (Time)**: Schedule an appointment time for our team to visit and repair your window frame. Enter the desired time in the format: HH:MM.
