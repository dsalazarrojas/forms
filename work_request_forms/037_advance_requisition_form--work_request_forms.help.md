# Advance Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to request resources, services, or items from the administration. You can use this form to ask for office supplies, IT hardware, software licenses, professional services, or travel expenses. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the "Office Supplies" option for general office needs, "IT Hardware" for computer and network equipment, "Software License" for software purchases, "Professional Services" for consulting or training services, or "Travel Expense" for travel-related requests.
2. Enter your employee name, department, and employee ID accurately.
3. Describe the item or service you are requesting, being specific about its specifics.
4. Choose the correct urgency level for your request, whether it's Routine, Urgent, or Emergency.
5. Enter the quantity and estimated unit cost of the requested item.
6. Calculate the total requisition amount by multiplying the quantity by the estimated unit cost.
7. Provide a brief description of the purpose of the requisition and how it will support your work.
8. Enter the target delivery date for the requested item.
9. If applicable, enter the project code or name associated with this requisition.
10. Enter the name of the supervisor or manager responsible for budget approval.
11. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
  - This field is required to identify the person making the request.
- **Department**: Enter the name of your department or team.
  - This field helps the administration locate the correct departmental budget for your request.
- **Employee ID**: Enter your unique employee ID.
  - This field ensures that the request is associated with the correct employee.
- **Item Description**: Describe the item or service you are requesting, being specific about its specifics.
  - This field helps the administration understand what you need.
- **Requisition Category**: Select the category for your request, such as Office Supplies, IT Hardware, Software License, Professional Services, or Travel Expense.
  - This field helps the administration categorize and prioritize your request.
- **Quantity Needed**: Enter the number of items or services you require.
  - This field helps the administration calculate the total cost of the requisition.
- **Estimated Unit Cost**: Enter the estimated cost per unit of the requested item.
  - This field helps the administration calculate the total requisition amount.
- **Total Requisition Amount**: Calculate the total cost by multiplying the quantity by the estimated unit cost.
  - This field shows the total cost of the requisition.
- **Purpose of Requisition**: Describe briefly how this requisition will support your work.
  - This field provides a justification for the request.
- **Required By Date**: Enter the target delivery date for the requested item.
  - This field helps the administration plan for timely delivery.
- **Urgency Level**: Select the level of urgency for your request, Routine, Urgent, or Emergency.
  - This field helps the administration prioritize your request.
- **Project Code or Name**: Enter the associated project code or name if applicable.
  - This field helps the administration locate the correct project budget.
- **Supervisor and Manager Name**: Enter the name of the supervisor or manager responsible for budget approval.
  - This field ensures that the correct person approves your request.
