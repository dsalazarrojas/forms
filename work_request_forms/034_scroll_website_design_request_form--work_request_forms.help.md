# Scroll Website Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scroll Website Design Request Form is designed to gather information from users who are requesting a website design service. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary details about your project and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the Project Details page (**page 1**) with your project's title and description.
2.  Select the **Required Date** (**page 2**) for your project.
3.  Choose the **Design Type** that suits your needs from the available options (Basic, Premium, Enterprise).
4.  Upload the **File 1** and **File 2** for your project (if applicable).
5.  Confirm your submission by checking the **Confirm Submission** box (**page 4**).
6.  Enter your **Confirm Email** (**page 4**).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Project Details (page 1)**: Enter your project's title and description.
*   **Required Date (page 2)**: Choose the date when your project requires completion.
*   **Design Type (page 2)**: Select the type of design service you need. Options include Basic, Premium, and Enterprise.
*   **File 1 (page 3)**: Upload a file related to your project.
*   **File 2 (page 3)**: Upload another file related to your project (if applicable).
*   **Confirm Submission (page 4)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of this form.
*   **Confirm Email (page 4)**: Enter your email address to confirm your submission.
