# Office Desk Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to enable users to book office desks for a given period of time. It allows users to select their desired desk, specify the booking date and time, and provide additional information such as contact details and assigned user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired desk from the "Select Desk" dropdown menu.
2. Choose the booking date and time using the date and time input fields.
3. Specify the booking duration in hours.
4. Enter a reason for the booking in the "Booking Reason" text field.
5. Enter your contact name, phone number, and email address in the corresponding text fields.
6. Select the user(s) who will be assigned to the desk from the "Assigned User" dropdown menu.
7. Add any optional comment in the "Comment" text area.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Desk** (`Select Desk`, select_one, required): Select the desk you want to book from the dropdown menu.
* **Booking Date** (`Booking Date`, date, required): Enter the date in YYYY-MM-DD format when you plan to book the desk.
* **Booking Time** (`Booking Time`, time, required): Enter the time in 24-hour format when you plan to book the desk.
* **Booking Duration** (`Booking Duration`, number, required): Specify the duration of the booking in hours.
* **Booking Reason** (`Booking Reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for booking the desk.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact E-mail** (`Contact E-mail`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Assigned User** (`Assigned User`, select_multiple, required): Select the user(s) who will be assigned to the desk.
* **Comment** (`Comment`, note, required): Enter any additional comment.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click to submit the form after filling in all fields.
