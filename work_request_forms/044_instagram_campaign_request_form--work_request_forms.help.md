# instagram_campaign_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The instagram_campaign_request_form is a form used to collect information for creating an Instagram campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Ensure that you have read and understood the guidelines.
- If you have previously submitted a campaign with similar details, please refrain from submitting another one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details**: 
	- This field is used to collect information about the person submitting the campaign.
- **Account Details**: 
	- This field is used to collect information about the Instagram account to be used for the campaign.
- **Campaign Details**:
	- This field is used to collect information about the campaign, such as its objectives and strategies.
- **Business Details**:
	- This field is used to collect information about the business behind the campaign, such as its name and contact information.
- **Campaign Requests**: 
	- This field is used to collect any specific requests or requirements for the campaign.
- **Note**: 
	- This field is used to provide any additional comments or notes about the campaign.
- **Email**: 
	- This field is used to collect the email address of the person submitting the campaign.
- **Phone Number**:
	- This field is used to collect the phone number of the person submitting the campaign.

# Tips
- Please double-check all information before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field or requirement, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
