# AR/VR/AI Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AR/VR/AI Maintenance Request Form is a tool for reporting maintenance issues related to Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and Artificial Intelligence (AI) equipment. This form is designed to collect relevant information to expedite the maintenance process and ensure that your issue is addressed promptly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address to enable communication regarding this request.
2. Select the department or team you belong to.
3. Specify the type of equipment that needs maintenance.
4. Enter the model or specification of the equipment.
5. Provide the serial number of the equipment (if available).
6. Describe the issue you are experiencing with the equipment.
7. Specify the severity of the issue.
8. Choose the impact of the issue on operations.
9. Provide the date when the issue first occurred.
10. Mention any attempted fixes or error messages displayed.
11. Choose the urgency level of the issue.
12. If desired, select a preferred resolution date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID or company documentation.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, required): Enter your work email address for communication about this request.
* **Requestor Department** (`requestor_department`, text, required): Select your department or team for record-keeping purposes.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of equipment that needs maintenance, such as AR Device, VR Headset, or AI Hardware.
* **Equipment Model** (`equipment_model`, text, required): Enter the model or specification of the equipment.
* **Equipment Serial Number** (`equipment_serial_number`, text, optional): If available, enter the serial number of the equipment for tracking purposes.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Specify where the equipment is located.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Describe the problem you are experiencing with the equipment.
* **Issue Severity** (`issue_severity`, select_one, required): Select the severity of the issue, which will determine the priority level of the maintenance request.
* **Impact on Operations** (`impact_on_operations`, select_one, required): Choose how the issue is affecting operations, such as Complete Shutdown, Significant Impact, or Minor Impact.
* **Issue Started Date** (`issue_started_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the issue first occurred.
* **Attempted Fixes** (`attempted_fixes`, text, optional): Describe any attempts made to fix the issue.
* **Error Messages** (`error_messages`, text, optional): Provide any error messages or codes displayed.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Select the urgency level of the issue, such as Immediate (within 2 hours), Same day, etc.
* **Preferred Resolution Date** (`preferred_resolution_date`, date, optional): If desired, select a preferred resolution date for the issue.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field explanation above for guidance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid duplication or errors.
