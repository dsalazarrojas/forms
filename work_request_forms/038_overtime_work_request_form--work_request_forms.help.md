# overtime_work_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "overtime_work_request_form" is used to submit a request to work beyond regular working hours for a specific job or project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the base information section, which requires your personal details and job information.
2. Then, provide manager information and job information relevant to the overtime request.
3. Specify the work period for which you are requesting overtime.
4. Select how your manager has confirmed your request in the "Manager Confirmation" field.
5. Finally, confirm or deny the accuracy of your request in the "Employee Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Information** (`base_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, email, and job title to provide context for your overtime request.
* **Manager Information** (`manager_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your manager's name, email, and job title to ensure your manager is aware of your request.
* **Job Information** (`job_info`, Text, Optional): Provide details about the job or project that requires overtime, such as the job title, description, or task number.
* **Work Period** (`work_period`, Text, Optional): Specify the dates or timeframe for which you are requesting overtime.
* **Manager Confirmation** (`manager_confirmation`, Select One, Optional): Indicate how your manager has confirmed your request, which can be either "Manager Approve" or "Manager Deny".
* **Employee Confirmation** (`employee_confirmation`, Select One, Optional): Confirm or deny the accuracy of your request, which can be either "I confirm the above request is accurate" or "I do not confirm the above request is accurate".
