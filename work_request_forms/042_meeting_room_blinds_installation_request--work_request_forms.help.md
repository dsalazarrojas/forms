# Meeting Room Blinds Installation Request - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Meeting Room Blinds Installation Request form is designed to collect necessary information from users to facilitate the installation of meeting room blinds. This form is used by facilities management or administrative staff to receive requests for installing or replacing meeting room blinds.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Details**: Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. This helps staff in processing your request efficiently.
2. **Room Information**: Enter the details of the meeting room you want to install blinds for. This includes providing information on the floor number and room number. 
3. **Blind Specifications**: Specify the type of blind or shade you require, including the size and color.
4. **Installation Details**: Indicate if you require installation of the blinds, the preferred time slot for installation, and the date of installation.
5. **Additional Notes**: If you have any specific requirements or instructions for the installation, include them in the 'Note' field.
6. **Assignment and Status**: If the form has already been submitted, you can view the assigned staff member and the status of the request.
7. **Submission Details**: Confirm the details of your submission, including the time and date you submitted the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: 
  * Contact Details (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any relevant contact information here.
* **Email** 
  * Email (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your work email address here.
* **Phone** 
  * Phone (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your work phone number here.
* **Room Info** 
  * Room Info (`room_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the details of the meeting room you are requesting blind installation for.
* **floor**
  * floor (`room_floor`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the floor number of the meeting room.
* **room_number**
  * room_number (`room_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the room number of the meeting room.
* **room_capacity**
  * room_capacity (`room_capacity`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the capacity of the meeting room.
* **Blind Specs** 
  * Blind Specs (`blind_specs`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the specifications of the blind or shade you require.
* **Blinds Type**
  * Blinds Type (`blind_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' if you are requesting blind installation or not.
* **Blind Size**
  * Blind Size (`blind_size`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the size of the blind or shade you require.
* **Blind Color**
  * Blind Color (`blind_color`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the color of the blind or shade you require.
* **Installation Time**
  * Installation Time (`installation_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the preferred time slot for installation.
* **Installation Date**
  * Installation Date (`installation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of installation.
* **Note**
  * Note (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Include any additional notes or instructions for the installation.
* **Assigned To**
  * Assigned To (`assigned_to`, `text`, required/optional): This field will display the staff member assigned to process your request.
* **Assigned Time**
  * Assigned Time (`assigned_time`, `time`, required/optional): This field displays the time the request was assigned.
* **Status**
  * Status (`status`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field displays the current status of your request.
* **Form Submitted By**
  * Form Submitted By (`form_submitted_by`, `text`, required/optional): This field displays the name of the person who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted Time**
  * Form Submitted Time (`form_submitted_time`, `time`, required/optional): This field displays the time of submission.
* **Form Submitted Date**
  * Form Submitted Date (`form_submitted_date`, `date`, required/optional): This field displays the date of submission.
