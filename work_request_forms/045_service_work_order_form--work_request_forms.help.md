# Service Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Work Order Form is designed to collect information from customers for a service work order. This form will help us understand the customer's needs and provide them with a proper service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the customer in the "customer_name" field.
2. Provide your phone number in the "customer_phone" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "customer_email" field.
4. Describe the service you are requesting in the "description" field.
5. Specify the type of service you need in the "service_type" field.
6. Select the date you need the service by in the "service_request_date" field.
7. If you have a work order number, enter it in the "work_order_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name** (text): Enter your name as the customer.
* **customer_phone** (text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **customer_email** (email): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **description** (text): Describe the service you are requesting in as much detail as possible.
* **service_type** (text): Specify the type of service you need.
* **service_request_date** (date): Select the date you need the service by.
* **work_order_number** (text): If you have a work order number, enter it here.
