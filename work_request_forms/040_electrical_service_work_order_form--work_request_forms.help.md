# Electrical Service Work Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Service Work Order Form is used to document and track electrical service work orders. It's essential to accurately capture all relevant information about a job, including its status, description, and completion dates.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the job number from the list of available options in the Job Number field.
2. Choose the appropriate job status from the dropdown menu in the Job Status field.
3. Enter the service technician's name in the Service Technician field.
4. Enter the date the job was completed in the Date Completed field.
5. Enter the date the job was opened or started in the Date Open field.
6. Enter the service order ID in the Service Order ID field.
7. Enter the location where the job was performed in the Location field.
8. Write a brief description of the job in the Job Description field.
