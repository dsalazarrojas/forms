# Telecom Service Upgrade Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to process their telecom service upgrade requests. Please use this guide to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Ensure that you enter a valid email address in the Email field.
3. Select your telecom service provider from the dropdown list.
4. Choose the correct service upgrade option (Upgrade, Downgrade, or Maintenance).
5. If you wish to schedule the upgrade, select a time in the Scheduling field.
6. Review your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your account or documentation.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account or used to contact you regarding your service upgrade request.
* **Telecom Service Provider**: Select your current telecom service provider from the dropdown list (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, or Sprint).
* **Service Upgrade Request**: Choose the type of service upgrade you require (Upgrade, Downgrade, or Maintenance). This will help our team understand your request accurately.
* **Scheduling**: If you have a preferred date and time for your service upgrade, select it using the time input field. Please note that scheduling is optional and may not be guaranteed.
