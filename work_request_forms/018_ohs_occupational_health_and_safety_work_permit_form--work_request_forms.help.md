# OHS (Occupational Health And Safety) Work Permit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for Occupational Health And Safety (OHS) work permits. It helps supervisors and employees ensure that work is performed safely by providing necessary details about the work being done, the risks involved, and the precautions taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **General Information** section with your name, site name, and other relevant details about the work you are performing.
2. Conduct a **Site Risk Assessment** and describe any potential hazards you may encounter.
3. Specify the **Personal Protection Equipment** you will use to minimize risks.
4. Complete the **Permit to Work** section by describing the work being performed and the date issued.
5. Define the **Work Area Definition** where the work will be performed.
6. Describe the **Hazard Control** measures you will take to mitigate risks.
7. Outline an **Emergency Plan** in case of an emergency.
8. Provide **Site Specifics** information, such as weather conditions, etc.
9. Sign the **Supervisor Signature** and **Employee Signature** sections to confirm understanding and agreement.
10. Choose the **Permit Status** from the options provided.
11. Enter the **Date Issued** and **Permit Expiration Date** if applicable.
12. Fill out the **Permit Issuer** section with your name and role.
13. Enter the **Site Name**, **Supervisor Name**, and **Employee Name** relevant to the work being performed.
14. Describe the **Work Type** and **Job Number** of the work being performed.
15. Enter the **Permit Number** and **Equipment Used** if applicable.
16. Specify the **Equipment Type** and **Equipment Condition** of the equipment being used.
17. Provide the **Emergency Contact** information in case of an emergency.
18. Add any additional **Other Info** relevant to the work being performed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (text): Enter your name, site name, and other relevant details about the work being performed.
* **Site Risk Assessment** (text): Describe any potential hazards you may encounter while working on the site.
* **Personal Protection Equipment** (text): List the protective gear you will use to minimize risks.
* **Permit to Work** (text): Describe the work being performed, including the date issued.
* **Work Area Definition** (text): Define the specific area where the work will be performed.
* **Hazard Control** (text): Describe the measures you will take to mitigate risks.
* **Emergency Plan** (text): Outline the steps to take in case of an emergency.
* **Supervisor Signature** (text): Sign to confirm you understand and agree to the permit conditions.
* **Employee Signature** (text): Sign to confirm you understand and agree to the permit conditions.
* **Permit Status** (select one): Choose the status of the permit from the options provided.
* **Date Issued** (date): Enter the date the permit was issued.
* **Permit Expiration Date** (date): Enter the date the permit expires.
* **Permit Issuer** (text): Enter your name and role as the permit issuer.
* **Site Name** (text): Enter the site name where the work will be performed.
* **Supervisor Name** (text): Enter the supervisor's name relevant to the work being performed.
* **Employee Name** (text): Enter your name as the employee performing the work.
* **Work Type** (text): Describe the type of work being performed.
* **Job Number** (text): Enter the job number associated with the work.
* **Permit Number** (text): Enter the permit number issued for the work.
* **Equipment Used** (text): List the equipment used for the work, if applicable.
* **Equipment Type** (text): Specify the type of equipment used for the work.
* **Equipment Condition** (text): Specify the condition of the equipment used for the work.
* **Emergency Contact** (text): Provide the emergency contact information.
* **Other Info** (text): Enter any additional information relevant to the work being performed.

## Tips
* Always fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure you understand and agree to the permit conditions before signing.
* Keep the permit up to date and review it regularly.
