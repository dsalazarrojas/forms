# Hotel Renovation Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Renovation Requisition Form is designed to collect and manage renovation requests from the hotel's operations team. This form ensures that all necessary information is gathered and communicated effectively, streamlining the renovation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Review the form carefully and ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
2.  Fill out the "Priority" field with the level of importance you assign to this renovation request. Options include:
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
    *   Urgent
3.  Enter the estimated completion date for the renovation project.
4.  Select the person(s) who submitted this form.
5.  Enter the actual completion date for the project, if known.
6.  Enter the total renovation cost.
7.  Select the assigned tool for this project.
8.  Provide a list of any additional contact details for the person submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Priority** (Required)
    *   The Priority field indicates the level of importance assigned to this renovation project.
* **Estimated Completion Date** (Optional)
    *   Enter the estimated date when the renovation project is expected to be completed.
* **Actual Completion Date** (Optional)
    *   Enter the date when the renovation project was actually completed.
* **Renovation Cost** (Optional)
    *   Enter the total cost for the renovation project.
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional)
    *   Select the tool assigned to the renovation project.
* **Submitted By** (Required)
    *   Select the person who submitted this form for the renovation project.
* **Notes** (Optional)
    *   Enter any additional notes or comments about the project.
* **Email** (Optional)
    *   Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Optional)
    *   Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Fax** (Optional)
    *   Enter the fax number of the contact person.
* **Address** (Required)
    *   Select the address of the contact person.
* **City** (Optional)
    *   Enter the city of the contact person.
* **State** (Optional)
    *   Enter the state of the contact person.
* **Zip** (Optional)
    *   Enter the zip code of the contact person.
* **Contact Name** (Optional)
    *   Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Job Title** (Optional)
    *   Enter the job title of the contact person.
* **Contact Company** (Optional)
    *   Enter the name of the company of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (Optional)
    *   Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Optional)
    *   Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Fax** (Optional)
    *   Enter the fax number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (Optional)
    *   Select the address of the contact person.
* **Contact Has Fax** (Optional)
    *   Select whether the contact person has a fax number.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by a hotel's operations team member to request a renovation project. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
