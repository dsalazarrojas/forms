# Special Handling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Handling Request Form is used by customers to submit their requests for special handling of their deliveries. The form allows customers to provide details about their requests, including delivery dates, locations, and methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the customer in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
3. Select the **Request Date** for when you would like to receive your delivery.
4. Choose your preferred **Delivery Location** from the list.
5. Select your preferred **Delivery Method** from the list.
6. Enter the address where you would like to be picked up from in the **Pickup Location** field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Your full name as the customer submitting this request.
* **Customer Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Operation ID**: A unique identifier for your request, used by internal staff for reference.
* **Request Date**: The date when you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Location**: The location where you would like to receive your delivery.
* **Delivery Method**: The method of delivery you prefer (e.g. "Yes" for standard delivery, "No" for in-store pickup).
* **Pickup Location**: The address where you need to be picked up from.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your request.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the form accurately and completely to avoid any confusion or errors.
* Ensure that you do not duplicate this form for the same customer within 7 days.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact our customer support team for assistance.
