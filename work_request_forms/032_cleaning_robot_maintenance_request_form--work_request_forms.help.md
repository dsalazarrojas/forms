# Cleaning Robot Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a comprehensive overview of your cleaning robot's maintenance and repair needs. This will help our team to better understand the issues you are experiencing and provide a more accurate diagnosis and service plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please provide as much detail as possible in each field. This will ensure that our team has the necessary information to provide the best possible service for your cleaning robot.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, required): Your first name
* **Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, required): Your last name
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Contact email address
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Contact phone number
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Your mailing address
* **City** (`city`, text, required): City or town
* **State/Province** (`state`, text, required): State or province
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Postal code
* **Robot Brand** (`robot_brand`, select_one, required): Manufacturer of your cleaning robot
* **Robot Model** (`robot_model`, text, required): Specific model number or name
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text, optional): Robot serial number
* **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, optional): When did you purchase the robot
* **Warranty Status** (`warranty_status`, select_one, required): Is your robot still under warranty
* **How Long Have You Owned the Robot** (`robot_age`, select_one, required): Approximate age
* **Issue Category** (`issue_category`, select_one, required): What type of problem are you experiencing
* **Detailed Problem Description** (`issue_description`, text, required): Please describe the issue in detail
* **When Did the Issue Start** (`issue_start_date`, date, required): Date when problem began
* **How Often Does the Issue Occur** (`issue_frequency`, select_one, required): Frequency of the problem
* **Error Messages or Codes** (`error_messages`, text, optional): Any error messages displayed
* **Troubleshooting Steps Attempted** (`troubleshooting_attempted`, text, optional): What have you tried to fix the issue
* **Service Type Needed** (`service_type_needed`, select_one, required): What type of service do you need
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`, date, required): When would you like service
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`preferred_appointment_time`, select_one, required): What time works best
* **Service Location Preference** (`service_location`, select_one, required): Where should service be performed
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): How urgently do you need service
* **Accessories Included for Service** (`accessories_included`, select_multiple, required): What are you sending in for service
* **Has the Robot Been Repaired Before** (`previous_repair_details`, select_one, required): Any previous repair history
* **Previous Repair Details** (`previous_repair_details`, text, optional): Describe previous repairs
* **How Did You Hear About Our Service** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): How did you find us
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Anything else you would like to share
* **Quote Needed** (`quote_needed`, select_one, required): Would you like a quote before service
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required): Your electronic signature
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date, required): Date of this request

## Tips

* Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible.
* If you have any additional information or attachments, please provide them with your submission.
* Our team will review your submission and contact you to discuss the next steps.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that this form is a one-time submission. If you have any further questions or concerns, you can contact us directly.
* If you are requesting a quote, please ensure that you have answered all required fields correctly.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
