# Course Cleanup Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request maintenance and updates for courses that are not currently being taught or have already been completed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the course title in the "Course Title" field.
2. If the course has a team lead, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Team Lead" field.
3. If you are requesting maintenance or updates for multiple teams, select all applicable teams from the "Team" field.
4. Enter the date of the maintenance or update in the "Date" field.
5. If you have specific schedule updates, enter them in the "Schedule Update" field.
6. If the course requires maintenance, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Maintenance Request" field.
7. Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
8. If you want to be contacted via email, enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. If you want to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
10. Enter the scheduled date of the maintenance or update in the "Scheduled Date" field.
11. Enter the due date for the maintenance or update in the "Due Date" field.
12. Enter the scheduled time of the maintenance or update in the "Scheduled Time" field.
13. Enter the end date of the maintenance or update in the "End Date" field.
14. Enter the end time of the maintenance or update in the "End Time" field.
15. If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter their email address in the "Team Lead Email" field.
16. If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter their phone number in the "Team Lead Phone" field.
17. If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Team Lead Schedule" field.
18. Enter any comments from the team lead in the "Team Lead Comments" field.
19. If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Submitted By" field.
20. If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter your email address in the "Submitted By Email" field.
21. If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter their phone number in the "Submitted By Phone" field.
22. If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Submitted By Schedule" field.
23. Enter any comments from the team lead in the "Submitted By Comments" field.
24. Enter the course name in the "Course Name" field.
25. Enter the current course status in the "Course Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Title** (1, text, required): Enter the title of the course you are requesting maintenance or updates for.
* **Team Lead** (2, select_one, optional): If the course has a team lead, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate their involvement.
* **Team** (3, select_multiple, optional): If you are requesting maintenance or updates for multiple teams, select all applicable teams.
* **Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the date of the maintenance or update.
* **Schedule Update** (5, text, optional): Enter any specific schedule updates you have.
* **Maintenance Request** (6, select_multiple, optional): If the course requires maintenance, select "Yes" or "No".
* **Comments** (7, note, optional): Enter any additional comments you have.
* **Email** (8, email, optional): If you want to be contacted via email, enter your email address.
* **Phone** (9, text, optional): If you want to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number.
* **Scheduled Date** (10, date, optional): Enter the scheduled date of the maintenance or update.
* **Due Date** (11, date, optional): Enter the due date for the maintenance or update.
* **Scheduled Time** (12, time, optional): Enter the scheduled time of the maintenance or update.
* **End Date** (13, date, optional): Enter the end date of the maintenance or update.
* **End Time** (14, time, optional): Enter the end time of the maintenance or update.
* **Team Lead Email** (15, email, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter their email address.
* **Team Lead Phone** (16, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter their phone number.
* **Team Lead Schedule** (17, select_multiple, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Team Lead Schedule" field.
* **Team Lead Comments** (18, note, optional): Enter any comments from the team lead.
* **Submitted By** (19, select_one, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, select "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitted By Email** (20, email, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter their email address.
* **Submitted By Phone** (21, text, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, enter their phone number.
* **Submitted By Schedule** (22, select_multiple, optional): If you are submitting on behalf of the team lead, select "Yes" or "No" from the "Submitted By Schedule" field.
* **Submitted By Comments** (23, note, optional): Enter any comments from the team lead.
* **Course Name** (24, text, optional): Enter the course name.
* **Course Status** (25, text, optional): Enter the current course status.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the course title and date are identical to the course you are requesting maintenance or updates for.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your team lead or the course administrator for clarification.
* This form is not intended for courses that are currently being taught or have not yet started.
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out this form to ensure that your request is properly processed.
