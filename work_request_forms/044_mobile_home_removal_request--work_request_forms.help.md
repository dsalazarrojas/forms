# mobile_home_removal_request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for individuals or their representatives who wish to report a mobile home on a site. It helps the site owners, managers, or relevant authorities understand the reasons for removal and gather the necessary information to process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide the site details where the mobile home is located.
2. Share your contact information so we can get in touch with you for further clarification or updates.
3. Clearly explain the reasons for removal, including any supporting information or evidence.
4. Attach any relevant documents or images that support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Site Details** (`<title>`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the site address, location, or any other relevant details about the site where the mobile home is located.
- **Contact Information** (`<title>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, or any other contact method we can use to get in touch with you for further clarification or updates.
- **Reasons For Removal** (`<title>`, `text`, required/optional): Clearly explain the reasons for removal, including any supporting information or evidence. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your request.
- **Uploads** (`<title>`, `text`, required/optional): Attach any relevant documents or images that support your request for removal. This could be photos, videos, or any other file types that help us understand your case.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when providing reasons for removal to ensure a smooth process.
- Attach clear and legible documents or images for the "Uploads" field.
- If you are unable to complete the form in one session, you can save your progress and resume later by using the "Save Progress" feature.
