# on_site_team_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about on-site team activities, progress, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the shift you are reporting on.
2. Enter the site location and any relevant updates.
3. Report your tasks completed and their progress.
4. Enter any necessary notes or comments.
5. Select the next shift.
6. Enter the shift start and end times, if applicable.
7. Enter the team size and task progress percentage.
8. Select the shift status and report any weather or site conditions.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Shift**: Enter the name of the shift you are reporting on.
* **Site**: Enter the site location and any relevant updates.
* **Activity**: Report your activities and tasks completed during the shift.
* **Task Progress**: Report the progress of your tasks.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of the shift, if applicable.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of the shift, if applicable.
* **Site Location**: Enter the site location.
* **Team Leader**: Enter the team leader's name.
* **Supervisor**: Enter the supervisor's name.
* **Team Member**: Enter the team member's name.
* **Shift Start Date**: Enter the start date of the shift, if applicable.
* **Shift End Date**: Enter the end date of the shift, if applicable.
* **Task Completed**: Select all tasks completed during the shift.
* **Shift Dedication**: Enter the dedication level of the shift (e.g., 1-10).
* **Site Condition**: Enter the site condition.
* **Weather**: Enter the weather conditions.
* **Shift Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Supervisor Notes**: Enter any notes for the supervisor.
* **Next Shift**: Select the next shift.
* **Team Size**: Enter the size of the team.
* **Task Progress Percent**: Enter the progress percentage of tasks completed.
* **Shift Status**: Enter the status of the shift.
* **Form Submitted By**: Enter your name, for reference.

## Tips
* Make sure to report accurate and up-to-date information.
* Enter all required fields.
* Review and edit your submission before submitting.
