# email_design_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The email_design_questionnaire form is a tool to gather information about customer preferences for their email communications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form with your contact information and preferences.
2.  Select the relevant fields for your email design needs.
3.  Provide detailed information in the text fields (Customer Details, Email Content, Email Subject, and Email Body).
4.  Choose the design preferences that best match your style.
5.  Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter your name, contact email address, and any other relevant details that the designer should know about you.

* **Email Content** (`email_content`, `text`, required: false): 
    Describe the main topic or theme you want to communicate in your email.

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `select_one`, required: false): 
    Choose how you would like to be contacted by email (Email Address, Phone Number, or Mobile Number). If unsure, select None.

* **Business Details** (`business_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Select the information that is relevant to your business (Business Name, Company Address, Business Phone Number, or Fax Number).

* **Visual Material** (`visual_material`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
    Select the visual elements that you would like to see in your email (Logo, Image, Icon, or Image Background).

* **Design Preferences** (`design_preferences`, `select_one`, required: false): 
    Choose a design style that suits your preference (Simple, Fancy, or Minimalist).

* **Email Subject** (`email_subject`, `text`, required: false): 
    Enter a brief and descriptive subject for your email.

* **Email Body** (`email_body`, `text`, required: false): 
    Type the main content of your email that you want to communicate.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible in the text fields.
- Select all applicable options for Personal and Business Details.
- Choose design preferences that align with your brand identity.
- Ensure that the Email Subject and Email Body are clear and concise.
- If you have any specific requirements or requests, include them in the Email Body field.
