# Equipment Safety Shield Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Safety Shield Replacement Request Form is a tool for equipment operators to request a replacement for their safety shield in case of loss, damage, or malfunction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you are the actual user of the equipment or have been authorized to make the request.
2. Enter the required information in the fields below, being as accurate and thorough as possible.
3. If you have any supporting documentation or photos, attach them to the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Safety Shield Replacement Request** (`equipment_safety_sheild_replacement_request`, text, optional): Briefly describe the reason for the replacement request.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the equipment, including its serial number, model, and any relevant specifications.
* **Issue** (`issue`, text, optional): Describe the issue with the safety shield, including any symptoms or errors you have encountered.
* **Photo** (`photo`, text, optional): Attach a photo of the safety shield, the issue, or any other relevant documentation that supports your request.
* **Safety Notes** (`safety_notes`, text, optional): Share any additional safety concerns or notes related to the equipment and the issue.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the safety shield is currently in use or where it was last used.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the equipment manual or manufacturer's documentation for guidance.
* Submitting multiple requests for the same equipment may lead to delays or cancellations of the replacement process.
