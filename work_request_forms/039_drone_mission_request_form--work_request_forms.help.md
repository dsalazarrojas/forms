# Drone Mission Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drone Mission Request Form is designed to collect information for drone missions, ensuring that all necessary details are gathered to facilitate the mission's execution. Please use this form to provide a comprehensive overview of your mission requirements and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise description of your drone mission in the **Drone Mission Details** field.
2. Enter your **User Details** in the **User Details** field, including your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
3. Specify the **Mission Requirements** in the **Mission Requirements** field, including any specific tasks or objectives for the mission.
4. Enter your **Submission** in the **Submission** field, which should include any additional information or attachments related to your mission.
5. If necessary, provide **Approval** or feedback in the **Approval** field.
6. Add any **Comments** regarding your mission in the **Comments** field.
7. Review and confirm your submission in the **Review** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Drone Mission Details** (`drone_mission_details`, text, optional): This field is for providing a clear and concise description of your drone mission. Please include all necessary details about the mission, such as the objectives, location, and any specific requirements or tasks.
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and other relevant contact information. This will help the mission team contact you regarding your request.
* **Mission Requirements** (`mission_requirements`, text, optional): Specify the specific requirements and objectives for the mission. This can include any tasks, deadlines, or other important details.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or attachments related to your mission. This can include photos, videos, or documents that support your request.
* **Approval** (`approval`, text, optional): If you have any approval or feedback for the mission team, please enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or suggestions for the mission team regarding your request.
* **Review** (`review`, text, optional): Double-check your submission to ensure it is accurate and complete before finalizing it.

## Tips
Please ensure that you have reviewed your submission carefully before submitting it. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
