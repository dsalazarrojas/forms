# Automatic Door Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who are requesting service for their automatic door systems. It will help the service team understand the customer's needs and provide the necessary information to complete the job efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and street address.
2. Select the type of service you need (e.g., repair, maintenance, or inspection).
3. Describe the issue with your automatic door system, including any symptoms you've experienced.
4. Choose the urgency level of your request, with options ranging from emergency to scheduled maintenance.
5. Select the type of safety concern, if applicable.
6. Provide your preferred service date and time, as well as your preferred time of arrival.
7. Indicate your availability for after-hours service, if desired.
8. Specify how to access the service location, including building access and on-site contact information.
9. If applicable, provide any previous service history, including the last service date and the previous service provider.
10. Finally, review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, required): This section is where you'll enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and street address.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`city`, required): Enter the city where you're located.
* **State** (`state`, required): Select the state where you're located from the provided options.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, required): Enter your 5-digit ZIP code.
* **Service Location** (`service_location`, optional): This is an optional field for describing the location where the service is needed.
* **Building Type** (`building_type`, required): Select the type of building where the service is needed from the provided options.
* **Door Location** (`door_location`, required): Select the location of the door where service is needed from the provided options.
* **Door Information** (`door_info`, optional): This is an optional field for providing additional details about the door.
* **Door Type** (`door_type`, required): Select the type of automatic door from the provided options.
* **Door Manufacturer** (`door_manufacturer`, optional): If you know the brand of your door, enter it here.
* **Door Model** (`door_model`, optional): If you know the model of your door, enter it here.
* **Installation Date** (`installation_date`, optional): If you know the installation date of your door, enter it here.
* **Number of Doors** (`number_of_doors`, required): Enter the number of doors that need service.
* **Service Information** (`service_info`, optional): This is an optional field for providing additional information about the service needed.
* **Type of Service** (`service_type`, required): Select the type of service you need from the provided options.
* **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, required): Describe the issue with your automatic door system.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, optional): Select any relevant symptoms you've experienced from the provided options.
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency`, required): Select the level of urgency for your request from the provided options.
* **Safety Concern** (`safety_concern`, required): Select any safety concerns from the provided options.
* **Safety Details** (`safety_details`, optional): If you have a safety concern, provide more details here.
* **Appointment Preferences** (`appointment_preferences`, optional): This is an optional field for expressing your service preferences.
* **Preferred Service Date** (`preferred_date`, required): Enter the date you prefer for service.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, optional): If you prefer a specific time for service, enter it here.

* **Access Information** (`building_access`, required): Select how you can access the service location from the provided options.
