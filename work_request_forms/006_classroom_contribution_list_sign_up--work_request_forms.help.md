# Classroom Contribution List Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect classroom contribution information from parents or guardians for specific weeks and students. Please ensure you fill out the form once per week and per student to avoid any duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with the student's information and contribution details.
- Select the contribution item and week of contribution that applies to your child.
- Provide a specific quantity or description of the contribution in the "Quantity or Specifics" field.
- If your child has any dietary notes or allergies, please mention them in the "Any dietary notes or allergens?" field.
- If you would like to receive a reminder, select "True" in the "Would you like a text reminder the day before?" field.
- You may leave the "Thank you for supporting our classroom!" field blank, it's optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Full Name** (`parent_full_name`, `text`, required): Fill out your full name so we can contact you regarding your contribution.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you for any further information or updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your child's name who is contributing.
* **Classroom Number or Grade** (`classroom_number`, `text`, required): Enter your child's classroom number or grade level.
* **Contribution Item** (`contribution_item`, `select_one`, required): Choose from the list of available contribution items you would like to contribute.
* **Week of Contribution** (`week_of_contribution`, `select_one`, required): Choose the week when you will be contributing.
* **Quantity or Specifics** (`quantity_description`, `text`, required): Provide a specific quantity or description of your contribution.
* **Any dietary notes or allergens?** (`dietary_notes`, `text`, required): If your child has any dietary notes or allergies, please mention them here.
* **Would you like a text reminder the day before?** (`reminder_opt_in`, `select_one`, required): Select "True" if you want a reminder the day before the contribution.
* **Thank you for supporting our classroom!** (`thank_you_note`, `note`, optional): This is an optional field for a thank you message.

Please fill out the form carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
