# District Council Assessment Visit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule and document visits from the district council. It helps gather essential information about the visit, including the purpose, location, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Form Title" field to give a concise and descriptive title to the visit.
2. Fill in the "Form Description" field to provide a brief description of the visit's purpose and scope.
3. Choose the type of assessment you require:
	* Smoke: For smoke-related assessments.
	* Heat: For heat-related assessments.
	* Multi Sensor: For multi-sensor assessments.
	* Carbon Monoxide: For carbon monoxide assessments.
4. Select the location(s) where the assessment will take place:
	* Office: For assessments conducted in the office.
	* Site: For assessments conducted at a specific site.
	* Other: For assessments conducted elsewhere.
5. Enter the date of the assessment using the date picker.
6. Enter the time of the assessment using the time picker.
7. Enter the duration of the assessment in hours.
8. Add any additional notes about the visit in the "Additional Notes" field.
9. Enter your contact number.
10. Enter your email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Provide a concise and descriptive title for the visit.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the purpose and scope of the visit.
* **Assessment Type** (`assessment_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of assessment you require.
* **Assessment Location** (`assessment_location`, select multiple, optional): Select the location(s) where the assessment will take place.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time** (`assessment_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the assessment.
* **Assessment Time Duration** (`assessment_time_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the assessment in hours.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the visit.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

Note: All fields are optional, except where noted.
