# Resort Amenities Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resort Amenities Requisition Form is used to request amenities or services from the resort. This form is intended for guests to submit requests for amenities and services they would like to receive during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) so we can contact you directly.
2. Select the amenities or services you would like to request (e.g., extra towels, extra toiletries, etc.).
3. Choose your preferred date and time for delivery or pickup of the requested amenities or services.
4. Let us know if you would like to request staff assistance with your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`resort_amenities_requisition_form`): This is the title of the form.
- **Guest Info** (`guest_info`): Enter your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you.
- **Amenity Request** (`amenity_request`): Describe the amenities or services you would like to request.
- **Room Number** (`room_number`): Enter your room number so we can locate you quickly.
- **Date** (`date`): Choose a date for delivery or pickup of the requested amenities or services.
- **Time** (`time`): Choose a time for delivery or pickup of the requested amenities or services.
- **Staff Info** (`staff_info`): Enter any information about your needs or requests that may require staff assistance.
- **Staff Request** (`staff_request`): Let us know if you would like staff to assist with your needs.
- **Request Type** (`request_type`): Select if this is a new or ongoing request.
- **Request Status** (`request_status`): Select the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
- **Form Comments** (`form_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
- **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you directly.
- **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you directly.
- **Fax** (`fax`): Enter your fax number (if applicable).
- **Submit** (`submit`): Confirm that you have reviewed and completed the form accurately.
- **Submit Status** (`submit_status`): Confirm the status of your request (Active or Inactive).
- **Staff Email** (`staff_email`): Enter the email address of the staff member assisting you.
- **Staff Phone** (`staff_phone`): Enter the phone number of the staff member assisting you.
- **Staff Fax** (`staff_fax`): Enter the fax number of the staff member assisting you.
- **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes from the manager.
- **Approver** (`approver`): Confirm if this request has been approved by a manager.
- **Approver Status** (`approver_status`): Confirm the status of your request as seen by the approver (Active or Inactive).
- **Approver Phone** (`approver_phone`): Enter the phone number of the approver.
- **Approver Email** (`approver_email`): Enter the email address of the approver.
- **Submitter** (`submitter`): Confirm if this request was submitted by a staff member.

Note: Please review and complete the form accurately, as it will be used to fulfill your requests and communicate with you directly. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
