# Warehouse Layout Redesign Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about the current state of the warehouse and your proposed changes to the layout. The goal is to gather input from users to help inform improvements to the warehouse's layout.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by describing your warehouse in general.
2.  List specific layout issues you've identified, including:
    -   Blockages or bottlenecks preventing efficient movement.
    -   Safety concerns such as uneven flooring, poor lighting, or inadequate fire safety measures.
3.  Specify your goals for the redesigned layout, including:
    -   Increased efficiency.
    -   Improved safety.
    -   Better workflow organization.
4.  Identify the required improvements, including:
    -   New features or amenities.
    -   Renovations or expansions.
5.  Upload any supporting files, such as images or videos, that demonstrate your proposed changes.
6.  Add any additional notes or comments about your design.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Warehouse Details (1)** (`text`, **Required: No**): Describe your warehouse in general, including its size, location, and existing features.
*  **Layout Issues (2)** (`select_multiple`, **Required: No**): Identify specific layout issues, such as:
    -   Option 1: Blockages or bottlenecks.
    -   Option 2: Safety concerns like uneven flooring, poor lighting, or inadequate fire safety measures.
    -   Option 3: Other (please specify).
*  **Goals (3)** (`text`, **Required: No**): Specify your goals for the redesigned layout, including:
    -   Increased efficiency.
    -   Improved safety.
    -   Better workflow organization.
*  **Required Improvements (4)** (`select_one`, **Required: No**): Identify the required improvements, including:
    -   Option 1: New features or amenities.
    -   Option 2: Renovations or expansions.
    -   Option 3: Other (please specify).
*  **File Uploads (5)** (`text`, **Required: No**): Upload any images, videos, or other files that demonstrate your proposed changes.
*  **Notes (6)** (`note`, **Required: No**): Add any additional notes or comments about your design.
