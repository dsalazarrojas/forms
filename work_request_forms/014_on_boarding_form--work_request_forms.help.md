# On Boarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The On Boarding Form is used to collect essential information from new users when they join the company, making it easier for management to onboard them properly and provide the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the guidelines below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2. Double-check the options in the "User Role" and "Status" fields to ensure you select the correct options.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Select the correct "On Boarding Date" when prompted.
5. Add any relevant "Manager Note" for further reference.
6. If necessary, add comments about your onboarding experience in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your unique user ID number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **User Role** (`user_role`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are a new user, "No" otherwise.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **On Boarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of your onboarding.
* **Manager Note** (`manager_note`, note, required/optional): Add any notes from your manager for further reference.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Active" if you are active, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are assigned a tool, "No" otherwise.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any comments about your onboarding experience.
