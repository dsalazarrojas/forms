# Claims Service Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Claims Service Report form is a crucial tool for service teams to document and report on services performed, ensuring accurate and efficient communication with clients. It captures essential details such as service dates, types, and status, and client feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Claims Service Report form effectively, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the service performed, including the date, type of service, and client details.
2. Fill in the `Date of Service` field with the actual date of service.
3. Select the type of service provided from the `Type of Service Provided` dropdown menu.
4. Update the status of the work in the `Current Status of Work` dropdown menu, indicating whether the work is completed, in progress, on hold, or awaiting approval or parts.
5. Provide a concise summary of the work performed in the `Summary of Work Performed Today` field.
6. If applicable, mention any materials or equipment used in the `Materials or Equipment Used` field.
7. Enter the total labor hours spent on the service in the `Total Labor Hours` field.
8. If any issues or delays were encountered, describe them in the `Any issues or delays encountered?` field.
9. Indicate whether follow-up work is required in the `Is follow-up work required?` dropdown menu.
10. If follow-up work is required, propose a follow-up date in the `Proposed Follow-up Date` field.
11. Record the client's satisfaction level in the `Is the client satisfied with today's work?` dropdown menu.
12. Finally, take a moment to record any internal notes about the service performed, including any discussions or observations made during the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. Date of Service
• `service_date` (Date of Service, Date, required): Enter the actual date of service.

### 3. Service Technician Name
• `service_technician` (Service Technician Name, Text, required): Enter the name of the service technician who performed the service.

### 4. Claim Reference Number
• `claim_reference` (Claim Reference Number, Text, required): Enter the reference number associated with the claim.

### 5. Client Name
• `client_name` (Client Name, Text, required): Enter the name of the client.

### 6. Type of Service Provided
• `service_type` (Type of Service Provided, Select One, required): Select the type of service provided from the dropdown menu.

### 7. Current Status of Work
• `status_update` (Current Status of Work, Select One, required): Update the status of the work, indicating whether the work is completed, in progress, on hold, or awaiting approval or parts.

### 8. Summary of Work Performed Today
• `work_description` (Summary of Work Performed Today, Text, required): Provide a brief summary of the work performed.

### 9. Materials or Equipment Used
• `materials_used` (Materials or Equipment Used, Text, optional): Mention any materials or equipment used during the service.

### 10. Total Labor Hours
• `hours_worked` (Total Labor Hours, Number, required): Enter the total labor hours spent on the service.

### 11. Any Issues or Delays Encountered?
• `issues_encountered` (Any issues or delays encountered?, Text, optional): Describe any issues or delays that occurred during the service.

### 12. Is Follow-up Work Required?
• `follow_up_needed` (Is follow-up work required?, Select One, required): Indicate whether follow-up work is required.

### 13. Proposed Follow-up Date
• `follow_up_date` (Proposed Follow-up Date, Date, optional): If follow-up work is required, propose a follow-up date.

### 14. Is the Client Satisfied with Today's Work?
• `client_satisfied` (Is the client satisfied with today's work?, Select One, required): Record the client's satisfaction level.

### 15. Client Acknowledgement
• `client_acknowledgement` (Client Acknowledgement, Note, optional): Take a moment to record the client's acknowledgement.

### 16. Technician Internal Notes
• `technician_notes` (Technician Internal Notes, Text, optional): Record internal notes about the service, including any discussions or observations made during the service.
