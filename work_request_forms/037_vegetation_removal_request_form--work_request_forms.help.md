This is a guide to help users understand and complete the Vegetation Removal Request Form.

## Purpose
The Vegetation Removal Request Form is designed to collect information about vegetation removal requests. This form is intended for use by property owners or their representatives to request the removal of unwanted vegetation on a specific property. This guide will walk you through how to complete the form, what each field means, and provide tips to ensure accurate and helpful submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as indicated by the *required* label.
2. Provide as much detail as possible in the text fields.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Request Form**
(Request Form, required)
* This field is the main request section of the form. Please describe the specific vegetation issue or concern you are experiencing, such as overgrown shrubs, dead trees, or weeds.

### **Property Details**
(Property Details, required)
* This field is used to identify the property where the vegetation issue exists. Please include the property's address or a brief description of the location.

### **Tree Details**
(Tree Details, required)
* This field is used to provide more information about the tree or trees in question. Please describe the size, species, and any other distinctive features of the tree.

### **Property Owner Form**
(Property Owner Form, required)
* This field is used to identify the property owner or their representative. Please include your name, contact information, and any relevant contact details.

### **Submit Request**
(Submit Request, required)
* This field is used to confirm your request and provide any additional comments or questions. Please ensure to include your expected response time and any other relevant details.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the vegetation issue.
* Include photos or videos of the affected area, if possible.
* Provide clear and concise contact information for property owner or representative.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

By following this guide, you should be able to accurately and effectively complete the Vegetation Removal Request Form.
