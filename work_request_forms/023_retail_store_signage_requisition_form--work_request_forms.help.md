# Retail Store Signage Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Store Signage Requisition Form helps store management and production teams coordinate the installation of store signage, ensuring that all necessary information is captured for efficient production and installation processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Select the store location where the signage will be installed.
2. Choose the type of signage you need (A-boards, Directional, Logo, Price, Promotional, or Welcome).
3. Provide a brief description of the signage you require.
4. Specify the quantity of signage needed.
5. Enter the desired installation date and time.
6. If applicable, include any additional notes or comments.
7. Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
8. Enter your store manager's contact information.
9. Enter your production coordinator's contact information.
10. Enter your production manager's contact information.
11. Enter your installation coordinator's contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Location** (`store_location`, text, Optional): Enter the store location where the signage will be installed.
* **Signage Type** (`signage_type`, select_one, Optional): Select the type of signage you need (A-boards, Directional, Logo, Price, Promotional, or Welcome).
* **Description** (`description`, text, Optional): Provide a brief description of the signage you require.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, Optional): Specify the quantity of signage needed.
* **Location** (`location`, text, Optional): Enter the location of the signage within the store.
* **Priority** (`priority`, select_one, Optional): Choose the priority level of the signage request (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, date, Optional): Enter the date and time when the signage is required for installation.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, time, Optional): Enter the due date for the installation of the signage.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, Optional): If applicable, include any additional notes or comments.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up purposes.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, Optional): Enter your store manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, Optional): Enter your store manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your store manager's phone number.
* **Store Manager** (`store_manager`, text, Optional): Enter your store manager's name.
* **Store Manager Email** (`store_manager_email`, email, Optional): Enter your store manager's email address.
* **Store Manager Phone** (`store_manager_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your store manager's phone number.
* **Production Coordinator** (`production_coordinator`, text, Optional): Enter your production coordinator's name.
* **Production Coordinator Email** (`production_coordinator_email`, email, Optional): Enter your production coordinator's email address.
* **Production Coordinator Phone** (`production_coordinator_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your production coordinator's phone number.
* **Production Manager** (`production_manager`, text, Optional): Enter your production manager's name.
* **Production Manager Email** (`production_manager_email`, email, Optional): Enter your production manager's email address.
* **Production Manager Phone** (`production_manager_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your production manager's phone number.
* **Installation Coordinator** (`installation_coordinator`, text, Optional): Enter your installation coordinator's name.
* **Installation Coordinator Email** (`installation_coordinator_email`, email, Optional): Enter your installation coordinator's email address.
* **Installation Coordinator Phone** (`installation_coordinator_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your installation coordinator's phone number.

## Tips
* Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* Ensure that your submission adheres to your store's official policies and procedures.
* If you're unsure about any field or requirement, consult with your store manager or production team members for clarification.
* Keep your contact information up to date to facilitate follow-up communication.
