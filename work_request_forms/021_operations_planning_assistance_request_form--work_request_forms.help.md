# Operations Planning Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide operations planning assistance requests. It's intended for individuals to request assistance from operations teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Enter a brief description of your request in the description field.
3. If you're requesting assistance on a specific date and time, select it in the date and time fields.
4. Indicate your request's priority level (optional).
5. Update the status of your request once it's been processed (required).
6. Specify whether you're requesting a 'Yes' or 'No' response for your request type (optional).
7. Provide the location where you're requesting assistance (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you about your request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your request for the operations team to understand your needs.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the specific date you're requesting assistance on.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the specific time you're requesting assistance on.
* **Priority** (`priority`, number, optional): Indicate the level of priority for your request, with higher numbers representing higher priorities.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Update the status of your request to let us know it's been processed or not.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one, optional): Specify whether you're requesting 'Yes' or 'No' response for your request.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Provide the location where you're requesting assistance.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have all required information before submitting the form to avoid delays.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the operations team for clarification.
* Please note that some fields may have specific requirements or constraints that must be met.
