This form is designed for stakeholders to provide a response to a Corrective and Preventative Action (CAPA) request. A CAPA is a process used to address a specific issue that has been identified in a production or non-production environment. The form will help to capture key information regarding the issue, the corrective actions taken, and the preventative actions planned to ensure the issue doesn't occur again in the future. The goal is to ensure that the issue is effectively and efficiently addressed, and that the necessary information is documented in a clear and concise manner.

The following guide will walk you through the process of completing the BPT CAPA Response (Stake Holder) Form 2019.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2.  Begin by providing CAPA information (Page 1).
3.  Enter the CAPA number and title, and select the issue identification date.
4.  Move on to Page 2, and provide stakeholder information (including their name, title, and department).
5.  Enter the contact information for the stakeholder (email and phone number).
6.  Identify the root cause of the issue (Page 3).
7.  Document the immediate action taken (Page 3).
8.  Describe the corrective action (Page 4).
9.  Identify the person responsible for the action and the target completion date (Page 4).
10. Describe the preventative action (Page 5).
11. Identify the person responsible for the prevention and the target completion date (Page 5).
12. Enter the prevention target date and the effectiveness check date (Page 5).
13. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### CAPA Information (Page 1)

*   **CAPA Number**: Enter the specific number assigned to this CAPA request.
*   **CAPA Title**: Enter a brief description of the issue.
*   **Issue Identification Date**: Enter the date the issue was first identified.

### Stakeholder Information (Page 2)

*   **Stakeholder Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this response.
*   **Stakeholder Title or Role**: Enter the title or role of the person.
*   **Department**: Enter the department of the stakeholder.
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the stakeholder.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the stakeholder.

### Root Cause Analysis (Page 3)

*   **Root Cause**: Describe the root cause of the issue.

### Immediate Action (Page 3)

*   **Immediate Action Taken**: Document the immediate action taken to address the issue.

### Corrective Action (Page 4)

*   **Corrective Action**: Describe the action taken to fix the issue.
*   **Responsible Person for Action**: Enter the person responsible for the corrective action.
*   **Target Completion Date**: Enter the date by which the action should be completed.

### Preventative Action (Page 5)

*   **Preventative Action**: Describe the action to prevent the issue from occurring in the future.
*   **Responsible Person for Prevention**: Enter the person responsible for the preventative action.
*   **Prevention Target Date**: Enter the date by which the preventative action should be completed.
*   **Effectiveness Check Date**: Enter the date to verify the effectiveness of the preventative action.
*   **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information regarding the issue.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
*   Be sure to enter the correct dates.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
