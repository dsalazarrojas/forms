# delivery-order-form-pedro - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information necessary for successful delivery of goods or services, including contact details, delivery instructions, shipping address, delivery date, and delivery time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details in the **Contact Details** field.
2. Provide any additional delivery instructions in the **Delivery Instructions** field.
3. Enter your shipping address in the **Shipping Address** field.
4. Select the **Delivery Date** for the delivery.
5. Choose the **Delivery Time** for the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information, such as name, email, and phone number.
* **Delivery Instructions** (`Delivery Instructions`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide any additional information about the delivery, such as special instructions or notes.
* **Shipping Address** (`Shipping Address`, `text`, required): This field is essential for delivering goods or services to the correct location.
* **Delivery Date** (`Delivery Date`, `date`, required): Choose the date you would like the delivery to take place.
* **Delivery Time** (`Delivery Time`, `time`, required): Select the time of day you would like the delivery to take place.

## Tips
* Double-check that the required fields, **Shipping Address**, **Delivery Date**, and **Delivery Time**, are completed correctly before submitting the form.
* Be as specific as possible when providing your shipping address and delivery date/time to ensure accurate and timely delivery.
