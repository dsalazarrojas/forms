# Corporation Fees Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an authorization for corporation fees related to vendor purchases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide your name and title in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date (optional) in the "Date" field if the request is related to a specific date.
6. Enter the amount of the corporation fees in the "Amount" field.
7. Enter the name of the vendor in the "Vendor Name" field.
8. Enter the purchase order number in the "Purchase Order" field.
9. Provide any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's official name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Provide your name and title for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date if the authorization request is related to a specific date.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the corporation fees.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the vendor related to the purchase.
* **Purchase Order** (`purchase_order`, text, optional): Enter the purchase order number related to the vendor purchase.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about the request.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to send the request to the authorized personnel.
