# Launch Ambassador Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Launch Ambassador Request Form is a simple form designed to collect information from potential ambassadors or influencers for a product or service launch. This form is meant to gather basic contact information, launch details, and role selection.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your contact information (name, email, phone number).
2.  Select the role you wish to take on as an ambassador or influencer.
3.  Provide details about the product or service launch (launch date, launch time, location).
4.  Write a message explaining why you're interested in being an ambassador or influencer.
5.  Select the terms and conditions and privacy policy you agree to.
6.  Enter your company information (name and job title).
7.  Select a contact person and enter their email.
8.  Submit the form to send your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Launch Ambassador Request Form** (`launch_ambassador_request_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required/optional): Enter your contact number.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Launch Date** (`launch_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the launch.
*   **Launch Time** (`launch_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the launch.
*   **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the launch.
*   **Message** (`message`, text, required/optional): Write a message explaining why you're interested in being an ambassador or influencer.
*   **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Select your desired role as an ambassador or influencer (ambassador, influencer, etc.).
*   **Status** (`status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the status of your request (pending, approved, denied, etc.).
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click the submit button to send your request.
*   **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required/optional): Check to agree to the terms and conditions.
*   **Privacy and Policy** (`privacy_and_policy`, note, required/optional): Check to agree to the privacy and policy.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the contact email of the contact person.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the contact person.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the job title of the contact person.
