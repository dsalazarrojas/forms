# Amplify Buildings Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Amplify Buildings Quote Request Form is designed to collect essential project information and requirements for a construction project. This form helps us understand your project needs and enables us to provide an accurate quote for your construction project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of project you are undertaking (e.g., new construction, renovation, etc.).
2. Provide your project name and description.
3. Enter your contact information (first name, last name, email, and phone number).
4. Enter your project location (address, city, state, and ZIP code).
5. Describe the building details (type, approximate square footage, etc.).
6. Specify the scope of work required (e.g., general contracting, design-build, etc.).
7. Provide any specific requirements or needs for your project.
8. Choose a timeline for your project (e.g., fixed deadline, flexible, etc.).
9. Select a budget range for your project.
10. Confirm if financing is secured.
11. Specify if you have plans and documents available.
12. Identify any additional services required (e.g., architecture, engineering, etc.).
13. Describe your sustainability goals (if any).
14. Select how you will decide on a contractor.
15. Confirm if you are getting other bids or if this is an exclusive deal.
16. Choose what factors will influence your decision on a contractor.
17. Add any additional comments or information.
18. Specify how you heard about us.
19. If referred, provide the name of the person who referred you.
20. Choose a preferred quote format (e.g., email, printed document, etc.).
21. Decide if you want a site visit.
22. Select the best time to contact you.
23. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Type** (`project_type`): Select the type of project you are undertaking (e.g., new construction, renovation, etc.).
* **Project Name** (`project_name`): Enter a name for your project (if applicable).
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Title/Position** (`title_position`): Enter your title or position.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address for quote delivery.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Project Address** (`project_address`): Enter the address where work will be performed.
* **City** (`project_city`): Enter the city of the project location.
* **State** (`project_state`): Enter the state of the project location.
* **ZIP Code** (`project_zip`): Enter the ZIP code of the project location.
* **Building Type** (`building_type`): Select the type of building you are working on (e.g., single-family home, office building, etc.).
* **Approximate Square Footage** (`square_footage`): Enter the approximate square footage of the building.
* **Number of Floors** (`number_of_floors`): Enter the number of floors in the building.
* **Building Age** (`building_age`): Select the age of the building (if it is an existing structure).
* **Scope of Work** (`services_needed`): Specify the scope of work required for your project (e.g., general contracting, design-build, etc.).
* **Services Needed** (`services_needed`): Select any additional services required for your project (e.g., architecture, engineering, etc.).
* **Detailed Project Description** (`project_description`): Describe your project in detail.
* **Sustainability Goals** (`sustainability_goals`): Describe any sustainability goals for your project (if applicable).
* **Decision Timeline** (`decision_timeline`): Select when you will decide on a contractor for your project.
* **Other Bids** (`other_bids`): Confirm if you are getting other bids or if this is an exclusive deal.
* **Selection Factors** (`selection_factors`): Select what factors will influence your decision on a contractor (e.g., price, timeline, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or information about your project.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about`): Specify how you heard about us.
* **Referral Name** (`referral_name`): Enter the name of the person who referred you (if applicable).
* **Preferred Quote Format** (`quote_format`): Select your preferred quote format (e.g., email, printed document, etc.).
* **Site Visit Needed** (`site_visit_needed`): Decide if you want a site visit.
* **Best Time to Contact** (`best_contact_time`): Select the best time to contact you.
* **Information Accuracy** (`information_accuracy`): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **May We Contact You** (`contact_permission`): Confirm that you are available for us to contact you.

Please ensure that you carefully review and complete each field accurately to enable us to effectively quote and execute your project. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us directly.
