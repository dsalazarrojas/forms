# Phone Repair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Phone Repair Form is a self-service form used for customers to submit a phone repair request. Please fill out this form to describe your issue and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Submit the form once finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name for our records.

### Customer Email
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get back to you.

### Customer Phone
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.

### Issue Description
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe the issue with your phone, including any error messages or error codes you've encountered.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
* If you are experiencing a critical issue with your phone, please contact us immediately at your nearest service center.
* We will get back to you as soon as possible to discuss further repairs and costs.
