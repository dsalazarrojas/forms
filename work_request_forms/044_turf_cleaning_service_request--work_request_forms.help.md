# Turf Cleaning Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Turf Cleaning Service Request form is for requesting a service for cleaning or maintenance of grass or turf areas. Users can submit a single request for a scheduled appointment, including their name, contact information, and specific service request details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information for the scheduled appointment, including the date and time you prefer for the service.
2. Enter your name, contact email, and phone number so we can get in touch with you to confirm the appointment.
3. Provide any additional service request or instructions in the "Service Request" field.
4. Submit the form to schedule your appointment.
