# Smart Maintenance Partner Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Maintenance Partner Contact Form is used to collect information about a maintenance partner, including their contact details and the details of the work request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the first section.
2. Enter your email address and phone number in the second section.
3. Select your preferred contact method in the third section.
4. Enter a brief message describing the work request in the fourth section.
5. Select the location of the work request in the fifth section.
6. Choose a date and time for the maintenance visit in the sixth section.
7. If you want to be assigned a specific tool, select "Yes" in the seventh section.
8. You can add any additional information or tags in the last section.

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Partner Name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Contact Method**: Select how you want to be contacted (Phone, Email, In person).
* **Message**: Enter a brief description of the work request.
* **Partner Company**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Location**: Select the location of the work request (Maintenance Shop, Home Office, Remote).
* **Date**: Choose the date for the maintenance visit.
* **Time**: Choose the time for the maintenance visit.
* **Assigned To**: If you want to be assigned a specific tool, select "Yes".

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Select only one contact method.
