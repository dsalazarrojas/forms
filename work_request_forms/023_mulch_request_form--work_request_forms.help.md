# Mulch Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mulch Request Form is designed to collect information about mulch requests, such as name, contact information, and mulch specifications. This form is meant to assist with the processing and management of mulch requests efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information ready. Then, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your name and contact information for communication.
3. Specify your mulch requests, including type and quantity.
4. Choose a submission option (Submit, Save, or Close) to finalize your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name or the name of the person making the request.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your address or contact location.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, optional): Enter your phone number or other contact method.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Leave a message or note for the request.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email** (`email`, text, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Mulch** (`mulch`, text, optional): Specify the type of mulch required.
* **Quantity** (`quantity`, number, optional): Choose the amount of mulch requested.
* **Mulch Type** (`mulch_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of mulch you need (Organic, Regular, or Wildflower).
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, optional): Choose how to submit your request (Submit, Save, or Close).
* **Time** (`time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of your request.
* **Mulch Type 2** (`mulch_type_2`, time, optional): Select a specific time for your mulch delivery (if applicable).

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's recommended to fill them out for efficient processing.
