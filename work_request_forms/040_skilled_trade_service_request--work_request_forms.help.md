# Skilled Trade Service Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to request services from our skilled trade team, including electricians, plumbers, and HVAC technicians. Please use this form to provide us with the necessary information about your service request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the service request details in the "Service Request" field.
2. Select the type of service required from the "Service Type" dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date and time for the service to be performed.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. Attach any relevant files or documents to support your request (optional).
6. Add any additional comments or information about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Service Request** (`service_request`): This field is for a brief description of the service you require.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`): Select the type of service you need from the options provided (Electrician, Plumber, or HVAC Tech).
* **Scheduling** (`scheduling`): Enter the date and time you would like the service to be performed.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Please provide a brief introduction, and any relevant details about your service request.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`): Upload any relevant files or documents to support your request.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Service Description** (`service_description`): Provide a more detailed description of the service you require.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Add any additional information or comments about your service request.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have provided all necessary information to ensure prompt service.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for assistance.
* Our skilled trade team will review your request and follow up with you shortly.
