# Fast Easy Offer - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fast Easy Offer form is designed to collect information for a quick and efficient offer or proposal to a real estate-related service or business. Please use this form to submit your offer or proposal and our team will follow up with you for further details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your answer to the question "Fast Easy Offer" from the dropdown menu.
2. Type in your real estate information and details.
3. Choose the type of real estate you are interested in.
4. Enter your contact details.
5. Select your answer to the question "Real Estate Type" from the dropdown menu.
6. Select your answer to the question "Property Type" from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter the address, city, state, and zip code of the real estate property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Fast Easy Offer** (1, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if you are submitting an offer or proposal, or "No" if you are not.
- **Real Estate Info** (2, text, required): Type in a brief description of the real estate property you are interested in.
- **Offer Info** (3, text, required): Type in any additional information about your offer or proposal.
- **Contact Details** (4, select_multiple, required): Choose "Yes" if you are available for contact, or "No" if you are not.
- **Real Estate Type** (5, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if the real estate is a specific type, or "No" if it is not.
- **Property Type** (6, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if the property is a specific type, or "No" if it is not.
- **Address** (7, text, required): Enter the address of the real estate property.
- **City** (8, text, required): Enter the city of the real estate property.
- **State** (9, text, required): Enter the state of the real estate property.
- **Zip Code** (10, text, required): Enter the zip code of the real estate property.
