# Digital Art Commission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to gather essential details about digital art commissions, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of requests. It is crucial to fill out the form accurately to avoid delays or miscommunication with the artist.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form. A field with an asterisk (*) next to it is required.
2. For commission type, select 'Yes' if you are commissioning the artist for a project, or 'No' if you are an artist looking to collaborate or partner with someone else.
3. Enter your artist name in the "Artist Name" field. This is the name that will appear on the commission agreement and any subsequent contracts.
4. For the "Price" field, enter the total price of the commission, including all costs and fees.
5. Select the correct "Image Count" and "Image Size" for your commission requirements.
6. Choose your preferred "Image Format" from the given options.
7. In the "Contact Information" section, provide your name, email, phone number, and address to ensure we can communicate effectively.
8. Select your preferred "Payment Method" for processing the commission.
9. In the "Commission Terms" field, provide any specific requirements or conditions for the commission.
10. Review the "Commission Notes" and "Artist Agreement" fields to ensure accuracy and clarity before submitting.
11. Once you have reviewed and completed the form, click the "Submit" button to finalize your commission request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name**: Enter your real name or artist name as it appears on your contract and any marketing materials.
* **Commission Type**: Select 'Yes' if you are commissioning the artist for a project, or 'No' if you are an artist looking to collaborate or partner with someone else.
* **Commission Date**: Enter the date you would like to start and complete the commission. This will help us set realistic expectations and timelines.
* **Description**: A brief and clear description of the project requirements, including any specific details or conditions.
* **Price**: The total price for the commission, including all costs and fees.
* **Payment Method**: Choose from available options for processing the commission payment.
* **Image Count**: Enter the number of images or pieces you require for the commission.
* **Image Size**: Select the desired image size according to the provided options.
* **Image Format**: Choose the format in which you want the images to be delivered.
* **Deadline**: Enter the desired completion date for the commission.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you would like to receive the commissioned work.
* **Contact Information**: Ensure accurate and complete contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for secure and direct communication.
* **Website**: Enter your website for additional contact or information if applicable.
* **Street**: Enter your street address for mail or delivery purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State**: Enter the state or province where you reside or work.
* **Postal Code**: Enter your zip code for mail or delivery purposes.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence or work.
* **Commission Terms**: Provide any specific requirements or conditions for the commission.
* **Commission Notes**: Add any additional comments or requirements for the commission.
* **Artist Statement**: Enter a brief statement about your artistic style or approach.
* **Artist Agreement**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the commission.
* **Submit**: Review and finalize your commission request before submitting the form.
