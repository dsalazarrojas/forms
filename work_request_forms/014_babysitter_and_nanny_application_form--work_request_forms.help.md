# Babysitter And Nanny Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information about you for consideration as a babysitter or nanny by GM Elit Group. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to increase your chances of being selected.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read all instructions and fields carefully before starting.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and documentation ready.
3. Fill in all required fields accurately.
4. Upload any required attachments, such as a photo or CV.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Legal Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, **Required**)
Your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.

### 2. **-- Contact and Personal Details --** (`contact_info_header`, note, **Not Required**)
This section contains information about your contact details and personal history. While not required, it's recommended for us to get to know you better.

### 3. **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **Required**)
Your active email address where you can be reached for further communication.

### 4. **Mobile Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **Required**)
Your mobile phone number for quick contact and communication.

### 5. **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, **Required**)
Your street, city, and zip code for official purposes, if required by law or regulation.

### 6. **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **Required**)
Your date of birth in dd/mm/yyyy format.

### 7. **Photo and Video Requirements** (`photo_submission_note`, note, **Not Required**)
This section explains the photo and video requirements for your application. Ensure you understand what's expected before proceeding.

### 8. **I have a professional photo or CV with photo ready for submission** (`photo_confirmed`, select_one, **Required**)
Please indicate if you have a professional photo or CV with a photo attached.

### 9. **-- Professional Experience --** (`experience_header`, note, **Not Required**)
This section is for you to describe your professional experience in childcare.

### 10. **Total Years of Professional Childcare Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, **Required**)
The total years of experience you have in childcare.

### 11. **Types of Roles Previously Held** (`previous_role_types`, select_multiple, **Required**)
Please select all the roles you have held, such as full-time nanny, part-time sitter, live-in nanny, newborn care specialist, or after-school sitter.

### 12. **Languages Spoken Fluently** (`languages_spoken`, text, **Required**)
List all languages you speak fluently.

### 13. **Childcare Related Certifications** (`certifications_held`, text, **Not Required**)
This is where you list any relevant certifications you have, such as CPR, First Aid, or Early Childhood Education degrees.

### 14. **Professional Reference Name** (`reference_name_1`, text, **Required**)
The name of your professional reference, such as a previous employer.

### 15. **Reference Phone or Email** (`reference_contact_1`, text, **Required**)
The contact information for your professional reference.

### 16. **I consent to a full background and criminal record check** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, **Required**)
Please indicate your consent to a full background and criminal record check.

### 17. **Why do you want to work with GM Elit Group?** (`motivation_text`, text, **Required**)
Write a brief explanation of why you want to work with GM Elit Group and what drives your passion for childcare.
