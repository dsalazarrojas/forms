# Manpower Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you request additional manpower for various roles within the organization. It's used to gather information necessary to fulfill your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required details about your request, such as the job title and department where the additional manpower is needed.
2. Provide information about the position you are requesting, including whether it's for a yes or no answer.
3. Specify the number of positions you need and the duration of the position.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the position.
5. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Details**: This is the main section of the form where you can enter the details about your request.
* **Requested Position**: Select 'Yes' if you are requesting a new position, 'No' if you are not.
* **Job Title**: Enter the title of the job you are requesting.
* **Department**: Enter the department where the additional manpower is needed.
* **Job Category**: Enter a brief description of the job category.
* **Number of Positions**: Enter the number of positions you need.
* **Duration of Position**: Enter the duration of the position in days, weeks, or months.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the position.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the position.
* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person for this request.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information about your request, enter it here.
