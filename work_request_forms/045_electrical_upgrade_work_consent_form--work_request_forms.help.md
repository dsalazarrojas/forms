# Electrical Upgrade Work Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electrical Upgrade Work Consent Form is used to collect necessary information for electrical upgrades, including consent, approval, and other relevant details. This form is typically used to ensure that the necessary parties are aware of and agree to the upgrade process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Upgrade Work" field, which indicates whether the electrical upgrade is needed.
2. Enter the scope of the upgrade in the "Scope" field.
3. Select the approval method for the upgrade: "Client", "Electrician", or "Both".
4. (Optional) Sign the form with your name and select the signature type (electronic or print).
5. Enter your name and email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter the work order details.
8. (Optional) Enter comments about the upgrade.
9. Select the upgrade type (General, Electrical, or Both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Upgrade Work** (`upgrade_work_consent_form`, select_one, required): This field asks if the electrical upgrade is needed.
* **Scope** (`electrical_upgrade_scope`, text, required): Enter the scope of the upgrade, which describes the work to be done.
* **Upgrade Approval** (`electrical_upgrade_approval`, select_multiple, required): Select the approval method for the upgrade. Options are "Client", "Electrician", or "Both".
* **Signature** (`electrical_upgrade_signature`, note, required for certain upgrades): This field is for signing the form with your name. If it's not required, you can skip it.
* **Signature Type** (`electrical_upgrade_signature_type`, select_one, required for signature field): Select the type of signature, which is either "Electronic" or "Print".
* **Signature Location** (`electrical_upgrade_signature_location`, text, required for signature field): Enter the location where the signature was made.
* **Name** (`electrical_upgrade_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`electrical_upgrade_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`electrical_upgrade_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Work Order** (`electrical_upgrade_work_order`, text, required): Enter the work order details.
* **Comments** (`electrical_upgrade_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the upgrade.
* **Upgrade Type** (`electrical_upgrade_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of upgrade (General, Electrical, or Both).
* **Upgrade Notes** (`electrical_upgrade_notes`, text, optional): Enter notes about the upgrade.
* **Signature 2** (`electrical_upgrade_signature_2`, note, optional): This field is for signing the form with your name for certain upgrades.
* **Email 2** (`electrical_upgrade_email_2`, email, optional): Enter your email address for certain upgrades.
* **Phone 2** (`electrical_upgrade_phone_2`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for certain upgrades.

Note: Some fields are not required for all upgrades, so be sure to check the form's requirements before filling it out.
