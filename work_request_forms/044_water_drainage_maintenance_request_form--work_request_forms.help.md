# Water Drainage Maintenance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Drainage Maintenance Request Form is designed to collect information about water drainage issues that need maintenance in your facility. This form will guide you through a series of questions to provide us with essential details about the problem, so we can quickly resolve it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1.  Choose the location where the issue was encountered.
2.  Describe the issue in as much detail as possible.
3.  (Optional) Attach photos of the problem for further reference.
4.  Select the priority level for the maintenance request.
5.  Choose a technician to assign to the request.
6.  Update the status of the request.
7.  Provide your date of submission.
8.  Enter the contact information of the resident or property manager.
9.  Add any additional notes about the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Maintenance Request**: Enter a brief description of the problem you have encountered.
* **Location**: Select the location where the issue was found.
* **Issue Description**: Provide a more detailed description of the problem.
* **Photos**: (Optional) Attach photos of the problem for further reference.
* **Priority**: Select the priority level for the request:
	+ **Low**: For minor issues that can be resolved at the next available opportunity.
	+ **Medium**: For issues that are causing some disturbance or discomfort.
	+ **High**: For critical issues that require immediate attention.
* **Assigned Technician**: Choose a technician to be assigned to the request.
* **Status**: Update the status of the request:
	+ **Option 1**: New request.
	+ **Option 2**: In progress.
	+ **Option 3**: Completed.
* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date you are submitting the request.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the contact information of the resident or property manager (email and phone number).
* **Notes**: Additional notes about the request.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the resident or property manager.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the resident or property manager.

**Tips**: Please be as detailed as possible in your description, and provide any relevant photos to help us better understand the issue. This will help us resolve the problem more efficiently.
