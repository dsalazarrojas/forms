# Repair Request Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Repair Request Survey" form is designed to collect customer contact information, feedback, and repair details to efficiently process and address customer repair requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Customer Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, Required/Optional): This field is used to gather the customer's contact details. Please enter the customer's name, address, phone number, and email.
*   **Customer Feedback** (`customer_feedback`, `text`, Required/Optional): Share your thoughts and experience with us in this field. This helps us improve our services and ensure a better customer experience.
*   **Repair Details** (`repair_details`, `text`, Required/Optional): Describe the issue with your item in as much detail as possible. This will help us understand the problem and provide the best possible solution.
