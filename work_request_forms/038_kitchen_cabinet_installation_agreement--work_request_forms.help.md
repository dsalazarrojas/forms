# kitchen_cabinet_installation_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information related to kitchen cabinet installation agreements between customers and contractors. It captures details about the customer, job requirements, customer authorization, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Customer Details section with your contact information and other relevant details.
2. Provide information about the job requirements in the Job Details section.
3. Verify your authorization and signature in the Customer Authorization and Customer Signature sections, respectively.
4. Enter your signature date and time in the Signature Date and Signature Time sections.
5. Enter your contractor's details, including name and phone number, in the Contractor Details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Details** (`customer_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, and other relevant contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Job Details** (`job_details`, text, required: false): Describe the job requirements, including the type of cabinets to be installed, materials needed, and any specific installation instructions.
* **Customer Authorization** (`customer_authorization`, text, required: false): Confirm that you have read and understood the agreement terms and that you are authorized to sign on behalf of your company or organization.
* **Installation Details** (`installation_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details related to the installation, such as the location and timeline.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, required: false): Sign your name to acknowledge that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`customer_signature_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **Signature Time** (`customer_signature_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you signed the agreement.
* **Customer Signature Date Optional** (`customer_signature_date_optional`, date, required: false): This field is an optional duplicate of the signature date for your records.
* **Customer Signature Time Optional** (`customer_signature_time_optional`, time, required: false): This field is an optional duplicate of the signature time for your records.
* **Contractor Details** (`contractor_details`, text, required: false): Enter your contractor's name and other relevant details.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, required: false): Enter your contractor's name.
* **Contractor Phone** (`contractor_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contractor's phone number.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before signing the agreement to avoid any issues or misunderstandings.
* Ensure you have read and understood the terms of the agreement before signing.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please seek clarification from the relevant personnel or the original form creator.
