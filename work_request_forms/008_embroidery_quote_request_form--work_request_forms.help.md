# Embroidery Quote Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses to request a quote for embroidery services. Fill out this form to provide us with necessary information about your business and your quote request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields to ensure your quote request is processed efficiently.
2. Enter your business details (business name, address, city, state, zip, country) to help us understand your business context.
3. Provide your contact information (name, email, phone) so we can reach out to you for further discussion.
4. Describe your quote request in the "Quote Description" field.
5. Optionally, provide additional notes about your business and quote request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Work Request** (text, optional): A brief description of the embroidery work you need help with. This can include the type of design, size, and material you need for your embroidery.
* **Business Name** (text, optional): The official name of your company or business.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): The name of the person responsible for this quote request.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): The email address of the person responsible for this quote request.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): The phone number of the person responsible for this quote request.
* **Quote Description** (text, optional): A detailed description of the quote request, including any specific requirements or deadlines.
* **Business Address** (text, optional): The physical address of your business.
* **Business City** (text, optional): The city where your business is located.
* **Business State** (text, optional): The state where your business is located.
* **Business Zip** (text, optional): The zip code of your business.
* **Business Country** (text, optional): The country where your business is located.
* **Business Contact** (text, optional): Any additional information about the person responsible for your business.
* **Business Fax** (text, optional): The fax number of your business.
* **Business Website** (text, optional): The website of your business.
* **Business Notes** (text, optional): Additional notes about your business or quote request.
* **Quote Request Date** (date, optional): The date you would like to receive a quote or completion of the project.

Please fill out the form carefully and completely, and we will get back to you as soon as possible to discuss your quote request.
