# PPV Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PPV Liability Waiver form is used to track client payments and agreements for services rendered.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete each field carefully and accurately to ensure that your information is correct.
2.  If you are unsure about any field, please ask your service provider for clarification.
3.  Make sure to review and agree to the terms of service before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Form Number** (`form_number`, number, required): This is a unique identifier for each service visit. Please ensure that this number is accurate and matches the corresponding visit record.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Please enter the client's name as it appears on file.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): This is the client's contact information for follow-up purposes.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address for communication purposes.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please note that this field is not typically used for payment purposes, but is often required for other services.
*   **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): This field is usually not used for payment purposes, but may be used to track appointment times.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): This field is for the client's electronic signature, which may be required for certain services.
*   **PPV Fee** (`ppv_fee`, number, required): Enter the total fee paid for the service visit.
*   **PPV Fee Unit** (`ppv_fee_unit`, text, required): This field should be left as it is, as it is set by default to the correct unit of currency (e.g. dollars or euros).
*   **Services** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): Select all services provided during the visit from the list provided.
*   **Additional Services** (`additional_services`, select_one, optional): Select any additional services provided during the visit.
*   **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of payment.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the payment method used for this service visit.
*   **PPV Notes** (`ppv_notes`, note, required): Enter any relevant notes about the service visit.
*   **PPV Agreement** (`ppv_agreement`, note, required): Enter your electronic agreement to the terms of service.
