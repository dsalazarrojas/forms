# healthcare_provider_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The healthcare provider referral form is a tool for healthcare professionals to collect and share essential information related to patient referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient details section with the relevant information about the patient.
2. Provide contact information for the patient in the contact info section.
3. Specify the referring provider in the referring provider section.
4. Add information about the healthcare provider in the healthcare provider info section.
5. Enter the referral details in the referral details section.
6. Complete the facility information section with the relevant details about the facility.
7. Add the facility contact information in the facility contact section.
8. Obtain patient consent for the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Details**: This section collects basic information about the patient, including any relevant medical history or conditions.
* **Patient Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact details, such as name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Referring Provider**: Name and contact information of the referring provider who is referring the patient.
* **Healthcare Provider Information**: Details about the healthcare provider who will be treating the patient.
* **Referral Details**: A description of the reason for the referral, including any specific instructions or requirements.
* **Facility Information**: Information about the facility where the patient will be receiving care.
* **Facility Contact Information**: Contact details for the facility where the patient will be receiving care.
* **Signature of Referring Provider**: A digital signature from the referring provider to confirm the referral.
* **Patient Consent**: A section to obtain consent from the patient for the referral process.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to collect and share patient information.
* Use this form in accordance with relevant healthcare regulations and guidelines.
* Double-check patient information to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Patient consent is required before processing the referral.
* Keep patient information confidential and secure.
