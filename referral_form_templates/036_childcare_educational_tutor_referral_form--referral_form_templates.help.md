# Childcare Educational Tutor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childcare Educational Tutor Referral Form is a tool used to refer a child for educational tutoring services. This form collects information about the child's needs, academic strengths and challenges, and preferences for tutoring, as well as the parent or guardian's contact information and consent for tutoring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Please ensure that you answer all fields accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, please review the form carefully to ensure that it is complete and accurate.
5. Once satisfied that the form is complete and accurate, please submit it for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, `date`, required): The date on which the referral is made.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`, `text`, required): The legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`, `text`, required): The legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, `date`, required): The date of the child's birth.
* **Child Age** (`child_age`, `number`, required): The current age of the child in years.
* **Child Grade Level** (`child_grade_level`, `select_one`, required): The current grade level of the child.
	+ Select from: Pre-K, Kindergarten, 1st Grade, 2nd Grade, 3rd Grade, 4th Grade, 5th Grade, 6th Grade, 7th Grade, 8th Grade
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_guardian_first_name`, `text`, required): The legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_guardian_last_name`, `text`, required): The legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): The primary email address for contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): The primary phone number for contact.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, required): The person making the referral.
	+ Select from: Teacher, Parent or Guardian, School Counselor, Childcare Provider, Healthcare Provider, Other
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, `select_one`, required): The primary reason for the referral.
	+ Select from: Reading difficulties, Math difficulties, Writing difficulties, General academic support, Homework help, Test preparation, Learning disability support, English language learning, Gifted enrichment, Other
* **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, `text`, required): Provide more details about the referral reason.
* **Child Academic Strengths** (`academic_strengths`, `text`, required): What are the child's academic strengths?
* **Child Academic Challenges** (`academic_challenges`, `text`, required): What specific challenges is the child facing?
* **Learning Style** (`learning_style`, `select_one`, required): How does the child learn best?
	+ Select from: Visual learner, Auditory learner, Kinesthetic learner, Reading and writing, Mixed styles, Unsure
* **Previous Tutoring Experience** (`previous_tutoring_experience`, `select_one`, required): Has the child had tutoring before?
	+ Select from: Yes, currently receiving, Yes, previously received, No, never had tutoring
* **Previous Tutoring Outcome** (`previous_tutoring_outcome`, `select_one`, optional): How effective was previous tutoring?
	+ Select from: Very effective, Somewhat effective, Not effective, Not applicable
* **Preferred Tutoring Schedule** (`preferred_tutoring_schedule`, `select_one`, required): When would tutoring sessions be preferred?
	+ Select from: Weekday mornings, Weekday afternoons, Weekday evenings, Weekend mornings, Weekend afternoons, Flexible schedule
* **Sessions Per Week** (`sessions_per_week`, `select_one`, required): How many sessions per week are needed?
	+ Select from: One session per week, Two sessions per week, Three sessions per week, Four or more sessions, As needed
* **Tutoring Location Preference** (`tutoring_location_preference`, `select_one`, required): Where should tutoring take place?
	+ Select from: At childcare center, At home, Online or virtual, At library or community center, No preference
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs_or_accommodations`, `text`, optional): Does the child require any special accommodations?
* **IEP or 504 Plan** (`iep_or_504_plan`, `select_one`, required): Does the child have an IEP or 504 plan?
	+ Select from: Yes, has IEP, Yes, has 504 plan, False, Being evaluated
* **Tutor Preferences** (`tutor_preferences`, `text`, optional): Any preferences for tutor characteristics?
* **Goals for Tutoring** (`goals_for_tutoring`, `text`, required): What are your goals for tutoring?
* **Consent for Tutoring** (`consent_for_tutoring`, `select_one`, required): Do you consent to your child receiving tutoring services?
	+ Select from: Yes, I consent, No, I do not consent, Need more information
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): The date of electronic signature.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
* The referral form is intended to be completed by the parent or guardian, as they are best placed to provide information about the child's needs and preferences.
* If you need assistance or clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
