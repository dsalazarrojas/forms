# Solar Referral Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients to refer a doctor for their medical condition. It is designed to collect essential information about the patient, their contact details, and doctor's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `Solar Referral Form` page with the patient's relevant information, such as their name and contact details.
2. On the next page, enter the patient's medical condition and details about their last visit, including the date of their last visit.
3. If the patient has a scheduled next visit, enter the date and any relevant details.
4. Select the doctor's specialty, department, and confirm if they have made a suggestion for the patient.
5. Enter the doctor's contact information, including their phone number, email, and fax number.
6. Finally, review the form for any missing information and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Solar Referral Form** (`id: 1`): A text field where you can enter a brief description of the form's purpose.
* **Solar Referral** (`id: 2`): Enter the patient's medical referral information.
* **Patient Name** (`id: 3`): Enter the patient's name.
* **Contact Person** (`id: 4`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`id: 5`): Enter the contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`id: 6`): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 7`): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`id: 8`): Enter the date of the patient's last visit.
* **Next Scheduled Visit** (`id: 9`): Enter the date of the patient's next scheduled visit.
* **Medical Condition** (`id: 10`): Enter the patient's medical condition.
* **Doctor Suggestion** (`id: 11`): Confirm if the patient has a doctor's suggestion.
* **Medical Specialty** (`id: 12`): Select the medical specialty.
* **Department** (`id: 13`): Select the department.
* **Doctor Phone** (`id: 14`): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Email** (`id: 15`): Enter the doctor's email address.
* **Doctor Fax** (`id: 16`): Enter the doctor's fax number.
* **Doctor Address** (`id: 17`): Enter the doctor's address.
* **Doctor Phone Number** (`id: 18`): Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Medical Record** (`id: 19`): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Insurance Number** (`id: 20`): Enter the patient's insurance number.
* **Date of Last Update** (`id: 21`): Enter the date of the last update.
* **Doctor Name** (`id: 22`): Enter the doctor's name.
* **Patient ID** (`id: 23`): Enter the patient's ID number.
* **Department Name** (`id: 24`): Enter the department name.
* **Department ID** (`id: 25`): Enter the department ID number.
