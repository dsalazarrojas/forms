# R&D Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The R&D Consultant Referral Form is a form used to refer potential R&D (Research and Development) consultants to our organization. This form is used to gather information about companies, their contact details, and the consultant's contact information for potential collaborations and partnerships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Enter the company address in the "Company Address" field.
3. Enter the consultant's name in the "Consultant Name" field.
4. Enter the consultant's email in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the consultant's phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company you are referring to.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company you are referring to.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of the R&D consultant you are referring.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the R&D consultant you are referring.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the R&D consultant you are referring.
