# Consulting Client Referral Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from referrers about a referred client, providing a structured way to gather essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill out the referrer's information.
2. Enter the referred client's information.
3. Provide a brief summary of the client's needs.
4. If applicable, select the urgency of the referral.
5. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Referrer Information
* **Referrer name** (Required, text): Enter the name of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer contact email** (Optional, email): For follow-up if needed.

### Client Information
* **Referred client name** (Required, text): Enter the name of the organization or person referred.
* **Client needs summary** (Required, text): Briefly describe the reasons for the referral.

### Urgency
* **Urgency** (Optional, select_one):
	+ Immediate
	+ Within 1 week
	+ Within 1 month
	+ Not urgent

### Tips
* Please fill out this form only if you are new to the client or have not already submitted a referral.
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals before submitting the referral.
* If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact the relevant team or manager for assistance.
