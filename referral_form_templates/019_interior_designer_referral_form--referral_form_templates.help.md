# interior_designer_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The interior designer referral form is a tool used by interior designers to refer clients to a specific design service. It helps gather essential information about the client's project details, preferences, and budget constraints. This information will aid the interior designer in determining the best course of action for their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project title and details in the `Project Details` section.
2. Select the client's preferences in the `Client Preferences` section by choosing one or more options from the dropdown menu.
3. Provide your details in the `Designer Details` section.
4. Enter the project budget in the `Project Budget` section by selecting one of the available options.
5. Choose the design style for the project in the `Design Style` section.
6. Select the project location in the `Project Location` section.
7. Choose the design services required for the project in the `Design Services` section by selecting one or more options from the dropdown menu.
8. Finally, enter the project deadline date in the `Project Deadline` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details** (`Project Details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the project, including any relevant details that will help us understand the project requirements.
* **Client Preferences** (`Client Preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the client's preferences, such as 'Yes' or 'No', to indicate their requirements.
* **Designer Details** (`Designer Details`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information so that we can get in touch with you for further discussions.
* **Project Budget** (`Project Budget`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available budget options to indicate the project budget constraints.
* **Design Style** (`Design Style`, select_one, optional): Choose the design style for the project, such as Modern or Traditional.
* **Project Location** (`Project Location`, select_one, optional): Select the location where the project will take place.
* **Design Services** (`Design Services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more design services required for the project, such as Space Planning or Furniture Layout.
* **Project Deadline** (`Project Deadline`, date, optional): Enter the project deadline date to help us plan and schedule the project.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please feel free to contact us for clarification.
* Please note that the project budget and deadline fields are not mandatory, but it's essential to provide accurate information to help us better understand the project requirements.
