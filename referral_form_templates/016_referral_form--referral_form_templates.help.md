# Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic data about a person being referred to our organization or service, as well as the referrer and their contact information. It is intended to help us understand who is referring to our organization or service and why.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Info section with your personal details.
2. Provide information about the person being referred (Referee Info).
3. Enter the details of the referral (Referral Info).
4. Enter your contact information (Contact Info).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`Personal Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your personal details such as name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Referee Info** (`Referee Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name, address, phone number, and email of the person being referred.
* **Referral Info** (`Referral Info`, `text`, required/optional): Provide details about the referral, such as the reason for the referral and any other relevant information.
* **Date of Referral** (`Date of Referral`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Reason** (`Referral Reason`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Referee Details** (`Referee Details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the referee.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Referee Phone** (`Referee Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person being referred.
* **Referee Email** (`Referee Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the person being referred.
* **Referral Phone** (`Referral Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the referral.
* **Referral Email** (`Referral Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Phone** (`Referrer Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Email** (`Referrer Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Phone** (`Referrer Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Email** (`Referrer Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the person making the referral.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as accurate as possible when entering the information.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact us for clarification.
* To avoid duplication of referrals, do not refer the same person to our organization or service more than once.
