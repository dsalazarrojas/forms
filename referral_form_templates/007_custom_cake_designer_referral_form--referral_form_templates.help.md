# Custom Cake Designer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information from referrers about potential customers they are referring to the custom cake designer. The referrer's contact information is collected for the custom cake designer's reference, and the form is intended to be used for referral purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter a title that accurately reflects the referral.
2.  Select one or multiple categories for the referral (Bakeries or Event Professionals).
3.  Provide a brief description of the referral.
4.  Enter the name and other relevant contact information for the referral.
5.  Answer the question about whether you have the referral's permission to share their information.
6.  Select the referral's preferred method of contact (Yes or No).
7.  Enter the referral's email address.
8.  Provide the referral's portfolio or relevant link to their portfolio.
9.  Enter any additional information or notes about the referral.
10.  Select the source of the referral (e.g., Google, Facebook, Instagram).
11.  Confirm that you are assigning a relevant tool to the referral (optional, but recommended).
12.  Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a title that briefly describes the referral. This will help the custom cake designer understand the context of the referral.
*  **Category** (`form_category`, select_multiple, required): Select one or multiple categories for the referral (Bakeries or Event Professionals).
*  **Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the referral.
*  **Referral Name** (`referral_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the referral.
*  **Permission to Share** (`phone`, select_one, required): Answer whether you have the referral's permission to share their information with the custom cake designer.
*  **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter the referral's email address.
*  **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required): Provide the referral's portfolio or a relevant link to their portfolio.
*  **Referral Message** (`referral_message`, text, required): Enter any additional information about the referral.
*  **Review** (`review`, text, required): Enter a review or feedback about the referral.
*  **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the review.
*  **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the review.
*  **Goals** (`goals`, text, required): Enter any goals or objectives for the referral.
*  **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or information about the referral.
*  **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Select the source of the referral (e.g., Google, Facebook, Instagram).
*  **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Confirm that you are assigning a relevant tool to the referral (if applicable).
