# Medical Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to refer patients to medical specialists for specialized care. It captures essential information about the patient, the reason for referral, and the specialist's comments and instructions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's information, including name, phone number, and any relevant medical conditions they may have.
2. Select the specialist to be referred to (Cardiologist, Dermatologist, Endocrinologist, Gastroenterologist, Nephrologist, Pulmonologist, Rheumatologist, or Other).
3. Choose any applicable medical conditions the patient may have.
4. Enter the reason for referral.
5. Enter the referral date and time.
6. Add any referring doctor information, if applicable.
7. Enter the patient's phone number, specialist's phone number, and any medical history or medication list.
8. Include any test results or specialist comments, if necessary.
9. Sign and date the form, as well as the referring doctor's signature and date.
10. Enter the submitted date and time, as well as the submitted by and submitted by doctor fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name and any other relevant information.
* **Specialist Name**: Select the specialist to be referred to. This will help the specialist's office determine the best course of action for the patient's care.
* **Reason for Referral**: Describe why the patient needs to be referred to the specialist.
* **Patient Conditions**: Select any applicable medical conditions the patient may have, such as allergies, asthma, or hypertension.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date when the referral was made.
* **Referring Doctor Info**: Add any information about the referring doctor, such as their name and phone number.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number for easy contact.
* **Specialist Phone**: Enter the specialist's phone number for easy contact.
* **Medical History**: Add any relevant medical history the patient may have.
* **Medication List**: List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Test Results**: Include any relevant test results or lab work.
* **Specialist Comments**: Add any comments or instructions from the specialist.
* **Follow Up Instructions**: Include any follow-up instructions from the specialist.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm the referral.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign and date the form, as well as the patient's signature.
* **Referring Doctor Signature**: Sign and date the form, as well as the referring doctor's signature.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the form was completed.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the form was completed.
* **Specialist Signature Date**: Enter the date when the specialist signed the form.
* **Specialist Signature Time**: Enter the time when the specialist signed the form.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted By Doctor**: Enter the name of the doctor who submitted the form.
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the specialist to whom the patient is being referred.

Note: This form is designed to be filled out by the referring doctor or healthcare professional and is intended to facilitate communication between the referring doctor and the specialist.
