# Printing Graphic Designer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect referrals for printing graphic designers. It helps us gather essential information about the design team's recommendations, team name, and other relevant details. Please fill out this form to refer a printing graphic designer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Referral Form** field with a brief description of the designer's work and why you are referring them.
2.  Choose whether you **Yes** or **No** to **Designer Recommendations**.
3.  Enter the **Team Name** where the designer is working on the project.
4.  Provide the **Designer Name**.
5.  Enter the **Contact Email** for the designer.
6.  Fill in the **Contact Phone** number for the designer.
7.  Choose whether the **Team Lead** is available to be contacted.
8.  Select the current **Project Status**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Form** (`printing_graphic_designer_referral_form`, text, required): Briefly describe the designer's work and why you are referring them.
* **Designer Recommendations** (`designer_recommendations`, select_multiple, required): Select whether the designer is recommended for further work or not.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the team the designer is working with.
* **Designer Name** (`designer_name`, text, required): Enter the designer's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email of the designer.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the designer’s contact phone number.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, select_one, required): Choose whether the team lead is available to be contacted.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Select the current project status.

## Tips
* Double-check that the form is complete and accurate before submitting.
* Make sure to provide a brief and clear description of the designer’s work in the **Referral Form** field.
* Ensure that the **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** are correct and up-to-date.
* Be clear and specific when selecting the **Project Status**.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact [insert contact information].
