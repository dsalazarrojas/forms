# Hospice Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information for hospice referrals. It gathers patient and contact information, medical details, billing information, comments, and notes. Please review the form carefully before completion to ensure you provide all necessary details for an accurate referral.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information and details needed to fill out each field accurately.
* Fill out the form in its entirety, following the instructions for each field.
* Verify that all required fields are filled out before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant identifying information.
* **Care Information** (`care_info`, text, optional): Describe the patient's care needs and any specific requirements or circumstances.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name, title, and contact details (phone number and email or address) for the referring healthcare professional or organization.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the patient's medical condition, diagnosis, and any relevant medical history.
* **Billing Information** (`billing_info`, text, required): Enter the billing information for the referred patient, including patient ID or account number, insurance provider, and any other relevant details.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or details regarding the referral.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or reminders for the referring healthcare professional or organization.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`, text, required): Include the signatures of both the referring healthcare professional or organization and the patient or their authorized representative.
