# Tech Co Working Space Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tech Co Working Space Referral Form is designed to collect information about a co-working space from a referral. This form helps to track and manage referrals for potential co-working space partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter the name of the person you are referring in the "Referral Name" field.
* Enter the phone number of the person you are referring in the "Referral Phone" field (optional).
* Enter the email of the person you are referring in the "Referral Email" field (optional).
* Enter the name of the co-working space in the "Co-Working Space Name" field.
* Select the type of co-working space from the dropdown menu "Co-Working Space Type" (Private, Public, or Shared).
* Enter the address of the co-working space in the "Co-Working Space Address" field (optional).
* If required, select the assigned tool for the co-working space from the dropdown menu "Assigned Tool" (Jotform or Chatbot).
* If required, select the follow-up method and time for the co-working space from the dropdown menus "Follow Up Method" and "Follow Up Time" (optional).
* If required, select the follow-up status for the co-working space from the "Follow Up Status" dropdown menu (New, In Progress, or Done).
* Enter the URL of the co-working space in the "Co-Working Space URL" field (optional).
* Enter the logo of the co-working space in the "Co-Working Space Logo" field (optional).
* Enter the phone number of the co-working space in the "Co-Working Space Phone" field (optional).
* Enter the email of the co-working space in the "Co-Working Space Email" field (optional).
* Enter a brief description of the co-working space in the "Co-Working Space Description" field (optional).
* Click "Submit" to submit the form.
