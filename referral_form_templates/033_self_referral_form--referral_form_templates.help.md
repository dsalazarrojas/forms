# Self Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Referral Form is designed to collect relevant and accurate information for self-referrals in a medical context. It is used to gather patient details, referral specifics, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field (format: MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Provide your address in the "address" field.
5. In the "Referral Information" field, select all the applicable options that describe the type of referral.
6. In the "Support and Services" field, select one option that describes the level of support you require.
7. In the "Additional Information" field, provide any other relevant details that will help us assist you.
8. In the "Contact Information" field, select all the contact methods you would like to be contacted through.
9. In the "Referral Contact" field, enter the name and title of the person or organization referring you.
10. In the "Referral Contact Info" field, enter the contact information of the referring person or organization.
11. In the "Referral Date" field, enter the date of the referral.
11. In the "Referral Time" field, enter the time of the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **address** (`patient_address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Referral Information** (`Referral Information`, select_multiple, required): Select all the applicable options that describe the type of referral you are seeking.
*   + **Primary Care Physician**: Select if you are seeking a referral for a primary care physician.
*   + **Specialist**: Select if you are seeking a referral for a specialist.
*   + **Community Health Center**: Select if you are seeking a referral for a community health center.
*   + **Other (Specify)**: Select if the type of referral is not listed above.
* **Support and Services** (`Support and Services`, select_one, required): Select one option that describes the level of support you require.
*   + **Counseling**: Select if you require counseling services.
*   + **Transportation**: Select if you require transportation services.
*   + **Home Visiting**: Select if you require home visiting services.
*   + **Homebound Case Management**: Select if you require homebound case management services.
*   + **Peer Support**: Select if you require peer support.
*   + **Other (Specify)**: Select if the level of support is not listed above.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, required): Provide any other relevant details that will help us assist you.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, select_multiple, required): Select all the contact methods you would like to be contacted through.
*   + **Phone**: Select if you would like to be contacted by phone.
*   + **Email**: Select if you would like to be contacted by email.
*   + **Fax**: Select if you would like to be contacted by fax.
*   + **Mail**: Select if you would like to be contacted by mail.
*   + **Other (Specify)**: Select if the contact method is not listed above.
* **Referral Contact** (`Referral Contact`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the person or organization referring you.
* **Referral Contact Info** (`Referral Contact Info`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the referring person or organization.
* **Referral Date** (`Referral Date`, date, required): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time** (`Referral Time`, time, required): Enter the time of the referral.

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure a smooth referral process. If you are unsure about any field, please contact your organization's referral team for assistance.
