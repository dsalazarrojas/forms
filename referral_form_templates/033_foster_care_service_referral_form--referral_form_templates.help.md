# Foster Care Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foster Care Service Referral Form is used to refer individuals to foster care services for evaluation and potential placement in the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with accurate and thorough information about the individual being referred.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed.
3. Review and verify the information on the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4. Sign and date the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Please enter the individual's first name. 
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Please enter the individual's last name. 
* **date_of_birth** (3, date, required: false): Please enter the individual's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. 
* **Referral Service** (4, select_multiple, required: false): Please select the foster care service that the individual is being referred to. 
* **Case Worker Name** (5, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the social worker referring the individual. 
* **Social Worker Notes** (6, note, required: false): Please enter any additional notes or comments from the social worker. 
* **Email** (7, email, required: false): Please enter the individual's email address. 
* **Phone** (8, text, required: false): Please enter the individual's phone number. 
* **Address** (9, text, required: false): Please enter the individual's address.
