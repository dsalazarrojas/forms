# Translation Localisation Expert Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Translation Localisation Expert Referral Form is used to collect information from potential translation specialists for the purpose of referral.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully to understand the type of information that is being collected.
2. If you are a translation specialist, fill out the form to provide your details and preferences.
3. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Double-check that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Translation Specialist** (Select Multiple):
  * Check if you are a translation specialist or not.
* **Name** (Text):
  * Enter your name.
* **E-mail** (E-mail):
  * Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text):
  * Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (Select One):
  * Select your job title from the given options.
* **Work Experience** (Select One):
  * Select your level of work experience from the given options.
* **Expertise Level** (Select Multiple):
  * Check your level of expertise in translation services.
* **Native Language** (Select One):
  * Select your native language from the given options.
* **Languages Known** (Text):
  * List the languages you are proficient in.
* **Other languages** (Text):
  * List any other languages you are proficient in.
* **Additional Skills** (Text):
  * Describe any additional skills you possess.
* **Other skills** (Text):
  * Describe any other skills you possess.
* **Resume** (Text):
  * Attach or copy and paste your resume.
* **Portfolio** (Text):
  * Attach or copy and paste your portfolio.
* **Social Media** (Text):
  * Provide your social media links.
* **Website** (Text):
  * Provide your website URL.
* **Contact Preference** (Select One):
  * Select your preferred method of contact.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (Select One):
  * Select your preferred method of communication.
* **Preferred Language** (Select One):
  * Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Location** (Select One):
  * Select your location.
* **Timezone** (Time):
  * Enter your timezone.
* **Other Timezone** (Text):
  * Enter any other timezone you are available in.
* **Available Time** (Time):
  * Enter your available time.
* **Submission** (Date):
  * Enter the date you are willing to submit your information.
* **Other languages (if any)** (Text):
  * List any other languages you are proficient in, if not already listed.
