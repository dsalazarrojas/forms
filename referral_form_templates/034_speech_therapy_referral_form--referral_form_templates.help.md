# Speech Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information for speech therapy referrals. The data collected will be used to make informed decisions about patient care.

## How To Complete This Form
-   Please fill out all required fields with accurate and clear information.
-   Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy.
-   Only select one option for fields that require a single selection.
-   Select all relevant options for fields that allow multiple selections.
-   Provide clear and concise answers to open-ended fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: Enter the patient's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Patient's Primary Language**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the patient speaks in English or if they have a primary language that may need to be considered for therapy.
* **Speech Therapy Goals**: Describe the patient's speech therapy objectives, such as improving communication skills or addressing a speech impediment.
* **Speech Therapy Needs Assessment**: Select all conditions or concerns that apply to the patient, such as 'Yes' for speech impediments or 'No' for cognitive impairments.
* **Referrals**: Provide any relevant referrals or contacts that may be necessary for the patient's care.
* **Patient History**: Enter any relevant medical history or background information that may impact the patient's care.
* **Speech Therapy Plan**: Describe the proposed plan for the patient's speech therapy, including goals, objectives, and expected outcomes.

## Tips
-   Please be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
-   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the healthcare team for assistance.
-   This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with external parties.
-   Review the form for completeness before submitting.
