# School Counselling Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a student's referral for school counselling. The form is intended to be completed by school staff members, such as teachers or administrators, to refer a student for counselling due to a specific issue or problem. The information collected will be used to assess the student's needs and provide guidance and support to help them succeed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information about the student you wish to refer for counselling.
2. Fill in the student's name and grade level.
3. Provide parent information, including name, phone number, and email (if applicable).
4. Describe the student's problem or issue that is causing concern.
5. Choose the reason for referral (e.g. academic concerns, behavior problems, etc.).
6. Ensure that you have obtained the consent of the student (and parent) for the disclosure of sensitive information.
7. Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to enter the name of the student being referred for counselling.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required): This field is used to select the student's grade level.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the name of the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, optional): This field is used to enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Student Problem** (`student_problem_info`, `text`, required): This field is used to describe the specific problem or issue that is causing concern for the student.
* **Behavior Referral** (`behavior_referral`, `text`, optional): This field is used to describe any behavior problems that are causing concern.
* **Behavior Referral Details** (`behavior_referral_details`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide more detailed information about the behavior problems.
* **Concerned By** (`concerned_by`, `text`, optional): This field is used to indicate who is concerned about the student's problem (e.g. teacher, parent, etc.).
* **Privacy and Confidentiality** (`privacy_and_confidentiality`, `text`, optional): This field is used to acknowledge that sensitive information may be disclosed.
* **Disclosure** (`disclosure`, `text`, optional): This field is used to confirm that you have obtained consent for the disclosure of sensitive information.
* **Agreed** (`agreed`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to confirm that you have obtained consent from the student (and parent) for the disclosure of sensitive information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, optional): This field is used to sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate and complete.
* **Concerned By** (`concerned_by`, `text`, optional): This field is used to indicate who is concerned about the student's problem (e.g. teacher, parent, etc.).
* **Student Referral Reason** (`student_referral_reason`, `text`, optional): This field is used to select the reason for referral (e.g. academic concerns, behavior problems, etc.).
* **Concerned By Parent** (`concerned_by_parent`, `text`, optional): This field is used to indicate if the parent is concerned about the student's problem.
* **Concerned By Teacher** (`concerned_by_teacher`, `text`, optional): This field is used to indicate if the teacher is concerned about the student's problem.

Note: Please review and confirm that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
