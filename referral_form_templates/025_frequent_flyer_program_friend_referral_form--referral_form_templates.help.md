# Frequent Flyer Program Friend Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to refer a friend to our Frequent Flyer Program. Fill it out to help us track your friend's information and get them on our list.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by filling out your member's name, friend's name, and friend's email address. These are required fields.
- Choose how your friend knows you (Member/Non-Member).
- Enter a date and time for when you referred your friend.
- You may also want to include a message for your friend, if you'd like.
- Finally, select how you submitted this form (User/System) and confirm that you referred your friend (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a program member.
* **Friend Name** (`friend_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your friend you're referring.
* **Friend's Email** (`friend_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your friend.
* **Friend's Phone** (`friend_phone`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter your friend's phone number.
* **Friend's IP** (`friend_ip`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter your friend's IP address.
* **Friend's Message** (`friend_message`, `note`, optional): Optional: Enter a message for your friend.
* **Referrer** (`referrer`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you, as a program member, referred your friend.
* **Referrer Title** (`referrer_title`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter your friend's title.
* **Referrer Company** (`referrer_company`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter your friend's company.
* **Referrer Address** (`referrer_address`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter your friend's address.
* **Referrer Phone** (`referrer_phone`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter your friend's phone number.
* **Member's Goals** (`member_goals`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter any goals your friend has with the program.
* **Member's Email** (`member_email`, `email`, optional): Optional: Enter your email address.
* **Member's Phone** (`member_phone`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter your phone number.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter when you joined the program.
* **Member Referrals** (`member_referrals`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter the number of referrals you've made.
* **Member Referrals Count** (`member_referrals_count`, `number`, optional): Optional: Enter the total number of referrals you've made.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_one`, required): Confirm how you submitted this form.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time this form was submitted.
* **Referrer Title** (`referrer_title`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter a title for your friend.
* **Referrer Company** (`referrer_company`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter a company for your friend.
* **Referrer Address** (`referrer_address`, `text`, optional): Optional: Enter an address for your friend.
* **Submitted By User** (`submitted_by_user`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether you or the system submitted this form.

Note that some fields are optional, but filling them out can provide more details for program administrators to process your referral.
