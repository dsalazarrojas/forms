This form is designed to collect information about a referral from a mechanic, which can be used for various purposes such as tracking referral sources, evaluating referral effectiveness, or providing feedback on referrals. The form should only be used for referrals that are related to mechanics, and not for other types of referrals.

To ensure that referrals are properly recorded and tracked, please make sure to complete all required fields and avoid duplicating existing referrals.

# Mechanic Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track referrals from mechanics for various purposes such as tracking referral sources, evaluating referral effectiveness, or providing feedback on referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form name and phone number in the first two fields.
2. Select the referral type from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of referral (e.g. friend, family, or colleague).
3. Enter any comments related to the referral in the text area.
4. Specify the source of the referral from the dropdown menu (e.g. friend, family, or colleague).
5. Choose a date and time for the referral if applicable.
6. If the referral has a monetary value, enter the amount.
7. Check the status of the referral (e.g. pending, approved, or refunded).
8. Provide any additional comments about the referral in the text area.
9. If applicable, enter the URL of the referral source.
10. If applicable, attach an image or document related to the referral.
11. If applicable, enter a link to a video related to the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Mechanic Referral Form Name** (`mechanic_referral_form_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the referral form, which is used to identify the referral.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Phone Number** (`mechanic_referral_form_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who referred the mechanic.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Email** (`mechanic_referral_form_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person who referred the mechanic.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Type** (`mechanic_referral_form_type`, select_multiple, required): Select from the dropdown menu to indicate the type of referral (e.g. friend, family, or colleague).
- **Mechanic Referral Form Comments** (`mechanic_referral_form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments related to the referral.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Source** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_source`, select_one, required): Select from the dropdown menu to specify the source of the referral (e.g. friend, family, or colleague).
- **Mechanic Referral Form Date** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the referral if applicable.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Time** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the referral if applicable.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Amount** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_amount`, number, optional): If the referral has a monetary value, enter the amount.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Status** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_status`, select_one, optional): Check the status of the referral (e.g. pending, approved, or refunded).
- **Mechanic Referral Form Comments** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about the referral.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Source URL** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_source_url`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the URL of the referral source.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Source Image** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_source_image`, text, optional): If applicable, attach an image related to the referral.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Source Document** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_source_document`, text, optional): If applicable, attach a document related to the referral.
- **Mechanic Referral Form Source Video** (`mechanic_referral_form_referral_source_video`, text, optional): If applicable, enter a link to a video related to the referral.

Note: This help guide is not exhaustive, and you should refer to the form schema for any additional fields or specific requirements.
