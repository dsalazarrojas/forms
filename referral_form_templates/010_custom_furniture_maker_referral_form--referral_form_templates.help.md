# Custom Furniture Maker Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custom Furniture Maker Referral Form is a tool designed to collect information about customers or visitors who are interested in the custom furniture making process. This form helps us understand how they heard about us and what motivated them to visit our studio.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Company, and Referral Source.
2. Enter any additional referral data if applicable.
3. If you are a contact person or representative, please provide your contact email and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is a required field.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company you are associated with. This is a required field.
* **Referral Source**: Select how you heard about us. Please choose one of the options: Online Advertisement, Social Media, Word of Mouth, or Other (and provide a brief explanation in the field below).
* **Date Visited**: Enter the date you visited our studio. This is a required field.
* **Time Visited**: Enter the time you visited our studio. (Optional)
* **Visited With**: Enter the person you visited with. (Optional)
* **Referred By**: Enter the person who referred you to us. (Optional)
* **Referral Data 1**, **Referral Data 2**, **Referral Data 3**: Enter any additional referral data as needed. (Optional)
* **Contact Person**: Enter your contact person's name. (Optional)
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address. (Optional)
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number. (Optional)
* **Address**: Enter your address. (Optional)
* **Referral Source Other**: If you chose "Other" as the Referral Source, enter a brief explanation. (Optional)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your visit. (Optional)
* **Social Media Links**: Enter your social media links. (Optional)
* **Other Info**: Enter any other relevant information about your visit. (Optional)
* **Company Website**: Enter your company website. (Optional)
