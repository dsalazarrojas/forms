# Transport Rental Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transport Rental Referral Form is designed to help users refer a driver for transport rental services. This form is meant to provide essential information about the driver and their agreement to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the driver's details in the "Driver Details" field.
2. Select whether you agree or do not agree to the terms and conditions in the "Agree and Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Refer A Driver** (`transport_rental_referral_form`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can enter the name of the driver you are referring for transport rental services.
* **Driver Details** (`transport_rental_referral_form_driver_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is where you can provide more information about the driver, such as their contact number or email address.
* **Agree and Submit** (`transport_rental_referral_form_agreement`, select_one, required):
	+ Select either "I Agree to the Terms and Conditions" or "I Do Not Agree to the Terms and Conditions" to acknowledge your agreement status.
	+ Note that this field is required, meaning you must select one of the options to complete the form.
