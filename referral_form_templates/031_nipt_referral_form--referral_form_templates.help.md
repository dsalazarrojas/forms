# NIPT Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The NIPT Referral Form is a document used to facilitate referrals for patients who require testing or medical attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, healthcare providers can follow these steps:

1. Enter patient information, including name, contact details, and other relevant medical background.
2. Provide lab information, including test name, date, and time.
3. Enter test results and any relevant notes or comments.
4. Confirm the consent status of the patient.
5. Enter referral information and any additional notes or comments.
6. Complete the form by entering the necessary details and submitting it for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (Patient Information, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name, contact details, and any other relevant medical background.
* **Mother Info** (Mother Info, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the patient's mother or primary caregiver.
* **Lab Information** (Lab Information, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the lab or testing facility, along with any relevant contact details.
* **Test Results** (Test Results, text, required/optional): Enter the results of the test or medical examination.
* **Consent Info** (Consent Info, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant details about the patient's consent, including the status and date.
* **Signature** (Signature, note, required/optional): Enter a note or comment for the signature or approval of the patient.
* **Test Name** (Test Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the test or medical examination.
* **Test Date** (Test Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the test or medical examination.
* **Test Time** (Test Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the test or medical examination.
* **Lab Name** (Lab Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the lab or testing facility.
* **Lab Phone** (Lab Phone, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the lab or testing facility.
* **Lab Address** (Lab Address, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the lab or testing facility.
* **Test Results Notes** (Test Results Notes, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the test results.
* **Lab Contact** (Lab Contact, text, required/optional): Enter the contact details of the lab or testing facility.
* **Patient Name** (Patient Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Provider Name** (Provider Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the healthcare provider or specialist.
* **Provider Info** (Provider Info, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the healthcare provider or specialist.
* **Consent Status** (Consent Status, select one, required/optional): Select the status of the patient's consent, such as "Consent Given", "Consent Pending", or "Consent Refused".
* **Referral Info** (Referral Info, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the referral.
* **Test Result Date** (Test Result Date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the test result.

* **Test Result Note** (Test Result Note, text, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the test result.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields marked as required are filled in accurately.
* Enter the correct consent status of the patient.
* Enter the correct contact details of the lab or testing facility.
* Verify the accuracy of all entered information before submitting the form.
* Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submission.
