# Adult And Dislocated Worker Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "Adult And Dislocated Worker Referral Form" is used to collect relevant information from dislocated or adult workers. This information will help us assess their job placement needs and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary information before starting the form. This will save time and reduce errors.
2. Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly, following the instructions provided.
3. If you are unsure about a field, seek guidance from a supervisor or colleague.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 2. Full Name

* **Full Name (worker_name)**: Please enter your full name, including any middle names or initials.

### 3. Date of Birth

* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your birthdate in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).

### 4. Email Address

* **Email Address (email_address)**: Enter your official email address for communication purposes.

### 5. Phone Number

* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.

### 6. Home Address

* **Home Address (home_address)**: Enter your current home address, including street address, city, and state.

### 7. City

* **City (city)**: Enter your city or town name.

### 8. State

* **State (state)**: Enter your state or province name.

### 9. ZIP Code

* **ZIP Code (zip_code)**: Enter your zip code for postal purposes.

### 11. Worker Classification

* **Worker Classification (worker_type)**: Select one of the following:
	+ Dislocated Worker
	+ Adult Worker
	+ Long-Term Unemployed
	+ Incumbent Worker

### 12. Reason for Referral

* **Reason for Referral (reason_for_referral)**: Briefly describe the reason for referral (e.g., job loss, layoff, education, etc.).

### 13. Date of Job Loss or Layoff

* **Date of Job Loss or Layoff (layoff_date)**: If applicable, enter the date of your job loss or layoff.

### 14. Previous Employer Name

* **Previous Employer Name (previous_employer)**: Enter your previous employer's name, if relevant.

### 15. Previous Job Title

* **Previous Job Title (job_title)**: Enter your previous job title, if relevant.

### 16. Years of Employment

* **Years of Employment (employment_duration)**: Enter the number of years you were employed at your previous job.

### 17. Education and Skills

* **Education and Skills (education_section)**: This is a note section for you to describe your highest level of education and any relevant skills.

### 18. Highest Level of Education

* **Highest Level of Education (highest_education)**: Select one of the following:
	+ Less than high school
	+ High school diploma or GED
	+ Some college or vocational training
	+ Associate degree
	+ Bachelor degree
	+ Master degree
	+ Doctorate

### 19. Key Skills and Competencies

* **Key Skills and Competencies (skills)**: Describe your key skills and competencies.

### 20. Professional Certifications or Licenses

* **Professional Certifications or Licenses (certifications)**: List any professional certifications or licenses you hold.

### 21. Job Search Goals and Target Positions

* **Job Search Goals and Target Positions (job_search_goals)**: Describe your job search goals and target positions.

### 22. Expected Salary Range

* **Expected Salary Range (wage_expectations)**: Enter your expected salary range for the job you are seeking.

### 23. Barriers to Employment

* **Barriers to Employment (employment_barriers)**: Briefly describe any barriers to employment, such as transportation, childcare, disability, etc.

Note to users: Please ensure that all fields marked as required are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about a field, please seek guidance from a supervisor or colleague. This will help us provide targeted support and placement services.
