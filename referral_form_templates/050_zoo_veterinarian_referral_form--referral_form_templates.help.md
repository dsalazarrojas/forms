# Zoo Veterinarian Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from zoos about their veterinary referral requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the animal's details in the corresponding fields, including its name, type, and description.
2. Provide contact information for the zoo, including their name, address, and contact number.
3. Describe the animal's health concerns and care requirements.
4. Enter the referral request date and follow-up date (if applicable).
5. Add any additional comments or photos as needed.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm receipt of the referral request.
7. Include a signature from a veterinarian to confirm the referral request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Animal Details (`zoo_veterinarian_referral_form`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Please enter the animal's details, including its name, type, and description.
* **Animal Name (`animal_name`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Enter the name of the animal.
* **Animal Type (`animal_type`, `select_one`, required: false):**
	* Select the type of animal (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Animal Description (`animal_description`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Provide a brief description of the animal.
* **Zoo Contact Information (`zoo_contact_information`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Enter the zoo's name, address, and contact number.
* **Veterinarian Name (`veterinarian_name`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Enter the name of the veterinarian making the referral request.
* **Animal Health Concerns (`animal_health_concerns`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Describe the animal's health concerns.
* **Care Requirements (`care_requirements`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Provide any additional care requirements for the animal.
* **Referral Request Date (`referral_request_date`, `date`, required: false):**
	* Enter the date of the referral request.
* **Follow-up Date (`follow_up_date`, `date`, required: false):**
	* Enter the follow-up date (if applicable).
* **Next Steps (`next_steps`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Add any next steps or instructions for the referral specialist.
* **Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Animal Photo URL (`animal_photo_url`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Provide a URL for an online photo of the animal.
* **Animal Photo (`animal_photo`, `note`, required: false):**
	* Add a physical photo of the animal (if needed).
* **Referral Request (`referral_request`, `text`, required: false):**
	* Describe the referral request.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- Attach any relevant photos or documents as needed.
- Use clear and concise language when describing the animal's health concerns and care requirements.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
