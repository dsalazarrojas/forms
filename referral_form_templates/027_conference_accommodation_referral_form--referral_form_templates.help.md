# Conference Accommodation Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about your preferences for conference accommodation so that our team can provide you with personalized hotel recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you've answered all the required questions.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form to help our team provide you with suitable accommodation options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Accommodation Referral** (`referral_intro`): This is a note field to provide a brief introduction to your stay preferences.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`): Enter your full name as the attendee.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Conference Information** (`conference_info`): This is a note field to provide event details.
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`): Enter the name of the conference or event.
* **Conference Dates** (`conference_dates`): Enter the event dates.
* **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`): Enter your arrival date.
* **Check-Out Date** (`check_out_date`): Enter your departure date.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`): Describe your lodging needs, e.g., single or double room.
* **Room Type Preference** (`room_type`): Choose the type of room you prefer (e.g., Standard Single, Standard Double, etc.).
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`): Enter the total number of guests.
* **Number of Rooms** (`number_of_rooms`): Enter the number of rooms you need.
* **Budget Range Per Night** (`budget_range`): Choose your preferred budget range (e.g., Budget under 100 dollars).
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`): Choose where you'd like to stay (e.g., Walking Distance to Venue).
* **Amenities Needed** (`amenities_needed`): Select the facilities you require (e.g., Free WiFi).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`): Select any special requirements (e.g., Wheelchair Accessible Room).
* **Smoking Preference** (`smoking_preference`): Choose whether you prefer non-smoking or smoking rooms.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`): Enter any specific needs or requests.
* **Transportation Needs** (`transportation_needs`): Describe how you plan to get to the conference.
* **Airport Pickup Needed** (`airport_pickup`): If you require transportation from the airport, select 'Yes Needed'.
* **Referral Priority** (`referral_priority`): Choose your urgency level (e.g., High Need Immediately).
* **Previous Conference Stays** (`previous_stays`): Indicate if you've stayed with us before.
* **Preferred Hotel Chain** (`preferred_hotel_chain`): Enter your preferred hotel brand.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any other information that may help us serve you better.
