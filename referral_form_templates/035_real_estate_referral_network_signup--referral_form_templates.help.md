# Real Estate Referral Network Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for real estate agents to join the Real Estate Referral Network. It is meant to collect information for internal use only and is not intended to be shared with any third-party vendors or partners without explicit consent.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required information in each field carefully.
2. Double-check your details before submission.
3. Review each field's specific explanation to ensure you understand the purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **real_estate_agent**: A text field for the name of the real estate agent.
* **real_estate_office**: A text field for the name of the real estate office.
* **real_estate_phone**: A text field for the phone number of the real estate agent.
* **real_estate_email**: A text field for the email address of the real estate agent.
* **real_estate_terms**: A text field for any additional terms or notes about the referral agreement.
* **real_estate_agreement**: A text field for the text of the referral agreement.
* **real_estate_agreement_2**: This field is a duplicate of real_estate_agreement. It is not clear why it is included in the form, and its purpose is unclear. Please fill this field only if you have a valid reason to do so.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, and your information will not be shared with any third-party vendors or partners without explicit consent.
