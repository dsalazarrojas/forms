# Café Bakery Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Café Bakery Referral Form is designed to help us understand how you came across our business and what makes you think we could be a good fit for a potential partnership or collaboration with your referred business. Please provide as much detail as you can so we can effectively follow up with them.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email, so we can get in touch with you to follow up on your referral.
2. Choose which category best describes your relationship with the referred business.
3. Enter the name and contact information of the referred business.
4. Select the type of business the referred business is.
5. Let us know what type of referral you're interested in (e.g., wholesale bakery supply, catering services, etc.).
6. If you're not sure about the estimated monthly volume of the referred business, it's okay! You can choose 'Not sure'.
7. Briefly explain why you're referring this business to us.
8. Indicate how you know the referred business (owner/employee, friend/family, etc.).
9. Choose a preferred time to contact the referred business (morning, afternoon, evening, etc.).
10. Confirm if you have permission to share the referred business's contact information with us.
11. If you're interested in referral rewards, let us know.
12. Finally, add any additional notes about your referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`referrer_name`): Please enter your name so we can get in touch with you to follow up.
* **Your Email** (`referrer_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you for further information about your referral.
* **Your Phone** (`referrer_phone`): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **I Am A** (`referrer_type`): This will help us understand how you know the referred business. Choose the category that best describes your relationship.
* **Referred Business Name** (`referred_business_name`): Enter the name of the referred business for our records.
* **Referred Contact Name** (`referred_contact_name`): Enter the primary contact person's name at the referred business.
* **Referred Contact Email** (`referred_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the referred contact for easy communication.
* **Referred Contact Phone** (`referred_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the referred contact.
* **Referred Business Type** (`referred_business_type`): Choose the category that best describes the type of business the referred business is.
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`): Select the type of referral you're interested in, and we'll take it from there.
* **Estimated Monthly Volume** (`estimated_volume`): If you're familiar with the referred business's volume, choose the category that best fits. If not, it's okay to choose 'Not sure'.
* **Reason for Referral** (`referral_reason`): Explain briefly why you're referring this business to us.
* **Your Relationship to Referred Business** (`relationship_to_business`): This helps us understand how you know the referred business. Choose the category that best describes it.
* **Best Contact Time** (`best_contact_time`): Indicate your preferred time to contact the referred business.
* **Do You Have Permission to Share This Contact** (`permission_to_share`): Confirm if you have permission from the referred business to share their contact information with us.
* **Referral Incentive Interest** (`referral_incentive`): If you're interested in referral rewards, let us know!
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Use this space to add any additional information about your referral.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough in your responses to help us better understand your referral.
- If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask, and we'll clarify.
- We'll follow up with you and the referred business to discuss further.
- This form is a starting point for potential partnerships.
