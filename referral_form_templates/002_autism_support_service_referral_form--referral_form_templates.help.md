# Autism Support Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Autism Support Service Referral Form is designed to gather essential information about individuals with ASD to facilitate their access to appropriate support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure a smooth referral process, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields (marked as "required").
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about the individual being referred.
3. Select relevant services from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referrer Name**: Enter the name of the professional making the referral.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the referring clinic, school, or agency.
* **Referrer Email**: Enter the professional email address for correspondence.
* **Referrer Phone Number**: Enter the direct contact number.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date this referral is being submitted.
* **Client Full Name**: Enter the legal name of the individual being referred.
* **Client Date of Birth**: Enter the individual's date of birth.
* **Gender**: Select the individual's gender (optional).
* **Primary Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Caregiver Contact Number**: Enter the best contact number for the family.
* **Autism Diagnosis Status**: Select the current status of the ASD diagnosis.
* **Primary Reason for Referral**: Provide a brief description of the individual's current needs or concerns.
* **Current Support Services**: Select all applicable services the individual is currently receiving.
* **Behavioral Concerns**: Describe any significant behavioral patterns or challenges.
* **Relevant Medical History**: Note any co-occurring conditions or medications.
* **Urgency Level**: Select the level of urgency for this referral.

Please ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately and comprehensively, and select relevant services to support the individual's needs.
