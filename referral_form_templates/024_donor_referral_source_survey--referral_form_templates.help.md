# Donor Referral Source Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Referral Source Survey is a form designed to gather information about how a donor found out about our organization. This form helps us understand our outreach efforts and identify areas for improvement in our referral sources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
2. Select all that apply for "what_doors_did_you_hear_about_us_from" on page 1.
3. If you answered "other" on page 1, please provide more information in the "other_referral_source" field on page 2.
4. On page 3, enter the number of referrals you've made to our organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_doors_did_you_hear_about_us_from** (`select_multiple`): Please select all the sources where you heard about our organization. You can choose from the options:
	+ Yes, a friend or family member
	+ No, through a promotional event
	+ No, online advertising
	+ No, other (please specify in the "other_referral_source" field)
* **other_referral_source** (`text`): If you selected "other" on page 1, please provide more information about where you heard about our organization.
* **how_many_doors_have_you_reflected_donations_to_us** (`number`): Enter the number of referrals you've made to our organization.

## Tips
- Please answer the questions honestly and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure or have already completed this form, you can disregard this guide.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, please reach out to us.
