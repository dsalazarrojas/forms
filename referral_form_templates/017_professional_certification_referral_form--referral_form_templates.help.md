# Professional Certification Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about professional certifications, including referrer details, candidate details, and certification information. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure the necessary information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the referrer's details, including their name, phone number, email, organization, job title, and relationship to the candidate.
2. Enter the candidate's details, including their name, contact information, position, and certification type.
3. Select the certification type and date for the certification.
4. Add any additional notes or comments.
5. Complete the referrer's signature, date, and time.
6. Complete the candidate's signature, date, and time.
7. Enter the referrer's termination date and time (if applicable).
8. Enter the candidate's comments (if applicable).
9. Select the tool assigned to the candidate (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer**: <i>This is the person submitting the referral form.</i> (`Referrer`, text, required)
* **Referrer's Phone**: <i>Please enter the referrer's phone number.</i> (`Referrer's Phone`, text, required)
* **Referrer's Email**: <i>Please enter the referrer's email address.</i> (`Referrer's Email`, email, required)
* **Referrer's Organization**: <i>Please enter the referrer's organization name.</i> (`Referrer's Organization`, text, required)
* **Referrer's Job Title**: <i>Please enter the referrer's job title.</i> (`Referrer's Job Title`, text, required)
* **Relationship**: <i>Please describe your relationship to the candidate.</i> (`Relationship`, text, required)
* **Candidate Name**: <i>Please enter the candidate's name.</i> (`Candidate Name`, text, required)
* **Candidate Contact Info**: <i>Please enter the candidate's contact information.</i> (`Candidate Contact Info`, text, required)
* **Position**: <i>Select the candidate's position.</i> (`Position`, select_one, required)
* **Certification Type**: <i>Select the type of certification.</i> (`Certification Type`, select_multiple, required)
* **Certification Date**: <i>Please enter the certification date.</i> (`Certification Date`, date, required)
* **Certification Expiration**: <i>Please enter the certification expiration date.</i> (`Certification Expiration`, date, required)
* **Notes**: <i>Please add any additional comments or notes.</i> (`Notes`, note, required)
* **Referrer's Signature**: <i>Enter the referrer's signature.</i> (`Referrer's Signature`, text, required)
* **Referrer Date**: <i>Please enter the referrer's date.</i> (`Referrer Date`, date, required)
* **Referrer Time**: <i>Please enter the referrer's time.</i> (`Referrer Time`, time, required)
* **Referrer Comments**: <i>Please add any comments from the referrer.</i> (`Referrer Comments`, note, required)
* **Candidate Signature**: <i>Enter the candidate's signature.</i> (`Candidate Signature`, text, required)
* **Candidate Date**: <i>Please enter the candidate's date.</i> (`Candidate Date`, date, required)
* **Candidate Time**: <i>Please enter the candidate's time.</i> (`Candidate Time`, time, required)
* **Candidate Comments**: <i>Please add any comments from the candidate.</i> (`Candidate Comments`, note, required)
* **Referrer Termination Date**: <i>Please enter the referrer's termination date (if applicable).</i> (`Referrer Termination Date`, date, required)
* **Referrer Termination Time**: <i>Please enter the referrer's termination time (if applicable).</i> (`Referrer Termination Time`, time, required)
* **Referrer Termination Comments**: <i>Please add any termination comments from the referrer (if applicable).</i> (`Referrer Termination Comments`, note, required)
* **Tool**: <i>Please select the tool assigned to the candidate (if applicable).</i> (`Tool`, text, required)

Tips:

* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and completely.
* Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant authority for clarification.
* This form is for professional certification referrals only.
* Please do not submit duplicate or incomplete forms.
