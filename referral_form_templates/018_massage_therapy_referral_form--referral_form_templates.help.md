# Massage Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Referral Form is designed to collect information about patients' medical history, insurance details, and referral sources for massage therapy sessions. This form helps healthcare professionals to refer patients to a massage therapist and provide necessary context for their treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the patient's first name in the **First Name** field.
*   Enter the patient's last name in the **Last Name** field.
*   Enter the patient's date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
*   Enter the patient's email address in the **Email** field.
*   Enter the patient's phone number in the **Phone** field.
*   Enter the patient's fax number (if applicable) in the **Fax** field.
*   Enter the name of the patient's primary insurance provider in the **Primary Insurance Provider Name** field.
*   Provide a brief description of the patient's medical history in the **Medical History** field.
*   Enter any relevant medical conditions or issues the patient is experiencing in the **Medical Condition** field.
*   Enter the name of the massage therapist the patient is referring to in the **Massage Therapist Name** field.
*   Enter the massage therapist's phone number in the **Massage Therapist Phone** field.
*   Enter the massage therapist's fax number (if applicable) in the **Massage Therapist Fax** field.
*   Enter the massage therapist's email address in the **Massage Therapist Email** field.
*   Provide any additional notes or comments about the patient's treatment in the **Massage Therapy Notes** field.
*   Enter the name of the doctor who referred the patient in the **Doctor Name** field.
*   Enter the doctor's contact information in the **Doctor Contact Info** field.
*   Enter the doctor's specialty in the **Doctor Specialty** field.
*   Enter the doctor's phone number in the **Doctor Phone** field.
*   Enter the doctor's fax number (if applicable) in the **Doctor Fax** field.
*   Enter the doctor's email address in the **Doctor Email** field.
*   Provide the source of the referral in the **Referral Source** field.
*   Enter the name of the doctor who referred the patient in the **Referring Doctor** field (if applicable).
*   Enter any additional comments or information about the patient's treatment in the **Massage Therapy Notes** field.
