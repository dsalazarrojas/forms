# pediatric_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pediatric referral form is used to collect basic information about a patient's medical and family background for pediatric referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's birthdate in the "Birthdate" field.
3. Provide the patient's address in the "Address" field.
4. Select the primary provider (e.g., "yes" or "no") in the "Primary Provider" field.
5. Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
6. Describe the patient's family background in the "Family Background" field.
7. For "Medications", select all applicable medications the patient is currently taking.
8. For "Allergies", select all known allergies the patient has.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`Patient Name`, required: false): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their official identification.
* **Birthdate** (`Birthdate`, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`Address`, required: false): Enter the patient's home address.
* **Primary Provider** (`Primary Provider`, required: false): Select "yes" if the patient has a primary healthcare provider, "no" otherwise.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, required: false): Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant medical conditions or treatments.
* **Family Background** (`Family Background`, required: false): Describe the patient's family background, including any relevant family medical history.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, required: false): Select all medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, required: false): Select all known allergies the patient has.

## Tips

- Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter for each field.
- Be as concise as possible while still providing necessary information.
- If unsure about any of the fields, it is best to err on the side of caution and select "unknown" or "no" where necessary.
