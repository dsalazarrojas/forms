# Software Hardware Supplier Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about software and hardware suppliers, which can be used for referrals to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the vendor's details on the first page.
2. Provide the contact information on the second page.
3. Enter the referral details on the third page.
4. Add any other relevant information on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vendor Details (Page 1: vendor_details)
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the vendor you want to refer to the organization.

### Contact Information (Page 2: contact_info)
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact information of the person or team responsible for the referral.

### Referral Details (Page 3: referral_details)
* **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the referral reason, product, or service.

### Software & Hardware (Page 4: software_hardware)
* **Software & Hardware** (`software_hardware`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the software or hardware that is being referred.

### Other Details (Page 5: other_details)
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, `text`, required/optional): Add any other relevant information about the vendor or referral.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with the organization's referral process.
* Double-check your submission before submitting.
