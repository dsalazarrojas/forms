# Tax Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual being referred for tax purposes. The form will help in gathering necessary details to make the referral process efficient and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name.
2. Fill in your Last name.
3. Enter your Address.
4. Enter your Email.
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. If you're comfortable sharing, provide your Date of birth.
7. If you're comfortable sharing, provide your Time of birth.
8. Choose the source of the referral (Client, Colleague, Friend, Family, or Other).
9. Enter the Tax year you are being referred for.
10. If you're comfortable sharing, provide your Social security number.
11. Enter the name of the Tax firm you are working with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you can be contacted.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of birth**: If you are comfortable sharing, enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of birth**: If you are comfortable sharing, enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Referral source**: Choose the source of the referral (Client, Colleague, Friend, Family, or Other).
* **Tax year**: Enter the tax year you are being referred for.
* **Social security number**: If you are comfortable sharing, enter your social security number for tax purposes.
* **Tax firm name**: Enter the name of the tax firm you are working with.

Note: The information collected from this form will be used for referral purposes only and will be kept confidential. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
