# Public Relations Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a Public Relations Consultant who has referred a new client to us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address for communication purposes.
3. Enter your Phone number for us to contact you.
4. Select the source of the referral from the Referral Source options (e.g., PR Consultant Website, PR Agency, Social Media).
5. Enter the name of the company that referred you to us.
6. Enter your Job Title at the company.
7. Provide the company's website.
8. Enter the name of the contact person at the company.
9. Enter the company's address.
10. Enter the city and state where the company is located.
11. Enter the country of the company.
12. Add any additional notes about the company in the Additional Notes field.
13. Add any notes about the referral in the Referral Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Select the source of the referral (e.g., PR Consultant Website, PR Agency, Social Media).
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company that referred you to us.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title at the company.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the company.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the company.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where the company is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where the company is located.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country where the company is located.
* **Additional Notes** (`company_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the company here.
* **Referral Notes** (`referral_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the referral here.
