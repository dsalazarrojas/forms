# Physician Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referral information from patients to facilitate efficient and accurate communication between healthcare providers. The form is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to gather necessary details for referral purposes, ensuring a smooth transition of care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide patient information, including demographic details and contact numbers.
2. Enter referral information, including the reason for referral and type of referral.
3. Add any other relevant information about the patient.
4. Enter patient notes, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Patient Information (Page 1)
* **Patient Information (text)**: Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
*   **Patient Information** is a required field, and it should include the patient's name, date of birth, and contact details to ensure accurate communication.

### Referral Information (Page 2)
* **Referral Information (text)**: Describe the referral reason and provide any relevant details.
*   **Referral Information** is an optional field, use it to provide any additional context for the referral.
### Referral Reason (Page 3)
* **Referral Reason (text)**: Enter a brief description of the reason for the referral.
*   **Referral Reason** is an optional field, use it to provide a clear and concise explanation of the referral.
### Referral Type (Page 4)
* **Referral Type**: Select one of the available referral types (Internal Referral, External Referral, or Transfer of Care).
*   **Referral Type** is an optional field, use it to indicate the type of referral, which will help the healthcare team to determine the course of action.
### Other Information (Page 5)
* **Other Information (text)**: Enter any additional details that may be relevant to the patient's referral.
*   **Other Information** is an optional field, use it to provide any extra information that may be helpful for the referral process.
### Patient Notes (Page 6)
* **Patient Notes (text)**: Enter any relevant patient notes or comments.
*   **Patient Notes** is an optional field, use it to provide any additional context about the patient's condition or status.
