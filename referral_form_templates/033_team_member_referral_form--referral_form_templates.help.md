# Team Member Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Member Referral Form is used to record information about team members who are being referred for potential hiring or promotion consideration within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the required information about the team member being referred, including their name, job title, and team member they report to.
2. Select the referral type (Internal, External, or Both) for the team member.
3. If applicable, provide additional details such as contact information and job description.
4. Complete any other required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team member being referred.
* **employee_email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the team member being referred.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the team member being referred.
* **referral_date** (`referral_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the referral.
* **department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the team member being referred.
* **job_description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job for which the team member is being referred.
* **manager_name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team member's manager.
* **report_date** (`report_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the team member reports to.
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter the team member's ID number.
* **team_member** (`team_member`, select_one, required): Select the team member being referred from the list provided.
* **referral_type** (`referral_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of referral (Internal, External, or Both) for the team member.
* **contact** (`contact`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or contact information for the team member.
* **employee_phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter the team member's phone number.
* **team_member_id** (`team_member_id`, number, optional): Enter the team member's ID number.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the team member being referred is a current or former employee of the organization.
- Use the correct referral type to reflect the nature of the referral.
- Complete all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate referral record.
