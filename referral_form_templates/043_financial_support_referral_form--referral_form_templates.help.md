# Financial Support Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Financial Support Referral Form is a tool used to help individuals or families in need of financial assistance. Please use this form to submit referrals to our organization for review and potential support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the type of referral you are submitting:
	* Individual: For single individuals seeking financial assistance.
	* Family: For families seeking financial assistance.
2. Enter your referral details for each type:
	* Program: Enter the specific program or service you are referring the individual or family to.
	* Service: Enter the specific service or resource you are referring the individual or family to.
	* Financial Support: Enter any specific financial support needs or requirements.
3. Provide contact information:
	* Contact Details: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email for follow-up purposes.
4. Add any Additional Info:
	* Additional Info: Enter any additional information that may be helpful for our organization to consider in the referral process.
5. Enter contact information for follow-up:
	* E-mail: Enter your email address for our organization to contact you about the referral status.
	* Phone: Enter your phone number for our organization to contact you about the referral status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual or Family Referral**: Please choose whether you are referring an individual or a family in need of financial assistance.
* **Program Referral**: If you are referring an individual or family, please enter the specific program or service they may be eligible for. For example, government assistance programs or local support services.
* **Service Referral**: If you are referring an individual or family, please enter any specific services or resources they may be eligible for. For example, food banks or clothing drives.
* **Financial Support Referral**: Please enter any specific financial support needs or requirements the individual or family may have.
* **Contact Details**: Please enter your name, address, phone number, and email for follow-up purposes.
* **Additional Info**: Please enter any additional information that may be helpful for our organization to consider in the referral process.
* **E-mail**: Please enter your email address for our organization to contact you about the referral status.
* **Phone**: Please enter your phone number for our organization to contact you about the referral status.
* **Date and Time**: This field is not currently used in the referral process, but it may be used in the future. Please do not enter any information here.

## Tips
* Please make sure to submit accurate and complete information to ensure a successful referral.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact our organization for assistance.
* You can submit multiple referrals at a time, but please make sure to review each one carefully before submission.
* Our organization will review and process the referrals as quickly as possible, but please note that it may take some time for us to contact you about the referral status.
