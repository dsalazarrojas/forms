## Freight Forwarder Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Freight Forwarder Referral Form is a tool to collect and manage referral information for freight forwarders. It helps track referrals, including their status, assigned tools, and follow-up activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of referral (Referral or Partner).
2.  Enter the name of the referral company.
3.  Provide the referral name and contact email.
4.  Enter the company address, city, state, and country.
5.  Choose the assigned tool and assigned group, if applicable.
6.  Select the status of the referral (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).
7.  Add any comments, follow-up date and status, and follow-up notes.
8.  Enter the name and contact information of the assigned user, if applicable.
9.  Enter the form ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1): Enter the name of the company being referred.
* **Referral Type** (2): Select whether the referral is from a company or partner.
* **Referral Name** (3): Enter the name of the person or company being referred.
* **Company Website** (4): Enter the website of the referral company.
* **Contact E-mail** (5): Enter the email of the person or contact for the referral.
* **Contact Phone** (6): Enter the phone number of the person or contact for the referral.
* **Company Address** (7): Enter the address of the referral company.
* **City** (8): Enter the city of the referral company.
* **State** (9): Enter the state of the referral company.
* **Postal Code** (10): Enter the postal code of the referral company.
* **Country** (11): Enter the country of the referral company.
* **Referral Date** (12): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Description** (13): Add a brief description of the referral.
* **Assigned Tool** (14): Select whether the referral has an assigned tool.
* **Assigned Group** (15): Select whether the referral has an assigned group.
* **Assigned User** (16): Select whether the referral has an assigned user.
* **Assigned Date** (17): Enter the date the referral was assigned.
* **Status** (18): Select the status of the referral (Approved, Not Approved, or Pending).
* **Comments** (19): Add any comments about the referral.
* **Follow-up Date** (20): Enter the follow-up date for the referral.
* **Follow-up Status** (21): Select the follow-up status of the referral.
* **Follow-up Notes** (22): Add any follow-up notes for the referral.
* **Assigned User Name** (23): Enter the name of the assigned user.
* **Assigned Tool Name** (24): Enter the name of the assigned tool.
* **Form ID** (25): Enter a unique ID for the form.

By following these steps and using this guide, you should be able to complete the Freight Forwarder Referral Form accurately and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your administrator or support team.
