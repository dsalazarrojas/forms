# ppc_campaign_manager_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals who refer customers to our business, allowing us to track and reward referrals based on the number of customers referred.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To begin, select the "Referral Form" page at the top of the form.
2.  In the "First Name" field, enter your first name as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
3.  In the "Last Name" field, enter your last name as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
4.  In the "Email" field, enter your work email address as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
5.  In the "Phone" field, enter your work phone number as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
6.  In the "Company" field, enter the name of your current or previous company as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
7.  In the "Website" field, enter the website of your current or previous company as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
8.  In the "Job Title" field, enter your current or previous job title as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
9.  In the "Department" field, enter the department you belong to as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work email address as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
*   **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the name of your current or previous company as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
*   **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter the website of your current or previous company as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current or previous job title as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.
*   **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you belong to as it appears on your business cards or other official documents.

## Tips

*   Please make sure to fill out each field as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to your referral contact for clarification.
