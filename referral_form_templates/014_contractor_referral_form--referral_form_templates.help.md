# Contractor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and ratings from referrers about their experience with a contractor. Your input will help us identify top-performing contractors and improve our referral services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Your Email" fields, which are required fields to ensure you can be contacted about your referral.
2. In the "Contractor Name" field, enter the name of the contractor you are referring.
3. If the contractor has a company name, enter it in the "Contractor Company" field.
4. Enter the contractor's contact information in the "Contractor Phone" and "Contractor Email" fields.
5. Choose the type of service provided by the contractor from the "Type of Service" dropdown menu.
6. Describe the service performed in the "Service Description" field.
7. Enter the location where the service was performed in the "Service Location" field.
8. Rate your overall satisfaction with the contractor's work using the "Overall Satisfaction" dropdown menu.
9. Assess the quality of work, professionalism, timeliness, and value for money provided by the contractor using the corresponding dropdown menus.
10. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you would hire this contractor again.
11. If applicable, provide a description of the contractor's greatest strengths and areas for improvement.
12. Enter your reason for making this referral in the "Why Are You Referring" field.
13. If you have any additional comments or suggestions, enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your name as the person making the referral.
* **Your Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about this referral.
* **Your Phone**: (Optional) Enter your contact number, but this field is not required.
* **Relationship**: Choose how you know the contractor (e.g., Previous Customer, Friend, Family Member).
* **Contractor Name**: Enter the name of the contractor you are referring.
* **Contractor Company**: (Optional) Enter the name of the contractor's company if applicable.
* **Contractor Phone**: Enter the contractor's contact number.
* **Contractor Email**: Enter the contractor's email address.
* **Type of Service**: Choose the type of service provided by the contractor (e.g., General Contracting, Electrical Work).
* **Service Description**: Describe the service performed by the contractor.
* **Service Location**: Enter the location where the service was performed.
* **Overall Satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the contractor's work (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied).
* **Quality of Work**: Rate the quality of work provided by the contractor (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Professionalism**: Rate the contractor's professionalism (Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Timeliness**: Rate how on time the contractor completed the work (Completed Early, On Time, Slightly Delayed, Significantly Delayed).
* **Value for Money**: Rate the value of the work compared to the price paid (Excellent Value, Good Value, Fair Value, Poor Value).
* **Would Hire Again**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you would hire this contractor again.
* **Strengths**: Describe the contractor's greatest strengths.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Describe areas for improvement the contractor could work on.
* **Why Are You Referring**: Enter your reason for making this referral.
* **Specific Skills or Expertise**: (Optional) Describe what makes this contractor stand out.
* **Best Suited For What Type of Work**: (Optional) Recommend types of projects this contractor is best suited for.
* **Do We Have Permission to Share**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether we can share your referral with others.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or suggestions you have.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when describing the service performed and your experience with the contractor.
* Choose the most accurate ratings for the contractor's quality of work, professionalism, timeliness, and value for money.
* Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you would hire this contractor again based on your experience.
* If you have any concerns or issues with the contractor, please mention them in the "Areas for Improvement" field.
