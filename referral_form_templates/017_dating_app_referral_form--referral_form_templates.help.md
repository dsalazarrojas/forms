# Dating App Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dating App Referral Form is designed to collect information about your relationships with your friends, which will help us improve and enhance your experience with our app.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Select the duration of time you have known your friend from the options provided in the `Known Friend Since` field.
5. Choose the method through which you met your friend from the options provided in the `How do you know friend?` field.
6. Select the type of relationship you have with your friend from the options provided in the `Friend Relationship` field.
7. Provide a brief description of the context in which you met your friend in the `Context of Referral` field.
8. Indicate whether your friend has given consent to be referred in the `Has given consent` field.
9. If your friend has given consent, enter the date they gave consent in the `Consent Given Date` field.
10. If you need to follow up with your friend, provide a brief question or note in the `Follow up question` field.
11. Enter any additional details about your friend in the `Friend Details` field.
12. Select the methods through which you will follow up with your friend, such as phone, email, or text.
13. Finally, indicate the status of your follow up with your friend, such as sent, not sent, or in progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text)**: The first name of the friend you are referring.
* **Email (Email)**: The email address of the friend you are referring.
* **Phone (Text)**: The phone number of the friend you are referring.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: The date of birth of the friend you are referring. (Note: This field is optional and not required for most people.)
* **Known Friend Since (Select Multiple)**: Select the duration of time you have known your friend. Options include 1 week ago - 1 year ago, 1-2 years ago, 2-5 years ago, 5+ years ago.
* **How do you know friend? (Select Multiple)**: Choose the method through which you met your friend. Options include From a social media platform, From a friend of a friend, Through a common event, Other.
* **Friend Relationship (Select One)**: Select the type of relationship you have with your friend. Options include Couples, Friend, Distant relative, Other.
* **Context of Referral (Text)**: Provide a brief description of the context in which you met your friend.
* **Has given consent (Select Multiple)**: Indicate whether your friend has given consent to be referred. Options include Given, Not Given, Unknown.
* **Consent Given Date (Date)**: If your friend has given consent, enter the date they gave consent.
* **Follow up question (Note)**: If you need to follow up with your friend, provide a brief question or note.
* **Follow up answer (Text)**: If you have a follow up question, enter your answer to it.
* **Friend Details (Text)**: Enter any additional details about your friend.
* **Follow up method (Select Multiple)**: Select the methods through which you will follow up with your friend. Options include Phone, Email, Text.
* **Follow up status (Select Multiple)**: Indicate the status of your follow up with your friend. Options include Sent, Not sent, In progress.
