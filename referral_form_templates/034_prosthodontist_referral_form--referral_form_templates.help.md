# Prosthodontist Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help prosthodontists gather information from dentists when referring a patient to their practice. It includes essential details about the patient, medical history, and other relevant information that the prosthodontist needs to know to provide the best care.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form with accurate and complete information**.
2. Ensure that you have the patient's permission to share their medical history and other sensitive information.
3. **Submit the form to the prosthodontist's office**, where it will be reviewed and used for further evaluation and treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide the patient's complete name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth** (`patient_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in MM/DD format.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's current address for contact and billing purposes.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, `text`, required: false): Provide the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, `text`, required: false): Enter the patient's email address, if applicable.
* **Dentist Referral** (`dentist_referral`, `text`, required: false): Describe the reason for referral from the dentist, if any.
* **Referral Reason** (`prosthodontist_referral_reason`, `text`, required: false): Provide the reason why the patient is referred to the prosthodontist for further care or treatment.
* **Referral Date** (`prosthodontist_referral_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the referral was made in MM/DD format.
* **Referral Time** (`prosthodontist_referral_time`, `time`, required: false): If applicable, enter the time the referral was made.
* **Referral Notes** (`prosthodontist_referral_notes`, `text`, required: false): Additional information or comments about the patient's condition or treatment needs.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Share any relevant medical history or health concerns that may affect the patient's treatment.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies or sensitivities that the patient has.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required: false): Record any medications the patient is currently taking, including dosages and frequencies.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, required: false): Provide information about the patient's current medical insurance coverage.
* **Patient Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, `text`, required: false): Explain how you know the patient (e.g., family member, friend, etc.).
* **Other** (`patient_relationship_other`, `text`, required: false): If the patient relationship is something other than family member or friend, provide a more detailed explanation.
