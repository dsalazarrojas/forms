# Crisis Management Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crisis Management Specialist Referral Form is used to gather essential information from clients or users seeking crisis management specialist services. This form helps the specialist understand the client's needs and provide the best possible support.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully before starting to complete it.
- Ensure that all required fields (marked as "required") are filled in accurately and completely.
- If a field is marked as "select_one" or "select_multiple", please select only one or multiple options from the provided choices that best suit your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`referral_form_fields_1`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`referral_form_fields_2`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Organization** (`referral_form_fields_3`, text, required): Provide the name of the organization or company you are representing.
- **Address** (`referral_form_fields_4`, text, required): Enter your official address, including street and any necessary additional details.
- **Email** (`referral_form_fields_5`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
- **Phone** (`referral_form_fields_6`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
- **Specialty** (`referral_form_fields_7`, text, required): Select the crisis management specialty you are seeking.
- **Description** (`referral_form_fields_8`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your situation or needs.
- **Date** (`referral_form_fields_9`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date related to your crisis management situation.
- **Time** (`referral_form_fields_10`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time related to your crisis management situation.
- **Category** (`referral_form_fields_11`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your crisis management situation.
- **Subcategory** (`referral_form_fields_12`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional subcategories that describe your crisis management situation.
- **Referral Reason** (`referral_form_fields_13`, text, required): Explain why you are seeking crisis management specialist services.
- **Additional Information** (`referral_form_fields_14`, text, optional): If there's any other information that might be relevant to your crisis management situation, please add it here.
- **Specialist** (`referral_form_fields_15`, text, optional): If you have a preferred specialist, please add their name here.
- **Referral Date** (`referral_form_fields_16`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of your crisis management situation.
- **Follow-up Date** (`referral_form_fields_17`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the desired follow-up date.
- **Specialist Availability** (`referral_form_fields_18`, select_one, optional): Select the specialist's availability.
- **Specialist Contact** (`referral_form_fields_19`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the specialist's contact information.
- **Referral Type** (`referral_form_fields_20`, select_one, optional): Select the method through which you prefer to be contacted.
- **Referral Source** (`referral_form_fields_21`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the source of your referral.
- **Specialist Response** (`referral_form_fields_22`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information for the specialist's response.
- **Specialist Notes** (`referral_form_fields_23`, text, optional): Add any notes or comments for the specialist.
- **Organization** (`referral_form_fields_24`, text, optional): Provide the name of your organization or company.
- **Review Date** (`referral_form_fields_25`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the review date.

## Tips
- Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the specialist's effective support.
- If you're unsure about any field or option, contact the specialist's team for clarification.
