# Hotel Referral Program Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who want to join the hotel's referral program. Fill out the form to submit your details and get notified about available opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details in sections **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Phone Number** (if required).
2. Enter your **Email** address correctly.
3. Fill in your **Street Address** (including any apartment or suite number if applicable).
4. Choose your **City** from the list provided.
5. Choose your **State** from the list provided.
6. Fill in your **Zip Code**.
7. Confirm that you want to submit this form by selecting **Yes** in **Are you sure you want to submit this form?**
8. Confirm that you have read and agree to the **Terms of Service** by selecting **I agree**.
9. Confirm that you give consent to share your data with the hotel by selecting **I agree**.
10. Select the correct **Date of Submission**, **Time of Submission** (24h), and **Time of Submission**.
11. Provide any relevant **Referral Code** (if provided).
12. Fill in the **Hotel Name**.
13. Provide the **Contact Number**.
14. Fill in the **Website**.
15. Enter your **Email for Hotel**.
16. Fill in the **Social Media**.
17. Leave any additional **Notes** if necessary.
18. Enter any **Comments** if necessary.
19. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name, such as John.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, such as Doe.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., example@hotel.com).
* **Phone Number** (if required): Enter your phone number, such as 123-456-7890.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address, including apartment or suite number if applicable.
* **City**: Choose your city from the list provided.
* **State**: Choose your state from the list provided.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code, such as 12345.
* **Are you sure you want to submit this form?**: Confirm that you want to submit this form by selecting **Yes**.
* **Terms of Service**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service by selecting **I agree**.
* **I give consent to share my data with the hotel**: Confirm that you give consent to share your data with the hotel by selecting **I agree**.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **Time of Submission**: Enter the time you are submitting this form.
* **Time of Submission (24h)**: Enter the time you are submitting this form in 24-hour format (e.g., 13:00).
* **Referral Code**: If provided, enter any referral code you may have.
* **Hotel Name**: Enter the name of the hotel.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number for the hotel.
* **Website**: Enter the hotel's website.
* **Email for Hotel**: Enter the email address of the hotel.
* **Social Media**: Enter any social media handle for the hotel.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional notes for the hotel.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments for the hotel.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form.
