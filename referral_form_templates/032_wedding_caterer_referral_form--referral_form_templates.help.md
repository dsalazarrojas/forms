**Wedding Caterer Referral Form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The Wedding Caterer Referral Form is designed to collect information about a wedding event. It will be used to create a referral for a wedding caterer. Fill out this form to provide detailed information about the wedding, including the date, time, venue, and contact details of the planner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the email address of the wedding planner in the Planner Email field.
3. Enter the phone number of the wedding planner in the Planner Phone field.
4. Provide the venue name and location in the Venue Name field.
5. Select the type of event from the Event Type field (Wedding Reception, Wedding Banquet, Wedding Brunch, or Wedding Ceremony).
6. Enter the number of guests attending the wedding in the Number of Guests field.
7. Enter the number of tables needed in the Number of Tables field.
8. Enter the number of servers needed in the Number of Servers field.
9. Enter any additional notes about the wedding in the Notes field.
10. Enter the name of the wedding planner in the Planner Name field.
11. Enter any additional notes about the wedding planner in the Planner Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number.
* **Wedding Date** (`Wedding Date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the wedding.
* **Wedding Time** (`Wedding Time`, time, **required**): Enter the time of the wedding.
* **Venue Name** (`Venue Name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the venue where the wedding will take place.
* **Caterer Name** (`Caterer Name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the caterer you would like to refer.
* **Event Type** (`Event Type`, select_multiple, **required**): Select the type of event (Wedding Reception, Wedding Banquet, Wedding Brunch, or Wedding Ceremony).
* **Number of Guests** (`Number of Guests`, number, **required**): Enter the number of guests attending the wedding.
* **Number of Tables** (`Number of Tables`, number, **required**): Enter the number of tables needed for the wedding.
* **Number of Servers** (`Number of Servers`, number, **required**): Enter the number of servers needed for the wedding.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, **optional**): Enter any additional notes about the wedding.
* **Planner Name** (`Planner Name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the wedding planner.
* **Planner Email** (`Planner Email`, email, **required**): Enter the email address of the wedding planner.
* **Planner Phone** (`Planner Phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of the wedding planner.
* **Planner Notes** (`Planner Notes`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional notes about the wedding planner.

**Tips**

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
* Use the correct format for email addresses and phone numbers (e.g., `john.doe@example.com` and `123-456-7890` respectively).
* If you're unsure about any field, ask a wedding planner or caterer for guidance.
* Double-check your work before submitting the form.
