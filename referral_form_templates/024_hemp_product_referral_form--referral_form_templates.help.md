# hemp_product_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The hemp_product_referral_form is a tool used to manage and track referrals for hemp products. This form helps gather information from contact persons who refer products to potential customers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the contact person's information.
2. Select the type of product being referred.
3. Add any additional relevant notes or information about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person** (1, text, required/optional):
Contact Person is the individual who referred the product.
* **Company** (2, text, required/optional):
Enter the name of the company the contact person represents.
* **Job Title** (3, text, required/optional):
Enter the contact person's job title.
* **Product Name** (4, text, required/optional):
Enter the name of the hemp product being referred.
* **Product Type** (5, select_one, required/optional):
Select the type of hemp product being referred, such as Hemp Products, CBD Products, Hemp Oils, Hemp Seeds, or Other.
* **Brand** (6, text, required/optional):
Enter the brand name of the hemp product being referred.
* **Product Image** (7, text, required/optional):
Enter the URL of a product image or a description of the product image.
* **Referred by** (8, text, required/optional):
Enter the name of the person who referred the contact person.
* **Referred On** (9, date, required/optional):
Enter the date when the referral was made.
* **Referred At** (10, time, required/optional):
Enter the time when the referral was made.
* **Notes** (11, note, required/optional):
Enter any additional notes or information about the referral.

## Tips

* Use the form to track and manage referrals in a structured and organized manner.
* Ensure the form is filled out completely and accurately for optimal results.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out.
