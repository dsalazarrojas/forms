# School Based Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Based Therapy Referral Form is designed to collect information for school-based therapy referrals. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary information about the student's referral status, school year, and other relevant factors.
2. Select the correct category and referral type from the options provided.
3. Enter the school name, contact name, contact email, and contact phone number.
4. Provide the student's grade level and birthdate.
5. Select the relevant services required for the student.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Category
* Category: This field allows you to select the category of the referral (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### Referral Type
* Referral Type: This field allows you to select the type of referral (e.g., "Option 1" or "Option 2").
### School Name
* School Name: Enter the name of the school the student is currently attending or has attended.
### Contact Name
* Contact Name: Enter the name of the contact person or representative.
### Contact Email
* Contact Email: Enter the email address of the contact person or representative.
### Contact Phone
* Contact Phone: Enter the phone number of the contact person or representative.
### Student Grade
* Student Grade: Select the grade level of the student (e.g., Pre-Kindergarten, Kindergarten, 1st, 2nd, etc.).
### Student Name
* Student Name: Enter the name of the student being referred.
### Student Birthdate
* Student Birthdate: Enter the birthdate of the student.
### School Year
* School Year: Select the school year the student is currently in (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### Referral Date
* Referral Date: Enter the date of the referral.
### Student Grade Level
* Student Grade Level: Select the grade level of the student (e.g., Pre-Kindergarten, Kindergarten, 1st, 2nd, etc.).
### Teacher Name
* Teacher Name: Enter the name of the teacher or representative who is making the referral.
### Referral Status
* Referral Status: Select the status of the referral (e.g., "Open", "Closed", "Pending").
### Referral Type Other
* Referral Type Other: Enter any additional information about the referral type.
### English Learner
* English Learner: Select whether the student is an English learner (e.g., "False" or "True").
### ESl Services
* ESl Services: Select whether the student requires ESL services (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### Academic Services
* Academic Services: Select whether the student requires academic services (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### English Services
* English Services: Select whether the student requires English services (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### Social Services
* Social Services: Select whether the student requires social services (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### Behavior Services
* Behavior Services: Select whether the student requires behavior services (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### Disability Services
* Disability Services: Select whether the student requires disability services (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
### Other Services
* Other Services: Enter any additional services required for the student.
### Notes
* Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes about the referral.
