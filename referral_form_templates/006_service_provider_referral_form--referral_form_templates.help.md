The Service Provider Referral Form is used by administrators to collect information from clients about potential new service providers that may be suitable for their needs. This form helps to gather data about the client's current service provider status, their interest in new service providers, and additional details about the referral. 

Before filling out the form, ensure that you are not already working with the new service provider being referred and that the referral is not already in progress.

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

# Service Provider Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients about potential new service providers.

## How To Complete This Form
### Please fill out the form with the following information

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **service_provider_referral_form_page**: This is the start of the form where you will be able to enter other fields that are relevant to the referral.
* **client_information**: Enter any relevant information about the client, such as their current situation or needs that may be relevant to the new service provider.
* **service_provider**: Indicate if the client is interested in the current service provider or not.
* **service_provider_id**: If you are already working with the new service provider or if this is a referral to someone you already have a relationship with, enter their ID here.
* **referral_date**: Enter the date you plan to refer the client to the new service provider, or if you have already done so.
* **referral_time**: Enter the time you plan to refer the client to the new service provider, or if you have already done so.
* **referral_reason**: Enter a brief note about why you are referring the client to the new service provider.
* **note**: Leave a note for yourself or other administrators to reference in the future.
