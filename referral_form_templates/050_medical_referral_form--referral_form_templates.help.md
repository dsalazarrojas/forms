# Medical Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Referral Form is used to gather information about a patient's medical condition, referral details, and contact information for the doctor or specialist being referred to. This form helps ensure accurate and efficient referral processes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your name and email address, which are required fields.
- Enter the patient's name, medical record number (if available), and referral reason.
- If the patient is a minor (under 18), you may need to provide additional information such as age and medical conditions.
- List any allergies or medical history the patient may have.
- Provide the doctor's name, phone number, and email address.
- Enter the specialist's name, email address, phone number, and facility name.
- Select the referral date and time.
- Write a brief referral note to provide context.
- Finally, confirm the specialist's details to ensure accurate communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name to identify yourself as the referring party.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication with the specialist.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name for accurate identification.
* **Medical Record Number** (`medical_record_number`, text, required): If available, enter the patient's medical record number for easier access.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Describe the reason for the referral.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age, if applicable.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any relevant medical conditions for the specialist's attention.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies or sensitivities the patient may have.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide any relevant medical history for the specialist's review.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, required): Enter the doctor's name who is referring the patient.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, required): Enter the doctor's phone number for easy contact.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, required): Enter the doctor's email address for communication.
* **Doctor Specialty** (`doctor_specialty`, text, required): Enter the doctor's specialty for accurate referral.
* **Doctor Facility** (`doctor_facility`, text, required): Enter the doctor's facility name for easy identification.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the referral date for timeline tracking.
* **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, required): Enter the referral time for scheduling coordination.
* **Referral Note** (`referral_note`, text, required): Write a brief note to provide context for the referral.
* **Specialist Name** (`specialist_name`, text, required): Enter the specialist's name for accurate identification.
* **Specialist Email** (`specialist_email`, email, required): Enter the specialist's email address for communication.
* **Specialist Phone** (`specialist_phone`, text, required): Enter the specialist's phone number for easy contact.
* **Specialist Facility** (`specialist_facility`, text, required): Enter the specialist's facility name for easy identification.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure that you have the patient's consent to proceed with the referral.
- Be as detailed as possible when providing information to ensure the specialist has all necessary context.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or healthcare team for guidance.
