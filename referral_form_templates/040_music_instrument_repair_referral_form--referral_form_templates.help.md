# Music Instrument Repair Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Instrument Repair Referral Form is used to gather information about a customer's musical instrument that requires repair or maintenance. This form helps you to collect relevant details about the instrument, the customer, and the repair or service needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of music instrument from the options provided.
2. Choose the condition of the instrument from the options.
3. Enter the customer's name.
4. Enter the customer's phone number.
5. Enter the customer's email address.
6. Describe the instrument and the issue.
7. Select the services required (setup, maintenance, or repairs).
8. Briefly describe the service needed.
9. Choose the referral source (music shop, instrument tech, instructor, or other).
10. Enter the date and time of the service appointment.
11. Add any additional comments or notes.
12. Enter any other service provider information (if applicable).
13. If the referral source is not listed, provide additional details.
