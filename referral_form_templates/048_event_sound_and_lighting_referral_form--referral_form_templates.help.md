# Event Sound And Lighting Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect referral information for events that require sound and lighting services. It helps event organizers and planners gather all necessary details to facilitate smooth referrals and communication with potential vendors.

## How To Complete This Form

To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide detailed information about the event, including its title, date, and location.
2. Specify the sound and lighting requirements for the event, including the number of speakers, microphones, and other equipment needed.
3. Enter referral details, such as the name and contact information of the referral source.
4. Add any additional information about the event that may be relevant to the referral process.
5. Provide payment details, including the estimated budget and payment terms.
6. Specify the follow-up details, such as the expected response time and contact information for follow-up.
7. Select the event status from the available options (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled).
8. Enter any comments or additional information about the event.
9. Provide any suggestions for the event, such as equipment or service recommendations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the event, including its title, date, and location.
* **Sound Details** (`sound_details`, `text`, required): Specify the sound requirements for the event, including the number of speakers, microphones, and other equipment needed.
* **Lighting Details** (`lighting_details`, `text`, required): Enter the lighting requirements for the event, including the type of lighting fixtures and the number of lights needed.
* **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the referral source.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required): Add any additional information about the event that may be relevant to the referral process.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Enter the estimated budget and payment terms for the event.
* **Follow-up Details** (`follow_up_details`, `text`, required): Specify the expected response time and contact information for follow-up.
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the event status from the available options (Confirmed, Pending, Cancelled).
* **Event Comments** (`event_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any comments or additional information about the event.
* **Event Suggestions** (`event_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Provide any suggestions for the event, such as equipment or service recommendations.
* **Follow-up Suggestions** (`follow_up_suggestions`, `text`, optional): Enter any follow-up suggestions or recommendations for the event.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Provide clear and concise information to help the referral process run smoothly.
* Use the follow-up details to ensure that you are contacted in a timely manner.
* Review the event status regularly to stay updated on the referral process.
* Use the event comments and suggestions sections to share any additional information or recommendations.
