# Business Loan Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Loan Referral Form is a tool used to gather information about businesses seeking financing through referrals. This form helps us understand the business's needs and assess their eligibility for financing.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the referrer's information: 
	* Enter your full name in the "Referrer Name" field.
	* Provide your email address in the "Referrer Email" field.
	* Enter your contact phone number in the "Referrer Phone" field.
	* (Optional) Enter your company name in the "Your Company" field if you represent a company.
2. Enter business information:
	* Enter the name of the business being referred in the "Business Name" field.
	* Provide the primary contact person's name in the "Business Contact Name" field.
	* Enter the contact person's email address in the "Business Contact Email" field.
	* Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Business Contact Phone" field.
	* Enter the business's full address in the "Business Address" field.
3. Select the business type:
	* Choose the type of business from the list in the "Business Type" field.
4. Provide business details:
	* Enter the number of years the business has been in operation in the "Years in Business" field.
	* Choose the estimated annual revenue from the list in the "Estimated Annual Revenue" field.
	* Choose the approximate financing need from the list in the "Estimated Loan Amount Needed" field.
	* Select the purpose of the loan from the list in the "Purpose of Loan" field.
	* Choose the urgency level for the loan from the list in the "Urgency Level" field.
	* Select the known credit quality of the business from the list in the "Known Credit Quality" field.
	* Select your relationship to the business from the list in the "Your Relationship to Business" field.
	* Select how you became aware of our lending services from the list in the "How Did You Hear About Our Lending Services" field.
5. Grant permission to share contact information:
	* Select "Yes - share all information" if you grant permission to share your contact information with the business.
	* Select "Yes - share name only" if you grant permission to share your name but not other contact information.
	* Select "No - keep anonymous" if you wish to keep your contact information private.
6. Provide any additional information:
	* Enter any other relevant details about this referral in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name**: Your full name.
* **Referrer Email**: Your email address.
* **Referrer Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Your Company**: (Optional) Enter the name of your company if you represent one.
* **Referrer Name**: Your full name.
* **Referrer Email**: Your email address.
* **Referrer Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Business Name**: The name of the business being referred.
* **Business Contact Name**: The primary contact person's name at the business.
* **Business Contact Email**: The contact person's email address.
* **Business Contact Phone**: The contact person's phone number.
* **Business Address**: The full business address.
* **Business Type**: The type of business (e.g., Retail, Manufacturing, etc.).
* **Years in Business**: The number of years the business has been in operation.
* **Estimated Annual Revenue**: The approximate annual revenue of the business.
* **Estimated Loan Amount Needed**: The approximate financing need of the business.
* **Purpose of Loan**: The reason for the loan (e.g., working capital, equipment purchase, etc.).
* **Urgency Level**: The level of urgency for the loan (e.g., immediate, soon, etc.).
* **Known Credit Quality**: The credit quality of the business (e.g., excellent, good, etc.).
* **Your Relationship to Business**: Your relationship to the business (e.g., owner, employee, etc.).
* **How Did You Hear About Our Lending Services**: The source of your awareness of our lending services (e.g., website, social media, etc.).
* **Permission to Share Contact Information**: Grant permission to share your contact information with the business.
* **Additional Information**: Any other relevant details about this referral.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are referring a business with which you have a legitimate relationship.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your internal representative for guidance.
* Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that we can follow up with the business effectively.
