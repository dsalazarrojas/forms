# Office Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Office Referral Form is designed to provide a comprehensive overview of a patient's information for referrals and office notes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the patient's name.
2. Provide the name of the referring service or organization.
3. Enter the date the referral was made.
4. Describe the reason for the referral.
5. List the patient's condition.
6. Specify the name of the referring provider.
7. Indicate the referral date and time (if applicable).
8. Add any office notes about the patient.
9. Select the status of the referral (Active, In Progress, or Closed).
10. Add any follow-up information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the patient.
* **Referring Service** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the service or organization making the referral.
* **Referring Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the referral was made.
* **Reason for Referral** (Text, Optional): Describe the reason for the referral.
* **Patient Condition** (Text, Optional): List the patient's medical condition.
* **Referring Provider** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the referring provider.
* **Referral Date Required** (Time, Optional): Enter the date and time when the referral is required (if applicable).
* **Office Notes** (Text, Optional): Add any notes about the patient.
* **Referral Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the status of the referral (Active, In Progress, or Closed).
* **Follow Up** (Text, Optional): Add any follow-up information.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide all necessary information about the patient.
- Ensure the form is not filled out multiple times for the same patient.
- Use the "Referral Status" field to track the progress of the referral.
- Use the "Office Notes" field to add any additional comments or notes about the patient.
- Update the "Referral Date Required" field if the referral date changes.
- Use the "Follow Up" field to schedule follow-up appointments or actions.
