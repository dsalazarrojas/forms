# monat_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referral information from clients, including names, contact details, and other relevant details. It's used to keep track of referrals and facilitate communication between you and your referral partners.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately, providing the required information for each field.
- Ensure you submit the form only once for each referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the person being referred.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the person being referred.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person being referred.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person being referred.
- **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the referral.
- **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the referral.
- **Referral Location** (`referral_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the referral was made.
- **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the partner or organization making the referral.
- **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the partner or organization making the referral.
- **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the partner or organization making the referral.

Note: While most fields are not required, it's essential to fill out as much information as possible to ensure accurate and reliable referral data.
