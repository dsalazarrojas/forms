# Patient Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Patient Referral Form is used to collect and document information about patients being referred to other healthcare professionals or facilities. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is captured and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the referral name in the first field.
2. Enter the patient's name in the second field.
3. Select the name of the referring doctor from the options provided in the third field.
4. Enter the patient's address in the fourth field.
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the fifth field.
6. Enter the patient's email address in the sixth field.
7. Select a date for the referral in the seventh field.
8. Sign and date the form as the referring doctor in the eighth field.
9. Sign the form as the patient in the ninth field.
10. Add any additional comments or notes in the tenth field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral name** (`referral_name`, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the referral, which should be a unique identifier for the referral.
* **Patient name** (`patient_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the patient being referred.
* **Referring Doctor** (`referring_doctor`, select one, optional)
	+ Select the name of the healthcare professional referring the patient.
* **Patient address** (`patient_address`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the patient's address, which may include street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Patient phone number** (`patient_phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient email** (`patient_email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter the patient's email address.
* **Referral date** (`referral_date`, date, optional)
	+ Select a date for the referral.
* **Referring doctor's signature** (`referring_doctor_signature`, text, optional)
	+ Sign and date the form as the referring doctor.
* **Patient's signature** (`patient_signature`, text, optional)
	+ Sign the form as the patient.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the referral, if necessary.
