# Advanced Course Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Advanced Course Referral Form is designed to collect information about a candidate referred to an advanced course. This form helps course administrators make informed decisions about candidate suitability for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the referrer's information, including their full name, position, organization, email, and phone number.
2. Enter the candidate's full name, email, phone number, education background, and course information.
3. Provide reasons for the referral, including academic performance, professional experience, and specific strengths.
4. Rate your recommendation for the candidate.
5. Add any additional comments about the candidate or referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Position** (Text, Required): Enter the job title or role of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Organization** (Text, Required): Enter the institution or company where the referrer works.
* **Referrer Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address for follow-up communication with the referrer.
* **Referrer Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number for contact with the referrer.
* **Candidate Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter the full name of the person being referred.
* **Candidate Email** (Email, Required): Enter the email address of the candidate.
* **Candidate Phone** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the candidate.
* **Candidate Education Background** (Text, Required): Describe the candidate's educational qualifications.
* **Course Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the advanced course for which the candidate is being referred.
* **Course Code** (Text, Required): Enter the official code or identifier for the course.
* **Academic Performance Reason** (Text, Required): Provide the reason why the candidate is recommended based on academic performance.
* **Professional Experience Reason** (Text, Required): Explain how the candidate's professional experience supports this referral.
* **Specific Strengths** (Text, Required): List the candidate's particular strengths relevant to this course.
* **Recommendation Rating** (Select One, Required): Select how strongly you recommend this candidate for the course.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any other information about the candidate or referral.

**Tips**

* Ensure that you accurately and thoroughly complete all required fields.
* Be as detailed as possible in your responses to provide valuable insights to the course administrators.
* If you need help with any part of the form, do not hesitate to contact your course administrator or support team.
