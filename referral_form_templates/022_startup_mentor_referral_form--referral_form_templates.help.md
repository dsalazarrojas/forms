# Startup Mentor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Mentor Referral Form is used to create and manage forms for various startup-related activities. This form is used by administrators to create new forms, assign them to tools, and track changes made to the forms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a category that best describes the form's purpose. You can choose from "Referral Form Templates" or "Other" if your form is not related to a startup activity.
2. Enter a short description of the form in the "Description" field. This should give an overview of what the form is used for.
3. Assign the form to a tool by entering the tool's name in the "Assigned Tool" field.
4. Specify the form's type by selecting the corresponding options in the "Form Type" field.
5. Fill in any additional fields as necessary and relevant to your form.
