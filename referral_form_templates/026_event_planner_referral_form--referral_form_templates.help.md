# event_planner_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event planner referral form is used to collect information from potential attendees who are being referred to an event by someone else.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and the name of the person you are referring to.
2. Enter the email address of the person you are referring to.
3. Enter the phone number of the person you are referring to.
4. Enter the address of the person you are referring to.
5. Enter a message for the person you are referring to, if you have one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Referral Name
* **Referral Name**: Enter the name of the person you are referring to.

### Referral Email
* **Referral Email**: Enter the email address of the person you are referring to.

### Referral Phone
* **Referral Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you are referring to.

### Referral Address
* **Referral Address**: Enter the address of the person you are referring to.

### Referral Message
* **Referral Message**: Enter a message for the person you are referring to, if you have one.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (though some are not required, it's still a good idea to provide as much information as possible).
* Double-check your email address to ensure it's correct.
* If you have any further questions or need help, please contact us.
