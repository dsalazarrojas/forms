# Social Services Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Services Referral Form is designed to collect information about a client's social services needs and preferences. This form will be used to gather data for referral and support purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Details field with any relevant information about the client.
2. Enter the client's name in the Client Name field.
3. Provide the client's address in the Address field.
4. Enter the client's phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. Enter the client's email address in the Email field.
6. Select the social services type that best describes the client's needs from the Social Services Type field.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the social worker assisting the client in the Social Worker field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe the client's social services needs and concerns.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide the client's address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
* **Social Services Type** (`service_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ **Homelessness**: The client is experiencing homelessness or housing instability.
	+ **Food Insecurity**: The client is experiencing food insecurity or lack of access to nutritious food.
	+ **Homelessness Prevention**: The client is at risk of experiencing homelessness.
	+ **Homelessness Intervention**: The client is experiencing homelessness and needs intervention services.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if the client's needs do not fit into one of the above categories.
* **Social Worker** (`social_worker_name`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the social worker assisting the client.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible to provide the best possible support for the client.
- If you are unsure about any field or have questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the social worker or support team.
- Remember to save and share the completed form with the client and relevant stakeholders to ensure that everyone is on the same page.
