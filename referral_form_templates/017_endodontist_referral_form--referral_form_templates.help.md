# Endodontist Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Endodontist Referral Form is a template used to capture patient and dentist information for endodontic treatment referrals. This form is intended for healthcare professionals to gather relevant details from patients and their referring dentists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Ensure you are using the correct version of this form, as it is a referral template designed for endodontic treatments.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the "Patient Last Name" field.
3. Enter the patient's first name in the "Patient First Name" field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient Date of Birth" field (in the format dd/mm/yyyy).
5. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone Number" field.
6. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
7. Enter the dentist's first name in the "Dentist First Name" field.
8. Enter the dentist's last name in the "Dentist Last Name" field.
9. Enter the dentist's phone number in the "Dentist Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Last Name** (`patient_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's surname.
* **Patient First Name** (`patient_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's given name.
* **Patient Date of Birth** (`patient_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Patient Phone Number** (`patient_phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required: false): Enter the patient's contact address.
* **Dentist First Name** (`dentist_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the referring dentist's given name.
* **Dentist Last Name** (`dentist_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the referring dentist's surname.
* **Dentist Phone Number** (`dentist_phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter the referring dentist's contact phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the relevant parties.
* Double-check the form before submitting to ensure it is complete and correct.
