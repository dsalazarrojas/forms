# Pediatric Dental Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pediatric Dental Referral Form is a tool used to gather essential information about a patient's medical history and dental needs. This form helps the healthcare team to make informed decisions about referrals and ensure the best possible care for the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the patient's information, including any relevant medical conditions, medical history, and current medications.
2. Select the dental clinics that you think the patient would be best suited for.
3. Input the date of the patient's last visit to the dental clinic.
4. Provide any additional information that you think would be helpful for the referral process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional): Enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant demographic information.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any applicable medical conditions that the patient has, such as Asthma, Cancer, or Diabetes.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies, medical conditions, or previous surgeries.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Referrals** (`referrals`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant information about previous referrals or medical evaluations.
* **Dental Info** (`dental_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant dental information, such as the patient's dental history or any previous dental treatments.
* **Dental Clinics** (`dental_clinics`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the type of dental clinics that you think the patient would be best suited for, such as Pediatric Dentist, Adult Dentist, or General Dentist.
* **Date of Last Visit** (`date_of_last_visit`, `date`, optional): Input the date of the patient's last visit to the dental clinic.

## Tips
* Be as accurate and comprehensive as possible when filling out this form to ensure that the patient receives the best possible care.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
