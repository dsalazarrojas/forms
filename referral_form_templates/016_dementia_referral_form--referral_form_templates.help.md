# Dementia Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a patient with suspected dementia or cognitive decline. It is designed to be completed by a healthcare professional, typically a GP or a specialist, who is referring a patient to a specialist for further evaluation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant information from the "Referral Type" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the patient's referral reason in the "Referral Reason" field.
3. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
5. Enter the patient's date of referral in the "Date of Referral" field in YYYY-MM-DD format.
6. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
7. Enter the patient's post code in the "Patient Postal Code" field.
8. Enter the patient's medical history in the "Medical History" field.
9. Enter the patient's family history in the "Family History" field.
10. Enter the patient's social history in the "Social History" field.
11. Select the patient's cognitive status from the options provided.
12. Select the referring doctor from the "Referring Doctor" dropdown menu.
13. Select the specialist who will be receiving the referral from the "Specialist Name" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Referral Type** (`referral_form_data`, select multiple, required/optional): Select the type of referral form to complete. Options include "Dementia Referral Form" and "Other".
- **Patient Referral** (`patient_referral`, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for referring the patient to a specialist.
- **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Date of Referral** (`date_of_referral`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of referral in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
- **Patient Postal Code** (`patient_postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's post code.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's medical history.
- **Family History** (`family_history`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's family history.
- **Social History** (`social_history`, text, required/optional): Enter the patient's social history.
- **Cognitive Status** (`cognitive_status`, text, required/optional): Select the patient's cognitive status.
- **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for referral.
- **Referring Doctor** (`referring_doctor`, select one, required/optional): Select the doctor referring the patient.
- **Specialist Name** (`specialist_name`, select one, required/optional): Select the specialist who will be receiving the referral.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all required fields.
- Select the correct referral type and reason.
- Enter the patient's information accurately.
- Select the correct referring doctor and specialist.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all information is complete and accurate.
