# Copywriter Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copywriter Referral Form is used to collect information from users who are referring a copywriter to our services. This form helps us track the details of the referred copywriter and follow up with them to provide more information about our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the first name of the referred copywriter in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the last name of the referred copywriter in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter the name of the company the referred copywriter is affiliated with in the "Company" field.
4. Enter the email address of the referred copywriter in the "Email" field.
5. Enter the phone number of the referred copywriter in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the referred copywriter.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the referred copywriter.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company the referred copywriter is affiliated with.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the referred copywriter. Make sure to enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the referred copywriter.
