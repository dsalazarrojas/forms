# Graphic Designer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Graphic Designer Referral Form is used to capture information about graphic designers for referral purposes. This form helps to collect details about the designer's name, contact information, job title, industry, job type, referral date, and status. The form is designed to streamline the referral process, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Graphic Designer Referral Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked as such in the form. These fields are:
	* Your Name
	* Email
	* Referral Name
	* Referral Status
	* Referral Job Type
	* Referral Date
2. Provide the necessary information for the referral, such as the designer's name, contact details, job title, industry, and job type.
3. Select the correct referral status from the options provided.
4. Add any additional comments or notes as necessary.
5. Submit the form to complete the referral.
