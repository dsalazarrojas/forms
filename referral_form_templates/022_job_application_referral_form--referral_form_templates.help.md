# Job Application Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referral information about a job posting, allowing users to share contact details and job posting information with a potential referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your referral details in the "Referral Form Input" field.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Enter the job posting information in the "Job Posting" field.
4. If applicable, provide employee details in the "Employee Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Form Input** (`referral_form_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description or details about the referral.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact name, email address, and phone number to reach out to you.
* **Job Posting** (`job_posting`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job posting, including the job title, department, and location.
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): If you're the employee being referred, enter your name, job title, and department to facilitate contact.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information in each field to ensure the referral is processed efficiently.
* You can use this form to initiate a connection or follow-up with a potential referral.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to enter the most essential information possible.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
