# Eco Tourism Local Guide Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to collect information about local guides in the eco-tourism industry. It helps tourists and travelers refer local guides for their trips.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and relevant information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Fields** (Page 1): This is the starting page of the form.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it would be displayed on the guide listing.
* **Name of Tour Operator** (`name_of_tour_operator`): Enter the name of the tour operator or travel agency you are referring.
* **Name of Referral Person** (`name_of_referral_person`): Enter the name of the person being referred.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`): Enter any contact details of the guide, such as phone number or email.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the guide or tour operator.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the phone number of the guide or tour operator.
* **Website** (`website`): Enter the website of the guide or tour operator.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the guide or tour operator.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where the guide or tour operator is located.
* **Region** (`region`): Enter the region where the guide or tour operator is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter the zip code of the guide or tour operator's address.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter the country where the guide or tour operator is located.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`): Enter any social media handle of the guide or tour operator.
* **Guide ID** (`guide_id`): Enter a unique ID for the guide.
* **Guide Type** (`guide_type`): Select the type of guide (e.g., hiking guide, birdwatching guide, etc.).
* **Rating** (`rating`): Enter a rating for the guide (1-5 stars).
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a short description of the guide or tour operator.
* **Image Upload** (`image_upload`): Upload an image for the guide or tour operator.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Agree to the terms and conditions of the guide referral service.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date you referred the guide.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time you referred the guide.
* **Select One** (`select_one`): Select one of the options listed.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`): Select multiple of the options listed.

## Tips
- Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure you have permission from the guide or tour operator to share their contact details and other information.
- This form is for referring local guides in the eco-tourism industry. If you are a guide, please fill in the form with your information. If you are a tourist or traveler, please ask the guide or tour operator to fill in their information.
