## Editorial Referral Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate editorial referrals submitted by users. It is intended to collect information from contributors who want to refer an editor to a particular article or content piece. The form is meant to be completed with basic information about the contributor and the content being referred.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details in the "contributor info" field, which includes your name and contact information.
2. In the "contribution details" field, describe the content that you are referring the editor to. Please provide a brief summary of the article's purpose and relevance.
3. In the "pitch and assets" field, share your personal pitch and any relevant assets (e.g., images, videos, etc.) that support your referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **contributor info (text, required):** This field is where you can enter your personal details, including your name and contact information.
* **contribution details (text, required):** This field is where you can describe the content being referred, including a brief summary of its purpose and relevance.
* **pitch and assets (text, required):** This field is where you can share your personal pitch and any relevant assets that support your referral.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions and rights to refer the content you are submitting.
- Be as concise as possible while providing enough information to help the editor understand the referral.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
