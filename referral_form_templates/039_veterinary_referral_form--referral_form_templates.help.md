# Veterinary Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer patients to specialists for further medical treatment or care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter patient information, including name, owner's name, and address.
2. Provide owner's contact information, including phone and email.
3. Specify the specialist or specialists to be referred to.
4. Enter referral details, including date and time of referral.
5. Choose the preferred contact method (phone, email, or fax).
6. Add any additional comments or notes.
7. Sign and date the form with the owner's and veterinarian's signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information (1)**:
	+ Enter the patient's name, including the name of the owner or caregiver.
* **Owner Information (2)**:
	+ Enter the owner's name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Contact Preferences (3)**:
	+ Choose the preferred contact method (phone, email, or fax).
* **Referral Details (4)**:
	+ Enter the date and time of referral.
* **Specialist Referral (6)**:
	+ Select the specialist or specialists to be referred to. Choose from the options: Specialist 1, Specialist 2, or Specialist 3.
* **Additional Comments (7)**:
	+ Add any additional comments or notes regarding the referral.
* **Owner Signature (10)**:
