# Farm Gift Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Farm Gift Referral Form is a tool used to collect information about farm referrals from farmers and their contacts. It helps to keep track of the referrals and their details for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Ensure you are using the correct format for the fields, for example, the date should be in YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Select all applicable rewards for the referral.
4. Add a description of the referral, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **name** (1): Please fill in your name as the farmer.
- **contact_number** (2): Enter your contact number for further communication.
- **referral_name** (3): Provide the name of the person or organization you are referring.
- **referral_phone** (4): Enter the referral's phone number.
- **number_of_referrals** (5): Enter the number of referrals you have made in the past.
- **rewards** (6): Select all the rewards you believe the referral is eligible for.
- **date** (7): Enter the date of the referral in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **time** (8): Enter the time of the referral in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
- **email** (9): Enter the referral's email address.
- **description** (10): Add a brief description of the referral, if necessary.
- **assigned_to** (11): Enter the name of the person or organization this referral is assigned to.
