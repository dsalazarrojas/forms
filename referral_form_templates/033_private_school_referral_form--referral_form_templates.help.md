# Private School Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who refer new students to private schools. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to provide the school with the necessary details for follow-up activities.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the required fields for the referral information: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Referral Reason.
- Select the relationship to the student from the options provided: Student, Parent, or Family Member.
- Indicate how you know the school through the options: Through a friend or Through a family member (or select Other if it doesn't fit).
- Enter the referral reason for bringing the new student to the school.
- Enter the student's name, school ID, start date, end date, and grade level.
- Select the student's programs of interest (e.g., English Language Arts, Math, Science, Social Studies).
- Enter any additional information about the student.
- Enter a message for the referrer to communicate their goals.
- Enter your name, email, and phone number as the referrer.
- Enter any follow-up notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)**: Enter the first name of the individual referring the student.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter the last name of the individual referring the student.
* **Email (3)**: Enter the email address of the individual referring the student.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter the phone number of the individual referring the student.
* **Referral Reason (5)**: Enter a brief description of why you're bringing this student to the school.
* **Relationship to Student (6)**: Select how you know the student: Student, Parent, or Family Member.
* **How did you know us? (7)**: Indicate how you know the school through the options: Through a friend or Through a family member (or select Other if it doesn't fit).
* **Follow up Question (8)**: Enter any follow-up questions you have for the school.
* **Follow up Note (9)**: Enter any additional follow-up notes or comments.
* **Follow up Next Steps (10)**: Enter any next steps for the school to follow up with you.
* **Date (11)**: Enter the date you're referring the student to the school.
* **Time (12)**: Enter the time you're referring the student to the school.
* **Referrer Name (13)**: Enter your name as the individual referring the student.
* **Referrer Email (14)**: Enter your email address as the individual referring the student.
* **Referrer Phone (15)**: Enter your phone number as the individual referring the student.
* **Referrer Goals (16)**: Enter your goals or expectations for the school to follow up with you.
* **Student Name (17)**: Enter the name of the student you're referring to the school.
* **Student School ID (18)**: Enter the school ID of the student.
* **Student Start Date (19)**: Enter the start date of the student's enrollment.
* **Student End Date (20)**: Enter the end date of the student's enrollment.
* **Student Grade Level (21)**: Enter the grade level of the student.
* **Student Programs (22)**: Select the programs of interest for the student (e.g., English Language Arts, Math, Science, Social Studies).
* **Student Additional Info (23)**: Enter any additional information about the student.
* **Referrer Message (24)**: Enter a message for the school to understand your goals.
* **Referrer Comment (25)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback for the school.
