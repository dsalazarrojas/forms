# Landscape Architect Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients or customers about a project referral for a landscape architect. It captures details about the project, referral source, recommended landscape architect, and follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with accurate information about the project and referral.
2. Choose the referral source and method to indicate how the referral was made.
3. Select the recommended landscape architect from the provided options.
4. Enter the landscape architect's contact information, including license, name, phone, email, and address.
5. Enter the landscape architect's experience and fee details.
6. Schedule a follow-up date and time for further communication.
7. Add any additional comments or follow-up notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project, including location and any notable features.
* **Project Location**: Enter the physical address of the project site.
* **Referral Source**: Select the source of the referral, such as a previous client or a marketing campaign.
* **Referral Method**: Choose how the referral was made, such as phone, email, or in-person.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral, such as a personal recommendation or a project need.
* **Recommended Landscape Architect**: Select the landscape architect's name from the provided options.
* **Follow-up Method**: Choose how you plan to follow-up with the landscape architect, such as phone or email.
* **Follow-up Date**: Schedule a date for the follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Time**: Schedule a time for the follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Note**: Add any notes about the follow-up conversation.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
* **Email**: Enter the landscape architect's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter the landscape architect's phone number.
* **Project Manager**: Enter the name and contact information of the project manager.
* **Project Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of the project contact.
* **Landscape Architect License**: Enter the landscape architect's license number.
* **Landscape Architect Name**: Enter the landscape architect's name.
* **Landscape Architect Phone**: Enter the landscape architect's phone number.
* **Landscape Architect Email**: Enter the landscape architect's email address.

* **Landscape Architect Experience**: Enter the landscape architect's experience level.
* **Landscape Architect Fee**: Enter the landscape architect's fee.
* **Landscape Architect Fee Currency**: Enter the currency of the landscape architect's fee.
* **Landscape Architect Fee Range**: Enter the fee range of the landscape architect.
