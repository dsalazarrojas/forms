# Nonprofit Fundraising Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help non-profit organizations find a suitable fundraising consultant for their needs. Please provide accurate and sufficient information to ensure a good match between the consultant and the non-profit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your non-profit's details, including its fundraising needs and goals.
2. Provide information about your organization's fundraising needs and expectations.
3. Check the consultant's education background and recommendations to find the best fit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nonprofit Fundraising Needs** (`Nonprofit Fundraising Needs`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your non-profit's current fundraising needs and challenges.
* **Consultant Recommendations** (`Consultant Recommendations`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas where you think a fundraising consultant would be most helpful (e.g., "Yes" to social media, "Yes" to grant writing).
* **Nonprofit Fundraising Goals** (`Nonprofit Fundraising Goals`, `text`, required): Describe your non-profit's fundraising goals, including specific targets and timelines.
* **Nonprofit Organization Information** (`Nonprofit Organization Information`, `text`, optional): Provide additional context about your non-profit, such as its mission, size, or location.
* **Consultant Education Background** (`Consultant Education Background`, `text`, optional): Share information about the consultant's educational background and qualifications.
* **Nonprofit Fundraising Needs Details** (`Nonprofit Fundraising Needs Details`, `text`, optional): Provide more details about your non-profit's fundraising needs, such as specific challenges or areas of concern.
* **Consultant Recommendations Details** (`Consultant Recommendations Details`, `text`, optional): Share specific details about the consultant's recommendations, such as their experience or approach to fundraising.
