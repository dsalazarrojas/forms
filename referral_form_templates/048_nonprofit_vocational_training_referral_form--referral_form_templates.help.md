# Nonprofit Vocational Training Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Vocational Training Referral Form is used to refer an individual to a vocational training program. It gathers information about the individual being referred, their interests, and the details of the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the individual's First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter the individual's Email address.
3. Indicate the Program of Interest by selecting an option from the dropdown list.
4. If necessary, enter the individual's Date of Birth.
5. Enter the individual's Phone number (optional).
6. Grant Permission to Share by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown list.
7. Choose the Referral Source by selecting an option from the dropdown list.
8. Check if the individual has Program Staff Member involvement by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown list.
9. Schedule a Follow-up Date (optional).
10. Enter the Submitter's Name and Email address (optional).
11. Enter the Submitter's Phone number (optional).
12. Add any additional Notes about the referral (optional).
13. Set the Form Submitted Date and Time automatically.
14. Select the Program options that apply to the individual being referred.
15. Choose the Training Center options that apply to the individual being referred.
16. Schedule a Referral Date (optional).
17. Check if the individual has a Staff Member assigned to them by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown list.
18. Check the Follow-up Status by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive' from the dropdown list.
19. Set the Form Submission Status by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive' from the dropdown list.
20. Check if the form was submitted by the individual being referred by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown list.
21. Check the Training Status by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive' from the dropdown list.
22. Check if the form was submitted correctly by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown list.
23. Enter the Submitter's IP address (optional).
24. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter the individual's first name.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter the individual's last name.
* **Email (3)**: Enter the individual's Email address.
* **Program of Interest (4)**: Select an option from the dropdown list that corresponds to the type of vocational training program the individual is interested in.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: If you need to know the individual's date of birth for the referral process, enter it here (optional).
* **Phone (6)**: Enter the individual's phone number (optional).
* **Permission to Share (7)**: Indicate whether or not to share the individual's information with the training program (select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Referral Source (8)**: Choose an option from the dropdown list that explains where the individual heard about the vocational training program.
* **Program Staff Member (9)**: Check if the individual has a staff member assigned to them (select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Follow-up Date (10)**: Schedule a follow-up date to check on the individual's progress with the training program (optional).
* **Submitter's Name (11)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form (optional).
* **Submitter's Email (12)**: Enter the Email address of the person submitting the form (optional).
* **Submitter's Phone (13)**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form (optional).
* **Notes (14)**: Add any additional notes about the referral (optional).
* **Form Submitted Date (15)**: Set automatically when the form is submitted.
* **Program (16)**: Select all programs that apply to the individual being referred.
* **Training Center (17)**: Select all training centers that apply to the individual being referred.
* **Referral Date (18)**: Schedule a referral date (optional).
* **Staff Member (19)**: Check if the individual has a staff member assigned to them (select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Follow-up Status (20)**: Check the follow-up status by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive' from the dropdown list.
* **Form Submission Status (21)**: Check the form submission status by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive' from the dropdown list.
* **Submitted By (22)**: Check if the individual submitted the form themselves (select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Training Status (23)**: Check the training status by selecting 'Active' or 'Inactive' from the dropdown list.
* **Form Submitted (24)**: Check if the form was submitted correctly (select 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Submitter's IP (25)**: Enter the IP address of the person submitting the form (optional).
