# refer_a_friend_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the user about their friend or acquaintance. The goal is to gather contact details, job title, address, phone number, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the fields with the correct information about your friend or acquaintance.
2. Provide a brief message if desired.
3. Select the appropriate reward option.
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`User Input`, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the user to input any additional information about their friend or acquaintance.
* **Friend Name** (`Friend Name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the name of the friend or acquaintance you are referring.
* **Friend Email** (`Friend Email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter the email address of the friend or acquaintance.
* **Referrer Name** (`Referrer Name`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your name.
* **Referrer Email** (`Referrer Email`, email, required: false)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Friend Phone** (`Friend Phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the phone number of the friend or acquaintance.
* **Referral Link** (`Referral Link`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any relevant referral links.
* **Referrer Link** (`Referrer Link`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter any relevant link about yourself.
* **Reward Option** (`Reward Option`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select 'Yes' if you would like to offer a reward for the referral.
* **Reward** (`Reward`, text, required: false)
	+ If you selected 'Yes' for Reward Option, enter the reward details here.
* **Prophets** (`Prophets`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select any relevant prophets or influencers related to the referral.
* **Message** (`Message`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter a brief message about your friend or acquaintance.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required: false)
	+ Click this button to submit the form.
* **Referrer Phone** (`Referrer Phone`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Friend Company** (`Friend Company`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the company name of the friend or acquaintance.
* **Friend Country** (`Friend Country`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the country of the friend or acquaintance.
* **Friend State** (`Friend State`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the state of the friend or acquaintance.
* **Friend Zip** (`Friend Zip`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the zip code of the friend or acquaintance.
* **Friend Address** (`Friend Address`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter the address of the friend or acquaintance.
* **Referrer Company** (`Referrer Company`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your company name.
* **Referrer Country** (`Referrer Country`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your country of work.
* **Referrer State** (`Referrer State`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your state of work.
* **Referrer Zip** (`Referrer Zip`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your zip code.
* **Referrer Address** (`Referrer Address`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your address.
* **Referrer Title** (`Referrer Title`, text, required: false)
	+ Enter your job title.
