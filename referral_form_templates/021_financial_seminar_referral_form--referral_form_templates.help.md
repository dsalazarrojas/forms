# Financial Seminar Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Seminar Referral Form is a tool used to collect referrals from individuals who have attended a financial seminar. This form helps track and follow up with attendees to ensure they have the necessary details to provide effective referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2.  Enter your email in the "Email" field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4.  Enter the name of the seminar you are referring in the "Seminar Name" field.
5.  Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
6.  Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
7.  Enter the location of the event in the "Location" field.
8.  Enter any additional details about the seminar in the "Seminar Details" field.
9.  Enter any referral details in the "Referral Details" field.
10.  Enter the name of the person making the referral in the "Referral Name" field.
11.  Enter the relationship between you and the person making the referral in the "Referral Relationship" field.
12.  Enter any additional notes about the referral in the "Referral Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for follow-up questions or discussions.
* **Seminar Name (Seminar Name)**: Enter the name of the seminar you are referring.
* **Event Date (Event Date)**: Enter the date of the event in the format YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS+TZ.
* **Event Time (Event Time)**: Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM:SS.
* **Location (Location)**: Enter the location of the event, such as a venue or city.
* **Seminar Details (Seminar Details)**: Enter any additional details about the seminar that might be helpful for follow-up.
* **Referral Details (Referral Details)**: Enter any details that you would like to share about the person being referred.
* **Referral Name (Referral Name)**: Enter the name of the person being referred.
* **Referrer (Referrer)**: Enter the name of the person making the referral (you).
* **Referral Relationship (Referral Relationship)**: Describe the relationship between you and the person making the referral.
* **Referral Note (Referral Note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the referral.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have not previously submitted a referral for the same seminar.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing referral details, so the person being referred can provide the best experience for the individual.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about the form, please contact the event organizers.
