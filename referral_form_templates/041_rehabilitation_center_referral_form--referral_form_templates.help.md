# Rehabilitation Center Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehabilitation Center Referral Form is used to collect information from potential patients referred to the center. This form is required to ensure that the patient's needs are properly assessed and that they can be referred to the most suitable program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please fill out all the required fields. The asterisk symbol (*) indicates a required field.
2.  Enter your information in the provided fields.
3.  If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Source** (Self, Family member, Doctor, Insurance company): This field indicates the source of the referral.
* **Referral Date** (Date): The date the referral was made.
* **Referral Time** (Time): The time the referral was made.
* **Phone** (Text): The phone number of the contact person.
* **Email** (Email): The email address of the contact person.
* **Fax** (Text): The fax number of the contact person.
* **Patient Name** (Text): The name of the patient.
* **Date Of Birth** (Date): The date of birth of the patient.
* **Address** (Text): The address of the patient.
* **Gender** (Male, Female, Other): The gender of the patient.
* **Race** (White, Black, Asian, Other): The race of the patient.
* **Language** (English, Spanish, Other): The language spoken by the patient.
* **Ethnicity** (Asian or Pacific Islander, White, Black, Other): The ethnicity of the patient.
* **Primary Care Provider** (Doctor, Nurse Practitioner, Clinic): The primary care provider for the patient.
* **Primary Care Facility** (Hospital, Clinic, Other): The facility where the patient receives their primary care.
* **Phone** (Text): The phone number of the patient's primary care provider.
* **Medical Condition** (Text): Any medical conditions that need to be referred.
* **Medication Allergies** (Text): Any medication allergies the patient has.
* **Note** (Text): Additional comments or notes about the patient.
* **User Input** (Text): Any additional information that can be provided about the patient.
* **Contact Info** (Text): The contact information of the person making the referral.
* **Medical History** (Text): The medical history of the patient.
* **Insurance** (Text): The insurance information of the patient.
* **Doctor Notes** (Text): Additional notes for the doctor.
* **Patient Info** (Text): The patient's information.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the rehabilitation center.
