# Retail Repair Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Repair Service Referral Form is a tool for retail store staff to quickly and accurately gather information from customers who require repair services. This form helps to streamline the repair service referral process, ensuring that all necessary details are collected and communicated to the relevant team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are filling out this form for a specific customer.
2. Fill out the required fields (Customer Name, Phone Number, Email, Repair Date, Service Requested, and Service Type).
3. Leave the optional fields (Contact Information and Customer Reference) blank if not applicable.
4. In the Service Requested field, select all applicable services required by the customer.
5. In the Service Type field, select the type of service required.
6. Add any relevant notes or comments about the customer or the service required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Please enter the customer's name as it appears on their identification or as they prefer to be addressed.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): If you have any additional contact information for the customer, please enter it here.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, required): Please enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter the customer's email address.
* **Repair Date** (`repair_date`, date, required): Please enter the date when the customer would like to repair the item.
* **Service Requested** (`service_requested`, select_multiple, required): Please select all applicable services required by the customer (Repair, Maintenance, Adjustment).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required): Please select the type of service required (Repair, Maintenance, Adjustment).
* **Customer Reference** (`customer_reference`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or reference for the customer, please enter it here.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Please add any relevant notes or comments about the customer or the service required.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that the customer's information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you're unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact your supervisor or colleague.
