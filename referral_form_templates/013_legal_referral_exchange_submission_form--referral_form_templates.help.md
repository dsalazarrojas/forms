# Legal Referral Exchange Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Referral Exchange Submission Form is designed to collect information for referrals in the legal department. This form helps gather essential details to ensure that referrals are handled efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:
1. Enter the **Case Number** in the designated field.
2. Choose the **Referral Type** by selecting from the provided options.
3. Select the **Referral Source** from the options provided.
4. Enter the **Client Name** in the text field.
5. Select the **Case Status** by choosing from the options.
6. Enter the **Follow-Up Date** in the date format (e.g., mm/dd/yyyy).
7. Enter the **Follow-Up Time** in the time format (e.g., 03:30 PM).
8. Select the **Follow-Up Method** by choosing from the options provided.
9. Enter the **Contact Name** in the text field.
10. Enter the **Contact Number** in the text field.
11. Enter **Follow-Up Notes** in the text field.
12. Enter the **Phone Number** in the text field.
13. Enter the **Email** in the text field.
14. Enter **Notes** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Number** (1, required): A unique identifier for the case.
* **Referral Type** (2, required): Select whether the referral is yes or no.
* **Referral Source** (3, required): Choose from yes or no.
* **Client Name** (4, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Case Status** (5, required): Select active or inactive.
* **Follow-Up Date** (6, required): Enter the follow-up date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Follow-Up Time** (7, required): Enter the follow-up time in the format 03:30 PM.
* **Follow-Up Method** (8, required): Select yes or no.
* **Contact Name** (9, required): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Number** (10, required): Enter the contact's phone number.
* **Follow-Up Notes** (11, required): Enter any notes related to the follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (12, required): Enter the phone number.
* **Email** (13, required): Enter the contact's email address.
* **Notes** (14, required): Enter any additional notes related to the case.
