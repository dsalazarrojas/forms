# Podiatry Surgical Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for podiatry surgical referrals, ensuring that relevant details are gathered for the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's details, including their name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Provide the clinical context, including the reason for referral and any supporting documents that may be relevant.
3. Select the surgeon's details, including their name and contact information.
4. Add any additional comments or notes as needed.
5. Schedule a follow-up date and time, and include any relevant notes.
