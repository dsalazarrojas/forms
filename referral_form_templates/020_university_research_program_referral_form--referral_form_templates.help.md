# University Research Program Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The University Research Program Referral Form is designed to facilitate the referral process for students, researchers, or other faculty members to participate in university research programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, providing all necessary information.
3. If you are referring someone else, please provide their information as well.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **universityResearchProgramReferralForm** (`university_research_program_referral_form`, text, required: false): This field is not required, but it's recommended to provide a brief description of the referral purpose.
* **FacultyAndAdvisorRecommendations** (`faculty_and_advisor_recommendations`, text, required: true): Provide a brief description of the faculty or advisor recommendation, including their name and department.
* **labOrUniversity** (`lab_or_university`, text, required: true): Indicate whether the research is related to a lab or university project.
* **nameOfFacultyMember** (`name_of_faculty_member`, text, required: true): Provide the name of the faculty member or researcher being referred.
* **department** (`department`, text, required: false): If applicable, provide the department where the research project is located.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter the email address of the referred individual.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): If applicable, provide the phone number of the referred individual.
* **date** (`date`, date, required: true): Enter the date when the research project will start or has started.
* **time** (`time`, time, required: true): Enter the time when the research project will start or has started.
* **note** (`note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments about the referral.
* **researchCandidateInformation** (`research_candidate_information`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the referred individual.
* **researchAreaOfInterest** (`research_area_of_interest`, text, required: false): Indicate the research area of interest.
* **researchProjectTitle** (`research_project_title`, text, required: true): Enter the title of the research project.
* **labOrUniversity** (`lab_or_university`, text, required: true): Indicate whether the research is related to a lab or university project.
* **nameOfFacultyMember** (`name_of_faculty_member`, text, required: true): Provide the name of the faculty member or researcher being referred.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or contact person for assistance.
