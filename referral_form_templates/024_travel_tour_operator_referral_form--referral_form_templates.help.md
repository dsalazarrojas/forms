# Travel Tour Operator Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for referring a travel tour operator to the travel agency. The form is used to collect information from the travel tour operator, including their contact details and referral information. The form will help the travel agency to track and manage referrals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the Travel Agency Information section with your travel agency's details.
2. Select the Referral option to indicate if there is a referral.
3. Enter the travel tour operator's contact details in the Travel Tour Operator section.
4. Choose the Referral Source from the options provided.
5. Provide the Referral Date and Referral Time if available.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
7. Fill in the Referral Reason if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agency Information** (`travel_tour_operator_referral_form`): Enter your travel agency's name and address.
* **Travel Tour Operator** (`travel_tour_operator`): Enter the name of the travel tour operator.
* **Referral** (`tour_operator_referral`): Select "Referral" to indicate if there is a referral.
* **Email** (`tour_operators_email`): Enter the travel tour operator's email address.
* **Phone** (`tour_operators_phone`): Enter the travel tour operator's phone number.
* **New Client Referrals** (`new_client_referrals`): Select "New Referral" if the travel tour operator refers a new client.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time** (`referral_time`): Enter the time of the referral.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
* **Additional Comments** (`form_comments`): Add any additional comments about the referral.
* **Travel Agency Website** (`travel_agency_website`): Enter the travel agency's website.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`): Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`): Select the source of the referral from the options provided.
* **Referral Source Other** (`referral_source_other`): Enter the source of the referral if not listed in the options.
* **New Client Phone** (`new_client_phone`): Enter the phone number of the new client.
* **Referral Source Referral from Other** (`referral_source_referral_from_other`): Select "Referral from Other" if the referral is from another source.
* **Referral Source Other Source** (`referral_source_other_source`): Enter the source of the other referral.
* **Referral Source Other Other** (`referral_source_other_other`): Enter any other source of the referral.
