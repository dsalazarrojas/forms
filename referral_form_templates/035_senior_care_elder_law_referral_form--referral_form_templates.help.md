# Senior Care Elder Law Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate referrals for senior care and elder law services. It helps gather necessary information about the client's situation, care team, and follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the basic information about the client on Page 1.
2. On Page 2, select the care team involved.
3. Enter the client's details on Page 3.
4. Enter the referral date on Page 4.
5. Provide follow-up details and select the follow-up method, frequency, and status on Pages 5, 6, and 7, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Basic Information (Page 1)**
	+ **Basic Information (basic_info**, text, required: false)**: Please provide a brief description of the client's situation.
* **Care Team (Page 2)**
	+ **Care Team (care_team, select_one, required: true)**: Select the care team involved in the client's situation. Options: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
* **Client Information (Page 3)**
	+ **Client Information (client_info, text, required: true)**: Enter the client's details, such as name, address, and contact information.
* **Referral Date (Page 4)**
	+ **Referral Date (referral_date, date, required: true)**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Follow-up Details (Page 5)**
	+ **Follow-up Details (follow_up_details, text, required: true)**: Provide any additional details about the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Method (Page 6)**
	+ **Follow-up Method (follow_up_method, select_one, required: true)**: Select the method used for follow-up. Options: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
* **Follow-up Frequency (Page 7)**
	+ **Follow-up Frequency (follow_up_frequency, select_one, required: true)**: Select the frequency of follow-up. Options: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.
* **Follow-up Status (Page 8)**
	+ **Follow-up Status (follow_up_status, select_one, required: true)**: Select the status of the follow-up. Options: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete each field accurately to ensure accurate data collection.
* If the 'Care Team' field is 'Other', please provide the specific details in the space provided.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields or their options, please reach out to our support team for clarification.
