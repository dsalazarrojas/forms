# Home Cleaning Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referral information from customers or clients who want to book services from the home cleaning service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Enter your name and email in the first two fields.
3. If you are referring someone else, provide their name and contact information in the Referral Name, Referral Phone Number, and Referral Email fields.
4. Select the type of service you want to book (e.g., one-time cleaning, regular cleaning schedule).
5. Choose a preferred time and date for the cleaning service.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about the referral in the Additional Comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Referral Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person you are referring.
* **Referral Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person you are referring.
* **Referral Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the person you are referring.
* **How did you find us?** (text, optional): Describe how you found the home cleaning service.
* **What services do you want?** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of service you want to book (e.g., one-time cleaning, regular cleaning schedule).
* **Preferred Scheduling Time** (time, optional): Choose a preferred time for the cleaning service.
* **Preferred Date** (date, optional): Choose a preferred date for the cleaning service.
* **Additional Comments** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
