# Retail Tailor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Tailor Referral Form is used to collect information about retail referrals, customer details, and tailor details. This form is intended for use by retail customers to provide information about their referral experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Customer Name** in the field provided.
2. Provide the **Referral Name** as it has been given to you.
3. Enter the **Customer Contact Number** of the person being referred.
4. Enter the **Customer Email** (if applicable).
5. Provide any additional **Referral Details** that would be helpful for the referral process.
6. Select the **Referral Type** (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
7. Enter the **Follow up Contact** date and time.
8. Enter the **Follow up Date** for the referral.
9. If applicable, enter the **Tailor Name** and **Tailor Phone** and **Tailor Email**.
10. Enter any **Note** about the referral.
11. Select the **Follow up Time** (if applicable).
12. For **Customer Service**, select any options that apply to the customer's experience.
13. Select the **Referral Status** (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
14. Enter the **Customer Service Rating** (if applicable).
15. Enter the **Customer Service Comment** (if applicable).
16. Select the **Follow up Status** (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
17. Enter the **Tailor Rating** (if applicable).
18. Enter the **Tailor Comment** (if applicable).
19. Enter the **Customer Comment** (if applicable).
20. Select the **Follow up Method** (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
21. Enter the **Follow up Note** (if applicable).
22. Enter the **Form Submitted By** with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the customer being referred.
* **Referral Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person referring the customer.
* **Customer Contact Number** (Text, Required): Enter the customer's contact number.
* **Customer Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the customer's email (if applicable).
* **Referral Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information about the referral.
* **Referral Type** (Select One, Required): Select the type of referral (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Follow up Contact** (Date, Required): Enter the date and time of the follow-up contact.
* **Follow up Date** (Date, Required): Enter the follow-up date for the referral.
* **Tailor Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the tailor involved in the referral.
* **Tailor Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the tailor's phone number.
* **Tailor Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the tailor's email (if applicable).
* **Note** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Follow up Time** (Time, Optional): Select the time for the follow-up contact (if applicable).
* **Customer Service** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select any options that apply to the customer's experience.
* **Referral Status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the referral (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Customer Service Rating** (Number, Optional): Enter a rating for the customer service (if applicable).
* **Customer Service Comment** (Text, Optional): Enter a comment about the customer service (if applicable).
* **Follow up Status** (Select One, Optional): Select the status of the follow-up contact (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Tailor Rating** (Number, Optional): Enter a rating for the tailor (if applicable).
* **Tailor Comment** (Text, Optional): Enter a comment about the tailor (if applicable).
* **Customer Comment** (Text, Optional): Enter a comment from the customer (if applicable).
* **Follow up Method** (Select One, Optional): Select the method of the follow-up contact (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Follow up Note** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes about the follow-up contact.
* **Form Submitted By** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the form submitter.
