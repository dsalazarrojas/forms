# Safe Deposit Box Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safe Deposit Box Referral Form is used to refer new customers for a safe deposit box account. This form collects necessary information for the referral process, including customer and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, including customer details and contact methods.
2. Select the referral status and follow-up status.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Info** (1, text, optional): Enter the customer's name and any relevant details.
* **Account Info** (2, text, optional): Enter the customer's account number or any relevant account details.
* **Referral Source** (3, text, optional): Enter the source of the referral (e.g., friend, advertisement).
* **Contact Method** (4, text, optional): Enter the method of contact (e.g., phone, email).
* **Contact Name** (5, text, optional): Enter the name of the person to contact.
* **Customer Referral** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select whether this is a customer referral or not.
* **Referral Status** (7, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the referral (Active or Inactive).
* **Contact Email** (8, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person to contact.
* **Contact Phone** (9, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person to contact.
* **Customer Referral Comments** (10, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the referral.
* **Follow-Up Status** (11, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the follow-up (Pending or Followed Up).
* **Follow-Up Comments** (12, text, optional): Provide any additional comments about the follow-up.
* **Assigned Agent** (13, text, optional): Enter the name of the agent assigned to the referral.
* **Notes** (14, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Customer Info** (15, text, optional): Enter the customer's information again.
* **Account Status** (16, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the account (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Contact Type** (17, select_one, optional): Select the type of contact (Phone, Email, or In-Person).
* **Follow-Up Date** (18, date, optional): Enter the date of the last follow-up.
* **Last Follow-Up Date** (19, date, optional): Enter the date of the last follow-up.
* **Last Follow-Up Notes** (20, text, optional): Enter any notes about the last follow-up.
* **Next Follow-Up Date** (21, date, optional): Enter the date of the next follow-up.
* **Next Follow-Up Notes** (22, text, optional): Enter any notes about the next follow-up.
* **Customer ID** (23, number, optional): Enter the customer's ID.
* **Account ID** (24, number, optional): Enter the account ID.
* **Created By** (25, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the referral.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Check if a customer already has a safe deposit box before creating a new referral.
* Use the referral status and follow-up status to track the progress of the referral.
* Enter any additional comments or notes to help with the referral process.
