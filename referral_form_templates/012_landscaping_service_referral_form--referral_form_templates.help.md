# Landscaping Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a referral form designed to capture information from customers who want to refer a friend or family member to the landscaping service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your information and the information about the person you are referring.
2. Select the type of service the referred individual is interested in (Residential, Commercial, or Both).
3. Briefly explain why you referred the person (optional, but recommended).
4. Indicate if you would like to be contacted about the referral (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
* **Client Name** (`client_info`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person you are referring.

### Landscaping Service Referral
* **Landscaping Service Type** (`service_referral`, select_one, required): Choose the type of service the referred individual is interested in:
	+ Residential
	+ Commercial
	+ Both

### Referral Reason
* **Why Did They Referee Us?** (`referral_reason`, text, optional): If you'd like, explain why you're referring this person (e.g. "We had a great experience with their team").

### Contact Request
* **Would You Like to be Contacted?** (`contact_request`, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes
	+ No

Note: Fields marked as "optional" can be skipped if not applicable.
