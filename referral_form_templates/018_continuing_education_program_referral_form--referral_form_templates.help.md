# Continuing Education Program Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to refer candidates for the Continuing Education Program. The form captures essential information about the candidate, including their contact details, program of interest, and qualifications. The purpose of this form is to help our team assess the candidate's suitability for the program and facilitate their application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address as the referrer.
2.  Identify the candidate's name, email address, and any additional contact details you would like to provide.
3.  Select the candidate's relationship with you as the referrer.
4.  Specify the candidate's program of interest and provide a brief summary of their background.
5.  Indicate the candidate's level of experience in their field.
6.  Select the candidate's highest level of education earned.
7.  Provide a reason for referring the candidate to the program.
8.  Select the candidate's key strengths and career objectives.
9.  Indicate whether the candidate requires financial aid or an employer is sponsoring their application.
10. If applicable, specify the preferred start date for the program.
11.  Provide any additional relevant details about the candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Name** (`referrer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the referrer.
* **Referrer Email** (`referrer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as the referrer.
* **Referrer Phone** (`referrer_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact number as the referrer.
* **Referrer Relationship** (`referrer_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you know the candidate (e.g., Employer, Colleague, Friend, etc.).
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the candidate.
* **Candidate Email** (`candidate_email`, `email`, required): Enter the candidate's email address.
* **Candidate Phone** (`candidate_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide the candidate's contact number.
* **Program of Interest** (`program_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the program the candidate is interested in (e.g., Business Administration, etc.).
* **Candidate Qualifications** (`candidate_qualifications`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the candidate's background and relevant experience.
* **Candidate Experience** (`candidate_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select the candidate's level of experience in their field.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the candidate's highest educational attainment (e.g., High School, Master's Degree, etc.).
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, `text`, required): Specify the reason for referring the candidate to the program.
* **Candidate Strengths** (`candidate_strengths`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the candidate's key strengths and skills.
* **Career Goals** (`career_goals`, `text`, optional): Provide the candidate's career objectives.
* **Financial Assistance Needed** (`financial_assistance_needed`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether the candidate needs financial aid.
* **Employer Sponsorship** (`employer_sponsorship`, `select_one`, optional): Check if an employer is sponsoring the candidate's application.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, optional): Specify the candidate's preferred start date.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Provide any other relevant details about the candidate.
