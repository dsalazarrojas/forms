# Relocation Consultant Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relocation Consultant Referral Form is used to gather information about a client's consultant or agent who referred them to the company. This form helps track referrals efficiently and ensures accurate communication with the consultant or agent.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
- Review the form carefully before submission.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and authorization to use this form.
- Do not duplicate referrals to prevent unnecessary work and potential conflicts with other consultants or agents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Enter the client's name, address, and contact details.
* **Contact Method**: Choose how you plan to contact the consultant or agent. Select at least one of the options: 'Phone: 1-800-555-1212', 'Email: [email]', or 'Text: 1-800-555-1234'.
* **Consultant Information**: Enter the name of the consultant or agent who referred the client to the company.
* **Referral Type**: Choose the type of referral. Select one of the options: 'Direct Referral', 'Social Media', or 'Email Marketing'.
* **Referral Information**: Enter any additional information about the referral.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the name of the consultant or agent who referred the client to the company.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Consultant Company**: Enter the name of the consultant's company.
* **Consultant Phone**: Enter the consultant's phone number.
* **Consultant Email**: Enter the consultant's email address. 
* **Client Referral Status**: Choose the status of the referral. Select one of the options: 'Active', 'Not Active', or 'Pending'.
* **Client Information**: Enter any additional information about the client.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the referral. 

Note: Fields marked as `required` must be filled out to successfully submit the form.
