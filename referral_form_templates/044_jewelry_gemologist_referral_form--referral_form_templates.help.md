# Jewelry Gemologist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture referral information for gemologists. It gathers details about referrals to other gemologists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Referral Information field with the details of the referral.
2. Enter the Referral Details in the second field.
3. Provide a Referral Date for the referral.
4. Specify the Referral Time if necessary.
5. Enter any Referral Notes about the referral.
6. Provide the Referral Email for the referred gemologist.
7. Enter the Referral Phone number for the referred gemologist.
8. Add any additional Referral Comments about the referral.
9. Select the Referral Status from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Gemologist Referral** (Text): Enter the details of the referral, including any relevant information.
* **Referral Details** (Text): Provide a brief description of the referral.
* **Referral Date** (Date): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time** (Time): Enter the time of the referral if necessary.
* **Referral Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Referral Email** (Email): Enter the email of the referred gemologist.
* **Referral Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the referred gemologist.
* **Referral Comments** (Note): Enter any additional comments about the referral.
* **Referral Status** (Select One): Select the status of the referral from the options provided: Completed, In progress, or Pending.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid duplicate or unnecessary referrals.
* Double-check the Referral Email and Referral Phone number for correct formatting.
* Use the Referral Status field to track the progress of the referral.
* Review the Referral Comments field for any additional context about the referral.
