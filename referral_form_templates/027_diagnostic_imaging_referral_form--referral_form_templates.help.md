# Diagnostic Imaging Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for diagnostic imaging referrals. It is intended to be used by healthcare professionals to gather necessary details for imaging studies. The form is divided into three pages, each covering specific aspects of the referral process. Please ensure you only submit the form if you intend to refer a patient for imaging studies. If this is not your intended purpose, do not complete this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct page number from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the first page.
3. Provide patient information, including their demographic details, on the second page.
4. Specify the imaging study details, such as the type of imaging and the reason for the referral, on the third page.
5. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page

* **First Page (Page 1)** (`first_page`, text, optional): This is the beginning of the form where you can enter your name and contact information to be associated with the referral.

### Second Page

* **Patient Information (Page 2)** (`second_page`, text, optional): On this page, enter the patient's demographic details, such as their name, date of birth, contact information, and insurance details. This information will help healthcare professionals to provide the best possible care.
*   **Imaging Details (Page 2)** (`second_page`, text, optional): In this section, specify the type of imaging study required, such as X-ray, CT scan, or MRI. You can also describe the reason for the referral.

### Third Page

* **Imaging Referral Details (Page 3)** (`third_page`, text, optional): On this page, enter the specific details of the imaging study, such as the location of the imaging study, and the expected results. This page is intended to provide comprehensive information for the imaging study.

## Tips
Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information for the patient. The more details you provide, the better the healthcare professionals can assist with the imaging study. If you're unsure about any information, please contact us for further guidance.
