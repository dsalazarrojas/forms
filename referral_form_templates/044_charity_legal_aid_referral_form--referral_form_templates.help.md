# Charity Legal Aid Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Legal Aid Referral Form is a tool for organizations to gather information about individuals seeking legal aid. It helps aid providers assess and prioritize cases, and ensure that clients receive the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address.
2. Enter your Contact Phone Number and Mailing Address.
3. Select your Date of Birth for eligibility assessment.
4. Choose your Preferred Language for communication.
5. Choose the Type of Legal Issue you are experiencing.
6. Describe the Legal Issue in the space provided.
7. Indicate the Urgency Level of your situation.
8. If you have a scheduled Court Date, select 'True' and provide the Court Date.
9. If applicable, indicate if there is an opposing party involved.
10. Enter the size of your household.
11. Select your Monthly Household Income.
12. Indicate if you receive Public Assistance.
13. If you have had previous legal help, select 'Yes' if it was in the past or 'True' if you are currently represented.
14. Select the source where you heard about this service.
15. Give your consent for follow-up contact.
16. If necessary, provide Additional Information about your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number.
* **Mailing Address**: Your full address for contact and follow-up.
* **Date of Birth**: This is for eligibility assessment purposes.
* **Preferred Language**: Please select your preferred language for communication.
* **Type of Legal Issue**: The primary concern for which you seek assistance.
* **Describe Your Legal Issue**: A detailed description of the legal issue you are experiencing.
* **Urgency Level**: The urgency of your situation, which helps aid providers prioritize cases.
* **Court Date**: If you have a scheduled court date, enter it here. If not, select 'False'.
* **Is There an Opposing Party?**: If there is another party involved, select 'Yes represented' or 'Yes not represented', otherwise select 'False'.
* **Household Size**: The number of people living in your household.
* **Monthly Household Income**: Before taxes, select your income level.
* **Do You Receive Public Assistance?**: If you receive government aid, select 'True'.
* **Have You Had Legal Help Before?**: If you have had previous legal help, select 'Yes in the past' or 'True' if you are currently represented.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?**: Select the source where you heard about this service.
* **Consent to Contact**: Give your permission for follow-up contact.
* **Additional Information**: Any other relevant details about your case.
