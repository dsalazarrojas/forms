# Young Women's Program Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Young Women's Program Referral Form" is a tool designed to collect and document referrals for the Young Women's Program. It helps ensure that all necessary information for the referral process is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Email address to enable communication for further steps.
3. Enter your Date of Birth to verify your age eligibility.
4. Enter your Address to facilitate contact and communication.
5. Provide your Phone number for follow-up and communication purposes.
6. Answer the questions regarding your current School Name, School Address, and Program Type (if applicable).
7. Specify the Referral Type and Program Type (if applicable).
8. Enter the date and time you referred this individual for the program.
9. If necessary, provide additional details about the program in the Program Details field.
10. Finally, confirm if you have taken any Next Steps to support the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth to verify your age eligibility.
* **Address** (`address`, text, **required**): Enter your address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up and communication.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, select one, **optional**): Choose whether you are referring from or are currently in this school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, **required**): Enter the address of your school for reference.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, select one, **optional**): Choose the type of program you are referring this individual for.
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, select multiple, **required**): Indicate the type of referral (e.g., academic, professional, etc.).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select multiple, **required**): Specify the type of program you are referring this individual for (if applicable).
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date you are referring this individual for the program.
* **Referral Time** (`referral_time`, time, **required**): Enter the time you are referring this individual for the program.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, note, **optional**): Provide any additional information or details about the program.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select one, **required**): Confirm whether you have taken any next steps or further actions to support the referral.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Check for any existing referrals before submitting to avoid duplication.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant authorities or seek clarification.
* This form is a critical tool for the referral process; ensure that it is completed carefully and thoroughly.
