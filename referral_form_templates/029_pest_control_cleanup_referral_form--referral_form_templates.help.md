# Pest Control Cleanup Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help our pest control service team understand your needs and provide you with the best possible assistance. Please fill out this form with accurate and detailed information to help us provide effective services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your first name and last name.
2. Enter your phone number (optional).
3. Provide your email address (optional).
4. Enter your home or service address.
5. Specify the location of the pest control service.
6. Choose the type of service you require (e.g., Pest Extermination, Fumigation, or Rodent control).
7. Describe the specific details of the service you need.
8. Indicate the priority level of the service (High, Medium, or Low).
9. Select the type(s) of pests you are experiencing (e.g., Cockroaches, Rats, or Bed bugs).
10. Choose the date and time of the service.
11. Enter any additional details about the pest control request.
12. Specify the status of the service (Active, Closed, or Pending).
13. Enter any notes for the technicians (optional).
14. Enter any comments for the service team (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Address**: Enter your home or service address.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the pest control service.
* **Type of Service**: Choose the type of service you require (e.g., Pest Extermination, Fumigation, or Rodent control).
* **Service Details**: Describe the specific details of the service you need.
* **Priority Level**: Indicate the priority level of the service (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Type of Pests**: Select the type(s) of pests you are experiencing (e.g., Cockroaches, Rats, or Bed bugs).
* **Date of Service**: Enter the date of the service.
* **Time of Service**: Enter the time of the service.
* **Service Date**: Enter the date of the service (optional).
* **Service Time**: Enter the time of the service (optional).
* **Additional Details**: Enter any additional details about the pest control request.
* **Status**: Specify the status of the service (Active, Closed, or Pending).
* **Service Notes**: Enter any notes for the technicians (optional).
* **Comments**: Enter any comments for the service team (optional).
