# Business Accelerator Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Accelerator Referral Form is used to recommend businesses to the accelerator for potential participation in their program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information, including your full name, email address, phone number, and relationship to the referred business.
2. Provide details about the business you are referring, including its name, industry, stage, team size, founder information, business overview, value proposition, and funding status.
3. Choose the reason why you are referring the business and your permission to contact the business about this referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Information** (`referrer_info`, `note`, optional): Provide a brief introduction to explain your connection to the referred business and why you are referring it to the accelerator.
* **Your Full Name** (`referrer_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the person making the referral.
* **Your Email** (`referrer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as the contact information for this referral.
* **Your Phone** (`referrer_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number as the contact information for this referral.
* **Your Relationship to Referral** (`referrer_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select how you know the referred business (Friend, Family Member, Colleague, Business Partner, Customer, Vendor, Mentor, Investor, Other).
* **Referred Business Information** (`referred_business_info`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about the business you are referring.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the business you are referring.
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, `text`, optional): Enter the website URL of the business you are referring.
* **Industry** (`industry`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry sector of the business (Technology, Healthcare, Finance, E-commerce, Education, Clean Energy, Food and Beverage, Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Other).
* **Business Stage** (`business_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select the current stage of the business (Idea Stage, Pre-Seed, Seed, Early Stage, Growth Stage, Expansion Stage, Mature).
* **Founding Date** (`founding_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date the business was founded.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_of_employees`, `select_one`, required): Select the current team size of the business (Solo Founder, 2-5 employees, 6-10 employees, 11-20 employees, 21-50 employees, More than 50 employees).
* **Founder Information** (`founder_info`, `note`, optional): Provide any details about the founder.
* **Founder Name** (`founder_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the primary founder.
* **Founder Email** (`founder_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the primary founder.
* **Founder Phone** (`founder_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact number of the primary founder.
* **Founder Background** (`founder_background`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the founder's experience.
* **Business Overview** (`business_overview`, `text`, required): Describe what the business does.
* **Value Proposition** (`value_proposition`, `text`, required): Explain the value proposition of the business.
* **Reason for Referral** (`referral_reason`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason why you are referring the business (Strong Growth Potential, Innovative Product, Experienced Team, Market Opportunity, Personal Recommendation, Other).
* **Current Funding Status** (`funding_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current funding status of the business (Bootstrapped, Friends and Family, Angel Investment, Venture Capital, Grant Funding, Not Raised).
* **Total Funding Raised** (`funding_amount`, `select_one`, optional): Select the approximate amount of funding raised.
* **Goals for Accelerator Program** (`accelerator_goals`, `text`, required): Describe what the business hopes to gain from the accelerator program.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any other relevant information.
* **Permission to Contact** (`permission_contact`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you have informed the business about this referral (Yes - They Expect Contact, Yes - But Surprised, No - Please Contact Me First).
