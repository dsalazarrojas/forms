# Behavior Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from referral sources about an individual's behavior concerns, allowing our team to understand the context and prioritize their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible for each field.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. Double-check for spelling and grammar errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the individual being referred.
* **Date of Birth** (`client_dob`, date, required): Enter the individual's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): If the client is a minor, enter their parent or guardian's name.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the referral source's phone number.
* **Primary Behavior Concerns** (`primary_behavior_concerns`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable behavior concerns from the list (Aggression, Non-compliance, Self-injury, Social Skills Deficits, Anxiety, Disruptive Behavior, Other).
* **Description of Primary Behavior** (`behavior_description`, text, required): Describe the behavior in observable and measurable terms.
* **Frequency of Behavior** (`behavior_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the frequency with which this behavior occurs (Multiple times per hour, Daily, 2-3 times per week, Weekly, Monthly or less).
* **Severity Level** (`behavior_severity`, select_one, required): Choose the overall impact on daily functioning (Mild - Minimal disruption, Moderate - Significant impact, Severe - High risk or major disruption, Crisis Level - Immediate safety concern).
* **Background and History** (`background_info_header`, note, optional): This field is for additional information about the individual's background and history.
* **Previous Interventions** (`previous_interventions`, text, optional): Describe any previous interventions or strategies that have been tried.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): List any medications that may impact the behavior.
* **Referral Source Name** (`referral_source_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or agency making the referral.
* **Referral Source Email** (`referral_source_email`, email, required): Enter the referral source's email address.
* **Referral Urgency** (`referral_urgency`, select_one, required): Choose the priority level of the referral (Low - Routine referral, Medium - Needs attention soon, High - Urgent priority).
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information or comments.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Be as thorough as possible when providing information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact our team for guidance.
