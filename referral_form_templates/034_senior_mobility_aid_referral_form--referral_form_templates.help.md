# Senior Mobility Aid Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Mobility Aid Referral Form is designed to collect information from healthcare professionals to refer senior patients to mobility aids. This form helps healthcare staff provide accurate and timely referrals to ensure seniors receive the necessary support.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out this form, please gather all necessary information about the senior patient. Then, complete each section in order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Senior Mobility Aid Referral Form** (text): This is the title of the form and the form's header.
- **Referral Details** (referral_details, text, required): Provide a clear and concise description of the senior patient's situation, including their mobility needs and any relevant medical conditions.
- **Care Team** (care_team, select_multiple, required): Indicate if the senior patient has a care team in place. Select 'Yes' if they do, 'No' if not.
- **Urgency Level** (urgency, select_one, required): Choose the urgency level of the referral, which can be High, Medium, or Low.
- **Priority** (priority, select_one, required): Select the priority level of the referral, which can be High, Medium, or Low.
- **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about the senior patient.
- **Contact Information** (contact_info, email, required): Enter the email address of the healthcare professional or organization responsible for the referral.
- **Phone Number** (phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the healthcare professional or organization responsible for the referral.
- **Date of Visit** (date_of_visit, date, optional): Enter the date of the visit or assessment for the senior patient.
- **Time of Visit** (time_of_visit, time, optional): Enter the time of the visit or assessment for the senior patient.

Note: All fields with a 'required' tag are mandatory and must be filled out. Fields with an 'optional' tag can be filled out but are not required.
