# Attorney Referral Network Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Attorney Referral Network Application Form is designed to help attorneys participate in the network and get noticed by potential clients. The form collects information about the attorneys' practices, preferences, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the contact information section:
    *   Attorney Name: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your bar license.
    *   Firm Name: Enter the name of your law firm or solo practice.
    *   Firm Address: Enter your firm's street address.
    *   Firm Phone: Enter your main office number.
    *   Email Address: Enter your professional email address.

2.  Next, fill out the practice information section:
    *   Years of Legal Practice: Enter the total number of years you have been practicing law.
    *   Primary Practice Areas: Select the main areas of law you practice (e.g., Corporate, Family Law, etc.).
    *   Practice Focus: Describe the typical client you work with (e.g., individuals, businesses, etc.).

3.  Then, fill out the case referral types section:
    *   Case Referral Types: Select the types of cases you can handle (e.g., plaintiff, defendant, etc.).

4.  Finally, fill out the referral preferences section:
    *   Referral Fee Structure: Select how you handle referral fees (e.g., standard split, hourly referral fee, etc.).
    *   Referral Fee Percentage: Enter the typical percentage offered for referral fees.
    *   Additional Information: Provide any other information about your practice that you think would be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attorney Name** (`Attorney Name`, `text`, `true`): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your bar license.
*   **Bar Number(s)** (`Bar Number(s)`, `text`, `true`): Enter all states where you are admitted to practice law.
*   **Years of Legal Practice** (`Years of Legal Practice`, `number`, `true`): Enter the total number of years you have been practicing law.
*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, `true`): Enter your professional email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, `true`): Enter your professional phone number.
*   **Firm Name** (`Firm Name`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of your law firm or solo practice.
*   **Firm Address** (`Firm Address`, `text`, `true`): Enter your firm's street address.
*   **City, State, ZIP** (`City, State, ZIP`, `text`, `true`): Enter your firm's city, state, and ZIP code.
*   **Firm Phone** (`Firm Phone`, `text`, `true`): Enter your main office number.
*   **Primary Practice Areas** (`Primary Practice Areas`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select the main areas of law you practice (e.g., Corporate, Family Law, etc.).
*   **Other Practice Areas** (`Other Practice Areas`, `text`, `false`): Enter any additional areas of law you practice.
*   **Practice Focus** (`Practice Focus`, `text`, `true`): Describe the typical client you work with (e.g., individuals, businesses, etc.).
*   **Case Referral Types** (`Case Referral Types`, `select_multiple`, `true`): Select the types of cases you can handle (e.g., plaintiff, defendant, etc.).
*   **Professional Certifications** (`Professional Certifications`, `text`, `false`): Enter any board certifications or specializations.
*   **Law School Attended** (`Law School Attended`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of your law school.
*   **Professional Memberships** (`Professional Memberships`, `text`, `false`): Enter any bar associations, networks, etc.
*   **Network Engagement** (`Network Engagement`, `note`, `false`): This section is a note section, and it's up to the user to provide any information they want.
*   **What Type of Referrals Do You Seek?** (`What Type of Referrals Do You Seek?`, `text`, `true`): Enter the types of cases or clients you are looking for.
*   **Referral Fee Structure** (`Referral Fee Structure`, `select_one`, `true`): Select how you handle referral fees (e.g., standard split, hourly referral fee, etc.).
*   **Referral Fee Percentage** (`Referral Fee Percentage`, `number`, `false`): Enter the typical percentage offered for referral fees.
*   **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, `false`): Provide any other information about your practice that you think would be helpful.

## Tips
*   This form is designed to be completed by attorneys who want to participate in the Attorney Referral Network.
*   Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to help the network match you with potential clients who fit your areas of expertise and referral fee structures.
*   If you have any questions or need help, contact the Attorney Referral Network for assistance.
