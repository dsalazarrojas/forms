# Therapeutic Support Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapeutic Support Referral Form is used to refer a patient to a support service provider. This form collects information from the user referring the patient and the patient's details.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the user's name and referral date.
2. Select the referral location and department.
3. Enter the referral reason and patient's name.
4. Enter the patient's date of birth (optional).
5. Enter the patient's address (optional).
6. Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
7. Enter the patient's email (optional).
8. Select the support services the patient may require.
9. Enter the name of the support provider (optional).
10. Enter any additional notes about the patient.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Enter the name of the person referring the patient.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Location**: Select the location where the patient will receive the support services.
* **Referral Department**: Select the department that will provide the support services.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter a brief description of why the patient needs support services.
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth (if known).
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's home address.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Support Services**: Select the support services the patient may require.
* **Support Provider**: Enter the name of the support provider (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information about the patient.

## Tips
* Ensure that the patient's referral is not already registered in the system before submitting this form.
* Verify that the patient does not have an existing referral form with a similar referral date or reason.
* Double-check the patient's details for accuracy.
