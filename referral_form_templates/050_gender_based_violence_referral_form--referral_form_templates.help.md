# Gender Based Violence Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gender Based Violence Referral Form is used to report incidents of gender-based violence, ensuring that all necessary information is collected and documented for further action and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name and other relevant details in the **Patient Name** field.
2. Select the appropriate response for the **Gender** field, which indicates whether the patient is experiencing gender-based violence.
3. Enter the date and time of the incident in the **Incident Date** and **Incident Time** fields, respectively.
4. Describe the incident in the **Incident Location** and **Incident Type** fields.
5. Enter the patient's current status and relationship to the service provider in the **Patient Status**, **Relationship**, and **Patient Contact** fields.
6. Select the appropriate response for the **Consent Given**, **Patient Consent**, and **Patient Address**, **Patient Phone**, and **Patient Email** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the patient is experiencing gender-based violence, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Age** (`age`, number, optional): Enter the patient's age.
* **Incident Date** (`incident_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Incident Time** (`incident_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the incident occurred at the service provider's location, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Describe the incident in detail.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the patient has given their consent for the report, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Patient Consent** (`patient_consent`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the patient has consented to the referral, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Patient Status** (`patient_status`, select_one, optional): Select the patient's current status.
* **Patient Contact** (`patient_contact`, text, optional): Enter the patient's contact information.
* **Relationship** (`patient_relationship`, text, optional): Describe the patient's relationship to the service provider.
* **Incident Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of incident.
* **Address** (`patient_address`, text, optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Phone** (`patient_phone`, text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email** (`patient_email`, email, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Notes** (`patient_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the patient.
