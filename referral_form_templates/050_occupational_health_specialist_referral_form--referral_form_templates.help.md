# Occupational Health Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Occupational Health Specialist Referral Form is designed to collect information about a patient's medical condition, symptoms, and treatment goals, with the aim of referring them to an occupational health specialist for medical evaluation and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Information** (page 1)
	* Enter the patient's name, age, and contact information.
2. **Medical Condition** (page 1)
	* Describe the patient's medical condition, including any relevant details.
3. **Symptoms** (page 1)
	* List the symptoms the patient is experiencing, including their severity and frequency.
4. **Treatment Goal** (page 1)
	* Specify the patient's treatment goals, such as reducing symptoms or improving quality of life.
5. **Referral Details** (page 1)
	* Provide any additional information about the referral, such as the reason for referral or the patient's medical history.
6. **Specialist Information** (page 1)
	* Enter any relevant information about the referral specialist, such as their name and contact number.
7. **Date and Time** (page 2)
	* Select the date and time for the referral appointment.
8. **Date and Time 2** (page 2)
	* Select the date and time for any follow-up appointments or consultations.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are completed for accurate referral.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the referral specialist or healthcare team for guidance.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the patient's medical condition and symptoms.
* Use the provided space for any additional information or notes.
* Double-check the selected dates and times for accuracy.
