# Press Release Writer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Press Release Writer Referral Form is designed to facilitate connections between press release writers and potential clients. Fill out this form to refer a press release writer to a potential client.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide details about the press release, such as its title.
2. Enter the name and email address of the person referring the press release writer.
3. Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Press Release Details (Page 1)
* **Press Release Details**: Enter a brief description of the press release, including its title. This will help the press release writer understand the context and requirements of the project.

### Name (Page 2)
* **Name**: Enter the name of the person referring the press release writer to the client. This will help the press release writer contact you to discuss further details.

### Email (Page 3)
* **Email**: Enter your email address so the press release writer can contact you about the referral. Ensure to use a valid and active email address that you regularly check.

### Company (Page 4)
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the referral. This will help the press release writer understand the context and requirements of the project.

## Tips
* Be concise and clear in your description of the press release details to help the press release writer understand the project requirements.
* Use a valid email address that you regularly check to ensure timely communication with the press release writer.
* If you have any additional information or context about the referral, feel free to include it in the description or email.
