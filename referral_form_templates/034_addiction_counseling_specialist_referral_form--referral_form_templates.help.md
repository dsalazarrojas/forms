# Addiction Counseling Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from referring providers for addiction counseling specialist referrals.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the referring provider's information, including their name, organization, and phone number.
2. Provide the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact number.
3. Specify the primary substance(s) of concern.
4. Indicate the urgency of the referral.
5. List any known co-occurring mental health conditions and known risks.
6. State the specific goals for the counseling referral.
7. Confirm that the patient has signed a Release of Information (ROI) for this referral.
8. Date and time of referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referring Provider Name**: Please enter the name of the healthcare professional making the referral.
	+ This is a required field, and it's essential for identifying the referring provider.
* **Referring Organization / Clinic**: Please enter the name of the organization or clinic that the referring provider is associated with.
	+ This information will help the addiction counseling specialist understand the referring organization's context.
* **Provider Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the referring provider.
	+ This will help the addiction counseling specialist contact the referring provider with any questions or concerns.
* **Patient Full Name**: Enter the full name of the patient being referred.
	+ This is a required field, and it's crucial for identifying the patient.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth.
	+ This is a required field, and it helps the addiction counseling specialist track the patient's age and medical history.
* **Patient Contact Number**: Enter the patient's contact number.
	+ This will help the addiction counseling specialist communicate with the patient.
* **Primary Substance(s) of Concern**: Specify the primary substance(s) that the patient is struggling with.
	+ This information will help the addiction counseling specialist tailor the treatment plan.
* **Urgency of Referral**: Indicate the urgency of the referral.
	+ Options: Routine, Urgent, Crisis.
	+ Please choose the urgency level that best describes the situation.
* **Known Co-occurring Mental Health Conditions**: List any known co-occurring mental health conditions the patient may have.
	+ This information will help the addiction counseling specialist understand the patient's overall health situation.
* **Known Risks**: Select any known risks the patient may be facing.
	+ Options: High overdose risk, History of withdrawal seizures, Homelessness / Housing instability, Self-harm history, Legal / Justice involvement.
	+ Please select all that apply.
* **Goals for Counseling Referral**: Specify the specific outcomes desired from the counseling referral.
	+ This information will help the addiction counseling specialist tailor the treatment plan.
* **Patient has signed a Release of Information (ROI) for this referral**: Indicate whether the patient has signed a ROI.
	+ This is a required field, and it confirms that the patient has given permission for the referral.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date and time of the referral.
	+ This is a required field, and it helps track the referral process.
