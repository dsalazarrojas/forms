# Film Editor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect referrals for potential collaborators or partners for our film editing team. We appreciate your help in identifying individuals who can assist us in improving our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with as much detail as possible.
2. Ensure the fields are filled out correctly, as indicated by their type (e.g. email for referral email field).
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Form Studio** (`referral_form_studio`, text, optional): Enter the name or title of the studio or organization the referral is from. This will help us track the origin of the referral.
* **Referral Contact Person** (`referral_form_contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who referred them to us.
* **Referral Email** (`referral_form_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Referral Phone** (`referral_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you referred them to us.
* **Referral Note** (`referral_form_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share about the referral.
