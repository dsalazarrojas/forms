# referral_partner_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from individuals or organizations interested in becoming a partner or have been referred to a partner. It's intended for external users, not internal employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Type in your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
6. Fill in your full address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field (optional).
8. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
9. Select your role in the "Role" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
10. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
11. Fill in your department in the "Department" field.
12. Provide a website for your organization in the "Website" field (optional).
13. Type in a brief description of yourself or your organization in the "Description" field (optional).
14. Enter any additional message or comments in the "Message" field (optional).
15. Indicate whether this referral is for you or your organization in the "Referral" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
16. Select any relevant partner types in the "Partner Type" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
17. Indicate if you or your organization are interested in partnering with us in the "Contact" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
18. Select your industry in the "Industry" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
19. Select your sector in the "Sector" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
20. Select your subsector in the "Subsector" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
21. Select your job category in the "Job Category" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
22. Select your job subcategory in the "Job Subcategory" field (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
23. Indicate whether you agree to our terms and conditions in the "Agreement" field (optional).
