# Speech Pathology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide essential information about patients referred to the Speech Pathology department within our institution. It helps us to efficiently process referrals and provide proper care and attention to our patients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient Phone** (text, required): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Healthcare Provider** (text, required): Enter the name of the patient's healthcare provider.
* **Referral Reason** (text, required): Briefly describe the reason for the referral.
* **Patient Email** (email, required): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Referral Date** (date, required): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Patient Age** (number, required): Enter the patient's age.
* **Referral Method** (select_one, required): Choose how the referral was made (Phone, Email, Online Portal).
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the patient's healthcare provider (if different from the one mentioned earlier).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the patient.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the Speech Pathology department for guidance.
* Do not submit duplicate referrals for the same patient.
