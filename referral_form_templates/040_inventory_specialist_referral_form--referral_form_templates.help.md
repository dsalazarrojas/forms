# Inventory Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inventory Specialist Referral Form is used to gather information about job postings and referrals from inventory specialists. The form is designed to collect relevant details about the job posting, including the candidate's name, job posting specifics, and contact information. It is essential to submit this form only once for each new job posting to prevent duplication of information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the candidate's name.
2. Select the job ID.
3. Choose the job posting from the options provided.
4. Provide the contact name.
5. Enter the email address.
6. Enter the phone number.
7. Select the job status (Open, Filled, or Closed).
8. Enter a brief description of the job.
9. Enter the job type.
10. Enter the job category.
11. Enter the job location.
12. Select the user who submitted the form.
13. Enter the department.
14. Enter the job level.
15. Enter the job position.
16. Enter the job title.
17. Enter the date the job was submitted.
18. Enter the time the job was submitted.
19. Enter the job status date.
20. Enter the job status time.
21. Enter any additional notes about the job posting.
22. Enter the second email address (if applicable).
23. Enter the second phone number (if applicable).
24. Enter the contact name for the second person (if applicable).
25. Select the user assigned to the job posting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate name** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the candidate.
* **Job ID** (`job_id`, number, optional): Enter the unique ID for the job posting.
* **Job posting** (`job_posting`, select_one, optional): Choose the job posting from the options provided.
* **Contact name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the contact name for the job posting.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address for the job posting.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number for the job posting.
* **Job status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the job status (Open, Filled, or Closed).
* **Job description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the job posting.
* **Job type** (`job_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of job posting.
* **Job category** (`job_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the job posting.
* **Job location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the job posting.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select the user who submitted the form.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department for the job posting.
* **Job level** (`job_level`, text, optional): Enter the level of the job posting.
* **Job position** (`job_position`, text, optional): Enter the position for the job posting.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the job posting.
* **Date submitted** (`job_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the job was submitted.
* **Time submitted** (`job_time`, time, optional): Enter the time the job was submitted.
* **Job status date** (`job_status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the job status.
* **Job status time** (`job_status_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the job status.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the job posting.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, text, optional): Enter the second email address (if applicable).
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the second phone number (if applicable).
* **Contact Name 2** (`contact_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the contact name for the second person (if applicable).
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Select the user assigned to the job posting.
