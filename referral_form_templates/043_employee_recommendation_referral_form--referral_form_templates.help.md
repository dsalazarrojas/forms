# Employee Recommendation Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to refer a candidate for a job opening. It's an opportunity for employees to recommend colleagues or external candidates who may be a good fit for a job in our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the first name and last name of the candidate being referred.
2. Select the job title of the candidate and the job they are being referred for.
3. Provide the candidate's employee ID if applicable.
4. Identify the person who referred the candidate.
5. Enter the candidate's email address so they can be contacted.
6. Enter the candidate's phone number.
7. Describe the job opening and its details.
8. Choose the type of job (full-time, part-time, or contract).
9. Select the department the job opening is in.
10. Choose the status of the job opening (open, pending, or closed).
11. Enter the date the job was posted.
12. Enter the date the job will close.
13. Enter the date the job opening occurred.
14. Enter the deadline for job applications.
15. Select the skills required for the job and recommended for the candidate.
16. Select the educational background recommended for the candidate.
17. Select the experience level recommended for the candidate.
18. Enter any job application instructions for the candidate.
19. Upload the candidate's resume.
20. Add any additional notes about the candidate.
21. Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
22. Enter the name of the person who updated the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter the first name of the candidate being referred.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter the last name of the candidate being referred.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Select the job title of the candidate and the job they are being referred for.
* **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, optional): Provide the candidate's employee ID if applicable.
* **Referred By** (Referred By, text, optional): Identify the person who referred the candidate.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the candidate's email address so they can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): Enter the candidate's phone number.
* **Job Opening** (Job Description, text, optional): Describe the job opening and its details.
* **Job Type** (Job Type, select_one, optional): Choose the type of job (full-time, part-time, or contract).
* **Department** (Department, select_multiple, optional): Select the department the job opening is in.
* **Job Status** (Job Status, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the job opening (open, pending, or closed).
* **Job Date Posted** (Job Date Posted, date, optional): Enter the date the job was posted.
* **Job Close Date** (Job Close Date, date, optional): Enter the date the job will close.
* **Job Opening Date** (Job Opening Date, date, optional): Enter the date the job opening occurred.
* **Job Application Deadline** (Job Application Deadline, date, optional): Enter the deadline for job applications.
* **Recommended Culture Fit** (Recommended Culture Fit, select_multiple, optional): Select the culture fit recommended for the candidate.
* **Recommended Skills** (Recommended Skills, select_multiple, optional): Select the skills required for the job and recommended for the candidate.
* **Recommended Education** (Recommended Education, select_multiple, optional): Select the educational background recommended for the candidate.
* **Recommended Experience** (Recommended Experience, select_multiple, optional): Select the experience level recommended for the candidate.
* **Job Application Instructions** (Job Application Instructions, text, optional): Enter any job application instructions for the candidate.
* **Resume Upload** (Resume Upload, text, optional): Upload the candidate's resume.
