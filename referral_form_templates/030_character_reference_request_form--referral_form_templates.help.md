# Character Reference Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request a character reference for a job applicant or academic program admission. The form is designed to collect relevant information about the relationship between the requesting organization and the applicant, as well as the applicant's character and skills. The submitted reference will be used to support the applicant's application and provide a more complete picture of their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information about the requesting organization and contact person.
2. Enter the applicant's information, including their full name, position or program applied for, key skills or traits needed, and reference information.
3. Rate the applicant's character and skills, including integrity, honesty, reliability, responsibility, and teamwork and collaboration.
4. Provide a final recommendation for the applicant.
5. Confirm that you are providing honest and fair feedback.
6. Add any additional comments or information about the applicant.
7. Indicate whether you would like to be contacted for a follow-up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Full Name**: The full name of the organization requesting the reference.
* **Requestor Full Name**: The name of the person handling the request.
* **Contact Email for Request**: The email address of the person to contact for follow-up and verification.
* **Applicant Full Name (Subject)**: The name of the person being referred.
* **Position or Program Applied For**: The job or program the applicant is applying for.
* **Key Skills or Traits Needed**: A brief description of the key skills or traits the applicant is expected to have.
* **Reference Full Name**: The name of the person being asked for a reference.
* **Reference Contact Email**: The email address of the person being asked for a reference.
* **Nature of Relationship**: A brief description of how you know the applicant.
* **Length of Relationship (Years)**: The length of time you have known the applicant.
* **Integrity and Honesty Rating**: A rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) of the applicant's integrity and honesty.
* **Reliability and Responsibility Rating**: A rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) of the applicant's reliability and responsibility.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration Rating**: A rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent) of the applicant's teamwork and collaboration.
* **Overall Recommendation Status**: A selection of whether to highly recommend, recommend, recommend with reservations, or not recommend the applicant.
* **Confirmation of Reference Details**: A confirmation that you are providing honest and fair feedback.
* **Date of Submission**: The current date.
* **Additional Comments or Information**: Any additional comments or information about the applicant.
* **Interest in Follow-up Contact**: An indication of whether to be contacted for a follow-up.

This form is designed to collect relevant information about the relationship between the requesting organization and the applicant, as well as the applicant's character and skills. The submitted reference will be used to support the applicant's application and provide a more complete picture of their qualifications. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible reference for the applicant.
