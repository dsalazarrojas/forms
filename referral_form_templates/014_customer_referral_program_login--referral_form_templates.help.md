# Customer Referral Program Login - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for customers to log in to the Customer Referral Program, allowing them to access their account and engage with the program benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name** (text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last name
* **Last name** (text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you'd like to receive important updates and communications from our program.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to help us contact you with any questions or updates regarding your account.
