# Artisan Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect detailed information about artisans being referred to the organization for various opportunities. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a different aspect of the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Your Information**:
	* Fill in your name and email address, as this is required information for the organization to contact you about the referral.
2. **Referrer Information**:
	* Fill in your name and email address, as this will be used to contact you about the referral.
	* Choose your relationship to the artisan from the list provided.
3. **Artisan Information**:
	* Fill in the artisan's name, email address (if available), and phone number (if available).
	* Select the type of artisan they are (e.g., jewelry, textiles, pottery, etc.).
4. **Referral Details**:
	* Provide a brief description of why you are referring the artisan.
	* Describe what makes the artisan exceptional.
	* Rate the quality of their work.
5. **Business Opportunities**:
	* Choose the type of opportunity being referred (e.g., market, retail partnership, etc.).
	* Provide a detailed description of the opportunity.
6. **Additional Information**:
	* Check if you give permission to share your contact information with the artisan.
	* Provide any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`referrer_name`): Fill in your full name.
* **Your Email Address** (`referrer_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the referral.
* **Your Phone Number** (`referrer_phone`): Provide your phone number as a backup contact method, if available.
* **Relationship to Artisan** (`relationship_to_artisan`): Choose how you know the artisan from the list provided.
* **Artisan Name** (`artisan_name`): Enter the artisan's full name.
* **Artisan Email Address** (`artisan_email`): Provide the artisan's email address, if available.
* **Artisan Phone Number** (`artisan_phone`): Enter the artisan's phone number, if available.
* **Type of Artisan** (`artisan_type`): Select the type of artisan from the list provided.
* **Why Are You Recommending This Artisan?** (`referral_reason`): Provide a brief description of why you're referring the artisan.
* **What Makes This Artisan Exceptional?** (`artisan_strengths`): Describe what makes the artisan exceptional.
* **Quality of Work** (`work_quality`): Rate the quality of the artisan's work from the provided scale.
* **Type of Opportunity Being Referred** (`opportunity_type`): Choose the type of opportunity being referred from the list provided.
* **Details About the Opportunity** (`opportunity_details`): Describe the opportunity in detail.
* **Permission to Share Information** (`contact_authorization`): Check if you give permission to share your contact information with the artisan.
* **Artisan Location** (`artisan_location`): Enter the city or region where the artisan is based.
* **Artisan Website or Social Media** (`online_presence`): Provide the artisan's website or social media handle, if available.
* **Date of Referral** (`referral_date`): Enter the date you are making the referral.
* **Would You Like to Hear About the Outcome?** (`follow_up_interest`): Check if you want updates on the referral.
* **Permission to Share My Information** (`confidentiality_consent`): Check if you give permission to share your contact information with the artisan.
* **Additional Comments or Notes** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or notes about the referral.
