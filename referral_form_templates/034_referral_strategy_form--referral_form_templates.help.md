# Referral Strategy Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about referrals to help track and analyze the effectiveness of our referral program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "Contact Name" and "Contact Email" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (optional).
3. Indicate whether this is a referral or not by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Referral Source" field.
4. Enter the reason for the referral in the "Referral Reason" field.
5. Enter the name of the customer you are referring in the "Customer Name" field.
6. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field (optional).
7. Enter the customer's email address in the "Customer Email" field.
8. Select the type of referral (e.g. "Yes" or "No") in the "Referral Type" field.
9. Enter any additional message from the customer in the "Customer Message" field (optional).
10. Enter any additional message from you as the referrer in the "Referrer Message" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Strategy Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a title for the referral.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, required): Indicate whether this is a referral or not.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the customer you are referring.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number (optional).
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Referral Type** (`referral_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of referral.
* **Customer Message** (`customer_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message from the customer (optional).
* **Referrer Message** (`referrer_message`, text, optional): Enter any additional message from you as the referrer (optional).
