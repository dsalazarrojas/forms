# Employment Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees or individuals who are referring candidates for job openings. It is an essential tool for facilitating communication and follow-up with candidates, and ensuring that they consent to being referred.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your name and contact details in the `referrer_name` and `referrer_contact` fields.
2. Enter the name and contact details of the candidate you are referring in the `candidate_name` and `candidate_contact` fields.
3. Briefly describe the position you are referring the candidate for in the `position_referred_for` field.
4. If applicable, provide a summary of the candidate's relevant skills and experience in the `candidate_skills_summary` field.
5. Confirm that you have the candidate's permission to share their contact details with the employer by selecting `Candidate consents` in the `referral_permission` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer name**: Enter your full name.
* **Referrer contact**: Provide your email address or phone number for follow-up regarding the referral.
* **Candidate name**: Enter the full name of the person being referred.
* **Candidate contact**: Provide the candidate's email address or phone number for employer follow-up.
* **Position referred for**: Enter the job title or vacancy reference for the position being referred.
* **Candidate skills summary**: This field is optional, but provide a brief summary of the candidate's relevant experience and skills if you want to give more context to the referral.
* **Permission to share contact details**: Select `Candidate consents` to confirm that you have the candidate's permission to share their contact details with the employer.
