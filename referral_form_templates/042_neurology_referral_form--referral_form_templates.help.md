# Neurology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Neurology Referral Form is designed to collect relevant information about patients who require neurological evaluation and treatment. This form helps medical professionals gather essential details about patients to ensure they receive the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, but be aware that some fields are optional.
2. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
3. If you are unsure about a field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info**: Please enter the patient's name, age, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Medical History**: Provide a brief summary of the patient's medical history, including any relevant allergies, previous surgeries, or ongoing health issues.
* **Neurological Info**: Enter a description of the patient's neurological symptoms or concerns.
* **Patient Status**: Enter the patient's current status, such as "stable" or "stable but requires further evaluation."
* **Specialist Info**: Select the specialist(s) the patient is seeing, such as a neurologist, neurosurgeon, or radiologist.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional details or comments that might be helpful for the patient's care.
* **Doctor Info**: Enter the doctor's name and contact information who will be seeing the patient.
* **Hospital Info**: Enter the hospital name and department where the patient will be seen.
* **Follow Up Info**: Enter any follow-up dates or instructions for the patient's care.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you need to make changes to the form, please use the version number to ensure continuity.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
