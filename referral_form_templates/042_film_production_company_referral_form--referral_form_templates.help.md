# Film Production Company Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Production Company Referral Form is designed to provide information about a film production company and facilitate referrals to other departments within the company. This form is used to gather data about potential new business opportunities with film production companies and to track the status of current leads.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Studio Name** field with the name of the film production company.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the **Contact Person** in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter the **Email** address of the contact person.
4. Enter the **Contact Number** of the contact person.
5. Select whether this is a **Production Company** that we should pursue business opportunities with.
6. Select the **Production Type** of the company (e.g., yes/no).
7. Enter the **Location** of the company.
8. Provide a brief **Description** of the company.
9. Indicate who **Submitted This Form** (e.g., a specific department within the company).
10. Indicate who this form was **Submitted To** (e.g., a specific department within the company).
11. Select the **Status** of the lead (e.g., active/inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Studio Name (1)**: Enter the name of the film production company.
* **Contact Person (2)**: Enter the name of the person we should contact for more information.
* **Email (3)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Number (4)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Production Company (5)**: Select yes if this is a production company that we should pursue business opportunities with.
* **Production Type (6)**: Select yes if this production company has a specific type (e.g., yes/no).
* **Location (7)**: Enter the location where the production company is based.
* **Description (8)**: Provide a brief description of the company.
* **Submitted By (9)**: Indicate who submitted this form within our company.
* **Submitted To (10)**: Indicate which department or person this form was submitted to.
* **Status (11)**: Select active or inactive depending on the current status of the lead.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and follow-up.
* Double-check the information for accuracy before submitting.
* Use this form to refer new business opportunities and track their status within the company.
