# Tenant Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tenant Referral Form is a tool used to gather information about a potential tenant, helping to assess their eligibility for a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields with the following information:

- **Contact Information**: Enter the potential tenant's name, phone number, and email address.
- **Renter Information**: Provide any relevant information about the potential tenant's rental history, such as previous rentals or landlord experiences.
- **Rental History**: Briefly describe the potential tenant's rental history, including previous properties rented and any issues encountered.
- **References**: List at least two personal references, including their name, phone number, and relationship to the potential tenant.
- **Employment Status**: Select the potential tenant's current employment status from the provided options.
- **Rental Requirements**: Specify any special rental requirements or requests the potential tenant has.
- **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about the potential tenant.
- **Lease Terms**: Select the type of lease agreement the potential tenant is interested in.
- **Rental Agreement**: Select the type of rental agreement the potential tenant is interested in.
- **Lease Terms Agreement**: Provide any additional comments or information about the lease terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the potential tenant's name, phone number, and email address.
- **Renter Information** (`text`, optional): Provide any relevant information about the potential tenant's rental history.
- **Rental History** (`text`, optional): Briefly describe the potential tenant's rental history, including previous properties rented and any issues encountered.
- **References** (`text`, optional): List at least two personal references, including their name, phone number, and relationship to the potential tenant.
- **Employment Status** (`select_one`, optional): Select the potential tenant's current employment status from the provided options.
- **Rental Requirements** (`text`, optional): Specify any special rental requirements or requests the potential tenant has.
- **Additional Comments** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the potential tenant.
- **Lease Terms** (`text`, optional): Specify any lease terms or conditions that apply to the potential tenant.
- **Rental Agreement** (`select_multiple`, required): Select the type of rental agreement the potential tenant is interested in.
- **Lease Terms Agreement** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the lease terms.
