# Art Frame Shop Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Frame Shop Referral Form is a tool used to collect and manage referrals from customers, artists, or other art professionals. It helps the shop to track and follow up on potential projects, ensuring timely and efficient communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Referrer name** field with the name of the person making the referral.
2. Enter the **Customer name** field with the name of the person or business being referred.
3. Provide the **Contact information** field with the preferred contact method (e.g., phone number or email).
4. Add a brief description of the project in the **Project description** field (if desired).
5. Choose the **Urgency** level for the project (Standard or Rush).
6. In the **Consent to share** field, select 'True' if the customer consents to be contacted, 'False' otherwise.
7. Optionally, add any additional information in the **Referral notes** field.
8. If applicable, enter the **Internal receipt ID** for shop use after acceptance.
9. Finally, select a date for **Follow-up date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Referrer name** (`referrer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person making the referral.
- **Customer name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person or business being referred.
- **Contact information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Provide the preferred contact method (e.g., phone number or email).
- **Project description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Type a brief description of the project or artwork details (if desired).
- **Preferred contact method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how the shop should contact the customer (Phone or Email).
- **Urgency** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Select the expected turnaround urgency (Standard or Rush).
- **Referral notes** (`referral_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information about the project or referral.
- **Consent to share** (`consent_to_share`, select_one, required): Select if the customer consents to be contacted (True or False).
- **Internal receipt ID** (`shop_receipt_id`, text, optional): Enter the internal receipt ID for shop use after acceptance.
- **Follow-up date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Select a date to follow up on the referral.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in the referral process.
* Be as specific as possible when providing project descriptions and contact information to ensure clear communication.
* If unsure about any field, please ask the shop staff for clarification.
* The shop will contact the customer according to the preferred contact method and urgency selected.
