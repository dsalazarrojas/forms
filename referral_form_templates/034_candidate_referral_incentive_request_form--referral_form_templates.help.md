# Candidate Referral Incentive Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request incentives for referring a candidate to the company. To be eligible, the referring employee must be a current employee of the company and have referred a candidate who has been successfully hired and completed 90 days of employment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Referring Employee Information**
	* `Employee Full Name`: Enter your full name as registered in HR.
	* `Employee ID`: Enter your official company ID.
	* `Department`: Enter the department you work in.
	* `Email Address`: Enter your company email address.
	* `Phone Number`: Enter your company phone number (optional).
2. **Candidate Information**
	* `Candidate Full Name`: Enter the full name of the referred candidate.
	* `Position Referred For`: Enter the position the candidate was referred for.
	* `Date of Referral`: Enter the date you first referred the candidate.
	* `Candidate Email Address`: Enter the email address of the referred candidate.
3. **Relationship and Assessment**
	* `Relationship to Candidate`: Select how you know the candidate (e.g. Former Colleague, Friend, Family Member, etc.).
	* `How long have you known the candidate`: Enter the length of time you have known the candidate (e.g. 5 years, worked together in 2020).
	* `Why is this candidate a good fit`: Briefly describe why you think the candidate is a good fit for the position.
4. **Incentive and Policy**
	* `Expected Incentive Amount`: Enter the expected incentive amount (refer to the company handbook for current rates).
	* `I have read the employee referral policy`: Select "Yes, I agree" to confirm you have read and understood the policy.
	* `I understand incentives are paid only after successful hire and probation`: Select "True" to confirm you understand the incentive payment terms.
5. **Submission**
	* `Submission Date`: Enter the date you are submitting this form.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when completing this form to ensure eligibility for the incentive.
* Ensure you have read and agree to the company's employee referral policy.
* Payout of incentives typically occurs after 90 days of the candidate's employment.
