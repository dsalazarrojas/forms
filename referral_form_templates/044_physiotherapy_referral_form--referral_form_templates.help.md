# Physiotherapy Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physiotherapy Referral Form is designed to collect information for a physiotherapy referral. It is intended for healthcare professionals to gather details about the patient's condition, medical history, and contact information for the physiotherapist.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, taking care to answer every required field.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Physiotherapy Referral** (1, Note, Required: No): This field is the main section where you'll enter information about the referral. Please enter a brief description of the patient's condition and the reason for the referral.
- **Physiotherapist** (2, Select One, Required: No): This field allows you to specify whether a physiotherapist will be involved in the referral. If you choose "Yes," this indicates that a physiotherapist will be working with the patient. If you choose "No," this suggests that a different type of professional will be handling the referral.
- **Patient Name** (3, Text, Required: No): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
- **Physiotherapist Name** (4, Text, Required: No): Enter the name of the physiotherapist or healthcare professional who will be working with the patient.
- **Patient DOB** (5, Date, Required: No): Provide the patient's date of birth.
- **Diagnosis** (6, Text, Required: No): Enter a brief description of the patient's diagnosis or condition.
- **Treatment Plan** (7, Text, Required: No): Describe the treatment plan or therapy approach that will be used for the patient.
- **Referral Reason** (8, Text, Required: No): Specify the reason for the referral. This can include any relevant medical history or condition that requires attention.
- **Physiotherapy Goals** (9, Text, Required: No): Define the objectives of the physiotherapy program for the patient.
- **Referral Date** (10, Date, Required: No): Enter the date of the referral.
- **Contact Details** (11, Note, Required: No): Provide contact information for the physiotherapist or healthcare professional responsible for the referral.
- **Medical History** (12, Note, Required: No): Add any relevant medical history that may be useful for the referring professional.
- **Other Referrals** (13, Select Multiple, Required: No): Check if there have been other referrals related to this patient.
- **Physiotherapist Phone** (14, Text, Required: No): Enter the contact phone number of the physiotherapist.

## Tips
- Review the completed form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
- Keep the form up-to-date and refer to it when needed.
- Store the form in a secure location to maintain patient confidentiality.
- If you have any questions or concerns, consult with a healthcare professional for guidance.
