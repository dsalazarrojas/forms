# Tech Data Protection Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect and document referrals to protect sensitive data. Referrals are used to escalate potential data protection issues to relevant teams for handling and resolution. The form helps to ensure that the requesters' information is captured accurately and efficiently, facilitating swift and effective resolution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the referral details, providing as much information as possible.
2. Select relevant categories to categorize the referral.
3. Indicate the protection level and risk level of the data.
4. Add any comments or additional information from the requester.
5. Submit the form to create a referral ticket.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Form**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form`, text, required: false)
	+ A brief description of the referral.
* **Referral Form Details**: (`referral_form_details`, text, required: false)
	+ Additional information about the referral.
* **Referral Status**: (`referral_status`, text, required: false)
	+ The current status of the referral.
* **Referral Form Status**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_status`, text, required: false)
	+ The status of the referral form.
* **Referral Form Priority**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_priority`, number, required: false)
	+ A numerical value indicating the priority level of the referral form.
* **Priority Level**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_priority_level`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select from High, Medium, or Low to indicate the priority level.
* **Requester**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester`, text, required: false)
	+ The name of the requester.
* **Requester Email**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_email`, email, required: false)
	+ The email address of the requester.
* **Requester Phone**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_phone`, text, required: false)
	+ The phone number of the requester.
* **Requester Notes**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_notes`, note, required: false)
	+ Additional information from the requester.
* **Tech Group**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_tech_group`, select_one, required: false)
	+ Select from IT, Security, or Privacy to indicate the relevant tech group.
* **Data Category**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_data_category`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select from Personal Data, Sensitive Data, or Non-Sensitive Data to indicate the category of data.
* **Data Category Type**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_data_category_type`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select from Internal Data or External Data to indicate the type of data.
* **Protection Level**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_protection_level`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select from Low, Medium, or High to indicate the protection level.
* **Risk Level**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_risk_level`, select_multiple, required: false)
	+ Select from Low, Medium, or High to indicate the risk level.
* **Requester Comments**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_comments`, text, required: false)
	+ Additional comments from the requester.
* **Requester Request Date**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_request_date`, date, required: false)
	+ The date of the referral request.
* **Requester**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester`, text, required: false)
	+ The name of the requester.
* **Requester Name**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester_name`, text, required: false)
	+ The name of the requester.
* **Requester Role**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester_role`, text, required: false)
	+ The role of the requester.
* **Requester Department**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester_dept`, text, required: false)
	+ The department of the requester.
* **Requester Manager**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester_manager`, text, required: false)
	+ The name of the requester's manager.
* **Requester Manager Phone**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester_manager_phone`, text, required: false)
	+ The phone number of the requester's manager.
* **Requester Manager Email**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester_manager_email`, email, required: false)
	+ The email address of the requester's manager.
* **Requester Manager Title**: (`tech_data_protection_referral_form_requester_requester_manager_title`, text, required: false)
	+ The title of the requester's manager.

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate and detailed information about the referral.
* Select relevant categories and priority levels to help the relevant teams handle the referral efficiently.
* Make sure to include the requester's contact information for further communication.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the relevant team for assistance.
