# Student Staff Reference Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a staff member's reference for a specific reason, such as a job or internship opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Decide if the staff member you are requesting a reference from is indeed a staff member (`staff_member` field). 
   - Select "Yes" if the person you are requesting a reference from is a staff member.
   - Select "No" if they are not a staff member.
2. Describe your relationship with the staff member (`relationship` field). 
   - Type a brief description of your relationship with the staff member.
3. Identify the department the staff member works in (`department` field).
   - Select the department where the staff member is employed.
4. Provide the start date of your relationship with the staff member (`start_date` field). 
   - Enter the date you started working with or having a relationship with the staff member.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **staff_member** (Select Multiple)
	+ Check if the staff member you are requesting a reference from is a staff member.
* **relationship** (Text)
	+ Describe your relationship with the staff member, such as "I worked as an intern with them" or "I have known them as a colleague".
* **department** (Select One)
	+ Select the department where the staff member is employed.
* **start_date** (Date)
	+ Enter the date you started working with or having a relationship with the staff member.

## Tips

* Make sure to get the staff member's consent before requesting a reference.
* Double-check that the request is not a duplicate.
* Answer each field carefully and accurately to ensure the reference is helpful.
