# DJ Service Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients who are interested in our DJ services. Please complete it accurately to help us better understand your requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your contact details in the contact_name, contact_email, and phone fields.
2. Select the type of client you are (New, Returning, or Other).
3. Choose the services you are looking for (Wedding, Corporate Event, Private Party, Bar/Bat Mitzvah, Club Night, or Other).
4. Provide a preferred contact date and time if necessary.
5. Enter any additional information or questions in the contact_message field.
6. Submit the form to initiate the referral process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_name**: Enter your name as the person contacting us.
* **contact_email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **contact_message**: Enter any additional information or questions you have about our services.
* **type**: Select whether you are a New Client, Returning Client, or Other.
* **services**: Choose the type of event you are looking for our DJ services for.
* **contact_date**: Enter a preferred contact date and time if necessary.
* **contact_time**: Enter a preferred contact time if necessary.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.
- Our team will review your information and get back to you as soon as possible.
