# sports_psychology_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports psychology referral form is a tool for parents or guardians to provide information about a student's involvement in school sports. This form helps the sports psychology services team to gather essential details about the student's needs and experiences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If a field is not applicable or not known, please leave it blank or note "Not Applicable" in the field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school the student is attending.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required): Enter the student's age.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required): Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 9th grade).
* **Parent Contact Name** (`parent_contact_name`, text, optional): If you're not the student's parent or guardian, please enter the name of the person you're referring to.
* **Parent Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent contact phone number.
* **Parent Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent contact email address.
* **Parent Contact Note** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the student's situation.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required): Select the student's gender (male, female, non-binary, or other).
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Explain the reason for the referral.
* **Student Athlete Status** (`student_athlete_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the student's athlete status (active, inactive, or unknown).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you're not the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you're not the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with the sports psychology services team for clarification.
- If you have any additional questions or concerns, feel free to contact the sports psychology services team.
