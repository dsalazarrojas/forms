# Athlete Scout Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from athletic scouts and referral sources about potential athletes for recruitment.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

* Start by completing the scout information section, which includes your name, organization or agency, email address, and phone number.
* Move on to the athlete information section, where you will provide details about the athlete, such as their full name, date of birth, current team or club, primary sport, position or event, and graduating class year.
* Next, rate the athlete's overall scouting grade, strengths, and areas for improvement.
* Then, provide a comparison to the current team roster and recommend a recruiting priority.
* If applicable, add supporting evidence, such as game footage links or athlete profiles.
* Finally, add any additional description of the scouting report and sign your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scout Full Name** (`scout_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as a scout.
* **Scout Organization or Agency** (`scout_org`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization or agency.
* **Scout Email Address** (`scout_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Scout Phone Number** (`scout_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the athlete.
* **Athlete Date of Birth** (`athlete_dob`, date, required): Enter the athlete's date of birth.
* **Current Team or Club** (`current_team`, text, required): Enter the name of the athlete's current team or club.
* **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`, text, required): Enter the name of the athlete's primary sport.
* **Position or Event** (`athlete_position`, text, required): Enter the athlete's position or event.
* **Graduating Class Year** (`grad_year`, number, required): Enter the athlete's graduating class year.
* **Overall Scouting Grade** (`scouting_grade`, select_one, required): Rate the athlete's overall scouting grade, where 1 is a low prospect and 10 is an elite prospect.
* **Key Strengths Noted** (`scout_strengths`, text, required): Describe what makes the athlete stand out.
* **Key Areas for Improvement** (`scout_improvements`, text, required): Identify areas where the athlete can improve.
* **Comparison to Current Team Roster** (`roster_comparison`, text, optional): Compare the athlete to the current team roster.
* **Recommended Recruiting Priority** (`recruiting_status`, select_one, required): Suggest a recruiting priority for the athlete.
* **Link to Game Footage** (`footage_link`, text, optional): Provide a link to game footage, if available.
* **Link to Statistics or Online Profile** (`profile_link`, text, optional): Provide a link to the athlete's statistics or online profile.
* **Description of Attached Scouting Report** (`document_description`, text, optional): Describe any files being sent separately.
* **Scout Electronic Signature** (`scout_signature`, text, required): Type your full name as the scout.
* **Date of Referral** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date of referral.
