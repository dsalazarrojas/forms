# Restaurant Internship Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals who have been referred for a potential internship position at a restaurant. The form will help the review team evaluate the referred candidate and make informed decisions about their suitability for the internship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the restaurant from the options provided, choose the position for which the candidate is being referred, enter the candidate's name, select the referral method, enter the referral source, and add any additional comments if necessary. For the reviewer section, please enter your name, position, phone number, and any comments you have about the candidate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Restaurant** (`restaurant`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the restaurant where the candidate is being referred. Please choose from the available options.
* **Position** (`position`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the position for which the candidate is being referred.
* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the candidate being referred.
* **Referral Method** (`referral_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the method by which the candidate was referred (e.g., colleague, manager, social media).
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, `text`, required: false): Enter the source of the referral (e.g., employee, manager, social media).
* **Referral Comments** (`referral_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about the candidate's referral.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments you have about the candidate.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as the reviewer.
* **Reviewer Position** (`reviewer_position`, `text`, required: false): Enter your position at the restaurant.
* **Reviewer Phone** (`reviewer_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number (format: (123) 456-7890).
* **Reviewer Email** (`reviewer_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address (format: [email]([a-zA-Z0-9.-])).
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, required: false): Add any comments you have about the candidate as the reviewer.
