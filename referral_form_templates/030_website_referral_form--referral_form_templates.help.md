# Website Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit information for a referral. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly with your referral details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. You can submit this form as many times as you need to, but please note that submitting duplicate referrals for the same website can be considered spam.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Code** (`referral_code`, text, required: false): Enter your referral code, if you have one.
* **Invitation Code** (`invitation_code`, text, required: false): Enter your invitation code to get verified (if applicable).
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name for reference.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Additional Info** (`note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or details about your referral.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the category that best describes your referral (public or private).
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required: false): Select the country where your website is located (USA, Canada, or Mexico).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* If you are unsure about a field, use your best judgment and provide the most relevant information.
