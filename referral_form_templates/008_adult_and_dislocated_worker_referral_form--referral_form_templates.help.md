# Adult And Dislocated Worker Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult And Dislocated Worker Referral Form is used to collect information about an individual's work history, skills, education, and job preferences. This information is used to help match them with potential job opportunities that fit their needs and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each field to ensure a more comprehensive match with job opportunities.
3. Review your responses for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name (required)
* The first name field is used to collect your name, which will be used for identification and communication.
### 2. Last Name (required)
* The last name field is used to collect your surname, which will be used for identification and communication.
### 3. Date of Birth (required)
* The date of birth field is used to determine your age and any age-related requirements.
### 4. Email Address (required)
* The email address field is used to ensure that you can be contacted about job opportunities and any other communications.
### 5. Phone Number (required)
* The phone number field is used for communication with you about job opportunities and other important details.
### 6. Street Address (required)
* The street address field is used to verify your current residence and determine your commute.
### 7. City (required)
* The city field is used to determine the location of your current residence and job opportunities.
### 8. State (required)
* The state field is used to determine the state or province you reside in, which may be relevant for job opportunities.
### 9. Zip Code (required)
* The zip code field is used to confirm your address and ensure correct mail delivery.
### 10. -- Employment History -- (required)
This section collects information about your past work experience.
### 11. Previous Occupation (required)
* The previous occupation field is used to collect the title of your last job.
### 12. Years of Experience in Field (required)
* The years of experience field is used to determine your level of experience in your field.
### 13. Reason for Separation from Last Job (required)
* The reason for separation field is used to understand why you left your last position.
### 14. Date of Separation (required)
* The date of separation field is used to determine when you left your last job.
### 15. -- Skills and Education -- (optional)
This section collects information about your education and skill set.
### 16. Highest Education Level (required)
* The highest education level field is used to determine your educational background.
### 17. Key Skills (required)
* The key skills field is used to list your main professional skills.
### 18. Relevant Certifications or Licenses (optional)
* The certifications field is used to list any relevant professional certifications or licenses.
### 19. -- Job Preferences -- (optional)
This section collects information about your desired employment criteria.
### 20. Desired Occupation or Field (required)
* The desired occupation field is used to determine the job title you are seeking.
### 21. Salary Expectations (optional)
* The salary expectations field is used to list your expected annual salary range.
### 22. Type of Employment Preferred (required)
* The type of employment field is used to determine your desired employment arrangement.
### 23. Willing to Relocate (required)
* The will to relocate field is used to determine if you are open to moving for work.
### 24. Transportation Available (required)
* The transportation field is used to determine if you have your own means of transportation.
