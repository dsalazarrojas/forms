# GED Preparation Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about individuals who are seeking preparation for the GED test. The goal of this form is to help identify the individual's needs and provide guidance on the next steps they need to take to prepare for the test.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. Answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use clear and concise language when completing the form.
4. Submit the form when you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Individual Information

* **Individual Information** (Required): Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Information** (Required): Enter your email address, phone number, and physical address.

### Program Information

* **Program Information** (Required): Enter the program or service you are seeking preparation for.

### Educational Background

* **Educational Background** (Required): Enter any relevant educational background information.

### Next Steps

* **Next Steps** (Required): Describe the next steps you plan to take to prepare for the GED test.

### Program Follow-up

* **Program Follow-up** (Required): Describe the program or service you would like to follow-up with after submitting this form.

### Educational Institution Info

* **Educational Institution Info** (Required): Enter any relevant information about the educational institution you are associated with.

### Program Institution Info

* **Program Institution Info** (Required): Enter any relevant information about the program or service you are seeking preparation for.

### Program Contact

* **Program Contact** (Required): Enter the name and contact information of the program or service contact.

### Submitter Information

* **Submitter Information** (Required): Enter your name and title or position.
* **Submitter Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Address** (Required): Enter your physical address.
* **Submitter Institutional Relationship** (Optional): Enter any relationship you have with the institution.
* **Institutional Info** (Required): Enter any relevant information about the institution you are associated with.
* **Program Services** (Required): Describe the program or service you are seeking preparation for.
* **Program Funding** (Optional): Enter any information about program funding.
* **Additional Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your preparation needs.

### Submit

* **Submit** (Not Required): Click this button to submit the form.

Note: Required fields are marked as (Required).
