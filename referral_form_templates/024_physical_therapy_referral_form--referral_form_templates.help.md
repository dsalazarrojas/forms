# Physical Therapy Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients or healthcare professionals for the referral process in physical therapy. It is meant to gather details about the patient's referral information, medical history, and contact information, as well as any insurance and billing requirements. The form is not intended for diagnosis or treatment purposes but rather to facilitate the referral process.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

*   Start by providing your referral information, which includes details about why you are referring the patient.
*   Then, provide the patient's personal details, such as name, any relevant medical history, and treatment plan.
*   Next, specify the patient's insurance and billing information, if applicable.
*   Finally, provide your contact information so that the physical therapist can reach out to you for further information or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Referral Information** (`referral_information`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief description of why you are referring the patient. Please include any relevant medical information or context that would be helpful for the physical therapist to understand the patient's situation.
*   **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide the patient's personal details, such as their name, any relevant medical history, or any special considerations that the physical therapist should be aware of.
*   **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): This field is for you to specify the patient's treatment plan, including any specific goals or objectives you have for the patient.
*   **Insurance and Billing** (`insurance_and_billing`, text, optional): If the patient has insurance coverage, please provide their insurance information, including the name of the insurance provider and policy number.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any relevant medical history for the patient, including any previous diagnoses, treatments, or procedures.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Finally, please provide your contact information, including your name, title, and contact number, so that the physical therapist can reach out to you for further information or clarification.

## Tips
*   Please fill out the form as thoroughly as possible, providing as much detail as you can.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the physical therapist or healthcare professional for clarification.
*   The more information you provide, the better equipped the physical therapist will be to understand the patient's needs and provide effective care.
