# Innovation Workshop Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Workshop Referral Form is designed to collect information about new ideas and initiatives that have the potential to drive business growth and development. This form helps us identify and evaluate new suggestions, and it's a great way to contribute to our Innovation Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** (if applicable) in the relevant fields.
2. Provide the **Referral Name** of the innovation or idea you're referring.
3. Choose whether this is a **Yes** or **No** for **Referral Type**, indicating whether it's a new idea or an updated version of an existing one.
4. Enter the source of the referral, such as a person or organization.
5. Enter your **Contact Phone** number and **Contact Email** so we can get in touch with you.
6. Select the **Nomination Status** of the innovation, whether it's **Active** or **Inactive**.
7. Enter the date and time of the nomination.
8. Provide a brief description of the innovation in **Nomination Notes** (optional).
9. Enter the name of the **Program Name** associated with the innovation.
10. Enter the name of the **Innovation**.
11. Enter a link to the **Innovation URL** if available.
12. Provide a brief description of the innovation in **Innovation Description**.
13. List the **Team Members** involved in the innovation.
14. (Optional) Enter more team members if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name is required for our records and will be used to address you.
* **Last Name**: Your last name is also required for our records and will be used to address you.
* **Email**: If applicable, your email address will be used to contact you about the innovation.
* **Referral Name**: The name of the innovation or idea you're referring.
* **Referral Type**: Indicate whether this is a new idea or an updated version of an existing one.
* **Referral Source**: The source of the referral, such as a person or organization.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Nomination Status**: The status of the nomination, whether it's **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **Nomination Date**: The date and time of the nomination.
* **Nomination Notes**: A brief description of the innovation.
* **Program Name**: The name of the program associated with the innovation.
* **Innovation Name**: The name of the innovation.
* **Innovation URL**: A link to the innovation if available.
* **Innovation Description**: A brief description of the innovation.
* **Team Members**: A list of team members involved in the innovation.
* **Team Member 1**, **Team Member 2**, **Team Member 3**, **Team Member 4**, **Team Member 5**, **Team Member 6**, **Team Member 7**, **Team Member 8**: Enter more team members if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about a field, please reach out to us for clarification.
* We appreciate your input and contributions to our Innovation Workshop!
