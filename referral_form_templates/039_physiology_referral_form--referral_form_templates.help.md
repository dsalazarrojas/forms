# Physiology Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physiology Referral Form is a comprehensive tool for documenting patient referrals to medical specialists, hospitals, or departments within a healthcare organization. It helps capture crucial information for efficient communication among clinicians and ensures that referrals are processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant options for "Patient Details" (Yes/No) to indicate whether the referral is for the patient.
2. Provide a brief description of the "Referral Reason" in the text field.
3. Choose the relevant medical specialties for "Medical Specialty" and "Referring Clinician" using the select options.
4. Enter the "Date of Referral" and "Expected Date of Result" in the respective date fields.
5. Select the required tests or procedures for "Test Ordered" using the select multiple options.
6. Enter the patient's current "Patient Condition", "Medical History", "Patient Allergies", and "Patient Notes" in the text fields.
7. Add any additional comments or instructions for "Test Comments" in the text field.
8. Specify the "Test Status" as "Active" or "Inactive" as required.
9. Sign and date the form with the "Physician Signature" and provide the current date and time.
10. Enter the "Hospital Name", "Hospital Location", "Department", "Phone", "Address", "Email", and "Department Phone" details.
11. Finally, confirm your contact information by providing the "Phone" and "Email" of the referring clinician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Physiological Referral Form** (`physiology_referral_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Patient Details** (`patient_details`): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the referral is for the patient.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`): Describe the reason for the referral in this text field.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`): Choose the relevant medical specialties for the referral.
* **Referring Clinician** (`referring_clinician`): Select the clinician referring the patient for further evaluation.
* **Date of Referral** (`date_of_referral`): Enter the date when the referral was made.
* **Expected Date of Result** (`expected_date_of_result`): Enter the expected date when the result of the referral will be available.
* **Test Ordered** (`test_ordered`): Select all the tests or procedures ordered for the patient.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`): Enter the patient's current medical condition.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Describe the patient's medical history.
* **Patient Allergies** (`patient_allergies`): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Test Comments** (`test_comments`): Add any additional comments or instructions for the test.
* **Patient Notes** (`patient_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the patient.
* **Test Status** (`test_status`): Specify the status of the test as "Active" or "Inactive".
* **Physician Signature** (`physician_signature`): Sign and date the form with your signature.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the current date when completing the form.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the current time when completing the form.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`): Enter the name of the hospital or department.
* **Hospital Location** (`hospital_location`): Enter the location of the hospital or department.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department name.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the referring clinician.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the hospital or department.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email of the referring clinician.
* **Department Phone** (`department_phone`): Enter the department phone number.
* **Hospital Fax** (`hospital_fax`): Not applicable as this field is not used in this form.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review the form carefully before sending it to the medical specialist or department.
* Verify that all selected options and entered information are accurate and relevant to the referral.
* Use a secure and reliable method to store and transmit the completed form.
* Keep a record of the form completion for future reference and audit trail purposes.
