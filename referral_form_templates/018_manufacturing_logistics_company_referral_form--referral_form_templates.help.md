# Manufacturing Logistics Company Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a referral from a manufacturing logistics company. It helps in facilitating efficient communication and processing of the referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Contact Information** section with your personal details.
2. In the **Company Information** section, provide details about the referred company.
3. In the **Contact Details** section, enter the contact information of the person being referred.
4. In the **Referral Details** section, describe the reason for the referral and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**:
  - **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your personal contact information so we can get back to you.
* **Company Information**:
  - **Company Information** (`company_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter details about the referred company.
* **Contact Details**:
  - **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional contact information for the referred company.
* **Referral Details**:
  - **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, `text`, required: false): Describe the reason behind the referral and any other relevant information.

## Tips
* Ensure you have a genuine reason for referring the company.
* Check that the referred company is not already a known business partner or collaborator.
* Double-check your contact information and company details for accuracy.
* Do not submit duplicate referrals for the same company.
