# Gaming Streaming Platform Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from creators who are referred to by other creators on the gaming streaming platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as a creator on the platform.
2. Answer the questions about your streaming platform preferences.
3. Provide your follow up time and any organized data you'd like to share.
4. Enter any faster follow up requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Creator**: <label> This is the field to enter your username or ID as a creator on the platform. This is required to identify you as a creator.
* **Streaming Platform**: <label> This is where you select which type of streaming platform you are affiliated with. This could be "Yes" for platforms like Twitch or "No" for platforms like YouTube. This field is optional.
* **Follow Up Time**: <label> This field is for entering the time you'd like to be followed up on. This will help the platform's team know when to reach out to you.
* **Organized Data**: <label> This is where you can provide any organized data you'd like to share with the platform's team. This could be relevant information about your streaming platform, such as your channel's statistics.
* **Faster Follow Ups**: <label> This field is for entering any faster follow up requests you may have. This could be if you need urgent support or have urgent questions. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure the platform's team can provide you with the best possible support.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the platform's support team.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can contact the platform's support team for clarification.
