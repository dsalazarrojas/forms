# Wedding Planner Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture referral information from potential clients interested in hiring a wedding planner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
3. Select the referral source from the options provided.
4. Choose whether the referral is for a new client, a new vendor, or both.
5. Enter a brief message about the referral in the "Referral Message" field.
6. Enter your own name as the wedding planner's name in the "Wedding Planner Name" field.
7. Enter your contact information in the "Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Planner Referral Form**: This is the title of the form.
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client you are referring.
* **Client Email**: Enter the email address of the client you are referring.
* **Referral Source**: Select where the client heard about you (Social Media, Online Search, Word of Mouth, etc.).
* **Event Date**: If you are referring a specific event, enter the date of the event.
* **Wedding Date**: Enter the date of the client's wedding.
* **Referral Type**: Select whether the referral is for a new client, a new vendor, or both.
* **Referral Message**: Enter a brief message about the client.
* **Wedding Planner Name**: Enter your name as the wedding planner.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information so the client can get in touch with you.
* **Website**: If applicable, enter your website so the client can learn more about you.
