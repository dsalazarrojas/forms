# Insurance Partner Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about insurance partners and their agents for referral purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field completely and accurately, as required.
2. Make sure to include all necessary information.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Your first name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Your last name, as it appears on your official documents.
* **Partner Type** (select_multiple):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are an insurance partner.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not an insurance partner.
* **Phone Number** (text): Your phone number, in the format of (###) ### ####.
* **Email** (email): Your official email address.
* **Insurance Carrier** (select_one):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are an insurance carrier partner.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not an insurance carrier partner.
* **Policy Number** (text): Your policy number, if applicable.
* **Agency Name** (text): The name of your agency.
* **Agency Address** (text): The address of your agency.
* **Agency Phone** (text): The phone number of your agency.
* **Agent Name** (text): The name of the agent.
* **Agent Contact Info** (text): The contact information of the agent.
* **Notes** (note): Additional information about your referral.
* **Follow-up Date** (date): The date you would like to be followed up on. 

Note: This form is only a guide, please review your answers carefully before submitting.
