# Business Marketing Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Marketing Referral Form is designed to capture information about a prospect referred to our marketing services from a known individual (referrer). The goal of this form is to provide a comprehensive snapshot of the prospect's marketing needs and challenges.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the referrer's contact information (name, email, phone).
2.  Enter the prospect's contact information (name, email, phone, company).
3.  Select the industry and company size of the prospect.
4.  Choose the marketing needs and pain points of the prospect.
5.  Indicate the prospect's decision timeline and decision maker status.
6.  Describe the prospect's current marketing situation and pain points.
7.  Indicate the relationship strength you have with the prospect.
8.  Select the source of how the prospect learned about us.
9.  Enter any additional notes or comments about the prospect.
10. Confirm or deny permission to mention your name when contacting the prospect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referrer Name** (`referrer_name`): Your full name as the referrer.
* **Referrer Email** (`referrer_email`): Your email address as the referrer.
* **Referrer Phone** (`referrer_phone`): Your phone number as the referrer.
* **Your Company** (`referrer_company`): The company you represent.
* **Prospect Name** (`prospect_name`): The full name of the prospect.
* **Prospect Title** (`prospect_title`): The job title of the prospect.
* **Prospect Company** (`prospect_company`): The name of the prospect's company.
* **Prospect Email** (`prospect_email`): The email address of the prospect.
* **Prospect Phone** (`prospect_phone`): The phone number of the prospect.
* **Prospect Industry** (`prospect_industry`): The industry of the prospect's company.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`): The number of employees at the prospect's company.
* **Marketing Needs Identified** (`marketing_needs`): Select all marketing needs you identified for the prospect.
* **Known Budget Range** (`budget_range`): The known budget range of the prospect.
* **Decision Timeline** (`decision_timeline`): When the prospect needs services by.
* **Decision Maker Status** (`decision_maker`): Is this person the primary decision maker.
* **Current Marketing Situation** (`current_marketing_situation`): Describe the current marketing situation of the prospect.
* **Pain Points Identified** (`pain_points`): Identify the marketing challenges the prospect is facing.
* **Relationship Strength** (`relationship_strength`): Indicate how well you know this prospect.
* **How Did Prospect Learn About Us** (`referral_source`): How did the prospect learn about our services.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`): Any other relevant information about the prospect.
* **Permission to Mention Your Name** (`follow_up_permission`): Can we mention your name when contacting the prospect.
