# X Ray Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the referral process for X Ray examinations, gathering crucial information about the patient and the referral context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the patient's name, date of birth, and referral date.
2. Select the doctor who referred the patient and their department.
3. Choose the patient's sex from the available options.
4. Select the medical reports required based on the patient's condition and the doctor's requirements.
5. Enter the name of the hospital where the X Ray examination will take place.
6. Enter the name of the doctor who will perform the examination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's full name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Referral Date** (`referral_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the referral was made.
* **Referring Doctor** (`referring_doctor`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the doctor who referred the patient.
* **Referring Doctor Department** (`referring_doctor_department`, `text`, required): Enter the department of the doctor who referred the patient.
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the patient's condition.
* **Referral Reason** (`referral_reason`, `text`, required): Specify the reason for the referral.
* **Patient Sex** (`patient_sex`, `select_one`, required): Choose the patient's sex from the available options.
* **Referral Status** (`referral_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the referral.
* **Medical Report** (`medical_report`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the medical reports required based on the patient's condition and the doctor's requirements.
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where the X Ray examination will take place.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who will perform the examination.
* **Doctor Department** (`doctor_department`, `text`, optional): Enter the department of the doctor who will perform the examination.
