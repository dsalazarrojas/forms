# Mental Health Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mental Health Referral Form is a tool used to collect information about individuals referred to mental health services. This form helps gather relevant details to facilitate efficient and effective referrals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "client_name" field.
2. Provide the client's phone number in the "client_phone" field.
3. Enter the client's email address in the "client_email" field.
4. Specify the reason for the referral in the "referral_reason" field.
5. Identify the source of the referral in the "referral_source" field.
6. Enter the client's date of birth (DOB) in the "client_dob" field.
7. Select the client's sex in the "client_sex" field.
8. Select the client's preferred language in the "preferred_language" fields.
9. Enter the date and time of the referral in the "referral_date" and "referral_time" fields.
10. Select the type of mental health professional(s) involved in the referral in the "mental_health_professional" fields.
11. Specify the client's status in the "client_status" field.
12. Select the mental health specialty(s) involved in the referral in the "mental_health_specialty" fields.
13. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "note" and "additional_comments" fields.
14. Describe the client's relationship with the referring professional in the "client_relationship" field.
15. Identify the person or organization referring the client in the "referral_by" and "referral_by_title" fields.
16. Select the referral status in the "mental_health_referral_status" field.
17. Enter any additional comments about the referral in the "mental_health_referral_comments" field.
18. Enter the date and time of the referral in the "mental_health_referral_date" and "mental_health_referral_time" fields.
19. Select whether the referral is urgent or non-urgent in the "mental_health_referral_form_24" field.
20. Select whether the client has been referred or not in the "mental_health_referral_form_25" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name (mental_health_referral_form_1)** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
- **Client Phone (mental_health_referral_form_2)** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter the client's phone number.
- **Client Email (mental_health_referral_form_3)** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
- **Referral Reason (mental_health_referral_form_4)** (`referral_reason`, text, required): Specify the reason for the referral.
- **Referral Source (mental_health_referral_form_5)** (`referral_source`, text, required): Identify the source of the referral.
- **Client Date of Birth (mental_health_referral_form_6)** (`client_dob`, date, required): Enter the client's date of birth.
- **Client Sex (mental_health_referral_form_7)** (`client_sex`, select_one, required): Select the client's sex (Male or Female).
- **Preferred Language (mental_health_referral_form_8)** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, required): Select the client's preferred language (English, Spanish, French).
- **Referral Date (mental_health_referral_form_9)** (`referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the referral.
- **Referral Time (mental_health_referral_form_10)** (`referral_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the referral.
- **Mental Health Professional (mental_health_referral_form_11)** (`mental_health_professional`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of mental health professional involved in the referral (Psychologist, Social Worker).
- **Client Status (mental_health_referral_form_12)** (`client_status`, select_one, required): Specify the client's status (Active, Inactive).
- **Mental Health Specialty (mental_health_referral_form_13)** (`mental_health_specialty`, select_multiple, required): Select the mental health specialty involved in the referral (Psychiatry, Psychology, Social Work).
- **Note (mental_health_referral_form_14)** (`note`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes.
- **Additional Comments (mental_health_referral_form_15)** (`additional_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the referral.
- **Client Relationship (mental_health_referral_form_16)** (`client_relationship`, text, required): Describe the client's relationship with the referring professional.
- **Referral By (mental_health_referral_form_17)** (`referral_by`, text, required): Identify the person or organization referring the client.
- **Referral By Title (mental_health_referral_form_18)** (`referral_by_title`, text, required): Identify the title of the person or organization referring the client.
- **Mental Health Referral Status (mental_health_referral_form_20)** (`mental_health_referral_status`, select_one, required): Select the referral status (Open, Closed, Declined).
- **Mental Health Referral Comments (mental_health_referral_form_21)** (`mental_health_referral_comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments about the referral.
- **Mental Health Referral Date (mental_health_referral_form_22)** (`mental_health_referral_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the referral.
- **Mental Health Referral Time (mental_health_referral_form_23)** (`mental_health_referral_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the referral.
- **Urgent Referral (mental_health_referral_form_24)** (`mental_health_referral_form_24`, select_one, required): Select whether the referral is urgent or non-urgent.
- **Client Referred (mental_health_referral_form_25)** (`mental_health_referral_form_25`, select_one, required): Select whether the client has been referred or not.
