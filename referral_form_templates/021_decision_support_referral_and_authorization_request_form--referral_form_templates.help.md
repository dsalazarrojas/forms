# Decision Support Referral And Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Decision Support Referral And Authorization Request Form is used to collect information about patients and their referral and authorization processes. This form helps streamline referral and authorization requests, making it easier for healthcare professionals and administrators to manage these tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "patient_name" field.
2. Select the referral date from the "referral_date" field.
3. Enter the doctor's name in the "doctor_name" field.
4. Provide the reason for the referral in the "reason" field.
5. Select the status of the referral in the "status" field.
6. Choose the department involved in the referral process in the "department" field.
7. Determine whether the referral is assigned to a specific department in the "assigned" field.
8. If applicable, select the tool assigned to the referral in the "assigned_tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **patient_name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name accurately to ensure proper identification.
* **referral_date** (date, required): Select the date of the referral for accurate record-keeping.
* **doctor_name** (text, required): Enter the doctor's name who is referring the patient.
* **reason** (text, required): Provide a clear and concise reason for the referral.
* **status** (select_one, required): Choose the status of the referral from the available options (Active or Inactive).
* **department** (select_multiple, required): Select all departments involved in the referral process.
* **assigned** (select_one, required): Determine whether the referral is assigned to a specific department.
* **assigned_tool** (text, optional): If applicable, select the tool assigned to the referral.

## Tips
- Double-check the patient's name and doctor's name for accuracy.
- Choose the correct status and department(s) for the referral.
- If the referral is not assigned, ensure that it's not mistakenly marked as "assigned".
- Use the "assigned_tool" field only if a tool is specifically assigned to the referral.
