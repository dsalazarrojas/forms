# Childcare Pediatric Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information for pediatric referrals from childcare centers. It helps in identifying concerns, providing detailed descriptions of the issues, and facilitating communication between caregivers and pediatric specialists. By completing this form, childcare centers can effectively refer children to the necessary services and ensure timely and appropriate interventions.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the **Referral Date** of the pediatric referral in the first field.
2.  Fill in the **Child First Name**, **Child Last Name**, and **Child Date of Birth** fields with the child's information.
3.  Select the child's **Child Gender** from the provided options.
4.  Complete the **Parent or Guardian First Name**, **Parent or Guardian Last Name**, and **Parent or Guardian Email** fields with the parent or guardian's details.
5.  Enter the **Childcare Center Name** and **Childcare Center Phone**.
6.  Provide the **Referring Staff Name**, **Referring Staff Role**, and their **Signature**.
7.  Select the primary **Referral Reason** from the provided options.
8.  Enter the **Referral Details** and **When Concerns First Observed**.
9.  Choose the **Concern Frequency**, **Concern Severity**, and **Impact on Daily Functioning**.
10. Describe the **Strategies Attempted** and their **Effectiveness**.
11. Indicate whether the child had previous **Evaluations** or **Services**.
12. List any **Parent or Guardian Concerns** and if they have given **Parent Consent for Referral**.
13. If desired, select a **Preferred Specialist or Clinic** for the referral.
14. Choose the **Urgency Level** and indicate the **Follow-Up Plan**.
15. Provide any **Additional Information** about the child.
16. Finally, type your full name as your electronic **Referring Staff Signature** and date it with the **Signature Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral Date**: Enter the date when this pediatric referral was made.
* **Referral Reason**: Choose the primary reason for this referral from the provided options.
* **Referral Details**: Provide a detailed description of the primary reason for this referral.
* **When Concerns First Observed**: Select when the concerns first became apparent.
* **Concern Frequency**: Choose how often these concerns are observed.
* **Concern Severity**: Rate the severity of these concerns.
* **Child First Name**: Enter the child's first name.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's last name.
* **Child Date of Birth**: Enter the child's birth date.
* **Child Age**: Enter the child's age in years.
* **Child Gender**: Select the child's gender.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's first name.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's last name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Childcare Center Name**: Enter the name of the childcare center referring the child.
* **Childcare Center Phone**: Enter the phone number of the childcare center referring the child.
* **Referring Staff Name**: Enter the name of the referring staff member.
* **Referring Staff Role**: Enter the role or title of the referring staff member.
* **Referral Details**: Provide detailed information about the referral reason.
* **Strategies Attempted**: Describe any strategies attempted to address these concerns.
* **Strategies Effectiveness**: Rate the effectiveness of the attempted strategies.
* **Previous Evaluations**: Indicate whether the child has had previous evaluations or assessments.
* **Previous Services**: Select any services the child has received.
* **Parent or Guardian Concerns**: List any concerns the parent or guardian may have.
* **Parent Consent for Referral**: Indicate whether the parent or guardian has consented to this referral.
* **Preferred Specialist or Clinic**: Select a preferred specialist or clinic for the referral (if desired).
* **Urgency Level**: Choose the urgency level of this referral.
* **Follow-Up Plan**: Describe the plan for follow-up after referral.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about the child.
* **Referring Staff Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Date your electronic signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete referrals.
- If unsure about any fields, consult with the referring staff or pediatric specialist.
- Double-check the entered information for accuracy and completeness.
- Be as specific as possible when filling in the fields.
- This form is intended to aid in effective communication between caregivers and pediatric specialists.
