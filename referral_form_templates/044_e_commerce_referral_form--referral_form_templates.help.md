# e_commerce_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_referral_form is designed to gather information about customers who refer other customers to an e-commerce business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your information in the provided fields.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Information 
 User Information (1): This section is used to collect basic details about the customer, such as their name and contact information.

* 1.1.  User Information: This field is where you will provide your name. Please enter your name to continue.

### 2. Contact Information 
 Contact Information (2): This section is used to collect additional contact details about the customer.

* 2.1.  Contact Information: Enter your contact email address and/or phone number here, so we can reach out to you with any updates.

### 3. Referral Information 
 Referral Information (3): This section is used to collect information about the person you are referring, such as their name and contact information.

* 3.1.  Referral Information: Enter the name and contact information of the person you are referring, so we can follow up with them.
