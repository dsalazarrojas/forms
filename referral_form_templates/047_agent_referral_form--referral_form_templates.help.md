# Agent Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for referrals from one agent to another. Fill it out for formal referrals.
## How To Complete This Form
To fill this form, follow the steps below:
1.  Select the referring agent and agency.
2.  Enter the referring agent's email for coordination and payment purposes.
3.  Provide the client's name.
4.  Enter the client's email address.
5.  Enter the client's phone number.
6.  Choose the type of referral service needed.
7.  Estimate the timeline for the client's need.
8.  Enter the agreed referral fee percentage.
9.  Confirm whether the client has been notified of this referral.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referring Agent Name**: Enter the name of the agent who is referring the client.
* **Referring Agency**: Enter the name of the agency or representative this agent works for.
* **Referring Agent Email**: Enter the referring agent's email address for communication and fee payment purposes.
* **Referred Client Name**: Enter the name of the client being referred.
* **Client Email Address**: Enter the email address of the client being referred.
* **Client Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the client being referred.
* **Type of Referral**: Select the type of service the client needs, e.g., Home Buyer, Home Seller, etc.
* **Estimated Timeline for Client Need**: Estimate the time frame the client is looking to meet their needs.
* **Agreed Referral Fee Percentage**: Enter the percentage of the gross commission agreed upon for the referral.
* **Has the Client Been Notified?**: Confirm whether the client has been informed of this referral.
