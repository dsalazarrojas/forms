# Property Utility Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a property and its utility services to facilitate referrals. It is meant to be completed by property managers to provide accurate and up-to-date information about their properties and tenants.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the Property Manager Information section with your details.
2.  Proceed to the Property Details section and enter the property's name and lease date.
3.  In the Utility Information section, provide details about the utilities provided to the property, including name, type, and status.
4.  Move on to the Tenant Information section and enter the tenant's name, date of birth, phone number, email, address, and zip code.
5.  In the Lease and Rent section, provide details about the lease term, rent, and lease status.
6.  Finally, add any additional comments about the property or tenant.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Property Manager Information
*   **Property Manager Name** (`property_manager_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property manager.
*   **Property Name** (`property_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the property.
*   **Lease Date** (`lease_date`, date, required): Enter the lease start date for the property.
### Property Details
*   **Utility 1** (`utility_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the first utility provided (e.g., electricity).
*   **Utility 1 Type** (`utility_1_type`, text, required): Enter the type of utility provided (e.g., electricity, water).
*   **Utility 1 Status** (`utility_1_status`, text, required): Indicate the status of the first utility (e.g., active, inactive).
*   **Utility 2** (`utility_2_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second utility provided (e.g., gas).
*   **Utility 2 Type** (`utility_2_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of second utility (e.g., gas, water).
*   **Utility 2 Status** (`utility_2_status`, text, optional): Indicate the status of the second utility (e.g., active, inactive).
### Tenant Information
*   **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tenant.
*   **Date of Birth** (`tenant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the tenant.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the tenant.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the tenant.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the address of the tenant.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of the tenant's address.
*   **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the tenant is a resident (Yes/No).
*   **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the tenant's address.
### Lease and Rent
*   **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date the tenant moved in.
*   **Lease Term** (`lease_term`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the lease is active (Yes/No).
*   **Rent** (`rent`, text, required): Enter the rent amount for the property.
*   **Lease Status** (`lease_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of the lease (Active/Inactive).
### Additional Information
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the property or tenant.
## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
*   Double-check the information entered before submitting the form.
*   Use clear and concise language when entering additional comments.
*   Review the form carefully to ensure all utilities and services are included.
