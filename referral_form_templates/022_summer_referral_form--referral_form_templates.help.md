# summer_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "summer_referral_form" is a form that collects information from users about their summer programs of interest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Info in the "User Info" field.
2. Provide your Referral Info in the "Referral Info" field.
3. Select the Summer Programs you are interested in from the "Summer Programs" field. You can choose more than one option.
4. Enter your Contact Info in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your user name or a unique identifier to help us identify your information.
* **Referral Info** (`text`, required: false): Please provide any referral information, such as a referral code or a name.
* **Summer Programs** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Please select the summer programs that you are interested in. Options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Contact Info** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your contact information, such as your name and email address.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out this form carefully and completely to ensure that we get the most accurate information about your preferences and needs.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
- You can choose multiple options for the "Summer Programs" field.
