# Electronics Repair Technician Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electronics Repair Technician Referral Form is used to collect information about electronics repair technician referrals. This form helps track the status of referrals and follow up with customers to ensure they are satisfied with the service received.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information about the referral.
2. Provide any additional details you think would be helpful for the referral follow-up process.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral**: Enter a brief description of the referral.
* **Technician**: Enter the name of the electronics repair technician who will be working with the customer.
* **Contact**: Enter the contact information for the customer, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the customer.
* **Referral Info**: Enter any relevant referral information, such as the customer's location.
* **Technician Referral**: Indicate whether or not the referral was made to a specific technician.
* **Form 7**: This field is not currently being used.
* **Referral Status**: Enter the status of the referral, such as "in progress", "completed", or "cancelled".
* **Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the referral.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Form 14**: Indicate whether or not the customer is a repeat referral source.
* **Technician Referral Info**: Enter any relevant information about the referral, such as the customer's comments.
* **Referral Source**: Indicate whether or not the referral was from a specific source.
* **Referral Method**: Enter the method used to make the referral, such as "in-person" or "email".
* **Referral Reason**: Enter the reason for the referral.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date of the referral.
* **Referral Time**: Enter the time of the referral.
* **Form 21**: This field is not currently being used.
* **Technician Referral Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the referral.
* **Referral Followup Date**: Enter the date to follow up with the customer.
* **Referral Followup Time**: Enter the time to follow up with the customer.
* **Followup Time**: Enter the time to follow up with the customer.

# Tips

* Be as thorough as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate and complete information is gathered.
* Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting it.
* Follow up with customers as needed to ensure they are satisfied with the service received.
