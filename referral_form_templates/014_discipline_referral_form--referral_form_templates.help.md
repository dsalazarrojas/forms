# Discipline Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discipline Referral Form is used to document incidents or behavior that may require disciplinary action. It is intended to gather information about the student's behavior, the circumstances surrounding the incident, and the actions taken by the teacher. This form helps maintain accurate and consistent records of student behavior and supports the decision-making process for disciplinary actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the Student Name (id: 1) and Student ID (id: 3) of the student involved.
2. Enter the Teacher Name (id: 2) of the teacher referring the student.
3. Provide a brief Reason for Referral (id: 4) explaining the incident or behavior that led to the referral.
4. Enter the Date (id: 11) and Time (id: 6) of the incident.
5. Describe the Details of Incident (id: 7) in as much detail as possible.
6. Select the Discipline Level (id: 9) that best describes the severity of the incident.
7. Enter the Action Taken (id: 8) by the teacher in response to the incident.
8. Sign and date the form with your Teacher Signature (id: 10) and Date (id: 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the student's name as it appears on their official records.
* **Teacher Name** (id: 2, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher submitting this referral.
* **Student ID** (id: 3, number, required: false): Enter the student's ID number as it appears on their records.
* **Reason for Referral** (id: 4, text, required: false): Briefly explain the reason for the referral, including the specific behavior or incident that led to the referral.
* **Date of Incident** (id: 5, date, required: false): Enter the date of the incident in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Incident** (id: 6, time, required: false): Enter the time of the incident in HH:MM:SS format.
* **Details of Incident** (id: 7, text, required: false): Provide a detailed description of what happened, including any relevant context or circumstances.
* **Action Taken** (id: 8, text, required: false): Describe the actions taken by the teacher in response to the incident.
* **Discipline Level** (id: 9, select_one, required: false): Select the level of discipline that is believed to be appropriate for the incident.
* **Teacher Signature** (id: 10, text, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm that you are the referring teacher.
* **Date** (id: 11, date, required: false): Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all necessary information before completing the form.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident or behavior.
* Select the most accurate Discipline Level for the incident.
* Sign and date the form to ensure accuracy and accountability.
