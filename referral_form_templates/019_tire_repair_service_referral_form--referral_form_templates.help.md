# Tire Repair Service Referral Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect referral data for the tire repair service. It is essential to understand that referrals are a valuable source of new business opportunities, and this form will help to track and follow up on these leads.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the referral data into the text area provided on the first page.
2. Provide your name, phone number, and email address on the second page.
3. If applicable, enter any referral details, such as the source of the referral and the lead status.
4. Set the follow-up date and time for the referral.
5. Assign the referral to a specific user.
6. Select the lead source from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Referral Form Data** (`referral_form_data`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the referral.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Referral Details** (`referral_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional details about the referral, such as the source and lead status.
* **Lead Status** (`lead_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the lead (Not Contacted, Contacted, Qualified).
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up_date`, date, optional): Set the date to follow up with the lead.
* **Follow-up Time** (`follow_up_time`, time, optional): Set the time to follow up with the lead.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the lead.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_multiple, optional): Select the user to assign the lead to.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, optional): Select the source of the lead (Online Search, Referral, Word of Mouth, Email Marketing, Print Ad).
