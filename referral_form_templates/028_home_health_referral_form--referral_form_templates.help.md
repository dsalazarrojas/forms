# Home Health Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from individuals or patients who require home health services. It helps healthcare professionals or social workers gather essential details for referral purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details as accurately as possible.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name**: A field to enter your first name.
*   **First Name** is a text field where you can enter your first name. It is not required, but please fill it out for reference purposes.

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: A field to enter your last name.
*   **Last Name** is a text field where you can enter your last name. It is not required, but please fill it out for reference purposes.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: A field to enter your date of birth.
*   **Date of Birth** is a date field where you can enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. It is not required.

### Phone
* **Phone**: A field to enter your phone number.
*   **Phone** is a text field where you can enter your phone number. It is not required.

### Email
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address.
*   **Email** is an email field where you can enter your email address. It is not required.

### Address
* **Address**: A field to enter your address.
*   **Address** is a text field where you can enter your address. It is not required.

### Zip Code
* **Zip Code**: A field to enter your zip code.
*   **Zip Code** is a number field where you can enter your zip code. It is not required.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the healthcare professional or social worker assisting you.
* The submitted form is for referral purposes only and will not be shared or used without consent.
