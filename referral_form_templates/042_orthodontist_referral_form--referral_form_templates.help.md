## Orthodontist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Orthodontist Referral Form is a tool used by referring parties (e.g., dentists or doctors) to refer patients to an orthodontist for treatment. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that the patient receives the best possible care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by ensuring that you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the patient's personal details, including their first and last name, contact phone number, email, and address.
3. Provide the patient's name and address of the office they are being referred to.
4. Fill in the insurance provider, policy, and group number if applicable.
5. Attach an insurance card if available.
6. Describe the treatment plan for the patient.
7. Sign and date the form with your doctor's signature.
8. Have the patient sign and date the form with their signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the patient's contact phone number.
* **Email** (text, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their ID or insurance card.
* **Dentist Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the dentist who referred the patient.
* **Office Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the office the patient is being referred to.
* **Address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the office the patient is being referred to.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter the city of the office the patient is being referred to.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter the state of the office the patient is being referred to.
* **Zip** (text, optional): Enter the zip code of the office the patient is being referred to.
* **Fax Number** (text, optional): Enter the fax number of the office the patient is being referred to.
* **Insurance Provider** (text, optional): Enter the insurance provider's name.
* **Insurance Policy** (text, optional): Enter the insurance policy number.
* **Insurance Group** (text, optional): Enter the insurance group number.
* **Insurance Card** (text, optional): Attach a copy of the insurance card.
* **Treatment Plan** (text, optional): Describe the treatment plan for the patient.
* **Doctor Signature** (note, required): Sign and date this field with your doctor's signature.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the referral.
* **Patient Signature** (note, required): Sign and date this field with the patient's signature.
* **Patient DOB** (date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Phone** (text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Patient Email** (text, optional): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the patient.

Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields and attached all necessary documents before submitting the form.
