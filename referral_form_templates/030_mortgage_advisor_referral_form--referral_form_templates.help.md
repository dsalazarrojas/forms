# Mortgage Advisor Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mortgage Advisor Referral Form is a tool to collect information about a potential mortgage advisor referral. The form is used to gather details about the client, their contact information, property, and other relevant details. This information will help the mortgage advisor to evaluate the potential referral opportunity and decide on the next course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Provide the property information, such as address and type.
3. Enter referral information, including the name of the person or organization referring the client.
4. Add any follow-up comments or details about the referral.
5. Indicate the preferred method of contact for the mortgage advisor.
6. Specify the source of the referral lead.
7. Update the status of the referral lead.
8. Provide any additional mortgage information relevant to the referral.
9. Add any extra details about the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (1)**: This field is used to collect the client's name and contact details.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter the contact information of the person who referred the client.
* **Property Info (3)**: Provide details about the property involved in the referral.
* **Referral Info (4)**: Enter the name of the person or organization who referred the client.
* **Follow Up (5)**: Add any follow-up comments or details about the referral.
* **Contact Preference (6)**: Specify the preferred method of contact for the mortgage advisor.
* **Lead Source (7)**: Indicate the source of the referral lead.
* **Lead Status (8)**: Update the status of the referral lead.
* **Mortgage Info (9)**: Provide any additional mortgage information relevant to the referral.
* **Lead Details (10)**: Add any extra details about the referral.
