# Pediatrician Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer patients to other healthcare professionals for further evaluation or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you only complete this form once for each referral.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or conditions.
* **Diagnosis** (`diagnosis`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the patient's current diagnosis (e.g., Asthma, Allergies, ADHD, Autism, Cancer).
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's contact information, including their address and phone number.
* **Medical Team** (`medical_team`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the healthcare professionals currently treating the patient (e.g., Family Member, General Practitioner, Specialist).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the patient's condition.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the referral.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the referral.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for reference.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the healthcare professional being referred to.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the healthcare professional who will be handling the referral (e.g., Doctor, Nurse).
* **Assigned At** (`assigned_at`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time the referral was assigned to.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the deadline for completing the referral.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `select_one`, required/optional): Set the priority level of the referral (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
* **Status** (`status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Set the status of the referral (e.g., New, In Progress, Done).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the referral.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the patient's date of birth.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the patient's address.
* **Physician Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the referring physician.
* **Physician Address** (`physician_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address as the referring physician.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number as the referring physician.

## Tips

* Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.
* Ensure you only submit the form once for each referral.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the referral coordinator or administrator.
