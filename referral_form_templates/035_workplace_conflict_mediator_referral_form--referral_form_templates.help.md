# Workplace Conflict Mediator Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for workplace conflict mediation referrals. It helps facilitate the referral process for potential workplace conflicts that may require mediation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. The first page, "workplace conflict mediator referral form", is optional. If you have additional information to provide, please fill it out. Otherwise, you can skip it.
2. On the second page, "mediator referral form", select the mediator that you believe is best suited for the conflict resolution process from the available options. This is a required field.
3. On the third page, "workplace conflict mediator form", select all applicable mediators that you want to include in the conflict resolution process. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mediator 1 (Select One)**: Select the mediator that you believe is best suited for the conflict resolution process.
* **Mediator 2 (Select Multiple)**: Select all applicable mediators that you want to include in the conflict resolution process.
* **Mediator 3 (Select One)**: Select an alternative mediator that may be beneficial for the conflict resolution process.

## Tips
Please note that this form is used to gather information for potential workplace conflicts that may require mediation. Ensure that the selected mediators are relevant to the specific conflict resolution process. If you're unsure about any part of the form, please refer to your supervisor or a relevant expert.
