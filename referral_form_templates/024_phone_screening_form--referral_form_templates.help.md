# phone_screening_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a job candidate, including their contact details, job details, and other relevant background information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what each section is asking for.
2. Fill out each section with the required information.
3. Be accurate and thorough in your answers.
4. If a field is marked as required, please ensure that it is completed.
5. If a field is marked as optional, you may leave it blank if not applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact info** (contact_info, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the candidate's contact information, such as name, phone number, and email address.
* **Candidate info** (candidate_info, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering additional information about the candidate, such as their job title, job location, and job type.
* **Job details** (job_details, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering specific details about the job the candidate is applying for, such as job title, job description, and job requirements.
* **Contact history** (contact_history, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the candidate's contact history, including previous employers, job titles, and employment dates.
* **Job applicant** (job_applicant, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the candidate's job applicant information, such as job seeker status and job type.
* **Work experience** (work_experience, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the candidate's work experience, including previous job titles, employers, and employment dates.
* **Education** (education, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the candidate's educational background, including degrees earned, institutions attended, and relevant courses or subjects studied.
* **Skills** (skills, text, optional)
    + This section is for listing the candidate's relevant skills or abilities, such as programming languages, software proficiency, or language proficiency.
* **Languages** (languages, text, optional)
    + This section is for listing the candidate's languages spoken or written, including proficiency levels.
* **References** (references, text, optional)
    + This section is for listing the candidate's professional references, including names, job titles, and contact information.
* **Medical history** (medical_history, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering any relevant medical information, including any disabilities or health conditions that may impact employment.
* **Emergency contacts** (emergency_contacts, text, optional)
    + This section is for listing the candidate's emergency contacts, including names, phone numbers, and relationships.
* **Contact details** (contact_details, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the candidate's contact details, such as address, phone number, and email address.
* **Job status** (job_status, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the candidate's job status, such as job seeker status and job type.
* **Job type** (job_type, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the type of job the candidate is applying for.
* **Job location** (job_location, text, optional)
    + This section is for entering the location of the job the candidate is applying for.

## Tips

* Please answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please leave it blank and ask for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the hiring team at form@gic.mx.
