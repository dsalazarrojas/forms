# nursery_year_end_feedback_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nursery year end feedback survey is designed to gather your feedback about your experience in our nursery school. Your input will help us improve our services and better care for our students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. Use the options provided for select fields, or provide a free-text answer if the field is not restricted.
3. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience**: A space for you to provide your overall experience with the nursery school. You can type in any comments or feedback you have.
* **Teaching Staff**: Please rate your experience with the teaching staff using the scale:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Average
	+ 4: Good
* **Facilities and Resources**: Please select all the facilities and resources that we have provided that you have used during the year. You can select multiple options.
	+ 1: Not available or not used
	+ 2: Occasionally used
	+ 3: Frequently used
	+ 4: Excellent quality
* **Communication**: A space for you to provide any feedback about our communication with you and your child.
* **Suggestions and Improvements**: A space for you to suggest any improvements we can make to our services.
* **Overall Rating**: Please rate your overall experience with the nursery school using the scale:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Average
	+ 4: Good
* **Care and Attention**: Please rate your experience with the care and attention given to your child using the scale:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Average
	+ 4: Good
* **Teacher-Student Relationship**: Please rate your experience with the teacher-child relationship using the scale:
	+ 1: Very poor
	+ 2: Poor
	+ 3: Average
	+ 4: Good
* **Feedback**: A space for you to provide any additional feedback about your experience with the nursery school.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number**: A space for you to enter your phone number.
* **Comments**: A space for you to provide any additional comments about your experience with the nursery school.
