# Egi's Form 1 - Help Guide
## Purpose
Egi's Form 1 is a form designed to collect data from users on two main topics: consent and feedback. This form is intended for schools to use as a template for their own forms, and it's open-source, allowing for modification and distribution under Creative Commons licensing.

This form is not affiliated with any specific form platforms, and it was generated using AI, ensuring that it's an original adaptation of public survey topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete Egi's Form 1, follow these steps:

1.  The first page of the form is called "Consent Form". This page is required for every user. Please fill out the form fields as accurately as possible.
2.  The second page of the form is called "Main Questionnaire". Similar to the first page, this page is also mandatory for every user.
3.  The third page of the form is called "Feedback Form" (optional). This page is for users who wish to provide additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Consent Form (page 1)
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, `text`, required)
    This field is where you provide your consent to participate in a study or activity at the school. Please write your consent in this text area.


### Main Questionnaire (page 2)
* **Main Questionnaire** (`main_questionnaire`, `text`, required)
    This section is intended for users to answer the main questions of the form. Please provide your answers as accurately as possible in this text area.


### Feedback Form (page 3, optional)
* **Feedback Form** (`feedback_form`, `text`, optional)
    If you have any additional comments or feedback about the form or the school, please use this text area to provide it.

## Tips
If you're having trouble completing any of the form pages, please contact us at form@gic.mx, and we'll be happy to assist you. We appreciate your feedback and look forward to helping you better.
