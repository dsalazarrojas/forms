# Student Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback from students about their overall experience at the school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by giving your feedback about your experience with our school. (Introduction)
2. Enter the name of the school you attended. (School Name)
3. Enter the name of the course you were taking. (Course Name)
4. Rate your overall experience with the school on a scale of 1-5. (How would you rate your overall experience with our school?)
5. Share any suggestions or comments you have about your experience. (Any suggestions or comments?)
6. Choose how likely you are to recommend this school to a friend (How likely are you to recommend this school to a friend?)
7. Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you. (What is your email address?)
8. Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information. (What is your phone number?)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, text, optional): This is where you can start by giving your feedback about your experience with our school.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the school you attended.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you were taking.
* **How would you rate your overall experience with our school?** (`rating`, number, optional): Rate your experience on a scale of 1-5.
* **Any suggestions or comments?** (`suggestion`, text, optional): Share any suggestions or comments you have about your experience.
* **How likely are you to recommend this school to a friend?** (`feedback`, select_one, optional): Choose how likely you are to recommend this school to a friend.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for any further information.
