# educational_leadership_culture_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "educational_leadership_culture_survey" form is designed to assess the leadership culture and climate of a school. It is used to gather feedback on how teachers perceive their school's leadership, communication, and decision-making processes. This form is not required for all users, and responses are not tracked individually.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Introduction**: Please provide a brief overview of your thoughts about the school's leadership climate.
2. **Leadership Climate**: Select the level of agreement you have with the school's leadership climate:
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
3. **Teacher Satisfaction**: If you are a teacher, please indicate your level of satisfaction with your role and responsibilities:
	* (Number input field. Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 10)
4. **Regular Feedback Frequency**: Indicate how frequently you receive regular feedback:
	* Daily
	* Weekly
	* Monthly
	* Quarterly
5. **Regular Communication**: Select the level of agreement you have with the regular communication you receive:
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
6. **Regular Feedback Sources**: Select all the sources from which you regularly receive feedback:
	* Supervisor
	* Colleague
	* Department Head
	* Principal
7. **Action Plans**: If applicable, please select all the action plans you are aware of:
	* Yes
	* No
8. **Feedback Quality**: Select the level of agreement you have with the quality of the feedback you receive:
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
9. **Decision Making**: Select the level of agreement you have with the school's decision making process:
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor
10. **Trust**: Select the level of agreement you have with the level of trust you have with the school's leadership:
	* Very Good
	* Good
	* Fair
	* Poor

## Tips
- If you are not a teacher, please skip questions that are not applicable to you.
- Please answer truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to add them in the "Introduction" field.
- This form is intended to be completed anonymously, so please do not include your name or any other identifying information.
