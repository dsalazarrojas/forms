# gentrification_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information on users' opinions and experiences with gentrification. We aim to understand users' awareness, attitudes, and concerns about gentrification, and how they perceive its consequences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. For multiple-choice questions, select all applicable options that apply to your experiences and opinions.
4. For open-ended questions, provide detailed and thoughtful responses.
5. If you have any additional comments or information that you'd like to share, feel free to add them in the space provided.
