# Data Driven Decision Making In Schools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information on the current state of data-driven decision making practices in schools. It will help the school administrators understand the prevalence and challenges of data-driven decision making in their schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply from the options on each question page.
2. Answer all questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: School Survey
* **School Survey** (`school_survey`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ This question asks if data-driven decision making is currently implemented in your school.
### Page 2: What is Data Driven Decision Making?
* **What is Data Driven Decision Making?** (`school_survey_question_1`, `text`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to describe data-driven decision making in your own words.
### Page 3: What is the Importance of Data Driven Decision Making?
* **What is the Importance of Data Driven Decision Making?** (`school_survey_question_2`, `number`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to rate the importance of data-driven decision making on a scale (e.g., 1-10).
### Page 4: How Do You Use Data Driven Decision Making?
* **How Do You Use Data Driven Decision Making?** (`school_survey_question_3`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to choose one way you use data-driven decision making in your school.
### Page 5: What Are the Barriers to Data Driven Decision Making?
* **What Are the Barriers to Data Driven Decision Making?** (`school_survey_question_4`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to select all the barriers that hinder data-driven decision making in your school.
### Page 6: What Support Do You Need to Improve Data Driven Decision Making?
* **What Support Do You Need to Improve Data Driven Decision Making?** (`school_survey_question_5`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to select all the support you need to improve data-driven decision making in your school.
### Page 7: School Survey
* **School Survey** (`school_survey_question_6`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ This question asks if there are any additional comments or concerns about data-driven decision making in your school.
### Page 8: School Survey
* **School Survey** (`school_survey_question_7`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ This question asks you to select all the areas where data-driven decision making is used in your school.
