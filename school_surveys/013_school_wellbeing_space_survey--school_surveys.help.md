# School Wellbeing Space Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a survey designed to gather feedback from students, staff, and faculty about their experiences with our school's wellbeing spaces. The survey aims to understand how often you use our wellbeing spaces, your level of satisfaction with them, and areas for improvement. Your input will help us to better understand your needs and make informed decisions to enhance the quality of our wellbeing spaces.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction and take a few minutes to answer the questions carefully.
2. Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Use the available options for each question and select all that apply.
4. You can submit any additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" and "Other comments" fields.
5. Fill in your contact information (email and name) so we can get back to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: Please take a few minutes to read this introduction carefully. It will give you an idea of what to expect from the survey.
* **Frequency**: How often do you use our wellbeing spaces? Select the option that best describes your frequency of use.
	+ Daily
	+ 1-2 times a week
	+ 3-4 times a week
	+ Rarely
* **Satisfaction**: How satisfied are you with our wellbeing spaces? Choose the option that reflects your level of satisfaction.
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Somewhat satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Improvement Areas**: Select all the areas you would like to see improved in our wellbeing spaces.
	+ More resources
	+ More space
	+ Better facilities
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Support Services**: Are support services available within the wellbeing spaces? Select all that apply.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
* **E-mail**: Please enter your email address so we can get back to you.
* **Name**: Enter your name for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Other comments**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please provide them here.
