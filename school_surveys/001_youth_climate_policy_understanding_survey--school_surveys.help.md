# Youth Climate Policy Understanding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to collect information about your understanding and awareness of climate policy. Your responses will help us better understand your thoughts, attitudes, and concerns related to climate change and policies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question based on your personal experiences and opinions.
2. Select the options that best match your thoughts on each question.
3. If you have any comments or suggestions, feel free to type them in the "Any Comments" field.
4. Please take approximately 5-10 minutes to complete this survey.
