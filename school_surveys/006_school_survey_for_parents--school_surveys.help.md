# School Survey For Parents - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents about their relationship with their child's school. The data collected will help the school administrators to understand the level of involvement and satisfaction of parents, as well as their ability to communicate with teachers, their level of support from the school, and their overall satisfaction with the school.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
- For questions that allow multiple answers, please select all that apply.
- For questions that ask for a specific method of communication, please select the one that is most applicable to you.
- For questions that ask for a numerical value (e.g., how often do you visit the school?), please answer with a specific number (e.g., 1-5 times per week).
- Please do not leave any fields blank unless you are unsure or do not have an opinion.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your relationship with your child?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to understand if you are a parent or guardian of the child.
* **How often do you visit your child's school?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to understand how frequently you interact with the school.
* **How well do you know your child's teacher?** (`number`): This question is to assess your level of knowledge about your child's teacher.
* **How much support do you receive from your child's school?** (`text`): This question is to understand the level of support you receive from the school.
* **What methods do you most reliably use to communicate with your child's school?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to understand your preferred method of communication with the school.
* **How did you get informed about the school's programs or events?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to understand how you stay informed about the school's activities.
* **How satisfied are you with the school?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to assess your overall satisfaction with the school.
* **What is the main challenge facing your child in school?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to understand any difficulties your child may be facing in school.
* **Would you recommend this school to others?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to understand if you would recommend the school to other parents.
* **Do you have another child attending the school?** (`number`): This question is to understand if you have multiple children enrolled in the school.
* **What is your child's grade level?** (`number`): This question is to understand the grade level of your child.
* **What is your educational background?** (`text`): This question is to understand your level of education.
* **How much do you agree with the following statement?** (`Yes`, `No`): This question is to understand your level of agreement with a specific statement.
* **Do you have any other comments?** (`text`): This question is to provide any additional feedback or comments.
