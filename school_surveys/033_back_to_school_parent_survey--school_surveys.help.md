# Back To School Parent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Back To School Parent Survey" is designed to gather your thoughts, concerns, and expectations for the upcoming school year. This information will help us understand your needs and provide a better experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address.
2. Provide your child's name.
3. Select your child's current grade level.
4. Rate your remote learning experience.
5. Indicate your child's readiness to return to school.
6. Rate your own readiness to return to school.
7. Select any areas of concern regarding your child's learning.
8. Indicate your level of concern for school safety.
9. Rate your satisfaction with health protocols.
10. Choose your preferred method of communication.
11. Select your desired level of involvement.
12. Specify the support you can provide at home.
13. List the devices your child has access to.
14. Rate your internet reliability.
15. (Optional) Share your biggest challenge for the upcoming year.
16. (Optional) Specify any support you need from the school.
17. (Optional) Share your opinion on school reopening.
18. (Optional) Provide any additional feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number. If not, leave this field blank.
- **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your child's full name.
- **Current Grade Level** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, required): Select your child's current grade level from the list.
- **Remote Learning Experience Rating** (`remote_learning_experience`, `select_one`, required): Rate your experience with remote learning.
- **Child Readiness for School** (`child_readiness`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how ready your child is to return to school.
- **Parent Readiness for School Year** (`parent_readiness`, `select_one`, required): Rate your own readiness to return to school.
- **Areas of Concern** (`learning_concerns`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any areas of concern regarding your child's learning.
- **School Safety Concerns** (`school_safety_concerns`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of concern for school safety.
- **Health Protocol Satisfaction** (`health_protocols`, `select_one`, required): Rate your satisfaction with health measures.
- **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication_preference`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how you would prefer to be contacted by the school.
- **Desired Involvement Level** (`involvement_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired level of involvement in your child's school experience.
- **Support Available at Home** (`support_at_home`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the support you can provide at home.
- **Technology Access at Home** (`technology_access`, `select_multiple`, required): List the devices your child has access to.
- **Internet Reliability** (`internet_reliability`, `select_one`, required): Rate your internet reliability.
- **Biggest Challenge** (`biggest_challenge`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Share your biggest challenge for the upcoming year.
- **Support Needed from School** (`school_support_needed`, `text`, optional): (Optional) Specify any support you need from the school.
- **Opinion on School Reopening** (`reopening_opinion`, `select_one`, required): Select your opinion on the school reopening.
- **Additional Feedback** (`additional_feedback`, `text`, optional): Share any additional thoughts or suggestions.

## Tips
- Make sure to review and submit the form carefully.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
