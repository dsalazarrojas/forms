# kids_career_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kids_career_survey form is designed to collect information about the career aspirations and preferences of students. It will help us understand their career choices, interests, and satisfaction levels in various aspects of their job.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have a genuine interest in exploring your future career options.
2. Select all the options that apply to you from each field.
3. Enter your answers in the required fields (marked as "required").
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What will they choose (page 1)
* **What will they choose**: This field is asking about the career choices you are likely to make in the future. Please select all the options that apply to you.

### Career choices (page 1)
* **Career choices**: This field is asking about your current career choices. Please select the one that best describes your current career path.

### Age
* **age**: This field is asking about your age. Please enter a number to indicate your age.

### Education level (page 2)
* **Education level**: This field is asking about your current education level. Please select the one that best describes your current education status.

### Career interests (page 2)
* **Career interests**: This field is asking about your career interests. Please select all the options that apply to you.

### Job satisfaction (page 3)
* **Job satisfaction**: This field is asking about your job satisfaction level. Please select the one that best describes your current job satisfaction level.

### Job fulfillment (page 3)
* **Job fulfillment**: This field is asking about your job fulfillment level. Please select the one that best describes your current job fulfillment level.

### Career growth (page 3)
* **Career growth**: This field is asking about your career growth potential. Please select the one that best describes your career growth prospects.

### Future plans (page 4)
* **Future plans**: This field is asking about your future career plans. Please enter a short description of your future plans.

### Job facts (page 4)
* **Job facts**: This field is asking about your job-related facts. Please select all the options that apply to you.

### Job environment (page 4)
* **Job environment**: This field is asking about your job environment preferences. Please select the one that best describes your desired work environment.

Please note that some fields may not be required, but it's still important to complete the form accurately and honestly. If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
