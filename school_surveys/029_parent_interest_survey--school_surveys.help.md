# Parent Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from parents about their child's interests, extracurricular activities, and contact details. The data collected will help us create a more comprehensive profile of each student, enabling us to better understand their needs and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your child's information.
2. For the `School Interests` field, select all the activities your child is interested in.
3. For the `Extracurriculars` field, select all the activities your child is involved in.
4. Enter your contact information in the corresponding fields, including `Contact Name`, `Email`, and `Phone`.
5. Enter your contact address and other details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Interest** (`parent_interest_survey_1`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your child's interests.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **School Interests** (`school_interests`, select_one, optional): Select all the interests your child is involved in, such as Band, Choir, Drama, Sports, Dance, Clubs/Organizations, Art, Music.
* **Extracurriculars** (`extracurriculars`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the extracurricular activities your child is involved in.
* **Why Survey** (`why_survey`, note, optional): Provide a brief explanation for why your child is interested in these activities.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **City** (`contact_city`, text, optional): Enter your city or town.
* **State** (`contact_state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Country** (`contact_country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, optional): Enter your child's school ID (if applicable).
* **School Survey ID** (`school_survey_id`, text, optional): Enter your child's school survey ID (if applicable).
* **Date Surveyed** (`date_surveyed`, date, optional): Enter the date you completed this survey.
* **Time Surveyed** (`time_surveyed`, time, optional): Enter the time you completed this survey.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance. We appreciate your participation in helping us create a more comprehensive profile of each student.
