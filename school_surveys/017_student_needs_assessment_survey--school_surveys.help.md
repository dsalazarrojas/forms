# Student Needs Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about students' needs and experiences at school. By completing this form, you will help your school better understand what you require and need to support you in your academic and personal development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what it's asking for.
2. Start by filling in the "School Survey" page.
3. Move on to the "Student Survey" page.
4. Complete the "Academic Survey" page.
5. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Survey**: This page gathers information about your school experience. Answer the questions based on your current school.
* **Student Survey**: This page collects data about your personal and demographic information. Please answer accurately to help us get to know you better.
* **Academic Survey**: This page focuses on your academic performance and needs. Be honest when answering, as this information will help us tailor support to your academic requirements.

## Tips
- Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any question, don't hesitate to contact the form author for clarification.
- Please take your time and complete the form carefully, as it's designed to help us better understand your needs.
