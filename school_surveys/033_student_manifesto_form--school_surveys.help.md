# Student Manifesto Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Manifesto Form is designed to gather information about a student's beliefs, goals, and aspirations, as well as their values, behaviors, and interests. The form's 12 fields cover various aspects of a student's life, from their emotional state to their career objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Answer all fields, as each one is optional (although some may be marked as "required" in some contexts).
2.  For fields with multiple options (e.g., select_one, select_multiple), choose the response that best represents your answer.
3.  For text fields, provide a brief and clear answer.
4.  If you're unsure about an option or question, feel free to ask for clarification or assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Surveys** (`school_survey`, text, not required): This field is for any comments or feedback you have about your school or academic experience.
* **Student Beliefs** (`student_beliefs`, select_one, not required): Choose how happy you are with your life.
* **Personal Goals** (`personal_goals`, text, not required): Describe your short-term and long-term personal goals.
* **Student Aspirations** (`student_aspirations`, select_multiple, not required): Select all the career goals that appeal to you.
* **Student Values** (`student_values`, time, not required): How important is it for you to prioritize time for your activities?
* **Student Behaviors** (`student_behaviors`, date, not required): Record the date of your last regular check-up or medical appointment.
* **Student Purpose** (`student_purpose`, text, not required): What drives your actions and decisions? 
* **School Experience** (`school_experience`, select_one, not required): How would you rate your school experience?
* **Student Attitudes** (`student_attitudes`, select_multiple, not required): Select your general attitude towards life.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, select_one, not required): What activities do you enjoy doing in your free time? 
* **Student Hobbies** (`student_hobbies`, select_multiple, not required): Choose all your favorite hobbies. 
* **Student Career Goals** (`student_career_goals`, text, not required): Describe your career objectives. 

Remember, all fields are optional, so please answer honestly and completely, according to your needs and preferences. If you need help or have questions, feel free to ask.
