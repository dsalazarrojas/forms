# School Transport Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Transport Survey is a form used to collect information about school transportation services. It helps in understanding the availability and effectiveness of these services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in the required information for each field, if any.
- Select the relevant options for the "category" field.
- Enter your description, if you have any comments or details about the school transportation services.
- Select the assigned tools or equipment for the "assigned_tool" field.
- Select the relevant options for the "school_transport_survey_1" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_transport_survey** (`school_transport_survey_form`, text, optional): This field is used to enter any additional information about the school transportation services. You can provide any comments or details about the services.
* **category** (`category`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select the category of the school transportation services. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the service is available or not.
* **description** (`description`, text, optional): This field is used to provide a detailed description of the school transportation services, if any.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select the assigned tools or equipment for the school transportation services. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the tools are available or not.
* **school transport survey** (`school_transport_survey_1`, select_multiple, optional): This field is used to select the school transportation services, similar to the "category" field. You can choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the service is available or not.
