# High School Senior Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from high school seniors about their academic and extracurricular experiences, interests, and plans after graduation. It is intended to help schools understand the strengths, challenges, and interests of their students.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select your school year from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your grade point average (GPA) on a scale of 0-4.0.
4. Rate your academic commitment to your high school on a scale of 1-10.
5. Select up to 3 teachers who are most influential to you.
6. Choose one extracurricular activity that you participate in.
7. Indicate if you plan to attend college.
8. Select all college plans that apply to you.
9. Enter the frequency of your participation in school events.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Year**: Select the year you are currently a senior in high school.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your high school records.
* **Grade Point Average**: Enter your cumulative GPA, on a scale of 0-4.0.
* **Academic Commitment**: Rate your academic commitment to your high school on a scale of 1-10.
* **Teachers**: Select up to 3 teachers who have had a significant influence on your high school experience.
* **Extracurricular Activities**: Choose one extracurricular activity that you participate in, such as sports, band, or choir.
* **College Plans**: Indicate if you plan to attend college, and select all college plans that apply to you.
* **College Planned Attendance**: Indicate if you plan to attend college, and choose all plans that apply.
* **High School Experience**: Enter the frequency of your participation in school events, such as events, clubs, or sports.
