# Sunday School Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by teachers, administrators, or parents to assess the performance of Sunday school teachers, gather feedback, and record comments or suggestions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the evaluation form on this page.
2. Enter your name or title in the "Teacher name" field.
3. Add any comments or feedback you'd like to share with the teacher in the "Comments" field.
4. Optionally, provide more detailed feedback in the "Feedback" field.
5. Submit your evaluation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher name** (Teacher name, text, required/optional): Enter the name or title of the teacher you are evaluating. This helps the teacher know who is providing feedback about their performance.
- **Comments** (Comments, text, required/optional): Write any comments or suggestions you have about the teacher's performance. This is your chance to share your thoughts and help the teacher improve.
- **Feedback** (Feedback, text, required/optional): If you have more detailed feedback or suggestions, you can type them in this field. This helps the teacher understand areas of improvement and how to grow as an educator.
- **Evaluation form** (Evaluation form, text, required/optional): This field is for the form itself, and it's automatically generated based on your input. You can ignore this field as it's not something you should fill out.
