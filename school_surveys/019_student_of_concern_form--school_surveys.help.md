# Student Of Concern Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Of Concern Form is used to document and report incidents or concerns that may affect a student's academic success or well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you have a legitimate reason to complete the form, and that it's not for a duplicate concern.
*   Select the page that best matches the concern you're reporting.
*   Fill in the required and optional fields as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Of Concern Form (Page 1)
*   **Student Of Concern Form**: This is the main page of the form, where you can briefly describe the concern or incident.
*   **Additional Information (Page 2)**: Provide any additional details about the concern, such as dates, times, or descriptions.
*   **Supporting Information (Page 3)**: Attach any supporting documents or files related to the concern.
*   **Student (Page 4)**: Select the student's gender to ensure accurate reporting.
*   **Date of Event (Page 5)**: Enter the date of the incident or concern, if known.

## Tips
*   Be thorough and clear in your description to ensure accurate reporting.
*   Use supporting documents to help justify the concern.
*   If unsure about any information, contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
