# School Curriculum Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect feedback on the effectiveness and quality of the school's curriculum. Your input will help the school administration understand what works well and what can be improved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and select the fields that apply to you.
2. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
3. Provide specific examples or examples when possible.
4. Don't forget to include any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you think about our school curriculum?** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This is an open-ended field where you can share your overall thoughts about the curriculum.
* **How effective do you think this curriculum is?** (`overall_effectiveness`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Please select from the scale of Highly effective, Somewhat effective, Neutral, Somewhat ineffective, Highly ineffective.
* **Which subjects do you think are taught well?** (`subject_specific_feedback`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select the subjects that you think are taught well, such as Mathematics, Language arts, Science, etc.
* **How well do you think our teachers perform?** (`teacher_performance`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How engaging do you think our curriculum is?** (`student_engagement`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Highly engaging, Somewhat engaging, Not engaging at all.
* **Do you have any suggestions for the school administration?** (`suggestions`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Share any suggestions or ideas you may have for improving the curriculum.
* **How involved are you as a parent?** (`parent_involvement`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Very involved, Somewhat involved, Not involved at all.
* **How good is communication between teachers and students?** (`teacher_communication`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How important do you think curriculum evaluation is?** (`curriculum_evaluation`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Very important, Somewhat important, Not very important.
* **Do you have any additional comments?** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Share any additional thoughts or comments you may have.
* **Demographic information** (`demographic_info`, `note`, required: false)
	+ Please provide your demographic information, including your age, gender, school attended, etc.
* **What is your age?** (`age`, `number`, required: false)
	+ Select your age.
* **What is your gender?** (`gender`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select your gender.
* **Which school do you attend?** (`school_attended`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select the school you attend.
* **How would you describe your relationship with your teacher?** (`student_teacher_relationship`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How satisfied are you with the courses offered?** (`course_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Highly satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Highly dissatisfied.
* **How enjoyable do you find the courses?** (`course_enjoyment`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Highly enjoyable, Somewhat enjoyable, Neutral, Somewhat unenjoyable, Highly unenjoyable.
* **How difficult do you find the courses?** (`course_difficulty`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Highly difficult, Somewhat difficult, Neutral, Somewhat easy, Highly easy.
* **How relevant are the courses to your life?** (`course_relevance`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Highly relevant, Somewhat relevant, Neutral, Somewhat irrelevant, Highly irrelevant.
* **How valuable do you think the courses are?** (`course_evaluation`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Highly valuable, Somewhat valuable, Neutral, Somewhat not valuable, Highly not valuable.
* **How available are the teachers?** (`teacher_availability`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Always available, Most of the time, Some of the time, Rarely, Never.
* **How competent do you think the teachers are?** (`teacher_competence`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Highly competent, Somewhat competent, Neutral, Somewhat incompetent, Highly incompetent.
* **How good is communication between parents and teachers?** (`parent_communication`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
* **How often do you participate in extracurricular activities?** (`student_participation`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ Select from the scale of Often, Sometimes, Rarely, Never.

Tips:

* Be honest and thorough in your answers.
* Please provide specific examples or examples when possible.
* Don't forget to include any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
