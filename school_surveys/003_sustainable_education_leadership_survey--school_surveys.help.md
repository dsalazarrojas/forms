# Sustainable Education Leadership Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Education Leadership Survey is a questionnaire designed to gather information on school sustainability practices, policies, and challenges. This data will be used to support schools in improving their sustainability efforts and provide insights for better decision-making.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the school name from the dropdown menu or enter it manually.
2. Answer the category question, indicating whether or not the school has implemented sustainability practices.
3. Enter the school location.
4. Provide the contact email and phone number of the person responsible for sustainability practices.
5. Select the rating scale for the sustainability ratings.
6. Describe the sustainability challenges faced by the school.
7. Provide the leadership perspectives on sustainability.
8. Enter the rating scale values.
9. Describe the rating scale description.
10. Indicate whether the school has a high, low, or mixed population.
11. Identify the school type.
12. Determine the school level (high or low).
13. Indicate whether the school is large or small.
14. Choose the school region.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Category** (`category_name`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether or not the school has implemented sustainability practices. 
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your school.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Provide the email address of the person responsible for sustainability practices.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for sustainability practices.
* **Sustainability Ratings** (`sustainability_ratings`, select_multiple, optional): Select the rating scale for the sustainability ratings. 
* **Sustainability Challenges** (`sustainability_challenges`, text, optional): Describe the sustainability challenges faced by your school.
* **Leadership Perspectives** (`leadership_perspectives`, text, optional): Provide the leadership perspectives on sustainability.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`, number, optional): Enter the rating scale value.
* **Rating Scale Description** (`rating_scale_description`, text, optional): Describe the rating scale.
* **School Population** (`school_population`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the school has a high, low, or mixed population. 
* **School Type** (`school_type`, select_one, optional): Identify the school type. 
* **School Level** (`school_level`, select_one, optional): Determine the school level (high or low). 
* **School Size** (`school_size`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the school is large or small.
* **School Region** (`school_region`, select_one, optional): Choose the school region. 

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all the questions truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, please describe it in the corresponding field.
* Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us at [contact information].
