# Teacher Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Engagement Survey is designed to collect feedback and ratings from students about their experiences with a teacher. This feedback will help us evaluate the performance of the teachers and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the rating question with a number to indicate your overall satisfaction with the teacher.
2. Type in your thoughts about the teacher's performance in the "What do you think about the teacher?" field.
3. Share any specific feedback or comments about the teacher's teaching style or behavior in the "Feedback 1" field.
4. If you have any additional feedback or comments, you can type them in the "Feedback 2" field.
5. Finally, answer another rating question with a number to confirm your overall satisfaction with the teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, number, required: false): Rate your overall satisfaction with the teacher on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is "very dissatisfied" and 5 is "very satisfied".
* **What do you think about the teacher?** (`teacher_feedback`, text, required: false): Share your thoughts about the teacher's performance, such as their teaching style, behavior, or any positive or negative experiences you've had with them.
* **Feedback 1** (`teacher_feedback_1`, text, required: false): Provide specific feedback or comments about the teacher's teaching style or behavior.
* **Feedback 2** (`teacher_feedback_2`, text, required: false): If you have any additional feedback or comments, type them in here.
* **Overall Teacher Rating 2** (`teacher_rating`, number, required: false): This is another rating question to confirm your overall satisfaction with the teacher.
