# Learning Modality Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your teaching style, classroom environment, and practices related to learning modalities. This will help us better understand how you implement learning modalities in your classroom and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options from the drop-down menu for each question.
2. For open-ended questions, provide a brief description of your response.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, but feel free to leave the ones marked as "optional" blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your teaching style? (teacher_survey)**: This question aims to understand how you approach teaching. Please select the option that best describes your teaching style.
* **How do you usually present information in class? (classroom_environment)**: Describe how you typically present information in class, e.g., through lectures, discussions, or multimedia resources.
* **How do you usually interact with students? (student_interaction)**: Choose how you usually interact with students, e.g., one-on-one, group discussions, or online platforms.
* **What kind of assessments do you use in your class? (assessment_strategy)**: Select the type of assessments you use to evaluate student progress, e.g., quizzes, exams, or projects.
* **How do you give feedback to students? (feedback_strategy)**: Select how you provide feedback to students, e.g., written, verbal, or peer feedback.
* **Which learning modality do you use most often? (student_modality)**: Choose the learning modality you use most frequently.
* **How often do you use this learning modality? (student_modality_frequency)**: Select how often you use this learning modality.
* **What kind of training have you had as a teacher? (teacher_training)**: Select the type of training you have received as a teacher.
* **How much do you integrate technology into your lessons? (technology_integration)**: Choose how much you incorporate technology into your teaching.
* **How satisfied are you with teaching this subject? (teacher_satisfaction)**: Select your level of satisfaction with teaching this subject.
* **How satisfied are your students with this subject? (student_satisfaction)**: Choose your students' level of satisfaction with this subject.
* **How often do you measure student satisfaction? (student_satisfaction_frequency)**: Select how often you measure student satisfaction.
* **Any other comments or information? (note)**: Use this section to provide any additional information or comments about your teaching practices.
* **Date**: Enter the date when you completed this form.
* **Time**: Enter the time when you completed this form.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further questions).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for further questions).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address (if you want to be contacted for further questions).
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (if you want to be contacted for further questions).
* **Were you aware of the Learning Modality Survey? (teacher_survey_awareness)**: Select if you were aware of this survey before completing it.
* **How many times have you filled out this survey? (teacher_survey_frequency)**: Select how many times you've completed this survey.
* **How helpful is the survey in improving your teaching? (teacher_survey_help)**: Choose how helpful you find this survey in improving your teaching practices.
* **What was most unsatisfactory about the survey? (teacher_survey_dissatisfaction)**: Select the aspect of the survey that you found most unsatisfactory.
* **Are you aware of the form? (form_awareness)**: Select if you are aware of this form and its purpose.
* **How often do you fill out forms? (form_filling)**: Select how often you fill out forms like this one.

Note: Please keep in mind that this form is intended to be completed only once, and responses will be treated confidentially. Your feedback is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
