# Student Technology Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the technology needs of students, teachers, and schools. It is intended to gather data on what technology devices and tools are required to support students' learning and teaching needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant sections for each category (Student, Teacher, School).
2. Fill in the required fields for each section, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. If you need to provide additional comments or notes, use the "Comments or notes" fields.
4. Ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Survey
* **Student Survey** (`student_survey`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of the survey for the students.
* **Student Technology Needs** (`student_technology_needs`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the technology needs of the students.
* **What technology do you need?** (`teacher_technology_needs_select`, `select_one`, required: true): Select one of the following technology needs:
	+ Laptop
	+ Smartphone
	+ Tablet
* **Student Technology Needs Select Multiple** (`student_technology_needs_select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select multiple technology needs:
	+ Laptop
	+ Smartphone
	+ Tablet
* **What date is this survey for?** (`student_survey_date`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date of the survey.
* **What time is this survey for?** (`student_survey_time`, `time`, required: false): Please enter the time of the survey.
* **Comments or notes** (`student_survey_note`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes for the student survey.
* **Student email** (`student_survey_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student phone** (`student_survey_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
