# School Model Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The School Model Survey is a form designed to collect information from school stakeholders (parents/guardians) about their preferences and feedback for a school's model or model version. This form helps the school administration understand the needs and expectations of the school community, informing decisions about future school models or revisions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer all questions honestly.
2. Select the options that best match your preferences.
3. Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the school model.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Preferences (Page 1)**: This section asks you about your preferences as a parent/guardian. Please select 'Yes' or 'No' for each question.
* **Learning Method (Page 1)**: This question asks about your preferred way of learning. Select 'Yes' if you have a preference, 'No' otherwise.
* **School Model (Page 1)**: This section asks about your preference for the school model. Select multiple options that best match your preferences.
* **Additional Comments (Page 1)**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the school model.
* **Email Address (Page 1)**: This is an optional field for you to provide your email address if you'd like to receive updates or information about the school model.
* **Phone Number (Page 1)**: This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **School Name (Page 1)**: If applicable, please provide the name of your child's school.
* **School Type (Page 2)**: This question asks about your preference for the school type. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each option.
* **Location (Page 2)**: This question asks about your school's location. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each option.
* **School Level (Page 2)**: This question asks about your school's level (High or Low). Select the one that best fits your child's school.
* **School Model (Page 2)**: This section asks about your preference for the school model. Select multiple options that best match your preferences.
* **Additional Comments 2 (Page 2)**: Provide any additional comments or thoughts you have about the school model.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* Select only the options that best match your preferences.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration.
* Your feedback is valuable and helps inform decisions about the school model. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form!
