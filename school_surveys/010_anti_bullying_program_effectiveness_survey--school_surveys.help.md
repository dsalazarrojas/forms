# Anti Bullying Program Effectiveness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the effectiveness of an anti-bullying program in schools. The survey aims to gather feedback from various stakeholders, including students, parents, teachers, and staff, to assess the program's impact, identify areas for improvement, and inform future development and implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your role among the options provided.
2. Choose your school district from the text field.
3. Choose the type of educational institution you are associated with.
4. Indicate your level of familiarity with the anti-bullying program.
5. Indicate your level of participation in program activities.
6. Report your observations of bullying incidents in the past 6 months.
7. Select all types of bullying incidents you have observed.
8. Rate the perceived impact of the program on bullying.
9. Report any changes in student behavior you have noticed.
10. Indicate whether you have reported a bullying incident in the past year.
11. Rate your satisfaction with the reporting process.
12. Rate the effectiveness of interventions in addressing bullying.
13. Evaluate the quality of prevention education provided by the program.
14. Assess the responsiveness of staff to bullying incidents.
15. Indicate how safe you feel at school.
16. Rate the effectiveness of program components, specifically educational workshops.
17. Rate the quality of communication about the program.
18. Assess the availability of support resources, such as counseling and hotlines.
19. Provide recommendations on whether the program should continue or not.
20. Suggest any improvements to the program.
21. Provide any additional feedback or comments.
22. Enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Who is completing this survey**: Please select the category that best describes your role or position within the school.
  * Student
  * Parent or Guardian
  * Teacher or Faculty
  * School Administrator
  * Counselor
  * Other Staff
* **School District**: Enter the name of your school district.
* **School Type**: Choose the type of educational institution you are associated with.
  * Public elementary
  * Public middle
  * Public high school
  * Private school
  * Charter school
  * Other
* **Awareness of Anti-Bullying Program**: Indicate your level of familiarity with the program.
  * Very familiar
  * Familiar
  * Somewhat familiar
  * Not familiar
* **Program Participation**: Indicate your level of participation in program activities.
  * Yes, actively
  * Yes, occasionally
  * No, but aware of them
  * No, not aware of activities
* **Bullying Observed**: Report your observations of bullying incidents in the past 6 months.
  * Yes, frequently
  * Yes, occasionally
  * Yes, rarely
  * No, never
* **Types of Bullying Observed**: Select all types of bullying incidents you have observed.
  * Physical bullying
  * Verbal bullying
  * Social or relational bullying
  * Cyberbullying
  * Sexual harassment
  * Discriminatory bullying
  * None observed
* **Program Impact**: Rate the perceived impact of the program on bullying.
  * Significant positive impact
  * Moderate positive impact
  * Slight positive impact
  * No noticeable impact
  * Negative impact
* **Change in Student Behavior**: Report any changes in student behavior you have noticed.
  * Significant improvement
  * Some improvement
  * No change
  * Behavior has worsened
  * Not applicable
* **Reported Bullying Incident**: Indicate whether you have reported a bullying incident in the past year.
  * Yes, multiple times
  * Yes, once
  * No, but considered it
  * No, never needed to
* **Satisfaction with Reporting Process**: Rate your satisfaction with the reporting process.
  * Very satisfied
  * Satisfied
  * Neutral
  * Dissatisfied
  * Very dissatisfied
  * Did not report
* **Effectiveness of Interventions**: Rate the effectiveness of interventions in addressing bullying.
  * Very effective
  * Effective
  * Somewhat effective
  * Ineffective
  * Very ineffective
  * Have not observed interventions
* **Prevention Education**: Rate the quality of prevention education provided by the program.
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Average
  * Poor
  * Very poor
* **Staff Responsiveness**: Rate the responsiveness of staff to bullying incidents.
  * Always responsive
  * Usually responsive
  * Sometimes responsive
  * Rarely responsive
  * Never responsive
* **Feeling of Safety**: Indicate how safe you feel at school.
  * Very safe
  * Safe
  * Neutral
  * Unsafe
  * Very unsafe
* **Program Components Rating**: Rate the effectiveness of program components, specifically educational workshops.
  * Excellent
  * Good
  * Average
  * Poor
  * Not applicable
* **Communication About Program**: Rate the quality of communication about the program.
  * Excellent communication
  * Good communication
  * Average communication
  * Poor communication
  * No communication
* **Availability of Support Resources**: Assess the availability of support resources, such as counseling and hotlines.
  * Very accessible
  * Accessible
  * Somewhat accessible
  * Not accessible
  * Unaware of resources
* **Program Continuation**: Provide your recommendation on whether the program should continue or not.
  * Yes, expand it
  * Yes, continue as is
  * Yes, but with modifications
  * No, discontinue
  * Not sure
* **Suggested Improvements**: Provide any suggestions for improving the program.
* **Additional Feedback**: Provide any additional comments or observations about the program.
* **Survey Completion Date**: Enter the date of submission.

Note: The date field is automatically filled in upon form submission.
