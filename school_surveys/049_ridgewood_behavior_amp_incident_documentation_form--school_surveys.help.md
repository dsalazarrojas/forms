# ridgewood_behavior_incident_documentation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ridgewood Behavior Incident Documentation Form is used to report and document incidents that have occurred at Ridgewood, allowing staff and administration to track and respond to incidents in a timely and accurate manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the incident location where the incident occurred.
2. Select the date and time the incident occurred using the date picker.
3. Enter the name of the person who reported the incident.
4. Describe the incident in detail, including any relevant details.
5. List any actions taken in response to the incident.
6. Indicate whether parents were made aware of the incident and sign off on the response.
7. Sign off as the parent/guardian.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **incident_location**: <small>This is the location where the incident occurred, such as the name of the school or specific classroom.</small> 
* **incident_time**: <small>This is the date and time the incident occurred, which can be selected using the date picker calendar.</small> 
* **reporter**: <small>This is the name of the person who reported the incident.</small> 
* **description**: <small>This is a detailed description of what happened during the incident, including any relevant details.</small> 
* **actions_taken**: <small>This is a list of any actions taken in response to the incident, such as first aid provided or steps taken to prevent future incidents.</small> 
* **parent_communication**: <small>This indicates whether parents/guardians were made aware of the incident, and whether they were involved in the response. Select 'Yes' if parents/guardians were informed, and 'No' otherwise.</small> 
* **parent_signature**: <small>This is the signature of a parent/guardian, confirming that they were informed and acknowledged the incident.</small> 

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when describing the incident to ensure accurate documentation.
- Make sure to list all actions taken in response to the incident, including any follow-up actions.
- If parents/guardians were involved, ensure they sign off on the response to confirm they were informed.
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact your supervisor or school administration for guidance.
