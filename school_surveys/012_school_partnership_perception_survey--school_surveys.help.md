# School Partnership Perception Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "School Partnership Perception Survey" is designed to help users gauge the perception of schools and their partnership relationships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the questions carefully and answer based on your genuine experience and perception of the school partnership.
2. For questions with options, select the one that best describes your experience.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise answer in the designated space.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Partnership Survey** (`school_partnership_survey`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the school partnership survey, including any notable events, projects, or initiatives.
- **School Partnership Category** (`school_partnership_category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes your level of satisfaction with the school partnership. Options: Highly Satisfied, Moderately Satisfied, Not at all Satisfied
- **School Partnership Quality** (`school_partnership_quality`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the quality of the school partnership. This could include factors such as responsiveness, communication, and overall effectiveness.
- **School Partnership Involvement** (`school_partnership_involvement`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all that apply to describe your level of involvement in the school partnership. Options: Highly Involved, Moderately Involved, Not at all Involved
- **School Partnership Awareness** (`school_partnership_awareness`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes your level of awareness regarding the school partnership. Options: Highly Aware, Moderately Aware, Not at all Aware
- **School Partnership Communication** (`school_partnership_communication`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes the communication level with the school partnership. Options: Highly Communicative, Moderately Communicative, Not at all Communicative
- **School Partnership Participation** (`school_partnership_participation`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of your level of participation in the school partnership. 
- **School Partnership Comments** (`school_partnership_comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or suggestions for the school partnership, please include them in this field. 

## Tips
- Answer based on your genuine experience and perception.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification. 
- Your honest feedback is invaluable in helping us improve the school partnership.
