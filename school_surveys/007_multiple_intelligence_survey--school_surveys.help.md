# multiple_intelligence_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you understand your multiple intelligences, which are the various abilities and skills you possess. The survey assesses different types of intelligence, such as linguistic, logical, spatial, and intrapersonal, to provide insights into your strengths and weaknesses.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and select your preferred answers for each section.
2. Choose your preferred time of day for when you feel most productive or alert.
3. If you want to provide additional comments or thoughts, you can do so in the "comments" section.
4. Enter a valid email address if you want to be notified about your results.
5. Click the "submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **intro** (text): Please provide a brief introduction to yourself, including any relevant background information or context.
- **questions** (select_one): Choose one or more of the following options that best describe your strengths:
  - **Linguistic**: I am skilled at languages and enjoy communicating through words.
  - **Logical**: I am good at problem-solving and analyzing information.
  - **Spatial**: I am skilled at understanding and working with visual information.
  - **Intrapersonal**: I am good at understanding myself and others.
- **time** (select_multiple): Choose one or more of the following options that best describe your most productive time of day:
  - **Morning**: I am most productive in the morning.
  - **Afternoon**: I am most productive in the afternoon.
  - **Evening**: I am most productive in the evening.
- **date** (date): You can enter the date you prefer to complete this survey (optional).
- **time** (time): You can enter the time you prefer to complete this survey (optional).
- **comments** (note): If you want to provide additional thoughts or insights, you can do so here.
- **email** (email): Enter a valid email address if you want to be notified about your results (optional).
- **submit** (text): Confirm that you have completed and reviewed your answers.

Please note that all fields are optional, and you can complete the form at your own pace.
