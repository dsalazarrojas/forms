# Teacher Observation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Observation Form is a tool for teachers to record their observations about other teachers or teaching assistants in the classroom. This form helps to gather data on the effectiveness of teaching assistants and other teachers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the title of the observation in the "Teacher Observation Title" field.
* Rate the effectiveness of the observed teacher or assistant in the "Rating (1-5)" field.
* Enter the date of the observation in the "Date" field.
* If a teaching assistant was present during the observation, select "Assistant" in the "Was a teaching assistant present?" field.
* List the classes that were observed in the "What classes were observed?" field.
* If applicable, enter the number of classes observed in the "How many classes did you observe?" field.
* If applicable, enter the duration of the observation in the "How long did you observe this class?" field.
* Enter any additional notes about the observation in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Observation Title** (`teacher_observation_form`): Enter the title of the observation, for example "Monday Morning Class Observation".
* **Rating (1-5)** (`teacher_observation_form_rating`): Rate the effectiveness of the observed teacher or assistant on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Date** (`teacher_observation_form_date`): Enter the date of the observation.
* **Was a teaching assistant present?** (`teacher_observation_form_teacher_assistant`): Select "Assistant" if a teaching assistant was present during the observation.
* **Who was being observed?** (`teacher_observation_form_teacher_observed`): Enter the name of the teacher or assistant being observed.
* **By whom?** (`teacher_observation_form_teacher_observing`): Enter the name of the person who conducted the observation.
* **What classes were observed?** (`teacher_observation_form_classes_held`): List the classes that were observed, for example "Math 101 and Science 202".
* **How many classes did you observe?** (`teacher_observation_form_num_classes`): Enter the number of classes observed, for example "2".
* **How long did you observe this class?** (`teacher_observation_form_duration`): Enter the duration of the observation, for example "30 minutes".
* **Notes** (`teacher_observation_form_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the observation, for example "The observed teacher was able to effectively manage classroom behavior and deliver clear instructions."
