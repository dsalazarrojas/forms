# Student Adaptation To College Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Adaptation To College Questionnaire is designed to collect demographic information and experiences of students during their initial stages of college life. This helps the institution understand student needs and challenges, enabling them to provide better support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have a valid Student ID number ready.
2. Fill out the Demographic Information field with your correct details.
3. Choose your Gender from the available options.
4. Enter your Address in the provided text box.
5. Enter your Age in the correct format (numerical value).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (student_id, number, Optional): Enter your unique Student ID number to identify yourself as a student.
* **Demographic Information** (demographic_info, text, Optional): Enter your demographic details such as your name, contact number, and any other relevant information.
* **Gender** (demographic_info_gender, number, Optional): Choose your Gender from the provided options.
* **Address** (demographic_info_address, text, Optional): Enter your Address in the format of "Street Number, Street Name, City, State, ZIP" (e.g., "123 Main Street, New York, NY 10001").
* **Age** (demographic_info_age, number, Optional): Enter your Age in the correct format (numerical value).
