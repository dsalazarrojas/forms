# First Day Of School Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The First Day Of School Questionnaire is designed to gather information from students on their previous schooling experiences and background information to better understand their needs and provide personalized support throughout the academic year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Review each section to ensure you understand the information being requested.
3. Select or type in the required information as prompted.
4. Click on the "Submit" button to finalize your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Previous Schooling Info** (`previous_schooling_info`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to describe any relevant information about your previous schooling experiences. Please provide as much detail as necessary to help us understand your background.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, select multiple, optional):
	+ This field allows you to indicate what you are interested in learning or exploring this year. Select all that apply:
		+ Yes (I am interested in learning more about a particular subject or topic)
		+ No (I do not have any specific interests)
* **Family Background** (`family_background`, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to describe any relevant background information about your family that may be helpful to us in providing support. Please provide as much detail as necessary.
