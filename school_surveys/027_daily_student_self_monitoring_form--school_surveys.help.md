# Daily Student Self Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Daily Student Self Monitoring Form is a tool for teachers to collect information from students on their daily activities, attendance, and interactions. This form helps teachers keep track of student progress, note areas for improvement, and have a record of student performance and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student name.
2. Select the date of the day.
3. Enter the time you arrived at school.
4. Enter the time you left school.
5. Enter the subject of your class.
6. Enter the class room number.
7. Enter your teacher's name.
8. Enter your teacher's email (if available).
9. Enter your teacher's phone number (if available).
10. Add any comments about your day with your teacher.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as written on your student ID or attendance sheet.
* **Date** (`date_today`, date, optional): Select the date of the day you're filling out the form.
* **Time In** (`time_in`, time, optional): Enter the time you arrived at school.
* **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, optional): Enter the time you left school.
* **Class Subject** (`class_subject`, text, optional): Enter the subject of your class today.
* **Class Room** (`class_room`, text, optional): Enter the room number of your class.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your teacher.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your teacher's email address (if available).
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's phone number (if available).
* **Teacher Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your day with your teacher.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Fill out the form at the end of each day.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your teacher for clarification.
* If you have any issues or concerns, use the teacher comments field to express them.
* Remember, this form is a tool to help you and your teacher track your progress and stay on the same page.
