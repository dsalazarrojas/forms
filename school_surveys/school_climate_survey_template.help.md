# school_climate_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Climate Survey is designed to gather insights on the school environment from students, staff, and parents. This survey aims to understand their satisfaction, sense of belonging, perception of the learning environment, and suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as they apply to your experience at the school.
2. Select your responses from the available options.
3. For the text-based questions, provide your thoughts and comments in the designated spaces.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the overall school climate?** (`Very satisfied`, `single`, **Required**)
	+ This question assesses your general satisfaction with the school climate.
* **How often do you feel a sense of belonging at school?** (`Always`, `Often`, `Sometimes`, `Rarely`, `Never`, `single`, **Required**)
	+ This question gauges your sense of belonging and connection to the school community.
* **Which of the following best describe the learning environment at school?** (`Supportive`, `Engaging`, `Challenging`, `Respectful`, `Safe`, `Inclusive`, **Multiple**, **Required**)
	+ This question allows you to select all the words that best describe your experience with the learning environment.
* **What would you improve or change about the school climate?** (`text`, **Optional**)
	+ Share your thoughts on how the school climate could be improved.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback regarding the school environment?** (`text`, **Optional**)
	+ If you have any other suggestions or concerns, please add them here.

## Tips
- Be honest and truthful in your responses.
- Select all the options that apply to your experience.
- Provide specific examples or details when answering the text-based questions.
- If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
