# Career Choice Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about students' career aspirations and goals, helping to inform the development of career counseling services that cater to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your student name (if providing your name is your preference) and other basic information on the first page.
2. On the second page, select up to three subjects you enjoyed most in school.
3. On the third page, choose areas of career interest, and select up to three options.
4. If applicable, mark the factors that influence your career choice.
5. Indicate your interest in career counseling and select your preferred support types.
6. On the final page, choose when you expect to make a decision about your career path and provide any additional comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name (student_name)** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name (or leave blank if you'd like to remain anonymous).
* **Current academic year (current_year)** (`current_year`, text, required): Type the grade or year of study you are currently in.
* **Subjects enjoyed most (subjects_enjoyed)** (`subjects_enjoyed`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three subjects you enjoyed most in school.
* **Preferred career areas (preferred_career_areas)** (`preferred_career_areas`, select_multiple, required): Choose up to three areas of career interest.
* **Factors influencing career choice (influence_factors)** (`influence_factors`, select_multiple, optional): Select factors that influence your career choice.
* **Interest in career counseling (career_counseling_interest)** (`career_counseling_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate your interest in career counseling.
* **Preferred support types (preferred_support_types)** (`preferred_support_types`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three types of support that would be helpful to you.
* **Expected timeline for career decision (expected_timeline)** (`expected_timeline`, select_one, optional): Select when you plan to make a career decision.
* **Additional comments (additional_comments)** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share about your career aspirations and goals.
