# Self Awareness Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Awareness Questionnaire is a tool designed to help individuals gain a better understanding of their own self-awareness levels. It is meant to be completed by individuals who are looking to assess and improve their personal growth, relationships, career, and overall well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Self Awareness Questionnaire, follow these steps:

1. Take a few minutes to read through each page carefully.
2. Answer all questions truthfully, as this will help you gain the most from the exercise.
3. Choose the best response that reflects your current feelings and thoughts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is a brief introduction to the questionnaire. Please take a few minutes to read through this page before starting the questionnaire.
* **How would you rate yourself on a scale of 1-10?**: Rate yourself on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest. Be honest with yourself and rate based on how you feel right now.
* **What do you feel most comfortable with?**: Choose the option that best describes how you feel about yourself. This question is designed to help you identify areas where you may be feeling uncomfortable or uncertain.
* **What is your major life area of concern?**: Select all areas that you feel are your major concerns. This will help you identify areas that need improvement or attention.
* **How do you feel about yourself?**: Choose the option that best describes how you feel about yourself. This question is designed to help you develop a positive relationship with yourself.
* **Rate yourself on a scale of 1-10**: Rate yourself on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being the lowest and 10 being the highest. This is an opportunity to reflect on your self-worth and self-esteem.
* **How confident are you in yourself?**: Choose the option that best describes how you feel about your abilities and capabilities. This question is designed to help you develop confidence and self-assurance.
* **What do you feel is your greatest strength?**: Choose all areas that you feel are your greatest strengths. This will help you identify your areas of excellence.
* **What areas do you feel you need to improve on?**: Select all areas that you feel need improvement or attention. This will help you set goals and priorities for personal development.
* **Do you have any final thoughts or insights about yourself?**: Take a few minutes to reflect on your answers and see if there is anything else you would like to add or share about your self-awareness levels. This is your chance to provide any additional thoughts or insights.

## Tips

* Be honest and truthful with yourself throughout the questionnaire.
* Take your time to reflect on each question carefully.
* Don't worry if you are unsure about some questions, as this is a self-reflection exercise and there are no right or wrong answers.
* Use this questionnaire as a tool to aid in personal development and self-improvement.
