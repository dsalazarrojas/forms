# Student Financial Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about students' financial well-being and support needs. It aims to understand their financial aid, income, and access to financial wellness programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by *).
2. Select the options that best fit your financial aid and employment status.
3. Enter your income information.
4. Indicate whether you have access to financial wellness programs.
5. If you have access to other financial wellness programs, please specify them.
6. Describe any other financial wellness programs you participate in.
7. Specify any school support services you use.
8. If you use other school support services, please provide details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your university email address or other contact email you use for financial aid and support.
* **Financial Aid** (`Financial Aid`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all applicable financial aid programs you have:
		- Pell Grant
		- Federal Work-Study
		- Federal Perkins Loan
		- Institutional
		- Private
* **Income** (`Income`, number, optional): Enter your annual income from all sources.
* **Student Employment** (`Student Employment`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'True' if you are employed part-time or full-time.
	+ Select 'False' if you are not employed.
	+ Select 'Other (please specify)' if your employment status is not listed.
* **Financial Challenge** (`Financial Challenge`, date, optional): Select the date when you first experienced a financial challenge or crisis.
* **Financial Goal** (`Financial Goal`, text, optional): Describe your short-term or long-term financial goal(s).
* **School Financial Aid Offered** (`School Financial Aid Offered`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'True' if you have received financial aid from the school.
	+ Select 'False' if you have not received financial aid from the school.
	+ Select 'Not Applicable' if you are not eligible for school financial aid.
* **Financial Wellness Programs** (`Financial Wellness Programs`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all financial wellness programs you participate in or have access to:
		- Financial Literacy
		- Financial Planning
		- Financial Wellness Program
		- Other (please specify)
* **Other Financial Wellness Programs** (`Other Financial Wellness Programs`, text, optional): List any other financial wellness programs not listed above.
* **Other Financial Wellness Programs Details** (`Other Financial Wellness Programs Details`, text, optional): Provide details about the other financial wellness programs you participate in.
* **School Support Services** (`School Support Services`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all support services you use:
		- Academic Advising
		- Career Counseling
		- Financial Aid Office
		- Student Life
		- Other (please specify)
* **School Support Services Details** (`School Support Services Details`, text, optional): Describe any additional details about the support services you use.

Note: This help guide is for end-users to understand the purpose and how to complete the form. It aims to provide a clear and concise explanation of each field.
