# distance_learning_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students about their distance learning experience. Your responses will help us understand what works well and what needs improvement, allowing us to create a more engaging and effective online learning environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the options that best describe your experience with each question.
2. If applicable, provide detailed comments and suggestions in the text boxes.
3. Ensure you have filled out all required fields (indicated by a red star).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Virtual Class Experience** (`Virtual Class Experience`, select_one): Select 'Yes' if you have taken online classes, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Teacher Support** (`teacher_support`, select_multiple): Choose all the ways your teacher provided support during the virtual class, such as 'Yes - Regular check-ins', 'Yes - Quick responses', or 'Yes - None of the above'.
* **Teacher Response Time** (`teacher_response_time`, time): Estimate how quickly your teacher responded to your questions and concerns during the virtual class. Use the time input field to enter the time in hours and minutes (e.g., 1:30 or 2 hours 30 minutes).
* **Comments and Suggestions** (`comments_and_suggestions`, text): Provide any comments or suggestions you have about your distance learning experience. Be specific and detailed in your feedback.
* **Technical Issues** (`technical_issues`, select_one): Select 'Yes' if you encountered technical issues during the virtual class, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Ease of Use** (`ease_of_use`, number): Rate how easy or difficult you found the virtual class to be on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very difficult and 5 being very easy.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number): Rate your overall experience with the virtual class on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Favorite Feature** (`favorite_feature`, select_one): Select the feature that you liked most about the virtual class (e.g., 'Live sessions', 'Pre-recorded videos', or 'Interactive exercises').
* **Least Improvement** (`least_improvement`, select_one): Select the area that needs improvement (e.g., 'Technical issues', 'Teacher support', or 'Course materials ').
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Provide any additional comments or concerns you have about your distance learning experience.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, text): Suggest improvements or changes to make future virtual classes more engaging and effective.
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, number): Rate your teacher's performance on a scale of 1-5, with 1 being very poor and 5 being excellent.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, note): Provide any comments or feedback about the course materials, such as relevance, usefulness, or quality.

Note: If you have any issues accessing or navigating this form, please contact us for assistance.
