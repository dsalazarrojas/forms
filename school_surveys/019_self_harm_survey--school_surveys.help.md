# Self Harm Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your experiences with self-harm, with the aim of providing support and resources to help you address this issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question to the best of your ability. You can select multiple answers for some questions, and provide additional information in the text fields. If you need help or support while completing this form, please contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you know what self-harm means?** (`self_harm_knowledge`, text, optional): This is the starting question to gauge your understanding of self-harming behaviors. Please answer truthfully and provide any comments or insights you may have.
* **Have you ever self-harmed?** (`self_harm_experiences`, select_one, optional): This question asks whether you have personally experienced self-harming behaviors. Choose 'Yes' if you have, and 'No' if you have not.
* **How often do you self-harm?** (`self_harm_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the frequencies at which you self-harm from the options provided. This will help us understand the pattern and severity of your self-harming behaviors.
* **What methods have you used to self-harm?** (`self_harm_methods`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the methods you have used to self-harm from the options provided. This will help us understand your self-harming behaviors better.
* **How old were you when you first self-harmed?** (`self_harm_age`, date, optional): This question asks about the age at which you first started self-harming. Please provide your age at that time.
* **What age did you start self-harming?** (`self_harm_age_start`, number, optional): This question asks about the age at which you first started self-harming. Please provide your age in years.
* **What age did you stop self-harming?** (`self_harm_age_end`, number, optional): This question asks about the age at which you stopped self-harming. Please provide your age in years.
* **Who have you talked to about self-harming?** (`self_harm_support`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the people you have talked to about self-harming from the options provided. This will help us understand your support system.
* **What source(s) did you seek help from about self-harming?** (`self_harm_support_sources`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the sources you have sought help from about self-harming from the options provided. This will help us understand the help you received.
* **Can you think about ways to prevent self-harming in the future?** (`self_harm_prevent`, select_one, optional): This question asks whether you can think about preventing self-harming. Choose 'Yes' if you can, and 'No' if you cannot.
* **What methods do you think can help prevent self-harming?** (`self_harm_prevent_methods`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the methods you think can help prevent self-harming from the options provided. This will help us understand your prevention strategies.
* **Other methods that might help prevent self-harming?** (`self_harm_prevent_methods_specify`, text, optional): If you have any additional methods or ideas that can help prevent self-harming, please provide them in this text field.
* **Is there anything else you want to tell us about self-harming?** (`self_harm_note`, note, optional): This is an open-ended question for any additional comments or insights you may have about self-harming.
* **Do you have a preferred method of contact for support?** (`self_harm_phone`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred method of contact for support from the options provided. This will help us reach out to you effectively.
* **Do you have a preferred method of email for support?** (`self_harm_email`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you prefer to receive emails or not from the options provided. This will help us contact you via email if you prefer it.
