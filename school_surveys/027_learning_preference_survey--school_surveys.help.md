# Learning Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information about your learning preferences and habits. Your responses will help us understand how you learn best and what factors influence your learning process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your primary learning style from the options below:
	* Reading
	* Watching Videos
	* Listening
	* Doing Projects
	* Other (please specify)
2. Answer the following questions about your learning habits:
	* **reading**: How many hours per week do you spend reading?
	* **watching videos**: How many hours per week do you spend watching videos?
	* **doing projects**: How many hours per week do you spend on school projects?
	* **other**: If you select "other" as your primary learning style, please specify what it is.
3. Rate the importance of different factors in your learning process:
	* **teacher influence**: How important is your teacher's influence on your learning?
	* **teacher support**: How important is your teacher's support on your learning?
	* **teacher direction**: How important is your teacher's guidance on your learning?
	* **peer support**: How important is support from your peers on your learning?
	* **self-direction**: How important is self-directed learning on your learning?
	* **self-influence**: How important is self-motivation on your learning?
	* **time management**: How important is time management on your learning?
	* **time commitment**: How important is the time commitment on your learning?
	* **self-discipline**: How important is self-discipline on your learning?
	* **motivation**: How important is motivation on your learning?
	* **goal setting**: How important is setting learning goals on your learning?
	* **self-efficacy**: How important is self-confidence in your learning?
	* **teacher feedback**: How important is feedback from your teacher on your learning?
	* **student feedback**: How important is feedback from your peers on your learning?
	* **parent feedback**: How important is feedback from your parents on your learning?
	* **parent influence**: How important is influence from your parents on your learning?
	* **parent discipline**: How important is discipline from your parents on your learning?
	* **other educational resources**: If you select "other" as an important factor, please specify what it is.
