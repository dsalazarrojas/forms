# English Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your English proficiency level and gather information about your language abilities, background, and usage patterns. It will help us understand your strengths and weaknesses in understanding, speaking, writing, and reading English, as well as your native language and language usage habits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question carefully, selecting the most accurate option for your English proficiency level and language abilities.
2. Fill out all the fields, but you can skip any questions that don't apply to you.
3. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this information will help us better understand your language needs and provide more effective support.
