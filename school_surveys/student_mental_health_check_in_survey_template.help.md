# student_mental_health_check_in_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Student mental health check-in survey** is a self-assessment tool designed to help students share their current mental well-being, identify areas that need support, and provide feedback to the school on how to improve mental health resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select all relevant options that apply to you.
3. Answer each text field with a few sentences or a short paragraph.
4. Be as specific as possible when sharing your thoughts and feelings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you describe your current overall well-being?** (`well-being`, `single`, required): This question asks about your general feeling of being happy, content, or comfortable. Please choose the option that best describes your current state.
* **Which of the following emotions have you experienced frequently recently?** (`emotion`, `multiple`, required): Select all the emotions that you've felt often, such as happy, stressed, anxious, sad, or overwhelmed.
* **How has your mental health affected your academic performance?** (`academic_performance`, `single`, required): Choose how your mental health has impacted your grades, attendance, or motivation.
* **What sources of support do you currently utilize?** (`support`, `multiple`, required): Select all the sources that help you cope with mental health challenges, such as friends, family, teachers, or online resources.
* **If you needed mental health support, how comfortable would you be seeking help from school resources?** (`comfortable`, `single`, required): Indicate how comfortable you would feel in seeking help from school counselors, teachers, or administrators.
* **What is one thing that has been most helpful for your mental health recently?** (`helpful`, `text`, optional): Share one specific thing that has helped you cope with mental health challenges.
* **What could the school do to better support student mental health?** (`suggestion`, `text`, optional): Provide suggestions on how the school can improve mental health resources and support.

## Tips
* Be honest and authentic in your responses.
* Remember that this form is for your own benefit, and it's okay to share your feelings and needs.
* If you're unsure about any question or option, just do your best to answer based on your feelings.
