# School Lunch Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about your experiences and opinions regarding school lunches. Your feedback and input are valuable to us, and your responses will contribute to improving the quality and selection of school lunches.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question honestly, and provide as much detail as you can.
2.  Make sure to review each field carefully to ensure you fill out all required fields.
3.  If a field is optional, feel free to skip it unless you have a comment or feedback about that particular subject.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **School Lunch Survey** (`school_lunch_survey`, `text`, required/optional): A free-text field to record your thoughts or experiences about the school lunch survey. Please type your answer in the space provided.
*  **How Satisfied Are You** (`how_satisfied`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how satisfied you are with the school lunch options available.
*  **Food Quality** (`food_quality`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply regarding the quality of the food you've experienced.
*  **Menu Selection** (`menu_selection`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all that apply regarding the types of menu options you've seen or experienced at school.
*  **School Lunch Frequency** (`school_lunch_frequency`, `number`, required/optional): If you've had lunch at school, enter the number of times you've done so.
*  **Lunch Break Time** (`lunch_break_time`, `time`, required/optional): If you've had lunch at school, enter the time of day you usually take a lunch break.
*  **School Meals** (`school_meals`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether you've had meals at school.
*  **Lunch Cost** (`lunch_cost`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose all that apply regarding the perceived cost of school meals.
*  **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
*  **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address (if you choose to provide it).
*  **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if you choose to provide it).

## Tips

- Be honest and thorough in your responses.
- You can skip optional fields unless you have comments or feedback for that subject.
- If you've already submitted this form, don't submit it again.
