# Class Poll - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class Poll form is designed to collect feedback and suggestions from students on their experiences, opinions, and preferences related to their class. This form helps the instructor understand the students' perspectives, allowing for improvements and adjustments to be made to the class.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, but only answer the questions that apply to you.
2. Be honest and provide your genuine thoughts and opinions.
3. Take the time to think through each question carefully.
4. If you have any additional feedback or concerns, use the open feedback field to express them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`Student Name`, text, optional): This is where you can enter your name, or you can leave it blank if you prefer to remain anonymous.
* **Student ID** (`Student ID`, text, optional): If you're comfortable providing your ID, you can enter it here. It's entirely up to you, and it's optional.
* **Class Name** (`Class Name`, text, required): This field is for you to enter the name of your class, as it helps the instructor understand which class you're referring to.
* **Poll Date** (`Poll Date`, date, required): Select the date of the class or the time period when you experienced what you're being asked about.
* **Question 1 - What is your preference** (`Question 1`, select_one, required): Choose your preference from the options provided for the first question. This will help the instructor understand your opinion on the matter.
* **Question 2 - Which would you choose** (`Question 2`, select_one, required): Similar to the first question, select your preference from the options provided.
* **Question 3 - Rate your agreement - scale 1-5** (`Question 3`, number, optional): If you have an opinion, rate your agreement from 1 to 5. If you don't have an opinion, you can leave it blank.
* **Question 4 - Select all that apply** (`Question 4`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the options that apply to you from the list provided. You can select as many or as few as you like.
* **Question 5 - How satisfied are you** (`Question 5`, select_one, optional): Select how satisfied you are with your experience from the options provided.
* **Any additional feedback or comments** (`open feedback`, text, optional): If you have any additional feedback or comments, type them here.
* **Suggestions for improvement** (`Suggestions`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions for the class or instructor, type them here.
* **Any concerns or issues to address** (`Concerns`, text, optional): If you have any concerns or issues with the class, type them here.

## Tips
* Be honest and provide thoughtful feedback.
* Keep your answers concise and clear.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask the instructor for clarification.
