# Reopening School Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather feedback from parents or guardians on their preference for their child's school attendance after a closure.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the first question, "Do you want your child to go to school?" by selecting one of the two options: "Yes" or "No". This question is required.
2. If you selected "Yes" in the first question, please answer the second question, "How many days a week would you want your child to attend school?", by entering a number of days between 0 and 7 (inclusive). This question is required.
3. Answer the third question, "Would you want your child to go to school on holidays?" by selecting one or both of the options: "Yes" or "No". This question is not required.
4. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School** (school_reopening_survey, select_multiple, Not Required): Please select "Reopening School" from the dropdown menu.
* **Do you want your child to go to school?** (school_reopening_survey_question, select_one, Required): Choose "Yes" if you would like your child to attend school, or "No" if you would not.
* **How many days a week would you want your child to attend school?** (school_reopening_survey_question_2, number, Required): Enter a whole number between 0 and 7, indicating the number of days you would like your child to attend school per week.
* **Would you want your child to go to school on holidays?** (school_reopening_survey_question_3, select_multiple, Not Required): Select "Yes" if you would like your child to attend school on holidays, or "No" if you would not.
 
## Tips
* Please answer this form carefully and honestly, as your responses will help inform school policies.
* Ensure you only submit one response per household.
* If you have already submitted this form, do not resubmit.
