# Student Peer Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Peer Evaluation Form is designed to allow students to rate and provide feedback on their peers' performance and behavior in a class. The form is typically filled out at the end of the semester or academic year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any other required information, if applicable.
2. Select a rating score for each of your peers (Rating Score 1-10).
3. Enter any feedback for each of your peers.
4. Provide additional feedback if desired.
5. Enter the date and time when you completed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter your name as a student.
* **Rating Score 1-10**: Rate your peers' performance on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feedback 1-6**: Enter any feedback you have for your peers.
* **Additional Feedback**: Enter any additional feedback you have for your peers.
* **Date**: Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time**: Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Rating Scale**: Select the rating scale option that best fits your rating score.
* **Rating Scale Option**: Select one of the rating scale options that best fits your rating score.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes about your peer's performance.
