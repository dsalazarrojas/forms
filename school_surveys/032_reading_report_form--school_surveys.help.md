# Reading Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Reading Report Form is used to collect information from students about their reading progress, level, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Make sure to enter your student name, date submitted, reading level, reading progress, comments (if any), email, and phone number.
- You can select from the provided options for "Reading Level" and do not need to enter a custom value.
- You can enter any comments about your reading progress in the "Comments" field if needed.
- Enter your email address and phone number in the respective fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a student.
* **Date Submitted (Date Submitted)** (`date_submitted`, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Reading Level (Reading Level)** (`reading_level`, select_one, optional): Select your current reading level from the provided options:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Other (select "Other" and enter your own level in the field)
* **Reading Progress (Reading Progress)** (`reading_progress`, text, optional): Describe your current reading progress, including any challenges or obstacles you're facing.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your reading progress.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
