# Preschool Graduation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Graduation Questionnaire is designed to collect feedback and survey information from teachers and parents regarding a child's preschool graduation. This form is meant to capture the child's readiness for formal education, their social and emotional development, and other relevant aspects of their preschool experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand each question or field.
2. Answer each question thoroughly and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Use the available text fields to provide written comments or feedback.
4. Select the appropriate options for the survey questions, such as "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Teacher Feedback (text)**:
	+ This field is for the teacher to provide written feedback about the child's performance, strengths, and areas for improvement.
	+ Enter your observations, comments, or suggestions for the child's future development.
- **Parent Survey (select one)**:
	+ This question asks if you, as a parent, are ready for your child to start formal education.
	+ Select "Yes" if you feel your child is ready, and "No" if you need more time or support before making the decision.
- **Graduation Survey (select multiple)**:
	+ This question asks about your child's social and emotional development, such as their ability to share, cooperate, and show kindness.
	+ Select all the options that apply to your child's behavior or personality.

## Tips
* Please answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* If you are unsure about any question, feel free to provide additional comments or feedback in the text fields.
* This form is meant to be a collaborative effort between teachers and parents to help the child's development and prepare them for formal education.
