# Opinion Survey On School Phone Policies - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather your opinions on the school's phone policies. Your responses will help us understand your thoughts, suggestions, and feedback on the current policies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name on the first two fields.
2. Provide your Email address in the third field.
3. Select your Department from the fourth field (e.g., School Administration, School Staff, Student).
4. Choose your Role at the school from the fifth field (e.g., Student, Faculty, Staff).
5. Write a brief Opinion on the sixth field.
6. (Optional) Add any additional Comment about your experience with the school's phone policies.
7. Enter the Date and Time of when you are filling out this form.
8. Describe your thoughts on the School Phone Policy in the seventh field.
9. Rate your overall satisfaction with the school's phone policies on the eighth field (using the number 1-5).
10. Provide Feedback on the last field about your experience with the school's phone policies.
11. (Optional) Suggest any changes or improvements to the school's phone policies.
12. Select your level of agreement with the current phone policies from the eleventh field (e.g., Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither Agree nor Disagree).
13. Select your level of disagreement with the current phone policies from the twelfth field (e.g., Strongly Disagree, Somewhat Disagree, Neither Agree nor Disagree).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Choose your department (e.g., School Administration, School Staff, Student).
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Select your role at the school (e.g., Student, Faculty, Staff).
* **Opinion** (`opinion`, text, required): Briefly describe your opinion on the school's phone policies.
* **Comment** (`comment`, note, optional): Add any additional comment about your experience with the school's phone policies.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you are filling out this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you are filling out this form.
* **School Phone Policy** (`school_phone_policies_survey`, text, required): Describe your thoughts on the school's phone policy.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the school's phone policies using 1-5 scale.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required): Provide feedback on your experience with the school's phone policies.
* **Suggestion** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Suggest any changes or improvements to the school's phone policies.
* **Agree** (`agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of agreement with the current phone policies (e.g., Strongly Agree, Somewhat Agree, Neither Agree nor Disagree).
* **Disagree** (`disagree`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of disagreement with the current phone policies (e.g., Strongly Disagree, Somewhat Disagree, Neither Agree nor Disagree).

Note: If you need help or have questions, please contact us.
