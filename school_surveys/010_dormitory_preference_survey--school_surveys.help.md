# Dormitory Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Dormitory Preference Survey, a series of questions designed to gather information about your dormitory preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you understand each question.
2. Fill out the form by entering your responses in the designated fields.
3. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First page (form_first_page)**: This is the beginning of the survey.
* **Second page (form_second_page)**: Continue providing your responses to the questions.
* **Third page (form_third_page)**: More questions for you to answer.
* ...
* **Twentieth page (form_twentieth_page)**: This is the end of the survey.
* **Twenty-first page (form_twentyfirst_page)**: This page is not expected to be filled out, as it's not part of the survey (it might be a placeholder or a page not used in the survey).
* **Twenty-second page (form_twentysecond_page)**: This page is also not expected to be filled out.
* **Twenty-third page (form_twentythird_page)**: Another page not used in the survey.
* **Twenty-fourth page (form_twentyfourth_page)**: Not part of the survey.
* **Twenty-fifth page (form_twentyfifth_page)**: This is the last page of the survey, and you should be finished here.

Note: Since the form has no validation or required fields, you can fill out as many or as few pages as you like, but be aware that missing information might affect the survey's accuracy and usefulness.
