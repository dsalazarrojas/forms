# School Family Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback and opinions from students, families, and staff members about various aspects of a school environment. The goal is to help the school identify areas for improvement and provide better services to its students and families.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the relevant section based on your role (Student, Family, or Staff Member).
2. Fill out the corresponding fields for your section.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in your answers.
4. Select the relevant option in "School Support 2" if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Feedback (Student Survey)**: This section is for students to provide feedback on their school experience. It is required for all students.
* **Family Feedback (Family Survey)**: This section is for parents to provide feedback on their experience with the school. It is required for all parents.
* **School Staffing (School Staffing)**: This section is for staff members to provide feedback on the school's staffing levels. It is required for all staff members.
* **Parent Involvement (Parent Involvement)**: This section is for parents to provide feedback on their involvement in their child's education. It is not required but highly recommended.
* **School Environment (School Environment)**: This section is for staff members to provide feedback on the school's environment. It is not required but highly recommended.
* **Communication (Communication)**: This section is for staff members to provide feedback on communication between school staff and parents. It is required for all staff members.
* **Teacher Involvement (Teacher Involvement)**: This section is for teachers to provide feedback on their involvement with students. It is not required but highly recommended.
* **Prophets (Prophets)**: This section is for staff members to provide feedback on prophets. It is not required but highly recommended.
* **Parent Education (Parent Education)**: This section is for parents to provide feedback on educational programs for parents. It is required for all parents.
* **School Support (School Support)**: This section is for staff members to provide feedback on school support services. It is required for all staff members.
* **Student Survey Note (Student Survey Note)**: This section is for students to provide additional comments on their survey. It is not required but highly recommended.
* **Family Survey Note (Family Survey Note)**: This section is for families to provide additional comments on their survey. It is not required but highly recommended.
* **School Support 2 (School Support 2)**: This section is for staff members to select multiple options for school support services. It is required for all staff members.
