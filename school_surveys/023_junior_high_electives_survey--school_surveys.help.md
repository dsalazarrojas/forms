# Junior High Electives Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Junior High Electives Survey is a form used to gather information from junior high students, teachers, or parents about their elective preferences and other relevant details. This form helps the school administration and teachers plan and organize electives for the upcoming semester.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your student ID number in the "Student ID" field.
4. Select your grade level from the available options in the "Grade Level" field.
5. Type your current course in the "Current Course" field.
6. Check the boxes for the electives you are interested in, in the "Elective Preferences" field.
7. Enter any special requests you have in the "Special Requests" field (optional).
8. If applicable, enter any past courses you've taken that are relevant to your elective preferences.
9. Enter your parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
10. Enter your parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
11. Enter your own phone number in the "Student Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to help the school administration and teachers address you correctly.
* **Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name to help the school administration and teachers address you correctly.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number to help the school administration track your progress and records.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, text, required): Select your current grade level (e.g., 6-8) to help the school administration understand your elective preferences.
* **Current Course** (`current_course`, text, required): Type the name of your current course to help the school administration understand your course history and elective preferences.
* **Elective Preferences** (`elective_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Check the boxes for the electives you are interested in taking. This will help the school administration plan and schedule electives for the upcoming semester.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or accommodations, enter them here to help the school administration and teachers understand your needs.
* **Past Courses** (`past_courses`, text, required): If applicable, enter any past courses you've taken that are relevant to your elective preferences.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your parent's email address to ensure they are informed about your elective preferences and progress.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your parent's phone number to ensure they can be reached with any urgent matters.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Enter your own phone number to ensure you can be reached in case of emergency or important updates.
