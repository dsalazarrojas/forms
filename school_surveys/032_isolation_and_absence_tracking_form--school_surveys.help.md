# Isolation And Absence Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Isolation And Absence Tracking Form is used to document instances of student isolation or absence in a school setting. This form helps teachers and administrators track and monitor these instances accurately.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the teacher's name in the "teacher name" field.
2. If necessary, enter the student's ID number in the "student id" field.
3. Enter the start and end times of the isolation or absence in the "start time" and "end time" fields, respectively.
4. Provide a brief reason for the isolation or absence in the "reason" field.
5. Choose the date of the incident in the "date" field.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note" field.
7. If you need to contact the student, enter their email address in the "email" field.
8. If you need to reach out to the student, enter their phone number in the "phone" field.
9. If multiple reasons apply, select all relevant options in the "select multiple" field.
10. If only one reason applies, select the correct option in the "select one" field.
11. If this is a case of absence, mark "True" in the "is absence" field; otherwise, mark "False".
12. If the times are in 24-hour format, enter them accordingly in the "start time 24hr" and "end time 24hr" fields.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **teacher_name** (teacher name, required): Enter the name of the teacher who is submitting this form.
* **student_id** (student id, optional): Enter the ID number of the student involved, if applicable.
* **start_time** (start time, optional): Enter the start time of the isolation or absence.
* **end_time** (end time, optional): Enter the end time of the isolation or absence.
* **reason** (reason, required): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the student's isolation or absence.
* **date** (date, required): Choose the date of the incident.
* **note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding the situation.
* **email** (email, optional): If you need to contact the student, enter their email address.
* **phone** (phone, optional): If you need to reach out to the student, enter their phone number.
* **select_multiple** (select multiple, optional): Select all applicable reasons for the student's isolation or absence.
* **select_one** (select one, optional): Select the primary reason for the student's isolation or absence.
* **is_absence** (is absence, optional): Mark "True" if this is a case of absence; otherwise, mark "False".
* **start_time_24hr** (start time 24hr, optional): Enter the start time in 24-hour format.
* **end_time_24hr** (end time 24hr, optional): Enter the end time in 24-hour format.
