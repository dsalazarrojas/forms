# Digital Learning Experience Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather feedback about your digital learning experience in our online courses. Your responses will help us understand what works well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your User Name in the first field.
2. Provide your school's name in the second field.
3. Enter your email address to help us contact you (if necessary).
4. Describe the title of the course you took.
5. Rate your teaching experience with our platform using the options provided (1, 2, or 3).
6. If you have any feedback or comments about your experience, please enter them in the text field.
7. Choose the correct date and time you completed the survey.
8. Indicate your level of agreement with the provided statements.
9. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your phone number (if you provided it earlier).
10. If you have any further comments or feedback, please enter them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your User Name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the school you are currently or previously affiliated with.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you (if necessary).
* **Course Title** (`course_title`, text, required/optional): Describe the title of the course you took.
* **Teaching Style** (`teaching_style`, text, required/optional): Rate your experience with our teaching style using the options provided (1, 2, or 3).
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required/optional): Share any comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Survey Date** (`survey_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you completed this survey.
* **Survey Time** (`survey_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time you completed this survey.
* **Teaching Experience** (`teaching_experience`, select_one, required/optional): Rate your experience with our teaching style using the options provided (1, 2, or 3).
* **Teaching Experience Other** (`teaching_experience_other`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments about your teaching experience, please enter them here.
* **Agree** (`agree`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your level of agreement with the provided statements.
* **Other Agree** (`other_agree`, text, required/optional): If you have any further comments or feedback about the provided statements, please enter them here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your email address (1, 2, or 3).
* **Confirm Email Other** (`confirm_email_other`, text, required/optional): If you have any further comments or feedback about your email address, please enter them here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have any further comments or feedback about your phone number, please enter them here.
* **Other Phone** (`other_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback about your phone number, please enter them here.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your school's phone number (if applicable).
* **Agree Phone** (`agree_phone`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate your level of agreement with the provided statements about your phone experience.
* **Other Agree Phone** (`other_agree_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have any further comments or feedback about the provided statements, please enter them here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required/optional): If you have any further comments or feedback about your phone number, please enter them here.
