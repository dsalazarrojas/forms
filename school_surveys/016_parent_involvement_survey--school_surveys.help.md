# Parent Involvement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Involvement Survey is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their involvement in their child's education. This survey aims to help schools or educational institutions gain insights into the level of parent involvement and tailor their communication and engagement strategies accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct option for each field, as indicated by the label.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the person indicated in the contact fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Parent Involvement Level (`parent_involvement_level`)

* Label: Parent Involvement Level
* Type: Select One
* Options: 1, 2, 3, 4
* Explanation: This question asks about the level of your involvement in your child's education. Choose the option that best describes your level of involvement.

### 2. Parent Involvement Frequency (`parent_involvement_frequency`)
