# Parent Roles And Responsibilities Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information about the parent/guardian's level of involvement and engagement in their child's education. It will help the school understand the strengths and challenges of each parent/guardian, ultimately contributing to a more personalized and supportive educational experience for the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct parent/guardian role (Father, Mother, Guardian, or Other).
2. Select the level of communication with the school (text field).
3. Enter the time spent on school attendance (time field).
4. Choose the level of parent involvement (select one: High, Medium, Low).
5. Add any additional notes about parent support (note field).
6. Enter the child's performance (number field).
7. Select the level of parent engagement (select multiple: High, Medium, Low).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Roles And Responsibilities** (`parent_roles`, `select_multiple`): Choose the parent/guardian role from the options: Father, Mother, Guardian, or Other. This selection will help the school understand the primary contact person for the child.
* **Communication** (`communication`, `text`): Enter a brief description of how often you communicate with the school (e.g., daily, weekly, occasionally).
* **School Attendance** (`school_attendance`, `time`): Enter the time spent on school attendance, such as the number of hours per week.
* **Parent Involvement** (`parent_involvement`, `select_one`): Choose the level of involvement from the options: High, Medium, Low, indicating how actively you engage with your child's education.
* **Parent Support** (`parent_support`, `note`): Add any additional notes about the type of support you provide to your child.
* **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, `number`): Enter a numerical value representing the child's performance, for example, grades or scores.
* **Parent Engagement** (`parent_engagement`, `select_multiple`): Choose the level of engagement from the options: High, Medium, Low, indicating how actively you engage with your child's education.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, please reach out to the school for clarification.
- This form is confidential and will only be used to create a comprehensive picture of parent/guardian roles and responsibilities.
- Please fill out this form for each parent/guardian in the household.
