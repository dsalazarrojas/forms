# Field Trip Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback and information from students and teachers about a field trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event type (e.g., Field Trip).
2. Enter the event name and date.
3. Provide feedback and comments as a student or teacher.
4. Rate your overall experience and satisfaction level.
5. Add any additional comments or comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event or trip you attended.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the type of event you attended. You can choose from the following options:
		- Field Trip
		- Other
* **Event Type Other** (`event_type_other`, text, optional): If you selected "Other," provide a brief description of the type of event.
* **Trip Date** (`trip_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Feedback as Student** (`student_feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about the trip.
* **Feedback as Teacher** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts and feelings about the trip.
* **Trip Location** (`trip_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the trip.
* **Trip Duration** (`trip_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the trip.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the event or trip.
* **Student Participation** (`student_participation`, number, optional): Rate how much you enjoyed the trip (1-5).
* **Teacher Participation** (`teacher_participation`, number, optional): Rate how much you enjoyed the trip (1-5).
* **Student Interest** (`student_interest`, number, optional): Rate how interested you were in the trip (1-5).
* **Teacher Interest** (`teacher_interest`, number, optional): Rate how interested you were in the trip (1-5).
* **Student Rating** (`student_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience (1-5).
* **Teacher Rating** (`teacher_rating`, number, optional): Rate your overall experience (1-5).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction level (1-5).
* **Trip Budget** (`trip_budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget for the trip.
* **Trip Cost** (`trip_cost`, number, optional): Enter the cost of the trip.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, optional):
	+ Rate your overall experience:
		- Very Good
		- Fair
		- Poor
		- Very Poor
		- Extremely Poor
* **Additional Comments** (`trip_organization`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or thoughts.
