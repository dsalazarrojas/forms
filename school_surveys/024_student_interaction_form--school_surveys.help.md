# Student Interaction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Interaction Form is designed to facilitate communication and collaboration between teachers and students. By completing this form, teachers and students can exchange information about student progress, concerns, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

* Please fill out the form with the required fields and provide any necessary information.
* If a field is not required, feel free to fill it in if you have relevant information.
* Use the drop-down menu for the student_subjects field to select all applicable subjects.
* For the student_interaction_date field, select the date of the interaction.
* Use the teacher_name field to enter the teacher's name.
* The notes field is for any additional comments or notes regarding the student interaction.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration.
* The form can be completed by both teachers and students.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Interaction Form** (`1`, text, not required): This is the main title of the form.
* **Student Name** (`2`, text, not required): Enter the student's name.
* **Student Grade** (`3`, number, not required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Student Subjects** (`4`, select_multiple, not required): Select all applicable subjects for the student, for example, Math, English, Science, etc.
* **Student Interaction Date** (`5`, date, not required): Select the date of the last interaction with the student.
* **Teacher Name** (`6`, text, not required): Enter your name as a teacher.
* **Notes** (`7`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the student interaction.
* **Teacher Email** (`8`, email, not required): Enter your school email address.
* **Teacher Phone** (`9`, text, not required): Enter your school phone number.
* **Student Feedback** (`10`, text, not required): Enter any feedback from the student.
* **Student Concern** (`11`, text, not required): Enter any concerns you have about the student.
* **Teacher Feedback** (`12`, text, not required): Enter any feedback for the teacher to the student.
* **Student Progress** (`13`, select_one, not required): Select the student's current progress status, e.g., "Student is on track," "Student needs extra help," or "Student is struggling."
* **Next Steps** (`14`, text, not required): Enter any next steps or actions to be taken for the student.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information gathered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If unsure about any field, contact the school administration for guidance.
* Use the form as a means to facilitate communication and improve student progress.
