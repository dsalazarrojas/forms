# High School Student Climate Change Awareness Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about high school students' awareness and perception of climate change. The purpose of this survey is to understand how students think about climate change, its causes, effects, and possible solutions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please read each question carefully and answer honestly. You can select one or multiple options for questions that require a choice. For questions that ask for free-form text, provide as much detail as you can.
