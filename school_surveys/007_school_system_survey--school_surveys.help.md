# School_System_Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about schools and their systems to help evaluate and improve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer all required and optional questions as accurately as possible.
2. Use the select multiple options for "school_survey_questions" to provide multiple questions that will be asked in the survey.
3. Provide any additional information in the "school_survey_comments" field.
4. Rate the importance of each of the five important categories.
5. Enter your email address in the "school_survey_email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "school_survey_phone" field.
7. Enter the date and time of the survey in the "school_system_survey_date" and "school_system_survey_time" fields, respectively.
8. Enter any additional information in the "school_system_survey_additional_1", "school_system_survey_additional_2", and "school_system_survey_additional_3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school_system_survey_school_surveys**: Enter the name of the school system being surveyed.
* **school_survey_questions**: Select all questions that you want to ask in the survey.
	+ Example: What is the school's mission statement?
* **school_survey_comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the school system.
* **school_survey_important_1**: Rate the importance of "Very Important" or "Not Important" for the first category.
* **school_survey_important_2**: Rate the importance of "Very Important" or "Not Important" for the second category.
* **school_survey_important_3**: Rate the importance of "Very Important" or "Not Important" for the third category.
* **school_survey_important_4**: Rate the importance of "Very Important" or "Not Important" for the fourth category.
* **school_survey_important_5**: Rate the importance of "Very Important" or "Not Important" for the fifth category.
* **school_survey_email**: Enter your email address.
* **school_survey_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **school_system_survey_date**: Enter the date of the survey.
* **school_system_survey_time**: Enter the time of the survey.
* **school_survey_notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about the survey.
* **school_system_survey_additional_1**: Enter any additional information about the school system.
* **school_system_survey_additional_2**: Enter any additional information about the school system.
* **school_system_survey_additional_3**: Enter any additional information about the school system.
