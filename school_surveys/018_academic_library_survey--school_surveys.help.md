# Academic Library Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Library Survey is designed to gather feedback from users on their experience with the library's services and resources. The survey will help the library understand areas of strength and weakness, and inform decisions to enhance the user experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2.  Select the options that best fit your experience with the library.
3.  Provide detailed answers where applicable.
4.  Skip any questions that do not apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Status** (Required): Please select the category that best describes your relationship with the library.
    * Undergraduate student
    * Graduate student
    * Faculty member
    * Staff member
    * Alumni
    * Visitor
    * Other
* **Frequency of Visits** (Required): Indicate how often you visit the library.
    * Daily
    * Several times a week
    * Once a week
    * Several times a month
    * Once a month
    * Rarely
    * First time
* **Primary Purpose for Using the Library** (Not required): Please select all that apply to your main reason for using the library.
    * Research
    * Study space
    * Access to databases
    * Borrow books
    * Attend workshops
    * Meet with colleagues
    * Use computers/software
    * Other
* **Overall Satisfaction with Library Services** (Required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the library (1-5 scale).
* **Library Space Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the library's physical space (1-5 scale).
* **Quiet Study Areas Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the availability of quiet study areas (1-5 scale).
* **Collaborative Spaces Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with collaborative work spaces (1-5 scale).
* **Technology Resources Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the library's technology resources (1-5 scale).
* **Staff Assistance Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the help provided by library staff (1-5 scale).
* **Digital Resources Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the library's digital resources (1-5 scale).
* **Book Collection Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the library's book collection (1-5 scale).
* **Online Catalog Rating** (Required): Rate your satisfaction with the online catalog system (1-5 scale).
* **Library Hours Suitability** (Required): Indicate whether the library hours are suitable for your needs.
    * Very suitable
    * Suitable
    * Neutral
    * Not very suitable
    * Not suitable at all
* **Additional Services Desired** (Not required): If there are any additional services you would like to see offered by the library, please specify.
* **Biggest Challenge** (Not required): If you have experienced a challenge while using the library, please describe it.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (Not required): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please provide them.
