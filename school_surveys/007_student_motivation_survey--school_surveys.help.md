# Student Motivation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Motivation Survey is a tool for collecting information about a student's motivation, engagement, and support levels. This form helps educators understand the student's needs and strengths, enabling them to provide targeted support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level.
3. Enter the parent's name.
4. Select the student's motivation level (High, Low, or Medium).
5. Enter the student's goal, strength, and weakness.
6. Check if the student is involved in school activities.
7. Check if the student receives school support.
8. Enter the student's awareness level.
9. Enter the parent's contact information.
10. Provide any additional comments from the student.
11. Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
12. Check if the student has a school counselor.
13. Enter any comments from the school counselor (if applicable).
14. Select the date and time of the survey.
15. (Optional) Enter the form's ID and description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name.
* **Student grade level** (2, number, required/optional): Select the student's grade level.
* **Parent name** (3, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's name.
* **Student motivation level** (4, select_one, required): Select High, Low, or Medium.
* **Student goal** (5, text, required/optional): Enter the student's goal.
* **Student strength** (6, text, required/optional): Enter the student's strength.
* **Student weakness** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the student's weakness.
* **Student need** (8, text, required/optional): Enter the student's need.
* **School involvement** (9, select_multiple, required): Check if the student is involved in school activities.
* **School support** (10, select_multiple, required): Check if the student receives school support.
* **Student awareness** (11, number, required/optional): Enter the student's awareness level.
* **Parent contact** (12, text, required): Enter the parent's contact information.
* **Student comments** (13, note, required/optional): Enter any comments from the student.
* **Student email** (14, email, required/optional): Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **School counselor** (15, select_one, required): Check if the student has a school counselor.
* **School counselor comments** (16, note, required/optional): Enter any comments from the school counselor (if applicable).
* **Student survey date** (17, date, required): Select the date of the survey.
* **Student survey time** (18, time, required/optional): Select the time of the survey.
* **Form ID** (19, number, required/optional): Enter the form's ID.
* **Category** (20, select_one, required/optional): Select the category (School Surveys or Student Assessments).
* **Description** (21, note, required/optional): Enter a description of the form.
* **Assigned tool** (22, select_one, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to complete the form.
* **Output file** (23, select_one, required/optional): Select the output file format (e.g., 007 student motivation survey--school surveys.yaml).
* **Student survey type** (24, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of survey (student motivation or student engagement).
* **Form ID** (25, number, required/optional): Enter the form's ID again.
