# Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect users' perceptions, knowledge, and opinions on foreign conflicts. It will help us understand their awareness and attitudes towards these conflicts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each question carefully and answer accordingly.
2. Choose the options that best describe your knowledge, feelings, and opinions on foreign conflicts.
3. You can skip any questions that you are unsure or don't feel comfortable answering.
4. If you have any additional comments or thoughts, you can write them in the "Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Foreign Conflict Awareness Survey** (`intro_page`, note, not required): This is an introduction to the form, explaining its purpose and what to expect.
* **What do you know about foreign conflicts?** (`general_knowledge`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply to the foreign conflicts you are familiar with:
	+ Afghanistan
	+ Syria
	+ Yemen
	+ South Sudan
	+ Ukraine
* **What are your main sources of information about foreign conflicts?** (`info_sources_1`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply to the sources you use to learn about foreign conflicts:
	+ Newspapers
	+ TV and radio
	+ Social media
	+ Books
	+ Other
	+ Online news websites
	+ Podcasts
	+ None of the above
* **Other sources of foreign conflict information** (`info_sources_2`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply to additional sources you use to learn about foreign conflicts:
	+ Friends
	+ Family
	+ Online forums
	+ Social media influencers
	+ Other
* **How much do you know about this conflict?** (`conflict_knowledge`, select_one, not required): Rate your level of knowledge about the conflict:
	+ A lot
	+ A little
	+ Not much
	+ None
	+ Unsure
* **How do you feel about this conflict?** (`conflict_feeling`, select_multiple, not required): Check all that apply to how you feel about the conflict:
	+ Concerned
	+ Indifferent
	+ Angry
	+ Frustrated
	+ Sad
	+ Scared
	+ None
* **How much risk do you perceive for the United States?** (`perceived_risk`, select_multiple, not required): Rate your perception of the risk for the United States:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
	+ None
	+ Unsure
* **How much do you think the United States will contribute to a resolution of the conflict?** (`perceived_contribution`, select_multiple, not required): Rate your perception of the United States' contribution:
	+ High
	+ Moderate
	+ Low
	+ None
	+ Unsure
* **Should the United States get involved in this conflict?** (`international_involvement`, select_multiple, not required): Decide whether the United States should get involved:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Unsure
* **Do you support or oppose United States involvement in this conflict?** (`us_support`, select_multiple, not required): Decide whether you support or oppose United States involvement:
	+ Support
	+ Oppose
	+ Unsure
* **Additional comments** (`comments`, note, not required): If you have any additional thoughts or comments, you can write them here.
