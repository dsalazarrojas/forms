# Packing School Lunch Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Packing School Lunch Survey is a tool designed to gather information about school lunches and their impact on students. This survey aims to understand what students like and dislike about their lunches, how they feel about their hunger levels, and what their favorite foods are.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

* Start on the first page and work your way through each question.
* Read each question carefully and select the answer that best describes your thoughts.
* For select questions, choose all that apply.
* For date questions, enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* You can add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, `note`, required: false): This page provides an introduction to the survey and explains its purpose.
* **What do children like about their lunches?** (`question_1`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the options that apply to what children like about their lunches.
	+ option 1: They enjoy eating a variety of foods.
	+ option 2: They like the taste of school lunches.
	+ option 3: They find school lunches convenient.
* **Do children feel hungry?** (`question_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the options below:
	+ option 1: Yes, often.
	+ option 2: Rarely.
	+ option 3: Never.
* **Do children feel hungry after 10 minutes?** (`question_3`, `time`, required: false): Please select a time frame to indicate when children usually feel hungry after eating their school lunch:
	+ 10 minutes.
	+ 30 minutes.
	+ 1 hour.
	+ Never.
* **Favorite food** (`question_4`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following options:
	+ option 1: Ham sandwich.
	+ option 2: Fresh fruit.
	+ option 3: Yogurt.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required: false): Please add any additional comments or thoughts about school lunches.
* **What do parents do?** (`question_5`, `date`, required: false): Please enter the date when parents usually pack or prepare lunches:
	+ MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Favorite food 2** (`question_6`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select one of the following options:
	+ option 1: Pizza.
	+ option 2: Chicken nuggets.
	+ option 3: Salad.

## Tips
* Please answer each question honestly and to the best of your knowledge.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select the option that best describes your thoughts.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, please add them in the "Notes" field.
