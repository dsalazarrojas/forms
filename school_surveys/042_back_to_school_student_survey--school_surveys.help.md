# Back To School Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Back To School Student Survey is a tool for the school administration to understand your thoughts, preferences, and concerns as you return to school. Your responses will help us tailor our services and support to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student name in the required field.
2. Select your current grade level from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred learning format (In person, Hybrid, or Remote).
4. If applicable, select any support services you may need (Tutoring, Counseling, Health services, or Technology support).
5. Briefly describe your extracurricular interests.
6. Rate your perception of school safety (Not safe, Somewhat safe, Safe, or Very safe).
7. Indicate if you require mental health support.
8. Add any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.
9. Complete the form by selecting a date for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **Grade**: Select your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.).
* **Preferred learning format**: Choose how you'd like to learn best (In person, Hybrid, or Remote).
	+ In person: You'd like to learn through face-to-face interactions with teachers and peers.
	+ Hybrid: You'd like a mix of in-person and online learning.
	+ Remote: You'd prefer online learning without on-campus presence.
* **Biggest concern this school year**: If applicable, briefly describe your biggest concern.
* **Support services needed**: Select any support services you require (Tutoring, Counseling, Health services, or Technology support). You can choose one or multiple services.
* **Extracurricular interest**: Briefly describe your interests outside of the classroom.
* **Feedback on school safety**: Rate how safe you feel on campus (Not safe, Somewhat safe, Safe, or Very safe).
* **Mental health needs**: Indicate if you require support.
* **Additional comments**: Share any other thoughts or feedback you'd like to express.
* **Submission date**: Select a date for form submission.

Note: Required fields are marked as such.
