# Student Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Questionnaire is designed to gather feedback and suggestions from students about their school experience. This form is used to collect information about the student's school and their opinions on various aspects of their educational experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Enter your honest and constructive feedback and suggestions for each question.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **school** (text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **teachers** (text, optional): Describe your experiences with your teachers. 
* **classroom** (text, optional): Describe your classroom's facilities and environment. 
* **school_rating** (number, optional): Rate your school from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **teacher_rating** (number, optional): Rate your teacher from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **classroom_rating** (number, optional): Rate your classroom from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **suggestions** (text, optional): Share any suggestions you have for improving your school.
* **comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback you have about your school.
* **suggestions 2** (text, optional): Share any more suggestions you have for improving your school.

## Tips
Please be as honest and constructive as possible when completing this form. Your feedback and suggestions are valuable to us and help us improve your school.
