# School Extracurricular Activities Parent Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather your feedback and opinions on your child's school extracurricular activities. Your input will help us identify areas of improvement and provide better services to our students and parents.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please answer all the questions to the best of your ability.
- You can select multiple options for the parent_club_interests field.
- If you have any comments or feedback, please type them in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Club Interests** (`parent_club_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the parent club interests that your child is interested in. For example, you can choose "School Clubs", "Sports Teams", "Drama Club", "Student Council", or "Other" if none of the above options match.
* **What Do You Like About Our School's Extracurricular Activities?** (`parent_feedback`, text, optional): Please describe what you like about our school's extracurricular activities.
* **Parent Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you for any further questions or actions.
* **Parent Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Any Comments?** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or feedback, please type them here.
