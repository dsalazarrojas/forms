# School Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from students and faculty about their experience and satisfaction at their school. The information collected will help us understand your thoughts and suggestions, which will be used to improve our services and provide a better environment for students and staff.

## How To Complete This Form

* To begin, simply select the page you'd like to answer, and you will see a list of questions and fields that apply to that page.
* For each question or field, provide as much detail as you can.
* You can always ask for help if you have questions or need clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school. This will help us identify which school you are providing feedback about.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is a text box where you can share any thoughts or concerns you may have. Feel free to write as much or as little as you like.
* **Questions** (`questions`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Very Satisfied: Select this option if you are extremely satisfied with your experience at your school.
	+ Somewhat Satisfied: Choose this option if you are slightly satisfied with your experience.
	+ Neutral: Select this option if you neither strongly nor weakly agree with the other options.
	+ Dissatisfied: Choose this option if you are slightly dissatisfied with your experience.
	+ Very Dissatisfied: Select this option if you are extremely dissatisfied with your experience.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Share any thoughts or suggestions you may have. This is an open-ended field where you can type as much or as little as you like.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you if we need more information.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: false): Note: This is a duplicate label, you may disregard this field and only fill out one of them.

Note: Please do not fill out the duplicate field of Contact Info. You can choose to fill out either the email or phone number field, not both.
