# Youth Sports Parent Communication Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents to provide feedback on the communication quality and preferences of the youth sports team, helping the team to better understand their needs and improve communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions and fill out the fields as prompted. If you have any additional feedback or comments, please feel free to type them in the "Notes" field. The form is divided into several sections, each with clear labels and instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How would you rate the overall parent communication quality?**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the quality of communication from the team. This will help us understand how well we're doing in keeping you informed.
* **What channel do you prefer for communication?**: Select your preferred channel for communication, such as Channel 1, 2, 3, or 4. This will help us know how you want to receive information from us.
* **What would you like to see improved in communication?**: Share any areas for improvement in our communication with you. This is your chance to tell us what we can do better.
* **Email**: If you would like to provide an email address, please type it in this field. We'll make sure to keep you informed via email.
* **Phone**: If you would like to provide a phone number, please type it in this field. We'll call you if we need to.
* **Notes**: If you have any additional feedback or comments, please type them in this field. We value your input and will use it to improve our communication.
* **Is there any additional feedback you would like to provide for the team?**: This is your chance to share any other thoughts or comments about our communication.
* **What type of information would you like to receive from the team?**: Select which types of information you would like to receive from us, such as team schedule and game details, team announcements and reminders, or other.
* **Would you like the team to send you announcements?**: Let us know if you would like to receive announcements from us.
* **Would you like to receive game schedule and details?**: Let us know if you would like to receive schedule and details for games.
* **Additional feedback**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them with us.

## Tips
* Be honest: We value your feedback and appreciate your honesty. Your input will help us improve our communication and serve you better.
* Be specific: Try to be specific with your feedback, it will help us understand your needs better.
* Keep it concise: Please keep your answers brief and to the point.
