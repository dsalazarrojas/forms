# Early Childhood Engagement Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Early Childhood Engagement Survey" is designed to gather feedback from parents, teachers, and school administrators on the quality of early childhood services. This information helps schools identify areas of improvement, understand stakeholder engagement, and make informed decisions for better services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the introduction, which provides an overview of the survey and its purpose.
2. Select your role among the options (Parent, Teacher, School Administrator).
3. For each section, choose the relevant options that reflect your experiences and opinions.
4. Provide comments if you have additional insights or concerns.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction to Survey**: This text box is for you to read and understand the survey's purpose and context.
* **Parent Survey Section**: Select your level of engagement with your child's education (Always Engaged, Sometimes Engaged, Never Engaged).
	+ If you choose "Always Engaged," select the options that best describe your level of engagement.
* **How often do you engage with your child's education?**: Choose how frequently you interact with your child's education (Almost Daily, Several times a week, Rarely).
* **How would you rate the quality of your child's education?**: Rate the quality of your child's education (Excellent, Good, Satisfactory).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns you have regarding your child's education.
* **Teacher Section**: Select your level of availability to your child's education (Always Available, Sometimes Available, Rarely Available).
* **How often do you engage with your child's teachers?**: Choose how frequently you interact with your child's teachers (Almost Daily, Several times a week, Rarely).
* **How supportive are you of your child's teacher?**: Rate your level of support for your child's teacher (Very Supportive, Somewhat Supportive, Not Supportive at All).
* **Comments 2**: Add any additional comments or concerns you have regarding your child's education.
* **School Survey Section**: Select your opinion on the school's quality (Excellent, Good, Fair).
* **How would you rate the quality of your school?**: Rate the quality of your school (5-Star, 4-Star, 3-Star).
* **How supportive is your school?**: Rate your school's level of support (Very Supportive, Somewhat Supportive, Not Supportive at All).
* **Comments 3**: Add any additional comments or concerns you have regarding your school.
* **School Comments**: Add any final comments or concerns you have regarding your school.
* **Submission**: Choose how you wish to proceed with your submission (Submit, Save and Resume Later, Do Not Submit).

By following this guide, you'll be able to complete the "Early Childhood Engagement Survey" accurately and efficiently, providing valuable feedback to enhance the quality of early childhood services.
