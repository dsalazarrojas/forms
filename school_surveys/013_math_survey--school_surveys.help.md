# Math Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Math Survey form is designed to collect information from students about their favorite math subjects, comments, and other related details.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
  1. Answer each field as accurately as possible.
  2. Provide your favorite math subjects (yes/no).
  3. Add any comments about math-related topics.
  4. Provide your name.
  5. Enter your email address.
  6. Enter your phone number.
  7. Choose a date for a math-related event or activity.
  8. Select a time for a math-related event or activity.
  9. Choose whether you have favorite math subjects (yes/no).
  10. Select any math subjects that you enjoy studying (yes/no).
  11. Select any math subjects that you enjoy studying (yes/no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Math Survey 1** (`math_survey_1`, text, not required): This field is a free-text field for students to write their thoughts or comments about math-related topics.
* **Favorite Math Subject** (`favorite_math_subject`, select_multiple, not required): 
  Please select all the math subjects that you like. You can choose multiple subjects.
* **Comments** (`teacher_comments`, note, not required): This field is for students to add any additional comments or remarks about math-related topics.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, not required): Please enter your name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, not required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Math Event Date** (`math_survey_8`, date, not required): This field is for students to choose a date for a math-related event or activity.
* **Math Event Time** (`math_survey_9`, time, not required): This field is for students to select a time for a math-related event or activity.
* **Yes to Favorite Subject** (`math_survey_10`, select_one, not required): This field is asking if you have favorite math subjects.
* **Studying Subjects** (`math_survey_11`, select_multiple, not required): Please select all math subjects that you enjoy studying.
* **Studying Subjects** (`math_survey_12`, select_multiple, not required): This field is asking if you enjoy studying math subjects.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields carefully and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help.
- If you have any comments, please add them in the "Comments" field.
