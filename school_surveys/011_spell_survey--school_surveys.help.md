# Spell Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spell Survey is a form intended to gather information from users about their experiences or perceptions related to a specific spell. It is used to collect data and feedback from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form on the first page, labeled "form_start".
2. On the next page, select the relevant answer for each question, including "spell_type", "question1", "question2", and "spellcast".
3. Provide any additional information or comments in the "word_list", "word_list_2", "note", and "phone" fields, if applicable.
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field, if you would like to receive further information or updates.
5. Once you have answered all questions, click the "submit" button to send the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_start**: This field is used to indicate the start of the form. Please enter your name.
* **spell_type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have had the opportunity to practice the spell in question.
* **word_list**: Enter a list of words that relate to the spell or topic you are surveying, if applicable.
* **word_list_2**: Enter another list of words that relate to the spell or topic you are surveying, if applicable.
* **question1**: Enter your response to the first question, if applicable.
* **question2**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to answer the second question.
* **spellcast**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have experienced any issues with the spell or topic.
* **note**: Enter any additional comments or observations you would like to share, if applicable.
* **email**: Enter your email address if you would like to receive further information or updates.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to provide an alternative contact method.
* **submit**: Click this button to send the survey once you have completed all fields.
* **form_end**: This field is used to indicate the end of the form. Please do not enter anything in this field.
