# Student Inclusivity Advocacy Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps to gather information about the student's experience on campus and identify areas where the campus can improve to create a more inclusive and safe space.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the options that apply to you.
2. Answer all the questions honestly, and be as detailed as you can.
3. If you need help or are unsure about any of the questions, please feel free to ask for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Climate** (`student_climate`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' whether you feel included and supported on campus. This field helps us understand your comfort level with your campus experience.
* **Discrimination Experiences** (`discrimination_experiences`, select_one, required/optional): Choose 'Yes' if you have experienced discrimination, or 'No' if you haven't. This field helps us understand your experiences with discrimination on campus.
* **Advocacy Interests** (`advocacy_interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' which of the listed interests you are interested in advocating for. This field helps us understand which areas you are passionate about.
* **Campus Climate** (`campus_climate`, text, required/optional): Describe how your campus environment supports your well-being. Please answer in as much detail as you can.
* **Discrimination Support** (`discrimination_support`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' whether you have received support for discrimination experiences. This field helps us understand how you have been supported on campus.
* **Campus Resources** (`campus_resources`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' which of the listed resources you find helpful for your well-being. This field helps us understand what resources you rely on for support.
* **Student Organizations** (`student_organizations`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' whether you participate in student organizations. This field helps us understand how you engage with your campus community.
* **Campus Services** (`campus_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' which of the listed services you use for your well-being. This field helps us understand how you access services on campus.
* **Student Life** (`student_life`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' which of the listed areas of student life you are interested in. This field helps us understand how you engage with your campus life.
* **Campus Leadership** (`campus_leadership`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' whether you are involved in campus leadership. This field helps us understand how you contribute to your campus community.

## Tips
* Please answer all the questions honestly, and be as detailed as you can.
* If you need help or are unsure about any of the questions, please feel free to ask for support.
* The information gathered in this form will help us create a more inclusive and safe space on campus. Thank you for taking the time to help us understand your experiences!
