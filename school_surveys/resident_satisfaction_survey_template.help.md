# Resident satisfaction survey template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback from residents on their overall satisfaction with their living experience at the school. It includes questions about their satisfaction, facilities, sense of belonging, and participation in school activities.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately, using the provided options for multiple-choice questions.
*   For the "What could improve your experience?" field, provide specific, concrete suggestions.
*   For the "Additional comments" field, share any general feedback or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **How satisfied are you with your overall living experience at the school?**
    *   Select one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
    *   This question aims to gauge your overall satisfaction with your living experience.
*   **How would you rate the quality of the school's facilities?**
    *   Select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor.
    *   This question assesses your perception of the quality of school facilities.
*   **Do you feel a sense of belonging and support within the school community?**
    *   Select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, Strongly disagree.
    *   This question investigates your sense of belonging and support within the school community.
*   **Which types of school activities or programs do you most often participate in or find valuable?**
    *   Select one or more options: Academic workshops, Social events, Sports/Clubs, Volunteer opportunities, Wellness programs.
    *   This question seeks to understand your participation in various school activities and programs.
*   **What is one thing that could significantly improve your experience as a resident?**
    *   Provide a specific, concrete suggestion for improvement.
    *   This question is designed to gather ideas for improvement.
*   **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**
    *   Share any general feedback or suggestions you may have.
    *   This question is an open-ended field for any additional thoughts or ideas.
