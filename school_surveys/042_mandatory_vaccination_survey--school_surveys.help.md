# Mandatory Vaccination Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about patient vaccinations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. You will be directed to the first page of the form, which is the "mandatory_vaccination_survey" page.
3. This page is an introduction page and does not require any input.
4. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
5. On the next page, you will be asked to select whether the patient has complied with the vaccination requirement.
6. Please select "True" if the patient has complied, and "False" otherwise.
7. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
8. On the next page, you will be asked to enter the vaccination date.
9. Enter the date of the vaccination using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
10. Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the next page.
11. On the next page, you will be asked to enter the vaccination dose number.
12. Enter the dose number of the vaccination (e.g., 1st, 2nd, etc.).
13. Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mandatory Vaccination Survey** (page 1, mandatory_vaccination_survey)
	+ This is an introduction page and does not require any input.
* **Patient Compliance** (page 2, patient_compliance)
	+ Select "True" if the patient has complied with the vaccination requirement, "False" otherwise.
* **Doctor Confirmation** (page 3, doctor_confirmation)
	+ Select "True" if the doctor has confirmed the vaccination, "False" otherwise.
* **Vaccination Date** (page 4, vaccination_date)
	+ Enter the date of the vaccination using the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Vaccination Dose** (page 5, vaccination_dose)
	+ Enter the dose number of the vaccination (e.g., 1st, 2nd, etc.).
