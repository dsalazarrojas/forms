# Student Engagement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Engagement Survey is designed to gather information on student engagement and satisfaction with a course or subject matter. It is meant to help instructors and educators understand how to improve student engagement and overall learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and options carefully.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Select one option for each question that best represents your thoughts and experiences.
4. Take as much time as you need to complete the form, but try to answer the questions in the order they appear.

	+ Intermediate: You have some experience in this subject or course, but still learning.
	+ Advanced: You have significant experience in this subject or course.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your answers, as this form is meant to gather accurate and meaningful feedback.
* If you have any additional comments or thoughts that do not fit into any of the provided options, please use the "Please provide any feedback" field to share them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to your instructor or course administrator.
