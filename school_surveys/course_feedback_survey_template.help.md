# course_feedback_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback on the course experience, including the content, organization, instructor, and overall satisfaction. Your responses will help improve the course and provide valuable insights for future students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions according to your honest experience.
2. Select all that apply for multiple-choice questions.
3. Provide detailed comments for open-ended questions.
4. Review your responses carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How satisfied were you with this course overall?**
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the course content and experience.
	+ Please select one of the options: Very satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, or Very dissatisfied.
* **Which topics or sessions did you find most valuable? (select all that apply)**
	+ This question asks about the specific topics or sessions that you found most valuable or helpful.
	+ Select all the options that apply:
		- Lectures
		- Group discussions
		- Practical exercises
		- Guest speakers
		- Readings
		- Labs
* **Was the course material well organized and easy to follow?**
	+ This question asks about your opinion on the organization and clarity of the course material.
	+ Please select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **How effective was the instructor in teaching the material?**
	+ This question asks about your opinion on the instructor's performance.
	+ Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How would you describe the course workload?**
	+ This question asks about the intensity or balance of the course workload.
	+ Please select one of the options: Too heavy, Just right, Too light.
* **How likely are you to recommend this course to a friend or colleague?**
	+ This question asks about your willingness to recommend the course to others.
	+ Please select one of the options: Very likely, Likely, Neutral, Unlikely, or Very unlikely.
* **What specific aspects of the course could be improved?**
	+ This question asks for any specific comments or suggestions you have about the course.
	+ Please provide detailed comments or suggestions.
* **Do you have any additional comments or suggestions?**
	+ This question allows you to add any additional thoughts or comments about the course.
	+ Please provide any relevant feedback or suggestions.
