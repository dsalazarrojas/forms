# Class Officer Speech Submission - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Class Officer Speech Submission form is a tool to collect information from students who are interested in running for class officer positions. This form helps to gather relevant details about the candidate, including their contact information, position sought, and campaign speech.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the Full Name field.
2. Enter your student ID number in the Student ID Number field.
3. Select your grade level from the Grade Level dropdown list.
4. Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
5. Select the position you are seeking from the Position Sought dropdown list.
6. Enter your previous leadership experience in the Previous Student Government or Leadership Experience field.
7. Write a brief statement in the Why Are You Running for This Position field.
8. Enter your campaign speech or statement in the Campaign Speech or Statement field.
9. Estimate the approximate length of your speech in seconds in the Approximate Length of Speech field.
10. Select the format of your speech delivery from the Format of Speech Delivery dropdown list.
11. Enter your campaign theme or slogan in the Campaign Theme or Slogan field (optional).
12. List your key promises or initiatives if elected in the Key Promises or Initiatives if Elected field.
13. Enter your platform goals for your class in the Platform Goals for Your Class field.
14. Enter a statement about your qualifications for this position in the Your Qualifications for This Position field.
15. Indicate whether you have a candidate photo to provide in the Candidate Photo Provided field.
16. Provide any additional campaign materials or links in the Additional Campaign Materials or Links field (optional).
17. Agree to follow school election policies and conduct rules in the I Agree to Follow School Election Policies and Conduct Rules field.
18. Certify that the speech and information you are submitting are your own work in the I Certify This Speech and Information Are My Own Work field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number as it appears on your student ID card.
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level from the dropdown list.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Position Sought**: Select the position you are seeking from the dropdown list.
* **Previous Student Government or Leadership Experience**: Describe any previous leadership experience you have had in student government or related activities.
* **Why Are You Running for This Position**: Write a brief statement about why you are running for this position.
* **Campaign Speech or Statement**: Type your campaign speech or statement in this field.
* **Approximate Length of Speech**: Estimate the approximate length of your speech in seconds.
* **Format of Speech Delivery**: Select the format of your speech delivery from the dropdown list.
* **Campaign Theme or Slogan**: Enter a brief statement about your campaign theme or slogan (optional).
* **Key Promises or Initiatives if Elected**: List any key promises or initiatives you will implement if elected.
* **Platform Goals for Your Class**: Enter a statement about your platform goals for your class.
* **Your Qualifications for This Position**: Describe your qualifications for this position.
* **Candidate Photo Provided**: Indicate whether you have a candidate photo to provide.
* **Additional Campaign Materials or Links**: Provide any additional campaign materials or links, such as a website or social media handles.
* **I Agree to Follow School Election Policies and Conduct Rules**: Agree to follow school election policies and conduct rules.
* **I Certify This Speech and Information Are My Own Work**: Certify that the speech and information you are submitting are your own work.

## Tips

* Make sure to proofread your submission for any errors or typos.
* Ensure that your speech is concise and within the estimated length.
* Provide clear and concise answers to all fields.
* Proofread your submission before submitting it.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses.
* Follow school election policies and conduct rules.
* Ensure that your speech and information are your own work.
