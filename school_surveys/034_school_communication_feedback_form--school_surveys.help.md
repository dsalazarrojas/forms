# School Communication Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback from school stakeholders on their current and preferred communication channels, the effectiveness of school communication, and any additional comments or suggestions they may have. Please complete this form to help us improve internal communication within the school.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Go through each page of the form, one at a time.
2.  Answer the questions or provide comments in the designated fields.
3.  For select fields (e.g., how you currently rely on for communication, which communication channels you prefer, etc.), choose one or more of the available options that best fit your response.
4.  For text fields (e.g., email, phone), enter your contact information as requested.
5.  Review your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
6.  Click the "Submit" button to submit your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (`Introduction`, `note`, required: `false`)
    This field is for you to provide any brief introduction or context for your feedback. It's entirely optional and not required for submission.
*   **How do you currently rely on for communication** (`How_do_you_currently_rely_on_for_Communication`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
    Please select one or more options that accurately represent how you currently rely on for communication. The available options are:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3
    *   Option 4
    *   Option 5
    *   Option 6
*   **Which communication channels do you prefer** (`Which_communication_channels_do_you_prefer`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
    Select one or more options that represent your preferred communication channels. The available options are:
    *   Option 1
    *   Option 2
    *   Option 3
    *   Option 4
    *   Option 5
    *   Option 6
*   **How effective is our communication** (`How_effective_is_our_communication`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
    Please select how effective you believe our communication is, using one of the available options:
    *   Effective
    *   Not Effective
*   **Feedback** (`Feedback`, `note`, required: `false`)
    Use this field to provide any additional feedback or comments about your experience with school communication. This field is entirely optional and not required for submission.
*   **Contact** (`Contact`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
    Select your preferred contact method for receiving communication:
    *   Email
    *   Phone
    *   None
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: `false`)
    Please enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: `false`)
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

**Tips**

*   Please answer the questions honestly and accurately.
*   Make sure to review your responses before submitting the form.
*   If you have any additional comments or feedback, please provide them in the "Feedback" field.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact information].
