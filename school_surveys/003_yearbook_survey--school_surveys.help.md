# Yearbook Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yearbook Survey is a form designed to collect information from students and teachers to create a yearbook, which is an annual publication that celebrates the achievements and memories of a school year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, optional): Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
* **School Year** (`school_year`, number, optional): Enter the year you are currently in school.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, number, optional): Enter the year you plan to graduate.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your student email address.
* **Teacher** (`teacher_name`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's name.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, optional): Enter your teacher's email address.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter your address (street, city, state, zip).
* **Teacher Address** (`teacher_address`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's address (street, city, state, zip).
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **Teacher City** (`teacher_city`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's city of residence.
* **Student State** (`student_state`, text, optional): Enter your state of residence.
* **Teacher State** (`teacher_state`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's state of residence.
* **Student Zip** (`student_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Teacher Zip** (`teacher_zip`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's zip code.
* **Teacher Country** (`teacher_country`, text, optional): Enter your teacher's country of residence.
* **Student Country** (`student_country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
* **Student School** (`student_school`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, text, optional): Enter your current grade level.
