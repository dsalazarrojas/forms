# Father's Day Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Father's Day Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your father's characteristics, preferences, and relationships. It helps to capture insights into what makes your dad special and what you appreciate about him.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Father's Day Questionnaire**: This is the title of the questionnaire that you will be filling out.
2. Fill out the following sections:
	* **Father's Name**: Enter your father's name.
	* **Father's Age**: Enter your father's age.
	* **What do you think of your dad?**: Share your thoughts about your dad in the text box.
	* **Best Dad Joke**: If you have a funny story or joke about your dad, share it in the text box.
	* **Father's favorite color**: Select the color you think your dad likes best.
	* **What does your dad do best?**: Select the activities your dad is good at.
	* **What does your dad like to do in his free time?**: Share what you know about your dad's hobbies or interests.
	* **Do you like your dad?**: Answer whether you like your dad or not.
	* **Do you like your dad's hobbies?**: Select whether you enjoy your dad's hobbies.
	* **What does your dad like to do?**: Choose an activity your dad enjoys.
	* **What does your dad like to do in his free time?**: Share more about your dad's interests or hobbies.
	* **Father's Day Message**: Check the boxes for any messages you'd like to send to your dad.
	* **Father's Day Message 2**: Share a second message or thought about your dad.
	* **Favorite Dad Memory**: Share a special memory or story about your dad.
	* **Favorite Dad Memory 2**: Share another special memory or story about your dad.
	* **What do you think of your dad?** (second question): Share your thoughts about your dad again.
	* **What do you like about your dad?**: Share what you appreciate about your dad.
	* **What do you dislike about your dad?**: Share any challenges or difficulties you face with your dad.
	* **What is your favorite Dad tradition?**: Select a tradition you enjoy doing with your dad.
