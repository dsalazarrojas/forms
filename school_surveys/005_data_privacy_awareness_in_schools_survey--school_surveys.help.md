# Data Privacy Awareness In Schools Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form aims to gather insights and opinions from staff, students, and parents on data privacy and security best practices within schools. It is designed to understand how data is collected, stored, and used in schools, as well as to identify areas for improvement in data protection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the category that best represents your role or group within the school (Staff, Student, or Parent).
2. For each question, select all that apply from the options provided.
3. Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
4. Use the provided options to guide your response.
5. If you have any additional information or comments, please use the "Other Question" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Title** (`id: 1`):
	+ A text field to enter the name of your school.
* **Category** (`id: 2`):
	+ Select the category that best represents your role or group within the school.
* **Description** (`id: 3`):
	+ A text field for additional comments or description.
* **Staff Question 1** (`id: 4`):
	+ Questions related to data collection and use, access, and security.
* **Staff Question 2** (`id: 5`):
	+ Questions related to data collection and use, access, and security, as well as data breach response and staff training.
* **Staff Question 3** (`id: 6`):
	+ Questions related to student data access, storage, and security, as well as the Data Protection Officer (DPO).
* **Staff Question 4** (`id: 7`):
	+ Questions related to student data collection and use, access, and security, including consequences for mishandling.
* **Staff Question 5** (`id: 8`):
	+ Questions related to data collection and use policy, reviews, and updates.
* **Student Question 1** (`id: 9`):
	+ Questions related to student data collection and use, access, and security.
* **Parent Question 1** (`id: 10`):
	+ Questions similar to Student Question 1.
* **Other Question 1** (`id: 11`):
	+ A text field for additional comments or information.
* **Other Question 2** (`id: 12`):
	+ Another text field for additional comments or information.
* ...
* **Other Question 15** (`id: 25`):
	+ Additional text fields for comments or information.

## Tips

* Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Use the provided options to guide your response.
* If you have any additional information or comments, please use the "Other Question" fields.
* If you are unsure about any question, please select "Unknown" or "Not Applicable" as an option.
* This form is designed to understand your opinions and insights on data privacy and security within schools. Your input will help improve data protection practices in schools.
