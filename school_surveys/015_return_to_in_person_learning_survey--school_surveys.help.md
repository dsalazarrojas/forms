# Return To In Person Learning Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Return To In Person Learning Survey" is designed to collect information on the context and conditions of in-person learning environments in schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions based on your experience and knowledge of in-person learning settings.
2. Select the relevant options for each question, considering the context and your experiences.
3. Fill out all the questions that are relevant to your situation, even if you're unsure or have limited experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Context** (`School Context`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ Choose all the options that describe your school context: 
		- Yes
		- No
* **In Person Interactions** (`In Person Interactions`, `number`, required: false): 
	+ Indicate the frequency of interactions among students, teachers, and peers during classes and other events. You can use numbers 1 to 4 to represent the frequency: Less than 1, Once or twice, 3-4 times, Almost daily.
* **Teacher Support** (`Teacher Support`, `select_one`, required: false): 
	+ Select the level of support you receive from teachers:
		- Yes
		- No
		- Sometimes
* **Teacher Interaction Frequency** (`Teacher Interaction Frequency`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ Choose the frequency of interactions with teachers:
		- Less than once a week
		- Once or twice a week
		- 3-4 times a week
		- 5-6 times a week
		- Almost daily
* **Student Interaction** (`Student Interaction`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ Select the level of interaction with peers during classes:
		- Rarely
		- Occasionally
		- Sometimes
		- Often
		- Frequently
* **Class Size** (`Class Size`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ Choose the size of your classes:
		- Small
		- Medium
		- Large
* **Peer Help** (`Peer Help`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ Select the level of help you receive from peers:
		- Rarely
		- Occasionally
		- Sometimes
		- Often
		- Frequently
* **Teacher Availability** (`Teacher Availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ Indicate the availability of teachers for help:
		- Always
		- Sometimes
		- Rarely
* **Teacher Support Quality** (`Teacher Support Quality`, `select_one`, required: false): 
	+ Select the quality of support from teachers:
		- Very High
		- High
		- Medium
		- Low
		- Very Low
* **Student Support** (`Student Support`, `select_one`, required: false): 
	+ Choose the level of support you receive from peers:
		- Very High
		- High
		- Medium
		- Low
		- Very Low
* **Student Learning Outcome** (`Student Learning Outcome`, `select_one`, required: false): 
	+ Select the level of learning outcome:
		- Very High
		- High
		- Medium
		- Low
		- Very Low
* **Student Engagement** (`Student Engagement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
	+ Select the level of engagement in classes:
		- High
		- Medium
		- Low

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when responding to the questions.
- If you're unsure or have limited experience, choose the options that best fit your situation.
- Take your time to complete the form, and don't hesitate to reach out if you need help or clarification.
