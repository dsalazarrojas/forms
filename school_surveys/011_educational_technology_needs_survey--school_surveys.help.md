# educational_technology_needs_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational technology needs survey is designed to gather information about the educational technology requirements of educational institutions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select "Submit" at the end of the form to submit your answers.
2. Each page has a text field where you can enter your response.
3. You can type your response in the text field provided.
4. If you need to add more information, you can click the "Add" button to create a new text field.
5. Once you have completed all the pages, please review your answers to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **what_is_education** (Page 1): 
	+ This field asks for a brief description of what education means to you.
* **introduction** (Page 2): 
	+ This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about your educational background or experience.
* **educational_background** (Page 3): 
	+ This field is for information about your educational background, such as your degree level and area of study.
* **educational_goals** (Page 4): 
	+ This field is for your goals related to education, such as improving student outcomes or increasing teacher engagement.
* **educational_technology_needs** (Page 5): 
	+ This field is for any specific educational technology needs you have, such as hardware, software, or other tools.
* **school_administration** (Page 6): 
	+ This field is for information about the school's administration, such as their policies and procedures.
* **instructional_methods** (Page 7): 
	+ This field is for information about the instructional methods used in your school, such as classroom instruction or online learning.
* **instructional_methods_2** (Page 8): 
	+ This field is for additional information about the instructional methods used in your school.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and specific as possible in your responses.
* If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them in your response.
* If you need to add more information, you can click the "Add" button to create a new text field.
* If you need help, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
