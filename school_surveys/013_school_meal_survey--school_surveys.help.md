# School Meal Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Meal Survey is designed to collect feedback and insights from school staff, students, and parents on the quality of school meals. This form helps administrators understand the needs and preferences of the school community, enabling data-driven decisions to improve the meal program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
2. You can save your progress and come back to the form later, if needed.
3. If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to contact your school administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Meal** (`school_meal`, text, required): Please describe the quality of the school meals you have experienced so far. This could be a brief comment, positive or negative.
* **School Food** (`school_food_quality`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options below that best describes your experience with the school food:
	+ Option 1: Excellent
	+ Option 2: Good
	+ Option 3: Fair
	+ Option 4: Poor
* **Student Meals Frequency** (`student_meals_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the options that apply to your experience with student meals:
	+ Option 1: Breakfast
	+ Option 2: Lunch
	+ Option 3: Snack
	+ Option 4: All of the above
