# High School Sports Participation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Sports Participation Survey is designed to gather information about sports participation from high school students. The survey aims to understand students' current and future sports interests and any potential barriers to participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the General Information section with your details.
2. In the sports_participation section, select all the sports you are currently participating in.
3. If you have any barriers that prevent you from participating in sports, list them in the sports_barriers section.
4. Think about which sports you might be interested in participating in the future and select those in the future_interest section.
5. Add any additional comments or thoughts in the Additional Comments section.
6. Enter your email address in the email field.
7. Provide your phone number in the phone field.
8. If you have a school ID, enter it in the school_id field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, required: false): This is the starting point of the form. Enter your general information here.
* **sports_participation** (`sports_participation`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the sports you are currently participating in from the available options: None, Basketball, Baseball, Soccer, Football, Volleyball, Tennis, Track, Cross, or Other.
* **sports_barriers** (`sports_barriers`, text, required: false): If you have any barriers that prevent you from participating in sports, list them here.
* **future_interest** (`future_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): Think about which sports you might be interested in participating in the future and select those from the available options: None, Basketball, Baseball, Soccer, Football, Volleyball, Tennis, Track, Cross, or Other.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or thoughts about sports participation.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number.
* **school_id** (`school_id`, number, required: false): If you have a school ID, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer all required fields (if any) to ensure your survey submission is complete.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us.
