# School Spotlight Submission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Spotlight Submission Form is used to collect information about a school's spotlight submissions. This form allows you to provide details about your school, its location, and its achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are filled in.
2. Enter the school's name in the "School Name" field.
3. Enter the school's website in the "School Website" field.
4. Enter the school's email address in the "School Email" field.
5. Enter the school's phone number in the "School Phone" field.
6. Enter the school's location details in the "School Address", "School District", "School Region", "School City", "School State", and "School Zip" fields.
7. Enter the school's latitude and longitude coordinates in the "School Latitude" and "School Longitude" fields.
8. Enter the school's elevation in the "School Elevation" field.
9. Enter the school's open time in the "School Open Time" field.
10. Enter the school's time zone in the "School Time Zone" field.
11. Enter up to three stories about the school in the "Story 1", "Story 2", and "Story 3" fields.
12. Enter up to three achievements of the school in the "Achievement 1", "Achievement 2", and "Achievement 3" fields.
13. Enter up to three media links related to the school in the "Media 1", "Media 2", and "Media 3" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **School Website** (`text`, required): Enter the website of your school.
* **School Email** (`email`, required): Enter the email address of your school.
* **School Phone** (`text`, required): Enter the phone number of your school.
* **School Address** (`text`, required): Enter the address of your school.
* **School District** (`text`, required): Enter the school district.
* **School Region** (`text`, required): Enter the school region.
* **School City** (`text`, required): Enter the city where the school is located.
* **School State** (`text`, required): Enter the state where the school is located.
* **School Zip** (`number`, required): Enter the zip code of the school.
* **School Latitude** (`number`, required): Enter the latitude coordinates of the school.
* **School Longitude** (`number`, required): Enter the longitude coordinates of the school.
* **School Elevation** (`text`, required): Enter the elevation of the school.
* **School Open Time** (`time`, required): Enter the open time of the school.
* **School Time Zone** (`time`, required): Enter the time zone of the school.
* **Story 1** (`text`, optional): Enter a story about the school.
* **Story 2** (`text`, optional): Enter another story about the school.
* **Story 3** (`text`, optional): Enter a third story about the school.
* **Achievement 1** (`text`, optional): Enter an achievement of the school.
* **Achievement 2** (`text`, optional): Enter another achievement of the school.
* **Achievement 3** (`text`, optional): Enter a third achievement of the school.
* **Media 1** (`text`, optional): Enter a media link related to the school.
* **Media 2** (`text`, optional): Enter another media link related to the school.
* **Media 3** (`text`, optional): Enter a third media link related to the school.
