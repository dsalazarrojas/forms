# Parent Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather feedback on your experience at the school. Your input will help us understand areas where we excel and areas where we need to improve. Please answer all questions to help us better serve you and your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select or enter your responses for each question. All fields are self-explanatory, and each question is explained further below. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Environment** (<type> select_one, required): Rate your child's experience in their school environment.
	+ This question assesses your opinion on the overall environment your child is in. Please choose one of the given options: Very Good, Good, or Poor.
* **Teacher Support** (<type> select_one, optional): Rate your child's teacher supportiveness.
	+ This question helps us understand how engaged and supportive their teachers are.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (<type> select_one, required): Select your overall satisfaction with the school.
	+ Rate your satisfaction with the school on a scale from High to Low.
* **Comments** (<type> text, optional): Share any thoughts or experiences you'd like to share with us.
	+ Please write a few lines about your experience at school.
* **School Events** (<type> select_one, optional): Frequency of school events.
	+ Select the number of school events your child participates in per week or month.
* **Parent Involvement** (<type> select_one, optional): Rate your involvement in parent activities.
	+ This question assesses your involvement in school activities, events, or programs.
* **Feedback** (<type> text, required): Share any thoughts or experiences you'd like to share with us.
	+ Please write a few lines about your experience at school.
* **Suggestions** (<type> text, optional): Share any suggestions or improvements you have for the school.
	+ Please write any ideas or suggestions you have for us to make your experience better.
