# Field Trip Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Interest Form is designed to allow students to express their interest in field trips. This form will help the school staff keep track of students who are interested in attending field trips, ensuring that everyone is properly registered and informed about the trip details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, email, phone number, and school name on the first page.
2. Enter your teacher's name, email, and phone number on the next page.
3. Enter your date of birth.
4. Choose the name of the field trip you are interested in and the date you plan to attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your student ID or official documents.
* **Student Email**: Enter your school email address to ensure we can contact you about the trip.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you with important updates.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of your teacher who recommended you for the trip.
* **Teacher Email**: Enter your teacher's email address to ensure we can contact them about the trip.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter your teacher's phone number in case we need to contact them with important updates.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Trip Name**: Choose the name of the field trip you are interested in attending.
* **Trip Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the field trip.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your interest is registered correctly.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact your teacher or school administrator.
* Please note that the form is not a guarantee of admission to the field trip. We will review and confirm your interest with your teacher before sending you any further information.
