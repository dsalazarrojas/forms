# student_satisfaction_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student satisfaction survey template is designed to collect feedback from students to improve their overall experience at the school. This form will help the school identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the student experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your level of satisfaction for each question, or choose "Not Applicable" if it doesn't apply to you.
2. Choose all the facilities you use regularly from the list of options.
3. Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improving your experience at the school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Satisfaction (Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied)**:
	+ This question asks about your overall satisfaction with your academic experience at the school.
* **Teaching and Instruction (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, Very Poor)**:
	+ This question rates the quality of teaching and instruction at the school.
* **Student Services (true, false, Somewhat, Not Applicable)**:
	+ This question inquires about the support you receive from student services like counseling and career services.
* **Campus Facilities (Library, Study spaces, Cafeteria, Gym/Recreation, Labs/Workshops, Student common areas)**:
	+ Select all the facilities that you use regularly.
* **Suggestions for Improvement (text)**:
	+ Share any ideas you have that could improve your experience at the school.
* **Additional Comments (text)**:
	+ Provide any further comments or suggestions you have about your experience at the school.
