# Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to gather student feedback on their experiences with the school.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Read the form carefully and answer all the required questions.
2. For each question, choose the answer that best reflects your opinion.
3. If you have any suggestions or comments, use the provided text fields to express them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What do you like about the curriculum?** (`student_survey_1`, text, optional): Please share your positive thoughts about the curriculum.
* **What do you dislike about the curriculum?** (`student_survey_2`, text, required): Please share your negative thoughts about the curriculum.
* **What do you like about the materials?** (`student_survey_3`, text, optional): Please share your positive thoughts about the materials.
* **What do you dislike about the materials?** (`student_survey_4`, text, required): Please share your negative thoughts about the materials.
* **What do you like about the facilities?** (`student_survey_5`, text, optional): Please share your positive thoughts about the facilities.
* **What do you dislike about the facilities?** (`student_survey_6`, text, required): Please share your negative thoughts about the facilities.
* **How often do you use the computer lab?** (`student_survey_7`, select_one, required): Please choose how often you use the computer lab.
	+ 1: Rarely
	+ 2: Occasionally
	+ 3: Often
	+ 4: Daily
	+ 5: Always
* **What time of day do you prefer to study?** (`student_survey_8`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose the times you prefer to study.
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
* **How would you rate your overall experience at school?** (`student_survey_9`, number, required): Please rate your overall experience on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **What is your school email address?** (`student_survey_10`, email, required): Please enter your school email address.
* **What is your school phone number?** (`student_survey_11`, text, required): Please enter your school phone number.
* **Do you have any suggestions about the school's online presence?** (`student_survey_12`, note, optional): If you have any suggestions, please share them here.
* **What would you like to see change in the school's online presence?** (`student_survey_13`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions, please share them here.
* **How would you rate the quality of online content?** (`student_survey_14`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose the quality of online content.
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **How often do you visit the school's website?** (`student_survey_15`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose how often you visit the website.
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **What would you like to see on the school website?** (`student_survey_16`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose what you would like to see on the website.
	+ More resources for students
	+ More resources for teachers
	+ Both
* **What do you like or dislike about the school's social media presence?** (`student_survey_17`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts about the school's social media presence.
* **What would you like to see change in the school's social media presence?** (`student_survey_18`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions, please share them here.
* **Do you follow the school's social media accounts?** (`student_survey_19`, select_multiple, optional): If you follow the school's social media accounts, choose "True." Otherwise, choose "False."
* **How would you rate the school's online community engagement?** (`student_survey_20`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose the quality of online community engagement.
	+ Good

	+ Poor
* **What do you like or dislike about the student portal?** (`student_survey_21`, text, optional): Please share your thoughts about the student portal.
* **What would you like to see change in the student portal?** (`student_survey_22`, text, optional): If you have any suggestions, please share them here.
* **Do you use the student portal regularly?** (`student_survey_23`, select_multiple, optional): If you use the student portal, choose "True." If not, choose "False."
* **How would you rate the student portal's user experience?** (`student_survey_24`, select_multiple, optional): Please choose the user experience of the student portal.
	+ Good

	+ Poor
* **Would you like to receive emails from the school?** (`student_survey_25`, select_multiple, optional): If you would like to receive emails, choose "True." If not, choose "False."
 
## Tips
* Answer all required questions truthfully.
* Be as specific as possible when sharing your suggestions and comments.
* If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact the school administration.
