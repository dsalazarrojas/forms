# Teaching Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your teaching experience and satisfaction. The data collected will help the school administration make informed decisions about staff development and resources allocation.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out this form in a quiet and comfortable setting, free from distractions.
- You can complete the form online or on a paper copy, depending on your preference.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact the school administration for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction**: This section is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please read it carefully before proceeding.
  - **Label**: Introduction
  - **Type**: Note
  - **Required**: No

- **Which subjects are you teaching?** (Section 1)
  - **Label**: Which subjects are you teaching?
  - **Type**: Select Multiple
  - **Options**: Select one or more subjects from the list:
    - Mathematics
    - Science
    - English
    - Physical Education
    - Arts
    - Music
    - Drama
    - Physical Activity

- **What subjects do you teach in section 1?** (Section 1 - Subject Selection)
  - **Label**: What subjects do you teach in section 1?
  - **Type**: Select Multiple
  - **Options**: Select one or more subjects from the list:
    - Mathematics
    - Science
    - Physical Education
    - Arts
    - Music
    - Drama
    - Physical Activity

- **What is your teaching role?** (Section 2)
  - **Label**: What is your teaching role?
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Options**: Select one of the following roles:
    - Teacher
    - Assistant Teacher
    - Head of Department
    - Principal/Head Teacher
    - Other

- **How would you rate your workload?** (Section 3)
  - **Label**: How would you rate your workload?
  - **Type**: Select One
  - **Options**: Select one of the following ratings:
    - High
    - Medium
    - Low

- **Comments** (Section 3)
  - **Label**: Comments
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Options**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.

- **How would you rate your job satisfaction?** (Section 4)
  - **Label**: How would you rate your job satisfaction?
  - **Type**: Number
  - **Options**: Select a number from 1 to 5, where:
    - 1: Very dissatisfied
    - 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
    - 3: Neutral
    - 4: Somewhat satisfied
    - 5: Very satisfied

- **Comments** (Section 5)
  - **Label**: Comments
  - **Type**: Text
  - **Options**: Please provide any additional comments or feedback you may have.
