# return_to_school_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "return_to_school_survey" form is designed to collect information from students and parents regarding their return to school plans and concerns. This form is meant to help schools prepare for the return of students and staff, ensuring a safe and healthy environment for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Contact info**: Fill in your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
2. **Student details**: Provide your student's details, such as their name and any relevant medical information.
3. **Parents concerns**: Answer questions about your concerns regarding your child's return to school, including vaccination status and COVID-19 exposure.
4. **School arrival and departure**: Enter your school's arrival and departure dates, if applicable.
5. **Contact method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (email or phone).
6. **School information**: Provide your school's address and email address.
7. **Student attendance**: Specify your student's attendance status (full-time, part-time, or remote).
8. **Student location**: Choose your student's location (on-site or remote).
9. **Student location 1/2/3**: You may be asked to specify your student's location (school or home) for each location question.
10. **School type**: Choose your school type (regular or online).
11. **Teacher information**: Answer questions about your teacher's attendance and location status.
12. **Submission date**: Date of submission.
