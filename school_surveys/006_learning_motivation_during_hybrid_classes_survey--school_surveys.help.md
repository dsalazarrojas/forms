# Learning Motivation During Hybrid Classes Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students who have participated in or are currently participating in hybrid classes. It aims to understand their motivation levels and reasons for their motivation, as well as gather feedback from teachers and parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Make sure you are filling out this form as a student who has participated in or is currently participating in hybrid classes.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Fill out the form only once. If you have already filled out this form, you will not be able to fill it out again.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Name** (`student-name`, text, required): Enter your student name as it appears on your school records.
  Please enter your full name as it is listed in your student records.
- **Motivation Level** (`motivation-level`, select_one, required): Choose how motivated you feel during hybrid classes.
  Choose one of the three options: High, Medium, or Low.
  Please select the option that best represents your motivation level during hybrid classes.
- **Motivation Reasons** (`motivation-reasons`, text, optional): If you selected "High" or "Medium" for the previous question, please explain why you feel that way.
  This is your opportunity to provide more details about your motivation levels.
  Please be as specific as possible in your answer.
- **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher-feedback`, text, optional): Please share any feedback you have for your teacher(s) about your hybrid class experience.
  This will help us understand how our teachers are doing in hybrid classes.
  Please be as detailed as possible in your answer.
- **Parent Feedback** (`parent-feedback`, text, optional): Please share any feedback you have for your parents about your hybrid class experience.
  This will help us understand how our parents are doing in hybrid classes.
  Please be as detailed as possible in your answer.
- **Student Feedback** (`student-feedback`, text, optional): Please share any feedback you have about your hybrid class experience.
  This will help us understand how you experience hybrid classes.
  Please be as detailed as possible in your answer.
  This is the second question of this type. If you want to provide feedback about the same topics, please feel free to add more details here.
- **Motivation Level 2** (`motivation-level-2`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the reasons that apply for why you feel motivated during hybrid classes.
  Please select all the reasons that apply from the options provided.
- **Student Feedback 2** (`student-feedback-2`, text, optional): Please share any feedback you have about your hybrid class experience.
  This is your opportunity to provide more details about your experience.
  Please be as specific as possible in your answer.
