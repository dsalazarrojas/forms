# Pre Class Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Class Survey is a form designed to gather information from students before the start of the new academic term. This form helps us understand your interests, goals, and expectations for the upcoming classes. By providing us with this information, you will help us tailor the course content to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin the survey.
2. Fill out the form by answering each question carefully.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and honest responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **whats_your_name (Text, Optional)**: Please enter your name to identify yourself.
* **what_is_your_major (Select One, Required)**: Select the major you are currently pursuing.
	+ Choose one of the following options: Computer Science, Mathematics, Arts and Humanities, Science and Technology, Social Sciences.
* **how_did_you_feel_last_year (Time, Required)**: Describe how you felt last year in a time range (e.g., "I felt motivated for 3 months, then demotivated for 2 months").
* **expected_outcome (Date, Required)**: Enter your expected graduation date or the date you plan to take a specific course.
* **what_topics_do_you_want_to_cover (Select Multiple, Required)**: Choose the topics you are interested in covering in this class.
	+ Select one or more of the following options: Python, Java, C++, JavaScript, SQL.
* **do_you_have_any_other_topics_in_mind (Text, Optional)**: If you have any other topics in mind that you would like to cover, please describe them here.
* **any_other_comments (Note, Optional)**: If you have any other comments or suggestions, please share them here.
* **email (Email, Optional)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
