# Faculty Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended to help users fill out the Faculty Evaluation Form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Go through each page of the form, carefully reading the questions and labels.
2. Answer each question based on your actual experience or involvement with the course or faculty being evaluated.
3. Fill out all required fields, and optional fields as needed.
4. Submit the completed form once you have finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Teacher Survey** (`teacher_survey`, `text`, required: false): This field is designed to collect feedback on individual teachers. Please provide any comments or suggestions you have about a specific teacher's teaching quality and effectiveness.
* **Teaching Quality** (`teaching_quality`, `number`, required: false): This field assesses the overall quality of the teacher's teaching. Choose a number from 1 to 10 to reflect your opinion.
* **Teaching Effectiveness** (`teaching_effectiveness`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to provide feedback on the teacher's effectiveness in communicating course material.
* **Department Teaching Quality** (`department_teaching_quality`, `text`, required: false): This field is designed to evaluate the overall teaching quality of the department.
* **Department Teaching Effectiveness** (`department_teaching_effectiveness`, `text`, required: false): This field assesses the overall effectiveness of the department's teaching.
* **Department Teaching Support** (`department_teaching_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select from a list the types of support the department provides for teaching (e.g. Teaching Support 1, Teaching Support 2, etc.).
* **Remote Classroom Efficiency** (`remote_classroom_efficiency`, `number`, required: false): This field assesses the efficiency of remote classroom experience.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, `select_one`, required: false): This field helps to determine if the course materials were adequate (Yes, No, Other).
* **Student Support** (`student_support`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select from a list the types of support the department provides for students (e.g. Support 1, Support 2, etc.).
* **Faculty Satisfaction** (`faculty_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): This field measures the overall satisfaction of the faculty.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, `select_one`, required: false): This field assesses your overall satisfaction with the course or faculty.
* **Faculty Teaching Style** (`faculty_teaching_style`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to provide feedback on the faculty's teaching style.
* **Course Evaluation** (`course_evaluation`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide feedback on the course.
* **Faculty Evaluation** (`faculty_evaluation`, `text`, required: false): This field is designed to gather feedback on the faculty.
* **Department Teaching** (`department_teaching`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide feedback on the department's teaching.
* **Course Satisfaction** (`course_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): This field measures the overall satisfaction of the course.
* **Department Satisfaction** (`department_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): This field assesses the overall satisfaction of the department.
* **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, `number`, required: false): This field measures the overall satisfaction of the students.
* **Overall Evaluation** (`overall_evaluation`, `select_one`, required: false): This field asks if you would evaluate the course or faculty as satisfactory (Yes, No, Other).
* **Department Faculty Evaluation** (`department_faculty_evaluation`, `text`, required: false): This field is designed to gather feedback on the department's faculty.
* **Department Remote Classroom Efficiency** (`department_remote_classroom_efficiency`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for you to provide feedback on the department's remote classroom efficiency.
* **Course Teaching Style** (`course_teaching_style`, `text`, required: false): This field allows you to provide feedback on the course's teaching style.
