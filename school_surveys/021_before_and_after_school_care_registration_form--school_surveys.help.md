# Before And After School Care Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Before and After School Care Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from parents or guardians for the efficient and secure care of their child during school hours. It is used to ensure that the program meets the child's specific needs and provides a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely, taking care to answer all required fields.
2. If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
3. Ensure that you have all necessary documents and information at hand before submitting the form.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent or Guardian Name** (1): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter the best number to reach you.
* **Home Address** (4): Enter your full street address.
* **Child Name** (5): Enter your child's full name.
* **Child Date of Birth** (6): Enter your child's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Child Gender** (7): Select your child's gender.
* **School Name** (8): Enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Grade Level** (9): Select your child's current grade level.
* **Teacher Name** (10): (Optional) Enter the name of your child's current teacher.
* **Type of Care Needed** (11): Select the type of care your child requires.
* **Days Per Week** (12): Select the number of days per week you need care.
* **Specific Days** (13): Select the days you need care.
* **Start Date** (14): Enter the date you need care to begin.
* **Arrival Time** (15): Enter the time your child will arrive at the program.
* **Pickup Time** (16): Enter the time your child will be picked up.
* **Transportation Method** (17): Select how your child will get to the program.
* **Authorized Pickup Persons** (18): List all individuals authorized to pick up your child.
* **Medical Conditions** (19): (Optional) Enter any medical conditions or allergies your child has.
* **Medications** (20): (Optional) List any medications your child takes.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (21): (Optional) Enter any food allergies or restrictions your child has.
* **Emergency Contact 1** (22): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (23): Enter the name and phone number of another emergency contact.
* **Physician Name** (24): Enter your child's doctor's name.
* **Physician Phone** (25): Enter your child's doctor's phone number.
* **Health Insurance** (26): (Optional) Enter your child's health insurance provider and policy number.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (27): (Optional) Enter any special needs or accommodations your child requires.
* **Behavioral Notes** (28): (Optional) Enter any behavioral considerations for your child.
* **Program Fee Acknowledgment** (29): (Optional) Select whether you understand the program fees.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (30): Select whether you agree to all program terms and conditions.
* **Registration Date** (31): (Optional) This field is automatically populated with today's date.

Note: Please ensure that you understand and agree to all terms and conditions before submitting the form.
