# School Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a school's status, progress, or other relevant details for internal use within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **School Audit Form**: Enter a brief description of the school being audited. This field is used to provide a concise summary of the school's information.
2. **Category**: Select the category that best describes the school. This is likely used to filter or categorize the school's data for future reference.
3. **School Audit Form Helps**: Enter any additional details or information about the school that might be relevant to the audit. This field is used to provide more in-depth information about the school.
4. **Input 1**: Enter any additional information that might be relevant to the audit. This field is intended for any other details that don't fit into the other fields.

* **School Audit Form** (school_audit_form, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the school being audited.
