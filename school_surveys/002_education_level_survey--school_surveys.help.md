# Education Level Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Level Survey is designed to gather data about students' education levels, background information, and other relevant details to help us better understand your experiences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully, providing as much information as possible.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each select_one and select_multiple fields.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Background**: This text field is for providing a brief description of your background as a student. Please enter any relevant information about your student experience, such as your current grade level, student ID number, or any other relevant details.
* **Education Level**: Select one option that best describes your current education level:
	+ High School
	+ College
	+ Master's Degree
* **Educational Institution**: Select all institutions where you have received or are currently receiving education:
	+ Local High School
	+ University of Chicago
	+ New York University
* **Age**: Enter your current age.
* **Gender**: Select one option that best describes your gender:
	+ Male
	+ Female
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Select all phone types you have:
	+ Mobile
	+ Home
* **Time Spent**: Enter the total amount of time you have spent on the education institution (e.g., hours, days, months, years).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Note**: This is an optional text field for any additional comments or notes about your education.
* **Date of Survey Creation**: This is automatically populated and indicates when the survey was created.
* **Teacher Background**: This text field is for providing any background information about your teacher, such as their name, title, or department.
* **Teacher Education Level**: Select one option that best describes your teacher's current education level:
	+ High School
	+ College
	+ Master's Degree
* **Teacher Educational Institution**: Select all institutions where your teacher has received or is currently receiving education:
	+ Local High School
	+ University of Chicago
	+ New York University
* **Teacher Educational Field**: Enter a text description of your teacher's area of expertise or study.
* **Date of Last Survey**: This is automatically populated and indicates when the last survey was conducted.
* **Student Survey ID**: This is automatically generated and serves as a unique identifier for each survey.
* **Total Hours Worked**: Enter the total hours you have worked in the educational institution.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for further clarification.
* This form is designed to collect your demographic information; all answers are kept confidential and only used for research purposes.
