# art_submission_form_template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The art submission form is designed to collect feedback on the school's art submission process. This feedback is crucial to identify areas of improvement and make the process more efficient and user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions honestly, providing your true experience with the art submission process.
2. Select all that apply for the "Which of the following applied to your experience?" question.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for the open-ended questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **How satisfied are you with the art submission process?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): 
This question asks about your overall satisfaction with the art submission process. Choose the option that best reflects your true feeling.
* **How often do you use this art submission form?** (`Regularly`, `Occasionally`, `Rarely`, `This is my first time`): 
This question is about how frequently you use the art submission form. Select the option that best describes your usage frequency.
* **Which of the following applied to your experience? (select all that apply)** (`Easy to use`, `Fast`, `Clear instructions`, `Technical issues`, `Confusing`, `Missing a feature`): 
This question allows you to select all the options that apply to your experience with the form. Be as specific as possible to provide helpful feedback.
* **What improvements would you suggest for the art submission form?** (`text`): 
Provide specific ideas and suggestions on how to improve the art submission process. This will help us make the process more efficient and user-friendly.
* **Do you have any additional comments or feedback?** (`text`): 
Use this space to add any additional comments, concerns, or suggestions that you might have about the art submission form.
