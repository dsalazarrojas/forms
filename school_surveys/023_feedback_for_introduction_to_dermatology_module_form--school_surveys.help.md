# Feedback for Introduction to Dermatology Module Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from users about their experience with the Introduction to Dermatology Module. It will help us improve the content and structure of the module to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to send your feedback.
2. For each field, provide as much detail as you need to answer the question or comment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Module Feedback** (`module_feedback`, `note`, optional): This field is a free-text area where you can provide any comments or suggestions about the Module. You can describe what you liked or disliked, what was unclear, or what could be improved.

* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, `number`, optional): This field allows you to rate your overall experience with the Module. You can choose a number from 1 to 5 to rate how well the Module met your needs.

* **Suggest Improvement** (`suggest_improvement`, `text`, optional): If you have any suggestions for improvement, please type them in this field. This could be a new idea for a feature, a bug fix, or any other way we could improve the Module.

* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): This field is another opportunity to provide any additional comments or thoughts about the Module. You can describe what worked well, what didn't, or any other feedback you think is relevant.

* **Other** (`category_other`, `text`, optional): If none of the above categories apply to your feedback, you can type in a brief description of the category that best fits your comments.
