# post_graduation_career_outcome_survey_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect insights from graduates on their career paths and experiences after graduation. This will help to inform future academic programs and student support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer the questions in the following fields:
   - **What is your primary activity after graduation?**: Select the best option that describes your current activity after completing your academic program.
   - **How relevant is your current employment to your academic major?**: Choose the option that best describes the relationship between your current job and your academic major.
   - **How satisfied are you with your post-graduation career path and current situation?**: Select how satisfied you are with your career path, with the options ranging from Very satisfied to Very dissatisfied.
   - **Which skills from your academic program have you used the most in your career or studies?**: Select all the skills that you have used or found useful in your career or studies. Examples include Critical thinking, Problem-solving, Communication, Teamwork, Technical skills, and Research skills.
   - **How well did your academic program prepare you for your post-graduation endeavors?**: Choose how well you think your academic program prepared you for your post-graduation endeavors.
   - **What were the biggest challenges you faced in your post-graduation transition?**: Provide a brief description of the biggest challenges you faced after graduation.
   - **What advice would you give to current students preparing for graduation and their careers?**: Share any advice or tips you would give to current students preparing for their graduation and career paths.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your primary activity after graduation?** (`Full-time employment`, `Part-time employment`, `Graduate studies`, `Entrepreneurship`, `Unemployed/seeking`, `Other`): This question helps us understand what graduates are doing immediately after graduation. Please select the best option that describes your current activity.
- **How relevant is your current employment to your academic major?** (`Directly related`, `Somewhat related`, `Not related`, `N/A (not employed)`): This question helps us understand the connection between your current employment and your academic major. Choose the option that best describes this relationship.
- **How satisfied are you with your post-graduation career path and current situation?** (`Very satisfied`, `Somewhat satisfied`, `Neutral`, `Somewhat dissatisfied`, `Very dissatisfied`): This question seeks your overall satisfaction with your career path after graduation. Please select how satisfied you are with your current situation.
- **Which skills from your academic program have been most useful in your career or studies?** (`Critical thinking`, `Problem-solving`, `Communication`, `Teamwork`, `Technical skills`, `Research skills`): This question aims to identify the skills from your academic program that you found most useful. Select all the skills that you have used or found useful in your career or studies.
- **How well did your academic program prepare you for your post-graduation endeavors?** (`Very well`, `Well`, `Adequately`, `Not very well`, `Not at all`): This question evaluates how well you think your academic program prepared you for your post-graduation endeavors. Choose how well you think your program prepared you.
- **What were the biggest challenges you faced in your post-graduation transition?**: This open-ended question invites you to share the biggest challenges you faced after graduation. Please provide a brief description of these challenges.
- **What advice would you give to current students preparing for graduation and their careers?**: This question invites you to offer any advice or tips you would give to current students preparing for their graduation and career paths. Please provide a brief description of your advice.

## Tips
- Ensure you answer all questions in a way that is relevant to your experience.
- Provide as much detail as possible in your answers, but keep them brief and concise.
- If an option does not apply to your situation, please select the closest available choice that reflects your experience.
