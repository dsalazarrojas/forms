# School Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the School Attendance Survey form, which is a critical tool for tracking student attendance data.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, ensure you have all the necessary information ready, such as student details and survey completion status.

1. **Enter Student Details**
	* **Student ID**: Enter the unique ID assigned to the student. This is a required field and must be completed accurately.
	* **Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
	* **Grade Level**: Select the student's current grade level from the dropdown menu. This field is also required.
2. **Enter School and Teacher Information**
	* **School Code**: Enter the school code as it appears on official records.
	* **School Name**: Enter the full name of the school.
	* **Teacher Code**: Enter the teacher code as it appears on official records.
	* **Teacher Name**: Enter the teacher's name as it appears on official records.
3. **Survey Completion Details**
	* **Date of Last Attendance**: Enter the date of the student's last recorded attendance.
	* **Time of Last Attendance**: Enter the time of the student's last recorded attendance.
	* **Attendance Note**: Add any relevant notes about the student's attendance. (Optional)
4. **Submit and Review**
	* **Submit**: Review your entries carefully and click "Submit" to submit the survey.
	* **Survey Status**: Select the status of the survey as "Completed" once it has been finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Number, required): Enter the unique ID assigned to the student.
* **Name** (`teacher_name`, Text, required): Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, Select One, required): Select the student's current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Date of Last Attendance** (`attendance_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the student's last recorded attendance.
* **Time of Last Attendance** (`attendance_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the student's last recorded attendance.
* **Attendance Note** (`attendance_note`, Note, Optional): Add any relevant notes about the student's attendance.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter the student's email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the student's phone number (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments about the student's attendance.
* **Submit** (`submit`, Select Multiple, required): Review and submit the survey as "Completed".
* **School Code** (`school_code`, Text, required): Enter the school code as it appears on official records.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, Text, required): Enter the full name of the school.
* **Teacher Code** (`teacher_code`, Text, required): Enter the teacher code as it appears on official records.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, Text, required): Enter the teacher's name as it appears on official records.
* **School Survey Comments** (`school_survey_comments`, Text, Optional): Add any comments about the school's performance.
* **Teacher Survey Comments** (`teacher_survey_comments`, Text, Optional): Add any comments about the teacher's performance.
* **Survey Completed At** (`survey_completed_at`, Date, required): Enter the date the survey was completed.
* **Survey Completed By** (`survey_completed_by`, Time, required): Enter the time the survey was completed.
* **Survey Status** (`survey_status`, Select One, required): Select the status of the survey as "Completed" once it has been finished.
* **School Survey ID** (`school_survey_id`, Number, required): Enter the unique ID for the school survey.
* **Teacher Survey ID** (`teacher_survey_id`, Number, required): Enter the unique ID for the teacher survey.
* **School Survey Date** (`school_survey_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the school survey was completed.
* **Teacher Survey Date** (`teacher_survey_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date the teacher survey was completed.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, Date, required): Enter the date the survey was submitted.

## Tips

* Ensure accuracy and attention to detail when completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to the relevant personnel for guidance.
* Double-check the survey status before submitting it to ensure it is accurate.
