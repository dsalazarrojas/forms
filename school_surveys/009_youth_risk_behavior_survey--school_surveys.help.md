# Youth Risk Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Risk Behavior Survey is a tool designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's behavior in school. This survey aims to identify potential risks and issues that may affect the student's well-being and educational performance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have read and understood the form's purpose.
2. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required to complete it accurately.
3. Complete the form by providing honest and thorough information about your child's behavior.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (text, General Information, required/optional): This section is a brief introduction to the form. Please provide a concise summary of your child's current behavior and any concerns you may have.
* **Parent Information** (text, Parent Information, required/optional): This section is for you to provide essential details about your child's parent or guardian. Ensure you include your name, contact information, and any relevant comments.
* **Survey Questions** (text, Survey Questions, required/optional): This is the main section of the form where you will provide detailed information about your child's behavior. Please be as thorough as possible and answer each question honestly.
