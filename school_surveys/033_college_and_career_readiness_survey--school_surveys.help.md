# College And Career Readiness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College And Career Readiness Survey is designed to help students assess their current level of college and career readiness. This survey asks students to provide information about their post-graduation plans, academic performance, and support needs. The survey is a valuable tool for students to gain a better understanding of their strengths and weaknesses, and for educators to provide targeted support to help them succeed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question honestly, based on your current situation.
2. Review each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
3. Use the options provided to select your answer.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your guidance counselor or academic advisor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Full Name**: Your full name, as you would like to be referred to.
	+ This is a required field, so please enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Your school email address.
	+ This is a required field, so please enter your school email address.
* **Grade Level**: Your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
	+ This is a required field, so please select your current grade level.
* **School Name**: The name of your high school.
	+ This is a required field, so please enter the name of your high school.
* **Survey Date**: The date you are completing this survey.
	+ This is a required field, so please enter the current date.
* **Post-Graduation Plans**: What you plan to do after graduation (e.g., Four-Year College, Community College, Trade or Vocational School, etc.).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Have You Applied to Colleges**: Your current application status (e.g., Yes Multiple, Yes One, In Progress, Not Yet, Not Planning To).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Number of Colleges Applied To**: The number of colleges you have applied to (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have applied to multiple colleges.
* **Intended Major or Career Field**: What you plan to study or pursue as a career.
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have an idea of what you want to study or pursue.
* **Career Interest Area**: What area of career interest you have (e.g., STEM, Business, Healthcare, etc.).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Have You Taken SAT or ACT**: Your test status (e.g., Yes Both, SAT Only, ACT Only, Not Yet, Not Planning To).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **SAT Score**: Your SAT score (if taken).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have taken the SAT.
* **ACT Score**: Your ACT score (if taken).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have taken the ACT.
* **Did You Use Test Prep**: How you prepared for the SAT or ACT (e.g., Formal Course, Online Prep, Self-Study, etc.).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have prepared for the tests.
* **FAFSA Completion Status**: The status of your FAFSA form (e.g., Completed, In Progress, Not Started, Not Applicable, Need Help).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Scholarship Applications**: The number of scholarships you have applied for (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have applied for scholarships.
* **How Concerned Are You About College Costs**: How concerned you are about college costs (e.g., Very Concerned, Concerned, Somewhat Concerned, Not Very Concerned, Not Concerned).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **How Many Colleges Have You Visited**: The number of colleges you have visited (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have visited colleges.
* **Meetings with College Counselor**: The number of meetings you have had with your college counselor (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have met with your college counselor.
* **AP or Dual Enrollment Courses Taken**: The number of AP or dual enrollment courses you have taken (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have taken AP or dual enrollment courses.
* **Current GPA**: Your current GPA (unweighted).
	+ This is a required field, so please enter your current GPA.
* **Study Habits Rating**: Your rating of your study habits (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Time Management Skills Rating**: Your rating of your time management skills (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Note-Taking Skills Rating**: Your rating of your note-taking skills (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Research Skills Rating**: Your rating of your research skills (e.g., Excellent, Very Good, Good, Fair, Poor).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Writing Confidence Level**: How confident you are in your writing (e.g., Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, Not Very Confident, Not Confident).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Math Confidence Level**: How confident you are in your math (e.g., Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, Not Very Confident, Not Confident).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Extracurricular Activities**: List any extracurricular activities you have participated in (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have participated in extracurricular activities.
* **Work Experience**: List any work experience you have (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have work experience.
* **Volunteer Experience**: List any volunteer experience you have (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have volunteer experience.
* **Leadership Roles**: List any leadership roles you have held (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have held leadership roles.
* **What Support Do You Need Most**: Select all the support services you need (e.g., College Application Help, Essay Writing Help, Financial Aid Guidance, etc.).
	+ This is a required field, so please select all the support services you need.
* **What Is Your Biggest Concern**: Describe your biggest concern about after graduation (if any).
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have a concern.
* **Overall Confidence in Readiness**: How confident you feel about your college and career readiness (e.g., Very Confident, Confident, Somewhat Confident, Not Very Confident, Not Confident At All).
	+ This is a required field, so please select one of the options provided.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or thoughts you would like to share.
	+ This field is optional, so you may answer if you have any additional comments.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Review each question carefully to ensure you understand what is being asked.
* Use the options provided to select your answer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your guidance counselor or academic advisor.
