# Student Behavior Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from students about their behavior and experiences at school. This data will be used to identify trends and patterns that can inform school administrators and educators in making decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the survey questions on page 1.
2. Enter the number of questions you answered on page 2.
3. Provide any comments about your behavior on page 3.
4. Enter your email address on page 4.
5. Enter your phone number on page 5.
6. Enter the date and time you completed the survey on page 6.
7. Enter the name of your school on page 7.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey questions** (`survey_questions`, text, required):
	+ Answer our questions. Please provide your responses to the questions listed on this page.
* **Questions Answered** (`questions_answered`, number, required):
	+ Number of questions
* **Behavior comments** (`behavior_comments`, text, optional):
	+ Comments about behavior
* **Your Email** (`email`, email, required):
	+ Your email address.
* **Your Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Your phone number.
* **Date Survey Completed** (`date_survey_completed`, date, required):
	+ Date survey completed.
* **Time Survey Completed** (`time_survey_completed`, time, optional):
	+ Time survey completed.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required):
	+ Your school name.
* **School Location** (`school_location`, text, optional):
	+ Your school location.
