# Student Loan Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Loan Survey is a form designed to collect student feedback on their loan experiences. It will help the financial aid office understand student satisfaction and preferences to improve their services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and truthfully.
2. Select the correct loan type (federal, private, or other) and provide information about your satisfaction with your loan.
3. Fill in any necessary financial aid information or comments.
4. Enter your loan amount.
5. Choose the date when your loan survey completed.
6. Provide your contact information, such as name and email.
7. Select who completed the survey (you or a financial aid officer).
8. Enter the name and department of the contact person.
9. Enter the contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Loan Survey (1)** (`student_loan_survey`, note, not required): This is the title of the form.
* **loan_type (2)** (`loan_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of loan you have (federal, private).
* **satisfaction (3)** (`satisfaction`, select_multiple, not required): Choose your level of satisfaction with your loan (very satisfied, somewhat satisfied, somewhat dissatisfied, or very dissatisfied).
* **school_financial_aid (4)** (`school_financial_aid`, number, not required): If you have any financial aid information, enter it here.
* **loan_amount (5)** (`loan_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount of your loan.
* **student_loan_survey_ending (6)** (`student_loan_survey_ending`, date, not required): Enter the date when you completed the survey.
* **contact_information (7)** (`contact_information`, text, not required): Enter your contact information (name and email).
* **school_financial_aid_information (8)** (`school_financial_aid_information`, text, not required): If you have any additional financial aid information, enter it here.
* **loan_terms (9)** (`loan_terms`, text, not required): Enter any loan terms or conditions you'd like to mention.
* **loan_provider (10)** (`loan_provider`, text, not required): Enter the name of your loan provider.
* **student_loan_survey_comments (11)** (`student_loan_survey_comments`, text, not required): Leave any comments about your loan experience here.
* **survey_completed_by (12)** (`survey_completed_by`, select_one, not required): Choose who completed the survey (you or a financial aid officer).
* **other_survey_completed_by (13)** (`other_survey_completed_by`, text, not required): If the survey was completed by someone else, enter their name.
* **contact_person (14)** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person in charge.
* **contact_department (15)** (`contact_department`, text, not required): Enter the department name.
* **contact_email (16)** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the contact email.

Please fill out the form carefully and honestly to help the financial aid office improve their services.
