# Student Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Technology Survey aims to gather information about your technology habits, preferences, and usage outside of school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, please follow these steps:

1. For each question, select the options that apply to you.
2. Answer each question honestly, and only answer the questions that you are familiar with.
3. You can select multiple options for each question where applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Uses Technology At Home** (`uses_technology_at_home`, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all the ways you use technology at home, such as a Laptop/Desktop, Mobile, Tablet, or Other.
* **Favorite Website** (`favorite_website`, Text):
	+ Enter the website that you enjoy visiting or use the most outside of school.
* **Favorite Social Media** (`favorite_social_media`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select your favorite social media platform, such as Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, or Other.
* **Technology Used For Learning** (`technology_used_for_learning`, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all the tools you use for learning outside of class, such as Google Drive, Online Quizzes, Educational Videos, or Other.
* **Technology Used For Leisure** (`technology_used_for_leisure`, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all the tools you use for entertainment or relaxation, such as YouTube, Netflix, Music Streaming, or Other.
* **How Many Hours Per Day Spent On Devices** (`how_many_hours_per_day_spent_on_devices`, Number, optional):
	+ Enter the average number of hours you spend on devices per day.
* **Favorite Device** (`favorite_device`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select your favorite device, such as a Smartphone, Laptop, Tablet, or Other.
* **Technology Used For Homework** (`technology_used_for_homework`, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all the tools you use for homework, such as Google Drive, Microsoft Word, Online Quizzes, or Other.
* **Technology Used For Classwork** (`technology_used_for_classwork`, Select Multiple, optional):
	+ Choose all the tools you use for classwork, such as Google Drive, Online Quizzes, Educational Videos, or Other.
* **Favorite Device For Classwork** (`favorite_device_for_classwork`, Select One, optional):
	+ Select your favorite device for classwork, such as a Smartphone, Laptop, Tablet, or Other.
