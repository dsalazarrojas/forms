# School Withdrawal Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather information from students who are withdrawing from school due to various reasons. The purpose of this survey is to understand the reasons for withdrawal and to gather suggestions for improvement to prevent future withdrawals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
2. Select the correct grade level for the student if applicable.
3. Describe any disappointing experiences or reasons for withdrawal in the designated text fields.
4. Provide suggestions for improvement to prevent future withdrawals.
5. Enter the student's contact information (email and phone number) to allow us to follow up with them if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, `text`, required): Please enter your personal information such as your name and contact details.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their record.
* **Grade** (`grade`, `number`, optional): If applicable, please select the grade level of the student (First to Eighth Grade).
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Who Filled Out the Form** (`who_filled`, `text`, required): Please indicate who filled out the form (e.g. student or parent/guardian).
* **Thoughts** (`thoughts`, `text`, required): Describe your thoughts and feelings regarding the withdrawal.
* **Disappointing Experiences** (`disappointing_experiences`, `text`, required): Describe any disappointing experiences that led to the withdrawal.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestions`, `text`, required): Provide suggestions on how to improve the school environment to prevent future withdrawals.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select the grade level of the student (First to Eighth Grade).
* **Reasons for Leaving** (`reason`, `text`, required): Describe the main reason(s) for the student's withdrawal.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`suggestions_for_improvement`, `text`, required): Provide suggestions on how to improve the school experience.
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, `text`, required): Enter any additional comments or concerns.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
