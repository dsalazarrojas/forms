**Teacher Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide**

**Form Purpose:**
The Teacher Satisfaction Survey aims to collect feedback from teachers regarding their satisfaction with various aspects of your school. This form will help us understand what you value most as a teacher, what needs improvement, and how we can better support you. Please take a few minutes to complete this form and help us improve our services.

**How To Complete This Form:**

* Select "Submit" when you have filled out all the required fields.
* If you're having trouble or need help, contact your supervisor or HR representative.

**Field-by-Field Explanation:**

* **Teacher ID (2)**: Enter your unique teacher ID number.
* **Teacher Name (3)**: Enter your name as it appears on your school records.
* **Department (4)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you're satisfied with your department's resources and support.
* **School Year (5)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you're satisfied with the school year's resources and support.
* **Feedback (6)**: Provide any comments or feedback you have about your experience at the school.
* **Suggestions (7)**: Suggest any new ideas or improvements you would like to see at the school.
* **Needs (8)**: Describe any needs or concerns you have about your job or resources.
* **Contact Info (9)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you'd like to share your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Comments (10)**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Attachment (11)**: Attach any relevant documents or files to support your feedback.
* **Email (12)**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone (13)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Meeting Date (14)**: Choose the date of the meeting where you met with the school administration.
* **Meeting Time (15)**: Choose the time of the meeting where you met with the school administration.
* **Meeting Location (16)**: Enter the location of the meeting where you met with the school administration.
* **Meeting Notes (17)**: Provide any notes or discussion points from the meeting.
* **Follow-up Action (18)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you'd like to have follow-up actions taken on your feedback.
* **Follow-up Notes (19)**: Provide any additional notes or context for follow-up actions.
* **Next Steps (20)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you'd like to take next steps on your feedback.
* **Follow-up Status (21)**: Choose the status of follow-up actions (Active or Inactive).
* **Next Step (22)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you'd like to take the next step on your feedback.
* **Follow-up Next Steps (23)**: Choose "Yes" or "No" if you'd like to take next steps on your feedback.
* **Meeting Notes (24)**: Provide any notes or discussion points from the meeting.
* **Follow-up Notes (25)**: Provide any additional follow-up notes or context.

**Tips:**

* Be honest and open in your feedback, as it will help us understand your needs and improve our services.
* If you have any concerns or needs that aren't addressed in this form, please don't hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* If you have any attachments, please make sure they're relevant and concise.
* Thank you for taking the time to complete this form and help us improve your school experience.
