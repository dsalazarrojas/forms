# Teacher Of The Year Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Teacher Of The Year Nomination Form is used to identify and recognize an educator who has made significant contributions to their school or department.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the nominee:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Reason for Nomination
	* Nominator First Name
	* Nominator Last Name
	* Nominator Email
	* Nominator Phone
2.  Please make sure to fill all required fields. A blank field will prevent you from submitting the form.
3.  Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the first name of the person you are nominating.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Fill in the last name of the person you are nominating.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person you are nominating.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the person you are nominating.
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`, `text`, required): Explain why you are nominating this person.
* **Nominator First Name** (`nominator_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Last Name** (`nominator_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as the nominator.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number as the nominator.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in accurate information about the nominee.
* Do not self-nominate. If you wish to be recognized, please follow the usual internal recognition procedures.
* If the person you wish to nominate has already been recognized with this award, please do not resubmit their nomination.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department or school administration for clarification.
