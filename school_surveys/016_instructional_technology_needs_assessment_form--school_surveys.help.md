# Instructional Technology Needs Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instructional Technology Needs Assessment Form is a tool designed to help identify the instructional technology needs of a school or educational institution. This form will help administrators, teachers, or other stakeholders assess their current situation and plan for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions and fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the options that best describe your current situation in each field.
3. Be sure to provide a description of the school survey form, as it will be used to gather more information about your school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Classroom Setting** (`classroom_setting`, select_one, required): Choose the most relevant classroom setting that describes your current situation.
* **Device Accessibility** (`device_accessibility`, select_multiple, required): Select all the device accessibility options that apply to your current situation.
* **Teacher Training** (`teacher_training`, select_one, required): Choose the most relevant teacher training option that aligns with your needs.
* **Technical Support** (`technical_support`, select_multiple, required): Select all the technical support options that apply to your current situation.
* **Description** (`form_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your current situation or the purpose of the survey.
* **Completion** (`form_completion`, text, required): Enter the date and time you plan to complete the form.
* **Submission** (`form_submission`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submit the form.
* **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, note, required): Enter any comments or feedback from teachers about the form or the technology needs assessment process.
* **School Survey** (`school_survey`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your school or institution.
* **Teacher Survey** (`teacher_survey`, text, required): Enter any feedback or comments from teachers about their technology needs.
* **School Survey Form** (`school_survey_form`, text, required): Provide a description of the school survey form and its purpose.
* **Teacher Survey Form** (`teacher_survey_form`, text, required): Provide a description of the teacher survey form and its purpose.
* **School Survey Form Description** (`school_survey_form_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the school survey form and its purpose.
