# School Safety Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about school safety, allowing you to provide insights on safety concerns and areas for improvement in your school or district. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us understand your experiences and perceptions of safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question truthfully, based on your experiences and observations.
2. Choose the most relevant option for each question. If unsure, use the options provided.
3. Be sure to provide clear and concise answers, as they will help us understand your concerns and suggestions better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is there a security guard present on campus?**
  • (Security Guard, No Security Guard): This question asks about the presence of a security guard on campus.
* **Is there a school resource officer present on campus?**
  • (School Resource Officer, No School Resource Officer): This question inquires about the presence of a school resource officer on campus.
* **How often do you see a security guard?**
  • (Every Day, Most days, Rarely, Never): This question asks about the frequency of seeing a security guard on campus.
* **Have you experienced a physical attack on campus?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about any physical attacks or altercations on campus.
* **Have you witnessed any acts of vandalism on campus?**
  • (True, False): This question asks about any observed acts of vandalism or destruction of property on campus.
* **Have you witnessed graffiti on campus?**
  • (True, False): Similar to the previous question, this inquires about any observed graffiti on campus.
* **Have you experienced any incidents of harassment?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about any instances of harassment or intimidation on campus.
* **Have you witnessed any bullying incidents?**
  • (True, False): This question asks about any observed bullying or intimidation incidents on campus.
* **Have you witnessed any bullying incidents with weapons?**
  • (True, False): Similar to the previous question, this inquires about any observed bullying incidents involving weapons on campus.
* **Have you witnessed any violent conduct on campus?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about any observed violent behavior or altercations on campus.
* **How safe do you feel in this school?**
  • (Very Safe, Somewhat Safe, Not Safe, Not at all Safe): This question asks about your personal feelings of safety on campus.
* **Is this school in good repair?**
  • (Very Good Repair, Somewhat Good Repair, Needs Improvement, Needs Major Improvement): This question inquires about the physical condition of the school buildings and facilities.
* **Are there emergency exits and exit signs clearly marked?**
  • (True, False): This question asks about the clarity and visibility of emergency exits and signs on campus.
* **Are fire alarms functional?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about the functionality of fire alarms on campus.
* **Are there functioning emergency exit signs?**
  • (True, False): Similar to the previous question, this inquires about the functionality of emergency exit signs on campus.
* **Are emergency exit routes clearly marked?**
  • (True, False): This question asks about the clarity and visibility of emergency exit routes on campus.
* **Are emergency exits easily accessible?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about the ease of access to emergency exits on campus.
* **Have you ever experienced a fire on campus?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about any fires or incidents that have occurred on campus.
* **Are fire extinguishers readily available?**
  • (True, False): This question asks about the availability of fire extinguishers on campus.
* **Are teachers familiar with fire safety procedures?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about the knowledge and preparedness of teachers with regards to fire safety procedures.
* **Are students familiar with fire safety procedures?**
  • (True, False): Similar to the previous question, this inquires about the knowledge and preparedness of students with regards to fire safety procedures.
* **Are parents familiar with fire safety procedures?**
  • (True, False): This question inquires about the knowledge and preparedness of parents with regards to fire safety procedures.
* **Are emergency procedures readily available to students?**
  • (True, False): This question asks about the availability of emergency procedures, such as evacuation plans and protocols, to students.
