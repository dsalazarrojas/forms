# High School Student Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Student Check In Form is designed to collect information from students to aid school administrators and teachers in tracking student activity, attendance, and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested student information.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate details about the student's grade level, current school, and status.
3. If applicable, enter the teacher's name and teacher ID.
4. Enter any additional relevant information in the free-text fields as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, required): Enter the student's unique identifier number.
- **Student Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's full name.
- **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's grade level from the dropdown options (High School, Middle School, College, University).
- **Current School Name** (`current_school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's current school name (if applicable).
- **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's teacher's name (if applicable).
- **Teacher ID** (`teacher_id`, `number`, optional): Enter the teacher's unique identifier number (if applicable).
- **Location** (`student_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's current location.
- **Status** (`student_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the student's status from the dropdown options (On campus, Off campus).
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the student's date (if applicable).
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the student's time (if applicable).
- **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the student.
- **Student Phone Number** (`student_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
