# Cyberbullying And Online Aggression Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cyberbullying And Online Aggression Survey is designed to gather information from students about their experiences and perceptions of bullying and online aggression, aiming to help educators and administrators develop targeted strategies to prevent and address these issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each question carefully to ensure you provide accurate and genuine responses.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and thoroughly.
3. For select-type questions, choose the most relevant option that best describes your experience or perception.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (id: 2, label: Student Information): This is the first page of the survey, where you'll provide basic information about yourself as a student.
* **Bullying At School** (id: 3, label: Bullying At School): This question asks about your experiences with bullying at school, where you can select multiple options that apply to you.
* **Bullying Outside School** (id: 4, label: Bullying Outside School): This question is about your experiences with bullying outside of school, where you select one option that best describes your situation.
* **Bullying Frequency** (id: 5, label: Bullying Frequency): This question asks about how often you experience bullying, where you can select multiple options that apply to you.
* **Bullying Severity** (id: 6, label: Bullying Severity): This question is about the severity of the bullying you've experienced, where you select one option that best describes your situation.
* **Bullying Consequences** (id: 7, label: Bullying Consequences): This question asks about the consequences of bullying for you or others, where you can select multiple options that apply.
* **Bullying Prevention** (id: 8, label: Bullying Prevention): This question is about the measures that could help prevent bullying, where you select one option that best describes your opinion.
* **Bullying Support** (id: 9, label: Bullying Support): This question asks about the support you receive or offer to victims of bullying, where you can select multiple options that apply.
* **Bullying Consequences For Non-Participants** (id: 10, label: Bullying Consequences For Non-Participants): This question is about the consequences of not participating in bullying, where you can select multiple options that apply.
* **Bullying Consequences For Participants** (id: 11, label: Bullying Consequences For Participants): This question asks about the consequences for participants in bullying, where you can select multiple options that apply.
* **Bullying Frequency At School** (id: 12, label: Bullying Frequency At School): This question is about how often you experience bullying at school, where you select a date.
* **Bullying Frequency Outside School** (id: 13, label: Bullying Frequency Outside School): This question asks about how often you experience bullying outside of school, where you select a date.

**Tips**

* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* If you're unsure about an answer, choose the option that best describes your situation.
* Remember, your responses will help create a safer and more supportive school environment.
