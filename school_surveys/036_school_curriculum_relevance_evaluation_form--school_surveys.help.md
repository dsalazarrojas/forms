# School Curriculum Relevance Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Curriculum Relevance Evaluation Form is intended to gather feedback from users on the relevance and effectiveness of a school's curriculum. This form helps the school administration to understand the strengths and weaknesses of their curriculum and make informed decisions about future changes and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the introduction section (Page 1) to understand the context and purpose of this form.
2.  Answer the questions on the following pages by providing your honest and detailed feedback.
3.  Make sure to submit your feedback before leaving the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Introduction** (Page 1): This is a brief overview of the form and its purpose.
    - This page provides a general understanding of what this form is about and what you are expected to do.
- **Purpose** (Page 2): Describe the main goal and objective of the curriculum.
    - Please provide a brief description of why the curriculum was created and what it aims to achieve.
- **Description** (Page 3): Give a detailed description of the curriculum.
    - Explain the curriculum's content, structure, and any notable features.
- **Relevance** (Page 4): Select the level of relevance of the curriculum to educational goals and student needs.
    - You can choose from three options: "Curriculum aligns with educational goals", "Curriculum aligns with student needs", or "Curriculum is irrelevant".
- **Effectiveness** (Page 5): Rate the effectiveness of the curriculum.
    - You can rate the effectiveness as a number from 1 to 10.
- **Evaluation** (Page 6): Evaluate the overall evaluation of the curriculum.
    - Select from four options: "Very Good", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor".
- **Comments** (Page 7): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the curriculum.
    - This is your chance to share any observations, ideas, or concerns about the curriculum.
- **Email** (Page 8): Enter your email address for contact and correspondence.
    - Please ensure that your email address is accurate and valid.
- **Phone** (Page 9): Enter your phone number for further contact.
    - You can also use this field if you have further questions or concerns.
- **Submission** (Page 10): Submit your form before leaving.
    - Please ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting your form.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible in your answers to help the school administration understand your feedback.
- Use clear and concise language in your answers.
- Make sure to review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and relevance.
