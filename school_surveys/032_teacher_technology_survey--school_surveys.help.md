# Teacher Technology Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a survey designed for teachers to provide information about their use of technology in the classroom.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your school level from the options provided.
2. Choose the technology you have used for your teaching tasks.
3. Specify the technology you need.
4. Indicate the level of knowledge required for you to use the technology.
5. If needed, provide any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Level** (High School, Elementary School): This field asks about the type of school you teach in.
* **Teacher ID** (number): This is your identification number.
* **School Name** (text): This field is for you to provide the name of your school.
* **Subject Area** (text): This field is for you to specify the subject you teach.
* **Technology Used** (select multiple):
  + Desktop Computer: You have used a desktop computer for your teaching tasks.
  + Smartphone: You have used a smartphone for your teaching tasks.
  + Laptop: You have used a laptop for your teaching tasks.
  + Interactive Whiteboard: You have used an interactive whiteboard for your teaching tasks.
* **Technology Needed** (text): This field is for you to specify the technology you need for your teaching tasks.
* **Knowledge Required** (text): This field is for you to indicate the level of knowledge you think is required to use the technology.
* **Output File** (text): This field is for any output file or attachment related to your submission.
* **Assigned Tool** (text): This field is for you to specify any tools assigned to you.
* **Teacher Comments** (text): This field is for any comments or feedback you have about your experience with the technology.
* **Other Comments** (text): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you have.
* **Date** (date): This field is for the date you have submitted the survey.
* **Time** (time): This field is for the time you have submitted the survey.
* **Email** (email): This field is for your email address.
* **Phone** (text): This field is for your phone number.
* **Teacher Phone** (text): This field is for your teacher's phone number.
* **Teacher Email** (email): This field is for your teacher's email address.
* **Teacher Name** (text): This field is for your teacher's name.
* **Teacher ID** (number): This field is for your teacher's identification number.
* **School ID** (number): This field is for your school's identification number.
* **School** (text): This field is for the name of your school.
* **Assigned By** (text): This field is for the person who assigned you the technology.
* **Submitted By** (text): This field is for who submitted the survey on your behalf.
* **Comments** (text): This field is for any comments or feedback about your experience with the technology.
* **Survey Status** (select one):
  + Not Started: You have not started using the technology.
  + Completed: You have completed using the technology.
  + In Progress: You are in the process of using the technology.
  + On Hold: You are waiting for further instructions or approval before using the technology.
