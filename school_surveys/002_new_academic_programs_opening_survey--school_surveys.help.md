# compose - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "compose" form is a tool used to collect information about individual schools or educational institutions. It is intended to provide a structured way to gather and store data about various aspects of a school's characteristics and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the course of study from the dropdown list provided in the "course of study" field.
2. Enter the expected start date of the program in the "expected start date" field.
3. Enter the school's contact information, including:
	* Email
	* Contact number
	* School name
	* Location
	* Program of study
4. Provide details about the school's graduation date and field of study.
5. For the second school, repeat the same process as steps 2-4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **course of study** (`1`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the course of study that the school offers.
* **expected start date** (`2`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected start date of the program.
* **email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter the school's contact email.
* **contact number** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the school's contact number.
* **school name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the school.
* **location** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the location of the school.
* **program of study** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter the program of study offered by the school.
* **graduation date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Enter the graduation date of the program.
* **degree** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" if the school offers a degree, "No" otherwise.
* **field of study** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Enter the field of study offered by the school.
* **contact person** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact person's name.
* **contact address** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact address.
* **contact number** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number.
* **contact email** (`14`, `email`, required: false): Enter the contact email.
* **school url** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Enter the school's URL.
* **school description** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the school.
* **expected graduation date** (`17`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected graduation date of the program.
* **contact phone2** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Enter an alternate contact phone number.
* **contact email2** (`19`, `email`, required: false): Enter an alternate contact email.
* **field of study 2** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Enter an alternate field of study.
* **expected start date 2** (`21`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expected start date of the second program.
* **graduation date 2** (`22`, `date`, required: false): Enter the graduation date of the second program.
* **school name 2** (`23`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the second school.
* **contact number 2** (`24`, `text`, required: false): Enter an alternate contact number.
* **contact email 2** (`25`, `email`, required: false): Enter an alternate contact email.
