# Student Demographics Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect demographic information from students to better understand the diversity of our student body.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question carefully, and only select options that apply to you.
2.  For the text field, provide a brief description or answer in a few sentences.
3.  Make sure to select all that apply for the multi-select question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### What is your current age group?
* What is your current age group? (`<name>: Age Group`, `<type>: single`, `required`: yes) You must choose one option from the list.
*  Answer the question with the corresponding age group option that best describes you.

### How do you primarily identify your gender?
* How do you primarily identify your gender? (`<name>: Gender Identity`, `<type>: single`, `required`: yes) You must select one option from the list.
*  Choose the option that best describes how you identify your gender.

### What is your current academic level?
* What is your current academic level? (`<name>: Academic Level`, `<type>: single`, `required`: yes) You must choose one option from the list.
*  Answer the question with the corresponding academic level option that best describes your current academic status.

### What is your primary field of study or major?
* What is your primary field of study or major? (`<name>: Field of Study`, `<type>: text`, `required`: yes) Provide a brief description of your major or field of study in a few sentences.
*  Answer the question with a concise and clear description of your primary field of study or major.

### Which of the following best describes your ethnicity/racial background?
* Which of the following best describes your ethnicity/racial background? (`<name>: Ethnicity/Racial Background`, `<type>: multiple`, `required`: yes) You can select multiple options from the list.
*  Choose all the options that best describe your ethnicity or racial background.

### What is your current living situation?
* What is your current living situation? (`<name>: Living Situation`, `<type>: single`, `required`: yes) You must select one option from the list.
*  Answer the question with the corresponding living situation option that best describes your current living arrangements.

### Do you currently work while studying?
* Do you currently work while studying? (`<name>: Work Status`, `<type>: single`, `required`: yes) You must choose one option from the list.
*  Answer the question with the corresponding work status option that best describes your current work status.

### Is there anything else you'd like to share about your background or experiences at our school?
* Is there anything else you'd like to share about your background or experiences at our school? (`<name>: Additional Comments`, `<type>: text`, `required`: no) You can provide additional comments or information about your background or experiences at our school.
*  Answer the question with a brief description or comment in a few sentences.
 

## Tips

* Please answer each question carefully, and only select options that apply to you.
* If you have any difficulty in completing this form, or if you have questions, contact the school administration.
* You can provide additional comments or information in the text field.
* Remember, this form is designed to help the school understand your demographic profile, and your responses will help inform decisions and policies that impact you and the student community.
