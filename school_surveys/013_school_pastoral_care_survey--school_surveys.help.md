# School Pastoral Care Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Pastoral Care Survey is a form designed to gather data on the wellbeing support for students, parents, and staff in schools. This data will help schools evaluate their current state of wellbeing support and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the options that best describe the wellbeing support for students, parents, and staff.
2. Choose the frequency of data collection (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
3. Indicate how each form submission will be managed (e.g., manually reviewed, automated system, or both).
4. Enter any additional comments or feedback.
5. Enter the date and time of the submission.
6. Enter your contact information (email and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1** (`Form 1`, text, required/optional): This field is the introduction to the survey form. Please select the options that best describe the wellbeing support for your school.
* **Wellbeing Support for Students** (`wellbeing_support_for_students`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks you to rate the wellbeing support for students, using options such as Very Good, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Wellbeing Support for Parents** (`wellbeing_support_for_parents`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks you to rate the wellbeing support for parents, using options such as Very Good, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Wellbeing Support for Staff** (`wellbeing_support_for_staff`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field asks you to rate the wellbeing support for staff, using options such as Very Good, Good, Fair, and Poor.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, select_one, required): This field asks you to choose the frequency of data collection, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Each Form Submission** (`each_form_submission`, select_one, required): This field asks you to indicate how each form submission will be managed, such as Manually reviewed, Automated system, or Both.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you may have.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): This field asks for the date of the submission.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): This field asks for the time of the submission.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This field asks for your contact email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): This field asks for your contact phone number.
* **School Pastoral Care** (`school_pastoral_care`, select_one, required): This field asks you to rate the overall wellbeing support in your school, using options such as Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **School Pastoral Care Score** (`school_pastoral_care_score`, number, required/optional): This field asks you to enter a score out of a maximum value.
* **School Pastoral Care Score out of** (`school_pastoral_care_score_out_of`, number, required/optional): This field asks you to specify the maximum score value.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate when providing your responses.
- This survey is designed to gather data for improvement, not to penalize or criticize.
- Your input will help shape the wellbeing support services provided by your school.
