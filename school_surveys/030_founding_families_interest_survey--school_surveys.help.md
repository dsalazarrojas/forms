# Founding Families Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Founding Families Interest Survey is a form designed to collect information about families interested in being founding families to a school or educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand the purpose and the questions being asked.
2. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
3. Choose your level of interest in being a founding family by selecting "Yes" or "No".
4. Choose your level of involvement by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Select your involvement preferences by choosing one of the options provided.
6. Click the "Submit" button to submit your response.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Survey Form (Page 1)
* **School Survey Form** (`school_survey_form`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to share with the school.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
### Contact Details (Page 2)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for future communication.
### Family Interest (Page 3)
* **Family Interest** (`family_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you are interested in being a founding family by selecting "Yes" or "No".
### Involvement Preferences (Page 4)
* **Involvement Preferences** (`involvement_preferences`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred level of involvement by selecting "Yes" or "No".
### Submit (Page 5)
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your response. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have already submitted this form, please do not complete it again.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration.
