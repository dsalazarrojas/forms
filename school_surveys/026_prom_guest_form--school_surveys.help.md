We are creating a help guide for the "Prom Guest Form" to help end users understand its purpose, how to complete it, and what each field means. This form is used to collect information about a guest attending a school event, including personal details, contact information, and event specifics.

## Purpose
The "Prom Guest Form" is designed to collect information from guests attending a school event, such as the prom. This form helps school staff to track and verify the identity and attendance of guests, ensuring a safe and secure environment for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering the required fields: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Guest Name**, **Guest Phone**, and **Guardian Name**.
2. Choose the correct **Relationship** between the guest and student (Parent, Teacher, Family Member, or Other).
3. Select the **School District** (North, South, East, or West).
4. Choose the **Grade Level** (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
5. Select the **School Club** or clubs the guest is attending (multiple selections allowed).
6. Indicate if the guest has **Special Needs** (True or False).
7. Enter the **Event Date** and **Event Time** for the school event.
8. Enter the **Event Start Time** and **Event End Time** for the school event.
9. Add any additional **Comments** about the guest.
10. Enter the **Guest School** (optional).
9. Enter the **Guardian Email** (if different from guest's email).
9. Enter the **Guardian School** (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text, required, true)**: Enter the student's first name, as given.
* **Last Name (text, required, true)**: Enter the student's last name, as given.
* **Email (email, required, true)**: Enter the student's or guest's email address.
* **Phone (text, required, true)**: Enter the student's or guest's phone number.
* **School (text, optional, false)**: Enter the name of the school the student attends. This field is optional, but recommended for tracking and verification purposes.
* **Grade (number, optional, false)**: Enter the grade level of the student (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, etc.). This field is optional, but recommended for tracking.
* **Graduation Year (number, optional, false)**: Enter the graduation year of the student. This field is optional, but recommended for tracking.
* **Guest Name (text, required, true)**: Enter the name of the guest who is attending the school event.
* **Guest Phone (text, required, true)**: Enter the phone number of the guest.
* **Guest Email (email, optional, false)**: Enter the email address of the guest. This field is optional, but recommended for tracking.
* **Guest School (text, optional, false)**: Enter the name of the school the guest attends. This field is optional, but recommended for verification purposes.
* **Guardian Name (text, required, true)**: Enter the name of the guest's guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Phone (text, required, true)**: Enter the phone number of the guest's guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Email (email, optional, false)**: Enter the email address of the guest's guardian or parent. This field is optional, but recommended for tracking.
* **Guardian School (text, optional, false)**: Enter the name of the school the guest's guardian or parent attends. This field is optional, but recommended for verification purposes.
* **Relationship (select_one, required, true)**: Select the relationship between the guest and the student (Parent, Teacher, Family Member, or Other).
* **Comments (note, optional, false)**: Add any additional comments or notes about the guest.
* **District (select_one, required, true)**: Select the school district where the student attends (North, South, East, or West).
* **Grade Level (select_one, required, true)**: Select the grade level of the student (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **School Club (select_multiple, required, true)**: Select one or more school clubs the guest is attending (Band, Chorus, Debate, Drama, Orchestra, Robotics, Speech, Yearbook).
* **Special Needs (select_multiple, optional, false)**: Indicate if the guest has special needs (True or False).
* **Event Date (date, required, true)**: Enter the date of the school event.
* **Event Time (time, required, true)**: Enter the start time of the school event.
* **Event Start Time (time, required, true)**: Enter the start time of the school event.
* **Event End Time (time, required, true)**: Enter the end time of the school event.

This form is designed to provide a comprehensive and easy-to-use interface for collecting important information from guests attending school events. By following the steps outlined above, you will be able to successfully complete the "Prom Guest Form" and ensure the accuracy and completeness of the information collected.
