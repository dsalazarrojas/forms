# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for students to share their experiences and opinions about a course they have taken. By providing feedback, students help the instructors and course administrators to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance the learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: contact_info (Contact Info), contact_name (Contact Name), and student_satisfaction (Student Satisfaction).
2. Answer the questions on each page, providing as much detail as possible in the text fields (e.g., course_content, what_was_your_learning_experience, instructor_help).
3. For select_one and select_multiple fields (e.g., how_good_was_the_teaching, how_satisfactory_your_experience), select the options that best match your experience.
4. In the optional fields, provide any additional comments or feedback in the note field (what_do_you_want_to_comments).
5. Submit the form when you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `string`, required): This is the title of the course you are providing feedback about.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up questions or to receive any further communication.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Student Satisfaction** (`student_satisfaction`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the options that describe your experience, such as "Yes, I was satisfied with the course," "Yes, I was satisfied with the course content," etc.
* **Course Content** (`course_content`, `text`, optional): Provide a detailed description of the course content, including any strengths and weaknesses.
* **Teaching Quality** (`teaching_quality`, `select_one`, required): Select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the teaching quality of the course.
* **Learning Experience** (`learning_experience`, `text`, optional): Share your thoughts on your learning experience, including any challenges you faced or opportunities for improvement.
* **Instructor Help** (`instructor_help`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that describe the help you received from the instructor, such as "Yes, I received enough help from the instructor."
* **Class Size** (`class_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you were satisfied with the class size.
* **Course Materials** (`course_materials`, `number`, optional): Rate the course materials, from 1 to 5, with 1 being "not satisfactory" and 5 being "very satisfactory."
* **Course Length** (`course_length`, `number`, optional): Rate the course length, from 1 to 5, with 1 being "too short" and 5 being "just right."
* **Additional Comments** (`what_do_you_want_to_comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback you have about the course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the course you are providing feedback about.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the instructor who taught the course.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
