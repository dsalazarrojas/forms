# Daycare Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from parents and caregivers about their experience with our daycare center. The feedback will help us identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions to enhance our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Answer the questions on the first page, which asks about your initial experience with our daycare center.
2. Click on the "Submit" button to proceed to the next page.
3. On the second page, answer the remaining questions, which will ask about your experience with specific aspects of our services.
4. Select the options that best fit your answer to each question.
5. Fill in any additional information in the text fields as required.
6. Finally, submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title**: Enter a brief title for your feedback.
    - This will be used to identify your feedback and help us understand the main topic of your comment.
    - Keep it concise and relevant to your experience.
- **Feedback Question 1: What do you see first in our daycare?**
    - Please answer this question briefly, focusing on what you notice when you first enter or approach our daycare center.
    - This will help us understand your initial impression of our services.
- **Feedback Question 2: What do you see next?**
    - Answer this question by describing what you notice after exploring our daycare center further.
    - This will help us identify areas that may need improvement.
- **Feedback Question 3: What do you see at the end?**
    - Describe what you notice when you leave or end your visit to our daycare center.
    - This will help us understand how we can improve our goodbye procedures.
- **Submit: Did you submit your feedback?**
    - Choose "Yes" if you have submitted your feedback successfully.
    - Otherwise, select "No" and we'll be in touch with you to help you complete the form.
- **Overall Rating: How would you rate our services?**
    - Select a number between 1 and 10 to rate your overall experience.
    - This will help us understand your general impression of our services.
- **Suggestion**: Provide any additional comments or suggestions you have for improvement.
    - This is your chance to share any thoughts or ideas you may have for us to consider.
- **Email and Phone Number**: Enter your contact information so we can follow up with you about your feedback.
    - Please ensure you enter a valid email address and phone number.
- **Rating: How would you rate our services?**
    - Select from the available options to rate different aspects of our services.
    - This will help us understand your experience with different services.
- **Second Page: Select from the available options.**
    - Choose the options that best fit your answer to each question.
    - This will help us understand your experience with our services.
- **Overall Rating: How would you rate our services?**
    - Select from the available options to rate your overall experience.
    - This will help us understand your general impression of our services.
- **Date and Time: When did you visit our daycare center?**
    - Enter the date and time of your visit.
    - This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
