# Parent School Program Preference Poll Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a poll to gather feedback from parents about their preferences and priorities for the school program. It is designed to help the school administration understand the needs and expectations of parents, and to identify areas that require improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions carefully and select your preferences for each option.
2. Answer each question truthfully, and choose the options that best reflect your thoughts and feelings.
3. You can select multiple options for each question, so feel free to choose as many as apply.
4. When you're finished, submit your input by clicking the "Submit" button.
