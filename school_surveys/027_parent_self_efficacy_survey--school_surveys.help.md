# Parent Self Efficacy Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to help us understand how parents feel about their own self-efficacy, confidence, and support systems in helping their child's education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions to the best of your ability. Most questions are optional, so feel free to answer as many or as few as you like. If you need help or clarification on any question, you can ask your child's teacher or school staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: This is just a welcome message to get you started.
* **Parent role**: A text field where you can describe your relationship with your child.
* **Self Efficacy**: This is a numeric field where you can enter a value between 0 and 100 to indicate your self-efficacy level.
* **Confidence in abilities**: A select one field where you can choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your confidence in your abilities.
* **Parent Support Systems**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways you support your child (e.g., "Yes" to "Parenting classes" or "Counseling").
* **Child Outcomes**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the outcomes you've seen in your child (e.g., "Improved grades" or "Better behavior").
* **Child Characteristics**: A text field where you can describe your child's characteristics.
* **Parent Coping Mechanisms**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways you cope with challenges (e.g., "Talking to a therapist" or "Support group").
* **Teacher Support**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways your child receives support from teachers (e.g., "Regular check-ins" or "Individualized instruction").
* **Home Environment**: A text field where you can describe your home environment.
* **Community**: A text field where you can describe your community.
* **School Support**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways your child receives support at school (e.g., "Regular progress reports" or "Parent-teacher conferences").
* **Efficacy Scale**: A select one field where you can choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your self-efficacy level.
* **Parent Outcomes**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the outcomes you've seen in yourself (e.g., "Increased self-esteem" or "Better time management skills").
* **Efficacy Score**: A numeric field where you can enter a value between 0 and 100 to indicate your self-efficacy level.
* **Comments**: A text field where you can provide any additional comments or feedback.
* **Parent Involvement**: A text field where you can describe your level of involvement in your child's education.
* **Parent Educational Partnership**: A text field where you can describe your partnership with your child's educational providers.
* **Parent Cognitive Applications**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways you apply your knowledge (e.g., "Parenting books" or "Online courses").
* **Self-Reported Self Efficacy**: A select one field where you can choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your self-efficacy level as reported by you.
* **Home Environment Support**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways your home environment supports your child (e.g., "Structured routine" or "Safe space to learn").
* **Community Characteristics**: A text field where you can describe your community.
* **Teacher Characteristics**: A text field where you can describe your child's teacher.
* **Teacher Supportive Relationship**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways your child's teacher shows support (e.g., "Regular communication" or "Positive feedback").
* **Teacher Cognitive Support**: A select multiple field where you can choose all the ways your child's teacher provides cognitive support (e.g., "Extra help sessions" or "Individualized instruction").
