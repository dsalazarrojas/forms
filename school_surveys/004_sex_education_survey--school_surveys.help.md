# Sex Education Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sex Education Survey is a form designed to collect feedback from various categories of personnel (students, teachers, parents, health professionals, and counselors) about the sex education provided in schools. This survey aims to improve the sex education program by gathering honest and informed feedback from those directly involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select the relevant category of personnel you belong to from the "School Surveys" field.
* Provide honest feedback about the sex education provided in the corresponding text field.
* Ensure that your feedback is accurate and truthful.
* Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Surveys** (`school_surveys_form`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This field allows you to select whether you have completed sex education surveys for the school year.
	+ Choose "Yes" if you have completed the survey, otherwise choose "No".
* **Students Feedback** (`student_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Share your thoughts and opinions about the sex education provided to students.
* **Teachers Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your feedback about the sex education provided to teachers.
* **Parents Feedback** (`parent_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Share your thoughts and opinions about the sex education provided to parents.
* **Health Professionals Feedback** (`health_professional_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Provide your feedback about the sex education provided to health professionals.
* **Counselors Feedback** (`counselor_feedback`, text, optional):
	+ Share your thoughts and opinions about the sex education provided to counselors.
* **No coding used** (`no_coding_used`, text, optional):
	+ If you are not sure or do not have an answer to any of the above fields, please write "No coding used".
 
## Tips
- Make sure to review and understand the purpose of this survey before completing it.
- Ensure that your feedback is honest, accurate, and truthful.
- If you are not sure or do not have an answer to any field, please write "No coding used".
- Only authorized personnel should complete this form to avoid duplication and unauthorized access.
