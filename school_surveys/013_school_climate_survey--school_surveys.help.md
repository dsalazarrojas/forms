# School Climate Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is designed to gather opinions and experiences from students, teachers, and staff about the school climate, environment, and leadership characteristics. Your feedback is crucial in helping us identify areas of improvement and create a better school environment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form has multiple pages, each with a set of questions or fields.
2. For each field, please provide clear and concise answers based on your personal experience and opinion.
3. If a field has multiple options, select the one that best fits your answer.
4. If a field is marked as required, please make sure to answer it before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Climate** (Required): Describe your experience and feelings about the overall school climate.
* **School Environment** (Required): Describe the school environment and its impact on your daily life.
* **School Community** (Required): Describe your experience and feelings about the school community.
* **School Leadership** (Required): Describe your experience and feelings about the school leadership.
* **School Leadership Characteristics** (Required): Describe the leadership characteristics you value and appreciate in the school leadership.
* **School Leadership Supportive** (Required): Select "Yes" if you feel supported by the school leadership.
* **School Leadership Supportive Characteristics 1-18** (Not Required): Provide more details about the characteristics of supportive school leadership.

Note: Each field has a label and a hint, which is provided to help you understand what the field is about. If you need further clarification, please contact the school administration. 

## Tips
* Please answer each field carefully and honestly.
* If you're unsure about a field, ask yourself what you would want to happen to create a positive and supportive school environment.
* Take your time, and don't worry about the time limits.
