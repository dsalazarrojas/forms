# Chinese New Year Student Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This survey is designed to collect information from students about their knowledge, interests, and experiences related to Chinese New Year.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Select the correct options for each question.
3. Use the "Other" option when selecting multiple activities or cultures you are interested in.
4. Provide any additional comments or questions you have in the "Additional Comments" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_information`, `note`, required: false): This section is just a note field. You can provide any additional information about yourself, if you want to.
* **Your Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: true): Please enter your name as it is written on your report card or student ID.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your current grade level from the list.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, required: false): This is for your teacher's information only.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): This is for your school's information only.
* **Knowledge About Chinese New Year** (`knowledge_questions`, `note`, required: false): This section is just a note field. You can provide any information about what you know about Chinese New Year.
* **Had you heard of Chinese New Year before** (`heard_of_cny`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how much you know about Chinese New Year.
* **Do you know about the Chinese zodiac animals** (`know_zodiac`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how much you know about the Chinese zodiac animals.
* **Do you know which animal represents this year** (`know_current_year`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how much you know about the current zodiac animal.
* **Does your family celebrate Chinese New Year at home** (`celebrate_at_home`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how your family celebrates Chinese New Year.
* **Interest Level** (`interest_level`, `note`, required: false): This section is just a note field. You can provide any information about your interest level.
* **How interested are you in learning about Chinese New Year** (`interest_in_learning`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how interested you are in learning about Chinese New Year.
* **What activities would you enjoy most** (`interested_activities`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all the activities you would enjoy most during Chinese New Year celebrations.
* **Have you ever tried Chinese food** (`tried_chinese_food`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select your experience with Chinese food.
* **What is your favorite Chinese dish** (`favorite_chinese_dish`, `text`, required: false): If you have a favorite Chinese dish, please enter it here.
* **School Activities** (`school_activities`, `note`, required: false): This section is just a note field. You can provide any information about what you would like at school during Chinese New Year celebrations.
* **Would you like your school to celebrate Chinese New Year** (`want_school_celebration`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how you would like your school to celebrate Chinese New Year.
* **What activities should the school organize** (`suggested_activities`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Please select all the activities you would like the school to organize during Chinese New Year celebrations.
* **Would you like to learn to speak Chinese** (`want_to_learn_chinese`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how interested you are in learning to speak Chinese.
* **Why would you want to learn Chinese** (`reason_to_learn`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the reasons why you would want to learn Chinese.
* **Cultural Curiosity** (`cultural_curiosity`, `note`, required: false): This section is just a note field. You can provide any information about your interest in other cultures.
* **Are you interested in learning about other cultures** (`interested_other_cultures`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how interested you are in learning about other cultures.
* **Which cultures are you interested in learning about** (`cultures_interested_in`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the cultures you are interested in learning about.
* **Does your family have special traditions for holidays** (`family_traditions`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how your family celebrates holidays.
* **Would you like to share your family traditions with the class** (`share_traditions`, `select_one`, required: true): Please select how you would like to share your family traditions with the class.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required: false): Please use this section to provide any additional comments or questions you have.
