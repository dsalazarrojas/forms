# Student Basic Needs Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information about students' basic needs, demographics, and preferences to help the school understand their needs and provide targeted support.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out each page of the form thoroughly, following the instructions on each page.
