# Online Parent Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about their child, including contact details, interests, and relationships. Please fill in the form with the required and optional fields below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Review each field carefully and provide the required information.
2. If a field has options, select the one that best fits your situation.
3. If you don't see an option that matches your situation, select "Other" and provide a brief explanation in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information**: This field collects your contact details. Please provide your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Preferred**: Choose how you would like to be contacted in case of any updates or concerns about your child. You can select from Email, Phone, or Text.
* **Language**: Indicate which languages you or your child speak. This will help the school to better communicate with you.
* **School Interests**: Select the interests your child has, such as Art, Athletics, Music, or Drama.
* **Emergency Contact**: Identify who should be contacted in case of an emergency. You can select from Parent, Grandparent, Other, or specify below.
* **Emergency Contact Other (Specify)**: If you selected "Other" for Emergency Contact, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Student-Teacher Relationship**: Describe your relationship with your child's teacher. You can select from Student, Parent of a Student, Teacher, or Other.
* **Student-Parent Relationship**: Identify your relationship with your child. You can select from Parent, Guardian, Grandparent, or Other.
* **Age**: Enter your child's age.
* **Grade Level**: Select your child's grade level from K-3, 4-5, 6-8, or 9-12.
* **Section**: Indicate which section your child is in, such as A, B, C, or D.
* **Student's Teacher**: If you have a preferred teacher for your child, please provide their name.
* **Other (Specify)**: If you have a relationship with a teacher that is not listed, please provide a brief explanation.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or information about your child, please provide them here.
* **Student's Signature**: If your child is old enough, please have them sign the form to acknowledge that they have reviewed and understood the information provided.
* **Parent's Signature**: Please sign the form to confirm that you have reviewed and provided accurate information about your child.
