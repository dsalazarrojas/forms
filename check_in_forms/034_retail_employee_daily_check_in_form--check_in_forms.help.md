# retail_employee_daily_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the attendance of retail employees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the store ID to ensure that you are recording attendance for the correct store.
2. Enter the shift start time to record the start of your shift.
3. Enter the employee ID to associate the attendance record with your identity.
4. Enter your employee name to provide your personal details.
5. Select your attendance status from the options provided.
6. Enter your email and phone number to ensure that you can be contacted if needed.
7. Enter the store name and address to provide location details.
8. Fill in the shift date and time to record the shift details.
9. Enter the shift start and end times to record the shift duration.
10. Enter your employee phone number for contact purposes.
11. Enter the store number for store location details.
12. Enter the Employee ID Number for employee identification.
13. Enter the Employee Number for employee identification.
14. Enter the Employee Code for employee identification.
15. Enter the check-in time to record your arrival time.
16. Enter the check-out time to record your departure time.
17. Enter the check-in date to record your arrival date.
18. Enter the check-out date to record your departure date.
19. Enter the Employee Code Number for employee identification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **store id** (`store_id`, text, optional): Enter the store ID where you are working.
* **shift start time** (`shift_start`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **employee id** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID for identification.
* **employee name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for personal details.
* **shift end** (`shift_end`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your shift.
* **attendance** (`attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Select your attendance status.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **store name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the store name where you are working.
* **store address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter the store address where you are working.
* **shift date** (`shift_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your shift.
* **shift time** (`shift_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your shift.
* **shift start time** (`shift_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your shift.
* **shift end time** (`shift_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your shift.
* **employee phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for employee details.
* **store number** (`store_number`, text, optional): Enter the store number for store location details.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the Employee ID Number for employee identification.
* **Employee Number** (`employee_number`, text, optional): Enter the Employee Number for employee identification.
* **Employee Code** (`employee_code`, text, optional): Enter the Employee Code for employee identification.
* **checkin time** (`checkin_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you arrived.
* **checkout time** (`checkout_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you left.
* **checkin date** (`checkin_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you arrived.
* **checkout date** (`checkout_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you left.
* **Employee Code Number** (`employee_code_number`, text, optional): Enter the Employee Code Number for employee identification.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
