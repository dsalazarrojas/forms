# Trail Visitor Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trail Visitor Check In Form is used to collect information from trail visitors for check-in and check-out purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the visitor's information.
2. Select the arrival type(s) from the options provided.
3. Add any additional check-in and check-out notes.
4. Review and submit the form to update or create a new check-in record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trail Visitor Check In** (`trail_visitor_check_in_form`): Please enter the visitor's name for the Trailhead Check In Form.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`): Enter the time the visitor is checking in.
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`): Enter the time the visitor is checking out.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`): Enter the date the visitor is checking in.
* **Trailhead Name** (`trailhead_name`): Enter the name of the trailhead the visitor is visiting.
* **Team Member** (`team_member`): Enter the name of the team member accompanying the visitor.
* **Trail Number** (`trail_number`): Enter the trail number the visitor is on.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the visitor's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the visitor's email address.
* **Check In Note** (`check_in_note`): Add any additional check-in notes or comments.
* **Arrivals Type** (`select_one_arrival_type`): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for the type of arrival.
* **Select Multiple Arrivals Types** (`select_multiple_arrival_types`): Select one or more of the arrival types from the options.
* **Check Out Note** (`check_out_note`): Add any additional check-out notes or comments.
* **Check In Count** (`check_in_count`): Enter the check-in count for the visitor.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Double-check the information entered for accuracy before submitting the form.
