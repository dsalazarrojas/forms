# Tandem Nutrition Weekly Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tandem Nutrition Weekly Check In Form is a tool for users to share their progress with the Tandem Nutrition program every week. It's a crucial part of the program, as it helps us track your health and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form every week to report your progress.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and honest information about your diet, nutrition, and any other relevant details.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_input** (`<text>`, optional): Enter any relevant information or comments about your progress. This field is optional but can be useful to provide context to your responses.
* **nutrition_info** (`<number>`, **required**): Please enter your current weight (in kg) to help us track your progress. This field is required.
* **weekly_check_in** (`<date>`, optional): Enter the date of the current week's check in. This field is optional but recommended for record-keeping purposes.
* **time_input** (`<time>`, optional): Enter the time you completed this form. This field is optional but helps us track your submission time for internal record-keeping purposes.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
