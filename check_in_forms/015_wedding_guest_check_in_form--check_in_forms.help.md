# Wedding Guest Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wedding Guest Check In Form is used to collect information and track the attendance of wedding guests. This form helps the wedding party to manage guest details, attendance, and any special messages or comments.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Guest First Name" field with the guest's first name.
2. Enter the guest's last name in the "Guest Last Name" field.
3. Provide the guest's email address in the "Guest Email" field.
4. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Guest Phone" field.
5. Add any special message for the wedding party in the "Message for Wedding Party" field (optional).
6. Select the guest's attendance status by choosing "Yes" or "No" in the "Is Guest Attending" field.
7. If applicable, add any additional comments in the "Guest Comment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest First Name** (`guest_first_name`, text, required): Enter the guest's first name.
- **Guest Last Name** (`guest_last_name`, text, required): Enter the guest's last name.
- **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter the guest's email address.
- **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, required): Enter the guest's phone number.
- **Message for Wedding Party** (`guest_message`, text, optional): Enter a special message for the wedding party (e.g., a birthday greeting).
- **Is Guest Attending** (`guest_attendance`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" if the guest is attending the wedding, "No" otherwise.
- **Guest Comment** (`guest_comment`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about the guest.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the guest information before submitting.
* If the guest's attendance status is "No", you can leave a note or a reason for their absence in the "Guest Comment" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please contact the wedding party directly.
