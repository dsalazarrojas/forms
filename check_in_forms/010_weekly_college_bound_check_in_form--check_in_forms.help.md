# Weekly College Bound Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from college-bound students for weekly check-in processes. It helps administrators and other staff members to track student progress and stay updated on their activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your Student ID in the **Student ID** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
6. Select a date for the check-in in the **Date** field.
7. Select a time for the check-in in the **Time** field.
8. Select one option from the **Select One** field.
9. Select multiple options from the **Select Multiple** field.
10. If applicable, enter the **Reporting Frequency ID** field.
