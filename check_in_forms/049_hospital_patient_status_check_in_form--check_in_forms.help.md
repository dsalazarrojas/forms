# Hospital Patient Status Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Patient Status Check In Form is used for updating the status of an existing hospital patient. The form collects details about the patient's check-in, patient information, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the patient's name
* Enter the patient's ID
* Select whether the patient is assigned to a doctor or not
* Enter the check-in date and time
* Enter the status update date and time
* Add any comments or notes
* Enter the hospital name and department
* Enter the contact number and email of the patient
* Enter the doctor and nurse names responsible for the patient

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checkin Details** (Checkin Date, Checkin Time): Enter the date and time when the patient checked in to the hospital.
* **Patient Info** (Patient Name, Patient ID, Hospital Name, Department): Enter the patient's name, ID, hospital name, and department.
* **Patient Status Update** (Patient Status Update 2, Patient Status Update 3): Enter any updates to the patient's status, such as changes to their condition or treatment.
* **Status Update Date** (Status Update Date 2, Status Update Date 3): Enter the date and time of the status update.
* **Comments** (Comments, Comments 2): Add any comments or notes about the patient's status or condition.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To): Select whether the patient is assigned to a doctor or nurse.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Number, Email): Enter the patient's contact number and email.
* **Doctor Name** (Doctor Name): Enter the name of the doctor responsible for the patient.
* **Nurse Name** (Nurse Name): Enter the name of the nurse responsible for the patient.
