# Airport VIP Lounge Guest Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Airport VIP Lounge Guest Check In form is a crucial tool for managing the guest experience in our VIP lounges. It collects essential information about each guest, ensuring that our staff is prepared to provide an exceptional experience during their visit. By filling out this form, guests can inform us about their specific needs, preferences, and requirements, allowing us to tailor our services to meet their expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Airport VIP Lounge Guest Check In form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select the airline you are flying with and your flight number.
3. Choose your check-in time to ensure that our staff is ready to welcome you when you arrive.
4. Select your access type to inform our staff about your entitlement to lounge access.
5. If you have a frequent flyer number, please provide it for any applicable benefits.
6. Specify the number of additional guests accompanying you.
7. Choose your dietary requirements and preferences for our lounge catering options.
8. Indicate whether you wish to access the quiet/work zone, use shower facilities (subject to availability), and estimate your stay duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your visit.
* **Airline** (`airline_name`, `text`, required): Choose the airline you are flying with from the options provided.
* **Flight Number** (`flight_number`, `text`, required): Enter your flight number for easy identification.
* **Check-in Time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, required): Select your estimated arrival time to check in with our staff.
* **Access Type** (`access_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the access type that applies to you, such as a First Class ticket or business class ticket.
* **Frequent Flyer Number** (`frequent_flyer_number`, `text`, optional): If you have a frequent flyer number, provide it for any applicable benefits.
* **Number of Additional Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Indicate the number of additional guests accompanying you.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any dietary requirements or preferences for our lounge catering options.
* **Quiet/Work Zone** (`work_zone_requested`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you wish to access the quiet/work zone.
* **Shower Facilities** (`shower_facilities`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you wish to use shower facilities (subject to availability).
* **Estimated Stay Duration** (`estimated_stay_duration`, `select_one`, required): Select your estimated stay duration in the lounge.
* **Attending Lounge Agent Name** (`staff_signature`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the staff member attending to you.
* **Agreement to Code of Conduct and House Rules** (`agreement_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your agreement to the lounge code of conduct and house rules.
