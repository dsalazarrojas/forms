# Coworking Space Member Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from members about their check-in experience at the coworking space. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we need to improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. If you need help or clarification on any field, please ask.
2. Select "Not Satisfied", "Very Satisfied", or "Extremely Satisfied" for the "rating" field.
3. Choose a date for the "check_in_date" field.
4. Enter your name in the "member_name" field.
5. Enter the time of your check-in for the "check_in_time" field.
6. Type the name of the coworker you met during check-in for the "coworker_name" field.
7. Share your feedback in the "feedback" field.
8. Enter your email for the "email" field.
9. Provide your phone number in the "phone_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Date (check_in_date)**: Select a date from the calendar view, or type in the date using the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Rating (rating)**: Choose one of the three options to rate your experience:
	+ Not Satisfied: We fell short of your expectations.
	+ Very Satisfied: We met your expectations.
	+ Extremely Satisfied: We exceeded your expectations.
* **Member Name (member_name)**: Enter your name as you would like it to be printed in our records.
* **Check In Time (check_in_time)**: Enter the time you arrived for our records.
* **Coworker Name (coworker_name)**: If you met a coworker during check-in, type their name here.
* **Feedback (feedback)**: Share your thoughts and suggestions about your experience.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions or discussions.
