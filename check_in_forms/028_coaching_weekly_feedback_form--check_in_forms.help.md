# Coaching Weekly Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect weekly feedback from coaching sessions, ensuring consistent and continuous improvement of coaching skills.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the specific week of sessions being reviewed.
2. Enter the name and email address of the coach.
3. Rate the effectiveness of the sessions, including overall session effectiveness, athlete engagement, and communication effectiveness.
4. Provide feedback on coaching quality, including the quality of instruction, communication effectiveness, and feedback provided.
5. Evaluate the progress of athletes, including overall progress and specific areas of improvement.
6. Identify any challenges or concerns that arose during the sessions.
7. Indicate any athletes needing additional attention.
8. Specify the focus areas for the next week's sessions.
9. Make any necessary coaching adjustments.
10. Provide an overall rating of the week's sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week Of (id: 3)** (`feedback_week`, date, required): Enter the date of the week being reviewed.
* **Coach Name (id: 2)** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coach providing the feedback.
* **Your Name (id: 4)** (`respondent_name`, text, required): Enter your name providing the feedback.
* **Email Address (id: 5)** (`respondent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Number of Sessions Held This Week (id: 7)** (`sessions_held`, number, required): Enter the number of sessions held during the week.
* **Overall Session Effectiveness (id: 8)** (`session_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of the sessions on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Athlete Engagement Level (id: 9)** (`athlete_engagement`, select_one, required): Rate the engagement level of athletes on a scale from Very engaged to Not engaged.
* **Coaching Quality (id: 11)** (`coaching_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of instruction on a scale from Excellent to Needs improvement.
* **Communication Effectiveness (id: 12)** (`communication_effectiveness`, select_one, required): Rate the effectiveness of coaching messages on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Quality of Feedback Given (id: 13)** (`feedback_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of feedback provided on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
* **Overall Athlete Progress (id: 15)** (`athlete_progress`, select_one, required): Rate the progress of athletes on a scale from Significant progress to No progress.
* **Areas of Progress (id: 16)** (`progress_areas`, text, optional): Specify the areas of progress made by athletes.
* **Challenges or Concerns This Week (id: 18)** (`challenges_this_week`, text, optional): Identify any challenges or concerns that arose during the sessions.
* **Athletes Needing Additional Attention (id: 19)** (`athletes_needing_attention`, text, optional): Indicate any athletes needing additional attention.
* **Focus Areas for Next Week (id: 21)** (`focus_next_week`, text, required): Specify the focus areas for the next week's sessions.
* **Coaching Adjustments Needed (id: 22)** (`adjustments_needed`, text, optional): Suggest any coaching adjustments that may be necessary.
* **Overall Week Rating (id: 24)** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the overall week's rating on a scale from Excellent to Poor.
