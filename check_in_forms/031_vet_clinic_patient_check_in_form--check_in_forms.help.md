# Vet Clinic Patient Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a patient's visit to the vet clinic, including their personal details, medical history, and visit details. This information will be used to create a medical record for the patient and to provide accurate and effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the first page of the form.
2.  Enter the patient's first name.
3.  Enter the patient's last name.
4.  Enter the patient's email address.
5.  Enter the patient's phone number.
6.  Enter the patient's species.

**Page 2**

*   **Age**: Enter the patient's age.
*   **Weight (lbs)**: Enter the patient's weight.
*   **Height (in)**: Enter the patient's height.
*   **Owner Name**: Enter the patient's owner's name.
*   **Owner Email**: Enter the patient's owner's email address.
*   **Owner Phone**: Enter the patient's owner's phone number.

**Page 3**

*   **Reason for Visit**: Enter the reason for the patient's visit.
*   **Visit Type**: Select the type of visit (Emergency, Regular Check-In, or Other).
*   **Medical Notes**: Enter any medical notes or comments.
*   **Medical Record Number**: Enter the patient's medical record number.
*   **Arrival Time**: Enter the time the patient arrived at the clinic.
*   **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of the patient's scheduled appointment.
*   **Pet Name**: Enter the patient's name.
*   **Owner Address**: Enter the patient's owner's address.

**Page 4**

*   **Veterinarian Notes**: Enter any notes from the veterinarian.
*   **Medication**: Enter any medication the patient is taking.
*   **Allergies**: Enter any allergies the patient has.
*   **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions the patient has.
*   **Previous Visit Date**: Enter the date of the patient's previous visit.
*   **Owner Comments**: Enter any comments from the patient's owner.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to ensure the patient's information is up-to-date.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or advice from the veterinarian.
*   If you need to add any additional information not listed here, please contact the clinic staff.
*   This form is designed to be completed by the patient's owner or a representative of the patient.
