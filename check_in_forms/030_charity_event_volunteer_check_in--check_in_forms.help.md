# Charity Event Volunteer Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for charity event volunteers, ensuring they are properly assigned to tasks, receive necessary resources, and are accounted for throughout the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the **Volunteer Name** field.
2. Provide your email address in the **Email Address** field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field. This will be used to contact you in case of emergencies.
4. Enter the name of the charity event you are participating in in the **Event Name** field.
5. Select the date of the event in the **Event Date** field.
6. Choose your arrival time in the **Check-In Time** field.
7. Select the role assigned to you by event organizers in the **Assigned Role** field. This could be registration desk, usher, or other roles as listed.
8. Specify the location where you will be working in the **Assigned Location** field.
9. Enter the shift time and duration you have been assigned in the **Shift Time** field.
10. If available, select the name and number of the person who will supervise your work in the **Supervisor Name** field.
11. If applicable, indicate if you have received a uniform in the **Uniform Received** field.
12. If you have a uniform, select the size from the options provided.
13. List the materials you have received for the event, such as a name badge, clipboard, etc. in the **Materials Received** field.
14. Indicate if you have completed any prior training for this event in the **Training Completed** field.
15. Provide the name and number of your emergency contact person in the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone** fields.
16. If you have any health concerns or allergies, list them in the **Any Health Concerns** field.
17. If you will be leaving the event at a specific time, select it in the **Check-Out Time** field.
18. Enter the total hours you have worked during the event in the **Total Hours Worked** field.
19. Finally, confirm your check-in by selecting **I confirm my check-in** in the **Volunteer Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the charity event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Check-In Time**: Choose your arrival time.
* **Assigned Role**: Select the role assigned to you by event organizers.
* **Assigned Location**: Specify the location where you will be working.
* **Shift Time**: Enter the shift time and duration assigned to you.
* **Supervisor Name**: If available, select the person who will supervise your work.
* **Uniform Received**: Indicate if you have received a uniform.
* **Uniform Size**: If you have a uniform, select the size.
* **Materials Received**: List the materials you have received for the event.
* **Training Completed**: Indicate if you have completed prior training for this event.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the number of your emergency contact person.
* **Any Health Concerns**: List any health concerns or allergies you have.
* **Check-Out Time**: If you will be leaving the event at a specific time, select it.
* **Total Hours Worked**: Enter the total hours you have worked during the event.
* **Volunteer Signature**: Confirm your check-in by selecting **I confirm my check-in**.
