# Pet Boarding Check In Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for pet owners to provide critical information about their pets while they are at the pet boarding facility. It aims to ensure the safe and healthy care of their pets during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Please note that all fields are optional, except for those marked as "Required"**.
- **You can navigate between pages using the navigation buttons at the bottom or top of the form**.
- **If you encounter any issues, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Details** (`pet_boarding_check_in_form_2`, `text`, required): This field is mandatory for providing essential information about your pet, including its species, age, and weight.
* **Pet Health Status** (`pet_health_status_form`, `text`, optional): Here, you can describe your pet's health status, including any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_form`, `text`, optional): If your pet has any pre-existing medical conditions or allergies, please provide details here.

## Tips
- **Please provide accurate and up-to-date information** about your pet for their proper care and attention.
- **You can always reach out to the pet boarding staff if you have any questions or concerns**.
- **Remember to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for any further assistance**.
