# 21 Day Weekly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 21 Day Weekly Check In form is designed to help participants of a 21-day program track their progress, mood, and challenges on a weekly basis. This form helps you provide valuable insights for yourself or others in the program. Please ensure you are using this form within the context of the 21-day program schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details, including your name and email address.
2. Select the week number (1-3) that this check-in covers.
3. Choose the check-in date for the selected week.
4. Rate your overall mood for the week using the provided options.
5. Enter your average sleep hours per night.
6. Report the total minutes of planned activity for the week.
7. Select any main challenges you encountered during the week (select all that apply).
8. Write any progress notes or observations.
9. Decide if you want a follow-up check-in or coach contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant full name**: Enter your first and last name to identify yourself as a participant in the program.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address so that we can reach you for follow-up or further guidance.
* **Week number**: Select which week (1-3) of the 21-day program this check-in covers.
* **Check-in date**: Enter the date for which you are checking in.
* **How would you rate your overall mood this week?**: Select the option that best matches your overall feeling during the week.
* **Average sleep hours per night**: Enter the average number of hours you slept each night.
* **Total minutes of planned activity for the week**: Enter the total minutes of structured exercise or activity you have planned for the week.
* **Main challenges encountered (select all that apply)**: Select any challenges you faced during the week, such as motivation, time constraints, or minor injuries.
* **Progress notes or observations**: Write any notes about your progress, successes, or setbacks.
* **Do you want a follow-up check-in or coach contact?**: Select whether you want a follow-up check-in or coach contact for further guidance.

## Tips

Please make sure to fill out this form for each week of the 21-day program, and use this opportunity to reflect on your progress and challenges. It's essential to be honest and thorough in your answers to get the most out of this exercise.
