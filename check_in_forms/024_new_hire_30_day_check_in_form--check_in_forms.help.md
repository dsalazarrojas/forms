# New Hire 30 Day Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire 30 Day Check In Form is used to collect important information about new employees 30 days after their start date. This form helps the HR department to verify that all necessary paperwork and processes have been completed for the new employee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your Employee ID, Employee Name, and Job Title.
2. Check the Department field to indicate if the new hire is in a new department.
3. Enter your Manager Name and Start Date.
4. Add any additional Notes about the new hire.
5. Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (employee_id, number, required): Enter your Employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Department** (department, select, optional): Check 'Yes' if the new hire is in a new department.
* **Job Title** (job_title, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Manager Name** (manager_name, text, optional): Enter your manager's name.
* **Start Date** (start_date, date, optional): Enter the date the new hire started work.
* **Notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or observations about the new hire.
