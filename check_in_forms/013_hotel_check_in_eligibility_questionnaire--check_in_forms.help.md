# Hotel Check In Eligibility Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Check In Eligibility Questionnaire is a form designed to collect demographic information from hotel guests to help hotel management assess their eligibility for check-in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Guest Name" field. This is the only identifying information required.
2. If you are comfortable sharing your date of birth, please select it in the "Birthdate" field. This information is optional.
3. If you are a guest from a specific country, select your nationality from the list. This information is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Birthdate** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you wish to provide your date of birth for hotel management's reference. 
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, `select_one`, optional): Select your country of origin from the list to help hotel management understand your background.
