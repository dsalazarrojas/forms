# Vacation Home Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Vacation Home Check In Form" is a crucial document that helps the homeowner or property manager to gather essential information from guests upon their arrival. By completing this form, guests ensure that their needs are met, and the property is prepared for their stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Guest Name field with the name of the guest.
2. Enter the Guest Email address of the guest.
3. Provide the Guest Phone number of the guest.
4. Enter the Guest Arrival Date.
5. Enter the Guest Arrival Time.
6. Enter the Expected Arrival Time.
7. Enter the Guest Party Size, which is the number of people in the guest's party.
8. Enter the Number of Children traveling with the guest.
9. Add any Additional Notes or requests in the Guest Notes field.
10. Finally, sign the Guest Signature field with the guest's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the guest.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the guest.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the guest.
* **Guest Arrival Date** (`guest_arrival_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the guest's arrival.
* **Guest Arrival Time** (`guest_arrival_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the guest's arrival.
* **Expected Arrival Time** (`guest_expected_arrival_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the expected time of the guest's arrival.
* **Guest Party Size** (`guest_number_of_guests`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of people in the guest's party.
* **Number of Children** (`guest_number_of_children`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of children traveling with the guest.
* **Additional Notes** (`guest_note`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or requests from the guest.
* **Guest Signature** (`guest_signature`, `text`, required/optional): Sign with the guest's signature.
