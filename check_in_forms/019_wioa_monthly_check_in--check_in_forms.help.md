# WIOA Monthly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and record participant progress and work hours for WIOA (Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act) programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name or participant ID in the "Participant" field.
2. Enter the name of your program in the "Program Name" field.
3. Enter your job title or position in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter the start date of your work period in the "Start Date" field.
5. Enter the end date of your work period in the "End Date" field.
6. Enter the number of hours you worked during the month in the "Hours Worked" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
8. If you have any specific notes or comments related to your WIOA program, enter them in the "WIOA Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant** (`participant`, text, required): This field is used to identify the participant who is completing this form.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started work on your program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you ended work on your program.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, required): Enter the total number of hours you worked during the month.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any comments or feedback you have about your work or progress.
* **WIOA Notes** (`wioa_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments specific to your WIOA program.
