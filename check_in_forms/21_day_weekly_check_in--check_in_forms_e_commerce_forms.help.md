# 21 Day Weekly Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 21 Day Weekly Check In form is designed to track and reflect on progress within the 21-Day Transformation Program. It's used for accountability, monitoring, and evaluation of participants' adherence to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Full Name** (id: 2) as registered for the program.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** (id: 3) associated with your program purchase.
3.  Select the **Date of Check-In** (id: 4) as today's date.
4.  Choose the **Which week are you completing?** (id: 5) that just ended.
5.  Optionally, track your **Current Weight** (id: 7) in pounds or kilograms.
6.  Rate your **Energy Level** (id: 8) from 1 (completely exhausted) to 10 (best energy ever).
7.  Enter your **Average Sleep per Night** (id: 9) in hours.
8.  Report the number of **Scheduled Workouts Completed** (id: 10) this week.
9.  Select your **Overall Mood** (id: 12) from the provided options.
10. Rate your **Program Adherence** (id: 16) from 100% to 0%.
11. Rate your **Satisfaction with Workouts & Meal Plans** (id: 18) from 1 to 10.
12. Select the **Support Needed for Next Week** (id: 19) options that apply.
13. Set your **Goals for Next Week** (id: 20) in the text field.
14. Enter any **Additional Comments or Feedback** (id: 21) in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name (id: 2)**: Enter your name as registered for the program to ensure accurate tracking and communication.
*   **Email Address (id: 3)**: Enter the email associated with your program purchase to receive updates and notifications.
*   **Date of Check-In (id: 4)**: Today's date for accurate tracking of progress and milestones.
*   **Which week are you completing? (id: 5)**: Select the week you just finished to reflect on your progress.
*   **Current Weight (id: 7)**: Optional tracking metric; enter your weight in pounds or kilograms if you wish to track it.
*   **Energy Level (id: 8)**: Rate your energy level from 1 (completely exhausted) to 10 (best energy ever).
*   **Average Sleep per Night (id: 9)**: Enter your average sleep duration in hours.
*   **Scheduled Workouts Completed (id: 10)**: Report the number of scheduled workouts completed.
*   **Overall Mood (id: 12)**: Select how you felt mentally and emotionally this week from the provided options.
*   **Satisfaction with Workouts & Meal Plans (id: 18)**: Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 10.
*   **Support Needed for Next Week (id: 19)**: Select the options that apply to receive the help you need.
*   **Goals for Next Week (id: 20)**: Set your goals and focus areas for the upcoming week.
*   **Additional Comments or Feedback (id: 21)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to share.

**Tips**

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form to provide valuable insights for your progress.
*   Use this opportunity to reflect on your experiences, identify areas for improvement, and seek support when needed.
*   Don't hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields.
*   This form is a tool to help you succeed in the 21-Day Transformation Program; feel free to use it as a resource for your growth and development.
