# fisheries_worker_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fisheries_worker_check_in_form is used to collect information from a fisheries worker about their job site, dates, and contact details. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the job site from the options provided in the "Job Site" field.
2. Enter the date of the job in the "job_date" field.
3. Enter the time of arrival in the "time_arrived" field.
4. Enter the time of departure in the "time_departed" field.
5. Optionally, enter a contact number in the "contact_number" field if applicable.
6. Optionally, enter a contact name in the "contact_name" field if applicable.
7. Optionally, enter a contact email in the "contact_email" field if applicable.
8. Optionally, enter an emergency contact number in the "emergency_contact_number" field if applicable.
9. Optionally, enter an emergency contact name in the "emergency_contact_name" field if applicable.
10. Optionally, enter an emergency contact email in the "emergency_contact_email" field if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Job Site** (`Job Site`, select_one): Select the correct job site from the options provided.
* **job_date** (`job_date`, date): Enter the date of the job.
* **time_arrived** (`time_arrived`, time): Enter the time of arrival.
* **time_departed** (`time_departed`, time): Enter the time of departure.
* **contact_number** (`contact_number`, text): If applicable, enter the contact number of the worker's contact person.
* **contact_name** (`contact_name`, text): If applicable, enter the name of the worker's contact person.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email): If applicable, enter the email of the worker’s contact person.
* **emergency_contact_number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text): If applicable, enter the contact number of the worker's emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): If applicable, enter the name of the worker's emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email): If applicable, enter the email of the worker's emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the fishery's administration.
* This form is part of the fishery's operations and is used to facilitate communication and record-keeping.
