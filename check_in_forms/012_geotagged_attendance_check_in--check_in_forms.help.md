# Geotagged Attendance Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geotagged Attendance Check In form is used to collect attendance information for events that have been geotagged, allowing participants to provide their details and event-specific data. This form is typically used for events that are associated with a specific location or region.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the event information provided on the first page to ensure you are filling out the correct form.
2. Enter your participant information on the participant info page, including your name, email, phone number, and workgroup (if applicable).
3. Select your participant's location on the participant location page.
4. Add any additional notes or comments on the notes page.
5. Review and submit the form to confirm your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information** (`event_info`, text): This page provides a brief summary of the event details. Please review this information carefully to ensure you are filling out the correct form.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text): This page collects your participant information, including your name, email address, phone number, and workgroup (if applicable). Please enter your details accurately and truthfully.
* **Geotags** (`geotags`, text): This field is not currently used in the form and is reserved for future development.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text): This page provides a space for you to sign your name, verifying your attendance. Please enter your signature accurately and sign your name clearly.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text): This field is for the participant's name. Please enter your name as you would like it to appear in the participant list.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email): This field is for the participant's email address. Please enter your email address correctly.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text): This field is for the participant's phone number. Please enter your phone number accurately.
* **Participant Workgroup** (`participant_workgroup`, select_one): This field is to select the participant's workgroup. Please choose your workgroup from the options provided.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, select_multiple): This field is to select the participant's location. Please choose your location from the options provided.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date): This field is for the event date. Please enter the date in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time): This field is for the event time. Please enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text): This field is for the event location. Please enter the location accurately.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): This field is for any additional notes or comments about the event. Please enter your comments or notes accurately.
