# High School Student Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Student Check In Form is used to collect and track student information for internal purposes. This includes student name, school affiliation, status, grade level, class enrollment, contact information, and any additional notes or information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select your school affiliation from the "School ID" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your current location in the "Student Location" field.
4. Select your current status from the "Status" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your grade level from the "Grade Level" dropdown menu.
6. Select your current class(es) from the "Class" dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, enter any additional contact information for the student in the "Contact Info" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or information about the student in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the student.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, optional): Enter the school ID or affiliation number for the student.
* **Student Location** (`student_location`, text, optional): Enter the current location of the student.
* **Status** (`student_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the student (Active or Inactive).
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, optional): Select the current grade level of the student (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Class** (`student_class`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current class(es) the student is enrolled in (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Contact Info** (`student_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the student.
* **Notes** (`student_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or information about the student.
* **Additional Info** (`student_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information that may be relevant to the student's check-in process.

## Tips
* Please make sure to accurately enter student information to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* If you are completing this form on behalf of another student, please ensure you have their permission to do so.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant school administrator for clarification.
