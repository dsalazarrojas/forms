# Childcare Daily Mood and Behavior Check-In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help staff collect daily feedback about the well-being and behavior of children in their care. It is used to track key aspects of the child's daily experience, including their mood, energy level, social interaction, physical symptoms, and any behavior concerns. This information will help staff provide better care and support to the children.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the check-in from the calendar.
2. Enter the time of the check-in.
3. Fill in the child's first and last name.
4. Enter the child's current age.
5. Enter the assigned room or class.
6. Select the child's overall mood.
7. Select the child's energy level.
8. Select how the child is interacting with others.
9. Select how the child is interacting with their peers.
10. Select how the child is interacting with adults.
11. Describe the child's emotional state.
12. Select how the child handled separation from their parent or guardian.
13. Select the child's appetite.
14. Select the child's sleep quality from the previous night.
15. Select any physical symptoms the child is experiencing.
16. Select any behavior concerns.
17. Select any comfort items the child needs today.
18. If necessary, provide additional support needed by the child.
19. Enter any additional observations from staff.
20. Select if parents should be notified of any concerns.
21. Enter your name as the staff member completing the check-in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-In Date**: Enter the date of the check-in using the calendar provided.
* **Check-In Time**: Enter the time of the check-in.
* **Child First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the child.
* **Child Age**: Enter the current age of the child in years.
* **Child Room or Class**: Enter the assigned room or class of the child.
* **Overall Mood**: Select the child's overall mood from the provided options.
* **Energy Level**: Select the child's energy level from the provided options.
* **Social Interaction**: Select how the child is interacting with others from the provided options.
* **Interaction with Peers**: Select how the child is interacting with their peers from the provided options.
* **Interaction with Adults**: Select how the child is interacting with adults from the provided options.
* **Emotional State**: Describe the child's emotional state in the provided options.
* **Separation from Parent**: Select how the child handled separation from their parent or guardian from the provided options.
* **Appetite**: Select the child's appetite from the provided options.
* **Sleep Quality Previous Night**: Select the child's sleep quality from the previous night from the provided options.
* **Physical Symptoms**: Select any physical symptoms the child is experiencing from the provided options.
* **Behavior Concerns**: Select any behavior concerns from the provided options.
* **Comfort Items Needed**: Select any comfort items the child needs today from the provided options.
* **Additional Support Needed**: If the child needs additional support, enter it in the provided text box.
* **Staff Notes**: Enter any additional observations from staff.
* **Parent Notification Needed**: Select if parents should be notified of any concerns from the provided options.
* **Staff Member Name**: Enter your name as the staff member completing the check-in.

## Tips
* Be as objective as possible when completing the form.
* If you are unsure of any information, consult with other staff members or supervisors.
* Complete the form for each child in your care daily.
* Ensure to follow up with parents and other relevant staff about any concerns or issues that arise.
