# Art Gallery Visitor Preference Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Visitor Preference Check In form is a simple form designed to gather visitor information and preferences for the art gallery. It's intended to be completed by visitors when they arrive at the gallery.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Visitor name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field, which will be used for follow-up and receipts.
3. Select your reason for visiting the gallery from the "Purpose of visit" dropdown menu.
4. Choose any relevant art styles or programs you are interested in from the "Interests" dropdown menu.
5. Select whether you would like to receive updates and events from the gallery.
6. If you have a favorite artist, you can enter their name in the "Favorite artist" field.
7. Answer "Yes" if you are interested in membership information.
8. Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred contact method" dropdown menu.
9. Provide any feedback about your check in experience in the "Visit feedback" field.
10. If you have any additional requests or notes, you can enter them in the "Additional requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email**: Enter your contact email address, which will be used for follow-up and receipts.
* **Purpose of visit**: Select the reason you are visiting the gallery today. This will help us understand the purpose of your visit and provide relevant information and updates.
* **Interests**: Choose any art styles or programs you are interested in, such as painting, sculpture, or photography.
* **Receive updates and events**: Select whether you would like to receive updates and events from the gallery.
* **Favorite artist**: If you have a favorite artist, enter their name here. This is optional.
* **Interested in membership**: Answer "Yes" if you are interested in becoming a member of the gallery.
* **Preferred contact method**: Select whether you prefer to be contacted by email or phone.
* **Visit feedback**: Provide any feedback about your check-in experience. This helps us understand how to improve our service.
* **Additional requests**: If you have any additional requests or notes, enter them here.
