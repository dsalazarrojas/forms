# Weight Management Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track their weight management goals, habits, and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field at the top of the page. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the text field below. This is a required field.
3. Select your **Weight Management Goals** from the dropdown menu. Please choose one or more goals that best describe your objectives.
4. Enter the number of **Workout Days per Week** you exercise. For example, "3" for three days a week.
5. Enter the total number of **Exercise Minutes per Week** you spend exercising.
6. List the **Healthy Foods Consumed** in the text field. This could include fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, etc.
7. List the **Unhealthy Foods Consumed** in the text field. This could include sugary snacks, fried foods, etc.
8. Enter your **Water Consumption** in the text field, including any relevant units (e.g., gallons, liters, etc.).
9. Check the **Weight Management Check In Date** field if you want to mark a specific date for your weight management check in. This is not required.
10. Select a relevant **Weight Management Check In Time** from the time dropdown menu. This field seems redundant, but it's included for completeness.
11. Add any **Comments** about your progress, goals, or concerns in the text area. This is not required.
