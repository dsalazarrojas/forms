# Weight Management Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track their weight management goals, habits, and progress.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your **First Name** in the text field at the top of the page. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the text field below. This is a required field.
3. Select your **Weight Management Goals** from the dropdown menu. Please choose one or more goals that best describe your objectives.
4. Enter the number of **Workout Days per Week** you exercise. For example, "3" for three days a week.
5. Enter the total number of **Exercise Minutes per Week** you spend exercising.
6. List the **Healthy Foods Consumed** in the text field. This could include fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, etc.
7. List the **Unhealthy Foods Consumed** in the text field. This could include sugary snacks, fried foods, etc.
8. Enter your **Water Consumption** in the text field, including any relevant units (e.g., gallons, liters, etc.).
9. Check the **Weight Management Check In Date** field if you want to mark a specific date for your weight management check in. This is not required.
10. Select a relevant **Weight Management Check In Time** from the time dropdown menu. This field seems redundant, but it's included for completeness.
11. Add any **Comments** about your progress, goals, or concerns in the text area. This is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: 
  * **(required)**: Enter your real name as it appears on your government-issued ID or driver's license.
  * **(text)**: This field accepts a string of text characters.
* **Email**: 
  * **(required)**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
  * **(text)**: This field expects a string of text that resembles an email address.
* **Weight Management Goals**: 
  * **(required)**: Choose one or more goals that align with your objectives. Options include:
    * Goal 1
    * Goal 2
    * Goal 3
* **Workout Days per Week**: 
  * **(optional)**: Enter the number of days you exercise per week, e.g., 3, 5, 7.
  * **(number)**: This field expects a numerical value.
* **Exercise Minutes per Week**: 
  * **(optional)**: Enter the total number of minutes you spend exercising per week.
  * **(number)**: This field expects a numerical value.
* **Healthy Foods Consumed**: 
  * **(optional)**: List the healthy foods you've eaten or consumed recently.
  * **(text)**: This field accepts a string of text characters.
* **Unhealthy Foods Consumed**: 
  * **(optional)**: List the unhealthy foods you've eaten or consumed recently.
  * **(text)**: This field accepts a string of text characters.
* **Water Consumption**: 
  * **(optional)**: Enter the amount of water you've consumed recently, including units if necessary.
  * **(text)**: This field accepts a string of text characters.
* **Weight Management Check In Date**: 
  * **(optional)**: Check a date you'd like to mark for your weight management check in.
  * **(date)**: This field expects a date in a recognizable format.
* **Weight Management Check In Time**: 
  * **(optional)**: Choose a time you'd like to mark for your weight management check in.
  * **(time)**: This field expects a time in a recognizable format.
* **Comments**: 
  * **(optional)**: Add any thoughts, concerns, or progress updates in this text area.
  * **(note)**: This field accepts a string of text characters.
