# Office Check In Form - Help Guide

This form is designed to track office visits and attendance, including check-in and check-out times, visit reasons, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. Enter your basic information

* **Check In Form** (`check_in_form`, text, required): Enter your name in this field.

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required): Enter your employee ID number.

* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your visit.
* **Reason For Visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, optional): Provide a brief reason for your visit.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your visit.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person you met.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person.

* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, optional): Choose the person assigned to handle your visit.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you for the visit.
* **Check In Status** (`check_in_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your check-in status.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the form.
* **Form Submitted From** (`form_submitted_from`, select_one, optional): Choose where the form was submitted from.
* **Check In Time Duration** (`check_in_time_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your visit.
* **Check In Frequency** (`check_in_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you check in.
* **Check In Type** (`check_in_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of visit.
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, time, optional): Mark your check-out time.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your check-out.
* **Office Address** (`office_address`, text, optional): Enter the office address.
* **Office Location Map** (`office_location_map`, text, optional): Enter the office location map.

* **Office Status** (`office_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the office status.
