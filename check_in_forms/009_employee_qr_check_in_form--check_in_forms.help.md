# Employee QR Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee QR Check In Form is a quick and easy way to record employees' attendance information when they start or end their shift.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
2. Select your shift from the "Shift" field.
3. Enter the current date and time in the "Check In Time" field.
4. If you are checking out, enter the date and time you will be checking out in the "Check Out Time" field. (This field is optional.)
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Notes" field.
6. If you have an email address or phone number you'd like to be reached, you can enter it in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
