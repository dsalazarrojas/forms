# Nurse Shift Start Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nurse Shift Start Check In Form is used to confirm or deny the start of a nurse's shift. This form is essential for tracking and recording the shift start times and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start of your shift by answering the "shift start checkin" question. Answer is either "Yes" or "No".
2. Enter the date you started your shift in the "shift start checkin form date" field.
3. Enter the time you started your shift in the "shift start checkin form time" field.
4. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
5. Enter any notes or comments about your shift start in the "shift start checkin form notes" field.
6. Enter the name of the hospital where you are working.
7. If you have a role (e.g., lead, supervisor) select "Yes", otherwise select "No".
8. If you are checking in as a team lead, select "Yes" or "No".
9. Enter the time you checked in for your shift in the "checkin time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shift start checkin** (Select One, Yes/No, required: false): Answer whether you are starting your shift or not.
* **shift start checkin form date** (Text, , required: false): Enter the date you started your shift (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **shift start checkin form time** (Time, , required: false): Enter the time you started your shift (format: hh:mm).
* **name** (Text, , required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **shift start checkin form notes** (Note, , required: false): Enter any notes or comments about your shift start.
* **hospital** (Text, , required: false): Enter the name of the hospital where you are working.
* **role** (Select One, Yes/No, required: false): Select "Yes" if you have a role such as a lead or supervisor, otherwise select "No".
* **team leads** (Select Multiple, Yes/No, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are checking in as a team lead, otherwise select "No".
* **checkin time** (Time, , required: false): Enter the time you checked in for your shift (format: hh:mm).
