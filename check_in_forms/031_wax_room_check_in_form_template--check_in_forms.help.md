## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the purpose and usage of the Wax Room Check In Form Template.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the date of the service delivery on the "Check In Date" field.
2. In the "Check In Fields" field, provide any relevant comments or special instructions for the technician.
3. Enter the client's "Client ID".
4. Enter the associate's "Associate ID", if applicable.
5. Select the service type from the "Service" dropdown menu. This could be "Bikini", "Half Leg", "Half Arm", etc.
6. Indicate if the client has any "Wax Type" issues. If they do, select "Yes" or "No".
7. In the "Condition" field, select all that apply from the list of available options. These might include "Newly Wax" or "Re-waxing (New)" or "Re-waxing (Maintenance)".
8. Enter any additional comments or instructions in the "Comments" field.
9. Upload any relevant "Photos" to support the service delivery.
10. Enter the client's name, email, and phone number for easy communication.
11. Enter the technician's name, email, and phone number for easy communication.
