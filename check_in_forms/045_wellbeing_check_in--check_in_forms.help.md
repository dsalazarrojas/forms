# wellbeing_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wellbeing check-in form collects information about an individual's wellbeing. This form should be used to report on a person's current wellbeing state, which can be used for tracking changes or as a reference point for future interactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field, in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your **Date Of Birth** using the calendar input field.
6. Select your **Time Of Day** using the time input field.
7. Enter your **Wellbeing Score** (as a number) in the "Wellbeing Score" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date Of Birth** (date, optional): Select your date of birth from the calendar input field.
* **Time Of Day** (time, optional): Select your time of day from the time input field.
* **Wellbeing Score** (number, optional): Enter a number to represent your current wellbeing score.

## Tips
* Be accurate when filling out this form, as the information collected will be used for tracking and reference purposes.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for assistance.
