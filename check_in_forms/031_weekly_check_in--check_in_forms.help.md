# Weekly Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Weekly Check In form is designed to collect feedback and self-evaluation from users, typically for internal purposes such as employee check-ins or peer reviews. It is best used by those seeking a quick way to gauge their mood and well-being over the week.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title.
2. Answer the questions in the spaces provided, focusing on your current emotional state and any relevant experiences from the week.
3. Ensure you answer each question to the best of your ability, providing as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekly Check In** (`form_title`, required): A text field where you can title your weekly check in, such as "Weekly Update" or "Feeling Good Week".
* **How are you** (`question1`, optional): Describe your current mood and overall feeling. This could be good, bad, or neutral.
* **What do you like** (`question2`, optional): List any enjoyable activities or positive experiences from your week.
* **What don't you like** (`question3`, optional): If applicable, share any challenging or unpleasant experiences from your week.
* **What would you like to see** (`question4`, optional): Suggest new ideas or improvements for future weeks.
* **How did you feel** (`question5`, optional): Reflect on any strong emotions you experienced this week, such as excitement or sadness.
* **How did you feel 2** (`question6`, optional): Another question asking for a more detailed emotional reflection.
* **How did you feel 3** (`question7`, optional): Similar to the previous question, but with a different focus.
* **How did you feel 4** (`question8`, optional): Another question asking for emotional feedback.
* **How did you feel 5** (`question9`, optional): Yet another question asking for emotional feedback.
* **How did you feel 6** (`question10`, optional): Another question asking for emotional feedback.
* **How did you feel 7** (`question11`, optional): Another question asking for emotional feedback.
* **How did you feel 8** (`question12`, optional): Another question asking for emotional feedback.
* **How did you feel 9** (`question13`, optional): Another question asking for emotional feedback.
* **How did you feel 10** (`question14`, optional): The final question asking for emotional feedback.
* **Question 15** (`question15`, optional): A catch-all question for any other thoughts or reflections you'd like to share.

Note: All questions are designed to be open-ended and allow for personal reflection. You can answer as much or as little as you'd like, depending on your comfort level.
