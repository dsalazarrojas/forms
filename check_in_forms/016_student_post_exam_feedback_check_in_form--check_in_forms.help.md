# Student Post Exam Feedback Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback and information from students after an exam. It is designed to capture details about the student's performance, feedback, and next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Provide feedback on your performance and experiences with the exam.
3. Share any learning gaps or areas you need improvement.
4. List any follow-up plans or next steps you have for your academic journey.
5. Leave any final thoughts or comments.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (<name>, text, required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student Email (<email>, email, required)**: Enter your email address associated with your student account.
* **Student Phone (<phone>, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Teacher Name (<name>, text, required)**: Enter the name of your teacher or instructor.
* **Teacher Email (<email>, email, required)**: Enter the email address associated with your teacher's account.
* **Teacher Phone (<phone>, text, optional)**: Enter your teacher's phone number (if they have one).
* **Teacher Department (<department>, text, required)**: Enter the department your teacher is affiliated with.
* **School Name (<name>, text, required)**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Subject (<subject>, text, required)**: Enter the subject you are taking or have taken.
* **Course (<course>, text, required)**: Enter the course name.
* **Grade (<grade>, text, optional)**: Enter your current grade or level in the course.
* **Section (<section>, text, required)**: Enter the section you are taking or have taken.
* **Class (<class>, text, required)**: Enter the class you are taking or have taken.
* **Subjects Taught (<subjects_taught>, text, required)**: Enter the subjects your teacher is teaching.
* **Submit (<submit>, text, optional)**: Click this button to submit your feedback and information.
