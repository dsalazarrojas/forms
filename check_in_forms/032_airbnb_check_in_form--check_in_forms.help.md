# Airbnb Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests for a seamless check-in experience. It gathers necessary details such as personal information, reservation details, and agreement to house rules, policies, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your reservation number for easy identification.
3. Enter your email address and phone number so we can reach you if needed.
4. Select the date you plan to check-in.
5. Choose the check-in time.
6. Specify the number of guests staying with you.
7. List all guest names if they are not traveling with you.
8. Select how you will verify your ID and provide the ID number.
9. Check the options for ID issuing country.
10. Provide vehicle registration (if parking is required).
11. Confirm you have read and understood the property rules.
12. Agree to the noise policy.
13. Agree to the smoking policy.
14. Specify if pets are allowed in the property.
15. Confirm the security deposit status.
16. Confirm you have received WiFi information.
17. Choose how you will receive the keys.
18. Indicate if you would like a property tour.
19. Confirm if you have an emergency contact person.
20. Add any special instructions or pre-existing issues.
21. Sign the form digitally.
22. Sign as the host to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Reservation Number**: Enter the booking reference number you received from us.
* **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached.
* **Check-in Date**: Select the date you plan to check-in to the property.
* **Check-in Time**: Select the time you plan to check-in to the property.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of guests staying with you.
* **All Guest Names**: List all guest names who will be staying with you.
* **ID Verification**: Select how you will verify your ID.
*   - **Yes, Passport**: You will show a passport.
   - **Yes, Driver License**: You will show a driver's license.
   - **Yes, National ID**: You will show a national ID.
   - **Verified online**: Your ID was already verified online.
* **ID Number**: Enter your ID number for verification.
* **ID Issuing Country**: Enter the country that issued your ID.
* **Vehicle Registration**: If parking is required, enter your vehicle registration number.
* **Property Rules Acknowledged**: Confirm you have read and understood the property rules.
*   - **Yes, acknowledged**: You have read and understood the rules.
*   - **False**: You have not read the rules.
* **Noise Policy Agreement**: Confirm your agreement to the noise policy.
*   - **Yes, I agree**: You agree to the noise policy.
*   - **False**: You do not agree.
* **Smoking Policy**: Confirm your agreement to the smoking policy.
*   - **Yes, I agree**: You agree to the smoking policy.
*   - **False**: You do not agree.
* **Pet Policy**: Confirm if pets are allowed in the property.
*   - **No pets**: Pets are not allowed.
*   - **Yes, approved pets**: Pets are allowed.
* **Security Deposit Paid**: Confirm the status of your security deposit.
*   - **Yes, through platform**: You paid through the platform.
*   - **Yes, direct payment**: You paid directly to the host.
*   - **Not required**: No deposit was required.
* **WiFi Password Provided**: Confirm if you have received WiFi information.
*   - **True**: You have received the WiFi information.
*   - **False**: You have not received the WiFi information.
* **Key Collection Method**: Choose how you will receive the keys.
*   - **In-person handover**: You will receive the keys in person.
*   - **Lockbox**: The keys will be provided in a lockbox.
*   - **Smart lock code**: The keys will be provided via a smart lock code.
*   - **Keyless entry**: The keys will be provided digitally.
* **Property Tour Needed**: Confirm if you would like a property tour.
*   - **Yes, please**: A tour is required.
*   - **No, familiar with property**: You are already familiar with the property.
*   - **Self-guided tour**: You would like a self-guided tour.
* **Emergency Contact Provided**: Confirm if you have an emergency contact person.
*   - **True**: You have an emergency contact person.
*   - **False**: You do not have an emergency contact person.
* **Special Instructions**: Add any specific instructions or pre-existing issues you would like to share with the host.
*   - **Host instructions**: Add any special instructions or pre-existing issues you would like to share with the host.
*   - **None**: There are no special instructions.
* **Initial Property Condition**: Add any pre-existing issues you have noticed about the property.
*   - **None**: There are no issues.
*   - **Issues**: Enter the pre-existing issues you have noticed.
* **Guest Signature**: Sign the form digitally to confirm your information.
*   - **Digital signature**: Sign the form digitally.
* **Host Signature**: Sign as the host to confirm.
*   - **Host signature**: Sign as the host to confirm.
* **Check-in Completed**: Confirm the status of your check-in.
*   - **Yes, completed**: Your check-in is completed.
*   - **Partial**: Your check-in is partially completed.
*   - **False**: Your check-in is not completed.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.
*   If you have any pre-existing issues with the property, please list them under "Special Instructions".
*   If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their information under "Emergency Contact Provided".
