# Staff Development Day Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Development Day Sign In form is designed to track and record information about employees' participation in staff development sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Session Name field with the name of the session you are signing in for.
2. Provide your Name.
3. Enter your Email (if available).
4. Enter your Phone number (if available).
5. Check the Attendance field to indicate if you are attending the session.
6. If you are not attending the session, please provide a valid reason for non-attendance in the Comments field.
7. Select your Department, Role, and Learning Areas relevant to the session you are signing in for.
8. Select the sessions you attended.
9. If you are not attending the session due to a valid reason (e.g., conflicting schedules), please provide that reason in the Goals and Outcomes field.
10. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required): This field is where you enter the name of the session you are signing in for.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): This field is where you enter your name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This is where you enter your email address (if available).
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your phone number (if available).
- **Attendance** (`attendance`, select_one, required): This field is where you select whether you are attending the session or not.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): This field is where you enter the time of the session.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): This field is where you enter any additional comments or feedback about the session.
- **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): This is where you select your department (e.g., Sales, Marketing, Support).
- **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you select your role (e.g., Manager, Staff, Admin).
- **Learning Areas** (`learning_areas`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you select the learning areas relevant to the session.
- **Goals and Outcomes** (`goals_and_outcomes`, text, optional): This field is where you enter any goals or outcomes related to the session.
- **Session Attended** (`session_attended`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you select the sessions you attended.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
- If you are experiencing technical difficulties while filling out the form, please contact the IT department for assistance.
