# Mutual Aid Weekly Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mutual Aid Weekly Check In form is used to help individuals assess their well-being across various dimensions of their lives. This form is designed to support self-reflection and provide a snapshot of one's current well-being status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer them based on your current well-being status.
2. For each question, select the number that best describes your current well-being.
3. Make sure to answer all questions honestly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Weekly Check-in** (1, number): This question asks you to rate your overall well-being for the week, using a scale of 1-10.
* **Wellbeing: Self** (2, number): This question asks you to rate how you have been caring for yourself, considering aspects like self-care, personal growth, and emotional regulation. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Support** (3, number): This question asks you to rate how you have been taking care of others, including loved ones, friends, and community. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Caring** (4, number): This question asks you to rate how you have been taking care of others, including family, friends, and community. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Healthy** (5, number): This question asks you to rate your physical health and well-being. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Safety** (6, number): This question asks you to rate how safe you feel, both in your personal space and in your daily activities. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Housing** (7, number): This question asks you to rate the safety and adequacy of your living arrangements. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Culture** (8, number): This question asks you to rate how well you are able to maintain a sense of belonging and community. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Emotional** (9, number): This question asks you to rate how you are managing your emotions, including stress, anxiety, and overall emotional regulation. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Physical** (10, number): This question asks you to rate your physical health and well-being, including energy levels and physical function. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Food** (11, number): This question asks you to rate how you have been taking care of your physical needs, including food and nutrition. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Water** (12, number): This question asks you to rate your access to clean water and hydration. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Sleep** (13, number): This question asks you to rate your sleep quality and duration. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Health** (14, number): This question asks you to rate your overall health and well-being. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Financial** (15, number): This question asks you to rate your financial stability and security. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Support System** (16, number): This question asks you to rate the availability and quality of support in your life, including friends, family, and community. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Transport** (17, number): This question asks you to rate your access to transportation and mobility. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Connection** (18, number): This question asks you to rate your sense of connection and belonging to your community. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Leisure** (19, number): This question asks you to rate how much you have been engaging in activities that bring you joy and relaxation. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Hope** (20, number): This question asks you to rate your overall sense of hope and optimism. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Frustration** (21, number): This question asks you to rate how much you have been experiencing frustration or anger. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Help** (22, number): This question asks you to rate how much you have been seeking or receiving help when needed. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Connect** (23, number): This question asks you to rate how connected you feel to your community and others. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Community** (24, number): This question asks you to rate your sense of belonging to your community. Rate from 1 (not at all) to 10 (very much).
* **Wellbeing: Notes** (25, text): This question is for any additional comments or thoughts you have about your well-being. Please feel free to share any information that will help us better understand your situation.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Take your time to review each question carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for support.

This guide is designed to be as clear and concise as possible, but if you need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
