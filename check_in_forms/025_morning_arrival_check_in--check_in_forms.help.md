# Morning Arrival Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Morning Arrival Check In form is used to report your morning arrival status and any specific needs or requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:
1. Select your morning arrival status (Yes/No).
2. Provide your employee status.
3. Report your health status.
4. Enter any morning arrival notes (if applicable).
5. Select your attendance status (Active/Inactive).
6. Indicate if you have any special requests.
7. Enter your contact number.
8. Add your email address.
9. Confirm your morning arrival check in status (Yes/No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Morning Arrivals** (Yes/No): Check if you are arriving for the morning shift.
* **Employee Status**: Report your current employee status.
* **Health Status**: Provide any health-related information or concerns.
* **Morning Arrival Notes**: Enter any notes or comments about your morning arrival.
* **Attendance Status** (Active/Inactive): Select your current attendance status.
* **Special Requests**: Indicate if you have any special requests or requirements.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Morning Arrival Check In Form 1** (Yes/No): Confirm your morning arrival check in status.
