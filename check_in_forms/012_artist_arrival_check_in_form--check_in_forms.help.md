# Artist Arrival Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Arrival Check In Form is designed to collect essential information from artists or performers arriving at a venue for an event. This form helps event staff and coordinators to manage arrivals, accommodations, and technical requirements for the artists or performers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and band or group name (if applicable)
2. Provide the time and date you will be arriving at the venue
3. Enter the scheduled performance time
4. Provide the name of the venue where you will be performing
5. Enter the name and contact information of the person you'll be working with at the venue
6. Provide your contact phone number and email address
7. If applicable, select the number of team members arriving with you
8. Indicate if you will be bringing any equipment (e.g., instruments, sound equipment, etc.)
9. Check if load-in assistance is required
10. Confirm if a technical rider has been submitted
11. Indicate if you need accommodation or hospitality services
12. Enter any special requests or notes
13. Provide parking location and instructions
14. Enter the name and contact information of the stage manager or contact assigned to you

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist or Performer Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on promotional materials and in the event program.
* **Band or Group Name (if applicable)**: If you are part of a band or group, enter the name here.
* **Check-In Time**: Enter the time you will be arriving at the venue.
* **Check-In Date**: Enter the date you will be arriving at the venue.
* **Scheduled Performance Time**: Enter the time you will be performing during the event.
* **Venue Name**: Enter the name of the venue where you will be performing.
* **Contact Person at Venue**: Enter the name and contact information of the person you will be working with at the venue.
* **Artist Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Artist Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Number of Team Members Arriving**: If you are bringing a team with you, select the number of team members arriving.
* **Equipment Arriving**: Select the type of equipment you will be bringing (e.g., instruments, sound equipment, lighting gear, etc.).
* **Load-In Assistance Required**: Check if you need help with loading-in your equipment.
* **Technical Rider Submitted**: Confirm if a technical rider has been submitted.
* **Accommodation or Hospitality Needed**: Indicate if you need accommodations or hospitality services.
* **Special Requests or Notes**: Enter any special requests or notes you may have.
* **Parking Location and Instructions**: Enter the location and instructions for parking.
* **Stage Manager or Contact Assigned**: Enter the name and contact information of the stage manager or contact assigned to you.
