# Playgroup Child Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Playgroup Child Attendance Form" is a tool designed to help playgroup administrators track the attendance of children in their care. This form helps to gather essential information, such as child names, drop-off and pick-up times, status, and other relevant details, to ensure a smooth and safe experience for all children.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child attending the playgroup.
2. **Drop-off**: Enter the time when the child was dropped off at the playgroup. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 3:00 pm).
3. **Pick-up**: Enter the time when the child was picked up from the playgroup. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 3:00 pm).
4. **Status**: Select one of the following options:
	* **Absent**: The child did not attend the playgroup.
	* **Present**: The child attended the playgroup.
	* **Late**: The child arrived late to the playgroup.
5. **Status Note**: Provide any additional information about the child's status.
6. **Dropped by**: Select one of the following options:
	* **Parent**: The child was dropped off by a parent.
	* **Caregiver**: The child was dropped off by a caregiver.
	* **Staff**: The child was dropped off by a staff member.
7. **Dropped by Note**: Provide any additional information about who dropped off the child.
8. **Dropped at**: Enter the time when the child was dropped off, if different from the "Drop-off" field. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 3:00 pm).
9. **Dropped by Staff**: Select one of the following options:
	* **Yes**: The child was dropped off by a staff member.
	* **No**: The child was not dropped off by a staff member.
10. **Staff Signature**: The staff member responsible for picking up the child must sign this field to confirm they received the child.
11. **Signature Date**: Enter the date of the signature in DD/MM/YY format.
12. **Signature Time**: Enter the time of the signature in 24-hour format.
13. **Playgroup ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to the playgroup.
14. **Form submitted by**: Enter the name of the staff member submitting the form.
15. **Form submission time**: Enter the time when the form was submitted in 24-hour format.
16. **Dropped by Caregiver**: Enter the name of the person who dropped off the child.
17. **Dropped by Caregiver Note**: Provide any additional information about who dropped off the child.
18. **Dropped by Caregiver Relationship**: Select one of the following options:
	* **Relative**: The child's caregiver is a relative.
	* **Friend**: The child's caregiver is a friend.
	* **Other**: The child's caregiver is someone else.
19. **Drop-off status**: Select one of the following options:
	* **On Time**: The child was dropped off on time.
	* **Early**: The child was dropped off early.
	* **Late**: The child was dropped off late.
20. **Drop-off status note**: Provide any additional information about the drop-off status.
21. **Dropped by Staff ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to the staff member who dropped off the child.
22. **Dropped by Caregiver ID**: Enter the ID number assigned to the caregiver who dropped off the child.
23. **Dropped by Caregiver Note**: Provide any additional information about who dropped off the child.
24. **Dropped by Caregiver Name**: Enter the name of the person who dropped off the child.
