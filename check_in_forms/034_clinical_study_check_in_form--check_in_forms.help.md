# Clinical Study Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track participant check-ins after each study visit, enabling researchers to monitor participant engagement, adherence, and health status changes between visits.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
- For each field, ensure you provide accurate and clear information to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any field or require clarification, please contact the study staff or coordinator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant ID Number**: A unique identifier for the participant, typically provided by the study staff.
* **Study Visit Number**: The number of the current study visit.
* **Date of Visit**: The date of the current study visit.
* **-- Participant Health Update --**: This section is for providing an update on any new or ongoing health issues or symptoms since the last visit.
* **Have you experienced any new health issues since your last visit?**: A yes/no question asking if the participant has experienced any new health issues since the last visit.
* If yes, please describe any new symptoms or health changes: This field is for providing a detailed description of any new symptoms or health changes.
* **Have you started any new medications or supplements since the last visit?**: A yes/no question asking if the participant has started any new medications or supplements since the last visit.
* **Current Participant Weight (lbs)**: The participant's current weight in pounds.
* **Current Blood Pressure Reading**: The participant's current blood pressure reading, in the format e.g. 120-80.
* **-- Study Protocol Compliance --**: This section is for providing an update on the participant's compliance with the study protocol since the last visit.
* **Have you followed the study protocol as instructed since last visit?**: A yes/ partially/ no question asking if the participant has followed the study protocol as instructed since the last visit.
* If partially or no, please explain any deviations: This field is for providing a detailed explanation of any deviations from the protocol.
* **-- Logistics and Next Steps --**: This section is for coordinating logistics for upcoming visits, including scheduling and reminders.
* **Scheduled date for next study visit**: The date scheduled for the next study visit.
* **Preferred method for upcoming visit reminders**: The participant's preferred method for receiving reminders for upcoming visits.
* **Check-in Staff Member Name**: The name of the staff member responsible for checking in the participant.

## Tips

- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the study staff or coordinator.
- This form should be filled out for each study visit.
