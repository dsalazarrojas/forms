# Online Coaching Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online coaching check-in form is designed to collect information about client interactions with assigned coaches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above and fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's name.
*   **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the check-in session.
*   **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the check-in session.
*   **Progress Update** (`progress_update`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the progress made during the session.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any relevant notes or comments about the client.
*   **Assigned Coach** (`assigned_coach`, `text`, required: false): Enter the coach's name or ID.
*   **Client Status** (`client_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the client's status (Active or Inactive).
*   **Client Progress** (`client_progress`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client’s progress.
*   **Next Check In Date** (`next_check_in_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date for the next check-in session.
*   **Next Check In Time** (`next_check_in_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time for the next check-in session.
