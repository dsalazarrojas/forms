# Museum Exhibition Volunteer Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about volunteers attending a museum exhibition. It will help us track who, when, and how you will be participating. Please ensure you provide accurate information to help us coordinate the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date you will be attending the exhibition in the "Exhibition Date" field.
2. Choose the time of your shift from "Exhibition Time".
3. Enter your name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Volunteer Email" field.
5. Add your phone number in the "Volunteer Phone" field.
6. Select the shift you will be assigned to from the "Shift" dropdown menu.
7. Confirm your availability for the shift using "Confirmed".
8. Add any comments or additional information in the "Comments" field.
9. (Optional) If you're submitting the form on behalf of someone else, enter their name in the "Form Submitted By" field.
10. Leave "Form Submitted At" as is.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Date** (`exhibition_date`, date, required): Enter the date you will be attending the exhibition.
* **Exhibition Time** (`exhibition_time`, time, required): Select the time of your shift.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Volunteer Email** (`volunteer_email`, email, optional): Provide your email address.
* **Volunteer Phone** (`volunteer_phone`, text, optional): Add your phone number.
* **Shift** (`assigned_shift`, select_one, optional): Choose the shift you will be assigned to from 08:00-12:00, 12:00-16:00, or 16:00-20:00.
* **Confirmed** (`confirmed`, select_multiple, optional): Check "True" if you are confirmed for the shift, and "False" if you are not.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or information about your shift.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, optional): If you're submitting on behalf of someone else, enter their name.
* **Form Submitted At** (`form_submitted_at`, date, optional): Leave this field as is, it is automatically populated.
