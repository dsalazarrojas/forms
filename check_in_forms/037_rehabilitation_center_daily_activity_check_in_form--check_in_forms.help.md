# Rehabilitation Center Daily Activity Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehabilitation Center Daily Activity Check In Form is used to document the daily activities and observations of the residents at the rehabilitation center. It helps staff members to stay updated on the progress and experiences of the residents and ensure that they are receiving the necessary care and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form fields carefully to ensure that you have all the necessary information.
2. Complete each field according to the instructions below.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Observation
* Session Observation (`session_observation`, note, required: false): This field is for making any relevant observations or comments about the session or activity.
* **Example:** "The resident was engaged and cooperative throughout the session."

### Session Notes
* Session Notes (`session_notes`, note, required: false): This field is for adding any additional notes or comments about the session or activity.
* **Example:** "The resident had difficulty with their balance during the session, but with support, they were able to participate."

### Participant Participation
* Participant Participation (`participant_participation`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to indicate whether the resident participated in the session or activity.
* **Example:** "True" (the resident participated) or "False" (the resident did not participate)

### Activity
* Activity (`activity`, select_one, required: false): This field allows you to select the activity that took place during the session.
* **Example:** "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Evening"

### Resident Name
* Resident Name (`resident_name`, text, required: false): This field is for entering the resident's name.
* **Example:** "John Doe"

### Program
* Program (`program`, text, required: false): This field is for selecting the program that the resident is enrolled in.
* **Example:** "Physical Therapy"

### Staff Member
* Staff Member (`staff_member`, select_one, required: false): This field is for selecting the staff member who led the session or activity.
* **Example:** "Staff Member 1", "Staff Member 2", or "Staff Member 3"

### Start Time
* Start Time (`start_time`, time, required: false): This field is for entering the start time of the session or activity.
* **Example:** "9:00 AM"

### End Time
* End Time (`end_time`, time, required: false): This field is for entering the end time of the session or activity.
* **Example:** "12:00 PM"

## Tips
* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, consult with a staff member for guidance.
