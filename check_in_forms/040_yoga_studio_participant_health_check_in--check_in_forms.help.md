# Yoga Studio Participant Health Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Yoga Studio Participant Health Check In" form is used to gather essential information from participants before they join a yoga class at the studio. This form helps us to ensure that you have a safe and healthy environment for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read the form carefully and fill it out accurately.
2. Answer all questions honestly and truthfully.
3. Make sure to sign the waiver to confirm that you have read and understand the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your personal details such as your name, address, phone number, and email address. This information will help us to contact you in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Information** (`Emergency Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide the name, phone number, and relationship of a family member or friend who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
- **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any medical conditions that you have, such as allergies, pregnancy, or recent surgery. This will help us to take necessary precautions for your safety.
- **Medical Conditions Notes** (`Medical Conditions Notes`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your medical conditions that may affect your participation in the yoga class.
- **Signed Waiver** (`Signed Waiver`, `select_one`, required): Please select "I have read and understand the waiver below" to confirm that you have read and understood the waiver. This is a mandatory requirement to participate in the yoga class.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
- Please allow us to review your form before you participate in the yoga class.
- We appreciate your cooperation and understanding in keeping our studio safe and healthy for all participants.
