# Client Weekly Health Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Weekly Health Check In Form is a tool to help you report on your physical and mental health, as well as any challenges or concerns you may be experiencing. This form will help us understand your overall well-being and provide the necessary support to ensure your health and happiness.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct answers for each question based on your current situation.
2. Fill in the text fields with any additional information or comments.
3. Submit the form to share your feedback with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your unique employee identifier, if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department or team name, if applicable.
* **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, date, required): Select the date of the current week to ensure we're tracking your health on the correct period.
* **How is your overall health today?** (`overall_health`, select_one, required): Choose a rating from Very Poor to Excellent to report your current health status.
* **Energy Level - 1 to 10** (`energy_level`, number, required): Rate your energy level, with 1 being Very Low and 10 being Very High.
* **Sleep Quality Last Night** (`sleep_quality`, select_one, required): Choose a rating from Very Poor to Excellent to report your sleep quality.
* **Hours of Sleep Last Night** (`hours_slept`, number, optional): If you're feeling well, enter the number of hours you slept.
* **Any Physical Symptoms or Discomfort?** (`physical_symptoms`, text, optional): Describe any physical symptoms or discomfort you're experiencing, such as headache, body aches, or cough.
* **Are you feeling ill or unwell?** (`feeling_ill`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Slightly' to report your current health status.
* **If ill, what are your symptoms?** (`illness_symptoms`, text, optional): Describe your symptoms, such as fever, cough, or nausea.
* **Do you have a fever?** (`fever_present`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to report if you have a fever.
* **Have you been exposed to anyone who is ill?** (`exposed_illness`, select_one, optional): Choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Unsure' if you've been exposed to someone who is ill.
* **COVID-19 Test Result (if taken)** (`covid_status`, select_one, optional): Choose Not Tested, Positive, Negative, or Inconclusive to report your COVID-19 test result.
* **How is your mood today?** (`mood_today`, select_one, required): Choose a rating from Very Poor to Excellent to report your current mood.
* **Stress Level - 1 to 10** (`stress_level`, number, required): Rate your stress level, with 1 being No stress and 10 being Extreme stress.
* **Anxiety Level** (`anxiety_level`, select_one, optional): Choose from None, Minimal, Moderate, High, or Very High to report your anxiety level.
* **Overall Emotional Wellbeing** (`emotional_wellbeing`, select_one, required): Choose a rating from Poor to Excellent to report your current emotional wellbeing.
* **Working From** (`work_from_location`, select_one, optional): Choose Office, Home, Hybrid, or Other to report where you're working from.
* **Do you feel able to work effectively?** (`feeling_able_to_work`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes', 'No', or 'Partially' to report your ability to work effectively.
* **Exercise This Week** (`exercise_this_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of days of moderate to vigorous exercise you've had this week.
* **How would you rate your hydration?** (`hydration`, select_one, optional): Choose from Poor to Excellent to report your current hydration status.
* **How would you rate your nutrition?** (`nutrition`, select_one, optional): Choose from Poor to Excellent to report the quality and consistency of your meals.
* **Do you need any health or wellness support?** (`need_support`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to report if you need support.
* **What type of support would help?** (`support_type`, text, optional): If you need support, describe the type of support you would find helpful.
* **Additional Comments or Concerns** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or concerns you may have about your health or wellbeing.

**Tips**

* Please answer honestly and accurately to ensure we can provide the best support for you.
* If you're unsure or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
* This form is for your health and wellbeing; please take the time to complete it thoughtfully.
