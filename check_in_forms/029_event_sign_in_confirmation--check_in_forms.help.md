# Event Sign In Confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to confirm an attendee's sign-in and gather some details for event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Select the session you are attending.
3. Choose your check-in time.
4. If you expect to be checked on site due to traffic or other reasons, select the speed check option that best describes your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sign In Confirmation** (`event_sign_in_confirmation`, text, required): This field is not a field in the classical sense, but rather a title that identifies the form. Do not enter any information here.
* **Guest** (`guest`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Session** (`session`, text, optional): Select the session you are attending. This might be a presentation, workshop, or other event activity.
* **Check-in time** (`checkin_time`, time, optional): Choose your check-in time. This could be when you expect to arrive or have already arrived.
* **On site speed check** (`on_site_speed_check`, select_one, optional): If you expect to be checked on site due to traffic or other reasons, select the speed check option that best describes your situation. This field has three options: Speed, Normal, and Late.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your name correctly as it appears on your identification.
- If you're attending multiple sessions, you might need to complete this form multiple times.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact event organizers for assistance.
