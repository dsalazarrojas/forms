# Returning Player Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and verify player information, including their details, policies, and consents, in order to ensure a smooth and secure experience for all players and event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Player Details** section with your personal information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
2. In the **Policy Acknowledgments** section, carefully read and acknowledge each policy by checking the boxes provided.
3. The **Team Details** section is used to provide additional information about your team, if applicable.
4. In the **Season Prep** section, you can add any notes or comments about your preparation for the season.
5. Select one option from the **Player Information** dropdown menu to specify your player information.
6. Fill in your **Contact Details** in the provided text fields.
7. In the **Player Consent** section, select all the options that apply to you.
8. Finally, in the **Season Policies** section, select all the policies you have read and agree to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Details** (player_details): 
  - A text field where you can enter your player's personal details, such as name and email address.
* **Policy Acknowledgments** (policy_acknowledgments): 
  - A section where you can read and acknowledge each policy by checking the boxes provided.
* **Team Details** (team_details): 
  - A text field where you can enter additional information about your team, if applicable.
* **Season Prep** (season_prep): 
  - A text field where you can add any notes or comments about your preparation for the season.
* **Player Information** (player_info): 
  - A dropdown menu where you can select one option that best describes your player information.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details): 
  - A text field where you can enter your contact details, such as phone number and address.
* **Player Consent** (player_consent): 
  - A multiple-choice selection where you can choose all the options that apply to you.
* **Season Policies** (season_policies): 
  - A multiple-choice selection where you can choose all the policies you have read and agree to.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read each policy and acknowledge it before proceeding.
- Fill in all required fields to ensure your information is complete and accurate.
- If you are unsure about any option or field, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
- Submit the form only after you have completed all the required fields and acknowledged all policies.
