# Cybersecurity Incident Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check in cybersecurity incidents reported to the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your information as the user reporting the incident in the "User Information" section.
2.  Provide details about the incident in the "Incident Details" section.
3.  Select the status and type of the incident in the "Status" and "Type" sections, respectively.
4.  Choose the category of the incident in the "Category" section.
5.  Enter a description of the incident in the "Description" section.
6.  Specify who reported the incident in the "Reported By" section.
7.  Enter the date and time the incident was reported in the "Reported At" section.
8.  Select the time of the last update in the "Last Updated" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information
* **User Information** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information as the user reporting the incident.
*   **Incident Details** (`incident_details`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the incident, including any relevant details or context.
*   **Status** (`incident_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the incident from the options "Active" or "Inactive".
*   **Type** (`incident_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether the incident is "Yes" or "No" in relation to the incident type.
*   **Category** (`incident_category`, select_one, optional): Select whether the incident category is "Yes" or "No".
*   **Description** (`incident_description`, text, optional): Enter a detailed description of the incident.
*   **Reported By** (`incident_reported_by`, text, optional): Specify who reported the incident.
*   **Reported At** (`incident_reported_at`, date, optional): Enter the date and time the incident was reported.
*   **Last Updated** (`incident_last_updated`, time, optional): Select the time of the last update to the incident.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that the incident is properly processed and addressed.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please refer to your supervisor or the organization's cybersecurity policy for guidance.
*   Please note that the organization's cybersecurity team will review and respond to the incident as soon as possible.
