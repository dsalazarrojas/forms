# Arrival And Departure Information - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record information about your arrival and departure details for our facility. This information will help us to manage your stay and provide you with the best experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you enter the correct information for your group.
3. If you have any special requests or accommodations, please specify them in the "Special Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name**: Please enter the name of the guest(s) who will be arriving or departing.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the guest(s) to contact them.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the guest(s) for emergency contact purposes.
* **Party Size - Number of Guests**: Specify the number of guests in your group.
* **Type of Guest**: Select the type of guest (Individual, Family, Group, or Business) to help us cater to your needs.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date of your arrival.
* **Expected Arrival Time**: Enter the time of your expected arrival.
* **Transportation Method**: Select the mode of transportation you will use to arrive (Car, Bus, Train, Plane, Taxi or Rideshare, or Other).
* **Where are you coming from**: Enter the city and state or airport code of your departure location.
* **Arrival From**: Enter the location from which you will be arriving.
* **Accommodation Details**: Provide information about the room or space you will be using.
* **Room Number or Space Assignment**: Enter the room or space number assigned to you.
* **Room or Accommodation Type**: Select the type of room or accommodation you will be using (Single room, Double room, Suite, Cabin, Tent, or Other).
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date of your departure.
* **Expected Departure Time**: Enter the time of your expected departure.
* **Length of Stay in Nights**: Specify the number of nights you will be staying with us.
* **Early Checkout Needed**: If you need an early check-out, select the time you require (False, Yes - before 10 AM, Yes - before 8 AM).
* **Special Requests or Accommodations**: Enter any special requests or accommodations you may have (e.g., mobility assistance, dietary restrictions).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth check-in process. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
