# tech_conference_entry_check_in - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees who are checking in for a tech conference. Please provide accurate and complete information for a seamless check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address for contact information.
3. Enter the correct check-in time and date, as this will help with event organization and scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the attendee.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter the attendee's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the attendee's email address to facilitate contact and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the attendee's phone number for further contact.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of check-in for accurate scheduling.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of check-in for accurate event organization.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional event-specific information, such as the session or activity the attendee is attending.
* **Badge Number** (`badge_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the assigned badge number for the attendee.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the attendee.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are checking in for the correct event and date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the event staff for assistance.
* If you are checking in multiple people, please fill out this form for each attendee separately.
