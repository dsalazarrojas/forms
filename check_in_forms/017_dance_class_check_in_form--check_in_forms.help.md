# Dance Class Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Class Check In Form is designed to help teachers and staff quickly and accurately record attendance and other relevant information for students attending dance classes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the student is present for the class.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the date of attendance in the "Date" field.
4. Enter the student's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter any notes or comments about the student in the "Notes" field.
7. Enter the name of the teacher in the "Teacher" field.
8. Enter the time of check out in the "Check Out Time" field.
9. Enter the date of check out in the "Check Out Date" field.
10. Select the attendance status (All, Late, On Time, or Absent) in the "Attendance Status" field.
11. Select the dance style (Ballet, Tap, Jazz, or Hip Hop) in the "Dance Style" field.
12. Enter the name of the class in the "Class Name" field.
13. Enter the level of the class in the "Class Level" field.
14. Enter the day of the class in the "Class Day" field.
15. Enter the time of the class in the "Class Time" field.
16. Enter any notes from the teacher in the "Teacher Notes" field.
17. Enter the count of students in the "Student Count" field.
18. Select the type of class (Recreational, Technique, Performance, or Other) in the "Class Type" field.
19. Select the dance level of the student (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Proficient) in the "Dance Level" field.
20. Select the dance style of the student (Ballet, Tap, Jazz, or Hip Hop) in the "Dance Style" field.
21. Select the dance experience of the student (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Other) in the "Dance Experience" field.
22. Enter the location of the class in the "Class Location" field.
23. Enter the name of the teacher in the "Teacher" field.
24. Enter the gender of the student in the "Gender" field.
25. Enter the age of the student in the "Age" field.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and update previous entries regularly to ensure accurate records.
* Use this form for every class to maintain consistency.
* Use the "Check In" field to quickly indicate if the student is present or not.
* Use the "Notes" field to record any issues or comments about the student.
* Select the correct options from the "Attendance Status", "Dance Style", "Class Type", "Dance Level", "Dance Style", "Dance Experience", and "Class Type" fields based on the student's actual status.
