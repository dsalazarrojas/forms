# Pharmaceutical Representative Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for pharmaceutical representatives to check in with their schedule, company, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name as a pharmaceutical representative in the "Rep Name" field.
2. Enter your company name in the "Company Name" field.
3. Select the date of your check-in from the calendar in the "Check In Date" field.
4. Select the time of your check-in from the time options in the "Check In Time" field.
5. Enter a brief description of your schedule for the day in the "Rep Schedule" field.
6. Select the current status of your check-in as "Approved", "Declined", or "Pending" in the "Check In Status" field.
7. Optionally, provide any additional notes about your check-in in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rep Name (text, required):** Enter your name as a pharmaceutical representative.
* **Company Name (text, optional):** Enter your company name.
* **Check In Date (date, required):** Select the date of your check-in from the calendar.
* **Check In Time (time, required):** Select the time of your check-in from the time options.
* **Rep Schedule (text, optional):** Enter a brief description of your schedule for the day.
* **Check In Status (select_one, required):** Select the current status of your check-in as "Approved", "Declined", or "Pending".
* **Notes (note, optional):** Provide any additional notes about your check-in.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately enter your name and company name to ensure proper identification.
* Select a correct date and time for your check-in to ensure accuracy.
* Be concise and clear in your schedule description.
* Select the correct status for your check-in to ensure proper reporting.
* Use the "Notes" field for any additional information or comments.
