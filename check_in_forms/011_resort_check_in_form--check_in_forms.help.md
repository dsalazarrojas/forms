# Resort Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture guest's check-in information and any special requests they may have during their stay at the resort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Start by entering your user details, then select your date and time of arrival, followed by your preferred arrival method. Enter your guest's name and any other relevant contact information. Finally, add any special requests or notes and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (text): Enter your user's name.
* **Date of Arrival** (date): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the resort.
* **Time of Arrival** (time): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the resort.
* **Arrival Method** (select_one): Select how you plan to arrive at the resort (e.g., Arrive at Front Desk, Arrive by Taxi, Arrive by Private Transfers).
* **Guest Name** (text): Enter the name of the guest(s) checking in.
* **Check In Number** (number): Enter the check-in number assigned to you by the resort.
* **Room Number** (number): Enter the room number you've been assigned.
* **Special Requests** (note): Enter any special requests or notes you may have.
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter a contact number for the guest or their representative.
* **Email** (email): Enter an email address for the guest or their representative.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **User Input 2** (text): Enter any additional user information.
* **User Input 3** (text): Enter any additional user information.
