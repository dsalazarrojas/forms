# Daycare Child Arrival Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about a child's arrival at a daycare center or childcare service. It's crucial for the smooth operation of the facility and ensuring the well-being of the child. Please fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure a successful check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your form before submitting it to the daycare staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrival Details** (`arrival_details`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information about the child's arrival, such as the child's mood or any specific needs they may have.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name as the parent or caregiver of the child.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, optional): Record the exact time of the child's arrival at the daycare center or childcare service.
* **Drop Off Details** (`drop_off_details`, `text`, optional): If the child is being dropped off, please provide any additional information about their drop-off, such as how the child was transported or any relevant contact information for their return pick-up.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the child's name accurately and completely.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the child's birth date for accurate identification and handling.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the daycare staff for assistance.
