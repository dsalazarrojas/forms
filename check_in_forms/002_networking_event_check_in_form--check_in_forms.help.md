# Networking Event Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event attendees check in and provide their information for the networking event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `event_name` field with the name of the event you are currently attending.
2. Enter your `name` and `company` in the respective fields.
3. Provide your `contact_number` (if available).
4. Enter your `email` address.
5. Select your `role` from the provided options (e.g., CEO, Manager, Team Member, or Other).
6. Choose your `department` from the provided options (if applicable) - if no department is available, you can leave this field blank.
7. Enter the number of people in your `team_size`.
8. Enter the duration of time you spent at the event (`time_spent_at_event`).
9. Add any additional `note` (if you have something to share).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_name**: Enter the name of the event you are currently attending. (Required)
* **name**: Enter your name. (Required)
* **company**: Enter your company name. (Required)
* **contact_number**: If you have a contact number, please enter it. (Optional)
* **email**: Enter your email address. (Required)
* **role**: Select your role at your company (e.g., CEO, Manager, Team Member, or Other). (Required)
* **department**: Choose your department (if applicable) - if no department is available, you can leave this field blank. (Optional)
* **team_size**: Enter the number of people in your team. (Required)
* **time_spent_at_event**: Enter the duration of time you spent at the event. (Required)
* **note**: Add any additional comments or notes. (Optional)

## Tips
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact the event organizers.
* Make sure to enter accurate and truthful information.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organizers to clarify.
* This form is designed to be completed quickly and efficiently, so please try to fill it out as accurately as possible.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the event organizers.
