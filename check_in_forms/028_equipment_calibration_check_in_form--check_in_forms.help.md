# equipment_calibration_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_calibration_check_in_form is used to record the check-in of equipment after it has undergone calibration. The form is completed by maintenance teams or equipment managers to verify that the equipment is ready for use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Equipment Id field with the unique identifier of the equipment being checked in.
2. Enter the Calibration Date if the calibration process has taken place on a specific date. If not, leave this field blank.
3. Type in the name of the technician who performed the calibration.
4. Provide any comments or notes about the calibration process in the Comments field (optional).
5. If applicable, select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Equipment Type field to indicate whether the equipment has been calibrated or not.
6. Finally, choose the status of the calibration process: New, In Progress, or Completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Equipment Id** (Equipment Id, text, required): This field is used to input the unique identifier of the equipment being checked in. Please enter the correct ID for accurate tracking.
- **Calibration Date** (Calibration Date, date, optional): This field is used to record the date of the calibration process. If the calibration date is not known, leave this field blank.
- **Technician** (Technician, text, required): Type in the name of the technician who performed the calibration.
- **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Provide any comments or notes about the calibration process in this field.
- **Equipment Type** (Equipment Type, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if the equipment has been calibrated, 'No' otherwise.
- **Status** (Status, select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the calibration process: New, In Progress, or Completed.
 
## Tips
- Ensure to fill out all required fields (Equipment Id and Technician) accurately.
- Enter the correct Equipment Type if applicable.
- Choose the correct status of the calibration process accurately.
- If the calibration date is unknown, leave it blank.
