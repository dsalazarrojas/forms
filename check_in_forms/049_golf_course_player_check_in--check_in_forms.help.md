# Golf Course Player Check In - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather relevant information from golf course players for check-in purposes. This includes selecting their tee time, providing their contact information, and indicating their service needs and group size.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your tee time by choosing from the available options.
2. Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
3. Indicate the number of players in your group.
4. Select any players in your group who have agreed to be contacted.
5. Provide any additional service needs for your group.
6. Indicate your group size.
7. Confirm your group details by selecting the correct option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Check In** (`player_check_in`, text, optional): Enter your name for check-in purposes.
* **Tee Time** (`tee_time`, select_one, required): Select your tee time from the available options.
* **Number of Players** (`num_players`, number, optional): Enter the number of players in your group.
* **Player Names** (`player_names`, select_multiple, optional): Select any players in your group who have agreed to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, select_one, optional): Select your group size from the available options.
* **Service Needs** (`service_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select any service needs for your group.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, required): Confirm your group details by selecting the correct option.

## Tips
* Ensure you select your tee time correctly to avoid conflicts with other players.
* Enter your phone number and email address accurately to ensure timely communication.
* Be honest about your group size and service needs to ensure a smooth experience for everyone.
