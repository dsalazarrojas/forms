# Team Task Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Task Check In Form is designed to gather information about team tasks and their status. This form is intended for team members to check in on their tasks, providing updates on their progress, blockers, and comments. It helps the project lead and team lead to track the status of tasks and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for "Team Task Check In" (page 1), "Project" (page 2), "Task" (page 3), and other fields as required.
2. Enter your comments and comments about task status in the "Comments" (page 4) field.
3. Select the relevant options for "Blockers" (page 5), "Blocker Comments" (page 6), and other fields as required.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" (page 7) field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" (page 8) field.
6. Select the relevant options for "Team Members" (page 9), "Location" (page 10), "Timezone" (page 11), "Timezone Offset" (page 12), and "User Timezone" (page 13) fields.
7. Select the relevant options for "Blocker Lead" (page 14) and "Project Lead" (page 15) fields.
8. Enter your comments in the "Blocker Comments" (page 16) field.
9. Select the relevant options for "Team Lead Status" (page 17), "Project Lead Status" (page 18), and other fields as required.
