# customer_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic customer information for checking in customers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the customer's information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the customer's last name.
* **Reason for visit** (`reason_for_visit`, text, optional): Enter the reason for the customer's visit.
* **Date of visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date of the customer's visit in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time of the customer's visit in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Reservation ID** (`reservation_id`, text, optional): Enter the customer's reservation ID if applicable.
* **Customer ID** (`customer_id`, text, optional): Enter the customer's ID if applicable.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`, note, optional): Enter any additional instructions or notes about the customer's visit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting.
* If a field is not applicable, you can leave it blank.
* If you need to add additional information, use the "Instructions" field.
