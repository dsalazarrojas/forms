# Fitness Weekly Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Weekly Check In Form is designed to gather information from fitness members to help coaches monitor and provide feedback on their progress. This form is intended to be filled out on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Enter your Member Email, Coach Name, and Check In Date and Time (if required).
3. Describe your Workout Goal and Progress Photos (if applicable).
4. Add any Comments or Coach Comments (if applicable).
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Email (`member_email`):
* Member Email (email, optional): Enter your email address to associate with your weekly check-in.

### Coach Name (`coach_name`):
* Coach Name (text, optional): Enter the name of your coach or mentor.

### Workout Goal (`work_out_goal`):
* Workout Goal (text, optional): Describe your fitness goals for the week, such as "run 3 miles" or "lift 10kg."

### Progress Photos (`progress_photos`):
* Progress Photos (text, optional): Upload or link photos of your progress, such as workout selfies or progress shots.

### Comments (`comments`):
* Comments (text, optional): Add any additional comments you want to share with your coach.

### Coach Comments (`coach_comments`):
* Coach Comments (text, optional): Leave notes or guidance for your coach to review.

### Check In Date (`check_in_date`):
* Check In Date (date, optional): Enter the date of your weekly check-in.

### Check In Time (`check_in_time`):
* Check In Time (time, optional): Enter the time of your weekly check-in.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to collect and use this data before proceeding.
* If you are unsure, please contact the form author at form@gic.mx.
* This form is intended for fitness coaches to monitor and provide feedback on member progress.
