# Museum Group Tour Pre Visit Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Group Tour Pre Visit Check In form is designed to collect essential information from tour group leaders and members before a visit to the museum. This form helps the museum staff prepare for the visit, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the group and the staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled in. The fields marked as "required" are essential for a successful visit.
3. Use the provided options for fields like "Group Type" and "Age Group" to help the staff understand your group's needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tour Leader Name** (`tour_leader_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person leading the tour group.
* **Tour Date** (`tour_date`, date, required): Choose the date of your visit to the museum. Please use the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Group Size** (`group_size`, number, required): Indicate the number of people in your group.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Provide a contact number where the museum staff can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): If you have a specific address for the group, feel free to provide it to help the museum staff with parking or transportation arrangements.
* **Group Type** (`group_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of group you're leading:
	+ School
	+ Corporate
	+ Other
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, select_multiple, optional): Select the age group(s) of the people in your group (Adult, Child, Senior).
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Choose the estimated time your group will arrive at the museum.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): If there are any special needs or requirements for your group, please describe them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact's phone number, please provide it.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): If you have an emergency contact's email address, please provide it.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you may have about your visit.
