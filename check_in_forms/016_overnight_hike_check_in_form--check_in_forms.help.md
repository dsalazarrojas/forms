# overnight-hike-check-in-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check in participants for overnight hikes, ensuring a smooth and safe experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal information (participant name, email, phone, address, and any additional notes) in the "Participant Info" section.
2. Enter your name and any other details that apply to you in the "Participant Name" section.
3. Provide your email address for communication with the event organizers and leaders.
4. Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
5. Enter your address for easy contact and navigation.
6. Select a date and time for your expected arrival and departure from the "Participant Datetime" section.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments you have for the event organizers or leaders in the "Participant Notes" section.
8. Provide information about the event, including the event name, start time, finish time, start date, location, and any additional notes.
9. Enter your name and contact information as the event leader or organizer in the "Event Info" section.
10. Enter any emergency contact information and notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info**: Enter your personal details for easy contact and reference.
- **Participant Name**: Enter your name for identification purposes.
- **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for communication with the event organizers and leaders.
- **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
- **Participant Address**: Enter your address for easy contact and navigation.
- **Participant Datetime**: Select a date and time for your expected arrival and departure.
- **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments you have for the event organizers or leaders.
- **Event Info**: Provide information about the event, including its name, start time, finish time, start date, location, and any additional notes.
- **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
- **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
- **Event Finish Time**: Enter the finish time of the event.
- **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date of the event.
- **Event Location**: Enter the event location.
- **Event Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.
- **Leader Name**: Enter your name as the event leader or organizer.
- **Leader Email**: Enter your email address for communication with participants.
- **Leader Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
- **Event Status**: Enter the current status of the event (e.g., "confirmed", "cancelled", " postponed").
- **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
- **Notes to Leader**: Enter any notes or comments for the event leader or organizer.
- **Notes from Leader**: Enter any notes or comments from the event leader or organizer.
- **Notes from Participant**: Enter any notes or comments from the participant.
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Notes**: Enter any emergency contact information or notes.
