# Event Bracelet Pickup Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Event Bracelet Pickup Registration process. Please fill out the form to collect event information and assign a unique identifier to each attendee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you have received a wristband number before submitting the form.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Info (Page 1)** (`id: 1`): This is the main section of the form, where you will enter your event information.
* **Bracelet Type (Page 2)** (`id: 2`): Please select the type of bracelet you have been assigned.
* **First Name (Page 3)** (`id: 3`): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Page 4)** (`id: 4`): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Page 5)** (`id: 5`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Page 6)** (`id: 6`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth (Page 7)** (`id: 7`): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Wristband Number (Page 8)** (`id: 8`): Enter the unique identifier assigned to you for event access control and attendee tracking.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have received your wristband number before completing the form.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
