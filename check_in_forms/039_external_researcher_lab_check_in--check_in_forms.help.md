# External Researcher Lab Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the details of a researcher's visit to a lab. It includes information about the researcher, the lab, and the visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Researcher Name** of the person visiting the lab.
2. Enter the **Lab Name** where the researcher will be visiting.
3. Select the **Arrival Time** of the researcher's visit.
4. Add any relevant **Researcher Notes** about the visit.
5. Sign the form with your **Researcher Signature**.
6. Enter any **Lab Notes** about the visit.
7. Sign the form with the **Lab Signature**.
8. Enter the **Contact Name** of the person the researcher will be visiting with.
9. Enter the **Contact Email** of the person the researcher will be visiting with.
10. Enter the **Contact Phone** number of the person the researcher will be visiting with.
11. Enter the **Lab Contact** name of the person at the lab who will be interacting with the researcher.
12. Enter the **Lab Phone** number of the lab.
13. Enter the **Visit Date**, which is the date of the visit.
14. Select the **Visit Time**, when the researcher will arrive at the lab.
15. Enter the **Visit Length**, how long the visit will last.
16. Enter the **Visit Researcher Count**, how many researchers will be visiting during this time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Researcher Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the researcher visiting the lab.
* **Lab Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the lab where the researcher will be visiting.
* **Arrival Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the researcher's arrival at the lab.
* **Researcher Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes about the researcher's visit.
* **Researcher Signature** (text, optional): Sign the form with your researcher's signature.
* **Lab Notes** (note, optional): Add any notes about the lab's interaction with the researcher.
* **Lab Signature** (text, optional): Sign the form with the lab's signature.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person the researcher will be visiting with.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email of the contact person the researcher will be visiting with.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person the researcher will be visiting with.
* **Lab Contact** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person at the lab who will be interacting with the researcher.
* **Lab Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the lab.
* **Visit Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the visit.
* **Visit Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the visit.
* **Visit Length** (text, optional): Enter the length of the visit.
* **Visit Researcher Count** (text, optional): Enter the number of researchers visiting during this time.
