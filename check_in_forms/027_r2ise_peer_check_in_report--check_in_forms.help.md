# r2ise_peer_check_in_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "r2ise_peer_check_in_report" form is used to record peer check-ins, which include signing in, selecting check-in type, choosing location, and recording check-in status, date, and time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your sign-in information in the "Sign In" field.
2. Select the type of check-in you are performing in the "Check In Type" field.
3. If applicable, enter your location in the "Location" field.
4. Record your check-in status by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Check In" field.
5. Enter the date of your check-in in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the time of your check-in in the "Time" field.
7. Optionally, if needed, enter the date range and time range for your check-in in the "Date Range" and "Time Range" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Sign In
• **Sign In** (Text): Enter your sign-in information.

### Peer Check In
• **Peer Check In** (Text): Enter your peer check-in information.

### Check In Type
• **Check In Type** (Select One): Select the type of check-in you are performing.
	+ Options: peer, peer sign in

### Location
• **Location** (Text): Enter your location, if applicable.

### Check In
• **Check In** (Select Multiple): Record your check-in status.
	+ Options: Yes, No

### Date
• **Date** (Date): Enter the date of your check-in.

### Time
• **Time** (Time): Enter the time of your check-in.

### Date Range
• **Date Range** (Text): Optionally, enter the date range for your check-in, if needed.

### Time Range
• **Time Range** (Text): Optionally, enter the time range for your check-in, if needed.
