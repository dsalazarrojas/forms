# Warehouse Delivery Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Warehouse Delivery Check In Form is used to document and track delivery details of items stored in the warehouse. This form helps ensure that all relevant information is accurately captured and recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the delivery details in the relevant fields, such as delivery location, status, and weight.
2. Select the delivery status from the options provided.
3. Choose the correct unit of measurement for the delivery length, width, and height.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about the delivery.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the delivery, including any relevant details.
* **Driver Details** (`driver_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person delivering the items.
* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the make, model, and any other relevant details about the vehicle used for delivery.
* **Delivery Location** (`delivery_location`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple locations where the delivery will take place.
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_one, required): Choose a status for the delivery from the options provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the delivery.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of the person responsible for the delivery.
* **Dock Assignment** (`dock_assignment`, select_one, required): Select the dock where the delivery will take place.
* **Route Assignment** (`route_assignment`, select_one, required): Select the route that the delivery will follow.
* **Weight of Delivery** (`delivery_weight`, number, required): Enter the weight of the delivery in kilograms.
* **Size of Delivery** (`delivery_size`, number, required): Enter the size of the delivery in centimeters.
* **Length of Delivery** (`delivery_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the delivery in centimeters.
* **Width of Delivery** (`delivery_width`, number, required): Enter the width of the delivery in centimeters.
* **Height of Delivery** (`delivery_height`, number, required): Enter the height of the delivery in centimeters.
* **Length of Delivery Unit** (`delivery_length_unit`, select_one, required): Choose the unit of measurement for the length of delivery.
* **Width of Delivery Unit** (`delivery_width_unit`, select_one, required): Choose the unit of measurement for the width of delivery.
* **Height of Delivery Unit** (`delivery_height_unit`, select_one, required): Choose the unit of measurement for the height of delivery.
