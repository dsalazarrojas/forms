# Workshop Participant Expectation Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect workshop participant information and ensure a smooth and productive workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked as such in the form.
2. For fields with options, select the relevant option that best describes your situation.
3. Provide a clear and concise answer for fields with a text type.
4. For fields with a select_multiple type, select all relevant options.
5. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Expectations** (`workshop_expectations`): Please describe your expectations from the workshop, what you hope to achieve, or what you want to get out of it.
* **Participant Readiness** (`participant_readiness`): Select 'True' if you feel ready for the workshop, 'False' otherwise.
* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`): Select all topics that interest you the most.
* **Workshop Participant Comments** (`workshop_participant_comments`): Please provide any additional comments or information about your participation in the workshop.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`): If you have a phone number, please enter it. This is optional.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): If you have been assigned a tool for the workshop, please enter its name. This is optional.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`): If you know your Form ID, please enter it. This is optional.
* **Category** (`category`): Please select the relevant category for the workshop.
* **Description** (`description`): Briefly describe the workshop and its purpose.
* **Title** (`title`): Enter the title of the workshop.
* **Output File** (`output_file`): If you have a file to upload, please do so. This is optional.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the workshop organizers for clarification.
