# employee_feedback_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_feedback_check_in_form is a tool for employees to provide feedback on their check-in experience, helping managers to identify areas of improvement and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Check In Form" field and enter your comment or description.
2.  Provide your feedback on your check-in experience in the "Feedback" field.
3.  Rate your satisfaction level with your check-in experience using the "Rating" field.
4.  Optionally, provide additional comments or suggestions in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Form (checkin_form)**:
	+ **Label:** Check In Form
	+ **Type:** Text field
	+ **Purpose:** Enter a brief comment or description of your check-in experience.
* **Feedback**:
	+ **Label:** Feedback
	+ **Type:** Text field
	+ **Purpose:** Provide your feedback on your check-in experience, such as any issues or concerns you faced.
* **Rating (rating)**:
	+ **Label:** Rating
	+ **Type:** Number field
	+ **Purpose:** Rate your overall satisfaction level with your check-in experience, where 1 is low and 5 is high.
* **Comments (comments)**:
	+ **Label:** Comments
	+ **Type:** Text field
	+ **Purpose:** Provide additional comments or suggestions for improvement, if any.

## Tips
* Please be honest and thorough in your feedback to help us improve our check-in process.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please describe them clearly and concisely.
* Ratings and comments will be reviewed and addressed by management to make data-driven decisions.
